# Photography Session Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment and contact information from customers after a photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone** fields.
2. Provide details about your photography session, including the **Session Date**, **Session Time**, and **Service Type** (e.g., Photography Session, Portrait Session, Wedding Session).
3. Select the payment method you would like to use (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Credit Card).
4. Enter the payment amount and payment date.
5. Choose whether you would like to receive the invoice electronically or in print.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your photography session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your photography session.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of photography service you received (e.g., Photography Session, Portrait Session, Wedding Session).
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount due for the session.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method you would like to use for payment (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Credit Card).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to make the payment.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Invoice Sent To** (`invoice_sent_to`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you would like to receive the invoice electronically or in print.

Tips:

* Please ensure that the payment information is accurate and complete to avoid any delays in processing your payment.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your invoice, please contact us at your convenience.
* We appreciate your prompt payment to help us continue providing exceptional service.
