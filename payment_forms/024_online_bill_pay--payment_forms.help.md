# online_bill_pay - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_bill_pay form is used to collect information for online bill payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields: Pay Amount, Invoice Number, Merchant ID, Transaction ID, and Total Amount.
* Select the payment method and status.
* Choose the account type (if applicable).
* Enter any other relevant information, such as email, phone number, and billing address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pay Amount** (`amount`, required): Enter the amount to be paid.
* **Pay Date** (`pay_date`, required): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_number`, required): Enter the invoice number associated with the payment.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, optional): Select the account type.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, optional): Select the account type.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter the phone number.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, optional): Select the billing address.
* **Pay Method** (`pay_method`, required): Select the payment method used.
* **Pay Status** (`pay_status`, optional): Select the payment status.
* **Pay Result** (`pay_result`, required): Select whether the payment was successful or not.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, optional): Enter any error message related to the payment.
* **Gateway Response** (`gateway_response`, optional): Enter the response from the payment gateway.
* **Merchant ID** (`merchant_id`, required): Enter the merchant ID.
* **Transaction ID** (`transaction_id`, required): Enter the transaction ID.
* **Currency** (`currency`, optional): Select the currency used for the payment.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, optional): Select the tax rate applied to the payment.
* **Tax Amount** (`tax_amount`, optional): Select whether tax was applied to the payment.
* **Shipping Fee** (`shipping_fee`, optional): Select whether shipping fee was applied to the payment.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, required): Select the total amount paid.
* **Submit** (`submit`, optional): Submit the form to process the payment.
