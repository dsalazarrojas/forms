# Purchase Receipt Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purchase Receipt Verification Form is used by customers to provide information about a purchase they made with a business. This form helps the business verify that the purchase was indeed made and is a part of their payment process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with accurate and honest information about your purchase.
2. Make sure to select the correct payment method you used for the purchase.
3. Upload a clear image of the receipt if required.
4. Sign the form with your signature if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please fill out your name and contact information accurately.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you made the purchase.
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount paid for the purchase.
* **Receipt Image** (`receipt_image`, text, optional): If required by the business, please upload a clear image of your receipt.
* **Receipt Information** (`receipt_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the receipt and any relevant details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you used for the purchase, such as Cash, Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): If required by the business, please sign the form with your signature.
* **Business Signature** (`business_signature`, text, optional): If required by the business, please sign the form with an employee or representative's signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact the business for assistance.
* If you are required to upload a receipt image, please make sure it is clear and legible.
