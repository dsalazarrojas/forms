# paypal_newsletter_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "paypal_newsletter_subscription_form" is a tool for users to sign up for a newsletter subscription. It collects information about the user and their preferences for the subscription duration and format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your information in the "User info" section.
2. Choose your preferred format (digital or print) in the "Format pref" section.
3. Select the subscription duration that suits your needs (1 month, 3 months, or 6 months) in the "Subscription duration" section.
4. Choose how often you'd like to receive the newsletter ("Yes" or "No") in the "Subscription frequency" section.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Add any additional information in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User info (text)
 * **User info** (`text`, required/optional): This is the user's personal details, which can include any information they want to provide to help with subscription management.

### Format pref (select one)
 * **Format pref** (`select_one`, required/optional): This section asks whether you'd like to receive the newsletter in a digital or print format. Please choose one of these options.

### Subscription duration (select one)
 * **Subscription duration** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the duration of your subscription. Options are 1 month, 3 months, or 6 months.

### Subscription frequency (select multiple)
 * **Subscription frequency** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): This section asks whether you'd like to receive the newsletter "Yes" or "No" on a regular basis. Please select one or both options.

### Email (email)
 * **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive the newsletter.

### Phone (text)
 * **Phone** (`text`, required/optional): This field is intended for users to enter their phone number, but it's not currently being used. If you have any concerns or need to reach out to us, please use the "Notes" field instead.

### Notes (note)
 * **Notes** (`note`, required/optional): If you have any additional information or concerns, please add them here.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as possible for accurate and efficient processing.
* You can skip any fields if you're unsure or don't have the information.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please use the "Notes" field to contact us.
