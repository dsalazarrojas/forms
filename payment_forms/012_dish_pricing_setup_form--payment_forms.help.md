# Dish Pricing Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dish Pricing Setup Form is a crucial tool for managing menu pricing and updates. This form collects and processes essential information for setting up and maintaining accurate menu prices.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your options for "Menu Items". Choose whether or not to include menu items in the pricing setup.
2. Enter the effective price for the selected menu items.
3. Choose from the available options for price updates (Update 1, Update 2, Update 3, Update 4, or Update 5).
4. Select the update frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly).
5. Set a price update date for the selected price updates.
6. Choose a price update time (if applicable).
7. Select the currency for the effective price (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, or JPY).
8. Provide a reason for the price update (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
9. Describe the menu items (optional, but recommended for better understanding and reference).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menu Items** (select_one): Choose whether or not to include menu items in the pricing setup. This field allows you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether to include menu items.
* **Effective Price** (number): Enter the effective price for the selected menu items. This is the price that will be applied to the menu.
* **Price Updates** (select_one): Select from the available options (Update 1, Update 2, Update 3, Update 4, or Update 5) to choose the type of price update to be applied.
* **Update Frequency** (select_one): Choose the update frequency for the selected price updates (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly).
* **Price Update Date** (date): Set a price update date for the selected price updates.
* **Price Update Time** (time): Choose a price update time (if applicable).
* **Currency** (select_one): Select the currency for the effective price (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, or JPY).
* **Price Update Reason** (text): Provide a reason for the price update (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
* **Menu Items Description** (text): Describe the menu items (optional, but recommended for better understanding and reference).
