# Employee Reimbursement Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable employees to submit payment requests for expenses related to work-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Employee ID in the "Employee Id" field.
2. Enter the payment date in the "Payment Date" field.
3. Select the payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
4. Provide a brief description of the expense in the "Description" field (optional).
5. Enter the amount of the expense in the "Amount" field.
6. Select your current employee status from the "Employee Status" dropdown menu (optional).
7. Select the status of your payment request from the "Payment Status" dropdown menu (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID to identify you as the requestor.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you paid for the expense.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you paid for the expense. Options include Bank, Cash, and Credit Card.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the expense (e.g., travel, training, etc.).
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount paid for the expense.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current status as an employee. Options include Active and Inactive.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your payment request. Options include Pending, Approved, and Rejected.
