# music_program_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_program_donation_form is a form designed to collect information for the music program donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of the donation from the "category" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the music program in the "description" field.
3. Enter the amount donated in the "amount" field.
4. Fill out the donor contact information in the "donor_name," "donor_email," "donor_phone," "donor_address," "donor_city," and "donor_state" fields.
5. Select the school from the "school_id" field.
6. Choose the payment method from the "payment_method" field.
7. Specify the type of payment from the "payment_type" field.
8. Enter the date and time of the gift in the "gift_date" and "gift_time" fields.
9. Add any additional comments in the "notes" field.
10. Select the assigned user from the "assigned_user" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_id**: This number is used to identify the form.
* **category**: Select the category of the donation (e.g., Payment Forms or Donor Forms).
* **description**: A brief description of the music program.
* **amount**: The amount donated.
* **donor_name**: Donor name.
* **donor_email**: Donor email address.
* **donor_phone**: Donor phone number.
* **donor_address**: Donor mailing address.
* **donor_city**: City of the donor.
* **donor_state**: State of the donor.
* **donor_zip**: Zip code of the donor.
* **school_id**: The school associated with the donation.
* **payment_method**: The method of payment (e.g., Online or In-Person).
* **payment_type**: The type of payment (e.g., One Time, Ongoing, or Monthly).
* **gift_date**: The date of the gift.
* **gift_time**: The time of the gift.
* **notes**: Any additional comments about the donation.
* **assigned_user**: The user assigned to the donation.
* **assigned_tool**: The tool assigned to the donation.
* **notes**: Any additional comments about the donation.
