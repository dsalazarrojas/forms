# international_digital_services_invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate an international digital services invoice for a customer. It collects relevant information for billing and payment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the customer's billing information.
2. Enter the customer's email address.
3. Enter the billing amount.
4. Select the payment method.
5. Enter payment details in the notes field.
6. Select the billing status.
7. Enter the billing date and time.
8. Select any additional custom fields (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Billing Information** (`billing_info`, text, optional): Enter the customer's billing information, including their name and address.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Billing Amount** (`billing_amount`, number, optional): Enter the billing amount for the international digital services used.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method for the international digital services (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional payment details in the notes field.
* **Billing Status** (`billing_status`, select_one, optional): Select the billing status for the international digital services (e.g., Option 4, Option 5, or Option 6).
* **Billing Date** (`billing_date`, date, optional): Enter the billing date for the international digital services.
* **Billing Time** (`billing_time`, time, optional): Enter the billing time for the international digital services.
* **Custom Fields** (`custom_fields`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional custom fields required for the international digital services (e.g., Option 7, Option 8, or Option 9).

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all relevant fields to generate a complete international digital services invoice.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
