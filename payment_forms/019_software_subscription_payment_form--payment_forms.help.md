# software_subscription_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The software_subscription_payment_form is designed to collect payment information and subscription details from users for their software subscriptions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the software_subscription_payment_form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided (credit card or bank transfer).
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. Provide your address for billing or subscription purposes.
4. Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
5. Enter your Date of Birth for subscription eligibility verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more payment methods you want to use for your software subscription. Options include credit card and bank transfer.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification for subscription purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Provide your billing address for subscription purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your subscription.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter your Date of Birth for subscription eligibility verification.

## Tips
- Make sure to select only one payment method from the options provided.
- Ensure your email address is correct and active to receive subscription updates.
- Enter your Date of Birth accurately to ensure subscription eligibility verification.
