# watch_design_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer and order information for pre-orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Details** field with your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your **Watch Details** in the corresponding field, including the type of watch you are pre-ordering.
3. Provide **Order Info** such as the date of purchase, purchase date, and order notes in the respective field.
4. Enter the total price of the pre-ordered items.
5. Add any additional information about your payment method in the **Payment Info** field.
6. If you have any special instructions or comments for the order, add them in the **Order Notes** field.
7. Read and agree to the **Order Terms** in the provided field.
8. Finally, confirm your order by filling out the **Confirm Order** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to contact you about your pre-order.
* **Watch Details** (`watch_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of watch you are pre-ordering.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the date of purchase, purchase date, and any other order-related information.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, text, required/optional): Enter the total price of the pre-ordered items.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about your payment method, such as credit card details.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or instructions about your pre-order.
* **Order Terms** (`order_terms`, text, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the pre-order.
* **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, text, required/optional): Confirm your order by filling out this field.
