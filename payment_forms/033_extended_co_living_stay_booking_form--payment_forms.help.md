# extended_co_living_stay_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The extended co-living stay booking form is a tool used to book and manage co-living stays for tenants. It captures essential information about the tenant, their stay details, and payment preferences. This form is used for internal purposes and is essential for efficient and accurate co-living stay management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information accurately and completely.
2. Review each field to ensure that you are providing the correct type of data.
3. For fields with options, select the relevant choice that applies to your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name**: `tenant_name` (<label: Tenant Name, type: text, required: false>): Enter the name of the person booking the co-living stay.
* **Email**: `email` (<label: Email, type: email, required: false>): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: `phone` (<label: Phone, type: text, required: false>): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: `address` (<label: Address, type: text, required: false>): Enter your current address for billing purposes.
* **Preferred Room Type**: `preferred_room_type` (<label: Preferred Room Type, type: select_multiple, required: false>): Select the type of room you prefer for your stay. Options: Yes, No.
* **Preferred Room Size**: `preferred_room_size` (<label: Preferred Room Size, type: select_one, required: false>): Select the size of room you prefer for your stay. Options: Yes, No.
* **Check In Date**: `check_in_date` (<label: Check In Date, type: date, required: false>): Enter the date you plan to check in for your stay.
* **Check Out Date**: `check_out_date` (<label: Check Out Date, type: date, required: false>): Enter the date you plan to check out from your stay.
* **Booking Length**: `booking_length` (<label: Booking Length, type: number, required: false>): Enter the number of days you plan to stay.
* **Booking Status**: `booking_status` (<label: Booking Status, type: select_one, required: false>): Select the status of your booking. Options: Active, Inactive.
* **Payment Method**: `payment_method` (<label: Payment Method, type: select_multiple, required: false>): Select your payment method. Options: Yes, No.
* **Payment Amount**: `payment_amount` (<label: Payment Amount, type: number, required: false>): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Terms**: `payment_terms` (<label: Payment Terms, type: text, required: false>): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
