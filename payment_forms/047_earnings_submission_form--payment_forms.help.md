# Earnings Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earnings Submission Form is a tool used to submit earnings-related information by employees or their representatives. This form ensures accurate and complete submission of data such as pay date, pay amount, job number, employee name, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate through the form's multiple pages to fill in the required fields.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in before proceeding to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (<employee_name>, text, required/optional)**: Please enter the name of the employee whose earnings you are submitting.
* **Job Number (<job_number>, text, required/optional)**: Enter the job number associated with the employee's job.
* **Pay Date (<pay_date>, date, required/optional)**: Select the date when the payment was made.
* **Pay Amount (<pay_amount, number, required/optional)**: Enter the amount paid to the employee.
* **Notes (<notes, note, required/optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the earnings submission.
* **Email (<email, email, required/optional)**: Enter the email address of the employee or their representative.
* **Phone (<phone, text, required/optional)**: Enter the phone number of the employee or their representative.
* **Job Title (<job_title, text, required/optional)**: Enter the job title of the employee whose earnings are being submitted.
