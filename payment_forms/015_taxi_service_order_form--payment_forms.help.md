# Taxi Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Taxi Service Order Form is a tool designed to collect customer information for taxi service orders. By filling out this form, customers can provide necessary details for a smooth and efficient pickup and payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Information** section with your name and any other relevant contact details.
2. Enter your **Pickup Details** in the text field.
3. Select your preferred **Taxi Type** from the options provided.
4. Choose whether the order is for **Date and Time**.
5. Enter the **Date** of your pickup.
6. Select the **Start Time** for your pickup.
7. Enter the **End Time** of your pickup.
8. Enter your **Address** where the taxi will be picking you up.
9. Provide your **Email** address.
10. Enter your **Phone** number.
11. Add any **Notes** about your order.
12. Choose whether a **Assigned Taxi** is required.
13. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, required): This section collects the customer's name and contact details.
* **Pickup Details** (Pickup Details, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your pickup details.
* **Taxi Type** (Taxi Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of taxi service you require (e.g., standard, luxury, etc.).
* **Date and Time** (Date and Time, select_one, optional): Choose whether the order is for a date and time (e.g., yes for a specific date and time).
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of your pickup (if selected in the previous field).
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, optional): Enter the start time of your pickup (if selected in the previous field).
* **End Time** (End Time, time, optional): Enter the end time of your pickup (if selected in the previous field).
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter the address where the taxi will be picking you up.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Assigned Taxi** (Assigned Taxi, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether a specific taxi is assigned to you for this order.
* **Assigned Taxi** (Assigned Taxi, select_multiple, optional): This field is currently marked as optional but has the same label as another field, this might be a duplication. The field can be removed and the original label and option can be reused.
