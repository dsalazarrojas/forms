# Public Transit Improvement Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Transit Improvement Donation Form is designed to allow users to support the improvement of public transit systems. This form collects information about the user's preferred payment method, donation amount, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
2. Enter the amount you wish to donate.
3. Fill in your name, email, phone number, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, Select Multiple, required): Choose how you want to pay for your donation (Cash, Card, Check, or Payroll).
* **donation_amount** (`donation_amount`, Number, required): Enter the amount you want to donate.
* **name** (`name`, Text, required): Enter your full name.
* **email** (`email`, Email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **address** (`address`, Note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your donation (optional).

## Tips

- Make sure to enter a valid email address for our team to contact you.
- If you have any specific requests or comments, please include them in the address field.
- You can select multiple payment methods if you want to.
- If you're unsure about the payment options, you can always contact our team for help.
