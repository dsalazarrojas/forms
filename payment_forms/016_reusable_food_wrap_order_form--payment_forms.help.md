# Reusable Food Wrap Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place orders for reusable food wraps. It helps the food wrap business process orders more efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details on **Order Details** page 1.
   This page is where you will enter the main information about your order.

2. Enter your **Customer Info** on page 2.
   Here, you will add your contact details, such as name and email address.

3. Choose a **Payment Method** on page 3.
   Select the method you'd like to use for payment. Currently, you have options for Payment Method 1 and Payment Method 2.

4. Enter the date for your **Order Date** on page 4.
   This is where you will select the date for your order.

5. Enter the time for your **Order Time** on page 5.
   Here, you will choose the time for your order.

6. Add any additional **Order Notes** on page 6.
   You can use this field to provide any specific details or requests for your order.

7. Complete your **Customer Name** on page 7.
   Enter your full name to ensure proper identification.

8. Enter your **Customer Email** on page 8.
   Add your email address so the food wrap business can contact you for any updates.

9. Enter your **Customer Phone** on page 9.
   Provide your phone number for any further communication.

10. Enter the **Order Total** on page 10.
   Add the total amount for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (Page 1, Text, Optional): Enter the main information about your order here.
* **Customer Info** (Page 2, Text, Optional): Enter your contact details such as name and email address.
* **Payment Method** (Page 3, Select One, Optional): Choose the method you'd like to use for payment.
* **Order Date** (Page 4, Date, Optional): Select the date for your order.
* **Order Time** (Page 5, Time, Optional): Choose the time for your order.
* **Order Notes** (Page 6, Note, Optional): Add any specific details or requests for your order.
* **Customer Name** (Page 7, Text, Optional): Enter your full name to ensure proper identification.
* **Customer Email** (Page 8, Email, Optional): Add your email address so the food wrap business can contact you for any updates.
* **Customer Phone** (Page 9, Text, Optional): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Order Total** (Page 10, Number, Optional): Add the total amount for your order.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure your contact information is up-to-date and correct.
* If you have any special requests or comments, use the order notes field.
