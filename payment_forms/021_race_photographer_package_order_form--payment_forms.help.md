# Race Photographer Package Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order a photographer package for a race event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Photographer Details** section with the name and contact information of the photographer or studio you are ordering from.
2. Enter the details of the **Event**, including the event name, date, and location.
3. Provide the details of the **Order**, including the package type, quantity, and any special requests.
4. Choose the **Delivery Details** section to select how you would like to receive the ordered items.
5. In the **Payment Details** section, select the payment method and enter the total amount due.
6. Enter the **Order Total** to ensure accuracy.
7. In the **Delivery Preference** section, select the delivery method for the ordered items.
8. Enter the **Email** and **Phone** contact information for communication with the photographer or studio.
9. Upload or enter the **File Name**, **File Size**, **File Type**, and **File Location** to complete the order.
10. In the **File Comments** section, enter any additional comments or instructions for the photographer or studio.
11. Review and submit the form to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Details**: Enter the name and contact information of the photographer or studio.
* **Event Details**: Enter the event name, date, and location.
* **Order Details**: Enter the package type and quantity, and any special requests.
* **Delivery Details**: Select how you would like to receive the ordered items.
* **Payment Details**: Select the payment method and enter the total amount due.
* **Order Total**: Ensure accuracy by entering the total amount due.
* **Delivery Preference**: Select the delivery method for the ordered items.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication with the photographer or studio.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication with the photographer or studio.
* **File Name**: Enter the name of the file(s) to be transferred.
* **File Size**: Enter the size of the file(s) to be transferred.
* **File Type**: Enter the type of file to be transferred.
* **File Location**: Enter the location of the file to be transferred.
* **File URL**: Enter the URL of the file to be transferred.
* **File Description**: Enter a brief description of the file to be transferred.
* **File Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the photographer or studio.
