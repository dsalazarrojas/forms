# Leadership Workshops Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Workshops Donation Form is used to collect donations for leadership workshops, conferences, and events. It ensures that the donation process is smooth and efficient while providing valuable insights into donor preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your First Name**: Type your first name in the text box provided.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: Type your last name in the text box provided.
3. **Enter your Email**: Provide your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
4. **Specify the Amount**: Choose the amount you wish to donate to the leadership workshop.
5. **Select a Donation Method**: Choose the method of donation that suits you best from the provided options.
6. **Select a Payment Method**: Choose one or more payment methods that you are comfortable with.
7. **Specify the Date**: Choose the date you would like to attend the leadership workshop.
8. **Specify the Time**: Choose the time you would like to attend the leadership workshop.
9. **Add Notes (Optional)**: Provide any additional information or requests you may have.
10. **Specify your Preferences**: Choose your preferred option from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (Text, Text, Optional)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
  - Example: John Doe
  - **Last Name (Text, Text, Optional)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
    - Example: John Doe
  - **Email (Text, Email, Optional)**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.
    - Example: johndoe@example.com
  - **Amount (Number, Number, Optional)**: Choose the amount you wish to donate to the leadership workshop.
    - Example: 100
  - **Donation Method (Select One, Select One, Optional)**: Choose the method of donation that suits you best from the provided options.
    - Example: Option 2
  - **Payment Method (Select Multiple, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Choose one or more payment methods that you are comfortable with.
    - Example: Option 1, Option 3
  - **Date (Date, Date, Optional)**: Choose the date you would like to attend the leadership workshop.
    - Example: 2024-03-01
  - **Time (Time, Time, Optional)**: Choose the time you would like to attend the leadership workshop.
    - Example: 10:00 AM
  - **Notes (Note, Note, Optional)**: Provide any additional information or requests you may have.
    - Example: "I would like to attend the workshop on the 1st floor."
  - **Preferences (Select One, Select One, Optional)**: Choose your preferred option from the provided options.
    - Example: Option 1
