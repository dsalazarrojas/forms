# Farewell Gift Contribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect farewell gifts from individuals who want to contribute to a particular farewell event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief welcome message to the individual being farewelled.
2.  Enter your name and a message to the individual being farewelled.
3.  Select the contribution amount you would like to contribute.
4.  Choose the type of contribution you would like to make (e.g., monetary, gift, etc.).
5.  Select the method of contribution (e.g., online, in-person, etc.).
6.  Enter your contact phone number, email, and payment method details.
7.  Confirm that the contribution has been paid or select the payment status.
8.  If necessary, provide additional payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome Message** (`welcome_message`):
  * This field allows you to write a brief welcome message to the individual being farewelled.
* **Name** (`contributor_name`):
  * Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded in the contribution history.
* **Message** (`contributor_message`):
  * Write a message to the individual being farewelled, expressing your sentiments or sharing memories.
* **Contribution Amount** (`contribution_amount`):
  * Enter the amount you would like to contribute (if applicable).
* **Contribution Type** (`contribution_type`):
  * Select the type of contribution you would like to make (e.g., monetary, gift, etc.).
* **Contribution Method** (`contribution_method`):
  * Choose how you will make your contribution (e.g., online, in-person, etc.).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
  * Enter your phone number for the event organizers to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`):
  * Enter your email address for the event organizers to contact you.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`):
  * Select your preferred method of payment (e.g., credit card, check, etc.).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`):
  * Confirm that your contribution has been paid or select "Not Paid" if it hasn't.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`):
  * If applicable, select the date the contribution was made.
* **Notes** (`notes`):
  * Add any additional notes or comments about your contribution.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory fields. Please complete them as required. If a field is not applicable, simply skip it.
