# private_chef_service_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "private_chef_service_renewal_form" is designed for customers to renew their private chef services. This form will help you provide accurate and relevant information to facilitate the renewal process efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Street Address** in the "Street" field.
4. Enter your **City** in the "City" field (optional).
5. Select your **State** from the available options in the "State" field (optional).
6. Enter your **Zip Code** in the "Zip" field.
7. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
9. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
10. Select how you **heard about us** from the available options in the "How did you hear about us?" field.
11. For **Select multiple**, choose one or more options from the available selections. (This field is optional).
12. Enter any **Additional Information** in the "Additional Information" field (optional).
13. Select a **Date** and **Time** for the renewal, using the available input fields.
14. Select your **Hour**, **Minute**, and **Second** for the renewal, using the available input fields.
15. Enter your **Integer** value in the "Integer" field (optional).
16. Enter your **Decimal** value in the "Decimal" field (optional).
17. Enter your **Payment** method in the "Payment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Street** (`street_address`, text, required): Provide your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city (if applicable).
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Select your state from the available options.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the specified format.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Select how you heard about us from the available options.
* **Select multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more options from the available selections.
* **Additional Information** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Select Date** (`select_date`, text, required): Select a date for the renewal.
* **Select Time** (`select_time`, text, required): Select a time for the renewal.
* **Select Hour** (`select_hour`, text, required): Select an hour for the renewal.
* **Select Minute** (`select_minute`, text, required): Select a minute for the renewal.
* **Select Second** (`select_second`, text, optional): Select a second (if required).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a date (if required).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter a time (if required).
* **Text** (`text`, text, optional): Enter text (if required).
* **Integer** (`integer`, number, optional): Enter an integer value (if required).
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value (if required).
* **Payment** (`payment`, text, required): Enter your payment method.
