# Direct Debit Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Debit Authorization Form is used by customers to grant permission for regular recurring payments to be deducted from their account. This form helps customers provide necessary information to set up direct debit arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** and **Customer Email** to identify yourself as the customer.
2. Select the **Direct Debit Date** for when you want the payments to start.
3. Choose your **Direct Debit Frequency** (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Monthly, Bi-Monthly, Annually) to specify how often payments will be made.
4. Select your **Payment Method** (e.g., ACH, Wire Transfer, Check) for processing the payments.
5. Enter the **Authorization Amount**, if applicable.
6. Specify the **Direct Debit Start Date** and **Direct Debit End Date** if you have a specific timeframe for the direct debit arrangement.
7. Add any **Notes** about the direct debit arrangement.
8. If applicable, select the **Assigned Tool** and **Form ID** for internal reference.
9. Specify the **Category** of the form as "Payment Forms" or "Other".
10. Enter a **Description** of the direct debit arrangement, if needed.
11. Enter the **Customer Phone** and **Customer Address** for additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your account.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your valid email address associated with your account.
* **Direct Debit Date**: Select the date when you want the direct debit payments to start.
* **Direct Debit Frequency**: Choose how often payments will be made (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Select how you want to receive the payments (e.g., ACH, Wire Transfer, Check).
* **Authorization Amount**: Enter the amount you've authorized for the direct debit (if required).
* **Direct Debit Start Date**: Set the start date for the direct debit arrangement.
* **Direct Debit End Date**: Set the end date for the direct debit arrangement (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions about the direct debit arrangement.
* **Assigned Tool**: Identify the tool associated with this direct debit arrangement (if applicable).
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the form for internal reference (if applicable).
* **Category**: Choose whether the form is for "Payment Forms" or "Other".
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the direct debit arrangement (if needed).
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number associated with your account.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your physical address associated with your account.
* **Output File**: This field is for internal use and will automatically generate a file with the information entered in this form.
