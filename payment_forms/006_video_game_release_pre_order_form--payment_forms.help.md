# video_game_release_pre_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to pre-order video games, allowing customers to provide essential information about their orders, including release dates, game titles, quantities, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
   * **Release Date**: The date when the customer would like to pre-order the game.
   * **Game Title**: The title of the video game being pre-ordered.
   * **Price**: The price the customer is willing to pay for the game.
   * **Quantity**: The number of copies the customer would like to pre-order.
   * **Customer Contact Information**: The customer's name and phone number.
   * **Payment Method**: The method of payment the customer prefers (card, bank transfer, or cash).
   * **Payment Status**: The payment status of the pre-order (paid or unpaid).

2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
   * **Customer Email**: The customer's email address for further contact.
   * **Order Notes**: Any additional comments or special instructions for the pre-order.

* **Release Date**: The date when the customer would like to pre-order the game.
* **Game Title**: The title of the video game being pre-ordered.
