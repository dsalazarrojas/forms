# technology_access_donation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect donation information from individuals who are donating technology access to support various causes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out the fields as accurately and completely as possible.
3. Ensure you provide the correct payment information to facilitate proper processing and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donations Details** (`donation_details`, text, optional): This field is a general field for any additional information about the donation.
* **Technology Access Level** (`technology_access_level`, select_one, optional): Select the level of technology access you are offering. Options include High and Low.
* **Frequency of Donation** (`frequency_of_donation`, select_one, optional): Indicate how frequently you will be donating. Options include Yes and No.
* **Donor Details** (`donor_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about the donor.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about the payment.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Frequency of Donation Options** (`frequency_of_donation_frequency_options`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which frequency options you select. Options include Yes and No.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_information_payment_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate the type of payment. Options include Yes and No.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the donor.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_information_payment_method`, select_one, optional): Indicate the payment method. Options include Yes and No.
* **Payment Card** (`payment_information_payment_card`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are using a payment card. Options include Yes and No.
* **Payment Card Exp Month** (`payment_information_payment_card_exp_month`, select_one, optional): Select the expiration month of the payment card.
* **Payment Card Exp Year** (`payment_information_payment_card_exp_year`, select_one, optional): Select the expiration year of the payment card.
* **Payment Card Exp Code** (`payment_information_payment_card_exp_code`, select_one, optional): Select the expiration code of the payment card.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that all payment information is correct to facilitate proper processing and follow-up.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
