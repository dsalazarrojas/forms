# Painting Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about the delivery of paintings, including details about the recipient and the delivery address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct type of recipient from the options provided.
2. Enter the name of the recipient.
3. Enter the email address of the recipient (if applicable).
4. Enter the delivery address details.
5. If necessary, provide any special instructions for the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the delivery, such as the date, time, or any specific delivery instructions.
* **Recipient** (`recipient`, select_one, optional): Select the type of recipient for the painting, such as "Art Gallery" or "Private Collector".
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the recipient, if different from the name provided in the "Recipient" field.
* **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the recipient, if provided (e.g. for tracking or follow-up).
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where the painting will be delivered, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special delivery instructions, such as "Please deliver to the loading dock" or "Deliver to the reception desk".
