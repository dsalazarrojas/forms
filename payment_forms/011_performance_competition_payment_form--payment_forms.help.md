# Performance Competition Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Competition Payment Form is designed to collect competition-related details and payment information from participants. This form helps organizers gather necessary data for efficient payment processing and participant management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Competition Details**: Enter a brief description of the competition you are participating in, including any relevant details such as competition name, date, and location.
2. **Participant Info**: Provide your basic contact and identification information, including your name and email address.
3. **Payment Info**: Enter your payment method and details (e.g., payment amount, payment method).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Competition Details**: A field where you can enter a brief description of the competition you are participating in.
  - **Type:** Text field
  - **Required:** No (optional)
  - **Hint:** Enter competition name, date, and location (if relevant)
- **Participant Info**: A field where you can enter your basic contact and identification information.
  - **Type:** Text field
  - **Required:** No (optional)
  - **Hint:** Enter your name and email address for communication
- **Payment Info**: A field where you can enter your payment method and details.
  - **Type:** Text field
  - **Required:** No (optional)
  - **Hint:** Enter payment amount and method (e.g., bank transfer, PayPal)

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check your input for spelling and formatting errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
