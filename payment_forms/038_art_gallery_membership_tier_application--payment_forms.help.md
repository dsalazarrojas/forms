# Art Gallery Membership Tier Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Membership Tier Application form allows applicants to sign up for a membership to the gallery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select your desired membership tier and duration from the available options.
3. Choose your preferred payment method.
4. Confirm your auto-renewal option.
5. Select your areas of interest.
6. If this is a gift, provide the recipient's details.
7. Review your application and agree to the membership terms and privacy policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for digital membership updates and communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for gallery contact and communication purposes.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your address for physical welcome kit and magazine delivery.
* **Membership Tier**: Select the membership tier that best suits your interests:
	+ **Student - Artist ($40 - year)**: For students and artists.
	+ **Individual Patron ($75 - year)**: For individuals who want to support the gallery.
	+ **Dual - Family ($120 - year)**: For two or more people.
	+ **Sustaining Member ($250 - year)**: For those who want to sustain the gallery's programs.
	+ **Curator's Circle ($1,000 - year)**: For those who want to support the gallery's curatorial programs.
	+ **Director's Visionary ($5,000 - year)**: For those who want to support the gallery's vision and mission.
* **Membership Duration**: Select the duration of your membership:
	+ **1 Year**
	+ **2 Years (10% Discount)**: A two-year membership with a 10% discount.
	+ **Lifetime (Available for Curator's Circle and above)**: A lifetime membership for Curator's Circle and above tiers.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method:
	+ **Credit - Debit Card**
	+ **PayPal**
	+ **Apple Pay - Google Pay**
	+ **Direct Bank Transfer (Invoice will be sent)**
* **Enrollment in Auto-Renewal**: Select whether you want your membership to auto-renew annually:
	+ **Yes - Automatically renew my membership annually**
	+ **No - I will renew manually**
* **Areas of Interest**: Select all areas of the gallery that interest you:
	+ **Contemporary Exhibitions**
	+ **Educational Workshops**
	+ **Artist Talks and Lectures**
	+ **VIP Opening Receptions**
	+ **Gallery Tours**
	+ **Film and Digital Media**
* **Is this a Gift Membership?**: Select whether this membership is a gift:
	+ **False**
	+ **Yes (Specify recipient details in notes)**
* **Gift Recipient Details**: If this is a gift, enter the recipient's name and email address.
* **Application Date**: The date you are submitting your application.
* **Agree to the Membership Terms and Privacy Policy**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the membership terms and privacy policy.
