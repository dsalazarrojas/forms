# Truck Loan Payment Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Truck Loan Payment Tracker form is used to collect and track loan payments on trucks. This form helps users to gather essential details about a loan, including loan number, date, amount, type, and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the loan number as it appears on the loan documents in the **Loan Number** field.
2. Enter the loan date in the format mm/dd/yyyy in the **Loan Date** field.
3. Enter the loan amount in dollars in the **Loan Amount** field.
4. Enter the borrower's name in the **Borrower Name** field.
5. Select the loan type from the list in the **Loan Type** field (commercial, lease, or personal).
6. Select the payment status from the list in the **Payment Status** field (overdue, current, or late).
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field (optional).
8. Enter the payment date in the format mm/dd/yyyy in the **Payment Date** field.
9. Enter the payment amount in dollars in the **Payment Amount** field.
10. Enter the next payment date in the format mm/dd/yyyy in the **Next Payment Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Number**: Enter the loan number as it appears on the loan documents.
* **Loan Date**: Enter the loan date in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the loan amount in dollars.
* **Borrower Name**: Enter the borrower's name.
* **Loan Type**: Select from the list: commercial, lease, or personal.
* **Payment Status**: Select from the list: overdue, current, or late.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the loan (optional).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the payment date in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount in dollars.
* **Next Payment Date**: Enter the next payment date in mm/dd/yyyy format.
