# Grocery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order details and customer information from users for the purpose of processing grocery orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the form and select "Order Details" as the first page.
2. Fill in the required information in the "Order Details" page.
3. Proceed to the "Customer Info" page.
4. Fill in the required information for the customer.
5. Proceed to the "Payment Info" page.
6. Fill in the payment details.
7. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: A text field to enter your order details (e.g., items to purchase, quantities, and any special instructions). (text, text, required/optional: true)
* **Customer Info**: A text field to enter your customer information (e.g., name, phone number, and address). (text, text, required/optional: false)
* **Payment Info**: A text field to enter your payment details (e.g., payment method, amount, and any additional instructions). (text, text, required/optional: false)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
