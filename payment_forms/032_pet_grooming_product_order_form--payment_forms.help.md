# Pet Grooming Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and product details for pet grooming services. It is meant to be filled out by pet owners to provide their contact and product order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Provide details about the product you would like to order, including any specific products or services you need.
3. Enter your payment information.
4. Add any additional review information if you have any comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information (1)
* **Customer Name** (optional): This field is for the pet owner's name. Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Pet Name** (optional): This field is for the name of the pet you are ordering for.
* **Address** (optional): This field is for your residential or business address.
* **Email** (optional): This field is for your email address.
* **Phone Number** (optional): This field is for your phone number.

### Product Information (2)
* **Product Details** (optional): This field is for describing the product you would like to order, including any specific details or instructions.

### Payment Information (3)
* **Name on Card** (optional): This field is for the name as it appears on your credit card or bank account.
* **Card Number** (optional): This field is for your credit or debit card number.
* **Expiration Date** (optional): This field is for the expiration date of your card.
* **CVV** (optional): This field is for the three-digit code found on your card.

### Review Information (4)
* **Additional Comments** (optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the form or our services.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields, even if you have opted to include additional information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
