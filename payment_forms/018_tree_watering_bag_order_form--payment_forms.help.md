# Tree Watering Bag Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tree Watering Bag Order Form is a structured document used to capture customer information and order details for tree watering bags.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer name.
2. Enter the customer's phone number.
3. Enter the customer's shipping address.
4. Enter the customer's email address.
5. Enter the product ID for the tree watering bag being ordered.
6. Enter the quantity of tree watering bags ordered.
7. Select the payment method (credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
8. Enter the order date and time.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
10. Select the person to which the order will be assigned.
11. Select the order status (pending, shipped, or received).
12. Enter the payment date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
### Shipping Address
* **Shipping Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the customer's shipping address.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
### Product ID
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, text, required): Enter the product ID for the tree watering bag being ordered.
### Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of tree watering bags ordered.
### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method (credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer).
### Order Date
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the order date and time.
### Order Time
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required): Enter the order time.
### Note
* **Note** (`note`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
### Assign To
* **Assign To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person to which the order will be assigned.
### Order Status
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the order status (pending, shipped, or received).
### Payment Date
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the payment date.
### Form Submitted By
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the person who submitted the form.
### Form Submitted On
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
### Form Submitter IP Address
* **Form Submitter IP Address** (`form_ip_address`, text, required): Enter the IP address of the form submitter.
### Form Submitter User Agent
* **Form Submitter User Agent** (`form_user_agent`, text, required): Enter the user agent of the form submitter.
### Form Submitter Browser
* **Form Submitter Browser** (`form_browser`, text, required): Enter the browser used by the form submitter.
### Form Submitter Platform
* **Form Submitter Platform** (`form_platform`, text, required): Enter the platform used by the form submitter.
### Form Submitter Device
* **Form Submitter Device** (`form_device`, text, required): Enter the device used by the form submitter.
