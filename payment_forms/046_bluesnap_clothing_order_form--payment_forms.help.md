# BlueSnap Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BlueSnap Clothing Order Form is a tool used to collect information from customers for processing and fulfilling their clothing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Order Your Clothing" page.
2. Select the type of clothing item you wish to order (e.g., T-Shirt, Jeans, etc.).
3. Choose your preferred color and size for the item.
4. Enter your shipping information, including address, city, state, zip code, and country.
5. Select your preferred shipping method (Standard, Express, or Overnight).
6. Choose your payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, or PayPal).
7. Enter your order date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for order updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for order updates.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you want your order to be delivered.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like your order to be delivered.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you would like your order to be delivered.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter the zip or postal code where you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Item Type**: Select the type of clothing item you want to order (e.g., T-Shirt, Jeans, etc.).
* **Color Preference**: Choose the color you prefer for your clothing item.
* **Size Selection**: Choose the size you prefer for your clothing item.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of clothing items you want to order.
* **Material Preference**: Choose the material you prefer for your clothing item.
* **Price Per Item**: Enter the price per item, if you're using a price per unit pricing system.
* **Total Order Amount**: Enter the total amount you want to order, if you're using a price per unit pricing system.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method you prefer (Standard, Express, or Overnight).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, Debit Card, or PayPal).
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or requests for your order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to place your order.
