# Income Driven Repayment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your income and family size to determine your repayment plan for income-driven repayment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the relevant information. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate information. If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from a representative of your lender.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Income** (`1`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your current income.
* **Family Size** (`2`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the number of family members you support.
* **Dependent Children** (`3`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the number of dependent children you have.
* **Annual Family Income** (`4`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your family's annual income.
* **Monthly Payment** (`5`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your monthly payment.
* **Payment Frequency** (`6`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check if your payments are made on a regular schedule or not.
* **Income Frequency** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check if your income is received on a regular basis or not.
* **Income Verification Status** (`8`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check the status of your income verification.
* **Annual Income** (`9`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your annual income.
* **Monthly Income** (`10`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your monthly income.
* **Income Source** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check if your income comes from a source such as an employer or self-employment.
* **Payment Method** (`12`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check how you make your payments.
* **Payment Plan** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check if you have a payment plan in place.
* **Payment Status** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check the status of your payments.
* **Employer Plan** (`15`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check if you have an employer-sponsored payment plan.
* **Employer Verification Status** (`16`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check the status of your employer verification.
* **Income Verification** (`17`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a note field, not applicable to filling out.
* **Payment Schedule** (`18`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check your payment schedule.
* **Notes** (`19`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a note field, not applicable to filling out.
* **Employer** (`20`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check if you have an employer.
* **Address** (`21`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Check if you have an address.
* **Date of Birth** (`22`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone** (`23`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`24`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
