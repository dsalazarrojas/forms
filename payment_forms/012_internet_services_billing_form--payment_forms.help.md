# Internet Services Billing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect billing information for internet services and other related services such as TV, mobile, and home phone services. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to facilitate the billing process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email Address.
4. Enter your Address.
5. Input your Phone Number.
6. Select the Service Type (Internet, TV, Mobile, or Home Phone).
7. Choose your Service Plan (Basic, Premium, Ultimate, or Pro).
8. Select your Payment Method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
9. Enter any additional payment details in the Note field.
10. Enter the Date of Service for the billing period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your current address for billing purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the type of service you're using (Internet, TV, Mobile, or Home Phone).
* **Service Plan** (`service_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose your service plan from the available options (Basic, Premium, Ultimate, or Pro).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any additional payment details if necessary.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the Date of Service for the billing period.
