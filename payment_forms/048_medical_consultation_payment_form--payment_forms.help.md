# Medical Consultation Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record payment information for medical consultations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
*   Begin by filling out the patient's name, medical specialty, and procedure details.
*   Select the payment method and date, if applicable.
*   Enter the payment amount, if required.
*   Enter any additional payment method details, if necessary.
*   Finally, confirm the payment status and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name as it is known to the medical specialist.
*   **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of medical specialty (e.g., General Practice, Cardiology, Dermatology, etc.).
*   **Procedure** (`procedure`, `text`, required: false): Describe the procedure performed during the consultation.
*   **Medical Specialist** (`medical_specialist_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the medical specialist who performed the procedure.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how you would like to pay (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash, etc.).
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount paid for the consultation.
*   **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of payment.
*   **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of payment.
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the payment.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the patient or their representative.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the patient or their representative.
*   **Medical License** (`medical_license`, `text`, required: false): Enter the medical license number or license details.
*   **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the medical license number or license details.
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment status (e.g., Paid, Pending, Unpaid).
*   **Payment Status Note** (`payment_status_note`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the payment status.
*   **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of payment (e.g., Consultation Fee, Medical Procedure Fee, Other).
*   **Other Payment Type** (`other_payment_type`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other payment type details.
*   **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select an additional payment method (e.g., Yes, No).
*   **Payment Amount 2** (`payment_amount_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount paid for the second payment method.
*   **Payment Date 2** (`payment_date_2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the second payment.
*   **Payment Time 2** (`payment_time_2`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the second payment.
*   **Note 2** (`note_2`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the second payment.
*   **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the patient or their representative for the second payment.
*   **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the patient or their representative for the second payment.
*   **Medical License 2** (`medical_license_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the medical license number or license details for the second payment.
*   **Payment Status 2** (`payment_status_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment status for the second payment (e.g., Paid, Pending, Unpaid).
*   **Payment Status Note 2** (`payment_status_note_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the second payment status.
*   **Payment Type 2** (`payment_type_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of payment for the second payment (e.g., Consultation Fee, Medical Procedure Fee, Other).
*   **Other Payment Type 2** (`other_payment_type_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other payment type details for the second payment.
