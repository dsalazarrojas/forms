# Organic Product Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organic Product Delivery Form is a tool to help customers place an order for an organic product. This form requires basic customer information and order details. It ensures accuracy and efficiency in the ordering process, allowing for seamless processing and delivery of the organic product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the "Customer Information" page.
	* Enter your name and contact details in the required fields.
2.  Proceed to the "Delivery Address" page.
	* Provide your complete address for delivery, including house number and street name.
3.  Select the "Organic Product" you wish to order.
4.  Enter your preferred "Delivery Date" for the product.
5.  Choose a suitable "Delivery Time" slot for your order.
6.  On the "Special Instructions" page, provide any additional details for the delivery.
7.  Enter the total amount for your order (optional but recommended).
8.  Select your preferred "Payment Method".
9.  Finally, confirm your order by selecting the appropriate options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact details here.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required/optional): Provide your address for delivery, including house number and street name.
- **Organic Product** (`organic_product`, select_one, required/optional): Select the organic product you wish to purchase.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you prefer for delivery.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a suitable time slot for your order.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional contact information if needed.
- **Special Instructions** (`order_instructions`, text, required): Enter any special instructions or requirements for the delivery.
- **Total** (`total`, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount for your order if you wish (recommended but optional).
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method for your order.
- **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm your order by selecting the appropriate options.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly for successful order processing and delivery.
* Review your entries before submission to avoid errors.
* This form serves as a guide; please refer to specific product or service instructions for additional details.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, do not hesitate to contact us.
