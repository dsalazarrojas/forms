# Elite Club Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Elite Club Refund Form is a tool used to request a refund from the Elite Club. This form is designed to help members of the Elite Club to report their dissatisfaction with a product or service, and to initiate the refund process. Please use this form only if you are an Elite Club member and you have a valid reason to request a refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "refund_request" field and enter a brief description of the reason for your refund request.
2. Enter the "refund_date" field with the date you would like to receive the refund.
3. Choose the "refund_method" field by selecting one of the available options: Full Refund, Partial Refund, or Store Credit.
4. Enter the "refund_amount" field with the amount of refund you are requesting.
5. In the "reasons_for_refund" field, select all reasons that apply to your refund request from the list of available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Refund Request** (`refund_request`, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of the reason for your refund request.
* **Refund Date** (`refund_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date you would like to receive the refund.
* **Refund Method** (`refund_method`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you would like to receive the refund: Full Refund, Partial Refund, or Store Credit.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, **required**): Enter the amount of refund you are requesting.
* **Reasons for Refund** (`reasons_for_refund`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all reasons that apply to your refund request from the list of available options.
