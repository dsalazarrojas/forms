# easter_basket_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The easter_basket_registration_form is a form used to collect user information for an Easter-themed event registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your School Name where you are associated with.
5. Enter your Name as a teacher or representative of the school (Teacher Name field).
6. If you have children participating in the event, please enter the Child Count.
7. Click "Submit" to complete your registration.

### First Name
* <label> First Name </label> (**required**, text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.

### Last Name
* <label> Last Name </label> (**required**, text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.

### Email
* <label> Email </label> (**optional**, email): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.

### Phone
* <label> Phone </label> (**optional**, text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### School Name
* <label> School Name </label> (**optional**, text): Enter your school name where you are associated with.

### Teacher Name
* <label> Teacher Name </label> (**optional**, text): Enter your name as a teacher or representative of the school.

### Child Count
* <label> Child Count </label> (**optional**, number): If you have children participating in the event, enter the number of children.
