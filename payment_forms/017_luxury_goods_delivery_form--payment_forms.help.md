# Luxury Goods Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to the delivery of luxury goods. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details such as delivery address, date, time, shipping cost, customer information, and order comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery address in the "delivery_address" field.
2. Enter the delivery date in the "delivery_date" field.
3. Enter the delivery time in the "delivery_time" field.
4. Enter the shipping cost in the "shipping_cost" field.
5. Enter any additional customer information in the "customer_info" field.
6. Enter any comments about the order in the "order_comments" field.
7. Enter the customer's contact information in the "customer_contact" field.
8. Select the shipping status from the options in the "shipping_status" field.
9. Enter any additional notes about the order in the "order_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: This field collects the customer's delivery address.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: This field collects the date for the delivery.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: This field collects the time for the delivery.
* **Shipping Cost (shipping_cost)**: This field collects the cost of shipping.
* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: This field collects any additional information about the customer.
* **Order Comments (order_comments)**: This field collects comments about the order.
* **Customer Contact (customer_contact)**: This field collects the customer's contact information.
* **Shipping Status (shipping_status)**: This field collects the status of the shipping (e.g., Available, On Order, On Delivery).
* **Order Notes (order_notes)**: This field collects notes about the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure smooth delivery processing.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact the luxury goods delivery team for further assistance.
- This form is designed to be simple and efficient, so please do not hesitate to reach out if you need help with any part of the form.
