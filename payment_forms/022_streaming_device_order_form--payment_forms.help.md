# Streaming Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Device Order Form is used to capture the essential details required to complete a streaming device order. Please follow this guide to ensure a smooth and accurate submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details on the "Order Details" page (page 1). You can enter any additional information you would like to include with your order.
2. Provide your shipping address on the "Shipping Address" page (page 2).
3. Enter your payment information, including your preferred payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay) and payment method (e.g., Credit Card, ACH, EFT) on the "Payment Info" page (page 3).
4. Select your preferred shipping method (e.g., Standard, Express) and any additional shipping costs on the "Shipping Method" page (page 4).
5. Choose the type of streaming device you wish to order on the "Device Type" page (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to include with your order.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Please enter your complete shipping address, including any necessary contact details.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment information, including your preferred payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay) and payment method (e.g., Credit Card, ACH, EFT).
- **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay).
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, ACH, EFT).
- **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred shipping method (e.g., Standard, Express).
- **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): Enter any additional shipping costs if applicable.
- **Device Type** (`device_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of streaming device you wish to order (e.g., Streaming Device A, Streaming Device B, Streaming Device C).
