# puppy_training_purchase_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and process payments for puppy training services. It gathers essential details from the user, including payment method, client details, and schedule preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out this form, please start by providing your client details, which includes selecting the relevant options for your client.
- Next, choose the desired training schedule that suits your needs.
- Then, select the payment method and date for the transaction.
- If necessary, provide the training cost and location details.
- Finally, add any additional notes or comments regarding the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the options that apply to your client to provide a comprehensive picture of their needs.
* **Training Schedule** (`training_schedule`, select_one, required: true): Choose the schedule that suits your training needs.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the payment method for this transaction.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Training Cost** (`training_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the cost of the training service.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required: false): Provide the location details where the training will take place.
* **Trainer Info** (`trainer_info`, text, required: false): Add any relevant trainer information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Include any additional notes or comments about the training.
* **Client Booking** (`client_booking`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the client's booking.
* **Payment Form** (`payment_form`, text, required: false): Enter the payment form details.
