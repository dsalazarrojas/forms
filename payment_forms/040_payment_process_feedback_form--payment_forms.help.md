# payment_process_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The payment_process_feedback_form is designed to gather user feedback on their experience with the payment process. This form is intended to be a simple and effective way to collect feedback, allowing users to express their thoughts and feelings about their interactions with the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
- Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at example@example.com.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How did you feel about the payment process?** (`how_did_you_feel`, select_one, required)
  This question is asking about your emotional experience with the payment process. Choose the best answer that reflects how you felt about it.
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Poor
- **Was the payment successful?** (`was_payment_successful`, select_multiple, required)
  This question is asking about the outcome of the payment process. Choose all that apply from the options provided.
  * Yes
  * No
- **What were the reasons for your feedback?** (`reasons_for_feedback`, text, required)
  This is an open-ended question where you can provide any reasons why you felt about the payment process. Please be as detailed as possible.
- **Do you have an account with us?** (`do_you_have_an_account`, select_one, optional)
  This question is asking if you have a prior account with the service. Choose the best answer that applies to you.
  * Yes
  * No
- **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, email, optional)
  If you would like to get in touch with us regarding your feedback, please provide your email address here.
