# paypal_sponsor_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "paypal_sponsor_donation_form" is a form designed for sponsors to provide their information for payment processing. It helps us gather the required details for efficient and secure payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Fill in the Sponsor Name, Donation Amount, Email, Phone, Sponsor Comments, Payment Method, and if applicable, Other Payment Method fields as needed.
3. Select the preferred Payment Method from the dropdown list.
4. For non-PayPal payments, specify the Other Payment Method in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor who is making the donation.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation being made. This should be a numerical value, as indicated by the "number" type.
* **Email**: Enter the sponsor's email address. This field is used for communication and will be used if an invoice needs to be sent.
* **Phone**: Enter the sponsor's phone number. This field is optional but can be helpful for further communication.
* **Sponsor Comments**: Enter any additional comments or details about the donation.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to make the payment. Options are Pay with PayPal and Check or bank transfer.
* **Other Payment Method**: If you have chosen Check or bank transfer, enter the specifics of the payment method in this text field. 

## Tips
- Ensure all the required fields are filled in correctly.
- If using a non-PayPal method, make sure to provide clear details about the payment method.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to reach out to the team.
