# E Commerce Single Purchase Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers after a purchase, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of their payment and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your details and payment information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid any errors.
3. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and other relevant contact information.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the item or service you purchased, including the order number or reference number.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your payment method and relevant payment details.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure that you have filled in all required fields to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
