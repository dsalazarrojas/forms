# Afterpay Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for clothing orders, allowing for efficient processing and fulfillment of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number accurately to facilitate order updates and confirmation.
2.  Provide your shipping address, which is required for order delivery.
3.  If your billing address is different from your shipping address, enter the billing address in the designated field.
4.  Select the type of clothing item you wish to order, its category, color, size, and quantity.
5.  Choose your preferred price range and brand for the clothing item.
6.  If necessary, indicate any special requirements or customizations for the item.
7.  Select your preferred shipping method from the available options.
8.  Choose your preferred payment method, which is Afterpay for this form.
9.  Finally, read and agree to the store's terms and conditions by selecting "I Agree" from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Full Name**: Enter your full name accurately to help us process your order efficiently.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for delivery updates and any other order-related communications.
*   **Shipping Address**: Provide your complete shipping address to ensure correct delivery of your order.
*   **Billing Address (if different from Shipping)**: If your billing address is different from your shipping address, enter it here.
*   **-- Clothing Items --**: This section indicates the start of the clothing item details.
*   **Item Name**: Enter the name of the clothing item you wish to order.
*   **Item Category**: Select the category of the clothing item (Tops, Bottoms, Dresses, etc.).
*   **Color**: Specify the color of the clothing item.
*   **Size**: Select the size of the clothing item.
*   **Quantity**: Indicate how many of this item you would like to order.
*   **Price Range**: Select your preferred price range for this item. If you are not sure, you can skip this field.
*   **Brand Preference**: If you have a preferred brand, enter it here. This is optional.
*   **Special Requirements**: If you have any special requirements or customizations for the item, mention them here. This is optional.
*   **Shipping Preference**: Select your preferred shipping method (Standard, Express, Next Day, etc.).
*   **Payment Method**: Select Afterpay as your payment method for this order.
*   **Terms Agreement**: Please read and agree to the store's terms and conditions by selecting "I Agree".
