# phone_case_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The phone_case_pre_order_form is a pre-order form designed to collect customer and order information for accurate order processing.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the customer information section with your name and contact details.
2. Provide shipping information, if necessary.
3. Fill in the payment information section with your payment method details.
4. Enter the order details in the designated field.
5. Optionally, confirm your order by filling in the confirmation field.
6. Add any additional comments in the comments field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required): This section is used to collect your name and contact details. Please fill in this field with your accurate information to ensure smooth order processing.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any special shipping instructions, you can provide them here.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required): Fill in this field with your payment method details, such as credit card information or other payment options.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required): Enter the details of your order, including phone case specifications.
* **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, `text`, optional): If you want to confirm your order, fill in this field to ensure accuracy.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, provide them here.
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate order processing.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
