# Beverage Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential customers who want to subscribe to a beverage service. It will help us provide you with a quote or initiate a subscription process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your account details, including your first name, last name, business name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your delivery address, including your street address, city, state or province, and postal code.
3. Select your subscription frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or custom schedule).
4. Choose your preferred delivery day and time.
5. Select your beverage categories (soft drinks, water, juices, etc.).
6. Enter your estimated monthly consumption.
7. If you have any brand or product preferences, enter them in the "specific brands or products preferred" field.
8. List any dietary restrictions or preferences you may have.
9. If you want to create a custom product mix, select "yes, create custom selection".
10. Enter your minimum delivery quantity (if applicable).
11. Choose your payment method (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, etc.).
12. If your billing address is different from your delivery address, enter your billing address.
13. Select your auto-renewal preference (auto-renew monthly, ask before each renewal, or one-time order only).
14. If you have a promotional or discount code, enter it.
15. Accept the subscription terms and conditions.
16. Enter any special instructions or notes.
17. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business Name**: Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address we will use to send you subscription updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Street Address**: Enter your delivery address, including the street name and number.
* **City**: Enter the city where your delivery address is located.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where your delivery address is located.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code for your delivery address.
* **Subscription Frequency**: Select how often you want to receive your beverage subscription (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or custom schedule).
* **Preferred Delivery Day**: Select the day of the week you would like to receive your delivery (Monday to Sunday).
* **Preferred Delivery Time**: Select the time of day you would like to receive your delivery (morning, afternoon, evening, or flexible).
* **Beverage Categories**: Select all the beverage categories you are interested in (soft drinks, water, juices, etc.).
* **Estimated Monthly Consumption**: Enter an approximate number of units you expect to consume per month.
* **Specific Brands or Products Preferred**: If you have any brand or product preferences, enter them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences**: If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences (e.g. sugar-free, vegan), select them here.
* **Do You Want a Custom Product Mix**: If you want to create a custom product mix, select "yes, create custom selection".
* **Minimum Delivery Quantity**: If applicable, enter the smallest order quantity you want to receive.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Billing Address (if different)**: If your billing address is different from your delivery address, enter it here.
* **Auto-Renewal Preference**: Select how you want to be renewed (auto-renew monthly, ask before each renewal, or one-time order only).
* **Promotional or Discount Code**: If you have a promotional or discount code, enter it here.
* **I Accept the Subscription Terms and Conditions**: Select "I accept" to agree to the subscription terms and conditions.
* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Enter any special instructions or notes you may have.

Note: Please fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth subscription process.
