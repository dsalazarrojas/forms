# Hotel Deposit Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Deposit Refund Form is a document used to request a refund of a hotel deposit. Guests can use this form to provide necessary information for the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please ensure that you have already made a hotel deposit and are now requesting a refund.
* Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Deposit Refund Form Data** (`hotel_deposit_refund_form_data`, text, optional): This field is not required, but it's a good place to provide any additional information about your deposit refund request.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required): Enter the exact amount of the deposit you are requesting to be refunded.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it is registered with us.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as it is registered with us.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason`, select_one, required): Select the reason for your refund request from the options provided:
	+ Refund Requested Due to Overbooking
	+ Refund Requested Due to Personal/Professional Obligations
	+ Refund Requested Due to Other (please specify)

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult our hotel policies or contact us for assistance.
* Once you have completed the form, submit it to us for processing. We will review your request and get back to you promptly.
