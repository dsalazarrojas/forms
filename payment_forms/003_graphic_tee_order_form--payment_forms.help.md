# Graphic Tee Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphic Tee Order Form is designed to help customers place orders for graphic tees. Fill out the form with your details to complete your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Shipping Address.
4. Enter your City.
5. Enter your State.
6. Enter your Zip code.
7. Select your preferred Payment Method.
8. Confirm your Email address to ensure accuracy.
9. Enter the Total Amount for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your shipping address where your order will be delivered.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city where you would like to receive your order.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state where you would like to receive your order.
* **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code for your shipping address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Cash on Delivery).
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, required/optional): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total amount for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email and zip code for accuracy.
* Select your preferred payment method to ensure a smooth and secure payment process.
* Enter your total amount correctly to avoid any discrepancies during processing.
