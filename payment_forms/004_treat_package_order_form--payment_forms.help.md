# Treat Package Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for treat packages, allowing customers to select their desired options, provide necessary customer information, and specify payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you want to order a treat package by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' in the "Treat Bundle Order" field.
2. If you select 'Yes', you will be able to proceed with the rest of the form.
3. If you select 'No', you will not be able to complete the rest of the form.
4. Fill out the "Customer Information" field to provide your name, email, phone number, and other relevant details.
5. Select your preferred "Delivery Schedule" date using the calendar tool.
6. Enter your "Order Details" in the provided text field.
7. Select your preferred "Payment Method" by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
8. Enter your "Payment Details" in the provided text field.
9. Enter your "Order Date" and "Delivery Date" using the calendar tool.
10. Select your preferred "Delivery Time" using the time picker tool.
11. Enter any additional "Order Notes" in the provided text field.
12. Select the type of "Package Type" you want to order by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
13. Select the size of the "Package Size" you want to order by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
14. Finally, select the "Order Status" by choosing 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Treat Bundle Order**: This field allows you to select whether you want to order a treat package. If you choose 'Yes', you will be able to proceed with the rest of the form.
* **Delivery Schedule**: This field allows you to select a date for the delivery of your treat package. Please use the calendar tool to select a date.
* **Customer Information**: This field is used to provide your customer information, including your name, email, phone number, and other relevant details.
* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of your order in this text field.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your customer name in this text field.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your customer email in this text field.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your customer phone number in this text field.
* **Payment Method**: Select whether you want to pay for the order using an online method or not.
* **Payment Details**: Enter your payment details in this text field.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you want to place your order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select a date for the delivery of your order.
* **Delivery Time**: Select a time for the delivery of your order.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your order in this text field.
* **Package Type**: Select the type of package you want to order by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Package Size**: Select the size of the package you want to order by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Order Status**: Select the status of your order by choosing 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
