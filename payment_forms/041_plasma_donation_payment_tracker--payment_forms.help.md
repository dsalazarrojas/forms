# plasma_donation_payment_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track payments for plasma donations. It provides a structured way to record the donor's information, donation date, amount, payment status, payment method, and payment date.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow these steps:
    *   Enter the Donor Information in the "Donor Information" field.
    *   Select the Donation Date by entering the date of the donation in the "Donation Date" field.
    *   Enter the Donation Amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
    *   Select the Payment Status by choosing between "Paid", "Unpaid", or "Pending" in the "Payment Status" field.
    *   Select the Payment Method by choosing between "Cash", "Check", or "Credit Card" in the "Payment Method" field.
    *   Enter the Payment Date by entering the date of the payment in the "Payment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donor Information** (`donor_info`):
    *   This field is used to store the donor's information, such as their name, contact details, etc.
*   **Donation Date** (`donation_date`):
    *   This field tracks the date of the donation.
*   **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`):
    *   This field tracks the amount of plasma donated.
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`):
    *   This field indicates whether the payment for the donation has been made (Paid), is still unpaid (Unpaid), or is pending (Pending).
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`):
    *   This field indicates the method of payment used for the donation (Cash, Check, Credit Card).
    *   *   Note that this field is required.
*   **Payment Date** (`payment_date`):
    *   This field tracks the date of the payment.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any discrepancies in the payment tracking.
*   If the payment status is "Pending", make sure to update it once the payment is made.
*   Use the correct payment method to ensure that it matches with the payment records.
*   If any field is unclear, please contact the system administrator for assistance.
