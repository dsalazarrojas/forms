# Public Garden Maintenance Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for the maintenance of public gardens. It helps us understand who is donating, how much they are willing to donate, and how they would like to be contacted about their donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
3. Specify the amount you would like to donate.
4. Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Check, or Credit Card).
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can include them in the "Additional Notes" field.
6. Confirm your donation by selecting "True" (yes), "False" (no), or "Unsure".
7. If applicable, enter the date and time of your donation for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you.
* **Donation Amount**: Specify the amount you would like to donate for the public garden maintenance.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred method of payment (Cash, Check, or Credit Card).
* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional details about your payment method, such as the check number or credit card type.
* **Date of Payment**: If you've made a payment, enter the date you made it. This helps us keep track of our records.
* **Time of Payment**: If you've made a payment, enter the time you made it.
* **Additional Notes**: Leave any additional comments or messages you'd like to include with your donation.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm your donation by selecting "True" (yes), "False" (no), or "Unsure". This helps us know if your donation is valid or not.
* **Date of Confirmation**: If you confirmed your donation, enter the date you confirmed it.
