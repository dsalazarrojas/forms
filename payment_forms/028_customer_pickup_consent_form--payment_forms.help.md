# Customer Pickup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Pickup Consent Form is designed to collect information from customers who are picking up items, including contact details and pickup preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Select the relevant pages and fill in the required fields. Some fields are marked as 'optional', but please note that some fields may be required for certain pages or sections.
2. Ensure you have filled in all required fields before proceeding.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
