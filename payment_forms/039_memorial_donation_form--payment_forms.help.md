# Memorial Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Memorial Donation Form is used to collect information for memorial donations, including donor details and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide a donation amount in the Donation Amount field.
3. Specify the purpose of the donation in the Purpose of Donation field.
4. Select the payment type from the options provided (Cash, PayPal, Credit Card, or Bank Deposit).
5. If you are making a donation through a payment method other than bank deposit, confirm your payment in the Confirm Payment field.
6. Fill in your personal details such as Email, Phone Number, and Address (Street, City, State/Province, and Zip/Postal Code) if applicable.
7. Enter the Name of Memorialized Person, Date of Birth, Time of Death, Date of Donation, Time of Donation, and Memorial Date, Memorial Time, and Address if applicable.
8. Confirm your Address in the Confirm Address field.
9. Enter your donation details such as Donation Date and Donation Time, if applicable.
10. Finally, provide a message in the Special Instructions field (optional) and confirm your payment method again in the Confirm Payment field.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
* Remember that the Memorial Donation Form is a confidential and secure process.
* If you are making a donation, you will be prompted to confirm your payment method.
