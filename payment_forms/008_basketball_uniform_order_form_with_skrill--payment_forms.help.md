# Basketball Uniform Order Form With Skrill - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering basketball uniforms with Skrill as the payment method. It requires accurate team, coach, and player information to process and deliver the orders correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the team name, coach name, coach email, and coach phone number in the top section.
2. Choose the uniform style, primary color, and secondary color in the style section.
3. Enter player 1's full name, jersey number, and uniform size in the player information section.
4. Enter player 2's full name, jersey number, and uniform size in the player information section.
5. Specify how many uniforms are needed and whether to include shorts.
6. If paying with Skrill, enter your Skrill email address.
7. Select a payment method (Skrill, Credit/Debit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer) and enter relevant information if needed.
8. Enter your full shipping address.
9. Provide any additional notes or instructions in the special instructions section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter your team's official name. (Required)
* **Coach Name**: Enter your coaching name. (Required)
* **Coach Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you. (Required)
* **Coach Phone**: Enter your phone number. (Required)
* **Uniform Style**: Choose one of the four uniform design options: Classic Home and Away, Modern Reversible, Elite Performance, or Custom Design. (Required)
* **Primary Color**: Select the main uniform color: Black, White, Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Purple, Navy, or Gray. (Required)
* **Secondary Color**: Choose the accent color to complement the main uniform color. (Required)
* **Player 1 Name**: Enter Player 1's full name. (Required)
* **Player 1 Number**: Enter Player 1's jersey number. (Required)
* **Player 1 Size**: Choose the uniform size: Youth Small, Youth Medium, Youth Large, Adult Small, Adult Medium, Adult Large, Adult XL, Adult XXL. (Required)
* **Player 2 Name**: Enter Player 2's full name. (Required)
* **Player 2 Number**: Enter Player 2's jersey number. (Required)
* **Player 2 Size**: Choose the uniform size for Player 2: Youth Small, Youth Medium, Youth Large, Adult Small, Adult Medium, Adult Large, Adult XL, Adult XXL. (Required)
* **Total Number of Players**: Enter the total number of uniforms needed. (Required)
* **Add Matching Shorts**: Select whether to include matching shorts. (Required)
* **Add Warm-up Suits**: Select whether to include warm-up suits. (Optional)
* **Custom Logo**: Upload or describe your team's custom logo. (Optional)
* **Payment Method**: Select one of the payment options: Skrill, Credit/Debit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer. (Required)
* **Skrill Email Address**: Enter your Skrill email address for payment. (Optional)
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your full shipping address. (Required)
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any additional notes or instructions. (Optional)
