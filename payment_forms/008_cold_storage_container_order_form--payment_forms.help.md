# Cold Storage Container Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide customers through the process of placing an order for cold storage containers, which are specialized containers used for the safe transport and storage of goods that require temperature control. It collects essential information for the delivery of high-quality, safe products to customers. The form ensures that all necessary details are accurately captured to fulfill the order correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reviewing the form sections and understanding what each field is for.
- Fill in the required fields, marked as such, with accurate and complete information.
- Use the available options for fields like shipping address, container type, and temperature requirements to accurately reflect your needs.
- For fields like quantity and accessories, select all that apply.
- Double-check the form for any required fields marked as such before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Company Name (if applicable)**: If you represent a company, enter your company name here.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your direct contact number.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your complete street address.
* **Billing City**: Enter your city name.
* **Billing State or Province**: Enter your state or province name.
* **Billing ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the complete street address for shipping.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city name for shipping.
* **Shipping State or Province**: Enter the state or province name for shipping.
* **Shipping ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the postal code for shipping.
* **Container Type**: Select the type of container required.
* **Container Size**: Select the size of the container needed.
* **Temperature Requirement**: Choose the required temperature range.
* **Duration of Temperature Maintenance**: Select the duration for which temperature must be maintained.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of containers needed.
* **Accessories Needed**: Select any additional items required (e.g., gel packs, dry ice).
* **Temperature Data Logger Required**: Select if a logger is needed for monitoring during transit.
* **Custom Labeling Required**: Select if special labeling is required.
* **Required Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you need the containers delivered.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose the delivery speed you require.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for the order.
* **Card Type**: If paying with card, select the type.
* **Card Number**: Enter your card number.
* **Card Expiry Date**: Enter your card's expiry date (MM/YY).
* **CVV Security Code**: Enter your card's 3 or 4-digit code.
* **Cardholder Name**: Enter your name as it appears on the card.
* **Purchase Order Number**: If using a purchase order, enter the number here.
* **Special Instructions or Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance**: Accept the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes I Accept."

Note: This guide assumes that all fields are filled out as accurately and completely as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the customer support team for assistance.
