# Udon-Making Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist users in completing the Udon-Making Class Registration form. This form is for registering for a Udon-Making Class, a cooking class where you can learn various udon dishes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details.
2. Choose your preferred sessions for the class.
3. Select your payment method and confirm your registration.
4. Provide any additional comments or information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name in the format "First Name Last Name".
* **Email (3)**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date (5)**: Select the date you want to attend the class in the "YYYY-MM-DD" format.
* **Time (6)**: Select the time slot you prefer to attend the class.
* **Session (7)**: Choose the sessions you are interested in attending. You can select multiple sessions.
* **Sessions (8)**: Similar to Session 7, choose the sessions you prefer.
* **Sessions 1-3 (9-11)**: Select the sessions you are interested in attending for each of the three slots.
* **Session Price (12)**: Enter the price for the sessions you selected, if any.
* **Payment Method (13)**: Select how you plan to pay for the class (Cash, Online Payment, or Offline Payment).
* **Registration Fee (14)**: Enter the registration fee if applicable.
* **Registration (15)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and valid information for all fields.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact the class organizers/administrators.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid mistakes.
