# Concert Organizer Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concert Organizer Check Out Form is used to collect payment and order information from the end-user. This form is necessary for processing and delivering the event tickets smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all the required information before starting the form.
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Purchaser name** (purchaser_name):
  * This is the name of the person completing the checkout.
  * Please enter your correct name as it will be used for communication and ticket delivery.
- **Organization name** (organization):
  * This field is required for billing and communication purposes only when the purchase is on behalf of an organization.
  * If not purchasing on behalf of an organization, leave this field blank.
- **Billing email** (billing_email):
  * Enter your email for receiving invoices and receipts.
  * Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Payment method** (payment_method):
  * Select one of the payment methods offered: Credit card, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Offline payment.
  * Ensure you are familiar with the selected payment method.
- **Ticket package selected** (ticket_package):
  * Choose one of the available packages: General admission, VIP package, or Group package.
  * Ensure it corresponds to the number of tickets you are purchasing.
- **Number of tickets** (quantity):
  * Enter the total number of tickets you are purchasing.
  * This information is crucial for processing the order.
- **Billing address** (billing_address):
  * Enter your street address for billing purposes.
  * This is required for accurate billing and delivery.
- **Order notes** (order_notes):
  * Enter any special delivery preferences or instructions.
  * This information helps the event organizers with order delivery and processing.
- **Contact phone** (contact_phone):
  * Enter a phone number for order questions or concerns.
  * This is optional but recommended for efficient communication.
- **Confirmation and next steps** (confirmation_note):
  * Read this section for information about processing and delivery time frames.
  * No input is required here.
