# Personal Training Package Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to order a personal training package from our organization. Please fill out the form below to provide the necessary information for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired package type from the options provided.
2. Provide your client's name.
3. Enter your client's email address.
4. Enter your client's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Package Type** (`package_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of personal training package you would like to order.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the client's email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any issues with your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [support email or contact information].
