# Payment Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a payment made by a user. It is used to record a payment declaration, which is a formal statement of payment made by an individual or organization. The form is designed to be completed by users to accurately report their payments and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as described below.
2. Review your entries carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Name (User Name)

* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
 
### Payment Date (payment_date, `date`, required: false)

* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which the payment was made.

### Transaction Amount (transaction_amount, `number`, required: false)

* **Transaction Amount** (`transaction_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of the transaction made. This field must be a numerical value.

### Tips

* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your supervisor or department administrator for clarification.
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully to maintain the integrity of the payment record.
* If any field is required, it is marked as such in the form.
* Please review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
