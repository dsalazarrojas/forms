# Treadmill Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a pre-order request for a treadmill.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Customer E-mail" field. This is required if you want to receive updates about your order.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field. This is not required but can be helpful for us to contact you.
4. Select the date you would like to receive your order on in the "Date Requested" field. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Choose the quantity of treadmills you would like to order.
6. Enter the name of the supplier you are purchasing from in the "Supplier Name" field.
7. Select the status of your order from the options provided in the "Order Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Treadmill Pre Order** (`pre_order`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the order you would like to place.
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date Requested** (`date_requested`, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity`, number, required): Select the number of treadmills you would like to order.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supplier you are purchasing from.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your order from the options provided.
