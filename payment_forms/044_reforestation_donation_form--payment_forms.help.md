# Reforestation Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reforestation Donation Form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations making a donation to support reforestation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your project name in the Project Name field, if you are making a donation towards a specific reforestation project.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Email field to receive a confirmation of your donation.
3. Enter the amount of your donation in the Donation Amount field. This can be any whole number value.
4. If you are an individual, enter your First Name and Last Name fields. If you are an organization, you can leave these fields blank.
5. If you are an organization, enter your Organization Name in the Organization Name field.
6. Enter your Street, City, State, Zip, and Country fields. This information will help us understand where your donation is coming from.
7. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field, this information will help us stay in touch with you about your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name (Project Name)** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reforestation project you are making a donation towards. If you do not have a specific project in mind, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive a confirmation of your donation.
* **Donation Amount (Donation Amount)** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of your donation. This can be any whole number value.
* **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, if you are an individual making a donation.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, if you are an individual making a donation.
* **Organization Name (Organization Name)** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization, if you are making a donation on behalf of an organization.
* **Street (Street)** (`street`, text, optional): Enter the street address where your donation is coming from.
* **City (City)** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where your donation is coming from.
* **State (State)** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where your donation is coming from.
* **Zip (Zip)** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code where your donation is coming from.
* **Country (Country)** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country where your donation is coming from.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, this information will help us stay in touch with you about your donation.
