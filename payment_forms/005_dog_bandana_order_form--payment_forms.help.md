This form is used for customers to place an order for a custom dog bandana, a stylish piece of dog fashion that can be customized with a customer's name, design, and even their own custom message. The form asks for customer information (name and email), contact information (phone number), bandana type and design type, and payment details (payment method and amount). This form is designed to be completed by customers who are interested in ordering a custom dog bandana, ensuring that the correct information is collected for a smooth and accurate order fulfillment process.

# Dog Bandana Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Bandana Order Form is a tool used to collect information from customers to create a custom dog bandana with their preferred design and message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field provided.
2. Type in your email address for communication purposes.
3. If you want to be contacted, provide your phone number (optional).
4. Select the type of bandana you would like to order (Standard or Custom).
5. Choose the design type you prefer (Pawprint, Name, Monogram, or Monogram with Name).
6. If you are ordering a custom bandana, enter a custom message to be printed on it.
7. Upload a custom design file (optional, only for custom bandanas).
8. Select a payment method (PayPal or Stripe).
9. Enter the amount you would like to pay for the custom bandana.
10. Provide any additional payment details in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **Customer Email**: Type in your email address where we can contact you for order updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: If you want to be contacted for any reason, provide your phone number (e.g., for custom design questions).
* **Bandana Type**: Choose between Standard and Custom for the type of bandana you prefer.
* **Design Type**: Select from Pawprint, Name, Monogram, or Monogram with Name for the design of the bandana.
* **Custom Message**: Enter any custom message you want printed on the bandana (only for custom designs).
* **Custom Design File**: Upload a design file (e.g., image or document) if you ordered a custom bandana.
* **Payment Method**: Choose between PayPal and Stripe as the payment method.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to pay for the custom bandana.
* **Payment Details**: Provide any additional information about your payment (e.g., order number).
