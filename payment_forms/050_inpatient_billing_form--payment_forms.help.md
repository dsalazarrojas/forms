## Inpatient Billing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inpatient Billing Form is used to capture inpatient billing information for patients. It is intended for healthcare providers to generate invoices and track patient payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of service in the "Date of Service" field in the format "MM/DD/YY".
3. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
4. Select the patient's insurance plan in the "Insurance Plan" field.
5. Enter the diagnosis in the "Diagnosis" field.
6. Enter the medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
7. Enter the total charges in the "Total Charges" field.
8. Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
9. Enter the payment date in the "Payment Date" field in the format "MM/DD/YY".
10. Enter the billing provider's name in the "Billing Provider" field.
11. Select the payment status in the "Payment Status" field.
12. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
13. Enter the account number in the "Account Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required): Enter the date of service in the format "MM/DD/YY".
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter the patient's full address.
* **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`, select_one, required): Select the patient's insurance plan from the available options.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, required): Enter the patient's diagnosis.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Total Charges** (`total_charges`, number, required): Enter the total charges.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method from the available options.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the payment date in the format "MM/DD/YY".
* **Billing Provider** (`billing_provider`, text, required): Enter the billing provider's name.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable payment statuses from the available options.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter the account number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately to ensure correct billing information.
* Select the correct payment method and status to reflect the payment process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your healthcare provider for guidance.
