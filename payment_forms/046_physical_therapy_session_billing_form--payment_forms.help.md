# Physical Therapy Session Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Session Billing Form is used to record payment details for a physical therapy session. It helps the billing team track and process payments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review and ensure that all entered information is correct.
3. Sign and date the form, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: A brief description of the patient's details.
* **Patient Name (2)**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification card or record.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Billing Address (4)**: Enter the address where the patient's billing invoice should be sent to.
* **Insurance (5)**: Check if the patient has insurance coverage.
* **Patient Insurance (6)**: Indicate if the patient has insurance coverage.
* **Payment Amount (7)**: Enter the total payment amount for the session.
* **Payment Method (8)**: Select the payment method used (e.g., American Express, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, or Check).
* **Payment Date (9)**: Enter the date the payment was made.
* **Payment Time (10)**: Enter the time the payment was made.
* **Submitter (11)**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submission Date/Time (12)**: Date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submitter IP (13)**: IP address of the submitter.
* **Patient DOB (14)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Confirm Patient DOB (15)**: Confirm the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Signature (16)**: The patient should sign here if required.
* **Submitter Signature (17)**: The submitter should sign here if required.
* **Notes (18)**: Add any additional comments or remarks about the payment.
* **Insurance Provider (19)**: Enter the address of the insurance provider.
* **Insurance (20)**: Enter the phone number of the insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider (21)**: Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Payment Status (22)**: Indicate the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Pending).
* **Payment Note (23)**: Add any additional comments or remarks about the payment.
* **Billing Provider (24)**: Enter the name of the billing provider.
* **Submitter Name (25)**: Enter your name as the submitter.

Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to help the billing team process the payment efficiently.
