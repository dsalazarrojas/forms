# Insurance Relief Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Relief Donation Form is a tool for users to provide donations to support relief efforts such as disaster relief or charity fundraising.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Select a payment method from the dropdown menu (e.g., Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check).
3. If you selected Card as your payment method, choose your Card Type from the dropdown menu (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover).
4. Enter your email address to receive a confirmation of your donation.
5. Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the Note field.
7. Click the Submit Form button to complete the donation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This field is for you to provide any additional information about your donation.
	+ Label: User Input
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Donation Amount**: This field is for you to specify the amount you want to donate.
	+ Label: Donation Amount
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Payment Method**: This field is for you to select how you want to make your donation (e.g., Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check).
	+ Label: Payment Method
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Card Type**: If you selected Card as your payment method, this field appears for you to choose your Card Type (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover).
	+ Label: Card Type
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No
* **Email**: This field is for you to enter your email address to receive a confirmation of your donation.
	+ Label: Email
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: Yes
* **Phone Number**: This field is for you to enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
	+ Label: Phone Number
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Note**: This field is for you to enter any additional comments or notes about your donation.
	+ Label: Note
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No
* **Submit Form**: This button is for you to complete the donation process.
	+ Label: Submit Form
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
