This form is designed to collect information and donations from individuals who wish to support the military and their families. The form is intended for those who want to contribute financially to military support projects, which can be selected from a list of options. The form also asks for personal contact information to allow for follow-up communication. The form is not intended for general donations, but specifically for military support purposes.

# Military Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and donations from individuals who wish to support the military and their families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your input in the first field, `User Input 1`.
2. Select your desired donation amount from the `Donation Amount` field.
3. Choose the type of military support project you wish to contribute to from the `Donation Type` field. You can choose from three options:
	* Military Support
	* General Donation
4. If you wish to support a specific project not listed, enter the project name and details in the `Other (tell us about it)` field.
5. Enter your name in the `Name` field.
6. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
7. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
8. (Optional) If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them in the `Note` field.
9. Select your preferred payment method from the `Payment Method` field. You can choose from three options:
	* Credit Card
	* PayPal
	* Bank Transfer
10. Click the `Submit` button to complete and submit your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input 1** (text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Donation Amount** (number, required):
	+ Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Donation Type** (select_one, required):
	+ Choose the type of military support project you wish to contribute to.
* **Military Support Project** (select_multiple, required):
	+ Select one or more projects you would like to support.
* **Other (tell us about it)** (text, optional):
	+ If you have a specific project in mind that is not listed, enter the details here.
* **Name** (text, required):
	+ Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email** (email, required):
	+ Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone** (text, required):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Note** (note, optional):
	+ Leave any additional comments or requests you have.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required):
	+ Choose how you wish to make your donation.
* **Submit** (text, required):
	+ Click to complete and submit your donation.
