# Themed Party Favor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Themed Party Favor Order Form is used to collect information for a party or event order. This form helps you place an order for party favors, gifts, or food for your party or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Form Details**: Enter a brief description of your order in this text field.
2. Share your **Name**: Type in your full name.
3. Enter your **Email**: Provide your email address so we can contact you.
4. Add your **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up inquiries.
5. Choose the **Date and Time** for your event.
6. Select the **Type of Order**: Choose from Party Favors, Gifts, or Food.
7. Add any **Additional Information**: Use this field to provide more details about your order.
8. Select your **Payment Method**: Pick from Credit Card, Check, or Online Transfer.
9. Enter **Total Items**: Count the total number of items you are ordering.
10. Enter **Total Cost**: Calculate the total cost of your order.
11. Review your order details before submitting your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Details**: A brief text description of your order. 
  - Type a short description of your order, e.g., "Birthday party for kids."
- **Name**: Your full name or the name of the person or business making the order.
  - Type your name to be identified by our customer service team.
- **Email**: Your email address where you can be reached for any order updates.
  - Enter your email to get updates on your order status and any follow-ups.
- **Phone Number**: Your phone number for any urgent or follow-up inquiries.
  - Enter your phone number for any phone calls or texts from our team.
- **Date and Time**: The date and time of your event, e.g., "2024-02-27 14:00."
  - Enter the date and time of your event so we can schedule the order delivery.
- **Order Type**: Choose between Party Favors, Gifts, or Food.
  - Select the type of order you are making, e.g., Party Favors for a kid's birthday.
- **Additional Information**: Any extra details about your order, e.g., number of guests or specific preferences.
  - Use this field to provide more details about your order if needed.
- **Payment Method**: Choose between Credit Card, Check, or Online Transfer.
  - Select how you plan to pay for your order, e.g., credit card for online order.
- **Total Items**: Count the total number of items you are ordering.
  - Enter the total number of items to ensure accurate order processing.
- **Total Cost**: Calculate the total cost of your order based on your items.
  - Enter the total cost of your order for accurate billing.
- **Payment Status**: Choose between Paid, Pending, or Cancelled.
  - Select the current status of your payment.
- **Order Status**: Choose between Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled.
  - Select the status of your order after we have processed it.
- **Order Total**: The total amount you are paying for your order.
  - This is the total amount you are paying for your order.
- **Payment Total**: The total amount you are paying for your order.
  - This is the total amount you are paying for your order.
- **Customer Service Details**: Any details about your customer service experience.
  - This field is for any comments or feedback about our customer service.
- **Party Details**: Any details about the party or event.
  - This field is for any specific details about the party or event you're hosting.
- **Event Details**: Any details about the event or party.
  - This field is for any specific details about the event or party you're hosting.
- **Thank You Message**: A message to express your appreciation.
  - Write a message to thank us for our service.
