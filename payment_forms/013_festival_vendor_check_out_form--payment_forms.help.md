# festival_vendor_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The festival_vendor_check_out_form is used to process payments for festival vendors, gathering essential information for payment processing and vendor payment status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready to complete the form.
2. Fill out the required fields marked as "Required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID** (1, number, Required): Enter the unique identifier for the event you are participating in.
* **Vendor Name** (2, text, Required): Enter your name as a festival vendor.
* **Vendor Address** (3, text, Required): Enter your contact address.
* **Vendor Phone** (4, text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (5, date, Required): Select the date of the festival event.
* **Payment Method** (6, select_one, Required): Choose how you will be making the payment: Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Amount Paid** (7, number, Required): Enter the total amount paid.
* **Notes** (8, text, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the payment or event.
* **Vendor Email** (9, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Vendor Tax ID** (10, text, Optional): Enter your tax ID number.
* **Vendor Account Number** (11, text, Optional): Enter your account number.
* **Vendor Payment Status** (12, select_multiple, Required): Select the status of your payment: Paid, Pending, or Unpaid.
* **Vendor Payment Method** (13, select_one, Required): Choose how you made the payment: Online, Offline, or Bank Transfer.
* **Vendor Payment Date** (14, date, Optional): If paid, enter the date of payment.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check the event ID to ensure it matches the event you are participating in.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact the festival organizers for assistance.
