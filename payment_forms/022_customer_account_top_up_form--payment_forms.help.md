# Customer Account Top Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to allow customers to make a one-time payment or top up from their account. It guides the customer through three pages: entering their customer information, selecting a payment method, and reviewing their submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, navigate to the "Enter Customer Information" page.
2.  On this page, enter your name, email, and any other relevant details as required.
3.  Once satisfied with your entry, click the "Next" button to proceed.
4.  On the "Select Payment Method" page, choose your preferred payment method.
5.  On the "Review and Submit" page, double-check your entry and click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Enter Customer Information**: This is the first page of the form. Enter your name, email, and other relevant customer information as required.
*   **Select Payment Method**: On this page, choose your preferred payment method from the available options (if any).
*   **Review and Submit**: This is the final page of the form. Double-check your entries and ensure everything is accurate before submitting the form.
