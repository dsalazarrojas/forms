# Solar Community Center Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Community Center Donation Form is a tool used to collect donations from individuals and organizations to support the center's initiatives. This form will help us understand who is making a donation, what they are donating, and how they want to receive their donation acknowledgement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Input information, including your name and any additional comments or questions you may have.
2. Select the amount you wish to donate from the dropdown options.
3. Choose how you wish to make your donation (Cash, Check, Online).
4. Select the type of project you wish to support (Renewable Energy, Economic Development, Education).
5. Provide a brief description of your donation (optional).
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (1)**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or questions you may have. You can enter your name, a brief message, or any other information you think is relevant to your donation.
* **Donation Amount (2)**: Select the amount you wish to donate to the Solar Community Center. You can choose from a series of predefined options or enter a custom amount using the text field.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your donation.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you regarding your donation.
* **Donation Method (5)**: Choose how you wish to make your donation. Options are:
	+ Cash: A one-time payment method.
	+ Check: A check payment method.
	+ Online: An online payment method.
* **Payment Type (6)**: Choose the type of donation you wish to make. Options are:
	+ One-time: A single, one-time donation.
	+ Recurring: A recurring donation (e.g., monthly).
* **Recurring Donation Interval (7)**: If you selected a recurring donation, choose the interval at which you wish to make your donation (Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, Annually).
* **Project Type (8)**: Select the type of project you wish to support with your donation. Options are:
	+ Renewable Energy: Projects focused on renewable energy.
	+ Economic Development: Projects focused on economic development.
	+ Education: Projects focused on education.
* **Description (9)**: This is an optional field where you can provide a brief description of your donation. This can be used to provide further context for your donation.
* **Submit (10)**: Click this button to send your completed form to us.
