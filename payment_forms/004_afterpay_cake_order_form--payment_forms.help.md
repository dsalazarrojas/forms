# Afterpay Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Afterpay Cake Order Form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in purchasing a cake through the Afterpay payment method. This form guides you through the process of ordering a cake, including providing personal details, cake preferences, and delivery information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Customer Information page, which includes your name, contact number, and email address.
2. Move on to the Cake Details page, where you can select the size and flavor of the cake, as well as the type of frosting.
3. If necessary, provide any special instructions or messages for the cake on the Cake Customization page.
4. On the Delivery Information page, choose how you would like to receive your cake and provide the address if applicable.
5. Finally, review and submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
- **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number for order updates and communication.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and future communications.
- **Delivery/Pickup Date**: Select the date you need the cake delivered or picked up.
- **Preferred Delivery/Pickup Time**: Select your preferred time for delivery or pickup.
- **Cake Size**: Choose the size of the cake you'd like to order, from 6-inch to 12-inch.
- **Cake Flavor**: Select your preferred cake flavor, from Vanilla to Custom.
- **Frosting Type**: Select the type of frosting you'd like, from Buttercream to Fondant.
- **Filling Options**: Choose any fillings you'd like inside your cake, such as None, Raspberry Jam, or Fresh Fruit.
- **Theme or Occasion**: Provide a brief description of the occasion or theme for the cake.
- **Special Instructions**: Enter any special decorations, messages, or dietary requirements for the cake.
- **Delivery Option**: Choose how you would like to receive your cake, either through delivery or pickup.
- **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where the cake should be delivered (if applicable).
- **Estimated Cost**: Enter your estimated budget for the cake.
- **Payment Method**: Select Afterpay as your payment method.
- **Terms Agreement**: Check that you agree to the bakery's terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Be as specific as possible when providing information about the cake and delivery details.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, please include them in the Special Instructions field.
* Review your order carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
