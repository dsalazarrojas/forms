# custom_notebook_and_pen_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Custom Notebook and Pen Order Form** is used for business customers to place orders for custom notebooks and pens. The form is designed to collect information about the customer's order preferences, contact details, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Custom Name** in the text field at the top of the form.
2. Enter your **Contact Information** in the text field, including your email address and phone number.
3. Select the **Pen Color** you prefer from the list of options.
4. Choose the **Paper Type** for your notebook from the list of options.
5. Select the **Pen Style** you prefer from the list of options.
6. Choose the size of the **Notebook Size** you want from the list of options.
7. Enter the **Order Quantity** you would like to order.
8. Enter any **Special Message** you would like to include in your order.
9. Provide your **Shipping Address** in the text field.
10. Enter your **Shipping Phone** number in the text field.
11. Select the payment method you would like to use from the list of options (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
12. Enter the **Total Cost** of your order.
13. Enter the **Tax Cost** of your order.
14. Enter the **Shipping Cost** of your order.
15. Enter the **Subtotal Cost** of your order.
16. Enter the **Discount** amount applied to your order.
17. Enter the **Final Cost** of your order.
18. Select the **Payment Status** of your order (Paid, Pending).
19. Choose the **Order Date** and **Order Time** for your order.
20. Provide any **Notes** or additional information about your order.
21. If desired, enter any **Customer Notes** for your order.
22. Enter a **Store Order Reference**, if applicable.
23. Enter any **Payment Terms** or conditions for your order.
24. Finally, read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** of the sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Name** (required): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the order.
* **Contact Information** (required): Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Pen Color** (optional): Choose the color of pen you would like to order.
* **Paper Type** (optional): Choose the type of paper you would like to order.
* **Pen Style** (optional): Choose the style of pen you would like to order.
* **Notebook Size** (optional): Choose the size of notebook you would like to order.
* **Order Quantity** (required): Enter the number of notebooks and pens you would like to order.
* **Special Message** (required): Enter any special message you would like to include in your order.
* **Shipping Address** (required): Enter your shipping address so we can deliver your order.
* **Shipping Phone** (required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your shipment.
* **Payment Method** (optional): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Total Cost** (required): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Tax Cost** (required): Enter the tax cost of your order.
* **Shipping Cost** (required): Enter the shipping cost of your order.
* **Subtotal Cost** (required): Enter the subtotal cost of your order.
* **Discount** (required): Enter the discount amount applied to your order.
* **Final Cost** (required): Enter the final cost of your order.
* **Payment Status** (optional): Choose the payment status of your order.
* **Order Date** (required): Choose the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Time** (required): Choose the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional notes or information about your order.
* **Customer Notes** (optional): Enter any notes or information about your customer.
* **Store Order Reference** (optional): Enter a reference number for your order.
* **Payment Terms** (optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions for your order.
* **Terms and Conditions** (optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the sale.
