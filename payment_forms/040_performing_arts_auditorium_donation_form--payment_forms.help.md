# Performing Arts Auditorium Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations from individuals for the Performing Arts Auditorium. Donations can be made for a one-time gift or as a recurring gift.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of gift you would like to make (one-time or recurring).
2. Enter the amount of your donation.
3. Choose if this is a one-time or recurring gift.
4. Enter your name for donor records.
5. Provide your email address so we can contact you.
6. Enter your phone number for further contact.
7. If you have any message for us, please enter it here.
8. Select the date of your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **One-Time Gift**: Select "Yes" if you would like to make a one-time gift. If you choose this option, your donation will be processed as a one-time payment.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate. Please use numbers only (e.g., 100, 20, etc.).
* **Recurring Gift**: Select "Yes" if you would like to make a recurring gift. If you choose this option, we will process your donation on a regular basis (the frequency of which will be determined by your preference).
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name for our records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation and any other important updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Acknowledgement Message**: If you have any message or request, please enter it here.
* **Donation Date**: Select the date on which you would like to make your donation.

Note: All fields are optional except for the "Donor Name" field, which is required for our records.
