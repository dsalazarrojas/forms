# Product Purchase Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Purchase Order Form is used to gather information from customers for processing product orders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first name field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the last name field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the email field. This will be used for order confirmation and communication.
4. Enter the name of the **Product** you wish to purchase.
5. Enter the desired **Quantity** of the product.
6. Enter the **Unit Price** of the product.
7. If applicable, enter the **Discount Percentage** you've received.
8. The system will automatically calculate the **Total Price** based on the information you provided.
9. Select the **Payment Method** (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) from the options provided.
10. Choose the **Order Date** you wish to complete the order on.
11. Indicate the current **Shipping Status** of the order (Shipped, In Transit, or Not Shipped).
