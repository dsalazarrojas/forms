# Food Packaging Film Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details about your food packaging film order, including order date, shipping date, product specifications, and customer information. It's crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your order is processed correctly and on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the order date and time you want to place your order.
2. Choose the shipping date and time.
3. Specify the product details, including any specific requirements or specifications.
4. Add any order notes or comments.
5. Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
6. Enter your customer name, email, and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Date
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you want to place your order.

### Order Time
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you want to place your order.

### Shipping Date
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you want the order to be shipped.

### Shipping Time
* **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you want the order to be shipped.

### Product Specifications
* **Product Specifications** (`product_specifications`, text, required: false): Describe the product details, including any specific requirements or specifications.

### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false, options: Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer): Choose how you want to pay for your order (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).

### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer placing the order.

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the customer placing the order.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer placing the order.

## Tips
Please ensure that you double-check your order details before submitting the form to avoid any errors or misunderstandings. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
