# Pet Grooming Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Grooming Subscription Form is a tool for pet owners to schedule a subscription-based pet grooming service. It is designed to capture the necessary information for the groomer to provide quality service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the necessary details about your pet, including type and name.
2. Choose the subscription package that suits your needs.
3. Select the type of subscription you prefer.
4. Specify a date and time for the appointment.
5. Confirm your contact phone number for follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of pet you have (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **Pet Name** (Text, Required): Enter your pet's name.
* **Pet Info** (Text, Optional): Add any additional information about your pet (e.g., allergies, medical conditions).
* **Subscription Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose between a subscription type (e.g., monthly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Subscription Package** (Select One, Optional): Select a subscription package that suits your needs (e.g., basic, premium, etc.).
* **Appointment Date** (Date, Optional): Choose a date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (Time, Optional): Choose a time for the appointment.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up information.
