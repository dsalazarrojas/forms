# creditcard_authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "creditcard_authorization" form is used to collect credit card information for payment processing or verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the required and relevant information in the corresponding fields.
2. Make sure to fill out all the fields, but note that filling out all fields is not mandatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (1, text, required/optional):
	+ This field is a text field where you can enter any relevant information you want to provide.
* **Card Number** (2, text, required/optional):
	+ Enter the 16-digit credit card number.
* **Expiration Date** (3, date, required/optional):
	+ Enter the expiration date of the credit card in MM/DD format (e.g., 12/2025).
* **Card Type** (4, select_one, required/optional):
	+ Select the type of credit card you possess:
		- Visa
		- Mastercard
		- Amex
		- Others
* **CVV** (5, number, required/optional):
	+ Enter the three or four-digit Card Verification Value (CVV) printed on the back of your credit card.
* **Expiration Year** (6, number, required/optional):
	+ Enter the expiration year of the credit card.
* **Expiration Month** (7, number, required/optional):
	+ Enter the expiration month of the credit card.
* **Cardholder Name** (8, text, required/optional):
	+ Enter the name of the credit card holder.

## Tips
- Please ensure to enter accurate and valid credit card information to avoid any errors or issues with payment processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, it's always a good idea to consult with a payment specialist or the system administrator for clarification.
