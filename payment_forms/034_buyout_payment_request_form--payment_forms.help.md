# Buyout Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a buyout payment request for a company or department.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact information in fields 2-4.
	* Enter your name in the "Requester Name" field (field 2).
	* Enter your email address in the "Requester Email" field (field 3).
	* Enter your phone number in the "Requester Phone" field (field 4).
2. Enter the payee's contact information in fields 6-10.
	* Enter the payee's name in the "Payee Name" field (field 6).
	* Enter the payee's address in the "Payee Address" field (field 7).
	* Enter the payee's city and state in the "Payee City" and "Payee State" fields (fields 8 and 9, respectively).
	* Enter the payee's zip code in the "Payee ZIP Code" field (field 10).
3. Select the type of buyout in field 12.
	* Choose from the following options: Employment Buyout, Contract Settlement, Equity Buyout, Lease Termination, Partnership Dissolution, or Other.
4. Enter the total payment amount in field 13.
5. Select the payment terms in field 14.
	* Choose from the following options: Lump Sum, Installments, Wire Transfer, Check, or Other.
6. Enter the payment timeline in field 15.
	* Enter the date the payment should be made.
7. If applicable, attach any supporting documentation in field 16.
8. Enter a brief business justification for the payment in field 17.
9. If desired, add any additional approval notes in field 18.
10. If desired, enter a budget code or cost center in field 19.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (field 1): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Requester Name** (field 2): Enter your full name.
* **Requester Email** (field 3): Enter your email address.
* **Requester Phone** (field 4): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (field 5): Enter your department or division (optional).
* **Payee Name** (field 6): Enter the name of the person to be paid.
* **Payee Address** (field 7): Enter the full address for the payee.
* **Payee City** (field 8): Enter the city of the payee's address.
* **State** (field 9): Enter the state of the payee's address.
* **Payee ZIP Code** (field 10): Enter the zip code of the payee's address.
* **Tax ID or SSN** (field 11): Enter the payee's tax ID or SSN.
* **Type of Buyout** (field 12): Select the type of buyout.
* **Buyout Amount** (field 13): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Terms** (field 14): Select the payment terms.
* **Payment Timeline** (field 15): Enter the date the payment should be made.
* **Supporting Documentation Attached** (field 16): Indicate if any supporting documentation is attached.
* **Business Justification** (field 17): Enter a brief business justification for the payment.
* **Additional Approval Notes** (field 18): Enter any additional notes for approval.
* **Budget Code or Cost Center** (field 19): Enter a budget code or cost center (optional).

Note: If any field is highlighted or marked as "required", make sure to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure proper processing of your request.
