# Event Order Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Order Tracking Form is designed to track the status of events, including event information, attendee details, payment status, and specific requests made by event organizers or attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply select the relevant information and enter it in the corresponding text fields. The form is divided into four sections:
- Event Information (Section 1)
- Attendee Information (Section 2)
- Payment Status (Section 3)
- Specific Requests (Section 4)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Information** (Section 1): Enter the title, date, location, or any other relevant information about the event.
* **Attendee Information** (Section 2): Provide details about the attendees, such as names, contact numbers, or other relevant contact information.
* **Payment Status** (Section 3): Check or enter the current status of event payments, whether they are paid or outstanding.
* **Specific Requests** (Section 4): Make any specific requests or comments about the event, including any special arrangements or requirements.

## Tips
* Be as concise as possible when providing information for each field.
* Ensure that the event and attendee information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use clear and specific language when describing payment status or specific requests.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that it accurately reflects the status of each event.
