# Magazine Press Release Form By Asiah Amsterdamm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for creating and submitting magazine press releases. It provides space for the title, description, category, file attachment, date, time, contact phone number, and contact email address of the press release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Title** of the magazine press release.
2. Provide a **Description** of the press release, including any relevant details or background information.
3. Select the **Category**, which can be either a "Payment Forms" or "Other".
4. Attach a **File** related to the press release, if necessary.
5. Enter the **Date** of the press release.
6. Enter the **Time** of the press release, if applicable.
7. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
8. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Magazine Press Release Form** (text): Enter the title of the magazine press release.
* **Description** (text): Provide a brief description of the press release, including any relevant details or background information.
* **Category** (select one): Select the category of the press release, either "Payment Forms" or "Other".
* **File** (text): Attach a file related to the press release, if necessary.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the press release.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the press release, if applicable.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that the title accurately reflects the content of the press release.
* Provide a clear and concise description to help readers understand the press release's purpose.
* Select the correct category for the press release.
* Attach necessary files to support the press release.
* Double-check the date and time entries for accuracy.
* Use your actual phone number for contact purposes.
* Enter your actual email address for contact purposes.
