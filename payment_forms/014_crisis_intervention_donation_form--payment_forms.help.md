# crisis_intervention_donation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The crisis intervention donation form is designed to collect information from donors who wish to make a financial contribution to support crisis intervention efforts. This form helps us understand the donor's details and the amount they would like to donate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
	* Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
	* You can leave the optional fields blank if they do not apply to your situation.
	* Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Donor Information
**Donor Information** (`crisis_intervention_donation_form_1`, text, required)
Provide a brief description of the donor, including name, contact information, and any other relevant details we should know about you.

### Donation Amount
**Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required)
Enter the exact amount you would like to donate. Please use numeric values only.

### Billing Information
**Billing Information** (`billing_information`, text, optional)
If you wish to receive a receipt or invoice for your donation, please provide your billing address or any other relevant billing information.

### Payment Details
**Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional)
If you have any specific payment instructions or information, please provide it here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to help us process your donation efficiently.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, contact our support team for assistance.
* Thank you for your support for crisis intervention efforts!
