# Cosmetic Product Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cosmetic Product Reorder Form is used to collect customer information for reordering cosmetic products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name, as it appears on their order.
2. **Reorder Items**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to reorder the items.
3. **Reorder Quantity**: Enter the quantity of items to be reordered.
4. **Reorder Date**: Enter the date you want to reorder the items.
5. **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
6. **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
7. **Customer Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the customer (optional).
8. **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being reordered.
9. **Reorder Price**: Enter the price of the reordered product.
10. **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
11. **Customer City**: Enter the customer's city.
12. **Customer State**: Enter the customer's state or province.
13. **Customer Zip**: Enter the customer's zip or postal code.
14. **Customer Country**: Enter the customer's country.
15. **Shipping Name**: Enter the shipping name.
16. **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address.
17. **Shipping City**: Enter the shipping city.
18. **Shipping State**: Enter the shipping state or province.
19. **Shipping Zip**: Enter the shipping zip or postal code.
20. **Shipping Country**: Enter the shipping country.
21. **Customer Order ID**: Enter the customer's order ID.
22. **Order Status**: Select the current order status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
23. **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer (optional).
24. **Shipping Instructions**: Enter any special shipping instructions (optional).
25. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the customer (optional).
