# Customer Shipping Location Details - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Shipping Location Details" form is used to collect and verify the shipping address and contact information of customers for the purpose of facilitating smooth and accurate shipping and delivery of products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the customer's shipping address is up-to-date and accurate.
2. Enter the customer's first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter the customer's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the customer's city in the "City" field.
5. Select the customer's country of residence from the options provided in the "Country" field.
6. Enter the customer's postal/zip code in the "Postal Code" field.
7. If applicable, indicate if the customer lives in a specific region by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Region" field.
8. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This field is used to collect the customer's first name.
* **Address**: This field is used to collect the customer's shipping address. Please ensure it is accurate and complete.
* **City**: This field is used to collect the customer's city of residence.
* **Country**: This field is used to collect the customer's country of residence. Please select the correct option from the provided list.
* **Postal Code**: This field is used to collect the customer's postal/zip code.
* **Region**: This field is used to indicate if the customer lives in a specific region. Please select "Yes" or "No" if applicable.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect the customer's phone number. Please ensure it is accurate and complete.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect the customer's email address. Please ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
