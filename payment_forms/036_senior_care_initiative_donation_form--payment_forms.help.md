# senior_care_initiative_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect donations for the senior care initiative, a program aimed at supporting local seniors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Fill out the donation amount field with the amount you wish to donate (optional).
* Select the payment method you prefer for the donation (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer). Please note that this field is required.
* Enter a valid email address to receive any communication related to your donation (optional).
* Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted for further information or updates (optional).
* Enter your name for identification purposes (optional).
* Enter your address, city, and state to help us track your donation (optional).
* Enter your zip code to help us track your donation (optional).
* Select the date you wish to make the donation (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate to support the senior care initiative.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method you prefer for this donation. You can choose from Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive any communication related to your donation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted for further information or updates.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address to help us track your donation.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where you reside to help us track your donation.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where you reside to help us track your donation.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code to help us track your donation.
* **Date** (`donation_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you wish to make the donation.
