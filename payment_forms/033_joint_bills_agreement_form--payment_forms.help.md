# Joint Bills Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a joint bill agreement between a biller and a roommate, including their names and contact information, bill frequency and amount, and due date for payments.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill in the form with accurate and comprehensive information about the joint bill agreement.
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Roommate Name**: Please enter the name of the roommate who will be receiving the bill.
*   **Roommate Email**: Enter the email address of the roommate who will be receiving the bill.
*   **Number of Roommates**: Enter the total number of roommates who will be sharing the bill.
*   **Biller Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity paying the bill.
*   **Biller Email**: Enter the email address of the person or entity paying the bill.
*   **How often bills are paid**: Select the frequency at which bills are paid (e.g., Once, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually).
*   **Biller Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or entity paying the bill.
*   **Biller Address**: Enter the physical address of the person or entity paying the bill.
*   **Bill Amount**: Enter the total amount of each bill.
*   **Due Date**: Enter the date by which payments are due.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If you are unsure about any field, ask the biller or roommate for clarification.
*   This form is a joint agreement, so make sure both parties are aware of and agree to the terms outlined in the form.
