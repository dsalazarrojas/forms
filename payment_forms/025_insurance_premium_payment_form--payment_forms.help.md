# insurance_premium_payment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information for insurance premiums. It is intended for customers to provide their payment details, including payment type, amount, and date of birth, to facilitate accurate and timely processing of their premium payments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Payment Information" page with the required details.
2. Select the payment type from the options provided (Cash or Credit Card).
3. Enter the payment amount using numerical values only.
4. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Information** (`form_1`, text, required): Fill in your payment information, including the payment type and amount.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options: Cash or Credit Card.
* **Payment Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount using numerical values only.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter the payment amount correctly to avoid any errors.
* Double-check your date of birth to ensure it is correct.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
