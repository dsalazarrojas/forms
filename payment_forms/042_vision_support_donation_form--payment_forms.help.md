# Vision Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to make a donation to the Vision Support cause. It allows you to specify the amount of your donation, how often you want to receive it, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your desired donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Choose how often you want to receive your donation under "Frequency" field.
3. Select the type of donation you wish to make under "Donation Type" field.
4. Enter your email address if you want to be informed about your donation status in the "Donor Email" field.
5. Optionally, specify a one-time or recurring donation start date under "Donation Date" and "Donation Time" fields.
6. Add any notes about your donation under "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate. This can be a one-time or recurring amount.
* **Frequency**: Choose how often you want to receive your donation, either "Monthly", "Quarterly", or "Annually".
* **Interval**: If you choose "Monthly" or "Quarterly" frequency, select how often you want to receive your donation in this field.
* **Donation Type**: Select the type of donation you wish to make, such as "General", "Project", or "Event".
* **Donor Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be informed about your donation status.
* **Donation Date**: Optionally, specify the date you want your donation to start (if it's a one-time donation).
* **Donation Time**: Optionally, specify the time you want your donation to start (if it's a one-time donation).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Amount**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Donation Amount". Please disregard this field for now.
* **Frequency Frequency**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Frequency" field. Please disregard this field for now.
* **Donation Type**: Select the type of donation you wish to make, such as "General", "Project", or "Event".
