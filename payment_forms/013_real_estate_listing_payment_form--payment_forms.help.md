# Real Estate Listing Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information related to real estate listings, including property listing details, payment amount, and other relevant payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the correct **Property Listing Details** page.
2. Fill in the **Payment Amount** field with the amount to be paid.
3. Choose the **Package** option that applies to the payment.
4. Enter the **Property Address** where the payment is related.
5. Select the **Listing Status** of the property.
6. Choose the **Payment Method** for the payment.
7. Enter your **Contact Email** for communication purposes.
8. Enter your **Contact Phone** number for communication purposes.
9. Add any **Additional Comments** about the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Listing Details**: Enter a brief description of the property listing being paid for.
* **Package**: Choose the package type for the payment.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid for the property listing.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being paid for.
* **Listing Status**: Select the status of the listing (Active or Inactive).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date when the payment should be made.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how the payment will be made (yes or no).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments about the payment.
