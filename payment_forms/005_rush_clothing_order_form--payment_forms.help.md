# Rush Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers to process their rush clothing orders efficiently and accurately.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Customer Info section with your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
2.  Select your preferred delivery method from the Shipping section.
3.  Choose the clothing items you wish to purchase from the Item Details section.
4.  Select the specific items you'd like to order from the Order Items section.
5.  Provide your payment information in the Payment Info section.
6.  Choose your preferred payment method from the Payment Method section.
7.  Enter the total price of your order in the Total Price section.
8.  Confirm your order details in the Confirm Order section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (Customer Info)**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Shipping (Delivery Info)**:
	+ Select your preferred delivery method: Express, Standard, or Local Pickup.
* **Item Details (Item Details)**:
	+ Choose the type of clothing item you'd like to order: T-Shirt, Shirt, Jacket, or Hat.
* **Order Items (Order Items)**:
	+ Select the specific items you'd like to order. For example, you can order 1 or 2 T-Shirts, 1 or 2 Jackets, etc.
* **Payment Info (Payment Info)**:
	+ Enter your payment information (e.g., credit card number, expiration date, etc.).
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**:
	+ Select your preferred payment method: Credit Card or PayPal.
* **Total Price (Total Price)**:
	+ Enter the total price of your order.
* **Confirm Order (Confirm Order)**:
	+ Confirm the details of your order to ensure accuracy.
