# Online Donation Transaction Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Donation Transaction Data Consent Form is a mandatory form that collects and documents donation transactions data for compliance and transparency purposes. It is essential for all donors to fill out this form to ensure that their financial institution and other relevant parties understand and agree to the terms of their transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Choose the correct options for each field based on your understanding and agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donation Transaction Data** (`Donation Transaction Data`, select_multiple, Required): Please select whether you agree to share your donation transaction data with your financial institution and other relevant parties.
  - Choose 'Yes' if you agree to share your data.
  - Choose 'No' if you do not agree to share your data.
- **Consent** (`Consent`, select_one, Required): Please select whether you give your consent for your donation transaction data to be shared.
  - Choose 'Yes' if you give your consent.
  - Choose 'No' if you do not give your consent.
- **Email Address** (`Email Address`, text, Optional): This field is for your email address. Please enter your email address if you want your financial institution to communicate with you regarding your donation transaction data.
- **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, Optional): This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number if you want your financial institution to contact you regarding your donation transaction data.
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, Optional): This field is for your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth for identity verification purposes.
- **Social Security Number** (`Social Security Number`, text, Optional): This field is for your social security number. Please enter your social security number for identity verification purposes.
- **Email Consent** (`Email Consent`, select_one, Required): Please select whether you give your email consent for your financial institution to contact you.
  - Choose 'Yes' if you give your email consent.
  - Choose 'No' if you do not give your email consent.
- **Phone Consent** (`Phone Consent`, select_one, Optional): This field is for your phone consent. Please select whether you give your phone consent for your financial institution to contact you.
  - Choose 'Yes' if you give your phone consent.
  - Choose 'No' if you do not give your phone consent.
- **Data Use** (`Data Use`, select_multiple, Required): Please select the types of data you are willing to share with your financial institution and other relevant parties.
  - Choose 'Yes' for each option you agree to share.
  - Choose 'No' for each option you do not agree to share.
- **Transaction Amount** (`Transaction Amount`, number, Optional): This field is for your transaction amount. Please enter your transaction amount if you want to record it.
- **Transaction Frequency** (`Transaction Frequency`, select_multiple, Optional): This field is for your transaction frequency. Please select the frequency at which you make donations.
- **Transaction Date** (`Transaction Date`, date, Required): Please select the date of your transaction.
- **Transaction Time** (`Transaction Time`, time, Optional): This field is for your transaction time. Please select the time of your transaction.
- **Donor Name** (`Donor Name`, text, Optional): This field is for your name. Please enter your name if you want it to be recorded.
- **Donor Email** (`Donor Email`, email, Optional): This field is for your email. Please enter your email if you want your financial institution to contact you.
- **Donor Phone** (`Donor Phone`, text, Optional): This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number if you want your financial institution to contact you.
- **Donor Address** (`Donor Address`, text, Optional): This field is for your address. Please enter your address if you want your financial institution to know your location.
- **Donor Notes** (`Donor Notes`, note, Optional): This field is for any additional notes you want to provide about yourself as a donor.
- **Donor City** (`Donor City`, text, Optional): This field is for your city. Please enter your city if you want your financial institution to know your location.
- **Donor State** (`Donor State`, text, Optional): This field is for your state. Please enter your state if you want your financial institution to know your location.
- **Donor Zip** (`Donor Zip`, text, Optional): This field is for your zip code. Please enter your zip code if you want your financial institution to know your location.
- **Donor Country** (`Donor Country`, text, Optional): This field is for your country. Please enter your country if you want your financial institution to know your location.
- **Donation Frequency** (`Donation Frequency`, select_multiple, Optional): This field is for the frequency at which you make donations. Please select the frequency at which you make donations.
- **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, number, Optional): This field is for the amount you donate. Please enter the amount you donate if you want to record it.
- **Notes** (`Notes`, note, Optional): This field is for any additional notes you want to provide about your donation.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you understand and agree to the terms of your transaction data before filling out this form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- If you need help, do not hesitate to contact your financial institution for assistance.
