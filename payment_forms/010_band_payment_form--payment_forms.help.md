# Band Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information from customers or clients who are paying for a band or musician's services, such as live performances, merchandise, or other services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
   * Payer full name, email, and phone number
   * Band name
   * Payment type (e.g., performance fee, merchandise purchase, etc.)
   * Event date and location
   * Payment amount
   * Currency and method of payment
   * Installment plan (e.g., full payment, 50% deposit and balance due later, etc.)
   * Balance due date (if applicable)
   * Invoice number and promo or discount code (if applicable)
   * Tip or gratuity amount (if applicable)
2. Optionally, provide additional information:
   * Company or organization name (if paying on behalf of a business)
   * Address associated with the payment method
   * Special instructions or payment notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payer Full Name**: The name of the person making the payment.
* **Email Address**: The email address of the person making the payment, used for payment confirmation and communication.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number of the person making the payment, used for contact and communication.
* **Company or Organization**: If the payment is being made on behalf of a business, enter the company or organization name.
* **Band Name**: The name of the band or musician being paid.
* **Payment Type**: Select the type of payment you are making (e.g., performance fee, merchandise purchase, etc.).
* **Event Date**: The date of the event or service being paid for.
* **Event Location**: The venue name and address of the event.
* **Payment Amount**: The total amount of the payment in dollars.
* **Currency**: Select the currency of the payment (e.g., US dollar, Euro, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Card Type**: If paying by credit card, select the type of card (e.g., Visa, MasterCard, etc.). (Note: This field is not always required)
* **Installment Plan**: Select how you would like to pay (e.g., full payment, 50% deposit, etc.).
* **Deposit Amount**: If paying a deposit, enter the amount.
* **Balance Due Date**: If paying in installments, enter the date when the remaining balance is due.
* **Invoice Number**: If applicable, enter the reference invoice number.
* **Promo or Discount Code**: If applicable, enter the discount code.
* **Tip or Gratuity**: Enter any optional tip amount.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address associated with the payment method.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or payment notes.
* **Receipt Preference**: Select how you would like to receive the receipt (e.g., email, printed at event, etc.).
* **Terms and Conditions**: Select whether you agree to the payment terms.
* **Refund Policy Acknowledgment**: Select whether you have read and understood the refund policy.
* **Additional Payment Notes**: Enter any additional information about the payment.
