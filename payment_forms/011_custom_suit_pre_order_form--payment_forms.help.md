# custom_suit_pre_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers about their custom suit orders, including delivery details and customer contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Custom Suit Form with your order details.
2. Enter your delivery date in the <i>Delivery Date</i> field.
3. Enter your delivery time in the <i>Delivery Time</i> field.
4. Enter your customer details in the <i>Customer Details</i> field.
5. Select the type of order you are placing in the <i>Order Type</i> field.
6. Choose your preferred payment method from the <i>Payment Method</i> dropdown list.
7. Enter the customer's name in the <i>Customer Name</i> field.
8. Enter the customer's email address in the <i>Customer Email</i> field.
9. Enter the customer's phone number in the <i>Customer Phone</i> field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Suit Form (id: 1)**: Enter your custom suit details in this field.
* **Measurements (id: 2)**: Enter any additional measurements you have for the custom suit.
* **Delivery Date (id: 3)**: Enter a date for the delivery of the custom suit. This date should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delivery Time (id: 4)**: Enter the time of the delivery of the custom suit. This time should be in the format HH:MM:SS AM/PM.
* **Customer Details (id: 5)**: Enter any additional details about the customer, such as their size or preferences.
* **Order Type (id: 6)**: Select the type of order you are placing, e.g. standard, express, etc.
* **Payment Method (id: 7)**: Select the payment method you would like to use for this order. The options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Customer Name (id: 8)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email (id: 9)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (id: 10)**: Enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the above explanation for clarification.
