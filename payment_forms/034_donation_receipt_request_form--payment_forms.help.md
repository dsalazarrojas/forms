# Donation Receipt Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a donation receipt from the user. It's designed to collect information needed to issue a donation receipt after a donation has been made.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
- Fill in your First Name.
- Fill in your Last Name.
- Enter your Email address.
- Select the date you made the donation (or select the current date if you're not sure).
- Enter the amount of the donation.
- Provide a brief explanation of the purpose of the donation.
- If applicable, enter the name of the organization you donated to.
- Enter your contact phone number and address.
- Select your state from the list (optional).
- Enter your ZIP code.
- Fill in the name of the organization you donated to (if different from your name).
- Enter the email address of the contact person (if different from the email you provided earlier).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to the organization.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to the organization.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address.
* **Donation Date**: Select the date you made the donation. If unsure, select the current date.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation you made.
* **Purpose**: Provide a brief explanation of the purpose of the donation.
* **Organization**: If you donated to a different organization, enter its name here.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **State**: Select your state from the list.
* **ZIP**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Organization Name**: If you donated to a different organization, enter its name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person (if different from your email provided earlier).

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure of any information, select the current date or leave blank if not applicable.
