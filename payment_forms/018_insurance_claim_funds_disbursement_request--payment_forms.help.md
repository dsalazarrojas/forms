# Insurance Claim Funds Disbursement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request disbursement of funds related to an insurance claim. It is used to gather information about the claim, the claimant, and the claim's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the claim information section with details about the claim.
2.  Provide information about the claimant, including their contact details.
3.  Answer questions about the claimant's relationship with the individual submitting the claim.
4.  Select the status of the claim (e.g., active, inactive).
5.  Select the payment method for the claim (e.g., bank transfer, check).
6.  Enter the date of claim submission, denial, or resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claim Information** (`claim_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the claim, including the claim's details.
* **Claimant Information** (`claimant_info`, `text`, optional): Provide information about the individual submitting the claim.
* **Claimant Contact** (`claimant_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the claimant's contact details, such as phone number and email.
* **Claimant Relationship** (`claimant_relationship`, `select_one`, optional): Answer "Yes" if the claimant is an employee or family member, or "No" if they are not.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, `text`, optional): Enter details about the insurance policy.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the insurance company handling the claim.
* **Policy Information** (`policy_info`, `text`, optional): Enter additional information about the policy.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of the claim.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the payment method for the claim (e.g., bank transfer, check).
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the claim was submitted or resolved.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the claim (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Claim Submitted** (`claim_submitted`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Denied** (`claim_denied`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the claim was denied.
* **Claim Resolved** (`claim_resolved`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the claim was resolved.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
* Use specific options and formats for fields with select options or date fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the claims administrator for assistance.
