# Fire Department Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Department Support Donation Form is a form used to collect donations for the department's support. This form is designed to be a simple and straightforward way to accept contributions for a good cause.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your First Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Provide your Address.
5. Enter your City.
6. Select your state.
7. Enter your zip code.
8. Choose the amount you would like to donate.
9. Select the payment type (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
10. For Credit Card option, you will be required to choose your credit card type.
11. Provide your Card expiration date.
12. Enter your Card Security Code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* First Name (<name>, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* Email (<name>, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* Phone (<name>, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* Address (<name>, text, required/optional): Please enter your home or work address.
* City (<name>, text, required/optional): Enter your city or location.
* State (<name>, text, required/optional): Enter your state or province.
* Zip (<name>, text, required/optional): Enter your zip or postal code.
* Donation Amount (<name>, number, required/optional): Please enter the amount you wish to donate.
* Payment Type (<name>, select_one, required/optional): Select a payment type: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* Card Type (<name>, select_multiple, required/optional): For Credit Card, choose your credit card type (Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
* Card Expiration Date (<name>, date, required/optional): Enter your credit card expiration date.
* Card Security Code (<name>, text, required/optional): Enter the three-digit code on the back of your credit card.
