## Inflation Relief Refund Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to process Inflation Relief Refund requests from customers who wish to claim reimbursement for inflation-related financial difficulties. To be eligible for a refund, applicants must submit a valid reason for their request and supporting documents to prove their claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide a valid date of application for when you submitted your original claim.
3. Enter a clear reason for your refund request, explaining the circumstances that led to your financial difficulties.
4. Attach any relevant supporting documents that support your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (applicant_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Applicant Email** (applicant_email, email, required): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you with updates on your application.
* **Applicant Phone** (applicant_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number, including your area code.
* **Applicant Address** (applicant_address, text, required): Enter your full address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Application** (date_of_application, date, required): Enter the date you originally submitted your application for the Inflation Relief Refund.
* **Reason for Refund Request** (reason_for_refund_request, text, required): Clearly describe the reason why you are requesting a refund, providing details of your financial difficulties caused by inflation.
* **Supporting Documents** (supporting_documents, text, required): Attach any supporting documents that prove your claim, such as bank statements or receipts for cancelled services.
