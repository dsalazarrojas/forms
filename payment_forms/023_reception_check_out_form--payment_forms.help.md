# Reception Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information from customers or clients. It is likely used in a business or commercial setting where payments are processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including email address (if applicable).
2. Select the current payment status (Paid or Unpaid).
3. Choose the payment method used (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
4. Enter the amount paid and the date and time of payment.
5. Add any comments regarding the payment.
6. Review and agree to the payment terms.
7. Provide any additional payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your contact information, including your name and address. This field is not required but is useful for our records.
* **Email (Customer Email)**: Enter your email address if you have one. This field is required to ensure we can contact you.
* **Payment Status (Payment Status)**: Select either "Paid" or "Unpaid" to indicate the current payment status.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Choose the method used to make the payment from the options provided.
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount)**: Enter the amount paid. This field is not required but is useful for our records.
* **Payment Date (Payment Date)**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Time (Payment Time)**: Enter the time of the payment.
* **Comments (Comments)**: Add any comments or notes regarding the payment.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)**: Review and agree to the payment terms.
* **Payment Details (Payment Details)**: Provide any additional payment details.
* **Form Submitted At (Form Submitted At)**: This field is not required and shows the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Total (Total)**: This field is not required and shows the total amount paid.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be honest about the payment status and method.
* Add comments and payment details as needed.
* Review the payment terms before submitting the form.
