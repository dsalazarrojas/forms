# Luxury Business Gift Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Luxury Business Gift Order Form is a tool for businesses to order luxury gifts for their clients. It helps collect information about the client and their event details to create a personalized gift order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Client's First Name
	* Client's Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone Number
	* Event Date
	* Event Time
	* Gift Option 1
	* Gift Option 2
	* Gift Option 3

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client's First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Client's Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter the client's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Gift Option 1** (`gift_option_1`, select_one, required): Choose a gift option from the list.
* **Gift Option 2** (`gift_option_2`, select_one, required): Choose a gift option from the list.
* **Gift Option 3** (`gift_option_3`, select_one, required): Choose a gift option from the list.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the gift order.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address (if not already provided).
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number (if not already provided).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, optional): Click this button to cancel the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional notes about the gift order, use the "Note" field to enter them.
* If you need to cancel the form, click the "Cancel" button.
