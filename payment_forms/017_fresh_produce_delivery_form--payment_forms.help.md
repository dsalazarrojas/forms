# fresh_produce_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fresh_produce_delivery_form is designed to collect customer information and delivery details for the successful delivery of fresh produce.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information in the Customer Info section.
2. Enter your delivery details in the Delivery Details section.
3. Fill out your payment information in the Payment Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: (`customer_info`, `text`, not required)
	+ Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Delivery Details**: (`delivery_details`, `text`, not required)
	+ Specify your delivery preferences, including date, time, and any special instructions.
* **Payment Info**: (`payment_info`, `text`, not required)
	+ Enter your payment method and any relevant payment details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate delivery.
* Double-check your information for errors before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
