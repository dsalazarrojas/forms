# Vendor Payment Portal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Payment Portal Registration Form is a form used to collect vendor information for payment processing. It is designed to be completed by vendors, and each vendor is expected to fill out the form only once for each vendor entity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Info (page 1)
	* This section is used to collect general information about you as a vendor. Please fill out the following fields:
		+ **User Info**: This field is used to collect your name, company name, and other relevant information.
2. Provide your Contact Info (page 2)
	* This section is used to collect your contact information. Please fill out the following fields:
		+ **Contact Info**: This field is used to collect your contact information, such as your name, phone number, and email.
3. Choose your Payment Method (page 3)
	* This section is used to select your payment method. Please choose one of the following options:
		+ **Payment Method**: This field is used to select the payment method you wish to use (e.g. credit card, bank transfer).
4. Provide your Vendor Details (page 4)
	* This section is used to collect your vendor details. Please fill out the following fields:
		+ **Vendor Details**: This field is used to collect information about your company, such as your street address and state.
5. Accept our Agreement (page 5)
	* This section is used to accept our agreement. Please read and agree to the following terms:
		+ **Agreement**: This field is used to accept our terms and conditions.
6. Set up your Payment Schedule (page 6)
	* This section is used to set up your payment schedule. Please select a payment method and due date for your payment:
		+ **Payment Schedule**: This field is used to set up your payment schedule.
7. Review Payment Terms (page 7)
	* This section is used to review payment terms. Please read and agree to the following terms:
		+ **Payment Terms**: This field is used to review payment terms.
8. Provide Additional Vendor Terms (page 8)
	* This section is used to provide additional vendor terms. Please fill out the following fields:
		+ **Vendor Terms**: This field is used to provide additional vendor terms.
9. Set up Additional Payment Terms (page 9)
	* This section is used to set up additional payment terms. Please select a payment method and due date for your payment:
		+ **Payment Terms 2**: This field is used to set up additional payment terms.
10. Review Agreement Terms (page 10)
	* This section is used to review agreement terms. Please read and agree to the following terms:
		+ **Agreement Terms**: This field is used to review agreement terms.
11. Review Terms and Conditions (page 11)
	* This section is used to review terms and conditions. Please read and agree to the following terms:
		+ **Terms and Conditions**: This field is used to review terms and conditions.
12. Review Payment Due Date (page 12)
	* This section is used to review payment due date. Please select a payment due date:
		+ **Payment Due Date**: This field is used to review payment due date.
13. Update Last Updated Information (page 13)
	* This section is used to update last updated information. Please select a last updated date:
		+ **Last Updated**: This field is used to update last updated information.
14. Select Additional Payment Method (page 14)
	* This section is used to select additional payment method. Please choose one of the following options:
		+ **Payment Method 2**: This field is used to select additional payment method.
15. Review Additional Payment Terms (page 15)
	* This section is used to review additional payment terms. Please read and agree to the following terms:
		+ **Payment Terms 2**: This field is used to review additional payment terms.
