# Custom Carpeting Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Carpeting Order Form is a tool for customers to provide information about their custom carpeting orders. This form will be used to create a custom order that meets the customer's specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Information" section with your contact details.
2. Provide the dimensions and preferences of the custom carpeting order.
3. Enter the total cost of your order.
4. Select your custom carpeting options.
5. Estimate the delivery date and time for your order.
6. Double-check the order details to confirm accuracy.
7. Click submit to finalize your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer Information, text, required: false)**: Enter your name, contact number, and any other relevant details to help us process your order efficiently.
* **Dimensions and Preferences (Dimensions and Preferences, text, required: false)**: Describe the dimensions and any specific preferences for your custom carpeting order.
* **What is the total cost of your order? (total_cost, number, required: false)**: Please enter the total cost of your order, including any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Select your custom carpeting options (custom_carpeting_options, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose from the options provided to customize your order to your liking.
	+ Select at least one option from the list.
* **Estimated Delivery Date and Time (estimated_delivery, text, required: false)**: Estimate the delivery date and time for your order.
* **Is this correct? (confirmation, checkbox, required: false)**: Double-check your order details by confirming that the information provided is accurate.
	+ Select either Option A or Option B to confirm your order.
