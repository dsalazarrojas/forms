# Seafood Fresh Catch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seafood Fresh Catch Order Form is a form designed to collect information from customers for ordering seafood products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Ensure you are ordering seafood products.
2.  Fill out the order date, catch type, catch size, quantity, unit price, total price, payment method, note, customer email, customer phone, customer order details, assigned user, form submitted by, form completed by, form verified by, form submitted time, form completed time, form verified time, payment status, customer name, customer address, customer city, customer country, and customer zip code accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you want to place the order.
* **Catch Type** (`catch_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of seafood product you want to order (Fresh Catch, Frozen Seafood, or Frozen Fish).
* **Catch Size** (`catch_size`, number, optional): Enter the amount of seafood you want to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of units of the seafood product you want to order.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the price per unit of the seafood product.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you want to use (Credit Card or PayPal).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional note from you.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Order Details** (`customer_order_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your order.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): Select the staff member assigned to your order.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Completed By** (`form_completed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who completed the form.
* **Form Verified By** (`form_verified_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who verified the form.
* **Form Submitted Time** (`form_submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Form Completed Time** (`form_completed_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the form was completed.
* **Form Verified Time** (`form_verified_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the form was verified.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the payment status (Paid or Pending).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, optional): Enter your state.

## Tips
Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
