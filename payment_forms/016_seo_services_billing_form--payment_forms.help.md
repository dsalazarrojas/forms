# seo_services_billing_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SEO Services Billing Form is used to collect billing information for SEO services provided to clients. This form is a crucial part of the billing process, as it ensures accurate and complete information is captured for every client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide billing details, such as the client's name and address.
2. Select the services provided to the client.
3. Choose a payment method and date.
4. Specify the payment amount and terms.
5. Add any additional comments or notes as required.
6. Verify the payment status and terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Billing Details** (`1`, `text`, **required**): Enter the client's billing details, including their name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Services Selected** (`2`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the services provided to the client from the options available.
* **Payment Preferences** (`3`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any special payment preferences or instructions for the client.
* **Payment Method** (`4`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose the payment method to be used for this transaction.
* **Payment Date** (`5`, `date`, **optional**): Select the date on which the payment is due.
* **Payment Amount** (`6`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the total amount to be paid for the services provided.
* **Payment Term** (`7`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the terms of payment, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Client Details** (`8`, `text`, **required**): Enter the client's details, such as name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Billing Cycle** (`9`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose the billing cycle for this transaction.
* **Billing Frequency** (`10`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the billing frequency, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Notes** (`11`, `note`, **optional**): Add any additional notes or comments about the client.
* **Contact Details** (`12`, `text`, **required**): Enter the contact details of the person responsible for the billing.
* **Contact Name** (`13`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`14`, `email`, **optional**): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the billing.
* **Contact Phone** (`15`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the billing.
* **Contact Address** (`16`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the contact address of the person responsible for the billing.
* **Contact Notes** (`17`, `note`, **optional**): Add any additional notes or comments about the contact person.
* **Billing Total** (`18`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the total amount to be billed for the services provided.
* **Payment Status** (`19`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose the payment status, such as "Paid" or "Due".
* **Payment Terms** (`20`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the payment terms, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Notes** (`21`, `note`, **optional**): Add any additional notes or comments about the billing.
* **Additional Comments** (`22`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or notes about the billing.
* **Service Details** (`23`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional details about the services provided.
* **Payment Terms Description** (`24`, `text`, **optional**): Enter a description of the payment terms.
* **Payment Notes** (`25`, `note`, **optional**): Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.
