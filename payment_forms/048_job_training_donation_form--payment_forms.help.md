# Job Training Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Training Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors who are making a job training donation to the organization. This form helps to track and process the donation accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the donation amount you would like to donate.
2. Provide your name and contact information (phone, email, and address).
3. Indicate whether you are associated with the training program (program name) and if so, which program(s).
4. Select the frequency of your gift (yes or no).
5. If you have a recurring donation, select the start and end dates of the donation period.
6. Specify the frequency of the recurring donation (yes or no).
7. Enter any notes about your donation.
8. If you have a contact phone, email, or address, please provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **donation_amount** (1): Enter the amount you would like to donate to the job training program.
* **donor_name** (2): Enter your name as the donor.
* **organization_name** (3): Enter the name of the organization or company associated with this donation.
* **program_name** (4): Select 'Yes' if you are associated with this training program, otherwise select 'No'.
* **frequency_of_gift** (5): Select 'Yes' if you want to donate more than once.
* **recurring_donation_start_date** (6): Enter the start date of your recurring donation (if applicable).
* **recurring_donation_end_date** (7): Enter the end date of your recurring donation (if applicable).
* **recurring_donation_frequency** (8): Select 'Yes' if your donation is recurring.
* **notes** (9): Enter any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **contact_phone** (10): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **contact_email** (11): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **contact_address** (12): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **donor_relationship** (13): Select 'Yes' if you have a relationship with the training program.
* **donor_source** (14): Select 'Yes' if you are donating from a source (e.g., company).
* **training_programs** (15): Select any additional training programs you are associated with.
* **additional_donation_frequency** (16): Select 'Yes' if you have an additional donation frequency.
* **donation_frequency** (17): Select the frequency of your donation (e.g., monthly, annually).
* **job_title** (18): Select 'Yes' if you are donating as part of a job title.
