# Deposit Payment Receipt - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Deposit Payment Receipt" form is used to document and record payment receipts for deposits made by lessees to landlords. It helps landlords keep track of these transactions and ensures accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the landlord's name in the "Landlord Name" field.
2. Enter the lessee's name in the "Lessee Name" field.
3. Provide the lessee's contact number in the "Lessee Contact Number" field.
4. Enter the lessee's address in the "Lessee Address" field.
5. Enter the amount received from the lessee in the "Amount Received" field.
6. Select the payment date in the "Payment Date" field.
7. Sign the "Lessee's Signature" field to confirm the information provided.
8. (Optional) Enter the document date in the "Document Date" field.
9. (Optional) Enter the lessee's phone number in the "Lessee's Phone" field.
10. (Optional) Enter the amount received by the lessee in the "Lessee's Amount Received" field.
11. (Optional) Select the payment date of the lessee in the "Lessee's Payment Date" field.
12. (Optional) Enter the lessee's phone number in the "Lessee Phone Lessee" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Name (1)**: Please enter the landlord's name.
* **Lessee Name (2)**: Please enter the lessee's name.
* **Lessee Contact Number (3)**: Please enter the lessee's contact number.
* **Lessee Address (4)**: Please enter the lessee's address.
* **Amount Received (5)**: Please enter the amount received.
* **Payment Date (6)**: Please enter the payment date.
* **Lessee's Signature (7)**: Please sign here to confirm the information provided.
* **Document Date (8)**: (Optional) Please enter the document date (not required).
* **Lessee's Phone (9)**: (Optional) Please enter the lessee's phone number.
* **Lessee's Amount Received (10)**: (Optional) Please enter the amount received by the lessee.
* **Lessee's Payment Date (11)**: (Optional) Please select the payment date of the lessee.
* **Lessee Phone Lessee (12)**: (Optional) Please enter the lessee's phone number (optional, but not clearly labeled as such; best-effort interpretation).
