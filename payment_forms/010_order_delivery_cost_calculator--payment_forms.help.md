# Order Delivery Cost Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users calculate the estimated delivery cost for an order based on various parameters. 

## How To Complete This Form
To use the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the relevant details in the "User Input" field. This is where you can provide any additional information that may affect the delivery cost calculation.
2. Enter the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field, so we can accurately calculate the delivery cost based on distance or zone-based calculations.
3. Select the cost currency from the dropdown menu in the "Cost Currency" field. This will be used to display the calculated delivery cost.
4. Choose the type of delivery cost from the dropdown menu in the "Cost Type" field. This will affect how the delivery cost is calculated.
5. Enter the estimated or actual delivery cost in the "Estimated Delivery Cost" or "Actual Delivery Cost" field, depending on whether you have a pre-estimated or actual cost available.
6. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field if you need to provide further information about the order.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, so we can contact you if needed.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, so we can contact you if needed.
9. Click "Submit" to save your input and calculate the estimated delivery cost.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information that may affect the delivery cost calculation.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the delivery address to help us accurately calculate the delivery cost based on distance or zone-based calculations.
* **Delivery Cost** (`delivery_cost`, text, optional): Enter the estimated or actual delivery cost, if available.
* **Cost Currency** (`cost_currency`, select, optional): Choose the currency to display the calculated delivery cost in. Options: AUD, USD, EUR, CAD, JPY, GBP, CNY.
* **Cost Type** (`cost_type`, select, optional): Choose how the delivery cost is calculated. Options: Flat Rate, Distance-Based, Zone-Based, Weight-Based, Other.
* **Estimated Delivery Cost** (`estimated_delivery_cost`, number, optional): Enter the estimated delivery cost, if available.
* **Actual Delivery Cost** (`actual_delivery_cost`, number, optional): Enter the actual delivery cost, if available.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you if needed.
* **Chat** (`chat`, text, optional): Enter a chat reference, if available.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to save your input and calculate the estimated delivery cost.
