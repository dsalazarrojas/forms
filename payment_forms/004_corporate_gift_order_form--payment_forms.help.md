# Corporate Gift Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for companies to order gifts for their employees or clients. It collects information about the gift selection, recipient details, delivery preferences, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the gift(s) you would like to order from the Gift Selections page.
2. Enter the recipient's details, including their name and email.
3. Choose your preferred delivery method and address.
4. If necessary, add a gift message and select a specific gift option.
5. Enter the gift price and tax rate.
6. Calculate the total cost including shipping and tax.
7. Choose your payment method.
8. Add any additional gift notes or messages.
9. Select the order date and time (if applicable).
10. Confirm that the order is submitted by the authorized person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gift Selections** (`gift_selections`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more gift options from the available list.
* **Recipient Details** (`recipient_details`, text, required): Enter the recipient's name and email address.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, required): Choose your preferred delivery method and address.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Gift Card Details** (`gift_card_details`, select_one, required): Select whether a gift card is included in the order.
* **Gift Option Details** (`gift_option_details`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional gift options.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): Enter the recipient's name.
* **Gift Option** (`gift_option`, select_one, required): Select the specific gift option.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose the delivery method (e.g. shipping, pickup).
* **Recipient E-mail** (`recipient_email`, email, required): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Recipient Phone** (`recipient_phone`, text, required): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the delivery address.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your company's address.
* **Gift Note** (`gift_note`, note, required): Add any gift message or notes.
* **Gift Price** (`gift_price`, number, required): Enter the gift price.
* **Gift Total** (`gift_total`, number, required): Calculate the total gift cost.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, number, required): Enter the tax rate.
* **Tax Total** (`tax_total`, number, required): Calculate the tax total.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required): Enter the shipping cost.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): Calculate the total cost including tax and shipping.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, note, required): Add any gift message.
