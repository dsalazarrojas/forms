# Paystubs - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paystubs form is used to collect information about employee paystubs for tracking and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Select the status of the paystubs for the current period in the `paystubs_1` field.
3. Enter the start date of the pay period in the `start_date` field.
4. Enter the end date of the pay period in the `end_date` field.
5. Select the pay periods for which the employee is eligible for payment in the `pay_periods`, `payperiods_1` to `payperiods_15` fields.
6. Confirm the pay period status for each selected period in the `pay_period`, `payperiods_2` to `payperiods_15` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Paystubs** (`paystubs_1`): Select the current status of the paystubs for the employee (Yes/No).
* **employee_name** (`employee_name_1`): Enter the employee's name for whom the paystubs are being processed.
* **start_date** (`start_date_1`): Enter the start date of the pay period.
* **end_date** (`end_date_1`): Enter the end date of the pay period.
* **pay_periods** (`pay_periods_1`): Select the pay periods for which the employee is eligible for payment (Yes/No) for each pay period.
* **pay_period** (`pay_period_1`): Confirm the pay period status (Yes/No) for each selected pay period.
* **payperiods_2** to **payperiods_15** (`payperiods_2` to `payperiods_15`): Select the pay periods for which the employee is eligible for payment (Yes/No) for each pay period.
