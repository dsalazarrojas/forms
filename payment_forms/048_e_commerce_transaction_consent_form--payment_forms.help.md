# E Commerce Transaction Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information from customers or their authorized representatives about a transaction made through our e-commerce platform. It ensures that customers are aware of and agree to the terms of the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Customer Name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the Transaction Amount in the "Transaction Amount" field.
3. Enter the date of the transaction in the "Transaction Date" field.
4. Select the type of transaction made ("Credit", "Debit", or "Other") in the "Transaction Type" field.
5. Select all applicable names that should receive the payment information ("John", "Jane", or "Joe") in the "Prophet Name" field.
6. Enter your Customer Email in the "Customer E-mail" field (optional but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter the name of the customer who made the transaction.
*   **Transaction Amount (transaction_amount)**: Enter the total amount of the transaction.
*   **Transaction Date (transaction_date)**: Enter the date on which the transaction was made.
*   **Transaction Type (transaction_type)**: Select the type of transaction made (Credit, Debit, or Other). Please note that this field may not be required to be filled, but it’s good to be as specific as possible.
*   **Prophet Name (prophet_name)**: If multiple people are authorized to receive the payment, select all their names from the dropdown list (John, Jane, Joe). This is a required field as it’s essential to know to whom the payment should be directed.
*   **Customer E-mail (customer_email)**: Enter the customer's email (optional but recommended). If provided, this will help us get in touch with the customer for any questions or issues related to the transaction.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all fields that are required (Customer Name, Prophet Name). Although not all fields are mandatory, filling out every section will help us better understand and process your request.
*   Use the correct format for dates in the "Transaction Date" field.
*   Be precise with the customer name and email to ensure accuracy.
*   For the "Transaction Type" field, select the most accurate option to reflect the nature of the transaction.
*   Select all relevant names in the "Prophet Name" field to ensure that the payment reaches the correct individuals.
