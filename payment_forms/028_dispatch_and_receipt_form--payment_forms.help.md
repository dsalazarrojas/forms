# Dispatch And Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record shipping and receiving information for items, including details about the item itself, the shipping address, shipping cost, payment status, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Shipping Address** field with the address where the item was shipped from.
2. Enter the **Dispatch Date** and **Dispatch Time** in the respective date and time fields.
3. Provide details about the item in the **Item Details** field.
4. Select the **Item Status** from the options provided.
5. Enter the quantity of the item in the **Item Quantity** field.
6. Provide additional shipping details in the **Shipping Details** field.
7. Enter the **Shipping Cost** of the item.
8. Select the **Payment Method** and **Payment Status** from the respective options.
9. If necessary, enter the **Receipt Date** and **Receipt Time** for the item.
10. Add any additional **Receipt Details**.
11. Enter any **Remarks** or **Notes** about the shipment or receipt.
12. Optionally, enter the **Email**, **Phone**, and **Customer Address** of the recipient.
13. Select the **Dispatching Party**, **Receiving Party**, and **Assigned User** from the respective options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where the item was shipped from.
- **Dispatch Date**: Enter the date the item was dispatched.
- **Dispatch Time**: Enter the time the item was dispatched.
- **Item Details**: Provide details about the item being shipped.
- **Item Status**: Select the status of the item (Active/Inactive).
- **Item Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the item.
- **Shipping Details**: Provide additional details about the shipping process.
- **Shipping Cost**: Enter the cost of shipping the item.
- **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment for the item (Yes/No).
- **Payment Status**: Select the status of payment for the item (Active/Inactive).
- **Receipt Date**: Enter the date the item was received.
- **Receipt Time**: Enter the time the item was received.
- **Receipt Details**: Provide additional details about the receipt process.
- **Remarks**: Enter any remarks or notes about the shipment or receipt.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the shipment or receipt.
- **Email**: Enter the email of the recipient.
- **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the recipient.
- **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the customer.
- **Customer Email**: Enter the email of the customer.
- **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
- **Dispatching Party**: Select whether the item was dispatched by the company (Yes/No).
- **Receiving Party**: Select whether the item was received by the company (Yes/No).
- **Assigned User**: Select the user assigned to the shipment or receipt (Yes/No).
