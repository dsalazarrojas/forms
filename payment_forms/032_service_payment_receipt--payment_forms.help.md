# Service Payment Receipt - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Payment Receipt form is used to collect payment information for services rendered to customers. It is intended for new services that have not been previously invoiced or receipted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the customer's name in the Customer Name field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the Customer Email field.
3. Enter a brief description of the service provided in the Service Description field.
4. Enter the service charge for the service provided in the Service Charge field.
5. Enter the date of payment in the Payment Date field.
6. Enter the total payment amount in the Total Payment field.
7. Select the payment method used from the available options (Cash or Card).
8. Add any additional notes about the receipt in the Receipt Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's name accurately and truthfully.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the service provided.
* **Service Charge** (`service_charge`, `number`, required: false): Enter the service charge amount.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of payment.
* **Total Payment** (`total_payment`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more payment methods used (Cash or Card).
* **Receipt Notes** (`receipt_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the receipt.
