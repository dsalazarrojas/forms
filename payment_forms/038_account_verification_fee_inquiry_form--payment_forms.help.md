# Account Verification Fee Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit inquiries about account verification fees. Please fill out the form to provide detailed information about your concern.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
3.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
4.  If applicable, enter your company or organization name in the "Company or Organization Name" field.
5.  Enter your **Account Number or ID** in the "Account Number or ID" field.
6.  Select the **Account Type** that best describes your account in the "Account Type" field.
7.  Select the **Type of Fee Inquiry** that best describes your concern in the "Type of Fee Inquiry" field.
8.  If relevant, enter the **Fee Amount Charged** in the "Fee Amount Charged" field.
9.  If relevant, enter the date of the charge in the "Date of Charge" field.
10. Select the **Type of Verification** that best describes your concern in the "Type of Verification" field.
11. Provide a detailed description of your inquiry in the "Detailed Description of Your Inquiry" field.
12. Select your **Preferred Response Method** in the "Preferred Response Method" field.
13. Enter any **Additional Comments** you'd like to include in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Company or Organization Name (if applicable)** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you're inquiring about a business or organization account, enter the name of your company or organization.
*   **Account Number or ID** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter your account number or ID to help us identify your account.
*   **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of account you have with us: Individual, Business, Corporate, Non-profit, or Other.
*   **Type of Fee Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of fee inquiry that best describes your concern.
*   **Fee Amount Charged (if applicable)** (`fee_amount_charged`, number, optional): If you're inquiring about a specific fee, enter the amount charged.
*   **Date of Charge (if applicable)** (`charge_date`, date, optional): If you know the date when the fee was charged, enter it.
*   **Type of Verification** (`verification_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of verification that best describes your concern.
*   **Detailed Description of Your Inquiry** (`inquiry_details`, text, required): Please provide a detailed description of your inquiry to help us better understand your concern.
*   **Preferred Response Method** (`preferred_response_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be responded to: via Email, Phone, or Mail.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional information to include with your inquiry, enter it here.
