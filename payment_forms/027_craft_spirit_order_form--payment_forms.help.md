# Craft Spirit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Spirit Order Form is a simple, user-friendly form designed to collect necessary information from customers about their craft spirit orders. This form is intended to be completed by customers who want to place a new order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Order Info** in the first field. This is where you can provide any relevant details about your order, such as the name of the product you're interested in or any specific requests you have.
2. Provide your **Contact Info** in the second field. This includes your name, email address, and phone number, which will be used to contact you about your order.
3. Enter your **Shipping Info** in the third field. This is where you can provide your shipping address and any other details that might be helpful for delivery.
4. In the fourth field, select your **Payment Method**. This is where you can choose between "Card Number" and "EFT" (Electronic Fund Transfer) options. Please ensure you select the option that is most suitable for you.
5. Finally, add any additional **Order Details** in the last field. This is an opportunity to provide any further context or information about your order that might be useful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Info** (1, text, required/optional): This field is where you can enter any relevant details about your order. Be as specific as possible to ensure our team can process your order accurately.
* **Contact Info** (2, text, required/optional): This field is where you can enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. We will use this to contact you about your order.
* **Shipping Info** (3, text, required/optional): This field is where you can enter your shipping address and any other details that might be helpful for delivery.
* **Payment Method** (4, select_one, required): This field requires you to select a payment method between "Card Number" and "EFT". Please choose the option that is most suitable for you.
* **Order Details** (5, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any further context or information about your order that might be useful.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any delays in processing your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
