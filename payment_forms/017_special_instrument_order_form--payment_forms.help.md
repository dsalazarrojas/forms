# Special Instrument Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Instrument Order Form is designed for customers to place a special order for a musical instrument. The form gathers payment details, delivery information, custom order instructions, and other relevant data to facilitate the order processing and delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the payment details, including payment method, amount, and any additional instructions
* Provide delivery details, including delivery address and any special delivery instructions
* If applicable, provide custom instrument order information, including instrument type and model
* Review and fill in any additional fields as required or necessary for your order

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Details**: This section collects payment method and amount information for processing. Please select the correct payment method (Credit Card, E-check, or Other) and enter the payment amount.
* **Delivery Details**: This section collects delivery address and any special delivery instructions. Please ensure that this information is accurate and complete.
* **Custom Instrument Order**: If you have specific custom instrument order instructions, please provide them here. This field is optional but helps us process your order smoothly.
* **Instrument Type**: Select the type of instrument you are ordering (Guitar, Violin, or Other). This field is essential for accurate instrument delivery.
* **Instrument Model**: If you know the model of the instrument you are ordering, please provide it here.
* **Order Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or instructions you have for your order. Please use it to provide any necessary details.
* **Submit**: Please select Yes or No to confirm that you have reviewed and filled in this form correctly.
* **File Uploads**: If you have any additional documents or files to upload (e.g., custom order instructions, music sheets), please upload them here.
* **Payment Method**: This field collects the method of payment (Credit Card, E-check, or Other).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for your order.

## Tips

* Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact our support team for clarification
* If you are making a custom order, please fill in the custom instrument order field to help us process your order smoothly
* Ensure that you select the correct payment method and amount for your order
* Review and upload any additional files or documents as required or necessary for your order
