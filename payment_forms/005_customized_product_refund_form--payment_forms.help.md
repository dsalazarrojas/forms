# Customized Product Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to initiate a refund for their customized product. Please fill out this form with your details, and we will review and process your refund request as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each section.
2. Click submit to send your completed form to our team for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_input)**: Enter your customer name or ID.
* **Product ID (product_id)**: Enter the ID of the customized product you want to return.
* **Reason for Return (reason_for_return)**: Explain the reason why you are requesting a refund for the product.
* **Return Date (return_date)**: Enter the date you would like to receive the refund on (if applicable).
* **Refund Amount (refund_amount)**: Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Shipping Information (shipping_info)**: Provide any relevant shipping details, such as tracking numbers or delivery addresses.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select the method through which you paid for the customized product (PayPal, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Customer Notes (customer_notes)**: If you have any additional comments or information regarding your refund request, please enter them here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required information accurately to ensure smooth refund processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our support team.
