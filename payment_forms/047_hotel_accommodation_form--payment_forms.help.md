# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for hotel reservations. It gathers details such as guest information, accommodation details, and special requests for the reservation. Fill in the form accurately to ensure that your hotel reservation is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3. If you need to add any additional information, use the "Additional Info" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Hotel Reservation** (reservation_details, text, required): Enter the title of the hotel reservation.
- **Guest Information** (guest_info, text, required): Enter the guest's information, such as their name and any other relevant details.
- **Accommodation Type** (accommodation_type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of accommodation you require (Room, Suite, or Apartment).
- **Accommodation Dates** (accommodation_date, date, required): Enter the check-in date for the reservation.
- **Check-in Time** (accommodation_time, time, required): Enter the check-in time for the reservation.
- **Guest Phone** (guest_phone, text, optional): Enter the guest's phone number.
- **Email** (email, email, required): Enter the guest's email address.
- **Special Requests** (special_requests, text, required): Enter any special requests or requirements you have for the reservation.
- **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the reservation.
- **Additional Info** (additional_info, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the reservation.
- **Name** (guest_name, text, required): Enter the guest's name.
- **Gender** (guest_gender, select_one, optional): Choose the guest's gender (Male, Female, or Other).
- **Date of Birth** (guest_dob, date, required): Enter the guest's date of birth.
- **Nationality** (guest_nationality, text, required): Enter the guest's nationality.
