# Massage Therapy Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Subscription Form is used to capture client information and payment details for massage therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client, as provided by them or through previous interactions.
2. **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
3. **Payment Type**: Select one of the following options:
	* Yes: The client is willing to make payment.
	* No: The client is not willing to make payment.
4. **Payment Frequency**: Select one or more of the following options:
	* Yes: The client is willing to make payments in this frequency.
5. **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
6. **Billing Address**: Enter the client's billing address.
7. **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
8. **Start Date** and **End Date**: Enter the dates when the subscription starts and ends.
9. **Subscription Status**: Select one of the following options:
	* Active: The subscription is active.
	* Inactive: The subscription is inactive.
