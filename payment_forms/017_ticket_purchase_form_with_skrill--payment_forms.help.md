# Ticket Purchase Form With Skrill - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to purchase tickets for an event. It includes fields for event details, ticket type, quantity, ticket price, currency, name, email, phone number, and payment method.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:


1.  Select the correct **Ticket Type** from the options provided (Regular, VIP, Front Row).
2.  If you need to purchase **Quantity** of tickets, enter the number here.
3.  Enter the **Ticket Price** for the selected ticket type.
4.  Choose the **Currency** for the ticket price (EUR, USD, or GBP).
5.  Enter your **Name** as it should be displayed on the tickets.
6.  Enter your **Email** address for correspondence.
7.  Enter your **Phone** number for further communication.
8.  Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Skrill or PayPal).
9.  If you've selected **Skrill**, enter any **Payment Method Details** in the text field.
10. Enter any additional **Note** you'd like to include with your order.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Ticket Type (Regular, VIP, Front Row)**: Choose the type of ticket you want to purchase.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the number of tickets you'd like to buy.
*   **Ticket Price**: Enter the price for the selected ticket type.
*   **Currency (EUR, USD, or GBP)**: Select the currency for the ticket price.
*   **Name**: Enter your name as it will be displayed on the tickets.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and order updates.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
*   **Payment Method (Skrill or PayPal)**: Choose your preferred payment method.
*   **Payment Method (Other)**: If you've selected **Other**, enter the name of your other payment method.
*   **Other Payment Method**: If you've selected **Other**, enter any additional information about your payment method.
*   **Note**: Enter any note or message you'd like to include with your order.


## Tips
Before submitting, ensure that all required fields (if any) are filled in. If you're unsure about any field, review this guide for more information or contact our support team if needed.
