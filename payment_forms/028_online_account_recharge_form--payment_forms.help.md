# Online Account Recharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Account Recharge Form is used for customers to provide information related to their accounts, facilitating the recharging process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Customer Name in the designated field.
2. Provide your Account Number if required.
3. Select the type of account you are recharging (Prepaid, Digital Wallet, or Membership).
4. Enter the Amount you wish to recharge.
5. Choose a Payment Method (Card, Bank Transfer, or Online Pay).
6. Enter your Email address if required.
7. Add your Phone number if required.
8. Add any additional Note if necessary.
9. Select the tool assigned to you (chatjimmy or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Account Number**: If required, enter your account number to facilitate the recharge process.
* **Account Type**: Choose the type of account you are recharging (Prepaid, Digital Wallet, or Membership).
* **Amount**: Enter the exact amount you wish to recharge.
* **Payment Method**: Select one or more of the following payment methods (Card, Bank Transfer, or Online Pay).
* **Email**: If required, enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Note**: Add any additional information or comments related to your account recharge.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool you are using to recharge your account (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Form ID**: (This field is not intended for end-user interaction.)
