# Care Credit Payment Receipt - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record payment details for administrative purposes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the `payer_name`, the name of the person or organization making the payment.
2. Enter the `payment_date`, the date on which the payment was received.
3. Enter the `payment_amount`, the total amount paid.
4. Choose the `payment_method`, selecting one of the available options (Credit card, Debit card, Bank transfer, or Cash).
5. Optionally, enter a `transaction_id` for internal reference.
6. Enter a brief description of the service paid for in `service_description`.
7. If applicable, enter the name of the issuing organization or staff in `issued_by`.
8. Enter the `contact_email` for any questions or further information related to the receipt.
9. Add any additional payment notes in `notes`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payer name (payer_name)**: The name of the person or organization making the payment.
* **Payment date (payment_date)**: The date on which the payment was received. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Payment amount (payment_amount)**: The total amount paid. This should be a numeric value.
* **Payment method (payment_method)**: Select one of the available payment options (Credit card, Debit card, Bank transfer, or Cash).
* **Transaction id (transaction_id)**: An internal reference number for the transaction. Optional, but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Service description (service_description)**: A brief description of the service paid for.
* **Issued by (issued_by)**: The name of the issuing organization or staff member.
* **Contact email (contact_email)**: For receipt questions or further information.
* **Notes (notes)**: Additional notes about the payment.
