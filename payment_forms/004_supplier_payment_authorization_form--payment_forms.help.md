# Supplier Payment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Payment Authorization Form is used to initiate or approve payments to a supplier. It requires information such as supplier selection, payment date, and payment amount, among other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the supplier you wish to make a payment to from the drop-down list on page 1.
2. Enter the payment date on page 2.
3. Enter the payment amount on page 3.
4. Add any additional notes if necessary on page 4.
5. Enter the supplier's contact phone number on page 5.
6. Enter the supplier's email address on page 6.
7. Upload any relevant files on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier** (`supplier_payment_authorization_form_page_1`, select_one): Select the supplier you wish to make a payment to. Choose from the list of available suppliers.
* **Select Date** (`supplier_payment_authorization_form_page_2`, date): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Enter Amount** (`supplier_payment_authorization_form_page_3`, number): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Add Notes** (`supplier_payment_authorization_form_page_4`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about the payment. This field is optional.
* **Contact Phone** (`supplier_payment_authorization_form_page_5`, text): Enter the supplier's contact phone number.
* **Enter Email** (`supplier_payment_authorization_form_page_6`, email): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Select File** (`supplier_payment_authorization_form_page_7`, text): Upload any relevant files or documents related to the payment.
* **Select File 2** (`supplier_payment_authorization_form_page_8`, text): Upload any additional relevant files or documents related to the payment.
