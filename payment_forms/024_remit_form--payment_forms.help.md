# remit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The remit_form is a form used to collect information about remittances, which are payments made from one person or organization to another. This form is used to gather details about the sender and receiver of the payment, including their names, addresses, phone numbers, and bank information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the sender's name in the "Sender Name" field.
2. Enter the sender's address in the "Sender Address" field.
3. Enter the receiver's name in the "Receiver Name" field.
4. Enter the receiver's address in the "Receiver Address" field.
5. Select the payment method from the "Payment Method" field dropdown menu.
6. Enter the payment amount in the "Amount" field.
7. Enter the payment date in the "Payment Date" field.
8. Enter the payment time in the "Payment Time" field.
9. Enter the receiver's email address in the "Receiver Email" field (optional).
10. Enter any additional note about the payment in the "Note" field (optional).
11. Enter the sender's phone number in the "Sender Phone" field (optional).
12. Enter the sender's phone label in the "Sender Phone Label" field (optional).
13. Enter the receiver's phone number in the "Receiver Phone" field (optional).
14. Enter the receiver's phone label in the "Receiver Phone Label" field (optional).
15. Enter the sender's country in the "Sender Country" field.
16. Enter the receiver's country in the "Receiver Country" field.
17. Enter the sender's city in the "Sender City" field.
18. Enter the receiver's city in the "Receiver City" field.
19. Enter the sender's province (or state) in the "Sender Province" field.
20. Enter the receiver's province (or state) in the "Receiver Province" field.
21. Enter the sender's postal code in the "Sender Postal" field.
22. Enter the receiver's postal code in the "Receiver Postal" field.
23. Enter the sender's bank in the "Sender Bank" field.
24. Enter the receiver's bank in the "Receiver Bank" field.
25. Enter the remitter (the person or organization making the payment) in the "Rmitter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sender Name (Sender Name)**: Enter the full name of the person sending the payment.
* **Sender Address (Sender Address)**: Enter the sender's full address, including street address, province, and postal code.
* **Receiver Name (Receiver Name)**: Enter the full name of the person receiving the payment.
* **Receiver Address (Receiver Address)**: Enter the receiver's full address, including street address, province, and postal code.
* **Amount (Amount)**: Enter the amount of the payment in the local currency.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Select the method of payment from the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Date (Payment Date)**: Enter the date of the payment in the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Payment Time (Payment Time)**: Enter the time of the payment in the format HH:MM.
* **Receiver Email (Receiver Email)**: Enter the email address of the person receiving the payment (optional).
* **Note (Note)**: Enter any additional notes about the payment (optional).
* **Sender Phone (Sender Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person sending the payment (optional).
* **Sender Phone Label (Sender Phone Label)**: Enter a label for the sender's phone number (optional).
* **Receiver Phone (Receiver Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person receiving the payment (optional).
* **Receiver Phone Label (Receiver Phone Label)**: Enter a label for the receiver's phone number (optional).
* **Sender Country (Sender Country)**: Enter the country of the person sending the payment.
* **Receiver Country (Receiver Country)**: Enter the country of the person receiving the payment.
* **Sender City (Sender City)**: Enter the city of the person sending the payment.
* **Receiver City (Receiver City)**: Enter the city of the person receiving the payment.
* **Sender Province (Sender Province)**: Enter the province or state of the person sending the payment.
* **Receiver Province (Receiver Province)**: Enter the province or state of the person receiving the payment.
* **Sender Postal (Sender Postal)**: Enter the postal code of the person sending the payment.
* **Receiver Postal (Receiver Postal)**: Enter the postal code of the person receiving the payment.
* **Sender Bank (Sender Bank)**: Enter the name of the bank of the person sending the payment.
* **Receiver Bank (Receiver Bank)**: Enter the name of the bank of the person receiving the payment.
* **Rmitter (Rmitter)**: Enter the person or organization making the payment.
