# Accounting Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Accounting Service Subscription Form" is a form used to collect information from clients who wish to subscribe to accounting services. It includes details about the company, contact person, and subscription preferences, as well as payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information about the company subscribing to accounting services.
2. Complete all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (id: 1)**: Enter the legal name of the company subscribing to accounting services. This will be used for billing and communication purposes.
* **Contact Person (id: 2)**: Provide the full name of the primary contact person within the company. This information will be used for all future communication regarding the subscription.
* **Contact Email (id: 3)**: Enter the business email of the primary contact person for communication about the subscription.
* **Contact Phone (id: 4)**: Enter the phone number for the primary contact person for quick inquiries and updates about the subscription.
* **Business Address (id: 5)**: Provide the complete business address including street, city, state, and zip code. This information will be used for mailing and shipping purposes.
* **Service Package (id: 6)**: Select the desired accounting service package from the options provided. This will determine the scope of services included in the subscription.
* **Billing Cycle (id: 7)**: Choose how frequently you want to be billed. Options are monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually.
* **Subscription Start Date (id: 8)**: Select the date when you would like the subscription to begin.
* **Services Included (id: 9)**: Select all the services that are part of your subscription package. These services will be provided according to your selected package.
* **Payment Method (id: 10)**: Choose the method of payment for your subscription. Options include credit card, debit card, bank transfer, check, or ACH/electronic check.
* **Credit Card Number (id: 11)**: If you selected credit card as the payment method, enter your credit card number.
* **Card Expiration Date (id: 12)**: If you selected credit card as the payment method, enter the expiration date of your card (MM/YY).
* **Billing Zip Code (id: 13)**: If you selected a payment method that requires a billing zip code, enter it here.
* **Terms Agreement (id: 14)**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the subscription.
