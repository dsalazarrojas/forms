# Diamond Ring Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect pre-order information from customers for a diamond ring. It is intended to be used as a starting point for customers who wish to place an order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide the required pre-order information in the "Pre-Order Information" section.
2.  If desired, select any additional engravings in the "Engraving Requests" section.
3.  Enter your delivery information in the "Delivery Information" section, if applicable.
4.  Review your order in the "Review" section to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre-Order Information (`pre_order_info`, text, required)**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information required for us to process your pre-order.
* **Engraving Requests (`engraving_requests`, select multiple, optional)**: Choose from our selection of engravings to add a personal touch to your ring.
* **Delivery Information (`delivery_info`, text, optional)**: Enter your shipping address and any other delivery details required for timely and accurate shipping.
* **Payment Information (`payment_info`, text, optional)**: This field is not necessary for pre-ordering and will be collected later in the payment process.
* **Review (`review`, text, optional)**: Double-check your order to ensure accuracy before submitting.
