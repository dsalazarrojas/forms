# Echeck.Net Rent Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to process eCheck rent payments for the eCheck.Net platform. It collects essential information for accurate and secure payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** and **Last name** in the designated fields.
2. Provide your **Address** for payment processing purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone number** for contact information.
4. If you have an **Email address**, enter it for correspondence and updates.
5. Enter your **eCheck.Net Account Name** and **Account Number** for payment reference.
6. Select the type of **Transaction** you are making (Debit, Credit, or Pre-Authorized).
7. Enter the **Date** of the payment (if applicable).
8. Fill in the **Amount** of the payment (if applicable).
9. If applicable, sign with your **Authorized Signature**.
10. Fill in your **Authorized Name**, **Authorized Title**, and the **Authorized Date** for verification purposes.
11. Select one or more **Authorized Date Type** (Lease, Rent, or Utility).
12. If needed, add any **Authorized Note**.
13. Finally, select the **Payment Terms** if you have any specific payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Enter your complete address for payment processing purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for correspondence and updates.
* **eCheck.Net Account Name**: Enter your eCheck.Net account name for reference.
* **eCheck.Net Account Number**: Enter your eCheck.Net account number for reference.
* **eCheck.Net Transaction Type**: Select the type of transaction you are making (Debit, Credit, or Pre-Authorized).
* **eCheck.Net Date**: Enter the date of the payment (if applicable).
* **eCheck.Net Amount**: Fill in the amount of the payment (if applicable).
* **Authorized Signature**: Sign with your authorized signature if applicable.
* **Authorized Name**: Enter the name of the person authorized to make the payment.
* **Authorized Title**: Enter the title of the person authorized to make the payment.
* **Authorized Date**: Enter the date authorized for the payment.
* **Authorized Date Type**: Select one or more date types (Lease, Rent, or Utility).
* **Authorized Note**: Add any note if needed.
* **Payment Terms**: Select any special payment terms if applicable.
