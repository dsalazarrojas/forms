# Slime Ingredient Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place a order for slime ingredients, including the selection of items, optional accessories, payment details, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available options for each field to ensure you select the correct items and accessories.
2. Select the items you wish to purchase on the Main Page.
3. Choose any accessories you'd like to add to your order on the Main Page.
4. Enter your payment details on the Payment page.
5. Provide your shipping information on the Shipping page.
6. Review your order total before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (Page 1, `title`/`text`/`required`): This is the main page where you can select the items you wish to purchase.
* **Ingredient** (Page 2, `ingredient`/`select_multiple`/`required`): Choose multiple items from the list of available options.
* **Accessory** (Page 3, `accessory`/`select_one`/`required`): Select a single accessory to add to your order.
* **Payment** (Page 4, `payment`/`text`/`required`): Enter your payment details, including any necessary card information.
* **Shipping** (Page 5, `shipping`/`text`/`required`): Provide your shipping information, including name, address, and any additional details.
* **Total** (Page 6, `total`/`number`/`required`): Review your total order amount before submitting the form. This field will automatically calculate based on your selections.

## Tips
* Double-check your selections and calculations before submitting your order.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with your order, contact our customer support team.
* Make sure to review and verify your shipping information to ensure timely delivery. 

Please note that this form is designed to be simple and easy to use. If you have any further questions, please don't hesitate to contact our support team for help.
