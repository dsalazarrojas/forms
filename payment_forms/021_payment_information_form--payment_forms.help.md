# Payment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential payment information from users, including details about the payment name, shipping address, mobile number, and payment mode, among others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "payment name" field with the name of the payment you are making.
2. Enter your shipping address in the "shipping address" field.
3. Provide your mobile number in the "mobile number" field.
4. Enter your Facebook URL (if applicable) in the "Facebook URL" field.
5. Select your preferred payment mode from the options provided in the "payment mode" field.
6. Choose the payment date from the calendar provided in the "payment date" field.
7. Select any relevant scheduling options from the list in the "scheduling" field.
8. Enter any additional payment mode details in the "payment mode details" field.
9. Enter the shipping city in the "shipping city" field.
10. Enter the shipping zip code in the "shipping zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **payment name** (text): Enter the name of the payment.
* **shipping address** (text): Enter your shipping address.
* **mobile number** (text): Enter your mobile number.
* **Facebook URL** (text): Enter your Facebook URL (if applicable).
* **payment mode** (select_one): Select your preferred payment mode from the options provided.
* **payment date** (date): Choose the payment date from the calendar provided.
* **scheduling** (select_multiple): Select any relevant scheduling options from the list.
* **payment mode details** (text): Enter any additional details about your payment mode.
* **shipping city** (text): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **shipping zip** (text): Enter the zip code of your shipping address.

Note that some fields are not required, and you should only fill out the fields that are relevant to your payment information.
