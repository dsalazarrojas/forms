# Business Card Exchange Kit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide customers through the process of ordering business card kits. It collects contact information, business card specifications, and payment details to ensure accurate fulfillment of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including full name, email address, phone number, and company name.
2. Specify the number of business cards you need.
3. Choose a design template that suits your business needs.
4. Select the primary color and any special finishes you want.
5. Provide your shipping address and select the shipping method.
6. Review and confirm your order total.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Enter your complete name to help us identify you correctly.
* **Email Address** (`3`, `email`, `required`): Enter your primary business email to ensure we can contact you for order updates.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number for any urgent matters.
* **Company Name** (`5`, `text`, `required`): Enter your company name to associate it with your order.
* **Job Title** (`6`, `text`, `required`): Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Number of Business Cards** (`8`, `number`, `required`): Specify how many business cards you need for your order.
* **Card Design Option** (`9`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose from available design templates to match your business identity.
* **Primary Card Color** (`10`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the color of your business cards from the available options.
* **Special Finishes** (`11`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select any special finishes you want, such as glossy or matte.
* **Shipping Address** (`13`, `text`, `required`): Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
* **City** (`14`, `text`, `required`): Enter the city name for the shipping address.
* **State or Province** (`15`, `text`, `required`): Enter the state or province code for the shipping address.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (`16`, `text`, `required`): Enter the zip or postal code for the shipping address.
* **Country** (`17`, `text`, `required`): Enter the country of delivery.
* **Shipping Method** (`18`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the shipping method that suits your needs.
* **Order Total** (`20`, `number`, `required`): Review the total amount in the currency specified.
* **Payment Method** (`21`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the payment method you prefer.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Review the order summary carefully before confirming the order.
- If you need to make any changes, contact our customer support.
