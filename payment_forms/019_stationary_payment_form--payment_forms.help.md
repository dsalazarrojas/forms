# Stationary Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stationary Payment Form is used for collecting payment information from customers or representatives of stores for transactions that take place in a fixed location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Store Name** and **Store Address** to identify the location of the transaction.
2. Fill in the **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Phone Number** fields with the relevant information.
3. Select the **Payment Method** (cash, credit card, or bank transfer) and enter the **Payment Amount**.
4. Enter the **Order ID** and **Date** and **Time** of the transaction if applicable.
5. Add any additional notes or information in the **Note** field.
6. Finally, click the **Submit** button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Store Name** (`id: 1`, text, required): Enter the name of the store where the transaction took place.
- **Store Address** (`id: 2`, text, required): Enter the address of the store where the transaction took place.
- **Customer Name** (`id: 3`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer who made the transaction.
- **Customer Email** (`id: 4`, email, required): Enter the email address of the customer who made the transaction.
- **Phone Number** (`id: 5`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the customer or store representative.
- **Payment Method** (`id: 6`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment used for the transaction (cash, credit card, or bank transfer).
- **Product Cost** (`id: 7`, number, required): Enter the cost of the product or service provided.
- **Payment Amount** (`id: 8`, number, required): Enter the amount paid by the customer.
- **Order ID** (`id: 9`, text, required): Enter the ID of the order if applicable.
- **Date** (`id: 10`, date, required): Enter the date of the transaction.
- **Time** (`id: 11`, time, required): Enter the time of the transaction.
- **Note** (`id: 12`, note, required): Add any additional notes or information about the transaction.
- **Submit** (`id: 13`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form.
- **Store Category** (`id: 14`, select_one, required): Select the category of the store (school, office, or home).

Note: The **Submit** button is used to submit the form, and all other fields are required to be completed before submitting the form. 

Tips: Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely to ensure proper processing of the transaction.
