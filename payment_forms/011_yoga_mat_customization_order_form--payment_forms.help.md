# Yoga Mat Customization Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place custom orders for yoga mats with specific details such as customer information, mat customization options, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
2. Select your preferred color for the custom mat in the "Color" field.
3. Choose the material for the custom mat in the "Material" field.
4. Select a pattern for the custom mat in the "Pattern" field.
5. Enter the quantity of custom mats you'd like to order in the "Quantity" field.
6. Set the price for the custom mat in the "Price" field.
7. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
8. Click the "Submit Form" button to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter your name and other relevant contact information to help us process your order correctly.
* **Mat Info**: Enter a brief description of the custom mat you'd like to order, if necessary.
* **Size**: Select the size of the custom mat you'd like to order (e.g., 1/2 inch, 3/4 inch, etc.).
* **Color**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Green
	+ Blue
	+ Red
* **Material**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Cotton
	+ Rubber
	+ PVC
* **Pattern**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Solid
	+ Striped
	+ Polka Dots
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of custom mats you'd like to order.
* **Price**: Enter the price for the custom mat.
* **Payment Method**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Submit Form**: Click this button to complete your order and submit your payment information.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a color, material, and pattern that match your preferences for a unique and personalized custom mat.
* Double-check your customer information and payment method to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
