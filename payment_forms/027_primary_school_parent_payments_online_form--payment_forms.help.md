# primary_school_parent_payments_online_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The primary_school_parent_payments_online_form is designed to collect basic information and payment details from primary school parents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Basic Information** field (page 1) with your child's name, grade level, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your **Contact Information** (page 1) accurately, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide **Payment Details** (page 1) about the payment you are making, including the amount and method of payment.
4. In the **Payment Method Additional Information** field (page 2), provide any additional context or notes about your payment.
5. The **Comments** (page 2) field is optional and can be used to add any comments or remarks about the payment.
6. In the **Submission Status** field (page 2), indicate the status of your submission, such as "Completed" or "Partial."
7. Finally, enter any **Submission Comments** (page 3) you would like to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Information** (`Basic Information`, `text`, required): Enter your child's basic information to ensure we have the correct details on file.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Enter your accurate contact information to enable us to contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required): Provide the payment amount and method to facilitate the payment process.
* **Payment Method Additional Information** (`Payment Method Additional Information`, `text`, optional): Add any additional context or notes about your payment method.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments or remarks about your payment or the payment process.
* **Submission Status** (`Submission Status`, `text`, optional): Indicate the status of your submission, such as "Completed" or "Partial."
* **Submission Comments** (`Submission Comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about your submission or the form completion process.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly and accurately. The form is designed to collect relevant information from parents, and any errors or omissions may cause delays or issues with the payment process. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
