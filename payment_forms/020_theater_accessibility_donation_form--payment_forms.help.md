# Theater Accessibility Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Accessibility Donation Form is used to collect donations from individuals or organizations to support making theater performances more accessible and inclusive for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you would like to make a donation ('Yes' or 'No').
2. Enter the amount of your donation, if you have chosen to donate.
3. Choose your preferred payment method ('Yes' or 'No', indicating whether you would like to pay by this method).
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you.
5. Enter your name to associate with your donation.
6. If you have a message for us, please enter it in the "Message" field.
7. Select a date that you would like to support (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation** (Yes, No, required: false):
	+ This option is to indicate whether you would like to make a donation to support theater accessibility initiatives.
* **Donation Amount** (Number, required: false):
	+ This field is only required if you select "Yes" for the "Donation" option. Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Payment Method** (Yes, No, required: false):
	+ This option is to choose the preferred payment method for your donation. If you select "Yes", you will be asked to choose the payment method in the next field.
* **Email** (Email, required: false):
	+ This field is for your email address so we can contact you for any further information or communication related to your donation.
* **Name** (Text, required: false):
	+ This is the field to enter your name to be associated with your donation.
* **Message** (Note, required: false):
	+ This is the field to enter any personal message or comment related to your donation.
* **Date** (Date, required: false):
	+ This field is to choose a date you would like to support a performance.
* **Time** (Time, required: false):
	+ This field is to choose a time for the performance you would like to support.
