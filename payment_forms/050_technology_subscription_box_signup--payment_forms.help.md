# Technology Subscription Box Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to sign up customers for a technology subscription box service. Fill out the form with your details to create or manage your subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Select your desired Plan Selection from the options provided.
4. If applicable, enter your Phone number.
5. For your subscription preferences, select your Delivery Frequency and Delivery Address.
6. If you have a payment method, enter your Card Number and CVV.
7. Finally, provide your Expiration Date, Expiration Month, and Expiration Year for your payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to receive subscription updates.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for subscription-related calls or notifications.
* **Plan Selection** (select_one, required): Choose your desired plan from the options provided.
* **Delivery Frequency** (select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred delivery frequency for your subscription box.
* **Delivery Address** (text, optional): Enter your delivery address if different from your billing address.
* **Card Number** (text, optional): Enter your credit card number for payment.
* **CVV** (text, optional): Enter your credit card security code (CVV) for payment verification.
* **Expiration Date** (date, optional): Enter the expiration date of your payment method.
* **Expiration Month** (select_one, optional): Select the expiration month of your payment method.
* **Expiration Year** (number, optional): Enter the expiration year of your payment method.
* **Month** (select_multiple, optional): Select the months you would like to receive your subscription box.
* **Year** (select_one, optional): Select the year you would like to start your subscription.

## Tips
* Ensure your email address is accurate to receive updates and notifications.
* Enter your phone number if you would like to receive subscription-related calls or notifications.
* Check your plan selection to ensure it aligns with your subscription preferences.
* Double-check your delivery frequency and address to ensure correct delivery. 
* Enter accurate payment details for successful transactions.
