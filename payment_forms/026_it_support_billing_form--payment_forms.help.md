# it_support_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect billing information for IT support services. It is designed to facilitate the payment process and provide a record of customer transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your billing information, including your name, address, and contact number.
2. Choose the payment method you would like to use (credit card, bank transfer, or cash).
3. Select the type of service you are requesting support for (server, network, or cloud).
4. Enter the amount you would like to pay.
5. Review and agree to the billing terms and conditions.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes for the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **billing_info**: Enter your billing information, such as your name, address, and contact number.
* **payment_method**: Choose your preferred payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or cash).
* **customer_info**: Provide information about the customer, including their name, address, and contact number.
* **service_type**: Select the type of service you are requesting support for (server, network, or cloud).
* **amount**: Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **billing_terms**: Review and agree to the billing terms and conditions.
* **payment_terms**: Enter any additional payment terms or instructions.
* **payment_details**: Provide any additional payment details.
* **customer_name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **customer_address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **customer_note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the customer.
* **payment_status**: Select the status of the payment (pending or paid).

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form.
