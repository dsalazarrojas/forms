## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Transfer Information
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date): Enter the date of the transfer in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Transfer Time** (`transfer_time`, time): Enter the time of the transfer in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Transfer Number or Reference ID** (`transfer_number`, text): Enter the unique ID or number assigned to the transfer.
* **Transfer Amount** (`transfer_amount`, number): Enter the exact amount of cash transferred.
* **Currency** (`currency`, text): Select the currency used for the transfer.

### Sender Information
* **Sender Name** (`sender_name`, text): Enter the name of the person sending the cash.
* **Sender Phone Number** (`sender_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the sender (optional).
* **Sender Department or Company** (`sender_department`, text): Enter the department or company of the sender (optional).

### Receiver Information
* **Receiver Name** (`receiver_name`, text): Enter the name of the person receiving the cash.
* **Receiver Phone Number** (`receiver_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the receiver (optional).
* **Receiver Department or Company** (`receiver_department`, text): Enter the department or company of the receiver (optional).

### Transfer Details
* **Purpose of Transfer** (`transfer_purpose`, text): Select the reason for the transfer.
* **Type of Transfer** (`transfer_type`, select_one): Select the type of transfer (Cash Advance, Reimbursement, Loan, Payment, Deposit, Withdrawal, Other).
* **Method of Transfer** (`method_of_transfer`, select_one): Select the method of transfer (Hand-to-hand, Bank Transfer, Courier, Wire Transfer, Check, Other).

### Condition and Status
* **Condition of Cash** (`cash_condition`, select_one): Select the condition of the cash (New or Good Condition, Worn but Usable, Damaged, Mixed Denominations, Other).
* **Receipt Given to Receiver?** (`receipt_given`, select_one): Select whether a receipt was given to the receiver (Yes, No, Verbal Acknowledgment Only).

### Verification
* **Verified By (Independent Party)** (`verified_by`, text): Enter the name of the witness or verifier.
