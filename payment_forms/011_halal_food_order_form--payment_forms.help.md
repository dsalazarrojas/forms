# Halal Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halal Food Order Form is designed for customers to provide information about their halal food preferences and payment methods for smooth order processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Customer Info** in the first field.
2. Specify your **Food Preferences** in the second field.
3. Mention any **Special Requests** you may have in the third field.
4. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options in the fourth field.
5. Confirm your order by typing it in the **Submit Order** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (Required)**: Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information for the order.
* **Food Preferences**: Specify your halal food preferences, including any dietary restrictions or special requests.
* **Special Requests (Optional)**: If you have any additional requests or instructions for your order, type them here.
* **Payment Info (Required)**: Select your preferred payment method from the available options (Cash, Bank Transfer, Card, Online Payment).
* **Submit Order (Required)**: Confirm your order by typing it in this field, ensuring it matches your previous entries for accuracy.
