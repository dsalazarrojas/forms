# Alcohol Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Alcohol Delivery Form" collects information necessary for a secure and efficient alcohol delivery order. This form ensures that customers provide accurate contact information, order details, and payment methods while confirming their age and agreement to delivery terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your legal name and confirm that you are 21 or older.
2.  Provide your date of birth for age verification.
3.  Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and any other communications from us.
4.  Enter a phone number where we can contact you for delivery confirmation and potential updates.
5.  Enter your delivery address to ensure accurate delivery of your order.
6.  Enter any special delivery instructions if needed.
7.  Select the date you prefer for delivery.
8.  Choose your preferred delivery time window from our available options.
9.  Select any alcohol products you wish to order. Each selection will be charged separately.
10.  Enter any special requests for specific brands or items not listed.
11.  Calculate and enter the subtotal (total before fees and tax).
12.  Enter the delivery fee, which is based on the distance to your location.
13.  Enter the service fee for processing your order.
14.  If you wish, enter a tip for your delivery driver.
15.  Enter the final total (subtotal + delivery fee + service fee + tip).
16.  Select a payment method from our available options.
17.  Confirm that you have a valid ID available for delivery.
18.  Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.
19.  If applicable, enter any promotional code for a discount.
20.  Review your order carefully and ensure all information is accurate before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Full Name** (`customer_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name for age verification purposes.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to confirm you are 21 or older.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for order confirmation and any communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number for delivery confirmation and updates.
*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter your full address for accurate delivery.
*   **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions for your delivery.
*   **Preferred Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select the date you prefer for delivery.
*   **Preferred Time Window** (`delivery_time_window`, select_one, required): Select your preferred delivery time from our options.
*   **Beer Selection** (`beer_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select beer options you wish to order. Each selection will be charged separately.
*   **Wine Selection** (`wine_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select wine options you wish to order. Each selection will be charged separately.
*   **Spirits Selection** (`spirits_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select spirits options you wish to order. Each selection will be charged separately.
*   **Mixer Selection** (`mixer_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select mixer options you wish to add. Each selection will be charged separately.
*   **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests for brands or items not listed.
*   **Order Subtotal** (`subtotal`, number, required): Calculate and enter the total before fees.
*   **Delivery Fee** (`delivery_fee`, number, required): Enter the delivery fee based on distance.
*   **Service Fee** (`service_fee`, number, required): Enter the service processing fee.
*   **Tip Amount** (`tip_amount`, number, optional): If desired, enter a tip for your delivery driver.
*   **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, required): Enter the final total.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
*   **Age Verification** (`age_verification`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are 21 or older.
*   **Valid ID Available** (`id_present`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will present a valid ID at delivery.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to our delivery terms.
*   **Promotional Code** (`promotional_code`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any promotional code for a discount.

## Tips

*   Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review your order carefully before submitting.
*   If you have any special requests, please include them so we can best fulfill your needs.
*   For accurate delivery and processing, please confirm your age, address, and ID availability.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
