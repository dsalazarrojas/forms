# EFT Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EFT Authorization Form is a document used to authorize electronic funds transfers between a business and its bank. It ensures the legitimacy and authenticity of the business's identity and financial information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Eft Authorization Form**: Fill in this field with any comments or information you'd like to provide in conjunction with the authorization.
2.  **Business Details**:
    *   **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
    *   **Business Address**: Enter your business's address.
    *   **Business Phone**: Enter your business's phone number.
    *   **Business Email**: Enter your business's email address.
    *   **Business Fax**: Enter your business's fax number (if applicable).
    *   **Business License**: Enter your business's license or registration number.
    *   **Business Registration**: Enter your business's registration number.
    *   **Business IBAN**: Enter your business's IBAN (International Bank Account Number).
3.  **Authorization Details**:
    *   **Authorization Details**: Enter the authorization details as required by your bank.
4.  **Authorized Amount**:
    *   **Authorized Amount**: Enter the authorized amount for the electronic funds transfer.
5.  **Authorized Currency**:
    *   **Authorized Currency**: Enter the authorized currency for the electronic funds transfer.
6.  **Authorized Party**:
    *   **Authorized Party**: Select the party authorized for the electronic funds transfer.
7.  **Authorized By**:
    *   **Authorized By**: Select the person authorized for the electronic funds transfer.
8.  **Authorized Date**:
    *   **Authorized Date**: Enter the date of authorization for the electronic funds transfer.
9.  **Authorized Time**:
    *   **Authorized Time**: Enter the time of authorization for the electronic funds transfer.
10. **Notes**:
    *   **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
11.  **Email**:
    *   **Email**: Enter your email address.
12. **Phone**:
    *   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
13. **Comments**:
    *   **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
14. **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
15. **Business Address**: Enter your business's address.
16. **Business Phone**: Enter your business's phone number.
17. **Business Email**: Enter your business's email address.
18. **Business Fax**: Enter your business's fax number (if applicable).
19. **Business License**: Enter your business's license or registration number.
20. **Business Registration**: Enter your business's registration number.
21. **Business IBAN**: Enter your business's IBAN (International Bank Account Number).
22. **Bank Name**: Enter your bank's name.
23. **Bank Branch**: Enter your bank's branch name.
24. **Bank AB**: Enter your bank's AB (Bank Account Number).
25. **Bank IBAN**: Enter your bank's IBAN (International Bank Account Number).

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check all the information provided to ensure accuracy and authenticity.
*   Use this form for legitimate business purposes only, as it contains sensitive business and financial information.
*   Review the form carefully to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
*   Ensure the authorized party is aware of the authorization and the terms thereof.
