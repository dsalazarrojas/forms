# Digital Payment Record Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Payment Record Consent Form is used to record and confirm digital payment transactions. It is designed to collect user input, contact details, and other relevant information associated with the payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your User Input information.
2. Select your Contact Details (Email, Phone, or Other).
3. Enter your Authorization information.
4. Enter the Date and Time of the Transaction.
5. Indicate your Digital Payment Record Consent.
6. Optionally, add any additional Payment Details.
7. Add any relevant Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `number`, required): Enter your unique identifier (ID) number.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the way you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, `text`, required): Enter your authorization information.
* **Date of Transaction** (`date_of_transaction`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the transaction in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Transaction** (`time_of_transaction`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the transaction in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Digital Payment Record Consent** (`digital_payment_record_consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your consent for the digital payment record.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant notes about the transaction.

## Tips

- Always ensure that you have not submitted this form for the same transaction ID.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or contact the administrator.
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to avoid any errors or disputes.
