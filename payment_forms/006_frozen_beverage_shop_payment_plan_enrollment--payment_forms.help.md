## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Customer Information" page.
2.  Fill out the "Customer Information" page with your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
3.  Click on the "Plan Information" page.
4.  Enter the plan you are interested in and any additional details.
5.  Click on the "Billing Information" page and enter your billing details.
6.  Read the "Payment Terms" page to understand the payment terms and conditions.
7.  Click on the "Agreement" page and select "I Agree" if you agree to the terms and conditions.
8.  Review and submit the form.
