# Plant Based Milk Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers about their plant-based milk orders. It helps us determine if a customer wants to place an order and, if so, captures details about the order such as quantity, delivery preferences, and customer contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to place an order.
2. Enter the quantity of plant-based milk you would like to order.
3. Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate your delivery preference.
4. Enter your name.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Add any order notes or special requests.
8. (Optional) Enter the total order cost, if you have a specific price in mind.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Type** (`order_type`): Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to place an order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the amount of plant-based milk you would like to order.
* **Delivery Option** (`delivery_option`): Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate your delivery preference.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name so we can contact you.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`): Add any special requests or notes about your order.
* **Total Order Cost** (`total_order_cost`): (Optional) Enter the total cost of your order if you have a specific price in mind.
