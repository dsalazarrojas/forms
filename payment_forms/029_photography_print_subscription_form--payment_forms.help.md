# Photography Print Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to subscribe to a photography service. It captures the necessary information to fulfill their subscription requests and ensure business operations run smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Select the correct options for each field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
4. Enter your email address and phone number in the correct format (e.g., `email@example.com` for email and `123-456-7890` for phone).
5. Review all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photography Print Subscription** (1, text, required/optional):
	+ This field is a brief description of your photography print subscription request.
* **Print Size** (2, select_one, required/true):
	+ Select "Yes" if you want the photography print in a larger size, or "No" if you prefer the standard size.
* **Paper Type** (3, select_one, required/true):
	+ Choose the type of paper you would like for the print (e.g., matte, glossy, or others).
* **Print Frequency** (4, select_multiple, required/true):
	+ Select the frequency of printings you would like (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
* **Email** (5, email, required/false):
	+ Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (6, text, required/false):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Note** (7, note, required/false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about your subscription request.
* **First Name** (8, text, required/true):
	+ Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (9, text, required/true):
	+ Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Address** (10, text, required/false):
	+ Enter your address for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **City** (11, text, required/false):
	+ Enter your city for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **State** (12, text, required/false):
	+ Enter your state for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **Zip** (13, text, required/false):
	+ Enter your zip code for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **Order Date** (14, date, required/true):
	+ Enter the date you would like to fulfill the subscription order.
* **Order Time** (15, time, required/false):
	+ Enter the time you would like the order to be fulfilled.
* **Order Note** (16, note, required/false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Note 2** (17, note, required/false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Order Note 3** (18, note, required/false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Payment Method** (19, select_one, required/true):
	+ Select the payment method you prefer (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
* **Payment Amount** (20, number, required/true):
	+ Enter the payment amount for the subscription.
* **Payment Frequency** (21, select_one, required/true):
	+ Select the payment frequency (e.g., monthly, quarterly).
* **Payment Note** (22, note, required/false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about your payment.
* **First Page Note** (23, note, required/false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about your subscription order.
