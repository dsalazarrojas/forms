# Racing Bikes Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Racing Bikes Pre Order Form is used to gather information for pre-ordering bikes. This form is used to select and customize bike preferences, payment method, and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a bike type from the "Select a bike" field. You can choose from Road Bike, Mountain Bike, or Hybrid Bike.
2. If you have any customization requests, enter them in the "Customization request" field.
3. Choose your preferred delivery date from the "Preferred delivery date" field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment method" field. You can choose from Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
7. Enter any additional information about the order in the "Additional info" field.
8. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" from the "Order confirmation" field.
9. Specify your delivery location from the "Delivery location" field.
10. Confirm your payment by selecting "Yes" from the "Payment confirmation" field.
11. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special request" field.
12. Enter your order number in the "Order number" field.
13. Enter the date of the order in the "Order date" field.
14. Select the payment status of the order from the "Payment status" field. You can choose from Paid or Pending.
15. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment amount" field.
16. Select the bike color from the "Bike color" field. You can choose from Black, Red, or Blue.
17. Select the bike size from the "Bike size" field. You can choose from Small, Medium, or Large.
18. Select the type of bike from the "Bike type" field. You can choose from Road Bike, Mountain Bike, or Hybrid Bike.
19. Select the delivery status of the order from the "Delivery status" field. You can choose from Delivered or Pending.
20. Enter any comments about the delivery in the "Comments" field.
21. Enter any comments about the delivery in the "Delivery comments" field.
22. Enter any order notes in the "Order note" field.
23. Enter any additional comments about the form submission in the "Form submission comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a bike**: This field allows you to choose the type of bike you are interested in. Please select one or more bikes that you are interested in. 
* **Customization request**: If you have any customization requests, enter them here. This could be any special features you would like on your bike such as a customized paint job or special handlebars. 
* **Preferred delivery date**: This is the date you would like to receive your bike. Please enter the date in the format dd/mm/yyyy (e.g. 24/02/2023). 
* **Payment method**: This is how you would like to pay for your bike. You can choose from Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer. 
* **Email address**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your order. 
* **Phone number**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order. 
* **Additional info**: If you have any additional information about your order, enter it here. 
* **Order confirmation**: Confirm that you have submitted this form to place your order. 
* **Delivery location**: Please select where you would like to receive your bike. You can choose from Store, Home, or Office. 
* **Payment confirmation**: Confirm your payment method. 
* **Special request**: If you have any special requests, enter them here. 
* **Order number**: Please enter your order number so we can track your order. 
* **Order date**: Please enter the date of your order so we can track when your order was placed. 
* **Payment status**: Select the status of your payment. You can choose from Paid or Pending. 
* **Payment amount**: Please enter the amount you paid for your bike. 
* **Bike color**: Please select the color of your bike. You can choose from Black, Red, or Blue. 
* **Bike size**: Please select the size of your bike. You can choose from Small, Medium, or Large. 
* **Bike type**: Please select the type of bike you are interested in. You can choose from Road Bike, Mountain Bike, or Hybrid Bike. 
* **Delivery status**: Select the status of your delivery. You can choose from Delivered or Pending. 
* **Comments**: If you have any comments about your delivery, enter them here. 
* **Delivery comments**: If you have any comments about your delivery, enter them here. 
* **Order note**: Please enter any notes about your order here. 
* **Form submission comments**: If you have any comments about this form submission, enter them here. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth and efficient order process.
- If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Please be patient and we will contact you as soon as your order is ready.
