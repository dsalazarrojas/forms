# private_jet_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book private jet travel services. It gathers information about the travel details, passenger information, and payment terms to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the travel details, including the departure city, arrival city, travel dates, and departure time.
2. Provide the passenger's name and any additional special requirements.
3. Choose the travel class and class type.
4. Select the payment method and terms.
5. Provide any relevant payment status and type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **departure_city** (`departure_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of departure.
* **arrival_city** (`arrival_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of arrival.
* **travel_dates** (`travel_dates`, `date`, required): Enter the travel dates.
* **travel_class** (`travel_class`, `select_one`, required): Choose the travel class type.
* **travel_class_type** (`travel_class_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the specific travel class type.
* **payment_terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required): Provide the payment terms.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the payment method.
* **payment_details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required): Enter the payment details.
* **travel_documents** (`travel_documents`, `number`, required): Enter the number of travel documents.
* **travel_documents_type** (`travel_documents_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of travel documents.
* **special_requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, required): Enter any special requirements.
* **special_requirements_type** (`special_requirements_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of special requirements.
