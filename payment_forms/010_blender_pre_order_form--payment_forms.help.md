# Blender Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to allow customers to pre-order a Blender appliance before its general release. By filling out this form, customers can secure their place in the queue and choose their preferred options for their pre-order.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start the pre-order process.
2.  Enter your First Name and Last Name to identify yourself.
3.  Enter your Email Address to receive updates on your pre-order status.
4.  Enter your Phone Number for communication purposes.
5.  Provide your Shipping Address for delivery of the pre-ordered product.
6.  Enter your City and State or Province for correct shipping.
7.  Enter your ZIP or Postal Code for accurate shipping.
8.  Select your Country of residence.
9.  Choose your preferred Blender Model from the available options.
10. Choose your preferred Color for the pre-ordered Blender.
11. Enter the Number of Units you wish to pre-order.
12. Select any Optional Accessories you would like to include with your pre-order.
13. (Optional) Enter the Price Per Unit if you are aware of it for your pre-order.
14. (Optional) Enter the Total Pre-Order Amount if you are aware of it for your pre-order.
15. (Optional) Enter the Deposit Amount Required for your pre-order.
16. Enter the Expected Delivery Date for your pre-order.
17. Select your preferred Payment Method from the available options.
18. Agree to the Pre-Order Terms and Conditions.
19. Finally, confirm your Pre-Order Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`customer_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
*   **Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
*   **Email Address** (`customer_email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for you to receive updates on your pre-order status.
*   **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, Text, Required): Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
*   **City** (`shipping_city`, Text, Required): Enter the city where you would like to receive your pre-ordered Blender.
*   **State or Province** (`shipping_state`, Text, Required): Select your state or province of residence for correct shipping.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (`shipping_zip`, Text, Required): Enter your ZIP or postal code for accurate shipping.
*   **Country** (`shipping_country`, Text, Required): Enter your country of residence.
*   **Blender Model** (`blender_model`, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred Blender model from the available options (Professional Pro 3000, Home Chef 2000, Smoothie Master 1500, Compact Blend 800).
*   **Preferred Color** (`blender_color`, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred color for the pre-ordered Blender (Black, White, Stainless Steel, Red, Blue).
*   **Number of Units** (`quantity`, Number, Required): Enter the number of units you wish to pre-order.
*   **Optional Accessories** (`accessories`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any optional accessories you would like to include with your pre-order (Extra container, Recipe book, Travel mug kit, Cleaning brush set, Extended warranty).
*   **Price Per Unit** (`unit_price`, Number, Optional): Enter the price per unit for your pre-order if you are aware of it.
*   **Total Pre-Order Amount** (`total_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the total pre-order amount if you are aware of it.
*   **Deposit Amount Required** (`deposit_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the deposit amount required for your pre-order.
*   **Expected Delivery Date** (`expected_delivery`, Date, Optional): Enter the expected delivery date for your pre-order.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Apple Pay).
*   **I accept the pre-order terms and conditions** (`terms_accepted`, Select One, Required): Agree to the pre-order terms and conditions.
*   **Pre-Order Date** (`preorder_date`, Date, Required): Enter your pre-order date.

## Tips

*   Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
*   Review the pre-order terms and conditions carefully before agreeing.
*   Ensure you have entered the correct payment method and details.
*   Keep an eye on your email for updates on your pre-order status.
