# Customer Bill Pay - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Bill Pay" form is used for customers to pay their bills. This form collects information from the customer to process a payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the "Biller Name" in the "Biller Name" field. This is the name of the person or company the customer is paying.
2. Enter the "Bill Amount" in the "Bill Amount" field. This is the amount the customer needs to pay.
3. Select the "Payment Method" by choosing an option from the dropdown menu. This determines how the payment will be made (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
4. Enter the "Payment Date" in the "Payment Date" field. This is the date the payment is due.
5. Enter the "Account Number" in the "Account Number" field. This is the account number associated with the bill.
6. Enter the "Email" in the "Email" field. This is the email address of the customer.
7. Enter the "Phone" in the "Phone" field. This is the phone number of the customer.
8. Enter the "Address" in the "Address" field. This is the address of the customer.
9. Enter the "City" in the "City" field. This is the city of the customer's address.
10. Select the "State" by choosing an option from the dropdown menu. This determines the state where the customer lives.
11. Enter the "Zip" in the "Zip" field. This is the zip code of the customer's address.
12. Enter the "Country" in the "Country" field. This is the country of the customer's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Biller Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person or company the customer is paying.
* **Bill Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount the customer needs to pay.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Select how the payment will be made (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Payment Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the payment is due.
* **Account Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the account number associated with the bill.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the customer.
* **City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city of the customer's address.
* **State** (Select One, Optional): Select the state where the customer lives.
* **Zip** (Text, Optional): Enter the zip code of the customer's address.
* **Country** (Text, Optional): Enter the country of the customer's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct payment method and account number to ensure timely processing of the payment.
* Enter the correct email address and phone number to contact the customer in case of any issues.
* Double-check the address and city to ensure the payment is sent to the correct location.
* If the payment method is "Other", please provide a description of the payment method in the "Other" field.
