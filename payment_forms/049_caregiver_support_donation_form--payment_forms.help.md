# Caregiver Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations in support of caregivers, providing an opportunity for individuals and organizations to contribute to their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, fill in your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of donation you would like to make (one-time or recurring).
3. Choose how much you would like to donate, selecting from pre-set amounts or specifying an other amount if necessary.
4. Decide on your preferred method of payment.
5. If you have selected a recurring donation, specify how long you would like to commit to this arrangement.
6. If applicable, choose whether you would like a tax-deductible receipt.
7. Select how you would like to be recognized publicly for your donation.
8. Decide whether you would like to keep your donation anonymous.
9. Choose how you would like to pay (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, etc.).
10. Fill in your billing address, including city, state or province, and postal code.
11. Select whether you would like to receive updates from us.
12. If your employer offers a matching gift program, let us know.
13. Provide any additional comments or information about your donation.
14. Finally, confirm that you accept the terms and conditions of the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name as you would like to be recognized.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive donation receipt and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, `text`, optional): Include your area code and phone number for easy contact.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Type of Donation** (`donation_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose one-time or recurring donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `select_one`, required): Select a pre-set amount or enter an other amount if necessary.
* **Other Donation Amount** (`donation_amount_other`, `number`, optional): If you chose Other, enter your donation amount.
* **Monthly Recurring Donation Amount** (`monthly_recurring`, `number`, optional): If you selected a recurring donation, enter the amount per month.
* **What Should Donation Support?** (`donation_focus`, `select_one`, required): Choose the area of support for your donation.
* **Public Recognition Preference** (`caregiver_recognition`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you would like to be recognized for your donation.
* **Make This an Anonymous Donation** (`anonymous_donation`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you would like to keep your donation private.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred method of payment.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your billing address for payment purposes.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city for your billing address.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province for your billing address.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code for your billing address.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter the country for your billing address.
* **Monthly Commitment Duration** (`monthly_commitment`, `select_one`, optional): If you selected a recurring donation, choose the duration of your commitment.
* **Tax Deductible Donation Requested** (`tax_deductible`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, choose whether you would like a tax-deductible receipt.
* **Subscribe to Updates** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would like to receive updates from us.
* **Employer Matching Gift Information** (`matching_gift`, `select_one`, optional): If your employer offers a matching gift program, let us know.
* **Donation Frequency Notes** (`donation_frequency`, `text`, optional): Add any special instructions for your donation.
* **Other Ways to Support** (`additional_support`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose from any additional ways you would like to support.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Add any special requests or information about your donation.
* **I Accept the Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions of the donation.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter all fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the explanation above for clarification.
* If you are making an anonymous donation, please be aware that your information will not be shared publicly.
