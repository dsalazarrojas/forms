# season_tickets_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for pre-ordering season tickets. Fill it out to reserve your spot for the upcoming season.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Reservation ID**: Enter a unique reservation ID, if provided by the team.
2. **Customer Name**: Enter the full name of the customer.
3. **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
4. **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number (optional).
5. **Ticket Type**: Select the type of ticket you're interested in:
	* **Season Ticket**: A full season ticket package.
	* **Half-Season Ticket**: A half season ticket package.
	* **Single Game Ticket**: A ticket for a single game.
6. **Seat Number**: Select the seat number(s) you'd like to reserve (multiple seats can be chosen).
7. **Game Date**: Choose the date of the game you'd like to attend.
8. **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the ticket(s) (will be calculated automatically).
9. **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method:
	* **Credit Card**: Pay with a credit card.
	* **PayPal**: Pay through PayPal.
	* **Bank Transfer**: Pay via bank transfer.
10. **Customer Signature**: Provide your signature to confirm the order.
11. **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team for which you're pre-ordering tickets.
12. **Venue**: Enter the venue where the game will be held.
13. **Season Ticket Price**: Enter the price of the season ticket.
14. **Game Time**: Enter the time of the game (optional).
15. **Ticket Type Price**: Enter the price of the ticket type (optional).
16. **Season Ticket Total**: Enter the total price of the season ticket (will be calculated automatically).
17. **Season Ticket Discount**: Enter the discount amount for the season ticket (optional).
18. **Discount Type**: Choose the type of discount:
	* **Percentage**: A percentage-based discount.
	* **Flat**: A flat-rate discount.
	* **Tiered**: A tiered discount (price levels based on quantity).
19. **Discount Amount**: Enter the discount amount.
20. **Season Ticket Note**: Add any additional notes about the season ticket.
21. **Customer Note**: Add any additional notes about the customer's order.
22. **Season Ticket Status**: Select the status of the order:
	* **Unpaid**: The order is not yet paid.
	* **Paid**: The order is paid in full.
23. **Team Logo**: Enter the team's logo (optional).

Note: Fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out.
