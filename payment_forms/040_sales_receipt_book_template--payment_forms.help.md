# Sales Receipt Book Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Receipt Book Template is a form used to record sales transactions, document sales activity, and generate reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields. While most fields are not required, it's best to provide as much information as possible for accurate record-keeping.
2. Enter the company name and address in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide the date of the sales transaction.
4. Enter the customer's name and any relevant details.
5. Enter the amount received from the customer.
6. Specify the reason for the payment, e.g., "Sales of product A" or "Service charge for B".
7. Enter the receipt number, if applicable.
8. Enter the name of the person receiving the payment, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your company's location.
* **Company Contact Number** (`company_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your company's contact phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the sales transaction. This field is a date picker field for easy input.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or business making the purchase.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount received from the customer. This field is a numeric input field.
* **Reason for Payment** (`reason_for_payment`, text, optional): Specify the reason for the payment, e.g., "Sales of product A" or "Service charge for B".
* **Receipt Number** (`receipt_number`, text, optional): Enter the receipt number, if applicable.
* **Receiver Name** (`receiver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person receiving the payment, if applicable.
