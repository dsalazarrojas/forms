# Diagnostic Imaging Refund Status Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diagnostic Imaging Refund Status Check form is used to track and update the status of refunds for diagnostic imaging services provided to patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility that provided the imaging services to the patient.
2. **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient who received the imaging services.
3. **Facility ID**: Enter the ID of the facility that provided the imaging services to the patient.
4. **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the refund was submitted.
5. **Imaging Type**: Select the type of imaging service provided to the patient (e.g., MRI, CT, etc.).
6. **Refund Status**: Select the current status of the refund (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied).
7. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the refund status.
8. **Patient ID**: Enter the ID of the patient who received the imaging services (optional).
9. **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date the follow-up was completed (optional).
10. **Follow-up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Pending).
11. **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the follow-up status (optional).
12. **Facility Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person at the facility (optional).
13. **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility (optional).
14. **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility (optional).
15. **Facility Email**: Enter the email address of the facility (optional).
16. **Facility Fax**: Enter the fax number of the facility (optional).
17. **Facility Website**: Enter the website of the facility (optional).
18. **Billing Contact Name**: Enter the name of the billing contact person at the facility (optional).
19. **Billing Address**: Enter the address of the billing contact (optional).
20. **Billing Phone**: Enter the phone number of the billing contact (optional).
21. **Billing Email**: Enter the email address of the billing contact (optional).
22. **Billing Fax**: Enter the fax number of the billing contact (optional).
23. **Billing Website**: Enter the website of the billing contact (optional).
24. **Insurance Information**: Enter any additional information about the insurance coverage (optional).
25. **Additional Info**: Enter any additional comments or information about the patient or refund (optional).
