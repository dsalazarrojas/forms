# Designer Chair Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Designer Chair Pre Order Form is a form designed to collect information from customers who want to pre-order a designer chair. This form will help our team process the order accurately and ensure that customers receive their chair on the desired delivery date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred delivery option(s) from the dropdown list.
2. Choose the model of chair you'd like to order.
3. If you have a preferred delivery time, select it from the available options. (Note: If you don't select a delivery time, our default delivery time will be used.)
4. Enter a deposit (if applicable).
5. Provide your full name.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Enter your address.
9. Add any additional order notes (if needed).
10. Select your preferred payment option(s).
11. Choose a desired delivery date.
12. Select a delivery time window for your chair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Preferences**: 
  - Select the delivery method that suits you best from the dropdown list. (e.g., delivery at home, pick-up at store, etc.)

* **Chair Model**: 
  - Choose the model of chair you'd like to order from the list of available options.

* **Delivery Time**: 
  - Select a delivery time that suits you best from the available options. (Note: If you don't select a delivery time, our default delivery time will be used.)

* **Deposit**: 
  - Enter the deposit amount (if applicable).

* **Full Name**: 
  - Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

* **Email**: 
  - Enter your email address that we can use to contact you regarding your order.

* **Phone**: 
  - Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you regarding your order.

* **Address**: 
  - Enter your address where you'd like your chair to be delivered.

* **Order Notes**: 
  - Add any additional notes about your order, such as special requests or instructions.

* **Payment**: 
  - Select the payment option that suits you best from the list of available options.

* **Delivery Date**: 
  - Choose a desired delivery date for your chair.

* **Delivery Time Window**: 
  - Select a delivery time window for your chair to be delivered within.
