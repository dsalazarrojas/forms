# Performer Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about payments made by performers, which is crucial for accounting and financial management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `event_organizer` field with your event organizer details.
2. Select the payment method from the available options (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex).
3. Choose the payment status (Active, Inactive, Paid, Not Paid, or Partially Paid).
4. If necessary, provide additional payment details in the `payment_status_note` field.
5. Indicate whether payment method type is 'Yes' or 'No' in the `payment_method_type` field.
6. Confirm or deny payment method in the `payment_method` field.
7. Enter the payment amount in the `payment_amount` field.
8. Use the `payment_details` field to provide any additional information about the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_organizer**: Enter the name or ID of the event organizer.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method used for this transaction (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex).
* **payment_status**: Choose the status of the payment (Active, Inactive, Paid, Not Paid, or Partially Paid).
* **payment_status_note**: Provide any additional information about the payment status.
* **payment_date**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **payment_amount**: Enter the amount paid.
* **payment_details**: Provide any additional details about the payment.
* **payment_status_note**: This field is for additional comments or notes about the payment status.
* **payment_method_type**: Indicate whether the payment method type is 'Yes' or 'No' (not entirely clear what this field means; assume it's a boolean indicating payment method type).
* **payment_method**: Confirm or deny the payment method (not clear how this field differs from `payment_method_type`; assume it's a simple confirmation of payment method).
* **payment_amount**: Enter the payment amount.
* **payment_details**: Provide any additional details about the payment.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurately and thoroughly filled out to maintain accurate financial records.
* Use the `payment_details` field for any additional information that doesn't fit in other fields.
* If a payment status changes (e.g., from 'Active' to 'Paid'), update the `payment_status` field accordingly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the event organizer or the relevant documentation for guidance.
