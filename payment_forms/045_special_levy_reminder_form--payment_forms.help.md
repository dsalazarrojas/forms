# Special Levy Reminder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Levy Reminder Form is used to create and manage special levy reminder records for properties with outstanding levies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Property Name" field with the name of the property with the outstanding levy.
2. Enter the "Notice Date" in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Select the "Notice Period" from the available options.
4. Fill in the "Resident Response" field with the response from the resident.
5. Enter the "Acknowledgement Date" in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Select the "Acknowledgement Status" from the available options.
7. Select the "Paid Status" from the available options.
8. Enter the "Total Due" amount.
9. Enter the "Outstanding Balance" amount.
10. Select the "Paid Status" from the available options.
11. Select the "Payment Status" from the available options.
12. Select the "Payment Method" from the available options.
13. Enter the "Payment Date" in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
14. Enter the "Paid Amount" amount.
15. Enter the "Contact Name".
16. Enter the "Contact Email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the property with the outstanding levy.
- **Notice Date** (`notice_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the notice was sent to the resident.
- **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, required): Select the period of the notice.
- **Resident Response** (`resident_response`, text, required): Record the response from the resident regarding the levy.
- **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the acknowledgement was received from the resident.
- **Acknowledgement Status** (`acknowledgement_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the acknowledgement (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid, Pending).
- **Paid Status** (`paid_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the payment (True, False).
- **Total Due** (`total_due`, number, required): Enter the total amount due for the levy.
- **Outstanding Balance** (`outstanding_balance`, number, required): Enter the outstanding balance of the levy.
- **Paid Status** (`paid_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the payment (True, False).
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the payment (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid, Pending).
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment (Bank, Check, Other).
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the payment was made.
- **Paid Amount** (`paid_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount paid for the levy.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient tracking and management of special levies.
- Review and verify the information provided before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
