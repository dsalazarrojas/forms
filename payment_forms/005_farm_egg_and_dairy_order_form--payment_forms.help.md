# Farm Egg And Dairy Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Egg And Dairy Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for farm egg and dairy orders. This form is used to gather the necessary details to process the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information on the first page, including your name and contact information.
2. Proceed to the next page where you can enter your order details, including farm products and any additional information required.
3. On the final page, enter your payment information and confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info**: Enter your customer name and other relevant details to help us process your order.
  * Type in your name and any other contact information you'd like to provide.
- **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information to reach out to us for any further assistance.
  * Provide your name, phone number, and any other contact details you'd like.
- **Order Info**: Enter the details of your order, including any farm products you're interested in.
  * Provide a clear and detailed description of your order requirements.
- **Farm Products**: Select the farm products you'd like to order.
  * Choose the farm products you'd like to purchase.
- **Payment Info**: Enter your payment information to complete the transaction.
  * Enter your payment method and any relevant payment details.
- **Order Confirmation**: Confirm that the order details are accurate before submitting.
  * Review the order details to ensure everything is correct.
- **Customer Details**: Enter any additional customer details, if necessary.
  * Provide any additional information that may be required for your order.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  * Enter your email address to receive any updates or notifications.
- **Phone Number**: Indicate whether you want to receive calls or messages from us.
  * Select 'Yes' if you're available to receive calls or messages.
- **Order Total**: Enter the total amount of your order.
  * Enter the total amount of your purchase.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to review your order details carefully before submitting.
- Enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
