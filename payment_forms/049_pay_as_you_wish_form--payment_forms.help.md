# pay_as_you_wish_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pay_as_you_wish_form" is a form that allows you to specify your payment preferences for donations. This form is used to collect information about your donation amount, payment frequency, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your desired payment preferences.
2. Ensure that all required fields (*) are filled out accurately.
3. Select your preferred payment method (Credit card, Bank transfer, or Check).
4. Choose your payment frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
5. Provide your email address and phone number for communication and payment processing purposes.
6. Enter your preferred payment date and time.
7. Select your status (Active or Inactive).
8. Select your frequency type (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
9. Select your frequency unit (Year, Month, or Day).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation amount** (1, number, *): Specify the amount you want to donate.
* **How often do you want to make recurring payments?** (2, select_multiple, ): 
	+ This field is optional, but selecting one or more options will help us understand your payment frequency.
* **Email** (3, email, *): Enter your email address for communication and payment processing purposes.
* **Phone** (4, text, ): Enter your phone number for communication and payment processing purposes.
* **Notes** (5, note, ): Add any additional notes or comments about your donation.
* **Payment method** (6, select_one, ): Choose how you want to make your payment (Credit card, Bank transfer, or Check).
* **Payment date** (7, date, *): Enter the date you want to make your payment.
* **Payment time** (8, time, *): Enter the time you want to make your payment.
* **Status** (9, select_one, ): Select your status (Active or Inactive).
* **Frequency type** (10, select_one, ): Choose the type of frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Frequency unit** (11, select_one, ): Choose the unit of frequency (Year, Month, or Day).

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid any issues with payment processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing accurate and complete information to ensure smooth payment processing.
