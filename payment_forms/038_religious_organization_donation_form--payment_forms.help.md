# Religious Organization Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect donor information for donations made to religious organizations. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the donation is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Donor Information". Please answer this question truthfully to help the organization understand whether you are a donor or not.
2. Provide a donation amount in the required field. This will help the organization process the donation correctly.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Donation Frequency". This question helps the organization understand if you plan to donate regularly or not.
4. Select the payment method you prefer from the options provided. If none of the options apply, you can specify "Other" and provide the method in the text field below.
5. If you select "Other" for payment method, please provide the alternative payment method in the text field.
6. Add any additional notes you'd like to share with the organization in the "Additional Notes" field.
7. Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for any updates or information related to your donation.
8. Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone for any updates or information related to your donation.
9. Enter your street address for the organization's records.
10. Enter your city for the organization's records.
11. Enter your state for the organization's records.
12. Enter your postal/zip code for the organization's records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, select multiple, required): This question determines whether you are a donor or not. Please answer truthfully.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the organization. Please ensure this field is accurate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select one, required): This question helps the organization understand if you plan to donate regularly.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, required): Choose the payment method you prefer.
* **Other (If above payment methods do not apply)** (`payment_method_other`, text, optional): If none of the above payment options apply, please specify the alternative payment method.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share with the organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for any updates or information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone for any updates or information.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, optional): Enter your street address for the organization's records.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city for the organization's records.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state for the organization's records.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip/postal code for the organization's records.
