# Youth Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Retreat Registration Form is a form designed to collect information from youth participants and their emergency contacts for the purpose of organizing a youth retreat. This form helps to ensure that all participants have necessary contact information and dietary preferences recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name to complete this field.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name to complete this field.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
4. **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name in this field. This is not required.
5. **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Parent, Guardian, Relative, or Other). If you select Other, enter a brief description in the **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** field.
6. **Address**: Enter your address to ensure that we can contact you in case of an emergency.
7. **City**: Enter the city where you live.
8. **State**: Enter the state where you live.
9. **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
10. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
11. **Email**: Enter your email address.
12. **Dietary Preferences**: Select the type of food you prefer (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, or No Preference).
13. **Emergency Contact Relationship Other**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, enter a brief description of your emergency contact relationship.
14. **Participant Type**: Select whether you are a Youth or Adult participant.
15. **Participant Type Other**: If you are a Participant Type Other, enter a brief description of your participant type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (4, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact if you have one.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (5, select_multiple, optional): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Address** (6, text, optional): Enter your address to ensure we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **City** (7, text, optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (8, text, optional): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code** (9, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone Number** (10, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Email** (11, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Dietary Preferences** (12, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of food you prefer.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** (13, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, enter a brief description of your emergency contact relationship.
* **Participant Type** (14, select_one, required): Select whether you are a Youth or Adult participant.
* **Participant Type Other** (15, text, optional): If you are a Participant Type Other, enter a brief description of your participant type.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the form. Optional fields can be left blank if you don't have the information or prefer not to enter it.
