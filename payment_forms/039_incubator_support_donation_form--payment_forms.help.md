# incubator_support_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations in support of an incubator. Fill out this form to provide your donation amount, contact information, and any additional notes about your donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your desired donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Select the frequency of your donation from the "Recurring Payment?" dropdown menu.
3. Choose how often you would like to make the donation (e.g., "Weekly", "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Annually") in the "Donation Frequency" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
5. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
8. Enter any additional notes about your donation in the "Donation Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Recurring Payment?** (`Recurring Payment?`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Select "Monthly" if you would like to make a recurring monthly donation.
* **Donation Frequency** (`Donation Frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Choose how often you would like to make the donation (e.g., "Weekly", "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Annually").
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your name.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required/optional: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Donation Note** (`Donation Note`, `note`, required/optional: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your donation.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, `number`, required/optional: false): Not required to be filled out.
