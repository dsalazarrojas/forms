# SEO Services Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO Services Payment Form is designed to collect payment information from users who have opted for SEO services. This form is only for payment purposes and should not be used for any other purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select your payment method from the options provided.
2. Enter your card number, expiration date, CVV, and zip code for card payments.
3. If paying by card, enter your billing name, email, phone number, address, city, and state for delivery purposes.
4. Fill in the necessary information accurately to avoid any errors during payment processing.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one):
	+ This field asks you to select the method by which you want to make the payment.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, number):
	+ This is the 16-digit number on your credit or debit card.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date):
	+ This is the expiration date of your card, in the format MM/YY.
* **CVV** (`cvv`, number):
	+ This is the 3-digit number on the back of your card.
* **CVV 2** (`cvv_2`, text):
	+ This field is currently not used in our payment process, but please fill it out as it's part of the form schema.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number):
	+ This is the zip code for your billing address.
* **Billing Name** (`billing_name`, text):
	+ This is the name of the person to be billed.
* **Email** (`email`, email):
	+ This is the email address of the person to be billed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text):
	+ This is the phone number of the person to be billed.
* **Address** (`address`, text):
	+ This is the address of the person to be billed.
* **City** (`city`, text):
	+ This is the city of the person to be billed.
* **State** (`state`, text):
	+ This is the state of the person to be billed.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text):
	+ This is the zip code of the person to be billed.

## Tips
- Fill in the form accurately to avoid any errors.
- Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or doubts, feel free to contact us for support.
