# Court Appointed Special Advocate Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Court Appointed Special Advocate Fund Donation Form is designed to collect donations for the critical services provided by Court Appointed Special Advocates (CASAs) to vulnerable children and families.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your information as a donor by filling out the **donor_information** field.
2. Enter your address in the **address** field, including your city, state, and zip code.
3. Enter your address (if different from above) in the **address_2** field.
4. Choose your payment method from the **payment_info** options (Credit card, Check or money order, or Other).
5. Enter the **amount** of your donation.
6. Select the **frequency** of your donation from the options (One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
7. If applicable, select the **donation_frequency** option to specify how often you'd like to make this donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information** (`donor_information`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details as the donor.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional): Enter your address, including city, state, and zip code.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional address if different from the above.
* **Zip** (`zip`, Number, Optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, Select One, Optional): Choose how you'd like to make your donation (Credit card, Check or money order, or Other).
* **Amount** (`amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select how often you'd like to make this donation (One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): If you selected **One-time** for the above field, choose how often you'd like to make this type of donation (If applicable). 

Note: The CASAs appreciate any contribution and look forward to serving those in need with your help!
