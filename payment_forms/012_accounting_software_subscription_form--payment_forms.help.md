# Accounting Software Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Software Subscription Form is designed to gather information from your company to subscribe to our accounting software. This form will help us understand your subscription preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the legal name of your company in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide the full name of the primary contact person in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the business email address for communication regarding your subscription in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the phone number for communication with us regarding your subscription in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Enter the complete business address, including street, city, state, and zip code, in the "Business Address" field.
6. Select the desired accounting software plan that suits your company's needs from the "Software Plan Selected" dropdown menu.
7. Choose the billing cycle frequency that best suits your company's needs from the "Billing Cycle" dropdown menu.
8. Select a start date for your subscription from the calendar.
9. If applicable, choose all required features for your business from the "Features Needed" dropdown menu.
10. Enter the number of users who will need access to the software in the "Number of Users" field.
11. Select the payment method you prefer from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
12. If you have selected "Credit Card" or "Debit Card" as your payment method, please provide the card number and expiration date in the corresponding fields.
13. Enter the billing zip code associated with your payment method in the "Billing Zip Code" field.
14. Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the subscription by selecting "Yes, I agree to the terms and conditions" from the "Terms Agreement" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the legal name of your company as it appears on your business cards or license.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the full name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address we can use for communication with your company regarding your subscription.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number for communication with us regarding your subscription.
* **Business Address**: Enter the complete address, including street, city, state, and zip code, for your business.
* **Software Plan Selected**: Choose the accounting software plan that best suits your company's needs.
* **Billing Cycle**: Choose the billing frequency that best suits your company's financial needs.
* **Subscription Start Date**: Select the date when you wish to begin your subscription.
* **Features Needed**: Select all features required for your business, if any. 
* **Number of Users**: Enter the number of users who will need access to the software.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your subscription.
* **Credit Card Number** (if applicable): If you have selected "Credit Card" or "Debit Card" as your payment method, please enter the card number.
* **Card Expiration Date** (if applicable): If you have selected "Credit Card" or "Debit Card" as your payment method, please enter the expiration date of your card.
* **Billing Zip Code**: Enter the zip code associated with your payment method.
* **Terms Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of your subscription.
