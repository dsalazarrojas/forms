# Hospital Charity Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Charity Donation Form is a tool for individuals to donate to the hospital's charity efforts, providing financial support for the patients and the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your desired donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Select your preferred donation frequency from the "How Often Would You Like to Donate" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred payment method from the "How would you like to pay" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter your city in the "City" field.
9. Enter your state in the "State" field.
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
11. Enter any additional comments or details in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount (required: false)**: Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **How Often Would You Like to Donate (required: false)**: Choose how frequently you would like to make your donations (Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, Annually, or One time).
* **How would you like to pay (required: false)**: Select the method by which you would like to pay for your donation (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
* **Email (required: false)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Name (required: false)**: Enter your full name.
* **Phone (required: false)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (required: false)**: Enter your full address.
* **City (required: false)**: Enter the name of the city you live in.
* **State (required: false)**: Enter the name of the state you live in.
* **Zip (required: false)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Payment Terms (required: false)**: Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Status (required: false)**: Select the status of your payment (Pending, Approved, Declined, or Canceled).
* **Notes (required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or details about your donation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the hospital's charity team.
* Don't hesitate to reach out if you need assistance with submitting your donation.
