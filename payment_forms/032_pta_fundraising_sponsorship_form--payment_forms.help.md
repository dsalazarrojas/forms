# PTA Fundraising Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors who want to support the Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) for various fundraising activities. It helps the PTA committee track and acknowledge donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Type in your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. For the Donation Type field, select either 'Yes' or 'No' depending on your willingness to donate.
6. Fill in the Amount Donated field with the amount you're willing to donate.
7. Choose your Payment Frequency from the options provided. If 'Yes' is selected, you'll be prompted to specify your payment frequency in the Other Payment Frequency field.
8. Choose a date for your Payment Date.
9. In the acknowledgement section, read and agree to the statement provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Your first name as it appears on official documents and in PTA records.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Your last name as it appears on official documents and in PTA records.
* **Email** (email, required): Your valid email address where we can contact you for further instructions or confirmation.
* **Phone** (text, required): Your mobile or home phone number where we can reach you for any updates.
* **Donation Type** (select_one, required): Confirm whether you're willing to donate to the PTA or not.
* **Amount Donated** (number, required): Fill in the amount you're willing to donate to the PTA.
* **Payment Frequency** (select_one, required): Choose when you're willing to make your donation (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', or specify a different frequency in Other Payment Frequency).
* **Payment Date** (date, required): Choose a date when you're willing to make your donation.
* **Other Payment Frequency** (text, optional): If you selected 'Yes' for Payment Frequency, provide an alternative payment frequency (e.g., 'Monthly', 'Quarterly', etc.).
* **I acknowledge this donation and understand that** (note, required): Read and agree to the terms of the donation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you select 'Yes' for Payment Frequency, be prepared to specify an alternative frequency if needed.
* Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
