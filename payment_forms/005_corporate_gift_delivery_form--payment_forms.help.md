# Corporate Gift Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Gift Delivery Form is designed to collect information about the recipient and the gift details for official company gifts or business-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the recipient's details in the **Recipient Details** field.
2. Choose the delivery preferences for the gift in the **Delivery Preferences** field.
3. Provide the gift details in the **Gift Details** field.
4. Fill in any relevant information about the recipient in the **Recipient Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient Details**: <label> Enter the recipient's name, title, and any other relevant details. (text, required)
* **Delivery Preferences**: <label> Choose how you want the gift to be delivered, e.g., in-person, by mail, or other methods. (text, required)
* **Gift Details**: <label> Describe the gift you would like to send, including any specific items or items to be excluded. (text, required)
* **Recipient Info**: <label> Provide any additional information about the recipient that may be helpful for the gift delivery. (text, optional)

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to ensure proper gift delivery.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
