# Dividend Distribution Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the receipt or non-receipt of dividend payments made to shareholders. It helps to track any errors or discrepancies that may have occurred during the distribution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're filling it out for the correct purpose.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. For fields marked as "optional," provide any additional information that you think is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distribution Form**: This is a placeholder section to confirm the distribution form has been filled out for this shareholder.
* **Distribution Date**: Enter the date when the dividend payment was distributed to you.
* **Declaration Date**: Enter the date you declared for tax purposes or any other relevant date.
* **Shareholder Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your shareholder records.
* **Dividend Amount**: Enter the amount you received in the dividend payment.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the dividend payment. You can also use the "Other" option and provide a description if it doesn't match one of the listed options.
* **Payment Method (2)**: This is a duplicate field of "Payment Method" for confirmation purposes.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the bank where the payment was received.
* **Bank Account**: Enter your bank account number where the payment was received.
* **IBAN**: Enter your International Bank Account Number (IBAN) if you have one.
* **Address**: Enter your address where the payment was received.
* **Shareholder Number**: Enter your shareholder number as it appears on your records.
* **Shareholder Number Other**: Enter a description for any other shareholder number not listed.
* **Tax ID**: Enter any tax ID you have for tax purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence from the options provided. You can also use the "Other" option and provide a description.
* **City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code or zip code of your residence.
* **Country Other**: Enter a description for any other country not listed.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **Postal Code Other**: Enter a description for any other postal code not listed.
* **Country Code**: Select your country code from the options provided. You can also use the "Other" option and provide a description.
* **Country Code Other**: Enter a description for any other country code not listed.
