# Season Pass Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide season pass information and details for customers or users. To access their season pass, fill out the form with accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your User Input in the field provided.
2. Provide your Email address in the field below.
3. Select your preferred Payment Method.
4. Choose your Application Type (Season Pass, Guest Pass, or Membership Pass).
5. Select your Season Start and Season End dates.
6. Enter any additional Notes about your application.
7. Enter your Organization and Season Pass ID if applicable.
8. Select your Season Name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (1)** (text, required: false): Please enter any relevant information about yourself.
* **User Email (2)** (email, required: false): Enter your email address associated with your season pass.
* **Payment Method (3)** (select_one, required: false): Choose how you will be paying for your season pass (Credit Card or Other).
* **Application Type (4)** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of pass you are applying for (Season Pass, Guest Pass, or Membership Pass).
* **Season Start (5)** (select_one, required: false): Select the month when your season pass is supposed to start.
* **Season End (6)** (select_one, required: false): Select the month when your season pass is supposed to end.
* **Submit (7)** (text, required: false): Enter any other information you'd like to provide about your application.
* **Organization (8)** (select_multiple, required: false): Select your organization or company if you are purchasing passes for multiple people.
* **Notes (9)** (note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your application.
* **Season Pass ID (10)** (text, required: false): Enter your season pass ID number.
* **Season Name (11)** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the season for which you are applying for a pass.
