# Seasonal Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Product Order Form is designed to gather information for seasonal product orders. Please fill out this form to place a seasonal product order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: customer name, order date, order time, and order total.
2. Select the correct options for order status, seasonal product, and payment method.
3. Enter any additional customer information, such as email and phone number.
4. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Product Order** (`seasonal_product_order`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the seasonal product order.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter the payment method and any additional payment information.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, required): Enter any additional product information.
* **Seasonal Product** (`seasonal_product`, select_one, required): Select whether the order is for a seasonal product or not.
* **Seasonal Products** (`seasonal_products`, select_multiple, required): Select which seasonal products are being ordered.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method (Yes or No).
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, required): Enter a unique ID for the order.

Note: This form is for seasonal product orders only. Any other type of order will not be accepted.
