# Fitness App Premium Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for signing up for a premium membership of the fitness app, which includes various plans and pricing options. Users will need to select their preferred plan, provide billing details, and choose a payment method to complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred plan from the "Plan Selection" field. You can choose from pre-defined options or select "Other" if none of the provided plans suit your needs.
2. Enter your payment details in the "Payment Details" field. This is a numerical field where you can enter the amount you wish to pay.
3. Provide your billing address in the "Billing Address" field. This is a text field where you can enter your billing address.
4. Enter your billing city in the "Billing City" field.
5. Select your country of residence from the list of options in the "Billing Country" field or choose "Other" if not listed.
6. Enter any additional onboarding details in the "Onboarding Details" field. This is a text field for any extra information the user wants to provide.
7. Select your membership plan duration from the "Membership Plan" field. You can choose from pre-defined options or select "Other" if none of the provided plans suit your needs.
8. Enter your billing name in the "Billing Name" field.
9. Enter your billing phone number in the "Billing Phone" field.
10. Enter your billing email address in the "Billing Email" field. Please note that this is a required field for email validation.
11. Enter your plan pricing in the "Plan Pricing" field.
12. Enter your billing zip code in the "Billing Zip" field.
13. Select the date and time for onboarding in the "Onboarding Date" and "Onboarding Time" fields.
14. Choose your membership plan type from the "Membership Plan Type" field. You can choose from pre-defined options or select "Other" if none of the provided plans suit your needs.
15. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field. You can choose from pre-defined options or select "Other" if none of the provided options suit your needs.
16. Enter your billing state in the "Billing State" field.
17. Add any billing notes in the "Billing Notes" field. This is a text field for any extra information.
18. Enter the price of your membership plan in the "Membership Plan Price" field.
