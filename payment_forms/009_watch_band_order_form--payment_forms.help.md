# Watch Band Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Watch Band Order Form is used to collect information for the purchase of watch bands. This form helps users submit their order details, including their personal contact information, selection preferences, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select from the dropdown menus for "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
3. Enter your personal contact information in the "First Name", "Email", "Address", "Phone", and "Email 2" fields.
4. Provide a specific quantity and price for the watch band in the "Quantity" and "Price" fields.
5. Add any additional notes in the "Note", "Size", "Size 2", and "Address 2" fields.
6. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary information.
