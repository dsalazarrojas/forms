# Meal Subscription Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and preferences for meal subscription orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
2. Select your preferred meals in the "Meal Selection" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
3. Choose your dietary preferences in the "Dietary Preferences" field.
4. Select your delivery schedule in the "Delivery Schedule" field.
5. Enter your customer payment information in the "Customer Payment Info" field.
6. Choose your payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
7. If you have any special instructions, please enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
8. Finally, enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the customer's name, address, and other contact information. Please fill out this field with your accurate details.
- **Meal Selection** (`meal_selection`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or multiple meal options that you would like to subscribe to.
- **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your dietary preferences, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
- **Delivery Schedule** (`delivery_schedule`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time you would like to receive your meal delivery.
- **Customer Payment Info** (`customer_payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your payment information, such as credit card details or bank account number.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
- **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special requests or comments, please enter them here.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to ensure smooth order processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our customer support team for assistance.
- We appreciate your feedback and suggestions to improve our meal subscription service.
