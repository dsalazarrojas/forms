# Marathon Sponsorship Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Sponsorship Donation Form is a simple form designed to collect information from potential donors for the marathon sponsorship program. This form will help us understand the level of interest and willingness of donors to support the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
* For the "Donations Amount" field, please enter the amount you would like to donate.
* For the "Payment Type" field, select either "Yes" or "No" depending on your ability to make a payment.
* For the "Donor Name" field, enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* For the "Email" field, enter your valid email address.
* For the "Phone" field, enter your valid phone number.
* For the "Date" field, select the date you would like to make the payment.
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donations Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate to the marathon sponsorship program. This field is optional.
* **Payment Type**: Select either "Yes" or "No" depending on your ability to make a payment. This field is optional.
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you. This field is optional.
* **Date**: Select the date you would like to make the payment. This field is optional.
Note: All fields are optional, but please fill in all the fields that apply to you. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
