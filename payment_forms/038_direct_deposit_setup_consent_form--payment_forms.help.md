# Direct Deposit Setup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Deposit Setup Consent Form is used to set up direct deposit for payroll processing. It requires accurate and correct information to ensure secure and timely payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User ID.
2. Provide your Bank Account information (Account Number and Routing Number).
3. Enter the Account Holder Name.
4. Enter your contact information (Contact First Name, Contact Last Name, and Contact Email).
5. Sign and date the Signature Date and Signature Time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (1, number, required): Enter your Employee ID as provided by your employer.
* **Bank Account** (2, text, required): Enter your bank's account number.
* **Account Holder Name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the account holder (usually yourself or another person you've authorized).
* **Routing Number** (4, number, required): Enter your bank's routing number.
* **Account Number** (5, number, required): Enter your account number as provided by your bank.
* **Bank Name** (6, text, optional): Enter your bank's name for easier reference.
* **Contact First Name** (7, text, optional): Enter the contact person's first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (8, text, optional): Enter the contact person's last name.
* **Contact Email** (9, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email for communication.
* **Signature Date** (10, date, required): Date and sign the form to indicate your consent.
* **Signature Time** (11, time, required): Time and sign the form to indicate your consent.

## Tips
Ensure all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly to prevent errors and delays in payment processing. If you're unsure about any information, please contact your HR representative or payroll department.
