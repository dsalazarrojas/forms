# Afterpay Fitness Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Afterpay Fitness Equipment Order Form" is designed to collect essential customer information for the purpose of placing a fitness equipment order. This form ensures we have accurate data for order fulfillment, delivery, and communication with customers. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form with your details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Customer Full Name** to help us identify you accurately.
2. Provide your **Email Address** to receive order confirmations and updates.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for delivery and update notifications.
4. Fill in your **Shipping Address** completely for smooth delivery.
5. If your billing address is different from your shipping address, mark **Yes** for "Billing Address Same as Shipping" and enter your **Billing Address**.
6. In the **Fitness Goals and Needs** section, choose your primary fitness goal(s) from the list.
7. Select your **Exercise Experience Level** to help us recommend suitable equipment.
8. Choose the **Equipment Category** that interests you.
9. Enter the specific **Equipment Type** you're looking for.
10. If applicable, select your **Brand Preference** for the equipment.
11. Specify the **Desired Features** of your equipment needs.
12. Choose your space constraints from **Small Space (apartment), Medium Space**, or **Large Space (garage/gym)**.
13. Set your **Budget Range** according to your available budget.
14. Select your **Assembly Preference** (Pre-assembled, DIY Assembly, or Professional Assembly Service).
15. Choose your **Shipping Preference** from **Standard (5-7 business days), Express (2-3 business days), White Glove Delivery, or Store Pickup**.
16. Select **Afterpay (Buy Now, Pay Later)** as your payment method.
17. Finally, agree to the **Terms Agreement** to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (1): Enter your full name to help us identify you accurately.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email for order confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your phone number for delivery and update notifications.
* **Shipping Address** (4): Enter the complete address for delivery.
* **Billing Address Same as Shipping** (5): If your billing address is different from your shipping address, select "Yes" and enter your billing address.
* **Fitness Goals and Needs** (7): Choose your primary fitness goal(s) from the list.
* **Exercise Experience Level** (9): Select your current fitness level to help us recommend suitable equipment.
* **Equipment Category** (11): Select the category of fitness equipment you're interested in.
* **Specific Equipment Type** (12): Enter the specific type of equipment you're looking for.
* **Brand Preference** (13): If you have a preferred brand, enter it here.
* **Desired Features** (14): Specify the features you need in your equipment.
* **Space Constraints** (15): Select your space constraints to help us choose equipment that fits.
* **Budget Range** (16): Set your available budget for the equipment.
* **Assembly Preference** (17): Choose your preference for assembly (Pre-assembled, DIY Assembly, or Professional Assembly Service).
* **Shipping Preference** (18): Select your preferred shipping method.
* **Payment Method** (19): Select Afterpay as your payment method for the "Buy Now, Pay Later" service.
* **Terms Agreement** (20): Agree to the store's terms and conditions.

Note: This help guide aims to provide a clear and concise explanation of each field's purpose and requirement to ensure customers complete the form accurately and efficiently.
