# subscription_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The subscription_renewal_form is designed for customers to provide information about their subscription renewal. It collects customer and subscription details, and allows customers to make payment arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the subscription_renewal_form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information in the "Customer Info" field. This includes your name and contact details.
2. Select the subscription renewal option that best suits your needs in the "Subscription Info" field.
3. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field, if applicable.
4. Review your information carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and other relevant customer details.
* **Subscription Info** (`Subscription Info`, text, optional): Select the subscription renewal option that best suits your needs. Please choose from the options listed to ensure accurate processing of your renewal.
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, text, optional): If you wish to make payment arrangements, enter your payment details here. This field is not required but is necessary for processing payment.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, note, optional): Review your information carefully before clicking this button. This will submit your form and initiate the renewal process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid delays in processing your subscription renewal.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
