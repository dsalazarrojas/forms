# Spring Fair Ticket Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect preorders for tickets to the Spring Fair. Please fill out the form with your information and order details to complete your preorder.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Specify the number of tickets you would like to order.
3. Choose your preferred payment method: Cash or Credit Card.
4. Provide any additional note or message, if needed.
5. Enter your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, optional): This is a free-form text field where you can enter any additional information related to your order.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be recorded for the tickets.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmations about your order.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of tickets you would like to preorder.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the tickets per unit (likely a single ticket).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the preorder (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method: Cash or Credit Card.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional note or message related to your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for further information).
* **Email 10** (`email_10`, `email`, optional): This field is likely a duplicate of the email field, but we'll assume it's for additional contact information. Enter your email address again, if needed.
* **User Input 11** (`user_input_11`, `text`, optional): Another free-form text field for any additional information related to your order.

## Tips
- Please note that some fields are optional, but we appreciate complete and accurate information for your order.
- Ensure that your email address is accurate to receive updates and confirmations about your order.
- You can enter any additional notes or messages for special requests or questions.
