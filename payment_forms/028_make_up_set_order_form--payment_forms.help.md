# Make Up Set Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for payment processing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the Order Form with the necessary details.
2. Select the Payment Method you would like to use.
3. Enter the Total Amount of your order.
4. Provide any Order Notes if necessary.
5. Enter your Customer Address and Customer Phone for shipping and contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form (text)**: Enter the necessary details about your order.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Select your preferred payment method. Options are credit card and paypal.
* **Total Amount (number)**: Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Order Notes (text)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Customer Address (text)**: Enter your address for shipping purposes.
* **Customer Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all necessary information is provided to facilitate the payment process.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
