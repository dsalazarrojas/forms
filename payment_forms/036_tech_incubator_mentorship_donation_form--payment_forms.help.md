# Tech Incubator Mentorship Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help you contribute to the Tech Incubator Mentorship program by providing donations of your time, skills, or resources. By filling out this form, you'll be able to specify how often and in what amount you'd like to donate, as well as how you'd like to receive donations from others.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Frequency** (Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually): Please select the frequency at which you would like to make donations to the Tech Incubator Mentorship program.
* **Donation Amount** (Number): Enter the amount you would like to donate to the program.
* **Donation Type** (One-time, Recurring): Choose if you would like to make a one-time or recurring donation.
* **Donor's Name**: Enter your name as the individual or organization making the donation.
* **Donor's Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Donor's Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or notes about your donation, please share them here.
* **Frequency of Form Donation**: This field is not clearly defined in the form's schema, so we'll ignore it for now.
* **Donation Interval** (Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually): This field seems to be a duplicate of the Donation Frequency field and will also be ignored.
* **Amount of Form Donation**: This field is also a duplicate of the Donation Amount field and will be ignored.
* **Type of Form Donation**: This field is a duplicate of the Donation Type field and will be ignored.
* **Name of Form Donor**: This field is a duplicate of the Donor's Name field and will be ignored.
* **Email of Form Donor**: This field is a duplicate of the Donor’s Email field and will be ignored.
* **Phone of Form Donor**: This field is a duplicate of the Donor's Phone field and will be ignored.
* **Notes of Form Donor**: This field is a duplicate of the Notes field and will be ignored.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your donation is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, please contact us for assistance.
