# Membership Dues Conditional Logic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps the organization understand individual membership dues payers' preferences and conditions, informing their membership dues policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the option that best describes what you want to do with your membership dues.
2. Choose the payment plan(s) that apply to you.
3. Select how often you pay your membership dues.
4. Enter the amount you pay per payment period (if applicable).
5. Select a date for your payment (if applicable).
6. If you have any additional comments or information, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you want to do? (membership_dues_conditional_logic_survey_page1)**: Select the option that best describes your situation. For example, if you want to pay your dues on a monthly basis, select "Option 1".
* **Which payment plan? (membership_dues_payment_plan)**: Choose the payment plan(s) that apply to you. For example, if you're paying for multiple members, select both "Option 4" and "Option 5".
* **How often do you pay? (membership_dues_payment_frequency)**: Select how often you pay your membership dues. For example, if you pay monthly, select "Option 6".
* **How much do you pay? (membership_dues_payment_amount)**: Enter the amount you pay per payment period. This field is only required if you've selected an option that requires payment amount.
* **When do you pay? (membership_dues_payment_date)**: Select a date for your payment. This field is only required if you've selected an option that requires a payment date.
* **Additional Comments (membership_dues_additional_comments)**: Type any additional comments or information about your payment situation.
* **Email (membership_dues_email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your membership dues.
* **Phone (membership_dues_phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your membership dues.
