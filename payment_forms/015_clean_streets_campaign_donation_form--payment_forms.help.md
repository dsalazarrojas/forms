# Clean Streets Campaign Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clean Streets Campaign Donation Form is a tool used to collect donations from individuals who want to support the campaign's efforts to keep our community clean and green.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your full name in the field labeled "Donor Full Name".
*   Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is where you will receive your donation receipt.
*   Select your preferred method of payment by choosing one of the options provided in the "Preferred Payment Method" field.
*   Choose how your donation should be used by selecting one of the options in the "How should we use your donation?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Donor Full Name (Donor Full Name)** (`text`, required): Enter your full name in this field.
*   **Email Address (Email Address)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address in this field to receive your donation receipt.
*   **Phone Number (Donor Phone)** (`text`, optional): This field is optional, but it can be used for further communication if needed.
*   **-- Donation Amount -- (Donation Amount)** (`note`, optional): This field indicates that you will choose a donation amount in the following fields.
*   **Select Amount (Select Amount)** (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred donation amount from the provided options.
*   **Other Amount ($)** (`number`, optional): Enter your custom donation amount if you selected the "Other Amount" option.
*   **Donation Frequency (Donation Frequency)** (`select_one`, required): Choose how often you would like to donate, e.g., One-time, Monthly, or Annually.
*   **How should we use your donation? (How should we use your donation?)** (`select_one`, required): Choose how your donation should be used, e.g., General Campaign Fund, Street Cleaning Supplies, etc.
*   **Is this donation in honor or memory of someone? (Is this donation in honor or memory of someone?)** (`select_one`, optional): If your donation is in honor or memory of someone, choose this option.
*   **In Honor/Memory of** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the person you are making the donation in honor or memory of.
*   **I wish to remain anonymous (I wish to remain anonymous)** (`select_one`, required): Choose whether or not you wish to remain anonymous.
*   **Are you interested in volunteering for a cleanup event? (Are you interested in volunteering for a cleanup event?)** (`select_one`, optional): If you are interested in volunteering, choose this option.
*   **-- Payment Method -- (Payment Method --)** (`note`, optional): This field is an indicator that you will be redirected to a secure payment processor.
*   **Preferred Payment Method (Preferred Payment Method)** (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.
*   **-- Together for a cleaner city -- (Together for a cleaner city --)** (`note`, optional): This field serves as a final message from the campaign.

Note: This form is subject to change as the campaign's needs and goals evolve. If you encounter any difficulties or have questions, feel free to reach out to the campaign team for assistance.
