# custom_heat_transfer_patch_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture all the necessary information for a custom heat transfer patch order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all required fields marked as such. 
2. Ensure that all fields with the 'required' property set to true are filled out.
3. Double check that all entered information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the product being ordered.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter the shipping address where the order will be delivered.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, `select_one`, required): Select the state of the shipping address from the list of options.
* **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method from the list of options.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the product being ordered.
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, `number`, required): Enter the weight of the product being ordered.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the order from the list of options.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the order.
* **Product Description** (`product_description_alt`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional description of the product.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, `text`, optional): Enter an image description or URL for the product.
* **Product Image Alternative** (`product_image_alt`, `text`, optional): Enter an alternative image description or URL for the product.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the payment method.
* **Form Signature** (`form_signature`, `text`, optional): Enter the signature of the form submitter.
* **Color Name** (`color_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the color of the product.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields with the 'required' property set to true are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double check that all entered information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us.
