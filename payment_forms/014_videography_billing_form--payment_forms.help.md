# videography_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from customers for video production services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in the form with your information.
2. Select a payment method from the options provided.
3. Enter your billing address details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **videography_billing_form** (`videography_billing_form`, text, required/optional): Please enter the title of your videography project.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to make your payment (Payment, EFT, or Bank).
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter your billing address to ensure timely processing of your payment.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, text, optional): Enter your city for billing purposes.
* **Billing Postal Code** (`billing_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code for billing.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in the form correctly to avoid delays in processing your payment.
- Double-check your payment method and billing address to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
