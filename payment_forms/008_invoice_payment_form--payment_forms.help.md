# Invoice Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information for invoices from users. It is intended for both internal and external purposes, such as in-app payments, online payments, or offline payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the **User Information** field.
2. Fill in the **Invoice Details** field with relevant information about the invoice you are paying for.
3. Select a **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
4. If you selected **Credit Card** or **Bank Transfer**, enter the required information in the relevant fields.
5. Enter the **Total Amount** you are paying.
6. Choose the **Expiration Date** and **Expiration Month** for the payment method (if applicable).
7. Enter the **CVV** and **CVV Expiration** for the payment method (if applicable).
8. Select the **Amount** to be paid.
9. Choose the **Payment Date** for the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant details as requested.
* **Invoice Details**: Enter details about the invoice you are paying for, such as invoice number, date, etc.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to make the payment. You can select from the options **Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other**.
* **Card Number**: If you selected **Credit Card**, enter your credit card number.
* **Expiration Date** (optional): Select the expiration date of your payment method.
* **Expiration Month** (optional): Select the expiration month of your payment method.
* **CVV**: Enter the security code found on the back of your credit card.
* **CVV Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of your security code.
* **Amount**: Enter the total amount you are paying.
* **Payment Date**: Choose the date you would like to make the payment.
