# industrial_mixer_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information, order details, shipping information, payment details, and order total and confirmation for industrial mixers. It is intended for customers or sales teams to input data for new orders, ensuring accurate and complete information for processing and fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer information in the **Customer Information** section.
2. Provide details of your order in the **Order Details** section.
3. Fill in your shipping information in the **Shipping Information** section.
4. Enter your payment information in the **Payment Information** section.
5. Enter the total amount of your order in the **Order Total** section.
6. Finally, confirm your order in the **Order Confirmation** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, company name, and contact information for the order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the products or mixers you want to order.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address, including city, state, and zip code.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer information.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the order details.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing your order. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our sales team.
