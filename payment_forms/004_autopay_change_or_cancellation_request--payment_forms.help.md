# Autopay Change Or Cancellation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Autopay Change Or Cancellation Request form is used to request changes or cancellations to an Autopay account. This form is essential for modifying account details, updating payment methods, or terminating the account altogether.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and authorization to complete the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Select the appropriate nature of the request (Update Payment Method, Change Billing Cycle, or Cancellation of Autopay).
4. Specify the requested effective date for the change or cancellation.
5. If necessary, select the details to be updated (Credit/Debit Card Number, Bank Account Information, Expiration Date, or Billing Address).
6. Provide a reason for the update or cancellation, if applicable.
7. Finally, confirm the authorization by typing your full name as a digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your account name as it appears on your account.
* **Account Number**: Unique customer or account ID.
* **Email Address**: For confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Primary contact number.
* **Nature of Request**: Choose the type of change you wish to make (Update Payment Method, Change Billing Cycle, or Cancellation of Autopay).
* **Requested Effective Date**: When you want the change or cancellation to take effect.
* **Details to be Updated**: Select all applicable fields to be updated (Credit/Debit Card Number, Bank Account Information, Expiration Date, or Billing Address).
* **Reason for Update or Cancellation**: Briefly describe why you are making this change or cancellation.
* **I authorize this change/cancellation**: Type your full name as a digital signature to confirm authorization.
* **Do you understand that changes may take 1-2 billing cycles?**: Confirm that you understand the implications of the change or cancellation, including the potential delay in processing.

## Tips

* Ensure you review all fields carefully to avoid errors or omissions.
* Make sure to select the correct nature of the request to avoid confusion.
* Use the correct effective date for the change or cancellation.
* If updating payment details, ensure you have the necessary information ready.
* If cancelling the autopay, confirm that you understand the implications.
* Double-check your digital signature for accuracy.
