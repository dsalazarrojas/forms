# Payment Authorization & Insurance Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Authorization & Insurance Waiver Form is used to gather participant information, payment authorization, and uploaded waivers for a program or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your payment authorization by signing the "Payment Consent" field.
3. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions by signing the "Waiver Acknowledgment" field.
4. Upload the required waivers for the service or program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number in the format (123) 456-7890.
* **Participant E-mail** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address in the format user@domain.tld.
* **Payment Consent** (`payment_consent`, text, required): Sign to confirm your payment authorization.
* **Waiver Acknowledgment** (`waiver_acknowledgment`, text, required): Sign to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Upload Waiver 1** (`uploaded_waiver_file`, text, required): Upload the first waiver for the service or program.
* **Upload Waiver 2** (`uploaded_waiver_file_2`, text, required): Upload the second waiver for the service or program.
* **Upload Waiver 3** (`uploaded_waiver_file_3`, text, required): Upload the third waiver for the service or program.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check that the uploaded waivers are the correct versions and have been signed by the participant.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
