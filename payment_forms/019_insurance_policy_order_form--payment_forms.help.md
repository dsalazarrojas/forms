## Purpose
The "Insurance Policy Order Form" is a form designed to collect information from customers interested in purchasing an insurance policy. This form will be used to gather essential details such as contact information, insurance type, policy start and end dates, and policy status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred **Insurance Type** from the options provided (Life, Health, Auto, Home, or Other).
6. Enter the **Policy Start Date** (if applicable).
7. Enter the **Policy End Date** (if applicable).
8. Select the **Policy Status** (Active or Inactive).
9. Enter the **Policy Amount** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, Text, Required: false)
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, Text, Required: false)
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`user_email`, Email, Required: false)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, Text, Required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, Select One, Required: false)
	+ Select the type of insurance you are interested in (Life, Health, Auto, Home, or Other).
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, Date, Required: false)
	+ Enter the start date of your policy (if applicable).
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, Date, Required: false)
	+ Enter the end date of your policy (if applicable).
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, Select One, Required: false)
	+ Select the status of your policy (Active or Inactive).
* **Policy Amount** (`policy_amount`, Number, Required: false)
	+ Enter the amount of your policy (if applicable).
