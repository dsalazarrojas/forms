# Adaptive Sports Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adaptive Sports Program Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors who want to support the Adaptive Sports Program. This form helps the program understand donor preferences, contact information, and donation details, allowing for efficient and accurate processing of donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your full name and email address on page 1.
2. Enter your phone number (optional).
3. Provide your mailing address.
4. Select your desired donation amount and frequency (one-time, monthly, or annual).
5. Choose how you'd like your gift to be applied (general operations, equipment purchase, athlete scholarship program, or specific sports).
6. Check if your donation is in honor or memory of someone.
7. If you're making a tribute, enter the honoree's name.
8. Choose how you'd like to be recognized for your contribution (listed in the annual report or remaining anonymous).
9. Select your preferred payment method (credit/debit card, electronic check/ACH, or PayPal/digital wallet).
10. Finally, indicate if you're interested in volunteering for events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name / Organization Name** (Donor Info, required): Enter your full name or the organization name if you're donating on behalf of a group.
* **Email Address** (Donor Info, required): Enter your email address for donation receipts and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Donor Info, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Mailing Address** (Donor Info, required): Enter your complete mailing address for tax-deductible receipts.
* **Donation Amount ($)** (Donation Details, required): Enter the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Apply My Gift To** (Donation Details, required): Choose how you'd like your gift to be applied (general operations, equipment purchase, athlete scholarship program, or specific sports).
* **Donation Frequency** (Donation Details, required): Select the frequency of your donation (one-time, monthly, or annual).
* **Is this gift in honor or memory of someone?** (Tribute, optional): Check if this is a tribute gift.
* **Honoree Name** (Tribute, optional): Enter the name of the person being honored or remembered.
* **Recognition Preference** (Tribute, required): Choose how you'd like to be recognized for your contribution (listed in the annual report or remaining anonymous).
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Tribute, required): Select your preferred payment method (credit/debit card, electronic check/ACH, or PayPal/digital wallet).
* **Are you interested in volunteering for adaptive sports events?** (Volunteer, optional): Check if you're interested in volunteering for events.
* **I understand that this donation is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law** (Acknowledgement, required): Confirm that you understand the tax-deductible status of your donation.
