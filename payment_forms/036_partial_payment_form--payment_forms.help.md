# Partial Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Partial Payment Form is designed to collect information from users regarding a partial payment they wish to make. This form serves as a platform for users to provide payment details that will be used to facilitate the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Select the relevant payment options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional information in the text fields.
4. Ensure you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **partial_payment_form_1** (Number, Optional)
*This field is meant to capture a specific numeric value.*

### 2. **select_payment_options** (Select One, Optional)
*Select the payment option that best describes your partial payment.*

### 3. **partial_payment_form_3** (Date, Optional)
*Enter the date for the partial payment.*

### 4. **partial_payment_form_4** (Time, Optional)
*Enter the time for the partial payment.*

### 5. **partial_payment_form_5** (Text, Optional)
*Enter any additional information about the partial payment.*

### 6. **partial_payment_form_6** (Email, Optional)
*Enter your email address.*

### 7. **partial_payment_form_7** (Text, Optional)
*Enter any additional comments or details.*

### 8. **partial_payment_form_8** (Note, Optional)
*Enter any notes or comments.*

### 9. **partial_payment_form_9** (Text, Optional)
*Enter any additional information.*

### 10. **partial_payment_form_10** (Text, Optional)
*Enter any additional information.*

### 11. **select_multiple_payment_options** (Select Multiple, Optional)
*Select the payment options that best describe your partial payment.*

### 12. **partial_payment_form_12** (Text, Optional)
*Enter any final comments or details.*
