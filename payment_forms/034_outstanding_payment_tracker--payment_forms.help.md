# outstanding_payment_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outstanding payment tracker form is designed to help you monitor and track payments that are pending, due, or overdue. This form allows you to input details about payments and set up follow-up reminders for payments that require it.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, go through the following steps:
- Click on the "Main" page and input the payment details such as payment method and amount due.
- Click on the "Payment Details" page and input the payment due date and any additional details.
- Click on the "Follow-up Reminders" page and set up follow-up reminders for payments that require it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter a brief description of the payment.
* **Payment Details** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional details about the payment, such as payment terms or notes.
* **Due Date** (`3`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date the payment is due.
* **Amount** (`4`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the total amount due for the payment.
* **Status** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate whether the payment is active or inactive.
* **Payment Status** (`6`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Pending", "Paid", or "Overdue" to indicate the current payment status.
* **Payment Method** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Cash", "Credit Card", "PayPal", or "Bank Transfer" to indicate the payment method.
* **Payment Terms** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any payment terms or notes.
* **Notes** (`9`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any notes or comments about the payment.
* **Payment Due Date** (`10`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date the payment is due.
* **Payment Amount** (`11`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the total amount due for the payment.
* **Payer Details** (`12`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any details about the payer.
* **Follow-up Reminders** (`13`, `number`, required: `false`): Set up the number of follow-up reminders for this payment.
* **Follow-up Reminders Frequency** (`14`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether follow-up reminders are enabled.
* **Follow-up Reminders Email** (`15`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter an email address for follow-up reminders.
* **Follow-up Reminders Phone** (`16`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter a phone number for follow-up reminders.
* **Follow-up Reminders Message** (`17`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the follow-up reminder message.
* **Follow-up Reminders Date** (`18`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date for follow-up reminders.
* **Follow-up Reminders Status** (`19`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the follow-up reminders status.
* **Follow-up Reminders Frequency Status** (`20`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the follow-up reminders frequency status.
* **Follow-up Reminders** (`21`, `number`, required: `false`): Set up the number of follow-up reminders for this payment.
* **Follow-up Reminders Next Reminder Date** (`22`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the next follow-up reminder.
* **Follow-up Reminders Notes** (`23`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any notes or comments for follow-up reminders.
* **Follow-up Reminders Status** (`24`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the follow-up reminders status.
* **Follow-up Reminders Notes** (`25`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any notes or comments for follow-up reminders.

## Tips
* Double-check the payment details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to set up follow-up reminders for payments that require it.
* Keep the follow-up reminders messages concise and clear.
* Review the outstanding payment tracker form regularly to ensure timely payments and updates.
* Contact the relevant department if you are unsure about a payment.
