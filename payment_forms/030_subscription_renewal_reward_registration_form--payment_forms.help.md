# Subscription Renewal Reward Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your subscription renewal reward. Please fill out the form with your personal details and reward preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "First Name" field with your first name.
2. Fill out the "Last Name" field with your last name.
3. Enter your "Email" address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Describe your rewards in the "Rewards" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Select your state in the "State" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
10. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
11. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
12. If you are registering a secondary reward, repeat steps 1-11 with the same fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Rewards, Address, City, State, Zip, Date of Birth, Note) labeled with "1" at the end of each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Rewards**: Describe your rewards.
* **Address**: Your address.
* **City**: Your city.
* **State**: Select your state.
* **Zip**: Your zip code.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes.
* **Email 1**: Your secondary reward's email address.
* **Phone 1**: Your secondary reward's phone number.
* **Rewards 1**: Describe your secondary reward.
* **Address 1**: Your secondary reward's address.
* **City 1**: Your secondary reward's city.
* **State 1**: Select your secondary reward's state.
* **Zip 1**: Your secondary reward's zip code.
* **Date of Birth 1**: Enter your secondary reward's date of birth.
* **Note 1**: Add any additional notes for your secondary reward.
* **Email 2**: Your third reward's email address.
* **Phone 2**: Your third reward's phone number.
* **Rewards 2**: Describe your third reward.
* **Address 2**: Your third reward's address.
* **City 2**: Your third reward's city.
* **State 2**: Select your third reward's state.
* **Zip 2**: Your third reward's zip code.
* **Date of Birth 2**: Enter your third reward's date of birth.
* **Note 2**: Add any additional notes for your third reward.
