# Short Term Rental Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests who are booking a short-term rental.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information and submit it. You can save your progress and come back later to finish the form if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Guest Name** (text, required/optional: false)
Enter the name of the guest, if applicable.

### **Check In Date** (date, required/optional: false)
Provide the date the guest will be checking in.

### **Check Out Date** (date, required/optional: false)
Provide the date the guest will be checking out.

### **Room Type** (select_one, required/optional: false)
Select the type of room the guest requires. Options are:
  - Studio
  - One Bedroom
  - Two Bedroom
  - Three Bedroom

### **Room Number** (number, required/optional: false)
Enter the number of the room the guest requires.

### **Room Description** (text, required/optional: false)
Provide a brief description of the room the guest requires.

### **Special Requests** (note, required/optional: false)
List any special requests or amenities the guest requires during their stay.

### **Payment Method** (select_one, required/optional: false)
Select the payment method the guest will use. Options are:
  - Credit Card
  - PayPal
  - Bank Transfer

### **Payment Amount** (number, required/optional: false)
Enter the payment amount for the booking.

### **Email** (email, required/optional: false)
Provide the email address of the guest.

### **Phone** (text, required/optional: false)
Enter the phone number of the guest.

### **Message** (text, required/optional: false)
Enter any additional message or comments for the host or guest.
