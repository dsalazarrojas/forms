# invoice_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details about customer payments, including their payment method, due date, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's name and other relevant information about them. This section is not required but can help with identifying the customer.
2. **Contact Info**: Enter the customer's contact information, which can include their address, phone number, or any other relevant contact details.
3. **Billing Info**: Enter the billing information for the customer's payment, which can include their billing address, invoice number, or any other relevant billing details.
4. **Payment Method**: Select the payment method the customer used to make the payment, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
5. **Payment Details**: Enter any relevant payment details, which can include the payment amount or any other payment-related information.
6. **Due Date**: Enter the due date for the payment, which is typically the date when the payment is expected.
7. **Payment Status**: Select the current payment status, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
8. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.
9. **Email**: Enter the customer's email address for communication.
10. **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for communication.
