# Charity Event Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Charity Event Delivery Form is designed to facilitate the delivery of goods and services to those in need. By completing this form, you will help us understand your charity's needs and ensure that your delivery is handled efficiently and securely.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your charity's name in the Organization Name field.
2. Provide your name and title in the Contact Person Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Contact Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Contact Phone field.
5. Choose the delivery date in the Delivery Date field.
6. Select the preferred delivery time in the Delivery Time field.
7. Enter the full address where you would like to receive the delivery in the Delivery Address field.
8. Select the type of delivery from the Type of Delivery dropdown menu.
9. Enter the estimated quantity of items or people to be delivered in the Estimated Quantity field.
10. If necessary, provide any special delivery requirements, such as access instructions or parking information, in the Special Requirements field.
11. Enter the name of the recipient in the Recipient Name field.
12. Enter the recipient's phone number in the Recipient Phone field.
13. Choose the payment method for the delivery from the Payment Method for Delivery dropdown menu.
14. If applicable, enter the delivery fee amount in the Delivery Fee Amount field.
15. Select whether a signature is required upon delivery from the Is Signature Required dropdown menu.
16. Optionally, provide additional notes about the delivery in the Additional Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name**: Enter your charity's full name, for example, "St. Jude's Food Bank".
- **Contact Person Name**: Your name and title, for example, "Jane Doe, Director".
- **Contact Email**: Enter your official email address for contact purposes.
- **Contact Phone**: Your primary phone number for contact.
- **Delivery Date**: Select a date for when you would like to receive the delivery.
- **Delivery Time**: Select a time for the delivery.
- **Delivery Address**: Enter the full address where you would like to receive the delivery.
- **Type of Delivery**: Choose the type of goods or services being delivered (e.g., Food and meals).
- **Estimated Quantity**: Enter the estimated number of items or people to be delivered.
- **Special Requirements**: If necessary, provide any special delivery instructions or accessibility information.
- **Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the delivery.
- **Recipient Phone**: Enter the recipient's phone number for contact.
- **Access Instructions**: If the delivery location has restricted access, provide instructions.
- **Parking Information**: If the delivery location has specific parking requirements, provide details.
- **Payment Method for Delivery**: Choose the payment method (e.g., Prepaid, Credit card on delivery).
- **Delivery Fee Amount**: If applicable, enter the fee amount for the delivery.
- **Is Signature Required**: Choose whether a signature is required upon delivery.
- **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional information about the delivery.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure efficient and secure delivery.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
