# End Of Year Payroll Data Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The End Of Year Payroll Data Form is used to collect and track employee data at the end of each year for payroll purposes. This data is used to calculate taxes, benefits, and other relevant employee costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the last day worked in the "Date Of Last Day Worked" field.
3. Enter the total hours worked for the year in the "Hours Worked" field.
4. Enter the wages paid to the employee for the year in the "Wages Paid" field.
5. Check the insurance options that apply to the employee (Health, Dental, Vision, Long Term Care, Other) in the respective fields.
6. Enter any additional benefits not listed above in the "Other Benefits" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date Of Last Day Worked** (date): Enter the date on which the employee's employment ended.
* **Hours Worked** (number): Enter the total number of hours worked by the employee for the year.
* **Wages Paid** (number): Enter the total wages paid to the employee for the year.
* **Health Insurance** (text): Check if the employee has health insurance coverage. If yes, enter the type of coverage (e.g. Individual, Family).
* **Dental Insurance** (text): Check if the employee has dental insurance coverage. If yes, enter the type of coverage (e.g. Individual, Family).
* **Vision Insurance** (text): Check if the employee has vision insurance coverage. If yes, enter the type of coverage (e.g. Individual, Family).
* **Long Term Care Insurance** (text): Check if the employee has long term care insurance coverage. If yes, enter the type of coverage (e.g. Individual, Family).
* **Other Insurance** (text): Check if the employee has any other insurance coverage not listed above. If yes, enter the type of coverage.
* **Other Benefits** (text): Enter any additional benefits not listed above, such as stock options, bonuses, etc.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure correct payroll calculations.
* If an employee has multiple insurance coverage types, select all that apply.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact HR or payroll administration for guidance.
