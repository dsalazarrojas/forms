# Financial Seminar Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Seminar Admission Form is designed to collect information from attendees and their representatives for the purpose of registering them for a financial seminar. This form is used to ensure that the necessary details are collected for the attendees' participation and to facilitate a smooth and organized seminar experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee's details, including their name, session selection, and session date.
2. Select the payment method and amount.
3. Provide your contact information and any necessary additional details.
4. Confirm your session attendance.
5. Specify the event location and time.
6. Fill in any additional event details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (Required): This field is for the name of the attendee(s) registering for the seminar.
* **Session Selection** (Required): Select the session you would like to attend from the available options.
* **Session Date** (Required): Choose the date of the session you selected.
* **Event Date** (Required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Contact Info** (Required): Provide your email and phone number so we can contact you.
* **Payment Method** (Required): Select the payment method you will be using (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Amount** (Required): Enter the amount you will be paying for the seminar.
* **Attendee Message** (Optional): If you have any comments or questions, you can include them here.
* **Event Message** (Optional): If you have any comments or feedback about the event, you can include them here.
* **Event Communication** (Optional): Select which communication methods you would like to be used (e.g., email, phone, email and phone).
* **Attendee Signature** (Optional): If you would like to sign your name, you can do so here.
* **Attendee Agreement** (Required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Event Agreement** (Optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the event terms and conditions.
* **Payment Status** (Required): Confirm your payment status (e.g., paid, pending, declined).
* **Session Attendance** (Required): Select which sessions you will be attending.
* **Event Location** (Required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Time** (Required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (Required): Enter the duration of the event in minutes.
* **Event Details** (Required): Fill in any additional details about the event.
* **Session Details** (Optional): If you have any comments or feedback about the session, you can include them here.
* **Attendee Info** (Required): Select which attendee information you would like to provide (e.g., name, email, phone number).
* **Event Notes** (Optional): If you have any comments or feedback about the event, you can include them here.
* **Event Organizer** (Optional): Select the event organizer.
* **Event Status** (Optional): Select the status of the event (e.g., scheduled, rescheduled, cancelled).
* **Event Comments** (Required): Fill in any comments about the event.
