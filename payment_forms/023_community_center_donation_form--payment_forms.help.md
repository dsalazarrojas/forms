# Community Center Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Center Donation Form is a tool for individuals or organizations to contribute to the community center's operations. This form gathers information about the donor and their donation, and is used to track and acknowledge donations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name or the name of your organization in the **Full Name of Donor** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone Number** field (optional).
4. Enter your mailing address in the **Mailing Address** field (Street, City, State, and Zip).
5. Select the type of donation you would like to make from the **Type of Donation** dropdown menu.
6. Enter the amount of your donation in the **Donation Amount** field.
7. Choose which fund you would like your donation to support from the **Which fund should your donation support** dropdown menu.
8. If this is a memorial or honorary gift, enter the name of the person being honored in the **If this is a memorial or honorary gift, please provide details** field.
9. Check whether you would like to remain anonymous or be listed in donor lists.
10. Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Personal Check, or Bank Transfer).
11. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the **Any Additional Comments or Instructions** field (optional).
12. Finally, confirm that you understand that a tax-deductible receipt will be sent to your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Donor** (id: 2): Enter your full name or the name of your organization.
* **Email Address** (id: 3): Enter your email address, which will be used for donation receipt and confirmation.
* **Contact Phone Number** (id: 4): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Mailing Address** (id: 5): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Type of Donation** (id: 7): Select from one-time gift, recurring monthly gift, annual pledge, or memorial/honorary gift.
* **Donation Amount** (id: 8): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Which fund should your donation support** (id: 9): Choose from General Operations, Youth Programs, Senior Services, Facility Renovation, Arts and Culture Programs, or Scholarshop Fund.
* **If this is a memorial or honorary gift, please provide details** (id: 10): Enter the name of the person being honored.
* **I wish to remain anonymous** (id: 11): Check to remain anonymous or be listed in donor lists.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (id: 13): Select from Credit Card (Online), PayPal, Personal Check, or Bank Transfer.
* **Any Additional Comments or Instructions** (id: 14): Enter any additional comments or instructions (optional).
* **Date of Donation** (id: 16): Confirm the date of your donation.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter** (id: 17): Check to receive the newsletter.
* **I understand that a tax-deductible receipt will be sent to my email** (id: 18): Confirm that you understand the process.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are not duplicating a previous donation.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or donation process, please contact the community center staff.
* A tax-deductible receipt will be sent to your email address after submitting the form.
