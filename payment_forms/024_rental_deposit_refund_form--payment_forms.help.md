# rental_deposit_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary details for handling rental deposit refunds. It helps tenants or property managers provide required information to facilitate the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form

### Please complete each section accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Details**
  (Tenant Details, text, optional): This section asks for basic information about the tenant, such as name, contact number, and address. Please fill in as much as you can to help the authority or department process your request.
* **Deposit Details**
  (Deposit Details, text, optional): Enter information about the deposit, including deposit amount and purpose (e.g., for a specific rental property).
* **Payment Details**
  (Payment Details, text, optional): If you are requesting a deposit refund, please provide details about the payment method used for the deposit (e.g., bank transfer, check). Be as clear as possible to avoid delays in the refund process.

### Tips
* If you are unsure about any information or have questions about the refund process, contact the relevant authority or department for assistance.
* Make sure to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to avoid delays or rejections.
* Keep a record of your submission for future reference.
