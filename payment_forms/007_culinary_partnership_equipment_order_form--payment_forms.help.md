# CulinaryPartnershipEquipmentOrderForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CulinaryPartnershipEquipmentOrderForm is a form used for partners to order equipment for their businesses. Fill out this form to place a new order with a partner's required information and equipment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "address line 1", "address line 2", "city", and "state" fields.
6. Enter your zip code in the "zip code" field. Note: This field accepts numbers only.
7. Select the type of equipment you wish to order from the "equipment type" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the quantity of the desired equipment in the "quantity" field.
9. Select the date for the equipment installation in the "date of installation" field. Note: This field accepts dates in MM/DD/YYYY format.
10. If you have any additional information or instructions, please enter it in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2 (optional).
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment you wish to order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the desired equipment.
* **Date of Installation**: Select the date for the equipment installation in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional information or instructions, enter it here.
