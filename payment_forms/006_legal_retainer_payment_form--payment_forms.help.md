# legal_retainer_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record and manage legal retainer payments from clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow these steps:
	* Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
	* Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
	* Select the retainer term ("Yes" or "No") in the "Retainer Term" field.
	* Select the retainer type (Flat Fee or Hourly Rate) in the "Retainer Type" field.
	* Enter the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field (optional).
	* Select the payment status (Draft, Under Review, or Paid) in the "Payment Status" field.
	* Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
	* Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
	* Select the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card) in the "Payment Method" field.
	* Enter any additional payment terms or notes in the "Payment Note" field.
	* Enter any additional terms or agreements in the "Additional Terms" field.
	* Enter the payment date and time in the "Payment Date" field.
	* Assign a user to manage the client's account in the "Assigned User" field.
	* Enter the assigned user's email address in the "Assigned User Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Retainer Term** (`retainer_term`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the retainer is active, "No" otherwise.
* **Retainer Type** (`retainer_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of retainer (Flat Fee or Hourly Rate).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, date, optional): Enter the payment terms.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the payment status (Draft, Under Review, or Paid).
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card).
* **Payment Note** (`payment_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional payment terms or notes.
* **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms or agreements.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, time, optional): Enter the payment date and time.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Assign a user to manage the client's account.
* **Assigned User Email** (`assigned_user_email`, email, optional): Enter the assigned user's email address.
