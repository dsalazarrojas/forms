# Custom Shed Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Shed Order Form is designed to collect customer information for custom shed orders. It ensures accurate and complete orders are received and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
2. **Order Type**: Select the type of shed you would like to order (Single or Double).
3. **Shed Size**: Choose the size of the shed you need.
4. **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
5. **Customer Phone**: Enter your primary phone number, which we will use to contact you.
6. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on your order status.
7. **Shed Color**: If you have a color preference for your shed, enter it here (optional).
8. **Order Details**: Add any additional notes about your order.
9. **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method you would like to use (Credit Card or PayPal).
10. **Customer Address**: Enter your complete address for accurate shipping and delivery.
11. **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments about your order (optional).
12. **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter your postal code for accurate shipping and delivery.
13. **City**: Enter the city you reside in or would like to receive the shed.
14. **State**: Enter your state or province.
15. **Country**: Enter your country of residence (if applicable).
16. **Phone Number 1**: Enter your primary phone number, which we will use to contact you.
