# Vacation rental billing form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vacation rental billing form is used to collect and record payment information for vacation rentals. This form is essential for accurate financial reporting and to ensure that all rental payments are properly registered and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are using the form for a vacation rental billing purpose. If you are unsure, please review the form's purpose and requirements to confirm.
2.  Complete each field with the required information.
3.  Review and confirm the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Payment Information (Page 1)** 
    (`payment_information`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for collecting payment details. Enter the necessary payment information for the vacation rental.
*   **Rental Details (Page 1)** 
    (`rental_details`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for collecting rental specific details. Enter any relevant rental-related information here.
*   **Contact Information (Page 1)** 
    (`contact_information`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for entering contact information. Enter the relevant contact details of the person paying the rental fee.
*   **Payment Method (Page 1)** 
    (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field is for selecting the payment method. Choose the payment method used for the rental (Yes, No).
*   **Payment Terms (Page 1)** 
    (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for entering payment terms. Enter any special payment terms or conditions.
*   **Billing Address (Page 1)** 
    (`billing_address`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for entering the billing address. Enter the billing address of the person paying the rental fee.
*   **Guest Details (Page 1)** 
    (`guest_details`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for entering guest details. Enter any relevant guest information.
*   **Total Rent (Page 2)** 
    (`total_rent`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is for entering the total rent amount. Enter the total rent amount for the vacation rental.
*   **Tax Included (Page 2)** 
    (`tax_included`, `select_one`, required: `false`): This field is for selecting whether tax is included in the rent. Choose whether tax is included (0, 1).
*   **Tax Amount (Page 2)** 
    (`tax_amount`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is for entering the tax amount. Enter the tax amount if tax is not included in the rent.
*   **Additional Fees (Page 2)** 
    (`additional_fees`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is for entering additional fees. Enter any additional fees charged for the rental.
*   **Payment Status (Page 2)** 
    (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): This field is for selecting the payment status. Choose the payment status (Active, Inactive).
*   **Payment Method (Page 2)** 
    (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field is for selecting the payment method. Choose the payment method used for the rental (Yes, No).
