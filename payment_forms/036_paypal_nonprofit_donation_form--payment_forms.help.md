# PayPal Nonprofit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Nonprofit Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors for internal tracking and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Donor Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Donor Email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Donor Phone` field.
4. Specify the amount of the donation in the `Donation Amount` field.
5. Choose a donation frequency from the options provided.
6. Select a preferred payment method from the available options.
7. If desired, add comments in the `Donor Comment` field.
8. Select the level of satisfaction with the service.
9. Optionally, enter a date in the `Date` field.
10. Optionally, enter a time in the `Time` field.
11. Click the `Submit` button to send the form.
