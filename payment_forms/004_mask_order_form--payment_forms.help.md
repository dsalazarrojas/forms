# Mask Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mask Order Form is a simple text-based form designed to collect contact, mask, and order information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information on page 1.
2. Specify the mask details on page 2.
3. Provide order information on page 3.
4. Enter shipping details on page 4.
5. Add payment information on page 5.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`page_1`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Mask Details** (`page_2`, `text`, `Optional`): Specify the type, color, and other details about the mask you want to order.
* **Order Information** (`page_3`, `text`, `Optional`): Provide any additional information about your order, such as any custom requests.
* **Shipping Information** (`page_4`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter your shipping address, including your name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Information** (`page_5`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter your payment method and any other relevant payment information.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors or miscommunications.
* Ensure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
