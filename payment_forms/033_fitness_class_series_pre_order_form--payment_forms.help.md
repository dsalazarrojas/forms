# Fitness Class Series Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to pre-order a fitness class series by providing your schedule preferences, participant preferences, payment details, and confirm session information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the schedule preferences, including the days you are interested in attending.
2. Provide your participant preferences, including your participant type and any additional participant preferences.
3. Enter your payment details, including your chosen payment method and payment date.
4. Confirm your session schedule and confirm that you have a schedule conflict or not.
5. Review and confirm your participant's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Schedule Preferences** (`schedule_preferences`, `text`, required: false): This section allows you to select the days you are interested in attending the fitness class series.
	+ Please select all the days you are interested in attending from the options: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, `text`, required: false): This section allows you to specify any additional participant preferences.
	+ Enter any additional information about your participant preferences.
- **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required: false): This section allows you to enter your payment details.
	+ Choose your payment method from the options: Credit card, PayPal, Bank transfer.
	+ Enter your payment date.
- **Confirm Session** (`confirm_session`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to confirm your session schedule.
	+ Check if you have a schedule conflict or not.
- **Participant Acknowledgments** (`participant_acknowledgements`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to confirm that you have read and understand the participant acknowledgments.
	+ Review and confirm your participant's name.
- **Schedule Preferences Schedule** (`schedule_preferences_schedule`, `select_one`, required: false): This section is used to select the schedule for your preferred schedule.
	+ Select all the days you are interested in attending from the options: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun.
- **Participant Preferences Participant Type** (`participant_preferences_participant_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section is used to select the participant type.
	+ Choose from the options: Male, Female, Other.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_details_payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section is used to select your payment method.
	+ Choose from the options: Credit card, PayPal, Bank transfer.
- **Payment Date** (`payment_details_payment_date`, `date`, required: false): This section is used to enter your payment date.
- **Schedule Conflict** (`confirm_session_schedule_conflict`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section is used to confirm if you have a schedule conflict or not.
	+ Choose from the options: No conflict, Conflicted.
- **Confirm Session Confirmation** (`confirm_session_confirmation`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to confirm your session confirmation.
- **Confirmed** (`confirm_session_confirmed`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to confirm that your session is confirmed.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_acknowledgements_participant_name`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to enter your participant's name.

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
