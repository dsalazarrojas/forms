# Cancel E-Transfer Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the cancellation of an e-transfer, which is an electronic transfer of funds between banks. This form helps us process your cancellation request and ensures that all necessary information is provided to facilitate the cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Provide a valid reason for cancelling the e-transfer in the "Reason for Cancellation" field.
3. Confirm that you are the authorized account holder.
4. Review and confirm that you understand the processing timeline and fees associated with the cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your name as registered with your financial institution.
* **Bank Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter your primary account number.
* **Registered Email Address** (`registered_email`, email, required): Enter the email linked to your bank account.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Transaction Details** (`transaction_details_section`, note, required): This section is used to provide context for your e-transfer, including the transaction date and amount.
*   **Transaction Reference Number** (`transaction_reference`, text, required): Enter the transaction reference number found in your e-transfer confirmation notification.
*   **Recipient Contact Info** (`recipient_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the original transfer recipient.
*   **Amount of Transfer** (`transfer_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the transfer in CAD.
*   **Date of Original Transfer** (`original_transfer_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sent the funds.
*   **Request Info** (`request_info_section`, note, required): Provide a brief explanation for why you are cancelling the e-transfer.
*   **Reason for Cancellation** (`cancellation_reason`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided reasons for cancelling the e-transfer.
*   **Elaborate on Reason** (`reason_elaboration`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the situation if necessary.
*   **Security Answer Provided** (`security_answer`, text, required): Enter the answer you provided for the e-transfer recipient.
*   **Confirmation** (`confirmation_section`, note, required): Review and sign this form to confirm your request.
*   **Authorized Account Holder Confirmation** (`authorization_confirm`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are the authorized account holder.
*   **Agreement to Processing Fees** (`fee_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree or disagree with the processing fees associated with the cancellation.
*   **Acknowledgment of Processing Timeline** (`timeline_ack`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand it may take up to 5 business days for the funds to reappear in your account.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
