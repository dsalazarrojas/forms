# refund_request_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The refund_request_verification_form is used to verify refund requests from customers, ensuring that all necessary information is provided to process their requests accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the refund_request_verification_form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form header for any important instructions or guidelines.
2.  Enter your customer name in the customer_name field.
3.  Provide the order ID in the order_id field.
4.  Select the relevant product ID from the product_id field.
5.  Enter a brief reason for the refund request in the reason field.
6.  Upload or provide links to any supporting documents in the supporting_document fields.
7.  Enter any additional comments in the additional_comments field.
8.  Click the Save and Submit button to save your progress and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Form Header**: The form header provides an introduction and guidelines for completing the form.
*   **customer_name**: Enter the name of the customer who is requesting the refund.
*   **order_id**: Provide the ID number of the order being refunded.
*   **product_id**: Select the product ID from the provided options to associate with the refund request.
*   **reason**: Enter a brief reason for the refund request.
*   **supporting_document_1**: Upload or provide a link to a supporting document for the refund request.
*   **supporting_document_2** to **supporting_document_15**: Continue uploading or providing links to additional supporting documents as needed.
*   **additional_comments**: Enter any additional comments or information related to the refund request.
*   **Submit**: Save and submit the form to complete the refund request.
