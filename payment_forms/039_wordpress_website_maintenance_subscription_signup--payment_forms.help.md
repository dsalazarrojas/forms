# WordPress Website Maintenance Subscription Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to subscribe to website maintenance services. The gathered details help our team tailor plans to their specific needs and stay in touch with users for updates or questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Company Name (if applicable) in their respective fields.
2. Enter your Email Address.
3. Select the desired Plan (Monthly) frequency from the drop-down menu.
4. Choose the Payment Method (PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check).
5. If necessary, provide any specific Payment Terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter its name.
* **Plan (Monthly)** (`plan_monthly`, select_multiple, required): Select from Monthly Payment Plan, 6 Months Payment Plan, or 12 Months Payment Plan to determine how often you want to pay for our services.
* **First Payment** (`first_payment`, date, optional): If you want to specify a particular date for your first payment, enter it here.
* **Recurring Payment** (`recurring_payment`, date, optional): If you want to set a recurring payment schedule, enter the date here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to make your payments: PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): If you have any specific requirements or agreements regarding payment, enter them here.
