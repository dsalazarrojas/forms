# Free Health Checkup Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to provide donation details for free health checkup services. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your donation is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Donation details**: This section is where you provide the specifics of your donation. Enter your details accurately to avoid any confusion in the processing of your donation.
2. **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the provided options. This will determine how your donation will be processed.
3. **Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate. This will help us understand your generous contribution.
4. **Email**: If you have an email address, please provide it for any follow-up communication or updates on your donation.
5. **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, please provide it for any further communication or to receive updates on your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation details**: 
	+ Name: `donation_details`
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Explanation: Enter the amount and purpose of your donation. This is a required field.
* **Payment method**:
	+ Name: `payment_method`
	+ Type: Select one from the provided options (Credit card, Bank transfer, Other)
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Explanation: Select the method through which you wish to donate (Credit card, Bank transfer, Other).
* **Amount**:
	+ Name: `amount`
	+ Type: Number field
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Explanation: Enter the exact amount you wish to donate.
* **Email**:
	+ Name: `email`
	+ Type: Email field
	+ Required: No
	+ Explanation: If you have an email address, please enter it for any follow-up communication or updates on your donation.
* **Phone Number**:
	+ Name: `phone`
	+ Type: Text field for phone number
	+ Required: No
	+ Explanation: If you have a phone number, please enter it for any further communication or to receive updates on your donation.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Select a payment method that is suitable for you.
* Enter your contact information if you wish to receive updates or follow-up communication.
