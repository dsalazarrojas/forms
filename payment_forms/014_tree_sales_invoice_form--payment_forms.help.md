# Tree Sales Invoice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tree Sales Invoice Form is used to create a sales invoice for tree sales, ensuring accurate and timely payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form on page 1. Make sure to review the form before proceeding.
2. On page 2, fill out the customer information fields: customer info.
3. On page 3, enter the tree information: tree info.
4. On page 4, select the date the tree was delivered: date delivered.
5. Finally, enter the amount due for the sale: amount due.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's name and any relevant details to assist with payment processing.
* **Tree Info**: Enter the details of the tree being sold, including any relevant identification numbers or descriptions.
* **Date Delivered**: Select the date the tree was delivered to the customer.
* **Amount Due**: Enter the total amount due for the sale, in numerical format.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any information, please reach out to the sales team for assistance.
* This form is for sales transactions only; if you're seeking a refund, please use the refund form.
* If you've already submitted a sales invoice for this transaction, please do not resubmit; contact the sales team instead.
