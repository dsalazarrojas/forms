# Tuition Payment Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tuition Payment Validation Form is used to validate tuition payments for students. It collects information about the student, payment details, and other relevant data to ensure accurate and secure processing of payments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter Student ID**: Enter the student ID of the student making the payment.
2. **Enter Student Name**: Type the name of the student making the payment.
3. **Enter Student School**: Enter the name of the student's school.
4. **Enter Student ID**: Enter the ID of the student making the payment.
5. **Enter Tuition Amount**: Enter the amount of the tuition payment.
6. **Enter Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment.
7. **Select Payment Method**: Choose how the payment was made (Cash, Credit Card, Online Banking).
8. **Select Payment Status**: Choose the status of the payment (Confirmed, Pending, Rejected).
9. **Add Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about the payment.
10. **Enter Email**: Enter the email of the student or person making the payment (optional).
11. **Enter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the student or person making the payment (optional).
12. **Enter Tuition Term**: Enter the tuition term for which the payment is being made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (1, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the user making the payment.
* **Student ID** (2, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the student making the payment.
* **Tuition Amount** (3, number, required): Enter the amount of the tuition payment.
* **Payment Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (5, select_one, optional): Choose how the payment was made (Cash, Credit Card, Online Banking).
* **Payment Status** (6, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the payment (Confirmed, Pending, Rejected).
* **Notes** (7, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the payment.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): Enter the email of the student or person making the payment.
* **Phone** (9, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the student or person making the payment.
* **Student Name** (10, text, required): Enter the name of the student making the payment.
* **Student School** (11, text, required): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Tuition Term** (12, text, required): Enter the tuition term for which the payment is being made.
