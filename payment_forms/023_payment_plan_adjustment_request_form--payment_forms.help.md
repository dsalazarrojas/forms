# Payment Plan Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers request adjustments to their existing payment plans. It allows them to specify the type of adjustment they want to make, the amount involved, and any relevant notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as the customer making the request.
2. Select the type of adjustment you want to make: Payment Plan Adjustment, Cancellation Adjustment, or Refund Adjustment.
3. If applicable, enter the amount you want to adjust.
4. Choose the payment method you would like to use for the adjustment.
5. Add any additional notes about the adjustment.
6. Click the Submit button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Adjustment Type** (`adjustment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of adjustment you want to make: Payment Plan Adjustment, Cancellation Adjustment, or Refund Adjustment.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the specific amount you want to adjust.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want to apply the adjustment to, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the payment method you would like to use for the adjustment: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any specific payment terms you want to follow.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the adjustment.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to send your request for payment plan adjustment.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact your customer support for assistance.
- Ensure that you have a valid payment plan in place before submitting this form.
- Adjustments are reviewed and processed within a reasonable timeframe after submission. You will receive an email notification once the adjustment has been processed.
