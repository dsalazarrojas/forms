# Gas Bill Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment details and customer information for gas bill payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Payment Details** page with the required information.
2.  Move on to the **Customer Information** page and fill out the necessary details.
3.  Finally, select the desired **Payment Method** for the bill payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Details** (payment_details, text, required/optional): This page is where you will enter the essential details of your gas bill payment, such as due date, amount, and any other relevant information that you want to provide.
* **Customer Information** (customer_info, text, required/optional): This page is where you will enter your personal customer information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details that you want to provide.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, text, required/optional): This page is where you select the method of payment you would like to use for your gas bill payment.
