# simple_product_purchase_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers when they place an order for a product. It helps us understand the customer's order details and process it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product Name** in the "Product Name" field. This is the name of the product the customer is ordering.
2. Enter the **Product Price** in the "Product Price" field. This is the price of the product the customer is ordering.
3. Enter the **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field. This is the name of the customer placing the order.
4. Enter the **Order Quantity** in the "Order Quantity" field. This is the number of products the customer is ordering.
5. Enter the **Customer Email** in the "Customer Email" field. This is the email address of the customer.
6. Enter the **Customer Phone** in the "Customer Phone" field. This is the phone number of the customer.
7. Enter the **Product Image URL** in the "Product Image URL" field. This is the URL of the product image.
8. Enter a brief **Product Description** in the "Product Description" field. This is a brief description of the product being ordered.
9. Enter the **Customer Address** in the "Customer Address" field. This is the address of the customer.
10. Select the **Customer City** from the dropdown list in the "Customer City" field. This is the city where the customer is located.
11. Select the **Customer State/Province** from the dropdown list in the "Customer State/Province" field. This is the state or province where the customer is located.
12. Enter the **Customer Zip** in the "Customer Zip" field. This is the zip code of the customer.
13. Enter a brief **Form Comments** in the "Form Comments" field. This is any additional comments the customer wants to add.
14. Select the **Order Date** in the "Order Date" field. This is the date when the order was placed.
15. Select the **Order Time** in the "Order Time" field. This is the time when the order was placed.
16. Select the order status from the list in the "Order Status" field. This determines the status of the order.
17. Enter the **Customer Country** in the "Customer Country" field. This is the country where the customer is located.
18. Select the **Customer State/Province** from the dropdown list in the "Customer State/Province" field. This is the state or province where the customer is located.
19. Enter the **Order Notes** in the "Order Notes" field. This is any additional notes about the order.
20. Enter the **Order Total** in the "Order Total" field. This is the total cost of the order.
21. Select the **Payment Method** from the dropdown list in the "Payment Method" field. This determines how the customer will pay for the order.
22. Enter the **Customer Shipping** in the "Customer Shipping" field. This is the shipping address of the customer.
23. Enter the **Customer Tax ID** in the "Customer Tax ID" field. This is the tax ID of the customer.
24. Select the **Order Date/Time** in the "Order Date/Time" field. This is the date and time when the order was placed.
25. Enter the **Customer IP** in the "Customer IP" field. This is the IP address of the customer.
