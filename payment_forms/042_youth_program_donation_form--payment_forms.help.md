# Youth Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Program Donation Form is designed to collect donation information from donors who wish to support a youth program. This form will help us gather essential details about the donor, the donation amount, and payment information to process your donation efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Donor Information section with your details.
2. Provide the donation amount and any additional information related to your donation.
3. Enter your payment information to process the payment successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (donator_info)**: This is where you provide details about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
* **Donation Information (donation_info)**: This is where you specify the amount you'd like to donate and any additional notes or comments you have about your donation.
* **Payment Information (payment_info)**: This is where you enter your payment details to process the payment successfully. Please ensure that you enter accurate payment information to avoid any processing issues.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
