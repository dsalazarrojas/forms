# Cultural Event Vendor Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cultural Event Vendor Check Out Form" is a crucial tool for vendors to confirm their payment status for a cultural event. This form helps event organizers gather essential details from vendors, ensuring accurate payment records and facilitating event management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Vendor Name** (<vendor_name>).
2. Enter the **Event ID** (<event_id>).
3. Provide the name and contact details of the **Contact Person** (<contact_person>).
4. Enter the **Contact Email** (<contact_email>).
5. Enter the **Contact Phone Number** (<contact_phone>).
6. Confirm the **Total Payment** (<total_payment>).
7. Select the **Payment Method** (<payment_method>), which can be Credit, Debit, or Other.
8. Enter the **Payment Amount** (<payment_amount>).
9. Confirm the **Payment Status** (<payment_status>), which can be Paid, Not Paid, or Partially Paid.
10. Enter any additional **Note** (<note>).
11. Select the **Assigned Tool**, if applicable, from the options: Chatjimmy, Jenny, or Other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name**: Enter your name or the name of your company/organization. This is a required field.
* **Event ID**: Enter the ID number for the cultural event you are participating in. This is a required field.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for communication with you. This field is required.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. This field is required.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. This field is required.
* **Total Payment**: Enter the total amount paid or due for the event. This field is required.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you have made the payment, which can be Credit, Debit, or Other. This field is required.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount paid or due for the event. This field is required.
* **Payment Status**: Confirm the payment status, which can be Paid, Not Paid, or Partially Paid. This field is required.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the payment process. This field is optional.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, select the tool assigned to you for the event, which can be Chatjimmy, Jenny, or Other. This field is not required.
* **Assigned Tool** (if not required): If the field is not applicable to you, simply leave this field blank, as it is not a required field.
