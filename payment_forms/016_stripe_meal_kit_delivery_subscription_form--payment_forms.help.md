Stripe Meal Kit Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
=================================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and subscription preferences for meal kit delivery. It is meant to be completed by customers who wish to subscribe to a meal kit delivery service. The form gathers necessary details such as user contact information, payment methods, and subscription preferences to enable the service to deliver meal kits accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill out the "User info" section with your name and any other relevant details.
3. Choose a subscription plan ("Yes" or "No") in the "Subscription plan" section.
4. Enter your billing address in the "Billing address" section.
5. Select a delivery date in the "Delivery date" section.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" section.
7. Enter your email in the "Email" section.
8. (Optional) Choose your payment method ("Yes" or "No").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User info** (`user_info`): Text Field
The "User info" section is where you can enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.

*   **User info** (user_info, Text Field, **required**)
    This field is a text field where you can enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.

### 2. **Payment info** (`payment_info`): Number Field
This field is intended for numerical input, but you can leave it blank if you don't have any payment information.

*   **Payment info** (payment_info, Number Field, **optional**)
    You can enter any numerical information you have related to your payment, but it is not required.

### 3. **Subscription plan** (`subscription_plan`): Select One Field
You have two options for subscription plans: "Yes" and "No". Please select the option that best suits your needs.

*   **Subscription plan** (subscription_plan, Select One Field, **required**)
    Choose whether you want to subscribe to the meal kit delivery service.

### 4. **Billing address** (`billing_address`): Text Field
Enter your billing address here.

*   **Billing address** (billing_address, Text Field, **required**)
    Enter the address where you would like to receive your meal kits.

### 5. **Delivery date** (`delivery_date`): Date Field
Select a delivery date for your meal kits.

*   **Delivery date** (delivery_date, Date Field, **required**)
    Choose the date you would like to receive your meal kits.

### 6. **Phone number** (`phone`): Text Field
Enter your phone number.

*   **Phone number** (phone, Text Field, **required**)
    Enter the phone number you would like to associate with your subscription.

### 7. **Email** (`email`): Email Field
Enter your email address.

*   **Email** (email, Email Field, **required**)
    Enter the email address you would like to associate with your subscription.

### 8. **Payment method** (`payment_method`): Select Multiple Field
You have two options for payment methods: "Yes" and "No". Please select the option that best suits your needs.

*   **Payment method** (payment_method, Select Multiple Field, **optional**)
    Choose your preferred payment method (if applicable)
