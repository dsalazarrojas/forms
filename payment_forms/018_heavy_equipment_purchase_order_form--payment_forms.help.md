# Heavy Equipment Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about heavy equipment purchases, including contact information, shipping details, and order specifics. It ensures accurate and efficient processing of purchase orders for heavy equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Contact Info" page and fill in the following:
   * **Contact Info**: Enter the buyer's name, company name, and contact information for easy communication.
2. Proceed to the next page and enter the following:
   * **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this purchase.
   * **Shipping Address**: Enter the buyer's shipping address for accurate delivery.
3. Choose the payment method from the drop-down menu:
   * **Payment Method**: Select the payment option that suits your needs.
4. Enter the following details on the next page:
   * **Equipment Type**: Choose the type of heavy equipment being purchased.
   * **Order Date and Time**: Enter the date and time the order is due.
   * **Equipment Model**: Select the specific model of the equipment being purchased.
   * **Order Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the equipment ordered.
   * **Order Description**: Enter a brief description of the order.
5. Continue to the next page and enter the following:
   * **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the purchase, including currency.
   * **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the person responsible for this purchase.
   * **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for this purchase.
6. Select the status of the equipment from the drop-down menu:
   * **Equipment Number**: Choose the specific number of the equipment being purchased.
   * **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the equipment being purchased.
7. Choose the shipping method for the equipment:
   * **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method that suits your needs.
8. Enter the priority and due date of the order:
   * **Order Priority**: Enter the priority level of the order.
   * **Order Due Date**: Enter the date the order is due.
9. Finally, enter the total cost including tax:
   * **Total Cost Including Tax**: Enter the total cost including tax.
