# Simmer Pot Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a simmer pot. Please fill out the form with your order details, payment preferences, and delivery instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by entering a brief description of the order in the "Simmer Pot Purchase" field.
- Provide more details about your order in the "Order Details" field.
- Choose your preferred payment method(s) in the "Payment Methods" field.
- Select your preferred delivery options in the "Delivery Preferences" field.
- Enter your preferred delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
- Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
- Select your preferred payment method for the order in the "Payment Method 1", "Payment Method 2", and "Payment Method 3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Simmer Pot Purchase**:
  - This field is used to enter a brief description of the order.
  - Example: "Order of 5 simmer pots with special seasoning".
- **Order Details**:
  - This field is used to provide more details about the order.
  - Example: "Deliver to the kitchen counter, please handle with care".
- **Payment Preferences**:
  - This field is used to specify your preferred payment options.
  - Example: "Credit card, PayPal".
- **Payment Methods**:
  - This field is used to select your preferred payment methods for the order.
  - Options:
    + Yes: You will pay for the order using this method.
    + No: You will not pay for the order using this method.
- **Delivery Preferences**:
  - This field is used to specify your preferred delivery options.
  - Options:
    + Yes: You want to receive the order by email.
    + No: You do not want to receive the order by email.
- **Delivery Address**:
  - This field is used to enter the address where you want the order delivered.
  - Example: "123 Main Street, Anytown, USA 12345".
- **Delivery Date**:
  - This field is used to enter the date you want to receive the order.
  - Format: MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Delivery Time**:
  - This field is used to enter the time you want to receive the order.
  - Format: HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Payment Amount**:
  - This field is used to enter the payment amount for the order.
  - Example: "10.00".
- **Payment Method 1**, **Payment Method 2**, **Payment Method 3**:
  - These fields are used to select your preferred payment methods for the order.
  - Options:
    + Yes: You will pay using this method.
    + No: You will not pay using this method.
