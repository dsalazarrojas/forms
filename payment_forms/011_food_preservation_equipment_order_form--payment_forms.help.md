# food_preservation_equipment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The food_preservation_equipment_order_form is designed to help customers order food preservation equipment, such as freezers, refrigerators, or other related products. This form is likely used by businesses or stores that sell food preservation equipment to their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required information on the "equipment_list" page, listing the equipment you are interested in ordering.
2. Proceed to the "customer_info" page and fill in your contact and order details.
3. Review and confirm your order on the "payment_info" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **equipment_list** (`text`, optional): List the food preservation equipment you are interested in ordering.
* **customer_info** (`text`, optional): Provide your contact information and order details, including any relevant notes or comments.
* **payment_info** (`text`, optional): Enter your payment information for the order.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or need assistance with your order, contact us at contact: form@gic.mx.
- Make sure to double-check your order details to ensure accuracy.
- If you need to order additional items, you can repeat the form or contact us directly.
