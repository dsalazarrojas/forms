# Sanitizer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sanitizer Order Form is used to process orders for sanitizers. Please provide the required information for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text): Enter your name for the order.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Quantity** (Number): Specify the quantity of sanitizers you would like to order.
* **Payment Method** (Select): Choose how you would like to make the payment:
	+ PayPal
	+ Stripe
* **Order Comments** (Note): Provide any comments about your order.
* **Shipping Address** (Text): Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **Order Date** (Date): Specify the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Shipping Time** (Time): Specify the desired shipping time.
* **Order Total** (Number): The total cost of the order will be automatically calculated.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text): Specify the tool you would like to be assigned for the order.
* **App ID** (Text): Enter your App ID for identification purposes.
* **Form ID** (Text): This field will automatically be populated with the current form ID.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to avoid errors in the form submission process.
* Be cautious with language input, as this form can be filled out in multiple languages.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
