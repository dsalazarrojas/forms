# online_taxi_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for users to book a taxi service, providing details for pick up and drop off addresses, dates, times, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your pick up address in the "Pick up address" field.
2. Enter your drop off address in the "Drop off address" field.
3. Choose your pick up date using the calendar provided in the "Pick up date" field.
4. Select your pick up time using the time picker provided in the "Pick up time" field.
5. Enter the taxi fare in the "Taxi fare" field.
6. Choose the type of trip by selecting one or more options in the "Trip type" field (e.g., "Trip to work", "Trip home", or "Social trip").
7. Select the payment method by choosing one of the options in the "Payment method" field (e.g., "Credit card", "PayPal", or "Bank transfer").
8. Add any additional notes about your trip in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
11. Enter any assigned tool or equipment in the "Assigned tool" field.
12. Enter the name of the assigned user in the "Assigned user" field.
13. Enter the form ID in the "Form ID" field.
14. Enter the category of the trip in the "Category" field.
15. Enter a description of the trip in the "Description" field.
16. Enter the created date and time of the booking in the "Created at" field.
17. Enter the updated date and time of the booking in the "Updated at" field.
18. Enter the output file name in the "Output file" field.
19. Enter a title for the booking in the "Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pick up address**: Enter the address where you want to be picked up.
* **Drop off address**: Enter the address where you want to be dropped off.
* **Pick up date**: Choose the date for your pick up.
* **Pick up time**: Choose the time for your pick up.
* **Taxi fare**: Enter the fare for your trip.
* **Trip type**: Select one or more of the following options: "Trip to work", "Trip home", or "Social trip".
* **Payment method**: Select one of the following options: "Credit card", "PayPal", or "Bank transfer".
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your trip.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned tool**: Enter any assigned tool or equipment.
* **Assigned user**: Enter the name of the assigned user.
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID.
* **Category**: Enter the category of the trip.
* **Description**: Enter a description of the trip.
* **Created at**: Enter the created date and time of the booking.
* **Updated at**: Enter the updated date and time of the booking.
* **Output file**: Enter the output file name.
* **Title**: Enter a title for the booking.

This form collects information for taxi service bookings, ensuring that users provide necessary details for a smooth and efficient experience.
