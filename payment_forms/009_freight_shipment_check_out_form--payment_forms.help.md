# Freight Shipment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Freight Shipment Check Out Form is a general-purpose form for users to provide information about a freight shipment. This form is designed to collect data for payment-related tasks. It can be used for various purposes, such as generating invoices or tracking payment status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title and description. The form title will appear at the top of the form, and the description will provide additional context about the form's purpose.
2. Select a category for the form from the options provided. For payment-related tasks, please select "Payment Forms" as the default category.
3. Enter the date and time of the shipment. This will help in tracking the shipment's timeline.
4. Provide any additional notes about the shipment if necessary.
5. Enter the email address and phone number of the person or organization associated with the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): The form title is a brief description of the shipment. This will appear at the top of the form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required): This is a longer description of the shipment. Please provide a detailed description of the shipment and its purpose.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Please select a category for the form from the options provided. This helps in organizing the form according to its purpose. 
*   Select "Payment Forms" for payment-related tasks.
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the shipment.
* **Form Time** (`form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the shipment.
* **Form Notes** (`form_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the shipment.
* **Form Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization associated with the shipment.
* **Form Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization associated with the shipment.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
*   Be as detailed as possible when filling in the form description and notes.
*   Use the correct category to ensure the form is used for the intended purpose.
*   Double-check the date and time of the shipment for accuracy.
