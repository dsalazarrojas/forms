# jazz_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jazz Event Registration Form is used to collect user information and event details for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the registration process.
2. Fill out the User Info section with your basic contact details.
3. Move on to the Event Data section to input the event-related information.
4. In the Payment Method section, select your preferred payment method (Cash, Card, or Bank Transfer).
5. Provide your Email and Phone Number details, as they are required fields.
6. Finally, add any additional notes about your registration in the Note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your basic contact details (Name, Address, etc.).
- **Event Data** (`text`, required/optional): Provide details about the event you're registering for (Event name, Date, etc.).
- **Payment Method** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method (Cash, Card, or Bank Transfer).
- **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`text`, required): Enter your Phone Number for communication purposes.
- **Note** (`note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your registration (e.g., special requests or preferences).
