# stripe_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations using Stripe payment processing, allowing users to easily and securely make payments. The form collects relevant information and options to facilitate a smooth payment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address on **stripe_donation_form_page_1**. This is the first step to create a payment profile.
2. Select the desired amount on **stripe_donation_form_page_6**. You can choose between '5' or '10'.
3. On **stripe_donation_form_page_7**, select the payment method you prefer, multiple options are available.
4. Enter any additional information you'd like to provide on **stripe_donation_form_page_25**. This will help us understand your preferences.
5. Review your payment details and confirm on **stripe_donation_form_page_28**.
