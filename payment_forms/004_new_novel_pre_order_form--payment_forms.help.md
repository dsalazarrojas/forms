# New Novel Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to pre-order a new book. The information collected will be used to process the order.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
- Enter your book title in the "Book Title" field.
- Enter your name in the "Author" field.
- Select "Signed" or "Gift" in the "Signed or Gift" field if applicable.
- Enter your shipping name in the "Shipping Name" field.
- Enter your shipping email address in the "Shipping Email" field.
- Enter your shipping phone number in the "Shipping Phone" field (optional).
- Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field (optional).
- Enter your shipping city in the "Shipping City" field (optional).
- Enter your shipping state in the "Shipping State" field (optional).
- Enter your shipping zip code in the "Shipping Zip" field.
- Select the country where the book will be shipped from in the "Shipping Country" field.
- If desired, select a shipping date in the "Shipping Date" field.
- Select the shipping method from the "Shipping Method" field (optional).
- If needed, provide additional comments about your order in the "Order Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Book Title** (Text, required): Enter the title of the book you'd like to pre-order.
* **Author** (Text, required): Enter the name of the author of the book.
* **Signed or Gift** (Select Multiple, optional): Check if you'd like the book to be signed or gifted.
* **Shipping Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the person or company you'd like to ship the book to.
* **Shipping Email** (Text, required): Enter the email address of the person or company you'd like to ship the book to.
* **Shipping Phone** (Text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or company you'd like to ship the book to.
* **Shipping Address** (Text, optional): Enter the street address where you'd like the book to be shipped.
* **Shipping City** (Text, optional): Enter the city where the book will be shipped.
* **Shipping State** (Text, optional): Enter the state where the book will be shipped.
* **Shipping Zip** (Text, required): Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (Select One, required): Select the country where the book will be shipped from.
* **Shipping Date** (Date, optional): Select the date you'd like the book to be shipped.
* **Shipping Method** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the shipping method you'd like to use.
* **Order Comments** (Text, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests you'd like to include with your order.
