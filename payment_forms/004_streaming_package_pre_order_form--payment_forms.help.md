# streaming_package_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The streaming package preorder form is used to process a streaming package with a customer. It helps you collect necessary information to create or reserve a streaming plan for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired streaming plan(s) on the **streaming_plan** field.
2. Choose the start date of the streaming plan in the **start_date** field.
3. Provide a brief description of the customer in the **customer_details** field.
4. Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number in the **customer_name**, **customer_email**, and **customer_phone** fields respectively.
5. Add any additional information about the customer in the **customer_note** field.
6. Choose the action to take after form submission from the **form_submission_actions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Streaming plan (**streaming_plan**)
* This field allows you to choose one or more streaming plans (Basic, Premium, and Deluxe) for the customer.

### Start date (**start_date**)
* Enter the start date of the streaming plan.

### Customer details (**customer_details**)
* Provide a brief description of the customer.

### Customer name (**customer_name**)
* Enter the customer's name.

### Customer email (**customer_email**)
* Enter the customer's email.

### Customer phone (**customer_phone**)
* Enter the customer's phone number.

### Customer note (**customer_note**)
* Add any additional information about the customer.

### Form submission actions (**form_submission_actions**)
* Choose what action to take after form submission: Save and close, Save and continue, or Cancel.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct streaming plan and start date to ensure accurate processing.
* Enter accurate customer contact information to ensure smooth communication.
* Use the **customer_note** field to capture any special requirements or instructions.
* Choose the correct form submission action based on your needs.
