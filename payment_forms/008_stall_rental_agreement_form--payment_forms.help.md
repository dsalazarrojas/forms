# Stall Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stall Rental Agreement Form is used to document the terms and conditions of renting a stall at an event. It is a legal contract between the stall owner and the event organizer.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure a clear understanding of the rental terms.
2. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Terms** (`rental_terms`, text, optional): Describe the terms of the rental, including the dates of the event, event location, and any specific details about the rental.

* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the vendor is willing to participate in the rental. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.

* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the event, such as its name, date, time, and location.

* **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which terms the vendor accepts, such as 'Yes' for each term listed.

* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for the rental, if applicable.

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether payment is due upon signing or upon delivery of goods. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No'.

* **Vendor Signature** (`vendor_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the agreement to confirm acceptance of the terms.

* **Event Signature** (`event_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the agreement to confirm acceptance of the terms by the event organizer.

* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the agreement has been signed, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
