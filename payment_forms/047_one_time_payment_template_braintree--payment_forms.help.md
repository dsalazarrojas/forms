# one_time_payment_template_braintree - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to process one-time payments using Braintree, a popular payment gateway service. It is used to collect payment details such as payment method, amount, and additional information for a single transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a payment method from the list (e.g., Braintree, Stripe, PayPal, etc.).
2. Enter the payment amount in the designated field.
3. Enter your email address to enable communication.
4. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Enter the date and time for the payment (if applicable).
6. Provide any additional information or context about the payment (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_label**: Enter a brief description of the one-time payment template.
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_payment_method**: Select the payment method (e.g., Braintree, Stripe, PayPal, etc.).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_amount**: Enter the one-time payment amount.
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_currency**: Select the payment currency (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP, etc.).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_description**: Enter a brief description of the payment (if applicable).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_date**: Enter the date of the payment (if applicable).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_time**: Enter the time of the payment (if applicable).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_note**: Enter any additional information or context about the payment (if applicable).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_status**: Select the payment status (e.g., pending, approved, declined, etc.).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_status_note**: Enter any additional information about the payment status (if applicable).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_recurring_status**: Select the recurring payment status (e.g., one time, recurring, etc.).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_recurring_note**: Enter any additional information about the recurring payment (if applicable).
* **one_time_payment_template_braintree_additional_info**: Enter any additional information or context about the payment (if applicable).
