# Pest Control Service Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers who are using the pest control service. It's used to record the details of the service provided and is intended to be filled out once per service session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the required fields.
2. Use the correct format for the date (MM/DD/YYYY) and time (HH:MM) fields.
3. Select the correct payment method from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name (Text)
* This is the customer's name. Please enter the full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Required: No**

### Service Address (Text)
* This is the address where the service was performed. Please enter the full address as it appears on the service invoice.
* **Required: No**

### Service Date (Date)
* This is the date on which the service was performed. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Required: No**

### Service Time (Time)
* This is the time at which the service was performed. Please use the format HH:MM.
* **Required: No**

### Service Price (Number)
* This is the total amount paid for the service. Please enter the amount exactly as it appears on the service invoice.
* **Required: No**

### Payment Method (Select One)
* This is the method by which the payment was made. Please select from the options provided:
	+ Cash
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
* **Required: No**

### Payment Total (Number)
* This is the total amount paid for the service. Please enter the amount exactly as it appears on the service invoice.
* **Required: No**

### Customer Email (Email)
* This is the customer's email address. Please enter the address exactly as it appears on their identification.
* **Required: No**

### Customer Phone (Text)
* This is the customer's phone number. Please enter the number exactly as it appears on their identification.
* **Required: No**

Note: If you're having trouble understanding any of the fields or need further clarification, please contact us for assistance.
