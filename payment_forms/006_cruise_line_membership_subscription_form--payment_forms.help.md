# cruise_line_membership_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cruise line membership subscription form is used to subscribe to a membership program offered by a cruise line company. This form requires customers to provide their member information, contact information, payment details, and other relevant details to complete their membership subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the member information section, providing your name, phone number, and any other required information.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address and any other relevant details.
3. Select your membership type, level, and payment terms.
4. Provide your payment details, including the payment amount, payment method, and billing cycle.
5. Enter your shipping information, including your address, city, state, and zip code.
6. Set your start and expiration dates for your membership subscription.
7. Select your payment status and subscription status.
8. Review and confirm your membership subscription information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Information** (`new_member_info`): Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant member information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your email address and any other contact details.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Select your payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal) and enter your payment amount.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`): Enter your address, city, state, and zip code to complete your shipping information.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`): Select your membership type (Basic, Premium, or Platinum).
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`): Select your membership level (Basic, Premium, or Platinum).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Select your payment terms (1/3, 1/4, or 1/6).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the payment amount.
* **Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`): Select your billing cycle (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter your start date for your membership subscription.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`): Enter your expiration date for your membership subscription.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select your payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select your payment status (Pending, Paid, or Unpaid).
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`): Select your subscription status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`): Select your membership status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Cancellation Terms** (`cancellation_terms`): Select your cancellation terms (1/3, 1/4, or 1/6).
* **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`): Enter your cancellation date.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes for your membership subscription.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments for your membership subscription.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct payment method and billing cycle to avoid any issues with your membership subscription.
* Review your membership subscription information carefully before submitting to ensure it is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team.
