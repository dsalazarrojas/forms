# Miscalculation Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and request a refund for a specific billing miscalculation that has occurred on your account. Please provide detailed information about the error and your request for a refund.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **Form Data** (`miscalculation_refund_form_data`): A brief description of the miscalculation that has occurred on your account. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure the issue is properly understood.
2. **Reason** (`reason`): The reason for requesting a refund. Please provide a clear explanation of why you are requesting a refund.
3. **Billing Error Date** (`billing_error_date`): The date on which the billing error occurred.
4. **Billing Error Details** (`billing_error_details`): A detailed description of the error, including any relevant screenshots or documentation that support your claim.
5. **Billing Amount** (`billing_amount`): The amount that was incorrectly charged or credited to your account.
6. **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Your name or the name of the person who is requesting the refund.
7. **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Your email address or the email address of the person who is requesting the refund.
8. **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Your phone number or the phone number of the person who is requesting the refund.
9. **Customer Message** (`customer_message`): Any additional comments or questions you may have about the refund request.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure the issue is properly understood.
* If you have any relevant supporting documentation, please attach it to your form submission.
* A response will be sent to you with further instructions or updates on your refund request.
