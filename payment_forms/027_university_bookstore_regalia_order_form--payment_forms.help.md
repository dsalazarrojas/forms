# University Bookstore Regalia Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for ordering regalia, which includes academic regalia, such as gowns, hoods, and caps, from the university bookstore. The form collects information for both the user and the order details. This form is intended for university staff, students, and faculty who wish to purchase regalia for official ceremonies and events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and any relevant university ID.
2. Select if you are affiliated with the university (Yes/No).
3. Choose the type of regalia you wish to order (multiple options available).
4. Select your preferred color (multiple options available).
5. Choose the size of the regalia (multiple options available).
6. Provide delivery information, including shipping method and address.
7. Specify your shipping method (multiple options available).
8. Enter your shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
9. Choose your preferred payment method (multiple options available).
10. Enter the price of the order.
11. Set the order date.
12. Specify the delivery date.
13. Choose the status of the pickup.
14. Enter your payment status.
15. Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
16. Enter your email address.
17. Enter your phone number.
18. Enter your fax number.
19. Enter your address.
20. Specify your department.
21. Enter your employee ID.
22. Add any comments or notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, text, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your full name for order reference.
* **University Id** (University Id, select_one, required): Select if you are affiliated with the university.
* **Regalia Type** (Regalia Type, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of regalia you wish to order, such as gowns, hoods, or caps.
* **Color** (Color, select_one, required): Select your preferred color for the regalia.
* **Size** (Size, select_one, required): Choose the size of the regalia you need.
* **Delivery Info** (Delivery Info, text, required): Provide any additional information for delivery purposes.
* **Shipping Method** (Shipping Method, select_multiple, required): Select how you would like to receive your order, such as via mail or in-person pickup.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, required): Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter your city for shipping purposes.
* **State** (State, text, required): Enter your state for shipping purposes.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, required): Enter your zip code for shipping purposes.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Price** (Price, text, required): Enter the price of the order.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, date, required): Set the date for the order to be placed.
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date, date, required): Set the expected delivery date for the order.
* **Pickup Status** (Pickup Status, text, required): Specify the status of the pickup for the order.
* **Payment Status** (Payment Status, text, required): Enter your payment status for the order.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Fax** (Fax, text, required): Enter your fax number for communication purposes.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter your department for reference purposes.
* **Employee Id** (Employee Id, text, required): Enter your employee ID for reference purposes.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
