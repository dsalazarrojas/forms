# Charity Meal Program Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Charity Meal Program Donation Form is a tool for individuals and organizations to donate to the charity meal program. The form allows you to provide details about your donation, including your name, contact information, and the type of donation you are making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields. These fields are required.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, which is also a required field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number**, which is not mandatory.
4. Fill in your **Mailing Address**, which is also a required field.
5. Select the **Meal Program** you are donating to from the options provided. This field is required.
6. Choose the type of **Donation** you are making from the options provided. This field is required.
7. If you are making a monetary donation, select the **Donation Amount** from the options provided. If not, select "Not applicable".
8. Choose how often you would like to **Donate** from the options provided. This field is not mandatory.
9. Select the **Payment Method** you would like to use for your donation from the options provided. This field is not mandatory.
10. If you are donating food, select the **Food Items Donating** from the options provided. This field is not mandatory.
11. Choose the **Delivery Method** for your donation from the options provided. This field is required.
12. If you are dropping off your donation, select the **Preferred Drop-off Time** from the options provided. This field is not mandatory.
13. If you need a **Tax Receipt**, select "Yes, send receipt" from the options provided. This field is required.
14. Select whether you would like to be listed on the **Donor List** from the options provided. This field is required.
15. If you are interested in **Volunteering**, select "Yes definitely" from the options provided. This field is not mandatory.
16. If your employer **Matches Donations**, select "True" from the options provided. This field is not mandatory.
17. If you would like to **Dedicate This Donation**, you can do so in the text field provided. This field is not mandatory.
18. Finally, provide any **Additional Comments** about your donation. This field is not mandatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor First Name** (`donor_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to complete the form.
* **Donor Last Name** (`donor_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to complete the form.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email to receive updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact number for any further questions or updates.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full address to receive any physical deliveries or updates.
* **Meal Program** (`program_name`, `select_one`, required): Select the meal program you are donating to from the options provided.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of donation you are making from the options provided.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `select_one`, optional): Select the amount of your donation if it's monetary.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you would like to donate from the options provided.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the method you would like to use for your donation from the options provided.
* **Food Items Donating** (`food_items`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the food items you are donating from the options provided.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to deliver your donation from the options provided.
* **Preferred Drop-off Time** (`drop_off_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select a time that works best for you to drop off your donation.
* **Tax Receipt Needed** (`tax_receipt`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes, send receipt" if you need a tax receipt for your donation.
* **Recognition Preference** (`recognition_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be recognized for your donation from the options provided.
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes definitely" if you are interested in volunteering.
* **Does Your Employer Match Donations** (`corporate_match`, `select_one`, optional): Select "True" if your employer matches donations.
* **Dedicate This Donation** (`dedication`, `text`, optional): Provide any dedication you would like to make with your donation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your donation.
