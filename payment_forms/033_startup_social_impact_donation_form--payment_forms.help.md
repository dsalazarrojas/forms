# Startup Social Impact Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Social Impact Donation Form is designed to collect donations from individuals or organizations supporting social impact startups. Fill out this form to contribute to a social impact startup's mission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Optionally, provide additional details if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed as the donor.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Type of Donation** (`donation_type`, select_one, required: true): Choose between One-time and Recurring donations.
  * One-time: A single donation made at once.
  * Recurring: Regular, continuous donations made at a set interval.
- **Frequency of Donation** (`donation_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how frequently you'd like to make your donation.
  * Monthly: Donation made every month.
  * Quarterly: Donation made every quarter.
  * Annually: Donation made once a year.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: true): Choose your preferred payment method.
  * Credit Card: Donate using a credit card.
  * Bank Transfer: Donate through a bank transfer.
  * PayPal: Donate using PayPal.
- **Message** (`message`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or messages you'd like to include with your donation.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): If you're donating on behalf of a company, enter its name.
- **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): Enter the company's website if you're donating on their behalf.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the company's address if you're donating on their behalf.
- **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the company's address if you're donating on their behalf.
- **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of the company's address if you're donating on their behalf.
- **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of the company's address if you're donating on their behalf.
- **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the country of the company's address if you're donating on their behalf.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the company's phone number if you're donating on their behalf.
