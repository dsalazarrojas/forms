# Extended Stay Studio Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extended Stay Studio Booking Form is designed for guests to book extended stays at the studio. This form is to be filled out by guests to provide their personal details and payment information for their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the type of room you want to book**: Choose from Single, Double, or Triple options.
2. **Enter your room preferences**: Select one or more rooms from the Studio options.
3. **Enter your arrival date**: Choose a date to arrive at the studio.
4. **Enter your departure date**: Choose a date to leave the studio.
5. **Provide your personal details**: Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
6. **Enter your contact information**: Enter your Phone number and Address.
7. **Choose your payment method**: Select from PayPal or Credit Card options.
8. **Enter the total amount**: Enter the total amount for your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Type**: The type of room you want to book (Single, Double, Triple).
* **Room**: The type of room you prefer (Studio, Studio 2, Studio 3, Studio 4).
* **Arrival Date**: The date you plan to arrive at the studio (in date format, e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **Departure Date**: The date you plan to leave the studio (in date format, e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Address**: Your address.
* **Method**: The payment method you prefer (PayPal or Credit Card).
* **Total Amount**: The total amount for your stay.
