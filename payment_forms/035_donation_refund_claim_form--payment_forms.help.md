# Donation Refund Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The donation refund claim form is a form used by donors to request a refund for their donations. This form gathers information about the donor's details and the donation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Donor ID.
2. Provide a reason for your refund request.
3. Enter the date of your donation.
4. Enter your email address.
5. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
6. (Optional) Enter a message if you wish to provide additional information about your refund request.
7. Select the payment method used for your donation.
8. Enter the amount of your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor ID** (`1`, `number`, `required`): Enter your Donor ID.
* **Reason for Refund** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting a refund.
* **Donation Date** (`3`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of your donation.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Message** (`6`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your refund request.
* **Payment Method** (`7`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the payment method used for your donation. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Credit Card
	+ EFT
* **Amount** (`8`, `number`, `required`): Enter the amount of your donation.
