# e-commerce_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to submit a refund request for e-commerce transactions. It guides the user through the process of providing the necessary information for processing a refund, including selecting a refund status, providing order details, and specifying reasons for the refund.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Choose whether you want to submit a refund request or cancel the request by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Submit refund request" dropdown menu.
2.  Enter your order ID, which is a unique identifier for your transaction.
3.  Provide a reason for the refund in the "Reason for refund" field.
4.  Enter the date of the order, the total amount of the order, and the name of the customer.
5.  Enter the email address and phone number of the customer.
6.  Specify the amount of the refund and reason for the refund.
7.  Enter the order number, which is a unique identifier for your order.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit refund request (Submit_refund_request)**: Select "Yes" to submit a refund request or "No" to cancel the request.
* **Order ID (order_id)**: Enter the unique identifier for your order.
* **Reason for refund (reason_for_refund)**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for the refund.
* **Order date (order_date)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order total (order_total)**: Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Customer name (customer_name)**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer email (customer_email)**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer phone (customer_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Refund amount (refund_amount)**: Specify the amount of the refund.
* **Refund reason (refund_reason)**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for the refund.
* **Order number (order_number)**: Enter the unique identifier for your order.
## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that the refund request is processed correctly.
* If you are experiencing technical difficulties while filling out the form, contact support for assistance.
