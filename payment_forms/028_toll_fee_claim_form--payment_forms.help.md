# Toll Fee Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Claimant Information section with the details of the claimant, including their ID, name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the date and description of the toll fee.
3. Provide the amount of the toll fee claimed.
4. Enter the amount that has been reimbursed to the claimant.
5. Select the claimant's status.
6. Fill in the submitter's information, including their name, email, phone number, company, address, city, province, postal code, and country.
7. Add any additional comments if necessary.
8. Sign the form digitally.
9. Confirm that all information is correct by selecting the "I agree" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Information**: Provide details about the claimant, including their ID, name, email, and phone number.
* **Claimant Id**: Enter the claimant's ID number.
* **Claimant Name**: Enter the claimant's name.
* **Claimant Email**: Enter the claimant's email address.
* **Claimant Phone**: Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Claimant Company**: Enter the claimant's company name (optional).
* **Toll Fee Date**: Enter the date when the toll fee occurred.
* **Toll Fee Description**: Describe the toll fee that was incurred.
* **Toll Fee Claimed Amount**: Enter the amount of the toll fee claimed.
* **Claimant Reimbursed Amount**: Enter the amount that has been reimbursed to the claimant.
* **Claimant Status**: Select the current status of the claimant (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Submitter Information**: Provide details about the person submitting the claim, including their name, email, phone number, company, address, city, province, postal code, and country.
* **Submitter Comments**: Add any additional comments about the claim.
* **Submitter Signature**: Sign the form digitally.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitter Remaks**: Enter any additional comments or information about the submission (optional).
* **Submitter Agree**: Confirm that all information is correct and that you agree to the submission.
