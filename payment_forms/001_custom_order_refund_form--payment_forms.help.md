# Custom Order Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Order Refund Form is used to process refunds for custom orders. This form is essential for handling customer refunds efficiently and accurately. Before starting, ensure that the order is indeed eligible for a refund and that you have the necessary permissions to process the refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide detailed information about the order in the **Order Details** field.
2. Enter the customer's contact information in the **Customer Info** field.
3. Specify the reason for the refund in the **Reason for Refund** field.
4. Enter the amount to be refunded in the **Refund Amount** field.
5. Choose the payment method used for the original payment in the **Payment Method** field.
6. Select a refund date and time in the **Refund Date** and **Refund Time** fields.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the order in the **Order Notes** field (optional).
8. Provide any customer comments or feedback in the **Customer Comments** field (optional).
9. Update the refund status to **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected** in the **Refund Status** field.
10. Add any additional notes or details about the refund in the **Additional Notes** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of the order, including the order number, customer name, and date of purchase.
* **Customer Info (2)**: Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number.
* **Reason for Refund (3)**: Specify the reason why the customer is requesting a refund, e.g., "Item not received," "Item not as described," or "Order cancellation."
* **Refund Amount (4)**: Enter the exact amount to be refunded, including any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Payment Method (5)**: Select the payment method used for the original payment, e.g., credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
* **Refund Date (6)**: Select a date for the refund, ideally the date the refund will be processed.
* **Refund Time (7)**: Select a time for the refund, ideally when the refund will be processed.
* **Order Notes (8)**: Add any additional information about the order that may be relevant to the refund process.
* **Customer Comments (9)**: Provide any comments or feedback from the customer, which may be helpful for future reference.
* **Refund Status (10)**: Update the refund status to **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected** to indicate the current status of the refund.
* **Additional Notes (11)**: Add any additional information or details about the refund that are not covered in other fields.
