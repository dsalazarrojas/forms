# non_profit_donation_template_worldpay_us - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for non-profit donations using the Worldpay US payment processor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Donation Amount field with the amount you wish to donate.
2. Choose the frequency of your donation from the Frequency field options.
3. Select the type of donation from the Donation Type field options.
4. If applicable, provide the Donation Date.
5. Enter the Donation Time.
6. Choose the Donation Frequency from the options.
7. Provide your Email address.
8. Enter your Phone number.
9. Enter your Name.
10. Enter your Address.
11. Enter your City.
12. Enter your State.
13. Enter your Zip code.
14. Enter your Card Number.
15. Enter your CVV.
16. Select your Exp. Month from the options.
17. Select your Exp. Year from the options.
18. Select your Exp. Month-Day from the options.
19. Enter your Cardholder Name.
20. Enter your Organization.
21. Enter your Tax ID.
22. Enter any custom fields you wish to add.
23. Enter the Payment Processor Fee.
24. Enter the Total Dollar Amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount (1)** (`donation_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Frequency (2)** (`frequency`, Select One, Optional): Choose how often you want to make this donation.
* **Donation Type (3)** (`donation_type`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of donation you're making.
* **Donation Date (4)** (`donation_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your donation.
* **Donation Time (5)** (`donation_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of your donation.
* **Donation Frequency (6)** (`donation_frequency`, Select One, Optional): Select how often you want to make this donation.
* **Email (7)** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (8)** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Name (9)** (`name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name.
* **Address (10)** (`address`, Text, Optional): Enter your address.
* **City (11)** (`city`, Text, Optional): Enter your city.
* **State (12)** (`state`, Text, Optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip (13)** (`zip`, Text, Optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Card Number (14)** (`card_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your card number.
* **CVV (15)** (`cvv`, Text, Optional): Enter your CVV (Card Verification Value).
* **Exp. Month (16)** (`exp_month`, Select One, Optional): Select the expiration month of your card.
* **Exp. Year (17)** (`exp_year`, Select One, Optional): Select the expiration year of your card.
* **Exp. Month-Day (18)** (`exp_month_day`, Select One, Optional): Select the expiration month and day of your card.
* **Cardholder Name (19)** (`cardholder_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your cardholder name.
* **Organization (20)** (`organization`, Text, Optional): Enter your organization.
* **Tax ID (21)** (`tax_id`, Text, Optional): Enter your tax ID.
* **Custom Fields (22)** (`custom_fields`, Text, Optional): Enter any custom fields you wish to add.
* **Payment Processor Fee (23)** (`payment_processor_fee`, Number, Optional): Enter the payment processor fee.
* **Total Dollar Amount (24)** (`total_dollar_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the total dollar amount.
