# ITIN Preparation Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ITIN Preparation Service Form is used to prepare and update information for the Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Birth Date (MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Select your Primary Citizenship Status.
7. Select the Tax Year you are applying for.
8. Select the type of TIN Application you are submitting.
9. Select the status of your TIN Application.
10. Select any relevant TIN Status Updates.
11. Select the status of your Social Security Number.
12. Select the status of your TIN (Frozen, Suspended, Canceled, Amended, Duplicate, Voided, or Inactivated).
13. Select your Tax Filing Status.
14. Enter your Address (Address 1, Address 2, and Address 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your current email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your current phone number for communication purposes.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Primary Citizenship Status**: Select your current citizenship status.
* **Tax Year**: Select the tax year for which you are applying for or updating your ITIN.
* **TIN Application Type**: Select the type of TIN application you are submitting.
* **TIN Application Status**: Select the status of your current TIN application.
* **TIN Status Update**: Select any relevant TIN status updates.
* **Social Security Number**: Select the status of your Social Security Number.
* **TIN Frozen**: Select the status of your TIN (Frozen, Suspended, Canceled, Amended, Duplicate, Voided, or Inactivated).
* **TIN Suspended**: Select the status of your TIN (Frozen, Suspended, Canceled, Amended, Duplicate, Voided, or Inactivated).
* **TIN Canceled**: Select the status of your TIN (Frozen, Suspended, Canceled, Amended, Duplicate, Voided, or Inactivated).
* **TIN Amended**: Select the status of your TIN (Frozen, Suspended, Canceled, Amended, Duplicate, Voided, or Inactivated).
* **TIN Duplicate**: Select the status of your TIN (Frozen, Suspended, Canceled, Amended, Duplicate, Voided, or Inactivated).
* **TIN Voided**: Select the status of your TIN (Frozen, Suspended, Canceled, Amended, Duplicate, Voided, or Inactivated).
* **TIN Inactivated**: Select the status of your TIN (Frozen, Suspended, Canceled, Amended, Duplicate, Voided, or Inactivated).
* **Tax Filing Status**: Select your current tax filing status.
* **Address 1**: Enter your current address.
* **Address 2**: Enter your current address.
* **Address 3**: Enter your current address.
