# Payment Plan Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for payment plan workshop registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your User Details, including your name and any other relevant user information.
2.  Select a Registration Date for the workshop.
3.  Choose a Payment Plan that suits your needs.
4.  Select any applicable Fees for your registration.
5.  Enter the Total Cost of your selected payment plan.
6.  Choose a Payment Method for your registration.
7.  Enter your Email address.
8.  Enter your Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your name and any other relevant user information.
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you plan to register for the workshop.
- **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a payment plan from the available options that suits your needs.
- **Fees** (`fees`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any applicable fees for your registration.
- **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total cost of your selected payment plan.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a payment method for your registration.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the workshop administrators for clarification.
- Ensure you have selected the correct payment plan and payment method for your registration.
