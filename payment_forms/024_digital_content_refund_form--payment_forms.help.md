# Digital Content Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process refunds for digital goods or services purchased through this platform. To complete the form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, fill out the fields as follows:

1. **Select Multiple Options** (Submit Form):
	* Choose 'Yes' if you're submitting this form for a refund.
	* Choose 'No' otherwise.
2. **Order ID** (Order ID):
	* Enter the unique identifier associated with the order for which you're requesting a refund.
3. **Reason** (Reason):
	* Provide a brief explanation for the reason you're requesting a refund.
4. **Date of Purchase** (Date of Purchase):
	* Enter the date when you made the purchase.
5. **Product Name** (Product Name):
	* Enter the name of the digital product for which you're requesting a refund.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Form** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose 'Yes' to submit this form for a refund.

## Tips
