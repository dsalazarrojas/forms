# Soft Play Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soft Play Rental Agreement Form is a digital form designed to collect essential information from customers for rental purposes. It is intended for use in the context of renting out a facility, such as a soft play area, for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information about the rental details, including event date and time, event duration, and event start and end times.
2. Enter your payment details, including the total amount due and payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, bank transfer, or cheque).
3. Confirm your payment terms, including whether you agree to the payment terms.
4. Enter your customer details, including address, phone number, and email.
5. Confirm your payment method details, such as credit card or bank transfer information.
6. Sign the agreement by selecting "Yes" for "Terms Agreed".
7. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Details (1)**: Enter the date and time of the event. 
* **Terms (2)**: Please provide a brief description of the terms for this event. 
* **Payment Confirmation (3)**: Confirm that you understand the payment terms. 
* **Payment Terms (4)**: Confirm that you understand the payment terms. 
* **Customer Details (5)**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email. 
* **Event Date (6)**: Enter the date of the event. 
* **Event Time (7)**: Enter the time of the event. 
* **Event Duration (8)**: Enter the length of the event. 
* **Event Start Time (9)**: Enter the start time of the event. 
* **Event End Time (10)**: Enter the end time of the event. 
* **Total Amount (11)**: Enter the total amount due. 
* **Customer Address (12)**: Enter your address. 
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your phone number. 
* **Email (14)**: Enter your email. 
* **Payment Method (15)**: Select the payment method you would like to use. 
* **Payment Terms Accepted (16)**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the payment terms. 
* **Terms and Conditions (17)**: Please read and understand the terms and conditions of the rental agreement. 
* **Agreement Signed (18)**: Sign the agreement by selecting "Yes" to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions. 
* **Payment Method Details (19)**: Enter your payment method details, such as credit card information. 
* **Bank Transfer Details (20)**: Enter bank transfer details. 
* **Cheque Details (21)**: Enter cheque details. 
* **PayPal Details (22)**: Enter PayPal details. 
* **Credit Card Details (23)**: Enter credit card details. 
* **Terms Agreed (24)**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions. 
* **Submit (25)**: Click "Yes" to submit the form and complete the agreement.

Please note that some fields are optional, but all required fields must be filled out in order to submit the form.
