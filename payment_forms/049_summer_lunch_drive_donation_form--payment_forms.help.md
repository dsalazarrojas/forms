# Summer Lunch Drive Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for the Summer Lunch Drive, a campaign to support a good cause. The form is meant to be filled out by individuals or organizations willing to contribute to the cause.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Summer Lunch Drive Donation Form" page.
2. Fill out the form by entering the required information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Summer Lunch Drive Donation Form** (`summer_lunch_drive_donation_form`, text, **required**):
	+ This is the form title, and is displayed as a heading at the top of the form page.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, **required**):
	+ Enter your name or the name of your organization.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, **required**):
	+ Enter the amount you would like to donate to the Summer Lunch Drive.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Select your preferred payment method from the available options (American Express, Cash, PayPal, Visa).
	+ This will determine how your donation will be processed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted about your donation.
	+ This is not required, but it will allow us to get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
	+ This is not required, but it will allow us to contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Comments** (`note`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to attach to your donation.
	+ This is a free text field, so you can write as much or as little as you'd like.
* **Donate** (`submit`, text, optional):
	+ Click this button to submit your completed form and complete your donation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* If you have any trouble filling out the form, please don't hesitate to contact us for help.
* We appreciate your donation to the Summer Lunch Drive, and thank you for your support!
