# Specialist Billing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for billing specialists to collect payment information and status from patients or clients. It's used to streamline the billing process and ensure accurate and efficient payment tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the page that corresponds to your relevant information:
	* **Patient Info** (page 1): Enter the patient's or client's basic details.
	* **Billing Info** (page 2): Fill out the billing details relevant to your payment.
	* **Payment Method** (page 3): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
	* **Payment Details** (page 4): Enter the payment date and time.
	* **Additional Information** (page 5): Enter any further details required for payment processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Patient Name)** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's or client's name.
* **Patient Info (Patient Email)** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's or client's email address.
* **Patient Info (Patient Phone)** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's or client's phone number.
* **Billing Info (Billing Provider)** (`billing_provider`, text, optional): Enter the billing provider's name or company name.
* **Billing Info (Billing Address)** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the billing provider's address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following payment methods:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Check
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, optional): Enter the payment time.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following payment statuses:
	+ Draft
	+ Pending
	+ Complete
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's or client's address.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's or client's medical record number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the required fields for accurate and efficient payment processing.
* Use the correct payment method and date/time to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the billing specialist for assistance.
