# Tournament Vendor Signage Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for ordering tournament vendor signage. The form's purpose is to collect information from users to create a sign for their event or organization. The form is used to gather the necessary details to create a professional-looking sign that will be displayed at the tournament event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the category for your event or organization.
2. Provide a brief description of the event or organization.
3. Select the payment method for your order.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Choose a date for your event (if applicable).
7. Choose a time for your event (if applicable).
8. Add any notes or comments about your order.
9. Optionally, assign a specific tool for your event.
10. Enter a form ID (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category**: This field allows you to choose a category for your event or organization.
	+ Select one option: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **description**: This field is for a brief description of your event or organization.
	+ Enter a short text explanation.
* **payment_method**: This field allows you to select multiple payment methods for your order.
	+ Choose one or more options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **email**: This field is for entering your email address.
	+ Enter a valid email address (e.g., [yourname@example.com](mailto:yourname@example.com)).
* **phone**: This field is for entering your phone number.
	+ Enter a 10-digit phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **date**: This field is for choosing a date for your event (if applicable).
	+ Select a date in the format DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 01/02/2023).
* **time**: This field is for choosing a time for your event (if applicable).
	+ Select a time in the format HH:MM (e.g., 10:00).
* **notes**: This field is for adding any additional comments or notes about your order.
	+ Enter a text explanation.
* **assigned_tool**: This field is for optionally assigning a specific tool for your event.
	+ Enter a tool name (e.g., "Tool A", "Tool B", etc.).
* **form_id**: This field is for entering a form ID (if required).
	+ Enter a unique identifier for this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose a valid category and description to create a clear and accurate sign.
* Select all relevant payment methods for your order.
* Enter a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Be specific with the date and time if applying for a time-sensitive event.
* Use this opportunity to add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* If required, enter a valid form ID.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
