# Paint And Sip Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students register for a Paint and Sip class. It collects required information to confirm the student's registration and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Please ensure that all fields with a red asterisk (*) are mandatory.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it, as once submitted, it cannot be altered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`4`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Class Type** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of class you want to register for (e.g., Acrylic Painting, Watercolor, etc.).
* **Student Name** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter your name as displayed on your ID or government-issued ID.
* **Guardian Name** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your guardian or parent (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Address** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter your address for delivery or billing purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Choose your payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, etc.).
* **Amount Paid** (`11`, `number`, required): Enter the amount paid.
* **Payment Details** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the payment details (e.g., transaction number, date, etc.).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`13`, `note`, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Registration Date** (`14`, `date`, required): Enter the date you register for the class.
* **Class Time** (`15`, `time`, required): Choose your class time slot.
* **Course Details** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the class you registered for.
* **Instructor Name** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Registration Status** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Choose your registration status (e.g., Pending, Completed, etc.).
* **Payment Status** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Choose your payment status (e.g., Pending, Completed, etc.).
* **Notes** (`20`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Payment Gateway** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Choose the payment gateway you used (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, etc.).
* **Course ID** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you are registered for the course or not.
* **Class Level** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Choose your class level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, etc.).
* **Course Duration** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the duration of the course (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Total Hours** (`25`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours of the course.
