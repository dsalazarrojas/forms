# Inclusive Community Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inclusive Community Fund Donation Form is a tool for users to contribute to the Inclusive Community Fund. To submit a donation, complete the form by providing your name, email, and donation amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your donation amount in the "Donation amount" field.
6. Click on "Submit" again to send your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Name, text, required):** Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in updates and communication from the organization.
* **Email (Email, email, required):** Provide a valid email address where you can be contacted for updates and information from the organization.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required):** Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for further information or updates.
* **Donation Amount (Donation amount, number, required):** Enter the amount you would like to donate to the Inclusive Community Fund. Please ensure the amount is a valid positive number.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need further information, do not hesitate to contact the organization using the provided phone number or email.
* The organization will use the submitted information to track donations and provide updates to donors.
