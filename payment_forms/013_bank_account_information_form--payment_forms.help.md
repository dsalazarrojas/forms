# Bank Account Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect bank account information for various purposes such as direct deposit, vendor payments, customer reimbursements, and automated billing. The form asks for details like account holder identification, financial institution information, and account specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Identify yourself or the business associated with the account.
2.  Provide your Tax Identification Number (SSN or EIN).
3.  Enter your registered mailing address with the financial institution.
4.  Enter your primary contact email for confirmation and secure communication.
5.  Enter your bank name and account details like routing number and account number.
6.  Select the type of account you are using (e.g., Checking, Savings, Money Market).
7.  Choose the account's primary currency.
8.  Specify the purpose of the account collection (e.g., direct deposit, vendor payments, automated billing).
9.  Confirm the accuracy of the provided details and agree on verification via test deposit.
10.  Provide a date when the account details become active.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Account Holder Identification --
*   **Individual or Business Name** (`entity_name`): Enter the name as registered with your financial institution.
*   **Tax Identification Number (SSN or EIN)** (`tax_id_number`): Enter your Tax Identification Number as issued by the IRS or your EIN if you're a business.
*   **Registered Mailing Address** (`physical_address`): Enter your address as registered with your bank.
*   **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_email`): Enter your primary email for secure communication.

### -- Financial Institution Details --
*   **Bank Name** (`institution_name`): Enter your bank name as it's registered with your account.
*   **Routing Number (9 Digits)** (`routing_number`): Enter your bank's routing number, a 9-digit code that identifies your financial institution.
*   **Account Number** (`account_number`): Enter your account number as issued by your bank.
*   **Type of Account** (`account_type`): Choose the type of account you're using (e.g., Checking, Savings, Money Market).
*   **Account Currency** (`currency_type`): Select the primary currency for your account (e.g., USD, CAD, EUR, GBP, Other).

### -- Purpose of Account Collection --
*   **What will this account be used for?** (`collection_purpose`): Choose the purpose of account collection (e.g., direct deposit, vendor payments, automated billing).

### Verification and Accuracy Statement
*   **I confirm the banking details provided are correct** (`security_confirmation`): Confirm that the details you've entered are accurate.
*   **I authorize the verification of this account via test deposit** (`security_confirmation`): Authorize verification of the account details via a test deposit.

### Authorized Signer
*   **Authorized Signer Name** (`authorized_signer`): Enter the name of the person authorized to provide these account details.

**Note:** The form requires accurate and detailed information to prevent errors in account collection and verification processes. Ensure you enter correct details for the form and confirm them accurately for secure and efficient account management.
