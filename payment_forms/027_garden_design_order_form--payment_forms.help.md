# Garden Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garden Design Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for fulfilling their garden design orders. It is intended for customers interested in purchasing a garden design service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the option for "garden_design_package" and choose whether you would like to purchase a design.
2. If you selected "Yes" for "garden_design_package", enter your "payment_amount" and select your preferred "payment_method".
3. Enter your "customer_name", "customer_email", and "customer_phone" for contact information.
4. Provide details about your project in the "order_details" field.
5. Set a "project_deadline" for the project completion.
6. Enter your estimated "project_budget" for the project.
7. Submit the form to proceed with your garden design order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Request (project_request)**: Enter a brief description of your garden design project.
* **Garden Design Package (garden_design_package)**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in purchasing a garden design service. If not, select "No".
* **Payment Amount (payment_amount)**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the garden design service. This field is only visible if you selected "Yes" for "garden_design_package".
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select the payment method you would like to use for the service. This field is only visible if you selected "Yes" for "garden_design_package".
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Provide a detailed description of your project requirements and preferences.
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter your email address as the customer.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number as the customer.
* **Project Deadline (project_deadline)**: Set a deadline for the project completion.
* **Project Budget (project_budget)**: Enter your estimated budget for the project. 

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurately filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any further questions or clarifications, please contact us directly. 

Note: This help guide is designed to provide a clear understanding of each field and its purpose. However, if you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please feel free to ask for clarification.
