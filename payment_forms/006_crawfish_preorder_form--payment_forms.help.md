# Crawfish Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crawfish Preorder Form is designed for customers to preorder their crawfish products. This form collects necessary information from customers to ensure that their order is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field labeled "customer_name".
2. Provide your shipping address in the field labeled "shipping_address".
3. If you have any special notes or instructions for your order, enter them in the field labeled "order_notes".
4. Select the type and quantity of crawfish you would like to order from the options listed for "order_items".
5. Choose a shipping date and time in the field labeled "shipping_date" and "shipping_time" respectively.
6. Enter the number of crawfish you would like to order in the field labeled "order_quantity".
7. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the order in the field labeled "order_price".
8. Select your preferred payment method from the options listed for "payment_method".
9. Enter your email address in the field labeled "customer_email".
10. If you would like to provide any additional information, enter it in the field labeled "customer_message".
11. If you have any comments or notes for the vendor, enter them in the field labeled "vendor_message".
12. Finally, select the status of your order from the options listed for "order_status".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name** (text, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **shipping_address** (text, required): Provide your shipping address in this field.
* **order_notes** (text, optional): If you have any special notes or instructions for your order, enter them here.
* **order_items** (select multiple, optional): Select the type and quantity of crawfish you would like to order.
* **shipping_date** (date, optional): Choose a shipping date for your order.
* **shipping_time** (time, optional): Choose a shipping time for your order.
* **order_quantity** (number, optional): Enter the number of crawfish you would like to order.
* **order_price** (number, optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the order.
* **payment_method** (select one, optional): Select your preferred payment method.
* **customer_email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **customer_phone** (text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number, enter it here.
* **customer_message** (text, optional): If you have any additional information, enter it here.
* **vendor_note** (text, optional): If you have any comments or notes for the vendor, enter them here.
* **vendor_message** (text, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes for the vendor, enter them here.
* **order_status** (select one, optional): Select the status of your order.
