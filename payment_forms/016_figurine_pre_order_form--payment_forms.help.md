# Figurine Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Figurine Pre Order Form is a form for customers to provide their order details for a pre-ordered figurine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your **Customer Name** and **Customer Email** to ensure we can contact you about your pre-ordered figurine.
2. Enter your **Shipping Address** so we can deliver your figurine to the correct location.
3. Choose a **Payment Method** from the options provided: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
4. Provide the **Figurine Name** of the item you are pre-ordering.
5. Select a **Pre-Order Date** for your figurine.
6. Enter the **Figurine Price** of the item.
7. Indicate the **Quantity Ordered**, the amount of figurines you are pre-ordering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your invoice and delivery information.
* **Customer Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your pre-ordered figurine.
* **Shipping Address** (text, required/optional): Enter your address where you would like your figurine to be delivered.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your pre-ordered figurine: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Figurine Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the figurine you are pre-ordering.
* **Pre-Order Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date you would like your figurine to be delivered by.
* **Figurine Price** (number, required/optional): Enter the price of the figurine you are pre-ordering.
* **Quantity Ordered** (number, required/optional): Enter the number of figurines you are pre-ordering.

## Tips
* Please ensure your shipping address is accurate to avoid delays in delivery.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us using the contact information provided.
