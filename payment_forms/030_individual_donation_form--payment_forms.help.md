# individual_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individual donors about their donations, including personal details, donation specifics, payment methods, and date of donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **donor info** page with your name, email, and other personal information (if applicable).
2. On the **donation details** page, provide a brief description or purpose of your donation.
3. On the **payment info** page, select the payment method you will use for your donation (e.g. option 1, option 2, option 3, or option 4).
4. On the **date of donation** page, choose the date you intend to make your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Info (donor_info)**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant personal information as requested.
* **Donation Details (donation_details)**: Describe the purpose or specifics of your donation, such as the project or cause it will support.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)**: Choose from the available payment options (option 1, option 2, option 3, or option 4) to select how you will make your donation (e.g. credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Date of Donation (date_of_donation)**: Select the date on which you intend to make your donation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the required marker ().
* Double-check your selections and information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, contact our support team for help.
