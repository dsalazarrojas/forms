# PayPal Business Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate payment from customers for services rendered or goods sold. It's meant for businesses to gather the necessary information from their customers before creating a payment request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter a brief title for your payment request** (`form_title`):
    * This field should include a short and descriptive name for the payment you're requesting.
    * A good title can help you and your customer quickly identify the purpose of the payment.
2. **Enter the customer's name** (`customer_name`):
    * This is the customer's name as it appears on their identification or business card.
    * Please make sure to spell their name correctly.
3. **Enter the customer's email address** (`customer_email`):
    * This is the customer's email address where the payment request will be sent to.
    * Use the format `example@example.com`.
4. **Enter the customer's phone number** (`customer_phone`):
    * This is the customer's phone number where they can be reached for payment inquiries.
    * Please enter the phone number without any leading zeros or special characters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **PayPal Business Payment Form** (`form_title`):
    * This field is where you enter the title of the payment request.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`):
    * This field is where you enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`):
    * This field is where you enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`):
    * This field is where you enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the customer's contact information before submitting the form.
* If the customer's information is incorrect, you might need to ask for clarification or update their details before sending the payment request.
* Use this form for payment requests that require a more personalized approach, such as service-based payments or customized orders.
