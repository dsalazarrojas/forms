# electrical_component_end_cover_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and process orders for electrical components from suppliers. It helps the customer or supplier provide necessary information for processing the order, ensuring timely delivery, and facilitating payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Provide the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the purchase order number in the "Purchase Order Number" field.
4. (Optional) Enter the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field, if applicable.
5. Select the desired delivery method from the "Delivery Method" options (e.g., Online, Email, Phone, In Person, Pickup).
6. Select the preferred payment method(s) from the "Payment Method" options (e.g., Bank Transfer, Credit Card).
7. Enter the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
8. Choose the delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
9. Enter any additional notes for this order in the "Additional Notes" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Select the order status from the "Status" options (e.g., Open, Completed, Cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the supplier's name.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Purchase Order Number**: Enter the purchase order number.
* **Delivery Address**: (Optional) Enter the delivery address if different from the supplier's address.
* **Delivery Method**: Select how you would like to receive the delivery (e.g., Online, Email, Phone, In Person, Pickup).
* **Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method(s) (e.g., Bank Transfer, Credit Card).
* **Payment Terms**: Enter the payment terms.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the delivery time.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes for this order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Status**: Select the order status (e.g., Open, Completed, Cancelled).
