# Jet Ski Rental Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a jet ski rental. Please complete it to provide us with the necessary information to prepare for your rental.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required information. Each page is labeled with a corresponding page number.
- Start by completing the Customer Info page, which includes fields for your name, address, and phone number.
- Next, move on to the Start Time and End Time pages, where you can select the start and end times of your rental.
- After that, choose the Jet Ski Type that you'd like to rent.
- On the Rental Duration page, select the length of time you'd like to rent the jet ski for.
- Finally, select the Rental Date on which you'd like to make your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (1, text, required): Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Start Time** (2, date, required): Select the start time of your rental.
* **End Time** (3, time, required): Select the end time of your rental.
* **Jet Ski Type** (4, select_one, required): Choose the type of jet ski you'd like to rent (Ski1, Ski2, Ski3).
* **Rental Duration** (5, number, required): Select the length of time you'd like to rent the jet ski for, in hours.
* **Rental Date** (6, date, required): Select the date of your rental.
* **Phone Number** (7, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with your reservation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
