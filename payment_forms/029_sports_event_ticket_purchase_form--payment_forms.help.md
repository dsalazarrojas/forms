# sports_event_ticket_purchase_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to purchase sports event tickets. Please fill out the form below to initiate the ticket purchasing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled out all required fields marked as *Required*.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of ticket you wish to purchase from the "Ticket Type" dropdown options.
6. Enter the quantity of tickets you would like to purchase.
7. Enter the total cost of the tickets you are purchasing in the "Ticket Price" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Required, text): Enter your full name to help us identify you and process your ticket purchase.
* **Email** (Required, email): Enter a valid email address to receive confirmation of your ticket purchase.
* **Phone** (Required, text): Enter your phone number for verification purposes.
* **Ticket Type** (Required, text): Select the type of ticket you wish to purchase. This will help us process your request correctly.
* **Quantity** (Required, number): Enter the number of tickets you would like to purchase.
* **Ticket Price** (Required, number): Enter the total cost of the tickets you are purchasing.
