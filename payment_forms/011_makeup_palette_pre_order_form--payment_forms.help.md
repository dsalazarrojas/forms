# Makeup Palette Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect pre-order information for a custom makeup palette, allowing you to specify your preferences for palette type, price, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are critical for processing your pre-order.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Info** (id: 2, `type: select_multiple`, required: true): Select "Yes" to indicate you would like to include shipping information in your pre-order.
* **Palette Type** (id: 3, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" if you would like to specify the palette type for your pre-order.
* **Price** (id: 5, `type: number`, required: true): Enter the price of the pre-order.
* **Payment Method** (id: 6, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate you would like to specify the payment method for your pre-order.
* **Payment Info** (id: 7, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional payment information or details.
* **Shipping Method** (id: 8, `type: select_one`, required: true): Select "Yes" to indicate you would like to specify the shipping method.
* **Special Requests** (id: 9, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any special requests or comments about your pre-order.
* **Email** (id: 10, `type: email`, required: true): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (id: 11, `type: text`, required: true): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Delivery Preferences** (id: 12, `type: date`, required: false): Select your preferred date for delivery.
* **Palette Image** (id: 13, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a photo or reference for your palette design.
* **Palette Name** (id: 14, `type: text`, required: true): Enter the name of your custom palette.
* **Palette Description** (id: 15, `type: text`, required: true): Enter a brief description of your custom palette.
* **Pre-Order Date** (id: 16, `type: date`, required: false): Select your preferred date for pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Time** (id: 17, `type: time`, required: false): Select your preferred time for pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Notes** (id: 18, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Comments** (id: 19, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about your pre-order.
* **Pre-Order Address** (id: 20, `type: text`, required: true): Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Pre-Order City** (id: 21, `type: text`, required: true): Enter the city of your address.
* **Pre-Order State** (id: 22, `type: text`, required: true): Enter the state of your address.
* **Pre-Order Zip** (id: 23, `type: number`, required: true): Enter your zip code.
* **Pre-Order Country** (id: 24, `type: text`, required: true): Enter the country of your address.
* **Submit** (id: 25, `type: text`, required: true): Click this button to submit your pre-order.
