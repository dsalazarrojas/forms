# Conference Speaker Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for conference speakers to provide their payment information for a conference talk or presentation. It's used to ensure accurate and timely payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your speaker information: Enter your full name, email address, phone number, organization, and professional title.
2. Provide payment details: Choose the payment type, amount, and currency for your payment.
3. Select payment method: Choose how you want to receive your payment.
4. Add additional details: If applicable, provide your bank name, account number, routing number, account type, and tax ID or SSN.
5. Confirm payment details: Verify that the payment information is accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Full Name** (Required): Your complete legal name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Contact email.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Contact number.
* **Organization** (Required): Your company or institution.
* **Professional Title** (Required): Your position.
* **Mailing Address** (Required): Full address for payment.
* **City** (Required): Your city.
* **State or Province** (Required): Your state.
* **Postal Code** (Required): Your ZIP code.
* **Country** (Required, select_one): Choose your country (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, European Union, or Other).
* **Session or Presentation Title** (Required): Name of your talk.
* **Session Date** (Required): When you will present.
* **Session Time** (Required): Your time slot.
* **Payment Type** (Required, select_one): Choose the type of compensation (Honorarium, Speaking Fee, Travel Reimbursement, Per Diem, Combination).
* **Payment Amount** (Required): Agreed amount.
* **Currency** (Required, select_one): Choose the payment currency (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, or Other).
* **Payment Method** (Required, select_one): Choose how you want to receive payment (Direct Deposit, Check, Wire Transfer, PayPal, or Venmo).
* **Bank Name** (Optional): For direct deposit.
* **Account Number** (Optional): Bank account.
* **Routing Number** (Optional): Bank routing number.
* **Account Type** (Optional, select_one): Choose between Checking or Savings.
* **Tax ID or SSN** (Optional): For tax reporting.
* **W-9 Form Submitted** (Required, select_one): If applicable, choose Yes Attached or Will Submit Separately (Not Required).
* **Invoice Required** (Required, select_one): If you need to submit an invoice, choose Yes Will Submit or No Not Required.
* **Invoice Number** (Optional): If applicable.
* **Travel Reimbursement Amount** (Optional): If applicable.
* **Travel Receipts Submitted** (Optional, select_one): If you need to submit receipts, choose Yes All Receipts Attached or Partially or Will Submit Later or Not Applicable.
* **Expected Payment Timeline** (Optional, select_one): When you expect payment (Within 30 Days, Within 60 Days, After Event, As Per Contract).
* **Special Payment Instructions** (Optional): Any additional notes.
* **I Confirm Payment Details Are Accurate** (Required, select_one): Verify that the information is accurate before submitting the form.
