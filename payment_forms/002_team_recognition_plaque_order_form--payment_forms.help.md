# Team Recognition Plaque Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Recognition Plaque Order Form is designed to help team leaders order recognition plaques for their teams. This form captures essential information about the team and the order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the team leadership and department name fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the employee name and email address of the team member responsible for ordering the plaque.
3. Provide any additional order details in the order details field.
4. If applicable, select the team plaque type and color.
5. Choose the team size.
6. List the team members in the team members field.
7. Enter the shipping address, city, state, and zip code for the order.
8. Select the country of shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team leadership** (`team_leadership`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the team leader responsible for ordering the plaque.
* **Department name** (`department_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the department the team belongs to.
* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the team member responsible for ordering the plaque.
* **Email** (`employee_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the team member responsible for ordering the plaque.
* **Order details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the order.
* **Team plaque type** (`team_plaque_type`, select one, options: [‘Yes’ ‘No’], required: false): Check if the team plaque type is needed.
* **Team plaque color** (`team_plaque_color`, select multiple, options: [Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, Grey, Black, Pink, Brown], required: false): Select the color of the team plaque.
* **Team size** (`team_size`, select one, options: [Small, Medium, Large], required: false): Choose the size of the team.
* **Team members** (`team_members`, text, required: false): List the team members in the team members field.
* **Shipping address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter the shipping address.
* **City** (`shipping_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city.
* **State** (`shipping_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state.
* **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required: false): Select the country of shipment.

Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accurate information is provided. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact your administrator.
