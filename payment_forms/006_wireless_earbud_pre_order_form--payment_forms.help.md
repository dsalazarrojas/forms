# Wireless Earbud Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wireless Earbud Pre Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from customers to process their pre-orders for wireless earbuds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Enter your shipping address in the Shipping Address field.
6. Enter the order total in the Order Total field.
7. Choose the shipping date in the Shipping Date field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the Payment Method field.
9. (Optional) Add any notes or comments in the Notes field.
10. (Optional) Leave a customer message in the Customer Message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name to help us get to know you better.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates on your order status.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to help us contact you if needed.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping address where we will ship your order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, `date`, required): Choose the shipping date of your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you want to pay for your order: cash, credit card, paypal, or apple pay.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Leave any notes or comments about your order, such as any special requests.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, `text`, optional): Leave a message for us to get to know you better.
 
# Tips
* Make sure to enter correct information to avoid any errors or issues with your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
