# Document Authentication Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Authentication Payment Form is a crucial tool for verifying the legitimacy of documents related to payments. It helps ensure that the documents are genuine and accurate, preventing any potential issues or discrepancies during the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields, marked as "required" in the form. These fields are:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Document Type (Yes/No)
	* Document Number
	* Date
	* Payment
2. Ensure that you enter accurate and valid information in each field.
3. Select "Submit" to send the form for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form** (`form`, `text`, required: false): This is the main content field where you'll enter your comments or information related to the document.
* **Document Type** (`document_type_form_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the document type is authentic, 'No' otherwise.
* **Document Number** (`document_number_form_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unique ID number of the document.
* **Date** (`document_date_form_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the document, in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Payment** (`document_payment_form_payment`, `number`, required: false): Enter the payment amount or details.
* **Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on the document.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as it appears on the document.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as it appears on the document.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click to send the form for verification.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues during the payment process.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult the relevant authorities or seek clarification before submitting the form.
* This form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel only.
