# Headphone Line Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Headphone Line Pre Order Form is for users to pre-order a headphone line. It's used to collect contact information and order details from users who are interested in purchasing a headphone line. If you've already pre-ordered or have other reasons for filling out this form, please don't fill it out.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Choose a date and time for your pre-order.
5. Add any additional order details in the text field.
6. Select the quantity of headphone lines you want to pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your pre-order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your pre-order.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose the date you'd like to pre-order the headphone line.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time you'd like to pre-order the headphone line.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Add any additional details about your pre-order in this text field.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Select how many headphone lines you'd like to pre-order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or questions, you can add them in the "Order Details" text field.
* We'll contact you via email and phone to confirm your pre-order and provide further instructions.
