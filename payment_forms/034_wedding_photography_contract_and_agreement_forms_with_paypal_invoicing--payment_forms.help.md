# Wedding Photography Contract And Agreement Forms With Paypal Invoicing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment from clients for wedding photography services. It includes fields for client details, payment information, and service details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the correct format (e.g. `john.doe@example.com`).
3. Select your **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu (PayPal, Stripe, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
4. If applicable, enter the **Invoice Date** and **Invoice Time** for the payment.
5. Enter the **Photo Session** details in the text field.
6. Select the **Photo Packages** you would like to purchase from the multiple select menu (e.g. Package 1, Package 2, Package 3).
7. Enter the number of **Photo Prints** you would like to purchase.
8. Enter the number of **Photo Albums** you would like to purchase.
9. Enter the number of **Photo Shares** you would like to purchase.
10. Enter the number of **Photo Drops** you would like to purchase.
11. Enter the number of **Photo Folio** you would like to purchase.
12. Enter the additional details for **Photo Stitching**, **Photo Montage**, **Photo Retouching**, **Photo Restoration**, **Photo Color Correction**, **Photo Composition**, **Photo Toning**, and **Additional Retouching** in the text fields.
13. Enter the **Retouching Rate** and **Additional Retouching Rate** for each service in the number fields.
14. Select the **Digital Delivery** and **Physical Delivery** options for the services.
15. Finally, please provide a **Signature** to confirm the contract.
