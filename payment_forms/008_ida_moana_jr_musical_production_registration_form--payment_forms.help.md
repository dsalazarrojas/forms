# ida_moana_jr_musical_production_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ida_moana_jr_musical_production_registration_form is used to collect and store information about a musical production registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review all fields to ensure you have provided all the required information.
2. Fill out each field with the relevant details.
3. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Name** (`production_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the musical production.
* **Production Type** (`production_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of production (e.g., Children's Theatre, Musical, Other).
* **Production Dates** (`production_dates`, `date`, required: false): Enter the dates of the production.
* **Production Time** (`production_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the production time.
* **Production Address** (`production_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the production.
* **Production Contact** (`production_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact information for the production.
* **Production Email** (`production_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the production.
* **Production Description** (`production_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a description of the production.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter the tool assigned to the production.
* **Assigned Contact** (`assigned_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact assigned to the production.
* **Production Status** (`production_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the production (e.g., Draft, Published, Archived).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the payment terms (e.g., PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment method (e.g., PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment status (e.g., Paid, Unpaid).
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the registration date.
* **Production Cost** (`production_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the cost of the production.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* Review and update the form as needed to ensure accurate and complete information.
