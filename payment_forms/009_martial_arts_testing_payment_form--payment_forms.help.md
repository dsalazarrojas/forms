# Martial Arts Testing Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information for martial arts testing. It is divided into several sections to ensure that you provide the necessary details for your payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of testing you are applying for (Testing for Belt or Testing for Promotion or Other).
2. Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
3. Enter your student's name.
4. Enter your student's phone number.
5. Enter your student's email address.
6. Choose how you would like to make the payment (Credit Card, PayPal, or Cash).
7. Enter the payment amount.
8. Enter the name of your martial arts school (Dojo).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Testing Type
* **Testing Type**: This field is used to select the type of testing you are applying for (Testing for Belt, Testing for Promotion, or Other).
*   <label>Testing Type</label> (`testing_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of testing you are applying for, such as Testing for Belt or Testing for Promotion.

### Test Name
*   <label>Test Name</label> (`test_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the test you are applying for.

### Student Name
*   <label>Student Name</label> (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your student's name as it appears on their official identification.

### Student Phone
*   <label>Student Phone</label> (`student_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your student's phone number.

### Student Email
*   <label>Student Email</label> (`student_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your student's email address.

### Payment Method
*   <label>Payment Method</label> (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to make the payment (Credit Card, PayPal, or Cash).

### Payment Amount
*   <label>Payment Amount</label> (`payment_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount you would like to pay for the testing.

### Dojo Name
*   <label>Dojo Name</label> (`dojo_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your martial arts school (Dojo).

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the payment information is accurate to avoid any issues with payment processing.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure that you have provided all the necessary information.
