# Budget Liquidation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Liquidation Form is used to gather information about a borrower's loan situation. This form is intended to help the lender assess the borrower's financial status and make informed decisions regarding the loan. Please ensure that you are submitting this form for the correct purpose and that the information is accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the borrower's Full Name in the "Borrower Full Name" field.
2. Enter the borrower's Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter the borrower's Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the borrower's Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Enter the loan number in the "Loan Number" field.
6. Enter the Original Loan Amount and Outstanding Loan Balance in the "Original Loan Amount" and "Outstanding Loan Balance" fields respectively.
7. Specify the reason for the liquidation in the "Purpose of Liquidation" field.
8. Select the type of asset being liquidated from the options provided in the "Type of Asset Being Liquidated" field.
9. Provide a detailed description of the asset in the "Asset Description" field.
10. Enter the estimated value of the asset in the "Estimated Total Asset Value" field.
11. Select the proposed liquidation timeline from the options provided in the "Proposed Liquidation Timeline" field.
12. Enter the expected proceeds from the liquidation in the "Expected Proceeds from Liquidation" field.
13. Specify the proposed payment plan in the "Proposed Payment Plan" field.
14. If applicable, enter the name of any intermediary or broker involved in the sale in the "Sale Through Intermediary or Broker" field.
15. If applicable, enter the estimated fees of any intermediary or broker in the "Estimated Broker or Intermediary Fees" field.
16. Indicate whether a professional appraisal has been completed in the "Professional Appraisal Completed" field.
17. Indicate whether the liquidation is due to financial hardship in the "Financial Hardship Claim" field.
18. Finally, enter the submission date in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Borrower Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter the borrower's full name.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter the borrower's email address.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter the borrower's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, required): Enter the borrower's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Loan Number** (5, text, required): Enter the loan number.
* **Original Loan Amount** (6, number, required): Enter the original loan amount.
* **Outstanding Loan Balance** (7, number, required): Enter the current outstanding balance.
* **Purpose of Liquidation** (8, text, required): Specify the reason for the liquidation.
* **Type of Asset Being Liquidated** (9, select_multiple, required): Select the type of asset being liquidated from the options provided.
* **Asset Description** (10, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the asset.
* **Estimated Total Asset Value** (11, number, required): Enter the estimated value of the asset.
* **Proposed Liquidation Timeline** (12, select_one, required): Select the proposed liquidation timeline from the options provided.
* **Expected Proceeds from Liquidation** (13, number, required): Enter the expected proceeds from the liquidation.
* **Proposed Payment Plan** (14, text, required): Specify the proposed payment plan.
* **Sale Through Intermediary or Broker** (15, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of any intermediary or broker involved in the sale.
* **Estimated Broker or Intermediary Fees** (16, number, optional): If applicable, enter the estimated fees of any intermediary or broker.
* **Professional Appraisal Completed** (17, select_one, required): Indicate whether a professional appraisal has been completed.
* **Financial Hardship Claim** (18, select_one, required): Indicate whether the liquidation is due to financial hardship.
* **Submission Date** (19, date, required): Enter the submission date.
