# Freelancer Registration Fee Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for freelancers to register and pay for their registration fee. It collects necessary details and payment information to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Freelancer Details in the first section.
2. Enter the Registration Fee amount in the second section.
3. Choose your preferred Payment Method from the third section.
4. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions in the fourth section.
5. Choose to Submit or Cancel your registration in the final two sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Freelancer Details** (`Freelancer Details`, `text`, required): This is the section where you need to provide your basic information as a freelancer. Please fill out this section with your details accurately.
- **Registration Fee** (`Registration Fee`, `number`, required): This is where you need to enter the amount of the registration fee. Please ensure you enter the correct amount.
- **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided. You can select multiple options if needed.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `note`, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of registration. This is an important step to ensure you understand the terms.
- **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your intention to submit your registration.
- **Cancel** (`Cancel`, `select_one`, required): Cancel your registration if you change your mind or need to stop the process.

## Tips
Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and honestly. Review your details before submitting your registration.
