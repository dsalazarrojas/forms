# Customized Apron Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect custom order information from customers, including their contact details, apron style and color preferences, order details, and payment methods. Please provide the following information to complete your customized apron order:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your customer information in the "customer_info" field. This field is not required but is a good opportunity to introduce yourself and share any relevant information about your order.
2. Fill out the "customer_name" field with your actual name, as it will be used for communication purposes.
3. Choose your desired "apron_style" from the dropdown menu to select from one of the available options.
4. Select one or multiple "apron_color" options to customize the color of your apron.
5. Pick your desired "size" from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the "quantity" of aprons you would like to order.
7. Choose your "material" preference from the dropdown menu.
8. If you have any custom message, you can type it in the "custom_message" field.
9. Select the type of custom message you would like to have on your apron from the "custom_message_type" dropdown menu.
10. If applicable, enter your custom message in the "custom_message_text" field.
11. Enter the date of your order in the "order_date" field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
12. If applicable, select the time of your order in the "order_time" field.
13. Enter your "email" for communication purposes.
14. Add your "phone" number for contact purposes.
15. If you have a "company" affiliation, enter it for our records.
16. Enter your "address" for delivery purposes.
17. Enter your "zip" code for delivery purposes.
18. If you have any order notes, use the "order_note" field for this purpose.
19. Enter the total value of your order in the "order_total" field.
20. Select your "payment_method" options from the dropdown menu.
21. Enter the actual "payment_amount" you are paying.
22. Select the "order_status" of your order from the dropdown menu.
23. Enter any additional "notes" about your order in the field.
24. Select the "payment_status" of your payment from the dropdown menu.
25. Finally, select who the "assigned_to" should be handling your order from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info**: Enter your customer information, if any.
* **customer_name**: Fill in your actual name for communication purposes.
* **apron_style**: Choose your desired apron style from the dropdown menu.
* **apron_color**: Select one or multiple apron colors to match your style.
* **size**: Pick your desired apron size from the dropdown menu.
* **quantity**: Enter the number of aprons you would like to order.
* **material**: Choose your desired apron material from the dropdown menu.
* **custom_message**: If you have a custom message, type it here.
* **custom_message_type**: Select the type of custom message you would like on your apron.
* **custom_message_text**: If applicable, enter your custom message text.
* **order_date**: Enter the date of your order in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **order_time**: If applicable, select the time of your order.
* **email**: Enter your email for communication purposes.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **company**: If you have a company affiliation, enter it here.
* **address**: Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **zip**: Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **order_note**: If you have any notes, use this field for this purpose.
* **order_total**: Enter the total value of your order.
* **payment_method**: Select your payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **payment_amount**: Enter the actual payment amount.
* **order_status**: Select the status of your order from the dropdown menu.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **payment_status**: Select the status of your payment from the dropdown menu.
* **assigned_to**: Finally, select who should handle your order from the dropdown menu.
