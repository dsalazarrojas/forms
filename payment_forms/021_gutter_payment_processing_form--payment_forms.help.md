# gutter_payment_processing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for processing payments related to gutters, which can include services or products associated with gutter installation, maintenance, or repair. The goal is to provide a clear and concise way to gather the necessary payment information for these specific transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant details about the payment, such as the payment amount and method.
2. Select the correct payment status (Accepted, Failed, or Pending) and payment type (Cash, Card, or Online Payment).
3. Provide any additional service or job details if necessary.
4. Schedule the payment if applicable.
5. Review and submit the form to process the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Details**: (`gutter_payment_form`, text, required) - Enter the details about the payment, including the amount and any relevant notes.
* **Service Details**: (`service_details`, text, optional) - Provide any additional information about the service or job involved.
* **Schedule Form**: (`schedule_form`, text, optional) - Enter any scheduling details for the payment or service.
* **Payment Method**: (`payment_method`, text, optional) - Choose the payment method (Cash, Card, or Online Payment).
* **Job Details**: (`job_details`, text, optional) - Enter any additional job or service details.
* **Payment Amount**: (`payment_amount`, number, optional) - Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Terms**: (`payment_terms`, text, optional) - Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Schedule Time**: (`schedule_time`, time, optional) - Enter the schedule time for the payment or service.
* **Payment Status**: (`payment_status`, select_one, optional) - Select the payment status (Accepted, Failed, or Pending).
* **Payment Type**: (`payment_type`, select_multiple, optional) - Choose the payment type (Cash, Card, or Online Payment).
