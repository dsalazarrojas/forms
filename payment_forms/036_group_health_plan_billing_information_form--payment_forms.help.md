# Group Health Plan Billing Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect billing information from groups who are interested in participating in a group health plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employer's details in the "Employer Details" field.
2. Provide the billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
3. Enter the billing contact information in the "Billing Contact" field.
4. Enter the plan details in the "Plan Details" field.
5. Select the payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
6. Enter the total amount in the "Total Amount" field.
7. Enter the tax rate in the "Tax Rate" field.
8. Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
9. Enter any additional payment terms or information in the "Payment Terms" field.
10. Provide any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Details (id: 1, Employer Details, text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the employer, their contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Billing Address (id: 2, Billing Address, text, required: false)**: Enter the billing address where payments will be sent.
* **Billing Contact (id: 3, Billing Contact, text, required: false)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for billing.
* **Plan Details (id: 4, Plan Details, text, required: false)**: Enter the details of the group health plan, including any relevant dates or numbers.
* **Payment Info (id: 5, Payment Info, select_one, required: false)**: Select from one of the following options for payment:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Total Amount (id: 6, Total Amount, number, required: false)**: Enter the total amount owed.
* **Tax Rate (id: 7, Tax Rate, number, required: false)**: Enter the tax rate to be applied to the payment.
* **Payment Method (id: 8, Payment Method, select_one, required: false)**: Select from one of the following options for payment:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Payment Terms (id: 9, Payment Terms, text, required: false)**: Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Additional Info (id: 10, Additional Info, text, required: false)**: Enter any additional information about the payment or billing process.
