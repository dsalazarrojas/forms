# Payment Term Definition Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Term Definition Request Form is used to collect information from users about payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully.
3. Ensure the form is filled out correctly to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Term Request** (select_one: Payment Term Request): Choose 'Yes' if payment terms are requested.
* **Payment Term** (select_one: Payment Term): Choose 'Yes' if payment terms are required.
* **Department** (select_multiple: Department): Choose all departments that will be responsible for payment terms. 
* **Date of Request** (date: Date of Request): Enter the date the payment term request was made.
* **Time of Request** (time: Time of Request): Enter the time the payment term request was made.
* **Reason for Request** (text: Reason for Request): Enter a brief description of the reason for the payment term request.
* **Comments** (text: Comments): Enter any additional comments about the payment term request.
* **Submitted By** (text: Submitted By): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted On** (date: Submitted On): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Approved By** (text: Approved By): Enter the name of the person who approved the request.

Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory, please ensure to fill them out accurately.
