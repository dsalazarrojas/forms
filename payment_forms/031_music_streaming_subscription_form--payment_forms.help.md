# Music Streaming Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your music streaming preferences and subscription details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form carefully, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Start on the first page, "Music Streaming Details", where you'll be asked to provide basic information about your music preferences.
3. On the second page, "Payment Info", you'll be asked to enter a number, which is assumed to be a payment method identifier.
4. On the third page, "Plan Selection", you'll choose one of the available plan options (Apple Music, Spotify, Tidal, or other options may be available in the future).
5. On the fourth page, "Payment Method", you'll be asked to select multiple payment methods you're interested in (Yes/No).
6. On the fifth page, "Email", you'll enter your email address.
7. On the sixth page, "Phone", you'll enter your phone number.
8. On the seventh page, "Payment Plan", you'll be asked to enter a number.
9. On the eighth page, "Start Date", you'll select a date for your subscription to begin.
10. Finally, on the ninth page, "Subscription Plan", you'll confirm your subscription plan choice (Yes/No).
