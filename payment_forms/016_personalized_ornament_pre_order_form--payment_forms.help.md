# Personalized Ornament Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your personal information to create a customized ornament just for you. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate details for your special gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Provide your address for delivery or any other relevant information.
6. Select a date and time for the delivery or event when you would like the ornament to be delivered.
7. Select a time of day for the delivery or event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it will appear on the ornament.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it will appear on the ornament.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for any updates or contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up contact.
* **Address** (`address`, note, optional): Enter your address for delivery or any other relevant information.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the delivery or event when you would like the ornament to be delivered.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time of day for the delivery or event.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, please include them in the "Address" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Please allow sufficient time for production and delivery of the customized ornament.
