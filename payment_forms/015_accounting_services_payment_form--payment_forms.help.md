# Accounting Services Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit payments for accounting services, ensuring that all necessary information is collected to process payments accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the payment details, including:
	* Payer name and email
	* Invoice number
	* Total amount due
2. Select the currency and payment method
3. Enter the payment date
4. Provide any additional information or instructions as necessary

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payer name** (`payer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual or company being invoiced.
* **Payer email** (`payer_email`, email, required): Enter the email address to send the receipt to.
* **Invoice number** (`invoice_number`, text, required): Enter the reference number for the invoice or billing ID.
* **Amount due** (`amount_due`, number, required): Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Currency** (`currency`, select_one, required): Select the currency for the payment (USD, EUR, GBP, Local).
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay (Credit card, Bank transfer, PayPal, Check).
* **Billing address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the address for card or invoice billing.
* **Payment date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date payment is being made.
* **Payment reference** (`payment_reference`, text, optional): Enter the transaction ID or bank reference.
* **Receipt requested** (`receipt_requested`, select_one, required): Indicate if an emailed receipt is required (True or False).
* **Authorizing signature** (`authorizing_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as electronic authorization.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special billing instructions or information.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check the currency and payment method to ensure they match your needs.
* If you require an emailed receipt, please ensure you enter a valid email address.
* If you are paying by credit card, please ensure you provide the correct billing address.
