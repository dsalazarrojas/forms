# payment_reconciliation_support_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "payment_reconciliation_support_contact_form" is a support form used by customers to contact our payment reconciliation team for assistance with payment discrepancies or other payment-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your contact number and email.
2. Choose the type of support you are seeking (e.g., Discrepancy, Reconciliation, or Other).
3. Describe the issue you are experiencing.
4. Upload any supporting documents relevant to your request (e.g., images, invoices, or other attachments).
5. Enter any additional comments or messages regarding your issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer** (`Customer`, `text`, not required): Provide your customer name or company name, if applicable.
* **Issue** (`Issue`, `text`, not required): Please describe the payment issue you are experiencing (e.g., dispute, refund, or other discrepancies).
* **Request Type** (`Request Type`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the type of support you require (e.g., Discrepancy, Reconciliation, or Other).
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact number to enable us to contact you for further assistance.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you for further assistance.
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or details about your issue.
* **Upload Document 1** (`Upload Document`, `text`, not required): Upload any relevant documents to support your claim (e.g., invoices, receipts, or other supporting documents).
* **Upload Document 2** (`Upload Document 2`, `text`, not required): Upload any additional documents or attachments as supporting evidence (e.g., images, emails, or other relevant documents).

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorization to complete this form.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue.
* Attach any relevant documents or evidence to support your claim.
* Use the "Other" option for requests that do not fit into the provided categories.
