# Staff Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Reference Form is a tool used by the summer camp staff members to provide a professional reference for a current or former staff member. This form allows staff members to submit contact information for a reference, which can be used by the camp to verify employment history or performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Staff Reference Form" link to access the form.
2. Enter the required information, including the first and last name of the staff member, their email address, and phone number.
3. Enter the name of the referee making the reference.
4. Enter the title and organization of the referee.
5. Enter the referee's position and length of service.
6. Enter the staff member's position and years of service.
7. Provide any additional comments about the staff member.
8. Choose whether or not to allow the camp to contact the referee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: This field is not required, but it's a good idea to enter a brief title to help identify the purpose of the form.
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the staff member you are making the reference for.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the staff member you are making the reference for.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the staff member you are making the reference for.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member you are making the reference for.
* **Referee Name**: Enter the name of the person making the reference.
* **Referee Title**: Enter the title of the person making the reference.
* **Referee Organization**: Enter the organization of the person making the reference.
* **Referee Position**: Enter the position of the person making the reference.
* **Referee Length of Service**: Enter the length of time the referee has known the staff member.
* **Staff Position**: Enter the position of the staff member being referenced.
* **Staff Years of Service**: Enter the years of service of the staff member being referenced.
* **Referee Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the staff member you are making the reference for.
* **Agree Terms**: Check the box to allow the camp to contact the referee, or uncheck to keep their contact information private.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the staff member you are making the reference for.
* Use a professional format when entering the referee's title and position.
* Proofread the form carefully to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.
