# summer-activities-staff-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in working as staff members for summer activities. It aims to collect information about their background, availability, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide your name and contact email address.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Indicate your employment status, such as full-time, part-time, or internship.
4. Select the areas of work experience you have, such as camping, teaching, or recreation.
5. Choose your education level, which can be high school, bachelor’s degree, or master’s degree.
6. Enter the dates you are available to work.
7. Choose the days you are available to work, such as Monday to Sunday.
8. Select the duration of time you are available to work, which can be 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or 3 hours.
9. Enter the location where you are available to work.
10. Provide your work phone number and home phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **summer_activities_Staff_Application_Form**: This is the title of the form, which is required for identification purposes.
* **Name**: Please enter your name in this field.
* **Contact_Email**: Enter your contact email address here. This will be used for communication and contact purposes.
* **Date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Employment_Status**: Select your current employment status:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Internship
* **Work_Experience**: Select the areas of work experience you have:
	+ Camping
	+ Teaching
	+ Recreation
* **Education_Level**: Choose your education level:
	+ High School
	+ Bachelor’s Degree
	+ Master’s Degree
* **Available_Dates**: Enter the dates you are available to work in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Available_Days**: Choose the days you are available to work:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Available_Time**: Select the duration of time you are available to work:
	+ 30 minutes
	+ 1 hour
	+ 2 hours
	+ 3 hours
* **Work_Location**: Enter the location where you are available to work.
* **Work_Phone**: Provide your work phone number.
* **Home_Phone**: Provide your home phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for dates (mm/dd/yyyy).
* Choose the correct options for employment status, work experience, education level, and available duration.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
