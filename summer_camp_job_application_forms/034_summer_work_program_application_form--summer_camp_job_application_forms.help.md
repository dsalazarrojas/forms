# Summer Work Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information from applicants for the summer work program. Please fill out the form with accurate and relevant information to ensure a successful evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your Personal Information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide your Contact Information, including your email and phone number.
3. Share your Work Experience, including any relevant work history or skills.
4. List your Education background.
5. Select the skills you have that are relevant to the summer work program.
6. Choose the type of job you are applying for.
7. Enter your availability to work and your preferred work schedule.
8. Add any additional Notes about your application.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Provide your phone number.
11. Enter the date and time of submission.
12. (These fields are for internal use only and do not need to be filled out.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Personal Information** 
Personal Information: This field is for your name, address, and any other relevant details about yourself. Please fill out this field with accurate and relevant information.

### 2. **Contact Information**
Contact Information: This field is for your contact email and phone number. Please enter your current contact information for future communication.

### 3. **Work Experience**
Work Experience: This field is for sharing any relevant work history or skills you have that are relevant to the summer work program. Please provide detailed information about your previous work experience.

### 4. **Education**
Education: This field is for sharing your educational background. Please list any relevant education or training you have received.

### 5. **Skills**
Skills: This field is for selecting the skills you have that are relevant to the summer work program. Please choose all the skills that apply to you.

### 6. **Job Type**
Job Type: This field is for choosing the type of job you are applying for. Please select the job type that best fits your qualifications and experience.

### 7. **Work Availability**
Work Availability: This field is for entering your availability to work. Please enter the date and time range you are available to start working.

### 8. **Work Schedule**
Work Schedule: This field is for entering your preferred work schedule. Please select the days and time you are available to work.

### 9. **Notes**
Notes: This field is for adding any additional comments or notes about your application. Please provide any relevant information that will help us evaluate your application.

### 10. **Email**
Email: This field is for entering your email address. Please enter a valid email address that we can use for communication.

### 11. **Phone Number**
Phone Number: This field is for entering your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number that we can use for communication.

### 12-15. **Internal Use Only**
These fields are not for public display and are used for internal record keeping purposes only. Please do not fill out this section.

## Tips
- Please fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your application and look forward to reviewing it.
