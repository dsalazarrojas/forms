# Training Instructor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Instructor Application Form is a tool used by potential instructors to apply to work at our summer camp. It is intended for candidates who are interested in leading training sessions and activities during the camp season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. This is where we will contact you with further information about your application.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Describe your Physical Capability in the "Physical Capability" field. This is important for us to understand any limitations that may impact your ability to perform certain tasks.
6. If you have any health conditions that may impact your ability to work, please describe them in the "Health Condition" field.
7. Provide the name of an emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
8. Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person, including their phone number, in the "Emergency Contact Phone" and "Emergency Contact Address" fields.
9. Specify your relation to the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Relation" field.
10. List your relevant Education and Experience in the "Education" and "Experience" fields.
11. If you hold any training certifications, please specify them in the "Training Certification" field.
12. If applicable, describe any First Aid training you have received in the "First Aid" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it is listed on your identification.
- **Last Name**: Enter your full last name as it is listed on your identification.
- **Email**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to reach you.
- **Physical Capability**: Describe any physical limitations that may impact your ability to perform certain tasks. This can include mobility issues, hearing or vision impairments, or other physical abilities that may require accommodation.
- **Health Condition**: If you have any health conditions that may impact your ability to work, please describe them in this field.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of a person we can contact in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Relation**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
- **Education**: List any relevant education or certifications you have obtained that align with our program.
- **Experience**: Describe your relevant work or volunteer experience.
- **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
- **Training Certification**: If applicable, list any relevant training certifications you have obtained.
- **First Aid**: If you have received First Aid training, please describe it in this field.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields, but feel free to provide additional information if you have it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your interest in becoming a training instructor at our summer camp, and we look forward to reviewing your application.
