# Camp Staff Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interest in joining our summer camp staff. We use the information you provide to assess your suitability for the role and to contact you for further discussion or application.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your **Contact Information** (fields 2, 4, 5, 6, 7) with the required details.
2.  Choose the **Preferred Age Group to Work With** (field 9) that you are most comfortable working with.
3.  Provide your **General Summer Availability** (field 10) in terms of weeks or dates.
4.  Let us know how you **Heard About This Opportunity** (field 11).
5.  Choose the **Best Method of Contact** (field 12) for us to follow up with you.
6.  Confirm your agreement to our **Follow-up** (field 13) to discuss further.
7.  Finally, click **Submit** to send your Expression of Interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Your Full Name (2)**: Please enter your name.
*   **Contact Email (3)**: Your email address where we can contact you.
*   **Current Age (4)**: Enter your current age.
*   **Current School or Organization (5)**: If you are currently at school or work, please provide the name of it.
*   **What draws you to camp staff work? (6)**: Explain what motivates you to apply for this role.
*   **Special Skills or Talents (7)**: Select all the areas where you have skills or interests in.
*   **Previous Camp Experience (8)**: Choose if you have been a camper or staff member before.
*   **Preferred Age Group to Work With (9)**: Select the age group you are most comfortable working with.
*   **General Summer Availability (10)**: Indicate your availability for the summer.
*   **How did you hear about this opportunity? (11)**: Choose the source of your knowledge about this job posting.
*   **Best Method of Contact (12)**: Select the best way for us to contact you.
*   **Consent to Follow-up (13)**: Confirm that you are willing to be contacted for further discussion or application.
*   **Date of Submission (14)**: This field is automatically populated when you submit the form.
