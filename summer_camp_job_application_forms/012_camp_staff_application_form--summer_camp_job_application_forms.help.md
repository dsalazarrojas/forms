# Camp Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Staff Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants interested in working as staff at our summer camp. This form is used to collect personal contact information, position preferences, availability, and other relevant details that will help us consider your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Current Address, City, State, and Zip Code).
2. Choose the position you are interested in (Cabin Counselor, Activity Director, Kitchen Staff, Nurse, Program Director, Maintenance, or Other).
3. Select your availability to start working and the duration you can commit to (Full summer, 4-7 weeks, 2-3 weeks, 1 week, or Flexible).
4. Provide details about your previous camp or youth work experience, if any.
5. List your special skills or certifications (e.g., Lifeguard, First Aid, CPR).
6. Mention any relevant education or training you have received.
7. If applicable, mention languages spoken.
8. Provide information about two professional references, including their name and relationship to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your first name as you prefer to be known.
- **Last Name**: Your last name as you prefer to be known.
- **Email Address**: A valid email address we can use to contact you.
- **Phone Number**: A valid phone number we can use to contact you.
- **Current Address**: Your current residential address.
- **City**: The city where you reside.
- **State**: The state where you reside.
- **Zip Code**: Your postal code.
- **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Position Interested In**: Choose the position you are interested in working as. 
- **When can you start**: Select the date you can start working.
- **How long can you commit**: Choose the duration you can commit to working.
- **Previous camp or youth work experience**: If applicable, describe your previous experience working at a camp or in youth work.
- **Special skills or certifications**: List any special skills or certifications you have (e.g., Lifeguard, First Aid, CPR).
- **Relevant education or training**: Mention any relevant education or training you have received.
- **Languages spoken**: If applicable, list languages you speak.
- **Reference 1 Name and Phone**: Provide the name and phone number of your first reference.
- **Relationship to Reference 1**: Describe your relationship to your first reference.
- **Reference 2 Name and Phone**: Provide the name and phone number of your second reference.
- **Relationship to Reference 2**: Describe your relationship to your second reference.
- **Additional Information or Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional information or comments about your application.

Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to increase your chances of getting considered for a position at our summer camp!
