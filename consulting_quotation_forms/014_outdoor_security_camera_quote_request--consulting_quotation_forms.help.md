# Outdoor Security Camera Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers requesting a quote for outdoor security camera installation. It will help you gather all necessary details about the customer, property, and camera preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Customer Info" section.
2. Enter your property details in the "Property Details" section.
3. Specify the project scope in the "Project Scope" section.
4. Choose your contact preferences in the "Contact Preferences" section.
5. Indicate your installation preferences in the "Installation Preferences" section.
6. Specify your camera preferences in the "Camera Preferences" section.
7. Provide any follow-up information in the "Follow Up Info" section.

* **Property Details** (`Property Details`, text, required: false): Describe your property, including its location and any notable features.
* **Project Scope** (`Project Scope`, text, required: false): Outline the specific features or goals of your project.
* **Contact Preferences** (`Contact Preferences`, text, required: false): Check if you'd like to be contacted by phone, email, or in-person.
* **Installation Preferences** (`Installation Preferences`, text, required: false): Indicate if you have any preferences for camera installation, such as time of day.
* **Camera Preferences** (`Camera Preferences`, text, required: false): Specify the type of cameras you're interested in, such as IP or PTZ cameras.
* **Follow Up Info** (`Follow Up Info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information to help us follow up on your quote.
