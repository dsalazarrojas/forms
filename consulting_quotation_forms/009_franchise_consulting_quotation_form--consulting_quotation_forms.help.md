# Franchise Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from both consultants and customers in the context of a franchise consulting quotation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
* Fill out the form in the following order:
	+ Page 1: Provide your consultant information.
	+ Page 2: Enter your contact information.
	+ Page 3: Enter your customer information.
	+ Page 4: Enter quotation information.
	+ Page 5: Select the services you will provide.
	+ Page 6: Choose the dates of service.
	+ Page 7: Enter the time of service.
	+ Page 8: Enter your quotation amount.
	+ Page 9: Enter the total cost.
	+ Page 10: Sign and date the form as the consultant.
	+ Page 11: Sign and date the form as the customer.
	+ Page 12: Select the agreement you have with the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant Information (Page 1)** (`consultant_info`, `text`, required/optional): This is the section where you, as the consultant, will enter your information, such as your name, title, and contact details.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Customer Information (Page 3)** (`customer_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Quotation Information (Page 4)** (`quotation_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the details of the quotation you are providing to the customer.
* **Services (Page 5)** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the services you will provide to the customer.
* **Dates (Page 6)** (`dates`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the dates for the services you will provide.
* **Time (Page 7)** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of service.
* **Quotation (Page 8)** (`quotation`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount of the quotation.
* **Total Cost (Page 9)** (`total_cost`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the services.
* **Consultant Signature (Page 10)** (`consultant_signature`, `note`, required/optional): Sign and date this form as the consultant.
* **Customer Signature (Page 11)** (`customer_signature`, `note`, required/optional): Sign and date this form as the customer.
* **Customer Agreement (Page 12)** (`customer_agreement`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the agreement you have with the customer.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorities to provide the services.
* Read and understand the agreement with the customer before signing.
