# Commercial Proposal Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for submitting commercial proposals to the organization. It is a comprehensive form that gathers essential information about the vendor or company, proposal details, and other relevant information for evaluation and consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's information, including name, address, and contact details.
2. Provide your contact person's name and email address for proposal communications.
3. Add your proposal's details, including title, category, and submission date.
4. Select the relevant proposal category and project timeline.
5. Enter your proposed budget and pricing structure.
6. Choose your payment terms.
7. If applicable, upload your detailed proposal document and provide references.
8. Provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (2)**: The legal name of your company as per your registration documents.
* **Company Address (3)**: Your company's full business address.
* **Contact Person (4)**: Name of the primary contact person for this proposal.
* **Contact Email (5)**: Email address for proposal communications with us.
* **Contact Phone (6)**: Direct phone number for easy contact.
* **Proposal Details (7)**: Additional information about the proposal being submitted.
* **RFP Reference Number (8)**: If this proposal is related to a specific RFP, provide the reference number.
* **Proposal Title (9)**: Title or name of your proposal.
* **Proposal Category (10)**: Select the category of services you are proposing.
* **Submission Date (11)**: Date of proposal submission.
* **Project Timeline (12)**: Estimated project duration in months.
* **Budget Proposal (13)**: Total proposed budget in USD.
* **Pricing Structure (14)**: How you structure your pricing (Fixed, Hourly, Retainer, Cost Plus, Performance Based).
* **Payment Terms (15)**: Proposed payment terms (Net 15, Net 30, Net 45, Net 60, Milestone Based, Upfront Payment).
* **Team Size (16)**: Number of team members assigned to the project.
* **Relevant Experience (17)**: Years of relevant experience in this field.
* **Certifications Held (18)**: Relevant professional certifications (if none, select 'None').
* **Proposal Document (19)**: Upload your detailed proposal document.
* **References Available (20)**: Can you provide client references (Yes - Included, Yes - Upon Request, No).
* **Additional Comments (21)**: Any additional information or comments.
