Nominate An Organizer For Support - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to nominate an individual or group for support. Fill out this form to provide information about the nominee and the reasons for their nomination. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are nominating the correct individual or group and providing all required information.
2. Select the appropriate fields for the nomination.
3. Fill out the nomination form fields with the relevant information.
4. Select the options that apply to the nomination.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
6. Verify that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
