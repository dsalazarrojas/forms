# Workplace Safety Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Innovation Award nomination form is designed to help individuals and organizations submit nominations for recognizing and celebrating innovations that have made a positive impact on workplace safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief title for the nomination.
2. Enter the name and email address of the person submitting the nomination (the nominator).
3. Enter the organization and department of the nominator (if applicable).
4. Enter the nominator's job title, manager's name, and manager's email address (if applicable).
5. Select the nomination date and time (if applicable).
6. Enter the location of the nomination (if applicable).
7. Enter any additional details about the nomination.
8. Agree that all the information provided is accurate.
9. Enter any additional information that you want to add to the nomination (if necessary).
10. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title of Nomination** (id: 3, text, required): Enter a brief title that summarizes the nomination.
* **Nominator Name** (id: 4, text, required): Enter the full name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (id: 5, email, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone** (id: 6, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Job Title** (id: 7, text, optional): Enter the job title of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Organization** (id: 8, text, required): Enter the name of the organization submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Department** (id: 9, text, optional): Enter the department of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Job Function** (id: 10, text, optional): Enter the job function of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Manager Name** (id: 11, text, optional): Enter the name of the manager of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Manager Job Title** (id: 12, text, optional): Enter the job title of the manager of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Manager Phone** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the manager of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Manager Email** (id: 14, email, optional): Enter the email address of the manager of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nomination Date** (id: 15, date, required): Enter the date when the innovation was implemented or recognized.
* **Nomination Time** (id: 16, time, optional): Enter the time when the innovation was implemented or recognized (if applicable).
* **Nomination Location** (id: 17, text, optional): Enter the location where the innovation was implemented or recognized (if applicable).
* **Nomination Details** (id: 18, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the innovation.
* **I agree that all the information provided is accurate** (id: 19, text, required): Confirm that all the information provided is accurate.
* **Is there anything else you want to add to this nomination?** (id: 20, text, optional): Enter any additional information that you want to add to the nomination (if necessary).
* **Type** (id: 21, text, optional): Enter the type of information you want to add (if necessary).
* **Description** (id: 22, text, optional): Enter the description of the additional information (if necessary).
* **File** (id: 23, text, optional): Enter the file associated with the additional information (if necessary).
* **Date** (id: 24, date, optional): Enter the date of the additional information (if necessary).
* **Comments** (id: 25, text, optional): Enter any comments about the additional information (if necessary).
