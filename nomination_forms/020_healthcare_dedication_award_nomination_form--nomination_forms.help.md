# Healthcare Dedication Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for nominating an individual or team for the Healthcare Dedication Award. Please fill out this form to submit a nomination for an outstanding healthcare professional who has demonstrated exceptional dedication and service to the healthcare industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Nomination Title.
2. Enter the Nominee's Name.
3. Provide a brief description of the nomination.
4. Select the Submission Date and Time.
5. Choose the Nomination Category and Type (Award 1, Award 2, or Award 3).
6. Select the Nominator's name from the list.
7. Fill in the Reason for Nominating.
8. Optionally, enter the Submission Date and Time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the nominee you are nominating.
* **Nomination Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the nominee's work or achievements.
* **Submission Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are submitting the nomination. The format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Submission Time** (Number, Optional): Enter the time you are submitting the nomination. Note: This is for tracking purposes only.
* **Nominator** (Select One, Optional): Choose the name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Reason for Nominating** (Text, Optional): Describe why you are nominating the healthcare professional.
* **Nomination Category** (Select One, Optional): Select the award category for the nomination.
* **Nomination Type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable award types for the nomination.
* **Submitter** (Select One, Optional): Choose the name of the person submitting the nomination.

Note: Some fields are not required, but please ensure that you provide as much information as possible to make the nomination strong.
