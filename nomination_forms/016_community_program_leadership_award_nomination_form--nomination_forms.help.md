# Community Program Leadership Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Program Leadership Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information from community members and leaders about outstanding individuals who have made a significant impact within the community. This form helps identify and recognize individuals who have demonstrated exceptional leadership qualities and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the form's instructions and fields.
2. Provide accurate and complete information about the nominee, including their full name, email address, phone number, and role within the community.
3. Describe the nominee's leadership qualities and specific projects or initiatives they've led.
4. Share how the nominee has impacted the community, focusing on tangible results.
5. Confirm your relationship with the nominee and willingness to provide a testimonial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the person nominating the community leader.
* **Nominator Email Address** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person nominating the community leader.
* **Nominator Phone Number** (`nominator_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person nominating the community leader.
* **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Organization/Role** (`nominee_organization_role`, `text`, required): Describe the nominee's role or position within the community.
* **Nominee Email Address** (`nominee_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person being nominated, if applicable.
* **Nominee Phone Number** (`nominee_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person being nominated, if applicable.
* **Nominee Service Years** (`nominee_service_years`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of years the nominee has served in the community.
* **Leadership Qualities** (`leadership_qualities_desc`, `text`, required): Describe the nominee's leadership qualities and strengths.
* **Specific Project/Initiative** (`specific_project_initiative`, `text`, required): Share a specific project or initiative led by the nominee.
* **Community Impact** (`community_impact_desc`, `text`, required): Share how the nominee has impacted the community, focusing on tangible results.
* **Testimonial Willingness** (`testimonial_willingness_check`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you're willing to provide a testimonial about the nominee.
* **Relationship to Nominee** (`relationship_to_nominee_check`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your relationship with the nominee.
* **Nomination Deadline** (`nomination_deadline_note`, `note`, not required): A reminder that nominations must be submitted by Nov 30th.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the current date.

Note that this form is designed to collect specific information about community leaders and their contributions. Please be accurate and honest in your answers, and ensure that you're not submitting duplicate nominations. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your internal stakeholders for guidance.
