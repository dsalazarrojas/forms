# Digital Media Content Creator Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Media Content Creator Award Nomination form is used to collect information about individuals or teams that are being nominated for the Digital Media Content Creator Award. This form is used by the award committee to review and evaluate nominations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal contact details (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone).
2. Select the category you feel the nominee best fits into (Digital Media Artist, Digital Media Content Creator, Digital Media Editor, etc.).
3. Enter the work title of the nominee.
4. Provide a brief nomination reason.
5. If applicable, select the award recipient(s) that the nominee has been nominated for.
6. If applicable, upload any supporting files for the submission.
7. Add any additional notes for the committee to review.
8. Select the submission status (Draft, Published, In Progress).
9. Choose a final submission option (Draft, Published, In Progress).
10. Enter the award committee's name and contact details (if different from yours).
11. Add any additional information or description for the submission link.
12. Enter the submission link and label.
13. Provide any additional notes for the committee to review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the first name of the nominee.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the last name of the nominee.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the nominee.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the nominee.
* **Category** (select_one, required): Select the category that the nominee best fits into.
* **Work Title** (text, optional): Enter the work title of the nominee.
* **Nomination Reason** (text, optional): Provide a brief reason for nominating this individual/team.
* **Award Recipients** (select_multiple, optional): Select the award recipient(s) that the nominee has been nominated for.
* **Submission Files** (note, optional): Upload any supporting files for the submission.
* **Additional Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes for the committee to review.
* **Committee Reviewers** (select_one, required): Select a committee reviewer for this submission.
* **Date Submitted** (date, required): Enter the date the submission was made.
* **Time Submitted** (time, optional): Enter the time the submission was made.
* **Submission Status** (select_one, required): Select the current status of the submission (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Additional Info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information for the submission.
* **Award Committee Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the award committee.
* **Award Committee Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the award committee.
* **Award Committee Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the award committee.
* **Submission Link** (text, optional): Enter the submission link.
* **Submission Link Label** (text, optional): Enter a label for the submission link.
* **Submission Link URL** (text, optional): Enter the URL for the submission link.
* **Submission Link Description** (text, optional): Enter a description for the submission link.
* **Award Committee Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes for the committee to review.
* **Review Notes** (note, optional): Add any notes for the reviewers to review.
* **Final Submission** (select_one, required): Select the final submission option (Draft, Published, In Progress).
* **Submission Link URL** (text, optional): Enter the URL for the submission link.
