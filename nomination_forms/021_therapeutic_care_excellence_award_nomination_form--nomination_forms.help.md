# Therapeutic Care Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit nominations for the Therapeutic Care Excellence Award, which recognizes outstanding therapeutic care and services provided by healthcare professionals and hospitals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the nominee, including their name, contact email, phone number, and the care type they have provided.
2. Select the hospital where the nominee works from the options provided.
3. Confirm the care type(s) the nominee has provided by selecting all that apply.
4. If necessary, provide any further comments or information about the nominee.
5. Submit the form to complete the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Name** (name, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the healthcare professional or hospital being nominated.

### **Email** (email, email, required: false)
Enter the email address of the healthcare professional or hospital being nominated. This will be used to contact them if needed.

### **Phone** (phone, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the healthcare professional or hospital being nominated. This will be used to contact them if needed.

### **Hospital** (hospital, select_one, required: false)
Select the hospital where the nominee works.

### **Care Type** (care_type, select_multiple, required: true)
Select all the care types provided by the nominee. This could include services such as:
	+ Type A
	+ Type B
	+ Type C

### **Nominee** (nominee, text, required: false)
If necessary, provide any further comments or information about the nominee.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Nominations must be for healthcare professionals or hospitals that have provided outstanding care and services.
* Self-nominations and promotional nominations are not accepted.
