# Emerging Librarian Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emerging Librarian Nomination Form is designed to collect information for nominations of librarians who demonstrate exceptional skills and qualifications for a librarian position. This form is intended to gather essential details about the nominee, which will be reviewed by a committee to determine their eligibility for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have reviewed the purpose and requirements of this form and have a clear understanding of the nominee's qualifications.
2. Fill out the form completely, providing the required information for each field.
3. Enter the nominee's contact information, including their email address.
4. If relevant, attach supporting documentation to provide additional context about the nominee's qualifications.
5. Review your completed form carefully before submitting it for consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`): A text field where you can provide a brief overview of the nominee's qualifications and experience.
* **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`): A text field where you can attach any relevant documentation, such as certificates, articles, or letters of recommendation, that support the nominee's qualifications.
* **Nominee Contact Info** (`nominee_contact_info`): An email field where you can enter the nominee's email address. This will allow the committee to contact the nominee with any follow-up questions or requests for additional information.
