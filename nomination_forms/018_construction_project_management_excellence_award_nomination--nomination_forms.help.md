# Construction Project Management Excellence Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather accurate and detailed information for the Construction Project Management Excellence Award nomination process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, paying attention to the required fields denoted by an asterisk (\*).
2. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in each section.
3. If you have supporting documents, select 'True' for the 'Supporting documents' field; otherwise, select 'False'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominee name**: Enter the name of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Project name**: Enter the name of the project being nominated.
* **Nomination reason**: Provide a detailed explanation of why this nominee deserves the award.
* **Key achievements**: List measurable successes or innovations achieved by the nominee (optional, but recommended).
* **Supporting documents**: Indicate if you have included supporting documents with the nomination (True or False).
* **Nominator name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator contact email**: Provide your email address for follow-up questions (optional but recommended).

## Tips
* Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that only one nomination per project is submitted.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, reach out to the award organizers.
