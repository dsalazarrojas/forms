# Startup Incubator Venture Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for nominating startups for an award. The goal is to highlight exceptional startups that meet the criteria set by the awarding organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the startup's name and a short name for easier reference.
2. Provide a brief description of the startup.
3. Choose the nomination reason that best fits the startup's achievements.
4. Select the startup's location.
5. Enter the contact email and phone number of the person nominating the startup.
6. Choose the reviewer that will be reviewing the nomination (optional).
7. Enter the date of the nomination and review.
8. Select the startup category and tags that apply.
9. Add any additional notes or file attachments.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Startup Name**: Enter the full name of the startup, as it appears on their website or marketing materials. (text)
* **Short Name**: Enter a short and catchy name for the startup, which will be used for easy reference. (text)
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the startup, highlighting its unique features and achievements. (text)
* **Nomination Reason**: Choose the reason why you are nominating this startup, from the options provided:
	+ Innovative Technology
	+ Job Creation
	+ Market Disruption
	+ Social Impact
	+ Other
* **Location**: Select the location where the startup is based, from the options provided:
	+ USA
	+ Canada
	+ Other
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person or organization nominating the startup. (email)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or organization nominating the startup. (text)
* **Reviewer**: Choose the reviewer that will be reviewing the nomination (optional):
	+ Incubator
	+ Accelerator
	+ Other
* **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination (yyyy-mm-dd). (date)
* **Reviewed Date**: Enter the date the nomination was reviewed (yyyy-mm-dd). (date)
* **Status**: Select the current status of the nomination:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Category**: Select the category that best fits the startup's industry or focus:
	+ AI
	+ Biotech
	+ EdTech
	+ Healthcare
	+ Sustainability
	+ Other
* **Tags**: Add any additional tags that describe the startup, from the options provided:
	+ Award
	+ Nomination
	+ Startup
	+ Venture
	+ Other
* **File**: Add any supporting files, such as documents or images, to aid the review process. (text)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the startup. (note)
* **Created At**: This field displays the date and time the nomination was created. (time)
* **Updated At**: This field displays the date and time the nomination was last updated. (time)
* **Nomination Type**: Select the type of nomination, from the options provided:
	+ Internal
	+ External
	+ Other
* **Nomination URL**: Enter the URL of the startup's website or social media page. (text)
* **Nomination Status**: Select the current status of the nomination:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of the submission (yyyy-mm-dd). (date)
* **Reviewed By**: Select the person or organization that reviewed the nomination:
	+ Admin
	+ Reviewer
	+ Other
* **Status History**: View the history of the nomination's status changes. (note)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the startup. (note)
