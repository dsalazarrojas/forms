# Pharmaceutical Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Pharmaceutical Innovation Award nomination process. It requires detailed information from both the nominator and the nominee to assess their eligibility and potential for the award.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information (Company, Name, Phone, Email) accurately and completely.
2. Provide your relationship with the nominee.
3. Enter the nominee's contact information (Name, Title, Organisation, Phone, Email, Address).
4. Select a nomination date.
5. Add any additional comments or information about the nominee.
6. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company**: Your company name, which will be displayed as the nominator.
* **Name**: Your name, which will be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Email**: Your email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Nominator Name**: Not applicable, as the nominator's name is already provided in the first field.
* **Nominee Name**: The name of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nominee Title**: The title or position of the nominee.
* **Nominee Organisation**: The organisation where the nominee works or is associated with.
* **Nominee Phone**: The contact phone number of the nominee.
* **Nominee Email**: The email address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Address**: The address of the nominee (not required but recommended for correspondence).
* **Nominee Country**: The country where the nominee is based or works (not required but recommended for international context).
* **Nominator Address**: Not required but recommended for correspondence with the nominator.
* **Nominator Phone**: Your contact phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Nominator Email**: Your email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Nomination Date**: The date of the nomination, in the format yyyy-mm-dd.
* **Nominator Title**: Not applicable, as the nominator's title is already implied.
* **Nominator Organisation**: Not applicable, as this field is already part of the first field.
* **Nominator Country**: Not applicable, as this field is not required.
* **Nominator Relationship**: A description of your relationship with the nominee, e.g., colleague, mentor, or supervisor.
* **Nominator Comment**: Additional comments or context about the nomination, which will help in evaluating the nominee's work.
* **Nominator Additional Info**: Extra information that will aid in understanding the nominee's achievement, which can be any relevant details about the nominee or their work.
