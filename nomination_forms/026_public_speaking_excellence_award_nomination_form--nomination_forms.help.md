# Public Speaking Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for award nominations for public speaking excellence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are:
	* Speaker Type
	* Organization
	* Speaker Link
	* Nominator Name
	* Nominator Title
	* Nominator Organization
	* Nominator Email
	* Nominator Phone
	* Submission Agreement
	* Submission Acknowledgments
	* Submission Agreement Signed
2. Fill in the other fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Speaker Type
* *Speaker Type* (`speaker_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of speaker, either Student, Teacher, or Other.

### 2. Speaker Name
* *Speaker Name* (`nomination_form_speaker_name`, text, optional): The name of the speaker being nominated.

### 3. Speaker Title
* *Speaker Title* (`nomination_form_speaker_title`, text, optional): The title of the speaker, if applicable.

### 4. Event Name
* *Event Name* (`nomination_form_event_name`, text, optional): The name of the event where the speaker spoke, if applicable.

### 5. Event Date
* *Event Date* (`nomination_form_event_date`, date, optional): The date of the event where the speaker spoke, if applicable.

### 6. Event Time
* *Event Time* (`nomination_form_event_time`, time, optional): The time of the event where the speaker spoke, if applicable.

### 7. Speaker Bio
* *Speaker Bio* (`nomination_form_speaker_bio`, note, optional): A brief description of the speaker's bio.

### 8. Organization
* *Organization* (`nomination_form_organization`, text, required): The organization where the speaker is from or affiliated with.

### 9. Speaker Link
* *Speaker Link* (`nomination_form_speaker_link`, text, required): A link to the speaker's professional website or social media profile.

### 10. Nominator Name
* *Nominator Name* (`nomination_form_nominator_name`, text, required): The name of the person nominating the speaker.

### 11. Nominator Title
* *Nominator Title* (`nomination_form_nominator_title`, text, required): The title of the person nominating the speaker.

### 12. Nominator Organization
* *Nominator Organization* (`nomination_form_nominator_organization`, text, required): The organization where the nominator is from or affiliated with.

### 13. Nominator Email
* *Nominator Email* (`nomination_form_nominator_email`, email, required): The email address of the person nominating the speaker.

### 14. Nominator Phone
* *Nominator Phone* (`nomination_form_nominator_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the person nominating the speaker.

### 15. Speaker Agreement
* *Speaker Agreement* (`nomination_form_speaker_agreement`, select_multiple, required): The speaker's agreement to be nominated and participate in the award process.

### 16. Submission Agreement
* *Submission Agreement* (`submission_agreement`, select_multiple, required): The agreement that the nominator will submit the nomination accurately and in good faith.

### 17. Submission Terms
* *Submission Terms* (`submission_terms`, note, required): A note about submission terms and conditions.

### 18. Submission Acknowledgments
* *Submission Acknowledgments* (`submission_acknowledgments`, note, required): A note about submission acknowledgments and responsibilities.

### 19. Submission Agreement Signed
* *Submission Agreement Signed* (`submission_agreement_signed`, select_multiple, required): The agreement that the nominator has signed and submitted the nomination.

### 20. Back to Top
* *Back to Top* (`back_to_top`, text, optional): A link to go back to the top of the form.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields.
