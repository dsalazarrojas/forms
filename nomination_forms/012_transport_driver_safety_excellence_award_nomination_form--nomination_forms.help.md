# Transport Driver Safety Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transport Driver Safety Excellence Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information about drivers who have demonstrated exceptional safety performance and deserve recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose "Nomination Details" and select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to nominate a driver.
2. Enter the name of the driver you want to nominate.
3. Provide a clear description of why this driver deserves the award.
4. Select the date of the nomination.
5. Enter the URL of a photo of the driver.
6. Enter the URL of a safety video related to the driver.
7. Enter the vehicle registration number of the driver.
8. Enter the name of the fleet operator.
9. Enter the phone number of the fleet operator.
10. Enter the email address of the fleet operator.
11. Enter the name of the safety officer.
12. Enter the email address of the safety officer.
13. Enter the phone number of the safety officer.
14. Enter the name of the award recipient.
15. Enter the phone number of the award recipient.
16. Enter the email address of the award recipient.
17. Select the award date.
18. Select the award category (e.g., Driver Excellence Award, Safety Award, Other).
19. Provide a brief explanation of the award achievement.
20. Upload supporting documents (optional).
21. Select the nomination status (e.g., Pending Review, Approved, Declined).
22. Enter any comments.
23. Enter comments from the nominator.
24. Enter comments from the submitter.
25. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Details** (Required, Select Multiple):
	+ Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to nominate a driver.
* **Driver Name** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the name of the driver you want to nominate.
* **Nomination Reason** (Required, Text):
	+ Provide a clear description of why this driver deserves the award.
* **Nomination Date** (Required, Date):
	+ Select the date of the nomination.
* **Driver Photo URL** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the URL of a photo of the driver.
* **Safety Video URL** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the URL of a safety video related to the driver.
* **Vehicle Registration** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the vehicle registration number of the driver.
* **Fleet Operator Name** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the name of the fleet operator.
* **Fleet Operator Phone** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the phone number of the fleet operator.
* **Fleet Operator Email** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the email address of the fleet operator.
* **Safety Officer Name** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the name of the safety officer.
* **Safety Officer Email** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the email address of the safety officer.
* **Safety Officer Phone** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the phone number of the safety officer.
* **Award Recipient Name** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the name of the award recipient.
* **Award Recipient Phone** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the phone number of the award recipient.
* **Award Recipient Email** (Required, Text):
	+ Enter the email address of the award recipient.
* **Award Date** (Required, Date):
	+ Select the award date.
* **Award Category** (Required, Select One):
	+ Select the award category (e.g., Driver Excellence Award, Safety Award, Other).
* **Award Achievement** (Required, Text):
	+ Provide a brief explanation of the award achievement.
* **Supporting Documents** (Optional, Note):
	+ Upload supporting documents.
* **Nomination Status** (Required, Select One):
	+ Select the nomination status (e.g., Pending Review, Approved, Declined).
* **Comments** (Optional, Text):
	+ Enter any comments.
* **Nominator Comments** (Optional, Text):
	+ Enter comments from the nominator.
* **Submitter Comments** (Optional, Text):
	+ Enter comments from the submitter.

Please note that only the fields marked as "Required" are mandatory and must be filled in order to submit the form.
