# Chapter of the Year Award Nomination - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>

This form is designed to collect information for the Chapter of the Year Award nomination. The purpose of this award is to recognize the outstanding achievements and contributions of our chapters. To be eligible for this award, a chapter must have demonstrated exceptional growth, community impact, innovation, and financial responsibility. This form will guide you through the process of nominating your chapter for this prestigious recognition.

The nomination process is designed to be straightforward and comprehensive. Please ensure that you have all necessary information and supporting documents before starting the nomination process.

<end thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of several sections and fields, each with its own specific requirements and guidelines. Please follow these steps to complete the form successfully:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each section and field's purpose.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
3. Attach supporting evidence and documents as required.
4. Submit the nomination form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Full Name** (id: 2, required: true): Enter the full name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email Address** (id: 3, required: true): Enter the email address associated with the nominator for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
* **Nominator Phone Number** (id: 4, required: true): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Your Relationship to the Chapter** (id: 5, required: true): Select the most accurate description of your relationship to the chapter.
* **Chapter Full Legal Name** (id: 7, required: true): Enter the formal name of the chapter as registered with the national body.
* **Chapter ID or Code** (id: 8, required: true): Enter the unique code assigned to the chapter.
* **Chapter Region or Location** (id: 9, required: true): Select the geographical region where the chapter is located.
* **Chapter President or Lead Officer** (id: 10, required: true): Enter the name of the chapter's president or lead officer.
* **Membership Growth in the Past Year** (id: 12, required: true): Provide a brief summary of the chapter's membership growth and retention successes.
* **Community Impact and Service Projects** (id: 13, required: true): List the chapter's major service contributions and their outcomes.
* **Examples of Innovation and Engagement** (id: 14, required: true): Describe how the chapter has innovated its programming.
* **Financial Responsibility and Fundraising** (id: 15, required: true): Provide a brief summary of the chapter's financial responsibility and fundraising success.
* **Statement of Overall Excellence** (id: 16, required: true): Explain why this chapter should be named Chapter of the Year.
* **Links to Supporting Evidence** (id: 18, required: false): Provide links to supporting evidence such as photos, press releases, or digital portfolios.
* **Regional Leader Endorsement** (id: 19, required: true): Select whether you have an endorsement from your regional leader.
* **Confirmation of Accuracy** (id: 21, required: true): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **Nomination Date** (id: 22, required: true): Enter the current date.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (id: 23, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful in the nomination process.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all necessary information and supporting documents before starting the nomination process.
* Review the form carefully to avoid errors and incomplete fields.
* Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
* Submit the form once you have completed all sections.
