# Financial Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Innovation Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information about individuals or teams who have made significant contributions to the field of financial innovation. This form is used to evaluate nominees and potentially award them for their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for the nominee's industry from the "Financial Industry Category" field.
2. Enter the nominee's name in the "Nominee Name" field.
3. Enter the nominee's organization in the "Organization" field.
4. Enter the nominee's position in the "Position" field.
5. Select whether the nominee is eligible for the award in the "Financial Innovation Award Category" field.
6. Provide a description of the nominee's work in the "Description" field.
7. Upload any supporting documents for the nomination in the "Supporting Documents" field.
8. Enter the scores for the nomination in the "Score 1", "Score 2", and "Score 3" fields.
9. Upload any additional files for the nomination in the "Additional Files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Industry Category (Select Multiple):**
	+ This field allows you to select the nominee's industry category.
* **Nominee Name:**
	+ Enter the name of the person or team being nominated.
* **Organization:**
	+ Enter the name of the organization associated with the nominee.
* **Position:**
	+ Enter the nominee's position or title.
* **Financial Innovation Award Category (Select One):**
	+ Select whether the nominee is eligible for the award.
* **Description:**
	+ Provide a brief description of the nominee's work and its significance.
* **Supporting Documents:**
	+ Upload any supporting documents that demonstrate the nominee's work and qualifications.
* **Score 1, Score 2, Score 3:**
	+ Enter scores to evaluate the nomination. These scores will be used to assess the nominee's work and determine their eligibility for the award.
* **Additional Files:**
	+ Upload any additional files that support the nomination.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not nominating the same person for the same award.
* Please provide clear and concise descriptions of the nominee's work and its significance.
* Please upload any supporting documents that demonstrate the nominee's qualifications.
* Please enter scores that accurately reflect the nominee's work and qualifications.
