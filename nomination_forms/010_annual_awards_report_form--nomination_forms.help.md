# Annual Awards Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Awards Report Form is used to collect information about awards programs and events held by the organization, ensuring accuracy and consistency in the reporting process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your full name and contact email address.
2. Enter the name of your organization and select the year for which you are submitting the report.
3. Provide the official name of the awards program.
4. Select all applicable categories for which awards were given.
5. Enter the number of awards given and the number of nominees or individuals/teams nominated.
6. Choose the process used to select winners.
7. Enter the details of the award ceremony, including date, number of attendees, and location.
8. Enter the types of awards given, such as trophies, plaques, or cash prizes.
9. Provide a list of winners.
10. Optionally, enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`reporter_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name as the person submitting the report.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, **optional**): If you wish to provide a contact number, enter it here.
* **Organization Name** (`organization`, `text`, **required**): Enter the official name of your organization.
* **Reporting Year** (`reporting_year`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the year for which you are submitting the report.
* **Award Program Name** (`award_program_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the official name of the awards program.
* **Award Categories** (`award_categories`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all categories for which awards were given.
* **Total Awards Given** (`total_awards_given`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total number of awards given.
* **Total Nominees** (`total_nominees`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total number of nominees or individuals/teams nominated.
* **Selection Process** (`selection_process`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the process used to select winners.
* **Committee Members** (`committee_members`, `text`, **optional**): List key committee members who contributed to the awards process.
* **Award Ceremony Date** (`award_ceremony_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the award ceremony.
* **Ceremony Attendance** (`ceremony_attendance`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the approximate number of attendees.
* **Ceremony Venue** (`ceremony_venue`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the location of the award ceremony.
* **Budget Allocated** (`budget_allocated`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the total budget allocated for the awards program.
* **Budget Spent** (`budget_spent`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the actual amount spent on the awards program.
* **Types of Awards Given** (`award_prizes`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all types of awards given, such as trophies or cash prizes.
* **Winners List** (`winner_names`, `text`, **required**): Enter a list of winners or attach supporting documentation.
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select metrics used to measure success, such as attendance numbers or social media reach.
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any challenges faced during the awards process.
* **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any lessons learned or recommendations for improvement.
* **Recommendations for Next Year** (`recommendations`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any suggestions for improving the awards process.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, **optional**): Attach any supporting documentation or information.
* **Report Status** (`report_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the current status of the report.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or information.
