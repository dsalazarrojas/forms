# Insurance Provider Nomination Tracking Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and monitor the nomination process for insurance providers. It collects and organizes information about vendors, nominees, and the status of nominations. The form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured and updates are recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to provide the correct information for each field based on the vendor's details.
- Enter the vendor's name, phone number, and email address if available.
- Nominate the insurance provider, providing the reason for the nomination.
- Update the nomination status and date of the last update.
- Enter the date when benefits started and ended if applicable.
- Ensure that the agency contact information, such as phone number and email address, is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Nomination Form 
* **Nomination Form** (Text, Required): This is the main section of the form where you will enter the details of the insurance provider nomination.
* **Vendor Information** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the vendor that may be relevant to the nomination.
* **Provider Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the insurance provider being nominated.
* **Nominee Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the nominee, the individual whose nomination this is.
* **Provider Type** (Select, Optional): Choose the type of insurance provider (e.g., Health, Life, Annuity).
* **Nomination Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination Reason** (Text, Optional): Enter the reason for the nomination.
* **Agency** (Text, Optional): Enter the agency's name if applicable.
* **Reviewer Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the reviewer if applicable.
* **Reviewer Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes from the reviewer if applicable.
* **Decision Update** (Select, Optional): Choose the status of the nomination (Open, Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Nomination Update** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the last update.
* **Benefits Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when benefits started.
* **Benefits End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when benefits ended.
* **Nomination Status** (Select, Optional): Choose the status of the nomination (Open, Completed).
* **Agency Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the agency's name if applicable.
* **Agency Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the agency's contact information (phone number and email address) if applicable.
* **Agency Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the agency's phone number if applicable.
* **Agency Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the agency's email address if applicable.
* **Reviewer Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the reviewer's phone number if applicable.
* **Reviewer Email** (Text, Optional): Enter the reviewer's email address if applicable.
* **Nominee Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the nominee's phone number if applicable.
* **Nominee Email** (Text, Optional): Enter the nominee's email address if applicable.
* **Vender Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the vendor if applicable.
* **Vender Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the vendor's phone number if applicable.
* **Vender Email** (Text, Optional): Enter the vendor's email address if applicable.

## Tips

- Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out as required or indicated.
- If a field is not applicable, enter 'N/A' or mark it as such.
- Review the form carefully to ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately.
- Update the form regularly to reflect changes in the nomination status and updates.
