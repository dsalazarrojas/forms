# Data Solutions Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Data Solutions Excellence Award nomination form. The form is used to gather detailed information about a nominee, their background, skills, and achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much information as possible about the nominee.
2. Make sure to answer all the required fields, as indicated by the * required* notation.
3. Use the provided space to expand on your answers, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nomination Page 1
* **Nomination Page 1** (`nomination_form_page_one`, text, required):
    Enter the nominee's name.
* **Nomination Page 2** (`nomination_form_page_two`, text, required):
    Enter the nominee's title or position.
* **Nomination Page 3** (`nomination_form_page_three`, text, required):
    Briefly describe the nominee's background.
* **Nomination Page 4** (`nomination_form_page_four`, text, required):
    Explain the nominee's skills and expertise.
* **Nomination Page 5** (`nomination_form_page_five`, text, required):
    Describe the nominee's achievements and accomplishments.
* **Nomination Page 6** (`nomination_form_page_six`, text, required):
    Provide any relevant work or academic experience.
* **Nomination Page 7** (`nomination_form_page_seven`, text, required):
    Explain why you are nominating this person for the award.
* **Nomination Page 8** (`nomination_form_page_eight`, text, required):
    Provide additional comments or remarks about the nominee.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field before submitting, to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact [Contact information: form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* You can use the provided space to expand on your answers, if necessary.
