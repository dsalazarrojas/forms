# Jewish Professional Award Nomination - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Jewish Professional Award is designed to recognize individuals who have made significant contributions to their field or community. The award seeks to honor those who have demonstrated excellence in their profession and have made a positive impact on others.

## How To Complete This Form
To nominate someone for the Jewish Professional Award, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the nomination details page, providing a brief description of the nominee's achievements and why they are deserving of the award.
2. Enter the nominator's details, including their name and contact information.
3. Provide the nominee's name, job title, and organization, if applicable.
4. Specify the year of the nomination.
5. Indicate whether supporting documents are required or not.
6. Enter the contact name and email of the person completing the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Nomination Details** (`nomination_details`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the nominee's achievements and why they are deserving of the award.
- **Nominator** (`nominator`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person nominating the individual.
- **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the full name of the individual being nominated.
- **Nominee Job Title** (`nominee_job`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the individual being nominated.
- **Nominee Organization** (`nominee_organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the organization the individual is associated with.
- **Nomination Year** (`nomination_year`, text, required: false): Specify the year of the nomination.
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, options: 'Yes', 'No', required: false): Indicate whether supporting documents are required or not.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person completing the nomination.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person completing the nomination.
