## Purpose
The Nonprofit Impact Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information from the user about an organization that has had a significant positive impact on society. This form is intended to identify and recognize organizations that are making a difference in their communities through their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide user information (page 1)
2. Enter the organization's information (page 2)
3. Describe the nomination's information (page 3)
4. Enter your contact information (page 4)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, optional)
	+ This field is for the user to provide their name and contact information.
	+ Please enter your name and any relevant contact details so that we can reach out to you if we need more information.
* **Organization Information** (`organization_info`, optional)
	+ Enter the name and address of the organization you are nominating.
	+ This will help us verify the organization's existence and location.
* **Nomination Information** (`nomination_info`, optional)
	+ Describe the organization's impact on society, focusing on specific projects or initiatives that have had a positive effect.
	+ Be as detailed as possible, providing examples and evidence of the organization's achievements.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, optional)
	+ Enter your name and contact information for follow-up communication.
	+ This will help us reach out to you if we need more information about your nomination.</thinking>
