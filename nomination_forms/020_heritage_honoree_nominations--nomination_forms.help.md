# Heritage Honoree Nominations - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit nominations for the Heritage Honoree Award, which recognizes individuals who have made significant contributions to the field of heritage conservation. To be eligible, nominations must be submitted through this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Nominate Someone**: Enter the name of the individual you would like to nominate in the "Nominate Someone" field.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Name of the Honoree**: Enter the name of the individual being nominated.
	* **Nominator Name**: Enter your name and title, if applicable.
	* **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Year of Nomination**: Enter the year of the nomination.
	* **Relationship to the Honoree**: Describe your relationship to the nominee.
	* **Nominator Title**: Enter your title (if applicable).
	* **Nominator Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
	* **Nominator Organization Title**: Enter your organization's title.
	* **Nominator Address**: Enter your organization's address.
	* **Nominator City**: Enter your city.
	* **Nominator State**: Enter your state.
	* **Nominator Zip**: Enter your zip code.
	* **Nominator Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Nominator Phone Ext**: Enter your phone extension (if applicable).
	* **Nominator Note**: Enter any additional comments or information about the nominee.
