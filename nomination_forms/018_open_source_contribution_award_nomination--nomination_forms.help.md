# Open Source Contribution Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Source Contribution Award Nomination form is designed to collect information from nominators and nominees to recognize outstanding contributions to the open source community. This form is used to gather detailed information about the nominee's work, their impact on the community, and the nominator's relationship with the nominee.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Nomination Form** (Page 1) with the necessary details about the nominee.
2.  Fill out the **Nominator Form** (Page 2) with your information as the person making the nomination.
3.  Fill out the **Review Form** (Page 3) with your review and evaluation of the nominee's work.
4.  Fill out the **Review Form** (Page 4) for additional comments and evaluation of the nominee's work, if needed.
5.  Fill out the **Submission Review** (Page 5) with a final evaluation and comments on the nomination.
6.  Fill out the **Submit** (Page 6) form to submit the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nomination Form (Page 1)**: This page collects basic information about the nominee, including their name and contact details.
*   **Nominator Form (Page 2)**: This page asks you to provide your details as the person making the nomination, including your name and contact information.
*   **Review Form (Page 3)**: This page is for you to provide a detailed evaluation of the nominee's work and its impact on the community.
*   **Review Form (Page 4)**: This page is for additional comments and evaluation of the nominee's work, if needed.
*   **Submission Review (Page 5)**: This page is for you to finalise your evaluation and comments on the nomination before submission.
*   **Submit (Page 6)**: This is the last page, where you submit the completed nomination.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have filled out all the necessary fields accurately and thoroughly before submitting the nomination. Also, make sure to proofread your responses carefully for any errors or typos. If you need assistance or have any questions, please reach out to our support team at form@gic.mx.
