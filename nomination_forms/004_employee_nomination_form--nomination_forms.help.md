# Employee Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Nomination Form" is used to recognize and award employees for their outstanding contributions and achievements. Please use this form to nominate employees who have demonstrated exceptional performance, initiative, or dedication to their role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all employees you wish to nominate from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the employee's name in the text field.
3. Enter the employee's email address in the email field.
4. Enter the employee's job title.
5. Select the date of the nomination.
6. Select the time of the day when the nomination took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination** (`Yes/No`, required): This field allows you to select the employees you wish to nominate. Please choose all employees that you believe deserve recognition.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the employee you are nominating. Please ensure that the name is accurate and consistent with the employee's official personnel records.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, optional): This field is used to enter the employee's email address. Please ensure that the email is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, optional): This field is used to enter the employee's job title or position.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, optional): This field is used to select the date of the nomination. Please choose the correct date when the nomination took place.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): This field is used to select the time of the day when the nomination took place. Please choose the correct time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
