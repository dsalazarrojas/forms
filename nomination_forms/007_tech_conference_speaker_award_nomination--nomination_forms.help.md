# Tech Conference Speaker Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Conference Speaker Award Nomination form is used to nominate speakers for a tech conference. This form helps the conference organizers to discover emerging talent and recognize outstanding contributions to the tech industry. You can use this form to nominate a speaker that you believe has made significant contributions to the tech world.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the speaker's name in the **speaker_name** field.
2. Provide a brief description of the speaker's presentation in the **presentation_title** field.
3. Select the date of the speaker's presentation in the **presentation_date** field.
4. Specify the time of the speaker's presentation in the **presentation_time** field.
5. Evaluate the speaker's presentation on a scale of 1-10 in the **evaluation_scale** field.
6. Optionally, add any additional comments about the speaker's presentation in the **comments** field.
7. Provide your information as the submitter in the **Submitter Information** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
9. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **speaker_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the speaker being nominated.
* **presentation_title** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the speaker's presentation.
* **presentation_date** (date, required): Select the date of the speaker's presentation.
* **presentation_time** (time, required): Specify the time of the speaker's presentation.
* **evaluation_scale** (number, required): Evaluate the speaker's presentation on a scale of 1-10.
* **comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about the speaker's presentation.
* **Submitter Information** (text, required): Provide your information as the submitter.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of the speaker's presentation in the **presentation_title** field.
* Use the **evaluation_scale** field to provide a numerical evaluation of the speaker's presentation.
* Use the **comments** field to provide additional feedback or suggestions about the speaker's presentation.
* Fill in your contact information accurately and completely in the **Submitter Information**, **email**, and **phone** fields.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
