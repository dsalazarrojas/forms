# Holiday Recognition Awards Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Recognition Awards Nomination form is used to recognize and appreciate employees who have demonstrated exceptional performance, commitment, or contributions during a specific holiday period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are nominating during the correct year (Award Recognition Year).
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, **required**): This is the name of the employee you wish to nominate.
* *Please enter the employee's name as it appears in our employee directory.*

### Award Recognition Year
* **Award Recognition Year** (award_recognition_year, number, **required**): This is the year during which the employee demonstrated exceptional performance, commitment, or contributions.
* *Enter the correct year for the holiday period you are recognizing the employee's achievements.*

### Reason for Recognition
* **Reason for Recognition** (reason_for_recognition, text, **required**): This field is for you to describe why the employee should be recognized.
* *Be as specific as possible, highlighting the employee's achievements and why they stand out from their peers.*

### Nomination Type
* **Nomination Type** (nomination_type, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the type of nomination you're submitting.
* *Select 'Yes' if you're certain the employee deserves recognition, 'No' otherwise.*

### Nominee Name
* **Nominee Name** (nominee_name, text, **required**): This is the name of the employee being nominated.
* *Please enter the nominee's name as it appears in our employee directory.*

### Nomination Message
* **Nomination Message** (nomination_message, text, **required**): This field is for you to add any additional comments or context about the nomination.
* *This can be a personal message to the nominee or a description of their achievements.*

### Manager Approval
* **Manager Approval** (manager_approval, select_one, **required**): Confirm if the nominee's manager has approved this nomination.
* *Select 'Yes' if the manager has approved, 'No' otherwise.*

### Department
* **Department** (department, select_one, **required**): This is the department the nominee works in.
* *Choose the department the nominee belongs to.*

### Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (employee_id, number, **required**): This is the employee's ID number.
* *Please enter the nominee's ID number as it appears in our employee directory.*

### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (manager_name, text, **required**): This is the name of the nominee's manager.
* *Please enter the manager's name as it appears in our employee directory.*

### Reason for Nomination
* **Reason for Nomination** (reason_for_nomination, text, **required**): This field is for you to describe why the nominee deserves recognition.
* *Be as specific as possible, highlighting the nominee's achievements and why they stand out from their peers.*

### Team Lead
* **Team Lead** (team_lead, select_one, **required**): This field indicates if the nominee is a team lead or not.
* *Select 'Yes' if the nominee is a team lead, 'No' otherwise.*

### Team Lead Approval
* **Team Lead Approval** (team_lead_approval, select_one, **optional**): If the nominee is a team lead, confirm their team lead approval.
* *Select 'Yes' if the team lead has approved, 'No' otherwise.*

### Nomination Status
* **Nomination Status** (nomination_status, select_one, **optional**): Choose the current status of the nomination.
* *Select 'Active' if the nomination is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.*

### Recognition Date
* **Recognition Date** (recognition_date, date, **required**): This is the date when the recognition will take place.
* *Enter the date for the recognition event.*

### Recognition Time
* **Recognition Time** (recognition_time, time, **required**): This is the time for the recognition event.
* *Enter the time for the recognition event.*

### Nomination Justification
* **Nomination Justification** (nomination_justification, text, **optional**): This field is for you to add any additional comments or justification for the nomination.
* *This can be used to elaborate on the reason for recognition.*

### Nomination Comment
* **Nomination Comment** (nomination_comment, text, **optional**): This field is for you to add any additional comments about the nomination.
* *This can be used to elaborate on any additional information about the nomination.*

### Nomination Email
* **Nomination Email** (nomination_email, email, **optional**): This is the email address to be used for the recognition event.
* *Enter the email address to be used for the recognition event.*

### Team Lead Email
* **Team Lead Email** (team_lead_email, email, **optional**): If the nominee is a team lead, this is their email address.
* *Enter the team lead's email address.*

### Manager Email
* **Manager Email** (manager_email, email, **optional**): This is the email address of the nominee's manager.
* *Enter the manager's email address.*

### Recognition Phone
* **Recognition Phone** (recognition_phone, text, **optional**): This is the phone number to be used for the recognition event.
* *Enter the phone number to be used for the recognition event.*

### Team Lead Phone
* **Team Lead Phone** (team_lead_phone, text, **optional**): If the nominee is a team lead, this is their phone number.
* *Enter the team lead's phone number.*

### Manager Phone
* **Manager Phone** (manager_phone, text, **optional**): This is the phone number of the nominee's manager.
* *Enter the manager's phone number.*

### Submission Status
* **Submission Status** (submission_status, select_one, **optional**): Choose the current status of the submission.
* *Select 'Active' if the submission is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.*
