# Leaders Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leaders Nomination Form is used to submit nominations for potential leaders within the organization. The form helps identify individuals who possess exceptional leadership skills and qualities that make them suitable for leadership roles.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, navigate to the Nomination Form page.
2. Start by completing the "Nomination Form" page with any information you'd like to share about the individual you wish to nominate.
3. Proceed to complete the "Leaders Name" field with the name of the individual being nominated.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the individual you are nominating is being considered for a specific leadership position.
5. Provide a brief explanation for why you are nominating the individual in the "Reason For Nomination" field.
6. Once you have completed all the required fields, click submit to submit your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form**: Enter any relevant information about the individual you wish to nominate, such as their skills, achievements, and qualifications.
* **Leaders Name**: Enter the name of the individual you are nominating.
* **Position Nominated**: Answer "Yes" if you are considering the individual for a specific leadership position, or "No" if you are nominating them for any leadership role.
* **Reason For Nomination**: Provide a brief explanation for why you are nominating the individual, such as their exceptional leadership qualities, skills, or achievements.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are not duplicating nominations for the same individual, as this may lead to confusion and incorrect nominations being processed.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the relevant authorities for clarification or guidance.
* Be specific and concrete when providing explanations for your nominations, as this will help the evaluation process.
