# Supply Chain Efficiency Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Efficiency Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information for nominating companies or individuals for supply chain efficiency awards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as these are mandatory to submit the nomination.
2.  Enter the correct nomination category for the nominee.
3.  Enter the nominee's email address and phone number for communication.
4.  Provide the nominee's name, company name, and address.
5.  Select the type of organization (Non-Profit, For-Profit, or Government).
6.  Add any additional comments or notes in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nomination Form
*   **Nomination Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description of the nomination.
*   **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company being nominated.
*   **Nomination Title**: Enter a title for the nomination, e.g., "Supply Chain Efficiency Award Nomination".
*   **Category**: Select one of the following categories: Award, Nomination, or Recognition.
*   **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination.
*   **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the nominee.
*   **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the nominee.
*   **Nominee Name**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
*   **Nominator**: Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address or the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **Nominator Phone**: Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
*   **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization.
*   **Address**: Enter the address of the organization.
*   **City**: Enter the city of the organization.
*   **State**: Enter the state of the organization.
*   **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the organization.
*   **Country**: Enter the country of the organization.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the nomination.
*   **Company Type**: Select the type of organization (Non-Profit, For-Profit, or Government).
*   **Submit**: Click the submit button to send the nomination form.
*   **Back to Top**: Click this button to go back to the top of the form.
*   **Back to Previous**: Click this button to go back to the previous page.
*   **Help Text**: This is a note section providing additional information or guidance.
*   **Error Message**: This field will display any error messages if there are any issues with the form submission.
*   **Submit Success Message**: This field will display a success message if the form is submitted successfully.
