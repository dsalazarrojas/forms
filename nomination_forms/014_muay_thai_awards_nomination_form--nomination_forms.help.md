# muay_thai_awards_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals for an award. It is a tool to collect necessary information about the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the Nomination Form page.
2. Provide a brief reason for the nomination on the Nomination Reason page.
3. Select the Award Category that best matches the nomination.
4. Enter the Award Year for which the nomination is made.
5. Enter the Phone Number and Email Address of the nominee.
6. Enter the name of the Fighter being nominated.
7. Enter the name of the Award being nominated for.
8. You can add additional information in the Additional Information field.
9. Finally, review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a brief description of the nominee.
- **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief reason for the nomination.
- **Award Category** (`award_category`, `text`, required: true): Select the category that best matches the nomination.
- **Award Year** (`award_year`, `number`, required: true): Enter the year of the award for which the nomination is made.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the nominee.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: true): Enter the email address of the nominee.
- **Fighter Name** (`fighter_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the fighter being nominated.
- **Award Name** (`award_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the award being nominated for.
- **Nomination Form 2** (`nomination_form_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is for additional information about the nomination.
- **Additional Information** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information about the nomination.
