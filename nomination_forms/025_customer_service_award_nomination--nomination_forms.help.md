# Customer Service Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Award Nomination form is used to nominate colleagues for a customer service award. It allows you to recognize and appreciate outstanding service provided to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate and truthfully information about the nominee and nominator.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.
- Review the submission date, time, and status fields for accuracy.
- Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send it to the designated authority for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Award Nomination** (`form_title`, `text`): Enter a brief description of the award nomination, if desired.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Employee** (`employee`, `text`, required): Enter the employee ID or full name of the person being nominated (if known).
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the department or team the nominee works in.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, `text`, required): Briefly explain why the nominee deserves the award.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, `email`, required): Enter the nominee's email address.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the nominee's phone number (if available).
* **Nominee Job Title** (`nominee_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's job title.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, `text`, required): Enter your title or position.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Nominator Department** (`nominator_department`, `text`, required): Enter the department or team you belong to.
* **Nominator Job Title** (`nominator_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you submitted the nomination.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you submitted the nomination.
* **Submission Review** (`submission_review`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you want the submission to be reviewed.
* **Submission Review Date** (`submission_review_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you want the submission to be reviewed.
* **Submission Review Time** (`submission_review_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you want the submission to be reviewed.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the submission (e.g., Approved, Rejected, In Progress).
* **Submission Status Comment** (`submission_status_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter a comment about the submission status.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
