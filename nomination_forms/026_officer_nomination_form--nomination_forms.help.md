# Officer Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Officer Nomination Form is designed to collect nominations for officers within the organization. It ensures that all nominated individuals have the necessary information and qualifications for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the category of the position you are nominating the candidate for.
2. Briefly describe the reason for nominating the candidate in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the first name and last name of the candidate.
4. Provide the candidate's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the candidate's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Select the position the candidate is being nominated for from the "Position" dropdown menu.
7. Explain the basis for the nomination in the "Basis of Nomination" field.
8. Describe the qualifications of the candidate in the "Qualification of Candidate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Category`, Select One, Required: False): Select the category of the position you are nominating the candidate for. This will help the evaluation committee to understand the context of the nomination.
* **Description** (`Description`, Text, Required: False): Briefly describe the reason you are nominating the candidate. This will help to provide context for the evaluation.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, Text, Required: False): Enter the first name of the candidate.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, Text, Required: False): Enter the last name of the candidate.
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, Required: False): Provide the candidate's email address to enable communication and further evaluation.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, Text, Required: False): Enter the candidate's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Position** (`Position`, Select One, Required: False): Select the position the candidate is being nominated for from the provided options.
* **Basis of Nomination** (`Basis of Nomination`, Text, Required: False): Explain the reason for nominating the candidate. This will help to demonstrate the candidate's fit for the position.
* **Qualification of Candidate** (`Qualification of Candidate`, Text, Required: False): Describe the qualifications of the candidate.
