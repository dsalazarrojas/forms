# Community Service Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals for a community service award. It collects information about the nominee, the nominator, and the award category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the nominee's full name and email address.
2. Provide your own full name and email address.
3. Select the most relevant award category for the nominee.
4. Describe the nominee's achievements and impact in detail.
5. (Optional) Provide an approximate number of years the nominee has been involved in community service.
6. (Optional) List any supporting documents you will provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominee Full Name (`nominee_name`, text, required)**: Please enter the full name of the person being nominated for the award.
* **Nominee Contact Email (`nominee_contact`, email, optional)**: Enter the nominee's email address, or leave this field blank if you don't have access to it.
* **Nominator Full Name (`nominator_name`, text, required)**: Enter your full name as the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominator Email Address (`nominator_email`, email, required)**: Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **Award Category (`award_category`, select_one, required)**: Select the most relevant category for the nominee's service. Choose from:
	+ Outstanding Volunteer
	+ Community Leader
	+ Youth Service
	+ Lifetime Achievement
* **Justification for Nomination (`nomination_justification`, text, required)**: Describe the nominee's achievements and impact in detail. Be specific about their service and how it has benefited the community.
* **Years of Service (`years_of_service`, number, optional)**: If known, enter an approximate number of years the nominee has been involved in community service.
* **Supporting Documents Summary (`supporting_documents`, text, optional)**: List any documents you will provide to support the nomination.

## Tips
* Make sure to have the nominee's permission before filling out this form.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the nominee's achievements and impact.
* If you have any additional documentation or supporting evidence, list it in the supporting documents summary field.
* This form is only for nominations, not applications. Nominations will be reviewed and verified by the community service committee.
