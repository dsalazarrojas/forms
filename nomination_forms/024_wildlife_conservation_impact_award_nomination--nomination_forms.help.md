# wildlife_conservation_impact_award_nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wildlife conservation impact award is given to individuals or organizations that have made significant impacts in wildlife conservation. This award recognizes their outstanding contributions to the protection and preservation of wildlife and their habitats.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization or individual's name and contact information in the "Nomination Organization" field.
2. List the individual(s) or people nominating the organization or individual in the "Nominating Individual(s)" field.
3. Enter the name of the person or team responsible for nominating the organization or individual in the "Nomination Nominator" field.
4. Select the category of the nomination from the "Nomination Category" field. Options include "Wildlife Research", "Wildlife Conservation", and "Wildlife Protection".
5. Enter a title for the nomination in the "Nomination Title" field.
6. Describe the nomination's impact in the "Nomination Description" field. Be as specific as possible about the impact and accomplishments.
7. Enter the area where the impact occurred in the "Nomination Impact Area" field.
8. Enter the location of the impact in the "Nomination Location" field.
9. Enter the start date of the impact in the "Nomination Impact Start Date" field.
10. Enter the end date of the impact in the "Nomination Impact End Date" field.
11. Enter the length of the impact in the "Nomination Impact Length" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Organization** (`Nomination Organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization or individual being nominated.
* **Nomination Contact** (`Nomination Contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the organization or individual being nominated.
* **Nominating Individual(s)** (`Nominating Individual(s)`, text, required/optional): List the individual(s) or people nominating the organization or individual.
* **Nomination Nominator** (`Nomination Nominator`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or team responsible for nominating the organization or individual.
* **Nomination Category** (`Nomination Category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the nomination from the options provided. Options include "Wildlife Research", "Wildlife Conservation", and "Wildlife Protection".
* **Nomination Title** (`Nomination Title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for the nomination.
* **Nomination Description** (`Nomination Description`, text, required/optional): Describe the nomination's impact in this field. Be as specific as possible about the impact and accomplishments.
* **Nomination Impact Area** (`Nomination Impact Area`, text, required/optional): Enter the area where the impact occurred.
* **Nomination Location** (`Nomination Location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the impact.
* **Nomination Impact Start Date** (`Nomination Impact Start Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the impact.
* **Nomination Impact End Date** (`Nomination Impact End Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the impact.
* **Nomination Impact Length** (`Nomination Impact Length`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the impact.
