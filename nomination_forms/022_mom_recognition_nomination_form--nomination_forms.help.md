# Mom Recognition Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mom Recognition Nomination Form is a tool for nominating your mom for recognition. Use this form to provide details about your mom's accomplishments and why you think she deserves to be recognized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
2. Make sure to enter your mom's name in the "Nominee Name" field.
3. Enter your own contact information in the "Submitter Name", "Submitter Position", "Submitter Email", and "Submitter Phone" fields.
4. Select the nomination type and category that best fit your mom's accomplishments.
5. Provide a detailed explanation for why you are nominating your mom in the "Nomination Reason" field.
6. You can add additional comments or notes about your mom's accomplishments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form_1`, text, required): This field is not applicable, as it is just the title of the form.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are nominating for recognition.
* **Nominee Job Title** (`nominee_job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title or position of the person you are nominating.
* **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`, select one, required): Select the type of recognition you think your mom deserves, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Nomination Category** (`nomination_category`, select multiple, optional): Select the category or categories that best fit your mom's accomplishments.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Enter a detailed explanation for why you are nominating your mom.
* **Nomination Details** (`nomination_details`, text, optional): Provide additional details about your mom's accomplishments.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, optional): Enter your position or title, if applicable.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting the nomination.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you are submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`, text, optional): Enter your location, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide additional comments or notes about your mom's accomplishments.
