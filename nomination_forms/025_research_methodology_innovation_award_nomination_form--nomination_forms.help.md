# Research Methodology Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Methodology Innovation Award Nomination Form is designed to collect nominations for the Research Methodology Innovation Award. It is a crucial component of the award process, ensuring that deserving nominees receive recognition for their outstanding work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate award category from the drop-down menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the nominee's innovation.
3. Enter the nominee's name, title, and email address.
4. Add a brief biography of the nominee.
5. Specify the date and time of the innovation.
6. Upload any additional information or supporting documents.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Award Category** (`award_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes the nominee's innovation. This will help the evaluation committee to properly evaluate the nomination.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Enter a concise and clear description of the nominee's innovation, explaining why it deserves recognition.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's title or position.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, `email`, required): Enter the nominee's email address for communication purposes.
* **Nominee Bio** (`nominee_bio`, `text`, required): Provide a brief biography of the nominee, highlighting their relevant experience and qualifications.
* **Date of Innovation** (`date_of_innovation`, `date`, required): Specify the date the innovation was implemented.
* **Time of Innovation** (`time_of_innovation`, `time`, required): Specify the exact time the innovation was implemented.
* **Nomination Form Upload** (`nomination_form_upload`, `text`, required): Upload any additional information or supporting documents that will help the evaluation committee to assess the nomination.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or details that may be helpful for the evaluation committee to consider.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* If you have any supporting documents, please upload them in the specified format.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Make sure to review the submission guidelines before submitting the form.
