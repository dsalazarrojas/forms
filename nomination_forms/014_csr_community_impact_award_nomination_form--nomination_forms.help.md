# CSR Community Impact Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSR Community Impact Award Nomination Form is designed to recognize and acknowledge individuals or organizations that have made significant contributions to the community, promoting a better world for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the nomination criteria and ensure the nominee aligns with the selected category.
2. Provide accurate and clear information for all required fields.
3. Attach relevant supporting documents as needed.
4. Ensure the consent for publicity checkbox is checked correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominator name** (`Nominator name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator email** (`Nominator email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for correspondence purposes.
* **Nominee name** (`Nominee name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the individual or organization being nominated.
* **Impact summary** (`Impact summary`, `text`, required): Describe the nominee's impact in 250 words, focusing on their contributions and achievements.
* **Community reach** (`Community reach`, `number`, optional): Estimate the number of people affected by the nominee's work or initiative.
* **Supporting documents reference** (`Supporting documents reference`, `text`, optional): If relevant, provide links or filenames of supporting documents, such as reports or photos.
* **Nomination category** (`Nomination category`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the four categories:
	+ Youth leadership
	+ Environmental impact
	+ Health and wellbeing
	+ Community services
* **Consent for publicity** (`Consent for publicity`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to indicate if the nominee is comfortable with their details being published publicly.

## Tips
* Ensure the nomination is accurate and thorough, as this will help the nominee receive the recognition they deserve.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the nominee or the relevant department before submitting the form.
* Attach supporting documents only if they are relevant and necessary for the nomination.
* Double-check the consent for publicity checkbox to ensure the nominee is comfortable with public recognition.
