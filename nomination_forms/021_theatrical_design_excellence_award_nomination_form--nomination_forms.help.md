# Theatrical Design Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for nominating productions for the Theatrical Design Excellence Award. To ensure accurate and complete submissions, please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly. Before starting, check if you've already submitted a nomination with the same production name and production type, to avoid duplicate submissions. If you're unsure, it's always best to contact us for clarification. </thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For fields like "Production Type" and "Category", select from the available options.
3. Enter your contact information in "Nomination Contact Email" and "Nomination Contact Phone".
4. Provide a clear and concise "Nomination Message" explaining why you're nominating this production.
5. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form**: Enter the title of this form, which is a required field to identify the nomination.
* **Production Name**: Enter the name of the production you're nominating. This should be a clear and concise name.
* **Production Type**: Select the type of production from the available options (Drama, Comedy, Musical, etc.). This helps us understand the production's genre.
* **Production Description**: A short description of the production, providing context for the nomination.
* **Supporting Materials**: If applicable, attach any relevant documents or images that support your nomination.
* **Nomination Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization nominating the production.
* **Nomination Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the person or organization nominating the production.
* **Nomination Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person or organization nominating the production.
* **Nomination Message**: A clear and concise message explaining why you're nominating this production.
* **Nomination Type**: Select the type of nomination from the available options (Technical, Design, Production, etc.).
* **Submission Status**: Select the current status of your submission (In Progress, Completed, Pending).
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer assigned to this production.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email of the reviewer assigned to this production.
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer assigned to this production.
* **Reviewer Message**: A clear and concise message from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Status**: Select the current status of the review (In Progress, Completed, Pending).
* **Award Year**: Select the year the award is being submitted for.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date the review was conducted.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Submission Notes**: Any additional notes about the submission.
* **Reviewer Notes**: Any additional notes from the reviewer.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or feedback about the submission.
* **Category**: Select the category of the production (Theater, Dance, Music, etc.).
* **Production Location**: Enter the location where the production took place.
* **Nomination Submission**: A brief summary of the submission.
