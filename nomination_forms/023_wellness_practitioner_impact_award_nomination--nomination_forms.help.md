# Wellness Practitioner Impact Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Practitioner Impact Award Nomination form is used to gather information for a nomination process recognizing the impact of a wellness practitioner. This form helps in collecting and tracking the nomination details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the practitioner's name in the "practitioner_name" field.
2. Select the practitioner's category from the drop-down menu in the "practitioner_category" field.
3. Enter the nominator's contact information in the "nominator_name", "nominator_email", "nominator_phone", and "nominator_address" fields.
4. Describe the practitioner's achievements and impact in the "practitioner_achievements" field.
5. Enter the date of the nomination in the "nomination_date" field.
6. Rate the practitioner's impact in the "practitioner_rating" field.
7. Enter any additional justification or comments for the nomination in the "practitioner_award_nomination_justification" field.
8. Verify the practitioner's signature in the "practitioner_esignature_verification" field (optional).
9. Sign and date the nomination by filling out the "award_nomination_form_completion_date" and "award_nominator_esignature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **practitioner_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the practitioner being nominated.
* **practitioner_category** (select_one, required): Select the category of the practitioner being nominated.
* **nominator_name** (text, required): Enter your name and title or position as the nominator.
* **nominator_email** (email, required): Enter your email address as the nominator.
* **nominator_phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number as the nominator.
* **nominator_address** (text, required): Enter your address as the nominator.
* **practitioner_achievements** (note, required): Describe the practitioner's achievements and impact.
* **practitioner_category** (select_one, required): Select the category of the practitioner being nominated.
* **practitioner_rating** (number, required): Rate the practitioner's impact using a numerical rating.
* **practitioner_award_nomination_justification** (note, required): Enter any additional justification or comments for the nomination.
* **practitioner_reference** (text, required): Enter any relevant reference information about the practitioner.
* **practitioner_esignature** (text, required): Enter your signature as the nominator.
* **practitioner_award_nominator_signature** (text, required): Enter your signature as the nominator.
* **nominator_esignature** (text, required): Enter your signature as the nominator.
* **award_nomination_form_completion_date** (date, required): Date of the nomination form completion.
* **award_nominator_esignature** (text, required): Enter your signature as the nominator.
* **practitioner_esignature_verification** (text, required): Verify the practitioner's signature.
* **practitioner_esignature_verification_2** (text, required): Verify the practitioner's signature.
* **practitioner_esignature_verification_3** (text, required): Verify the practitioner's signature.
* **practitioner_esignature_verification_4** (text, required): Verify the practitioner's signature.

Note: The "practitioner_esignature" and related fields can be removed as they appear to be redundant.
