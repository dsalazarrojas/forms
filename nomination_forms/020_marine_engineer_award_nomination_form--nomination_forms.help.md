# marine_engineer_award_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marine Engineer Award Nomination Form is designed to collect nominations for individuals who have made significant contributions to the field of marine engineering. Please fill out this form to submit your nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with all required information.
2.  Ensure the accuracy and up-to-datedness of the provided data.
3.  Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Title** (`nomination_title`, `text`, required: false): A brief title that summarizes the reason for the nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of the individual being nominated.
* **Organization Affiliation** (`organization_affiliation`, `text`, required: false): The name of the organization or institution with which the nominee is affiliated.
* **Nominee Contact Email** (`nominee_contact_email`, `email`, required: false): A valid email address for the nominee.
*   **Nominee Contact Phone** (`nominee_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): A valid phone number for the nominee.
*   **Nominee Address** (`nominee_address`, `text`, required: false): The address of the nominee.
*   **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of the person making the nomination.
*   **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, `text`, required: false): The title or position of the nominator.
*   **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required: false): A valid email address for the nominator.
*   **Nominator Organization** (`nominator_organization`, `text`, required: false): The name of the organization or institution making the nomination.
