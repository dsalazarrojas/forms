# Biotech Firm Research Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for nominating individuals or teams in the biotech industry for research awards. It captures information about the nominator and the research being nominated, including details on the award category, research title, and supporting materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (First Name, Last Name, Professional Title, Organization, and Email Address) to verify your identity.
2. Choose the correct Award Category from the select_one field.
3. Provide details on the research being nominated (Research Title, Research Summary, Research Field, and Research Duration).
4. Highlight the Key Achievements and Description of Innovation related to the research.
5. Evaluate the Impact Assessment and write a Nomination Statement to justify the nomination.
6. Optionally, provide related publications and attach your CV or Resume if required.
7. Confirm the eligibility status and indicate if there are any exceptions.
8. Disclose any conflicts of interest and provide a description if applicable.
9. Confirm if you have previously nominated this individual or team and indicate the submission status.
