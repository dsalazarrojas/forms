# Player Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Nomination Form is designed to allow players, coaches, or staff to nominate their peers for recognition or awards. Nominations are used to identify individuals who have made outstanding contributions to the team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the First Name and Last Name of the individual you are nominating.
2.  Enter the Email and Phone number of the individual being nominated, if applicable.
3.  Select the Date of the event or achievement being recognized.
4.  Enter the Team Name or department/section being recognized.
5.  Choose the Nomination Type (Player, Coach, or Staff).
6.  Enter a brief Nomination Reason for the individual's recognition.
7.  Review and submit your nomination for review and consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the first name of the individual you are nominating.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the last name of the individual you are nominating.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the individual being nominated, if applicable.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual being nominated, if applicable.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select the date of the event or achievement being recognized.
* **Team Name** (text, optional): Enter the team name or department/section being recognized.
* **Nomination Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of nomination (Player, Coach, or Staff).
* **Nomination Reason** (text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the individual's recognition.

## Tips
- Ensure that the nomination is accurate and authentic.
- Nominations will be reviewed for accuracy and completeness before being considered for recognition.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
