# Nomination For Judicial Positions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from the nominee and the nominator to facilitate the nomination process for judicial positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the nominee's name.
2. Enter the nominator's name.
3. Enter the nominee's full name.
4. Enter the nominee's mailing address.
5. Enter the nominee's phone number.
6. Enter the nominee's email.
7. Provide a brief description of why the nominee is being nominated.
8. Attach any relevant supporting documents.
9. List the nominee's judicial qualifications.
10. Enter the date of nomination.
11. Enter the current status of the nomination.
12. Enter any additional comments.
13. Enter the name of the person organising this nomination.
14. Enter the contact number of the nominee organiser.
15. Enter the email of the nominee organiser.
16. Enter the location of the nomination.
17. Enter the nominee's judicial experience.
18. Enter the nominee's bar number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee's Name** (`nomination_details`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominator's Name** (`nomination_nominator`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person nominating this judicial officer.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee's Mailing Address** (`nominee_address`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's mailing address.
* **Nominee's Phone Number** (`nominee_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's phone number.
* **Nominee's Email** (`nominee_email`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's email.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of why the nominee is being nominated.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, required): Attach relevant supporting documents.
* **Nominee's Judicial Qualifications** (`judicial_qualifications`, `note`, required): List the nominee's judicial qualifications.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of nomination.
* **Nomination Status** (`nomination_status`, `text`, required): Enter the current status of the nomination.
* **Comments** (`nomination_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Nomination Organiser** (`nomination_organiser`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person organising this nomination.
* **Nomination Organiser Contact Number** (`nomination_organiser_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the nominee organiser.
* **Nomination Organiser Email** (`nomination_organiser_email`, `text`, required): Enter the email of the nominee organiser.
* **Nomination Location** (`nomination_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the nomination.
* **Nominee's Judicial Experience** (`nominee_judicial_experience`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's judicial experience.
* **Nominee's Bar Number** (`nominee_bar_number`, `text`, required): Enter the nominee's bar number.
