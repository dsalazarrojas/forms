# Self Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Nomination Form is designed to gather information from individuals or teams about their self-nomination for recognition, awards, or other purposes. This form is used to provide a detailed and comprehensive overview of the nominee's category, position, reason, and supporting information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting a category that best describes the nomination or project.
2. Choose one or more positions relevant to the nomination or project.
3. Enter the reason behind the nomination or project.
4. Optionally, provide additional comments and supporting details about the nominee or project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`self_nomination_form`, text): Enter the name of the form, which will be displayed as the title of the nomination or project.
* **Category** (`self_nomination_form_category`, select_one): Select one category that best describes the nomination or project.
* **Position** (`self_nomination_form_position`, select_multiple): Choose one or more positions relevant to the nomination or project.
* **Reason** (`self_nomination_form_reason`, text): Enter a brief description of the reason behind the nomination or project.
* **Support** (`self_nomination_form_support`, note): Provide any additional supporting details or evidence about the nomination or project.
* **Additional Comments** (`self_nomination_form_additional_comments`, text): Add any additional comments or details about the nominee or project.
* **File** (`self_nomination_form_file`, text): Upload any relevant files or documents supporting the nomination or project.
* **Email** (`self_nomination_form_email`, email): Enter the email address of the nominee or team member.
* **Phone** (`self_nomination_form_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the nominee or team member.
* **Date** (`self_nomination_form_date`, date): Enter the date of the event or project completion.
* **Time** (`self_nomination_form_time`, time): Enter the time of the event or project completion.
* **Category 2** (`self_nomination_form_nomination_form_category`, select_one): Select a category for the project or event (second instance of "Category").
* **Position 2** (`self_nomination_form_position_2`, select_multiple): Choose one or more positions for the project or event (second instance of "Position").
* **Support 2** (`self_nomination_form_support_2`, note): Provide supporting details for the project or event (second instance of "Support").
* **Additional Comments 2** (`self_nomination_form_additional_comments_2`, text): Add additional comments for the project or event (second instance of "Additional Comments").
* ... (Additional fields are available for further details and comments)

**Tips**

* Please provide accurate and detailed information to support the nomination or project.
* Ensure that all fields are completed as required.
* Upload any relevant files or documents to support the nomination or project.
* Proofread your submission carefully before submitting the form.
