# Outstanding Young Alumni Recognition Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the Outstanding Young Alumni Recognition program. This program aims to recognize and honor young alumni who have achieved outstanding accomplishments and made a positive impact in their field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the nomination form and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields with the accurate information.
3. Make sure to double-check the email addresses for formatting (e.g. [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form** (`title`, text, required: false): This is the main form section where you can provide a brief description of the outstanding young alumnus.
* **Nominee Details** (`nominee_details`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the nominee's achievements and qualifications.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the university attended by the nominee.
* **University Department** (`university_name_2`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the department where the nominee attended.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Name 2** (`nominee_name_2`, text, required: false): Please enter any other names of the nominee (e.g. maiden names).
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the nominee.
* **Nominee Contact Email 2** (`nominee_contact_email_2`, email, required: false): Please enter any other contact email of the nominee.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Name 2** (`nominator_name_2`, text, required: false): Please enter any other names of the nominator.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email as the nominator.
* **Nominator Contact Email 2** (`nominator_contact_email_2`, email, required: false): Please enter any other contact email of the nominator.

Note: Some fields are marked as "required: false", which means you can leave them blank if they are not applicable. However, be cautious not to leave all fields blank, as it may lead to incomplete nominations.
