# Occupier Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Occupier Of The Year Nomination Form is a tool used to collect nominations for an award or recognition. It allows users to submit nominations for individuals they believe should be considered for an award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information for the nomination.
2. Fill in the fields as requested.
3. Ensure that the submission type is chosen correctly.
4. Attach any supporting evidence for the nomination.
5. Verify that the nominator's information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form**: This is the main form where you can enter your nomination details.
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the person you are nominating.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person you are nominating.
* **Title**: Enter the title or position of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the person you are nominating. Please ensure that it's a valid email address.
* **Nominee Position**: Provide a brief description of the person's position or role. (Optional)
* **Supporting Evidence**: You can attach any supporting evidence or documents that can help justify the nomination.
* **Submission Type**: Choose the type of submission (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date when the submission was made. (Optional)
* **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address as the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter your phone number as the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Title**: Enter your title or position.
* **Nominator Organization**: Enter your organization or company.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- Attach any relevant supporting evidence to strengthen your nomination.
