# organization_awards_nomination_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to nominate an individual or team for an award within the organization. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way to submit nominations, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the Nomination Form with the required details about the nominee.
3. Enter the Award Name, Nominee Name, and Nominee Email to identify the individual or team being nominated.
4. Provide a brief description of the nominee's work or achievement in the Award Description field.
5. Select the relevant award categorization (e.g., Gold, Silver, or Bronze) from the Award Categorization dropdown list.
6. If necessary, add a personal message to support your nomination in the Nomination Message field.
7. Confirm whether this is a nomination form submission (yes/no/maybe).
8. Finally, submit the form to initiate the nomination process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form**: Enter a brief title for the nomination (e.g., "Nomination for John Doe for Excellence in Teamwork").
* **Award Name**: Enter the name of the award for which you are nominating the individual or team.
* **Nominee Name**: Enter the full name of the individual or team being nominated.
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the nominee, so they can be contacted about the nomination.
* **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the nominee, if available.
* **Award Description**: Briefly describe the reasons why you are nominating the individual or team.
* **Nomination Deadline**: Enter the date by which the nomination should be processed (if applicable).
* **Award Categorization**: Select the relevant award categorization (e.g., Gold, Silver, or Bronze) from the list.
* **Nomination Message**: Provide any additional information to support the nomination.
* **Nomination Form Submission**: Confirm whether this is a nomination form submission (yes/no/maybe).
* **Award Recipient**: Enter the name of the award recipient (not to be filled unless you are certain about it).

Note: Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
