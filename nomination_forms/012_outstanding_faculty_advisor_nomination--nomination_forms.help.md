# Outstanding Faculty Advisor Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outstanding Faculty Advisor Nomination form is designed to collect nominations for faculty advisors who have made significant contributions to the university. The form requires minimal information to ensure that the nomination is accurate and complete. Please review the fields below for more details on what to enter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the faculty advisor being nominated.
2. Choose their department and position from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the faculty advisor's email and phone number.
4. Select the year they were nominated for this award.
5. Provide a brief reason for the nomination.
6. Write a short biography for the nominee.
7. Upload a photo of the nominee (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the faculty advisor being nominated.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the faculty advisor being nominated.
- **University** (`university`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the university where the faculty advisor is located.
- **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Select the department of the faculty advisor from the drop-down menu.
- **Faculty Position** (`faculty_position`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the position of the faculty advisor (e.g., Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, Professor).
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the faculty advisor.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the faculty advisor.
- **Award Nomination Year** (`award_nomination_year`, number, required/optional): Select the year the faculty advisor was nominated for this award.
- **Reason for Nomination** (`award_nomination_reason`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of why the faculty advisor deserves this award.
- **Nominee Biography** (`nominee_biography`, text, required/optional): Write a brief biography of the faculty advisor, including any relevant experiences or achievements.
- **Nominee Photo** (`nominee_photo`, text, required/optional): Upload a photo of the faculty advisor (optional).
