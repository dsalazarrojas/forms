**Monthly Performance Nomination Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect nominations for employee performance and to identify those who have demonstrated exceptional abilities. Please nominate employees who have gone above and beyond their job duties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee(s) you wish to nominate.
2. Enter the name of the nominee (nominator) in the "Nominator Name" field.
3. Choose the nominator's department from the options provided.
4. Select the nomination date (month and year).
5. Optionally provide the nominator's phone number.
6. Select the type of form you want to use for the nomination (Form 1, 2, or 3).
7. Provide any additional comments about the nomination.
8. Enter the nominator's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination** (Team Member, Department, optional): Select one or more team members you would like to nominate.
* **Nominator Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Department** (select_one, optional): Select the department of the person making the nomination.
* **Nomination Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the nomination in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Nominator Phone** (text, optional): Provide the phone number of the person making the nomination.
* **Nomination Form** (select_multiple, optional): Select the type of form you want to use for the nomination (Form 1, 2, or 3).
* **Nomination Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments about the nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person making the nomination.

## Tips
* Please review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct department and form type for the nomination.
* You can provide comments to support your nomination if needed.
* The form can be completed by anyone, including team members, managers, or department heads.
