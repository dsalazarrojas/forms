# EdTech Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect nominations for the EdTech Innovation Award. The purpose of this award is to recognize and celebrate outstanding innovations in the field of education technology.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by indicating if you are nominating a school (`School Nomination`) with either 'Yes' or 'No'.
2. Fill out the `Nominee Name` and `Nominee Email` fields with the correct information for the person being nominated.
3. Choose the correct `Nominee Role` from the options provided.
4. Fill out the `School Name` field with the name of the school the nominee is associated with.
5. Select the correct `Innovation Category` from the options provided.
6. Choose the correct `Nomination Year` from the options provided.
7. Provide a brief description of the nominee in the `Nominee Bio` field.
8. Fill out the `Nominee LinkedIn` field with the nominee's LinkedIn profile link.
9. Provide your contact information by filling out the `Nominator Name`, `Nominator School`, `Nominator Email`, `Nominator Phone`, and `Nominator Job Title` fields.
10. Describe why you are nominating this person in the `Nominator Innovation` and `Nominator Justification` fields.
11. Ensure that you have the nominee's agreement by checking the `Nominee Agreement` box.
12. Check if the nominee agrees with the nomination by checking the `Award Recipient Agreement` box.
13. If the nominee has won the award previously, indicate this in the `Award Year` field.
14. If the nominee is a previous award winner, select the correct `Award Category` from the options provided.
15. Fill out the `Award Recipient Name`, `Award Recipient Innovation`, `Award Recipient LinkedIn`, `Award Recipient School`, `Award Recipient Email`, and `Award Recipient Phone` fields with the correct information for the award winner.
16. Ensure that the award winner's agreement is recorded by checking the `Award Recipient Agreement Signed` box.
17. Finally, select the `Submit` button to complete the form and submit your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Nomination** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the nominee is a school by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Nominee Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Fill out the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Fill out the email address of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Role** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the role of the person being nominated from the options provided.
* **School Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Fill out the name of the school the nominee is associated with.
* **Innovation Category** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of innovation for which the nominee is being nominated.
* **Nomination Year** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Choose the year of the nomination from the options provided.
* **Nominee Bio** (`8`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the nominee.
* **Nominee LinkedIn** (`9`, `text`, required): Fill out the nominee's LinkedIn profile link.
* **Nominator Name** (`10`, `text`, required): Fill out your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator School** (`11`, `text`, required): Fill out the name of the school you are associated with.
* **Nominator Email** (`12`, `email`, required): Fill out your email address as the nominator.
* **Nominator Phone** (`13`, `text`, required): Fill out your phone number as the nominator.
* **Nominator Job Title** (`14`, `text`, required): Fill out your job title as the nominator.
* **Nominator Innovation** (`15`, `text`, required): Describe why you are nominating this person.
* **Nominator Justification** (`16`, `text`, required): Describe why you think the nominee deserves the award.
* **Nominee Agreement** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Ensure that the nominee agrees with the nomination by checking the box.
* **Award Year** (`18`, `select_one`, required): If the nominee has won the award previously, indicate this in the `Award Year` field.
* **Award Category** (`19`, `select_one`, required): If the nominee is a previous award winner, select the correct `Award Category` from the options provided.
* **Award Recipient Name** (`20`, `text`, required): Fill out the name of the award winner.
* **Award Recipient Innovation** (`21`, `text`, required): Fill out the innovation of the award winner.
* **Award Recipient LinkedIn** (`22`, `text`, required): Fill out the award winner's LinkedIn profile link.
* **Award Recipient School** (`23`, `text`, required): Fill out the name of the award winner's school.
* **Award Recipient Email** (`24`, `email`, required): Fill out the award winner's email address.
* **Award Recipient Phone** (`25`, `text`, required): Fill out the award winner's phone number.
* **Award Recipient Agreement** (`26`, `select_one`, required): Ensure that the award winner agrees with the nomination by checking the box.
* **Award Recipient Agreement Signed** (`28`, `select_one`, required): Ensure that the award winner's agreement is recorded by checking the box.
* **Nominator Agreement Signed** (`27`, `select_one`, required): Ensure that you, as the nominator, agree with the nomination by checking the box.
* **Submit** (`29`, `text`, required): Select the `Submit` button to complete the form and submit your nomination.
* **Back** (`30`, `text`, required): Select the `Back` button to go back to the previous page.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to increase the chances of your nomination being accepted.
* Review the form carefully to avoid any errors before submitting.
* If you encounter any issues with the form, contact the form administrator for assistance.
