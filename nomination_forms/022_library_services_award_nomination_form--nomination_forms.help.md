# Library Services Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Services Award Nomination Form is used to nominate library services for recognition. The form is designed to help library administrators submit their outstanding library services to be considered for an award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled out a nomination form for the same library location and year before, to avoid duplicate submissions.
2. Gather all the necessary information about your library's service, including a brief description.
3. Choose the correct answer for "Library Services Award" and "Nomination Type".
4. Fill in the "Library" name and "Library Contact" information.
5. Enter the "Nomination Year" and "Nomination Name" fields.
6. Provide a detailed description of the nominated library service in the "Nomination Description" field.
7. Enter the library's contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Library Services Award**: Choose "Yes" if you would like to nominate your library service for an award.
* **Nomination Form**: A brief description of the library service you are nominating.
* **Library**: Enter the name of the library submitting the nomination.
* **Nomination Year**: Enter the year you are nominating for.
* **Nomination Type**: Choose "Yes" if this is an original nomination, "No" if you are nominating again.
* **Nomination Name**: Enter a descriptive name for the nominated library service.
* **Nomination Description**: A detailed description of the library service, including any supporting evidence or documentation.
* **Library Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the library's authorized representative.
* **Library Email**: Enter the email address of the library's authorized representative.
* **Library Phone**: Enter the phone number of the library's authorized representative.
