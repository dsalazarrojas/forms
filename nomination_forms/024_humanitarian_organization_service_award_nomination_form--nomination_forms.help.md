# Humanitarian Organization Service Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Humanitarian Organization Service Award Nomination Form is used to collect information for the nomination of individuals or organizations for the Humanitarian Organization Service Award.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the award type (Service Award or Humanitarian Award).
2.  Fill in your personal information, including name, organization, title, email, phone number, department, job title, and years of service.
3.  If necessary, provide additional comments in the "Nominator Additional Comments" field.
4.  Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Agreed" in the "Nominator Agree" field.
5.  Upload any relevant files, if necessary.
