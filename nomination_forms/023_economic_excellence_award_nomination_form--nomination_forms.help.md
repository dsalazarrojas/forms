# Economic Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Economic Excellence Award Nomination Form is designed to gather information for the nomination of individuals or organizations for an economic excellence award. It is an essential tool for the nomination process, ensuring that all required details are provided accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  **Gather relevant information**: Before filling out the form, ensure that you have all the necessary details about the nominee. This includes their name, contact information, and specific achievements or contributions they wish to be recognized for.

2.  **Fill in the nomination form**: Start by providing general information about the nomination, such as the applicant's name and email (if necessary), and then specify the committee name.

3.  **Submit a nomination**: Once all the details are filled, make sure to provide the nominator's name and their email address for further communication. You can also enter a URL of any relevant nomination form if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nomination Data**: This field is used for the general information about the nomination. Please provide a description of the nominee's achievements or contributions.
*   **Applicant Name**: This field is for the name of the individual or organization being nominated. Ensure it is accurate and correctly spelled.
*   **Applicant Email**: This field is for the contact email of the applicant. If the applicant has a public email address, please enter it here.
*   **Committee Name**: This field is for the name of the committee reviewing or evaluating the nomination. If you're unsure, please leave it blank.
*   **Nominator Name**: This field is for the name of the person or organization making the nomination. This is your own name or the name of your organization.
*   **Nominator Email**: This field is for the email of the person or organization making the nomination. This should be your email address or your organization's contact email.
*   **Nomination Form URL**: If there's a specific nomination form URL, please enter it here. This can be a link to a public form or any other related URL.

## Tips

*   Double-check your input for accuracy, especially for fields marked as required.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or leave it blank as instructed.
*   Ensure that you have all required details before submitting the form.
*   Review all sections carefully and ensure consistency across all fields.

This help guide aims to assist users in understanding the purpose and usage of the Economic Excellence Award Nomination Form, making the process more efficient and reducing confusion. By following these steps and explanations, users should be able to fill out the form accurately and effectively.
