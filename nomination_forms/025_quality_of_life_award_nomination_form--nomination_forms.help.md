# quality-of-life-award-nomination-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal details and nominations for the Quality of Life Award. Please note that this form will be reviewed and verified by the internal team to ensure the nomination is valid and relevant to the award's purpose. To avoid duplication of efforts, please do not submit nominations that have already been submitted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out your personal details on the Personal Details page.
2. Select one category from the Category Selection page.
3. Enter your supporting statements on the Supporting Statements page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, not required):
	+ This section collects your personal contact information.
* **Category Selection** (`category_selection`, select_one, required):
	+ Select one category that best describes your nomination. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Supporting Statements** (`supporting_statements`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your supporting statements to justify why you are nominating someone for the Quality of Life Award.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have permission from the person you are nominating before submitting this form.
* Double-check your personal details for accuracy before submission.
* Supporting statements should be concise and to the point, explaining why the nominee deserves the award.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact the internal team.

Note: The "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3" in the Category Selection field should be replaced with actual category names that reflect the award's purpose and categories.
