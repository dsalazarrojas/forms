# Technical Working Group Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate a member or members to a technical working group.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the nominee's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the nominee's expertise and availability.
3. Select the committee reviewing the nomination.
4. Provide any additional comments or information about the nominee.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form**: A description of the nominee's qualifications and experience.
	+ This is the main form where you will enter the details of the nominee.
* **Nomination Details**: A brief description of the nomination and its purpose.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to provide context about the nomination.
* **Nominee Details**: Details about the nominee, including their name, email, and phone number.
	+ This field is used to contact the nominee for further information.
* **Expertise**: A description of the nominee's technical expertise and experience.
	+ This field helps the committee determine whether the nominee has the necessary expertise.
* **Participation Availability**: Details about the nominee's availability to participate in the working group.
	+ This field helps the committee determine whether the nominee can participate in the working group.
* **Nominee Name**: The full name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Email**: The email address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone**: The phone number of the nominee.
* **Nomination Date**: The date the nomination was submitted.
* **Participation Start**: The date the nominee can start participating in the working group.
* **Participation End**: The date the nominee can end participating in the working group.
* **Participation Frequency**: The frequency of the nominee's participation in the working group.
* **Nominee Employer**: The employer of the nominee.
* **Nominee Job Title**: The job title of the nominee.
* **Nominee Department**: The department of the nominee.
* **Committee Reviewing**: The committee that will review the nomination.
* **Committee Nominated By**: The committee that nominated the individual for the working group.
* **Nominee Availability**: Details about the nominee's availability.
* **Nominee Comments**: Any additional comments or information about the nominee.
* **Nominee Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or information about the nominee.
* **Committee Comments**: Any additional comments or information from the committee.
* **Committee Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or information from the committee.
* **Form Submitted By**: The person who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date**: The date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By**: The person who submitted the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the nominee.
* Check the eligibility criteria before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further information, contact the committee.
