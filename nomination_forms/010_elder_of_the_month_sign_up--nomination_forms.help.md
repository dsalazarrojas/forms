# Elder Of The Month Sign Up - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Elder of the Month" nomination form is a tool used to collect information about individuals who are nominated for recognition and rewards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the month for which you would like to nominate someone (nomination_month).
2. Provide a brief story about the nominee (elder_story).
3. Enter the name of the person being nominated (nominee_name).
4. Enter the age of the nominee (nominee_age).
5. Enter the occupation or field of work of the nominee (nominee_occupation).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Month**: (`nomination_month`, date, optional)
	+ Select the month for which you would like to nominate someone. This will help us track nominations over the year.
* **Elder Story**: (`elder_story`, text, optional)
	+ Provide a brief story or description about why you are nominating this individual. This can include their achievements, accomplishments, or characteristics that make them a great nominee.
* **Nominee Name**: (`nominee_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the full name of the person being nominated. This will be used to verify their identity and ensure they are a real person.
* **Nominee Age**: (`nominee_age`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the age of the nominee. This will help us understand their life stage and experiences.
* **Nominee Occupation**: (`nominee_occupation`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the occupation or field of work of the nominee. This can include their profession, hobby, or other areas of expertise.

## Tips
* Only authorized personnel can submit nominations. 
* Please verify that the nomination is not already listed in the database to avoid duplicates. 
* If a nomination is submitted multiple times, only the first one will be counted.
