# Corporate Sustainability Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Sustainability Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information about a company's sustainability initiatives and achievements. This form is used to submit nominations for the Corporate Sustainability Award, which recognizes outstanding corporate sustainability practices and achievements. Please ensure that you are eligible to submit a nomination before filling out this form. To avoid duplication, please review our previous submissions to ensure that your nomination is not already on file.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the Nomination Form with a clear and concise title and description of your company's sustainability initiatives.
*   Choose the relevant sustainability focus area (Environment, Social, or Economic) that best describes your company's sustainability initiatives.
*   Indicate whether your company has achieved any of the listed sustainability initiatives.
*   Provide the name and contact information of the person submitting the nomination.
*   Click on the "Submit" button to complete your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nomination Form** (text): Enter a brief title and description of your company's sustainability initiatives. This should provide a clear overview of your company's achievements and initiatives.
*   **Company Details** (text): Enter your company's name and other relevant information.
*   **Sustainability Focus Area** (select one): Choose the sustainability focus area that best describes your company's initiatives. This can be Environment, Social, or Economic.
*   **Sustainability Initiatives** (select multiple): Indicate whether your company has achieved any of the listed sustainability initiatives.
*   **Nomination Type** (select one): Choose whether you are submitting a Nomination or a Submission.
*   **Nomination Title** (text): Enter a title for your nomination.
*   **Nomination Description** (text): Enter a description of your nomination.
*   **Nomination Year** (number): Enter the year of your nomination.
*   **Nominator Name** (text): Enter your name and contact information.
*   **Nominator Job Title** (text): Enter your job title.
*   **Nominator Email** (email): Enter your email address.
*   **Nominator Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
*   **Nominator Organization** (text): Enter your organization's name.
*   **Nominator Address** (text): Enter your address.
*   **Nominator Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments about your nomination.
*   **Back to Top** (text): This is not a field to fill out. You can click on it to go back to the top of the form.
*   **Help** (note): This is not a field to fill out. Click on it for more information about the form.

Please note that this help guide is a general guidance and may not cover all the specific requirements and guidelines of the Corporate Sustainability Award. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
