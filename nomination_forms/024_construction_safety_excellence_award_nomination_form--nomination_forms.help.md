# Construction Safety Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to recognize and celebrate individuals or teams that have demonstrated exceptional safety achievements or contributions in the construction industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the date of your nomination.
2. Select the award category that best describes the nominee's achievements.
3. Provide the full name of the nominee.
4. Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the nominee (if applicable).
5. Enter the job title or position of the nominee.
6. Enter your name as the nominator.
7. Enter your company name as the nominator.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number (optional).
10. Specify how you know the nominee (e.g., supervisor, coworker, subordinate, etc.).
11. Explain why you're nominating the person or team.
12. Describe the specific safety accomplishments or contributions of the nominee.
13. Explain the impact of their actions on improving safety.
14. Enter the time period during which these achievements occurred.
15. If applicable, enter the number of incident-free days.
16. If applicable, describe any safety programs or initiatives started.
17. If applicable, describe safety training provided.
18. If applicable, describe any innovative safety practices.
19. If applicable, provide statistics or metrics showing improvement.
20. If applicable, list any supporting documents or evidence.
21. If applicable, confirm if references are provided.
22. If applicable, provide names and contact information for references.
23. Confirm if the nominee has received previous safety awards.
24. If applicable, enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Date** (1): Enter the date you are submitting this nomination.
* **Award Category** (2): Select the category that best describes the nominee's achievements.
	+ Options:
		- Individual Safety Excellence
		- Team Safety Excellence
		- Safety Innovation
		- Safety Leadership
		- Zero Incident Achievement
		- Safety Improvement
* **Nominee Name** (3): Enter the full name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Company** (4): Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the nominee (if applicable).
* **Nominee Role** (5): Enter the job title or position of the nominee.
* **Nominator Name** (7): Enter your name as the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominator Company** (8): Enter your company name.
* **Nominator Email** (9): Enter your email address.
* **Nominator Phone** (10): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Relationship to Nominee** (11): Specify how you know the nominee.
	+ Options:
		- Supervisor
		- Coworker
		- Subordinate
		- Client
		- Safety Professional
		- Other
* **Reason for Nomination** (12): Explain why you're nominating this person or team.
* **Safety Achievements** (13): Describe specific safety accomplishments or contributions.
* **Impact of Safety Contributions** (14): Explain how the nominee's actions improved safety.
* **Time Period of Achievements** (15): Enter the time period during which these achievements occurred.
* **Incident-Free Days** (16): If applicable, enter the number of incident-free days.
* **Safety Initiatives Led** (17): If applicable, describe any safety programs or initiatives started.
* **Training Contributions** (18): If applicable, describe safety training provided.
* **Innovative Safety Practices** (19): If applicable, describe any new or creative safety solutions.
* **Measurable Safety Results** (20): If applicable, provide statistics or metrics showing improvement.
* **Supporting Documents** (21): If applicable, list any supporting documents or evidence.
* **References Provided** (22): Confirm if references are provided.
* **Reference Contacts** (23): If applicable, provide names and contact information for references.
* **Previous Awards** (24): Confirm if the nominee has received previous safety awards.
* **Additional Comments** (25): Enter any other supporting information.
* **Nominator Signature** (26): Type your full name as the signature.

This form is designed to guide you through the nomination process and help you complete it accurately. Please answer each field thoroughly and concisely, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification.
