# Professional Association Officer Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit nominations for the officers of the Professional Association. Please use this form to cast your vote for the candidate of your choice for each available officer position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the officer position for which you would like to nominate a candidate.
2. Enter the First Name and Last Name of the candidate.
3. Enter a valid email address and phone number for the candidate.
4. Select the chapter associated with the candidate.
5. Enter the year in which the candidate was elected.
6. Enter any additional comments about the candidate.
7. Confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Officer Position (id: 1, label: Officer Position)**: Select the officer position for which you would like to nominate a candidate.
* **First Name (id: 2, label: First Name)**: Enter the first name of the candidate.
* **Last Name (id: 3, label: Last Name)**: Enter the last name of the candidate.
* **Contact Email (id: 4, label: Contact Email)**: Enter a valid email address for the candidate.
* **Contact Phone (id: 5, label: Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number for the candidate.
* **Chapter Name (id: 6, label: Chapter Name)**: Select the chapter associated with the candidate.
* **Election Year (id: 7, label: Election Year)**: Enter the year in which the candidate was elected.
* **Officer Name (id: 8, label: Officer Name)**: Enter the name of the candidate (if different from the one entered in First Name and Last Name fields).
* **Vote Cast (id: 9, label: Vote Cast)**: Enter the date when the candidate was voted.
* **Vote Time (id: 10, label: Vote Time)**: Enter the time when the candidate was voted.
* **Comments (id: 11, label: Comments)**: Enter any additional comments about the candidate.
* **Submit (id: 12, label: Submit)**: Click this button to submit your nomination.
* **Confirm (id: 13, label: Confirm)**: Confirm your submission before it is saved.
* **Confirm Message (id: 14, label: Confirm Message)**: Enter a message to confirm your submission.
* **Submitted By (id: 15, label: Submitted By)**: The name of the user who submitted the nomination.
* **Submitted At (id: 16, label: Submitted At)**: The date and time when the nomination was submitted.
* **Form ID (id: 17, label: Form ID)**: A unique identifier for the form.
* **User ID (id: 18, label: User ID)**: The ID of the user who submitted the nomination.
* **Created At (id: 19, label: Created At)**: The date when the nomination was created.
* **Updated At (id: 20, label: Updated At)**: The date when the nomination was last updated.
* **Assigned To (id: 21, label: Assigned To)**: The ID of the user assigned to this form.
* **Assigned At (id: 22, label: Assigned At)**: The date when the form was assigned to a user.
* **Created By (id: 23, label: Created By)**: The name of the user who created the form.
* **Updated By (id: 24, label: Updated By)**: The name of the user who last updated the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address for the candidate.
* Enter a valid phone number for the candidate.
* Confirm your submission before it is saved.
* Do not submit duplicate nominations.
