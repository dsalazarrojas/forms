# Investigator Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate an Investigator for an award. It is designed to collect information about the nominee and the reason for the nomination.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name and contact information of the Investigator you are nominating (Nominee Information).
2. Select the award you wish to nominate the Investigator for (Award).
3. Provide a brief reason for the nomination (Reason for Nomination).
4. Add any additional comments or information about the nominee.
5. Enter your own name and contact information (Your Information).
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee Information (<nominee_info>, text)**: Enter the full name and contact information of the Investigator you are nominating, including their name, email address, and phone number.
* **Award (<award, select_one>):** Select the award you wish to nominate the Investigator for from the options provided (Best Investigator, Outstanding Investigator, Exemplary Investigator).
* **Reason for Nomination (<reason_for_nomination, text>):** Enter a brief reason for nominating the Investigator. This could be a description of their achievements, contributions, or qualities that make them deserving of the award.
* **Additional Comments (<additional_comments, text>):** If you have any additional information or comments about the nominee, enter them here.
* **Your Information (<submitter_info, text>):** Enter your name and contact information, so that we can follow up with you about the nomination.
* **Name (<submitter_name, text>):** Enter your name.
* **Email (<submitter_email, email>):** Enter your email address.
* **Phone (<submitter_phone, text>):** Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the nominee is aware of the nomination before submitting their information.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact email or phone number].
