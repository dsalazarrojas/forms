# Superlative Selection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Superlative Selection Survey is a form designed to collect nominations for awards in various categories. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form and provide explanations for each field.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to select the correct award title, category, and nominee details.
2. Provide a brief reason for the nomination, if required.
3. Vote for the awards by selecting the correct category from the options.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification. (id: 1, type: text)
* **Email**: Enter your email address. (id: 2, type: email)
* **Title**: Select your title (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.). (id: 3, type: select_one)
* **Award Name**: Enter the name of the award you are nominating for. (id: 4, type: text)
* **Nominee**: Enter the name of the person or organization being nominated. (id: 5, type: text)
* **Reason for Nomination**: Provide a brief reason for the nomination. (id: 6, type: note)
* **Vote for**: Indicate whether you want to vote for this nomination. (id: 20, type: select_one)
* **Vote for 1**: Select the category you want to vote for. (id: 21, type: select_one)
* **Vote for 2**: Select an additional category you want to vote for, if applicable. (id: 22, type: select_one)
* **Award Title**: Enter the title of the award you are nominating for. (id: 10, type: text)
* **Award Category**: Select the category for the award. (id: 11, type: select_one)
* **Award Description**: Enter a description of the award. (id: 12, type: text)
* **Recipient**: Enter the name of the recipient of the award. (id: 13, type: text)
* **Award Year**: Enter the year the award was given. (id: 14, type: text)
* **Award Year Ending**: Enter the ending year of the award. (id: 15, type: time)
* **Submit**: Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields. (id: 16, type: text)
* **Award Form Submit**: Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields. (id: 17, type: text)
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information regarding the award. (id: 18, type: text)
* **Nomination Form Submit**: Submit the nomination form once you have completed all the required fields. (id: 19, type: text)
* **Vote**: Indicate whether you want to vote for this nomination. (id: 20, type: select_one)
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information regarding the nomination. (id: 23, type: text)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Select the correct category for the award and vote for it accordingly.
* Provide a clear and concise reason for the nomination, if required.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
