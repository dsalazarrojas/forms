This help guide is for the "el_nido_tour_reservation_form" form, which is used to collect information from customers for tour reservations. The form is designed to be completed by customers who wish to book a tour with our company. It is not necessary for customers to complete this form in order to book a tour, but it will help us to better understand their preferences and provide a more personalized experience.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the booking date for the tour you wish to book.
2. Provide your customer name, ensuring to include your correct spelling and capitalization.
3. Enter your customer email address, which will be used to contact you with tour details and updates.
4. Enter your customer phone number, which can be used to reach you in case of any issues with your tour.
5. Add any customer notes or special requests you may have for your tour.
6. Leave a message or comment about your tour preferences.
7. Enter your customer address, including the street number and city.
8. Enter the city where the tour will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the tour you wish to book.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address we will use to contact you with tour details and updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, which can be used to reach you in case of any issues with your tour.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, required: false): If you have any special requests or notes for your tour, enter them here.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, required: false): Leave a message or comment about your tour preferences.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Enter your customer address, including the street number and city.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where the tour will take place.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with completing the form, contact our support team.
* You can also contact us with any questions or concerns regarding your tour.
