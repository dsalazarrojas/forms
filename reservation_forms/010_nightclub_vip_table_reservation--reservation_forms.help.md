# Nightclub VIP Table Reservation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from VIPs who have reserved a table at a nightclub. The form helps the venue's staff prepare for the VIP's visit and provide an exceptional experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your basic information, including any reservation details that are required fields.
2. Share any special preferences or requests with us, so we can tailor your experience.
3. Choose your preferred payment method.
4. Leave a review or feedback if you'd like to help us improve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Form** (`reservation_form`, text, optional): Enter your reservation details, if any.
* **Reservation Info** (`reservation_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your reservation.
* **VIP Details** (`vip_details`, text, optional): Share any special details about your VIP experience.
* **VIP Preferences** (`vip_preferences`, text, optional): Let us know about your personal preferences or requests.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know.
* **Bottle Service** (`bottle_service`, text, optional): Indicate if you'd like to have bottle service during your visit.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, optional): Enter any details about your guests, such as their names and contact information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, optional): Choose your preferred payment method for the night.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, text, optional): If you have a preferred payment gateway, let us know.
* **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on your experience at the nightclub.
* **Review Message** (`review_message`, text, optional): Enter a message about your experience to help us improve.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your reservation.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your reservation.
* **VIP Name** (`vip_name`, text, optional): Enter your VIP name (if applicable).

Note: Some fields may seem redundant or optional, but filling them out can help ensure that your experience is tailored to your needs. Take a moment to review each field carefully and provide any details that you feel are relevant.
