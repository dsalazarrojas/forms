# Free Client Consultation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to schedule a free consultation with our team. It's intended for potential clients to provide their basic contact information and schedule a meeting with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Fill out your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a Date of visit from the calendar and choose a specific date for your consultation.
6. Choose a Time of visit for your consultation from the available time slots.
7. Review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name for our records.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Use a valid email address for us to contact you about your consultation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the "XXX-XXX-XXXX" format, including the area code and country code (if applicable).
* **Date of visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required: false): Choose the date you would like to have your consultation.
* **Time of visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Select the time for your consultation from the available options.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter accurate and correct contact information to ensure we can contact you about your consultation.
- Review your input carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
