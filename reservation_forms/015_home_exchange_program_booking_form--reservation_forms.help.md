# Home Exchange Program Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to book a home exchange program, a service that allows people to live in someone else's home for free, but in return for taking care of it while the homeowner is away.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your booking request in the `form_title` field.
2. Select the category of your booking request in the `category` field.
3. Provide a brief description of your booking request in the `form_description` and `form_description_2` fields.
4. Schedule a review or assessment of your stay in the `review` field.
5. Choose your preferred follow-up time in the `follow_up` field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the `email` field.
7. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
8. Leave any general notes or comments in the `form_note` and `form_description_3` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of this form to provide a clear description of your booking request.
* **reservation_form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): This field seems to be a placeholder or used for internal reference.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of your booking request. This will help us understand the type of booking you are making.
* **description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your booking request.
* **description** (`form_description_2`, text, required/optional): Provide additional details about your booking request.
* **review** (`review`, time, required/optional): Schedule a review or assessment of your stay.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred follow-up time. Options are "Faster" and "Smoother".
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **note** (`form_note`, note, required/optional): Leave any general notes or comments about your booking request.
* **details** (`form_description_3`, note, required/optional): This field seems to be used for additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your booking request is complete and we can process it efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review and follow up with you regarding your booking request according to your preferred schedule.
