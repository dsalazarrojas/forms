# Back Number Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Back Number Reservation Form" is a form designed to collect information from players to reserve numbers for their jerseys. This form is crucial for team administrators to make informed decisions about number reservations, taking into account the player's preference and team requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your Full Name**: Fill in your first and last name in the "Full Name" field.
2. **Enter your Email Address**: Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field to confirm your identity.
3. **Enter your Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. **Select your Team Name**: Choose your team from the list of options in the "Team Name" field.
5. **Choose your Jersey Size**: Select your jersey size from the options in the "Jersey Size" field.
6. **Select your Preferred Jersey Number**: Choose your first choice jersey number from the options in the "Preferred Jersey Number" field.
7. **Choose Your Second (optional) Jersey Number**: If you want, choose a backup jersey number in the "Second Choice Number" field.
8. **Choose Your Third (optional) Jersey Number**: If you want, choose an alternative jersey number in the "Third Choice Number" field.
9. **Explain Number Significance**: If you selected a specific jersey number, explain why it's important to you in the "Number Significance" field.
10. **List numbers you have worn before**: If applicable, list the numbers you have worn before in the "Numbers Worn Previously" field.
11. **Select your Position**: Choose the position you play in the "Position Played" field.
12. **Years on Team**: Indicate how long you have been on the team in the "Years on Team" field.
13. **Priority Request**: If this number is important for a special reason, select "Yes, special significance" in the "Is This a Priority Request" field.
14. **Leadership Role**: Check if you are a team captain, assistant captain, coach, or a player in the "Are You Captain or Coach" field.
15. **Agree to Terms**: Confirm that you agree to the number assignment decision in the "Agreement to Terms" field.
16. **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
