# Guest Shuttle Route Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Shuttle Route Request Form is designed to collect information from guests about their preferences for a shuttle service, allowing the service to provide tailored and convenient transportation options.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the Guest Shuttle Request form on **Page 1**.
2.  Proceed to **Page 2: Passenger Details** and input your passenger information.
3.  On **Page 3: Additional Details**, enter any additional remarks or comments about your request.
4.  Go to **Page 4: Route Details** and specify your preferred route.
5.  If necessary, on **Page 5: Transportation**, choose your preferred mode of transportation.
6.  Review and confirm your information on **Page 6: Review & Confirm**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Guest Shuttle Request** (`guest_shuttle_request_page`, text, required): Enter the specific request for the shuttle service you need.
*   **Passenger Details** (`passenger_details_page`, text, required): Provide details about the passenger(s) requesting the shuttle service.
*   **Additional Details** (`request_details_page`, text, required): Enter any additional information or remarks related to your shuttle request.
*   **Route Details** (`route_details_page`, text, required): Specify the preferred route for the shuttle service.
*   **Transportation** (`transportation_details_page`, text, optional): If you have a preferred mode of transportation, select it here.
*   **Review & Confirm** (`review_details_page`, text, required): Review your submission and confirm that the information is accurate.
