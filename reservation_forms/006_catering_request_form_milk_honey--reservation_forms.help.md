# Catering Request Form Milk & Honey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Request Form for Milk & Honey is a comprehensive form designed to help us better understand your event needs and preferences. Please fill out this form to provide us with essential information about your event, and we will use it to create a tailored experience for you and your guests. This form is required for all event catering requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Information" section with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the "Event Information" section, provide the date, time, and location of your event.
3. Choose the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, Birthday Party, etc.).
4. Indicate the number of guests expected.
5. Select the service style and preferences for your event (e.g., Buffet, Plated Service, etc.).
6. Choose whether bar service is needed and specify the bar staffing requirements.
7. Select the types of beverage services you would like to have.
8. If needed, select additional services (e.g., linens, floral arrangements, etc.).
9. Provide any special setup requirements or additional services needed.
10. Indicate your approximate budget range per guest.
11. Add any additional notes or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_organization`, text, optional): If you are a business or organization, please enter your name.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of your event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the full address of your event location.
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, Birthday Party, etc.).
* **Expected Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Indicate the number of guests expected.
* **Service Style** (`service_style`, select_one, required): Choose the service style for your event (e.g., Buffet, Plated Service, etc.).
* **Bar Service Needed?** (`bar_service_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether bar service is needed.
* **Bar Staffing Requirements** (`bar_staffing`, text, optional): Specify the bar staffing requirements.
* **Beverage Services** (`beverage_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of beverage services you would like to have.
* **Preferred Cuisine Type** (`cuisine_type`, select_one, required): Select the preferred cuisine type for your event.
* **Special Dietary Needs** (`dietary_needs`, text, optional): If you have any special dietary needs, please specify.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services needed (e.g., linens, floral arrangements, etc.).
* **Special Setup Requirements** (`setup_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special setup requirements.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, text, optional): Indicate your approximate budget range per guest.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requests** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or special requests.
* **Best time to contact you** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional): Indicate the best time to contact you back.
