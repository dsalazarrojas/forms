# Renewable Energy Campus Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Campus Booking Form is used to collect reservation data for campus visits. It can be used for a variety of purposes such as general tours, educational visits, events, or other specific reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reservation data in the "Reservation Form Data" page.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" page.
3. Specify the group size and visit purpose in the corresponding fields.
4. Choose the visit dates and preferred date for your visit.
5. Enter the start and end times for your visit.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
7. Confirm your submission by choosing "Yes" in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Form Data** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your reservation.
* **Contact Details** (2, text, required/optional): Provide your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Group Size** (3, number, required/optional): Specify the number of people in your group.
* **Visit Purpose** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the reason for your visit (General Tour, Educational Visit, Event, or Other).
* **Visit Dates** (5, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the dates you plan to visit the campus.
* **Preferred Date** (6, date, required/optional): Enter the preferred date for your visit.
* **Start Time** (7, time, required/optional): Enter the start time for your visit.
* **End Time** (8, time, required/optional): Enter the end time for your visit.
* **Notes** (9, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes for your visit.
* **Comments** (10, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes for your visit.
* **Submit** (11, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your submission by choosing "Yes".
