# Seat Selection For Travel - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seat Selection For Travel form is a tool used by travelers to provide information about their upcoming travel plans. This includes personal details, trip specifics, and special requirements. Accurate completion of the form helps us plan and prepare for your trip effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your traveler name in the "Traveler Name" field.
2.  Enter the date of your travel in the "Travel Date" field.
3.  Select the time of your travel in the "Travel Time" field.
4.  Indicate the destination of your trip in the "Destination" field.
5.  Enter your flight number in the "Flight Number" field.
6.  Choose your preferred travel class from the "Travel Class" dropdown menu, which is a critical part of seat selection.
7.  If you have a specific seat preference (e.g., left, right, front, or back), select it in the "Seat Preferred" field.
8.  If you have any special requests, such as extra amenities or services, select "Yes" in the "Special Request" field.
9.  Optionally, provide additional comments or details about your trip in the "Notes" field.
10. Enter your email address and phone number to allow us to contact you about your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveler Name**: Please enter your name.
* **Travel Date**: Enter the date of your travel.
* **Travel Time**: Select the time of your travel.
* **Destination**: Enter the destination of your trip.
* **Flight Number**: Enter your flight number.
* **Travel Class**: Select your preferred travel class: Economy, Business, or First.
* **Seat Preferred**: Choose your preferred seat location: Left, Right, Front, or Back.
* **Special Request**: Select "Yes" if you have any special requests; "No" otherwise.
* **Notes**: Optionally, provide any additional comments or details about your trip.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your trip.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled before submission.
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before saving your entry.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
