# Restaurant Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Reservation Form is used to collect information for a restaurant reservation. Fill out this form to make a reservation at the restaurant with the details of your visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the reservation date and time that suits you best.
2. Choose the number of people in your party.
3. Enter your contact name and email to ensure we can get back to you about your reservation.
4. Enter your contact phone number so we can contact you if needed.
5. Select the restaurant you are making the reservation for.
6. Add any additional notes or instructions for your reservation or for our staff.
7. Finally, review your submission to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date**: Select the date you would like to make your reservation.
* **Reservation Time**: Select the time you would like to make your reservation.
* **Party Size**: Enter the number of people in your party (e.g., 2 people).
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name, so we can get back to you about your reservation.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address, so we can contact you about your reservation.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you if needed.
* **Restaurant Name**: Select the restaurant you are making the reservation for.
* **Reservation Time Note**: Add any additional notes or instructions for your reservation (e.g., "Please reserve a table by the window").
* **Contact Note**: Add any additional notes or instructions for our staff (e.g., "We have a special request for the birthday party").
