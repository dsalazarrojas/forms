# Conference Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Booking Request Form is used to reserve a spot for an individual or a group at a conference or event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the conference you wish to attend from the list of available options.
2. Provide your personal details, including your name, email address, phone number, and job title (if applicable).
3. Choose your preferred meal options and accommodation needs.
4. Select your payment method.
5. Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Conference** (Select Conference, required): Choose the conference you wish to attend from the available options.
* **Conference Date** (Conference Date, required): Event dates for the selected conference.
* **First Name** (First Name, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, required): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization** (Organization, required): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Registration Type** (Registration Type, required): Choose the ticket category for your attendance (e.g., Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, etc.).
* **Number of Attendees** (Number of Attendees, required): Enter the total number of guests.
* **Meal Preferences** (Meal Preferences, optional): Choose your preferred meal options (e.g., Continental Breakfast, Full Hot Breakfast, etc.).
* **Breakfast** (Breakfast, optional): Select your preferred breakfast option.
* **Lunch** (Lunch, optional): Select your preferred lunch option.
* **Dinner** (Dinner, optional): Select your preferred dinner option.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Dietary Restrictions, optional): Enter any dietary allergies or restrictions.
* **Accommodation Needed** (Accommodation Needed, optional): Choose if you need hotel accommodation.
* **Number of Nights** (Number of Nights, optional): Enter the duration of your hotel stay.
* **Room Type** (Room Type, optional): Choose your preferred hotel room type.
* **Payment Information** (Payment Information, optional): Review your billing details.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, optional): Enter any additional needs or requests.
* **Terms Acceptance** (Terms Acceptance, required): Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Ensure you select a conference that has not been fully booked.
* Some fields are mandatory, so please ensure you fill out all required fields.
* Review your choices carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional needs or requests, please enter them in the Special Requests field.
