# Hotel Guest Preference Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather important information about your preferences and needs during your stay at our hotel. Please take a few minutes to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure we can provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read each field carefully and fill out the required information.
2. If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please specify them in the relevant fields.
3. Make sure to provide your arrival and departure dates accurately.
4. Fill out the contact information fields to ensure we can reach you for any important updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Start** (form_start, note, optional): This is the starting point of the form. Please do not fill out anything here.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (dietary_restrictions, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, such as vegetarian or gluten-free, please specify them here.
* **Room Requirements** (room_requirements, text, optional): If you have any specific room requirements, such as extra beds or non-smoking, please list them here.
* **Special Requests** (special_requests, text, optional): If you have any other special requests, such as early check-in or late check-out, please specify them here.
* **Guest's Diet** (guest_diet, select_one, optional): Do you require any specific dietary options during your stay? (Yes/No)
* **Other Dietary Options** (guest_diet_other, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for Guest's Diet, please specify your dietary requirements here.
* **Room Type** (room_type, select_multiple, optional): What type of room do you prefer? (Please select all that apply)
* **Other Room Type** (room_type_other, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for Room Type, please specify your preferred room type here.
* **Arrival Time** (arrival_time, time, optional): What time are you planning to arrive at the hotel?
* **Departure Time** (departure_time, time, optional): What time are you planning to leave the hotel?
* **Arrival Date** (arrival_date, date, optional): What date are you planning to arrive at the hotel?
* **Departure Date** (departure_date, date, optional): What date are you planning to leave the hotel?
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact information so we can reach you for any updates.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, optional): Please provide your phone number for any emergency or urgent updates.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Please provide your email address for any updates or confirmations.
* **Additional Notes** (additional_notes, text, optional): Is there anything else you would like to let us know about your stay?
