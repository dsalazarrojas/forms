# Student Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to express your interest in requesting accommodation on campus. Please submit this form to let us know about your needs so we can provide you with the necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the fields as instructed.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Submit the form after you have reviewed and agreed with the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required: false): Please select the date you are looking to reserve the accommodation.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter your university-provided email address.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are looking for accommodation.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Please select the start date of your accommodation period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Please select the end date of your accommodation period.
* **Accommodation Request** (`accommodation_request`, text, required: false): Describe the type of accommodation you are looking for.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your personal phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
