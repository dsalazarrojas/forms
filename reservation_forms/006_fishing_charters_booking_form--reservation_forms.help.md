# Fishing Charters Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their fishing charter preferences, including their contact information, trip details, and other relevant booking details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with your name and email address in the "customer" field.
- Provide a contact method (phone number or email) in the "contact" field.
- Describe your desired trip details, including the type of fishing charter, date, and duration of the trip in the "trip details" field.
- Choose the type of fishing charter you're interested in from the "trip type" dropdown menu.
- Select the date and time for your trip in the "date" and "time" fields.
- Specify the duration of your trip in the "duration" field.
- Provide the name of your captain (if applicable) in the "captain" field.
- Include any additional notes or special requests in the "notes" field.
- Verify that your contact email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date in the "email" and "phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer** (`customer`, `text`): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `text`): Enter your phone number or email address so we can reach you.
* **Trip details** (`trip_details`, `text`): Describe your desired fishing charter trip, including the type of fish you want to catch, the location, and any specific requirements or requests.
* **Trip type** (`trip_type`, `select_one`): Choose the type of fishing charter that suits your needs, such as a morning or afternoon charter.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`): Select the date for your fishing charter trip.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`): Select the time for your fishing charter trip.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `number`): Specify the length of your fishing charter trip in hours.
* **Captain** (`captain`, `text`): If you've chosen a captain, enter their name here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`): Include any additional information or special requests you have for your fishing charter trip.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Verify that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Verify that your phone number is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`): Once you've completed all the fields, click this button to submit your booking request.
