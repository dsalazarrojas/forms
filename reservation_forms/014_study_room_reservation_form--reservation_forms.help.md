# Study Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Room Reservation Form is used to reserve a study room for a specific date and time period. This form helps the library manage study room availability and ensure that students have a reserved space for studying and studying-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date for the reservation from the calendar.
2. Enter the start and end time of the reservation (e.g., 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM).
3. Enter your name and email (required fields).
4. If you have a phone number, enter it (optional).
5. Choose the room type (e.g., Academic, Social).
6. Select the study space where you would like to reserve the room (e.g., Library, Study lounge).
7. If needed, add any notes for the librarian or staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (study_room_reservation_form)**: This is the title of the form, which is "Study Room Reservation Form".
* **Date (date)**: Select the date for which you want to reserve the room. Please ensure it is within the available dates provided by the calendar.
* **Start Time (start_time)**: Enter the start time of your reservation. This is the time when you would like to begin using the room.
* **End Time (end_time)**: Enter the end time of your reservation. This is the time when you would like to end using the room.
* **End Date (end_date)**: This field is currently not being used, and you can ignore it.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your institutional email address, which will be used for communication about your reservation and any updates.
* **Phone (phone)**: If you have a phone number, enter it to ensure we can contact you if there are any changes or updates.
* **Room Request (room_request)**: Choose the type of room you need for your study session. Options are:
	+ Small room
	+ Medium room
	+ Large room
* **Room Type (room_type)**: Select the type of room you would like to reserve (e.g., Academic, Social).
* **Study Space (study_space)**: Choose where you would like to reserve the room (e.g., Library, Study lounge).
* **Note (note)**: If you have any additional information or requests, such as a specific room location or any special accommodations, enter it here.
