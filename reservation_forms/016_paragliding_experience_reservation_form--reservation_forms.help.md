# Paragliding Experience Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paragliding Experience Reservation Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in experiencing paragliding, a type of adventure sky sports activity. This form will help the paragliding staff to better understand your needs and preferences and to provide a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the fields as accurately as possible.
2. If you have any questions or doubts, do not hesitate to contact the paragliding staff.
3. Ensure that you are not duplicating your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **experience_details** (Text): This field is where you can describe your previous paragliding experience, if any. If this is your first time, please provide any relevant information that will help us to provide a safe and enjoyable experience.
* **location** (Select One): Please choose a location where you would like to have the paragliding experience. Our staff will ensure that the location is safe and suitable for the activity.
* **reservation_date** (Date): Please select the date when you would like to have the paragliding experience. Please note that availability might be limited, so it's recommended to book in advance.
* **reservation_time** (Time): Please select the time slot when you would like to have the paragliding experience. Again, availability might be limited, so please book early to avoid disappointment.
* **number_of_adventurers** (Number): Please indicate how many people will be participating in the paragliding experience.
* **age** (Number): Please provide your age to help us ensure that the experience is suitable for you.
* **contact_number** (Text): This field is where you can provide your contact number, so that we can reach out to you to confirm the reservation.
* **email** (Email): Please provide your email address, so that we can communicate with you about the reservation and any further instructions.
* **provider** (Select Multiple): Please choose one or more providers that will be conducting the paragliding experience. This will help us to provide the best possible experience for you.
* **comments** (Text): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or requests that you may have for the paragliding experience.
