# Vineyard Guesthouse Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vineyard Guesthouse Booking Form is designed to collect essential information from guests for their stay at the vineyard guesthouse. This form helps our administration team to process booking requests and tailor their service to meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. **Checkin**: Select the date and time you would like to check-in.
2. **Checkout**: Choose the date and time you would like to check-out.
3. **Guests**: Enter the total number of guests staying with you.
4. **Rooms**: Select the number of rooms you would like to book.
5. **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your stay.
6. **Credit Card Number**, **CVV**, **Expiration Date**, and **Card Type**: If you choose to pay by credit card, enter these details here.
7. **Form Submission**: Please let us know if you would like to receive any additional information or confirm your booking submission.
8. **Special Requests**, **Reservation Dates**, **Reservations Times**, **Room Types**, **Room Names**, and **Special Requests2**: Answer the questions about your stay, including any special requests and reservation details.
9. **Message**: If you have any further questions or requests, please let us know in the message field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
* If you have any questions or requests, please use the message field to let us know.
* If you are paying by credit card, please fill out the credit card details accurately.
* Please note that some fields may be optional, but it is helpful to provide as much information as possible to ensure we can meet your needs during your stay.
