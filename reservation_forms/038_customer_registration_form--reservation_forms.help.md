# Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register customer information for reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. For your reservation, select a date and time in the "Customer Reservation Date" and "Customer Reservation Time" fields.
5. Add any additional notes about your reservation in the "Customer Note" field.
6. If applicable, select the assigned tool and reservation status from the respective dropdown menus.
7. Optionally, add any comments about your reservation in the "Customer Reservation Comment" field.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Customer Reservation Date**: Select the date of your reservation.
* **Customer Reservation Time**: Select the time of your reservation.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional notes about your reservation.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, select the tool that you have been assigned to use for your reservation.
* **Customer Reservation Note**: Add any comments or observations about your reservation.
* **Customer Reservation Comment**: Add any additional comments about your reservation.
* **Customer Reservation Status**: Select the current status of your reservation from the options provided.
* **Customer Reservation Category**: Enter a category for your reservation (e.g., equipment, vehicle, etc.).
* **Customer Reservation Form ID**: Enter the ID of the reservation form you are submitting.
