# Family Friendly Villa Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users booking a family-friendly villa. It will be used to gather details about the user and their preferences to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay at the villa.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- First, fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email. These are required fields that will help us verify your identity and contact information.
- Next, enter your Contact Number. This will help us get in touch with you if needed.
- Now, enter the Number Of Guests in your party. This will help us ensure we accommodate your group size.
- Please select your Arrival Date from the calendar.
- In the "What are your goals?" field, tell us a little about the purpose of your stay. This will help us tailor our service to your needs.
- Enter the Length Of Stay for your booking. This will help us prepare for your stay.
- Select your Preferred Language from the options provided.
- Choose the type of accommodation that suits your needs. You can select multiple options if applicable.
- Enter your Preferred Time Of Arrival. This is the time you would like to arrive at the accommodation.
- If you have any Special Requests, please let us know. This is optional, but it will help us make your stay more enjoyable.
- Enter the Accommodation Address, if you have already selected a specific accommodation type. This is optional but recommended.
- Finally, select a Reservation Date that suits your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, text, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Number Of Guests** (Number Of Guests, number, required): Please enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Arrival Date** (Arrival Date, date, required): Select the date you would like to arrive at the accommodation.
* **What are your goals?** (What are your goals?, text, required): Briefly tell us about the purpose of your stay.
* **Length Of Stay** (Length Of Stay, number, required): Enter the number of days you plan to stay at the accommodation.
* **Preferred Language** (Preferred Language, select_one, required): Choose your preferred language to ensure effective communication.
* **What type of accommodation** (What type of accommodation, select_multiple, required): Select the type of accommodation that suits your needs, e.g., 1 Bedroom, 2 Bedrooms, 3 Bedrooms.
* **Preferred Time Of Arrival** (Preferred Time Of Arrival, time, required): Enter the time you would like to arrive at the accommodation.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, optional): If you have any special requests, please let us know so we can make your stay more enjoyable.
* **Accommodation Address** (Accommodation Address, note, optional): Enter the address of your selected accommodation, if applicable.
* **Reservation Date** (Reservation Date, date, required): Select the date you would like to make the reservation.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): You can add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
