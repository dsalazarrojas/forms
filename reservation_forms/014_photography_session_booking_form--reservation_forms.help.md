# Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a photography session with us. Please complete it to provide all necessary information for your session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Double-check that you have selected the correct options for each field.
4. Review the terms and conditions carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you regarding your session.
* **Number of People Included** (`number_of_people_included`, number, required): Specify how many people will be attending the session.
* **Select Photography Session Date** (`select_photography_session_date`, select_one, required): Choose the date for your session. Please select 'Yes' if the session date is desired.
* **Select Photography Session Time** (`select_photography_session_time`, select_one, required): Choose the time for your session. Please select 'Yes' if the session time is desired.
* **Select Location** (`select_location`, select_one, required): Choose the location for your session. Please select 'Yes' if the location is desired.
* **Session Type** (`select_photography_session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of photography session you want (e.g. portrait, event, etc.).
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your session needs (e.g. props, outfits, etc.).
* **Deposit Payment** (`deposit_payment`, text, optional): Provide your deposit payment information.
* **Contact Agreement** (`contact_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Check 'Yes' to agree to our contact methods (email, phone, etc.).
* **Copyright Agreement** (`copyright_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Check 'Yes' to agree to our copyright and usage policies.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Provide your payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to pay for your session (e.g. credit card, etc.).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount for your session.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, text, required): Check 'Paid' to indicate payment has been made.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of payment.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional session notes or requests.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Session Address** (`session_address`, text, required): Enter the session address.
* **Payment Address** (`payment_address`, text, required): Enter the payment address.
* **Session Notes 2** (`session_notes_2`, text, optional): Provide any additional session notes or requests.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms 2** (`payment_terms_2`, text, required): Provide any additional payment terms and conditions.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.

Please review this information carefully and submit the form when you are ready.
