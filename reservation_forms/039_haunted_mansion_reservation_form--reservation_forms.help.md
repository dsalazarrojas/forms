# haunted_mansion_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Haunted Mansion Reservation Form is a web-based form used to book a spot at the Haunted Mansion, a popular tourist destination. This form collects visitor information to ensure a smooth experience for all guests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the 'Submit' button to start filling in the form.
2. Select a reservation date in the 'Reservation Date' field, if you wish to book a specific date. 
3. Enter the number of guests in the 'Number of Guests' field.
4. Provide your contact email in the 'Contact Email' field.
5. Enter your name in the 'Name' field.
6. Enter your phone number in the 'Phone Number' field.
7. Click the 'Submit' button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred reservation date in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help the staff plan your visit accordingly.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the total number of people in your group, including children. This will help us prepare for your visit.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your reservation and any last-minute updates.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded for our records.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number where we can reach you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill in all the required fields accurately to avoid any inconveniences during your visit.
- If you need assistance or have any questions, contact us at the Haunted Mansion's contact email address.
- Please note that the Haunted Mansion staff may contact you to confirm your reservation and provide further instructions.
