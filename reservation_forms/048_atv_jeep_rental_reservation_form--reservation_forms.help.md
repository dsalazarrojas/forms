# ATV Jeep Rental Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to reserve an ATV Jeep rental. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your reservation is processed correctly and that you have a smooth rental experience.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the customer information section, which includes your full name, date of birth, driver's license number, email address, phone number, and emergency contact details.
2. Select the desired rental dates and duration for your reservation.
3. Choose the type of vehicle you require, including the number of vehicles needed.
4. Indicate your experience level with off-road vehicles and select any additional equipment you need.
5. If necessary, specify any special requests or notes for your rental.
6. Choose the level of insurance coverage you require.
7. Select your preferred payment method.
8. Finally, confirm that you agree to the rental terms and safety guidelines.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your ID.
  * Required field.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
  * Required field.
- **Driver License Number**: Enter your valid driver license number.
  * Required field.
- **Email Address**: Enter your current email address for reservation confirmation.
  * Required field.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
  * Required field.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
  * Required field.
- **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  * Required field.
- **Rental Dates**: Select the dates for your rental period.
  * Required field.
- **Rental Duration**: Enter the number of days you need the vehicle.
  * Required field.
- **Vehicle Type Requested**: Choose the type of vehicle you wish to rent.
  * Required field.
- **Number of Vehicles**: Choose how many vehicles you need.
  * Required field.
- **Experience Level**: Indicate your level of experience with off-road vehicles.
  * Required field.
- **Additional Equipment Needed**: Select any additional equipment you require, such as helmets or GPS devices.
  * Optional field.
- **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any special requirements or notes for your rental.
  * Optional field.
- **Insurance Coverage**: Choose the level of insurance coverage you require.
  * Required field.
- **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
  * Required field.
- **Terms and Conditions Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to the rental terms and safety guidelines.
  * Required field.
