# Fishing Permit Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable fishermen to reserve a fishing permit for a specific date, time, and location. This form collects information such as name, contact details, permit type, and reservation status, allowing the authorities to process the request and ensure that the permit is issued correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
	* Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
	* Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
	* Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
2. Optionally, you can also provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
3. Enter the date you wish to go fishing in the **Reservation Date** field.
4. Select the time you want to fish in the **Reservation Time** field.
5. Specify the type of permit you are interested in (e.g., recreational, commercial, etc.) in the **Permit Type** field.
6. Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you in the **Number of Guests** field.
7. Enter the start and end dates of your permit period in the **Permit Start Date** and **Permit End Date** fields.
8. Select the current reservation status of your permit request in the **Reservation Status** field.
9. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can add them in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to go fishing.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required): Enter the time you want to fish.
* **Permit Type** (`permit_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of permit you are interested in (e.g., recreational, commercial, etc.).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you.
* **Permit Start Date** (`permit_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your permit period.
* **Permit End Date** (`permit_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your permit period.
* **Reservation Status** (`reservation_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your permit request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Fishing Location** (`fishing_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you plan to fish.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you have.
