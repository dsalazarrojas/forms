# Flight Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for flight reservations, which will be used to book and manage flight schedules. To fill in the form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is not a required field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This is not a required field.
5. Select your **Departure Date** by choosing a date from the calendar or entering it manually. This is not a required field.
6. Select your **Return Date** by choosing a date from the calendar or entering it manually. This is not a required field.
7. Select your **Departure Time** by choosing a time from the clock or entering it manually. This is not a required field.
8. Enter your **Flight Number** in the "Flight Number" field. This is not a required field.
9. Enter your **Seat Number** in the "Seat Number" field. This is not a required field.
10. Select your **Flight Class** by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the options. This is not a required field.
11. Enter your **Seat Type** in the "Seat Type" field. This is not a required field.
12. Select your **Reservation Date** by choosing a date from the calendar or entering it manually. This is not a required field.
13. Select your **Reservation Time** by choosing a time from the clock or entering it manually. This is not a required field.
14. Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field. This is not a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (email, not required): Enter a valid email address associated with your reservation.
* **Phone** (text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Departure Date** (date, not required): Select or enter the date you will depart.
* **Return Date** (date, not required): Select or enter the date you will return.
* **Departure Time** (time, not required): Select or enter the time of departure.
* **Flight Number** (text, not required): Enter the flight number associated with your reservation.
* **Seat Number** (text, not required): Enter the seat number you have reserved.
* **Flight Class** (select_one, not required): Choose between "Yes" and "No" for your flight class.
* **Seat Type** (text, not required): Enter the type of seat you have reserved.
* **Reservation Date** (date, not required): Select or enter the date of the reservation.
* **Reservation Time** (time, not required): Select or enter the time of the reservation.
* **Customer Name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the customer associated with the reservation.
