# Swim Lane Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swim Lane Reservation Form is designed for customers who wish to reserve a swim lane at your swim facility. The form is divided into two main pages: "Swim Lane Reservation" and "Reservation Details". Completing this form will allow you to reserve a swim lane and provide any necessary information for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose "Yes" on the first page if you want to reserve a swim lane.
2. On the second page, select "Yes" if you have any swim lane availability issues.
3. Choose the time slot you prefer for the reservation.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Swim Lane Reservation** (`Yes/No`, required: false): Please select "Yes" if you would like to reserve a swim lane.
- **Reservation Details** (`Yes/No`, required: false): If you have any issues with swim lane availability, please select "Yes" here.
- **Swim Lane Availability** (`date`, required: false): Please enter the date for your swim lane reservation.
- **Select Time** (`time`, required: false): Select the time slot you prefer for your reservation.
- **Number of People** (`number`, required: false): Enter the number of people accompanying you for your swim lane reservation.
- **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with reservation confirmation and updates.
- **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with reservation confirmation and updates.
- **Swim Lane Availability Note** (`note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes about your swim lane reservation.

## Tips
- Please make sure to complete all fields that are marked as required.
- If you have any issues with swim lane availability, please select "Yes" on the second page and provide additional information in the "Swim Lane Availability Note" field.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with your reservation, do not hesitate to contact us.
