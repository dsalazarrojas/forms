# Mitigation Bank Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for reserving a spot in a mitigation bank, which is a protected area or land used to offset environmental impact from a specific development or activity. The form will help you reserve a spot in the mitigation bank and provide essential details about your reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Scroll through each page of the form to understand what information is required for each section.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3.  Review the form to ensure that you have filled in all the necessary information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`): This field is used to provide general information about your reservation, such as the date, time, or other relevant details.
* **Credit Details** (`credit_details`): Please enter any available credit information, such as your credit card details or other relevant financial information.
* **Reservation Details** (`reserve_details`): This field is used for additional details about your reservation, such as the size of the area you want to reserve or other relevant information.
* **Review Details** (`review_details`): Enter any relevant comments or notes about your reservation, such as any issues you have or questions you'd like to ask.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Enter any other information that you feel is relevant to your reservation, such as any specific requests or concerns.
