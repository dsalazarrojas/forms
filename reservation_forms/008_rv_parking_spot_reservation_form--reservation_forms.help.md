# RV Parking Spot Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RV Parking Spot Reservation Form is designed for users to reserve a parking spot for their RVs or other vehicles. The form gathers necessary information for efficient parking spot allocation and communication with the user.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the RV Parking Spot Reservation Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the desired **Reservation Date** for your parking spot reservation.
2. Select the **Start Time** you would like to arrive at the parking spot.
3. Choose the type of RV you have from the **RV Type** dropdown menu (Motorhome, Trailer, Caravan, Tent, or Other).
4. Provide the number of **Guest Count** for your vehicle.
5. Select the method of contact you would like to use for communication (Phone, Email, Text, or Chat).
6. Enter your **Contact Phone Number** (if selected as a contact method).
7. Enter your **Contact Email** (if selected as a contact method).
8. Choose the payment method for your reservation (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
9. If you have selected "Other" as a payment method, provide more information in the **Other Payment Method** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date**: Enter the date you would like to make a reservation.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time for your arrival at the parking spot.
* **RV Type**: Choose the type of RV you have (Motorhome, Trailer, Caravan, Tent, or Other).
* **Guest Count**: Enter the number of guests accompanying you.
* **Contact Method**: Select the method you would like to use for communication (Phone, Email, Text, or Chat).
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you selected Phone as a contact method.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address if you selected Email as a contact method.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method for your reservation (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Other Payment Method**: If you selected "Other" as a payment method, provide more information about the alternative payment method.
