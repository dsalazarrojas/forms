# Housing Cart Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Cart Reservation Form is used to record and manage reservations for housing. It's essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that the reservation system is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the reservation system to ensure that the housing unit is currently available.
2. Fill out the form with the relevant information, making sure to provide accurate details.
3. Save your changes to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **reservation_form** (text, required/optional): This field is where you'll type out a brief description of the reservation request. It's where you can provide any necessary details or context.
* **name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization making the reservation.
* **phone** (number, optional): Add the phone number of the person or organization making the reservation.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the reservation. This will help in scheduling and keeping track of the reservation.
* **equipment id** (select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if the equipment is available, and 'No' if it is not.
* **equipment** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you need to use the equipment, and 'No' if you don't.
* **description** (text, optional): Provide any additional details about the reservation, such as special requests or requirements.
* **notes** (note, optional): Add any notes or comments about the reservation.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Double-check the reservation system before completing the form.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact the relevant staff member or department.
