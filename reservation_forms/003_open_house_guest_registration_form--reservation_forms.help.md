# Open House Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open House Guest Registration Form is designed to collect contact information and event-specific data from guests interested in attending an open house event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select the type of event you are interested in from the "Event Type" field.
6. If you are an agent, fill in your name and phone number in the "Agent Name" and "Agent Phone" fields.
7. Add any additional event details in the "Event Details" field.
8. Enter the address of the property you are interested in in the "Property Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Phone** (Text): Provide your phone number.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Event Type** (Select One): Select the type of event you are interested in (open house, property viewing, or inspection).
* **Agent Name** (Text): If you are an agent, enter your name.
* **Agent Phone** (Text): If you are an agent, enter your phone number.
* **Event Details** (Text): Add any additional event details or notes.
* **Property Address** (Text): Enter the address of the property you are interested in.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for informational purposes only and does not imply any commitment or obligation.
* By filling out this form, you are registering your interest in the event.
