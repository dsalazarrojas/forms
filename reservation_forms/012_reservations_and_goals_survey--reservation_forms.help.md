# reservations_and_goals_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "reservations_and_goals_survey" form is designed to collect information about your reservations and goals. This data will help us understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to provide you with more accurate and efficient services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select the relevant options for each field. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as "Required".
3. If you have any additional comments or messages, you can use the "message_to_host" field.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **reservation_type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the type of reservation you are interested in (Hotel, Restaurant, or Event).
* **reservation_date** (Date, Optional): Select the date of your reservation. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **start_time** (Time, Optional): Choose the start time of your reservation. Please use the 24-hour format "HH:MM".
* **end_time** (Time, Optional): Choose the end time of your reservation. Please use the 24-hour format "HH:MM".
* **number_of_guests** (Number, Optional): Specify the number of guests attending your reservation.
* **message_to_host** (Text, Optional): If you have any special requests or comments, please include them here.
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* You can only submit the form once per session. If you need to make multiple reservations, please contact us separately.
