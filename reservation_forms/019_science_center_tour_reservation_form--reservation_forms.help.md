# science_center_tour_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from schools and other groups for a guided or self-guided tour of the science center.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "School Name" field with the name of the school or group visiting the science center.
2. Enter the "Contact Person" responsible for making the reservation.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. If necessary, provide a phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose the "Date Of Visit" and "Time Of Visit" for the tour.
6. Select the "Number Of Students" and specify their "Age Range".
7. Choose the type of tour (Guided or Self-guided) and select any additional tour options (such as educational or recreational).
8. Specify any "Special Needs" or "Language" requirements.
9. Add any additional "Comments" or notes about the visit.
10. Select the "School District" the visit is from.
11. Optionally, provide the "School Address", "Student Names", and "Student Contact Info" for further reference.
12. Confirm the visit by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, required): Enter the name of the school or group visiting the science center.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required): Enter the person responsible for making the reservation.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): If necessary, enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, optional): Choose the date of the tour.
* **Time Of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, optional): Choose the time of the tour.
* **Number Of Students** (`number_of_students`, optional): Enter the number of students attending the tour.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, optional): Select the age range of the students.
* **Tour Type** (`tour_type`, optional): Choose between Guided or Self-guided tour.
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, optional): Select the date of the tour.
* **Tour Time** (`tour_time`, optional): Choose the time of the tour.
* **Tour Duration** (`tour_duration`, optional): Enter the duration of the tour.
* **Number Of Adults** (`number_of_adults`, optional): Enter the number of adults attending the tour.
* **Number Of Children** (`number_of_children`, optional): Enter the number of children attending the tour.
* **Group Type** (`group_type`, optional): Choose between Educational, Recreational, or Event tour.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, optional): If necessary, mark any special needs of the group.
* **Language** (`language`, optional): Choose the language spoken by the group.
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the visit.
* **School District** (`school_district`, optional): Select the school district the visit is from.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, optional): Enter the address of the school or group.
* **Student Names** (`student_names`, optional): Enter the names of the students attending the tour.
* **Student Contact Info** (`student_contact_info`, optional): Enter the contact information of the students.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, optional): Confirm the visit by selecting "True" or "False".
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the school or group.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, optional): Enter a valid email address of the school or group.
* **School Contact** (`school_contact`, optional): Enter the contact person of the school or group.
* **Field 29** (`field_29`, optional): This field is not used in the form and can be ignored.
