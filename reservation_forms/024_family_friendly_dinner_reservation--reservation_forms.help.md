# Family Friendly Dinner Reservation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Friendly Dinner Reservation form is designed to collect information from users for a family-friendly dinner reservation. This form allows users to provide their preferences for dinner time, guest count, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields below to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the required fields with your details, starting with the "Dinner Time" field on page 1.
3. If you have any special requests, type them into the "Special Requests" field.
4. Select "Yes" if you are a family member, or "No" if you are not.
5. Enter any additional comments or messages in the "Message or Comments" field.
6. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dinner Time** (`reservation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to have dinner.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to have dinner.
* **Dinner Time & Date** (`reservation_date_time`, date, optional): Enter the time and date you would like to have dinner.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) of your guest(s).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, optional): Type any special requests or comments.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Message or Comments** (`message`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or messages.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, optional): Type any additional special requests.
* **Family Member** (`family_member`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are a family member, or "No" if you are not.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate submission.
* Use the "Special Requests" field to convey any special requests or comments.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field label for clarification.
