# Playdate Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by parents to schedule a playdate for their children at a play center. It is a straightforward form that requires minimal information but is essential for booking a specific time slot.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the required information to book a playdate for your child at the play center.
- Select the day of the week you would like to book the playdate for your child.
- Enter your name and email address so that we can communicate with you.
- Provide your phone number so that we can contact you in case of any issues.
- If your child has any allergies, please list them in the "Allergies" field.
- Indicate whether you give consent for your child to participate in activities at the play center.
- Enter the name of the play center where you would like to book the playdate.
- Select a date and time for the playdate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Playdate** (`schedule_playdate`, select_one, required): Select the day of the week you would like to book the playdate for your child.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Parent Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can communicate with you.
* **Parent Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, note, optional): If your child has any allergies, please list them here. For example, "dairy, peanut, gluten-free".
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you give consent for your child to participate in activities at the play center. Choose "Yes" or "No".
* **Play Center Name** (`playcenter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the play center where you would like to book the playdate.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to book the playdate. Please use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required): Select the time you would like to book the playdate. Please use the format HH:MM.

Note: The "Reservation Date" and "Reservation Time" fields should be filled out in the correct format to ensure that your playdate is booked correctly.
