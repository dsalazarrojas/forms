# Hbcdjs Packages Enquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for an Hbcdjs Packages Enquiry. You can use it to send a message or request about packages or services related to Hbcdjs.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2.  If you have a phone number, provide it in the Phone field. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
3.  Enter your Email address in the Email field. This is a required field.
4.  Select the Date you are inquiring about.
5.  Choose the Time you are inquiring about.
6.  If you have a message or comment, provide it in the Message field. This is an optional field, so you can leave it blank if you don't have anything to say.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name in this field.
*   **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name in this field.
*   **Phone (text)**: If you have a phone number, enter it here. This field is optional.
*   **Email (email)**: Enter your email address in this field. This is a required field.
*   **Date (date)**: Select the date you are inquiring about.
*   **Time (time)**: Choose the time you are inquiring about.
*   **Message (note)**: If you have any comments or messages, enter them here. This field is optional.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, Date, and Time).
*   Enter accurate information, especially your email address, to ensure that you receive a response to your enquiry.
*   Keep your message brief and clear if you have anything to add in the Message field.
*   If you need any further assistance or clarification, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
