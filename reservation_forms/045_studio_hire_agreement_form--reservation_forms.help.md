# Studio Hire Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create and manage agreements between studios and their clients or partners. It collects essential information about the studio, contact details, contract terms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the studio information in the **Studio Information** section.
2. Provide your contact details in the **Contact Information** section. (Required)
3. Enter the contract details in the **Contract Details** section. (Required)
4. Review and acknowledge the terms in the **Review and Acknowledgement** section. (Required)
5. Add any other details you need in the **Other Details** section.
6. Review the terms and conditions in the **Terms and Conditions** section.
7. Leave any additional comments or notes in the **Review Section 2** section.
8. Sign and date the agreement in the **Signature and Date** section. (Required)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Studio Information (id: 1)**: Enter your studio's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (id: 2)**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and other contact details.
* **Contract Details (id: 3)**: Describe the terms of the agreement, including the scope of work, payment terms, and any other important details.
* **Review and Acknowledgement (id: 4)**: Review the agreement carefully and acknowledge that you understand and accept the terms.
* **Other Details (id: 5)**: Add any additional information you need to share with the other party.
* **Review Section 2 (id: 6)**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions (id: 7)**: Review the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Review Section 3 (id: 8)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the agreement.
* **Signature and Date (id: 9)**: Sign and date the agreement, indicating your acceptance of the terms.
* **Other (id: 10)**: Add any additional information you need.
* **Terms (id: 11)**: Enter the terms of the agreement, if not already included in the contract details.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the agreement carefully before signing.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid delays or issues.
* Save a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
