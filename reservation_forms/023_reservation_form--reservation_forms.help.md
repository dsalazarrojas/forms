# Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a reservation. Please ensure you have a legitimate reason for making a reservation before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field for its purpose and required status.
3. Submit the form once all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Form (1, text, required: false)**: Please enter the name of the reservation or a brief description of your stay. This field is not required, but it helps the reservation team with identifying and organizing your reservation.
* **Guest Name (2, text, required: false)**: Enter the full name of the guest(s) making the reservation. This field is optional but helps us to address the guest(s) by their name.
* **Arrival Date (3, date, required: false)**: Enter the date of arrival in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Departure Date (4, date, required: false)**: Enter the date of departure in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Room Type (5, select_one, required: false)**: Choose the type of room you require for your stay from the options provided. Please select 'Yes' for an available room type or 'No' if you are unsure or do not require a specific room type.
* **Number of Guests (6, number, required: false)**: Enter the number of guests expected to be staying in the room.
* **Phone (7, text, required: false)**: Enter your contact phone number. This field is optional but helps us to contact you with any updates or concerns.
* **E-mail (8, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address. This field is optional but helps us to reach you with any updates or concerns via email.
* **Note (9, note, required: false)**: Enter any special requests or requirements for your reservation, such as a preferred room location or additional services needed.
* **Reservation Date (10, date, required: false)**: Enter the date of the reservation in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY). Please note that this field is not to be confused with the arrival date.
* **Arrival Time (11, time, required: false)**: Enter the time of arrival in the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM). Please note that this field is not required but helps us to plan for your check-in.
* **Departure Time (12, time, required: false)**: Enter the time of departure in the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM). Please note that this field is not required but helps us to plan for your check-out.
* **Message (13, text, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or concerns about your reservation. This field is optional but helps us to better understand your needs.
