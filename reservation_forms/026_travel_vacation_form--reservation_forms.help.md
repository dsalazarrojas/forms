# travel_vacation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_vacation_form is used to collect information from customers and travel agents to help plan and book travel vacations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Complete the **Booking Details** section with the relevant information for your travel reservation.
2.  In the **Travel Dates** section, enter your travel dates (start and end dates for your trip).
3.  In the **Travel Type** section, select the type of travel you are interested in (Domestic, International, or Both).
4.  In the **Travel Agent** section, select the travel agent that will assist you with your travel booking (Travel Agent 1, Travel Agent 2, or Travel Agent 3).
5.  Enter your travel agent's email address in the **Email** field.
6.  Enter your travel agent's phone number in the **Phone** field.
7.  In the **Notes** section, add any additional notes or comments about your travel preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant booking details such as the type of accommodation or activities you are interested in.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, `text`, required: false): Enter your travel start and end dates to help us provide you with accurate travel recommendations.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of travel you are interested in (Domestic, International, or Both).
*   *Note: You can select multiple options by holding down Ctrl (PC) or Command (Mac) while selecting.*
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the travel agent that will assist you with your travel booking (Travel Agent 1, Travel Agent 2, or Travel Agent 3).
* **Email** (`travel_agent_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your travel agent's email address.
* **Phone** (`travel_agent_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your travel agent's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your travel preferences.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible travel recommendations.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our support team.
*   Please review your submission carefully before submitting it to us.
