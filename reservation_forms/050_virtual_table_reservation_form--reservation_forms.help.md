# virtual_table_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for virtual table reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all required and optional fields.
2. Provide accurate information for each field as it relates to your virtual table reservation.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your reservation.
* **Table Number** (`table_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of the table you are reserving.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your reservation.
* **Reservation Note** (`reservation_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your reservation.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the guest(s) in your party.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the guest(s) in your party.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the reservation is assigned to a staff member or not.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the reservation is assigned a tool or not.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of your reservation (Active or Inactive).
