# Dog Daycare Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dog Daycare Reservation Form" is designed to help us prepare for your pet's visit to our dog daycare. This form gathers essential information about your pet and your reservation, ensuring our staff is well-prepared to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Select a reservation date for your pet's visit.
* Choose the pickup time that suits you best.
* Indicate the type of pet you are bringing (e.g., Dog, Cat, or Other).
* Provide your pet's name.
* Enter your contact details, including the contact person, phone number, and email address.
* Optionally, update the reservation status (e.g., Active, Cancelled, Pending, or Reserved).
* Add any additional reservation notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date**: Choose a date for your pet's visit to our dog daycare.
* **Pickup Time**: Select a pickup time that suits you best to collect your pet after their visit.
* **Pet Type**: Indicate the type of pet you are bringing (e.g., Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name to help our staff get to know your furry friend.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name of the person to contact in case of any issues during your pet's visit.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for any communication or updates.
* **Reservation Status**: Optionally update the reservation status (e.g., Active, Cancelled, Pending, or Reserved).
* **Reservation Notes**: Add any additional notes about your pet's visit, such as medical conditions or special requirements.
