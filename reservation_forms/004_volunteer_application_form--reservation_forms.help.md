# Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Application Form is used to collect information from volunteers who are interested in joining your organization's volunteer efforts. This form helps us understand your availability, interests, and any specific skills you may possess that could be beneficial to our cause. By submitting this form, you'll be considered for volunteer opportunities that match your skills and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Provide your Email address in the third field. This will be used for communication purposes only.
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth field.
5. Select a Date that you are available to volunteer on in the fifth field.
6. Choose a Time that you are available to volunteer on in the sixth field.
7. If you have any additional information or notes about your volunteer availability or experience, please type it in the seventh field.
8. Select a Category that best describes your volunteer interests or availability from the eighth field.
9. If you have any additional information that you'd like to share with us, please type it in the ninth and final field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`volunteer_application_form_1`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`volunteer_application_form_2`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`volunteer_application_form_3`, `email`, required): This is the email address we will use to contact you regarding your volunteer opportunities.
* **Phone** (`volunteer_application_form_4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date** (`volunteer_application_form_5`, `date`, required): Select a date that you are available to volunteer on.
* **Time** (`volunteer_application_form_6`, `time`, required): Choose a time that you are available to volunteer on the selected date.
* **Notes** (`volunteer_application_form_7`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information or notes about your volunteer availability or experience, please type it in here.
* **Category** (`volunteer_application_form_8`, `select_one`, required): Select a category that best describes your volunteer interests or availability.
* **Additional Information** (`volunteer_application_form_9`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information that you'd like to share with us, please type it in this field.

Please note that this form is required to be completed for all volunteer applicants. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
