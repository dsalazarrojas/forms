# Helicopter Tour Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Helicopter Tour Reservation Form is used to gather information for helicopter tour reservations. This form is designed to collect contact details and tour specifics to facilitate the reservation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that the contact information is correct and complete.
3. Choose the desired start and end dates for the tour.
4. Select the start and end times for the tour.
5. Enter the reservation ID (if provided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Start Date**: Choose the date you would like to start the tour.
* **Start Time**: Choose the time you would like to start the tour.
* **End Date**: Choose the date you would like to end the tour.
* **End Time**: Choose the time you would like to end the tour.
* **Reservation ID**: Enter your reservation ID if provided.

## Tips
- Double-check that all fields are filled in accurately before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have entered your contact information correctly.
- If you are unsure of any of the fields, please contact the tour operator for assistance.
