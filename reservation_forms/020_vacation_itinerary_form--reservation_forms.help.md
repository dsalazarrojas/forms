# Vacation Itinerary Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vacation Itinerary Form is a tool used to collect information about a travel reservation. This form is used to gather details about travel dates, destinations, and other relevant trip information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Travel Dates" field and select the start and end dates of your trip.
2. In the "Departure City" and "Arrival City" fields, enter the names of the cities you will be traveling from and to.
3. In the "Travel Duration" field, enter the number of days you will be traveling.
4. Select the travel modes you will be using from the "Travel Mode" field, such as 'Yes' and 'No' for flight or other modes.
5. Choose the travel options from the "Travel Mode Options" field. This field is used to select specific options for the travel modes you selected.
6. If you are booking a travel package with an agency, select the "Travel Agency" options to choose the agency you will be using.
7. Finally, enter the name of the client (e.g. the person booking the travel) in the "Client Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Dates**: A date picker field to select the start and end dates of your trip.
* **Departure City**: A text field to enter the city where you will be departing from.
* **Arrival City**: A text field to enter the city where you will be arriving at.
* **Travel Duration**: A numeric field to enter the number of days you will be traveling.
* **Travel Mode**: A multiple selection field to choose the modes of travel you will be using (e.g. 'Yes' and 'No' for flight).
* **Travel Mode Options**: A single selection field to select specific options for the travel modes you selected.
* **Travel Agency**: A multiple selection field to select the travel agency you will be using.
* **Client Name**: A text field to enter the name of the client booking the travel.
