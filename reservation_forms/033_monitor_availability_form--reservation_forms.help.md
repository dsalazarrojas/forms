# Monitor Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monitor Availability Form is used to indicate your availability to monitor a studio during a certain time period. It is crucial that you fill out this form accurately in order to ensure that there is adequate staffing coverage for your shift.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select your studio status for the time period:
	* Choose "Yes" if you are available to monitor the studio
	* Choose "No" if you are not available to monitor the studio
2.  Select your monitor type:
	* Choose "Yes" for each type of monitoring that you are available for
	* Choose "No" for each type of monitoring that you are not available for
3.  Select your availability for each day of the week:
	* Choose "Yes" for each day you are available to monitor the studio
	* Choose "No" for each day you are not available to monitor the studio
4.  Select your availability for each hour of the day:
	* Choose "Yes" for each hour you are available to monitor the studio
	* Choose "No" for each hour you are not available to monitor the studio
5.  Enter your start and end times:
	* Enter the time you start monitoring the studio
	* Enter the time you end monitoring the studio
6.  Enter your duration:
	* Enter the number of hours you are available to monitor the studio
7.  Select your frequency:
	* Choose "Yes" if you are available to monitor the studio on a regular basis
	* Choose "No" if you are not available to monitor the studio on a regular basis
8.  Enter a note:
	* Enter any additional information about your availability
9.  Enter your email address:
	* Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted for work scheduling
10. Enter your phone number:
	* Enter your phone number to ensure you can be contacted for work scheduling

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **required_studio**: Select whether you are available to monitor the studio for this period
	+ If you choose "Yes", you will be considered available to monitor the studio
	+ If you choose "No", you will not be considered available to monitor the studio
*  **monitor_type**: Select which types of monitoring you are available for
	+ Choose "Yes" for each type of monitoring you are available for
	+ Choose "No" for each type of monitoring you are not available for
*  **day_of_week**: Select which days of the week you are available to monitor the studio
	+ Choose "Yes" for each day you are available to monitor the studio
	+ Choose "No" for each day you are not available to monitor the studio
*  **hours_of_day**: Select which hours of the day you are available to monitor the studio
	+ Choose "Yes" for each hour you are available to monitor the studio
	+ Choose "No" for each hour you are not available to monitor the studio
*  **start_time**: Enter the time you start monitoring the studio
*  **end_time**: Enter the time you end monitoring the studio
*  **duration**: Enter the number of hours you are available to monitor the studio
*  **frequency**: Select whether you are available to monitor the studio on a regular basis
	+ Choose "Yes" if you are available to monitor the studio on a regular basis
	+ Choose "No" if you are not available to monitor the studio on a regular basis
*  **note**: Enter any additional information about your availability
*  **email**: Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted for work scheduling
*  **phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure you can be contacted for work scheduling
