# Yurt Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a Yurt at the campsite. It collects information from the guest, including their name, email, phone number, and payment method, as well as dates for the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To reserve a Yurt, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, making sure to provide your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
3. Choose your reservation start and end dates.
4. Enter your guest arrival and departure dates.
5. Add any additional information you would like to provide about your stay.
6. Review your reservation and confirm or cancel it as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: A text field where you can enter any additional information about your stay.
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it should be on the reservation.
* **Guest Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you about your reservation.
* **Guest Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you about your reservation.
* **Yurt Name**: Enter the name of the Yurt you are interested in reserving.
* **Reservation Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your reservation.
* **Reservation End Date**: Enter the date you would like to end your reservation.
* **Guest Arrival Date**: Enter the date you plan to arrive at the campsite.
* **Guest Departure Date**: Enter the date you plan to depart from the campsite.
* **Payment Method**: Choose a payment method from the options provided (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your stay.
* **Confirm Reservation**: Check 'True' to confirm your reservation or 'False' to cancel it.
* **Cancel Reservation**: Check 'True' to cancel your reservation or 'False' to confirm it.
