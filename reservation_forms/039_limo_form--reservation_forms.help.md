## limo_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "limo_form" is used to create and manage limousine reservations. This form is required for all limousine bookings to ensure accurate information is captured for the service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the Pickup Location in the "Pickup Location" field.
2. Select the Start Time and End Time for the reservation.
3. Enter the Number of People and Number of Passengers.
4. Choose the Pickup Date and Pickup Time.
5. Select the End Date.
6. Choose the Vehicle Type from the available options.
7. Optionally, provide Special Instructions for the reservation.
8. Enter the Phone Number and Email for contact purposes.
9. Enter the Address where the pickup will take place.
10. Enter any additional Notes for the reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Location (Text)**: Enter the address or location where you would like to be picked up.
* **Start Time (Time)**: Enter the time you would like the limousine to arrive.
* **End Time (Time)**: Enter the time you would like the limousine to end the service.
* **Number of People (Number)**: Enter the number of people attending the event or trip.
* **Number of Passengers (Number)**: Enter the number of people that will be traveling in the limousine.
* **Pickup Date (Date)**: Enter the date you would like to be picked up on.
* **Pickup Time (Time)**: Enter the time you would like to be picked up.
* **End Date (Date)**: Enter the date the service will end.
* **Vehicle Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of vehicle you would like to use for the service. Options include Limo, Luxury Limo, Sedan, and SUV.
* **Special Instructions (Text)**: Optionally, enter any special requests or instructions for the service.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Address (Text)**: Enter the address where the pickup will take place.
* **Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional notes for the reservation.
