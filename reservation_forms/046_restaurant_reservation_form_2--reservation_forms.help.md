# Restaurant Reservation Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to make a personal reservation at a restaurant. Please fill out the form below to provide the necessary information for your reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following fields:
	* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for confirmation and updates.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted if needed.
	* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of people in your party.
	* **Date**: Select the date you would like to make your reservation.
	* **Time**: Select the time for your reservation.
	* **Prophet's Reservation Type**: Choose the type of seating you prefer.
	* **Special Requests**: Add any special requests you may have, such as dietary restrictions or special occasions.
	* **Reservation Date**: Select the date of your reservation (if different from the initial date).
	* **Reservation Time**: Select the time of your reservation (if different from the initial time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted if needed.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of people in your party.
* **Date**: Select the date you would like to make your reservation.
* **Time**: Select the time for your reservation.
* **Prophet's Reservation Type**: Choose the type of seating you prefer.
	+ Select "Prophet's" for a special seating area.
	+ Select "Table" for a standard table.
	+ Select "Booth" for a booth seating area.
* **Special Requests**: Add any special requests you may have, such as dietary restrictions or special occasions.
* **Reservation Date**: Select the date of your reservation (if different from the initial date).
* **Reservation Time**: Select the time of your reservation (if different from the initial time).
