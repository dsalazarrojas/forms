# Tennis Court Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tennis Court Reservation Form is a form used to reserve a tennis court for a specific date and time. This form is intended to collect information necessary for processing reservation requests, including tennis court selection, date and time of reservation, player information, and reservation status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired tennis court from the list of available options.
2. Enter the date and time of the reservation using the date and time fields.
3. Enter information for the players participating in the reservation (up to 4 players).
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the reservation.
5. Select the reservation status (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tennis Court** (`tennis_court`): Select the specific tennis court you wish to reserve.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`): Enter the date of the reservation using the date field. Format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`): Enter the time of the reservation using the time field. Format is HH:MM.
* **Tennis Player 1** (`tennis_player1`), **Tennis Player 2** (`tennis_player2`), **Tennis Player 3** (`tennis_player3`), **Tennis Player 4** (`tennis_player4`): Enter the names of the players participating in the reservation.
* **Reservation Notes** (`reservation_notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the reservation.
* **Reservation Status** (`reservation_status`): Select the status of the reservation (if applicable).

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out, and that the correct tennis court and reservation status are selected.
* If a player is not participating in the reservation, leave the corresponding field blank.
* Use the reservation notes field to add any additional context or information about the reservation.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
