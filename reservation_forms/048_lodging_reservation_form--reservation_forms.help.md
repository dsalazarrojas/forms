# Lodging Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book lodging for individuals visiting a police department. It captures personal and visit details for efficient communication and accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields with your personal details.
2. Provide your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
3. Select your **Date of Arrival** and **Date of Departure** for the lodging stay.
4. Choose the **Reason for Visit** to inform the department of your purpose.
5. If you are a police officer, provide your **Police ID** and **Police Force**.
6. If you are not a police officer, provide your **Unit** and **Department**.
7. Fill in the **Contact Number** of the person you are visiting.
8. If you are a police officer, fill in your **Officer Name** and **Officer Position**.
9. Provide your **Officer ID** for security purposes.
10. Specify the **Lodging Location** where you would like to be accommodated.
11. Select the **Accommodation Type** that suits your needs (Hotel, Resort, Inn, Motel, or Campsite).
12. Choose the **Room Type** for your lodging (Single, Double, Triple, Quad, or Suite).
13. Specify the number of **Nights** you plan to stay.
14. Enter the number of **Guests** accompanying you.
15. Make any **Special Requests** you may have.
16. Describe your **Special Requests Details**, if applicable.
17. Provide any **Comments**, if you wish to leave a message.
18. Finally, fill in the **Created By** and **Updated By** fields, which are automatically populated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Arrival**: Select the date you plan to arrive.
* **Date of Departure**: Select the date you plan to depart.
* **Reason for Visit**: Choose why you are visiting the police department.
* **Police ID**: Enter your police officer ID, if you are a police officer.
* **Police Force**: Enter the police force you belong to, if you are a police officer.
* **Unit**: Enter the unit you belong to, if you are not a police officer.
* **Department**: Enter the department you are visiting, if you are not a police officer.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person you are visiting.
* **Officer Name**: Enter your name, if you are a police officer.
* **Officer Position**: Enter your position, if you are a police officer.
* **Officer ID**: Enter your officer ID for security purposes.
* **Lodging Location**: Specify where you would like to be accommodated.
* **Accommodation Type**: Choose the accommodation type that suits your needs.
* **Room Type**: Choose the room type for your lodging.
* **Number of Nights**: Enter the number of nights you plan to stay.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests accompanying you.
* **Special Requests**: Make any special requests you may have.
* **Special Requests Details**: Describe your special requests, if applicable.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or messages.
* **Created By**: Automatically populated with your name, not editable.
* **Updated By**: Automatically populated with the person who made the last update, not editable.
