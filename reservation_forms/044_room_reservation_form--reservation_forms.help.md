# Room Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for room reservations at an establishment. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details such as the reservation date, start and end times, contact information, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired date for the room reservation from the calendar input field.
2. Choose the start and end times for the reservation, if applicable.
3. Enter your first name, last name, and email address for contact purposes.
4. Optionally, provide your phone number for further contact.
5. If necessary, add any additional comments to provide context or specific requests for the room reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Room Reservation Date** (`room_reservation_form_date`, date): Select the desired date for the room reservation.
- **Start Time** (`room_reservation_form_start_time`, time): Choose the start time for the reservation, if applicable.
- **End Time** (`room_reservation_form_end_time`, time): Choose the end time for the reservation, if applicable.
- **First Name** (`room_reservation_form_contact_first_name`, text): Enter your first name for contact purposes.
- **Last Name** (`room_reservation_form_last_name`, text): Enter your last name for contact purposes.
- **Email** (`room_reservation_form_email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`room_reservation_form_phone`, text): Optionally, provide your phone number for further contact.
- **Additional Comments** (`room_reservation_form_additional_comments`, text): If necessary, add any additional comments to provide context or specific requests for the room reservation.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the date and time selections for accuracy.
- Use a valid email address to ensure that you receive any correspondence related to your reservation.
- Provide clear and detailed comments if you have specific requests or needs for the room reservation.
