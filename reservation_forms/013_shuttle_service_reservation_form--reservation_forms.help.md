# shuttle_service_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers or employees to reserve a shuttle service, providing necessary information for transportation arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Ensure all relevant information is accurate and complete.
3. Review and edit your submission before finalizing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (user_info, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as name and email.
- **Trip Details** (trip_details, text, optional): Describe the trip specifics, including destination, travel dates, and any special instructions.
- **Reservation Details** (reservation_details, text, optional): Provide details about your reservation, including pickup and drop-off times and any other relevant information.
- **Payment Information** (payment_info, text, optional): If applicable, include any payment information, such as a payment method or invoice number.
- **Special Requests** (special_requests, text, optional): If you have any special requests, such as equipment or assistance needs, enter them here.
- **Submit** (submit, text, optional): Click this button to finalize your submission.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.
- Review and edit your submission before finalizing.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the shuttle service provider for support.
