# michelle_mckay_color_street_on_hand_inventory - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "michelle_mckay_color_street_on_hand_inventory" form is designed to help you collect information on your on-hand inventory of color streets. This form is intended to gather specific details about your current stock of colored streets, including their quantity, date, and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the "reservation_form" field. This field does not require a specific format, so enter your answer in a way that makes sense for you.
2. Move on to the "color_streets_on_hand_inventory" field. Enter the current number of color streets you have in stock.
3. In the "on_hand_inventory_date" field, select the date when you last counted your on-hand inventory of color streets.
4. Finally, in the "on_hand_inventory_time" field, select the time when you last counted your on-hand inventory of color streets.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **reservation_form**: This field is a free-form text input where you can enter any relevant information about your reservation.
    - Enter any relevant details about your reservation, if applicable.
- **color_streets_on_hand_inventory**: This field is used to record the number of color streets you have in stock.
    - Enter the current quantity of color streets in your inventory.
- **on_hand_inventory_date**: This field is a date input where you can select the date when you last counted your on-hand inventory of color streets.
    - Choose the date when you last updated your inventory count.
- **on_hand_inventory_time**: This field is a time input where you can select the time when you last counted your on-hand inventory of color streets.
    - Choose the time when you last updated your inventory count.
