# Laboratory Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Reservation Form is a tool for reserving laboratory sessions or resources. It is used to gather information about your request and ensure that the laboratory is available for you on the desired date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Reservation Form section with a brief description of your reservation request.
2. Select the department(s) you wish to access in the department field.
3. Choose the intended use of your laboratory reservation in the Intended Use field.
4. Select the date for your reservation in the Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Form** (`reservation_form`, text, optional): A brief description of your reservation request.
* **Department** (`department`, select multiple, optional): Select the department(s) you wish to access. This will help us ensure that the relevant personnel are aware of your request.
* **Intended Use** (`intended_use`, text, optional): A description of the purpose or project for which you are reserving the laboratory.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date for your reservation request. Please ensure that you select a date when the laboratory is available.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer back to this help guide or contact the relevant personnel.
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your reservation is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
