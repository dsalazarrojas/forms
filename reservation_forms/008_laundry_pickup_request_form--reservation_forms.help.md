# Laundry Pickup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laundry Pickup Request Form is a tool used to request laundry service from our laundry service providers. The form is designed to gather all the necessary information required to process your request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of service you require from the options provided.
2. Select the date and time you would like your laundry to be picked up.
3. Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you to confirm the details of your request.
4. Add any special instructions or notes regarding the collection and handling of your laundry.
5. Confirm the details of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wash Service Type**: Select the type of service you require, such as wash, dry, fold, etc.
	+ This field allows you to specify the type of service you need, such as wash, dry, fold, or other options.
* **Pick Up Date**: Select the date you would like your laundry to be picked up.
	+ Enter the date you would like your laundry to be collected, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Pick Up Time**: Select the time you would like your laundry to be picked up.
	+ Enter the time you would like your laundry to be collected, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person making the request.
	+ This field is used to identify the person making the request.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person making the request.
	+ This field is used for contact purposes only.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the person making the request.
	+ This field is used for contact purposes only.
* **Laundry Service Provider**: Enter the name of the laundry service provider.
	+ This field is used to specify the service provider you are requesting from.
* **Laundry Collection Address**: Enter the address where the laundry will be collected from.
	+ This field is used to specify the collection location.
* **Laundry Collection Instructions**: Add any special instructions or notes regarding the collection of your laundry.
	+ This field is used to provide any additional information about the collection process.
* **Laundry Type**: Enter the type of laundry you would like to be collected.
	+ This field is used to specify the type of laundry you are requesting.
* **Special Instructions**: Add any special instructions or notes regarding the handling of your laundry.
	+ This field is used to provide any additional information about the handling of your laundry.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
	+ This field is used to provide any additional information about your request.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that the details of your request are correct.
	+ This field is used to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed upon the details of your request.
