# Pottery Session Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pottery Session Reservation Form is designed to collect information for a pottery session. Fill out this form to book a session and provide additional details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Select the Start Date and End Date for your session.
6. Choose the Pottery Session you want to book.
7. Specify the number of students attending your session.
8. Add any Additional Info related to your session.
9. Enter the Submitter's information (First Name, Email, Phone).
10. Select the Created By and Updated By individuals.
11. Specify the Created At, Updated At, and Deleted At dates.
12. Select the Deleted By individual.
13. Choose the Notes for your session.
14. Enter the Submitter IP, User Agent, OS, Browser, and Device details.
15. Enter the Submitter Referrer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Start Date**: Select the start date for your session.
* **End Date**: Select the end date for your session.
* **Pottery Session**: Choose the pottery session you want to book from the dropdown list.
* **Student Count**: Enter the number of students attending your session.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any extra details about your session.
* **Submitter**: Enter the first name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Created By**: Select the person who created the form (John, Jane, or Jim).
* **Updated By**: Select the person who updated the form (John, Jane, or Jim).
* **Created At**: Enter the date and time when the form was created.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time when the form was updated.
* **Deleted At**: Enter the date and time when the form was deleted.
* **Deleted By**: Select the person who deleted the form (John, Jane, or Jim).
* **Notes**: Select the notes for your session from the dropdown list.
* **Submitter IP**: Enter the IP address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter User Agent**: Enter the User Agent of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter OS**: Enter the operating system of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Browser**: Enter the browser used by the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Device**: Enter the device used by the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Referrer**: Enter the referrer URL of the person submitting the form.
