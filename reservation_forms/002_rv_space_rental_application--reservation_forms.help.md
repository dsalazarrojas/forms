# rv_space_rental_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "rv_space_rental_application" form is used to collect essential information from individuals or businesses seeking to rent a space. This form helps property managers or landlords make informed decisions on rental applications by gathering details about the tenant's background, lease preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of each section.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, providing all required information.
3.  Submit the form once you have finished filling out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **reservation_form** (1, text, optional): This field is a text input field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your reservation.
* **lease_signings** (2, select_multiple, optional): Select all lease signing options that apply to your rental agreement.
* **tenant_background_checks** (3, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to indicate the status of your background checks.
* **guest_registration** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select all guest registration options that apply to your rental agreement.
* **weekly_tenants** (5, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to indicate your rental term.
* **background_check_status** (6, select_one, optional): Select the status of your background check.
* **lease_agreement** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select all lease agreement options that apply to your rental agreement.
* **rent_details** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select all rent details options that apply to your rental agreement.
* **payment_details** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select all payment details options that apply to your rental agreement.
* **notes** (10, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your rental agreement.
* **tenant_name** (11, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **tenant_email** (12, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **tenant_phone** (13, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **unit_type** (14, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to indicate the type of unit you are interested in.
* **unit_size** (15, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to indicate the size of the unit you are interested in.
* **lease_start** (16, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start your lease.
* **lease_end** (17, date, optional): Enter the date you would like your lease to end.
* **payment_method** (18, select_multiple, optional): Select all payment methods that apply to your rental agreement.
* **deposit_amount** (19, number, optional): Enter the deposit amount you are willing to pay.
* **rent_rate** (20, number, optional): Enter the rent rate you are willing to pay.
* **rent_term** (21, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to indicate the rent term.
* **move_in_date** (22, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to move in.
* **move_out_date** (23, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to move out.
* **rent_status** (24, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to indicate the rent status.
* **notes** (25, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your rental agreement.
