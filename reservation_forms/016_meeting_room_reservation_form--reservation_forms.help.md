# Meeting Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Room Reservation Form is used to reserve a meeting room for a specific purpose. Please provide detailed information for the form to ensure accurate room allocation and meeting arrangement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Click the "Submit" button to complete the reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Name ID**: Enter a unique ID for your name.
* **Meeting Topic**: Enter the topic of the meeting or event.
* **Room**: Select "Yes" if you wish to reserve the meeting room; otherwise, select "No".
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the meeting in the format "yyyy-mm-dd".
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the meeting in the format "yyyy-mm-dd".
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the meeting in the format "hh:mm".
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the meeting in the format "hh:mm".
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.

## Tips
- Make sure to select "Yes" if you need a meeting room.
- Enter the exact start and end dates and times of the meeting.
- Ensure the duration is accurate to avoid scheduling conflicts.
