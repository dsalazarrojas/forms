# Photography Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Photography Client Agreement Form" is a tool used to gather information from clients about their photography needs and preferences for a photo session. This form helps us understand what type of session you are looking for, what dates and times work best for you, and what services you are interested in.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your session details, including the date and time of your photo session.
2. Enter your client name.
3. Provide your email address and phone number so we can stay in touch with you.
4. Choose whether or not you are interested in our photo subjects/services.
5. Agree to the terms of the photo session.
6. If you have any specific requests or questions, please use the space provided for photo location and photo agreement to share your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details**: Enter the date and time of your photo session.
* **Client Name**: Enter your name as the client.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address so we can stay in touch with you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date of Photo Session**: Enter the date of your photo session.
* **Time of Photo Session**: Enter the time of your photo session.
* **Location of Photo Session**: Enter the location of your photo session.
* **Type of Photo Session**: Choose the type of photo session you are interested in. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Subjects**: Choose which photo subjects you are interested in. You can select only one option.
* **Client Agreement**: This is a space for you to agree to the terms of the photo session. Please read the terms carefully and agree to them by typing "I agree".
* **Agreed Terms**: This is a space for you to type any specific terms or conditions you would like to include or exclude from the photo session.
* **Photo Prophets**: This field is for any specific individuals that you would like to include or exclude from the photo session.
* **Date of Agreement**: Enter the date you agree to the terms of the photo session.
* **Agreed Terms (again)**: This is a duplicate field for you to agree to the terms of the photo session again. Please make sure to agree to the terms in the "Client Agreement" field as well.
