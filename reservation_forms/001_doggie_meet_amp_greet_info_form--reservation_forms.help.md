# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from pet owners for a meet-and-greet session with their dog. It helps us prepare for the meeting and ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the pet and our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your pet's name in the field labeled "pet_name".
2. Enter your name and contact information in the fields "owner_name" and "owner_phone".
3. Provide your dog's breed, size, and date of birth in the fields "pet_breed", "dog_size", and "dog_dob".
4. Choose a suitable size category for your dog from the options provided in "dog_size".
5. Select the appointment time that suits you best in the field "appointment_time".
6. Choose the services you would like to book during the meet-and-greet session from the "services" field.
7. Finally, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_name** (text, required): Enter your pet's name, e.g., "Buddy the Golden Retriever".
* **pet_breed** (text, optional): Provide your dog's breed if you know it (e.g., "Golden Retriever").
* **owner_name** (text, required): Enter your name as the pet's owner.
* **owner_phone** (text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your pet's meet-and-greet, please provide your phone number.
* **dog_dob** (date, required): Enter your dog's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **dog_size** (select one, required): Choose the size category that best describes your dog from the options provided:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ Extra Large
* **appointment_time** (time, required): Select the time slot for your meet-and-greet session.
* **meet_date** (date, optional): If you'd like to schedule a meet-and-greet session on a specific date, you can provide it here.
* **sitter_phone** (text, optional): If you'd like to provide an alternative contact number for your pet's sitter, you can enter it here.
* **sitter_email** (email, optional): If you'd like to provide an email address for your pet's sitter, you can enter it here.
* **services** (select multiple, required): Choose the services you'd like to book during the meet-and-greet session:
	+ Meet & Greet
	+ Walk
	+ Feed
	+ Sleep Over
