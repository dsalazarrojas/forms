# Lodge Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for making reservations at a lodge. Fill in the required information to help us prepare for your stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of your stay: "Business" or "Leisure".
2. Choose your arrival date.
3. Choose your departure date.
4. Enter the number of guests.
5. Enter the name of the person you'd like us to contact about your stay (if different from the lead).
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Optionally, provide a message about your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business or Leisure** (required): Choose between business or leisure travel. Please note that if you select "Business", you'll be contacted to verify your purpose of stay.
* **Arrival** (required): Select the date you plan to arrive at the lodge.
* **Departure** (required): Select the date you plan to check-out of the lodge.
* **Guests** (optional): Enter the number of guests in your group.
* **Contact Person** (optional): Enter the name of the person you'd like us to contact about your stay (if different from the lead).
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address.
* **Message** (optional): Provide any additional information about your reservation.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your dates to avoid any errors.
- If you have any special requests or need further assistance, please use the "Message" field.
