# Festival Organizer Village Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from festival organizers for booking purposes, including contact details, festival information, and logistical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact person's name and phone number in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Provide the name of the festival in the "Festival Name" field.
4. Specify the type of village or area where the festival will be held in the "Village Type" field.
5. Fill in the "Arrival Date" and "Departure Date" fields with the dates of your visit.
6. Enter the arrival and departure times of your festival in the "Arrival Time" and "Departure Time" fields, respectively.
7. If applicable, fill in the number of visitors in the "Total Visitors" field.
8. Provide any additional vehicle details in the "Vehicle" field, if necessary.
9. Specify the type of accommodation needed in the "Accommodation Type" field.
10. Add any notes about your booking in the "Booking Notes" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this booking.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Festival Name**: Enter the name of the festival.
* **Village Type**: Enter the type of village or area where the festival will be held.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date of your arrival.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date of your departure.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time of your arrival.
* **Departure Time**: Enter the time of your departure.
* **Total Visitors**: Enter the total number of visitors for your festival.
* **Vehicle**: Enter any additional vehicle details if needed.
* **Accommodation Type**: Enter the type of accommodation required.
* **Booking Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your booking.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling in the dates and times to avoid any conflicts with the festival schedule.
* Make sure to provide all the necessary information to ensure a smooth booking process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the festival organizers for assistance.
