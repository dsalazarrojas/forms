# Children Party Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Party Reservation Form is designed for parents or guardians to provide information about their child's party details, including contact information, child's details, and party preferences. This form will be used to book a party reservation for the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Parent or Guardian Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Child Name
	* Child Age
	* Total Number of Guests
	* Preferred Party Date
	* Preferred Party Time
	* Party Duration
	* Party Theme
	* Party Location
2. Choose the services and activities you want to include:
	* Include Catering Service
	* Entertainment Services Needed (e.g. DJ or Music, Face Painting, Balloon Artist, Magician, Games and Activities, Character Appearance)
3. Provide additional information:
	* Food Preferences and Allergies
	* Any Guest Dietary Restrictions
	* Any Special Needs or Requests

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This section is used to collect your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the party details.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child whose party you are booking.
* **Child Age**: Enter the age of the child.
* **Total Number of Guests**: Enter the total number of guests including the birthday child.
* **Preferred Party Date**: Choose a date for the party.
* **Preferred Party Time**: Choose a time for the party.
* **Party Duration**: Choose the length of the party from the options provided.
* **Party Theme**: Choose a theme for the party from the options provided.
* **Party Location**: Choose where the party will take place.
* **Include Catering Service**: Decide if you want to include catering service.
* **Entertainment Services Needed**: Choose all the entertainment services you want to include.
* **Include Party Decorations**: Decide if you want to include party decorations.
* **Include Cake and Desserts**: Decide if you want to include cake and desserts.
* **Any guest dietary restrictions**: Enter any dietary restrictions of the guests.
* **Any special needs or requests**: Enter any special needs or requests for the party.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Please confirm that you have read and agree to the party reservation terms and conditions.

Note: This guide is designed to be a helpful resource for users to understand the form and its requirements. Please review and update as needed to ensure accuracy and clarity.
