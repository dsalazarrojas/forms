# Entertainment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Reservation Form is used for users to submit information about events they would like to host or participate in.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing event details, including the date and time of the event.
2. Choose the type of event you would like to host or participate in.
3. Enter the name of the performer or performer's name if you are the organizer.
4. Add any additional contact information, such as email or phone number, if applicable.
5. Provide a detailed description of the event.
6. Choose the payment method and status of the event.
7. If applicable, add additional details about the payment, including the total amount paid.
8. Select the status of the event and the service provider.
9. Confirm the event by providing a confirmation email or phone number.
10. Finally, confirm the payment confirmation status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`id: 1`): Enter the name of the event you would like to host or participate in.
* **Date** (`id: 2`): Select the date of the event.
* **Time** (`id: 3`): Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Type** (`id: 4`): Select the type of event you would like to host or participate in.
* **Performer Name** (`id: 5`): Enter the name of the performer.
* **Performer Details** (`id: 6`): Add any additional details about the performer.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 7`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 8`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 9`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Event Description** (`id: 10`): Provide a detailed description of the event.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 11`): Choose the payment method for the event.
* **Payment Status** (`id: 12`): Select the payment status of the event.
* **Total Amount** (`id: 13`): Enter the total amount paid for the event.
* **Payment Details** (`id: 14`): Add any additional details about the payment.
* **Event Status** (`id: 15`): Select the status of the event.
* **Service Provider Name** (`id: 16`): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Phone** (`id: 17`): Enter the phone number of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Email** (`id: 18`): Enter the email address of the service provider.
* **Notes** (`id: 19`): Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Confirmation** (`id: 20`): Provide a confirmation of the event.
* **Confirmation Email** (`id: 21`): Enter the email address for the confirmation.
* **Confirmation Phone** (`id: 22`): Enter the phone number for the confirmation.
* **Confirmation Status** (`id: 23`): Select the status of the confirmation.
* **Service Provider Status** (`id: 24`): Select the status of the service provider.
* **Payment Confirmation Status** (`id: 25`): Select the status of the payment confirmation.
