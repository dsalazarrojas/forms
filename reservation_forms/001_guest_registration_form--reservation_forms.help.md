## Guest Registration Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Guest Registration Form is used to collect information from guests during their stay at our establishment. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details that will enable us to provide a comfortable and enjoyable stay for each guest. The collected data will be used for room allocation, billing, and other related administrative tasks.

### How To Complete This Form

- Start by entering your **First Name** in the text field.
- Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
- Enter your **Email** (if you have one) in the standard email format (e.g., example@example.com).
- Provide your **Phone Number** for us to contact you.
- Select your **Date of Arrival** in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
- Enter the **Time of Arrival** in the 24-hour time format (HH:MM).
- Choose your preferred **Room Type** from the options: **Suite**, **Deluxe**, or **Standard**.
- Enter the **Reservation Duration** in days (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
- Select your **Check-in** and **Check-out** dates in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
- Finally, add any **Comments** you may have regarding your stay.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your full first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your full last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Optional**): Enter your email address (if you want us to contact you).
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Date of Arrival** (`date_of_arrival`, Date, **Optional**): Select your arrival date in the dd/mm/yyyy format.
* **Time of Arrival** (`time_of_arrival`, Time, **Optional**): Enter your arrival time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, Select One, **Optional**): Choose from **Suite**, **Deluxe**, or **Standard** room types.
* **Reservation Duration** (`reservation_duration`, Number, **Optional**): Enter the number of days you plan to stay.
* **Check in** (`check_in`, Date, **Optional**): Select your check-in date in the dd/mm/yyyy format.
* **Check out** (`check_out`, Date, **Optional**): Select your check-out date in the dd/mm/yyyy format.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, **Optional**): Add any comments or special requests.
