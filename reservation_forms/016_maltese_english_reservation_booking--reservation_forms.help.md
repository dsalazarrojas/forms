# maltese_english_reservation_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to book and confirm their reservations with us. It is a general inquiry form for collecting contact details and reservation information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Reservation Form** information on the first page.
2. Provide your **Contact Details** on the second page.
3. If necessary, submit your **Confirm Form** on the third page to confirm your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Form (Text, Required)**: Enter your reservation information here. Please provide the required details as specified on this page.
* **Contact Details (Text, Required)**: Enter your name, email, and/or phone number to ensure we can contact you about your reservation. Please provide accurate and complete contact information.
* **Confirm Form (Text, Optional)**: If your reservation has been processed and you are ready to confirm, enter the confirmation code or reference number provided by our team. If not processed yet, please disregard this field.

## Tips
* Please ensure your contact details are accurate and up-to-date to allow us to contact you efficiently.
* If you need help or have any questions about your reservation, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* For any issues or discrepancies with your reservation, kindly provide the confirmation code or reference number for reference.
