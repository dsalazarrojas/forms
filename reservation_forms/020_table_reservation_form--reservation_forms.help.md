# Table Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect reservation information from customers, including their personal details, preferences, and special requirements. The data collected will be used to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable dining experience for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reservation details in the "Reservation Form" field.
2. Provide your personal information in the "Customer Info" field.
3. Select a date and time that suits you in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
4. Specify the number of guests you will be bringing in the "Number of Guests" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
6. Indicate if you have any special requirements in the "Special Requirements" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Form** (`reservation_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your reservation request.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for your reservation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for your reservation.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Specify the number of people you will be bringing.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your reservation.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you have any special requirements, such as a birthday celebration.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`, note, optional): Add any extra details about your reservation.
