# promotional_tour_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used for customers to reserve a promotional tour. It collects information about the customer's reservation, tour preferences, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Tour** field with the type of tour you are interested in (e.g., "tour a", "tour b", or "tour c").
2. Enter your **Start Date** and **End Date** for the tour. Make sure to use the correct date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
3. If you have any, enter your **Tour Description** to help our staff understand your specific needs.
4. Provide your **Customer Email** address and **Customer Phone** number for us to contact you.
5. Enter your **Customer Address** to help our staff contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tour
* **Tour**: Select the type of tour you are interested in booking (e.g., "tour a", "tour b", or "tour c").

### Reservation Details
* **Reservation Details**: Enter a brief description of your reservation, if needed.

### Start Date
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your tour (yyyy-mm-dd).

### End Date
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your tour (yyyy-mm-dd).

### Tour Description
* **Tour Description**: Enter any additional details about your tour (optional).

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.

### Customer Address
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address so we can contact you.
