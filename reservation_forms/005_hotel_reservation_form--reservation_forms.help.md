# Hotel Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Reservation Form is a tool for customers to provide their information for a hotel reservation. It is designed to collect relevant details for the hotel staff to process the customer's reservation and tailor their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in the reservation form on the first page.
- Enter your guest information, including name, phone number, and email address.
- Select your preferred room type (single, double, or suite).
- Choose your check-in and check-out dates.
- Enter your room number, if known.
- Add any additional notes, if necessary.
- Confirm that you have reviewed your details before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Form** (Reservation Form, text, required/optional): This is the main reservation form section, where you can enter your reservation details.
* **Guest Information** (Guest Information, text, required/optional): Please enter your name, phone number, and email address to ensure we can contact you about your stay.
* **Room Type** (Room Type, select_one, required/optional): Choose a room type that suits your needs. Options are Single room, Double room, and Suite.
* **Check-in Date** (Check-in Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to check-in.
* **Check-out Date** (Check-out Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to check-out.
* **Room Number** (Room Number, text, required/optional): If you know your room number, please enter it here.
* **Guest Name** (Guest Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name for our records.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your stay.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your stay.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or requests you may have for your stay.
* **Confirm** (Confirm, select_one, required/optional): Please confirm that you have reviewed your details before submitting.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required/optional): Once you have completed your details, click this button to submit your reservation request.
