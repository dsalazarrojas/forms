# Travel Agency Booking Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agency Booking Form Template is designed for travelers to book services with a travel agency. It collects general information, package details, flight information, hotel information, additional notes, payment information, travel agency contact details, and confirms the booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **General Information** page with your details.
2. Provide information about the **Package** you wish to book.
3. Enter your **Flight Information**.
4. Add details about your **Hotel** stay.
5. If you have any **Additional Notes**, you can write them here.
6. Enter your **Payment Information** for the booking.
7. Provide your contact details as part of the **Travel Agency Contact** information.
8. Finally, confirm your **Booking**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, phone number, or any other relevant contact information.
* **Package Information** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Describe the type of package you are interested in booking (e.g., tour, cruise, etc.).
* **Flight Information** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your flight details, including departure and arrival airports, dates, and times.
* **Hotel Information** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your hotel stay, including dates and room preferences.
* **Additional Notes** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Add any special requests or comments about your booking.
* **Payment Information** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your payment details, including method and amount.
* **Travel Agency Contact** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact details for the travel agency.
* **Confirm Booking** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Confirm that you are submitting the booking request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
* Use a clear and concise format for your writing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the travel agency directly.
