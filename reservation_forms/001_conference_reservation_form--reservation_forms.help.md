# Conference Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Conference Reservation Form is designed to gather details from conference attendees and speakers to ensure a smooth experience. This form helps us prepare for the event and accommodate the needs of all attendees. Please fill out this form to book your conference space and accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and organization name (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your contact email and phone number for reservation confirmation.
3.  Choose your preferred dates for check-in and check-out.
4.  Select the type of room accommodation required.
5.  Specify the number of guests attending the conference.
6.  If required, select the audio-visual equipment needed for sessions.
7.  Indicate if catering is required during the conference.
8.  List any dietary restrictions or preferences you have.
9.  If necessary, mention any special requests, such as accessibility needs.
10.  Provide a billing reference or PO number if you have an account with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Text, Required): Enter the primary contact or guest's name.
* **Organization or company** (Text, Optional): Provide the affiliated organization or company if applicable.
* **Contact email** (Email, Required): Enter your contact email for reservation confirmation.
* **Contact phone** (Text, Optional): Include your country code if international.
* **Check in date** (Date, Required): Enter the expected arrival date at the venue or lodging.
* **Check out date** (Date, Required): Enter the expected departure date.
* **Room type** (Select, Required): Choose your preferred accommodation type, such as Single room, Double room, or Suite.
* **Number of attendees** (Number, Required): Enter the total number of guests you will be bringing.
* **Audio-visual requirements** (Select, Optional): Choose the equipment needed for sessions, like Projector, Wired microphone, Wireless microphone, Sound system, or Video conferencing.
* **Catering needed** (Select, Required): Indicate if you need catering during the conference.
*   **Dietary restrictions or preferences** (Text, Optional): List any allergies or special diets.
*   **Special requests** (Text, Optional): Mention any accessibility needs or other accommodations.
*   **Billing reference or PO number** (Text, Optional): Provide an optional billing reference or Purchase Order number if you have an account with us.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your conference experience is comfortable and productive. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
