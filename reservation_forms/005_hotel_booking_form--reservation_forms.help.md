# Hotel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for hotel room bookings. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your booking is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field carefully, making sure to provide accurate information.
2. Review each page to ensure that you have completed all required fields.
3. Once you have completed all pages, review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Step 1**: Note: This is an introductory page with a brief message.
- **Step 2**: Select a travel date from the options provided.
  This field is required to determine the dates of your travel.
  Please select the start and end dates of your trip.
- **Step 3**: Enter your name.
  This field is required to confirm your identity.
  Please enter your full name, as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Step 4**: Enter a contact phone number.
  This field is required for hotel staff to contact you in case of any issues.
  Please enter a valid phone number with your country code.
- **Step 5**: Enter your email address.
  This field is required to send you booking confirmations and updates.
  Please enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
- **Step 6**: Select the number of guests.
  This field is required to determine the number of people in your group.
  Please select the number of guests that will be staying with you.
- **Step 7**: Select your accommodation type (e.g., single, double, suite).
  This field is required to ensure that we provide the correct accommodation for you.
  Please select the type of accommodation that suits your needs.
- **Step 8**: Enter the number of rooms required.
  This field is required to determine the number of rooms to book.
  Please enter the number of rooms you need for your group.
- **Step 9**: Enter a valid credit card number for payment.
  This field is required for payment processing.
  Please enter the number on your credit card without any dashes or spaces.
- **Step 10**: Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value) for your credit card.
  This field is required for payment processing.
  Please enter the 3-digit CVV found on the back of your credit card.
- **Step 11**: Select your preferred check-in date and time.
  This field is required to determine your arrival time.
  Please select a date and time that works best for you.
- **Step 12**: Select your preferred check-out date and time.
  This field is required to determine your departure time.
  Please select a date and time that works best for you.
- **Step 13**: Select whether you have a loyalty program or rewards card.
  This field is optional and is used for loyalty program points.
  Please indicate whether you have a loyalty card or not.
- **Step 14**: Select your preferred date.
  This field is required to determine the date of your travel.
  Please select a date that works best for you.
- **Step 15**: Select your preferred time.
  This field is required to determine the time of your travel.
  Please select a time that works best for you.
- **Step 16**: Enter a message for the hotel staff.
  This field is optional and is used to provide additional information to the hotel staff.
  Please enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
- **Step 17**: Select the number of adults in your group.
  This field is required to determine the number of adults in your group.
  Please select the number of adults that will be staying with you.
- **Step 18**: Select the number of children in your group.
  This field is required to determine the number of children in your group.
  Please select the number of children that will be staying with you.
- **Step 19**: Enter your email address.
  This field is required to send you booking confirmations and updates.
  Please enter a valid email address that you regularly check.

  This field is optional and is used to provide additional information to the hotel staff.

  This field is required to determine the type of room you want to book.
