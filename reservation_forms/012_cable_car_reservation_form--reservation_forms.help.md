# Cable Car Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you make a reservation for the cable car experience. Please fill out the required fields to ensure a smooth and enjoyable visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Primary Guest Name (field 1) - Enter the full name of the person making the reservation.
2. Enter your Email Address (field 2) - This will be used for confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your Phone Number (field 3) - This will be used for contact on the day of your visit.
4. Select your Reservation Date (field 4) - Choose the date you would like to visit the cable car.
5. Select your Preferred Time Slot (field 5) - Choose from the available time slots for your visit.
6. Enter the Number of Adults (field 6), Number of Children (field 7), and Number of Seniors (field 8) - These are the ages of the individuals in your group.
7. Select the Total Party Size (field 9) - This is the sum of the adults, children, and seniors.
8. Select the Ticket Type (field 10) - Choose from the available experience types offered by the cable car.
9. Select your Accessibility Needs (field 11) - If anyone in your party requires assistance, please choose the relevant option.
10. If necessary, describe your Accessibility Details (field 12) - Please provide more information about the assistance needed.
11. If applicable, select a Special Occasion (field 13) - This will help us make your visit more enjoyable.
12. If you'd like, add a Photo Package (field 16) or Dining Reservation (field 17) to your experience.
13. Let us know how you heard about the cable car (field 18) - This helps us improve our marketing efforts.
14. Finally, if you have any Additional Requests (field 19), please enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Guest Name (field 1)** (`primary_guest_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the full name of the person making the reservation.
* **Email Address (field 2)** (`email_address`, email, **Required**): This will be used for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number (field 3)** (`phone_number`, text, **Required**): Enter your contact number for the day of your visit.
* **Reservation Date (field 4)** (`reservation_date`, date, **Required**): Choose the date you would like to visit the cable car.
* **Preferred Time Slot (field 5)** (`preferred_time`, select_one, **Required**): Choose from the available time slots for your visit.
* **Number of Adults (field 6)** (`number_of_adults`, number, **Required**): Enter the number of adults in your group, aged 13 and above.
* **Number of Children (field 7)** (`number_of_children`, number, **Required**): Enter the number of children in your group, aged 3-12.
* **Number of Seniors (field 8)** (`number_of_seniors`, number, **Optional**): Enter the number of seniors in your group, aged 65 and above.
* **Total Party Size (field 9)** (`total_party_size`, number, **Required**): This is the total number of individuals in your group.
* **Ticket Type (field 10)** (`ticket_type`, select_one, **Required**): Choose from the available experience types offered by the cable car.
* **Accessibility Needs (field 11)** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, **Required**): If anyone in your party requires assistance, please choose the relevant option.
* **Accessibility Details (field 12)** (`accessibility_details`, text, **Optional**): If necessary, describe the specific needs of your party.
* **Special Occasion (field 13)** (`special_occasion`, select_one, **Optional**): If you're celebrating a special occasion, let us know.
* **Weather Acknowledgment (field 14)** (`weather_acknowledgment`, note, **Optional**): Acknowledge that the cable car operates weather permitting.
* **Cancellation Policy (field 15)** (`cancellation_policy`, note, **Optional**): View our cancellation policy for more information.
* **Photo Package Interest (field 16)** (`photo_package_interest`, select_one, **Optional**): If you're interested in adding a photo package to your experience.
* **Dining Reservation Interest (field 17)** (`dining_reservation`, select_one, **Optional**): If you're interested in a dining reservation at the summit.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (field 18)** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, **Optional**): How did you learn about the cable car?
* **Additional Requests (field 19)** (`additional_requests`, text, **Optional**): If you have any additional requests or questions, please enter them here.
