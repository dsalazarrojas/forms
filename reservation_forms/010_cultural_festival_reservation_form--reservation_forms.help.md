# cultural festival reservation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a spot at the cultural festival. It helps us gather information about each participant and their preferences, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Participant Information" section with your details.
2. Provide your reservation details in the "Reservation Details" section.
3. If you have any special requests, please let us know in the "Special Requests" section.
4. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
5. Enter your participant email address in the "Participant Email" field.
6. Add your participant phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
7. Select the date for the event in the "Event Date" field.
8. Choose the time for the event in the "Event Time" field.
9. Select one of the options in the "Participant Type" dropdown menu.
10. If you'd like to specify the reservation status, select one or more of the options in the "Reservation Status" field.
11. Add any additional notes about your reservation in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`text`, required: false): This section is where you can provide your basic information.
* **Reservation Details** (`text`, required: false): Describe the details of your reservation.
* **Special Requests** (`text`, required: false): If you have any special requests, please specify them here.
* **Participant Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as it should be displayed on our records.
* **Participant Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Participant Phone** (`text`, required: false): Add your phone number for easy contact.
* **Event Date** (`date`, required: false): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`time`, required: false): Choose the time of the event.
* **Participant Type** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Reservation Status** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following:
	+ Active
	+ Cancelled
	+ Confirmed
* **Notes** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional information you'd like to share.

Please note that this form is not required to be filled out in its entirety. Each field is optional, and you can fill out only the information you're comfortable sharing.
