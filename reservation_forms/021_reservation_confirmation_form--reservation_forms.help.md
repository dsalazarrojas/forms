# Reservation Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The reservation confirmation form is used to confirm a reservation made by a customer. This form is typically completed by the customer to provide additional details and confirm their reservation once the initial reservation has been made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Review the reservation details provided to ensure they are accurate.
2. Fill out the form with your correct details, making sure to fill in all required fields.
3. Select the required options for the select one and select multiple fields.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the note field.
5. Submit the form to confirm your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **reservation_confirmation** (1, text, required/optional): This field is the main confirmation field for the reservation. Please fill this in with your reservation details.
* **name** (2, text, required/optional): This field is to be filled with your name.
* **phone_number** (3, text, required/optional): This field is to be filled with your phone number.
* **email** (4, email, required/optional): This field is to be filled with your email address.
* **date** (5, date, required/optional): This field is to be filled with the date of your reservation.
* **time** (6, time, required/optional): This field is to be filled with the time of your reservation.
* **select one** (7, select_one, required/optional): Select one option from the list to confirm your reservation preferences.
* **select multiple** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple options from the list to confirm your reservation preferences.
* **note** (9, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your reservation.
