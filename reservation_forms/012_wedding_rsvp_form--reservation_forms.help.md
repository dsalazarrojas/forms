# Wedding rsvp form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to indicate your attendance at a wedding. Please fill out the form with your details, including your name, contact information, and RSVP response, to help the wedding hosts keep track of your attendance and facilitate communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name and last name (if applicable) in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Choose your RSVP response from the dropdown menu in the "RSVP" field.
4. If you are attending the wedding, enter the number of guests you are bringing in the "Number of guests" field.
5. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special requests" field.
6. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
7. Select your preferred method of RSVP (phone, email, or in person) in the "RSVP method" field.
8. If you selected phone as your preferred method, enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
9. If you selected email or in person as your preferred method, enter your contact email or name in the "Contact email/phone" field.
10. Enter your preferred contact method (phone or email) in the "Contact method" field.
11. Select your preferred contact method (phone or email) in the "Contact method 2" field.
12. If you are unable to attend, enter a phone number or email in the "Contact phone" field.
13. If you selected phone as your preferred contact method, enter your phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
14. If you selected email as your preferred contact method, enter your email address in the "Contact email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **RSVP** (Select one, required): Choose whether you are attending or not attending the wedding.
* **Number of guests** (Number, optional): If attending, enter the number of guests you are bringing.
* **Special requests** (Text, optional): If you have any special requests, enter them here.
* **Comments** (Text, optional): Enter any additional comments you want to share with the wedding hosts.
* **RSVP method** (Select one, required): Choose how you want to receive updates about your RSVP status.
* **Contact method** (Select one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted for further details.
* **Contact phone** (Text, optional): If you selected phone as your preferred contact method, enter your phone number.
* **Contact email** (Email, optional): If you selected email as your preferred contact method, enter your email address.
* **Contact address** (Text, optional): Enter the address you can be contacted at.
* **Contact city** (Text, optional): Enter the city you can be contacted in.
* **Contact state** (Select one, optional): Choose the state you can be contacted in.
* **Contact zip** (Text, optional): Enter the zip code where you can be contacted.
* **Contact country** (Text, optional): Enter the country where you can be contacted.
* **Contact comments** (Text, optional): Enter any additional comments about contact.
