# team-project-reservation-with-time-booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to book a time slot for a team project with specific details. It gathers information about the user, team, project, and booking details to facilitate the reservation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your user information in the first section.
*   Enter your team information in the second section.
*   Provide project details in the third section.
*   Enter your booking information in the fourth section.
*   Enter your school and college information in the subsequent sections (if applicable).
*   Enter your time booking details in the last section.
*   Finally, submit the form to complete the reservation.
