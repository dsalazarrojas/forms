# meeting_reservations - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or update meeting reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Reservations Form** (`meeting_reservations_page_1`, text, optional): This is the main meeting reservation section. Provide a brief description of the meeting, if necessary.
* **Organizer's Name** (`meeting_reservations_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person organizing the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Organizer's Email** (`meeting_reservations_organizer_email`, email, optional): Enter the organizer's email address.
* **Organizer's Phone** (`meeting_reservations_phone`, text, optional): Enter the organizer's phone number.

**Tips**
* Double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the meeting organizer for clarification.
