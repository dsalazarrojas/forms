# Desired Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Desired Accommodation Request Form is used to collect information from guests about their accommodation preferences and requirements for a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the reservation date(s) using the date picker.
2. Select the check-in and check-out dates using the date picker.
3. Choose the type of accommodation you require using the select multiple options.
4. Select the number of guests using the number field.
5. Choose the source of your reservation using the select one option.
6. Select the accommodation provider using the select one option.
7. Choose the room amenities you require using the select multiple options.
8. Enter any special requests in the text field.
9. Enter the accommodation address if applicable.
10. Enter your contact name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date**: Enter the date(s) you want to book the accommodation for. This field is optional but recommended for accurate booking and availability checks.
* **Check In**: Enter the date you want to check in for your stay. This field is optional and can be left blank if you are unsure.
* **Check Out**: Enter the date you want to check out for your stay. This field is optional and can be left blank if you are unsure.
* **Type of Accommodation**: Choose the type of accommodation you require, such as Hotel, Hostel, Motel, Bungalows, or Other. Select all that apply.
* **Room Type**: Select the type of room you require, such as Single, Double, Triple, Quadruple, or Suite. You can only choose one option.
* **Guest Count**: Enter the number of guests that will be staying with you.
* **Reservation Source**: Choose how you found out about this accommodation, such as Web, Email, Phone, or Social Media. Select only one option.
* **Accommodation Provider**: Choose the accommodation provider you want to book with, such as Hotel, Hostel, Bungalow, or Resort. Select only one option.
* **Room Amenities**: Choose the amenities you require in your room, such as TV, AC, Fridge, Internet, or Pool. Select all that apply.
* **Guest Details**: Enter any additional information about your guests, such as their name, age, or special requirements.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you have, such as extra bed or pillows.
* **Accommodation Address**: Enter the address of the accommodation if different from your billing address.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name and title (e.g., Mr., Ms., Dr.).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact the accommodation provider for clarification.
* Be aware that some fields are optional, but providing accurate information will help streamline the booking process.
