# Yacht Docking Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help yacht owners and users make a docking reservation at a marina or docking facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the text fields.
2. Enter your email address in the email field. This will be used for communication and confirmation purposes.
3. Provide your phone number in the phone field. This will help us to contact you in case of any questions or issues.
4. Select a date for your desired docking reservation using the date field.
5. Choose a time for your docking reservation using the time field.
6. Specify if you need a slip of a certain size in the slip size field, and if you do, select 'Yes' from the options.
7. Provide the name of the marina where you plan to dock your yacht.
8. Indicate if you need to make a reservation start and end using the reservation start and end fields, and if you do, select 'Yes' from the options.
9. Add any additional notes about your reservation in the notes field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for your docking reservation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your docking reservation.
* **Slip Size** (`slip_size`, select_one, optional): If you need a slip of a certain size, select 'Yes' from the options.
* **Marina Name** (`marina_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the marina where you plan to dock your yacht.
* **Reservation Start** (`reservation_start`, select_multiple, optional): If you need to make a reservation start, select 'Yes' from the options.
* **Reservation End** (`reservation_end`, select_multiple, optional): If you need to make a reservation end, select 'Yes' from the options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your reservation, if needed.
