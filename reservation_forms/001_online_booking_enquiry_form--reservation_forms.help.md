# Online Booking Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for guests and customers to contact your business for online booking enquiries. It allows them to provide their contact information and any feedback they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information as follows:

1. **Contact Information**: Please enter your name and contact details in this field.
2. **Guest Information**: Provide any additional information about your guest(s) you want to book.
3. **Feedback**: If you have any feedback or comments, please enter them here.
4. **Feedback**: You can provide any additional details about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Name, Email, Phone)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Guest Information**: Provide any details about the guest(s) you want to book, such as age, date of birth, or other relevant information.
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback or comments you may have about our services or booking process.
* **Feedback (again)**: If you have any additional feedback or comments, you can enter them here as well.
