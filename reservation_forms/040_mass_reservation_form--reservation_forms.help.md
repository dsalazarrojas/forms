# Mass Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mass Reservation Form is used to collect information about mass reservations, including details about the reservation, attendees, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Form Data** field with a brief description of the mass reservation.
2. Provide **Mass Details** to help us better understand the mass reservation.
3. Perform a **Health Check** to ensure all attendees are fit to participate.
4. Select a suitable **Appointment** date and time.
5. Choose the **Slots** that will be occupied during the mass reservation.
6. Enter the **Contact Details** of the attendees, including their names, emails, and phone numbers.
7. Enter the **Email** address of the attendees.
8. Enter the **Phone** number of the attendees.
9. Enter the **Address** of the attendees.
10. Add any **Notes** about the mass reservation.
11. Enter the **Form Submission** date and time.
12. Enter the **Form Revision Date** and **Form Revision Time** if the form needs to be revised.
13. Select the **Form Revision Status** from the available options.
14. Add any **Form Revision Notes** and **Form Revision User Notes** if necessary.
15. Finally, enter the **Form Submission Notes** and select the **Form Revision Status** for the form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**: A brief description of the mass reservation.
* **Mass Details**: Information about the mass reservation.
* **Health Check**: Ensure all attendees are fit to participate.
* **Appointment**: A date and time for the mass reservation.
* **Slots**: Select the slots that will be occupied during the mass reservation.
* **Attendees**: Enter the contact details of all attendees.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the attendees.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the attendees.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the attendees.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the mass reservation.
* **Form Submission**: Enter the date and time of form submission.
* **Form Revision Date**: Enter the date of form revision.
* **Form Revision Time**: Enter the time of form revision.
* **Form Revision Status**: Select the status of the form revision.
* **Form Revision Notes**: Add any additional notes about the form revision.
* **Form Revision User**: Enter the user who revised the form.
* **Form Submission Notes**: Add any additional notes about the form submission.
* **Form Revision Status**: Select the status of the form submission.
