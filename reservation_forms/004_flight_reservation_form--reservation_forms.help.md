# flight_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flight_reservation_form is a form used for booking flights. It collects essential information from users, including their name, contact details, and travel dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the flight_reservation_form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Choose a travel date in the **Travel Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your given name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your surname as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you plan to travel.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact your support team for assistance.
