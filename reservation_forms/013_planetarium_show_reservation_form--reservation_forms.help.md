# Planetarium Show Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Planetarium Show Reservation Form is a tool for customers to book and manage show reservations at a planetarium. This form allows customers to provide show details, contact information, and reservation status, enabling staff to efficiently manage reservations and communicate with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Reservation Form" section with the relevant show details, such as show title, description, and image.
2. Select the "Show Status" (Active or Inactive) to indicate the current status of the show.
3. Enter the "Contact Name", "Contact E-mail", and "Contact Phone" details of the person responsible for the reservation.
4. Enter the "Show Date", "Show Start Time", and "Show End Time" for the reserved show.
5. Provide a "Show Duration" in minutes.
6. Enter the "Show Price" in numerical format.
7. Leave "Reservation Notes" for any additional comments or notes about the reservation.
8. Select the "Reservation Status" (Reserved, Confirmed, or Canceled).
9. Select "Reserved By" whether it is "Yes" or "No".
10. Fill in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" for the person making the reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Form (text)**: Enter the details of the show reservation.
* **Show Details (text)**: Provide a brief description of the show, including the show time and duration.
* **Date and Time (date)**: Enter the date and time of the show.
* **Show Time (time)**: Enter the start and end time of the show.
* **Duration (number)**: Enter the duration of the show in minutes.
* **Show Title (text)**: Enter the title of the show.
* **Show Description (text)**: Enter a brief description of the show.
* **Show Image (text)**: Upload an image related to the show.
* **Contact Name (text)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the show.
* **Contact E-mail (email)**: Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the show.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the show.
* **Show Status (select_one)**: Select the status of the show (Active or Inactive).
* **Show Date (date)**: Enter the date of the show.
* **Show Start Time (time)**: Enter the start time of the show.
* **Show End Time (time)**: Enter the end time of the show.
* **Show Duration (number)**: Enter the duration of the show in minutes.
* **Show Price (number)**: Enter the price of the show.
* **Reservation Notes (note)**: Leave additional comments or notes about the reservation.
* **Reservation Status (select_one)**: Select the status of the reservation (Reserved, Confirmed, or Canceled).
* **Reserved By (select_multiple)**: Select whether the show is reserved by "Yes" or "No".
* **Contact Title (text)**: Enter the title of the person responsible for the show.
* **Reservation ID (text)**: Enter the reservation ID number.
* **Reservation Number (number)**: Enter the reservation number.
* **Customer Name (text)**: Enter the name of the customer making the reservation.
* **Customer Email (email)**: Enter the email of the customer making the reservation.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the provided fields to the best of your abilities to ensure clear and concise communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the planetarium staff for assistance.
