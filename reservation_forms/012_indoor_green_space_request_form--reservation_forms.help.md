# indoor-green-space-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The indoor green space request form is used to collect information for indoor green space requests. This includes details about the purpose of the event, date, time, location, and other relevant information to facilitate planning and management of indoor green space reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Reservation Purposes** field with the type of event you are requesting (e.g. Meeting, Event, Training).
2. Enter the **Event Date** when your event is scheduled to take place.
3. Enter the **Start Time** of your event.
4. Enter the **Duration** of your event in hours or minutes.
5. Indicate the **Attendee Count** for your event.
6. Check any special needs you may require for your event (e.g. Wheelchair, Service Animal, Service Animal Assistant).
7. Indicate if you are requesting **Reservations** for your event.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about your event in the **Comments** field.
9. Enter the location where your event will take place in the **Location** field.
10. Enter the name and contact information of your internal contact person.
11. Enter the name and contact information of your external contact person.
12. Enter your name and contact information (requestor).
13. Enter the name and contact information of the organization you are representing (if applicable).
14. Enter any additional comments or notes about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Purposes**: Select the type of event you are requesting (e.g. Meeting, Event, Training).
* **Event Date**: Enter the date your event is scheduled to take place.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your event in hours or minutes.
* **Attendee Count**: Indicate the number of attendees for your event.
* **Special Needs**: Check any special needs you require for your event (e.g. Wheelchair, Service Animal, Service Animal Assistant).
* **Reservations**: Indicate if you are requesting reservations for your event.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your event.
* **Location**: Enter the location where your event will take place.
* **Internal Contact Name**: Enter the name of your internal contact person.
* **Internal Contact Email**: Enter the email of your internal contact person.
* **Internal Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your internal contact person.
* **External Contact Name**: Enter the name of your external contact person.
* **External Contact Email**: Enter the email of your external contact person.
* **External Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your external contact person.
* **Requestor Name**: Enter your name.
* **Requestor Email**: Enter your email.
* **Requestor Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **External Request Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are representing (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your event.
* **Internal Requester Title**: Enter your title or position.
* **Internal Requester Email**: Enter your internal contact email.
* **Internal Requester Phone**: Enter your internal contact phone number.
* **Date Started**: Enter the date you started the request.
* **Date Ended**: Enter the date your request was completed.
