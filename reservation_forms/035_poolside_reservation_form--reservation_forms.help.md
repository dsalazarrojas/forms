# Poolside Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poolside Reservation Form is designed for customers to provide their poolside reservation information, allowing our team to effectively manage reservations and provide an excellent customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the reservation details, such as date and time of reservation.
2. Enter your customer details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide any additional contact details, such as address and phone number.
4. If applicable, add any cabana or chair reservation details.
5. Add any chair or table reservation details.
6. Add any notes or special requests.
7. Enter your email and phone number.
