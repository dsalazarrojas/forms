# Concert VIP Seating Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concert VIP Seating Reservation Form is designed to gather information from users to confirm their VIP seating reservations at a concert event. This form will help us ensure that we have the necessary details to provide an exceptional experience for you and your guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and contact email address so we can reach out to you for confirmation and follow-up.
2. Choose your preferred VIP package from the available options.
3. Specify the number of seats you would like to reserve.
4. If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know.
5. Confirm your payment status.
6. Select your expected arrival time for VIP entry.
7. Provide the names of all guests included in your reservation.
8. Enter your contact phone number for follow-up.
9. Read and review the reservation confirmation instructions for pickup and entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: Please enter your full name for the reservation.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you for confirmation and follow-up.
* **VIP package selected**: Choose the type of VIP experience you are interested in, such as front row seating, backstage access, meet-and-greet, or hospitality suite.
* **Number of seats**: Enter the number of seats you would like to reserve for the VIP area.
* **Special requests**: If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know here. This could include dietary restrictions or accessibility requirements.
* **Payment status**: Confirm the status of your payment, whether it's paid, pending, or requires an invoice.
* **Expected arrival time**: Enter your expected time of arrival for VIP entry.
* **Guest names**: Enter the names of all guests included in your reservation.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you for follow-up.
* **Reservation confirmation instructions**: Review the instructions on pickup and entry for your VIP experience.
