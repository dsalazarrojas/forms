# thanksgiving_turkey_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The thanksgiving_turkey_reservation_form is designed to collect information from users who want to reserve a turkey for a Thanksgiving celebration. This form will help you manage reservations and possibly generate a report or output file.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the reservation date.
2. Select the party size.
3. Enter your name.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Choose the turkey size you want to reserve.
7. Select the reservation time.
8. Add any additional notes or comments.
9. If applicable, select the tools assigned to the reservation.
10. Finally, review your reservation details and click "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date** (reservation_date): Enter the date you want to make the reservation for.
* **Party size** (party_size): Select the number of people in your party.
* **Name** (name): Enter your name.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Turkey size** (turkey_size): Choose the size of turkey you want to reserve (small, medium, large).
* **Reservation Time** (reservation_time): Select the time you want to make the reservation for.
* **Note** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about your reservation.
* **Assigned To** (assigned_tool): If applicable, select the tools assigned to the reservation.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (assigned_tool_2): If applicable, select additional tools assigned to the reservation.
* **Category** (category): Select the category for your reservation (e.g., Reservation Forms, Other).
* **Description** (description): Enter a brief description of your reservation.
* **Form ID** (form_id): Enter a unique ID for your form.
* **Output File** (output_file): Enter a filename for the output file.
* **Assigned Tool 3** (assigned_tool_3): If applicable, select additional tools assigned to the reservation.
* **Form Id 2** (form_id_2): Enter another unique ID for your form.
