# Boat Berth Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Berth Application Form is used to gather essential information from vessel owners or operators to reserve a berth in a marina or harbor. It is essential to have all the required details correct to ensure a smooth and efficient reservation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the Applicant name and their contact email address to ensure we can get in touch with you for any updates or queries.
2. Enter the name of your vessel, its length in feet, and registration number (if applicable).
3. Choose your preferred berth type: Fixed dock slip, Floating dock slip, or Mooring buoy.
4. If you have a preferred start date, enter it. Please note that this is an optional field and does not guarantee availability.
5. If your vessel has insurance, please enter the name of the insurance provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Applicant and Vessel Details
* **-- Applicant and Vessel Details --**:
	+ This section is a header for the form.

### Applicant Name
* **Applicant Name**: <i>Full name for contact purposes</i>
	+ Enter the name of the person responsible for the vessel and contact information.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email**: <i>For berth allocation notices and updates</i>
	+ Enter the email address to receive important updates and notifications about your berth.

### Vessel Details
* **Vessel Name**: <i>Name of the boat</i>
	+ Enter the name of your vessel, such as "Sailboat X" or "Motor Yacht Z".
* **Vessel Length (feet)**: <i>Enter the length of your vessel in feet</i>
	+ Measure the length of your vessel and enter it accurately.
* **Vessel Registration Number**: <i>Registration or official number (if applicable)</i>
	+ If your vessel is registered, enter its registration or official number for identification purposes.

### Berth Type
* **Preferred Berth Type**: <i>Choose your preferred type of berth</i>
	+ Select from: Fixed dock slip, Floating dock slip, or Mooring buoy. Each berth type has its own unique characteristics and amenities.

### Additional Information
* **Insurance Provider**: <i>Enter the name of your vessel insurance provider (if applicable)</i>
	+ If your vessel has insurance, enter the name of your insurance provider for identification purposes.
* **Desired Start Date**: <i>Optional: When your vessel prefers to start</i>
	+ If you have a preferred start date, enter it. Please note that this does not guarantee availability.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all required fields completed accurately to avoid delays in the reservation process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the marina administration for assistance.
* Please review your submitted form carefully before sending it in to ensure it is accurate and complete.
