# Church Conference Room Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Conference Room Booking Form is designed to provide detailed information about events and meetings to be held at the church, including room selection, time, and any specific needs or requests. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please ensure you have all the necessary information ready to provide accurate and complete answers. Here are the general steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Select the conference room you need from the options provided.
3. Enter the date and start and end times for your event.
4. Choose the room arrangement you require.
5. Enter the expected number of attendees.
6. Select the audio visual equipment and any other necessary resources required for your event.
7. Choose the catering option or select that no catering is needed.
8. Check if your event is recurring, and if so, provide the recurrence pattern.
9. Check if you will need reserved parking.
10. Confirm your understanding of the security deposit policy.
11. Confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Your Full Name** (`requester_name`, text, required)
Enter your full name as the person responsible for the booking.

### 2. **Email Address** (`email`, email, required)
Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.

### 3. **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required)
Enter your phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.

### 4. **Church Department or Organization** (`organization`, text, required)
Enter the department or organization you are representing.

### 5. **Conference Room Selection** (`room_selection`, select_one, required)
Choose the conference room that best suits your needs from the options provided.

### 6. **Date of Event** (`event_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the event or meeting.

### 7. **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required)
Enter the start time of the event or meeting.

### 8. **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required)
Enter the end time of the event or meeting.

### 9. **Room Setup Style** (`setup_style`, select_one, required)
Choose the room arrangement you require for your event or meeting.

### 10. **Expected Number of Attendees** (`expected_attendees`, number, required)
Enter the expected number of attendees for your event or meeting.

### 11. **Audio Visual Equipment Needed** (`av_equipment`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the audio visual equipment you need for your event or meeting.

### 12. **Catering Services** (`catering`, select_one, required)
Choose the catering option that suits your needs or select that no catering is needed.

### 13. **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional)
Enter any special requests or notes about your event or meeting.

### 14. **Is This a Recurring Event** (`recurring_event`, select_one, required)
Check if your event is a one-time event or if it's a recurring event.

### 15. **If Recurring, Specify Pattern** (`recurrence_pattern`, text, optional)
If your event is recurring, specify the pattern.

### 16. **Will You Need Reserved Parking** (`parking_needed`, select_one, optional)
Check if you will need reserved parking for your event or meeting.

### 17. **Security Deposit Acknowledgment** (`security_deposit`, select_one, required)
Check that you understand and agree to the security deposit policy.

### 18. **Cancellation Policy Agreement** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required)
Check that you understand and agree to the cancellation policy.
