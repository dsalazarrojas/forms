# Photography Photo Package Order Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Photo Package Order Form 2 is a form used to gather information from clients about their photography package needs for events such as weddings, birthday celebrations, or other gatherings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event from the "Reservation Event Type" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the desired photography package type.
3. Enter the date and time of the event.
4. Enter the number of guests attending the event.
5. Provide the location of the event.
6. Enter your name and contact phone number.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Enter a brief description of the event.
9. If necessary, include any special requests.
10. Enter any details about the package you'd like to order.
11. Finally, provide any payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Event Type (id: 1)**: Select the type of event you are planning, such as "Wedding" or "Birthday".
* **Package Type (id: 2)**: Choose the type of photography package you are interested in, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Date and Time (id: 3)**: Enter the date and time of the event.
* **Number of Guests (id: 4)**: Enter the number of people attending the event.
* **Location (id: 5)**: Enter the physical address or location of the event.
* **Contact Name (id: 6)**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Phone (id: 7)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (id: 8)**: Enter your email address.
* **Event Description (id: 9)**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Special Requests (id: 10)**: If you have any special requests or instructions, enter them here.
* **Package Details (id: 11)**: Enter any additional details about the package you are interested in.
* **Payment Information (id: 12)**: Enter any relevant payment information, such as your payment method or account details.
