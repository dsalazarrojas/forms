# Hillary_Owen_Church_Registration_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals for a specific event or service at Hillary Owen Church. It is intended to gather relevant information for event planning, attendance, or service sign-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Enter your Email in the standard email format (e.g. example@domain.com) in the third field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth field.
5. Select a Reservation Time that suits your schedule.
6. Choose your Meal Reservations (Yes/No).
7. Enter any additional Notes for event or service staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your standard email address (e.g. example@domain.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, `time`, required): Select a time that suits your schedule for the event or service.
* **Meal Reservations** (`meal_reservations`, `select_multiple`, required): Please choose whether you will have meal reservations or not. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for event or service staff.

## Tips
- Ensure your Email and Phone numbers are accurate to avoid any issues with communication.
- Select a Reservation Time that suits your schedule to plan accordingly.
- Choose your Meal Reservations honestly, as it helps with food and drink planning.
- Enter any Notes that might be useful for event or service staff to know.
