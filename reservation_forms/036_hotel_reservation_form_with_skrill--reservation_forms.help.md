# Hotel Reservation Form With Skrill - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Reservation Form With Skrill is designed to collect guest details, including their name, email, phone number, and date of stay. Additionally, it allows users to select the desired room type(s) and quantity, and specify any additional room type requirements. The form ensures that users can provide all necessary information to complete their hotel reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your guest information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for any updates.
3. Select your check-in and check-out dates.
4. Choose the desired room type(s).
5. Specify the room quantity you require.
6. Select any additional room type requirements, if necessary.
7. Fill in the room number and guest count if applicable.
8. Confirm your room details for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Info (1)** (text, required: false): This field is the guest's basic information, providing space to enter any details about the guest.
  - **Name (2)** (text, required: false): Enter your name to be associated with your reservation.
- **Email (3)** (email, required: false): Input your email address so that we can contact you.
- **Phone (4)** (text, required: false): Please enter your contact phone number for any updates.
- **Check In Date (5)** (date, required: false): Select your arrival date to the hotel.
- **Check Out Date (6)** (date, required: false): Select your departure date from the hotel.
- **Room Type (7)** (select multiple, required: false): Choose one or more room type from the options, such as Single, Double, or Triple.
  - **Room Type 2 (10)** (select multiple, required: false): This field is for specifying additional room type requirements, such as Suite or King.
- **Room Number (8)** (number, required: false): Fill in the room number if you have one in mind.
- **Guest Count (9)** (number, required: false): Enter the number of people in your party.
- **Room Quantity (11)** (number, required: false): Enter how many rooms you require.
- **Room Number 2 (13)** (number, required: false): If applicable, enter an additional room number or quantity.
- **Check Out Date 2 (12)** (date, required: false): Input your departure date for this room, if different than the main date.
- **Room Type 2 (10)** (select multiple, required: false): Choose an additional room type for this room, if required.
