# vehicle_rental_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a vehicle rental for a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the reservation date.
2. Provide the pick up information (location, time, etc.).
3. Select the type of vehicle you need (e.g., Ford, SUV, Van, Truck).
4. Enter the vehicle make and model.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the reservation.
* **Pick Up** (`pick_up`, text, optional): Enter the location and time of pick up.
* **Drop Off** (`drop_off`, text, optional): Enter the location and time of drop off.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of vehicle you need (e.g., Ford, SUV, Van, Truck).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.

## Tips
* Ensure that the reservation date is accurate and realistic.
* Double-check the pick up and drop off locations to avoid any confusion.
* Select the correct vehicle type to ensure that you get the correct vehicle.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant documentation or contact the vehicle rental service provider.
