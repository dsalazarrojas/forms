# Piercing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piercing Request Form is a form designed to gather information from customers about their piercing requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information about your piercing request before starting the form.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, taking care to provide the correct information.
3. Review your completed form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant customer information.
* **Piercing Type** (`piercing_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of piercing you are requesting (e.g. Derby, Nose, Ear, etc.).
* **Piercing Date** (`piercing_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to have your piercing performed.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, optional): Select the time of day you would like to have your piercing performed.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where you would like to have your piercing performed.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your piercing request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, `text`, optional): If applicable, select the assigned technician's name or ID.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and thorough information will help us better serve your piercing request needs.
