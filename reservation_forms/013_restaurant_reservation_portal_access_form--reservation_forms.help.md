# Restaurant Reservation Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Reservation Portal Access Form is a tool that allows customers to access their reservation information on the restaurant's portal. This form helps customers manage their reservations, update their booking details, and receive notifications about their upcoming reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Reservation Date** (Step 1): This is the date of your reservation. Make sure to enter the correct date of your reservation.
2. Enter the **Reservation Time** (Step 2): This is the time of your reservation. Ensure it matches the time you were told when you made the reservation.
3. Enter the **Number of Guests** (Step 3): This is the number of people attending your reservation. If you have a large group, enter the correct number.
4. Enter your **Customer Phone** (Step 4): This is your phone number. Enter the correct number so we can reach you with updates or concerns.
5. Enter your **Email** (Step 5): This is your email address. Enter the correct email address so we can send you updates, confirmations, or notifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date**: Enter the date of your reservation.
* **Reservation Time**: Enter the time of your reservation.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of people attending your reservation.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. 

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication with the restaurant.
* If you experience any issues or have concerns, contact us for assistance.
