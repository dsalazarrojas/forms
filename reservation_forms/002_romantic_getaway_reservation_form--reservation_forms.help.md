# Romantic Getaway Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for booking a romantic getaway reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields with your personal details.
2. Choose the **Date of Arrival** and **Date of Departure** for your trip.
3. Select the **Number of Guests** who will be accompanying you on the trip.
4. Select the type of **Room Type** that suits your needs (Single, Double, or Suite).
5. Choose your **Breakfast Options** (Continental, Full, or None).
6. Write any **Special Requests** you have in the space provided.
7. Enter your **Phone** number in case we need to contact you.
8. Leave a **Message** with any additional information about your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Arrival**: Choose the date you will arrive at the getaway location.
* **Date of Departure**: Choose the date you will depart from the getaway location.
* **Number of Guests**: Select the number of people who will be accompanying you on the trip.
* **Room Type**: Select the type of room that suits your needs (Single, Double, or Suite).
* **Breakfast Options**: Choose the breakfast options you would like to have during your stay.
* **Special Requests**: Write any special requests you have for your stay, such as a preferred room location or any specific needs.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Message**: Leave any additional information about your reservation, such as any special instructions or requests.
