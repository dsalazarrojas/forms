# Property Reservation Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Reservation Payment Form is designed to facilitate payments for properties. Users will need to enter relevant details such as property information and payment methods.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the property for which you are making the payment.
2. Enter the customer's name.
3. Choose the payment method and date.
4. Enter the reservation ID, payment amount, and other details as required.
5. Provide additional notes if necessary.
6. Enter the customer's email and phone number.
7. Select the payment status and other relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Id**: Enter the ID of the property for which you are making the payment.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the full name of the customer.
* **Payment Date**: Choose the date of the payment.
* **Reservation Id**: Enter the ID of the reservation.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (Yes/No).
* **Card Type**: If applicable, select the type of card used.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment (Active/Inactive).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the payment.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount of the payment (if not already entered in the Amount field).
* **Payment Method Other**: If no other payment method is selected, enter the alternative method used.
* **Reservation Status**: Select the status of the reservation (Active/Inactive).
* **Payment Refund**: Select whether a refund is applicable (Yes/No).

## Tips
* Double-check the property and customer information before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all necessary details before filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
