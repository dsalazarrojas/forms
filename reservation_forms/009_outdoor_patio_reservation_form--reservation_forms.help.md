# outdoor_patio_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve an outdoor patio at our establishment. Please complete this form to request a spot for your desired date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose a date for your reservation using the date picker input.
2. Select the time of your visit.
3. Enter the size of your party (number of guests).
4. Provide your name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to make your reservation. If left blank, your reservation will be considered for the current date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of your visit.
* **Party Size** (`party_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests attending.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
 
## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your email address for correctness.
* If you need to make any changes or cancellations, please contact us directly.
* We will do our best to accommodate your requested date and time, but availability may be subject to change.
