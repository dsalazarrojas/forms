# Wedding rsvp template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect RSVPs from guests to a wedding event, allowing the wedding planner to track and manage the guest list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the guests attending from the list provided.
2. Enter the guest's name and other relevant information if necessary.
3. Choose the RSVP type (Wedding, Not Attending, Maybe).
4. Provide the guest's email address, phone number, and other contact information if necessary.
5. Add any special dietary restrictions or requests.
6. Provide any special instructions or comments for the guest.
7. Confirm if the RSVP has been sent or not.
8. Track the status of the RSVP, including the date it was sent and if comments were received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guests Attending**: Select the guests who are attending the wedding. This will help the wedding planner to track the number of guests attending.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest who is attending or not attending the wedding.
* **RSVP Type**: Choose the RSVP type (Wedding, Not Attending, Maybe) to indicate the guest's status.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the email address of the guest if they have one. This will help the wedding planner to contact the guest if necessary.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the guest if they have one. This will help the wedding planner to contact the guest if necessary.
* **Date**: Enter the date the RSVP was sent to the guest.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or special requests from the guest.
* **Special Dietary Restrictions**: Select any special dietary restrictions or requests from the guest.
* **Special Instructions**: Add any special instructions or comments from the guest.
* **Special Requests**: Add any special requests from the guest.
* **RSVP Sent**: Confirm if the RSVP has been sent to the guest or not.
* **RSVP Status**: Track the status of the RSVP, including if it is pending, confirmed, or declined.
* **Date Sent**: Track the date the RSVP was sent to the guest.
* **Comments Sent**: Confirm if comments or special requests were sent to the guest.
* **Comments Received**: Confirm if comments or special requests were received from the guest.
* **Comments Status**: Track the status of comments or special requests, including if they are open or closed.

Note: The fields marked as optional are not required, but can be filled in if necessary. 

## Tips

* Make sure to review the information carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check the RSVP type and status to ensure accurate tracking.
* Use the special dietary restrictions and instructions fields to communicate any special requests from the guest.
* Use the comments field to add any additional comments or requests from the guest.
* Make sure to send the RSVP confirmation and comments to the guest if necessary.
* Review the status of comments or special requests regularly to ensure timely action.
