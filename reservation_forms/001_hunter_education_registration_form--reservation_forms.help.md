# Hunter Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for hunter education registration. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your Email address, Phone number, and Hunter ID.
4. Select your Education Status, which may be Valid, Invalid, or Unknown.
5. Enter the name of your Education Provider.
6. Select the location and date for your Field Day.
7. Enter your Hunter Education Certificate information, including Provider, Issue Date, Expiration Date, Number, and Provider 2.
8. Enter the rest of the Hunter Education information, including Number 2, Issue Date 2, and Expiration Date 2.
9. Enter any additional Hunter Education information, including Number 3, Issue Date 3, and Expiration Date 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Hunter ID** (`hunter_id`, number, required): Enter your hunter ID number.
* **Education Status** (`education_status`, select_one, required): Select your education status from the options provided.
* **Education Provider** (`education_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your education provider.
* **Field Day Location** (`field_day_location`, select_one, required): Select the location of your field day from the options provided.
* **Field Day Date** (`field_day_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your field day in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Field Day Time** (`field_day_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your field day.
* **Field Day Organizer** (`field_day_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the field day organizer.
* **Hunter Education Certificate** (`hunter_education_certificate`, text, required): Enter your hunter education certificate number.
* **Hunter Education Level** (`hunter_education_level`, select_one, required): Select your hunter education level from the options provided.
* **Hunter Education Number** (`hunter_education_number`, text, required): Enter your hunter education number.
* **Hunter Education Issue Date** (`hunter_education_issue_date`, date, required): Enter the date your hunter education was issued.
* **Hunter Education Expiration Date** (`hunter_education_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the date your hunter education expires.
* **Hunter Education Provider** (`hunter_education_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your hunter education provider.
* **Hunter Education Number 2** (`hunter_education_number_2`, text, required): Enter your second hunter education number.
* **Hunter Education Issue Date 2** (`hunter_education_issue_date_2`, date, required): Enter the date your second hunter education was issued.
* **Hunter Education Expiration Date 2** (`hunter_education_expiration_date_2`, date, required): Enter the date your second hunter education expires.
* **Hunter Education Provider 2** (`hunter_education_provider_2`, text, required): Enter the name of your second hunter education provider.
* **Hunter Education Number 3** (`hunter_education_number_3`, text, required): Enter your third hunter education number.
* **Hunter Education Date of Birth** (`hunter_education_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of your hunter education.
* **Hunter Education Issue Date 3** (`hunter_education_issue_date_3`, date, required): Enter the date your third hunter education was issued.
* **Hunter Education Expiration Date 3** (`hunter_education_expiration_date_3`, date, required): Enter the date your third hunter education expires.
