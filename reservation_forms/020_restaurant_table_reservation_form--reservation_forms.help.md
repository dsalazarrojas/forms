# Restaurant Table Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track information for table reservations at a restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill out the reservation date in the "Reservation Date" field. This field is used to track the date of the reservation.
2.  Select the reservation time in the "Reservation Time" field. This field is used to track the time of the reservation.
3.  Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field. This field is used to store the customer's name.
4.  Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field. This field is used to store the customer's email.
5.  Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This field is used to store the customer's phone number.
6.  Select the status of the reservation in the "Status" field. This field has three options: Cancelled, Confirmed, or Pending.
7.  Enter any additional reservation note in the "Reservation Note" field. This field is used to store any additional information about the reservation.
8.  Enter the restaurant reservation ID in the "Restaurant Reservation ID" field. This field is used to track the ID of the reservation.
9.  Enter the name of the restaurant in the "Restaurant Name" field. This field is used to store the name of the restaurant.
10. Enter the user ID in the "User ID" field. This field is used to track the user who made the reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reservation Date
*   **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the reservation. 

### Reservation Time
*   **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of the reservation. 

### Customer Name
*   **Customer Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name. 

### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address. 

### Phone Number
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number. 

### Status
*   **Status** (`reservation_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the reservation. Options: Cancelled, Confirmed, Pending. 

### Reservation Note
*   **Reservation Note** (`reservation_note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional reservation note. 

### Restaurant Reservation ID
*   **Restaurant Reservation ID** (`restaurant_reservation_id`, number, required: false): Enter the restaurant reservation ID. 

### Restaurant Name
*   **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the restaurant. 

### User ID
*   **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required: false): Enter the user ID. 

## Tips
*   Always ensure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any confusion or miscommunication. 
*   The customer's email address and phone number should be accurate and valid. 
*   The status of the reservation should be updated as soon as the reservation is made, cancelled, or confirmed. 
*   Any additional reservation notes should be kept up to date and accurate. 
*   The restaurant reservation ID and user ID should be accurate and valid. 
*   Double-check the restaurant name before submitting the form. 
*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
