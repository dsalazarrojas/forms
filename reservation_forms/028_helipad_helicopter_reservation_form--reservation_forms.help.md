# Helipad Helicopter Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Helipad Helicopter Reservation Form is a crucial tool for reserving a helicopter and ensuring smooth flight operations. The form captures essential information about the user, facility, venue, flight details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **User Information** page with your details, which includes your name, contact details, and other essential information.
2. Move on to the **Facility Information** page and provide the necessary details about the helicopter facility.
3. Fill out the **Venue Information** page with the venue details, if applicable.
4. In the **Flight Details** page, enter the necessary flight information, including the flight type and duration.
5. On the **Arrival and Departure** page, enter the dates and times you plan to arrive and depart.
6. If applicable, select the **Facilities** you require for your flight.
7. Add any additional **Notes** you'd like to include about your reservation.
8. Finally, enter your **Contact Phone** and **Email** details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information):** Enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Facility Information (Facility Information):** Provide the necessary details about the helicopter facility, including the type and location.
* **Venue Information (Venue Information):** Enter the venue details, if applicable.
* **Flight Details (Flight Details):** Enter the flight information, including the type and duration.
* **Arrival (Arrival):** Enter the date and time you plan to arrive.
* **Departure (Departure):** Enter the date and time you plan to depart.
* **Facilities (Facilities):** Select the facilities you require for your flight (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Notes (Notes):** Add any additional information about your reservation.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone):** Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email (Email):** Enter your email address.
