# Rail Ticket Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for rail ticket booking, including booking details and passenger information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form page by page, beginning with the first page.
2. Enter your booking details, including relevant information such as date, time, and seat preferences.
3. Proceed to the next page and enter your passenger information, including name, contact number, and any special requests.
4. Continue filling out the form page by page, making sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, text, required): Enter the details of your rail booking, including date, time, and seat preferences.
* **Passenger Details** (`passenger_details`, text, required): Enter the information of the passengers traveling with you, including name, contact number, and any special requests.

**Tips**

* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to avoid delays or issues with your rail ticket booking.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please enter them in the "Passenger Details" field.
* If you are unsure about any information or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact the rail ticket booking service for help.
