# Airport Transfers Turkey Air Ports - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate booking airport transfers in Turkey for your travel arrangements. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
2. Select your preferred airport for pick-up and drop-off from the options provided.
3. Specify your arrival or departure details.
4. Enter your contact information, including email address and phone number.
5. Provide details for your transfer preferences, such as arrival time, flight number, and vehicle preference.
6. Confirm whether you need a return transfer and provide the date and time if applicable.
7. Indicate your payment method.
8. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for reservation confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Airport**: Select the Turkish airport you need pick-up and drop-off from the options provided:
	+ Istanbul Ataturk
	+ Istanbul Sabiha Gokcen
	+ Ankara Esenboga
	+ Izmir Adnan Menderes
	+ Antalya
	+ Other (if not listed)
* **Arrival or Departure**: Indicate whether this booking is for arrival or departure:
	+ Arrival
	+ Departure
	+ Round Trip
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you are arriving in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter your expected arrival time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Flight Number**: Enter your flight number if known (not required).
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where you want to be picked up.
* **Dropoff Location**: Enter the location where you want to be dropped off.
* **Number of Passengers**: Indicate the number of people in your group.
* **Number of Pieces of Luggage**: Estimate the number of luggage pieces you have.
* **Vehicle Type Preference**: Select your preferred vehicle type:
	+ Standard Sedan
	+ SUV
	+ Van
	+ Minibus
	+ Any
* **Return Transfer Required**: Confirm whether you need a return transfer:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Return Transfer Date**: Enter the date for return transfer in the format MM/DD/YYYY, if applicable.
* **Return Transfer Time**: Enter the preferred return transfer time in 24-hour format (HH:MM), if applicable.
* **Hotel Name**: Enter the name of your hotel for transfer information.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requirements or requests.
* **Payment Method**: Select your payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Cash
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ PayPal
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm your acceptance of the booking conditions.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
