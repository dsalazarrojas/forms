# Venue Reservation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a venue reservation for an event. Please fill it out to provide the necessary information for the venue's management to evaluate and potentially book the requested date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information for each field, marked as `required` in the form.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure proper evaluation of your request.
3. Review each field explanation below for more details on how to complete each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue where you are requesting to hold your event. This field is crucial for venue identification and should be filled in with the exact name of the venue.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date for which you are requesting the venue reservation. This date should reflect the day you plan to hold the event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Select the start time for your event. This is when the event will begin, and the venue's management will evaluate if it conflicts with other events or is feasible for the requested date.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Select the end time for your event. This is when the event will end, and the venue's management will ensure it doesn't conflict with other events or the venue's operating hours.
* **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, text, required): Provide the address of the venue where you are requesting the reservation. This helps the venue's management to locate the venue and ensure it's suitable for your event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the nature of the event. This will help the venue's management to understand your event better and make an informed decision.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you are hosting. This helps the venue's management to better evaluate and potentially cater to your event's specific needs. Options include:
	+ Meeting
	+ Party
	+ Conference
	+ Other
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): If you know the expected number of attendees, please provide it. This helps the venue's management to ensure they can accommodate your event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): If you have a primary contact person for this event, please provide their name. This helps the venue's management to communicate with the right person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you have a primary contact person for this event, please provide their phone number. This helps the venue's management to contact you for further questions or clarifications.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If you have a primary contact person for this event, please provide their email address. This helps the venue's management to communicate with you directly.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, please provide the name of the organization. This helps the venue's management to understand your affiliation and make informed decisions.
