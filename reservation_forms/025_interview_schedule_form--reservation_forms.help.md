# interview_schedule_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interview schedule form is a tool used to collect information from applicants for an interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the "Applicant details" section.
2. Provide details about the interview in the "Interview details" section.
3. Add any additional comments if needed in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant details** (`title`, `text`, required): This section is where you will enter the applicant's details, such as name, job title, or any other relevant information.
* **Interview details** (`title`, `text`, required): This section is where you will enter details about the interview, such as date, time, and location.
* **Comments** (`title`, `text`, optional): This is an optional section where you can add any additional comments or notes about the applicant or interview.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out this form.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Applicant details" and "Interview details" sections.
* Use the "Comments" section to add any additional information that may be relevant to the interview.
