# Hotel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Booking Form is designed to collect information from users for hotel reservations, including check-in and check-out dates, room preferences, contact information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your check-in date in the "Check In Date" field.
2. Enter your check-out date in the "Check Out Date" field.
3. Select your preferred room type from the options provided.
4. Choose the room capacity that suits your needs.
5. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
6. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Provide any additional message or notes in the "Message" field.
10. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
11. Enter the amount you would like to pay in the "Payment Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to check-in.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to check-out.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of room you would like to book.
* **Room Capacity** (`room_capacity`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the capacity that suits your needs.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional message or notes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you would like to pay.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields to avoid any issues with your booking.
