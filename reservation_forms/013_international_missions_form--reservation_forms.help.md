# International Missions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Missions Form is a document used to gather information about international missions, allowing users to provide details about their trips abroad. This form is designed to be completed by travelers, travelers' organizations, or travel agents to provide essential information for planning and executing international missions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide the category of your international mission.
2.  Enter a brief description of your international mission.
3.  Enter the name of the person responsible for the mission.
4.  Enter the email address of the person responsible for the mission.
5.  Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the mission.
6.  Select the countries you plan to visit during your international mission.
7.  Select the states or provinces you plan to visit during your international mission.
8.  Enter the city or town where you plan to stay during your international mission.
9.  Enter your zip code.
10.  Choose a date for your international mission.
11.  Select a time for your international mission.
12.  Enter any additional notes or details about your mission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Required/Optional: Optional)**: This field is to categorize the nature of your international mission, whether it's for tourism, business, or educational purposes. You can select from two options: "Category" or "Other".
* **Description (Required/Optional: Optional)**: This field is for providing a brief description of your international mission. Enter any relevant details about your trip.
* **Name (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the mission.
* **Email (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the mission.
* **Phone (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the mission.
* **Country (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Select all the countries you plan to visit during your international mission.
* **State (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Select all the states or provinces you plan to visit during your international mission.
* **City (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter the city or town where you plan to stay during your international mission.
* **Zip (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter your zip code for the purpose of identifying the location of your stay.
* **Date (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Choose the date for your international mission.
* **Time (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Select a time for your international mission.
* **Note (Required/Optional: Optional)**: Enter any additional notes or details about your mission.
