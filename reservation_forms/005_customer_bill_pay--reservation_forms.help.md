# customer_bill_pay - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "customer_bill_pay" form is used to collect and process customer payment information, such as billing amounts, payment methods, and due dates. This form helps ensure accurate and timely payments for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer's name in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the `Customer Email` field.
3. Enter the billing amount in the `Billing Amount` field.
4. Select the payment method from the list (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, Amex) in the `Payment Method` field.
5. Select the payment type from the list (e.g., Credit, Debit, Cash) in the `Payment Type` field.
6. Enter the due date in the `Due Date` field.
7. Select the payment status from the list (e.g., Paid, Pending, Unpaid) in the `Payment Status` field.
8. Select the payment terms from the list (e.g., Net 15, Net 30, Other) in the `Payment Terms` field.
9. Enter the billing address in the `Billing Address` field.
10. Enter the billing city in the `Billing City` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name (required).
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address (required).
* **Billing Amount**: Enter the total amount due for the billing.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you will use (e.g., Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
* **Payment Type**: Choose the payment type (e.g., Credit, Debit, Cash).
* **Due Date**: Enter the date payment is due.
* **Payment Status**: Select the current payment status (e.g., Paid, Pending, Unpaid).
* **Payment Terms**: Choose the payment terms (e.g., Net 15, Net 30, Other).
* **Billing Address**: Enter the customer's billing address.
* **Billing City**: Enter the customer's billing city.
