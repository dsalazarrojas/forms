# Travel Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Registration Form Template is designed for travel agencies to collect information from clients for trip planning, management, and customer relationship purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
	* Enter your Email and Phone Number (optional).
2. Specify your travel information:
	* Choose your Travel Agency from the provided options.
	* Select the Type of Travel (Hotel or Cruise).
3. Enter trip specifics:
	* If traveling to a hotel, enter the Hotel Name.
	* If traveling on a cruise, enter the Cruise Name (if applicable).
4. Choose your cabin preferences:
	* Select the type of cabin you'd like to stay in (Double, Single, or Suite).
5. Provide additional information:
	* If necessary, enter an Emergency Contact Number.
	* Enter any Special Requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if available (optional).
* **Travel Agency**: Select your travel agency from the provided options.
* **Travel Type**: Choose between Hotel or Cruise for your travel type.
* **Hotel Name**: Enter the name of the hotel you'll be staying at.
* **Cruise Name**: Enter the name of the cruise you'll be taking, if applicable.
* **Cabin Types**: Choose the type of cabin you'd like to stay in (Double, Single, or Suite).
* **Hotel Bedding**: Select the type of bedding you'd like to have in your hotel room (King, Queen, or Twin).
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter a contact number for someone you'd like to be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Travel Preferences**: Enter any additional travel preferences you may have.
* **Client Preferences**: Enter any preferences you have regarding service or amenities during your trip.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you may have for your trip.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you arrive at your destination.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date you depart from your destination.
* **Travel Starting Point**: Enter the starting location of your trip.
* **Travel Ending Point**: Enter the ending location of your trip.
* **Travel Duration**: Enter the duration of your trip.
* **Hotel Rating**: Rate the quality of your hotel stay (1-5 stars).
* **Travel Document**: Enter any travel documents you may have (e.g., passport number).
* **Travel Agency Employees**: Enter any travel agency employees you'd like to contact.
* **Travel Assistance**: Enter any travel assistance you may need.
* **Travel Insurance**: Enter any travel insurance information.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter a contact for emergency purposes.
