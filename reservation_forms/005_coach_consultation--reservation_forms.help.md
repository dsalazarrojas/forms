# Coach Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by individuals who wish to schedule a consultation with a coach. The form gathers essential information to ensure a successful and safe consultation experience. Please review and complete this form carefully to ensure you receive the correct support for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, take a moment to read through each section carefully. If you have any issues or doubts during the form completion, please contact your coach for assistance. To complete the form:

1. Select the "Consultation Type" that best suits your goals and needs.
2. Choose a preferred date and time for your consultation.
3. Answer the health screening questions honestly to ensure a safe environment.
4. Provide any existing injuries or physical limitations.
5. Clearly state your goals for the consultation.
6. Indicate your current fitness level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultation Type** (`consultation_type`, required/optional): Select the focus of your session, such as Fitness Assessment, Technique Review, or Goal Setting. This will help the coach tailor the consultation to meet your specific needs.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for consultation updates or follow-up questions.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, required): Select a date that works best for you, taking into account any conflicts with other appointments or events.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, required): Choose a time that suits you, considering any scheduling constraints.
* **Health Screening Questions** (`section_health`, optional): Answer honestly about any COVID-19 symptoms or physical limitations to ensure a safe consultation environment.
* **Any Existing Injuries** (`existing_injuries`, optional): List any physical limitations or injuries to inform the coach about your current condition.
* **Goals for this Consultation** (`goals`, required): Clearly state what you hope to achieve during the consultation. This will help the coach tailor the session to meet your objectives.
* **Current Athlete Level** (`athlete_level`, required): Select your current fitness level, whether Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Elite, to help the coach understand your starting point.
