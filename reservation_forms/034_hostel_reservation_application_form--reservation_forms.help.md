# Hostel Reservation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in reserving a room at the hostel. Fill out the form to provide your reservation details and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the reservation details section with your information (Page 1).
2. Proceed to the payment details section and provide your payment information (Page 2).
3. Review your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Details (Page 1)**: 
  * Enter your first and last name as they appear on your identification documents.
* **Reservation Details (Page 1)**: 
  * Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Payment Details (Page 2)**: 
  * Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, cash, etc.).
* **Payment Details (Page 2)**: 
  * Enter the amount you are willing to pay for your reservation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your input before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
