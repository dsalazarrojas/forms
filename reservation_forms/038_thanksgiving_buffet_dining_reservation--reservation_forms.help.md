# thanksgiving_buffet_dining_reservation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Buffet Dining Reservation form is used to gather information from customers interested in dining at the buffet during the Thanksgiving holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date for your reservation using the "Thanksgiving Day" field.
2. Enter the number of guests attending the buffet using the "number_of_guests" field.
3. Enter your name using the "name" field.
4. Enter your email address using the "Contact Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number using the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Thanksgiving Day**: Choose the date for your reservation. Please select the date you would like to dine at the buffet.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the total number of people you plan to attend the buffet.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your reservation.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached regarding your reservation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached regarding your reservation.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct date for your reservation to avoid conflicts with other guests.
* If you need to make changes to your reservation, please contact us at the phone number you provided.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please let us know in the email or phone number you provide.
