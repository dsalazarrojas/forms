# Tour Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Reservation Form is designed to collect information about users' preferences and feedback about tour reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on the first page.
2. Select the tour packages you are interested in on the second page.
3. Provide any feedback or comments you have on the third page.
4. Add any additional comments or information on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your name, email, phone number, or other contact details.
* **Select Tour Packages (Select Tour Packages)**: Choose the tour packages you are interested in. 
* **Feedback Questions (Feedback Questions)**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about the tour.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Add any other information you want to share.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you have any special requests or questions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
