# Production Space Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Space Reservation Form is used to reserve production spaces for meetings, workshops, or presentations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \* in the field label) are filled in.
- Fill out the Reservation Details, Studio Details, Scheduling, and other relevant fields.
- Double-check that all dates and times are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your reservation, such as the purpose of the meeting.
* **Studio Details** (`studio_details`, text, optional): Provide additional details about the studio you are reserving, if necessary.
* **Scheduling** (`scheduling`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours reserved.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your reservation.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your reservation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your reservation.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your reservation.
* **Reservation Duration** (`reservation_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your reservation in hours.
* **Reservation Status** (`reservation_status`, select_one, optional): Set the status of your reservation to Awaiting, Confirmed, Cancelled, or Denied.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments related to your reservation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user assigned to your reservation.
* **Studio** (`studio`, select_one, optional): Select the studio you are reserving.
* **User** (`user`, select_one, optional): Select the user responsible for your reservation.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, select_one, optional): Select the room number.
* **Floor** (`floor`, select_one, optional): Select the floor.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, optional): Select the room type (Private or Shared).
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select any equipment you need during your reservation (e.g., Projector, Whiteboard, Sound System).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements.
* **Reserved Until** (`reserved_until`, date, optional): Enter the date until which your reservation is valid.
* **Reserved By** (`reserved_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select users who have reserved the space.
* **Reserved Date** (`reserved_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your reservation was made.
* **Reserved Time** (`reserved_time`, time, optional): Enter the time your reservation was made.
* **Reserved Status** (`reserved_status`, select_one, optional): Set the status of your reservation to Awaiting, Confirmed, Cancelled, or Denied.

Note: This form is intended for single-use purposes and should not be duplicated.
