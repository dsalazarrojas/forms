# Ski Resort Parking Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests visiting the ski resort, allowing them to reserve a parking spot in advance. This helps the resort's staff manage parking during peak seasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that the fields are accurate and complete.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information** (`guest_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Select your estimated arrival time at the resort.
* **Parking Duration** (`parking_duration`, number, optional): Choose how long you will be staying at the resort.
* **Parking Location** (`parking_location`, text, optional): Select the parking area where you would like to park.
* **Parking Details** (`parking_details`, text, optional): Provide any special details about your parking needs or preferences.
* **Advance Payment** (`advance_payment`, number, optional): If you wish to secure your parking spot, select an advance payment amount.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, provide them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate arrival and parking details to ensure efficient parking management.
- Advance payment is not mandatory but recommended to secure your spot.
- If you need to make any changes to your reservation, please contact us directly.
- We will respond to your submission within a reasonable timeframe.
