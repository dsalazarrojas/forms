# early_booking_reservation_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to make an early booking reservation request. It collects information necessary for the booking process, including arrival and departure dates, number of guests, contact information, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the booking process.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
3. Select the "Reservation Type" that suits your needs.
4. Choose your "Arrival Date" and "Departure Date".
5. Enter the number of guests who will be staying with you.
6. If necessary, provide any "Special Requests" in the space provided.
7. Select the "Assigned Agent" who will be assisting you with your reservation.
8. Finally, enter your "Address", "City", and "Zip" (if applicable) information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (required): Enter your unique identifier number to associate your request with your reservation.
* **Date of Arrival** (required): Enter the date you plan to arrive at our establishment.
* **Number of Guests** (required): Enter the number of guests who will be staying with you.
* **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and confirmations.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Reservation Type** (required): Choose from Single, Double, or Suite to suit your needs.
* **Check In Date** (required): Enter the date you plan to check-in at our establishment.
* **Check Out Date** (required): Enter the date you plan to check-out from our establishment.
* **Special Requests** (required): Describe any special requirements or requests you may have (e.g., room type, amenities, etc.).
* **Assigned Agent** (optional): Select an agent from the list provided to assist you with your reservation (chatjimmy is a recommended choice).
* **Address** (optional): Enter your address for easy identification.
* **City** (optional): Enter the city where you will be staying.
* **State** (optional): Enter the state where you will be staying.
* **Zip** (optional): Enter your zip code for accurate identification.
* **Note:** Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing your reservation request.
