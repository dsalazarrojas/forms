# Library Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Reservation Form is a tool used to reserve books in the library. This form helps library staff to quickly and efficiently collect the necessary information for processing the reservation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Reservation Information page with the relevant details about the book you would like to reserve.
2. Enter the book title, author, and publication date in the respective fields.
3. Choose the book's condition from the available options.
4. Select the book's status from the available options.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
7. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Information** (`library_reservation_form`, text, not required): This page provides a space for you to enter the details about the book you would like to reserve.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the book you would like to reserve.
* **Author** (`author`, text, not required): Enter the author of the book you would like to reserve.
* **Publication Date** (`publication_date`, date, not required): Enter the publication date of the book you would like to reserve.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to reserve the book for.
* **Book Condition** (`book_copy_condition`, select_one, not required): Choose the condition of the book you would like to reserve from the available options (New, Used, Damaged).
* **Book Status** (`book_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the status of the book you would like to reserve from the available options (Available, Unavailable, Reserved).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the book you would like to reserve.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have entered all necessary information accurately and completely.
* Review your form for any errors before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or need help with completing the form, please contact library staff.
