# Slot Reservation Cancellation Notice - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Slot Reservation Cancellation Notice form is used to notify the system of a cancelled slot reservation. This form is intended for authorized personnel to record the cancellation of a slot reservation, providing a clear explanation and reason for the cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a reason for the cancellation in the "Reason" field.
4. Enter the date of the cancelled slot in the "Date" field.
5. Enter the time of the cancelled slot in the "Time" field.
6. Select "Yes" if the cancellation is due to a service issue, or "No" otherwise in the "Service" field.
7. Enter the appointment ID associated with the cancelled slot in the "Appointment ID" field.
8. Select any applicable services that were not rendered in the "Service Type" field.
9. Enter a clear and concise explanation for the cancellation in the "Cancellation Reason" field.
10. If necessary, provide additional information in the "Additional Information" field.
11. Enter the email address of the team member responsible for the cancellation in the "Team" field.
12. Enter the phone number of the team member responsible for the cancellation in the "Phone" field.
13. Sign the form with your digital signature in the "Signature" field.
14. Provide any comments or notes about the cancellation in the "Comments" field.
15. If this is a requested cancellation, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Cancellation Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`text`, required): Enter your name here.
* **Last Name (2)** (`text`, required): Enter your surname here.
* **Reason (3)** (`text`, required): Provide a brief explanation for the cancellation of the slot reservation.
* **Date (4)** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the cancelled slot in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time (5)** (`time`, required): Enter the time of the cancelled slot in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Service (6)** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if the cancellation is due to a service issue, or "No" otherwise.
* **Appointment ID (7)** (`number`, required): Enter the unique ID of the cancelled slot.
* **Service Type (8)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select any services that were not rendered during the cancelled slot.
* **Cancellation Reason (9)** (`text`, required): Enter a clear and concise explanation for the cancellation.
* **Additional Information (10)** (`note`, required): If necessary, provide any additional information about the cancellation here.
* **Team (11)** (`email`, required): Enter the email address of the team member responsible for the cancellation.
* **Phone (12)** (`text`, required): Enter the phone number of the team member responsible for the cancellation.
* **Signature (13)** (`text`, required): Sign the form with your digital signature.
* **Comments (14)** (`note`, required): Provide any comments or notes about the cancellation here.
* **Cancellation Request (15)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" if this is a requested cancellation, or "No" otherwise.
* **Reason Code (16)** (`number`, optional): Enter a reason code for the cancellation.
* **Slot (17)** (`number`, optional): Enter the slot number of the cancelled slot.
* **User (18)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name here, or leave blank if you're using a shared account.
* **Team ID (19)** (`number`, optional): Enter the ID of the team member responsible for the cancellation.
* **User ID (20)** (`number`, optional): Enter the ID of the user who requested the cancellation.
* **Status (21)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the status of the cancelled slot, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Updated At (22)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Created At (23)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was created.
* **Updated By (24)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Created By (25)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.

## Tips

* Please provide clear and concise explanations for all fields.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all information is correct.
* Do not submit forms with incomplete or inaccurate information.
