# Makeup Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information, event details, and makeup preferences for a service order. By filling out this form, customers can provide necessary details for a personalized makeup service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Customer information** section with your name and contact details.
2. Provide details about the event, if relevant.
3. Select your makeup preferences for the service.
4. If you have any allergies or special requests, please let us know in the **Allergy information** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer information (Customer information, text, required)**: Please enter your name and contact details (phone number and email or address) so we can get in touch with you to confirm the order.
* **Event details (Event details, text, optional)**: This field is for providing any additional information about the event, such as the date, time, or location. If you don't have any specific event details, you can leave this field blank.
* **Makeup preferences (Makeup preferences, text, required)**: Choose the type of makeup service you require, e.g. wedding, prom, or everyday look.
* **Allergy information (Allergy information, text, optional)**: If you have any allergies or special requests (e.g. no gluten-free products, vegan-friendly), please let us know so we can accommodate your needs.
