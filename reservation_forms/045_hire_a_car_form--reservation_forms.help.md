# Hire A Car Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hire A Car Form is designed to collect information for car rental purposes, allowing you to gather details from users such as name, pickup and dropoff dates and times, and email address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "user_name" field.
2. Select the date for pickup from the calendar provided in the "pickup_date" field.
3. Choose the time for pickup from the time picker in the "pickup_time" field.
4. Enter the date for dropoff from the calendar provided in the "dropoff_date" field.
5. Choose the time for dropoff from the time picker in the "dropoff_time" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **user_name** (text, required: false)
Enter your name.

### **pickup_date** (date, required: false)
Select the date for pickup.

### **pickup_time** (time, required: false)
Choose the time for pickup.

### **dropoff_date** (date, required: false)
Select the date for dropoff.

### **dropoff_time** (time, required: false)
Choose the time for dropoff.

### **email** (email, required: false)
Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields to avoid errors.
* If you need to make changes, you can modify your submission at any time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
