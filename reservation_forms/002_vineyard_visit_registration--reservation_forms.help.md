# Vineyard Visit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register visitors for a vineyard visit. It helps us to collect essential information about you and your group, ensuring a personalized experience during your visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and group information.
2. Provide details about your visit, including the date and time you plan to visit.
3. Share your preferences with us so we can tailor your experience according to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Information (visitor_info)** (text, Optional): Please provide your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Visit Details (visit_details)** (text, Optional): Tell us about your planned visit, including the date and time you want to come to the vineyard.
* **Guest Preferences (guest_preferences)** (text, Optional): Share your preferences, such as any special requests or requirements, so we can accommodate your needs.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all fields that are marked as "required" to ensure a successful submission.
* You can add or remove fields as per your preference, but please note that the more information you provide, the better we can tailor your experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
