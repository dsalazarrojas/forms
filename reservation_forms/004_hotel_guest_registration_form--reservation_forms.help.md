# Hotel Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from hotel guests, including their names, contact details, stay dates, and check-in/check-out times. The collected data can be used for guest services, such as providing personalized services, sending reminders, or for billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the date of your stay in the **Stay Date** field.
6. Choose the time you plan to check-in and check-out in the **Checkin Time** and **Checkout Time** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Info**: This is a brief description of the guest, which can be left blank if not necessary.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: A valid email address is required to ensure we can contact you during your stay.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you quickly in case of an emergency.
* **Stay Date**: Select the date you plan to stay at our hotel.
* **Checkin Time**: Choose the time you plan to check-in at our hotel.
* **Checkout Time**: Choose the time you plan to check-out from our hotel.
* **Stay Date**: This field is used to keep a record of your stay dates.
* **Checkin Time**: This field is used to record your check-in time.
* **Checkout Time**: This field is used to record your check-out time. 

## Tips
* Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in the guest info field.
