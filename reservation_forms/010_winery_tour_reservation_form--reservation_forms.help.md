# Winery Tour Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winery Tour Reservation Form is used to book and schedule a tour at a local winery.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps outlined in the instructions above to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tour Type** (Wine Tasting Tour, Wine and Cheese Pairing, Vineyard Tour, optional): This field allows you to select the type of tour you would like to reserve.
* **First Name** (optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for tour updates and confirmations.
* **Start Date** (optional): Select the date you would like to take the tour.
* **Start Time** (optional): Select the time you would like to start the tour.
* **End Date** (optional): Select the date the tour should end.
* **End Time** (optional): Select the time the tour should end.
* **Guests** (optional): Indicate the number of guests you will be bringing on the tour.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for tour confirmations and updates.
* **Tour Details** (optional): Write any additional information that will help us tailor the tour to your preferences.
* **Special Requests** (optional): Write any special requests you may have for your tour, such as dietary restrictions or mobility needs.

Note: All fields are optional, but if you're planning to reserve a tour, please ensure that all required fields are filled.
