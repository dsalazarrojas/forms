# Gala Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a gala reservation, including the host's and guest's contact details, the date, time, and number of guests, as well as any additional information or message for the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Provide the host's name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter the guest's name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
4. Select a date and time for the reservation.
5. Specify the number of guests attending.
6. Add any additional message or instructions for the reservation.
7. Choose the method by which the guest was invited (e.g. social media, word of mouth, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Host Name** (`host_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the host.
* **Host Email** (`host_email`, **email**, required): Enter the host's email address.
* **Host Phone** (`host_phone`, **text**, required): Enter the host's phone number.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, **text**, optional): Enter the name of the guest (if different from the host).
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, **email**, required): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, **text**, optional): Enter the guest's phone number (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, **date**, required): Select a date for the reservation.
* **Time** (`time`, **time**, required): Select a time for the reservation.
* **Guests Amount** (`guests_amount`, **number**, required): Specify the number of guests attending.
* **Message** (`message`, **text**, optional): Add any additional information or instructions for the reservation.
* **Message Type** (`message_type`, **select_one**, required): Choose the type of message (e.g. text, email, phone).
* **Invite Method** (`invite_method`, **select_one**, required): Choose the method by which the guest was invited (e.g. social media, word of mouth, etc.).
* **Other Method** (`other_method`, **text**, optional): Enter any other method of invitation if not listed above.
