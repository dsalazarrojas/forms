# Annual Dinner Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Dinner Reservation Form is a form designed to collect information from guests attending an annual dinner event. It helps us ensure that we have accurate information about each guest, including their dietary restrictions, beverage preferences, and any special requests or needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your primary guest's name and contact information (email address, phone number).
2. Select the number of guests attending.
3. Choose your primary guest's entrée selection.
4. Select any additional entrée selections for guests not listed.
5. Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies.
6. Select your beverage preferences.
7. If applicable, indicate if you are celebrating a special occasion.
8. Enter any special requests or needs, such as seating restrictions.
9. Select a preferred contact method (email, phone, or text message).
10. If desired, send reminder messages before the dinner event.
10. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Primary Guest Name**: This is the main contact name for the reservation.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address where we can send confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: This is the phone number where we can contact you.
* **Organization or Company**: You can specify your company name, if applicable.
* **Dinner Date**: This is the date of the dinner event.
* **Dinner Time**: This is the time of the dinner event.
* **Number of Guests**: Please enter the total number of guests attending.
* **Table Location Preference**: Choose where you would like to sit (e.g., near the window, center of the room, quiet corner).
* **Primary Guest Entrée Selection**: Choose your main course from the options provided.
* **Entrée Selections for Additional Guests**: If you are bringing guests, describe their entrée selections here.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Specify any food allergies or preferences.
* **Beverage Preferences**: Select the beverages you would like to have available.
* **Celebrating Special Occasion**: If applicable, mark if this is a special celebration (e.g., birthday, anniversary).
* **Name of Person Being Honored**: If applicable, enter the name of the person being celebrated.
* **Seating Restrictions**: If necessary, indicate who needs to sit together or apart.
* **Accessibility or Other Needs**: Specify any special accommodations needed.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, text message).
* **Send Reminder Messages**: Mark if you would like to receive reminder messages before the dinner event.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Provide any other information or comments.
