# golf_course_player_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
The golf course player check-in form is used to collect essential information from players before their visit, ensuring that the course staff has all necessary details to manage their experience efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Player Information in the "Player Information" section.
	* Make sure to enter your name and any other relevant information as requested.
2. Select your Tee Time by choosing a valid date and time for your reservation.
3. Choose your Group Size from the available options (1, 2, 4, 5, 6, or 8).
4. Indicate if any services are required, such as a Food and Beverage Cart or Tee Cart, by selecting the corresponding options.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field if you have specific requests or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information (id: 1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant information as requested.
* **Tee Time (id: 2)**: Select a valid date and time for your reservation.
* **Group Size (id: 3)**: Choose the number of players in your group (1, 2, 4, 5, 6, or 8).
* **Service Required (id: 4)**: Select any additional services needed, such as a Food and Beverage Cart or Tee Cart.
* **Notes (id: 5)**: Add any additional information or requests you have, such as special requests or equipment needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the golf course staff for assistance.
* Please note that some fields are required, so be sure to fill them out accordingly to avoid any issues with your reservation.
