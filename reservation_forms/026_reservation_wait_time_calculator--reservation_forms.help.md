# Reservation Wait Time Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reservation Wait Time Calculator form is designed to help customers provide feedback on their wait time experience at our restaurant. It helps us understand how long customers had to wait before being seated and how their experience was impacted by our service performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your customer name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the size of your table to help us tailor our service.
3. Choose the time you arrived at the restaurant.
4. Rate your experience with our service performance (Good, Excellent, or Fair).
5. If you want to add any further comments, please use the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please fill in your name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Table Size** (`table_size`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the size of the table you were seated at (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you arrived at the restaurant.
* **Wait Time** (`wait_time`, number, optional): Enter how long (in minutes) you had to wait before being seated.
* **Wait Time Hours** (`wait_time_hours`, number, optional): This field is not used in this form; you can disregard it.
* **Service Performance** (`service_performance`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with our service performance (Good, Excellent, Fair).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your experience, if you wish.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further information.
