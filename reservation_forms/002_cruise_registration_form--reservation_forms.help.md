# cruise_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cruise registration form is designed to collect information from customers about their cruise travel plans. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring we can provide the best experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your contact information, including your name, contact number, and email.
- Provide your travel document number (if applicable).
- Share your itinerary details, including flight numbers and dates.
- Select your preferred cruise line.
- Choose your room type and room number.
- Enter your flight details, including flight numbers and times.
- Select your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (text): Enter your name for our records.
* **passenger_name** (text): Enter the name of the passenger(s) traveling with you.
* **date_of_birth** (date): Format: YYYY-MM-DD. Your date of birth for our records.
* **travel_document** (text): Provide your travel document number (if applicable).
* **itinerary** (text): Share your flight itinerary details.
* **contact_number** (text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **cruise_line** (select multiple): Choose your preferred cruise line.
* **travel_agency** (text): Enter the name of your travel agency.
* **special_requests** (note): Enter any special requests or needs.
* **arrival_date** (date): Format: YYYY-MM-DD. Your arrival date for our records.
* **departure_date** (date): Format: YYYY-MM-DD. Your departure date for our records.
* **room_type** (select one): Choose your room type.
* **room_number** (number): Enter your room number.
* **cabin_number** (number): Enter your cabin number.
* **cabin_name** (text): Enter your cabin name.
* **flight_number** (text): Enter your flight number.
* **flight_date** (date): Format: YYYY-MM-DD. Your flight date.
* **flight_time** (time): Enter your flight time.
* **arrival_time** (time): Enter your arrival time.
* **flight_carrier** (text): Enter your flight carrier name.
* **flight_number2** (text): Enter your second flight number.
* **flight_number3** (text): Enter your third flight number.
* **flight_number4** (text): Enter your fourth flight number.
* **payment_method** (select one): Choose your payment method.
