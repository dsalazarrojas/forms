# Noths_Ark_Restaurant_Reservation_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Noths_Ark_Restaurant_Reservation_Form" is designed to collect information from users for restaurant reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with your first name.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Input your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the Check In Date for your visit.
5. Choose the Check In Time for your visit.
6. Enter the Reservation Date for your reservation.
7. Enter the Number of Guests in your group.
8. If you have any special requests or notes, please enter them in the Special Request field.
9. Indicate the Reservation Status of your request.
10. Enter the Number of Children in your group, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, false)
	+ This field is for your first name.
* **Email** (2, email, false)
	+ This field is for your email address.
* **Phone** (3, text, false)
	+ This field is for your phone number.
* **Check In Date** (4, date, false)
	+ This field is for the date you plan to check in.
* **Check In Time** (5, time, false)
	+ This field is for the time you plan to check in.
* **Reservation Date** (6, date, false)
	+ This field is for the date of your reservation.
* **Number of Guests** (7, number, false)
	+ This field is for the number of people in your group.
* **Special Request** (8, note, false)
	+ This field is for any special requests or notes you have.
* **Reservation Status** (9, select_one, false)
	+ This field is for indicating the status of your reservation.
* **Number of Children** (10, number, false)
	+ This field is for the number of children in your group.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your reservation request is processed correctly. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the restaurant directly.
