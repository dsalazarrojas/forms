# Restroom Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for the rental of a restroom facility. It is intended for customers seeking to rent a restroom and for managers/administrators to collect and track rental requests and approvals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the event details in the "Event Info" field. This section is required.
2. Next, fill in the "Rental Period" field with the start and end dates of the rental period.
3. Enter the "Rental Location" field with the desired location for the rental.
4. Select the number of "Rental Units" needed from the options provided.
5. Enter the "Rental Price" field with the price per day or hour for the rental.
6. Choose the "Payment Method" from the options provided.
7. Select the "Approval Status" as either "Approved" or "Not Approved".
8. Fill in the "Contact Person" field with the person to contact in case of an emergency.
9. Enter the "Contact Email" field with the email address of the contact person.
10. Enter the "Contact Phone" field with the phone number of the contact person.
11. Enter the "Approval Person" field with the person who approved the rental.
12. Enter the "Approval Date" field with the date of approval.
13. Enter the "Approval Time" field with the time of approval.
14. Enter the "Approval Location" field with the location of approval.
15. Enter the "Customer Info" field with any additional information about the customer.
16. Enter the "Customer Address" field with the address of the customer.
17. Enter the "Customer City" field with the city of the customer.
18. Enter the "Customer State" field with the state of the customer.
19. Enter the "Customer Country" field with the country of the customer.
20. Enter the "Customer Postal Code" field with the post office code of the customer.
21. Enter the "Customer Notes" field with any additional notes about the customer.
22. Enter the "Customer Comments" field with any comments about the customer.
23. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Info** (Event Info, text, required): This field is for the customer to provide information about the event, such as the name and purpose of the event.
* **Rental Period** (Rental Period, number, required): This field is for the customer to input the start and end dates of the rental period.
* **Rental Location** (Rental Location, text, required): This field is for the customer to enter the desired location for the rental.
* **Rental Units** (Rental Units, select multiple, required): This field is for the customer to select the number of rental units needed.
* **Rental Price** (Rental Price, number, required): This field is for the customer to enter the price per day or hour for the rental.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select one, required): This field is for the customer to choose the payment method from the options provided.
* **Approval Status** (Approval Status, select one, required): This field is for the manager/administrator to select the approval status as either "Approved" or "Not Approved".
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): This field is for the customer to enter the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): This field is for the customer to enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): This field is for the customer to enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Approval Person** (Approval Person, text, required): This field is for the manager/administrator to enter the person who approved the rental.
* **Approval Date** (Approval Date, date, required): This field is for the manager/administrator to enter the date of approval.
* **Approval Time** (Approval Time, time, required): This field is for the manager/administrator to enter the time of approval.
* **Approval Location** (Approval Location, text, required): This field is for the manager/administrator to enter the location of approval.
* **Approval Comments** (Approval Comments, note, not required): This field is for the manager/administrator to enter any comments about the approval.
* **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter any additional information about themselves.
* **Customer Address** (Customer Address, text, required): This field is for the customer to enter their address.
* **Customer City** (Customer City, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their city.
* **Customer State** (Customer State, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their state.
* **Customer Country** (Customer Country, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their country.
* **Customer Postal Code** (Customer Postal Code, text, not required): This field is for the customer to enter their post office code.
* **Customer Notes** (Customer Notes, note, not required): This field is for the customer to enter any additional notes about themselves.
* **Customer Comments** (Customer Comments, note, not required): This field is for the customer to enter any comments about themselves.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): This field is for the customer to submit the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the customer support team.
