# Designer Entry Form Open Runway Video Series - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on various aspects of a video series for designers. It is intended to gather information on the category, description, company, partners, niche, format, episode structure, models, streets, style, target audience, episode length, episode count, show style, main streets, occasional streets, formal streets, other streets, other show style, other format, other niche, other targets, other partners, and other participants, other episodes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose a category for your video series from the provided options.
2. Provide a brief description of your video series.
3. Enter the name of your company.
4. List any partners involved in your project.
5. Select the niche or target audience of your video series.
6. Choose the format(s) of your video series (e.g., casual, formal, semi-formal, party).
7. Describe the structure of each episode.
8. Provide any additional information on models used.
9. Enter any additional information on streets.
10. Select the style(s) of your video series.
11. Enter any target audience information.
12. Enter the expected episode length.
13. Enter the number of episodes.
14. Select the style of your show.
15. Enter any main streets information.
16. Enter any occasional streets information.
17. Enter any formal streets information.
18. Enter any other streets information.
19. Enter any other show style information.
20. Enter any other format information.
21. Enter any other niche information.
22. Enter any other target information.
23. Enter any other partner information.
24. Enter any other participant information.
25. Enter any other episode information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select the category of your video series (e.g., Reservation Forms)
* **Description**: A brief description of your video series.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Partners**: List any partners involved in your project.
* **Niche**: Select the niche or target audience of your video series.
* **Format**: Select the format(s) of your video series (e.g., casual, formal, semi-formal, party).
* **Episode Structure**: Describe the structure of each episode.
* **Models**: Provide any additional information on models used.
* **Streets**: Enter any additional information on streets.
* **Style**: Select the style(s) of your video series.
* **Target Audience**: Enter any target audience information.
* **Episode Length**: Enter the expected episode length.
* **Episode Count**: Enter the number of episodes.
* **Show Style**: Select the style of your show.
* **Main Streets**: Enter any main streets information.
* **Occasional Streets**: Enter any occasional streets information.
* **Formal Streets**: Enter any formal streets information.
* **Other Streets**: Enter any other streets information.
* **Other Show Style**: Enter any other show style information.
* **Other Format**: Enter any other format information.
* **Other Niche**: Enter any other niche information.
* **Other Targets**: Enter any other target information.
* **Other Partners**: Enter any other partner information.
* **Other Participants**: Enter any other participant information.
* **Other Episodes**: Enter any other episode information.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and concise information for each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or enter a brief explanation.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to ask.
