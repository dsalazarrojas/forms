# Car Service Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect reservation details for car services. To complete this form, you will need to provide the required information for the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the **Requester name** field.
2. Provide a best number to reach you in the **Contact phone** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Contact email** field for confirmation and updates.
4. Select the type of service you require in the **Service type** field.
5. Enter the date of service in the **Preferred date** field.
6. (Optional) If applicable, enter the estimated pickup time in the **Preferred time** field.
7. Enter the exact pickup address in the **Pickup address** field.
8. Enter the drop off destination address in the **Drop off address** field.
9. If you have any special instructions or requirements, such as passenger mobility assistance, enter them in the **Special instructions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Requester Name
* **Requester name (Requester_name)** (`text`, **required**: true): Enter your full name as the person making the reservation.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact phone (Contact_phone)** (`text`, **required**: true): Provide a best number to reach you for confirmation and updates.

### Contact Email
* **Contact email (Contact_email)** (`email`, **required**: true): Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.

### Service Type
* **Service type (Service_type)** (`select_one`, **required**: true): Select the type of service you require from the options: **Departure**, **Arrival**, **Round trip**, or **Special request**.

### Preferred Date
* **Preferred date (Preferred_date)** (`date`, **required**: true): Enter the date of service.

### Preferred Time
* **Preferred time (Preferred_time)** (`time`, **required**: false): Estimated pickup time (if provided, optional).

### Pickup Address
* **Pickup address (Pickup_address)** (`text`, **required**: true): Enter the exact pickup location.

### Drop off Address
* **Drop off address (Drop off_address)** (`text`, **required**: true): Enter the destination address.

### Special Instructions
* **Special instructions or accessibility needs (Special_instructions)** (`text`, **required**: false): If you have any special requirements, such as passenger mobility assistance, enter them here.

**Tips**

* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct service type and provide the correct addresses for pickup and drop off.
* If you have any special instructions or requirements, do not forget to include them in the **Special instructions** field.
* If you need to make any changes to your reservation, please contact us directly.
