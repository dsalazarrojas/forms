# Photography Session Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for photography sessions. It ensures that the photographer has all the necessary details to provide an excellent experience for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number in the designated fields.
2. **Contact Info**: Provide your address, city, state, and zip code to help the photographer get in touch with you.
3. **Scheduling**: Specify your preferred date and time for the photography session.
4. **Session Details**: Describe the type of photography session you are interested in (e.g., portrait, wedding, etc.).
5. **Photographer Info**: If you have any specific photographer preferences or requirements, please mention them here.
6. **Additional Info**: Share any additional information about your session that the photographer should know.
7. **Payment Details**: If you have any payment-related questions or concerns, please specify them here.
8. **Session Preferences**: Specify any specific preferences or requirements you have for your session.
