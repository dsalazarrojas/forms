# Thanksgiving Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Reservation Form is used to collect information from customers for a Thanksgiving reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Customer Name" field with the name of the person reserving the table.
2. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
4. Select the date of the reservation from the calendar picker or enter it manually in the "Date" field.
5. Choose the time of the reservation from the dropdown menu in the "Time" field.
6. Enter the party size in the "Party Size" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Reservation Notes" field.
8. Select the specific time of the reservation from the dropdown menu in the "Reservation Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person reserving the table.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of the reservation from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the reservation from the dropdown menu.
* **Party Size** (`party_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of people in the party.
* **Reservation Notes** (`reservation_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the reservation.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, select_one, required): Select the specific time of the reservation from the dropdown menu.
 
Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
