# Reservation Credit Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reservation Credit Request Form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in receiving credit or refunds for their reservations. This form helps us understand their requirements and preferences for contact and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Determine whether you would like to request credit or not, and select the correct option (Yes/No). This will help us understand your initial request.
2. Provide your guest details (if applicable).
3. Specify the type of credit you are requesting (e.g., refund, credit towards a future stay, etc.).
4. Indicate your contact preferences (Yes/No), so we can tailor our communication to your needs.
5. Enter your contact information (email address and phone number).
6. Indicate any special requests you may have for your reservation (e.g., early check-in/checkout, specific room type, etc.).
7. Select the date and time of your preferred communication (for follow-up or resolution).
8. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
9. Optionally, enter your phone number for us to contact you by phone.
10. If you have any additional comments or requests, please use the "Message" field.
11. Finally, submit the form to send us your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Credit Request** (Yes/No, required): This field determines if you would like to request credit or not. Please select one of the options to indicate your preference.
* **Guest Details** (text, optional): If you are requesting credit on behalf of a guest, provide their details here.
* **Credit Request** (text, optional): Describe the type of credit you are requesting. For example, if you would like a refund, please specify the amount and reason.
* **Contact Preferences** (Yes/No, optional): Indicate how you would like to be contacted (by email or phone).
* **Contact Information** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you about your request.
* **Contact Information** (phone, optional): Provide your phone number if you would like us to contact you by phone.
* **Special Requests** (select multiple, required): Select any additional requests you have for your reservation (e.g., early check-in/checkout, specific room type, etc.).
* **Date Time** (date, required): Select the date and time when you would like to be contacted about your request.
* **Message** (note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please include them here.
* **Submit** (text, required): Finally, submit the form to send us your request.
