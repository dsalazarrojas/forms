# Coronavirus 19 Health Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients' health status, symptoms, contact history, travel history, and exposure risk factors related to the coronavirus 19.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer each question based on your current situation.
3. If you're unsure about any question, consult a healthcare professional for guidance.
4. Ensure you fill out all the fields, even if you're unsure or have no information to provide.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (1)
	+ This field is for your name and contact information.
* **Symptoms** (2)
	+ Describe any symptoms you're currently experiencing or have experienced in the last 24 hours.
* **Contact History** (3)
	+ List any close contacts you've had with others, including family members, friends, or colleagues.
* **Travel History** (4)
	+ If you've traveled recently, provide details about your destination, duration, and mode of transportation.
* **Medical History** (5)
	+ List any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies.
* **Exposure Risk** (6)
	+ Assess your risk of exposure to the virus based on your contact history and travel.
*	+ Select **High**, **Moderate**, or **Low** risk level.
* **Exposure Location** (7)
	+ If you're in an area where you may have been exposed to the virus, describe the location.
* **Exposure Timeframe** (8)
	+ If you're at a higher risk of exposure, select the timeframe when the exposure occurred.
* **Exposure Source** (9)
	+ If you know the source of your exposure (e.g., a specific person or location).
* **Travel Restrictions** (10)
	+ If you're subject to travel restrictions, indicate whether you're restricted or not.
* **Medical Attention** (11)
	+ If you've received medical attention, describe the situation.
* **Travel History Details** (12)
	+ Provide more details about your travel history, if necessary.
* **Symptoms Details** (13)
	+ Describe your symptoms in more detail.
* **Exposure Risk Reason** (14)
	+ If you've been exposed to the virus, describe the reason.
* **Medical Condition** (15)
	+ If you have a medical condition, describe it.
* **Medical Condition Details** (16)
	+ Provide more details about your medical condition.
* **Contact List** (17)
	+ If you have a list of close contacts, provide it.
* **Exposure Risk Factors** (18)
	+ If you have any risk factors that may increase your exposure, describe them.
* **Travel Details** (19)
	+ Provide details about your travel, if necessary.
* **Country** (20)
	+ If you've traveled to another country, select it.
* **Date** (21)
	+ If you've traveled recently, provide the date.
* **Time** (22)
	+ If you've traveled, provide the time.
* **Medical Attention** (23)
	+ If you've received medical attention, describe the situation.
* **Travel Location** (24)
	+ If you've traveled, provide the location.
* **Exposure Risk Factors Details** (25)
	+ If you have any risk factors that may increase your exposure, describe them.

## Tips

1. Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
2. If you're unsure about any question, consult a healthcare professional for guidance.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.
4. Ensure you fill out all the fields, even if you're unsure or have no information to provide.
