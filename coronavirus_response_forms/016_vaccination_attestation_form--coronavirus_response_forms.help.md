# Vaccination Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccination Attestation Form is designed to capture information about a patient's vaccination history. This includes details about the patient's medical practitioner, vaccination dates, medical record numbers, and locations.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately, taking care to fill in all required fields.
*   Ensure that all information is up-to-date and correct.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to avoid mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Information**: A form to capture patient details, such as name and date of birth.
    (patient\_info, text, optional)
*   **Medical Practitioner**: A form to capture the name of the patient's medical practitioner.
    (medical\_practitioner, text, optional)
*   **Vaccination Information**: A form to capture details about the patient's vaccination history.
    (vaccination\_info, text, optional)
*   **Vaccination Date**: A form to capture the date of the patient's most recent vaccination.
    (vaccination\_date, date, optional)
*   **Medical Record Number**: A form to capture the patient's medical record number.
    (medical\_record\_number, text, optional)
*   **Medical Record Location**: A form to capture the patient's medical record location.
    (medical\_record\_location, text, optional)
*   **Patient Signature**: A form to capture the patient's signature.
    (patient\_signature, time, optional)
*   **Practitioner Signature**: A form to capture the practitioner's signature.
    (practitioner\_signature, time, optional)

## Tips
*   Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to avoid mistakes.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your organization's relevant department for clarification.
