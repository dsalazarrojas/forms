# coronavirus_intake_form_for_massage_therapy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The coronavirus_intake_form_for_massage_therapy is a medical intake form designed to collect necessary information from patients who are interested in seeking massage therapy services. This form helps the medical professional reviewing it to assess the patient's medical history, current health status, and other relevant factors to provide safe and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. If a field is not required but relevant to your situation, fill it in if needed.
3. Review and submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **coronavirus_intake_form** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): A brief description of the patient's reason for seeking massage therapy services.
* **massage_therapist_info** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Information about the patient's massage therapist, if applicable.
* **medical_conditions** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): A list of any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns the patient is currently experiencing.
* **medical_history** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): A brief description of the patient's medical history, including past illnesses, surgeries, or medical procedures.
* **vaccination_status** (`5`, `select_one`, required: `false`): A selection of the patient's vaccination status, such as "Vaccinated", "Not vaccinated", or "Prefer not to answer".
* **fever** (`6`, `number`, required: `false`): The patient's current fever status, if applicable.
* **cough** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): A selection of the patient's current cough status, such as "Mild", "Moderate", or "Severe".
* **difficulty_breathing** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): A selection of the patient's current difficulty breathing status, such as "None", "Mild", "Moderate", or "Severe".
* **travel_history** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): A brief description of the patient's recent travel history, including destinations and dates.
* **medical_conditions** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Another field for listing any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns the patient is currently experiencing.
* **medications** (`11`, `text`, required: `false`): A list of any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **allergies** (`12`, `text`, required: `false`): A list of any allergies or sensitivities the patient is experiencing.
* **health_insurance** (`13`, `select_one`, required: `false`): A selection of whether the patient has health insurance or prefers not to answer.
* **emergency_contact** (`14`, `text`, required: `false`): Contact information for the patient's emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_number** (`15`, `text`, required: `false`): The phone number of the patient's emergency contact person.
* **doctor_name** (`16`, `text`, required: `false`): The name of the patient's primary care physician or doctor.
* **doctor_phone** (`17`, `text`, required: `false`): The phone number of the patient's primary care physician or doctor.
* **doctor_email** (`18`, `email`, required: `false`): The email address of the patient's primary care physician or doctor.
* **doctor_address** (`19`, `text`, required: `false`): The address of the patient's primary care physician or doctor.
* **medical_history** (`20`, `text`, required: `false`): Another field for describing the patient's medical history.
* **medical_history_details** (`21`, `text`, required: `false`): A field for providing more detailed information about the patient's medical history.
* **medical_procedure** (`22`, `text`, required: `false`): A field for listing any upcoming or recent medical procedures.
* **medical_procedure_details** (`23`, `text`, required: `false`): A field for providing more information about the patient's medical procedures.
* **consent** (`24`, `select_one`, required: `false`): A selection of whether the patient consents to treatment or not.
* **submit** (`25`, `select_one`, required: `false`): A selection to submit or cancel the form.

Please note that the fields marked with `required: false` are optional and may not be applicable to every patient.
