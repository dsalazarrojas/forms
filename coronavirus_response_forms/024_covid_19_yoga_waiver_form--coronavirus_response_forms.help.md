# Covid 19 Yoga Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information and obtain waivers from participants and emergency contacts during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately.**
2. **Review each section carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Participant Information

* **Participant Name** (1): Enter the name of the participant who is signing the waiver.
* **Contact Info** (2): Enter the contact information for the participant, including their email address or phone number.
* **Contact Phone** (3) (Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person, if different from the participant's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (4) (Optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (5) (Optional): Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person.

### Page 2: Signature

* **Participant Signature** (7): Sign and date the participant's signature to acknowledge they understand the risks associated with COVID-19.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (8) (Optional): Sign and date the emergency contact person's signature, if they are providing emergency contact information.

### Page 3: Acknowledgement and Consent

* **Waiver Acknowledgement** (9): Read and understand the COVID-19 waiver, acknowledging the risks associated with the virus and taking responsibility for your own health.
* **Emergency Contact Consent** (10) (Optional): Read and understand the emergency contact's consent, acknowledging their role as an emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Make sure to sign and date your signature if you are a participant or emergency contact.
* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the yoga studio staff for clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for any medical or health advice.
