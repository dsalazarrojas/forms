# COVID 19 Vaccination Eligibility Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an individual's characteristics, medical history, and living situation to determine their eligibility for COVID-19 vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information. All fields are marked as required.
2. Select the correct options for each field from the provided choices.
3. Make sure to provide supporting documentation if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Certification Date** (id: 1, required/optional: true): Date of certification. Enter the date you are filling out this form.
* **First Name** (id: 2, required/optional: true): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 3, required/optional: true): Enter your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 4, required/optional: true): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (id: 5, required/optional: true): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Phone Number** (id: 6, required/optional: true): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (id: 7, required/optional: true): Enter your contact email address.
* **Home Address** (id: 8, required/optional: true): Enter your street address.
* **City** (id: 9, required/optional: true): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (id: 10, required/optional: true): Enter the state where you live.
* **ZIP Code** (id: 11, required/optional: true): Enter your postal code.
* **What is your age category** (id: 12, required/optional: true): Select your age category from the options provided.
* **Are you a healthcare worker** (id: 13, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you work in the healthcare industry, 'False' otherwise.
* **Describe your healthcare role** (id: 14, required/optional: false): If you answered 'True' to the previous question, provide a description of your healthcare role.
* **Are you an essential worker** (id: 15, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you work in an essential industry, 'False' otherwise.
* **What type of essential work do you do** (id: 16, required/optional: false): If you answered 'True' to the previous question, select the type of essential work you do.
* **Do you have any underlying medical conditions** (id: 17, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you have any medical conditions that may impact your eligibility.
* **Please specify your medical conditions** (id: 18, required/optional: false): If you answered 'True' to the previous question, provide a description of your medical conditions.
* **Do you live in a long-term care facility** (id: 19, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you live in a long-term care facility, 'False' otherwise.
* **Do you live in a correctional facility** (id: 20, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you live in a correctional facility, 'False' otherwise.
* **Are you currently experiencing homelessness** (id: 21, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you are homeless, 'False' otherwise.
* **Do you have a disability** (id: 22, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you have a disability, 'False' otherwise.
* **Do you live with someone who is high-risk** (id: 23, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you live with someone who is high-risk, 'False' otherwise.
* **Are you currently pregnant** (id: 24, required/optional: false): Select 'True' if you are currently pregnant, 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you previously tested positive for COVID-19** (id: 25, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you have previously tested positive for COVID-19, 'False' otherwise.
* **When did you test positive** (id: 26, required/optional: false): If you answered 'True' to the previous question, enter the approximate date you tested positive.
* **What is your current COVID-19 vaccination status** (id: 27, required/optional: true): Select your current vaccination status from the options provided.
* **Based on your responses, you may be eligible under which category** (id: 28, required/optional: true): This field will be automatically populated based on your answers to the previous questions.
* **Do you have supporting documentation** (id: 29, required/optional: true): Select 'True' if you have supporting documentation, 'False' otherwise.
* **What documentation can you provide** (id: 30, required/optional: false): If you answered 'True' to the previous question, select the type of documentation you have.
* **I certify that the information provided is true and accurate to the best of my knowledge** (id: 31, required/optional: true): Select 'True' to confirm the truthfulness of the information provided.
* **Signature Date** (id: 32, required/optional: true): Date you sign this form.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
- Make sure to provide supporting documentation when necessary.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
