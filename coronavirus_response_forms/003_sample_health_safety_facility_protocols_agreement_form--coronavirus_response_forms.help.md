# Sample Health Safety Facility Protocols Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's experience with health safety protocols in a facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure you have not previously submitted this information for the same facility.
3. Review each section carefully to ensure accurate and complete submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): The name of the facility where you work or visit.
  - Please enter the name of the facility, e.g., "ABC Company HQ".
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Your name as an employee or visitor.
  - Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
- **Role** (`role`, text, required): Your role or position in the facility.
  - Please enter your job title or role in the facility, e.g., "Sales Manager" or "Facility Manager".
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your work email address.
  - Please enter your work email address as used by your company or organization.
- **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required): The address of the facility.
  - Please enter the street address of the facility, including the building number and postal code.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): The phone number of the facility.
  - Please enter the phone number of the facility, including the area code.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): The name of the person you would contact in case of an emergency.
  - Please enter the name of the person who would be contacted in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): The relationship between you and the emergency contact.
  - Please select the relationship between you and the emergency contact, e.g., "Child", "Spouse", "Parent", or "Other".
- **Facility Safety Protocols** (`facility_safety_protocols`, text, required): A description of the facility's safety protocols.
  - Please enter a brief description of the facility's safety protocols, e.g., "Follow all OSHA guidelines".
- **Employee Safety Protocols** (`employee_safety_protocols`, text, required): A description of your personal safety protocols.
  - Please enter a brief description of your personal safety protocols, e.g., "Wear PPE when working with hazardous materials".
- **Safety Protocol Review Date** (`safety_protocol_review_date`, date, required): The date of the last review of the safety protocols.
  - Please enter the date of the last review of the safety protocols, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **Emergency Contact Protocol Review Date** (`emergency_contact_protocol_review_date`, date, required): The date of the last review of the emergency contact protocol.
  - Please enter the date of the last review of the emergency contact protocol, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **Emergency Contact Protocol Review By** (`emergency_contact_protocol_review_by`, text, required): The person who reviewed the emergency contact protocol.
  - Please enter the name of the person who reviewed the emergency contact protocol.
- **Emergency Contact Protocol Reviewed By** (`emergency_contact_protocol_reviewed_by`, text, required): The person who reviewed the emergency contact protocol.
  - Please enter the name of the person who reviewed the emergency contact protocol.
- **Facility Protocols Revision Comments** (`facility_protocols_revision_comments`, text, optional): Any comments on the facility's safety protocols.
  - Please enter any comments or suggestions on the facility's safety protocols.
- **Review By** (`review_by`, text, optional): The person who reviewed the protocols.
  - Please enter the name of the person who reviewed the protocols.
- **Facility Safety Protocol Review By** (`facility_safety_protocol_review_by`, text, optional): The person who reviewed the facility's safety protocols.
  - Please enter the name of the person who reviewed the facility's safety protocols.
- **Facility Safety Protocol Review Date** (`facility_safety_protocol_review_date`, date, optional): The date of the review of the facility's safety protocols.
  - Please enter the date of the review of the facility's safety protocols, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
- **Facility Safety Protocol Reviewed By** (`facility_safety_protocol_reviewed_by`, text, optional): The person who reviewed the facility's safety protocols.
  - Please enter the name of the person who reviewed the facility's safety protocols.
- **Facility Safety Protocol Revision Comments** (`facility_safety_protocol_revision_comments`, text, optional): Any comments on the facility's safety protocols.
  - Please enter any comments or suggestions on the facility's safety protocols.
- **Facility Evacuation Plan** (`facility_evacuation_plan`, text, optional): A description of the facility's evacuation plan.
  - Please enter a brief description of the facility's evacuation plan.
- **Facility Emergency Contact Plan** (`facility_emergency_contact_plan`, text, optional): A description of the facility's emergency contact plan.
  - Please enter a brief description of the facility's emergency contact plan.
- **Facility Emergency Contact** (`facility_emergency_contact`, text, optional): The emergency contact person's name.
  - Please enter the name of the person who would be contacted in case of an emergency.
- **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, optional): The address of the facility.
  - Please enter the street address of the facility, including the building number and postal code.
- **Facility Safety Inspection Frequency** (`facility_safety_inspection_frequency`, text, optional): The frequency of the facility's safety inspections.
  - Please enter the frequency of the facility's safety inspections, e.g., "Monthly" or "Quarterly".
