# COVID 19 Daily Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Daily Health Screening Form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess an individual's health status and fitness to enter certain areas or facilities during the COVID-19 pandemic. This form gathers information about the user's symptoms, exposure history, and recent activities to ensure their safety and the safety of others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have completed this form only once for each screening occasion.
2. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing truthful information about your health status.
3. Answer each question based on your knowledge and understanding of your current health situation.
4. Submit the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Screening Date** (`screening_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the screening.
* **Screening Time** (`screening_time`, Time, Required): Enter the time of the screening.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **ID or Badge Number** (`id_number`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter your ID or badge number for verification purposes.
* **Department or Area** (`department_area`, Text, Optional): Enter the department or area you belong to.
* **Body Temperature** (`temperature`, Number, Required): Enter your current body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Temperature Screening Result** (`temperature_pass`, Select One, Required): Select 'Passed (Below 100.4F)' if your temperature is below 100.4F or 'Failed (100.4F or Above)' if it's above.
* **Fever or Chills in Past 24 Hours** (`fever`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you have experienced fever or chills in the past 24 hours.
* **Persistent Cough** (`cough`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you have a persistent cough.
* **Shortness of Breath or Difficulty Breathing** (`shortness_of_breath`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you experience shortness of breath or difficulty breathing.
* **Fatigue or Malaise** (`fatigue`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you experience fatigue or malaise.
* **Loss of Taste or Smell** (`loss_of_taste_smell`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you have lost your sense of taste or smell.
* **Headache or Body Aches** (`headache`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you experience a headache or body aches.
* **Exposed to Confirmed COVID-19 Case in Past 14 Days** (`known_exposure`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you have been exposed to a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days.
* **Close Contact with Someone with Symptoms** (`close_contact`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you have had close contact with someone who has symptoms.
* **Recent Travel or Visited High-Risk Area** (`travel`, Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you have recently traveled or visited a high-risk area.
* **Screening Result** (`cleared`, Select One, Required): Select 'Cleared to Enter' if you are cleared to enter after screening, 'Denied Entry' if you are not cleared, or 'Refer to Medical' if you need further medical evaluation.
* **Screener Name** (`screener_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the screener who conducted the health screening.
