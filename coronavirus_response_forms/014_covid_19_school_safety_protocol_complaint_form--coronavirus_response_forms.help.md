# COVID 19 School Safety Protocol Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The COVID-19 School Safety Protocol Complaint Form is designed to collect information from individuals who have experienced or witnessed a safety concern related to COVID-19 in a school setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Please review each section carefully and ensure that the information you provide is accurate and detailed.
2.  Complete all required fields, as indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3.  If you have any attachments or evidence to support your complaint, please attach them to the form.
4.  Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send it to the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Complaint Date*:** Please enter the date you experienced or witnessed the safety concern.
*   **Your Full Name*:** Please enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.
*   **Your Email*:** Please enter your contact email address so that the school can follow up with you.
*   **Your Phone*:** Please enter your contact phone number so that the school can contact you.
*   **Your Relationship to the School*:** Please select the category that best describes your relationship to the school (Parent or guardian, Student, Teacher or staff, Administrator, Community member, or Other).
*   **Student Name:** If you are submitting a complaint on behalf of a student, please enter the student's name.
*   **Student Grade Level:** If you are submitting a complaint on behalf of a student, please select the student's grade level.
*   **School Name*:** Please enter the name of the school where the safety concern occurred.
*   **Date of Incident*:** Please enter the date the safety concern occurred.
*   **Time of Incident:** If possible, please enter the time the safety concern occurred.
*   **Location of Incident*:** Please enter the location where the safety concern occurred.
*   **Category of Complaint*:** Please select the primary category of your complaint from the list below:
    *   Mask policy violation
    *   Social distancing violation
    *   Inadequate cleaning or sanitation
    *   Poor ventilation
    *   Lack of health screening
    *   Inadequate contact tracing
    *   Failure to report positive cases
    *   Discrimination related to COVID-19
    *   Retaliation for raising concerns
    *   Other safety concern
*   **Individuals Involved*:** Please enter the names or descriptions of individuals involved in the incident.
*   **Detailed Description of Complaint*:** Please provide a detailed description of the incident or issue you are reporting.
*   **Have you reported this issue before?*:** Please indicate if you have previously reported this issue.
*   **What was the response to previous reports?*:** If you have previously reported this issue, please describe the response you received.
*   **Were there any witnesses?*:** Please select whether there were any witnesses to the incident.
*   **Witness Information:** If there were witnesses, please enter their names and contact information.
*   **Do you have any evidence to support your complaint?*:** If you have evidence, such as photos or documents, please indicate that you have them.
*   **What outcome are you seeking?*:** Please describe the outcome you desire from the school's response to your complaint.
*   **Would you like to remain anonymous?*:** Please indicate whether you prefer to remain anonymous.
*   **How should we contact you?*:** Please select your preferred contact method.
*   **How urgent is this complaint?*:** Please select the urgency level of your complaint.
*   **Additional Comments:** Please enter any additional information that may be helpful to the school.
*   **Signature Date*:** Please enter the date you submitted this complaint.

## Tips

*   If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please do not hesitate to contact the school.
*   Please provide accurate and detailed information to support your complaint.
*   This form is not intended for anonymous complaints; however, you have the option to request anonymity.
*   The school will follow up with you to ensure that your complaint is addressed and to keep you updated on the status of your complaint.
