# Volunteer COVID 19 Self Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your health status and travel history in relation to COVID-19. The form is intended to identify potential risks and take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer each question as accurately and completely as possible.
2.  If you are unsure about any question, please choose the "Other (please specify)" option and provide additional information.
3.  If you have any further information or questions, please provide it in the "Additional Information" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required: false)
    Please provide your name, phone number, and email address.
*   **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required: false)
    Describe your recent travel history, including any countries or cities you have visited, dates, and mode of transportation used.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false)
    If you have been in contact with anyone diagnosed with COVID-19 or are concerned about exposure, describe the circumstances.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required: false)
    Check the boxes that correspond to any symptoms you have experienced in the past few days, including fever, cough, shortness of breath, etc.
*   **Other Symptoms** (`other_symptoms`, text, required: false)
    If you have experienced any other symptoms that are not listed, please describe them.
*   **Health Information** (`health_info`, text, required: false)
    Describe your current health status, including any pre-existing medical conditions.
*   **Additional Health Information** (`health_info_2`, text, required: false)
    Provide any additional information about your health status or concerns.
*   **Contact Other Information** (`contact_other_info`, text, required: false)
    If you have any contact information for medical professionals or emergency contacts, please provide it.
*   **Travel History 2** (`travel_history_2`, text, required: false)
    Describe any additional travel history or plans.
*   **Health Status 1** (`health_status`, text, required: false)
    Describe your current health status.
*   **Health Status 2** (`health_status_2`, text, required: false)
    Describe your overall health status and any concerns you may have.
*   **Health Status 3** (`health_status_3`, text, required: false)
    If you have any further concerns about your health, describe them.
*   **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, required: false)
    Describe any specific details about your recent or upcoming travel plans.
*   **Additional Health Information 1** (`health_info_3`, text, required: false)
    Provide any additional information about your health status.
*   **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, text, required: false)
    Describe your vaccination status, including any COVID-19 vaccinations.
*   **Additional Health Information 2** (`health_info_4`, text, required: false)
    Provide any additional information about your health status.
*   **Travel History 3** (`travel_history_3`, text, required: false)
    Describe any additional travel history or concerns.
*   **Health Status 4** (`health_status_4`, text, required: false)
    Describe your current health status and any concerns.
*   **Additional Health Information 3** (`health_info_5`, text, required: false)
    Provide any additional information about your health status.
*   **Test Results** (`test_results`, text, required: false)
    If you have recently been tested for COVID-19, describe the results.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false)
    Please submit your completed form.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
*   If you are unsure about any question, choose the "Other (please specify)" option and provide additional information.
*   If you have any further information or questions, please provide it in the "Additional Information" field at the end of the form.
