# COVID 19 Symptom Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Symptom Questionnaire is a form used to collect information about your symptoms, recent exposure, and test results related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name in the **Respondent Name** field.
2. Select the current date in the **Date of Screening** field.
3. If you have a contact phone number, include it in the **Contact Phone Number** field.
4. Answer the questions about your symptoms and recent exposure to COVID-19.
5. If you have a COVID-19 test result, provide it in the **COVID Test Result** field.
6. If you have any additional information or concerns, enter them in the **Additional Information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Screening**: Select the current date.
* **Contact Phone Number**: If you have a contact phone number, enter it. Please include your country code if you are outside your local area.
* **Have you had a fever in the last 14 days?**: Answer 'True' if you have had a fever of 38C or higher.
* **Do you have cough or shortness of breath?**: Select 'True' if you are experiencing either of these symptoms.
* **Recent exposure to a confirmed COVID case**: Answer 'True' if you have had close contact within 14 days.
* **COVID test result if available**: Select 'Positive' if you have tested positive, 'Negative' if you have tested negative, or 'Not tested' if you have not been tested.
* **Additional information**: Enter any other concerns or relevant history you think is important to share.
