# COVID 19 Vaccine Registration, Consent And Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users understand the form's requirements and how to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your full name as required by the "Patient Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Provide your street address in the "Street Address" field.
6.  Enter your city, state, and ZIP code in the "City, State, ZIP" field.
7.  Select your current vaccination status from the "Vaccination Status" field.
8.  Choose the dose of vaccination you are seeking in the "Dose Being Sought" field.
9.  If applicable, select your preferred vaccine type from the "Vaccine Type Preference" field.
10.  If applicable, list any known allergies or medical conditions that may affect your vaccination in the "Known Allergies" and "Medical Conditions" fields.
11.  List any current medications you are taking in the "Current Medications" field.
12.  Confirm if you are pregnant or breastfeeding in the "Pregnant or Breastfeeding" field.
13.  Confirm if you have received any other vaccines in the past 14 days in the "Other Vaccines in Past 14 Days" field.
14.  Confirm if you currently have a fever or illness in the "Currently Have Fever or Illness" field.
15.  Confirm that you understand the benefits and risks of the vaccination in the "Understand Benefits and Risks" field.
16.  Select "I consent" to confirm your agreement to receive the COVID-19 vaccination.
17.  Choose your preferred appointment date in the "Preferred Appointment Date" field.
18.  Choose your preferred appointment time in the "Preferred Appointment Time" field.
19.  Finally, enter your electronic signature in the "Electronic Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Full Name**: Enter your full name as required for registration purposes.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
*   **City, State, ZIP**: Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
*   **Vaccination Status**: Select your current vaccination status.
*   **Dose Being Sought**: Choose the dose of vaccination you are seeking.
*   **Vaccine Type Preference**: Select your preferred vaccine type, if applicable.
*   **Known Allergies**: If you have any known allergies, list them in this field.
*   **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may affect your vaccination, if applicable.
*   **Current Medications**: List any current medications you are taking.
*   **Pregnant or Breastfeeding**: Confirm your pregnancy or breastfeeding status.
*   **Other Vaccines in Past 14 Days**: Confirm if you have received any other vaccines in the past 14 days.
*   **Currently Have Fever or Illness**: Confirm if you currently have a fever or illness.
*   **Understand Benefits and Risks**: Confirm that you understand the benefits and risks of the vaccination.
*   **I Consent to COVID-19 Vaccination**: Select "I consent" to confirm your agreement to receive the COVID-19 vaccination.
*   **Preferred Appointment Date**: Choose your preferred appointment date.
*   **Preferred Appointment Time**: Choose your preferred appointment time.
*   **Electronic Signature**: Enter your electronic signature in this field.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that everything is correct.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare provider or staff for assistance.
