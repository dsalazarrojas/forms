# COVID 19 Phase 3 Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The COVID 19 Phase 3 Vaccination Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from potential participants in the Phase 3 vaccine program. This form helps assess eligibility, collect medical history, and obtain consent from individuals who meet the criteria.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form carefully, section by section.
2. Answer each question truthfully, as your eligibility and medical history are being assessed.
3. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and implications of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Age**: Enter your current age, as the Phase 3 program includes ages 16-39 and certain essential workers.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Street Address**: Enter your full street address.
* **City**: Enter the name of the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Which Phase 3 category applies to you?**: Select one of the three options: Age 16-39 years, Essential worker - moderate exposure risk, or Both.
* **Essential Industry (if applicable)**: If you selected Essential worker, choose the industry category that applies to you (e.g., Food service, Hair salon and barbershop, etc.).
* **Job Title**: If you selected Essential worker, enter your job title.
* **Do you have underlying health conditions?**: Select "True" if you have any health conditions, and "False" otherwise.
* **List your health conditions**: If you selected "True", list your health conditions.
* **Known Allergies**: If you have any allergies, list them.
* **Current Medications**: If you are taking any medications, list them.
* **Previous COVID-19 vaccine doses**: Select how many doses of the COVID-19 vaccine you have received (None, 1 dose, 2 doses, or 3+ doses).
* **I confirm I meet Phase 3 eligibility criteria**: Select "True" if you understand and meet the eligibility criteria.
* **I consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine**: Select "True" if you agree to receive the vaccine.
* **I have received vaccine information and understand it**: Select "True" if you have read and understand the vaccine information provided.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any question, contact the registration team for clarification.
* This form is a critical part of the registration process; fill it out carefully and honestly to ensure you receive proper assessment and care.
