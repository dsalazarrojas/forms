# Covid Screening Questionnaire For Visitors - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid Screening Questionnaire for Visitors is designed to gather information about potential COVID-19 exposure and symptoms in order to identify potential risks and take necessary precautions to ensure the safety of all visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text areas.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the visitor's contact information, which will be used to communicate with them about any next steps or follow-up questions.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please describe any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies that may affect your health.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): List any symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Travel Information** (`travel_info`, text, optional): Provide information about your recent travel history, including any countries or cities visited.
* **Exposure Risk** (`exposure_risk`, text, optional): Describe any potential exposure to COVID-19, such as close contact with someone who has tested positive.
* **Contact Tracing Info** (`contact_trace_info`, text, optional): If you have been in contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID-19, provide their name and contact information.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Indicate what you plan to do next, such as getting tested or self-isolating.
* **Medical Facilities** (`medical_facilities`, text, optional): Describe any medical facilities or treatment you have undergone.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide emergency contact information in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you are experiencing any medical conditions related to COVID-19.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed information about your travel history.
* **Exposure History** (`exposure_history`, text, optional): Describe any history of exposure to COVID-19.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, text, optional): Provide any test results, such as a positive or negative test.
* **Next Steps 2** (`next_steps2`, text, optional): Indicate what you plan to do next.
* **Test Results 2** (`test_results2`, text, optional): Provide any additional test results.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history2`, text, optional): Describe any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies.
* **Symptoms 2** (`symptoms2`, text, optional): List any additional symptoms.
* **Exposure Risk 2** (`exposure_risk2`, text, optional): Describe any potential exposure to COVID-19.
* **Emergency Contact Info 2** (`emergency_contact_info2`, text, optional): Provide emergency contact information.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition2`, text, optional): If you are experiencing any medical conditions related to COVID-19.
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info2`, text, optional): This field is similar to the "Contact Information" field.
* **Exposure History 2** (`exposure_history2`, text, optional): Describe any history of exposure to COVID-19.
* **Travel Information 2** (`travel_info2`, text, optional): Provide detailed information about your travel history.
* **Exposure Risk 3** (`exposure_risk3`, text, optional): This field is similar to "Exposure Risk".
* **Test Results 3** (`test_results3`, text, optional): This field is similar to "Test Results".
