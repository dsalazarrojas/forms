# Day Camp Staff Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Day Camp Staff Health Screening Form is a tool used by day camps to collect health information from their staff members. This information is crucial in maintaining a safe and healthy environment for both staff and campers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your staff name in the "Staff Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Staff Email" field.
3. Describe your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
4. List any symptoms you are experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
5. Share your travel history in the "Travel History" field.
6. Indicate your vaccination status in the "Vaccination Status" field.
7. List any medication allergies in the "Medication Allergies" field.
8. Confirm your first aid training status in the "First Aid Training" field.
9. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
10. Describe any medical conditions in the "Medical Condition" field.
11. Share your medical certification status in the "Medical Certification" field.
12. Confirm your emergency medical certification status in the "Emergency Medical Certification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as a staff member of the day camp.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Share your medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or surgeries.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required/optional): List any symptoms you are currently experiencing.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required/optional): Share your travel history, including any recent travel to high-risk areas.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, text, required/optional): Indicate if you are fully vaccinated or have any vaccinations that are up-to-date.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, required/optional): List any medications you are allergic to.
* **First Aid Training** (`first_aid_training`, text, required/optional): Confirm if you have completed first aid training.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): Describe any current or past medical conditions.
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, text, required/optional): Share your medical certification status.
* **Emergency Medical Certification** (`emergency_medical_certification`, text, required/optional): Confirm if you have emergency medical certification.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure the safety of yourself and others.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- This form is a mandatory requirement for all staff members to complete.
- Completed forms will be kept confidential and only accessed by authorized personnel.
