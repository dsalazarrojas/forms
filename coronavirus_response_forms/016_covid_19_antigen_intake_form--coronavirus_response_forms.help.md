# Covid 19 Antigen Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and medical history related to COVID-19 antigen testing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Please enter your name, date of birth, and contact information (phone number, email, or address).
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Medical History**: Describe any previous medical conditions, allergies, or surgeries.
* **Vaccination Status**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have received any COVID-19 vaccines.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any ongoing medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, etc.
* **Medication Use**: Describe any current medications you are taking.
* **Travel History**: Report any travel history related to COVID-19 outbreaks or high-risk areas.
* **Vaccine Information**: Answer if you have received any COVID-19 vaccines.
* **Test Results**: Report your test results for COVID-19 antigen tests, with multiple fields for different test types.

**Note:** This form has multiple fields for test results, which may seem repetitive. Please ensure to fill in the relevant information accurately, as each field is intended for a specific test type or patient scenario. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
