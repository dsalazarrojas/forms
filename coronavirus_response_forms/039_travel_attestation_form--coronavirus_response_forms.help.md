To ensure the form is completed correctly, please follow these steps:

1. **Ensure your travel documents are accurate and complete**: Before starting, make sure you have all necessary documents, such as boarding passes, ID cards, and any other required documents, ready for submission.

2. **Select the correct category**: Choose the category that best describes the purpose of your travel. This will help us understand the context of your attestation.

3. **Provide a brief description**: Write a short description of your travel, including the dates and destinations of your trip.

4. **Choose the correct subcategory**: If you answered "Yes" to the category question, select the subcategory that best describes your travel.

5. **Answer the questions**: The questions section will ask you to provide any additional details related to your travel. This is an optional field and only required if necessary.

6. **Add a signature**: Indicate if you have attached your signature to the documents submitted.

7. **Attach any additional files**: If required, attach any supporting documents that may be needed for verification.

# Travel Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Attestation Form is used to verify the authenticity of travel documents submitted by users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above and ensure that you have all necessary documents ready for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Attestation Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): This is the title of your travel form, which should be a brief description of your trip.
* **Travel Attestation Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): This field is for a brief description of your travel, including the dates and destinations of your trip.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes the purpose of your travel. Options include:
	+ Coronavirus Response Forms
	+ General Forms
	+ Other Forms
* **File** (`form_file`, text, optional): If required, attach any supporting documents that may be needed for verification.
* **Subcategory** (`form_category_2`, select_one, optional): If you answered "Yes" to the category question, select the subcategory that best describes your travel. Options include:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Questions** (`form_questions`, note, optional): This is an optional field for providing any additional details related to your travel.
* **Signature** (`form_signature`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have attached your signature to the documents submitted. Options include:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **File 2** (`form_file_2`, text, optional): If required, attach any additional supporting documents that may be needed for verification.
