# COVID 19 Employee Self Certification To Return To Work - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees return to work after a COVID-19 related absence. To ensure accurate and complete information, please follow the instructions carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer truthfully to the best of your knowledge.
- Ensure all required fields are completed.
- If a question is unclear, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Answer all questions honestly, even if it requires collaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Certification Date**
Certification Date (`certification_date`, date, required): Enter today's date, as this will serve as a reference point for your COVID-19 related absence.

### 2. **Employee Name**
Employee Name (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per your employee identification records.

### 3. **Employee ID**
Employee ID (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee identification number as per company records.

### 4. **Department**
Department (`department`, text, required): Enter your department as per your job role.

### 5. **Position/Title**
Position/Title (`position`, text, required): Enter your job title as per your role in the company.

### 6. **Last Date of Work**
Last Date of Work (`last_work_date`, date, required): Enter the last date you worked before your COVID-19 absence.

### 7. **Proposed Return Date**
Proposed Return Date (`proposed_return_date`, date, required): Enter the date you intend to return to work.

### 8. **COVID-19 Status**
COVID-19 Status (`covid_status`, select_one, required): Indicate your COVID-19 status from the provided options:
  - Recovered from confirmed COVID-19 diagnosis
  - Recovered from suspected COVID-19
  - Completed quarantine after exposure
  - Other

### 9. **COVID-19 Test Result**
COVID-19 Test Result (`covid_test_result`, select_one, required): Indicate your COVID-19 test result from the provided options:
  - Negative test
  - Never tested
  - Prefer not to disclose

### 10. **Symptom-Free**
I am symptom-free (`symptom_free`, select_one, required): Indicate your current symptom status from the provided options:
  - Yes, completely symptom-free
  - Yes, but mild residual symptoms
  - No, still have symptoms
  - Unsure

### 11. **Fever-Free Duration**
Fever-Free Without Medication (`fever_free_duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of days without fever medication.

### 12. **Medical Clearance Obtained**
Medical Clearance Obtained (`medical_clearance`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have obtained medical clearance from a healthcare provider:
  - Yes
  - No
  - Not required

### 13. **Physician or Healthcare Provider Name**
Physician or Healthcare Provider Name (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your healthcare provider.

### 14. **Quarantine Completion**
Quarantine Completion (`quarantine_completion`, select_one, required): Indicate your quarantine completion status:
  - Yes, completed
  - Partial completion
  - Not applicable
  - Unsure of requirements

### 15. **Isolation Conditions**
Isolation Conditions (`isolation_conditions`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you meet the isolation conditions:
  - Yes, I meet conditions
  - No, I do not meet conditions
  - Unsure

### 16. **Household Members Have COVID-19?**
Household Members Have COVID-19? (`household_members_covid`, select_one, optional): Indicate if any household members have COVID-19:
  - No
  - Yes
  - Unsure

### 17. **Vaccination Status**
Vaccination Status (`vaccination_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate your current vaccination status:
  - Fully vaccinated
  - Partially vaccinated
  - Not vaccinated
  - Prefer not to say

### 18. **Work Accommodations Needed Upon Return**
Work Accommodations Needed Upon Return (`work_accommodations_needed`, text, optional): If necessary, enter any modified duties or schedule changes you will require upon return.

### 19. **Health Insurance Notice**
I understand health insurance may require documentation (`health_insurance_notice`, select_one, required): Acknowledge your understanding of potential documentation requirements:
  - I acknowledge
  - I do not acknowledge

### 20. **Accuracy Certification**
I certify that the information above is accurate and complete (`accuracy_certification`, select_one, required): Confirm the accuracy and completeness of your input:
  - I certify
  - I do not certify

### 21. **Supervisor Name for Notification**
Supervisor Name for Notification (`supervisor_notification`, text, required): Enter your direct supervisor's name.

### 22. **Return to Work Agreement**
Return to Work Agreement (`return_to_work_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to return to work under company COVID-19 protocols:
  - I agree
  - I do not agree
  - Have questions

## Tips
- Double-check each question carefully before submitting the form.
- If unsure, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Remember to review the form carefully before submitting.
