# COVID 19 Vaccination Status Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's COVID-19 vaccination status, including their vaccination history, appointment details, and health status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's full name in the "Patient Full Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter the patient's ID or medical record number in the "Patient ID or Medical Record Number" field.
4. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. If applicable, enter the date of the patient's first doctor appointment in the "Date of First Doctor Appointment" field.
7. Select the type of appointment from the "Type of Appointment" dropdown menu.
8. If applicable, enter the name of the healthcare provider in the "Healthcare Provider Name" field.
9. If applicable, enter the name of the clinic or hospital where the patient was vaccinated in the "Clinic or Hospital Name" field.
10. Select the patient's COVID-19 vaccination status from the "COVID-19 Vaccination Status" dropdown menu.
11. If applicable, select the type of vaccine the patient received from the "Vaccine Type Received" dropdown menu.
12. If applicable, enter the date of the first and second doses of the vaccine in the "Date of First Dose" and "Date of Second Dose" fields.
13. If applicable, enter the date of the last booster dose in the "Date of Last Booster Dose" field.
14. Enter the location where the patient was vaccinated in the "Location Where Vaccinated" field.
15. Select whether the patient has a vaccination card available in the "Vaccination Card Available" dropdown menu.
16. If applicable, describe any side effects the patient experienced from the vaccine in the "Additional Notes or Concerns" field.
17. Select whether the patient has been diagnosed with COVID-19 from the "Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19" dropdown menu.
18. If applicable, enter the date of diagnosis or positive test in the "Date of Diagnosis or Positive Test" field.
19. Select the current status if the patient was diagnosed with COVID-19 from the "Current status if diagnosed" dropdown menu.
20. If not fully vaccinated, select a reason from the "If not fully vaccinated, reason" dropdown menu.
21. Select whether the patient intends to get vaccinated from the "Do you intend to get vaccinated" dropdown menu.
22. Add any additional notes or concerns in the "Additional Notes or Concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's ID or medical record number.
* **Email Address**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date of First Doctor Appointment**: Enter the date of the patient's first doctor appointment, if applicable.
* **Type of Appointment**: Select the type of appointment, such as "New patient", "Follow-up", "Routine checkup", etc.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider, if applicable.
* **Clinic or Hospital Name**: Enter the name of the clinic or hospital where the patient was vaccinated, if applicable.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Select the patient's COVID-19 vaccination status, such as "Fully vaccinated", "Partially vaccinated", etc.
* **Vaccine Type Received**: Select the type of vaccine the patient received, if applicable.
* **Date of First Dose**: Enter the date of the first dose of the vaccine, if applicable.
* **Date of Second Dose**: Enter the date of the second dose of the vaccine, if applicable.
* **Date of Last Booster Dose**: Enter the date of the last booster dose of the vaccine, if applicable.
* **Location Where Vaccinated**: Enter the location where the patient was vaccinated, such as "Hospital", "Pharmacy", "Clinic", etc.
* **Vaccination Card Available**: Select whether the patient has a vaccination card available, such as "True", "False", "Lost or misplaced".
* **Additional Notes or Concerns**: Enter any additional notes or concerns, if applicable.
* **Experienced side effects from vaccine**: Select whether the patient experienced any side effects from the vaccine, such as "True", "False", "Unsure".
* **Date of Diagnosis or Positive Test**: Enter the date of diagnosis or positive test, if applicable.
* **Current status if diagnosed**: Select the current status if the patient was diagnosed with COVID-19, such as "Recovered", "Currently affected", etc.
* **If not fully vaccinated, reason**: Select a reason if the patient is not fully vaccinated, such as "Medical contraindication", "Personal choice", etc.
* **Do you intend to get vaccinated**: Select whether the patient intends to get vaccinated, such as "True", "False", "Unsure", etc.
* **Additional Notes or Concerns**: Enter any additional notes or concerns, if applicable.
