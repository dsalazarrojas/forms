# COVID 19 Vaccine Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in being placed on a waitlist for COVID-19 vaccines. Please answer the questions accurately to help us assess your priority status for vaccine distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions on each page. The form is divided into several pages, and each page has its own questions related to specific areas of information collection. Please fill in the required fields, and you can leave the optional fields blank if you do not have the information requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, not required): Please select your gender from the options provided. This information will not affect your priority status, but it will help us understand your demographics.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your waitlist status.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter a phone number where we can contact you with updates about your waitlist status.
* **Is This Your Primary Phone** (`primary_phone`, `select_one`, not required): Please select `True` if this is your primary phone number, and `False` otherwise. This information will help us determine your contact preferences.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Please enter your street address where we can contact you or send you vaccine information.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Please enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Please enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Please enter your zip code.
* **County** (`county`, `text`, not required): Please enter the county where you reside. This information is optional and not required for priority determination.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select your COVID-19 vaccination status from the options provided. This will help us understand your current vaccination status.
* **Which Dose Are You Interested In** (`dose_interest`, `select_one`, required): Please select the dose of the vaccine you are interested in receiving.
* **Vaccine Type Preference** (`vaccine_type_preference`, `select_one`, not required): Please select the type of vaccine you prefer from the options provided. This information is not required but will help us understand your preferences.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, not required): Please select your employment status from the options provided. This information is optional and not required for priority determination.
* **Age 65 or Older** (`age_65_plus`, `select_one`, required): Please select `True` if you are 65 or older, and `False` otherwise. This information is required for priority determination.
* **Chronic Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_one`, required): Please select `True` if you have a chronic medical condition, and `False` otherwise. This information is required for priority determination.
* **Describe Your Condition** (`condition_details`, `text`, not required): Please describe your chronic medical condition if you have selected `True` in the previous question.
* **Date Joined Waitlist** (`signup_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you joined the waitlist in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Please select `I agree` to the terms and conditions of the waitlist. This is a required field to proceed.
