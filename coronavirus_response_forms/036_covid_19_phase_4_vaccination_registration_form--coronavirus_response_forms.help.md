# COVID 19 Phase 4 Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking COVID-19 vaccination in Phase 4. It covers demographic information, health background, vaccination history, and consent agreements.

## How to Complete This Form

### To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Age, Email Address, Phone Number, Street Address, City, State, and ZIP Code.
2. Confirm your eligibility for Phase 4 vaccination by selecting "True" or "False".
3. If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's name.
4. Select your Age Category: Child (under 16) or Adult (16 years and older).
5. Indicate if you have any underlying health conditions.
6. Confirm if you have any known allergies.
7. Select whether you have had a severe allergic reaction to vaccines.
8. Provide your vaccination history, including prior doses received.
9. Confirm if you have had a COVID-19 infection.
10. Confirm you have read and understood vaccine information.
11. Agree to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
12. Enter the date you are registering for the vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Age** (`Age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`Street Address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`State`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (`ZIP Code`, text, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Age Category** (`Age Category`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a Child (under 16) or Adult (16 years and older).
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`Parent or Guardian Name (if under 18)`, text, required): If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Phase 4 Eligibility** (`Phase 4 Eligibility`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are eligible for Phase 4 vaccination by selecting "True".
* **Underlying Health Conditions** (`Underlying Health Conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select any underlying health conditions you have, such as asthma, diabetes, or heart disease.
* **Known Allergies** (`Known Allergies`, text, required): Enter any known allergies you have.
* **Severe Allergic Reaction** (`Severe Allergic Reaction`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have had a severe allergic reaction to vaccines.
* **Prior COVID-19 Vaccine Doses** (`Prior COVID-19 Vaccine Doses`, select_one, required): Indicate how many doses of COVID-19 vaccine you have received prior to this registration.
* **COVID-19 Infection** (`COVID-19 Infection`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have had COVID-19 infection.
* **Consent and Agreement** (`Consent and Agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understood vaccine information.
* **I have read and understand vaccine information** (`I have read and understand vaccine information`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understood vaccine information.
* **I consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine** (`I consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Registration Date** (`Registration Date`, date, required): Enter the date you are registering for the vaccine.

### Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for clarification.
* This form is intended to provide information, not to collect medical opinions or diagnoses. If you have any concerns, please consult a healthcare professional.
