# Funeral Travel Permit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Travel Permit Application Form is a document used to facilitate travel for individuals attending funerals or other end-of-life events. It requires accurate and up-to-date information to prevent delays or rejections of the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, phone number, and email address in the "Applicant Name", "Phone", and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the date of the funeral or the event you are attending in the "Funeral Date" field.
3. Provide flight information, including the flight number, departure, and arrival airport codes in the "Flight Information" field.
4. Specify the reason for your travel in the "Travel Purpose" field.
5. Enter the destination airport code or name in the "Travel Destination" field.
6. If applicable, enter the date you plan to return from your travel in the "Return Date" field.
7. Indicate the duration of your travel in hours in the "Travel Duration" field.
8. List any travel documents you will be bringing with you in the "Travel Documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the permit.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Funeral Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the funeral or event you are attending. Please use the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Flight Information** (text, optional): Include flight number, departure, and arrival airport codes. For example: "Flight #1234, ORD (ORD), JFK".
* **Travel Purpose** (text, optional): Describe the reason for your travel, such as attending a funeral or visiting a family member.
* **Travel Destination** (text, optional): Enter the airport code or name of your travel destination.
* **Return Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you plan to return from your travel. Please use the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Travel Duration** (number, optional): Enter the duration of your travel in hours.
* **Travel Documents** (text, optional): List any travel documents you will be bringing with you, such as your ID, passport, or visa.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays or rejections of the application.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or our support team for assistance.
