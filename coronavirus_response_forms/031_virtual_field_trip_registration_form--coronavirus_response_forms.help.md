# Virtual Field Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Field Trip Registration Form is a simple online survey designed to collect demographic information and contact preferences from participants for an educational Virtual Field Trip program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email in the field labeled "Email".
4. Select your preferred contact method from the field labeled "Contact Preferences".
5. Enter your School Name (if applicable) in the field labeled "School Name".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents. (Text field, Optional)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents. (Text field, Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you. (Email field, Optional)
* **Contact Preferences**: Select all that apply from the dropdown menu:
	+ **Parent**: If you're a parent or guardian registering a student.
	+ **Teacher**: If you're a teacher or school staff member.
	+ **Both**: If you're both a parent/student and a teacher or staff member.
* **School Name**: If you are a teacher or school staff member, enter your school name here. (Text field, Optional)

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are a teacher or school staff member, please enter your school name correctly to ensure you are registered under your school's account.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
