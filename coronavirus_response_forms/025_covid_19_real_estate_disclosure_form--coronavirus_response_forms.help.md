# COVID 19 Real Estate Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The COVID 19 Real Estate Disclosure Form is designed to protect both real estate agents and brokers, and clients by gathering critical information regarding COVID-19 health status and liability waivers. This form ensures that all parties are aware of and acknowledge potential risks associated with in-person property visits during the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    *   Fill in the Property Address field with the exact address of the property you are visiting.
    *   Enter your Client Full Name in the Client Full Name field.
    *   Select your relationship to the transaction from the Relationship to Transaction options (e.g., Buyer, Seller, Tenant, Landlord, or Other).
    *   Fill in the Disclosure Date field with the date you completed this form.
    *   Acknowledge the Risk Acknowledgment section and sign to confirm you understand the risks involved.
    *   Select I acknowledge for the Risk Tours Acknowledgment field.
    *   Choose I agree for the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) agreement field.
    *   If you have not been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the last 14 days, select I certify for the No Diagnosis Certification field.
    *   If you have not experienced any COVID-19 symptoms in the last 14 days, select I certify for the No Symptoms Certification field.
    *   Select I acknowledge and agree for the Liability Waiver Acknowledgment field.
    *   Fill in the Realtor or Agent Name field with the name of your realtor or agent.
    *   Fill in the Brokerage Name field with the name of your brokerage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Address:** Please enter the exact address of the property you are visiting.
*   **Client Full Name:** Enter your full name.
*   **Relationship to Transaction:**
    *   **Buyer:** Select this option if you are the buyer of the property.
    *   **Seller:** Select this option if you are the seller of the property.
    *   **Tenant:** Select this option if you are a tenant of the property.
    *   **Landlord:** Select this option if you are a landlord of the property.
    *   **Other:** Select this option if your relationship to the transaction is not listed.
*   **Disclosure Date:** Enter the date you completed this form.
*   **Risk Acknowledgment:** This is an important section where you acknowledge that you understand the risks involved with in-person property visits. Please sign and select I acknowledge.
*   **Risk Tours Acknowledgment:** Select I acknowledge to confirm that you understand the risks involved with in-person property visits.
*   **PPE Agreement:** Select I agree to confirm that you will wear Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) during all tours.
*   **No Diagnosis Certification:** Select I certify if you have not been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the last 14 days.
*   **No Symptoms Certification:** Select I certify if you have not experienced any COVID-19 symptoms in the last 14 days.
*   **Liability Waiver Acknowledgment:** Select I acknowledge and agree to confirm that you understand and waive the agent and brokerage from liability related to COVID-19 exposure.
*   **Realtor or Agent Name:** Enter the name of your realtor or agent.
*   **Brokerage Name:** Enter the name of your brokerage.

**Tips**

*   Always complete this form before in-person property visits.
*   Ensure you understand the risks involved with in-person property visits.
*   Be honest when completing this form.
*   If you have any concerns, ask your realtor or agent for assistance.
