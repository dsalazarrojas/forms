# distancing_and_barriers_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on social distancing measures and barriers that are in place to prevent the spread of the coronavirus. This information will help in identifying areas where more measures can be taken to ensure public safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready to be filled out.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Barriers** (Required, Select Multiple): This section is used to select which barriers are in place. Select all the barriers that are applicable to your location.
	+ None
	+ Physical Barriers (e.g. gates, fences, etc.)
	+ PPE (Personal Protective Equipment, e.g. masks, gloves, etc.)
	+ Signage (e.g. signs, posters, etc.)
	+ Signaling (e.g. flags, lights, etc.)
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Time of Day** (Required, Time): This field is used to indicate what time of day the social distancing measures are in place. Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g. 08:00 for 8am).
* **Date** (Required, Date): This field is used to indicate the date on which the social distancing measures are in place. Enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g. 02/14/2024).
* **Location** (Optional, Text): This field is used to provide the location where the social distancing measures are in place. If this field is not filled, it will be assumed that the measures are in general.
* **Number of People** (Required, Number): This field is used to indicate the number of people who are expected to be present during the social distancing measures. Enter the number as an integer.
* **Distance** (Required, Number): This field is used to indicate the physical distance between people. Enter the distance in meters or feet.
* **Time of Visit** (Required, Time): This field is used to indicate the time at which you visited the location. Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g. 12:00 for 12pm).
* **Date of Visit** (Required, Date): This field is used to indicate the date of visit. Enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g. 02/14/2024).
* **Additional Comments** (Optional, Text): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information about the social distancing measures and barriers in place.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready before filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* The information collected from this form will be used to identify areas where more measures can be taken to ensure public safety.
