# Student COVID 19 Vaccine Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to provide their personal information and attestation status regarding their COVID-19 vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter the date of your first vaccine dose in the "Vaccine Dose 1 Date" field.
6. Enter the date of your second vaccine dose in the "Vaccine Dose 2 Date" field.
7. If applicable, enter the date of your third vaccine dose in the "Vaccine Dose 3 Date" field.
8. Select "True" or "False" to indicate if you are interested in receiving the vaccine.
9. Select "True" or "False" to indicate if you have already been vaccinated.
10. Enter your Student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
11. If you have a guardian or emergency contact, fill in their name in the "Guardian Name" field.
12. If you have a guardian or emergency contact with an email address, enter it in the "Guardian Email" field.
13. If you have a guardian or emergency contact with a phone number, enter it in the "Guardian Phone" field.
14. Select the relationship between you and your guardian or emergency contact in the "Relationship to Student" field.
15. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
16. If your emergency contact has an email address, enter it in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
17. If your emergency contact has a phone number, enter it in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
18. Select the relationship between your emergency contact and the student in the "Relationship to Student" field.
19. Enter the address of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
20. Select the province of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Province" field.
21. Enter the city of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact City" field.
22. Enter the postal code of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Postal Code" field.
23. Enter the country of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Country" field.
24. Fill in the student attestation in the "Student Atestation" field.
25. Select your student signature (either "Student Signature 1" or "Student Signature 2").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (1)**: Enter your student name.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Vaccine Dose 1 Date (5)**: Enter the date of your first vaccine dose.
* **Vaccine Dose 2 Date (6)**: Enter the date of your second vaccine dose.
* **Vaccine Dose 3 Date (7)**: Only required if you have received a third dose of the vaccine.
* **Interested in Vaccine (8)**: Select "True" if you are interested in receiving the vaccine, "False" otherwise.
* **Already Vaccinated (9)**: Select "True" if you have already been vaccinated, "False" otherwise.
* **Student ID (10)**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Guardian Name (11)**: Enter the name of your guardian or emergency contact.
* **Guardian Email (12)**: Enter the email address of your guardian or emergency contact.
* **Guardian Phone (13)**: Enter the phone number of your guardian or emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Student (14)**: Select the relationship between you and your guardian or emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name (15)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (16)**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (17)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Student (18)**: Select the relationship between your emergency contact and the student.
* **Emergency Contact Address (19)**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Province (20)**: Select the province of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact City (21)**: Enter the city of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Postal Code (22)**: Enter the postal code of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Country (23)**: Enter the country of your emergency contact.
* **Student Atestation (24)**: Fill in the student attestation.
* **Student Signature (25)**: Select your student signature (either "Student Signature 1" or "Student Signature 2").
