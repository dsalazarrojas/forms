# coronavirus_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect coronavirus survey data from users to help us understand the public's response to the current pandemic situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the first page "Home".
2. On this page, you can enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share. This is a text field where you can write as much or as little as you like.
3. Click on the "Symptoms" page.
4. Select all the symptoms that apply to you from the list of options provided. You can select multiple symptoms.
5. Click on the "Impact" page.
6. Enter the date when your symptoms first appeared or when you first experienced the impact of the pandemic.
7. Click on the "Test Result" page.
8. Select your test result from the list of options provided.
9. Click on the "Vaccination" page.
10. Enter any information about your vaccination status or any relevant details.
11. Click "Submit" to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home (Text)** (`title`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share about your response.
* **Symptoms (Select Multiple)** (`symptoms`, optional): Select all the symptoms that apply to you from the list of options provided. This will help us understand which symptoms are most prevalent among users.
* **Impact (Date)** (`impact`, optional): Enter the date when your symptoms first appeared or when you first experienced the impact of the pandemic.
* **Test Result (Select One)** (`test_result`, optional): Select your test result from the list of options provided.
* **Vaccination (Text)** (`vaccination`, optional): Enter any information about your vaccination status or any relevant details.

## Tips
Please ensure you are completing the form honestly and accurately. If you have any doubts or questions, please refer to this help guide for clarification.
