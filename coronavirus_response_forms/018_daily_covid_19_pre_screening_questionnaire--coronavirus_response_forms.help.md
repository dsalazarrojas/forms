# DailyCovid19PreScreeningQuestionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DailyCovid19PreScreeningQuestionnaire is a form designed to gather information from individuals to assess their risk level for COVID-19. It is used to determine potential symptoms, medical history, and other factors that may affect an individual's risk level.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question accurately and completely.
3. Provide any relevant information about your medical history and travel history.
4. Check the boxes for any symptoms you are experiencing.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name.
* **age** (`age`, `number`, optional): Please enter your age.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_one`, optional): Select any of the following symptoms you are experiencing:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Sore throat
	+ Runny nose
	+ Headache
	+ Fatigue
	+ Chest pain
* **travel_history** (`travel_history`, `text`, optional): Please describe any recent travel history.
* **contact_history** (`contacthistory`, `text`, optional): Please describe any recent contact history.
* **medical_condition** (`medicalcondition`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any of the following medical conditions you have:
	+ Diabetes
	+ Asthma
	+ Heart disease
	+ Cancer
	+ Liver disease
	+ Kidney disease
* **previous_vaccine** (`previousvaccine`, `select_one`, optional): Have you received the COVID-19 vaccine?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **consent** (`consent`, `note`, optional): This is a note field for consent.
* **signature** (`signature`, `text`, optional): Please sign and date this form to confirm your responses.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately to help us make an informed decision.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is for general use and is not intended for emergency situations. If you are experiencing severe symptoms, please seek immediate medical attention.
