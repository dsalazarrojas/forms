# COVID 19 Vaccine Appointment Standby List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who wish to be added to a standby list for COVID-19 vaccine appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the header information section, which includes your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number, date of birth, and zip code in the corresponding fields.
3. If you have a preferred vaccine type, select it from the dropdown menu. If you have no preference, leave this field blank.
4. Choose your preferred appointment time from the dropdown menu, or select 'No preference' if you are open to scheduling at any time.
5. Indicate how flexible you are with appointment dates by selecting 'Very flexible', 'Somewhat flexible', or 'Limited availability'.
6. If you have any accessibility needs, select the corresponding option from the dropdown menu.
7. Provide any additional information or preferences in the 'Additional Information or Preferences' field (optional).
8. Finally, confirm your wish to be added to the standby list by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address we will use to contact you about availability and other important updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you about your appointment.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the standard MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code as it appears on your address.
* **Preferred Vaccine Type (if any)**: Select the type of vaccine you prefer from the dropdown menu. If you have no preference, leave this field blank.
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: Choose your preferred appointment time from the dropdown menu, or select 'No preference' if you are open to scheduling at any time.
* **How flexible are you with appointment dates?**: Indicate how flexible you are with appointment dates by selecting 'Very flexible', 'Somewhat flexible', or 'Limited availability'.
* **Do you have any accessibility needs?**: Select the corresponding option from the dropdown menu to indicate any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Additional Information or Preferences**: Provide any additional information or preferences you would like to share with us.
* **I wish to be added to the standby list**: Confirm your desire to be added to the standby list by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
