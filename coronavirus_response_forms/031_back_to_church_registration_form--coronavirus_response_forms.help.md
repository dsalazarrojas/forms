# Back To Church Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Back To Church Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from you and your household to ensure safe and efficient attendance at our church services. By providing this information, you'll help us maintain accurate records and prevent over-capacity situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Review each field carefully and ensure the information provided is accurate.
2.  Fill out all required fields.
3.  Use the drop-down options for fields like "Preferred Service Time."
4.  If you have any special requests or needs, use the text fields accordingly.
5.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Primary Attendee Name** (attendee_primary_name, text, required): Provide your name as the primary attendee from your household.

- **Email Address** (contact_email_church, email, required): This will be used for confirmation and important updates. Please use a valid email address that you have access to regularly.

- **Phone Number** (contact_phone_church, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for us to reach out to you if needed.

- **Total number of people in your household attending** (household_size_attending, number, required): Include all family members, including infants, to help us plan for seating and services.

- **Preferred Service Time** (service_time_preference, select_one, required): Choose your desired service time from the options provided: 8:30 AM, 10:30 AM, or 5:00 PM Evening Service.

- **Health Screening Declaration** (health_screening_header, note, required): Please be honest in answering this section for the safety of all attendees.

- **Has anyone in your party had a fever, cough, or shortness of breath?** (covid_symptoms_check, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if anyone in your household is experiencing these symptoms.

- **Have you been in contact with anyone who tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 14 days?** (exposure_history_check, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you or anyone in your household has been in contact with someone who tested positive.

- **Have you traveled internationally or to a high-risk area in the last 14 days?** (travel_history_check, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you or anyone in your household has traveled to a high-risk area.

- **I agree to wear a mask and follow all social distancing protocols** (safety_protocol_agreement, select_one, required): Please select 'I agree' to ensure a safe and healthy environment for all.

- **Do you have any special seating needs?** (seating_requirements, text, optional): If you have any specific seating requirements, such as wheelchair access, please describe them here.

- **Any prayer requests you'd like to share?** (prayer_requests_church, text, optional): If you have any prayer requests or intentions, this is the place to share them with our community.
