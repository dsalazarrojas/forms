# COVID 19 Vaccine Pre Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Vaccine Pre-Screening Form is a tool used by healthcare providers to assess patients' medical history and determine whether they are safe to receive the COVID-19 vaccine. This form helps providers gather essential information to make informed decisions about vaccine administration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the patient's medical history, including any allergies, previous vaccinations, and COVID-19 diagnoses.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, taking into account the patient's current health status and medical history.
3. Use the provided options to select the best answer for each question.
4. Be sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's ID or medical record number, if available. This field is not required.
* **Screening Date**: Enter the date you are screening the patient for the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Pre-Screening Questions**: Review the patient's medical history, including allergies and previous vaccinations.
* **History of severe allergic reactions**: Has the patient had anaphylaxis to vaccines or medications? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **Known allergies to vaccine components**: Has the patient had allergic reactions to any vaccine components? Answer: `True`, `False`, or `Unsure`. If unsure, provide notes or further information.
* **Currently ill with fever or acute illness**: Is the patient currently ill with a fever or acute illness? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **History of myocarditis or pericarditis**: Has the patient had myocarditis or pericarditis in the past? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **Seizure disorder or neurological condition**: Does the patient have a seizure disorder or neurological condition? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **Severely immunocompromised**: Is the patient severely immunocompromised due to factors such as HIV, cancer treatment, or transplant? Answer: `True`, `False`, or `Unsure`. If unsure, provide notes or further information.
* **Currently on immunosuppressive therapy**: Is the patient taking immunosuppressive therapy? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **History of blood clotting disorders**: Has the patient had blood clotting disorders in the past? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **Currently taking anticoagulants or blood thinners**: Is the patient taking anticoagulants or blood thinners? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **Thrombocytopenia or low platelet count**: Does the patient have thrombocytopenia or a low platelet count? Answer: `True`, `False`, or `Unsure`. If unsure, provide notes or further information.
* **Pregnancy and Reproduction**:
	+ **Currently pregnant**: Is the patient currently pregnant? Answer: `True`, `False`, `Unsure`, or `Not applicable`.
	+ **Currently breastfeeding**: Is the patient currently breastfeeding? Answer: `True`, `False`, or `Not applicable`.
* **Recent Vaccinations**:
	+ **Recently received live vaccine**: Has the patient recently received a live vaccine? Answer: `True` or `False`.
	+ **Date of live vaccine**: If yes, enter the date of the live vaccine in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Prior COVID-19 vaccination**:
	+ **Previously received COVID-19 vaccine**: Has the patient previously received a COVID-19 vaccine? Answer: `True` or `False`.
	+ **Vaccine type**: If yes, enter the type of vaccine the patient received.
	+ **Date of prior vaccine**: If yes, enter the date the patient received the vaccine in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	+ **Severe reaction to prior COVID-19 vaccine**: Has the patient had a severe reaction to the COVID-19 vaccine? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **COVID-19 History**:
	+ **Prior COVID-19 diagnosis**: Has the patient been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past? Answer: `True`, `False`, or `Unsure`. If unsure, provide notes or further information.
	+ **Date of COVID-19 diagnosis**: If yes, enter the date of diagnosis in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	+ **Currently positive or active COVID-19**: Is the patient currently positive or have active COVID-19? Answer: `True` or `False`.
* **Screening Conclusion**:
	+ **Pre-screening Result**: Enter the pre-screening result as determined by the healthcare provider. Options include: `Approved - safe to vaccinate`, `Defer - contact physician first`, `Requires evaluation - see medical provider`, or `Not approved - contraindication`.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider completing this form.
* **Screening Notes or Comments**: Enter any notes or comments from the healthcare provider.

## Tips

* Please complete the form with care, as the information collected will inform the patient's eligibility for COVID-19 vaccination.
* If unsure about any answers or need further information, provide notes or further information in the relevant fields.
* Review the patient's medical history and current health status carefully before making a decision about vaccine eligibility.
* Be sure to enter the correct date for each field, as this will inform future vaccine eligibility determinations.
* If the pre-screening result is "Defer" or "Requires evaluation", the patient should be referred to another healthcare provider for further evaluation.
* If the patient is not eligible for vaccine due to contraindication, provide clear reasons in the screening notes or comments.
