# Coronavirus Business Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Business Reopening Survey is a tool to gather information from businesses about their response to the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief description of your business.
2. Select the **Category** that best describes your business (e.g., "Coronavirus Response Forms" or "Other").
3. Provide a **Description** of your business and its current situation.
4. Select the **Form Status** of your business (e.g., "Open", "Closed", or "Pending").
5. Choose the **Business Type** of your business (e.g., "Restaurant", "Store", or "Other").
6. Select the **Business Status** of your business (e.g., "Open", "Closed", or "Pending").
7. If you require help or support, select **Need Help** ("True" or "False").
8. Optionally, provide any additional **Comments** about your situation.
9. If you have an attachment (e.g., a document or image) that supports your submission, you can upload it in the **Attachment** field.
10. Finally, provide your **Email** and **Phone** contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): A brief description of your business.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): The category that best describes your business (e.g., "Coronavirus Response Forms" or "Other").
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A description of your business and its current situation.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, optional): The status of your business (e.g., "Open", "Closed", or "Pending").
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, optional): The type of business you operate (e.g., "Restaurant", "Store", or "Other").
* **Business Status** (`business_status`, select_one, optional): The current status of your business (e.g., "Open", "Closed", or "Pending").
* **Need Help** (`need_help`, select_one, optional): If you require help or support, please select "True".
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments about your situation.
* **Attachment** (`form_attachment`, note, optional): If you have an attachment that supports your submission, you can upload it here.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Your email contact information.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
