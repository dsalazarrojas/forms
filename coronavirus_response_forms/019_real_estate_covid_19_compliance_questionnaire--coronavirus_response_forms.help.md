# real_estate_covid_19_compliance_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from real estate agents or owners to assess their compliance with health and safety measures during the COVID-19 pandemic.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the correct answers to each question based on your situation.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `note`, required): Please provide your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted by the responding party, such as "buyer" or "seller".
* **Have you or any household member have any symptoms of COVID-19** (`have_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply.
	+ If you are unsure or do not see your symptoms listed, contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply.
* **Have you or any household member been exposed to anyone with COVID-19** (`contact_exposure`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply.
* **Are there any travel restrictions that would prevent us from showing the property** (`travel_restrictions`, `select_one`, required): If you have any travel restrictions, describe them briefly in the space provided.
* **Request to contact the buyer or seller** (`contact_request`, `select_one`, required): Choose who you would like to contact regarding your property concerns, such as "buyer" or "seller".
* **Other (please specify)** (`contact_request_other`, `select_one`, optional): If you selected "other" in the previous question, please describe briefly who you would like to contact.

Note: This form is only to be completed by real estate agents or owners, and the information collected is intended for assessment and improvement of industry compliance with health and safety measures.
