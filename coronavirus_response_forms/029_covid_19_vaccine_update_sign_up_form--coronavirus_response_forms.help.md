# COVID 19 Vaccine Update Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to stay informed about the COVID-19 vaccine, including its availability, eligibility, and distribution. By completing this form, you will be able to receive updates about the vaccine and potentially get notifications about locations where you can get vaccinated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Submit" at the end of the form to save your information.
2. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your phone number if you want to receive SMS updates.
4. Enter your ZIP code to help us identify your location.
5. Select your age group (Under 18, 18-30, 31-50, 51-65, 65+).
6. Indicate your vaccination eligibility group (Healthcare worker, Essential worker, Age 65 or older, Underlying medical condition, General population, Unsure).
7. If you have an underlying medical condition, describe it in the text field.
8. Select your openness to getting vaccinated (Yes, definitely, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably not, Definitely not).
9. If you have concerns about the vaccine, select the level of concern (No concerns, Some concerns, Many concerns, Significant concerns).
10. If you have concerns, describe them in the text field.
11. Select your preferred method of communication (Email, Text Message, Phone Call, Mail, All methods).
12. Choose how often you want to receive updates (As soon as vaccine available, Weekly updates, Monthly updates, Only major changes, No preference).
13. Select your preferred location for vaccination (Medical clinic, Hospital, Pharmacy, Community center, Mobile clinic, Home vaccination, No preference).
14. Indicate how far you are willing to travel for vaccination (Walking distance only, Within 5 miles, Within 10 miles, Within 25 miles, Any distance).
15. Select your availability to get vaccinated on weekdays (Monday through Friday) (Yes, anytime, Early morning, Afternoon, Evening, Not available).
16. Select your availability to get vaccinated on weekends (Saturday and Sunday) (Yes, anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Not available).
17. Indicate your willingness to receive COVID-19 vaccine updates (I consent, I do not consent).
18. Agree to the privacy terms (I agree, I do not agree).
19. Confirm that all provided information is accurate (Confirmed accurate, Needs correction).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`update_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`update_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email Address** (`update_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (`update_phone`, `phone`, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to receive SMS updates.
- **ZIP Code** (`update_zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code to help us identify your location.
- **Age Group** (`update_age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select your age category (Under 18, 18-30, 31-50, 51-65, 65+).
- **Vaccination Eligibility Group** (`update_eligible_group`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your vaccination eligibility group (Healthcare worker, Essential worker, Age 65 or older, Underlying medical condition, General population, Unsure).
- **Underlying Medical Conditions** (`update_medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe any underlying medical conditions you have.
- **Openness to Vaccination** (`update_willingness`, `select_one`, required): Select your openness to getting vaccinated (Yes, definitely, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably not, Definitely not).
- **Concerns About the Vaccine** (`update_concerns`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of concern you have about the vaccine (No concerns, Some concerns, Many concerns, Significant concerns).
- **Concerns Details** (`update_concerns_details`, `text`, optional): If you have concerns, describe them in this text field.
- **Preferred Update Method** (`update_notification_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your preferred method of communication (Email, Text Message, Phone Call, Mail, All methods).
- **Update Frequency** (`update_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you want to receive updates (As soon as vaccine available, Weekly updates, Monthly updates, Only major changes, No preference).
- **Preferred Vaccination Location** (`update_location_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred location for vaccination (Medical clinic, Hospital, Pharmacy, Community center, Mobile clinic, Home vaccination, No preference).
- **Willingness to Travel Distance** (`update_distance_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how far you are willing to travel for vaccination (Walking distance only, Within 5 miles, Within 10 miles, Within 25 miles, Any distance).
- **Availability to Get Vaccinated** (`update_time_availability`, `select_one`, required): Select your availability to get vaccinated on weekdays (Monday through Friday) (Yes, anytime, Early morning, Afternoon, Evening, Not available).
- **Availability to Get Vaccinated on Weekends** (`update_weekend_availability`, `select_one`, required): Select your availability to get vaccinated on weekends (Saturday and Sunday) (Yes, anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Not available).
- **Consent to Receive COVID-19 Vaccine Updates** (`update_consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to receive COVID-19 vaccine updates (I consent, I do not consent).
- **Agreement to Privacy Terms** (`update_privacy_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the privacy terms (I agree, I do not agree).
