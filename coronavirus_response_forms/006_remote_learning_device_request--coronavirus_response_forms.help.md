# remote_learning_device_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "remote_learning_device_request" form is designed to collect information from schools and teachers to request remote learning devices for their students. This form is used for administrative purposes to process and fulfill device requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the student and school before filling out the form.
2. Enter the student's name, grade, and school name in the respective fields.
3. Enter the school's email address, phone number, and address (if applicable).
4. If applicable, enter the teacher's name, email address, and phone number.
5. Choose the type of device needed (e.g., Laptop, Tablet, Chromebook, iPad, or Desktop).
6. Specify the quantity of devices requested.
7. Indicate the condition of the devices (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student for whom the device is being requested.
* **Student Grade**: Select the grade level of the student (e.g., 6th Grade, 7th Grade, 8th Grade, etc.).
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where the student attends.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school (if applicable).
* **Teacher Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the teacher requesting the device.
* **Teacher Email**: If applicable, enter the email address of the teacher.
* **Teacher Phone**: If applicable, enter the phone number of the teacher.
* **Device Type**: Choose the type of device being requested (e.g., Laptop, Tablet, Chromebook, iPad, or Desktop).
* **Quantity**: Specify the number of devices being requested.
* **Device Condition**: Indicate the condition of the device (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Student Email**: If applicable, enter the email address of the student.
* **Parent Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email**: If applicable, enter the email address of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete the form and submit the request.
