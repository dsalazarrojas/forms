# COVID 19 Esthetician Room Cleaning Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Esthetician Room Cleaning Check Form is used by staff members to report on their cleaning activities in the esthetician room during the COVID-19 pandemic. The form helps ensure that the room is properly maintained and sanitized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Check the date and time of your cleaning check and fill in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your name and room number to identify yourself and the room being cleaned.
3. Select the shift you were working during the cleaning check.
4. Answer each question regarding the cleaning status of the room, marking "Yes" or "No" or "Partial" as applicable.
5. Assess the overall cleanliness of the room.
6. Report any issues or areas of concern.
7. Note any action items that need to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Date** (`check_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your cleaning check in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Check Time** (`check_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your cleaning check in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a staff member performing the cleaning check.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required): Enter the number of the room being cleaned.
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_one, required): Select the shift you were working during the cleaning check (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night).
* **Floor Cleaned and Disinfected** (`floor_cleaned`, select_one, required): Check if the floor is cleaned and disinfected (Yes, No, Partial).
* **Walls Cleaned** (`walls_cleaned`, select_one, required): Check if the walls are cleaned (Yes, No, Partial).
* **Treatment Table Cleaned and Disinfected** (`treatment_table_cleaned`, select_one, required): Check if the treatment table is cleaned and disinfected (Yes, No, Partial).
* **Mirrors and Glass Cleaned** (`mirrors_cleaned`, select_one, required): Check if mirrors and glass are cleaned (Yes, No).
* **Equipment and Tools Disinfected** (`equipment_disinfected`, select_one, required): Check if equipment and tools are disinfected (Yes, No, Partial).
* **Instruments Properly Sterilized** (`tools_sterilized`, select_one, required): Check if instruments are properly sterilized (Yes, No).
* **Linens Changed and Laundered** (`linens_changed`, select_one, required): Check if linens are changed and laundered (Yes, No).
* **Trash Properly Disposed** (`trash_disposed`, select_one, required): Check if trash is properly disposed (Yes, No).
* **Appropriate Disinfectant Used** (`disinfectant_used`, select_one, required): Check if appropriate disinfectant is used (Yes, No).
* **Hand Sanitizer Restocked** (`sanitizer_available`, select_one, required): Check if hand sanitizer is restocked (Yes, No).
* **PPE Available in Room** (`ppe_available`, select_one, required): Check if PPE is available in the room (Yes, No).
* **Overall Cleanliness Assessment** (`overall_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Assess the overall cleanliness of the room (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, Unacceptable).
* **Issues or Areas of Concern** (`issues_noted`, text, required): Report any issues or areas of concern.
* **Action Items** (`action_taken`, text, required): Note any action items that need to be taken.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_review`, text, optional): This field is for the manager's review and approval.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately report the cleaning status of each area to ensure that the room is properly maintained.
* Report any issues or concerns to help us improve the cleaning process.
* Use this form regularly to track the cleaning status of the esthetician room.
