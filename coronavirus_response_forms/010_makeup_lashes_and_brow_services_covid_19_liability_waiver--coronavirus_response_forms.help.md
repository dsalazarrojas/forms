# Makeup, Lashes And Brow Services COVID 19 Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain liability waivers from clients who have received or will receive services from a makeup, lashes, and brow services business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4. Select all services provided to you in the "Services Rendered" field.
5. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field if required.
6. Sign the "Signature" field after completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (1, text, required): Enter the title of the form in this field. This is typically the name of the form and the business.
* **Client Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Date Of Birth** (3, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format if required.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter a valid 10-digit phone number.
* **Email** (5, email, optional): Enter a valid email address if required.
* **Address** (6, text, required): Enter your complete address.
* **Services Rendered** (7, select_multiple, required): Select all services provided to you from the available options.
* **Signature** (8, text, required): Enter your signature after completion.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Enter accurate information in all fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the business directly.
