# Mortgage Deferral Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Deferral Request Form is used to request a temporary halt or suspension of payments on a mortgage loan. This form helps the borrower to provide necessary information for processing their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your loan ID, which can be found on your loan documents or by contacting your loan officer.
5. Enter the loan amount you are requesting to defer in the "Loan Amount" field.
6. Provide a brief explanation of the reason for your deferral request in the "Reason for Deferral" field.
7. Choose the start date when you expect the deferral to begin, using the calendar picker in the "Expected Deferral Start Date" field.
8. Choose the end date when you expect the deferral to end, using the calendar picker in the "Deferral End Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it is shown on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it is shown on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information or updates.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information or updates.
* **Loan ID** (loan_id, text, required): Enter the ID number of your loan as it is shown on your loan documents or provided by your loan officer.
* **Loan Amount** (loan_amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the loan you are requesting to defer.
* **Reason for Deferral** (reason_for_deferral, text, required): Please provide a brief explanation of the reason for your deferral request.
* **Expected Deferral Start Date** (expected_deferral_start_date, date, required): Choose the date when you expect the deferral to begin using the calendar picker.
* **Deferral End Date** (deferral_end_date, date, required): Choose the date when you expect the deferral to end using the calendar picker.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Make sure to double-check your entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* We will review your request and get back to you as soon as possible with an update on the status of your deferral request.
