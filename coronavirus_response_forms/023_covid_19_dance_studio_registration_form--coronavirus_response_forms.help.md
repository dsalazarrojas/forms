# COVID 19 Dance Studio Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Dance Studio Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from students for safe and enjoyable dance studio experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the class details such as date and time.
2. Select the type of class you're registering for (e.g., Ballet, Jazz, Hip Hop, etc.).
3. Enter your instructor's name.
4. Provide the studio location.
5. Fill in your personal details such as name, student ID, phone number, email, and date of birth.
6. Select your experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional).
7. Answer the health screening questions, including health screening completed, temperature check, COVID-19 symptoms, and exposure to a COVID-19 positive person.
8. Provide your vaccination status (Fully vaccinated with booster, Fully vaccinated without booster, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Prefer not to disclose).
9. Finally, acknowledge that you have read and understand the studio's safety protocols and accept the COVID-19 liability waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Date** (id: 2): Enter the date of the class you're registering for.
* **Class Time** (id: 3): Enter the time of the class you're registering for.
* **Class Type** (id: 4): Select the type of class you're interested in (e.g., Ballet, Jazz, Hip Hop, etc.).
* **Instructor Name** (id: 5): Enter your instructor's name.
* **Studio Location** (id: 6): Enter the location of the dance studio.
* **Student Name** (id: 8): Enter your name.
* **Student ID** (id: 9): Enter your student ID number.
* **Phone Number** (id: 10): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (id: 11): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Date of Birth** (id: 12): Enter your date of birth.
* **Experience Level** (id: 13): Select your experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional).
* **Health Screening Completed** (id: 15): Answer whether you've completed the health screening.
* **Temperature Check** (id: 16): Answer whether you have a normal temperature.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms Present** (id: 17): Answer whether you have COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Symptom Types** (id: 18): Describe any COVID-19 symptoms if you answer yes.
* **Exposure to COVID-19 Positive Person** (id: 19): Answer whether you've been exposed to a COVID-19 positive person.
* **Vaccination Status** (id: 21): Select your vaccination status.
* **Safety Protocols Acknowledged** (id: 23): Confirm that you've read and understand the studio's safety protocols.
* **Liability Waiver Accepted** (id: 24): Confirm that you accept the COVID-19 liability waiver.
* **Registration Date** (id: 25): Enter the date you're registering for the class.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you answer all required questions truthfully.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, reach out to the dance studio staff.
* This form is subject to change as the pandemic progresses, so please check back for updates.
