# COVID 19 Testing Demographic Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and health information from individuals in the household to help us better understand and manage COVID-19 testing and public health measures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, one page at a time.
2. Use the provided hints to guide you through each field.
3. Ensure that all family members fill out the form to provide a comprehensive view of the household.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date**: The date on which you are giving consent to provide your demographic and health information for public health purposes.
* **First Name**: Your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender**: Your self-identified gender.
* **Sex Assigned at Birth**: The sex assigned to you at birth. Please note that this field is for medical records and may not reflect your current identity.
* **Race**: One or more of the following options that you identify with:
	+ American Indian or Alaska Native
	+ Asian
	+ Black or African American
	+ Native Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander
	+ White
	+ Other
* **Ethnicity**: Your self-identified ethnicity.
* **Preferred Language**: The language you would prefer to communicate in:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ Chinese
	+ Vietnamese
	+ Korean
	+ Tagalog
	+ French
	+ Arabic
	+ Other
* **Need for Interpreter**: If you need assistance with language, select "Yes".
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
* **Home Address**: Your street address.
* **City**: Your city of residence.
* **State**: Your state of residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Your postal code.
* **Count of Residents**: Number of people living in your household.
* **Children under 18**: Number of children under 18 years old living in your household.
* **Adults 65 or Older**: Number of adults 65 or older living in your household.
* **Current Employment Status**: Your current employment status:
	+ Employed full-time
	+ Employed part-time
	+ Self-employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Student
	+ Retired
	+ Unable to work
	+ Other
* **Occupation**: Your current job or profession (optional).
* **Healthcare Worker**: If you are a healthcare worker, select "Yes".
* **Essential Worker**: If you are an essential worker, select "Yes".
* **Work Environment**: Where you primarily work:
	+ At home remotely
	+ In an office
	+ In a healthcare facility
	+ In a retail store
	+ In a school
	+ In a restaurant
	+ Outdoors
	+ Other
* **Health Insurance**: Your current health insurance status:
	+ Yes private insurance
	+ Yes Medicaid
	+ Yes Medicare
	+ Yes VA benefits
	+ No uninsured
* **Underlying Medical Conditions**: Any underlying medical conditions you have:
	+ None
	+ Heart disease
	+ Lung disease
	+ Diabetes
	+ Obesity
	+ Cancer
	+ Kidney disease
	+ Liver disease
	+ Immunocompromised
	+ Pregnancy
	+ Smoking history
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Your current COVID-19 vaccination status:
	+ Fully vaccinated with booster
	+ Fully vaccinated
	+ Partially vaccinated
	+ Not vaccinated
* **Consent to COVID-19 PCR Test**: If you consent to receive a COVID-19 PCR test, select "Yes I consent".
* **Data Collection Consent**: If you consent to the collection of your demographic and health information for public health purposes, select "Yes I consent".
* **Results Delivery Consent**: If you consent to receive your test results via your preferred contact method, select "Yes I consent".
* **HIPAA Acknowledgment**: If you have received the HIPAA Privacy Notice, select "Yes acknowledged".

## Tips
* Ensure that all family members complete the form completely and honestly.
* If you need assistance with language, please select "Yes" for "Need for Interpreter".
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for support.
* We will keep your data confidential and use it for public health purposes only.
