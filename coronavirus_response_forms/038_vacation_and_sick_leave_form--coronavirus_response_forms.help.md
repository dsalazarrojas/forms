# Vacation And Sick Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report your absence from work due to vacation or sickness. Please fill out this form to ensure that your absence is properly recorded and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the dates you will be absent from work.
2. Choose the reason for your absence (vacation or sick leave).
3. Enter the number of days you will be absent.
4. (Optional) Select the employee ID(s) of the employee(s) who will be affected by your absence.
5. Enter the email and phone number of your manager.
6. Provide any comments or explanations for your absence.
7. Select who submitted the form (HR, Manager, or Employee).
8. Enter the email and phone number of the person who submitted the form.
9. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vacation dates** (1): Enter the dates you will be absent from work.
* **Start date** (2): Enter the first day you will be absent from work.
* **End date** (3): Enter the last day you will be absent from work.
* **Number of days** (4): Enter the number of days you will be absent from work.
* **Reason for absence** (5): Choose the reason for your absence (vacation or sick leave).
* **Employee ID** (6): Select the employee ID of the employee(s) who will be affected by your absence. You can choose multiple IDs if necessary.
* **Manager email** (7): Enter the email address of your manager.
* **Manager phone** (8): Enter the phone number of your manager.
* **Comments** (9): Enter any comments or explanations for your absence.
* **Form submitted by** (10): Select who submitted the form (HR, Manager, or Employee).
* **Submitted by email** (11): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted by phone** (12): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
