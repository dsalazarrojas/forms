# Current FIS Saline COVID 19 Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals regarding their experiences with COVID-19, which can help health professionals and researchers better understand the pandemic and develop effective strategies to combat it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth, which should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide your Address, Phone Number, and Email if applicable.
4. Select all applicable Symptoms you are experiencing from the list.
5. Choose any relevant Medical Conditions you have.
6. Select any relevant Test Results you have received.
7. Indicate whether you have been hospitalized due to COVID-19.
8. Choose any medications you are currently taking.
9. Indicate whether you have received any vaccinations for COVID-19.
10. Provide the date of your test result, if applicable.
11. Describe your Medical History, if applicable.
