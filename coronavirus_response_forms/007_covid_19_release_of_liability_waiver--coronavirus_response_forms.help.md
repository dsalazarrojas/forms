# COVID 19 Release Of Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from participants about their health status, emergency contact, and liability release for COVID-19 related activities or services. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and relevance.
3. Sign the form to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waiver Date**: Date of this waiver. This field is required to keep track of the date you signed the form.
* **Participant Full Name**: Legal name of the participant. This field is required to identify you.
* **Date of Birth**: MM/DD/YYYY. This field is required to ensure you are at least 18 years old to sign this form.
* **Home Address**: Street address. This field is required to contact you.
* **City**: City of residence. This field is required to contact you.
* **State**: State or province. This field is required to contact you.
* **ZIP Code**: Postal code. This field is required to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number. This field is required to contact you.
* **Email Address**: Email address. This field is required to contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Full name of the emergency contact. This field is required.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of the emergency contact. This field is required.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Relationship to you. This field is required.
* **What activity or service are you participating in**: Select activity or service. This field is required.
* **Are you currently experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms**: Health screening. This field is required.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days**: Test history. This field is required.
* **Have you had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days**: Exposure history. This field is required.
* **I understand that participating in this activity may expose me to COVID-19**: Risk acknowledgment. This field is required.
* **I understand that my participation is voluntary and I assume all risks**: Voluntary participation. This field is required.
* **I agree to follow all COVID-19 safety protocols including mask wearing and social distancing**: Safety protocols agreement. This field is required.
* **I hereby release and discharge the organization from any liability arising from COVID-19 exposure during this activity**: Liability release. This field is required.
* **I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the organization from any claims related to COVID-19**: Indemnification agreement. This field is required.
* **I confirm that I am at least 18 years old and have the legal capacity to sign this waiver**: Legal capacity. This field is required.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Full name of the parent or guardian. This field is optional for minors.
* **Guardian Signature Date**: Date signed by the guardian. This field is optional for minors.
* **Participant Signature Date**: Date of agreement. This field is required.
* **Witness Name**: Full name of the witness. This field is optional.
* **Witness Signature Date**: Date witnessed. This field is optional.
