# Return To In Person School Information And Commitment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents to confirm their child's return to in-person school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name.
2. Enter the student's grade.
3. Enter the student's email address.
4. Enter the parent's name and email address.
5. Select the preferred contact method for the parent (Email, Phone, or Text).
6. Mark whether the student is committed to returning to in-person school or not.
7. If the student is committed, enter the number of days absent and the reason for absence.
8. Enter the date and time range when the student will be absent.
9. Enter the parent's date and time range when they will be absent.
10. Provide any additional comments about the student's absence.
11. Confirm parent consent.
12. Confirm student consent.
13. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parents Information** (Required)
	+ A place for parents to confirm their child's return to in-person school.
* **Parent Name** (Required)
	+ Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (Required)
	+ Enter the parent's email address.
* **Student Name** (Required)
	+ Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade** (Required)
	+ Enter the student's grade.
* **Parent Phone** (Required)
	+ Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Required)
	+ Select how the parent would like to be contacted.
* **Are Students Committed** (Required)
	+ Mark whether the student is committed to returning to in-person school or not.
* **Comments** (Optional)
	+ Enter any additional comments about the student's absence.
* **Parent Consent** (Required)
	+ Confirm parent consent.
* **Student Consent** (Required)
	+ Confirm student consent.
* **Student Absent Days** (Required)
	+ Enter the number of days the student will be absent.
* **Reason for Absence** (Required)
	+ Enter the reason for the student's absence.
* **Student Absent Date Range** (Required)
	+ Enter the date range when the student will be absent.
* **Student Absent Time Range** (Required)
	+ Enter the time range when the student will be absent.
* **Parents Absent Date Range** (Required)
	+ Enter the date range when the parent will be absent.
* **Parents Absent Time Range** (Required)
	+ Enter the time range when the parent will be absent.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your school administration for guidance.
* Do not duplicate this form for any other students.
