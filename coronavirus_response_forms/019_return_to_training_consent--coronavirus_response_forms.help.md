# Return To Training Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your willingness and ability to follow the safety guidelines and protocols in place for the training environment. Completing this form will help us assess your risk of transmitting or contracting the coronavirus and take necessary actions to maintain a safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. You may refer to the pages below for more detailed explanations on each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Training Facility Rules** (Page 1)
  * **Training Facility Rules** (`return_to_training_consent_1`, text, Optional): Please describe any rules or restrictions you are aware of or have been informed about for the training facility. This could include any COVID-19 related guidelines, personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements, or other safety protocols.

- **Personal Protective Equipment** (Page 2)
  * **Personal Protective Equipment** (`return_to_training_consent_2`, select_one, Optional): Please select whether you will be bringing and using personal protective equipment (PPE) during the training, such as masks, gloves, or face shields. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.

- **Reporting Incidents** (Page 3)
  * **Reporting Incidents** (`return_to_training_consent_3`, text, Optional): If you have any concerns or experience any issues during the training, please describe them here. This could be anything from symptoms of COVID-19 to equipment malfunctions or other safety hazards.

- **Emergency Contact** (Page 4)
  * **Emergency Contact** (`return_to_training_consent_4`, text, Optional): Please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person who should be notified in case of an emergency while you're at the training.

- **Emergency Contact Information** (Page 5)
  * **Emergency Contact Information** (`return_to_training_consent_5`, text, Optional): Provide additional contact information (e.g. phone number, email) for the emergency contact person mentioned above.

- **Medical Emergency** (Page 6)
  * **Medical Emergency** (`return_to_training_consent_6`, text, Optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in training, or if you have experienced any medical conditions related to COVID-19, describe them here.

- **Acknowledgement** (Page 7)
  * **Acknowledgement** (`return_to_training_consent_7`, select_multiple, Optional): Acknowledge that you have read and understood the training safety guidelines and protocols. Please select 'Yes' for each of the following: You have reviewed and understand the guidelines, You have a plan in place to follow these guidelines, and You will comply with these guidelines and protocols.

- **Additional Comments** (Page 8)
  * **Additional Comments** (`return_to_training_consent_8`, text, Optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns not covered in the previous questions, please share them here.

**Tips**: Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly to help us ensure a safe training environment. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
