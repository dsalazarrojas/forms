# COVID 19 Positive Diagnosis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees who have tested positive for COVID-19 to report their diagnosis details to the workplace. It helps the organization take necessary precautions and provide support.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your employee name and ID in the respective fields.
2. Select the type of test you underwent and the date of the test.
3. Provide the date when you first started experiencing symptoms and the last day you were at work.
4. List any colleagues you had close contact with at work (within 6 feet for 15+ minutes).
5. Select your current health status.
6. If applicable, enter your expected return date, subject to medical clearance.
7. Confirm that you understand the information will be used for workplace safety purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your official personnel record.
* **Employee ID** (text): Enter your employee ID as it appears on your official personnel record.
* **Department** (text): Enter your department name as it appears on your official personnel record.
* **Date of Positive Test** (date): Enter the date you received a positive COVID-19 test result.
* **Type of Test Performed** (select_one): Select the type of test you underwent, such as PCR or Rapid Antigen.
* **Date Symptoms Started** (date): Enter the date when you first started experiencing symptoms.
* **Last Day at Workplace** (date): Enter the last day you were at work before being diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Close Contacts at Work** (text): List names of colleagues you had close contact with at work (within 6 feet for 15+ minutes).
* **Current Health Status** (select_one): Select your current health status, such as recovering at home or hospitalized.
* **Expected Return Date** (date): Enter your expected return date to work, subject to medical clearance.
* **I understand this information will be used for workplace safety purposes** (select_one): Confirm that you understand the purpose of this form and the information will be used for workplace safety purposes.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely.
* If you have already submitted a previous version or your status has been updated, do not fill out this form again.
* If you are unsure about any section, refer to the field explanations above for guidance.
