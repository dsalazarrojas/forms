# COVID 19 Vaccine Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your facility's plans and preparations for COVID-19 vaccine distribution. This information will help public health officials understand your facility's capacity and needs, and provide guidance and support to ensure successful vaccine deployment.

## How To Complete This Form
The form is divided into several sections, each addressing specific aspects of vaccine planning. Please complete the following pages:

1. **Page 1: Contact Information**
   - **Facility or Organization Name**: Enter the name of your facility or organization.
   - **Type of Facility**: Select the type of facility or organization you are representing (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Pharmacy, etc.)
   - **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for this facility.
   - **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
   - **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.

2. **Vaccine Planning and Preparedness**
   - **Facility has pandemic vaccine plan in place**: Select 'True' if your facility has a plan, 'False' if you don't, and 'In development' if a plan is underway.
   - **Plan last reviewed or updated**: If a plan exists, provide the date it was last reviewed or updated.

3. **Vaccination Capacity**
   - **Expected Number of Vaccine Recipients**: Estimate the expected number of people to be vaccinated at your facility.
   - **Daily Vaccination Capacity**: Enter the number of people that can be vaccinated per day.
   - **Estimated Weeks to Vaccinate All**: If applicable, estimate the number of weeks needed to vaccinate all expected recipients.

4. **Storage and Equipment**
   - **Ultra-low Freezers available**: Enter the number of ultra-low freezers (capable of storing -80 degrees Celsius) available at your facility.
   - **Total ultra-low freezer capacity**: Enter the total capacity of ultra-low freezers in terms of vaccine doses.
   - **Standard Freezers available**: Enter the number of standard freezers (capable of storing -20 degrees Celsius) available at your facility.
   - **Total standard freezer capacity**: Enter the total capacity of standard freezers in terms of vaccine doses.
   - **Refrigerators available**: Enter the number of refrigerators (capable of storing 2-8 degrees Celsius) available at your facility.
   - **Total refrigerator capacity**: Enter the total capacity of refrigerators in terms of vaccine doses.
   - **Temperature monitoring equipment**: Select 'True' if you have temperature monitoring equipment, 'False' if not, and 'Partial' if partially available.
   - **Backup power system in place**: Select 'True' if you have a backup power system, 'False' if not, and 'In process' if a backup is underway.

5. **Staff Training and Readiness**
   - **Staff trained in cold chain management**: Select 'True' if staff are trained, 'False' if not, and 'Some staff' if partially trained.
   - **Staff trained in vaccine administration**: Select 'True' if staff are trained, 'False' if not, and 'Some staff' if partially trained.
   - **Staff trained in emergency response**: Select 'True' if staff are trained, 'False' if not, and 'Some staff' if partially trained.

6. **Communication Plan**
   - **Plan to communicate with public about vaccines**: Select 'True' if a plan exists, 'False' if not, and 'In development' if a plan is underway.
   - **Plan for reporting vaccination data**: Select 'True' if a plan exists, 'False' if not, and 'Unsure' if unsure.

7. **Anticipated Challenges**
   - **Challenges anticipated**: Select all challenges that apply from the list provided.

8. **Strategies to Address Challenges**
   - Provide a brief description of strategies to address each challenge identified.

9. **Support Needed from Government or Public Health**
   - Briefly describe any support needed from government or public health authorities.

Please ensure that you have reviewed the entire form for accuracy and completeness before submitting. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact your supervisor or facility administrator.
