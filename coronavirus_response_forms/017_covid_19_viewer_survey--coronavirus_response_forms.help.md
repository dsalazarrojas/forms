# COVID 19 Viewer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the COVID-19 viewer survey form, explaining each field and its purpose. Please read this guide to ensure you complete the form accurately and with confidence.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your details, if you choose to do so.
2. Select an age group that best describes you.
3. Indicate how COVID-19 has impacted you personally.
4. Choose how concerned you are about health risks.
5. Assess the severity of the COVID-19 pandemic.
6. Share your opinion on the government's response.
7. Evaluate the media's coverage of the pandemic.
8. Consider the importance of vaccination.
9. Share your views on public health measures.
10. Predict when the pandemic will end.
11. Identify your trusted information sources.
12. Express your concerns about the economic impact.
13. If you wish, add additional comments or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, Optional): Please enter your name, if you choose to do so. This is optional and will help us understand who is taking the survey.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address, if you choose to do so. This will help us contact you for follow-up, if needed.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, Required): Choose an age group that best describes you:
	+ Under 18
	+ 18-29
	+ 30-44
	+ 45-59
	+ 60-74
	+ 75 or older
* **Region or Location** (`location`, `text`, Optional): If you'd like to specify a region or location, please enter it here. This helps us understand the geographical diversity of responses.
* **COVID-19 Has Personally Impacted Me** (`pandemic_impact`, `select_one`, Required): Indicate how COVID-19 has personally impacted you:
	+ Very significantly
	+ Somewhat
	+ Minimally
	+ Not at all
* **Level of Health Concern** (`health_concern`, `select_one`, Required): How concerned are you about health risks:
	+ Extremely concerned
	+ Very concerned
	+ Somewhat concerned
	+ Not very concerned
	+ Not concerned at all
* **Severity of the Pandemic** (`pandemic_severity`, `select_one`, Required): Assess the severity of the COVID-19 pandemic:
	+ Extremely serious
	+ Very serious
	+ Moderately serious
	+ Slightly serious
	+ Not serious at all
* **Opinion on Government Response** (`government_response`, `select_one`, Optional): Share your opinion on the government's response:
	+ Strongly approve
	+ Approve
	+ Neutral
	+ Disapprove
	+ Strongly disapprove
* **Opinion on Media Coverage** (`media_coverage`, `select_one`, Optional): Evaluate the fairness of media reporting on COVID-19:
	+ Very fair and balanced
	+ Somewhat fair
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat biased
	+ Very biased
* **Importance of Vaccination** (`vaccination_importance`, `select_one`, Required): Choose how important vaccination is to you:
	+ Extremely important
	+ Very important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not very important
	+ Not important at all
* **Opinion on Public Health Measures** (`public_health_measures`, `select_one`, Optional): Share your views on public health measures like masks, distancing, and quarantine:
	+ Strongly support
	+ Support
	+ Neutral
	+ Oppose
	+ Strongly oppose
* **When Will Pandemic End** (`pandemic_timeline`, `select_one`, Optional): Predict when you think the pandemic will end:
	+ Within next few months
	+ Within next year
	+ More than a year away
	+ Will never fully end
	+ Do not know
* **Most Trusted Information Sources** (`trusted_information_sources`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Identify your most trusted sources of information on the pandemic:
	+ Healthcare providers
	+ Government health agencies
	+ Academic institutions
	+ News organizations
	+ Social media
	+ Friends and family
	+ Religious leaders
* **Concern About Economic Impact** (`economic_impact_concern`, `select_one`, Optional): Express your concern about the economic impact:
	+ Very concerned
	+ Somewhat concerned
	+ Neutral
	+ Not very concerned
	+ Not concerned at all
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): If you have any additional thoughts or concerns about the pandemic, please share them here.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* You can skip any field you prefer not to answer.
* The form is designed to be as clear and concise as possible.
* If you have any further questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for participating in this survey! Your input is valuable.
