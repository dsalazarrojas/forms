# Immunization Consent Form CPESN Pharmacy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immunization Consent Form CPESN Pharmacy is a form designed to document patient consent for immunization. This form helps gather essential information from patients before proceeding with immunization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Select your preferred payment options from the **payment_preference** field.
3. Schedule an appointment for the immunization in the **schedule_appointment** field (if applicable).
4. Enter your phone number for communication and appointment reminders in the **phone_number** field.
5. Provide your email address for secure contact and appointment updates in the **email** field.
6. Share any relevant medical details or allergies in the **medical_details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name (Text)**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification card or medical records.
* **payment_preference (Select Multiple)**: Choose how you would like to pay for the immunization: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **schedule_appointment (Date)**: Select a date and time for your scheduled appointment. This is optional and only required if you need to schedule an appointment.
* **phone_number (Text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication and appointment reminders.
* **email (Email)**: Enter your email address for secure contact and appointment updates.
* **medical_details (Text)**: Describe any relevant medical details or allergies that may be relevant to your immunization. This can include medical history, allergies, or previous reactions to vaccines.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review and sign the form before submitting it.
* Ensure you understand the payment options and schedule appointment requirements.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to our staff.
