# COVID 19 Vaccination Center Front Desk Daily Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential data from front desk staff at COVID-19 vaccination centers to report key metrics and incidents that occur during their shift. The form helps centers track their daily performance, identify areas of improvement, and ensure efficient vaccine distribution. It also helps front desk staff report any issues, incidents, or concerns that need attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Date**: Enter the date of the report using the date field. This is the date when you're submitting the report.
2. **Vaccination Center Details**: Enter the name of your vaccination center, its address, city, state, and ZIP code. This helps us track the center's performance and provide accurate information to the public.
3. **Shift Information**: Select the type of shift you worked (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Full Day) and enter the start and end times of your shift.
4. **Appointments and Vaccinations**: Report the number of appointments scheduled, patients registered, informed about the vaccine, and vaccinated. This helps us track the center's capacity and vaccine distribution.
5. **Equipment and Staffing**: Report any equipment issues, staffing levels, and vaccine inventory status. This helps us identify potential problems and improve resource allocation.
6. **Patient Interactions**: Report any patient complaints, compliments, and adverse events. This helps us understand patient satisfaction and potential issues.
7. **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the team.
8. **Supervisor Name and Signature**: If you have a supervisor or manager, please enter their name and sign the form with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Report Date**: (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date when submitting the report.
- **Vaccination Center Name**: (`vaccination_center_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your vaccination center.
