# covid_19_informed_consent_for_in_person_physical_therapy_services - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information for informed consent from clients for in-person physical therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form carefully and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
- Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, please ask your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical Condition** (`What Is Your Medical Condition`, select_multiple): Please select all applicable medical conditions that you are seeking physical therapy for. This will help us understand your specific needs and provide you with the best possible care.
- **Contact Method** (`How Do You Wish To Be Contacted`, select_one): Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up or emergency purposes. This will help us reach you in case we need to communicate with you.
- **Patient Name** (`What Is Your Name`, text): Please enter your full name as it is to be used for medical purposes.
- **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text): Enter a contact number where we can reach you during business hours.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): If you have an email address where we can reach you, please enter it.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their name and number.
- **Relationship To Patient** (`Relationship To Patient`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship you have with the patient (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).
- **Emergency Contact Number** (`Emergency Contact Number`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's contact number.
- **Tips**
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
