# COVID 19 Nail Salons Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Nail Salons Treatment Consent Form is designed to ensure a safe and healthy experience for all clients. This form gathers information about the client's health status, allergies, and consent for treatment to minimize the risk of COVID-19 transmission and any potential allergic reactions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and instructions.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information (Page 1)
* **Full Name** (*): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Phone Number** (*): Provide your phone number for contact and scheduling purposes.
* **Email Address** (*): Enter your email address for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose your preferred appointment time.

### Health Assessment (Page 2)
* **COVID-19 Symptoms in past 14 days**: Indicate if you have experienced any COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days.
* **List Symptoms if Applicable**: If you answered "True" to the previous question, list any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Temperature above 100.4F in past 24 hours**: Indicate if you have had a temperature above 100.4F in the past 24 hours.
* **Exposed to COVID-19 in past 14 days**: Indicate if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **Tested Positive for COVID-19 in past 10 days**: Indicate if you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days.
* **Vaccination Status**: Select your current vaccination status.
* **Pre-existing health conditions**: Indicate if you have any pre-existing health conditions.
* **Describe conditions if Applicable**: If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe your conditions.
* **Describe conditions if Applicable**: If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe your conditions.

### Allergies and Sensitivities (Page 3)
* **Known Allergies to nail products**: Indicate if you have any known allergies to nail products.
* **List Allergies**: If you answered "True" to the previous question, list your allergies.
* **Current skin conditions or cuts**: Indicate if you have any current skin conditions or cuts.
* **Describe skin conditions**: If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe your conditions.

### Consent and Agreement (Page 4)
* **I understand COVID-19 transmission risks**: Select "Yes, I Understand" to confirm you understand the risks.
* **I acknowledge safety measures in place**: Indicate if you acknowledge the safety measures in place.
* **I consent to proceed with service**: Select "Yes, I Consent" to confirm you consent to treatment.
* **Consent Date**: Select the date you give your consent.

Note: Please read the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully to ensure a safe and healthy experience for yourself and the nail salon team.
