# Covid 19 Attendance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record the attendance status of patients visiting a medical practice. It is crucial to fill this form accurately to ensure that the patient's attendance status is correctly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that you are not duplicating any existing entries.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please consult the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Practice** (`medical_practice`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical practice where the patient visited.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient who visited the medical practice.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's appointment.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Select the attendance status of the patient. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Present
	+ Absent
	+ Rescheduled
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the patient's attendance.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, select_multiple, required): Select the contact method of the patient.
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ In Person
* **Medical Practice Name** (`medical_practice_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical practice.
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the staff member who attended to the patient.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, text, required/optional): Enter the medical specialty of the patient.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of appointment.
	+ Routine
	+ Emergency
	+ Follow-up
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's condition.
* **Medical Note** (`medical_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional medical notes related to the patient's visit.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the patient's visit.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the patient.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the patient.
* **Medical Practice Address** (`medical_practice_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Zip** (`medical_practice_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the medical practice.
* **Staff Number** (`staff_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the staff number of the medical practice.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical license status of the staff member.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Medical Specialty Type** (`medical_specialty_type`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of medical specialty.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of the patient.
* **Medical Practice Phone** (`medical_practice_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Fax** (`medical_practice_fax`, text, required/optional): Enter the fax number of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Email** (`medical_practice_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the medical practice.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you are duplicating any existing entries, please do not submit this form.
