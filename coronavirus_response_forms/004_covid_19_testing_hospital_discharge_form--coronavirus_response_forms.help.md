# COVID 19 Testing Hospital Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Testing Hospital Discharge Form is used to gather information about a patient's COVID-19 status, hospital stay, and post-discharge care instructions. This form is essential for ensuring that the patient receives proper care and follow-up after discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review each field and ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
- Enter patient information in the correct format (e.g., date fields should be in MM-DD-YYYY format).
- Select the correct options for select fields (e.g., gender, final COVID-19 test result).
- If a patient has a follow-up appointment scheduled, enter the date in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Full Name** (Patient Full Name, text, required)
  - Enter the patient's complete legal name (e.g., John Doe).
- **Medical Record Number** (Medical Record Number, text, required)
  - Enter the patient's hospital MRN or patient ID number.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required)
  - Enter the patient's birthdate in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 01-15-1990).
- **Gender** (Gender, select_one, required)
  - Select the patient's gender (Male, Female, Other, Prefer Not to Say).
- **Patient Phone Number** (Patient Phone Number, text, required)
  - Enter the patient's primary contact number.
- **Patient Email Address** (Patient Email Address, email, optional)
  - Enter the patient's primary email address for follow-up communication.
- **Hospital Admission Date** (Hospital Admission Date, date, required)
  - Enter the date the patient was admitted to the hospital.
- **Hospital Discharge Date** (Hospital Discharge Date, date, required)
  - Enter the date the patient is being discharged from the hospital.
- **Attending Physician Name** (Attending Physician Name, text, required)
  - Enter the name of the primary treating physician.
- **Hospital Department** (Hospital Department, text, required)
  - Enter the department where the patient was treated.
- **COVID-19 Status at Discharge** (COVID-19 Status at Discharge, note, required)
  - Enter a brief description of the patient's current COVID-19 status.
- **Final COVID-19 Test Result** (Final COVID-19 Test Result, select_one, required)
  - Select the result of the most recent COVID-19 test (Positive, Negative, Inconclusive, Not Tested).
- **Date of Final COVID Test** (Date of Final COVID Test, date, required)
  - Enter the date of the most recent COVID-19 test.
- **Patient Vaccination Status** (Patient Vaccination Status, select_one, required)
  - Select the patient's current COVID-19 vaccination status (Unvaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, Fully Vaccinated, Boosted).
- **Discharge Instructions** (Discharge Instructions, note, required)
  - Enter any post-discharge care instructions or guidance.
- **Activity Restrictions** (Activity Restrictions, select_multiple, optional)
  - Select any activity restrictions for the patient's post-discharge recovery (Complete Bed Rest, Limited Activity, Light Activity Allowed, Return to Normal Activity, No Restrictions).
- **Recommended Isolation Period - Days** (Recommended Isolation Period - Days, number, optional)
  - Enter the number of days recommended for post-discharge isolation.
- **Follow-Up Appointment Date** (Follow-Up Appointment Date, date, optional)
  - Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
- **Medications Prescribed at Discharge** (Medications Prescribed at Discharge, text, optional)
  - List any medications prescribed for the patient at discharge.
- **Additional Discharge Instructions** (Additional Discharge Instructions, text, optional)
  - Enter any additional discharge instructions or guidance.
- **Patient Cleared for Discharge** (Patient Cleared for Discharge, select_one, required)
  - Select the physician's confirmation of discharge readiness (Patient is medically cleared for discharge, Conditional discharge with follow-up required, Patient requests early discharge).
