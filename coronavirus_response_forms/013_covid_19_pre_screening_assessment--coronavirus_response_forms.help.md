# COVID-19 Pre-Screening Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help identify potential COVID-19 cases and provide guidance on further action. Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct answer for each question.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Assessment** (Required): Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number for follow-up and contact.
* **-- Symptoms Checklist --** (Not Required): Answer the following questions about your current symptoms:
	+ **Fever (100.4 F or higher)** (Required): Answer 'True' if you have a fever of 100.4 F or higher.
	+ **New or worsening dry cough** (Required): Answer 'True' if you have a new or worsening dry cough.
	+ **Unusual fatigue or muscle aches** (Required): Answer 'True' if you have unusual fatigue or muscle aches.
	+ **New loss of taste or smell** (Required): Answer 'True' if you have a new loss of taste or smell.
* **-- Exposure and Travel --** (Not Required): Answer the following questions:
	+ **Have you traveled outside the state/country in the last 14 days?** (Required): Answer 'True' if you have traveled outside the state/country in the last 14 days.
	+ **Have you had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the last 14 days?** (Required): Answer 'True' if you have had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the last 14 days.
