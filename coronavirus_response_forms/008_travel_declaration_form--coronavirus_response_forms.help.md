# Travel Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Declaration Form is a tool used to gather information about an individual's travel history and health status, especially in relation to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields. While some fields are optional, filling them out will provide more detailed information and help in the assessment of your travel and health status.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can ask for assistance from a healthcare professional or the person administering the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical Status
• Medical Status (Free of COVID-19, Has Fever, Has Cough, Has Fatigue, Has Sore Throat, Has Runny Nose/Sore Throat, Has Shortness of Breath, Has Other (please specify), Unknown): Please select all the medical conditions related to COVID-19 that you are experiencing.

### Travel History
• Travel History: Please describe your recent travel history, including any countries or regions you have visited.

### Symptoms
• Symptoms (Fever, Cough, Fatigue, Sore Throat, Runny Nose/Sore Throat, Shortness of Breath, Other (please specify)): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing related to COVID-19.

### Recent Travel
• Recent Travel: If you have recently traveled, please select the date of your most recent travel.

### Travel Date
• Travel Date: If you have traveled in the past, please select the date of your last travel.

### Return Date
• Return Date: If you have traveled recently and are expected to return, please select the date of your return.

### Contact Details
• Contact Details: Please provide your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.

### Travel Reason
• Travel Reason: Please describe the reason for your recent travel.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for any commercial purposes.
