# Quarantine Birthday Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and track quarantine birthday events. It's a way for participants to share their details and preferences with the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Party Details page with information about the birthday party.
2. Provide your Participant Details on the second page.
3. Choose how you would like to connect with other participants on the third page.
4. Enter the number of participants attending the event on the fourth page.
5. Write a birthday wish or a message to share with others on the fifth page (optional).
6. If you have any special requests, you can add them on the sixth page (optional).
7. Review and fill in the Form Submission page with any additional information or comments.
8. Enter the Form Owner's name or ID on the seventh page.
9. Enter the Tool ID on the eighth page (optional).
10. Finally, select the date and time you submitted the form on the ninth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Details** (`party_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the birthday party, such as "John's 30th birthday" or "Quarantine Birthday Bash".
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Provide your name and any relevant details about yourself.
* **Connection Details** (`connection_details`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to connect with other participants (e.g., Zoom, Second Life).
* **Number of Participants** (`participant_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of participants attending the event.
* **Birthday Wish** (`birthday_wish`, text, optional): Write a birthday wish or a message to share with others.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, add them here.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your form submission.
* **Form Owner** (`form_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name or ID of the form owner.
* **Tool ID** (`tool_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the tool used to access this form (e.g., Zoom, Google Forms).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Select the date you submitted this form.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Select the time you submitted this form.
