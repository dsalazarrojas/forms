# roc_performance_therapy_client_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients regarding their medical conditions, treatment plans, and consent to receive services for performance therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out each section as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. Be sure to review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
3. If you are unsure about any section, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Consent** (`14`, `select_one`, required): This field provides an opportunity for you to choose one of the following options regarding your consent:
	+ Client Consent Form 1
	+ Client Consent Form 2
	+ Client Consent Form 3
* **Client Name** (`4`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information** (`5`, `text`, required): Please provide your contact information, including your address and any other relevant contact details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`7`, `text`, required): Please enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`8`, `text`, required): Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, or friend).
* **Medical Condition** (`9`, `text`, required): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in this field.
* **Medical Condition Details** (`10`, `text`, required): Please provide more information about your medical conditions, including any relevant medical history or symptoms.
* **Treatement Plan** (`11`, `text`, required): Please describe your treatment plan, including any medications or therapies you are currently undergoing.
* **Treatement Plan Details** (`12`, `text`, required): Please provide more information about your treatment plan, including any specific goals or objectives.
* **Insurance Information** (`13`, `text`, required): Please provide your insurance information, including your policy number and provider.
* **Client Notes** (`15`, `text`, required): Please provide any relevant notes or information about your condition or treatment plan that you would like to share with your healthcare provider.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
