# Conference Exhibitor Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Conference Exhibitor Interest Survey is a form designed to collect information from potential exhibitors about their interests and needs for the conference exhibition. This information will help the conference organizers tailor the exhibition experience to meet your needs and ensure a successful and enjoyable experience for all exhibitors.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2.  Be as detailed as possible when providing additional information in optional fields.
3.  If you have any special requirements or requests, please specify them in the "Special Requirements" field.
4.  If you have any other comments or information that may be helpful for the conference organizers, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Industry**: Select the sector that best describes your organization's industry.
* **Company Size**: Select the number of employees in your organization.
* **Company Location**: Enter the city where your organization's headquarters is located.
* **Exhibitor Interest**: Provide a brief explanation of your exhibition plans.
* **Interest Level**: Indicate how interested you are in exhibiting at the conference.
* **Booth Size Preference**: Select the size of booth you would prefer to have.
* **Previous Exhibitor**: Indicate if you have exhibited at this conference before or if this is your first time exhibiting.
* **Exhibition Goals**: Select all the goals you hope to achieve by exhibiting at the conference.
* **Products to Display**: Describe the products or services you plan to showcase.
* **Special Requirements**: Specify any special requirements you may have for the exhibition.
* **Budget Range**: Select the budget range you are willing to allocate for the exhibition.
* **Sponsorship Interest**: Indicate if you are interested in additional sponsorship opportunities.
* **Sponsorship Level**: Select the tier of sponsorship you are interested in.
* **Marketing Materials**: Select any marketing materials you need from the organizers.
* **Decision Timeline**: Indicate when you will make your decision to exhibit.
* **Additional Services**: Select any additional services you may need from the organizers.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Indicate how you became aware of the conference.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other information that may be helpful for the conference organizers.
