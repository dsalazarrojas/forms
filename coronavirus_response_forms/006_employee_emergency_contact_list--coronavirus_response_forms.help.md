# Employee Emergency Contact List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and maintain an up-to-date list of emergency contacts for employees in the organization. This information is crucial in case of an emergency or unexpected event that requires immediate attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate fields to fill in the information for each employee.
2. Enter the employee's name, contact name, phone number, email, and address, as well as other relevant details such as department, job title, and location.
3. Fill in the emergency contact's name, phone number, email, and address for each employee.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: (`employee_name`, text, required) Enter the name of the employee.
* **Contact Name**: (`contact_name`, text, required) Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, required) Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: (`contact_email`, email, required) Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Emergency Name**: (`emergency_name`, text, required) Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone**: (`emergency_phone`, text, required) Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: (`emergency_relationship`, text, required) Describe the relationship between the employee and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Address**: (`emergency_address`, text, required) Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Department**: (`department`, text, required) Enter the department or team that the employee belongs to.
* **Job Title**: (`job_title`, text, required) Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Location**: (`location`, text, required) Enter the location where the employee is usually based.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required) Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required) Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required) Describe the relationship between the employee and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required) Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Employee ID**: (`employee_id`, number, required) Enter the ID number of the employee.
* **Department ID**: (`department_id`, number, required) Enter the ID number of the department.
* **Job Title ID**: (`job_title_id`, number, required) Enter the ID number of the job title.
* **Location ID**: (`location_id`, number, required) Enter the ID number of the location.
* **Employee Name ID**: (`employee_name_id`, number, required) Enter the ID number of the employee's name.
* **Contact Name ID**: (`contact_name_id`, number, required) Enter the ID number of the contact's name.
* **Emergency Name ID**: (`emergency_name_id`, number, required) Enter the ID number of the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Name ID**: (`emergency_contact_name_id`, number, required) Enter the ID number of the emergency contact's name.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use the same format for entering all emergency contact information for consistency.
* If there are any discrepancies or inconsistencies, report them to your supervisor or HR representative.

This help guide is designed to assist users in understanding the purpose and requirements of the Employee Emergency Contact List form. It's essential to follow these guidelines to ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly, which is crucial for maintaining a secure and efficient emergency contact list.
