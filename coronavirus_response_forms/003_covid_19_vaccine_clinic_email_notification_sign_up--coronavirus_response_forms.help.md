# COVID 19 Vaccine Clinic Email Notification Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals register for email notifications about COVID-19 vaccine availability at a specific clinic. By filling out this form, you will receive updates about vaccine availability, making it easier to plan your vaccination schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please review the fields carefully and ensure you answer each required field accurately.
* Select the correct age range and priority status for your vaccination.
* Indicate your willingness to travel for vaccination.
* Confirm your eligibility for email notifications and provide your contact information (including your phone number and email address).
* Make any other necessary selections for your vaccination status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, required)**: Please enter your first name as it is spelled on official documents.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required)**: Enter your last name as it is spelled on official documents.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required)**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive vaccine availability notifications. You will receive regular updates about vaccine availability at this address.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number to receive text-based updates about vaccine availability (if you choose this option in the notification frequency selection).
* **Age Range (Age Range, select_one, required)**: Select your age group for vaccination purposes:
	+ 18-25
	+ 26-35
	+ 36-50
	+ 51-65
	+ 65+
* **Zip Code or Service Area (Zip Code or Service Area, text, optional)**: Enter your zip code or indicate your service area for vaccination purposes.
* **What is your vaccination priority status? (What is your vaccination priority status?, select_one, required)**: Select your priority status for vaccination:
	+ Healthcare worker
	+ Essential worker
	+ Elderly or immunocompromised
	+ General population
	+ Prefer not to say
* **How often would you like to receive notifications? (How often would you like to receive notifications?, select_one, optional)**: Select your desired notification frequency:
	+ Every update
	+ Daily summary
	+ Weekly summary
	+ As needed
* **Have you received your first vaccine dose? (Have you received your first vaccine dose?, select_one, required)**: Indicate if you have received your first vaccine dose:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
	+ Not applicable
* **How far are you willing to travel for vaccination? (How far are you willing to travel for vaccination?, select_one, optional)**: Select how far you are willing to travel for vaccination:
	+ Within 5 miles
	+ Within 10 miles
	+ Within 25 miles
	+ No limit
* **I agree to receive email notifications about vaccine availability (I agree to receive email notifications about vaccine availability, select_one, required)**: Confirm your agreement to receive email notifications:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **I consent to my information being used for vaccine notification purposes (I consent to my information being used for vaccine notification purposes, select_one, required)**: Confirm your consent to use your information for vaccine notification purposes:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
