# COVID 19 Pledge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to take a proactive stance against the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the Pledger Information section, which includes your name, email address, phone number, and organization (if applicable).
2.  In the Pledge Commitment section, write down your personal commitment to fight COVID-19.
3.  In the Additional Actions section, describe any other ways you plan to help fight COVID-19.
4.  In the Pledge Signature section, confirm your commitment by selecting "Yes, I pledge."
5.  Finally, select a date for your pledge.

*   **I will stay home when I am sick**: Select "True" if you plan to stay home when you are sick.
*   **I will wear a mask**: Select "True" if you plan to wear a mask when around others.
*   **I will practice social distancing**: Select "True" if you plan to practice social distancing.
