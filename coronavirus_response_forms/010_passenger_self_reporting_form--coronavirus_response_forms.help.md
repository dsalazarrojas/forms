# Passenger Self Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from passengers who have come into contact with a COVID-19 positive individual on a flight. This helps the public health authorities to quickly identify and contact individuals who may have been infected with the virus.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the questions and what information is required.
2. Enter your contact information (name and phone number).
3. Answer questions about your travel history and recent contact with a COVID-19 positive individual.
4. Indicate whether you are experiencing any symptoms of COVID-19.
5. Enter any additional comments or details in the "Contact Details" field.
6. If prompted, enter your email address.
7. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name and phone number so we can contact you regarding any further questions or concerns.
* **Travel History and Contact** (`travel_details`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select whether you have:
	+ Travelled recently on a flight or other mode of transport that brought you into contact with a COVID-19 positive individual.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please indicate if you are experiencing any of the following:
	+ Yes, I have symptoms of COVID-19.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `note`, required/optional): Please enter any additional comments or details about your recent travel history or contact with a COVID-19 positive individual.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you regarding any further questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding any further questions or concerns.

## Tips

* If you are experiencing symptoms or have come into contact with a COVID-19 positive individual, please submit this form only once and do not duplicate the form submission.
* Ensure that your phone and email numbers are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact your local public health authority for further guidance.
