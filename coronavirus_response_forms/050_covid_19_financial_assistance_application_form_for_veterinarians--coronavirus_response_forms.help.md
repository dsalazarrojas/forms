# COVID 19 Financial Assistance Application Form For Veterinarians - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for financial assistance related to the economic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on your veterinary practice.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and honest information about your business and financial situation.
2. Make sure to include all required fields and provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date**: Date you are submitting this application
* **Veterinarian Name**: Your full legal name
* **Veterinary License Number**: Your state veterinary license number
* **License State**: State where you are licensed
* **Area of Specialization**: Select your area of specialization (e.g., Small Animal, Large Animal, etc.)
* **Email Address**: Your email address (must be a valid email)
* **Phone Number**: Your practice's phone number
* **Street Address**: Your practice's street address
* **City**: City where your practice is located
* **State/Province**: State or province where your practice is located
* **Zip Code**: Your practice's zip code
* **Veterinary Practice Name**: Name of your veterinary practice
* **Practice Owner**: Select whether you are the practice owner or not
* **Type of Practice**: Select your practice type (e.g., Private Practice, Corporate Clinic, etc.)
* **Practice Location**: Select your practice location (e.g., Urban, Suburban, etc.)
* **Annual Income Before COVID-19**: Approximate pre-pandemic income
* **Current Annual Income**: Estimated income for current year
* **Percentage of Income Lost**: Due to COVID-19 impact
* **How Has COVID-19 Impacted Your Practice?**: Describe the specific impact of COVID-19 on your practice
* **Business Challenges Faced**: Select all challenges faced by your practice due to COVID-19 (e.g., Loss of revenue/client base, etc.)
* **Average Monthly Practice Expenses**: Approximate monthly expenses for your practice
* **Number of Employees**: Number of staff employed by your practice
* **Employees Laid Off Due to COVID-19**: Number of staff affected
* **Outstanding Business Loan**: Approximate balance of outstanding business loan
* **Personal Savings Available**: Approximate amount of personal savings
* **Personal Debts**: Approximate amount of personal debts (e.g., student loans, credit cards, etc.)
* **Financial Assistance Already Received**: Describe any financial assistance already received
* **Total Assistance Received**: Dollar amount of financial assistance received
* **Amount of Assistance Requested**: How much financial assistance you are requesting
* **How Will You Use the Financial Assistance?**: Describe the intended use of funds
* **Statement of Financial Hardship**: Explain your financial situation in detail
* **Expected Recovery Timeline**: Select your expected recovery timeline (e.g., 1-3 months, etc.)
