# COVID 19 Employee Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about an employee's current health status, potential exposure to COVID-19, and work capacity in relation to the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Employee ID**, and **Department** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** accurately.
3. Rate your **Overall Health** using the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Indicate if you are currently experiencing any symptoms related to COVID-19, such as Fever or chills, Cough, etc.
5. If you are experiencing symptoms, describe their severity: Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Not applicable.
6. Select your **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** from the provided options: Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Prefer not to answer.
7. If you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, select the correct option: False, Yes, currently, or Yes, previously.
8. If you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, provide the date of diagnosis.
9. Indicate if you have had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the last 14 days.
10. Rate your ability to perform your job duties safely using the provided options: Yes, fully capable, Yes, with accommodations, or No, not at this time.
11. Select your work environment from the options: Remote, On-site, or Hybrid.
12. Choose the recommended action for your situation from the options: Continue normal work, Work from home, Medical leave, or Requires medical evaluation.
13. If applicable, add any additional notes about your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as per your official identification.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID as per your official identification.
* **Department**: Enter your department as per your official records.
* **Date of Assessment**: Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your official phone number.
* **Overall Health**: Rate your overall health from the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Current Symptoms**: Indicate if you are experiencing any symptoms related to COVID-19.
* **Symptom Severity**: If you are experiencing symptoms, select their severity: Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Not applicable.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Select your vaccination status from the provided options: Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Prefer not to answer.
* **COVID-19 Diagnosis**: If you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, select the correct option.
* **Date of COVID-19 Diagnosis**: If you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, provide the date of diagnosis.
* **Close Contact with COVID-19 Case**: Indicate if you have had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the last 14 days.
* **Work Readiness**: Rate your ability to perform your job duties safely using the provided options: Yes, fully capable, Yes, with accommodations, or No, not at this time.
* **Work Environment**: Select your work environment from the options: Remote, On-site, or Hybrid.
* **Recommended Action**: Choose the recommended action for your situation.
* **Additional Notes**: If applicable, add any additional notes about your current situation.
