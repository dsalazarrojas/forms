# Interaction History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Interaction History Form is designed to record and document the interactions between patients and healthcare providers. This form is essential for maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of patient interactions, which can be used to improve communication, reduce errors, and enhance patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "patient-name" field.
2. Enter the date of the patient's visit in the "patient-date-of-visit" field.
3. Provide the location where the interaction took place in the "patient-location" field.
4. Select the type of interaction that occurred (Healthcare, Contact, or Discussion) in the "interaction" field.
5. Enter the date of the visit in the "visit-date" field.
6. Indicate whether the patient's mobile phone was connected or not in the "app-connected" field.
7. Enter the name of the application used for the interaction in the "app-name" field.
8. Enter the patient's email address in the "patient-email" field.
9. Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient-phone-number" field.
10. Specify the patient's relationship with the healthcare provider in the "patient-relationship" field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the patient in the "patient-notes" field.
12. Enter the name of the healthcare provider in the "provider-name" field.
13. Enter the provider's email address in the "provider-email" field.
14. Enter the provider's phone number in the "provider-phone-number" field.
15. Enter any additional notes about the healthcare provider in the "provider-notes" field.
16. Enter any medical notes about the patient in the "patient-medical-notes" field.
17. Enter any medical notes about the healthcare provider in the "provider-medical-notes" field.
18. Specify the provider's relationship with the patient in the "provider-relationship" field.
19. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form in the "form-submitted-by" field.
