# Student COVID 19 Self Certification And Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student COVID 19 Self Certification And Verification Form is a self-attesting form designed to collect information from students to identify potential COVID-19 cases and prevent the spread of the virus. This form is intended to be filled out by students who have been exposed to or suspect they may have COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers based on your current health status.
2. Enter your student ID if required.
3. Select the date on which you started experiencing symptoms or were diagnosed with COVID-19.
4. Answer whether you are experiencing symptoms or not.
5. Select your current temperature (optional).
6. Answer whether you have a travel history or not.
7. Select whether you have come into contact with anyone with COVID-19 or not.
8. Answer whether you have any underlying medical conditions or not.
9. Select whether you have attended school since the onset of symptoms or not.
10. Confirm whether you are certified as COVID-19 positive or not.
10. Enter the date of your certification (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Screening** (`daily_screening`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you have been experiencing daily screening or not.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date on which you started experiencing symptoms or were diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether you are experiencing symptoms or not.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, required): Select your current temperature.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have a travel history or not.
* **Contact** (`contact`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether you have come into contact with anyone with COVID-19 or not.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have any underlying medical conditions or not.
* **School Attendance** (`school_attendance`, select_one, required): Select whether you have attended school since the onset of symptoms or not.
* **Certification** (`certification`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are certified as COVID-19 positive or not.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your certification (if applicable).
