# Covid 19 Daily Status Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Daily Status Update Form is a critical tool for healthcare professionals to report daily updates on coronavirus cases. It helps track patient status, vaccination status, and medical team information, ensuring timely and accurate reporting.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Provide the patient's date of birth in the **patient_dob** field.
3. Select the patient's **Vaccination Status** from the dropdown menu.
4. If applicable, enter the date of vaccination in the **vaccination_date** field.
5. Enter the name of the medical team member responsible for the patient in the **Medical Team Name** field.
6. Enter the email address of the medical team member in the **Medical Team Email** field.
7. Enter the patient's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
8. (Optional) Enter the name of the hospital where the patient is receiving treatment in the **Hospital Name** field.
9. (Optional) Enter the patient's medical record number in the **Medical Record Number** field.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the full name of the patient, including first and last names.
* **patient_dob**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Vaccination Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Not Vaccinated
	+ Partially Vaccinated
	+ Fully Vaccinated
* **vaccination_date**: If the patient is vaccinated, enter the date of vaccination in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Team Name**: Enter the name of the medical team member responsible for the patient.
* **Medical Team Email**: Enter the email address of the medical team member.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Hospital Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of the hospital where the patient is receiving treatment.
* **Medical Record Number**: (Optional) Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.
