# COVID 19 Triage Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID 19 Triage Form is designed to collect data on a patient's symptoms, medical history, and vital signs to guide healthcare providers in making timely and effective decisions regarding patient care and isolation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct date and time of the patient's triage.
2. Enter the patient's first and last names.
3. Provide the patient's date of birth.
4. Choose the patient's gender.
5. Enter the patient's phone number for contact.
6. Fill out the patient's home address, city, and state.
7. Enter the patient's zip code.
8. Describe the patient's chief complaint.
9. Select the date when the patient's symptoms started.
10. Choose all the current symptoms the patient is experiencing.
11. Enter the patient's current temperature.
12. Report the patient's heart rate.
13. Report the patient's respiratory rate.
14. Enter the patient's blood pressure.
15. Report the patient's oxygen saturation level.
16. Select the level of breathing difficulty the patient is experiencing.
17. Choose whether the patient is experiencing chest pain or pressure.
18. Select the patient's mental status.
19. Choose any underlying medical conditions the patient has.
20. Select the patient's age category.
21. Report the patient's vaccination status.
22. Select the patient's triage category.
23. Choose whether the patient requires isolation.
24. Select the level of care the patient needs.
25. Determine if a COVID-19 test is ordered.
26. Enter the name of the triage nurse.
27. Enter the name of the triage provider.
28. Provide any additional triage notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Triage Date** (`1`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the patient's triage, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Triage Time** (`2`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the patient's triage.
* **Patient First Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's legal first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's legal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select the patient's gender: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, or Other.
* **Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Home Address** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's street address.
* **City** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's city.
* **State** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's state.
* **Zip Code** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's postal zip code.
* **Chief Complaint** (`12`, `text`, required): Describe the patient's main reason for visiting.
* **When did symptoms start** (`13`, `date`, required): Select the date when the patient's symptoms started.
* **Current Symptoms** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the symptoms the patient is currently experiencing.
* **Temperature** (`15`, `number`, required): Report the patient's current temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Heart Rate** (`16`, `number`, required): Report the patient's heart rate in beats per minute.
* **Respiratory Rate** (`17`, `number`, required): Report the patient's respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
* **Blood Pressure** (`18`, `text`, required): Report the patient's blood pressure in systolic-over-diastolic format.
* **Oxygen Saturation** (`19`, `number`, required): Report the patient's oxygen saturation level in percentage.
* **Difficulty Breathing** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of the patient's breathing difficulty: No difficulty, Mild - with exertion, Moderate - at rest, or Severe - significant distress.
* **Chest Pain or Pressure** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient is experiencing chest pain or pressure: False, Mild, Moderate, or Severe.
* **Mental Status** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Select the patient's mental status: Alert and oriented, Confused, Difficult to awaken, or Unresponsive.
* **Underlying Medical Conditions** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose any underlying medical conditions the patient has.
* **Patient Age Category** (`24`, `select_one`, required): Select the patient's age category: Under 18, 18-49, 50-64, 65-74, or 75 or older.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Select the patient's vaccination status: Fully vaccinated with booster, Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Unknown.
* **Triage Category** (`26`, `select_one`, required): Select the patient's triage category: Level 1 - Immediate resuscitation, Level 2 - Emergent, Level 3 - Urgent, Level 4 - Less urgent, or Level 5 - Non-urgent.
* **Isolation Required** (`27`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the patient requires isolation: Yes immediate isolation, Yes with precautions, or No isolation needed.
* **Level of Care Needed** (`28`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of care the patient needs: Home care with self-isolation, Outpatient follow-up, Urgent care evaluation, Emergency department, Hospital admission, or ICU admission.
* **COVID-19 Test Ordered** (`29`, `select_one`, required): Determine whether a COVID-19 test is ordered: Yes PCR test, Yes rapid antigen test, No test needed, or Test already done.
* **Triage Nurse Name** (`30`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the triage nurse.
* **Triage Provider Name** (`31`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the triage provider.
* **Additional Triage Notes** (`32`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional information about the patient that might be helpful for the triage process.

## Tips

* Be sure to complete the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure the best outcomes for the patient.
* Select the correct options and enter the required information to help healthcare providers make informed decisions.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the patient or their representative for clarification.
* Remember that some fields are marked as "required," which means that they are essential to the form's purpose and must be filled out.
