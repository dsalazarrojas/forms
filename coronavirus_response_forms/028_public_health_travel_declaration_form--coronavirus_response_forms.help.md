# public_health_travel_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report recent travel history and potential exposure to COVID-19 for public health purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about your travel history, including any international travel.
2. Fill out the travel details, including any relevant information about your travel.
3. Enter your contact information so we can reach you if needed.
4. Answer questions about your potential medical exposure, including any recent illnesses.
5. Complete any other fields as required or requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel History** (`1`, `travel_history`, required/required): This section asks about your recent travel, including international travel. Please select "Yes" for international travel.
* **Travel Details** (`2`, `travel_details`, required/optional): Describe your recent travel in this field, including any dates and places visited.
* **Contact Info** (`3`, `contact_info`, required/required): Enter your email address or phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Medical Info** (`4`, `medical_info`, optional/optional): If you have recently been ill, select "Yes" here.
* **Medical Attention** (`5`, `medical_attention`, optional/optional): If you have recently sought medical attention, enter the date you visited the medical facility.
* **Medical Symptoms** (`6`, `medical_symptoms`, required/required): Describe any recent symptoms you have experienced.
* **Travel Documents** (`7`, `travel_documents`, optional/optional): If you have any travel documents, such as boarding passes or receipts, attach them here.
* **Contact Trace** (`8`, `contact_trace`, optional/optional): If you were in contact with anyone who has recently traveled, provide their contact information.
* **Travel History Notes** (`9`, `travel_history_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information about your travel history.
* **Travel Details Notes** (`10`, `travel_details_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information about your recent travel.
* **Medical Info Notes** (`11`, `medical_info_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information about your medical exposure.
* **Travel Risk Assessment** (`13`, `travel_risk_assessment`, optional/optional): Select the level of risk associated with your travel, such as Low, Moderate, High, or Critical.
* **Travel Risk Assessment Notes** (`14`, `travel_risk_assessment_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information to support your risk assessment.
* **Travel History ID** (`15`, `travel_history_id`, optional/optional): If you have a travel ID number, enter it here.
* **Travel History Type** (`16`, `travel_history_type`, optional/optional): Select the type of travel, such as air, land, or sea.
* **Contact Info Notes** (`17`, `contact_info_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information about your contact information.
* **Contact Trace Notes** (`18`, `contact_trace_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information about your contact with recently traveled individuals.
* **Travel Risk Assessment Notes** (`19`, `travel_risk_assessment_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information to support your risk assessment.
* **Medical History Notes** (`20`, `medical_history_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information about your medical history.
* **Other Travel History** (`21`, `other_travel_history`, optional/optional): Describe any other travel you may have done recently.
* **Other Travel Details** (`22`, `other_travel_details`, optional/optional): Describe any other travel details you may have.
* **Travel Restrictions** (`23`, `travel_restrictions`, optional/optional): If you have any travel restrictions, describe them here.
* **Submit Travel Form** (`24`, `submit_travel_form`, optional/optional): Click submit to complete the form.
* **Submit Travel Form Notes** (`25`, `submit_travel_form_notes`, optional/optional): Add any additional information to support your submission.

Note: This form is intended to be used for public health purposes only. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
