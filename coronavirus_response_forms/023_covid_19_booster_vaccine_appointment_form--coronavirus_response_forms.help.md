# COVID 19 Booster Vaccine Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to schedule a COVID-19 booster vaccine appointment. It collects necessary information to ensure accurate and complete data is provided to facilitate the vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred appointment date and time.
2. Fill out your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.
3. Provide your medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, and recent illnesses.
4. Choose your preferred booster vaccine type (if applicable).
5. Confirm your emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you would like to receive your vaccine booster.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`appointment_time`, `select_one`, required): Choose a time slot for your vaccine booster appointment.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, optional): Enter your age.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your health insurance company.
* **Insurance ID Number** (`insurance_id`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your health insurance ID number.
* **Initial COVID-19 Vaccine Received** (`initial_vaccine`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of vaccine you initially received.
* **Date of Initial Vaccination** (`initial_vaccine_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you received your initial vaccine.
* **Previous Booster Shot Dates** (`previous_booster_dates`, `text`, optional): If you have had previous boosters, enter the dates.
* **Booster Vaccine Preference** (`booster_vaccine_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred vaccine type (if applicable).
* **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): If you have allergies to vaccine components, enter them.
* **Current Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, enter them.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): If you are taking any medications, list them.
* **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, `select_one`, optional): If you are pregnant or trying to become pregnant, choose your status.
* **Recent Illness** (`recent_illness`, `select_one`, required): Choose your recent illness status.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): In case of a medical emergency, enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): In case of a medical emergency, enter your emergency contact's phone number.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.
- Review and understand the instructions and guidelines provided.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
- This form is intended for individuals to schedule a COVID-19 booster vaccine appointment.
