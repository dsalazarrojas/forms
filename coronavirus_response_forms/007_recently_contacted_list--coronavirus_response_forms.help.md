# Recently Contacted List - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Recently Contacted List" form is used to record details of patients who have recently been in contact with healthcare providers. This form is designed to provide a centralized repository of information about patient contacts, helping healthcare staff to quickly access and review relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter the contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
4. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Enter the date and time of contact in the "Contact Date" and "Contact Time" fields.
7. Enter the contact location in the "Contact Location" field.
8. Enter the contact note in the "Contact Note" field.
9. Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor" field.
10. Enter the doctor's phone number in the "Doctor Phone" field.
11. Enter the doctor's email in the "Doctor Email" field.
12. Enter the doctor's relationship in the "Doctor Relationship" field.
13. Enter any additional notes for the doctor in the "Doctor Note" field.
14. Enter the doctor's phone number 2 in the "Doctor Phone 2" field.
15. Enter the doctor's email 2 in the "Doctor Email 2" field.
16. Enter the doctor's relationship 2 in the "Doctor Relationship 2" field.
17. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
18. Enter the patient's email in the "Patient Email" field.
19. Enter the patient's relationship in the "Patient" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recently contacted**: Enter a brief description of the patient contact.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the patient ID number.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Date**: Enter the date of contact.
* **Contact Time**: Enter the time of contact.
* **Contact Location**: Enter the location of contact.
* **Contact Note**: Enter any additional notes about the contact.
* **Contact Type**: Enter the type of contact.
* **Doctor**: Enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone**: Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email**: Enter the doctor's email.
* **Doctor Relationship**: Enter the doctor's relationship to the patient.
* **Doctor Note**: Enter any additional notes about the doctor.
* **Doctor Phone 2**: Enter the doctor's phone number 2.
* **Doctor Email 2**: Enter the doctor's email 2.
* **Doctor Relationship 2**: Enter the doctor's relationship 2.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email.
* **Patient**: Enter the patient's relationship to the doctor.
* **Additional note**: Enter any additional notes about the patient.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out for each patient contact, so please fill out each field carefully and accurately.
