# COVID 19 Symptom Screening Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on symptoms, travel history, and exposure to a confirmed case for individuals suspected of having or being at risk for COVID-19.
## How To Complete This Form
Please complete each section of the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any doubts or questions while filling out the form, refer to the field explanations below.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Screening date**: Enter the date you were screened for COVID-19, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Do you currently have any of the following symptoms**: Select all that apply from the list of options provided. This includes:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Sore throat
	+ Loss of taste or smell
* **Recent international travel in past 14 days**: Select "True" if you have recently traveled internationally in the past 14 days, and "False" otherwise.
* **Exposure to a confirmed case**: Select "True" if you have been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days, and "False" otherwise.
* **Measured temperature in celsius**: Enter your current body temperature in celsius, if available. This field is optional.
* **Do you have a healthcare provider to contact**: Select "True" if you have a healthcare provider you can contact for follow-up, and "False" otherwise.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your screening, such as symptoms or concerns you may have.

Note: If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
