Thermal Scanning Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
====================================================

## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in participating in thermal scanning, a non-invasive and non-contact method of measuring body temperature. The purpose of this form is to obtain the necessary information for thermal scanning, including your user information, consent, temperature check, email, phone number, and date and time of participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Select your consent to participate in thermal scanning.
3. Select the temperature check that best matches your body temperature.
4. If you have any additional notes or comments, please add them in the "Note" field.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter the date and time of your participation.
