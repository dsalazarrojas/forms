# Gym Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from our customers about their experiences and concerns regarding our gym reopening during the coronavirus pandemic. Your feedback is essential for us to understand your needs and preferences and make informed decisions to ensure a safe and enjoyable environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your name and answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2.  Provide as much information as you can about your answers.
3.  If you have any comments or concerns, please feel free to express them in the designated text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is your name?** (`user_info`, text, required) - Please enter your name so we can address you personally in our communication.
* **2. Why did you not visit the gym last week?** (`last_week_visit`, text, required) - Help us understand why you did not visit the gym last week.
* **3. What is the earliest date you would feel safe visiting the gym again?** (`feel_safe_again`, date, required) - Please indicate the earliest date you feel comfortable visiting the gym.
* **4. What safety measures do you expect the gym to have in place?** (`safety_measures`, select_multiple, required) - Choose the safety measures you think are essential for a safe environment.
	+ Hand Sanitizer
	+ Temperature Check
	+ Mask Required
	+ Social Distancing
	+ Reduced Capacity
* **5. How often do you visit other places like restaurants?** (`visit_other_places`, number, required) - Rate how frequently you visit other places like restaurants.
* **6. What do you expect from us when you visit the gym again?** (`visit_expectation`, text, required) - Let us know what services or facilities we should provide.
* **7. How do you prefer to communicate with us?** (`communicate_with_us`, select_one, required) - Choose your preferred contact method.
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Message
* **8. Do you have any other comments or concerns?** (`other_comments`, note, required) - Share any other thoughts or concerns.
* **9. How often do you plan to visit the gym this week?** (`visit_frequency`, select_one, required) - Indicate your plan for this week.
	+ This week
	+ Next week
	+ Next month
* **10. How do you prefer to be notified?** (`notification_preference`, select_one, required) - Choose your preferred notification method.
	+ Email
	+ Phone
* **11. Do you have any friends or family members that visit the gym?** (`friends_family_member`, select_one, required) - Indicate whether you have friends or family members visiting the gym.
	+ True
	+ False
* **12. Are there any other gyms in your area?** (`gym_in_your_area`, select_one, required) - Confirm whether you have other gyms in your area.
	+ True
	+ False
* **13. Other gyms** (`other_gyms`, text, non-mandatory) - If you know of other gyms in your area, please mention them here.
* **14. Are you planning to visit the gym again?** (`visit_gym_again`, select_one, non-mandatory) - Indicate your plan to visit the gym again.
	+ This week
	+ Next week
	+ Next month
* **15. How would you rate your last visit?** (`rating`, number, non-mandatory) - Rate your last visit from 0 to 10.
* **16. Do you have any other comments?** (`other_comments_2`, text, non-mandatory) - Share any other thoughts or concerns.
* **17. How satisfied are you with our service?** (`rating_2`, number, non-mandatory) - Rate your satisfaction with our service from 0 to 10.
