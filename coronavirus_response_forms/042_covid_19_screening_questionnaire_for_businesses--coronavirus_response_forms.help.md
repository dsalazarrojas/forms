# Covid 19 Screening Questionnaire For Businesses - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from businesses about their potential exposure to COVID-19. It is used to assess the risk of exposure and provide guidance on necessary precautions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please answer "Not sure" or ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, optional): A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Business Contact Info** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter your business contact information, such as your name and phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who is filling out the form.
* **Have you had symptoms?** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have had any COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days. Choose from the options:
	+ False: You have not had any symptoms.
	+ True: You have had symptoms.
	+ Not sure: You are unsure or have not checked.
* **Have you traveled in the last 14 days?** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have traveled in the last 14 days. Choose from the options:
	+ False: You have not traveled.
	+ True: You have traveled.
	+ Not sure: You are unsure or have not checked.
* **Location where you visited** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate where you visited in the last 14 days. Choose from the options:
	+ Store
	+ Restaurant
	+ Other
	+ Home
* **Have you contacted any of the following places?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if you have visited any of the following places. Choose from the options:
	+ Store
	+ Restaurant
	+ Other
	+ Home
* **How frequently do you visit?** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how frequently you visit. Choose from the options:
	+ Daily
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Have you self-isolated?** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have self-isolated in the last 14 days. Choose from the options:
	+ True: You have self-isolated.
	+ False: You have not self-isolated.
	+ Not sure: You are unsure or have not checked.
* **Days self-isolated** (`10`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you have self-isolated.
* **Reason for self-isolation** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for self-isolation.
* **Exposed anyone else?** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have exposed anyone else to COVID-19. Choose from the options:
	+ True: You have exposed someone else.
	+ False: You have not exposed anyone else.
	+ Not sure: You are unsure or have not checked.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please answer "Not sure" or ask for clarification.
* This form is a duplicate of a previous form, and it is recommended to fill out the original form instead.
