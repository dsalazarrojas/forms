# COVID 19 Immunization Screening and Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to screen and gather information from individuals who are candidates for receiving a COVID-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each section carefully and answer all questions honestly.
- Ensure you understand each question and provide accurate information.
- If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Review all answers before submitting the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Participant Name)** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Current Illness/Fever** (`current_illness`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are currently experiencing any illness or fever.
* **Severe Allergies** (`severe_allergies`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any severe allergies.
* **Allergies List** (`allergies_list`, text, optional): If you have severe allergies, please list them.
* **Immunocompromised or on Immunosuppressive Therapy** (`immunocompromised`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy.
* **Thrombosis Disorder History** (`thrombosis_disorder`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a history of thrombosis or blood clotting disorders.
* **Previous Vaccine Reaction** (`vaccination_reaction`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had a severe reaction to previous vaccines.
* **Vaccination History** (`previous_covid_vaccine`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have received a COVID-19 vaccine previously.
* **Previous Vaccine Type** (`previous_vaccine_type`, select_one, optional): If you have received a COVID-19 vaccine, please select the type.
* **Previous Vaccine Date** (`previous_vaccine_date`, date, optional): If you have received a COVID-19 vaccine, enter the date.
* **Received Information** (`received_information`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have received and reviewed information about the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Questions Answered** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): Indicate if your questions have been answered satisfactorily.
* **Consent to Vaccination** (`consent_vaccine`, select_one, required): Indicate if you consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
