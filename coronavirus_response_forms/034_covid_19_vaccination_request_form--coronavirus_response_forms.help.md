# Covid 19 Vaccination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Vaccination Request Form is designed to collect information about individuals who have been vaccinated against COVID-19. This form helps public health professionals track and monitor vaccination records, allowing for more effective contact tracing and follow-up if needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your Date of birth in the "Date of birth" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
5. If applicable, enter your Phone number in the "Phone number" field.
6. Choose the type of vaccination you received (Dose 1, Dose 2, or Dose 3) from the "Vaccination type" dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, enter the date you received the vaccination in the "Vaccination date" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
8. Enter the time you received the vaccination in the "Vaccination time" field (in the format HH:MM).
9. If applicable, select all the doses you received (Dose 1, Dose 2, or Dose 3) from the "Vaccination dose" dropdown menu.
10. Provide a brief reason for your vaccination in the "Reason for vaccination" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required): Your first name is the first name you were given at birth.
* **Last name** (text, required): Your last name is the surname you were given at birth.
* **Date of birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 06/12/1990).
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Vaccination type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of vaccination you received (Dose 1, Dose 2, or Dose 3).
* **Vaccination date** (date, optional): If you received a vaccination, enter the date it was administered in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 06/12/2021).
* **Vaccination time** (time, optional): If you received a vaccination, enter the time it was administered in the format HH:MM.
* **Vaccination dose** (select_multiple, optional): Select all the doses you received (Dose 1, Dose 2, or Dose 3).
* **Reason for vaccination** (text, optional): Briefly explain why you requested this vaccination.
