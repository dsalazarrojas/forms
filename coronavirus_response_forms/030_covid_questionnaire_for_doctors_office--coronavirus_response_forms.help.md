# Covid Questionnaire For Doctors Office - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid Questionnaire For Doctors Office is designed to collect information from patients in a doctor's office setting to help the medical staff assess and provide proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all the required fields (if any).
- Answer each question accurately and honestly.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info (id: 1)
* **Patient Info** (`name`, `type: text`, required: false): Fill in your name and any other relevant patient information that you think the medical staff might need to know.

### Contact Info (id: 2)
* **Contact Info** (`name`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email (if applicable). This will help the medical staff stay in touch with you.

### Symptoms (id: 3)
* **Symptoms** (`type: text`, required: false): Describe your symptoms, including any recent or ongoing issues you've been experiencing.

### Travel History (id: 4)
* **Travel History** (`type: text`, required: false): Share any recent travel history, including locations and dates of travel.

### Medical History (id: 5)
* **Medical History** (`type: text`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history, including previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.

### Submit (id: 6)
* **Submit** (`type: text`, required: false): Once you've completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any question, ask the medical staff for clarification.
- If you've made a mistake, you can always ask to edit or correct your answers before submitting.
- Answer all questions accurately and honestly to ensure the medical staff can provide the best possible care.
