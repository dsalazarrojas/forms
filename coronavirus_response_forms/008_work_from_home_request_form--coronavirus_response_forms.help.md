# Work From Home Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request to work from home for a specific period. Please fill it out if you need to work from home.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* Employee name
	* Department
	* Start Date
	* End Date
	* E Signature

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it is recorded in the company database.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Required): Choose your department from the list of options (not visible in this version).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Required): Select the date when you want to start working from home.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Required): Select the date when you want to end your work from home arrangement.
* **E Signature** (`e_signature`, Text, Required): Type or write your electronic signature, confirming that you've checked with your manager and are aware of the company policies.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Make sure you have approval from your manager before submitting this form.
- Keep in mind that this form is subject to changes according to company policies.
