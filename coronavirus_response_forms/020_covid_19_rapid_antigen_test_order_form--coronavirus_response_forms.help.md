# COVID 19 Rapid Antigen Test Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for medical professionals or facilities to order Rapid Antigen Test Kits for COVID-19 screening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Provider or Facility Name** in the field labeled "Provider or Facility Name".
2. Fill in the **Medical License or DEA Number** field, which is required for professional-grade kits.
3. Provide the **Primary Contact Person** details, including their name and any necessary contact information.
4. Enter the **Contact Phone Number** and **Contact Email Address**.
5. Select the **Quantity of Antigen Test Kits** you wish to order, keeping in mind that kits are sold in packs of 25.
6. Choose the **Bulk Discount Category** that applies to your order based on the quantity of test kits.
7. Select your **Preferred Shipping Method** from the options provided.
8. If applicable, provide a **Purchase Order (PO) Number** for your order.
9. Enter any **Special Shipping Instructions** if necessary.
10. Agree to the **Terms and Conditions** of sales and distribution.
11. Confirm that the tests will be used for **Professional Screening Only** by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider or Facility Name**: Enter the name of your healthcare facility or the name of the individual or organization placing the order.
* **Medical License or DEA Number**: Enter your medical license or DEA number if you are a healthcare professional.
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information for the primary person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Quantity of Antigen Test Kits**: Select the number of kits you wish to order, keeping in mind that kits are sold in packs of 25.
* **Bulk Discount Category**: Select the discount category that applies to your order based on the quantity of test kits.
* **Preferred Shipping Method**: Choose your preferred shipping method from the options provided.
* **Purchase Order (PO) Number**: Enter the PO number for your order if you have one.
* **Special Shipping Instructions**: Enter any special shipping instructions if necessary.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to the terms and conditions of sales and distribution.
* **Professional Use Certification**: Confirm that the tests will be used for professional screening only.
