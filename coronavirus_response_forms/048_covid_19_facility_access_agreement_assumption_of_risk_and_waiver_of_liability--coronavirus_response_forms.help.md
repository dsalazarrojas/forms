# COVID 19 Facility Access Agreement, Assumption Of Risk And Waiver Of Liability - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal agreement that requires visitors to acknowledge the risks associated with COVID-19 and provide liability waivers for facilities that allow them to access. It ensures that visitors are aware of and assume the risks of COVID-19 exposure during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your agreement date.
2. Provide your full legal name.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Enter your residential address.
5. Enter your city.
6. Enter your state or province.
7. Enter your zip code.
8. Provide your phone number.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter the name of the facility you plan to visit.
11. Select the type of facility you will be visiting (e.g., Commercial Office, Fitness Center, Event Venue).
12. Enter the reason for your visit.
13. Choose a date for your visit to start.
14. Choose a date for your visit to end (if applicable).
15. Select whether you acknowledge COVID-19 as a serious respiratory illness.
16. Acknowledge that COVID-19 can spread through close contact despite facility precautions.
17. Indicate that you assume the risk of COVID-19 exposure, including potential illness, hospitalization, or death.
18. Confirm that you understand facility COVID-19 precautions (e.g., masking, distancing, sanitizing, capacity limits).
19. State whether you currently have COVID-19 symptoms.
20. Declare that you have not been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
21. Declare that you have not tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days.
22. State whether you are under quarantine or isolation.
23. Agree to release the facility from liability for COVID-19.
24. Indemnify the facility for COVID-19 related claims.
25. Confirm that you will comply with all facility safety guidelines.
26. Certify that the information provided is accurate.
27. Confirm that you agree to access the facility under these terms, including all liability waivers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): The date you agree to this form.
* **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`visitor_dob`, date, required): Your date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Your residential address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): The city you reside in.
* **State/Province** (`state`, text, required): The state or province you reside in.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Your zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your email address.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): The name of the facility you plan to visit.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, select_one, required): The type of facility you will be visiting (e.g., Commercial Office, Fitness Center, Event Venue).
* **Reason for Facility Access** (`access_reason`, text, required): The purpose of your visit.
* **Scheduled Visit Start Date** (`visit_start_date`, date, required): The date you plan to start your visit.
* **Scheduled Visit End Date** (`visit_end_date`, date, optional): The date your visit will end (if applicable).
* **COVID-19 Risk Acknowledgment** (`covid_risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): You must acknowledge that COVID-19 is a serious respiratory illness.
* **Transmissible Risk Understanding** (`transmissible_risk`, select_one, required): You must understand that COVID-19 can spread through close contact despite facility precautions.
* **Assumption of Risk** (`assumption_of_risk`, select_one, required): You must indicate that you assume the risk of COVID-19 exposure, including potential illness, hospitalization, or death.
* **Precautions Understanding** (`precautions_understood`, select_one, required): You must confirm that you understand facility COVID-19 precautions (e.g., masking, distancing, sanitizing, capacity limits).
* **Symptoms Attestation** (`symptom_attestation`, select_one, required): You must state whether you currently have COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Exposure Declaration** (`exposure_declaration`, select_one, required): You must declare that you have not been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **Positive Test Declaration** (`test_positive_declaration`, select_one, required): You must declare that you have not tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days.
* **Quarantine Declaration** (`quarantine_declaration`, select_one, required): You must state whether you are under quarantine or isolation.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): You must agree to release the facility from liability for COVID-19.
* **Indemnification** (`indemnification`, select_one, required): You must indemnify the facility for COVID-19 related claims.
* **Safety Compliance** (`safety_compliance`, select_one, required): You must confirm that you will comply with all facility safety guidelines.
* **Information Accuracy** (`information_accuracy`, select_one, required): You must certify that the information provided is accurate.
* **Final Consent** (`final_consent`, select_one, required): You must confirm that you agree to access the facility under these terms, including all liability waivers.
