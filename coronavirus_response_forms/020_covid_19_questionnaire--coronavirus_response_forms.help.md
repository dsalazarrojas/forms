# COVID 19 Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather essential information from households affected by the COVID-19 pandemic to identify their needs and provide appropriate support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by entering the household contact information on page 1.
2. On the second page, report the number of members living in your household.
3. Check any of the current needs that apply to your household.
4. If your household has experienced income loss due to the pandemic, select the "True" option.
5. If your household requires a referral to services, select the "True" option.
6. Provide your preferred language for follow-up communications.
7. Choose the best way for us to contact you.
8. Consent to share your information with partner agencies to enable us to provide support.
9. Finally, date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Household Contact Information 
* **Household contact information** (`household_contact`, text, required/optional): This is the primary contact person for the household.
### Number of Household Members 
* **Number of household members** (`number_of_household_members`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of people living in the household.
### Current Needs 
* **Current needs** (`current_needs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check any of the following needs that apply to your household:
	+ Food assistance
	+ Financial support
	+ Medical care
	+ Housing support
	+ Childcare
### Income Loss 
* **Income loss due to pandemic** (`income_loss`, select_one, required/optional): Select "True" if your household has experienced income loss.
### Urgent Needs Description 
* **Urgent needs description** (`urgent_needs_description`, text, required/optional): Describe any priority needs your household has.
### Referral Needed 
* **Referral needed** (`referral_needed`, select_one, required/true): Select "True" if your household requires a referral to services.
### Preferred Language 
* **Preferred language** (`preferred_language`, text, required/optional): Enter your preferred language for follow-up communications.
### Contact Preference 
* **Contact preference** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the best way for us to contact you:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ SMS
### Consent to Share 
* **Consent to share information** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required/true): Select "True" if you consent to share your information with partner agencies.
### Submission Date 
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required/true): Enter the date and time of submission.
