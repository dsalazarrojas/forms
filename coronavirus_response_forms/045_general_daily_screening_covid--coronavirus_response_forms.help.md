# prophets_general_daily_screening_covid - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are experiencing symptoms or have been diagnosed with COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **first name** and **last name** in the first two fields.
2. Select your **symptom date** in the next field. This should be the date when you first experienced symptoms.
3. Choose your **symptom start time** in the next field. This should be the time when you first experienced symptoms.
4. Enter your **temperature** if you are experiencing symptoms.
5. Select your **travel history** in the fields below. Please choose all that apply.
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
6. Select your **contact trace** in the field below.
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
7. Select your **medical condition** in the field below.
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
8. Enter any **notes** you have in the free text area below.
9. Enter your **email address** and **phone number** for contact purposes.
10. Enter any additional **notes** you have in the free text area below.
