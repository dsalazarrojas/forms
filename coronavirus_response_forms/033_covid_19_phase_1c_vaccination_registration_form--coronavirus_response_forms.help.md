# COVID 19 Phase 1C Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for COVID-19 Phase 1C Vaccination Registration. It's designed to gather information about your eligibility, contact details, health status, and vaccination history to register you for the Phase 1C vaccination program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Age, Email Address, Phone Number, and Street Address, City, State, and ZIP Code).
2. Select your eligibility reason from the list provided (Age 40-64 with high-risk conditions, Essential worker not covered in other phases, or Both).
3. If you have any high-risk medical conditions, select all that apply from the list.
4. If you're an essential worker, select your role from the list.
5. Answer the health information questions (Known Allergies, Current Medications, History of severe vaccine reactions, and Previous COVID-19 vaccine doses received).
6. Confirm that you meet the Phase 1C eligibility requirements and consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
7. Finally, select the registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required, Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Required, Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Required, Date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (Required, Number): You must be 40-64 years old.
* **Email Address** (Required, Email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required, Text): Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Street Address** (Required, Text): Enter your street address.
* **City** (Required, Text): Enter your city.
* **State** (Required, Text): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (Required, Text): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Why are you eligible for Phase 1C?** (Required, Select One): Select Age 40-64 with high-risk conditions, Essential worker not covered in other phases, or Both.
* **High-Risk Medical Conditions** (Optional, Select Multiple): If you have any of the listed conditions, select all that apply.
* **Essential Worker Role** (Optional, Select One): If you're an essential worker, select your role from the list.
* **Known Allergies** (Optional, Text): List any known allergies.
* **Current Medications** (Optional, Text): List any current medications.
* **History of severe vaccine reactions?** (Required, Select One): Select True if you've had a severe reaction to a vaccine, False otherwise.
* **Previous COVID-19 vaccine doses received** (Required, Select One): Select the number of doses you've received.
* **I certify I meet Phase 1C eligibility requirements** (Required, Select One): Select True if you meet the eligibility requirements, False otherwise.
* **I consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine** (Required, Select One): Select True if you consent to receive the vaccine, False otherwise.
* **Registration Date** (Required, Date): Select the date you're registering for the vaccination.

## Tips
* Ensure that you've filled in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer back to the form's purpose and eligibility requirements.
