# Release of Information Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect release information from individuals or organizations. It is an original template created using AI, and not affiliated with any form platform. The form is meant to gather details such as patient information, contact information, and release dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your patient's information in the "patient_info" field.
2. Enter your contact details in the "contact_info" field.
3. Select the method of contact (phone, email, or fax) in the "contact_type" field.
4. Provide the date of release in the "release_date" field.
5. Enter the time of release in the "release_time" field.
6. Enter the reason for release in the "release_reason" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "release_note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter your patient's details, such as name, address, and date of birth.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name and address.
* **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, select one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or fax).
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to release the information.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to release the information.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for releasing the information.
* **Release Note** (`release_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the release of information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can refer to this guide for clarification.
- This form is for informational release purposes only and is not intended for any other use.
