# Moderna COVID-19 Vaccine Appointment Scheduling And Consent Form CPESN - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moderna COVID-19 Vaccine Appointment Scheduling And Consent Form CPESN is a form used to schedule appointments for the Moderna COVID-19 vaccine and obtain consent from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all required fields. Some fields are optional, but may be required for proper scheduling and administration of the vaccine.
2. Enter your patient's information in the "Patient Information" field, including their name and any relevant medical history.
3. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field, including your name, phone number, and email address.
4. Answer the "Current Health Status" questions by selecting from the available options.
5. If applicable, provide your insurance information in the "Insurance Info" field.
6. Enter any relevant vaccination details in the "Vaccine Details" field.
7. Review and agree to the "Consent to Terms and Conditions" by typing your name and date.
8. Sign electronically by typing your name in the "Electronic Signature" field.
9. Select a date and time for your appointment in the "Vaccine Schedule" field.
10. Enter any relevant information about the vaccination in the "Vaccination Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (patient_info, text, false): This field is where you will enter your name and any relevant medical history.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, false): This field is where you will provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Current Health Status** (health_status, select_multiple, false): This field will ask you about your current health status. Please select from the options provided.
* **Insurance Info** (insurance_info, text, false): This field is where you will enter your insurance information, if applicable.
* **Vaccine Details** (vaccine_details, text, false): This field is where you will enter any relevant vaccination details.
* **Consent to Terms and Conditions** (consent_terms, text, false): This field is where you will review and agree to the terms and conditions of the vaccination.
* **Electronic Signature** (e_signature, text, false): This field is where you will sign electronically by typing your name.
* **Vaccine Schedule** (vaccine_schedule, date, false): This field is where you will select a date and time for your appointment.
* **Vaccination Information** (vaccination_info, text, false): This field is where you will enter any relevant information about the vaccination.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully and answer all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your healthcare provider.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid delays or cancellations of your appointment.
