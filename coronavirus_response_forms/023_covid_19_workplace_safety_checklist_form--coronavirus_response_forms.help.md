# Covid 19 Workplace Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Workplace Safety Checklist Form is designed to collect information about an employee's health and safety status in the workplace during the COVID-19 pandemic. This form ensures that employees follow proper protocols to prevent the spread of the virus and provide a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), which are:
	+ employee_name
	+ employee_id
* Answer the following questions to the best of your ability:
	+ shift_start_time
	+ temperature_check
	+ face_mask_worn
	+ physical_distancing
	+ hand_hygiene
	+ symptoms
	+ exposure_contact
	+ vaccination_status
	+ shift_end_time
	+ next_shift_start_time

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shift_start_time**: Enter the time you started your shift.
* **employee_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID card or as it is known in the company.
* **employee_id**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **temperature_check**: Check if your body temperature is under 100.4°F (38°C) or higher.
* **face_mask_worn**: Check the boxes if you are wearing a face mask while working:
	+ True (yes, you are wearing a face mask)
	+ False (no face mask)
* **physical_distancing**: Check the boxes if you are maintaining physical distancing while working:
	+ Maintaining 6 feet distance (yes)
	+ Not maintaining 6 feet distance (no)
* **hand_hygiene**: Check the boxes if you are practicing hand hygiene:
	+ Wearing hand sanitizer (yes)
	+ Not wearing hand sanitizer (no)
* **symptoms**: Check the boxes if you are experiencing any symptoms:
	+ None (no symptoms)
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Sore throat
* **exposure_contact**: Check the boxes if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19:
	+ No contact (no exposure)
	+ Exposed to someone with COVID-19 (yes)
	+ Exposed to someone with a high-risk behavior (yes)
* **vaccination_status**: Check the boxes to indicate your vaccination status:
	+ Fully vaccinated
	+ Partly vaccinated
	+ Not vaccinated
* **shift_end_time**: Enter the time you ended your shift.
* **next_shift_start_time**: Enter the time of your next shift start.
* **next_shift_start_time**: Enter the time of your next shift start.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete the form accurately and honestly to help maintain a safe working environment.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, consult with your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
* Complete the form for each shift, as this information helps track your health and safety status over time.
