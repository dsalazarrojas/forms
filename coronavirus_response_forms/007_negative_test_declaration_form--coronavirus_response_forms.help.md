# Negative Test Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Negative Test Declaration Form is a mandatory requirement for all travelers who are returning to the country after traveling abroad. This form is used to declare your negative test results and provide essential information about your travel history.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form with the required information about your travel history and test results.
2.  Select the correct options for the "Traveler" field.
3.  Choose your correct flight number from the options.
4.  Select the correct test result from the options.
5.  Enter your signature and declaration date.
6.  Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler**: Select your name from the options. This field is used to identify you as a traveler.
* **Date of Return**: Enter your expected date of return to the country. This date is used to track your travel history.
* **Flight Number**: Enter your flight number as shown on your boarding pass or ticket. This is a crucial piece of information for contact tracing purposes.
* **Airline**: Select your airline from the options. This field helps us understand your travel history.
* **Test Result**: Select your test result from the options. A negative test result is required to declare your health status.
* **Declaring Authority**: Enter the name of the authority that issued your test result. This is usually a hospital or healthcare provider.
* **Declaration Date**: Enter the date you are declaring your test result. This is the date you are submitting this form.
* **Test Facility**: Enter the name of the test facility where your test was taken. This is where you got your test result.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to declare your identity and attest to the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Test Result 1-9**: Select your test result from the options for each corresponding test date.
* **Flight Number**: Enter your flight number as shown on your boarding pass or ticket.
