# COVID 19 Vaccination Notice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's COVID-19 vaccination status, including their notice date, personal details, vaccination history, and vaccination card availability. This form is used to provide documentation for various purposes such as employment, school, or travel requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Notice Date**: Enter the date of the notice.
2. **Personal Details**:
	* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY format).
	* **Gender**: Select your gender.
3. **Contact Information**:
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email.
4. **Vaccination History**:
	* **What is your vaccination status**: Select your current vaccination status.
	* **First Dose Date**: If you received the first dose, enter the date you received it.
	* **First Dose Location**: If you received the first dose, enter where you received it.
	* **First Dose Vaccine Brand**: If you received the first dose, select the brand of the vaccine you received.
	* **Second Dose Date**: If you received the second dose, enter the date you received it.
	* **Second Dose Location**: If you received the second dose, enter where you received it.
	* **Second Dose Vaccine Brand**: If you received the second dose, select the brand of the vaccine you received.
	* **Booster Dose Date**: If you received the booster dose, enter the date you received it.
	* **Booster Dose Location**: If you received the booster dose, enter where you received it.
	* **Booster Dose Vaccine Brand**: If you received the booster dose, select the brand of the vaccine you received.
5. **Vaccination Card Availability**:
	* **Do you have your vaccination card**: Select whether you have your vaccination card or not.
6. **Provider Information**:
	* **Vaccination Provider Name**: If you received the vaccine, enter the name of the provider who administered it.
	* **Vaccination Provider Phone**: If you received the vaccine, enter the provider's contact phone number.
7. **Notice Purpose**:
	* **Why are you submitting this notice**: Select the reason for submitting the notice (e.g., employment, school, travel).
8. **Additional Information**:
	* **Are you interested in receiving additional doses**: Select your interest in receiving additional doses when eligible.
	* **Did you experience any side effects**: Select any side effects you experienced after the vaccination.
	* **How severe were the side effects**: Select the severity level of the side effects.
9. **Certification**:
	* **I certify that the information provided is true and accurate**: Select whether you can certify the accuracy of the information provided.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and true.
* If you received the first dose, second dose, or booster dose, enter the dates and locations accordingly.
* If you received any side effects, be as specific as possible when describing them.
* Ensure that you select the correct reason for submitting the notice.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the contact person listed above.
