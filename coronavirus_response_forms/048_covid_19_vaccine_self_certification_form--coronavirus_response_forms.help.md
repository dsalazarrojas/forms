# COVID 19 Vaccine Self Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals self-certify their eligibility for the COVID-19 vaccine. It collects personal and health information to determine if the individual meets the vaccination requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill in your personal details

* **Full Name** (<applicant_name>): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (<applicant_email>): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (<applicant_phone>): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Step 2: Provide Health and Eligibility Information

* **Date of Birth** (<applicant_dob>): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Reason for Vaccine Eligibility** (<qualification_type>): Select one of the provided options (Healthcare worker, Essential worker, Age 65 or older, High-risk medical condition, Caregiver for elderly or at-risk, Other qualifying reason).
* **Detailed Description of Qualification** (<detailed_qualification>): Explain why you qualify for vaccination.
* **Health Information**:
	+ **High-risk medical condition** (<health_condition>): If you have a high-risk medical condition, please describe it.
	+ **Currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms** (<current_symptoms>): Select 'True' if you are currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
	+ **Tested positive for COVID-19 in past 3 months** (<positive_test_recent>): Select 'True' if you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 3 months.
	+ **Allergies or contraindications** (<vaccine_allergies>): Select 'True' if you have allergies or contraindications to the vaccine components.
	+ **History of anaphylaxis or severe allergic reactions** (<severe_reactions>): Select 'True' if you have a history of anaphylaxis or severe allergic reactions.
* **Pregnancy and Breastfeeding Status**:
	+ **Currently pregnant** (<pregnant>): Select 'True' if you are currently pregnant.
	+ **Currently breastfeeding** (<breastfeeding>): Select 'True' if you are currently breastfeeding.
* **Prior COVID-19 Vaccination**:
	+ **Previously received COVID-19 vaccine** (<prior_vaccine>): Select 'True' if you have previously received the COVID-19 vaccine.
	+ **Number of doses received** (<prior_doses>): If you have received the vaccine, enter the number of doses you received.
	+ **Vaccine type** (<prior_vaccine_type>): If you have received the vaccine, enter the type of vaccine you received.
* **Certification**:
	+ **I certify under penalty of perjury that the above information is true and accurate** (<certify_truthfulness>): Select 'I certify'.
	+ **I understand providing false information may result in penalties** (<certify_understanding>): Select 'I understand'.
	+ **I meet the eligibility requirements for vaccination** (<certify_qualification>): Select 'I certify'.
* **Digital Signature or Full Name as Signature** (<digital_signature>): Enter your name as a digital signature, or your full name as a physical signature.
* **Date of Certification** (<certification_date>): Enter the date you are certifying this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and truthful.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for assistance.
* Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting.
* If you need help or have further questions, please contact your healthcare provider or local authorities.
