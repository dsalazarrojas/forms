# Coronavirus Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Self-Assessment Form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to quickly and accurately assess potential COVID-19 patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately, as any discrepancies may impact the assessment.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, denoted by a asterisk (\*).
3. Use the options provided for select fields.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for assistance from a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select all symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as Fever, Cough, Sore Throat, etc.
* **Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`): Indicate if the patient has had contact with anyone with COVID-19 or if they have traveled to a high-risk area.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`): Enter the patient's travel history, including dates and locations.
* **Recent Events** (`recent_events`): Select any recent events or exposures the patient may have had.
* **Destination** (`travel_destination`): Select the destination of the patient's most recent travel.
* **Symptoms Duration** (`symptoms_duration`): Enter the duration of the patient's symptoms.
* **Self-Isolated** (`self_isolated`): Indicate if the patient has self-isolated since symptoms began.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Select the patient's medical condition, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Select the patient's medical history, if applicable.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`): Select the patient's test results, if applicable.
* **Treatment Status** (`treatment_status`): Select the patient's treatment status, if applicable.
* **Hospitalization Status** (`hospitalization_status`): Select the patient's hospitalization status, if applicable.
* **Contact** (`patient_contact`): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Email** (`patient_email`): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Professional Info** (`medical_professional_info`): Enter the medical professional's name, title, and contact information.
* **Medical Professional Email** (`medical_professional_email`): Enter the medical professional's email address.
* **Medical Professional Phone** (`medical_professional_phone`): Enter the medical professional's phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Select 'Submit' to complete the assessment and 'Cancel' to discard the form.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Patient Info 2** (`patient_info_2`): Enter any additional patient information, if applicable.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`): Enter the date of the patient's most recent travel.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`): Enter the location of the patient's most recent travel.
* **Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment`): Select the patient's self-assessment, if applicable.
* **Patient Info 1** (`patient_info_1`): Enter any additional patient information, if applicable.

This guide is for healthcare staff only. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and complete this form before proceeding.
