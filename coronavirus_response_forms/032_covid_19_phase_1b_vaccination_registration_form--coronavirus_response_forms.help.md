# COVID 19 Phase 1B Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the COVID-19 Phase 1B vaccination program for personnel who are eligible. This form will help our staff understand your medical history and ensure a safe and effective vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Enter your information accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure you have provided all required information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Badge or Employee Number** (`badge_number`, text, required): Enter your badge or employee number as issued by your agency.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your official phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your official street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city or city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your official ZIP code.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Select your employment type from the provided options:
	+ Law Enforcement
	+ Fire Personnel
	+ Both Law Enforcement and Fire
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your agency.
* **Years of Service** (`years_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have served in your agency.
* **Known Allergies** (`known_allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies you have.
* **Pre-existing Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any pre-existing medical conditions you have.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Have you received COVID-19 vaccine before?** (`previous_vaccine`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have received the COVID-19 vaccine before.
* **History of severe allergic reactions to vaccines?** (`severe_reaction`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have a history of severe allergic reactions to vaccines.
* **I confirm I am eligible for Phase 1B vaccination** (`confirm_phase_1b`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you confirm you are eligible for Phase 1B vaccination.
* **I consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine** (`consent_vaccination`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): The date you are registering for the vaccination program.

Please note that this form is for registration purposes only and will be reviewed by our staff to ensure accurate and safe vaccination administration. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
