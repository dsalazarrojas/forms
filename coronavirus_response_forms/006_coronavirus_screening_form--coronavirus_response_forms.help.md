# Coronavirus Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Screening Form is a tool designed to gather information from patients or individuals who may have been exposed to or infected with COVID-19. This form helps healthcare professionals identify potential cases and take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of the virus.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions carefully and accurately to ensure proper medical screening.
2. Enter your name and contact information to help healthcare professionals reach out to you with further instructions or updates.
3. Report any medical history or previous contact with someone who has COVID-19.
4. Answer the symptom questions honestly to help identify potential cases.
5. Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your situation.
