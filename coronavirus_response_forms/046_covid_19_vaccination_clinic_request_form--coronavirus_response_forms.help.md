# COVID 19 Vaccination Clinic Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request for a COVID-19 vaccination clinic. Complete this form to provide information about your organization's needs and requirements for a successful clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following information:

1. **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the vaccination clinic.
2. **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Date of Birth**: Enter your legal first name, last name, and date of birth.
3. **Phone Number** and **Email Address**: Enter your contact information.
4. **Home Address**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code**: Enter your address, city, state, and ZIP code.
5. **Organization Name** (optional): Enter the name of the organization or group you are representing.
6. **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization or group you are representing.
7. **Number of People Requesting Vaccination**: Enter the number of people in your group who will be receiving vaccinations.
8. **Number of Healthcare Workers**, **Number of Essential Workers**, and **Number of High-Risk Individuals** (if applicable): Enter the number of healthcare workers, essential workers, and high-risk individuals in your group.
9. **Preferred Clinic Date**: Select the date for the first preferred clinic.
10. **Alternative Clinic Date** (if applicable): Select the date for the second preferred clinic.
11. **Preferred Clinic Time** (if applicable): Select the time preference for the clinic.
12. **Preferred Clinic Location**: Select where you would like the clinic to be held.
13. **Vaccine Brand Preference**: Select your preferred brand of vaccine.
14. **Special Populations**: Check if your group includes special populations such as the elderly, children, or individuals with disabilities.
15. **Accessibility Needs**: Check if you need accommodations such as wheelchair accessibility or sign language interpreters.
16. **Has your organization hosted a vaccination clinic before?**: Check if your organization has experience with hosting a vaccination clinic.
17. **Describe the proposed clinic facility**: Describe the proposed location for the clinic, including space details.
18. **Parking Availability**: Check if parking is available for the clinic.
19. **Electricity Availability**: Check if electricity is available for medical equipment.
20. **Internet Availability**: Check if internet is available for registration.
21. **Volunteer Staff Support**: Check if your organization can provide volunteer staff to support the clinic.
22. **Support Needs**: Select the types of support you need from the vaccination team.
23. **Additional Comments or Special Requests**: Enter any additional comments or requests you have.

* **ZIP Code (11)**: Postal code.
* **Organization Name (12)**: Name of the organization or group.
* **Organization Type (13)**: Type of organization or group.
* **Number of People Requesting Vaccination (14)**: Group size.
* **Number of Healthcare Workers (15)**: If applicable.
* **Number of Essential Workers (16)**: If applicable.
* **Number of High-Risk Individuals (17)**: If applicable.
* **Preferred Clinic Date (18)**: First choice.
* **Alternative Clinic Date (19)**: Second choice.

* **Preferred Clinic Location (21)**: Where to hold clinic.
* **Vaccine Brand Preference (22)**: If any.

* **Describe the proposed clinic facility (26)**: Space details.
