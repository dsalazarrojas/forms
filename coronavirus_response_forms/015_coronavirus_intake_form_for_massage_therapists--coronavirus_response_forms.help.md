# Coronavirus Intake Form For Massage Therapists - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from massage therapists about their experiences with the coronavirus. The information collected will be used to assess the risk of exposure, monitor symptoms, and ensure that the therapist is taking necessary precautions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter Your Information**: Fill in your client's name and email address in the designated fields.
2. **Provide Date of Service**: Enter the date of the last massage therapy session in the "Date of Service" field. If this is your first session, you can leave this field blank.
3. **Describe Symptoms**: Enter any new or changed symptoms you have experienced since your last session in the "Symptoms" field.
4. **Contact History**: Describe any recent travel or close contact with someone infected with COVID-19 in the "Contact History" field.
5. **Temperature Check**: Enter your body temperature in Fahrenheit in the "Temperature" field. If you don't have access to a thermometer, please leave this field blank.
6. **Exposure**: List any recent exposure to known or suspected COVID-19 in the "Exposure" field.
7. **New or Changed Symptoms Since Last Visit**: Describe any new or changed symptoms you have experienced since your last visit in the "Symptoms Since Last Visit" field.
8. **Health Insurance**: If applicable, enter your health insurance information in the "Health Insurance" field.
9. **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions you have or are being treated for in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date of the last massage therapy session in the YYYY-MM format.
* **Symptoms**: Enter any new or changed symptoms you have experienced.
* **Contact History**: Describe any recent travel or close contact with someone infected with COVID-19.
* **Temperature**: Enter your body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Exposure**: List any recent exposure to known or suspected COVID-19.
* **Symptoms Since Last Visit**: Describe any new or changed symptoms you have experienced since your last visit.
* **Health Insurance**: If applicable, enter your health insurance information.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions you have or are being treated for.
