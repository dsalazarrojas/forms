# Immunization Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immunization Consent Form is used to gather essential information from patients or individuals requiring immunization. This form helps healthcare professionals collect relevant details about the patient's medical history, allergies, medical conditions, and consent for immunization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name
2. Enter your Last name
3. Provide your Date of birth
4. Enter your Age
5. Enter your Address
6. Enter your Phone number
7. Describe your Medical history
8. List any Allergies you have
9. Select any Medical condition you have
10. Select the type of Vaccination you need
11. Enter the Doctor's name
12. Enter the Doctor's contact information
13. Give your Consent for immunization
14. Date of consent
15. Schedule a Follow-up appointment
16. Enter any additional Doctor note
17. Enter any additional Patient note
18. Enter any Follow-up note
19. Enter any Medical certificate information
20. Enter Medical certificate ID
21. Enter Doctor's license information
22. Enter Doctor's license number
23. Enter Medical organization information

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Medical history**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Allergies**: Select any allergies you have from the list.
* **Medical condition**: Select any medical condition you have from the list.
* **Vaccination type**: Select the type of vaccination you need from the list.
* **Doctor name**: Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor contact**: Enter your doctor's contact information.
* **Consent**: Select 'I agree' to give your consent for immunization.
* **Consent date**: Date of your consent.
* **Follow-up appointment**: Schedule a follow-up appointment for immunization.
* **Doctor signature**: Leave a note for the doctor.
* **Patient signature**: Sign your consent as a patient.
* **Doctor note**: Leave any additional note for the doctor.
* **Patient note**: Leave any additional note for your record.
* **Follow-up note**: Leave a follow-up note for your record.
* **Medical certificate**: Enter any medical certificate information.
* **Medical certificate ID**: Enter your medical certificate ID.
* **Doctor license**: Enter your doctor's license information.
* **Doctor license number**: Enter your doctor's license number.
* **Medical organization**: Enter your medical organization information.

Note: Some fields are not required but are optional for additional information purposes. Make sure to review each field carefully and fill out only the necessary information accurately.
