# COVID 19 Pandemic Complaints Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from individuals or organizations affected by the COVID-19 pandemic to understand their experiences and gather data to improve our response.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your personal contact information:
  * Full Name
  * Email Address
  * Phone Number (optional)
2. Select the type of complaint or feedback:
  * Choose from: Complaint, Compliment, Suggestion, Question, or Other
3. Choose the category:
  * Government Response, Healthcare Services, Business Closure, Economic Impact, Mental Health, Education Disruption, Safety Measures, Vaccination Information, or Other
4. Provide a brief summary:
  * Describe the issue in 50-100 words
5. Provide details:
  * Describe the incident or observation in detail
6. (Optional) Provide date and location:
  * Date of the incident or observation
  * Location of the incident or observation
7. Assess impact:
  * How has this affected you personally?
  * How has this affected your business or organization?
8. (Optional) Provide evidence:
  * Do you have evidence or documentation to support your claim?
9. Follow-up preference:
  * Do you want to be contacted for follow-up?
  * How would you like to be contacted (Email, Phone, In-person, No preference)?

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for additional contact.
* **Organization or Workplace**: Your organization or workplace name (if applicable).
* **Your Role**: Your role or position in the organization or community (Employee, Business owner, Customer, Community member, Healthcare worker, Student, Other).
* **Type of Feedback**: Choose from: Complaint, Compliment, Suggestion, Question, or Other.
* **Complaint or Feedback Type Category**: Choose from: Government Response, Healthcare Services, Business Closure, Economic Impact, Mental Health, Education Disruption, Safety Measures, Vaccination Information, or Other.
* **Brief Summary**: Describe the issue in 50-100 words.
* **Detailed Description**: Describe the incident or observation in detail.
* **Date of Incident or Observation**: The date of the incident or observation.
* **Location**: The location of the incident or observation.
* **Severity Level**: How severe was the impact (Low, Moderate, High, Critical).
* **Do you have evidence or documentation?**: Yes or No.
* **Do you want follow-up on this complaint?**: Yes or No.
* **Do you want follow-up?**: Choose from: Email, Phone, In-person, No preference.
* **Survey Submission Date**: The date you are submitting this survey.

Please note that some fields may be marked as "required" but you can always skip or provide placeholder information if you're unsure or don't have the information available.
