# COVID 19 Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business information and supporting documents to evaluate your business's eligibility for COVID-19 grant funds.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to answer all required questions.
2. Attach all necessary supporting documents, as listed in the "Supporting Documents" section.
3. Review and understand the terms and conditions of the grant before submitting the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your business name exactly as it appears on your business registration.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of business you operate from the list (e.g., Retail, Food and Beverage, etc.).
* **Business Registration Number** (`business_registration`, `text`, `required`): Enter your business registration number, such as your EIN or Tax ID.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your business address exactly as it appears on your business registration.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your business email address.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the business owner or principal officer.
* **Owner Title** (`owner_title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the title of the business owner or principal officer.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter the email address of the business owner or principal officer.
* **Number of Employees** (`employees_count`, `number`, `required`): Enter the number of employees in your business.
* **Annual Revenue 2019** (`annual_revenue`, `number`, `required`): Enter your business's annual revenue for 2019.
* **Current Annual Revenue** (`current_revenue`, `number`, `required`): Enter your business's current annual revenue.
* **How COVID-19 Has Impacted Your Business** (`impact_description`, `text`, `required`): Describe the impact of COVID-19 on your business.
* **Grant Amount Requested** (`grant_amount_requested`, `number`, `required`): Enter the amount of grant you are requesting.
* **Purpose of Grant Funds** (`grant_purpose`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the purpose of the grant funds from the list (e.g., Payroll, Rent or Mortgage, etc.).
* **Justify Why Grant Is Needed** (`grant_justification`, `text`, `required`): Explain why you need the grant funds.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the supporting documents you have attached.
* **I Certify the Information Above Is True and Accurate** (`certification`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "Yes" to certify that the information above is true and accurate.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you are submitting the application.
* **I Understand Fraudulent Applications May Result in Penalties** (`acknowledgment`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "Yes" to acknowledge that you understand the terms and conditions of the grant.
