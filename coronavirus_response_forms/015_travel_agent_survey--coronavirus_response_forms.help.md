# Travel Agent Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for travel agents to report and respond to the impact of the Coronavirus pandemic on their businesses. It collects information on the number of travel agents affected, the severity of the impact, and the expected financial recovery of affected travel agencies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information for your travel agency:
	* **Contact Name**: Your name and title.
	* **Email**: Your email address where you want to be reached for follow-up questions.
	* **Phone**: Your phone number in case we need to contact you.
2. Provide information on your agency:
	* **Location**: Your agency's location.
	* **Challenges Faced**: Briefly describe the challenges your agency has faced due to the pandemic.
3. Indicate the severity of the impact:
	* **Affected Sector**: Select the sectors that were affected by the pandemic (e.g., Airline, Hotel, Tour Company).
	* **Affected Duration**: Select the duration of the pandemic's impact on your agency (e.g., Less than 1 week, 1-2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, 4-6 weeks, More than 6 weeks).
	* **Affected Date**: If applicable, provide the date when the impact began.
4. Provide numbers:
	* **Affected Travel Agencies**: List the travel agencies affected by the pandemic.
	* **Affected Financial Loss**: Enter a number that best describes the financial loss due to the pandemic.
	* **Affected Financial Loss Percentage**: If applicable, provide the percentage of financial loss.
5. Estimate the recovery:
	* **Affected Financial Recovery**: Select how you expect your agency to recover from the pandemic (e.g., None, Partial, Full).
	* **Affected Financial Recovery Description**: Briefly describe the steps you plan to take to recover from the pandemic.
	* **Affected Financial Recovery Amount**: If applicable, enter a number that best describes the expected recovery amount.
