# General Lockdown Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Lockdown Questionnaire is used to collect information from students and teachers during a lockdown situation. It helps administrators assess student and teacher responses to ensure their well-being, attendance, and understanding of the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out each required field. Required fields are marked with a "*Required*".
2.  For fields with multiple options, select only the options that apply to you.
3.  Enter your answers in the respective fields. Ensure your answers are accurate and relevant.
4.  Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (text)** (`student_name`, `text`, *Required*): Enter your student name as per the ID provided to you.
* **Student Grade (number)** (`student_grade`, `number`, *Required*): Enter your grade level.
* **Student Attendance (select one)** (`student_attendance`, `select_one`, *Optional*): Select "Yes" if you are on site or "No" if you are off site.
* **Teacher Name (text)** (`teacher_name`, `text`, *Required*): Enter your teacher's name as per the ID provided to you.
* **Teacher Email (email)** (`teacher_email`, `email`, *Required*): Enter your teacher's email address.
* **Student Note (note)** (`student_note`, `note`, *Optional*): Enter any note relevant to you as the student.
* **Student Phone (text)** (`student_phone`, `text`, *Optional*): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Student Email (email)** (`student_email`, `email`, *Optional*): Enter your email address.
* **Student Response (text)** (`student_response`, `text`, *Optional*): Enter your response about the lockdown situation.
* **Teacher Response (text)** (`teacher_response`, `text`, *Required*): Enter your response as the teacher about the lockdown situation.
* **Teacher Attendance (number)** (`teacher_attendance`, `number`, *Required*): Enter your attendance status as the teacher (0-100%).
* **Teacher Note (note)** (`teacher_note`, `note`, *Optional*): Enter any note relevant to you as the teacher.
* **Teacher Phone (text)** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, *Optional*): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Teacher Survey (select multiple)** (`teacher_survey`, `select_multiple`, *Optional*): Select all options that apply to you as the teacher.
* **Student Survey (select multiple)** (`student_survey`, `select_multiple`, *Optional*): Select all options that apply to you as the student.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy and relevance of your answers.
* Use a clear and concise format for your responses.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or a designated contact person for clarification.
