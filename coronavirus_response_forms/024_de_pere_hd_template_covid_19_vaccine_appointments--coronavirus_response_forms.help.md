# de_pere_hd_template_covid_19_vaccine_appointments - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the purpose and completion of the de_pere_hd_template_covid_19_vaccine_appointments form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the appointment details, such as the date and time of the appointment.
2. Select the type of vaccine you received (Pfizer, Moderna, or J&J) if applicable.
3. Indicate if you received the first or second dose.
4. If you received multiple doses, specify the interval between doses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vaccine Appointment Scheduling (appointment_details)**: This is the section where you'll provide details about your appointment, including the date and time.
* **Date (appointment_date)**: Enter the date you attended your vaccine appointment.
* **Time (appointment_time)**: Enter the time of your vaccine appointment.
* **Vaccine Type (vaccine_type)**: Select all the types of vaccine you received (Pfizer, Moderna, or J&J). If you received multiple vaccines, select all that apply.
* **J&J (vaccine_type_select_jj)**: If you received the J&J vaccine, select "Yes" to indicate that you received it.
* **Moderna (vaccine_type_select_moderna)**: If you received the Moderna vaccine, select "Yes" to indicate that you received it.
* **Pfizer (vaccine_type_select_pfizer)**: If you received the Pfizer vaccine, select "Yes" to indicate that you received it.
* **First Dose (first_dose)**: Enter the number of doses you received.
* **Second Dose (second_dose)**: Enter the number of doses you received.
* **Dose Interval (dose_interval)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the interval between doses.

## Tips
- Double-check that you've entered the correct date and time of your appointment.
- If you received multiple vaccines, ensure you've selected all applicable options for vaccine type.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with the healthcare staff who scheduled your appointment for clarification.
