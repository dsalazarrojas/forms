# Reopening Attendance Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the attendance of students who are being considered for readmission to our school. It helps us gather essential information about the student's status and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the student.
2. Ensure that you are not duplicating any existing records.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name**: Enter the full name of the student, including their surname and given names.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person who can be reached for further contact, usually a parent or guardian.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who can be reached for further contact.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yy).
* **School Grade**: Select the current grade level of the student from the options provided.
* **Attendance**: Indicate whether the student is currently attending school or not by selecting one or more of the options.
* **Reason for Absence**: Briefly explain the reason why the student is not attending school, if applicable.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to catch any errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the relevant department or supervisor for guidance.
