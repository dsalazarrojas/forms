# Vaccination Clinic Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccination Clinic Registration Form is a tool used by clinic staff to register patients, gather contact information, and collect clinic data. This form is intended to ensure that clinic staff have all the necessary details to follow up with patients after their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
2. Provide your Email and Contact Phone Number (if applicable).
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Yes or No).
4. Enter your Clinic Name, Clinic Address, Clinic Phone Number, and Clinic Email (if applicable).
5. Select the type of Vaccine(s) you will be receiving (Yes or No).
6. Enter any additional Clinic Notes.
7. Fill in the Contact Name, Contact Title, and Contact Role (if applicable).
8. Add any Contact Notes (if applicable).
9. Specify the Clinic Services you will be receiving (if applicable).
10. Check your Vaccination Status and Follow-up Status (if applicable).
11. Enter any Follow-up Notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* **First Name (1)** (`first_name`, `text`, Optional): Your given name.

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name (2)** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Your surname.

### Date of Birth (3)
* **Date of Birth (3)** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Email (4)
* **Email (4)** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address.

### Email Hint (5)
* **Email Hint (5)** (`email_hint`, `text`, Optional): A hint for your email address (if needed).

### Phone (6)
* **Phone (6)** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your phone number.

### Contact Type (7)
* **Contact Type (7)** (`contact_type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Yes or No).

### Contact Phone Number (8)
* **Contact Phone Number (8)** (`contact_phone_number`, `text`, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### Contact Email Number (9)
* **Contact Email Number (9)** (`contact_email_number`, `text`, Optional): Enter your contact email number.

### Address (10)
* **Address (10)** (`address`, `text`, Optional): Enter your address.

### Vaccine Type (11)
* **Vaccine Type (11)** (`vaccine_type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the type of vaccine you will be receiving (Yes or No).

### Clinic Name (12)
* **Clinic Name (12)** (`clinic_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your clinic's name.

### Clinic Address (13)
* **Clinic Address (13)** (`clinic_address`, `text`, Optional): Enter your clinic's address.

### Clinic Phone (14)
* **Clinic Phone (14)** (`clinic_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your clinic's phone number.

### Clinic Email (15)
* **Clinic Email (15)** (`clinic_email`, `text`, Optional): Enter your clinic's email address.

### Clinic Hours (16)
* **Clinic Hours (16)** (`clinic_hours`, `text`, Optional): Enter your clinic's operating hours.

### Clinic Notes (17)
* **Clinic Notes (17)** (`clinic_notes`, `text`, Optional): Enter any additional notes for your clinic.

### Contact Name (18)
* **Contact Name (18)** (`contact_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter the contact person's name.

### Contact Title (19)
* **Contact Title (19)** (`contact_title`, `text`, Optional): Enter the contact person's title.

### Contact Role (20)
* **Contact Role (20)** (`contact_role`, `text`, Optional): Enter the contact person's role.

### Contact Notes (21)
* **Contact Notes (21)** (`contact_notes`, `text`, Optional): Enter any additional contact notes.

### Clinic Services (22)
* **Clinic Services (22)** (`clinic_services`, `text`, Optional): Specify the services your clinic offers.

### Vaccination Status (23)
* **Vaccination Status (23)** (`vaccination_status`, `text`, Optional): Check your vaccination status.

### Follow-up Status (24)
* **Follow-up Status (24)** (`follow_up_status`, `text`, Optional): Check your follow-up status.

### Follow-up Notes (25)
* **Follow-up Notes (25)** (`follow_up_notes`, `text`, Optional): Enter any follow-up notes.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the clinic staff.
* Remember to keep your contact information up-to-date to ensure you receive follow-up contact and updates from the clinic.
