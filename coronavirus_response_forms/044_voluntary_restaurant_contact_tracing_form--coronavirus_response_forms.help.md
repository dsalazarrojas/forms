# Voluntary Restaurant Contact Tracing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from restaurant employees and their contacts to help facilitate contact tracing during the coronavirus pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the General Info section with your basic contact and employee information.
2. In the Employee Info section, provide details about your health status and any recent exposures.
3. In the Contact Info section, enter information about your contacts, including their health status and exposures.
4. Answer the questions in the Health Info section to provide more detailed health information about yourself and your contacts.
5. In the Exposure Info section, describe any potential exposures to the virus.
6. Complete the Travel Info section by providing information about your recent travels and exposures.
7. If applicable, answer the Other Health Info section with any additional health information.
8. Finally, complete the form by submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`General Info`, `text`, required): This section collects your name, email, and phone number.
* **Employee Info** (`Employee Info`, `text`, required): This section asks for your health status, any recent exposures, and if you're experiencing any symptoms.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required): Enter the name, contact information, and health status of your contacts.
* **Health Info** (`Health Info`, `text`, required): Provide more detailed health information about yourself and your contacts.
* **Exposure Info** (`Exposure Info`, `text`, required): Describe any potential exposures to the virus.
* **Travel Info** (`Travel Info`, `text`, required): List your recent travels and exposures.
* **Other Health Info** (`Other Health Info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional health information.
* **Employee Travel Info** (`Employee Travel Info`, `text`, optional): If you're an employee, enter your recent travels and exposures.
* **Contact Travel Info** (`Contact Travel Info`, `text`, optional): If your contact has traveled recently, enter their travel history.
* **Employee Other Info** (`Employee Other Info`, `text`, optional): If you're an employee, enter any other relevant health information.
* **Contact Other Info** (`Contact Other Info`, `text`, optional): If your contact has other health information that's relevant to this form, enter it here.
* **Employee Exposure Info** (`Employee Exposure Info`, `text`, optional): If you're an employee, enter any potential exposures to the virus.
* **Contact Exposure Info** (`Contact Exposure Info`, `text`, optional): If your contact has had potential exposures, enter that information.
* **Other Exposure Info** (`Other Exposure Info`, `text`, optional): Enter any other exposures relevant to this form.
* **Employee Contact History** (`Employee Contact History`, `text`, optional): If you're an employee, enter your contact history.
* **Contact Contact History** (`Contact Contact History`, `text`, optional): If your contact has a contact history, enter it here.
* **Employee Other Contacts** (`Employee Other Contacts`, `text`, optional): If you're an employee, enter any other contacts relevant to this form.
* **Contact Other Contacts** (`Contact Other Contacts`, `text`, optional): If your contact has other contacts relevant to this form.
* **Employee Travel History** (`Employee Travel History`, `text`, optional): If you're an employee, enter your travel history.
* **Contact Travel History** (`Contact Travel History`, `text`, optional): If your contact has a travel history, enter it here.
* **Employee Other History** (`Employee Other History`, `text`, optional): If you're an employee, enter any other relevant history.
* **Contact Other History** (`Contact Other History`, `text`, optional): If your contact has other relevant history, enter it here.
