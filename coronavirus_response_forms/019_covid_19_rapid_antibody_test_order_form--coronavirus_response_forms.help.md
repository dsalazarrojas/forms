# COVID 19 Rapid Antibody Test Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who need to order COVID-19 rapid antibody tests for various reasons, including home testing, clinical trials, workplace screening, or educational purposes. It's essential to ensure accurate and efficient ordering and shipping of the tests.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the required fields, starting with the customer's information.
2.  Enter your name, organization name (if applicable), and address for shipping and billing.
3.  Specify the number of test kits you want to order, ensuring it's at least one kit.
4.  Choose the type of kit you need, including IgG/IgM Rapid Test or Total Antibody Test.
5.  Enter your payment confirmation ID from the receipt.
6.  Select the intended use for the test, such as home testing or clinical trials.
7.  Specify the date you need the test by.
8.  Enter your email address for shipping tracking updates.
9.  Read and agree to the compliance and disclaimer section, ensuring you understand that the test is for informational purposes only.
10.  Acknowledge that you understand the test result is not a diagnosis of a current infection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (1): Enter your full name to complete the order.
* **Organization Name** (2): Leave this field blank if you're an individual order. If you're placing the order on behalf of an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Shipping Address** (3): Enter the address where you want the test kit shipped to. This is required for accurate delivery.
* **Billing Address** (4): If your billing address is different from your shipping address, enter it here.
* **Number of Test Kits Ordered** (5): Enter the number of test kits you need to order. Minimum order is one kit.
* **Kit Type** (6): Choose between IgG/IgM Rapid Test and Total Antibody Test.
* **Payment Confirmation ID** (7): Enter the ID from your receipt to complete the payment process.
* **Intended Use** (8): Select the intended use for the test, such as home testing or clinical trials.
* **Date Needed By** (9): Specify the date you need the test kit by for accurate delivery.
* **Email for Shipping Tracking** (10): Enter your email address where you want to receive shipping updates.
* **Compliance and Disclaimer** (11): Read and agree to the disclaimer that the test is for informational purposes only.
* **I Acknowledge** (12): Confirm that you understand the test result is not a diagnosis of a current infection.
