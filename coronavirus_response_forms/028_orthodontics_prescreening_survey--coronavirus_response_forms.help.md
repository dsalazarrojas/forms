# Orthodontics PreScreening Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your health and well-being in order to help us better understand your needs and provide you with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. For each question, select one of the options provided in the dropdown menu.
2. Make sure to answer all questions carefully and accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any question, select "Not sure" as your answer.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to review and submit your responses.
