# COVID 19 Vaccine Information And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that patients understand the benefits and risks of the COVID-19 vaccine, and that they have been adequately informed and screened before receiving the vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. **Vaccine Information Review**: This section is for you to review the information about the vaccine, including its benefits and risks. Please read carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any information.
6. **Received Fact Sheet**: Check if you have received the vaccine fact sheet, which provides detailed information about the vaccine.
7. **Understand Vaccine Type**: Confirm that you understand the type of vaccine to be administered.
8. **Understand Benefits**: Confirm that you understand the benefits of the vaccine.
9. **Understand Risks**: Confirm that you understand the known risks and side effects of the vaccine.
10. **Unknown Risks**: Acknowledge that you understand that there may be potential unknown risks associated with the vaccine.
11. **Alternatives**: This section is for you to review alternative options for vaccination, if available.
12. **Understand Alternatives**: Confirm that you understand alternative options for vaccination.
13. **Understand Consequences**: Confirm that you understand the consequences of declining vaccination.
14. **Medical History**: This section is for you to disclose any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
15. **Medical Conditions Disclosed**: Confirm that you have disclosed all medical conditions.
16. **Medications Disclosed**: Confirm that you have disclosed all medications.
17. **Allergies Disclosed**: Confirm that you have disclosed all allergies.
18. **Pregnancy Disclosed**: Confirm that you have accurately reported your pregnancy status.
19. **Screening**: This section is for you to confirm that you have been screened for medical conditions and are feeling well.
20. **Feel Well**: Confirm that you are feeling well and not acutely ill.
21. **No Fever**: Confirm that you do not have a fever or elevated temperature.
22. **No COVID-19 Symptoms**: Confirm that you are not experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
23. **Recent Vaccination**: Confirm that you have disclosed any recent vaccinations.
24. **Informed Consent**: This section is for you to confirm that you have been informed of your rights and that you have made an informed decision.
25. **Voluntary Participation**: Confirm that you voluntarily choose to be vaccinated.
26. **Informed Decision**: Confirm that you made this decision based on complete information.
27. **All Questions Answered**: Confirm that all your questions have been answered.
28. **Right to Refuse**: Confirm that you understand that you can refuse the vaccination without penalty.
29. **Authorization**: This section is for you to confirm that you authorize the healthcare provider to vaccinate you.
30. **Authorize Vaccination**: Confirm that you authorize the healthcare provider to vaccinate you.
31. **Administering Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider administering the vaccine.
32. **Date of Consent**: Enter the date of consent.

## Tips

* Please complete the form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask the healthcare provider or contact your healthcare provider's office for assistance.
* Make sure to review the vaccine information carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any information.
* Confirm that you have made an informed decision based on complete information.
