# COVID 19 Contact Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a contact who has had exposure to a confirmed case of COVID-19. It is essential for contact tracing and quarantine procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Investigation Date**, which is the date on which this form is being filled out.
2. Enter the **Case Reference Number**, which is the ID number of the confirmed case.
3. Enter your **Name**, as the person conducting this investigation.
4. Enter the **Contact's Name**, which is the full name of the exposed person.
5. Enter the **Contact's Phone Number**, which is the best phone contact information for the exposed individual.
6. Enter the **Contact's Email Address**, which is the exposed person's email address (if applicable).
7. Enter the **Contact's Date of Birth**, which is the exposed person's date of birth (if applicable).
8. Enter the **Contact's Address**, which is the exposed person's street address.
9. Enter the **City**, which is the city where the contact took place.
10. Enter the **Zip Code**, which is the zip code where the contact took place.
11. Enter the **Confirmed Case Name**, which is the name of the person who is COVID-19 positive.
12. Enter the **Date of Exposure**, which is when the contact with the confirmed case occurred.
13. Select the **Type of Exposure**, which is how the contact occurred (e.g., household, workplace, school, etc.).
14. Enter the **Location of Exposure**, which is where the exposure took place.
15. If applicable, enter the **Duration of Exposure**, which is the length of time the contact took place.
16. Select the **Proximity During Exposure**, which is how close the contact was to the confirmed case.
17. Select the **Masking Status During Exposure**, which is whether either person was wearing a mask.
18. Select the **Ventilation of Exposure Location**, which is the ventilation status of where the contact took place.
19. Select if the contact is **Experiencing Symptoms**, which is whether the contact is showing symptoms of COVID-19.
20. If applicable, enter the **Symptom Onset Date**, which is when the symptoms started (if experiencing symptoms).
21. Select all **Symptoms Reported** by the contact, which is a list of all applicable symptoms.
22. Select if the contact has been **tested for COVID-19**.
23. If tested, enter the **COVID-19 Test Date**.
24. If tested, enter the **Test Type**, which is the type of test used.
25. Select if the contact was **advised to Quarantine**.
26. If applicable, enter the **Recommended Quarantine Duration**.
27. Select if the contact is **willing to comply with Isolation**.
28. Enter the **Follow-Up Plan**, which is the plan for future contact with the exposed individual.
29. Enter any **Additional Notes**, which is any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investigation Date (1)**: Enter the date on which this form is being filled out.
* **Case Reference Number (2)**: Enter the ID number of the confirmed case.
* **Investigator Name (3)**: Enter your name as the person conducting this investigation.
* **Contact Name (4)**: Enter the full name of the exposed person.
* **Contact Phone Number (5)**: Enter the best phone contact information for the exposed individual.
* **Contact Email (6)**: Enter the exposed person's email address (if applicable).
* **Contact Date of Birth (7)**: Enter the exposed person's date of birth (if applicable).
* **Contact Address (8)**: Enter the exposed person's street address.
* **City (9)**: Enter the city where the contact took place.
* **Zip Code (10)**: Enter the zip code where the contact took place.
* **Confirmed Case Name (11)**: Enter the name of the person who is COVID-19 positive.
* **Date of Exposure (12)**: Enter the date when the contact with the confirmed case occurred.
* **Type of Exposure (13)**: Select how the contact occurred (e.g., household, workplace, school, etc.).
* **Location of Exposure (14)**: Enter the location where the exposure took place.
* **Duration of Exposure (15)**: If applicable, enter the length of time the contact took place.
* **Proximity During Exposure (16)**: Select how close the contact was to the confirmed case.
* **Masking Status During Exposure (17)**: Select whether either person was wearing a mask during exposure.
* **Ventilation of Exposure Location (18)**: Select the ventilation status of where the contact took place.
* **Symptoms Current (19)**: Select if the contact is experiencing any symptoms.
* **Symptom Onset Date (20)**: If experiencing symptoms, enter when the symptoms started (if applicable).
* **Symptoms Reported (21)**: Select all applicable symptoms reported by the contact.
* **Tested for COVID-19 (22)**: Select if the contact has been tested for COVID-19.
* **Test Date (23)**: If tested, enter the date of the test.
* **Test Type (24)**: If tested, enter the type of test used.
* **Quarantine Advised (25)**: Select if the contact was advised to quarantine.
* **Recommended Quarantine Duration (26)**: If applicable, enter the recommended duration of quarantine.
* **Quarantine Compliance (27)**: Select if the contact is willing to comply with isolation.
* **Follow-Up Plan (28)**: Enter the plan for future contact with the exposed individual.
* **Additional Notes (29)**: Enter any other relevant information.
