# Post Vaccination Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect post-vaccination information from patients to ensure their health and well-being after receiving the vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's personal details as they appear on their medical records.
2. Ensure that all questions are completed with the best possible accuracy, and if you're unsure about anything, please do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Name** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact information, such as phone number or email address.
* **Date of Vaccination** (`vaccination_date`, date, optional): Record the date the patient received the vaccine.
* **Time of Vaccination** (`vaccination_time`, time, optional): Record the time the patient received the vaccine.
* **Physician Information** (`physician_info`, select one, optional): Select the type of physician who administered the vaccine (Primary Care or Specialist).

### Page 2: Patient Health
* **Vaccine Side Effects** (`vaccine_side_effects`, select multiple, optional): Check any side effects the patient experienced after vaccination, such as Fever, Dizziness, or Headache.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, select one, optional): Record the patient's medication history, such as Aspirin, Ibuprofen, or Acetaminophen.

### Page 3: Medical History
* **Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, select one, optional): Check if the patient has any allergies, such as Allergic Reaction, Food Allergy, or Insect Sting.
* **Important Info** (`patient_important_info`, select one, optional): Record if the patient has any important information, such as true or false.

### Page 4: Additional Comments
* **Patient Response** (`patient_response`, text, optional): Leave any comments or responses from the patient.
* **Doctor Comments** (`physician_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments from the doctor or healthcare professional.
* **Form Notes** (`form_notes`, note, optional): Record any additional notes regarding the form completion.
* **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, note, optional): Record any comments from the patient.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any question or have any concerns, please consult with the patient's healthcare professional.
* This form is meant to be used for post-vaccination purposes, so please use it only for this specific event.
