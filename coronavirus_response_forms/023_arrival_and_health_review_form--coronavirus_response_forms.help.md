# Arrival And Health Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Arrival And Health Review Form" is designed to gather information about your health status, travel history, and vaccination status upon arrival. This form is essential to ensure your safety and the safety of others, as well as to identify any potential risks or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all necessary information.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to facilitate contact and communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Planned Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your planned arrival to track your travel history.
* **Fever or Feeling Feverish** (`fever`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you have a fever or feel feverish. If you're unsure, select "Not sure."
* **Dry Cough or Persistent Cough** (`cough`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you have a dry cough or persistent cough.
* **Difficulty Breathing or Shortness of Breath** (`difficulty_breathing`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you experience difficulty breathing or shortness of breath.
* **Loss of Taste or Smell** (`loss_of_taste_smell`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you have experienced loss of taste or smell.
* **Fatigue or Weakness** (`fatigue`, `select_one`, optional): Answer "True" if you feel unusually fatigued or weak. This is an optional field.
* **Sore Throat** (`sore_throat`, `select_one`, optional): Answer "True" if you have a sore throat. This is an optional field.
* **Close Contact with Confirmed COVID-19 Case** (`close_contact_confirmed`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case within the last 14 days.
* **Close Contact with Someone Showing Symptoms** (`close_contact_symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you had close contact with someone showing symptoms within the last 14 days.
* **Recent Travel** (`recent_travel`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you have traveled in the last 14 days, especially to high-risk areas.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `text`, optional): If you answered "True" to the previous question, enter the destination you traveled to.
* **Part of a Vulnerable Population** (`vulnerable_population`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if you are part of a vulnerable population, such as the elderly, those with compromised immune systems, or chronic illness.
* **Current Medications or Health Conditions** (`medications`, `text`, optional): If you have any medications or health conditions affecting your immune system, please list them here.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Fully vaccinated," "Partially vaccinated," "Not vaccinated," or "Prefer not to say" to indicate your current vaccination status.
* **Agreement to Follow Health and Safety Protocols** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required): Answer "I agree" to confirm that you will follow all health and safety protocols.
