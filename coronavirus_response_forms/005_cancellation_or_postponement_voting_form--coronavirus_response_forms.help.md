# Cancellation Or Postponement Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for attendees and stakeholders to submit their vote on whether to cancel or postpone an event due to concerns about the coronavirus pandemic. The form helps the event organizers understand the attendees' opinions and make informed decisions about the event's fate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and truthfully to ensure your voice is heard.
2. Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you for updates.
3. Select your organization affiliation, event name, and original event date to help us understand the context.
4. Choose your vote from the available options.
5. If you have concerns or thoughts, provide them in the text field.
6. Confirm your vote by selecting "Yes - this is my final vote".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter Full Name** (`1`): Enter your full name.
* **Voter Email Address** (`2`): Enter your email address so we can contact you for updates.
* **Voter Phone Number** (`3`): Optional - enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization Affiliation** (`4`): Select your role or organization (e.g., attendee, vendor, speaker).
* **Event Name** (`5`): Enter the name of the event in question.
* **Original Event Date** (`6`): Enter the originally scheduled event date.
* **Event Location** (`7`): Optional - enter the event location.
* **Your Vote Decision** (`8`): Choose your vote from the available options:
	+ Cancel the event entirely
	+ Postpone to a later date
	+ Proceed as planned
	+ Convert to virtual format
	+ Hybrid option - both in-person and virtual
	+ Undecided - need more information
* **Reason for Your Vote** (`9`): If you chose to cancel or postpone, explain why.
* **COVID-19 Concern Level** (`10`): Select your level of concern about COVID risks:
	+ Very concerned - high risk
	+ Moderately concerned
	+ Slightly concerned
	+ Not concerned
	+ I am vaccinated and feel safe
* **Current Health Status** (`11`): Select your current health status:
	+ Healthy - no symptoms
	+ At higher risk
	+ Live with someone at higher risk
	+ Recently recovered
	+ Prefer not to say
* **Vaccination Status** (`12`): Optional - select your COVID-19 vaccination status:
	+ Fully vaccinated and boosted
	+ Fully vaccinated
	+ Partially vaccinated
	+ Not vaccinated
	+ Prefer not to disclose
* **Preferred Postponement Timeline** (`13`): Optional - select the timeframe for postponement:
	+ 1 to 3 months
	+ 3 to 6 months
	+ 6 to 12 months
	+ Next year
	+ No preference
	+ Not applicable
* **Interest in Virtual Option** (`14`): Select whether you would participate virtually:
	+ Yes - prefer virtual
	+ Yes - as backup option
	+ Maybe - depends on circumstances
	+ No - prefer in-person only
* **Financial Impact of Cancellation** (`15`): Select the financial impact of cancellation on you:
	+ Significant financial loss
	+ Moderate financial impact
	+ Minor financial impact
	+ No financial impact
	+ Would save money
* **Would You Need a Refund** (`16`): Select whether you need a refund:
	+ Yes - full refund needed
	+ Yes - partial refund
	+ No - donate as credit
	+ No - already refunded
	+ Not applicable - did not pay
* **Alternative Suggestions** (`17`): Optional - provide any ideas for alternatives.
* **How Should We Communicate Updates** (`18`): Select your preferred method of communication:
	+ Email
	+ Phone call
	+ Text message
	+ Social media
	+ Organization website
* **Willing to Help with Rescheduling** (`19`): Optional - select whether you can help with rescheduling:
	+ Yes - happy to help
	+ Maybe - depending on time commitment
	+ No - unable to help
* **Additional Comments** (`20`): Optional - provide any other thoughts or concerns.
* **Vote Confirmation** (`21`): Confirm your vote by selecting "Yes - this is my final vote".
* **Vote Submission Date** (`22`): Enter the date of your vote submission.
