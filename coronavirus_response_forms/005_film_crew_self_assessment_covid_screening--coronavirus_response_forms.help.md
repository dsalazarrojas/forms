# Film Crew Self Assessment COVID Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the COVID-19 self-assessment form for film crew members. The form is designed to help identify potential risks of exposure to COVID-19 and ensure the health and safety of the crew members and the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly. Select the options that best apply to your situation.
2. Please select all relevant options for each question where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1:**
	+ **Have you had any symptoms?**: This question is asking whether you have experienced any symptoms of COVID-19, such as a cough, fever, or loss of taste or smell. Please select "Yes" if you have had any of these symptoms, or "No" if you have not.
* **Have you travelled to a high-risk area?**: This question is asking about your recent travel history, especially if you have visited areas with high COVID-19 prevalence. Select "Yes" if you have traveled to a high-risk area, or "No" if you have not.
* **Have you had contact with anyone with COVID-19?**: This question is asking whether you have had close contact with anyone who has been diagnosed with COVID-19. Select "Yes" if you have had contact with someone with COVID-19, or "No" if you have not.
* **Have you had a fever?**: This question is asking about your body temperature. A fever is a common symptom of COVID-19. If you have had a fever, please select "Yes" and answer the next question to provide more information about the severity of the fever. If you have not had a fever, select "No".
* **If yes, how high was your temperature?**: This question is only applicable if you selected "Yes" for the previous question. If you had a fever, please enter the temperature in degrees Celsius.
* **Have you experienced any loss of taste or smell?**: This question is asking about your senses. Please select all that apply if you have experienced loss of taste or smell.
* **Have you taken any medication?**: This question is asking whether you are taking any medications. Please select all that apply if you are taking any medications.
* **Have you had any contact with someone who has COVID-19?**: This question is similar to the previous one, asking about your contact with someone who has been diagnosed with COVID-19. Select "Yes" if you have had contact with someone with COVID-19, or "No" if you have not.
* **Have you had any travel history?**: This question is asking about your recent travel history, especially if you have traveled internationally or domestically. Select all that apply if you have had any travel history.
* **Have you been in contact with anyone who has COVID-19?**: This question is similar to the previous one, asking about your contact with someone who has been diagnosed with COVID-19. Select "Yes" if you have had contact with someone with COVID-19, or "No" if you have not.
* **Have you had any fever over 38°C?**: This question is asking whether you have had a fever over 38°C (100.4°F). Select "Yes" if you have had a fever over 38°C, or "No" if you have not.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* Select all relevant options for each question where applicable.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please consult with your supervisor or a medical professional.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to your film crew management team or a healthcare professional.
