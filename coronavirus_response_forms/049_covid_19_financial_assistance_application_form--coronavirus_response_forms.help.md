# COVID 19 Financial Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information from individuals who are experiencing financial hardship due to the COVID-19 pandemic. This information will be used to assess eligibility for financial assistance and determine the amount of aid you may receive.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the application date and time at the top of the form.
2.  Enter your personal details, including your full legal name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
3.  Select your current employment status and occupation.
4.  Provide information about your income, including your annual income before COVID-19, your current monthly income, and the percentage of income lost due to the pandemic.
5.  Enter the number of people in your household and the number of dependents you have.
6.  List your monthly essential expenses, including rent, food, utilities, and medications.
7.  Select your housing status and enter your monthly rent or mortgage payment.
8.  If you have any outstanding debts, enter the approximate amount.
9.  If you have incurred any medical expenses related to COVID-19, enter the approximate amount.
10.  If you have received any government or other assistance, describe the type and amount received.
11.  Enter the amount of financial assistance you are requesting and the purpose of the aid.
12.  Write a statement explaining your financial situation and how the pandemic has affected you.
13.  Finally, select the expected timeline for your income to recover.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Application Date**: This is the date and time you are submitting your application. Please ensure it is accurate and complete.
    *   **Type: Date**
*   **Applicant Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
    *   **Type: Text**
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
    *   **Type: Date**
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
    *   **Type: Email**
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
    *   **Type: Text**
*   **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status:
    *   **Type: Select One** (Employed but reduced hours, Unemployed, Self-employed, Gig worker, Freelancer, Other)
*   **Occupation**: Enter your primary job or profession.
    *   **Type: Text**
*   **Employer Name**: If you are currently employed, enter your employer's name.
    *   **Type: Text** (Optional)
*   **Annual Income Before COVID-19**: Enter your approximate annual income before the pandemic.
    *   **Type: Number**
*   **Current Monthly Income**: Enter your current monthly income.
    *   **Type: Number**
*   **Income Reduction Percentage**: Enter the percentage of income lost due to the pandemic.
    *   **Type: Number**
*   **Household Size**: Enter the number of people in your household.
    *   **Type: Number**
*   **Number of Dependents**: Enter the number of dependents you have.
    *   **Type: Number**
*   **Monthly Essential Expenses**: Enter your approximate monthly essential expenses.
    *   **Type: Number**
*   **Housing Status**: Select your housing status:
    *   **Type: Select One** (Own home, Renting, Living with family, Homeless or at risk, Other)
*   **Monthly Rent or Mortgage Payment**: Enter your approximate monthly rent or mortgage payment.
    *   **Type: Number** (Optional)
*   **Current Savings Balance**: Enter your approximate current savings balance.
    *   **Type: Number** (Optional)
*   **Outstanding Debts**: Enter the approximate amount of any outstanding debts.
    *   **Type: Number** (Optional)
*   **COVID-19 Related Medical Expenses**: Enter the approximate amount of any COVID-19 related medical expenses.
    *   **Type: Number** (Optional)
*   **Government or Other Assistance Received**: Describe the type and amount of any government or other assistance received.
    *   **Type: Text**
*   **Total Assistance Received**: Enter the total amount of any government or other assistance received.
    *   **Type: Number** (Optional)
*   **Amount of Assistance Requested**: Enter the amount of financial assistance you are requesting.
    *   **Type: Number**
*   **Purpose of Financial Assistance**: Enter the purpose of the financial aid you are requesting.
    *   **Type: Text**
*   **Statement of Financial Hardship**: Enter a description of your financial situation and how the pandemic has affected you.
    *   **Type: Text**
*   **Expected Timeline for Income Recovery**: Select when you expect your income to recover:
    *   **Type: Select One** (1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, Over 1 year, Uncertain)

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for assistance.
*   Please note that the information collected will be used to assess your eligibility for financial assistance and determine the amount of aid you may receive.
