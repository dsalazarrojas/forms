# COVID 19 Vaccine Interest Form For Businesses - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from businesses and organizations interested in on-site COVID-19 vaccination programs for their employees. We will use this information to understand the needs and preferences of businesses like yours.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for your business.
2. Select your business type from the provided options.
3. Provide your contact person's name, title, email, and phone number.
4. Enter your business address, city, and state.
5. Select your preferred location for vaccination.
6. Choose your preferred vaccination type and timeline.
7. Let us know if you have any logistical needs or accommodations.
8. If you have any challenges or concerns about the program, please let us know.
9. Finally, let us know if you would like to follow up with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business or Organization Name**: Provide the name of your business or organization.
* **Type of Business or Organization**: Select the type of business or organization you represent (e.g., Retail, Healthcare, Manufacturing, etc.).
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Business Address**: Enter the address of your business.
* **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where your business is located.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of your business.
* **Business Phone Number**: Enter the main phone number of your business.
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the number of employees in your business.
* **Interest in on-site vaccination program for employees**: Select your level of interest in the on-site vaccination program.
* **Estimated number of employees who would participate**: Enter the estimated number of employees who would participate in the program.
* **Preferred Timeline for Vaccination Program**: Select your preferred timeline for the vaccination program.
* **Vaccine Type Preference**: Select your preferred type of vaccine.
* **Preferred Location for Vaccination**: Select your preferred location for the vaccination program.
* **Facility available on-site for vaccinations**: Select whether your business has a facility available on-site for vaccinations.
* **Preferred Hours for Vaccination Program**: Select your preferred hours for the vaccination program.
* **Provide paid time off for vaccination**: Select whether you will provide paid time off for employees to get vaccinated.
* **Type of support needed from government**: Check all the types of support you need from the government.
* **Anticipated challenges for program**: Enter any anticipated challenges for the program.
* **Specific accommodations or needs**: Enter any specific accommodations or needs you may have.
* **Follow-up discussion**: Let us know if you would like to follow up with us after submitting this form.</thinking>

Note: This help guide will be available online and will be provided to businesses that submit the form.
