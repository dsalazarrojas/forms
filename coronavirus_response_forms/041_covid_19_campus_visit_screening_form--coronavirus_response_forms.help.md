# COVID 19 Campus Visit Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Campus Visit Screening Form is a mandatory form designed to ensure the safety and health of all visitors on campus. It collects information about your travel history, health status, and contact details to facilitate contact tracing and reduce the risk of COVID-19 transmission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your full name in the **Visitor Name** field.
*   Provide your email address in the **Email Address** field for contact tracing purposes.
*   Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field for emergency contact.
*   Select the date of your visit in the **Date of Visit** field.
*   Choose the time of your arrival in the **Time of Arrival** field.
*   Select the purpose of your visit from the drop-down menu in the **Purpose of Visit** field.
*   Enter the building you are visiting in the **Destination Building** field.
*   Identify your host's name in the **Host Name** field.
*   If applicable, enter your host's department in the **Host Department** field.
*   Enter your current body temperature in the **Current Temperature** field in Fahrenheit.
*   Select any current symptoms you are experiencing from the options in the **Current Symptoms** field.
*   Check if you have had known exposure to COVID-19 within the past 14 days in the **Known COVID-19 Exposure** field.
*   Indicate whether you have had a recent positive test result for COVID-19 within the past 10 days in the **Recent Positive Test** field.
*   Select your vaccination status regarding the COVID-19 vaccine in the **Vaccination Status** field.
*   Indicate your recent travel history within the past 14 days in the **Recent Travel** field.
*   Specify the areas on campus you plan to access in the **Campus Areas to Access** field.
*   Choose how long you plan to stay on campus in the **Estimated Duration** field.
*   Enter your vehicle information (e.g., license plate number) in the **Vehicle Information** field if applicable.
*   Specify where you will park in the **Parking Location** field if applicable.
*   Acknowledge that you are aware of and will follow campus policies regarding mask-wearing and distancing in the **Campus Policies Acknowledged** field.
*   Agree to wear a mask while on campus in the **Mask Agreement** field.
*   Consent to participate in contact tracing if necessary in the **Contact Tracing Consent** field.
*   Declare that the information provided is accurate in the **Health Declaration** field.
*   Enter the time of your departure when leaving campus in the **Time of Departure** field if applicable.
*   Sign the form with your full name in the **Visitor Signature** field.
*   Enter the name of the screener who is approving your entry in the **Screener Name** field if applicable.
*   Sign with the screener's name in the **Screener Signature** field if applicable.
*   Once you have completed the form, submit it for screening approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Visitor Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact tracing purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
*   **Date of Visit**: Select the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time of Arrival**: Choose the time you plan to arrive on campus.
*   **Purpose of Visit**: Select the reason for your visit from the drop-down menu.
*   **Destination Building**: Enter the building or location you are visiting.
*   **Host Name**: Identify your host's name.
*   **Host Department**: If applicable, enter your host's department or office.
*   **Current Temperature**: Enter your current body temperature in Fahrenheit.
*   **Current Symptoms**: Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing from the options.
*   **Known COVID-19 Exposure**: Check if you have had known exposure to COVID-19 within the past 14 days.
*   **Recent Positive Test**: Check if you have had a recent positive test result for COVID-19 within the past 10 days.
*   **Vaccination Status**: Select your vaccination status regarding the COVID-19 vaccine.
*   **Recent Travel**: Indicate your recent travel history within the past 14 days.
*   **Campus Areas to Access**: Specify the areas on campus you plan to access.
*   **Estimated Duration**: Choose how long you plan to stay on campus.
*   **Vehicle Information**: Enter your vehicle information (e.g., license plate number) if applicable.
*   **Parking Location**: Specify where you will park if applicable.
*   **Campus Policies Acknowledged**: Check that you are aware of and will follow campus policies regarding mask-wearing and distancing.
*   **Mask Agreement**: Check that you agree to wear a mask while on campus.
*   **Contact Tracing Consent**: Check that you consent to participate in contact tracing if necessary.
*   **Health Declaration**: Declare that the information provided is accurate.
*   **Time of Departure**: Enter the time of your departure when leaving campus if applicable.
*   **Visitor Signature**: Sign with your full name.
*   **Screener Name**: Enter the name of the screener who approved your entry if applicable.
*   **Screener Signature**: Sign with the screener's name if applicable.
*   **Entry Approved**: Select the status of your entry based on the screener's decision.
