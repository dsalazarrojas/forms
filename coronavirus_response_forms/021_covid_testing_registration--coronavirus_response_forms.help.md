# Covid Testing Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect information from individuals who have undergone Covid testing, including patient demographics, test results, and relevant medical history. The purpose of this form is to facilitate the processing and reporting of Covid test results, and to ensure accurate tracking of contact tracing and follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing information for each field as requested.
2.  Make sure to select the correct test type from the dropdown menu (e.g. Covid, Rapid, PCR, or Other).
3.  Enter your test date and appointment time, if applicable.
4.  Provide your healthcare provider's information, if applicable.
5.  Enter your test results, including any relevant medical history.
6.  Specify your test status (Not Tested, Tested Positive, Tested Negative, or Tested Inconclusive).
7.  Provide any additional information that may be relevant for contact tracing or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant patient information.
*  **Test Results** (`test_results`, text, optional): Report the results of your Covid test, including any symptoms or medical history.
*  **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms you have experienced, if applicable.
*  **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter your appointment time, if you have one.
*  **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you underwent testing.
*  **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): Name and title of the healthcare provider who administered your test.
*  **Test Type** (`test_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of test you underwent (Covid, Rapid, PCR, or Other).
*  **Result** (`result`, text, optional): Report any notable results or complications from your test.
*  **Follow-up Info** (`follow_up_info`, text, optional): Provide any follow-up information, such as next steps or further instructions.
*  **Test Status** (`test_status`, select_one, required): Specify your test status (Not Tested, Tested Positive, Tested Negative, or Tested Inconclusive).
*  **Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`, text, optional): Enter any contact tracing information, if applicable.
*  **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your test or treatment.
