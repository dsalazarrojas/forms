# Face Mask Requirement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Face Mask Requirement Survey is a form used to report whether workers are required or not to wear face masks in their work environment. It helps management or HR teams to understand the current face mask policy and its effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to your situation.
- Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
- If you're not sure about a question, try to provide the best answer you can based on your work environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Worker Type** (`worker_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the types of workers that apply to you, such as 'Yes' for workers who are required to wear face masks.
* **Face Mask Requirement** (`face_mask_requirement`, select_one, required): Select whether you are required or not to wear face masks based on your work environment.
* **Reason for no Requirement** (`reason_for_no_requirement`, text, optional): If you selected "No" for Face Mask Requirement, provide a brief reason why you are not required to wear face masks in your work environment.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information you want to provide about your face mask requirement, such as any relevant context or notes.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer accurately and honestly to help management or HR teams make informed decisions about face mask policy.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- This form is meant to be completed only once.
