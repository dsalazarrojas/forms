# COVID 19 Emergency Paid Sick Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for employees to request emergency paid sick leave due to a COVID-19 related reason. It helps the company's HR department track and process such requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Make sure you have attached any necessary documents or information as required in the field notes or hint.
3. Review and verify your information carefully before submitting the form.
4. Ensure all necessary fields are filled out correctly as indicated in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: <employee_name> (<employee_id>)
	+ A field to enter your full name and employee ID.
* **Employee ID**: <employee_id>
	+ Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department**: <department>
	+ Enter your department name or number.
* **Position Title**: <position_title>
	+ Enter your job title or position.
* **Direct Supervisor Name**: <supervisor_name>
	+ Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Request Date**: <request_date>
	+ Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Type of Leave Requested**: <leave_type>
	+ Select the type of leave you are requesting:
		- COVID-19 Illness
		- COVID-19 Exposure Quarantine
		- Care for Family Member with COVID
		- Childcare Closure due to COVID
		- School Closure due to COVID
		- Other COVID-related
* **Leave Start Date**: <leave_start_date>
	+ Enter when you need to begin your leave.
* **Anticipated Leave End Date**: <leave_end_date>
	+ Enter when you expect to return to work.
* **Total Days of Leave Requested**: <total_days_requested>
	+ Enter the number of workdays you need.
* **Reason for Leave**: <reason_for_leave>
	+ Explain why you need paid sick leave.
* **COVID-19 Status**: <covid_status>
	+ Select your current health situation:
		- Tested positive for COVID-19
		- Symptoms consistent with COVID-19
		- Awaiting test results
		- Exposed to someone with COVID-19
		- Caring for someone with COVID-19
		- Other
* **Symptom Onset Date**: <symptom_onset_date>
	+ If applicable, enter when your symptoms started.
* **COVID-19 Test Date**: <test_date>
	+ If tested, enter the date you were tested.
* **Test Result**: <test_result>
	+ Select your test result:
		- Positive
		- Negative
		- Pending
		- Not tested
		- Prefer not to disclose
* **Healthcare Provider Contact**: <healthcare_provider_contact>
	+ Enter the name of your doctor or clinic treating you.
* **Expected Return to Work Date**: <return_to_work_date>
	+ Enter when you can return to work.
* **Paid Sick Leave Balance**: <paid_leave_available>
	+ Enter your approximate paid sick leave balance.
* **Can You Work From Home During This Period?**: <work_from_home_possible>
	+ Select whether you can work from home:
		- Yes
		- No
		- Partial/Limited
		- Not applicable
* **Coverage Plan During Absence**: <coverage_plan>
	+ Describe how your work responsibilities will be covered during your absence.
* **Essential Information for Coverage**: <essential_information>
	+ Provide important details for coverage staff.
* **Number of Household Members Quarantining**: <household_members_quarantine>
	+ If applicable, enter the number of household members also quarantining.
* **Experiencing Financial Hardship**: <financial_hardship>
	+ Select whether you are experiencing financial hardship:
		- No hardship
		- Mild hardship
		- Significant hardship
		- Prefer not to answer
* **Additional Support or Resources Needed?**: <additional_support_needed>
	+ Describe any additional support or resources you need.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: <emergency_contact>
	+ Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: <emergency_phone>
	+ Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact.

Note to user: This form is not a substitute for a discussion with your supervisor or HR representative. In case of any questions or concerns, please contact them directly for further assistance.
