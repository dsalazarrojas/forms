# COVID 19 Customer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their health status, safety protocols, and vaccination history to ensure a safe environment during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read this guide to understand each section and field.
2. Answer the questions accurately, based on your personal experience.
3. Ensure that you are aware and understand the risks associated with COVID-19 and take responsibility for your own health and safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (1, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Customer ID** (3, `text`, required): Provide your ID number or any unique identifier as per your institution's policy.
* **Phone Number** (4, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (5, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Date of Visit** (6, `date`, required): Provide the date you visited the facility or intend to visit.
* **Location Visited** (7, `text`, required): Describe the location you visited or intend to visit.
* **Completed Health Screening** (9, `select_one`, required): Check if you have completed the health screening.
* **Temperature Screening** (10, `select_one`, required): Select your temperature status.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms Present** (11, `select_one`, required): Report if you have any symptoms of COVID-19.
* **Describe Symptoms** (12, `text`, optional): Provide a description of your symptoms if you have any.
* **Contact with COVID-19 positive person** (13, `select_one`, required): Report if you have had contact with a COVID-19 positive person.
* **Vaccination Status** (15, `select_one`, required): Report your vaccination status.
* **Prior COVID-19 Diagnosis** (16, `select_one`, required): Report if you have been diagnosed with COVID-19 before.
* **I acknowledge the risks associated with COVID-19** (18, `select_one`, required): Check if you acknowledge the risks.
* **I understand the safety protocols in place** (19, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of safety protocols.
* **I accept responsibility for my own health and safety** (20, `select_one`, required): Take responsibility for your health and safety.
* **I consent to attend and receive services** (21, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent to visit and receive services.
* **All information provided is truthful and accurate** (22, `select_one`, required): Confirm the accuracy of your responses.
* **Customer Signature** (24, `text`, required): Sign your name, signifying your consent.
* **Date of Signature** (25, `date`, required): Provide the date of your signature.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete the form accurately and truthfully.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you need help or clarification, contact the relevant facility or service provider.
