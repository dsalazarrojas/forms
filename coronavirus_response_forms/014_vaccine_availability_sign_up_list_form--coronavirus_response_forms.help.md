# Vaccine Availability Sign Up List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccine Availability Sign Up List Form is designed to provide users with a way to sign up for vaccine availability information for a specific date and time. This form is intended for individuals who are interested in getting vaccinated or have access to vaccines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you are interested in getting vaccinated.
2. Choose the time you are interested in getting vaccinated.
3. Indicate if you have access to vaccines.
4. Enter your first name.
5. Enter your last name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`vaccine_availability_date`, date, required: false): Please select the date for which you are interested in getting vaccinated. You can choose any date in the past or future.
* **Time** (`vaccine_availability_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of day you are interested in getting vaccinated.
* **Vaccines** (`vaccine_availability_vaccines`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you have access to vaccines or "No" if you do not have access to vaccines. This field is optional, but it helps us understand your situation.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the date and time you select are correct to avoid duplicate entries.
* If you have access to vaccines, please make sure to indicate this correctly in the "Vaccines" field.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.
