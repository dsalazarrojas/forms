Beauty Salon Service Survey - Help Guide
=============================

### Purpose

This form is designed to help us collect feedback from our customers about their experience at our salon. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better care for our clients in the future.

To ensure that we get a comprehensive understanding of your experience, please answer each question carefully.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button after completing all questions.
2. Fill out each field as instructed below.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`): This is a text box where you can input your full name. You can remain anonymous if you choose, as this field is optional.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`): Please enter the date of your visit to our salon. This will help us track your experience over time.
* **Service Received** (`service_received`): Select the service you received during your visit from the options provided. For example, if you got a haircut, select "Haircut."
* **Stylist or Technician Name** (`stylist_name`): If you remember the name of your stylist or technician, please input it here. This will help us give credit to our team members for their hard work.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate how satisfied you were with your experience on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`): Rate the cleanliness of our salon on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **COVID-19 Safety Measures** (`covid_safety_measures`): Rate how well we followed COVID-19 safety protocols on a scale from Excellent to Poor, or select "Not Applicable" if not applicable.
* **Safety Protocols Observed** (`safety_protocols_observed`): Select all the safety measures you noticed during your visit from the list provided. This will help us ensure we follow protocols effectively.
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`): Rate how friendly and welcoming our staff were on a scale from Very Friendly to Very Unfriendly.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`): Rate the quality of service you received on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Wait Time** (`wait_time`): Rate how long you waited for your service on a scale from No Wait to More than 30 minutes.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`): Rate how well you felt the service was worth the price on a scale from Excellent Value to Very Poor Value.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_recommend`): Rate how likely you are to recommend us to friends on a scale from Very Likely to Very Unlikely.
* **Will You Return?** (`will_return`): Select how likely you are to visit us again on a scale from Definitely Yes to Definitely No.
* **What Did You Like Most?** (`favorite_aspect`): In this text box, please input what you enjoyed the most about your experience.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_improvement`): In this text box, please input areas where we can improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): If you have any other feedback, please input it here.
* **May We Contact You?** (`contact_permission`): Select "True" if you are okay with us following up with you about your feedback, or "False" if not.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory to complete the form.
