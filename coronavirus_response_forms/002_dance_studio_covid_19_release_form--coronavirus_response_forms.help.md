# Dance Studio COVID 19 Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dance Studio COVID 19 Release Form is a document used to collect information from students regarding their COVID-19 status, including declarations, release statements, and permissions. This form helps the studio ensure that students have the necessary permissions and declarations to participate in classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Covid 19 Declaration section with your current COVID-19 status.
2. Provide a Release Statement indicating that you understand and agree to the studio's policies and procedures.
3. Obtain Parent Permission from a parent or guardian if you are under 18 years old.
4. If applicable, indicate which Faculty are conducting the class(es) you will be participating in.
5. Select which Classes you will be participating in.
6. Indicate who submitted this form (Admin or Faculty).
7. Provide the Date and Time of form submission.
8. Select who submitted this form (Admin or Faculty).
9. If necessary, add a Note to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Covid 19 Declaration**
* **Covid 19 Declaration** (`covid_19_declaration`, text, required/optional): 
Please provide a statement declaring your current COVID-19 status.

### 2. **Release Statement**
* **Release Statement** (`release_statement`, text, required/optional): 
Please provide a statement indicating that you understand and agree to the studio's policies and procedures.

### 3. **Parent Permission**
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, text, required/optional): 
Please obtain permission from a parent or guardian if you are under 18 years old.

### 4. **Faculty Conducting**
* **Faculty Conducting** (`faculty_conducting`, select_one, required/optional): 
Select which faculty members are conducting the classes you will be participating in.

### 5. **Classes**
* **Classes** (`classes`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
Select which classes you will be participating in.

### 6. **Form Submitted By**
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, required/optional): 
Select who submitted this form (Admin or Faculty).

### 7. **Date**
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): 
Enter the date you submitted this form.

### 8. **Time**
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): 
Enter the time you submitted this form.

### 9. **Form Submitted At**
* **Form Submitted At** (`form_submitted_at`, date, required/optional): 
The date and time the form was submitted will be automatically populated.

### 10. **Submitted By**
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required/optional): 
Select who submitted this form (Admin or Faculty).

### 11. **Note**
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): 
Add any additional information you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out this form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the dance studio staff.
* This form is subject to change, so please check for updates regularly.
