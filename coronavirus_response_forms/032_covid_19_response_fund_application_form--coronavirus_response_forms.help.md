# COVID 19 Response Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations apply for the COVID-19 Response Fund. It will guide you through the application process, ensuring that you provide all the necessary information for a smooth and successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and documents required for the application before starting the form.
2. Fill in the details about your organization, including its name, legal status, and primary contact person.
3. Provide contact information for your primary contact, including their email address and phone number.
4. Specify the geographic area you serve and the primary funding category for your project.
5. Enter the amount of funding requested and provide a brief project summary.
6. Describe the expected outcomes of your project.
7. Confirm that the information provided is accurate and authorized by your organization.
8. Provide an electronic signature from an authorized representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applying Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Legal Status**: Select the category that best describes your organization (501(c)(3) Nonprofit, Government Agency, Community Group (Fiscal Sponsor required), or Other).
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Geographic Area Served**: List the counties, cities, or neighborhoods that your organization serves.
* **Primary Funding Category**: Select the category that best describes the focus of your project (Frontline Healthcare, Basic Needs (Food/Housing), Small Business Support, Education/Youth, or Mental Health).
* **Amount Requested**: Enter the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Brief Project Summary**: Describe how your project will address COVID-19 related needs, following the 250-word limit.
* **Expected Outcomes**: Explain what you hope to achieve with this funding.
* **I certify that the information provided is accurate and authorized by the organization**: Confirm that the information provided is true and authorized by your organization.
* **Authorized Representative Signature**: Provide an electronic signature from an authorized representative of your organization.
