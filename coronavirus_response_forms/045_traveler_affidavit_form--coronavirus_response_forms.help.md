# Traveler Affidavit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Traveler Affidavit Form is designed to collect information about travelers' trips, including their travel history, destination, and documentation requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each page carefully and fill out the form completely.
2.  If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact the agency or organization responsible for administering this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`): Select the country or region you traveled to, or are planning to travel to. You can choose either 'United States' or 'Other' as options.
* **Traveler Name** (`traveler_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`): Enter the location you traveled to or are planning to travel to.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`): Enter the date you traveled to or are planning to travel to.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`): Enter the duration of your trip in days.
* **Travel Origin** (`travel_origin`): Select where your trip originated from. You can choose 'Home', 'Work', 'School', or 'Other'.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`): Select where your trip is headed. You can choose 'Home', 'Work', 'School', or 'Other'.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`): Enter the reason for your trip.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`): Enter the date you plan to return.
* **Return Location** (`return_location`): Enter the location you will return to.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`): Enter any relevant travel history, such as previous trips or travel-related health issues.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`): Select the type of travel. You can choose 'Domestic', 'International', 'Business', or 'Pleasure'.
* **Travel Documentation** (`travel_documentation`): Select the type of travel documentation you have.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`): Enter the purpose of your trip.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`): Enter the travel agency you used, if applicable.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the contact person or organization.
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' as to whether you have any travel documentation required.
* **Travel Document Type** (`travel_document_type`): If you selected 'Yes', select the type of document you have.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`): Enter your contact address.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign your name to confirm your identity.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or information about your trip.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact the agency or organization responsible for administering this form.
* Review and submit the form carefully before submission.
