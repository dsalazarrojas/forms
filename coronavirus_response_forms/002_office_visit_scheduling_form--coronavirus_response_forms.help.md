# Office Visit Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for patients or visitors to schedule an office visit with your healthcare provider. Please ensure you are using this form for the intended purpose and not duplicating efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Name, which should match the name you will be using to visit the office.
2. Enter your Email address to help us contact you with confirmation and any necessary information.
3. Select a date for your office visit.
4. Choose a preferred time slot for your visit.
5. Specify the length of your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Your full name, as it should appear on any medical records.
* **Email**: Your contact email address, used for confirmation and follow-up.
* **Schedule Visit**: The date you would like to come in for your office visit.
* **Preferred Time**: The time of day you would prefer to visit the office. Please be aware that we will do our best to accommodate this request.
* **Visit Length**: The length of time you plan to spend at the office.

## Tips
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please include them in the "Reason for Visit" or "Special Requests" fields (not included in this form). This will help us better prepare for your appointment.
* If you would like to schedule a series of visits, please create a separate form for each appointment.
* Please enter dates in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` and times in the format `HH:MM`.
