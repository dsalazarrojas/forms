# Remote Work Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through how to complete the Remote Work Readiness Assessment form.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into several sections with multiple-choice questions. Please choose your answers based on your current situation.
2.  Select the option that best answers each question from the provided options.
3.  Please answer all questions, even if you're not sure or don't have a clear answer.
