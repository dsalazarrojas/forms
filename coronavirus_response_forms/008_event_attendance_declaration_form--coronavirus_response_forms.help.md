# Event Attendance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare attendance at events and gather information about the event and its attendees, specifically during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event planner or organizer.
2. Provide a brief description of the event, including any relevant details such as the event name, date, and location.
3. Select the date of the event.
4. Enter the location of the event.
5. Enter the time of the event.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not you will be attending the event.
7. Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
8. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Planner** (`event_planner`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or organization planning the event.
  • This is the person or organization responsible for the event, and should be the person you contact with any questions or concerns.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the event, including details such as the name, date, and location.
  • This field is used to provide context for your attendance, and should include any relevant information about the event.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the event.
  • This field is used to track when the event will take place, and should match the date of your attendance.
- **Location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
  • This field is used to track where the event will take place, and should match the location of your attendance.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
  • This field is used to track the start time of the event, and should match the time of your attendance.
- **Attendees** (`attendees`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate you are attending the event, or "No" if you are not.
  • This field is used to track who is attending the event, and should reflect your actual attendance.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
  • This field is used to gather your contact information, and should be a valid email address that we can use to reach you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
  • This field is used to gather your contact information, and should be a valid phone number that we can use to reach you.
- **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
  • This field is used to provide any additional information that may be relevant to your attendance.
