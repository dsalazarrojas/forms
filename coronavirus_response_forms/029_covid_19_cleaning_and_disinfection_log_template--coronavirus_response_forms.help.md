# COVID 19 Cleaning And Disinfection Log Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This template is designed to record and document the cleaning and disinfection process of surfaces and equipment in a facility to prevent the spread of COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Date of cleaning and disinfection: Enter the date when the cleaning and disinfection process was performed.
2.  Time of cleaning: Optional. Enter the time when the cleaning and disinfection process was performed.
3.  Location/Facility Name: Enter the name of the building or area being cleaned.
4.  Specific Area or Room: Describe the area or room that was cleaned, such as the reception area or a specific office.
5.  Staff Member Name: Enter the name of the staff member who performed the cleaning and disinfection process.
6.  Supervisor Name: Optional. Enter the name of the supervisor who oversaw the process.
7.  -- Personal Protective Equipment --: Optional. Describe the personal protective equipment used by the staff member, such as gloves and masks.
8.  Gloves Used: Select the type of gloves worn by the staff member during the cleaning and disinfection process.
9.  Mask Worn: Optional. Select the type of mask worn by the staff member during the cleaning and disinfection process.
10. Eye Protection Used: Optional. Select whether eye protection, such as goggles or a face shield, was used.
11. Protective Gown Worn: Optional. Select whether a protective gown was worn.
12. -- Disinfectants Used --: Optional. Describe the disinfectants used during the cleaning and disinfection process.
13. Disinfectant Product Used: Enter the brand name or type of disinfectant used.
14. Disinfectant Concentration: Optional. Enter the concentration of the disinfectant as per the manufacturer's instructions.
15. Contact Time: Optional. Describe how long the disinfectant was left on surfaces.
16. -- Surfaces Cleaned --: Optional. Describe the surfaces cleaned and disinfected during the process.
17. High-Touch Surfaces Disinfected: Select whether high-touch surfaces, such as door handles and switches, were disinfected.
18. Restrooms Cleaned and Disinfected: Select whether restrooms were cleaned and disinfected.
19. Floors Cleaned: Select whether floors were cleaned and disinfected.
20. Break Room Cleaned: Select whether the break room was cleaned and disinfected.
21. Equipment and Machinery Cleaned: Select whether equipment and machinery were cleaned and disinfected.
22. Furniture and Upholstered Items Cleaned: Select whether furniture and upholstered items were cleaned and disinfected.
23. HVAC and Ventilation Systems Inspected: Select whether the HVAC and ventilation systems were inspected.
24. Contaminated Waste Properly Disposed: Select whether biohazard containers were used for waste disposal.
25. Issues or Concerns Encountered: Describe any issues or concerns encountered during the cleaning and disinfection process.
26. Cleaning Completion Status: Select the status of the cleaning and disinfection process.
