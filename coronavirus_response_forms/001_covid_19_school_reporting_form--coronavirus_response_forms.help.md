# COVID 19 School Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for schools to report their COVID-19 related data, including vaccination status, cases, and operations. Please use this form to provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us monitor and respond to the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the reporting period (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly) for which you are submitting this report.
2. Enter the name of your school.
3. Enter the name of your school district.
4. Enter the number of total staff members at your school.
5. Enter the number of fully vaccinated staff members.
6. Enter the number of partially vaccinated staff members (optional).
7. Enter the number of fully vaccinated students.
8. Enter the number of partially vaccinated students (optional).
9. Report the number of confirmed positive cases among staff and students for the selected reporting period.
10. Select the current outbreak status (No active cases, Sporadic cases, Cluster outbreak, or School closure).
11. Report the number of students in quarantine.
12. Select the in-person class status (Full in-person, Hybrid learning, or Remote only).
13. Indicate whether any facilities (gym, cafeteria, library, etc.) are closed.
14. Enter your name and contact information for reporting purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (school_name): This is the name of your educational institution.
* **School District** (school_district): This is the name of your school district.
* **Reporting Period** (reporting_period): Select the timeframe for this report (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Report Date** (report_date): Enter the date you are submitting this report.
* **Staff Section**: This is a note field for any staff-related information.
* **Total Staff Members** (total_staff): Enter the total number of staff members at your school.
* **Staff Vaccinated** (staff_vaccinated): Enter the number of fully vaccinated staff members.
* **Staff Partially Vaccinated** (staff_partially_vaccinated): Enter the number of partially vaccinated staff members (optional).
* **Student Section**: This is a note field for any student-related information.
* **Total Students Enrolled** (total_students): Enter the total number of students enrolled at your school.
* **Students Vaccinated** (students_vaccinated): Enter the number of fully vaccinated students.
* **Students Partially Vaccinated** (students_partially_vaccinated): Enter the number of partially vaccinated students (optional).
* **Recent COVID Cases** (cases_section): This is a note field for any recent COVID cases information.
* **Confirmed Positive Cases This Week** (positive_cases_this_week): Enter the number of confirmed positive cases among staff and students for the selected reporting period.
* **Cases Among Staff** (staff_cases): Enter the number of confirmed positive cases among staff for the selected reporting period.
* **Cases Among Students** (student_cases): Enter the number of confirmed positive cases among students for the selected reporting period.
* **Current Outbreak Status** (current_outbreak_status): Select the current outbreak status (No active cases, Sporadic cases, Cluster outbreak, or School closure).
* **Students in Quarantine** (quarantine_count): Enter the number of students in quarantine.
* **School Operations** (facilities_section): This is a note field for any information about school operations.
* **In-Person Classes Status** (in_person_status): Select the in-person class status (Full in-person, Hybrid learning, or Remote only).
* **Any Facilities Closed** (facilities_closed): Indicate whether any facilities (gym, cafeteria, library, etc.) are closed.
* **Report Submitted By - Name** (contact_person_name): Enter your name for reporting purposes.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (contact_phone): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
