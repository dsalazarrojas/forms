## How To Complete This Form
Before starting to fill out the form, make sure you have access to the required information and have reviewed the form's requirements carefully. The form should only be submitted once per facility for a specific outbreak.

	* `Facility Name`: Enter the name of your facility.

	* `Facility Address`: Enter the facility's address.
	* `Facility Phone Number`: Enter the facility's phone number.
2. **Outbreak Details**
	* `Report Date`: Enter the date of the report.
	* `Number of Confirmed COVID-19 Cases`: Enter the number of confirmed COVID-19 cases in the facility.
	* `Number of Suspected Cases`: Enter the number of suspected COVID-19 cases in the facility (optional).
	* `Number of Recovered Cases`: Enter the number of recovered cases (optional).
	* `Number of Deaths`: Enter the number of deaths (optional).
3. **Affected Population**
	* `Number of Staff Affected`: Enter the number of staff members affected by the outbreak (optional).
	* `Number of Patients or Residents Affected`: Enter the number of patients or residents affected by the outbreak (optional).
	* `Number of Visitors Affected`: Enter the number of visitors affected by the outbreak (optional).
4. **Outbreak Investigation**
	* `Suspected Source of Outbreak`: Choose the suspected source of the outbreak (e.g., Community transmission, Staff member, etc.).
	* `Investigation Status`: Choose the current investigation status (e.g., Ongoing, Initial assessment, Complete, etc.).
5. **Response Measures**
	* `Measures Implemented`: Choose the response measures taken by the facility to address the outbreak (e.g., Isolation protocols, Enhanced cleaning, Testing program, etc.).
6. **Reporting Official**
	* `Official Name`: Enter the name of the official reporting the outbreak.
