# COVID 19 Case Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Case Investigation Form is used to collect information from individuals who have been diagnosed with COVID-19. This data is crucial for tracking and monitoring the spread of the virus, identifying high-risk areas and individuals, and aiding public health officials in making informed decisions to contain the outbreak.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please ensure that you have the necessary information about the patient. The form is divided into the following sections:

*   **Case ID Number**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to this case.
*   **Report Date**: Enter the date when the case was reported.
*   **Patient Name**: Enter the full legal name of the patient.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
*   **Age**: Enter the patient's age, if applicable.
*   **Gender**: Select the patient's gender.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
*   **Email Address**: Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
*   **Street Address**: Enter the patient's street address.
*   **City**: Enter the patient's city.
*   **State**: Enter the patient's state.
*   **Zip Code**: Enter the patient's zip code.
*   **Symptom Onset Date**: Enter the date when the patient first experienced symptoms.
*   **COVID-19 Test Date**: Enter the date when the patient was tested for COVID-19.
*   **Test Result**: Select the test result (Positive, Negative, Inconclusive, Pending, or Not tested).
*   **Test Type**: Select the type of test taken (Rapid Antigen, PCR, Antibody, Other, or Unknown).
*   **Symptoms Reported**: Select all symptoms the patient is experiencing (if any).
*   **Hospitalization Status**: Select the patient's hospitalization status (Not hospitalized, Hospitalized, ICU, Discharged, or Deceased).
*   **Hospitalization Date**: Enter the date of hospitalization (if applicable).
*   **Underlying Conditions**: If applicable, enter any pre-existing conditions that may have increased the risk of severe illness.
*   **Vaccination Status**: Select the patient's vaccination status (Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Unknown).
*   **Last Vaccination Date**: Enter the date of the last vaccination (if applicable).
*   **Number of Close Contacts**: Enter the number of people exposed to the patient.
*   **Likely Exposure Setting**: Select where the patient likely contracted COVID-19 (Household, Workplace, School, Healthcare facility, Community event, Travel, Unknown, or Other).
*   **Investigation Notes**: Add any additional details about the case that are not covered in the other fields.
*   **Investigator Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the investigation.
