# Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refund Request Form is designed to allow customers to request a refund for a purchase made from our store. This form will help us process your refund request efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Provide clear and concise information for each field.
3. Double-check your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your name and contact information so we can communicate with you about your refund request.
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text, optional): Describe the reason for your refund request. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your concern.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, optional): Indicate the amount you are requesting for a refund. Please ensure it matches the original purchase amount.
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your order that may help us process your refund request.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays.
* If you have any supporting documents (e.g., receipts, invoices), please attach them to your form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
