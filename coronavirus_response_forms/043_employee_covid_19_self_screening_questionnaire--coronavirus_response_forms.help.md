# Employee COVID 19 Self Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for you to report your health status and any potential COVID-19 symptoms, travel history, and other relevant information. This helps your organization track and manage employee health risks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, being as accurate as possible.
2. Please answer all questions marked as "required".
3. If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
4. Please submit your completed form to your supervisor or HR representative.
