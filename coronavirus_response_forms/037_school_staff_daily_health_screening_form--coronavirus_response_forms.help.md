# School Staff Daily Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your health status and potential exposure risks as a school staff member to ensure a safe and healthy working environment. Please note that this form should be completed daily and honestly, and you are required to report any symptoms or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your Staff ID number, which can be found on your staff ID card.
2. Select the date you are completing this form for.
3. Enter your current body temperature if it's above 37.5°C (99.7°F) or normal.
4. Check all the symptoms you're experiencing, if any (e.g., Fever, Cough, Difficulty breathing).
5. If you have traveled outside the prophets in the past 14 days, please enter the name of the location in the "Travelled outside prophets" field.
6. Indicate if you have been in contact with anyone who has COVID-19 or have had close contact with someone who has traveled outside the prophets in the past 14 days.
7. Select whether you are at work at school or outside of the workplace today.
8. Answer if you are a healthcare worker.
9. Indicate if you have been vaccinated against COVID-19.
10. Describe your current health status (Good, Not well, Hospitalized).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff ID** (Number, required): Enter your Staff ID number, which can be found on your staff ID card.
* **Date** (Date, required): Select the date you are completing this form for.
* **Temperature** (Number, required): Enter your current body temperature if it's above 37.5°C (99.7°F) or normal.
* **Symptoms** (Select Multiple, required): Check all the symptoms you're experiencing, if any (e.g., Fever, Cough, Difficulty breathing).
* **Travelled outside prophets** (Text, optional): If you have traveled outside the prophets in the past 14 days, enter the name of the location.
* **Contact Trace** (Select One, required): Indicate if you have been in contact with anyone who has COVID-19 or have had close contact with someone who has traveled outside the prophets in the past 14 days.
* **School or Outside Workplace** (Select One, required): Select whether you are at work at school or outside of the workplace today.
* **Healthcare Worker** (Select One, required): Answer if you are a healthcare worker.
* **Vaccination Status** (Select One, required): Indicate if you have been vaccinated against COVID-19.
* **Health Status** (Select One, required): Describe your current health status (Good, Not well, Hospitalized).

## Tips

* Please fill out this form honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to this help guide for assistance.
* If you have any concerns or questions, reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
* If you have completed this form for today, you do not need to fill it out again.
