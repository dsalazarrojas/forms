# Lockdown Special Assistance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who require special assistance during the lockdown. The form is intended to be completed by the individual in need of assistance, providing essential details about themselves and their emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with your personal information and emergency contact details.
2. Answer the service requests questions to indicate if you require any assistance with services.
3. If necessary, provide medical information and emergency contact information.
4. Specify how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
5. Provide any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Individual Info** (`individual_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as phone number and email address.
- **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide details of your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and email address.
- **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required: false): If you have any medical conditions that need to be known, please provide details here.
- **Household Info** (`household_info`, text, required: false): If you need assistance for yourself and your household, please provide a description of your household composition.
- **Service Info** (`service_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the services you are requesting.
- **Services Requested** (`services_requests`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate which services you are requesting. Please select all that apply.
  - Yes: You are requesting assistance with this service.
  - No: You are not requesting this service.
- **Emergency Services Needed** (`emergency_services_needed`, select_one, required: true): Indicate if emergency services are required.
  - Yes: Emergency services are required.
  - No: Emergency services are not required.
- **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, note, required: false): If you have any other information that is not included in the above fields, please provide it here.
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Specify how you would like to be contacted. Please select one of the following options:
  - Yes: We will contact you through this method.
  - No: We will not contact you through this method.
- **Phone** (`phone`, number, required: false): If you have a phone number that you can be contacted on.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you have an email address that you can be contacted on.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide your address for delivery or visitation purposes.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_address`, text, required: false): Provide the address of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, number, required: false): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required: false): Provide the email address of your emergency contact.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): If you have any medical conditions, please provide details here.
- **Emergency Contact's Medical Condition** (`emergency_medical_condition`, text, required: false): If you have any medical conditions that need to be known about your emergency contact.
- **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure that your needs are understood correctly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
