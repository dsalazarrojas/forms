**COVID 19 Exposure Agreement Form - Help Guide**
=============================================

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a patient's exposure to COVID-19. It will help healthcare providers assess the risk of transmission and determine the necessary precautions to take to ensure the patient's safety and the safety of others. Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly to help us provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the required fields with your personal information, including your name, date of birth, medical record number, phone number, and email address.
3. Provide details about your exposure to COVID-19, including the date of exposure, type of exposure, and whether you were in contact with a confirmed case.
4. Indicate whether you understand the risks of COVID-19 exposure and the measures you took to protect yourself.
5. Agree to participate in testing, quarantine, and symptom monitoring if required.
6. Sign the liability release form, confirming that you release the healthcare facility from liability.
