# COVID 19 Precaution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Precaution Form is designed to ensure that all visitors and staff follow proper precautions to prevent the spread of COVID-19 while on site. This form collects essential information about the purpose of entry, individual's health, and other critical safety measures for a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your Full Name, which is required for our records.
2.  Select the Purpose of Entry from the list provided, depending on your visit's nature.
3.  Record your temperature measured by building security or staff, in Fahrenheit.
4.  Indicate whether you are wearing a mask properly, covering your nose and mouth.
5.  Confirm that you have applied hand sanitizer at the entry station.
6.  Agree to maintain a distance of at least 6 feet from others during your visit.
7.  Confirm that you will notify management if you develop symptoms of COVID-19 within 48 hours of your visit.
8.  Enter the name of the staff member who has authorized your entry.
9.  Record the Time of Entry, which is required for our records.
10.  (Optional) When leaving, record the Time of Exit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`visitor_staff_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Purpose of Entry** (`entry_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of your visit from the options provided: Employee - Work Shift, Visitor - Scheduled Meeting, Contractor - Maintenance, or Delivery/Service.
* **Recorded Temperature (F)** (`temp_check`, number, required): Enter the temperature measured in Fahrenheit by building security or staff.
* **Mask is being worn properly upon entry** (`mask_worn`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are wearing a mask that covers your nose and mouth.
* **Hand sanitizer was applied at the entry station** (`hand_sanitized`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have applied hand sanitizer as per health and safety protocols.
* **I agree to maintain a distance of at least 6 feet from others** (`distance_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to follow social distancing guidelines during your visit.
* **I agree to notify management if I develop symptoms within 48 hours of visit** (`notification_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will contact management if you experience any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Entry Authorized By (Staff Name)** (`authorized_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member who has authorized your entry.
* **Time of Entry** (`entry_time`, time, required): Record the time of your entry into the building.
* **Time of Exit** (`exit_time`, time, optional): Record the time of your exit, if applicable.

Please ensure you complete this form accurately and honestly, as it's essential for maintaining a safe and healthy environment for everyone. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our management team.
