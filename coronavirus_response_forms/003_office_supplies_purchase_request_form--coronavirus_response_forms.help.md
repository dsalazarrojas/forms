# office_supplies_purchase_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting office supplies, allowing users to specify the department, category, quantity, and other details of the requested items. The goal of this form is to gather information for the procurement team to process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the department for which the office supplies are being requested.
2. Choose the category of the requested items (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
3. Specify the quantity of the item you need.
4. Select the items you wish to request (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
5. Enter the date you would like to receive the office supplies.
6. Choose the time of day you would like to receive the supplies (if applicable).
7. Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates on your request.
8. Provide your phone number so that we can reach you if needed.
9. Enter your office address for delivery purposes.
10. Enter any additional message or comments about your request.
11. Choose an additional category for the requested items (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Supplies (1)** (`Office Supplies`, note, optional): This field is for selecting the department for which the office supplies are being requested. Simply select the department that corresponds to the office or section you are affiliated with.
* **Department (2)** (`Department`, text, optional): This field is for specifying the department. Enter the name of the department that aligns with the office supplies you are requesting.
* **Category (3)** (`Category`, select_one, optional): This field is for choosing the category of the requested items (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'). Select the category that best represents the items you need.
* **Quantity (4)** (`Quantity`, number, optional): This field is for specifying the quantity of the item you need. Enter the number of items you would like to receive.
* **Item (5)** (`Item`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting the items you wish to request (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'). Choose the items that you would like to include with your request.
* **Date (6)** (`Date`, date, optional): This field is for specifying the date you would like to receive the office supplies. Enter the date you would like the supplies to arrive on.
* **Time (7)** (`Time`, time, optional): This field is for choosing the time of day you would like to receive the supplies (if applicable). Select the time that suits your schedule best.
* **Email (8)** (`Email`, email, optional): This field is for entering your email address so that we can contact you with updates on your request.
* **Phone (9)** (`Phone`, text, optional): This field is for entering your phone number so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Address (10)** (`Address`, text, optional): This field is for entering your office address for delivery purposes. Enter the address where you would like the office supplies delivered.
* **Message (11)** (`Message`, text, optional): This field is for entering any additional comments or messages about your request. Provide any information that might be helpful for the procurement team to process your request efficiently.
* **Category 2 (12)** (`Category 2`, select_one, optional): This field is for choosing an additional category for the requested items (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'). Select the category that best represents the items you need.
