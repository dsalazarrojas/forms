# Order Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who wish to cancel their orders. Please use this form to provide a clear and respectful experience for your customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields with your order details and reason for cancellation.
2. If you have any additional comments or information, you may include it in the free text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text): Explain the reason why you are requesting to cancel your order. This will help us understand your situation and provide a more personalized resolution.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text): Enter the order number associated with your order. This will help us locate your order in our system.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, text): If applicable, please provide the total amount of the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter your name as it appears on your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address associated with your order. This will help us contact you with any further instructions or updates.
* **Additional Comments** (Note, not explicitly shown in form): If you have any additional information or comments about your order, please include it here.
