# Coronavirus Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you, as an individual, self-identify as a potential COVID-19 case to report your status to public health agencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the title of the form (required).
2. Select a category (required).
3. Provide a brief description of your situation (optional).
4. Check if you are HIPAA compliant (optional).
5. Indicate if you are an employee who can fill out this form (optional).
6. Check if this form requires no coding knowledge (optional).
7. (Optional) Enter any additional information as a hint for the form administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (form_title)**: A brief title of your form submission, which will be displayed to the public health agency. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **Category (form_category)**: Please select one of the provided categories to help the public health agency better understand your situation. This will help them allocate resources accordingly.
* **Description (form_description)**: A brief description of your situation can be helpful for the public health agency to better assist you. This is optional and can be left blank.
* **HIPAA Compliance (form_hipaa_compliance)**: Check this box if you are HIPAA compliant. This will help the public health agency understand your level of confidentiality.
* **Can Employees Complete (form_employees_can_complete)**: Indicate if you are an employee and can fill out this form. This will help the public health agency prioritize their response.
* **No Coding Required (form_no_coding_required)**: Check this box if you do not require any special coding skills for this form submission.
* **Hint 1 (form_hint_1)**: (Optional) Enter any additional information that you think would be helpful for the form administrator.
* **Hint 2 (form_hint_3)**: (Optional) Enter any additional information that you think would be helpful for the form administrator.
* **Hint 3 (form_hint_2)**: (Optional) Enter any additional information that you think would be helpful for the form administrator.

## Tips
Please fill out this form accurately and honestly. The more information you provide, the better the public health agency can assist you.
