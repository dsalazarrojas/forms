# COVID 19 Vaccine Notification Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in receiving COVID-19 vaccine notifications. It is intended to ensure that individuals receive accurate and relevant information about vaccine availability and related health updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Provide your preferred contact method(s) in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
6. Select your preferred language in the "Preferred Language" field.
7. Choose the type of notifications you want to receive in the "Type of Notifications You Want" field.
8. Select the frequency at which you would like to receive notifications in the "Notification Frequency" field.
9. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
10. Select your age group in the "Age Group" field.
11. Enter your city and state in the "City" and "State" fields.
12. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
13. Check your eligibility phase in the "Eligible Vaccination Phases" field.
14. Indicate whether you are a healthcare worker, essential worker, or have a high-risk condition in the "Are you a Healthcare Worker", "Are you an Essential Worker", and "Do you have a High-risk Health Condition" fields.
15. Select your vaccine type preference in the "Vaccine Type Preference" field.
16. Check the doses you are interested in receiving in the "Doses You Are Interested In" field.
17. Indicate whether you have been vaccinated for COVID-19 in the "Have You Been Vaccinated for COVID-19" field.
18. Agree to receive vaccine notifications and consent to the sharing of your information with health authorities in the "I Consent to Receive Vaccine Notifications" and "I understand that information may be shared with health authorities" fields.
19. Understand that you can unsubscribe from notifications at any time in the "I understand I can unsubscribe anytime" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This is a required field.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you would like to receive vaccine notifications. This is a required field.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for vaccine updates. This is a required field.
* **Alternate Phone Number**: This is an optional field for providing an alternate phone number where you can be reached.
* **Communication Preferences**: Select the contact methods you prefer, such as email, phone call, text message, or postal mail. This is a required field.
* **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language for receiving notifications. This can be English, Spanish, Mandarin, Vietnamese, or Somali. This is a required field.
* **Type of Notifications You Want**: Check the types of notifications you would like to receive, such as vaccine availability, appointment scheduling, and general health updates. This is a required field.
* **Notification Frequency**: Select how often you would like to receive notifications, such as daily, weekly, or as needed. This is a required field.
* **-- Personal Information --**: This is a note section where you can provide additional information about yourself.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group, which can be under 18, 18-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60-69, 70-79, or 80+. This is a required field.
* **City**: Enter your city where you reside. This is a required field.
* **State**: Enter your state where you reside. This is a required field.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code where you reside. This is a required field.
* **-- Eligibility Information --**: This is a note section where you can provide additional information about your eligibility phase.
* **Eligible Vaccination Phases**: Check the phases you are eligible for, such as Phase 1A, Phase 1B, and Phase 2. This is a required field.
* **Are you a Healthcare Worker**: Indicate whether you are a healthcare worker. This is a required field.
* **Are you an Essential Worker**: Indicate whether you are an essential worker. This is a required field.
* **Do you have a High-risk Health Condition**: Indicate whether you have a high-risk health condition. This is a required field.
* **-- Vaccine Preferences --**: This is a note section where you can provide additional information about your vaccine preferences.
* **Vaccine Type Preference**: Select your vaccine type preference, which can be Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, or no preference. This is a required field.
* **Doses You Are Interested In**: Check the doses you are interested in receiving, which can be first dose, second dose, booster dose, or all doses. This is a required field.
* **Have You Been Vaccinated for COVID-19**: Indicate whether you have been vaccinated for COVID-19. This is a required field.
* **-- Privacy and Consent --**: This is a note section where you can provide additional information about your privacy and consent.
* **I Consent to Receive Vaccine Notifications**: Agree to receive vaccine notifications. This is a required field.
* **I understand that information may be shared with health authorities**: Understand that your information may be shared with health authorities. This is a required field.
* **I understand I can unsubscribe anytime**: Understand that you can unsubscribe from notifications at any time. This is a required field.
