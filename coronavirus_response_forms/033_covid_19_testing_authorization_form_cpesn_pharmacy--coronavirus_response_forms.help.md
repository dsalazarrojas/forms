# COVID 19 Testing Authorization Form CPESN Pharmacy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to provide information and consent for COVID-19 testing at CPESN pharmacies. Please complete the form carefully to ensure accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for demographic and contact details.
2. Select the preferred pharmacy location for testing.
3. Choose a preferred appointment date and time.
4. Select a reason for testing.
5. Answer questions about current symptoms, vaccination status, and testing consent.
6. Provide signature and witness information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Authorization Date**: Enter the date of authorization for testing.
  (`authorization_date`, date, required)
* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
  (`patient_first_name`, text, required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
  (`patient_last_name`, text, required)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
  (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
* **Gender**: Select your gender.
  (`gender`, select_one, required)
* **Race**: Select your race for demographic reporting.
  (`race`, select_one, not required)
* **Ethnicity**: Select your ethnicity for demographic reporting.
  (`ethnicity`, select_one, not required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
  (`phone_number`, text, required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
  (`email_address`, email, required)
* **Home Address**: Enter your street address.
  (`address`, text, required)
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
  (`city`, text, required)
* **State**: Enter your state of residence.
  (`state`, text, required)
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
  (`zip_code`, text, required)
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the insurance company (if applicable).
  (`insurance_provider`, text, not required)
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your insurance member ID (if applicable).
  (`insurance_policy_number`, text, not required)
* **Group Number**: Enter your insurance group ID (if applicable).
  (`insurance_group_number`, text, not required)
* **Pharmacy Location**: Select the preferred pharmacy location for testing.
  (`pharmacy_location`, select_one, required)
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Enter your preferred appointment date for testing.
  (`preferred_appointment_date`, date, required)
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: Enter your preferred appointment time for testing.
  (`preferred_appointment_time`, time, not required)
* **Reason for Testing**: Select the reason for testing.
  (`reason_for_testing`, select_one, required)
* **Current Symptoms**: Answer if you are currently experiencing symptoms.
  (`current_symptoms`, select_one, required)
* **Vaccination Status**: Select your COVID-19 vaccination status.
  (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required)
* **Testing Consent**: Select your consent for COVID-19 testing.
  (`testing_consent`, select_one, required)
* **Results Delivery Consent**: Select your consent for receiving test results via phone, email, or text.
  (`results_delivery_consent`, select_one, required)
* **Data Sharing Consent**: Select your consent for sharing test results with public health authorities.
  (`data_sharing_consent`, select_one, required)
* **HIPAA Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge receipt of the HIPAA privacy notice.
  (`hipaa_acknowledgment`, select_one, required)
* **Financial Responsibility**: Select your understanding of financial responsibility for testing costs not covered by insurance.
  (`financial_responsibility`, select_one, required)
* **Terms and Conditions**: Select your agreement to the terms and conditions for COVID-19 testing.
  (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required)
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signature.
  (`patient_signature_date`, date, required)
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness (if applicable).
  (`witness_name`, text, not required)
* **Witness Signature Date**: Enter the date of the witness signature (if applicable).
  (`witness_signature_date`, date, not required)

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and carefully to ensure efficient processing.
* Ensure that you have provided all required information and agreements before submitting the form.
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting to avoid errors or omissions.
* If you are unsure about any information or questions, please consult our staff or contact us for assistance.
