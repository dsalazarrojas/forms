# LCHD Case Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's contact with the Local Health Department (LCHD) and their health status regarding COVID-19. Please complete this form to provide detailed information about the case.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, such as your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your address, city, and state to ensure we can contact you with further instructions or updates.
3. If you have a medical record number, please provide it to help us access your medical history.
4. Answer the questions regarding your symptoms, travel history, and any close contacts you have had.
5. If you have been tested for COVID-19, please indicate the result.
6. Report any hospitalization, ICU, or ventilation you have experienced.
7. Finally, provide any additional information about your health status, such as the outcome of your case and any recent death.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your active email address where you can be reached for further instructions or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current address for contact tracing purposes.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required: false): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, `date`, required: false): Report any recent travel history.
* **Close Contacts** (`close_contacts`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List any close contacts you have had recently.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, required: false): If you have a healthcare provider, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you have been tested for COVID-19.
* **Hospitalization** (`hospitalization`, `select_one`, required: false): Report if you have been hospitalized due to COVID-19.
* **ICU** (`icu`, `select_one`, required: false): Report if you have been in the Intensive Care Unit due to COVID-19.
* **Ventilated** (`ventilated`, `select_one`, required: false): Report if you have been ventilated due to COVID-19.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, `select_one`, required: false): Report the outcome of your case.
* **Death** (`death`, `select_one`, required: false): Report if you have passed away due to COVID-19.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, `date`, required: false): If you have passed away, report the date of death.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your gender.
* **Race** (`race`, `text`, required: false): Enter your race.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, required: false): If you have a medical record number, please provide it.

**Tips**

* Please answer each question accurately and honestly to help us assess your case.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact us for further instructions.
* If you have any additional information to provide, please include it in the comments section.
* Thank you for your cooperation and understanding in this process.
