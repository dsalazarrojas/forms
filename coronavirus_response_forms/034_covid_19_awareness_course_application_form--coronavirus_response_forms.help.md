# COVID 19 Awareness Course Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Awareness Course Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in enrolling in the COVID-19 awareness course. The form collects personal and professional information, as well as course preferences and requirements, to ensure that the right course format is selected for each applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your preferences and requirements.
3. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
4. Ensure that you have accepted the terms and conditions.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for course communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Enter your profession or job title.
* **Employer** (`employer`, `text`, optional): Enter your company or organization (if applicable).
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry sector that applies to you:
	+ Healthcare
	+ Education
	+ Retail
	+ Food Service
	+ Hospitality
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Office Administration
	+ Public Service
	+ Student
	+ Other
* **Reason for Attending** (`reason_for_attending`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason why you want to take this course:
	+ Employer requirement
	+ Personal knowledge
	+ Community volunteer
	+ Career development
	+ Compliance requirement
	+ Other
* **Course Format Preference** (`course_format_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred course format:
	+ Online - Self-paced
	+ Online - Live virtual
	+ In-person
	+ Hybrid
* **Preferred Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to begin the course.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days and times you are available to attend:
	+ Weekday mornings
	+ Weekday afternoons
	+ Weekday evenings
	+ Weekend mornings
	+ Weekend afternoons
	+ Flexible schedule
* **Prior COVID-19 Knowledge** (`prior_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Select your current level of COVID-19 knowledge:
	+ None - Beginner
	+ Basic awareness
	+ Some training
	+ Advanced knowledge
* **Healthcare Background** (`healthcare_background`, `select_one`, required): Select your medical background:
	+ Yes - Healthcare professional
	+ Yes - Healthcare student
	+ No - No medical background
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the topics you want to learn about:
	+ Virus transmission
	+ Prevention measures
	+ Vaccine information
	+ Safety protocols
	+ Contact tracing
	+ Community health
	+ Workplace safety
	+ Mental health
* **Certification Needed** (`certification_needed`, `select_one`, required): Select if you need a certificate:
	+ Yes - For employer
	+ Yes - For records
	+ No - Knowledge only
* **Employer Sponsorship** (`employer_sponsorship`, `select_one`, required): Select if your employer is sponsoring your enrollment:
	+ Yes - Full sponsorship
	+ Yes - Partial sponsorship
	+ No - Self-funded
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
	+ Invoice to Employer
	+ Purchase Order
	+ Other
* **Group Registration** (`group_registration`, `select_one`, required): Select if you are registering with a group:
	+ No - Individual
	+ Yes - 2-5 people
	+ Yes - 6-10 people
	+ Yes - More than 10
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people registering with you (if applicable).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): Enter any accommodations you may need to access the course.
* **Language Preference** (`language_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the language you prefer for course materials:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Other
* **How Did You Hear About Course** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about the course:
	+ Employer
	+ Social media
	+ Website
	+ Friend or colleague
	+ Search engine
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any questions or notes you have about the course.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Select to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to confirm your application.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed the form.

Please review and follow these guidelines carefully to ensure a smooth and accurate application process.
