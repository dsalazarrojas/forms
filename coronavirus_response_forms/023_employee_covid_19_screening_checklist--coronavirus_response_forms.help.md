# Employee Covid 19 Screening Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report COVID-19 symptoms or potential exposure to COVID-19 among employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly. Select "Yes" or "No" for each symptom or condition as it applies to your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning Screening** (`morning_screening`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  This question asks about your health status in the morning, which helps to identify potential cases of COVID-19 among employees.
* **Have Fever** (`have_fever`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "Fever" if you have a temperature above normal, or "No Fever" if you do not have a fever.
* **Have Cough** (`have_cough`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have a cough, or "False" if you do not have a cough.
* **Have Shortness Of Breath** (`have_shortness_of_breath`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have difficulty breathing or feeling short of breath, or "False" if you do not.
* **Have Diarrhea or Vomiting** (`have_diarrhea_or_vomiting`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have experienced diarrhea or vomiting, or "False" if you do not.
* **Have Body Ache** (`have_body_ache`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have a body ache, or "False" if you do not.
* **Have Runny Eyes** (`have_runny_eyes`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have runny eyes, or "False" if you do not.
* **Have Sore Throat** (`have_sore_throat`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have a sore throat, or "False" if you do not.
* **Have Nasal Congestion** (`have_nasal_congestion`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have nasal congestion, or "False" if you do not.
* **Have Headache** (`have_headache`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have a headache, or "False" if you do not.
* **Have General Feeling Sick** (`have_general_feeling_sick`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have a general feeling of illness, or "False" if you do not.
* **Should Stay Home** (`should_stay_home`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if the organization recommends that you stay home, or "False" if they do not.
* **Recommended to Wear Mask** (`recommended_mask`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you are recommended to wear a mask, or "False" if you are not.
* **Have Underlying Medical Condition** (`have_underlying_med_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have an underlying medical condition, or "False" if you do not.
* **Age Over 65** (`age_over_65`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you are over 65 years old, or "False" if you are not.
* **Have Immunocompromised** (`have_immunocompromised`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select "True" if you have a weakened immune system, or "False" if you do not.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, this information is used to track and monitor potential cases of COVID-19 among employees.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* Please note that this form is not a medical diagnosis tool, and any decisions regarding your health should be discussed with a medical professional.
