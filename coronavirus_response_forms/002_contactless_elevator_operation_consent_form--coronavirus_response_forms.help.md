# Contactless Elevator Operation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from residents to help manage contactless elevator operation. It is essential for residents to understand the risks associated with COVID-19 transmission and provide their consent for this new mode of elevator operation.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Provide your unit number as it appears on your building's directory or assignment document.
3. Enter the name of your building to ensure we have accurate information on record.
4. Enter your email address to receive notifications and confirmations.
5. Provide your primary contact phone number for emergency situations.
6. Review the consent statement and select 'Yes, I Consent' to confirm your agreement to contactless elevator operation.
7. Indicate whether you understand the potential health risks associated with contactless elevator operation.
8. If you require alternative access due to a disability or medical condition, select the relevant option.
9. Specify the number of household members living in your unit.
10. Estimate your daily elevator usage frequency.
11. Provide the full name and phone number of an emergency contact person (if applicable).
12. Specify the date of your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Full Name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Unit Number** (id: 2, text, required): Enter the number assigned to your unit by the building management.
* **Building Name** (id: 3, text, required): Enter the name of your building for accurate record-keeping.
* **Email Address** (id: 4, email, required): Provide your email address for confirmation and record purposes.
* **Phone Number** (id: 5, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number for emergency situations.
* **Consent Statement** (id: 6, note, required): Read and agree to the use of contactless elevator controls.
* **I Agree to Contactless Operation** (id: 7, select_one, required): Select 'Yes, I Consent' to confirm your agreement.
* **Understanding of Health Risks** (id: 8, select_one, required): Select 'Yes, I Understand' to acknowledge the potential health risks.
* **Alternative Access Needed** (id: 9, select_one, required): Indicate whether you require alternative access due to a disability or medical condition.
* **Number of Household Members** (id: 10, number, required): Specify the number of people living in your unit.
* **Estimated Daily Elevator Use** (id: 11, select_one, optional): Estimate how many times you use the elevator daily.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 12, text, optional): Provide the full name of your emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Signature Date** (id: 14, date, required): Specify the date of your consent.
* **Additional Accommodations Requested** (id: 15, text, optional): Describe any additional accommodations you may require.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure that we can provide the best possible service to you.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your building management.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
