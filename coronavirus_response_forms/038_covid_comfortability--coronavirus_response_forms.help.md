# COVID Comfortability - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees express their comfort levels with various aspects of their work environment, such as on-site work, commuting, in-person meetings, and management support, related to the COVID-19 pandemic. The gathered data will inform management decisions and help create a safe and comfortable work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Employee Information**: Please provide your employee name, employee ID, and department or team.
2. **On-site Work Comfort**:
	* Answer how comfortable you are working on-site, using the scale: Very comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, or Very uncomfortable.
	* Answer whether you feel safe in the office, using the scale: Very safe, Safe, Somewhat safe, Unsafe, or Very unsafe.
3. **Commuting**:
	* Answer how comfortable you are using public transit for your commute, using the scale: Very comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, Very uncomfortable, or Not applicable.
	* Choose your primary method of commuting, using the options: Personal vehicle, Public transit, Walk or bike, Carpool, Remote - no commute, or Other.
4. **In-Person Meetings**:
	* Answer how comfortable you are with small in-person meetings (groups of under 10 people), using the scale: Very comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, or Very uncomfortable.
	* Answer how comfortable you are with large in-person meetings (groups of 20 or more people), using the scale: Very comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, or Very uncomfortable.
5. **Work Arrangement Preferences**:
	* Choose your preferred work arrangement, using the options: Full on-site, Hybrid - 3-4 days on-site, Hybrid - 1-2 days on-site, Full remote, or Flexible.
6. **Workstation Spacing**:
	* Answer whether you have adequate workstation spacing, using the scale: Yes, very adequate, Adequate, Somewhat adequate, Inadequate, or Very inadequate.
7. **Management Support**:
	* Answer how supported you feel by management, using the scale: Very supported, Supported, Neutral, Not supported, or Very unsupported.
8. **COVID-19 Communication Clarity**:
	* Answer how clear you think company communication about COVID-19 has been, using the scale: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, or Very unclear.
9. **Suggested Improvements**:
	* Provide any suggestions for improvements to create a more comfortable work environment.
10. **Additional Comments or Concerns**:
	* Enter any other feedback or concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Your full name, as per your employee ID badge.
* **Employee ID**: Your employee number.
* **Department or Team**: Your department or team name.
* **Survey Date**: Date of this survey.
* **How Comfortable Are You Working On-Site**: Answer how comfortable you are working on-site.
* **Do You Feel Safe in the Office**: Answer whether you feel safe in the office.
* **Are Current Safety Measures Adequate**: Answer whether you think current safety measures are adequate.
* **How Comfortable Are You Using Public Transit**: Answer how comfortable you are using public transit for your commute.
* **Your Primary Commute Method**: Choose how you get to work.
* **Comfort with Small In-Person Meetings**: Answer how comfortable you are with small in-person meetings.
* **Comfort with Large In-Person Meetings**: Answer how comfortable you are with large in-person meetings.
* **Preferred Work Arrangement**: Choose your preferred work arrangement.
* **Do You Have Adequate Workstation Spacing**: Answer whether you have adequate workstation spacing.
* **Do You Feel Supported by Management**: Answer how supported you feel by management.
* **Is COVID-19 Communication Clear**: Answer how clear you think company communication about COVID-19 has been.
* **What Would Improve Your Comfort at Work**: Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns**: Enter any other feedback or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact your HR representative or manager.
* The information collected will be used to inform management decisions and create a safe and comfortable work environment.
