# COVID 19 Vaccine Interest Form For Individuals - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals about their interest in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine, including their demographic details, health status, and concerns. The form is used to gauge the demand for the vaccine and to identify potential barriers to vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has multiple sections with various fields to collect your information. Please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **City** to identify yourself.
2. Choose your preferred method of contact, either **Email**, **Phone**, **Text message**, or **Mail**.
3. Indicate your interest in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine, either **Yes, definitely**, **Yes, probably**, **Unsure**, or **Definitely not**.
4. Select a timeline for vaccination, such as **As soon as possible**, **Within 1 week**, **Within 2 weeks**, or **Within 3 months**, or **Flexible**.
5. Choose your preferred vaccine type, such as **Pfizer**, **Moderna**, **Johnson and Johnson**, or **No preference**.
6. Indicate the number of doses you are interested in, such as **First dose**, **Second dose**, or **Booster dose**.
7. Select your **Age Group**, which is categorized into **18-29**, **30-39**, **40-49**, **50-59**, **60-69**, **70-79**, or **80+**.
8. Choose your **Gender**, which includes **Male**, **Female**, **Non-binary**, or **Prefer to specify**.
9. Enter your **Zip Code** to specify your location.
10. Indicate if you have any **Health Risk Status**, either **True** or **False**.
11. List any **Risk Factors** you have, such as chronic conditions.
12. Select if you have had **Chronic Conditions**, either **True**, **False**, or **Prefer not to answer**.
13. List any **Chronic Conditions** you have, if applicable.
14. Indicate if you have had **COVID-19 before**, either **True**, **False**, or **Unsure**.
15. Select if you have received the **COVID-19 vaccine before**, either **True** or **False**.
16. If yes, specify the **Vaccine Type and Date** you received it.
17. Select if you have any **Main concerns about vaccination**, such as **Safety**, **Side effects**, or **Effectiveness**.
18. Describe your **Main concerns** in detail.
19. Specify what information would help you decide about the vaccine, if applicable.
20. Indicate if you consent to be contacted about vaccine availability, either **True** or **False**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, as per your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `yyyy-mm-dd` (e.g., 1999-12-31).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to contact you about vaccine availability.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to contact you about vaccine availability.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your preferred phone number to contact you about vaccine availability.
* **Contact Preference**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, either **Email**, **Phone**, **Text message**, or **Mail**.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact.
* **Are you interested in receiving COVID-19 vaccine**: Indicate your interest in the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Vaccine Type Preference**: Choose your preferred vaccine type.
* **Which Dose Are You Interested In**: Indicate the number of doses you are interested in receiving.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group, which is categorized into different age ranges.
* **Gender**: Choose your gender, which will be kept confidential.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live to specify your location.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live to specify your location.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code to specify your location.
* **Do you consider yourself at higher risk**: Indicate if you have any health conditions or risk factors that make you more susceptible to serious disease.
* **What are your risk factors**: List any risk factors you have, such as chronic conditions.
* **Have chronic health conditions**: Indicate if you have any chronic health conditions.
* **List any chronic conditions**: Enter any chronic conditions you have, if applicable.
* **Have you had COVID-19 before**: Indicate if you have had COVID-19 before.
* **Have you received COVID-19 vaccine before**: Indicate if you have received the COVID-19 vaccine before.
* **If yes, which vaccine and when**: Specify the vaccine type and date you received it.
* **Main concerns about vaccination**: Select any main concerns you have about vaccination.
* **Describe your concerns**: Enter your concerns in detail.
* **What information would help you decide**: Specify what information would help you decide about the vaccine.
* **I consent to be contacted about vaccine availability**: Indicate if you consent to be contacted about vaccine availability.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to help us better understand your needs and concerns.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* You can also submit any additional information or questions in the **Additional Comments** field, if applicable.
* Once you submit the form, we will contact you about vaccine availability and other relevant information.
