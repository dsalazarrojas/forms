# COVID 19 Antibody Screening Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for patients who have been referred to a testing location and are required to provide consent for a COVID-19 antibody test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter your ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19?" and select the date of diagnosis if applicable.
7. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "Do you currently have COVID-19 symptoms?".
8. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19?".
9. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "I understand the purpose of this antibody test".
10. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "The testing process has been explained to me".
11. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "Risks and benefits have been explained to me".
12. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "I understand how my results will be kept confidential".
13. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "I consent to COVID-19 antibody screening test".
14. Choose how you would like to receive your test results ("Email", "Phone", "In-person", or "Patient portal").
15. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the question "I consent to follow-up contact if needed".
16. Sign your name in the "Patient Signature" field to confirm you have read and understood this consent form.
17. Enter the date in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth (required)**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1995-02-25).
* **Patient ID (required)**: Enter your ID number as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number (required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address (required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Testing Location (required)**: Enter the testing location where you will be tested.
* **Test Date (required)**: Enter the date of the test in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-03-15).
* **Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19? (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, "No" if you have not, or "Unsure" if you are unsure.
* **Date of COVID-19 diagnosis (optional)**: If you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, enter the date of diagnosis in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2020-01-01).
* **Do you currently have COVID-19 symptoms? (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you currently have COVID-19 symptoms, "No" if you do not, or "Unsure" if you are unsure.
* **Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19? (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19, "No" if you have not, or "Unsure" if you are unsure.
* **I understand the purpose of this antibody test (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you understand the purpose of the test, "No" if you do not.
* **The testing process has been explained to me (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you understand the testing process, "No" if you do not.
* **Risks and benefits have been explained to me (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you understand the risks and benefits, "No" if you do not.
* **I understand how my results will be kept confidential (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you understand how your results will be kept confidential, "No" if you do not.
* **I consent to COVID-19 antibody screening test (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you consent to the test, "No" if you do not.
* **Preferred method for receiving results (required)**: Choose how you would like to receive your test results ("Email", "Phone", "In-person", or "Patient portal").
* **I consent to follow-up contact if needed (required)**: Choose "Yes" if you consent to follow-up contact, "No" if you do not.
* **Patient Signature (required)**: Sign your name to confirm you have read and understood this consent form.
* **Date (required)**: Enter the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-03-15).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your information accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is a required document for testing and your signature is needed to confirm you have read and understood the consent form.
