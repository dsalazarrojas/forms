# Film Crew COVID 19 Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from film crew members regarding their COVID-19 health status, travel history, and other relevant details. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure the well-being of the crew and the successful execution of film productions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand each section and its corresponding questions.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information, taking note of any medical conditions or exposure history that might affect your health or the health of others on the crew.
3. Make sure to sign and date the declaration, as indicated in the 'Signature' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: Provide general information about yourself, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal data.
* **Medical info**: Report any medical conditions or health issues you may have, including allergies, illnesses, or previous surgeries.
* **Exposure Risk**: Describe any potential risks of exposure to COVID-19, including recent travel, contact with infected individuals, or other relevant factors.
* **Health Questions**: Answer all the questions in this section honestly and thoroughly, as they are crucial for understanding your current health status.
* **Contact Info**: Provide contact details for emergency contact, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Travel Info**: Report any recent travel history, including destinations and dates.
* **Travel History**: Describe any previous travel history, including countries or regions visited.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any pre-existing medical conditions, including chronic illnesses or allergies.
* **Vaccination History**: Report any vaccinations or immunizations you have received.
* **Exposure History**: Describe any potential exposure to COVID-19, including contact with infected individuals or recent travel.
* **Travel Restrictions**: Indicate if you have any travel restrictions or quarantine requirements.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide information for an emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Submission Info**: Leave this section blank, as it's for administrative purposes only.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the declaration, as indicated in this section.
* **Declaration Done**: Fill out this section to confirm you've read and understood the declaration.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that might be relevant to your health status or travel history.
* **Declaration Date**: Fill out the date on which you declare your health status.
* **Declaration Time**: Fill out the time on which you declare your health status.
* **Declaration Status**: Choose your current declaration status (Pending, Declared, Declined, or Unknown).
* **Declaration Status Date**: Fill out the date on which your declaration status changed.
* **Submission Date**: Fill out the date on which you submitted the form.
* **Submitter**: Choose who submitted the form (Admin, Crew Member, Team Member, or Unknown).
* **Submission Method**: Choose how you submitted the form (Email, Form, Webform, or Unknown).
* **Submitted To**: Indicate if the form was submitted to anyone (Yes or No).
* **Tool Name**: Choose the tool used for submission (Chatjimmy, Another Tool, or Unknown).

This help guide is designed to assist film crew members in completing the COVID-19 health declaration form accurately and effectively. Please take the time to review each section carefully and fill out the form with honest and complete information.
