# COVID 19 Rapid Response Funding Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to support organizations responding to the COVID-19 pandemic by providing rapid funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's details, including name, mission statement, and contact information.
2. Describe your organization's current financial status.
3. Specify the amount of funding you are requesting and the primary purpose of the funding.
4. Provide a detailed description of your need and the population impacted by the pandemic.
5. Outline your timeline for implementing the funding.
6. Certify that the information provided is accurate and the funds will be used for the stated purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name as it appears on your letterhead or website.
* **Tax ID (EIN)**: Enter your organization's EIN (Employer Identification Number) as issued by the IRS.
* **Organization Mission Statement**: Describe your organization's mission statement in 1-2 sentences.
* **Primary Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Amount Requested ($)**: Specify the maximum amount of funding you are requesting, up to $25,000.
* **Primary Purpose of Funding**: Select the primary purpose of the funding from the options provided:
	+ Emergency Relief
	+ Healthcare Supplies
	+ Food Insecurity
	+ Community Outreach
	+ Operational Support
* **Detailed Description of Need**: Describe how the COVID-19 pandemic has created a need for funding.
* **Population Impacted**: Describe the group of people this funding will serve.
* **Timeline for Implementation**: Describe when the funds will be deployed.
* **Brief Summary of Current Financial Status**: Describe your organization's current financial status.
* **I Certify the Information Provided is Accurate and the Funds will be Used for the Stated Purpose**: Check the "True" option to certify that the information provided is accurate and the funds will be used for the stated purpose.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when completing each field to support the review process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please review your application carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
