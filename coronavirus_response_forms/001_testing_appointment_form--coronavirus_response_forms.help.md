# Testing Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Testing Appointment Form is designed to collect relevant information from patients and medical staff for testing appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your details, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Enter your patient name, phone number, and email address.
3. Select your disease diagnosis from the drop-down menu.
4. Choose a schedule date and time for your appointment.
5. Enter the doctor's name, phone number, and email address.
6. Set the appointment duration and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Phone Number** (`patient_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Disease Diagnosis** (`disease_diagnosis`, select_one, optional): Select the disease that you are being tested for, such as COVID-19, Malaria, or Influenza.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of your appointment.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your appointment.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone Number** (`doctor_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, optional): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, number, optional): Set the duration of your appointment.
* **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your appointment, such as Confirmed, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
