# Beauty Salon Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Salon Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from clients to ensure a safe and comfortable experience for both the client and the salon staff. This form is used to collect contact and appointment information, medical history, and other relevant details to help the salon provide personalized service and maintain a healthy environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form with the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. For fields like `client_email` and `client_phone`, enter your actual email and phone numbers.
3. In the `service_date` field, select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
4. For fields like `hair_length` and `hair_texture`, select the options that best match your hair characteristics.
5. In the `chemical_treatments` field, select all applicable chemical treatments you have undergone in the last 6 months.
6. In the `allergies` field, list any known allergies to hair products or chemicals.
7. The `covid_symptoms` field asks about any COVID-19 symptoms you may be experiencing.
8. In the `covid_exposure` field, indicate whether you have been exposed to COVID-19 in the last 14 days.
9. For fields like `temperature_check` and `mask_policy`, select the options that best describe your consent or preference.
10. In the `liability_waiver` field, select the option that indicates your understanding and agreement with the salon's liability waiver.
11. Finally, provide any additional comments or requests in the `additional_comments` field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (`client_name`)**: Please enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (`client_email`)**: Enter your actual email address where you can be contacted for appointment reminders and other salon communications.
* **Phone Number (`client_phone`)**: Enter the best number to reach you for appointment confirmations or last-minute updates.
* **Appointment Date (`service_date`)**: Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Hair Color Preference (`hair_color_preference`)**: Describe your preferred hair color or shade.
* **Hair Cut and Style Preference (`hair_cut_style`)**: Describe the cut and style you would like to achieve.
* **Current Hair Length (`hair_length`)**: Select the current length of your hair.
* **Hair Texture (`hair_texture`)**: Select the natural texture of your hair.
* **Previous Chemical Treatments (`chemical_treatments`)**: Select all chemical treatments you have undergone in the last 6 months.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities (`allergies`)**: List any known allergies to hair products or chemicals.
* **Scalp Conditions (`scalp_conditions`)**: Select any scalp conditions you may have.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms Check (`covid_symptoms`)**: Indicate whether you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Recent COVID-19 Exposure (`covid_exposure`)**: Indicate whether you have been exposed to COVID-19 in the last 14 days.
* **Temperature Check Consent (`temperature_check`)**: Select whether you agree to have your temperature taken upon arrival.
* **Mask Policy Acknowledgment (`mask_policy`)**: Select whether you understand and agree to follow the salon's mask policy.
* **Liability Waiver (`liability_waiver`)**: Select whether you understand and agree to the salon's liability waiver.
* **Additional Comments or Requests (`additional_comments`)**: Provide any other information you would like to share with the salon staff.
* **Date of Signature (`signature_date`)**: Select the current date.
