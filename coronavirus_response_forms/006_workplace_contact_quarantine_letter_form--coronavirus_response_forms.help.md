# Workplace Contact Quarantine Letter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for employees to report their workplace contact quarantine information to their employer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Information** (`employer_info`, text, required): This is the section for providing general information about your workplace and employment.
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, required): Fill in your employee ID, name, and job title.
* **Employee Exposure** (`employee_exposure`, text, required): Describe your exposure to COVID-19, including the date and location of exposure.
* **Workplace Contact Quarantine** (`workplace_contact_quarantine`, text, required): Outline the quarantine procedure your employer will follow for you to return to work.
* **Date of Last Day Worked** (`date_of_last_day_worked`, date, required): Fill in the date of your last day of work.
* **Date of Return to Work** (`date_of_return_to_work`, date, required): Fill in the expected date of your return to work.
* **Workplace Contact Quarantine Letter** (`workplace_contact_quarantine_letter`, text, required): Write a note to your workplace about your quarantine status.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): This field is currently not used in the form. Please ignore it.
 
## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure that the employer information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Make sure to include all relevant details about your exposure to COVID-19.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all fields are completed.
* This form is an original adaptation and may not cover all possible scenarios or requirements. If you are unsure, please contact your HR representative or manager for guidance.
