# COVID 19 Guest Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Guest Declaration Form is designed to gather information from guests visiting the facility, ensuring a safe and healthy environment. It's essential to complete this form accurately to help us take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each section and question carefully.
- Answer all questions accurately and honestly.
- Provide complete and detailed information in the text fields.
- Select the correct options for the select fields.
- Make sure to review and confirm the accuracy of the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Full Name** (2): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official government-issued identification.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Check-in Date** (5): Enter the date you plan to check-in to the facility.
* **Check-out Date** (6): Enter the date you plan to check-out from the facility.
* **Room Number or ID** (7): Enter the room number or ID assigned to you during check-in.
* **Number of Guests in Party** (8): Enter the number of people traveling with you.
* **Home Address or City** (9): Enter your home address or city for contact purposes.
* **Any COVID-19 symptoms in past 14 days** (11): Choose "True" if you have experienced any COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days. If not, choose "False".
* **Symptoms present** (12): If you answered "True" to the previous question, list any symptoms you have experienced.
* **Temperature today** (13): If you have a fever, enter your current temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.
* **Exposed to COVID-19 in past 14 days** (14): Choose "True" if you have been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days. If not, choose "False" or "Unknown" if unsure.
* **Tested positive for COVID-19 recently** (15): Choose "True" if you have tested positive for COVID-19 recently. If not, choose "False".
* **Vaccination Status** (16): Select your current vaccination status: Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Where did you travel from** (18): Enter the location you traveled from.
* **Mode of Transportation** (19): Select the mode of transportation you used for travel.
* **Traveled from high-risk COVID-19 area** (20): Choose "True" if you have traveled from a high-risk COVID-19 area. If not, choose "False" or "Unknown" if unsure.
* **I confirm all information above is accurate** (22): Choose "Yes, I Confirm" to confirm that all information provided is accurate.
* **I consent to health checks and facility rules** (23): Choose "Yes, I Consent" to agree to health checks and facility rules.
* **I consent to data collection for contact tracing** (24): Choose "Yes, I Consent" to agree to data collection for contact tracing.
* **Declaration Date** (25): This field will automatically be populated with the current date.

## Tips

* Double-check all questions before submitting the form to ensure accurate and honest responses.
* If unsure or unclear about any question, ask facility staff for guidance.
* Review and follow facility rules and guidelines for a safe and healthy environment.
