# Covid Questionnaire For Patients - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients about their exposure to COVID-19 and their personal medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Select all the options that apply to you.
3. Fill out all required fields.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exposed
* **Exposed** (`Exposed`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the ways you may have been exposed to COVID-19, such as traveling to a known outbreak area or being in contact with someone who has COVID-19.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the symptoms you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past. 
* **Travel History** (`Travel History`, text, required: false): Please describe your travel history, including any recent travel to areas with known outbreaks.
* **Medical Practice** (`Medical Practice`, text, required: false): Please describe your current medical practice, including any medications you are taking or have taken recently.
* **Exposed Symptoms** (`Exposed Symptoms`, text, required: false): Please describe any symptoms you are experiencing that you believe may be related to your exposure to COVID-19.
