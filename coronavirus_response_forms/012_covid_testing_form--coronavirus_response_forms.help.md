# Covid Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's COVID-19 testing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "patient-info" field.
2. Provide details about the testing experience in the "testing-info" field.
3. Confirm or decline consent in the "consent" field.
4. Enter the contact person's contact information, including phone number and email (if applicable).
5. Select the test location.
6. Select the type of test conducted.
7. Enter any follow-up information, including any results or next steps.
8. Enter the date and time of the test.
9. Enter the result of the test.
10. Enter any additional notes or contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient-info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name and any other relevant details.
* **Testing Info** (`testing-info`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the testing experience, including any symptoms or results.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Confirm that the patient has been informed and is consenting to the test.
* **Contact Info** (`contact-info`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name and contact information, including phone number and email (if applicable).
* **Contact Person** (`contact-person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact for follow-up.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact-phone`, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact-email`, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Test Location** (`test-location`, select_one, required): Select the location where the test was conducted.
* **Test Type** (`test-type`, select_one, required): Select the type of test conducted.
* **Follow-up Info** (`follow-up-info`, text, required): Enter any follow-up information, including any next steps or results.
* **Test Date** (`test-date`, date, required): Enter the date of the test.
* **Test Time** (`test-time`, time, required): Enter the time of the test.
* **Test Result** (`test-result`, text, required): Enter the result of the test.
* **Test Result Date** (`test-result-date`, date, required): Enter the date of the test result.
* **Test Result Time** (`test-result-time`, time, required): Enter the time of the test result.
* **Test Result Location** (`test-result-location`, text, required): Enter the location where the test was conducted.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact-notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or contact information.
