# (P) Recovered After Testing Positive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The (P) Recovered After Testing Positive Form is a tool used to track and document the recovery status of individuals who have tested positive for COVID-19. This form helps healthcare providers, employers, and other relevant parties to assess and manage the recovery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Full name as it appears on your identification.
	* Date of birth.
	* Phone number (a contact number where you can be reached).
	* Email address.
	* Current address (street address, city, state, and zip code).
2. Provide test information:
	* Date when your sample was collected for the COVID-19 test.
	* Type of test performed (e.g., PCR Test, Rapid Antigen Test, Antibody Test, or Other).
	* Location where the test was conducted.
3. Report your symptoms:
	* If you experienced COVID-19 symptoms, select "True" and report the date when symptoms first appeared.
	* If you experienced symptoms, list them from the options provided (e.g., Fever or chills, Cough, Shortness of breath, etc.).
	* If your symptoms have resolved, report the date when they ended.
4. Provide recovery information:
	* Date when you ended your isolation period.
	* If you've received medical clearance to return to work, select "True".
5. Add any relevant employer information:
	* Name of your employer.
	* Contact person at your workplace.
	* Workplace contact phone number.
	* Planned return to work date.
6. Add any additional comments or notes:
	* Any other relevant information about your recovery process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone number**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address.
* **Current address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Date of positive COVID-19 test**: Enter the date when your sample was collected for the test.
* **Type of test performed**: Select the type of test that was performed (e.g., PCR Test, Rapid Antigen Test, Antibody Test, or Other).
* **Location where test was conducted**: Enter the name of the location where the test was performed.
* **Did you experience COVID-19 symptoms?**: If you had symptoms, select "True" and report the date when symptoms first appeared.
* **Which symptoms did you experience? (Select all that apply)**: Select all the symptoms you experienced from the options provided.
* **Date symptoms resolved (if applicable)**: If your symptoms have resolved, report the date when they ended.
* **Date you ended isolation period**: Enter the date when you ended your isolation period.
* **Have you received medical clearance to return to work?**: If you've received medical clearance, select "True".
* **Name of employer**: Enter the name of your employer.
* **Contact person at workplace**: Enter the name of the contact person at your workplace.
* **Workplace contact phone number**: Enter the contact phone number of your workplace.
* **Planned return to work date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Enter any other relevant information about your recovery process.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information before completing this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult your healthcare provider or contact the relevant authority.
* This form is for tracking and documenting the recovery process, so make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
