# Student Daily Wellness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from students about their daily well-being, including their temperature, symptoms, and interactions with school administrators. It helps the school administration track student health and provide timely interventions when necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name, email, and phone number.
2. Report your current temperature.
3. List any symptoms or concerns you are experiencing.
4. Select the school administrator you are currently interacting with.
5. Fill in their contact information (phone number and email).
6. Provide any additional comments or concerns.
7. Complete the date and time you are filling out this form.
8. Indicate your current location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Temperature** (`student_temperature`, number, required): Report your current body temperature in degrees Fahrenheit or Celsius.
* **Student Symptoms** (`student_symptoms`, text, required): List any symptoms or concerns you are experiencing, such as headaches, nausea, or difficulty breathing.
* **School Administrator** (`school_administrator`, select_one, required): Select the school administrator you are currently interacting with.
* **School Administrator Phone** (`school_administrator_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the school administrator you are interacting with.
* **School Administrator Email** (`school_administrator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the school administrator you are interacting with.
* **School Administrator Comments** (`school_administrator_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about your interaction with the school administrator.
* **Date Today** (`date_today`, date, required): Fill in the current date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Today** (`time_today`, time, required): Fill in the current time in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Indicate your current location.

## Tips
- Ensure each student completes their own form.
- Only submit one form per student.
- Complete all required fields.
