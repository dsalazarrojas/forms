# JUL COVID 19 Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on individuals. It is intended to be filled out by those who have experienced or are experiencing the effects of the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Ensure that you have not filled out this form before.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Survey Name** (`survey_name`, text, required)
* This field is used to enter the name of the survey you are taking part in.
* Please enter the name of the survey as it appears on the survey invitation or as you have been told by your supervisor/manager.

### 2. **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required)
* This field is used to record the date you took the survey.
* Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 3. **Age** (`age`, number, required)
* This field is used to record your age.
* Please enter your age in whole numbers.

### 4. **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required)
* This field is used to record your gender.
* Please select the option that best represents your gender.

### 5. **Health Status General** (`health_status_general`, text, required)
* This field is used to describe your general health status during the pandemic.
* Please provide a brief description of how the pandemic has affected your health.

### 6. **Have Symptoms** (`have_symptoms`, select_one, required)
* This field is used to record whether you have experienced symptoms during the pandemic.
* Please select the option that best describes your symptoms.

### 7. **Income Change** (`income_change`, number, required)
* This field is used to record any changes in your income due to the pandemic.
* Please enter the percentage change as a whole number (-100 to 100).

### 8. **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required)
* This field is used to record your current employment status.
* Please select the option that best describes your employment status.

### 9. **Social Connectivity** (`social_connectivity`, select_multiple, required)
* This field is used to record your social connections during the pandemic.
* Please select all the options that apply to you.

### 10. **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional)
* This field is used to record your social media usage during the pandemic.
* Please select all the options that apply to you.
Note: This field is not required.

### 11. **Tips**
* Please make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to our support team.
