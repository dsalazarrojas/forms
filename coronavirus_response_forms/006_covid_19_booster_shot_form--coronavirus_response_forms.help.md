# COVID 19 Booster Shot Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in receiving the COVID-19 booster shot. It will help us understand your vaccination history, medical conditions, and concerns, and schedule a booster shot appointment if you consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Patient Information section with your full name, date of birth, and email address.
2. Provide your phone number and initial vaccine type received.
3. If applicable, fill out the Vaccination History section with your date of first and second doses.
4. If you are interested in receiving the booster shot, select your level of interest.
5. In the Booster Considerations section, select any concerns you have about the booster shot.
6. If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, select "True" and describe them in the space provided.
7. If you have any known allergies to vaccine components, select "True" and describe them.
8. Finally, consent to receiving the booster shot by selecting "Yes, I Consent" and choose a preferred appointment date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Name** (Text)
Full Name: Enter your full name, including any titles (e.g. Mr., Mrs., etc.).

### 2. **Date of Birth** (Date)
Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g. 01/01/2020).

### 3. **Email Address** (Email)
Email Address: Enter your valid email address.

### 4. **Phone Number** (Text)
Phone Number: Enter your phone number.

### 5. **Initial Vaccine Type** (Select One)
Initial Vaccine Type: Select the type of vaccine you received for the initial dose. Options are:
* Pfizer
* Moderna
* Johnson and Johnson
* AstraZeneca
* Other

### 6. **Date of First Dose** (Date)
Date of First Dose: Enter the date of the initial dose you received.

### 7. **Date of Second Dose** (Date)
Date of Second Dose: Enter the date of the second dose you received (if applicable).

### 8. **Interest in Booster Shot** (Select One)
Interest in Booster Shot: Select how interested you are in receiving the booster shot. Options are:
* Yes, Very Interested
* Yes, Somewhat Interested
* No, Not Interested
* Unsure

### 9. **Vaccination History** (Note)
Vaccination History: This section is for additional information about your vaccination history. You can add any relevant details here.

### 10. **Initial Vaccine Type** (Select One)
Initial Vaccine Type: (This field is already covered by **Initial Vaccine Type** field 5, please skip this one)

### 11. **Booster Considerations** (Note)
Booster Considerations: This section is for listing any concerns you have about the booster shot. Select all that apply from the options below.

### 12. **Concerns** (Select Multiple)
Concerns: Select any of the following concerns you have:
* Safety Concerns
* Side Effects
* Effectiveness Questions
* Medical Conditions
* No Concerns

### 13. **Medical Conditions** (Select One)
Medical Conditions: Select "True" if you have any pre-existing medical conditions.

### 14. **Medical Conditions Description** (Text)
Medical Conditions Description: Describe any pre-existing medical conditions you have.

### 15. **Allergies** (Select One)
Allergies: Select "True" if you have any known allergies to vaccine components.

### 16. **Allergy Description** (Text)
Allergy Description: Describe any known allergies to vaccine components.

### 17. **Consent** (Select One)
Consent: Select "Yes, I Consent" to receive the booster shot.

### 18. **Preferred Appointment Date** (Date)
Preferred Appointment Date: Enter a date for your preferred appointment for the booster shot.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
* A representative will review your form and schedule an appointment if you consent to receive the booster shot.
