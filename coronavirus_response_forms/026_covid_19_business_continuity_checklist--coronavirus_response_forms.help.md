# COVID 19 Business Continuity Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses assess their readiness and continuity in the event of a COVID-19 pandemic or other disruptions. The form guides businesses through a series of questions to identify potential risks and areas for improvement, creating a comprehensive plan to respond to and recover from disruptions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the assessment date, business name, and type of business.
2. Move on to the next section and answer each question accordingly.
3. Fill out the notes section with additional information or comments as needed.
4. Review each section and answer the overall readiness question at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): The date when this self-assessment is performed.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, text, required): Enter your industry or sector.
* **Assessment Lead Name** (`assessment_lead`, text, required): The person completing this form.
* **-- Health and Safety Measures --** (`note_health_measures`, note, required): Note any health and safety measures in place.
* **COVID-19 policies and procedures established** (`covid_policy_established`, select_one, required): Whether your business has documented policies for COVID-19.
* **Regular sanitization protocol implemented** (`sanitization_protocol`, select_one, required): Whether your business has a regular sanitization protocol in place.
* **Adequate PPE supplies available** (`ppe_supplies`, select_one, required): Whether your business has stocked up on personal protective equipment (PPE).
* **Social distancing measures implemented** (`distancing_measures`, select_one, required): Whether your business has implemented social distancing measures.
* **Isolation area designated for sick employees** (`isolation_area`, select_one, optional): Whether a separate area for sick employees is available.
* **Employee health screening protocol** (`health_screening`, select_one, optional): Whether your business has a temperature check and questionnaire process in place.
* **-- Staffing and Continuity --** (`note_staff_continuity`, note, optional): Note any staffing and continuity measures in place.
* **Remote work capabilities established** (`remote_work_capable`, select_one, required): Whether your business has remote work capabilities.
* **Critical role cross-training completed** (`cross_training`, select_one, optional): Whether multiple employees are trained for key positions.
* **Succession plan documented** (`succession_plan`, select_one, optional): Whether a plan is in place for key personnel absence.
* **Employee communication plan activated** (`communication_plan`, select_one, optional): Whether regular updates and guidelines are shared with employees.
* **-- Operations and Systems --** (`note_operations`, note, optional): Note any operations and systems measures in place.
* **Critical supplies backup obtained** (`critical_supplies`, select_one, required): Whether essential supplies have been secured.
* **IT systems backup and recovery tested** (`IT_backup`, select_one, optional): Whether IT systems backups and disaster recovery plan are in place.
* **Vendor and supplier communication maintained** (`vendor_communication`, select_one, optional): Whether you have a plan to receive essential supplies.
* **-- Financial and Legal --** (`note_financial`, note, optional): Note any financial and legal measures in place.
* **Financial reserves assessed** (`financial_reserves`, select_one, optional): Whether cash flow and emergency funds are identified.
* **Business insurance coverage verified** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one, optional): Whether policies are updated for pandemic-related losses.
* **Eligible assistance programs identified** (`assistance_programs`, select_one, optional): Whether you have researched PPP, SBA loans, or grants.
* **Legal and compliance requirements reviewed** (`legal_review`, select_one, optional): Whether you have reviewed legal and compliance requirements with legal counsel.
* **Overall Business Continuity Readiness** (`overall_readiness`, select_one, required): How prepared your business is.
* **Top Priority Actions Needed** (`priority_actions`, text, optional): List critical items to address.

## Tips

* Please answer each section carefully and thoroughly.
* Use the notes section to add any additional information or comments as needed.
* Review each section carefully and answer the overall readiness question at the end.
* This form is a starting point for your business continuity planning, and it's essential to customize it according to your specific needs and requirements.
