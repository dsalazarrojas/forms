# Covid 19 Tanning Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to tan, ensuring they meet the necessary health and safety standards for tanning activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have read and understood the form's purpose.
2. Enter your information in the provided fields:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Address**: Enter your current address for contact and billing purposes.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address for secure communication.
	* **Age**: Enter your age for age verification purposes.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification.
	* **Date of Tan**: Enter the date you last tanned.
	* **Consent to Tan**: Check the box if you consent to tan.
	* **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions, please describe them.
	* **Medical Condition Other**: If your medical condition is not listed, describe it here.
	* **Doctor Name**: If you have a doctor, enter their name for reference.
	* **Doctor Contact Number**: If you have a doctor, enter their contact number.
	* **Doctor Contact Email**: If you have a doctor, enter their email address.
	* **Doctor Contact Address**: If you have a doctor, enter their contact address.
	* **Medical Document**: If you have any medical documents (e.g., certificates, notes), please upload or describe them.
	* **Medical Document Other**: If your medical document is not listed, describe it here.
	* **Medical Insurance Provider**: If you have medical insurance, enter the name of the provider.
	* **Medical Insurance Provider Other**: If your medical insurance provider is not listed, describe it here.
	* **Tan Facility**: If you have a preferred tanning facility, enter their name.
	* **Tan Facility Other**: If your tanning facility is not listed, describe it here.
	* **Date of Next Tan**: Enter the date you plan to tan next.
	* **Date Of Next Tan 2**: Enter the date of your next tan, if it's different from the previous one.
	* **Consent to Next Tan**: Check the box if you consent to tan on your next visit.
	* **Note**: Add any additional information or notes about your tanning history or medical conditions.
