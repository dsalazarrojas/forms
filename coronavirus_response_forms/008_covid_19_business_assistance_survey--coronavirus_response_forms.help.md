# COVID 19 Business Assistance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the impact of COVID-19 on businesses, helping us understand the current situation and provide targeted assistance. Please fill out the form accurately and only once to ensure we receive a comprehensive picture of the business landscape.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully, starting from the top and moving down. Each section has a specific set of fields that require your attention. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): This is the name of your business. Please enter the official name of your company.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of business you operate, such as Retail, Food Service, Healthcare, Manufacturing, or Professional Services.
* **Business Ownership** (`ownership_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of business ownership, such as Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, Non-Profit, or Other.
* **Number of Employees** (`employees`, number, required): Please enter the number of employees currently working for your business.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person we can contact for further information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Revenue Impact** (`revenue_impact`, select_one, required): Please select the impact of COVID-19 on your business revenue, such as Severe Decline, Moderate Decline, Slight Decline, No Impact, or Increased Revenue.
* **Current Operating Status** (`operating_status`, select_one, required): Please select the current operating status of your business, such as Fully Open, Partially Open, Temporarily Closed, or Permanently Closed.
* **Employee Challenges** (`employee_challenges`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply from the list of employee challenges, such as Staff Shortages, Payroll Issues, Health Concerns, Childcare, Transportation, or Other.
* **Assistance Needed** (`assistance_needed`, select_multiple, required): Please select all that apply from the list of types of assistance needed, such as Funding, PPE Supplies, Testing, Consulting, Regulatory Guidance, or Other.
* **Priority Assistance Needed** (`priority_assistance`, text, optional): If you have any specific priority assistance needed, please describe it in this field.
* **Business Outlook** (`outlook`, select_one, required): Please select your business outlook for the next 6 months, such as Very Optimistic, Somewhat Optimistic, Neutral, Somewhat Pessimistic, or Very Pessimistic.
