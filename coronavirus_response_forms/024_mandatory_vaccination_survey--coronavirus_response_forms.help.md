# Mandatory Vaccination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their vaccination status, including the type of vaccine administered, the date and dosage of the vaccination, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **Patient Identifier** in the first field.
2. Provide the patient's **Patient Name** in the second field.
3. Enter the patient's **Date Of Birth** in the third field.
4. Enter the patient's **Medical Provider** in the fourth field.
5. Enter the date the **Date Of Vaccination** was administered in the fifth field.
6. Enter the **Dosage Of Vaccination** administered in the sixth field.
7. Specify the **Vaccine Administered** in the seventh field.
8. Record the date the **Date Last Updated** was updated in the eighth field.
9. Provide any relevant **Medical History** in the ninth field.
10. Select the patient's **Patient Compliance** in the tenth field.
11. Select the **Vaccination Certificate** option that best describes the patient's vaccination status in the eleventh field.
12. Enter any **Additional Comments** in the twelfth field (optional).
13. Enter the **Email Contact** of the patient's medical provider in the thirteenth field (optional).
14. Enter the patient's **Phone Contact** in the fourteenth field (optional).
15. Enter the patient's **Fax Contact** in the fifteenth field (optional).
16. Enter the patient's **Address Contact** in the sixteenth field (optional).
17. Enter the patient's **Medical Provider Contact** in the seventeenth field (optional).
18. Enter the **Date Last Contact** in the eighteenth field (optional).
19. Enter the patient's **Patient Signature** in the nineteenth field.
20. Enter the medical provider's **Medical Provider Signature** in the twentieth field.
21. Enter the date of the **Date Signed** in the twenty-first field.
22. Enter the date the **Vaccination Administered Date** was administered in the twenty-second field.
23. Enter the person who administered the **Vaccination Administered By** in the twenty-third field.
24. Enter the type of **Vaccine Type** administered in the twenty-fourth field.
25. Select the patient's **Patient Vaccination Status** in the twenty-fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identifier** (`Patient Identifier`, `Text`, Required): Enter the patient's identifier, such as a patient ID number or medical record number.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `Text`, Required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, `Date`, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Medical Provider** (`Medical Provider`, `Text`, Required): Enter the name of the medical provider who administered the vaccination.
* **Date Of Vaccination** (`Date Of Vaccination`, `Date`, Required): Enter the date the vaccination was administered.
* **Dosage Of Vaccination** (`Dosage Of Vaccination`, `Number`, Required): Enter the dosage of the vaccination administered.
* **Vaccine Administered** (`Vaccine Administered`, `Text`, Required): Describe the type of vaccine administered.
* **Date Last Updated** (`Date Last Updated`, `Date`, Required): Record the last update date of the form.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `Text`, Required): Record any relevant medical history or previous medical conditions.
* **Patient Compliance** (`Patient Compliance`, `Select Multiple`, Required): Select all applicable compliance options (e.g. "Compliance 1", "Compliance 2", "Compliance 3", "Compliance 4", "Compliance 5").
* **Vaccination Certificate** (`Vaccination Certificate`, `Select One`, Required): Select the type of vaccination certificate that best describes the patient's vaccination status.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `Note`, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's vaccination status.
* **Email Contact** (`Email Contact`, `Email`, Optional): Enter the email address of the patient's medical provider (if applicable).
* **Phone Contact** (`Phone Contact`, `Text`, Optional): Enter the phone number of the patient's medical provider (if applicable).
* **Fax Contact** (`Fax Contact`, `Text`, Optional): Enter the fax number of the patient's medical provider (if applicable).
* **Address Contact** (`Address Contact`, `Text`, Optional): Enter the address of the patient's medical provider (if applicable).
* **Medical Provider Contact** (`Medical Provider Contact`, `Text`, Optional): Enter the name of the patient's medical provider (if applicable).
* **Date Last Contact** (`Date Last Contact`, `Date`, Optional): Record the last contact date of the patient's medical provider.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `Text`, Required): Enter the patient's signature.
* **Medical Provider Signature** (`Medical Provider Signature`, `Text`, Required): Enter the medical provider's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`, `Date`, Required): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Vaccination Administered Date** (`Vaccination Administered Date`, `Date`, Required): Enter the date the vaccination was administered.
* **Vaccination Administered By** (`Vaccination Administered By`, `Text`, Required): Enter the name of the person who administered the vaccination.
* **Vaccine Type** (`Vaccine Type`, `Text`, Required): Enter the type of vaccine administered.
* **Patient Vaccination Status** (`Patient Vaccination Status`, `Select Multiple`, Required): Select all applicable vaccination status options (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your medical provider for guidance.
* Double-check for spelling and formatting errors before submitting the form.
