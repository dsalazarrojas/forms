# Social Club Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect health information from social club members to assess their health status and travel history. It helps the club administrators make informed decisions about club activities and gatherings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. If you are unsure about any question, ask your club administrator for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional information you want to share with the club administrators.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, optional): Please enter your contact information so that club administrators can reach out to you if needed.
* **Temperature** (`Temperature`, number, optional): If you have a fever, please enter the number of degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are experiencing any of the following symptoms: 
  - Fever
  - Cough
  - Shortness of breath
  - Chest pain or discomfort
  - Muscle aches or pain
  - Diarrhea
  - Nausea or vomiting
  - Runny nose or stuffy nose
  - Sore throat
  - Fatigue or weakness
  - Headache
  - Sore throat
* **Travel History** (`Travel History`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the countries you have recently visited.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, optional): If you have recently visited a specific country, enter the name of the country.
* **Health Status** (`Health Status`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Active' if you are feeling well, 'Inactive' if you are not feeling well.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you need help with this form, ask your club administrator.
- If you have any concerns or questions after completing this form, please reach out to your club administrator.
