# Covid 19 Vaccine Exemption Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to declare medical exemptions for the Covid-19 vaccine. It requires personal and medical information from both the patient and their doctor.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out your personal details (Patient Name, Date of Birth, Patient’s Name, Patient’s Address, Patient’s Phone, Patient’s Email, Patient’s Date of Birth) in page 1.
- Select your doctor's details (Doctor's Specialty, Doctor's Title, Doctor's Initials, Doctor's Phone, Doctor's Address, Doctor's Email, Doctor's Date of Birth, Doctor's NPI, Doctor's License) in page 2.
- Provide the reason for exemption in page 3.
- Sign the form with your doctor's signature and provide the date and time of signing, and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)** (text, required): Enter your real name as it appears on your official government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth (2)** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Vaccine Type (3)** (text, required): Enter the type of vaccine exemption you are declaring.
* **Reason for Exemption (4)** (text, required): Provide a clear and honest reason for your exemption.
* **Signature (5)** (note, required): Sign the form with your doctor's signature.
* **Patient ID (6)** (text, required): Enter a unique identifier for you as a patient.
* **Doctor’s Signature (7)** (select multiple, required): Select your doctor from the list (Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, Dr. Jane).
* **Date Signed (8)** (date, required): Enter the date of signing in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Time Signed (9)** (time, required): Enter the time of signing in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location Signed (10)** (text, required): Enter the location where the form was signed.
* **Hospital Name (11)** (text, required): Enter the name of the hospital where the exemption was declared.
* **Patient’s Name (12)** (select one, optional): Select your name from the list (John Doe, Jane Doe, Bob Smith).
* **Doctor’s Specialty (13)** (text, required): Enter your doctor's specialty or area of expertise.
* **Patient’s Address (14)** (text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Patient’s Phone (15)** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Patient’s Email (16)** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Patient’s Date of Birth (17)** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Doctor’s Title (18)** (text, required): Enter your doctor's title or job description.
* **Doctor’s Initials (19)** (text, required): Enter your doctor's initials.
* **Doctor’s Phone (20)** (text, required): Enter your doctor's contact phone number.
* **Doctor’s Address (21)** (text, required): Enter your doctor's address.
* **Doctor’s Email (22)** (email, required): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Doctor’s Date of Birth (23)** (date, required): Enter your doctor's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Doctor’s NPI (24)** (text, required): Enter your doctor's NPI or National Provider Identifier.
* **Doctor’s License (25)** (text, required): Enter your doctor's license number.

## Tips
- Ensure you answer all questions truthfully.
- Ensure you have your doctor's signature and contact information correct.
- Review the form for accuracy before submission.
