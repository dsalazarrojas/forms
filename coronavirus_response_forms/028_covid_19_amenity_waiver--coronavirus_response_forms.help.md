# COVID 19 Amenity Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Amenity Waiver form is used to collect essential information from guests visiting a facility during the pandemic, ensuring they understand the safety rules and risks associated with their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required personal details:
	* Guest Name: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address for confirmation and communication purposes.
	* Phone Number: Enter your best contact number in case we need to reach you.
	* Date of Visit: Choose the date you plan to visit the facility.

2. Provide amenity information:
	* Amenity Type: Select all amenities you plan to use during your visit.

3. Health and safety information:
	* Health Screening: Indicate if you have any COVID-19 symptoms.
	* Recent Exposure: Confirm if you have had recent exposure to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
	* Vaccination Status: Indicate your COVID-19 vaccination status if applicable.

4. Risk Acknowledgment and Liability Release:
	* Risk Acknowledgment: Confirm you understand the risks associated with visiting during the pandemic.
	* Liability Release: Sign to release the facility from liability.
	* Assumption of Risk: Acknowledge you assume all risks associated with your visit.

5. Capacity and Compliance:
	* Capacity Limitations: Confirm you understand that capacity may be limited due to the pandemic.
	* Compliance Agreement: Agree to follow all rules and guidelines in place.

6. Symptom Reporting:
	* Symptom Reporting: Confirm you will report any COVID-19 symptoms that develop during your visit.

7. Minor Information (if applicable):
	* Is Guest a Minor: Confirm if the guest is under 18 years old.
	* Parent or Guardian Name: Enter the name of the parent or guardian if the guest is a minor.
	* Parent or Guardian Signature: Enter your signature as the parent or guardian.
	* Guest Signature: Enter your signature as the guest.
	* Date Signed: Enter the date you signed the form.
	* Witness Name (optional): Enter the name of the witness if applicable.
	* Witness Signature (optional): Enter the signature of the witness if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the facility staff for assistance.
* This form is an essential part of our COVID-19 health and safety protocol, so please take the time to fill it out carefully.
* If you are a minor, please ensure your parent or guardian signs and dates the form.
