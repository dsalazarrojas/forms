# Covid 19 Waxing Boutique Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a waiver form for clients of a boutique or wellness center that offers waxing services. It is designed to collect important information about each client, including their contact details, waiver of liability, and consent for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **Client Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Contact Number** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field.
4. Select a **Date** for the services you are requesting.
5. In the **Activity Name** field, describe the specific waxing service you are requesting.
6. Sign your name in the **Client Signature** field to indicate your consent and understanding of the waiver.
7. A representative of the boutique will also sign the **Witness Signature** field to confirm they have read and explained the waiver to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Date**: Select the date for which you are scheduling the waxing service.
* **Activity Name**: Describe the specific waxing service you are requesting (e.g., eyebrow wax, face wax, etc.).
* **Client Signature**: Sign your name in this field to indicate your understanding and consent to the terms outlined in this waiver.
* **Witness Signature**: A representative of the boutique will sign this field to confirm they have explained the waiver to you and that you have understood the terms.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can provide you with your services as scheduled.
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please inform us prior to scheduling your appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this waiver, please don't hesitate to ask our staff.
