# Scouts COVID 19 Self Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Scouts COVID 19 Self-Assessment Form is a crucial tool for event organizers to report their status regarding COVID-19. This form helps event organizers assess their risk level and make informed decisions about their events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you are filling out the form for yourself and not for someone else.
3. Please note that some fields are required and must be filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Name**: Enter your name as the event organizer.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further correspondence.
* **Do you or anyone in your household currently have or have had symptoms?**: Answer this question truthfully. Select 'True' if you or anyone in your household is experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Have you or anyone in your household traveled outside your home in the last 14 days?**: Answer 'True' if you or anyone in your household have traveled outside your home in the last 14 days. Select 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you or anyone in your household come into contact with someone that has been diagnosed with COVID-19?**: Answer 'True' if you or anyone in your household have come into contact with someone that has been diagnosed with COVID-19. Select 'False' otherwise.
* **Are you or anyone in your household self-isolating?**: Answer 'True' if you or anyone in your household is self-isolating. Select 'False' otherwise.
* **Do you or anyone in your household have a medical condition that would make you more susceptible to COVID-19?**: Answer 'True' if you or anyone in your household has a medical condition that would make you more susceptible to COVID-19. Select 'False' otherwise.
* **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions for this form**: Select 'I Agree' to agree to the terms and conditions. Select 'I Do Not Agree' otherwise.
* **Electronic Signature**: This is not a field that requires an actual signature. Instead, it is a field to indicate that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions. Please select 'I Agree' in the field above to complete this field.
 
## Tips

- Make sure to answer truthfully and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, contact your local health organization for guidance.
- This form is not a medical diagnosis tool. If you have any concerns, please contact your local health organization or a medical professional.
