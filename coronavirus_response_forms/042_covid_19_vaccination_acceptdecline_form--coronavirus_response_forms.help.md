# COVID 19 Vaccination AcceptDecline Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your willingness to receive the COVID-19 vaccine, gathering information on demographic data, medical conditions, and vaccination preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date you are submitting this form.
2. Enter your first name, last name, and date of birth.
3. Choose your gender.
4. Enter your phone number and email address.
5. Provide your home address, city, state, and zip code.
6. Answer questions about your healthcare worker status, essential worker status, and if you have any underlying medical conditions.
7. Indicate if anyone in your household has high-risk medical conditions.
8. Confirm if you have previously tested positive for COVID-19.
9. Select if you want to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
10. If declining, select your reason for declining the vaccine.
11. (Optional) Provide more details about your medical contraindication.
12. (Optional) Select if you might be interested in vaccination in the future.
13. (Optional) Choose the information you need to make an informed decision.
14. Confirm if you have received COVID-19 vaccine education.
15. Confirm if you have been provided with vaccine information materials.
16. Indicate if all your questions were addressed.
17. (Optional) Provide any remaining questions you have.
18. Confirm if your decision is voluntary.
19. Confirm if you understand the implications of your decision.
20. Give permission to contact you for follow-up.
21. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Date** (`form_date`, date): The date you are submitting this form.
* **First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Home Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your home street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Healthcare Worker** (`healthcare_worker`, select_one, required): Confirm if you are a healthcare worker.
* **Essential Worker** (`essential_worker`, select_one, required): Confirm if you are an essential worker.
* **Underlying Conditions** (`underlying_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select if you have any of the listed high-risk medical conditions.
* **Household High-Risk** (`household_high_risk`, select_one, required): Confirm if anyone in your household has high-risk conditions.
* **Previous COVID-19 Infection** (`previous_covid_infection`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have previously tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Previous Infection Date** (`previous_infection_date`, date, not required): Enter the approximate date you tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Vaccine Decision** (`vaccination_decision`, select_one, required): Select if you want to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Decline Reason** (`decline_reason`, select_one, not required): If declining, select your reason for declining the vaccine.
* **Medical Contraindication Details** (`medical_contraindication_details`, text, not required): Provide more details about your medical contraindication.
* **Future Vaccination Interest** (`future_vaccination_interest`, select_one, not required): Select if you might be interested in vaccination in the future.
* **Information Needed** (`information_needed`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the information you need to make an informed decision.
* **Education Provided** (`education_provided`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have received COVID-19 vaccine education.
* **Education Materials Provided** (`education_materials_given`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have been provided with vaccine information materials.
* **Questions Addressed** (`questions_addressed`, select_one, required): Confirm if all your questions were addressed.
* **Remaining Questions** (`remaining_questions`, text, not required): Provide any remaining questions you have.
* **Voluntary Decision** (`decision_voluntary`, select_one, required): Confirm if your decision is voluntary.
* **Decision Understanding** (`decision_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm if you understand the implications of your decision.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, select_one, required): Give permission to contact you for follow-up.
* **Signature Date** (`patient_signature_date`, date, required): Sign and date the form.

Note:
The fields marked as "required" are mandatory. The fields without this mark are optional and only apply if you want to provide more information or context to your answer.

Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure healthcare professionals provide you with the best care. If you have questions or need help with a specific field, don't hesitate to reach out.
