# COVID 19 Vaccine Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for scheduling a COVID-19 vaccine appointment. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that you receive the correct vaccine and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, date of birth, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your preferred appointment date and time from the calendar.
3. Choose your preferred vaccine type if available.
4. If you have any accessibility needs, please select the corresponding options.
5. Enter your emergency contact details, including name and phone number.
6. Confirm that all provided information is accurate.
7. Choose your preferred method of appointment reminder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your current street address.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_appointment_date`, `date`, **required**): Select a date from the calendar for your preferred appointment date.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred appointment time slot from the options provided.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the type of appointment you need, such as First Dose, Second Dose, or Booster Dose.
* **Preferred Vaccination Location** (`vaccination_location`, `select_one`, **required**): Select a clinic location for your appointment.
* **Vaccine Preference** (`vaccine_preference`, `select_one`, **optional**): If available, choose your preferred vaccine type.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all the accessibility needs you require, such as Wheelchair Accessible Entrance or Sign Language Interpreter.
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, `select_one`, **optional**): If you need transportation assistance, select True.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the person to contact in an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your health insurance provider or indicate that you are uninsured.
* **Confirm Information** (`confirm_information`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm that all provided information is accurate.
* **Appointment Reminder** (`appointment_reminder`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose your preferred method of appointment reminder.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid errors.
* If you have any accessibility needs, please select all applicable options.
* If you need transportation assistance, select True.
* Review and confirm all provided information before submission.
* Choose your preferred method of appointment reminder for convenience.
