# Passenger Disclosure And Attestation To The United States Of America - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for passengers to disclose and attest to their travel information and reasons for visiting the United States of America, particularly in relation to the coronavirus response.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of your travel: Choose from the options "Coronavirus Response Forms" or "Other" to indicate the purpose of your visit.
2. Provide a brief description of your travel: Enter a short explanation of your trip, including the date, destination, and any relevant details.
3. Upload your output file: Select the file named "003_passenger_disclosure_and_attestation_to_the_united_states_of_america--coronavirus_response_forms.yaml" to upload your output file.
4. Enter assigned tool ID: Provide the ID of the tool assigned to you for this form.
5. Enter form ID: Enter the ID "80527" for this form.
6. Save and submit: Once you have completed all the required fields, click "Save and Submit" to complete the form.
