# neighborhood_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The neighborhood_contact_form is a contact form for block captains in the neighborhood. It allows users to provide their contact details, such as their name, phone number, and email, to the block captain for efficient communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your block captain from the options provided.
2. Enter your phone number and other contact details as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Block Captain** (`block_captain`, select_one, required): Select the block captain that you are in need of contact information from.
* **Block Captain Phone Number** (`block_captain_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that the block captain can contact you.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Neighborhood Address** (`neighborhood_address`, text, optional): Enter your neighborhood address for the block captain to reference.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter a message for the block captain to know your needs or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that the block captain can contact you via email.
* **Block Captain Name** (`block_captain_name`, text, optional): Enter the block captain's name if you know it.
