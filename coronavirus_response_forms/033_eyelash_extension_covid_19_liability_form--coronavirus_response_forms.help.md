# Eyelash Extension COVID 19 Liability Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers and employees regarding their COVID-19 liability waiver and response to the current pandemic. It is designed to ensure that both parties are aware of and agree to the necessary precautions and protocols to be followed during the eyelash extension session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate response for each question on page 1 of the form, either "Yes" or "No".
2. On page 3, fill out the detailed COVID-19 liability waiver form, providing all required information.
3. On page 4, fill out the customer information form with your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
4. On page 5, select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have read and understood the liability waiver.
5. On page 6, select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you are aware of and agree to follow the necessary precautions and protocols during the eyelash extension session.
6. On page 7, enter your saloon license number (if applicable).
7. On page 8, enter the name of the employee performing the eyelash extension session.
8. On page 9, enter the phone number of the employee performing the eyelash extension session.
9. On page 10, enter the license number of the employee performing the eyelash extension session (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coronavirus Response Forms** (`coronavirus_response_forms_form_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select "Yes" if you have had any symptoms of COVID-19 or have been exposed to someone with COVID-19 in the past two weeks. If you select "No", please confirm that you are not experiencing any symptoms and have not been exposed.
* **Salon Forms** (`salon_form_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select "Yes" if you have had any contact with a person with COVID-19 or have traveled to an area with a high COVID-19 risk. If you select "No", please confirm that you have not had any contact and have not traveled to a high-risk area.
* **A detailed COVID 19 liability waiver to be filled out by your customers** (`eyelash_extension_covid_19_liability_waiver_form_1`, note, required: false): This is a detailed form that you must fill out, providing information about your health and any potential risks associated with the eyelash extension session.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info_1`, text, required: false): Please fill out your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signoff_1`, select_one, required: false): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understood the liability waiver.
* **Customer Signature 2** (`customer_signoff_2`, select_one, required: false): Please select "Yes" to confirm that you are aware of and agree to follow the necessary precautions and protocols during the eyelash extension session.
* **Saloon License** (`saloon_license_1`, text, required: false): If you have a saloon license, please enter the number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name_1`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee performing the eyelash extension session.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone_1`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the employee performing the eyelash extension session.
* **Employee License** (`employee_license_1`, text, required: false): If the employee has a license, please enter the number.
