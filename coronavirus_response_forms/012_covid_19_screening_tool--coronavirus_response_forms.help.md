# Covid 19 Screening Tool - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Screening Tool is a form designed to collect information about your health status, exposure risks, and symptoms. This information will help us assess your risk of infection and provide guidance on next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

1. **User Info**: Enter your name and contact information so we can contact you with the results of your screening.
2. **Date Screened**: Select the date you first experienced symptoms or were exposed to COVID-19.
3. **Screening Location**: Enter the location where you were screened or first experienced symptoms.
4. **Contact Person**: If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the person who screened you or can be contacted on your behalf.
5. **Contact Relationship**: Select how you know the contact person (e.g., "Spouse", "Parent", "Healthcare Provider", etc.).
6. **Symptoms**: Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list.
7. **Other Symptoms**: If you have symptoms not listed, provide them here.
8. **Travel History**: Share your travel history, including any recent travel to COVID-19 affected areas.
9. **Healthcare Worker**: If you are a healthcare worker, select "Yes" to confirm.
10. **Exposure Risk**: Select "Yes" if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19.
11. **Travel Exposure Risk**: Select "Yes" if you have traveled to a COVID-19 affected area.
12. **Symptoms Duration**: Enter how long you have been experiencing symptoms (e.g., hours, days, weeks).
13. **Fever/Fever**: Enter the number of times you have had a fever or experienced feverish feelings.
14. **Cough**: Enter the number of times you have coughed.
15. **Shortness of Breath**: Enter the number of times you have experienced shortness of breath.
16. **Headache**: Enter the number of times you have experienced a headache.
17. **Fatigue**: Enter the number of times you have experienced fatigue.
18. **Sore Throat**: Enter the number of times you have experienced a sore throat.
19. **Muscle Pain**: Enter the number of times you have experienced muscle pain.
20. **Diarrhea**: Enter the number of times you have experienced diarrhea.
21. **Abdominal Pain**: Enter the number of times you have experienced abdominal pain.
22. **Nausea**: Enter the number of times you have experienced nausea.
23. **Vomiting**: Enter the number of times you have experienced vomiting.
24. **Rash**: Enter the number of times you have experienced a rash.
25. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your health status.
