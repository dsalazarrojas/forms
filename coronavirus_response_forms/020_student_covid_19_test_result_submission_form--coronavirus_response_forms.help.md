# Student COVID 19 Test Result Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student COVID 19 Test Result Submission Form is a digital form designed to collect and document COVID-19 test results of students. It is intended to facilitate the tracking and management of test results.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student ID.
2. Select the date of the test.
3. Choose the test result from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the test result.
5. Enter the student's name.
6. Enter the student's email address.
7. Enter the teacher's email address.
8. Select the date of the test result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (1)**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Test Date (2)**: Select the date the test was conducted.
* **Test Result (3)**: Choose the result of the test from the dropdown menu:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Pending
* **Student Name (4)**: Enter the name of the student who was tested.
* **Test Result Notes (5)**: Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the test result.
* **Student Email (6)**: Enter the email address of the student who was tested.
* **Teacher Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the teacher or staff member who administered the test.
* **Test Result Date (8)**: Select the date the test result was received.
