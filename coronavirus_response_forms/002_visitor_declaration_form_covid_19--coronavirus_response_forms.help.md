# Visitor Declaration Form COVID 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Declaration Form COVID 19 is used to collect information about visitors arriving on the premise to track their health status and ensure their safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Arrive at**: Enter the time and date the visitor arrived at the location.
2. **Visitor Name**: Enter the name of the visitor as per the visitor's identity document or badge.
3. **Employee Name** (if applicable): Enter the name of the employee the visitor is visiting, if applicable.
4. **Employee Phone Number** (if applicable): Enter the phone number of the employee the visitor is visiting, if applicable.
5. **Reason for Visit**: Briefly describe the purpose of the visitor's visit.
6. **Country of Origin**: Select the country of the visitor's origin from the dropdown options.
7. **Last Contacted Countries**: Select all countries the visitor has recently been in contact with. This includes the country of their origin.
8. **Last Contacted Details**: Provide any additional information about the visited countries.
9. **Visited Countries**: Select all countries the visitor has visited recently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Arrive at**: The time and date the visitor arrived at the location.
* **Visitor Name**: The name of the visitor as per their identity document or badge.
* **Employee Name** (if applicable): The name of the employee the visitor is visiting, if applicable.
* **Employee Phone Number** (if applicable): The phone number of the employee the visitor is visiting, if applicable.
* **Reason for Visit**: A brief description of the purpose of the visitor's visit.
* **Country of Origin**: The country of the visitor's origin.
* **Last Contacted Countries**: Select all countries the visitor has recently been in contact with, including their country of origin.
* **Last Contacted Details**: Any additional information about the visited countries.
* **Visited Countries**: Select all countries the visitor has visited recently.

## Tips
- Please complete this form accurately and truthfully.
- If the visitor's visit is extended, update the form accordingly.
- If a visitor leaves the premise, update their last contacted countries field to reflect this change.
- If a visitor is unable to complete the form for any reason, contact the relevant authorities for guidance and support.
