# Negative COVID 19 Test Reporting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report negative COVID-19 test results. Please fill out this form if you have been tested for COVID-19 and received a negative result.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form title at the top.
2. Enter your first name and last name.
3. Enter the date of the test.
4. Enter the date of the test result.
5. Select the test result as "Negative".
6. Enter the test location.
7. Check the symptoms you experienced.
8. Click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Negative COVID 19 Test Reporting Form**: This is the title of the form. You can fill it out to help identify the purpose of the form.
* **first_name**: Enter your first name here.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Date of Test**: Enter the date you were tested for COVID-19.
* **Date of Result**: Enter the date you received your test result.
* **Test Result**: Select "Negative" if your test result is negative.
* **Test Location**: Enter the location where you were tested for COVID-19.
* **Symptoms**: Check the symptoms you experienced if any. Possible symptoms include:
	+ Fever
	+ Sore Throat
	+ Cough
	+ Runny Nose/Headache
	+ Shortness of Breath
	+ Fatigue

Note: If you select "None" in the Test Result field, it is assumed that the test result is unknown.
