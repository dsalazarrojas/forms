# Pre Festival Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Festival Survey is designed to gather information from participants and attendees of the festival to ensure their health and safety. This form is intended to identify potential risks and take necessary measures to prevent the spread of infectious diseases. Your cooperation is essential in helping us protect you and our staff. Please note that your answers will be kept confidential and will not be shared with anyone without your explicit permission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly, as it may relate to your health and well-being.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
3. Be sure to select all applicable options in the select fields.
4. Enter accurate and complete information in the text fields.
5. If you have any medical information or conditions, please include this in the medical_condition field.
6. If you are experiencing symptoms or traveling, please answer the symptom and travel questions accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival ID** (1): Enter your unique festival identifier if you know it. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Participant Name** (2): Enter your name as it appears on your festival ticket or registration.
* **Contact Number** (3): Your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Email ID** (4): Your email address if you have one.
* **Symptoms** (5): If you are experiencing any symptoms, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Tested Positive** (6): If you have tested positive for COVID-19, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Vaccinated** (7): If you are fully vaccinated for COVID-19, select 'vaccinated'. Otherwise, select 'not vaccinated'.
* **Fever Felt** (8): If you have a fever, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Travel History** (9): Enter any travel history, if applicable.
* **Travel Restrictions** (10): Select all applicable restrictions you may have, such as travel bans or quarantines.
* **Mask Worn** (11): If you plan to wear a mask, select 'worn'. Otherwise, select 'not worn'.
* **Distance Measures** (12): Select all applicable distance measures you have implemented, such as social distancing or physical distancing.
* **Time** (13): If you have any time-sensitive information, such as travel times or festival schedule, enter it here.
* **Date** (14): Enter the date you arrived or traveled to the festival location.
* **Comments** (15): Any additional comments or information about your health or travel history.
* **Festival Location** (16): Enter the festival location you are attending.
* **Travel From** (17): If you are traveling from another location, enter it here.
* **Contact Person** (18): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Phone** (19): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Email** (20): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition** (21): If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Medical Info** (22): Enter any additional medical information.
* **Travel Reason** (23): If you are traveling for a reason, such as for work or medical, enter it here.
* **Festival Date** (24): Enter the date of the festival you are attending.
