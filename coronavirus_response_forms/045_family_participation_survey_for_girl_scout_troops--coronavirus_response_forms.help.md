# Family Participation Survey For Girl Scout Troops - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help Girl Scout troops understand the level of participation from families regarding in-person activities. It collects information on comfort levels and preferences for various aspects of troop meetings and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of meetings you are comfortable with: in person or virtual.
2. Choose which activities you prefer:
	* Indoor
	* Outdoor
	* Virtual
3. Provide any additional comments about your preferences.
4. Enter your email address (optional).
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Rate your comfort level with in-person activities:
	* Not Comfortable
	* Somewhat Comfortable
	* Comfortable
	* Very Comfortable
	* Extremely Comfortable
7. Choose the frequency of troop meetings:
	* Regular
	* As scheduled
	* Less frequently
8. Indicate how often you participate in activities:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Occasionally
9. Provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **In Person Meetings** (`in_person_meetings`): Select the type of meetings you are comfortable with: `Meeting` or `Virtual`.
* **Level of Comfort** (`level_of_comfort`): Rate your comfort level with in-person activities from the options provided.
* **Activities** (`activities`): Choose which activities you prefer: `Indoor`, `Outdoor`, or `Virtual`.
* **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`): Rate your comfort level with in-person activities from the options provided.
* **Troop Meetings** (`troop_meetings`): Choose the frequency of troop meetings: `Regular`, `As scheduled`, or `Less frequently`.
* **Activities Frequency** (`activities_frequency`): Indicate how often you participate in activities: `Daily`, `Weekly`, or `Occasionally`.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to the troop leaders or administrators.
