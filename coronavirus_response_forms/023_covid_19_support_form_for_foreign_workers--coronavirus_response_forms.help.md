# COVID 19 Support Form For Foreign Workers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your COVID-19 experience, employment status, and personal details to help us better understand your needs and provide support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read each section carefully**: Each section has a specific purpose, and we recommend reading the explanations below to understand what is required.
2. **Fill out all required fields**: Each field with a red star icon is required. Please make sure to fill out these fields to ensure accurate data collection.
3. **Be as detailed as possible**: If you have any additional information or concerns you'd like to share, use the "Other needs or concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date**: Enter the date you submitted this application.
* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Country of Origin**: Select your home country.
* **Native Language**: Select your primary language.
* **Do you need an interpreter?**: Indicate if you require language assistance.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email (optional).
* **Current Address**: Enter your current street address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State**: Enter the state of your residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Current Visa or Work Status**: Select your current immigration status.
* **Current or Most Recent Employer**: Enter your current or most recent employer's name.
* **Employer Address**: Enter your employer's address (optional).
* **Type of Work**: Select your work industry.
* **Current Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
* **How has COVID-19 impacted your work situation?**: Select all applicable impacts.
* **What type of assistance do you need?**: Select all applicable types of assistance.
* **Household Size**: Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Number of Dependents**: Enter the number of dependents.
* **Monthly Household Income**: Enter your monthly household income before taxes (optional).
* **Do you have health insurance?**: Select your insurance status.
* **Are you currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms?**: Indicate if you're experiencing symptoms.
* **Describe your symptoms**: Provide details about your symptoms (optional).
* **Describe your current housing situation**: Provide details about your living conditions (optional).
* **Are there any other needs or concerns you'd like to share?**: Share any additional information.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.
