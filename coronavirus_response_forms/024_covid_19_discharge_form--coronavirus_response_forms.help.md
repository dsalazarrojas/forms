# COVID 19 Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Discharge Form is a document used to record and document a patient's discharge information after receiving medical treatment related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fields carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided below.
3. Verify your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification or medical record.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's ID or medical record number as provided by the hospital or healthcare provider.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (optional): Enter any emergency contact information for the patient, including name, phone number, and relationship.

### Hospitalization Details
* **Date of Admission**: Enter the date the patient was admitted to the hospital.
* **Date of Discharge**: Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital.
* **Hospital Name**: Enter the name of the hospital where the patient was treated.
* **Treating Physician Name**: Enter the name of the treating physician.

### Clinical Status at Discharge
* **Oxygen Requirement at Discharge**: Select the level of oxygen requirement for the patient at the time of discharge.
* **Recovery Status**: Select the patient's recovery status at discharge.
* **Vaccination Status at Discharge**: Select the patient's vaccination status at discharge.

### Post-Discharge Instructions
* **Isolation Period Required**: Select the length of isolation period required for the patient.
* **Activity Restrictions**: Select the level of activity restrictions for the patient.
* **Follow-up Appointment Scheduled**: Select whether a follow-up appointment has been scheduled for the patient.
* **Follow-up Appointment Date**: If a follow-up appointment has been scheduled, enter the date of the appointment.
* **Medications Prescribed**: Enter any medications prescribed for the patient at discharge.
* **Discharge Instructions Understood**: Select whether the patient understands the discharge instructions.

### Discharge Instructions
* **Discharge Signature Date**: Enter the date the patient was discharged.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (optional): Enter any emergency contact information for the patient, including name, phone number, and relationship.
