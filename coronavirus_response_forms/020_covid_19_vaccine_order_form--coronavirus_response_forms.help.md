# COVID 19 Vaccine Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from healthcare facilities about their COVID-19 vaccine order needs. It is intended for use by facilities that require vaccines for administration or distribution to their staff, patients, or the broader community. Completing this form helps ensure accurate and efficient ordering of vaccines, which is crucial for public health and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Section 1: Facility Information**
	* Provide your facility's name, address, and contact information.
	* Select the type of facility (e.g., hospital, clinic, pharmacy, etc.).
	* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
2. **Section 2: Contact Person**
	* Enter the name and title of the person placing the order.
	* Provide a direct phone number and email address for communication.
3. **Section 3: Vaccine Order Details**
	* Select the COVID-19 vaccine product(s) required.
	* Specify the number of doses needed.
	* Choose the vaccine formulation (e.g., adult, pediatric, etc.).
	* Indicate if you have mixed products ordered.
4. **Section 4: Delivery Details**
	* Provide the delivery address where the vaccines should be sent.
	* Choose a preferred delivery date.
5. **Section 5: Administration and Capacity**
	* Confirm you have the appropriate cold chain equipment for storage.
	* Specify the number of trained vaccine administrators available.
	* Indicate your daily vaccination capacity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: This is your healthcare facility's name, which will help us associate your order with your organization.
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of healthcare facility you represent (e.g., hospital, clinic, pharmacy, etc.).
* **Facility Address**: Enter your facility's street address for delivery and billing purposes.
* **City, State, ZIP Code**: Provide your location information for accurate delivery.
* **Facility Phone Number**: Enter your main contact phone number.
* **Facility Email Address**: Enter your main contact email address.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title or position of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone**: Enter a direct phone number for the contact person.
* **Contact Person Email**: Enter a direct email address for the contact person.
* **Vaccine Product Requested**: Select the COVID-19 vaccine product(s) needed.
* **Number of Vaccine Doses Requested**: Specify the number of doses required.
* **Vaccine Strength**: Choose the vaccine formulation (e.g., adult, pediatric, etc.).
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where vaccines should be delivered.
* **Preferred Delivery Date**: Choose a preferred delivery date.
* **Cold Storage Capacity - Doses**: If you have space for cold storage, specify the number of doses you can store.
* **Cold Chain Equipment**: Confirm if you have the necessary cold storage equipment (e.g., ultra-cold freezers, etc.).
* **Trained Administrators**: Specify the number of staff trained for vaccination administration.
* **Daily Vaccination Capacity - Doses**: Indicate your daily vaccination capacity.
* **I Authorize This Vaccine Order**: Confirm that you are authorized to place this order.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* Double-check your contact information for correct email and phone numbers.
* Be specific when selecting vaccine products and formulations.
* Confirm your delivery address and preferred delivery date.
* Review your vaccination administration details carefully.
* Review and confirm your order before submission.
