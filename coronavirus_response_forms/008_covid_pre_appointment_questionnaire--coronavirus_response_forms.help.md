# Covid Pre Appointment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals before their appointment at our facility to ensure a smooth and safe experience for everyone. Please provide the required information to help us prepare for your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the first field. This is required information to identify you.
2. Enter your **Contact Number** in the second field. This will help us contact you if we need to reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field. This is optional, but it will help us contact you with any important updates or information about your appointment.
4. If you have an **Age**, please enter it in the fourth field. This is an optional field, but it will help us tailor our communication to you.
5. Answer the question about **Medical Condition** by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No' in the fifth field. This will help us prepare for any necessary precautions or accommodations.
6. For the **Medical History** question, select all that apply in the sixth field. This will help us understand any relevant medical history that may impact your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Contact Number** (number, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached by phone or SMS.
- **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached by email.
- **Age** (number, optional): If you are 18 or older, please enter your age to help us tailor our communication.
- **Medical Condition** (select_one, optional): If you have a medical condition that may impact your appointment, select 'Yes' to let us know.
- **Medical History** (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to provide a comprehensive medical history for our records.
