# Outdoor Dining Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Outdoor Dining Application Form is used to request and manage outdoor dining operations during the COVID-19 pandemic. It gathers information necessary for authorities to determine the feasibility and approval of such requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the provided options for the payment type and choose the one that applies to you.
2.  Enter the date and time of your planned outdoor dining event.
3.  Provide the name of the host establishment.
4.  Enter the address of the host establishment.
5.  Enter the phone number of the host establishment.
6.  Enter the email of the host establishment.
7.  If applicable, enter the payment amount and payment method used for the outdoor dining event.
8.  Add any additional notes or descriptions about the event, such as a detailed plan or any specific requirements.
9.  Enter any additional notes or comments you have regarding the outdoor dining event.
10. Enter your name and contact information so that we can follow up with you about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Payment Type** (`payment_type`): Select the payment type for your outdoor dining request. You can choose from the available options, and this will help the authorities determine the payment method that needs to be used.
*   **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`): Enter the date of your planned outdoor dining event.
*   **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`): Enter the time of your planned outdoor dining event.
*   **Host Name** (`host_name`): Enter the name of the host establishment.
*   **Host Address** (`host_address`): Enter the address of the host establishment.
*   **Host Phone** (`host_phone`): Enter the phone number of the host establishment.
*   **Host Email** (`host_email`): Enter the email of the host establishment.
*   **Host Alternate Phone** (`host_phone2`): If the host establishment has an alternate phone number, enter it here.
*   **Host Alternate Email** (`host_email2`): If the host establishment has an alternate email, enter it here.
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): If applicable, enter the payment amount for your outdoor dining event.
*   **Description** (`description`): Add a description or detailed plan of your outdoor dining event.
*   **Additional Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments you have regarding the outdoor dining event.
*   **Additional Notes 2** (`notes2`): Enter any additional information or comments regarding the outdoor dining event.
*   **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`): Enter your name so that we can follow up with you about your application.
*   **Form Submitter Email** (`form_submitter_email`): Enter your email so that we can contact you about your application.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select all applicable payment methods used for your outdoor dining event. You can choose from the available options.
*   **Status** (`reservation_status`): Enter the status of your outdoor dining event.
*   **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Enter the payment status of your outdoor dining event.
*   **Host Email** (`host_email`): Enter the email of the host establishment.
*   **Host Phone** (`host_phone`): Enter the phone number of the host establishment.

## Tips

*   Always enter accurate and complete information in each field to avoid delays or errors in processing your application.
*   Ensure you understand the payment methods and amounts before submitting your application.
*   Review the status of your application regularly for updates.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about your application, contact the relevant authority or the form submitter.
