# Precautionary Coronavirus Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Precautionary Coronavirus Liability Release Form is a mandatory document for customers to confirm their understanding and acceptance of service terms and precautionary conditions related to the coronavirus. This form helps businesses protect themselves by ensuring customers acknowledge and accept the terms, reducing potential liabilities and disputes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the service terms and precautionary conditions provided. If you have any questions, please contact us.
2. Select the statement that best reflects your understanding of the service terms and precautionary conditions from the options provided.
3. Review and submit the form to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Terms and Precautionary Conditions** (`coronavirus_service_terms`, text, required): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or questions you have about the service terms and precautionary conditions. Please use this space to clarify any doubts or concerns you may have.
* **Precautionary Conditions** (`coronavirus_precautionary_conditions`, text, required): Describe any precautionary measures you are taking or would like to take to prevent the potential risks associated with the coronavirus.
* **Customer Consent** (`customer_consent`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to confirm that you have read and understood the service terms and precautionary conditions. You can select one of the two provided options to indicate your understanding.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, optional): This field is for you to provide your signature as proof of your understanding and acceptance of the service terms and precautionary conditions. Please note that this field is not required.

## Tips

* Please review the service terms and precautionary conditions carefully before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form must be completed honestly and accurately to ensure that your rights and responsibilities are clear.
