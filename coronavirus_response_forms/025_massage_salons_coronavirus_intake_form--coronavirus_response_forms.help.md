# Massage Salons Coronavirus Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess and manage the risk of coronavirus (COVID-19) transmission in massage salons. It collects information about client interactions, potential symptoms, and exposure risks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you complete all necessary fields.
2. Provide accurate and honest information about client interactions and potential symptoms.
3. Select relevant options from dropdown fields to indicate client status and any exposure risks.
4. Enter dates and times according to your system's date and time format.
5. Complete all required fields to ensure accurate and effective assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Details** (`salon_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your salon, including its name, address, and contact information.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the client, such as name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your salon's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the client's current health status by selecting one or more of the following options:
	+ Healthy
	+ Not Healthy
	+ Underlying Condition
	+ Underlying Condition with a Fever
	+ Underlying Condition with Fever and Shortness of Breath
	+ Underlying Condition with Fever, Shortness of Breath, and Chest Pain
	+ Hospitalized with COVID-19
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms the client has experienced, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Recent Travel** (`recent_travel`, text, optional): If the client has recently traveled, provide details about their travel history, including destination and dates of travel.
* **Recent Interaction** (`recent_interaction`, text, optional): Describe any recent interactions with the client, including phone calls, emails, or in-person visits.
* **Exposure Risk** (`exposure_risk`, text, optional): Indicate any potential exposure risks, such as contact with someone who has COVID-19 or recent travel to a high-risk area.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical conditions the client has, such as allergies or chronic illnesses.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the client's vaccination status:
	+ Not Vaccinated
	+ Partially Vaccinated
	+ Fully Vaccinated
	+ Vaccinated and Boosted
* **COVID-19 Test Results** (`covid19_test_results`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the client's COVID-19 test results:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Unknown
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms_2`, text, optional): Describe any additional symptoms the client is experiencing.
* **Test Result Date** (`test_result_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the client received their COVID-19 test results.
* **Test Result Time** (`test_result_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the client received their COVID-19 test results.
* **Medical Attention Required** (`medical_attention_required`, select_one, optional): Indicate if medical attention is required:
	+ False
	+ True
* **Medical Attention Provided** (`medical_attention_provided`, select_one, optional): Indicate if medical attention has been provided:
	+ False
	+ True
* **Follow-Up Visit** (`follow_up_visit`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of the follow-up visit:
	+ Not Scheduled
	+ Scheduled
	+ Completed
* **Follow-Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the follow-up visit.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the client's case.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name or title.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was deleted.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated by** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name or title of who last updated the form.
