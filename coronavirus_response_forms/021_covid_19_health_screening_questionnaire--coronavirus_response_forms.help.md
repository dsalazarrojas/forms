# COVID 19 Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your health status, exposure history, and risk factors related to COVID-19. It will guide you through a series of questions to help identify potential risks and provide recommendations for further action.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Personal Information
- Complete the following fields:
  - **Full Name** (`name`): Enter your full name as per your official identification.
  - **Age** (`age`): Enter your age in numerical format.
  - **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  - **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Step 2: Screening Information
- Choose the correct answers for the following questions:
  - **Do you have a fever or high temperature?** (`fever`): Answer 'True' if you have a fever or a high temperature, 'False' otherwise.
  - **Do you have a new or persistent cough?** (`cough`): Answer 'True' if you have a new or persistent cough, 'False' otherwise.
  - **Do you have shortness of breath?** (`breathlessness`): Answer 'True' if you have shortness of breath, 'False' otherwise.
  - **Unusual tiredness or fatigue?** (`fatigue`): Answer 'True' if you have unusual tiredness or fatigue, 'False' otherwise.
  - **Loss of taste or smell?** (`loss_sense`): Answer 'True' if you have lost taste or smell, 'False' otherwise.
  - **Sore throat or difficulty swallowing?** (`sore_throat`): Answer 'True' if you have a sore throat or difficulty swallowing, 'False' otherwise.
  - **Close contact with confirmed COVID-19 case?** (`close_contact`): Answer 'True' if you have close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case, 'False' otherwise. If unsure, select 'Unknown'.

### Step 3: Risk Factors
- Choose the correct answers for the following questions:
  - **Do you work in a healthcare setting?** (`work_healthcare`): Answer 'True' if you work in a healthcare setting, 'False' otherwise.
  - **Pre-existing medical conditions?** (`underlying_conditions`): Answer 'True' if you have pre-existing medical conditions, 'False' otherwise. If you have conditions, enter a brief description in the text field below.
  - **Immunocompromised?** (`immunocompromised`): Answer 'True' if you are immunocompromised, 'False' otherwise.
  - **Vaccination Status** (`vaccines`): Choose from Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated, or Unknown.

### Step 4: Screening Recommendation
- Choose the recommended action based on your answers:
  - Select the recommended action:
    - **Home Quarantine Recommended**
    - **Monitor for symptoms**
    - **Seek Medical Evaluation**
    - **No Action Needed**

### Tips
- Ensure you answer all questions truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any question, select 'Unknown' as an answer.
- Enter your information accurately and thoroughly.
- If you need help or clarification, contact us at [insert contact information].
