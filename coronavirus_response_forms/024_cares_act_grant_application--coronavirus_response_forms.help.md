# CARES Act Grant Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CARES Act Grant Application form is designed to support arts organizations in Orange County, primarily serving communities of color. It is intended for organizations that have been impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic and are seeking targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form in sections, starting with your organization's identity and contact information.
3. Describe your organization's mission, its impact on communities of color, and how the grant will help sustain it.
4. Provide detailed information about the financial impact of COVID-19 on your operations.
5. Specify the exact amount of grant funding you are requesting.
6. Explain how you plan to use the funds to address the pandemic's impact.
7. Report the number of staff and artists affected by the pandemic.
8. Confirm your organization's status as a registered 501(c)(3) and its physical location in Orange County.
9. Provide your organization's total annual operating budget before the pandemic.
10. Certify that all information provided is true and accurate.
11. Submit the form with the exact date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Name of Organization** (`legal_org_name`):
	+ This field is required to be filled out accurately, as it is the official name of your organization.
* **Federal Tax ID (EIN)** (`org_ein`):
	+ Enter your organization's 9-digit EIN number.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_person`):
	+ This is the person responsible for this application.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email_org`):
	+ Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
* **Organization Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`):
	+ Describe your organization's mission and purpose in a few sentences.
* **Does your organization primarily serve communities of color?** (`demographic_focus`):
	+ Select "True" if your organization's primary audience is communities of color.
* **Describe the financial impact of COVID-19 on your operations** (`covid_impact_narrative`):
	+ Provide detailed information about the financial impact of the pandemic on your organization.
* **Requested Grant Amount** (`requested_grant_amount`):
	+ Enter the exact amount of grant funding you are requesting, up to $25,000.
* **Planned Use of Funds** (`budget_utilization_plan`):
	+ Explain how the grant funds will be used to address the pandemic's impact.
* **Number of staff/artists affected by the pandemic** (`number_of_staff_affected`):
	+ Report the number of staff and artists affected by the pandemic.
* **Is your organization a registered 501(c)(3)?** (`non_profit_status_verification`):
	+ Select "True" if your organization is a registered 501(c)(3).
* **Is your organization physically located in Orange County?** (`orange_county_location`):
	+ Select "True" if your organization is located in Orange County.
* **Total Annual Operating Budget (Pre-COVID)** (`annual_operating_budget`):
	+ Enter your organization's total annual operating budget before the pandemic.
* **I certify that all information provided is true and accurate** (`certification_of_accuracy`):
	+ Select "I Certify" to confirm that all information is accurate.
* **Application Date** (`application_submission_date`):
	+ Enter the exact date of application.
