# covid_19_health_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information related to COVID-19 health concerns, specifically employee's health status, contact history, travel history, and symptom manifestation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information for each section:
	* Employee Information: Provide basic employee information (ID number, name, department, etc.)
	* Symptoms: Select all applicable COVID-19 symptoms that you are experiencing (e.g., Fever, Cough, Shortness of breath, etc.)
	* Contact History: Select all applicable contact history (e.g., family, friend, coworker, etc.)
	* Travel History: Select all applicable travel history (e.g., domestic, international, multiple countries)
	* Recent International Travel History:
		+ Enter recent international travel history in the text field.
	* Countries Visited: (Not required)
	* Symptoms Onset: Enter the date you first noticed your symptoms.
	* Symptoms Duration: Enter the duration of your symptoms in days.
	* Employee Health: Provide a brief description of your current health status.
	* Employee Status: Provide a brief description of your current status (e.g., working from home, on leave, etc.).
	* Location: Enter your current location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (Employee ID, Name, Department, etc.): Enter your basic employee information.
* **Symptoms** (Fever, Cough, Shortness of breath, etc.): Select all applicable COVID-19 symptoms that you are experiencing.
* **Contact History** (Family, Friend, Coworker, etc.): Select all applicable contact history.
* **Travel History** (Domestic, International, Multiple Countries, etc.): Select all applicable travel history.
* **Recent International Travel History**: Enter recent international travel history in the text field.
* **Countries Visited**: Select the countries you have visited (Not required).
* **Symptoms Onset**: Enter the date you first noticed your symptoms.
* **Symptoms Duration**: Enter the duration of your symptoms in days.
* **Employee Health**: Provide a brief description of your current health status.
* **Employee Status**: Provide a brief description of your current status (e.g., working from home, on leave, etc.).
* **Location**: Enter your current location.
