# COVID 19 Vaccine Intake Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for COVID-19 vaccination purposes. It captures essential details to ensure a safe and efficient vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name*: The patient's full name, as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth*: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age*: Enter the patient's age in years.
* **Gender*: Select the patient's gender.
* **Email Address*: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number*: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Street Address*: Enter the patient's street address.
* **City*: Enter the patient's city.
* **State*: Enter the patient's state.
* **Zip Code*: Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Chronic health conditions*: Describe any ongoing medical conditions the patient has.
* **Current medications*: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Known allergies*: List any known allergies the patient has.
* **Allergies to vaccine components*: Select if the patient has any allergies to vaccine components.
* **Immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy*: Select if the patient is immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy.
* **Pregnant or planning pregnancy*: Select if the patient is pregnant or planning pregnancy.
* **Breastfeeding*: Select if the patient is breastfeeding.
* **Prior COVID-19 vaccination doses*: Enter the number of prior COVID-19 vaccination doses the patient has received.
* **Type of prior vaccine*: Describe the type of prior vaccine received.
* **Date of last dose*: Enter the date of the last vaccine dose received.
* **Reactions to prior vaccines*: Describe any reactions to prior vaccines.
* **Prior COVID-19 diagnosis*: Select if the patient has a prior COVID-19 diagnosis.
* **Date of diagnosis*: Enter the date of the patient's prior COVID-19 diagnosis.
* **Currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms*: Select if the patient is experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Currently have a fever*: Select if the patient is currently experiencing a fever.
* **Informed consent*: Select to give informed consent for COVID-19 vaccination.
* **I understand benefits and risks*: Select to acknowledge understanding the benefits and risks of the COVID-19 vaccination.
* **All my questions have been answered*: Select to indicate that all questions have been answered.
* **I understand I can refuse without penalty*: Select to acknowledge understanding the right to refuse the vaccination.
* **Date of consent*: Enter the date of consent.
* **Prior COVID-19 vaccination history*: Please do not fill out this section if the patient has received a prior COVID-19 vaccination.

Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
