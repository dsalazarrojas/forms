# COVID 19 Vaccine Declination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare your willingness or unwillingness to receive the COVID-19 vaccine. It will gather information about your details, declination reasons, and acceptance of vaccination policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name, ID number, and date of birth.
2. Provide your email address and phone number.
3. If you have an employer or organization, enter their name and department or division.
4. Select the reason for declination and provide an explanation (if applicable).
5. Acknowledge the risks of not being vaccinated and understand your employer's vaccination policies.
6. Indicate if you may reconsider vaccination in the future.
7. Confirm your willingness to comply with testing protocols, masking requirements, and physical distancing if applicable.
8. Release your employer and healthcare provider from liability.
9. Confirm that this declination is voluntary and not coerced.
10. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee or Individual Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number as issued by your employer.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Employer or Organization Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current or former employer or organization.
* **Department or Division** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or division you belong to.
* **Job Title or Position** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Declination Statement** (`declination_statement`, select_one, required): Choose whether you agree or disagree with the declination statement.
* **Primary Reason for Declination** (`declination_reason`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided reasons for declination and provide an explanation (if applicable).
* **Explain Reason for Declination** (`reason_explanation`, text, optional): Provide an explanation for your chosen reason for declination.
* **Acknowledgment of Risks** (`acknowledge_risks`, select_one, required): Acknowledge the risks of not being vaccinated.
* **Understand Policies** (`understand_policies`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your employer's vaccination policies and consequences.
* **May Reconsider Vaccination in the Future** (`future_vaccination`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you may reconsider vaccination in the future.
* **Testing Protocols** (`willing_testing`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are willing to comply with testing protocols if applicable.
* **Masking Requirements** (`willing_masking`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are willing to comply with masking requirements if needed.
* **Physical Distancing** (`willing_distancing`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are willing to comply with physical distancing if required.
* **Liability Release** (`release_liability`, select_one, required): Release your employer and healthcare provider from liability.
* **Voluntary Declination** (`voluntary_declination`, select_one, required): Confirm that this declination is voluntary and not coerced.
* **Copy for Employee** (`copy_for_employee`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you want a copy of this declination form.
* **Date of Declination** (`signature_date`, date, required): Sign and date the form.
* **Witness or Healthcare Provider Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness or healthcare provider who signed the form.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Make sure to sign and date the form before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR department or healthcare provider.
