# COVID 19 Vaccine Clinic Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from patients before administering the COVID-19 vaccine. The information collected will help ensure a safe and effective vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and confirm the information you provide before submission.
4. If you have any questions or doubts, please reach out to your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name (Required)**: Enter your full name.
* **Check-In Time (Required)**: Select the time you plan to check in for your appointment.
* **Check-In Date (Required)**: Select the date you plan to check in for your appointment.
* **-- Appointment Information -- (Optional)**: This section allows you to provide any additional information about your appointment.
* **Scheduled Appointment Time (Required)**: Select the time of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Type (Required)**: Select the type of appointment you have (First dose, Second dose, Booster dose, Single dose vaccine, or Johnson and Johnson).
* **-- Confirmation and Screening -- (Optional)**: This section allows you to confirm or provide any additional information about your appointment.
* **Is your appointment information correct (Required)**: Confirm that the appointment information you provided is accurate.
* **Completed pre-appointment health screening (Required)**: Confirm that you have completed the pre-appointment health screening.
* **-- Health Screening Results -- (Optional)**: This section allows you to provide any additional health screening results.
* **Do you currently have a fever (Required)**: Confirm whether you currently have a fever.
* **Experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms today (Required)**: Confirm whether you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms (such as cough, difficulty breathing, loss of taste or smell).
* **Tested positive for COVID-19 in past 90 days (Required)**: Confirm whether you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 90 days.
* **Close contact with confirmed COVID-19 case recently (Required)**: Confirm whether you have had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case recently.
* **-- Vaccine History -- (Optional)**: This section allows you to provide any additional vaccine history information.
* **Prior COVID-19 vaccination doses received (Required)**: Enter the number of COVID-19 vaccination doses you have received previously.
* **Date of Last Dose (Optional)**: Enter the date of your last COVID-19 vaccination dose.
* **Any reactions to previous vaccine doses (Required)**: Confirm whether you have had any reactions to previous vaccine doses.
* **If yes, describe reaction (Optional)**: If you have had a reaction, describe it in this field.
* **-- Medical Information -- (Optional)**: This section allows you to provide any additional medical information.
* **Currently taking medications (Required)**: Confirm whether you are currently taking any medications.
* **If yes, list medications (Optional)**: If you are taking medications, list them in this field.
* **Do you have a blood clotting disorder (Required)**: Confirm whether you have a blood clotting disorder.
* **Any surgery planned in next 2 weeks (Required)**: Confirm whether you have any surgery planned in the next 2 weeks.
* **-- Consent and Acknowledgment -- (Optional)**: This section is for consent and acknowledgment of the risks and benefits of vaccination.
* **I understand the risks and benefits of vaccination (Required)**: Confirm that you understand the risks and benefits of vaccination.
* **Emergency contact information on file (Required)**: Confirm whether you have emergency contact information on file.
* **Ready to proceed with vaccination (Required)**: Confirm whether you are ready to proceed with vaccination.
* **If no, need to speak with provider (Optional)**: If you are not ready to proceed, you will need to discuss with your healthcare provider.
