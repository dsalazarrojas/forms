# Patient Safety Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Safety Check Form is used to quickly assess a patient's safety and identify potential concerns. This form is a tool to help healthcare providers ensure the safety and well-being of their patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. For each question, indicate the correct response based on the patient's current condition.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, use your best judgment and check with the patient or their medical team if necessary.
4. Make sure to review and sign the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Safety Check 1**: This is a free-form text field where you can write any comments or notes about the patient's safety.
  **Type:** Note
  **Required:** No, this is an optional field.

- **Patient Safety Check 2**: Choose all that apply from the list of options. This helps to quickly identify potential safety concerns.
  **Type:** Select Multiple
  **Required:** No, this is not a required field.

  *Option 1*: Check this box if the patient is experiencing any symptoms of COVID-19.
  *Option 2*: Check this box if the patient has a history of allergies or medical conditions that may impact their safety.

- **Patient Safety Check 3**: This is a date field where you can enter the date of the patient's last known safety check.
  **Type:** Date
  **Required:** No, this is not a required field.
