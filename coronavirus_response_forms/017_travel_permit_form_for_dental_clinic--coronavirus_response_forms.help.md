# Travel Permit Form For Dental Clinic - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their travel details and requirements for approval from the dental clinic staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your personal information: Enter your patient ID, name, date of birth, and contact phone and email in the respective fields.
2. Specify your travel details: Choose your travel destination, travel start date, travel end date, travel duration, and travel mode.
3. Verify with the doctor: Ensure you have the approval from the clinic doctor by selecting "approved" in the "Travel Status" field.
4. Complete and submit: Review and submit the form to the dental clinic staff for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your patient ID number or identifier.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name as per your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the required format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your current address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the reason for your visit to the clinic.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `select_one`, required): Choose your travel destination from the options provided.
* **Travel Start Date** (`travel_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your expected travel start date.
* **Travel End Date** (`travel_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your expected travel end date.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your travel in hours or days.
* **Travel Distance** (`travel_distance`, `number`, optional): Enter the distance you plan to travel in miles or kilometers.
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the modes of transportation you will be using for your travel.
* **Travel Notes** (`travel_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about your travel that is relevant to your visit.
* **Travel Status** (`travel_status`, `select_one`, required): Select "approved" to indicate that you have the clinic doctor's approval for your travel.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who will be reviewing your travel permit.
* **Doctor's License Number** (`doctor_license_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Doctor's License Type** (`doctor_license_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of medical license held by the doctor.
* **Doctor's Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `text`, optional): Enter the doctor's signature as proof of approval.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, optional): Enter your signature as confirmation of the information provided.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the clinic where the doctor is registered.
* **Clinic License Number** (`clinic_license_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the clinic's license number.
* **Clinic License Type** (`clinic_license_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of medical license held by the clinic.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the review by the clinic staff.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the dental clinic staff for assistance.
