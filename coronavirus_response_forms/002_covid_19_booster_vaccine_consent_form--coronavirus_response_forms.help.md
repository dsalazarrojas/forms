# COVID 19 Booster Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended to gather information about the patient's vaccination history and consent status for the COVID-19 booster vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your full name in the `Full Name` field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the `Date of Birth` field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your email address in the `Email Address` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
5. If you have been previously vaccinated, enter the date of your first dose of COVID-19 vaccine in the `Date of First COVID-19 Dose` field.
6. If you have received a second dose of COVID-19 vaccine, enter the date of your second dose in the `Date of Second COVID-19 Dose (if applicable)` field.
7. Select the manufacturer of your COVID-19 vaccine from the `COVID-19 Vaccine Manufacturer` dropdown list.
8. If you have any known allergies, list them in the `Do you have any known allergies?` field.
9. List any medications you are currently taking in the `List any medications you are currently taking` field.
10. Answer the question about previous adverse reactions to vaccines in the `Have you had any adverse reactions to vaccines?` field.
11. If you are immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy, select the corresponding option from the `Are you immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy?` dropdown list.
12. Read and understand the information about the booster vaccine and select the corresponding option from the `I have read and understood the information about the booster vaccine` dropdown list.
13. Confirm that all your questions have been answered to your satisfaction by selecting the corresponding option from the `All my questions have been answered to my satisfaction` dropdown list.
14. Select "I consent" to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccine in the `I consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccine` dropdown list.
15. Enter the date of your consent in the `Date of Consent` field.
16. Enter the name of your healthcare provider in the `Healthcare Provider Name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of First COVID-19 Dose** (`first_dose_date`, date, required): If you have been previously vaccinated, enter the date of your first dose of COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Date of Second COVID-19 Dose (if applicable)** (`second_dose_date`, date, optional): If you have received a second dose of COVID-19 vaccine, enter the date of your second dose.
* **COVID-19 Vaccine Manufacturer** (`vaccine_manufacturer`, select_one, required): Select the manufacturer of your COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Do you have any known allergies?** (`known_allergies`, text, optional): If you have any known allergies, list them here.
* **List any medications you are currently taking** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Have you had any adverse reactions to vaccines?** (`previous_adverse_reaction`, select_one, required): Answer the question about previous adverse reactions to vaccines.
* **Are you immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy?** (`immunocompromised`, select_one, required): If you are immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy, select the corresponding option.
* **I have read and understood the information about the booster vaccine** (`read_information`, select_one, required): Read and understand the information about the booster vaccine and select the corresponding option.
* **All my questions have been answered to my satisfaction** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): Confirm that all your questions have been answered to your satisfaction.
* **I consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccine** (`consent_booster`, select_one, required): Select "I consent" to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccine.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
