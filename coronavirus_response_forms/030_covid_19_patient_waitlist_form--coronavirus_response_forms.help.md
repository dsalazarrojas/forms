# COVID 19 Patient Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients for COVID-19 appointment registration. It helps the healthcare team to prioritize and manage the waitlist effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
5. **Are you an existing patient?**: Check 'Yes' if you are a registered patient, 'No' if you are not.
6. **Patient ID (if existing)**: If you are an existing patient, enter your patient ID number.
7. **Type of Appointment**: Select the type of appointment you are requesting (e.g., General check-up, COVID-19 related, etc.).
8. **Preferred Appointment Date**: Enter the preferred date for your appointment.
9. **Preferred Time Slot**: Select your preferred time slot for the appointment (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
10. **Visit Type**: Select how you would like to have your visit (e.g., In-person, Telehealth).
11. **Do you have COVID-19 symptoms?**: Check 'Yes' if you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
12. **Describe your symptoms**: Provide a brief description of your symptoms if you are experiencing any.
13. **Have you tested positive for COVID-19?**: Check 'Yes' if you have tested positive for COVID-19.
14. **Have you been exposed to COVID-19?**: Check 'Yes' if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19.
15. **Appointment Urgency**: Select the urgency level of your appointment request (e.g., Routine, Soon, Urgent, Emergency).
16. **Any special needs?**: Provide any special accommodations you may need for your appointment.
17. **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Text message).
18. **Waitlist Registration Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to avoid any delays or miscommunication with the healthcare team.
* If you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms, please provide a brief description of your symptoms.
* If you have any special needs or requirements for your appointment, please provide them in the 'Any special needs?' field.
* Please select the preferred contact method that you are most comfortable with.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please check the 'Unsure' option.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the healthcare team.
