# Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about your health status, travel history, and medical conditions related to COVID-19. Please answer the questions truthfully to help ensure the health and safety of yourself and those around you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Use the available options for select_one and select_multiple fields.
3. Enter text in the text fields to provide more detailed information.
4. Use the provided hints as guidance for each field.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel History** (travel_history, text, optional): Describe your travel history, including any countries or regions you have visited recently.
* **Symptoms** (symptoms, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you are currently experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Contact Trace** (contact_trace, text, optional): If you have been in contact with someone who has COVID-19, provide details about the contact.
* **Health Status** (health_status, select_one, optional): Report your current health status, including any symptoms or medical conditions.
* **Travel Details** (travel_details, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about your recent travel, such as flight numbers and dates.
* **Travel Type** (travel_type, select_one, optional): Select the type of travel you have done recently.
* **Travel Destination** (travel_destination, text, optional): Enter the country or region you traveled from.
* **Medical Attention** (medical_attention, date, optional): Record any medical attention you have received for COVID-19.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, optional): Describe any pre-existing medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Contact** (medical_contact, time, optional): Enter the date and time of your last medical contact.
* **Medical Location** (medical_location, text, optional): Enter the location of your last medical contact.
* **Medical Facility** (medical_facility, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical facility you visited.
* **Medical Treatment** (medical_treatment, note, optional): Provide any medical treatment or advice received.
* **Medical Result** (medical_result, select_one, optional): Report the result of any medical tests or examinations.
* **Medical Test** (medical_test, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have undergone a medical test.
* **Medical Notes** (medical_notes, note, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or information.
* **Patient Information** (patient_info, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information if applicable.
* **Patient Status** (patient_status, select_one, optional): Report the patient's status, including any medical conditions.
* **Patient Condition** (patient_condition, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or symptoms.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional): Enter any contact information for the patient.
* **Travel Frequency** (travel_frequency, text, optional): Enter the frequency of your recent travel.
* **Travel Duration** (travel_duration, number, optional): Enter the duration of your recent travel.
* **Travel Reason** (travel_reason, text, optional): Enter the purpose of your recent travel.
* **Travel Location** (travel_location, text, optional): Enter the location of your recent travel.
* **Travel Date** (travel_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your recent travel.

Note: This help guide is for general guidance and may not cover every possible scenario. Always follow the instructions provided by healthcare professionals and local authorities for COVID-19 protocols.
