# COVID 19 Membership Suspension Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from members regarding their suspension from a facility due to the COVID-19 pandemic. It is intended to ensure that members understand the terms and conditions of their suspension, including the duration of the suspension, reactivation process, and any benefits or fees waived during the suspension period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the member information section, including name, membership ID, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the membership type that corresponds to your current status.
3. Enter the reason for your suspension (e.g., health concern, positive test, etc.).
4. Provide additional details about the reason for your suspension, if applicable.
5. Enter the suspension start and end dates.
6. Specify the duration of your suspension in months.
7. Decide whether to freeze membership fees during the suspension period.
8. Review and agree to the reactivation terms, including the reactivation process and any fees waived.
9. Choose whether to automatically reactivate or manually reactivate when the suspension ends.
10. Indicate whether you will return to the facility before reactivation.
11. Confirm whether a health screening is required before returning.
12. Acknowledge the terms and conditions of the suspension.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your membership card or ID.
* **Membership ID** (`member_id`, text, required): Enter your membership ID number as it appears on your membership card or ID.
* **Email Address** (`member_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we have on file.
* **Phone Number** (`member_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we have on file.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of membership you hold, such as Gold, Silver, Bronze, Premium, or Standard.
* **Reason for Suspension** (`suspension_reason`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your suspension, such as Health Concern, Positive Test, Exposure, etc.
* **Additional Details** (`reason_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about the reason for your suspension, if applicable.
* **Suspension Start Date** (`suspension_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your suspension will begin.
* **Suspension End Date** (`suspension_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you expect your suspension to end.
* **Duration of Suspension** (`suspension_duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of your suspension in months.
* **Freeze Membership Fees** (`freeze_fees`, select_one, required): Decide whether to freeze your membership fees during the suspension period.
* **Reactivation Terms** (`reactivation_terms`, note, required): Review and agree to the reactivation terms, including the reactivation process and any fees waived.
* **Reactivation Process** (`reactivation_process`, text, required): Specify how you would like to reactivate your membership.
* **Reactivation Fees Waived** (`fee_waived`, select_one, required): Decide whether your reactivation fees will be waived.
* **Automatic Reactivation** (`auto_reactivation`, select_one, required): Choose whether to automatically reactivate or manually reactivate when the suspension ends.
* **Member will return to facility before reactivation** (`return_to_facility`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you will return to the facility before reactivation.
* **Health Screening Required** (`health_check_required`, select_one, required): Confirm whether a health screening is required before returning.
* **Acknowledge Suspension Terms and Conditions** (`acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the suspension.
* **Suspension Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you submitted this form.
