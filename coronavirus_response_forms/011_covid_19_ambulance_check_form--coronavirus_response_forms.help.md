# COVID 19 Ambulance Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for ambulances to check and report their current status before each shift. It will help ensure that all necessary checks and precautions are taken to maintain a safe and efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and confirm the inspection date and time.
2. Enter your ambulance unit number, inspector name, and inspector ID.
3. Check the exterior condition of the ambulance, including:
	* Exterior condition (e.g., Good, Fair, Needs repair, or Not acceptable).
	* Fuel level (e.g., Full tank, Three-quarters full, Half tank, or Less than half).
	* All lights operational (e.g., Yes, No, or Partial).
4. Check the status of equipment and supplies, including:
	* Personal protective equipment (PPE) stocked (e.g., Yes, No).
	* PPE items status (e.g., N95 masks, Surgical masks, Gloves, Face shields, Gowns, or Hand sanitizer).
	* Oxygen tank levels adequate (e.g., Yes or No).
	* Emergency medications stocked (e.g., Yes or No).
	* Defibrillator status and battery level (e.g., Fully charged and operational, Partially charged, Not operational, or Not present).
5. Check the stretcher and patient transport equipment condition (e.g., Good, Fair, Needs repair, or Unsafe for use).
6. Check the ambulance interior cleanliness and disinfection status (e.g., Yes, No, or Partial).
7. Check the biohazard waste containers status (e.g., Empty and ready, Partially filled, Full, or Missing).
8. Enter any issues or concerns regarding the ambulance overall status (e.g., Ready for service, Ready with minor issues noted, or Not ready for service).
9. Provide any additional action required to address issues or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Ambulance Unit Number**: Enter your ambulance's unit number.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector ID**: Enter your inspector ID.
* **Exterior Condition Check**: Evaluate the exterior condition of the ambulance (e.g., Good, Fair, Needs repair, or Not acceptable).
* **Fuel Level**: Check the fuel level of the ambulance (e.g., Full tank, Three-quarters full, Half tank, or Less than half).
* **All Lights Operational**: Check if all lights are operational (e.g., Yes, No, or Partial).
* **Siren Operational**: Check if the siren is operational (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Personal Protective Equipment Stocked**: Check if PPE is stocked (e.g., Yes or No).
* **PPE Items Status**: Select any items needing replenishment (e.g., N95 masks, Surgical masks, Gloves, Face shields, Gowns, or Hand sanitizer).
* **Oxygen Levels Adequate**: Check if oxygen tank levels are adequate (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Emergency Medications Stocked**: Check if emergency medications are stocked (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Defibrillator Status and Battery Level**: Check the defibrillator status and battery level (e.g., Fully charged and operational, Partially charged, Not operational, or Not present).
* **Stretcher and Patient Transport Equipment Condition**: Evaluate the condition of the stretcher and patient transport equipment (e.g., Good, Fair, Needs repair, or Unsafe for use).
* **Ambulance Interior Cleaned and Disinfected**: Check if the ambulance interior is cleaned and disinfected (e.g., Yes, No, or Partial).
* **Biohazard Waste Containers Status**: Check the biohazard waste containers status (e.g., Empty and ready, Partially filled, Full, or Missing).
* **Overall Ambulance Status**: Evaluate the overall status of the ambulance (e.g., Ready for service, Ready with minor issues noted, or Not ready for service).
* **Issues or Concerns**: Enter any issues or concerns regarding the ambulance overall status.
* **Action Required**: Enter any action required to address issues or concerns.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully evaluate each field to ensure accurate and thorough reporting.
* If unsure about any field, refer to your department's guidelines and policies for guidance.
* Complete the form as soon as possible after the inspection to ensure timely reporting.
