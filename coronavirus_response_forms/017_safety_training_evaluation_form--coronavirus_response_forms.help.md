# Safety Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the safety training given to employees, ensuring that they have received and comprehended the necessary safety information to perform their job tasks safely.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the employee's name in the first field.
2.  Enter the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
3.  Select the date when the employee was trained.
4.  Choose the type of safety training that was provided.
5.  Enter the name of the person who facilitated the training.
6.  Provide the phone number of the training facilitator.
7.  Enter the email address of the training facilitator.
8.  In the "Safety Training Questions" field, enter the questions that were asked during the training.
9.  Select all the training evaluations that apply to the employee.
10.  Add any additional comments about the training session.
11.  Enter the date when the form was submitted.
12.  Choose the date when the form will be reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee who received the safety training.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID number of the employee who received the safety training.
* **Date Trained** (`date_trained`, date, required/optional): Select the date when the employee was trained.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of safety training provided to the employee.
* **Training Facilitator Name** (`training_facilitator_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who facilitated the safety training.
* **Training Facilitator Phone** (`training_facilitator_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the training facilitator.
* **Training Facilitator Email** (`training_facilitator_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the training facilitator.
* **Safety Training Questions** (`safety_training_questions`, text, required/optional): Enter all the questions that were asked during the training session.
* **Training Evaluation** (`training_evaluation`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the training evaluations that apply to the employee.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments about the training session.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date when the form will be reviewed.
