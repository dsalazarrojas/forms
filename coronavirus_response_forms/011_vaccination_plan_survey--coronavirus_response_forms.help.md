# Vaccination Plan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a person's vaccination plan and status in response to the coronavirus pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your vaccination plan, including your current vaccination status.
2. Provide any relevant notes or comments about your vaccination status.
3. Enter the date of your next scheduled vaccination.
4. Enter any notes or comments about your vaccination plan.
5. Enter any follow-up schedule with your healthcare provider.
6. Enter the location where you receive your vaccinations.
7. Finally, enter the name of your doctor responsible for your coronavirus treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vaccination Plan** (`vaccination_plan`, text, required/optional): Enter your current vaccination plan, including any relevant details about your vaccination schedule.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your current vaccination status, such as "fully vaccinated", "partially vaccinated", or "not vaccinated".
* **Next Vaccination Date** (`next_vaccination_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your next scheduled vaccination.
* **Vaccination Status Note** (`vaccination_status_note`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about your vaccination status.
* **Follow-up Schedule** (`follow_up_schedule`, text, required/optional): Enter any follow-up schedule with your healthcare provider.
* **Vaccination Center** (`vaccination_center`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you receive your vaccinations.
* **Coronavirus Responsible Doctor** (`coronavirus_responsible_doctor`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your doctor responsible for your coronavirus treatment.
* **Vaccination Plan Note** (`vaccination_plan_note`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about your vaccination plan.
* **Vaccination Schedule** (`vaccination_schedule`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about your vaccination schedule.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your vaccination status or plan, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
* This form is for personal use only, and any commercial use is strictly prohibited.
