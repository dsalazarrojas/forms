# COVID 19 Patient Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Patient Screening Questionnaire is a tool used by healthcare professionals to quickly assess and manage potential COVID-19 cases. This form collects demographic information, medical history, and exposure risks to identify high-risk individuals and provide targeted care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your patient's demographic information, including their full name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Answer questions about your patient's symptoms, travel history, and potential exposure risks.
3. List any underlying medical conditions or current medications.
4. Provide your patient's insurance information.
5. If applicable, answer questions about your patient's pregnancy status and additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender identity.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for follow-up communications.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Are you currently experiencing any symptoms**: Select all applicable symptoms from the list.
	+ Fever or chills
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath or difficulty breathing
	+ Fatigue
	+ Muscle or body aches
	+ Headache
	+ New loss of taste or smell
	+ Sore throat
	+ Congestion or runny nose
	+ Nausea or vomiting
	+ Diarrhea
	+ None of the above
* **When did your symptoms start**: If you are experiencing symptoms, enter the date they began. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case**: Select 'True' if you have been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days.
* **Travel history**: Select 'True' if you have traveled outside your state or country in the past 14 days.
* **List locations traveled to**: If you have traveled, list the cities, states, or countries you have visited.
* **Do you have any underlying medical conditions**: Select all applicable conditions from the list.
	+ Cancer
	+ Chronic kidney disease
	+ COPD (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease)
	+ Immunocompromised state
	+ Serious heart conditions
	+ Obesity
	+ Sickle cell disease
	+ Smoking history
	+ Type 2 diabetes
	+ None of the above
* **List current medications**: If you take medications, list them, including dosage if applicable.
* **Primary Care Physician Name**: Enter the name of your regular doctor.
* **Physician Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your doctor's office.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance company.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your policy or member ID number.
* **Pregnancy Status**: If applicable, select 'True' to indicate you are pregnant.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns**: Enter any other information you would like to share with the healthcare team.

Note: This form requires a medical professional's review before any further action is taken.
