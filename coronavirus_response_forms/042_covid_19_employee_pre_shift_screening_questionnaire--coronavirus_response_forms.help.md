# COVID 19 Employee Pre Shift Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees about their COVID-19 status before starting their shift. The goal is to ensure that employees are cleared to work safely and to identify potential issues that require manager notification or further investigation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Screening Date**: Enter the date of your shift. This is the date you are scheduled to work.
2. **Shift Time**: Enter the time of your shift. This is the time you are scheduled to start work.
3. **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
4. **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
5. **Department**: Enter the department you work in.
6. **Body Temperature**: Enter your body temperature in Fahrenheit. If you are unsure, please enter "Not Sure".
7. **Do you have a fever?**: Select "Yes" if you have a temperature of 100.4 degrees F or higher.
8. **Do you have a cough?**: Select "Yes" if you have a new or worsening cough.
9. **Do you have shortness of breath?**: Select "Yes" if you experience difficulty breathing.
10. **Have you lost your sense of taste or smell?**: Select "Yes" if you have lost your sense of taste or smell.
11. **Do you have a sore throat?**: Select "Yes" if you have a sore throat.
12. **Do you have body aches?**: Select "Yes" if you have body aches.
13. **Do you feel unusual fatigue?**: Select "Yes" if you feel unusually tired.
14. **Do you have a headache?**: Select "Yes" if you have a headache.
15. **Do you have nausea or vomiting?**: Select "Yes" if you have nausea or vomiting.
16. **Do you have diarrhea?**: Select "Yes" if you have diarrhea.
17. **Have you been exposed to COVID-19?**: Select the option that best describes your exposure status in the past 14 days.
18. **Have you tested positive for COVID-19?**: Select "Yes" if you have tested positive in the past 10 days.
19. **Are you under quarantine or isolation?**: Select "Yes" if you are currently under quarantine or isolation.
20. **Vaccination Status**: Select your current vaccination status.
21. **Screening Result**: Select the outcome of your screening.
22. **Manager Notification Required**: Select "Yes" if you need to inform your manager about your symptoms or status.
23. **Additional Notes or Concerns**: Enter any other health information or concerns you want to report.
