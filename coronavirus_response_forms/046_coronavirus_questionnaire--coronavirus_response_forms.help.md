# coronavirus_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "coronavirus_questionnaire" form is designed to gather information about an individual's exposure to potential COVID-19 risks. It is used to identify people who may be at risk of infection or have been infected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the first page and select 'Have you been in any of the following places?'
2. Choose all the places where you have been in the last month from the provided options (airport, store, restaurant, public event)
3. Click on the next page and select the symptoms you have experienced in the last month
4. Choose all the symptoms you have experienced from the provided options (fever, sore throat, headache)
5. If you have traveled to any foreign countries in the last month, enter the name of the country or countries you have visited
6. If you have had close contact with any confirmed cases, enter 'True' or 'False'
7. If you have attended any public events in the last month, enter the name of the event
