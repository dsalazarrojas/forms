# PPE Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PPE Request Form is designed to collect information from users who are requesting Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) due to the COVID-19 pandemic. This form is intended to help us better understand the user's needs and provide timely assistance. Please note that you should only fill out this form if you are a member of the university community or a partner organization that requires PPE.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name, which is your name as it appears on your university ID or your name as a partner organization representative.
2. Enter your Phone Number, which is your primary contact number for communication.
3. Provide your current Address, which is your current physical location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name**: This is where you enter your name as it appears on your university ID or your name as a partner organization representative.
- **Phone Number**: This is where you enter your primary contact number for communication, including your mobile phone number or your office phone number.
- **Address**: This is where you provide your current physical location, including your building number and apartment or suite number if applicable.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- If you are requesting PPE for yourself, please fill out the form as a representative of your own unit or team.
- If you are requesting PPE for someone else, please fill out the form as the representative of the person requesting PPE.
- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions or authorizations to request PPE on behalf of someone else.
