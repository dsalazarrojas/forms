# COVID 19 Vaccination Employee Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Vaccination Employee Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from employees to register for the COVID-19 vaccination program. This form helps ensure that your vaccination information is up-to-date and accurate for administration and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, answering all the required questions.
2. Use the date format: MM/DD/YYYY for all date fields.
3. For select_one fields, select only one option that best represents your response.
4. For text fields, enter your response in the provided space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this form. Format: MM/DD/YYYY.
* **First Name** (`employee_first_name`): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`employee_last_name`): Enter your legal last name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your work department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your position.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`): Enter the office or site where you work.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`): Enter your work email address.
* **Alternate Email** (`work_email_alternate`): Enter your personal email address (optional).
* **Are you a healthcare worker?** (`healthcare_worker`): Check if you are a healthcare worker.
* **Do you have direct patient contact?** (`patient_facing_role`): Select how often you interact with patients.
* **Do you want to participate in the COVID-19 vaccination program?** (`vaccination_participation`): Choose your participation decision.
* **If declining, what is your reason?** (`decline_reason`): Select your reason for declining vaccination (if applicable).
* **Please describe your medical contraindication** (`medical_exemption_details`): If you have a medical contraindication, provide a brief description.
* **Have you already received COVID-19 vaccination?** (`previous_vaccination_status`): Select your vaccination status.
* **When did you receive your vaccine doses?** (`previous_vaccine_dates`): Enter the dates you received your vaccination (if applicable).
* **Where did you receive your vaccination?** (`previous_vaccine_location`): Enter the location where you received your vaccination (if applicable).
* **What vaccine brand did you receive?** (`vaccine_brand_received`): Select the vaccine brand you received.
* **Preferred Vaccination Date** (`preferred_vaccination_date`): Enter your preferred date for vaccination.
* **Preferred Vaccination Time** (`preferred_vaccination_time`): Enter your preferred time for vaccination (if applicable).
* **Do you have a vaccine brand preference?** (`vaccine_brand_preference`): Select your brand preference.
* **Do you have any underlying medical conditions?** (`underlying_conditions`): Select any conditions you may have.
* **Do you have any medication allergies?** (`medication_allergies`): Enter any medication allergies you may have (if applicable).
* **Can you take time off for vaccination and recovery?** (`work_schedule_flexibility`): Select your schedule flexibility.
* **Would you prefer on-site vaccination at work?** (`on_site_vaccination_preference`): Select your location preference.
* **Would you like to attend a vaccine education session?** (`education_session_attendance`): Select your interest in education.
* **Do you have any questions about the vaccine?** (`questions_about_vaccine`): Enter any questions you may have about the vaccine (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine if eligible** (`consent_to_vaccination`): Select your consent status.
* **I consent to share my vaccination information with HR and health authorities** (`data_sharing_consent`): Select your data sharing consent status.
* **Signature Date** (`employee_signature_date`): Enter the date you signed this form.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that your vaccination information is up-to-date and ready for administration.
