# COVID 19 Vaccine Eligibility Form (Lyon County, Kansas) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals to determine their eligibility for COVID 19 vaccines in Lyon County, Kansas. It gathers personal contact information, eligibility criteria, and preferred contact method for vaccine results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal contact information, including your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Phone Number, and Email Address.
2. Answer the eligibility criteria questions:
   - Are you 65 years of age or older?
   - Are you in Phase 1 (check all that apply)?
   - Are you a Kansas resident?
   - Do you reside in Lyon County?
3. If you work in Lyon County, provide your occupation.
4. If applicable, list any chronic health conditions you have.
5. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
6. Select your preferred contact method for vaccine results (Phone, Email, or Both).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Are you 65 years of age or older?** (Select One, Required): Choose 'Yes' if you are 65 years of age or older.
* **Are you in Phase 1 (check all that apply)?** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the categories that apply to you:
	+ Healthcare worker
	+ Long-term care facility resident
	+ Long-term care facility staff
* **Are you a Kansas resident?** (Select One, Required): Choose 'Yes' if you are a Kansas resident.
* **Do you reside in Lyon County?** (Select One, Required): Choose 'Yes' if you reside in Lyon County. If not, select 'No' and provide your occupation (if not a resident).
* **If you work in Lyon County, what is your occupation?** (Text, Optional): Enter your occupation if you are not a resident of Lyon County.
* **Do you have any chronic health conditions?** (Select One, Optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have chronic health conditions.
* **Please list your chronic health conditions** (Text, Optional): List your chronic health conditions if you have any.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (Select One, Required): Choose 'Yes' to confirm the accuracy of the provided information.
* **Date of Submission** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Preferred Contact Method for Results** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred contact method for vaccine results:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Both
