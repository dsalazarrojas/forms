# Coronavirus Suspected Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Coronavirus Suspected Patient Intake Form** is designed to collect essential information from patients suspected of having been exposed to or displaying symptoms of COVID-19. This information will aid healthcare professionals in their assessment and response to the patient's condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information ready.
* Enter the patient's **Patient Name** in the "Patient Name" field.
* Enter the patient's **Date of Birth** in the "Date Of Birth" field in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Describe the patient's **Symptoms** in the "Symptoms" field.
* Provide your **Contact Info** in the "Contact Info" field.
* Enter your **Contact Phone** number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Describe the patient's symptoms, including any fever, cough, shortness of breath, or other respiratory issues.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information, including your name, organization, and address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up and communication purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to collect this sensitive health information.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or requirement, please reach out to your supervisor or healthcare team for guidance.
