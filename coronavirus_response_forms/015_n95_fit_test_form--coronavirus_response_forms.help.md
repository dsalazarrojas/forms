# N95 Fit Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The N95 Fit Test Form is designed to collect information about participants' experiences with N95 mask fit tests. It helps us understand how well the N95 mask fits and any issues that may arise during the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Select one of the provided options for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Provide a brief introduction to the fit test.
* **Participant Information**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information.
* **Fit Tester Information**: Enter the name, title, and contact information of the fit tester who performed the test.
* **Participant's Fit Tester Rating**: Rate the fit tester's performance (Good or Fair).
* **Test Location**: Enter the location where the fit test was performed.
* **Participant N95 Mask Type**: Indicate if you have an N95 mask or if you do not.
* **N95 Fitting**: Describe your experience with the N95 mask fitting.
* **Test Date**: Enter the date of the fit test.
* **N95 Fitting Type**: Indicate if you had a good fit or not.
* **Participant Fit Tester Rating 2**: Rate the fit tester's performance (Good or Fair).
* **N95 Fitting Type 2**: Indicate if you had a good fit or not.
* **Participant N95 Fitting Rating 2**: Rate your experience with the N95 mask fitting (Yes or No).
* **Participant Comments**: Enter any comments about your experience with the fit test.
* **Participant Fitting Comments**: Enter any comments about the fit test.
* **Participant Fitting**: Rate your experience with the fit test (Yes or No).
* **N95 Fitting Fitting Type**: Indicate if you had a good fit or not.
* **N95 Fitting Fitting**: Rate your experience with the N95 mask fitting (Yes or No).
* **N95 Fitting Fitting Notes**: Enter any comments about your experience with the fit test.
* **Participant's Fit Tester Rating 2 2**: Rate the fit tester's performance (Good or Fair).
* **Participant N95 Fitting Type 2**: Indicate if you had a good fit or not.
* **Participant N95 Fitting Rating 2 2**: Rate your experience with the N95 mask fitting (Yes or No).
* **Participant Comments 2**: Enter any comments about your experience with the fit test.
* **Participant Fitting Comments**: Enter any comments about the fit test.
* **Participant Fitting**: Rate your experience with the fit test (Yes or No).
* **N95 Fitting Fitting Type**: Indicate if you had a good fit or not.
* **N95 Fitting Fitting**: Rate your experience with the N95 mask fitting (Yes or No).
* **N95 Fitting Fitting Notes**: Enter any comments about your experience with the fit test.

Note: This form is designed to collect information from multiple sources, so it may seem repetitive. Please answer each question honestly and accurately, and do not skip any fields.
