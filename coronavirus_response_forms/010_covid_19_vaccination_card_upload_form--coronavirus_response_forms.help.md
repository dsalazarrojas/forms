# COVID 19 Vaccination Card Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who have received COVID-19 vaccinations. It will help in verifying vaccination records, reporting vaccination status, or for other health-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Full Name as it appears on your vaccination card.
2. Enter your Date of Birth.
3. Enter your Email Address.
4. Enter your Phone Number.
5. Provide the vaccination information as requested (type of vaccine, dates of doses, etc.).
6. Upload the front and back of your vaccination card (if applicable).
7. If you are unable to upload the vaccination card, provide the required information in the Manual Entry Alternative section.
8. Confirm that the information provided is accurate and authentic by selecting "I agree" for the Certification and Certification questions.
9. Choose how you consent to share your vaccination information for the Intended Use of Vaccination Record.
10. Submit the form after reviewing and confirming the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your vaccination card.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Vaccine Type**: Select the type of COVID-19 vaccine you received (Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, AstraZeneca, Novavax, or Other).
* **Date of First Dose**: Enter the date of your first COVID-19 vaccine dose.
* **Date of Second Dose**: Enter the date of your second COVID-19 vaccine dose (if applicable).
* **Date of Booster Dose**: Enter the date of your booster dose (if applicable).
* **Vaccination Location**: Enter the location where you received your COVID-19 vaccination (e.g., clinic, hospital, pharmacy).
* **Fully Vaccinated**: Confirm if you are fully vaccinated or not.
* **Upload Front of Vaccination Card**: Upload the front of your vaccination card in PDF, JPG, or PNG format.
* **Upload Back of Vaccination Card**: If your vaccination card has a back, upload it in PDF, JPG, or PNG format.
* **Manual Entry Alternative**: Provide any missing information that you couldn't upload, as requested.
* **Unable to upload vaccination card image**: Confirm if you are unable to upload your vaccination card image.
* **Batch Number - First Dose**: Enter the batch number of your first vaccine dose (if applicable).
* **Batch Number - Second Dose**: Enter the batch number of your second vaccine dose (if applicable).
* **Batch Number - Booster Dose**: Enter the batch number of your booster dose (if applicable).
* **Healthcare Provider or Clinic Name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider or clinic.
* **Verification and Consent**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and authentic.
* **I certify that the information is accurate and authentic**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and authentic.
* **I consent to sharing my vaccination information**: Confirm that you consent to share your vaccination information.
* **Intended Use of Vaccination Record**: Choose how you intend to use the vaccination record (e.g., employment verification, travel, school enrollment, healthcare facility access, or Other).
