# Student COVID 19 Vaccination Status Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track the student's COVID-19 vaccination status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the student's COVID-19 vaccination status from the options provided.
2. Choose where the student received their vaccination (School, Community, or Other).
3. If required, provide the date of vaccination.
4. For teachers, select their own COVID-19 vaccination status and vaccination location.
5. Provide any additional student notes (if necessary).
6. Confirm that the student has received the form (or not).
7. Confirm that the teacher has reviewed the form (or not).
8. Choose whether teacher uploads are required (or not).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Please select the student's COVID-19 vaccination status. Choose from Fully vaccinated, Not vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, or Not applicable.
* **Vaccination Location** (`vaccination_location`, select_one, required): Where did the student receive their COVID-19 vaccination? Choose from School, Community, or Other.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, required): If required, please provide the date of vaccination.
* **Teacher Vaccination Status** (`teacher_vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Select your own COVID-19 vaccination status. Choose from Fully vaccinated, Not vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, or Not applicable.
* **Teacher Vaccination Location** (`teacher_vaccination_location`, select_one, required): Where did the teacher receive their COVID-19 vaccination? Choose from School, Community, or Other.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Please enter the student's ID number.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, number, required): Enter your teacher ID number.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, note, required): Provide any additional notes about the student.
* **Teacher Notes** (`teacher_notes`, note, required): Provide any additional notes about the teacher.
* **Submission Status** (`submitted`, select_one, required): Indicate the submission status of this form. Choose Not submitted or Submitted.
* **Review Status** (`reviewed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have reviewed this form. Choose Not reviewed or Reviewed.
* **Teacher Confirmation** (`teacher_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the student has received the form. Choose Not confirmed or Confirmed.
* **Teacher Uploads** (`teacher_uploads`, select_one, required): Indicate whether teacher uploads are required. Choose Uploads not required or Uploads required.
