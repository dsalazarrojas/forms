# COVID 19 Self Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help identify potential COVID-19 cases and provide recommendations for individuals who have had contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID-19 or are experiencing symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions accurately and honestly. The form is divided into several sections, and you will be asked about your name, date and time of screening, current temperature, symptoms, and other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (person_name_full)** (`person_name_full`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Date of Screening (screening_date_val)** (`screening_date_val`, `date`, required): Enter the date you filled out this form.
* **Time of Screening (screening_time_val)** (`screening_time_val`, `time`, required): Enter the time you filled out this form.
* **Current Temperature (current_temp_recorded)** (`current_temp_recorded`, `number`, required): Please enter your current temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Have you had a fever (100.4 F or higher) in the last 24 hours? (fever_24h_yn)** (`fever_24h_yn`, `select_one`, required): Answer yes if you have had a fever higher than 100.4 F in the last 24 hours.
* **Do you have a new or worsening cough? (symptom_cough_new_yn)** (`symptom_cough_new_yn`, `select_one`, required): Answer yes if you have a new or worsening cough.
* **Are you experiencing any difficulty breathing? (symptom_difficulty_breathing)** (`symptom_difficulty_breathing`, `select_one`, required): Answer yes if you are experiencing difficulty breathing.
* **Have you been in close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19 in 14 days? (exposure_pos_case_yn)** (`exposure_pos_case_yn`, `select_one`, required): Answer yes if you have been in close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 14 days.
* **Are you currently waiting for COVID-19 test results due to symptoms or exposure? (awaiting_test_results_yn)** (`awaiting_test_results_yn`, `select_one`, required): Answer yes if you are waiting for test results.
* **Based on your responses, the recommendation is (recommendation_outcome)** (`recommendation_outcome`, `select_one`, required): The recommendation will be provided based on your answers.
* **I certify that I have answered the above questions truthfully (self_screening_cert_yn)** (`self_screening_cert_yn`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm that you have answered the questions truthfully.
* **Signature (electronic_sig_full)** (`electronic_sig_full`, `text`, required): Please sign your name as a verification of your identity.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions accurately and honestly.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact your supervisor or health department for further guidance.
* This form is designed to provide recommendations based on your responses, but it is not a substitute for medical advice. If you have any medical concerns, please consult a healthcare professional.
