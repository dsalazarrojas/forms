# School Family Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their experiences and thoughts about the school. It's an opportunity for them to provide feedback, share their interests, and respond to surveys or polls.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required information, which is indicated by a *required* label.
*   Enter your name and contact information for the school.
*   Answer the questions and select your responses for the survey and interest sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school you are associated with.
*   **Teachers Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Provide the name of your child's teacher or the teacher you are in contact with at the school.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for the school to contact you.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Provide a contact number where the school can reach you.
*   **School Year** (`school_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the year you are currently or expect to be at the school.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required/optional): Share any thoughts, opinions, or suggestions you have about the school.
*   **What are your interests** (`parent_interest`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the interests that apply from the list provided:

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
*   Use clear and concise language when providing feedback.
*   If you have any attachments or documents to support your answers, you can attach them to the form.
*   If you're unsure about any question or section, feel free to ask the school administration for clarification.
