# COVID 19 Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to volunteer for COVID-19 related activities. It will help us understand your availability, skills, and experience to place you in a suitable role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and street address.
2. Select your employment status and preferred volunteer role.
3. Choose your available time slots and the duration of your commitment.
4. List any special skills or certifications you have.
5. Confirm your COVID-19 vaccination status.
6. Provide a reference name and phone number (if applicable).
7. Confirm your willingness to undergo a background check and receive training.
8. Indicate if you have your own transportation.
9. Confirm your availability and preferred volunteer hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`volunteer_name`): Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`): Enter your street address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (`city_state_zip`): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Select your current employment status:
	+ Full-time employed
	+ Part-time employed
	+ Student
	+ Retired
	+ Unemployed
	+ Self-employed
* **Current or Previous Occupation** (`occupation`): If you want to mention any occupation that could be useful, enter it here.
* **Volunteer Role Interested In** (`volunteer_role_interest`): Select the area you are interested in:
	+ Administrative support
	+ Community outreach
	+ Medical assistance
	+ Fundraising
	+ Logistics and supplies
	+ Contact tracing
	+ Other
* **Describe Preferred Role** (`role_description`): Write a brief description of the volunteer role you are interested in.
* **Availability** (`availability`): Choose when you are available to volunteer:
	+ Weekday mornings
	+ Weekday afternoons
	+ Weekday evenings
	+ Weekends
	+ Flexible
* **Hours Available Per Week** (`hours_per_week`): If you are available to volunteer for a specific number of hours per week, enter it here.
* **Volunteer Duration** (`duration_commitment`): Select the duration of your commitment:
	+ One-time event
	+ A few weeks
	+ One to three months
	+ Three to six months
	+ Six months or longer
* **Special Skills or Certifications** (`special_skills`): List any special skills or certifications you have, like first aid or medical training.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`): Confirm your COVID-19 vaccination status:
	+ Fully vaccinated
	+ Partially vaccinated
	+ Unvaccinated
	+ Prefer not to answer
* **Consent to Background Check** (`background_check_consent`): Confirm if you consent to a background check:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ No, I do not consent
* **Willing to Receive Training** (`training_willing`): Confirm if you are willing to receive training:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Have Own Transportation** (`transportation_own`): Confirm if you have your own transportation:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`): Enter the name of a person who can vouch for you.
* **Reference Phone Number** (`reference_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person who can vouch for you.

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest when filling the form.
- Use real email and phone numbers.
- Review the form before submitting it.
