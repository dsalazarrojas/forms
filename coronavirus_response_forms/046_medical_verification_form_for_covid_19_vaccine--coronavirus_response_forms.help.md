# Medical Verification Form For COVID 19 Vaccine - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track patient information, contact details, medical history, vaccination status, and vaccination schedule related to COVID-19 vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information in the "Patient Information" field.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Fill in the medical history related to the patient in the "Medical Information" field.
4. Check the vaccination status of the patient in the "Vaccine Status" field.
5. Record the patient's vaccination schedule in the "Vaccination Schedule" field.
6. Report any side effects of the vaccine in the "Vaccine Side Effects" field.
7. Enter the medical verification data in the numeric fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and ID number.
* **Contact Information** (text, required): Provide your contact details, such as name, phone number, and email.
* **Medical Information** (text, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Eligibility** (text, required): Check if the patient is eligible for vaccination.
* **Vaccine Status** (text, required): Record the patient's current vaccine status.
* **Vaccination Schedule** (text, required): Record the patient's vaccination schedule.
* **Vaccine Side Effects** (text, required): Report any side effects of the vaccine.
* **Medical Verification 1-17** (number, required): Enter the corresponding medical verification data.

Note: Some fields may have specific instructions or validation rules that are not listed here, so please follow the on-screen prompts or consult your healthcare provider for more information.
