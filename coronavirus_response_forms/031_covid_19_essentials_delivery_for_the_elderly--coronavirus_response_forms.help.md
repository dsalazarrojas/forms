# COVID 19 Essentials Delivery For The Elderly - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from elderly individuals who require essential items and services during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the recipient's personal information, including their name, age, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
2. Provide your delivery address and city or town.
3. Specify your emergency contact's name and phone number (if applicable).
4. If you have any health conditions or mobility limitations, please let us know.
5. Choose the essential items you need to be delivered (groceries, medications, etc.).
6. Select your preferred delivery frequency.
7. Specify your budget for the delivery and your preferred payment method.
8. If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please let us know.
9. If you require any additional assistance, please provide details.
10. Finally, select the date you would like your delivery to be made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, `text`, required): This field is where you should enter your name as the elderly individual receiving the essential items and services.
* **Age** (`recipient_age`, `number`, required): Please enter your age to help us better understand your needs.
* **Phone Number** (`recipient_phone`, `text`, required): This is where you should enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email Address** (`recipient_email`, `text`, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it so we can keep you updated.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Please provide the address where you would like the essential items delivered.
* **City or Town** (`city_town`, `text`, required): Enter the city or town where the delivery address is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Please enter your zip code to ensure accurate delivery.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Special Access Instructions** (`access_instructions`, `text`, optional): If you have any special instructions for access to your delivery location, please enter them here.
* **Chronic Health Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any chronic health conditions, please let us know.
* **Mobility or Physical Limitations** (`mobility_issues`, `select_one`, required): Please select the level of mobility or physical limitations you experience.
* **Grocery Items Needed** (`grocery_items`, `text`, required): Specify the grocery items you need to be delivered.
* **Medications or Medical Supplies** (`medications`, `text`, optional): If you need any medications or medical supplies, please let us know.
* **Delivery Frequency Requested** (`delivery_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the delivery frequency that suits your needs.
* **Budget for Delivery** (`budget_amount`, `number`, optional): If you have a budget in mind, please enter it.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please let us know.
* **Additional Assistance Needed** (`additional_assistance`, `text`, optional): If you require any additional assistance, please provide details.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you would like your delivery to be made.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will review your submission and get back to you as soon as possible.
* If you have any further assistance requests, please indicate them in the "Additional Assistance" field.
