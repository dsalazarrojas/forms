# Parent Acknowledgement Form For Covid 19 School Procedures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parent Acknowledgement Form For Covid 19 School Procedures" is a document that aims to gather information from parents regarding the school's Covid-19 procedures and protocols. This form will be used to track and store the responses of parents, ensuring that everyone is aware of and agrees to the procedures in place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Carefully read the terms and conditions and ensure you understand them.
2. Sign the acknowledgment section to confirm that you have read and agree with the terms and conditions.
3. Fill in your school's information, including school name, email, phone number, address, and zip code.
4. Provide your personal contact details, including your name, email, phone number, address, and zip code.
5. Choose your acknowledgment status (Completed, Declined).
6. Add any additional comments if necessary.
7. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): A section where you will be presented with the terms and conditions of the school's Covid-19 procedures. It is essential to read and understand these terms before proceeding.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`): A field where you need to sign and confirm that you have read and agreed with the terms and conditions.
* **E Signature** (`e_signature`): A dropdown menu to select the person who will sign the acknowledgement section.
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`): The date you will acknowledge the terms and conditions.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): The name of the school.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): A field where you can provide additional contact information for the school.
* **Terms and Conditions Text** (`terms_and_conditions_text`): A text field where you can provide any additional terms and conditions.
* **E Signature Confirmation** (`e_signature_confirmation`): A dropdown menu to confirm that you have signed the acknowledgement section.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement** (`terms_and_conditions_agreement`): A dropdown menu to confirm that you agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Acknowledgement Text** (`acknowledgement_text`): A text field where you can provide any additional comments.
* **Acknowledgement Date/Time** (`acknowledgement_date_time`): A field where you can provide the date and time of your acknowledgment.
* **Acknowledgement Status** (`acknowledgement_status`): A dropdown menu to select the status of your acknowledgment (Completed, Declined).
* **Acknowledgement Comment** (`acknowledgement_comment`): A text field where you can add any additional comments.
* **School Email** (`school_email`): The email address of the school.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`): The phone number of the school.
* **School Address** (`school_address`): The address of the school.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`): The zip code of the school.
* **School City** (`school_city`): The city of the school.
* **School State** (`school_state`): The state of the school.
* **School Country** (`school_country`): The country of the school.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): The name of the parent.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`): The email address of the parent.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`): The phone number of the parent.
* **Parent Address** (`parent_address`): The address of the parent.
* **Parent Zip** (`parent_zip`): The zip code of the parent.
