# COVID 19 Tattoo Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information for individuals who wish to get a tattoo that represents their COVID-19 vaccination journey. It includes questions about their medical history, vaccination details, and tattoo preferences to ensure a safe and informed decision-making process for both the client and the tattoo artist.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read through each question and fill out the form accordingly.
2. Answer all required questions, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Select relevant options from the drop-down menus.
4. Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. Ensure you provide accurate and honest information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date* (`consent_date`, date, required): The date you are giving your consent for the tattoo.
* **Patient Full Name* (`patient_full_name`, text, required): Your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth* (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender* (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Phone Number* (`phone_number`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Email Address* (`email_address`, email, required): Your contact email address.
* **Home Address (`address`, text, required): Your home address.
* **City* (`city`, text, required): The city where you reside.
* **State* (`state`, text, required): The state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code* (`zip_code`, text, required): The ZIP code of your location.
* **Which COVID-19 Vaccine Dose Will This Tattoo Represent* (`vaccine_dose_number`, select_one, required): Select the dose of the vaccine you received.
* **Which Vaccine Brand Did You Receive* (`vaccine_brand`, select_one, required): Choose the brand of the vaccine you received.
* **Date of Vaccination (`vaccination_date`, date, required): The date you were vaccinated.
* **Where Did You Get Vaccinated (`vaccination_location`, text, optional): The location where you received your vaccination.
* **What Tattoo Design Do You Prefer* (`tattoo_design_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the design for your tattoo from the options provided.
* **Where Would You Like the Tattoo Placed* (`tattoo_placement`, select_one, required): Select the location on your body where you'd like the tattoo.
* **What Size Tattoo Do You Want* (`tattoo_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your tattoo from the options provided.
* **Color Preference* (`color_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred color scheme for the tattoo.
* **Do You Have Any Medical Conditions That May Affect Tattooing (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Check any applicable medical conditions that may impact your tattooing process.
* **Do You Have Any Skin Allergies or Sensitivities (`skin_allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here.
* **Are You Taking Any Blood Thinner Medications (`blood_thinner_medication`, select_one, required): Check if you're taking any blood thinner medications.
* **Are You Currently Experiencing Any COVID-19 Symptoms (`current_symptoms`, select_one, required): Select if you're currently experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Have You Been Ill in the Past 14 Days (`recent_illness`, select_one, required): Check if you've been ill in the past 14 days.
* **I Understand That Tattooing Carries Risks Including Infection and Allergic Reactions (`risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand the risks involved with tattooing.
* **I Understand That Visiting the Tattoo Studio May Expose Me to COVID-19 (`covid_risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand the risk of exposure to COVID-19.
* **I Agree to Follow All Aftercare Instructions Provided (`aftercare_compliance`, select_one, required): Confirm you'll follow the aftercare instructions provided.
* **Additional Comments or Special Requests (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please list them here.
* **Signature Date (`patient_signature_date`, date, required): Date and time of your signature.

## Tips

* Please double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask your tattoo artist for guidance.
* Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure a smooth and safe tattooing process.
