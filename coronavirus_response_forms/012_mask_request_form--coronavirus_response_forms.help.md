# Mask Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for mask requests due to the coronavirus pandemic. It helps us track and manage mask resources to distribute them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information accurately.
2. Select the correct options for First Responder and Healthcare Organizations.
3. Choose the desired mask type and quantity.
4. Enter the location where the masks will be used.
5. Select a date for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mask Request**: <mask_request_form> (Text, Optional) - Enter a brief description of your mask request.
* **First Responder**: <first_responder> (Yes/No, Optional) - Check "True" if you are a first responder. This will help us prioritize your request.
* **Healthcare Organizations**: <healthcare_organizations> (Select Multiple, Optional) - Choose the type of healthcare organization you belong to (Physicians, Nurses, Hospitals, First Responders).
* **Mask Type**: <mask_type> (Select One, Optional) - Select the type of mask you need (N95, N99, N100).
* **Quantity**: <quantity> (Number, Optional) - Enter the quantity of masks you require.
* **Location**: <location> (Text, Optional) - Enter the location where the masks will be used.
* **Date**: <date> (Date, Optional) - Select the date for order tracking.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in the form accurately and completely.
* Check the options for First Responder and Healthcare Organizations carefully.
* If you need masks for multiple locations or dates, please submit multiple forms.
* We will review and process your request as soon as possible.

This help guide is designed to be straightforward and easy to follow, ensuring that users can efficiently complete the form and provide the necessary information to facilitate the distribution and management of masks during the pandemic.
