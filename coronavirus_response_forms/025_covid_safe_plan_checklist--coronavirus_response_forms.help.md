# COVID Safe Plan Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Safe Plan Checklist is a tool designed to assess the overall COVID-19 safety protocols in a facility. This guide will walk you through how to complete this form and what each field represents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the facility's current safety protocols and policies related to COVID-19.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully based on the facility's current situation.
3. Provide detailed answers to fields where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Facility Name
* **Field 1: Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required)
	+ Name of business or organization

### 2. Facility Address
* **Field 2: Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required)
	+ Address of the facility

### 3. Date of Safety Evaluation
* **Field 3: Date of Safety Evaluation** (`evaluation_date`, date, required)
	+ The date on which the safety evaluation was conducted

### 4. Evaluator Name
* **Field 4: Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required)
	+ Person conducting the assessment

### 5. Evaluator Title or Position
* **Field 5: Evaluator Title or Position** (`evaluator_title`, text, not required)
	+ Title or position of the evaluator (optional)

### 6. Adequate Handwashing Stations Available
* **Field 6: Adequate Handwashing Stations Available** (`handwashing_stations`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if there are adequate handwashing stations available

### 7. Hand Sanitizer Stations Throughout Facility
* **Field 7: Hand Sanitizer Stations Throughout Facility** (`hand_sanitizer`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if hand sanitizer stations are available throughout the facility

### 8. Adequate PPE Supplies in Stock
* **Field 8: Adequate PPE Supplies in Stock** (`ppe_supplies`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if adequate PPE (masks, gloves, face shields) are available

### 9. Adequate Cleaning and Disinfection Supplies
* **Field 9: Adequate Cleaning and Disinfection Supplies** (`cleaning_supplies`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if adequate cleaning and disinfection supplies are available

### 10. Cleaning Schedule Established and Posted
* **Field 10: Cleaning Schedule Established and Posted** (`cleaning_schedule`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if a cleaning schedule is established and posted

### 11. Adequate Ventilation System
* **Field 11: Adequate Ventilation System** (`ventilation`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if the facility has an adequate ventilation system

### 12. Physical Distancing Protocols Implemented
* **Field 12: Physical Distancing Protocols Implemented** (`physical_distancing`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if physical distancing protocols are in place

### 13. Signage Posted for Distancing Requirements
* **Field 13: Signage Posted for Distancing Requirements** (`distance_signage`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if signage is posted to indicate distancing requirements

### 14. Occupancy Capacity Limits Established
* **Field 14: Occupancy Capacity Limits Established** (`capacity_limits`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if capacity limits are established

### 15. Isolation Area Prepared
* **Field 15: Isolation Area Prepared** (`isolation_area`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if an isolation area is prepared for sick employees or customers

### 16. COVID-19 Safety Training Conducted
* **Field 16: COVID-19 Safety Training Conducted** (`training_completed`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if COVID-19 safety training has been conducted for all staff members

### 17. COVID-19 Safety Policies Documented
* **Field 17: COVID-19 Safety Policies Documented** (`policy_documentation`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if COVID-19 safety policies are documented

### 18. Emergency Response Plan Established
* **Field 18: Emergency Response Plan Established** (`emergency_response`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates if an emergency response plan is in place for suspected COVID cases

### 19. Overall COVID-19 Safety Compliance Status
* **Field 19: Overall COVID-19 Safety Compliance Status** (`overall_compliance`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicates the overall COVID-19 safety compliance status of the facility

### 20. Action Items or Recommendations
* **Field 20: Action Items or Recommendations** (`action_items`, text, not required)
	+ Any improvements or recommendations needed to maintain or improve the facility's COVID-19 safety protocols
