# Supplemental Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplemental Health Questionnaire is designed to gather additional information from patients about their health status, contact details, and medical history. This form helps healthcare providers assess their overall health and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required fields filled out. The form is divided into pages, each with its own set of fields. You will need to fill out every field on each page marked as "required."
2. Enter your information into each field. Be as accurate as possible.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`patient_information`, `text`, required/optional: required)
    Enter your name and any other relevant patient details that you feel are important for your healthcare provider to know.
* **Date of Birthday (Page 1)** (`patient_date_of_birthday`, `date`, required/optional: required)
    Please select your date of birth using the calendar widget or manually enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Phone (Page 1)** (`patient_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional: required)
    Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Medical History (Page 1)** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional: optional)
    Describe any pre-existing medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical treatments that you want to share with your healthcare provider. This information is not mandatory but can help us better understand your situation.

**Note:** If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact us.

**Tips:**

* Ensure your entries are correct and accurate.
* Be as detailed as possible.
* If you have any issues while filling out this form, contact us for assistance.
