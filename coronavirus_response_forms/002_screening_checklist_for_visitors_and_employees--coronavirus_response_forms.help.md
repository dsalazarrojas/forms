# Screening Checklist For Visitors And Employees - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help identify potential risks associated with visitors and employees entering or working on your premises. It is intended for use by facilities managers, HR representatives, or anyone responsible for ensuring the safety and well-being of others on-site. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into three sections, each with questions for visitors and employees.
2. Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. You can submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Visitor Screening** (`visitor_screening`, `text`, required): Answer questions regarding the visitors' health and travel history, such as recent travel, symptoms, or contact with someone with COVID-19.
* **Employee Screening** (`employee_screening`, `text`, required): Answer questions about the employees' health, travel history, and recent contact with someone with COVID-19.
* **Employee Screening** (`employee_screening`, `text`, required): Answer questions about the employees' health, travel history, and recent contact with someone with COVID-19.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough when answering questions.
* If you have any concerns or doubts, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is intended to be a guide, and you should always follow your company's guidelines and protocols for visitor and employee screening.
* You can save and print this form for reference or submit it digitally.
* If you need to make any changes or modifications, please let us know and we'll assist you.
* Thank you for helping keep our workspace safe and healthy.
