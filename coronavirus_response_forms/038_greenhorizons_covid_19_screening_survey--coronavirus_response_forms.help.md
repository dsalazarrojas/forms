# Greenhorizons COVID 19 Screening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greenhorizons COVID 19 Screening Survey is a form used to gather information about patients' potential exposure to the coronavirus. This information helps prioritize healthcare resources and identify patients who need immediate attention.
## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
- Enter your age to help us better understand your risk factors.
- Select any symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
- Indicate if you have recently traveled within 14 days or more than 14 days ago.
- Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
- Enter your email address so we can send you updates on your follow-up appointment.
- Indicate if you have private or public healthcare.
- If you have a follow-up appointment, select the date and time.
- If you have a follow-up appointment, select the time of day for your appointment.
- If needed, add any additional notes about your condition or treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Age** (number, required): Enter your age to help us better understand your risk factors.
* **Symptoms** (select multiple, required): Select any symptoms you are experiencing, such as:
  • Fever
  • Cough
  • Shortness of Breath
  • Fatigue
  • Muscle and Joint Pain
* **Recent Travel** (select one, required): Indicate if you have recently traveled within 14 days or more than 14 days ago.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can send you updates on your follow-up appointment.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address, but it is not required for the form to be submitted.
* **Healthcare** (select one, required): Indicate if you have private or public healthcare.
* **Follow-Up** (date, optional): If you have a follow-up appointment, select the date and time.
* **Follow-up Time** (time, optional): If you have a follow-up appointment, select the time of day for your appointment.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your condition or treatment if needed.
