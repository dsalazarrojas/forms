# Hotel Reopening On Duty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Reopening On Duty Form is designed to gather information on hotels' reopening procedures and safety measures during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the form and ensure that you are familiar with the questions and options provided.
2. For each section, list specific precautions or measures that your hotel has taken or plans to take.
3. For text fields, provide detailed and descriptive information.
4. For multiple-choice fields, select all relevant options that apply to your hotel.
5. Please answer the questions based on your current hotel practices and policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Distancing Precautions** (Required)
	+ This field is intended to gather information on specific measures your hotel has taken or plans to take to maintain social distancing.
	+ Example: Hand Sanitizer Stations, Plexiglass Screens, Temperature Check, Social Distancing Signage
* **Safety Precautions** (Required)
	+ This field is intended to gather information on specific measures your hotel has taken or plans to take to maintain a safe environment for guests and staff.
	+ Example: Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), Cleaning Supplies, Contactless Delivery, Disinfection Supplies
* **Employee Safety Precautions** (Required)
	+ This field is intended to gather information on specific measures your hotel has taken or plans to take to protect employees.
	+ Example: Face Masks, Gloves, Hand Sanitizer
* **Guest Safety Precautions** (Required)
	+ This field is intended to gather information on specific measures your hotel has taken or plans to take to protect guests.
	+ Example: Face Mask Policy, Hand Sanitizer Stations, Temperature Check
* **Cleaning and Disinfecting** (Optional)
	+ This field is intended to gather information on specific procedures your hotel has taken or plans to take to maintain a clean and disinfected environment.
	+ Please provide detailed and descriptive information.
* **Employee Wellness** (Optional)
	+ This field is intended to gather information on specific measures your hotel has taken or plans to take to support employee wellness.
	+ Please provide detailed and descriptive information.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Optional)
	+ This field is intended to gather information on emergency contact information.
	+ Please list the contact information you would like to provide.
* **Reopening Confirmation** (Required)
	+ Please select 'Yes' if your hotel has reopened or plans to reopen soon, 'No' if your hotel is not planning to reopen.
	+ Please note that the decision to reopen or not will not affect the validity of the form.

## Tips

* Please provide detailed and descriptive information for text fields.
* Ensure that you have the necessary information and knowledge to answer the questions correctly.
* If you are unsure or have any questions, please contact your supervisor or manager.
