# Workplace Safety And Concerns Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about workplace safety concerns and employee responses to ensure a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. If you have any concerns or issues, describe them in the "Comments" field.
4. Attach any supporting documents or evidence in the "Attachments" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**: This page is a general introduction to the form. Please don't forget to sign it when you submit it.
* **Concerns**: Check all the concerns that apply to you.
	+ "Yes": If you're concerned about something.
	+ "No": If you're not concerned.
* **Comments**: Describe any additional comments or information about your concerns or experiences.
* **Additional Info**: Check "Yes" if you have any additional information about your concerns or experiences.
* **Employee**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Date**: Enter the date you're submitting this form (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time**: Enter the time you're submitting this form (in 12-hour clock format, e.g., 08:00 or 14:30).
* **Email**: Enter your company email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your company phone number.
* **Attachments**: Attach any supporting documents or evidence to help resolve your concerns.
* **Additional Comments**: Describe any additional comments or information about your concerns or experiences.

# Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
* If you have any concerns or issues that are not listed, feel free to add them in the "Comments" field.
* Make sure to sign the form when you submit it.
