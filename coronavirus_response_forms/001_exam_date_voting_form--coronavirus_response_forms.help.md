# Exam Date Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Date Voting Form is a tool used to collect responses regarding exam dates for a particular exam session. This form is designed to gather user feedback on the proposed exam dates, allowing the organization to understand which dates are most suitable for the majority of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the proposed exam dates listed on the form.
2. For each proposed date, check the corresponding "confirm" checkbox to indicate whether you agree or disagree with the date.
3. If you have any concerns or issues with a particular date, enter your comments in the "submit" text field.
4. Once you have reviewed and provided feedback on all dates, click the "submit" button to finalize your response.
