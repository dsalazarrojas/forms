# Coronavirus Molecular Testing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Molecular Testing Request Form is designed for individuals or their representatives to request molecular testing related to the Coronavirus. This form helps to gather relevant information for testing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions required for the form.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_input_1`, text, not required): Please enter any relevant information about yourself or the person you are requesting testing for.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name or the name of the person you are requesting testing for.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): If applicable, enter the date of birth for yourself or the person you are requesting testing for.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Provide your contact number or the contact number of the person you are requesting testing for.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email or the email of the person you are requesting testing for.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your address or the address of the person you are requesting testing for.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, not required): If applicable, describe any medical conditions you or the person you are requesting testing for may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select multiple, not required): Select any applicable medical conditions you or the person you are requesting testing for.
* **Test Center** (`test_center`, select one, required): Choose the test center where you or the person you are requesting testing for will be tested.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, not required): If applicable, enter the date for the test.
* **Test Time** (`test_time`, time, not required): If applicable, enter the time for the test.
* **Test Duration** (`test_duration`, text, not required): If applicable, enter the expected duration of the test.
* **Sample Receipt Date** (`sample_receipt_date`, date, not required): If applicable, enter the date for sample receipt.
* **Sample Receipt Time** (`sample_receipt_time`, time, not required): If applicable, enter the time for sample receipt.
* **Sample Receipt Date** ( duplicate of `sample_receipt_date`, date, not required): Please ignore, duplicate field.

Note: Fields marked as required are critical for submission, while fields not marked as required can be omitted.
