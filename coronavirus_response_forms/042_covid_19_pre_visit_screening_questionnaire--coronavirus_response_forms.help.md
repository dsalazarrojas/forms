# COVID 19 Pre Visit Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from visitors to help assess the risk of COVID-19 transmission and ensure a safe environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the scheduled visit date.
3. Answer the COVID-19 screening questions truthfully and accurately.
4. Confirm that you understand and will follow facility COVID-19 policies.
5. Confirm that you are authorized for this visit.
6. Select the date and time you submitted this screening form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (visitor_name): Your full name.
* **Email Address** (visitor_email): Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (visitor_phone): Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Scheduled Visit Date** (visit_date): The date you are scheduled to visit.
* **Reason for Visit** (reason_for_visit): Optional - please provide a brief description of the reason for your visit.
* **Screening Questions**:
	+ **Fever or Chills** (fever): Do you have a fever or chills?
	+ **Cough** (cough): Do you have a cough?
	+ **Shortness of Breath** (shortness_breath): Do you have shortness of breath?
	+ **Sore Throat** (sore_throat): Do you have a sore throat?
	+ **Lost Sense of Taste or Smell** (taste_smell): Have you lost your sense of taste or smell?
	+ **Body Aches** (body_aches): Do you have body aches?
* **Exposure History**:
	+ **Positive for COVID-19** (covid_positive): Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days?
	+ **Close Contact with Someone Positive** (close_contact): Have you had close contact with someone positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days?
	+ **Quarantine** (quarantine): Have you been under quarantine?
* **Visitor Attestation**:
	+ **Truthful Answers** (truthful_answers): I certify that my answers are truthful and accurate.
	+ **Understand Policies** (understand_policies): I understand and will follow facility COVID-19 policies.
	+ **Authorized Visit** (authorized_visit): I am authorized for this visit.
* **Screening Submission Date** (screening_date): The date and time this screening form was submitted.

Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to help us provide a safe environment for everyone. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask.
