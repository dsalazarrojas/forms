# COVID 19 Self Isolation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Self Isolation Form is used to track employee self-isolation status and gather information to ensure the employee's health and safety, as well as the safety of others in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name, department, and manager's name.
2. Select the reason for self-isolation from the options provided.
3. Enter the start and estimated end dates of isolation.
4. Indicate if the employee is capable of working from home during isolation.
5. Check if the employee is experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
6. Enter the date of a positive test, if applicable.
7. Enter the employee's email address for daily health check-ins.
8. Confirm that you have read and will follow the company's COVID-19 leave policy.
9. Certify that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's full name.
* **Department**: Enter the employee's department name.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Reason for Self-Isolation**: Select the reason for self-isolation from the options provided.
	+ Confirmed COVID-19 Positive: The employee has tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Symptoms Present: The employee is experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
	+ Close Contact with Positive Case: The employee has come into contact with someone who has tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Household Exposure: The employee has been exposed to someone in their household who has tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Returning from High-Risk Area: The employee has returned from an area with a high risk of COVID-19 transmission.
* **Start Date of Isolation**: Enter the date the employee began self-isolation.
* **Estimated End Date of Isolation**: Enter the estimated date the employee will end self-isolation.
* **Are you capable and planning to work from home during isolation?**: Indicate if the employee is capable and planning to work from home during isolation.
	+ True: Yes, the employee is capable and planning to work from home.
	+ False: No, the employee is not capable or planning to work from home.
	+ Partially: The employee is capable of working from home, but not full-time.
* **Are you currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms?**: Indicate if the employee is experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
	+ True: Yes, the employee is experiencing symptoms.
	+ False: No, the employee is not experiencing symptoms.
* **Date of Positive Test**: Enter the date the employee tested positive for COVID-19. Leave this field blank if not tested or negative.
* **Contact Email for Daily Health Check-ins**: Enter the employee's email address for daily health check-ins.
* **I have read and agree to follow the company COVID-19 leave policy**: Confirm that the employee has read and will follow the company's COVID-19 leave policy.
	+ True: Yes, the employee has read and agrees to follow the policy.
	+ I agree: The employee has read and agrees to follow the policy.
* **I certify the above information is accurate**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
	+ True: Yes, the information is accurate.
	+ False: No, the information is not accurate.
