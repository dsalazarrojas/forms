# Tourist Resorts Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps tourist resorts provide information about their reopening plans and policies in response to the coronavirus pandemic. It ensures that the resort's details, including general information and their stance on various pandemic-related measures, are accurate and up-to-date.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review each section carefully and ensure you're familiar with the information requested.
*   Enter your resort's general information (name, website, location, contact number, email, and social media details) in the first section.
*   Answer each question in the "General Questions" section with the corresponding option (Yes or No) based on your resort's policies and practices.
*   Be honest and accurate with your responses, as they will be used to update the tourist resort's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
*   **Name of Resort** (`name_of_resort`, text, optional): Enter your resort's name to help users identify it correctly.
*   **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Provide your resort's website URL for easy access to more information.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your resort's location to help users find it easily.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your resort's contact number for users to reach out to you directly.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your resort's email address for communication and updates.
*   **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Share your resort's social media platforms for users to stay connected.

### General Questions
*   **Question 1** (`general_question_1`, select_one, required): Indicate if your resort has implemented any social distancing measures. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 2** (`general_question_2`, select_one, required): Confirm if your resort is open to new guests. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 3** (`general_question_3`, select_one, required): Indicate if your resort has reduced capacity. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 4** (`general_question_4`, select_one, required): Confirm if your resort has changed its cancellation policies. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 5** (`general_question_5`, select_one, required): Indicate if your resort has altered its payment terms. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 6** (`general_question_6`, select_multiple, required): List any new measures your resort has implemented for staff safety. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 7** (`general_question_7`, select_one, required): Confirm if your resort has reduced its operating hours. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 8** (`general_question_8`, select_multiple, required): List any new measures your resort has implemented for guest safety. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 9** (`general_question_9`, select_one, required): Indicate if your resort has altered its menu or food services. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 10** (`general_question_10`, select_multiple, required): List any other measures your resort has implemented for the pandemic. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 11** (`general_question_11`, select_multiple, required): Confirm if your resort has introduced new cleaning and disinfection procedures. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 12** (`general_question_12`, select_one, required): Indicate if your resort has changed its rules on gatherings or events. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 13** (`general_question_13`, select_multiple, required): List any measures your resort has taken to address mental health and wellbeing. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 14** (`general_question_14`, select_one, required): Confirm if your resort has implemented a curfew or restricted access. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 15** (`general_question_15`, select_multiple, required): List any other measures your resort has taken for the pandemic. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 16** (`general_question_16`, select_one, required): Indicate if your resort has changed its policies on mask use. Options: Yes, No
*   **Question 17** (`general_question_17`, select_multiple, required): List any measures your resort has implemented for guests who have been in contact with someone with COVID-19. Options: Yes, No

## Tips
