# Covid 19 Crossfit Risk Assumption and Waiver of Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide Crossfit staff with information about your health conditions, medical history, and emergency contact information to assess and manage potential risks associated with participating in Crossfit activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, indicated by a asterisk (\*) symbol.
3. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact Crossfit staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, *): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address** (`address`, text, *): Enter your current address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, *): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, ): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, ): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, ): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, *): Select the relationship you have with your emergency contact (Parent, Child, Spouse, Other).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, ): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or health concerns you have.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, ): Describe any medical conditions you have that may affect your participation in Crossfit activities.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, ): Describe any medical procedures you have undergone or are currently undergoing.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, ): If applicable, enter the name of your primary care physician.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, ): If applicable, enter the phone number of your primary care physician.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, ): If applicable, enter the email address of your primary care physician.
* **Relationship with Doctor** (`doctor_relationship`, select_one, ): If applicable, select the relationship you have with your primary care physician (Self, Relative, Spouse, Other).
* **Doctor License Number** (`doctor_license_number`, text, ): If applicable, enter your doctor's license number.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, ): If applicable, enter your medical insurance provider and policy number.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, date, ): If applicable, enter the date of your last visit to your primary care physician.
* **Next Visit Date** (`next_visit_date`, date, ): If applicable, enter the date of your next scheduled visit to your primary care physician.
* **Medical Condition Description** (`medical_condition_description`, text, ): Describe any medical conditions you have that may affect your participation in Crossfit activities.
* **Medical Condition Symptoms** (`medical_condition_symptoms`, text, ): Describe any symptoms related to your medical conditions.
* **Other Medical History** (`medical_history_other`, text, ): Describe any other medical history or concerns you have.
* **Risk Assumption** (`risk_assumption`, text, *): Acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with participating in Crossfit activities and that you are aware of your own physical limitations.

Please note that all required fields are indicated by a asterisk (\*) symbol and must be filled out for the form to be complete.
