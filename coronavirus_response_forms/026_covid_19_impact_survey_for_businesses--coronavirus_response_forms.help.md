# COVID 19 Impact Survey For Businesses - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a data collection instrument to gather information about businesses affected by the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page carefully. You can navigate between pages by clicking the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (2, text, required): Enter your business name as it is known to the public.
* **Industry** (3, select_one, required): Select the type of industry your business belongs to from the provided options.
* **Number of Employees** (4, number, required): Enter the current number of employees in your organization.
* **Contact Person** (5, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for contact and communication.
* **Phone Number** (6, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (7, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Current Operating Status** (9, select_one, required): Select the current operating status of your business from the provided options.
* **Reason for closure or limitation** (10, text, optional): If your business is closed or has limitations, describe the reason in this field.
* **Capacity reduction percentage** (11, number, optional): If your business has reduced capacity, enter the percentage of reduction.
* **Monthly revenue loss** (13, select_one, required): Select the estimated monthly revenue loss due to the pandemic from the provided options.
* **Normal monthly revenue before pandemic** (14, number, optional): Enter the normal monthly revenue before the pandemic.
* **Increased operational expenses** (15, select_multiple, optional): Check the expenses that have increased due to the pandemic.
* **Staff reduction percentage** (17, number, optional): If you've reduced staff, enter the percentage of reduction.
* **Plans to rehire or hire new staff** (18, select_one, required): Select whether you have plans to rehire or hire new staff.
* **Employees working remotely** (19, select_one, required): Select the percentage of employees working remotely.
* **Primary challenges faced** (21, select_multiple, required): Select up to three primary challenges faced by your business due to the pandemic.
* **Safety measures implemented** (22, select_multiple, optional): Check the safety measures your business has implemented to mitigate the pandemic.
* **Main challenges faced** (21, select_multiple, optional): If not already selected, describe the main challenges faced by your business in this field.

This guide will help you fill out the form quickly and accurately. If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact the relevant department.
