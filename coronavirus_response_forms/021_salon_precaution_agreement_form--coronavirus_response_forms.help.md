# salon_precaution_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain an agreement and signatures from the salon owner, employee, and manager to follow social distancing and precaution measures to ensure a safe salon environment during the Coronavirus pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the social distancing and precaution measures outlined in the form.
2. Sign and date the agreement section.
3. Enter your name, email, phone number (if applicable), and date (if applicable) in the relevant fields.
4. Sign and date the signature sections for the salon owner, employee, and manager.
5. Complete the form in advance to avoid any delays or issues during your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Agreement** (required)
* The agreement section is where you will sign and date your commitment to follow the social distancing and precaution measures outlined in the form.
