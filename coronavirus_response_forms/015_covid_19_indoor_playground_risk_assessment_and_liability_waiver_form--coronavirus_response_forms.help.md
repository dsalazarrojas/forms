# Covid 19 Indoor Playground Risk Assessment And Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of COVID-19 risks and to obtain a liability waiver before visiting the indoor playground. Please complete this form carefully and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and completely.
3. Select the correct options for each field as needed.
4. Sign the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Assessment** (1): Please provide a written description of your understanding of COVID-19, including any concerns or questions you may have.
* **Understanding COVID-19** (2): Please provide a brief explanation of what you understand about COVID-19, if any.
* **Liability Waiver** (3): Select one of the options to acknowledge that you understand and accept the liability waiver.
* **Playground Safety Rules** (4): Select all that apply to indicate your understanding and agreement to abide by the playground's safety rules.
* **Contact Information** (5): Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Emergency Contact** (6): Please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact in case of an unexpected situation.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (7): Please provide any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Date** (8): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time** (9): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Note** (10): Please provide any additional comments or notes about your visit.
* **Sign Name** (11): Please sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood this form.
* **Sign Email** (12): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Sign Phone** (13): Please enter your phone number.
* **Sign Date** (14): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Sign Time** (15): Enter the time you sign this form.
* **Sign Location** (16): Select the location where you sign this form.
* **Sign Signature** (17): Select all that apply to indicate your signature.
* **Waiver Acknowledgement** (18): Select one of the options to acknowledge that you understand and accept the waiver.

Note: Completing this form is mandatory to enter the indoor playground.
