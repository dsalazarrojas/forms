# Plastic Surgery Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plastic Surgery Patient Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from patients before undergoing plastic surgery. This form helps the medical team at the hospital to gather crucial details about the patient's health history, medical conditions, allergies, and contact information, ensuring a smooth and safe surgical process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your general information, including your name, address, and contact number.
2.  Provide information about your health conditions, medical history, and any allergies you may have.
3.  Answer questions about your vital signs and medical history, including any previous surgeries, medical histories, and contact details for your next of kin.
4.  Choose whether you have given authorization for your medical team to contact you or your next of kin.
5.  Enter your email address and other contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, required): Enter your name, address, and contact number for the medical team to get in touch with you.
*   **Health Condition** (`health_condition`, `text`, required): Describe any health conditions you have, such as chronic diseases or medical conditions that may affect your surgery.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous surgeries, hospitalizations, or medical conditions.
*   **Medical History - Medications** (`medical_history_medications`, `text`, required): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
*   **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`, `text`, required): Describe any previous surgeries, including dates and results.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): Mention any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or substances that may affect your surgery.
*   **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, `text`, required): Provide your current vital signs, such as blood pressure, weight, height, and other relevant medical information.
*   **Anesthesia** (`anesthesia`, `text`, required): Answer questions about your experience with anesthesia and any previous complications.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information, including phone number and address.
*   **Next of Kin** (`next_of_kin`, `text`, required): Provide contact information for your next of kin, in case of an emergency.
*   **Medical History - Surgeon** (`medical_history_surgeon`, `text`, required): Describe your previous interactions with surgeons, including any complications or outcomes.
*   **Medical History - Anesthesist** (`medical_history_anesthesist`, `text`, required): Describe your experience with anesthesists and any complications or outcomes.
*   **Medical History - Surgeon 2** (`medical_history_surgeon_surgeon`, `text`, required): Describe your experience with surgeons, including any complications or outcomes.
*   **Medical History - Consultant** (`medical_history_consultant`, `text`, required): Describe your experience with medical consultants and any complications or outcomes.
*   **Patient Authorization** (`patient_authorization`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you have given authorization for your medical team to contact you or your next of kin.
*   **Medical History - Hospital** (`medical_history_hospital`, `text`, required): Describe your experience with hospitals, including any complications or outcomes.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for the medical team to get in touch with you.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for the medical team to get in touch with you.
*   **Patient Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address for the medical team to get in touch with you.
*   **Patient Authorization 2** (`patient_authorization2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you have given authorization for your medical team to contact you or your next of kin.

## Tips
*   Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the medical team has the necessary information for your surgery.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your doctor or nurse for assistance.
*   Remember to provide your email address and contact number for the medical team to get in touch with you.
*   Choose whether you have given authorization for your medical team to contact you or your next of kin.
*   If you have any medical history or allergies, please be as specific as possible when providing information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your doctor or nurse for assistance.
