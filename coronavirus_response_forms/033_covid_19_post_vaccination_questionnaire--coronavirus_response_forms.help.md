# Covid 19 Post Vaccination Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid-19 Post Vaccination Questionnaire is designed to collect information from patients after receiving the vaccination to ensure the effectiveness and safety of the vaccination program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please ensure that you have received the Covid-19 vaccination and are answering based on the specific vaccination you received.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional):
    This field is where you should enter any additional information about yourself, such as your medical history or any other relevant details.
* **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, select_one, optional):
    This field is for you to indicate if you are experiencing any side effects after receiving the vaccination. Select "Yes" if you are experiencing any side effects, or "No" if you are not.
* **Vaccine Dosage** (`vaccine_dosage`, number, optional):
    If you are unsure about the dosage of the vaccine you received, please enter it here.
* **Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, optional):
    If you have any known allergies, please list them here.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, select_one, optional):
    This field is where you select the site where you received the vaccination (arm, leg, abdomen, chest).
