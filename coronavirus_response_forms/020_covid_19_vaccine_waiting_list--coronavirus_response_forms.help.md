# Covid 19 Vaccine Waiting List - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage data for individuals interested in getting vaccinated against COVID-19. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select each field according to your preference and the information you have available.
2. Make sure all required fields are filled in.
3. Review all your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your personal phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your personal email address.
* **Birth Date** (`birthdate`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY'.
* **Gender** (`gender`, text, optional): Select your gender.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Pharmacy** (`pharmacy`, text, optional): Enter the name of your pharmacy.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Medical Condition Note** (`medical_condition_note`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your medical condition if necessary.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vaccination site.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to receive your vaccination.
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to receive your vaccination.
* **Vaccination Dose** (`vaccination_dose`, text, optional): Enter the type and number of the vaccination dose you plan to receive.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, text, optional): Enter your current vaccination status.
* **Vaccine Dose Number** (`vaccine_dose_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the vaccine dose you plan to receive.
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of vaccine you plan to receive.
* **Vaccination Site Address** (`vaccination_site_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the vaccination site.
* **Vaccination Site Phone** (`vaccination_site_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the vaccination site.
* **Vaccination Site Email** (`vaccination_site_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the vaccination site.
* **Follow Up Contact Date** (`follow_up_contact_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to be contacted for follow up.
* **Follow Up Contact Time** (`follow_up_contact_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to be contacted for follow up.
