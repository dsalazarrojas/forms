# self_certification_of_vaccination_status - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to self-certify their vaccination status, providing essential information for tracking and verifying their vaccination records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Employer ID and Name to associate your identity with your certification.
2. Enter your Employee ID and Name for your own identification.
3. Provide your email address for confirmation and contact purposes.
4. Indicate your vaccination status as Fully vaccinated, Partly vaccinated, or Not vaccinated.
5. Enter the Date of First Vaccination and Date of Last Vaccination, if applicable.
6. Select the type of vaccine you received (Moderna, Pfizer, or Johnson & Johnson).
7. Enter the Batch ID and Lot Number of the vaccine for tracking purposes.
8. Enter the Expiration Date of the vaccine for verification.
9. Enter the Certification Expiration Date to ensure the certification remains valid.
10. Sign and date the form as the certifying employee and the employer.
11. Enter the date the form was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer ID**: Enter the unique ID given to you by your employer for tracking purposes. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employment records. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Employee ID**: Enter your ID as assigned by the employer for identification. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Email**: Confirm your email address for contact purposes. (<name>, <type>: email, optional)
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for additional contact purposes. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Vaccination Status**: Indicate your vaccination status: Fully vaccinated, Partly vaccinated, or Not vaccinated. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Date of First Vaccination**: Enter the date you received your first vaccination dose, if applicable. (<name>, <type>: date, optional)
* **Date of Last Vaccination**: Enter the date you received your last vaccination dose, if applicable. (<name>, <type>: date, optional)
* **Vaccine Dose**: Enter the total number of vaccine doses you received. (<name>, <type>: number, required)
* **Vaccine Type**: Select the type of vaccine you received (Moderna, Pfizer, or Johnson & Johnson). (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Batch ID**: Enter the batch ID of the vaccine for tracking. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Lot Number**: Enter the lot number of the vaccine for tracking. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of the vaccine for verification. (<name>, <type>: date, optional)
* **Certification Expiration Date**: Enter the date this certification will expire. (<name>, <type>: date, required)
* **Certification Date**: Enter the date this form was certified. (<name>, <type>: date, optional)
* **Employer Signature**: Sign and date the form as the certifying employee. (<name>, <type>: note, required)
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date the form as the employee. (<name>, <type>: note, required)
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date this form was signed. (<name>, <type>: date, required)
* **Certifying Employee**: Enter the name of the employee certifying the form. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Certifying Employee ID**: Enter the ID of the certifying employee. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of your employer. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Employer Address**: Enter the address of your employer. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Email Confirmation**: Confirm your email address. (<name>, <type>: email, optional)
* **Phone Confirmation**: Confirm your contact phone number. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
