# Self Quarantine Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off due to a quarantine situation. It helps us to track and manage the number of employees who are quarantined and need to take time off.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the Employee name from the list of available options.
2. Enter the reason for requesting time off.
3. Enter the date you started your quarantine.
4. Enter the date you expect to return to work.
5. Enter your contact information (email or phone number).
6. Select your manager from the list of available options.
7. Enter any additional information from your manager.
8. Sign your name as the employee.
9. Sign your manager's name as confirmation.
10. Date the form with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee**: Please select your name from the list of options. This information will be used to identify you and track your request.
* **Reason for requesting time off**: Explain why you need to take time off due to a quarantine situation. This will help us understand the circumstances and provide the necessary support.
* **Quarantine start date**: Enter the date you started your quarantine. This will help us track the duration of your quarantine.
* **Return to work date**: Enter the date you expect to return to work. This will help us plan and prepare for your return.
* **Contact information**: Enter your email or phone number. This will help us contact you and provide updates on your request.
* **Manager**: Please select your manager from the list of options. They will review and approve your request.
* **Manager notes**: Enter any additional information from your manager. This will help them understand your request and provide guidance.
* **Employee signature**: Please sign your name as confirmation of your request.
* **Manager signature**: Please sign your manager's name as confirmation of your approval.
* **Date**: Date the form with the current date. This will ensure the form is up-to-date and accurate.
