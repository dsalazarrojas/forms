# Wisconsin COVID 19 Hardship Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wisconsin COVID 19 Hardship Attestation Form is a document that allows individuals to self-certify their status as experiencing COVID-19 related hardship. This form is used to determine eligibility for various programs and services that can provide relief during this time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2. Provide your household information in the "Household Info" field.
3. Enter your income information in the "Income Info" field.
4. Describe your current hardship situation in the "Hardship Info" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you are experiencing hardship in the "Select All" field.
6. Confirm your selection by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Select All" field.
7. If you are experiencing an unexpected hardship not listed, describe it in the "Other Hardship" field.
8. Provide more details about your hardship situation in the "Other Hardship Details" field.
9. Sign the "Signature" field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
10. Enter the current date in the "Date" field.
11. Optionally, enter the time of completion in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address to ensure we can contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **Household Info**: Provide information about the household members affected by the hardship, including their names and relation to you.
* **Income Info**: Enter your current income information, including your job title, income range, and any other relevant details.
* **Hardship Info**: Describe the specific hardship you are experiencing, such as lost income or increased expenses.
* **Select All**: Confirm that you are experiencing hardship by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Confirm Select All**: Confirm your selection in the "Select All" field to ensure accuracy.
* **Other Hardship**: If you are experiencing an unexpected hardship not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Other Hardship Details**: Provide more details about your hardship situation, if applicable.
* **Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Date**: Enter the current date to confirm when you completed the form.
* **Time**: Optionally, enter the time of completion for your records.
