# Protection Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Protection Pledge Form is a legal document that requires employees or students to confirm their understanding and commitment to protect themselves, their colleagues, or students from the spread of COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Employee Name** (or **Student ID Number**), which is a required field that needs to be accurately filled out.
2. Enter your **Date** (optional but recommended) in the format of mm/dd/yyyy. This will help us track any changes or updates to your protection pledge.
3. If you wish to specify the time you completed the form, enter your **Time** (optional) in the format of 12:00 AM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, number, **required**): Enter your student ID number if you are a student. This is not applicable for employees.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy to track any changes or updates to your protection pledge.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time in the format of 12:00 AM to specify when you completed the form.
