# Policy Change Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Change Notification Form is designed to collect information about policy changes within the company. This form is used to notify management of policy updates, modifications, or cancellations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the page with the policy change details.
2. Fill out the fields as required, providing accurate and concise information.
3. Submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Policy Changes** (Page 1):
	+ **Policy Changes**: A brief description of the policy change (required).
* **Employees Affected** (Page 2):
	+ **Employees Affected**: Check 'Yes' if any employees are affected by this policy change. (optional)
* **Policy Change Date** (Page 3):
	+ **Policy Change Date**: The date when the policy change was made or will take effect. (required)
* **Description** (Page 4):
	+ **Description**: A detailed description of the policy change, including its implications and effects. (required)
* **Effective Date** (Page 5):
	+ **Effective Date**: The date when the policy change will become effective. (required)
* **Email** (Page 6):
	+ **Email**: The email address of the employee submitting the form. (required)

## Tips
- Be accurate and concise when filling out the form.
- If unsure about a field, contact your supervisor or the relevant department for guidance.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
