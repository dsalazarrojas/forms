# Coronavirus Case Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about individuals who have been affected by the coronavirus. The form is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to gather relevant details about the case, including contact information, exposure sites, symptoms, and travel history. The purpose of this form is to facilitate the reporting of coronavirus cases to the relevant authorities and healthcare organizations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Submit Case" option at the bottom of the form. You will be asked to confirm your decision to report the case.
2. Enter the relevant details on each page of the form, including your contact information, exposure site, symptoms, and travel history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Case Information** (Page 2)
* **Case Information** (`case_info`, text, required): Please provide as much detail as possible about the case you are reporting.

### **Contact Information** (Page 3)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information, such as your name, email address, and phone number.

### **Exposure Site** (Page 4)
* **Exposure Site** (`exposure_site`, text, required): Describe the location where you or someone in your household was exposed to the coronavirus, such as a workplace, school, or other relevant location.

### **Exposure Duration** (Page 5)
* **Exposure Duration** (`exposure_duration`, number, required): Estimate the duration of the exposure, including the start and end dates.

### **Symptoms** (Page 6)
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Describe the symptoms you or someone in your household is experiencing, such as fever, cough, shortness of breath, etc.

### **Travel History** (Page 7)
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required): Provide information about any recent travel, including the location, date, and duration of travel.

### **Travel Location** (Page 8)
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, text, required): Specify the location(s) you or someone in your household has traveled to recently.

### **Healthcare Provider Info** (Page 9)
* **Healthcare Provider Info** (`healthcare_provider_info`, text, required): If you have seen a healthcare provider, please provide their name, contact information, and any relevant medical information.

### **Contact Number** (Page 10)
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person, such as a family member or healthcare provider.

### **Case Confirmed** (Page 11)
* **Case Confirmed** (`case_confirmed`, select_one, required): Select "Confirmed" if you have been confirmed to have the coronavirus, or "Not Confirmed" if you are waiting on test results or have not been tested.

### **Submit Case** (Page 12)
* **Submit Case** (`submit_case`, select_one, required): Select "Submit" to confirm your report, or "Cancel" to exit the report.
