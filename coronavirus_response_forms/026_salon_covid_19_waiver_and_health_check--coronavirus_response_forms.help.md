# Salon COVID 19 WAIVER AND HEALTH CHECK - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from clients to ensure a safe and healthy environment for all clients and staff in the salon. It's essential to complete this form accurately to avoid any potential risks associated with COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in the required information for each field.
3. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Client understands**
* **Client understands** (`client_understands`, `note`, required/optional): Please read and agree to the salon's COVID-19 policies and procedures. Check the box to confirm you understand and agree to these policies.

### 2. **Contact information**
* **Contact information** (`contact_info`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your contact information so we can reach you if needed. This includes your phone number and any other relevant contact details.

### 3. **Health Check**
* **Health Check** (`health_check`, `time`, required/optional): Report the last time you had a health check or any concerns you may have about your health.

### 4. **Travel history**
* **Travel history** (`travel_history`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate if you have recently traveled internationally or to any areas with high COVID-19 infection rates.

### 5. **Exposure risk**
* **Exposure risk** (`exposure_risk`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check if you have been exposed to anyone with COVID-19 symptoms or if you have been in close contact with someone who has tested positive.

### 6. **Waiver acknowledgement**
* **Waiver acknowledgement** (`waiver_acknowledgement`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the box to acknowledge that you have read and understood the salon's COVID-19 policies and procedures regarding health and safety.

### 7. **Waiver text**
* **Waiver text** (`waiver_text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments related to the waiver. This is an optional field for any further details or clarification.

### 8. **Review Date**
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time of your last review or update of this form.

Note: Some fields may be optional, but it's essential to fill them out accurately and honestly to ensure your safety and the safety of others.
