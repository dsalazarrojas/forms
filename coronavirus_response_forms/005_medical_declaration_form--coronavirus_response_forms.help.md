# Medical Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Declaration Form is designed to collect essential medical information from individuals to aid healthcare professionals in providing accurate and efficient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading this guide carefully to understand each field's purpose and what is required.
2. Fill in your travel details, including any recent trips or international travel.
3. Provide your contact information so we can reach you if necessary.
4. If you are experiencing any symptoms, select all that apply from the list.
5. Choose your current health status: Healthy, Sick, or Recovered.
6. In the medical declaration form submission field, please describe your medical situation, including any relevant medical history or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide any recent or upcoming travel, including countries visited or traveled through, as well as any dates.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information so we can reach you if needed. This will help us to keep you updated on your medical care.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as Fever, Cough, or Fatigue.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your current health status: Healthy, Sick, or Recovered.
* **Medical Declaration Form Submission** (`medical_declaration_form_submission`, text, required/optional): Describe your medical situation, including any relevant medical history or conditions. 

## Tips
* Be as accurate and honest as possible when filling out this form, as it is crucial for healthcare professionals to provide the best possible care.
* If you are unsure about any field or information, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Remember to update your contact information if your status changes.
* This form is designed to be used for medical purposes only; please do not use it for other purposes. 
* If you need help or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
