# COVID 19 Vaccine Card Replacement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a replacement COVID-19 vaccination card due to loss, damage, or other reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information (full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and current address).
2. Select the reason for the replacement card.
3. Provide details about your vaccination history, including the name of the original vaccinating facility, date of first dose, type of first dose vaccine, and any additional doses or boosters received.
4. Choose your preferred method of receiving the replacement card.
5. Verify your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your current street address.
* **Reason for Replacement** (`reason_for_replacement`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided reasons for requesting a replacement card.
	+ Lost Original Card
	+ Damaged or Illegible Card
	+ Card Was Never Provided
	+ Need Official Copy
	+ Name Change
	+ Other Reason
* **If Other, Please Explain** (`other_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a specific reason for the card replacement request.
* **Original Vaccinating Facility Name** (`vaccinating_facility`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the facility where you received your vaccination.
* **Facility City and State** (`facility_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the city and state of the facility where you received your vaccine.
* **First Dose Date** (`first_dose_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your first dose vaccination.
* **First Dose Vaccine Type** (`first_dose_vaccine`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of vaccine administered for your first dose.
	+ Pfizer-BioNTech
	+ Moderna
	+ Johnson & Johnson
	+ AstraZeneca
	+ Novavax
	+ Other
	+ Unsure
* **Second Dose Date** (`second_dose_date`, `date`, optional): If you received a second dose, enter the date of the second dose.
* **Second Dose Vaccine Type** (`second_dose_vaccine`, `select_one`, optional): If you received a different type of vaccine for your second dose, select one of the provided options.
	+ Same as First Dose
	+ Different from First Dose
	+ N/A
	+ Unsure
* **Number of Booster Doses Received** (`booster_doses_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of booster doses you have received.
* **Most Recent Booster Date** (`last_booster_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your most recent booster dose.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to receive your replacement card.
	+ Email
	+ Mail to Current Address
	+ Pick Up at Facility
	+ Digital Record Only
* **Type of ID for Verification** (`id_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of identification you will provide for verification.
	+ Driver License
	+ Passport
	+ State ID
	+ Medical Records
	+ Other
* **ID Number** (`id_number`, `text`, required): Enter the number on your chosen identification.
* **Consent to Verification** (`consent_statement`, `select_one`, required): Select if you consent to the verification of your vaccination record.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
