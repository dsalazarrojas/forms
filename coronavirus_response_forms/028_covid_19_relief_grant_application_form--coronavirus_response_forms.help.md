# COVID 19 Relief Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses affected by the COVID-19 pandemic to determine their eligibility for relief grants. The goal is to provide financial support to businesses that have experienced significant revenue loss due to the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your business's legal name as it appears on your business license or tax return.
3. Select the type of business entity you are applying as.
4. Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
5. Enter your business's annual revenue for 2019.
6. Calculate and enter the estimated percentage of revenue loss for 2020/2021 compared to 2019.
7. Enter the current number of employees in your business.
8. Enter the grant amount you are requesting.
9. Describe how you intend to use the grant funds.
10. Provide documentation to prove your business's existence.
11. Enter your business's physical address.
12. Enter your primary contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Business Name** (\*: required): Enter your business's legal name as it appears on your business license or tax return.
* **Business Type** (\*: required): Select the type of business entity you are applying as.
	+ Sole Proprietorship: A sole proprietorship is a business owned by a single individual.
	+ Partnership: A partnership is a business owned by two or more individuals with shared ownership and profits/losses.
	+ LLC: A limited liability company is a business structure that provides liability protection for its owners.
	+ S-Corp: A S-Corp is a type of corporation that provides liability protection for its shareholders.
	+ Non-Profit: A non-profit organization is a business that operates for a social cause or charity.
* **Years in Operation** (\*: required): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Annual Revenue Pre-COVID (2019)** (\*: required): Enter your business's annual revenue for 2019.
* **Estimated Percentage Revenue Loss** (\*: required): Calculate the estimated percentage of revenue loss for 2020/2021 compared to 2019.
* **Current Number of Employees** (\*: required): Enter the current number of employees in your business, including full-time and part-time employees.
* **Grant Amount Requested** (\*: required): Enter the grant amount you are requesting.
* **Intended Use of Funds** (\*: required): Describe how you intend to use the grant funds.
* **Documentation to Prove Business Existence** (\*: required): Provide documentation to prove your business's existence, such as:
	+ Business License
	+ Tax Return
	+ Articles of Incorporation
* **Business Address** (\*: required): Enter your business's physical address.
* **Primary Contact Email** (\*: required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **I Certify** (\*: required): I certify that all information in this application is true and complete.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us for assistance.
* We will review your application carefully and respond promptly with further instructions or questions.
* Please note that this form is not a guarantee of approval, and we will review each application on a case-by-case basis.
