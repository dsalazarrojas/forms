# COVID 19 Phase 1A Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about the individual's eligibility for the COVID-19 vaccination program, specifically for healthcare workers and long-term care residents.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Please answer the following questions to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address where you can be reached for vaccination appointments.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Which Phase 1A Category Applies to You?** (`phase_1a_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Healthcare worker
	+ Long-term care resident
	+ Both
* **Healthcare Facility Name (if applicable)** (`healthcare_facility`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the healthcare facility where you work.
* **Known Allergies** (`known_allergies`, `text`, required): List any known allergies you have.
* **Do you have Chronic Health Conditions?** (`chronic_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Answer with:
	+ False
	+ True
* **Are you Immunocompromised?** (`immunocompromised`, `select_one`, required): Answer with:
	+ False
	+ True
	+ Unsure
* **Have you received COVID-19 Vaccine Before?** (`previous_covid_vaccine`, `select_one`, required): Answer with:
	+ False
	+ True
* **Vaccine Preference (if available)** (`preferred_vaccine`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from:
	+ No preference
	+ Pfizer
	+ Moderna
	+ Johnson & Johnson
* **I certify that I meet Phase 1A Eligibility Criteria** (`certify_eligibility`, `select_one`, required): Answer with:
	+ True
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you are registering for the vaccination.

This guide provides step-by-step instructions and explanations for each field to help users complete the form accurately and efficiently.
