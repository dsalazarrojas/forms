# travel_movement_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel movement declaration form is used to declare individual travel movements to ensure public health safety measures are in place. It is essential to fill out this form when you have traveled outside.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out the form for the correct reason and only once for each travel event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Daily health temperature checks
* **Daily health temperature checks** (`daily_health_temperature_check`, text, not required): This field is not required, but please note any health checks or symptoms you may have experienced during your trip.

### Have you traveled outside?
* **Have you traveled outside** (`have_you_traveled_outside`, select_one, required): Please select the option that best describes your travel experience:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Travel date
* **Travel date** (`travel_date`, date, not required): This field is not required, but if you traveled on a specific date, please enter it.

### Travel location
* **Travel location** (`travel_location`, select_one, required): Please select the option that best describes your travel location:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Return time
* **Return time** (`return_time`, time, not required): This field is not required, but if you traveled for multiple days, please enter the time you returned.

### Reason for travel
* **Reason for travel** (`reason_for_travel`, text, not required): This field is not required, but if you would like to provide a reason for your travel, please do so.

### Travel frequency
* **Travel frequency** (`travel_frequency`, text, not required): This field is not required, but if you would like to provide information on how often you travel, please do so.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): This field is not required, but if you have any additional comments or information about your travel, please enter them.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have already filled this form for a travel event, please do not resubmit it.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
