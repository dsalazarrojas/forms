# Covid 19 Mandatory Daily Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect daily health information from patients, helping healthcare providers assess their condition and provide necessary care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate through the sections: 
   - Patient Information
   - General Health Questionnaire
   - Fever Questionnaire
   - Respiratory Questionnaire
   - Travel History
   - Symptom Questionnaire
   - Exposure History
   - Test Results
   - Vaccination Status
   - Contact Tracing
   - Emergency Contact
   - Medication
   - Allergies
   - Medical Conditions
   - Medical Procedure
   - Travel Restrictions
   - Other
   - Patient Signature
   - Medical Provider Info
   - Medical Provider Contact
   - Other Signature
   - Medical Procedure 2
   - Medical Condition 2
   - Contact Info
   - Medical Provider

2. Fill out each section with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`id: 1`, `title: Patient Information`, `label: Patient Information`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is intended for the patient to input their name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal information. 
* **General Health Questionnaire** (`id: 2`, `title: General Health Questionnaire`, `label: General Health Questionnaire`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section collects general information about the patient's health status, such as recent illnesses, medical conditions, and any other health-related information.
* **Fever Questionnaire** (`id: 3`, `title: Fever Questionnaire`, `label: Fever Questionnaire`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is designed to gather information about the patient's fever, including when the fever started and any recent medical treatment.
* **Respiratory Questionnaire** (`id: 4`, `title: Respiratory Questionnaire`, `label: Respiratory Questionnaire`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section collects information about the patient's respiratory system, including recent coughs, difficulty breathing, or other respiratory issues.
* **Travel History** (`id: 5`, `title: Travel History`, `label: Travel History`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section gathers information about the patient's recent travel history, including places visited and dates of travel.
* **Symptom Questionnaire** (`id: 6`, `title: Symptom Questionnaire`, `label: Symptom Questionnaire`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is intended to collect information about any recent symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as headaches, fatigue, or loss of taste or smell.
* **Exposure History** (`id: 7`, `title: Exposure History`, `label: Exposure History`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section gathers information about the patient's exposure to potential COVID-19 sources, such as close contacts or shared living spaces.
* **Test Results** (`id: 8`, `title: Test Results`, `label: Test Results`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is designed to collect information about any recent medical test results, including COVID-19 test results.
* **Vaccination Status** (`id: 9`, `title: Vaccination Status`, `label: Vaccination Status`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section gathers information about the patient's vaccination status, including any recent vaccinations or immunizations.
* **Contact Tracing** (`id: 10`, `title: Contact Tracing`, `label: Contact Tracing`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is intended to collect information about the patient's recent contacts, including names, addresses, and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`id: 11`, `title: Emergency Contact`, `label: Emergency Contact`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section gathers information about the patient's emergency contact, including name, address, phone number, and other relevant information.
* **Medication** (`id: 12`, `title: Medication`, `label: Medication`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is designed to collect information about any prescribed medications the patient is taking.
* **Allergies** (`id: 13`, `title: Allergies`, `label: Allergies`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section gathers information about any known allergies the patient has, including allergies to medications or substances.
* **Medical Conditions** (`id: 14`, `title: Medical Conditions`, `label: Medical Conditions`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section collects information about any pre-existing medical conditions the patient has, such as heart conditions, diabetes, or other chronic conditions.
* **Medical Procedure** (`id: 15`, `title: Medical Procedure`, `label: Medical Procedure`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is intended to gather information about any recent medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Travel Restrictions** (`id: 16`, `title: Travel Restrictions`, `label: Travel Restrictions`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section gathers information about any travel restrictions or limitations the patient has, such as quarantines or travel bans.
* **Other** (`id: 17`, `title: Other`, `label: Other`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is designed to collect any other relevant information about the patient's health status or medical history.
* **Patient Signature** (`id: 18`, `title: Patient Signature`, `label: Patient Signature`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is intended to gather the patient's digital signature, acknowledging that the form has been completed and understood.
* **Medical Provider Info** (`id: 19`, `title: Medical Provider Info`, `label: Medical Provider Info`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section gathers information about the patient's healthcare provider's contact information.
* **Medical Provider Contact** (`id: 20`, `title: Medical Provider Contact`, `label: Medical Provider Contact`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is intended to gather the contact information for the patient's healthcare provider.
* **Other Signature** (`id: 21`, `title: Other Signature`, `label: Other Signature`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is designed to gather the digital signature of the healthcare provider, acknowledging that the form has been reviewed.
* **Medical Procedure 2** (`id: 22`, `title: Medical Procedure 2`, `label: Medical Procedure 2`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is intended to collect information about any recent medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`id: 23`, `title: Medical Condition 2`, `label: Medical Condition 2`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section gathers information about any pre-existing medical conditions the patient has.
* **Contact Info** (`id: 24`, `title: Contact Info`, `label: Contact Info`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is designed to gather any additional contact information about the patient.
* **Medical Provider** (`id: 25`, `title: Medical Provider`, `label: Medical Provider`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is intended to gather the healthcare provider's name, address, and contact information.

## Tips

* Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
* Be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about a question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is intended to be filled out daily, so please be sure to complete it regularly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
