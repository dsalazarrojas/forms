# COVID 19 Vaccine Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in getting vaccinated against COVID-19 to aid in the vaccine distribution process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to enter all required fields. 
2. Use the provided options for questions that have multiple choices, and select all that apply.
3. If you have any additional information that you think would be helpful, use the text fields to provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`interested_name`, text, required): This is the first and last name of the person interested in getting vaccinated. Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`interested_email`, email, required): This is the email address where you can be contacted for vaccine updates and availability. Please enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`interested_phone`, text, required): This is the phone number where you can be reached for further information and updates. Please enter your valid phone number.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required): Please select how you would like to be contacted about vaccine availability and updates. You can choose between Email, Phone call, Text message, or No preference.
* **-- Vaccination Interest --** (`interest_section`, note, required): Please describe why you are interested in getting vaccinated. This is an optional but required field for us to understand your interest.
* **I am interested in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine** (`interested_vaccine`, select_one, required): Please select if you are interested in getting vaccinated. You can choose between Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **When would you like to be vaccinated** (`vaccine_timeline`, select_one, required): Please select when you would like to be vaccinated. You can choose between As soon as possible, Within 1 week, Within 1 month, Within 3 months, Flexible, or Not sure yet.
* **Vaccine Type Preference** (`vaccine_preference`, select_one, required): Please select the type of vaccine you would prefer to receive. You can choose between Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, No preference, or Unsure.
* **What dose are you looking for** (`dose_type`, select_one, required): Please select the dose of vaccine you are looking for. You can choose between First dose, Second dose, Booster dose, or Unsure.
* **-- Personal Information --** (`personal_info_section`, note, required): This section is to collect personal information about you for the vaccine distribution process. Please fill out the fields below accurately.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYY.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Please select your age group from the provided options.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Please enter your city name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Please enter your state name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Please enter your zip code as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **-- Health Status --** (`health_section`, note, required): This section is to collect information about your health status for the vaccine distribution process. Please fill out the fields below accurately.
* **Do you have health conditions that put you at higher risk** (`health_conditions`, select_one, required): Please select if you have any health conditions that put you at higher risk. You can choose between Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **If yes, describe your conditions** (`condition_description`, text, required): If you select Yes, please describe your health conditions in the fields below.
* **Have you had COVID-19 before** (`prior_covid`, select_one, required): Please select if you have had COVID-19 before. You can choose between Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **Have you received COVID-19 vaccine before** (`prior_vaccine`, select_one, required): Please select if you have received COVID-19 vaccine before. You can choose between Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **If yes, which vaccine** (`prior_vaccine_type`, text, required): If you select Yes, please enter the type of vaccine you received.
* **-- Concerns and Questions --** (`concerns_section`, note, required): Please describe any concerns or questions you have about the vaccine.
* **Do you have concerns about the vaccine** (`vaccine_concerns`, select_one, required): Please select if you have any concerns about the vaccine. You can choose between Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **If yes, describe your concerns** (`concern_description`, text, required): If you select Yes, please describe your concerns in the fields below.
* **What additional information would help you** (`information_needed`, text, required): Please enter any additional information that would help you with the vaccine distribution process.
* **-- Follow-Up --** (`follow_up_section`, note, required): Please provide any contact information for us to follow up with you.
* **I consent to be contacted about vaccine availability** (`contact_consent`, select_one, required): Please select if you consent to be contacted about vaccine availability. You can choose between Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **I would like to receive vaccine information updates** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, optional): Please select if you would like to receive vaccine information updates. You can choose between Yes, No, or Unsure.
