# Health And Wellness Center Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health And Wellness Center Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals registering to use the center's services. This form helps us to create a record of your personal, contact, family, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information you feel comfortable sharing.
2. Enter your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
3. If applicable, enter family member information, including their name and relationship to you.
4. Identify your emergency contact person, including their name and contact information.
5. Review and submit your completed form to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Info
• **Personal Info** (Personal Info, text, required: false): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed at the center.

### Contact Info
• **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required: false): Enter your email address and phone number as we will use this to contact you.

### Family Info
• **Family Info** (Family Info, text, required: false): If you have family members registered with us, please list their names and relationships (e.g., spouse, child).

### Emergency Contact
• **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person we can reach out to in case of an urgent situation.

## Tips
Please ensure to fill out this form accurately and completely, as this information is used to create a record of your registration. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
