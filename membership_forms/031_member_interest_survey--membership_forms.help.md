# Member Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your interests and preferences as a member of our community. The data collected will help us better understand your needs and tailor our services to suit your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number on the first three fields.
2. Select the category that best describes you from the "Which category do you belong to?" field.
3. Choose the events or programs that you are most interested in from the "Which events or programs are you most interested in?" field.
4. Enter your date of birth and date of completion of degree, if applicable.
5. Optionally, you can add any comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
6. If you have any other interests, you can add them in the "What are some of your interests?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name** (`member_interest_survey`, text, required): Enter your full name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Which category do you belong to?** (`member_category`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following categories: beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
- **Which events or programs are you most interested in?** (`event_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all the events or programs that interest you from the options provided.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
- **Date of completion of degree** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of completion of degree in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2010-01-01) (only if applicable).
- **Comments** (`note`, note, required): Add any comments or notes you have.
- **What are some of your interests?** (`member_interests`, note, required): Add any other interests you have.
