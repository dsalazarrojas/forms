# Members Joining Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Members Joining Form is a tool for collecting information from individuals who wish to join a group, community, or organization. This form will help us track and process your membership request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with your details. You can do this by typing your responses into the text fields and selecting the "Submit" button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Join Request** (`join_request`, text, optional)
The Join Request field is a text area where you can briefly describe why you are interested in joining.

### **Full Name** (`name`, text, optional)
Enter your full name as you would like to be recognized in our records.

### **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Please provide a valid email address associated with your membership request.

### **Address** (`address`, text, optional)
Enter your address where you would like to be reached for any further communication or membership updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields accurately and correctly.
* Ensure your email address is a valid one to help us contact you efficiently.
* If you have any trouble filling in the form or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
