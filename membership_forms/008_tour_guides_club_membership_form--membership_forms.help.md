# Tour Guides Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as per your preference. The form is split into four sections: General Info, Member Info, Tour Preferences, and Membership Plan.
2. In each section, you will find a field with an optional label. You can type in the information accordingly.
3. Once you have filled in all or some of the fields, click the submit button (not shown in this form representation) to complete and submit your application.
