# Hunting Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in joining a hunting club. It helps the club's administrators to verify the identity and membership status of potential members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. If you have any favorite prophets, list them in the "Prophets" field.
3. Enter your Email address (if you have one).
4. Provide your Phone Number (if you have one).
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. For deceased members, please enter your Date of Death.
7. Select your preferred Hunting License from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter your Hunting License Expiry Date (if applicable).
9. If you are already a member, select your Member Since duration.
10. Set your Annual Hunters Fees (if applicable).
11. Indicate your Hunting License Status.
12. Choose your Membership Status.
13. Select your preferred Hunting Club.
14. Choose the authority that issued your Hunting License.
15. Select your Hunting License Type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Prophets** (Select Multiple, Required): If you have any favorite prophets, list them here.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted by the club.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by the club.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Date of Death** (Date, Required for deceased members): Enter your date of death for deceased members.
* **Hunting License** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred hunting license.
* **Hunting License Expiry** (Date, Optional): Enter your hunting license expiry date if applicable.
* **Member Since** (Time, Optional): Select your member since duration if you are already a member.
* **Annual Hunters Fees** (Number, Optional): Enter your annual hunters fees if applicable.
* **Hunting License Status** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate your hunting license status.
* **Membership Status** (Select One, Required): Choose your membership status.
* **Hunting Club** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred hunting club.
* **Hunting License Issued By** (Select One, Required): Choose the authority that issued your hunting license.
* **Hunting License Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your hunting license type.

Note: Please make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and truthfully to ensure proper membership enrollment.
