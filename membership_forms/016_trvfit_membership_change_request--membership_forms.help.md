# TRVFIT Membership Change Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TRVFIT Membership Change Request form is used to submit a request to change your membership status. This form is typically used by members who need to update their membership details, such as membership type or status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide a brief description of the membership change request** (`Membership Change Request`)
	* This field is a free-text field where you can provide a brief explanation for your membership change request.
2. **Provide detailed information about the membership change** (`Membership details`)
	* In this field, provide a detailed explanation of the reason for your membership change request.
3. **Select the new membership status** (`membership_status`)
	* Choose the new status you would like to have after the membership change from the dropdown list:
		+ Option 1
		+ Option 2
		+ Option 3
4. **Choose the new membership type** (`membership_type`)
	* Select the new type of membership you would like to have after the membership change from the dropdown list:
		+ Option 1
		+ Option 2
		+ Option 3
5. **Enter the effective date of the membership change** (`membership_effective_date`)
	* Enter the date when the membership change should take effect.
6. **Enter the end date of the membership** (`membership_end_date`)
	* Optional: Enter the date when the membership will end. Leave this field blank if you are not terminating or changing your membership.
7. **Add any relevant notes** (`membership_status_notes`)
	* In this field, add any additional comments or notes about the membership change request.
8. **Explain the reason for the membership change** (`membership_change_reason`)
	* Provide a brief explanation for the membership change request.
9. **Select the effective time of the membership change** (`membership_effective_time`)
	* Optional: Enter the time when the membership change should take effect.
10. **Enter any category or subgroup** (`membership_category`)
	* Optional: Enter the category or subgroup associated with the membership change request.
11. **Select the new types of membership** (`membership_type`)
	* Choose all the new types of membership you would like to have after the membership change from the list:
		+ Option 1
		+ Option 2
		+ Option 3

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Double-check the membership details carefully to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact the membership administrator for assistance.
