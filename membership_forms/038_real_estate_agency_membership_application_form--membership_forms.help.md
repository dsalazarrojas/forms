# real_estate_agency_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real estate agency membership application form is used to collect information from real estate agencies for membership purposes. This form ensures that the agency meets the necessary requirements to become a member of our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section with your agency's details.
2. Provide your agency's information, including its license number and type.
3. List the properties associated with your agency.
4. Enter any additional details about the properties.
5. Enter the address information of your agency.
6. Identify the contact person and their contact person phone number.
7. Select the membership type and agency type.
8. Enter the agency membership status and agency license number (if applicable).
9. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: This section is for entering your agency's contact information, including name, address, and phone number. Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Agency Info**: This section is for entering your agency's information, including its license number and type. Make sure to enter the correct license number and type.
* **Properties**: This section allows you to list the properties associated with your agency. Please enter the number of properties your agency manages or represents.
* **Property Details**: This section is for providing any additional details about the properties listed above.
* **Address Info**: This section is for entering your agency's address information, including street address and city.
* **Contact Person**: This section is for identifying the contact person for your agency.
* **Contact Person Phone**: This section is for entering the contact person's phone number.
* **Agency Details**: This section is for providing any additional information about your agency.
* **Member Type**: This section is for selecting the type of member you are applying to be (Yes/No).
* **Agency Type**: This section is for selecting the type of agency you are applying with (Yes/No).
* **Agency License Number**: This section is for entering your agency's license number (if applicable).
* **Agency License Country**: This section is for entering the country where your agency is licensed to operate (if applicable).
* **Agency Membership Number**: This section is for entering your agency's membership number (if applicable).
* **Contact Person Title**: This section is for entering the title of the contact person.
* **Email**: This section is for entering your agency's email address.
* **Notes**: This section is for providing any additional comments or notes about your agency.
* **Real Estate License Number**: This section is for entering your agency's real estate license number (if applicable).
* **Agency Membership Status**: This section is for selecting the agency membership status (Active/Inactive).
* **Membership Type**: This section is for selecting the membership type (Yes/No).

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the form accurately.
* Double-check your contact person's information for accuracy.
* Ensure that the properties listed are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
