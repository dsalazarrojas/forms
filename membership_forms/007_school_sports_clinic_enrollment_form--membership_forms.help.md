# School Sports Clinic Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to enroll students into a school sports clinic program, which provides sports-related activities and coaching to students during school hours.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter Your Information**: Please fill out this section with your personal details.
	* `Student Name`: Enter the name of the student enrolled.
	* `Guardian Name`: Enter your name (if you're the parent or guardian of the student).
	* `Guardian Phone`: Enter your phone number.
	* `Email`: Enter your email address (used for communication and payment processing).
2. **Select Sports and Team**: Choose the sport and team type for the student.
	* `Sports`: Choose from a list of available sports offered by the school, such as Football, Basketball, etc.
	* `Team`: Select the team type (Boys, Girls, Co-Ed).
3. **Payment Details**: Provide payment information for the student's enrollment.
	* `Payment Method`: Choose from available payment methods (Online Payment, Cash, Check).
	* `Payment Schedule`: Enter the date of the payment.
	* `Payment Amount`: Enter the amount of the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student enrolled.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your name (if you're the parent or guardian of the student).
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address (used for communication and payment processing).
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, required): Enter the student's school ID.
* **Sports** (`sport`, select_one, required): Choose from a list of available sports offered by the school.
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, required): Select the team type for the student (Boys, Girls, Co-Ed).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose from available payment methods (Online Payment, Cash, Check).
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, date, required): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Submit**: This is the submit button to finalize the enrollment form.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Make sure to double-check your personal and payment details before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the school staff for assistance.
