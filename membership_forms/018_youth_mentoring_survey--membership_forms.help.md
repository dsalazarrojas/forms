# Youth Mentoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from participants in a youth mentoring program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the contact information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Answer the participant details questions to provide context and background about yourself.
3. For each question in the program specifics section, answer in as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: `false`): This section collects your name and any other contact information you feel is relevant to provide context for your feedback.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: `false`): This section asks for more background information about you and your involvement with the program.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide any details about the program that you think would be helpful in understanding your feedback.
* **Participant Engagement** (`participant_engagement`, `number`, required: `false`): Answer this question with the number of times you have participated in the program.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose how satisfied you are with the program, with options ranging from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Participant Outcomes** (`participant_outcomes`, `text`, required: `false`): Please describe any positive or negative outcomes you have experienced as a result of the program.
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the program's goals, as understood from your perspective.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `checkbox`, required: `false`): Check any of the provided options that apply to your experience with the program.
* **Program Evaluation** (`program_evaluation`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide any general comments or evaluations of the program.
* **Program Progress** (`program_progress`, `number`, required: `false`): Answer this question with the number of progress you feel you've made through the program.
* **Participant Retention** (`participant_retention`, `number`, required: `false`): Answer this question with the number of times you've continued participating in the program.
* **Program Fidelity** (`program_fidelity`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe how well the program has met its goals and objectives.
* **Program Improvement** (`program_improvement`, `text`, required: `false`): Suggest any ways the program could be improved.
* **Participant Satisfaction 2** (`participant_satisfaction_2`, `number`, required: `false`): Choose your level of satisfaction with the program again.
* **Program Outcomes 2** (`program_outcomes_2`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check all that apply of the provided outcomes that you have experienced or not.
* **Program Engagement 2** (`program_engagement_2`, `number`, required: `false`): Answer this question with the number of times you have participated in the program.
* **Participant Recommended** (`participant_recommended`, `radio`, required: `false`): Check whether you would recommend the program to others.
* **Participant Suggest** (`participant_suggest`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any suggestions or comments for the program.
* **Participant Outcomes 2** (`participant_outcomes_2`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check all that apply of the provided outcomes that you have experienced or not.
* **Program Satisfaction 2** (`program_satisfaction_2`, `number`, required: `false`): Choose your level of satisfaction with the program again.

## Tips
Please fill out the form in its entirety, and be as detailed as possible in your responses. If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to ask.
