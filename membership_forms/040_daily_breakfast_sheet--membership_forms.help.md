# Daily Breakfast Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Breakfast Sheet form is designed to collect and organize information about guests for breakfast arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page for the guest you are arranging breakfast for.
2. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest" field.
3. Enter the number of guests in the "Guest count" field.
4. Choose the time of breakfast from the "Breakfast time" dropdown list.
5. Select the items you would like to serve from the "Menu" field.
6. If necessary, add any additional information about the guest in the "Additional information" field.
7. Enter the guest's email in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. (Optional) Enter the room number of the guest in the "Room number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest** (Guest, text, optional): Enter the guest's name.
* **Guest count** (Guest count, number, optional): Enter the number of guests in the party.
* **Breakfast time** (Breakfast time, time, optional): Choose the time of breakfast from the list.
* **Menu** (Menu, select_multiple, optional): Select the items you would like to serve (Bread, Eggs, Meat, Juice, Fruit).
* **Additional information** (Additional information, note, optional): Add any additional details about the guest's preferences or requirements.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Room number** (Room number, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the room number of the guest. 

## Tips
* Double-check that you have entered all required information for each guest.
* Make sure to update the form for each day or group of guests, as needed. 
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your supervisor or hospitality staff.
