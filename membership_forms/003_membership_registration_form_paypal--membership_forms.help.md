# Membership Registration Form PayPal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect membership registration information from users who want to join a membership program associated with a PayPal account.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the form completely, ensuring all required fields are provided.
2.  For the "Options" fields (fields with "select_multiple" or "select_one" type), choose one or more relevant options from the given list.
3.  In the "Payment" field, enter a numerical value to represent the user's payment method.
4.  Ensure that you have the required payment method (PayPal) selected in the "Pay With PayPal" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Membership Name** (`Membership Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name associated with this membership.
*   **Address** (`Address`, text, required: false): Enter your address, which will be used to contact you.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, which will be used for communications related to this membership.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, which will be used for additional contact methods.
*   **Occupation** (`Occupation`, text, required: false): Enter your occupation, which will be used to categorize your membership.
*   **Special Skills** (`Special Skills`, text, required: false): List any special skills you have, which will be used to further categorize your membership.
*   **Options** (`Options`, select multiple, required: false): Choose one or more of the given options to describe your membership type. 
    - Options are:
        - Professional Associate
        - Student Associate
*   **Membership Options** (`Membership Options`, select one, required: false): Choose the type of membership you are applying for.
    - Options are:
        - Professional Associate
        - Student Associate
*   **Pay With PayPal** (`Pay With PayPal`, select one, required: false): Ensure that you have selected this option to use PayPal as your payment method.
*   **Payment** (`Payment`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value for this field. It is assumed that this value is related to the payment method chosen.
*   **Name** (`Name`, note, required: false): This field appears to be a duplicate of the "Membership Name" field and is likely a placeholder.
*   **Payment** (`Payment`, note, required: false): This field appears to be a duplicate of the "Payment" field and is likely a placeholder.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have filled out all fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the support team for clarification.
- This form does not validate or check for errors, so please review your submission before submission.
