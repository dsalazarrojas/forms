# Anytime Fitness 7 Day Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anytime Fitness 7 Day Exit Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from members about their experience with our fitness facilities. Your input will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve on to better serve our members in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your name and membership ID, if applicable.
2.  Choose your answer from the options provided for each question.
3.  Select the primary reason for cancellation.
4.  Write down any suggestions or comments in the text fields.
5.  Choose your overall rating of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): This is where you enter your name, so please make sure it's accurate and spelled correctly.
* **Membership ID** (`member_id`, `text`, optional): If you have an ID, please enter it here. This will help us track your membership.
* **Membership exit or cancellation date** (`exit_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you cancelled your membership.
* **Membership duration** (`membership_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long you were a member of Anytime Fitness:
	+ Less than 1 month
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2+ years
* **How often did you use the facility?** (`facility_usage`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you visited the facility:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times per week
	+ Weekly
	+ A few times per month
	+ Rarely
	+ Never used
* **What was your primary fitness goal?** (`primary_fitness_goal`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary fitness goal:
	+ Weight loss
	+ Muscle building
	+ Cardiovascular fitness
	+ General fitness and health
	+ Rehabilitation or recovery
	+ Social connection
	+ Other
* **Did you achieve your fitness goal?** (`goal_achievement`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you achieved your goal:
	+ Yes, completely
	+ Partially achieved
	+ Not achieved
	+ Too soon to say
* **Quality of facilities and equipment** (`facilities_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of our facilities and equipment:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good

	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Facility cleanliness** (`cleanliness_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness of our facilities:
	+ Excellent

* **Condition and maintenance of equipment** (`equipment_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition and maintenance of our equipment:
	+ Excellent

	+ Very poor
* **Friendliness of staff** (`staff_friendliness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the friendliness of our staff:
	+ Very friendly
	+ Friendly

	+ Unfriendly
	+ Very unfriendly
* **Staff knowledge and helpfulness** (`staff_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Rate the knowledge and helpfulness of our staff:
	+ Very knowledgeable and helpful
	+ Knowledgeable and helpful
	+ Adequate
	+ Not very helpful
	+ Unhelpful
* **Quality of group fitness classes** (`class_offerings`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the quality of our group fitness classes:
	+ Excellent variety and quality

* **Elaborate on your reason for cancellation** (`cancellation_details`, `text`, optional): If you have more to add, please write here.
* **What could we improve?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions, please write them here.
* **Would you consider rejoining in the future?** (`willing_return`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you'd like to rejoin:
