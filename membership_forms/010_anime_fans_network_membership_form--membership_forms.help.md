# Anime Fans Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anime Fans Network Membership Form is designed to collect basic information from users who want to join the Anime Fans Network. This information will be used to manage network communications and interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the Membership Information section.
2. Provide your full name, which will be used to identify you in the membership records.
3. Enter your email address, which will be used for membership communications.
4. Select your favorite anime title, which is optional but helps the network understand your interests.
5. Choose your preferred membership tier, which determines the level of access to network resources.
6. Select your interests within the network, which helps us tailor our communications and activities to your preferences.
7. Agree to the community guidelines, which is a requirement for joining the network.
8. Choose your preferred contact method, which is optional but helps us reach you more efficiently.
9. If you'd like, enter your planned date of joining, which helps us track membership history.
10. Finally, provide any additional notes you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address for membership communications.
* **Favorite Anime Title** (`favorite_anime`, text, optional): Optional but helpful for tailoring network activities.
* **Membership Tier** (`membership_tier`, select_one, required): Choose from Free, Supporter, or Premium to determine your level of access to network resources.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from Events, Watch parties, Fan art, Cosplay, or Discussion forums.
* **Agree to Community Guidelines** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the community guidelines to join the network.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, optional): Choose from Email, Phone, or Messaging app to determine how we should reach you.
* **Date Joining** (`join_date`, date, optional): Enter today or planned date of joining for membership history.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Share any additional information you'd like to help us know you better.
* **Agree to Community Guidelines** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the community guidelines to join the network.
