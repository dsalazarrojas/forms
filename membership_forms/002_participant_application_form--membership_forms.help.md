# Participant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Participant Application Form" is a tool designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in participating in various events, programs, or activities. This form is a helpful resource for organizers and administrators to gather essential details about participants, including their event-related preferences and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the participant application form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Participant Application Form" link to access the form.
2. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields (marked as "Required" or having an asterisk \*).
3. If a field is optional, feel free to fill it in if you have relevant information, but it is not mandatory.
4. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required): This field is where you can enter your name, contact information, or any other relevant details about yourself as a participant. Please make sure to fill this field accurately and comprehensively.

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): In this field, you should provide information about the event(s) you are interested in participating in or attending. Include the event title, date, location, or any other relevant details you think would be helpful for the organizers.

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments, questions, or thoughts that you'd like to share with the organizers, please use this field to include them. This is an optional field, so feel free to skip it if you don't have any notes to add.
