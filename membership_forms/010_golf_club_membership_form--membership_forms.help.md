# Golf Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us collect your information so we can process your membership application. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your membership details are up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the respective fields. These are your personal details that we'll use for communication.
*   Select your preferred **Membership Type** (Club Member, Social Member, Junior Member, or Corporate Member) from the dropdown menu. This will determine your membership category.
*   Fill in your **Address**, **City**, **State**, and **Country** where you reside.
*   Provide your **Zip Code** for accurate location identification.
*   If you have an **Emergency Contact**, please fill out their **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Phone**, and **Relationship**.
*   Mention **How You Heard** about our golf club in the respective field.
*   If you have some **Additional Comments** about your membership application, you can add them here.
*   For existing members, please fill out your **Member Since**, **Member Status** (Active, Pending, Inactive, or Cancelled), **Payment Status** (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid, or Partially Unpaid), **Payment Method** (Credit Card, Bank Draft, Cheque, or PayPal), and **Payment Date** and **Payment Amount** if applicable.
*   Finally, provide any **Notes** about your membership application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Last Name**: Your surname.
*   **Email**: The email address we can use for communication.
*   **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
*   **Membership Type**: The type of membership you wish to apply for.
*   **Address**: The address where you reside.
*   **City**: The city where your address is located.
*   **State**: The state where your address is located.
*   **Country**: The country where your address is located.
*   **Zip Code**: The postal code for your address.
*   **Date Of Birth**: Your date of birth for age verification (only for junior members).
*   **Emergency Contact First Name**: The first name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Last Name**: The last name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Your relationship with your emergency contact.
*   **How Heard**: How you heard about our golf club.
*   **Additional Comments**: Any additional information about your application.
*   **Member Since**: The date when you became a member.
*   **Member Status**: Your current member status.
*   **Payment Status**: Your current payment status.
*   **Payment Method**: The method you used for payment.
*   **Payment Date**: The date of payment.
*   **Payment Amount**: The total amount paid.
*   **Notes**: Any notes about your membership application.
*   **Total Payment**: The total payment made.
*   **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about your membership application.
