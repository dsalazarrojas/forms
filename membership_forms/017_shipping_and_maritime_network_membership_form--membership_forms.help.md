# Shipping And Maritime Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping and Maritime Network Membership Form is designed for individuals or entities interested in joining the Shipping and Maritime Network. This form will help us gather necessary information about you or your entity to facilitate a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with accurate and truthful information.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Double-check your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name, as you would like to be referred to in the network.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your last name, as you would like to be known in the network.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for network updates and communications.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): If you have a contact number, please enter it for efficient communication within the network.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Please enter your date of birth in the standard 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
* **Shipping License Number** (`shipping_license_number`, text, required: false): If you have a shipping license number, please enter it for network verification purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if you consider them optional.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
