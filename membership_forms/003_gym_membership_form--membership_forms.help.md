# Gym Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gym Membership Form is used to collect information from gym members about their membership status. This form will help us understand their membership type, emergency contact, membership start and end dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your membership information on the first page.
2. Enter your member details on the second page.
3. Fill out the emergency contact information on the third page.
4. Select your membership type on the fourth page.
5. Confirm your understanding of the membership terms on the fifth page.
6. Choose your payment terms on the sixth page.
7. Enter your membership start and end dates on the seventh and eighth pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Information** (`membership_info`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your membership, such as the type of membership you are interested in (e.g., individual, family, etc.) or any special circumstances that may affect your membership.

* **Member Info** (`member_info`, text, required: false): Provide your name and contact details to help us get in touch with you about your membership.

* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false): If we need to contact someone on your behalf, please provide their name and contact information.

* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you are interested in a 'Yes' or 'No' membership type. 

* **Membership Terms** (`membership_terms`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you have read and understand the membership terms by selecting 'Yes'.

* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you agree to pay membership fees according to the agreed-upon payment schedule.

* **Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you would like your membership to start.

* **Membership End Date** (`membership_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you would like your membership to end.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
- We appreciate your honest and clear responses in this form.
