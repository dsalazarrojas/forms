# Church Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Membership Form is a necessary step for registration as a church member. This guide will walk you through the form's sections and fields to ensure a smooth and accurate completion process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name, middle name, and last name in the "Full Legal Name" field.
2. Enter your preferred name in the "Preferred Name" field, if you have one.
3. Type in your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be your primary contact email for church communications.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your home address in the "Home Address" field, including street, city, state, and zip code.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field, using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
7. Select your marital status from the options provided in the "Marital Status" field.
8. If applicable, enter your spouse's name in the "Spouse Name" field.
9. Indicate whether you have children in the "Do You Have Children" field.
10. If you have children, provide their names and ages in the "Children Names and Ages" field.
11. Indicate your baptism status from the options provided in the "Have You Been Baptized" field.
12. If not baptized at this church, please specify the church of baptism and location.
13. Select the date you would like to start your membership on in the "Desired Membership Start Date" field.
14. Choose how you heard about our church from the provided options in the "How Did You Hear About Our Church" field.
15. Share a brief testimony about your faith journey in the "Tell Us About Your Faith Journey" field.
16. Indicate whether you have completed the membership class from the options provided in the "Have You Completed Membership Class" field.
17. Select your spiritual gifts from the options provided in the "What Are Your Spiritual Gifts" field.
18. Choose areas where you would like to serve from the options in the "Volunteer Interests" field.
19. Indicate whether you would like to join a small group from the options provided in the "Would You Like to Join a Small Group" field.
20. Indicate your tithing practice from the options provided in the "Do You Practice Tithing" field.
21. Select whether you agree with the church's statement of faith from the options provided in the "Do You Agree with Our Statement of Faith" field.
22. Select whether you agree with the church's covenant from the options provided in the "Do You Agree to Our Church Covenant" field.
23. Sign the form with your full name in the "Your Signature" field.
24. Date the form with the date of completion in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Preferred Name**: Enter a name you would like to be known by within the church community.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email for church communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number for church staff and members.
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status.
* **Spouse Name**: If applicable, enter your spouse's name.
* **Do You Have Children**: Indicate whether you have children.
* **Children Names and Ages**: If you have children, list their names and ages.
* **Have You Been Baptized**: Select your baptism status.
* **Previous Church Affiliation**: If not baptized at this church, enter the name and location of your previous church.
* **Desired Membership Start Date**: Select the date you would like to start your membership.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Church**: Choose how you heard about our church.
* **Tell Us About Your Faith Journey**: Share a brief testimony about your faith journey.
* **Have You Completed Membership Class**: Indicate whether you have completed the membership class.
* **What Are Your Spiritual Gifts**: Select all areas where you would like to serve.
* **Volunteer Interests**: Choose areas where you would like to serve.
* **Would You Like to Join a Small Group**: Indicate whether you would like to join a small group.
* **Do You Practice Tithing**: Indicate your tithing practice.
* **Do You Agree with Our Statement of Faith**: Select whether you agree with our statement of faith.
* **Do You Agree to Our Church Covenant**: Select whether you agree with our church covenant.
* **Your Signature**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Date**: Date the form with the date of completion.
