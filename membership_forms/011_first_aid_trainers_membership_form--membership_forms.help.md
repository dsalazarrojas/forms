# First Aid Trainers Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about first aid trainers' background and interest in first aid training. It's meant to help assess their qualifications and suitability for membership or certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Answer the questions based on your interest and background in first aid training.
2.  Fill out your personal contact information and emergency contact details.
3.  Provide your history with first aid training, including completed sessions and certifications.
4.  Answer additional questions about your interest in first aid training and your training frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What type of first aid training are you interested in** (`first_aid_training_interest`, select_multiple): Choose all types of first aid training you are interested in from the provided options. For example, "Yes" for first aid, CPR, or AED training.
  - **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your full name as you would like to be known on the form.
  - **Organization** (`organization`, select_one): If you are representing an organization, choose "Yes" to indicate your affiliation. Otherwise, choose "No".
  - **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
  - **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
  - **Address** (`address`, text): Enter your address to be used for communication or membership purposes.
  - **Training History** (`training_history`, select_multiple): Check all types of first aid training you have completed. For example, first aid, CPR, or AED training.
  - **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
  - **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, or guardian).
  - **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, text): Add any additional notes about the emergency contact.
  - **Member Interest** (`member_interest`, select_multiple): Indicate your interest in first aid training for membership or certification. This may include being a trainer, instructor, or participant.
  - **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Enter any additional information or comments about your background and qualifications.
  - **First Aid Training Completed** (`first_aids_trained`, select_multiple): Check all types of first aid training you have completed, such as first aid, CPR, or AED training.
  - **Date Completed** (`training_date_completed`, date): Enter the date you completed the first aid training, if applicable.
  - **Hours Completed** (`training_hours_completed`, number): Enter the number of hours of first aid training completed, if applicable.
  - **First Aids Trained** (`first_aids_trained`, number): Enter the number of first aid trainings you have completed, if applicable.
  - **Last Aid Training Session** (`last_aid_training_session`, select_one): Confirm that you have attended a first aid training session.
  - **Last Session Date** (`last_session_date`, date): Enter the date of your last first aid training session, if applicable.
  - **Last Session Hours** (`last_session_hours`, number): Enter the number of hours of your last first aid training session, if applicable.
  - **Last Aids Trained** (`last_aids_trained`, number): Enter the number of first aids trained in your last session, if applicable.
  - **Last Training Session** (`last_training_session`, select_one): Confirm that you have attended a first aid training session in the past.
  - **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_multiple): Indicate how frequently you have attended first aid training sessions in the past.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- Review each field's label and instructions to ensure you understand what's required.
- Don't hesitate to contact the organization or support team if you need help or clarification.
- Be honest and accurate when providing your background and qualifications.
- Keep your answers concise and clear, using the provided options when possible.
