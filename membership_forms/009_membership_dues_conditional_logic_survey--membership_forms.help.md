# Membership Dues Conditional Logic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about membership dues from users, allowing organizations or clubs to determine the frequency and type of dues a user is eligible for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by entering your membership information in the "Membership Dues" section.
2.  Select your premium type from the options provided.
3.  Choose the frequency of your premium from the options provided.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Select the frequency of your dues from the options provided.
7.  Select the type of dues you are eligible for.
8.  Finally, provide your date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Membership Dues** (`dues_premium`, text, optional): This is the main section where you can enter your membership information.
*   **feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you may have.
*   **Premium Type** (`select_premium_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of premium you are eligible for.
*   **Premium Freq** (`select_premium_frequencies`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of your premium.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Dues Freq** (`select_dues_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of your dues.
*   **Dues Type** (`select_dues_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of dues you are eligible for.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are eligible for your dues.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are eligible for your dues.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your information is accurately recorded.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can leave them blank or contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
*   Remember to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
