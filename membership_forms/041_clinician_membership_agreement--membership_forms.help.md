# Clinician Membership Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clinicians who want to apply for membership with our healthcare organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Clinician Membership Agreement**: This is the title of the form, which is simply a heading to identify the purpose of the form.
2. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
4. **Professional Credentials** (`credentials`, text, required): Enter your professional credentials, such as MD, DO, NP, etc.
5. **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
6. **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
7. **Professional License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your professional license number.
8. **License State** (`license_state`, text, required): Enter the state where you hold your professional license.
9. **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiry`, date, required): Enter the date when your license expires.
10. **Medical Specialty** (`specialty`, select_one, required): Select the medical specialty you have.
	* Family Medicine
	* Internal Medicine
	* Pediatrics
	* Emergency Medicine
	* Surgery
	* Cardiology
	* Oncology
	* Neurology
	* Psychiatry
	* Other
11. **Board Certification Status** (`board_certification`, select_one, required): Select the status of your board certification.
	* Board Certified
	* Board Eligible
	* Not Applicable
12. **Board Certification Details** (`board_certification_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your board certification, if applicable.
13. **DEA Number** (`dea_number`, text, optional): Enter your DEA number, if applicable.
14. **NPI Number** (`npn_number`, text, required): Enter your NPI number.
15. **Malpractice Insurance Carrier** (`malpractice_carrier`, text, required): Enter the name of your malpractice insurance carrier.
16. **Malpractice Policy Number** (`malpractice_policy_number`, text, required): Enter the number of your malpractice policy.
17. **Coverage Amount** (`malpractice_coverage_amount`, select_one, required): Select the coverage amount of your malpractice policy.
	* $100000/$300000
	* $200000/$600000
	* $300000/$900000
	* $1000000/$3000000
	* Other
18. **Malpractice Insurance Expiration** (`malpractice_expiry`, date, required): Enter the date when your malpractice insurance expires.
19. **Current Hospital Privileges** (`hospital_privileges`, text, optional): List the hospitals where you have privileges.
20. **Years in Practice** (`years_in_practice`, select_one, required): Select the number of years you have been in practice.
	* Less than 1 year
	* 1-3 years
	* 3-5 years
	* 5-10 years
	* 10-20 years
	* 20+ years
21. **Current Practice Setting** (`practice_setting`, select_one, required): Select the setting where you currently practice.
	* Private Practice
	* Hospital Employed
	* Academic Medical Center
	* Group Practice
	* Government
	* Other
22. **Availability** (`availability`, text, required): Enter the days and hours you are available.
23. **Membership Type Requested** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of membership you are requesting.
	* Full Active
	* Associate
	* Affiliate
	* Locum Tenens
	* Telemedicine
24. **Professional References** (`references`, text, required): Enter the names and contact information of your professional references.
25. **Any Disciplinary History** (`disciplinary_history`, select_one, required): Select whether you have a disciplinary history.
	* True
	* False
26. **Disciplinary History Details** (`disciplinary_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your disciplinary history, if applicable.
27. **Any Criminal History** (`criminal_history`, select_one, required): Select whether you have a criminal history.
	* True
	* False
28. **Criminal History Details** (`criminal_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your criminal history, if applicable.
29. **Are You Able to Perform Essential Functions** (`health_status`, select_one, required): Select whether you are able to perform essential functions.
	* True
	* False
30. **I Accept Membership Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Select whether you accept the membership terms and conditions.
	* Yes I Accept
	* No I Do Not Accept
31. **I Have Read and Agree to Abide by Bylaws** (`bylaws_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Select whether you have read and agree to abide by the bylaws.
	* Yes I Acknowledge
	* No I Do Not Acknowledge
32. **Applicant Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Enter your full name.
33. **Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and documents required for membership before completing this form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare organization for assistance.
