# Hybrid Team Membership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hybrid Team Membership Application Form is designed for individuals to apply for membership in a hybrid team or organization. This form collects personal details, contact information, and other relevant details about the applicant. The form is intended to ensure that applicants provide accurate and comprehensive information to facilitate the review and consideration of their membership application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First name** and **Last name** in their respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email address**, **Phone number**, and **Date of birth**.
3. Select your **Experience level** (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) to help the team understand your current level of expertise.
4. Describe your **Skills** that are relevant to the hybrid team or organization.
5. Provide a brief **Bio** about yourself, including any relevant work experience or personal details.
6. If you have an **Emergency contact**, **Emergency phone number**, **Emergency email**, **Referee**, or **Referee phone number**, please fill in their respective fields with accurate information.
7. Describe your **Referee experience** and mention your **Referee relation**, providing context for who the referee is and why you're working with them.
8. If you have a **Team member**, **Role**, or **Team lead**, select your level of involvement (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
9. Add a note about your **Role** and **Team lead role** to provide further context.
10. Enter the **First Name 2** and **Last Name 2** of a second person, if applicable.
11. Finally, click the **Submit** button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Experience**: Select your current level of experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) to help the team understand your skills and expertise.
* **Skills**: Describe your relevant skills or areas of expertise.
* **Bio**: Provide a brief description of yourself, including any relevant work experience or personal details.
* **Availability**: Indicate if you are available to work on projects or collaborations.
* **Emergency contact**: Enter the name, **Emergency phone number**, and **Emergency email** of a contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Referee**: Enter the name of the referee who can vouch for your experience and skills.
* **Referee phone number**: Enter the phone number of the referee.
* **Referee email**: Enter the email address of the referee.
* **Referee experience**: Describe your experience working with the referee.
* **Referee relation**: Describe your relation with the referee.
* **Team member**: Select your level of involvement with the team (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Role**: Select your role within the team (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Team lead**: Select if you are leading the team.
* **Team lead role**: Provide a note about your role as team lead.
* **First Name 2** and **Last Name 2**: If you are applying on behalf of another person, enter their first and last name.
* **Email 2**: Enter the email address of the person you are applying on behalf of.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your application.
