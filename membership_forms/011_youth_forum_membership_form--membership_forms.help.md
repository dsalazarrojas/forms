# Youth Forum Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or modify membership profiles for youth users. It collects basic contact information and other details necessary for membership management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the first-name field with the youth's first name as it is commonly known.
2. Enter the email address of the youth or their parent/guardian.
3. Provide the date of birth of the youth.
4. Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
5. Enter the address of the youth or their parent/guardian.
6. Provide the contact number of the youth or their parent/guardian.
7. Enter any relevant emergency contact information.
8. Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First-name**: Enter the youth's first name as it is commonly known.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the youth or their parent/guardian.
* **Date-of-birth**: Enter the date of birth of the youth.
* **Parent-or-guardian-name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the youth or their parent/guardian.
* **Contact-number**: Enter the contact number of the youth or their parent/guardian.
* **Emergency contact info**: Enter any relevant emergency contact information.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
