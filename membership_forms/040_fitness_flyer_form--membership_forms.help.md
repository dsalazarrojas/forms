# Fitness Flyer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is meant to capture basic information from customers who want to enroll in a fitness program at your facility. It includes enrollment date, program selection, name, email, phone number, age, fitness goals, membership type, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Enrollment Date** field with the date you want to start the fitness program.
2. Select your **Program Selection** from the provided options (Enroll in a fitness program, Get a workout plan, Get a gym membership, or Get a free trial class).
3. Enter your **Name** as it appears on your government-issued ID.
4. Enter your **Email** address that we can use to communicate with you.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** so we can reach out to you if necessary.
6. Select your **Age** group (not required but helpful for tailoring the program to your needs).
7. Choose your **Fitness Goals** from the provided options (Enroll in a fitness program, Get a workout plan, Get a gym membership, or Get a free trial class).
8. Select your desired **Membership Type** (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
9. Add any additional **Notes** about your preferences or requests (not required but useful for us to tailor your experience).
9. Select the **Program Start Date** and **Program End Date** for your fitness program (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Date** (ID 1, Date): Enter the date you want to start your fitness program.
* **Program Selection** (ID 2, Select Multiple): Choose the type of program you want to enroll in (Enroll in a fitness program, Get a workout plan, Get a gym membership, or Get a free trial class).
* **Name** (ID 3, Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (ID 4, Email): Enter your email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (ID 5, Text): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if necessary.
* **Age** (ID 6, Number): Select your age group (not required but helpful for tailoring the program).
* **Fitness Goals** (ID 7, Select One): Choose your fitness goals from the provided options (Enroll in a fitness program, Get a workout plan, Get a gym membership, or Get a free trial class).
* **Membership Type** (ID 8, Select One): Select your desired membership type (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Notes** (ID 9, Text): Add any additional notes or requests about your preferences (not required).
* **Program Start Date** (ID 10, Date): Select the date your fitness program will start.
* **Program End Date** (ID 11, Date): Select the date your fitness program will end.
