# Athletic Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you fill out the Athletic Club Membership Form accurately and efficiently. Please review each page and field carefully to ensure you provide the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the member profile page**.
2. Navigate through each page of the form, and fill out the required fields for that page.
3. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
- **Residential Address** (`residential_address`, text, required): Enter your current address where you can be reached during business hours.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Occupation** (`occupation_text`, text, optional): If you have a profession, enter it here. This is not required but may help us tailor services to your needs.
- **Membership Tier** (`membership_tier`, select_one, required): Select the membership plan that best fits your needs.
- **Primary Sport Interest** (`primary_sport_interest`, text, required): Enter the sport or activity you are most interested in.
- **Primary Goals for Joining** (`joining_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the provided options.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Do you have any chronic medical conditions?** (`chronic_conditions_notes`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, provide a brief explanation.
- **Do you have any exercise restrictions or limitations?** (`exercise_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any exercise limitations, provide a brief explanation.
- **Preferred Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`, select_one, required): Select your preferred billing frequency.
- **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_choice`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
- **I agree to abide by all Club Rules and Bylaws** (`rules_agreement`, select_one, required): Select `I agree` to accept the terms.
- **I have read and agree to the Club Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy_agreement`, select_one, required): Select `I agree` to accept the terms.
- **Member Electronic Signature** (`member_signature`, text, required): Type your full name for the electronic signature.
- **Date of Enrollment** (`signup_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are enrolling.
