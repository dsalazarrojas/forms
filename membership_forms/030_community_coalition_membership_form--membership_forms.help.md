# Coalition Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coalition Membership Form is designed for new membership applications from organizations or individuals interested in joining the coalition. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information for the membership process, including contact details, membership type, and areas of interest. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **organization name** or **individual name** in the **Organization name** field. This field is required and should reflect the group or individual joining the coalition.
2.  Provide the **Primary contact name** of the representative. This field is required and should be the name of the person who will be the main point of contact for the coalition.
3.  Enter the **Contact email** for the primary contact. This field is required and should be a valid email address for coalition communications.
4.  If you have a **Contact phone number**, enter it in the corresponding field. This is an optional field and should be a valid phone number.
5.  Select the **Membership type** that best describes your organization or individual status in the coalition. You can choose from **Organizational member**, **Individual member**, or **Supporting partner**.
6.  If applicable, select the **Areas of interest** relevant to your membership. You can choose from **Housing**, **Transportation**, **Public health**, or **Economic development**.
7.  If you have any **Contribution details**, enter them in the corresponding field. This field is optional and should describe any financial or in-kind support you can provide.
8.  Confirm your acceptance of the **Agreement to coalition bylaws** by selecting **I agree**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name** (text, required): Enter the name of the group or individual joining the coalition.
* **Primary contact name** (text, required): Enter the name of the representative who will be the main point of contact for the coalition.
* **Contact email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for coalition communications.
* **Contact phone number** (text, optional): Enter a valid phone number, if applicable.
* **Membership type** (select_one, required): Select the classification that best describes your organization or individual status in the coalition.
* **Areas of interest** (select_multiple, optional): Select the priority focus areas relevant to your membership.
* **Contribution details** (text, optional): Enter a description of any financial or in-kind support you can provide.
* **Agreement to coalition bylaws** (select_one, required): Confirm your acceptance of the membership terms by selecting **I agree**.

## Tips

* Please ensure the email address you provide is valid and checked regularly for coalition communications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the coalition representative for assistance.
* This form is for new membership applications only. If you are already a member, please contact the coalition representative for membership maintenance or updates.
