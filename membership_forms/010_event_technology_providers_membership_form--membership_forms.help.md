# Event Technology Providers Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Technology Providers Membership Form is a tool to help us gather information from individuals or organizations that provide event technology solutions. This information will be used to manage and communicate with these providers effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information about your organization or yourself.
- Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
- Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the form as it appears on the screen.
- **Technology Service Providers** (`technology_service_providers`, select multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you provide technology services to events, or 'No' if not.
- **Provider Technology** (`provider_technology`, select one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you provide technology to events, or 'No' if not.
- **Technology Services** (`technology_services`, select multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' for any services your organization or you provides.
- **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization or your name.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter a contact name and/or email address.
- **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your main address.
- **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional address information.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL.
- **Logo** (`logo`, text, optional): Enter your logo URL or a description.
- **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Enter any additional description.
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Enter any agreement or contract information.
- **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select multiple, optional): Check your membership type. Options include 'Yes', 'No', and any other types you may have. 
- **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, select multiple, optional): Check your membership level. Options include 'High', 'Low'.
- **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select multiple, optional): Check your membership status. Options include 'Active', 'Inactive'.
- **Membership Date** (`membership_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you joined our community.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your membership will end.
- **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, number, optional): Enter the length of your membership in days or months.
- **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your membership will be renewed.
- **Membership Status Date** (`membership_status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your membership status will be updated.
- **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, select multiple, optional): Check your renewal status. Options include 'Active', 'Inactive'.
- **Membership Type Other** (`membership_type_other`, text, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have any other membership types.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
