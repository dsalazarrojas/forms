# Student Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Sports Registration Form is used to register students for the school sports program. It helps the sports team to gather important information about the student, such as their name, grade level, and preferred sport.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name in the "Student name" field.
2.  Select the student's grade level from the "Student grade" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent phone" field.
4.  Select the sport(s) the student is interested in playing by checking the box next to the corresponding sport(s) in the "Student sport" field.
5.  Choose a date and time the student is available to play in the "Date available" and "Time available" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`text`, required): Enter the student's name exactly as it appears on their ID or in school records.
* **Student grade** (`select_one`, required): Select the student's grade level from the dropdown menu.
*   *  Grade 1: First grade
*   *  Grade 2: Second grade
*   *  Grade 3: Third grade
*   *  Grade 4: Fourth grade
*   *  Grade 5: Fifth grade
*   *  Grade 6: Sixth grade
*   *  Grade 7: Seventh grade
*   *  Grade 8: Eighth grade
* **Parent phone** (`text`, required): Enter the parent's phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" (e.g., "123-456-7890").
* **Student sport** (`select_multiple`, required): Check the box next to each sport the student is interested in playing.
	+ Baseball: Baseball team tryouts are held at the baseball field on Friday afternoons.
	+ Basketball: Basketball games are held at the school gym on Tuesday evenings.
	+ Soccer: Soccer games are held at the school field on Wednesday afternoons.
	+ Softball: Softball games are held at the school field on Thursday mornings.
* **Date available** (`date`, required): Enter the date the student is available to start playing sports in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., "2022-09-01").
* **Time available** (`time`, required): Enter the time the student is available to start playing sports in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., "14:30").

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the student's name exactly as it appears on their ID or in school records.
* If you are unsure of the student's grade level, check the school directory or contact the school administration office.
* If the student is interested in playing multiple sports, check the box next to each sport they are interested in playing.
