# wellness_coaching_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a user's well-being and fitness goals to create a personalized wellness coaching plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best fit your current situation and habits. Please provide honest and accurate answers, as this information will be used to create a tailored coaching plan for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current fitness level** (`general_info`, `select_one`, `High/Low`)
	+ Choose your current level of fitness, either High or Low.
* **What are your health and fitness goals** (`health_goals`, `select_multiple`, `Yes/No`)
	+ Check all the goals that apply to you.
* **Do you have any medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, no options)
	+ Provide a brief description of any medical conditions or history.
* **Do you have any fitness history** (`fitness_history`, `text`, no options)
	+ Describe your current or past fitness experiences.
* **What is your current workout routine** (`current_workout`, `text`, no options)
	+ Describe your current workout routine, including frequency and intensity.
* **How many hours do you have available per week** (`available_time`, `number`, no options)
	+ Enter the number of hours you have available for wellness activities.
* **What is your current diet** (`current_diet`, `text`, no options)
	+ Describe your current eating habits.
* **How often do you exercise per week** (`exercise_frequency`, `number`, no options)
	+ Enter the number of times you exercise per week.
* **How many hours do you sleep per night** (`sleep_schedule`, `number`, no options)
	+ Enter the number of hours you sleep per night.
* **How many cups of water do you drink per day** (`current_water_intake`, `number`, no options)
	+ Enter the number of cups of water you drink per day.
* **What is your stress level (0-10)** (`stress_level`, `number`, `0-10`)
	+ Enter a number from 0 to 10 that reflects your current stress level.
* **What is your current occupation** (`current_occupation`, `text`, no options)
	+ Describe your current occupation or profession.
* **Do you have any medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, no options)
	+ List any medical conditions you have.
* **Do you take any supplements** (`supplements`, `text`, no options)
	+ List any supplements you take.
* **Do you take any medications** (`medications`, `text`, no options)
	+ List any medications you take.
* **Do you have any family health history** (`family_history`, `text`, no options)
	+ Describe any family health history.
* **Do you see a medical professional regularly** (`medical_professional`, `text`, no options)
	+ Describe your relationship with a medical professional.
* **What type of insurance do you have** (`current_insurance`, `text`, no options)
	+ Describe your insurance coverage.
* **Is there an emergency contact person** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, `Yes/No`)
	+ Check if you have an emergency contact person.
* **How would you describe the relationship between you and your emergency contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, no options)
	+ Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **What is the phone number of your emergency contact** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, no options)
	+ Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **What is the email of your emergency contact** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, `email`, no options)
	+ Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **What is the address of your emergency contact** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, no options)
	+ Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **What is the name of your medical professional** (`medical_professional_name`, `text`, no options)
	+ Enter the name of your medical professional.
* **What is the phone number of your medical professional** (`medical_professional_phone`, `text`, no options)
	+ Enter the phone number of your medical professional.

**Tips**
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Please provide detailed and clear information to help us create a comprehensive coaching plan.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
