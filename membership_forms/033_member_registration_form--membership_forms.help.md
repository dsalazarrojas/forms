# member_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The member registration form is designed to collect personal details, address, contact information, and interest areas of a member. This form is used to create or update a member's profile, and it should only be completed by members or authorized staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, surname, and email address.
2. Provide your address details, including your street address, apartment number, post code, city, and country.
3. Enter your contact details, including your phone number.
4. Select your interest areas.
5. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`name` (text), `surname` (text), `title` (text)): Enter your full name, surname, and title.
* **Address Details** (`street_address` (text), `apartment_number` (text), `post_code` (text), `city` (text), `country` (text)): Enter your street address, apartment number, post code, city, and country.
* **Contact Details** (`email` (email), `phone` (text)): Enter your email address and phone number.
* **Interest Areas** (`interest_area_1` (select_one), `interest_area_2` (select_one)): Select one or more interest areas that apply to you.
* **Submit** (`submit` (text)): Once you have completed all the fields, click the submit button to save your changes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct purpose and that the information provided is accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the member or staff for clarification.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
