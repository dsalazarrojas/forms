# Church Membership Renewal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew your membership and recommit to the mission of the church. Please complete it to help us better understand your spiritual journey and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and select the correct options for each field.
2. Review your answers carefully to ensure they are accurate.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Full Name** (`Member Full Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your membership card.
* **Member ID (if known)** (`Member ID (if known)`, `number`, optional): If you have a member ID, please enter it here. This can be a helpful reference for our records.
* **Primary Email** (`Primary Email`, `email`, required): Please enter your primary email address. This will help us keep you informed about church news and events.
* **I wish to renew my membership and recommit to the mission of this church** (`I wish to renew my membership and recommit to the mission of this church`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Yes, I am recommitting
	+ No, I am moving to another congregation
	+ I have questions before renewing
* **How would you describe your spiritual journey this past year?** (`How would you describe your spiritual journey this past year?`, `text`, optional): Take a moment to reflect on your spiritual journey this past year. What has God been teaching you? How has your relationship with Him grown?
* **Current areas of service or ministry** (`Current areas of service or ministry`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all areas where you currently serve or are interested in serving:
	+ Ushering
	+ Teaching
	+ Choir
	+ Technical Team
	+ Cleaning
	+ Administration
* **Are there new areas where you would like to serve?** (`Are there new areas where you would like to serve?`, `text`, optional): If you're looking to expand your service, list any new areas you'd like to explore.
* **How can the church pray for you in this new season?** (`How can the church pray for you in this new season?`, `text`, optional): Let us know how we can specifically pray for you during this time of renewal.
* **Has your contact information changed?** (`Has your contact information changed?`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ No changes
	+ Yes, please update me
* **Expected frequency of attendance** (`Expected frequency of attendance`, `select_one`, optional): If you're able, please select how often you expect to attend church services:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Occasionally
* **Date of Renewal** (`Date of Renewal`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you would like to renew your membership on.

## Tips
- Please be honest in your answers, and take the time to reflect on your spiritual journey and your role within the church.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, don't hesitate to reach out to the church administration for guidance.
- Thank you for taking the time to renew your membership and recommit to the church's mission!
