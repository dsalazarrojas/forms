# Software Premium Membership Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Premium Membership Access Form is designed to help us understand your company's requirements and preferences for accessing our premium software services. This information will help us tailor our support and services to best meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the user info section with your company's details.
2. Select your preferred premium plan and upgrade intent.
3. Provide your company's contact information.
4. Indicate the type of team that will be using our services.
5. Answer any additional questions to help us better understand your company's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your company's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Premium Plan** (`premium_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred premium plan from the options provided.
* **Upgrade Intent** (`upgrade_intent`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you are upgrading for yourself or another team member.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your company's size from the provided options.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your company's type from the options provided.
* **SaaS Team** (`saas_team`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if your company has a SaaS team.
* **Customer Success Team** (`customer_success_team`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if your company has a customer success team.
* **IT Admin** (`it_admin`, `select_one`, required: false): Select if your company's IT admin is available for support.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of inquiry you have, whether for upgrading or feature requests.
* **Other Inquiry** (`other_inquiry`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other information you think is relevant to your inquiry.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your company's needs.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your submission.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's URL.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required: false): Enter your role in the company.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name.
* **Email Domain** (`email_domain`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's email domain.
* **Phone Domain** (`phone_domain`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's phone domain.

Please note that some fields are optional and should only be filled if relevant to your company's circumstances.
