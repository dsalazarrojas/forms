# Community Membership Application Form Golden State Roleplay - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who want to join the Golden State Roleplay community. Please note that this form is used to gather information about you, and it's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Welcome to Golden State Roleplay --** (1): This is a note that welcomes you to the community and provides a brief introduction.
* **Full Name** (2): Your real-life name is required for identification purposes.
* **Discord Username** (3): This is your username on the Discord platform, in the format of `Username#0000`.
* **Your Age** (4): You must be at least 16 years old to join the community.
* **Date of Birth** (5): This is your date of birth, which is required for age verification.
* **Steam HEX ID** (6): This is used for server whitelisting and is required for access to certain features.
* **Have you been in a roleplay community before?** (7): Answer `True` if you have experience in a roleplay community, `False` otherwise.
* **If yes, which communities have you been part of?** (8): Provide the names of the communities you have been part of, if applicable.
* **Which department are you interested in joining?** (9): Choose one of the available departments: Civilian Operations, San Andreas Highway Patrol, Los Santos Police Department, Blaine County Sheriffs Office, or San Andreas Fire and Rescue.
* **Why do you want to join Golden State Roleplay?** (10): Please provide a brief explanation of your motivation for joining the community.
* **Character Background Story** (11): Briefly describe your primary character's background.
* **Do you have a working, high-quality microphone?** (12): Answer `True` if you have a working microphone, `False` otherwise.
* **Have you read and understood all community rules?** (13): Answer `True` if you have read and understood the rules, `False` otherwise.
* **-- Terms and Conditions --** (14): This is a note that reminds you to follow all community guidelines by submitting this application.
* **Today's Date** (15): This is the date you are submitting the application.
* **Email Address** (16): This is your email address for contact purposes, required for communication and other notifications.
