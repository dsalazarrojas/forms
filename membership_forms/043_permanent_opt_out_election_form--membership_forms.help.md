# Permanent Opt Out Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to permanently opt out of certain services, communications, and subscriptions provided by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the "User Information" section.
2. In the "Opt Out Services" section, select the services you would like to opt out of.
3. In the "Opt Out Communications" section, select the communications you would like to opt out of.
4. In the "Opt Out Subscriptions" section, select the subscriptions you would like to opt out of.
5. Read the "Confirm Opt Out" section carefully to understand the implications of permanently opting out.
6. Enter your email address in the "Confirm Opt Out Email" section to confirm your opt-out request.
7. Finally, confirm your permanent opt-out decision by selecting the corresponding option in the "Permanent Opt Out" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required: false): This section requires you to enter your user information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Opt Out Services** (`Opt Out Services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the services you would like to opt out of by choosing from the available options. This includes services such as newsletters, promotional emails, or other notifications.
* **Opt Out Communications** (`Opt Out Communications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the communications you would like to opt out of, such as emails, phone calls, or letters.
* **Opt Out Subscriptions** (`Opt Out Subscriptions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the subscriptions you would like to opt out of, including any recurring or premium services.
* **Confirm Opt Out** (`Confirm Opt Out`, `text`, required: false): Read this section carefully to understand the implications of permanently opting out of the selected services, communications, and subscriptions.
* **Confirm Opt Out Email** (`Confirm Opt Out Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to confirm your opt-out request.
* **Permanent Opt Out** (`Permanent Opt Out`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to confirm your permanent opt-out decision, or "No" to cancel your request.
