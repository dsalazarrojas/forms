# Spa Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Membership Subscription Form is designed to help spa management staff collect essential information from clients, making it easier to manage memberships and subscriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Client Details section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your desired subscription details, including type, frequency, and payment method.
3. Choose your payment gateway and plan.
4. Enter your membership information, including start and end dates, payment amount, and payment status.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Additional Information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: This section collects your personal details, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Subscription Details**: Select your subscription type, frequency, and payment method.
* **Payment Details**: Choose your payment gateway, plan, and payment status.
* **Membership Information**: Enter your membership start and end dates, payment amount, and payment status.
* **Additional Information**: Add any additional notes or comments about your membership.

## Tips
* Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review your submission carefully before confirming.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our support team.
