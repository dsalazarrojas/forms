# League Tournament Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to register for a league tournament. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the team and tournament settings for the league.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `main_page` section with any necessary information or details about your team.
2. Enter your team's information in the `team_info` field. (Note: This is a numerical field, so enter a number if applicable.)
3. Select whether your team is participating in the league (`league_type`) by choosing from the options provided.
4. Provide the name of the tournament in the `tournament_name` field.
5. Enter the date and time of the tournament in the `date_and_time` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **main_page**: Enter any necessary information or details about your team.
* **team_info**: Enter a numerical value if applicable.
* **league_type**: Select 'Yes' if your team is participating in the league, or 'No' if not.
* **tournament_name**: Enter the name of the tournament.
* **date_and_time**: Enter the date and time of the tournament.
