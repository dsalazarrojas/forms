# Church Choir Membership Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential new members of the church choir. It asks for personal details, musical experience, and availability to help us understand your fit and preferences for joining the choir. Please provide honest and accurate answers to help us get to know you better.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct answer for each field, as indicated by the options provided.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Required)**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Provide your valid email address.
* **Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth (Required)**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Home Address (Required)**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Are you a member of this church (Required)**: Select your current status.
	+ Yes, active member
	+ Yes, but not active
	+ No, but interested
	+ Visiting from another church
* **What is your vocal range (Required)**: Select the range you are most comfortable singing.
	+ Soprano
	+ Alto
	+ Tenor
	+ Bass
	+ Not sure
* **Do you have previous choir experience (Required)**: Select the level of your experience.
	+ Yes, extensive experience
	+ Yes, some experience
	+ No, but I can read music
	+ No experience at all
* **Can you read sheet music (Required)**: Select your level of reading ability.
	+ Yes, fluently
	+ Yes, but need practice
	+ 'False'
* **Do you play any musical instruments (Optional)**: Select all instruments you play.
	+ Piano
	+ Guitar
	+ Drums
	+ Violin
	+ Flute
	+ Trumpet
	+ Organ
	+ None
* **Which rehearsal times work for you (Required)**: Select all times that fit your schedule.
	+ Sunday morning before service
	+ Sunday afternoon
	+ Wednesday evening
	+ Thursday evening
	+ Saturday morning
	+ Flexible schedule
* **Are you willing to participate in special performances (Required)**: Select your willingness to participate.
	+ Yes, always
	+ Yes, most of the time
	+ Only occasionally
	+ No, regular rehearsals only
* **Would you be interested in becoming a section leader (Optional)**: Select your interest level.
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
	+ Maybe in the future
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone (Required)**: Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Choir Robe Size (Required)**: Select the size of your choir robe.
	+ Extra Small
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ Extra Large
	+ 2XL
	+ 3XL
* **Do you have any other skills to contribute (Optional)**: Share any other skills you can offer.
* **How did you hear about the choir (Optional)**: Select how you found out about the choir.
	+ Church announcement
	+ Friend or family member
	+ Church website
	+ Social media
	+ During church service
	+ Other
* **Why do you want to join the choir (Optional)**: Share your motivation for joining the choir.
