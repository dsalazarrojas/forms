# creative_pinellas_conflict_of_interest_policy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide will assist users in understanding the purpose and process of completing the Creative Pinellas Conflict of Interest Policy form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing the necessary information for each field.
2. Ensure that you have reviewed and understood the purpose of the form.
3. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the individual completing the form.
- **Member Position** (`position`, text, optional): Enter the position or role of the individual completing the form, such as board member or committee member.
- **Board Member** (`board_member`, text, optional): Enter the name of the board on which the individual is serving or intends to serve.
- **Committee** (`committee`, text, optional): Enter the name of the committee on which the individual is serving or intends to serve.
- **Other Committee** (`other_committee`, text, optional): Enter any other committees on which the individual is serving or intends to serve.
- **Conflict of Interest Declaration** (`conflict_of_interest_declaration`, note, optional): This field is used for the individual to declare any potential conflicts of interest.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This field is for the individual to sign and date their declaration.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the form is completed.
- **Form Completed Date** (`form_completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the form was completed.
- **Form Completed By** (`form_completed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual completing the form.
- **Other Form Completed** (`other_form_completed`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the form.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization with which the individual is affiliated.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person or individual.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or individual.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or individual.
- **Conflict of Interest Declaration 2** (`conflict_of_interest_declaration_2`, note, optional): This field is used for the individual to declare any additional potential conflicts of interest.
- **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information.
