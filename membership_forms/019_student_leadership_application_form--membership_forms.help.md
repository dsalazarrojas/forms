# Student Leadership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about students who are applying for leadership positions in their school. The form is used by the school's leadership team to evaluate student applications and select candidates for leadership roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate and concise information about yourself. Please make sure to proofread your answers for any mistakes or typos.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (Required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Student Position** (Required): Describe the leadership position you are applying for.
* **Student Responsibilities** (Required): List your responsibilities as a student leader.
* **School Year** (Required): Enter the year you expect to graduate or complete your leadership position.
* **Leadership Skills** (Required): List your relevant leadership skills and experiences.
* **Leadership Experience** (Required): Describe your relevant leadership experiences, both in and out of the school.
* **School Committees** (Optional): If you have experience with school committees, list them here.
* **Student Clubs** (Optional): If you have experience with student clubs, list them here.
* **Community Service** (Optional): If you have experience with community service, describe it here.
* **Leadership Certifications** (Optional): If you have any leadership certifications, list them here.
* **References** (Optional): Provide the names and contact information of 2-3 people who can vouch for your leadership abilities.
* **Expectations** (Required): Clearly and concisely state your expectations from the leadership team, including any specific goals or objectives.
* **Agreement Section** (Required): Sign and date the agreement section, indicating that you understand the expectations and responsibilities of the leadership position.
* **Student Leadership Application Agreement** (Required): Sign and date this section, indicating that you agree to the terms of the application.
* **School Year Ending** (Required): Enter the year you expect to graduate or complete your leadership position.
* **Student Signature** (Required): Sign and date your signature, indicating that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **School Signature** (Required): Sign and date your signature, indicating that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **Contact Information** (Required): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Person** (Optional): If there is a specific person who should be contacted for further information, enter their name and contact information.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number.
