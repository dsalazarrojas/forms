This form is intended for individuals who wish to become members of a fishing club. To become a member, an individual must provide their personal details and preferred membership type. The form will guide users through this process, and the required fields are First name and Last name.

The form is divided into sections. Users should fill out the form in order, one section per page.

# Fishing Club Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal details and membership preferences from potential members of the fishing club. By filling out this form, you will be able to join the club and choose your preferred membership type.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in the order of the pages, one section at a time.
2. For each field, make sure to enter the required information. Required fields are First name and Last name.
3. When asked for a Date of birth, use the format dd/mm/yyyy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (3, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Membership type** (4, select one, optional): Choose your preferred membership type:
	+ Fishing
	+ Social
	+ Family
	+ Other
* **Payment method** (5, select multiple, optional): Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ Bank transfer
	+ PayPal
	+ Credit card
* **Email** (6, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (7, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the club administrators.
