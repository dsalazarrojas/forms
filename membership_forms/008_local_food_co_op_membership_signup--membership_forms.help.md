# Local Food Co Op Membership Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals or households to join Local Food Co Op and become a member.

## How To Complete This Form
Fill out the following fields to complete your membership registration:
1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "first_name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "last_name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
4. Enter your contact number in the field labeled "contact_number".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed on your membership records.
* **last_name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed on your membership records.
* **email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address to help us contact you about local food opportunities.
* **contact_number** (text, required: false): Enter your primary contact number for Local Food Co Op.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is up-to-date in case we need to contact you.
* If you're already a member, please don't fill out this form again.
* If you have any questions or issues, feel free to contact us at Local Food Co Op.
