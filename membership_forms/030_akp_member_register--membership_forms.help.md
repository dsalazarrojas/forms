# Akp Member Register - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Akp Member Register is used to register new members for the Akp - KSSC organization. It is a crucial tool for collecting membership information and storing it securely for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details such as first name, last name, date of birth, gender, contact number, email address, and residential address in the 'Member Personal Details' section.
2. Choose your membership category from the available options in the 'Membership Information' section.
3. Select your primary interest or activity from the options in the 'Membership Information' section.
4. Provide emergency contact information, including the contact person's name and phone number.
5. Agree to the terms of data consent in the final field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as per your official identification document.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per your official identification document.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the available options.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
* **Residential Address**: Enter your current residential address.
* **Membership Category**: Choose your membership category from the available options.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you are registering with the organization.
* **Previous Membership ID (if renewal)**: Enter your previous membership ID if you are renewing your membership.
* **Primary Interest or Activity**: Select your primary interest or activity from the available options.
* **Emergency Contact Full Name**: Enter the emergency contact person's full name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **I consent to AKP-KSSC storing my data for membership purposes**: Agree to the terms of data consent to allow the organization to store your data securely.
