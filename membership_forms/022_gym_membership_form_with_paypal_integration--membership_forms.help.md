# Gym Membership Form With PayPal Integration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Form With PayPal Integration is designed to guide you through the process of signing up for a gym membership. This form collects the necessary information from you, including your details, payment information, and membership plan selection. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **New Membership**: Enter a title for your new membership (e.g., "Jane Doe Membership"). This is the title of your membership account. This field is optional.
2. **Member Details**: Enter your details in this section. Please provide accurate information.
3. **Payment Details**: Enter your payment details in this section. Make sure to enter the correct information for your chosen payment method.
4. **Confirm Payment**: Review the payment details you entered to ensure they are accurate.
5. **Review and Submit**: Once you have reviewed the payment details, click the submit button to complete your membership application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Plan**: Select the membership plan that suits your needs. You can choose from:
	+ **Monthly Membership**: Ideal for those who want to commit to a monthly payment schedule.
	+ **Yearly Membership**: Suitable for those who want to pay annually.
	+ **Monthly Plus**: Additional features included with this plan.
* **Membership Terms and Conditions**: Please accept our membership terms and conditions by reading and agreeing to them.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for your selected membership plan.
* **Payment Method**: Select a payment method that suits you:
	+ **PayPal**: Use your PayPal account to complete the payment.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Use a bank transfer to complete the payment.
	+ **Cash**: Pay in cash at the gym reception.
* **Confirm Payment Method**: Confirm the payment method you selected.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date you want to start your membership.

## Tips

* Make sure to select a membership plan that suits your needs and budget.
* Double-check your payment details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the gym's support team for assistance.
