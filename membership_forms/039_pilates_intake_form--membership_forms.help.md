# Pilates Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pilates Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients who are new to or currently practicing pilates. This form helps pilates studios, gyms, or fitness establishments gather the necessary data to create a safe and effective workout environment for their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the General Info section to share your basic details.
2. In the Health History section, provide any medical conditions or health-related issues you have.
3. In the Fitness Levels section, describe your current fitness level.
4. In the Goals section, specify your objectives for participating in pilates.
5. For the Emergency Contact section, provide the contact information of an emergency contact person.
6. The Medical Conditions, Medications, Physical Abilities, and Allergies sections are for listing any relevant medical conditions, medications, or allergies you have.
7. In the Medical Record section, provide your current medical record information (if applicable).
8. Finally, the Emergency Transport section is for specifying your emergency transport information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (General Info)**: Enter your name, contact information (email and phone number), and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Health History (Health History)**: Describe any medical conditions or health-related issues you have. This could include chronic illnesses, allergies, or injuries.
* **Fitness Levels (Fitness Levels)**: Describe your current fitness level, such as beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Goals (Goals)**: Specify your objectives for practicing pilates, such as improving flexibility, strengthening your core, or enhancing overall well-being.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: Provide the name, contact information, and relationship of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)**: List any medical conditions you have, such as heart problems, diabetes, or chronic pain.
* **Medications (Medications)**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Physical Abilities (Physical Abilities)**: Describe your physical abilities and limitations, such as mobility or flexibility issues.
* **Emergency Services (Emergency Services)**: Specify if you have any emergency medical service needs or requirements.
* **Allergies (Allergies)**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical Record (Medical Record)**: Provide your current medical record information (if applicable).
* **Emergency Transport (Emergency Transport)**: Specify the transportation method you would like to use in case of an emergency, such as car or ambulance.
