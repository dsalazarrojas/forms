# fitness_training_cancellation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Training Cancellation Form is designed to be used by gym members to request cancellation of their fitness training sessions. This form is intended to be used for legitimate membership cancellations and should be used when a member has a valid reason for cancellation. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Fitness Training Cancellation Form" title to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the category "Membership Forms" to indicate that this is a membership cancellation request.
3. In the "Description" field, provide a clear and concise explanation for your cancellation request.
4. Select the "Cancel Reason" field to choose the reason for your cancellation.
5. In the "Further Action" field, select whether the gym management should take further action such as refund or re-schedule the session.
6. In the "Session Date" and "Session Time" fields, select the date and time of the session you wish to cancel.
7. Enter your contact details including email and phone number if you would like the gym management to contact you regarding your cancellation.
8. Once you have completed all the required fields, click submit to send your cancellation request to the gym management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fitness Training Cancellation Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for your cancellation request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select "Membership Forms" to indicate this is a membership cancellation request.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Explain the reason for your cancellation request.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback you may have regarding your cancellation request.
* **Information** (`information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information regarding your cancellation request.
* **Cancel Reason** (`cancel_reason`, text, required): Select the reason for your cancellation request.
* **Cancel Session** (`cancel_session`, select_one, required): Select the session you wish to cancel.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the session you wish to cancel.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the session you wish to cancel.
* **Further Action** (`further_action`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the gym management should take further action such as refund or re-schedule the session.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like the gym management to contact you regarding your cancellation.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like the gym management to contact you regarding your cancellation.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have selected the correct category to avoid confusion with general inquiries or support requests.
* Enter a clear and concise description for your cancellation request.
* Enter your contact details if you would like the gym management to contact you regarding your cancellation.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct session to cancel.
* Once you have completed all the required fields, click submit to send your cancellation request to the gym management.
