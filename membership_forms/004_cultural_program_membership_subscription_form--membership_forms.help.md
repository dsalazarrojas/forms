# Cultural Program Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural program membership subscription form is designed to collect information from users who want to subscribe to a cultural program. This form is used to gather organizational information, program type, and other relevant details necessary for the subscription process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the organization information section, providing details about your organization.
2. Select the program type that best describes your organization's involvement in the cultural program.
3. If required, provide the primary contact person's information, including their name and contact details.
4. In the billing information section, provide any necessary details about the billing process.
5. Review the membership terms, paying attention to any specific rules or regulations associated with the subscription.
6. In the payment info section, provide any necessary payment details.
7. Specify your communication preferences, such as how you would like to be contacted or updated.
8. Provide any additional relevant information in the additional fields section.
9. Confirm your membership details by reviewing and confirming the provided information.
10. Finally, review the organization name and confirm that it is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Information** (`organizationalinformation`, `text`, required: false): This is where you provide details about your organization, such as its name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Program Type** (`programtype`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of program your organization is involved in (arts, cultural, educational, historical, or other).
* **Primary Contact** (`primarycontact`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of the primary person associated with your organization's involvement in the cultural program.
* **Billing Information** (`billinginformation`, `text`, required: false): If necessary, provide details about the billing process, such as invoicing, payment methods, or other relevant billing information.
* **Membership Terms** (`membershipterms`, `note`, required: false): Review and agree to the membership terms, which include any specific rules or regulations associated with the subscription.
* **Payment Info** (`paymentinfo`, `text`, required: false): Provide any necessary payment details, such as payment methods, due dates, or other relevant payment information.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communicationpreferences`, `text`, required: false): Specify how you would like to be contacted or updated about the program, such as email, phone, or mail.
* **Additional Fields** (`additionalfields`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional relevant information not covered in the previous fields.
* **Confirm Membership** (`confirmmembership`, `text`, required: false): Review and confirm the provided information to complete the membership subscription process.
* **Organization Name** (`organizationname`, `text`, required: false): Review and confirm the organization name to ensure it is correct and accurately reflects the subscription details.
