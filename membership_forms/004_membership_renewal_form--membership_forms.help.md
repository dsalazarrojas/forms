# Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Renewal Form is designed to collect essential information from existing members of the organization to process their membership renewal. This form will help the administration to update the member's details and ensure their membership status is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the membership renewal form, follow these steps:

* Enter your Member Name to identify yourself as a member of the organization.
* Provide your Phone Number for contact purposes.
* Enter your Email Address to ensure that we can contact you regarding your membership.
* Enter your Address for record keeping purposes.
* If you have a Membership ID, please enter it to facilitate the renewal process.
* Select a Renewal Date for your membership to be renewed from.
* Select an Expiration Date for your membership to expire on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name** (member_name, text, required): Enter your name as a member of the organization.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter your address for record keeping purposes.
* **Membership ID** (membership_id, number, optional): If you have a membership ID, please enter it to facilitate the renewal process.
* **Renewal Date** (renewal_date, date, optional): Select a date for your membership to be renewed on.
* **Expiration Date** (expiration_date, date, optional): Select an expiration date for your membership to expire on.
