# STRENGTH EMPIRE Consult Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients regarding their fitness goals and training frequency. This form is designed to be completed by users who have multiple goals and want to provide additional details about each goal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Complete each page thoroughly, as all fields are required unless otherwise specified.
2. Start by completing the first page, which includes the client's name, email, phone number, and initial goal information.
3. On subsequent pages, you will be asked to provide additional goal information, including training frequency and type, training experience level, and available time slots.
4. Please note that some fields have select options, which are provided to help guide your completion.
5. Once you have completed all pages, click "Submit" to review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **What are your goals?** (`goal`, `text`, required): Please enter your primary fitness goal.
* **When do you want to achieve them by?** (`goal_date`, `date`, required): Please select the date by which you want to achieve your goal.
* **How often do you train?** (`goal_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you currently train.
* **What type of training do you do?** (`training_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the types of training you currently do.
* **What level of training experience do you have?** (`training_level`, `select_one`, required): Please select your current level of training experience.
* **What time of day do you have available?** (`available_time`, `time`, required): Please select the time slot you have available for training.
* **How many hours a week are you willing to commit?** (`availability`, `number`, required): Please select the number of hours per week you are willing to commit to training.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to add?** (`notes`, `note`, optional): This is an optional field for any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
* **How many times a week do you work out?** (`goal_frequency_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select how often you currently work out for your second goal.
* **What level of training experience do you have?** (`training_level_2`, `select_one`, required): Please select your current level of training experience for your second goal.
* **Goal 2** (`goal_2`, `text`, required): Please enter your second fitness goal.
* **Goal 2 Date** (`goal_date_2`, `date`, required): Please select the date by which you want to achieve your second goal.
* **Goal 2 Frequency** (`goal_frequency_2`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you want to achieve your second goal.
* **Frequency 3** (`goal_frequency_3`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you want to achieve your third goal.
* **Frequency 4** (`goal_frequency_4`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you want to achieve your fourth goal.
* **Frequency 5** (`goal_frequency_5`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you want to achieve your fifth goal.
* **Frequency 6** (`goal_frequency_6`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you want to achieve your sixth goal.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Please review your information before clicking "Submit".
