# Frequent Traveler Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frequent Traveler Membership Renewal Form is used for renewal of Frequent Traveler membership, which offers exclusive benefits to frequent travelers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Birth Date, Member ID, Loyalty Program, Travel Agency, and Renewal Date.
2. Select the correct Loyalty Program and Travel Agency from the provided options.
3. Enter any comments or feedback in the Comments field.
4. If you have a contact number and email, enter them in the Contact Number and Contact Email fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Birth Date** (date): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Member ID** (number): Enter your Frequent Traveler membership ID.
* **Loyalty Program** (select multiple): Select one or more from the available Loyalty Programs (Program A, B, or C).
* **Travel Agency** (select multiple): Select one or more from the available Travel Agencies (Agency A, B, or C).
* **Renewal Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to renew your membership for.
* **Membership Status** (select one): Select your current membership status (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (note): Enter any comments or feedback you have about your membership.
* **Contact Number** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Review your information carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
