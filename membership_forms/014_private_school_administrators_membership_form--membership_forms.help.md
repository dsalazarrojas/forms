# Private School Administrators Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from private school administrators, aiming to create a network of school administrators. Please help us by filling out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your administrator name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter the name of your school.
4. Select the type of your school (public, private, or charter).
5. Select your title within the school (principal, vice principal, dean, or director).
6. Tell us how long you have been an administrator.
7. Enter your school location.
8. Provide your contact information for further communication.
9. In the last field, explain why you want to join our network.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Administrator Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it is written on your business card or ID.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official school email address.
* **School Name** (`school`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Type** (`school_type`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following: Public, Private, Charter.
* **Administrator Title** (`administrator_title`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply: Principal, Vice Principal, Dean, Director.
* **How long have you been an administrator?** (`administrator_since`, select_one, optional): Choose how long you have been an administrator: Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your school.
* **Administrator Contact** (`administrator_contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information for further communication.
* **Why Do You Want To Join Our Network?** (`why_join`, text, optional): Explain briefly why you want to join our network.

## Tips
* Please fill out all fields to help us get a complete picture of the school administrators in our network.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
