# Sunflower Tech Club - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and payment information from users who want to join the Sunflower Tech Club.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "User First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "User Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your password in the "Password" field.
5. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
6. Confirm your password in the "Confirm Password 2" field.
7. Select a payment method (credit/debit card or PayPal) in the "Payment Method" field.
8. If paying with a credit/debit card, enter your card number in the "Payment Card Number" field.
9. Enter your card expiration date in the "Payment Expiration" field.
10. Enter your CVV (card verification value) in the "Payment CVV" field.
11. Enter your zip code for credit card billing address in the "Payment Zip" field.
12. Enter your billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
13. Enter your city in the "City" field.
14. Enter your state in the "State" field.
15. Enter your country in the "Country" field.
16. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
17. Select whether you accept the terms and conditions in the "Accept Terms" field.
18. Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions in the "Confirm Accept Terms" field.
19. Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions again in the "Confirm Accept Terms 2" field.
20. Enter your payment information again in the "Payment Method2" field.
21. Enter your address again in the "Address2" field.
22. Enter your city again in the "City2" field.
23. Enter your state again in the "State2" field.
24. Enter your country again in the "Country2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required): Enter your password.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, required): Confirm your password.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select a payment method (credit/debit card or PayPal).
* **Payment Card Number** (`payment_card_number`, text, optional): Enter your card number (if paying with a credit/debit card).
* **Payment Expiration** (`payment_expires`, text, optional): Enter your card expiration date (if paying with a credit/debit card).
* **Payment CVV** (`payment_cvv`, text, optional): Enter your CVV (card verification value) (if paying with a credit/debit card).
* **Payment Zip** (`payment_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for credit card billing address (if paying with a credit/debit card).
* **Billing Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your billing address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Accept Terms** (`accept_terms`, select_one, required): Select whether you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Confirm Accept Terms** (`confirm_accept_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Password 2** (`confirm_password_2`, text, required): Confirm your password.
* **Confirm Accept Terms 2** (`confirm_accept_terms_2`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Payment Method2** (`payment_method2`, select_one, required): Select a payment method (credit/debit card or PayPal) again.
* **Address2** (`address2`, text, optional): Enter your address again.
* **City2** (`city2`, text, optional): Enter your city again.
* **State2** (`state2`, text, optional): Enter your state again.
* **Country2** (`country2`, text, optional): Enter your country again.
* **Confirm Accept Terms 2** (`confirm_accept_terms_2`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you accept the terms and conditions again.
