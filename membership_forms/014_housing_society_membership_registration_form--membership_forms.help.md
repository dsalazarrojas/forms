# Housing Society Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Society Membership Registration Form is used to collect personal and contact information from individuals interested in joining the housing society. This form helps the society to track and manage member details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**.
2. Enter your **Last Name**.
3. Provide a valid **Email** address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the required format (DD/MM/YY).
6. Choose your **Membership Type**: Member or Non-Member.
7. Select the **Membership Duration** (optional).
8. Enter your **Address** (optional).
9. Enter your **Post Office Box** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Membership Type**: Choose whether you are a Member or Non-Member of the housing society.
* **Membership Duration**: Select the duration of your membership if you choose to be a member. (Note: This field is optional)
* **Address**: Enter your address if you are a member (optional).
* **Post Office Box**: Enter your post office box address if you need to (optional).
