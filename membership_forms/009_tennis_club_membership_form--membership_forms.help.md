# Tennis Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect membership information from individuals who wish to join or maintain their membership with the tennis club. The form will help the club administrators to gather essential details about the member's personal, contact, and membership status, as well as their preferences and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Personal Details section, which includes fields for name, email, and other contact information.
2. In the Emergency Contact section, select 'Yes' if you would like to have an emergency contact person notified in case of an emergency, or 'No' if you do not want to designate an emergency contact.
3. The Relationships section allows you to indicate if you have any relationships with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
4. In the Membership Details section, you can provide additional information about your membership status and type.
5. Please select your desired Membership Status (Active or Inactive) and Membership Type (e.g., Individual, Couple, etc.).
6. In the Payment Info section, you can provide any necessary payment details.
7. The Payment Method section lets you confirm if you have a payment method (e.g., bank transfer, credit card, etc.).
8. Finally, in the Membership Expiration Date section, you can enter your desired membership expiration date.
9. In the Tennis Club section, you can provide your club information (name, address, email, phone).
10. Please fill in the required fields and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Details
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like to have an emergency contact person notified in case of an emergency.
* **Relationships** (`emergency_contact_relationships`, `select_one`, optional): If you selected 'Yes' for emergency contact, select the type of relationship you have with the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like to have an emergency contact person notified in case of an emergency.

### Membership Details
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select your desired membership type (e.g., Individual, Couple, etc.).
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your desired membership status (Active or Inactive).

### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any necessary payment details.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred payment method (e.g., bank transfer, credit card, etc.).

### Membership Expiration Date
* **Membership Expiration Date** (`membership_expiration_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your desired membership expiration date.

### Tennis Club
* **Tennis Club** (`tennis_club`, `text`, optional): Enter your tennis club name.
* **Tennis Club Address** (`tennis_club_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your tennis club address.
* **Tennis Club Email** (`tennis_club_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your tennis club email.
* **Tennis Club Phone** (`tennis_club_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your tennis club phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the club administrators for assistance.
* The information collected will be used to maintain your membership status and provide necessary services.
