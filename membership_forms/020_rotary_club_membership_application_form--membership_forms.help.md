# Rotary Club Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rotary Club Membership Application Form is used by individuals interested in joining a Rotary Club to express their interest and provide necessary contact and membership information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your reason for applying to the Rotary Club in the "Application Reason" field. This section is optional, but it's a good opportunity to tell us about your interest in joining our club.
2. In the "Previous Affiliations" field, list any other organizations or clubs you have been a part of in the past. This will help us understand your background and experience.
3. Select your reason for joining the Rotary Club from the provided options in the "Reason for Joining" field. Choose one or more of the following options:
	* Membership benefits
	* Volunteer opportunities
	* Networking
4. In the "Privileges" field, describe the opportunities you are interested in and how you plan to participate in club activities.
5. In the "Responsibilities" field, acknowledge your understanding and commitment to your responsibilities as a club member, including paying dues, attending regular meetings, and participating in club events.
6. In the "Contact Details" field, select from the following options to provide us with your contact information:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone
7. Confirm that you have carefully reviewed and understood the information provided in the "Confirm" field.
8. Finally, submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.
