# vip_membership_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vip membership renewal form is used for renewing a Vip membership. It is a crucial part of maintaining accurate and up-to-date information about members' statuses.

## How To Complete This Form
To renew a membership using this form, please follow these steps:

- Ensure you have access to the membership system.
- Select the membership status as Active or Inactive.
- Enter the membership number, name, address, contact number, renewal date, and renewal fee.
- You can add notes for reference, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Membership Number (Membership Number, number, required)
* Enter the Vip membership number of the member whose membership is being renewed.

### Name (Name, text, required)
* Enter the full name of the member whose membership is being renewed.

### Address (Address, text, required)
* Enter the address of the member whose membership is being renewed.

### Contact Number (Contact Number, text, required)
* Enter the contact number of the member whose membership is being renewed.

### Membership Status (Membership Status, select_multiple, required)
* Select the status of the membership as Active or Inactive.

### Renewal Date (Renewal Date, date, required)
* Select the date when the membership is set to renew.

### Renewal Fee (Renewal Fee, number, required)
* Enter the renewal fee for the member.

### Notes (Notes, note, optional)
* Add any additional comments or notes about the member's membership.

### Email (Email, email, required)
* Enter the email address of the member whose membership is being renewed.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to prevent errors and ensure successful membership renewal.
