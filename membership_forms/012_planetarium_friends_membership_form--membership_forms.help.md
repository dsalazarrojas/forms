# Planetarium Friends Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Planetarium Friends Membership Form is designed to track your membership details, payment information, and status updates. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that you receive the best experience from our planetarium. We use this form to understand your preferences and needs to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes (optional).
5. **Membership Level**: Choose your current membership level: Bronze, Silver, or Gold.
6. **Monthly Donation**: Enter the amount you donate every month to support our planetarium.
7. **Membership Start Date**: Enter the date when your membership started.
8. **Membership End Date**: Enter the date when your membership is expected to end (if applicable).
9. **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to make your payment: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
10. **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount you paid for your membership.
11. **Payment Status**: Mark the status of your payment as Pending, Approved, or Declined.
12. **Membership Status**: Check all statuses that apply: Active, Inactive, or Pending (for members with pending membership status).
13. **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your membership.
14-17: **None** (these fields are optional and do not require any input).

- **Monthly Donation**: A required field where you enter the amount you donate monthly.
- **Membership Start Date**: A required field where you enter the date your membership started.
- **Membership End Date**: A required field where you enter the date your membership is expected to end.
- **Payment Method**: A required field where you choose how you want to make your payment: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
- **Payment Amount**: A required field where you enter the total amount you paid for your membership.
- **Payment Status**: A required field where you mark the status of your payment as Pending, Approved, or Declined.
- **Membership Status**: A required field where you check all statuses that apply: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
- **Notes**: An optional field where you can add any additional information or comments about your membership.
- **None**: These four fields are optional and do not require any input.

## Tips
- Double-check all required fields before submitting the form to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Make sure to enter a valid email address to receive notifications and updates about your membership.
- Be honest when marking your payment status as Pending, Approved, or Declined.
- Update your membership status accurately, as this affects your membership benefits and privileges.
