# Church Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Membership Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming members of the church. This form will guide you through the necessary steps and fields to provide your personal, contact, family, and faith background information. It also assesses your commitment to the church's doctrine and values.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form section by section, starting with the personal information section.
2. Answer all required fields (denoted by *) carefully and honestly.
3. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, *): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, *): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, *): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1999).
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, *): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, *): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, *): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, *): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, *): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, *): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): If you are a foreign national, enter your country of origin.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your marital status.
* **Spouse Name (if applicable)** (`spouse_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your spouse's name if applicable.
* **Number of Children** (`children_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of children you have.
* **Faith Tradition or Denomination** (`faith_tradition`, `text`, optional): Enter your faith tradition or denomination.
* **Baptism Status** (`baptism_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your baptism status.
* **Date of Baptism (if applicable)** (`baptism_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your baptism if applicable.
* **Desired Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date you wish to start your membership.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Church** (`referred_by`, `text`, optional): Enter how you heard about the church.
* **Areas of Ministry Interest (Select all that apply)** (`ministry_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all areas of ministry interest that apply to you.
* **Skills or Talents You Can Share** (`skills_talents`, `text`, optional): Enter any skills or talents you can share with the church.
* **I Agree with the Church Doctrine and Values** (`doctrinal_agreement`, `select_one`, *): Select your level of agreement with the church's doctrine and values.
* **Other** (Note: This field is not present in the original YAML but was included for completeness): This is a placeholder for any additional comments or information you wish to provide.
