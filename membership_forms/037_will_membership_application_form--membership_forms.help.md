# WILL Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The WILL Membership Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the WILL membership program. This form helps the organization to understand user preferences and requirements for the membership program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user data in the User Data field.
2. Provide your name in the Name field.
3. Enter your address in the Address field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
6. Select the desired Membership Level in the Membership Level field.
7. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
8. Enter the Time of Birth (if applicable) in the Time of Birth field.
9. Provide any additional Note about the membership application in the Note field.
10. Enter your Payment Details in the Payment Details field.
11. Select the preferred Payment Method in the Payment Method field.
12. Enter the Payment Date in the Payment Date field.
13. Select the desired Membership Type in the Membership Type field.
14. Select the Membership Status in the Membership Status field.
15. Enter the Membership Start Date in the Membership Start Date field.
16. Enter the Membership End Date in the Membership End Date field.
17. Select the Membership Term in the Membership Term field.
18. Provide any additional Membership Notes in the Membership Notes field.
19. Select the preferred Payment Terms in the Payment Terms field.
20. Enter any additional User Notes in the User Notes field.
21. Enter the Payment Terms Details in the Payment Terms Details field.
22. Enter any additional User Data 1 in the User Data 1 field.
23. Enter any additional Payment Terms 2 in the Payment Terms 2 field.
24. Enter any additional User Data 2 in the User Data 2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Data** (User Data, text, optional): Enter any additional information you want to provide about yourself.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Membership Level** (Membership Level, select_one, optional): Select the desired membership level (Basic, Premium, or VIP).
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (Time of Birth, time, optional): Enter your time of birth.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Enter any additional note about the membership application.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, optional): Enter your payment details.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, optional): Select the preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Other).
* **Payment Date** (Payment Date, date, optional): Enter the payment date.
* **Membership Type** (Membership Type, select_multiple, optional): Select the desired membership type (Basic, Premium, or VIP).
* **Membership Status** (Membership Status, select_one, optional): Select the membership status (New, Renewal, Upgrade, or Downgrade).
* **Membership Start Date** (Membership Start Date, date, optional): Enter the membership start date.
* **Membership End Date** (Membership End Date, date, optional): Enter the membership end date.
* **Membership Term** (Membership Term, select_one, optional): Select the membership term (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Membership Notes** (Membership Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional membership notes.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, select_multiple, optional): Select the preferred payment terms (One Time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **User Notes** (User Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional user notes.
* **Payment Terms Details** (Payment Terms Details, text, optional): Enter any additional payment terms details.
* **User Data 1** (User Data 1, text, optional): Enter any additional user data 1.
* **Payment Terms 2** (Payment Terms 2, text, optional): Enter any additional payment terms 2.
* **User Data 2** (User Data 2, text, optional): Enter any additional user data 2.
