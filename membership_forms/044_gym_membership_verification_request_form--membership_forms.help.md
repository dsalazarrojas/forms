# Gym Membership Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Verification Request Form is used to verify the membership of a gym member. This form is required for certain administrative or legal purposes, such as updating member records or verifying member identity. Please complete this form carefully to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your membership ID.
2. Enter your email address that is linked to your membership account.
3. Enter your phone number as it appears on your membership ID.
4. Select the type of document you are uploading, such as Member ID Card or Proof of Residence.
5. Upload the required document.
6. Check the box to consent to share your personal information with our team.
7. Check the box to consent to use your personal information for administrative or legal purposes.
8. Sign your name to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Member Name)** (`member_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your membership ID.
* **Email** (`member_email`, email, required): Enter the email address linked to your membership account.
* **Phone** (`member_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number as it appears on your membership ID.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of document you are uploading, such as Member ID Card or Proof of Residence.
* **Document Upload** (`document_upload`, text, required): Upload the required document.
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Check the box to give our team permission to share your personal information with relevant parties.
* **Consent to Use** (`consent_to_use`, select_one, required): Check the box to grant our team permission to use your personal information for administrative or legal purposes.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your identity.
