# Membership Program Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you identify areas of improvement for your membership program and create a plan to enhance its effectiveness. It will guide you through a series of questions to assess your current program's state, goals, and challenges, and outline the steps to reach your target program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided text fields, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Focus on your current membership program's current state and the desired state you aim to achieve.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this will help you create a realistic and effective plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the current state of your program?**: Describe the current situation of your membership program, including its strengths and weaknesses.
* **What is the target state of your program?**: Define the desired state of your program, highlighting the goals and aspirations you aim to achieve.
* **What are some common issues with your current program?**: Identify the challenges and difficulties you face with your current membership program.
* **What are some common issues with your target program?**: List the challenges and obstacles you anticipate or expect with your target program.
* **What steps will you take to improve your current program?**: Outline the actions and strategies you plan to implement to enhance your current program.
* **What steps will you take to improve your target program?**: Describe the measures and initiatives you intend to take to reach your target program.
* **How will you measure the success of your program?**: Define the metrics and methods you will use to evaluate the effectiveness of your program.
* **What are some common barriers to measuring success?**: Identify the challenges and obstacles you may face when measuring the success of your program.
* **How does this program align with your overall organizational goals?**: Explain how your membership program fits into your organization's broader objectives and priorities.

## Tips
- Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering each question.
- Compare your current and target programs to identify areas of improvement.
- Think critically about the challenges and obstacles you may face.
- Use the insights and plan you create to drive positive change and growth in your membership program.
