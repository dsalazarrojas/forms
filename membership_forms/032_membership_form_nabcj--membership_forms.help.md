# membership_form_nabcj - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or update membership records for individuals or organizations, capturing essential details such as name, contact information, and address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are submitting for a valid reason (e.g., membership application, update, or inquiry).
2. Fill in the required fields marked as 'required' by the system.
3. Double-check that you have entered accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Member Type** (`member_type`, select_one, required): Select whether you are an "Individual" or "Organization".
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country of your address.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time (if applicable).

## Tips
* Ensure the 'Member Type' field is correctly filled to ensure accurate record categorization.
* Enter accurate email and phone numbers to facilitate communication and updates.
* Double-check your address for correct city, country, state, and zip code.
* Use valid date and time format if required.
