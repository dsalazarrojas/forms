# media_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for membership in a media-related group or organization. It collects personal information and contact details to verify the user's identity and eligibility for membership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure all required fields are filled out.
2. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth if you want to provide this information for verification purposes. If you don't provide this information, it will not affect your membership application.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address as it is written on your identification documents.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are eligible for membership based on your location. This field is used to verify your eligibility for membership.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code to help us verify your address.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact us for assistance.
