# Member Engagement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Member Engagement Report Form is designed to gather information about our members, including their member names, contact details, and engagement status. This form will help us to track and analyze our member engagement and provide insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the member's **Member Name** in the designated text field.
2. Provide the member's **Email** address if available.
3. Enter the member's **Phone** number if available.
4. Select the **Date Last Contacted** by using the date picker.
5. Choose the **Membership Status** of the member from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name** (<name>, text, required: false): Enter the name of the member as it appears on their membership record.
* **Email** (<email>, email, required: false): Enter the member's email address (if available).
* **Phone** (<phone>, text, required: false): Enter the member's phone number (if available).
* **Date Last Contacted** (<date_last_contacted, date, required: false): Select the date when you last contacted the member using the date picker.
* **Membership Status** (<membership_status, select_one, required: false): Choose the current membership status of the member from the dropdown menu (active, inactive, inactive pending).
