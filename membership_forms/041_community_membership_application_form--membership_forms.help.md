# Community Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Membership Application Form is designed to collect information from potential community members for the purpose of processing their membership applications. This form is intended for individuals who wish to become members of the community and participate in its activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your membership record.
2. Provide your email address for membership confirmation and updates.
3. Enter a phone number for contact purposes (optional).
4. Select your preferred membership type from the available options.
5. Choose the areas of interest that apply to you (optional).
6. Confirm your agreement to the community's code of conduct.
7. Enter the details of an emergency contact person (optional).
8. Add any additional notes or information that you would like to share with the community (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your membership record.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address for membership confirmation and updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Membership type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Select your preferred membership tier from the available options.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address for membership materials (optional).
* **Areas of interest** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the areas of interest that apply to you from the available options.
* **Agree to code of conduct** (`agree_to_code_of_conduct`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the community's code of conduct.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the details of an emergency contact person (optional).
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information that you would like to share with the community (optional).
