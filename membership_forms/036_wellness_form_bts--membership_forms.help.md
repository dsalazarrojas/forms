# Wellness Form BTS - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about users, their wellness, and preferences. It seems to be a duplicate or backup form to gather additional data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, following the instructions for each field.
2. Enter your user name in the "User Input" field.
3. Enter your email in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select one option from the "Select One" field.
6. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
7. Enter a note in the "Note" field.
8. Fill out the "Form ID" field with the given information.
9. Select the assigned tool from the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Select the category from the "Category" field.
11. Enter a description in the "Description" field.
12. Save the form to generate an output file.
13. Repeat the process for the second part of the form.
