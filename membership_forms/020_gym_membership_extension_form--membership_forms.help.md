# Gym Membership Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a membership extension for active, suspended, or cancelled/ expired gym memberships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the requested information.
2. Make sure to select the correct membership type (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
3. Choose the correct current membership status (Active, Suspended, Cancelled, or Expired).
4. Provide the start and end dates for the membership renewal, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Enter the payment amount.
6. Select the payment method (if applicable) and the extension terms.
7. Submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **membership_type**: Choose the type of membership you currently have (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **current_membership_status**: Choose the status of your membership (Active, Suspended, Cancelled, or Expired).
* **renewal_start_date**: Enter the date you would like your membership to be renewed.
* **renewal_end_date**: Enter the date you would like your membership to end (if you are extending your membership for a specific period).
* **payment_amount**: Enter the total amount to be paid for the membership extension.
* **extension_terms**: Select the extension terms (Month-to-Month or 6-Month Extension).
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method you would like to use for the membership extension (Credit Card, Cash, or Direct Debit).
* **payment_terms**: If you have any special payment terms, provide them in the space below.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as required: true).
* If you have any questions or issues while filling the form, contact the gym administration.
* Ensure that you enter the correct dates in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
