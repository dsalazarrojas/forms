# Golf Club Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Club Membership Renewal Form is a tool for active golf club members to renew their membership with the club. This form is used to collect necessary information for the club's administration and management, ensuring that members' data is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to the "Membership Renewal" question to indicate your willingness to renew your membership.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
6. Enter any additional payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
7. Enter any notes or comments about your membership in the "Note" field.
8. Choose your category preference from the "Category" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the output file in the "Output File" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Renewal**: This field allows you to indicate your willingness to renew your membership. Please select "Yes" if you wish to continue your membership, or "No" if you wish to cancel your membership.
* **Name**: This field is for your name, which will be used for club records and communication.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address, which will be used for official club communication.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number, which will be used in case of emergencies or important updates.
* **Payment Method**: This field allows you to choose how you wish to pay for your membership renewal. Please select from the available options.
* **Payment Details**: If you have any specific payment details or requirements, enter them here.
* **Note**: This field is for any additional notes or comments about your membership. Please use it to communicate any important details or questions with the club administration.
* **Category**: This field allows you to choose a category for your membership. Please select from the available options.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is for the name of the tool assigned to you. If applicable, enter the name of the tool issued to you by the club.
* **Output File**: This field is for any output file related to your membership. If applicable, enter the file name or location.
* **Form ID**: This field is not currently used.
* **Category**: This field is a duplicate of the "Category" field above.
* **Description**: This field is not currently used.
* **Form Category**: This field is not currently used.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is a duplicate of the "Assigned Tool" field above.
* **Form Title**: This field is not currently used.
* **Form Description**: This field is not currently used.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field or its purpose, please contact the club administration for clarification.
* The form should only be filled out once a year, during the renewal period.
