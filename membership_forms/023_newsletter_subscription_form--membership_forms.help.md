# Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to subscribe to our newsletter. By filling out this form, customers can provide their contact information and payment details to receive our newsletter. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the field labeled "Customer Name".
2. Provide your email address in the field labeled "Email".
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the field labeled "Phone Number".
4. Choose your subscription type (PayPal or Check/Postal) in the field labeled "Subscription Type".
5. Select your preferred payment method (PayPal or Check/Postal) in the field labeled "Payment Method".
6. Provide any additional payment information in the field labeled "Payment Info".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscribe To Our Newsletter** (`newsletter_subscription_form`, note, not required): This is the main field for the form, and it serves as a placeholder for the form's purpose.
* **Customer Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter a valid email address where we can send the newsletter. This will be used to send newsletter issues to your inbox.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you about your subscription.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_one, not required): Choose how you want to subscribe to our newsletter, either with PayPal or Check/Postal.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Select your preferred payment method for subscribing to our newsletter, either PayPal or Check/Postal.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional payment information, such as a payment address or other details.
