# data_to_policy_training_and_mentorship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Data to Policy Training and Mentorship Application" form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in participating in the data science policy training and mentorship program. This form helps us understand your preferences, availability, and expectations from the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly to ensure accurate information is collected.
2. Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Month** (`date_of_birth_month`, `number`, required: false): Enter the month of your birth (1-12).
* **Year** (`date_of_birth_year`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year of your birth.
* **What are you interested in** (`interest`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all areas you are interested in, using "Yes" or "No" options.
* **When are you available** (`availability`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you are available for the mentorship program, using "Yes" or "No" options.
* **Time of Day** (`availability_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of day when you are available. 
* **Day of Week** (`availability_day`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the day of the week when you are available.
* **Which program are you interested in** (`program_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all programs you are interested in, using "Yes" or "No" options.
* **What day(s) of the week are you available** (`availability_week`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the days of the week when you are available.
* **What are your mentorship goals** (`mentorship_goals`, `text`, required: false): Enter your goals for the mentorship program.
* **What do you expect from the mentorship** (`mentorship_expectations`, `text`, required: false): Enter your expectations from the mentorship program.
* **How do you prefer to communicate** (`communication_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your preferred communication method, using "Yes" or "No" options.
* **Do you have any mentorship experience** (`mentorship_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose if you have any mentorship experience, using "True" or "False" options.
* **Have you ever been involved in a mentorship program in the past** (`mentorship_referrals`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose if you have ever been involved in a mentorship program in the past, using "True" or "False" options.
* **If yes, what was the name of the program** (`mentorship_referrals_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the program if you have ever been involved in one.
* **How long were you involved in the program** (`mentorship_referrals_duration`, `text`, required: false): Enter how long you were involved in the program.
* **What was your experience like** (`mentorship_referrals_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter your experience in the mentorship program.
* **What was the outcome of the program** (`mentorship_referrals_outcome`, `text`, required: false): Enter the outcome of the mentorship program.
* **Would you recommend the program** (`mentorship_referrals_recommend`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose if you would recommend the program, using "True" or "False" options.
* **If yes, why** (`mentorship_referrals_recommend_reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter why you would recommend the program.
* **If no, why not** (`mentorship_referrals_referral_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter why you wouldn't recommend the program.

**Tips:**

1. Make sure to fill out all fields accurately.
2. If you're unsure about any field, please leave it blank and we will contact you to clarify.
3. Please be honest in your responses as we use this information to better tailor the mentorship experience to your needs.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
