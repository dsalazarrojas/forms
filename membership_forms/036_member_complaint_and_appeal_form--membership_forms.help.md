# Member Complaint And Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track member complaints and appeals. It helps members to report any issues they encounter, and it aids in the resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for each field:
	* Member Complaint Form: Enter a detailed description of your complaint.
	* Member Appeal Form: Enter the reason for your appeal.
	* Date of issue: Enter the date when the issue occurred.
	* Date of complaint: Enter the date when you reported the complaint.
	* Type of complaint: Select the type of complaint you are reporting (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
	* Evidence provided (upload files): Attach any relevant evidence or supporting documents to your complaint.
	* Resolution details: Enter any relevant information about the resolution or resolution process.
	* Member Name: Enter your name as a member.
	* Member Email: Enter your email address.
	* Contact Number: Enter your contact number.
	* Member Address: Enter your address.
	* City: Enter your city.
	* State: Enter your state.
	* ZIP: Enter your ZIP code.
	* Appeal by: Select the reason for your appeal (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
	* Appeal date: Enter the date of your appeal.
	* Facility issue: Select the type of facility issue you are experiencing (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
	* Member Complaint Status: Select the status of your complaint (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
	* Resolution Status: Select the status of the resolution (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
	* Member comment: Enter any additional comments or information.
	* Staff comment: Enter any comments or feedback from staff.
	* Resolution details 2: Enter any additional resolution details.
	* Additional comments: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
