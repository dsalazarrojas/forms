# Mug Club Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a membership application for the Mug Club, where you can sign up to become a member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your details in the "Mug Club Details" field.
2. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
3. Add your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Choose whether you agree to the club's rules by selecting "Agree" or "Decline" from the "Agree to Rules" dropdown menu.
6. If you are agreeing to the rules, select a date for your agreement in the "Agreement Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mug Club Details** (Required/Optional): A field to provide your details about yourself, such as your name and interests.
* **Address** (Required/Optional): Enter your address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (Required/Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (Required/Optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Agree to Rules** (Required/Optional): Choose whether you agree or decline to abide by the club's rules.
* **Agreement Date** (Required/Optional): If you agree to the rules, select a date to confirm your agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your details accurately and honestly.
* Double-check your address, phone number, and email address for correctness.
* Select "Agree" only if you understand and accept the club's rules.
* If you select "Decline", you will not be able to participate in the club's activities.
* Make sure to choose a date for your agreement if you select "Agree".
