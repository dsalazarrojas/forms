# Crew List Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit crew information for a particular crew. This form is designed to gather essential details about the crew, including their name, contact information, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form fields as prompted.
2. Enter your member's name in the "Member Name" field.
3. Enter your member's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your member's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter any additional information or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Form**: This is the submit button, used to send the form data to the server after completion.
* **Member Name**: Enter the full name of the member you are submitting information about. This is a text field.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the member you are submitting information about. This is a valid email address field that should be in a standard "name@domain.com" format.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the member you are submitting information about. This is a text field.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about the member. This is a free-form text field where you can enter any relevant details.
