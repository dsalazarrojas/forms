# Social Movement Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for a social movement community. It is a registration form for people who want to join the community and become part of its activities and discussions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Registrant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Registrant Email" field. Please note that this field is required.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Registrant Phone" field.
4. Enter your occupation in the "Registrant Occupation" field.
5. Enter your main motivation for joining the community in the "Main Motivation" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Registrant Address" field.
7. Enter any additional notes you'd like to share in the "Registrant Notes" field.
8. Select all the interests that apply to you in the "Registrant Interests" field. This field is a multiple-select field with options "Movement", "Social", and "Community".
9. In the "Agree to Community Rules" field, select the option that best describes your agreement to follow the community rules.
10. Finally, select the date of interest from the calendar in the "Date of interest" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Name**: Enter your full name, as you would like to be referred to by the community.
* **Registrant Email**: A valid email address is required for us to contact you and keep you updated on community news and activities.
* **Registrant Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you in case of any last-minute updates or changes to community activities.
* **Registrant Occupation**: A brief description of your occupation or role.
* **Main Motivation**: A brief description of why you want to join the community.
* **Registrant Address**: Enter your current address to help us understand where you're coming from.
* **Registrant Notes**: Any additional information you'd like to share about yourself, your interests, or anything else relevant to your application.
* **Registrant Interests**: Select all the interests that apply to you. This helps us tailor our activities and communications to better suit your needs.
* **Agree to Community Rules**: By selecting "I agree to the Community Rules", you agree to follow the community's rules and guidelines. If you do not agree, you will not be able to participate in the community.
* **Date of interest**: A date when you'd like to start participating in the community activities.
