# life-member-reaffirmation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for life members to update their information and specify their preferences for communication and information use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please review each field and provide the requested information. You can enter your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and other required fields. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the organization's administration for guidance.
2.  Select how you want to be contacted (phone, email, or both) from the "How do you want to be contacted?" field.
3.  Choose how you will use the information provided (marketing, research, or both) from the "How will you use this information?" field.
4.  Review and submit the form when you have completed the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it is currently spelled on any official documents or records you may have.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it is currently spelled on any official documents or records you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address. This is where you will be contacted if you select to be contacted by email or phone.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number. If you select to be contacted by phone, this number will be used.
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (`select_one`, required: true): Select one of the options: "By Phone", "By Email", or "By Both".
*   *If you select "By Phone" or "By Both", the phone number you enter above will be used.*
*   *If you select "By Email", the email address you enter above will be used.*
*   *Please note that not selecting "By Phone" will not stop you from being contacted by email.*
* **How will you use this information?** (`select_one`, required: true): Select one of the options: "For Marketing Purposes", "For Research", or "For Both".
*   *Choose "For Marketing Purposes" if you would like to receive information about upcoming events and promotions from the organization.*
*   *Choose "For Research" if you would like to receive surveys or other research-related information from the organization.*
*   *Please note that not selecting "For Research" will not stop you from receiving marketing information.*
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is not required but will help the organization have a record of your birthdate.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you last visited the organization. This is not required but will help the organization have a record of your visit time.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the organization's administration for guidance.
