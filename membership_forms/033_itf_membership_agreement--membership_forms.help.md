# itf_membership_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about new membership applicants, including demographic data, emergency contact information, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "New Member Information" section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your email and phone number for our records.
3. If applicable, enter information about your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and email.
4. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide a brief description.
5. Review and agree to the membership terms and conditions by selecting "True" in the "Consent Terms" field.
6. If you have an emergency contact relationship other than "Parent", "Partner", or "Friend", please select "Other" in the "Relationship" field and provide a description of your relationship.
