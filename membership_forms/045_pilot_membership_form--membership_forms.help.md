# Pilot Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pilot Membership Form is a document used to collect information from pilots who wish to join a membership organization. This form helps us to understand your qualifications, experience, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Select the correct options for the membership interest and join membership fields.
3. Enter your pilot-specific details, such as education, license, and certifications.
4. Provide your contact information, including phone, mobile, and email.
5. Add any additional comments or information you think is relevant to your application.
6. Sign the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Interest** (`membership_interest`): This field asks for your interest in joining the membership organization. Please select one of the available options: Air Force, Navy, or Army.
* **Join Membership** (`join_member`): This field indicates your intention to join the membership organization. Select True to indicate your interest in joining, and False otherwise.
* **Become Member** (`become_member`): Similar to the previous field, this indicates your intention to become a member of the organization. Select True to indicate your interest, and False otherwise.
* **Pilot Education** (`pilot_education`): This field is for you to provide more information about your education or training related to flying or aviation.
* **Pilot License** (`pilot_license`): Enter your pilot's license number if applicable.
* **Pilot Certifications** (`pilot_certifications`): Enter any relevant pilot certifications you hold.
* **Pilot Language** (`pilot_language`): Enter the language you are most comfortable with or proficient in.
* **Pilot Flight Hours** (`pilot_flight_hours`): Enter the total flight hours you have logged.
* **Pilot Flight Experience** (`pilot_flight_experience`): Enter the number of years you have been flying.
* **Pilot Formation Hours** (`pilot_formation_hours`): Enter the number of hours you spend in formation flying.
* **Pilot Last Flight** (`pilot_last_flight`): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Pilot Last Flight Date** (`pilot_last_flight_date`): Enter the date of your last flight.
* **Pilot Last Flight Type** (`pilot_last_flight_type`): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Pilot Last Flight Hours** (`pilot_last_flight_hours`): Enter the hours spent in your last flight.
* **Pilot Phone** (`pilot_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Pilot Mobile** (`pilot_mobile`): Enter your mobile number.
* **Pilot Email** (`pilot_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Pilot Additional Comments** (`pilot_additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or information you think is relevant to your application.
* **Pilot Signature** (`pilot_signature`): Sign the form to confirm your application.
* **Pilot License Number** (`pilot_license_number`): Enter your pilot's license number if applicable.
* **Pilot Issuing State** (`pilot_issuing_state`): Enter the state where you were issued your pilot's license.
* **Pilot Issued Date** (`pilot_issued_date`): Enter the date your pilot's license was issued.
* **Pilot Issuing Authority** (`pilot_issuing_authority`): Enter the authority that issued your pilot's license.
* **Pilot Additional Info** (`pilot_additional_info`): Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your application.
