# studio_membership_waiver_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The studio membership waiver form is used to collect important information from members and their emergency contacts. This form helps us to verify membership details and emergency contact information for our members. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Name** in the "name" field.
2. Enter your **Contact Email** in the "contact_email" field.
3. Read and agree to the **Membership Terms** in the "membership_terms" field.
4. Provide your **Emergency Contact Name** in the "emergency_contact_name" field.
5. Enter your **Emergency Contact Phone** in the "emergency_contact_phone" field.
6. Describe your **Emergency Contact Relationship** in the "emergency_contact_relationship" field.
7. Enter the **Date of Emergency Contact** (if applicable) in the "date_of_emergency_contact" field.
8. Provide your **Emergency Contact Address** in the "emergency_contact_address" field.
9. Enter any other **Emergency Contact Information** in the "emergency_contact_other_contact_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name (Text, required: False)**: This field is for your personal name and contact information. Please enter your name as it should appear on your membership documents.
* **contact_email (Email, required: False)**: This field is for your contact email address. We will use this to reach out to you with updates and membership information.
* **agreement (Text, required: False)**: This field is for you to read and agree to our membership terms.
* **membership_terms (Text, required: False)**: This field shows our membership terms for you to read and understand.
* **emergency_contact_name (Text, required: False)**: This field is for your emergency contact's name.
* **emergency_contact_phone (Text, required: False)**: This field is for your emergency contact's phone number.
* **emergency_contact_relationship (Text, required: False)**: This field is for describing your relationship to your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, family member, business partner).
* **date_of_emergency_contact (Date, required: False)**: This field is for the date you last had contact with your emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_address (Text, required: False)**: This field is for your emergency contact's address.
* **emergency_contact_other_contact_info (Text, required: False)**: This field is for any other important contact information for your emergency contact.
