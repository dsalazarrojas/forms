# New Member Fitness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a new member's fitness level, medical history, and lifestyle habits. This data will be used to create a personalized fitness plan tailored to the member's needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Each question is designed to help us understand your current fitness level and medical history. Please note that some questions may not be applicable to you, and that's okay. Answer only what you feel comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current weight in kilograms.
* **Blood Pressure Systolic** (`blood_pressure_systolic`, `number`, required: false): Enter your resting blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
* **Blood Pressure Diastolic** (`blood_pressure_diastolic`, `number`, required: false): Enter your diastolic blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, `number`, required: false): Enter your average blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
* **Resting Heart Rate** (`resting_heart_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter your resting heart rate in beats per minute (bpm).
* **Exercise Heart Rate** (`exercise_heart_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter your exercise heart rate in beats per minute (bpm).
* **Resting Blood Pressure** (`resting_blood_pressure`, `number`, required: false): Enter your resting blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
* **Exercise Blood Pressure** (`exercise_blood_pressure`, `number`, required: false): Enter your exercise blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
* **Blood Cholesterol** (`blood_cholesterol`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current blood cholesterol level.
* **Smoker** (`smoker`, `select_one`, required: false): Do you currently smoke?
	+ 'True' for yes, 'False' for no.
* **Alcohol User** (`alcohol_user`, `select_one`, required: false): Do you consume alcohol regularly?
	+ 'True' for yes, 'False' for no.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any medical conditions you currently experience.
	+ Hypertension
	+ Diabetes
	+ Heart disease
	+ High cholesterol
	+ Stroke
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any medications you are currently taking.
	+ Statins
	+ Beta blockers
	+ Diuretics
	+ Pain relievers
	+ Blood pressure meds
	+ Blood thinners
	+ Insulin
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any allergies you have.
	+ Peanuts
	+ Shellfish
	+ Dairy
	+ Gluten
	+ Soy
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
	+ Mom
	+ Dad
	+ Brother
	+ Sister
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical notes or information.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you exercise.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Never
* **Exercise Duration** (`exercise_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of your exercise sessions in minutes.
* **Exercise Type** (`exercise_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of exercise you typically do.
	+ Cardio
	+ Strength training
	+ Flexibility
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Blood Glucose** (`blood_glucose`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current blood glucose level.
* **Resting Heart Rate 2** (`resting_heart_rate_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter your resting heart rate in beats per minute (bpm).
* **Exercise Heart Rate 2** (`exercise_heart_rate_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter your exercise heart rate in beats per minute (bpm).
* **Resting Blood Pressure 2** (`resting_blood_pressure_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter your resting blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
* **Exercise Blood Pressure 2** (`exercise_blood_pressure_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter your exercise blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
