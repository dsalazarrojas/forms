# Wellness Community Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Community Membership Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from prospective members who wish to join our community. This form ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date details about you, allowing us to provide the best possible experience and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Use the appropriate option or selection for each field.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, accurately.
5. Choose the correct membership type (Individual, Couple, or Family).
6. Select your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer or Credit Card).
7. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code correctly.
8. Provide your date of birth.
9. Enter the membership start and end dates if applicable.
10. Check your membership status (Active or Inactive).
11. Optionally, you can provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Form** (`1`, `membership_form`, text, not required): Enter your desired membership name or title.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `contact_information`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your contact information.
* **Name** (`3`, `contact_name`, text, not required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`4`, `contact_email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`5`, `contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Membership Type** (`6`, `membership_type`, select_one, not required): Select your membership type:
	+ Individual
	+ Couple
	+ Family
* **Membership Level** (`7`, `membership_level`, select_multiple, not required): Select your membership level:
	+ Basic
	+ Premium
* **Payment Type** (`8`, `payment_type`, select_one, not required): Select your preferred payment method:
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Credit Card
* **Payment Method** (`9`, `payment_method`, select_multiple, not required): Select your payment method:
	+ Online
	+ Offline
* **Address Line 1** (`10`, `address_line_1`, text, not required): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`11`, `address_line_2`, text, not required): Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (`12`, `city`, text, not required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`13`, `state`, text, not required): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip Code** (`14`, `zip_code`, text, not required): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`15`, `date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`16`, `time_of_birth`, time, not required): Enter your time of birth.
* **Note** (`17`, `note`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`18`, `email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`19`, `phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Membership Start Date** (`20`, `membership_start_date`, date, not required): Enter your membership start date.
* **Membership End Date** (`21`, `membership_end_date`, date, not required): Enter your membership end date.
* **Note** (`22`, `note`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Membership Status** (`23`, `membership_status`, select_one, not required): Select your membership status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Assigned Tool** (`24`, `assigned_tool`, text, not required): Enter the assigned tool.
* **Assigned User** (`25`, `assigned_user`, text, not required): Enter the assigned user.
