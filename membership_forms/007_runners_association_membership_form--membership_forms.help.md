# Runners Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to register as a member of the Runners Association. The association brings together individuals who share a passion for running, allowing members to connect, share experiences, and participate in running events. By filling out this form, you will provide essential details for your membership, enabling us to get to know you better and offer tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each field with accurate and complete information**. Please note that some fields are not mandatory, and you can fill them out as needed.
2. **Review your details carefully** before submitting to ensure accuracy.
3. **Submit the form once you have filled out all required and desired fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name**: <label>
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our records and in association activities.

### 2. **Email**: <label>
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### 3. **Phone**: <label>
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for any further communication or event coordination.

### 4. **Membership Level**: <label>
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the membership level that suits your running experience and goals. Our levels are:
	+ Level 1: Basic Membership
	+ Level 2: Silver Membership
	+ Level 3: Gold Membership

### 5. **Payment Method**: <label>
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer

### 6. **Payment Date**: <label>
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): If you choose to pay immediately, select the payment date. Otherwise, this will be filled out later.

### 7. **Agreement Terms**: <label>
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, `note`, required: false): Read and accept our terms and conditions.

### 8. **Runner Name**: <label>
* **Runner Name** (`runner_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the runner you are registering.

### 9. **Runner Age**: <label>
* **Runner Age** (`runner_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age as a runner.

### 10. **Runner Occupation**: <label>
* **Runner Occupation** (`runner_occupation`, `text`, required: false): Describe your occupation related to running.

### 11. **Runner Achievements**: <label>
* **Runner Achievements** (`runner_achievements`, `note`, required: false): Share your notable running achievements for our records.

## Tips

* Ensure all details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you face any issues while filling out the form, contact our support team for assistance.
* Review and edit your membership details before submission.
* Our association will use the provided information to tailor services to your needs and interests.
