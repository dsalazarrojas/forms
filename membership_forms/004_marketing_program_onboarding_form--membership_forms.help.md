# marketing_program_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for onboarding participants to a marketing program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Go through each page, filling out text fields with the requested information.
2. Be sure to fill out all required fields as they are marked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency Details** (`agency_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for providing information about the agency or organization associated with you or your team.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for listing the members of your team, or anyone else involved with you in the marketing program.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for providing a brief description of you or your team, including any relevant background or qualifications.

## Tips
- Be concise and accurate when filling out each field.
- Double-check the required fields.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact form@gic.mx.
