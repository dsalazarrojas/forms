# Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This membership application form is designed to collect information and payment details from prospective members for the purpose of enrolling them in a membership program. The form guides you through a series of fields that will help the administration team understand your interest and preferences for membership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by providing your Member Information in the Member Information section (page 1).
2. Select your chosen membership plan from the Membership Plan options (Monthly, Annually, Biannually) in the Membership Plan section (page 2).
3. Enter your payment details in the Payment Details section (page 3).
4. Upload your CV in the Upload CV section (page 4).
5. Review your information and click the Submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Member Information** (`member_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your name and any other identifying information as a prospective member. Please enter your details accurately and truthfully.
- **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to choose your preferred membership plan (Monthly, Annually, Biannually) for the program. Select the plan that suits your budget and requirements.
- **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required/optional): In this field, provide the necessary payment information to process your membership. This includes any relevant payment methods such as check or credit card details.
- **Upload CV** (`upload_c_v`, text, required/optional): This field is used to upload any supporting documents or certifications required for the membership application, such as a CV or resume.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): This button is used to review and submit your application for membership.
