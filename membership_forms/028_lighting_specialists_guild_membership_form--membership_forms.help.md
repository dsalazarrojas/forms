# Lighting Specialists Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information from individuals interested in joining the Lighting Specialists Guild. It will be used to initiate further communication and potential follow-up actions for membership consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) or denoted as "required" in the field description.
2. Ensure the email address you provide is valid and can be used for communication.
3. Enter a clear and concise description of your professional title and experience.
4. Provide a contact number for work or a mobile device that can be used for communication.
5. If you have a work phone number, please include it for work-related communication.
6. Enter your address, including your street address and any additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your membership application.
### Name (Page 1)
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to be recognized in the guild.
### Email (Page 1)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication with the guild.
### Job Title (Page 1)
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter a clear description of your professional title and experience in the lighting industry.
### Organization (Page 1)
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name for guild records.
### Work Experience (Page 1)
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience in the lighting industry.
### Contact Number (Page 1)
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter a contact number for work or a mobile device that can be used for communication.
### Work Phone (Page 1)
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): If you have a work phone number, please include it for work-related communication.
### Address (Page 1)
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your street address and any additional contact information.
### Address
This form is divided into one page, but the fields can be filled out in any order. Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide valid and accurate information to increase your chances of being accepted for membership.
* You can attach any additional documents or information that support your membership application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the guild administration for further assistance.
