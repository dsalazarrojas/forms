# Interior Decorators Society Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Interior Decorators Society Membership Form is designed to collect information from users who wish to join the society. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences as a potential member.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.
2. Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in society materials.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in society materials.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about upcoming events and important updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone or SMS.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your mailing address, line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your mailing address, line 2.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state or province where you reside or work.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the postal code of your location.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required: false): Enter the country where you reside or work.
* **Member Type** (`member_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your membership type:
	+ Home: I am a home-based interior decorator
	+ Professional: I am a professional interior decorator or design firm
	+ Both: I am both a home-based and professional interior decorator
* **How Found Out** (`how_found_out`, `select_one`, required: false): How did you hear about the society?
	+ Social Media: Social media advertisement or post
	+ Online Research: Online search or research
	+ Friend/Colleague: Friend or colleague recommendation
	+ Other: Please specify in the "Other" field below
* **How May We Help You** (`how_may_help`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How would you like us to communicate with you?
	+ Online Workshops: Receive online workshop invites and updates
	+ Social Media: Receive social media updates and messages
	+ Local Chapter: Join local chapter meetings and events
	+ Conferences: Receive conference invites and updates
	+ Other: Please specify in the "Other" field below
* **Bio** (`bio`, `note`, required: false): A brief bio or description of your work and experience.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, `note`, required: false): A brief description of your portfolio or projects.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `note`, required: false): A brief description of your resume or CV.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): If you have specific payment terms, please specify them here.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): If you have already made a payment, please specify the amount.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `text`, required: false): How often would you like to make payments? If applicable.
* **Join Date** (`join_date`, `date`, required: false): The date you would like to join the society.
* **Join Time** (`join_date_time`, `time`, required: false): The time you would like to join the society. 

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any special requests or preferences, please include them in the "Payment Terms" field.
* We will be in touch with you after processing your application to confirm your membership details.
