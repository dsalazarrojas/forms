# Saor Nap 2019 Leeside - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who want to sign up for the 2019 Saor Nap Leeside membership. The gathered information includes basic details, motivation, goals, contact information, and membership options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the basic information section, which includes fields for your name and other personal details.
2. Then, in the Motivation section, please describe why you want to join the 2019 Saor Nap Leeside.
3. In the Goals section, specify what you hope to achieve or accomplish by becoming a member.
4. In the Membership Options section, select the membership option that best suits your needs.
5. Finally, fill out the contact information section with your email, phone number, and address.
6. You can add any additional note if you have any other information you want to provide.
7. Once you have completed all the sections, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Information** (`basic_information`, `text`, required/optional): This is the basic contact information for the applicant. Please fill out your name and other personal details.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe why you want to join the 2019 Saor Nap Leeside.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required/optional): Specify what you hope to achieve or accomplish by becoming a member.
* **Membership Options** (`membership_options`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the membership option that best suits your needs.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required/optional): Fill out your contact information, including your email, phone number, and address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional note if you have any other information you want to provide.
* **First Name** (`basic_information_first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Fill out your first name.
