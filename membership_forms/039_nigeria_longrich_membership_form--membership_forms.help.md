# nigeria_longrich_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nigeria Longrich membership program is a platform that offers exclusive benefits and resources to its members. To join, you will need to complete this form, providing us with your basic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the first page and review the form details.
2. Click on any of the pages to view them in more detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nigeria Longrich Membership Form** (`membership_form`): This is the title of the form that summarizes its purpose.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address associated with your new member account. Make sure to enter it correctly, as this will be your primary contact channel for membership communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Input your phone number associated with your new member account. This will be used for membership-related updates and notifications.
* **Password** (`password`): Create a strong and unique password for your member account. Make sure to store it securely to avoid account access issues.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`): Re-enter your password to confirm it. This is a security measure to ensure password accuracy.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Once you have filled out all the required fields, click this button to submit your membership application.

## Tips
- Be accurate when inputting your email and phone number as these will be your primary contact channels.
- Choose a strong and unique password to secure your account.
- If you encounter any issues, contact our support team for assistance.
