# Fitness Membership Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for new or existing members to pay for and manage their fitness membership. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select Membership Enrollment**: Choose the duration of your membership by selecting 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 12 Months.
2. **Enter Your Information**: Fill in your name, email address, and phone number.
3. **Choose a Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided (Bank Transfer, Check, Online Banking, Credit Card, or PayPal).
4. **Specify Payment Details**: Enter the payment amount and payment date.
5. **Select Membership Plan**: Select the type of membership plan you want to join.
6. **Calculate Total Cost**: The total cost will be automatically calculated based on your selected membership duration and payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Enrollment** (`membership_enrollment`): Select the duration of your membership.
* **Member Name** (`member_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your membership card or ID.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address as registered with the gym.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose your preferred payment method.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Enter the details of your bank transfer.
	+ **Check**: Enter the details of your check (payee name, check number, bank name, and branch).
	+ **Online Banking**: Enter your online banking credentials (bank name, account number, username, and password).
	+ **Credit Card**: Enter your credit card details (card number, expiration date, and security code).
	+ **PayPal**: Log into your PayPal account to complete the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the payment amount.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the payment date.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the payment time.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`): Select the type of membership you want to join (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 12 Months).
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`): This field will automatically display the calculated total cost based on your selected membership duration and payment method.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct membership plan and payment method for your membership payment.
* Double-check your payment details before submitting the form.
* You can ask the gym staff if you need any assistance with completing this form.
