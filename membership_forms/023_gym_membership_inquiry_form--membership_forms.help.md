# Gym Membership Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information from potential gym members, allowing us to understand their needs and provide them with personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective text fields.
3. Select your preferred membership type (Basic, Premium, or Elite) and level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) in the respective select fields.
4. Choose which classes you are interested in by selecting from the list (Yoga, Weightlifting, or Cardio).
5. If applicable, enter the dates you are available for appointment or membership sign-up.
6. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A text field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Inquiry**: A text field to provide any additional information about your membership inquiry.
* **Membership Type**: A select field to choose your preferred membership type (Basic, Premium, or Elite).
* **Membership Level**: A select field to choose your preferred membership level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Interested In Classes**: A select field to choose which classes you are interested in (Yoga, Weightlifting, or Cardio).
* **Available For Appointment**: A date field to enter a date for available appointment.
* **Available For Membership**: A date field to enter a date for available membership sign-up.
* **Note**: A text field for any additional comments or notes.
