# Sales Representative Membership Renewal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Representative Membership Renewal form is designed to allow sales representatives to renew their membership with the organization. This form will collect the necessary information for the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Navigate to the appropriate page within the application.
2.  Review and ensure the form is complete and accurate.
3.  Fill out the **Sales Representative**, **Renewal Request**, **Signature**, and **Sales Representative Membership Renewal Date** fields.
4.  Select the **Membership Type** as required (Member or Non-member).
5.  Enter **Payment Details** and **Payment Method** as necessary.
6.  Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Sales Representative**: Select the sales representative's name from the provided options.
*   **Renewal Request**: Describe the reason for renewing membership.
*   **Signature**: Provide a signature to confirm the request.
*   **Sales Representative Membership Renewal Date**: Enter the date of the membership renewal.
*   **Sales Representative Membership Renewal Expiration Date**: Enter the date of the membership expiration.
*   **Membership Type**: Select the type of membership (Member or Non-member).
*   **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details.
*   **Payment Method**: Enter the payment method used.
