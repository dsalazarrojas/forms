# Executive Officer Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Officer Nomination Form is used to collect information from individuals who would like to be considered for executive officer positions within an organization. This form helps to gather basic contact information about the nominee and the person nominating them, facilitating the nomination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Nomination Form** page (page 1).
2. Enter the nominee's **Nominee Name** and **Nominee Email**.
3. Enter the nominee's **Nominee Phone**.
4. Enter the **Voter Name** and **Voter Email**.
5. Enter the **Voter Phone** (optional).
6. Select the **Nomination Date**.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, optional):
	+ This is the page where you can enter your nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the individual you would like to nominate.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, required):
	+ Enter the email address of the individual you would like to nominate.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the individual you would like to nominate.
* **Voter Name** (`voter_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your name as the person nominating the individual.
* **Voter Email** (`voter_email`, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address as the person nominating the individual.
* **Voter Phone** (`voter_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number as the person nominating the individual.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date you are nominating the individual. This can be a future date if you are nominating someone for a future position. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter the correct information for the nominee and yourself as the person nominating. 
* If you need help or clarification on any of the fields, feel free to reach out to the relevant contacts or authorities. 

Please note that the nomination process may involve additional steps and forms depending on your organization's specific needs.
