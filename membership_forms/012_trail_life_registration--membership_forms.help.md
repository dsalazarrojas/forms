# Trail Life Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Trail Life Registration form is designed to collect information about family members and their interests. This form is used to gather data that will be used to tailor the Trail Life program to the needs of each family. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that you and your family have the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Family Name field with the name of your family.
2. Enter your family's email address in the Family Email field.
3. Add your family's phone number in the Family Phone field.
4. Provide your name in the Name field.
5. Enter your age in the Age field.
6. Enter your zip code in the Zip Code field.
7. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the Interested in Leadership field based on your interest level.
8. Select multiple items from the Family Interests field that apply to your family.
9. Provide any additional notes about your family in the Notes field.
10. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
11. Enter the time of birth in the Time of Birth field.
12. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the Family Type field based on your family type.
13. Select multiple items from the Family Relationship field that apply to your family.
14. Enter the age of your youth family member(s) in the Youth Age field.
15. Select multiple items from the Youth Interests field that apply to your family.
16. Provide any additional notes about your youth family member(s) in the Youth Notes field.
17. Select multiple items from the Youth Leadership field that apply to your family.
18. Select multiple items from the Family Leadership field that apply to your family.
19. Provide any additional notes about your family in the Additional Notes field.
20. Select '12345' or '67890' in the Assigned to field.
21. Enter the date and time you submitted this form in the Date Submitted and Time Submitted fields.
22. Select 'AM' or 'PM' in the Time Submitted AM/PM field.
23. Select who submitted this form in the Submitted By field.
24. Leave the Form ID field blank, as it will be automatically populated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Name**: The name of your family.
* **Family Email**: The email address of your family.
* **Family Phone**: The phone number of your family.
* **Name**: Your name.
* **Age**: Your age.
* **Zip Code**: The zip code of your location.
* **Interested in Leadership**: Do you or your family have leadership experience? (Select 'Yes' or 'No')
* **Family Interests**: Select all interests that apply to your family (Select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Notes**: Any additional notes about your family.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth**: The time of your birth.
* **Family Type**: What type of family are you? (Select 'Yes' or 'No')
* **Family Relationship**: Select all relationships that apply to your family (Select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Youth Age**: The age of your family's youth member(s).
* **Youth Interests**: Select all interests that apply to your family's youth member(s) (Select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Youth Notes**: Any additional notes about your family's youth member(s).
* **Youth Leadership**: Do your family's youth member(s) have leadership experience? (Select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Family Leadership**: Do you have leadership experience in your family? (Select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional notes about your family.
* **Assigned to**: The Trail Life program ID that you are assigned to.
* **Date Submitted**: The date and time you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted**: The time you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted AM/PM**: The AM/PM of the time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted By**: Who submitted this form.
* **Form ID**: This field will be automatically populated and should be left blank.
