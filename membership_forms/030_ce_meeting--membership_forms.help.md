# CE Meeting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CE Meeting form is designed for users to register for Continuing Education meetings, providing essential information for attendees and meeting organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in.
3. Review the form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Required, text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Required, text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Required, email): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Required, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization or Company** (`organization`, Optional, text): If you are affiliated with an organization or company, enter it here.
* **Preferred Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, Required, date): Choose a date for the meeting from the calendar.
* **Preferred Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, Required, time): Choose a time for the meeting from the provided options.
* **Expected Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, Required, number): Enter the expected duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Type of Meeting** (`meeting_type`, Required, select_one): Select the type of meeting you are registering for (e.g., Continuing Education, Training Session, etc.).
* **Meeting Topic or Title** (`meeting_topic`, Required, text): Enter the title or topic of the meeting.
* **Topic Description** (`topic_description`, Required, text): Describe the topic of the meeting in brief.
* **CE Credits Available** (`ce_credits`, Optional, number): If applicable, enter the number of CE credits available.
* **Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`, Required, select_one): Select the format of the meeting (e.g., In-Person, Virtual - Zoom, etc.).
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, Optional, text): Enter the location of the meeting, if applicable.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendee_status`, Required, select_one): Choose your attendance status (e.g., Registered, Confirmed, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, Optional, text): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`, Optional, text): If you require any special accommodations, enter them here.
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (`additional_notes`, Optional, text): Enter any additional notes or questions you have.
