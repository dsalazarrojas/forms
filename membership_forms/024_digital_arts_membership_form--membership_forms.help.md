# Digital Arts Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect membership information from individuals and organizations interested in joining the Digital Arts community. It gathers contact details, member type, and other relevant information to facilitate the membership application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have entered the correct information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details**: This field is intended for a brief description of your application.
* **Member Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be known within the Digital Arts community.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Member Type**: Select the category that best describes your affiliation with Digital Arts (Artist/Artisan, Creative Community, Arts Organization, or Other).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number or other contact information.
* **Address**: Enter your address or the address of your organization (if applicable).
* **Member Since**: If you have been a member before, enter the date you joined.
* **How did you know us**: Select all methods by which you know about Digital Arts (Social Media, Event/Festival, Artist/Community, Word of mouth, or Other).
* **How did you hear**: Select how you heard about Digital Arts (Social Media, Friend, Family, Online Search, or Other).
* **Why Member**: Describe in a few sentences why you would like to be a member of Digital Arts.
* **Contact Person**: If you are representing an organization, enter the contact person's name.
* **Organization**: Indicate if you are representing an organization (Yes or No).
* **Membership Type**: Select the type of membership you are applying for (Regular, Associate, Student, or Lifetime).

## Tips

- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact us directly.
- We will review your application once you have submitted the form.
- You will receive further instructions via email regarding the status of your application.
