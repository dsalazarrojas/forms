# Member Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process member reimbursement requests for membership fees, meeting expenses, and other related expenses. The form collects information about the member, the expense, and the payment method used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your member's information on the first page.
2. Select the expense date from the calendar.
3. Choose the type of expense (e.g., membership fee, meeting expenses, or other).
4. Enter the expense amount.
5. Select the payment method (e.g., cheque, wire transfer, or other).
6. Provide any additional notes about the reimbursement.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Information** (`member_info`, text, required): Enter your member's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, required): Select the date the expense occurred using the calendar.
* **Expense Type** (`prophets_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of expense (e.g., membership fee, meeting expenses, or other).
	+ Select "Membership Fee" for membership fee reimbursements.
	+ Select "Meeting Expenses" for meeting-related expenses.
	+ Select "Other" for any other type of expense.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, required): Enter the exact amount of the expense.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how the member was reimbursed (e.g., cheque, wire transfer, or other).
	+ Select "Cheque" for cheque payments.
	+ Select "Wire Transfer" for wire transfer payments.
	+ Select "Online Payment" for online payments.
	+ Select "Other" for any other payment method.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Provide any additional information about the reimbursement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the membership team for assistance.
* This form is used to process reimbursement requests, so ensure you're submitting correct and complete information.
