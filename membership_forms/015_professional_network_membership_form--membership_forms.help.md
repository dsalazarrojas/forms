# Professional Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a comprehensive membership profile for individuals and companies in the professional network.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as such in the form.
2. Use the correct formatting for date and time fields (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM AM/PM, respectively).
3. Enter your contact information in the format of [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
4. For fields like skills, interests, and background, be as detailed as possible.
5. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Community Manager Name**: Enter the name of the community manager who will be managing this membership profile.
* **Community Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the community manager who will be managing this membership profile.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your professional name that will be displayed on the platform.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company, if applicable.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Company Website**: Enter the URL of your company website, if applicable.
* **Why Join**: Briefly describe why you're joining the professional network.
* **Join Date**: Enter the date you joined the network (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Join Time**: Enter the time you joined the network (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Manager Title**: Enter your professional title or position.
* **Manager Organization**: Enter the name of your organization, if applicable.
* **Member Since**: Enter the length of time you've been a member (e.g., 1 month, 2 years).
* **Membership Type**: Select the type of membership (Individual, Company, Both).
* **Manager Headline**: Enter a brief headline that summarizes your professional experience.
* **Manager Bio**: Enter a brief bio that describes your background and experience.
* **Manager Image**: Upload a profile picture or logo, if applicable.
* **Manager Background**: Enter a brief description of your background and experience.
* **Manager Education**: Enter your educational background or qualifications.
* **Manager Interest**: Enter your professional interests or areas of expertise.
* **Manager Experience**: Enter a brief description of your professional experience.
* **Manager Location**: Enter your work location or office location.
* **Manager Skills**: Enter your professional skills or areas of expertise.
* **Member Image**: Upload a profile picture or logo, if applicable.
* **Manager Languages**: Enter your languages spoken or proficiency levels.
* **Manager Social Media**: Enter your social media profiles or handles, if applicable.
