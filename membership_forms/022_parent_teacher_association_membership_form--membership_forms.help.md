# Parent Teacher Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents and teachers for membership purposes, including demographic information, contact details, and payment information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
* Fill in the parent's and teacher's name
* Enter the parent's and teacher's email addresses
* Select the membership type (yes or no)
* Select the payment method (yes or no)
* Enter the payment date and time
* Enter the payment amount
* Add any comments or notes
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (`parent_information`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's information, such as their name and contact details.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required: false): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required: false): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Parent Number** (`parent_number`, number, required: false): Enter the parent's contact number (e.g., phone number).
* **Teacher Number** (`teacher_number`, number, required: false): Enter the teacher's contact number (e.g., phone number).
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the parent/teacher is a member of the association.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether payment method is accepted.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, required: false): Enter the payment time.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the payment amount.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the membership or payment.
## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your association administrator for clarification.
