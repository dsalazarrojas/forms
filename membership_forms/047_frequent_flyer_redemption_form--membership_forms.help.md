# Frequent Flyer Redemption Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Frequent Flyer Redemption Form is designed to gather information from Frequent Flyer program members for the purpose of processing their redemption. Completing this form will help us understand your flight history and preferences, ensuring accurate and efficient redemption processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your airline of choice from the "Frequent Flyer Program" dropdown menu.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Flight Type" and "Flight Frequency" questions.
3. Enter your passenger's name and date of birth.
4. Enter your email address and phone number.
5. Provide details about your recent flight, including date, time, flight from, flight to, and flight class.
6. Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your flight status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Frequent Flyer Program**: Select the airline you are associated with (e.g. Air Canada, Air France, American Airlines, etc.)
* **Passenger Name**: Enter the name of the passenger who is associated with this Frequent Flyer account.
* **Passenger Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the passenger who is associated with this Frequent Flyer account.
* **Email**: Enter your email address associated with this Frequent Flyer account.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number associated with this Frequent Flyer account.
* **Flight Type**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if this flight is a redemption.
* **Flight Details**: Provide a brief description of your flight, including any relevant details such as flight number or seat number.
* **Flight Date**: Enter the date of your recent flight.
* **Flight Time**: Enter the time of your recent flight.
* **Flight From**: Enter the departure airport of your recent flight.
* **Flight To**: Enter the arrival airport of your recent flight.
* **Flight Class**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your flight was booked in a higher class (e.g. business or first class).
* **Flight Frequency**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your flight was a redeemable flight.
* **Flight Mileage**: Enter the total mileage earned on your recent flight.
* **Flight Status**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your current flight status.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each question carefully and accurately, as this information will be used to process your redemption.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
