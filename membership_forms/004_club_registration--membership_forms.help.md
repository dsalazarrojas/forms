# Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in joining the club. Please fill out this form to register as a new member and provide necessary details for club administration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your complete legal name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your primary email address for club communications.
3. Provide your contact phone number.
4. Enter your age.
5. Select your gender identity from the provided options.
6. Enter your profession or student status.
7. Choose the club chapter you would like to join.
8. Select your preferred membership level.
9. If applicable, enter the name of the member who referred you.
10. Indicate if you were a previous member.
11. State your goals for joining the club.
12. Choose your preferred meeting times.
13. Select if you are interested in volunteering.
14. List any skills you can contribute to the club.
15. Choose your payment method for membership fees.
16. If necessary, provide any accessibility accommodations.
17. Agree to follow the club's code of conduct.
18. Sign the liability waiver.
19. Authorize the use of your photo in club materials.
20. Add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your complete legal name to be registered as a club member.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): This is the primary contact email for club communications.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for club use.
* **Age** (4, number, required): Enter your current age.
* **Gender** (5, select_one, required): Choose how you identify your gender.
* **Occupation** (6, text, optional): Enter your current profession or student status.
* **Club Chapter** (7, select_one, required): Choose the club chapter you would like to join.
* **Membership Tier** (8, select_one, required): Select your desired membership level.
* **Referred By** (9, text, optional): If another member referred you, enter their name.
* **Were You a Member Before** (10, select_one, required): Indicate if you have been a member before.
* **What Do You Hope to Gain** (11, text, optional): State your goals for joining the club.
* **Preferred Meeting Times** (12, select_multiple, optional): Choose when you are available to attend meetings.
* **Interested in Volunteering** (13, select_one, optional): Select if you are interested in volunteering.
* **Skills You Can Contribute** (14, text, optional): List any skills you can offer to the club.
* **Payment Option** (15, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay membership fees.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (16, text, optional): If necessary, enter any accessibility needs.
* **I Agree to Follow Club Code of Conduct** (17, select_one, required): Confirm agreement to respect all members and rules.
* **I Sign the Liability Waiver** (18, select_one, required): Understand and agree to the liability terms.
* **Photo Release Authorization** (19, select_one, optional): Select if you authorize the use of your photo in club materials.
* **Additional Comments** (20, text, optional): Add any additional information or comments.
