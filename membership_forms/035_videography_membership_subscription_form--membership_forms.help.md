# Videography Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to subscribe to a videography service, providing an easy and streamlined way to capture their contact and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address correctly.
3. Select your preferred Payment Method.
4. If you've chosen 'Other' as your Payment Method, enter the method in the 'Other (Specify)' field.
5. Choose your preferred Plan.
6. If you've chosen 'Other' as your Plan, enter the plan details in the 'Plan Other' field.
7. Select your preferred Billing Cycle.
8. If you've chosen 'Other' as your Billing Cycle, enter the cycle details in the 'Billing Cycle Other' field.
9. Enter the Payment Amount.
10. Select your preferred Payment Frequency.
11. If you've chosen 'Other' as your Payment Frequency, enter the frequency details in the 'Payment Frequency Other' field.
12. Choose your preferred Payment Terms.
13. If you've chosen 'Other' as your Payment Terms, enter the terms details in the 'Payment Terms Other' field.
14. Enter your Subscription Start Date and Subscription End Date.
15. Fill in your Address details, including Zip, City, State, and Country.
16. Enter your Phone number.
17. If required, enter your Fax number.
18. If required, enter your Business Name and Business URL.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose a payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Other (Specify)** (Text, Optional): If you selected 'Other' as your Payment Method, enter the method in this field.
* **Plan** (Select One, Required): Choose a subscription plan that suits your needs.
* **Plan Other** (Text, Optional): If you've chosen 'Other' as your Plan, enter the plan details in this field.
* **Billing Cycle** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred billing cycle.
* **Billing Cycle Other** (Text, Optional): If you've chosen 'Other' as your Billing Cycle, enter the cycle details in this field.
* **Payment Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the amount you wish to pay.
* **Payment Frequency** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred payment frequency.
* **Payment Frequency Other** (Text, Optional): If you've chosen 'Other' as your Payment Frequency, enter the frequency details in this field.
* **Payment Terms** (Select One, Required): Select your preferred payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Other** (Text, Optional): If you've chosen 'Other' as your Payment Terms, enter the terms details in this field.
* **Subscription Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you wish to start your subscription.
* **Subscription End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date your subscription will end.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address for billing or contact purposes.
* **Zip** (Text, Optional): Enter your zip code for your address.
* **City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city where your business or residence is located.
* **State** (Text, Optional): Enter the state where your business or residence is located.
* **Country** (Text, Optional): Enter the country where your business or residence is located.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Fax** (Text, Optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Business Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your business name if applicable.
* **Business URL** (Text, Optional): Enter your business URL if applicable.
