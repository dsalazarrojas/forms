# Annual Membership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather valuable feedback from our members to help us improve our membership services. Your input will enable us to understand your experiences and preferences, allowing us to make informed decisions to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field as instructed, providing accurate and honest feedback.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as requested.
3. Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`member_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email Address** (`member_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for follow-up communication.
* **Member ID Number** (`member_id`, text, optional): If you have a member ID number, please enter it here.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of membership you hold with us (Standard, Premium, VIP, Corporate, Family, Trial).
* **How long have you been a member?** (`membership_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your membership (Less than 6 months, 6 months - 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, More than 5 years).
* **Primary use of membership** (`primary_use`, text, optional): Describe the main reason you joined our membership.
* **How often do you use the membership?** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how frequently you utilize our services (Daily, Several times per week, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Features you use most** (`features_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the features you frequently use.
* **Satisfaction with available features** (`feature_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with our features (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **Membership provides good value** (`value_received`, select_one, required): Choose whether you believe our membership provides good value (Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree).
* **Price is fair for the benefits** (`price_fairness`, select_one, required): Rate your perception of the price for our membership (Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree).
* **Highest price you would pay** (`cost_would_pay`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the highest price you would pay for our membership.
* **Quality of customer support** (`customer_support_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of our customer support (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Very poor).
* **Technical experience with platform** (`technical_experience`, select_one, required): Describe how easy or difficult it is for you to use our platform (Very easy to use, Easy to use, Neutral, Difficult to use, Very difficult to use).
* **Features you wish were included** (`missing_features`, text, optional): If there are features you wish we had, please list them.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`improvements_suggested`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for us to better serve you.
* **Interest in upgrading membership level** (`upgrade_interest`, select_one, required): Express your interest in upgrading your membership level (Very interested, Interested, Possibly, Not interested, Not applicable).
* **Will you renew your membership?** (`renewal_intention`, select_one, required): Choose your intention to renew your membership (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **If considering cancellation - reason why** (`cancellation_reason`, text, optional): If you are considering cancellation, please explain why.
* **Would you recommend to friends or colleagues?** (`recommend_friends`, select_one, required): Rate your likelihood of recommending our membership to others (Definitely, Probably, Unsure, Probably not, Definitely not).
