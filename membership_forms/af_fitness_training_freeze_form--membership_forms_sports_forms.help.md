# AF Fitness Training Freeze Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AF Fitness Training Freeze Form is a necessary document for members to fill out when they need to pause their fitness program due to various reasons. This form helps the fitness center management team to understand the member's needs and make necessary adjustments to their membership upon their return.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the member's personal information:
	* Full name: Enter your legal name as it appears on your membership card.
	* Member ID: Enter your unique membership identification number.
	* Email address: Enter your email address associated with your membership.
	* Phone number: Enter your contact phone number.
2. Provide details about the freeze:
	* Start date of freeze: Specify the date when you would like the freeze to begin.
	* Expected end date of freeze: Enter the date when you expect to return to your fitness program.
	* Duration of freeze: This will be calculated based on the selected dates.
	* Reason for freeze: Select the primary reason for requesting the freeze (e.g. Medical reasons, Vacation/travel, Work commitment, Financial hardship, Relocation, or Other).
	* If you selected "Other," please describe the reason in the Specify other reason field.
3. Health and membership information:
	* Current membership type: Select your current membership type (e.g. Basic, Premium, VIP, Student, or Senior).
	* Do you need medical clearance to resume training? (Optional)
	* Any health restrictions we should be aware of? (Optional)
4. Confirm and finalize:
	* I acknowledge the freeze policy and terms: Confirm that you understand and agree to the freeze policy and terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your membership card.
* **Member ID**: Enter your unique membership identification number.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address associated with your membership.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Start date of freeze**: Specify the date when you would like the freeze to begin.
* **Expected end date of freeze**: Enter the date when you expect to return to your fitness program.
* **Duration of freeze**: This will be calculated based on the selected dates.
* **Reason for freeze**: Select the primary reason for requesting the freeze (e.g. Medical reasons, Vacation/travel, Work commitment, Financial hardship, Relocation, or Other).
* **Specify other reason**: If you selected "Other," enter a description of the reason for the freeze.
* **Current membership type**: Select your current membership type (e.g. Basic, Premium, VIP, Student, or Senior).
* **Do you need medical clearance to resume training?**: If you have a medical condition, indicate if you need medical clearance to resume training.
* **Any health restrictions we should be aware of?**: If you have any health restrictions or concerns, describe them here.
* **Current assigned trainer**: If you have a trainer assigned to you, enter their name.
* **Special requests for when you return**: If you have any specific requests or changes you'd like to make to your program upon return, enter them here.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **I acknowledge the freeze policy and terms**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the freeze policy and terms.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Enter any other information you'd like to share.
