This form is designed to collect information from prospective members of a support group for women. The form will help the group administrators to get a clear understanding of the member's needs and circumstances, and to provide relevant support and resources.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Member Information (page 1)**: This section is used to collect basic contact information about the member. Please fill in your name, contact method (mobile, phone, or email), and any other relevant contact information.

2. **Address (page 1)**: This section is used to collect the member's address. Please fill in your complete address.

3. **Emergency Contact (page 2)**: If you would like to provide an emergency contact, please fill in their name, relationship, and contact information. This will help the group administrators to know who to reach out to in case of an emergency.

4. **Emergency Information (page 2)**: If you have any medical conditions or special needs, please describe them here. This will help the group administrators to be aware of any potential concerns.

5. **Medical Info (page 3)**: Please select whether you have any medical conditions that require special attention.

6. **Medical Condition (page 3)**: If you have a medical condition, please describe it briefly.

7. **Medical Notes (page 3)**: If you have any additional medical information, please provide it here.

8. **Support Group Info (page 4)**: Please provide any information about your experience with the support group, such as how you found out about it or what brought you to this group.

9. **Member Since (page 4)**: Check if you are already a member of the support group, and if yes, which month and year you started.

10. **Member Since Date (page 4)**: If you have a specific start date for your membership, please enter it here (MM/DD/YYYY).

11. **Membership Status (page 5)**: Select your current membership status in the support group (pending, active, expired, or cancelled).

12. **Additional Info (page 5)**: Any other information that you would like to share with the group administrators can be added here.

**Tips**

* Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your membership application.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to the group administrators.
* Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form, and we look forward to welcoming you to the support group!
