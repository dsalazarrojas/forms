# Membership Dues Renewal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Dues Renewal form is used to update the status of existing members to ensure they are current with their membership. This form should only be used for members who wish to renew their membership and have not already done so.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Renew" to confirm your membership renewal.
2. Enter your Membership ID.
3. Choose a renewal date for your membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Renew** (`membership_dues_renewal_form`, select_one, required): 
This field is a confirmation field to ensure that you are intentionally renewing your membership. Please select "Yes" to continue.
* **Membership ID** (`membership_dues_renewal_membership_id`, number, required): 
This is the unique identifier for your membership. Please enter your membership number as it appears on your membership card or in our database.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required): 
Choose a date to renew your membership on. Please note that the system will automatically update your membership status once the form is submitted.
