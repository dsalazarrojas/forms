# Community Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Contact Form is a tool for community members to reach out to the organization with inquiries, concerns, or requests. Fill out this form to get help, provide feedback, or ask questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your contact information (Contact name field).
2. Provide your email address where you'd like to be contacted (Contact email field). This is where our response will be sent.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the Contact phone field. This is optional and only for follow-up discussions.
4. If you'd like to provide an affiliation or organization name, enter it in the Organization field. This is optional.
5. In the Reason for contact field, briefly describe the purpose or topic of your inquiry.
6. In the Preferred response method field, choose how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In person).
7. Finally, select the Urgency level that best describes the speed at which you need a response (Low, Medium, or High).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact name**: Enter your name as it appears on your contact information.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true
- **Contact email**: Enter your email address where you'd like to be contacted.
  • Type: email
  • Required: true
- **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up discussions (optional).
  • Type: text
  • Required: false
- **Organization**: Enter your organization or affiliation (optional).
  • Type: text
  • Required: false
- **Reason for contact**: Briefly describe the purpose or topic of your inquiry.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true
- **Preferred response method**: Choose how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In person).
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: false
- **Urgency level**: Select the speed at which you need a response (Low, Medium, or High).
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: false
