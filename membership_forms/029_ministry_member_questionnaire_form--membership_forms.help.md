# Ministry Member Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from ministry members about their contact details, communication preferences, and experiences. This will help the ministry team understand their members better and tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Choose the options that best describe your experiences and preferences.
3. If you have any additional contact information that is not listed, please provide it in the "Additional Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact_information): This is the primary contact information that we will use to reach out to you.
* **Have you ever attended a church service?** (communication_skills): Check if you have ever attended a church service.
* **What instruments do you play?** (music_skills): Select all the instruments you play or have experience playing.
* **Have you ever worked in a church setting?** (hospitality_skills): Check if you have ever worked in a church setting.
* **How do you prefer to be contacted?** (contact_method): Choose your preferred method of contact.
* **Music formation?** (music_formation): If you have any music training or experience, provide the date of your last music formation.
* **Church service?** (church_service): If you have served in a church, provide the date and time of your last church service.
* **Additional Contact Information** (contact_information2): If you have any additional contact information that is not listed, please provide it here.
* **Contact Information 3** (contact_information3): This is another field for additional contact information.
* **Contact Information 4** (contact_information4): This field is for additional contact information, and you can enter a number to provide more details.
* **Contact Information 5** (contact_information5): This is another field for additional contact information, and you can enter a number for more details.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
