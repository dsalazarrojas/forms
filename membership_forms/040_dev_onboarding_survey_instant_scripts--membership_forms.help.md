# dev_onboarding_survey_instant_scripts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the onboarding progress of developers onboarding into a new system or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the script language you are using for the project.
2. Choose the type of script you are working on (Backend or Frontend).
3. Indicate your current onboarding status (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
4. Enter the date and time you started onboarding, if applicable.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to share about your onboarding progress.
6. If necessary, provide your contact information (email and phone number).
7. Enter any additional information you would like to note about your onboarding progress.
8. Enter the completion date and time, if applicable.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes you would have about your onboarding completion.
10. If you have any completion status notes, enter them here.
11. Enter any completion comments or notes.
12. Enter any additional completion information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Script Language** (JavaScript, Python, Ruby): Select the language you are using for the script.
* **Script Type** (Backend, Frontend): Indicate the type of script you are working on (Backend or Frontend).
* **Onboarding Status** (Completed, In Progress, Not Started): Choose your current onboarding status.
* **Onboarding Date** (date): Enter the date you started onboarding, if applicable.
* **Onboarding Time** (time): Enter the time you started onboarding, if applicable.
* **Onboarding Note** (text): Enter any additional notes or comments about your onboarding progress.
* **Onboarding Email** (email): If necessary, enter your contact email address.
* **Onboarding Phone** (text): If necessary, enter your contact phone number.
* **Onboarding Status Note** (text): Enter any additional notes about your onboarding status.
* **Onboarding Comment** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes about your onboarding progress.
* **Onboarding Reference** (text): Enter any additional information about your onboarding progress.
* **Onboarding Next Steps** (text): Enter any additional information about your next steps.
* **Onboarding Additional Info** (text): Enter any additional information about your onboarding progress.
* **Onboarding Completion Date** (date): Enter the date you completed onboarding, if applicable.
* **Onboarding Completion Time** (time): Enter the time you completed onboarding, if applicable.
* **Onboarding Completion Note** (note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your onboarding completion.
* **Onboarding Completion Email** (email): If necessary, enter your contact email address for completion.
* **Onboarding Completion Phone** (text): If necessary, enter your contact phone number for completion.
* **Onboarding Completion Status** (Completed, In Progress, Not Started): Choose your completion status.
* **Onboarding Completion Status Note** (text): Enter any additional notes about your completion status.
* **Onboarding Completion Comment** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes about your onboarding completion.
* **Onboarding Completion Reference** (text): Enter any additional information about your onboarding completion.
* **Onboarding Additional Completion Info** (text): Enter any additional information about your onboarding completion progress.
* **Onboarding Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes about your onboarding progress.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Enter as much detail as possible about your onboarding progress.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your project manager or support team.
