# Fitness Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Needs Assessment Form is designed to gather information about a submitter's fitness needs and medical history to create an accurate fitness plan tailored to their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the Submitter Information section with your name, email, and phone number. This is the only required field in this section.
- In the Personal Info section, provide a brief description of your current fitness level, goals, and any physical characteristics that may affect your workout (e.g., height, weight, body fat percentage).
- For the Current Fitness Level, select from the provided options (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
- If you have any medical conditions, please list them in the Physical Characteristics field.
- In the Physical Health section, provide any relevant information about your health, such as injuries or medical conditions.
- If applicable, select any relevant medical conditions or allergies you may have in the Medical Conditions, Allergies, and Injury Status sections.
- Finally, confirm that the form is submitted by checking the Submit By boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Information (1)** (`Submitter Information`, `text`, required): Please fill out your name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Personal Info (2)** (`Personal Info`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of yourself, including your current fitness level (beginner, intermediate, advanced), and any relevant physical characteristics.
* **Current Fitness Level (4)** (`Current Fitness Level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of your current fitness level: beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Physical Characteristics (5)** (`Physical Characteristics`, `text`, required): List any physical characteristics that may affect your workout, such as height, weight, or body fat percentage.
* **Height (6)** (`height`, `number`, required): Please enter your height in inches.
* **Weight (7)** (`Weight`, `number`, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Body Fat Percentage (8)** (`bodyfat`, `number`, required): Measure your body fat percentage.
* **Contact Info (9)** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required): Provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Email (10)** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (11)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Physical Health (12)** (`Physical Health`, `text`, required): Describe your current physical health status.
* **Health History (13)** (`health`, `text`, required): Describe any relevant health conditions you may have.
* **Injury Status (14)** (`Injury Status`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you have any current injuries, true or false.
* **Medical Conditions (15)** (`Medical Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Check any medical conditions you may have, true or false.
* **Medications (16)** (`Medications`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you take any medications, yes or no.
* **Allergies (17)** (`Allergies`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you have any allergies, yes or no.
* **Submitted By (18)** (`Submitted By`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that the form was submitted by you, yes or no.
* **Submitted By Role (19)** (`Submitted By Role`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your role when submitting the form, yes or no.
* **Submitted By Department (20)** (`Submitted By Department`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm the department that submitted the form, yes or no.
* **Submitted Date (21)** (`Submitted Date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitter Signature (22)** (`Submitter Signature`, `note`, required): Please provide a note with your signature.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled out to complete the form successfully. If you have any questions or need further guidance, please don't hesitate to reach out.
