# Fitness Club Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect membership information from users interested in becoming a member of a fitness club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact details section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the fitness goals section, describe your fitness goals and objectives.
3. In the payment preferences section, select your preferred payment method and frequency.
4. Choose your membership type (Full Membership, Limited Membership, or Trial Membership).
5. Select your current membership status (Active or Inactive).
6. If you are an existing member, enter the date you started your membership (Member Since).
7. If you are a new member, enter the date your membership will end (Member Until).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number in the contact details section. This information will be used for communication and verification purposes.

### Fitness Goals
* **Fitness Goals** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your fitness goals and objectives in this section. This will help us understand your motivations for joining the fitness club.

### Payment Preferences
* **Payment Preferences** (`<name>`, `text`, required): In this section, select your preferred payment method (e.g. credit card, bank transfer, etc.) and frequency (e.g. monthly, yearly, etc.).

### Membership Type
* **Membership Type** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your membership type (Full Membership, Limited Membership, or Trial Membership) to proceed with your application.

### Membership Status
* **Membership Status** (`<name>`, `select_one`, optional): Select your current membership status (Active or Inactive). If you are not an existing member, select "Inactive".

### Member Since
* **Member Since** (`<name>`, `date`, optional): If you are an existing member, enter the date you started your membership.

### Member Until
* **Member Until** (`<name>`, `date`, optional): If you are a new member, enter the date your membership will end.
