# Church Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from individuals who wish to become members of the church. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your membership application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name, date of birth, and email address on page 1.
2. On page 2, select your membership type from the options provided (Regular, Family, Associate, or Youth).
3. If you have an emergency contact person, fill in their name and phone number on page 3.
4. On page 4, choose any areas of interest for volunteering that apply to you.
5. Finally, read and agree to the membership consent statement on page 5, which outlines the church's policies and procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your first and last names as they appear on official documents.
* **Date of birth**: Use YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1995-02-12).
* **Email address**: Provide a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code if you are outside the country.
* **Mailing address**: Fill in your street address, city, and postal code.
* **Membership type**: Choose the type of membership that best describes your relationship with the church.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Areas of interest for volunteering**: Select any areas of interest that apply to you, such as Worship, Outreach, or Youth ministry.
* **Membership consent and acknowledgement**: Carefully read and agree to the church's policies and procedures outlined in this statement.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address and phone number are up-to-date and correct.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the church office for assistance.
