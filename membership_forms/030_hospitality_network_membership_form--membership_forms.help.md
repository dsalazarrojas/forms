# hospitality_network_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospitality_network_membership_form is designed for individuals to submit their information to join or interact with a hospitality network. This form helps the network administrators collect necessary information about its members and their preferences.

## How to Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. On the Form Info page, enter your name and any other relevant details as requested.
2. On the Preferences page, indicate your interests or preferences within the hospitality network.
3. On the Payment page, if applicable, enter your payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Info
* **Form Info** (`name`, `text`, **required**: no): Enter your name and any other relevant details as requested.
### Preferences
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, `text`, **required**: no): Indicate your interests or preferences within the hospitality network.
### Payment
* **Payment** (`payment`, `text`, **required**: no): If applicable, enter your payment details.
