# Membership Waiver And Liability Release - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from membership applicants or existing members to sign a waiver and liability release agreement. This ensures that all signers are aware of the potential risks involved and acknowledges their consent to participate in activities provided by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide the contact information for an emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
3. Date and sign the "Consent" field to confirm your understanding and agreement to the waiver and liability release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **membership_waiver_and_liability_release** (`membership_waiver_and_liability_release`, text, optional): A text field where you can provide any additional information or comments related to the waiver and liability release. This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information for an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an accident or injury.
* **consent** (`consent`, date, required): Date and sign your name in this field to confirm your understanding and agreement to the waiver and liability release. This is a required field and must be completed before submitting the form.
