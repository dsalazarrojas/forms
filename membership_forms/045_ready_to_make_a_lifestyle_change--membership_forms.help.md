# ready_to_make_a_lifestyle_change - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help users assess their readiness to make a lifestyle change. It collects information on various factors that influence a person's decision to make a change, such as health goals, exercise frequency, available time, motivation, goal duration, current level, goal priority, available money, and other available money sources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and make sure you understand the questions before proceeding.
2. For each page, select the answers that best fit your situation.
3. Be honest when answering the questions. The information collected will be used to help you assess your readiness to make a lifestyle change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction (text)** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and explain why you are interested in making a lifestyle change.

### Health Goals (Page 2)
* **Health goals (select_multiple)** (`health_goals`, required: false): This question asks about your goals related to health. Select all the goals that are important to you, such as "Goal 1", "Goal 2", or "Goal 3".

### Exercise Frequency (Page 3)
* **Exercise frequency (number)** (`exercise_frequency`, required: false): This field asks about your current exercise frequency. Please enter the number of times you exercise per week.

### Available Time (Page 4)
* **Available time (time)** (`available_time`, required: false): This field asks about your available time for exercise and other lifestyle activities. Please select a time that best fits your schedule.

### Motivation (Page 5)
* **Motivation (text)** (`motivation`, required: false): This is a text field where you can explain what motivates you to make a lifestyle change.

### Goal Duration (Page 6)
* **Goal duration (number)** (`goal_duration`, required: false): This field asks about the duration of your goal. Please enter the number of months or years you plan to achieve your goal.

### Current Level (Page 7)
* **Current level (select_one)** (`current_level`, required: false): This question asks about your current level of fitness or skills related to your goal. Select "Beginner", "Intermediate", or "Advanced".

### Goal Priority (Page 8)
* **Goal priority (select_multiple)** (`goal_priority`, required: false): This field asks about the priority of your goal. Select all that apply, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".

### Available Money (Page 9)
* **Available money (number)** (`available_money`, required: false): This field asks about your available money for exercise and lifestyle activities. Please enter the amount you have available.

### Available Money Other (Page 10)
* **Available money other (text)** (`available_money_other`, required: false): This is a text field where you can explain other sources of money available for exercise and lifestyle activities, such as sponsorships, grants, or other financial resources.

### Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, please contact your [support contact information].
