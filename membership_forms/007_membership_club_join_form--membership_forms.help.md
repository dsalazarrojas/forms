# membership_club_join_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to join a membership club. Users will be required to fill in their details to process their membership application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your Email in the **Email** field.
4. Fill in your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the **Payment Method**. You can choose between **Card** and **Bank Transfer**.
6. Choose your desired **Membership Plan**. You can select **Monthly**, **Annual**, or **One Time Fee**.
7. If you want to make a payment, fill in the **Payment Amount** field.
8. You can add any additional notes in the **Notes** field.
9. If you want to set a **Membership Start Date**, fill in the date.
10. If you want to set an **Membership End Date**, fill in the date.
11. You can specify the **Membership Duration** in terms of time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to make your payment, either by **Card** or **Bank Transfer**.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you want to pay.
* **Membership Plan**: Select which plan you want to join, either **Monthly**, **Annual**, or **One Time Fee**.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your application.
* **Membership Start Date**: Choose the start date for your membership.
* **Membership End Date**: Choose the end date for your membership.
* **Membership Duration**: Specify the duration of your membership in terms of time.
