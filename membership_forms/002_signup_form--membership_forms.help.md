# Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The signup form is designed to collect information for new members of a club or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Membership Level
2. Select the appropriate options for each of the other fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Format your date of birth in the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available membership options.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple membership status options.
* **Terms & Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or acknowledge the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Enter your confirmation to submit the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the membership administrator.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
