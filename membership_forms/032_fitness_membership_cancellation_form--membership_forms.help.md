# Fitness Membership Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by fitness center staff to process membership cancellations. Members can be cancelled for various reasons, which are tracked and documented through this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* **Membership Number**: Enter the member's membership number for accurate tracking and identification.
	* **Cancellation Reason**: Select the reason for the cancellation from the provided options or type "Other (please specify)" if the reason is not listed.
	* **Date of Cancellation**: Enter the date the membership was cancelled.
2. If applicable, provide any additional details or comments for the gym staff to reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Number**: This field is used to identify the member's membership account. Please enter the membership number as provided by the gym upon enrollment.
* **Cancellation Reason**: Select the most accurate reason for the cancellation from the provided options or type in a custom reason if not listed. This helps the gym staff understand the situation and take further action if necessary.
* **Date of Cancellation**: Enter the date the membership was officially cancelled, ensuring accurate record-keeping and communication with the member.
* **Cancellation Date Confirmation**: This field is used to confirm the cancellation date. Please enter the date to finalize the cancellation process.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about the cancellation reason, it's best to select "Other (please specify)" and provide a brief explanation in the comments section.
* Double-check the membership number for accuracy to avoid errors.
* Ensure the cancellation date is entered correctly to avoid any confusion with the member or gym staff.
