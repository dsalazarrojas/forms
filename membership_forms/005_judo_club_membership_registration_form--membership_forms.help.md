# Judo Club Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect membership information and waiver details from potential new members of the Judo Club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Fill in the form fields as prompted.
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
4. Sign the waiver form to acknowledge and accept the club rules and privacy policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Club Rules** (`club_rules`, text, optional): This field is intended for club rules and policies that new members should be aware of. It's a read-only field, but you can review and print the rules for future reference.
- **Waiver** (`waiver`, text, optional): This field is a legal waiver that you'll need to sign to acknowledge and agree to the club's rules and policies. Please make sure to read and understand it carefully before signing.
- **Parent's Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of the parent or guardian of the member, if applicable.
- **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter the birth date of the member.
- **Judo Experience** (`judo_experience`, text, optional): If you have any prior experience with judo, please describe it briefly.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): This field is not clearly defined; however, it might be intended for payment terms, such as membership fees or dues. Please provide accurate information, and review the field carefully.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign the form by typing your signature.
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): Provide the email address of your emergency contact person.
- **Judo Experience** (`judo_experience`, text, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the "Judo Experience" field from page 5. If you have prior experience, please describe it briefly.
- **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, text, optional): This field likely contains the club's privacy policy statement; read it carefully before agreeing to it.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
- Review the club rules and waiver form carefully before signing.
- If you have any issues or doubts while filling out the form, contact the club administrators.

Please note that some field labels might not be clear or might be intended for other purposes. If you're unsure about any field, you can contact the club administrators for clarification.
