# Fitness App Premium Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness App Premium Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential premium members of the fitness app. This form helps the fitness app team assess your suitability for a premium membership and tailor the onboarding process to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the **Member Details** page with your name and contact information.
2.  Select your preferred **Membership Plan** from the given options.
3.  Provide your **Payment Details**, including any relevant information such as card details or other payment methods.
4.  If necessary, add any **Onboarding Details** to help the fitness app team understand your specific needs.
5.  Use the **Additional Information** field to add any relevant comments or notes to support your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Member Details**
	+ Name: Please enter your full name.
	+ Contact Information: Enter your email address and phone number.
	+ Required: Yes
*  **Plan Selection**
	+ Membership Plan 1: Select this plan for basic benefits.
	+ Membership Plan 2: Select this plan for advanced benefits.
	+ Membership Plan 3: Select this plan for premium benefits.
	+ Required: Yes
*  **Payment Details**
	+ Enter your payment information, including card details or other payment methods.
	+ Required: Yes
*  **Onboarding Details** (Optional)
	+ Enter any additional onboarding information to help the fitness app team understand your specific needs.
	+ Required: No
*  **Additional Information** (Optional)
	+ Add any relevant comments or notes to support your application.
	+ Required: No

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, including Member Details, Plan Selection, and Payment Details.
- Use the Additional Information field to add any relevant context or comments that may aid the fitness app team in processing your application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator at <form@gic.mx>.
