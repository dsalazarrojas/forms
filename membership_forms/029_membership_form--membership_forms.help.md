# Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The membership form is designed to allow individuals to sign up for membership with the organization and provide necessary information to facilitate the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the information on the first page, including your contact details.
2.  On the second page, choose your desired membership plan.
3.  Confirm your membership status and member since details on the third page.
4.  Complete any remaining fields as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Sign Up** (`sign_up_page`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information to start the sign-up process.
*  **Membership Plans** (`membership_plan`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the membership plan that suits your needs.
*  **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact details to enable us to reach you.
*  **Confirm Membership** (`confirm_membership`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Confirm your membership status to ensure we can process your application correctly.
*  **Confirm Details** (`confirm_details`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm the details we have on file to avoid any discrepancies.
*  **Member Since** (`member_since`, `date`, required/optional): Indicate your member since status to update our records accordingly.
 
## Tips
- Please note that the form is not intended for non-members who do not wish to join.
- If you're an existing member, log in to our system and use the member profile form instead.
- The membership status and member since options are not required but will apply a premium membership status and update your member since status accordingly if selected.
