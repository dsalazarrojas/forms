# Counselors Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect membership information from applicants for the Counselors Guild.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as required.
2. For address fields, fill in each line of the address in the corresponding field (e.g., Address 1, Address 2, Address 3, Address 4).
3. Select the correct Membership Status and Certification status.
4. Enter the correct dates in the Membership Date and Last Updated fields.
5. If necessary, add additional comments or information in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address 1** (`address1`, `text`, **required**): Enter the first line of your address.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, `text`, **required**): Enter the second line of your address.
* **Address 3** (`address3`, `text`, **required**): Enter the third line of your address.
* **Address 4** (`address4`, `text`, **required**): Enter the fourth line of your address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, **required**): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, **required**): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, **required**): Enter your zip code.
* **Membership Date** (`membership_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of your membership.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, `date`, **required**): Enter the last date you updated your information.
* **Last Updated Time** (`last_updated_time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the last time you updated your information.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Select whether you have membership or not.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your current membership status.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Membership Details** (`membership_details`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional membership details.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select whether you are certified or not.
* **Specialty** (`specialty`, `text`, **required**): Enter your specialty.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or information.
