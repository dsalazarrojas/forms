# Library Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register members of the library. It is a basic form designed to collect member information and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Registration Form** (Page 1) with your name and any other relevant information.
2. On the **Member Info** page (Page 2), provide your personal details, such as your member ID or other unique identifier.
3. On the **Contact Details** page (Page 3), enter your contact information, such as your address, phone number, or email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Registration Form** (Page 1)
* **Registration Form** (`text`, `required`: `false`): This is the starting point for your membership registration. Fill out this page with your name and any other relevant information.

### **Member Info** (Page 2)
* **Member Info** (`text`, `required`: `false`): This page is used to provide your personal details, such as your member ID or other unique identifier.

### **Contact Details** (Page 3)
* **Contact Details** (`text`, `required`: `false`): On this page, enter your contact information, such as your address, phone number, or email.
