# Stage Performers Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Stage Performers Network Membership Form is designed for performers to submit their details and performance history to be considered for membership in the network.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Performer Details page (page 1) with your relevant information.
2. Move on to the Performance History page (page 2) and provide a detailed account of your performance experience and history.
3. If you wish to save and submit your application, select the "Save and Submit" option on the Membership page (page 3).
4. Review and confirm your selections before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Details (page 1)**
	+ **Performer Details**: This page collects basic information about you as a performer, including your name, contact details, and other relevant biographical data.
* **Performance History (page 2)**
	+ **Performance History**: This page requires a detailed account of your performance experience and history. Please provide a clear and concise description of your past performances, including any notable events, venues, or achievements.
* **Membership (page 3)**
	+ **Membership**: This field is optional and allows you to specify any additional information you'd like to provide about your membership application. You can also use this field to request specific membership options or accommodations.
* **Submit (page 4)**
	+ **Submit**: Select "Save" to save your application and proceed to the next step, or select "Save and Submit" to complete and submit your membership application.
* **Save and Submit (page 5)**
	+ **Save and Submit**: This is the final step to complete and submit your membership application. Ensure that all information is accurate and complete before selecting this option.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field or requirement, reach out to the administrators for guidance.
* Review and confirm your selections before submitting your application.
