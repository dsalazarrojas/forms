# Wellness Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from the user to create a comprehensive profile for their wellness journey. This form ensures that the user receives the best possible care by collecting general information, medical information, contact information, allergies, medications, medical history, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before completing it.
2. Fill in the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to provide all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Enter your general information such as your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical Information**: Provide any existing medical conditions, allergies, and medications you are taking.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information such as phone number and email.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the contact information for your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and email.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, including food, environmental, or medical allergies.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history, including medical conditions, surgeries, or hospitalizations.
* **Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions you currently have.
* **Emergency Medical Info**: Provide any emergency medical information, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
* **Medical Professionals**: List any medical professionals you have seen in the past.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical notes or instructions.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide any emergency contact information.
* **Insurance**: Enter any insurance information.
* **Medical History Info**: Provide any relevant medical history information.
* **Medical History Date**: Enter the date of your medical history.
* **Medical History Start Date**: Enter the date when your medical history started.
* **Medical History End Date**: Enter the date when your medical history ended.
* **Medical History Length**: Enter the length of your medical history.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Relationship**: Enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Note**: Enter any notes about your emergency contact.
* **Medical Notes Info**: Enter any medical notes or instructions.
* **Medical Notes Date**: Enter the date of your medical notes.
* **Medical Notes Time**: Enter the time of your medical notes.
