# Donor Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Membership Renewal Form is a form designed to help existing donors update their membership information and renew their support. It is crucial for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that members can continue participating in our programs and receiving exclusive benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required information before proceeding.
2. Fill in your **General Information** section with your name and contact details.
3. Provide your **Contact Information**, including your phone number and address.
4. Update your **Membership Details** with your current membership level and type.
5. Choose your preferred **Membership Plan** from the available options.
6. Confirm your **Payment Information** and select your payment method.
7. Provide your **Notes** to the organization if necessary.
8. Enter your **Membership Renewal Date**, **Membership Plan Expiration Date**, **Membership Plan Start Date**, and **Membership Plan End Date** accurately.
9. Select your **Member Relationship** as an individual or organization.
10. Enter your **Email**, **Phone**, **Address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip**, and **Country** correctly.
11. Enter your **Payment Date** when you made the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first section of the form where you can provide your name and contact details.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to capture your contact information, including your phone number and address.
* **Payment Information** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This section is crucial for processing payments and tracking donations.
* **Membership Details** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to update your membership status, including your membership level and type.
* **Membership Plan** (`5`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section allows you to choose between different membership plans, which dictate your benefits and privileges.
* **Membership Level** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section allows you to select your membership level, which determines the benefits and privileges you receive.
* **Expiration Date** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to track when your membership is set to expire.
* **Renewal Reason** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or reasons for your membership renewal.
* **Donation Amount** (`9`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to track the amount you donated.
* **Payment Method** (`10`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section allows you to choose between different payment methods.
* **Payment Status** (`11`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section is used to track your payment status.
* **Notes** (`12`, `note`, required/optional): This field is a free-text section for you to provide additional comments or notes.
* **Membership Plan Renewal Date** (`13`, `time`, required/optional): This field is used to track when your membership was renewed.
* **Membership Plan Expiration Date** (`14`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to track when your membership is set to expire.
* **Membership Plan Start Date** (`15`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to track when your membership started.
* **Membership Plan End Date** (`16`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to track when your membership is set to end.
* **Member Relationship** (`17`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section is designed to capture your relationship with the organization (individual or organization).
* **Email** (`18`, `email`, required/optional): This field is used to capture your email address.
* **Phone** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture your phone number.
* **Address** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture your address.
* **City** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture your city.
* **State** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture your state.
* **Zip** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture your zip code.
* **Country** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to capture your country.
* **Payment Date** (`25`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to track when your payment was made.
