# Science Museum Family Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Museum Family Membership Form is designed for families or individuals to sign up for a Science Museum membership. This form collects necessary contact and family information to process your membership application.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the 'Submit' button to initiate the form processing.
2. Fill out the form with your contact and family information as required.
3. Review each field for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Science Museum Family Membership Form**: This is the title of the form, which is not editable. Please ignore it.
* **name**: Enter your first name.
* **surname**: Enter your last name.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **address**: Enter your address, including street number, apartment number, and any additional details.
* **city**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **state**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **zip**: Enter your postal zip code.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **membership_type**: Select your membership type:
	+ Family Membership: For families with children under 18 years old.
	+ Individual Membership: For individuals without children.
	+ Senior Membership: For senior citizens aged 65 or above.
	+ Student Membership: For students with a valid student ID card.
* **number_of_children**: Enter the number of children under 18 years old in your family.
* **name_of_children**: Select the names of your children:
	+ None: If you have no children.
	+ 1, 2, 3, 4, 5: Select one of the options for the number of children.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **birth_date**: This field is currently redundant and can be ignored.
* **birth_time**: This field is not currently in use and can be ignored.
* **birth_date**: This field is currently redundant and can be ignored.
* **note**: Enter any additional comments or notes for your membership application.

Please fill out the form accurately and efficiently, and do not hesitate to contact the Science Museum if you have any questions or need further assistance.
