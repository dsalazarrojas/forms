# Club Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Membership Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential members to process their membership application. The form is used to gather personal details, membership preferences, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth and efficient membership experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Select your membership type and membership duration that suits your needs.
3. Choose your preferred payment method for membership fees.
4. Enter your emergency contact information, including their name and contact number.
5. Select the activities and events you are interested in, as this will help us tailor the club experience to your needs.
6. Tell us how you heard about the club, so we can improve our outreach efforts.
7. If applicable, inform us about any dietary restrictions you may have, so we can accommodate your needs.
8. Please select your T-Shirt size for club merchandise (optional).
9. Agree to the club's terms and conditions and accept our privacy policy.
10. Optionally, subscribe to our newsletter for updates and news.
11. Finally, confirm the date of this membership application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`member_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`member_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`member_email`, `email`, required): Your primary contact email for club communications.
* **Phone Number** (`member_phone`, `text`, required): Your best contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the membership level that suits your needs (e.g., Basic Member, Premium Member, etc.).
* **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you want your membership to last (e.g., 1 Month, 3 Months, 1 Year, etc.).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your membership (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Club Interests** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the activities and events you are interested in (e.g., Social Events, Sports Activities, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_club`, `select_one`, optional): Tell us how you heard about the club (e.g., Friend or Family, Social Media, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): Inform us about any allergies or dietary needs for events (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.).
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select your T-Shirt size for club merchandise (e.g., XS, S, M, etc.).
* **Agree to Club Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to the club's terms and conditions.
* **Accept Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you accept our privacy policy.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter_subscription`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether to subscribe to our newsletter for updates and news.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this membership application.
