# Membership Subscription Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect subscription details from users. It ensures that you provide all necessary information for a successful membership subscription.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select a membership plan (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly)
2. Choose a subscription duration (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months)
3. Select a payment method (Bank Transfer, Check Payment, Online Payment)
4. Enter your email address
5. Provide a valid phone number
6. Enter your address
7. (Optional) Provide any additional notes
8. Select your membership status (Active, Inactive)
9. Select your membership type (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of membership you would like to subscribe to (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly)
* **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, select_one, **required**): Choose how long you would like your subscription to last (e.g., 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months)
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you will make your payment (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check Payment, Online Payment)
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your valid email address to receive important updates and notifications
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter a valid phone number for any urgent communication
* **Address** (`address`, text, **required**): Enter your address for delivery or any other relevant purposes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about your subscription
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_one, **required**): Select your current membership status (e.g., Active, Inactive)
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of membership you would like to subscribe to (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly)
