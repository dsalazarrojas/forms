# Wabc | Membership Form 2020 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture membership information for users.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "First name" field with your first name.
2. Fill in the "Last name" field with your last name.
3. Select your desired "Membership level" from the provided options.
4. Select your current "Membership status".
5. Enter your "Email" address.
6. Enter your "Phone" number.
7. Enter your "Date of birth".
8. Fill in the "Join date" of your membership.
9. Enter your address details in the "Address 1", "Address 2", "Address 3", and "Address 4" fields.
10. Select your "Post code" location from the provided options.
11. Enter any additional "Notes" you want to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (text, required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name (text, required)**: Enter your last name.
* **Membership level (select_one, optional)**: Choose your desired membership level from the options (Basic, Premium, or Elite).
* **Membership status (select_multiple, optional)**: Select the current status of your membership (Active or Inactive).
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth (date, optional)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Join date (date, optional)**: Enter the date you joined the membership.
* **Address 1 (text, optional)**: Enter your address line 1.
* **Address 2 (text, optional)**: Enter your address line 2.
* **Address 3 (text, optional)**: Enter your address line 3.
* **Address 4 (text, optional)**: Enter your address line 4.
* **Post code (select_one, optional)**: Select your location from the options (London, Birmingham, Leeds, or Other).
* **Notes (note, optional)**: Enter any additional notes about your membership.
