# member_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from members about their overall satisfaction with our services. It will help us understand what we are doing well and what we can improve to better serve our members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions on the overall satisfaction page, which will be the first page you see when opening the form.
2. On each question page, enter your responses to the questions as prompted.
3. When you have completed all the questions, move on to the next page.
4. Review all the pages and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false): This is a rating of your overall satisfaction with our services, on a scale that you can select from.
  - This field will help us understand how satisfied you are with our services.
  - Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.
- **Question 1** (`question1`, text, required: false): This is a question where you can enter any thoughts or comments you may have about our services.
  - This field is for any additional information or feedback you would like to share with us.
  - You can enter as much or as little as you like.
- **Question 2** (`question2`, text, required: false): This is another question where you can enter any thoughts or comments you may have about our services.
  - This field is similar to question 1, where you can share any additional information or feedback you would like to provide.
  - Enter as much or as little as you like.
- **Question 3** (`question3`, text, required: false): This is another question where you can enter any thoughts or comments you may have about our services.
  - This field is similar to question 1 and 2, where you can share any additional information or feedback you would like to provide.
  - Enter as much or as little as you like.
- **Question 4** (`question4`, text, required: false): This is another question where you can enter any thoughts or comments you may have about our services.
  - This field is similar to question 1, 2, and 3, where you can share any additional information or feedback you would like to provide.
  - Enter as much or as little as you like.
- **Question 5** (`question5`, text, required: false): This is the last question where you can enter any thoughts or comments you may have about our services.
  - This field is similar to question 1, 2, 3, and 4, where you can share any additional information or feedback you would like to provide.
  - Enter as much or as little as you like.
- **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): This is a rating of your overall satisfaction with our services, on a scale that you can select from.
  - This field is similar to the "Overall Satisfaction" field, but is more specific to rating your satisfaction.
  - Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.
