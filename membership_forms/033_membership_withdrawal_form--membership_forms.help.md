# Membership Withdrawal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Membership Withdrawal Form is a document used to inform the organization of a member's decision to withdraw from the membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Select the correct options for each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Withdrawal Form** (`membership_withdrawal_form`, text, not required): This is the main text box where you can enter a brief description of the member's reasons for withdrawal.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, text, not required): This field is used to gather more details about the member's decision to withdraw, if any.
* **Date of Last Membership Event** (`date_of_last_membership_event`, date, not required): Enter the date of the last membership event attended by the member.
* **Time of Last Membership Event** (`time_of_last_membership_event`, time, not required): Enter the time of the last membership event attended by the member, if applicable.
* **Member to Withdraw** (`member_to_withdraw`, select_one, not required): Select 'Yes' if the member is withdrawing from the organization.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, text, not required): This field is used to gather more details about the member's decision to withdraw, if any.
* **Select Additional Info** (`select_additional_info`, select_multiple, not required): Check all additional information relevant to the member's withdrawal.
* **Confirm Withdraw** (`confirm_withdraw`, select_one, not required): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the member has been informed of the withdrawal.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or details about the member's withdrawal.
* **Confirm Additional Info** (`confirm_additional_info`, select_one, not required): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the additional information is accurate.
* **Membership Cancellation Date** (`membership_cancellation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when the membership will be canceled.
* **Membership Cancellation Time** (`membership_cancellation_time`, time, not required): Enter the time when the membership will be canceled, if applicable.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's best to fill them out for complete and accurate information.
