# Art Gallery Membership Tier Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Membership Tier Application form is designed to collect information from potential members who are interested in becoming a member of the art gallery. This form helps us understand your interests and preferences, and allows us to tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
2. Select your desired membership tier.
3. Choose your payment method.
4. Specify your preferences for receiving newsletter, event invitations, and other communications.
5. Indicate your interest in volunteering and areas of interest.
6. If applicable, add a donation to your membership.
7. Specify how you heard about the gallery and if you were referred by someone.
8. Add any additional comments or special interests.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any special interests or needs, be sure to mention them.
* If you are interested in volunteering, select the areas where you are interested in volunteering.
* If you would like to add a donation, specify the amount.
* Confirm that you accept the membership terms and conditions.
