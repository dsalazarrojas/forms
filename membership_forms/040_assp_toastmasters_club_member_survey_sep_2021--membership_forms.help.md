# ASSP Toastmasters Club Member Survey Sep 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ASSP Toastmasters Club Member Survey Sep 2021 is designed to gather feedback from club members on their experiences and perceptions of the club. This survey will help the club leadership to understand areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the club experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

* Review each question carefully and select the response that best answers the question.
* For questions with multiple response options, select all that apply.
* Provide clear and concise answers in the text fields.
* Submit the form when you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member ID** (`member_id`, text, optional): Enter your unique member identification number.
* **How long have you been a member of our Toastmasters club** (`membership_duration`, select_one, required): Select the approximate duration of your membership. Options are:
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **On average, how many meetings do you attend per month** (`meeting_attendance`, select_one, required): Select your typical monthly attendance. Options are:
	+ None
	+ 1-2
	+ 3-4
	+ All meetings
* **Which meeting format do you prefer** (`meeting_format_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred meeting format. Options are:
	+ In-person
	+ Virtual
	+ Hybrid
	+ No preference
* **How satisfied are you with the number of speaking opportunities at meetings** (`speech_opportunities_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction level from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **How would you rate the quality of evaluations you receive** (`evaluation_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of feedback from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **How valuable do you find the educational aspects of our club** (`educational_value_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the educational value from 1 (not valuable) to 5 (very valuable).
* **Are you satisfied with the leadership opportunities available** (`leadership_opportunities`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction. Options are:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **How would you rate the overall atmosphere and culture of our club** (`club_atmosphere_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the club atmosphere from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Are you satisfied with the current meeting times** (`meeting_times_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your satisfaction level. Options are:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Would you be interested in attending special workshops or training sessions** (`workshop_interest`, select_one, optional): Select your interest level. Options are:
	+ True
	+ definitely
	+ possibly
	+ False
	+ not interested
* **What areas would you like to see improved in our club** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas you think need improvement. Options are:
	+ Meeting structure
	+ Guest speakers
	+ Networking opportunities
	+ Meeting length
	+ Meeting location
	+ Communication
	+ Educational content
	+ Leadership development
* **How often do you use Toastmasters International online resources** (`online_resources_utilization`, select_one, optional): Select your usage frequency. Options are:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Are you currently paired with a mentor** (`mentor_assignment`, select_one, optional): Select if you have an assigned mentor. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not interested
* **Would you be interested in a mentoring program** (`mentoring_interest`, select_one, optional): Select if you'd like to participate in mentoring. Options are:
	+ True
	+ as a mentee
	+ as a mentor
	+ as both
	+ False
* **Would you be interested in joining special interest groups** (`special_interest_groups`, select_multiple, optional): Select topics you're interested in. Options are:
	+ Leadership
	+ Storytelling
	+ Business presentations
	+ Technical speaking
	+ Humorous speaking
	+ Table Topics
	+ Advanced communication
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any other thoughts about our club.
* **How likely are you to recommend our club to others** (`recommend_likelihood`, number, required): Rate likelihood from 0 (not at all likely) to 10 (extremely likely).

Note: The required fields are marked with `required: true` and the optional fields are marked with `required: false`.
