# Sustainable Living Group Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals as members, non-members, volunteers, or leaders in the sustainable living group. The form collects information such as first name, email, phone number, and address for each applicant. The membership type (e.g., Member, Non-Member, Volunteer, Leader) and other relevant details are also collected through this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
4. Choose your membership type from the "Membership Type" field (Member, Non-Member, Volunteer, or Leader).
5. Select your membership status (Active, Inactive, Suspended, or Pending) in the "Member Status" field.
6. Choose your role (Admin, Member, or Leader) in the "Role" field.
7. Indicate whether you have a leadership role ("Yes" or "No") in the "Role Type" field.
8. Fill in your organization (if applicable) in the "Organization" field.
9. Provide your participant details (if applicable) in the "Participant Details" field.
10. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
11. Provide your city in the "City" field.
12. Enter your state in the "State" field.
13. Fill in your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
14. Enter the date you joined the group in the "Date Joined" field.
15. Note any additional information in the "Notes" field (if applicable).
16. Enter the last updated date and time in the "Last Updated" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose your membership type (Member, Non-Member, Volunteer, or Leader).
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization (if applicable).
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, note, required): Provide any relevant participant details (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Date Joined** (`date_joined`, date, required): Enter the date you joined the group.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, time, required): Enter the last updated date and time (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Provide any additional information (if applicable).
* **Member Status** (`member_status`, select_one, required): Choose your member status (Active, Inactive, Suspended, or Pending).
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role (Admin, Member, or Leader).
* **Role Type** (`role_type`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a leadership role ("Yes" or "No").
