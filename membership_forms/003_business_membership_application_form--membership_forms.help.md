# Business Membership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses interested in becoming members of our organization. The form is divided into sections that cover personal and business information, membership details, and agreement acceptance. Completing this form will help us understand your business needs and preferences, and will guide our decision-making process for your membership application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your business information, including your business name, industry type, and years in operation.
3. Select your desired membership tier and preferred start date (if applicable).
4. Specify the reasons why you are interested in joining our organization.
5. Confirm your understanding of our membership agreement.
6. Review all sections carefully and make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use for communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name.
* **Industry** (`business_industry`, select_one, required): Select your primary industry or sector.
* **Years in Business** (`business_years_operating`, number, required): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, text, required): Enter your city and state.
* **Desired Membership Tier** (`membership_tier`, select_one, required): Select the membership level that best suits your business needs.
* **Reasons for Joining** (`reasons_for_membership`, select_multiple, required): Choose the benefits you are interested in.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`heard_about_us`, select_one, optional): Select the source of your referral.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when you would like your membership to begin.
* **Agreement Acceptance** (`agreement_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm your acceptance of our membership agreement.
