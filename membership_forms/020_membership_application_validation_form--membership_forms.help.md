# Membership Application Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate membership applications, ensuring that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. For optional fields, you can leave them blank or fill them in as needed.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, *): Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, *): Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, *): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (4, text, *): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Membership Type** (5, select_one, *): Select the type of membership you are applying for (Individual, Organization, or Business).
* **Membership Start Date** (6, date, *): Enter the start date of your membership, if applicable.
* **Membership End Date** (7, date, *): Enter the end date of your membership, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (8, select_multiple, *): Select the payment method you intend to use (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Address** (9, text, *): Enter your physical address.
* **Country** (10, select_multiple, *): Select your country of residence.
* **Notes** (11, note, *): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your application.
* **Assigned Tool** (12, text, ): This field is used internally, so you can leave it blank.
* **Form IDs** (13, text, ): This field is used internally, so you can leave it blank.
* **Category** (14, text, ): Enter the category you belong to, if applicable.
* **Output File** (15, text, ): This field is not applicable, so you can leave it blank.

Note: The asterisk (*) indicates a required field.
