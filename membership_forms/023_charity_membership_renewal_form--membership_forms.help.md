# Charity Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Membership Renewal Form is used to collect information from members who wish to renew their membership with our organization. This form will help us keep your membership status up-to-date and ensure you receive the benefits you're entitled to.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the "Member Information" section.
	* First Name: Your first name as a member of our organization.
	* Last Name: Your last name as a member of our organization.
	* Membership ID Number: Your unique member ID number.
	* Email Address: The email address we use for member communications.
	* Phone Number: Your contact phone number.
	* Mailing Address: Your address for postal communications.
2. Select your current and new membership level in the "Membership Renewal" section.
	* Current Membership Level: Choose the level you're currently at.
	* New Membership Level: Choose the level you're renewing at.
3. Provide payment information in the "Payment Information" section.
	* Membership Renewal Cost: The amount due for renewal.
	* Payment Method: Select how you'd like to pay (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
4. Set up auto-renewal and communication preferences.
	* Set Up Auto-Renewal: Choose whether you'd like to automatically renew in future years.
	* Receive Newsletter and Updates: Decide if you'd like to receive membership communications.
