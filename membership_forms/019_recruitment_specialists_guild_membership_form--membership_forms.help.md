# Recruitment Specialists Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Specialists Guild Membership Form is used to collect and store data about potential and existing members of the guild. The form is designed to be comprehensive and help the members and administrators manage the membership status, plan, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your name and other relevant details.
2. Indicate whether you are a new or existing member.
3. Select your membership type (if applicable).
4. Provide your address for communication and membership purposes.
5. Specify your membership start and end dates (if applicable).
6. Add any additional contact information or notes about yourself.
7. Confirm your membership terms and payment information.
8. Select your payment method and status (if applicable).
9. Update your membership status and plan details as necessary.
10. Provide any additional information about your membership plan and payment fee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: true): Please enter your name and other relevant details for identification purposes.
* **Membership Type** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, required: false): If you are a new member, select 'Yes' to indicate you are interested in joining. If you are an existing member, confirm your membership type.
* **Membership Plan** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your membership plan type (if applicable) to determine your membership benefits and fees.
* **Address** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your address for communication and membership purposes.
* **Membership Start Date** (`id: 5`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date you started your membership (if applicable).
* **Membership End Date** (`id: 6`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date your membership will end (if applicable).
* **Contact Information** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): Add any additional contact information for yourself or your representative.
* **User Notes** (`id: 8`, `type: note`, required: false): Add any notes about yourself or your membership.
* **Membership Terms** (`id: 9`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Confirm your understanding of the membership terms and conditions.
* **Payment Information** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional payment details (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`id: 11`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your payment method (if applicable) to determine your payment terms.
* **Membership Status** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Confirm your current membership status (Active or Inactive).
* **Member Role** (`id: 13`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your role within the guild (if applicable).
* **Payment Status** (`id: 14`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Confirm your payment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Plan Fee** (`id: 15`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter your membership plan fee (if applicable).
* **Membership Plan Fee Currency** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your membership plan fee currency (if applicable).
* **Membership Plan Duration** (`id: 17`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your membership plan duration (if applicable).
* **Membership Plan Start Date** (`id: 18`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the start date of your membership plan (if applicable).
* **Membership Plan End Date** (`id: 19`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the end date of your membership plan (if applicable).
* **Payment Plan Duration** (`id: 20`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the duration of your payment plan (if applicable).
* **Membership Plan Status** (`id: 21`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Confirm your membership plan status (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Plan Fee Status** (`id: 22`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Confirm your membership plan fee status (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Plan Fee Invoice** (`id: 23`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Confirm your membership plan fee invoice (Yes or No).
* **User Information 2** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Contact Information 2** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information for yourself or your representative.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to avoid any errors or confusion.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact the guild administrators for assistance.
* Review and confirm your information regularly to ensure it is up-to-date and accurate.
* This form is designed to help you manage your membership status and plan details; please use it as your primary resource for guild information.
