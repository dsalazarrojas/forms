# Membership Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from existing members to renew their membership with the organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the membership renewal form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Information** (`member_info`, text, required): This is the section where you will provide your current membership information. Please enter your name and any other relevant details as required.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): If you have moved or changed your address, please provide your new address. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Renewal Information** (`renewal_info`, text, required): This is where you will provide any necessary information for the renewal process. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Next Meeting** (`next_meeting`, text, optional): If you know of an upcoming meeting, please provide the date and location. This will help the organization plan for your attendance.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): Please provide any necessary payment information, such as payment method and amount.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): This is your primary contact information, which will be used to reach out to you regarding your membership renewal.
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, text, optional): If you know when you joined the organization, please provide the date.
* **Comments** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any comments or additional information, please add them here.
* **Membership Details** (`membership_details`, text, required): Please provide any additional information about your membership status or any changes that have occurred since your last renewal.
