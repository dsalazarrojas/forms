# Join The Bold Leaders Community - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your motivations for joining the Bold Leaders Community. By filling out this form, you'll be able to connect with fellow members who share similar interests, goals, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following steps:
1. Provide your First Name and Last Name to help us identify you.
2. Enter your Email to receive updates and notifications from us.
3. Share your Phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by us or other community members.
4. In the What motivated you to join the Bold Leaders Community? field, briefly explain your reasons for joining.
5. Choose which Interest Areas you'd like to connect with (e.g., Yes, No).
6. Select your role in your organization (e.g., Yes, No).
7. Describe your leadership or management experience, if any.
8. Share your goals for joining the Bold Leaders Community.
9. Specify what you expect to gain from joining the community.
10. Write a Briefly describe yourself section to help us get to know you better.
11. Select any interests you have outside of work (e.g., Yes, No).
12. Choose whether you have experience with Prophets (e.g., Yes, No).
13. Select any mentors you've had or would like to have (e.g., Yes, No).
14. Pick any skills you possess (e.g., Yes, No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for reference and introduction.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for reference and introduction.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for updates, notifications, and community engagement.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by us or other community members.
* **What motivated you to join the Bold Leaders Community?**: Share a brief reason for why you're joining the community (e.g., personal growth, professional development).
* **Interest Areas**: Choose any interest areas you'd like to connect with, such as hobbies or areas of expertise.
* **What is your role in your organization?**: Select your role in your organization, such as leader, manager, or team member.
* **What experience do you have in leadership or management?**: Describe your leadership or management experience, if any.
* **What are your goals for joining the Bold Leaders Community?**: Share your goals for joining the community (e.g., networking, learning, personal growth).
* **What do you expect to gain from joining the Bold Leaders Community?**: Specify what you hope to achieve or gain from being part of the community.
* **Briefly describe yourself**: Write a brief paragraph about yourself, your interests, and your goals.
* **What interests do you have outside of work?**: Choose any interests you have outside of work, such as hobbies or sports.
* **Prophets**: Select whether you have experience with or are interested in Prophets (e.g., mentors, coaches).
* **Mentors**: Select any mentors you've had or would like to have.
* **Skills**: Choose any skills you possess (e.g., leadership, communication, problem-solving).
