# Leadership Forum Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leadership Forum Membership Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in joining the leadership forum, a platform for sharing knowledge and best practices among professionals in the field of leadership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided. This field is not required, but it helps us get to know you better.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field provided. This field is also not required, but it provides additional context about you.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@domain.com). This field is not required, but it ensures we can contact you efficiently.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field provided. This field is not required, but it helps us communicate with you effectively.
5. Enter your **Address** in the text field provided. This field is not required, but it provides additional context about you.
6. In the **Professional History** text field, describe your professional background and any relevant experience. This field is not required, but it helps us understand your expertise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@domain.com).
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Professional History** (Professional History, text, optional): Describe your professional background and any relevant experience.
