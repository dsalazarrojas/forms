# Philanthropists Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential members who wish to join the Philanthropists Guild. It is used to collect personal and membership preferences information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address Line 1", "Address Line 2", "City", "State", and "Zip Code" fields.
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7. Select your preferred amount to donate in the "How much do you want to donate?" field.
8. Choose your desired frequency of dues payment in the "Frequency of Dues" field.
9. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
10. Indicate how long you have been supporting us in the "How long have you been supporting us?" field.
11. Choose what you value most about our organization in the "What do you value most?" field.
12. Select how you found us in the "How did you find us?" field.
