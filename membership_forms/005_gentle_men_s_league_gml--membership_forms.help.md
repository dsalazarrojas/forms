# Gentle Men's League Gml - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gentle Men's League Gml form is designed to collect information from users about themselves, including their first name, last name, address, email, phone number, date of birth, member type, category, and any additional description or details. The form helps the Gentle Men's League organization to better understand its members and their characteristics, and to provide personalized services or benefits to its members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First name and Last name in the corresponding fields. This information will be used to identify you as a member.
2. Fill in your Address 1, Address 2, and City fields. This will help us to know your address and location.
3. Enter your Email address in the format of `username@domain.com` or `email@domain.com`.
4. Provide your Phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX-XXX-XXXXX`.
5. Enter your Date of birth in the format of `MM/DD/YYYY`.
6. Select your Member type (Member or Non-Member) in the dropdown list.
7. Select your Category (General, Executive, or Honorary) in the dropdown list.
8. Enter a brief Description of yourself, if you wish to provide more information about yourself.
9. Enter the person who created this member record (optional).
10. Enter the tool that is assigned to this member record (optional).
11. Select the Category for this member record (optional).
12. Enter any additional details in the Description field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: Enter your first name.
- **Last name**: Enter your last name.
- **Address 1**: Enter your street address or building number.
- **Address 2**: Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
- **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
- **Email**: Enter your email address in the format of `username@domain.com` or `email@domain.com`.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX-XXX-XXXXX`.
- **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- **Member type**: Select whether you are a Member or Non-Member.
- **Category**: Select your Category (General, Executive, or Honorary).
- **Description**: Enter a brief description of yourself.
- **Created by**: Enter the person who created this member record (if different from yourself).
- **Assigned tool**: Enter the tool assigned to this member record (if applicable).
- **Output file**: Enter the output file name (if applicable).
- **Category 1**: Select the Category for this member record (if applicable).
