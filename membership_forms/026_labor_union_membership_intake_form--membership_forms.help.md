# labor_union_membership_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for new members to provide their personal details and information about their labor union membership status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your New Member Details in the **New Member Details** field.
2. Provide your Work History in the **Work History** field.
3. Select your **Employment Status** from the available options (Active, Non-active, Retired, or Terminated).
4. If you are not currently receiving benefits, please indicate your **Benefits** status (if applicable).
5. Select your **Dues Status** from the available options (Paid, Unpaid, or Pending Payment).
6. Choose your **Savings Status** from the available options (Active, Non-active, or Retired).
7. Enter your **Savings Amount**.
8. Provide any additional **Notes** about your membership.
9. If applicable, enter your **Agreement** details.
10. Enter the date of your **Agreement** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
11. If applicable, enter the name of the person who signed the **Agreement**.
12. If applicable, enter the name of the person who signed the **Agreement**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Member Details**: This is where you provide your name, address, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Work History**: Please provide a brief description of your work history, including your job title, work period, and any relevant details.
* **Employment Status**: This field asks for your current employment status, which can be Active, Non-active, Retired, or Terminated.
* **Benefits**: If you are not receiving benefits, please indicate this and provide further details if needed.
* **Dues Status**: Select your current dues status, which can be Paid, Unpaid, or Pending Payment.
* **Savings Status**: Choose your current savings status, which can be Active, Non-active, or Retired.
* **Savings Amount**: Enter the amount you have saved.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your membership.
* **Agreement**: If you have entered into an agreement with the labor union, please provide the details of that agreement.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the agreement in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Agreement Signatory**: If applicable, enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.
* **Signed by**: If applicable, enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.
