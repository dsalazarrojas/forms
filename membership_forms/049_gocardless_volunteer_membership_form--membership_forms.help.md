# GoCardless Volunteer Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and demographic information from individuals interested in joining or already part of the GoCardless volunteer membership program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Choose your date of birth using the "date_of_birth" field in a mm/dd/yyyy format.
6. Double-check your information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name**: Enter your first name (given name) in this field.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes. This will be used to communicate with you about GoCardless.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number to help us reach you if needed.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth using the mm/dd/yyyy format. This will help us verify your identity if needed.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter the correct format for the "date_of_birth" field (mm/dd/yyyy).
* You can use the "phone" field to enter a phone number with a country code if needed.
