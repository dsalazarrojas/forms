# Digital Marketing Group Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Marketing Group Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential members who want to join the group. This form is a straightforward way to gather essential details about the member, such as their name, contact email, and membership type. By filling out this form, you will be able to join the Digital Marketing Group and become a part of our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the Member Name field.
2.  Enter your contact email in the Contact E-mail field.
3.  If you have a phone number, enter it in the Contact Phone field.
4.  Select your membership type by choosing from Regular Member, Premium Member, or Guest.
5.  If you have been a member since, select the correct date in the Member Since field.
6.  Choose your Member Role by selecting from Marketing Manager, Content Creator, Designer, or Developer.
7.  Once you have completed all the fields, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name in this field. This will be your display name within the group.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that you check regularly. This will be used as your contact email within the group.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will be your contact phone number within the group.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your membership type from the options provided. This will determine your level of access and benefits within the group.
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, date, required/optional): If you have been a member for some time, select the correct date. This will be displayed as your member since date within the group.
* **Member Role** (`member_role`, select_one, required/optional): Select your role within the group from the options provided. This will be used to identify your position and expertise within the group.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, note, required/optional): This is a note field and will not be used for any specific functionality.
