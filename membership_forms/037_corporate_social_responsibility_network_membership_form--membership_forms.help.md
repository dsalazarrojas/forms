# corporate_social_responsibility_network_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate Social Responsibility Network Membership Form is designed to collect information about individuals interested in joining the corporate social responsibility network. This form is part of our efforts to build a community of individuals committed to making a positive impact in our industry and beyond. To ensure that you are eligible to join this network, please review the requirements carefully and complete the form accurately. If you have already submitted your membership application, you will be notified of any issues or updates via email.
 
## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, ensure you have a clear and complete set of answers for all required fields. To complete the form:
1. Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
2. Enter your company name as it is known publicly.
3. (Optional) Select your job title to help us understand your role.
4. Enter your valid email address that we will use to communicate with you.
5. Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (name, text, **required**): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Company Name** (company_name, text, **required**): Enter your company name as it is known publicly.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, **optional**): If applicable, enter your job title to help us understand your role within your company.
* **Email Address** (email, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address that we will use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, **required**): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
