# organo_gold_join_my_team_stormy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to help us understand more about you and your interest in joining our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
3. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Name, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required): Enter your primary email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Role** (Role, Select One, Required): Choose your desired role in our team, such as Team Lead, Team Member, or Team Lead - Stormy.
* **Message** (Message, Text, Optional): Add any additional information about your interest in joining our team.
* **Company** (Company, Text, Optional): If you represent a company, enter its name so we can keep in touch with them if needed.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, Text, Optional): Enter your job title at your current or previous company if applicable.
* **How did you hear about us?** (How did you hear about us?, Text, Optional): Let us know how you found out about our opportunity, e.g., social media, word of mouth, job board, etc.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and comprehensively as possible, so we can get in touch with you and discuss your interest in joining our team.
