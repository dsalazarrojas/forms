# designer_entry_form_open_runway_video_series - Help Guide
## Purpose
The designer_entry_form_open_runway_video_series form is designed to collect information from fashion designers for the Open Runway Video Series. This form will help us understand your work and experiences as a designer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing information on your target audience (e.g., younger or lay audiences), collaboration experience (e.g., with 9jaStudio or Mediascape), and other relevant details.
2. Select the correct options for each field, and make sure to save your progress and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Open Runway Video Series** (`form_open_runway_video_series`): This field is not applicable as it is a title and not a field that requires input.
* **Company xto** (`company_xto`): This field is for you to enter the name of your company.
* **Niche Younger lay and managerial audiences** (`niche_younger_lay_managerial_audiences`): Please select all the audiences that your fashion business is relevant to. You can choose one or more options (e.g., younger, lay, or managerial audiences).
* **Collaboration 9jaStudio** (`collaboration_9jastudio`): Please select all the collaborators your fashion business has worked with. You can choose one or more options (e.g., 9jaStudio or Mediascape).
* **Participation Request** (`participation_request`): Check the box if you would like to participate in the Open Runway Video Series.
* **Fashion Businesses** (`fashion_businesses`): Please select the type of fashion businesses your work with (e.g., homegrown or other).
* **Episode Structure** (`episode_structure`): This field is for you to describe the structure of your fashion episodes.
* **Mini Fashion Show** (`mini_fashion_show`): This field is for you to describe a mini fashion show related to your work.
* **Main Streets** (`main_streets`): This field is for you to describe any fashion shows or events held on main streets.
* **Casual, Formal and Semi-Formal, Party and Occasional Wears** (`casual_formal_semi_formal_party_occasional_wears`): This field is for you to describe the types of clothing your work encompasses.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or information regarding your form submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields and save your progress frequently to avoid losing work. 
* If you need help, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
