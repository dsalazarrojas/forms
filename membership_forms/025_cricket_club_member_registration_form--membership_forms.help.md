# cricket_club_member_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cricket Club Member Registration Form is designed to collect information from interested individuals who want to become members of the Cricket Club. This form is intended for people who are willing to provide their personal details to be considered for membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself and why you're interested in joining the club.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed within the club.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number or mobile number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for club updates and notifications.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your physical address for club events and activities.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Use a valid email address and phone number for effective communication.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the club administrators for assistance.
