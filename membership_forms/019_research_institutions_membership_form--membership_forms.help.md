# research_institutions_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "research_institutions_membership_form" gathers information from users about their research experience and institutional membership to facilitate membership applications, surveys, or other research-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the correct radio buttons for each field based on your response. Ensure you answer all required fields.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. It should be a valid email address.
3.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Use the "mm/dd/yyyy" format for the date.
5.  Enter the date of your joining in the "Date of Joining" field. Use the "mm/dd/yyyy" format for the date.
6.  Answer "Yes" or "No" to the questions in "Research Areas", "Research Interests", "Organization Details", "Member Role", "Contact Information", "Member Since", and "Member Type".
