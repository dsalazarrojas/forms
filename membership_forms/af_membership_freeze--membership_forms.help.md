# AF Membership Freeze - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AF Membership Freeze form is used to freeze a member's account for a specific period due to various reasons such as injury, health issues, financial hardship, or personal reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Choose your membership type and years of membership.
3.  Specify the reason for freezing your account and the expected end date of the freeze.
4.  Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the freeze.
5.  Add any additional comments or notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your legal full name to be able to identify yourself.
*   **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Enter the email address you want to receive notifications.
*   **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, **optional**): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
*   **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **optional**): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
*   **Age** (`age`, `integer`, **optional**): If you prefer not to give your date of birth, you can simply enter your age.
*   **Mailing address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your street, city, state, and zip to receive mail.
*   **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, **optional**): Name of emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `phone`, **optional**): Phone number of emergency contact person.
*   **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, `text`, **required**): Enter your membership number.
*   **Membership type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your membership type (Regular, Premium, Student, Senior).
*   **Years of membership** (`years_of_membership`, `integer`, **optional**): Enter the number of years you have been a member.
*   **Experience level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose your experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional).
*   **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, **optional**): Specify where you heard about the organization (Social media, Friend/Family, Website, Flyer/Poster, Other).
*   **Freeze start date** (`freeze_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you wish to start the freeze.
*   **Freeze end date** (`freeze_end_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the expected end date of the freeze.
*   **Reason for freeze** (`freeze_reason`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the reason for freezing your account (Injury/Health, Personal Leave, Financial Hardship, Other).
*   **Reason for freeze (other)** (`freeze_reason_other`, `text`, **optional**): Enter details if you selected "Other" as the reason for freeze.
*   **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Check all documents that apply to your situation (Medical Certificate, Work Leave Letter, Financial Statement, Other).
*   **Consent to freeze terms** (`consent_acknowledge`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose to agree to the terms and conditions of the freeze.
*   **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional information or comments about your freeze.
