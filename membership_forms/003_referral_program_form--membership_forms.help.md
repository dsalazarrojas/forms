# Referral Program Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage referrals within the membership program. It is designed to capture essential information about existing and potential customers (clients and referrals) to create new referrals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Client Details:
	* Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
	* Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
	* Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Number" field.
2. Enter the Referral Details:
	* Enter the referral's name in the "Referral Name" field.
	* Enter the referral's email address in the "Referral Email" field.
	* Enter the referral's phone number in the "Referral Number" field.
3. Add Referral Comments:
	* Enter any additional information about the referral in the "Referral Comments" field.
4. Add User Referral Details:
	* Enter any additional information about the referral process in the "User Referral Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Client Details (Referral Program Form Client)**: Enter the name of the client you are referring.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client you are referring.
* **Client Email**: Enter the email address of the client you are referring.
* **Client Number**: Enter the phone number of the client you are referring.
* **Referral Details**: Enter any additional information about the referral.
* **Referral Name**: Enter the name of the referral.
* **Referral Email**: Enter the email address of the referral.
* **Referral Number**: Enter the phone number of the referral.
* **Referral Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the referral.
* **User Referral Details**: Enter any additional information about the referral process.
