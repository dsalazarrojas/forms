# Political Party Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about new and existing members of a political party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as a new member.
2. Provide your party information, including any relevant details about your affiliation.
3. Enter your address, email, and contact number to ensure we can contact you for updates and notifications.
4. Select your role within the party (Member, Volunteer, or Officer).
5. Choose your membership type (Full Member or Associate Member).
6. Select your membership level (Bronze, Silver).
7. Add any membership notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Member** (text, required): Enter your name as a new member.
* **Party Information** (text, required): Provide any details about your affiliation with the party.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address to ensure we can contact you for updates and notifications.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for updates and notifications.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter your contact number for updates and notifications.
* **Date Joined** (date, optional): Enter the date you joined the party if applicable.
* **Role** (select_one, required): Select your role within the party (Member, Volunteer, or Officer).
* **Member Since** (number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been a member if applicable.
* **Membership Status** (select_multiple, required): Select your current membership status (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Type** (select_one, required): Select your membership type (Full Member or Associate Member).
* **Membership Level** (select_multiple, required): Select your membership level (Bronze or Silver).
* **Membership Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional membership notes if necessary.
