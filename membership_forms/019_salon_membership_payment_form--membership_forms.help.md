# Salon Membership Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salon Membership Payment Form is used to collect information from clients for a salon membership payment. This form helps the salon staff to efficiently collect payment details and process memberships for clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Salon Membership Details in the first field.
2. Select your preferred Payment Method.
3. Enter the Amount of the membership payment.
4. Choose your Membership Type.
5. Enter your Client Details, including First Name, Last Name, and Email.
6. Enter your Phone number.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Membership Details**: Enter your salon membership details to help us process your payment efficiently. This field is not required.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided: Credit Card, Paypal, or Bank Transfer. This field is not required, but it helps us to process your payment accurately.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the membership payment. This field is not required, but it helps us to process your payment accurately.
* **Membership Type**: Choose your membership type from the options provided: Monthly Membership, Yearly Membership, or One-Time Membership. This field is not required.
* **Client Details**: Enter your client details, including First Name, Last Name, and Email. This field is not required.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This field is not required, but it helps us to identify you efficiently.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is not required, but it helps us to contact you efficiently.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is not required, but it helps us to contact you efficiently.
* **Salon Membership Details (note)**: This field is not visible to the user, it is a note for internal use.
