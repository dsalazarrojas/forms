# Athletic Fitness Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the Athletic Fitness Membership Form, explaining each section and field to ensure a smooth and informed completion process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reviewing each section of the form, which is divided into categories for your convenience.
2.  Begin with the contact and personal information, where you will provide your name, email address, phone number, and residential address.
3.  In the fitness profile and background section, you will be asked about your current physical activity level, previous gym experience, and primary motivation for joining the club.
4.  The lifestyle and health section covers questions about your sleep habits, dietary preferences, and any chronic medical conditions or recent surgeries.
5.  Move on to the membership and commitment section, where you will select your desired training frequency, best time of day for training, and the type of membership plan that suits your needs.
6.  Finally, provide your electronic signature and the date of signature to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please provide your full name, as this will be used as your member ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication and membership updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact and appointment scheduling.
* **Residential Address** (`residential_address`, `text`, required): Provide your current residential address for membership verification.
* **Current Physical Activity Level** (`current_activity_level`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options:
  - Sedentary - Little to no exercise
  - Lightly Active - 1-2 days per week
  - Moderately Active - 3-5 days per week
  - Very Active - 6-7 days per week
* **Previous Gym or Fitness Club Experience** (`previous_gym_experience`, `text`, required): List any previous memberships or experiences with gyms or fitness clubs.
* **Primary Motivation for Joining** (`membership_motivation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
  - Weight Loss and Management
  - Muscle Gain and Hypertrophy
  - Cardiovascular Endurance
  - General Health and Wellness
  - Competition Preparation
  - Stress Relief and Mental Health
* **Current Body Weight - kg** (`current_weight`, `number`, required): Enter your current body weight in kilograms.
* **Current Standing Height - cm** (`current_height`, `number`, required): Enter your current height in centimeters.
* **Estimated Body Fat Percentage - %** (`body_fat_percentage`, `number`, required): Enter your estimated body fat percentage, if known.
* **Do you currently smoke or use tobacco products?** (`smoking_status`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options:
  - True
  - False
* **Average Hours of Sleep per Night** (`average_sleep`, `number`, required): Enter your average hours of sleep per night.
* **Primary Dietary Preference** (`dietary_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options:
  - Omnivore
  - Vegetarian
  - Vegan
  - Keto / Low-Carb
  - Paleo
  - Other
* **Do you have any chronic medical conditions or recent surgeries?** (`chronic_medical_conditions`, `text`, required): Enter any chronic medical conditions or recent surgeries.
* **Targeted Training Frequency - Sessions per Week** (`training_frequency_goal`, `number`, required): Select your desired training frequency per week.
* **Best Time of Day for Training** (`best_training_time`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options:
  - Early Morning (before 9am)
  - Mid-day (11am to 2pm)
  - Evening (5pm to 8pm)
  - Late Night (after 9pm)
* **Membership Plan Selection** (`membership_plan_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options:
  - Basic - Facility Access Only
  - Premium - Classes Included
  - Elite - Personalized Coaching Included
* **Desired Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to start your membership.
* **Commit to following all safety guidelines and club rules** (`safety_guideline_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options:
  - I commit
  - Other
* **Member Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name for electronic signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`signing_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your electronic signature.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review each section and field carefully to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
* If you are unsure about any field or require further clarification, please contact the fitness club staff.
* This form is designed to create a personalized membership plan, so please provide honest and detailed information.
* The electronic signature will be used as a confirmation of your commitment to following club rules and safety guidelines.
