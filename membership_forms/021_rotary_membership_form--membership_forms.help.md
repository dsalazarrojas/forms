# Rotary Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for individuals to apply for Rotary membership. The application process collects contact and membership details to facilitate the evaluation and approval process. To ensure a smooth and efficient application process, please read this guide carefully before filling out the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your background and experience. Please keep it concise and within the character limit.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information or clarification.
* **Membership Details** (`membership_details`, text, optional): Please describe your interest in becoming a Rotary member, including your motivations and qualifications. Be as specific as possible to help us understand your fit with the organization.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This field is for your final confirmation that you have filled out all the required fields accurately. Please review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Tips
* Be thorough but concise in your answers to ensure a smooth and efficient evaluation process.
* Please use correct email address and phone number formats.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
