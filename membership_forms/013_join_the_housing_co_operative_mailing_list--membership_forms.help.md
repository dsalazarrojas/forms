# join_the_housing_co_operative_mailing_list - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to join the housing co-operative's mailing list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, How did you hear about us?, How can we contact you?, Mailing List, Email Frequency, Email Format, and Language.
2. Choose your preferred Email Format (HTML or Plain text) and Email Frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
3. Select your preferred Language (English, Spanish, or French).
4. Decide on how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
5. Optionally, add any additional Comments.
6. Review your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **How did you hear about us?** (Select One, required): Choose how you found out about the housing co-operative (Social Media, Word of Mouth, Online Search, or Other).
* **How can we contact you?** (Select One, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mail, or Other).
* **Mailing List** (Select One, required): Choose whether you'd like to join our mailing list (True or False).
* **Comments** (Text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your subscription.
* **Email Frequency** (Select One, required): Choose how frequently you'd like to receive emails (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Email Format** (Select One, required): Choose the format of your emails (HTML or Plain text).
* **Language** (Select One, required): Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, or French).
* **How can we send you emails?** (Select One, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (True, False, or Other).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that you receive emails successfully.
* If you're already subscribed to our mailing list, you will not be able to submit your information successfully.
* If you have any issues with submitting the form, please contact our administration team.
