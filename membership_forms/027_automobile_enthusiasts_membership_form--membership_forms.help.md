# Automobile Enthusiasts Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automobile Enthusiasts membership form is designed to collect information from potential members to better understand their interests and demographics. This information will be used to tailor events and activities to the club's members' needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your address, including street address, city, and zip code.
3. Select your membership level (Basic, Premium, Platinum, or VIP).
4. Choose your membership duration (3 months, 6 months, 1 year, or 2 years).
5. Set your annual membership fee.
6. Indicate your interests and preferences, such as vehicle types and favorite brands.
7. Select any vehicles you own, including primary and other vehicles.
8. Choose activities you are interested in, such as car shows, track days, and social events.
9. Set your membership start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Fill in your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
- **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
- **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
- **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
- **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, select_one, required): Choose your membership level (Basic, Premium, Platinum, or VIP).
- **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, select_one, required): Choose your membership duration (3 months, 6 months, 1 year, or 2 years).
- **Annual Membership Fee** (`annual_fee`, number, required): Enter your annual membership fee.
- **Vehicle Types of Interest** (`vehicle_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply (Classic Cars, Muscle Cars, Sports Cars, Luxury Vehicles, Motorcycles, Trucks, Off-Road Vehicles, Electric Vehicles).
- **Favorite Automobile Brands** (`favorite_brands`, text, optional): List your favorite automobile brands.
- **Primary Vehicle Year** (`primary_vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of your primary vehicle.
- **Primary Vehicle Make and Model** (`primary_vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make and model of your primary vehicle.
- **Other Vehicles Owned** (`other_vehicles`, text, optional): Describe any additional vehicles you own.
- **Interested in Club Activities** (`interested_activities`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply (Car Shows, Track Days, Road Trips, Charity Events, Workshop and Training, Social Events, Online Forums).
- **Membership Start Date** (`membership_date`, date, required): Set your membership start date.
