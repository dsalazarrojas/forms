# nobility_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nobility membership application form is used to collect information from individuals seeking to join a noble organization. It is essential to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, one person per submission.
2. Provide your name and title as per the "New Member Name" and "Title" fields.
3. State your declaration of noble intent in the "Declaration of Noble Intent" field.
4. Select your noble order membership status and relationship to the noble family in the corresponding fields.
5. Provide your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
6. You may add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Member Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, ID).
* **Title** (2, text, required: false): Enter your official title or position (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Ms., Prof.).
* **Declaration of Noble Intent** (3, text, required: false): Briefly explain your reasons for applying for membership in the noble society.
* **Noble Order Member of** (4, select_one, required: false): Select "True" if you are a member of a noble order or "False" if you are not.
* **Noble Family Relationship** (5, text, required: false): Describe your relationship to a noble family member (e.g., parent, sibling, spouse).
* **Noble Society Member of** (6, select_multiple, required: false): Select any noble societies you are a member of (e.g., other, other).
* **Nobility Application Status** (7, select_one, required: false): Select your current application status (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Additional Comments** (8, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or information you think would be helpful for the noble society to review your application.
* **Email** (9, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (10, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Do not submit on behalf of someone else, as this will result in rejection of your application.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the noble society's administration.
