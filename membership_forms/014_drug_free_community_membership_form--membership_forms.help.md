# Drug Free Community Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drug Free Community Membership Form is designed to collect membership information from individuals interested in joining a community that promotes a drug-free lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Membership Form" section on the first page with your basic details.
2. Provide your member details on the "Member Details" section on the second page.
3. Click on "Submission" on the third page to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Form** (Membership Form, text, required/optional): This is the first page where you can enter your basic information such as name, address, and contact details.
* **Member Details** (Member Details, text, required/optional): On this page, please provide any additional member information, such as your interests or hobbies, to help the community administrators get a better understanding of your background.
* **Submission** (Submission, text, required/optional): This is the final page where you can review your application before submitting it to the community administrators for review. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before clicking on "Submission".

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the community's rules and regulations before applying for membership.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for clarification or support.
