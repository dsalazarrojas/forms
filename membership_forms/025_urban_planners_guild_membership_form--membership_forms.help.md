# urban_planners_guild_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The urban planners guild membership form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to become members of the urban planners guild. This form is used to gather necessary details such as name, email, phone number, address, experience, member type, and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: "Name", "Email", "Address", "Organization", and "Submit".
2. You may optionally provide your phone number, member type, experience, and additional notes.
3. For the repeat fields, select all applicable options for "Member Types", "Organization Repeat", "Address Repeat", and "Experience Years Repeat".
4. Make sure to review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Details (Page 1)
* **Member Details (id: 1)**: Provide your member details, including name and other relevant information.
* **Name (id: 2)**: Enter your name as it is spelled.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address (id: 5)**: Enter your address for mail or other communications.
* **Date of Birth (id: 6)**: Enter your date of birth.
### Experience (Page 2)
* **Experience Years (id: 7)**: Provide your years of experience in urban planning.
* **Member Type (id: 8)**: Select your type of member, either "Member", "Non-Member", or other.
* **Member Type Options (id: 14)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Member Type" and can be ignored.
* **Experience Years Repeat (id: 18)**: If you have multiple years of experience, select all applicable options.
* **Experience Years Repeat 2 (id: 23)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Experience Years Repeat" and can be ignored.
### Organization (Page 3)
* **Organization (id: 9)**: Provide your organization information.
* **Organization Repeat (id: 16)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Organization" and can be ignored.
* **Organization Repeat 2 (id: 25)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Organization Repeat" and can be ignored.
### Additional Information (Page 4)
* **Member Details Repeat (id: 11)**: Select all applicable options for "Member Details".
* **Member Details Repeat 2 (id: 22)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Member Details Repeat" and can be ignored.
* **Note (id: 10)**: Provide any additional notes about your membership.
* **Note Repeat (id: 19)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Note" and can be ignored.
* **Note Repeat 2 (id: 24)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Note Repeat" and can be ignored.
### Submission
* **Submit (id: 12)**: Review and submit your form to complete the membership process.
* **Submit 2 (id: 20)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Submit" and can be ignored.
* **Cancel (id: 13)**: Cancel the form to reset the fields.
* **Cancel Repeat (id: 21)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Cancel" and can be ignored.
### Member Type Selection
* **Member Types (id: 15)**: Select all applicable member types.
* **Member Types (id: 22)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Member Types" and can be ignored.
### Address Selection
* **Address Repeat (id: 17)**: Select all applicable addresses.
* **Address Repeat (id: 20)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of "Address Repeat" and can be ignored.
