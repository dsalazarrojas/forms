# Learning And Development Society Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals to join the Learning And Development Society. Please fill out the form to become a member and receive access to exclusive resources and events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Make sure to complete all required fields. If a field is marked as optional, feel free to leave it blank if you don't want to provide that information.
2. **Choose your membership type**: Select "Member" if you want to join the society and receive member benefits, or "Non-Member" if you only want to stay informed about society events and resources.
3. **Select your membership level**: Choose from "High" or "Low" membership level, depending on your preferences.
4. **Enter your details**: Fill out your first name, last name, email, date of birth, and phone number (if desired).
5. **Add any additional comments or message**: If you have any questions or would like to provide more information, use the message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email**: Your email address, where we will contact you with updates and events.
* **Date of Birth**: Your birthdate, which we will use for demographic purposes only.
* **Membership Type**: Choose between "Member" and "Non-Member" to determine your relationship with the society.
* **Membership Level**: Select "High" for premium benefits or "Low" for standard benefits.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address, if desired.
* **Phone**: Your phone number, if desired.
* **Message**: Any additional comments or questions you have for us.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you are already a member, select "Yes" if you are assigned a tool or "No" if not. If you are new, please select "No" as you will be assigned a tool after joining.
