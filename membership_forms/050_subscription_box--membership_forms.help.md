# Subscription Box - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscription Box form is used to collect information from members about their subscription preferences, including their subscription box ID, name, email, subscription plan, and shipping address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **subscription_box_id**, a unique identifier for your subscription box.
2. Enter your **name**, a brief description of your subscription box.
3. Enter your **email**, a valid email address associated with your subscription box.
4. Select your **subscription_plan**, either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are subscribed to a plan.
5. Select your **subscription_frequency**, either "Yes" or "No" to indicate how often you want to receive your subscription box.
6. Select your **subscription_frequency_options**, a list of frequency options (e.g., "Yes", "No") to further specify your subscription preferences.
7. Select your **shipping_address**, a list of shipping options (e.g., "Yes", "No") to determine shipping details.
8. Enter any additional **notes** about your subscription box.
9. Select your **assigned_tool**, a text field for any tools assigned to your subscription box.
10. Select your **category**, either "Yes" or "No" to categorize your subscription box.
11. Enter a brief **description** of your subscription box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **subscription_box_id**: A unique identifier for your subscription box.
* **name**: A brief description of your subscription box.
* **email**: A valid email address associated with your subscription box.
* **subscription_plan**: Select "Yes" if you are subscribed to a plan, "No" otherwise.
* **subscription_frequency**: Select "Yes" if you want to receive your subscription box frequently, "No" otherwise.
* **subscription_frequency_options**: Select multiple options (e.g., "Yes", "No") to further specify your subscription preferences.
* **shipping_address**: Select multiple shipping options (e.g., "Yes", "No") to determine shipping details.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes about your subscription box.
* **assigned_tool**: Enter a text description of any tool assigned to your subscription box.
* **category**: Select "Yes" if you want to categorize your subscription box, "No" otherwise.
* **description**: Enter a brief description of your subscription box.
