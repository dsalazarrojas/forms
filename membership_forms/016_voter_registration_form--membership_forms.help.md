# Voter Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect voter registration information from citizens and non-citizens of the area. This form is used to create a new voter registration or update an existing one.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Select how you would like to register (as a Citizen or Non-Citizen).
2. Enter your First Name, Middle Name, and Last Name in the respective text fields.
3. Enter your Email address to receive important updates or confirm your registration.
4. Enter your Date of Birth (in the format MM/DD/YYYY) for age verification.
5. Enter your Birth Date (in the format MM/DD/YYYY) to verify your birth date.
6. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
7. If you are updating your information, please provide a reason for the update.
8. If necessary, enter any additional information in the "Update Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Register As** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you are a Citizen or Non-Citizen.
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Middle Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your middle name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for important updates and confirmations.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY for age verification.
* **Birth Date** (Date, Optional): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Update Reason** (Text, Optional): If updating your information, provide a reason for the update.
* **Update Info** (Text, Optional): If necessary, enter any additional information for the update.
