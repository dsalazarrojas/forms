# Membership Form Of Young Revolutionary Student's Association - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect membership information from individuals who wish to join the Young Revolutionary Student's Association. Please provide the required information to complete the membership process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your membership application.
2. Provide a description of your application.
3. Choose the assigned tool for your membership.
4. (Optional) Provide additional information in the fields labeled "description_1" to "description_19".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`): Choose the category that best describes your membership application.
  * Membership Forms
  * Other

* **Description** (`description`, `text`): Describe your membership application in this field.
  * This field is required to be filled out.

* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`): Choose the assigned tool for your membership.
  * chatjimmy
  * Other

* **Form ID** (`form_ids`, `text`): You can provide additional information in this field if needed (optional).

* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`): You can provide additional information in this field if needed (optional).

* **Description 1-19** (`description_1` to `description_19`, `text`): These fields are optional and can be used to provide additional information about your membership application.
  * Please use these fields to provide any relevant details that are not covered in the "Description" field.
