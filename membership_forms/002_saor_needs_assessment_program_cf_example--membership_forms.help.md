# saor_needs_assessment_program_cf_example - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "saor_needs_assessment_program_cf_example" form is a template designed to collect descriptive information about a program or initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of your program in the "Program Description" field.
2. (Optional) Upload an image that represents your program in the "Program Image" field.
3. (Optional) Upload an image that highlights the price or cost associated with your program in the "Price Card Image" field.
4. Enter a title for your program in the "Program Title" field.
5. (Optional) Enter an alternate title for your program in the "Alternate Program Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your program.
* **Program Image** (`program_image`, text, required: false): Upload an image that represents your program.
* **Price Card Image** (`price_card_image`, text, required: false): Upload an image that highlights the price or cost associated with your program.
* **Program Title** (`program_title`, text, required: false): Enter a title for your program.
* **Alternate Program Title** (`program_title_alt`, text, required: false): Enter an alternate title for your program.

## Tips
* Ensure that the fields are filled out accurately and consistently with your program's information.
* Use clear and concise language when providing descriptions and titles.
* Upload images that are relevant and high-quality to enhance the visual representation of your program.
