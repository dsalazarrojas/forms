# Direct Primary Care Membership Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Primary Care Membership Enrollment form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals enrolling in Direct Primary Care (DPC) membership. This form is not a contract or legal document and should only be filled out once.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Choose your plan selection from the options provided.
3. Select your member information as applicable.
4. Enter your address in the correct format.
5. Provide your contact information and email address for DPC management and communication purposes.
6. Enter your phone number for contact verification and communication.
7. Review your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Direct Primary Care Membership Enrollment** (`enrollment_form`): Enter your name and other basic information about yourself.
* **Plan Selection** (`plan_selection`): Select the plan that suits your needs from the provided options. Please note that this selection is not a commitment and can be updated later.
* **Member Information** (`member_info`): Select any member benefits or services you wish to be part of. If you're unsure, you can select "No" for all options.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your physical address for DPC management and communication purposes. Please use the correct format (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345").
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your name and phone number for DPC staff to contact you for updates, questions, or issues.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for DPC updates, newsletters, or important notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number for DPC staff to contact you directly. This will help ensure timely communication and updates.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays or issues with your membership.
* Use a valid email address that you check regularly to receive important DPC notifications.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask DPC staff for guidance.
* You will receive a confirmation email after successful submission. Review and keep it for your records.
