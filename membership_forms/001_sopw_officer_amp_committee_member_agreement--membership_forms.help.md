# SOPW Officer & Committee Member Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by SOPW (Special Operations Planning Workshop) committee members, including officers, to document their agreement on various matters related to the committee's activities and decisions. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all members are aware of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
	* Member Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Agreement
	* Date
	* Time
	* Signature
	* Member Title
	* Officer Title
	* Role
	* Agreement Date
	* Agreement Time
	* Officer Agreement
	* Committee Agreement
	* Member Agreement
	* Officer Agreement Date
	* Officer Agreement Time
	* Committee Agreement Date
	* Committee Agreement Time
	* Member Agreement Signature
	* Officer Agreement Signature
	* Committee Agreement Signature
	* Agreement Status (choose "Accepted" or "Rejected")
	* Date Submitted

2. Fill in the optional field, which is:
	* Reason for Rejection (only required if "Agreement Status" is set to "Rejected")

3. Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Name
* **Member Name**: A text field to enter the name of the individual member.
*   **Email**: An email field to input the member's email address.
*   **Phone**: A text field to enter the member's phone number.
*   **Agreement**: A note field for the member to express their agreement on the matters at hand.
*   **Date**: A date field to input the date of the agreement.
*   **Time**: A time field to input the time of the agreement.
*   **Signature**: A text field for the member to sign their name and confirm their agreement.
*   **Member Title**: A text field to enter the member's title within the organization.
*   **Officer Title**: A text field to enter the officer's title within the organization.
*   **Role**: A text field to define the member's role within the committee.
*   **Agreement Date**: A date field to record the date of the agreement.
*   **Agreement Time**: A time field to record the time of the agreement.
*   **Officer Agreement**: A note field to capture the officer's agreement.
*   **Committee Agreement**: A note field to capture the committee's agreement.
*   **Member Agreement**: A note field to capture the member's agreement.
*   **Officer Agreement Date**: A date field to record the officer's agreement date.
*   **Officer Agreement Time**: A time field to record the officer's agreement time.
*   **Committee Agreement Date**: A date field to record the committee's agreement date.
*   **Committee Agreement Time**: A time field to record the committee's agreement time.
*   **Member Agreement Signature**: A text field for the member to sign their agreement.
*   **Officer Agreement Signature**: A text field for the officer to sign their agreement.
*   **Committee Agreement Signature**: A text field for the committee to sign their agreement.
*   **Agreement Status**: A dropdown list to select the status of the agreement (Accepted, Rejected, or Pending).
*   **Reason for Rejection**: A text field to provide a reason for rejecting the agreement (only required if "Agreement Status" is set to "Rejected").
*   **Date Submitted**: A date field to record the date the form was submitted.

### Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Make sure to input accurate and complete information in all fields.
- If the agreement is rejected, provide a clear reason for rejection.
