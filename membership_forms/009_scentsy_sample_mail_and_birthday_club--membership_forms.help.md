# Scentsy Sample Mail And Birthday Club - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scentsy Sample Mail And Birthday Club form is designed to collect demographic information from customers who wish to sign up for the club. This information will be used to tailor mailings and birthday messages to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification or official documents.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification or official documents.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Please enter a phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Birthday** (`form_birthday`, date, optional): Please enter your birthday in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Interests** (`form_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all interests that apply.
	+ Yes: You are interested in receiving mailings and birthday messages.
	+ No: You do not want to receive mailings and birthday messages.
* **Subscription** (`form_subscription`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the subscription options.
	+ Option 1: This is the default option.
	+ Option 2: This is an alternative option.
	+ Option 3: This is another option.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, note, optional): Please enter any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Select all interests that apply.
* Make sure to select only one subscription option.
* Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.
