# Alumni Association Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from alumni to manage their membership status and provide them with relevant updates and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details such as full name, graduation year, and email address.
2. Provide your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
3. Enter your degree or program, if applicable.
4. Specify your current affiliation (e.g., employer or city).
5. Choose your membership type (Standard, Lifetime, or Student).
6. Select all areas of interest that apply to you (e.g., Networking, Events, Mentoring, Fundraising).
7. Add any additional comments or notes, if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Graduation year** (graduation_year, number, required): Enter the year you graduated.
* **Email address** (email, email, required): Enter your preferred contact email address.
* **Phone number** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Degree or program** (degree_program, text, optional): Enter your degree or program if you have graduated (e.g., BA Biology).
* **Current affiliation** (current_affiliation, text, optional): Enter your current employer or city.
* **Membership type** (membership_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of membership you are applying for (e.g., Standard, Lifetime, Student).
* **Areas of interest** (areas_of_interest, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas of interest that apply to you (e.g., Networking, Events, Mentoring, Fundraising).
* **Additional comments** (comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
