# Gift Ideas For Loved Ones - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find the perfect gift for your loved ones by gathering information about their name, age, interests, and budget preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the recipient's name in the "Recipient Name" field.
2. Enter the recipient's age in the "Age" field.
3. Select the recipient's interests in the "Interests" field. You can choose one or more of the options provided.
4. If you know the recipient's budget, you can enter it in the "Budget" field. 
5. Select the recipient's preferred budget type (e.g., Cash, Card, or Gift) in the "Budget Type" field.
6. In the "Gift Suggestion" field, type any additional details or gift ideas you have for the recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipient Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the person you are buying a gift for.
* **Age** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the recipient's age.
* **Interests** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the recipient's interests (e.g., Gift Ideas, Hobby).
* **Budget** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the recipient's budget (optional).
* **Budget Type** (Select One, Required): Select the recipient's preferred budget type (e.g., Cash, Card, Gift).
* **Gift Suggestion** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide any additional information or gift ideas you have for the recipient.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information about the recipient to get the best gift ideas.
- If you're unsure about the recipient's interests or budget, you can leave those fields blank.
- You can always come back and edit the form if you need to make changes or add more information later.
