# Party Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Invitation Form is used to create a party invitation for a social gathering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of party you are hosting by choosing from the options in the "Party Type" field.
2. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
3. Enter the guest's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the guest's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the party in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Party Type**: (1, select_one): Choose the type of party you are hosting (Dinner Party, Pool Party, Baby Shower, etc.).
  - This field is used to determine the type of party and will help us better understand the nature of the event.
- **Guest Name**: (2, text): Enter the guest's name.
  - This field is required and should be filled in with the guest's name.
- **Address**: (3, text): Enter the guest's address.
  - This field is required and should be filled in with the guest's address.
- **Phone**: (4, text): Enter the guest's phone number.
  - This field is required and should be filled in with the guest's phone number.
- **Email**: (5, email): Enter the guest's email address.
  - This field is required and should be filled in with the guest's email address.
- **Notes**: (6, note): Add any additional notes about the party (optional).
  - This field is optional and can be used to add any extra information about the party.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required fields (Guest Name, Address, Phone, and Email).
- Use a valid email address for the "Email" field.
- Use a valid phone number for the "Phone" field.
- Be specific about the type of party in the "Party Type" field.
