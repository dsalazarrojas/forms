# Employee Change Of Address Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report a change of address for an employee. Please provide the required information to update the employee's address in the system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's **First Name** and **Last Name**.
2. Enter the **Old Address** where the employee used to live.
3. Enter the **New Address** where the employee lives now.
4. Provide a **Reason** for the address change (optional).
5. Select the **Date of Change**, when the address change took place.
6. Select the **Date of Notification**, when the employee notified the management of the address change.
7. Enter the **Employee ID** of the employee who changed their address.
8. Enter the **Manager ID** of the employee's manager.
9. Select the **Department** where the employee works.
10. Select the **Teams** the employee is part of (optional).
11. Enter the employee's **Email** (optional).
12. Enter the employee's **Phone** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Type the employee's first name.
* **Last Name**: Type the employee's last name.
* **Old Address**: Enter the employee's old home address.
* **New Address**: Enter the employee's new home address.
* **Reason**: Provide a reason for the address change (optional).
* **Date of Change**: Select the date when the address change took place.
* **Date of Notification**: Select the date when the employee notified the management of the address change.
* **Employee ID**: Type the Employee ID number of the employee who changed their address.
* **Manager ID**: Type the ID number of the employee's manager.
* **Department**: Select the Department where the employee works (e.g. Marketing, Operations, Sales).
* **Teams**: Select the teams the employee is part of (e.g. Team 1, Team 2, Team 3).
* **Email**: Type the employee's email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Type the employee's phone number (optional).
