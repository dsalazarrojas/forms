# Babysitter Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitter Information Form is a tool used by parents or guardians to gather essential information from potential babysitters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the sitter's full name in the "Babysitter name" field.
2. Provide your mobile number in the "Babysitter phone" field.
3. Optional: Enter your email address in the "Babysitter email" field.
4. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact for the sitter in the "Emergency contact for sitter" field.
5. If applicable, enter your years of experience in the "Years of experience" field.
6. Select any relevant certifications you hold from the dropdown menu in the "Certifications held" field.
7. Enter your availability in the "Availability days and times" field.
8. Provide references' contact details in the "References contact details" field.
9. Indicate your consent for a background check in the "Consent for background check" field.
10. Share any additional information you think is relevant in the "Additional information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Babysitter name**: Enter your full name.
* **Babysitter phone**: Provide your mobile number.
* **Babysitter email** (Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency contact for sitter**: Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact for you.
* **Years of experience**: Enter your years of experience as a babysitter.
* **Certifications held**: Select any certifications you hold that are relevant to your role as a babysitter.
	+ **CPR**: You hold CPR certification.
	+ **First aid**: You hold first aid certification.
	+ **Childcare certificate**: You hold a childcare certificate.
* **Availability days and times**: Enter your available weekdays and evenings.
* **References contact details**: Provide contact details for two references.
* **Consent for background check**: Indicate whether you consent for a background check.
* **Additional information**: Share any additional information you think is relevant to your role as a babysitter.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Please be aware that some fields are marked as required, which means you must provide information for these fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the form administrator for clarification.
