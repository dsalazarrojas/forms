# solo_travel_sign_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The solo travel sign-out form is designed to collect and track information about solo travelers, allowing travel managers to quickly access essential details about their trip, including contact information, travel dates, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the traveler's details, including their name and any additional information as required.
2. Provide contact information for the traveler, including a contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
3. Enter the travel dates, including the travel date and return date.
4. Specify the traveler's mode of transportation and travel duration.
5. Add any additional travel notes or comments.
6. Enter the emergency contact's information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
7. Select the relationship of the emergency contact.
8. Choose the travel agency used for the trip.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Traveler details**: Enter the traveler's name and any additional information as required.
- **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for the traveler.
- **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number for the contact person.
- **Email**: Enter an email address for the contact person.
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
- **Contact Person Phone**: Enter the phone number for the emergency contact person.
- **Contact Person Email**: Enter the email address for the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact person (e.g., Parent, Friend, Family Member, Other).
- **Travel Mode**: Choose the mode of transportation used for the trip (e.g., Car, Train, Bus, Air, Other).
- **Travel Duration**: Enter the length of the trip in hours or minutes.
- **Travel Distance**: Enter the distance traveled in kilometers or miles.
- **Travel Status**: Select the status of the trip (Confirmed, In Progress, Canceled, Unknown).
- **Travel Note**: Add any additional information about the trip.
- **Travel Agency**: Select the travel agency used for the trip (e.g., Agency A, Agency B, Agency C, Agency D, Agency E).
- **Travel Agency ID**: Enter the ID of the travel agency used.
- **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the trip.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the travel manager for assistance.
- This form is used to track and manage travel information for solo travelers.
