# Condolence Message Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow individuals to submit their condolences and memories to the family of the deceased. This is a way for you to share your thoughts, prayers, or stories about the person who has passed away, and to offer support and love to the family during this difficult time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the bottom of this page.
2. Enter your name and email address (if you wish to provide it) in the respective fields.
3. Select your relationship to the deceased from the drop-down menu.
4. Share a message of condolence in the message field, if you wish.
5. If you have a shared memory or story, you can share it in the "Shared Memory or Story" field.
6. If you have a prayer or poem, you can share it in the "Prayer, Poem, or Verse" field.
7. Decide whether you wish to have your message shared publicly on the memorial page.
8. If you wish to mention a donation made in memory, you can do so in the respective field.
9. Let us know if you would like the family to be able to contact you.
10. If you wish to provide a contact phone number, you can do so in the respective field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`sender_name`, `text`, `required`): This is where you enter your name, which will be associated with your message.
* **Relationship to the Deceased** (`relationship_to_deceased`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the relationship you have to the deceased, such as family, friend, or colleague.
* **Your Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, `required`: no): This is an optional field for you to enter your email address, which will be used for administrative purposes only. 
* **Message of Condolence** (`condolence_message`, `text`, `required`): This is where you share your message of condolence, thoughts, and prayers.
* **Shared Memory or Story** (`shared_memory`, `text`, `required`: no): Feel free to share a special moment or anecdote about the deceased.
* **Prayer, Poem, or Verse** (`prayer_or_verse`, `text`, `required`: no): This is an optional field to share a prayer, poem, or verse.
* **Permission to Share** (`permission_to_share`, `select_one`, `required`): Please decide whether you wish to have your message shared publicly on the memorial page.
* **Donation Made** (`donation_made`, `text`, `required`: no): If applicable, please mention a donation made in memory of the deceased.
* **Contact Request** (`contact_request`, `select_one`, `required`: no): Let us know if you would like the family to be able to contact you.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, `required`: no): If you requested contact, please provide your phone number.
