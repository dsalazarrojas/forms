# Memory Book Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Memory Book Submission Form is a form used to collect users' stories, photos, and other relevant information about their experiences and memories.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, please select "Tell me a story" (id: 1) and type or paste your story in the text field.
2. If you have any photos related to your story, you can select the "Upload a photo" (id: 2) option and choose from the options provided.
3. Enter the date (id: 3) of the experience or memory you are submitting, if applicable.
4. Enter the time (id: 4) of the experience or memory you are submitting, if applicable.
5. If you have another story to share, type or paste it in the "Another Story" (id: 5) field.
6. If you have another photo to share, select the "Another Photo" (id: 6) option and choose from the options provided.
7. Enter the Name (id: 7), Phone Number (id: 8), and Email (id: 9) of the recipient of the experience or memory you are submitting.
8. Add any additional Notes (id: 10, id: 20) you have about the experience or memory.
9. If the recipient has an address, enter the Address (id: 12), Country (id: 13), Region (id: 14), City (id: 15), State (id: 16), and ZIP (id: 17) fields.
10. Enter the Phone (id: 18) number of the recipient, if applicable.
11. Enter the Email Address (id: 19) of the recipient, if applicable.
12. You can add any additional Notes 2 (id: 20) if needed.
13. Finally, click on the "Submit" (id: 25) button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tell me a story (id: 1)**: A text field for users to type or paste their story about the experience or memory they are submitting.
* **Upload a photo (id: 2)**: A dropdown select field that allows users to choose a photo to upload related to their story.
* **Date (id: 3)**: A date field for users to enter the date of the experience or memory they are submitting.
* **Time (id: 4)**: A time field for users to enter the time of the experience or memory they are submitting.
* **Another Story (id: 5)**: A text field for users to type or paste another story about the experience or memory they are submitting.
* **Another Photo (id: 6)**: A select multiple field that allows users to choose multiple photos related to their another story.
* **Name (id: 7)**: A text field for users to enter the name of the recipient.
* **Phone Number (id: 8)**: A text field for users to enter the phone number of the recipient.
* **Email (id: 9)**: An email field for users to enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Notes (id: 10)**: A note field for users to add any additional notes about the experience or memory they are submitting.
* **Name of Recipient (id: 11)**: A text field for users to enter the name of the recipient.
* **Address (id: 12)**: A text field for users to enter the address of the recipient.
* **Country (id: 13)**: A text field for users to enter the country of the recipient.
* **Region (id: 14)**: A text field for users to enter the region of the recipient.
* **City (id: 15)**: A text field for users to enter the city of the recipient.
* **State (id: 16)**: A text field for users to enter the state of the recipient.
* **ZIP (id: 17)**: A text field for users to enter the ZIP of the recipient.
* **Phone (id: 18)**: A text field for users to enter the phone number of the recipient.
* **Email Address (id: 19)**: An email field for users to enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Notes 2 (id: 20)**: A note field for users to add any additional notes.
* **Name 2 (id: 21)**: A text field for users to enter the name of the recipient.
* **Phone 2 (id: 22)**: A text field for users to enter the phone number of the recipient.
* **Email 2 (id: 23)**: An email field for users to enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Notes 3 (id: 24)**: A note field for users to add any additional notes.
* **Submit (id: 25)**: A text field for users to click on to submit their form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any issues with uploading photos, ensure you have the necessary permissions and that the file size is within the limit.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting.
