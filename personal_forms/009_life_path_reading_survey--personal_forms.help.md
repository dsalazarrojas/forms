# Life Path Reading Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Life Path Reading Survey is a form designed to gather personal details from users for a life path reading session. This form is intended for users who are seeking guidance on their life path, and it is not for commercial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please fill out all the required fields (none of the fields are marked as required, but please make sure to fill out all the fields that you are comfortable sharing with us).
* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your birth date in the "Birth Date" field (in the YYYY-MM-DD format).
* In the "Reading Details" field, please provide any relevant information that you would like to share about your life path reading session.
* Choose your preference for "Reading Preferences" by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
* Choose your preferences for "Client Preferences" by selecting multiple options from the list.
* In the "Reading Insights" field, please provide any insights or thoughts that you gained from the reading session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Reading Details** (`reading_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information that you would like to share about your life path reading session.
* **Reading Preferences** (`reading_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose your preference for this reading session by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferences for this reading session by selecting multiple options from the list.
* **Reading Insights** (`reading_insights`, text, optional): Provide any insights or thoughts that you gained from the reading session.
