# Personal Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Liability Waiver form is used to collect essential information from clients about their medical conditions, emergency contacts, and other relevant details before participating in training sessions or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "client_name" field. Please ensure that the name is spelled correctly.
2.  Provide your emergency contact's name in the "emergency_contact_name" field.
3.  Describe your relationship with the emergency contact in the "client_relationship" field.
4.  Provide the emergency contact's information, including phone number and email, in the respective fields.
5.  If applicable, describe any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your participation in the training session.
6.  If you have medical insurance, provide the necessary information in the "medical_insurance" field.
7.  Finally, submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **client_name** (Required: False)
Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **emergency_contact_name** (Required: False)
Provide the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.

### **client_relationship** (Required: True)
Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse, etc.).

### **emergency_contact_info** (Required: False)
Provide the contact information (phone number, email, or address) for the emergency contact.

### **medical_info** (Required: False)
Describe any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your participation in the training session.

### **emergency_contact_phone** (Required: False)
Enter the emergency contact's phone number.

### **medical_insurance** (Required: False)
If you have medical insurance, provide the necessary information (policy number, provider, etc.).

### **emergency_contact_email** (Required: False)
Enter the emergency contact's email address.

### **emergency_contact_relationship** (Required: False)
Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., child, parent, etc.).
