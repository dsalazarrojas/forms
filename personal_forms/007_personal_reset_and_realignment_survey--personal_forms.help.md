# Personal Reset And Realignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Reset And Realignment Survey is a tool to help you reset and realign your personal goals, coaching, and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please note that some questions are optional, and you can skip them if not applicable.
3. Use the text fields to provide detailed answers.
4. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more options that apply to you.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Reset And Realignment Survey Page 1** (`Personal Reset And Realignment Survey Page 1`, text, optional): This page is for you to introduce yourself and provide any context or background information related to your goals and priorities.
- **Coaching** (`Coaching`, select_one, optional): Please answer "Yes" if you have had coaching in the past or "No" if not.
- **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, text, optional): Describe your coaching goals, if any.
- **Reflection Insights** (`reflection_insights`, text, optional): Write down any insights you have gained from your reflection, if any.
- **Coaching Priorities** (`coaching_priorities`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the following items apply to you:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Describe your next steps or actions planed for your personal reset and realignment.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by individual users, and the information gathered will be used to help them reset and realign their personal goals, coaching, and priorities.
