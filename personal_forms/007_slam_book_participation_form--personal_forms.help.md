# Slam Book Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and comments from users about a class or event, allowing for the gathering of personal details, contact information, and any additional messages or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Select your class from the **Class** field.
4. Enter your school name in the **School** field.
5. If you have any fun details or interesting facts, enter them in the **Fun detail** field.
6. You can leave a message for the teacher in the **Message to Teacher** field.
7. You can also leave a message for the class in the **Message to Class** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
9. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
10. Enter your physical address in the **Address** field.
11. Enter your zip code in the **Zip** field.
12. Enter your city in the **City** field.
13. Enter your state or province in the **State** field.
14. Enter your country in the **Country** field.
15. If you have any other comments or messages, enter them in the **Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Class** (`user_class`, text, optional): Select your class or group.
* **School** (`user_school`, text, optional): Enter your school name.
* **Fun detail** (`fun_detail`, text, optional): If you have any interesting or fun details, enter them here.
* **Message to Teacher** (`message_to_teacher`, text, optional): Leave a message for your teacher.
* **Message to Class** (`message_to_class`, text, optional): Leave a message for your class.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`user_address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Zip** (`user_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`user_city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`user_state`, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Country** (`user_country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Message** (`user_message`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or messages, enter them here.
