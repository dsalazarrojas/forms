# Marriage Boundaries Journal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you reflect on and track your relationship boundaries and dynamics with your partner. It's a journaling tool for you to explore and understand your feelings, needs, and behaviors in your marriage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest with yourself and your partner about your thoughts, feelings, and experiences.
3. Use this form as a tool for personal growth and self-awareness, rather than for formal documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Relationship** (`partner_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your partner, including any positive or negative patterns and dynamics.
* **Boundaries** (`boundaries`, text, optional): What are your boundaries and expectations for physical and emotional intimacy with your partner?
* **Emotions** (`emotions`, text, optional): How do you feel about the state of your relationship? Are there any specific emotions that are causing you distress or concern?
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): How do you communicate with your partner, and are there any areas where you feel misunderstood or unheard?
* **Conflict** (`conflict`, text, optional): Are there any areas of conflict or tension in your relationship? If so, describe them and how you've tried to address them.
* **Boundaries Conversation** (`boundaries_conversation`, text, optional): Have you talked to your partner about your boundaries and feelings? If not, consider using this form as a starting point for a conversation.
* **Feelings** (`feelings`, text, optional): How do you feel about your relationship, and are there any feelings that you're struggling to express or discuss with your partner?

## Tips
* Be honest and open with yourself and your partner.
* Use this form regularly to track your progress and changes in your relationship.
* Consider using this form as a conversation starter with your partner to discuss your feelings and boundaries.
* Remember that this form is for personal growth and self-awareness, not for formal documentation or diagnosis.
