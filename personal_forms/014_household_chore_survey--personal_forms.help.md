# Household Chore Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct a survey about household chores within a household. It is designed to help households understand who is responsible for each task, how tasks are distributed, and how fair each member finds the distribution of tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to each household member by choosing the name of the member from the dropdown menu.
2. For each household member, select the chores they are responsible for from the list of options.
3. For each task, rate the fairness of distribution by selecting "Fair", "Not Fair", or "Very Not Fair" from the options.
4. Provide a reason for your rating by typing in the text field.
5. Make any suggestions for improvement in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Member 1**: Select the name of the household member from the dropdown menu to choose the member for which you are reporting on.
* **Household Member 2**: Select the name of the second household member from the dropdown menu.
* **Household Member 3**: Select the name of the third household member from the dropdown menu.
* **What Chores Do You Take Care Of?**: Select all the chores you are responsible for from the list of options, such as Cooking, Cleaning, or Laundry.
* **Who Is Responsible For Each Task?**: Enter the names of the household members responsible for each task in this text field.
* **How Would You Rate The Fairness Of Each Task?**: Rate the fairness of each task by selecting "Fair", "Not Fair", or "Very Not Fair" from the options.
* **Reason For Your Rating**: Enter a brief explanation for your rating in this text field.
* **Do You Have Any Suggestions For Improvement?**: Enter any suggestions you have for improving the fairness and distribution of tasks in this text field.
