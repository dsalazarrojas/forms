The form is intended for users to request peer-to-peer payments from their colleagues, friends, or family. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected and documented to facilitate smooth and secure transactions. Before proceeding, ensure that you have the necessary authorization and approval to request a payment, and that this form is not a duplicate of an existing request.

# Peer To Peer Payment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a payment request between two individuals. Fill in your details to initiate a payment request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details as the payment requestor.
2. Select the date on which you would like to make the payment.
3. Enter the amount you would like to pay.
4. Choose the payment method (Online Payment, Offline Payment, or Other).
5. Enter the recipient's name.
6. Enter the recipient's email address (required for online payments).
7. (Optional) Enter the recipient's phone number.
8. Provide a brief message explaining the reason for the payment request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **payment_requester** (text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information as the payment requestor.
* **request_date** (date, required): Select the date on which you would like to make the payment.
* **amount** (number, required): Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **method** (select one, required): Choose the payment method you will use (Online Payment, Offline Payment, or Other).
* **recipient_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the recipient's name.
* **recipient_email** (email, required): Enter the recipient's email address, required for online payments.
* **recipient_phone** (text, optional): Enter the recipient's phone number (optional, but recommended for offline payments).
* **request_message** (text, required): Provide a brief message explaining the reason for the payment request.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the recipient has a valid email address for online payments.
* If using offline payments, ensure the recipient has a valid phone number or alternative contact information.
* Review the form carefully to ensure you have filled in all required fields.
