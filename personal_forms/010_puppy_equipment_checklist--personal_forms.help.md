# Puppy Equipment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your puppy's equipment needs. Please provide the necessary details to help us better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Basic Info" to enter general information about your puppy.
2. Click on "Supply Information" to enter specific supply needs for your puppy.
3. Click on "Crate" to enter details about your puppy's crate preferences.
4. Click on "Collar Size" to select the size of collar your puppy would need.
5. Click on "Harness Size" to select the size of harness your puppy would need.
6. Click on "Leash Length" to select the length of leash your puppy would need.
7. Click on "Phone" to enter your phone number.
8. Click on "Email" to enter your email address.
9. Click on "First Name" to enter your first name.
10. Click on "Last Name" to enter your last name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info (Basic Info)**:
	+ Enter your puppy's basic information, including any relevant details about your puppy.
* **Supply Information (Supply Information)**:
	+ Enter any specific supply needs your puppy would require.
* **Crate (Crate)**:
	+ Describe your puppy's crate preferences, including size and type.
* **Collar Size (Collar Size)**:
	+ Select the size of collar your puppy would need.
* **Harness Size (Harness Size)**:
	+ Select the size of harness your puppy would need.
* **Leash Length (Leash Length)**:
	+ Select the length of leash your puppy would need.
* **Phone (Phone)**:
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Email (Email)**:
	+ Enter your email address.
* **First Name (First Name)**:
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**:
	+ Enter your last name.

## Tips

* Please ensure you enter accurate and complete information about your puppy's needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* You can also use the help guide to double-check your entries before submitting the form.
