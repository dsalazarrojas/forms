# Best Movies Watchlist Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Best Movies Watchlist Checklist form is a tool for users to create and track their own watchlist of favorite movies. This form helps users to keep track of the movies they want to watch, have already watched, and rate them according to their preference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address (if you wish to share your watchlist with others).
2. Select the type of movie watchlist you are creating (e.g., Personal, Family, etc.).
3. Add movies to your watchlist by filling in the movie title, selecting the genre, and indicating whether you have watched it or not.
4. Rate each movie if you have already watched it.
5. Specify your preferred viewing method (e.g., Theater, Streaming at Home, etc.).
6. Choose your favorite streaming service.
7. Select your favorite movie genre.
8. Set a viewing goal for this month.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about your watchlist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address if you wish to share your watchlist with others. This is optional.
* **Type of Watchlist**: Select the type of movie watchlist you are creating (e.g., Personal, Family, etc.).
* **Movie Title 1**: Enter the title of the first movie on your watchlist.
* **Movie 1 Genre**: Select the genre of the first movie.
* **Movie 1 Watched**: Indicate whether you have watched the first movie.
* **Movie 1 Rating**: Rate the first movie if you have watched it.
* **Movie 2**: Enter the title of the second movie on your watchlist.
* **Movie 2 Genre**: Select the genre of the second movie.
* **Movie 2 Watched**: Indicate whether you have watched the second movie.
* **Movie 2 Rating**: Rate the second movie if you have watched it.
* **...**: Repeat the above steps for movies 3 to 5.
* **Preferred Viewing Method**: Choose how you usually watch movies (e.g., Theater, Streaming at Home, etc.).
* **Favorite Streaming Service**: Select your favorite platform to stream movies.
* **Favorite Genre**: Select your favorite movie genre.
* **Viewing Goals**: Set a viewing goal for this month.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Add any extra information about your watchlist.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field completely to ensure that your watchlist is accurate and up-to-date.
* You can add or remove movies as needed to fit your viewing goals.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
