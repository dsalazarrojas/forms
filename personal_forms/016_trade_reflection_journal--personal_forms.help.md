## trade reflection journal - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the date of the trade from the calendar or enter it manually.
2.  Choose the type of trade (e.g., Trade or Non-Trade).
3.  Write a brief description of the trade in the Trade Details field.
4.  Reflect on what you learned from the trade in the Trade Reflection field.
5.  List any action items from the trade in the Trade Action Items field.
6.  Add any additional notes about the trade in the Trade Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Trade Date** (`trade_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the trade. This field allows you to select the date of the trade using a calendar or enter it manually.
*   **Trade Type** (`trade_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of trade (e.g., Trade or Non-Trade). This field helps categorize the trade for easier analysis and reflection.
*   **Trade Details** (`trade_details`, text, required/optional): Write a brief description of the trade, including any relevant details. This field gives you space to document the specifics of the trade and what happened.
*   **Trade Reflection** (`trade_reflection`, text, required/optional): Reflect on what you learned from the trade, including any insights or takeaways. This field is for noting the lessons learned from the experience.
*   **Action Items** (`trade_action_items`, text, required/optional): List any action items or tasks resulting from the trade, such as changes to your process or future actions. This field helps you track the practical applications of the trade.
*   **Trade Notes** (`trade_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes or thoughts about the trade, including observations or questions. This field provides space for any extra context or information not captured in other fields.

## Tips

*   The form is designed to be flexible, so fill in only the fields that apply to your trade. You can leave fields blank if they don't have relevance to your experience.
*   Be as brief or as detailed as you need to be in each field, depending on your needs and the specifics of the trade.
*   Use the form as a tool for reflection and learning, rather than just for record-keeping. Think about what you can apply from the trade to your future experiences.
