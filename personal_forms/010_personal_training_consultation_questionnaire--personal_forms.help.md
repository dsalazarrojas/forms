# Personal Training Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to consult with personal trainers for guidance on their training goals and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide detailed and honest answers to the following questions to help us better understand your goals and current situation.
2. If any field is required, it must be filled out before proceeding to the next page.
3. Please note that some fields have specific formats or requirements for answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exercise Goals**: (`exercise_goals`, text, required)
	+ Please describe your short-term and long-term exercise goals. This could include specific fitness goals, such as weight loss, muscle gain, or improved endurance.
* **Injury History**: (`injury_history`, text, required)
	+ List any previous injuries or medical conditions that may impact your training.
* **Emergency Contact**: (`emergency_contact`, text, optional)
	+ Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person, in case of any medical concerns during training.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, required)
	+ Format: 123-456-7890
	+ Please enter your phone number so we can reach out to you with any important updates or concerns.
* **Email Address**: (`email_address`, email, optional)
	+ Format: user@example.com
	+ Please enter your email address so we can send you updates or reminders about your training schedule.
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Conditions**: (`medical_conditions`, text, required)
	+ List any current medical conditions that may impact your training, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or joint issues.
* **Training Experience**: (`training_experience`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the best description of your current training experience:
		+ Beginner
		+ Intermediate
		+ Advanced
* **Training Frequency**: (`training_frequency`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select the frequency of your current training:
		+ Daily
		+ 3 times a week
		+ 2-3 times a month
		+ Rarely
* **Injury Prevention**: (`injury_prevention`, note, required)
	+ Please provide any additional information about how you prevent injuries during training, such as stretching or proper form.
