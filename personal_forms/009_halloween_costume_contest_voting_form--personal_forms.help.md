# Halloween Costume Contest Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Costume Contest Voting Form is designed to allow users to vote for their favorite costumes in a fun and easy-to-use interface. The form collects the user's vote, their preferred contestant's name, email (if provided), phone number (if provided), and a brief explanation of why they like the costume.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite costume from the list. You can choose multiple options.
2. Enter the name of the contestant you are voting for.
3. If you have an email address associated with your name, enter it. This is optional.
4. If you have a phone number associated with your name, enter it. This is optional.
5. Choose why you think the costume is the best by selecting an option from the list.
6. Enter a brief explanation of why you like the costume.
7. Add any comments you'd like to share about the costume.
8. Click the "Submit" button to cast your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vote for the best costume**: Choose one or multiple options from the list of costumes provided. You can select multiple options if you think two or more costumes are equally amazing.
* **Contestant Name**: Enter the name of the contestant whose costume you are voting for. This will be associated with your email and phone number if you provide them.
* **Contestant's Email**: This is the email address you use to vote for your favorite costume. This is optional, but please make sure you have permission from the owner of the email address to use it in this form.
* **Contestant's Phone**: This is the phone number you use to vote for your favorite costume. This is optional, but please make sure you have permission from the owner of the phone number to use it in this form.
* **Cast Your Vote**: Choose why you think the costume is the best by selecting one of the options provided:
	+ Best Costume
	+ Best Performance
	+ Best of Show
* **Why do you like this costume?**: Enter a brief explanation of why you like the costume. This will help other users understand your selection.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments you'd like to share about the costume. This is optional, but can provide valuable feedback to the contestant.
* **Submit**: Click this button to cast your vote and submit your form.
