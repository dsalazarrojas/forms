# Personality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on various aspects of personality, emotional intelligence, and psychological traits. The form is composed of multiple pages, each with different questions related to these areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and understand the questions asked.
2. Answer each question by selecting the corresponding option (Yes or No).
3. Make sure to answer all questions on the pages that are marked as required.
4. Skip questions that are not relevant or necessary for your needs.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
