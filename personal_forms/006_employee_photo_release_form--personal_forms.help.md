# Employee Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the employee's written permission to take their photo for company-related purposes, such as for an employee directory or security badge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Fill out your first name and last name.
* Enter a brief description of the photo to be taken.
* Indicate whether you are releasing or not releasing your photo.
* If releasing, submit the form.
* Sign and date both pages of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Photo Description** (photo_description, text): Briefly describe the purpose of the photo (e.g., employee directory, security badge).
* **Photo Release** (employee_photo_release, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are releasing or not releasing your photo. If releasing, please select "True".
* **Submit** (submit, text, required): This is not a fill-in field, but rather a button to submit the form.
* **First Page Signature** (first_page_signature, date, required): Sign and date the first page of the form.
* **Second Page Signature** (second_page_signature, date, required): Sign and date the second page of the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are signing both pages to complete the form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form should be kept in your personnel file for future reference.
