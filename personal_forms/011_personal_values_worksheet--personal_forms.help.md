# Personal Values Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Personal Values Worksheet is a form designed to gather information about an individual's personal values, goals, and submission details. This form is intended to be a tool for self-reflection, progress tracking, or assessment in a coaching or counseling context.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, which are marked with a \*.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Select only relevant options that apply to you.
4. Ensure you have a stable internet connection before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Self Reflection** (`self_reflection`, `text`, required/optional): Reflect on your personal values, experiences, and thoughts in this text field.
* **Core Values** (`core_values`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the core values that are important to you, such as honesty, kindness, or fairness.
* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, `select_one`, required): Choose a specific goal that you are currently working towards.
* **Jotform Data** (`jotform_data`, `note`, optional): This is a note field for any additional information or comments that you may want to add.
* **Organized Submission** (`organized_submission`, `text`, optional): Describe your submission process and any organizational strategies you use.
* **Submission Tracking** (`submission_tracking`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last submission.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `time`, required): Record the time spent on your goal.
* **Email Notification** (`email_notification`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for notification purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Coach Counselor Support** (`coach_counselor_support`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of coach or counselor support you prefer.
* **Support Resources** (`support_resources`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any support resources that you have access to.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): Record the time of your submission.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about your submission.
* **Followup Questions** (`followup_questions`, `text`, optional): List any follow-up questions or concerns you have.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, optional): Describe any next steps you plan to take.
* **Review Form** (`review_form`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a review option for your submission.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments for review.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Confirm your submission.
* **Back** (`back`, `text`, optional): Use this button to go back to the previous page.
* **Self Reflection 2** (`self_reflection_2`, `text`, optional): This is another text field for further self-reflection.
* **Core Values 2** (`core_values_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional core values that are important to you.
* **Goal Setting 2** (`goal_setting_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a specific goal that you are currently working towards.
* **Organized Submission 2** (`organized_submission_2`, `text`, optional): Describe your organized submission process.
* **Submission Tracking 2** (`submission_tracking_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last submission.
