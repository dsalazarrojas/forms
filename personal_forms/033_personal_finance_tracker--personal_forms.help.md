# personal_finance_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personal finance tracker form is designed to help users monitor and manage their income and expenses over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your total income for the selected period (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
2. Choose the source of your income (e.g., salary, investments, etc.).
3. Select the category of your expense (e.g., Rent, Bills, etc.).
4. Enter the date of your expense.
5. Enter a brief description of your expense.
6. Select the period for which you are tracking expenses (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
7. Select the period for which you are tracking expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **income** (id: 1): Enter your total income for the selected period.
* **expenses** (id: 2): Enter your total expenses for the selected period.
* **savings** (id: 3): Note: This field is currently labeled with "savings" but its actual purpose is unclear. Assuming it's intended to track other savings or investments, enter your total savings or investment income for the selected period.
* **income_source** (id: 4): Enter the source of your income (e.g., salary, investments, etc.).
* **expense_category** (id: 5): Select the category of your expense (e.g., Rent, Bills, etc.).
* **expense_date** (id: 6): Enter the date of your expense.
* **expense_description** (id: 7): Enter a brief description of your expense.
* **income_period** (id: 8): Select the period for which you are tracking income (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
* **expense_period** (id: 9): Select the period for which you are tracking expenses.
* **notes** (id: 10): Enter any additional notes or comments about your financial situation.
