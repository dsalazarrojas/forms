# Wedding Day Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Day Questionnaire form is a tool to gather and record the essential details of a wedding event. It helps the event planners in organizing and preparing for the big day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Basic Info** section with the basic details of the wedding, such as the date and time of the event.
2. In the **Contact Details** section, provide the contact information of the event planners, including their names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
3. The **Ceremony Details** section is where you'll provide information about the ceremony, such as the location and time.
4. In the **Special Add-ons** section, select the major sponsors and event organizers that will be involved in the wedding.
5. Finally, select the ceremony time and date in the **Event Date** and **Event Time** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Day Questions**: This section is where you can provide any additional information or questions about the wedding event. This is a free-form text field where you can type in anything you want.
* **Basic Info**: This section is where you provide the basic details of the wedding event, including the date and time.
* **Contact Details**: This section is where you provide the contact information of the event planners, including their names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
* **Ceremony Details**: This section is where you provide information about the ceremony, such as the location and time.
* **Special Add-ons**: This section is where you select the major sponsors and event organizers that will be involved in the wedding.
* **Major Sponsors**: This is a selection field where you select whether or not there will be major sponsors.
* **Event Organizers**: This is a selection field where you select whether or not there will be event organizers.
* **Ceremony Time**: This is a time field where you select the time of the ceremony.
* **Ceremony Location**: This is a text field where you provide the location of the ceremony.
* **Event Date**: This is a date field where you select the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: This is a time field where you select the time of the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field.
* If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact the event planners.
* The form is not required, but it will help the event planners in their preparations and communication with the participants.
