# Personal Aspirations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Aspirations Survey is designed to help individuals identify and clarify their personal goals, motivations, and aspirations. It is a tool for self-reflection and planning, allowing users to better understand themselves and their goals. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you define your personal aspirations and create a plan to achieve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided fields, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select_one fields (e.g., What kind of goal is this?), choose only one option that best describes your goal.
3. For select_multiple fields (e.g., Who supports you in achieving this goal?), select all relevant options.
4. For text fields (e.g., What is your personal aspiration?), type in your answer.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your main goal** (`goal_description`, text, required): Please enter a brief description of your main goal. This could be a career goal, personal goal, or any other type of goal that is important to you.
* **What kind of goal is this** (`goal_type`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best fits your goal. Options are:
	+ Financial
	+ Career
	+ Personal
	+ Health
	+ Education
* **When do you want to achieve this goal** (`goal_urgency`, date, optional): If you have a specific deadline for achieving your goal, enter the desired completion date. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **How often do you plan to work on this goal** (`goal_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how regularly you plan to work on your goal. Options are:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
* **How important is this goal to you** (`goal_importance`, select_multiple, required): Select all levels of importance that apply to your goal. Options are:
	+ Very Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Very Important
	+ Not at All Important
* **Who supports you in achieving this goal** (`goal_support`, select_multiple, required): Select all individuals or groups that support you in achieving your goal. Options are:
	+ Family
	+ Friends
	+ Colleagues
	+ Mentor
	+ Self
* **What is your personal aspiration** (`personal_aspiration`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your personal aspiration.
* **What challenges do you face while achieving this goal** (`goal_challenges`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you face while trying to achieve your goal.
* **What barriers do you face while achieving this goal** (`goal_barriers`, text, optional): Describe any obstacles or barriers that prevent you from achieving your goal.
* **What resources do you need to achieve this goal** (`goal_resources`, text, optional): List any resources you need to achieve your goal.
* **How do you plan to achieve this goal** (`goal_planner`, text, optional): Describe your plan to achieve your goal.
* **How do you track progress on this goal** (`goal_tracker`, text, optional): Explain how you track your progress on your goal.
* **What reflection do you have about this goal** (`goal_reflection`, text, optional): Provide any reflection or insights about your goal.
* **What kind of review do you have about this goal** (`goal_review`, text, optional): Describe any review or evaluation of your goal's progress.
