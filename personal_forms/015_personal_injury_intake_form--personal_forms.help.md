# Personal Injury Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed information about personal injuries, which can be used for medical or legal purposes. The purpose of this form is to gather all the necessary information about the injury, including the incident or accident that caused it, the details of the injury, and any relevant personal information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your contact information in the first section.
3. Provide details about the incident or accident that resulted in your injury in the second section.
4. Describe your injury in the third section.
5. Share your medical history in the fourth section.
6. Provide your insurance information in the fifth section.
7. Answer questions about your employment status and work-related information in the sixth section.
8. Provide any additional information about the accident or injury in the seventh section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the first section where you can enter your contact information, including your full name, address, phone number, and email. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.

### Incident Info
* **Incident Info** (`incident_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to provide details about the incident or accident that resulted in your injury. Please describe what happened, including the date, time, and location of the incident.

### Injury Details
* **Injury Details** (`injury_details`, `text`, required: false): In this section, you will describe the injury you experienced, including its severity and any symptoms you are currently experiencing. Be as detailed as possible.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to share your medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or allergies. Please be honest and accurate in your response.

### Insurance Info
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, `text`, required: false): If you have insurance that covers your medical expenses, please provide the necessary information about your insurance provider, including the policy number and any relevant contact information.

### Employment Info
* **Employment Info** (`employment_info`, `text`, required: false): In this section, you will answer questions about your employment status and work-related information. Please be honest and accurate in your response.

### Work Info
* **Work Info** (`work_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to provide any additional work-related information that may be relevant to your injury, including your job duties and any relevant documentation.

### Accident Info
* **Accident Info** (`accident_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the final section where you can provide any additional details or information about the accident or injury that may not be covered in the previous sections. Please be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* Ensure that the information you provide is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact us for clarification.
* If you have any supporting documentation, such as medical records or police reports, please attach them to the form.
