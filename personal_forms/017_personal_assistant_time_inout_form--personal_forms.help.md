# Personal Assistant Time InOut Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Assistant Time InOut Form is used to track time spent on tasks or projects, hours rendered, and other relevant details. This form helps to collect accurate data for time tracking, project management, or reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. For fields not required, you can skip them or leave them blank.
3. Use the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field to select the relevant tool used during your work.
4. If you have any comments or feedback, use the "Comments and Feedback" field to share them.
5. Ensure that you fill in the correct time in and time out dates and times in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time In Form** (`time_in`): Enter the time you started working on the task or project.
* **Hours Rendered** (`hours_rendered`): Enter the total hours spent on the task or project.
* **Rendered** (`rendered`): Enter any additional comments or details about your work.
* **Comments and Feedback** (`comments`): Share any comments or feedback about the task or project.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information if you need to be reached for further clarification.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool you used for the task or project from the provided options.
* **Time In Date** (`time_in_date`): Enter the date you started working on the task or project.
* **Time In Time** (`time_in_time`): Enter the time you started working on the task or project.
* **Time Out Time** (`time_out_time`): Enter the time you finished working on the task or project.
* **Rendered Time** (`rendered_time`): Enter any additional comments or details about your work time.
* **Comments Text** (`comments_text`): Select the text that best describes your comments from the provided options. 

## Tips
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the form's administrators or support team for guidance.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the space provided to ensure your entries are clear and concise.
