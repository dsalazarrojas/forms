# Personal Purpose Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Purpose Assessment is a self-discovery tool designed to help you assess your personal purpose and identify areas for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field explanation below to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question with the most honest and accurate response possible.
3. Select the most relevant options for each question.
4. Review your answers and selected options to ensure they accurately reflect your current situation.
5. Use the form as a starting point for further self-reflection and exploration of your personal purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose. Read it carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
* **Goals** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or multiple options that reflect your personal and professional goals. Examples include: "To achieve financial stability", "To start a new career", "To improve relationships", etc.
* **Values** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Select one option that best reflects your core values. Examples include: "Honesty", "Integrity", "Hard work", etc.
* **Purpose** (`4`, `text`, optional): Write a brief description of your personal purpose, including your motivations, aspirations, and goals.
* **Self-Discovery** (`5`, `text`, optional): Write a brief description of your self-discovery process, including any challenges or successes you've experienced.
* **Personal Growth** (`6`, `text`, optional): Describe your personal growth, including any areas you've worked on or achieved progress in.
* **Relationships** (`7`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationships, including any positive or negative interactions or experiences.
* **Career Development** (`8`, `text`, optional): Describe your career development, including any progress or goals related to your professional life.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Select one option that reflects your overall satisfaction with your current life situation. Examples include: "Highly satisfied", "Moderately satisfied", "Not satisfied at all", etc.
* **Personal Purpose Score** (`10`, `number`, optional): This score is generated based on your answers and selected options. It is not meant to be taken literally and is used as a rough estimate.
* **Personal Purpose Summary** (`11`, `text`, optional): Summarize your personal purpose and key takeaways from this form.
* **Recommendations** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Select one option for recommendations for further growth and development.
* **Review** (`13`, `text`, optional): Write a brief review of your form experience and any additional comments.
* **Feedback** (`14`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback on the form or its questions.
* **Next Steps** (`15`, `text`, optional): Describe your next steps for continued personal growth and development.
* **Conclusion** (`16`, `text`, optional): Summarize your key takeaways and next steps.
* **Evaluation** (`17`, `number`, optional): This score is generated based on your answers and selected options.
* **Score** (`18`, `number`, optional): This score is similar to the Personal Purpose Score and is used to measure progress.
* **Overall Score** (`19`, `number`, optional): This score is generated based on your answers and selected options.
* **Final Comments** (`20`, `text`, optional): Provide any final comments or thoughts.
* **Final Score** (`21`, `number`, optional): This score is generated based on your answers and selected options.
* **Final Recommendations** (`22`, `select_one`, optional): Select one option for final recommendations.
* **Final Evaluation** (`23`, `number`, optional): This score is generated based on your answers and selected options.
* **Submission Completed** (`24`, `select_one`, optional): Select one option to confirm form submission.

Note: The scores and generated text are not meant to be taken literally and are used as a rough estimate of your personal purpose and progress.
