# Personal Values Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Personal Values Survey is a form designed to help you reflect on and explore your personal values. It's a self-guided journey through a series of questions and prompts that will help you understand and articulate your values, how you prioritize them, and how you share them with others.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. Use the provided space to elaborate on your answers if needed.
4. Don't hesitate to skip questions that don't apply to you.
