# Babysitter Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitter Checklist is a comprehensive form designed to provide essential information about your household and children to the babysitter. This form helps prevent misunderstandings and ensures that the sitter has all the necessary details for a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Read this guide carefully** before proceeding with the form.
2.  **Gather all necessary information** about your household and children before filling out the form.
3.  **Answer each question carefully and truthfully**, providing as much detail as possible.
4.  **Make sure the sitter has a copy of this form**, either physically or digitally, for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Household Information --**
    *   **Household Information**: This is a note section where you can provide key details about your household that the sitter might find useful, such as household rules or special needs.
*   **Parent contact name and phone**
    *   **Parent Contact Name and Phone**: Provide your name and full phone number so the sitter can reach you in case of an emergency or if they need to ask a question.
*   **Child names and ages**
    *   **Child Names and Ages**: List each child's name and age, including any relevant health information.
*   **Allergies and medications**
    *   **Allergies and Medications**: Include any allergies, medical conditions, or medications your child is taking, along with dosages and any specific instructions.
*   **Emergency procedures**
    *   **Emergency Procedures and Contacts**: List any emergency contact numbers, such as the police and fire department, and any specific procedures the sitter should follow in case of an emergency.
*   **Bedtime routine**
    *   **Bedtime Routine**: Describe the steps the sitter should take to put your children to bed, including any specific bedtime routines or requirements.
*   **Feeding instructions**
    *   **Feeding Instructions**: Provide the sitter with information about your child's feeding schedule, including any specific instructions for formula, food allergies, or dietary restrictions.
*   **Household rules**
    *   **Household Rules**: List any specific household rules or restrictions the children are subject to, such as screen time or pet restrictions.
*   **Check out and lock up instructions**
    *   **Check out and Lock up Instructions**: Leave instructions for the sitter on how to properly check out of your home, including any alarm codes, lock combinations, or specific tasks they should complete before leaving.
*   **Additional notes for sitter**
    *   **Additional Notes for Sitter**: This is a free text section where you can include any additional information or notes for the sitter.

**Tips**

*   Make sure the sitter has a copy of this form for future reference.
*   Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before leaving the children in the sitter's care.
*   Discuss any questions or concerns you have with the sitter before leaving the children with them.
