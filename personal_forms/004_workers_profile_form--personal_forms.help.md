# workers_profile_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or edit worker profiles, which are used by HR or personnel management teams to manage worker information in a database or system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form and understanding the fields and their corresponding labels.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select your department status in the "Department" field.
6. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
7. Select a date in the "Hire Date" field for when you were hired.
8. Enter the name of your manager in the "Manager Name" field.
9. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
10. Enter your departmental email address in the "Departmental Email" field.
11. Select your employee status in the "Employee Status" field.
12. Select whether you report to a manager in the "Reporting Manager" field.
13. Enter your manager's email address in the "Reporting Manager Email" field.
14. If you have a profile picture, you can upload it in the "Employee Profile Picture" field.
15. Enter a brief description of your job in the "Job Description" field.
16. Enter your departmental phone number in the "Departmental Phone" field.
17. Enter your job title (again) in the "Job Title" field (this field is likely a duplicate and can be removed).
18. Enter your reporting manager's phone number in the "Reporting Manager Phone" field.
19. Enter your departmental phone number in the "Departmental Phone Number" field (this field is likely a duplicate and can be removed).
20. Enter your reporting manager's phone number in the "Reporting Manager Phone Number" field (this field is likely a duplicate and can be removed).
21. Select whether you are part of a department in the "Department" field.
22. Select your job level (high or low) in the "Job Level" field.
23. Select your job function (yes or no) in the "Job Function" field.
24. Select whether you are part of a team in the "Team" field.
25. Select whether your reporting manager is part of a team in the "Reporting Manager Team" field.
26. Select the job level of your reporting manager in the "Reporting Manager Job Level" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Department (4)**: Select whether you are part of a department.
* **Job Title (5)**: Enter your job title.
* **Hire Date (6)**: Select a date for when you were hired.
* **Manager Name (7)**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email (8)**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Departmental Email (9)**: Enter your departmental email address.
* **Employee Status (10)**: Select your employee status (active or inactive).
* **Reporting Manager (11)**: Select whether you report to a manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email (12)**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Employee Profile Picture (13)**: Upload your profile picture (this field is optional).
* **Job Description (14)**: Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Departmental Phone (15)**: Enter your departmental phone number (this field is likely a duplicate and can be removed).
* **Job Title (16)**: This field is a duplicate of field 5 and can be removed.
* **Reporting Manager Phone (17)**: Enter your manager's phone number (this field is likely a duplicate and can be removed).
* **Departmental Phone Number (18)**: This field is a duplicate of field 15 and can be removed).
* **Reporting Manager Phone Number (19)**: This field is a duplicate of field 17 and can be removed).
* **Department (20)**: Select whether you are part of a department (this field is a duplicate of field 4).
* **Job Level (21)**: Select your job level (high or low).
* **Job Function (22)**: Select whether you are part of a job function (yes or no).
* **Team (23)**: Select whether you are part of a team.
* **Reporting Manager Team (24)**: Select whether your reporting manager is part of a team.
* **Reporting Manager Job Level (25)**: Select the job level of your reporting manager (high or low).
