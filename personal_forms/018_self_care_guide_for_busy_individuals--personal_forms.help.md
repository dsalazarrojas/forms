# Self Care Guide For Busy Individuals - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps busy individuals set and track their self-care goals and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your self-care goal in the "Self-Care Goal" field. This is the specific area of self-care you want to focus on, such as exercise, meditation, or journaling.
2. Select all the self-care activities you want to practice regularly from the "Self-Care Activity" dropdown menu. You can choose one or more activities that align with your goal.
3. Indicate how frequently you want to perform each selected self-care activity. This will help you track your progress and stay accountable.
4. Enter the date and time you want to schedule each self-care activity in the "Self-Care Time of Day" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Self-Care Goal** (`self_care_goal`, `text`, optional): Enter a specific area of self-care you want to focus on, such as exercise or meditation.
- **Self-Care Activity** (`self_care_activity`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more activities that align with your self-care goal, such as walking or yoga.
- **Self-Care Frequency** (`self_care_frequency`, `number`, optional): Indicate how frequently you want to perform each selected self-care activity, using a number (e.g., 3 times a week).
- **Self-Care Time of Day** (`self_care_time_of_day`, `date`, optional): Schedule each self-care activity for a specific date and time, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips
* Be honest with yourself about your self-care goals and activities, and don't be too hard on yourself if you miss a session.
* Start small and gradually increase the frequency and duration of your self-care activities.
* Mix and match activities to create a routine that suits your lifestyle and preferences.
* Review and update your self-care schedule regularly to track your progress and make adjustments as needed.
