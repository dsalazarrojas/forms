# Halloween Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information about an event, specifically Halloween preparations. It is used to gather details such as event name, dates, location, and contact information of individuals involved in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact email.
2. Enter the event name and dates.
3. Provide the event location (if applicable).
4. Specify the time required for the event (if applicable).
5. Enter the name and contact information (email and phone number) of individuals involved in the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Address**: This field is not currently used.
* **Event Name** (text): Enter the name of the event.
* **Start Date** (date): Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (date): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Event Type** (text): This field is intended for you to specify the type of event, but it is not required.
* **Location** (text): Enter the location of the event, if applicable.
* **Time Required** (time): Enter the time required for the event, if applicable.
* **Name 1** (text): Enter the name of the individual involved in the event.
* **Email 1** (email): Enter the email address of the individual involved in the event.
* **Phone 1** (text): Enter the phone number of the individual involved in the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the purpose and instructions above.
