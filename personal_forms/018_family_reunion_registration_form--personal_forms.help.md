# Family Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals and families attending a family reunion. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a successful and enjoyable reunion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information.
2. If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know by filling out the "Special Request" field.
3. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Your full name as it should be listed on the family reunion registration list.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address where we can reach you with updates and information about the reunion.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number where we can reach you for any last-minute questions or requests.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or needs, such as accommodations or dietary restrictions, please let us know in this field.
