**Form Purpose:**
The daily gratitude journal is designed to help users reflect on their daily experiences and feelings, allowing them to focus on the positive aspects of their lives. By completing this form, users will be able to identify the things they are grateful for and how they make them feel, helping them cultivate a more positive and mindful approach to life.

**How To Complete This Form:**

1.  **Select a Date:** Choose the date for which you want to reflect on your experiences and feelings.
2.  **Enter the Time:** Select the time of day when you are reflecting on your gratitude.
3.  **Gratitude Expressions:** Write down the things you are grateful for, such as people, experiences, or events that have positively impacted you.
4.  **Feelings:** Write down how you feel when thinking about these things, in as much detail as possible.
5.  **What Works:** Select one or more options to indicate what works for you, or choose "Other" and describe what else works for you.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **Gratitude Date (gratitude_date)**: Enter the date for which you want to reflect on your gratitude.
* **Gratitude Time (gratitude_time)**: Select the time of day when you are reflecting on your gratitude.
* **What I Am Grateful For (gratitude_expressions)**: Write down the things you are grateful for, such as people, experiences, or events that have positively impacted you.
* **How I Feel When I Think About These Things (gratitude_feelings)**: Write down how you feel when thinking about the things you are grateful for, in as much detail as possible.
* **What Works (gratitude_what_works)**: Select one or more options to indicate what works for you, or choose "Other" and describe what else works for you.
