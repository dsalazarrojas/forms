# Household Budget Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Budget Tracker form is designed to help users monitor and manage their household's income, expenses, and financial goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your household's monthly income.
2.  Indicate if you have any household members.
3.  Enter your monthly expenses.
4.  Set your savings goal.
5.  Select the type of your household budget (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
6.  Choose the categories your expenses fall under (e.g., Housing, Transportation, Food).
7.  Select the frequency of your income (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
8.  Select the frequency of your expenses (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
9.  Choose how often you want to track your household budget (Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly).
10.  Enter any additional notes or comments about your income, expenses, or tracking frequency.
