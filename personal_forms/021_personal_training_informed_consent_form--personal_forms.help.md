# Personal Training Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Informed Consent Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your medical history, emergency contact information, and other relevant details to ensure your safety and well-being during training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact" field.
5. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
6. If applicable, provide an additional emergency contact's name and phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone 2" field.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which will be used as your contact information.
9. Select a date for your training session in the "Date" field.
10. Select a time for your training session in the "Time" field.
11. Choose one option from the "Select One" field.
12. Choose multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
13. You can add any additional notes about your training session in the "Note" field.
14. Enter a decimal value for the "Decimal" field.
15. Enter an integer value for the "Integer" field.
16. Enter another decimal value for the "Decimal 2" field.
17. Enter another integer value for the "Integer 2" field.
18. Enter a decimal value for the "Decimal 3" field.
19. Enter another integer value for the "Integer 3" field.
20. Enter a decimal value for the "Decimal 4" field.
21. Enter another integer value for the "Integer 4" field.
22. Enter a decimal value for the "Decimal 5" field.
23. Enter another integer value for the "Integer 4" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)** (text, required): Please enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name (2)** (text, required): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Date of Birth (3)** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Emergency Contact** (4) (text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (5) (text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (6) (text, optional): If you have an additional emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Address** (7) (text, required): Enter your address to help us locate you in case of an emergency.
* **Email** (8) (email, required): Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Date** (9) (date, required): Select a date for your training session.
* **Time** (10) (time, required): Select a time for your training session.
* **Select One** (11) (select_one, required): Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (12) (select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Note** (13) (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your training session.
* **Decimal** (14) (number, required): Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer** (15) (number, required): Enter an integer value.
* **Decimal 2** (16) (number, required): Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer 2** (17) (number, required): Enter an integer value.
* **Decimal 3** (18) (number, required): Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer 3** (19) (number, required): Enter an integer value.
* **Decimal 4** (20) (number, required): Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer 4** (21) (number, required): Enter an integer value.
* **Decimal 5** (22) (number, required): Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer 4** (23) (number, required): Enter an integer value.
