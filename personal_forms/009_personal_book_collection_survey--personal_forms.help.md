# personal_book_collection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a user's personal book collection, including their favorite book title, author, genre, and other relevant details. This data will be used to improve our service and provide more tailored recommendations for users who share similar interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. On the first page, enter your favorite book title in the "Favorite Book Title" field.
3. On the second page, enter your favorite book author in the "Favorite Book Author" field.
4. On the second page, select your favorite book genre from the "Book Genre" field.
5. On the second page, describe how you found out about the book in the "How Did You Find Out" field.
6. On the second page, describe your favorite book's characteristics in the "Favorite Book Characteristics" field.
7. On the second page, select the number of books in your collection in the "How Many Books In Your Collection" field.
8. On the second page, select your favorite character in the book in the "Favorite Character" field.
7. On the last page, enter any additional information or comments about your book collection in the "Personal Book Collection Survey Page 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Book Title (personal_book_collection_survey_page_1)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the title of your favorite book.
* **Favorite Book Author (favorite_book_author)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the author of your favorite book.
* **Book Genre (book_genre)** (`text`, required: false): Select the genre of your favorite book.
* **How Did You Find Out (how_did_you_find_out)** (`text`, required: false): Describe how you found out about your favorite book.
* **Favorite Book Characteristics (favorite_book_characteristics)** (`text`, required: false): Describe your favorite book's characteristics.
* **How Many Books In Your Collection (how_many_books_in_your_collection)** (`number`, required: false): Select the number of books in your collection.
* **Favorite Character (favorite_character)** (`text`, required: false): Select your favorite character in your favorite book.
* **Personal Book Collection Survey Page 2 (personal_book_collection_survey_page_2)** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments about your book collection.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate information about your book collection to ensure the best possible results.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us.
* This form will only take a few minutes to complete, and the more information you provide, the more accurate our recommendations will be.
