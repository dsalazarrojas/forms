# Personal Information Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect sensitive personal information from individuals for the purpose of sharing their medical history and authorization data with a medical provider.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select one of the options for "Medical History" to indicate whether you have a medical condition that requires sharing your medical history with your medical provider.
2.  Enter your first name, last name, and birthdate in the respective fields.
3.  Enter your contact information (phone, email, and address) to ensure that your medical provider can contact you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
4.  Identify an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
5.  Provide the phone number, email, and fax number of your emergency contact person.
6.  Enter your social security number, medical history, and authorization date.
7.  Select the type of medical provider you are working with (yes/no).
8.  If you are working with a medical provider, enter their address, phone number, email, and fax number.
9.  If you are working with a medical provider, enter their specialty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical History**: Select "Yes" if you have a medical condition that requires sharing your medical history with your medical provider.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Birthdate**: Enter your birthdate.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
*   **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security number.
*   **Medical History**: Enter your medical history information.
*   **Authorization Date**: Enter the date for which you are authorizing your medical provider to share your information.
*   **Authorization Period**: Select "Yes" if you are authorizing your medical provider to share your information for a period.
*   **Medical Authorization**: Select "Yes" if you are authorizing your medical provider to share your medical history.
*   **Medical Provider**: Select "Yes" if you are working with a medical provider.
*   **Medical Provider Address**: Enter the address of your medical provider.
*   **Medical Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of your medical provider.
*   **Medical Provider Email**: Enter the email of your medical provider.
*   **Medical Provider Fax**: Enter the fax number of your medical provider.
*   **Medical Provider License Number**: Enter the license number of your medical provider.
*   **Medical Provider NPI**: Enter the NPI number of your medical provider.
*   **Medical Provider Specialty**: Enter the specialty of your medical provider.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
*   Your responses are voluntary and will be kept confidential.
*   Your rights as a patient are protected, and you have the right to opt-out of sharing your information at any time.
