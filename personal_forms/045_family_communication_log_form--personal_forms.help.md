# Family Communication Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Communication Log Form is a tool for tracking and documenting family communications, decisions, and follow-ups. This form helps keep a record of interactions between family members, caregivers, and other relevant individuals, facilitating better communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, select the corresponding option for each section of the form. You can add or remove rows as needed to accommodate your specific situation. Make sure to provide clear and concise information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Conversations** (`family_conversations`, text, optional): Record any significant conversations or discussions that took place between family members, caregivers, or other relevant individuals. 
* **Family Decisions** (`family_decisions`, text, optional): Document any important decisions made during conversations or interactions. 
* **Follow-ups** (`follow_ups`, text, optional): Log any follow-up actions or next steps taken or planned. 
* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the caregiver or individual providing assistance. 
* **Co-parent Names** (`co_parent_names`, select multiple, optional): Select from the options to indicate if there are co-parents present or involved.
	+ Options: 'Yes' and 'No'
* **Household Members** (`household_members`, select multiple, optional): Select from the options to indicate the presence or absence of household members.
	+ Options: 'Yes' and 'No'
* **Household Member Relationships** (`household_member_relationships`, select multiple, optional): Select from the options to indicate the relationships between household members.
	+ Options: 'Yes' and 'No'
* **Family Members Residing** (`family_members_residing`, select multiple, optional): Select from the options to indicate if family members are residing in the household.
	+ Options: 'Yes' and 'No'
* **Family Conversation Dates** (`family_conversation_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of family conversations or interactions. 
* **Family Conversation Times** (`family_conversation_times`, time, optional): Enter the times of family conversations or interactions.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional): Provide any details about follow-up actions or next steps.
* **Family Conversation Notes** (`family_conversation_notes`, note, optional): Take notes on any important points or discussions during conversations.
* **Family Conversation Outcomes** (`family_conversation_outcomes`, text, optional): Record the outcomes or results of family conversations or interactions.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language in each field.
* You can add or remove rows as needed to accommodate your specific situation.
* This form is a personal and private record, so be mindful of confidentiality.
