# Trading Card Cataloging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trading Card Cataloging Form is designed to collect and organize information about trading cards for further analysis and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
2. Enter the title of the trading card.
3. Provide additional details about the card on the second page.
4. Select the rarity of the card from the options provided.
5. Specify any other rarity if not listed in the options.
6. Select the condition of the card from the options provided.
7. Specify any other condition if not listed in the options.
8. Enter the card number (if applicable).
9. Select the edition of the card from the options provided.
10. Specify any other edition if not listed in the options.
11. Enter the price of the card.
12. Enter the date and time of purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **card_info** (1) (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the trading card.
* **card_details** (2) (Text, Optional): Provide any additional details about the card, such as its rarity or condition.
* **rarity** (3) (Select One, Optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **other_rarity** (4) (Text, Optional): Specify any other rarity not listed in the options.
* **condition** (5) (Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple from the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **other_condition** (6) (Text, Optional): Specify any other condition not listed in the options.
* **card_number** (7) (Number, Optional): Enter the card number (if applicable).
* **edition** (8) (Select One, Optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **other_edition** (9) (Text, Optional): Specify any other edition not listed in the options.
* **price** (10) (Number, Optional): Enter the price of the card.
* **purchase_date** (11) (Date, Optional): Enter the date of purchase.
* **purchase_time** (12) (Time, Optional): Enter the time of purchase.
