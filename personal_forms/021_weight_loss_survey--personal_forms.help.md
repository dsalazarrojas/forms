# Weight Loss Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal data from users related to their weight loss journey, including their physical characteristics, lifestyle habits, and health information. The data collected will help us better understand the factors that influence weight loss and support users in their weight loss goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field and its relevance to weight loss.
2. Fill in your personal details such as weight, height, and age.
3. Select your exercise frequency, sleep duration, and water intake habits.
4. Provide information about your dieting background, medication use, and supplement use.
5. Choose your exercise preferences and food preferences.
6. Enter your motivation for weight loss.
7. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Background**: This field is a note section where you can provide any background information about your weight loss journey, such as how long you've been trying to lose weight or what your goals are.
* **Current Weight**: Enter your current weight in the corresponding unit (e.g., pounds or kilograms).
* **Target Weight**: Enter your target weight in the corresponding unit (e.g., pounds or kilograms).
* **Goal Weight Loss**: Enter the amount of weight you aim to lose.
* **Current Height**: Enter your current height in the corresponding unit (e.g., centimeters, meters, feet, or inches).
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Sex**: Select your sex (female or male).
* **Height Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for your height (e.g., centimeters, meters, feet, or inches).
* **Weight Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for your weight (e.g., kilograms or pounds).
* **Height**: Enter your height in the corresponding unit.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in the corresponding unit.
* **Height Change Per Month**: Enter the average height change per month (e.g., 1-2 cm).
* **Weight Change Per Month**: Enter the average weight change per month (e.g., 0.5 kg).
* **Dieting Background**: Describe your dieting background, such as any eating habits or plans you follow.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Select your exercise frequency (1-2 times a week, 3-4 times a week, 5-6 times a week, or 7 or more times a week).
* **Sleep Duration**: Select your average sleep duration per day (1-2 hours, 3-4 hours, 5-6 hours, 7-8 hours, or 9 hours or more).
* **Water Intake**: Enter your daily water intake.
* **Food Intake**: Enter your daily food intake.
* **Exercise Duration**: Enter the average duration of your exercise sessions (e.g., 30 minutes).
* **Stress Level**: Select your average stress level (0-5, 5-10, 11-15, 16-20, or Above 20).
* **Medication Use**: Select whether you use any medication for weight loss.
* **Supplement Use**: Select whether you use any supplements for weight loss (optional).
* **Food Preferences**: Select your food preferences (Vegetarian, Vegan, Omnivore, or Flexitarian).
* **Exercise Preferences**: Select your exercise preferences (Running, Swimming, Cycling, or Gym).
* **Motivation**: Describe your motivation for weight loss.

Remember to review your answers carefully before submitting the form, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification.
