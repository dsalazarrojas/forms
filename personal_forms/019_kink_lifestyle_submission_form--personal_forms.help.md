# Kink Lifestyle Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kink Lifestyle Submission Form is a questionnaire designed to gather information about your community interests, user details, boundaries, consent, and contact information. This form is intended to help us better understand your preferences and ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone involved. Please note that this form is not a mandatory requirement, and you can submit it only if you're comfortable sharing your interests and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each section is asking.
2. Select the options that apply to you for each field.
3. Fill in the text field with any additional information you'd like to provide.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Community Interests
* **Community Interests** (`Community Interests`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Check all the areas of interest you are comfortable exploring, such as BDSM, sex education, or social events.

### User Details
* **User Details** (`User Details`, Select One, required/optional): Indicate whether you are a new or existing member of the community, as this will help us understand your context and provide relevant resources.

### Boundaries
* **Boundaries** (`Boundaries`, Text, required/optional): If you have any specific boundaries or limitations you'd like to share, please provide a brief description here. This will help us understand what we can and can't discuss or share with you.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`Consent`, Select One, required/optional): Confirm that you are willing to share your contact information and participate in our community activities. If you're not comfortable, you can simply select "No" and we'll respect your boundaries.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, Email, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted about upcoming events or opportunities, please provide your email address here.
