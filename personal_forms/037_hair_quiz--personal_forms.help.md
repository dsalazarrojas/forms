# Hair Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your hair condition, type, length, thickness, condition history, and other related topics. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand your hair.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to you for each question.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions, as some of them are required.
4. When finished, review your answers to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Type** (hair_type): Select 'Yes' if you have this type of hair, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Hair Condition** (hair_condition): Select 'Yes' if your hair has this condition, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Hair Color** (hair_color): Please enter your hair color (e.g., red, blonde, brown, etc.).
* **Hair Length** (hair_length): Select the length of your hair, and 'No' for none.
* **Hair Thickness** (hair_thickness): Select 'Yes' if you have this level of hair thickness, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Hair Condition History** (hair_condition_history): Select 'Yes' if you have had this condition in the past, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Hair Falling** (hair_falling): Select 'Yes' if your hair is falling, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Hair Falling History** (hair_falling_history): Select 'Yes' if you've had this issue in the past, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Hair Falling Causes** (hair_falling_causes): Select all the causes that apply to your hair falling.
* **Hair Falling Remedies** (hair_falling_remedies): Select all the remedies you've tried for your hair falling.
* **Hair Grease** (hair_grease): Please enter any hair grease or product you're using.
* **Hair Grease History** (hair_grease_history): Select 'Yes' if you've used this grease in the past, and 'No' otherwise.
