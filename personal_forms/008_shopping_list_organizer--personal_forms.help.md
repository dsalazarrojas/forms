# Shopping List Organizer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shopping List Organizer form is designed to help you plan and organize your shopping trips. It allows you to record details about your grocery trip, including the items you want to buy, their quantity, price, and where you plan to buy them, as well as your budget and trip details.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Shopping List Organizer form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Grocery Trip Details" page and fill in the relevant information.
2. On the "Item Name" page, enter the name of the item you want to buy.
3. On the "Quantity" page, enter the quantity of the item you want to buy.
4. On the "Price" page, enter the price of the item.
5. On the "Store" page, select the store where you plan to buy the item (Grocery Store, Online Store, Local Market, or Other).
6. On the "Budget" page, select the budget for this shopping trip (you can choose from $100 to $1000, or Other).
7. On the "Date" page, select the date of the trip.
8. On the "Time" page, select the time of the trip.
9. On the "Notes" page, add any additional notes about the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grocery Trip Details (Grocery Trip Details)** (`note`, no): This is the first page of the form, providing space to enter a note about your trip. Enter any relevant details about your shopping trip here.
* **Item Name (Item Name)** (`text`, no): Enter the name of the item you want to buy.
* **Quantity (Quantity)** (`number`, no): Enter the quantity of the item you want to buy.
* **Price (Price)** (`number`, no): Enter the price of the item.
* **Store (Store)** (`select_one`, no): Select the store where you plan to buy the item (Grocery Store, Online Store, Local Market, or Other).
* **Budget (Budget)** (`select_multiple`, no): Select the budget for this shopping trip (you can choose from $100 to $1000, or Other).
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, no): Select the date of the trip.
* **Time (Time)** (`time`, no): Select the time of the trip.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`note`, no): Add any additional notes about the trip.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate trip planning.
* Use the "Other" option for stores that are not listed, and for budgets that do not fit into the provided options.
* You can add or remove pages as needed, and navigate between pages using the navigation buttons.
