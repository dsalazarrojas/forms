# Family Legacy Bylaws Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Legacy Bylaws Form is designed to help families record and share their core values, decision-making processes, and family history. This form is a tool for families to reflect on their values, how they make decisions, and their historical context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Complete each section thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the options provided for selecting decision-making structures and family history.
4. Save your progress regularly, as you can only move on to the next page when you have completed the current one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Family Values
 * **Family Values** (Text Field): This is a field where you can describe your family's core values that are essential to your identity and relationships. Think about the principles that guide your behavior and interactions within the family.

### Decision Making Structures
 * **Decision Making Structures** (Select Multiple): Select the decision-making structures that are used in your family. This could be structures like consensus, voting, or individual decision-making.

### Review Processes
 * **Review Processes** (Text Field): Describe the processes your family uses to review and discuss decisions. This could be regular meetings, a specific protocol, or an informal approach.

### Family History
 * **Family History** (Text Field): Share your family's historical context, including significant events, traditions, and cultural influences that shape your family's identity.

## Tips
* Take your time and be thorough in completing this form.
* Share your answers with other family members to ensure everyone is on the same page.
* Review and revise your answers as needed to ensure accuracy and consistency.

This form is designed to be a living document, and you can update it as your family's circumstances and values change over time.
