# Secret Santa Wish List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Wish List Form is a tool for users to submit their gift preferences and recipient information to facilitate the Secret Santa gift exchange process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form entirely with your own information.
2. Be honest about your gift preferences and recipient details.
3. Ensure that you've completed your form accurately and without duplicates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (id: 1)**: Enter your name as it will appear on the gift-giving list.
* **Wishes (id: 2)**: Describe the gift you would like to receive this holiday season. You can choose one or more of the available options, or type in your own wish.
* **Gift Recipient (id: 3)**: Enter the name of the person who will be receiving your gift.
* **Recipient Email (id: 4)**: Enter a real email address where the gift-giver can contact the recipient for their gift.
* **Gift Wish (id: 5)**: Select the specific gift you would like to receive from the available options or type in your own wish.
* **Personal Message (id: 6)**: Write a personal message for the gift-giver to include with your gift, if desired.
* **Gift Price Limit (id: 7)**: Choose a price limit for the gift you would like to receive, ensuring it fits within your budget.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form only once to avoid confusion and duplicates.
* Be open-minded when selecting gift options, and consider your interests and preferences when making your choices.
* If you have specific gift ideas, you can type them into the Gift Wish field instead of selecting from the available options.
