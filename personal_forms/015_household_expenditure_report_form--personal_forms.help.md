# Household Expenditure Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about household financial habits and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for the fields that have dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Household Income Earned
* **household_income_earned** (`select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Select all of the income ranges that apply to your household. Please choose one or more of the following ranges:
	+ $100,000
	+ $50,000
	+ $200,000

### Household Expenses
* **household_expenses** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the total amount of money you spend on household expenses each month:
	+ $500
	+ $2,500
	+ $10,000

### Household Expenses Breakdown (Optional)
* **household_expenses_breakdown** (`date`, Required/Optional): This field is not currently being used. If you need to report expenses for a specific date, please use this field. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.

### Type of Expense
* **expense_type** (`text`, Required/Optional): Describe the type of expenses your household has, such as rent, mortgage, or utility bills.

### Household Size
* **household_size** (`text`, Required/Optional): Provide the number of people living in your household.

### Income Distribution
* **income_distribution** (`text`, Required/Optional): Describe how income is distributed within your household, such as who earns it and who spends it.

### Family Members
* **family_members** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the primary family member who earns income:
	+ Father
	+ Mother
	+ Both
	+ None

### Income Frequency
* **income_frequency** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Select how often income is received:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Yearly

### Payment Method
* **payment_method** (`select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Select the methods your household uses to make payments:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
	+ Cash
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ None

### Payment Status
* **payment_status** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the current status of your household's payments:
	+ Pending
	+ Paid
	+ Overdue
	+ Cancelled
	+ Declined

### Additional Notes
* **notes** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your household's financial situation.
