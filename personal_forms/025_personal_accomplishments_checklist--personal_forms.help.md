# Personal Accomplishments Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Accomplishments Checklist is a tool designed to help you reflect on your personal achievements and progress towards your goals. This form guides you in tracking your accomplishments, identifying areas of improvement, and setting realistic expectations for future growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing accurate information about your personal accomplishments.
2. Answer each question carefully, considering your current situation and experiences.
3. Review your answers to ensure they reflect your true progress and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist`)
	+ This is a required field where you provide your name for identification purposes.
* **First win** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_first_win`)
	+ Check the date of your first notable achievement or win.
* **First win date** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_first_win_date`)
	+ Enter the date of your first notable achievement or win.
* **Last win** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_last_win`)
	+ Select the category that best describes the time since your last notable achievement or win:
		- Never
		- Today
		- Yesterday
		- Last week
		- Last month
		- Last 3 months
		- Last 6 months
		- Last year
* **Last win date** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_last_win_date`)
	+ Enter the date of your last notable achievement or win.
* **Achievements** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_achievements`)
	+ List some of your notable achievements or accomplishments.
* **Goals** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_goals`)
	+ Describe your current goals and objectives.
* **Goals date** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_goals_date`)
	+ Enter the date you set or plan to achieve your goals.
* **Progress towards goals** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_progress`)
	+ Rate your progress towards your goals on a scale of 1-10.
* **Lessons learned** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_lessons`)
	+ Share any valuable lessons or insights you've gained from your experiences.
* **Barriers to progress** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_barriers`)
	+ Identify any obstacles or challenges that hinder your progress.
* **Referrals** (`personal_accomplishments_checklist_referrals`)
	+ Select the sources that have influenced your progress:
		- Self
		- Friend
		- Family
		- Coach
		- Mentor
		- Social media
		- Online search
		- Other

Note that fields 6-20 are identical and are intended for tracking multiple referrals. Please fill out fields 6-20 with the same information as field 12.
