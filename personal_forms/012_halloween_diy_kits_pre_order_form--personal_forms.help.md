# Halloween DIY Kits Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-order form for Halloween DIY kits. It is used to collect customer information and kit preferences for the upcoming Halloween season. Fill out this form to let us know what you would like to order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields. Each field is labeled with its corresponding field name, and most fields are optional unless marked as required. Make sure to fill in all required fields as they are necessary for processing your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Street** (`3`, text, optional): Enter your street address (if different from your city).
* **City** (`4`, text, optional): Enter your city (if different from your state).
* **State** (`5`, text, optional): Enter your state (if different from your city).
* **Zip** (`6`, number, required): Enter a valid zip code.
* **Email** (`7`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`8`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Country** (`9`, select_one, optional): Select your country of residence (United States, Canada, or Mexico).
* **Kits** (`10`, select_multiple, required): Select which kits you would like to order (Basic, Advanced, or Deluxe).
* **Payment Method** (`11`, select_multiple, required): Select how you would like to make payment (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Amount** (`12`, number, required): Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **Date** (`13`, date, required): Select the date you would like to collect your order.
* **Time** (`14`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to collect your order (if any).
* **Note** (`15`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments about your order here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Select your preferred payment method carefully, as we will contact you with payment instructions once you have been confirmed.
