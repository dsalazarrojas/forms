The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals who are seeking to lose weight and need guidance or support in achieving their weight loss goals. **weight_loss_questionnaire_form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The weight loss questionnaire form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking to lose weight and receive guidance or support in achieving their weight loss goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Provide detailed and accurate information about your weight loss goals, expectations, and challenges.
3. If you have any medical conditions or take any medications, please indicate this in the "Medical Info" section.
4. If you have any exercise habits or routines, please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly in the "Exercise" section.
5. Finally, review your answers and submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information**: This section is a brief introduction to your weight loss goals and objectives. Please provide a short description of your motivation for seeking weight loss guidance.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you with any further questions or updates.
* **Weight Information**: This section is where you will provide your current weight and target weight. Please enter your weight in kilograms (kg) or pounds (lbs).
* **Diet**: Describe your current diet and eating habits. Please indicate if you have any food preferences or restrictions.
* **Medical Info**: Do you have any medical conditions or take any medications? Please tick "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **Exercise**: Do you regularly exercise or engage in physical activity? Please tick "Yes" or "No" accordingly.

### Weight Loss Information (Pages 2-5)
* **Current Weight**: Enter your current weight in kilograms (kg) or pounds (lbs).
* **Target Weight**: Enter your target weight in kilograms (kg) or pounds (lbs).
* **Weight Loss Goals**: Do you have any specific weight loss goals, such as a certain weight range or percentage of weight loss? Please describe your goals in this section.
* **Weight Loss Expectations**: What are your expectations for your weight loss journey? Please be realistic and specific about your goals and timelines.
* **Weight Loss Obstacles**: What challenges do you anticipate facing during your weight loss journey? Please be honest and provide any relevant details.
* **Weight Loss Support**: Are you seeking support from a healthcare professional or a weight loss program? Please indicate if you need any additional guidance or resources.
* **Weight Loss Frequency**: How often do you plan to work on your weight loss goals? Please select a frequency that suits you.
* **Weight Loss Duration**: How long do you plan to work on your weight loss goals? Please select a duration that suits you.
* **Weight Loss Progress**: Please track your progress and note any milestones achieved.
* **Weight Loss Advice**: Is there anything specific you would like guidance on regarding your weight loss journey?
* **Weight Loss Outcomes**: What are your expected outcomes or results from your weight loss journey? Please be specific.
* **Weight Loss Recommendations**: Are there any specific weight loss recommendations you would like to follow?
* **Weight Loss Follow Up**: Would you like to be followed up with regarding your weight loss progress?
* **Weight Loss Comments**: Is there anything else you'd like to share about your weight loss journey?

Please review your answers carefully and submit the form when you are finished. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
