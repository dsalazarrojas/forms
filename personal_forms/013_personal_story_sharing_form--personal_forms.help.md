# Personal Story Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Story Sharing Form is designed to collect personal stories and experiences from users. The information collected will be stored and potentially shared among users with similar experiences or backgrounds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field. This is a required field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. Select your **Organization** type from the dropdown menu: "Community Organization", "Educational Institution", or "Other".
5. Choose your **Role** in the organization: "Student", "Faculty", "Staff", or "Other".
6. Enter the **Date** of the event or experience that you'd like to share.
7. Enter the **Time** of the event or experience.
8. Provide your **Phone number** in the "Phone" field. This is optional.
9. Add any additional **Notes** about your experience or story in the "Notes" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Organization** (select multiple, optional): Select the type of organization you are affiliated with.
* **Role** (select one, optional): Choose your role in the organization.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the event or experience.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the event or experience.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you about your submission.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional information about your experience or story.
