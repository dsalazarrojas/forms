# Literature Personal Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential personal details from individuals interested in the literature program. Please complete this form accurately to ensure a successful evaluation and assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the instructions carefully before starting the form.
2. Fill in your name, email, phone number, date of birth, and address in the relevant fields.
3. For the "Program Participants" field, select "Yes" if you are interested in being part of the literature program or "No" if you are not.
4. In the "Program Interest" field, describe your interest in the literature program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (name)**: A field to enter your name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: A valid email address where you can be contacted for further information or updates.
* **Phone (phone)**: A contact phone number where you can be reached for additional questions or verification.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: The date you were born, used for verification and assessment purposes.
* **Address (address)**: Your current residential address.
* **Program Participants (program_participants)**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in being part of the literature program or "No" if you are not.
* **Program Interest (literature_program_interest)**: Briefly describe your interest in the literature program.

## Tips
- Ensure your answers are accurate and truthful to the best of your abilities.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to the program administrators for assistance.
- Double-check your submissions before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- Please submit the form only if you have the necessary permissions or authorizations to do so.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
