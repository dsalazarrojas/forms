# Secret Santa Form For Kids - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Form For Kids is a tool used to collect information for a Secret Santa gift exchange among children. It helps participants know who to get gifts for during the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the child who will receive the gift in the **Recipient Name** field.
2. Choose the gift idea you suggest for the recipient in the **Gift Suggestion** field. Options are: Santa, Elf, Friend, Family Member.
3. If you'd like, leave a message for Santa for the recipient in the **Message To Santa** field.
4. If you'd like, leave a message for the recipient's parents in the **Message To Parents** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the child who will receive the gift.
- **Gift Suggestion**: Suggest an idea for a gift that the recipient might like. You can choose from options: Santa, Elf, Friend, Family Member.
- **Message To Santa**: Leave a message to Santa with any information or thoughts about the recipient. This is optional.
- **Message To Parents**: Leave a message to the recipient's parents with any information or thoughts. This is optional.
