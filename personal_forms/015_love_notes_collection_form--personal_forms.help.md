# Love Notes Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Love Notes Collection Form, which is designed to collect love notes, messages, and memories from users about their significant other. The form is intended to help users express their feelings and thoughts about their partner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your significant other.
2. Select the date of the event or occasion.
3. Choose the location of the event or occasion.
4. Describe your love for your partner (optional).
5. Share a favorite memory with your partner (optional).
6. Write a special message or note for your partner (optional).
7. Share a love story about your partner (optional).
8. Attach a photo of you and your partner (optional).
9. Indicate if you'd like to send a love gift to your partner (optional).
10. Write any additional love notes or messages (optional).
11. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What's your significant other's name? (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the person you're expressing your love for.
* **2. Date of the event or occasion (Date, Required)**: Select the date of the event or occasion that's relevant to your love story.
* **3. Location of the event or occasion (Text, Required)**: Enter the location of the event or occasion that's relevant to your love story.
* **4. Describe your love for your partner (Text, Optional)**: Share your feelings and thoughts about your significant other. This is an optional field, but it's a great opportunity to express your love and admiration.
* **5. Do you have a favorite memory with your significant other? (Text, Optional)**: If you have a special memory with your partner, share it here.
* **6. Special message or note for your significant other (Text, Optional)**: Write a personal message or note for your partner.
* **7. Love story about your significant other (Text, Optional)**: Share a story about your relationship or love journey.
* **8. Attach a photo of you and your significant other (Text, Optional)**: Upload a photo that represents your love story.
* **9. Would you like to send a love gift to your significant other? (Text, Optional)**: Indicate if you'd like to send a love gift to your partner.
* **10. Additional love notes or messages (Text, Optional)**: Share any additional thoughts or feelings you'd like to express to your partner.
* **11. Submit (Text, Required)**: Click this button to complete the form.

Note: The fields with "Required" status are essential to complete the form, while the fields with "Optional" status can be filled in if the user wishes to provide more information.
