# Profile Picture Background Selection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to select the background style and upload a profile picture for your account.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a background style from the options provided.
2. If desired, upload a profile photo.
3. Optionally, indicate your consent for your image to be used for official purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **background_style**: Select the background style for your profile. Options: Yes, No.
	+ This option determines if you want to use a background style for your profile or not.
* **profile_photo**: Upload a profile photo.
	+ This field allows you to upload a photo to use as your profile picture.
* **consent**: Indicate your willingness to allow your image to be used for official purposes.
	+ If you select "I am willing to allow my image to be used for official purposes", your profile photo will be used for official purposes such as in our system's directory or other official settings. If you select "I am not willing to allow my image to be used for official purposes", your profile photo will not be used for official purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to select a background style that fits your personal preference.
- Choose a high-quality profile photo that represents you well.
- Consider the implications of allowing or not allowing your image to be used for official purposes before making your selection.
