# Personal Organizer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Organizer Feedback Form is a survey designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with personal organizers. Your input will help us improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much information as possible.
2. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability, and provide clear and concise responses.
3. Select the appropriate options for each field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details**: Please enter your name and other personal information as requested.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Feedback**: Please select all that apply regarding your experience with the personal organizer.
	+ Yes: You have used the personal organizer and would recommend it to others.
	+ No: You haven't used the personal organizer.
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date you completed the personal organizer and the time it took to complete.
* **Time**: Enter the time it took to complete the personal organizer.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback, as this will help us improve our services.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the space provided.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
