# Personal Budget Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Budget Tracking Form is a simple tool designed to help users track their income, expenses, and set financial goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read through the form carefully**: Familiarize yourself with each section and the fields within.
2. **Enter your monthly income**: Choose the option that best represents your monthly income from the "Monthly Income" field.
3. **List your expenses**: In the "Expenses" field, select all expenses that apply.
4. **Set your financial goals**: From the "Financial Goals" field, select a goal that aligns with your priorities.
5. **Track your progress**: In the "Budget Progress" field, record your progress over time.
6. **Add additional notes**: Use the "Additional Notes" field for any comments or thoughts.
