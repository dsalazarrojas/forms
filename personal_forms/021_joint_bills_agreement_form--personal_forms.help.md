# Joint Bills Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Bills Agreement Form is a document used for managing joint bills between flatmates or roommates. It collects information about the users, the division of expenses, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including your name and any other details required.
2. Fill in the details about your roommate(s) in the "Roommate Info" section.
3. Enter the bills information, including any relevant details about the bills.
4. Specify how the expenses will be divided (50-50, 60-40, 70-30, etc.).
5. Enter any additional expenses or notes if necessary.
6. Select a date for the confirmation and submission of the form.
7. Choose a date for submission of the form.
8. Provide your name for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and any other details required.
* **Roommate Info** (Text, Optional): Provide details about your roommate(s), such as their name and contact information.
* **Bills Info** (Text, Optional): Enter details about the bills you want to manage, such as rent, utilities, etc.
* **Division** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how the expenses will be divided between you and your roommate(s).
* **Expenses** (Number, Optional): Enter any additional expenses or costs.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the bills or agreement.
* **Confirmation Date** (Date, Optional): Select a date to confirm the agreement.
* **Submission Date** (Date, Optional): Select a date for submission of the form.
* **Submitted By** (Text, Optional): Enter your name for submission.

Note: Each field is optional, but filling out each field is recommended to ensure accurate and complete information about the joint bills agreement.
