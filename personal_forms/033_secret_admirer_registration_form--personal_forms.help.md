# Secret Admirer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Admirer Registration Form is used to register the contact information, details, and preferences of a secret admirer for a special event. This form is essential for ensuring that the admirer's wishes are considered and respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required details in the User Details section (id: 1).
2. Provide the desired details in the Crush Details section (id: 2).
3. Choose the delivery preferences (id: 3) that suit the admirer.
4. Enter the delivery address (id: 4) where the gift will be delivered (if applicable).
5. Write a message for the crush (id: 5).
6. Provide event details (id: 6).
7. Select the theme of the event (id: 7) (if applicable).
8. Select a date and time (id: 8 and id: 9) for the event.
9. Enter the admirer's email address (id: 10).
10. Enter the admirer's phone number (id: 11).
11. Add any additional notes (id: 12) about the event.
12. Confirm the submission by pressing the Submit button (id: 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (id: 1): Enter the secret admirer's name and other relevant details.
* **Crush Details** (id: 2): Describe the person the admirer has a crush on, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Delivery Preferences** (id: 3): Select 'Yes' if you want the gift to be delivered to the secret admirer, or 'No' if you do not want it delivered.
* **Delivery Address** (id: 4): Enter the address where the gift will be delivered (if delivery is preferred).
* **Crush Message** (id: 5): Write a heartfelt message to be delivered with the gift to the crush.
* **Event Details** (id: 6): Enter the date of the event.
* **Theme** (id: 7): Select 'Yes' if you want a specific theme for the event, or 'No' if you do not care about the theme.
* **Date and Time** (id: 8 and id: 9): Enter the date and time of the event.
* **Email** (id: 10): Enter the secret admirer's email address.
* **Phone** (id: 11): Enter the secret admirer's phone number.
* **Note** (id: 12): Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Submit** (id: 13): Confirm the submission of the form.
* **Back** (id: 14): This is not a required field, so ignore it.

Note: This guide is meant to be a general help for end users, not a comprehensive guide for every possible event scenario. If you have specific requirements or requests, please refer to your event coordinator.
