# Daily Personal Planner - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Personal Planner is a simple and user-friendly form designed to help you plan your day in a structured and efficient way. It allows you to input your tasks, appointments, and other activities for a better understanding of your daily commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in the form sections as per your daily schedule and activities.
* For each section, use the provided options or input your own text.
* Make sure to input your email address and phone number for easy reference.
* Once you have filled in all the required fields, submit the form to review and save your planner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Planner** (`main_page`, text, optional): This is the main page where you can write in your daily planner details. You can input your thoughts, tasks, and other activities to plan your day.
* **Daily Tasks** (`tasks`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more tasks from the available options: `Work`, `Personal`, `Errands`. These tasks are used to categorize your activities for easier reference.
* **Scheduled Appointments** (`appointments`, select_one, optional): Select a time slot for your appointment from the available options: `8:00 am - 9:00 am`, `10:00 am - 11:00 am`, `12:00 pm - 1:00 pm`, `2:00 pm - 3:00 pm`, `4:00 pm - 5:00 pm`, `6:00 pm - 7:00 pm`, `7:00 pm - 8:00 pm`, `8:00 pm - 9:00 pm`, `9:00 pm - 10:00 pm`. This field is used to input your scheduled appointments.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is where you can input any additional notes or comments about your activities.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Input your email address for easy reference.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Input your phone number for easy reference.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Input the time for any activity.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Input the date for any activity.
* **Submit** (`submit-button`, text, optional): Once you have filled in all the fields, click on this submit button to review and save your planner.

## Tips
* Make sure to input accurate and up-to-date information for better planning and reference.
* Use the provided options for tasks and appointments to categorize your activities.
* Input your email and phone number for easy reference.
* Use the notes field to add any additional comments or thoughts about your activities.
* Review and save your planner before closing the form.
