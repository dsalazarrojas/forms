# Personal Journal Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Journal Entry Form is designed to help users record and reflect on their thoughts, experiences, and events throughout the day. It's a tool for personal reflection and self-awareness, allowing users to track their progress, habits, and feelings over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the journal entry in the `entry_date` field. This is a required field, so please make sure to select the correct date.
2. Choose a time for the journal entry in the `entry_time` field. This is optional, but it can help you track when you're making this entry.
3. Enter your personal thoughts, experiences, or events in the `entry_content` field. This is also required, so please make a sincere effort to share your thoughts.
4. In the `tags` field, choose any relevant tags that describe your entry (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', etc.). This is optional, but it can help you categorize and reflect on your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **entry_date_label** (entry_date, date, required): Enter the date of the journal entry in this field. Make sure to select the correct date.
* **entry_time_label** (entry_time, time, optional): Enter the time of the journal entry if desired. This can help track when you're making the entry.
* **entry_content_label** (entry_content, text, required): Enter your personal thoughts, experiences, or events. This is the main field where you can express yourself.
* **tags_label** (tags, select_multiple, optional): Choose any relevant tags that describe your entry (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', etc.).
