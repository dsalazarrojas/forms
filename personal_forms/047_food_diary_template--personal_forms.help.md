# Food Diary Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Diary Template is a simple form to help users track their daily food intake. It consists of multiple pages, each covering different meals and additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Food Diary Form, providing an overview of your daily food intake.
2. Select your breakfast options from the Breakfast page.
3. Move on to the Lunch page and choose your lunch options.
4. Continue to the Dinner page and select your dinner options.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes page.
6. Finally, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Diary Form** (`food_diary_form`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your daily food intake.
* **Breakfast** (`breakfast_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from a variety of breakfast options: Bacon, Eggs, Pancakes, Orange, Yogurt.
* **Lunch** (`lunch_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from a list of lunch options: Sandwich, Salad, Soup, Pizza.
* **Dinner** (`dinner_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from a list of dinner options: Steak, Chicken, Fish, Salad.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional information or comments about your day.
* **Save And Share** (`save_and_share`, text, required: false): Enter your food diary name or description.
* **Save** (`save_button`, text, required: false): Submit your form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your food diary entry.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your food diary entry.
* **Food Log Form** (`food_log_form`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your food log.
* **Food Log** (`food_log`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from a list of food log options: Apples, Bananas, Carrots, Cherries, Oranges.
* **Food Log Form 2** (`food_log_form_2`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your food log 2.
* **Food Log 2** (`food_log_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from a list of food log 2 options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required: false): Enter your weight in kg or lbs.
* **Height** (`height`, number, required: false): Enter your height in cm or inches.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Goal Weight** (`goal_weight`, number, required: false): Enter your goal weight in kg or lbs.
* **Goal Weight Form** (`goal_weight_form`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your goal weight.
* **Goal** (`goal`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your goal.
* **Food Diary Form 2** (`food_diary_form_2`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your food diary 2.
* **Goal Form** (`goal_form`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your goal form.
* **Goal Form 2** (`goal_form_2`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your goal form 2.
* **Food Log Form 3** (`food_log_form_3`, text, required: false): Enter a description of your food log 3.
* **Food Log 3** (`food_log_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from a list of food log 3 options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Submit your form.
