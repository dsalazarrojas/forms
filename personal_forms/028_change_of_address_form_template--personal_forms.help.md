# Change Of Address Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Change of Address Form Template is a document designed to collect information for updating a customer's or individual's address in a system or database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date the form was submitted.
2. Enter your full name.
3. If applicable, enter your account or reference number.
4. Enter your customer ID (if applicable).
5. Enter your current email address.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Enter your former address details (street, city, state/province, zip/postal code, and country).
8. Enter the date you moved from your former address.
9. Enter the date you moved to your new address.
10. Select the type of move (residential, business, or both).
11. Indicate whether services should be continued at the new address.
12. Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission Date** (1): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Full Name** (2): Enter your full legal name.
* **Account or Reference Number** (3): If applicable, enter the account or reference number associated with your former address (if you have one).
* **Customer ID** (4): If applicable, enter your customer identification number (if you have one).
* **Email Address** (5): Enter your current contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (6): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Former Address - Street** (7): Enter the street of your former address.
* **Former Address - City** (8): Enter the city of your former address.
* **Former Address - State/Province** (9): Enter the state or province of your former address.
* **Former Address - Zip/Postal Code** (10): Enter the postal code of your former address.
* **Former Address - Country** (11): Enter the country of your former address (if applicable).
* **Date Moving From Former Address** (12): Enter the date you moved from your former address.
* **New Address - Street** (13): Enter the street of your new address.
* **New Address - City** (14): Enter the city of your new address.
* **New Address - State/Province** (15): Enter the state or province of your new address.
* **New Address - Zip/Postal Code** (16): Enter the postal code of your new address.
* **New Address - Country** (17): Enter the country of your new address (if applicable).
* **Date Moving to New Address** (18): Enter the date you moved to your new address.
* **Type of Move** (19): Select the type of move (residential, business, or both).
* **Service Continuation Requested** (20): Select whether services should be continued at the new address.
* **I Confirm the Above Information is Accurate** (21): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
