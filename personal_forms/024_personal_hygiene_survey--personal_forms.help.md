# Personal Hygiene Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on personal hygiene practices to understand individual habits and behaviors related to physical and mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Answer all questions carefully and accurately.
* Provide your best effort to answer all fields, including those that may seem irrelevant or uncomfortable.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
