# Couples Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Couples Survey is a form designed to gather information about a couple's relationship dynamics, focusing on communication style, conflict resolution, and overall relationship satisfaction. This form will help you gain insight into your relationship and provide valuable information for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and choose the response that best describes your situation.
2.  Complete each field according to your honest assessment of your relationship.
3.  Ensure you answer all questions, as they are all relevant to the form's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Introduction** (couples_survey_start, select_multiple, required: false)
This question asks if you and your partner have a romantic relationship. Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you do not.

### **2. Partner 1 Name** (partner1_name, text, required: false)
Enter the name of your partner. This field is not required but will help us get a better understanding of your relationship.

### **3. Partner 1 Age** (partner1_age, number, required: false)
Enter your partner's age. This field is optional but will provide more context to your answers.

### **4. Partner 2 Age** (partner2_age, number, required: false)
Enter your partner's age. This field is optional but will provide more context to your answers.

### **5. Partner 1 Gender** (partner1_gender, select_one, required: false)
Select your partner's gender.

### **6. Partner 2 Gender** (partner2_gender, select_one, required: false)
Select your partner's gender.

### **7. Relationship Type** (relationship_type, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the type of relationship you have with your partner (e.g., romantic relationship, friendship, family member, coworker, etc.).

### **8. Partner 1 Marital Status** (partner1_marital_status, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your partner's marital status (e.g., married, engaged, divorced, not married, etc.).

### **9. Partner 2 Marital Status** (partner2_marital_status, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your partner's marital status (e.g., married, engaged, divorced, not married, etc.).

### **10. Communication Style** (communication_style, select_multiple, required: false)
Rate your communication style with your partner (good, bad, average).

### **11. Conflict Frequency** (conflict_frequency, select_multiple, required: false)
Select how often you experience conflict with your partner (often, rarely, never).

### **12. Conflict Resolution** (conflict_resolution, select_multiple, required: false)
Select how you typically resolve conflicts with your partner (resolve, avoid, compromise).

### **13. Conflict Topics** (conflict_topics, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the topics that often lead to conflict with your partner (finances, family, intimacy, etc.).

### **14. Partner 1 Emotions** (partner1_emotions, number, required: false)

### **15. Partner 2 Emotions** (partner2_emotions, number, required: false)

### **16. Partner 1 Satisfaction** (partner1_satisfaction, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your level of satisfaction with your partner (satisfied, not satisfied).

### **17. Partner 2 Satisfaction** (partner2_satisfaction, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your partner's level of satisfaction with you (satisfied, not satisfied).

### **18. Partner 1 Trust** (partner1_trust, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your level of trust with your partner (trusted, not trusted).

### **19. Partner 2 Trust** (partner2_trust, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your partner's level of trust with you (trusted, not trusted).

### **20. Partner 1 Confidence** (partner1_confidence, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your level of confidence with your partner (high, low).

### **21. Partner 2 Confidence** (partner2_confidence, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your partner's level of confidence with you (high, low).

### **22. Partner 1 Fear** (partner1_fear, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your level of fear with your partner (fear, not fear).

### **23. Partner 2 Fear** (partner2_fear, select_multiple, required: false)
