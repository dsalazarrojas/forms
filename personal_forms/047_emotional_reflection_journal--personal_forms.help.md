# Emotional Reflection Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional Reflection Journal is a tool for tracking and reflecting on your emotional state over time. It helps you identify patterns and trends in your mood and intensity levels, allowing you to better understand yourself and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your mood for each day by choosing from the options provided.
2. Rate your intensity level for each day on a scale of 0-10.
3. Provide a brief explanation for your mood and intensity levels for each day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mood Today** (`mood_today_label`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how you feel today using the provided options: Very Happy, Happy, Neutral, Sad, Angry, or Anxious.
* **Intensity Today** (`intensity_today_label`, number, required: false): Rate your emotional intensity today on a scale of 0-10.
* **Mood Yesterday** (`mood_yesterday_label`, select_one, required: false): Reflect on how you felt yesterday, choosing one of the same options as today.
* **Intensity Yesterday** (`intensity_yesterday_label`, number, required: false): Rate your emotional intensity yesterday on a scale of 0-10.
* **Mood Tomorrow** (`mood_tomorrow_label`, select_multiple, required: false): Anticipate how you'll feel tomorrow, selecting one or multiple options.
* **Intensity Tomorrow** (`intensity_tomorrow_label`, number, required: false): Estimate your emotional intensity tomorrow on a scale of 0-10.
* **Mood Why** (`mood_why_label`, text, required: false): Share a brief explanation for your mood today.
* **Intensity Why** (`intensity_why_label`, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for your intensity today.
* **Mood Comments** (`mood_comments_label`, text, required: false): Add any additional thoughts or comments about your mood today.
* **Intensity Comments** (`intensity_comments_label`, text, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your intensity today.

## Tips
* Try to be as accurate and honest as possible when selecting your mood and intensity levels.
* Use the provided options as a guide, but feel free to add any additional comments or explanations if you need to.
* Reflecting on your emotional state regularly can help you develop greater self-awareness and make positive changes in your life.
