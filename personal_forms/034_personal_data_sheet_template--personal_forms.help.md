# personal_data_sheet_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects personal data for employees, including their name, contact information, job details, and work schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about yourself.
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields (marked as such) to ensure that your data is correctly recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
- **Email Address** (email): Enter your official email address.
- **Job Title** (text): Enter your official job title.
- **Job Title Abbreviation** (text): Enter an abbreviation for your job title (optional).
- **Department** (text): Enter the department you belong to (optional).
- **Job Level** (select multiple): Select one or more of the following options:
  - High
  - Low
- **Job Family** (select multiple): Select one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Job Function** (select multiple): Select one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Job Group** (select multiple): Select one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Location** (text): Enter your work location (optional).
- **Date Hired** (date): Enter the date you started working.
- **Date Departed** (date): Enter the date you left the job (if applicable).
- **Manager** (text): Enter the name of your manager (optional).
- **Manager** (text): Enter the name of your manager (optional, duplicate of field 'Manager' for redundancy).
- **Team** (text): Enter the name of your team (optional).
- **Reporting Line** (text): Enter your reporting line (optional).
- **Job Description** (text): Enter a brief description of your job (optional).
- **Work Hours** (time): Enter your work hours in 24-hour format (optional).
- **Work Days** (time): Enter your work days in 24-hour format (optional).
- **Work Hours Overnight** (time): Enter your work hours overnight in 24-hour format (optional).
- **Work Hours Weekends** (time): Enter your work hours on weekends in 24-hour format (optional).
- **Job Classification** (text): Enter a classification for your job (optional).
- **Job Category** (text): Enter a category for your job (optional).
- **Job Type** (text): Enter the type of job you perform (optional).
