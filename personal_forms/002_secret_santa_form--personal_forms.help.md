# Secret Santa Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Form is designed to collect information about a person's preferences and interests, enabling the Secret Santa organizers to choose a suitable gift that suits their taste and liking. This form is used to ensure that the gift-giver and the gift-receiver have a harmonious and enjoyable gift-exchange experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone (optional)**.
2. Set your **Budget** for the gift you're willing to spend.
3. Choose a **Gift** from the options provided.
4. Provide the **Recipient's Name**, **Date of Birth**, and a short **Gift Message** for the selected gift.
5. You may also add some extra notes about the recipient in the **Notes** field.
6. Select your **Favorite Color**, **Favorite Food**, **Favorite Hobby**, **Favorite Place**, **Favorite Singer**, **Favorite Band**, **Favorite Book**, **Favorite Movie**, **Favorite Game**, **Favorite Holiday**, **Favorite Sport**, **Favorite Athlete**, **Favorite Band Album**, **Favorite Comedian**, **Favorite TV Show**, and **Favorite TV Series** from the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Required)**: Fill in your name to let the gift-giver know who you are.
* **Email (Required)**: Provide your email address so the Secret Santa organizers can contact you.
* **Phone (Optional)**: If you'd like to provide your phone number for any reason, you can do so here.
* **Budget (Required)**: Set a budget for the gift you're willing to spend to ensure that the gift-giver knows how much to spend on you.
* **Gift (Required)**: Select a gift that you'd like to receive from the options provided.
* **Recipient Name (Required)**: Fill in the name of the person you're gifting for.
* **Date of Birth (Required)**: Provide the date of birth of the recipient.
* **Gift Message (Required)**: Add a short message for the gift you've selected for the recipient.
* **Notes (Optional)**: If you'd like to add any notes about the recipient, you can do so here.
* **Favorite Color, Favorite Food, Favorite Hobby, Favorite Place, Favorite Singer, Favorite Band, Favorite Book, Favorite Movie, Favorite Game, Favorite Holiday, Favorite Sport, Favorite Athlete, Favorite Band Album, Favorite Comedian, Favorite TV Show, Favorite TV Series, and Favorite Singer**: Select your favorite from each option to give the Secret Santa organizers a better insight of your interests.

Tips:

* Be as precise and honest as possible when filling the form to give accurate information.
* Make sure to set a reasonable and realistic budget for the gift.
* You can leave the **Phone**, **Notes**, **Favorite Holiday, Favorite Sport, Favorite Athlete, Favorite Band Album, Favorite Comedian, Favorite TV Show, and Favorite TV Series** fields empty if you're not comfortable filling them.
* If you have any doubts or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the Secret Santa organizers.
