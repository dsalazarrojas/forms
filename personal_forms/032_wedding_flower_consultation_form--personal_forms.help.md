# Wedding Flower Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Flower Consultation Form is designed to gather information from customers for wedding flower consultation purposes. Please fill in the form with your details to help us better understand your preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), and any other fields you'd like to provide for a more accurate consultation.
2. Select the type of flowers and flower color you prefer.
3. Choose the number of guests attending your wedding.
4. Enter the date of your wedding.
5. If you have any special requests, please let us know in the special requests field.
6. Provide your contact number so we can get in touch with you.
7. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name**: This is where you will provide your name.
	+ Please fill in the name you'd like to be referred to during the consultation.
* **What type of flowers**: This is where you select the type of flowers you are interested in.
	+ Choose from the list of options: Rose, Carnation, Orchid, Lillies, Sunflower.
* **What flower color**: This is where you select the flower color you prefer.
	+ Choose from the list of options: Red, Pink, Purple, White, Yellow, Green.
* **Number of guests**: This is where you select the number of guests attending your wedding.
	+ Choose from the list of options: 20, 40, 50, 60, 70.
* **Date of wedding**: This is where you enter the date of your wedding.
	+ Use the date format: dd/mm/yyyy (e.g., 01/06/2024).
* **Any special requests?**: This is where you can add any specific requests or instructions for your wedding flowers.
	+ Please provide any special requests you'd like to make.
* **Contact number**: This is where you provide your contact number.
	+ Please fill in your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Customer email**: This is where you provide your email address.
	+ Please fill in your email address so we can contact you if needed.
