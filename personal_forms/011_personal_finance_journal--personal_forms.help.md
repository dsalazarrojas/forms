# personal finance journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Finance Journal is designed to help you track your personal finance data in a structured and organized manner. It will collect information on income, expenses, savings goals, and other relevant details to provide a comprehensive view of your financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the income section with your current income details, including how often you receive income and the amount you earn.
2. Enter your expenses, including how often you have expenses and the amount you spend.
3. Set your savings goals, including the amount you aim to save and any specific goals you have in mind.
4. Identify your income source (e.g., salary, investment, freelance).
5. Identify your expense source (e.g., credit card, loan, utility bill).
6. Add any additional notes about your financial situation.
7. Optionally, enter a date to track your progress over time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Income**: Enter your current income details.
	+ **Type**: Enter how often you receive income (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually).
	+ **Amount**: Enter the amount you earn.
* **Expenses**: Enter your current expenses details.
	+ **Type**: Enter how often you have expenses (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
	+ **Amount**: Enter the amount you spend.
* **Savings Goal**: Set a goal for the amount you aim to save.
* **Income Frequency**: Choose how often you receive income.
* **Income Amount**: Enter the amount you earn.
* **Expense Frequency**: Choose how often you have expenses.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the amount you spend.
* **Savings Goal**: Set a goal for the amount you aim to save.
* **Income Source**: Choose your income source (e.g., salary, investment, freelance).
* **Expense Source**: Choose your expense source (e.g., credit card, loan, utility bill).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your financial situation.
* **Date**: Optionally, enter a date to track your progress over time.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to get the most out of your personalized financial report.
- Review and update your form regularly to reflect changes in your financial situation.
- Use the notes field to jot down any important financial events or decisions you've made.
