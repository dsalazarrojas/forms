# Marriage Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marriage Satisfaction Survey is a tool designed to collect information about relationships and overall satisfaction levels. It's essential to provide honest and accurate answers to help researchers and experts in the field better understand relationships and their aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please answer the questions as accurately and honestly as you can. You can choose from the options provided for each question, and you're not required to answer all the questions if you don't feel comfortable doing so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Length** (1): This question asks about the length of your current relationship. Please answer with the number of years you've been in the relationship.
* **Communication Style** (2): This question inquires about how you usually communicate with your partner. Answer with the frequency or style of communication you use (e.g., "most of the time," "rarely," etc.).
* **Conflict Resolution** (3): This question asks about your experience with conflicts in your relationship. Choose how you usually resolve conflicts with your partner (e.g., "talk it out," "avoid talking," etc.).
* **Intimacy Level** (4): This question asks about your level of intimacy with your partner. Please choose the level of intimacy you experience most frequently (e.g., "extremely high," "very high," etc.).
* **Emotional Intelligence** (5): This question asks about your partner's emotional intelligence. Choose how you rate their emotional intelligence (e.g., "very good," "somewhat good," etc.).
* **Conflict Resolution Frequency** (6): This question is similar to question 3 but asks for the frequency of conflict resolution (e.g., "most of the time," "rarely," etc.).
* **Trust Level** (7): This question asks about the level of trust in your relationship. Please choose how you rate your trust level (e.g., "extremely high," "very high," etc.).
* **Commitment Level** (8): This question asks about your level of commitment to your relationship. Choose how you rate your level of commitment (e.g., "extremely high," "very high," etc.).
* **Decision Making** (9): This question asks about how decisions are made in your relationship. Please answer with the frequency or style of decision making (e.g., "both partners agree," "one partner makes decision," etc.).
* **Emotional Support** (10): This question asks about the level of emotional support in your relationship. Choose how you rate your emotional support (e.g., "always," "most of the time," etc.).
* **Relationship Status** (11): This question asks about your current relationship status. Please choose your current status (e.g., "living together," "separated," etc.).
* **Partner Satisfaction** (12): This question asks about your partner's satisfaction with your relationship. Please choose how you rate their satisfaction (e.g., "very satisfied," "somewhat satisfied," etc.).
* **Communication Frequency** (13): This question asks about the frequency of communication in your relationship. Please answer with the number of times you communicate per week (e.g., 10, 5, etc.).
* **Conflict Resolution Frequency** (14): This question is similar to question 6 but asks for the frequency of conflict resolution.
* **Intimacy Frequency** (15): This question asks about the frequency of intimacy in your relationship. Please answer with the number of times you experience intimacy per week (e.g., 5, 10, etc.).
* **Emotional Intelligence Frequency** (16): This question is similar to question 5 but asks for the frequency of emotional intelligence.
* **Emotional Support Frequency** (17): This question asks about the frequency of emotional support in your relationship. Please answer with the number of times you receive emotional support per week (e.g., 5, 10, etc.).
* **Relationship Status Ever** (18): This question asks about your relationship status at any point in the past. Please choose your past relationship status (e.g., "living together," "separated," etc.).
* **Partner Satisfaction Ever** (19): This question asks about your partner's satisfaction at any point in the past. Please choose how they were satisfied (e.g., "very satisfied," "somewhat satisfied," etc.).
* **Communication Frequency Ever** (20): This question asks about the frequency of communication in your relationship at any point in the past. Please answer with the number of times you communicated per week (e.g., 5, 10, etc.).
* **Conflict Resolution Frequency Ever** (21): This question is similar to question 14 but asks for the frequency of conflict resolution at any point in the past.
* **Intimacy Frequency Ever** (22): This question asks about the frequency of intimacy in your relationship at any point in the past. Please answer with the number of times you experienced intimacy per week (e.g., 5, 10, etc.).
* **Emotional Intelligence Frequency Ever** (23): This question is similar to question 16 but asks for the frequency of emotional intelligence at any point in the past.
* **Emotional Support Frequency Ever** (24): This question asks about the frequency of emotional support in your relationship at any point in the past. Please answer with the number of times you received emotional support per week (e.g., 5, 10, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction Ever** (25): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your relationship at any point in the past. Please choose how satisfied you were (e.g., "very satisfied," "somewhat satisfied," etc.).

## Tips
Please answer the questions as accurately and honestly as you can. Remember, this survey is designed to help researchers and experts in the field better understand relationships and their aspects. Your input is valuable and will contribute to a better understanding of relationships.
