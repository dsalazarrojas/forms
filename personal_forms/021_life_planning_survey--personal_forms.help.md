# Life Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This life planning survey is designed to collect personal information from users, likely for HR or HR-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Write down your goals for the form to understand your objectives.
5. Select your highest and second highest priority for the task or project.
6. Add any additional notes about yourself.
7. Enter your date of birth.
8. Enter your age.
9. Choose your occupation from the given options.
10. Select your gender.
11. Select your ethnicity.
12. (Optional) Enter the date.
13. (Optional) Enter the time.
14. Provide your address.
15. Enter your second highest and third highest priority for the task or project.
16. Enter your date of entry.
17. Enter your age of entry.
18. Select your ethnicity (this field is similar to the previous one).
19. Provide your second address.
20. Select your highest and lowest priority for the task or project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, **required**): Write down your goals for this task or project.
* **Priority 1** (`priority1`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your highest priority for the task or project.
* **Priority 2** (`priority2`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your second highest priority for the task or project.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, **required**): Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of birth.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, **required**): Enter your age.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your occupation from the given options.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your gender.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your ethnicity.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **optional**): (Optional) Enter the date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **optional**): (Optional) Enter the time.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, **required**): Provide your address.
* **Goals 2** (`goals2`, `text`, **required**): Write down your second goals for this task or project.
* **Priority 4** (`priority4`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select your second highest and third highest priority for the task or project.
* **Priority 5** (`priority5`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select your second highest and third highest priority for the task or project.
* **Date Of Entry** (`date_of_entry`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of entry.
* **Age Of Entry** (`age_of_entry`, `number`, **required**): Enter your age of entry.
* **Ethnicity 2** (`ethnicity2`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select your ethnicity.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, `text`, **required**): Provide your second address.
* **Priority 6** (`priority6`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select your highest and lowest priority for the task or project.

Note: The fields with `required` status are required to be filled in by the user.
