# Family Reunion T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from family members to print their name on a custom T shirt for the family reunion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Select your preferred **T Shirt Size** from the options provided (Small, Medium, or Large).
4. Choose your preferred **T Shirt Color** from the available options (Red, Blue, or Green). You can select multiple colors if desired.
5. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you with any order updates.
6. Fill in your **Phone** number in case we need to reach you regarding your order.
7. Provide the **Date of Reunion** to ensure we print the T shirt before the event.
8. Select the **Time of Reunion** if you'd like to specify a particular time for the order.
9. Write any additional **Shirt Text** you'd like to include on the T shirt, such as your name, a favorite quote, or a fun message.
10. Provide any **Order Notes** you'd like to include with your order, such as any special requests or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name to be printed on the T shirt.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the surname to be printed on the T shirt.
* **T Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Select from the options: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **T Shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from the available colors: Red, Blue, or Green. You can select multiple colors if desired.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive any order updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any order-related contacts.
* **Date of Reunion** (`date_of_reunion`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the family reunion.
* **Time of Reunion** (`time_of_reunion`, `time`, required: false): If desired, select the time of the reunion for a more precise order fulfillment.
* **Shirt Text** (`shirt_text`, `note`, required: false): Write any text to include on the T shirt, such as a name, quote, or message.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or instructions for your order, such as special requests or instructions.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled accurately to ensure your T shirt is printed correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
