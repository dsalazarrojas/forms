# Party Planning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Planning Checklist is a form designed to help you plan and organize parties and events. It guides you through the most important aspects to consider when planning a party, including party details, guest lists, food and drink, entertainment, decorations, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the party details on page 1, including the host's name and any other relevant information.
2. On page 2, select the guests you want to invite, and add them to your guest list.
3. On page 3, choose the food and drink options that you want to provide for your guests.
4. On page 4, decide on the entertainment options, such as music or games.
5. On page 5, select the decorations you want to use for your party.
6. On page 6, choose the favors you want to give to your guests.
7. In the "Additional Notes" field, add any additional comments or information that you want to include.
8. Finally, check the "Completed" box to indicate that you have finished filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Host**: Enter the name of the person hosting the party.
* **Guest count**: Enter the number of guests you expect to attend the party.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the party.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the party.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the party.
* **Guest list**: Select the guests you want to invite and add them to your list.
* **Food and drink**: Choose the food and drink options you want to provide.
* **Entertainment**: Select the entertainment options you want to include.
* **Decor**: Choose the decorations you want to use for your party.
* **Favors**: Select the favors you want to give to your guests.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information you want to include.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person hosting the party.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person hosting the party.
* **Other**: Enter any other information you want to include.
* **Checklist**: Select the items you want to include on your party checklist.
* **Status**: Check the status of your party planning (completed, not completed, or ongoing).

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that you don't forget any important details.
* Use the "Back" and "Next" buttons to navigate through the pages of the form.
* If you want to save your progress, click on the "Save" button.
* If you want to cancel your party planning, click on the "Cancel" button.
