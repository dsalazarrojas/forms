# Employee Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Information Form is designed to collect essential details about employees, including their name, contact information, job role, and assigned tools. This form serves as an HR-related activity, and its completion will aid in maintaining accurate employee records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last names in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 01/01/1990).
3. Provide your job title and department in the respective fields.
4. Enter a brief description of your job role in the job description field.
5. Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
6. Enter your phone number (optional).
7. Select the tool you are assigned to use from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (text): Enter your last name here.
* **Date of birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Job title** (text): Enter your job title here.
* **Department** (text): Enter your department here.
* **Job description** (text): Enter a brief description of your job role.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Assigned tool** (select_one): Select the tool assigned to you from the dropdown menu.
