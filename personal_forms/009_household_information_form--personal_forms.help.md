# Household Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Information Form is a collection of questions designed to gather demographic information about the household. This information will help us better understand your household's characteristics and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If a question is not applicable to your household, please select "Not applicable" or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Address <(Required: No)>
* **Address** (`household_address`, `text`, Required: No): Please enter your household's address in this field. This will help us understand where you live and work.

### Type <(Required: Yes)>
* **Type** (`household_type`, `select_one`, Required: Yes): Please select the type of household you have. You can choose between "Single" or "Married".

### Size <(Required: No)>
* **Size** (`household_size`, `select_multiple`, Required: No): Please select the size of your household. You can choose between "Small", "Medium", or "Large".

### Source <(Required: No)>
* **Source** (`income_source`, `select_multiple`, Required: No): Please select the sources of income for your household. You can choose between "Work", "Investment", or "Government".

### Monthly Income <(Required: Yes)>
* **Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`, `number`, Required: Yes): Please enter your household's monthly income.

### Annual Income <(Required: No)>
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, `text`, Required: No): Please enter your household's annual income.

### Occupation <(Required: No)>
* **Occupation** (`household_occupation`, `select_multiple`, Required: No): Please select the occupation(s) of the household members. You can choose between "Student", "Employee", or "Retired".

### Occupation Description <(Required: No)>
* **Occupation Description** (`occupation_description`, `select_multiple`, Required: No): Please select a brief description of your occupation. You can choose between "Engineer", "Doctor", or "Artist".

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
