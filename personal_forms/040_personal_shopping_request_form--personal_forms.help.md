# Personal Shopping Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Shopping Request Form is designed to collect basic contact information and a message from users. This form is meant to be used by customers or users who want to inquire about shopping-related services or make inquiries about specific products. It will help our team provide personalized attention and support to users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This will help our team address you properly and get to know you better.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This will help our team verify your identity and get your full contact information.
3. Provide a valid **Email Address** in the "Email" field. This is how we will be able to contact you for follow-up questions or further assistance.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field. This will allow us to contact you for additional information or to schedule appointments.
5. Write your **Message** in the text area. This is the space where you can share your shopping needs, questions, or comments with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name, as we will use this to address you properly.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Provide your last name for us to get your full name and contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or further assistance.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you by phone for further information.
* **Message** (`message`, note, Optional): Write a detailed message about your shopping needs, questions, or comments. This is the main channel of communication for us to get to know you better. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can contact you efficiently and effectively.
* If you have any attachments or files to share with us, please attach them to the email or message we send you.
* Our team will review your form submission and get back to you as soon as possible.
