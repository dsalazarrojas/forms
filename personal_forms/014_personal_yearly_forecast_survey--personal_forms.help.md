# Personal Yearly Forecast Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Yearly Forecast Survey is a tool to help you reflect on your past, present, and future goals, growth, and relationships. It's designed to help you gain insights into your personal development and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Take your time to carefully read each question and think about your responses before selecting an answer.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about a question, don't worry! Just do your best to respond with a thoughtful answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Past Year Experience (1)**
* **past_year_experience**: A space to note any significant events or experiences from the past year. This can be a good place to reflect on what worked well and what didn't.

**Past Year Learning (2)**
* **past_year_learning**: A field to note any new skills or knowledge you acquired in the past year. This can be a helpful way to track your personal growth and development.

**Past Year Growth (3)**
* **past_year_growth**: A question to reflect on whether you felt you grew or developed as a person in the past year. Select 'Yes' if you feel you progressed in your personal growth, and 'No' if you felt you didn't.

**Past Year Achievements (4)**
* **past_year_achievements**: A field to list any notable accomplishments or achievements from the past year. You can select multiple options to indicate areas where you felt accomplished.

**Past Year Relationships (5)**
* **past_year_relationships**: A note field to write about any significant relationships or interactions from the past year. This can be a good place to reflect on how you connected with others.

**Past Year Goals (6)**
* **past_year_goals**: A question to reflect on whether you met or made progress towards your goals in the past year. Select 'Yes' if you achieved your goals, and 'No' if you didn't.

**Upcoming Year Goals (7)**
* **upcoming_year_goals**: A question to reflect on your goals for the upcoming year. Select 'Yes' if you're committed to achieving these goals, and 'No' if you're unsure or haven't set goals yet.

**New Year Resolution (8)**
* **new_year_resolution**: A field to list any resolutions or commitments you're making for the upcoming year. This can be a helpful way to set intentions and track progress.

**Past Year Pain Points (9)**
* **past_year_pain_points**: A field to identify any pain points or challenges you faced in the past year. Select multiple options to indicate areas where you struggled.

**Upcoming Year Focus (10)**
* **upcoming_year_focus**: A question to reflect on where you'd like to focus your efforts in the upcoming year. Select 'Yes' if you're committed to focusing on this area, and 'No' if you're unsure or haven't decided yet.

**Upcoming Year Obstacles (11)**
* **upcoming_year_obstacles**: A field to identify any potential obstacles or challenges you anticipate facing in the upcoming year. Select multiple options to indicate areas where you might struggle.

**New Year Pain Points (12)**

**Personal Growth (13)**

**New Year Goals (14)**

**Upcoming Year Focus Areas (15)**
