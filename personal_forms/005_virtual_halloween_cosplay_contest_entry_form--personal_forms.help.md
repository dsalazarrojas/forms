# Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to enter their submission for the Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user information, including your name and contact details.
2. Enter your cosplay name and select your costume designer.
3. Add a URL where your image can be found.
4. Write a brief description of your cosplay.
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Enter your contact email address.
7. Enter your contact phone number.
8. Choose the type of image you're submitting (if applicable).
9. Select your submission status (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please fill in your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **Costume Designer** (`costume_designer`, text, optional): If you didn't design the costume yourself, please enter the name of the costume designer.
* **Cosplay Name** (`cosplay_name`, text, optional): This is the name of your cosplay.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your image or video submission.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Write a brief description of your cosplay (e.g., character, inspiration, etc.).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address so we can get back to you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Image Type** (`image_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of image you're submitting (Image 1, Image 2, or Image 3).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your submission (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
