# Hospice License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for hospice providers to apply for a hospice license. It gathers information necessary for the review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your personal details:
	+ **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **User Email**: Enter your email address associated with your professional license.
* Provide your contact information:
	+ **Contact Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
* Choose your services and facility information:
	+ **Facility Type**: Select one of the following options: Residential, Home Health Agency, or Hospice.
	+ **Services**: Select all applicable services provided by your facility (Medical, Counseling, Spiritual).
	+ **License Status**: Choose the current status of your license (Pending, Active, Expired).
* Enter dates and addresses:
	+ **License Date**: Enter the date your license was issued.
	+ **License Expiration Date**: Enter the date your license is set to expire.
	+ **Hospice License Date**: Enter the date your hospice license was issued.
	+ **Date of License**: Enter the date your license was issued.
* Confirm additional information:
	+ **Facility Address**: Enter your facility's address (optional).
	+ **City 2**: Enter the city where your facility is located (optional).
	+ **State 2**: Enter the state where your facility is located (optional).
	+ **Zip Code 2**: Enter the zip code of your facility (optional).
	+ **County**: Enter the county where your facility is located (optional).
	+ **Zip Code 3**: Enter the zip code for your facility (optional).
	+ **Facility Type 2**: Enter your facility type (optional).
	+ **License Expiration Date 2**: Enter the date your license is set to expire (optional).
	+ **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your facility (optional).
