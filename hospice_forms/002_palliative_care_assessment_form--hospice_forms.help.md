# Palliative Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Palliative Care Assessment Form is designed to gather information about a patient's medical condition, care needs, and other relevant details to provide effective palliative care. This form is used by healthcare professionals to assess a patient's condition and identify their needs for palliative care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth (DOB) in the "Patient DOB" field.
3. If applicable, enter the patient's disease or medical condition in the "Disease Condition" field.
4. Report the patient's current pain level on a scale of 0-10 in the "Pain Score 0-10" field.
5. Enter the patient's current weight in the "Patient Weight" field.
6. Identify the primary caregiver or family member responsible for the patient's care in the "Primary Caregiver" field.
7. Select the appropriate Hospice ID from the dropdown menu in the "Hospice ID" field.
8. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medication List" field.
9. Identify the social worker assigned to the patient's care in the "Social Worker" field.
10. Enter the name of the nurse conducting the assessment in the "Nurse Name" field.
11. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Disease Condition**: Enter the patient's current disease or medical condition, if applicable.
* **Pain Score 0-10**: Report the patient's current pain level on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no pain and 10 is severe pain.
* **Patient Weight**: Enter the patient's current weight.
* **Primary Caregiver**: Identify the primary caregiver or family member responsible for the patient's care.
* **Hospice ID**: Select the appropriate Hospice ID from the dropdown menu.
* **Medication List**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Social Worker**: Identify the social worker assigned to the patient's care.
* **Nurse Name**: Enter the name of the nurse conducting the assessment.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
