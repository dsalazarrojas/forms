# Palliative Care Storytelling Film Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information for a palliative care storytelling project.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the patient from the options provided.
2.  Provide information about the storyteller, including their name, contact information, and title.
3.  Enter details about the film, including the title, description, date, time, location, video file, and video title.
4.  Provide consent to record, share, and publish the patient's story.
5.  Upload the video file and link to it.
6.  Enter the length of the story in minutes.
7.  Provide consent to delete the story in the future.
8.  Enter any additional contact information for the storyteller.

## Tips

*   This form should be completed by the patient or their representative.
*   Make sure to provide accurate and clear information about the patient's story and the filmmaker.
*   Ensure that the patient's consent is obtained for recording, sharing, and publishing their story.
*   Be mindful of the patient's privacy and dignity throughout the process.
*   Ensure that the video file is uploaded correctly and the link is accurate.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary information is provided before submitting.
