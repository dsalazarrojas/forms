# Purpose
The Memorial Planning Survey is a form designed to collect preferences for memorial and funeral planning. This form helps ensure that the wishes and needs of the deceased and their loved ones are respected and met during the memorialization process. It is essential to complete this form accurately to guarantee a smooth and respectful planning process.
# How To Complete This Form
To complete the Memorial Planning Survey, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question carefully, as your responses will help create a meaningful and personalized memorialization experience.
2. Select the options that best reflect your wishes and needs.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields (e.g., viewing preferences, other preferences).
# Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Memorial Preferences** (1): Select the options that best represent your memorialization preferences.
* **Funeral Home Preferences** (2): Choose the type of funeral home you prefer.
* **Cemetery Preferences** (3): Select the options that best represent your cemetery preferences.
* **Grave Preferences** (4): Choose the options that best represent your grave preferences.
* **Flower Preferences** (5): Select the type of flowers you prefer.
* **Memorial Preferences** (6): This field is a note for any additional memorial preferences.
* **Service Preferences** (7): Provide a brief description of your service preferences.
* **Visitation Preferences** (8): Choose a date for your preferred visitation.
* **Burial Preferences** (9): Select the options that best represent your burial preferences.
* **Ceremony Preferences** (10): Choose the type of ceremony you prefer.
* **Casket Preferences** (11): Select the type of casket you prefer.
* **Urn Preferences** (12): Choose the type of urn you prefer.
* **Headstone Preferences** (13): Select the type of headstone you prefer.
* **Ebalming Preferences** (14): Choose whether you want to be embalmed.
* **Autopsy Preferences** (15): Select whether you prefer a autopsy.
* **Organ Donation Preferences** (16): Choose whether you want to donate organs.
* **Medical Removal Preferences** (17): Select whether you want to remove organs.
* **Body Temperature Preferences** (18): Choose how you prefer to be kept.
* **Embalm Preferences** (19): Choose whether you want to be embalmed.
* **Viewing Preferences** (20): Provide a brief description of your viewing preferences.
* **Visitation Preferences 2** (21): Provide a brief description of your second visitation preferences.
* **Ceremony Preferences 2** (22): Provide a brief description of your second ceremony preferences.
* **Casket Preferences 2** (23): Provide a brief description of your second casket preferences.
* **Cemetery Preferences 2** (24): Provide a brief description of your second cemetery preferences.
* **Other Preferences** (25): Enter any other preferences you may have.
