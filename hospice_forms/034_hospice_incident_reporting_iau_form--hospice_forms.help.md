# Hospice Incident Reporting (IAU) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Incident Reporting (IAU) Form is a critical tool for documenting and reporting incidents that occur within a hospice setting.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Info** (Page 1):
  * Enter the name and address of the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (Page 2):
  * Select the type of incident that occurred from the provided options.
* **Date of Incident** (Page 3):
  * Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (Page 4):
  * Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Location** (Page 5):
  * Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Cause** (Page 6):
  * Select the reason or cause of the incident from the provided options.
* **Patient Info** (Page 7):
  * Enter any relevant information about the patient involved.
* **Patient Name** (Page 8):
  * Enter the name of the patient.
* **Patient Age** (Page 9):
  * Enter the age of the patient.
* **Patient DOB** (Page 10):
  * Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Contact** (Page 11):
  * Enter the patient's contact information.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields, but also provide as much detail as possible to assist in the investigation and reporting process.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please contact your supervisor or the designated incident reporter.
* Please review and follow the facility's incident reporting guidelines and procedures.
* This form is for reporting incidents that occurred within the facility, not for submitting routine or routine care information.
* If you have any concerns or need assistance, please reach out to the designated incident reporter or your supervisor.
