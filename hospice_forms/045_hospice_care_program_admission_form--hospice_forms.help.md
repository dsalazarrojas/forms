# Hospice Care Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to provide the necessary information for a patient to be admitted to a hospice care program. The form collects demographic data about the patient, their medical condition, and other relevant details that will help the care team to provide appropriate care and services.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, make sure you have all the necessary information about the patient. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions. Please complete each section carefully and accurately.

### Section 1: Patient Information

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Admission**: Enter the date the patient was admitted to the hospice care program.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number, if available.

### Section 2: Contact Information

* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person who can be contacted for further information.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person who can be contacted.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email address of the person who can be contacted.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the patient is currently residing.

### Section 3: Care Team and Medical Information

* **Care Team**: Select the care team that will be working with the patient. Please choose from the available options.
* **Medical Condition**: Enter a brief description of the patient's medical condition.
* **Social Worker**: Enter the name of the social worker who is working with the patient.
* **Case Manager**: Enter the name of the case manager who is working with the patient.

### Section 4: Program Information

* **Hospice Program**: Select the type of hospice program the patient will be enrolled in. Please choose from the available options.
* **Admission Status**: Select the admission status of the patient. Please choose from the available options.
* **Care Team Members**: Enter the names of the care team members who will be working with the patient.
* **Attending Physician**: Enter the name of the attending physician.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the physician who is currently treating the patient.
* **Patient Condition**: Enter a brief description of the patient's current condition.
* **Physician Signature**: Enter the signature of the attending physician.
* **Date of Last Admission**: Enter the date of the patient's last admission.
* **Last Admission Location**: Enter the location of the patient's last admission.
* **Discharge Status**: Select the discharge status of the patient. Please choose from the available options.
* **Physician Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the attending physician.
* **Patient Signature**: Enter the signature of the patient.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from the care team or medical staff.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should be used to support the patient's care and services.
