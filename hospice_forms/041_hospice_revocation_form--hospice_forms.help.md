# Hospice Revocation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to revoke hospice care services for a patient. It is essential to complete it accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's name in the patient name field.
2. Enter the name of the medical practitioner who is revoking the care in the medical practitioner field.
3. Enter the medical practitioner's license number (if available) in the medical practitioner license field.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the patient dob field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. Enter the name of the hospice providing care in the hospice name field.
6. Enter the reason for revoking the care in the reason for revocation field.
7. Enter the date of revocation in the date of revocation field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
8. If required by law or regulation, attach the relevant medical certificate to the medical certificate field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the full name of the patient, including any middle names or initials.
* **Medical Practitioner**: Enter the name of the medical practitioner revoking the care.
* **Medical Practitioner License**: Enter the medical practitioner's license number if available and required by law or regulation.
* **Patient Date of Birth (D.O.B.)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Hospice Name**: Enter the name of the hospice providing care.
* **Reason for Revocation**: Enter a brief description of the reason for revoking the care.
* **Date of Revocation**: Enter the date of revocation in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Medical Certificate**: If required by law or regulation, attach the relevant medical certificate to this field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check the information entered in each field before submission.
* Attach relevant medical certificates as required.
* If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting it.
