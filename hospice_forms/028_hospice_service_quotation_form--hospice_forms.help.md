# Hospice Service Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to provide information and quotations for hospice services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the fields provided and ensure you understand what information is required and optional.
2. Complete each field based on your knowledge and available information.
3. Be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate and comprehensive quotations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Service Quotation** (`service_quotation`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the service quotation, including any specific details about the services needed.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed information about the services required, including any specific requirements or preferences.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether the patient is taking any medications, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medication Info** (`medication_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the patient's medication, if necessary.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history, including previous treatments or conditions.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information that may be relevant.
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, text, optional): Describe the patient's current medical status, including any current conditions or concerns.
* **Hospice Info** (`hospice_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the patient's current hospice needs, including any specific requirements or preferences.
* **Care Quote** (`care_quote`, text, optional): Enter a quote or estimate for the care services needed.
* **Service Quote** (`service_quote`, text, optional): Enter a final quote or estimate for the total services, including any additional costs or services.
