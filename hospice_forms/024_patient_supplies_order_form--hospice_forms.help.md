# Patient Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's supply needs, including any medical supplies, services, or other items required for their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
2. Ensure you are providing information about a patient who requires medical attention or supplies.
3. Complete the "patient_info_1" to "patient_info_9" fields with any additional comments or details about the patient.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for "medical_supplies", "medical_services", "materials_used", and "patient_supplies" fields to indicate whether the patient requires these supplies or services.
5. Enter the quantity of each supply needed in the "patient_supply_quantity" field.
6. Enter the cost per unit of each supply in the "cost_per_unit" field.
7. Calculate the total cost of the supplies by multiplying the quantity by the cost per unit and entering the result in the "total_cost" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient-info**: Enter the patient's name.
* **patient-name**: Enter the patient's name again for confirmation.
* **patient-supplies**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient requires medical supplies.
* **medical-supplies**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient requires medical supplies.
* **materials-used**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient requires materials.
* **patient-supply-quantity**: Enter the quantity of each supply needed.
* **cost-per-unit**: Enter the cost per unit of each supply.
* **total-cost**: Calculate the total cost by multiplying the quantity by the cost per unit.
* **hospital-and-clinic-info**: Enter any additional information about the patient's hospital or clinic visit.
* **medical-staff**: Select "Doctor", "Nurse", or "Therapist" to indicate who will be attending to the patient.
* **medical-services**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient requires medical services.
* **patient-supplies**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient requires medical supplies.
* **patient-info-2**, **patient-info-3**, **patient-info-4**, **patient-info-5**, **patient-info-6**, **patient-info-7**, **patient-info-8**, **patient-info-9**: Enter any additional comments or details about the patient.
