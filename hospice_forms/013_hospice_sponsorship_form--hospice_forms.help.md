# Hospice Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for sponsorship support for a hospice organization, and it is intended for one-time submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the charity or organization you are sponsoring in the **Charity Name** field.
2. Provide the address of the charity or organization in the **Address** field.
3. Enter the city of the charity or organization in the **City** field.
4. Enter the state of the charity or organization in the **State** field.
5. Enter the zip code of the charity or organization in the **Zip** field.
6. Enter the contact phone number of the charity or organization in the **Phone** field.
7. Enter the email address of the charity or organization in the **Email** field (optional).
8. In the **Message** field, provide any relevant information about your sponsorship, such as the specific activities or services you wish to support.
9. Finally, enter the amount of your donation in the **Donation Amount** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Charity Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the charity or organization you are sponsoring.
* **Address** (2, text, required): Enter the address of the charity or organization.
* **City** (3, text, required): Enter the city of the charity or organization.
* **State** (4, text, required): Enter the state of the charity or organization.
* **Zip** (5, text, required): Enter the zip code of the charity or organization.
* **Phone** (6, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the charity or organization.
* **Email** (7, email, optional): Enter the contact email address of the charity or organization (if available).
* **Message** (8, note, required): Provide any relevant information about your sponsorship support.
* **Donation Amount** (9, number, required): Enter the amount of your donation. 

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the charity or organization.
* Double-check your donation amount for correctness.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, please contact the hospice organization directly.
