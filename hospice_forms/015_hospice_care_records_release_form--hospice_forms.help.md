## Purpose
The Hospice Care Records Release Form is used to gather essential information from patients for hospice care records release. This form helps healthcare providers and professionals accurately document the patient's information, medical history, and authorization details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, taking care to answer all required fields.
3.  Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization from the patient or their representatives.
