# End Of Life Care Plan - Help Guide

## Purpose
The End Of Life Care Plan form is designed to provide information about a patient's care and medical needs during their end of life. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical conditions, medications, and advance directives, as well as their care team and end of life care plan. Completing this form ensures that all relevant information is documented and communicated among the care team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, medical ID number, and date of birth in the **Patient Info** section.
2. **List Medical Conditions**: Select all medical conditions that apply to the patient from the **Medical Conditions** dropdown menu.
3. **Mention Medications**: Select all medications the patient is currently taking in the **Medications** dropdown menu.
4. **List Medical Procedures**: Enter any medical procedures the patient has undergone or is planning to undergo.
5. **Provide Medical Provisions**: Enter any medical provisions or supplies that are essential for the patient's care.
6. **Define Advance Directives**: Select the advance directives that apply to the patient from the **Advance Directives** dropdown menu.
7. **Identify Care Team**: Select all members of the care team, such as doctors, nurses, and family members.
8. **Name Care Facility**: Select the care facility where the patient resides or will reside.
9. **Determine End Of Life Care Plan**: Select the end of life care plan that aligns with the patient's needs and preferences.
10. **Add Other Information**: Provide any additional information about the patient's care and medical needs.
11. **Enter Family Information**: Provide any relevant information about the patient's family members or support system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info**: This section is used to input the patient's basic contact information, including name, medical ID number, and date of birth.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Medical Conditions**: This section allows you to select all medical conditions that apply to the patient from the dropdown menu.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
- **Medications**: This section allows you to select all medications the patient is currently taking in the dropdown menu.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: false
- **Medical Procedures**: This section is used to enter any medical procedures the patient has undergone or is planning to undergo.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Medical Provisions**: This section is used to enter any medical provisions or supplies that are essential for the patient's care.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Advance Directives**: This section allows you to select the advance directives that apply to the patient from the dropdown menu.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
- **Care Team**: This section allows you to select all members of the care team, such as doctors, nurses, and family members.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: false
- **Care Facility**: This section is used to select the care facility where the patient resides or will reside.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
- **End Of Life Care Plan**: This section is used to select the end of life care plan that aligns with the patient's needs and preferences.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
- **Other Info**: This section is used to provide any additional information about the patient's care and medical needs.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Family Info**: This section is used to provide any relevant information about the patient's family members or support system.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all relevant information is included.
* Enter as much detail as possible to ensure accurate and complete care planning.
* Consult with other healthcare professionals if you are unsure about any information required.
