# hospice_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect consent from patients or their representatives for hospice care. It gathers essential information about the patient's admission and demographic details, as well as their consent for care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of admission in the "Date Of Admission" field.
3. Enter the date of death, if applicable, in the "Date Of Death" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Date Of Admission** (`date_of_admission`, date, optional): Enter the date the patient was admitted to the hospice program.
* **Date Of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's passing, if applicable.
