# Hospice Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate the transfer of a patient between providers in a hospice care setting. It collects necessary information for the transfer process, including patient details, provider information, and transfer logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, contact details, and medical information.
2. Select whether the patient is transferring from one provider to another.
3. Enter the transfer date and time.
4. Provide a brief reason for the transfer.
5. Add any additional information or notes as needed.
6. Enter the contact information of the provider responsible for the patient's care (Signature Provider).
7. Confirm the transfer with the accepting provider.
8. Enter the contact information of the provider accepting the patient (Medical Record Provider).
9. Provide any supporting documents as necessary.
10. Sign the form to confirm the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Patient Name** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Transferring Provider** (`transferring_provider`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient is transferring from this provider to another.
* **Accepting Provider** (`accepting_provider`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient is transferring to this provider from another.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Transfer Time** (`transfer_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the transfer.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical information about the patient.
* **Transfer Reason** (`transfer_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief reason for the transfer.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional medical notes or details.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your signature to confirm the transfer.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's fax number.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical records or notes.
* **Supporting Docs** (`supporting_docs`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any supporting documents or information.
* **Transfer Confirmation** (`transfer_confirmation`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm the transfer with the patient or their family.
* **Signature Provider** (`signature_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the signature provider.
* **Transfer Confirmation Confirmation** (`transfer_confirmation_confirmation`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm the transfer confirmation with the accepting provider.
* **Medical Record Provider** (`medical_record_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the medical record provider.
* **Supporting Docs Provider** (`supporting_docs_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the supporting documents provider.
* **Transfer Reason Provider** (`transfer_reason_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reason for the transfer with the patient or their family.
* **Transfer Date Provider** (`transfer_date_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the date of the transfer with the patient or their family.
* **Transfer Time Provider** (`transfer_time_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the time of the transfer with the patient or their family.
* **Signature Provider Provider** (`signature_provider_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the signature provider.
* **Additional Info Provider** (`additional_info_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments from the patient or their family.

Please note that some fields may be optional or have specific instructions that are not explicitly stated in this guide. It is the user's responsibility to ensure they understand the requirements and purpose of each field before completing the form.
