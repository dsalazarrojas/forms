# Hospice Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about you and your preferences for participating in hospice care activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Volunteer Name in the first field.
2. Provide your Email address if you are comfortable sharing it with us.
3. Enter your Phone number if you would like to be contacted by us.
4. Choose the Volunteer Role that best describes your interest and skills.
5. Select how you would like to be contacted, either by Email, Phone, or Both.
6. Optionally, provide any additional information about yourself in the Message field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as it should be listed in our records.
* **Email**: Your email address for communication with us.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Volunteer Role**: Please select the role that best describes your interest and skills:
	+ General Volunteer: Helping with general hospice care tasks such as visiting patients, assisting with events, etc.
	+ Team Leader: Taking on leadership roles within the hospice care team, such as coordinating events or leading volunteer groups.
	+ Care Partner: Providing emotional support and companionship to patients and their families.
* **Availability**: The date you are available to start volunteering with us.
* **Time**: The time you are available to volunteer, if different from your availability date.
* **Date of Application**: The date you submitted this application.
* **Time of Application**: The time you submitted this application.
* **Contact Preference**: How you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email: We will send you emails about volunteer opportunities and events.
	+ Phone: We will call you to discuss volunteer opportunities or provide updates.
	+ Both: You are open to being contacted both by email and phone.
* **Message**: Any additional information about yourself that you would like to share with us.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your application.
