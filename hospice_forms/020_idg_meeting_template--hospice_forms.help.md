# idg_meeting_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The idg_meeting_template form is used to record meeting details, patient information, and follow-up actions. This form is part of the hospice services documentation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form correctly, follow the sequence of the form and each section logically. Make sure to complete each field accurately and ensure that you have answered all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (Meeting Details, text, not required): This field is used to enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Patient Info** (Patient Info, text, not required): Enter the patient's relevant information, such as name, ID, or any other identifying details.
* **Team Members** (Team Members, select_multiple, not required): Choose the team members who attended the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes** (Meeting Notes, text, not required): Enter any additional notes about the meeting.
* **Follow-up Action Items** (Follow-up Action Items, select_one, not required): Choose whether follow-up action items are required.
* **Follow-up Actions** (Follow-up Actions, select_multiple, not required): Choose which follow-up actions are required.
* **Next Meeting** (Next Meeting, date, not required): Enter the date of the next meeting.
* **Meeting Duration** (Meeting Duration, number, not required): Enter the duration of the meeting.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, not required): Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (Meeting Location, text, not required): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, not required): Enter the end time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Outcome** (Meeting Outcome, select_one, not required): Choose the outcome of the meeting.
* **Follow-up Status** (Follow-up Status, select_multiple, not required): Choose the current follow-up status.
* **Next Meeting Date** (Next Meeting Date, date, not required): Enter the date of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Time** (Next Meeting Time, time, not required): Enter the time of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Location** (Next Meeting Location, text, not required): Enter the location of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Duration** (Next Meeting Duration, number, not required): Enter the duration of the next meeting.
* **Follow-up Items** (Follow-up Items, text, not required): Enter any follow-up items that need to be addressed.
* **Team Member 1** (Team Member 1, text, not required): Enter the name of the team member 1.
* **Team Member 2** (Team Member 2, text, not required): Enter the name of the team member 2.
* **Team Member 3** (Team Member 3, text, not required): Enter the name of the team member 3.
* **Team Member 4** (Team Member 4, text, not required): Enter the name of the team member 4.
* **Follow-up Status (2)** (Follow-up Status (2), text, not required): Enter the follow-up status.
* **Next Meeting Date (2)** (Next Meeting Date (2), date, not required): Enter the date of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Time (2)** (Next Meeting Time (2), time, not required): Enter the time of the next meeting.

Please ensure to complete each section correctly and ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form. If you need help or clarification on any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or team leader for assistance.
