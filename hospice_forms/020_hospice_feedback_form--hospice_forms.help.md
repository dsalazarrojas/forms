# hospice_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients about their experience with the hospice care center. This includes information about their patient information, experience at the hospice, services used, services not used, suggestions for improvement, overall experience, comments, and patient signature.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Patient Information** page (page 1) with the patient's details.
2. On the **Experience at Hospice** page (page 2), provide a brief description of your experience at the hospice care center.
3. In the **Services Used** section, select all the services you received from the hospice care center (Nursing, Social Work, Chaplain).
4. In the **Services Not Used** section, select any services that you did not receive from the hospice care center (Nursing, Social Work, Chaplain).
5. In the **Suggestions for Improvement** section, type any suggestions you have for how the hospice care center could improve.
6. In the **Overall Experience** section, rate your overall experience at the hospice care center on a scale of 1-10.
7. In the **Comments** section, add any additional comments or observations about your experience.
8. Finally, sign the form with your **Patient Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (Page 1: patient_information): This page is where you will fill in your basic patient information.
* **Experience at Hospice** (Page 2: experience_at_hospice): Describe your experience at the hospice care center in a few sentences.
* **Services Used** (Page 3: services_used): Select all the services you received from the hospice care center (Nursing, Social Work, Chaplain).
* **Services Not Used** (Page 4: services_not_used): Select any services that you did not receive from the hospice care center (Nursing, Social Work, Chaplain).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (Page 5: suggestions_for_improvement): Type any suggestions you have for how the hospice care center could improve.
* **Overall Experience** (Page 6: overall_experience): Rate your overall experience at the hospice care center on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (Page 7: comments): Add any additional comments or observations about your experience.
* **Patient Signature** (Page 8: patient_signature): Sign the form with your signature.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please let us know so we can address them.
* Your feedback is important to us and helps us improve our services.
