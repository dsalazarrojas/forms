# hospice_care_patient_demographics_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The `hospice_care_patient_demographics_form` is used to collect patient demographic information for hospice care patients. This form is essential for healthcare providers to gather critical details about the patient's history, medical condition, and contact information to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to access and complete this form.
2. Complete the form fields as instructed, and make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select the patient's gender from the provided options: Male, Female, Other.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical record number or ID number.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the patient's marital status from the provided options: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Other.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's address, including the street address and apartment number.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the patient resides.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where the patient resides.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Caregiver Relationship** (`caregiver_relationship`, `select_one`, optional): Select the relationship between the caregiver and the patient from the provided options: Family Member, Friend, Health Care Professional, Other.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, optional): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient from the provided options: Spouse, Family Member, Friend, Other.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Patient Medical Insurance** (`patient_medical_insurance`, `select_one`, optional): Select the patient's medical insurance type from the provided options: Private, Medicaid, Medicare, Medicaid-Medicare, Other.
* **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's insurance plan or policy number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's current medical condition or diagnosis.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required): Enter the medication(s) the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or allergies.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `text`, required): Enter any medical procedures or surgeries the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Test** (`medical_test`, `text`, required): Enter any medical test results or results of previous medical tests.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, `text`, required): Enter any medication allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, `text`, required): Enter any medication side effects the patient has experienced.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the patient was discharged from a medical facility, if applicable.
