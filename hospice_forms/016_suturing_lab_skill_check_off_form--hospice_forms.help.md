# Suturing Lab Skill Check Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills of medical practitioners in suturing labs by evaluating their ability to perform specific procedures and techniques.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary knowledge and skills to answer the questions accurately.
2. Choose the correct answers for each question based on your knowledge and experience in suturing labs.
3. Fill in the answers for each question, making sure to provide detailed explanations where necessary.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Practitioner Name** (medical_practitioner_name, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your name as a medical practitioner.
* **Procedure** (procedure, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all the procedures you have experience in performing in suturing labs. Please select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes': You have experience in this procedure.
	+ 'No': You do not have experience in this procedure.
* **Suturing Device Type** (suturing_device_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of suturing device you are most familiar with:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Patient Safety Precautions** (patient_safety_precautions, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe any safety precautions you take when performing suturing procedures. Please provide a brief explanation.
* **Knowledge of Suturing Techniques** (knowledge_of_suturing_techiques, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe your knowledge and experience in different suturing techniques. Please provide a brief explanation.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Make sure to provide detailed explanations where necessary.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure or have any questions, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
