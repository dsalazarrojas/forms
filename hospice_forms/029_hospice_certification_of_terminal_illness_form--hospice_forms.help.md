# Hospice Certification Of Terminal Illness Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's terminal illness status, ensuring that they receive proper hospice care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
2. Provide information about the patient's medical condition, including their current status.
3. Select the type of hospice care the patient requires.
4. Enter the date the patient was certified for hospice care.
5. Confirm the certification status of the patient.
6. Specify the source of the hospice referral.
7. Record the date the patient was referred to the hospice.
8. Note the patient's last visit to the hospice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Describe the patient's medical condition, including their terminal illness status.
* **Hospice Info** (`hospice_info`): Enter the name of the hospice providing care to the patient.
* **Hospice Care** (`hospice_care`): Select the type of hospice care the patient requires (e.g., palliative care, comfort care, etc.).
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`): Enter the name and contact information of the patient's primary care physician.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`): Select the current status of the patient (Active, Inactive, or Deceased).
* **Hospice Certification Date** (`hospice_certification_date`): Enter the date the patient was certified for hospice care.
* **Hospice Certification Number** (`hospice_certification_number`): Enter the unique identifier for the patient's hospice certification.
* **Hospice Certification Status** (`hospice_certification_status`): Confirm whether the patient is certified for hospice care (Certified or Not Certified).
* **Hospice Referral Source** (`hospice_referral_source`): Enter the source of the hospice referral (e.g., doctor, hospital, etc.).
* **Hospice Referral Date** (`hospice_referral_date`): Enter the date the patient was referred to the hospice.
* **Hospice Last Visit** (`hospice_last_visit`): Record the date and time of the patient's last visit to the hospice.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out in collaboration with the patient's medical team, ensuring accurate and comprehensive information is collected.
