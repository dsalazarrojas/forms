# hospice_medical_equipment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospice medical equipment order form is used to document and manage the ordering and delivery of medical equipment for patients in hospice care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the patient's medical history in the Medical History field.
2. Enter the contact name, email, and phone number in the Contact Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone fields.
3. Enter the patient's name in the Patient Name field.
4. Select whether medical equipment is required in the Medical Equipment field.
5. Enter the quantity of equipment needed in the Equipment Quantity field.
6. Enter the date and time for the delivery in the Delivery Date and Delivery Time fields.
7. Enter the delivery address and contact information in the Delivery Address and Delivery Contact fields.
8. Select the payment terms and status in the Payment Terms and Payment Status fields.
9. Select the payment method in the Payment Method field.
10. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the Order Comments and Special Instructions fields.
11. Enter the billing contact's address, phone number, and email in the Billing Address, Billing Phone, and Billing Email fields.
12. Enter the status of the order and the date it was placed in the Order Status and Order Date fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: Enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or other health information.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact for further information or questions.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person to contact for further information or questions.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact for further information or questions. (Note: This field is optional, but it may be required by your organization.)
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient.
* **Medical Equipment**: Select whether medical equipment is required for the patient.
* **Equipment Quantity**: Enter the number of items of equipment required.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date when the equipment is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time when the equipment is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where the equipment will be delivered.
* **Delivery Contact**: Enter the contact information for the person or organization delivering the equipment.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms for the order.
* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status of the order.
* **Payment Method**: Enter the method of payment for the order.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the order.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address for billing purposes.
* **Billing Phone**: Enter the phone number for billing purposes.
* **Billing Email**: Enter the email address for billing purposes. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Order Status**: Enter the status of the order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date when the order was placed.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool or equipment ordered.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any medical notes or instructions.
* **Delivery Contact**: Enter the contact information for the person or organization delivering the equipment.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the order. (Note: This field is optional.)
