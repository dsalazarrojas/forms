# Hospice Notice Of Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from patients and their families about their medical organization, contact details, and care wishes. It is designed to ensure that patients and their families understand the services and benefits provided by the hospice and make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the patient's information, including their name, medical organization, contact phone number, address, and patient ID.
2. Enter the patient's contact email address and care wishes.
3. Select the date for the signature.
4. Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understand the services and benefits provided by the hospice.
5. If necessary, enter the title of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the patient's full name. Please enter the patient's legal name as it appears on their identification card or driver's license.
* **Medical Organization (2)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the patient's medical organization, such as the name of the hospital or clinic where they are receiving treatment.
* **Contact Phone (3)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the patient's contact phone number, where they can be reached for updates and information about their care.
* **Address (4)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the patient's address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Patient ID (5)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the patient's identification number, such as their insurance card ID or social security number.
* **Contact Email (6)** (`email`, required/optional): This field is for the patient's contact email address, where they can be reached for updates and information about their care.
* **Care Wishes (7)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the patient's wishes and preferences for their care, such as their end-of-life wishes or medical treatment options.
* **Signature Date (8)** (`date`, required/optional): This field is for the date when the patient signed this form.
* **Signature (9)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the patient's signature, confirming that they have read and understand the services and benefits provided by the hospice.
* **Medical Director (10)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the name and title of the patient's medical director or attending physician.
* **Contact Title (11)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for the title of the contact person, such as "Attorney-in-Fact" or "Healthcare Agent".

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and have the patient or their representative sign the form to confirm that they have read and understand the services and benefits provided by the hospice. If you have any questions or concerns, please consult with the patient's primary care provider or hospice team member.
