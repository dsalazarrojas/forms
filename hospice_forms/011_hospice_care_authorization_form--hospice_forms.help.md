# Hospice Care Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Care Authorization Form is a crucial document used to provide essential information for hospice care services. It is completed by patients, caregivers, or healthcare providers to gather necessary details for authorizing hospice care.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete the form, start by selecting "Patient Name" and entering the patient's full name.
*   Next, select the "Date" of admission and the "Time" of the appointment. If the time is not known, please leave the field blank.
*   Enter the Doctor's Name, Nurse's Name, and other relevant healthcare provider's information.
*   For "Care Team," select "Yes" if the patient will receive a care team, and "No" otherwise.
*   Finally, ensure that the Physician's Agreement, Nurse's Agreement, and Therapist's Agreement are all selected as "Yes" if you are authorized to provide care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name, as it appears on their identification or medical records.
*   **Date**: Enter the date of admission to the hospice care service.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of the appointment or admission, if known.
*   **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of the attending physician or doctor who has prescribed the treatment.
*   **Nurse's Name**: Enter the name of the nurse who will be providing care.
*   **Date of Admission**: Enter the date on which the patient began receiving hospice care services.
*   **Expected Length of Stay**: Enter the anticipated length of time the patient will receive hospice care services.
*   **Diagnosis**: Enter any diagnosis related to the patient's condition.
*   **Disease**: Enter any disease or medical condition affecting the patient.
*   **Condition**: Enter any additional details about the patient's condition.
*   **Therapist's Name**: Enter the name of any therapists or counselors involved in the patient's care.
*   **Therapist's Phone**: Enter the phone number of the therapist or counselor.
*   **Therapist's Email**: Enter the email of the therapist or counselor.
*   **Therapist's Agreement**: Select "Yes" if you are authorized to provide care as a therapist or counselor.
*   **Care Team**: Select "Yes" if the patient will receive a care team; otherwise, select "No".
*   **Physician's Agreement**: Select "Yes" if you are authorized to provide care as a physician.
*   **Physician's Name**: Enter the name of the attending physician or doctor who has prescribed the treatment.
*   **Physician's Title**: Enter the title of the physician, such as Dr. or MD.
*   **Physician's Phone**: Enter the phone number of the physician.
*   **Physician's Email**: Enter the email of the physician.
*   **Physician's License**: Enter the physician's license number or certification.
*   **Physician's Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the physician.
*   **Physician's Agreement**: Select "Yes" if you are authorized to provide care as a physician.
*   **Nurse Agreement**: Select "Yes" if you are authorized to provide care as a nurse.
*   **Therapist's License**: Enter the license number or certification of the therapist or counselor.
