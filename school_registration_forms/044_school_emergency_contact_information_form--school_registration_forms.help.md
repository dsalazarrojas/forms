# School Emergency Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect emergency contact information for students at a school. This information will be used in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the student's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Indicate the relationship you have with the student (e.g., parent, guardian, guardian's name) in the "Relationship to Student" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Enter your state in the "State" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
10. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
11. Indicate the relationship between you and the emergency contact in the "Relationship to Student" field.
12. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
13. Enter the emergency contact's address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
14. Enter the emergency contact's city in the "Emergency Contact City" field.
15. Enter the emergency contact's state in the "Emergency Contact State" field.
16. Enter the emergency contact's zip code in the "Emergency Contact Zip" field.
17. If you have any other phone numbers for the emergency contact, enter them in the "Other Phone" field.
18. If you have any other addresses for the emergency contact, enter them in the "Other Address" field.
19. If you have any other cities for the emergency contact, enter them in the "Other City" field.
20. If you have any other states for the emergency contact, enter them in the "Other State" field.
21. If you have any other zip codes for the emergency contact, enter them in the "Other Zip" field.
22. If you have any other relationships with the emergency contact, select the relevant option in the "Other Relationship" field.
23. If you have any other email addresses for the emergency contact, enter them in the "Other Email" field.
24. If you have any other notes about the emergency contact, enter them in the "Other Notes" field.
25. If you have any other relationships for the emergency contact that are not listed, select the relevant option in the "Other Relationship Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the student's first name.
- **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter the student's last name.
- **Relationship to Student** (3, select_one, required/optional): Indicate the relationship you have with the student (e.g., parent, guardian, guardian's name).
- **Email** (4, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (6, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
- **City** (7, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
- **State** (8, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
- **Zip** (9, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (10, text, required/optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
- **Relationship to Student** (11, select_one, required/optional): If you have an emergency contact, indicate the relationship they have with the student.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (12, text, required/optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (13, text, required/optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their address.
- **Emergency Contact City** (14, text, required/optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their city.
- **Emergency Contact State** (15, text, required/optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their state.
- **Emergency Contact Zip** (16, text, required/optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their zip code.
- **Other Phone** (17, text, required/optional): If you have any other phone numbers for the emergency contact, enter them.
- **Other Address** (18, text, required/optional): If you have any other addresses for the emergency contact, enter them.
- **Other City** (19, text, required/optional): If you have any other cities for the emergency contact, enter them.
- **Other State** (20, text, required/optional): If you have any other states for the emergency contact, enter them.
- **Other Zip** (21, text, required/optional): If you have any other zip codes for the emergency contact, enter them.
- **Other Relationship** (22, select_one, required/optional): If you have any other relationships with the emergency contact, select the relevant option.
- **Other Email** (23, email, required/optional): If you have any other email addresses for the emergency contact, enter them.
- **Other Notes** (24, text, required/optional): If you have any other notes about the emergency contact, enter them.
- **Other Relationship Other** (25, select_multiple, required/optional): If you have any other relationships for the emergency contact that are not listed, select the relevant option.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any other relationships with the emergency contact that are not listed, use the "Other Relationship Other" field to indicate them.
- If you have any other notes about the emergency contact, use the "Other Notes" field to include them.
- If you have any other phone numbers, addresses, cities, states, or zip codes for the emergency contact, use the corresponding "Other" fields to include them.
