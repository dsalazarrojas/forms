# Student Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Council Registration Form is used by school administrators to collect and store student information for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out. (Note: There are no required fields on this form, so it's up to the user's discretion to provide information.)
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name. This will be used as a reference for your registration.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This will be used as a reference for your registration.
- **Student ID**: Enter your student ID, if applicable. This is an optional field for storing your student ID number.
- **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached. This can be a phone number or a contact method of your choice.
- **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes, if necessary.
