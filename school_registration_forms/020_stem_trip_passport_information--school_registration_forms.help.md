# STEM Trip Passport Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants on a STEM-related trip, including contact and emergency contact details, participant's details, and more. This information will help the trip organizer ensure the participant's safety and provide a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your answers for consistency and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Passport** (`participant_passport`, text, optional): This field is intended for any additional documents or ID you may have that confirms your identity.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address. This will help the trip organizer get in touch with you in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Details** (`emergency_details`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact information. This ensures that they can be reached in case of an accident or medical emergency.
- **Participant Information 1** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself that's relevant to the trip, such as your academic background or any medical conditions.
- **Participant Information 2** (`participant_info_2`, text, optional): Similar to Participant Information 1, this field allows you to provide more details about yourself that are not covered in the first field.
- **Participant Information 3** (`participant_info_3`, text, optional): Another field for any additional information about yourself that's relevant to the trip.
- **Participant Information 4** (`participant_info_4`, text, optional): Another field for further details about yourself.
- **Participant Information 5** (`participant_info_5`, text, optional): Last field for any more information you'd like to share about yourself.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy and consistency.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact the trip organizer for clarification.
- Please provide your completed form back to the organizer to ensure your inclusion in the trip.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
