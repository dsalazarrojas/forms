# School Fee Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Fee Payment Form is designed to collect payment information from respondents, ensuring that school fees are paid accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, start by filling in your details in the "Respondent Details" field.
*   Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Type" dropdown menu.
*   Enter the date of payment in the "Payment Date" field.
*   If you need to order school supplies, enter your requirements in the "School Supplies Order" field.
*   If you need to order books, enter your requirements in the "Book Order" field.
*   Finally, enter any additional swag orders you require in the "School Swags Order" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent Details** (Text): Please fill in your details, including name and contact information.
*   **Payment Type** (Select One): Choose your preferred payment method:
    *   **Cash**: Pay in cash at the school's designated payment center.
    *   **Credit Card**: Use a credit card for secure online payment.
    *   **Direct Debit**: Set up automatic deductions from your bank account.
    *   **Cheque**: Pay by cheque, sent to the school's address.
    *   **Other**: Select this option for alternative payment methods.
*   **Payment Date** (Date): Enter the date of payment for the school fee.
*   **School Supplies Order** (Text): Enter any school supplies you need to purchase (e.g., pens, notebooks, or calculators).
*   **Book Order** (Text): Enter any book titles or ISBN numbers you need to purchase.
*   **School Swags Order** (Text): Enter any school swag items you require (e.g., water bottles, stickers, or pens with school logos).
