# School Exploration Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Exploration Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from prospective families interested in exploring the school as an option for their child(ren). The form is used to collect relevant details about the family's inquiry, such as their contact preferences and scheduling interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Prospective Family" field, providing a brief description of your family.
2. In the "Family Information" field, enter any additional relevant details about your family.
3. In the "Student Info" field, enter any relevant details about the student(s) you are inquiring about.
4. Choose the type of inquiry you are interested in (e.g., Informational Call, Campus Visit).
5. Enter any additional details about your inquiry in the "Inquiry Details" field.
6. Specify your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, In-person Meeting).
7. If you are interested in scheduling a visit, select the "Visit Scheduling" options that apply to you.
8. If you are interested in the admissions process, select the "Admissions Interest" options that apply to you.
9. If you would like to be contacted to follow up on your inquiry, select "Follow-up".
10. Finally, enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospective Family**: This field is where you can briefly describe your family, including your interests and goals for the school.
* **Family Information**: This field is where you can provide any additional relevant details about your family, such as family members or special circumstances.
* **Student Info**: This field is where you can enter any relevant details about the student(s) you are inquiring about, such as grade level or interests.
* **Inquiry Type**: Choose the type of inquiry you are interested in, such as Informational Call or Campus Visit.
* **Inquiry Details**: This field is where you can enter any additional details about your inquiry.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Specify how you would like to be contacted, such as by email or phone.
* **Visit Scheduling**: If you are interested in scheduling a visit, select the options that apply to you.
* **Admissions Interest**: If you are interested in the admissions process, select the options that apply to you.
* **Follow-up**: If you would like to be contacted to follow up on your inquiry, select "Follow-up".
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Schedule Call**: If you would like to schedule a call, select "Schedule Call".
* **Schedule Visit**: If you would like to schedule a visit, select "Schedule Visit".
* **Schedule Follow-up**: If you would like to schedule a follow-up, select "Schedule Follow-up".

## Tips

* Be as concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your inquiry.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
