# Class Media Center Visit Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to schedule a visit to the class media center. It helps the media center staff to gather essential information about the teacher's class and visit plans, ensuring that resources are allocated effectively to support the teachers' needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information to ensure that the media center can plan and prepare for your visit.
2. Choose the appropriate options for each field to indicate the purpose, frequency, and duration of your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Teacher Information --**
	+ **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher requesting the visit.
	+ **Teacher Email**: Enter the email address of the teacher, which will be used for communication.
	+ **Teacher Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the teacher, which will be used for quick communication.
* **Department or Grade Level**: Specify the department or grade level of the class.
* **Class Name or Section**: Enter the name or section of the class.
* **Number of Students in Class**: Provide the number of students in the class.
* **Grade Level**: Indicate the grade level of the class.
* **Class Period or Time Slot**: Specify the class period or time slot when the visit is requested.
* **-- Visit Request Information --**
	+ **Purpose of Media Center Visit**: Select the purpose of the visit from the provided options.
	+ **Preferred Dates for Visit**: Provide several date options for the visit.
	+ **Frequency of Visits Requested**: Choose the frequency of visits required.
	+ **Expected Duration of Visit**: Select the expected duration of the visit.
* **-- Planned Activities --**
	+ **Planned Activities or Instruction Topics**: Describe the activities or instruction topics that the class will be doing during the visit.
* **-- Additional Information --**
	+ **Have you visited the media center before with this class**: Indicate if this is a repeat visit or not.
	+ **Additional Notes or Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments about the visit.

Note: Please fill in the required fields accurately to ensure that the media center can plan and prepare effectively for your visit.
