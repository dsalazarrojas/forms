# School Year Itinerary Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Year Itinerary Survey is designed to collect information about students' plans and activities during the school year. This form will help school administrators get an overview of the students' academic and extracurricular activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category for your school year itinerary survey.
2. Enter a brief description of the survey.
3. Answer questions about your academic plans and course selections.
4. List your extracurricular activities.
5. Enter the completion date and time for the survey.
6. Provide your student name, email address, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`school_year_itinerary_survey`, `select_one`, *optional*): Select the relevant category for your survey.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, *optional*): Enter a brief description of the survey.
* **Academic Plans** (`school_year_itinerary_survey_1`, `text`, *optional*): Describe your academic plans for the school year.
* **Course Selections** (`school_year_itinerary_survey_2`, `text`, *optional*): List your course selections.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (`school_year_itinerary_survey_3`, `text`, *optional*): List your extracurricular activities.

* **Survey Completion Time** (`school_year_itinerary_survey_5`, `time`, *optional*): Enter the completion time for the survey.
* **Survey Completion Duration** (`school_year_itinerary_survey_6`, `number`, *optional*): Enter the duration of the survey completion.
* **Student Name** (`school_year_itinerary_survey_7`, `text`, *optional*): Enter your student name.
* **Email Address** (`school_year_itinerary_survey_8`, `email`, *optional*): Enter your email address.
