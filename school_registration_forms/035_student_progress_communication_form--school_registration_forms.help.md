# Student Progress Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Progress Communication Form is a tool for instructors to provide feedback and updates on a student's performance, and for students to communicate their progress and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that the student's progress and performance are up-to-date.
3. Use this form to provide feedback and updates to the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Progress Communication Form** (Text, Required: False): This field is where you can enter a brief description of the student's progress.
* **student_progress_communication_date** (Date, Required: True): This field is used to record the date of the communication.
* **Student Progress Communication Observation** (Text, Required: False): This field is where you can enter any observations or comments related to the student's progress.
