# Academic Arrangement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Academic Arrangement Application Form, a crucial step in the process of applying for academic arrangements such as exchange programs, study abroad, or joint degree programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. **Personal Information Section:**
	* Start by filling out the Personal Information section, which includes fields for your:
		+ Full legal name
		+ Email address
		+ Phone number
		+ Date of birth
		+ Nationality
		+ Home address
2. **Academic Background Section:**
	* Next, move on to the Academic Background section, where you will provide details about your:
		+ Current university or college
		+ Current program of study
		+ Current year of study
		+ Current GPA or academic standing
3. **Arrangement Details Section:**
	* In the Arrangement Details section, you will provide information about the arrangement you are applying for, including:
		+ Name of host institution
		+ Type of arrangement (e.g., Exchange program, Study abroad, etc.)
		+ Proposed start and end dates
		+ List of courses you plan to take
		+ Reason for applying
		+ Funding source
		+ Language proficiency

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full legal name:** Please enter your full legal name, as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email address:** Enter your active email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone number:** Enter your phone number that can be contacted for communication purposes.
* **Date of birth:** Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Nationality:** Choose your country of birth.
* **Home address:** Enter your permanent home address.
* **Current university or college:** Enter the name of your current institution.
* **Current program of study:** Enter the name of your current program of study.
* **Current year of study:** Choose the year you are currently studying (1st year, 2nd year, 3rd year, 4th year, etc.).
* **Current GPA or academic standing:** Enter your current GPA or academic standing.
* **Name of host institution:** Enter the name of the institution you are applying to.
* **Type of arrangement:** Choose the type of arrangement you are applying for (e.g., Exchange program, Study abroad, etc.).
* **Proposed start date:** Enter the date you propose to start the arrangement.
* **Proposed end date:** Enter the date you propose to end the arrangement.
* **List of courses:** Enter the list of courses you plan to take at the host institution.
* **Reason for applying:** Enter a brief explanation of why you are applying for this arrangement.
* **Funding source:** Choose how you plan to finance your arrangement (e.g., Scholarship, Self-funded, etc.).
* **Language proficiency:** Choose your language proficiency level (e.g., Native speaker, Advanced, Intermediate, etc.).
