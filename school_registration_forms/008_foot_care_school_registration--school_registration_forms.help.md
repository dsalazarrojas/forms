# Foot Care School Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foot Care School Registration form is designed to gather information from students registering for a course or program offered by the Foot Care School. This form will help the school to manage student registrations and course-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the registration form, please follow these steps:

1.  **School Registration Form**: Fill in the school name and any other required information in this field.
2.  **Student Name**: Enter your full student name.
3.  **Username**: Enter a unique username for your account.
4.  **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
5.  **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
6.  **Course Signup**: Select "Yes" if you are signing up for a course, or "No" otherwise.
7.  **Course Name**: Select the name of the course you are registering for.
8.  **Student Address**: Enter your student address.
9.  **City**: Enter the city of your student address.
10.  **State**: Enter the state of your student address.
11.  **Zip**: Enter the zip code of your student address.
12.  **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
13.  **Time of Registration**: Select "Yes" if you are registering at the time of registration, or "No" otherwise.
14.  **Payment Method**: Select one or more payment methods (Credit/Debit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
15.  **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
16.  **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your registration.
17.  **Course Duration**: Select the duration of the course you are registering for (1 month, 3 months, or 6 months).
18.  **Course Start Date**: Select "Yes" if you are starting the course at the selected date, or "No" otherwise.
19.  **Course End Date**: Select "Yes" if you are ending the course at the selected date, or "No" otherwise.
20.  **Course Start Time**: Select "Yes" if you are starting the course at the selected time, or "No" otherwise.
21.  **Course Teacher**: Select "Yes" if you are taking the course taught by the selected teacher, or "No" otherwise.
22.  **Course Teacher Notes**: Add any additional notes about the teacher.
23.  **Teacher Email**: Enter the email address of the course teacher.
24.  **Teacher Phone**: Enter the phone number of the course teacher.
25.  **Teacher Address**: Enter the address of the course teacher.
