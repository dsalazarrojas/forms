# Preschool Package Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a student for a preschool package, capturing essential details about the student and their parent.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
- Provide the required information for each field.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the parent's full name.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the parent's contact number.
- **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of session the student will attend (Session A, Session B, or Session C).
- **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the session.
- **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the session.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's full name.
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the parent's email address.
- **Parent Number** (`parent_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional parent number or identifier.
- **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
- **Channel Finding** (`channel_finding`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how the parent found out about the preschool package.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's full name (second entry for confirmation).
- **Preferred Channel** (`preferred_channel`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the preferred channels for communication (Parent Portal, Parent App, Website, Social Media, or Other).
- **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's gender.
- **Student Age** (`student_age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the student's age.
- **Student School** (`student_school`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's school.
- **Student Class** (`student_class`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's class.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's grade.
- **Student Section** (`student_section`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's section.
- **Student Image** (`student_image`, `text`, required/optional): Upload or enter the student's image URL.
- **Student Image URL** (`student_image_url`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's image URL.
- **Parent Image** (`parent_image`, `text`, required/optional): Upload or enter the parent's image URL.
- **Parent Image URL** (`parent_image_url`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the parent's image URL.
- **Channel Finding** (`channel_finding`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how the parent found out about the preschool package.
- **Preferred Session** (`preferred_session`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the preferred session for the student.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the student's registration.
