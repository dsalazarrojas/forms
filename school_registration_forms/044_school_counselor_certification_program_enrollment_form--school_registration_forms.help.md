# School Counselor Certification Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect applicant information and enrollment data for the School Counselor Certification Program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out the form as instructed. 
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information in the required fields.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional details or comments from the applicant.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school's name.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter your school's location or address.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program you are enrolling in.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the program you are enrolling in.
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of certification you are seeking (School Counselor, School Counselor Assistant, School Counselor Supervisor).
* **Certification Level** (`certification_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of certification you are seeking (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
* **Application Fee** (`application_fee`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the application fee.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method of payment (Online Payment, Offline Payment).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the payment status (Paid, Partially Paid, Not Paid).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the status of your submission (Approved, Rejected, Pending).

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information in the required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
