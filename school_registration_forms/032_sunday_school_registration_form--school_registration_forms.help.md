# Sunday School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunday School Registration Form is designed to collect information from students and their parents/guardians for the purpose of registration. This form will help us keep track of student's details, school information, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's **User Name** in the field provided.
2. Enter your student's **Email** address if you have one.
3. Provide your student's **Phone** number if available.
4. Enter your student's **Date of Birth** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Enter your student's **Address**, **City**, and **State**.
6. Enter your student's **Zip Code**.
7. If applicable, enter your **Parent Name** and **Parent Email**.
8. Enter the name of the **School** your student will be attending.
9. Select your student's **Class** (First, Second, or Third).
10. Enter your student's **Grade** level (if applicable).
11. Enter your student's **Guardian Phone** number if applicable.
12. Enter the name and **Emergency Contact** information.
13. Enter the **Emergency Phone** number and **Emergency Email** of the emergency contact.
14. Add any additional **Notes** about the student or their situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: A field to enter your student's name.
* **Email**: A field to enter your student's email address (if available).
* **Phone**: A field to enter your student's phone number (if available).
* **Date of Birth**: A field to enter your student's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address**: A field to enter your student's home address.
* **City**: A field to enter your student's city.
* **State**: A field to enter your student's state.
* **Zip Code**: A field to enter your student's zip code.
* **Parent Name**: A field to enter your student's parent's name (if applicable).
* **Parent Email**: A field to enter your student's parent's email address (if applicable).
* **School Name**: A field to enter the name of the school your student will be attending.
* **Class**: A dropdown menu to select the class your student will be attending (First, Second, or Third).
* **Grade**: A field to enter your student's grade level (if applicable).
* **Guardian Phone**: A field to enter your student's guardian's phone number (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact**: A field to enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone**: A field to enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email**: A field to enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Notes**: A field for any additional comments or information about the student.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your input for any typos or errors.
* If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
