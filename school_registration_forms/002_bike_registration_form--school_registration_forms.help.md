# Bike Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bike Registration Form is used to register bikes for students and staff at the school campus. This form helps to gather essential information about the bike and its owner to ensure accurate and complete records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each field carefully and fill out the required fields.
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you need help with any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (page 1)**: Enter your student details for bike registration purposes.
	+ First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your student ID.
	+ Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your student ID.
	+ Student ID Number: Enter your school identification number.
* **Grade Level (page 1)**: Select your current grade level, i.e., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, or Undergraduate.
* **School Email (page 2)**: Enter your official school email address.
* **Phone Number (page 2)**: Enter your contact number for notifications.
* **Bike Information (page 3)**: This is a note field for you to enter any additional bike details.
* **Bike Type (page 3)**: Select the type of bike you own, i.e., Road, Mountain, Hybrid, etc.
* **Brand (page 3)**: Enter the manufacturer's name of your bike.
* **Model (page 3)**: Enter the model name or number of your bike.
* **Color (page 3)**: Enter the primary color of your bike.
* **Serial Number (page 3)**: Enter the frame serial number for security purposes.
* **Bike Condition (page 3)**: Select the current condition of your bike, i.e., Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Repair.
* **Preferred Parking Location (page 4)**: Select where you would like to park your bike, i.e., North Campus Rack, South Campus Rack, etc.
* **Registration Purpose (page 4)**: Select the reason for your bike registration, i.e., Campus Parking Permit, Security Registration, etc.
* **Emergency Contact Name (page 5)**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (page 5)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Safety Equipment Owned (page 5)**: Select the safety gear you own, i.e., Helmet, Bike Lights, etc.
* **Terms Agreement (page 5)**: Select the option to acknowledge the campus biking rules and regulations.
* **Registration Date (page 6)**: The date of this registration will be automatically filled out.

Note: If you need assistance with any field or have questions, please contact your school administration for support.
