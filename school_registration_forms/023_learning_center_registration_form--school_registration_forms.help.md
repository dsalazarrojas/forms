# learning_center_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register new learners in the learning center. It is used to collect information from individuals who want to join the learning center, and it helps us to track and manage their details efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Learner** (required): Enter your name and details as the new learner.
* **Date of Birth** (required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number.
* **Learner Category** (optional): Choose your category from the options Student, Staff, or Faculty.
* **Learning Center** (optional): Enter the name of the learning center where you will be learning.
* **Registered By** (optional): Enter the name of the person who registered you.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct email address and phone number to ensure easy contact and communication.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
* Please review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
