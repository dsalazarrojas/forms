# High School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal information from students and their parents for high school admission processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as prompted. The form requires filling out all the fields to ensure accurate and complete information is captured.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your full first name in this field.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address to enable communication with the school.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Contact Information** (Parent Contact Information, text, required): Enter any relevant contact information for the parent or guardian.
* **Student First Name** (Student First Name, text, required): Enter the student's full first name.
* **Student Last Name** (Student Last Name, text, required): Enter the student's full last name.
* **Student Birthday** (Student Birthday, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **School Last Attended** (School Last Attended, text, required): Enter the name of the school the student last attended.
* **Student Grade Level** (Student Grade Level, number, required): Enter the student's current grade level.
* **Contact First Name** (Contact First Name, text, required): Enter your full first name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Last Name** (Contact Last Name, text, required): Enter your full last name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Guardian Name** (Guardian Name, text, required): Enter the guardian's full name.
* **Relationship** (Relationship, select one, required): Select the relationship between you and the guardian.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, required): Enter any medical conditions relevant to the student.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, required): Enter any medication the student takes.
* **Upload a file** (Upload a file, text, required): Upload a supporting file for the application. 
* **Upload another file** (Upload another file, text, optional): This field is optional and used to upload another file for supporting information.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out as required.
- If you have any doubts or queries, contact the school administration for further assistance.
