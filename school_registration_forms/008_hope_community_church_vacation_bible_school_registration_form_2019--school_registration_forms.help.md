# Hope Community Church Vacation Bible School Registration Form 2019 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hope Community Church Vacation Bible School Registration Form is used to collect registration information from students and their parents. This form is mandatory for all students attending the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the registration date of the student in the `Registration Date` field.
2. Fill in the student's `Name` field with their name.
3. Enter the parent's `Name` in the `Parent Name` field.
4. Fill in the student's contact information in the `Contact Number` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your child's registration.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please fill in the student's name as it appears on their ID or birth certificate.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Fill in the parent/guardian's name who will be responsible for the student's care.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Please enter the parent/guardian's phone number so we can contact them if needed.
* **School Class** (`school_class`, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level from the options provided:
	+ First Grade
	+ Third Grade
	+ Fifth Grade
	+ Pre-Kindergarten
* **School Year** (`school_year`, number, optional): Enter the student's school year if you know it.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level from the options provided:
	+ Preschool
	+ Pre-Kindergarten
	+ First Grade
	+ Second Grade
	+ Third Grade
	+ Fourth Grade
	+ Fifth Grade
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent/guardian's phone number for emergency contact.
* **Student Allergies** (`student_allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or medical conditions the student has.
* **Student Medical Conditions** (`student_medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions the student has.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the student.
