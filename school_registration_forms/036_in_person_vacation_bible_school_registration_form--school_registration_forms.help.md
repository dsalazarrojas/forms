# In Person Vacation Bible School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In-Person Vacation Bible School Registration Form is used to register students and their emergency contacts for VBS programs. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form correctly and provide explanations for each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by filling out the student information fields, such as First Name, Last Name, and Grade. Then, enter the emergency contact information, including the Emergency Contact First Name, Last Name, and Phone number. You will also need to select the emergency contact's relationship to the student and enter their email address. Finally, confirm whether the student attends school and enter the school information if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's last name.
* **Grade** (`Grade`, `number`, optional): Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent First Name** (`Parent First Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent's first name (if different from the student's first name).
* **Parent Last Name** (`Parent Last Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent's last name (if different from the student's last name).
* **Student Grade** (`Student Grade`, `number`, required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`Emergency Contact First Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`Emergency Contact Last Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's last name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select the emergency contact's relationship to the student (e.g., Mom, Dad, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`Emergency Contact Address`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`Emergency Contact City`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's city.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`Emergency Contact State`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's state.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (`Emergency Contact Zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's zip code.
* **Student Attends School** (`Student Attends School`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether the student attends school.
* **Student Attends School Name** (`Student Attends School Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's school name (if applicable).
* **Student Attends School Grade** (`Student Attends School Grade`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's grade level at school (if applicable).
* **Student Attends School City** (`Student Attends School City`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's school city (if applicable).
* **Student Attends School State** (`Student Attends School State`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's school state (if applicable).
* **Student Attends School Zip** (`Student Attends School Zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's school zip code (if applicable).
* **Student Attends School County** (`Student Attends School County`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's school county (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If the student does not attend school, you can skip the school fields.
* If the student attends school, enter the school information accurately and completely.
* Use the emergency contact relationship options to select the correct relationship to the student.
