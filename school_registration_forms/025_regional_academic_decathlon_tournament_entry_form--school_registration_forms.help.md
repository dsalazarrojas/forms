# Regional Academic Decathlon Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for schools to register their teams for the regional academic decathlon tournament. Please fill in all required fields to ensure successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the required fields marked as such.
2. Fill in all required fields with accurate information.
3. Ensure that you have not already registered for this competition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (school_name, text, true): Enter your school's name.
* **Team Name** (team_name, text, true): Enter your team's name.
* **School Code** (school_code, text, true): Enter your school's code.
* **School Address** (school_address, text, true): Enter your school's address.
* **Team Motto** (team_motto, text, true): Enter your team's motto.
* **Team Division** (team_division, select_one, true): Select 'Yes' if your team is part of a division.
* **School Type** (school_type, select_one, true): Select 'Yes' if your school is part of a type.
* **School Phone** (school_phone, text, true): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Email** (school_email, email, true): Enter your school's email address.
* **Team Members** (team_members, text, true): List your team members.
* **Team Coach** (team_coach, text, true): Enter your team coach's name.
* **Team Coach Phone** (team_coach_phone, text, true): Enter your team coach's phone number.
* **Team Coach Email** (team_coach_email, email, true): Enter your team coach's email address.
* **Team Members List** (team_members_list, note, true): List your team members (this field has a character limit).
* **Team Score** (team_score, number, true): Enter your team's score.
* **Team Ranking** (team_ranking, select_one, true): Select 'Yes' if your team is ranked.
* **Division 1 Name** (division_1_name, text, false): Optional, enter the name of your division 1.
* **Division 1 Score** (division_1_score, number, false): Optional, enter the score of your division 1.
* **Division 2 Name** (division_2_name, text, false): Optional, enter the name of your division 2.
* **Division 2 Score** (division_2_score, number, false): Optional, enter the score of your division 2.
* **Division 3 Name** (division_3_name, text, false): Optional, enter the name of your division 3.
* **Division 3 Score** (division_3_score, number, false): Optional, enter the score of your division 3.
* **Division 4 Name** (division_4_name, text, false): Optional, enter the name of your division 4.
* **Division 4 Score** (division_4_score, number, false): Optional, enter the score of your division 4.
* **Division 5 Name** (division_5_name, text, false): Optional, enter the name of your division 5.
* **Division 5 Score** (division_5_score, number, false): Optional, enter the score of your division 5.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid duplication checks.
* If a field is marked as optional, it's not required for submission, but it's still important to fill it in for completeness.
