# School Field Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Field Trip Registration Form is a tool used by school administrators to collect information from students and teachers for field trips. This form helps gather essential details such as student contact information, teacher consent, and payment details, ensuring a smooth and organized trip experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
3.  Provide the student's contact information in the "Student Contact" field (optional).
4.  Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
5.  Enter the teacher's email address in the "Teacher Email" field.
6.  Indicate whether the student has given their consent in the "Student Consent" field (optional).
7.  Provide emergency contact information 1 and 2 in the "Emergency Contact 1" and "Emergency Contact 2" fields (optional).
8.  Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" field (optional).
9.  Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
10. Enter the school's address in the "School Address" field (optional).
11. Enter the school's city in the "School City" field (optional).
12. Enter the school's province in the "School Province" field (optional).
13. Enter the school's postal code in the "School Postal" field (optional).
14. Provide the school contact information in the "School Contact" field (optional).
15. Indicate whether the teacher has given their consent in the "Teacher Consent" field (optional).
16. Add any additional notes from the teacher in the "Teacher Notes" field (optional).

*   **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their record.
