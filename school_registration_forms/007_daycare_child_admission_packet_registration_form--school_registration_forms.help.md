# Daycare Child Admission Packet Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information for a child's admission to the daycare. Please complete the form accurately to help us better understand your child's needs and provide the best services for them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Registration Information section with the child's details.
2. Provide your parent details in the Parent Details section.
3. Complete the Child Details section with information about your child.
4. Check if your child has any allergies or medical conditions in the Allergies or Medical Conditions section.
5. List any emergency contact information in the Emergency Contact section.
6. Enter any relevant medical information in the Medical Information section.
7. Enter your payment details for the services provided.
8. Indicate if you need to review or finalize your submission.
9. Select the review date and time if you choose to review your submission.
10. Confirm your submission by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`daycare_child_registration_form`, text, required): This is where you provide your child's name, address, phone number, and any other relevant information.
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, required): Please provide your name, address, and phone number as a parent/guardian.
* **Child Details** (`child_details`, text, required): Enter your child's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Allergies or Medical Conditions** (`allergy_or_medical_condition`, select multiple, optional): Check any allergies or medical conditions your child has, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): List the name, relationship, phone number, and address of a trusted emergency contact.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, note, optional): Enter any relevant medical information about your child.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Provide payment information for the services, including payment method and amount.
* **Review** (`review`, select one, optional): Indicate if you need to review or finalize your submission.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to review your submission.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): Select the time you plan to review your submission.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select one, required): Confirm your submission by selecting "Yes".
