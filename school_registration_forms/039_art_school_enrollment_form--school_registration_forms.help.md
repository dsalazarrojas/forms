# Art School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art School Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential information from students interested in enrolling in our art school program. This form will help us understand your background, interests, and needs to provide a smooth and effective enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each section carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
2. For fields marked as "required," you must provide a valid response.
3. For fields with options, select the most suitable choice for your situation.
4. If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification by contacting us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required)
Date of application. This is the date when you submit this form.

### 2. **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required)
Your legal first name.

### 3. **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required)
Your legal last name.

### 4. **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required)
Your email address for communication with the school.

### 5. **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required)
Your contact phone number for any updates or emergencies.

### 6. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
Your date of birth.

### 7. **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required)
Your full address, including street, city, and state.

### 8. **City** (`city`, text, required)
The city where you live.

### 9. **State** (`state`, text, required)
The state where you live.

### 10. **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required)
Your postal or zip code.

### 11. **Program Interest** (`program_interest`, select_one, required)
Select the art program you're interested in:

*   Fine Arts
*   Graphic Design
*   Photography
*   Sculpture
*   Painting
*   Digital Arts
*   Art History
*   Illustration
*   Animation
*   Other

### 12. **Degree Type** (`degree_type`, select_one, required)
Select the type of degree or program you're applying for:

*   Certificate Program
*   Associate Degree
*   Bachelor's Degree
*   Master's Degree
*   Non-Degree
*   Continuing Education

### 13. **Enrollment Term** (`enrollment_term`, select_one, required)
Select the term of enrollment:

*   Fall Semester
*   Spring Semester
*   Summer Semester
*   Full Year

### 14. **Enrollment Year** (`enrollment_year`, number, required)
The year you wish to enroll.

### 15. **Full Time Part Time** (`full_time_part_time`, select_one, required)
Select your enrollment status:

*   Full-Time
*   Part-Time

### 16. **High School Name** (`high_school_name`, text, optional)
Name of high school (if applicable).

### 17. **High School Graduation** (`high_school_graduation`, number, optional)
Year of graduation (if applicable).

### 18. **GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional)
Grade point average (if applicable).

### 19. **Previous Art Education** (`previous_art_education`, select_one, required)
Have you had prior art education:

*   True
*   False

### 20. **Previous Education Details** (`previous_education_details`, text, optional)
Describe any prior art education.

### 21. **Portfolio Submitted** (`portfolio_submitted`, select_one, required)
Have you submitted a portfolio:

*   True
*   False
*   Will Submit Later

### 22. **Artistic Mediums** (`artistic_mediums`, select_multiple, required)
Select the mediums you work with:

*   Drawing
*   Painting
*   Sculpture
*   Photography
*   Digital Art
*   Printmaking
*   Ceramics
*   Textiles
*   Mixed Media
*   Other

### 23. **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, required)
Describe your career goals.

### 24. **Why This School** (`why_this_school`, text, required)
Explain why you want to attend our art school.

### 25. **Financial Aid Needed** (`financial_aid_needed`, select_one, required)
Do you need financial aid:

*   True
*   False

### 26. **Scholarship Interest** (`scholarship_interest`, select_one, required)
Are you interested in scholarships:

*   True
*   False

### 27. **Housing Needed** (`housing_needed`, select_one, optional)
Do you need student housing:

*   Yes - On Campus
*   Yes - Off Campus Assistance
*   False

### 28. **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional)
Any accessibility requirements you may have.

### 29. **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required)
Name of emergency contact person.

### 30. **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required)
Contact phone number for emergency contact person.

### 31. **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required)
Relationship to student (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).

### 32. **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required)
Have you accepted the school terms and conditions:

*   True
*   False

### 33. **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required)
Sign with full name as student.

### 34. **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, optional)
Parent signature (if student is under 18).

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed accurately.
*   Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
*   Ensure that you have submitted a valid portfolio (if applicable).
*   Clarify any doubts or uncertainties with the form by contacting us.
