# Freshman Welcome Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Freshman Welcome Registration Form is designed to collect information about new freshmen students, ensuring a smooth transition to university life.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details: First name, last name, date of birth, email, phone number, and student ID.
2. Select your orientation date and time.
3. Enter your parent's name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
4. Indicate your relationship with the emergency contact.
5. Choose your student type, program, and interests.
6. Select your emergency contact's relationship with you.
7. Select your student attendance frequency and reason.
8. Enter any additional notes about yourself (optional).
9. Enter your student's phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID.
* **Orientation Date** (`orientation_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your orientation.
* **Orientation Time** (`select_orientation_time`, select_one, optional): Select the time of your orientation (10:00 am, 11:00 am, 12:00 pm).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your parent's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your parent's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, optional): Indicate your relationship with the emergency contact (Father, Mother, Friend, Relative, Other).
* **Student Type** (`student_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your student type (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Other).
* **Student Program** (`student_program`, select_multiple, optional): Select your student program (Arts, Business, Engineering, Other).
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your student interests (Art, Music, Sports, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Father, Mother, Relative, Friend, Other).
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Select your student attendance frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Student Attendance Reason** (`student_attendance_reason`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reason for your student attendance (Academic goals, Family obligations, Medical, Personal preferences, Other).
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about yourself.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* If a field is marked as optional, feel free to enter any additional information you'd like.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the school's administration.
