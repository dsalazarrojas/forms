# Principal For A Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Principal For A Day Registration" form is designed to gather information from schools interested in participating in the "Principal For A Day" program. This program allows students to take on the role of principal for a day, providing a unique opportunity for them to experience leadership and administrative tasks. This form helps schools register their interest and provide necessary contact and program-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Form Details" page with your school's information, including the school's name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Select the dates on which you would like to participate in the "Principal For A Day" program.
3. Confirm your consent to participate by selecting "Yes" in the "Consents" field.
4. Provide your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email.
5. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details** (`form_details`, `text`, required): Enter your school's name and provide a brief description of your participation in the program.
* **Dates** (`dates`, `text`, required): Select the dates on which you would like to participate in the "Principal For A Day" program.
* **Consents** (`consents`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your consent to participate by selecting "Yes".
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Provide your name, phone number, and email address for contact purposes.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your submission by selecting "Yes".
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required): Provide your name.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, `text`, optional): Optional comments to provide additional information about your submission.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter your school's name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required): Enter your school's address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `text`, required): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's fax number.
* **School Comments** (`school_comments`, `text`, optional): Optional comments to provide additional information about your school.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's website URL.
* **Principal For A Day** (`principal_for_a_day`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your principal for a day participation.
* **Principal For A Day Name** (`principal_for_a_day_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the principal for a day.
* **Principal For A Day School** (`principal_for_a_day_school`, `text`, required): Enter your school's name.
* **Principal For A Day Email** (`principal_for_a_day_email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address as the principal for a day.
* **Principal For A Day Phone** (`principal_for_a_day_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as the principal for a day.
* **Principal For A Day Notes** (`principal_for_a_day_notes`, `text`, optional): Optional notes about your principal for a day participation.
* **Principal For A Day Comments** (`principal_for_a_day_comments`, `text`, optional): Optional comments about your principal for a day participation.
