# School Registration Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Registration Time Off Form is used to record and track student time-off requests from teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose whether you want to request time off or not.
2. Select a reason for your time-off request (optional).
3. Select the start and end dates of your time-off request.
4. Provide any additional comments about your request (optional).
5. Enter your email address (optional).
6. Enter your name as a student (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Time Off** (`request_time_off`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you want to request time off, or 'No' if you do not want to.
  - This field determines whether or not you wish to make a time-off request.
- **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): If you want to provide a reason for your time-off request, enter it here.
  - This field is optional, but providing a reason can help the school administration understand your request.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the start date of your time-off request.
  - Enter the date you want your time-off to start.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the end date of your time-off request.
  - Enter the date you want your time-off to end.
- **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information to provide about your time-off request, enter it here.
  - This field is optional, and you can provide any additional information you think is relevant.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
  - This field is optional, but providing your email address can help the school administration contact you if they need more information.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a student.
  - This field is optional, and you can provide your name if you wish to.
