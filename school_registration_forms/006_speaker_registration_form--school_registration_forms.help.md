# Speaker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Registration Form is designed to collect essential details from speakers who will be presenting or participating in an event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form with accurate and relevant information.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Check the help guide for each field for specific guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the event schedule and marketing materials.
* **Speaker Email**: Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Speaker Phone**: Enter your contact phone number that we can use to reach you if needed.
* **Topic Title**: Enter a concise title of your presentation.
* **Topic Description**: Provide a brief description of your presentation.
* **Speaker Affiliation**: Enter your current or past affiliation (e.g., company, university, organization).
* **Speaker Bio**: Enter a brief bio about yourself, including relevant experience and qualifications.
* **Speaker Website**: Enter your personal or professional website URL (if applicable).
* **Speaker Headline Photo**: Upload a photo that will be displayed with your name in the event schedule and marketing materials.
* **Presentation Length (minutes)**: Enter the expected length of your presentation.
* **Presentation Date**: Enter the date of your presentation.
* **Presentation Time**: Enter the time of your presentation.
* **Speaker Type**: Select your role in the event (e.g., Keynote, Panelist, Regular Speaker).
* **Speaker Status**: Select your status in the event (e.g., Confirmed, Pending, Declined).
* **Speaker Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete your registration.

Note: The Speaker Interests, Speaker Experience, Speaker Education, Speaker Occupation, Speaker Language, Speaker Native Language, Speaker Gender, Speaker Ethnicity, and Speaker Additional Info fields are not currently used in the event management process. However, they may be used in future updates or customizations of the form.
