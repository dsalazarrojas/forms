# School Activity Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Activity Permission Form is used to gather information for school activities and events, ensuring that parents or guardians provide necessary permissions and details before their child participates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
* Fill out your child's name, activity name, start and end dates, location, and any additional comments.
* Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`school_activity_permission_form_parent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child participating in the activity.
* **Activity Name** (`school_activity_permission_form_activity_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school activity or event your child is participating in.
* **Start Date** (`school_activity_permission_form_start_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when the activity starts.
* **End Date** (`school_activity_permission_form_end_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when the activity ends.
* **Location** (`school_activity_permission_form_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the activity will take place.
* **Description** (`school_activity_permission_form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the activity.
* **Parent Email** (`school_activity_permission_form_parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so the school can contact you.
* **Parent Phone** (`school_activity_permission_form_parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so the school can contact you.
* **Additional Comments** (`school_activity_permission_form_additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to the school to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the school directly.
* The school will use the information provided to ensure your child's safety and well-being during the activity.
