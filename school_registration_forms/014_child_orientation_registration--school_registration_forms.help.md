# Child Orientation Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for parents or guardians to register their child for orientation. It collects information about the child's details, parent/guardian contact information, and preferences for orientation sessions. The form is required for all students who are new to the school or have not attended before.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the child's registration details, such as their date of birth, name, and contact information for their parents or guardians.
2. Select the preferred orientation session and date.
3. Choose who will attend the orientation (child, parent or guardian, or both).
4. Indicate if the child has any previous school experience or needs special programs.
5. Specify any transportation or meal plan needs.
6. List any medical conditions or allergies the child has.
7. Provide any additional comments or questions for the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): This is the date the child is registered for orientation.
* **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`, text, required): This is the legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`, text, required): This is the legal last name of the child.
* **Child Nickname** (`child_nickname`, text, optional): This is the child's preferred name or nickname.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, date, required): This is the child's birth date.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`, number, required): This is the child's current age in years.
* **Child Gender** (`child_gender`, select_one, required): Select the child's gender.
* **Grade Level Enrolling** (`grade_level_enrolling`, select_one, required): Select the grade level the child will be enrolling in.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`parent_guardian_first_name`, text, required): This is the legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`parent_guardian_last_name`, text, required): This is the legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, required): This is the primary email address for contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, required): This is the primary phone number for contact.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): This is the full street address of the child's residence.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): This is the city of the child's residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): This is the state or province of the child's residence.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): This is the postal or zip code of the child's residence.
* **Orientation Session Preference** (`orientation_session_preference`, select_one, required): Select the preferred orientation session (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend).
* **Preferred Orientation Date** (`orientation_date_preference`, date, required): Select the preferred date for orientation.
* **Who Will Attend Orientation** (`attending_orientation`, select_multiple, required): Select all who will attend the orientation (Child, Parent or Guardian, Both, Siblings, or Other family members).
* **Previous School Experience** (`previous_school_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate if the child has attended school or daycare before.
* **Special Programs Interest** (`special_programs_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select any special programs the child is interested in (Arts and Music, Sports and Athletics, STEM, Language Immersion, Special Education Services, Gifted and Talented, or None).
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if transportation services are needed.
* **Meal Plan Needed** (`meal_plan_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if the child will participate in the school meal program.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any medical conditions or allergies the child has.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions for the school.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the school administration.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
