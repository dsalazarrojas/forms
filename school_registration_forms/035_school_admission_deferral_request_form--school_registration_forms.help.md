# School Admission Deferral Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Admission Deferral Request Form is designed to collect information from students and schools to facilitate the deferral process of school admission. This form is used to gather relevant details for successful and smooth deferral handling.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Fill out the form in its entirety, including all required fields.
*   Provide accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Deferral Request Form (1)**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional comments about your request.
*   **Student Name (2)**: Enter the full name of the student who is requesting deferral.
*   **Start Date (3)**: Enter the date the student plans to start attending school.
*   **End Date (4)**: Enter the date the student plans to end their attendance.
*   **Reason for Deferral (5)**: Provide a brief explanation for the deferral request.
*   **Expected Start Date (6)**: Enter the date the student is expected to start attending school in the future.
*   **Other (7)**: If the reason for deferral is not listed, provide a brief explanation in the text field below.
*   **Other Details (8)**: Provide additional details about the other reason for deferral.
*   **Contact Details (9)**: Enter any contact information for the student or school, if necessary.
*   **Student DOB (10)**: Enter the date of birth of the student.
*   **Parent Signature (11)**: Sign the form as a parent or guardian of the student.
*   **Teacher Name (12)**: Select the name of the teacher who is recommending or supporting the deferral request.
*   **Teacher Signature (13)**: Sign the form as the teacher.
*   **School Name (14)**: Select the name of the school where the student will attend.
*   **Student Grade Level (15)**: Select all applicable grade levels that the student is eligible for.
*   **School Address (16)**: Enter the address of the school.
*   **School City (17)**: Enter the city of the school.
*   **School State (18)**: Enter the state of the school.
*   **School Zip (19)**: Enter the zip code of the school.
*   **School Phone (20)**: Enter the phone number of the school.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have provided all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
*   Review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submission.
