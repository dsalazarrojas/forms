# The Little Scholars Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Little Scholars Application Form is designed to collect essential information from students and their parents or guardians. This form is crucial for the school administration to verify the identity of new students and ensure they have all the necessary details to create a student account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to select the correct school from the options provided.
3. Enter the student's name, date of birth, and start date at school correctly.
4. Choose the grade level of the student from the options.
5. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is filling out this form.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, **required**): Enter the contact number of the parent or guardian. This should be a valid phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **School** (`school_name`, select_one, **required**): Select the school from the options provided. This will help the school administration to identify the student's school affiliation.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, **required**): Enter the student's name accurately and correctly.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, **required**): Enter the student's date of birth. This is crucial for age verification purposes.
* **Start Date** (`student_start_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date the student will start attending school.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, **required**): Choose the grade level of the student from the options provided.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, **required**): Enter the student's home address accurately and correctly.
* **Birthdate** (`student_birthdate`, date, **required**): Enter the student's birthdate. This is also crucial for age verification purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all the fields before submitting the form.
- If you need help or have any issues, please contact the school administration for assistance.
- Ensure that all the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
