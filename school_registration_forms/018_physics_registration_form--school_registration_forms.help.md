# Physics Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Registration Form is designed to collect information from students, teachers, or staff members related to physics registration. This form aims to gather essential details such as names, contact information, and academic data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and understand the required fields (marked as required) and optional fields.
2. Fill in the required fields (First Name, Last Name, and School ID) with accurate information.
3. Fill in the optional fields (Email, Phone, Physics Teacher, Class, Time) according to your preference.
4. For the "Physics Teacher" field, select one of the listed options (Mr. John, Mrs. Emma, or Mr. Smith).
5. For the "Class" field, select multiple classes (A, B, or C) according to your enrollment or preference.
6. You can leave the "Email" field blank if you do not have an email address or prefer not to share it.
7. You can leave the "Phone" field blank if you do not have a phone number or prefer not to share it.
8. For the "Physics Registration Form" has an "Note" field. You can use this field to add any additional information or comments you feel is necessary.
9. Once you have filled in all the required and optional fields, review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **School ID**: Enter your school ID number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Physics Teacher**: Select one of the listed options (Mr. John, Mrs. Emma, or Mr. Smith).
* **Class**: Select multiple classes (A, B, or C) according to your enrollment or preference.
* **Time**: You can leave this field blank or fill it in according to your availability or preference.
* **Note**: Use this field to add any additional information or comments you feel is necessary.
