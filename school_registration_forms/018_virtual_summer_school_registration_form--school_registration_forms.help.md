# Virtual Summer School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and parents to register for a virtual summer school program. The information collected will be used to create a personalized schedule and provide relevant resources and support during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to enter all required fields.
- Review and submit the form before confirming registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student registering for the program.
* **Student Grade Level**: Choose the student's grade level to help us tailor the program to their needs.
* **Student Birthdate**: Enter the student's birthdate to help us verify their age and eligibility.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address to receive program updates and notifications.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian registering the student.
* **Parent Email**: Enter the parent's or guardian's email address to receive program updates and notifications.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number for contact purposes.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the student's school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the student's school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the student's school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the student's school.
* **Student Start Date**: Choose the date the student plans to start the program.
* **Student End Date**: Choose the date the student plans to end the program.
* **Form Submission By**: Enter the date and time you are submitting this form.
* **School Start Date**: Choose the date the school plans to start the program.
* **School End Date**: Choose the date the school plans to end the program.
* **Student Name 2**: Enter the name of any additional students registering for the program.
* **Student Email 2**: Enter the email address of any additional students registering for the program.
* **Student Grade Level 2**: Choose the grade level of any additional students registering for the program.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you are registering multiple students, please fill out each student's information separately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
