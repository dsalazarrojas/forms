# Course Overview Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Course Overview Acknowledgement Form is a form designed to gather acknowledgement from students on course overview details. This form is intended to ensure that students and their guardians/parents have a clear understanding of the course goals, objectives, and expectations. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your student name in the **student_name** field.
2.  Enter your student email address in the **student_email** field.
3.  Enter your parent/guardian name in the **parent_name** field. (If applicable)
4.  Enter your student phone number in the **student_phone** field. (If applicable)
5.  Enter your course name (or the course you are currently working on/within) in the **course_name** field.
6.  Finally, submit the form once all fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **course_overview_acknowledgement_form**: This is the main field where you will be entering the course overview acknowledgement details. Please provide a brief explanation of what you understand about the course.
*   **student_name**: Enter your full name as a student.
*   **student_email**: Enter your student email address.
*   **parent_name**: Enter your parent/guardian name (if applicable).
*   **student_phone**: Enter your student phone number (if applicable).
*   **course_name**: Enter the name of the course you are currently working on/within.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your acknowledgement is complete and accurate.
*   If you are a student, please ensure that your parent/guardian is aware of this form and provides their acknowledgement as well.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your course instructor or administrator.
