# Optional Support Class Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows students to select their interests, grade level, and preferred times for support classes. It is an optional tool to help teachers and support staff better understand students' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your interest in the subject matter that resonates with you the most.
2.  Choose your grade level to help us understand your current academic situation.
3.  Select your preferred times for support classes, as you would like to attend them.
4.  Choose the days you are available to attend support classes.
5.  Enter your student email address so we can contact you for updates and notifications.
6.  Select the tutor you would like to work with, if applicable.
7.  Optionally, add any additional notes about your support needs or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Student Interest**: This field allows you to choose the subject matter that interests you the most. Please select the option that best describes your interest.
* **Select Grade Level**: This field asks for your current grade level to help us understand your academic situation. Please choose the number that corresponds to your current grade level.
* **Select Preferred Time**: This field gives you the flexibility to choose a time slot that fits your schedule best for support classes. Select one or more times that you are available to attend.
* **Select Session Days**: This field is required for you to indicate which days you are available to attend support classes. Select all the days that apply to you.
* **Student Email**: This is the email address where we will contact you for updates and notifications. Please enter your student email address.
* **Select Tutor**: This field is optional, but if you have a preferred tutor, you can select them here. Please choose the tutor that you would like to work with.
