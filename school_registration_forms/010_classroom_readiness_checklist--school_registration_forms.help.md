# Classroom Readiness Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to walk you through the completion of the Classroom Readiness Checklist form. This form is designed to help teachers ensure that their classroom is ready for the first day of school by checking off a series of critical tasks and items needed to be completed before the school year begins.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section of the form carefully, making sure you have addressed all the items listed.
2. Select the appropriate status for each item, using the provided options (Completed, In Progress, Needs Attention).
3. Fill in any necessary details, such as dates or specific items needed.
4. Review the Overall Readiness Level section to determine the overall state of your classroom.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name**:
	+ **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Room Number**:
	+ **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required): Enter your classroom number.
* **Inspection Date**:
	+ **Inspection Date** (`check_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your inspection.
* **All Desks and Chairs Are Accounted For and In Good Repair**:
	+ **All Desks and Chairs Are Accounted For and In Good Repair** (`furniture_ready`, select_one, required): Choose Completed if all desks and chairs are in good repair and accounted for, In Progress if some are missing or damaged, or Needs Attention if all are missing or severely damaged.
* **Basic Classroom Supplies Are Stocked**:
	+ **Basic Classroom Supplies Are Stocked** (`supplies_stocked`, select_one, required): Choose Completed if all basic supplies, such as markers, paper, and other essentials, are stocked, In Progress if some are missing or need to be replenished, or Needs Attention if all are missing or severely depleted.
* **Computer, Projector, and Speakers Are Functioning**:
	+ **Computer, Projector, and Speakers Are Functioning** (`tech_check`, select_one, required): Choose Completed if all technology is working properly, In Progress if some are not functioning, or Needs Attention if all are not functioning.
* **Fire Extinguisher, First Aid Kit, and Emergency Maps Are Present**:
	+ **Fire Extinguisher, First Aid Kit, and Emergency Maps Are Present** (`safety_items`, select_one, required): Choose Completed if all safety items are present and ready for use, In Progress if some are missing or need attention, or Needs Attention if all are missing or need immediate attention.
* **Bulletin Boards and Wall Displays Are Updated**:
	+ **Bulletin Boards and Wall Displays Are Updated** (`displays_ready`, select_one, required): Choose Completed if all bulletin boards and wall displays are updated and ready for the new year, In Progress if some are outdated or need updating, or Needs Attention if all are outdated or missing.
* **Name Tags and Cubby Labels Are Prepared**:
	+ **Name Tags and Cubby Labels Are Prepared** (`student_materials`, select_one, required): Choose Completed if all name tags and cubby labels are prepared and ready for use, In Progress if some are missing or need attention, or Needs Attention if all are missing or need immediate attention.
* **First Week Lesson Plans and Materials Are Organized**:
	+ **First Week Lesson Plans and Materials Are Organized** (`curriculum_ready`, select_one, required): Choose Completed if all first week lesson plans and materials are organized and ready for distribution, In Progress if some are missing or need attention, or Needs Attention if all are missing or need immediate attention.
* **Floors Are Clean and Trash Bins Are Empty**:
	+ **Floors Are Clean and Trash Bins Are Empty** (`cleaning_verified`, select_one, required): Choose Completed if the floors are clean and trash bins are empty, In Progress if some areas are dirty or need attention, or Needs Attention if all areas are dirty or overflowing.
* **Overall Classroom Readiness Level**:
	+ **Overall Classroom Readiness Level** (`overall_readiness`, select_one, required): Choose your overall classroom readiness level based on the above items (100% Ready, 75% Ready - minor issues, 50% Ready - needs work, or Not Ready).
* **List Specific Items or Repairs Needed**:
	+ **List Specific Items or Repairs Needed** (`items_needed`, text, optional): If there are any specific items or repairs needed, list them here.
* **Was a Maintenance Ticket Submited for Any Repairs?**:
	+ **Was a Maintenance Ticket Submited for Any Repairs?** (`maintenance_ticket`, select_one, required): Choose True if a maintenance ticket was submitted for any repairs, False if no tickets were submitted, or Not Applicable if you are a new teacher or not responsible for repairs.
* **Principal Review Date**:
	+ **Principal Review Date** (`principal_review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the principal's review of your classroom.
