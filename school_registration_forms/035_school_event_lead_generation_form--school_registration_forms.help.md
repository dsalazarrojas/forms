# School Event Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event information from school leads to generate potential leads for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event in the `event_name` field.
2. Enter the email address of the contact person in the `email` field.
3. Enter the name of the contact person in the `contact_name` field.
4. Enter the date and time of the event in the `event_date` and `event_time` fields, respectively.
5. Enter the location of the event in the `event_location` field.
6. Enter the name of the school associated with the event in the `school_name` field.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the `contact_phone` field.
8. If necessary, add any additional notes about the event in the `notes` field.
9. Select the type of event lead you are generating (Event, Lead, Generation) in the `event_lead_type` field.
10. Indicate the status of the lead (Interested, Not Interested, Follow up Needed) in the `lead_status` field.
11. If following up on the lead, enter the date and time for the next follow-up in the `follow_up_date` and `follow_up_time` fields, respectively.
12. Enter any next follow-up actions (Email, SMS, Phone, None, Follow up by phone, Follow up in-person) in the `next_follow_up_action` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. (Required)
* **contact_name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **event_date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **event_time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **event_location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **school_name**: Enter the name of the school associated with the event.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes about the event.
* **event_lead_type**: Select the type of event lead you are generating (Event, Lead, Generation).
* **lead_status**: Indicate the status of the lead (Interested, Not Interested, Follow up Needed).
* **follow_up_date**: If following up, enter the date for the next follow-up.
* **follow_up_time**: If following up, enter the time for the next follow-up.
* **follow_up_note**: Enter any note for the next follow-up action.
* **next_follow_up_action**: Select the next follow-up action (Email, SMS, Phone, None, Follow up by phone, Follow up in-person).
