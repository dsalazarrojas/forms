# Homeschool Cooperative Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeschool Cooperative Expression Of Interest form is designed to gather information from families interested in joining a homeschool cooperative. By completing this form, you will be able to express your family's interest in joining the co-op and provide the necessary information for the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer all required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure you have provided all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Interest** (1, text, required): This field is an opportunity for you to briefly describe your family's interest in joining the homeschool cooperative. Please share your reasons for joining and your expectations.
* **School Background** (2, text, required): In this field, please provide a brief description of your homeschooling background and experience. This will help us understand your approach to homeschooling and how it aligns with the co-op's philosophy.
* **Contact Info** (3, email, optional): If you have any questions or need more information about the co-op, please provide your email address or phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Homeschooling Method** (4, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options that best describe your homeschooling approach:
	+ Classical
	+ Charlotte Mason
	+ Literature-Based
	+ Eclectic
	+ Other (please specify)
* **School Formation** (5, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options that best describes your co-op preference:
	+ Co-op
	+ Co-op and Homeschool
	+ Homeschool
* **Family Values** (6, text, required): Describe your family's core values that guide your decision to homeschool.
* **Family Demographics** (7, number, optional): If you feel comfortable sharing, please provide your family size or other demographic information that you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Family Goals** (8, text, required): Share your family's goals for homeschooling, such as academic, social, or personal growth.
* **School Goals** (9, text, required): Describe your expectations for the co-op's academic and social goals.
* **Teacher Qualifications** (10, note, optional): If you have teaching experience or qualifications, please provide a brief description.
* **References** (11, text, optional): If you have any references from previous homeschooling experiences, please share them here.
* **Additional Comments** (12, text, optional): If you have any additional information that you would like to share with us, please use this field.
* **Co-op Fees** (13, number, required): Indicate the number of co-op fees you are willing to pay.
* **Co-op Commitment** (14, text, required): Describe your level of commitment to the co-op, including the time and effort you are willing to contribute.
* **Co-op Role** (15, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options that best describes your co-op role:
	+ Member
	+ Non-Member
* **Family Commitment** (16, text, required): Describe your family's commitment to the co-op, including the time and effort you are willing to contribute.
* **Additional Info** (17, text, optional): If you have any additional information that you would like to share with us, please use this field.
* **Submit Form** (18, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options to indicate your readiness to submit the form:
	+ I am ready to submit the form
	+ I need more information before submitting the form
