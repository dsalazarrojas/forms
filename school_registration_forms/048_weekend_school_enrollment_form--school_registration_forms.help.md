# Weekend School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the enrollment process for students in our weekend school program. It collects essential information from parents and students to register them for the program. Please note that only one form submission is allowed per student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required information for each field.
- Make sure to review the form carefully to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: A required field that collects the name of the parent or guardian who will be contacted regarding the student's enrollment.
* **Student Name**: A field where you can enter the name of the student being registered.
* **Date of Birth**: A field that requests the student's date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Contact Number**: A field to store the contact number of the parent or guardian where they can be reached for any updates or questions.
