# Pre Kindergarten Registration Program - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Kindergarten Registration Program is designed to collect essential information about a student to ensure a smooth onboarding process for the pre-kindergarten program. Please fill out the form carefully, following the provided instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting your application.
2. Fill out all the fields that apply to your situation.
3. Submit your form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Student Name)**: Please enter the student's name as per your records.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the parent's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the emergency contact information, including the contact person's name, phone, and relationship to the student.
* **Guardian Information**: Enter the guardian's name and contact information, following the same format as "Contact Information".
* **Educational Information**: Enter any relevant educational background or previous school experiences for the student.
* **School Information**: Enter information about the student's current or previous school experiences (if applicable).
* **Medical Information**: Report any relevant medical conditions or allergies that we should be aware of.
* **Student Info**: This field is for any additional student-specific information not covered by previous fields.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any relevant comments or additional information about the student's circumstances.
* **Signature**: Please sign and date this form to confirm your information is accurate.
* **Upload File**: You may upload any supporting documents for your student (e.g., birth certificate).
* **Upload File 2**: This is an optional second file upload.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email**: Enter the primary contact person's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the primary contact person's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the student's current residence or a parent's address.
