# Student Participation Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a participation certificate for a student, typically for courses or programs attended.

## How To Complete This Form
- Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm if you are requesting a participation certificate.
- Enter your full name as per your official identification documents.
- Provide your student ID if available.
- Enter your email address that is associated with your student account.
- Choose your student level of involvement (High or Low).
- Enter the course name and number (if applicable).
- Select a valid date for the certificate.
- Briefly explain the reason for your participation certificate request.
- Enter your school's name, address, city, state, and country.
- Enter the school's email address.
- Select if the student was assigned a tool for the program.
- Choose the status of your request (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request**: Confirm your participation request by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Student**: Enter your full name as per your official identification documents.
* **Student ID**: Provide your student ID, if available.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that is associated with your student account.
* **Student Level**: Choose your level of participation (High or Low).
* **Course**: Enter the name of the course you are requesting the certificate for (if applicable).
* **Certificate Date**: Select a valid date for the certificate.
* **Reason**: Briefly explain the reason for your participation certificate request.
* **School**: Enter your school's name.
* **School Address**: Enter your school's address.
* **School City**: Enter your school's city.
* **School State**: Enter your school's state.
* **School Country**: Enter your school's country.
* **School Zip**: Enter your school's zip code.
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Email**: Enter your school's email address.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select if the student was assigned a tool for the program.
* **Request Status**: Choose the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
