# Music School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music School Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in applying to the music school. This form helps us assess your suitability for the program and gather essential details about your musical interests, background, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field. This is a required field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field. This is a required field.
6. Enter your Address in the "Address" field. (Optional)
7. Enter the date you are applying to join the music school in the "Application Date" field. This is a required field.
8. Briefly describe your reason for applying to the music school in the "Application Reason" field. (Optional)
9. List your music interests in the "Music Interests" field. (Optional)
10. Describe any previous music experience you have in the "Previous Experience" field. (Optional)
11. Specify your available time for music practice in the "Available Time" field. (Optional)
12. Mention the number of days you can dedicate to music practice in the "Available Days" field. (Optional)
13. List your preferred musical instruments in the "Preferred Instruments" field. (Optional)
14. Describe your musical background and qualifications in the "Musical Background" field. (Optional)
15. Provide the name of the music school you have previously attended in the "Music School Attended" field. (Optional)
16. Provide a reference from your music teacher or mentor in the "Teacher Reference" field. (Optional)
17. Mention your musical degree or certification in the "Music Degree" field. (Optional)
18. Specify your highest level of English proficiency in the "Highest Level Of English" field. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required): A valid email address is required to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required): A working phone number is required to contact you.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, Optional): If you have a residential or postal address, please enter it here.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date you are applying to join the music school.
* **Application Reason** (`application_reason`, `text`, Optional): Briefly describe your reason for applying to the music school.
* **Music Interests** (`music_interests`, `text`, Optional): List your musical interests and areas of interest.
* **Previous Experience** (`previous_experience`, `text`, Optional): Describe any previous music experience you have.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, `text`, Optional): Specify your available time for music practice.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, `text`, Optional): Mention the number of days you can dedicate to music practice.
* **Preferred Instruments** (`preferred_instruments`, `text`, Optional): List your preferred musical instruments.
* **Musical Background** (`musical_background`, `text`, Optional): Describe your musical background and qualifications.
* **Music School Attended** (`music_school_attended`, `text`, Optional): If you have previously attended a music school, please mention it here.
* **Teacher Reference** (`teacher_reference`, `text`, Optional): Provide a reference from your music teacher or mentor.
* **Music Degree** (`music_degree`, `text`, Optional): If you have a music degree or certification, please mention it here.
* **Highest Level Of English** (`highest_level_of_english`, `text`, Optional): Specify your highest level of English proficiency.
