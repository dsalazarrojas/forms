# Online High School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to enroll in a high school program. Please fill out this form to provide essential information to complete the enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Fill out the "User Information" page with your name, student ID (if applicable), and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your current address on the "Student Address" page.
3. Enter your emergency contact information on the "Emergency Contact" page.
4. Select your preferred high school from the "School Selection" page. Make sure to choose at least one option.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can add them on the "Notes" page.
6. Enter your email address on the "Email" page (optional).
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your enrollment form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and student ID (if applicable) in this field.

### Student Address
* **Student Address** (`Student Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current address in this field.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.

### School Selection
* **School Selection** (`School Selection`, `select_multiple`, required): Select at least one of the options for your preferred high school.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your enrollment.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted by the school).

### Submit
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click this button to submit your completed form.
