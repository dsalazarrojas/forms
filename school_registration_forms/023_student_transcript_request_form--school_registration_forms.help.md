# Student Transcript Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow students to request a transcript from the school. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your student email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Enter the name of the school you attended in the "School Attended" field.
4. Enter the date of your graduation in the "Date of Graduation" field.
5. Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID Number" field.
6. Select the type of transcript request you require in the "Transcript Request" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.
8. Click the "Submit Request" button to submit your request.
9. Review the "Error Message" field for any issues with your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your student name to identify yourself as the requester.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your student email address to enable us to contact you about your transcript request.
* **School Attended** (`school_attended`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school you attended to help us identify your records.
* **Date of Graduation** (`date_of_graduation`, date, optional): Enter the date you graduated to ensure we provide the correct transcript.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, text, optional): Enter your student ID number to verify your identity.
* **Transcript Request** (`student_transcript_request`, select_one, optional): Select the type of transcript request you require:
	+ Option 1: Official Transcript
	+ Option 2: Unofficial Transcript
	+ Option 3: Duplicate Transcript
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your request.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): This button is used to confirm your submission.
* **Cancel** (`cancel_request`, text, optional): Click this button to cancel your request and return to the beginning of the form.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, text, optional): Review this field for any issues with your submission.

Note: The form will only be processed if you enter required fields and submit the form correctly.
