# Country Week Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants for the Country Week event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the Registration page (Page 1) with your basic information.
2. On the School Information page (Page 2), enter your school's details.
3. On the Country Focus page (Page 3), select the countries you are interested in participating in.
4. On the Availability page (Page 4), choose your availability for the event.
5. On the Activity page (Page 5), select the activities you are interested in participating in.
6. On the Participant Information page (Page 6), enter your personal details.
7. Review and confirm your submission on the Form Submissions page (Page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration** (Page 1, form_data):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will enter your basic information.
	+ Fill in the required details as prompted.
* **School Information** (Page 2, school_data):
	+ Enter your school's information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Country Focus** (Page 3, country_data):
	+ Select the countries you are interested in participating in the Country Week event.
* **Country Focus List 1** (Page 4, country_data_array1):
	+ Select the countries you are interested in participating in the Country Week event.
* **Country Focus List 2** (Page 5, country_data_array2):
	+ Select the countries you are interested in participating in the Country Week event.
* **Availability** (Page 6, country_availability):
	+ Choose your availability for the event, in the 24-hour time format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Activity 1** (Page 7, activity_data_array1):
	+ Select the activities you are interested in participating in.
* **Country Availability** (Page 8, country_availability_array):
	+ Choose your country availability for the event, in the 24-hour time format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Participant Information** (Page 9, participant_data):
	+ Enter your personal details, including name and contact information.
* **Participant Name** (Page 10, participant_name):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (Page 11, participant_email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (Page 12, participant_phone):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Country Focus List 1** (Page 13, country_data_array1):
	+ Select the countries you are interested in participating in the Country Week event.
* **Country Focus List 2** (Page 14, country_data_array2):
