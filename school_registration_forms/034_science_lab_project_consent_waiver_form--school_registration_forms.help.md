# Science Lab Project Consent Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal document that requires parents/guardians to grant consent for their child's participation in a science project. It ensures the school has the necessary legal clearance and that parents/guardians are aware of and agree to their child's involvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the project details and the implications of signing this document.
2.  If you have concerns or questions, use the "Parent Concerns" field to express them.
3.  If you agree with the project terms, select "I, the parent/guardian, hereby grant permission for my child to participate in this science project."
4.  If you do not agree or wish to specify other arrangements, use the "Parent Concerns" field to describe your concerns or choose "Other (please specify)" under "Waiver".
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, text, optional): A text field where you can provide any comments or concerns you have about the project.
  * If you have any questions or concerns, please describe them here.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name.
  * Enter your child's name as it appears on their ID or as you know it.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your child's email address.
  * If your child has a school email, please enter it here.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your child's phone number.
  * If your child has a phone, please enter their number here.
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  * This will be used to contact you with project updates or concerns.
- **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter your child's home address.
  * This will be used to send project updates or reminders.
- **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, text, optional): If you have any concerns, please describe them here.
  * This will help us understand and address them.
- **Waiver** (`waiver`, select_one, optional): Select one of the waiver options.
  * "I, the parent/guardian, hereby grant permission for my child to participate in this science project." or "I, the parent/guardian, do not grant permission for my child to participate in this science project."
## Tips

*   Before signing, make sure you understand the project's specifics and the implications of your decision.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the project administrators or teachers.
*   This form is for parental/guardian consent only. Please do not submit if you are not the legal parent/guardian.
*   Ensure that the project's terms align with your expectations and that you have discussed it with your child.
