# School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Registration Form is designed to collect basic information from students and parents to register them in a school. This form is used to gather details such as name, contact information, class selection, and dates of birth and start/end dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name in the **student_name** field.
2. Enter your student's email address in the **student_email** field.
3. Enter your name in the **parent_name** field.
4. Select the class your student is interested in from the **select_a_class** dropdown list.
5. Enter your student's date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field.
6. Enter the start date of the school year in the **start_date** field.
7. Enter the end date of the school year in the **end_date** field.
8. Enter your student's phone number in the **student_phone** field.
9. Enter your phone number in the **parent_phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name**: Enter the name of the student who is registering.
* **student_email**: Enter the email address of the student who is registering. This will be used as a contact point for the student.
* **parent_name**: Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **select_a_class**: Select the class that your student is interested in. This is a list of available classes in the school.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the school year, usually September 1st.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the school year, usually August 31st.
* **student_phone**: Enter your student's phone number for contact purposes.
* **parent_phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the school administration for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
