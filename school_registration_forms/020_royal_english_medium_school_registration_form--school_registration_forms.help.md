# Royal English Medium School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Royal English Medium School Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from students and their parents for the school's programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review the form fields to ensure you understand what information is required.
- Fill out each field accurately and completely.
- Submit the form once you have filled out all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information (Text)** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the student's information.
- **Student Name (Text)** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's full name.
- **Student Date of Birth (Date)** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Guardian Name (Text)** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the guardian's full name.
- **Guardian Date of Birth (Date)** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the guardian's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Student Email (Email)** (`6`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
- **Parent Phone (Text)** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
- **Student Gender (Select One)** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's gender (Male or Female).
- **Student Grade (Number)** (`9`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the student's grade level.
- **Guardian Grade (Number)** (`10`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the guardian's grade level.
- **Student English Medium (Select Multiple)** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the student's preferred English medium (English Medium 1, English Medium 2, English Medium 3).
- **Parent English Medium (Select Multiple)** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the parent's preferred English medium (English Medium 1, English Medium 2, English Medium 3).
- **Student English Medium Other (Text)** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any other English medium preference (e.g. bilingual).
- **Student English Medium Other Description (Text)** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a description of the other English medium preference.
- **School Name (Text)** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
- **Student Start Date (Date)** (`16`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the student's start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Student End Date (Date)** (`17`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the student's end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Student Fee (Number)** (`18`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the student's fee for the program.
- **Fee Term (Number)** (`19`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the fee term for the program.
- **Student Fee Note (Text)** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes about the student's fee.
- **Parent Note (Text)** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes about the parent.
- **School Address (Text)** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the school.
- **School Zip (Text)** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the school.
- **School Phone (Text)** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the school.
- **School Email (Email)** (`25`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the school.
