# School Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a school course registration process for internal administration and external student registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your school name.
2. Fill in your school course name.
3. Select the class you are registering for.
4. Select the lessons you are registering for.
5. Enter the registration date.
6. Enter your student ID number.
7. Select your student grade level.
8. Enter your course description.
9. Enter your course ID number.
10. Enter your school ID number.
11. Enter your school name.
12. Enter your school email address.
13. Enter your school phone number.
14. Enter your school address.
15. Enter your instructor's name.
16. Enter your instructor's title.
17. Enter the registration fee.
18. Select your payment method.
19. Enter the details of your payment method.
20. Enter your school's website URL.
21. Enter your school's Facebook URL.
22. Enter your school's Twitter URL.
23. Enter your school's Instagram URL.
24. Enter your school's description.
