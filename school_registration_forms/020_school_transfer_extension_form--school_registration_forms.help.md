# School Transfer Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The School Transfer Extension Form is used to update or extend the school transfer process for a student who is currently in school or has been in school in the past. This form collects additional information that is necessary to complete the student's transfer to their new school.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and complete information about the student, including their name, student ID, email, and guardian contact information.
2. Indicate the student's current status, including whether they are currently in school or not.
3. Enter the school information, including the school name, location, and contact phone number.
4. Explain the reason for the transfer and any additional comments or details.
5. Enter the date of the transfer and any additional comments or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number or student number.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Guardian Data** (`guardian_data`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter the guardian's or parent's name.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guardian's or parent's phone number.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the student's current status, which can be 'Student in School', 'Student not in School', or 'Other (specify)'.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the school the student is currently attending.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the student is currently attending.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the school the student is currently attending.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the school the student is currently attending.
* **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the student's transfer.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
* **School Name Extension** (`school_name_extension`, text, optional): Enter an extension to the school name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, optional): Enter the student's age.
* **Reason for Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the transfer extension.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations.

Note: All fields are optional, but please provide as much information as possible to complete the student's transfer process.
