# School Trip Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Attendance Form is a tool for schools to monitor student participation in organized trips. By completing this form, you will be able to quickly track student demographics, trip details, and attendance information.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant fields to complete the form, depending on your needs.
2. Enter the required information in each field.
3. Save your submission when you have finished filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Details** (`Trip Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the trip, the date it took place, and any relevant details about the trip.
* **Student Info** (`Student Info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the student's name, grade level, and any other relevant information.
* **Student Guardian** (`Student Guardian`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the student's guardian.
* **Parent Permission** (`Parent Permission`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm that the parent has given permission for the student to attend the trip.
* **Teacher Info** (`Teacher Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information as the teacher responsible for the trip.
* **Trip Dates** (`Trip Dates`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the start and end dates of the trip.
* **Student Attendance** (`Student Attendance`, `text`, required/optional): Track whether the student attended the trip, and provide any comments about their absence.
* **Trip Status** (`Trip Status`, `text`, required/optional): Update the trip status to reflect the attendance and any other relevant updates.
 

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate tracking of student participation.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your school's policies and procedures for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, and the data collected will be used to support school decision-making and reporting.
