# School Registration Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The School Registration Information Form is designed to gather essential details from students and teachers for the purpose of school administration. This form is used to collect information about students and teachers, including their names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, grades, and birth dates.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out this form, select the applicable option from the dropdown menu for the first field "School Registration Form".
2.  Fill in your student's name, email, and address in the respective text fields.
3.  Choose your student's grade from the available options in the "Student Grade" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter your student's birth date in the format 'month/day/year' in the "Student Birthdate" field.
5.  Provide your teacher's name, email, and contact phone number in the respective text fields.
6.  Ensure all required fields are filled before submission as they are marked as "True" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School Registration Form**: Select "Yes" to indicate that the student is registering for the school.
*   **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name.
*   **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
*   **Student Address**: Enter the student's home or mailing address.
*   **Student Grade**: Choose the student's grade level from the available options (1st, 2nd, 3rd).
*   **Student Birthdate**: Enter the student's birth date in the format 'month/day/year'.
*   **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
*   **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
*   **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's contact phone number.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid errors in the form submission.
*   Use a consistent format for dates and phone numbers for easy reading and processing.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above or contact the school administration for assistance.
