> # Class Enrollment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Class Enrollment Request Form is designed to gather information from students who wish to enroll in a class or program at our institution. Please complete this form to provide us with essential details about your identity, contact information, enrollment preferences, and academic background. This information will help us understand your needs and prepare you for your academic journey.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you would on any standard form.
2. Select the relevant options for fields with dropdown lists (e.g., gender, enrollment type, grade level).
3. Enter your accurate and complete information in the text fields (e.g., full name, email address, mailing address).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (<name>: <full_name>, <type>: text, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
* **Date of Birth** (<name>: date, required): Provide your birthdate in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (<name>: select_one, required): Choose your gender identity:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-binary
	+ Prefer to self-describe
* **Prior Student ID Number** (<name>: text, optional): If you have a prior student ID number, enter it here. If not, leave it blank.
* **Email Address** (<name>: email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (<name>: text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Mailing Address** (<name>: text, required): Enter your complete mailing address.
* **City** (<name>: text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (<name>: text, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Postal Code** (<name>: text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Type of Enrollment** (<name>: select_one, required): Choose your enrollment type:
	+ Full-time student
	+ Part-time student
	+ Audit
	+ Certificate program
	+ Other
* **Requested Class or Program** (<name>: text, required): Enter the specific class or program you wish to enroll in.
* **Current Grade Level** (<name>: select_one, required): Choose your current grade level:
	+ Elementary
	+ Middle School
	+ High School
	+ College
	+ Post-Graduate
	+ Adult Learner
	+ Other
* **Semester or Term Requested** (<name>: select_one, required): Choose the semester or term you wish to enroll in:
	+ Fall
	+ Spring
	+ Summer
	+ Winter
	+ Other
* **Current School or Institution** (<name>: text, required): Enter the name of your current school or institution.
* **Current GPA** (<name>: number, optional): Enter your current GPA on the 4.0 scale, if applicable.
* **Standardized Test Scores** (<name>: text, optional): Provide any relevant standardized test scores, such as SAT, ACT, or other test scores.
* **Academic Strengths and Interests** (<name>: text, optional): Briefly describe your academic strengths and interests.
* **Any Special Educational Needs** (<name>: text, optional): Describe any special educational needs you may have.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (<name>: text, required): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email Address** (<name>: email, required): Enter a valid email address for your parent or guardian.
* **I Certify** (<name>: select_one, required): Acknowledge that all information provided is accurate and complete:
	+ Yes, I certify
	+ False

Note: Please ensure you complete all required fields and provide accurate information.
