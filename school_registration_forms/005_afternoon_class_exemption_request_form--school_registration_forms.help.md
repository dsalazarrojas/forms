# Afternoon Class Exemption Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Afternoon Class Exemption Request Form is used to submit a formal request for exemption from an afternoon class. This form helps students or their parents/guardians to communicate the reason for the exemption request to the school administration.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your full name as a student in the **Student name** field.
2.  Provide your student ID number in the **Student ID number** field.
3.  Enter the name of the class from which you are requesting exemption in the **Class name** field.
4.  Clearly explain the reason for your exemption request in the **Reason for exemption** field.
5.  If applicable, provide a description of any supporting documents you would like to attach or submit in the **Supporting documents description** field.
6.  Enter a phone number for the parent or guardian that can be reached for follow-up in the **Parent or guardian contact number** field.
7.  Select your preferred outcome from the options: **Excused absence**, **Alternate assignment**, or **Deferred decision**.
8.  Enter the date you are submitting this request in the **Date submitted** field.
9.  If desired, write any additional administrative notes that the school can use for processing the request in the **Administrator notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student name**: Your full name as a student.
*   **Student ID number**: Your unique student identification number issued by the school.
*   **Class name**: The name of the class for which you are requesting exemption.
*   **Reason for exemption**: A clear and concise explanation of the reason for your exemption request.
*   **Supporting documents description**: A description of any documents attached or to be provided as evidence for your request (optional).
*   **Parent or guardian contact number**: A phone number for a parent or guardian that can be reached for follow-up.
*   **Preferred outcome**: Your preferred outcome from the options: **Excused absence**, **Alternate assignment**, or **Deferred decision**.
*   **Date submitted**: The date you are submitting this exemption request.
*   **Administrator notes**: Any additional administrative notes for the school to process the request (optional).
