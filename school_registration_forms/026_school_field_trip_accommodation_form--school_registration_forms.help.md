# School Field Trip Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Field Trip Accommodation Form is designed to help school staff plan and manage field trips by providing a structured format to collect essential information about the trip, including details about the accommodation, contact persons, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the field trip in the "Trip Name" field.
2. Choose a date for the field trip in the "Trip Date" field.
3. Select the arrival time in the "Arrival Time" field.
4. Select the departure time in the "Departure Time" field.
5. Choose the type of accommodation in the "Accommodation Type" field (e.g., School Accommodation, Hotel, Other).
6. Enter the number of students attending the field trip in the "Student Count" field.
7. Enter the address of the accommodation in the "Accommodation Address" field.
8. Enter the name and contact number of the contact person in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Number" fields.
9. Enter the email address and phone number of the school in the "School Email" and "School Phone" fields.
10. Enter the name of the accommodation provider in the "Accommodation Provider" field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the trip in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Name**: This field allows you to enter a name for the field trip. It can be a short description of the trip.
* **Trip Date**: This field is used to select a date for the field trip.
* **Arrival Time**: This field is used to select the time of arrival for the field trip.
* **Departure Time**: This field is used to select the time of departure for the field trip.
* **Accommodation Type**: This field allows you to choose the type of accommodation for the field trip (e.g., School Accommodation, Hotel, Other).
* **Student Count**: This field is used to enter the number of students attending the field trip.
* **Accommodation Address**: This field is used to enter the address of the accommodation.
* **Contact Person**: This field is used to enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number**: This field is used to enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **School Email**: This field is used to enter the email address of the school.
* **School Phone**: This field is used to enter the phone number of the school.
* **Accommodation Provider**: This field is used to enter the name of the accommodation provider.
* **Notes**: This field is used to enter any additional notes about the trip.
