# School Bus Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Bus Registration Form is designed to collect information about school bus trips, including trip details, bus specifications, and student information. This form is essential for organizing and managing school bus trips efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for the school bus trip from the drop-down menu in the "School Bus Trip ID" field.
2. Fill in the required fields: "Student Name", "Student Email", "Student Phone", "School Name", "School Bus Type", "Bus Number", "Bus Capacity", "Student Count", "Trip Date", "Trip Time", "Bus Status", "Trip Status", "School ID", "School District ID", "Route ID", "School Address", "School City", "School State", "School Zip", and "School Phone".
3. For "Student Name 2", "Student Email 2", "Student Phone 2", and other optional fields, you can enter any additional student information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Bus Trip ID** (`school_bus_trip_id`, select_one): Select the relevant school bus trip from the available options.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text): Enter the name of the student traveling on the bus. This is a required field.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email): Enter the email address of the student. This is a required field.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the student. This is a required field.
* **Notes** (`student_notes`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the student. This is an optional field.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text): Enter the name of the school. This is a required field.
* **Bus Type** (`bus_type`, select_one): Choose the type of bus being used for the trip (e.g., school bus, charter bus, etc.).
* **Bus Number** (`bus_number`, number): Enter the bus number for easy identification.
* **Bus Capacity** (`bus_capacity`, number): Enter the maximum number of students that can fit on the bus.
* **Student Count** (`student_count`, number): Enter the total number of students registered for the trip.
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, date): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Trip Time** (`trip_time`, time): Enter the scheduled time of the trip.
* **Bus Status** (`bus_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the bus (e.g., available, booked, cancelled, etc.).
* **Trip Status** (`trip_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the trip (e.g., active, cancelled, completed, etc.).
* **School ID** (`school_id`, text): Enter the school's identification number.
* **School District ID** (`school_district_id`, text): Enter the school district's identification number.
* **Route ID** (`route_id`, text): Enter the route identification number.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text): Enter the address of the school.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text): Enter the city of the school's location.
* **School State** (`school_state`, text): Enter the state of the school's location.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, text): Enter the zip code of the school's location.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the school.
* **Student Name 2** (`student_name2`, text): Enter the name of the second student (if any).
* **Student Email 2** (`student_email2`, email): Enter the email address of the second student (if any).
* **Student Phone 2** (`student_phone2`, text): Enter the phone number of the second student (if any).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid submission errors.
- Double-check the information for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school bus administration for assistance.
