# New Student Registration Packet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new student registration packet is a form used to gather information for students registering at a school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, you will need to fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form. This will help ensure that the student's details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter the student's unique identifier assigned by the school.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification or government ID.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`): Select the grade level the student is currently in or expected to be in.
- **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Student Gender** (`student_gender`): Select the student's gender.
- **Student Address** (`student_address`): Enter the student's current home address.
- **Parent First Name** (`student_parent_first_name`): Enter your first name as a parent or guardian.
- **Parent Last Name** (`student_parent_last_name`): Enter your last name as a parent or guardian.
- **Contact Information** (`student_info`): Enter any relevant contact information for the student, such as phone numbers or email.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and contact information for the person responsible for the student's safety and well-being in case of an emergency.
- **Parent Information** (`parent_info`): Enter any relevant contact information for yourself as a parent or guardian.
- **Student Program** (`student_program`): Select the student's program or course of study.
- **Student Education** (`student_education`): Enter any relevant medical or educational information about the student.
- **Student Medical Information** (`student_medical`): Enter any medical conditions or allergies the student may have.
- **Student Allergies** (`student_allergies`): Enter any allergies the student may have.
- **Student Immunizations** (`student_immunizations`): Enter any immunization records or details for the student.
- **Student Special Needs** (`student_special_needs`): Enter any special needs or requirements the student may have.
- **Student Medication** (`student_medication`): Enter any medication the student is taking.
- **Student Medication Administration** (`student_medication_administration`): Enter any instructions or details for administering medication to the student.
- **Student Medication Authorization** (`student_medication_authorization`): Enter any authorization or consent for administering medication to the student.
- **Student Medication Refills** (`student_medication_refills`): Enter any medication refills the student may need.
- **Student Medication Notes** (`student_medication_notes`): Enter any additional notes or details for the student's medication.
- **School Information** (`school_information`): Enter any information about the school the student is registering to, such as the school name or location.
