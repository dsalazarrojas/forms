# Playschool Trial Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information about the child and the parent/guardian visiting the school during the trial period. The information collected will help the school staff to better understand the child's needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
3. For fields that require a number or date, use the correct format as specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Information** (`child_info`): Enter the child's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`): Enter the parent/guardian's name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Consent Details** (`consent_details`): Enter any additional consent details as required.
* **Trial Visit Date** (`trial_visit_date`): Enter the date of the trial visit using the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`): Enter the time of the trial visit using the correct format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Time Duration** (`time_duration`): Enter the duration of the trial visit.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter any additional contact details as required.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the address where the child can be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the parent/guardian.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the parent/guardian.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`): Enter the child's name.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter the child's age.
* **Emergency Relation** (`emergency_relation`): Select the emergency contact relation (Mother, Father, or Guardian).
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`): Enter any additional consent details as required.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes as required.
* **Notes** (`notes2`): Enter any additional notes as required.
* **Trial Type** (`trial_type`): Select the type of trial (Trial or Registration).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select the payment status (Paid or Unpaid).
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`): Enter the amount paid.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Check the correct format for fields that require a number or date.
* Use clear and concise language when providing additional notes or consent details.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
