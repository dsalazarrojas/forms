## Registration Adjustment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Registration Adjustment Form is used by school registrants to make adjustments to their existing registration information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field (1).
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field (2).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (3).
4. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field (4).
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field (5).
6. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field (6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Info (4)**: Provide any additional contact information that you feel is relevant to your registration.
* **Address (5)**: Enter your physical address where you can be reached.
* **School Name (6)**: Enter the name of the school you are registered to attend.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the school administration for assistance.
