# Music School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music School Registration Form is a standard form used to collect information from potential students or their parents/guardians for the purpose of registering them in a music school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your student's age in the "Student Age" field.
3. Enter your student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
4. Indicate your preferred class days in the "Preferred Class Days" field. You can select multiple days.
5. Enter your student's start time for lessons in the "Start Time" field.
6. Fill in your name in the "Parent Name" field (if applicable).
7. Enter your email address in the "Parent Email" field (if applicable).
8. Indicate if you are a member of our music school in the "Are you a member of our music school?" field.
9. Check if you have experience in music school in the "Do you have experience in music school?" field.
10. Select how many music lessons you want to take in the "How many music lessons do you want to take?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (Student Name): A field to enter the student's name.
  * This field is a text field where you can enter your student's name.
  * Please ensure that the name you enter is accurate and complete.
- **Student Age** (Student Age): A field to enter the student's age.
  * This field is a numeric field where you can enter your student's age.
  * Please ensure that the age you enter is accurate.
- **Student Email** (Student Email): A field to enter your student's email address.
  * This field is an email field where you can enter your student's email address.
  * Please ensure that the email address you enter is accurate and active.
- **Preferred Class Days** (Preferred Class Days):
  * This field is a multiple-select field where you can select multiple days for your preferred class schedule.
  * Select the days of the week you prefer for your music lessons.
  * You can select multiple days.
- **Start Time** (Start Time): A field to enter your start time for lessons.
  * This field is a time field where you can enter your start time.
  * Please ensure that the time you enter is in a format that can be easily understood (e.g., 08:00, 13:00, etc.).
- **Parent Name** (Parent Name): A field to enter your name.
  * This field is a text field where you can enter your name.
  * Please fill this in if you are a parent or guardian.
- **Parent Email** (Parent Email): A field to enter your email address.
  * This field is an email field where you can enter your email address.
  * Please fill this in if you are a parent or guardian.
- **Are you a member of our music school?** (Are you a member of our music school?):
  * This field is a select-one field where you can choose between "Member" or "Not member".
  * Please select the correct option according to your status.
- **Do you have experience in music school?** (Do you have experience in music school?):
  * This field is a select-one field where you can choose between "True" and "False".
  * Please select the correct option according to your experience.
- **How many music lessons do you want to take?** (How many music lessons do you want to take?):
  * This field is a select-one field where you can choose between options.
  * Select the number of lessons you want to take according to your preference.
