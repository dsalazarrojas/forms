# Preschool Initial Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Initial Application Form is a necessary step in the enrollment process for children applying to a preschool program. It gathers essential information about the child and their parents/guardians to ensure a smooth and safe experience for the child during their time at the preschool.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose before proceeding.
- Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, as incomplete or incorrect information may lead to delays or complications in the enrollment process.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can ask the preschool staff or administration for clarification.
- Please note that you're responsible for the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`Application Form`, `number`, `required`): This field is not applicable in the traditional sense but is used for the form identifier. Please ignore it.
* **Child Name** (`Child Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the child's full name as it appears on their birth certificate or passport.
* **Parents Name** (`Parents Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the parent's/guardian's full name(s) as it appears on government-issued identification.
* **Parents Contact** (`Parents Contact`, `text`, `required`): Enter a contact number or email address of the parent/guardian that the preschool can use for communication.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter the child's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Preschool Address** (`Preschool Address`, `text`, `required`): Enter the address of the location you wish to send the child to for preschool.
* **Parent Signature** (`Parent Signature`, `note`, `required`): Sign your name as a parent/guardian to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Application Date** (`Application Date`, `time`, `required`): Enter the date and time you're submitting the application.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name and contact information of another person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `text`, `required`): List any medical conditions that the child has or is susceptible to.
* **Special Needs** (`Special Needs`, `text`, `required`): List any special needs that the child may have, such as a physical disability or a learning requirement.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, `required`): List any allergies that the child has.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, `text`, `required`): List any medications that the child is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, `required`): Provide a brief summary of the child's medical history.
* **Medical Provider** (`Medical Provider`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name and contact information of the child's medical provider.
* **Immunizations** (`Immunizations`, `text`, `required`): List any immunizations that the child has received.
