# School Registration Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Registration Renewal Form is used to collect and update information from students, parents, and guardians for school registration purposes. This form helps the school administration to keep track of student data, ensuring accuracy and completeness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to check the box next to each field to see if it's required or not.
3. Select the relevant options for the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
4. Upload any supporting documents as necessary.
5. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

* **Contact (5)** (`contact`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your contact information here.

* **School Registration Renewal Form (10)** (`school_registration_renewal_form`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in this field with the form title.
* **Notes (11)** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Please add any additional comments or notes here.
