# Committee Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Committee Preference Form is designed to help employees express their interest and availability for serving on various committees. It's a self-service form that allows employees to provide information about their preferences, skills, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what options are available.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, but only to the best of your abilities.
3. Select the options that best fit your preferences and experiences.
4. Don't hesitate to ask for help or clarification if you're unsure about any part of the form.
5. Once you've completed the form, submit it for review and consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preference Information** (`preference_information`, note, not required): This is a space for you to briefly introduce yourself and share any relevant information about your interests and experiences.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, not required): If you have an employee ID number, please provide it for reference purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Please select the department you work in from the list provided.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Please provide your current job title.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, select_one, required): Please select how long you've worked at the organization.
* **Committee Interest** (`committee_interest`, select_one, required): Please indicate your level of interest in serving on a committee.
* **Preferred Committees** (`preferred_committees`, select_multiple, not required): Please select the committees that interest you from the list provided.
* **Primary Interest** (`primary_interest`, select_one, not required): If you had to choose one committee, which one would it be?
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Please select the days and times you're available for meetings.
* **Meeting Frequency Preference** (`meeting_frequency_preference`, select_one, required): Please select how often you'd like to meet.
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, select_one, required): Please select how much time you can dedicate monthly to committee work.
* **Skills and Expertise** (`skills_expertise`, select_multiple, not required): Please select the skills or expertise you bring to the table.
* **Previous Experience** (`previous_experience`, select_one, required): Please indicate if you have previous experience serving on committees.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, not required): If you have any specific reasons for serving on a committee, please share them.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, not required): If you have any specific goals for serving on a committee, please share them.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, text, not required): If you have any concerns or doubts about serving on a committee, please share them.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, required): Please indicate if your supervisor has approved your participation.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, not required): If you have a supervisor, please provide their name.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): If you have any other information to share, please use this field.
