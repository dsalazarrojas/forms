# Preschool Admission Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Admission Contact Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians of children interested in enrolling in the preschool program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are submitting the form on behalf of the correct child.
2. Fill in your name and contact information (Parent Name, Email, and Parent Phone).
3. Choose your preferred language for communication (English, Spanish, French, or German).
4. Enter the child's date of birth (DOB).
5. Write a brief note about the child's school interest(s).
6. Provide any relevant message or information for the school staff.
7. If applicable, select the school staff member(s) you would like to contact (Teacher, Counselor, Administrator, or Other).
8. Enter the school's address (if different from your home address).
9. Provide the school's contact number (if different from your home number).
10. Review and submit the form to complete the enrollment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child. This will help us associate your contact information with the correct child.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the preschool program and any updates.
* **Parent Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can reach you for any further communication or questions.
* **Preferred Language**: Please select your preferred language for communication, such as English, Spanish, French, or German. This will help us tailor our communication to your needs.
* **Child's DOB**: Enter the child's date of birth to help us verify their age and eligibility for the preschool program.
* **Child's School Interest**: Write a brief note about the child's interests or strengths in school. This will help us understand their learning needs and preferences.
* **Parent Message**: Use this field to provide any relevant message or information about your child, such as special requirements or allergies.
* **School Staff**: Select the school staff members you would like to contact (if applicable). Please choose from Teacher, Counselor, Administrator, or Other.
* **School Address**: If your school's address is different from your home address, please provide it so we can ensure accurate communication.
* **School Contact Number**: If your school's contact number is different from your home number, please provide it for further communication.
* **Parent Message**: Provide any other relevant information or message you would like to share with the school staff.
* **School Staff**: Please select the school staff member(s) you would like to contact, such as Teacher, Counselor, Administrator, or Other.
