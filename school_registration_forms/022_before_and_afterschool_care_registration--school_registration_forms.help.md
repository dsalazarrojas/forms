# Before And Afterschool Care Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians to register their child for before and after-school care. The form asks for personal details, medical history, and emergency contact information to ensure the well-being and safety of the student. It also collects schedule and pickup information to plan for the child's care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's personal details:
	* Student Name: Enter the student's full legal name.
	* Date of Birth: Enter the student's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
	* Age: Enter the student's current age.
	* Gender: Select the student's gender.
2. Provide contact information:
	* Student Address: Enter the student's full street address.
	* School Name: Enter the school the student attends.
3. Add guardian information:
	* Guardian Name: Enter the full legal name of the primary guardian.
	* Guardian Relationship: Select the relationship of the guardian to the student (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent, etc.).
	* Guardian Email: Enter the primary contact email of the guardian.
	* Guardian Phone: Enter the best number to reach the guardian.
4. Enter additional guardian information (if applicable):
	* Second Guardian Name: If applicable, enter the full legal name of the second guardian.
	* Second Guardian Phone: If applicable, enter the phone number of the second guardian.
5. Provide medical and emergency contact information:
	* Physician Name: Enter the name of the student's doctor.
	* Physician Phone: Enter the doctor's phone number.
	* Emergency Contact 1: Enter the name and relationship of the first emergency contact.
	* Emergency Contact 2: Enter the name and relationship of the second emergency contact.
6. Medical information:
	* Medical Insurance: If applicable, enter the student's medical insurance provider and policy number.
	* Health History: List any significant health history of the student.
	* Current Medications: List all current medications with dosages.
	* Allergies: List any food, medication, or environmental allergies.
	* Dietary Restrictions: List any food restrictions or dietary needs.
7. Schedule and pickup information:
	* Transportation Plan: Describe how the student will get to and from the program.
	* Authorized Pickup List: List all people authorized to pick up the student.
8. Select schedule and start date:
	* Program Schedule: Select the days and times the student will attend the program.
	* Start Date: Enter the date the student will begin attending the program.
9. How did you hear about us:
	* How Did You Hear About Us: Select how the guardian found our program.
10. Accept terms and conditions:
	* Terms and Conditions: Accept or decline the program's terms and conditions.
11. Guardian signature:
	* Guardian Signature: Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I agree as parent or guardian."
