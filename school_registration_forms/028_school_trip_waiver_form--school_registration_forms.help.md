# School Trip Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Waiver Form is designed to gather essential information from parents regarding their child's participation in a school trip. This form is used to obtain waiver and consent from parents for their child to attend school trips.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read all the fields carefully and make sure you understand what information is required for each field.
- Complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Make sure to check the boxes for any applicable parent consent options.
- Review your completed form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Trip Date (Optional)**: This field is used to record the date of the school trip. Although not required, please provide the correct date to ensure accurate records.
- **Trip Length (Optional)**: This field is used to record the length of the school trip. Enter the number of days or hours the trip will last.
- **School Name**: Enter the name of your child's school, which will be participating in the trip.
- **Parent Consent (Required)**: Select all applicable parent consents by checking the boxes next to Parent A, Parent B, and Parent C. Please note that this field is required to ensure we have necessary permissions from all parents involved.
