# Graduation Application Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to declare their intention to graduate and provide essential details about their graduation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the correct academic program from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter your email address and phone number correctly.
4. Select your student status and any other relevant details.
5. Add any additional comments if necessary.
6. Choose the graduation ceremony date and time if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **expected_graduation_date (Date, Optional)**: Select the expected date of your graduation, following the format dd-mm-yyyy.
* **academic_program (Select One, Optional)**: Choose the academic program you are enrolled in.
* **email (Email, Optional)**: Enter your active and verifiable email address.
* **telephone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your active phone number.
* **student_id (Number, Optional)**: Enter your student ID number as per your institution's guidelines.
* **student_status (Select One, Optional)**: Choose your current student status (e.g., Active, Graduating, etc.).
* **student_status_2 (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select any additional student status relevant to your situation.
* **student_status_3 (Select One, Optional)**: Choose another student status (if applicable).
* **comments (Note, Optional)**: Add any additional comments or information about your graduation.
* **graduation_ceremony_date (Date, Optional)**: Select the date of your graduation ceremony, following the format dd-mm-yyyy.
* **graduation_ceremony_time (Time, Optional)**: Choose the time of your graduation ceremony (if applicable).
* **ceremony_location (Text, Optional)**: Enter the location of your graduation ceremony.
