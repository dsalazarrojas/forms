# High School Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The High School Enrollment Form is a collection of student and parent/guardian information that the school needs to verify during the enrollment process. Filling out this form will help the school administration to process your enrollment request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student information sections:
	* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student enrolling in the school.
	* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the student.
	* **Student Address**: Enter the permanent address of the student.
	* **Student Phone**: Enter the phone number of the student.
	* **Email**: Enter the email of the student.
2. Select the student's grade level:
	* Select the grade level of the student (9th, 10th, 11th, 12th) from the dropdown menu.
	* If the student is not in one of the listed grade levels, select "Other" and enter the grade level in the "Grade Level Other" field.
3. Enter the enrollment date:
	* Enter the date when the student is enrolling in the school.
4. Add any additional notes:
	* Use this field to provide any additional information that the student and parent/guardian want to share with the school administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info**: Enter the student's basic information such as name, address, phone, and email.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Name Other**: Enter any other names that the student goes by.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Name Other**: Enter any other names that the parent or guardian goes by.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Student Address**: Enter the permanent address of the student.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the phone number of the student.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the grade level of the student.
* **Student Grade Level Other**: Enter any other grade level not listed in the dropdown menu.
* **Enrollment Date**: Enter the date when the student is enrolling in the school.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter a unique ID number for the student (if applicable).
* **Student ID Number Other**: Enter any other ID number not provided above.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information that the student and parent/guardian want to share with the school administration.
