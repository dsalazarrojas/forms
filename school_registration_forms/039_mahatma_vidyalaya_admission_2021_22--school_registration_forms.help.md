# mahatma-vidyalaya-admission-2021-22 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from students applying to Mahatma Vidyalaya for the 2021-22 academic year.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure that you provide your contact information (Student Name, Student Email, Parent Name, and Contact Number) to enable us to reach out to you for any updates or queries.
- Enter your student's academic background information (Standard 10th Std. Roll Number and Standard 10th Std. Year of Passing) to help us track your progress.
- Provide your student's Date of Birth to verify their age and eligibility for admission.
- Enter the name of the school from which you are applying.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your parent/guardian.
- **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `dd/mm/yyyy`.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact number to enable us to reach out to you.
- **Name of School** (`school_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the school from which you are applying.
- **Standard 10th Std. Roll Number** (`standard_10th_std_roll_number`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your 10th standard roll number.
- **Standard 10th Std. Year of Passing** (`standard_10th_std_year_of_passing`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the year in which you passed your 10th standard.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and in its entirety to avoid any delays in the registration process.
- If you have any queries, please contact us and we will be happy to assist.
