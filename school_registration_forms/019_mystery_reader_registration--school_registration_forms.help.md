# Mystery Reader Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mystery Reader Registration form is for registering participants in a mystery reading event or activity. This form helps us gather information about your availability, preferences, and other relevant details to ensure we can better accommodate your needs during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mystery Reader Registration form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (first name, last name, email, phone number, address, city, state, zip, and country).
2. Indicate your availability for the event by selecting the correct options from the "availability" and "time" fields.
3. Select your reading preferences from the "reading preferences" field.
4. Choose your school affiliation, if any, by selecting from the "school" field.
5. Indicate your grade level or departmental affiliation by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "grade" and "department" fields.
6. Indicate your role or position at the school by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "role" field.
7. Finally, submit your registration by filling in your name and confirming you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_1**: Please fill in your name (e.g., John Doe).
* **volunteer_details**: Indicate if you are a volunteer (Yes/No).
* **volunteer_details_2**: Indicate if you are a volunteer again (Yes/No).
* **volunteer_details_3**: Repeat the question from above to ensure clarity (Yes/No).
* **availability**: Select the dates and times you are available for the event.
* **time**: Choose a time that works for you during the selected dates.
* **reading_preferences**: Choose the reading preferences that suit your needs (Yes/No, multiple selections allowed).
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **first_name**: Fill in your first name.
* **last_name**: Fill in your last name.
* **address**: Enter your address.
* **city**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **state**: Enter your state/region.
* **zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes about your registration.
* **agree**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions (Yes/No).
* **terms**: Fill in the terms and conditions of your registration.
* **school**: Choose the school(s) you are affiliated with (Yes/No, multiple selections allowed).
* **grade**: Indicate if you have a grade level affiliation (Yes/No).
* **department**: Fill in your department affiliation (e.g., English, Science, etc.).
* **role**: Indicate your role or position at the school (Yes/No).
* **submit**: Confirm your registration by filling in your name and agreeing to the terms and conditions.
* **submit_2**: (This field is likely redundant and can be removed or modified.)
