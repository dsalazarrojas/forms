# Class 3 Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from parents or guardians for their child to participate in school activities, including field trips, classroom parties, and special projects. It's a critical document that requires careful completion to ensure the child's safety and well-being.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's full name as per the school records.
2. Select the student's grade level from the given options.
3. Enter your name as the teacher.
4. Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
5. Select your relationship to the child from the given options.
6. Provide your email address for school communications.
7. Enter your phone number as the best contact method.
8. Enter your home address, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.
9. Select the type of activity the child will be participating in.
10. Provide a description of the activity.
11. Enter the date of the activity.
12. If applicable, enter the location of the activity.
13. Choose the method of transportation for the child.
14. If the child has any medical conditions, select 'True' and provide further details.
15. Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
16. Select 'Yes, I give consent' or 'No, I do not give consent' for the child's participation.
17. Choose whether to allow photos and videos for school records and publications.
18. If different from the parent, enter the name of the person authorized to pick up the child.
19. Provide any special instructions or notes for teachers or chaperones.
20. Finally, sign the form by your signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Full Name**: Enter the child's full name as per school records.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level the child is currently in.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Relationship to Student**: Select your relationship to the child from the given options.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address for school communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number as the best contact method.
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Type of Activity**: Select the type of activity the child will be participating in.
* **Activity Name or Description**: Provide a description of the activity.
* **Activity Date**: Enter the date of the activity.
* **Activity Location**: If applicable, enter the location of the activity.
* **Transportation Method**: Choose the method of transportation for the child.
* **Does Your Child Have Any Medical Conditions**: Select 'True' if the child has any medical conditions, and provide further details if needed.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Do You Give Consent for Your Child to Participate**: Select 'Yes, I give consent' or 'No, I do not give consent'.
* **Photo and Video Consent**: Choose whether to allow photos and videos for school records and publications.
* **Who Is Authorized to Pick Up Your Child**: If different from the parent, enter the name of the person authorized to pick up the child.
* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Provide any special instructions or notes for teachers or chaperones.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign the form by your signature, confirming consent.
* **Date Signed**: Date the form with the current date.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you have any concerns or questions, reach out to the school administration or teachers.
* Remember that this form requires your signature, confirming consent for your child's participation.
