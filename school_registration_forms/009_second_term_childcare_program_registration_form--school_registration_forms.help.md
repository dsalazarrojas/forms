# Second Term Childcare Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your child for the Second Term Childcare Program. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your child's registration is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Date of Birth
	* Contact Number
	* Email
	* Address
	* School Name
	* Program Name
	* Program Grade Level
	* Program Start Date
	* Program End Date

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number, in case the program needs to reach you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address, in case the program needs to reach you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address, to ensure the program can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's school name, to verify their affiliation.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the program name you are registering for (Second Term Childcare Program).
* **Program Grade Level** (`program_grade_level`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's grade level in the program.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the program.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, contact the program administrator.
* Ensure all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
