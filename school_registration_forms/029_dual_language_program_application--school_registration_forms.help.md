# Dual Language Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for parents or guardians of students to provide information about their child's application to the Dual Language Program.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has multiple sections, each with specific fields to fill out. Please review each section carefully to ensure you provide all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (page 1):
	+ This section is for parents or guardians to provide information about themselves.
* **Student Information** (page 2):
	+ This section is for parents or guardians to provide information about the student applying to the program.
	+ Please ensure that you fill out this section with the correct information about your child.
* **Contact Information** (page 3):
	+ This section is for parents or guardians to provide contact information for themselves and the student.
* **Program Information** (page 4):
	+ This section is for parents or guardians to provide information about the program they are applying for.
* **Family Information** (page 5):
	+ This section is for parents or guardians to provide information about their family and its relationship to the student.
* **Additional Information** (page 6):
	+ This section is for parents or guardians to provide any additional information that may be relevant to the application.
* **Signature** (page 7):
	+ This section is for parents or guardians to sign and date the application.
* **Submitted By** (page 8):
	+ This field is to select the person who is submitting the application.
* **Date** (page 8):
	+ This field is for the date on which the application was submitted.
* **Time** (page 8):
	+ This field is for the time at which the application was submitted.
* **Email** (page 8):
	+ This field is for the email address of the person submitting the application.
* **Phone** (page 8):
	+ This field is for the phone number of the person submitting the application.
* **Address** (page 8):
	+ This field is for the address of the person submitting the application.
* **Relationship** (page 8):
	+ This field is for the relationship between the person submitting the application and the student.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We recommend submitting the application electronically to ensure timely processing.
* Please keep a copy of the submitted application for your records.
