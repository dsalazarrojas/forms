# Online Language Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from students for the purpose of registering them in an online language class. The form collects details such as student contact information, class preferences, and schedule choices to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the required fields**: Indicate your choice of class and class date.
2. **Provide student information**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
3. **Select your language class preferences**: Choose from the options provided for class choice, language choice, and schedule.
4. **Submit your registration**: Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the submit button to register for the online language class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information
* **Student Name** (`student_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name in the format of first name, last name, e.g., John Doe.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check, as this will be used to communicate with you about your class schedule and any important updates.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if you would like to receive updates or notifications about your class.

### Class Selection
* **Class Choice** (`class_choice`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in taking the class.
* **Language Choice** (`language_choice`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple languages you are interested in learning from the provided options.
* **Class Date** (`class_date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a specific date you would like to start taking the class on.

### Additional Information
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, `time`, required/optional): Select your preferred schedule for the class, e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your class preferences or any other relevant information.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant school information or context, if necessary.

### Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate information to avoid any issues with your registration.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting your registration.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, contact your school administration for assistance.
