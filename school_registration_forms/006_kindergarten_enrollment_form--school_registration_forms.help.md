# kindergarten_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "kindergarten_enrollment_form" is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians to register their child for kindergarten. This form helps the institution or organization processing the application to gather the required details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by filling in the User Information section with your name, contact number, and email address.
2.  Proceed to the Guardian Information section, where you can provide details about yourself as the guardian, including name, address, and phone number.
3.  In the Student Information section, enter your child's details such as date of birth, name, and grade level.
4.  The Contact Information section is optional but will help the institution contact you if needed.
5.  If you have any additional information about your child or yourself, you can provide it in the Additional Information section.
6.  To ensure a smooth registration process, please upload a photo and any necessary documents as requested.
7.  Finally, add any relevant notes or comments in the Notes section.
8.  Once all sections are complete, click on the "Submit" button to finalize your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information

*   **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required): Enter your name, contact number, and email address for contact purposes.
*   **Guardian Information** (`Guardian Information`, text, required): Provide details about yourself as the guardian, including name, address, and phone number.
*   **Student Information** (`Student Information`, text, required): Enter your child's details such as date of birth, name, and grade level.
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, optional): This is an optional section for you to provide your contact information.
*   **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, optional): If you have any additional details, you can provide them here.
*   **Photo Upload** (`Photo Upload`, text, required): Upload a photo of your child for identification purposes.
*   **Document Upload** (`Document Upload`, text, required): Upload required documents related to your child's admission.
*   **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Add any relevant comments or notes about your child's application.
*   **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click this button to finalize your application.
