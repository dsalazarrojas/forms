# Homeschool Co Op Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a child in a homeschool co-op. It gathers essential information from parents/guardians to ensure that their child can participate in the co-op's activities and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on "Start" to begin filling out the form.
*   Fill out the form with the required and optional details.
*   Review your information carefully to ensure it's accurate.
*   Click "Submit" to send the form to the co-op's administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Parent Info
*   **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, not required): Enter your name and contact information, if you wish to provide it.

### Page 1: Student Info
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Enter your child's full name.
*   **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, not required): Enter your child's grade level (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
*   **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, not required): Enter additional information about your child (e.g., any health concerns, special needs, etc.).

### Page 2: School Year and Co-op
*   **School Year** (`school_year`, select_one, not required): Select the school year your child will be attending (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
*   **Co Op Name** (`co_op_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the co-op your child will be attending (if applicable).
*   **Co Op Address** (`co_op_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the co-op (if applicable).

### Page 3: Contact Info
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for future communication with the co-op.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for quick contact with the co-op.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all the fields are filled in accurately and consistently throughout the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please refer back to this help guide for clarification.
*   Double-check your form before submitting to avoid errors.
*   If you need assistance with any part of the form, please contact the co-op's administrators.
