# Intermediate Honors String Ensemble Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from students and parents for the Intermediate Honors String Ensemble program. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure successful registration and participation in the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your student's details, including their name, contact email, contact phone number, and contact phone extension (if applicable).
2. Select the instrument you would like to play in the Intermediate Honors String Ensemble.
3. Choose the correct rehearsal schedule (number of days per week) that fits your needs.
4. Choose your preferred performance days (number of times per week).
5. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have for the teacher or other parents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)** (`text`, required/optional): Please enter your student's name.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your student's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your student's contact phone number.
* **Contact Phone Extension (contact_phone_ext)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your student's contact phone extension (if applicable).
* **Parent Consent (parent_consent)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select which parent(s) would like to receive updates and information about the program. Options: Parent 1, Parent 2, Parent 3.
* **Instrument (instrument)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the instrument you would like to play in the Intermediate Honors String Ensemble. Options: Violin, Cello, Guitar, Drums.
* **Other Instrument (instrument_other)** (`text`, required/optional): If you select "Other" as your instrument, please specify it here.
* **Rehearsal Days (rehearsal_days)** (`number`, required/optional): Choose the number of days per week you would like to rehearse. Options: 2 days, 3 days, 4 days.
* **Practice Times (practice_times)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the number of practice times per week you would like to have. Options: 2 days, 3 days, 4 days, 5 days.
* **Teacher Comments (teacher_comments)** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any comments or questions you may have for the teacher.
* **Parent Comments (parent_comments)** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any comments or questions you may have for the teacher.
* **Teacher Confirmation (teacher_confirmation)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understand the program's details. Options: True, False.
* **Ensemble Placement (ensemble_placement)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select which ensemble placement you would like to join. Options: Ensemble 1, Ensemble 2, Ensemble 3.
* **Student Consent (student_consent)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understand the program's details. Options: True, False.
* **Teacher Confirmation (teacher_confirmation)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understand the program's details. Options: True, False.
* **Performance Days (performance_days)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the number of performance days per week you would like to have. Options: 1, 2, 3, 4.
* **Teacher Consent (teacher_consent)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understand the program's details. Options: True, False.
