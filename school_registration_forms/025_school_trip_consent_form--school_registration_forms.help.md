# school_trip_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information and consent from parents or guardians for a school trip.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent/guardian's name in the "name" field.
2. Provide the email address associated with the parent/guardian in the "email" field.
3. Enter the parent/guardian's phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the date of the school trip in the "trip_date" field.
5. Choose the duration of the trip in the "trip_duration" field.
6. Enter the destination of the trip in the "trip_destination" field.
7. Enter the student's name in the "name" field.
8. Choose the student's class from the available options in the "student_class" field.
9. Enter the student's grade level in the "student_grade" field.
10. Select the consent options for the student to attend the trip in the "consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **email** (2, email, required): Provide the email address associated with you.
* **phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **trip_date** (4, date, required): Select the date of the trip.
* **trip_duration** (5, number, required): Choose the duration of the trip.
* **trip_destination** (6, text, required): Enter the destination of the trip.
* **name** (7, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **class** (8, text, required): Choose the student's class from the available options.
* **grade** (9, number, required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Consent** (10, select_multiple, required): Select all options you agree to for the student's trip participation:
  * I agree to my child attending the school trip
  * I agree to the school trip being held on the specified date
  * I agree to my child attending the trip without parental accompaniment
