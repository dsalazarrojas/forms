# Preschool Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their child's enrollment in our preschool program. Please fill out this form to ensure a smooth and successful enrollment process for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field. Some fields are optional, but providing the required information is crucial for us to process your application successfully.
2. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information to avoid any discrepancies or errors.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information (parent_info)**: This field is for you, the parent or guardian, to provide information about yourself. Please fill out your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email. This information helps us stay in touch with you and keep you informed about your child's progress.
* **Student Information (student_info)**: This field is for you to provide essential information about your child, including their name, age, and any special needs they may have.
* **Guardian Information (guardian_info)**: If there is more than one person responsible for your child's care, please fill out this field with information about each guardian.
* **Enrollment Preferences (enrollment_preferences)**: Choose the days and times you would like your child to attend the preschool program. You can select multiple options to accommodate your preferences.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**: If you have any additional information or special requests, please use this field to provide any further details.
