# Toothbrushing Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toothbrushing Program Registration form is used to collect information from students and their parents to register them for the Toothbrushing Program. This program is intended to promote oral health and hygiene among students, and the form is used to gather necessary details to provide a smooth and efficient experience for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you about the program.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This is a required field.
6. If you are registering as a student, enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
7. If you are registering as a student, enter your Student ID in the "Student ID" field.
8. Select the type of program you are registering for from the "Program Type" dropdown menu. This will help us understand which program to direct you to.
9. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
10. For students under the age of 16, select "True" for "Parent Permission" to indicate that a parent or guardian has given permission for you to participate. For students over 16, select "False".
11. Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: This is the email address we will use to contact you about the program. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any issues or updates.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field.
* **Student Name**: If you are registering as a student, enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student ID**: If you are registering as a student, enter your student ID number.
* **Program Type**: Select the type of program you are registering for from the dropdown menu. This will help us direct you to the right program.
* **School Name**: Enter your school name as it is listed on your school records.
* **Parent Permission**: Select "True" if you are under 16 and a parent or guardian has given permission for you to participate. Select "False" if you are 16 or older.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
