# Primary School Pre Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Primary School Pre Registration Form Template efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your student's details on the "Student Details" page.
2. Move on to the "Parent Details" page to provide your contact information.
3. If your child has any medical conditions, provide the necessary information on the "Medical Conditions" page.
4. Ensure that you provide contact information for emergency contacts on the "Emergency Contacts" page.
5. Fill in the student's guardian details on the "Student Guardian Details" page.
6. Enter your student's home address on the "Address" page.
7. Attach relevant student documents as required on the "Student Documents" page.
8. Select your preferred school on the "School Selection" page.
9. Add any additional comments or information you think is necessary on the "Additional Comments" page.
10. Sign and date the form as the "Student Signature" and "Parent Signature" pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Details**: Enter your student's name, date of birth, and other required information about their identity.
- **Parent Details**: Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
- **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions your child may have, so that school staff can take necessary precautions.
- **Emergency Contacts**: Enter the names and contact information of individuals who can be reached in case of an emergency.
- **Student Guardian Details**: Fill in the guardian's contact information.
- **Address**: Enter your student's home address, including street, city, and state.
- **Student Documents**: Attach copies of required student documents, such as birth certificate, ID card, etc.
- **School Selection**: Choose the school where your child will be enrolled.
- **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information about your child or any special needs.
- **Student Signature**: Date and sign the form as the student.
- **Parent Signature**: Date and sign the form as the parent.
- **Emergency Contact Signature**: Date and sign the form as the emergency contact.
- **Date of Admission**: Enter the date your child will start attending the school.
- **Time of Admission**: Enter the time your child will start attending the school.
