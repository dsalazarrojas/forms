# Team Project Reservation With Time Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about team projects, including their titles, descriptions, start and end dates, team names, team members, booking times, dates, and status. It is intended to be a comprehensive registration process for team projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Enter a title for your team project in the "Project Title" field.
- Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
- Enter the start date of your project in the "Project Start Date" field.
- Enter the end date of your project in the "Project End Date" field.
- Enter the name of your team in the "Team Name" field.
- Enter the name of team members 1, 2, and 3 in their respective fields.
- Select the booking time for your project in the "Booking Time" field.
- Select the booking date for your project in the "Booking Date" field.
- Choose the status of your project from the options "Available", "Not Available", "In Progress", or "Completed" in the "Team Project Status" field.
- Enter the email address of your team or project lead in the "Email Address" field.
- Enter the phone number of your team or project lead in the "Phone Number" field.
- Provide any additional comments about your project in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Title** (`project_title`, required): Enter a brief title for your team project.
- **Project Description** (`project_description`, optional): Provide a short description of your project.
- **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, optional): Enter the start date of your project.
- **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, optional): Enter the end date of your project.
- **Team Name** (`team_name`, optional): Enter the name of your team.
- **Team Member 1** (`team_member_1`, optional): Enter the name of team member 1.
- **Team Member 2** (`team_member_2`, optional): Enter the name of team member 2.
- **Team Member 3** (`team_member_3`, optional): Enter the name of team member 3.
- **Booking Time** (`booking_time`, optional): Select the booking time for your project.
- **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, optional): Select the booking date for your project.
- **Team Project Status** (`team_project_status`, optional): Choose the status of your project.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, optional): Enter the email address of your team or project lead.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, optional): Enter the phone number of your team or project lead.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Provide any additional comments about your project.
