# english-medium-student-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from students registering in English language medium schools or institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your student email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Provide your student phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
4. Enter the name of the school you are registering with in the "School Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identity documents. This is a required field.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your personal email address where the school can contact you. This is a required field.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for school related matters. This is a required field.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school or institution you are registering with. This is a required field.
