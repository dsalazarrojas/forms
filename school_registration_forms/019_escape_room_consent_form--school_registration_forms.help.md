# Escape Room Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Escape Room Consent Form is a document that requires students to provide information about themselves and their guardians or parents for escape room activities. This form is meant to be filled out by students prior to participating in the escape room activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student information section with your name, school name (if applicable), and contact information.
2. Provide details about your guardian or parent's contact information.
3. Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
4. Enter the dates of your expected attendance and absence from school.
5. Choose the date and time of the escape room activity.
6. Enter the address of your school.
7. Select the escape room number where you will be participating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or school records.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school's name if applicable. This is not required for all students.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your school email address if available.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your personal phone number or student ID number.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter your guardian or parent's name.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, optional): Enter your guardian or parent's email address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): Enter your guardian or parent's phone number.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are a Student Guardian, Parent Guardian, or Both.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select whether the emergency contact is a Parent, Guardian, or Both.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the escape room activity.
* **Student Absence** (`student_absence`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to be absent from school prior to the escape room activity.
* **Escape Date** (`escape_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the escape room activity.
* **Escape Time** (`escape_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the escape room activity.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your school.
* **Escape Room Number** (`escape_room_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the escape room activity you will be participating in.

## Tips
Be sure to fill out all required fields and double check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
