# high_school_counselor_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The high school counselor information form is used to collect information about high school students and their families. This information will be used to provide support and guidance as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school year that applies to you.
2. Enter the student's name, grade level, school name, and parent name.
3. Provide the student's email, phone number, and address.
4. Enter the student's birthdate.
5. Enter the student's and parent's goals and objectives.
6. Enter the school and parent goals and objectives.
7. Select how you want to submit and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Year** (`school_year`, text, required/optional): Enter the school year that applies to you.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required): Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12).
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student's school.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department that the student belongs to (e.g., English, Math, Science).
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
- **Student Address** (`student_address`, note, optional): Enter the student's address.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent's name.
- **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent's email address.
- **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, note, optional): Enter the parent's address.
- **Student Birthdate** (`student_birthdate`, date, required): Enter the student's birthdate.
- **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, text, required): Enter the counselor's name.
- **Counselor Phone** (`counselor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the counselor's phone number.
- **Counselor Email** (`counselor_email`, email, optional): Enter the counselor's email address.
- **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the student.
- **Counselor Notes** (`counselor_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the counselor.
- **Student Goals** (`student_goals`, text, required): Enter the student's goals (e.g., academic success, personal development).
- **Student Objectives** (`student_objectives`, select_multiple, required): Select the student's objectives (e.g., academic success, personal development, social development, career development).
- **School Goals** (`school_goals`, text, required): Enter the school's goals.
- **School Objectives** (`school_objectives`, select_multiple, required): Select the school's objectives (e.g., academic success, personal development, social development, career development).
- **Parent Goal** (`parent_goal`, text, required): Enter the parent's goals.
- **Parent Objectives** (`parent_objectives`, select_multiple, required): Select the parent's objectives (e.g., academic success, personal development, social development, career development).
- **Submit and Save** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select how you want to submit and save the form (e.g., submit and save, other options).
