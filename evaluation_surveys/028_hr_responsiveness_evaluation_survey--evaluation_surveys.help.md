# HR Responsiveness Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Responsiveness Evaluation Survey is a tool used to measure the responsiveness of HR departments across different teams. By completing this survey, team members, managers, or department heads can provide valuable feedback on the effectiveness and responsiveness of their HR departments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review the survey questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Select the correct response for each question.
3. Provide any additional feedback in the "feedback" field.
4. Submit your completed survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
**Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, **Required: No**): This section is a general introduction to the survey.

### 2. Response Speed
**Response Speed** (`response_speed`, `select_one`, **Required: No**): This question asks if the HR department responds quickly to your queries. Select "Yes" if you feel they respond promptly, and "No" if you don't.

### 3. Professionalism
**Professionalism** (`professionalism`, `select_multiple`, **Required: No**): This question gauges the professionalism of the HR department. Select "Yes" if you feel they are professional, "No" if you don't. You can select multiple answers if you feel they have shown multiple positive attributes.

### 4. Effectiveness
**Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, `number`, **Required: No**): This question asks how effective you feel the HR department is. Rate them on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being not at all effective and 10 being very effective.

### 5. Feedback
**Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, **Required: No**): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional feedback you may have about the HR department.

### 6. Areas to Improve
**Areas to Improve** (`areas_to_improve`, `select_one`, **Required: No**): This question asks if there are areas where the HR department can improve. Select "Yes" if you feel there are areas they need to work on.

### 7. Response Speed Score
**Response Speed Score** (`response_speed_score`, `number`, **Required: No**): This is a numerical representation of your answer to "Response Speed". Rate them on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being very slow and 10 being very fast.

### 8. Professionalism Score
**Professionalism Score** (`professionalism_score`, `number`, **Required: No**): This is a numerical representation of your answer to "Professionalism". Rate them on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being unprofessional and 10 being very professional.

### 9. Effectiveness Score
**Effectiveness Score** (`effectiveness_score`, `number`, **Required: No**): This is a numerical representation of your answer to "Effectiveness". Rate them on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being not at all effective and 10 being very effective.

### 10. Overall Score
**Overall Score** (`overall_score`, `number`, **Required: No**): This is a numerical representation of your overall assessment of the HR department. Rate them on a scale from 1-10, with 1 being very poor and 10 being very excellent.

## Tips
* Answer honestly and accurately.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Remember that this form is meant to help the HR department improve, so be constructive in your feedback.
