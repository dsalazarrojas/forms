# Statement Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Statement Ranking Survey is designed to collect user preferences or opinions on various statements. The form is composed of multiple pages, each with different fields to gather specific information. This guide will walk you through the form's sections and help you understand how to complete it accurately.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by filling out the fields on each page, making sure to provide your honest opinions or rankings for each statement.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you're answering the questions accurately.
3.  Click "Submit" to complete the form once you have finished all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Select one** (Select one): Choose one of the options provided to answer the question. You are asked to select only one option from the available options.
*   **Select multiple** (Select multiple): Choose multiple options that apply to your answer. You can select as many options as you need.
*   **Date** (Date): Provide a date in the required format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Time** (Time): Enter a time in the required format (HH:MM).
*   **Note** (Note): Enter any additional information you'd like to share regarding your answer. This field is optional.
*   **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number.
*   **Text** (Text): Enter any additional text you'd like to share.
*   **Integer** (Integer): Enter a whole number without decimal points.
*   **Decimal** (Decimal): Enter a number with decimal points.

Note: This guide is a general explanation and may vary based on the specific requirements of the form fields. If you're unsure about any field, please refer back to the form's instructions or contact the form's administrators for further guidance.
