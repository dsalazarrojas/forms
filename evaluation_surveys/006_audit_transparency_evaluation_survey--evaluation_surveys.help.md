# Audit Transparency Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to evaluate the transparency of audit processes, ensuring that auditors are clear in their findings, communicate effectively, and provide accessible reports.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions based on your recent experience with an audit.
2. Choose the best option that reflects your assessment for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement in the "Improvement Suggestions" and "Additional Comments" fields, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required):
	+ This is the date the audit was completed.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required):
	+ This is your full name as the evaluator.
* **Role/Title** (`role_title`, text, required):
	+ This is your position within the organization or team that conducted the audit.
* **Audit Being Evaluated** (`evaluated_audit`, text, required):
	+ This is the name or ID of the audit being evaluated.
* **When Was the Audit Completed** (`audit_completion_date`, date, optional):
	+ This is the date the audit was completed, if available.
* **Clarity of Audit Findings** (`finding_clarity`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the clarity of the audit findings, from "Very Unclear" to "Very Clear".
* **Clarity of Recommendations** (`recommendation_clarity`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the clarity of the recommendations, from "Very Unclear" to "Very Clear".
* **Evidence Presentation Quality** (`evidence_presentation`, select_one, required):
	+ Evaluate the quality of the evidence presented, from "Poor" to "Excellent".
* **Overall Communication Transparency** (`communication_transparency`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the transparency of the audit communication, from "Very Non-Transparent" to "Very Transparent".
* **Transparency About Timeline** (`timeline_transparency`, select_one, required):
	+ Evaluate the transparency about the audit timeline, from "Not At All" to "Completely".
* **Transparency About Costs** (`cost_transparency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate the transparency about audit costs, from "Not Disclosed" to "Very Clear".
* **Explanation of Audit Methodology** (`methodology_explanation`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the explanation of the audit approach, from "Not Explained" to "Excellently Explained".
* **Clarity of Audit Scope** (`scope_clarity`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the clarity of the audit scope, from "Very Unclear" to "Very Clear".
* **Disclosure of Audit Limitations** (`limitation_disclosure`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the disclosure of audit limitations, from "Not Disclosed" to "Fully Disclosed".
* **Openness to Questions** (`open_communication`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the openness of auditors to questions, from "Very Closed" to "Very Open".
* **Progress Updates During Audit** (`progress_updates`, select_one, required):
	+ Evaluate the provision of progress updates, from "No Updates" to "Frequent Updates".
* **Transparency About Conflicts of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate the disclosure of potential conflicts of interest, from "Not Disclosed" to "Very Clear".
* **Accessibility of Final Report** (`report_accessibility`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the accessibility of the final report, from "Very Difficult" to "Very Easy".
* **Overall Transparency Rating** (`overall_transparency`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate the overall transparency, from "Very Poor" to "Excellent".
* **Suggestions for Improving Transparency** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any suggestions for improving audit transparency.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Add any other thoughts or comments about the audit transparency.

Note: Required fields are marked as "required" in the form definition.
