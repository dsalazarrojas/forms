# Enterprise LLM Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Enterprise LLM Evaluation Survey is designed to evaluate the adoption and implementation of Large Language Models (LLMs) in your organization. This survey will help us understand your experiences and opinions about LLMs and their implementation methods.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and concisely.
2. Choose the correct options for each question based on your experiences and opinions.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.
