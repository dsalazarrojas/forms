# Chronic Disease Prevention Policy Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the effectiveness and implementation of a policy aimed at preventing chronic diseases within an organization or institution. It is meant to assess your awareness, perception, and experiences with the policy, as well as identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your first and last name, email address, and your role within the organization.
2. Provide the name and description of the policy you are evaluating.
3. Select the focus area of the policy (e.g., cardiovascular disease, diabetes, etc.).
4. Choose how you are aware of the policy.
5. Select the sources from which you learned about the policy.
6. Rate the effectiveness of the policy.
7. Describe any measurable impact of the policy.
8. Indicate the level of adoption within the organization or community.
9. Identify any barriers to implementing the policy.
10. Assess the adequacy of resources for implementation.
11. Suggest any improvements or changes to the policy.
12. Specify any additional support needed for implementation.
13. Give your overall recommendation for the policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Your work or official email address.
* **Organization/Institution** (Required): The organization or institution you are a part of.
* **Your Role in Organization** (Required): Your job title or position within the organization.
* **Policy Name or Description** (Required): The name or description of the policy you are evaluating.
* **Policy Focus Area** (Required): Select the area of focus for the policy (e.g., Cardiovascular Disease, Diabetes, etc.).
* **Implementation Date** (Optional): The date the policy was implemented, if applicable.
* **How aware are you of this policy?** (Required): Choose how well you are aware of the policy.
* **How did you learn about this policy?** (Required): Select all the sources from which you learned about the policy.
* **How effective is this policy?** (Required): Rate the effectiveness of the policy.
* **Do you see measurable impact?** (Required): Describe any measurable impact of the policy.
* **Level of Community/Staff Adoption** (Required): Indicate the level of adoption within the organization or community.
* **What barriers hinder policy implementation?** (Optional): Describe any barriers to implementing the policy.
* **Are resources adequate for implementation?** (Required): Choose the adequacy of resources for implementing the policy.
* **Suggested Changes or Improvements** (Optional): Provide suggestions for improving the policy.
* **What additional support is needed?** (Optional): Describe any additional support required for implementing the policy.
* **Overall Recommendation for Policy** (Required): Give your overall recommendation for the policy (e.g., Continue as is, Continue with improvements, etc.).

**Tips**

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure of any field, use your best judgment or contact the relevant department for clarification.
* Remember to complete all required fields.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, use the space provided for improvement suggestions.

Please note that this form was generated automatically and does not replace human judgment or review by experts in the field. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or organization for guidance.
