# Story Evaluation Scorecard - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Story Evaluation Scorecard is a tool used to collect feedback and scores from multiple stakeholders (teachers, editors, and judges) on the quality and impact of a story.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the story you want to evaluate.
2. Provide numerical scores for each stakeholder group (Teacher, Editor, Judge).
3. Add any comments or feedback you have for each stakeholder group.
4. Submit your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Story Score (Required)
* **Story Score (story_score)**: This field requires you to assign a numerical score out of 10 to the story, indicating its overall quality and impact.

### 2. Teacher Score (Required)
* **Teacher Score (teacher_score)**: Provide a numerical score out of 10 from a teacher's perspective, taking into account the story's alignment with teaching goals and objectives.

### 3. Editor Score (Required)
* **Editor Score (editor_score)**: As an editor, provide a numerical score out of 10 evaluating the story's quality, coherence, and overall effectiveness.

### 4. Story Comments (Optional)
* **Story Comments (story_comments)**: Add any comments or feedback you have about the story's strengths and weaknesses.

### 5. Judge Comments (Optional)
* **Judge Comments (judge_comments)**: Provide comments or feedback on the story's overall quality and impact from a judge's perspective.

### 6. Teacher Comments (Optional)
* **Teacher Comments (teacher_comments)**: Add any comments or feedback from a teacher's perspective on the story's alignment with teaching goals and objectives.

### 7. Editor Comments (Optional)
* **Editor Comments (editor_comments)**: Provide comments or feedback on the story's quality, coherence, and overall effectiveness from an editor's perspective.
