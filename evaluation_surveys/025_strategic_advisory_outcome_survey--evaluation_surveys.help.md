# Strategic Advisory Outcome Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Strategic Advisory Outcome Survey is designed to collect feedback from clients on the effectiveness of our advisory service. This survey aims to measure client satisfaction, identify desired outcomes, and gather information on future needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please review each page and read the instructions and hints provided.
2.  Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3.  Select all applicable options for each page that apply to you.
4.  Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" page.
5.  Review your responses and submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Survey** (`intro_page`, `note`, required: `false`): This page provides an introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **Client Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_page`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your level of satisfaction with our advisory service, using a number from 1 to 5.
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes_page`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the outcomes that you achieved as a result of our advisory service.
*   *Outcome 1*: Indicate the outcome related to outcome 1.
*   *Outcome 2*: Indicate the outcome related to outcome 2.
*   *Outcome 3*: Indicate the outcome related to outcome 3.
* **Impact** (`impact_page`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the outcome that best describes how our advisory service impacted your business.
*   *Outcome 1*: Select outcome 1.
*   *Outcome 2*: Select outcome 2.
*   *Outcome 3*: Select outcome 3.
* **Future Needs** (`future_needs_page`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your future needs and expectations from our advisory service.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_page`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about our advisory service.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps_page`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the next steps you plan to take to further leverage our advisory service.
*   *Option 1*: Indicate next step 1.
*   *Option 2*: Indicate next step 2.
*   *Option 3*: Indicate next step 3.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating_page`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your overall satisfaction with our advisory service, using a number from 1 to 5.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info_page`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the contact information that you would like to use to get in touch with us.
*   *Contact Info 1*: Indicate contact info 1.
*   *Contact Info 2*: Indicate contact info 2.
*   *Contact Info 3*: Indicate contact info 3.
* **Submit** (`submit_page`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information to submit the form.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" page.
