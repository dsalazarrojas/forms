# Social Work Practice Innovation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Work Practice Innovation Evaluation Form is designed to support social workers and administrators in evaluating the effectiveness and implementation of new programs or interventions. This form helps to identify strengths and weaknesses, and inform future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information: Program Name, Evaluation Period, Start Date, End Date, Evaluator Role, and Final Comments are not required fields, but filling them out will help you get the most out of the form.
2. Choose the most accurate options for Program Type, Service Type, Evaluation Purpose, Evaluation Method, Evaluation Frequency, Program Fidelity, Program Adoption, Program Fidelity Adoption, and Rating Of Effectiveness.
3. If multiple outcomes occurred, select all relevant outcomes in Program Outcomes.
4. Enter your ratings for Rating Of Effectiveness, Rating Of Fidelity, and Rating Of Implementation.
5. Provide your ratings for Rating Of Satisfaction, Rating Of Improvement, and Rating Of Sustainability.
6. Enter your comments for Comments, Recommended Changes, Next Steps, and Final Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program or intervention being evaluated.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of program or intervention being evaluated (e.g. Human Service Program, Community Organization, Community-Based Program, or Other).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of service delivered (e.g. In-person Service, Remote Service, Online Service, Combination, or Other).
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, date, optional): Select a specific date or date range for the evaluation.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the evaluation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Role** (`evaluator_role`, select_one, optional): Select your role in the evaluation process (e.g. Agency Staff, Nonprofit Staff, Community Leader, or Other).
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, text, optional): Describe the goals of the program or intervention.
* **Evaluation Purpose** (`evaluation_purpose`, select_one, optional): Choose the purpose of the evaluation (e.g. Continuous Improvement, Research, Accountability, or Other).
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method of evaluation (e.g. Self-Assessment, Client Feedback, External Review, or Mixed).
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of the evaluation (e.g. Continuous, One-time, As Needed, or Other).
* **Program Fidelity** (`program_fidelity`, select_one, optional): Rate how well the new program or intervention was implemented (e.g. Not Implemented, Partially Implemented, Fully Implemented).
* **Program Adoption** (`program_adoption`, select_one, optional): Select who adopted the program (e.g. Agency Staff, Nonprofit Staff, Community Members, or Other).
* **Program Fidelity Adoption** (`program_fidelity_adoption`, select_one, optional): Rate how much of the program was adopted (e.g. None, Some, Most, or All).
* **Program Outcomes** (`program_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more outcomes that occurred as a result of the program or intervention.
* **Rating Of Effectiveness** (`rating_of_effectiveness`, number, optional): Rate your overall sense of whether the new program or intervention was effective (e.g. 1-5).
* **Rating Of Fidelity** (`rating_of_fidelity`, number, optional): Rate how well the new program or intervention was implemented (e.g. 1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the program or intervention.
* **Recommended Changes** (`recommended_changes`, text, optional): Suggest any changes or improvements to the program or intervention.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline any next steps or actions to be taken as a result of the evaluation.
* **Rating Of Satisfaction** (`rating_of_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the new program or intervention (e.g. 1-5).
* **Rating Of Improvement** (`rating_of_improvement`, number, optional): Rate the improvement of the new program or intervention (e.g. 1-5).
* **Rating Of Implementation** (`rating_of_implementation`, number, optional): Rate the implementation of the new program or intervention (e.g. 1-5).
* **Rating Of Sustainability** (`rating_of_sustainability`, number, optional): Rate the sustainability of the new program or intervention (e.g. 1-5).
* **Final Comments** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Enter any final comments or feedback about the evaluation process.
