# Healthcare Workforce Wellbeing Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the wellbeing and job satisfaction of the healthcare workforce. It asks questions about various aspects of work-related stress, collaboration, leadership support, and life satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Take a few minutes to read through each question carefully.
2. Select the answer that best matches your feelings or experiences.
3. Make sure to answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your overall wellbeing at work** (`wellbeing_scale_label`): Please select "Yes" if you generally feel good about your wellbeing at work, and "No" if you don't.
* **Current wellbeing score out of 5** (`wellbeing_score`): Rate your current wellbeing score out of 5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Emotional stress** (`emotional_stress`): How often do you feel emotionally drained at work? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Good work-life balance** (`work_life_balance`): Do you feel like you have a good work-life balance? Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you don't.
* **Physical stress** (`physical_stress`): How often do you feel physically exhausted after a shift? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Mental health problems** (`mental_health`): Have you experienced mental health problems while working? Select "Yes" if you have, and "No" if you haven't.
* **Collaboration with colleagues** (`team_collaboration`): How often do you feel like you are working collaboratively with your colleagues? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Leadership support** (`leadership_support`): How often do you feel supported by your supervisor? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Support from manager** (`manager_support`): How often do you receive support from your manager? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`): Do you feel satisfied with your job? Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you don't.
* **Job security** (`job_security`): Do you feel secure in your job? Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you don't.
* **Conflicts with manager** (`manager_conflict`): How often do you experience conflicts with your manager? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Conflicts with team** (`team_conflict`): How often do you experience conflicts with your team? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Work-life quality** (`work_life_quality`): How often do you feel like you have good work-life quality? Select "Good" if you do, and "Poor" if you don't.
* **Satisfaction with team** (`team_satisfaction`): How often do you feel satisfied with your team? Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you don't.
* **Overwhelmed with workload** (`workload`): How often do you feel overwhelmed with your workload? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Satisfaction with job 2** (`job_satisfaction_2`): Do you feel satisfied with your job? Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you don't.
* **Satisfaction with life 2** (`work_life_satisfaction_2`): How often do you feel satisfied with your life? Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you don't.
* **Good work-life quality 2** (`work_life_quality_2`): How often do you feel like you have good quality work-life? Select "Good" if you do, and "Poor" if you don't.
* **Conflicts with team 2** (`team_conflict_2`): How often do you experience conflicts with your team? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Manager support 2** (`manager_support_2`): How often do you receive support from your manager? Select "Yes" if it's often, and "No" if it's rarely or never.
* **Satisfaction with job 3** (`job_satisfaction_3`): How satisfied are you with your current job? Select "Yes" if you are satisfied, and "No" if you are not.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
* Select the answer that best matches your feelings and experiences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative.
