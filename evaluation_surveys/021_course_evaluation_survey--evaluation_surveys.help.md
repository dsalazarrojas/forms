# Course Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluations from students on various aspects of a course, including its content, teaching quality, learning experience, relevance, and any additional feedback.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the teaching quality of the course by choosing "High", "Medium", or "Low" from the dropdown menu.
2.  Describe your learning experience in the "Learning Experience" field, including any positive or negative experiences.
3.  Select the relevance of the course by choosing from the options "Very Relevant", "Somewhat Relevant", "Not Relevant", or "Not at all Relevant".
4.  Provide any additional feedback you may have about the course in the "Additional Feedback" field.
5.  If you want to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your email address in the "Email" field.
6.  If you want to be contacted via phone, provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7.  If you want to be contacted via email or phone, enter your course name in the "Course Name" field.
8.  If you want to be contacted via email or phone, enter the name of the instructor in the "Instructor Name" field.
9.  If you want to be contacted via email or phone, enter the department in the "Department" field.
10.  If you want to be contacted via email or phone, enter your student ID number in the "Student ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Content (1)**: Describe the overall content of the course.
* **Teaching Quality (2)**: Rate the effectiveness of this course's instructor in delivering the course content. Choose "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Learning Experience (3)**: Describe your learning experience in the course, including any positive or negative experiences.
* **Course Relevance (4)**: Select the relevance of the course to your learning needs. Choose from the options "Very Relevant", "Somewhat Relevant", "Not Relevant", or "Not at all Relevant".
* **Additional Feedback (5)**: Provide any additional feedback you may have about the course.
* **Email (6)**: If you want to be contacted for further discussion, provide your email address.
* **Phone Number (7)**: If you want to be contacted via phone, provide your phone number.
* **Course Name (8)**: If you want to be contacted via email or phone, enter your course name.
* **Instructor Name (9)**: If you want to be contacted via email or phone, enter the name of the instructor.
* **Department (10)**: If you want to be contacted via email or phone, enter the department.
* **Student ID (11)**: If you want to be contacted via email or phone, enter your student ID number.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and honestly.
* Please provide detailed descriptions where possible.
* Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
