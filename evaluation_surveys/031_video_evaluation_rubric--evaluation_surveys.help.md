# Video Evaluation Rubric - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate videos based on a set of criteria. It is used to provide feedback on video content, quality, and relevance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the video and assess it based on the following criteria:
    * Title
    * Description
    * Link to the video
    * Rating (1-5)
    * Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Video Title (<video_title>)
*   This field is where you enter the title of the video being evaluated.
*   Try to match the title as closely as possible to what the video actually is or represents.

### Video Description (<video_description>)
*   This field is where you enter a brief description of the video being evaluated.
*   Try to be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise.

### Video Link (<video_link>)
*   This field is where you enter the URL of the video being evaluated.
*   Make sure to enter the correct URL so that the reviewer can access the video.

### Video Rating (1-5) (<video_rating>)
*   This field is where you assign a rating to the video based on the criteria above.
*   1 is the lowest rating and 5 is the highest.

### Video Comment (<video_comment>)
*   This field is where you enter any additional comments or feedback on the video.
*   Try to be as specific as possible in your feedback.

## Tips
*   Review the video carefully before filling out this form.
*   Take your time to fill out this form thoroughly and accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
