# Food Resource SWOT Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Resource SWOT Analysis form is a tool for identifying and analyzing the key factors that can impact your food resource. It's used to assess the current situation, opportunities, and potential threats of your food resource.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On each page, provide your answers to the questions or fields.
2. Fill in the required fields with your thoughts and answers.
3. Review each page carefully before submitting your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Food Resource (Page 1: food_resource)
* **Food Resource** (`<number>`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of your food resource.

### Food Name (Page 2: resource_name)
* **Food Name** (`<text>`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your food resource.

### Strengths (Page 3: strengths)
* **Strengths** (`<text>`, text, required/optional): List the internal strengths of your food resource, such as its nutritional value, taste, or popularity.

### Weaknesses (Page 4: weaknesses)
* **Weaknesses** (`<text>`, text, required/optional): List the internal weaknesses of your food resource, such as its shelf life, storage requirements, or processing challenges.

### Opportunities (Page 5: opportunities)
* **Opportunities** (`<text>`, text, required/optional): Identify external opportunities that can positively impact your food resource, such as new market trends or collaborations.

### Threats (Page 6: threats)
* **Threats** (`<text>`, text, required/optional): List external threats that can negatively impact your food resource, such as supply chain disruptions or competitor activity.

### Next Steps (Page 7: next_steps)
* **Next Steps** (`<text>`, text, required/optional): Outline the next steps you plan to take to address the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats identified in this analysis.

### Assigned To (Page 8: assigned_to)
* **Assigned To** (`<select multiple>`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the individuals or teams responsible for implementing the next steps.

### Notes (Page 9: notes)
* **Notes** (`<note>`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your food resource's SWOT analysis.
 
## Tips
* Be specific and detailed in your answers.
* Review each page carefully before submitting your response.
* Take into account the strengths and weaknesses of your food resource to identify opportunities and threats.
* Use this analysis as a starting point for future planning and decision-making.
