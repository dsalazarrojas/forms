# Consulting Workflow Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to collect feedback from consultants on their current workflow, identify main pain points, and gather insights on potential areas for improvement. Your input will help us understand your challenges and provide valuable information to enhance our consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, providing your name, role, department (if applicable), and contact email.
2. Rate your current workflow satisfaction and describe your main pain points.
3. Indicate how often bottlenecks occur in your workflow.
4. List the primary tools and software used in your workflow.
5. Select the improvements you'd like to see in your workflow, such as automation of repetitive tasks, better handoffs between teams, or improved documentation.
6. Specify the expected outcomes or metrics you'd like to achieve from workflow changes.
7. Choose your preferred method of follow-up communication (email, phone, or virtual meeting).
8. Enter your email address for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name**: Please provide your full name for identification purposes.
* **Respondent role**: Enter your job title or role within the organization.
* **Department**: If you're part of a department, please enter it. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Current workflow satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your current workflow using the provided scale:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Main pain points**: Describe up to three major pain points you experience in your current workflow.
* **Frequency of process bottlenecks**: Indicate how often bottlenecks occur in your workflow:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Tools and software used**: List the primary tools and software used in your workflow. This will help us understand your current tools landscape.
* **Desired improvements**: Select one or more improvements you'd like to see in your workflow:
	+ Automation of repetitive tasks
	+ Better handoffs between teams
	+ Improved documentation
	+ Faster decision making
	+ Real-time reporting
* **Expected outcomes**: Specify the metrics or goals you expect from workflow changes.
* **Follow up preference**: Choose your preferred method of follow-up communication:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Virtual meeting
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
