# self-evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-evaluation form is designed to help users assess their own performance, goals, and areas of improvement. It is used to gather feedback and ratings from users on various aspects of their work or progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Use the correct format for each field, such as date and time formats.
3. Select the correct options for each select-type fields.
4. Provide thorough and honest answers to ensure accurate feedback and ratings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your work or school email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter your work or school phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of your department or division.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required): Enter your job function or role.
* **Job Division** (`job_division`, text, required): Enter your job division or department.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter an additional job title, if applicable.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Self Assessment** (`self_assessment`, number, optional): Enter a number rating from 1 to 10 to assess your performance.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide feedback on your performance, progress, or suggestions for improvement.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Enter a number rating from 1 to 10 to rate your performance.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: "Very Satisfied", "Satisfied", "Neutral", "Dissatisfied", "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the following options that apply: "1", "2", "3", "4", "5".
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date in the format "MM/DD/YY".
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Self Eval 1** (`self_eval_1`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have evaluated your performance.
* **Self Eval 2** (`self_eval_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to indicate your self-evaluation progress.
* **Self Eval 3** (`self_eval_3`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have evaluated your performance.
* **Self Eval 4** (`self_eval_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to indicate your self-evaluation progress.
* **Self Eval 5** (`self_eval_5`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have evaluated your performance.
* **Self Eval 6** (`self_eval_6`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to indicate your self-evaluation progress.
* **Self Eval 7** (`self_eval_7`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have evaluated your performance.
* **Self Eval 8** (`self_eval_8`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to indicate your self-evaluation progress.
* **Self Evaluation** (`self_evaluation`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have evaluated your performance.
