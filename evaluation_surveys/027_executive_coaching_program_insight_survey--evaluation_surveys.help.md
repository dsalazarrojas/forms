# Executive Coaching Program Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of the Executive Coaching Program, designed to collect valuable insights from participants to improve the program's effectiveness. The information gathered will help the program's administrators to tailor the program to meet the needs of its participants better.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting "Coaching Program" page.
2. Answer the questions as honestly as possible, providing clear and concise information about your current goal, desired goal achievement, coaching style, and frequency.
3. Make sure to submit your answers once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coaching Program** (`coaching_program`, Note, not required): This page is an introduction to the Executive Coaching Program. It serves as a starting point for the other questions that follow.
* **Current Goal** (`current_goal`, Text, not required): Please provide a clear and concise description of your current goal. This will help us understand your current objectives and expectations.
* **Goal Achievement** (`goal_achievement`, Number, not required): Please indicate your desired level of achievement for your current goal, using a number (e.g., 80, 90, or 100).
* **Coaching Style** (`coaching_style`, Select One, not required): Choose the coaching style that best suits your needs. Options are:
	+ Style 1
	+ Style 2
	+ Style 3
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, Text, not required): Describe the frequency of your desired coaching interactions. Please provide as much detail as possible about how often you would like to meet with your coach.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the required fields (if any) to ensure that your submission is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for help.
* You can submit your form as many times as you like, but please be aware that each submission will be reviewed and considered individually.

Please note that this form is not intended to be a comprehensive survey, but rather a tool to gather specific insights that will help us improve the Executive Coaching Program. Your feedback is highly valued and appreciated!
