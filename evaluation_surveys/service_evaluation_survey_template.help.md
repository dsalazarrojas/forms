# Service Evaluation Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Evaluation Survey Template is a tool used to gather feedback from service users to improve the overall quality of our service. This form is designed to be completed by users who have interacted with our service and are willing to share their opinions and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the answer that best describes your level of satisfaction with our service.
2. Indicate how often you interact with our service.
3. Choose all that apply from the options that best describe your experience with our service.
4. Type in any suggestions or comments you have for improving our service.
5. Share any additional feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with our service?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`):
	+ This question is designed to gauge your level of satisfaction with our service. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How often do you interact with our service?** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Rarely`, `Never`):
	+ This question helps us understand how frequently you use our service. Select the option that best describes your interaction frequency.
* **Which of the following best describes your experience with our service?** (`Easy to use`, `Fast`, `Reliable`, `Comprehensive`, `Missing feature`, `Confusing`):
	+ This question is designed to help us understand your experience with our service. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
* **What would you improve or change about our service?**:
	+ This question is an open-ended text field where you can provide any suggestions or comments you have for improving our service.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?**:
	+ This question is also an open-ended text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please be as concrete as possible.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us improve our service!
