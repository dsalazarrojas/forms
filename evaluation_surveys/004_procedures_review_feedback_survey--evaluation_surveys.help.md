# procedures_review_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings on procedures performed by staff or stakeholders. It allows reviewers to evaluate the overall impression of a procedure, provide suggestions for improvement, and rate the importance and frequency of the procedure. The form also collects information on the procedure ID, date, time spent on the procedure, rating, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the overall impression of the procedure by choosing "Yes" or "No".
2. Identify who provided the procedure to you, whether it was an internal or external staff member.
3. Enter the procedure ID number.
4. Enter the date the procedure was performed.
5. Report the time spent on the procedure.
6. Rate the procedure as "Very Important", "Important", "Somewhat Important", or "Not Important".
7. Indicate how frequently the procedure is performed, selecting all applicable options (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
8. Choose the type of procedure, selecting all applicable options (e.g., Internal, External, Both).
9. Identify who reviewed this procedure, whether it was staff, a stakeholder, or both.
10. Enter any additional comments about the procedure.
11. (Optional) Provide a rating score for the procedure.
12. (Optional) Leave any comments for the review.
13. (Optional) Enter the rating category for the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the overall impression of the procedure?** (`feedback`, select_one): This field asks you to indicate whether you overall have a positive or negative impression of the procedure by choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Who did you provide the procedure to?** (`staff`, select_one): Please select who provided you the procedure, whether it was an internal or external staff member.
* **Procedure ID** (`procedure_id`, number): Enter the unique number assigned to the procedure.
* **Date of Procedure** (`date`, date): Report the date the procedure was performed.
* **Time Spent on Procedure** (`time`, number): Report the amount of time spent on the procedure.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number): Rate the procedure as "Very Important", "Important", "Somewhat Important", or "Not Important".
* **What could be improved in this procedure?** (`suggestions`, text): Provide any suggestions on how the procedure could be improved.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text): Leave any additional comments about the procedure.
* **How important is this procedure?** (`rating_important`, select_one): Rate the procedure as "Very Important", "Important", "Somewhat Important", or "Not Important".
* **How frequently is this procedure performed?** (`rating_frequency`, select_multiple): Indicate how frequently the procedure is performed, selecting all applicable options (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **What type of procedure?** (`procedure_type`, select_multiple): Choose the type of procedure, selecting all applicable options (e.g., Internal, External, Both).
* **Who reviewed this procedure?** (`review_by`, select_one): Identify who reviewed this procedure, whether it was staff, a stakeholder, or both.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, number): Provide a numerical rating score for the procedure.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text): Leave any comments about the review.
* **Rating Category** (`rating_category`, text): Enter the rating category for the procedure.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text): Leave any additional comments about the procedure. (Note: This field is optional and has no direct effect on the overall review.)
