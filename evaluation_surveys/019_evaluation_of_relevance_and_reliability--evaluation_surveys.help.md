# Evaluation Of Relevance And Reliability - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help evaluate the relevance and reliability of a source.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Carefully read the form questions and make sure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
* Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
* Make sure to follow the required fields marked as "Required: True".
