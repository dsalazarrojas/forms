# Risk Culture Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gauge the level of risk awareness, perception, and culture within your organization. It is meant to be a starting point for discussions and improvement initiatives aimed at identifying areas of improvement and creating a more risk-informed culture.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form has multiple sections and questions, which are listed below. Each question represents an aspect of risk culture within your organization.
2.  Select the best possible answer for each question, based on your current situation within your department/unit/team.
3.  You may not need to answer every question; only fill out questions relevant to your role.
4.  Be as accurate and honest as possible when answering these questions.
5.  If you are unsure of a term or concept, try to answer based on your intuition or best effort.
