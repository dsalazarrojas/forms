# operational_discipline_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operational discipline assessment survey is designed to evaluate the level of adherence to guidelines and procedures within an organization. It's a self-assessment tool used to identify areas of improvement and promote a more disciplined operational environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the guidelines and procedures of the team or organization being assessed.
2. Answer the questions based on your direct involvement or knowledge of the operational activities and processes.
3. Provide honest and informed responses to each question.
4. Save your progress and come back to it when you're ready to submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operational Discipline Assessment** (1, text, required): This is the main section where you'll provide your assessment of the operational discipline. You'll need to describe the level of adherence to guidelines and procedures within the team or organization.
* **Operations** (2, select_multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the team or organization consistently follows established procedures.
* **Safety Practices** (3, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if safety practices are consistently applied.
* **Procedures** (4, select_multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if procedures are consistently followed.
* **Punctuality** (5, number, optional): Enter a number between 0 (low) and 10 (high) to score the team or organization's punctuality.
* **Communication** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if effective communication is consistently applied.
* **Overall Score** (7, number, optional): Enter a number between 0 (low) and 10 (high) to score the team or organization's overall discipline.
