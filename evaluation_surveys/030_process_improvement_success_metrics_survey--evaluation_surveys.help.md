# Process Improvement Success Metrics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather data on the success metrics of a process improvement project. It aims to collect information on various aspects of the project, including feedback, date of survey, contact information, and multiple metrics. The form is meant to be completed by process owners or team members to track and analyze the progress of their process improvement initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by filling out the Survey Form**: Enter your thoughts, observations, and comments about the process improvement project in the **Survey Form** field.
2.  Select a **Date of Survey**: Choose the date on which you are completing this survey.
3.  Provide your **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, or other contact details so that we can reach out to you if needed.
4.  Add any additional **Comments**: Provide any additional context or information about the project that you think is relevant.
5.  **Enter metrics 11-25**: Fill out metrics 11-25 as per the specific requirements of the project. These fields are meant to capture specific data points for the project's success metrics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey Form**: A text field for entering your thoughts, observations, and comments about the process improvement project.
*   **Feedback**: A text field for providing feedback on the project.
*   **Date of Survey**: A date field for selecting the date on which you are completing this survey.
*   **Contact Info**: A text field for providing contact information, such as name, email, or phone number.
*   **Comments**: A text field for adding any additional comments or context about the project.
*   **Metric 11-Metric 25**: Twenty-five text fields for entering specific data points for the project's success metrics.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information in each field to ensure the data is reliable and useful for analysis.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
*   This form is meant to be a starting point for the process improvement project, and any additional information or context you provide will help us better understand your project's progress.
