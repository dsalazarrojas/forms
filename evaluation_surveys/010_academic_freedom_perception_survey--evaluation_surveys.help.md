# Academic Freedom Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to understand your perception of academic freedom within your institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your role at the institution from the list.
2. Provide the number of years you have been affiliated with the institution.
3. Rate your freedom to teach, research, publish, and express opinions from 1 to 5, where 1 means "very restricted" and 5 means "completely free."
4. Choose any applicable restrictions affecting your academic freedom from the list.
5. Rate how well your institution protects your academic freedom (1-5 scale).
6. Rate how comfortable you are expressing dissenting opinions (1-5 scale).
7. Indicate your level of agreement with the statement "External forces influence curriculum decisions."
8. Choose whether you have avoided certain research topics due to potential controversy.
9. Rate the overall academic climate for free inquiry (1-5 scale).
10. Select the impact of perceived academic freedom on your work performance.
11. Provide any suggestions to enhance academic freedom at your institution.
12. Offer any additional comments about your institution's academic freedom environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Role at the Institution** (`respondent_role`): Select your primary role at the institution from the list.
* **Years at This Institution** (`years_at_institution`): Provide the number of years you have been affiliated with this institution.
* **Department/College** (`department_college`): Enter your academic department or college affiliation.
* **Freedom to Teach Rating** (`freedom_to_teach_rating`): Rate your freedom to choose course content from 1 (very restricted) to 5 (completely free).
* **Freedom to Research Rating** (`freedom_to_research_rating`): Rate your freedom to select research topics from 1 (very restricted) to 5 (completely free).
* **Freedom to Publish Rating** (`freedom_to_publish_rating`): Rate your freedom to publish research findings from 1 (very restricted) to 5 (completely free).
* **Freedom to Speak Rating** (`freedom_to_speak_rating`): Rate your comfort level in expressing opinions from 1 (very restricted) to 5 (completely free).
* **Perceived Restrictions** (`perceived_restrictions`): Choose any applicable restrictions affecting your academic freedom.
* **Institutional Support** (`institutional_support_rating`): Rate how well your institution protects your academic freedom from 1 (very poorly) to 5 (excellent).
* **Freedom to Disagree Rating** (`freedom_to_disagree_rating`): Rate your comfort level in expressing dissenting opinions from 1 (very restricted) to 5 (completely free).
* **Curriculum Control Concerns** (`curriculum_control_concerns`): Indicate your level of agreement with the statement "External forces influence curriculum decisions."
* **Research Topic Restrictions** (`research_topic_restrictions`): Choose whether you have avoided certain research topics due to potential controversy.
* **Academic Environment** (`academic_environment_rating`): Rate the overall academic climate for free inquiry from 1 (very restricted) to 5 (completely free).
* **Impact on Performance** (`freedom_impact_on_performance`): Select the impact of perceived academic freedom on your work performance.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`): Provide any suggestions to enhance academic freedom at your institution.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Offer any additional thoughts about your institution's academic freedom environment.

Note: Please answer each question honestly, and rate according to the provided scale.
