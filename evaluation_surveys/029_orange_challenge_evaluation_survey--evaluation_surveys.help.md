# Orange Challenge Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from participants of the Orange Challenge event. It aims to collect information on how participants experienced the event, their satisfaction, and areas for improvement. Please complete this form to help us understand your experience and provide valuable insights for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. **Surname**: Enter your surname in the "Surname" field.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Ensure it's correct to receive any updates or future contact from us.
4. **Rating Score**: Answer the "How would you rate this event?" question using the scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
5. **Feedback**: Provide your thoughts and opinions on the event in the "Feedback" field.
6. **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions you have for improving the event in the "Suggestions" field.
7. **Rating Quality**: Answer the "How would you rate the quality of this event?" question using the scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
8. **Satisfaction**: Answer the "How satisfied are you with this event?" question using the scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
9. **Areas of Improvement**: Share any areas where you think we can improve in the "Areas of Improvement" field.
10. **Contact Information**: Enter any contact information you'd like to share with us in the "Contact Information" field.
11. **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. (Note: This field is not required.)
12. **Category**: Select the category that best describes the event you attended from the "Category" dropdown list.
13. **Select Multiple**: Choose all the options that best describe what you liked most about the event from the "Select Multiple" field.
14. **Date**: Enter the date you attended the event in the "Date" field.
15. **Time**: Enter the time you attended the event in the "Time" field.
16. **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience in the "Note" field.
17. **Email Repeat**: Re-enter your email address for any specific purpose or updates in the "Email Repeat" field. (Note: This field is intended for participants who already provided an email address.)
18. **Phone Repeat**: Re-enter your phone number in the "Phone Repeat" field. (Note: This field is not required.)
19. **Category Repeat**: Re-select the category that best describes the event from the "Category Repeat" field.
20. **Select Multiple Repeat**: Re-select all the options that best describe what you liked most about the event from the "Select Multiple Repeat" field.
21. **Date Repeat**: Re-enter the date you attended the event in the "Date Repeat" field.
22. **Time Repeat**: Re-enter the time you attended the event in the "Time Repeat" field.
23. **Note Repeat**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience in the "Note Repeat" field.
24. **Rating Score Repeat**: Answer the "How would you rate this event?" question using the scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
25. **Improvement Areas Repeat**: Share any areas where you think we can improve in the "Improvement Areas Repeat" field.
