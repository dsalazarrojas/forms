# finance_policy_adoption_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the effectiveness and adoption of financial policies within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your personal experience and knowledge.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
3. Do not duplicate or repeat answers.
4. Complete all questions that are relevant to your experience and knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Finance Policy Understanding** (finance_policy_understanding, text, required: false): This question aims to gauge your understanding of the financial policies within the organization. Please provide a brief description of your understanding of these policies.
* **Policy Implementation** (policy_implementation, text, required: false): This question seeks to understand how well the policies are being implemented within the organization. Describe your experiences with policy implementation.
* **Policy Challenges** (policy_challenges, text, required: false): This question is meant to identify any difficulties or obstacles that hinder the effective adoption of financial policies. Please list any challenges you've faced.
* **Finance Policy Adoption** (finance_policy_adoption, number, required: false): This question asks you to estimate the level of adoption of financial policies within the organization. Use a scale of 1-5 to rate your estimation.
* **Finance Departments Involvement** (finance_depts_involvement, select_one, required: false): This question is to gauge the involvement of finance departments in the adoption and implementation of financial policies. Please select 'Yes' if finance departments are involved or 'No' if not.
* **Employee Feedback** (employee_feedback, select_multiple, required: false): This question is to understand the feedback provided by employees on financial policies. Please select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Policy Awareness** (policy_awareness, note, required: false): This field is for any comments or thoughts on policy awareness.
* **Compliance Teams Involvement** (compliance_teams_involvement, select_multiple, required: false): This question is to gauge the involvement of compliance teams in the adoption and implementation of financial policies. Please select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Employee Support** (employee_support, select_one, required: false): This question is to understand the level of support provided to employees on financial policies. Please select 'Yes' if employees receive support or 'No' if not.
