# Process Effectiveness Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a process. It has 10 sections, each covering a different aspect of the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Provide honest and thoughtful feedback in the text boxes provided.
3. Use the space provided to elaborate on your answers.
4. Do not leave any sections blank if possible.
5. If a section does not apply to you or your role in the process, just mention it in the notes provided.
