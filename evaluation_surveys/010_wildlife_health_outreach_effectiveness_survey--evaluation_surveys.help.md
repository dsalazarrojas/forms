# Wildlife Health Outreach Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of the wildlife health outreach program. Your responses will help us evaluate the program's impact on your knowledge and understanding of wildlife health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions based on your actual experience with the outreach session.
2. Be honest and provide accurate information.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date (session_date)**: Record the date of the outreach session you attended.
* **Session Time (session_time)**: Record the time of the outreach session you attended.
* **Knowledge Score Before (knowledge_score_before)**: Rate your initial knowledge level regarding wildlife health before attending the outreach session.
* **Knowledge Score After (knowledge_score_after)**: Rate your knowledge level after attending the outreach session, to measure the improvement.
* **Session Attendance (session_attendance)**: Choose how you attended the outreach session (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4).
* **Comments (comments)**: Provide any additional comments about your experience with the outreach program.
