# playwright_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the quality of a script.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Script Name.
2. Enter your Evaluator Name.
3. Choose the Date when you reviewed the script.
4. Choose the Time when you reviewed the script.
5. Select the Evaluation Script option that best describes your evaluation.
6. Select your Evaluator Role.
7. Provide Feedback on your evaluation.
8. Indicate whether the Evaluation Form has been completed.
9. Provide any additional Comments.
10. Choose your level of Evaluator Experience.
11. Indicate whether you received Evaluator Training.
12. Provide your Evaluator Rating.
13. Provide any Evaluator Comments.
14. Choose the time when you completed the Evaluation Form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Script Name**: Enter the name of the script being evaluated.
    * A text field for you to enter the script name.
- **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name as the evaluator.
    * A text field for you to enter your name.
- **Date**: Choose the date when you reviewed the script.
    * A date field with a calendar picker or date input field.
- **Time**: Choose the time when you reviewed the script.
    * A time field with a time picker or time input field.
- **Evaluation Script**: Select the option that best describes your evaluation.
    * A select one field with options to choose from.
- **Evaluator Role**: Select your role in the evaluation process.
    * A select multiple field with options to choose from.
- **Feedback**: Provide your feedback on the evaluation.
    * A text field for you to enter your feedback.
- **Evaluation Form Completed**: Indicate whether the form has been completed.
    * A select one field with "True" or "False" options.
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments on the evaluation.
    * A text field for you to enter your comments.
- **Evaluator Experience**: Choose your level of experience as an evaluator.
    * A number field for you to select your experience level.
- **Evaluator Training**: Indicate whether you received any training as an evaluator.
    * A select multiple field with "Yes" or "No" options.
- **Evaluator Rating**: Provide your rating of the script.
    * A number field for you to enter your rating.
- **Evaluator Comments**: Provide any additional comments on your evaluation.
    * A text field for you to enter your comments.
- **Evaluation Form Completed At**: Choose the date when you completed the form.
    * A date field with a calendar picker or date input field.
- **Evaluation Form Completed Time**: Choose the time when you completed the form.
    * A time field with a time picker or time input field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Review the script carefully before providing your evaluation.
- Be honest and accurate in your evaluation.
