# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey is used to gather feedback from users about their experience with a gym trainer. The form helps the trainers to improve their performance and better serve their clients' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a rating for the trainer's professionalism, communication skills, and overall satisfaction. For the motivation field, choose "High" or "Other".
2. Enter any additional comments you have about your experience with the trainer.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Rate the trainer's overall time spent on the session (evaluation_time).
6. Enter any additional comments, if desired.
7. Repeat steps 3-6 for the second evaluation.
8. Enter the date of the submission of this evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainer Professionalism** (`trainer_professionalism`, number, required): Rate the trainer's professionalism on a scale of 1-10.
* **Trainer Communication** (`trainer_communication`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the trainer's communication skills.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, select_one, required): This field has two options: "High" and "Other". Select "High" if you were motivated to exercise regularly or achieve your fitness goals. Select "Other" if you have a different reason for your motivation.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the trainer's services on a scale.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the trainer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Rate the trainer's overall time spent on the session on a scale.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments, if desired.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number 2** (`phone_number_2`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Rating Scale 2** (`rating_scale_2`, number, optional): Rate the trainer's overall performance on a scale.
* **Evaluation Date 2** (`evaluation_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time 2** (`evaluation_time_2`, time, optional): Rate the trainer's overall time spent on the session on a scale.
* **Additional Comments 3** (`additional_comments_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments, if desired.
* **Email 3** (`email_3`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number 3** (`phone_number_3`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Rating Scale 3** (`rating_scale_3`, number, optional): Rate the trainer's overall performance on a scale.
* **Evaluation Date 3** (`evaluation_date_3`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time 3** (`evaluation_time_3`, time, optional): Rate the trainer's overall time spent on the session on a scale.
* **Additional Comments 4** (`additional_comments_4`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments, if desired.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission of this evaluation.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide detailed feedback about your experience with the trainer.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
