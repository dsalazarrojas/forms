# Treasury Risk Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Risk Awareness Survey is designed to help identify and categorize potential risks within the treasury function. This survey is a crucial tool for maintaining risk awareness and mitigation within the department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the risk categories and select the ones that apply to your situation.
2. Select the financial institutions involved in the risk.
3. Choose the risk types that apply.
4. Assess the risk impact and mitigation.
5. Provide any additional comments or information about the risk.
6. Ensure all required fields are completed before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Risk Categories** (`page_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all risk categories that apply to the risk you are reporting. This may include operational, financial, strategic, or other risks.
* **Financial Institutions** (`page_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Identify the financial institutions involved in the risk, such as banks, brokers, or other third-party providers.
* **Risk Types** (`page_4`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the types of risk associated with the risk you are reporting. This may include liquidity, credit, market, or other risk types.
* **Internal Risk** (`page_5`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the risk types that apply to internal risk factors.
* **External Risk** (`page_6`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the risk types that apply to external risk factors.
* **Risk Impact** (`page_7`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the impact level of the risk. This may include high, medium, or low risk impact.
* **Risk Mitigation** (`page_8`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the risk mitigation strategies implemented to address the risk.
* **Additional Risk** (`page_9`, select_one, required: false): Identify any additional risk factors that are not listed in the risk categories.
* **Other Risks** (`page_10`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any other risk factors that are not listed.
* **Risk Severity** (`page_11`, number, required: false): Assess the severity level of the risk, using a numerical value.
* **Risk Priority** (`page_12`, number, required: false): Select the priority level of the risk, using a numerical value.
* **Risk Status** (`page_13`, select_multiple, required: false): Identify the status of the risk, whether it is active or inactive.
* **Timeline** (`page_14`, date, required: false): Record the submission date for the risk report.
* **Frequency** (`page_15`, time, required: false): Estimate the frequency of the risk occurrence.
* **Risk Owner** (`page_16`, text, required: false): Identify the risk owner or responsible party.
* **Risk Description** (`page_17`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the risk.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_18`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about the risk.
* **Risk Category** (`page_19`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the risk.
* **Risk Type** (`page_20`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of risk associated with the risk.
* **Financial Institution** (`page_21`, select_one, required: false): Identify the financial institution involved in the risk.
* **Risk Owner** (`page_22`, select_one, required: false): Identify the risk owner or responsible party.
* **Risk Description** (`page_23`, select_one, required: false): Select the description of the risk.
* **Comments** (`page_24`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about the risk.
* **Submission Date** (`page_25`, date, required: false): Record the date of submission for the risk report.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submission.
* Review and verify the accuracy of the information provided.
* Use the risk categories and types consistently across all risk reports.
* Keep the risk descriptions brief and concise.
* Use the submission date to track progress and changes over time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or risk management team.
