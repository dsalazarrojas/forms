# Wildlife Care Training Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of wildlife care training, allowing participants to provide their ratings and comments on their experience. The goal is to gather feedback that will help improve future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and provide your honest feedback.
2. For rating questions, select the answer that best reflects your opinion.
3. In the comments section, provide any additional information or suggestions you have for the training.
4. Enter your email address and phone number, so we can follow up with you if needed.
5. Fill in your participant name and ID, if applicable.
6. Select the date of your training session.
7. Enter the name and email of the training facilitator.
8. Finally, select the date of this evaluation survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wildlife Care Training Evaluation Survey (1)**: This is a text field where you can briefly describe your experience with the training.
* **How would you rate the training? (2)**: This is a rating scale with 4 options: Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor. Select the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **Comments (3)**: This is a comment field where you can provide any additional feedback or suggestions about the training.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you if required.
* **Participant Name (6)**: Enter your name, if you are a registered participant.
* **Participant ID (7)**: Enter your participant ID, if you are a registered participant.
* **Date of Training (8)**: Select the date of the training session you attended.
* **Facilitator Name (9)**: Enter the name of the training facilitator.
* **Facilitator Email (10)**: Enter the email of the training facilitator.
* **Date of Evaluation (11)**: Select the date of this evaluation survey.
* **Participant Notes (12)**: This is a comment field where you can provide any additional information or suggestions about the training.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, use the comments field.
* Make sure to enter your email and phone number, so we can contact you if needed.
* Double-check your answers and contact information before submitting the form.
