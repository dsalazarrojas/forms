# Consulting Expertise Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate and recognize expertise in a specific domain or area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Consulting Expertise Recognition Survey, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form. These are:
    *   Nominee name
    *   Expertise area
    *   Evidence of expertise
    *   Endorsement level
*   For the optional field, "Impact of work", you can provide more context about the nominee's contributions if you wish.
*   For the "Endorser name" field, please enter the name of the person submitting the endorsement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nominee name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person being evaluated for expertise.
*   **Expertise area** (`expertise_area`, text, required): Describe the specific domain or area of recognized expertise.
*   **Evidence of expertise** (`evidence_of_expertise`, text, required): Provide examples of the nominee's significant contributions or achievements that demonstrate their expertise.
*   **Impact of work** (`impact_of_work`, text, optional): Describe the measurable impact of the nominee's work, if you have any additional information to share.
*   **Endorsement level** (`endorsement_level`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to indicate the level of endorsement for recognition:
    *   Strong endorsement
    *   Moderate endorsement
    *   Endorse
    *   Do not endorse
*   **Endorser name** (`endorser_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the endorsement.

## Tips

*   Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct nominee.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the nominee or the relevant department/committee for guidance.
*   Please be specific and honest when providing endorsements to help the nominee and the organization accurately assess their expertise.
