# Transformation Consulting Success Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Transformation Consulting Success Survey" is designed to assess the overall performance of a transformation consulting project, helping us understand the effectiveness of our consulting efforts, identify areas for improvement, and inform future projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Choose the options that best describe your experience with the project.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please explain your response in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the project impact** (`project_impact`):
	+ Select the option that best describes the project's impact:
		- High: The project had a significant impact on the business or organization.
		- Medium: The project had a moderate impact on the business or organization.
		- Low: The project had a minimal impact on the business or organization.
* **Client satisfaction** (`client_satisfaction`):
	+ This question is required to gauge how satisfied you were with our consulting services.
* **Consultant satisfaction** (`consultant_satisfaction`):
	+ This question is required to gauge how satisfied you were with our consulting team's performance.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`):
	+ Please provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the project.
* **Client email** (`email`):
	+ Please provide your email address so we can contact you for further feedback or clarification.
* **Client phone** (`phone`):
	+ Please provide your phone number for any further questions or discussions.
* **Project start date** (`project_start_date`):
	+ Enter the date when the project started.
* **Project end date** (`project_end_date`):
	+ Enter the date when the project ended.
* **Time spent on project** (`time_spent_on_project`):
	+ Enter the number of hours or days you spent on the project.
* **Time spent on project (preferred)** (`time_spent_on_project_preferred`):
	+ If you had a preferred number of hours or days to spend on the project, enter it here.
* **Team size** (`team_size`):
	+ Select the number of team members involved in the project.
* **Team diversity** (`team_diversity`):
	+ Select the level of diversity within the team:
		- Low: Low team diversity (similar skills and experience).
		- Medium: Moderate team diversity (mix of skills and experience).
		- High: High team diversity (strongly diverse team).
* **Team collaboration** (`team_collaboration`):
	+ Select the level of collaboration within the team:
		- High: Strong team collaboration.
		- Medium: Moderate team collaboration.
		- Low: Low team collaboration.
* **Project complexity** (`project_complexity`):
	+ Select the level of project complexity:
		- Low: Simple project with few dependencies.
		- Medium: Moderate project complexity with some dependencies.
		- High: Highly complex project with many dependencies.
		- Very High: Extremely complex project with many intricate dependencies.
* **Team skills** (`team_skills`):
	+ Select the level of skills within the team:
		- Low: Limited skills and experience.
		- Medium: Moderate skills and experience.
		- High: Strong skills and experience.
* **Team experience** (`team_experience`):
	+ Select the level of experience within the team:
		- Low: Limited experience.
		- Medium: Moderate experience.
		- High: Strong experience.
* **Communication effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`):
	+ Select the level of communication effectiveness:
		- Low: Poor communication.
		- Medium: Moderate communication.
		- High: Strong communication.
* **Project scope** (`project_scope`):
	+ Select the level of project scope:
		- Low: Simple project with narrow scope.
		- Medium: Moderate project scope.
		- High: Broad project scope.
* **Project duration** (`project_duration`):
	+ Select the length of the project:
		- Less than 1 week.
		- 1-2 weeks.
		- 2-4 weeks.
		- More than 4 weeks.
* **Project scope deviation** (`project_scope_deviation`):
	+ Select the level of project scope deviation:
		- Low: No deviation from project scope.
		- Medium: Moderate deviation from project scope.
		- High: Significant deviation from project scope.
* **Consultant quality** (`consultant_quality`):
	+ Select the level of quality from our consulting team:
		- Low: Poor quality.
		- Medium: Moderate quality.
		- High: Strong quality.
* **Team quality** (`team_quality`):
	+ Select the level of quality within the team:
		- Low: Limited quality.
		- Medium: Moderate quality.
		- High: Strong quality.
* **Communication quality** (`communication_quality`):
	+ Select the level of communication quality:
		- Low: Poor communication.
		- Medium: Moderate communication.
		- High: Strong communication.
* **Client quality** (`client_quality`):
	+ Select the level of quality from the client:
		- Low: Poor quality.
		- Medium: Moderate quality.
		- High: Strong quality.
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback2`):
	+ Please provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the project.

## Tips

* Take your time to answer each question carefully, ensuring your responses are accurate and thorough.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our consulting services, so please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
