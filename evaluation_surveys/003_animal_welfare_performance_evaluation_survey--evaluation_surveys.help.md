# Animal Welfare Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the performance of animal welfare facilities, such as animal shelters, clinics, and sanctuaries, in maintaining high standards of care for animals. The evaluation assesses the quality of animal housing, nutrition, health, sanitation, enrichment, and other aspects of care. This information helps identify areas of improvement and provides recommendations for corrective actions to be taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Section 1: Evaluator Information**
	* Please provide your name, role at the organization, and date of evaluation.
2. **Section 2: Facility Information**
	* Provide the name of the facility being evaluated and select its type (e.g., animal shelter, veterinary clinic, etc.).
3. **Section 3: Animal Care Standards**
	* Rate the quality of:
		+ Housing and Living Conditions
		+ Nutrition and Feeding Practices
		+ Health and Medical Care
		+ Sanitation and Hygiene
		+ Enrichment and Socialization
	* Select the most positive aspects of the facility's strengths.
4. **Section 4: Staff and Operations**
	* Evaluate:
		+ Staff Competency and Training
		+ Safety and Emergency Protocols
		+ Record Keeping and Documentation
5. **Section 5: Additional Information**
	* Identify specific areas for improvement and recommended corrective actions.
	* Suggest a date for a follow-up evaluation.
6. **Section 6: Overall Status**
	* Rate the facility's overall compliance with animal welfare standards.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, `text`, required): Provide your full name as an evaluator.
* **Your Role at the Organization** (`evaluator_role`, `text`, required): Enter your position or title within the organization.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this assessment.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the animal shelter or clinic being evaluated.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of facility (e.g., animal shelter, veterinary clinic, etc.).
* **Housing and Living Conditions** (`housing_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of animal housing and living conditions.
* **Nutrition and Feeding Practices** (`nutrition_standards`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of animal nutrition and feeding practices.
* **Health and Medical Care** (`health_medical_care`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of medical care and health services.
* **Sanitation and Hygiene** (`sanitation_hygiene`, `select_one`, required): Rate cleanliness and sanitation practices.
* **Enrichment and Socialization** (`enrichment_socialization`, `select_one`, required): Rate animal enrichment and interaction activities.
* **Staff Competency and Training** (`staff_competency`, `select_one`, required): Rate staff knowledge and expertise.
* **Safety and Emergency Protocols** (`safety_protocols`, `select_one`, required): Rate safety procedures and emergency readiness.
* **Record Keeping and Documentation** (`record_keeping`, `select_one`, required): Rate animal records and documentation practices.
* **Facility's Greatest Strengths** (`strengths_areas`, `text`, optional): Describe the most positive aspects of the facility.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): Identify specific areas needing attention.
* **Recommended Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, `text`, optional): Provide specific steps to address issues.
* **Recommended Follow-Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Suggest a date for a follow-up evaluation.
* **Overall Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, `select_one`, required): Rate overall compliance with animal welfare standards.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any other observations or notes.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when evaluating each aspect of the facility.
* Provide specific examples or descriptions to support your ratings.
* Use the strengths and areas for improvement to suggest corrective actions.
* Consider the overall impact on animal welfare when evaluating the facility.
* Provide clear and concise answers in each section.
* Proofread carefully to ensure accurate submission.
