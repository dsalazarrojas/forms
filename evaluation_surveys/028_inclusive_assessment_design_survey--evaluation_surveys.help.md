# Inclusive Assessment Design Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from educators on the assessment design process, focusing on its inclusivity and effectiveness. The goal is to gather insights from educators who have experience with the assessment design and can provide informed opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and based on your experience and knowledge.
2. You may choose multiple options for the question "What roles do educators play in the assessment process?"
3. For questions with text fields, provide a brief description or example.
4. Select the options that best match your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What roles do educators play in the assessment process?** (`educator_roles`): This question aims to understand the different roles that educators take on when designing and implementing assessments. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **How would you describe your experience with the current assessment design?** (`experience`): This question asks about your hands-on experience with the assessment design process. Please provide a brief description of your experience, highlighting what you have learned and what you would like to improve.
* **What would you rate the overall quality of the assessment?** (`ratings`): This question rates the quality of the assessment based on your experience. Please rate it on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What strategies do you use when designing assessments?** (`strategies`): This question is open-ended and asks about the strategies you use when designing assessments. Please provide a brief description or example of your approach.
* **What is your feedback on the assessment feedback mechanism?** (`feedback`): This question asks about your thoughts on the feedback mechanism for the assessment. Please provide a brief description or example of what you think works well or could be improved.
* **How often do you use assessments to inform your work?** (`frequency`): This question asks about how frequently you use assessments to inform your work. Please select the option that best matches your practice.
* **How would you rate the assessment's ability to capture diverse perspectives?** (`diverse_perspectives`): This question rates the assessment's ability to capture diverse perspectives. Please select the option that best matches your opinion.
* **Have you ever received feedback on your assessments?** (`received_feedback`): This question asks whether you have ever received feedback on your assessments. Please select the option that best matches your experience.
* **How often do you receive feedback on your assessments?** (`received_feedback_frequency`): This question asks about how frequently you receive feedback on your assessments. Please select the option that best matches your experience.
* **What rating would you give to the assessment's ability to accommodate different needs?** (`inclusivity_rating`): This question rates the assessment's ability to accommodate different needs. Please select the option that best matches your opinion.
* **How would you rate the assessment's effectiveness in promoting inclusivity?** (`inclusivity_effectiveness`): This question rates the assessment's effectiveness in promoting inclusivity. Please select the option that best matches your opinion.
* **What strategies do you employ when designing assessments to be more inclusive?** (`inclusive_strategies`): This question is open-ended and asks about the strategies you use when designing assessments to be more inclusive. Please provide a brief description or example of your approach.
