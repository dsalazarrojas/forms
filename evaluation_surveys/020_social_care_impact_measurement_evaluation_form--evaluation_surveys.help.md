# Social Care Impact Measurement Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help social care service providers evaluate the impact of their services on their users. It collects data on various aspects of the service experience, including the quality of support, staff responsiveness, clarity of support options, and the overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction** (Page 1): This is a brief note providing context to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
2. **Service User Information** (Page 2):
	* **Service user**: Enter a brief description of the service user or user group you are evaluating.
3. **Service Provider Information** (Page 2):
	* **Service provider**: Enter a brief description of the service provider or organization being evaluated.
4. **Impact Areas** (Page 3):
	* **Impact areas**: Select one or more of the options that best describe the areas of impact of the service:
		+ Service user receives help with daily activities
		+ Service user gains increased independence
		+ Service user receives support with personal care
5. **Evaluation Questions** (Page 4):
	* **Evaluation questions**: Select one or more of the questions that best describe your experience with the service. Please answer them based on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not satisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied". The questions are:
		+ What was the quality of the support provided?
		+ How responsive were the staff?
		+ How clear were the support options?
		+ How well did your needs and preferences are met?
		+ Was your experience with the service user friendly?
6. **Evaluation Scale** (Page 6):
	* **Evaluation scale**: This is an optional field for you to provide a numerical value to your evaluation. Please enter a value from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not satisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".
7. **Evaluation Comments** (Page 7):
	* **Evaluation comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have on your experience with the service.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and based on your actual experience with the service.
* Please select only one or more options that best describe the impact areas and evaluation questions.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the evaluation comments section.
