# Consulting Impact Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure the impact of a consulting engagement on your client. It will help you capture key information about the client, project details, and outcomes achieved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are familiar with the client's project and engagement details.
2. Select the "Client Information" section and fill out the client's full legal name, email, and phone number.
3. Fill out the project name, start and end dates, and select the engagement type from the provided options.
4. Identify the primary objective of the project and select it from the options provided.
5. Enter an estimated value generated by the project if available.
6. Evaluate the overall outcome of the project by selecting the relevant rating from the options provided.
7. If applicable, describe the most valuable outcome achieved.
8. Select all impact areas where the project had a measurable impact.
9. Provide a satisfaction score out of 10 for the project.
10. Optionally, provide qualitative feedback and comments about the project's outcome and lessons learned.
11. Grant consent for the anonymized data to be shared for case studies.
12. Enter your name as the person submitting or responsible for the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`client_name`): Full legal name of the client organization or individual.
* **Client email** (`client_email`): Primary contact email for follow-up.
* **Project name** (`project_name`): Title or brief identifier for the engagement.
* **Project start date** (`project_start_date`): When the engagement began.
* **Project end date** (`project_end_date`): When the engagement concluded or is expected to conclude.
* **Engagement type** (`engagement_type`): Select the category that best describes the engagement from the provided options.
* **Primary objective** (`primary_objective`): Select the main objective for this engagement from the options provided.
* **Estimated value generated** (`estimated_value_generated`): Provide an approximate monetary value if available, in your local currency.
* **Outcome rating** (`outcome_rating`): Overall assessment of the engagement outcome, select from the provided options.
* **Most valuable outcome** (`most_valuable_outcome`): Brief description of the most important result achieved.
* **Impact areas** (`impact_areas`): Select all areas where the engagement had a measurable impact from the provided options.
* **Satisfaction score** (`satisfaction_score`): 1-10 rating, where 1 is lowest, and 10 is highest.
* **Qualitative feedback and comments** (`qualitative_feedback`): Provide feedback and comments about the project's outcome and lessons learned.
* **Consent to share** (`consent_to_share`): Grant consent for the anonymized data to be used for case studies.
* **Consultant name** (`consultant_name`): Person submitting or responsible for the report.

This guide will help you complete the form accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your project lead or internal team for assistance.
