# Financial Control Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Control Review Survey is a tool used to evaluate the effectiveness of internal controls in your area of responsibility. This form helps identify potential financial risks and areas for improvement, allowing you to strengthen your financial control environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and provide honest feedback.
2. Answer each question based on your actual experience and knowledge.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Control Review Survey Form** (Text field, Not Required):
	+ This field is the title of the form, which should be kept as is for proper identification.
* **Control Effectiveness** (Number field, Not Required):
	+ Rate the effectiveness of controls in your area of responsibility on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Financial Risks** (Select Multiple field, Not Required):
	+ Identify the financial risks you have encountered or are concerned about. Select all that apply from the list:
		+ Risk 1
		+ Risk 2
		+ Risk 3
* **Improvement Ideas** (Text field, Not Required):
	+ Provide any suggestions or ideas you have to strengthen the financial control environment.
* **Risk Priority** (Number field, Not Required):
	+ Rate the priority of each identified financial risk on a scale from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or support.
* Please submit your form when you have completed it, and ensure that it is reviewed and approved by your supervisor or relevant authorities.
