# Therapist Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and evaluation of a therapist's performance. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions and provide your honest opinions to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly, providing your real thoughts and opinions.
2. You can answer all or some of the questions, depending on your experience with the therapist.
3. You can add any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Feedback" section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the therapist you are evaluating.
* **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Overall Evaluation** (`overall_evaluation`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the therapist's performance (e.g., 1-5).
* **Therapist Strengths** (`therapist_strengths`, select_one, optional): Select the strengths of the therapist (e.g., "Strengths" or "None").
* **Therapist Weaknesses** (`therapist_weaknesses`, select_multiple, optional): Select the weaknesses of the therapist (e.g., "Strengths", "Weaknesses", or "None").
* **Areas For Improvement** (`therapist_improvement`, select_one, optional): Select areas where the therapist could improve (e.g., "Strengths", "Weaknesses", or "None").
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the therapist's communication (e.g., 1-5).
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Please add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the therapist.
* **Client Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm that you have completed this evaluation.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you completed this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Select the date you received care from the therapist.
* **Client Relationship** (`client_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the therapist.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, number, optional): Enter the length of time you received care from the therapist.
* **Evaluation Period Start Date** (`evaluation_period_start`, date, optional): Select the start date of your evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Period End Date** (`evaluation_period_end`, time, optional): Select the end date of your evaluation period.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **License** (`license`, text, optional): Enter your therapist's license number.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the practice where you received care from the therapist.
* **Evaluation Form ID** (`evaluation_form_id`, text, optional): Enter the unique ID of this evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Form Version** (`evaluation_form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of this evaluation form.

Please remember to sign and date your evaluation to confirm that it is complete and accurate.
