# Health Equity Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from evaluators on the Health Equity Initiative program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Use the information provided in the Health Equity Assessment section to guide your answers in the Program Details section.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Form** (`Evaluation Form`, `note`, optional): This is a placeholder page to introduce the form and provide any additional instructions or information.
* **Health Equity Assessment** (`Health Equity Assessment`, `number`, optional): This section asks you to assess the program's goals and outcomes based on a scale of 1-10. Please provide your honest assessment of the program's goals and outcomes.
* **Program Details** (`Program Details`, `text`, optional): Here, you can provide more general information about the program, such as its description, objectives, and any other relevant details.
* **Program Goals** (`Program Goals`, `select multiple`, optional): Please select all the goals that apply to the program. This will help us understand the program's objectives and priorities.
* **Program Outcomes** (`Program Outcomes`, `select one`, optional): Select the outcome that best describes the program's results.
* **Form Submission** (`Form Submission`, `date`, optional): This is where you can record the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`Evaluation Criteria`, `time`, optional): This section is currently not used in this form.
* **Evaluator Comments** (`Evaluator Comments`, `text`, optional): Here, you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Evaluator Signature** (`Evaluator Signature`, `email`, optional): This is where you can provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Evaluator Contact Info** (`Evaluator Contact Info`, `text`, optional): This section is meant to be filled out with your contact information.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, `date`, optional): This section is used to record the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, `time`, optional): This section is not used in this form.
* **Evaluator** (`Evaluator`, `text`, optional): Here, you can provide information about yourself, such as your name and title.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
