# Academic Text Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Text Evaluation Survey is a tool used to evaluate the quality and suitability of academic texts, such as papers or manuscripts, submitted for publication. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, explaining the purpose and requirements of each field.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the text**: Before starting the survey, carefully read and review the text under evaluation, taking note of its content, structure, and overall quality.
2. **Provide required information**: Ensure that you fill out all required fields, marked by an asterisk (\*) in the label or instructions.
3. **Rate and provide feedback**: For select_one questions, choose the answer that best reflects your evaluation. For text fields, provide detailed and constructive feedback, explaining your reasoning and suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Reviewer Information
* **Name of Reviewer**: Enter your full name and title, as it will appear with your evaluation.
* **Area of Expertise**: Select your primary academic field of expertise, as it relates to the text under review.
*   _Optional_: If you have any additional information or context about your review, you can provide it here, but it is not required.

### Manuscript Details
* **Title of the Text or Manuscript**: Enter the full title of the document under review.
* **Author ID or Pseudonym**: If the author has a pseudonym or ID for blind review purposes, enter it here.
*   _Optional_: If you have any additional information about the manuscript, you can provide it here, but it is not required.

### Evaluation Criteria
*   _Note_: This section is for your reference, but not for filling out. It outlines the criteria used in the evaluation.
*   _Rate the Clarity and Coherence_: Assess the logical flow and readability of the text.
*   _Rate the Methodological Rigor_: Evaluate the appropriateness of the methods used.
*   _Rate the Originality and Contribution to the Field_: Assess the significance, modality, or contribution of the text to the field.
*   _Rate the Quality of Writing and Grammar_: Evaluate the adherence to formal standards of academic writing.
*   _Optional_: You can add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

### Recommendations
*   _Key Strengths_: Describe what the work did well.
*   _Weaknesses and Revisions_: Identify areas for improvement.
*   _Final Recommendation_: Choose the best option for your final decision.
*   _Priority for Publication_: Assess the editorial urgency of the text.
