# event_evaluation_survey_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is an event evaluation survey designed to collect feedback from attendees. The purpose of this form is to gather information on their overall satisfaction with the event, the value of different sessions, and their likelihood to attend future events. Their feedback will help us improve future events and better meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Take a few minutes to review each question carefully.
* Answer each question honestly and thoughtfully.
* You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied were you with the event overall?**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the event. Please select one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Which sessions did you find most valuable? (Select all that apply)**: This question asks you to select all the sessions that you found most valuable during the event. Please select as many options as apply from: Keynote Presentation, Workshop A, Panel Discussion, Networking Session, Product Demo.
* **Was the event well organized and structured?**: This question is a simple yes or no question to gauge your perception of the event's organization.
* **How likely are you to attend our future events?**: This question asks about your likelihood of attending future events. Please select one of the five options: Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely.
* **What was the most impactful part of the event for you?**: This question allows you to share your thoughts on what made the event impactful for you.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for improvement?**: This text field is for any additional comments or suggestions you might have. We value your feedback and look forward to hearing your thoughts.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible in your answers to help us understand your perspective.
* If you have any difficulty filling out the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! It will help us improve future events and better serve you.
