# Supervised Practice Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supervised Practice Evaluation form is used to gather feedback on a trainee's performance during a supervised practice session. This form is intended for supervisors to evaluate trainees on their strengths and areas for improvement, while also providing feedback on their performance, session details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field, as required or as relevant to the session being evaluated.
2. Enter text responses in the relevant text fields (e.g. Session Feedback, Areas for Improvement, Supervisor Feedback).
3. Select relevant options from the dropdown menus (e.g. Strengths, Trainee Performance, Session Type).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Feedback** (1): Enter a brief description of the session, including any notable events or issues that occurred.
* **Performance Rating** (2): Rate the trainee's performance on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Session Goals** (3): Enter a brief description of the session goals, including any specific objectives or outcomes.
* **Strengths** (4): Select one or more strengths observed during the session (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Areas for Improvement** (5): Enter one or more areas for improvement, including specific suggestions or recommendations.
* **Supervisor Feedback** (6): Enter any feedback or comments from the supervisor.
* **Trainee Performance** (7): Select one or more observations of the trainee's performance (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Date Completed** (8): Enter the date the session was completed.
* **Time Completed** (9): Enter the time the session was completed.
* **Email** (10): Enter the trainee's email address.
* **Phone** (11): Enter the trainee's phone number.
* **Supervisor Name** (12): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Title** (13): Enter the supervisor's title.
* **Notes** (14): Enter any additional notes from the supervisor.
* **Additional Feedback** (15): Enter any additional feedback or comments.
* **Session Duration** (16): Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **Session Type** (17): Select the type of session (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Session Date** (18): Enter the date the session was held.
* **Session Time** (19): Enter the time the session was held.
* **Session Location** (20): Enter the location of the session.
* **Trainee Name** (21): Enter the trainee's name.
* **Trainee Title** (22): Enter the trainee's title.
* **Trainee Position** (23): Enter the trainee's position.
* **Supervisor Notes** (24): Enter any additional notes from the supervisor.
* **Form Comments** (25): Enter any comments or feedback on the form itself.

## Tips
* Be sure to complete the form thoroughly and accurately, as it will help provide valuable feedback for the trainee's development.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the form's instructions or ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to be a comprehensive guide for evaluating a trainee's performance, so take your time and be thorough in your responses.
