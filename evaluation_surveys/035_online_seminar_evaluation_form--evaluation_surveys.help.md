# Online Seminar Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Seminar Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from attendees about the seminar they participated in. This form helps us understand their satisfaction with the seminar, assess the effectiveness of the seminar, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field based on your experience with the seminar.
2. Provide honest feedback about your experience with the seminar.
3. Use the provided text fields to add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the seminar you attended.
  - This field is necessary for tracking and recording seminar attendance.

- **Attendance** (`seminar_attendance`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
  - Present: I attended the seminar.
  - Absent: I did not attend the seminar.
  - Unable to attend: I was unable to attend due to unforeseen circumstances.
  - This field is necessary for determining seminar attendance.

- **Satisfaction with the Seminar** (`seminar_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
  - Very Good: I was extremely satisfied with the seminar.
  - Good: I was satisfied with the seminar.
  - Fair: I was neutral about the seminar.
  - Poor: I was dissatisfied with the seminar.
  - This field is necessary for determining overall seminar satisfaction.

- **Seminar Topics Covered** (`seminar_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the topics that were covered during the seminar.
  - Relevant: The topics were relevant to my needs.
  - Partially Relevant: The topics were somewhat relevant to my needs.
  - Not Relevant: The topics were not relevant to my needs.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand the seminar's content relevance.

- **Seminar Facilitator** (`seminar_facilitator`, text, optional): Provide feedback about the seminar facilitator, if any.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand the facilitator's performance.

- **Seminar Materials** (`seminar_materials`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the seminar materials that were provided, if any.
  - Useful: The materials were useful for me.
  - Fair: The materials were somewhat useful.
  - Not Enough: The materials were not enough for me.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand the seminar materials quality.

- **Seminar Location** (`seminar_location`, text, optional): Provide feedback about the seminar location, if any.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand the seminar location suitability.

- **Seminar Rating** (`seminar_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the seminar on a scale of 1-5.
  - This field is necessary for determining overall seminar satisfaction.

- **Comments** (`seminar_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the seminar.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your thoughts and suggestions.

- **Would you Recommend** (`seminar_recommend`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
  - True: I would recommend the seminar to others.
  - False: I would not recommend the seminar to others.
  - Unable to say: I am neutral about recommending the seminar.
  - This field is necessary for determining seminar recommendation.

- **Follow Up** (`seminar_followup`, date, optional): Provide the expected date for your next seminar attendance.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your future attendance plans.

- **Suggestions for Improvement** (`seminar_suggestions`, note, optional): Add any suggestions for improving the seminar.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand areas for improvement.

- **Next Seminar** (`seminar_next_seminar`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
  - Within 1 month: I expect to attend the next seminar within a month.
  - Within 3 months: I expect to attend the next seminar within 3 months.
  - Within 6 months: I expect to attend the next seminar within 6 months.
  - Never: I do not plan to attend the next seminar.
  - This field is necessary for determining next seminar attendance.

- **Additional Comments** (`seminar_additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the seminar.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your thoughts and suggestions.

- **Suggestions for Next Seminar** (`seminar_next_seminar_suggestions`, note, optional): Add any suggestions for improving the next seminar.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand areas for improvement.

- **Attendance Frequency** (`seminar_attendance_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the frequency that you would like to attend seminars, if any.
  - Every Month: I would like to attend seminars once a month.
  - Every 3 months: I would like to attend seminars every 3 months.
  - Every 6 months: I would like to attend seminars every 6 months.
  - Rarely: I do not plan to attend seminars often.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your seminar attendance frequency.

- **Suggestions for Attendance Frequency** (`seminar_attendance_frequency_suggestions`, note, optional): Add any suggestions for improving seminar attendance frequency.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand areas for improvement.

- **Suggestions for Frequency of Seminars** (`seminar_frequency_suggestions`, note, optional): Add any suggestions for improving seminar frequency.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand areas for improvement.

- **Comments** (`seminar_attendance_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about seminar attendance.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your thoughts and suggestions.

- **Next Seminar Frequency** (`seminar_frequency_next_seminar`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
  - Within 1 month: I expect to attend the next seminar within a month.
  - Within 3 months: I expect to attend the next seminar within 3 months.
  - Within 6 months: I expect to attend the next seminar within 6 months.
  - Never: I do not plan to attend the next seminar.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand next seminar attendance.

- **Additional Comments** (`seminar_additional_next_seminar`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about next seminar attendance.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand your thoughts and suggestions.

- **Additional Frequency Suggestion** (`seminar_additional_frequency`, note, optional): Add any suggestions for improving seminar frequency.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us understand areas for improvement.

- **Satisfaction Scale** (`seminar_satisfaction_scale`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the seminar on a scale of 1-5.
  - This field is necessary for determining overall seminar satisfaction.
