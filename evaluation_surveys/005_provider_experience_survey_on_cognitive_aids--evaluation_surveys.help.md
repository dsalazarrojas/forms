# Provider Experience Survey On Cognitive Aids - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience using cognitive aids in clinical settings. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the design and implementation of these aids.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions based on your experience with cognitive aids.
2. For questions with rating scales (e.g., easiness, effectiveness, etc.), select the rating that best reflects your experience.
3. For questions with free-text fields (e.g., challenges, comments), provide as much detail as possible.
4. You can skip questions that are not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider** (`provider_id`, number, optional):
    Enter your unique provider ID (if you have one).
* **Easiness** (`rating_easiness`, number, optional):
    Select how easy it was to use the cognitive aid (1 = very difficult, 5 = very easy).
* **Effectiveness** (`rating_effectiveness`, number, optional):
    Select how effective the cognitive aid was in supporting your work (1 = not effective at all, 5 = very effective).
* **Usefulness** (`rating_usefulness`, number, optional):
    Select how useful the cognitive aid was in your work (1 = not useful at all, 5 = very useful).
* **Clarity** (`rating_clarity`, number, optional):
    Select how clear the cognitive aid was in its instructions and expectations (1 = not clear at all, 5 = very clear).
* **Familiarity** (`rating_familiarity`, number, optional):
    Select how familiar you are with the cognitive aid (1 = not familiar at all, 5 = very familiar).
* **Satisfaction** (`rating_satisfaction`, number, optional):
    Select how satisfied you are with the cognitive aid (1 = not satisfied at all, 5 = very satisfied).
* **Challenges in Aid Use** (`challenges_aid_use`, text, optional):
    Provide any challenges you faced when using the cognitive aid (e.g., difficulty in understanding instructions, lack of resources).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
    Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the cognitive aid.
* **Frequency of Aid Use** (`rating_frequency_aid_use`, number, optional):
    Select how often you use the cognitive aid (1 = never, 5 = always).
* **Importance of Aid Use** (`rating_importance_aid_use`, number, optional):
    Select how important the cognitive aid is in your work (1 = not important at all, 5 = very important).
* **Difficulty** (`rating_difficulty`, text, optional):
    Provide any difficulties you faced when using the cognitive aid.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_multiple, optional):
    Select one or more of the following options to describe your overall experience: Very Good, Good, Fair.
* **Frequency** (`rating_frequency`, text, optional):
    Provide information about how frequently you use the cognitive aid.
* **Importance** (`rating_importance`, text, optional):
    Provide information about the importance of the cognitive aid in your work.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional):
    Enter your name as a provider.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional):
    Enter your department.
* **Clinical** (`rating_clinical`, number, optional):
    Select how clinical the cognitive aid was in its application (1 = not clinical at all, 5 = very clinical).
* **Challenges Provider Clinical** (`challenges_provider_clinical`, text, optional):
    Provide any challenges you faced in using the cognitive aid clinically.
* **Challenges Systemic** (`challenges_systemic`, text, optional):
    Provide any challenges you faced in using the cognitive aid systemically.
* **Usefulness Provider Clinical** (`rating_usefulness_provider_clinical`, number, optional):
    Select how useful the cognitive aid was in clinical settings (1 = not useful at all, 5 = very useful).

## Tips
* Please answer questions based on your actual experience with cognitive aids.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to contact us.
