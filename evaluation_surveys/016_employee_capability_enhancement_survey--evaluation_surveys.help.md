# Employee Capability Enhancement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Capability Enhancement Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on various aspects of their work experience. It helps to collect data on employee perceptions and experiences, which can inform HR or management decisions to improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select your current role from the list provided.
* Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5.
* Choose the department you belong to.
* Rate your job skills level on a scale from 1 to 5.
* Indicate if you are satisfied with your growth opportunities. Rate on a scale from 1 to 5.
* Specify your availability.
* Rate your satisfaction with growth on a scale from 1 to 5.
* Provide your phone number for contact information.
* Select a date (optional).
* Indicate if you have availability (Yes, No, or Other).
* Rate your satisfaction with growth (optional).
* Add any additional comments or notes.

    This field asks for your current role within the company. Please select from the provided options (Team Lead, Manager, Team Member, Other, None).
* **How would you rate your overall job satisfaction?** (`job_satisfaction`, `number`, required)
    Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Which department do you belong to?** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Choose all departments that you belong to (Sales, Marketing, Engineering, Operations, Other).
* **Can you rate your job skills level?** (`skills_level`, `number`, required)
    Rate your job skills level on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Are you satisfied with your growth opportunities?** (`growth_opportunities`, `number`, required)
    Rate your satisfaction with growth opportunities on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **What is your availability?** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Specify your availability (Yes, No, Other).
* **Satisfaction With Growth** (`satisfaction_with_growth`, `number`, required)
    Rate your satisfaction with growth on a scale from 1 to 5.

    Provide your phone number for contact information.

    Select a date (optional).

    Indicate if you have availability (Yes, No, Other).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required)
    Add any additional comments or notes.

Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled. Fields marked as optional allow you to provide additional information or skip them if you prefer.
