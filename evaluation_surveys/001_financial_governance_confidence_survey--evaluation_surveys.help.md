# Financial Governance Confidence Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help the company's leadership understand the confidence of end-users in the financial governance processes. It will help identify areas of strength and weakness, allowing for targeted improvements and enhancements to be made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer based on your experience and understanding of the company's financial governance processes.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best reflects your answer.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the survey administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Governance Confidence Survey** (`page_1`): This is the title of the survey.
* **Financial governance confidence** (`page_2`): This question gauges your confidence in the board's ability to make decisions.
* **How often do you feel that the board is actively involved in the decision-making process?** (`page_3`): Choose the answer that best reflects how often you feel the board is involved in the decision-making process.
* **How confident are you that the board will be able to provide the necessary resources and support to achieve its objectives?** (`page_4`): Choose the answer that best reflects your confidence in the board's ability to provide resources and support.
* **How often do you use the board's reports?** (`page_5`): Choose the answer that best reflects how often you use the board's reports.
* **What is the primary purpose of the board in your company?** (`page_6`): Choose the answer that best reflects the primary purpose of the board in your company.
* **What is your perception of the board's effectiveness in achieving its objectives?** (`page_7`): Choose the answer that best reflects your perception of the board's effectiveness.
* **What is the primary function of the board?** (`page_8`): Choose the answer that best reflects the primary function of the board.
* **How important is the board's involvement in financial matters?** (`page_9`): Enter the number that best reflects how important the board's involvement is.
* **What is your perception of the board's involvement in financial matters?** (`page_10`): Choose the answer that best reflects your perception of the board's involvement.
* **How much of the time do you spend using the board's reports?** (`page_11`): Choose the answer that best reflects how much time you spend using the board's reports.
* **What is the quality of the board's reports?** (`page_12`): Choose the answer that best reflects the quality of the board's reports.

## Tips
* Please answer based on your actual experience and understanding of the company's financial governance processes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the survey administrators.
* This survey is designed to be completed by end-users who are familiar with the company's financial governance processes. If you're not sure about any of the questions, please contact the survey administrators for clarification.
