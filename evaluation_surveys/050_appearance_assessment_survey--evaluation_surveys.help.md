# Appearance Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess the appearance of services provided by our specialists, such as hair styling, nail services, skin treatments, and makeup applications. The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on the services provided, including overall satisfaction, cleanliness, and professionalism. This feedback will help us improve our services and better serve our clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Enter your Client Full Name.
3. Select the date of your visit to our facility.
4. Choose the service you received from our specialists (e.g., hair styling, nail services, etc.).
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the final result on a 1 to 5 scale.
6. Indicate if the final result matched your initial consultation.
7. Rate the professionalism and skill of our specialist on a scale of 1 to 5.
8. Select the cleanliness and comfort of our facility (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
9. If not required, rate the quality of products used on a scale of Superior, Standard, or Sub-par.
10. Indicate how likely you are to return to our facility for another service on a 0 to 10 scale.
11. If not required, provide any feedback on what you liked most about your new look.
12. If not required, provide any areas where we can improve your experience.
13. Finally, indicate if you give permission to use photos of your look for our portfolio.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (client_name, Text, Optional): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the results.
* **Date of Visit** (visit_date, Date, Required): Select the date you visited our facility for your service.
* **Service Received** (primary_service, Select One, Required): Choose the service you received from our specialists (e.g., hair styling, nail services, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Final Result** (overall_look_satisfaction, Number, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the final result on a 1 to 5 scale.
* **Did the result match your initial consultation?** (consultation_match, Select One, Required): Indicate if the final result matched your initial consultation.
* **Professionalism and Skill of the Specialist** (professionalism_rating, Number, Required): Rate the professionalism and skill of our specialist on a 1 to 5 scale.
* **Cleanliness and Comfort of the Facility** (facility_cleanliness, Select One, Required): Select the cleanliness and comfort of our facility (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Quality of Products Used** (product_quality_rating, Select One, Optional): Rate the quality of products used on a scale of Superior, Standard, or Sub-par.
* **How likely are you to return for another service?** (return_likelihood, Number, Required): Indicate how likely you are to return for another service on a 0 to 10 scale.
* **What did you like most about your new look?** (liked_aspect, Text, Optional): Provide any feedback on what you liked most about your new look.
* **Any areas where we can improve your experience?** (improvement_areas, Text, Optional): Provide any areas where we can improve your experience.
* **Permission to use photos of your look for our portfolio?** (photo_permission, Select One, Required): Indicate if you give permission to use photos of your look for our portfolio.
