# Restaurant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at a restaurant. This feedback will help the restaurant management or owners to identify areas for improvement and provide better services to their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Rate the restaurant on a scale from 1 to 5 or select one of the provided options for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.
4. Rate the overall quality of the restaurant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, optional): A field to enter the name of the restaurant being reviewed.
- **Overall Quality** (`overall_quality`, number, optional): Rate the overall quality of the restaurant, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the food served, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, number, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the restaurant, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Order Accuracy** (`order_accuracy`, number, optional): Rate the accuracy of the order, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Speed of Service** (`speed_of_service`, number, optional): Rate the speed of service, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, number, optional): Rate the quality of service, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Staff Friendly** (`staff_friendly`, number, optional): Rate the friendliness of the staff, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Staff Knowledge** (`staff_knowledge`, number, optional): Rate the knowledge of the staff, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Menu Diversity** (`menu_diversity`, number, optional): Rate the diversity of the menu, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Food Presentation** (`food_presentation`, number, optional): Rate the presentation of the food, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, number, optional): Rate the value for money, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Restaurant Environment** (`restaurant_environment`, number, optional): Rate the environment of the restaurant, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, number, optional): Rate the time of service, with a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your overall experience.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
- **Restaurant Rating** (`restaurant_rating`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to rate the overall quality of the restaurant.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your visit.
- **Staff Image** (`staff_image`, note, optional): Note any notable staff members who made a positive or negative impact on your visit.
- **Parking** (`parking`, number, optional): Rate the availability and quality of parking at the restaurant.
- **Restroom Quality** (`restroom_quality`, number, optional): Rate the cleanliness and quality of the restroom.
- **Restaurant Wi-Fi** (`restaurant_wifi`, number, optional): Rate the availability and quality of the restaurant's Wi-Fi.
