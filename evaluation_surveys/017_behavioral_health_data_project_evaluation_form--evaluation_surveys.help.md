# Behavioral Health Data Project Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Behavioral Health Data Project Evaluation Form is a comprehensive assessment tool designed to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of behavioral health data projects. This form helps project evaluators provide a thorough and systematic evaluation of a project's progress, strengths, and areas for improvement. The form is divided into several sections, allowing evaluators to assess different aspects of the project and provide a comprehensive evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Evaluator Name" field.
2. Select the date when you started evaluating the project.
3. Enter the project name.
4. Choose the duration of the project from the provided options (1-3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6-12 Months, 1-2 Years, More than 2 Years).
5. Choose the effectiveness of data collection methods used in the project.
6. Choose the level of participant engagement during the project.
7. Choose the quality of data collected.
8. Choose how well the project adhered to the timeline.
9. Choose how well the project managed its budget.
10. Choose the effectiveness of team coordination and communication.
11. Choose the adequacy of technology and tools used in the project.
12. Choose the measures taken for data security and privacy.
13. Choose how ready the data is for analysis.
14. Choose how well the project met its objectives.
15. Choose the key strengths of the project.
16. Describe any major challenges you faced during the project.
17. Provide recommendations for future projects.
18. Finally, choose an overall rating for the project (Excellent, Good, Adequate, Needs Improvement, Poor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (1): Please enter your name as the person completing this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date** (2): Choose the date when you started evaluating the project.
* **Project Name** (3): Enter the name of the project being evaluated.
* **Project Duration** (4): Choose the duration of the project from the options provided.
* **Effectiveness of Data Collection Methods** (5): Evaluate how well the data collection methods used captured the needed data.
* **Participant Engagement Level** (6): Choose the level of engagement from the participants during the project.
* **Quality of Data Collected** (7): Evaluate the quality of the data collected.
* **Adherence to Project Timeline** (8): Choose how well the project followed its timeline.
* **Budget Management** (9): Note that this field is not required for this form.
* **Team Coordination and Communication** (10): Evaluate the effectiveness of team coordination and communication.
* **Adequacy of Technology and Tools Used** (11): Choose the adequacy of technology and tools used in the project.
* **Data Security and Privacy Measures** (12): Evaluate the measures taken for data security and privacy.
* **Data Ready for Analysis** (13): Choose how ready the data is for analysis.
* **Project Objectives Met** (14): Evaluate how well the project met its objectives.
* **Key Strengths of the Project** (15): Describe any key strengths of the project.
* **Major Challenges Encountered** (16): Describe any major challenges you faced during the project.
* **Recommendations for Future Projects** (17): Provide recommendations for future projects.
* **Overall Project Rating** (18): Choose an overall rating for the project.
