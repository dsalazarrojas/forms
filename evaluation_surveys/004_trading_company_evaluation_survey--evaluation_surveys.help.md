# Trading Company Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers or partners on a company's performance, services, or products. It is meant to help the company improve its services or products based on the feedback and ratings received.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Enter your name of the company you are evaluating.
3. Enter the date of the evaluation, if applicable.
4. Choose a rating from the options provided (Excellence, Good, Fair, Poor).
5. Provide a comment on your rating, if desired.
6. Enter a number score (0-100) to represent your level of satisfaction.
7. Enter a comment on your satisfaction, if desired.
8. Choose whether you would recommend the company to others (True, False, Maybe).
9. Provide a reason for recommending or not recommending the company, if desired.
10. Enter any additional feedback on your experience with the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company you are evaluating.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation, if applicable.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required: false): Choose a rating from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, required: false): Provide a comment on your rating, if desired.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`, number, required: false): Enter a number score (0-100) to represent your level of satisfaction.
* **Satisfaction Comment** (`satisfaction_comment`, text, required: false): Provide a comment on your satisfaction, if desired.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you would recommend the company to others (True, False, Maybe).
* **Why Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend_reasons`, text, required: false): Provide a reason for recommending or not recommending the company, if desired.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Enter any additional feedback on your experience with the company.
* **Company Contact** (`company_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name or contact information of a company representative, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form honestly and completely.
* You can skip any fields that you don't have information for.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact the company directly.
