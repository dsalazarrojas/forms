# performance_appraisal_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the performance appraisal process to improve its effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read each question carefully and choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
2. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the text field at the end of the form.
3. Make sure to answer all questions accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the overall performance appraisal process?** (Single select):
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
	Please choose how satisfied you are with the overall performance appraisal process.
* **How clear were the performance expectations set for you?** (Single select):
	+ Very clear
	+ Mostly clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Not clear at all
	Please choose how clear you think the performance expectations were set for you.
* **How helpful was the feedback you received during your appraisal?** (Single select):
	+ Very helpful
	+ Moderately helpful
	+ Slightly helpful
	+ Not helpful
	Please choose how helpful you found the feedback you received during your appraisal.
* **Which of the following applied to your experience with the appraisal? (Select all that apply)** (Multiple select):
	+ Fair and unbiased
	+ Encouraged discussion
	+ Clear next steps
	+ Timely
	+ Felt rushed
	+ Unfair
* **What aspects of the appraisal process would you suggest improving?** (Text):
	+ Please provide any specific comments or suggestions you may have for improving the appraisal process.
* **Do you feel the appraisal accurately reflected your performance?** (Single select):
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
	Please choose how accurate you feel the appraisal was in reflecting your performance.
