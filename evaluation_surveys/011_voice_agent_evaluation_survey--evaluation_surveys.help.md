# Voice Agent Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voice Agent Evaluation Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from customers or end-users on their experiences with a voice agent. The survey assesses the performance, effectiveness, and communication style of the voice agent, aiming to improve customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Voice Agent Evaluation Survey, follow these steps:

- Please read each question carefully and rate the agent's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
- Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
- Provide your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, you can type them in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
- If you have any feedback on the service, you can type it in the "Feedback on Service" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rate the agent's performance (1-10)** (`rating_agent_performance`, `number`, `true`): Rate the agent's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Rate the agent's effectiveness (1-10)** (`rating_effectiveness`, `number`, `true`): Rate the agent's ability to effectively communicate and resolve issues.
* **Rate the agent's communication style (1-10)** (`communication_style`, `number`, `true`): Rate the agent's communication style, considering clarity, politeness, and attentiveness.
* **Rate the agent's problem-solving ability (1-10)** (`problem_solving_ability`, `number`, `true`): Rate the agent's ability to resolve problems and answer questions accurately.
* **Rate your overall experience (1-10)** (`overall_experience`, `number`, `true`): Rate your overall experience with the voice agent.
* **What suggestions do you have for improvement? (text)** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, `false`): Share your thoughts on how the voice agent can improve.
* **What is your feedback on service? (text)** (`feedback_on_service`, `text`, `false`): Provide your feedback on the service you received.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: This section is not required to be filled but serves as a welcome page.

Tips:

* Ensure the ratings are honest and accurate representations of your experiences with the voice agent.
* Use the provided fields to express any thoughts or suggestions you may have for improvement.
* If you have any feedback on the service, please provide it in the "Feedback on Service" field.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at [insert contact information].
