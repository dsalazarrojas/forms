# Economic Course Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluation data about a specific economic course. It is designed to help students, instructors, or course administrators understand the course's content, organization, and overall quality. This information will help identify areas of strength and weakness in the course, which can inform improvements and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of the course in the "Course Title" field.
2. Enter the name of the course instructor in the "Course Instructor" field.
3. Enter the instructor's department in the "Course Instructor Department" field (if applicable).
4. Describe the course in a few sentences in the "Course Description" field.
5. Specify the course level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced) in the "Course Level" field.
6. If the course level is not listed, enter "other" and describe it in the "Course Level Other" field.
7. Provide the start date of the course in the "Course Start Date" field.
8. Provide the end date of the course in the "Course End Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Course Title
* **Course Title**: <em>(text)</em>: Enter the title of the course, e.g., "Introduction to Microeconomics" or "Money and Finance."

### Course Instructor
* **Course Instructor**: <em>(text)</em>: Enter the name of the course instructor, e.g., "John Doe" or "Jane Smith."

### Course Instructor Department
* **Course Instructor Department**: <em>(text)</em>: Enter the department of the course instructor, e.g., "Economics" or "Business."

### Course Description
* **Course Description**: <em>(text)</em>: Describe the course in a few sentences, e.g., "This course covers the basics of microeconomics and its applications" or "This course explores the world of finance and investing."

### Course Level
* **Course Level**: <em>(text)</em>: Specify the course level, e.g., "beginner," "intermediate," or "advanced."

### Course Level Other
* **Course Level Other**: <em>(text)</em>: If the course level is not listed, enter "other" and describe it in this field.

### Course Level Scale
* **Course Level Scale**: <em>(text)</em>: Enter a number or description of the course level scale (e.g., 1-5 or beginner-advanced).

### Course Start Date
* **Course Start Date**: <em>(date)</em>: Enter the start date of the course in the format MM/dd/yyyy, e.g., 01/01/2020.

### Course End Date
* **Course End Date**: <em>(date)</em>: Enter the end date of the course in the format MM/dd/yyyy, e.g., 06/15/2022.

# Tips
* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or describe it in the "Course Level Other" field.
* This form is meant to be a general evaluation tool, so feel free to provide any relevant information that you think might be helpful for course improvement.
