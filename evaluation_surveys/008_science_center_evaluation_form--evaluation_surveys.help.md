# science_center_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The science center evaluation form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from users about their experience at the science center. This form helps the science center staff to understand what they are doing well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields (*).
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
4. Select the correct options from the list.
5. Type your answer in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, text, optional): Please provide a brief introduction about your visit/event to the science center.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Rate your overall experience at the science center. Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfactory" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Exhibits** (`exhibits`, select_multiple, optional): Have you visited any exhibits during your visit? Select all exhibits that you visited.
* **Programs** (`programs`, select_one, optional): Were you involved in any programs during your visit? Select "Yes" if you were, otherwise select "No".
* **Overall Experience 2** (`overall_experience_2`, number, required): Rate your overall experience at the science center again, this time considering all aspects of your visit. Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfactory" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Do you have any additional comments or suggestions about your visit to the science center? This is an open-ended field where you can provide any feedback or thoughts.
