# risk_control_maturity_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the Risk Control Maturity Survey is to assess the risk control capabilities and maturity level of your organization, evaluating its risk control practices, processes, and measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Go through each page of the form, and answer the questions according to your organization's current risk control practices and processes.
2.  Answer the questions truthfully and accurately, without any bias or influence from external parties.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or skip it, and proceed with the rest of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Risk Control Maturity** (1): This field asks for a numerical value to represent your organization's current risk control maturity level.
*   **Organizational Level** (2): This field asks for the level of risk control implementation in your organization. Please select the level that best describes your organization's current risk control practices.
*   **Risk Control Practices** (3): This field asks for the risk control practices that your organization currently uses. Enter your answers in the provided space, and please be specific and clear.
*   **Risk Management Approach** (4): This field allows you to describe your organization's risk management approach. Enter your answer in the provided space, and please be specific and clear.
*   **Process Maturity** (5): This field asks for a numerical value to represent your organization's current process maturity level.
*   **Risk Control Capabilities** (6): This field asks for the risk control capabilities that your organization currently has. Select all the capabilities that apply to your organization.
*   **Risk Management Process** (7): This field asks for the risk management process currently used by your organization. Select the process that best describes your organization's current risk management practices.
*   **Risk Assessment** (8): This field asks for a numerical value to represent your organization's current risk assessment level.
*   **Risk Management Plan** (9): This field asks for the risk management plan currently in place in your organization. Select the plan that best describes your organization's current risk management practices.
*   **Risk Control Measures** (10): This field asks for the risk control measures that your organization currently has. Select all the measures that apply to your organization.
*   **Review and Update** (11): This field asks for the date when your organization last reviewed its risk control practices. Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
*   **Risk Control Review** (12): This field asks for the date when your organization last reviewed its risk control practices. Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
*   **Risk Control Evaluation** (13): This field asks for the time when your organization last evaluated its risk control practices. Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
*   **Risk Control Comment** (14): This field asks for any comments or feedback about your organization's risk control practices. Enter your comments in the provided space.
*   **Risk Control Score** (15): This field asks for a numerical value to represent your organization's current risk control score.
*   **Risk Control Rating** (16): This field asks for the rating of your organization's risk control practices. Select the rating that best describes your organization's current risk control practices.
*   **Risk Control Recommendation** (17): This field allows you to provide any recommendations or suggestions for improving your organization's risk control practices. Enter your recommendations in the provided space.
*   **Risk Control Score 1** (18), **Risk Control Score 2** (19), **Risk Control Score 3** (20): These fields ask for additional numerical values to represent your organization's risk control scores.
