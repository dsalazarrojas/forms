# Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience at the spa. The survey consists of 13 questions that cover various aspects of the spa's comfort and services. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow the instructions below:

*   Start by filling out your contact information on the first page.
*   On the subsequent pages, answer each question based on your personal experience at the spa.
*   Rate the facility on a scale of 1 to 10 for each section, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
*   If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more information.
*   Finally, thank the user for their participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Spa Name** (`spa_name`, text, optional): This is the full name of the spa where you visited.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide the address of the spa you visited.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the spa if you wish to contact them directly.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted by the spa.
*   **Facility Rating** (`facility_rating`, number, required): Rate the comfort and cleanliness of the facility from 1 to 10.
*   **Ambiance Rating** (`ambiance_rating`, number, required): Rate the ambiance and atmosphere of the spa from 1 to 10.
*   **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`, number, required): Rate the cleanliness of the spa from 1 to 10.
*   **Service Quality Rating** (`service_quality_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of service from 1 to 10.
*   **Spa Staff Rating** (`spa_staff_rating`, number, required): Rate the friendliness and service of the spa staff from 1 to 10.
*   **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate the overall experience from 1 to 10.
*   **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
*   **Thank you for your participation!** (`thanks`, note, optional): Enter any final comments or feedback.
*   **Note: This field will be used to thank the user for their participation and provide any additional information.**
