# Training Evaluation Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from participants on the training program, helping to identify areas of improvement and areas of strength. Your input will contribute to creating a better experience for future trainees.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To ensure you can provide your honest opinion, please fill in the survey thoroughly and accurately.
2. Select all that apply for questions with multiple options.
3. For the open-ended question, provide as many suggestions as you see fit.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Overall, how satisfied were you with this training program?**
(Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)

*This question assesses your overall level of satisfaction with the program. Please select the answer that best reflects your experience.*

**Which sessions or topics did you find most valuable? (select all that apply)**
(Module 1: Core Concepts, Module 2: Advanced Techniques, Hands-on Workshop, Q&A Panel, Networking Opportunities)

*This question is designed to help us understand which topics or sessions resonated with you the most. Select all the sessions or topics that you found particularly valuable or interesting.*

**Was the training event well-organized and structured?**
(true, false, Partially)

*This question checks your experience with the event's organization and structure. Please select the option that best describes your experience.*

**How relevant was the training content to your professional goals?**
(Extremely relevant, Very relevant, Moderately relevant, Slightly relevant, Not relevant)

*This question assesses the relevance of the training content to your professional goals. Select the option that best reflects your experience.*

**How likely are you to recommend this training to a colleague?**
(Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely)

*This question evaluates your likelihood of recommending the program to others. Select the option that best reflects your willingness to endorse the program.*

**What suggestions do you have for improving future training programs?**
*This is an open-ended question where you can share any suggestions, ideas, or thoughts on how to improve future training programs. Be as specific as possible.*
