# Educational Sector Indicator Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect evaluations of the educational sector, focusing on indicators and metrics to measure the quality and effectiveness of institutions within that sector. This form is used to assess the performance of institutions in the educational sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Sector Id**, a unique identifier for the sector being evaluated.
2. Enter the **Institution Id**, a unique identifier for the institution being evaluated.
3. Enter the **Indicator Id**, a unique identifier for the indicator being evaluated.
4. Provide the **Sector Name** of the institution.
5. Provide the **Institution Name** and details.
6. Enter the **Indicator Name** and select the **Indicator Type** (Yes or No).
7. Enter the **Indicator Value** for the selected indicator type.
8. Enter any **Notes** regarding the evaluation.
9. Provide the **Institution Phone** number.
10. Enter the **Institution Email** address.
11. Optionally, provide the **Sector Url** and **Institution Url** for further information.
12. Enter the **Created At** timestamp when the evaluation was created.
13. Enter the **Submitted At** timestamp when the evaluation was submitted.
14. Enter the **Submitted By** name of the person who submitted the evaluation.
15. Finally, enter the **Updated At** timestamp when the evaluation was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sector Id (1)
• **Sector Id**: A unique identifier for the sector being evaluated. This field is required and should be entered accordingly.

### Institution Id (2)
• **Institution Id**: A unique identifier for the institution being evaluated. This field is required and should be entered accordingly.

### Indicator Id (3)
• **Indicator Id**: A unique identifier for the indicator being evaluated. This field is required and should be entered accordingly.

### Sector Name (4)
• **Sector Name**: The name of the sector being evaluated. This field is required and should be entered in text format.

### Institution Name (5)
• **Institution Name**: The name of the institution being evaluated. This field is required and should be entered in text format.

### Indicator Name (6)
• **Indicator Name**: The name of the indicator being evaluated. This field is required and should be entered in text format.

### Indicator Type (7)
• **Indicator Type**: Select whether the indicator is "Yes" or "No" to indicate its type. This field is required.

### Indicator Value (8)
• **Indicator Value**: Enter the value of the selected indicator type. This field is required and should be a number.

### Notes (9)
• **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or explanations regarding the evaluation. This field is required and should be entered in text format.

### Institution Phone (10)
• **Institution Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the institution. This field is required and should be entered in text format.

### Institution Email (11)
• **Institution Email**: Enter the contact email address of the institution. This field is required and should be entered in email format.

### Sector Url (12)
• **Sector Url**: Optionally, enter the URL of the sector for further information. This field is optional and should be entered in text format.

### Institution Url (13)
• **Institution Url**: Optionally, enter the URL of the institution for further information. This field is optional and should be entered in text format.

### Created At (14)
• **Created At**: The timestamp when the evaluation was created. This field is optional and should be entered in date format.

### Submitted At (15)
• **Submitted At**: The timestamp when the evaluation was submitted. This field is optional and should be entered in date format.

### Submitted By (16)
• **Submitted By**: The name of the person who submitted the evaluation. This field is required and should be entered in text format.

### Updated At (17)
• **Updated At**: The timestamp when the evaluation was last updated. This field is required and should be entered in time format.
