# Management Consulting Value Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from clients on their experience with our management consulting projects. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page of the form and fill out the fields as instructed below.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of the project you're referring to (optional).
* **Consultant Details**: Describe the consultant(s) involved in the project (optional).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: On a scale from 1-10, rate your overall satisfaction with our consulting services.
* **Project Value**: Indicate the level of value you received from the project (High, Medium, Low).
* **Project Outcome**: Describe the outcome of the project (Successful, Partially Successful, Not Successful).
* **Additional Comments**: If you'd like to add any additional comments about the project, enter them here (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can discuss any further questions or concerns you may have (optional).
* **Project Status**: Indicate the current status of the project (In Progress, Complete, Cancelled).
* **Consultant Rating**: On a scale from 1-10, rate the quality of service from the consultant (optional).
* **Overall Value**: On a scale from 1-10, rate the overall value you received from the project (optional).

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- If you're unsure about any question, please ask.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our services.
