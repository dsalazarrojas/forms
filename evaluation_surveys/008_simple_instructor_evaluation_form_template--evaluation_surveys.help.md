# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from students regarding a course or instructional experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as thoroughly as possible, providing detailed feedback and ratings where requested.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. If a field is optional, feel free to answer or leave it blank.
4. Once completed, submit the form to ensure your feedback is received.
