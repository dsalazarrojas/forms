# Care Coordination Program Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the effectiveness of the care coordination program. It is designed to be completed by program stakeholders, such as program managers, clinicians, and coordinators, to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the program's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the official program title in the "Program name" field.
2. Select the date of the evaluation in the "Evaluation period end date" field.
3. Choose your role within the program in the "Respondent role" field.
4. Answer the question "Have program goals been met?" with either "True", "Partially", or "False".
5. Provide a brief summary of the key outcomes observed in the "Key outcomes" field.
6. If applicable, provide a qualitative assessment of the cost-effectiveness of the program in the "Cost effectiveness assessment" field.
7. Describe any major obstacles encountered in the "Barriers and challenges" field.
8. Offer any actionable suggestions for improvement in the "Recommendations for improvement" field.
9. Add any additional comments or feedback about the program in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program name** (`program_name`, required): Enter the official title of the program being evaluated.
* **Evaluation period end date** (`evaluation_period`, required): Enter the date on which the evaluation was performed.
* **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`, required): Choose your role within the program (e.g., Manager, Clinician, Coordinator, Support staff).
* **Have program goals been met** (`program_goals_met`, required): Assessed by stakeholders, answer with either "True", "Partially", or "False".
* **Key outcomes** (`key_outcomes`, required): Provide a brief summary of the key outcomes observed.
* **Cost effectiveness assessment** (`cost_effectiveness`, optional): If applicable, provide a qualitative assessment of the cost-effectiveness of the program.
* **Barriers and challenges** (`barriers_and_challenges`, optional): Describe any major obstacles encountered.
* **Recommendations for improvement** (`recommendations`, required): Offer any actionable suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the program.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Provide a brief but clear summary of the key outcomes.
* Avoid unnecessary jargon or technical terms.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, feel free to reach out to the program administrators for guidance.
