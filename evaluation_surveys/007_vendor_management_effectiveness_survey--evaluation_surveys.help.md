# Vendor Management Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information and feedback from users about their experience with a vendor, with the goal of evaluating their effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly, answering questions based on your experience with the vendor.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Survey Info (1)
* **Survey Info**: A short description of the survey.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Company Name (2)
* **Company Name**: The name of the company you are providing feedback on.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Survey Date (3)
* **Survey Date**: The date of when you last interacted with the vendor.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: no

### Survey Time (4)
* **Survey Time**: The time of when you last interacted with the vendor.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: no

### Overall Rating (5)
* **Overall Rating**: Your rating of the vendor's overall performance.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no

### Performance Score (6)
* **Performance Score**: A score of how well the vendor performed in your experience.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no

### What Worked (7)
* **What Worked**: Feedback on what worked well in your experience with the vendor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### What Didn't (8)
* **What Didn't**: Feedback on what didn't work well in your experience with the vendor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Satisfactory (9)
* **Satisfactory**: A rating of how satisfactory the vendor's performance was.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no

### Maintaining (10)
* **Maintaining**: A rating of how well the vendor maintained their performance.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no

### Achieving (11)
* **Achieving**: Feedback on how well the vendor achieved their goals.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Improvement (12)
* **Improvement**: Feedback on areas for improvement for the vendor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Value (13)
* **Value**: A rating of the vendor's value to you or your organization.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no

### Relationship (14)
* **Relationship**: A rating of the quality of the relationship with the vendor.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no

### Overall Value (15)
* **Overall Value**: A rating of the overall value provided by the vendor.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no

### Overall Relationship (16)
* **Overall Relationship**: A rating of the overall quality of the relationship with the vendor.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: no

### Comments (17)
* **Comments**: Additional comments or feedback about your experience with the vendor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Important (18)
* **Important**: A rating of how important the vendor is to you or your organization.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no

### Not Important (19)
* **Not Important**: A rating of how not important the vendor is to you or your organization.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no

### Vendor Name (20)
* **Vendor Name**: The name of the vendor you are providing feedback on (Select all that apply).
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: yes

### Vendor Location (21)
* **Vendor Location**: The location of the vendor's office or business.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Vendor Phone (22)
* **Vendor Phone**: The phone number of the vendor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Vendor Email (23)
* **Vendor Email**: The email address of the vendor.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: no

### Submitter Name (24)
* **Submitter Name**: The name of the person submitting the feedback.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

### Submitter Email (25)
* **Submitter Email**: The email address of the person submitting the feedback.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: no
