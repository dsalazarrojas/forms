# environmental_health_program_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate the effectiveness of an environmental health program. It assesses the overall rating, performance, impact, and suggestions for improvement of the program. The feedback gathered from this form will help to identify areas of success and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction and review the program's information.
2. Provide your overall rating of the program (1-5).
3. Select the performance level of the program from the available options (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
4. Choose the impact of the program on the community from the available options (Positive, Negative, No impact).
5. Add any suggestions for improvement, if applicable.
6. Provide any additional comments about the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall rating (2)** (`overall_rating`, number, required/optional): This field allows you to rate the program overall, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Performance (3)** (`program_performance`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to select the performance level of the program from the available options (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Impact (4)** (`impact`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to choose the impact of the program on the community from the available options (Positive, Negative, No impact).
* **Suggestions (5)** (`suggestions`, text, required/optional): This field is for any suggestions or comments you may have for the program's improvement.
* **Additional comments (6)** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): This field is for any extra comments or feedback you may have about the program.
