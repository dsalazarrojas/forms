# NGO Accountability Rating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the accountability and transparency of NGOs. It consists of 13 fields, divided into 5 main sections. Please complete this form to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the NGO.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions in each section honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the appropriate responses based on your knowledge and understanding of the NGO.
3. Enter your email address and phone number (if available) to facilitate communication and follow-up.
4. Review your ratings before submitting the form.
5. Click the 'Submit' button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transparency (2)**: Select 'Yes' if the NGO provides clear and timely information about its operations, decisions, and finances. If you are unsure or lack information, select 'No'.
* **Engagement (3)**: Rate the level of engagement between the NGO and its stakeholders, including communication frequency and quality. Choose 'Yes' if the NGO engages effectively with its stakeholders; 'No' otherwise.
* **Accountability Practices (4)**: Select all applicable practices that the NGO implements regularly, such as 'Yes' for regular audits, financial reporting, and internal controls.
* **Impact Assessment (5)**: Rate the effectiveness of the NGO's programs and projects in achieving their intended outcomes. Choose 'Yes' if the outcomes are satisfactory; 'No' otherwise.
* **Governance Review (6)**: Evaluate the NGO's governance structure and the independence of its board of directors. Select 'Yes' if the board is independent; 'No' otherwise.
* **Stakeholder Feedback (7)**: Gather feedback from various stakeholders, including staff, donors, and beneficiaries. Choose 'Yes' if you have received feedback; 'No' otherwise.
* **Email (8)**: Enter your email address for communication and follow-up purposes. This field is required.
* **Phone (9)**: Provide your phone number for additional contact (optional).
* **Rating 1 (10)**: Enter a rating (1-5) based on your evaluation of the NGO's transparency.
* **Rating 2 (11)**: Enter a rating (1-5) based on your evaluation of the NGO's engagement.
* **Rating 3 (12)**: Enter a rating (1-5) based on your evaluation of the NGO's accountability practices.
* **Rating 4 (13)**: Enter a rating (1-5) based on your evaluation of the NGO's impact assessment.
* **Rating 5 (14)**: Enter a rating (1-5) based on your evaluation of the governance review.
* **Total Score (15)**: Enter the sum of the individual ratings for a comprehensive assessment of the NGO.
* **Submit (16)**: Click 'Yes' to submit the form and complete the evaluation.
