# Business Support Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with the Business Support Program. Your input will help us evaluate and improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as honestly as possible. You may take as much time as you need to complete it. If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).
* **When did you join the program** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you joined the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction with the Program** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program using the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Quality of Support Provided** (`support_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of support provided by the program team using the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Usefulness of Resources and Materials** (`resource_usefulness`, select_one, required): Rate the usefulness of resources and materials provided using the following options:
	+ Very Useful
	+ Useful
	+ Somewhat Useful
	+ Not Very Useful
	+ Not Useful at All
* **Mentor or Coach Effectiveness** (`mentor_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the mentor or coach provided using the following options:
	+ Highly Effective
	+ Effective
	+ Moderately Effective
	+ Ineffective
	+ Not Applicable
* **Business Improvements Since Joining** (`business_improvements`, select_multiple, required): Select all the improvements you have seen since joining the program:
	+ Increased Revenue
	+ Better Customer Service
	+ Improved Operations
	+ Marketing Strategy
	+ Financial Management
	+ No Significant Changes Yet
* **Most Valuable Aspect of the Program** (`most_valuable_aspect`, text, required): Enter the most valuable aspect of the program that helped you.
* **Areas Where the Program Could Improve** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest areas where the program could be improved.
* **Would You Recommend This Program** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Would you recommend this program to other businesses using the following options:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Future Support Areas You Need** (`future_support_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select the areas of support you would like to see in the future:
	+ Financial Planning
	+ Marketing Strategies
	+ Technology and Digital Tools
	+ Human Resources
	+ Supply Chain Management
	+ Other
