# wellbeing_support_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students on their experience with wellbeing support services. The purpose of this form is to evaluate the quality and impact of these services, helping us understand areas of improvement and ensure that students receive the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the option that best describes your experience with wellbeing support services in the "Wellbeing Support Quality" section.
2. Rate the impact of wellbeing support services on your wellbeing in the "Wellbeing Support Impact" section.
3. If you have any improvement ideas or suggestions for wellbeing support services, please let us know in the "Wellbeing Improvement Ideas" section.
4. In the "Wellbeing Support Quality Scale" sections, please rate your experience with each of the provided scales, from 1 to 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (Note): This section is an overview of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### Wellbeing Support Quality
* **Wellbeing Support Quality** (Select from options): This section asks you to describe your experience with wellbeing support services. Please select the option that best reflects your experience.

### Wellbeing Support Impact
* **Wellbeing Support Impact** (Scale from 1 to 6): This section assesses the impact of wellbeing support services on your wellbeing. Please rate your experience with the provided scale.

### Wellbeing Improvement Ideas
* **Wellbeing Improvement Ideas** (Text Input): If you have any ideas or suggestions on how to improve wellbeing support services, please provide them here.

* **Wellbeing Support Quality Scale 1-6** (Select from 1 to 6): This section provides six additional scales to assess your experience with wellbeing support services. Please rate your experience with each of the scales from 1 to 6.
