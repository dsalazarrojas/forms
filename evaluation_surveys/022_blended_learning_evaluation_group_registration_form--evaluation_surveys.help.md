# Blended Learning Evaluation Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your blended learning evaluation group for the evaluation program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your group's information, including the group leader's name, email address, phone number, and organization or institution.
2. Provide details about your group, such as the number of participants, their level of education or experience, and the focus area of your evaluation.
3. Choose the duration and start date of your evaluation program.
4. Select the blend of learning modalities you will use.
5. Confirm that all participants have access to necessary devices and that you are willing to share evaluation data.
6. Confirm your institutional review board approval status.
7. Provide any special requirements or needs your group has.
8. If your group has experience with blended learning, select how extensive that experience is.
9. Finally, outline your group's evaluation goals and any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Leader Name** (`group_leader_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the group coordinator.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization or Institution** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your school or organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter which department or division your group is from.
* **Group Name** (`group_name`, text, required): Enter a name for your evaluation group.
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, required): Enter how many people are in your group.
* **Participant Level** (`participant_level`, select_one, required): Select the education or experience level of your participants:
	+ Undergraduate Students
	+ Graduate Students
	+ Faculty
	+ K-12 Educators
	+ Corporate Trainees
	+ Mixed Group
* **Evaluation Focus Area** (`evaluation_focus`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more aspects your group is evaluating:
	+ Student Engagement
	+ Learning Outcomes
	+ Technology Effectiveness
	+ Instructor Satisfaction
	+ Course Design
	+ Assessment Methods
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to begin your evaluation program.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your evaluation program:
	+ 4 weeks
	+ 8 weeks
	+ 12 weeks
	+ 16 weeks
	+ Full Semester
* **Learning Modality** (`learning_modality`, select_one, required): Select the blend of learning modalities:
	+ 50 percent Online 50 percent In Person
	+ 70 percent Online 30 percent In Person
	+ 30 percent Online 70 percent In Person
	+ Fully Online with Synchronous Sessions
	+ HyFlex Model
* **Technology Access** (`technology_access`, select_one, required): Confirm that all participants have access to necessary devices:
	+ Yes All Have Devices
	+ Most Have Devices
	+ Limited Access
	+ Need Support
* **Data Sharing Consent** (`data_sharing_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are willing to share evaluation data:
	+ Yes Consent Given
	+ False
* **Institutional Review Board Approval Status** (`irb_approval`, select_one, required): Confirm your institutional review board approval status:
	+ Approved
	+ Pending
	+ Not Required
	+ Will Apply
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility or technical needs.
* **Previous Blended Learning Experience** (`previous_experience`, select_one, required): Select your group's experience with blended learning:
	+ Extensive Experience
	+ Some Experience
	+ Limited Experience
	+ No Experience
* **Evaluation Goals** (`evaluation_goals`, text, required): Outline what you hope to learn from the evaluation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
