# Design Thesis Review Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

This form is designed to collect feedback on the design thesis. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the thesis design and provide your honest feedback on its overall quality, content, form, and usability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the overall quality of the design?** (`design_thesis_review_survey_page_1`): Evaluate the overall quality of the design, using one of the following options: Good, Poor.
* **Thesis Designer ID** (`thesis_designer_id`): This field is intended to allow you to provide the ID of the thesis designer.
* **Faculty Name** (`faculty_name`): Enter the name of the faculty member who designed the thesis.
* **External Critic ID** (`external_critic_id`): Enter the ID of the external critic who evaluated the thesis.
* **Thesis Title** (`thesis_title`): Enter the title of the thesis.
* **Overall Quality Rating** (`overall_quality_rating`): Rate the overall quality of the thesis on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback on Content** (`feedback_on_content`): Provide feedback on the content of the thesis.
* **Feedback on Form and Layout** (`feedback_on_form_and_layout`): Provide feedback on the form and layout of the thesis.
* **Feedback on Visual Appearance** (`feedback_on_visual_appearance`): Provide feedback on the visual appearance of the thesis.
* **Feedback on Clarity** (`feedback_on_clarity`): Provide feedback on the clarity of the thesis.
* **External Critic Rating** (`external_critic_rating`): Enter the rating given by the external critic.
* **Feedback on Usability** (`feedback_on_usability`): Provide feedback on the usability of the thesis.
* **Design Thesis Critique** (`design_thesis_critique`): Provide a detailed critique of the thesis design.
* **Feedback on Effectiveness** (`feedback_on_effectiveness`): Provide feedback on the effectiveness of the thesis.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments you'd like to make.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date when the review was completed.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time when the review was completed.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* Make sure to provide feedback on all relevant fields.
* If you need to provide any additional information, use the "Additional Comments" field.
