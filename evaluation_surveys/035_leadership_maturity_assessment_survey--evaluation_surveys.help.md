# Leadership Maturity Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Maturity Assessment Survey is a tool designed to help leaders evaluate their own leadership practices and identify areas for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly, reflecting on your current leadership practices.
2. Choose the options that best describe your behaviors and skills for each question.
3. If you are unsure about any question, choose the option that seems most accurate.
4. Complete all pages (sections) of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page provides a brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **Leadership Maturity**: (id: 2) This question assesses your self-awareness and recognition of your own leadership style.
	+ **High**: I clearly understand my leadership strengths and weaknesses.
	+ **Medium**: I have some understanding of my leadership strengths and weaknesses.
	+ **Low**: I do not have a clear understanding of my leadership strengths and weaknesses.
* **Leadership Behaviors** (id: 3): This question evaluates the behaviors you exhibit as a leader.
	+ **Visionary**: I set clear and inspiring goals and vision for the team.
	+ **Communicative**: I communicate clearly and effectively with my team members.
	+ **Decisive**: I make timely and well-informed decisions.
* **Collaboration** (id: 4): This question assesses the degree to which you collaborate with your team members.
* **Team Dynamics** (id: 5): This question evaluates the dynamics within your team.
* **Communication** (id: 6): This question assesses the frequency of your communication with team members.
	+ **Regular**: I communicate regularly with my team members.
	+ **Occasional**: I communicate occasionally with my team members.
	+ **Rarely**: I rarely communicate with my team members.
* **Leadership Skills** (id: 7): This question evaluates the skills you possess as a leader.
	+ **Visionary**: I possess a clear vision for the team.
	+ **Strategic**: I develop and implement effective plans.
	+ **Decisive**: I make timely and well-informed decisions.
* **Leadership Development** (id: 8): This question assesses your approach to leadership development.
	+ **Visionary**: I inspire and motivate others.
	+ **Inspiring**: I encourage and support others in their growth and development.
	+ **Innovative**: I foster a culture of innovation and experimentation.
* **Organizational Learning** (id: 9): This question evaluates your approach to organizational learning.
	+ (Note: This field is a text field, but no options are provided. Please provide a brief description of how you would like to see your organization learn and adapt.)
