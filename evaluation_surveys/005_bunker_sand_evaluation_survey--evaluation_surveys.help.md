# Bunker Sand Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bunker Sand Evaluation Survey is designed to assess the condition and playability of sand traps on a golf course. The survey is used to collect information on the sand texture, compaction, color, and drainage performance, as well as the playability and maintenance needs of the bunkers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information and resources before starting the survey.
2.  Review the form carefully and complete it accurately.
3.  Follow the instructions for each field to ensure you are capturing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the person completing the survey.
*   **Evaluator Role** (`evaluator_role`, select_one, required): Select the role or position you hold (e.g., Golf Course Superintendent, Greenskeeper, Maintenance Staff, etc.).
*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this evaluation.
*   **Bunker Name or Number** (`bunker_name`, text, required): Identify the bunker being evaluated.
*   **Hole Number** (`hole_number`, number, required): Enter the number of the golf hole associated with the bunker.
*   **Bunker Type** (`bunker_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of bunker (e.g., Fairway Bunker, Greenside Bunker, etc.).
*   **Sand Texture Evaluation** (`sand_texture`, note, optional): Note any observations on the sand texture.
*   **Texture Rating** (`texture_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the sand texture (Excellent - Fine and Consistent, Good - Mostly Fine, etc.).
*   **Sand Compaction** (`sand_compaction`, select_one, required): Rate the sand compaction (Ideal, Slightly Loose, etc.).
*   **Sand Color Evaluation** (`sand_color`, note, optional): Note any observations on the sand color.
*   **Color Consistency** (`color_consistency`, select_one, required): Rate the color consistency (Very Consistent, Mostly Consistent, etc.).
*   **Color Appeal** (`color_appeal`, select_one, required): Rate the color appeal (Excellent, Good, etc.).
*   **Drainage Evaluation** (`drainage`, note, optional): Note any observations on the drainage performance.
*   **Drainage Rating** (`drainage_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the drainage performance (Excellent - Drains Immediately, Good - Drains Quickly, etc.).
*   **Drainage Issues Observed** (`drainage_issues`, text, optional): Enter any specific drainage issues observed.
*   **Playability Assessment** (`playability`, note, optional): Note any observations on the playability.
*   **Playability Rating** (`playability_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall playability (Excellent - Fair Challenge, Good - Appropriate Difficulty, etc.).
*   **Sand Depth** (`sand_depth`, select_one, required): Rate the sand depth (Perfect Depth, Slightly Shallow, etc.).
*   **Maintenance Needs** (`maintenance_needs`, select_multiple, required): Select the maintenance needs of the bunker (e.g., Raking, Sand Top-Up, etc.).
*   **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, required): Rate the urgency of attention needed (No Action Needed, Low Priority, etc.).
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other observations or comments.
