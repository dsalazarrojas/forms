The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on a manager's effectiveness in leading and supporting their team. This form is designed to be a helpful tool to improve leadership and employee experience within the organization. By providing an accurate and honest assessment, participants can help managers understand what they are doing well and where they need to improve. This form is intended for use within the organization, and its completion is voluntary. Please ensure that you have permission to participate and that you provide honest and constructive feedback.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate your satisfaction with your role at the organization.
2. Rate your manager's communication style.
3. Answer the question about whether your manager supports your professional development.
4. Indicate in which areas your manager excels.
5. Provide feedback on how often you receive constructive feedback from your manager.
6. Describe actions your manager could take to improve your work experience.
7. Share any additional comments about your manager's effectiveness.

Below are explanations for each field:

* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?**
	+ This question measures your overall satisfaction with your role and responsibilities within the organization. Please choose the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How would you rate communication from your manager?**
	+ This question assesses your manager's communication style. Choose the option that best describes your manager's communication effectiveness.
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development by your manager?**
	+ This question determines whether you feel supported in your growth and development as a professional.
* **In which areas does your manager excel? (select all that apply)**
	+ This question allows you to identify specific areas where your manager excels, such as communication, coaching, goal setting, recognition, support, or delegation.
* **How frequently do you receive constructive feedback from your manager?**
	+ This question measures the frequency of constructive feedback you receive from your manager.
* **What specific actions could your manager take to improve your work experience?**
	+ This open-ended question invites you to suggest actions your manager could take to enhance your work experience.
* **Do you have any additional comments about your manager's effectiveness?**
	+ This question provides an opportunity to share any additional insights or suggestions about your manager's effectiveness.
