# EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration form is a tool used to collect information from individuals who wish to join the EdTech Evaluation Research Panel. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about each panel member, including their name, affiliation, job title, department, and research interests. This information will be used to facilitate communication and collaboration within the panel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal details.
2. Choose your department from the dropdown list.
3. Select your research interests from the list.
4. Upload your CV or add a brief bio to introduce yourself.
5. Provide your LinkedIn, Twitter, and GitHub handles (if applicable).
6. Optionally, provide other social media handles for LinkedIn, Twitter, and GitHub.
7. Enter your research experience, publications, projects, and education.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, required: false): Enter your affiliation (e.g., university, company, or institution).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false): Choose your department (Research, Development, Operations, Finance, or Other).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required: false): Enter your job type (e.g., researcher, developer, etc.).
* **Role** (`role`, text, required: false): Enter your role or position.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more areas of research interest (AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, or Other).
* **Research Focus** (`research_focus`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your research focus or area of interest.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years of research experience.
* **CV** (`cv`, note, required: false): Upload your CV or add a brief bio.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): Enter your personal website or portfolio link.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, required: false): Add a brief bio to introduce yourself.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, required: false): Enter your LinkedIn handle.
* **Twitter** (`twitter`, text, required: false): Enter your Twitter handle.
* **Other (LinkedIn)** (`other_linkedin`, text, required: false): Enter any other LinkedIn handle.
* **Other (Twitter)** (`other_twitter`, text, required: false): Enter any other Twitter handle.
* **GitHub** (`github`, text, required: false): Enter your GitHub handle.
* **Other (GitHub)** (`other_github`, text, required: false): Enter any other GitHub handle.
* **Research Experience** (`research_experience`, number, required: false): Enter the number of research experiences.
* **Publications** (`publications`, number, required: false): Enter the number of publications.
* **Projects** (`projects`, number, required: false): Enter the number of projects.
* **Education** (`education`, number, required: false): Enter the number of educational qualifications.

Please fill out the form honestly and accurately, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
