# Hospital Green Energy Transition Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data from hospital administrators on their energy transition evaluation for going green. The form aims to evaluate the hospital's efforts to reduce energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and understand each field's requirements.
2. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
