# Social Impact Measurement Methods Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Impact Measurement Methods Survey is a tool used to understand how organizations measure social impact and to identify areas where more effective measurement methods and tools can be developed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Introduction: This section provides an overview of the survey and its purpose.
2. How do you currently measure social impact?:
	* Select all methods that your organization uses to measure social impact.
3. What are some of the biggest challenges you face when measuring social impact?:
	* Select all challenges that your organization faces.
4. What do you want to learn from your measurement practices?:
	* Please provide a brief description of what you hope to gain from your measurement practices.
5. What type of social enterprise is your organization?:
	* Select the type of social enterprise that your organization is.
6. What is the primary location of your organization?:
	* Select all locations where your organization operates.
7. How many years has your organization been measuring social impact?:
	* Please enter the number of years your organization has been measuring social impact.
8. Thank you for taking the time to help us understand your measurement practices!:
	* This section is just a note to thank the user for completing the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`survey_start`, `note`, not required): This section provides an overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **How do you currently measure social impact?** (`measurement_methods`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to select all methods that your organization uses to measure social impact.
* **What are some of the biggest challenges you face when measuring social impact?** (`identify_challenges`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to select all challenges that your organization faces when measuring social impact.
* **What do you want to learn from your measurement practices?** (`gather_insights`, `text`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to provide a brief description of what you hope to gain from your measurement practices.
* **What type of social enterprise is your organization?** (`social_enterprise_type`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to select the type of social enterprise that your organization is.
* **What is the primary location of your organization?** (`organization_location`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to select all locations where your organization operates.
* **How many years has your organization been measuring social impact?** (`years_experience`, `number`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to enter the number of years your organization has been measuring social impact.
* **Thank you for taking the time to help us understand your measurement practices!** (`survey_finish`, `note`, not required):
	+ This section is just a note to thank the user for completing the survey.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, `email`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, `text`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to enter your phone number.
* **Who is your contact person's name?** (`contact_name`, `text`, not required):
	+ This section asks you to enter the name of the contact person.
