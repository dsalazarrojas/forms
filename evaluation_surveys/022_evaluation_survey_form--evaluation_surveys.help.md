# Evaluation Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Evaluation Survey Form is a tool used to gather feedback from students, instructors, and schools about their experience and perceptions. It is a multi-page form with various fields for different aspects of the evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the most accurate options for each question.
3. Provide additional comments or feedback in the text fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for any general information you would like to share about your experience.
* **Course Questions** (`course_questions`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the option that best describes your experience with the course.
* **Course Comments** (`course_comments`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course.
* **Instructor Questions** (`instructor_questions`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose all the options that best describe your experience with the instructor.
* **Instructor Comments** (`instructor_comments`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback about the instructor.
* **School Questions** (`school_questions`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the option that best describes your experience with the school.
* **School Comments** (`school_comments`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback about the school.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ Use this field to provide any additional information or feedback you think is relevant.
* **Student Experience** (`student_experience`, number, required: false):
	+ If applicable, provide a numerical rating of your experience with the course, instructor, and school.

Please note that some fields may not be relevant to your experience, and you can leave them blank if that's the case.
