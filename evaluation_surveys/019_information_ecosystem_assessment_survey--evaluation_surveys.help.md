# Information Ecosystem Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the assessment of the information ecosystem. The purpose of this form is to collect data on the sources and flows of information within an organization, as well as any gaps or assessments that may be identified.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start on the **Introduction** page.
* On this page, enter a brief description of the information ecosystem you are assessing.
* Move on to the **Information Sources** page.
* On this page, select all relevant options that apply to the information sources used within the organization.
* Move on to the **Information Flows** page.
* On this page, select the option that best describes the flow of information within the organization.
* Move on to the **Gap Assessment** page.
* On this page, select the option that best describes the gap within the organization's information ecosystem.
* Move on to the **Additional Notes** page.
* On this page, enter any additional comments or observations you have about the information ecosystem.
* Move on to the **Email Address** page.
* On this page, enter your email address to receive any follow-up communications.
* Move on to the **Phone** page.
* On this page, enter your phone number for any follow-up communications.
* Move on to the **Date and Time** page.
* On this page, enter the date and time of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the information ecosystem you are assessing.
* **Information Sources** (`id: 2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant sources of information used within the organization, such as publications, data sets, or people.
* **Information Flows** (`id: 3`, `select_one`, optional): Select the best description of the flow of information within the organization, such as "manual", "automated", "human-centric", etc.
* **Gap Assessment** (`id: 4`, `select_one`, optional): Select the option that best describes the gap within the organization's information ecosystem.
* **Additional Notes** (`id: 5`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations you have about the information ecosystem.
* **Email Address** (`id: 6`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive any follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (`id: 7`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communications.
* **Date and Time** (`id: 8`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time of the assessment.

Note: This form is for internal use only. Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submission.
