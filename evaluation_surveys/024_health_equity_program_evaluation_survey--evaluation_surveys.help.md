# Health Equity Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Health Equity Program Evaluation Survey is designed to gather information on your experiences, opinions, and perceptions about the Health Equity Program. Your input is crucial in helping us evaluate the program's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer all the questions thoroughly and accurately.
* For select questions, choose the best option that describes your answer.
* For text fields, provide detailed explanations and examples when possible.
* For select multiple and select one questions, select all relevant options or choose one that best describes your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic_info**:
    + Name: demographic_info (id: 2)
    + Type: select multiple
    + Purpose: This section collects demographic information about you. Please select all that apply:
        - Yes, if you have experienced any health inequity issues related to the program.
        - No, if you have not experienced any health inequity issues related to the program.
* **health_outcomes**:

    + Type: text
    + Purpose: Describe any health outcomes you have experienced or observed as a result of the program.

## Tips

* Take your time to answer all questions accurately.
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect your experiences and opinions.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the program; we appreciate your input!
