# Mid Course Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from students about their experiences during the mid-course evaluation period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the required fields. The fields marked as "required" are essential for the survey's completion.
2. Fill in the fields with your honest opinions and experiences.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (id: 1)
* **Introduction** (Note): This field is a note field where you can write any general comments about the course.

### Teaching Quality (id: 2)
* **Teaching Quality** (Number): Rate the teaching quality of the course on a scale. This field is required to help instructors improve their teaching skills.

### Content Coverage (id: 3)
* **Content Coverage** (Text): Describe how well the course covered the necessary topics and materials.

### Learning Environment (id: 4)
* **Learning Environment** (Text): Describe the learning environment of the course, including the facilities, resources, and interactions.

### Instructor Performance (id: 5)
* **Instructor Performance** (Text): Evaluate the instructor's performance, including their attitude, communication, and availability.

### Content (id: 6)
* **Content** (Text): Provide any additional comments about the course content.

### Course Organization (id: 7)
* **Course Organization** (Text): Describe how well the course was organized and managed.

### Student Feedback (id: 8)
* **Student Feedback** (Note): This field is a note field where you can provide feedback to the instructor.

### Comments (id: 9)
* **Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the course.

### Contact Information (id: 10)
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address to receive a copy of the survey report.

### Time and Date (id: 11)
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time taken to complete the survey.
