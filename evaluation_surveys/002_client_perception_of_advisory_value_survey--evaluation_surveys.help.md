> # Client Perception Of Advisory Value Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about the quality of advisory services they have received from us. The purpose of this survey is to identify areas where we excel and areas where we can improve, ultimately helping us provide better services to our clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Select the most suitable option for each question based on your experience with our advisory services.
3. Provide detailed feedback in the 'areas of improvement' and 'additional comments' sections if you have specific thoughts or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the quality of advice received?** (`advisory_quality`, `select_one`, required): This question is about the overall quality of the advice you received from us. Choose a rating that best describes your experience: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor.

* **The advisory services provide good value for the investment** (`value_for_investment`, `select_one`, required): This question is about whether the services you received provided good value for the investment you made. Choose a rating that best describes your experience: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.

* **Have the recommendations had a positive strategic impact?** (`strategic_impact`, `select_one`, required): This question is about the impact our recommendations had on your business strategy. Choose a rating that best describes your experience: Significant impact, Moderate impact, Minimal impact, No impact yet, or Negative impact.

* **How effective is the communication with your advisor?** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): This question is about how well you were informed and updated throughout the advisory process. Choose a rating that best describes your experience: Very effective, Effective, Adequate, Somewhat lacking, or Very poor.

* **The advisor demonstrates strong knowledge and expertise** (`knowledge_expertise`, `select_one`, required): This question is about the advisor's level of knowledge and expertise. Choose a rating that best describes your experience: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.

* **How timely are the advisory deliverables and recommendations?** (`timeliness_delivery`, `select_one`, required): This question is about the timeliness of the advisory deliverables and recommendations. Choose a rating that best describes your experience: Always on time, Usually on time, Sometimes delayed, Often delayed, or Always delayed.

* **How likely are you to recommend this advisor to others?** (`likelihood_recommend`, `number`, required): This question is about your likelihood to recommend the advisor to others. Choose a rating based on a scale where 0 is not at all likely and 10 is extremely likely.

* **What areas could be improved in the advisory relationship?** (`areas_improvement`, `text`, optional): This question is for any additional thoughts or concerns you may have about the advisory relationship. Feel free to share specific areas that could be improved.

* **Are you planning to continue working with this advisor?** (`future_engagement`, `select_one`, required): This question is about your plans to continue working with the advisor. Choose a rating that best describes your experience: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Uncertain, Probably not, or Definitely not.

* **What additional services would be valuable to you?** (`additional_services`, `text`, optional): This question is for any additional services you think would be valuable to you in the future.

* **Additional comments or feedback** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, optional): This question is for any other thoughts or comments you may have about the advisory services. Please share any specific thoughts, concerns, or suggestions you may have.
