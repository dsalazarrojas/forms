# Risk Monitoring Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to evaluate the effectiveness of risk monitoring within your organization. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible to help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete this form by providing information about your organization's risk monitoring processes.
2. Answer each question carefully, and choose the best option that reflects your organization's current state.
3. If you need help with a question, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): This is an optional introduction section. If you need to provide some background information about your organization's risk monitoring processes, you can type it here.
* **Organization** (`2`, `number`, optional): Enter your organization's internal ID.
* **Monitoring Method** (`3`, `text`, optional): Describe the method your organization uses for risk monitoring (e.g., regular check-ins, risk assessments, etc.).
* **Risk Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of risk your organization is monitoring (e.g., Risk Type 1, Risk Type 2, Risk Type 3).
* **Risk Frequency** (`5`, `number`, optional): Enter the frequency at which your organization monitors risks (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Risk Impact** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Choose the impact level of the risks your organization is monitoring (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk Score** (`7`, `number`, required): Enter a score between 1 and 10 to assess the level of risk your organization is monitoring.
* **Risk Comment** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the risks your organization is monitoring.
* **Action Plan** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the actions your organization has taken or plans to take to address risks (e.g., Action 1, Action 2, Action 3).
* **Risk Level** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of risk your organization is monitoring (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Mitigation Action** (`11`, `text`, optional): Describe any actions taken or planned to mitigate risks.
* **Monitoring Frequency** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Choose the frequency at which your organization monitors risks (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Review Cycle** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Choose the cycle at which your organization reviews risks (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Risk Owner** (`14`, `number`, optional): Enter the ID of the person responsible for risk monitoring.
* **Risk Owner Email** (`15`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for risk monitoring.
* **Risk Owner Phone** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for risk monitoring.
* **Review Date** (`17`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the next risk review.
* **Review Time** (`18`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the next risk review.
* **Comments** (`19`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about risk monitoring.
* **Attachment Uploads** (`20`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of attachments uploaded.
* **Risk Monitoring Effectiveness** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Choose the effectiveness of your organization's risk monitoring process (e.g., Not Effective, Somewhat Effective, Very Effective).
* **Risk Monitoring Process** (`22`, `text`, optional): Describe your organization's risk monitoring process.
* **Risk Reporting** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether your organization reports risks (e.g., False, True).
* **Risk Review Cycle** (`24`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether your organization reviews risks (e.g., False, True).
* **Risk Rating** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Choose the rating of your organization's risk monitoring process (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
