# Virtual Wellness Challenge Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants who have completed the Virtual Wellness Challenge. The feedback will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. 
2. If you need help or clarification, feel free to ask.
3. Make sure to save your progress and review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **participant_id**
* `participant_id` (number, optional)

This field is for your participant ID. You will need this to identify your participation in the challenge.

### 2. **Name**
* `Name` (text, optional)

Please enter your full name for identification purposes.

### 3. **Experience**
* `Experience` (number, 1-5, optional)

Rate your experience in the Virtual Wellness Challenge on a scale from 1-5.

### 4. **Feedback**
* `Feedback` (text, optional)

Share any thoughts or comments about your experience with us.

### 5. **Suggestions**
* `Suggestions` (text, optional)

Provide any suggestions you may have for improving the Virtual Wellness Challenge.

### 6. **What did we do well?**
* `What did we do well?` (text, optional)

Tell us what we did well in the Virtual Wellness Challenge.

### 7. **What can we do better?**
* `What can we do better?` (text, optional)

Share with us what we can improve on in the Virtual Wellness Challenge.

### 8. **Program**
* `Program` (number, 1-5, optional)

Rate your experience with our program on a scale from 1-5.

### 9. **Program Improvement**
* `Program Improvement` (text, optional)

Provide any feedback on how we can improve our program.

### 10. **Would you like to be contacted?**
* `Would you like to be contacted?` (select one, optional)

Please select "Yes" if you'd like to be contacted regarding future challenges or programs.

### 11. **Email**
* `Email` (email, optional)

Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.

### 12. **Phone**
* `Phone` (text, optional)

Please enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.

### 13. **Comments**
* `Comments` (note, optional)

Any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
