# Nonprofit Board Governance Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Nonprofit Board Governance Practices Survey is designed to collect information about your nonprofit organization's governance practices. This data will be used to identify best practices and areas for improvement, helping organizations like yours to become more effective and efficient in their governance and leadership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question as honestly and accurately as possible.
2. Select all applicable options for each field, as some questions allow multiple responses.
3. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the form administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Board Size
* **Board size**: This question asks whether your nonprofit organization's board size is appropriate for effective governance.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board size is suitable for your organization's needs.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to reassess your board size for optimal effectiveness.

### Board Meetings
* **Board Meetings**: This question asks whether your board meetings are conducted regularly and are effective in decision-making.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board meetings are well-organized and productive.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to improve your meeting structure and frequency.

### Board Meeting Frequency
* **Board Meeting Frequency**: This question asks how frequently your board meets.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board meets frequently enough for effective governance.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to increase meeting frequency for better decision-making.

### Leadership Structure
* **Leadership Structure**: This question asks about your board's leadership structure and distribution of responsibilities.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board's leadership structure is clear and effective.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to reevaluate your leadership structure for better outcomes.

### Evaluation Process
* **Evaluation Process**: This question asks about the regularity and effectiveness of your board evaluation process.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board evaluations are thorough and constructive.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to improve your evaluation process for better governance.

### Conflict of Interest
* **Conflict of Interest**: Please describe any potential conflicts of interest that your board members may have.
	+ Please provide any relevant information about potential conflicts of interest, such as financial ties or personal relationships.

### Financial Management
* **Financial Management**: This question asks whether your board has adequate financial management practices in place.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board's financial management is sound and effective.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to improve your financial management practices.

### Financial Reporting
* **Financial Reporting**: This question asks whether your board receives timely and accurate financial reporting.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board receives regular and trustworthy financial updates.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to improve your financial reporting practices.

### Financial Committee
* **Financial Committee**: This question asks whether your board has a financial committee for overseeing financial matters.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board has a dedicated financial oversight committee.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to establish a financial committee for better fiscal management.

### Audit Committee
* **Audit Committee**: This question asks whether your board has an audit committee for financial oversight.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board has an audit committee to review financial records.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to establish an audit committee for improved financial integrity.

### Other Committee
* **Other Committee**: This question asks whether your board has any other committees for specific purposes.
	+ "Yes" indicates that you have other committees addressing specific needs.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to establish additional committees for better governance.

### Non-Profit Financial Management
* **Non-Profit Financial Management**: This question asks whether your board has adequate financial management practices for non-profit organizations.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board's financial management is effective for your non-profit organization.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to improve your financial management practices for better non-profit outcomes.

### Non-Profit Evaluation Frequency
* **Non-Profit Evaluation Frequency**: This question asks how frequently your board evaluates its financial management practices for non-profit organizations.
	+ "Yes" indicates that your board evaluates financial management regularly.
	+ "No" indicates that you may need to increase evaluation frequency for better non-profit governance.

Please review each field carefully and select the most accurate answer for your nonprofit organization.
