# Remediation Services Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remediation Services Survey form is designed to gather feedback from customers on the services provided by the organization, aiming to enhance service quality and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and provide honest feedback on the services you have received.
- Select the service type that best describes the service you received.
- Rate your satisfaction with the services provided using the provided scale.
- Write any comments or suggestions for service improvement.
- Enter your email address and contact number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select the type of service you received. Select all options that apply.
	+ Yes: Select this option if you received the service.
	+ No: Select this option if you did not receive the service.
* **What service received** (Text Field): This field is for you to describe the service you received. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (Select One): Rate your satisfaction with the services provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Quality** (Select One): Rate the quality of the service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Value** (Select One): Rate the value of the service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select One): Rate your overall satisfaction with the services provided on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Improvement** (Text Field): Share your thoughts on how the service can be improved. Be specific and provide concrete suggestions.
* **Service Improvement Ideas** (Text Field): If you have any ideas for improving the service, provide them here.
* **Comments** (Text Field): Leave any additional comments or feedback about the services provided.
* **Email** (Email Field): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Number** (Text Field): Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
