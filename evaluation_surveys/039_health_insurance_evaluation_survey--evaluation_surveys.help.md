# health_insurance_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experiences with health insurance plans. Your input will help us improve the quality of health insurance services for our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Policyholder Information** section with your details.
   - **Policyholder Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant information related to your policy.

2. Select your **Health Insurance Plan**:
   - Choose the type of health insurance plan you hold (e.g., Medicare, Blue Cross, Blue Shield, or Private Insurance).

3. Rate your **Rating**:
   - Enter a numerical rating for your experience (1-5).

4. Provide your **Satisfaction**:
   - Rate your satisfaction level with your health insurance plan (1-5).

5. Enter **Comments**:
   - Share your thoughts and experiences with your health insurance plan.

6. Rate your **Policyholder Rating**:
   - Rate your experience with the policyholder (1-5).

7. Choose your **Rating Scale**:
   - Select the rating scale that best matches your experience (1-5).

8. Enter the **Date**:
   - Select the date you last accessed your health insurance plan.

9. Enter the **Time**:
   - Enter the time you last accessed your health insurance plan.

10. Enter your **Email**:
   - Enter your email address to receive any follow-up communications or updates.

11. Enter your **Phone Number**:
   - Enter your phone number to receive any follow-up communications or updates.

12. Enter your **Policyholder Name**:
   - Enter the name of the policyholder you are providing feedback on.
