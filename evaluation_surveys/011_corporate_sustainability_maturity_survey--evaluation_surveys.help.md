# Corporate Sustainability Maturity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This corporate sustainability maturity survey is designed to evaluate an organization's performance in various sustainability domains. It assesses the organization's current level of sustainability maturity and identifies areas for improvement. The survey consists of 10 pages with different sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please start by selecting the option that best describes your organization's performance in the Corporate Sustainability Performance section.
2.  Choose the domains where your organization is currently active or interested in.
3.  Select the top 2-3 challenges that your organization is facing in terms of sustainability.
4.  Describe your organization's sustainability strategy and current initiatives.
5.  Provide a score between 1 and 10 to indicate your organization's current maturity level.
6.  Select the domains where your organization plans to be active in the future.
7.  In the "Future Sustainability Domains" section, provide a detailed description of your organization's plans and goals.
8.  Please provide a detailed description of your organization's current sustainability performance and areas for improvement.
9.  In the "Potential Benefits of Sustainability" section, describe how your organization can benefit from implementing sustainability practices.
10.  Select the option that best describes your organization's approach to benchmarking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Sustainability Performance** (`Corporate Sustainability Performance`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes your organization's current performance level in corporate sustainability.
* **Sustainability Domains** (`Sustainability Domains`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the domains where your organization is currently active or interested in.
* **Sustainability Challenges** (`Sustainability Challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the top 2-3 challenges that your organization is facing in terms of sustainability.
* **Sustainability Strategy** (`Sustainability Strategy`, `text`, required): Describe your organization's sustainability strategy and current initiatives.
* **Maturity Levels** (`Maturity Levels`, `number`, required): Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your organization's current maturity level.
* **Future Sustainability Domains** (`Future Sustainability Domains`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the domains where your organization plans to be active in the future.
* **Page 8** (`Page 8`, `text`, required): Please provide a detailed description of your organization's current sustainability performance and areas for improvement.
* **Potential Benefits of Sustainability** (`Potential Benefits of Sustainability`, `text`, required): In this section, describe how your organization can benefit from implementing sustainability practices.
* **Benchmarking** (`Benchmarking`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your organization's approach to benchmarking.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out each section as accurately and thoroughly as possible. The results will help your organization identify areas for improvement and provide a baseline for future sustainability performance.
