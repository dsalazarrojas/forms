# Change Leadership Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the leadership effectiveness of a manager in leading a change initiative. It consists of 21 questions that assess the manager's performance in various areas, including strategic planning, team engagement, communication, and results achievement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoughtfully.
2. Select the option that best describes your experience with the manager's leadership.
3. You may skip questions that do not apply to your experience or knowledge.
