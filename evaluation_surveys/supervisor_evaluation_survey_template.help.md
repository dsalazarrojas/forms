# supervisor_evaluation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supervisor evaluation survey is a tool used to gather feedback on a supervisor's performance and identify areas for improvement. This form helps to provide a clear understanding of the supervisor's strengths and weaknesses, allowing them to make data-driven decisions for their own development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with your supervisor.
3. Provide specific examples or details when possible.
4. Answer the open-ended questions thoroughly.
