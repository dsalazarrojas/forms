# conference_evaluation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The conference evaluation survey is designed to gather feedback from attendees to help organizers improve future conference experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question about your overall satisfaction with the conference.
2. Select all the aspects of the conference that you found most valuable (you can select multiple options).
3. Indicate whether you think the event was well-organized and managed.
4. Express your likelihood of attending the conference again in the future.
5. Share any suggestions you have for improving future conferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied were you with the conference overall?** (`Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied`): Please choose the option that best reflects your overall feeling about the conference.
* **Which aspects of the conference did you find most valuable?** (`Keynote Speeches, Panel Discussions, Workshops, Networking Events, Poster Sessions, Exhibition Hall`): Select all the options that apply to the aspects you found most valuable. This will help us understand what made the conference experience worthwhile for you.
* **Was the event well organized and managed?** (`true, false, Partially`): Answer this question honestly to help us evaluate the event's organization and management.
* **How likely are you to attend this conference again in the future?** (`Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not likely`): Select the option that reflects your willingness to attend future conferences like this one.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving future conferences?** (text field): Share your thoughts and ideas on how we can improve future conference experiences. Your feedback is valuable and will help shape the future of conferences in the industry.
