# Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Evaluation Survey is a tool used to gather feedback on employee performance and identify areas for improvement. It is meant to be completed by managers or evaluators who need to assess an employee's performance and provide constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the category of the performance evaluation from the "Evaluation Survey Category" dropdown menu.
2. Answer the "Performance Rating" question with a number to indicate the employee's performance level.
3. For each development area, rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
4. In the "Areas for Improvement" text field, provide specific comments on areas where the employee needs to develop their skills.
5. In the "Development Areas Comments" text field, provide feedback on the development areas identified.
6. In the "Evaluation Survey Comments" text field, provide any additional comments or feedback on the employee's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Survey Category (Evaluation Surveys, Other, Training)**: Select the category of the performance evaluation from the dropdown menu.
* **Performance Rating (Number)**: Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Development Areas (Number)**: Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1 to 5 for each development area.
* **Strengths (Number)**: Rate the employee's strengths on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Areas for Improvement (Text)**: Provide specific comments on areas where the employee needs to develop their skills.
* **Development Areas Comments (Text)**: Provide feedback on the development areas identified.
* **Evaluation Survey Comments (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the employee's performance.
