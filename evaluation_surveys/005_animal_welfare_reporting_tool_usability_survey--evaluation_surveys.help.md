# Animal Welfare Reporting Tool Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Welfare Reporting Tool Usability Survey is designed to gather feedback from users on the usability of the tool, helping the developers and administrators make improvements to enhance the user experience. Your input is valuable in shaping the future of the tool, and we appreciate your time and effort in completing this survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Respondent Information** section, providing your name, email address, and organization or shelter name.
2. Select your role within the organization or shelter.
3. Answer questions about your **Tool Usage Experience**:
	* How often you use the tool.
	* How long you've been using the tool.
4. Rate the tool's **Effectiveness**:
	* Ease of use.
	* Feature accessibility.
	* Data entry speed.
5. Rate the tool's **Features and Functionality**:
	* Select the features you use most frequently.
6. Identify areas for **Improvement**:
	* Biggest frustration with the tool.
	* Features or functionality missing.
7. Provide **Suggestions for Improvement**.
8. Evaluate the tool's **Training and Support**:
	* Quality of technical support.
	* Adequacy of training.
9. Finally, provide an **Overall Assessment**:
	* Recommend the tool to other organizations.
	* Overall satisfaction with the tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required)
Your name, as you'd like to be addressed.

### 2. **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required)
The email address we'll use for follow-up questions and correspondence.

### 3. **Organization or Shelter Name** (`organization`, `text`, required)
The name of your affiliated organization or shelter.

### 4. **Your Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required)
Select one of the following roles:
	* Shelter Manager.
	* Animal Care Staff.
	* Veterinarian.
	* Administrative Staff.
	* Volunteer.
	* Supervisor.
	* Other.

### 5. **How Often Do You Use This Reporting Tool** (`usage_frequency`, `select_one`, required)
Select one of the following frequencies:
	* Daily.
	* Several times per week.
	* Weekly.
	* Monthly.
	* Less frequently.

### 6. **How Long Have You Been Using This Tool** (`usage_duration`, `select_one`, required)
Select one of the following durations:
	* Less than 1 month.
	* 1-3 months.
	* 3-6 months.
	* 6-12 months.
	* Over 1 year.

### 7. **How Easy Is This Tool to Use** (`ease_of_use`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the overall ease of use:
	* Very easy - Intuitive.
	* Easy - Minor learning curve.
	* Moderate - Requires some training.
	* Difficult - Challenging.
	* Very difficult - Needs improvement.

### 8. **Are the Reporting Features Easy to Find and Access** (`feature_accessibility`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the feature organization and navigation:
	* Excellent - Very accessible.
	* Good - Accessible with minimal effort.
	* Adequate - Somewhat difficult to find.
	* Poor - Hard to locate features.
	* Very poor - Major navigation issues.

### 9. **How Quickly Can You Complete a Report** (`data_entry_speed`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the speed of data entry:
	* Very fast - Under 5 minutes.
	* Fast - 5-15 minutes.
	* Moderate - 15-30 minutes.
	* Slow - 30-60 minutes.
	* Very slow - Over 1 hour.

### 10. **How Confident Are You in the Accuracy of Submitted Data** (`data_accuracy`, `select_one`, required)
Rate your confidence in data accuracy:
	* Very confident - System validates well.
	* Confident - Good validation.
	* Neutral - Sometimes errors occur.
	* Not confident - Frequent errors.
	* Very not confident - Major issues.

### 11. **How Effective Is the Search and Filter Functionality** (`search_functionality`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the search and filtering features:
	* Excellent - Very powerful and helpful.
	* Good - Works well for most needs.
	* Adequate - Basic functionality.
	* Poor - Limited usefulness.
	* Very poor - Not functional.

### 12. **Which Features Do You Use Most Frequently** (`specific_features`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select the features you use most frequently (you can select multiple options).

### 13. **What Is the Biggest Frustration with This Tool** (`biggest_frustration`, `text`, required)
Describe your main challenges with the tool.

### 14. **What Features or Functionality Are Missing** (`missing_features`, `text`, required)
What would make the tool more useful for you?

### 15. **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, required)
Your specific recommendations for improvement.

### 16. **How Would You Rate the Technical Support Provided** (`support_quality`, `select_one`, required)
Select one of the following ratings:
	* Excellent - Responsive and helpful.
	* Good - Generally helpful.
	* Adequate - Acceptable.
	* Poor - Limited help.
	* Not available - No support received.

### 17. **Did You Receive Adequate Training** (`training_adequacy`, `select_one`, required)
Select one of the following:
	* Yes, very adequate training.
	* Yes, adequate training.
	* Neutral - Some training provided.
	* No, limited training.
	* No training provided.

### 18. **Would You Recommend This Tool to Other Organizations** (`recommend_tool`, `select_one`, required)
Select one of the following:
	* Yes, highly recommend.
	* Yes, recommend with reservations.
	* Neutral - It has strengths and weaknesses.
	* No, do not recommend.
	* No, not suitable for other organizations.

### 19. **Overall Satisfaction with the Tool** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required)
Rate your overall satisfaction:
	* Very satisfied.
	* Satisfied.
	* Neutral.
	* Dissatisfied.
	* Very dissatisfied.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as your feedback is invaluable to improving the tool.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the support team.
* Your responses will help shape the future of the Animal Welfare Reporting Tool, and we appreciate your input.
