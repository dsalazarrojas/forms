# Gym Staff Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Staff Training Effectiveness Survey is a tool to gather feedback from gym staff on the effectiveness of the training programs they have attended. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better training experiences for our staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Select the training program you have attended from the "Training Program" dropdown menu.
3. Rate the effectiveness of the training program by selecting one or more options from the "Rating" dropdown menu.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes about the training program in the "Notes" field.
5. Indicate who submitted the form, either "Staff", "Manager", or "Customer".
6. Select the date and time when you submitted the form.
7. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback report.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further questions or follow-up.
* **Training Program** (`Training Program`, select_one, required): Select the specific training program you have attended.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, select_multiple, required): Rate the effectiveness of the training program by selecting one or more options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the training program.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, select_one, required): Indicate who submitted the form, either "Staff", "Manager", or "Customer".
* **Submitted On** (`Submitted On`, date, required): Select the date when you submitted the form.
* **Submitted At** (`Submitted At`, time, required): Select the time when you submitted the form.

## Tips

* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Only provide feedback on training programs you have attended.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at [contact email or phone number].
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
