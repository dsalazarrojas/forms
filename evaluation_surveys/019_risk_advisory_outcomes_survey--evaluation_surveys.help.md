# Risk Advisory Outcomes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track data on the outcomes of a risk advisory.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have read and understood the instructions and the form before proceeding.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your assessment of the risk advisory.
3. Enter any relevant information in the fields marked as required.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **evaluation_survey** (id: 2, type: select_one, required: false): Select the type of evaluation survey that was performed (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **team size** (id: 3, type: number, required: false): Enter the size of the team involved in the risk advisory (e.g. 5).
* **total value** (id: 4, type: number, required: false): Enter the total value of the risk advisory (e.g. $10,000).
* **risk_assessment** (id: 5, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the risk assessment options that apply (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **evaluation_date** (id: 6, type: date, required: false): Enter the date on which the evaluation was performed (e.g. 2022-01-01).
* **evaluation_time** (id: 7, type: time, required: false): Enter the time at which the evaluation was performed (e.g. 14:00).
* **risk_description** (id: 8, type: note, required: false): Enter any additional information about the risk advisory (e.g. This risk advisory was performed for a software project).
* **email** (id: 9, type: email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person who performed the risk advisory (e.g. [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com)).
* **phone** (id: 10, type: text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person who performed the risk advisory (e.g. 01234 567890).
* **project_id** (id: 11, type: number, required: false): Enter the project ID associated with the risk advisory (e.g. PRJ-123).
* **project_name** (id: 12, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the project associated with the risk advisory (e.g. RiskyProject).
* **team_name** (id: 13, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the team involved in the risk advisory (e.g. Risk Team).
* **team_leader** (id: 14, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the team leader involved in the risk advisory (e.g. John Doe).
* **risk_level** (id: 15, type: select_one, required: false): Select the risk level (e.g. High, Medium, Low).
* **risk_score** (id: 16, type: number, required: false): Enter the risk score (e.g. 8).
* **risk_impact** (id: 17, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the risk impact options that apply (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **risk_probability** (id: 18, type: number, required: false): Enter the risk probability (e.g. 0.5).
* **risk_frequency** (id: 19, type: text, required: false): Enter the risk frequency (e.g. daily).
* **risk_awareness** (id: 20, type: select_one, required: false): Select the risk awareness option that applies (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **risk_impact_score** (id: 21, type: number, required: false): Enter the risk impact score (e.g. 9).
* **risk_score_awareness** (id: 22, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the risk score awareness options that apply (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **risk_risk_score_awareness** (id: 23, type: select_one, required: false): Select the risk risk score awareness option that applies (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **risk_risk_awareness** (id: 24, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the risk risk awareness options that apply (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **risk_risk_frequency_score** (id: 25, type: number, required: false): Enter the risk risk frequency score (e.g. 0.7).
* **risk_risk_frequency** (id: 26, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the risk risk frequency options that apply (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
