# Health Communication Improvement Project Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Communication Improvement Project Evaluation form is a tool used to gather feedback and evaluation on the project's overall rating, identify areas that require improvement, gather suggestions, and obtain contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief introduction to your feedback (page 1).
2. Rate the project overall (page 2).
3. Share any feedback you may have about the project (page 3).
4. Suggest any improvements or areas for enhancement (page 4).
5. Select your preferred rating for the first aspect of the project (page 5).
6. Select your preferred rating for the second aspect of the project (page 6).
7. Provide a brief suggestion for the first aspect of the project (page 7).
8. Provide a brief suggestion for the second aspect of the project (page 8).
9. Enter your email address (page 9).
10. Enter your phone number or any other contact information you'd like to share (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief introduction to your feedback.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the project overall, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share any feedback you may have about the project.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, required: false): Suggest any improvements or areas for enhancement.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred rating for the first aspect of the project.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred rating for the second aspect of the project.
* **Suggestion 1** (`suggestion_1`, text, required: false): Provide a brief suggestion for the first aspect of the project.
* **Suggestion 2** (`suggestion_2`, text, required: false): Provide a brief suggestion for the second aspect of the project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number or any other contact information you'd like to share.
