# Persuasive Text Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the user's evaluation of a persuasive text.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the questions on the first page, which asks for a brief introduction to the text.
2. Continue to the next page, where you will be asked to provide more detailed information about the text.
3. On this page, you will find a mix of text-based questions, numerical ratings, multiple-choice questions, and date/time selections.
4. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_this_form_for**: This is the first question on the form, where you are asked to briefly describe the purpose or context of the text. Please provide a short answer in text format.
* **introduction_text**: This question is also open-ended, asking you to provide a brief description of the text. Please answer in text format.
* **introduction_1**: This is a numerical rating question, where you will rate your overall impression of the text on a scale of 1-10. Please select a number that best represents your opinion.
* **introduction_2**: Similar to the previous question, this is another numerical rating question. Please rate your opinion of the text's content on a scale of 1-10.
* **introduction_3**: This question asks you to select the option that best describes your opinion about the text's structure. Please choose one of the three options provided.
* **introduction_4**: This question asks you to select all that apply from the list of options provided. Please choose one or more of the options that best describe your opinion about the text.
* **introduction_5**: This question asks for a date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD, representing the date you first read or encountered the text.
* **introduction_6**: This is a time-of-day question, where you will select a time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **introduction_7**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about the text. Please keep your answer brief.
* **introduction_8**: This is an email field, where you can provide your contact information (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions).
* **introduction_9**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about the text.
* **introduction_10**: This is a numerical rating question, where you will rate your overall opinion of the text on a scale of 1-10. Please select a number that best represents your opinion.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
* Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and be honest in your evaluation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
