This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a particular vendor. The purpose of this form is to help us understand the customer's satisfaction with the goods or services provided by the vendor, their communication, timeliness of delivery, price, vendor performance, culture, punctuality, tact, courtesy, and overall value received from the vendor. It is also used to identify areas for improvement for the vendor to enhance their performance.

Before proceeding, please note that this form is not intended to be a comprehensive evaluation of the vendor, but rather a general feedback tool to help us improve our services. Your input is valuable in helping us understand your experience and make informed decisions.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For select_one, select_one_multiple, and select_multiple fields, choose the corresponding option that best describes your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer in the space provided.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

* **Quality of Goods or Services** (`quality_of_goods_or_services`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply to describe the quality of the goods or services provided by the vendor.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your experience with the vendor's communication.
* **Timeliness of Delivery** (`timeliness_of_delivery`, number, required: false): Rate the vendor's timeliness of delivery on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Vendor Performance** (`vendor_performance`, select_one, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the vendor's performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Price** (`price`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the price of the goods or services provided by the vendor.
* **Vendor Culture** (`vendor_culture`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply to describe the vendor's culture.
* **Vendor Punctuality** (`vendor_punctuality`, select_one, required: false): Rate your experience with the vendor's punctuality.
* **Vendor Tact** (`vendor_tact`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply to describe the vendor's tact.
* **Vendor Courtesy** (`vendor_courtesy`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply to describe the vendor's courtesy.
* **Vendor Waste** (`vendor_waste`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply to describe the vendor's waste.
* **Overall Value** (`overall_value`, number, required: false): Rate the overall value you received from the vendor on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Vendor Performance Improvement** (`vendor_performance_improvement`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply to describe areas for improvement in the vendor's performance.
