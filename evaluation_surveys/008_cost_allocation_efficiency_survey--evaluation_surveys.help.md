# Cost Allocation Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost Allocation Efficiency Survey is a tool to assess the effectiveness and transparency of an organization's cost allocation process. It aims to identify areas of improvement and provide insights into the efficiency of cost allocation practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select one page at a time to complete the form, working from left to right.
2. For each field, provide detailed and honest responses to the questions asked.
3. You can use the space provided for each field to elaborate on your answers if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Current Transparency
* **Current Transparency** (`current_transparency`, `text`, **Optional**):
    + Explanation: This field is intended to gauge the current level of transparency in the cost allocation process. Describe the current state of transparency in your organization. What methods, tools, or procedures are being used to ensure costs are accurately and clearly allocated and reported? 

### Page 2: Cost Allocation Process
* **Cost Allocation Process** (`allocation_process`, `text`, **Optional**):
    + Explanation: This field is meant to explore the process currently in use for cost allocation within your organization. What steps are taken to allocate costs? Are there any specific rules, regulations, or standards followed? 

### Page 3: Cost Allocation Challenges
* **Cost Allocation Challenges** (`cost_allocation_challenges`, `text`, **Optional**):
    + Explanation: This field is designed to identify any challenges faced during the cost allocation process. Describe any difficulties or bottlenecks encountered in allocating costs, such as data inaccuracies, process inefficiencies, or resource constraints. 

## Tips
- Be thorough and honest in your responses to provide actionable insights.
- If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, please reach out to the form's creators for guidance.
- The information collected will help in enhancing the cost allocation process within the organization.
