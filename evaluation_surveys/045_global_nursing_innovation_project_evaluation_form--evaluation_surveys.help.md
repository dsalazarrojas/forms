# Global Nursing Innovation Project Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate projects under the Global Nursing Innovation Project, gathering essential information and feedback on their progress, goals, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your project name in the "Project Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Specify the target area for your project in the "Target Area" field.
4. Identify the population your project is targeting in the "Target Population" field.
5. Describe the methodology used for innovation in the "Innovation Methodology" field.
6. Outline the goals of your project in the "Innovation Goals" field.
7. Explain how you implemented your project in the "Project Implementation" field.
8. Discuss the project's sustainability in the "Project Sustainability" field.
9. Describe the innovation used in your project in the "Project Innovation" field.
10. Outline the capacity of your team in the "Team Capacity" field.
11. Explain the methodology used for evaluation in the "Evaluation Methodology" field.
12. List the tools used for evaluation in the "Evaluation Tools" field.
13. Specify the frequency of evaluation in the "Evaluation Frequency" field.
14. Describe the outcome of your project in the "Innovation Outcome" field.
15. Discuss the impact of your project in the "Innovation Impact" field.
16. Provide recommendations for future improvements in the "Innovation Recommendations" field.
17. Confirm your form submission in the "Form Submission" field.
18. Enter the date and time of evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" and "Evaluation Time" fields.
19. Enter your contact email in the "Email" field.
20. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
21. Leave a note if you want to add any additional comments in the "Note" field.
22. Enter the time of form submission in the "Form Submission Time" field.
23. Provide a score out of 100 for the project's evaluation in the "Evaluation Score" field.
24. Select the status of your form submission from the options: Active, Not Active, or Completed, in the "Form Submission Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name (1)**: Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description (2)**: Describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Target Area (3)**: Identify the specific area that your project is targeting.
* **Target Population (4)**: Specify the population that your project is targeting.
* **Innovation Methodology (5)**: Describe the approach used for innovation in your project.
* **Innovation Goals (6)**: Outline the objectives of your project.
* **Project Implementation (7)**: Explain how you implemented your project.
* **Project Sustainability (8)**: Discuss the long-term sustainability of your project.
* **Project Innovation (9)**: Describe the innovation used in your project.
* **Team Capacity (10)**: Outline the capacity of your team.
* **Evaluation Methodology (11)**: Explain the methodology used for evaluation.
* **Evaluation Tools (12)**: List the tools used for evaluation.
* **Evaluation Frequency (13)**: Specify the frequency of evaluation.
* **Innovation Outcome (14)**: Describe the outcome of your project.
* **Innovation Impact (15)**: Discuss the impact of your project.
* **Innovation Recommendations (16)**: Provide recommendations for future improvements.
* **Form Submission (17)**: Confirm your form submission.
* **Evaluation Date (18)**: Enter the date of evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time (19)**: Enter the time of evaluation.
* **Email (20)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (21)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note (22)**: Leave a note if needed.
* **Form Submission Time (23)**: Enter the time of form submission.
* **Evaluation Score (24)**: Provide a score out of 100 for the project.
* **Form Submission Status (25)**: Select the status of your form submission.
