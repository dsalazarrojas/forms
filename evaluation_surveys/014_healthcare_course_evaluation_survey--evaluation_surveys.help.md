# Healthcare Course Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from students who have taken a healthcare course. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the course's effectiveness, quality, and instructor performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information about the course.
2. Enter your response to each field according to your personal experience.
3. Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Course Name**
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course you took.

### 2. **Course Format**
* **Course Format** (`course_format`, select_one, required: false): Choose the format of the course, which was in person, online, or hybrid.

### 3. **Course Duration**
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, required: false): If you know, enter the duration of the course in hours or weeks.

### 4. **Instructor Name**
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course instructor.

### 5. **Course Rating**
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the course out of 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### 6. **Feedback**
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the course.

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive with your feedback. This will help us improve the course for future students.
* Make sure to provide all the necessary information about the course you took.
* If you're unsure about any field, please leave it blank or contact us for clarification.
