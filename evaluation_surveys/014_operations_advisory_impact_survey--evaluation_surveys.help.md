# operations_advisory_impact_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The operations advisory impact survey form is designed to collect feedback from teams and team leads on their engagement and perceived impact on operations. This feedback will help to improve communication, collaboration, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each page carefully and enter information as prompted.
2.  Make sure to enter information accurately and completely.
3.  For fields marked as required, please ensure that you enter the requested information.
