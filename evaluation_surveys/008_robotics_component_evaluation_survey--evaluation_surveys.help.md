# Robotics Component Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather evaluations of robotics components, helping robotics development teams identify areas of improvement and optimize their products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering a brief introduction or background information about the robotics component you are evaluating (Page 1).
2. Fill in the Robot Name field with the name of the component you are evaluating (Page 2).
3. Enter the name of the specific component being evaluated (Page 3).
4. Provide a numerical rating for the Performance of the component, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest (Page 4).
5. Enter any additional comments about the performance of the component (Page 5).
6. Provide a numerical rating for the Reliability of the component, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest (Page 6).
7. Enter any additional comments about the reliability of the component (Page 7).
8. Provide a numerical rating for the Cost of the component, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest (Page 8).
9. Enter any additional comments about the cost of the component (Page 9).
10. Provide a numerical rating for the Ease of Use of the component, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest (Page 10).
11. Enter any additional comments about the ease of use of the component (Page 11).
12. Finally, provide an overall rating for the component, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest (Page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (Page 1)
*   `Introduction` (text, required: false): Enter a brief introduction or background information about the component you are evaluating.

### 2. **Robot Name** (Page 2)
*   `Robot Name` (text, required: false): The name of the component you are evaluating.

### 3. **Component Name** (Page 3)
*   `Component Name` (text, required: false): The specific name of the component being evaluated.

### 4. **Performance Rating** (Page 4)
*   `Performance Rating` (number, required: false): Rate the performance of the component on a scale from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### 5. **Performance Comments** (Page 5)
*   `Performance Comments` (text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the performance of the component.

### 6. **Reliability Rating** (Page 6)
*   `Reliability Rating` (number, required: false): Rate the reliability of the component on a scale from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### 7. **Reliability Comments** (Page 7)
*   `Reliability Comments` (text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the reliability of the component.

### 8. **Cost Rating** (Page 8)
*   `Cost Rating` (number, required: false): Rate the cost of the component on a scale from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### 9. **Cost Comments** (Page 9)
*   `Cost Comments` (text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the cost of the component.

### 10. **Ease of Use Rating** (Page 10)
*   `Ease of Use Rating` (number, required: false): Rate the ease of use of the component on a scale from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### 11. **Ease of Use Comments** (Page 11)
*   `Ease of Use Comments` (text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the ease of use of the component.

### 12. **Overall Rating** (Page 12)
*   `Overall Rating` (number, required: false): Rate the component overall on a scale from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

## Tips
*   Please be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you need more information about a particular field, refer back to the pages where the field is located.
*   If you have any questions or need help, contact your supervisor or administrator.
