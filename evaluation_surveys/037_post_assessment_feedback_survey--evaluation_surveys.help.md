The Post Assessment Feedback Survey is an evaluation tool used to gather feedback from participants after an assessment event. It is essential to ensure that each participant understands and completes the form accurately to provide reliable and valuable feedback.

To complete this form, participants should follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2.  Answer every question, as the optional status of the form only suggests that participants can skip questions if they do not want to provide feedback on a particular point.
3.  For questions that have a scale rating, use the corresponding rating system to provide your rating.
4.  For open-ended questions, please provide clear and concise answers in the text boxes.

## Post Assessment Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Post Assessment Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from participants after an assessment event. This feedback will help us improve and refine the assessment process to ensure it meets the needs of all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review each question carefully and answer them honestly.
*   Answer every question, as the optional status of the form only suggests that participants can skip questions if they do not want to provide feedback on a particular point.
*   For questions that have a scale rating, use the corresponding rating system to provide your rating.
*   For open-ended questions, please provide clear and concise answers in the text boxes.
