# ohsms_maturity_assessment_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the maturity level of an organization's Operational Hazardous Materials and Safety Management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to complete this form.
2. Review the form's sections and questions, and ensure that you have the necessary information to answer them.
3. Answer the questions honestly based on your actual experience and knowledge of your organization's safety management system.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your colleagues or safety experts before providing an answer.
