# Performance Feedback Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Feedback Quality Survey is a tool used to gather feedback on the performance of individuals. The feedback collected through this form will help identify areas of improvement and provide constructive criticism.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Reviewee's name in the Reviewee field.
2. Enter your name in the Reviewer field.
3. Rate the performance of the Reviewee using the Rating 1, 2, and 3 fields.
4. Provide comments on the Reviewee's performance using the Comment 1, 2, and 3 fields.
5. Enter the date and time of the feedback in the Date and Time fields, respectively.
6. Add any additional comments or information in the Note field.
7. Enter your email address in the E-mail field, so that the Reviewee can contact you for further questions or clarification.
8. Enter your phone number in the Phone field, so that the Reviewee can contact you for further questions or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reviewee
* **Reviewee** (`reviewee_label`, text, optional): This field is for recording the name of the individual being evaluated. Enter the Reviewee's name as it appears on their official records or as they prefer to be addressed.

### Reviewer
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer_label`, text, optional): This field is for recording the name of the person providing the feedback. Enter your full name as it appears on official records or as it is commonly used for communication.

### Rating 1
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, number, optional): This field is for rating the performance of the Reviewee on a specific point. Enter a number from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.

### Rating 2
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, number, optional): This field is for rating the performance of the Reviewee on another specific point. Enter a number from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.

### Rating 3
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, number, optional): This field is for rating the performance of the Reviewee on yet another specific point. Enter a number from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.

### Comment 1
* **Comment 1** (`comment_1`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional comments on the performance of the Reviewee. Enter any specific points that you feel are important for improvement.

### Comment 2
* **Comment 2** (`comment_2`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional comments on the performance of the Reviewee. Enter any specific points that you feel are important for improvement.

### Comment 3
* **Comment 3** (`comment_3`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional comments on the performance of the Reviewee. Enter any specific points that you feel are important for improvement.

### Date
* **Date** (`date_label`, date, optional): This field is for recording the date the feedback was provided. Enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Time
* **Time** (`time_label`, time, optional): This field is for recording the time the feedback was provided. Enter the time in the format hh:mm.

### Note
* **Note** (`note_label`, note, optional): This field is for recording any additional comments or information not captured by the other fields. Enter any relevant details that are important for the Reviewee to know.

### E-mail
* **E-mail** (`email_label`, email, optional): This field is for recording your email address. Enter the email address you wish to be contacted at, so that the Reviewee can reach out to you with questions or clarification.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone_label`, text, optional): This field is for recording your phone number. Enter the phone number you can be reached at, so that the Reviewee can contact you with questions or clarification.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective when providing feedback.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Use specific examples when providing comments.
* Keep the feedback constructive and helpful.
* Proofread your feedback before submitting it.
* Use the form for its intended purpose only, and not for other reasons.
