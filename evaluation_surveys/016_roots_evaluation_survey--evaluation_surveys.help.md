# Roots Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect evaluations of "Roots" from customers or stakeholders. It aims to gather feedback on their overall experience, focusing on areas such as root knowledge, root health observations, and any management challenges they faced.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of each field.
2. Complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Fill in the form with your honest and accurate responses.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required: false): A brief introduction to your experience with our product/service. Please provide a concise and clear statement about your encounter.
* **Root Knowledge** (`root_knowledge`, number, required: false): Rate your level of root knowledge on a scale (e.g., 1-5 or 1-10).
* **Root Health Observations** (`root_health_observations`, text, required: false): Share any observations or concerns you have about the health of the "Roots" you encountered.
* **Management Challenges** (`management_challenges`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate any management challenges you faced during your interaction with our product/service. Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or insights that might be helpful for us to know about your experience. 

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

This help guide aims to assist users in navigating the Roots Evaluation Survey form efficiently and effectively, ensuring they provide valuable feedback for the improvement of our product/service.
