# Feedback And Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and reflections from event attendees to help us improve our events and services. Your responses will help us identify areas of strength and weakness, and inform decisions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the questions and select the options that best answer each question.
2. Provide detailed and thoughtful answers for the text fields (questions 2, 3, 4, 7, and 8).
3. Select your response for questions 5 and 6 from the given options.
4. Submit your completed form to let us know your thoughts and opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false):
    * This question helps us understand what type of event you attended (e.g. training, meeting, event).
* **What was the primary event purpose?** (`question1`, text, required: false):
    * Please describe the main objective of the event you attended. This will help us gauge the event's effectiveness in achieving its purpose.
* **What did you hope to achieve at this event?** (`question2`, text, required: false):
    * Share your expectations and goals for attending the event. Were they met?
* **What were some of the key takeaways from this event?** (`question3`, text, required: false):
    * Reflect on the most valuable learnings, insights, or outcomes you gained from the event. This will help us evaluate the event's impact.
* **What do you think we could improve on?** (`question4`, text, required: false):
    * Share your honest feedback on what we can do better. Your suggestions are valuable to us.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with this event?** (`question5`, select_one, required: false):
    * Select your level of satisfaction with the event, from Very Satisfied to Not Satisfied.
* **How likely are you to recommend this event to others?** (`question6`, select_one, required: false):
    * Indicate how likely you are to recommend this event to friends, colleagues, or peers, from Very Likely to Not Likely.
* **What did you enjoy most about this event?** (`question7`, text, required: false):
    * Share what aspects of the event were most enjoyable for you.
* **What did you find least satisfying about this event?** (`question8`, text, required: false):
    * Be honest about what aspects of the event were least satisfying or challenging for you.
