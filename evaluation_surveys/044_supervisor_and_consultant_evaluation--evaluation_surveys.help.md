# Supervisor And Consultant Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervisor And Consultant Evaluation form is used to gather feedback and evaluations from supervisors and consultants on their work relationship. This form helps to identify areas of improvement, recognize strengths, and foster a positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supervisor's name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Provide the consultant's name and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have received feedback from the consultant or not.
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have given feedback to the supervisor or not.
5. Optionally, you can submit the form by clicking the "submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the supervisor's name as it appears on their official documents or correspondence.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter the supervisor's email address, if available.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, optional): Enter the consultant's name as it appears on their official documents or correspondence.
* **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`, email, optional): Enter the consultant's email address, if available.
* **Supervisor Feedback** (`supervisor_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have received feedback from the supervisor, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Consultant Feedback** (`consultant_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have given feedback to the consultant, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Optionally, you can submit the form by clicking the "submit" button, which will record the current date.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the supervisor and consultant contact details.
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' carefully for the feedback fields, as this will help in maintaining accurate records.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the support team for assistance.
