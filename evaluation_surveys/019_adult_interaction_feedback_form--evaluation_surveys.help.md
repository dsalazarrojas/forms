# Adult Interaction Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from adults about their interactions with our service. The feedback will help us understand their experiences, identify areas for improvement, and refine our service to better meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  **Section Details**: This section is a note section where you can provide any additional information about your interaction.
2.  **Participant Name**: Enter your name or leave it blank if you wish to remain anonymous.
3.  **Date of Interaction**: This is the date on which the interaction occurred.
4.  **Facilitator or Representative Name**: Enter the name of the person you interacted with.
5.  **Section Communication**: This section is another note section to provide more context about your interaction.
6.  **Clarity of Information Provided**: Rate how clear the information was provided to you.
7.  **Active Listening Demonstrated**: Rate how well you felt heard and understood during the interaction.
8.  **Respect and Professionalism**: Rate the tone of the interaction.
9.  **Section Engagement**: Another note section to provide more context about your interaction.
10. **Opportunity to Ask Questions**: Rate how well you were able to ask questions during the interaction.
11. **Helpfulness of Responses**: Rate how helpful the responses to your questions were.
12. **Overall Satisfaction with Interaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the interaction.
13. **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any suggestions for how we can improve, enter them here.
14. **Would You Recommend This Service**: Finally, rate whether you would recommend our service based on your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (text, Optional)**: Enter your name or leave blank if you wish to remain anonymous.
* **Date of Interaction (date, Required)**: The date on which the interaction occurred.
* **Facilitator or Representative Name (text, Optional)**: The name of the person you interacted with.
* **Clarity of Information Provided (select_one, Required)**: Rate how clear the information was provided to you. 
  + Very Clear
  + Mostly Clear
  + Somewhat Clear
  + Unclear
* **Active Listening Demonstrated (select_one, Required)**: Rate how well you felt heard and understood during the interaction.
  + Strongly Agree
  + Agree
  + Neutral
  + Disagree
* **Respect and Professionalism (select_one, Required)**: Rate the tone of the interaction.
  + Excellent
  + Good
  + Fair
  + Poor
* **Opportunity to Ask Questions (select_one, Required)**: Rate how well you were able to ask questions during the interaction.
  + Sufficient
  + Somewhat Sufficient
  + Insufficient
* **Helpfulness of Responses (select_one, Required)**: Rate how helpful the responses to your questions were.
  + Very Helpful
  + Helpful
  + Not Helpful
* **Overall Satisfaction with Interaction (select_one, Required)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the interaction.
  + Very Satisfied
  + Satisfied
  + Neutral
  + Dissatisfied
* **Suggestions for Improvement (text, Optional)**: If you have any suggestions for how we can improve, enter them here.
* **Would You Recommend This Service (select_one, Optional)**: Finally, rate whether you would recommend our service based on your experience.
  + Definitely
  + Probably
  + Maybe
  + False
