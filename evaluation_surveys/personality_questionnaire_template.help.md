# personality_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a personality questionnaire designed to assess the respondent's traits, preferences, and behaviors. It consists of a series of questions that aim to understand their approach to new experiences, handling of unexpected challenges, motivations, values, and preferred activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. For question 1, "How would you describe your general approach to new experiences?", select the option that best fits your description.
2. For question 2, "Which of these environments makes you feel most energized?", select all the options that apply.
3. For question 3, "How do you typically handle unexpected challenges?", choose the option that best describes your approach.
4. For question 4, "What motivates you the most in your daily life?", select all the options that apply.
5. For question 5, "What is one quality you value most in others?", type your answer in the text field.
6. For question 6, "How do you prefer to spend your free time?", choose one of the options that best fits your preferences.
7. For question 7, "Is there anything else you would like to share about your personality or how you perceive yourself?", type your answer in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you describe your general approach to new experiences?**
	+ Type: single
	+ This question is designed to understand your overall attitude towards new experiences. Do you tend to be adventurous, cautious, or somewhere in between? Select the option that best describes your general approach.
* **Which of these environments makes you feel most energized? (select all that apply)**
	+ Type: multiple
	+ This question is designed to determine what environments or situations tend to make you feel most alive and motivated. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **How do you typically handle unexpected challenges?**
	+ Type: single
	+ This question is designed to understand how you manage unexpected setbacks or difficulties. Do you tend to act quickly, plan carefully, or take a more cautious approach?
* **What motivates you the most in your daily life? (select all that apply)**
	+ Type: multiple
	+ This question is designed to determine what drives you to take action or pursue activities. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **What is one quality you value most in others?**
	+ Type: text
	+ This question is designed to understand what characteristic you consider most important in others. Be as specific as possible when providing your answer.
* **How do you prefer to spend your free time?**
	+ Type: single
	+ This question is designed to understand how you typically spend your leisure time. Choose one of the options that best fits your preferences.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share about your personality or how you perceive yourself?**
	+ Type: text
	+ This is an opportunity to provide any additional insights or context that you feel is relevant to your personality or how you view yourself.
