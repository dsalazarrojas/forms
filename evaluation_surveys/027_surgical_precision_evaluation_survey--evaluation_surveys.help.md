# Surgical Precision Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the precision of surgical procedures. It gathers information on various aspects of the surgery, including case type, duration, peer and self-satisfaction, and other relevant factors. The form is used to assess the quality of surgical precision in different contexts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Answer each question based on your experience and observation.
3. Be as specific and detailed as possible in your responses.
4. Fill out the form completely, but do not feel obligated to answer questions that do not apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **case_type**: Indicate whether the case was of a simple or complex nature. Choose the option that best describes the case.
* **rating**: Provide your rating of the surgical precision based on your assessment. The options are:
	+ Not rated
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Unsatisfactory
	+ Not assessed
* **comments**: Provide any comments you have on the case.
* **rating_reason**: Explain the reason for your rating.
* **surgical_precisoin**: Enter the numerical value of surgical precision.
* **surgical_precision_out**: Enter the numerical value of surgical precision out.
* **case_type_out**: Indicate the type of case (None, Simple, Complex).
* **case_duration**: Indicate the duration of the case (Less than 30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, More than 1 hour).
* **peer_satisfaction**: Indicate your satisfaction with peer feedback.
* **self_satisfaction**: Indicate your satisfaction with the procedure.
* **peer_comments**: Provide any comments on peer feedback.
* **self_comments**: Provide any comments on your satisfaction.
* **surgical_precision_out_comments**: Enter any comments on surgical precision out.
* **case_duration_comments**: Enter any comments on the duration of the case.
* **case_type_comments**: Provide any comments on the type of case.
* **rating_out_comments**: Enter any comments on your rating.
* **other_comments**: Enter any other comments or feedback.

Please note that some fields are marked as "required" and others as "not required". Make sure to fill out only the fields that are required and leave the rest optional. 

This guide will help you complete the form efficiently and effectively, providing valuable insights into surgical precision.
