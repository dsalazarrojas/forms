# Digital Health Ecosystem Pilot Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from partners who have participated in a pilot evaluation of the Digital Health Ecosystem. This feedback will be used to improve and refine the ecosystem for future use.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has 10 fields that you are required to fill out. Please make sure to answer each question to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What were the benefits of the pilot?** (`pilot_benefits`, text, required/optional): This field is asking you to describe the advantages you experienced during the pilot. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **What were the challenges of the pilot?** (`pilot_challenges`, text, required/optional): This field is asking you to describe any difficulties or obstacles you faced during the pilot. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **What improvements would you suggest for this pilot?** (`pilot_improvements`, text, required/optional): This field is asking you to propose any changes or enhancements you think would make the pilot more effective.
* **What is your partner's name?** (`partner_name`, text, required/optional): This field is asking for your name or title as a partner in the evaluation process.
* **What is your partner's title?** (`partner_title`, text, required/optional): This field is asking for your title or position as a partner in the evaluation process.
* **Who is on your healthcare team?** (`healthcare_team`, text, required/optional): This field is asking about the team members who were involved in the pilot evaluation.
* **What was the start date of the pilot?** (`pilot_start_date`, date, required/optional): This field is asking for the start date of the pilot evaluation period.
* **What was the end date of the pilot?** (`pilot_end_date`, date, required/optional): This field is asking for the end date of the pilot evaluation period.
* **How long was the evaluation period?** (`evaluation_period`, number, required/optional): This field is asking about the duration of the evaluation period.
* **What was the start date of the evaluation period?** (`evaluation_start_date`, date, required/optional): This field is asking for the start date of the evaluation period.

## Tips

Please make sure to answer each question carefully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible. This will help us to better understand your experience with the Digital Health Ecosystem and make improvements for future use.
