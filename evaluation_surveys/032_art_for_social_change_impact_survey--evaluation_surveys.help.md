# Art For Social Change Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art For Social Change Impact Survey is designed to collect data on the social impact of arts-based projects. This survey will help us understand the effectiveness of these projects and gather feedback for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and project name (pages 1-2)
2. Provide information about the target population and outcomes measured (pages 3-4)
3. Report on the participation numbers and methods used (pages 5-6)
4. Describe the evidence collected (page 7)
5. Rate the overall impact of your project (page 8)
6. Share lessons learned from your experience (page 9)
7. Provide your email address to receive the evaluation summary (page 10)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (Respondent name, text, optional): Enter your name as the respondent.
* **Project name** (Project name, text, required): Name of the arts-based project you are evaluating.
* **Target population** (Target population, text, required): The communities or groups you are serving with your project.
* **Outcomes measured** (Outcomes measured, text, required): List the social impact outcomes that were assessed as a result of your project.
* **Participation numbers** (Participation numbers, number, optional): Approximate number of participants in your project.
* **Primary methods used** (Primary methods used, text, optional): List the main methods used to deliver your project (e.g., workshops, exhibitions, performance, outreach).
* **Evidence collected** (Evidence collected, text, optional): Describe the surveys, interviews, and media coverage related to your project.
* **Overall impact rating** (Overall impact rating, select_one, required): Rate the perceived impact of your project on a scale from Low to High.
* **Lessons learned** (Lessons learned, text, optional): Share key takeaways for future projects that you learned from your experience.
* **Contact for results** (Contact for results, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive the evaluation summary.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about a field, please contact us for clarification.
* This form is intended to be completed by people who have participated in arts-based projects with social impact. If you have completed this form before, please do not duplicate your response.
