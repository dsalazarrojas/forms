# Animal Network Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Network Evaluation Survey is designed to gather feedback from organizations within the Animal Network on their overall experience, satisfaction, and challenges faced. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and strengthen the network.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Organization Name**, which should be the full name of your organization.
2. Select your organization type from the list of provided options (e.g., Animal Welfare Nonprofit, Veterinary Clinic, Animal Sanctuary).
3. Enter the **Contact Person's Name** and their **Contact Email** address so we can reach out to you for follow-up questions or clarifications.
4. Indicate how long your organization has been part of the network (Less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1 to 3 years, 3 to 5 years, or More than 5 years).
5. Rate your **Overall Satisfaction** with the Animal Network (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
6. Evaluate the **Quality of Collaboration** with network members (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Applicable).
7. Rate the **Effectiveness of Network Communication** (Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, or Very Ineffective).
8. Indicate how often you **Share Resources** with other network members (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
9. Rate the **Level of Support** received from the network (Exceeded Expectations, Met Expectations, Below Expectations, or No Support Received).
10. Select all the **Training Needs** your organization would benefit from (e.g., Best Practices Documentation, Webinars and Workshops, One-on-One Consulting, etc.).
11. List any **Challenges Faced** as a network member (e.g., Communication Barriers, Limited Resources, etc.).
12. Provide any **Improvement Suggestions** for the network (optional).
13. Rate how likely you are to **Recommend the Network** to other organizations (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Very Unlikely).
14. Indicate whether you plan to **Continue Network Membership** (Yes Definitely, Probably Yes, Undecided, Probably No, or False).
15. Add any additional **Comments** or feedback you'd like to share (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Text): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (Select): Select the category that best describes your organization.
* **Contact Person Name** (Text): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Membership Duration** (Select): Indicate how long your organization has been part of the network.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select): Rate your overall satisfaction with the Animal Network.
* **Collaboration Quality** (Select): Evaluate the quality of collaboration with network members.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (Select): Rate the effectiveness of network communication.
* **Resource Sharing** (Select): Indicate how often you share resources with other network members.
* **Support Received** (Select): Rate the level of support received from the network.
* **Training Needs** (Select Multiple): Select all the training needs your organization would benefit from.
* **Challenges Faced** (Select Multiple): List any challenges faced as a network member.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (Text): Provide any improvement suggestions for the network.
* **Recommend Network** (Select): Rate how likely you are to recommend the Animal Network to other organizations.
* **Future Participation** (Select): Indicate whether you plan to continue your network membership.
* **Additional Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

Note: All fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) to denote that they require attention.
