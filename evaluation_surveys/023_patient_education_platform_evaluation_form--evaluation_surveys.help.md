# Patient Education Platform Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from patients about their experience with our patient education platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information to provide accurate feedback.
2. For each section, answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. If you have difficulty understanding a question or need clarification, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (`patient_education_platform_evaluation_form`, note, not required)
*This section is an introductory note about the form's purpose and instructions.*

### 2. **What is your current patient education platform?** (`patient_platform`, select_one, not required)
Please select the type of patient education platform you are currently using or familiar with.

### 3. **Rate the platform** (`platform_rating`, number, not required)
Rate the platform on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 4. **How often do you use the platform?** (`platform_usefulness`, select_multiple, not required)
Select how often you use the platform from 1 (less than once a week) to 5 (daily).

### 5. **Has the platform improved your education?** (`platform_improve_education`, select_one, not required)
Please select if the platform has improved your education.

### 6. **Has the platform improved your treatment?** (`platform_improve_treatment`, select_one, not required)
Please select if the platform has improved your treatment.

### 7. **How satisfied are you with the platform?** (`platform_satisfaction`, number, not required)
Rate your satisfaction with the platform on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 8. **What type of content do you typically seek on the platform?** (`platform_educational_content`, select_multiple, not required)
Select the types of content you look for on the platform.

### 9. **What do you like most about the platform?** (`platform_favourite_feature`, text, not required)
 Describe what you like most about the platform.

### 10. **Would you recommend this platform to a colleague?** (`patient_recommendation`, select_one, not required)
Please select if you would recommend this platform to a colleague.

### 11. **What could be improved in the platform?** (`platform_improvement`, text, not required)
Provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
