# Salon Evaluation Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback on your experience with our salon services and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For the text-based questions, provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How satisfied were you with your recent visit to our salon?
* **Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your recent visit to our salon. Choose the option that best describes your feelings.
 
### How often do you typically visit our salon?
* **Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Every few months, Rarely**: This question helps us understand how often you visit our salon. Select the option that best answers your regular attendance.
 
### Which of the following best describes your experience at our salon? (Select all that apply)
* **Friendly staff, Skilled stylists, Clean facilities, Relaxing atmosphere, Efficient service, Good value**: This question is asking for your overall opinion on your experience with our salon. Choose all the options that apply to your visit.
 
### What would you improve or change about our salon services or facilities?
* **Text-based field for suggestions or feedback**: This question gives you the opportunity to provide any additional comments or suggestions you think would improve our services or facilities.
 
### Do you have any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share?
* **Text-based field for additional feedback**: This is another chance to provide any further thoughts or comments about your experience with our salon.
 
## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
- Your input is valuable and helps us improve our services.
