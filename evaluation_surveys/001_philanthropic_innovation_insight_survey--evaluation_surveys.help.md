# Philanthropic Innovation Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about a specific philanthropic innovation from its contact person.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **Program Title**: Enter the title of the program or initiative you are providing data about.
2.  Enter the **Organization Name**: Type in the name of the organization that is implementing the program or initiative.
3.  Answer **Yes** or **No** for **Program Initiative**: Select **Yes** if the program or initiative is still active, and **No** if it is not.
4.  For **Select Program or Initiative**, choose the specific option that best matches your program or initiative from the dropdown list.
5.  Provide a brief **Program Description**: Describe your program or initiative in a few sentences.
6.  Enter the **Contact Name**: Type in the name of the contact person who is submitting the data.
7.  Enter the **Contact E-mail**: Type in the email address of the contact person.
8.  Enter the **Contact Phone**: Type in the phone number of the contact person.
9.  Provide any additional **Comments**: If you have any further comments or information to share.
10. Answer the **Evaluation Scales** questions by rating your program or initiative on a scale of 1-30. Each scale corresponds to a specific evaluation criteria.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title** (`Program Title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the program or initiative.
* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, text, required): Type in the name of the organization that is implementing the program or initiative.
* **Program Initiative** (`program_initiative`, select_multiple, not required): Answer **Yes** if the program or initiative is still active, and **No** if it is not.
* **Select Program or Initiative** (`program_initiative_options`, select_one, required): Choose the specific option that best matches your program or initiative from the dropdown list.
* **Program Description** (`Program Description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of your program or initiative.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, required): Type in the name of the contact person who is submitting the data.
* **Contact E-mail** (`Contact E-mail`, email, required): Type in the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Type in the phone number of the contact person.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or information you have.
* **Evaluation Scale 1.5** (`Evaluation Scale 1.5`, number, not required): Answer this question by rating your program or initiative on a scale of 1-30.
* **Evaluation Scale 5** (`Evaluation Scale 5`, number, not required): Answer this question by rating your program or initiative on a scale of 1-30.
* **Evaluation Scale 10** (`Evaluation Scale 10`, number, not required): Answer this question by rating your program or initiative on a scale of 1-30.
* **Evaluation Scale 15** (`Evaluation Scale 15`, number, not required): Answer this question by rating your program or initiative on a scale of 1-30.
* **Evaluation Scale 20** (`Evaluation Scale 20`, number, not required): Answer this question by rating your program or initiative on a scale of 1-30.
* **Evaluation Scale 25** (`Evaluation Scale 25`, number, not required): Answer this question by rating your program or initiative on a scale of 1-30.
* **Evaluation Scale 30** (`Evaluation Scale 30`, number, not required): Answer this question by rating your program or initiative on a scale of 1-30.

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill in as much information as possible.
