# Risk Perception Benchmark Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your risk perception and risk level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a description of the evaluation context in the "Evaluation Context" field.
2. Select the risk factors that you perceive as relevant to your evaluation context from the "Risk Factors" field.
3. Choose the risk factor(s) that you selected in the previous step as the most relevant from the "Selected Risk Factors" field.
4. Enter a numerical value for the risk level you perceive in the "Risk Level" field.
5. Provide a description of the risk level in the "Risk Level Description" field (optional).
6. Choose the category of the risk level from the options provided in the "Risk Level Category" field.
7. If applicable, provide a description of the risk level category in the "Risk Level Category 2 Description" field.
8. If applicable, select the category of the risk level category from the options provided in the "Risk Level Category 2" field.
9. Enter the risk level category 2 scale value (if applicable) in the "Risk Level Category 2 Scale" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Context**: This field allows you to provide a description of the evaluation context. Please keep it concise and clear to help us understand your evaluation context.
* **Risk Factors**: Select all the risk factors that you think are relevant to your evaluation context. You can select multiple risk factors.
*   **Selected Risk Factors**: Choose the risk factor that you selected in the previous step as the most relevant. This field will be pre-populated with the risk factors you selected in the "Risk Factors" field.
*   **Risk Level**: Enter a numerical value for the risk level you perceive. The risk level should be a number between 0 and 10, where 0 represents no risk and 10 represents extreme risk.
*   **Risk Level Description**: Provide a description of the risk level you entered in the "Risk Level" field. This is optional, but it helps us understand your risk perception better.
*   **Risk Level Category**: Choose the category of the risk level you perceive. Please select one of the provided categories to help us categorize your risk level.
*   **Risk Level Category 2**: This field is not explicitly used in this help guide. It seems to be a redundant field and can be removed.
*   **Risk Level Category 2 Description**, **Risk Level Category 2 Scale**, and **Risk Level Category 2 Category**: These fields are also not explicitly used in this help guide and can be removed.
*   **Risk Level Scale**: This field is not explicitly used in this help guide and can be removed.
