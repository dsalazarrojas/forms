# ESG Reporting Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ESG Reporting Awareness Survey is designed to gather information on users' experiences and perceptions related to ESG reporting. Your input will help us understand where we need to improve our ESG reporting practices and provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the category that best describes your ESG reporting experience.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience with ESG reporting.
3. Indicate how often you engage in ESG reporting.
4. Choose the method you use for ESG reporting.
5. Select the location where you perform ESG reporting.
6. Enter the date of your last ESG report.
7. Specify the time of your last ESG report.
8. Name the person responsible for your ESG report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, required/optional): Select the category that best describes your ESG reporting experience. Choose from the options: Category 1, Category 2, Category 3.
* **Description** (`description`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your experience with ESG reporting. This will help us understand your perspective.
* **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, required/optional): Indicate how often you engage in ESG reporting. Select from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Reporting Method** (`reporting_method`, required/optional): Choose the method you use for ESG reporting. Select from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Reporting Location** (`reporting_location`, required/optional): Select the location where you perform ESG reporting. Choose from the options: Location 1, Location 2, Location 3.
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your last ESG report.
* **Reporting Time** (`reporting_time`, required/optional): Specify the time of your last ESG report.
* **Reporting Responsible** (`reporting_responsible`, required/optional): Name the person responsible for your ESG report.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask for clarification before proceeding.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the survey, and don't hesitate to ask for help if needed.
