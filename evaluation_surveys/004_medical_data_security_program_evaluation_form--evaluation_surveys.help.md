# Medical Data Security Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the effectiveness and security measures of your medical data security program. This will help us assess the current state of your program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and answer the questions according to your organization's policies and procedures.
2. Provide detailed answers to each field, as they will help us understand your program's strengths and weaknesses.
3. Ensure that you answer all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Evaluation Form
• **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name and title or position within the organization.
• **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
### Page 2 - Program Scope
• **Program Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Describe your organization's data security policies and procedures.
• **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required): Specify your organization's goals for data security.
### Page 3 - Security Measures
• **Encryption** (`encryption`, `text`, required): Describe the encryption measures in place to protect sensitive data.
• **Access Controls** (`access_controls`, `text`, required): Explain your organization's access controls, such as user authentication or authorization.
• **Data Backup** (`data_backup`, `text`, required): Explain your organization's data backup procedures.
### Page 4 - Data Challenges
• **Technical Limitations** (`technical_limitations`, `text`, required): Identify any technical limitations affecting data security.
• **Regulatory Hurdles** (`regulatory_hurdles`, `text`, required): Describe any regulatory challenges you're facing in maintaining data security.
### Page 5 - Program Evaluation
• **Overall Program Effectiveness** (`overall_program_effectiveness`, `text`, required): Evaluate the overall effectiveness of your data security program.
• **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, required): Identify areas for improvement in your data security program.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Please provide specific examples or details when possible.
- If you're unsure about any question, please reach out to us for clarification.
