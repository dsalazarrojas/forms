# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gauge the awareness, attitudes, and opinions of youth towards climate change policy. The information collected will help inform policy makers and stakeholders to better understand the perspectives of young people on climate change issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by reading the introduction, which will provide some background information about the survey.
2. In Section One, you will be asked about your awareness of climate change.
3. Section Two is about your attitudes towards climate change.
4. In Section Three, you will be asked to provide policy ideas related to climate change.
5. Section Four is about demographic information, including your age, gender, school level, and region.
6. Section Five is for you to provide any other climate issues that you are concerned about.
7. Finally, in Section Six, you can provide any comments or feedback about the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section One - Awareness**: 
    * **Awareness of Climate Change** (`awareness_of_climate_change`, select_one, required: false): 
        * This question asks about your level of awareness of climate change. You can choose between 'Yes' and 'No' to indicate your awareness.
* **Section Two - Attitudes**: 
    * **Importance of Climate Change** (`climate_change_importance`, select_multiple, required: false): 
        * This question asks about the importance you give to climate change. You can select between 'Very Important' and 'Not Important' to indicate your level of importance.
* **Section Three - Ideas**:
    * **Policy Ideas** (`policy_ideas`, text, required: false): 
        * This question is for you to provide any policy ideas related to climate change.
* **Section Four - Demographic Information**: 
    * **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): 
        * Please enter your age.
    * **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): 
        * This question asks about your gender. You can choose between 'Yes' and 'No' to indicate your gender.
    * **School Level** (`school_level`, select_one, required: false): 
        * This question asks about your school level. You can choose between 'High' and 'Low' to indicate your school level.
    * **Region** (`region`, select_one, required: false): 
        * This question asks about your region. You can choose between 'Yes' and 'No' to indicate your region.
* **Section Five - Additional Information**: 
    * **Other climate issues you are concerned about** (`other_climate_issues`, text, required: false): 
        * This question is for you to provide any other climate issues that you are concerned about.
* **Section Six - Feedback**: 
    * **Any comments about the survey** (`comments`, text, required: false): 
        * This question is for you to provide any comments or feedback about the survey.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
* The information you provide will be used to help inform policy makers and stakeholders about the perspectives of young people on climate change issues.
