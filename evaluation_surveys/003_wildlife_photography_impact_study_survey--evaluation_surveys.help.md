# Wildlife Photography Impact Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the impact of wildlife photography on the environment, wildlife, and people.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully to ensure you understand what is expected of you.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wildlife Photography Impact Study Survey** (`wildlife_photography_impact_study_survey`, text, required):
This is the title of the survey. Please enter a brief description of the survey in this field.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional):
Please select one of the following categories that best describes your experience with wildlife photography:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Description** (`evaluation_survey_description`, text, optional):
Please provide a brief description of your experience with wildlife photography, including any notable events or observations that you would like to share.
* **Date of Survey** (`date_of_survey`, date, optional):
Please enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Time of Survey** (`time_of_survey`, time, optional):
Please enter the time you completed this survey.
* **Evaluaton Survey** (`evaluation_survey`, select_one, optional):
Please select one of the following options to indicate how you feel about wildlife photography:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required questions.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for help.
* Please provide accurate and honest responses.
* If you need to submit the form multiple times, please make sure to provide unique responses each time.
