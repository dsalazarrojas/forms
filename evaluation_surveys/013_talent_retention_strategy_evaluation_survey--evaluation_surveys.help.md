# Talent Retention Strategy Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the current state of talent retention within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each page carefully before answering the questions.
2. Answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
3. Save your progress and come back later if you need to.
4. Make sure to submit your completed form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (Page 1): Please provide a brief introduction about your current job role and position in the organization.
* **retention_satisfaction** (Page 2): Please rate your level of satisfaction with your current job role.
* **improvement_areas** (Page 3): Please list any areas where you think can be improved in the organization to increase talent retention.
* **improvement_suggestions** (Page 4): Please provide any suggestions you have for improving talent retention.
* **feedback** (Page 5): Please provide any feedback you have for the organization.
* **retention_initiatives** (Page 6): Please select all the initiatives that the organization should take to increase talent retention.
* **retention_initiatives_other** (Page 7): Please select "Yes" if you selected "Other" in the previous question, and enter your answer in the text field.
* **retention_initiatives_other_options** (Page 8): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please provide a brief description of what you think should be done.
* **communication** (Page 9): Please rate your current level of satisfaction with the communication with your manager(s).
* **communication_frequency** (Page 10): Please rate the frequency of communication with your manager(s).
* **communication_channels** (Page 11): Please select the channel(s) through which you prefer to communicate with your manager(s).
* **manager_leadership** (Page 12): Please rate your manager's leadership style.
* **recognition** (Page 13): Please rate the importance of recognition in your job satisfaction.
* **career_development** (Page 14): Please rate the importance of career development opportunities in your job satisfaction.
* **job_satisfaction** (Page 15): Please rate your current level of job satisfaction.
* **job_satisfaction_frequency** (Page 16): Please rate the frequency of your job satisfaction.
* **job_satisfaction_scale** (Page 17): Please select the scale you are using to measure your job satisfaction.
* **benefits** (Page 18): Please rate your current level of satisfaction with the benefits offered by the organization.
* **benefits_frequency** (Page 19): Please rate the frequency of your benefit satisfaction.
* **benefits_scale** (Page 20): Please select the scale you are using to measure your benefit satisfaction.
* **work_life_balance** (Page 21): Please rate the importance of work-life balance in your job satisfaction.
* **work_life_balance_frequency** (Page 22): Please rate the frequency of your work-life balance satisfaction.
* **work_life_balance_scale** (Page 23): Please select the scale you are using to measure your work-life balance satisfaction.
* **retention** (Page 24): Please rate the importance of retention in your job satisfaction.
* **open_comment** (Page 25): Please provide any other comments or suggestions you have.
