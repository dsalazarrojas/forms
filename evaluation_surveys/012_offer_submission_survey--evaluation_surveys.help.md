# Offer Submission Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluators provide structured feedback on submitted proposals or offers. The goal is to collect data on the quality and suitability of the proposals or offers, which will be used to guide future submissions and improve the proposal or offer process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the proposal or offer that you are evaluating and ensure that you have all necessary information and resources available.
2. Complete each field according to your evaluation, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Be honest and objective in your feedback, using the provided fields to provide constructive criticism where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposal Received** (`proposal_received`, text, optional): Enter the date when the proposal or offer was received.
* **Offer Received** (`offer_received`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score indicating the quality of the offer. Please use a scale from 0 to 100.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the evaluation was completed.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time spent evaluating the proposal or offer.
* **Score Weightage** (`score_weightage`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score indicating the weightage given to each evaluation criterion (e.g., 10 for "timeliness" and 5 for "content").
* **Score Quality** (`score_quality`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score indicating the overall quality of the proposal or offer. Please use a scale from 0 to 100.
* **Prophets Evaluated** (`prophets_evaluated`, text, optional): Enter any specific prophets or prophecies that were evaluated as part of this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, text, optional): Enter the specific criteria used to evaluate the proposal or offer.
* **Evaluation Summary** (`evaluation_summary`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of your evaluation, including any areas of improvement or strengths of the proposal or offer.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback that you have for the proposer or offeror.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, optional): Enter the overall score for the proposal or offer. Please use a scale from 0 to 100.
* **Score Breakdown** (`score_breakdown`, text, optional): Enter a breakdown of the scores for each evaluation criterion.
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the proposal or offer being evaluated.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the proposal or offer was submitted.

Note: If a field is marked as optional, it is not required to be filled out. However, providing as much information as possible will help to ensure a thorough and useful evaluation.
