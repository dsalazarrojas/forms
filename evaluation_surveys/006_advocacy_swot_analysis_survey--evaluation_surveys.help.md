# Advocacy SWOT Analysis Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey aims to help you analyze the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT) related to your advocacy work. A SWOT analysis is a useful tool for identifying areas of improvement, recognizing opportunities for growth, and understanding potential challenges that may impact your efforts. By completing this survey, you will be able to:

* Identify your organization's internal and external strengths and weaknesses
* Recognize opportunities and threats that may affect your advocacy work
* Develop strategic action items to address areas of improvement

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your organization's name and your role in the organization.
2. Answer the questions related to your organization's internal and external strengths and weaknesses, as well as opportunities and threats.
3. Rank your top three strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats in order of importance/concern.
4. Provide strategic action items to address areas of improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): Please enter your organization's name.
* **Your Role in the Organization** (`respondent_role`, required): Describe your role or position in the organization.
* **Advocacy Campaign Focus** (`advocacy_campaign_focus`, required): Briefly describe the primary focus of your advocacy campaign.
* **Internal Strengths** (`internal_strengths`, required): List the internal strengths of your advocacy organization.
* **Resource Strengths** (`resource_strengths`, required): Describe the resources that strengthen your advocacy efforts.
* **Stakeholder Support Strengths** (`stakeholder_support_strengths`, required): Describe the level of support you have from stakeholders.
* **Internal Weaknesses** (`internal_weaknesses`, required): List the internal weaknesses of your advocacy organization.
* **Resource Weaknesses** (`resource_weaknesses`, required): Describe the resource limitations that affect your advocacy work.
* **Capacity Weaknesses** (`capacity_weaknesses`, required): Describe the capacity limitations that affect your advocacy work.
* **External Opportunities** (`external_opportunities`, required): Describe the external opportunities that may impact your advocacy work.
* **Policy Opportunities** (`policy_opportunities`, required): Describe the policy changes or opportunities that may advance your cause.
* **Partnership Opportunities** (`partnership_opportunities`, required): Describe the partnerships that could strengthen your advocacy work.
* **External Threats** (`external_threats`, required): Describe the external threats that may impact your advocacy work.
* **Political Threats** (`political_threats`, required): Describe the political challenges or opposition that may exist.
* **Funding Threats** (`funding_threats`, required): Describe the funding risks or challenges that you face.
* **Top Three Strengths** (`top_three_strengths`, required): Rank your top three strengths in order of importance.
* **Top Three Weaknesses** (`top_three_weaknesses`, required): Rank your top three weaknesses in order of concern.
* **Top Three Opportunities** (`top_three_opportunities`, required): Rank your top three opportunities in order of potential.
* **Top Three Threats** (`top_three_threats`, required): Rank your top three threats in order of urgency.
* **Strategic Action Items** (`strategic_action_items`, required): Describe the strategic actions that should be taken based on this SWOT analysis.

## Tips

* Please answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
* If you are unsure about the format or requirements for a particular question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your responses will help us better understand your organization's SWOT analysis.
