# course_evaluation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The course evaluation survey template is designed to collect feedback from course participants to improve future course development and content quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your level of satisfaction with the course overall.
2. Choose the aspects of the course that you found most valuable.
3. Rate your experience with the course organization.
4. Indicate your likelihood of recommending the course to others.
5. Provide one suggestion for improvement.
6. Optionally, include any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied were you with the course overall?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Please select one of the given options to express your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **Which aspects of the course did you find most valuable?** (`Lectures`, `Practical exercises`, `Discussions`, `Instructor interaction`, `Course materials`, `Guest speakers`): Choose as many options as apply to the aspects of the course that resonated with you the most.
* **How well was the course organized?** (`Very well`, `Well`, `Moderately`, `Not very well`, `Poorly`): Select one option to rate your experience with the course organization.
* **How likely are you to recommend this course to others?** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): Choose one option to express your likelihood of recommending the course to friends, family, or colleagues.
* **What is one thing that could improve this course?** (text field): Provide one concrete suggestion on how to improve the course.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (text field): Feel free to share any additional thoughts or insights about your course experience.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, try to focus on concrete aspects of the course rather than general comments.
* You can include any comments or feedback you have about the course, no matter how minor they may seem.
