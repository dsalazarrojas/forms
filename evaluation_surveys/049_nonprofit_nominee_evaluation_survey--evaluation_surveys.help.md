# Nonprofit Nominee Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of a nonprofit nominee. The evaluation is focused on their leadership, service, and impact characteristics. Fill out this form to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the nominee's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2. Provide a score for the nominee's evaluation in the `evaluation_score` field.
3. Enter the nominee's name and organization in the `nominee_name` and `nominee_organization` fields.
4. Answer the questions regarding the nominee's leadership, service, and impact characteristics.
5. Provide a brief evaluation of the nominee's performance in the `evaluation_comments` field.
6. If you are a member of the selection committee, select "True" in the `committee_member` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, number, optional): Evaluate the nominee's performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Leadership Scale** (`leadership_scale`, number, optional): Evaluate the nominee's leadership skills on a scale of 1-10.
* **Service Scale** (`service_scale`, number, optional): Evaluate the nominee's service qualities on a scale of 1-10.
* **Impact Scale** (`impact_scale`, number, optional): Evaluate the nominee's impact on the community on a scale of 1-10.
* **Leadership Characteristics** (`leadership_characteristics`, select_one, optional): Select all the characteristics that describe the nominee's leadership skills.
	+ Nominee demonstrates strong leadership skills
	+ Nominee is highly organized
	+ Nominee is effective at communicating the vision of the nonprofit
	+ Nominee has a strong work ethic
* **Service Characteristics** (`service_characteristics`, select_one, optional): Select all the characteristics that describe the nominee's service qualities.
	+ Nominee provides excellent service to the community
	+ Nominee is committed to social justice
	+ Nominee has a strong understanding of community needs
	+ Nominee is passionate about their work
* **Impact Characteristics** (`impact_characteristics`, select_one, optional): Select all the characteristics that describe the nominee's impact on the community.
	+ Nominee has a strong understanding of the community they serve
	+ Nominee is effective at managing their time
	+ Nominee is highly organized
	+ Nominee is a strong communicator
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, optional): Provide a brief evaluation of the nominee's performance.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, optional): Enter the nominee's name.
* **Nominee Organization** (`nominee_organization`, text, optional): Enter the nominee's organization.
* **Committee Member** (`committee_member`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you are a member of the selection committee.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, optional): Enter the nominee's email address.
* **Committee Name** (`committee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the selection committee.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, optional): Enter your evaluation score.
* **Committee Name** (`committee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the selection committee.

Please note that all fields marked as "optional" are not required to be filled out. You can provide as much information as you deem necessary to help evaluate the nominee's performance.
