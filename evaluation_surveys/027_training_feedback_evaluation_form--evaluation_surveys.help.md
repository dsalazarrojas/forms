# Training Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from trainees after a training session. It helps us understand the effectiveness of the training, the quality of the content, and the overall experience of the trainee. Please use this form to provide valuable insights that will aid in improving our training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing feedback on the content of the training, including any positive or negative comments you may have.
2. Share your feedback on the trainer's performance, focusing on their ability to deliver the content effectively.
3. Offer suggestions for improvements on the organization and structure of the training materials.
4. Rate the effectiveness of the training, choosing one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback on the materials used during the training.
6. Finally, rate the overall effectiveness of the training and provide your email address and date and time of evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Feedback Evaluation** (`training_feedback_evaluation_form`, text, required): This is the main section where you can provide your overall feedback on the training.
* **Feedback on Content** (`feedback_on_content`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the content of the training, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Feedback on Trainer** (`feedback_on_trainer`, text, optional): Rate the trainer's performance, focusing on their ability to deliver the content effectively.
* **Feedback on Organization** (`feedback_on_organization`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for improvements on the organization and structure of the training materials.
* **Feedback on Communication** (`feedback_on_communication`, text, optional): Share your comments on the communication style and approach of the trainer.
* **Training Effectiveness** (`training_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the training, choosing one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback on Materials** (`feedback_on_materials`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback on the materials used during the training.
* **Overall Feedback** (`overall_feedback`, text, required): Rate the overall effectiveness of the training, providing a brief summary of your feedback.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date Evaluated** (`date_evaluated`, date, required): Select the date you completed the evaluation.
* **Time Evaluated** (`time_evaluated`, time, required): Select the time you completed the evaluation.
