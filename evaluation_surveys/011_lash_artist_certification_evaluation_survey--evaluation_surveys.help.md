# Lash Artist Certification Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Lash Artist Certification Evaluation Survey is designed to assess the quality of a course or training program. It is intended for learners who have completed the course to provide feedback on their experience, rating the course and instructor, and making recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each section of the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Rate the course and instructor using the provided rating scales.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the relevant fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Quality** (`course_quality`, number, optional): Rate the overall quality of the course, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Instructor Performance** (`instructor_performance`, number, optional): Rate the performance of the instructor, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Learner Confidence** (`learner_confidence`, number, optional): Rate how confident you feel about your abilities after completing the course, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Lash Technique** (`lash_technique`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the lash technique taught in the course.
* **Course Satisfaction** (`course_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the course.
* **Course Recommendation** (`course_recommendation`, select_one, true): Select one of the provided options to indicate whether or not you would recommend this course to others.
* **Trainer Feedback** (`trainer_feedback`, select_multiple, true): Select all the options that apply to provide feedback about the trainer.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the evaluation process.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the course or instructor.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Time** (`time`, time, false): Enter the date and time when you completed the survey.
* **Date** (`date`, date, false): Enter the date when you completed the survey.
* **Note** (`note`, note, false): Enter any additional notes about your experience with the course or instructor.
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, select_one, true): Rate the course overall, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, select_one, true): Rate the instructor, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Additional Instructor Rating** (`additional_instructor_rating`, select_multiple, true): Select all the options that apply to rate the instructor.
* **Additional Instructor Notes** (`additional_instructor_notes`, text, false): Provide any additional notes or feedback about the instructor.
* **Additional Course Notes** (`additional_course_notes`, text, false): Provide any additional notes or feedback about the course.
* **Evaluation Survey Completion** (`evaluation_survey_completion`, note, false): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, false): Enter the date when you completed the survey.
* **Survey Time** (`survey_time`, time, false): Enter the date and time when you completed the survey.
* **Survey Location** (`survey_location`, text, false): Enter the location where you completed the survey.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the survey process.
