# dental_clinic_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental clinic evaluation survey form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences and expectations from their visit to a dental clinic. This information will help the clinic to improve their services and better serve their patients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field, fill out the required information, and submit the form. Here are some general steps to follow:
1. Click on the "Services Provided" field and select all the services that were provided during your visit.
2. For the "Overall Experience" field, use the numeric scale to rate your overall experience, from 1 (Very Poor) to 10 (Very Good).
3. In the "Clinic Location" field, enter the name or address of the clinic you visited.
4. For "Staff Friendly", "Staff Professionalism", "Clinic Cleanliness", and "Communication" fields, select the level of service you experienced during your visit.
5. In the "Wait Time" field, enter the length of time you waited from the time you arrived at the clinic until you were seen by a staff member.
6. Enter the date of your visit in the "Date of Service" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Services Provided** (Select Multiple, id: 1): 
  Select all the services that were provided during your visit, such as dental cleaning, fillings, dental implants, root canals, crown, and denture.
* **Overall Experience** (Number, id: 2): 
  Rate your overall experience from 1 (Very Poor) to 10 (Very Good).
* **Clinic Location** (Text, id: 3): 
  Enter the name or address of the clinic you visited.
* **Staff Friendly** (Select One, id: 4): 
  Select the level of friendliness of the staff during your visit, from Very Good to Poor.
* **Staff Professionalism** (Select One, id: 5): 
  Select the level of professionalism of the staff during your visit, from Very Good to Poor.
* **Clinic Cleanliness** (Select One, id: 6): 
  Select the level of cleanliness of the clinic during your visit, from Very Good to Poor.
* **Communication** (Select One, id: 7): 
  Select the level of communication from the staff during your visit, from Very Good to Poor.
* **Wait Time** (Time, id: 8): 
  Enter the length of time you waited from the time you arrived at the clinic until you were seen by a staff member.
* **Date of Service** (Date, id: 9): 
  Enter the date of your visit.
* **Comments** (Note, id: 10): 
  Type any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
