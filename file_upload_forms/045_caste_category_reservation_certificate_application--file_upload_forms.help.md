# Caste Category Reservation Certificate Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Caste Category Reservation Certificate Application form is used to collect information for caste category reservation benefits. The form is divided into several sections, including application information, applicant information, address information, caste category selection, and supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the required documents and information ready.
2.  Enter your application date.
3.  Enter your reference or application number (if assigned).
4.  Fill out the applicant information section with your full name, date of birth, gender, national ID number, contact phone number, and email address.
5.  Enter your permanent address, city, state or union territory, and postal code.
6.  Select your caste category.
7.  Enter the full name of your caste.
8.  Upload the required supporting documentation, including a birth certificate, identity proof, address proof, and an affidavit or declaration form (if required).
9.  Certify that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Application Date (id: 2)**: Enter the date you are submitting your application in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Reference or Application Number (id: 3)**: If you have been assigned a reference or application number, enter it here.
*   **Full Name (id: 5)**: Enter your full name as per your official documents.
*   **Date of Birth (id: 6)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Gender (id: 7)**: Select your gender from the options provided.
*   **National ID Number (id: 8)**: Enter your Aadhar, Voter ID, or Passport number.
*   **Phone Number (id: 9)**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Email Address (id: 10)**: Enter your email address (optional).
*   **Permanent Address (id: 12)**: Enter your permanent address.
*   **City or Town (id: 13)**: Enter the city or town of your permanent address.
*   **State or Union Territory (id: 14)**: Enter the state or union territory of your permanent address.
*   **Postal Code (id: 15)**: Enter the postal code of your permanent address.
*   **Caste Category (id: 17)**: Select your caste category from the options provided.
*   **Caste Name (id: 18)**: Enter the full name of your caste.
*   **Religious Denomination (id: 19)**: Enter your religious denomination (optional).
*   **Upload Birth Certificate (id: 21)**: Upload your birth certificate as proof of date and place of birth.
*   **Upload Identity Proof (id: 22)**: Upload your identity proof (Aadhar, Voter ID, Passport, or similar).
*   **Upload Address Proof (id: 23)**: Upload your address proof (utility bill, rental agreement, or similar).
*   **Affidavit or Declaration Form (id: 24)**: Upload a signed affidavit or declaration form from an authorized official (if required).
*   **I certify that the information provided is accurate (id: 25)**: Confirm that the information you have provided is accurate.

## Tips
*   Ensure you have all the required documents and information before filling out the form.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Upload supporting documentation in the required format (e.g., PDF, JPEG, etc.).
