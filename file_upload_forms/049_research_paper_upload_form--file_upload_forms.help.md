# Research Paper Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Research Paper Upload Form" is designed to collect research paper submissions from users. This form is meant for users to upload their manuscripts, providing necessary metadata and file information for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for your research paper in the "Research Paper Upload Form" field.
2. Select the appropriate category for your submission (File Upload Forms or Other Forms).
3. Provide a brief description of your research paper in the "Description" field.
4. Select and upload your manuscript files in the "Manuscript Files" field.
5. Enter the size of your file(s) in the "File Size" field.
6. Select the file type of your manuscript in the "File Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Paper Upload Form**: Enter the title of your research paper in this field. This title will be used as a reference for your submission. (Text, Required)
* **File Upload Forms/Other Forms**: Select the correct category for your research paper submission. (Select, Optional)
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your research paper, including the main topic, methodology, and findings. This field is used to help reviewers understand the content of your submission. (Text, Required)
* **Manuscript Files**: Select and upload your research paper manuscript file(s). This field is used to collect the actual file(s) containing your research paper content. (Text, Optional)
* **File Size**: Enter the size of your file(s) in kilobytes (KB) or megabytes (MB). This field helps reviewers understand the file size and storage requirements. (Text, Required)
* **File Type**: Select the correct file type for your manuscript (e.g., docx, pdf, jpg, etc.). This field helps reviewers understand the file format and ensures compatibility. (Select, Optional)

## Tips
* Ensure you only upload relevant and accurate files and information to maintain data quality.
* Use the correct file type to ensure compatibility with our review system.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
