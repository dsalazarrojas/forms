# Catalytic Converter Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catalytic Converter Submission Form is designed to collect business and converter details for inspection, testing, and potential sale or trade-in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the business information section (fields 1-2).
2. Enter the converter details (fields 3-7).
3. Select the condition and quantity of the converter (fields 8-10).
4. Provide additional information and instructions (fields 11-16).
5. Confirm your submission and preferences (field 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Business Information --**
  * **Shop or Company Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of your business or company.
  * **Contact Person Full Name** (`contact_person`): Enter the full name of the person submitting the form.
  * **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter a valid email address for contact and communication.
  * **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number for contact and communication.
- **-- Converter Details --**
  * **Vehicle Make, Model, and Year** (`vehicle_make_model`): Enter the make, model, and year of the vehicle the converter was obtained from.
  * **Serial Number (if visible)** (`serial_number`): Enter the serial number if visible on the casing (if not, leave blank).
  * **Current Condition** (`converter_condition`): Select the current condition of the converter: New/OEM Surplus, Used - Good Condition, Used - Damaged Casing, or Scrap - Shell only.
  * **Quantity of Units** (`quantity_submitted`): Enter the quantity of converters being submitted.
  * **Please prepare photos of the serial number and overall unit for upload** (`photo_instruction`): Follow the instruction to prepare photos for upload.
- **-- Logistics and Request --**
  * **Location (City and State)** (`location_city_state`): Enter the city and state where the converter is to be picked up or dropped off.
  * **Preferred Method of Submission** (`pickup_delivery_preference`): Select the preferred method of submission: Ship to Warehouse, Request Local Pickup, or Drop off in person.
  * **Any additional notes or information** (`additional_details`): Enter any additional information or notes about the submission.
  * **I certify that these units were obtained legally** (`legal_declaration`): Select the option to certify that the units were obtained legally.
  * **Are you requesting a price quote** (`quote_request`): Select Yes if you are requesting a price quote.

Note:
* All fields with a red star (\*) are required.
