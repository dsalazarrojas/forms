# Jewelry Customer Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their jewelry design inquiry. It's meant to be used when a customer contacts you with a specific design request, and you need to gather more details to understand their needs accurately. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **first name** in the first name field.
2. Enter your **last name** in the last name field.
3. Enter your **email address** in the email field.
4. Provide your **phone number** in the phone number field.
5. Select the type of **jewelry** you're interested in from the jewelry type field.
6. Describe your **design requirements** in the design description field.
7. If you have a specific **project budget** in mind, enter it in the project budget field.
8. If you have any supporting documents or images for your design inquiry, upload them in the **file upload** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (Text, required: false): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last name** (Text, required: false): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (Email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can reach you back.
* **Phone number** (Text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Jewelry type** (Select multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all types that apply to your jewelry design inquiry.
	+ You can select more than one type.
* **Design description** (Text, required: false): Describe your jewelry design requirements in detail.
* **Project budget** (Number, required: false): If you have a specific budget in mind, enter it here.
* **File upload** (Text, required: false): Upload any supporting documents or images for your design inquiry.
* **Submit** (Note, required: false): Click this button to submit your design inquiry. 

## Tips
- Please ensure you've filled out all required information.
- For best results, be as specific as possible when describing your design requirements.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
