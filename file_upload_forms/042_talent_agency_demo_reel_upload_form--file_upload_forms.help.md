# Talent Agency Demo Reel Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for talent agencies to upload demo reels showcasing their talent's skills and abilities to get discovered by industry professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, including Talent Type, Demo Reel, and Agency Name (if applicable).
2. Optionally, provide a contact person, demo link, and demo reel description (casting note).
3. Upload your demo reel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Talent Type** (`Talent Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of talent you are representing, such as Actor, Model, Dancer, or Singer.
* **Talent Inquiry** (`Talent Inquiry`, text, required): Briefly describe the talent's specific area of expertise or interest.
* **Agency Name** (`Agency Name`, text, optional): If you are a talent agency, enter your agency's name for identification purposes.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and email for follow-up inquiries.
* **Demo Link** (`Demo Link`, text, optional): Provide a link to your demo reel (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo, etc.) if you cannot upload it.
* **Casting Note** (`Casting Note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or context about the talent or demo reel.
* **Upload Demo Reel** (`Upload Demo Reel`, text, required): Upload your high-quality demo reel for industry professionals to review. 

## Tips
* Make sure to upload a high-quality, professional demo reel that showcases the talent's skills and abilities.
* Use the correct format (e.g., MP4, MOV, etc.) for the uploaded file.
* Only upload demo reels from talents represented by your agency to avoid duplication.
