# Legal Guide Download Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users who need to download a legal guide. Please fill out this form with your details to receive the necessary legal guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To download a legal guide using this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First name** in the "First name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field. This field is also required.
3. Fill in your **Email address** in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address so we can send you the legal guide in the desired format.
4. Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Fill in the name of the **Legal guide** you would like to download in the "Legal guide" field.
6. Specify the **Purpose** of your request in the "Purpose" field. This will help us understand your needs.
7. If you represent a company, enter your **Company name** in the "Company" field.
8. If you work for a specific **Department**, enter it in the "Department" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of `user@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if desired.
* **Legal guide** (`file`, text, optional): Enter the name of the legal guide you need to download.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Explain the reason why you need the legal guide.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter its name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you work for a specific department, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we send you the correct legal guide and contact you if needed.
* If you are experiencing issues downloading the legal guide, please contact our support team.
* If you need to download multiple legal guides, contact our support team directly.
