# Presentation Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for presenters to submit their presentation information, including speaker bio, presentation title, date, time, and supporting files. This will aid in the evaluation and presentation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your speaker bio, including a brief description of your background and expertise.
2. Enter your presentation title, which should be a concise title that describes your presentation.
3. Select the presentation date and time from the provided calendar or type in the date and time manually.
4. If necessary, upload supporting files for your presentation, such as a presentation file or a video.
5. Choose the assigned tool for your presentation.
6. Add any submission comments or notes, if applicable.
7. Enter your email and phone number, if provided.
8. Select any additional files to be uploaded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission form** (`submission_form`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of your presentation.
* **Speaker bio** (`speaker_bio`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your background and expertise.
* **Presentation title** (`presentation_title`, `text`, optional): Enter a concise title that describes your presentation.
* **Presentation date** (`presentation_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of your presentation from the provided calendar or enter it manually.
* **Presentation time** (`presentation_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time of your presentation from the provided options or enter it manually.
* **Presentation description** (`presentation_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your presentation.
* **Upload files** (`upload_files`, `text`, optional): This field is used to upload supporting files for your presentation, such as a presentation file or a video.
* **Upload presentation file** (`upload_presentation_file`, `text`, optional): Upload your presentation file.
* **Upload video file** (`upload_video_file`, `text`, optional): Upload your video file, if applicable.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the assigned tool for your presentation from the provided options.
* **Submission comments** (`submission_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your submission.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, if provided.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if provided.
* **File uploads** (`file_uploads`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional files to be uploaded.
* **Other fields** (`other_fields`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose any other fields that you think are relevant to your presentation.
