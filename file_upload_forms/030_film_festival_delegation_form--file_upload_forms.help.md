# Film Festival Delegation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Festival Delegation Form is used by film festival participants to provide essential information about their project. This form is necessary to help festival organizers process participant applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Enter the name of your project in the "Participant project name" field.
3. Select the category of your project from the "Participant project category" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the URL of your project in the "Participant project URL" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Participant email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Participant phone" field.
7. Enter your address in the "Participant address" field.
8. Enter any additional notes you may have in the "Participant note" field.
9. Finally, enter any notes about your project in the "Participant project note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (text): A field where you can enter your name.
* **Participant project name** (text): Enter the name of your film project.
* **Participant project category** (select one): Select the category of your project:
	+ Action
	+ Drama
	+ Comedy
	+ Romance
	+ Horror
	+ Thriller
	+ Science fiction
	+ Documentary
* **Participant project URL** (text): Enter the URL of your project.
* **Participant email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Participant phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant address** (text): Enter your address.
* **Participant note** (text): Enter any additional notes you may have about yourself.
* **Participant project note** (text): Enter any additional notes about your project.
