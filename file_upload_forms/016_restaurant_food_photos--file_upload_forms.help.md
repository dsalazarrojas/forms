# restaurant_food_photos - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect photos and information from restaurants. It is designed to be completed by restaurant staff or administrators to provide photos and descriptions of their menu items and restaurants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you have taken food photos by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "take_food_photos" field.
2. If you have taken photos, select the number of photos uploaded in the "photos_uploaded" field.
3. Enter the menu items for your restaurant in the "menu_items" field.
4. Enter a description of the photos uploaded in the "description" field.
5. Enter the name of your restaurant in the "restaurant_name" field.
6. Enter the URL of your restaurant in the "restaurant_url" field.
7. Optionally, you may add additional comments or notes about the form in the "menu_item_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **take_food_photos (Yes/No)**: Select "Yes" if you have taken photos of your restaurant's food, or "No" if you have not.
* **menu_items (text)**: Enter the list of menu items for your restaurant, one per line.
* **photos_uploaded (1/2)**: Select the number of photos you have uploaded.
* **upload_photos (text)**: Enter a short description of the photos you have uploaded.
* **description (text)**: Enter a brief description of the form and any additional information about the restaurant or menu items.
* **restaurant_name (text)**: Enter the name of your restaurant.
* **restaurant_url (text)**: Enter the URL of your restaurant's website or social media page.
* **menu_item_name (text)**: This field is optional and can be used to add additional comments or notes about the menu items.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information about your restaurant and menu items.
* Use the "menu_items" field to enter a list of menu items, one per line.
* Use the "description" field to provide a brief description of the form and restaurant.
* Take clear and well-lit photos of your restaurant's food to provide an accurate representation of your menu items.
