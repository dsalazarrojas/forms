# New Employee Document Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and upload relevant documents from new employees for the onboarding process. The form helps to ensure that new employees have the necessary documentation to start working.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please upload your resume, proof of address, onboarding documents, and any other relevant documents required for your employment.
2.  Fill in the email field with your email address.
3.  Upload your proof of ID, diploma, degree, and any other identification documents as required by your employer.
4.  Upload any other identification documents not listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Resume**: Upload your resume, which is a document that outlines your work experience, education, and skills.
* **Upload Proof of Address**: Upload a document that confirms your address, such as a utility bill or lease agreement.
* **Upload Onboarding Documents**: Upload any documents related to your onboarding process, such as a letter of introduction or a new hire checklist.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Upload Proof of ID**: Upload a valid government-issued ID, such as a driver's license or passport.
* **Upload Diploma**: Upload your diploma or degree certificate if it's relevant to your role.
* **Upload Degree**: Upload any degree-related documents, such as a degree certificate or transcript.
* **Upload Other Document**: Upload any other document not listed above that's relevant to your employment.
* **Upload Contract**: Upload any contract-related documents, such as a employment contract or non-disclosure agreement.
* **Upload License**: Upload any license-related documents, such as a professional license or certification.
* **Upload ID Card**: Upload your ID card or employee ID card.
* **Upload Passport**: Upload your passport if you're an international employee.
* **Upload Other Passport**: Upload any other passport not listed above if it's relevant to your employment.
* **Upload Other Identification**: Upload any other identification documents not listed above.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all uploaded documents are relevant to your employment and required by your employer.
* Please keep your uploaded documents up-to-date and accurate.
* If you're unsure about any document, please consult your HR representative for guidance.
* This form is not a substitute for the actual onboarding process, but rather a supplement to it.
