# Tire Loading Information Label Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about tire loading requests for internal review and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the Main Page, answer the question "Do you have any tire loading requests?"
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the "vehicle_info" field to indicate if you have a request.
3. If you select 'Yes', please enter the date of the tire loading in the "tire_loading_info" field.
4. Enter your submission time in the "Submit" field.
5. Add any additional information in the "Note" field.
6. If you have an email address associated with your account, enter it in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (text, required: false): Enter any additional information or notes about your request.
* **vehicle_info** (select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' to indicate you have a tire loading request or 'No' otherwise.
* **tire_loading_info** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the tire loading.
* **Submit** (time, required: false): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Add any additional information or details about your request.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the "Note" field to add any additional context or information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team.
