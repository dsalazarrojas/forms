# Webinar Materials Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants about materials they wish to submit for a webinar. Please note that this form is only for participants who have been invited to the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: 
   - Participant phone number
   - Participant email
   - Webinar description
   - Participant name
2. If you wish to submit materials, fill out the corresponding fields:
   - Participant company (optional)
   - Participant title (optional)
   - Material name and description (optional)
   - Upload a file (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info**: This field is not currently used in the form. However, it is a placeholder for any additional information a participant may want to add.
- **Participant phone number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Participant email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your submissions.
- **Webinar description**: Briefly describe the topic of the webinar you are submitting materials for.
- **Participant name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or ID.
- **Material name**: If you are submitting materials, enter the name you have given them.
- **Material description**: Describe the materials you are submitting, including any relevant details.
- **Uploaded File**: If you are submitting materials, select the file you wish to upload. This should be a file type accepted by our system, as indicated in the upload prompt.
- **Participant company**: If you are submitting materials, enter the name of your company.
- **Participant title**: If you are submitting materials, enter your title or job function.
