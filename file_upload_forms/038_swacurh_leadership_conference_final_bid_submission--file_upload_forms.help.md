# Swacurh Leadership Conference Final Bid Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect final bid submissions from individuals or groups for the Swacurh Leadership Conference. It is designed to gather relevant information for the review and evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field (if applicable).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Ensure that this is a valid email address that you use regularly.
4. Enter the name of your school in the "School Name" field.
5. In the "Bid Submission" field, provide a clear and concise summary of your bid, including any relevant details and justifications.
6. Select the correct region from the dropdown list in the "Region" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself or your team.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Bid Submission** (`bid_submission`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise summary of your bid, including any relevant details and justifications.
* **Region** (`region`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct region from the list:
	+ Northeast Region
	+ Northwest Region
	+ Southeast Region
	+ Southwest Region
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your bid (if needed).
