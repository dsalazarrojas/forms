# Tech Competition Project Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Competition Project Submission Form is designed to collect project submission information from teams competing in tech competitions. This form is a simple and straightforward way to gather details about each project, including any potential issues that may have arisen during the submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your team's project details in the "project_details" field.
2. Confirm that you have uploaded all required files for your project submission in the "confirmation" field.
3. Provide any additional comments or information about your project in the "additional_comments" field.
4. Review and submit the form to finalize your project submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **team_entries** (<name> `team_entries`, <type> `text`, required: false)
The "team_entries" field is where you can enter your team's details, such as your team name, members, or any other relevant information.

### **project_details** (<name> `project_details`, <type> `text`, required: false)
The "project_details" field is where you can enter a brief description of your project, including any relevant details about your project's features, technology, or achievements.

### **confirm_project_submission** (<name> `confirmation`, <type> `select_multiple`, required: false)
The "confirm_project_submission" field is a multiple-choice field where you can select one or more options to indicate whether you have uploaded all required files for your project submission. The options are:
* No Issues with Submission
* Some Issues with Submission
* Major Issues with Submission

### **additional_comments** (<name> `additional_comments`, <type> `text`, required: false)
The "additional_comments" field is where you can provide any additional comments or information about your project, such as any challenges you faced during the submission process or any questions you may have.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have uploaded all required files for your project submission before confirming that there are no issues with your submission.
* If you have any issues with your submission, select the relevant option in the "confirm_project_submission" field to notify the organizers.
* Use the "additional_comments" field to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the organizers to review your project.
