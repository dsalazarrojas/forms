# Bid Submission And Team Choice Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect bid submission information and team details from potential bidders. It includes fields for submitter information, bid opportunity details, proposed solution and proposal, team composition, document uploads, and compliance and acceptance. The form is intended for bidders to provide a complete and accurate representation of their bid and team, allowing the reviewing entity to assess and compare bids.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the submitter information: 
    - Name
    - Email Address
    - Phone Number

    These fields are mandatory and are used for contact purposes.

2. Enter bid opportunity details:
    - Bid or Project Title
    - Bid ID or Reference Number
    - Bid Amount (in the selected currency)
    - Submission Date

    Please ensure accuracy in these fields as they are crucial for bid tracking and evaluation.

3. Provide a brief executive summary, proposed solution, key value proposition, and proposed timeline or delivery schedule.

4. Complete the team composition section:
    - Number of Team Members
    - Team Lead or Project Manager Name
    - Team Lead Experience (years of relevant experience)
    - Key Team Resources or Roles
    - Team's Key Expertise Areas

    This section is essential in demonstrating your team's capabilities and strengths.

5. Upload required documents:
    - Detailed Proposal Document
    - Pricing or Cost Schedule
    - Supporting Documents (references, case studies, etc.)
    - Certifications and Qualifications
    - Client References

    Please ensure all documents are complete and accurate.

6. Confirm compliance and acceptance:
    - I Accept the Tender/RFP Terms
    - Required Insurance Coverage Confirmation
    - Confirm All Required Licenses and Permits

    This section ensures you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the bid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the bid.
* **Organization or Company Name**: Enter the name of the entity submitting the bid.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for submission confirmation and contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Bid or Project Title**: Enter the name of the bid opportunity.
* **Bid ID or Reference Number**: Enter the bid ID or reference number.
* **Bid Amount**: Enter the total value of your bid in the selected currency.
* **Currency**: Select the currency in which your bid amount is denoted.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Executive Summary**: Briefly describe your proposed solution and project overview.
* **Proposed Solution**: Describe your proposed solution in detail.
* **Key Value Proposition**: Explain why you are the best fit for the project.
* **Proposed Timeline or Delivery Schedule**: Outline your project timeline or delivery schedule.
* **Number of Team Members**: Enter the number of people on your team.
* **Team Lead or Project Manager Name**: Enter the name of your team lead or project manager.
* **Team Lead Experience**: Enter your team lead or project manager's years of relevant experience.
* **Key Team Resources or Roles**: List the primary team members and their roles or resources.
* **Team's Key Expertise Areas**: Enumerate the areas of expertise your team has.
* **Detailed Proposal Document**: Upload your detailed proposal document.
* **Pricing or Cost Schedule**: Upload your pricing or cost schedule.
* **Supporting Documents**: Upload any supporting documents, such as references or case studies.
* **Certifications and Qualifications**: Upload any relevant certifications or qualifications.
* **Client References**: Upload any client references or testimonials.
* **I Accept the Tender/RFP Terms**: Select whether you accept all terms, accept with exceptions, or do not accept.
* **Required Insurance Coverage Confirmation**: Confirm whether you have required insurance coverage.
* **Confirm All Required Licenses and Permits**: Confirm whether you meet all required licenses and permits.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant details.

## Tips

- Please ensure accuracy and completeness in all fields.
- Use proper formatting and structure for uploaded documents.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before accepting or rejecting them.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
