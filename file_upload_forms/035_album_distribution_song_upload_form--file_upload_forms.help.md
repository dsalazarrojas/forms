# Album Distribution Song Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect metadata for your song album, which will be used for distribution on various digital platforms. Please fill in the required fields to ensure your music meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the official title of your album.
2. Provide the name of the primary artist(s) appearing on the album.
3. Enter the name of the record label responsible for the release (or 'Independent' if self-released).
4. Select the digital release date for the album.
5. List the total number of tracks on the album, and enter the titles in the correct order.
6. Confirm that you have uploaded high-resolution WAV files (24-bit, 44.1kHz minimum).
7. Enter the names of the songwriters and composers.
8. If applicable, enter the already assigned ISRC codes for each track.
9. Indicate whether the release contains explicit lyrics.
10. Confirm that you have the rights and ownership for all compositions and masters.
11. Choose your distribution territories (Worldwide or specific regions).
12. Upload your artwork (minimum 3000x3000px, RGB, JPG/PNG).
13. Specify how you will be sending your artwork (Uploaded to portal or Sending via external link).
14. Indicate the status of your artwork upload.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Official Album/EP Title** (`album_title`, text, required): Enter the official title of your album.
* **Primary Artist Name** (`primary_artist`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary artist(s) as it should appear on digital streaming platforms.
* **Record Label Name** (`record_label`, text, required): Enter the name of the record label responsible for the release, or 'Independent' if self-released.
* **Digital Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): Select the digital release date for the album.
* **Total Number of Tracks** (`total_track_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of tracks on your album.
* **Tracklist Order (Song Titles)** (`tracklist_order`, text, required): List the song titles in the correct order, one per line.
* **Audio Quality Confirmation** (`audio_format_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that all files are high-resolution WAV (24-bit, 44.1kHz minimum).
* **Songwriter(s) Full Legal Names** (`songwriter_credits`, text, required): Enter the full legal names of the songwriters and composers.
* **ISRC Codes (If already assigned)** (`isrc_codes`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the ISRC codes for each track.
* **Does this release contain explicit lyrics?** (`explicit_content`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the release contains explicit lyrics.
* **Cover Artwork Status** (`artwork_check`, select_one, required): Choose how you will be sending your artwork (Uploaded to portal or Sending via external link).
* **Distribution Territories** (`distribution_territories`, select_one, required): Choose the distribution territories for your release (Worldwide or Specific Regions).
* **I declare I own or have legal license to all masters and compositions** (`rights_ownership_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have the rights and ownership for all compositions and masters.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Double-check your artwork meets the minimum requirements (3000x3000px, RGB, JPG/PNG) before uploading.
* Make sure to list the correct number of tracks on your album.
* If uploading artwork, ensure it is in the correct format.
* If sending artwork via external link, provide it in a shareable format (e.g., a link to a Google Drive or Dropbox file).
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult the provided hints or contact support for further guidance.
