# Accounting Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Document Upload Form is designed to collect information from users to upload and track accounting documents for internal tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information:
	* Client ID or Name: Enter the client's ID or name as it appears on the document.
	* Document Category: Select the category of the uploaded document (Receipts, Invoices, Bank Statements, etc.).
	* Document Date: Enter the date shown on the document.
	* Total Amount (if applicable): Enter the total amount if the document is an invoice or receipt.
	* Fiscal Year: Choose the year the document pertains to.
	* Urgency Level: Choose the level of urgency for the document.
	* Contact for Clarification: Enter the email of the person who can answer questions about this document.
2. Upload the document:
	* Make sure to follow the instructions in the upload instruction box for optimal upload experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client ID or Name**: Enter the client's ID or name as it appears on the document. This is for internal tracking purposes.
* **Document Category**: Select the category of the uploaded document from the list provided to accurately categorize it.
* **Document Date**: Enter the date shown on the document to ensure the correct date is recorded.
* **Total Amount (if applicable)**: If the document is an invoice or receipt, enter the total amount to provide accurate accounting information.
* **Fiscal Year**: Choose the year the document pertains to from the options provided.
* **Urgency Level**: Choose the level of urgency for the document, which will aid in prioritization and follow-up.
* **Upload Instructions**: Follow the instructions provided for optimal upload experience. This will ensure that your uploaded documents are clear, legible, and complete.
* **Contact for Clarification**: Enter the email of the person who can answer questions about this document, in case there is any confusion or need for clarification.
* **Phone for Clarification** (not shown on form, but provided for additional information): This field is not visible on the form but is available for users to enter their phone number in case they need to be contacted for further clarification.

Note: Please ensure all uploaded documents are in a format that can be easily read and understood by the accounting team. Use the internal portal for large batch uploads.
