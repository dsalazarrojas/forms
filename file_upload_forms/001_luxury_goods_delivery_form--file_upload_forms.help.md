# Luxury Goods Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Luxury Goods Delivery Form is a tool used for delivering luxury goods. It is designed to collect essential details for the successful execution of such deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

*   Select a date for the delivery from the calendar provided for the delivery date field.
*   Choose a time for the delivery from the options available for the delivery time field.
*   Enter the address of where the delivery will take place in the delivery address text field.
*   Type in the name of the recipient who will receive the luxury goods in the recipient name field.
*   Type in your name as the sender in the sender name field.
*   Provide a unique identifier for the order in the order id field.
*   Include any additional delivery instructions in the delivery instructions text field.
*   If applicable, upload any files or documents relevant to the delivery in the file uploads field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: no): Select a date for the delivery from the calendar provided. This date will be the primary date used for scheduling the delivery.
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: no): Choose a time for the delivery from the options available. This time will be used to ensure timely coordination with the recipient.
*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: no): Enter the exact address where the delivery will occur. This includes the building number and any additional details that might be necessary for the delivery team to find the location.
*   **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required: no): Type in the name of the recipient who will receive the luxury goods. This will help the delivery team know who to hand over the items to.
*   **Sender Name** (`sender_name`, text, required: no): Type in your name as the sender of the luxury goods. This will allow the recipient to identify you as the person sending the item.
*   **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, required: no): Provide a unique ID for the order. This can be used for reference and tracking purposes.
*   **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, text, required: no): Include any specific instructions related to the delivery, such as parking or elevator availability.
*   **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, text, required: no): If necessary, upload any relevant files or documents related to the delivery. This can include receipts, invoices, or other supporting documentation.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check the accuracy of all entered information to avoid delays or miscommunication.
*   Use the correct format for dates and times as specified in the fields.
*   Be detailed in the delivery address to avoid confusion.
*   Use clear and concise language when inputting delivery instructions.
*   Only upload relevant files to avoid unnecessary uploads.
