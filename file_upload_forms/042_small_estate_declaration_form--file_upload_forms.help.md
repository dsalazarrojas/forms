# small_estate_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The small estate declaration form is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information about a small estate. This form is crucial for the review and management of estates, ensuring that all relevant details are accurately captured and submitted for further action.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow these steps:
    - Start by entering the family information in the **Family Information** field.
    - Provide the **Estate Name** in the **Estate Name** field.
    - Enter the **Estate Owner** in the **Estate Owner** field.
    - Add the **Address** of the estate in the **Address** field.
    - Provide any additional **Property Details** if necessary.
    - Indicate whether you have any **Supporting Documents** by selecting the relevant option.
    - Confirm if the next of kin is involved or not by selecting the appropriate option.
    - Confirm if the estate has an administrator or not by selecting the correct option.
    - If required, upload any relevant **documents** and **additional files**.
    - Double-check the information provided and click on **Confirm** to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Family Information**: Enter the family information of the estate owner(s).
*  **Estate Name**: Provide the name of the estate to be declared.
*  **Estate Owner**: Enter the name and title of the estate owner.
*  **Address**: Enter the address of the estate.
*  **Property Details**: Enter any additional details about the property or estate if necessary.
*  **Supporting Documents**: Check if you have any supporting documents to attach.
*  **Next of Kin**: Confirm if the next of kin is involved or not.
*  **Estate Admin**: Confirm if the estate has an administrator or not.
*  **Upload Documents**: Upload any relevant documents if required.
*  **Upload Additional Files**: Upload any additional files if necessary.
*  **Review and Submit**: Double-check the information and submit the form for review.
