# Mug Design Contest Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect submissions for a mug design contest, gathering information about the design entry, including the design category, description, and supporting file.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of your design entry from the options provided.
2. Provide a brief description of your design.
3. If you have a file to accompany your design, upload it to this field.
4. Enter your email address and name to receive updates about your submission.
5. Enter your full name.
6. If you want to be contacted, provide your contact number.
7. Enter your address and city to provide shipping details.
8. Select your state from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Design Entry** (`Mug Design Entry` / `select_one` / required: false):
Select the category of your design entry from the options provided. This is the main category for your mug design.
* **Design Description** (`Design Description` / `text` / required: true):
A brief description of your design will help us understand it better.
* **Design File** (`Mug Design File` / `text` / required: false):
If you have a file to accompany your design, upload it here. This can be a PDF or any other file type.
* **Email** (`Email` / `email` / required: true):
Enter your email address to receive updates about your submission.
* **Full Name** (`Full Name` / `text` / required: true):
Enter your full name.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number` / `text` / required: false):
Provide your contact number if you want to receive calls or messages from us.
* **Address** (`Address` / `text` / required: true):
Enter your address to provide shipping details.
* **City** (`City` / `text` / required: true):
Enter your city to provide shipping details.
* **State** (`State` / `select_one` / required: false):
Select your state from the provided options to provide shipping details.

## Tips
- Ensure your email address is correct to receive updates about your submission.
- Be sure to enter your full name as it will appear on the mug.
- Provide accurate shipping details to ensure your mug is delivered correctly.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
