# Food Item Screening Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Item Screening Declaration Form is designed to gather accurate and complete information about food items to be screened for regulatory compliance. This form is used to ensure that food items meet the necessary standards and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide detailed information about the supplier, including their name, address, and contact details.
2. Describe the product, including its name, description, and any relevant details.
3. Enter any declaration or notes regarding the product (optional).
4. Upload any supporting documentation related to the product.
5. Review and submit the form carefully to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Details** (`supplier_details`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is used to collect information about the supplier, including their name, address, and contact details. Please enter the supplier's name and address in this field.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is used to describe the product, including its name, description, and any relevant details. Be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate screening.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, `note`, optional):
	+ This field is used for any declaration or notes regarding the product. If you have any additional information or comments, please enter them here.
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for uploading any supporting documentation related to the product. You can attach files such as certificates, labels, or other relevant documents.
* **Upload** (`upload_document`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is used to upload the supporting documentation. Please ensure that the uploaded file is relevant and accurate.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is used to submit the form. Please review all information carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
