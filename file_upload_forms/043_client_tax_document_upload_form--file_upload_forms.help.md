# Client Tax Document Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather and upload various tax documents related to a client's tax return. It ensures that all relevant documents are collected and submitted accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the tax year for which the documents are being uploaded.
3. Enter the client's email address and phone number.
4. Choose the client's filing status (e.g., Single, Married filing jointly, etc.).
5. Upload any required tax documents as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client as it appears on their tax return.
* **Tax Year**: Select the year for which the documents are being uploaded.
* **Email Address**: Enter the client's email address to ensure they can be contacted if necessary.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the client's phone number for additional communication.
* **Filing Status**: Choose the filing status that applies to the client (e.g., Single, Married filing jointly, etc.).
* **W2 Forms**: If the client is employed, upload all W2 forms received.
* **1099 Forms**: If the client has self-employment income, interest, dividends, etc., upload 1099 forms related to these income sources.
* **Business Income Documents**: Upload profit and loss statements, invoices, etc., related to business income.
* **Deduction Receipts and Documentation**: Upload medical, charitable, business expenses, etc., relevant to the client's tax return.
* **Mortgage Statements**: Upload Form 1098 or lender statements related to mortgage interest.
* **Property Tax Statements**: Upload real estate or personal property tax statements.
* **Investment Documents**: Upload 1099-B, 1099-DIV, brokerage statements, etc., related to investments.
* **Education Expense Documents**: Upload 1098-T or receipts related to education expenses.
* **Prior Year Tax Return**: Upload the previous year's tax return for reference.
* **Other Documents**: Upload any other relevant tax documents not listed above.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Add any additional information or explanations for the tax documents uploaded.

Note: Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues with the tax return process.
