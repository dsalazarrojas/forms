# photo-competition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information from entrants participating in a photo competition. You will need to fill out the form with your details and a link to your submitted photo.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Entrant Name" and "Entrant Email" fields.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Entrant Phone" field.
3. Provide a link to your submitted photo in the "Photo URL" field.
4. Enter the name of the person who uploaded your photo in the "Uploader Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **photo-competition-form** (`photo_competition_form`, text, optional): This is the main form title.
* **Entrant Name** (`entrant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the entrant.
* **Entrant Email** (`entrant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the entrant.
* **Entrant Phone** (`entrant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the entrant.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, optional): Paste the URL of your submitted photo.
* **Uploader Name** (`uploader_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who uploaded your photo.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter your information accurately and completely.
* If you're having trouble finding the uploader's name, ask them for clarification.
* Check your photo URL to make sure it links correctly to the submitted photo.
