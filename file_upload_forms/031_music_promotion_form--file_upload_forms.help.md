# music_promotion_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music promotion form is used to collect information for music promotion purposes. This includes details about the artist, their music, and promotional materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the artist's info in the Artist Info section.
2. Upload a music file in the Music File section.
3. Select the music type in the Music Type section.
4. Choose the genre(s) of the music in the Genre section.
5. Enter the music length in the Music Length section.
6. Select the release date in the Release Date section.
7. Select the contact information for the artist in the Artist Contact section.
8. Select the manager's information in the Manager Info section.
9. Select the label's information in the Label Info section.
10. Enter a description of the music in the Music Description section.
11. Upload a promotional image for the music in the Music Promotional Image section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Info**: Enter the artist's name, email, and any other relevant information in this section.
* **Music File**: Upload a file that represents the music to be promoted. This could be a file in a format such as MP3 or WAV.
* **Music Type**: Select a type of music, for example, pop, rock, or hip-hop.
* **Genre**: Choose the genre(s) of the music, such as electronic, jazz, or classical.
* **Music Length**: Enter the length of the music in minutes and seconds, for example, 3:00.
* **Release Date**: Select the date the music was released.
* **Artist Contact**: Select the contact information for the artist, such as their email or phone number.
* **Manager Info**: Select the manager's information, such as their name or email.
* **Label Info**: Select the label's information, such as their name or email.
* **Music Description**: Enter a brief description of the music, such as its style or genre.
* **Music Promotional Image**: Upload a promotional image that represents the music, such as a photo of the artist or album cover.
* **Tips**: This form is designed to be straightforward and easy to complete. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the help guide or contact your administrator for assistance.
