# Diary Article Submission Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a template for submitting diary articles. It is used to collect information about the article, including its title, category, uploaded file, article text, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the article in the "Diary Article Submission" field.
2. Select the category of the article from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Upload the file associated with the article in the "File Upload" field.
4. Enter the article text in the "Article Text" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Diary Article Submission
* **Diary Article Submission** (`diary_article_submission_form`, text, required): Enter the title of the article being submitted.

### Category
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the article from the dropdown menu.

### File Upload
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): Upload the file associated with the article.

### Article Text
* **Article Text** (`article_text`, text, optional): Enter the text of the article being submitted.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click the button to submit the form and process the article submission.

### Article Submitted Title
* **Article Submitted Title** (`article_submitted_title`, text, optional): This field will be automatically populated with the title of the submitted article.

### Date Submitted
* **Date Submitted** (`article_submitted_date`, date, optional): This field will be automatically populated with the date and time of submission.

### Author
* **Author** (`article_author`, text, optional): Enter the author's name associated with the article submission.

### Status
* **Status** (`article_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the article from the dropdown menu.

### Published Date
* **Published Date** (`article_published_date`, date, optional): This field will be automatically populated with the date the article was published.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to submit articles before proceeding.
* Review the submission guidelines and policies before submitting an article.
* Double-check the accuracy of the submitted information before confirming the submission.
* If you are experiencing issues or have questions about the submission process, contact your designated support team.
* Make sure to upload the correct file type (e.g., PDF, image, etc.) and size.
* If the article is published, ensure that it meets the necessary publication standards and requirements.
