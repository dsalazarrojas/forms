# Scientific Conference Researcher Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scientific Conference Researcher Admission Form is designed to collect essential information from researchers interested in presenting their research at the conference. This form helps us understand their background, file uploads, and presentation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and event name (if applicable) in the first field.
2. Upload your supporting files, such as research papers or presentation materials, to the second field.
3. Enter a brief description of yourself, including your institutional affiliation and any relevant research experience, in the Researcher Info field.
4. Select one of the presenter options if you wish to present your research at the conference.
5. Specify the topic of your research in the Research Topic field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the scientific conference or event you're interested in.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, Optional): Upload any supporting files, such as research papers or presentation materials, to demonstrate your research.
* **Researcher Info** (`researcher_info`, text, Optional): Briefly describe yourself, including your institutional affiliation and any relevant research experience.
* **Presenter Request** (`presenter_request`, select_one, Optional): Select one of the options below if you wish to present your research at the conference.
	+ Option 1: Yes, I would like to present my research as a speaker.
	+ Option 2: No, I do not want to present my research.
* **Research Topic** (`research_topic`, text, Optional): Specify the topic of your research for our reference.
