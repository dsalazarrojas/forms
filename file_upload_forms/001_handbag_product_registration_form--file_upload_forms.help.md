# Handbag Product Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Handbag Product Registration Form is designed to collect customer and product information for handbag products. This form will help you gather essential details about the customer and the product itself, including ownership and purchase details, and even allow you to upload files related to the handbag.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as needed.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (Page 1)** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please enter the customer's name and any other relevant information you think is necessary to register the handbag product.
* **Ownership Details (Page 1)** (`ownership_details`, text, optional): Describe the ownership details of the handbag, such as ownership period, purchase date, or any other relevant information.
* **Purchase Info (Page 1)** (`purchase_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the purchase of the handbag, including where it was purchased from, and any other relevant information.
* **File Upload (Page 1)** (`file_upload`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files related to the handbag product, such as images, videos, or documents.
