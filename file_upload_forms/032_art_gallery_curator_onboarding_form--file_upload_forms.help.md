# Art Gallery Curator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Curator Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information from new curators, facilitating their integration into the gallery team. This form is crucial for ensuring a smooth onboarding process and effective collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the curator's profile information, including Full Name, Professional Title, Primary Email Address, Phone Number, and Area of Specialization.
2. Next, provide the required documents, including a Digital Portfolio Link and a reminder for the Curriculum Vitae (CV) to be emailed or uploaded separately.
3. Specify your availability, including the Desired Start Date, Contract Type, and Expected Weekly Hours (if applicable).
4. Choose whether you will require dedicated office space in the gallery.
5. Finally, enter the necessary contact information for the emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear in the gallery's records.
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, text, required): Enter your professional title as you would like it to be displayed in the gallery's records.
* **Primary Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for gallery communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Area of Specialization** (`specialization_area`, text, required): Specify your area of expertise or specialization in the art field.
* **Digital Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, required): Provide a link to your online portfolio or curated exhibitions.
* **Curriculum Vitae (CV) - Reminder to email or upload separately** (`cv_upload_note`, note, optional): A note to email or upload your CV separately, as we do not accept file uploads via this form.
* **Desired Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required): Specify your desired start date for the onboarding process.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, required): Choose between Permanent Full-time, Permanent Part-time, Project-based (Guest Curator), and Annual Retainer.
* **Expected Weekly Hours (For part-time or project-based)** (`weekly_hours_expected`, number, optional): If applicable, specify your expected weekly hours.
* **Will you require dedicated office space in the gallery?** (`office_space_required`, select_one, required): Choose between True and No - Working Remotely.
* **Information for Business Cards (If applicable)** (`business_card_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information for your business cards, such as preferred name and title.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_timestamp`, date, required): Record the date and time you submitted this form.
