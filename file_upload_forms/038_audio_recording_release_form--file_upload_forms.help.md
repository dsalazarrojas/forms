# Audio Recording Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to release audio recordings to a third party for use in any media. It ensures that the releasor (the recording artist) grants the necessary rights to the releasee (the producer or company) and outlines the terms of the release, including compensation and usage rights.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and terms of the release.
2. Fill out the "Releasor Full Name" field with your full name as the recording artist.
3. Fill out the "Releasee (The Producer or Company)" field with the name of the company or individual you are releasing the rights to.
4. Provide a brief description of the recorded content in the "Description of the Recorded Content" field.
5. Enter the location where the recording was made in the "Location of Recording" field.
6. Enter the date the recording was made in the "Date of Recording" field.
7. Select the correct option in the "I grant the Releasee the perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable right to use my recording" field to indicate your agreement to grant rights.
8. Choose the correct compensation term in the "Compensation Terms" field to outline the terms of payment.
9. Enter your preferred name for credit attribution in the "Preferred Name for Credit/Attribution" field.
10. Select "I Warrant" in the "I warrant that I have the right to grant these permissions" field to indicate that you have the necessary rights.
11. If you are under 18, indicate that you are a minor and provide the name and signature of your parent or guardian.
12. Sign the form with your digital signature in the "Releasor Digital Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Releasor Full Name (The Performer)**: The name of the recording artist.
* **Releasee (The Producer or Company)**: The name of the company or individual you are releasing the rights to.
* **Description of the Recorded Content**: A brief description of the recorded content, e.g., Podcast Episode 12, Vocal Performance for Project X.
* **Location of Recording**: The location where the recording was made.
* **Date of Recording**: The date the recording was made.
* **I grant the Releasee the perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable right to use my recording**: Select "I Agree to Grant Rights" to confirm that you grant the necessary rights.
* **Compensation Terms**: Choose the term that outlines the payment terms for the release, e.g., One-time flat fee, Royalty based agreement, No financial compensation, Experience/Exposure only.
* **Preferred Name for Credit/Attribution**: Enter your preferred name to be credited for the recording.
* **I warrant that I have the right to grant these permissions**: Select "I Warrant" to confirm that you have the necessary rights.
* **Is the participant under 18 years of age?**: Select "True" if you are under 18.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian if you are under 18.
* **Witness Name (if required)**: Enter the name of the witness if required.
* **Date of Execution**: Enter the date and sign the form.
* **Releasor Digital Signature**: Sign the form with your digital signature.

Note: This form is a sample and should be reviewed and understood carefully before use. It is recommended that both parties review and agree on the terms before signing. This form is not a substitute for legal advice.
