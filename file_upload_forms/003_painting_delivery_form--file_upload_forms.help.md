# Painting Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Painting Delivery Form" is a document that requests information for painting delivery purposes. This form is required to be filled out by customers to provide their delivery preferences when they purchase a painting. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the recipient's details, including name and phone number (if desired).
2. Enter the recipient's email address (if desired).
3. Provide delivery instructions for the painting.
4. Specify any additional special instructions for the delivery process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipient details** (`recipient_details`, text, required): Enter the recipient's name and phone number to enable us to efficiently contact them for delivery.
* **Recipient name** (`recipient_name`, text, not required): Enter the recipient's name for our record-keeping purposes.
* **Recipient email** (`recipient_email`, email, not required): Enter the recipient's email address to send updates about the painting delivery process.
* **Recipient phone** (`recipient_phone`, text, not required): Enter the recipient's phone number to contact them about the painting delivery.
* **Delivery instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, note, not required): Provide any specific instructions for the delivery process, such as parking instructions or delivery location.
* **Special instructions** (`special_instructions`, select_multiple, not required): Specify any additional special instructions for the delivery process, such as 'Pickup', 'Delivery', or 'Shipping'.
