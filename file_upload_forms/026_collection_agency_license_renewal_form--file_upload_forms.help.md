# Collection Agency License Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for renewing a Collection Agency License. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays or rejections in the license renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary documents and information before starting the form.
2.  Fill out each field accurately and completely, and ensure you have attached all required documents.
3.  Review the form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collection Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter the legal business name of your collection agency.
* **Agency Type** (`agency_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business structure of your agency (Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, or Other).
* **Current License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter the current license number of your collection agency.
* **Current License Expiry Date** (`license_expiry`, date, required): Enter the date when your current license expires.
* **Renewal Request Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are requesting to renew your license.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Title** (`primary_contact_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the street address of your business.
* **City** (`business_city`, text, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`business_state`, text, required): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`business_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your business.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, optional): If different from the business address, enter the mailing address of your business.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, select_one, required): Select the number of years your business has been in operation (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, 10-20 years, Over 20 years).
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required): Enter the total number of staff members in your agency.
* **Number of Collectors** (`number_of_collectors`, number, required): Enter the number of licensed collectors in your agency.
* **Services Provided** (`services_provided`, select_multiple, required): Select all services your agency provides (First Party Collections, Third Party Collections, Debt Buying, Skip Tracing, Legal Collections, Medical Collections, Commercial Collections, Consumer Collections).
* **Surety Bond Information** (`bond_information`, text, required): Enter the name and amount of your surety bond provider.
* **Bond Amount** (`bond_amount`, text, required): Enter the coverage amount of your bond.
* **Bond Expiry Date** (`bond_expiry`, date, required): Enter the date when your bond expires.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Enter the provider of your liability insurance.
* **Insurance Coverage Amount** (`insurance_coverage`, text, required): Enter the policy coverage of your insurance.
* **Insurance Expiry Date** (`insurance_expiry`, date, required): Enter the date when your insurance expires.
* **Complaints Filed Against Agency** (`complaints_filed`, select_one, required): Select the number of complaints filed against your agency (None, 1-2, 3-5, 6-10, Over 10).
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, optional): If any complaints were filed, describe them briefly.
* **Disciplinary Actions** (`disciplinary_actions`, select_one, required): Select any disciplinary actions taken against your agency (None, Warning Issued, Fine Assessed, License Suspended, License Revoked).
* **Continuing Education Completed** (`continuing_education`, select_one, required): Select if all, some, or no staff members have completed the required training.
* **Financial Statement** (`financial_statement`, text, required): Upload a current financial statement of your agency.
* **Bond Certificate** (`bond_certificate`, text, required): Upload a copy of your surety bond certificate.
* **Insurance Certificate** (`insurance_certificate`, text, required): Upload a copy of your liability insurance certificate.
* **Sample Collection Letters** (`sample_collection_letters`, text, optional): Upload any sample collection letters as examples.
* **Renewal Fee Payment** (`renewal_fee`, text, required): Enter the amount of the renewal fee.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to pay the renewal fee (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Wire Transfer, ACH).
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, select_one, required): Select if you are the authorized person signing this form (Yes I Certify, Other).

## Tips

1.  Ensure you have attached all required documents before submitting the form.
2.  Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, contact the agency for clarification.
4.  Submit the form with all required fields filled out and attached documents.
