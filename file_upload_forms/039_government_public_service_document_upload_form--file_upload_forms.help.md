# Government Public Service Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Public Service Document Upload Form is designed to collect documents from citizens for government public service organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each page one by one.
2. Please answer all required fields to ensure your document is processed correctly.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Form** (`upload_form`, text, required): This is the first page where you will upload your document. Please select your document from your computer or mobile device and click the "Upload" button to attach it to your form. Make sure to choose the correct file type and size.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required): This page is where you will provide your personal details such as name, address, and contact information. Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* **Document Details** (`document_details`, text, required): This page is where you will provide additional information about the document you are uploading, such as the date of issue and expiration date. Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
* **Upload Confirmation** (`upload_confirmation`, text, required): This is the final page where you will review and confirm your uploaded document. Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the correct file type and size before uploading your document.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you encounter any issues while uploading your document, contact the government public service organization for assistance.
