# Tumor Sample Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for tumor sample submissions from users. It is meant to be filled out by users who are submitting tumor samples for analysis or examination. The form requires the user to provide their name, contact information, address, email, phone number, date, time, specimen ID, disease, tumor size, tumor stage, biopsy, surgery, sequencing, and upload supporting documentation and samples. The form also requires a signature and verification of submission details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your name, contact information, address, and other requested details.
2. Select the correct options for disease, tumor stage, biopsy, surgery, sequencing, and consent.
3. Upload any supporting documentation or samples as required.
4. Provide a signature confirming your submission details.
5. Set the verification date and time for the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Name (1)
**User Name**: *Enter your name*

*   This is the name of the person submitting the tumor sample.

### Contact Information (2)
**Contact Information**: *Note: this field is not required but may be used for follow-up purposes*

*   Enter your contact information, including any additional details that may be relevant for communication.

**Address**: *Optional field for additional address information*

### Email (4)
**Email**: *Enter your email address*

*   This is the email address that will be used for communication and sample follow-up.

**Phone**: *Enter your phone number*
