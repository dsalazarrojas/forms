# App Link And Photo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for app developers and publishers to submit their app for review and publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the submission date in the date format.
2. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Select the type of submission you are making (App link only, Photos only, or Both app link and photos).
5. Enter the app's name and category.
6. Enter a brief description of the app (required).
7. Enter the current app version.
8. Enter the app's release or launch date (optional).
9. Upload a high-resolution icon or logo image of the app.
10. Upload multiple screenshots of the app.
11. Upload promotional or banner images of the app (optional).
12. Enter the link to the app's demo video on YouTube or Vimeo (optional).
13. Enter the developer or company name that created the app.
14. Enter the developer's website.
15. Enter any additional relevant links.
16. Enter any additional notes or comments about the app.
17. Confirm that you agree to the submission terms.
