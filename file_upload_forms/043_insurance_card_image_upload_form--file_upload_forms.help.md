# Insurance Card Image Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to upload an image of their insurance card for verification and processing purposes. Please ensure that you are using this form in a legitimate and authorized context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Upload Insurance Card Image" field.
2. Select or take a clear image of your insurance card.
3. Ensure the image is well-lit, in focus, and legible.
4. Click the "Submit" button to upload the image.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Insurance Card Image**:
	+ **Upload Insurance Card Image** (insurance_card_image_upload, text, required)
	+ This is the main field where you can upload a digital image of your insurance card. Make sure to use a clear and well-lit image.
* **Date of Birth**:
	+ **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **Name of Policyholder**:
	+ **Name of Policyholder** (name_of_policyholder, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the policyholder as it appears on the insurance card.
* **Policy Number**:
	+ **Policy Number** (policy_number, text, required)
	+ Enter the policy number as it appears on the insurance card.

## Tips
* Make sure the uploaded image is in a format that can be easily read (e.g., JPEG, PNG).
* Avoid uploading low-quality or distorted images.
* Ensure that you are uploading a valid insurance card image.
