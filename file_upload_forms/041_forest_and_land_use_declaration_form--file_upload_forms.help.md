# forest_and_land_use_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forest and Land Use Declaration Form is designed for landowners or managers to accurately report their land use categories and specific fields within those categories. This form helps collect information for administrative, research, or planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your land use from the options provided (Landuse or Other).
2. Choose the specific fields within the selected category that apply to your land use (up to 7 fields can be selected).
3. Fill out the text fields with accurate and detailed information about your land use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category_file_upload_forms`, select_one): Select the category of your land use (Landuse or Other). This field is used to group your land use into a broad category for administrative or research purposes.
* **Land Use Declaration Form Fields** (`land_use_declaration_form_fields`, select_multiple): Choose the specific fields that apply to your land use (up to 7 fields can be selected). This field allows you to specify multiple fields within the selected category.
* **Land Use Declaration Form Fields 1-8** (`land_use_declaration_form_fields_1-8`, text): Fill out the text fields with accurate and detailed information about your land use. Each of these fields corresponds to a specific aspect of your land use, such as Field 1, Field 2, and so on.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct category and fields for your land use to ensure accurate data collection.
* Use specific and detailed information in the text fields to provide a clear understanding of your land use.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the provided options or consult with the form owner or manager.
