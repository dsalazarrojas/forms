# Art Gallery Painting Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting paintings for a gallery competition. It gathers information about the artist, artwork, and preferences for entry and display.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form by following these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the bottom of the page to save your progress.
2. Answer each field according to the provided instructions and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Date** (1, `entry_date`, required): Enter the date you would like to enter the competition.
* **Artist Name** (2, `artist_name`, required): Enter your full name or business name.
* **Studio Name** (3, `studio_name`, not required): If you are representing a studio, enter its name.
* **Email** (4, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (5, `phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (6, `address`, required): Enter your full address.
* **City** (7, `city`, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (8, `state`, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip Code** (9, `zip_code`, required): Enter your postal or zip code.
* **Website** (10, `website`, not required): If you have a website or portfolio, enter it here.
* **Social Media Handle** (11, `social_media_handle`, not required): If you have a social media handle, enter it here.
* **Tournament Category** (12, `tournament_category`, required): Select the category of your entry (e.g., Oil Painting).
* **Painting Title** (13, `painting_title`, required): Enter the title of your painting.
* **Painting Dimensions** (14, `painting_dimensions`, required): Enter the width and height of your painting in inches (e.g., 24 x 36).
* **Painting Year** (15, `painting_year`, required): Enter the year you created your painting.
* **Painting Description** (16, `painting_description`, required): Briefly describe your painting.
* **Artist Statement** (17, `artist_statement`, not required): Briefly describe your artistic background and vision.
* **Years Experience** (18, `years_experience`, required): Select your level of painting experience.
* **Previous Competitions** (19, `previous_competitions`, required): Indicate if you have entered competitions before.
* **Previous Competition Details** (20, `previous_competition_details`, not required): If you have entered competitions before, list them here.
* **Artwork Value** (21, `artwork_value`, not required): Select the estimated value of your artwork.
* **Insurance Coverage** (22, `insurance_coverage`, required): Indicate if you have insurance for your artwork.
* **Delivery Method** (23, `delivery_method`, required): Select how you will deliver your artwork (e.g., personally or by shipping).
* **Framing Status** (24, `framing_status`, required): Indicate if your artwork is framed or not.
* **Hanging Hardware** (25, `hanging_hardware`, required): Indicate if your artwork has hanging hardware.
* **Entry Fee Payment** (26, `entry_fee_payment`, required): Select how you will pay the entry fee.
* **Terms Accepted** (27, `terms_accepted`, required): Indicate if you accept the tournament terms and conditions.
* **Image Upload Consent** (28, `image_upload_consent`, required): Indicate if you consent to uploading images of your work.
* **Publicity Consent** (29, `publicity_consent`, required): Indicate if you consent to your work being used for promotion.
* **Additional Comments** (30, `additional_comments`, not required): Enter any additional information about your entry.
