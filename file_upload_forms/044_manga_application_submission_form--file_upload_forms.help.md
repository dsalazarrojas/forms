# Manga Application Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manga Application Submission Form is a form used to collect information from creators to showcase their manga series on our platform. This form helps us understand the creator's intent, work, and requirements for submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Creator Entry** field with your name and contact information.
2. Upload a **Cover Image** of your manga series.
3. Write a brief **Pitch** summarizing your work and its unique features.
4. Select the **Character Designer** responsible for the series.
5. Choose the **Manga Artist** who illustrated the series.
6. Enter your **Email Address** for communication purposes.
7. Provide your **Phone Number** for further contact.
8. Select the **Submission Date** when you're ready to showcase your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Entry** (text): Enter your name and contact information for further communication.
* **Cover Image** (text): Upload a high-quality image of your manga series cover.
* **Pitch** (text): Summarize your manga series in a brief and compelling way. 
* **Character Designer** (select_one): Select the person responsible for designing your manga series characters.
* **Manga Artist** (select_multiple): Select all the artists involved in the illustration of your manga series.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number for further contact and discussion.
* **Submission Date** (date): Select the date you're ready to showcase your work on our platform.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the **Cover Image** is in high quality and relevant to your manga series.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise **Pitch** that accurately represents your work.
* Select the correct **Character Designer** and **Manga Artist** for your series.
* Double-check your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** for accuracy.
* Choose the correct **Submission Date** that aligns with your schedule and goals.
