# Fictional Pet Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fictional Pet Contest Entry Form is designed for users to participate in the Fictional Pet Contest. The form collects information about the user's contest entry, including a story, artwork, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of your contest entry in the **Contest Entry** field.
2. Provide a story related to your contest entry in the **Story** field.
3. Upload your artwork in the **Artwork Upload** field.
4. Enter the name of the artist who created the artwork in the **Artist Name** field.
5. Select the type of artwork in the **Artwork Type** field.
6. Enter your email address in the **Contact Email** field.
7. Enter your phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
8. Read and agree to the submission terms in the **Submission Agreement** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contest Entry (1)**: Enter a short description of your contest entry.
* **Story (2)**: Provide a brief story related to your contest entry.
* **Artwork Upload**: Upload your artwork related to the contest entry. Please note that uploaded files will be reviewed before being accepted into the contest.
* **Artist Name (4)**: Enter the name of the artist who created the artwork.
* **Artwork Type (5)**: Select the type of artwork you are submitting (e.g., Painting, Drawing, Sculpture, Photography, Digital Art).
* **Contact Email (6)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your entry.
* **Contact Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your entry.
* **Submission Agreement (8)**: Check the box to indicate that you agree with the submission terms. This is a required field.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information in all fields to ensure your entry is eligible for the contest.
- Ensure your artwork is uploaded in a suitable format for the contest.
- If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
