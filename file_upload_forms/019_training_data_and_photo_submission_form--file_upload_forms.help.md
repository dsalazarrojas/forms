# Training Data And Photo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Data And Photo Submission Form" is a form used to collect and submit data and photos from training participants for training programs or workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Training Data And Photo Submission**: Fill in the form fields as instructed, ensuring you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. **Upload a File**: If required, upload the specified file(s) as prompted.
3. **Review and Submit**: Review your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Data And Photo Submission**: Provide a brief description of your training data.
* **Upload a File**: Upload the file specified (likely with instructions in the form).
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as the training participant.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address used for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Training Data**: Enter any additional training data required or relevant.
* **Photo Upload**: Upload any photos associated with your training experience.
* **Training Completed**: Select 'Yes' if you have completed the training, or 'No' if you haven't.
* **Training Completed Date**: If you've completed the training, select the date you finished.
* **Training Completed Time**: If you've completed the training, select the time you finished.
* **Training Completed Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you completed the training (if not already specified).
* **Training Program ID**: Enter the ID of the training program you participated in.
* **Training Program Name**: Enter the name of the training program you participated in.
* **Training Instructor ID**: Enter the ID of the training instructor.
* **Training Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the training instructor.
* **Training Data and Photo Submission Form Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your training experience.
* **Training Program Type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if the training program is of a specific type.
* **Training Program Length**: Enter the length of the training program.
* **Training Start Date**: Enter the start date of the training program.
* **Training Start Time**: Enter the start time of the training program.
* **Training End Date**: Enter the end date of the training program.
* **Training End Time**: Enter the end time of the training program.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location of the training program.
* **Training Location Address**: Enter the address of the training location.
* **Uploaded File Description**: Add a description of the uploaded file if necessary.
