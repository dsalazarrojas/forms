# Local Housing Planning Permission Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the process of completing the Local Housing Planning Permission Application form. This form is used to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking local housing planning permission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "planning-permission-request" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are requesting planning permission.
2. Enter your property address in the "property-address" field.
3. Provide your name in the "applicant-name" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, in the "contact-info" field.
5. Upload a document related to your application in the "upload-document" field.
6. Describe the document you uploaded in the "document-description" field.
7. Add any comments you would like to share in the "submitter-comments" field.
8. Enter your signature as a submitter in the "submitter-signature" field.
9. Enter your name as a submitter in the "submitter-name" field.
10. Enter your role as a submitter in the "submitter-role" field.
11. Enter your department as a submitter in the "submitter-department" field.
12. Enter your position as a submitter in the "submitter-position" field.
13. Enter your phone number as a submitter in the "submitter-phone" field.
14. Enter your website as a submitter in the "submitter-website" field.
15. Enter your social media as a submitter in the "submitter-social-media" field.
16. Enter your email as a submitter in the "submitter-email" field.
17. Enter your organization's name as a submitter in the "submitter-organization" field.
18. Enter your title as a submitter in the "submitter-title" field.
19. Enter your departmental title as a submitter in the "submitter-departmental-title" field.
20. Enter your job function as a submitter in the "submitter-job-function" field.
21. Enter your organization's name as a submitter in the "submitter-organization-name" field.
22. Enter your organization's website as a submitter in the "submitter-organization-website" field.
23. Enter your organization's address as a submitter in the "submitter-organization-address" field.
24. Enter your organization's phone number as a submitter in the "submitter-organization-phone" field.
25. Enter your organization's email as a submitter in the "submitter-organization-email" field.
