# Casting Fill Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from actors for casting purposes. It is intended to gather details about the actor's personal and professional information, as well as their materials and links, to help the casting team evaluate their submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name in the **Full Legal Name** field.
2. If your stage name is different from your legal name, enter it in the **Stage Name** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field, using the international format if applicable.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field, which will be used for communication purposes.
5. Select your union affiliation from the options provided in the **Union Affiliation** field.
6. If you are represented by a talent agency or agent, enter their name in the **Talent Agency or Agent Name** field. If you are self-represented, enter "Independent".
7. Enter your height in the **Height** field, using the format e.g. "5 foot 10 inches".
8. Select your eye color from the options provided in the **Eye Color** field.
9. Select your hair color from the options provided in the **Hair Color** field.
10. If applicable, enter your demo reel link, online resume link, and special skills in the **Demo Reel Link**, **Online Resume Link**, and **Special Skills** fields respectively.
11. Finally, ensure that you have a headshot ready for upload, as indicated in the **Headshot Instruction** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Stage Name**: If different from your legal name, enter your stage name here.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the international format if applicable (e.g. +1 123 456 7890).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Union Affiliation**: Select your union affiliation from the options provided. If you are not affiliated with any union, select "Non-Union".
* **Talent Agency or Agent Name**: If you are represented by a talent agency or agent, enter their name here. If you are self-represented, enter "Independent".
* **Height**: Enter your height in the format "e.g. 5 foot 10 inches".
* **Eye Color**: Select your eye color from the options provided.
* **Hair Color**: Select your hair color from the options provided.
* **Demo Reel Link**: If you have a demo reel, enter the link here.
* **Online Resume Link**: If you have an online resume, enter the link here.
* **Special Skills**: Enter any special skills you possess, such as dialects, martial arts, or musical instruments.
* **Primary Location Base**: Enter your primary location base, in the format "City, State".
* **Headshot Instruction**: Please ensure you have a headshot ready for upload. We will contact you for file submission if the system is busy.
* **Physical Attributes Header**: This section is for your information only.
* **Physical Attributes** is not applicable in this form.

**Tips**:

* Please ensure that you have all necessary materials and links ready before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the casting team for assistance.
