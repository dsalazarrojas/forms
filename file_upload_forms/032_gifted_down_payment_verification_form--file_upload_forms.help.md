# Gifted Down Payment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gifted Down Payment Verification Form is designed to collect and verify information about gifted funds for down payments. It helps in accurately tracking and recording gifted funds received for down payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information for each field carefully, as described in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
2.  Upload the relevant file if required.
3.  Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, required): Fill in your details accurately, including your name, email, or any other relevant information as required.
* **Gifted Fund Info** (`Gifted Fund Info`, `text`, required): Provide details of the gifted funds, including the source and purpose of the funds.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the exact amount of the gifted funds received.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the gifted funds were received.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time when the gifted funds were received.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the gifted funds, if needed.
* **Upload File** (`Upload File`, `text`, required): Upload a file that supports your gift claim, such as a receipt or invoice. Please ensure the file is in a compatible format (e.g., PDF, image, etc.).
