# Remote Vehicle Tune Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Vehicle Tune Request form is used to collect vehicle-specific information from customers to assist the customer service team with vehicle-related requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as you will not be able to submit the form without them.
2. Select the correct options from the select fields (vehicle make, model, customer goals, file type).
3. Upload a file with vehicle details (in the "File Details" field).
4. Select the correct customer goals and file type for your vehicle.
5. Enter any additional notes about your vehicle.
6. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle ID** (1): *Please enter the vehicle's ID*
	+ This is the unique identifier for your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (2): *Please select the vehicle's make*
	+ Choose the make of your vehicle from the list of available options.
* **Vehicle Model** (3): *Please select the vehicle's model*
	+ Choose the model of your vehicle from the list of available options.
* **Customer Goals** (4): *Please enter your customer goals*
	+ Enter a brief description of what you are hoping to achieve with your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (5): *Please enter the vehicle's year*
	+ Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **File Details** (6): *Please upload a file with vehicle details*
	+ Upload a file containing information about your vehicle, such as a photo or repair manual.
* **Vehicle Details** (7): *Please enter vehicle details*
	+ Enter any additional information about your vehicle that may be relevant to your request.
* **File Type** (8): *Please select the file type*
	+ Choose the type of file you are uploading (e.g. image, document, etc.).
* **File URL** (9): *Please enter the file URL*
	+ Enter the URL of the file you are uploading.
* **Customer Goals List** (10): *Please select customer goals*
	+ Choose the customer goals that apply to your vehicle.
* **Customer Goals List Value** (11): *Please select customer goals list value*
	+ Choose the customer goals list value that apply to your vehicle.
* **Notes** (12): *Please enter notes*
	+ Enter any additional information about your request that may be helpful for the customer service team to know.
* **Submit** (13): *Please submit your request*
	+ Once you have completed all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to send your request to the customer service team.
