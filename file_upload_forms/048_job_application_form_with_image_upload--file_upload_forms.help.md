# Job Application Form With Image Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Application Form With Image Upload is a general-purpose form designed for job applicants to submit their information and documents for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your first name, last name, email address, and any other information you feel is relevant.
2. Upload your resume to provide a detailed account of your work experience and qualifications.
3. Upload an image (e.g. a photo or a scan of your ID) to verify your identity.
4. Select the job title and type that matches your current or desired profession.
5. Indicate your current job status (Active or Inactive) and if other, provide a brief explanation.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Resume upload** (text, required): Upload your resume in a supported file format (e.g. PDF, DOCX, TXT).
* **Image upload** (text, required): Upload an image to verify your identity (e.g. a photo or a scan of your ID).
* **Job title** (text, required): Select the title of the job or profession you are applying for.
* **Job type** (text, required): Select the type of job you are applying for (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Internship).
* **Job status** (select_one, required): Choose your current job status (Active or Inactive). If you select "Inactive", please provide a brief explanation in the next field.
* **If other, please specify** (text, optional): If your job status is not listed, provide a brief explanation to clarify.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to submit your application.
