# Pitch Deck Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pitch Deck Submission Form is a crucial tool for startups and entrepreneurs to submit their pitch deck to the relevant audience. It captures essential information that helps the audience understand the startup's idea, product, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*), and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
2. Select the correct category from the dropdown menu that best describes your pitch deck.
3. Enter the name of your startup, including the name of the company and the product/service being pitched.
4. Select the date and time of submission, using the date and time picker or typing in the format mm/dd/yyyy and hh:mm AM/PM.
5. Describe the investment being sought, including the amount and type of investment.
6. Provide a brief description of the pitch deck and its contents.
7. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are filled out correctly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pitch Deck** (`pitch_deck`, `text`, required): Enter a title for your pitch deck.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best describes your pitch deck. Options are:
	+ category1
	+ category2
	+ category3
* **Name of Startup** (`name_of_startup`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your startup, including the company name and product/service being pitched.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, `date`, required): Select or type the date of submission, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time of Submission** (`time_of_submission`, `time`, required): Select or type the time of submission, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Description of Investment** (`description_of_investment`, `text`, optional): Describe the investment being sought, including the type and amount of investment.
* **Amount Invested** (`amount_invested`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount already invested in your startup.
* **Amount Requested** (`amount_requested`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of investment being requested.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your pitch deck and its contents.
* **Tips**:
	+ Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly before submitting.
	+ If you need help or have questions, contact us at [support email/phone number].
