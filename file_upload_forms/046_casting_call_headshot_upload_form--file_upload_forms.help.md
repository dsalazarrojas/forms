# Casting Call Headshot Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casting Call Headshot Upload Form is designed to collect essential information from actors for casting purposes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in the provided fields.
2. Upload your headshot photo as per the specified format.
3. Review and agree to the submission and release agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Date**: This field is used to record the date of submission.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
	+ Required: Yes
* **Production Title**: Enter the name of the film, show, or project you are applying for.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Role(s) Applied For**: List the roles you are applying for.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Full Name**: Enter your legal or stage name.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Date of Birth**: Record your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Age Range**: Select your age range from 18-65+.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Height**: Record your height.
	+ Required: No
* **Hair Color**: Select your hair color from the provided options.
	+ Required: No
* **Eye Color**: Select your eye color from the provided options.
	+ Required: No
* **Ethnicity**: Enter your ethnicity.
	+ Required: No
* **Headshot Photo**: Upload a professional headshot in JPG/PNG format.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Additional Photos**: Upload 2-3 more photos if available.
	+ Required: No
* **Years of Acting Experience**: Record your years of acting experience.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Previous Acting Credits**: List your previous acting credits.
	+ Required: No
* **Do you have an agent or manager?**: Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Agent or Manager Name and Contact**: If you have an agent or manager, enter their name and contact information.
	+ Required: No
* **Submission and Release Agreement**: Select 'Yes, I agree' to certify that the submitted photos are of yourself and the submission is accurate.
	+ Required: Yes

Note: Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly for a successful submission.
