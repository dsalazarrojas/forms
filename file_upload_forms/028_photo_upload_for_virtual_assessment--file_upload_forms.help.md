# photo_upload_for_virtual_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to upload photos for a virtual assessment. Please use this form to provide accurate and relevant information about the photos you are uploading.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter a brief title for the photos you are uploading.
2. Select the category of the assessment (e.g., "File Upload Forms" or "Other").
3. Provide a short description of the context in which the photos were taken or the items being assessed.
4. Select the photos you would like to upload (up to 3 photos).
5. Enter your email address.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photo Upload For Virtual Assessment** (`title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the photos you are uploading.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the assessment (e.g., "File Upload Forms" or "Other").
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a short description of the context in which the photos were taken or the items being assessed.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Select the photos you would like to upload (up to 3 photos).
* **Upload** (`upload`, select_multiple, required): Select the photos you would like to upload.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete and submit the form.
* **Additional Notes** (`description2`, note, optional): Add any additional information about the photos or assessment.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date the photos were taken.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time the photos were taken.
* **File** (`file`, text, optional): Enter any additional file information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. 

Note: The "Note" fields are for additional comments or information that are not required but can be useful for further context.
