# Museum Visitor Story Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Museum Visitor Story Submission Form is designed to help the museum collect feedback and information from visitors, ensuring that their experiences and opinions are heard and considered. The form is meant to be completed once per visit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Welcome to the museum!** - This section is an introduction, and you can type any information you'd like to share with the museum staff.
2. **Tell us about your visit**: Share your story and thoughts about your visit. Be as detailed as you'd like.
3. **Attach photos of your visit**: You can upload up to [insert number] photos from your visit.
4. **What would you like to feedback**: If you have any comments or suggestions about your visit, please share them here.
5. **Your email address**: Please enter your email address so the museum staff can contact you if needed.
6. **Your phone number**: This is optional, but if you'd like the museum to follow up with you, you can enter your phone number.
7. **Museum name**: Please type the name of the museum you visited.
8. **Date of visit**: Enter the date you visited the museum.
9. **Time of visit**: Enter the time you spent at the museum.
10. **How would you rate your visit**: Rate your visit as Very Good, Good, or Bad.
11. **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to express.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the museum!** (`museum_visitor_story_submission_form_first_page`): This is the first section of the form, where you can type a brief introduction or any information about your visit.
* **Tell us about your visit** (`story`): Share your story and thoughts about your visit. This is a required field, so please make sure to fill it out.
* **Attach photos of your visit** (`photos`): You can upload photos from your visit to share with the museum staff.
* **What would you like to feedback** (`feedback`): If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them here. This is optional, but it's a good opportunity to provide feedback.
* **Your email address** (`email`): Please enter your email address so the museum staff can contact you if needed.
* **Your phone number** (`phone`): This is optional, but if you'd like to be contacted, you can enter your phone number.
* **Museum name** (`museum_name`): Please type the name of the museum you visited.
* **Date of visit** (`date_visit`): Enter the date you visited the museum.
* **Time of visit** (`time_visit`): Enter the time you spent at the museum.
* **How would you rate your visit** (`rating`): Rate your visit as Very Good, Good, or Bad.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Share any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to express.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers, as this will help the museum staff understand your experience.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the museum staff directly.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback and help us improve your experience!
