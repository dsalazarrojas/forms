# Electric Toothbrush Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electric Toothbrush Warranty Claim Form is used to initiate the warranty claim process for your electric toothbrush. This form collects necessary information to process your claim, and it helps our team to efficiently assist you with your warranty claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are logged in to the system.
2. Fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. (Optional) If you have any supporting documents, click the "Add File" button to upload them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electric Toothbrush Warranty Claim** (text, required): Enter a description of your electric toothbrush that you are claiming.
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **File Upload** (file, optional): You can upload any supporting documents, such as proof of purchase or product documentation, to help us process your claim.
* (Attachments 1-20) (text, optional): You can upload up to 20 supporting documents.

Tips: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely. If you have any supporting documents, please upload them to support your claim. If you are unsure about any information or have any issues with the form, contact our support team for assistance.
