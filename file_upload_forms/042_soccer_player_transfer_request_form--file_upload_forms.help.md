# Soccer Player Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Player Transfer Request Form is used to collect information about a player's transfer, including details about the player, transfer information, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's details in the Player Details section.
2. Provide information about the transfer, including the transfer type and date, in the Transfer Information section.
3. Enter any additional transfer details in the Transfer Details section.
4. Enter the club's name and location if applicable.
5. Enter the player's name.
6. Select the transfer date.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, required: false): Enter the player's name, date of birth, and other relevant contact information.
* **Transfer Information** (`transfer_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the transfer, including the reason for the transfer and any other relevant details.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Transfer Type** (`transfer_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of transfer:
	+ Player Transfer
	+ Loan
	+ Free Agent
* **Player Status** (`player_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of the player:
	+ Active
	+ Suspended
	+ Retired
* **Transfer Fee** (`transfer_fee`, number, required: false): Enter the transfer fee.
* **Transfer Details** (`transfer_details`, note, required: false): Enter any additional transfer details, such as the reason for the transfer.
* **Club Name** (`club_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the club.
* **Club Location** (`club_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the club.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the player.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, required: false): Confirm the transfer date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant personnel or seek guidance.
* Review and submit the form carefully before finalizing the transfer request.
