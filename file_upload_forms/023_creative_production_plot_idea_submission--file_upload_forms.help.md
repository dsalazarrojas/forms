# Creative Production Plot Idea Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Production Plot Idea Submission form is designed to collect creative production plot ideas from writers, directors, or other creatives. It is intended to gather basic information about the plot, story, and attachments related to the idea.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Form Submission Details" field with a brief description of the submission, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant context.
2. Enter a brief summary of the plot idea in the "Plot Idea" field.
3. Provide a title for the story in the "Story Title" field.
4. Enter a brief description of the story in the "Story Description" field.
5. List relevant keywords for the story in the "Story Keywords" field.
6. Upload any relevant file attachments, such as scripts, concept art, or other supporting materials.
7. Enter any additional information about the submission in the "Additional Info" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission Details**: A brief description of the submission, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant context. (Required)
* **Plot Idea**: A brief summary of the plot idea. (Optional)
* **Story Title**: A title for the story. (Optional)
* **Story Description**: A brief description of the story. (Optional)
* **Story Keywords**: Relevant keywords for the story. (Optional)
* **File Attachments**: Upload any relevant file attachments, such as scripts, concept art, or other supporting materials. (Optional)
* **Attachment 1-17**: Up to 17 file attachments can be uploaded. (Optional)
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the submission. (Optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise title for the story.
* Keep the plot idea and story description brief and to the point.
* Use relevant keywords to help search and categorize the submission.
* Attach any relevant supporting materials, such as scripts or concept art, to provide context and examples.
* Use the "Additional Info" field to provide any additional context or information about the submission.
