# Product Photography Reshoot Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a reshot of a product's photography due to various reasons. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your product information and reshot requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: <label>This is the title of the form, for identification purposes only.</label>. This field is <strong>optional</strong>, but it's suggested to be filled for organization.
* **Product Name**: <label>The name of the product that you would like to have a reshot for.</label>. Please provide the actual name of the product or item.
* **Product SKU**: <label>Enter the unique identifier of the product, for identification and referencing.</label>. This helps us locate your product quickly.
* **Product Description**: <label>A brief description of the product, to aid in understanding the product's context.</label>. Please provide a short description to help us know what the product is.
* **Product URL**: <label>Enter the URL of the product, for further reference and verification.</label>. This helps us confirm the product's existence and accessibility.
* **Referral Image**: <label>Any image you have that refers to the product, for context and reference.</label>. This can be a screenshot or a photo of the product.
* **Deadline**: <label>Choose a date and time by which you would like the reshot to be completed.</label>. Please be realistic with your deadline.
* **Reshoot Date**: <label>Provide the date of the reshot, if you already have a specific date in mind.</label>. This helps us schedule the reshot accordingly.
* **Reshoot Reason**: <label>Explain the reason for the reshot, to help us understand the context.</label>. Provide a brief reason for the reshot, so we can handle your request effectively.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have checked the product's existing photography record before submitting a reshot request to avoid duplication of efforts.
- Be as clear and concise as possible when filling in the form.
- Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for us to understand your reshot request.
