# Student Essay Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Essay Upload Form is designed to collect and store student essays for educational assessments and evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to upload the essay and that it is not a duplicate submission.
2. Enter the essay to upload in the designated text field.
3. Provide your student's information, including their name, email, and other relevant details.
4. Enter the date the essay was submitted.
5. Optionally, provide your teacher's name and other class/school details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Essay to Upload**: Enter the essay to be uploaded and stored for educational assessment.
* **Student**: Enter your student's name.
* **Student Email**: Enter your student's email (if applicable).
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the essay was submitted.
* **Teacher**: Enter your teacher's name (if applicable).
* **Class**: Enter your class name (if applicable).
* **School**: Enter your school name (if applicable).

## Tips
- Ensure that the essay meets the required format and size constraints.
- Verify that the essay is properly formatted for upload and storage.
- Use clear and descriptive labels for your uploaded files to help identify them easily.
