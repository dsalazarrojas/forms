# Consular ID Card Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew a consular ID card for individuals who wish to update their identification information. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in sections 1-13, including your current identification information.
2. Upload required supporting documents as per section 15-21.
3. Answer the questions in sections 14 and 17-18 for your background information and reason for renewal.
4. Finally, submit your application by signing and dating section 27.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your current ID.
* **2. Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
* **3. Place of Birth** (`place_of_birth`, text, required): Enter the city and country of your birth.
* **4. Nationality** (`nationality`, text, required): Select the country of your citizenship.
* **5. Current ID Number** (`current_id_number`, text, required): Enter the ID number from your expiring ID card.
* **6. Current ID Expiration Date** (`id_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your current ID card expires.
* **7. Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your full residential address.
* **8. City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **9. State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state, province, or region of your residence.
* **10. Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **11. Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`, text, required): Select the country where you currently live.
* **12. Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for contact purposes.
* **13. Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **14. Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, required): Select your current marital status from the options provided.
* **15. Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required): Enter your current profession or job title.
* **16. Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current employer or company, if applicable.
* **17. Reason for Renewal** (`reason_for_renewal`, select_one, required): Select the reason why you are renewing your ID card from the options provided.
* **18. Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of your emergency contact, if applicable.
* **19. Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact, if applicable.
* **20. Upload Passport Photo** (`document_upload_photo`, text, required): Upload a recent passport-style photograph of yourself.
* **21. Upload Current ID** (`document_upload_current_id`, text, required): Upload a front and back copy of your current ID card.
* **22. Upload Proof of Address** (`document_upload_proof_address`, text, required): Upload a utility bill or bank statement as proof of your residential address.
* **23. Upload Birth Certificate** (`document_upload_birth_certificate`, text, optional): Upload a certified copy of your birth certificate, if applicable.
* **24. Have You Previously Renewled This ID** (`previous_renewals`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you have previously renewed your ID card, if applicable.
* **25. Special Requests or Accommodations** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requirements or notes, if applicable.
* **26. Declaration** (`declaration`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to declare that all information provided is true and accurate.
* **27. Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
