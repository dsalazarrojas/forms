# Playwriting Contest Submission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect playwriting submissions for a contest. It's intended to gather information about the script, submitter, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Fill out your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure it's correct and valid.
4. Enter the name of your script in the **Script Name** field.
5. Upload your script file in the **Script File** field. Make sure it's a valid file type (e.g., PDF, DOCX, TXT).
6. Select the genre of your script from the **Genre** field dropdown list (Drama, Comedy, Tragedy, Romance, Sci-Fi, Mystery, Horror).
7. Choose the type of submission from the **Submission Type** field dropdown list (Full Length, One Act, Short Story, Script Fragment).
8. Choose the type of script from the **Script Type** field dropdown list (Screenplay, Television, Stageplay, Novel).
9. Fill out the name of the person submitting the script in the **Submitter Name** field.
10. Provide the email address of the person submitting the script in the **Submitter Email** field.
11. Optionally, provide the submitter's contact information in the **Submitter Contact Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for correspondence. (Required)
* **Script Name**: Enter the name of your script.
* **Script File**: Upload a valid file type of your script (e.g., PDF, DOCX, TXT). 
* **Genre**: Choose the genre of your script from the provided options.
	+ Drama
	+ Comedy
	+ Tragedy
	+ Romance
	+ Sci-Fi
	+ Mystery
	+ Horror
* **Submission Type**: Choose the type of submission from the provided options.
	+ Full Length
	+ One Act
	+ Short Story
	+ Script Fragment
* **Script Type**: Choose the type of script from the provided options.
	+ Screenplay
	+ Television
	+ Stageplay
	+ Novel
* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the script.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the script. (Required)
* **Submitter Contact Info**: Optionally, enter the contact information of the person submitting the script.

## Tips
* Ensure you're submitting an original and unpublished work.
* Be honest about the type of script and submission.
* You may submit multiple scripts, but fill out one submission form per script.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
