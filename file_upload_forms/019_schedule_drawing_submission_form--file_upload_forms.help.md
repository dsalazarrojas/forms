# Schedule Drawing Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule Drawing Submission Form is designed to collect information about a submitted drawing and its associated details. This form is intended for submitters to provide clear and accurate information about their submissions for the review process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **User's Information:**
	* Fill in your name and any additional contact information.
	* Please provide a valid date for the submission.
2.  **File Title and Description:**
	* Briefly describe the drawing you are submitting.
3.  **File Type:**
	* Check the box if the drawing is of a specific type.
4.  **File Category:**
	* Check the box if the drawing belongs to a specific category.
5.  **Date of Submission:**
	* Enter the date you are submitting the drawing.
6.  **Time of Submission:**
	* Enter the time you are submitting the drawing (optional).
7.  **Location of Submission:**
	* Enter the location where you are submitting the drawing (optional).
8.  **User's Contact Information:**
	* Provide a valid phone number where you can be reached.
9.  **User's Email Address:**
	* Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
10. **Additional Comments:**
	* If you have any additional information or notes, provide them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User's Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for you to enter your name and any additional contact information.
* **File Title and Description** (`file_details`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Use this field to briefly describe the drawing you are submitting.
* **File Type** (`file_type`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Check this box if the drawing is of a specific type. This field is optional but helps with organization and categorization.
* **File Category** (`file_category`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Check this box if the drawing belongs to a specific category. This field is also optional.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_submission`, `date`, required: true):
	+ Enter the date you are submitting the drawing.
* **Time of Submission** (`time_submission`, `time`, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of submission if applicable. This field is not required but helps with scheduling and coordination.
* **Location of Submission** (`location_submission`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Use this field to enter the location where you are submitting the drawing. This field is optional.
* **User's Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: true):
	+ Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **User's Email Address** (`user_email`, `email`, required: true):
	+ Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false):
	+ If you have any additional information or notes, provide them here.
* **Submit**:
	+ Once all fields are filled, press the submit button to submit your form.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Use proper date and time formatting as per the fields' specifications.
* Enter a valid phone number and email address in the respective fields.
