# Artist Page Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects essential information about an artist, including personal details and professional data. It is designed to create or update artist pages on various platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, start by providing your first and last names in the designated fields. Next, enter your stage name (if different from your real name), email address, phone number, and website URL (if applicable). You may also provide your Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube channel handles if you have them. 

For your social media handles, you can enter any public URLs that link to your profiles.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`artist_first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`artist_last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Stage Name or Artist Name** (`artist_stage_name`, text, **required**): Enter your stage name or the name by which you are commonly known.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, **optional**): Enter the URL of your personal website or portfolio.
* **Instagram Handle** (`instagram_handle`, text, **optional**): Enter your Instagram profile URL.
* **Twitter Handle** (`twitter_handle`, text, **optional**): Enter your Twitter profile URL.
* **YouTube Channel URL** (`youtube_channel`, text, **optional**): Enter your YouTube channel URL.
* **Spotify Artist Profile URL** (`spotify_profile`, text, **optional**): Enter your Spotify artist profile URL.
* **Primary Genre or Style** (`primary_genre`, text, **required**): Select the primary genre or style of your music, art, or creative work.
* **Sub-genres or Related Styles** (`subgenres`, text, **optional**): Enter a comma-separated list of sub-genres or related styles to your work.
* **Artist Biography** (`biography`, text, **required**): Write a brief 150-300 word biography that describes your background, interests, and accomplishments.
* **Years in Industry** (`years_in_industry`, number, **optional**): Enter the number of years you have been in the industry.
* **Notable Achievements or Awards** (`notable_achievements`, text, **optional**): Enter any notable achievements or awards you have received.
* **Profile Photo Upload** (`profile_photo`, text, **required**): Upload a professional headshot or portrait photo.
* **Additional Artist Images** (`artist_images`, text, **optional**): Upload performance photos, behind-the-scenes shots, or other relevant images.
* **Recent Releases or Projects** (`recent_releases`, text, **optional**): Enter the title of your recent album, single, exhibition, or project.
* **Upcoming Events or Performances** (`upcoming_events`, text, **optional**): Enter the dates and titles of your upcoming events or performances.
* **Open to Collaborations** (`collaboration_interests`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether you are open to collaborations, specific types only, or not interested.
* **Booking Availability** (`booking_availability`, text, **optional**): Enter your availability for booking and performance requests.
* **Additional Information or Special Notes** (`additional_information`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional information that you would like to share about yourself.
