# Children's Book Illustration Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations to submit illustrations for children's books. It collects necessary information to ensure a smooth submission process, including contributor details, project specifics, and artistic style preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contributor information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your experience level as an artist.
3. Provide project details, including book title, brief book description, target age group, book genre, and number of pages to illustrate.
4. Select the file formats and resolution of the illustrations you will submit.
5. Upload your illustrations.
6. Confirm your rights ownership and agree to the submission terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`contributor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be published.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Brief Artist Biography** (`artist_bio`, `text`, optional): Tell us about your artistic background and experience.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your experience level as an artist.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the book you are submitting.
* **Brief Book Description** (`book_description`, `text`, optional): Summarize the story of the book.
* **Target Age Group** (`target_age_group`, `select_one`, required): Choose the target age group for the book.
* **Book Genre** (`genre`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all genres that apply to the book.
* **Number of Pages to Illustrate** (`page_number`, `number`, required): Enter the number of pages you will illustrate.
* **Project Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, required): Enter the deadline for the project.
* **Total Illustrations Provided** (`total_illustrations`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of illustrations you will submit.
* **File Formats of Illustrations** (`file_format`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all file formats you will provide (e.g., PNG, JPG, PDF, EPS, PSD, SVG, Other).
* **Upload Illustrations** (`illustration_upload`, `text`, required): Upload your illustrations (Maximum 50MB per file).
* **Color or Black and White** (`color_specification`, `select_one`, required): Choose the color specification for your illustrations (Full color, Black and white, Grayscale, Mixed).
* **Image Resolution (DPI)** (`resolution`, `number`, required): Enter the resolution of your illustrations (DPI).
* **Artistic Style** (`art_style`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all artistic styles you prefer (e.g., Watercolor, Digital, Pencil, Ink, Acrylic, Mixed media, Vector, Collage).
* **Style Notes or Specifications** (`style_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any special instructions for your illustrations.
* **Confirm Rights to Submitted Illustrations** (`rights_ownership`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you own all rights to the submitted illustrations.
* **Agree to Submission Terms** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to the submission terms.

**Tips**

* Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
* Ensure that your email address and phone number are correct and up-to-date.
* Attach relevant and suitable illustrations for the project.
* Confirm that you have all necessary rights and permissions for the illustrations.
* Review the submission terms carefully before agreeing.
