# Manuscript Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for authors to submit their manuscripts for review, publication, or other internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Author Name** (optional but recommended for proper record-keeping).
2. Provide the **Manuscript Title**, which should be a brief and descriptive title of your manuscript.
3. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used for communication purposes.
4. If applicable, provide a **Phone** number for further contact.
5. Upload your **Manuscript File**, either in **Doc**, **PDF**, **Image**, **Audio**, or **Video** format.
6. Select the **Submission Date** when you submitted your manuscript.
7. Choose the **Submission Time** when you submitted your manuscript.
8. Indicate the **Manuscript Status**, such as "In progress," "Complete," "Pending," or "Rejected."
9. Add any **Comments** you'd like to include with your submission.
10. Select the person or **Assigned To** who will be handling your manuscript.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Author Name**: Enter your name as the author of the manuscript.
* **Manuscript Title**: Enter a brief and descriptive title of your manuscript.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid 10-digit phone number, if applicable.
* **Manuscript File**: Upload your manuscript file in one of the supported formats (Doc, PDF, Image, Audio, or Video).
* **File Type**: Select the type of file you are uploading (Doc, PDF, Image, Audio, or Video).
* **Submission Date**: Select the date you submitted your manuscript.
* **Submission Time**: Select the time you submitted your manuscript.
* **Manuscript Status**: Indicate the current status of your manuscript (In progress, Complete, Pending, or Rejected).
* **Comments**: Add any comments you'd like to include with your submission.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person or team member assigned to handle your manuscript.
