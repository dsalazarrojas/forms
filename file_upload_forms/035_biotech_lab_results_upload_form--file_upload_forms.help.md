# Biotech Lab Results Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biotech Lab Results Upload Form is designed to collect and share laboratory results, providing a platform for researchers to upload data files, descriptions, and other relevant information. This form helps streamline the process of sharing results, facilitating collaboration and ensuring accuracy in research.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Researcher Information** section with your details, including your first and last name, researcher ID, email address, phone number, and department or division.
2. Enter your **Lab Name** and select the correct **Department** from the options provided.
3. Complete the **Project Information** section with the project's name, identification number, and **Principal Investigator**'s name.
4. Select the **Experiment Type** from the options provided and enter a brief description of the **Experiment Details**.
5. Enter the **Experiment Date**, **Sample Type**, and **Number of Samples**.
6. Provide a **Results Summary**, including a brief description of the findings.
7. Select the **Status** of the results, such as **Preliminary**, **Final**, or **Pending Review**.
8. Confirm that quality control was performed and select the status accordingly.
9. Upload your **Result Files** and select the **File Format** from the options provided.
10. Describe each file in the **File Description** field and enter the **Total File Size**.
11. If necessary, upload **Additional Files** and provide a brief description for each.
12. Describe the **Analysis Method** used to generate the results and select the **Software Used** from the options provided, if applicable.
13. Enter any **Statistical Methods** employed in the analysis.
14. Make any **Observations**, including notable results or issues.
15. Enter a brief description of **Next Steps** recommended.
16. Complete the **Review Information** section with the **Reviewers Name**, **Review Status**, and any relevant comments.
17. If necessary, provide a **Review Comments** section for the reviewer.
18. Confirm that the results were approved and enter the **Approval Date**.
19. Enter the **Submission Timestamp**, confirming when the results were submitted.
20. Finally, confirm the accuracy of your submission by selecting the correct option in the **Researcher Confirmation** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_first_name`, `researcher_last_name`, `researcher_id`, `email`, `phone`): Enter your personal and professional details, including your first and last name, researcher ID, email address, and phone number. These are required fields and serve as a way for the platform to contact and validate your identity.
* **Lab Name** (`lab_name`): Enter the name of your laboratory or institution.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department or division associated with your laboratory or institution.
* **Project Information** (`project_name`, `project_code`, `principal_investigator`): Enter the project's name, identification number, and the name of the principal investigator.
* **Experiment Details** (`experiment_details`): Provide a brief description of the experiment, explaining its purpose and methodology.
* **Experiment Date** (`experiment_date`): Enter the date when the experiment was conducted.
* **Experiment Type** (`experiment_type`): Select the type of experiment that was performed, such as PCR Analysis, Western Blot, etc.
* **Sample Type** (`sample_type`): Enter the type of samples tested, such as Blood, Tissue, etc.
* **Number of Samples** (`sample_count`): Enter the number of samples that were tested.
* **Sample IDs** (`sample_ids`): Enter the identification numbers for each sample.
* **Results Information** (`results_information`): Upload and describe the results, including any relevant images or files.
* **Results Summary** (`results_summary`): Provide a brief summary of the findings.
* **Results Status** (`results_status`): Select the status of the results, such as Preliminary, Final, or Pending Review.
* **Quality Control** (`quality_control`): Confirm that quality control was performed on the results.
* **File Upload Section** (`file_upload_section`): This is a note field indicating that this is the section where you will upload your result files.
* **Result Files** (`result_files`): Upload the actual data files for the results, and enter their formats in the **File Format** field.
* **File Format** (`file_format`): Select the format of each uploaded file from the options provided (e.g., PDF, Excel, CSV, Image, etc.).
* **File Description** (`file_description`): Describe each file, explaining what it contains.
* **Total File Size** (`file_size`): Enter the approximate size of each file in MB.
* **Additional Files** (`additional_files`): If necessary, upload any supplementary files and provide a brief description for each.
* **Metadata** (`metadata`): Enter any additional information about the files.
* **Analysis Details** (`analysis_details`): Describe the analysis methods used to generate the results.
* **Analysis Method** (`analysis_method`): Enter the method used to analyze the data.
* **Software Used** (`software_used`): If applicable, select the software used for analysis.
* **Statistical Methods** (`statistical_methods`): Enter any statistical tests applied during analysis.
* **Observations** (`observations`): Make any notable observations or comments about the results.
* **Anomalies or Issues** (`anomalies`): Enter any unexpected results or issues encountered during analysis.
* **Preliminary Conclusions** (`conclusions`): Enter any initial conclusions based on the results.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Recommend any follow-up actions based on the results.
* **Review Information** (`review_information`): This is a note field for reviewers, where they can enter their review information.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`): Select the current review status, such as Pending Review, Under Review, Approved, etc.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Enter the date of approval.
* **Submission Timestamp** (`submission_timestamp`): Enter the date and time when the results were submitted.
* **Researcher Confirmation** (`researcher_signature`): Confirm that the results are accurate and that you have validated your submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have reviewed and validated your results before uploading them.
* Use the correct file formats and descriptions to facilitate efficient review and analysis.
* Be accurate and clear in your submissions to avoid confusion and miscommunication.
