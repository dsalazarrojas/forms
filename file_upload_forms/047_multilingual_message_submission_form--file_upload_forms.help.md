# Multilingual Message Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multilingual Message Submission Form is designed to gather messages from users in different languages. This form allows you to submit a message, choose the language, and attach files, if needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your message in the **Form Title** field. This is not a required field, but it helps to identify your message.
2. Type your message in the **Form Message** field. This field is required.
3. Select the language of your message from the **Form Language** field. You can choose from English, Spanish, French, Arabic, or Chinese.
4. Enter a subject for your message in the **Form Subject** field. This field is optional.
5. If needed, attach files to your message in the **Form Attachments** field. This field is optional.
6. Provide any additional information in the **Form Additional Info** field. This field is optional.
7. Finally, click **Form Submit** to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (form_title, text, optional): Enter a title for your message to help identify it.
  - This field is not required but can be useful for organizational purposes.
- **Form Message** (form_message, text, required): Enter the content of your message.
  - This field is required to submit the form.
- **Form Language** (form_language, select_one, optional): Select the language of your message.
  - Choose from English, Spanish, French, Arabic, or Chinese.
- **Form Subject** (form_subject, text, optional): Enter a subject for your message.
  - This field is not required but can be useful for priority or categorization purposes.
- **Form Attachments** (form_files, text, optional): Attach files to your message.
  - This field is not required but can be useful for supporting documentation.
- **Form Additional Info** (form_additional_info, text, optional): Provide any additional information.
  - This field is not required but can be useful for further clarification.
- **Form Submit** (form_submit, note, required): Click to send your message.
  - This field is required to submit the form.
