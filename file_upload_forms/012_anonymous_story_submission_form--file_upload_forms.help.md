# Anonymous Story Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for anonymous story submission, where users can share their experiences, stories, or narratives without revealing their identities. The form collects various aspects of the story, including title, content, theme, intended audience, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **Title of Your Story** field with a brief name for your narrative.
2. In the **Narrative** field, type or paste your story, experience, or narrative.
3. Select the **Primary Theme** of your story from the options provided (Personal Growth, Workplace Experience, Community Event, Travel, Health Journey, or Other).
4. If you want to restrict who reads your story, select the **Intended Audience** from the options (public, specific groups, or no selection).
5. If your story contains sensitive or triggering content, mark **Does this story contain sensitive or triggering content?** as **True**.
6. Enter the **Date of Experience (Approximate)**, if applicable.
7. Choose the **Submission Category** for your story from the options (Fiction, Non-Fiction, Poetry, Essay, or other).
8. Decide whether you **Consent to this being published anonymously?** (I consent to anonymous publication or Do not publish, for review only).
9. Finally, optionally provide your **Why are you sharing this story today?** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title of Your Story** (required): Give your story a brief name.
* **Narrative** (required): Share your story, experience, or narrative here.
* **Primary Theme** (required): Select the primary theme of your story from the options (Personal Growth, Workplace Experience, Community Event, Travel, Health Journey, or Other).
* **Intended Audience** (optional): If you want to restrict who reads your story, select the intended audience from the options.
* **Does this story contain sensitive or triggering content?** (required): Mark as True if your story contains sensitive or triggering content.
* **Date of Experience (Approximate)** (optional): Enter the date of your experience, if applicable.
* **Submission Category** (required): Choose the category for your story (Fiction, Non-Fiction, Poetry, Essay, or other).
* **Consent to this being published anonymously?** (required): Decide whether you consent to anonymous publication or not.
* **Why are you sharing this story today?** (optional): Provide an optional explanation for sharing your story.
