# Stage Construction Blueprint Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Construction Blueprint Application Form is designed to collect relevant information from users who want to apply for stage construction blueprints. This form helps the organization gather required contact details, project information, and other necessary documents, making it easier for them to review and process the applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address. Please ensure to enter accurate and up-to-date details so that the organization can reach out to you for further discussion.

### Project Info
* **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, optional): Here, you should provide a brief description of your project, including the project's name, location, and a brief overview. This will help the organization understand the scope and requirements of your project.

### Blueprint Files
* **Blueprint Files** (`blueprint_files`, text, optional): If you have any relevant files or documents related to your project, you can upload them here. This could include images, videos, or other relevant files that support your application.

### Review Stage Construction
* **Review Stage Construction** (`review_stage_construction`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional information or comments about your project's stage construction requirements. Please be concise and clear in your explanation.

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing any other relevant information that you feel is necessary for the organization to review your application. Please use this space to elaborate on your project's specifics or provide any supporting details.

## Tips
- Ensure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact the organization.
- The organization will review your application and contact you with further instructions or questions.
