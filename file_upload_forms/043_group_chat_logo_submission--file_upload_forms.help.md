# Group Chat Logo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to submit their group chat logo for collection and verification purposes. The user's group chat logo will be processed and reviewed for accuracy. Please follow the provided instructions to ensure a smooth and successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click the 'Upload' option to initiate the file upload process.
2. Select all relevant files by clicking on the 'Select all files to upload' option, located on page 2.
3. Enter your contact details in the 'Contact Details' section (page 2).
4. Ensure that your email address is correctly formatted in the 'Email' field (page 3).
5. Optionally, provide your phone number in the 'Phone' field (page 3).
6. Optionally, add any additional notes about your submission in the 'Note' field (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload (Page 1)** (`upload`, `text`, required: false): This is the first page where you will upload your group chat logo file. You can either drag and drop your file or click on the 'Select files to upload' option. Make sure to select all necessary files.
* **Contact Details (Page 2)** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing your contact information. Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date contact details to facilitate smooth communication.
* **Select all files to upload (Page 2)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): On this page, select all files you want to upload by checking the respective checkboxes.
* **Email (Page 3)** (`email`, required: false): Enter your correctly formatted email address. This is necessary for us to contact you for further information and clarification.
* **Phone (Page 3)** (`phone`, required: false): If you want to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Note (Page 4)** (`note`, required: false): This section allows you to add any additional comments or explanations for your submission. If you have any further details about your group chat logo, please use this field to provide them.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all necessary files for accurate and complete submission.
* Check your email address for correct formatting before submitting.
* Ensure your phone number is correctly formatted.
* Provide accurate contact details for efficient communication.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
