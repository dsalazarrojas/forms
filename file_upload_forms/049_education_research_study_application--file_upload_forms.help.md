# Education Research Study Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for an education research study. Please fill out this form to provide details about your research study, and we will review and consider your application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Review each field to ensure it is accurate and complete.
3. If any field is not applicable or you are unsure what to fill out, contact your supervisor or the research department for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study Title** (`study_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief and clear title of your research study.
* **Study Description** (`study_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your research study, including its purpose and objectives.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if your research falls within one of the listed areas, or 'No' if it does not. This helps us understand the scope of your study.
* **Other** (`research_area_other`, text, required/optional): If you answered 'No' to the previous question, please provide a brief explanation of the research area not listed.
* **Study Objectives** (`study_objectives`, text, required/optional): List the objectives of your research study.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, time, required/optional): Enter the duration of your study in hours, minutes, or days.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your study will start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your study will end.
* **Study Lead Person** (`study_lead_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person leading your study.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your study.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, text, required/optional): Enter the source of funding for your study.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurately and completely filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or the research department for guidance.
