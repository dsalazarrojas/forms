# School Announcement Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for school administrators to submit new announcements to be published on the school's communication channels. The form collects relevant information to ensure accurate and timely dissemination of important school updates, events, and news.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and descriptive information.
2. Select the correct category and schedule for the announcement.
3. Upload any relevant files or attachments.
4. Specify the recipients and publication settings for the announcement.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes if necessary.
6. Review and submit the form to send it to the school's communication team for review and publication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (required)**: Enter a clear and concise title that describes the announcement, such as "Parent-Teacher Conference Rescheduled".
* **Category (required)**: Choose the category that best fits the announcement, e.g., Academic, Administrative, Events, or News.
* **Description (required)**: Write a detailed description of the announcement, including relevant details and context.
* **File (required)**: Upload any supporting files or documents related to the announcement.
* **Date and Time (required)**: Select the date and time for the announcement's publication.
* **Schedule (required)**: Choose when you would like the announcement to be published, e.g., Before School, Lunchtime, or After School.
* **Recipients (required)**: Determine who will receive the announcement, e.g., Staff, Parents, Students, or Teachers.
* **Publication (required)**: Decide whether the announcement is public, private, or restricted to School Staff only.
* **Comments (optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the announcement if you need to provide more context.
* **Email and Phone (optional)**: Provide your contact information if you would like to be reached for further questions or clarification.
* **Uploads (required)**: Upload any additional files or attachments relevant to the announcement.
* **Publications (required)**: Determine how the announcement will be published, e.g., on the school's website, social media, or internal channels.
