This form is designed to record the transfer of wildlife eggs from one location to another. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the egg transfer process, including the conservation location, egg weight, transfer date, and other relevant details. Before starting the form, please note that you should only submit information for a single egg transfer record at a time.

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct transfer event.
2. Enter the **Conservation Location**, which is the location where the egg was collected or transferred from.
3. Enter the **Egg Weight** of the egg, which is its weight in units of your choice (e.g., grams, kilograms, or pounds).
4. Enter the **Transfer Date** of the egg, which is the date of transfer in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Choose the **Egg Condition** of the egg from the options: Healthy, Damaged, or Unknown.
6. Select the **Assigned User** from the options provided, or choose "Other" and enter the name of the assigned user.
7. Optionally, provide additional information in **Notes**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields.
8. Fill out **Conservation Location 2**, **Egg Weight 2**, and other relevant details for a second egg transfer record, if applicable.
9. Ensure all required fields are filled in for each egg transfer record before submitting.

## wildlife_egg_transfer_record_form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps above and ensure you fill out each field accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conservation Location**: Enter the location where the egg was collected or transferred from.
* **Egg Weight**: Enter the weight of the egg in units of your choice (e.g., grams, kilograms, or pounds).
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date of transfer in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Egg Condition**: Choose the condition of the egg from the options: Healthy, Damaged, or Unknown.
* **Transfer Time**: Enter the time of transfer, if known.
* **Assigned User**: Select the user assigned to this transfer record from the options provided, or choose "Other" and enter the name of the assigned user.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about the transfer record, if applicable.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Conservation Location 2**: Enter the location where the second egg was collected or transferred from, if applicable.
* **Egg Weight 2**: Enter the weight of the second egg in units of your choice, if applicable.
* **Transfer Date 2**: Enter the date of transfer for the second egg in the format MM/DD/YYYY, if applicable.
* **Egg Condition 2**: Choose the condition of the second egg from the options: Healthy, Damaged, or Unknown, if applicable.
* **Notes 2**: Provide any additional information about the second transfer record, if applicable.
* **Email 2**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the second egg, if applicable.
* **Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for the second egg, if applicable.
* **Transfer Time 2**: Enter the time of transfer for the second egg, if applicable.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely, and submit only one egg transfer record at a time.
