# Qualified Report Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from submitters for a qualified report. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your report is reviewed and accepted correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "upload_reports" field.
2. Enter the name of the file(s) you are uploading.
3. Enter your "priority_ratings" value on a scale of 1-5.
4. Enter the date you are submitting the report in the "report_date" field.
5. If required, enter the time you are submitting the report in the "report_time" field.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "report_priority" field.
7. Enter your comments in the "comments" field.
8. Fill out your name and email address in the "submitter_name" and "submitter_email" fields, respectively.
9. You can optionally fill out your phone number in the "submitter_phone" field.
10. You can optionally fill out comments for yourself in the "submitter_comment" field.
11. Enter the name of your department in the "submitter_department" field.
12. Enter your job title in the "submitter_title" field.
13. Enter your role in the "submitter_role" field.
14. Enter the location of your team in the "submitter_location" field.
15. Enter the date you are submitting as in "submitter_date" field.
16. If required, enter the time you are submitting as in "submitter_time" field.
17. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "submitter_priority" field.
18. Enter comments for your team in the "submitter_comments" field.
19. You can upload files for your report in "submitter_upload_file", "submitter_upload_file_2", "submitter_upload_file_3", and "submitter_upload_file_4" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **upload_reports** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you are uploading reports, otherwise select "No".
* **supporting_files** (text, required): Enter the name of the file(s) you are uploading.
* **priority_ratings** (number, required): Enter your priority ratings value on a scale of 1-5.
* **report_date** (date, required): Enter the date you are submitting the report.
* **report_time** (time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting the report.
* **report_priority** (select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" if you are submitting the report with high priority.
* **comments** (note, optional): Enter comments for your report.
* **submitter_name** (text, required): Fill out your name.
* **submitter_email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **submitter_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **submitter_comment** (note, optional): Enter comments for yourself.
* **submitter_department** (text, optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **submitter_title** (text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **submitter_role** (text, optional): Enter your role.
* **submitter_location** (text, optional): Enter the location of your team.
* **submitter_date** (date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting.
* **submitter_time** (time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting.
* **submitter_priority** (select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" if you are submitting with high priority.
* **submitter_comments** (note, optional): Enter comments for your team.
* **submitter_upload_file** (text, optional), **submitter_upload_file_2** (text, optional), **submitter_upload_file_3** (text, optional), **submitter_upload_file_4** (text, optional): Upload the files for your report.
