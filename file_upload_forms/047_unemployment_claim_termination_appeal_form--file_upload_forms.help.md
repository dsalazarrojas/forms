# Unemployment Claim Termination Appeal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit an appeal for the termination of unemployment benefits. If you are a claimant who has been terminated from benefits and wish to appeal this decision, please complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter your name and address to ensure that your appeal is properly addressed.
2. Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for additional information.
3. Select the reason for the termination of your benefits from the provided options.
4. Enter the date of your termination.
5. Provide a brief explanation for the termination.
6. Sign the form to confirm your identity.
7. If applicable, provide your organization's name and address.
8. Enter your organization's phone number.
9. Describe the termination request.
10. Attach any supporting documents you may have.
11. Attach any additional files relevant to your appeal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, required): Enter your address as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for additional information.
- **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, select_one, required): Select the reason for the termination of your benefits from the provided options.
- **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your termination.
- **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for the termination.
- **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm your identity.
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name if you are an employee.
- **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, required): Enter your organization's address if you are an employee.
- **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, text, required): Enter your organization's phone number if you are an employee.
- **Termination Request** (`termination_request`, text, required): Describe the termination request.
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required): Attach any supporting documents you may have.
- **File 1** (`file1`, text, required): Attach any additional files relevant to your appeal.
