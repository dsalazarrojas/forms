# Architectural Symposium Proposal Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for presenters to submit a proposal for the Architectural Symposium.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has three sections: **Presenter Information**, **Proposal Details**, and **Presentation Format** and **Supporting Materials**. Follow the instructions below to complete each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Presenter Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the symposium program.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you with further instructions or questions.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you with further instructions or questions.
* **Organization or Institution**: Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Title or Position**: Enter your title or position.
* **Brief Bio or Background**: Enter a brief professional background summary.
* **Proposal Title**: Enter the title of your presentation.
* **Proposal Category**: Select one of the following categories: Design innovation, Sustainability, Technology, Urban planning, Historic preservation, Education, Professional practice, or Other.
* **Proposal Description**: Enter a 100-200 word summary of your proposal.
* **Learning Outcomes for Attendees**: What will participants learn from your presentation?
* **Preferred Format**: Select one of the following formats: 45-minute talk, 20-minute talk, Panel discussion, Workshop, Demonstration, or Other.
* **Presentation Length**: Enter the length of your presentation.
* **Technical Requirements**: Enter any technical requirements for your presentation (e.g., projector, microphone).
* **Presentation Slides (if available)**: Upload your presentation slides as a PDF or PowerPoint file.
* **Project Images or Supporting Media**: Upload any project images or supporting media (e.g., videos).
* **Additional Documents**: Upload any additional documents relevant to your proposal (e.g., papers, case studies).
* **Submission Date**: Select the date you would like to submit your proposal by.

Note: All fields marked as **required** must be completed in order to submit the form.
