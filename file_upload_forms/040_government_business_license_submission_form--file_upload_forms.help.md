# Government Business License Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Business License Submission Form is designed to collect necessary information from business owners or their representatives to facilitate the submission of a business license application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" to provide accurate and complete information about your business.
2. Review the "Business Type" field to select the correct type of business.
3. Enter the "Business Location" and "Address" fields to provide details about your business location.
4. Select the required "License Type" to specify the type of license you need.
5. Upload any required documents in the "Document Upload" field.
6. Review and review the information before clicking the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Business License Application (1)**: Enter a brief title for your business license application.
* **Business Type (2)**: Choose the type of business you are applying for. This includes Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, or Limited Liability Company (LLC).
* **Business Location (3)**: Enter the location of your business, such as a city or town.
* **Address (4)**: Enter the physical address of your business, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Information (5)**: Enter contact details for your business, such as your name and email address.
* **License Type (6)**: Select the type of license you require, such as General Business License or Specialized Business License.
* **Document Upload (7)**: Upload any required documents for your license application.
* **Submit (8)**: Review and submit your completed application.
