# Recruitment Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Submission Form is a form designed for candidates to submit their job applications to the company. It is meant to collect all necessary information for the recruitment team to review and process the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Upload your resume and other required documents.
2. Fill in your job title, work experience, job type, job status, job category, and job tags.
3. Write a brief job description.
4. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
5. Optionally, upload a cover letter and additional documents.
6. Review and submit the form.
