# Trading Card Stats Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trading Card Stats Form is a tool for collecting data about a trading card. This form helps users to provide detailed information about a card, which can be used for inventory management, tracking, and other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of card from the "Card Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the name of the set the card belongs to.
3. Enter the card number (a unique identifier for the card).
4. Choose whether the card is rare or not.
5. Specify the condition of the card (e.g., good, fair, poor).
6. Enter the grade of the card (e.g., 1-10).
7. Enter the current price of the card.
8. Select the date the card was purchased.
9. Enter the price at which the card was purchased.
10. Enter the price at which the card was sold.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Card Type**: Select the type of card from the dropdown menu. This will help determine how the card is categorized and tracked.
* **Set Name**: Enter the name of the set the card belongs to. This is a crucial piece of information for identifying and tracking cards.
* **Card Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the card. This is a key piece of information for verifying the card's existence and tracking its history.
* **Rarity**: Choose whether the card is rare or not. This will affect how the card is valued and tracked.
* **Condition**: Specify the condition of the card (e.g., good, fair, poor). This will help determine the card's value and condition.
* **Grade**: Enter the grade of the card (e.g., 1-10). This is a measure of the card's quality or rarity.
* **Price**: Enter the current price of the card. This will help track the card's value over time.
* **Purchase Date**: Select the date the card was purchased. This will help track the card's history and ownership.
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the price at which the card was purchased. This will help track the card's financial history.
* **Sale Price**: Enter the price at which the card was sold. This will help track the card's financial history and sales history.
