# Storage Tank Permit Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Storage Tank Permit Application form is used to gather information from applicants who are seeking to obtain a Storage Tank Permit. This form is part of the initial application process and is required to be completed accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate response to each question carefully, as it will be reviewed by our team.
2. Enter your facility information in the "Facility Information" field, ensuring that it accurately represents your storage tank facility.
3. Provide detailed information about the tank you are applying for in the "Tank Details" field.
4. Include your contact information in the "Contact Details" field.
5. Review and select "Submit Form" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Information** (`facility_info`): Enter a brief description of your storage tank facility, including its location, size, and any other relevant details.
* **Tank Details** (`tank_details`): Provide detailed information about the tank you are applying for, including its size, type, and any other relevant specifications.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`): This field is not currently in use, however, please be prepared to upload any required documents as needed.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`): Select "Yes" to submit your application, or "No" to indicate that you need to review or modify your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in the application process.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Please note that incomplete or inaccurate applications will not be accepted.
