# APC Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request for a waiver of Article Processing Charge (APC) fees for authors who are unable to pay the fees associated with publishing their work in the journal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **Author Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name.
2. **Author Email**: Provide your professional email address for communication about this request.
3. **Author Institution**: Enter the name of your affiliated institution.
4. **Manuscript Title**: Enter the title of the manuscript for which you are requesting a waiver.
5. **Journal Name**: Enter the name of the journal to which you are submitting your manuscript.
6. **Manuscript ID**: If available, provide the manuscript ID assigned by the journal.
7. **Reason for Waiver Request**: Select the primary reason for your waiver request from the list of options.
8. **Waiver Reason Details**: If applicable, provide additional details about your reason for waiver request.
9. **Institutional Funding Status**: Select your institution's funding status.
10. **Country Economic Status**: Select the economic status of your country according to World Bank classification.
11. **Career Stage**: Select your current career stage.
12. **Previous Publication Record**: If applicable, enter the number of articles you have previously published in this journal or similar journals.
13. **Alternative Funding Sources**: Select whether you have explored other sources of funding for the APC.
14. **Waiver Amount Requested**: Select the percentage of the APC fee you are requesting to be waived.
15. **Supporting Documentation**: If applicable, upload any documents that support your waiver request (financial statements, institutional letters, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Author Full Name** (`author_full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your complete legal name to allow the editorial team to contact you about your waiver request.
* **Author Email** (`author_email`, email, **required**): Provide your professional email address for communication about this request.
* **Author Institution** (`author_institution`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your affiliated institution to help the editorial team verify your institutional funding status.
* **Manuscript Title** (`manuscript_title`, text, **required**): Enter the title of the manuscript for which you are requesting a waiver.
* **Journal Name** (`journal_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the journal to which you are submitting your manuscript.
* **Manuscript ID** (`manuscript_id`, text, **not required**): If available, provide the manuscript ID assigned by the journal.
* **Reason for Waiver Request** (`reason_for_waiver_request`, select_one, **required**): Select the primary reason for your waiver request from the list of options.
* **Waiver Reason Details** (`waiver_reason_details`, text, **not required**): If applicable, provide additional details about your reason for waiver request.
* **Institutional Funding Status** (`institutional_funding_status`, select_one, **required**): Select your institution's funding status.
* **Country Economic Status** (`country_economic_status`, select_one, **required**): Select the economic status of your country according to World Bank classification.
* **Career Stage** (`career_stage`, select_one, **required**): Select your current career stage.
* **Previous Publication Record** (`previous_publication_record`, number, **not required**): If applicable, enter the number of articles you have previously published in this journal or similar journals.
* **Alternative Funding Sources** (`alternative_funding_sources`, select_one, **required**): Select whether you have explored other sources of funding for the APC.
* **Waiver Amount Requested** (`waiver_amount_requested`, select_one, **required**): Select the percentage of the APC fee you are requesting to be waived.
* **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`, text, **not required**): If applicable, upload any documents that support your waiver request (financial statements, institutional letters, etc.).
