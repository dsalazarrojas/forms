# Property Sale Outcome Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the outcome of a property sale, including the status of the property and any relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please go through each page and fill in the necessary information according to the instructions below.
2. Please fill in the property status (e.g., sold, not sold, etc.) on page 2.
3. Provide a brief reason for non-sale (if applicable) on page 3.
4. Enter the property address on page 4.
5. Enter the price of the property on page 5.
6. Enter the name of the salesperson involved in the sale on page 6.
7. Enter the name of the agency involved in the sale on page 7.
8. Enter the date and time of the sale on page 8.
9. If necessary, enter the reason for non-sale on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Status** (`Property Status`, select, required): Choose the current status of the property from the available options.
* **Reason for Non-Sale** (`Reason for Non-Sale`, text, optional): If the property was not sold, please provide the reason for non-sale.
* **Property Address** (`Property Address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Price** (`Property Price`, number, required): Enter the price of the property.
* **Salesperson** (`Salesperson`, text, required): Enter the name of the salesperson involved in the sale.
* **Agency** (`Agency`, text, required): Enter the name of the agency involved in the sale.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the sale.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the sale.
* **Reason for Non-Sale (if Applicable)** (`Reason for Non-Sale`, text, optional): If the property was not sold, enter the reason for non-sale.
