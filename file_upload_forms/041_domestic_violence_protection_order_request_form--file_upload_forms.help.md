# Domestic Violence Protection Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for a domestic violence protection order. A domestic violence protection order is a legal document that helps keep individuals safe from abuse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out Domestic Violence Protection Order Request Form 1, providing a brief description of your situation.
2. Enter your **Name**, which is a required field.
3. Select your **Relationship** with the person you are seeking protection from. This will help us better understand your situation.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth**, which is a required field.
5. Provide your **Address**, which is a required field.
6. Enter a detailed **Description** of your situation, which is a required field.
7. Enter your **Phone Number** (optional, but recommended for contact purposes).
8. Enter your **Email Address**, which is a required field.
9. Attach any relevant **File** (optional, but recommended for supporting evidence).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Domestic Violence Protection Order Request Form 1**: A brief description of your situation.
* **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Relationship**: This is the relationship between you and the person you are seeking protection from. Select 'Yes' if you are currently in a relationship with this person, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Your current address, where you are in need of protection.
* **Description**: A detailed description of your situation, including the abuse you have experienced and any relevant circumstances.
* **Phone**: Your phone number, which can be used for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **File**: Any relevant documentation or evidence that supports your request.
