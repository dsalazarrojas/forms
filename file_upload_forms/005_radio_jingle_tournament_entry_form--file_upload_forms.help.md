# radio_jingle_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The radio jingle tournament entry form is designed to collect information from jingle writers and event organizers for a radio broadcasting or advertising industry event. It gathers details such as jingle title, description, length, and format, as well as event information like date, time, location, and organizer name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the "Jingle Entry" field with the title of your jingle.
2. Enter the "Name of Jingle Writer" field with the name of the person who created the jingle.
3. Provide the "Email Address of Jingle Writer" field with the email address of the jingle writer.
4. Upload a "Jingle File" by clicking on the "Upload Jingle File" button.
5. Select the "Jingle Length" field from the options provided.
6. Enter the "Radio Station Name" field with the name of the radio station where the jingle will be played.
7. Enter the "Event Organizer Name" field with the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
8. Choose the "Event Date" field from the calendar provided.
9. Enter the "Event Time" field with the start time of the event.
10. Enter the "Event Location" field with the address of the event venue.
11. Enter the "Event Address" field with the street address of the event venue.
12. Enter the "Event City" field with the city of the event location.
13. Enter the "Event State" field with the state of the event location.
14. Enter the "Event Zip" field with the zip code of the event location.
15. You may add any "Event Notes" you want to share with the event organizers.
16. Upload a photo of the "Jingle Singer" by clicking on the "Upload Jingle Singer Image" button.
17. Upload an audio file of the "Jingle Audio" by clicking on the "Upload Jingle Audio File" button.
18. Confirm that you are over 18 years old by selecting "Yes, I am over 18" in the "Submit" field.
19. Confirm that you have submitted the form correctly by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Submit" field.
20. Finally, upload a video of the "Jingle Video" by clicking on the "Upload Jingle Video" button.
21. Upload an image of the event "Upload Event Image" by clicking on the "Upload Event Image" button.
22. Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Jingle Entry** (`Jingle Entry`, text, required): Enter the title of your jingle.
* **Name of Jingle Writer** (`Name of Jingle Writer`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who created the jingle.
* **Email Address of Jingle Writer** (`Email Address of Jingle Writer`, email, required): Enter the email address of the jingle writer.
* **Upload Jingle File** (`Upload Jingle File`, text, required): Upload a jingle audio or video file.
* **Jingle Length** (`Jingle Length`, select one, required): Select the length of your jingle from the options provided.
* **Radio Station Name** (`Radio Station Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the radio station where the jingle will be played.
* **Event Organizer Name** (`Event Organizer Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required): Choose the date of the event from the calendar provided.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, text, required): Enter the address of the event venue.
* **Event Address** (`Event Address`, text, required): Enter the street address of the event venue.
* **Event City** (`Event City`, text, required): Enter the city of the event location.
* **Event State** (`Event State`, text, required): Enter the state of the event location.
* **Event Zip** (`Event Zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the event location.
* **Event Notes** (`Event Notes`, note, optional): Add any event notes you want to share with the event organizers.
* **Upload Jingle Singer Image** (`Upload Jingle Singer Image`, text, required): Upload a photo of the jingle singer.
* **Upload Jingle Audio File** (`Upload Jingle Audio File`, text, required): Upload an audio file of the jingle.
* **Upload Jingle Video** (`Upload Jingle Video`, text, required): Upload a video of the jingle.
* **Upload Event Image** (`Upload Event Image`, text, required): Upload an image of the event.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select multiple, required): Confirm that you are over 18 years old and that you have submitted the form correctly.
* **Confirm Submit** (`Confirm Submit`, select one, optional): Confirm that you have submitted the form correctly.
* **Upload Event Image** (`Upload Event Image`, text, required): Upload an image of the event.
* **Confirm Submit Hint** (`Confirm Submit Hint`, note, optional): This field is for any hints to confirm submission.
