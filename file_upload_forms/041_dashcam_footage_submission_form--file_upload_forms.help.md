# Dashcam Footage Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dashcam Footage Submission Form is designed to collect dashcam footage and relevant details associated with an incident. This form helps our team analyze and review the footage to provide a comprehensive understanding of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Dashcam Video** field with a brief description of the incident or a link to the video file.
2. Provide **Incident Details** in the **Incident Details** field, including a brief description of what happened.
3. Enter the date of the incident in the **Date of Incident** field using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the vehicle information, including **Vehicle Information**, **Vehicle Make**, **Vehicle Model**, and **Vehicle Year**.
5. Enter the driver and passenger information, including **Driver License**, **Vehicle License**, and **Vehicle VIN**.
6. Enter your **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address** for contact purposes.
7. Enter your insurance information, including **Insurance Company**, **Policy Number**.
8. Enter the location of the incident in the **Location** field.
9. Sign and submit the form by entering your name in the **Form Signature** field.
10. Finally, click **Submit** to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dashcam Video**: A description of the dashcam footage or a link to the video file.
* **Incident Details**: A brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Date of Incident**: The date of the incident in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Vehicle Information**: Details about the vehicle involved in the incident, including the make, model, and year.
* **Driver Information**: Details about the driver of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Passenger Information**: Details about the passengers of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Witness Information**: Details about any witnesses involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Damage**: A description of any damage to the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Police Report**: A link or attachment to a police report related to the incident.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information related to the incident.
* **Form Signature**: A signature confirming that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
* **Email**: Contact email address of the submitter.
* **Phone**: Contact phone number of the submitter.
* **Address**: Residential or work address of the submitter.
* **Location**: Location where the incident occurred.
* **Insurance Company**: Name of the insurance company providing coverage for the vehicle.
* **Policy Number**: Policy number for the vehicle's insurance policy.
* **Driver License**: Driver's license number or ID.
* **Vehicle License**: Vehicle registration number or license plate number.
* **Vehicle VIN**: Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **Vehicle Year**: Year of manufacture for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Make of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Model**: Model of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Insurance Company**: Name of the insurance company providing coverage for the vehicle.
* **Policy Number**: Policy number for the vehicle's insurance policy.
* **Vehicle VIN**: Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **Vehicle Year**: Year of manufacture for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Make of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Model**: Model of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Submit**: Final step to submit the form.

Please note that all fields are required unless otherwise specified.
