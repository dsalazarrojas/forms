# 3D Photo Download Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 3D Photo Download Request form is designed to collect necessary information for downloading 3D photos. Fill out this form to ensure that your 3D asset is delivered correctly and with the correct permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details:
   * Enter your full name in the **Requester full name** field.
   * Provide your organization or studio name if applicable in the **Organization / Studio (if applicable)** field.
2. Choose your preferred contact details:
   * Enter your email address in the **Requester email address** field.
   * Include your country code in the **Requester phone number** field if outside local area.
3. Add your project details:
   * Enter your project or usage title in the **Project or usage title** field.
   * Provide the 3D asset identifier or filename in the **Asset identifier or filename** field.
4. Specify usage and delivery preferences:
   * Select the **Intended usage** of the 3D photo from the list.
   * Choose all applicable **Distribution channels** where the asset will be published or shared.
   * Choose your preferred **Preferred delivery format** for the 3D asset.
5. Choose a license and agree to terms:
   * Select a standard license from the **License requested** dropdown list.
   * Select **Yes** in the **I agree to the licensing terms and credit requirements** field to proceed with processing.
6. Add any additional notes or instructions:
   * Enter any additional information or instructions in the **Additional notes or instructions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester full name**: Enter the person who will receive the download, typically you or your organization.
- **Organization / Studio (if applicable)**: Provide the name of your organization or studio, if applicable.
- **Requester email address**: Enter your email address for delivery and license notices.
- **Requester phone number**: Include your country code for international calls.
- **Project or usage title**: Enter a short project name for tracking purposes.
- **Asset identifier or filename**: Provide the 3D asset ID, filename, or link.
- **Intended usage**: Select the primary intended use of the 3D photo.
- **Distribution channels**: Choose all applicable places where the asset will be published or shared.
- **Preferred delivery format**: Select your preferred 3D asset format for download.
- **Resolution / texture requirements**: Enter any specific resolution or texture requirements.
- **License requested**: Select a standard or extended license for the 3D asset.
- **I agree to the licensing terms and credit requirements**: Confirm that you agree to the license terms and credit requirements.
- **Additional notes or instructions**: Include any additional information or instructions for delivery or special handling.
