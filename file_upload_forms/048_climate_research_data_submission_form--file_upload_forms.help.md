# Climate Research Data Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect climate research data submissions from researchers. It gathers information about the researcher's identity, research study, methodology, data types, data format, and publication status. This form is likely used to track and manage climate research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and email address as the primary researcher.
2. Enter your institution or organization name.
3. Fill in the study or research project title and provide a brief summary of your research.
4. Choose the primary research focus of your study.
5. Select the geographic scope of your research.
6. Specify the time period covered by your research.
7. Choose the research methodology used.
8. Indicate the types of data included in your submission.
9. Select the primary data format.
10. Estimate the approximate dataset size.
11. Describe any quality assurance measures applied to your data.
12. Upload the primary data file.
13. Upload secondary data or supporting files (optional).
14. Upload data documentation or a readme file.
15. (Optional) Upload a detailed methodology document.
16. Select the publication status of your research.
17. Choose a preferred data license.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Researcher Name**: Enter your full name as the primary researcher.
* **Email Address**: Enter your institutional email address.
* **Institution**: Enter your institution or organization name.
* **Study or Research Project Title**: Enter a clear and concise title for your research study.
* **Study Description**: Provide a brief summary of your research (about 150-200 words).
* **Primary Research Focus**: Select one of the provided options that best describes the focus of your research.
* **Geographic Scope**: Specify the geographic area covered by your research (e.g., global, regional, local).
* **Time Period Covered**: Enter the time period covered by your research (e.g., years, date range).
* **Research Methodology**: Select the methodology used in your research (e.g., observational, modeling, experimental).
* **Types of Data Included**: Select all the types of data included in your submission (e.g., climate measurements, model output, satellite data).
* **Primary Data Format**: Enter the format of your primary data file (e.g., CSV, NetCDF, HDF5).
* **Approximate Dataset Size**: Estimate the size of your dataset in megabytes or gigabytes.
* **Quality Assurance Measures**: Describe any quality assurance measures applied to your data.
* **Upload Primary Data File**: Upload your main dataset file.
* **Upload Secondary Data or Supporting Files**: Upload additional datasets or supporting files (optional).
* **Upload Data Documentation or Readme**: Upload a document with metadata and variable descriptions.
* **Upload Detailed Methodology Document**: Upload a detailed description of your research methodology (optional).
* **Publication Status**: Select the publication status of your research (e.g., published, in review, not intended for publication).
* **Preferred Data License**: Choose a preferred data license for your research.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete and accurate.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions and answers.
* Use the provided options for the research methodology and data types to ensure consistency.
* Ensure that your data files are in a format that is easily accessible and readable.
