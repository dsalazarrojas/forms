# speaker_presentation_upload_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from speakers who want to upload a presentation to be presented at an event or conference. It includes fields to provide details about the presentation, speaker, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your presentation in the **Form Title** field.
2. Choose the category for your presentation from the **Category** field.
3. Provide a brief description of your presentation in the **Description** field.
4. Upload your presentation file.
5. Enter details about the session where your presentation will be presented, if applicable.
6. Provide your name and speaker profile picture.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Add your speaker biography.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title of your presentation.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category for your presentation (e.g. File Upload Forms, Content Forms).
* **Description** (`form_description`, note, optional): A brief description of your presentation.
* **Speaker File** (`speaker_file`, text, optional): Upload your presentation file.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, note, optional): Enter details about the session where your presentation will be presented.
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the speaker.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Speaker Profile Picture** (`speaker_profile_picture`, text, optional): Enter a URL for your speaker profile picture.
* **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`, text, optional): Enter a brief bio about yourself as the speaker.
