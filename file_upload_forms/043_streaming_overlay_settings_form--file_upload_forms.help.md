# streaming_overlay_settings_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "streaming_overlay_settings_form" is used to collect settings for streaming overlays, including branding, uploads, streamer preferences, team preferences, team uploads, team branding, and channel ID uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired settings for your streaming overlay.
2. Fill in the required fields.
3. Review your selections before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Branding** (`branding`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose whether to display branding on your streaming overlay.
* **Uploads** (`uploads`, `text`, required): Enter your upload settings for streaming.
* **Streamer Preferences** (`streamer_preferences`, `text`, required): Enter your streamer preferences for streaming.
* **Streamer Uploads** (`streamer_preferences_uploads`, `text`, required): Enter your streamer upload settings.
* **Team Preferences** (`team_preferences`, `text`, required): Enter your team preferences for streaming.
* **Team Uploads** (`team_preferences_uploads`, `text`, optional): Enter your team upload settings (optional).
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, required): Enter your team ID for streaming.
* **Channel ID** (`channel_id`, `text`, required): Enter your channel ID for streaming.
* **Streamer ID** (`streamer_id`, `text`, required): Enter your streamer ID for streaming.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the start date for your streaming.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Choose the end date for your streaming.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Choose the start time for your streaming.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Choose the end time for your streaming.
* **Stream Status** (`stream_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the stream status for your streaming (Active or Inactive).
* **Stream Status Uploads** (`stream_status_uploads`, `text`, required): Enter your stream status upload settings.
* **Streamer ID Uploads** (`streamer_id_uploads`, `text`, optional): Enter your streamer ID upload settings (optional).
* **Streamer Preferences Uploads** (`streamer_preferences_uploads`, `text`, required): Enter your streamer preferences upload settings.
* **Team ID Uploads** (`team_id_uploads`, `text`, optional): Enter your team ID upload settings (optional).
* **Team Preferences Uploads** (`team_preferences_uploads`, `text`, required): Enter your team preferences upload settings.
* **Streamers Preferences Uploads** (`streamer_preferences_text_uploads`, `text`, optional): Enter your streamer preferences upload settings (optional).
* **Branding Uploads** (`branding_uploads`, `text`, required): Enter your branding upload settings.
* **Team Branding** (`team_branding`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether to display team branding on your streaming overlay.
* **Streamers Branding** (`streamer_branding`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether to display streamers branding on your streaming overlay.
* **Channel ID Uploads** (`channel_id_uploads`, `text`, required): Enter your channel ID upload settings.
