# Mining Operations Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about mining operations, including site details, operational status, and supporting documents. It's crucial for us to have accurate and up-to-date information for efficient management and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by reviewing the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the mining operation.
3.  Use the correct operational type (e.g., Mining, Extraction, Processing, or Transportation) and status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
4.  Attach any necessary supporting documents for the operation.
5.  Enter the site's name, address, and contact person's details.
6.  Fill in the operational start and end dates, if applicable.
7.  Enter the site's latitude and longitude.
8.  Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Mining Operations Registration**:
    *   **Site Details**: Enter a brief description of the site, including its name and address.
*   **Site Name** (`site_name`): Enter the name of the site.
*   **Site Address** (`site_address`): Enter the physical address of the site.
*   **Site Contact** (`site_contact`): Enter the contact information for the site (e.g., person or organization).
*   **Operational Details** (`operational_details`): Enter a brief description of the operational activities at the site.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of the company operating the site.
*   **Operational Type** (`operational_type`): Select the correct operational type (e.g., Mining, Extraction, Processing, or Transportation).
*   **Operational Status** (`operational_status`): Select the current operational status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): Attach any necessary documents to support the operation.
*   **Site Map** (`site_map`): Enter the date of the site map (not currently implemented in the form).
*   **Site Opening Date** (`site_opening_date`): Enter the date the site started operating.
*   **Site Closing Date** (`site_closing_date`): Enter the date the site stopped operating.
*   **Operational Start Date** (`operational_start_date`): Enter the date the operation started.
*   **Operational End Date** (`operational_end_date`): Enter the date the operation ended.
*   **Latitude** (`latitude`): Enter the site's latitude coordinate.
*   **Longitude** (`longitude`): Enter the site's longitude coordinate.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the contact information for the site's manager or operator.
