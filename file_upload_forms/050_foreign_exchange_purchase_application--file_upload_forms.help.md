# Foreign Exchange Purchase Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foreign Exchange Purchase Application is a form used to collect information from customers for foreign exchange purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the **customer_name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **customer_email** field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **customer_phone** field.
4. Enter the amount you wish to purchase in the **amount_to_purchase** field.
5. Select the **purchase_currency** you are interested in purchasing from the dropdown menu.
6. Select the **selling_currency** you are currently holding from the dropdown menu.
7. The **exchange_rate** field is not required, but if you know the exchange rate, please enter it.
8. To upload additional supporting documentation, click on the **upload_file** field and select your file.
9. Finally, click the submit button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name to help us identify you.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Amount to Purchase** (`amount_to_purchase`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount you wish to purchase.
* **Purchase Currency** (`purchase_currency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the currency you wish to purchase.
	+ Options: USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, INR
* **Selling Currency** (`selling_currency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the currency you are currently holding.
	+ Options: USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, INR
* **Exchange Rate** (`exchange_rate`, `number`, required: false): If you know the exchange rate, please enter it.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, `text`, required: false): To upload additional supporting documentation, click on this field and select your file.
* **File** (`file`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently in use. It may be used for future features or development.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete all required fields to ensure that your application is thoroughly evaluated.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing accurate and complete information.
