# Funeral Home Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors to a funeral home, allowing staff to efficiently manage and provide services to those visiting the facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **Visitor Information** in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** including your contact details in the text field.
3. Indicate whether you would like to request **Visitor Access** to the funeral home by selecting "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
4. If you have a specific **Schedule Visit**, please select a date and time from the time picker.
5. You can **Attach Files** or documents relevant to the visit by typing in the text field provided.
6. If you have any additional **Message** regarding your visit, please type it in the text field.
7. Please enter your **Email** address for communication purposes.
8. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field.
9. After filling all the necessary fields, click the **Submit** button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Information**: This field is where you can provide any relevant information about yourself or the group you are visiting.
* **Contact Information**: Please enter your contact details, including your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Visitor Access Request**: This field determines whether you or your group would like to be granted access to the funeral home. Select "True" if you would like access, and "False" if not.
* **Schedule Visit**: This field allows you to select a specific date and time for your visit.
* **Attachments**: You can attach any relevant files or documents related to your visit here.
* **Message**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please type them in this field.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address for the funeral home staff to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for the staff to contact you.
* **Submit**: After completing all the fields, click this button to submit your request.
