# Writ Of Garnishment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Writ Of Garnishment Request Form is used to request a writ of garnishment for a specific amount from an individual or entity. This form requires information about the request details, file uploads, garnishment amount, garnishment start and end dates, garnishment amount type, payee, garnishment address, garnishment state, garnishment zip, garnishment city, garnishment county, garnishment phone, garnishment email, garnishment case number, garnishment case type, and garnishment other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Writ Of Garnishment Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Request Details" field with a brief description of the request.
2. Upload any necessary files in the "File Uploads" field.
3. Enter the garnishment amount in the "Garnishment Amount" field.
4. Select the garnishment start and end dates using the calendar in the "Garnishment Start Date" and "Garnishment End Date" fields.
5. Choose the garnishment amount type from the list in the "Garnishment Amount Type" field.
6. Enter the details about the garnishment amount in the "Garnishment Amount Details" field.
7. Fill out the "Payee" field with the name and address of the person or entity to be garnished.
8. Enter the garnishment address in the "Garnishment Address" field.
9. Enter the garnishment state in the "Garnishment State" field.
10. Enter the garnishment zip in the "Garnishment Zip" field.
11. Enter the garnishment city in the "Garnishment City" field.
12. Enter the garnishment county in the "Garnishment County" field.
13. Enter the garnishment phone in the "Garnishment Phone" field.
14. Enter the garnishment email in the "Garnishment Email" field.
15. Enter the garnishment case number in the "Garnishment Case Number" field.
16. Choose the garnishment case type from the list in the "Garnishment Case Type" field.
17. Enter any other details in the "Garnishment Other" field.
18. Enter any additional details in the "Garnishment Other Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details (request_details)**: Enter a brief description of the request.
* **File Uploads (file_uploads)**: Upload any necessary files to support the request.
* **Garnishment Amount (garnishment_amount)**: Enter the amount to be garnished.
* **Garnishment Start Date (garnishment_start_date)**: Select the start date of the garnishment.
* **Garnishment End Date (garnishment_end_date)**: Select the end date of the garnishment.
* **Garnishment Amount Type (garnishment_amount_type)**: Choose the type of garnishment amount (e.g. Bank Account, Credit Card, Other).
* **Garnishment Amount Details (garnishment_amount_details)**: Enter any additional details about the garnishment amount.
* **Payee (payee)**: Enter the name and address of the person or entity to be garnished.
* **Garnishment Address (garnishment_address)**: Enter the address of the person or entity to be garnished.
* **Garnishment State (garnishment_state)**: Enter the state where the garnishment will occur.
* **Garnishment Zip (garnishment_zip)**: Enter the zip code of the person or entity to be garnished.
* **Garnishment City (garnishment_city)**: Enter the city where the garnishment will occur.
* **Garnishment County (garnishment_county)**: Enter the county where the garnishment will occur.
* **Garnishment Phone (garnishment_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person or entity to be garnished.
* **Garnishment Email (garnishment_email)**: Enter the email of the person or entity to be garnished.
* **Garnishment Case Number (garnishment_case_number)**: Enter the case number associated with the garnishment.
* **Garnishment Case Type (garnishment_case_type)**: Choose the type of case associated with the garnishment.
* **Garnishment Other (garnishment_other)**: Enter any other details about the garnishment.
* **Garnishment Other Details (garnishment_other_details)**: Enter any additional details about the garnishment.
