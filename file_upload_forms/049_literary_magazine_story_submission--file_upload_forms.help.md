# Literary Magazine Story Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit your literary story for review and potential publication in our magazine. Please fill out this form carefully, and ensure that you're not submitting the same story multiple times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you select the correct category for your submission (File Upload Forms or Other).
3. Upload your story file (PDF, Word, or compatible format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (form_title): A brief title for your story.
* **Category** (category): The category your story belongs to (File Upload Forms or Other).
* **Description** (description): A short description of your story.
* **File Upload** (file_upload): Upload your story file in a supported format (PDF, Word, or other compatible formats).
* **Submission Form** (story_submission_form_fields): Optional form with additional submission details. Currently, this is not being used in this form version.
* **Story Title** (story_title): A title for your story.
* **Author** (story_author): Your name as the author.
* **Story Content** (story_content): The actual content of your story. Please paste or upload your story here.
* **Submission Status** (submission_status): The status of your submission (Active or Inactive). This will be updated by our system.
* **Submission Date** (submission_date): The date you submitted your story.
* **Submission Time** (submission_time): The time you submitted your story.

## Tips
* Ensure that you've filled out all required fields before submitting your form.
* Only submit one story at a time to avoid duplication.
* Please be patient as our review process may take some time. We'll get back to you once we've reviewed your submission.
