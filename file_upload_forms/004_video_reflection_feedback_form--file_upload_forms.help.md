# Video Reflection Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide feedback on video reflections, which can be used for various purposes such as training, evaluation, or coaching. The form is intended to collect information about the video content, including questions and additional comments or questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the address that we will use to contact you about your feedback.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This will help us identify you as the person providing feedback.
3. Answer Question 1 by typing in the "Question 1" field. This question is designed to gather specific feedback about the video content.
4. If you need help with Question 1, you can find additional guidance in the "Question 1 Hint" field.
5. Answer Question 2 by typing in the "Question 2" field. This question is also designed to gather specific feedback about the video content.
6. If you need help with Question 2, you can find additional guidance in the "Question 2 Hint" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can enter them in the "Additional Questions" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (`email_2`, `Email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your feedback.
* **Name** (`name_2`, `Name`, required/optional): Enter your name to help us identify you as the person providing feedback.
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, `text`, required/optional): Answer Question 1 about the video content.
* **Question 1 Hint** (`question_1_hint`, `text`, required/optional): Find additional guidance for Question 1 if you need help.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, `text`, required/optional): Answer Question 2 about the video content.
* **Question 2 Hint** (`question_2_hint`, `text`, required/optional): Find additional guidance for Question 2 if you need help.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about the video content.
* **Submit** (`submit_2`, `text`, required/optional): Click this button to submit your feedback.
