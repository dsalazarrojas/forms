# marketing_client_design_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from clients or users about their marketing project design requirements. It is used to upload files, track project status, and manage deadlines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Project Details" field with a brief description of your project.
2. Select the relevant files to upload by choosing "Design File", "Mockup File", or "Other File" from the "Files" dropdown list.
3. Indicate whether you have uploaded the files by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Uploaded Files" dropdown list.
4. Enter your contact email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the project status from the "Project Status" dropdown list.
7. Choose the tool assigned to the project from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown list.
8. Enter the project deadline date in the "Project Deadline" field.
9. Enter the submission date and time in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields.
10. If necessary, enter a form ID in the "Form ID" field.
11. Enter the name of the user who created the form in the "Created By" field.
12. Enter the name of the user who updated the form in the "Updated By" field.
13. Enter the date and time when the form was updated in the "Updated At" field.
14. Enter the date when the form was created in the "Form Created Date" field.
15. Enter the date when the form was last updated in the "Form Updated At" field.
16. Enter the name of the user who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
17. Enter the ID of the form in the "Form ID" field.
18. Enter the date and time when the form was submitted in the "Submitted At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): A brief description of your project.
* **Files** (`files`, select_multiple, optional): Select the files to upload for your project. Choose from "Design File", "Mockup File", or "Other File".
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have uploaded the files for your project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your project. Choose from "New", "In Progress", "Done", or "Cancelled".
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool assigned to your project.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the deadline date for your project.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your project.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted your project.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): If necessary, enter the ID of the form.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user who created the form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user who updated the form.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, text, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Form Created Date** (`form_created_date`, text, optional): Enter the date when the form was created.
* **Form Updated At** (`form_updated_at`, text, optional): Enter the date when the form was last updated.
* **User** (`user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, text, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, text, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the form.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, text, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
