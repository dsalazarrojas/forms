# Home Insurance Hotel Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps hotel guests submit a reimbursement claim for damages or losses incurred during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all the required information.
2. Attach any supporting documents as requested.
3. Submit the form to the hotel staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Please fill out your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, required): Enter your current address where you can be contacted.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of claim you are submitting (e.g., Claim For Home Damage, Claim For Theft, Claim For Other).
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident or when you first noticed the issue.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are seeking reimbursement for.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required): Attach any relevant receipts, photos, or other documentation to support your claim.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Additional Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents_2`, text, required): Attach any additional documentation or evidence to support your claim.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, time, required): Date and time when you submitted this form.
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, time, required): Date and time when you completed this form.
* **Claimant Relationship** (`claimant_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Choose your relationship with the hotel (e.g., Self, Spouse, Parent, Child, Other).
* **Other Relationship** (`other_relationship`, text, required): If you selected "Other", please specify your relationship.
* **Claimant Insurance** (`claimant_insurance`, text, required): If you have insurance, please provide the policy number or details.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hotel staff or representative who assisted you.
* **Agent Email** (`agent_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the hotel staff or representative who assisted you.
* **Agent Phone** (`agent_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the hotel staff or representative who assisted you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or concerns you have.
* **Insurance ID** (`insurance_id`, text, required): Enter your insurance ID or policy number.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): Enter your policy number or details.
* **Agent Relationship** (`agent_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the hotel staff or representative who assisted you.
* **Agent Address** (`agent_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the hotel staff or representative who assisted you.

Remember to attach any supporting documentation and submit the form to the hotel staff for review and processing.
