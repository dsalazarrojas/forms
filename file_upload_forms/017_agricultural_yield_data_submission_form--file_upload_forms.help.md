# Agricultural Yield Data Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information on agricultural yield data from farmers or their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
3. Ensure all fields are complete and valid before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Name**: Please enter the name of your farm.
* **Field ID**: Specify the source of the harvest (e.g., Field A, Field B, etc.).
* **Crop Type**: Select the type of crop harvested (e.g., Corn, Soybeans, etc.).
* **Harvest Date**: Enter the date the crop was harvested (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Total Weight**: Specify the total weight of the harvested crop (in lbs, tons, bushels, etc.).
* **Moisture Content**: Enter the percentage of moisture content in the harvested crop.
* **Grade or Quality**: Select the grade or quality of the crop (e.g., Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3, Feed Grade, Reject).
* **Storage Location**: Enter the storage location of the harvested crop (e.g., Silo A, Warehouse B, etc.).
* **Operator Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity that harvested the crop.
* **Machinery Used**: If applicable, enter the machinery used for harvesting (e.g., Combine ID, Truck ID, etc.).
* **Uploads**: Please upload any relevant weigh tickets and photos to the portal.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or issues that occurred during the harvest.
