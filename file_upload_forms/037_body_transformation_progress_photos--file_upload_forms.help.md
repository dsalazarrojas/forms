# Body Transformation Progress Photos - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Body Transformation Progress Photos form is designed for clients to track their progress in a body transformation program. This form helps coaches and trainers monitor your progress and provide guidance to help you achieve your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we have on file.
	* **Date of Progress Update**: Select the date of your progress update.
	* **Weeks in Program**: Enter the number of weeks since you started the program.
	* **Current Weight in Pounds**: Enter your current weight in pounds.
	* **Weight Change Since Start**: Enter the weight change since you started the program (optional).
	* **Energy Levels**: Select your current energy levels (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
	* **Workout Intensity Level**: Select your workout intensity level (Exceeding Target, Meeting Target, Below Target, Not Consistently Working Out).
	* **Nutrition Plan Adherence**: Select your nutrition plan adherence (Perfect, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
2. Add optional fields:
	* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
	* **Chest Measurement in Inches**: Enter your current chest measurement in inches (optional).
	* **Waist Measurement in Inches**: Enter your current waist measurement in inches (optional).
	* **Hip Measurement in Inches**: Enter your current hip measurement in inches (optional).
	* **Arm Circumference in Inches**: Enter your arm circumference in inches (optional).
	* **Thigh Circumference in Inches**: Enter your thigh circumference in inches (optional).
	* **Front Body Progress Photo**: Upload a front-facing photo of your progress.
	* **Side Body Progress Photo**: Upload a side-facing photo of your progress.
	* **Back Body Progress Photo**: Upload a back-facing photo of your progress (optional).
	* **Clothing During Photos**: Describe the clothing you wore during the photos (optional).
	* **Visible Physical Changes**: Select all visible physical changes you've noticed (Muscle Definition, Fat Loss, Posture Improvement, Skin Appearance, Overall Tone, Strength Visible, No Visible Change, Other).
	* **Progress Notes and Observations**: Share any comments or observations about your progress.
	* **Challenges Faced**: Share any challenges you've faced this week.
	* **Goals for Next Week**: Set goals for the next week.
