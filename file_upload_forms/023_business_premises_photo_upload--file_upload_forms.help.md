# Business Premises Photo Upload - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to upload photos of a business premises for various purposes such as insurance documentation, property inspection, damage assessment, and routine documentation. The form is designed to collect relevant information about the premises, including its address, type, and size, as well as details about the inspection and any observed damage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all required fields completed.
2. Enter the full street address of the premises in the "Property Address" field.
3. Select the category of the premises from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
4. For the "Inspection Date" field, select the date when the photos were taken.
5. If you are submitting photos for a specific purpose (e.g. insurance documentation), select the corresponding option from the "Purpose of Inspection" dropdown menu.
6. Upload photos of the premises in each of the required fields (e.g. front exterior, side views, roof condition, etc.).
7. If applicable, describe any observed damage in the "Damage Description" field and upload photos of the damaged areas.
8. Add any additional notes or observations in the "Additional Notes" field.
9. Review and submit the form when all required fields have been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address**: Enter the full street address of the premises.
* **Property Type**: Select the category of the premises (e.g. office building, retail store, warehouse, etc.).
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date when the photos were taken.
* **Purpose of Inspection**: Select the reason for submitting photos (e.g. insurance documentation, property inspection, etc.).
* **Front Exterior Photo**: Upload a photo of the front of the premises.
* **Side Views Photo**: Upload a photo of the sides of the premises.
* **Roof Condition Photo**: Upload a photo of the roof area of the premises.
* **Entry Area Photo**: Upload a photo of the main entrance area.
* **Main Space Photo**: Upload a photo of the primary interior area.
* **Utilities and Systems Photo**: Upload a photo of the HVAC, electrical, and plumbing systems.
* **Damage Observed**: Select whether any damage was observed.
* **Damage Description**: Describe any observed damage.
* **Damage Photos**: Upload photos of damaged areas.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any other relevant observations or comments.

Please note that this form requires the submission of multiple photos for each field, and the submission is subject to review and approval before publication.
