# Food Photo Nutrition Analysis Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect photos and details of food items for nutritional analysis. Please submit a photo of the meal, select the relevant details, and provide the date of consumption. This information will be used to generate a report on the nutritional content of the food.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Upload a clear photo of the meal you want to analyze.
2. Select the relevant details about the food intake (e.g., "Yes" if you consumed it, "No" if not).
3. Enter the date you consumed the meal (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meal Photo** (`meal_photo`, text, required: false)
	+ Please upload a clear and legible photo of the meal.
* **Food Intake Details** (`food_intake_details`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if you consumed the food, and "No" if not.
* **Meal Date** (`meal_date`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date you consumed the meal (if applicable).
