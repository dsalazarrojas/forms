# Legal Counsel Claim Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Counsel Claim Submission form is designed to collect necessary information from clients or individuals seeking legal counsel services. The form gathers details about the claim, file attachments, supporting documents, legal counsel information, client information, submission information, and review status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Claim Details" section with the necessary information about your claim.
2. Attach any relevant files related to your claim in the "File Attachments" section.
3. Provide any supporting documents that may be relevant to your claim in the "Supporting Documents" section.
4. Enter your contact information as the legal counsel in the "Legal Counsel Info" section.
5. Enter your client's information in the "Client Info" section.
6. Fill in the "Submission Info" section with any additional information about your submission.
7. Indicate the review status of your submission in the "Review Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing a brief description of your claim.
* **File Attachments** (`file_attachments`, text, required/optional): This field is for uploading any relevant files related to your claim.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required/optional): This field is for attaching any supporting documents related to your claim.
* **Legal Counsel Info** (`legal_counsel_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing your contact information as the legal counsel.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering your client's information.
* **Submission Info** (`submission_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing additional information about your submission.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, text, required/optional): This field is for indicating the current status of your review.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your claim is processed correctly.
- Double-check your submissions for accuracy and completeness.
- If you need further assistance or clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
