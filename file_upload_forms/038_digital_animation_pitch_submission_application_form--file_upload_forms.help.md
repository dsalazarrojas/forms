# digital_animation_pitch_submission_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital animation pitch submission application form is a tool for collecting detailed information about your digital animation project. It's intended for internal use within the organization, where submitted projects will be reviewed and evaluated by relevant teams.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  On the first page, provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
2.  Select the type of animation you're submitting (2D, 3D, or Live-Action) in the "animation_type" field.
3.  Choose the genre of your animation (Adventure, Action, Comedy, or Sci-Fi) by selecting all applicable options in the "genre" field.
4.  Choose the style of your animation (Traditional, Computer-Generated, or Stop-Motion) in the "animation_style" field.
5.  If applicable, specify the length of your animation in the "animation_length" field.
6.  Upload any relevant files related to your project in the "Upload File 1", "Upload File 2", and "Upload File 3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): A brief description of your project, outlining its key features and objectives.
* **Animation Type** (`animation_type`, select_one, required: false): The type of animation you're submitting (2D, 3D, or Live-Action).
* **Genre** (`genre`, select_multiple, required: false): The genre of your animation (Adventure, Action, Comedy, or Sci-Fi). You can select multiple options.
* **Animation Style** (`animation_style`, select_one, required: false): The style of your animation (Traditional, Computer-Generated, or Stop-Motion).
* **Animation Length** (`animation_length`, number, required: false): The length of your animation. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Upload File 1** (`file_upload_1`, text, required: false): Upload any relevant files related to your project.
* **Upload File 2** (`file_upload_2`, text, required: false): Upload any additional files related to your project.
* **Upload File 3** (`file_upload_3`, text, required: false): Upload any further files related to your project.

## Tips

* Be as descriptive as possible in the "Project Description" field to ensure that your project is understood accurately.
* If you're unsure about the genre or style of your animation, refer to our internal documentation or contact the internal team for guidance.
* Ensure that the "Animation Length" field is only filled in if it's applicable to your project.
* Upload only relevant files to support your project submission.
