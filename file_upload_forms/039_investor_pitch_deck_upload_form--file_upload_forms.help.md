# Investor Pitch Deck Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investor Pitch Deck Upload Form is designed to collect information about pitch decks from users, typically investors. It can be used to share reviews, thoughts, or feedback on pitch decks, which is a valuable resource for companies seeking investment opportunities and investors evaluating potential investments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling out the "Pitch Deck Information" field, where you can provide a brief description of the pitch deck you are uploading or sharing.
2. Next, fill out the "Company Information" field with details about the company related to the pitch deck.
3. If you wish to share your review or feedback about the pitch deck, fill out the "Review Information" field.
4. Finally, enter your "User Information" for identification and contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pitch Deck Information (Pitch Deck Information, text, required/optional)**: Enter a brief description of the pitch deck you are uploading or sharing.
- **Company Information (Company Information, text, required/optional)**: Provide details about the company the pitch deck is associated with.
- **Review Information (Review Information, text, required/optional)**: Share your review or feedback about the pitch deck if you wish.
- **User Information (User Information, text, required/optional)**: Enter your information for identification and contact purposes.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the pitch deck information is accurate and up-to-date.
