# Content Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Upload Form is a tool for uploading various types of content, including images, videos, documents, and more. It's essential to use this form to ensure that your content is stored and used according to our terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Uploader Information" section with your details, including your full name and email address.
2. Select your relationship to the content you're uploading (e.g., original creator, authorized representative, or employee).
3. Describe the content in the "Content Information" section, including its title, type, and a brief description.
4. Specify the sensitivity level of the content.
5. Confirm that you have the necessary rights to upload the content.
6. Agree to our terms of service.
7. Choose a date for the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Uploader Information (Uploader Details Header)
* **Full Name** (required): Your full name, as it appears in your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (required, email): The email address associated with your account.
* **Your Relationship to the Content** (required, select_one): Select one of the following options:
	+ Original Creator/Owner
	+ Authorized Representative
	+ Employee/Staff Member
	+ Other

### Content Information
* **Title of the Content** (required, text): A concise title for the content you're uploading.
* **Type of Content** (required, select_one): Select one of the following options:
	+ Image/Photo (JPG, PNG)
	+ Video (MP4, MOV)
	+ Document (PDF, DOCX)
	+ Audio (MP3, WAV)
	+ Compressed Archive (ZIP, RAR)
* **Brief Description of Content** (required, text): A short description of the content, limited to 100 words.
* **Number of Files being Uploaded** (required, number): The total number of files being uploaded.
* **Main File Name Reference** (optional, text): A reference for the main file being uploaded, e.g., project_final_v1.mp4.
* **Legal and Terms**
* **I Confirm I have the rights to Upload this content** (required, select_one): Confirm that you have the necessary rights to upload the content:
	+ Yes, I have full rights
	+ Yes, I have permission from the owner
* **Content Sensitivity**
* **Content Sensitivity** (required, select_one): Select one of the following options:
	+ Public - Can be used anywhere
	+ Internal Only - Not for external use
	+ Highly Confidential - Secure storage only
* **Agree to Terms**
* **I Agree to the Storage and Usage Terms** (required, select_one): Confirm that you agree to our terms of service:
	+ I Agree
* **Date of Submission** (required, date): Choose a date for the submission.

This guide is designed to help you complete the Content Upload Form successfully and ensure that your content is processed and stored correctly. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
