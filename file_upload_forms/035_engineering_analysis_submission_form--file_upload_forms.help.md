# Engineering Analysis Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from engineers and reviewers during the submission and review process for documents and projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what fields you need to fill out.
2. Fill out the Submission inputs section with the necessary information:
	* Submission inputs: This section includes fields that you, as the engineer, need to fill out.
	* Document upload: Upload any supporting documents related to the submission.
3. For Reviewer comments, fill out the fields that apply to you as the reviewer:
	* Reviewer name: Enter your name as the reviewer.
	* Reviewer role: Enter your job title or role as the reviewer.
	* Reviewer email: Enter your email address as the reviewer.
	* Reviewer phone: Enter your phone number as the reviewer.
	* Reviewer comments: Enter any comments or notes related to the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission inputs**: This is the main input section for the engineer. It includes the following fields:
	+ **Document upload**: Upload any supporting documents related to the submission. (Optional)
	+ **Project stakeholders**: Check if the project has any stakeholders. (Optional)
	+ **Review context**: Provide a brief review context. (Optional)
	+ **Analysis package**: This field is currently empty. (Not used in current implementation)
	+ **Submission review**: Provide a detailed submission review. (Optional)
* **Reviewer comments**: This section is for the reviewer. It includes the following fields:
	+ **Reviewer name**: Enter your name as the reviewer.
	+ **Reviewer role**: Enter your job title or role as the reviewer.
	+ **Reviewer email**: Enter your email address as the reviewer.
	+ **Reviewer phone**: Enter your phone number as the reviewer.
	+ **Reviewer comments**: Enter any comments or notes related to the submission.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully to avoid any issues with submission.
- Review the submission form carefully before submitting it.
