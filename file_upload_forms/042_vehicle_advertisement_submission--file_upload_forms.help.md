# Vehicle Advertisement Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Advertisement Submission form is a tool for sellers to provide detailed information about the vehicles they are trying to sell. This form helps us gather essential data about the vehicle, such as seller information, vehicle details, images, and listing price.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Upload images of the vehicle (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Information (Seller Information, Text, Optional)**: Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your vehicle submission.
* **Vehicle Details (Vehicle Details, Text, Optional)**: Provide a brief description of the vehicle, including its make, model, year, and any other relevant details.
* **Images (Images, Text, Optional)**: Upload one or more images of the vehicle to help us get a better understanding of it. This can include photos of the interior, exterior, and any other relevant details.
* **Listing Price (Listing Price, Number, Optional)**: Enter the price you are asking for the vehicle.
* **Location (Location, Text, Optional)**: Enter the location where the vehicle is currently located, or where it is being sold.
