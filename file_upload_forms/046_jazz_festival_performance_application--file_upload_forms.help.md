# Jazz Festival Performance Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will help you fill in the Jazz Festival Performance Application form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review the fields carefully and fill them in accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Performer**: Select "Yes" if you are a performer who wishes to participate in the festival.
*   **Name**: Enter your name as it will be displayed in promotional materials for the festival.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use for communication and confirmation purposes.
*   **Contact Number**: Provide your contact number for easier communication with the festival team.
*   **Date**: If you have a specific date in mind, enter it here.
*   **Time**: If you have a preferred time, enter it here.
*   **Bio**: Briefly describe yourself and your musical style.
*   **Genre**: Select the genre of music you will be performing.
*   **Instrument**: Indicate the instrument(s) you will be playing.
*   **Audio File**: Upload a relevant audio file, such as a demo or a clip.
*   **Video File**: Upload a video showcasing your performance or style.
*   **Image File**: Upload an image for promotional use.
*   **Attachment**: Upload any additional information or documents that may be relevant.
*   **Performer Image**: Upload a profile picture or a representative image.
*   **Additional Files**: If you have any other files you'd like to upload, select "Yes" here.
*   **Confirm**: Please review and confirm that you have read and understood this form.
*   **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your identity.
*   **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that you feel is relevant.
*   **Agreement**: Acknowledge the festival rules and regulations.
*   **Contact Info**: Enter any relevant contact information for the performance.
*   **Address**: Enter your address for mailing purposes.
*   **City**: Enter the city where you will perform.
*   **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
*   **State**: Enter your state or province.
*   **Country**: Enter your country of origin.
