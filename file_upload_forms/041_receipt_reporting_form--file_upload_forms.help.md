# Receipt Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receipt Reporting Form is used for reporting receipts and expenses associated with a purchase. It provides a structured way to submit detailed information about the receipt, including purchase date and time, total cost, category of purchase, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
* Fill out each field with the required information.
* For fields marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple", select the appropriate options from the list.
* For fields marked as "text", enter your information in the text field.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
