# Mobile Application License Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for mobile application license applications, which are used by developers or publishers to submit their mobile applications for approval and licensing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself or your company.
2. **Mobile App Details** (`mobile_app_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your mobile application, including its name, version, and any other relevant details.
3. **Mobile App Screenshots** (`file_upload`, text, optional): Upload 3-5 screenshots of your mobile app in JPEG or PNG format.
4. **Additional Documents** (`file_upload_1`, text, optional): Upload any other relevant documents (e.g., business plans, marketing materials, etc.).
5. **Contact Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter the email address you want to be contacted on.
6. **Contact Phone Number** (`phone_input`, text, optional): Enter the phone number you want to be contacted on.
7. **Date of Release** (`date_input`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to release your mobile app.
8. **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three options from the list provided.
9. **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list provided.
10. **Date of Submission** (`date_input_1`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
11. **Time of Submission** (`time_input`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting this application.
12. **Select Multiple 1** (`select_multiple_1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three options from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This field is for you to enter any relevant information about yourself or your company. This can include your name, company name, or any other details you want to provide.
* **Mobile App Details**: This field is for you to provide a brief description of your mobile application. Be as concise as possible, but make sure to include the name, version, and any other relevant details.
* **Mobile App Screenshots**: Please upload 3-5 screenshots of your mobile app in JPEG or PNG format. These screenshots should be clear and well-labeled, so we can easily understand your application.
* **Additional Documents**: If you have any other relevant documents (e.g., business plans, marketing materials), please upload them here.
* **Contact Email**: This is the email address we will use to contact you if we need more information or clarification.
* **Contact Phone Number**: This is the phone number we will use to contact you if we need to discuss further.
* **Date of Release**: Enter the date you plan to release your mobile app.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose up to three options from the list provided. These options might include different application types or categories.
* **Select One**: Choose one option from the list provided. This option might be related to a specific application type or category.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date you are submitting this application.
* **Time of Submission**: Enter the time you are submitting this application.
* **Select Multiple 1**: Choose up to three options from the list provided. These options might include different application types or categories.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) and double-check your submission before submitting it. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us!
