# Trade Certification Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for trade certification, including details about themselves and any required supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Applicant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. (Optional) Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Upload any required supporting documents in the "File" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Trade Certification Enrollment Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the trade certification you are enrolling in.
- **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your certificate.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address, which may be required for certification purposes.
- **File** (`file`, text, required): Upload any required supporting documents, such as ID or certification proof.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit your application for review and processing.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out correctly and accurately to avoid delays in processing your application.
- Use a clear and concise format for your file uploads.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the certification authority for assistance.
