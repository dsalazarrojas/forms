# heroic-legacy-exhibition-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a heroic legacy exhibition registration, allowing artists to showcase their work and connect with potential clients or exhibition organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a **form-title** for your exhibition.
2.  Provide your **participant-name** and **participant-email**.
3.  Write a brief **artwork-description** of your artwork.
4.  Upload a preview of your **artwork-preview** (this will be further explained in the next section).
5.  Provide your **participant-website**.
6.  Select your preferred social media **participant-social-media-links** (this is a multiple-selection field).
7.  Choose whether you **agree-to-terms**.
8.  Select the **artwork-category** of your artwork.
9.  Enter the price of your artwork.
10. Click the submit button once you have filled out all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **form-title** (`exhibition_legacy_exhibition_registration`, text, required/optional): A brief title for your exhibition.
*  **participant-name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the participant or artist.
*  **participant-email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication.
*  **artwork-description** (`artwork_description`, note, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your artwork.
*  **upload-artwork-preview** (`file_upload`, text, required/optional): This field is for uploading a preview of your artwork (image, video, or other digital formats). The exact upload requirements will depend on the system's specifications.
*  **participant-website** (`participant_website`, text, required/optional): Enter a link to your personal or professional website.
*  **participant-social-media-links** (`participant_social_media_links`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more social media platforms you have a presence on (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube).
*  **agree-to-terms** (`participant_agree`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree with the terms and conditions of the exhibition.
*  **artwork-category** (`artwork_category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the category that your artwork belongs to (e.g., Painting, Print, Digital Art, Sculpture, Other).
*  **artwork-price** (`artwork_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of your artwork, if applicable.
