# speaker_registration_and_material_upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about speakers and their session materials for a conference or event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the speaker's information:
	* **Speaker Info**: Enter the speaker's name, image, and other relevant details.
	* **Speaker Name**: Enter the speaker's name as they want it to be displayed on the event program.
	* **Speaker Image**: Upload a photo of the speaker (optional).
2. Provide speaker session information:
	* **Session Info**: Enter a brief description of the session.
	* **Session Date**: Choose the date of the session.
	* **Session Time**: Choose the time of the session.
	* **Room Number**: Specify the room number for the session.
	* **Session Room**: Specify the session room name.
	* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the session.
3. Add speaker's bio and contact information:
	* **Speaker Bio**: Enter a brief bio of the speaker.
	* **Speaker Phone**: Enter the speaker's phone number.
	* **Speaker Email**: Enter the speaker's email address.
	* **Speaker Website**: Enter the speaker's website (if applicable).
	* **Speaker Affiliation**: Enter the speaker's affiliation or institution.
	* **Language**: Specify the language of the speaker.
4. Upload session materials:
	* **Speaker Video**: Upload a video recording of the session (if available).
	* **Speaker Audio**: Upload an audio recording of the session (if available).
	* **Speaker PPT**: Upload the speaker's presentation slides (if available).
