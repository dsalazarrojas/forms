# Identity Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request identity verification from the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information on the "Personal Information" section.
2. Select the correct verification details on the "Verification Details" section.
3. Upload any relevant files in the "File Uploads" section.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Information" section.
5. Confirm your identity with our team in the "Verification Confirmation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Personal Information** (`personal_info_1`, text, not required)
Please fill in your details, such as your name and contact information.

### **Verification Details** (`verification_details_1`, text, not required)
Select the correct verification details as per your situation.

### **File Uploads** (`file_uploads_1`, text, not required)
Upload any relevant files to support your identity verification request.

### **Additional Information** (`additional_info_1`, text, not required)
Provide any additional comments or notes to help our team assess your request.

### **Verification Confirmation** (`verification_confirmation_1`, text, not required)
Confirm your identity with our team to proceed with the verification process.
