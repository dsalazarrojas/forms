# couples_anthology_submission_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for authors to submit their work to a publication, specifically for a couple's anthology.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the type of entry you are submitting (fiction or nonfiction).
2.  Fill out the first and last name of the authors.
3.  Fill out the title of the entry.
4.  Enter the actual entry being submitted.
5.  Select the date and time of submission.
6.  Enter any additional files related to the entry.
7.  Enter any cover letter or additional information.
8.  Select the status of the submission (Pending, In Review, Rejected, Accepted, or Withdrawn).
9.  Enter any additional comments or notes.
10. Enter the email and phone number of the contact person.
11. Enter any additional contact information.
12. Enter the website and social media of the authors.
13. Enter the publication of the authors.
14. Enter the length of the entry in words.
15. Enter the title of the anthology the entry is part of.
16. Enter information about the publication of the anthology.
17. Enter the date and time of publication of the anthology.
