# Custom Jingle Collaboration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Jingle Collaboration Form is a tool used to collect information about custom jingle projects, including project details, client information, and collaboration settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure you have all necessary approvals and information in place before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: <form_title> (Text, Optional) - This is the title of the form, which should be descriptive and concise.
* **Project ID**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_project_id> (Number, Optional) - Enter the project ID number for reference.
* **Client**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_client> (Text, Optional) - Enter the name of the client for the project.
* **Contact Person**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_contact_person> (Text, Optional) - Enter the name of the contact person for the project.
* **Project Name**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_project_name> (Text, Optional) - Enter the name of the project.
* **Collaboration Type**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_collaboration_type> (Select One, Optional) - Choose the type of collaboration (Collaboration, Co-Creation, or Co-Production).
* **Target Language**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_target_language> (Select Multiple, Optional) - Select the target language for the collaboration.
* **Project Status**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_project_status> (Select One, Optional) - Choose the project status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Client Budget**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_client_budget> (Number, Optional) - Enter the client budget for the project.
* **Jingle Length**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_jingle_length> (Number, Optional) - Enter the length of the jingle (in seconds).
* **Jingle Type**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_jingle_type> (Select One, Optional) - Choose the type of jingle (16-Second, 30-Second, 60-Second, or 15-Second).
* **Delivery Date**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_delivery_date> (Date, Optional) - Enter the expected delivery date.
* **Delivery Time**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_delivery_time> (Time, Optional) - Enter the expected delivery time.
* **Collaborator**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_collaborator> (Text, Optional) - Enter the name of the collaborator.
* **Collaborator Phone**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_collaborator_phone> (Text, Optional) - Enter the phone number of the collaborator.
* **Collaborator Email**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_collaborator_email> (Email, Optional) - Enter the email of the collaborator.
* **Project Files**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_project_files> (Text, Optional) - Enter any relevant project files.
* **Approvals**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_approvals> (Select Multiple, Optional) - Choose the approval status (Approve, Reject, or Pending Review).
* **Collaborator Signature**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_collaborator_signature> (Text, Optional) - Enter the collaborator's signature.
* **Collaboration Date**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_collaboration_date> (Date, Optional) - Enter the date of collaboration.
* **Collaborator Time**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_collaborator_time> (Time, Optional) - Enter the collaboration time.
* **Agency**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_agency> (Text, Optional) - Enter the name of the agency.
* **Notes**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_notes> (Text, Optional) - Enter any additional notes.
* **Agree to Terms**: <custom_jingle_collaboration_form_agree_terms> (Select One, Optional) - Choose whether you agree to the terms (I Agree or I Do Not Agree).

## Tips
- Ensure you have all necessary approvals and information in place before submitting the form.
- Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting it.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or project manager for guidance.
