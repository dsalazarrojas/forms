# Home Remedy Recipe Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect home remedy recipes from users, allowing us to gather and organize valuable health-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled in correctly.
2. Upload a photo of the remedy to help us better understand the recipe.
3. Provide a rating for the remedy, on a scale of 1-5, indicating its effectiveness.
4. Write a comment to describe your experience with the remedy.
5. Enter your contact information for us to follow up with you.
6. Finally, enter relevant tags to help us categorize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Remedy Recipe Submission Form** (`home_remedy_recipe_submission_form`): A text field where you can enter the name of your recipe.
* **Upload a Photo** (`upload_photo`): Upload a clear and high-quality photo of the remedy. This will help us better understand the recipe.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate the remedy's effectiveness, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Review** (`review`): Enter a detailed description of your experience with the remedy.
* **Comment** (`comment`): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share about the remedy.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you submitted the remedy.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter your name, so we can address any follow-up questions.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address, so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Tags** (`tags`): Enter keywords related to your remedy, to help us categorize your submission.
* **Tags 2-5** (`tags_2`, `tags_3`, `tags_4`, `tags_5`, `tags_6`, `tags_7`, `tags_8`, `tags_9`, `tags_10`): More tags to help us further categorize your submission.

Please note that some fields are required for a successful submission. Make sure to fill them out carefully before submitting your form.
