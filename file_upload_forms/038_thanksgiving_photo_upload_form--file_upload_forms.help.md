# Thanksgiving Photo Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Photo Upload Form is designed to collect information and photos for a special occasion related to Thanksgiving. This form may be used for event organizers to collect photos from attendees or for individuals to share their Thanksgiving memories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Click the "Upload Photo" field to select and upload your photo.
2.  Enter the "Event Organizer" field with the person or group responsible for organizing the event, if applicable.
3.  If relevant, enter the "Event Date" field with the date of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Photo** (`Upload Photo`, text, required/optional): Upload a photo related to the Thanksgiving event. You can browse for a photo on your device or select one from a provided link. Please ensure the photo is clear and relevant to the event.
* **Event Organizer** (`Event Organizer`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the person or group responsible for organizing the event. This can be an individual, a committee, or a team.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required/optional): If relevant, enter the date of the event. This will help provide context to the uploaded photo and aid in organization and tracking of events.
