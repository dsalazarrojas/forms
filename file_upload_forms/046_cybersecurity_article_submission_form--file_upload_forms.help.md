# cybersecurity_article_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for cybersecurity article submission. It is used to collect information about the article, author, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are submitting a unique article and not duplicating an existing one.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Manuscript**: Enter a brief summary of the article.
- **Title**: Enter a title for the article.
- **Author**: Enter your name as the author of the article.
- **Affiliation**: Enter your affiliation or institution related to the article.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Manuscript Type**:
  - **Manuscript**: Select if the article is a manuscript.
  - **Review**: Select if the article is a review.
  - **Other**: Select if the article is not a manuscript or review.
  - **Please specify**: Enter the type of article if it is not a manuscript or review.
- **Supporting Materials**: Enter any supporting materials related to the article.
- **Category**:
  - **Manuscript**: Select if the article is a manuscript.
  - **Review**: Select if the article is a review.
  - **Please specify**: Enter the category if it is not a manuscript or review.
- **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission.
- **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission.
- **Editor Note**: Enter any notes from the editor.
- **Manuscript Type Other (please specify)**: Enter the type of article if it is not a manuscript or review.
- **Category Other (please specify)**: Enter the category if it is not a manuscript or review.
- **Supporting Materials (2)**: Enter any additional supporting materials related to the article.
- **Supporting Materials Other (please specify)**: Enter any other supporting materials related to the article.
- **Submission Review Process**:
  - **Review**: Select if the article is under review.
  - **Edit**: Select if the article is in edit.
  - **Publish**: Select if the article is published.
  - **Please specify**: Select if the article is not under review, edit, or published.
- **Submission Status**:
  - **Pending**: Select if the article is pending.
  - **Approved**: Select if the article is approved.
  - **Rejected**: Select if the article is rejected.
  - **Please specify**: Select if the article is not pending, approved, or rejected.
- **Submission Date (please specify)**: Enter the date of submission if it is not known.
- **Submission Time (please specify)**: Enter the time of submission if it is not known.
- **Editor Note (please specify)**: Enter any notes from the editor if it is not known.
- **Submission Review Process (please specify)**: Select if the article is under review.
- **Submission Status (please specify)**: Select if the article is not pending, approved, or rejected.
- **Submission Date 2**: Enter the date of submission if it is not known.
- **Submission Time 2**: Enter the time of submission if it is not known.

Note: Please ensure that you carefully review the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness of information.
