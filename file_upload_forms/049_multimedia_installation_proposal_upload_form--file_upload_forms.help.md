# Multimedia Installation Proposal Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Multimedia Installation Proposal Upload Form is designed to collect information from users who are submitting multimedia installation proposals. The form is used to gather details about the proposal, including supporting files, proposal details, gallery information, venue information, event information, schedule, and proposal status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you are submitting a proposal that is relevant to the multimedia installation.
2. Fill in the "Supporting Files" section with any relevant files that support your proposal.
3. In the "Proposal Details" section, provide a brief description of your proposal.
4. Select the correct gallery for your proposal from the "Gallery Info" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the correct venue for your proposal from the "Venue Info" dropdown menu.
6. Select the correct event for your proposal from the "Event Info" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the start and end times for your proposal in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields respectively.
8. Choose a date for your proposal in the "Date" field.
9. Select one or more schedules for your proposal from the "Proposal Scheduling" dropdown menu.
10. Finally, select the status of your proposal from the "Proposal Status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supporting Files**: Upload any relevant supporting files for your proposal.
* **Proposal Details**: Provide a brief description of your proposal.
* **Gallery Info**: Select the gallery that your proposal is relevant to.
* **Venue Info**: Choose the venue where your proposal will be installed.
* **Event Info**: Select the event related to your proposal.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your proposal.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your proposal.
* **Date**: Choose a date for your proposal.
* **Proposal Scheduling**: Select one or more schedules for your proposal.
* **Proposal Status**: Select the status of your proposal.
