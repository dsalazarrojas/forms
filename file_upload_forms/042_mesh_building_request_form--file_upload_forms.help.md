# Mesh Building Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mesh Building Request Form is a tool for users to submit requests for custom mesh building projects. It's a comprehensive form that gathers all the necessary information to facilitate the project's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Form Details section with a title and any relevant comments.
2. Select the mesh type that best suits your project needs.
3. Choose the mesh size required for your project.
4. Select the fabrication method and material that you would like to use.
5. Upload any relevant files related to your project.
6. Provide your contact information, including name, email, phone number, and requested date and time.
7. Set your follow-up preferences, including frequency and method.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your project.
9. Finally, submit the form to request your custom mesh building project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details (1)**: This is the title of your project. Please provide a brief description of your project.
* **Mesh Type (2)**: Select the type of mesh you would like to have built, such as I-beam mesh, Triangular mesh, etc.
* **Mesh Size (3)**: Enter the size of the mesh you require.
* **Fabrication Method (4)**: Choose the method by which you would like your mesh to be fabricated, such as Water jet cutting, Laser cutting, etc.
* **Material (5)**: Select the material you would like to use for your mesh, such as Aluminum, Steel, Titanium, etc.
* **Quantity (6)**: Enter the quantity of mesh you require.
* **File Upload (7)**: If you have any relevant files related to your project, please upload them here.
* **Notes (8)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Requestor Name (9)**: This is your name.
* **Contact Email (10)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Requested Date (12)**: Enter the date you would like to receive your mesh.
* **Requested Time (13)**: Enter the time you would like to receive your mesh.
* **Follow-up (14)**: Set your follow-up preference, such as Yes, No, etc.
* **Follow-up Frequency (15)**: If you have chosen to follow-up, select the frequency, such as Every 1 week, Every 2 weeks, etc.
* **Follow-up Method (16)**: If you have chosen to follow-up, select the method, such as Phone, Email, Meeting, etc.
* **Follow-up Comments (17)**: Provide any additional comments about your follow-up preferences.
* **Additional Comments (18)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Project Status (19)**: Set the status of your project, such as Active, Completed, On Hold, etc.
* **Project Notes (20)**: Provide any notes about your project.
* **Requestor Comments (21)**: Provide any comments or feedback about your project.
* **Client Name (22)**: If applicable, enter the name of your client.
* **Requestor Company (23)**: If applicable, enter the name of your company.
* **Project Manager Name (24)**: If applicable, enter the name of your project manager.
* **Assigned Tool (25)**: If applicable, enter the tool assigned to your project.
 
## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
