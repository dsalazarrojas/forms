# Budget Cover Design Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Cover Design Submission form is used to collect information about a cover design submission for a book or publication. This form helps us understand the designer's details, design concept, and submission requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and contact information (Designer Name, Email Address, and Phone Number).
2.  Enter the name of the design company or studio.
3.  Enter the title of the project or book.
4.  If applicable, enter the author's name.
5.  Select the genre or category of the project.
6.  Describe the design concept and inspiration.
7.  Indicate the design software used.
8.  Specify the file format and resolution of the design file.
9.  Confirm if the file is print-ready or needs minor adjustments.
10. Upload your design file.
11. Enter the submission deadline.
12. Confirm that you own or have the necessary rights to all elements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Designer Name** (`Designer Name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`designer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`designer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Design Company or Studio Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you have a design company or studio name, enter it.
* **Project or Book Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the project or book.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the author's name.
* **Genre or Category** (`genre_category`, select_one, required): Choose the genre or category of the project.
* **Design Description** (`design_description`, text, required): Describe the design concept.
* **Design Inspiration or Concept** (`design_inspiration`, text, optional): Enter what inspired your design.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Describe who the book is for.
* **Design Software Used** (`software_used`, select_one, optional): Choose the design software used.
* **File Format Provided** (`file_format`, text, required): Specify the file format of the design file.
* **File Resolution** (`file_resolution`, text, optional): Enter the file resolution.
* **Print-Ready File** (`print_ready`, select_one, required): Indicate if the file is print-ready or needs adjustments.
* **Design File** (`design_file`, text, required): Upload your design file.
* **Portfolio or Website URL** (`portfolio_url`, text, optional): Enter your portfolio or website URL.
* **Submission Deadline** (`submission_deadline`, date, required): Enter the submission deadline.
* **Confirm All Rights and Licenses** (`usage_rights`, select_one, required): Confirm that you own or have the necessary rights to all elements.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the format and resolution of the design file before uploading.
* Be clear and concise in describing the design concept and inspiration.
* Confirm all necessary rights and licenses before submitting.
* Make sure to meet the submission deadline to avoid delays.
