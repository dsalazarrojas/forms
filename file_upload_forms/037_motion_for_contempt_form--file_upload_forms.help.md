# Motion For Contempt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document details about a contempt motion in a legal context. It gathers information about the court and judge involved, the date and time of the motion, and any supporting files.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the status of the motion from the options provided.
2. Enter the name of the court and the judge involved in the motion.
3. Enter the date and time of the motion.
4. Upload any supporting files that are required.
5. Select the type of file being uploaded (e.g. PDF, DOCX, etc.).
6. If a file is required, select the "True" option.
7. Enter a label and description for the uploaded file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Motion For Contempt Form**: This is the title of the form, which is used to identify it.
* **Court Order Violation Details**: A field to enter any details about the court order that was violated.
* **Individual Attorneys Legal Clinic**: A field to select whether the motion is related to an individual attorney, a legal clinic, or both.
* **Date Of Violation**: The date of the violation (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time Of Violation**: The time of the violation (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Time Zone**: The time zone of the violation.
* **Motion Status**: The status of the motion (e.g. Filed, Dismissed, Sua Sponte).
* **Date Of Hearing**: The date of the hearing (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Court Name**: The name of the court involved in the motion.
* **Judge Name**: The name of the judge involved in the motion.
* **Case Number**: The case number related to the motion.
* **Motion Number**: The motion number related to the motion.
* **Motion Date**: The date of the contempt motion (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Motion Time**: The time of the contempt motion (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Motion Status 2**: The status of the contempt motion (e.g. Filed, Dismissed, Sua Sponte).
* **Court Address**: The address of the court involved in the motion.
* **Motion Reason**: The reason for filing the contempt motion.
* **Motion Description**: A description of the contempt motion.
* **File Upload**: A field to upload any supporting files.
* **File Upload Label**: A label for the uploaded file.
* **File Upload Description**: A description for the uploaded file.
* **File Type**: The type of file being uploaded (e.g. PDF, DOCX, etc.).
* **File Size**: The size of the uploaded file.
* **File Upload Required**: A field to select if a file is required.
* **File Upload Max Size**: The maximum size of the uploaded file.
