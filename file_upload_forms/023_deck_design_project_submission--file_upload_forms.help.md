# Deck Design Project Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and submit deck design projects. The form is intended for students or architects to provide project details, files, drawings, and measurements related to their deck design projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project details on page 1, including any relevant information about your project.
2. Upload project files on page 2, such as images or documents related to your project.
3. Provide project drawings on page 3, which should include any visual representations of your project design.
4. Enter project measurements on page 4, which should include any specific measurements or dimensions associated with your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details** (page 1, text): Enter a brief description of your deck design project, including any relevant project title, dates, and details.
- **Project Files** (page 2, text): Upload or link to any relevant files related to your project, such as images, documents, or videos.
- **Project Drawings** (page 3, text): Provide visual representations of your deck design, including drawings or sketches that showcase your project's layout, design, and features.
- **Project Measurements** (page 4, text): Enter specific measurements or dimensions related to your project, such as deck size, beam size, or material specifications.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide clear and concise information for each field to ensure accurate project submission.
- Use proper formatting and attachment options for project files to ensure they can be easily accessed and reviewed.
- Double-check your project measurements to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
