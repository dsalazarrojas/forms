# Storytelling Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Storytelling Tournament Entry Form is a digital entry form designed to collect participant information and submissions for a storytelling competition or tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Click on the "Start" button** to initiate the form.
2. **Enter your participant information**:
	* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be identified in the competition.
	* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for communication and verification purposes.
3. **Submit your files**:
	* **File 1**: Upload a file related to your story, such as an image or document.
	* **File 2**: Upload a second file related to your story, such as another image or document.
4. **Write your story entry**: In the **Entry Text** field, provide a short description or summary of your story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Storytelling Tournament Entry Form** (`story_tournament_entry_form`): This is a title label for the form and does not require a specific action.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name to identify you as a participant in the competition.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address for communication and verification purposes.
* **File 1** (`file_upload_1`): Upload a relevant file for your story, such as an image or document.
* **File 2** (`file_upload_2`): Upload a second relevant file for your story, such as another image or document.
* **Entry Text** (`entry_text`): Write a short description or summary of your story in the text area provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure successful form submission.
* Use the correct file types and sizes to ensure proper uploading and viewing of your files.
* Review your entry for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* If you experience any issues during the submission process, contact form administrators for assistance.

Note: This guide is for the Storytelling Tournament Entry Form, and its content and structure may vary based on the specific form requirements and fields.
