# Conditional Logic Document Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Conditional Logic Document Submission" form is designed to gather and manage document submissions while considering individual requirements and circumstances. It's intended to guide users through a structured submission process, ensuring that all necessary information and files are provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Submitter name" field with your name as the person submitting the document.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact email" field for submission confirmation and follow-up purposes.
3. Select the type of document being submitted from the "Document type" dropdown list.
4. If required, provide the "Upload instructions" specific to the selected document type.
5. Enter the "File upload reference" of the uploaded file or its identifier.
6. Optionally, provide a brief description of the document in the "Description of document" field.
7. If assistance is needed with the upload, select "True" in the "Do you require assistance with your upload" field.
8. In the "Additional documents" field, list any other files to be submitted.
9. Read and acknowledge the "Confidentiality notice" about document handling.
10. Optionally, provide a unique "Submission reference" for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the document.
* **Contact email** (2, email, required): Provide your contact email address for submission confirmation and follow-up purposes.
* **Document type** (3, select_one, required): Select the type of document being submitted (e.g., Resume, ID, Certificate, Contract, or Other).
* **Upload instructions** (4, note, required if document type selected): Provide specific instructions for the upload based on the selected document type.
* **File upload reference** (5, text, required): Enter the reference or identifier of the uploaded file.
* **Description of document** (6, text, optional): Briefly describe the uploaded document.
* **Do you require assistance with your upload** (7, select_one, optional): If you need help with the upload, select "True".
* **Additional documents** (8, text, optional): List any other files to be submitted.
* **Confidentiality notice** (9, note, optional): Read and acknowledge the notice about document handling.
* **Submission reference** (10, text, optional): Provide a unique reference for tracking purposes.
