# Fashion Show Portfolio Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Show Portfolio Upload Form is a tool for models to upload and showcase their portfolios, providing a platform for them to share their work with fashion industry professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, which include:
    *   Model Portfolio Upload (1)
    *   Name (4)
    *   Contact Info (5)
    *   Submit (9)
2.  Optional fields include:
    *   Model Details (3)
    *   Email (6)
    *   Portfolio URL (7)
    *   Portfolio Type (8)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Model Portfolio Upload (1)** (`model_portfolio_upload`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your model portfolio.
*   **Upload Portfolio (2)** (`upload_portfolio`, text, required): Upload a file containing your portfolio images or content.
*   **Model Details (3)** (`model_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your model portfolio, such as experience or achievements.
*   **Name (4)** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
*   **Contact Info (5)** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact details, including your address, phone number, and other relevant information.
*   **Email (6)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and contact purposes.
*   **Portfolio URL (7)** (`portfolio_url`, text, optional): If you have a website or online presence, enter the URL for easy access.
*   **Portfolio Type (8)** (`portfolio_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of portfolio you are uploading (fashion, runway, commercial, or editorial).
*   **Submit (9)** (`submit`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether this is an initial or existing portfolio submission.

## Tips
*   Make sure to upload a clear and relevant portfolio that showcases your work and style.
*   Be thorough when filling out the contact information to ensure you can be easily reached.
*   Clearly describe your model details to help industry professionals understand your experience and skills.
*   Use a professional email address for contact purposes.
*   Choose the correct portfolio type to categorize your submission accurately.
