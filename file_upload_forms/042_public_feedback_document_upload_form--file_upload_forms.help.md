# public_feedback_document_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_feedback_document_upload_form is a form designed to collect public feedback from users, allowing them to upload documents and provide comments. This form is public-facing, meaning it is accessible to anyone with a link to the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Upload File" field to select a document from your computer or device.
2. In the "Comment Text" field, type a brief description of the document you uploaded.
3. If you have any supporting documents related to the feedback, click on the "Supporting Documents" field and select them.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, if you want to be contacted about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload File**:
	+ Upload a document from your computer or device to accompany your feedback.
* **Comment Text**:
	+ Type a brief description of the document you uploaded to provide context.
* **Supporting Documents**:
	+ Select any additional documents that are relevant to your feedback.
* **Email Address**:
	+ Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that the document you upload is in a suitable format for review.
* Be concise and clear when providing your comment and email address.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to submit your feedback.
