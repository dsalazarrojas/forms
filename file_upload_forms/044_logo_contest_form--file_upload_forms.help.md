# logo_contest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to upload logos and provide information about a contest. It is meant to be filled out by contest participants who want to submit their logo designs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload your logo file by selecting the correct file type from the "File Type" dropdown menu and uploading your file. You can choose from "Logo", "Image", or "Document" options.
2. Enter the name of the contest you are participating in.
3. Choose a category for your logo submission.
4. Add any relevant tags to your submission.
5. Provide a brief description of your logo.
6. If you want to upload a different logo, you can do so by providing the URL of the logo.
7. Enter the alt text for your logo, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Logo** (`logo_file`, text, required): Upload your logo file by selecting the correct file type from the "File Type" dropdown menu.
* **Contest Name** (`contest_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contest you are participating in.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Choose a category for your logo submission.
* **Tag** (`tag`, text, required): Add any relevant tags to your submission.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your logo.
* **File Type** (`file_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of file you are uploading (Logo, Image, or Document).
* **Upload Logo** (`upload_logo`, text, optional): This field is not necessary. If you want to upload a different logo, you can do so by providing the URL of the logo.
* **Logo URL** (`logo_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the logo you want to upload.
* **Logo Alt** (`logo_alt`, text, optional): Enter the alt text for your logo, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload a high-quality logo that is relevant to the contest you are participating in.
* Use the correct file type for your logo (Logo, Image, or Document).
* Enter accurate and concise information in the "Contest Name" field.
* Use relevant categories and tags to help your submission be easily found.
* Keep your "Description" brief and to the point.
* Use the "Logo URL" field only if you want to upload a different logo.
* Enter the alt text for your logo if it is a graphical image.
