# Vehicle Damage Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Damage Discharge Form is used to document vehicle damage incidents, providing a structured way for claimants to report details about their vehicle's condition after a collision or other incident.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out this form completely and accurately** with the required information for your vehicle and yourself as the claimant.
2. **Provide images** of the damaged vehicle, if applicable.
3. **Make sure to include any supporting documentation**, such as photos or videos, that help illustrate the damage.
4. **Submit the form** once all fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Registration Number**: This is the number assigned to your vehicle by the government and is usually found on the vehicle's registration certificate. It helps identify your vehicle and ensures that we have the correct information about your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: If you know the make of your vehicle, please provide it here. If not, it's okay to leave this field blank.
* **Vehicle Model**: If you know the model of your vehicle, please provide it here. If not, it's okay to leave this field blank.
* **Vehicle Year**: The year of manufacture of your vehicle. Please enter a 2 or 4 digit number.
* **Vehicle License Plate**: The number plate of your vehicle, which helps identify it.
* **Damage Description**: Please describe the damage to your vehicle in as much detail as possible, including any injuries or other effects it may have had on you or others.
* **Damage Image**: If you have images of the damage, please upload them here. This will help us better understand the extent of the damage.
* **Vehicle Damage Insurer**: The name of the insurance company that covers your vehicle. If you don't know this information, it's okay to leave this field blank.
* **Claimant Contact Name**: Your name and contact information, which we'll use to get in touch with you about your claim.
* **Claimant Email**: Your email address, which we'll use to communicate with you about your claim.
* **Claimant Phone**: Your phone number, which we'll use to get in touch with you about your claim.
* **Vehicle Year of Manufacture**: The year your vehicle was manufactured. If you don't know this information, it's okay to leave this field blank.
* **Vehicle Make Year**: The year your vehicle was produced. If you don't know this information, it's okay to leave this field blank.
* **Vehicle Color**: The color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type**: The type of vehicle you own.
* **Vehicle Body Type**: The type of vehicle body your vehicle has.
* **Vehicle Engine Number**: The engine number of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Damage Details**: Additional information about the damage to your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Damage Location**: The location where the damage occurred.
* **Vehicle Damage Date**: The date of the damage.
* **Vehicle Damage Time**: The time of the damage.
* **Vehicle Damage Image Upload**: If you have more images of the damage, please upload them here.
* **Vehicle Insurer Details**: If you have any additional information about your insurer, please provide it here.
* **Vehicle Insurer Phone**: If you have the phone number of your insurer, please provide it here.

Please complete this form carefully and accurately, providing as much information as possible to help us process your claim efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
