# Book Spine Contest Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Spine Contest Submission form is intended for participants to submit their book spine design entries for evaluation. It is essential to review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all required fields are accurately filled out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Full Name** in the **Full Name** field. This is a required field and will be used to contact you regarding contest updates.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** in the **Email Address** field. This is also a required field and will be used to contact you about contest-related matters.
3.  Provide your **Phone Number** in the **Phone Number** field, although this is not required.
4.  Select your **Age Group** from the drop-down menu. This is a required field that will help us categorize your submission.
5.  Enter the **Title of the Book** you are submitting in the **Which Book Spine Will You Design** field. This is a required field as it pertains to the submission.
6.  Enter the **Author of the Book** in the **Book Author** field. This is also a required field.
7.  Choose the **Design Medium** used in creating your book spine design from the drop-down menu. This is a required field and will help us understand the submission process.
8.  Briefly describe your **Design Inspiration** in the **Design Inspiration** field. This is a required field and will be used to evaluate your submission.
9.  Upload a clear **Spine Design Photo** in the **Upload Spine Design Photo** field. This is a required field and will be used for evaluation.
10.  Upload a photo showing the **Book Title and Your Name** in the **Upload Title Card or Description** field. This is a required field and is necessary for proper submission evaluation.
11.  Provide the **Design Dimensions** of your book spine in the **Design Dimensions** field, although this is not required.
12.  Confirm that you have read and accept the **Contest Rules** in the **I Agree to the Contest Rules** field. This is a required field and is necessary for participation.
13.  Select whether you grant permission to **Display Your Work** at the library in the **Permission to Display Work** field. This is a required field.
14.  Add any **Additional Notes** about your submission in the **Additional Notes** field, which is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name in this field to help us contact you about the contest.
*   **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in this field to receive contest updates.
*   **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you regarding the contest.
*   **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Choose your age category from the given options.
*   **School or Organization** (`school_organization`, `text`, optional): If you are submitting as part of a school or organization, enter the name in this field.
*   **Which Book Spine Will You Design** (`book_title_choice`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the book you are designing the spine for.
*   **Book Author** (`book_author`, `text`, required): Enter the author of the book you are submitting.
*   **Design Medium** (`design_medium`, `select_one`, required): Choose the medium you used for your book spine design.
*   **Design Inspiration** (`design_inspiration`, `text`, required): Briefly describe what inspired your design.
*   **Upload Spine Design Photo** (`spine_design_image`, `text`, required): Upload a photo of your book spine design.
*   **Upload Title Card or Description** (`book_title_card`, `text`, required): Upload a photo showing the book title and your name.
*   **Design Dimensions** (`design_dimensions`, `text`, optional): Enter the dimensions of your book spine design if you feel it's necessary.
*   **I Agree to the Contest Rules** (`contest_rules_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and accept the contest rules.
*   **Permission to Display Work** (`permission_to_display`, `select_one`, required): Select whether we can display your work at the library.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about your submission if you want to. 

## Tips

*   Please review the form carefully before submission to avoid any duplication of entries.
*   Ensure all required fields are accurately filled out.
*   Upload clear and relevant images to support your submission.
*   Follow the submission guidelines for proper evaluation.
*   Read and understand the contest rules before submitting your entry.
