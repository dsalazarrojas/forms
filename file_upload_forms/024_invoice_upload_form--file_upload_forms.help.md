> # Invoice Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to upload their invoices for processing. It is designed to gather essential details about the invoice, including the uploaded file, description, and upload date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "File" field to select and upload your invoice document.
2. Enter a brief description of the invoice in the "Description" field.
3. If required, enter the "Date" and "Upload Time" fields for specific timestamping.
4. Optionally, enter the "Uploaded By" field with your name or initials for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **File** (`file`, text): Select and upload your invoice document from your computer or device. Ensure the file is in a supported format.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date of the invoice. This is an optional field, but if you need to specify a date for the invoice, enter it here.
* **Description** (`description`, text): Provide a brief description of the invoice, helping us understand the contents.
* **Uploaded By** (`uploaded_by`, text): Enter your name or initials for tracking purposes.
* **Upload Time** (`upload_time`, time): Enter the time you uploaded the invoice. This is an optional field, but if you need to track the timestamp, fill it out.
* **Upload Date** (`upload_date`, date): The upload date is automatically populated based on the system date. This field is for information purposes only and should not be edited.
