# Advertisement And Article Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advertisement And Article Upload Form is a submission mechanism for uploading advertisements and articles. Fill out this form to share your content with us for review and publication consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address to identify you as the submitter.
2. Select the category or tags that best describe your content.
3. Choose the type of content you are uploading (e.g., image, text, etc.).
4. Enter a brief title and a requested publication date for your content.
5. Optionally, provide additional information, such as a link to a large file or a shareable link.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email Address** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact and notification purposes.
* **Organization / Agency** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you represent an organization, enter its name.
* **Type of Content** (`content_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of content you are uploading (e.g., image, text, etc.).
* **Title / Headline** (`title_headline`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title for your content.
* **Requested Publication Date** (`publication_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date when you would like your content to be published.
* **Category / Tags** (`category_tags`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select relevant categories for your content.
* **Link to File (Dropbox/Drive)** (`file_upload_link`, `text`, optional): If your file is large, provide a shareable link to it.
* **Article Text (if applicable)** (`content_body`, `text`, optional): If you are uploading text content, paste it directly in this field.
* **Editor Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Add notes for the editorial team if needed.
* **Terms of Submission** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have the rights to publish your content.
