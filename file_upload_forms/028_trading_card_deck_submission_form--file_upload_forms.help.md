# trading_card_deck_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trading card submission form is designed to collect information from players who wish to submit their trading card decks for a tournament, league, or showcase. This form helps the organizers and administrators of these events to gather the necessary details to review and verify the submitted decks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed:
	* **Tournament Name**: Enter the name of the tournament in which you are submitting your deck.
	* **League or Showcase**: Select the type of event for which you are submitting your deck: League, Showcase, or Tournament.
	* **Deck Cards**: List the cards in your deck, separated by commas.
	* **Player Name**: Enter your player name.
	* **Deck Image**: Upload a relevant image of your deck.
	* **Player Image**: Upload a relevant image of yourself (if applicable).
	* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your deck.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Tournament Name**
* Label: Tournament Name
* Type: text
* Required: false
* Explanation: Enter the name of the tournament in which you are submitting your deck. This helps the organizers to identify the event and match your deck to the correct tournament.

### **League or Showcase**
* Label: League or showcase
* Type: select_one
* Required: false
* Explanation: Select the type of event for which you are submitting your deck: League, Showcase, or Tournament. This helps the organizers to filter and categorize the submissions.

### **Deck Cards**
* Label: Deck Cards
* Type: text
* Required: false
* Explanation: List the cards in your deck, separated by commas. Please be as detailed as possible and include card names, numbers, and any other relevant information.

### **Player Name**
* Label: Player Name
* Type: text
* Required: false
* Explanation: Enter your player name. This will be used to credit your submission and identify you as the player.

### **Deck Image**
* Label: Deck Image
* Type: text
* Required: false
* Explanation: Upload a relevant image of your deck to help the organizers review and verify your submission.

### **Player Image**
* Label: Player Image
* Type: text
* Required: false
* Explanation: Upload a relevant image of yourself (if applicable) to help the organizers get a better understanding of the player behind the deck.

### **Notes**
* Label: Notes
* Type: text
* Required: false
* Explanation: Add any additional notes or comments about your deck, such as deck philosophy, strategy, or any other information that may be relevant to the organizers and judges.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when listing your deck cards.
* Use commas to separate each card, for example: "Card 1, Card 2, Card 3, etc."
* Make sure to upload relevant images to help the organizers review and verify your submission.
