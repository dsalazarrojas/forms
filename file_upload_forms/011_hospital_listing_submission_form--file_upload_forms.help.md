# Hospital Listing Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Listing Submission Form is used for healthcare professionals and organizations to list their hospitals for various medical services and facilities. This form is intended to gather information about individual hospitals, and it's recommended to complete it only once for each hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about your hospital.
2. Upload a file (if necessary) to support your submission.
3. Provide your contact details.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your hospital listing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your hospital.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, optional): If you have any supporting documents or files to attach with your submission, use this field to upload them.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your hospital listing.
