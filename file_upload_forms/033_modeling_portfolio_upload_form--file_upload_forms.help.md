# Modeling Portfolio Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user-provided modeling portfolios for evaluation and review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
- Fill in the required information: **title** (`title`, text, required). Please enter the title of your modeling portfolio.
- Upload your portfolio: **upload_file** (`upload_file`, text, optional). You can attach files of any format, including PDF, DOCX, or images.
- Enter your name: **name** (`name`, text, optional). Please enter your name as it would appear on your portfolio.
- Enter your email: **email** (`email`, email, optional). Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
- Describe your portfolio: **portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional). Please provide a brief description of your portfolio.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **upload_file**: This field allows you to upload your modeling portfolio. You can attach files of any format, including PDF, DOCX, or images. Please note that file size and type are restricted.
* **name**: This field is optional, but please enter your name as it would appear on your portfolio. This will help us to identify you correctly.
* **email**: This field is optional, but please enter your email address for contact purposes. We will use this to get in touch with you about your portfolio.
* **portfolio**: This field is optional, but please provide a brief description of your portfolio. This will help us to understand your work and provide better evaluation.
