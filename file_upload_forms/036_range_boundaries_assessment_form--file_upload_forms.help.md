# Range Boundaries Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Range Boundaries Assessment Form is designed to collect data on range boundaries for a specific geographic area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please upload any relevant files associated with your assessment, such as images, documents, or videos. You can do this in the designated "File Upload" fields.
* In the "Range Boundaries Observed" field, provide a text description of the range boundaries you've observed.
* In the "Boundary Clarity" field, rate the clarity of the range boundaries you've observed, using a scale or your best judgment.
* In the "Observations" field, provide any additional observations or comments related to the range boundaries.
* In the "Notes" field, add any additional notes or comments that might be relevant to your assessment.
