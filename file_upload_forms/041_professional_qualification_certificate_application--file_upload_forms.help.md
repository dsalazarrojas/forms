# professional-qualification-certificate-application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an application for a professional qualification certificate. Please fill out the form carefully to provide all necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Upload the required file as per the guidelines in the "File Upload" section.
2. Fill out the "Name" field with your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your "Date of Birth" in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your valid "Email" address that is associated with your identification documents.
5. Provide your "Phone" number for contact purposes.
6. Enter your "Address" as it appears on your identification documents.
7. Enter the "Country of Issuance" where the certification was issued.
8. Enter the name of the "Issuing Organization" that issued the certification.
9. Specify the "Verification Status" of your certification (e.g., verified, pending, revoked).
10. If required, provide additional "Verification Requests" in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **File Upload** (File Upload, text, required): Upload the file containing your professional qualification certificate or relevant documents as per the guidelines provided. Please ensure the file is in a supported format and meets the required size and content requirements.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your identification documents.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Country of Issuance** (Country of Issuance, text, required): Enter the country where the certification was issued.
* **Issuing Organization** (Issuing Organization, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization that issued the certification.
* **Verification Status** (Verification Status, text, required): Specify the current status of your certification (e.g., verified, pending, revoked).
* **Verification Requests** (Verification Requests, text, optional): If required, provide additional verification requests in this text field.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues with the verification process.
* Upload a file that meets the required format and size specifications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authority or department for assistance.
