# Insurance Payment Proof Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information related to insurance payments, including policy details, proof of loss, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant insurance type from the options provided.
2. Enter the policy number.
3. Provide the policy owner's name.
4. Enter the date of the incident.
5. Enter the date of the injury.
6. Enter the name of the insurance company.
7. Describe the proof of loss.
8. Provide any additional proof of claim if required.
9. Describe the proof of settlement.
10. Enter any other relevant information.
11. Select the total amount in the currency of your choice.
12. Choose your payment method.
13. Enter the date and time of payment.
14. Enter any additional details about your payment method.
15. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Insurance Type** (`user_input_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of insurance that this payment is for.
* **Policy Number** (`user_input_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the policy number associated with this payment.
* **Policy Owner** (`user_input_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the policy owner.
* **Date of Incident** (`user_input_4`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the incident related to this payment.
* **Date of Injury** (`user_input_5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the injury related to this payment.
* **Insurance Company** (`user_input_6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the insurance company that this payment is for.
* **Proof of Loss** (`user_input_7`, `text`, required: false): Describe the proof of loss related to this payment.
* **Proof of Claim** (`user_input_8`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional proof of claim if required for this payment.
* **Proof of Settlement** (`user_input_9`, `text`, required: false): Describe the proof of settlement related to this payment.
* **Other** (`user_input_10`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other information related to this payment.
* **Other Details** (`user_input_11`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details related to this payment.
* **Other Documents** (`user_input_12`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other documents related to this payment.
* **Other Information** (`user_input_13`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other information related to this payment.
* **Other Date** (`user_input_14`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of any other information related to this payment.
* **Other Time** (`user_input_15`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of any other information related to this payment.
* **Other Amount** (`user_input_16`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount of this payment in your preferred currency.
* **Other Comments** (`user_input_17`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments related to this payment.
* **Total Amount** (`user_input_18`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount of this payment in your preferred currency.
* **Total Amount Currency** (`user_input_19`, `text`, required: false): Enter the currency of the total amount.
* **Payment Method** (`user_input_20`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the method of payment used for this payment.
* **Payment Date** (`user_input_21`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Time** (`user_input_22`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of payment.
* **Payment Method Details** (`user_input_23`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about the payment method used.
* **Payment Method** (`user_input_24`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the payment method used.
* **Submit** (`user_input_25`, `text`, required: false): Click "Submit" to complete and submit this form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact our support team.
