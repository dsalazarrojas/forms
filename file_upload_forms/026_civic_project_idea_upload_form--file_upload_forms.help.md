# Civic Project Idea Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect project details and proposer information for civic project proposals. It guides the submitter through a step-by-step process to provide all necessary information for proposal evaluation and consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your name and email address to ensure we can get in touch with you for further project updates and questions.
2. Enter the title of your proposed project.
3. Specify the impacted neighborhood or area for your project.
4. Describe the community problem your project addresses.
5. Provide a detailed project description and solution.
6. List potential project partners or stakeholders.
7. Estimate the budget required for your project.
8. Specify the expected project duration.
9. Upload proposal documents and images, including using the project title as the subject line.
10. Confirm that you understand that your proposal may be made public.
11. Select the date your proposal was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (proposer_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (proposer_email): Enter your valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number** (proposer_phone): Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes.
* **Proposed Project Title** (project_title): Enter a clear and concise title for your project.
* **Impacted Neighborhood or Area** (project_location): Describe the specific area where your project will take place.
* **What community problem does this project address?** (community_problem): Describe the problem your project seeks to address.
* **Detailed Project Description/Solution** (proposed_solution): Provide a detailed description of your project and how it addresses the identified problem.
* **Potential Partners or Stakeholders** (project_partners): List any local businesses, schools, or nonprofits that may be involved in your project.
* **Estimated Budget Requirement** (budget_estimate_range): Select the estimated budget range for your project from the provided options.
* **Estimated Project Duration** (project_timeline): Enter the expected project duration in months or years.
* **Note - Please send detailed proposal documents and images to proposals-civic.gov** (file_upload_note): This is a note for uploading proposal documents and images.
* **I understand that this proposal may be made public** (agreement_public_record): Confirm that you understand that your proposal may be shared publicly.
* **Date of Proposal** (submission_date): Select the date you submitted your proposal.
