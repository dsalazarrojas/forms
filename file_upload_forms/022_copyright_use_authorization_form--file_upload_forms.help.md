# copyright_use_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The copyright_use_authorization_form is a form used to collect and verify copyright information for items that need to be uploaded to the platform. This form helps ensure that the correct copyright holder and license details are recorded accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the correct copyright holder's name, email address, and other relevant contact information.
2. If applicable, fill out the copyright license details, including the start, end, and expiration dates.
3. Make sure to fill out the correct renewal fee and currency if the license is due for renewal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Copyright Holder** (`copyright_holder`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the copyright holder.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the copyright holder.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of the copyright holder.
* **Copyright Holder Email** (`copyright_holder_email`, text, required: false): Enter the email of the copyright holder.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter the organization name associated with the copyright holder.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the copyright holder.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where the copyright holder is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where the copyright holder is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of the copyright holder.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the country of the copyright holder.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, required: false): Enter the department name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, required: false): Enter the fax number of the contact person.
* **Copyright License Number** (`copyright_license_number`, number, required: false): Enter the copyright license number.
* **Copyright License Type** (`copyright_license_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the copyright license is applicable.
* **Start Date of Copyright** (`copyright_license_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the copyright license.
* **End Date of Copyright** (`copyright_license_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the copyright license.
* **Expiration Date of Copyright** (`copyright_license_expiration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the expiration date of the copyright license.
* **Renewal Date of Copyright** (`copyright_license_renewal_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the copyright license renewal.
* **Renewal Notice Date of Copyright** (`copyright_license_renewal_notice_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the copyright license renewal notice.
* **Renewal Fee of Copyright** (`copyright_license_renewal_fee`, number, required: false): Enter the renewal fee of the copyright license.
* **Renewal Fee Currency** (`copyright_license_renewal_fee_currency`, text, required: false): Enter the currency of the renewal fee.
