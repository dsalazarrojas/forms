# Band Media Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Band Media Upload Form is a tool designed to streamline the process of sharing media assets and information with event organizers, promoters, and booking agents. This form helps ensure that all required details are provided in a clear and concise manner, facilitating a smooth communication process between the band and the intended parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the Band Name field with the legal name of the band for contract and promotional purposes.
2.  Provide the name and contact information of the primary contact person for communication and coordination.
3.  Enter the email address of the contact person for communication and follow-up.
4.  Add public links for each of the requested media assets, such as press photos, electronic press kits, stage plots, and technical riders.
5.  Complete the social media and streaming links as applicable.
6.  If necessary, add any additional media information or instructions not covered in the provided fields.
7.  Review and agree to the content usage authorization at the end of the form to confirm understanding of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Band Name** (`band_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the legal name of the band for contracts and promotional purposes.
*   **Contact Person** (`primary_contact`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the person who will be the primary contact for communication and coordination.
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter the email address of the contact person for communication and follow-up.
*   **High-Resolution Press Photo Link** (`press_photo_link`, `text`, **required**): Provide a link to a high-resolution press photo with a minimum of 300 DPI.
*   **Electronic Press Kit (EPK) or Bio PDF Link** (`band_bio_link`, `text`, **required**): Share a detailed history and career highlights of the band in a PDF format or link.
*   **Stage Plot and Input List Link** (`stage_plot_link`, `text`, **required**): Offer a link to the stage plot and input list for sound engineering preparation.
*   **Technical Rider Link** (`tech_rider_link`, `text`, **optional**): List the required equipment and backline in a technical rider document.
*   **Hospitality Rider Link** (`hospitality_rider_link`, `text`, **optional**): Provide a link to the list of catering and dressing room requirements.
*   **Official Website** (`website_url`, `text`, **optional**): Share the official website of the band.
*   **Spotify Artist Profile Link** (`spotify_link`, `text`, **optional**): Share the Spotify profile link of the band.
*   **Instagram Handle** (`instagram_handle`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the Instagram handle of the band.
*   **Additional Media Information** (`additional_media_notes`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any additional media information or special instructions.
*   **Content Usage Authorization** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Agree to the usage of the provided media for promotional purposes, confirm the band owns the rights to all provided media, and confirm agreement to be contacted regarding upcoming opportunities.

## Tips

*   Ensure all media assets are public links for easy access.
*   Double-check all information for accuracy and completeness before submission.
*   Review the content usage authorization to understand terms of media usage and sharing.
