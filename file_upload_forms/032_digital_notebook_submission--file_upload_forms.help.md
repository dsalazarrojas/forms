# Digital Notebook Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Notebook Submission form is designed for students to upload their digital notebooks for review and assessment by teachers. It ensures a structured submission process, reducing the likelihood of duplicated efforts and incorrect submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form sections and ensure you have all required information ready.
* Select the correct option for "File Uploads" to indicate if you have uploaded files or not.
* Fill in the necessary fields: "student_name", "teacher_name" and "teacher_email" if applicable.
* If you have uploaded files, ensure to enter the correct file paths in the "uploaded_files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **File Uploads (select_multiple)**: Choose 'Yes' if you have uploaded files to be reviewed, or 'No' if you don't have any files to submit.
* **Uploaded Files (text)**: Type in the paths of the files you have uploaded for review.
* **Student Name (text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Teacher Name (text)**: Enter the name of your teacher who is reviewing your notebooks.
* **Teacher Email (text)**: Enter your teacher's email address for further contact.
* **Teacher Phone (text)**: Enter your teacher's phone number for further contact (optional).

## Tips
* Double-check the fields for accuracy to avoid submission errors.
* Ensure you have the correct file paths for uploaded files.
* If you have any issues or doubts, contact your teacher or system administrator for assistance.
