# Anthology Submission Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from authors and writers submitting their works for consideration in an anthology. The collected information will be used to review and evaluate the submission for inclusion in the anthology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your legal name in the "Legal Name" field.
2.  If you use a pen name, enter it in the "Pen Name (if different)" field.
3.  Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4.  Write a brief bio about yourself in the "Author Biography" field (max 150 words).
5.  Enter the title of your work in the "Title of Work" field.
6.  Select the primary genre of your work from the "Primary Genre" dropdown menu.
7.  Enter the approximate word count of your work in the "Approximate Word Count" field.
8.  Provide a brief summary or abstract of your work in the "Brief Summary/Abstract" field.
9.  Select the original file format of your work from the "Original File Format" dropdown menu.
10. If your work has been previously published, mark "True" in the "Has this work been previously published?" field.
11. If your work has been previously published, provide the publication details in the "If yes - where was it published?" field.
12. Finally, select the relevant option from the "I agree to the anthology submission terms" field to indicate your agreement to the submission terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Name** (`author_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Pen Name (if different)** (`pen_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a pen name, enter it here.
* **Contact Email** (`author_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Author Biography** (`author_bio`, `text`, required, max 150 words): Write a brief bio about yourself.
* **Title of Work** (`work_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of your work.
* **Primary Genre** (`primary_genre`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary genre of your work from the dropdown menu: Science Fiction, Fantasy, Mystery, Horror, Literary Fiction, Non-Fiction.
* **Approximate Word Count** (`word_count`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate word count of your work.
* **Brief Summary/Abstract** (`work_summary`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary or abstract of your work.
* **Original File Format** (`file_format`, `select_one`, required): Select the original file format of your work from the dropdown menu: .doc / .docx, .pdf, .rtf, .txt.
* **Has this work been previously published?** (`previously_published`, `select_one`, required): Mark "True" if your work has been previously published.
* **If yes - where was it published?** (`publication_history`, `text`, optional): If your work has been previously published, provide the publication details.
* **I agree to the anthology submission terms** (`terms_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the relevant option from the dropdown menu to indicate your agreement to the submission terms.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have read and understood the anthology submission terms and conditions before proceeding. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the anthology administrators.
