# Regulatory Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect regulatory data and other relevant information related to file uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete all required fields accurately and consistently.
- Check that your data doesn't already exist in our database to avoid duplication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, text, required/optional): This section is for uploading files related to regulatory data. Please select the relevant file(s) according to the provided options.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name or relevant identifier.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information for follow-up or any questions you may have.
* **Regulatory Information** (`regulatory_info`, select_one, optional): Select the relevant regulatory information related to your upload(s).
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable compliance status for your upload(s).
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Please provide any supporting documents relevant to your regulatory data.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to make regarding your upload(s).
* **Update Date** (`update_date`, date, optional): If you're updating existing data, please select the date of the update.
* **Upload Date** (`upload_date`, date, optional): If you're uploading new data, please select the date of upload.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Regulatory Data** (`regulatory_data`, text, optional): Please enter or paste the relevant regulatory data.
* **Regulatory Updates** (`regulatory_updates`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant regulatory updates.
* **Audit Info** (`audit_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional audit information.
