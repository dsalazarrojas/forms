# Music Sound Engineer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for music sound engineers to provide their contact information, professional background, and availability for interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your category from the options provided.
2. Enter a brief description of your background and experience.
3. Upload your resume.
4. Share your portfolio.
5. Provide audio samples for review.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
6. Select your availability for interviews.
7. Choose your payment terms.
8. Select a date and time for the interview.
9. Choose a location for the interview.
10. Provide any additional information that you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): Select the category that best describes your profession (e.g., Sound Engineer, Music Producer, DJ).
* **Description** (`description`, note): Enter a brief description of your background and experience in the music sound engineering field.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text): Upload your resume for review.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text): Share your portfolio with us, including any relevant work samples.
* **Audio Samples** (`audio_samples`, text): Provide audio samples for review.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text): Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Website** (`website`, text): Enter your website URL, if applicable.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple): Select your social media profiles (e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram).
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple): Select your work experience (e.g., Studio Technician, Freelance Music Engineer, Music Director).
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, select_multiple): Select your educational background (e.g., Degree in Music Engineering, Bachelor of Music Technology).
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple): Select your skills and software proficiency (e.g., Ableton, Logic, Pro Tools, Adobe Audition).
* **Certification** (`certification`, select_multiple): Select any relevant certifications (e.g., Certified Sound Engineer, Certified Audio Specialist).
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple): Select your availability for interviews.
* **Rate** (`rate`, number): Enter your hourly rate.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, select_multiple): Select your available work schedule (e.g., 8am-12pm, 12pm-4pm, 4pm-8pm, 8pm-12am).
* **Vacation Days** (`vacation_days`, number): Enter the number of vacation days you are willing to take.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, select_one): Select the notice period for your employment (e.g., 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 120 days).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple): Select your payment terms (e.g., Pay Per Hour, Project Basis, Hourly, Flat Fee).
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date): Select the date for the interview.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time): Select the time for the interview.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text): Enter the location for the interview.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note): Provide any additional information that you would like to share.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your resume and portfolio accurately reflect your experience and qualifications.
* Be prepared to provide detailed answers to any questions related to your background and availability.
* If you have any special requests or accommodations, please let us know in the additional information field.
