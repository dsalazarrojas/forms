# Client Asset Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Asset Upload Form is designed to collect project assets from clients and enable the agency to understand their requirements and usage constraints for these assets.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your project name and client name.
2. Provide your email address as the submitter.
3. Select the type of assets being provided (e.g., logos, branding guidelines, images, etc.).
4. Describe the assets and their purpose.
5. Specify any usage instructions or constraints for the assets.
6. Confirm that you authorize the agency to use the assets for the specified project.
7. Confirm that you own the rights to the assets or have permission to use them.
8. Confirm whether the uploaded assets are final, production-ready.
9. Choose how the assets are being provided (e.g., file upload, cloud link, etc.).
10. Enter the date of submission.
11. Add any additional comments for the creative team (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name / ID**: Enter the name of your project to help us identify it.
* **Client Name**: Enter your client's name to associate the assets with their company or organization.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Type of Assets Being Provided**: Choose the type of assets you are uploading (e.g., logos, branding guidelines, images, etc.).
* **Description and Purpose of Assets**: Describe the assets and their intended use.
* **Specific Usage Instructions or Constraints**: Provide any specific usage rules or limitations for the assets.
* **I authorize the agency to use these assets for the specified project**: Confirm that you grant permission for the agency to use the assets.
* **I confirm that I own the rights to these assets or have permission to use them**: Confirm your ownership or permission to use the assets.
* **Is this the final, production-ready version?**: Confirm whether the uploaded assets are final and production-ready.
* **How are the assets being provided?**: Choose how the assets are being provided (e.g., file upload, cloud link, etc.).
* **Asset Link (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.)**: If you are providing a link to the assets, ensure that permissions are set to 'Anyone with the link can view'.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Additional Comments for the Creative Team**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the creative team (optional).
