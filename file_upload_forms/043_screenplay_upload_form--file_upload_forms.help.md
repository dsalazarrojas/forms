# Screenplay Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Screenplay Upload Form is a tool for users to upload and manage their screenplays. This form is intended for internal use and is not publicly accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Upload Your Script**: Enter the title of your screenplay in the "Upload Your Script" field.
2. **Enter Author Information**: Enter your first and last name in the "Author First Name" and "Author Last Name" fields.
3. **Title and Categorization**: Enter the title of your screenplay and select the category and genre that best describe it.
4. **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. **File Uploads**: Upload any supporting files you have for your screenplay, such as images or documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Your Script**: This field is where you can enter the title of your screenplay.
* **Author First Name**: This field is for entering your first name.
* **Author Last Name**: This field is for entering your last name.
* **Title**: This field is for entering the title of your screenplay.
* **Category**: This field is a select-one field where you can choose a category for your screenplay.
* **Genre**: This field is a select-multiple field where you can choose multiple genres for your screenplay.
* **Email**: This field is for entering your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is for entering your phone number.
* **File 1**: This field is for uploading a file to support your screenplay.
* **File 2**: This field is for uploading another file to support your screenplay.
* **File 3**: This field is for uploading a third file to support your screenplay.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your screenplay can be properly processed.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out fields.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your internal support team for assistance.
