# Product Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Product Donation Request Form** is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations requesting product donations. The form is used to gather basic information about the donor, the product being donated, shipping details, and pickup preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To fill out the form, follow these steps:
	* Scroll through each page to find the relevant fields.
	* Type in your details for each field as prompted.
2. The form has several sections, each with its own page. Make sure to complete all the required fields for each section.
3. If a field is marked as optional, you can leave it blank if you do not have the information or prefer not to provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Basic Information (Page 1: Basic Information)
* **Title (Text)** (Required): Enter your name or the name of the organization making the request.
* **Description (Text)** (Optional): Provide a brief description of your request, including any relevant details.

### Donor Information (Page 2: Donor Information)
* **Name (Text)** (Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your records.
* **Email Address (Text)** (Required): Enter your valid email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number (Text)** (Optional): Enter your phone number for easy communication.

### Product Information (Page 3: Product Information)
* **Product Name (Text)** (Required): Specify the name of the product you are requesting.
* **Product Description (Text)** (Optional): Provide a brief description of the product you are requesting.
* **Quantity (Text)** (Required): Enter the quantity of the product you are requesting.

### Shipping Details (Page 4: Shipping Details)
* **Shipping Address (Text)** (Required): Enter the shipping address where the product will be delivered.
* **Shipping City (Text)** (Required): Enter the city name of the shipping address.
* **Shipping State (Text)** (Required): Enter the state or province of the shipping address.
* **Shipping ZIP/Postal Code (Text)** (Required): Enter the ZIP or postal code of the shipping address.

### Pickup Preferences (Page 5: Pickup Preferences)
* **Pickup Date (Text)** (Required): Select the date you prefer to pick up the donated product.
* **Pickup Time (Text)** (Required): Select the time you prefer to pick up the donated product.

### Donation Request (Page 6: Donation Request)
* **Donation Request (Text)** (Optional): If you have any specific requests or requirements for the donation, you can mention them here.

### Review and Confirmation (Page 7: Review and Confirmation)
* **Review Your Information (Text)** (Required): Check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* **Confirm Donation (Text)** (Required): Confirm that you have provided all required information to complete the form.
