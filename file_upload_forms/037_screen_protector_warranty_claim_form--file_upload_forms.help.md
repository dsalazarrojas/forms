# Screen Protector Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a warranty claim for a screen protector product. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth claims process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before starting.
- Make sure you have all required information readily available.
- Enter your information in the format specified in each field.
- Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information.
- **Customer Address** (text, optional): Enter your address for shipping and contact purposes.
- **Customer Order Number** (number, optional): Enter the order number associated with your purchase. If unsure, contact customer support for assistance.
- **Customer Order Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you purchased the screen protector. If unsure, check your order confirmation email or receipt.
- **Supporting Documentation** (text, optional): Attach any relevant documents or images that support your claim.
- **Select Supporting Documentation** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of supporting documentation you are attaching.
- **Select Supporting Documentation Label** (select_one, optional): Choose the label for your attached supporting documentation.

## Tips
- Be sure to attach clear and legible supporting documentation to aid the claims process.
- If you are unsure about any information, contact customer support for assistance.
