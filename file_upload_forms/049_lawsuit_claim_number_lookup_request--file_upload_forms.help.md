# Lawsuit Claim Number Lookup Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a Lawsuit Claim Number, which is used to identify a specific case in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your contact information, including your name, firm name, phone number, and email address.
* Provide the case number for which you are requesting the Lawsuit Claim Number.
* If available, upload supporting documents related to the case.
* Specify any notes about the case.
* Select the status of the case (Active or Inactive).
* Select whether the case has been uploaded (Yes or No).
* Select the type of claim (Yes or No).
* Select whether you are the submitter (Yes or No).
* Provide the submitter's phone number and email address if you are the submitter.
* Provide the date and time you received the case.
* Finally, submit the form to complete your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your name.
* **Firm Name** (`firm_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your firm's name.
* **Firm Phone** (`firm_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your firm's phone number.
* **Firm Email** (`firm_email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter your firm's email address.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the case number for which you are requesting the Lawsuit Claim Number.
* **Supporting Documents** (`documents`, text, optional): This field is used to upload any supporting documents related to the case.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is used to enter any notes about the case.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date you submitted the request.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): This field is used to enter the time you submitted the request.
* **Lawsuit Claim Number** (`lawsuit_claim_number`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the Lawsuit Claim Number, if available.
* **File Uploaded** (`file_uploaded`, text, optional): This field is used to indicate if a file has been uploaded.
* **Selected Lawsuit Claim** (`selected_lawsuit_claim`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select whether the case has been selected as a Lawsuit Claim.
* **Selected Lawsuit Claim Number** (`selected_lawsuit_claim_number`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the Lawsuit Claim Number, if available.
* **Selected Lawsuit Claim Type** (`selected_lawsuit_claim_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select the type of claim (Yes or No).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select whether you are the submitter (Yes or No).
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the submitter's address.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the submitter's company name.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date you received the case.
* **Time Received** (`time_received`, time, optional): This field is used to enter the time you received the case.
* **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the status of the case (Active or Inactive).
