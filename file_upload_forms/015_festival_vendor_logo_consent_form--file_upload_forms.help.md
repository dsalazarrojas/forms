# Festival Vendor Logo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Vendor Logo Consent Form is a crucial document that allows festival organizers to collect necessary information from vendors for the festival event. By filling out this form, vendors can provide their consent for their logo to be used in festival marketing materials and ensure that their brand assets are correctly represented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Vendor Logo" field with your festival vendor's logo.
2. Enter your "Brand Assets" in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your "Festival Organizer" name or contact information.
4. Choose the "Vendor Permission" option that applies to your situation.
5. Enter your "Vendor Name" and any additional contact information such as "Email" and "Phone Number".
6. Add a brief "Description" of your brand and event.
7. Fill out the "Event Name", "Event Date", "Event Time", and "Festival Location" fields with the relevant information.
8. Provide your "Vendor License" number if applicable.
9. Select the "Submission Date" of when you would like to submit the form.
10. Finally, assign the "Assigned Tool" if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Vendor Logo** (`vendor_logo`, text): Please upload your festival vendor's logo.
- **Brand Assets** (`brand_assets`, text): Enter any additional brand assets that you would like to submit.
- **Festival Organizer** (`festival_organizer`, text): Provide the name or contact information of the festival organizer.
- **Vendor Permission** (`vendor_permission`, select_multiple): Select the permission option that applies to your situation:
	+ Vendor 1
	+ Vendor 2
	+ Vendor 3
- **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text): Enter your festival vendor's name.
- **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Description** (`description`, note): Add a brief description of your brand and event.
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text): Enter the name of the event where your brand will be featured.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date): Enter the date of the event where your brand will be featured.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time): Enter the time of the event where your brand will be featured.
- **Festival Location** (`festival_location`, text): Enter the location of the festival where your brand will be featured.
- **Vendor License** (`vendor_license`, text): Provide your vendor license number if applicable.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date): Select the date when you would like to submit this form.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): Assign the tool you would like to use for this event.
