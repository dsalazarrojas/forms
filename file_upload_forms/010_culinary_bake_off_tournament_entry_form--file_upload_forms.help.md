# Culinary Bake Off Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for contestants to enter a culinary bake off competition, providing information and images of their creations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid email address or other contact information.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* Category: Select up to three categories that best describe your culinary entry.
	* Entry Image 01: Upload a photo of your entry.
	* Entry Image 02: Upload a photo of your entry.
	* Entry Image 03: Upload a photo of your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Category`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to three categories that best describe your culinary entry. These will be used to categorize and filter entries in the competition.
* **Entry Image 01** (`None`, `text`, required): Upload a photo of your entry, ideally in a format like JPEG or PNG.
* **Entry Image 02** (`None`, `text`, required): Upload another photo of your entry.
* **Entry Image 03** (`None`, `text`, required): Upload a third photo of your entry.

## Tips
* Please ensure each category is accurate and not duplicated.
* Use clear, high-quality images to showcase your entry.
* Only upload images in formats supported by the platform.
