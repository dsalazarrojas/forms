# Church Expense Voucher Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Expense Voucher Form is used by church staff to request reimbursement for expenses related to official church activities. Use this form to submit your reimbursement requests for approval and payment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details in the "Expense Voucher Information" section.
2. Select the Ministry/Department that should be charged for the expense in the "Ministry/Department to be Charged" dropdown.
3. Enter the date you requested reimbursement for in the "Date of Request" field.
4. Select the type of expense (e.g., Supplies/Materials, Equipment Purchase, etc.) in the "Type of Expense" dropdown.
5. Enter a brief description of the expense in the "Purpose of Expense" field.
6. Enter the date the expense was incurred in the "Date of Purchase/Expense" field.
7. Enter the total amount to be reimbursed in the "Total Amount to be Reimbursed ($)" field.
8. Indicate if sales tax was paid on the purchase in the "Was sales tax paid on this purchase?" dropdown.
9. Attach digital receipts or confirm that you will provide physical copies in the "Receipts and Documentation" section.
10. If applicable, enter the number of miles driven for mileage claims.
11. Select how you would like to receive payment in the "Reimbursement Method" section.
12. If selecting Electronic/Direct Deposit, enter the last 4 digits of your bank account number.
13. Enter your electronic signature, certifying that the expenses are legitimate.
14. Enter the name of your supervisor who approved this expense.
15. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requested By**: Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Ministry/Department to be Charged**: Select the correct department responsible for the expense (e.g., General Administration, Worship/Music, etc.). This will help the accounting team process your reimbursement request correctly.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date you submitted this reimbursement request.
* **Type of Expense**: Choose the category of expense (e.g., Supplies/Materials, Equipment Purchase, etc.). This helps the accounting team understand the nature of the expense.
* **Date of Purchase/Expense**: Enter the date the expense was incurred.
* **Purpose of Expense**: Enter a brief description of the expense (e.g., VBS craft supplies, piano tuning, youth pizza).
* **Total Amount to be Reimbursed ($)**: Enter the total cost of the expense.
* **Was sales tax paid on this purchase?**: Indicate if sales tax was paid on the purchase.
* **Have you attached digital receipts?**: Attach digital receipts or confirm that you will provide physical copies. Clear photos or PDFs are required.
* **If Mileage - number of miles driven**: If applicable, enter the number of miles driven for mileage claims.
* **How would you like to receive payment?**: Select how you would like to receive your reimbursement (e.g., Paper Check, Electronic/Direct Deposit, etc.).
* **Banking Info (Last 4 of account)**: If selecting Electronic/Direct Deposit, enter the last 4 digits of your bank account number.
* **Requestor's Electronic Signature**: Enter your electronic signature, certifying that the expenses are legitimate.
* **Supervisor Approval (Name)**: Enter the name of your supervisor who approved this expense.
