# Garden Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for clients to provide details about their garden design order. It includes fields to specify the project type, location, contact information, description, and payment details. The form also allows clients to upload relevant files related to the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether a garden design project is applicable to the client by responding to the "project_type" question.
2. Provide the location of the garden design project.
3. Enter the client's contact details, including name, phone number, and email address.
4. Enter a description of the project.
5. Choose a design service package.
6. Enter the total price of the project.
7. Upload relevant files related to the project.
8. Sign the order form digitally.
9. Enter the date and time of signature.
10. Choose a payment method (e.g., cash, credit card, or bank transfer).
11. Confirm the payment status.
12. Enter the payment amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Garden Design Order Form** (text, required): This is the title of the form.
* **project_type** (select_one, optional): Check whether a garden design project is applicable to the client.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the garden design project.
* **client_name** (text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **contact_number** (text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **description** (text, optional): Enter a description of the project.
* **design_service** (text, optional): Select a design service package.
* **package_option** (select_one, optional): Choose a service package for the project.
	+ Options: "Package 1", "Package 2", "Package 3"
* **total_price** (number, optional): Enter the total price of the project.
* **upload_file** (text, optional): Upload relevant files related to the project.
* **signature** (note, optional): Sign the order form digitally.
* **signature_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of signature.
* **signature_time** (time, optional): Enter the time of signature.
* **payment_method** (select_one, optional): Choose a payment method (e.g., cash, credit card, or bank transfer).
	+ Options: "Cash", "Credit Card", "Bank Transfer"
* **payment_status** (select_one, optional): Confirm the payment status.
	+ Options: "Unpaid", "Paid"
* **payment_amount** (number, optional): Enter the payment amount.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled.
* Double-check the accuracy of the provided information before submission.
* Make sure to choose the correct payment method and status.
* Upload relevant and necessary files related to the project.
* Sign the form digitally after completion.
