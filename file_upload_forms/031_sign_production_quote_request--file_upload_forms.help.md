# Sign Production Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for custom sign production. It collects relevant information about the customer's requirements, production details, and production preferences. Please fill out this form to provide accurate information to ensure a smooth and efficient quote process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
2. Specify the production details, such as the type of sign you want to produce, its size, and finish.
3. Choose the production method, color, and material for your sign.
4. Select the requested sign size and type from the provided options.
5. Choose the production location.
6. Provide any additional customer message or production notes.
7. Upload the required files (up to 7 files).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your customer information, including your name and contact details.
* **Production Details** (`production_details`, text, required: false): Describe the production details, such as the type of sign you want to produce.
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your quote request.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, required: false): Upload any relevant files, such as images or documents.
* **Production Method** (`production_method`, select_one, required: true): Choose the production method from the provided options.

* **Production Finish** (`production_finish`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the finish for your sign production.
