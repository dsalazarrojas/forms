# Submit Your Article Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Submit Your Article Form is designed to collect information about your article submission. Please ensure that you are submitting an original work or an adaptation of an existing work that meets the required category. You are free to use and modify this form as you see fit, just give credit to the original creators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Submit Your Article** field with a title that accurately describes your article.
2. Upload a file related to your article using the **File Upload** field.
3. Choose a category that best fits your article from the **Category** dropdown list.
4. Add any relevant tags for your article (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Your Article**: Enter the title of your article in this field. This will be used to identify and categorize your submission.
* **File Upload**: Upload a file related to your article. Please ensure that the file is relevant to the category you choose and is not a duplicate of an existing work. This field is required.
* **Category**: Select a category that best fits your article from the available options. This helps us to organize and process your submission efficiently.
* **Tags**: Add any relevant tags for your article (optional). This will help us to better understand and index your submission.

## Tips
* Please use a unique and accurate title that reflects the content of your article.
* Ensure that the uploaded file is relevant to the category and is not a duplicate of an existing work.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or the category selection, contact us at form@gic.mx for further assistance.
