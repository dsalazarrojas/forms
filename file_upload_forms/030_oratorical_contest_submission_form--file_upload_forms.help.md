# Oratorical Contest Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oratorical Contest Submission Form is designed for participants to submit their entries for an oratorical contest. The form collects participant details, uploaded materials, and event information, ensuring that all necessary data is gathered for the contest organizers to review and evaluate participant submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Details**: Provide your name, any additional details you'd like to share about yourself, and any other relevant information.
2. **Uploaded Materials**: If you have any materials you'd like to attach to your submission (e.g., images, documents, or videos), upload them here.
3. **Event Organizer Details**: Provide the event organizer's information, including their email and phone number.
4. **Participant Statement**: Write a statement about yourself and your submission.
5. **Event Name**: If you're submitting for a specific event, provide the name of the event.
6. **Event Date and Time**: If you're submitting for a specific event, provide the date and time of the event.
7. **Event Organizer Email and Phone**: Provide the event organizer's email and phone number.
8. **Participant Statement Files**: If you have files to attach to your participant statement, you can do so here.
9. **Participant Agreement**: Check the box to agree to participate in the contest.
10. **Event Agreement**: Check the boxes for the statements you agree to regarding the contest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (1)**: Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share about yourself. This field is not required.
* **Uploaded Materials (2)**: If you have any files or materials you'd like to attach to your submission, upload them here. This field is not required.
* **Event Organizer Details (3)**: Enter the event organizer's information, including their email and phone number. This field is not required.
* **Participant Statement (4)**: Write a statement about yourself and your submission. This field is not required.
* **Event Name (5)**: If you're submitting for a specific event, enter the name of the event. This field is not required.
* **Event Date and Time (6)**: If you're submitting for a specific event, enter the date and time of the event. This field is not required.
* **Event Organizer Email (8)**: Enter the event organizer's email. This field is not required.
* **Event Organizer Phone (9)**: Enter the event organizer's phone number. This field is not required.
* **Participant Statement Files (10)**: If you have files to attach to your participant statement, you can do so here. This field is not required.
* **Participant Agreement (11)**: Check the box to agree to participate in the contest. This field is required.
* **Event Agreement (12)**: Check the boxes for the statements you agree to regarding the contest. This field is required.

## Tips

* Ensure that you've reviewed and agreed to the participant and event agreements before submitting your entry.
* Only attach files that are relevant to your submission and event.
* Double-check that all event organizer information is accurate and up-to-date.
