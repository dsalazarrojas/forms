# Bed Bug Case Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bed Bug Case Evaluation Form is designed to collect information from a person reporting a bed bug case. This form helps assess the severity and details of the infestation, as well as gather contact information for follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the "Name of Person Reporting" field and enter your name.
2. Enter your "Contact Email" in the provided field.
3. Enter your "Contact Phone Number" in the provided field.
4. Select the "Property Address" of the infested location.
5. Enter the "Unit or Apartment Number" of the infested location.
6. Select all areas where bed bugs have been observed from the "Specific Area(s) of Infestation" dropdown list.
7. Enter the "Date when activity was first noticed".
8. Select the type(s) of evidence observed from the "Type of Evidence Observed" dropdown list.
9. Select the severity of the infestation from the "Perceived Severity of Infestation" dropdown list.
10. Indicate if any previous treatments have been attempted by selecting "True" or "False".
11. Provide any details of previous treatments in the "Details of previous treatments" field.
12. If you have photos, enter the number of "Evidence Photos" available for upload.
13. Enter any additional observations or notes in the "Additional Observations or Notes" field.
14. Select the "Submission Date" of the form being submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Person Reporting (`reporter_name`)**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone Number (`contact_phone`)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Property Address (`property_address`)**: Enter the address of the infested property.
* **Unit or Apartment Number (`unit_number`)**: Enter the unit or apartment number of the infested location.
* **Specific Area(s) of Infestation (`area_of_infestation`)**: Select all areas where bed bugs have been observed.
* **Date when activity was first noticed (`initial_discovery_date`)**: Enter the date when you first noticed the bed bug activity.
* **Type of Evidence Observed (`evidence_type_observed`)**: Select the type(s) of evidence observed.
* **Perceived Severity of Infestation (`infestation_severity_perception`)**: Select the severity of the infestation from the provided options.
* **Have any previous treatments been attempted? (`previous_treatments_attempted`)**: Indicate if any previous treatments have been attempted.
* **Details of previous treatments (`treatment_history_details`)**: Provide details of previous treatments.
* **Number of Evidence Photos available for upload (`evidence_photos_count`)**: Enter the number of evidence photos available for upload.
* **Additional Observations or Notes (`additional_observations`)**: Enter any additional observations or notes.
* **Submission Date (`evaluation_request_date`)**: Select the date of submission.

Note: The fields marked as required are mandatory and must be completed to submit the form. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
