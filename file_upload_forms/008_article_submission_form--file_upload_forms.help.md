# Article Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect article submission information from authors for publication in a journal or online platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start with the **Author Information** section, providing all required details.
2. Move on to the **Article Information** section and fill in the article title, abstract, category, and word count.
3. In the **File Upload** section, upload your article file in the specified format.
4. Proceed to the **Publication Details** section and disclose any prior publication and provide details.
5. In the **Rights and Licensing** section, confirm your copyright ownership and select the preferred publishing license.
6. Finally, confirm your agreement with the submission terms and conditions and the accuracy of the submitted information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Author Information**
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address for verification and correspondence.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for editorial purposes.
* **Institution or Organization**: Optional. If affiliated, enter your institution or organization name.
* **Author Biography**: Briefly describe your biography for publication purposes.
### **Article Information**
* **Article Title**: Enter the complete and final title of your article.
* **Abstract or Summary**: Provide a 150-300 word summary of your article.
* **Keywords or Tags**: Enter 3-5 relevant keywords or tags for article categorization.
* **Article Category**: Select the primary subject category of your article.
* **Word Count**: Enter the approximate number of words in your article.
### **File Upload**
* **Article File Upload**: Upload your article in DOC, DOCX, PDF, or TXT format.
### **Publication Details**
* **Has this article been published elsewhere?**: Disclose any prior publication.
* **Where and when was it published?**: If previously published, provide details.
### **Rights and Licensing**
* **I own the copyright to this work**: Confirm you hold the copyright.
* **Preferred Publishing License**: Select the license for your article.
### **Terms and Confirmation**
* **I agree to the submission terms and conditions**: Confirm you accept the publishing guidelines.
* **I confirm the accuracy of the submitted information**: By submitting, you certify all information is accurate.
