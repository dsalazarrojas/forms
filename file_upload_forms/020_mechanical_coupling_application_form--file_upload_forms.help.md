# Mechanical Coupling Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Coupling Application Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals or organizations requiring mechanical coupling services. This form helps our team understand your needs and preferences, ensuring a smooth and efficient service experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Requester Information" section.
3. Provide a detailed description of your request in the "Request Details" section.
4. Select the time of your request in the "Request Time" section.
5. Upload any relevant supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" section.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section (though this field is currently not used).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Information**: Please provide your name and email address to help us contact you about your request.
* **Request Details**: Describe your request in as much detail as possible, including any specific requirements or deadlines.
* **Request Time**: Select the time of your request, which will help us schedule our services accordingly.
* **Supporting Documents**: Upload any documents that support your request, such as receipts, invoices, or contracts. This will help us better understand your needs.
* **Phone**: Currently not used, but please enter your phone number for your records.
