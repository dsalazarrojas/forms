# Sneaker Design Contest Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to submit their sneaker design entries for a contest. It will collect information about the participant's artwork, including its type and format, and provide an opportunity for additional details or descriptions to be shared.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field on page 1.
2. On page 2, upload your artwork by clicking on the "Artwork Files" field, and select the relevant file type (2D or 3D) from the dropdown menu.
3. In the "Artwork Description" field, describe your artwork in a few sentences.
4. On page 3, provide more details about your submission, including any additional information you'd like to share about your artwork.
5. On page 4, click on the "Submit" button to complete your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (page 1, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the submission.
* **Artwork Files** (page 2, text, optional): Upload your artwork by selecting the relevant file type (2D or 3D) from the dropdown menu.
* **Artwork Description** (page 2, note, optional): Describe your artwork in a few sentences to provide more context.
* **Submission Details** (page 3, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your submission.
* **Artwork Description 2** (page 4, note, optional): Describe your artwork again, if necessary.
* **Artwork Files 2** (page 4, text, optional): Upload an additional file or image for your artwork.
* **Submission Details 3** (page 4, text, optional): Provide even more details about your submission.
* **File Type (2D or 3D)** (page 4, select, required): Select the type of artwork you are submitting (2D or 3D).
* **File Format** (page 4, select, required): Select the format of your artwork (JPEG or PNG).
* **Submit Button 2** (page 4, text, optional): This is a placeholder button, do not fill in anything here.
* **Submit** (page 1, text, required): Click this button to complete your entry.

## Tips
* Ensure you only submit one unique entry per form.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your artwork.
* Only upload files that are relevant to your submission.
* Make sure to select the correct file type and format for your artwork.
