# Documentary Pitch Submission Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting a documentary pitch to a festival or a production company. It captures essential details about the documentary, its creators, and production team, allowing the applicant to provide a comprehensive and structured submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the submission details (field 1).
2. Confirm whether the pitch is a submission (field 2).
3. Provide the documentary title (field 3) and director details (field 4).
4. Enter the director's name (field 5), email (field 6), and phone number (field 7).
5. Attach any supporting materials (field 8) or upload them (field 9).
6. Indicate whether the documentary has been to a production festival (field 10).
7. Enter the funder details (field 11), funder's name (field 12), email (field 13), and phone number (field 14).
8. Provide a budget breakdown (field 15) and the budget (field 16).
9. Enter the production company (field 18), production team (field 19), location (field 20), contact person (field 21), and notes (field 22).
10. Upload any additional notes (field 23) if necessary.
11. Finally, click the "Submit" button (field 24) to complete the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitting A Documentary Pitch** (`submission_details`): A brief description of the documentary pitch, including the genre, synopsis, and key highlights.
* **Submission** (`submitting_a_documentary_pitch`): Indicate whether this is a submission.
* **Title** (`documentary_title`): Enter the title of the documentary.
* **Director Details** (`director_details`): Provide a brief description of the director's experience and qualifications.
* **Name** (`director_name`): Enter the name of the director.
* **Email** (`director_email`): Enter the director's email address.
* **Phone** (`director_phone`): Enter the director's phone number.
* **Supporting Materials** (`supporting_materials`): Attach any supporting materials, such as a trailer or a press kit.
* **Upload Supporting Materials** (`upload_supporting_materials`): Upload supporting materials if not attached.
* **Production Festival** (`production_festival`): Indicate whether the documentary has been to a production festival.
* **Funder Details** (`funder_details`): Provide a brief description of the funder's involvement.
* **Funder Name** (`funder_name`): Enter the funder's name.
* **Funder Email** (`funder_email`): Enter the funder's email address.
* **Funder Phone** (`funder_phone`): Enter the funder's phone number.
* **Budget Breakdown** (`budget_breakdown`): Provide a breakdown of the budget.
* **Budget** (`budget`): Enter the budget amount.
* **Budget Details** (`budget_details`): Provide additional details about the budget.
* **Production Company** (`production_company`): Enter the production company's name.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`): Enter the production team's name.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the documentary was produced.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional notes about the documentary.
* **Upload Notes** (`upload_notes`): Upload any additional notes.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click to complete the submission.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`): Click to cancel the submission.
