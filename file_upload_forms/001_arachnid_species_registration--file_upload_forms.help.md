# Arachnid Species Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arachnid Species Registration form is designed to collect detailed information about newly discovered arachnid species. This form helps researchers and scientists to properly classify and document the species, ensuring accurate and comprehensive data.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in your information as the researcher who discovered the species.
2.  Enter the complete name of the researcher.
3.  Provide a valid primary contact email address.
4.  Enter the scientific or common name of the species.
5.  Select the date of discovery.
6.  Enter the location where the species was discovered.
7.  Choose the primary habitat type of the species.
8.  Describe the condition of the specimen.
9.  Upload clear photos of the specimen.
10. Upload an optional photo of the habitat.
11. Enter the size of the specimen in millimeters.
12. Describe any distinctive features or markings of the species.
13. Describe any observed behavior.
14. (Optional) Choose a research priority level.
15. Select whether data can be shared with other researchers.
16. Add any additional notes or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Full Name**: Enter your full name as the researcher who discovered the species.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that can be used as the primary contact point for communication.
* **Arachnid Species Name**: Enter the scientific or common name of the species.
* **Date of Discovery**: Select the date when the species was discovered.
* **Discovery Location**: Enter the exact location where the species was discovered.
* **Habitat Type**: Select the primary habitat type of the species, such as forest, grassland, desert, urban, or aquatic.
* **Specimen Condition**: Choose the condition of the specimen, which can be alive, preserved, photographed, or not applicable.
* **Specimen Photos**: Upload clear photos of the specimen to aid in proper documentation.
* **Habitat Photo**: (Optional) Upload a photo of the discovery location, which can be helpful for context.
* **Specimen Size - mm**: Enter the body length of the species in millimeters.
* **Distinctive Features**: Describe any distinctive features or markings of the species.
* **Observed Behavior**: Describe any observed behavior of the species, such as web-building or hunting.
* **Research Priority**: (Optional) Select the research priority level, which can be low, medium, high, or critical.
* **Data Sharing Consent**: Select whether data can be shared with other researchers, with options for 'True', 'False', or 'Ask me first'.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or observations about the species.
