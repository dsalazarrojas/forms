# Dealer Spotlight Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Spotlight Submission Form is designed to collect information from dealers for a spotlight feature. It's a digital form that helps us understand and highlight dealers' profiles, images, videos, and other key details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section with the required and optional information as instructed.
2. Ensure you provide valid and accurate data for each field.
3. Click submit to send the form once you've filled it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dealer Information** (`dealer_info`, text, required: false): This is the general information about the dealer. Provide your name, and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
* **Profile Image** (`profile_image`, text, required: false): Upload a suitable image that represents your dealership's profile.
* **Profile Video** (`profile_video`, text, required: false): Share a video that showcases your dealership's personality or offerings.
* **Dealer Name** (`dealer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your dealership's name.
* **Dealer Address** (`dealer_address`, text, required: false): Provide your dealership's address.
* **Dealer Phone** (`dealer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your dealership's phone number.
* **Dealer Email** (`dealer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your dealership's email address.
* **Dealer Website** (`dealer_website`, text, required: false): Share your dealership's website.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter your company's name if different from your dealership's name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required: false): Provide your company's address.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, required: false): Enter your company's email address.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required: false): Share your company's website.
* **Spotlight Image** (`spotlight_image`, text, required: false): Upload an image for your dealership's spotlight feature.
* **Spotlight Video** (`spotlight_video`, text, required: false): Share a video for your dealership's spotlight feature.
* **Spotlight Text** (`spotlight_text`, text, required: false): Write a brief text about your dealership's spotlight feature.
* **Spotlight Date** (`spotlight_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your dealership's spotlight feature.
* **Spotlight Time** (`spotlight_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your dealership's spotlight feature.
* **Spotlight Note** (`spotlight_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your dealership's spotlight feature.
* **Newsletter** (`newsletter`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you'd like to receive your newsletter, from Monthly, Semi-Monthly, Quarterly, and other options.

Note: The newsletter field is a one-time selection, so choose the frequency that best suits your needs.
