# uk_immigration_permission_to_stay_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UK Immigration Permission to Stay Application Form is used to gather essential information for immigration purposes. This includes details about the applicant's identity, address, contact information, employment, education, travel history, and other relevant information that may affect their application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Introduction: Provide your name, date of birth, and nationality to help us understand your identity.
2. Contact Information: Enter your contact number, email address, and address in the UK.
3. Page 2-3: Fill out the address in the UK and contact number on page 2, and enter your email address on page 3.
4. Employment: Provide details about your employment status, including your occupation and company name.
5. Education: Enter your education details, including your highest qualification and institution name.
6. Work/Travel History: Provide information about your work and travel history, including your job title, company name, and travel dates.
7. Proof of Employment: Attach proof of your employment, such as a letter from your employer.
8. Proof of Education: Attach proof of your education, such as a degree certificate.
9. Proof of Address: Attach proof of your address, such as a utility bill.
10. E-Signature: Sign the form electronically using the E-Signature field.
11. Date: Enter the date of submission.
12. E-Sign Date: Enter the date of electronic signature.
13. E-Signature 2: Select 'Yes' if you have previously signed the form.
14. Confirmation of Application: Review and confirm that you have filled out the form correctly.
15. Submission of the Form: Submit the form to complete your application.
16. Review and Confirm Submission: Review your submission and confirm that everything is correct.
17. Final Signature: Sign the form again to confirm your submission.
