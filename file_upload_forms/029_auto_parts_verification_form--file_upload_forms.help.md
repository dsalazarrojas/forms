# Auto Parts Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Parts Verification Form is used to collect information about an auto part for verification purposes. This form is designed to gather details about the part, including its name, manufacturer part number, and condition, to verify its authenticity and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the technician's name in the "Technician Name" field. This is the person performing the verification.
2. Enter the name of the shop or facility where the verification is taking place in the "Shop Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the verification in the "Verification Date" field.
4. Enter the 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle related to the part.
5. Enter the make, model, and year of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Make, Model, and Year" field.
6. Enter the name of the part in the "Part Name" field.
7. Enter the manufacturer's part number in the "Manufacturer Part Number" field.
8. Select the source of the part from the options "OEM", "Aftermarket", or "Recycled".
9. Select the condition of the part from the options "New", "Like New", "Fair", or "Defective".
10. Answer whether the part's packaging was intact or not from the options "True" or "False".
11. Confirm whether the part fits the vehicle's specifications from the options "True" or "False".
12. Enter any notes or discrepancies found during the inspection in the "Verification Notes or Discrepancies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the person performing the verification.
* **Shop Name**: Enter the name of the repair facility or parts center where the verification is taking place.
* **Verification Date**: Enter the date of the verification.
* **Vehicle VIN**: Enter the 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle related to the part.
* **Vehicle Make, Model, and Year**: Enter the make, model, and year of the vehicle.
* **Part Name**: Enter the common name of the part.
* **Manufacturer Part Number**: Enter the official ID on the part or box.
* **Part Source**: Select the origin of the replacement part from the options "OEM", "Aftermarket", or "Recycled".
* **Part Condition Check**: Select the condition of the part from the options "New", "Like New", "Fair", or "Defective".
* **Packaging Intact**: Answer whether the part's packaging was intact or not.
* **Mounting Points Match**: Confirm whether the part fits the vehicle's specifications.
* **Verification Notes or Discrepancies**: Enter any notes or discrepancies found during the inspection.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is filled out for each part separately to avoid duplication of effort and maintain accurate records.
* This form should only be used for parts that have been received for verification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
