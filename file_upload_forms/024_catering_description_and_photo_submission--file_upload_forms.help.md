# Catering Description and Photo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your catering business, including your company name, contact details, services offered, and menu options. The form also requires you to submit up to three photos of your business, which will be used to showcase your work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked as such with an asterisk (*) next to the field label.
2. Enter your company name, contact person, email address, phone number, and business address.
3. Select the types of events and services you offer.
4. Choose your price range and cuisine specialty.
5. Submit up to three photos of your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor or Company Name**: (`vendor_name`, `text`, required) This field is where you enter your catering business name.
* **Contact Person**: (`contact_person`, `text`, required) This field is where you enter the name of the person who will be handling the account.
* **Email Address**: (`email`, `email`, required) This field is where you enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone`, `text`, required) This field is where you enter your contact phone number.
* **Website URL**: (`website`, `text`, optional) This field is where you enter your business website (if applicable).
* **Business Address**: (`business_address`, `text`, required) This field is where you enter your business location.
* **Years in Business**: (`years_in_business`, `number`, optional) This field is where you indicate how long your business has been operating.
* **Service Area**: (`service_area`, `text`, required) This field is where you specify the areas you serve.
* **Cuisine Specialty**: (`cuisine_specialty`, `select_one`, required) This field allows you to select the type of cuisine your business specializes in (e.g., American, Italian, etc.).
* **Service Types Offered**: (`service_types`, `select_multiple`, required) This field is where you select the services you offer (e.g., Full Service Catering, Drop-off Catering, etc.).
* **Event Types Serviced**: (`event_types`, `select_multiple`, required) This field is where you select the types of events you cater (e.g., Weddings, Corporate Events, etc.).
* **Minimum Guest Capacity**: (`capacity_min`, `number`, optional) This field is where you indicate the smallest event size you can handle.
* **Maximum Guest Capacity**: (`capacity_max`, `number`, optional) This field is where you indicate the largest event size you can handle.
* **Price Range**: (`price_range`, `select_one`, required) This field is where you select your average cost per person (e.g., Budget, Moderate, etc.).
* **Business Description**: (`business_description`, `text`, required) This field is where you describe your catering business.
* **Signature Dishes**: (`signature_dishes`, `text`, optional) This field is where you specify your most popular dishes.
* **Dietary Options Available**: (`dietary_options`, `text`, optional) This field is where you specify the dietary options available (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, etc.).
* **Photo 1 Description**: (`photo_1_description`, `text`, optional) This field is where you describe the first photo.
* **Photo 2 Description**: (`photo_2_description`, `text`, optional) This field is where you describe the second photo.
* **Photo 3 Description**: (`photo_3_description`, `text`, optional) This field is where you describe the third photo.
* **Certifications**: (`certifications`, `text`, optional) This field is where you specify any certifications you have.
* **Insurance Status**: (`insurance_status`, `select_one`, required) This field is where you indicate whether you have liability insurance.
* **References Available**: (`references_available`, `select_one`, optional) This field is where you indicate whether you can provide references.
* **Additional Comments**: (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional) This field is where you provide any additional information about your business.

Tips: Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we receive complete and accurate information about your catering business.
