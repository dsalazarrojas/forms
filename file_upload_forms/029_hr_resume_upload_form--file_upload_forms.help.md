# HR Resume Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Resume Upload Form is used to upload resumes of candidates for HR-related positions, allowing HR personnel to gather accurate and complete information from candidates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Upload Resume**: This field allows you to upload your resume. Please ensure that it is in a supported file format (e.g., PDF, DOCX, TXT).
2. **Contact**: Enter your name or title that will be displayed on the uploaded resume (e.g., John Doe, Jane Smith).
3. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
5. **Submitted On**: Select the date you submitted your resume.
6. **Uploaded On**: Select the date your resume was uploaded.
7. **Uploaded By**: Indicate whether your resume has been uploaded by you or someone else.
8. **Uploaded By Title**: Enter the title of the person who uploaded your resume (e.g., John Doe, Jane Smith).
9. **Uploaded By ID**: Enter the ID number of the person who uploaded your resume.
10. **Uploaded By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who uploaded your resume.
11. **Uploaded By Email**: Enter the email address of the person who uploaded your resume.
12. **Phone 2**, **2-title**, 2-email, 3-title, 3-phone, 3-email**: These fields are duplicate fields for the "Uploaded By" section, likely intended for additional uploaders or secondary contact information. Use them as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required): Enter your resume details in a supported file format.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required): Enter your name or title that will be displayed on the uploaded resume.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, required): Select the date you submitted your resume.
* **Uploaded On** (`uploaded_on`, `date`, required): Select the date your resume was uploaded.
* **Uploaded By** (`uploaded_by`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your resume has been uploaded by you or someone else.
* **Uploaded By Title** (`uploaded_by_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the person who uploaded your resume.
* **Uploaded By ID** (`uploaded_by_id`, `number`, required): Enter the ID number of the person who uploaded your resume.
* **Uploaded By Phone** (`uploaded_by_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person who uploaded your resume.
* **Uploaded By Email** (`uploaded_by_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person who uploaded your resume.
* **Phone 2** (`uploaded_by_2_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person who uploaded your resume as secondary contact.
* **2-title** (`uploaded_by_2_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the person who uploaded your resume as secondary contact.
* **2-email** (`uploaded_by_2_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person who uploaded your resume as secondary contact.
