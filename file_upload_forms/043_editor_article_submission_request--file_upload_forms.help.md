# Editor Article Submission Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for editors who want to submit their article requests to the publication team. It's a simple and straightforward form that helps us to efficiently collect the necessary information for review and publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **submission_request_form** field with a brief description of your article submission.
2. If you have any attachments or files related to your submission, upload them in the **submission_request_form_file_uploads** field.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes about your submission in the **editor_comments** field.
4. Select the date and time of your article submission using the **article_submission_date** and **article_submission_time** fields.
5. Confirm that you have read and understand the submission terms in the **submission_confirmation** field.
6. Finally, submit the form to complete your submission request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **submission_request_form** (`submission_request_form`, text, required): A brief description of your article submission. Please provide a clear and concise summary of your work.
* **submission_request_form_file_uploads** (`submission_request_form_file_uploads`, text, not required): If you have any attachments or files related to your submission, upload them here. Please note that file size limits may apply.
* **editor_comments** (`editor_comments`, text, not required): Additional comments or notes about your submission. This is an optional field, but it's a great opportunity to provide further context or clarification.
* **article_submission_date** (`article_submission_date`, date, not required): Select the date of your article submission. This will help us to track the submission timeline.
* **article_submission_time** (`article_submission_time`, time, not required): Select the time of your article submission. This will help us to track the submission timeline.
* **submission_confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, note, not required): Confirm that you have read and understand the submission terms. This is a required field to ensure that you are aware of our submission guidelines.
* **editor_article_submission_request_form_confirmation** (`editor_article_submission_request_form_confirmation`, text, not required): A confirmation that you have completed the form correctly. Please use this field to confirm that you have submitted the correct and complete information.
