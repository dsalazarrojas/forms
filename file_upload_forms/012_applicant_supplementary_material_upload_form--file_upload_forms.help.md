# Applicant Supplementary Material Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect additional materials submitted by applicants to support their applications. These materials may include academic transcripts, professional certifications, portfolios, recommendation letters, proof of identity, and financial support documents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are only uploading materials related to this specific application.
2. Enter your Applicant Full Name, which is required.
3. Enter your email address, which is also required.
4. Enter the Application Reference Number found in your initial application confirmation email.
5. Select the Category of Supplementary Materials you are uploading (e.g., Academic Transcripts, Professional Certifications, etc.).
6. Provide a description of each uploaded document.
7. If uploading more than one document, provide a description for each.
8. Briefly explain how these documents support your application.
9. Confirm that the uploaded documents are authentic copies of the original materials.
10. Finally, select the Date of Submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Application Reference Number**: Enter the unique number found in your initial application confirmation email.
* **Category of Supplementary Materials**: Select the category that best describes the type of materials you are uploading (e.g., Academic Transcripts, Professional Certifications, etc.).
* **Description of First Document, Description of Second Document (Optional), Description of Third Document (Optional)**: Provide a brief description of each uploaded document, e.g., "Undergraduate Degree Certificate" or "Professional Certification".
* **Additional Context for the Provided Materials**: Briefly explain how these documents support your application (e.g., "These documents demonstrate my relevant skills and experience for this position").
* **I Certify that the documents uploaded are authentic copies of the original materials**: Confirm that the uploaded documents are true copies of the original materials.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date you are submitting these materials.
