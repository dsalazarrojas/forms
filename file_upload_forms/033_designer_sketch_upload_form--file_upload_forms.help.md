# Designer Sketch Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information related to design work from users. It allows them to upload sketches, files, and other relevant details such as collaboration requests and their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as needed. You can leave any fields blank if you don't have the information or prefer not to provide it. Here are the steps to follow:

1. Scroll down to the "Upload" section and select the type of file you want to upload (sketch, file, or collaboration request).
2. Click on the "Upload" button to select the file from your device.
3. In the "Collaboration Request" section, fill out the field if you have a collaboration request.
4. In the "Contact" section, fill out the "Email", "Phone", and "Name" fields if you want to provide your contact information.
5. Fill out the "Title" field if you want to provide a title for your uploaded content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Sketch**: Upload your design sketch.
* **Upload File**: Upload any file related to your design work.
* **Upload Collaboration Request**: If you have a collaboration request, provide the details here.
* **Upload Design**: Upload any additional design information.
* **Email**: Provide your email address (if you want to be contacted).
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number (if you want to be contacted).
* **Name**: Provide your name (if you want to be identified).
* **Title**: Provide a title for your uploaded content (if you want to add a descriptive title).
* **Portfolio**: This field is currently not in use (not shown in the form fields, but included as it was in the YAML input schema).
