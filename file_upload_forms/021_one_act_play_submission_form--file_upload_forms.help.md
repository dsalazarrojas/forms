# one_act_play_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a one-act play for consideration. It is designed to collect information about the play and the submitter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your play.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for "Script type", "Production type", and "Upload type" according to your play's characteristics.
3. If you need to upload a file, select "Yes" in the "Upload type" field and upload your file in the designated area.
4. Provide your email address, phone number, and name as the submitter.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **One act play entry (id: 1)**: Enter a brief description of your one-act play.
* **Title (id: 2)**: Enter the title of your play.
* **Playwright Name (id: 3)**: Enter the name of the playwright.
* **Play Length (id: 4)**: Enter the length of your play.
* **Script Type (id: 5)**: Select "Yes" if your play is a script, otherwise select "No".
* **Production Type (id: 6)**: Select "Yes" if your play has been produced, otherwise select "No".
* **Production Team (id: 7)**: Select "Yes" if your play was produced by a team, otherwise select "No".
* **Submission Date (id: 8)**: Enter the date you are submitting your play.
* **Submission Time (id: 9)**: Enter the time you are submitting your play.
* **Email (id: 10)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (id: 11)**: Enter your phone number.
* **File (id: 12)**: This is a text field for a file name, if you are uploading a file.
* **File Name (id: 13)**: Enter the name of the file you are uploading.
* **Upload Type (id: 14)**: Select "Yes" if you are uploading a file, otherwise select "No".
* **Other (id: 15)**: If you are uploading a file and it is not listed in the options, enter the type of file here.
* **Notes (id: 16)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Form Submission (id: 17)**: This is a text field for any additional comments about your submission.
* **Production Location (id: 18)**: Enter the location where your play was produced.
* **Other (id: 19)**: If your production location is not listed, enter it here.
* **Other Production Location (id: 20)**: Enter any additional information about your production location.
* **Submitter (id: 21)**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email (id: 22)**: Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone (id: 23)**: Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Name (id: 24)**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Note (id: 25)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the submitter.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you are uploading a file, ensure that it is in a compatible format.
* Double-check your submission for any errors before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
