# Graphic Design Entry Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to help you understand how to complete and use the Graphic Design Entry Upload Form. The form is intended to collect and process graphic design entries submitted by users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as instructed below.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by a star *) to ensure successful submission of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Entry**: 
*   This field is where you upload a description of your graphic design entry. Please provide a brief and clear description of your work.
*   **Description**: 
*   This field is where you can provide more details about your graphic design entry. Please describe your design in a few sentences.
*   **File**: 
*   This field is where you can upload a file related to your graphic design entry. Please make sure the file is in a compatible format (e.g. PDF, JPEG, GIF) and is no larger than 10MB.
*   **Designer Name**: 
*   This field is where you can enter your name as the designer of the graphic design entry. Please provide your name as it appears on your business card or resume.

Note: All fields are optional, but please make sure to complete all required fields to ensure successful submission of the form.
