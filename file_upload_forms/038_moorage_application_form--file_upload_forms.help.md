# Moorage Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moorage Application Form is used to submit a request for a moorage, which can be a harbor or marina where boats can be docked or stored.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including moorage category, address, and email.
2. Select the moorage type (single or multiple).
3. Enter the start and end dates of the moorage period.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about the moorage application.
5. Optionally, attach any supporting documents or files to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (harbor/marina)**: Select the type of moorage you are applying for. This field helps us understand the location and type of moorage you need.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the moorage location.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address so we can reach you with updates about your moorage application.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up questions.
* **Moorage Type (single/multiple)**: Choose whether you are applying for a single or multiple moorage slots.
* **Moorage Start**: Enter the start date of your moorage period.
* **Moorage End**: Enter the end date of your moorage period.
* **Moorage Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your moorage application.
* **Moorage Attachment**: You can attach any supporting documents or files to your application.
* **Form Notes**: Any additional notes about your application will be recorded here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use a valid email address and phone number.
* If you have any supporting documents, attach them to the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review and process your application as soon as possible.
* We will keep you updated on the status of your application.
