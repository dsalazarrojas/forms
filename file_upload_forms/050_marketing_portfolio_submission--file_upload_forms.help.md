# Marketing Portfolio Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Portfolio Submission form is designed to collect relevant information about a marketing portfolio, including project highlights, portfolio upload, contact details, and other supporting information. This form is intended to help us understand your marketing portfolio and facilitate a smooth submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Marketing Portfolio Submission form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your marketing portfolio.
2. Provide a description of your portfolio.
3. Upload your portfolio (if required).
4. Enter your project highlights.
5. Enter your contact details, including first name, last name, email address, phone number, and address.
6. Review your submission for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Portfolio Submission**: Enter a brief title for your marketing portfolio.
* **Project Highlights**: Describe your project highlights, including any notable achievements or accomplishments.
* **Upload Portfolio**: If required, upload your portfolio to support your submission.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and address.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **E-Mail**: Enter your email address. Please ensure it is valid and accurately formatted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.

## Tips
* Ensure that your submission is accurate and complete to avoid duplication and delays in the submission process.
* Double-check your information for formatting and spelling errors before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* We appreciate your prompt and complete submission of your marketing portfolio.
