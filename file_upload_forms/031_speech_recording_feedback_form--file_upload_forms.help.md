# Speech Recording Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation on a speech recording, allowing users to provide a structured assessment of the recording's quality, content, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Upload a file containing the speech recording, if applicable.
3. Provide feedback and comments on the recording.
4. Submit the form to complete the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speech Recording Feedback** (1, text, required): This field is where you can provide a brief summary of your feedback on the speech recording. Please be concise and clear in your description.
* **File Upload** (2, text, required): If you have a file containing the speech recording, please upload it here. This will help the evaluator review the recording more efficiently.
* **Evaluator Name** (3, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or contact card.
* **Evaluation Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date you evaluated the speech recording.
* **Evaluation Time** (5, time, required): Enter the time you evaluated the speech recording.
* **Evaluation Score** (6, number, required): Provide a numerical score out of a maximum of 5 to evaluate the recording's quality.
* **Feedback** (7, note, required): This field is for providing more detailed feedback on the recording's content, structure, and delivery. Please be specific and provide examples or suggestions for improvement.
* **Comments** (8, text, required): Use this field to add any additional comments or observations you'd like to share about the recording.
* **Submitter Name** (9, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the feedback.
* **Submitter Email** (10, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **File Upload** (2, text): You may choose to upload a file containing additional feedback or attachments.
