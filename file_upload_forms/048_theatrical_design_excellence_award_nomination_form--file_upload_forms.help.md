# Theatrical Design Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theatrical Design Excellence Award Nomination Form is a tool used to nominate theatrical designs for recognition and award. This form is used to gather information about the nominated design, including its category, description, and supporting materials. It also collects contact information for the submitter, including name, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the nominated design in the "Theatrical Design Excellence Award Nomination Form" field.
2. Select the category of the nominated design from the provided options.
3. Provide a brief description of the nominated design in the "Description" field.
4. Upload supporting materials related to the nominated design in the "Upload Supporting Materials" field.
5. Enter your name as the submitter in the "Submitter Name" field.
6. Enter your email address as the submitter in the "Submitter E-mail" field (optional).
7. Enter your phone number as the submitter in the "Submitter Phone" field.
8. Select a submit date for the nomination in the "Submit Date" field.
9. Upload supporting files related to the submitter in the "Supporting Files" field.
10. Verify the submission status of your nomination by checking the "Submission Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Theatrical Design Excellence Award Nomination Form**:
    Title of the nominated design (text field).
- **Category**:
    Select one of the following options: Arts, Theater, or Other.
- **Description**:
    Briefly describe the nominated design.
- **Upload Supporting Materials**:
    Upload files or documents related to the nominated design.
- **Submitter Name**:
    Enter your name as the submitter.
- **Submitter E-mail**:
    Enter your email address as the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter Phone**:
    Enter your phone number as the submitter.
- **Submit Date**:
    Select a date for the submission.
- **Supporting Files**:
    Upload files or documents related to the submitter.
- **Submission Status**:
    Select one of the following options: Pending, Approved, or Denied.
- **Submitter Name**:
    Enter the name of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter E-mail**:
    Enter the email address of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter Phone**:
    Enter the phone number of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter Date**:
    Select a date for the submitter.
- **Submitter Files**:
    Upload files or documents related to the submitter.
- **Submitter Comments**:
    Enter any comments or notes about the submitter.
- **Submitter Comments Text**:
    Enter any additional comments or notes about the submitter (optional).
- **Submission Status**:
    Select one of the following options: Pending, Approved, or Denied.
- **Submitter By Name**:
    Enter the name of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter By E-mail**:
    Enter the email address of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter By Phone**:
    Enter the phone number of the submitter (optional).
