# garden_center_customer_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The garden center customer inquiry form is designed to collect information from customers about their queries, questions, or concerns related to the garden center. This form is intended for customers to provide their contact information and any relevant files they may need to support their inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field. This is a required field, so make sure to fill it out.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This field is optional, but it's useful for us to contact you back.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This field is also optional.
4. Describe your customer question or concern in the "Customer Question" field. This field is optional.
5. For each of the "File Upload" fields, click the button to select a file from your computer. You can upload up to 3 files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you back.
- **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
- **Customer Question** (customer_question, text, optional): Describe your question or concern in as much detail as possible.
- **File Upload 1** (file_upload_1, text, optional): Click the button to select a file from your computer to support your inquiry.
- **File Upload 2** (file_upload_2, text, optional): Click the button to select another file from your computer.
- **File Upload 3** (file_upload_3, text, optional): Click the button to select the last file from your computer.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields, including "Customer Name".
- If you have any attachments or documents to support your inquiry, please upload them using the "File Upload" fields.
- If you have any follow-up questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
