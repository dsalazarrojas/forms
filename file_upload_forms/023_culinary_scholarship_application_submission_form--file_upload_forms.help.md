# Culinary Scholarship Application Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the necessary information and documents for your culinary scholarship application. Please fill out the form carefully and ensure you have attached all required documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions in the "Application Info" section.
2. If applicable, select "Yes" if you have attachments to upload, otherwise, select "No".
3. Upload any necessary attachments, such as your resume, certification, and transcript.
4. Enter your personal statement.
5. Enter your references.
6. Upload any other attachments you need to submit.
7. Enter your email address, phone number, and address.
8. Upload your completed application.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete your submission.
