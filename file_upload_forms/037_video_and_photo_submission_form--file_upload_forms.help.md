# Video And Photo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit videos and photos for review, sharing, or other purposes. It's an ideal platform for artists, designers, or individuals showcasing their talents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Video Upload" field to attach a video file from your computer or device.
2. Select the "Photo Upload" field to attach a photo file from your computer or device.
3. Provide a title for your submission in the "Title" field.
4. Add a description of your work in the "Description" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field so we can contact you about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Upload** (`video_upload`, text, optional): Attach a video file from your computer or device to showcase your work.
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, text, optional): Attach a photo file from your computer or device to showcase your work.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Provide a title for your submission, e.g., "My Artwork", "Project XYZ", etc.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Describe your work in detail, including any relevant information about the submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach a clear and high-quality video or photo file.
* Use a descriptive title that accurately represents your work.
* Provide a detailed description of your submission for better context.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
