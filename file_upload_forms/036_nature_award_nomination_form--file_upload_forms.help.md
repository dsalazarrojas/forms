# Nature Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a nominee for the "Nature Award". It is not a survey or a questionnaire, but a formal nomination submission. Please ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information about the nominee.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Nomination Form** (Page 1)
	* Fill out the nomination form with the required information about the nominee.
2. **Supporting Images** (Page 2)
	* Upload any supporting images or documents that demonstrate the nominee's work or achievements.
3. **Entry Details** (Page 3)
	* Provide additional information about the nominee, such as their entry category and submission format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (Page 1)
	* **Name**: The full name of the nominee, as it appears on their professional or personal ID.
	* **Title**: The title or position of the nominee, as it appears on their professional or personal ID.
	* **Email**: The email address of the nominee or nominator.
	* **Phone**: The phone number of the nominee or nominator.
* **Supporting Images** (Page 2)
	* **Image 1**: Upload one or more images that support the nominee's work or achievements.
* **Entry Details** (Page 3)
	* **Entry Category**: Select the category that best describes the nominee's work or achievements.
	* **Submission Format**: Select the format of the submission, e.g., video, image, or written work.
	* **Submission Title**: Enter a brief title for the submission.
	* **Submission Description**: Provide a brief description of the submission.
	* **Submission Link**: Enter the link to the submission, if applicable.
	* **Nominee's Statement**: Enter any additional statements or comments from the nominee.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information about the nominee.
* If you are submitting a nomination for a team or group, please list all team members and their roles.
* If you are submitting a nomination for a deceased person, please provide a statement explaining the circumstances of their passing.
* Please proofread your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
