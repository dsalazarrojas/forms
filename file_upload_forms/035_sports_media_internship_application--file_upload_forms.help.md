# Sports Media Internship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants to provide information about themselves for sports media internship opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the sports media internship application form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Personal Details** section with your basic information.
2.  Complete the **Contact Information** section with your contact details.
3.  You can **upload your resume** if you have one.
4.  Provide **References** if you have any professional or academic references.
5.  Share your **Work Experience** in the sports industry or relevant fields.
6.  Describe your **Media Background**, including any media-related experience or training.
7.  List your **Skills**, such as writing, photography, or video production.
8.  Finally, provide your **Availability** for the internship duration and schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Details** (`personal_details`): Fill out this section with your basic information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Provide your contact information, including your address and social media profiles.
*   **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`): If you have a resume, upload it for us to review. This is optional.
*   **References** (`references`): If you have any professional or academic references, list them here.
*   **Work Experience** (`work_experience`): Share your relevant work experience in the sports industry or related fields.
*   **Media Background** (`media_background`): Describe your media-related experience or training.
*   **Skills** (`skills`): List any relevant skills you have, such as writing, photography, or video production.
*   **Availability** (`availability`): Specify your availability for the internship duration and schedule.

## Tips

*   Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   You can choose to provide any or all of the requested information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
