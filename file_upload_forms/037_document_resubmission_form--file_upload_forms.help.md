# Document Resubmission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Resubmission Form is designed to collect and track resubmissions of documents for review and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Upload File" field to select and upload your document.
2. Fill in your name and the date of resubmission in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload File** (`file_upload_1`, text, required/optional): Upload a file that requires resubmission. This can be any file type, and please ensure it is the correct and complete document.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name and/or username to associate with the document.
* **Resubmission Date** (`resubmission_date`, date, optional): Specify the date of the resubmitted document. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD".

## Tips
* Be precise and accurate in your file selection and resubmission date to avoid confusion or errors.
* Ensure you have the necessary information and files ready before proceeding.
* Review and verify your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to form@gic.mx.
