# Uniform Drawing Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists to submit their uniform drawings for review, feedback, and potential publication. It is intended to be used in events such as contests, exhibitions, or other artistic showcases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Artist Info" text field with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as an artist.
2. In the "Drawing Info" text field, provide a brief description of your submitted artwork, including its title, medium, and any other relevant information.
3. Check the "Submit" dropdown menu to confirm whether you would like to submit your artwork for review. If you choose "Yes", your submission will be reviewed and considered for publication. If you choose "No", your submission will not be reviewed, but you can still provide feedback or ask questions about the event or submission process.
4. To upload your artwork file, select the "File Upload" field and browse to the location of your file. Please ensure that your file is in the correct format and size for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Info (artist_info)**: A text field where you can provide your name, contact information, and any other details about yourself as an artist.
* **Drawing Info (drawing_info)**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of your submitted artwork, including its title, medium, and any other relevant information.
* **Submit (submit)**: A dropdown menu that allows you to choose whether to submit your artwork for review. If you choose "Yes", your submission will be reviewed and considered for publication.
* **File Upload (upload_file)**: A field where you can upload your artwork file. Please ensure that your file is in the correct format and size for review.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your file is in a compatible format and size for review.
* If you are having trouble uploading your file, try checking the file size and format.
* If you have any questions or need feedback, please contact the event organizers or check the event website for more information.
