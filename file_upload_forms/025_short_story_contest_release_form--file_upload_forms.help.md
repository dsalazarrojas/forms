# Short Story Contest Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Story Contest Release Form is used for participants in a short story contest to release their entries into the public domain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the guidelines below.

1. Start by entering your story submission, as prompted.
2. Review the participant permission section to ensure you've given the correct permissions for your work to be released.
3. Enter your name as the participant, as it will be displayed with their story.
4. Enter your age, if you're a minor (less than 18 years old).
5. Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
6. Enter your contact information, including school name and contact information (if applicable).
7. Enter the title of your short story.
8. Select the genre of your story.
9. Enter the length of your story (in words).
10. Select the type of your story.
11. Enter the rating of your story (on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest).
12. Select the status of your story (e.g. "complete" or "in progress").
13. Enter any notes about participant permission (if applicable).
14. Enter the date and time you granted permission for your story to be released.
15. Enter your signature, as proof of permission.
16. Enter your school's signature, if applicable.
17. Select the type of participant permission.
18. Enter the version of the participant permission (if applicable).
19. Enter the release date and time.
20. Enter the release signature, as proof of release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Story Submission**: Enter the title and content of your short story.
* **Participant Permission**: Select all the options that apply to release your story into the public domain. This includes permission to share, publish, and distribute your work without any restrictions.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as the participant who submitted the story.
* **Participant Age**: Enter your age, if you're a minor (less than 18 years old).
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Participant Contact**: Enter your contact information, including school name and contact details (if applicable).
* **Participant School**: Enter the name of your school, if you're a student.
* **Story Title**: Enter the title of your short story.
* **Story Genre**: Select the genre of your story (e.g. sci-fi, fantasy, romance, etc.).
* **Story Length**: Enter the length of your story in words.
* **Story Type**: Select the type of your story (e.g. short story, novel, essay, etc.).
* **Story Rating**: Enter the rating of your story on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
* **Story Status**: Select the status of your story (e.g. "complete" or "in progress").
* **Participant Permission Note**: Enter any notes about participant permission, if applicable.
* **Participant Permission Date**: Enter the date you granted permission for your story to be released.
* **Participant Permission Time**: Enter the time you granted permission for your story to be released.
* **Participant Signature**: Enter your signature, as proof of permission.
* **Participant School Signature**: Enter your school's signature, if applicable.
* **Release Authorized**: Select the person who authorized the release of your story.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date your story was released.
* **Release Time**: Enter the time your story was released.
* **Release Signature**: Enter the signature of the person who released your story.
* **Participant Permission Type**: Select the type of participant permission.
* **Participant Permission Version**: Enter the version of the participant permission, if applicable.
* **Participant Permission Version**: This field is for the participant to record the version of the permission they granted.

## Tips
- Please review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- Ensure you've filled in all required information accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult with a contest official or administrator.
- The participant permission section is essential to release your story into the public domain.
- The release authorized section is for the person who authorized the release of your story.
