# Healthcare Research Study Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect submission of healthcare research studies from researchers. It helps standardize the submission process and ensures that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the basic information about you and your research study.
2. Enter your name as the researcher.
3. Enter your research institution.
4. Provide a title for your research study.
5. Briefly describe the objective of your research.
6. Enter the location where the research will take place.
7. Upload any relevant files related to your research.
8. Select the start and end dates of your research study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Researchers name
* **Researchers name** (text, required): Enter your name as the researcher.
### research_institution
* **Research Institution** (text, required): Enter the name of the institution where you are conducting the research.
### research_title
* **Research Title** (text, required): Provide a brief title for your research study.
### research_objective
* **Research Objective** (text, required): Describe the main objective of your research study.
### research_location
* **Research Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the research will take place.
### File Uploader 1
* **File Uploader 1** (text, required): Upload any file related to your research study. You can upload multiple files if necessary.
### File Uploader 2
* **File Uploader 2** (text, required): Upload any additional files related to your research study.
### research_start_date
* **Research Start Date** (date, required): Enter the start date of your research study.
### research_end_date
* **Research End Date** (date, required): Enter the end date of your research study.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the submission is complete.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the form administrators for assistance.
* You can upload multiple files for each field, if necessary.
