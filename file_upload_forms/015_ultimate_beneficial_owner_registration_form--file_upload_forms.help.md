# ultimate_beneficial_owner_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ultimate_beneficial_owner_registration_form" is used to register the beneficial owner of a company or entity. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any potential issues or delays in the registration process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the beneficial owner's name and address in the "Beneficial Owner" and "Beneficial Owner Address" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the beneficial owner's date of birth in the "Beneficial Owner Date of Birth" field, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the beneficial owner's nationality and residence status from the drop-down menus.
4. Enter the beneficial owner's phone number in the "Beneficial Owner Phone" field.
5. Enter the beneficial owner's email address in the "Beneficial Owner Email" field.
6. Enter any identifying details in the "Beneficial Owner Identifying Details" field.
7. Determine the beneficial owner's relationship status and ownership percentage.
8. Enter the beneficial owner's documents information.
9. Enter any additional notes about the beneficial owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Beneficial Owner** (`beneficial_owner`, `text`, required): Enter the beneficial owner's name.
* **Beneficial Owner Address** (`beneficial_owner_address`, `text`, required): Enter the beneficial owner's address.
* **Beneficial Owner Date of Birth** (`beneficial_owner_dob`, `date`, required): Enter the beneficial owner's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Beneficial Owner Nationality** (`beneficial_owner_nationality`, `select_one`, required): Select the beneficial owner's nationality.
* **Beneficial Owner Residence** (`beneficial_owner_residence`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the beneficial owner's residence status.
* **Beneficial Owner Nationality List** (`beneficial_owner_nationality_list`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the beneficial owner's nationality.
* **Beneficial Owner Residence List** (`beneficial_owner_residence_list`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the beneficial owner's residence status.
* **Beneficial Owner Phone** (`beneficial_owner_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the beneficial owner's phone number.
* **Beneficial Owner Email** (`beneficial_owner_email`, `email`, required): Enter the beneficial owner's email address.
* **Beneficial Owner Documents** (`beneficial_owner_documents`, `text`, required): Enter any documents information.
* **Beneficial Owner Identifying Details** (`beneficial_owner_identifying_details`, `text`, required): Enter any identifying details about the beneficial owner.
* **Beneficial Owner Relationship** (`beneficial_owner_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Determine the beneficial owner's relationship status.
* **Beneficial Owner Ownership Percentage** (`beneficial_owner_ownership_percent`, `number`, required): Enter the beneficial owner's ownership percentage.
* **Beneficial Owner Date** (`beneficial_owner_date`, `date`, not required): Enter any date related to the beneficial owner.
* **Beneficial Owner Time** (`beneficial_owner_time`, `time`, not required): Enter any time related to the beneficial owner.
* **Beneficial Owner Note** (`beneficial_owner_note`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional notes about the beneficial owner.
* **Beneficial Owner Ultimate Beneficiary ID** (`beneficial_owner_ult_ben_id`, `text`, not required): Enter the beneficial owner's ultimate beneficiary ID.
* **Beneficial Owner Ownership Status** (`beneficial_owner_ownership_status`, `text`, not required): Enter the beneficial owner's ownership status.
