# News Entry Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The News Entry Submission Form is a tool for newsroom editors and end-users to submit news entries. This form captures the necessary information to create a new news entry and allows editors to review and manage the submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages and fields to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary information for the news entry.
3. Ensure you fill out all required fields.
4. Review your submission before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit News Entry** (`submit_news_entry`, `text`, `required`): Enter a brief description of the news entry.
* **News Entry Details** (`news_entry_details`, `note`, `required`): Provide a detailed description of the news entry, including any relevant details or context.
* **News Entry Image** (`news_entry_image`, `text`, `required`): Upload an image related to the news entry (Note: this field may not be required for all submissions).
* **Newsroom Editor** (`newsroom_editor`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the newsroom editor who will be assigned to review and manage the submission. (Note: Please select one of the available editors: editor1, editor2, or editor3).
* **News Entry Attachments** (`news_entry_attachments`, `text`, `required`): Attach any relevant files or documents to support the news entry.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name or username as the submitter of the news entry.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address as the submitter of the news entry.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number as the submitter of the news entry.
