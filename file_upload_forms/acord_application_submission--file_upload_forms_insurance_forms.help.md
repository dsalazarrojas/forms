# ACORD Application Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for an ACORD (Association for Cooperative Insurance Planning) application. This form is used to gather necessary details for the application process, including personal and contact information, application specifics, risk assessment, and submission documents. The form ensures that all required information is provided for a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name exactly as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address to receive application updates and correspondence from us.
3. Enter your phone number with area code for contact purposes.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Provide your Social Security Number (SSN) for identity verification.
6. Enter your mailing address where mail is received.
7. If applicable, enter your property address (if different from your mailing address).
8. Select the type of application (new, renewal, transfer, or amendment).
9. Choose the type of insurance coverage (homeowners, renters, auto, commercial, life, or health).
10. Enter the policy period start and end dates.
11. Enter the desired coverage amount in dollars.
12. If willing, enter your annual premium budget.
13. Answer whether you have had any claims in the past 5 years.
14. If applicable, provide details of the most recent claim (date, type, and amount of loss).
15. List known risk factors that may affect insurability (select all that apply).
16. Attach supporting documents with your application.
17. Add any additional comments or notes relevant to your application.
18. Sign the form electronically by typing your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (ID 2, Required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address** (ID 3, Required): Enter your email address for application updates and correspondence.
* **Phone number** (ID 4, Optional): Include your area code for contact purposes.
* **Date of birth** (ID 5, Required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Social Security Number (SSN)** (ID 6, Required): Enter your Social Security Number for identity verification.
* **Mailing address** (ID 7, Required): Enter your current address where mail is received.
* **Property address** (ID 8, Optional): If your property address is different from your mailing address, enter it here.
* **Application date** (ID 10, Required): Enter the date of this application.
* **Type of application** (ID 11, Required): Choose the type of application (new, renewal, transfer, or amendment).
* **Type of insurance coverage** (ID 12, Required): Select the type of insurance coverage (homeowners, renters, auto, commercial, life, or health).
* **Policy period start date** (ID 13, Required): Enter the date when coverage should begin.
* **Policy period end date** (ID 14, Required): Enter the date when coverage should expire.
* **Coverage amount** (ID 15, Required): Enter the desired coverage amount in dollars.
* **Annual premium budget** (ID 16, Optional): If willing, enter your maximum annual premium budget.
* **Have you had any claims in the past 5 years?** (ID 18, Required): Answer with 'True' or 'False'.
* **Details of most recent claim** (ID 19, Optional): If applicable, provide date, type, and amount of loss.
* **Known risk factors** (ID 20, Optional): Select all factors that may affect insurability.
* **Supporting documents** (ID 22, Required): List all attached documents with this application.
* **Additional comments or notes** (ID 23, Optional): Add any relevant comments or notes.
* **Applicant signature** (ID 24, Required): Type your full name to sign electronically.
