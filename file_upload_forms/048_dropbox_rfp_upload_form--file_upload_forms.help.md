# Dropbox RFP Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dropbox RFP Upload Form is a simple form used to collect relevant information for a Request for Proposal (RFP) process. This form allows users to upload a file and provide contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the first page and click on the "File Upload" field.
2. Select or drag and drop the file you'd like to upload from your computer.
3. Provide your contact information on the subsequent pages:
	* Enter your name and phone number on the second page.
	* Enter your email address on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### File Upload (Page 1)
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): Upload your RFP file here. You can select or drag and drop a file from your computer.

### Deadline (Page 2)
* **Deadline** (`date`, date, required): Enter the deadline for the RFP.

### Name (Page 2)
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.

### Phone (Page 2)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Email (Page 3)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the deadline and your contact details.
* Only upload files in a supported format (e.g. PDF, Word, Excel).
* If you have any issues uploading the file, try checking your file size or format.
