# App Creation And Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting and publishing mobile apps.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for fields with select_one types.
3. Upload relevant files when required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission date** (1, `date`, required): This is the date you are submitting the app for review.
* **Developer or company name** (2, `text`, required): Enter the name of the developer or company creating the app.
* **Developer email address** (3, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the developer or company.
* **Developer phone number** (4, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the developer or company.
* **Application name** (5, `text`, required): Enter the official name of the app.
* **Application description** (6, `text`, required): Describe what the app does.
* **Application category** (7, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the app from the options provided.
* **Target audience** (8, `text`, required): Describe who the app is intended for.
* **Application price** (9, `text`, required): Enter the price of the app in dollars.
* **Submitting to Apple App Store** (10, `select_one`, required): Select whether the app is submitted to Apple App Store.
* **Submitting to Google Play Store** (11, `select_one`, required): Select whether the app is submitted to Google Play Store.
* **Submitting to other app stores** (12, `text`, optional): List the names of other app stores the app is submitted to (if applicable).
* **App version number** (13, `text`, required): Enter the version number of the app.
* **Minimum OS requirement** (14, `text`, required): Enter the oldest version of the operating system that supports the app.
* **App file for submission** (15, `text`, required): Upload a compiled app binary for review.
* **App icon** (16, `text`, required): Upload a high-resolution icon for the app.
* **App screenshots** (17, `select_one`, required): Select whether app screenshots are provided.
* **Promotional graphic or banner** (18, `text`, optional): Upload a promotional graphic or banner for the app.
* **Privacy policy URL** (19, `text`, required): Enter the URL of the app's privacy policy.
* **Terms of service URL** (20, `text`, required): Enter the URL of the app's terms of service.
* **Content rating** (21, `select_one`, required): Select the age appropriateness of the app content.
* **I confirm all content is original or licensed** (22, `select_one`, required): Confirm that all content is original or licensed.
* **App testing status** (23, `select_one`, required): Select the testing status of the app.
* **Additional submission notes** (24, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the submission.
