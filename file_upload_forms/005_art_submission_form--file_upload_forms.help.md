# Art Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Submission Form is designed for artists to submit their artworks for review and consideration. This form captures essential information about the artist, artwork, and submission details. Please ensure you fill out all required fields to increase the chances of your artwork being considered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your art submission information, following the guidelines and instructions on each page.
2. Provide accurate and complete answers to all required fields.
3. Submit your form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Full Name** (`artist_name`, text, **true**): Enter your full name as it appears on your documentation or ID.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, **true**): Enter your valid email address.
* **Brief Artist Bio** (`artist_bio`, text, **true**): Write a 200-word maximum description of yourself and your artistic background.
* **Title of the Work** (`artwork_title`, text, **true**): Enter the title of the artwork you are submitting.
* **Submission Category** (`category`, select_one, **true**): Choose the best category for your artwork from the options provided (Painting, Sculpture, Photography, Mixed Media, Digital Art).
* **Dimensions and Weight** (`dimensions_weight`, text, **true**): Enter the dimensions and weight of your artwork (e.g., 24x36 inches, 5 lbs).
* **Year Completed** (`year_completed`, number, **true**): Enter the year you completed the artwork.
* **Statement About the Piece** (`artist_statement`, text, **false**): Provide a description of the inspiration or meaning behind your artwork (optional).
* **Please ensure high resolution images are ready for upload** (`upload_notice`, note, **false**): Please prepare high-resolution images of your artwork before submitting.
* **I certify this is my original work** (`authenticity_confirmation`, select_one, **true**): Choose to confirm that the artwork is your original work.

**Tips:**

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure you have high-resolution images ready for upload.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
