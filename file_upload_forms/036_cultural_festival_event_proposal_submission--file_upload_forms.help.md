# cultural-festival-event-proposal-submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for festival and event organizers to submit their proposals for consideration. The form collects event details, including title, description, schedule, and other relevant information. Ensure that the proposal you submit is accurate and complete to ensure your event is successfully considered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (if any).
2. Provide a clear and concise title for your event.
3. Enter the name and contact information of the event organizer.
4. Write a detailed proposal text describing your event.
5. Specify the schedule for your event, including start and end dates.
6. Provide the name of any performers participating in the event.
7. Upload any supporting proposal file (if necessary).
8. Enter your contact email address.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required: false): Enter a clear and concise title for your event.
* **Organizer Name** (`organizer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Proposal Text** (`proposal_text`, note, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your event proposal. This field is a note field, so you can write as much or as little as you need.
* **Schedule Start Date** (`schedule_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date your event is scheduled to start.
* **Schedule End Date** (`schedule_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date your event is scheduled to end.
* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of any performers participating in your event.
* **Proposal File** (`proposal_file`, text, required: false): Upload any supporting proposal file if necessary.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, note, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your event.

Note: This form is a general-purpose form and not intended for any specific event submission, please use it for festival and event proposals only. If you are unsure about any field or its purpose, please refer to the event organizer's guidelines for more information. 

This form does not collect personally identifiable information and does not store any data that can be used to identify a specific individual. It is intended for general use and not for any personal, financial, or sensitive information.
