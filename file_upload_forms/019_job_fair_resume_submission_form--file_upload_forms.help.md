# Job Fair Resume Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Fair Resume Submission Form is a tool for job seekers to submit their resume and relevant information to potential employers participating in a job fair. This form is designed to streamline the process of gathering necessary information from candidates and making it easily accessible to hiring managers or recruiters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
* Upload your resume as a PDF or other accepted file type.
* Fill out your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number (optional).
* Select your university and job title.
* Choose your job type and category.
* Enter your job ID (if available).
* Provide your location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Resume** (`resume_upload`, `text`, required):
	+ This field allows you to upload your resume as a PDF or other accepted file type.
* **Resume File** (`resume_file`, `text`, required):
	+ Please enter the name of your uploaded resume file.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your resume.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your contact phone number (not required, but recommended).
* **University** (`university`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the university you graduated from.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose your current or desired job type from the options provided.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose your current or desired job category from the options provided.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your current or desired location.
* **Job ID** (`job_id`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your job ID if you have one.

## Tips
* Make sure your resume is in a PDF format to ensure it's easily readable.
* Keep your contact information up-to-date and accurate.
* Be specific when selecting your job type and category to ensure you're matched with relevant job postings.
