# Comments And Media Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and media from users about a specific product or service. It allows you to submit comments, media, and additional information about your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit your comment and media, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Select the type of comment you are submitting (General Comment, Question, Suggestion, Complaint, Compliment, Bug Report, Feature Request, or Other).
3. Enter a brief subject line and a detailed description of your comment.
4. If you have media to submit (images, videos, or documents), select the type of media you are uploading and attach it to the form.
5. Choose your priority level for the issue (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).
6. Indicate whether you have media to submit.
7. Select whether you want to be contacted about your submission.
8. Decide if you want your comment to be displayed publicly.
9. Confirm that you agree to the submission terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name to submit your comment and media.
* **Email Address** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up if needed.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **-- Comment Type --** (`section_header_comment_type`, `note`, optional): Select the type of comment you are submitting (General Comment, Question, Suggestion, Complaint, Compliment, Bug Report, Feature Request, or Other).
* **Comment Category** (`comment_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of your comment.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required): Enter a brief subject line for your comment.
* **-- Your Message --** (`section_header_message`, `note`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your comment.
* **Your Comment or Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Enter your comment or message in detail.
* **Priority Level** (`priority`, `select_one`, required): Choose the priority level for your issue (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).
* **Do You Have Media to Submit** (`has_media`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have media to submit.
* **Media Type** (`media_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of media you are uploading (Image, Video, Audio, Document, Screenshot, or Other).
* **Media Files** (`media_files`, `text`, optional): Attach your media files here.
* **Media Description** (`media_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the media you are uploading.
* **-- Context --** (`section_header_context`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional context about your submission.
* **Related Product or Service** (`related_product`, `text`, optional): Enter the product or service related to your submission.
* **Related URL** (`related_url`, `text`, optional): Enter a link to the relevant page.
* **Device Used** (`device_used`, `select_one`, optional): Select the device you are using.
* **Browser Used** (`browser_used`, `select_one`, optional): Select the browser you are using.
* **-- Permissions --** (`section_header_permissions`, `note`, optional): This section is used to indicate your permission settings.
* **May We Contact You** (`contact_permission`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want to be contacted about your submission.
* **Public Display Permission** (`public_display`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want your comment to be displayed publicly.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to the submission terms.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
