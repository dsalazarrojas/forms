# Sports Facility Fence Wrap Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for processing custom printing orders for sports facilities. It gathers necessary information about the customer, the facility, and the order details to ensure timely and accurate delivery of the printed items.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the **Customer Email** field.
2. Enter the name of the **Facility Name** where the printing will be performed.
3. Select the **Facility Type** from the provided options (Sports, Venue, or Print Shop).
4. Upload an **Artwork** file if you need custom artwork for the printing.
5. Enter the **Uploader Information** related to the artwork upload.
6. Select the **Payment Method** (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
7. Enter the **Order Quantity** of the printing order.
8. Add any **Order Notes** if necessary.
9. Provide the **Shipping Address** where the printed items will be shipped.
10. Enter the **Billing Address** for payment and order processing purposes.
11. Select the **Payment Status** (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
12. Upload any additional **File** if necessary.
13. Enter **Additional File Information** about the uploaded file.
14. Enter the **Facility Location** where the printing will be performed.
15. Enter the **Facility Phone** number.
16. Enter the **Facility Fax** number.
17. Enter the **Facility Contact Person** name.
18. Enter the **Facility Contact Email** address.
19. Enter the **Facility Website** URL.
20. Add any **Facility Notes** if necessary.
21. Enter the **Order Date** of the printing order.
22. Enter the **Order Due Date** for the printing order.
23. Select the **Order Status** (Approved, Approved - In Progress, Completed, or Rejected).
24. Upload **Proof of Payment** if necessary.
25. Enter the **Proof of Payment Information**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and further communication.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the printing will be performed.
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility (Sports, Venue, or Print Shop).
* **Artwork Upload**: Upload a file related to the artwork design for the printing.
* **Uploader Information**: Provide any additional information related to the artwork upload.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Order Quantity**: Enter the number of printed items ordered.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where the printed items will be shipped.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address for payment and order processing purposes.
* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Upload Additional File**: Upload any additional file related to the order.
* **Additional File Information**: Provide any additional information about the uploaded file.
* **Facility Location**: Enter the location of the facility where the printing will be performed.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility Fax**: Enter the fax number of the facility.
* **Facility Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person at the facility.
* **Facility Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person at the facility.
* **Facility Website**: Enter the website URL of the facility.
* **Facility Notes**: Add any additional notes about the facility.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the printing order.
* **Order Due Date**: Enter the due date for the printing order.
* **Order Status**: Select the status of the order (Approved, Approved - In Progress, Completed, or Rejected).
* **Upload Proof of Payment**: Upload proof of payment if necessary.
* **Proof of Payment Information**: Enter any additional information about the proof of payment.
