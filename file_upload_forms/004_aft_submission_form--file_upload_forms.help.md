# AFT Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AFT Submission Form is a tool for requesters to submit new requests or updates to existing submissions for AFT review and processing. This form is designed to gather essential information to ensure efficient and effective processing of requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Requester name" and "Requester email" fields respectively. These fields are required to ensure we can contact you for follow-up and confirmation.
2. Choose the type of AFT request from the "Request type" dropdown menu. This will help us determine the nature of your request.
3. Provide a brief description of your submission in the "Brief description of submission" field. This will help us understand the context and purpose of your request.
4. If applicable, upload any supporting documents in the "Supporting documents" field. This can include files that will aid in processing your request.
5. Set a desired completion date if you have a specific deadline for the request. This is optional.
6. Select the priority level of your request from the "Priority level" dropdown menu. This will help us prioritize your request among others.
7. Enter any additional notes or information in the "Additional notes" field. This can include any details that will help us process your request efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the contact person for this request.
* **Requester email** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
* **Request type** (`request_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the nature of your AFT request (New submission, Update to existing, Inquiry).
* **Brief description of submission** (`description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your submission to help us understand the context and purpose.
* **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, `note`, required): Upload any files required for review (e.g., images, videos, or other relevant attachments).
* **Desired completion date** (`desired_completion_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, provide a target date for completion.
* **Priority level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the urgency of your request (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information to help us process your request efficiently.
