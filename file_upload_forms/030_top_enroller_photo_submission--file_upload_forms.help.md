# top_enroller_photo_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow top-enrollers to submit their photos to commemorate their achievement. This form is for users who have reached the top-enroller level in a specific program or contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you are a top-enroller.
2. Upload a photo to represent yourself as a top-enroller.
3. Provide any additional details about the participant.
4. Enter your name to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: Confirm that you are a top-enroller by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
* **Participant Photo**: Upload a photo that best represents you as a top-enroller. The photo can be of you, your team, or the achievement itself.
* **Participant Details**: Provide any additional information about the participant, such as the program or contest you participated in.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name to confirm your identity as a top-enroller. This is a required field.
