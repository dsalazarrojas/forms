# Casting Call Submission Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit applications for acting auditions. To submit a casting call, please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your submission. Make sure you are not duplicating a submission for the same production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the submission date.
2. Enter your Casting Call ID or reference number from the original announcement.
3. Provide your Production Title (name of the project you are auditioning for).
4. Enter your Full Name (legal or professional name).
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Enter your Email Address and Phone Number.
7. Enter your City and State of residence.
8. Choose your Primary Role Applied For.
9. If applicable, choose Secondary Roles you are interested in.
10. Upload your Headshot Photo and Resume or CV.
11. If applicable, provide a Demo Reel or Video Link and Additional Photos.
12. If applicable, provide information on Years of Acting Experience, Previous Film/TV/Theater Credits, and Special Skills or Talents.
13. If applicable, provide Agent or Manager Contact information.
14. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Casting Call ID or Reference** (`casting_call_id`, required): From the original casting announcement.
* **Production Title** (`production_title`, required): Name of the project you are auditioning for.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Legal or professional name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Your phone number.
* **City and State** (`city_state`, required): Where you are based.
* **Primary Role Applied For** (`primary_role`, required): Choose your primary role applied for.
* **Secondary Roles** (`secondary_roles`, optional): Choose secondary roles you are interested in.
* **Headshot Photo** (`headshot_upload`, required): Upload a professional headshot (JPG/PNG/PDF).
* **Resume or CV** (`resume_upload`, required): Upload your acting resume (PDF/DOC/DOCX).
* **Demo Reel or Video Link** (`reel_video`, optional): Upload YouTube, Vimeo, or similar platform link.
* **Additional Photos** (`additional_photos`, optional): Upload up to 3 additional photos.
* **Years of Acting Experience** (`years_of_experience`, required): Your years of acting experience.
* **Previous Film/TV/Theater Credits** (`previous_work`, optional): List major credits or projects.
* **Special Skills or Talents** (`special_skills`, optional): List special skills or talents (Languages, Accents, Stunts, Sports, etc.).
* **Agent or Manager Contact** (`agent_contact`, optional): If applicable, provide agent or manager contact information.
