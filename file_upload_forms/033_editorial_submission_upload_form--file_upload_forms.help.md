# editorial_submission_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for editorial submissions. It allows users to upload relevant files and provide information about themselves as contributors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the first page.
2. Click on the "File Upload" field to select the file you want to upload.
3. Choose the file you want to upload from your computer or other devices.
4. Fill out the "Contributor" field with your name.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Once you've filled out all the required and optional fields, click on the "Submit" button to submit your editorial submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **File Upload**: Upload a relevant file to support your editorial submission. This field is not required.
* **Contributor**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is not required.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
- If you're having trouble uploading a file, try checking the file size and format to ensure they meet the required specifications.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
