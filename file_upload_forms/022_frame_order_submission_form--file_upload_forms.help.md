# Frame Order Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to submit their frame orders, providing the necessary information for the order to be processed and fulfilled.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Order Submission Form with your name, contact information, and order details.
2. If you have artwork to upload, attach it to the Artwork Upload field. You can upload a single file or multiple files.
3. Select your delivery or pickup preferences from the dropdown options in the Delivery or Pickup Preferences field.
4. Manage your data collection and form submission in the Manage Data Collection and Form Submission field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Submission Form** (`order_submission_form`, text, optional): Fill in your name, contact information, and order details in this field. This form is used to get a better understanding of your frame order.
* **Artwork Upload** (`artwork_upload`, text, optional): Upload any artwork associated with your order. You can attach a single file or multiple files to this field.
* **Delivery or Pickup Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, optional): Choose your delivery or pickup preferences from the options provided. This helps us determine the best way to deliver or pickup your order.
* **Manage Data Collection and Form Submission** (`manage_submission`, text, optional): This field is used to manage data collection and form submission. Please refer to the instructions for this field for more information on how to use it.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid delays or miscommunication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can use this form for any type of frame order submission, but please refer to the terms and conditions for any specific guidelines or limitations.
