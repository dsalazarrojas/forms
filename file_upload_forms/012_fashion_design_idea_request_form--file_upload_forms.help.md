# Fashion Design Idea Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit fashion design ideas to be assessed and developed. Please fill in the required fields to describe your design concept and attach any relevant files or images.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a concise and descriptive title of your design concept.
2. Select a **Category** from the options provided to help us understand the type of design you are submitting.
3. Write a **Description** of your design concept in the space provided.
4. If applicable, upload **Files** related to your design concept using the **File Upload** field.
5. If applicable, upload **Images** related to your design concept using the **Image Upload** field.
6. Indicate whether your design concept includes any **References** by selecting the appropriate option.
7. Provide a **Timeline** for when you would like to see your design concept developed or implemented.
8. Set a **Deadline** for when you would like to see your design concept developed or implemented.
9. Click the **Submit** button to submit your design concept.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of your design concept. Please make it concise and descriptive.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best fits your design concept from the options provided.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Write a detailed description of your design concept in this field.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, required): If you have any relevant files related to your design concept, upload them here.
* **Image Upload** (`image_upload`, text, required): If you have any relevant images related to your design concept, upload them here.
* **Design Concept** (`design_concept`, text, required): Describe your design concept in detail.
* **References** (`references`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether your design concept includes any references by selecting the appropriate option.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, required): Provide a timeline for when you would like to see your design concept developed or implemented.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required): Set a deadline for when you would like to see your design concept developed or implemented.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your design concept for review and development.
