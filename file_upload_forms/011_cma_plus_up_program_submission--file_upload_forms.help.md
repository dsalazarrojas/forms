# CMA Plus Up Program Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CMA Plus Up Program Submission form is used to collect information from applicants for the CMA Plus Up program. This program provides funding to support projects and initiatives that benefit the community. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and supporting documents before submitting your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the applicant.
2. Provide your contact email address for communication purposes.
3. Specify the CMA program or funding stream you are requesting.
4. Enter the requested amount of funding in local currency.
5. Provide a brief summary of how the funds will be used in the project.
6. List the names of supporting documents uploaded with your application.
7. (Optional) Enter the number of staff or volunteers in your organization.
8. (Optional) Provide the expected start and end dates for your project.
9. (Optional) Enter the name of the person responsible for financial reporting.
10. Finally, select the date of application submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Name (`applicant_name`, text, required)
* The field is for your name as the applicant, whether it is an individual or an organization.

### Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, required)
* Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your application.

### Program Requested (`program_requested`, text, required)
* Choose the CMA program or funding stream you are interested in applying for.

### Request Amount (`request_amount`, number, required)
* Enter the exact amount of funding you are requesting in local currency.

### Project Summary (`project_summary`, text, required)
* Provide a brief summary of how the funds will be used in the project.

### Supporting Documents List (`supporting_documents`, text, optional)
* List the names or descriptions of any supporting documents uploaded with your application.

### Organization Size (`organization_size`, number, optional)
* If your organization has staff or volunteers, enter the number of them.

### Project Timeline (`project_timeline`, text, optional)
* Enter the expected start and end dates for your project.

### Fiscal Contact Name (`fiscal_contact`, text, optional)
* If someone else is responsible for financial reporting, enter their name.

### Submission Date (`submission_date`, date, required)
* Enter the date you are submitting your application.

# Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* Provide clear and concise information in each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
