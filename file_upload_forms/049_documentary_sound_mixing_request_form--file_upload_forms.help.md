# Documentary Sound Mixing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to request sound mixing services for their documentary project. Please fill out the required fields to ensure prompt and accurate processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the sound mixing work required for your documentary.
2. Upload the sound file(s) associated with your project.
3. Specify the deadline for the sound mixing work.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your project.
5. Enter your name and contact information so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sound Mix Brief (required)**: A brief description of the sound mixing work needed for your documentary. Please provide a clear and concise summary of the project's requirements.
* **Sound Mix File (required)**: Upload the sound file(s) associated with your project. Ensure the file is in the correct format and size for processing.
* **Mix Deadline (optional)**: If you have a specific deadline for the sound mixing work, please enter it here. This will help us prioritize your request.
* **Mix Note (optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your project that may help us during the sound mixing process.
* **Client Name (required)**: Enter your name as our client representative.
* **Contact Email (required)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Contact Phone (required)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any questions or updates.
* **Submit**: Once you've filled out all required fields, click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your request.
