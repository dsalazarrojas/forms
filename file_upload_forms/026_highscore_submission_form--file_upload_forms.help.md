# Highscore Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Highscore Submission Form is designed to gather essential information from users to track and maintain accurate highscore records. This form is used to collect data on user-submitted highscores, ensuring that the records are up-to-date and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **User Information**
	* **User Information (id: 1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to identify yourself as the submitter.
2. **File Uploads (id: 2)**: Select 'Yes' if you are uploading files to support your highscore entry.
3. **Highscore Entry (id: 3)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are submitting a highscore entry.
4. **Date Submitted (id: 4)**: Enter the date on which you submitted the highscore entry.
5. **Proof Link (id: 5)**: Provide a link to any supporting proof or evidence required for the highscore entry.
6. **Review**
	* **Review (id: 6)**: Enter a brief review or comment about your highscore entry.
7. **Contact Information (id: 7-10)**: Enter your email and phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
8. **Proof Details (id: 11-12)**: Provide a description of your proof and upload any supporting files.
9. **Review Schedule (id: 13-14)**: Set a date and time for the review to be completed.
10. **User Confirmation (id: 15-20)**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
11. **Submit (id: 21)**: Click the 'Submit' button to complete and submit the highscore entry.
