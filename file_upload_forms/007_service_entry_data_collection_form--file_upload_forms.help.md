# service_entry_data_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their service entries, including customer details, order information, service details, status, additional information, frequency, service type, and notes. This data is essential for our services team to efficiently process and manage customer requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (* indicates required field).
2. Review each field for any hints or explanations provided.
3. Provide accurate information about your service entry.
4. Submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (1)** (`service_entry_customer_info`, `text`, *): This field is used to collect basic customer information, such as name, email, and contact number. Please provide your name and any relevant contact details.
* **Order Information (2)** (`service_entry_order_info`, `text`, *): This field is used to collect details about the order related to your service entry. Please provide order details such as order number, date, and order total.
* **Service Details (3)** (`service_entry_service_details`, `text`, *): This field is used to collect specific details about the service entry, such as the service type and service description. Please provide a brief description of your service entry.
* **Service Status (4)** (`service_entry_service_status`, `text`, *): This field is used to indicate the current status of your service entry. Please select the current status from the available options, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Additional Information (5)** (`service_entry_additional_info`, `text`, ): This field is used to provide any additional information about your service entry. Please use this field for any further details that are not covered in the previous fields.
* **Service Frequency (6)** (`service_entry_frequency`, `text`, ): This field is used to indicate how frequently you plan to use our services. Please select the frequency from the available options, such as "Once", "Weekly", "Monthly", etc.
* **Service Type (7)** (`service_entry_service_type`, `text`, ): This field is used to indicate the type of service you are using. Please select the service type from the available options, such as "Consulting", "Development", "Testing", etc.
* **Notes (8)** (`service_entry_notes`, `text`, ): This field is used to provide any additional notes or comments about your service entry. Please use this field for any further comments or questions.

## Tips
* Be thorough when filling in the required fields (* indicates required field).
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us through our support channel.
