# Travel Blog Video Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Blog Video Upload Form is designed to help you upload videos related to travel blog content.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Video Title" field with a brief and descriptive title for your video.
2. Provide a detailed description of your video in the "Video Description" field.
3. Select the type of content you are uploading (video, image, document, etc.) in the "Video Type" field.
4. Add relevant tags to your video in the "Tags" field.
5. Choose the license type for your video (all, restricted, public) in the "License" field.
6. Enter the author of the video in the "Author" field.
7. Select the date and time of the video upload in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. Enter a valid phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter the URL of your video in the "URL" field (if applicable).
11. Add any additional comments or notes about your video in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Video Title** (`video_title`, text, required): Enter a brief and descriptive title for your video.
*   **Video Description** (`video_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your video.
*   **Video Type** (`video_type`, select multiple, required): Select the type of content you are uploading (video, image, document, etc.).
*   **Tags** (`video_tags`, text, required): Add relevant tags to your video.
*   **License** (`video_license`, select one, required): Choose the license type for your video (all, restricted, public).
*   **Author** (`video_author`, text, required): Enter the author of the video.
*   **Date** (`video_date`, date, required): Select the date of the video upload.
*   **Time** (`video_time`, time, required): Select the time of the video upload.
*   **Phone** (`video_phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number.
*   **Email** (`video_email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address.
*   **URL** (`video_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of your video (if applicable).
*   **Comments** (`video_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your video.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and consistently.
*   Use relevant tags to help your video get discovered.
*   Choose the correct license type for your video content.
