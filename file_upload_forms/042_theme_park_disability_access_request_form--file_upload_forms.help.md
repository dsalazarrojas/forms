# Theme Park Disability Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theme Park Disability Access Request Form is a form designed to collect information about visitors with disabilities to provide them with an accessible experience at the theme park. This form is a required step in the park's accessibility accommodations process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your visitor information, including your name and contact details.
2. Select the date and time of your visit.
3. Provide details about your accessibility needs, including any requests or accommodations you require.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number.
5. Leave any additional contact notes for our staff, if needed.
6. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Information** (`visitor_info`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant visitor information.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your visit to the theme park.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of your visit to the theme park.
* **Accessibility Request** (`accessibility_request`, `text`, required): Describe your accessibility needs and any requests you have for accommodations.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, `text`, required): Provide more detailed information about your accessibility needs, if needed.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email so we can reach you with updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you with updates.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional contact notes for our staff, if needed.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional review notes for our staff, if needed.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Review your form and submit it to us.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your accessibility needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email or phone number.
* A member of our team will review your form and contact you to discuss your accessibility accommodations.
