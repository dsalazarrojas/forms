# Character Merchandise Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit character merchandise proposals and files for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Submitter Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Enter the name of the proposed merchandise in the "Product Name" field.
4. Add a brief description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
5. If applicable, enter the suggested retail price in the "Suggested Retail Price" field.
6. Attach design mockups or photos in the "Product Files" field. Please include relevant links or references.
7. Provide the estimated production time in the "Manufacturing Lead Time" field.
8. Choose the status of the licensing agreement from the drop-down menu.
9. If applicable, add any additional notes or information in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as the creator or vendor of the merchandise.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Enter your contact email address for follow-up communication.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the proposed merchandise.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, Text, Required): Provide a brief description of the merchandise.
* **Suggested Retail Price** (`suggested_retail_price`, Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the suggested retail price of the merchandise.
* **Product Files** (`product_files`, Text, Required): Attach design mockups or photos of the merchandise. Please include relevant links or references.
* **Manufacturing Lead Time** (`manufacturing_lead_time`, Number, Optional): Enter the estimated production time in weeks.
* **Licensing Agreement Status** (`licensing_agreement_status`, Select One, Required): Choose the status of the licensing agreement from the drop-down menu.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, Text, Optional): Add any other relevant information about the merchandise.
