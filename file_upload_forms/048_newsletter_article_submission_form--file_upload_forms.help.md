### Newsletter Article Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect articles for newsletters. Please use it to submit your articles for consideration for publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Submit Article**: This is the first page of the form, where you can submit your article.
2.  **Article Content**: In this field, provide the content of your article. You can type in the text as much as you want.
3.  **Article Title**: Enter a title for your article that will be displayed in the newsletter.
4.  **Submitter Information**: Provide some information about yourself, such as your name and contact email or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Submit Article**: This page is where you start by submitting your article. Simply click the "Submit" button to begin.
*   **Article Content**: In this text field, you can write as much as you want about your article. Please make sure to keep it concise and clear, as it will be edited for publication.
*   **Article Title**: This field is for the title of your article. It should be descriptive and attention-grabbing so that readers can quickly understand what your article is about.
*   **Submitter Information**: This field is for you to provide some information about yourself, such as your name, email, or phone number. This is for editorial purposes and will not be shared publicly unless you give explicit consent.

## Tips

*   Make sure to proofread your article before submitting it, as it will be reviewed for clarity and accuracy.
*   Keep your article within a reasonable length, about 300-400 words.

This help guide is meant to assist you in understanding the form's purpose and how to fill it out. If you have any further questions or need additional clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.

Please note that this form is designed to be used for newsletter submissions only and should not be used for other purposes.

We appreciate your understanding and look forward to receiving your article submissions!
