# Construction Permit Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Permit Upload Form is used to collect information from applicants and verify that all required documents and calculations are attached for construction projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the form link or navigate to the form page.
2.  Select the "Applicant Identification" section and fill in the required fields with your details.
3.  Provide your name, company name (if applicable), email address, and phone number.
4.  Move on to the "Project Details" section and enter your project site address, including city, state, and ZIP code.
5.  In the "Document Verification" section, confirm that the permit application document has been uploaded to the portal (if applicable) or indicate that it will be sent via mail.
6.  Upload your site plan, structural calculations, and signed contract copy as required.
7.  In the "Additional Information" section, provide any additional details or comments.
8.  Confirm whether expedited review is requested and if the permit has been previously denied.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Applicant** (`applicant_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Company Name (If Applicable)** (`company_name`): If you are representing a company, enter its name here.
* **Primary Email Address** (`applicant_email`): Enter your primary email address, where updates will be sent.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`): Enter your phone number in the format `(555) 555-5555`.
* **Project Site Address** (`project_address`): Enter the address of the project site, including city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Permit Type Requested** (`permit_type_req`): Select the main permit category for your project.
* **Existing Permit Number (If Applicable)** (`permit_number_ref`): If you are submitting a renewal or revision, enter the existing permit number.
* **Brief Description of Work** (`description_of_work`): Describe the project work in 500 characters or less.
* **Permit Application Document Uploaded** (`permit_docs_uploaded`): Confirm that the permit application document has been uploaded to the portal (if applicable) or indicate that it will be sent via mail.
* **Site Plan Uploaded** (`site_plan_uploaded`): Confirm whether a site plan has been uploaded (if required).
* **Structural Calculations Uploaded** (`structural_calcs_uploaded`): Confirm whether structural calculations have been uploaded (if required).
* **Signed Contract Copy Uploaded** (`contract_copy_uploaded`): Confirm whether a signed contract copy has been uploaded (if required).
* **Expedited Review Requested?** (`expedited_review_req`): Check if you are requesting an expedited review.
* **Has this permit been previously denied?** (`previous_denials`): Confirm whether the permit has been previously denied.
* **Reason for Previous Denial** (`denial_reason_desc`): If the permit was previously denied, provide a reason.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of submission.

Note: This form is a critical document for construction projects. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and correctly. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the authorities for clarification.
