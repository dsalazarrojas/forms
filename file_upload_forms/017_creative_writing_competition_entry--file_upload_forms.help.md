# Creative Writing Competition Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect entries for the Creative Writing Competition. It allows users to provide a title, description, and upload a file for their entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a unique Form ID to identify your entry.
2. Select the category that best fits your entry.
3. Provide a title for your entry.
4. Enter a description of your entry.
5. Choose the file type and upload the corresponding file.
6. Decide whether category uploads are allowed (yes or no).
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (`1`, `number`, `not required`): Enter a unique ID to identify your entry.
* **Category** (`2`, `text`, `not required`): Choose the category that best fits your entry (e.g., poetry, fiction, non-fiction).
* **Title** (`3`, `text`, `not required`): Provide a title for your entry.
* **Description** (`4`, `text`, `not required`): Enter a brief description of your entry.
* **Category Other** (`5`, `text`, `not required`): If none of the above categories fit, enter a custom category.
* **File Type** (`6`, `text`, `not required`): Choose the type of file you are uploading (e.g., document, image, video).
* **File** (`7`, `text`, `not required`): Upload the file associated with your entry.
* **Submit** (`8`, `text`, `not required`): Click the submit button to submit your entry.
* **Category Uploads** (`9`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select whether category uploads are allowed (yes or no).
* **Form ID** (`10`, `text`, `not required`): Enter the same Form ID again to confirm.
