# Magazine Writing Challenge Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from authors submitting their written work for review and consideration for publication in a magazine. It gathers contact details, submission information, and publication status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the contact information fields: first name, last name, and email address. 
2. Enter your phone number in the designated field. 
3. Provide a brief description of the manuscript in the `manuscript` field. 
4. Write a short title describing the submission in the `entry_title` field. 
5. Give a detailed description of the entry in the `entry_details` field. 
6. Choose the type of entry you are submitting (fiction or non-fiction). 
7. If applicable, provide your school affiliation in the `school_affiliation` field. 
8. List the publication this entry will be submitted to (not visible in the example). 
9. Choose the category of the submission (not visible in the example). 
10. Select the status of the submission from the provided options (pending, published, or rejected). 
11. Add any comments regarding the submission. 
12. Specify who submitted the form (user or organization). 
13. Enter the date and time the submission was made in the `submission_date_time` field. 
14. Enter the date of the submission in the `submission_date` field. 
15. Provide your name and email address in the `author_details_name` and `author_details_email` fields. 
16. Enter your phone number in the `author_details_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **manuscript**: Brief description of the manuscript.
* **entry_title**: A short title describing the submission.
* **entry_details**: Detailed description of the entry.
* **entry_type**: Choose the type of entry (fiction or non-fiction).
* **school_affiliation**: If applicable, provide your school affiliation.
* **publication**: Enter the publication this entry will be submitted to.
* **category**: Choose the category of the submission (not visible in the example).
* **submission_status**: Select the status of the submission (pending, published, or rejected).
* **comments**: Add any comments regarding the submission.
* **form_submitted_by**: Specify who submitted the form (user or organization).
* **submission_date_time**: Enter the date and time the submission was made.
* **submission_date**: Enter the date of the submission.
* **author_details_name**: Enter your name.
* **author_details_email**: Enter your email address.
* **author_details_phone**: Enter your phone number.
