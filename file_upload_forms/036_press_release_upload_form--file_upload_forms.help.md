# press_release_upload_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for uploading press releases. It helps our system to process and manage the releases efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the title of the press release in the "Press Release" field.
2. Enter the date of the upload in the "Upload Date" field.
3. Upload the relevant file related to the press release in the "File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Press Release** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the press release you are uploading.
* **Upload Date** (`upload_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are uploading the press release.
* **File** (`file`, text, optional): Upload the file associated with the press release.
