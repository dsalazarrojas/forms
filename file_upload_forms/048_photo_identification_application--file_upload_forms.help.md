# Photo Identification Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Identification Application form is designed to collect necessary information for photo identification purposes. This form asks for a title, description, and other relevant details to verify an individual's identity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a title for the photo identification application, describing the purpose of the application.
2. Categorize the application as required.
3. Provide a description of the photo identification application.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" for Businesses and Institutions questions, depending on your association with the relevant businesses or institutions.
5. Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the photo identification application.
6. Make sure to select one of the options for Businesses and Institutions questions.
7. Enter the name of the relevant Organisation.
8. Enter the name of the Businesses and Institutions Organisation.
9. Answer "Yes" or "No" for the Businesses and Institutions question.
10. Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the photo identification application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* 1. **Photo Identification Application Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required): Enter a descriptive title for the photo identification application.
* 2. **Category** (`form_category`, `text`, required): Categorize the application as required.
* 3. **Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the photo identification application.
* 4. **Businesses and Institutions** (`form_businesses_institutions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" if you are associated with the relevant business or institution, or "No" if not.
* 5. **Photographs** (`form_photographs`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant photographs or image details.
* 6. **Additional Information** (`form_additional_information`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the photo identification application.
* 7. **Businesses and Institutions** (`form_business_institution`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options for Businesses and Institutions.
* 8. **Organisation** (`form_organisation`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the relevant Organisation.
* 9. **Businesses and Institutions Organisation** (`form_businesses_institutions_organisation`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the Businesses and Institutions Organisation.
* 10. **Businesses and Institutions** (`form_business_institution_2`, `text`, required): Select one of the options for Businesses and Institutions.
* 11. **Additional Information** (`form_additional_information_2`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the photo identification application.
* 12. **Businesses and Institutions** (`form_business_institution_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for the Businesses and Institutions question.
