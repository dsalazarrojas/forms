# Artist Demo Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your demo tracks for review, festival bookings, collaboration opportunities, or other purposes. Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the artist information section with your name, email, phone number, location, and website or social media link.
2. Provide a brief bio (100-200 words) about yourself and your music.
3. Specify your primary and secondary genres.
4. Enter the title of your demo project.
5. Specify the number of tracks, total duration, and upload your demo file.
6. List the tracks and credits for each song.
7. Provide information about the production process.
8. Specify the purpose of your submission and confirm that you own the rights to the work.
9. Agree to the submission terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Artist or Band Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name as you want to be credited.
*   **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, **required**): Provide your primary contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Location or City** (`artist_location`, `text`, **optional**): Specify where you are based.
*   **Website or Social Media** (`artist_website`, `text`, **optional**): Link to your online presence.
*   **Primary Genre** (`primary_genre`, `text`, **required**): Select your main music genre.
*   **Secondary Genres** (`secondary_genres`, `text`, **optional**): List other genres you work with.
*   **Artist Biography** (`artist_bio`, `text`, **required**): Write a brief bio (100-200 words) about yourself and your music.
*   **Years Active** (`years_active`, `number`, **optional**): Specify how long you have been making music.
*   **Demo Title** (`demo_title`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of your demo project.
*   **Number of Tracks in Demo** (`number_of_tracks`, `number`, **required**): Specify how many songs are included.
*   **Total Duration of Demo** (`demo_duration`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the total length in minutes.
*   **Demo File Upload** (`demo_file_upload`, `text`, **required**): Upload your demo MP3 or audio file.
*   **Streaming Link or URL** (`streaming_link`, `text`, **optional**): Link to your music on Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube, etc.
*   **Track Listing and Credits** (`track_listing`, `text`, **required**): List each song with composer/producer credits.
*   **Production Notes** (`production_notes`, `text`, **optional**): Provide information about the production process.
*   **Purpose of This Submission** (`submission_purpose`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the reason for submitting your demo.
*   **I Confirm I Hold Copyright to This Work** (`copyright_confirmation`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm you own the rights to this work.
*   **I Agree to the Submission Terms** (`submission_agreement`, `select_one`, **required**): Accept the submission guidelines.
