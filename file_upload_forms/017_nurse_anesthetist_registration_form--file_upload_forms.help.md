# Nurse Anesthetist Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration details from nurse anesthetists for various purposes such as licensure, certification, or professional development. Completing this form accurately and fully will help ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Complete all required fields (indicated by asterisk * below).
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details (1)** (`Registration Details`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing your name, title, or any other relevant details about your registration.
* **Medical Licenses (2)** (`Medical Licenses`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the provided medical licenses that apply to your registration.
* **Medical Specialties (3)** (`Medical Specialties`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one medical specialty that best describes your area of expertise.
* **Address (4)** (`Address`, `number`, optional): Enter your address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone (5)** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Medical School (6)** (`Medical School`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your medical school if applicable.
* **Hospital Affiliation (7)** (`Hospital Affiliation`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your hospital affiliation if applicable.
* **Work Experience (8)** (`Work Experience`, `date`, optional): Enter your work experience in the format of a date (e.g., 01-01-2020).
* **Medical Degree (9)** (`Medical Degree`, `text`, optional): Enter your medical degree if different from your name.
* **Years of Experience (10)** (`Years of Experience`, `number`, optional): Enter your years of experience as a nurse anesthetist.
* **Emergency Contact (11)** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the information of your emergency contact person.
* **Upload Documents (12)** (`Upload Documents`, `text`, optional): Upload relevant documents for your registration (e.g., license, certification, medical degree, etc.).
* **Upload Signature (13)** (`Upload Signature`, `text`, optional): Upload your signature for registration verification.
* **Upload License (14)** (`Upload License`, `text`, optional): Upload your medical license for registration verification.
* **Upload Medical Certificate (15)** (`Upload Medical Certificate`, `text`, optional): Upload your medical certificate for registration verification.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when providing information.
- Use clear and legible handwriting for uploaded documents and signatures.
- Ensure uploaded documents and signatures are relevant to your registration.
