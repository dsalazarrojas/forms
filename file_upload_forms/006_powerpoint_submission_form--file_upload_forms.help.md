# PowerPoint Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PowerPoint Submission Form is designed to collect information for submissions to our organization. This form is meant to be used for general submission purposes, such as proposals, reports, or other formal applications. Please ensure that you are using this form for its intended purpose and follow the guidelines below for a successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a clear and concise title for your submission.
2. Upload any supporting files required for your submission.
3. Enter the date and time of your submission.
4. Provide submission details in the space provided.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Enter your contact phone number (optional).
7. Enter your name and title.
8. Enter the name of your organization.
9. Enter the department or office of the submission.
10. Enter the location of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title for your submission.
* **Upload File 1** (`file_upload_1`, text, optional): Upload any supporting files required for your submission.
* **Upload File 2** (`file_upload_2`, text, optional): This field is for uploading any additional files required for your submission.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, required): Enter the date when you submitted your proposal.
* **Time of Submission** (`time_of_submission`, time, required): Enter the time when you submitted your proposal.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, note, required): Provide a brief description of your submission, including any relevant details or comments.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you have a contact phone number, enter it here.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department or office of your submission.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of your submission.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out in order to successfully submit the form.
