# Photo Contest Free Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic contact information from users to enter a photo contest. It's a simple and easy-to-use form that doesn't require any sensitive or unnecessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name (if you wish to provide it).
2. Enter your email address (if you wish to provide it).
3. (Optional) Enter your phone number (if you wish to provide it).
4. Enter any additional information or comments in the "photo_contest_free_entry" field (if you wish to provide it).
5. Click the submit button to submit your entry.

* **email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is an optional email field where you can enter a valid email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is an optional text field where you can enter your phone number.
