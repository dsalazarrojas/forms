# Casting Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casting Inquiry Form is used for talent to inquire about a casting opportunity, providing detailed information about themselves and their availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact details in the "Talent Name", "Talent Email", and "Talent Phone Number" fields.
2. Select the type of production you are inquiring about (Film, Television, Theater, Commercial, Web Series, or Other).
3. Enter the title of the production (e.g. "The Avengers" or "The Great Gatsby").
4. Select the role you are interested in (Lead, Supporting, Featured Extra, Extra, or Recurring).
5. Estimate the screen time for the role (if applicable).
6. Provide a headshot and resume submission information (submit or not submit).
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about your submission (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`):
	+ Enter the date you are inquiring about (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Production Title** (`production_title`):
	+ Enter the title of the production (e.g. "The Avengers" or "The Great Gatsby").
* **Production Type** (`production_type`):
	+ Select the type of production (Film, Television, Theater, Commercial, Web Series, or Other).
* **Talent Name** (`talent_name`):
	+ Enter your name as a talent.
* **Talent Email** (`talent_email`):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Talent Phone Number** (`talent_phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Talent Age or Age Range** (`talent_age`):
	+ Enter your age or age range (if applicable).
* **Character Role** (`character_role`):
	+ Enter the name and brief description of the character you are interested in playing.
* **Role Description** (`role_description`):
	+ Enter the character's background, personality, and key traits.
* **Role Size** (`role_size`):
	+ Select the role type (Lead, Supporting, Featured Extra, Extra, or Recurring).
* **Estimated Screen Time** (`screen_time_estimate`):
	+ Enter the estimated screen time for the role (if applicable).
* **Headshot Submitted** (`headshot_uploaded`):
	+ Select if you have submitted a headshot (True or False).
* **Resume Submitted** (`resume_uploaded`):
	+ Select if you have submitted a resume (True or False).
* **Demo Reel or Portfolio Link** (`demo_reel_link`):
	+ Enter a URL to a video samples or online portfolio.
* **Previous Relevant Work** (`previous_work`):
	+ Enter any previous relevant work or productions similar to this role.
* **Shooting Dates** (`shooting_dates`):
	+ Enter the shooting dates (if applicable).
* **Can work at shooting location** (`location_availability`):
	+ Select if you are available to work at the shooting location (True, False, or Needs relocation assistance).
* **Special Skills or Requirements** (`special_skills`):
	+ Enter any special skills or requirements (stunts, languages, accents, etc.).
* **Union Status** (`union_status`):
	+ Select your union status (Union, Non-union, or Either).
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your submission (optional).
