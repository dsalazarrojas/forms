# Image Annotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from clients or vendors for image annotation tasks. Please fill out the following fields to provide a complete and accurate request.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form title and provide a brief description of the project.
2.  Upload the required image file for annotation.
3.  Specify your project's requirements and deadlines.
4.  Provide contact information for you and your team.
5.  Confirm that you've read and agree to our terms and conditions and data privacy agreement.
6.  Set a submission date and time for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Please provide a brief title for your image annotation project.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A brief description of the project will help us understand what you need.
*   **Image Upload** (`image_upload`, file, required): Upload the image that needs to be annotated.
*   **Requirements** (`requirements`, text, optional): Specify any specific requirements for the annotation process.
*   **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required): Set a deadline for the completion of the annotation task.
*   **Team Upload** (`team_upload`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of your team.
*   **Team Requirements** (`team_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any specific requirements for your team's interaction with our service.
*   **Team Deadline** (`team_deadline`, date, required): Set a deadline for your team's submission.
*   **Vendor Upload** (`vendor_upload`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of your vendor.
*   **Vendor Requirements** (`vendor_requirements`, text, required): Specify any specific requirements for the annotation process.
*   **Vendor Deadline** (`vendor_deadline`, date, required): Set a deadline for the completion of the annotation task by the vendor.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide a valid phone number.
*   **Team Email** (`team_email`, email, required): Provide the email address of your team member.
*   **Team Phone** (`team_phone`, text, required): Provide the phone number of your team member.
*   **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address for your vendor.
*   **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, required): Provide a valid phone number for your vendor.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Agree to our terms and conditions.
*   **Data Privacy Agreement** (`data_privacy_agreement`, note, required): Agree to our data privacy agreement.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Set a submission date.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Set a submission time.
*   **Submission Deadline** (`submission_deadline`, date, required): Set a deadline for submission.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but please note that you will be required to fill out all required fields.
