# monologue_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit entries to a monologue tournament, including a written or recorded monologue and accompanying images (headshot and two photos).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the respective fields.
2. Provide a text-based Monologue in the designated field.
3. Upload a Headshot image in the "Headshot" field.
4. Upload a Monologue Video Recording in the "Monologue Video Recording" field.
5. (Optional) Upload two Photo 1 and Photo 2 images in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication and verification purposes.
* **Monologue**: Write your monologue for the tournament. This can be any type of speech, such as a written essay or a recorded video.
* **Headshot**: Upload a headshot image to provide a visual identity.
* **Monologue Video Recording**: Upload your monologue in video format. Please ensure the recording is clear and well-structured.
* **Photo 1**: (Optional) Upload a photo to showcase yourself. This field is not required.
* **Photo 2**: (Optional) Upload another photo to showcase yourself. This field is not required.
* **Photo 2**: (Optional) Same as Photo 1, this field is for another photo of yourself. 

Note: The optional fields (Photo 1 and Photo 2) are not required to complete the form, but they can be useful for additional context.
