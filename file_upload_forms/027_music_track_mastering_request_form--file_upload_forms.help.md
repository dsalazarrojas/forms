# Music Track Mastering Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Track Mastering Request Form is designed to help artists and producers request mastering services for their music tracks. This form gathers essential information about the track, the mixing and mastering preferences, deadlines, and other details that will help our mastering team process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the music track info, including a brief description of the track.
2.  Select the mix file you want to upload for mastering.
3.  Specify your mastering preferences, such as compression, EQ, etc.
4.  Enter any deadlines for the project.
5.  Add any additional notes you have for the mastering engineer.
6.  Provide your artist email for communication and updates.
7.  Finally, add any studio-specific information that's relevant to the mastering request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Music Track Info**: Enter a brief description of the music track you want to master. This will help our mastering team familiarize themselves with your work.
*   **Upload Mix**: Select the mix file you want to upload for mastering. Choose "audio file," "audio file 2," or "audio file 3" based on the mix version you are submitting.
*   **Mastering Preferences**: Specify your preferred mastering settings, such as compression, EQ, etc. This will help our engineers tailor the mastering process to your taste.
*   **Deadlines**: Enter any relevant deadlines for the project, such as a specific delivery date or a preferred timeline.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions you'd like to share with our mastering team.
*   **Artist Email**: Enter your artist email address so we can contact you for updates, questions, or further details.
*   **Studio Info**: Provide any relevant studio information, such as the studio equipment used or any specific settings that would help our team with the mastering process.
