# Wine And Meal News - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Wine And Meal News" form is designed to collect data on wine reviews, including wine and meal pairing suggestions. This form helps users provide their expertise and preferences for wine pairing, enabling others to discover new combinations and improve their wine selection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **File Upload Form**: This field is not applicable in this form version.
2. **Food Recipe**: Please provide a brief description of the food recipe you'd like to pair with wine.
3. **Wine Pairing**: Enter a short description of the wine pairing suggestion for the meal you've chosen.
4. **Wine Rating**: Provide a rating for the wine, if applicable.
5. **Store Location**: Enter the store location where you'd like to purchase the wine or meal.
6. **Client Name**: Please enter your name for reference.
7. **Client Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
8. **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number for direct contact.
9. **Store Info**: Briefly describe the store where you found the wine or meal.
10. **Wine Info**: Enter any additional information about the wine.
11. **Pairing Notes**: Add any personal notes about the wine pairing.
12. **Wine Price**: Enter the price of the wine.
13. **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store where you found the wine.
14. **Review Date**: Enter the date of your review.
15. **Review Time**: Enter the time of your review (HH:MM AM/PM).
16. **Review Location**: Enter the location of your review.
17. **Review Type**: Choose the type of review (e.g., restaurant, event, etc.).
18. **Review Notes**: Enter any additional comments about your review.
19. **Review Status**: Select the status of your review (Approved, Pending, Rejected).
20. **Review Notes Public**: Enter any public comments about your review.
21. **Wine Type**: Select the type of wine (Red, White, Rose).
22. **Wine Vintage**: Enter the vintage of the wine.
23. **Wine Taste**: Describe the taste of the wine.
24. **Wine Smell**: Describe the smell of the wine.
25. **Wine Color**: Enter the color of the wine.
