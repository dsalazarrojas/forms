# public_sector_advisory_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from program managers for public sector advisory programs. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the program, its experience, and the applicant's availability. By filling out this form, program managers can provide a comprehensive application for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and title in the "program_manager_info" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your program in the "program_name" field. This could be a name of a specific initiative or a general program title.
3. Share any relevant experience you have with similar programs in the "program_experience" field. This can be a brief summary of your past involvement.
4. Specify your availability for the advisory program by providing a date for when you can be available in the "availability" field.
5. Attach any relevant supporting documents that demonstrate your qualifications or expertise in the "supporting_documents" field. This could include resumes, letters of recommendation, or certifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **program_manager_info** (Program Manager Info, required)
* This field is for your name and title. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents, and include your title as it appears on your business card or contact information.

### **program_name** (Program Name, optional)
* This field is for a brief description of your program. Provide a name of a specific initiative or a general program title that accurately represents your work.

### **program_experience** (Program Experience, optional)
* This field is for sharing any relevant experience you have with similar programs. Provide a brief summary of your past involvement or accomplishments.

### **availability** (Availability, optional)
* This field is for specifying your availability to participate in the advisory program. Enter a date that represents when you can be available for the program.

### **supporting_documents** (Supporting Documents, required)
* This field is for attaching any relevant documents that demonstrate your qualifications or expertise. This could include resumes, letters of recommendation, or certifications that support your application.
