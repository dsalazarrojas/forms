# Licence Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information to determine whether your driver's license is eligible for renewal. Please fill out the form with your current information and any relevant details about your license and traffic history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current license type from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your license.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter your current address in the format you would like it to be displayed.
5. Answer whether or not you have any outstanding traffic offenses.
6. Enter the number of years you have held this license.
7. Enter the date of your last license renewal in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
8. (Optional) Enter your contact number for further contact.
9. (Optional) If you have experienced new addresses or employment changes, enter the details.
10. (Optional) Answer whether or not you have been involved in any traffic accidents since your last license renewal.
11. Enter your license renewal date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
12. If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current license type?**: Please select one of the options provided. If you are unsure of your license type, it is likely one of the following: Driver's License, Vehicle License, or Other (for example: Commercial License).
* **What is your name as it appears on the license?**: Please enter your full name exactly as it appears on your license. This should match the name on your license.
* **What is your date of birth?**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy, where mm is the month and dd is the day.
* **What is your current address?**: Please enter your current address exactly as it appears on your license. If you have moved, please enter your current address.
* **Do you have any outstanding traffic offenses?**: Answer with either 'True' or 'False'.
* **How long have you held this license?**: Enter the number of years you have held this license.
* **When did you last renew your license?**: Enter the date of your last license renewal in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **What is your contact number?** (Optional): This is for further contact and is not required.
* **Do you have any new addresses or employment changes?** (Optional): If you have experienced new addresses or employment changes, please enter the details.
* **Have you been involved in any traffic accidents since your last license renewal?** (Optional): Answer with either 'True' or 'False'.
* **What is your license renewal date?**: Enter your license renewal date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Additional comments**: If you have any additional comments, please enter them here. 

Note: Please ensure you answer all required fields as accurately and completely as possible.
