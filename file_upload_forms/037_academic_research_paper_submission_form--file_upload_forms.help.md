# Academic Research Paper Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting academic research papers to our institution. It is used to collect information about the paper, its authors, and the submission process. The form is intended to provide a structured format for submitting manuscripts and to ensure consistency and quality control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email, and institutional affiliation in the "Submitting Author Name", "Submitting Author Email", and "Submitting Author Institution" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the title of your research paper in the "Paper Title" field.
3. Write a concise summary of your research paper in the "Abstract" field (approximately 150-300 words).
4. Identify the primary research field or discipline for your paper in the "Research Field" field.
5. Enter 3-5 keywords that describe the main topics of your paper in the "Keywords" field.
6. Select the type of paper you are submitting (e.g., original research, review article, case study, etc.) in the "Type of Paper" field.
7. List any co-authors and their institutional affiliations in the "Co-authors" field.
8. Estimate the word count of your manuscript in the "Word Count" field.
9. Provide any funding sources that supported your research in the "Funding Sources" field (if applicable).
10. Declare any potential conflicts of interest in the "Conflict of Interest Statement" field.
11. Confirm whether ethical approval was obtained for your research (if human subjects were involved) in the "Ethical Approval" field.
12. Upload your manuscript file (PDF, DOC, or DOCX format) in the "Manuscript File Upload" field.
13. Optionally, upload any supplementary materials (figures, tables, datasets, etc.) in the "Supplementary Materials" field.
14. Finally, confirm that you certify that this submission is original work and has not been published elsewhere by selecting "Yes, I certify" in the "Submission Certification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitting Author Name (2)**: Full name of the primary author submitting the paper.
* **Submitting Author Email (3)**: Professional email for correspondence about this submission.
* **Submitting Author Institution (4)**: University or organization affiliation of the submitting author.
* **Paper Title (5)**: Full title of the research paper being submitted.
* **Abstract (6)**: Concise summary of the research paper (approximately 150-300 words).
* **Research Field (7)**: Primary field or discipline of the research.
* **Keywords (8)**: 3-5 keywords that describe the main topics of the paper.
* **Type of Paper (9)**: What type of paper is this? (e.g., original research, review article, case study, etc.).
* **Co-authors (10)**: List all co-authors with their institutions.
* **Word Count (11)**: Approximate word count of the manuscript.
* **Funding Sources (12)**: List any funding sources that supported this research (if applicable).
* **Conflict of Interest Statement (13)**: Declare any potential conflicts of interest.
* **Ethical Approval (14)**: Was ethical approval obtained for this research (if human subjects were involved)?
* **Manuscript File Upload (15)**: Upload the main manuscript file (PDF, DOC, or DOCX format).
* **Supplementary Materials (16)**: Upload any supplementary materials (figures, tables, datasets, etc.) (if applicable).
* **Submission Certification (17)**: Confirm that you certify that this submission is original work and has not been published elsewhere.
