# Speaking Contest Video Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data from users who want to participate in the Speaking Contest by uploading a video entry with their relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Upload Form" button.
2. Select or upload your video entry.
3. Fill in the required fields with your information:
   - **Entry Title** (`entry_title`): Enter a brief title for your video entry.
   - **Speaker's First Name** (`speaker_first_name`): Enter the first name of the person speaking in the video.
   - **Speaker's Last Name** (`speaker_last_name`): Enter the last name of the person speaking in the video.
   - **School/Club Name** (`school_club_name`): Enter the name of the school or club where the video was recorded.
   - **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event where the video was recorded.
   - **Entry Link** (`entry_link`): Enter the link to the uploaded video entry.
4. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Upload Form** (`upload_form`)
- The "Upload Form" is a button to start the video upload process.
- Please select or upload your video entry to proceed.

### **Entry Title** (`entry_title`)
- Enter a brief title for your video entry.

### **Speaker's First Name** (`speaker_first_name`)
- Enter the first name of the person speaking in the video.

### **Speaker's Last Name** (`speaker_last_name`)
- Enter the last name of the person speaking in the video.

### **School/Club Name** (`school_club_name`)
- Enter the name of the school or club where the video was recorded.

### **Event Name** (`event_name`)
- Enter the name of the event where the video was recorded.

### **Entry Link** (`entry_link`)
- Enter the link to the uploaded video entry.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your entry is properly categorized and submitted.
- If you're having trouble uploading your video, try using a different browser or checking your internet connection.
