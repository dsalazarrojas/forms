# Class Reunion Photo Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Reunion Photo Upload form is used to collect photos and information from alumni and their families for the class reunion. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on different aspects of the photo and the submitter's relationship with the photo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Upload Photos" button to begin the form.
2.  Fill out the contributor information section, including:
    *   **Full Name**: Your full name as it should appear on the reunion materials.
    *   **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached.
    *   **Phone Number**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
    *   **Graduation Year or Class Year**: The year you graduated.
3.  Provide photo information in the next section, including:
    *   **Photo Description**: A brief description of the photo.
    *   **Location or Event Where Photo Was Taken**: The location or event where the photo was taken.
    *   **Approximate Year Photo Was Taken**: The year the photo was taken (if known).
    *   **Names of People in the Photo**: If you can identify the people in the photo, list their names.
4.  Choose how to share the photo in the permissions section, including:
    *   **I confirm I have the right to share this photo**: Confirm that you have the right to share the photo.
    *   **May we publicly share this photo in reunion materials**: Choose how you want the photo to be used (publicly, only among attendees, with restrictions).
5.  If you choose to share the photo publicly, you may be asked to provide additional information on sharing restrictions or special instructions.
6.  Choose your preferred method for submitting the photo in the file upload section:
    *   **Upload file to this form**: Upload the photo directly to the form.
    *   **Email photo separately**: Email the photo to the reunion organizers.
    *   **Mail physical photo**: Mail the photo to the reunion organizers.
    *   **Share Google Drive or Dropbox link**: Share a link to the photo on Google Drive or Dropbox.
    *   **Other method**: Choose your own method for submitting the photo.
7.  If you choose an alternative submission method, provide the contact information for the submitter.
8.  Finally, you may provide an optional message or note for the reunion organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name**: Your full name as it should appear on the reunion materials.
    *   **Required**: This field is required for the photo to be used in the reunion materials.
*   **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached.
    *   **Required**: This field is required for the reunion organizers to contact you.
*   **Phone Number**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
    *   **Optional**: Providing your phone number is optional, but recommended for further communication.
*   **Graduation Year or Class Year**: The year you graduated.
    *   **Required**: This field is required for the photo to be linked to the correct class reunion.
*   **Photo Description**: A brief description of the photo.
    *   **Required**: This field helps the reunion organizers understand the significance of the photo.
*   **Location or Event Where Photo Was Taken**: The location or event where the photo was taken.
    *   **Optional**: If known, please provide the location or event where the photo was taken.
*   **Approximate Year Photo Was Taken**: The year the photo was taken (if known).
    *   **Optional**: If you know the year the photo was taken, please provide it.
*   **Names of People in the Photo**: If you can identify the people in the photo, list their names.
    *   **Optional**: Only list names if you can identify them.
*   **I confirm I have the right to share this photo**: Confirm that you have the right to share the photo.
    *   **Required**: This field ensures that you have permission to share the photo.
*   **May we publicly share this photo in reunion materials**: Choose how you want the photo to be used (publicly, only among attendees, with restrictions).
    *   **Required**: This field determines how the photo will be used in the reunion materials.
*   **Photo Credit or Attribution Name**: If you took the photo, provide the name of the photographer.
    *   **Optional**: Only provide this information if you took the photo.
*   **Photo Sharing Restrictions or Special Instructions**: If you have any restrictions or special instructions for sharing the photo, provide them here.
    *   **Optional**: Only provide this information if you have any restrictions or special instructions.
*   **File Size**: The size of the photo file.
    *   **Recommended**: Please keep the file size under 10MB.
*   **Preferred Submission Method**: Choose how you want to submit the photo.
    *   **Required**: This field determines how the photo will be submitted.
*   **Contact Information for Alternative Submission**: If you choose an alternative submission method, provide the contact information for the submitter.
    *   **Optional**: Only provide this information if you choose an alternative submission method.
*   **Personal Message or Note**: An optional message or note for the reunion organizers.
    *   **Optional**: Feel free to provide any additional information about the photo or your experience.
*   **I certify that all information is accurate and permissions obtained**: Confirm that all information provided is accurate and that you have permission to share the photo.
    *   **Required**: This field ensures that all information provided is accurate and that you have permission to share the photo.

## Tips

*   Be as accurate as possible when providing information about the photo and your relationship with it.
*   Ensure that you have the right to share the photo before submitting it.
*   Choose a submission method that is convenient for you.
*   If you have any restrictions or special instructions for sharing the photo, please provide them.
*   Keep the file size under 10MB to ensure smooth upload.
*   Finally, provide an optional message or note for the reunion organizers.

This help guide will ensure that the users understand the purpose of the form, how to complete it, and what each field means. The explanations will be clear, concise, and practical, making it easy for users to submit their photos and information for the class reunion.
