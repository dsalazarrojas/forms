# Cover Letter Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cover Letter Submission Form is a tool for submitting cover letters and other supporting documents for review and consideration. This form is designed to gather the necessary information required for a successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure all fields are complete and correct before submitting the form.
3. Attach any additional supporting documents, such as a resume or cover letter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: (`first_name`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter your first name. Please enter your first name in the format "John" or "Jane".
* **Last Name**: (`last_name`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter your last name. Please enter your last name in the format "Smith" or "Johnson".
* **Job Title**: (`job_title`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the job title you are applying for. Please ensure it matches the job description you are applying for.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Resume**: (`resume`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to attach your resume. Please attach a PDF or text file with your updated resume.
* **Cover Letter**: (`cover_letter`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to attach your cover letter. Please attach a PDF or text file with your updated cover letter.
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Date of Birth Year**: (`date_of_birth_year`, number, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter only the year of your date of birth.
* **Date of Birth Month**: (`date_of_birth_month`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to select the month of your date of birth.
* **Date of Birth Day**: (`date_of_birth_day`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field is used to select the day of your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth**: (`time_of_birth`, time, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter your time of birth in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Note**: (`note`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter your phone number. Please ensure it is a valid phone number.
