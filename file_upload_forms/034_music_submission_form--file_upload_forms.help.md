# Music Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect metadata about music submissions for review and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete the form by providing the required and optional information for each field.
2. Enter a title for your music submission.
3. Provide your artist name and email address.
4. Upload files associated with your music submission.
5. Optionally, provide additional music submissions if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Submission Form**: This field is the title of your music submission.
	+ (ID: 1, Type: text, Required: no)
* **Submit your music**: This field is for you to enter a brief title for your music submission.
	+ (ID: 2, Type: text, Required: no)
* **Artist Name**: This field is for you to enter your name as the artist who created the music.
	+ (ID: 3, Type: text, Required: no)
* **Artist Email**: This field is for you to enter your email address.
	+ (ID: 4, Type: email, Required: no)
* **File Upload**: This field is for you to upload files associated with your music submission, such as audio files.
	+ (ID: 5, Type: text, Required: no)
* **Submit your music again**: This field is for you to enter another brief title for your music submission, if needed.
	+ (ID: 6, Type: text, Required: no)
* **Artist Name 2**: This field is for you to enter another name as the artist who created the music (if it's a collaboration).
	+ (ID: 7, Type: text, Required: no)
* **Artist Email 2**: This field is for you to enter another email address (if it's a collaboration).
	+ (ID: 8, Type: email, Required: no)
* **File Upload 2**: This field is for you to upload more files associated with your music submission, if needed.
	+ (ID: 9, Type: text, Required: no)

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all the required fields and upload any necessary files.
- If you have multiple artists or collaborators, use the corresponding fields to provide their information.
- You can upload multiple files in the "File Upload" field, separated by commas or new lines.
