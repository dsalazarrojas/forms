# Parental Consent Form Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Consent Form Upload form is used to collect information from parents or guardians regarding their consent for a child to participate in certain activities or programs. This form helps to ensure that parents or guardians are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the activity or program their child is participating in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the appropriate response to the "Parental Consent Form" question on page 1.
2. Enter the parent or guardian's signature in the "Signature" field, which is a text field for their name to sign.
3. If necessary, provide any additional information in the "Additional Information" field. This is a text field for any additional comments or details.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
5. Provide the parent or guardian's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter any additional parent or guardian details in the "Parent or Guardian Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental Consent Form** (`parent_consent_form_upload`, select_one): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the parent or guardian gives consent for their child to participate in the activity or program.
* **Signature** (`consent_signature_upload`, text): Enter the parent or guardian's signature to confirm their consent.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information_upload`, text): Provide any additional information or comments that the parent or guardian wants to include.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments that the parent or guardian wants to include.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Details** (`submitter_info`, text): Enter any additional details about the parent or guardian that is relevant to the activity or program.
