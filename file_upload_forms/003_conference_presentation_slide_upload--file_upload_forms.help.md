# Conference Presentation Slide Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Presentation Slide Upload form is used to collect information from speakers about their presentation slides for a conference. This includes speaker details, presentation title, session information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your speaker name in the Speaker full name field.
2. Provide your email address in the Speaker email field.
3. Enter the Presentation title as it will be displayed in the program.
4. If known, provide the Session name or number.
5. Choose the preferred file format for your presentation slides (e.g., PDF, PowerPoint, Keynote, or Other).
6. Provide a secure upload link or instructions for your presentation slides.
7. Check if you consent to recording your presentation. If yes, it will be published post-event.
8. Finally, specify your relationship to the speaker (e.g., presenter, co-presenter, assistant, or organizer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker full name** (`speaker_name`, **text**, **required**): Enter your full name as it will be displayed in the program.
* **Speaker email** (`speaker_email`, **email**, **required**): Enter your email address for confirmation and corrections.
* **Presentation title** (`presentation_title`, **text, **required**): Enter the exact title of your presentation as it will be displayed in the program.
* **Session name or number** (`presentation_session`, **text, **optional**): If known, provide the scheduled session name or number.
* **Preferred file format** (`slide_file_format`, **select_one, **required**): Choose the preferred file format for your presentation slides (e.g., PDF, PowerPoint, Keynote, or Other).
* **File upload link or instruction** (`file_upload_link`, **text, **required**): Provide a secure upload link or instructions for your presentation slides.
* **Special presentation instructions** (`special_instructions`, **text, **optional**): Enter any special instructions or requirements for your presentation (e.g., AV needs, video playback, or animations).
* **Consent to recording** (`consent_recording`, **select_one, **required**): Check if you consent to recording your presentation, which will be published post-event.
* **Submitter relationship** (`submitter_relationship`, **select_one, **optional**): Specify your relationship to the speaker (e.g., presenter, co-presenter, assistant, or organizer).

## Tips
* Please ensure that your speaker email is correct to receive confirmation and corrections.
* Use the preferred file format to ensure that your slides are displayed as intended.
* If you have any special instructions, please provide them to ensure a smooth presentation experience.
