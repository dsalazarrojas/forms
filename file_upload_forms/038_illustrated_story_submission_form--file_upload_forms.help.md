# Illustrated Story Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Illustrated Story Submission Form is a platform for artists and creators to submit their work for review and publication. This form helps gather information about the submission, including details about the artwork, contributor, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the submission details (id: 1) with a brief description of your artwork.
2. Provide the artwork title (id: 19) and description (id: 25) to give more context about your artwork.
3. Enter your contact information, including name (id: 4), email (id: 5), and phone number (id: 6).
4. If applicable, provide a link to the artwork URL (id: 7) or upload a file for the artwork (this is not a field in the provided YAML but will be assumed to be a part of the form in general).
5. Select the submission type (id: 20) as either "Contests", "Publication", or "School Showcases".
6. Choose whether the submission is "True" or "False" for submission agreement (id: 10) and submission review (id: 14).
7. Select the submission status (id: 15) as "Draft", "Published", or "Selected for publication".
8. Optionally, provide additional comments (id: 16) and notes (id: 23) about the submission.
9. Enter the submission review details (id: 18) and submission status details (id: 17) if needed.
10. If you have any additional reviewer details, you can enter them in the reviewer details field (id: 18).
11. Finally, confirm your submission agreement (id: 11) and submission agreement date and time (id: 12, id: 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Details (id: 1)**: Enter a brief description of your artwork.
* **Artwork Title (id: 19)**: Enter the title of your artwork.
* **Artwork Description (id: 25)**: Enter the description of your artwork.
* **Name (id: 4)**: Enter your name as the contributor of the artwork.
* **Email (id: 5)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Phone (id: 6)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Artwork URL (id: 7)**: If your artwork is already published online, provide the link to the artwork.
* **Submission Permission (id: 8)**: This field is a note field for you to add any additional permission information about your submission.
* **Submission Permission Details (id: 9)**: This field is for you to add more details about your submission permission.
* **Submission Agreement (id: 10)**: Select "True" to agree to our submission guidelines or "False" to disagree.
* **Submission Agreement Details (id: 11)**: If you selected "True", you can provide more details about your agreement.
* **Submission Agreement Date (id: 12)**: Enter the date of your submission agreement.
* **Submission Agreement Time (id: 13)**: Enter the time of your submission agreement.
* **Submission Review (id: 14)**: Select "True" to indicate that you have reviewed your submission or "False" to indicate that you have not.
* **Submission Status (id: 15)**: Select "Draft" for an unpublished submission, "Published" for a published submission, or "Selected for publication" for a submission that has been selected for publication.
* **Submission Comments (id: 16)**: Enter any additional comments you have about your submission.
* **Submission Status Details (id: 17)**: Enter any additional details about your submission status.
* **Reviewer Details (id: 18)**: Enter any additional reviewer details.
* **Submission Type (id: 20)**: Select the type of submission as "Contests", "Publication", or "School Showcases".
* **Submission Date (id: 21)**: Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time (id: 22)**: Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submission Notes (id: 23)**: Enter any additional notes about your submission.
* **Submission Notes Details (id: 24)**: Enter any additional details about your submission notes.
