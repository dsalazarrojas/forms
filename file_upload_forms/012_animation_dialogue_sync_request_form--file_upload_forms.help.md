# Animation Dialogue Sync Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animation Dialogue Sync Request Form is used to submit new projects that require animation dialogue sync services. This form helps us understand your project's specific needs, including project details, dialogue specifications, and any special requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide project details in the "Project Information" section.
2. Specify the type and style of animation project in the "Project Type and Specifications" section.
3. Choose the type of dialogue content and audio format required.
4. Upload relevant files, such as the script or dialogue list, storyboard, and reference video.
5. Provide a project timeline, including start and completion dates.
6. Specify budget and payment terms.
7. Add any additional project information or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name (Required)** (`project_name`, `text`): This is the name of your animation project. Enter the name of your project to help us identify and understand your project's scope.
* **Client or Studio Name (Required)** (`client_name`, `text`): This is the name of your organization or studio. Enter the name of your company to help us identify and contact the primary contact person.
* **Project Manager Email (Required)** (`project_manager_email`, `email`): This is the email address of your project manager. Enter the email address of the person responsible for this project.
* **Project Manager Phone (Optional)** (`project_manager_phone`, `text`): If you have a contact phone number for your project manager, enter it here.
* **Type of Animation Project (Required)** (`project_type`, `select_one`): Choose the type of animation project from the options: Feature Film, TV Series, Short Film, Commercial or Advertisement, Training Video, Web Series, Music Video, or Other.
* **Animation Style (Required)** (`animation_style`, `select_one`): Choose the animation style: 2D Traditional, 3D CGI, Stop-Motion, Motion Capture, Hybrid, or Other.
* **Total Video Length (Required)** (`video_length`, `select_one`): Choose the estimated length of the final project: 30 seconds or less, 1-5 minutes, 5-15 minutes, 15-30 minutes, or 30+ minutes.
* **Target Language for Dialogue (Required)** (`target_language`, `select_one`): Choose the primary language for the dialogue: English, Spanish, French, Mandarin, Japanese, Multiple Languages, or Other.
* **Type of Dialogue Content (Required)** (`dialogue_type`, `select_multiple`): Choose the type of dialogue content from the options: Voice-over Narration, Character Dialogue, Ambient Sound Effects, Music Sync, or ADR (Additional Dialogue Recording).
* **Preferred Audio Format (Optional)** (`audio_format`, `select_one`): Choose the preferred audio format for the dialogue files: WAV, MP3, AAC, or AIFF. You can also choose No preference if you don't have a preference.
* **Specific Sync Requirements (Optional)** (`sync_requirements`, `text`): If you have any special requirements or notes, enter them here.
* **Script or Dialogue Document (Optional)** (`script_attachment_note`, `note`): If you have a script or dialogue list, upload it here.
* **Storyboard or Animatic (Optional)** (`storyboard_note`, `note`): If you have a storyboard or animatic, upload it here.
* **Reference Video or Rough Cut (Optional)** (`reference_video_note`, `note`): If you have a reference video or rough cut, upload it here.
* **Project Start Date (Optional)** (`project_start_date`, `date`): Enter the start date for the project dialogue sync work.
* **Required Completion Date (Required)** (`sync_completion_date`, `date`): Enter the date by which the dialogue sync must be completed.
* **Project Budget Range (Optional)** (`project_budget`, `select_one`): Choose the estimated budget range for the project: Under 1000, 1000-5000, 5000-10000, 10000-25000, or Over 25000.
* **Preferred Payment Terms (Optional)** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`): Choose the preferred payment terms: Full payment upfront, 50% deposit, 50% on completion, or Flexible - to be discussed.
* **Additional Project Information (Optional)** (`additional_info`, `text`): If you have any other details or special requests, enter them here.

Note: Fields with asterisk (*) are required. Please fill out the required fields to ensure that we can process your project request efficiently.
