# Magazine Content Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Magazine Content Inquiry Form is a tool for contributors and editors to inquire about magazine content, including contributor information, story ideas, and review materials. This form helps facilitate communication between contributors and editors, ensuring timely and accurate information for editorial decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your contributor information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide any relevant story ideas you'd like to submit or are currently working on.
3. Share any review materials related to your story ideas, such as drafts or research data.
4. Indicate whether the editor has made an editorial decision for your submission (Editorial Decision).
5. If applicable, report the status of your pitch (Pitch Status).
6. Provide information on data collection methods and deadlines.
7. Specify the review and submission deadlines for your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contributor Information (contributor_info)**: Enter your name and contact details to enable editors to contact you about your submissions.
* **Story Ideas (story_ideas)**: Share your story ideas, including any relevant details or context.
* **Review Materials (review_materials)**: Upload any review materials, such as drafts or research data, to support your story ideas.
* **Editorial Decision (editorial_decision)**: Check 'Yes' if an editor has made an editorial decision on your submission. 
* **Pitch Status (pitch_status)**: Report the status of your pitch, selecting one of the available options (Pending, In Progress, Published, Rejected).
* **Data Collection (data_collection)**: Enter the number of data points collected for your submission.
* **Review Deadline (review_deadline)**: Specify the review deadline for your submission.
* **Submission Deadline (submission_deadline)**: Enter the submission deadline for your work.
