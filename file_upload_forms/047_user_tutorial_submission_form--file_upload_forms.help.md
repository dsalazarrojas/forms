# User Tutorial Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Tutorial Submission Form is designed to collect information about a user's tutorial submission. This form will guide you through the steps to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Submit Form` field with a clear and concise title for your tutorial submission.
2. Select the appropriate `Category` from the provided options.
3. Enter a short description of your tutorial in the `Description` field.
4. Choose any relevant tags for your tutorial submission from the provided options.
5. Upload a file related to your tutorial submission, if required.
6. Enter your name, email, website, company, title, phone, country, state, city, and zip code in the respective fields.
7. Provide a signature and date for your submission.
8. Optionally, add any comments about your submission.
9. Finally, confirm your agreement and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title for your tutorial submission.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best fits your tutorial submission.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a short description of your tutorial submission.
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple, required): Select any relevant tags for your tutorial submission.
* **File** (`file`, text, required): Upload a file related to your tutorial submission.
* **Tags Value** (`tags_value`, text, required): Enter the value of the selected tags.
* **Category Value** (`category_value`, text, required): Enter the value of the selected category.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, required): Upload a file related to your tutorial submission.
* **File Value** (`file_value`, text, required): Enter the value of the uploaded file.
* **User** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the user submitting the tutorial.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Website** (`user_website`, text, required): Enter your website URL.
* **Company** (`user_company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Title** (`user_title`, text, required): Enter your title.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Country** (`user_country`, text, required): Enter your country of residence.
* **State** (`user_state`, text, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **City** (`user_city`, text, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **Zip** (`user_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Comments** (`user_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments about your submission.
* **Agreement** (`user_agreement`, note, required): Confirm that you agree with the submission terms.
* **Signature** (`user_signature`, date, required): Enter your signature.
* **Date** (`user_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Time** (`user_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your submission.

Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to ensure your tutorial submission is processed correctly.
