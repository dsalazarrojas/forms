# Immigration Document Upload Form - Help Guide

This form is used to upload immigration documents for processing and verification. Please ensure you're using the correct documents as specified on this form.

**Purpose**
--------

The Immigration Document Upload Form is a tool to upload various types of immigration documents for processing and verification purposes. It helps in maintaining an organized and secure repository of documents.

**How To Complete This Form**
-------------------------

### Step 1: Select the Document Type

Choose the type of document you wish to upload from the list of available options:
*   Passport
*   Visa
*   ID
*   Other

### Step 2: Confirm the Upload

Confirm that you are uploading the correct document:
*   Select 'Yes' to confirm the upload
*   Select 'No' to cancel the upload

### Step 3: Provide Document Information

Enter the details of the document, including its type, status, and reason for upload.

### Step 4: Select Upload Status

Select the status of the document:
*   Pending
*   Uploaded

### Step 5: Enter Upload Information

Enter your name and the date and time you uploaded the document.

### Step 6: Add Additional Notes (Optional)

Add any additional comments or notes for the uploaded document.

### Step 7: Confirm Upload Complete

Confirm that the upload is complete:
*   Select 'Yes' to confirm the upload is complete

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Document Upload**: (page 1) Select the type of document you wish to upload from the list of available options.
*   **Applicant Information**: (page 2) Choose whether the upload is for an individual or an organization.
*   **Document Info**: (page 3) Enter the details of the document, including its type, status, and reason for upload.
*   **Upload Document**: (page 4) Upload the file.
*   **Confirm Upload**: (page 5) Confirm that you are uploading the correct document.
*   **Confirm Upload Confirmation**: (page 6) Confirm that you have confirmed the upload.
*   **Type**: (page 7) Select the document type.
*   **Upload Status**: (page 8) Select the status of the document.
*   **Reason for Upload**: (page 9) Enter the reason for upload.
*   **Upload By**: (page 10) Enter your name.
*   **Upload Date**: (page 11) Enter the date and time you uploaded the document.
*   **Upload Time**: (page 12) Enter the time you uploaded the document.
*   **Upload User**: (page 13) Enter your name (same as upload_by).
*   **Upload Note**: (page 14) Add additional comments or notes for the uploaded document.
*   **Document Status**: (page 15) Select the document status.
*   **Document Type ID**: (page 16) Select the ID for the document type.
*   **Uploaded By**: (page 17) Enter your name (same as upload_by).
*   **Uploaded Date**: (page 18) Enter the date you uploaded the document.
*   **Uploaded Time**: (page 19) Enter the time you uploaded the document.
*   **Uploaded User**: (page 20) Enter your name (same as upload_by).
*   **Uploaded Note**: (page 21) Add additional comments or notes for the uploaded document.
*   **Document Name**: (page 22) Enter the name of the document.
*   **Document Path**: (page 23) Select the upload location for the document.

This form is designed to guide users through the process of uploading immigration documents for processing and verification. It ensures that users provide accurate and relevant information for each field, maintaining an organized and secure repository of documents.
