# Group Statistics Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect statistics and information from group members to provide insights and updates on team performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Reporting Period** from the drop-down menu. This is an optional field, but it helps to filter the submissions based on the period you're reporting on.
2. Enter the **Group Performance Update** in the text field below. This is an optional field, but it's a good place to provide any additional information or comments about the group's performance.
3. If you have any **Supporting Documentation**, you can upload it here. This can be a helpful attachment to provide more context or evidence to support your submission.
4. Enter the **Team Manager Name** and **Group Manager Name** as they are typically known in your organization. These fields are optional, but they help to provide context and accountability.
5. Select the **Team Members** who are part of your team from the list of options. Select all the members who are part of your team, as this is a required field.
6. Enter the **Team Leader Name** and **Team Name** as they are typically known in your organization. These fields are optional, but they help to provide additional context.
7. Finally, select a **Date** for when you're submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Performance Update** (id: 1): A text field where you can provide any updates or comments about your team's performance.
* **Reporting Period** (id: 2): Select the reporting period from the drop-down menu, such as Quarter 1, Quarter 2, etc.
* **Supporting Documentation** (id: 3): A text field for uploading any additional documentation or evidence to support your submission.
* **Team Manager Name** (id: 4): Enter the name of your team manager.
* **Group Manager Name** (id: 5): Enter the name of your team leader.
* **Date Submitted** (id: 6): Select the date when you're submitting this form.
* **File Attachment** (id: 7): A text field for uploading any attachments or files to support your submission.
* **Team Name** (id: 8): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Leader Name** (id: 9): Enter the name of your team leader.
* **Team Members** (id: 10): Select all the members who are part of your team. This is a required field.
* **Team Performance Update** (id: 11): A text field for providing any updates on your team's performance.

## Tips

* Make sure to select all the team members who are part of your team in the **Team Members** field.
* Provide context with the **Group Performance Update** and **Team Performance Update** fields.
* Use the **Supporting Documentation** field to provide any additional evidence or attachments to support your submission.
* Select the correct **Reporting Period** from the drop-down menu to filter the submissions based on the period you're reporting on.
