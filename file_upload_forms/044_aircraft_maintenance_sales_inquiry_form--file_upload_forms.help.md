# Aircraft Maintenance Sales Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Aircraft Maintenance Sales Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from potential customers who are seeking maintenance services for their aircraft. This form will help us understand their needs and provide a comprehensive quote for the required services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form. These fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the form itself.
2. Select the correct options for each dropdown field, and select multiple options for fields that allow you to select multiple choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your organization or company name here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your position or title here.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number here.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your business address here.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city here.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province here.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code here.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter the country here.
* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your inquiry here.
* **Service Type Needed** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the service type you need:
  - Routine maintenance
  - Major overhaul
  - Engine maintenance
  - Avionics upgrade
  - Airframe repair
  - Component repair
  - Inspection services
  - Emergency AOG
  - Consulting
  - Other
* **Aircraft Make** (`aircraft_make`, `text`, required): Enter your aircraft's manufacturer here.
* **Aircraft Model** (`aircraft_model`, `text`, required): Enter your aircraft's model here.
* **Aircraft Year** (`aircraft_year`, `number`, optional): Enter the year of manufacture here (if known).
* **Aircraft Registration** (`aircraft_registration`, `text`, required): Enter the aircraft's registration or tail number here.
* **Aircraft Serial Number** (`aircraft_serial`, `text`, optional): Enter the aircraft's manufacturer serial number here (if known).
* **Total Airframe Hours** (`total_airframe_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the total airframe hours here (if known).
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, `text`, optional): Enter your engine's manufacturer and model here (if known).
* **Engine Hours** (`engine_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the engine hours here (if known).
* **Maintenance Needed** (`maintenance_description`, `text`, required): Describe the maintenance work required.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`, `select_one`, required): Select the urgency level for this service:
  - Emergency, AOG
  - Urgent, within 1 week
  - Soon, within 1 month
  - Routine, flexible timing
  - Planning for future
* **Preferred Timeline** (`preferred_timeline`, `text`, required): Enter the preferred time for the service here.
* **Service Location Preference** (`location_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select where you would like the service to be done:
  - At your facility
  - At our location
  - On-site at airport
  - Mobile service
  - Flexible
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the estimated budget range for this service:
  - Under $10,000
  - $10,000-$50,000
  - $50,000-$100,000
  - $100,000-$500,000
  - Over $500,000
  - Need estimate first
* **Current Maintenance Provider** (`current_maintenance_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter your current maintenance provider here (if known).
* **Reason for Inquiry** (`reason_for_inquiry`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for your inquiry:
  - New aircraft acquisition
  - Dissatisfied with current provider
  - Relocating
  - Specific expertise needed
  - Cost considerations
  - Capacity issues
  - Other
* **Additional Services Interested In** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all additional services you're interested in:
  - Annual inspections
  - Engine overhaul
  - Avionics installation
  - Interior refurbishment
  - Exterior painting
  - Component repair
  - 24/7 support
  - Maintenance tracking
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Select where you heard about us:
  - Online search
  - Industry referral
  - Previous customer
  - Trade show
  - Advertisement
  - Other
* **Documents Attached** (`documents_attached`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have documents to attach:
  - True
  - False
* **Document Types** (`document_types`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select what documents you are attaching:
  - Maintenance records
  - Logbooks
  - Work orders
  - Inspection reports
  - Photos
  - Other
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_contact_time`, `select_one`, required): Select the best time to contact you:
  - Morning
  - Afternoon
  - Evening
  - Anytime
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information here.
* **Contact Signature** (`contact_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your digital signature here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and honest information in the form.
* If you have any attachments, please select the type of documents you are attaching.
* If you're unsure about any field or option, please ask our team for clarification.
