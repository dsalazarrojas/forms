# Undergraduate Application CV Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information and supporting documents for undergraduate applications. It is essential that you only submit one application per person. Before proceeding, please review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Upload your resume (CV) in the "CV" field.
6. Check the "Academic Program" field to select your chosen program.
7. Enter your current or previous academic institution in the "Academic Institution" field.
8. Upload your transcript from your previous institution in the "Transcript" field.
9. Upload your letter of reference in the "Letter of Reference" field.
10. Click "Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **CV** (text, required/optional): Upload your resume (CV) in PDF format.
* **Academic Program** (select, required/optional): Select your chosen academic program.
*   *Note: This field is a dropdown menu with available options.*
*   *If you are unsure about the options, please contact your admissions team.*
*   *Please select only one option.*
* **Academic Institution** (text, required/optional): Enter your current or previous academic institution.
*   *This field is where you provide information about your current or previous educational background.*
*   *Please ensure you provide accurate information.*
* **Transcript** (text, required/optional): Upload your transcript from your previous institution.
*   *This is a document that outlines your academic progress and achievements.*
*   *Please upload a valid PDF document.*
*   *Please ensure the document is from your current or previous institution.*
* **Letter of Reference** (text, required/optional): Upload your letter of reference from a professional referee.
*   *This is a document that vouches for your character and abilities.*
*   *Please ensure you upload a valid PDF document.*

Tips:
* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing your application.
* Please ensure all documents are in PDF format.
* Please do not submit multiple applications per person.
* Please do not upload fake or forged documents.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your admissions team for assistance.
