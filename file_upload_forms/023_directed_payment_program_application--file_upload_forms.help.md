# directed_payment_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information and supporting documents for a directed payment program application. Please ensure you have all required materials before starting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Page** (`directed_payment_program_application_1`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title for your directed payment program application. This is the primary identifying information for your application.

2. **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, required): Select all documents that apply to your application from the provided options:
    - Option 1: Document 1
    - Option 2: Document 2
    - Option 3: Document 3

3. **Details** (`details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your application, including any relevant background information or context.

4. **Approvals** (`approvals`, select_one, required): Indicate the approval level for your application:
    - Option 1: Level 1
    - Option 2: Level 2

5. **More Details** (`details_2`, text, optional): Provide additional details if necessary. This field is not required but may be necessary for further context.

6. **Supporting Document Uploads** (`details_3`, text, optional): If you have documents to upload, enter their details here. This field is not required but may be necessary for supporting your application.

7. **Additional Details** (`details_4`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information not covered in the previous fields.

8. **Review and Submit** (`details_5`, text, required): Carefully review your application form, ensuring all fields are complete and accurate. Enter a confirmation that your information is correct before submitting.

* Ensure you have all necessary supporting documents and information before starting.
* Review each field carefully to avoid duplication of effort or missing critical information.
