# Customer ID Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer ID Upload Form is designed to collect and upload customer IDs for further processing or verification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form fields as instructed below.
2. Upload a file by clicking the upload button.
3. Review and adjust your entries as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's ID, which can be a unique identifier used for customer tracking or other purposes.
* **Document Date** (`document_date`, date, required: false): Provide the date the customer's document or ID was created or issued.
* **Support Team** (`support_team`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are uploading the customer ID on behalf of the support team.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the status of the customer's membership, whether it's Active or Inactive.
* **Account Service** (`account_service`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the customer has an active account service.
* **Account Status** (`account_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the customer's account, whether it's Active or Inactive.
* **Account Service Type** (`account_service_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the customer has a specific type of account service.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, required: false): Upload a file relevant to the customer ID, such as a document or image.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the customer is of a specific type.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the customer has received service from your organization.
