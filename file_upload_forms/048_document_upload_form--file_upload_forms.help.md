# Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to upload documents and select the type of document being uploaded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Document Upload Form" link or button.
2. Fill in the title of the document (required).
3. Select the type of document you are uploading (optional).
4. Upload your document by filling in the "Document File" field or by providing a URL to the document if you prefer (optional).
5. Click the "Upload" button to submit your document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Document Upload Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your document.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of document you are uploading.
* **Document File** (`document_file`, text, optional): Either upload a document from your computer or provide a URL to the document.
* **Upload** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your document for upload.
* **Document URL** (`file_url`, text, optional): This field is optional. If you're providing a URL to a document instead of uploading it, fill in this field. Note: If you select "Yes" for the "Document Type" field, this field becomes unnecessary.
