# Family Memory Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Memory Sharing Form is designed to gather family history, relationships, and memories from family members. It's a collection of questions and fields to help you share and record your family's story, media, and experiences. This form is required for the following family members: family members (id 3), family events (id 6), family relationships (id 6), family travel (id 9). It's not required for file upload (id 5), family media (id 2), family interests (id 8), family occupation (id 10), and file upload (id 4).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Family Story" field (id 1) and enter your family's story. You can type in your memories and experiences in this text box.
2. Select all the family members who are currently a part of your family. You will find this option in the "Family Members" field (id 3).
3. Choose whether or not your family has experienced any significant events. Select all the events that apply in the "Family Events" field (id 4) (if this field is not required, you don't need to fill it out).
4. For the "Family Media" field (id 2), select all the types of media that are relevant to your family. You will find this option in the "Family Media" field.
5. Check if you have any relationships within your family. You will find this option in the "Family Relationships" field (id 6) (if this field is not required, you don't need to fill it out).
6. Select all the hobbies that are enjoyed by your family. You will find this option in the "Family Hobbies" field (id 7) (if this field is not required, you don't need to fill it out).
7. Check if you have any interests that are shared by your family. You will find this option in the "Family Interests" field (id 8) (if this field is not required, you don't need to fill it out).
8. Select if you have any family travel experiences. You will find this option in the "Family Travel" field (id 9) (if this field is not required, you don't need to fill it out).
9. Enter your family's occupation(s). You will find this option in the "Family Occupation" field (id 10) (if this field is not required, you don't need to fill it out).
10. If you have any additional files or documents related to your family, you can upload them in the "File Upload" field (id 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Story (id 1)**: The field where you can share your family's story, memories, and experiences. Enter a brief description of your family's past.
* **Family Members (id 3)**: Select all the family members that are currently a part of your family. This field is required.
* **Family Media (id 2)**: Select all the types of media that are relevant to your family. This field is required.
* **Family Events (id 4)**: Select all the events that are relevant to your family. This field is not required.
* **File Upload (id 5)**: If you have any additional files or documents related to your family, you can upload them here (if this field is not required, you don't need to fill it out).
* **Family Relationships (id 6)**: Check if you have any relationships within your family. This field is not required.
* **Family Hobbies (id 7)**: Select all the hobbies that are enjoyed by your family. This field is not required.
* **Family Interests (id 8)**: Check if you have any interests that are shared by your family. This field is not required.
* **Family Travel (id 9)**: Select if you have any family travel experiences. This field is not required.
* **Family Occupation (id 10)**: Enter your family's occupation(s). This field is not required.
