# legal_transcript_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user information and a scanned copy of the legal transcript to process requests for access to legal transcriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Information (Page 1).
2. Provide your Contact Details (Page 2).
3. Upload a scanned copy of the legal transcript (Page 3).
4. If you have any agreement related to the transcript, please provide it as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please fill in your name, email, and any other relevant information you'd like us to know.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your phone number and any other contact information we may need to reach out to you.
* **Legal Transcript** (`legal_transcript`, text, optional): Scan a copy of the legal transcript and upload it as a document. Please make sure it is clear and legible.
* **agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): If there is any agreement or contract related to the legal transcript, please attach a copy of it as a document. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all relevant fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are having trouble uploading a file, please check that it is in a supported format and within the allowed size limit.
* If you need further assistance, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
