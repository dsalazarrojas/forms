# E Bike Rebate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Bike Rebate Application Form is designed to allow end-users to apply for an E Bike rebate. To be eligible, the user must have purchased an E Bike and be willing to provide minimal information about their purchase and themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the required fields (Applicant Name, Email, Phone, E Bike Details, and Date of Purchase). These are all mandatory fields.
2.  Fill out the remaining fields with accurate and as much detail as possible.
3.  Attach required supporting documents and E Bike pictures.
4.  Submit the form and wait for further instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E Bike Rebate Request (1)**: This is a note field to describe your E Bike rebate request. It is optional and should be used to provide any additional information about your request.
* **Applicant Name (2)**: This field is for your name, please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further instructions.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further instructions.
* **E Bike Details (5)**: Enter the make and model of your E Bike, along with any other relevant information about your purchase.
* **Date of Purchase (6)**: Enter the date you purchased your E Bike.
* **Type of E Bike (7)**: Select the type of E Bike you purchased from the provided options.
* **Price Range (8)**: Enter the price range of your E Bike.
* **E Bike Pictures (9)**: Attach a picture of your E Bike for further review.
* **Other Documents (10)**: Attach any other documentation related to your E Bike purchase, such as receipt or warranty.
* **Address (11)**: Enter your mailing address where you would like to receive your rebate.
* **Zip Code (12)**: Enter your zip code.
* **City (13)**: Enter your city name.
* **State (14)**: Enter your state name.
* **E Bike Rebate Certification (15)**: Attach the E Bike rebate certification, if you have it.
* **Supporting Documents (16)**: Attach any other documentation that supports your rebate request.
* **E Bike Rebate Request Comments (17)**: Enter any additional comments you would like to provide about your rebate request.
* **E Bike Model Pictures (18)**: Attach pictures of your E Bike model for further review.
