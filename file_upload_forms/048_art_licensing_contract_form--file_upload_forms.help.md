# Art Licensing Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create and document an art licensing contract between a Licensor and a Licensee. It outlines the terms and conditions of the contract, including usage rights, territorial limits, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Licensor Name (Artist or Owner)" field and enter the name of the Licensor.
2. Select the "Licensee Name (Company or Organization)" field and enter the name of the Licensee.
3. Select the "Primary Contact at Licensee" field and enter the name of the primary contact person at the Licensee.
4. Select the "Title of Artwork(s)" field and enter the title(s) of the artwork(s) being licensed.
5. Select the "Asset Format to be Provided" field and choose the format in which the artwork should be provided (e.g., TIFF - RAW, JPEG - PNG, Physical Original, Vector Graphics, etc.).
6. Select the "Scope of Permitted Use" field and choose the specific use(s) of the artwork that are permitted (e.g., Commercial Merchandising, Editorial, Marketing and Advertising, Web, etc.).
7. Select the "Exclusivity" field and choose whether the license is exclusive or non-exclusive.
8. Select the "Geographic Territory" field and choose the geographic region in which the license applies (e.g., Worldwide, North America, Europe, etc.).
9. Select the "Duration of License Term" field and choose the length of time for which the license applies (e.g., 1 Year, 3 Years, 5 Years, Perpetual).
10. If applicable, enter the "One-time Flat Licensing Fee" amount.
11. Enter the "Royalty Percentage" (if applicable) as a numeric value (e.g., 5.0%).
12. Enter the "Payment Schedule Details" (e.g., 50% upfront, 50% on delivery).
13. Select the "How should the artist be credited?" field and enter the desired attribution requirement for the artwork (e.g., "Artist Name, 'Year of Creation', etc.).
14. Select "I Agree" to acknowledge agreement with the terms and conditions outlined in the contract.
15. Enter the "Digital Signature of Licensor" in the designated field.
16. Enter the "Digital Signature of Licensee" in the designated field.
17. Select the "Date of Agreement" field and choose the date on which the contract was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Licensing Parties --**
	+ The "Licensing Parties" section outlines the names of both the Licensor and the Licensee.
* **Licensor Name (Artist or Owner)**
	+ Enter the name of the artist or owner of the artwork.
* **Licensee Name (Company or Organization)**
	+ Enter the name of the company or organization using the artwork.
* **Primary Contact at Licensee**
	+ Enter the name of the primary contact person at the Licensee organization.
* **Title of Artwork(s)**
	+ Enter the title(s) of the artwork(s) being licensed.
* **Asset Format to be Provided**
	+ Choose the format in which the artwork should be provided (e.g., TIFF, JPEG, Physical Original, Vector Graphics).
* **Scope of Permitted Use**
	+ Choose the specific use(s) of the artwork that are permitted (e.g., Commercial Merchandising, Editorial, Marketing and Advertising, Web, etc.).
* **Exclusivity**
	+ Choose whether the license is exclusive or non-exclusive.
* **Geographic Territory**
	+ Choose the geographic region in which the license applies (e.g., Worldwide, North America, Europe, etc.).
* **Duration of License Term**
	+ Choose the length of time for which the license applies (e.g., 1 Year, 3 Years, 5 Years, Perpetual).
* **One-time Flat Licensing Fee**
	+ Enter the amount of the one-time flat licensing fee (if applicable).
* **Royalty Percentage (If Applicable)**
	+ Enter the royalty percentage as a numeric value (e.g., 5.0%).
* **Payment Schedule Details**
	+ Enter the payment schedule details (e.g., 50% upfront, 50% on delivery).
* **Attribution Requirement**
	+ Enter the desired attribution requirement for the artwork.
* **I Agree**
	+ Select "I Agree" to acknowledge agreement with the terms and conditions outlined in the contract.
* **Digital Signature of Licensor**
	+ Enter the digital signature of the Licensor.
* **Digital Signature of Licensee**
	+ Enter the digital signature of the Licensee.
* **Date of Agreement**
	+ Select the date on which the contract was signed.
