# Sea Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sea Access Request Form is used by individuals to request access to restricted areas for sea-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the "Requestor Name" and "Requestor Email" fields.
2. Select the type of vessel you are requesting access with (e.g., Boat, Yacht, Ship).
3. If required, provide your vessel's length.
4. Select the status of your request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied).
5. If necessary, add any additional comments about your request.
6. Upload any relevant files or documents.
7. Specify the individual assigned to process your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the person requesting access.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with your request.
* **Vessel Type** (`vessel_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of vessel you are requesting access with (e.g., Boat, Yacht, Ship).
* **Vessel Length** (`vessel_length`, `number`, optional): If required, enter the length of your vessel.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you are requesting access.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you are requesting access.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Request Comment** (`request_comment`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about your request.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, `text`, optional): Upload any relevant files or documents to support your request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): Specify the individual or team member assigned to process your request.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your supervisor or the form administrator.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
