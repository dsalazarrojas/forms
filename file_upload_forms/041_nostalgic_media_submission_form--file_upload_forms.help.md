# Nostalgic Media Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nostalgic Media Submission Form is a tool used to collect user-generated content, such as artwork, historical events, and creative writing samples. This form is designed to be comprehensive and allow users to showcase their creations in a specific format, providing essential metadata to describe the submission. The form is used to collect data for archival and display purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields.
2. Provide your **Email Address** (if you want to be contacted for any updates or further information).
3. Choose the type of submission you are uploading (Image, Video, or other types of media).
4. Enter a descriptive **Title** for your submission.
5. Add a **Description** of your submission.
6. Select the relevant **Categories** for your submission.
7. Choose the desired **Permissions** for your submission (Public or Private).
8. Add **Tags** that describe your submission.
9. Choose the current **Status** of your submission (In-Progress, Approved, or Archived).
10. Select the correct **Date** and **Time** of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
  * **Type:** text, **Required:** true
  * This field is used to collect the first name of the user submitting the content.
  * **Hint:** Enter your first name
  * **Example:** John
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
  * **Type:** text, **Required:** true
  * This field is used to collect the last name of the user submitting the content.
  * **Hint:** Enter your last name
  * **Example:** Doe
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
  * **Type:** email, **Required:** false
  * This field is used to collect the email address of the user, in case they need to be contacted.
  * **Hint:** Enter your email address
  * **Example:** johndoe@example.com
- **Submission Title**: Enter a descriptive title for your submission.
  * **Type:** text, **Required:** true
  * This field is used to describe the submission in a brief and concise manner.
  * **Hint:** Enter a descriptive title for your submission.
  * **Example:** My Artistic Masterpiece
- **Submission URL**: Enter the URL of your submission.
  * **Type:** text, **Required:** false
  * This field is used to collect the URL of the submission, if applicable.
  * **Hint:** Enter the URL of your submission
  * **Example:** https://example.com/my-art
- **Submission File**: Select the type of media you are submitting (Image, Video, etc.).
  * **Type:** select_one, **Required:** true
  * This field determines the type of file uploaded.
  * **Options:** Image, Video, Other
  * **Example:** Image
- **Submission Description**: Enter a detailed description of your submission.
  * **Type:** text, **Required:** true
  * This field is used to provide context and information about the submission.
  * **Hint:** Enter a detailed description of your submission.
  * **Example:** A detailed description of my artwork
- **Submission Categories**: Select the categories that best describe your submission.
  * **Type:** select_multiple, **Required:** true
  * This field determines the category of the submission.
  * **Options:** Art, History, Music, Literature
  * **Example:** Art, History
- **Submission Permissions**: Choose the desired permissions for your submission (Public or Private).
  * **Type:** select_one, **Required:** true
  * This field determines the accessibility of your submission.
  * **Options:** Public, Private
  * **Example:** Public
- **Submission Tags**: Select additional tags that describe your submission.
  * **Type:** select_multiple, **Required:** true
  * This field determines additional tags for your submission.
  * **Options:** Art, History, Music, Literature
  * **Example:** Art, History
- **Submission Status**: Choose the current status of your submission.
  * **Type:** select_one, **Required:** true
  * This field determines the current status of your submission.
  * **Options:** In-Progress, Approved, Archived
  * **Example:** In-Progress
- **Submission Date**: Enter the date of your submission.
  * **Type:** date, **Required:** true
  * This field determines the date of your submission.
  * **Hint:** Enter the date of your submission.
  * **Example:** 2022-01-01
- **Submission Time**: Enter the time of your submission.
  * **Type:** time, **Required:** true
  * This field determines the time of your submission.
  * **Hint:** Enter the time of your submission.
  * **Example:** 12:00

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the required fields carefully and accurately.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the administrators.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure it meets the requirements.
