# online_class_assignment_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_class_assignment_upload_form is a tool used to collect and manage information related to online class assignments. This form is used to record and track student and teacher interactions, submission and due dates, and assignment status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*). 
2. Enter the title of the assignment.
3. Enter the student's name.
4. Enter the teacher's name.
5. Upload the file related to the assignment.
6. Enter the submit date and time.
7. (Optional) Enter the submit location.
8. Enter the teacher's email.
9. (Optional) Enter the student's email.
10. Select the assignment status (Active or Inactive).
11. (Optional) Enter comments for the teacher and student.
12. (Optional) Enter the file URL.
13. (Optional) Enter comments for the teacher and student.
14. (Optional) Enter the grade and score.
15. (Optional) Enter the due date.
16. (Optional) Enter the teacher's rating and student's rating.
17. (Optional) Enter the submission date and time.
18. (Optional) Enter who submitted the assignment and to whom it was submitted.
19. (Optional) Enter the submitted on date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assignment Title** (`assignment_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the assignment.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **File** (`file_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the file related to the assignment.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, required): Upload the file related to the assignment. Note: You can upload files in various formats such as .docx, .pdf, etc.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submit Location** (`submit_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the assignment was submitted (if applicable).
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the teacher.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the student.
* **Assignment Status** (`assignment_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the assignment (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter comments for the teacher and student.
* **File URL** (`file_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the file.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comment`, text, optional): Enter comments for the teacher.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comment`, text, optional): Enter comments for the student.
* **Grade** (`grade`, text, optional): Enter the grade of the assignment.
* **Score** (`score`, text, optional): Enter the score of the assignment.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the due date of the assignment.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, text, optional): Enter the rating of the teacher.
* **Student Rating** (`student_rating`, text, optional): Enter the rating of the student.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter who submitted the assignment.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, optional): Enter to whom the assignment was submitted.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Ensure that you are uploading the correct file format.
* Check the submission date and time for accuracy.
* Enter accurate information for teacher and student comments.
* Review and double-check the assignment status.
* Use correct format for dates and times (e.g., DD/MM/YY for dates, HH:MM for times).
* Use proper email formats for teacher and student emails.
