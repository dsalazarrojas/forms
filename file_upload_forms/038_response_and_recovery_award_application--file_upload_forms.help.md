The "Response And Recovery Award Application" form is designed to collect information from nominators for an award that recognizes outstanding contributions to response and recovery efforts. This form is intended to be completed by individuals or organizations who wish to nominate someone for this award. The form is not meant to be completed by the nominee themselves, but rather by a third-party nominator who can provide an objective assessment of the nominee's work.

Before starting the form, please ensure that you are not duplicating a nomination that has already been submitted. You can check the nomination list to see if a similar nomination has been made.

## Response And Recovery Award Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Response And Recovery Award Application" form is used to nominate individuals or organizations for an award that recognizes their outstanding contributions to response and recovery efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Nomination Form with the required and optional information.
2. Provide a brief impact summary of the nominee's work in the Impact Summary field.
3. Attach any supporting documents or files related to the nomination in the Supporting Documents field.
4. Select the correct committee from the Committee Name field (if applicable).
5. Enter the committee's contact information in the Committee Email and Committee Phone fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nomination Form (page 1)
• **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the nominee's work and why they are being nominated for the award.

### Impact Summary (page 2)
• **Impact Summary** (`impact_summary`, text, optional): Please provide a brief impact summary of the nominee's work. This should include details about the impact they have made and how their contributions have benefited the response and recovery efforts.

### Supporting Documents (page 3)
• **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Please attach any supporting documents or files related to the nomination, such as letters of recommendation or photos of the nominee's work.

### Committee Member Info (page 4)
• **Committee Member Info** (`committee_member_info`, text, optional): Please provide information about the committee member who is nominating the individual or organization.

### Committee Name (page 5)
• **Committee Name** (`committee_name`, select_one, optional): Please select the correct committee from the list. The committee will review and evaluate the nomination.

### Committee Email (page 6)
• **Committee Email** (`committee_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of the committee member who is nominating the individual or organization.

### Committee Phone (page 7)
• **Committee Phone** (`committee_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of the committee member who is nominating the individual or organization.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Please attach any supporting documents or files related to the nomination.
* Please enter the correct committee contact information (if applicable).
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
