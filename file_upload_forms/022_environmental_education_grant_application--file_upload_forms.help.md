# environmental_education_grant_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The environmental education grant application form is designed for grantmakers to provide information about their environmental education programs and projects. This form helps the organization assess and review grant applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Grantmaker Info" section with your name, contact information, and grantmaker's name (if applicable).
2. Provide details about your project in the "Project Details" section.
3. Enter your program budget and funding request in the "Budget" and "Funding Request" sections, respectively.
4. Explain why your project is a good fit for a grant in the "Grant Justification" section.
5. Indicate whether your project aligns with the organization's environmental education programs in the "Environmental Education Programs" section.
6. Add any additional information about your school in the "School Info" section (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grantmaker Info (Grantmaker Info)** (`grantmaker_info`, text, optional): This section is for grantmakers to provide their name, contact information, and any additional details about themselves.
* **Project Details (Project Details)** (`project_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Program Info (Program Info)** (`program_info`, text, optional): Describe your program in detail, including its scope and any relevant context.
* **Budget (Budget)** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Funding Request (Funding Request)** (`funding_request`, number, optional): Specify the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Grant Justification (Grant Justification)** (`grant_justification`, text, optional): Explain why your project is a good fit for a grant and how it aligns with the organization's goals and objectives.
* **Environmental Education Programs (Environmental Education Programs)** (`environmental_education_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether your project aligns with the organization's environmental education programs by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **School Info (School Info)** (`school_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your school, including its name and location (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions and explanations.
* Proofread your form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
