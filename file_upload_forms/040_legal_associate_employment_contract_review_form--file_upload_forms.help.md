# Legal Associate Employment Contract Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from legal associates regarding the employment contract of a new hire. It aims to ensure the contract meets all necessary requirements and gather any concerns or issues that may arise during the review process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the employment contract in question and ensure you have access to it.
2. Complete the fields as instructed below, and fill in any necessary information.
3. Please fill in your name and email address in the "Email" field.
4. For the "Contract Review Feedback" section, provide any feedback or comments you have about the contract.
5. For the "Document Concerns" section, list any concerns you may have about the document.
6. For the "Employment Agreement" section, select "Yes" if the employment contract meets all necessary requirements, or "No" if it does not.
7. For the "File Upload" section, attach any relevant files or documents that support your feedback.
8. For the "Date of Review", select the date when you reviewed the contract.
9. For the "Time of Review", select the time when you reviewed the contract.
10. For the "Comments" section, add any additional comments or notes you may have.
11. For the "Phone" field, enter your phone number.
12. Finally, sign the "Signature" field with your name to confirm that you have reviewed the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Review Feedback**: 
  This field is where you can provide any feedback or comments about the contract. Please be as detailed as possible and make sure to include any concerns or issues you may have.
* **Document Concerns**: 
  This field is where you can list any concerns you may have about the document. Please be specific and detailed in your concerns.
* **Employment Agreement**: 
  In this field, select "Yes" if the employment contract meets all necessary requirements, or "No" if it does not meet any requirements. If you select "No", please provide an explanation or justification for your choice.
* **File Upload**: 
  This field allows you to attach any relevant files or documents that support your feedback. Please upload any files that may help us understand your concerns or issues.
* **Date of Review**: 
  Select the date when you reviewed the contract. Make sure to select the correct date, as this will help us track the review process.
* **Time of Review**: 
  Select the time when you reviewed the contract. This will help us track the review process and ensure that all reviewers have reviewed the contract at the same time.
* **Comments**: 
  This field is where you can add any additional comments or notes you may have about the contract. Please be as detailed as possible and provide any relevant information.
* **Email**: 
  Fill in your email address to ensure we can contact you for further clarification or details.
* **Phone**: 
  Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further clarification or details.
* **Signature**: 
  Sign with your name to confirm that you have reviewed the contract and provide your feedback. This is a required field and must be filled out for the form to be submitted.

Please note that filling in this form is a mandatory part of the review process. Failure to complete this form may result in delayed or incomplete review of the employment contract.
