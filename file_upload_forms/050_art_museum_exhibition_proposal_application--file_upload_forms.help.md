# Art Museum Exhibition Proposal Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Museum Exhibition Proposal Application is a form designed for curators, artists, and organizers to submit exhibition proposals to the museum. 

## How To Complete This Form
To successfully fill out this form, please ensure all required fields have accurate and complete information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lead Applicant - Curator Name** (`id: 2`): Provide the name of the individual or organization leading the proposal submission.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Contact Email** (`id: 3`): Enter a valid email address that the museum can use to contact you with further information or inquiries.
    *   Type: `email`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Contact Phone** (`id: 4`): Enter your phone number for further contact.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Organizational Affiliation** (`id: 5`): If you are associated with an organization, please provide its name.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **Working Title of the Exhibition** (`id: 7`): Provide a concise title for the proposed exhibition.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Executive Summary** (`id: 8`): Write a 300-word summary of the exhibition's theme and importance.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Primary Artistic Focus** (`id: 9`): Select the primary artistic focus of your exhibition (e.g., Contemporary, Historical, Multi-disciplinary, Community-based, or Thematic).
    *   Type: `select_multiple`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **List of Proposed Artists** (`id: 10`): List the key artists involved in the exhibition.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Preferred Exhibition Timeframe** (`id: 12`): Choose when the exhibition should take place (Spring, Summer, Fall, or Winter).
    *   Type: `select_one`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Estimated Gallery Space Needed** (`id: 13`): Enter the square footage required for the exhibition space.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Special Installation Requirements** (`id: 14`): If any special installation requirements apply, mention them here.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **Estimated Total Project Budget** (`id: 15`): Enter the total budget for the exhibition, including currency.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Types of Support Requested** (`id: 18`): Select the types of support you are requesting from the museum (curatorial, space, marketing, educational, financial co-sponsorship).
    *   Type: `select_multiple`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Date of Submission** (`id: 20`): Enter the date you are submitting the proposal.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Lead Applicant Digital Signature** (`id: 21`): Add your digital signature.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`

## Tips

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to increase your chances of successfully submitting your proposal.
