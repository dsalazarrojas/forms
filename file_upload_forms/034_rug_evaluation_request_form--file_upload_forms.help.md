# Rug Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rug Evaluation Request Form is designed to collect information about a rug to be evaluated. It will be used by our evaluation team to assess the rug's condition, material, size, and other relevant details. Please fill out this form as accurately and completely as possible to ensure that we can provide you with the most accurate and efficient evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the rug.
2. Review the form for completeness and correctness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rug Evaluation Request Form (Text)** (`rug_evaluation_request_form`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the rug and its purpose for the evaluation.
* **Rug Details (Text)** (`rug_details`, Text, Optional): Describe the rug's details, such as its material, design, and any other notable features.
* **Rug Photos (Text)** (`rug_photos`, Text, Optional): Attach photos of the rug for our evaluation team to review.
* **Evaluation Request (Select One)** (`evaluation_request`, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of evaluation you require:
	+ Appraisal: A detailed written report on the rug's value and condition.
	+ Restoration Estimate: A cost estimate for any necessary repairs or restoration.
	+ Resale Inquiry: A report on the rug's market value and resale potential.
* **Appraisal Value (Number)** (`appraisal_value`, Number, Optional): Enter the estimated appraisal value of the rug.
* **Restoration Estimate (Number)** (`restoration_estimate`, Number, Optional): Enter the estimated cost of restoration or repair.
* **Resale Price (Number)** (`resale_price`, Number, Optional): Enter the estimated resale price of the rug.
* **Rug Condition (Text)** (`rug_condition`, Text, Optional): Describe the rug's condition, including any flaws or defects.
* **Material (Text)** (`material`, Text, Optional): Specify the rug's material, such as wool, silk, or synthetic.
* **Size (Number)** (`size`, Number, Optional): Enter the rug's size.
* **Color (Text)** (`color`, Text, Optional): Describe the rug's color.
* **Date Found (Date)** (`date_found`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the rug was found or acquired.
* **Date Sold (Date)** (`date_sold`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the rug was sold or acquired.
* **Notes (Text)** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the rug.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Attach clear and legible photos of the rug for our evaluation team to review.
* Review the form for completeness and correctness before submitting it.
