# Freelance Article Submission Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for freelance writers to submit their articles for review and potential publication. It is a one-time upload process where you provide detailed information about your submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form and start filling out the fields below.
2. Fill in your details as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload File** (`Upload File`, `text`, required: false): This is the first step in the submission process. Please upload a clean and well-formatted version of your article as a text file (`.txt`, `.md`, or `.docx`).
* **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the publication.
* **Contributor Email** (`contributor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for further questions or to send you updates about your submission.
* **Contributor Phone** (`contributor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with any urgent matters.
* **Contributor Notes** (`contributor_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or context about your submission that you think would be helpful for our editorial team.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when you submitted your article for review.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when you submitted your article for review.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information or context about your submission that you think would be helpful for our editorial team.
* **Editorial Comments** (`editorial_comments`, `note`, required: false): Our editorial team may leave comments or suggestions for you. This field will display these comments once they are provided.
