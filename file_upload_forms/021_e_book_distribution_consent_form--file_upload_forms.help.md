# E Book Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent for the electronic book (E-Book) distribution. It is a critical step in the E-Book distribution process, ensuring that all relevant details are captured before the distribution process begins. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient E-Book distribution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and materials ready.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, ensuring they accurately reflect your requirements.
3. Fill in the required text fields with the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### E Book Distribution Consent Form (1)
* **E Book Distribution Consent Form** (`text`, required): This field should contain the title of the E-Book.

### Digital Publication Approval (2)
* **Digital Publication Approval** (`select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if the E-Book is approved for digital publication, and 'No' if it is not.

### Platform Preferences (3)
* **Platform Preferences** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the platforms where the E-Book should be distributed, such as 'Yes' for Amazon, 'Yes' for Apple, 'No' for other platforms.

### Signed Consent (4)
* **Signed Consent** (`select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the E-Book has been signed, and 'No' if it has not.

### Organized Data Collection (5)
* **Organized Data Collection** (`select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the E-Book's data is organized, and 'No' if it is not.

### E Book Title (6)
* **E Book Title** (`text`, required): Fill in the title of the E-Book.

### E Book Isbn (7)
* **E Book Isbn** (`text`, required): Fill in the ISBN of the E-Book.

### Authors (8)
* **Authors** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the authors of the E-Book.

### Platform (9)
* **Platform** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the platforms where the E-Book should be distributed.

### Digital Rights Management (10)
* **Digital Rights Management** (`select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the E-Book has digital rights management, and 'No' if it does not.

### E Book Formats (11)
* **E Book Formats** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the formats in which the E-Book should be distributed.

### Language Version (12)
* **Language Version** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the language versions of the E-Book.

### Publication Date (13)
* **Publication Date** (`date`, required): Fill in the publication date of the E-Book.

### E Book File Size (14)
* **E Book File Size** (`number`, required): Fill in the file size of the E-Book.

### File Format (15)
* **File Format** (`select_one`, required): Select the format of the E-Book file.

### File Upload (16)
* **File Upload** (`text`, required): Upload the E-Book file.

### E Book Price (17)
* **E Book Price** (`number`, required): Fill in the price of the E-Book.

### E Book Price Currency (18)
* **E Book Price Currency** (`select_one`, required): Select the currency of the E-Book price.

### E Book Rights Owner (19)
* **E Book Rights Owner** (`text`, required): Fill in the E-Book rights owner.

### E Book Rights (20)
* **E Book Rights** (`text`, required): Fill in the E-Book rights.

### E Book Rights Notes (21)
* **E Book Rights Notes** (`text`, required): Fill in any additional E-Book rights notes.

### E Book Distributor (22)
* **E Book Distributor** (`text`, required): Fill in the E-Book distributor.

### E Book Publisher (23)
* **E Book Publisher** (`text`, required): Fill in the E-Book publisher.

### E Book Author (24)
* **E Book Author** (`text`, required): Fill in the E-Book author.

### E Book Publisher Contact (25)
* **E Book Publisher Contact** (`text`, required): Fill in the E-Book publisher contact details.

### Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your administrator or supervisor for guidance.
