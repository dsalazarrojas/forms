# Empowerment Content Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Empowerment Content Submission Form is a data collection tool designed to gather information from users about their personal experiences related to empowerment content. This form is part of a broader initiative to better understand the needs and perspectives of individuals and communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter a reference or identifier for a file you have uploaded elsewhere in the "file" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your experience in the "description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address.
* **File** (`file`, text, optional): Please enter a reference or identifier for a file you have uploaded elsewhere.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any relevant context or information about your personal development or social impact efforts.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email address you provide is accurate and valid.
* You can enter any reference or identifier for a file that you have uploaded elsewhere, such as a URL, document, or database ID.
* The description should be clear and concise, providing enough information for reviewers to understand your experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
