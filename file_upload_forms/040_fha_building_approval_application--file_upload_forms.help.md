# fha_building_approval_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for project stakeholders to provide building project details, including project address, project manager information, and document uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Project Details** page with the project's name, address, and other relevant information.
2. On the **Building Details** page, enter the type of building and its intended use.
3. Provide the project's **Documents** page with any necessary supporting files.
4. Enter the **Project Manager**'s name and email address.
5. Enter your own **Submitter** information, including your phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`): Enter the project's name and address to provide context for the project.
* **Building Details** (`building_details`): Describe the type of building and its intended use.
* **Documents** (`documents`): Upload any supporting documents related to the project.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`): Enter the project manager's name and contact information.
* **Project Manager Email** (`project_manager_email`): Enter the project manager's email address.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): Enter the project's location.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Enter the type of property.
* **Building Type** (`building_type`): Enter the building type.
* **Property Use** (`property_use`): Enter the intended use of the property.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Enter the current project status.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name and identify yourself as the submitter of the form.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address to enable contact.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number to enable contact.
* **Project Status Update** (`project_status_update`): Provide an update on the project's current status.
* **Document Upload 1-8** (`document_upload_1`, `document_upload_2`, etc.): Upload any additional documents related to the project.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with **required**.
* Ensure that all uploaded documents are in a suitable format (e.g., PDF or JPEG).
* Proofread your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
