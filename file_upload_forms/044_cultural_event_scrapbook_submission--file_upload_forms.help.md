## Cultural Event Scrapbook Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Event Scrapbook Submission form is a tool for users to share their experiences and memories from cultural events. It helps to collect information about various events, such as concerts, festivals, exhibitions, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Go to the first page of the form, where you will find the following fields:
    *   **event_name**: Enter the name of the event you attended.
    *   **event_date**: Select the date of the event.
    *   **location**: Enter the location where the event took place.
*   On the next page, you will find additional fields:
    *   **description**: Share a brief description of your experience at the event.
    *   **photos**: Add any photos you took or came across that capture memories from the event.
    *   **stories**: Share any interesting stories or moments that stood out during the event.
    *   **reflections**: Reflect on your experience and share any insights or takeaways you gained.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **event_name**: The name of the event you attended. Enter the title or name of the event, such as a concert, festival, or exhibition.
*   **event_date**: The date of the event. Select the date when the event took place, if known. If not, you can also enter a range of dates or a date range.
*   **location**: The venue or location of the event. Enter the address or name of the location where the event occurred.
*   **description**: Share a brief summary of your experience at the event. This can include your favorite moments, interesting facts, or notable performances.
*   **photos**: Upload any photos you took or came across that capture memories from the event. These can be images of performers, attendees, or event scenery.
*   **stories**: Share any interesting or memorable stories from the event. This can include interactions with attendees, notable moments, or personal anecdotes.
*   **reflections**: Reflect on your experience and share any insights or takeaways you gained from the event. This can include what you learned, how you felt, or what you would do differently next time.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
*   Use the provided fields to share as much information as you can.
*   If a field is not applicable or not relevant, you can leave it blank.
