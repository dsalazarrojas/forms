# Tenant Move In/Out Photo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the submission of photos related to a tenant's move-in or move-out activities. This form is used to collect information and images that can help property managers and owners track and record the status of tenant move-ins and move-outs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the relevant move-in or move-out date**: Choose the date for which you want to submit photos. This date can be either the move-in date or the move-out date.
2. **Upload the move-in or move-out photo**: Click on the "Move In Photo" or "Move Out Photo" field and upload the relevant photo from your device or computer.
3. **Enter tenant and property manager signatures**: If required, enter the signatures of both the tenant and the property manager.
4. **Add any comments or notes**: Use the "Comments" field to provide any additional information or context related to the move-in or move-out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Move In Date**: Select the date on which the tenant moved into the property.
* **Move Out Date**: Select the date on which the tenant moved out of the property.
* **Move In Photo**: Upload a photo that confirms the tenant's move-in to the property. This can be a picture of the tenant, the rental unit, or any other relevant image.
* **Move Out Photo**: Upload a photo that confirms the tenant's move-out from the property. Like the move-in photo, this can be a picture of the tenant, the rental unit, or any other relevant image.
* **Tenant Signature**: If required, enter the signature of the tenant, confirming their presence in the property at the time of the move-in or move-out.
* **Property Manager Signature**: If required, enter the signature of the property manager, confirming their acceptance of the tenant's move-in or move-out.
* **Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or notes related to the move-in or move-out.
