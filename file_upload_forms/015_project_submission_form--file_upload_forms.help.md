# Project Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Submission Form is a digital form designed to collect project ideas, details, and requirements from users. This form helps us gather essential information to better understand your project needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Ideas** (`project_ideas`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your project idea. You can type up to 100 characters to give us a sense of what you're thinking.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your project. This field helps us understand the specifics of your project.
* **Project Materials** (`project_materials`, text, optional): If you're submitting a project that requires specific materials, please type them in this field. This field is not limited to a specific length, but be concise for our review process.
* **Project Supplies** (`project_supplies`, select_one, optional): Select the type of supplies you need for your project from the available options. This helps us better understand your project's needs.
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, text, optional): Share a brief statement about the artist or artist team involved in your project. This field is not limited by length, so feel free to be as creative and detailed as you like.

## Tips
Remember that all fields are optional, but providing more information about your project can help us better understand and review it. If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact us!
