The purpose of this form is to collect complaints or concerns about paternity, which can be related to various issues such as disputes over parental rights, custody, or child support. This form is intended for individuals who wish to report or express their concerns about paternity-related matters, and it provides a structured format for them to provide relevant details and documentation.

To ensure the integrity of the process, the form has been designed to be comprehensive and easy to use. However, it's essential to complete it accurately and thoroughly to facilitate efficient and effective handling of the complaint.

**Paternity Complaint Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect complaints or concerns about paternity-related issues. Please provide details about your complaint or concern, and attach any relevant documentation or evidence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct complaint type from the options provided.
3. Provide a detailed description of your complaint or concern.
4. Enter the case number and type.
5. Specify the date and time of the occurrence.
6. Attach any relevant files or supporting documentation.
7. Enter your contact email and phone number.
8. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`): Provide a brief description of the complaint or concern.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`): Attach any relevant files or documents that support your complaint or concern.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`): Select the type of complaint or concern you are reporting.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`): Provide a detailed description of your complaint or concern.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`): Enter the case number associated with your complaint or concern.
* **Case Type** (`case_type`): Select the type of case associated with your complaint or concern.
* **Date of Occurrence** (`date_of_occurrence`): Enter the date when the complaint or concern occurred.
* **Time of Occurrence** (`time_of_occurrence`): Enter the time when the complaint or concern occurred.
* **File** (`file`): Attach any relevant files or documents that support your complaint or concern.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Review and submit your completed form.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`): Provide a statement or testimony from a witness or third party (optional).
* **Supporting Document 1** to **Supporting Document 12** (`supporting_document1`, `supporting_document2`, ..., `supporting_document12`): Attach any additional documents or evidence that support your complaint or concern (optional).
