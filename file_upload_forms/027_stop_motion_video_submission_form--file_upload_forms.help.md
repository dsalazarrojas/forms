# Stop Motion Video Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for users to submit stop motion videos and accompanying metadata. To ensure that your submission is successful, please review the following instructions carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears in your profile or as you would like it to appear on the entry.
2.  **Title**: Enter a brief title for your entry. This will appear on the entry page and should accurately reflect the content of your video.
3.  **Video ID**: Enter the unique ID associated with your video submission. This should be provided to you by the festival organizers.
4.  **Video URL**: If you have a publicly accessible URL for your video, enter it here.
5.  **File 1**: If you have additional materials to attach with your submission, such as images or documents, upload them here.
6.  **File 1 Type**: Enter the type of file you uploaded (e.g., image, document, etc.).
7.  **Entry Type**: Select whether this entry is a yes or no.
8.  **Entry Name**: Enter a name for your entry that will appear on the entry page.
9.  **Entry Description**: Enter a brief description of your entry, including details about the video and its content.
10. **Date**: Enter the date you would like the entry to be listed on the calendar.
11. **Time**: Enter the time you would like the entry to be listed.
12. **Entry Image**: If you have an image you would like to attach to your entry, upload it here.
13. **Entry Video**: If you have an additional video you would like to attach, upload it here.
14. **School**: Enter the name of your school or institution affiliated with this entry.
15. **School URL**: If you have a URL for your school's website, enter it here.
16. **Festival**: Enter the name of the festival or event this entry is associated with.
17.  **Category**: Enter the category or genre of your entry.
18.  **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person or team submitting this entry.
19.  **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person or team submitting this entry. This will be the primary point of contact for festival organizers.
20.  **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or team submitting this entry.
21.  **Contact Message**: Enter a brief message or note for festival organizers.
22.  **Tags**: Enter any tags or keywords associated with your entry, separated by commas.
