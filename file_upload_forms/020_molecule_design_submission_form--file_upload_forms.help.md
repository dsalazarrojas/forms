# Molecule Design Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Molecule Design Submission Form is a tool for users to submit their molecule designs for review or discussion. It collects information about the molecule, including its name, structure, and context, as well as allows users to upload supporting files.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the molecule name in the "Molecule Name" field.
2. Provide a description of the molecule's structure in the "Molecule Structure" field.
3. Add context to your submission in the "Context" field.
4. Choose the type of supporting files you want to upload (e.g., "Supporting files for review", "Other files for context", or "Files for reference").
5. Upload the selected supporting files in the "Files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Molecule Name** (`molecule_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your molecule design. This field is optional, but please provide a brief and descriptive name for your molecule.
* **Molecule Structure** (`molecule_structure`, text, required/optional): Describe the structure of your molecule, including its components and how they interact.
* **Context** (`context`, text, required/optional): Add any relevant context or background information about your molecule design.
* **Supporting Files** (`supporting_files`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose the type of files you want to upload to support your submission (e.g., "Supporting files for review", "Other files for context", or "Files for reference").
* **Files** (`files`, text, required/optional): Upload the selected supporting files for your molecule design.

## Tips
* Ensure that you're submitting a new molecule design and not a duplicate.
* Provide clear and concise descriptions for each field to help reviewers understand your submission.
* Use the "Supporting Files" field to upload relevant files that support your molecule design.
* If you have any questions or need help with completing this form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
