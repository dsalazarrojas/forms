# medicare-hospital-stay-waiver-extension-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicare Hospital Stay Waiver Extension Request form is used to request an extension of a pre-authorized Medicare hospital stay waiver. This form must be completed and submitted for patients who require additional hospital stay time due to extenuating circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary information and supporting documents to complete the form accurately.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
3. Upload any required medical records or supporting documents.
4. Select the relevant options for Care Team Review, Patient Authorization, and Review and Sign.
5. Review and confirm the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and hospital ID number to identify the patient.
* **Medical Record Upload** (`medical_record_upload`, text, optional): Upload any supporting medical records or documentation to support the waiver request.
* **Care Team Review** (`care_team_review`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate the care team's review status:
	+ Option 1: Review in progress
	+ Option 2: Review completed
	+ Option 3: Not reviewed
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is used to capture any additional comments or notes from the care team.
* **Facility Info** (`facility_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the facility, such as the room number or unit.
* **Patient Authorization** (`patient_authorization`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the options to indicate the patient's authorization status:
	+ Option 1: Authorized
	+ Option 2: Not authorized
	+ Option 3: Pending
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents, such as lab results or test reports, to support the waiver request.
* **Review and Sign** (`review_and_sign`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate the review and sign status:
	+ Option 1: Review and sign
	+ Option 2: Not signed
	+ Option 3: Not reviewed

## Tips
- Ensure you have the necessary information and supporting documents before submitting the form.
- Double-check the patient's information and authorization status before submitting the form.
- Attach all required supporting documents to ensure a complete and accurate submission.
