# submit_your_recipe - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user-submitted recipes with specific details such as category, cooking time, and serving size. The form allows users to submit their recipes and view existing ones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the "Recipe Title" field**: Enter the title of your recipe in the text field.
2. **Upload a "Recipe Image"**: You can upload an image that represents your recipe. This will help display your recipe in a more engaging way.
3. **Write a "Recipe Description"**: Enter a brief description of your recipe in the text field.
4. **Select a "Category"**: Choose the category of your recipe from the dropdown menu. Categories include:
	* Beef
	* Pork
	* Chicken
	* Vegetarian
	* Seafood
5. **Enter "Cooking Time"**: If your recipe has a specific cooking time, enter it in the number field. This field is optional.
6. **Enter "Serving Size"**: If your recipe serves a specific number of people, enter it in the number field. This field is optional.
7. **List "Ingredients"**: If your recipe has specific ingredients, list them in the text field. This field is optional.
8. **Click "Submit Recipe"**: Once you have filled out all the fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your recipe. You can also click "Cancel" to discard your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipe Title (text, required)**: Enter the title of your recipe.
* **Recipe Image (text, optional)**: Upload an image that represents your recipe.
* **Recipe Description (text, optional)**: Enter a brief description of your recipe.
* **Category (select_one, required)**: Choose the category of your recipe from the dropdown menu.
* **Cooking Time (number, optional)**: If your recipe has a specific cooking time, enter it.
* **Serving Size (number, optional)**: If your recipe serves a specific number of people, enter it.
* **Ingredients (text, optional)**: List the ingredients used in your recipe.
* **Submit Recipe (select_one, required)**: Choose "Submit" to submit your recipe or "Cancel" to discard your submission.
