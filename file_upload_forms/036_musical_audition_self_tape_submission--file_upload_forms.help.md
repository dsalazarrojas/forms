# Musical Audition Self Tape Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you submit a self-tape video for a musical audition. It is used to provide information about your performances, skills, and materials in a structured and clear way. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your submission is complete and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Performer Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Contact Email** in the second field (optional but recommended for communication purposes).
3. Provide a brief description of the **Audition Title** in the third field.
4. In the **Audition Description** field, add any additional details about your performance that you'd like to share.
5. Select the **Performance Dates** for your upcoming performances.
6. Upload a video file for your **Self Tape** in the format of MP4.
7. Attach your **Resume** to showcase your experience and skills.
8. Upload a photo for your **Headshot**.
9. Attach your **Materials** such as sheet music, scripts, or other relevant documents.
10. Upload any additional **Self Tapes** you'd like to submit.
11. If applicable, attach any **Additional Materials** you think would be helpful for your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Performer Name**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** true
  * **Explanation:** Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. This will be used to identify you throughout the audition process.
- **Contact Email**: 
  * **Type:** email
  * **Required:** false
  * **Explanation:** This is an optional field for you to provide your contact email address. Please use the format `name@domain.com`. It will help us contact you for follow-up questions.
- **Audition Title**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** true
  * **Explanation:** Enter a brief title of the audition you're applying for, such as "Singing Role" or "Dance Performance".
- **Audition Description**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** false
  * **Explanation:** If you have any additional information about the audition or performance, please add it here.
- **Performance Dates**: 
  * **Type:** select_multiple
  * **Required:** true
  * **Explanation:** Select the dates of your upcoming performance, such as "American Theater" or "Regional Theater".
- **Self Tape Video (MP4)**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** true
  * **Explanation:** Upload a video file in MP4 format showcasing your performance.
- **Resume**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** true
  * **Explanation:** Attach your resume to showcase your experience and skills.
- **Headshot**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** true
  * **Explanation:** Upload a photo of yourself.
- **Materials**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** true
  * **Explanation:** Attach any relevant materials such as sheet music or scripts.
- **Self Tapes**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** true
  * **Explanation:** Upload any additional self-tape videos you'd like to submit.
- **Additional Materials**: 
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** false
  * **Explanation:** If you have any additional materials you'd like to submit, please attach them here.
