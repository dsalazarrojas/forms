# Degree Evaluation Fee Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Degree Evaluation Fee Payment Form is a tool for collecting information related to degree evaluation, including academic documents and payment details. This form is specifically designed for use within the higher education industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your degree evaluation request.
2.  Attach your academic documents as required.
3.  Enter your online payment information.
4.  Provide any payment details as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Degree Evaluation Request** (required):
    This field is used to describe your request for degree evaluation.
* **Academic Documents** (optional):
    Attach your academic documents, if required.
* **Online Payment** (optional):
    Enter your online payment information.
* **Payment Details** (optional):
    Provide any additional payment details needed to complete your payment. 

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all the required fields.
* If you're unsure about what to enter in the `Academic Documents` field, please refer back to your original degree evaluation request.
* If you need assistance with completing this form, don't hesitate to reach out to the support team.
* This form is a tool to help facilitate your degree evaluation process, please ensure accuracy and completeness of your entries.
