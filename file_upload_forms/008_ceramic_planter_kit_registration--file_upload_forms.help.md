# Ceramic Planter Kit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ceramic Planter Kit Registration form is used to record important information about a ceramic planter kit that has been purchased or registered. This form helps us to track the registration of the product, gather feedback, and improve our product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields:

1. **Registration Date**: Enter the date the product was registered with us.
2. **Customer Name**: Enter the full name of the person who purchased the product.
3. **Email Address**: Enter the email address associated with the purchase.
4. **Phone Number** (optional): Enter the contact phone number of the customer.
5. **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where the product was delivered.
6. **Purchase Date**: Enter the date the product was purchased.
7. **Purchase Location**: Select where you purchased the kit from the list of options.
8. **Order Number** (optional): Enter the order confirmation number if available.
9. **Planter Kit Model**: Select the type of planter kit purchased.
10. **Planter Color**: Select the color of the planter.
11. **Kit Contents Complete**: Confirm if all listed items were included in the kit.
12. **Missing Items** (if applicable): List any missing components from the kit.
13. **Condition on Arrival**: Describe the condition of the product when received.
14. **Intended Use**: Select the intended use of the planter.
15. **Gardening Experience Level**: Rate your gardening experience level.
16. **Photo Upload** (optional): Upload a photo of your registered planter.
17. **Newsletter Signup**: Check if you want to receive gardening tips and product updates.
18. **How Did You Hear About Us** (optional): Select how you became aware of our product.
19. **Would Recommend**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend this product to others.
20. **Additional Comments** (optional): Share any additional feedback about your purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): The date the product was registered with us.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): The full name of the person who purchased the product.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): The email associated with the purchase for registration confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): The contact phone number of the customer.
* **Shipping Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): The address where the product was delivered.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): The date the product was purchased.
* **Purchase Location** (`purchase_location`, select_one, required): Where the product was purchased from the list of options.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): The order confirmation number if available.
* **Planter Kit Model** (`planter_kit_model`, select_one, required): The type of planter kit purchased.
* **Planter Color** (`planter_color`, select_one, required): The color of the planter.
* **Kit Contents Complete** (`kit_contents_complete`, select_one, required): Confirm if all listed items were included in the kit.
* **Missing Items** (`missing_items`, text, optional): List any missing components from the kit.
* **Condition on Arrival** (`condition_on_arrival`, select_one, required): Describe the condition of the product when received.
* **Intended Use** (`intended_use`, select_one, required): Select the intended use of the planter.
* **Gardening Experience Level** (`gardening_experience`, select_one, required): Rate your gardening experience level.
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, text, optional): Upload a photo of your registered planter.
* **Newsletter Signup** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Check if you want to receive gardening tips and product updates.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Select how you became aware of our product.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend this product to others.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback about your purchase.
