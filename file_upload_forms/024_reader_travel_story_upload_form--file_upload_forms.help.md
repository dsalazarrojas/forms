# Reader Travel Story Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reader Travel Story Upload Form is designed for readers to share their travel stories with a wider audience. The form helps to collect detailed information about the story, including its title, author, content, location, and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form description field to provide a brief summary of your story.
2. Select the files you would like to upload with your story.
3. Choose relevant tags to categorize your story.
4. Enter the story text in the story text field.
5. Select the story photos.
6. Choose the story date and time.
7. Select the story status.
8. Enter any additional story notes.
9. Select the story title and author.
10. Finally, click on the "Save and Submit" button to upload your story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your travel story.
* **Files** (`file_uploads`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the files you would like to upload with your story. You can choose one or multiple files from the options.
* **More Files** (`file_uploads_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select additional files to upload with your story, if needed.
* **Story Text** (`story_text`, `text`, required): Enter the detailed text of your travel story.
* **Story Photos** (`story_photos`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the story photos.
* **Tags** (`tags`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose relevant tags to categorize your story.
* **Story Date** (`story_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your travel story.
* **Story Time** (`story_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your travel story.
* **Story Location** (`story_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of your travel story.
* **Story Rating** (`story_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your story.
* **Story Title** (`story_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of your travel story.
* **Story Author** (`story_author`, `text`, required): Enter your name or username as the author of your travel story.
* **Story Content** (`story_content`, `text`, required): Enter the detailed content of your travel story.
* **Story Comments** (`story_comments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any relevant comments for your story.
* **Story Tags** (`story_tags`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose relevant tags for your story.
* **Story Date Time** (`story_date_time`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time of your travel story.
* **Story Status** (`story_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of your story.
* **Story Notes** (`story_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes for your story.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to upload your story.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, optional): Cancel your story upload process.
* **Back** (`back`, `text`, optional): Go back to previous step.
* **Save** (`save`, `text`, optional): Save your story without uploading.
* **Delete** (`delete`, `text`, optional): Delete your story.
* **Preview** (`preview`, `text`, optional): Preview your story before uploading.
* **Upload** (`upload`, `text`, optional): Upload your files to the story.
* **Save and Submit** (`save_and_submit`, `text`, required): Save and upload your story.
* **Save and Preview** (`save_and_preview`, `text`, optional): Save and preview your story.
* **Save and Send** (`save_and_send`, `text`, optional): Save and send your story.
* **Save and Download** (`save_and_download`, `text`, optional): Save and download your story.
