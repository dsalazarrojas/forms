# Safe Home Fund Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safe Home Fund Application form is designed to collect information from users to evaluate their home safety and determine eligibility for support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the form with the requested information.
2. For each field, enter the required information to ensure accurate and complete submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself and your home.
* **Upload Documents** (`file_attachment`, text, optional): You can upload any relevant documents to support your application.
* **Home Safety Details** (`home_safety_details`, text, optional): Describe the safety concerns or hazards in your home.
* **Follow Up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional follow-up information or requests.
* **Non Profit Organization** (`nonprofit_organization`, select_one, optional): Select the type of organization providing support, such as Non-Profit Org, Community Program, or Government Agency.
* **Faster Follow Up** (`faster_follow_up`, date, optional): If you require faster follow-up, enter a specific date for follow-up.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): Enter your home address for reference.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for follow-up.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your application.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any supporting documents, please upload them to the "Upload Documents" field.
* If you have any follow-up requests, please specify them in the "Follow Up Details" field.
* If you require faster follow-up, please enter the date for follow-up in the "Faster Follow Up" field.
