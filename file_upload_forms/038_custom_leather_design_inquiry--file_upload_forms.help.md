# Custom Leather Design Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Leather Design Inquiry form is a tool for customers to submit their design concepts, material preferences, and project expectations for a custom leather design project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, click on the "Customer Information" page.
2. Fill in your contact information, including name, email, and any other relevant details.
3. On the "Design Details" page, provide a clear and concise description of your design concept.
4. On the "Material Preferences" page, select your preferred materials for the project.
5. On the "Upload Images" page, upload any relevant images that illustrate your design concept.
6. On the "Project Expectations" page, describe your project timeline, budget, and any other relevant expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer Information)** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Design Concept (Design Details)** (`design_details`, text, optional): Describe your design concept in a clear and concise manner, including any relevant inspiration or reference points.
* **Material Preferences (Material Preferences)** (`material_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred materials from the available options.
* **Upload Images (Upload Images)** (`upload_images`, number, optional): Upload any images that illustrate your design concept, up to [number] images. Please ensure that images are clear and well-formatted.
* **Project Expectations (Project Expectations)** (`project_expectations`, text, optional): Describe your project timeline, budget, and any other relevant expectations.
