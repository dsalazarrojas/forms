# Tech Prototype Design Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and files from users related to tech prototype design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "File Uploads" page.
2. You can upload one or more files by selecting them from your computer or device. The form does not restrict the type of file that can be uploaded.
3. Fill in the "tech_concept", "product_name", "accelerator_name", and "lab_name" fields with relevant information.

* **File Uploads**: Upload one or more files related to the tech prototype design. This field is not required.

* **accelerator_name** (`accelerator_name`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of the accelerator used in the design or development process.
