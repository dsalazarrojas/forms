# Interoperable Communications Plan Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interoperable communications plan submission form is used to gather information from partner agencies that want to submit their plans. This form will help us assess the completeness and correctness of the submitted plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the partner agency field with the name of your agency.
2. Enter the submission date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Provide your contact person's name and email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Upload a file with your interoperable communications plan.
6. Provide a name for the uploaded file.
7. Select the type of file you uploaded.
8. Enter the size of the uploaded file.
9. Choose whether your plan is ready for review ('Yes') or not ('No') in the review workflow field.
10. Select the review status of your plan as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Agency**: Enter the name of your partner agency. This will help us identify your agency and provide you with correct support.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting your interoperable communications plan. Use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for submitting this plan and their email address.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Upload File**: Upload your interoperable communications plan file. Ensure the file is in a supported format (e.g., pdf, docx, etc.).
* **File Name**: Enter a name for the uploaded file. This will help us identify the file correctly.
* **File Type**: Select the type of file you uploaded (e.g., PDF, Word Document, etc.).
* **File Size**: Enter the size of the uploaded file in bytes (e.g., 1234567).
* **Review Workflow**: Select whether your plan is ready for review ('Yes') or not ('No').
* **Review Status**: Select the status of your review as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

## Tips

* Ensure that the file you upload is in a supported format.
* If your plan is not ready for review, please select 'No' in the review workflow field.
* If your review status is 'Inactive', please explain the reason in the submission form or provide additional information to support your status.
