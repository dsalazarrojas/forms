# Inheritance Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about inheritance in real estate, including estate details, heir details, key dates, centralization status, and multiple file uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the title of the estate.
2. Fill in the name and details of the heir(s).
3. Enter the key dates related to the estate.
4. Check the centralization status of the inheritance.
5. Upload the required documents using the file upload fields.

* **Estate Details** (estate_details): Enter the title of the estate you are reporting about.
* **Heir Details** (heir_details): Enter the name(s) of the heir(s) involved in the estate.
* **Key Dates** (key_dates): Enter the key dates related to the estate, such as the date of death or key events.
