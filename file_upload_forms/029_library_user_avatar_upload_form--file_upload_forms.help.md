# Library User Avatar Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library User Avatar Upload Form is used to upload a profile picture or avatar for library users. This form allows users to provide a personalized image that represents them in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your profile in the text box at the top of the form.
2. Select the category of information you would like to associate with your profile from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide a brief description of yourself in the text box.
4. Check the consent checkbox to agree that your information is accurate.
5. Enter your contact details in the text box, including any relevant membership numbers.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your profile that will be displayed alongside your avatar.
* **form_category** (`form_category`, select one, optional): Select the category of information you would like to associate with your profile, such as "File Upload Forms" or "Information Forms".
* **form_description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself to help others get to know you better.
* **form_consent** (`form_consent`, text, optional): Check this box to confirm that the information you provide is accurate.
* **form_contact_details** (`form_contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact details, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **form_membership_numbers** (`form_membership_numbers`, text, optional): Enter any relevant membership numbers that you would like to associate with your profile.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to enter a clear and concise title and description that accurately represents you.
* Only check the consent box if you are sure that the information you provide is accurate.
* Use relevant contact details that can be used to reach you.
