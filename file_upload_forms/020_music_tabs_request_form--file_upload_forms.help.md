# Music Tabs Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Tabs Request Form is used to submit a request for a music tab for a specific song.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you are requesting a music tab ("Submit Music Tab Request") and select the option that applies to your request.
2. Enter your name in the "Who is requesting music tab" field, so we can contact you with any updates or questions about your request.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, so we can contact you with any updates or questions about your request.
4. Enter the name of the song you are requesting a tab for in the "Song Name" field.
5. Enter the URL of the musician or artist that performs the song in the "Musician URL" field.
6. Enter the name of the musician that performs the song in the "Musician" field.
7. Choose the type of music tab you are requesting ("Song Type") from the available options.
8. If applicable, enter the song key in the "Song Key" field.
9. Choose the instrument you would like the music tab for from the available options ("Instrument").
10. Enter the date you would like the music tab for, if you have a specific date in mind.
11. Review your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Music Tab Request** (Select)
	+ Select whether you are requesting a music tab (Yes/No)
* **Who is requesting music tab** (Text)
	+ Enter your name
* **Email Address** (Email)
	+ Enter your email address
* **Music File** (Text)
	+ Enter any additional information about the music file (e.g., "mp3", "wav")
* **Song Name** (Text)
	+ Enter the name of the song you are requesting a tab for
* **Musician URL** (Text)
	+ Enter the URL of the musician or artist that performs the song
* **Musician** (Text)
	+ Enter the name of the musician that performs the song
* **Song Type** (Select One)
	+ Choose the type of music tab you are requesting:
		- Tab
		- Sheet Music
		- Other
		- Cover
		- Arrangement
* **Song Key** (Text)
	+ Enter the key of the song (e.g., "C Major", "G Minor")
* **Instrument** (Select One)
	+ Choose the instrument you would like the music tab for:
		- Piano
		- Guitar
		- Banjo
		- Drums
		- Violin
		- Other
* **Request Date** (Date)
	+ Enter the date you would like the music tab for

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your selections and email address for accuracy.
* If you have any additional information or questions, please include it in the "Music File" field.
