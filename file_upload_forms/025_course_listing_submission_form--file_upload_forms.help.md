# Course Listing Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Course Listing Submission Form is designed for submitting courses for listing and review. It is crucial to ensure that courses are accurately and correctly submitted to avoid duplication and non-relevant submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Page 1**
	* Fill out the form completely and correctly, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. **Pages 2-9**
	* For each page, provide the necessary information as requested in the field labels.
	* Ensure that you select the correct submission type and status for your course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Page 1 Title** (Required)
	* Enter a brief title for your course submission.
### Page 2
* **Yes/No, Class Details** (Optional)
	* Select 'Yes' if your course submission is for a class, 'No' otherwise.
* **Class Details** (Optional)
	* Describe the class and its details if selected as 'Yes'.
### Page 3
* **Class Description** (Optional)
	* Provide a detailed description of the class, including its goals and objectives.
### Page 4
* **Submission Type** (Optional)
	* Select the type of submission (Yes/No) to indicate whether your course submission is for a class or not.
### Page 5
* **Submission Date** (Optional)
	* Enter the date of your course submission.
### Page 6
* **Submission Time** (Optional)
	* Enter the time of your course submission.
### Page 7
* **Submission Status** (Optional)
	* Select the status of your course submission (Active/Inactive).
### Page 8
* **Submission Date** (Optional)
	* Enter the date of your course submission.
### Page 9
* **Submission Time** (Optional)
	* Enter the time of your course submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* Double-check your submission before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authority for clarification.
