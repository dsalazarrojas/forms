# Legal Document Submission Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to upload and manage legal documents, including tracking their status and capturing submitter and approver information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Upload Document**: Upload the legal document that you would like to submit for approval. Click on the "Browse" button to select a file from your computer.
2. Complete the required fields: 
   * **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the document.
   * **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
   * **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Click the "Submit" button to submit the document for review.
4. The approver will review your document and update the status of the submission as either "Approved" or "Rejected".
5. If the approver updates the status, they will also be able to enter comments on the document.
6. The approver will be able to enter their signature and IP address for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Document** (`file_upload_1`): This field allows you to upload a legal document. Click on the "Browse" button to select a file from your computer.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`): This field is for your name as the person submitting the document.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): This field will automatically be populated with the current date and time of submission.
* **Status** (`status`): This field will be updated by the approver to indicate the current status of the submission: "Awaiting Approval", "Approved", or "Rejected".
* **Comments** (`comments`): This field is where the approver can enter any comments or feedback on the submission.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`): This field is for the name of the person reviewing the submission.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`): Enter the email address of the person reviewing the submission.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person reviewing the submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time of submission by the approver.
* **Approver Date** (`approver_date`): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time of approval or rejection.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`): This field is for the approver to enter their signature.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`): This field is for the submitter to enter their signature.
* **Submitter IP Address** (`submitter_ip_address`): This field captures the IP address of the submitter at the time of submission.
* **Approver IP Address** (`approver_ip_address`): This field captures the IP address of the approver at the time of approval or rejection.
* **Status** (`status`): This field will be updated by the approver to indicate the current status of the submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid errors in the submission process.
* Review the comments and feedback from the approver to improve your submission.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to upload and review documents.
* Use a valid email address and phone number for communication purposes.
* The submission date and time fields will automatically be populated, but the approver date and time fields will only be populated after the approver has reviewed and updated the submission.
