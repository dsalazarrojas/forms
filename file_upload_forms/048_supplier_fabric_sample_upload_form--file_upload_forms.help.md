# Supplier Fabric Sample Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Fabric Sample Upload Form is designed to collect and store supplier information for fabric sample submissions. This form serves as a centralized hub for storing key details about a supplier's fabric sample, including identification, contact, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Select a page to start from (this form has only one page).
2. Enter the supplier's ID number.
3. Enter the supplier's name and contact details.
4. Enter the supplier's email address (this is a required field).
5. Optionally, enter additional details about the fabric sample.
6. Upload images of the fabric sample (optional).
7. Enter the supplier's address and phone number (these are required fields).
8. Enter the supplier's fax number (optional).
9. Add any notes about the supplier (optional).
10. Enter the weight of the fabric sample in grams (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier ID** (`supplier_id`, number, required): Enter a unique ID number for the supplier.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's name.
* **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's contact details.
* **Supplier Email** (`supplier_email`, email, required): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Fabric Sample Details** (`fabric_sample_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the fabric sample.
* **Fabric Sample Images** (`fabric_sample_images`, text, optional): Upload images of the fabric sample (optional).
* **Supplier Address** (`supplier_address`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Supplier Phone** (`supplier_phone`, text, required): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Supplier Fax** (`supplier_fax`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's fax number.
* **Supplier Notes** (`supplier_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the supplier.
* **Sample Weight** (`fabric_sample_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the fabric sample in grams.
