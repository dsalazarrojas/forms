# Generic Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generic Submission Form is a versatile form designed to collect information for any type of request or submission. This form helps gather personal details, supporting documents, and specific request information in a structured manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the second field.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the third field.
4. Upload a **Supporting Document**, if required.
5. If applicable, enter your **Comments** in the free-text field.
6. Select the **Request Type** that best describes your submission from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter the **Request Date** in the date field.
8. If necessary, enter the **Request Time** in the time field.
9. Select any applicable **Category** from the dropdown menu.
10. Enter any additional **Comments 2** in the free-text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your personal name.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Supporting Document** (`text`, required): Upload a supporting document related to your submission.
* **Comments** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your submission.
* **Request Type** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of request you are submitting, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Request Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of your request.
* **Request Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of your request, if applicable.
* **Category** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select any categories that apply to your submission.
* **Comments 2** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your submission.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submission.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided help guide for clarification.
- If you're submitting sensitive or personal health-related information, please use the appropriate form designed for that purpose.
