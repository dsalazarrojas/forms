# Assessment Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assessment Upload Form is designed to collect and record assessment data for employees or trainees. This form should be completed once per assessment, ensuring accurate and efficient data management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Trainer or Evaluator Name**: This is the name of the person who conducted the assessment.
2. Enter the **Trainee or Employee Name**: This is the name of the person being assessed.
3. Enter the **Employee ID Number**: This is the unique identifier for the employee or trainee.
4. Enter the **Assessment Title or Code**: This is a unique identifier for the assessment.
5. Select the **Date of Evaluation**: This is the date the assessment was conducted.
6. Enter a brief **Brief Summary of Performance Results**: This is a key takeaway from the assessment.
7. Rate the **Work Ethics and Attitude Rating**: This is a score from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
8. Rate the **Overall Performance Rating**: This is a score from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
9. (Optional) Enter **Recommended Further Training or Development**: This is a suggestion for future development opportunities.
10. Confirm that the data is accurate with **Data Entry Confirmation**: Choose from the options to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainer or Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the person who conducted the assessment.
* **Trainee or Employee Name**: Enter the name of the person being assessed.
* **Employee ID Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the employee or trainee.
* **Assessment Title or Code**: Enter a unique identifier for the assessment.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Select the date the assessment was conducted.
* **Brief Summary of Performance Results**: Enter a key takeaway from the assessment.
* **Work Ethics and Attitude Rating**: Rate from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) for the employee's work ethics.
* **Overall Performance Rating**: Rate from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) for the employee's overall performance.
* **Recommended Further Training or Development**: (Optional) Suggest future development opportunities.
* **Data Entry Confirmation**: Confirm that the data accurately reflects the assessment results.

## Tips
* Ensure that the uploaded files are clearly named with the employee ID.
* Double-check the accuracy of the data entered in this form to maintain data integrity.
* Only complete this form once per assessment to prevent duplication.
* For ratings, please use the scoring system provided: 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
