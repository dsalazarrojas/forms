# Engineering Diploma Image Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting engineering diplomas for administrative or official purposes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the submit button to start.
2. Fill out the main page with the required information, including your name, address, and other contact details if applicable.
3. Upload a clear image of your diploma by selecting the "Diploma Image" field.
4. If required, select "Yes" for "Supporting Documents" to upload additional supporting documents.
5. Fill out "Document 1", "Document 2", "Document 3", and "Document 4" fields with any relevant information.
6. Select the correct date and time of issue and completion from the calendars for "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
8. Optionally, leave a note in the "Note" field if necessary.
9. Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.
