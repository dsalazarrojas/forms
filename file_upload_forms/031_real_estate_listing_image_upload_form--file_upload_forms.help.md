# real_estate_listing_image_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for real estate professionals and property owners to upload images for their listings. The form collects information about the property details and multiple image uploads with captions. This form is meant to simplify the image upload process, allowing users to easily showcase their properties in a more comprehensive and visually appealing format.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form link to access the form page.
2. Scroll through each page, filling out the required fields for each page.
3. On the "real_estate_images" page, select the images you want to upload from the available options.
4. Enter a caption for each image in the "real_estate_image_caption" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_details** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the property, including its address, location, or any other relevant details.
* **real_estate_images** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose up to 3 images to upload from the available options. Selecting multiple images will allow you to upload different images for different parts of the property or listing.
* **real_estate_image_caption** (Text, Optional): Add a caption for each image, describing the image's purpose or context within the property listing.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, as they are essential for the image upload process.
* Use clear and descriptive captions for each image, as they will be displayed alongside the images in the property listing.
* If you have more than three images to upload, you can always create a new listing or use our contact form to request assistance.
