# Fireplace Specification Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fireplace Specification Submission form is designed to gather detailed information from users to facilitate efficient fireplaces installation, maintenance, and management. This form helps our team to process and review the submission data accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all the required information ready before starting the submission process.
2. Fill in the submission details (page 1).
3. Review and confirm that you meet the submission requirements (page 2).
4. Prepare the quotes and attachments needed for the submission (page 3).
5. Manage your data and attachments (page 4).
6. Select the submission status: "submit" or "save for later" (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **submission_details** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your submission.
* **review_requirements** (Note, Optional): Review the submission requirements to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
* **prepare_quotes** (Text, Optional): Prepare any additional quotes or attachments required for the submission.
* **manage_data** (Text, Optional): Manage your data and attachments relevant to the submission.
* **submit** (Select One, Required): Select "submit" to complete the submission process or "save for later" to save the form for completion at a later time.
