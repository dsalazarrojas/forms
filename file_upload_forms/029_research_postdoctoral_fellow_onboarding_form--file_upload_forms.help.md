# researchPostdoctoralFellowOnboardingForm - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Research Postdoctoral Fellow Onboarding Form is designed to gather essential information about research postdoctoral fellows onboarding into a specific program. This form is used to ensure accurate and efficient onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the appropriate job type from the Job Type field.
2. Enter your research area of interest in the Research Area of Interest field (if applicable).
3. Upload required documents (e.g., ID, proof of employment, etc.) in the Upload Documents field.
4. Enter your employee information in the Employee Information field.
5. Enter your contact information in the Email and Phone fields.
6. Enter your supervisor's name and other relevant details in the Supervisor Name field.
7. Enter the research funding source and location in the Research Funding Source and Research Location fields (if applicable).
8. Specify the duration and status of your research project in the Research Duration and Research Project Status fields (if applicable).
9. Enter the date of your last contact and last update in the Date of Last Contact and Date of Last Update fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **File Uploads** (`fileuploads`, text, optional): This field is for uploading any required documents related to your onboarding.
* **Employee Information** (`employeeinformation`, text, optional): Please provide your name and other relevant employee details.
* **Job Title** (`jobtitle`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Lab** (`lab`, text, required): Enter your lab or research location.
* **Research Area** (`researcharea`, text, required): Enter your research area.
* **Upload Documents** (`uploaddocuments`, text, required): Upload any required documents.
* **Submit** (`submitbutton`, text, optional): This field is for submitting the form.
* **Date of Birth** (`dateofbirth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Type** (`jobtype`, select_one, required): Select your job type (e.g., Research Scientist, Postdoctoral Research Scientist, Research Fellow).
* **Research Fellowship Awarding Organization** (`researchfellowshipawardingorganization`, text, required): Enter the organization funding your research fellowship.
* **Research Area of Interest** (`researchareaofinterest`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your research area of interest.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisorname`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Research Funding Source** (`researchfundingsource`, text, optional): Enter the source of your research funding.
* **Research Location** (`researchlocation`, text, required): Enter the location of your research.
* **Research Duration** (`researchduration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of your research project.
* **Lab Location** (`lablocation`, text, optional): Enter the location of your lab.
* **Research Project Name** (`researchprojectname`, text, required): Enter the name of your research project.
* **Research Project Description** (`researchprojectdescription`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your research project.
* **Research Project Status** (`researchprojectstatus`, select_one, required): Select the status of your research project (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Date of Last Contact** (`dateoflastcontact`, date, required): Enter the date of your last contact.
* **Date of Last Update** (`dateoflastupdate`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last update.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately. If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or relevant authorities for clarification.
