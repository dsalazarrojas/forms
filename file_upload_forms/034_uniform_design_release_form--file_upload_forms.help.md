# Uniform Design Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Uniform Design Release Form is a form that collects metadata and information for the submission of uniform designs. It is intended to be filled out by authorized individuals who have been granted permission to upload design files. The form will help the design review team track and manage the submitted designs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are authorized to upload design files. Select 'True' from the first field "Are you authorized to upload design files?"
2. Enter the necessary information for the design files, including description, date, and assigned tool.
3. If additional files are required, upload them in the "Upload additional files" field.
4. Sign the form with your signature.
5. Add any additional notes or details if necessary.
6. Enter the Form ID and Category for the design.
7. If applicable, enter the assigned tool for the design and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you authorized to upload design files?** (permissions): This field checks whether you have been granted permission to upload design files.
* **Upload design files** (upload_design_files): Upload the uniform design files.
* **Upload additional files** (upload_additional_files): If there are additional files associated with the design, upload them here.
* **Description** (description): Enter a brief description of the design.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the submission (optional).
* **Signature** (signature): Sign the form with your name, as a proof of authentication.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (phone): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned tool** (assigned_tool): Select the assigned tool used for the design.
* **Form ID** (form_id): Enter the ID for the form, as assigned by the system.
* **Category** (category): Select the category of the design.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (assigned_tool_2): (Optional) Enter the assigned tool for the design 2.
* **Notes 2** (notes_2): (Optional) Add any additional notes or details 2.
* **Form ID 2** (form_id_2): (Optional) Enter the ID for the form 2.
* **Description 2** (description_2): (Optional) Enter a brief description of the design 2.
* **Category 2** (category_2): (Optional) Select the category 2 of the design.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required fields accurately.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
* Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting the form.
