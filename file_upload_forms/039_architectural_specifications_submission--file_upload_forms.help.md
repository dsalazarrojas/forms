# Architectural Specifications Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect architectural specifications from submitters, including submitter information, project details, and specification document upload. It ensures that all required information is accurately and thoroughly documented for review and approval purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the submitters' information:
	* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the specifications.
	* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the specifications.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the specifications.
	* **Organization or Firm**: Enter the name of the organization or firm submitting the specifications.
2. Provide project information:
	* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when the specifications were submitted.
	* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project.
	* **Project Number or Code**: Enter the reference identifier for the project.
	* **Project Address**: Enter the address of the project location.
3. Specify project phase:
	* **Specification Phase**: Select the relevant phase of the specification from the given options: Schematic, Design development, Construction documents, Bid specifications, As-built, or Other.
4. Add specification details:
	* **Specification Document Title**: Enter the title of the specification document.
	* **CSI Section Number**: Enter the Construction Specifications Institute section number.
	* **Scope and Description**: Describe the scope and coverage of the specifications.
	* **Key Performance Requirements**: List the key performance requirements.
5. Upload relevant documents:
	* **Specification Document**: Upload a PDF or Word file containing the specifications.
	* **Related Drawings or Plans**: Provide any supporting drawings or plans related to the specifications.
	* **Supporting Materials or References**: Add any supporting materials, standards, or references related to the specifications.
6. Add any revision notes:
	* **Revision Notes or Changes**: Provide any notes on changes or revisions made to the specifications.

Note: Ensure that you fill in all required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible, and do not hesitate to reach out if you need any further clarification or assistance.
