# Nonprofit Volunteer Resume Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Nonprofit Volunteer Resume Upload form. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Upload Resume" field and select the relevant file from your computer or device.
2. In the "Name" field, enter your name as it appears on your resume or ID.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. In the "Volunteer Role" field, select the specific role you have experience in or are interested in applying for.
5. In the "Skills" field, select all the skills that apply to you.
6. If you have any relevant experience, enter it in the "Relevant Experience" field.
7. If you want to upload other relevant files, click on the "Upload Other Files" field and select the files from your computer or device.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, required): Upload your resume as a PDF or other supported file type. Make sure it is relevant to the volunteer role you are applying for.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your resume or ID.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Volunteer Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select the specific role you have experience in or are interested in applying for. If you are unsure, select "Other" and describe your experience in the "Relevant Experience" field.
- **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all the skills that apply to you. This will help the organization assess your suitability for different volunteer positions.
- **Relevant Experience** (`experience`, text, not required): If you have experience that is not reflected in your resume, enter it here.
- **Uploaded Resume** (`uploaded_resume`, text, not required): View a copy of the uploaded resume.
- **Uploaded Other Files** (`uploaded_other_files`, text, not required): View a copy of the uploaded other files.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload a relevant resume that matches the volunteer role you are applying for.
* Enter all the skills that apply to you to increase your visibility for suitable volunteer positions.
* Be honest and clear when entering your experience and skills.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the organization's support team for guidance.
