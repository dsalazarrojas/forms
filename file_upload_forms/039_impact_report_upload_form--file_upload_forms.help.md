# Impact Report Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Impact Report Upload Form is designed to gather information about incidents that occurred on site, allowing for tracking and analysis of potential issues. This form is essential for reporting incidents that may have caused harm to people, equipment, or the environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title with a brief description of the report.
2. Attach any supporting documents related to the incident in the "Supporting Documents" field.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number (if applicable).
4. Select the date and time of the incident.
5. If necessary, add any notes about the incident in the "Notes" field.
6. Click "Submit" to finalize your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Details** (`report_details`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of the incident.
* **Supporting Documents** (`report_files`, `text`, required: true): Attach any files related to the incident.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): The date the incident occurred.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): The time the incident occurred.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Any additional notes about the incident.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: true): Click to submit your report after filling out all required fields.
