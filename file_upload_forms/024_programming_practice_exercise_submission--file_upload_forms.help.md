### # Programming Practice Exercise Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit your programming practice exercises for feedback and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and clear details about your exercise.
3. Submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exercise ID** (`exercise_id`, `number`, **Required: no**): This field is used to identify the exercise you're submitting.
* **Exercise Name** (`exercise_name`, `text`, **Required: no**): Enter a brief description of your exercise.
* **Exercise Link** (`exercise_link`, `text`, **Required: no**): Provide a link to your exercise submission (e.g., GitHub or Pastebin).
* **Exercise Description** (`exercise_description`, `text`, **Required: no**): Briefly describe your exercise in your own words.
* **Exercise File** (`exercise_file`, `text`, **Required: no**): If applicable, attach a file related to your exercise (e.g., code, screenshot, or diagram).
* **Mentor Feedback** (`mentor_feedback`, `text`, **Required: no**): Share your mentor's feedback or comments about your exercise submission.
