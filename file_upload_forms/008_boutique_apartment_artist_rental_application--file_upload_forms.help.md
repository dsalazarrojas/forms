# Boutique Apartment Artist Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boutique Apartment Artist Rental Application form is designed to collect necessary information from artists interested in renting a space for a short-term or long-term stay. This form helps the property management team assess the artist's needs and preferences for the rental space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. Provide your preferred contact email in the "Contact email" field.
3. If applicable, share your portfolio URL in the "Portfolio URL" field.
4. Specify your preferred residency dates in the "Proposed residency dates" field.
5. Indicate your primary art medium in the "Primary art medium" field.
6. Describe your space requirements in the "Space requirements" field.
7. Upload your portfolio files or images using the "File uploads" field.
8. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (required): Enter your full name as the artist applicant.
* **Contact email** (required): Provide your preferred email address for contact and communication.
* **Portfolio URL** (optional): Share a link to your online portfolio or samples of your work.
* **Proposed residency dates** (required): Specify your preferred start and end dates for the residency.
* **Primary art medium** (optional): Indicate your primary art medium, e.g., painting, sculpture, installation, photography, etc.
* **Space requirements** (optional): Describe your space requirements, including size and storage needs.
* **File uploads** (optional): Attach your portfolio files or images using the upload tool.
* **Additional notes** (optional): Add any other relevant details for the application.
