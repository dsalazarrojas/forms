# stop_box_measurement_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stop_box_measurement_submission form is used for measuring and submitting details about a "stop box" or item. This form is intended for internal use within your organization and is used to collect information from submitters about the item's dimensions, type, and condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
4. Select the type of box or item being measured in the "Box Type" field.
5. Enter the length, width, and height of the box or item in the "Box Length", "Box Width", and "Box Height" fields, respectively.
6. Select whether the box has a measurement system in the "Measurement System" field.
7. Provide any additional comments about the box or item in the "Submitter Comments" field.
8. (Optional) Upload photos of the box or item in the "Photos" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Name** (required): Enter your name as it should appear on your submission.
* **Submitter Email** (required): Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Submitter Phone** (required): Enter your phone number for quick contact or additional information.
* **Box Type** (required, select): Select the type of box or item being measured or submitted.
* **Measurement System** (required, select): Select whether the box has a measurement system.
* **Box Length** (required): Enter the length of the box or item measured in inches or centimeters.
* **Box Width** (required): Enter the width of the box or item measured in inches or centimeters.
* **Box Height** (required): Enter the height of the box or item measured in inches or centimeters.
* **Box Depth** (optional): Enter the depth of the box or item measured in inches or centimeters if applicable.
* **Submitter Comments** (optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the box or item.
* **Photos** (optional): Upload photos of the box or item for reference and inspection.
