# historical_photo_archive_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect historical photo submissions from individuals, including metadata such as the submission date, location, and description. This form is designed to accommodate uploads of multiple historical photos and allow contributors to specify the license and copyright holder information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **User Input**: Enter your name and email address in the "User Input" field. This information will be used to contact you about your submission.
2.  **File Upload**: Upload your historical photo by clicking on the "File Upload" button. You can upload multiple files if you select the "Multiple Uploads" option.
3.  **Submission Details**: Complete the required fields for "Submission Date", "Submission Time", "Submission Location", and "Submission Type". These fields will help us understand the context of your historical photo.
4.  **Contributor Information**: Enter your name and email address in the "Contributor Name" and "Contributor Email" fields. This information will be used to credit you as the contributor of the historical photo.
5.  **Submission License and Copyright**: Select the license under which you would like your historical photo to be released (Public Domain, Creative Commons, or Other) and specify the copyright holder.
6.  **Additional Information**: Enter any additional notes or historical context about your submission in the "Historical Context" field.
7.  **Review and Submit**: Review all fields to ensure they are complete and accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (text, not required): Enter your name and email address to contact you about your submission.
- **File Upload** (text, not required): Upload your historical photo or document to our archive.
- **Submission Date** (date, not required): Enter the date of the historical event or event related to your photo.
- **Submission Time** (time, not required): Enter the time of the historical event or event related to your photo.
- **Submission Location** (text, not required): Enter the location of the historical event or event related to your photo.
- **Submission Type** (select_one, not required): Select the type of submission (Historical Photo, Document, or Other).
- **Multiple Uploads** (select_multiple, not required): Select to upload multiple historical photos or documents.
- **Contributor Name** (text, not required): Enter your name as the contributor of the historical photo.
- **Contributor Email** (email, not required): Enter your email address as the contributor of the historical photo.
- **Submission Description** (text, not required): Enter a brief description of your historical photo.
- **Submission License** (select_one, not required): Select the license under which you would like your historical photo to be released (Public Domain, Creative Commons, or Other).
- **Upload License** (select_one, not required): Select the license under which you would like your file to be uploaded (Public Domain, Creative Commons, or Other).
- **Copyright Holder** (text, not required): Enter the name of the copyright holder of your historical photo.
- **Historical Context** (text, not required): Enter any additional historical context or notes about your submission.
- **Notes** (note, not required): Enter any additional notes about your submission.
- **Additional Notes** (text, not required): Enter any additional information about your submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* You can upload multiple historical photos or documents if you select the "Multiple Uploads" option.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
