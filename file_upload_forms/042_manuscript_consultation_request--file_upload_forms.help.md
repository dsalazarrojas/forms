# Manuscript Consultation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manuscript Consultation Request form is designed for users to request manuscript consultation services. Its purpose is to gather information about the user's manuscript, preferences for the consultation, and any additional context that might be relevant for the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the **User** field. This is where the consultation team will contact you to discuss your manuscript.
2. Upload your manuscript file in the **File** field. Please make sure to upload a valid file type (e.g., .docx, .pdf, .txt, etc.).
3. Provide a brief description of your project in the **Project Details** field. This will help the consultation team understand your manuscript better.
4. Select your preferred consultation time slot from the **Session Preferences** field. You can choose from 08:00 AM, 09:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, or 01:00 PM.
5. If you have any additional comments or information about your manuscript, please add them in the **Additional Comments** field.
6. Select the type of consultation you require from the **Consultation Type** field. You can choose from Developmental Consultation, Line Editing and Proofreading, Content Editing, Ghostwriting and Research, or Coaching and Guidance.
7. Optionally, you can schedule a specific date and time for the consultation in the **Consultation Date** and **Consultation Time** fields.
8. Finally, provide any next steps or instructions for the consultation team in the **Next Steps** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User**: Enter your email address where the consultation team can contact you.
* **File**: Upload your manuscript file to be reviewed.
* **Project Details**: Briefly describe your project to help the consultation team understand your manuscript.
* **Session Preferences**: Select your preferred consultation time slot.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information about your manuscript.
* **Consultation Type**: Choose the type of consultation you require.
* **Consultation Date**: Optionally, schedule a specific date for the consultation.
* **Consultation Time**: Optionally, schedule a specific time for the consultation.
* **Next Steps**: Provide any next steps or instructions for the consultation team.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload a valid file type (e.g., .docx, .pdf, .txt, etc.).
* Be specific when selecting your consultation time slot to ensure the consultation team can accommodate your request.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the **Project Details** field to help the consultation team understand your manuscript.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please add them in the **Additional Comments** field.
