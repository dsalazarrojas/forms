# Assignment And Lesson Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an assignment or lesson submission from students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student name, ID, and email fields.
2. Select the correct course and assignment title.
3. Choose the correct submission type.
4. Upload the main assignment file.
5. If applicable, upload additional supporting files.
6. Fill out the submission date and due date.
7. Confirm that you are submitting original work.
8. Confirm that citations are included.
9. If submitting a group project, provide the group members' names.
10. Sign your name to confirm submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, required): This is your full name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, required): This is your student ID number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): This is your email address for contact purposes.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, required): This is the name of the course you are submitting for.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, required): This is the code for the course you are submitting.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, required): This is the name of your instructor.
* **Assignment Title** (`assignment_title`, required): This is the title of the assignment or lesson you are submitting.
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Assignment Only
	+ Lesson Materials
	+ Both Assignment and Lessons
	+ Project
	+ Lab Report
	+ Essay
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, required): This is the date you are submitting the assignment or lesson.
* **Assignment Due Date** (`due_date`, required): This is the date the assignment or lesson was originally due.
* **Upload Main Assignment File** (`file_upload_1`, required): Upload your primary submission file.
* **Upload Additional File** (`file_upload_2` and `file_upload_3`, optional): You can upload additional supporting files if necessary.
* **File Format** (`file_format`, required): Choose the format of your submission file:
	+ PDF
	+ Word Document
	+ PowerPoint
	+ Excel Spreadsheet
	+ Image
	+ Video
	+ Code File
	+ Other
* **Word Count** (`word_count`, optional): If your submission is a word document, please provide the word count.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, optional): Add any comments or notes for your instructor.
* **Is This a Late Submission?** (`late_submission`, required): Choose one of the following:
	+ No - On Time
	+ Yes - With Permission
	+ Yes - Without Permission
* **Reason for Late Submission** (`late_reason`, optional): If you chose "Yes - With Permission" or "Yes - Without Permission", explain the reason for your late submission.
* **I Confirm This Is My Original Work** (`academic_integrity`, required): Choose one of the following:
	+ Yes I Confirm
	+ No
* **Are Citations Included?** (`citations_included`, required): Choose one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Not Applicable
* **Is This a Group Project?** (`group_project`, required): Choose one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Group Members Names** (`group_members`, optional): If you chose "Yes" for the group project, list the names of all group members.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, required): Sign your name to confirm submission.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your instructor for clarification.
* Please review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure it is complete and accurate.
* If you need help with formatting or uploading files, please refer to your instructor's instructions.
