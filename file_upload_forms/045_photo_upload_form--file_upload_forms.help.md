# Photo Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows users to upload a photo, provide a description, and optionally specify an email address. Users must have an account with a valid email address to use this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Upload Photo" button to begin.
2. Select and upload a photo from your computer or device.
3. Enter a description of the photo.
4. If you have an account, enter your email address.
5. Click on "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Photo** (`user_upload`, text, optional): Upload a photo from your computer or device.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the photo.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an account, enter your email address.
* **Email Hint** (`email_hint`, text, optional): Not required, but can be used for reference.
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, number, optional): Not used in this form.
* **Uploaded Photo** (`uploaded_photo`, note, optional): A preview of the uploaded photo.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Not used in this form.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): This field is not relevant to uploading a photo.
