# property_onboarding_analysis_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property onboarding analysis form is designed to collect information about a property in order to onboard it into the system. This form is used by property owners to provide detailed information about their property, including its address, type, size, year built, owner's contact information, property status, and other relevant details. The form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and accurate, reducing the risk of errors or missed data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the property address.
2. Select the property type.
3. Enter the property size.
4. Enter the year the property was built.
5. Enter the owner's contact information, including phone and email.
6. Check the property status.
7. Add any additional property notes if necessary.
8. Upload any supporting property documents or attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address**: Enter the exact address of the property, including house number and street name. This will help the system locate the property in our database.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property from the dropdown list. This will help us to categorize the property correctly.
* **Property Size**: Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Property Year Built**: Enter the year the property was built.
* **Property Owner**: Enter the owner's name and contact information.
* **Property Condition**: Check the boxes for any existing conditions or defects of the property.
* **Property Location**: Enter a brief description of the property's location.
* **Neighbourhood**: Enter the neighbourhood where the property is located.
* **Property Status**: Select the current status of the property.
* **Property Note**: Add any additional notes about the property.
* **Property Owner Phone**: Enter the property owner's phone number.
* **Property Owner Email**: Enter the property owner's email address.
* **Property Owner Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information for the property owner.
* **Property Insurance Info**: Enter any relevant insurance information about the property.
* **Property Documents**: Upload any supporting documents for the property.
* **Property Attachments**: Upload any attachments related to the property.
* **Property Pictures**: Upload any pictures of the property.
* **Property Videos**: Upload any videos related to the property.
* **Property Audio**: Upload any audio files related to the property.
* **Property Notes**: Add any additional notes about the property.
* **Property Tags**: Enter any relevant tags for the property.
* **Property Description**: Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Property Features**: Enter a list of any unique features of the property.
* **Property Amenities**: Enter a list of any amenities offered by the property.
* **Neighbourhood Map**: Upload a map showing the property's location in relation to nearby amenities and attractions.

Please note that some fields are optional and only required if necessary.
