# Skin Lesion Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Lesion Assessment Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect and record information about the characteristics of a skin lesion. This form helps identify key features of the lesion and aid in proper diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the patient's medical history and any relevant information about the skin lesion.
2. Answer the questions about the size, color, type, location, and duration of the skin lesion.
3. Upload relevant photos of the skin lesion (if applicable).
4. Submit and close the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's name, age, or any other relevant identifying information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any previous skin conditions or allergies.
* **Skin Lesion Information** (`skin_lesion_info`): Answer the questions about the size, color, type, location, and duration of the skin lesion.
	+ **Size** (`skin_lesion_info_0`): Measure the size of the skin lesion in centimeters or inches.
	+ **Color** (`skin_lesion_info_1`): Describe the color of the skin lesion (e.g., red, blue, green, etc.).
	+ **Type** (`skin_lesion_info_2`): Choose the type of skin lesion from the provided options (e.g., benign, malignant, etc.).
	+ **Location** (`skin_lesion_info_3`): Specify the location of the skin lesion on the body (e.g., face, arm, leg, etc.).
	+ **Duration** (`skin_lesion_info_4`): Estimate the duration of the skin lesion in days or weeks.
	+ **Description** (`skin_lesion_info_5`): Provide a detailed description of the skin lesion.
* **Photos** (`photos`): Upload up to 4 photos of the skin lesion from different angles.
* **Submit and Close** (`submit_and_close`): Click "Submit" to save the form and "Close" to exit the form.

Note: The fields with "_2" in their names (e.g., `skin_lesion_size_2`) are duplicates of the same type of field as in `skin_lesion_info`. These fields allow for additional data entry for multiple skin lesions.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the skin lesion.
* Upload clear and well-lit photos of the skin lesion from different angles.
* Review and edit the form as needed before submitting.
