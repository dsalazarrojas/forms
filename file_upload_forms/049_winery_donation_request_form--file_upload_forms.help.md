# Winery Donatin Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winery Donatin Request Form is a tool used to gather information from wineries to process their donation requests. This form is intended to help our internal teams review and understand the winery's donation needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") in the form.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Winery Donatin Request Form** (`winery_donatin_request_form`, text, optional): This field is an optional title for your donation request. Please enter a brief title to help us identify your request.
* **Winery Address** (`winery_address`, text, optional): This field is where you will enter the address of the winery making the donation request. Please ensure to enter the correct address to help us process your request accurately.
* **Winery Contact** (`winery_contact`, text, optional): This field is where you will enter the contact information for the winery making the donation request. Please ensure to enter the correct contact information to help us communicate with the winery.
* **Donation Request Details** (`donation_request_details`, text, optional): This field is where you will provide a description of the donation you are requesting. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, select multiple, optional): This field is where you will select whether the event you are requesting a donation for is taking place on a specific date. Please select 'Yes' if the event is taking place and 'No' otherwise.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): This field is where you will upload any supporting documents related to your donation request. Please ensure to upload documents that are relevant to your request.
* **Followup Status** (`followup_status`, select one, optional): This field is where you will select the status of the follow-up on your donation request. Please select 'Supporting', 'Not Supporting', 'Not Reviewed', or 'Reviewing'.
* **Followup Comments** (`followup_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you will provide any comments related to the follow-up on your donation request. Please ensure to provide clear and concise comments.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you will provide comments on the review of your donation request. Please ensure to provide clear and concise comments.
* **Event Inquiries** (`event_inquiries`, text, optional): This field is where you will provide any inquiries related to the event taking place. Please ensure to provide clear and concise comments.
 
 Note: Please do not submit duplicate requests, as they will not be processed. If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact our support team.
