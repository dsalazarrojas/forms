# Career Services Resume Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Career Services Resume Submission form is designed to help you, as a student, effectively communicate your career goals and needs to our career services team. This form will guide you through a series of questions that will help us tailor our support to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Your Full Name" field. This is the name that will be associated with your career services support.
2. If you have a student ID, please enter it in the "Student ID" field. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is how we will contact you for follow-up questions or feedback.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, including the area code.
5. Specify your major or field of study in the "Major or Field of Study" field.
6. Enter your expected graduation date in the "Expected Graduation Date" field.
7. Select the level of your degree from the dropdown menu in the "Degree Level" field.
8. Enter your current GPA in the "GPA" field.
9. Upload your resume in the "Upload Your Resume" field. Please use PDF, Word, or plain text format.
10. If you have a cover letter, you can optionally upload it in the "Upload Cover Letter" field.
11. Provide a link to your LinkedIn profile in the "LinkedIn Profile URL" field, if applicable.
12. If you have a portfolio or personal website, provide the URL in the "Portfolio or Personal Website" field.
13. Enter your years of professional experience, including internships, in the "Years of Professional Experience" field.
14. Select all the career services you are interested in from the list in the "What Career Service Are You Requesting?" field.
15. In the "Specific Feedback or Areas You Want Help With" field, specify any additional feedback or areas you would like us to improve on.
16. Choose your current job search status from the dropdown menu in the "Current Job Search Status" field.
17. Select the types of jobs or industries you are interested in from the list in the "Target Job Positions or Industries" field.
18. Specify your geographic preferences for employment in the "Geographic Preferences for Employment" field.
19. Enter your desired salary range in the "Desired Annual Salary Range" field.
20. Select your preferred employment type from the list in the "Preferred Employment Type" field.
21. Choose the desired start date for your new position in the "Desired Start Date" field.
22. List your key skills or technical competencies in the "Key Skills or Technical Competencies" field.
23. Specify any professional certifications or licenses you hold in the "Professional Certifications or Licenses" field.
24. Indicate if you need visa sponsorship in the "Visa Sponsorship Required?" field.
25. Select your preferred method of follow-up contact in the "Preferred Follow-up Method" field.
26. Choose the level of priority for your request from the dropdown menu in the "Priority Level for Review" field.
27. Enter any additional information or notes you would like us to know in the "Additional Information or Notes" field.
