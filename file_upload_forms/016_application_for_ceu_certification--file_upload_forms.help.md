# Application For CEU Certification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for Continuing Education Units (CEU) certification for a course or workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and complete information.
2. Upload the supporting document (if required).
3. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as per your official government-issued ID.
- **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you would like to receive your certificate.
- **Course title** (`course_title`, `text`, required): Enter the exact title of the course or workshop you completed.
- **Date completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed the course.
- **CEU hours claimed** (`ceu_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the decimal number of CEU hours you claim for this course.
- **Supporting document filename** (`supporting_document`, `text`, optional): If applicable, indicate the filename of the uploaded supporting document.
- **Issuing institution** (`institution`, `text`, optional): Enter the organization that delivered the course.
- **Verification preference** (`verification_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would like to receive verification (Email, Phone, or Manual review).
- **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
- Upload a supporting document if applicable.
- Review your information carefully before submission.
- If you have any doubts or issues, contact your organization's administration.
