# Submit Your Music - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to submit your music for review or consideration. Please use this guide to learn how to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, ensure you have all the information required to complete the form.
2. Fill out each field with the relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Music** (`upload_music`, text, optional): Upload your music file here. You can upload any type of audio file that you have created. This field is not required, but if you don't upload a file, we won't be able to review your music.
* **Music Title** (`music_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your music. This is the name you want to give to your music, such as a song title or an album name.
* **Artist** (`artist`, text, optional): Enter the name of the artist who created the music. This is the name of the person or group that created the song or album.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are submitting your music for review. This should be the date you are submitting your music for consideration, not the date it was created.

## Tips
* Make sure you have all necessary permissions to share your music.
* Ensure your music meets any submission requirements specified by the reviewing party.
* Double-check your submission before uploading and submitting it.
