# Environmental Fieldwork Documentation Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Environmental Fieldwork Documentation Release Form is a tool for fieldwork teams to release their documentation to the public. This form ensures that all necessary information is properly shared and stored securely. Fill out this form to release your environmental fieldwork documentation for public use.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the Environmental Fieldwork Documentation Release Form.
2. Click on the "Release" page to start filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Environmental Fieldwork Documentation Release

- **Environmental Fieldwork Documentation**: This field is used to upload your environmental fieldwork documentation. Please enter a brief description of your project, highlighting its purpose, goals, and any notable findings.

### Release

- **Consent**: Check the box to indicate that you have received permission from your team and relevant stakeholders to release your documentation. Choose "Yes" to confirm that you have obtained consent.

### Project Details

- **Project Name**: Enter the name of your project, such as "Ecosystem Health Study" or "Wildlife Habitat Restoration."

- **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team responsible for the project, including all team members and their roles.

### Additional Information

No other fields are available, so you've reached the end of the form!

## Tips

- Make sure to upload the correct documentation for your project.
- Verify that you have the necessary permissions and consents before releasing your documentation.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it, as any missing information may delay or prevent the release of your documentation.
