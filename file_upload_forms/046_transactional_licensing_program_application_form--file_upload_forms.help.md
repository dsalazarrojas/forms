# Transactional Licensing Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information necessary for the evaluation and approval of your application for the Transactional Licensing Program. Please provide detailed and accurate information to help facilitate a smooth review process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all required fields (marked as "Required") are filled out.
3. Attach relevant files if you need to provide supporting evidence.
4. Review and submit your completed form to the Transactional Licensing Program administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 2. **Name (Name)** (Text, Required)

*   This field is for you to provide your name as it appears on official documents or records.

### 3. **Email (Email, Required)**

*   This field is for you to provide a valid email address where you can be contacted for further instructions or clarifications.

### 5. **License Type (Select One, Required)**

*   Please choose one of the three options:
    *   Full License
    *   Limited License
    *   No License

### 7. **Date of Interest (Date, Required)**

*   Provide the date that this interest relates to, if not immediately applicable, provide the date when it will be relevant.

### 8. **Time of Interest (Time, Required)**

*   Enter the time of interest or relevant time frame for this Transactional Licensing Program.

### 9. **Description (Text, Required)**

*   Please provide a detailed description of your interest or the transaction you are applying for.

### 11. **Agreement (Text, Required)**

*   Please provide your agreement to the terms and conditions of the Transactional Licensing Program.

Note: Please attach any relevant supporting files to the "File 1", "File 2", and "File 3" fields as instructed.
