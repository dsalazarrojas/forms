# Credit Report Hard Inquiry Dispute Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation
## Tips

### 1. **Credit Report Hard Inquiry Dispute Request**
* **Credit Report Hard Inquiry Dispute Request**: This is the title of the form, which should not be changed.

### 2. **Document 1**
* **Document 1**: Upload any supporting documents related to the dispute. This is a required field.

### 3. **Document 2**
* **Document 2**: Upload any additional supporting documents related to the dispute. This is a required field.

### 4. **Document 3**
* **Document 3**: Upload any other supporting documents related to the dispute. This is a required field.

### 5. **Additional Documents**
* **Additional Documents**: Provide any other supporting documents related to the dispute. This field is optional.

### 6. **Customer Authorization**
* **Customer Authorization**: Provide your authorization for the dispute. This is a required field.

### 7. **Customer Signature**
* **Customer Signature**: Sign and date the document as proof of your identity. This is a required field.

### 8. **Dispute Details**
* **Dispute Details**: Describe the dispute and provide a clear explanation of the issue. This is a required field.

### 9. **Dispute Description**
* **Dispute Description**: Provide a detailed description of the dispute. This is a required field.

### 10. **Dispute Amount**
* **Dispute Amount**: Enter the amount related to the dispute. This is a required field.

### 11. **Dispute Type**
* **Dispute Type**: Choose the type of dispute (Credit Account, Credit Card, or Other). This is a required field.

### 12. **Dispute Start Date**
* **Dispute Start Date**: Enter the start date of the dispute. This is a required field.

### 13. **Dispute End Date**
* **Dispute End Date**: Enter the end date of the dispute. This is a required field.

### 14. **Dispute Status**
* **Dispute Status**: Choose the status of the dispute (Open, Closed, or Pending). This is a required field.

### 15. **Dispute Resolution**
* **Dispute Resolution**: Choose the resolution of the dispute (Accepted, Denied, or Disputed). This is a required field.

### 16. **Resolution Reason**
* **Resolution Reason**: Provide a reason for the resolution (if applicable). This field is optional.

### 17. **Customer Name**
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer. This is a required field.

### 18. **Customer Email**
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address. This is a required field.

### 19. **Customer Phone**
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number. This is a required field.

### 20. **Customer Address**
* **Customer Address**: Provide your address (optional). This field is optional.

### 21. **Credit Bureau**
* **Credit Bureau**: Choose the credit bureau related to the dispute (Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion). This is a required field.

### 22. **Credit Score**
* **Credit Score**: Enter your current credit score. This is a required field.

### 23. **Credit Limit**
* **Credit Limit**: Enter your credit limit. This is a required field.

### 24. **Credit Utilization**
* **Credit Utilization**: Enter your credit utilization ratio. This is a required field.

### 25. **Submit**
* **Submit**: Click to submit the form to initiate the dispute resolution process. This field is optional, but necessary to complete the submission.
