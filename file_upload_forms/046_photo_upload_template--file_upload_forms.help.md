# photo_upload_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect metadata associated with uploaded photographs. It is suitable for any use case where photo upload is involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form fields as follows:
* Upload a photo by using the upload button.
* Provide the date the photo was taken.
* Identify the photographer who took the photo.
* Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* Add a description of the photo.
* Enter the title of the photo.
* Choose the license under which the photo is to be shared.
* Enter the type of license if you have selected a restricted license.
* If applicable, provide the copyright details.
* Upload the photo URL if it's not already uploaded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Photo**: Upload a photograph.
* **Date Taken** (date): The date the photo was taken.
* **Photographer**: The person who took the photo.
* **Location** (text): The location where the photo was taken.
* **Description** (text): A description of the photo.
* **Photo Title** (text): A title for the photo.
* **Photo License** (select one): Choose from "Private", "Public", or "Restricted".
* **Photo License Type** (text): Enter the type of license if you have selected "Restricted".
* **Copyright** (text): If the photo has copyright, enter the details.
* **Photo URL** (text): Enter the URL where the photo can be found if it's not already uploaded.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to upload the photo.
* Choose the correct license to ensure that your photo is shared as intended.
* Enter accurate date, location, and copyright details to help identify the photo correctly.
* If you have any issues with uploading, try resizing or reformatting the photo.
