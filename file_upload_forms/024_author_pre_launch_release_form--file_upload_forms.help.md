# Author Pre Launch Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to manage the pre-launch review process for your work. It gathers essential information about your submission, book, and reviewer permissions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the required fields filled in and the rest of the form is filled in accurately.
2. Fill in your submission date and title.
3. Provide your full name, email, and phone number.
4. Select the type and genre of your work.
5. Enter the planned launch date and pre-launch period.
6. Upload your manuscript file.
7. Specify the file format of your work.
8. Choose how many advance copies you want to share.
9. Select the reviewers you want to share with and whether they need to sign a non-disclosure agreement.
10. Set the embargo date for reviews, if any.
11. Choose which rights to grant to reviewers.
12. Decide on the permission to use in marketing and social media sharing.
13. Specify whether reviewers can publish excerpts from your work.
14. Choose who to contact with any questions or concerns.
15. Provide an email address for review inquiries.
16. Add any additional instructions or notes for reviewers.
17. Agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Author Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Author Email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Author Phone**: Enter your contact phone number. (This is optional.)
* **Work Title**: Enter the title of your book or work.
* **Type of Work**: Select the type of your work (Novel, Non-Fiction, etc.).
* **Genre**: Enter the primary genre of your work.
* **Target Audience**: Specify who your work is intended for.
* **Planned Launch Date**: Enter the date your work is planned to be launched.
* **Pre-Launch Period**: Select how long before the official launch you want reviewers to have access.
* **Manuscript File Upload**: Upload your manuscript file.
* **File Format**: Select the format of your manuscript file (PDF, EPUB, etc.).
* **Number of Review Copies Allowed**: Specify how many advance copies you want to share.
* **Reviewer Restrictions**: Select the individuals or groups you want to share your work with.
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement Required**: Decide if reviewers need to sign a non-disclosure agreement.
* **Embargo Date for Reviews**: Set a date for when reviews can be published.
* **Rights Being Granted**: Select the rights you want to grant to reviewers (Print Edition Review, eBook Review, etc.).
* **Permission to Use in Marketing**: Decide if reviews can be used for marketing purposes.
* **Social Media Sharing Allowed**: Decide if reviewers can share on social media.
* **Excerpt Publishing Permission**: Decide if reviewers can publish excerpts from your work.
* **Who to Contact with Questions**: Enter the contact person or group for review inquiries.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address for review inquiries.
* **Additional Instructions or Notes**: Add any special requirements or notes for reviewers.
* **I Agree to These Terms**: Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
