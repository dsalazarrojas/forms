# soil_release_polymer_product_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about soil release polymer product inquiries. It is designed to collect relevant details from customers or internal stakeholders to better understand their needs and requirements for specific polymer products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the product inquiry questions to provide details about the product you are inquiring about.
2. Select your contact details to provide your name, email, and phone number.
3. If necessary, provide technical details about your project or requirements.
4. Upload any relevant files related to your inquiry.
5. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Inquiry
* **Product Inquiry** (`product_inquiry`, `text`, optional): This field allows you to enter any details or questions you have about the product. Provide a brief description of the product you are inquiring about.
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select the relevant details for your customer, whether it's "Yes" or "No". This helps us understand your role in the project.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the relevant details for your contact information, whether it's "Yes" or "No". This helps us understand how you'd like to be contacted.
### Technical Needs
* **Technical Needs** (`technical_needs`, `select_one`, optional): If you have any specific technical requirements or needs, select "Yes" for this field.
### Product Specifications
* **Product Specifications** (`product_specifications`, `select_one`, optional): If you have any specific product specifications or requirements, select "Yes" for this field.
### Upload File
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, `text`, optional): Please upload any relevant files related to your inquiry, such as images, documents, or specifications.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to provide about your inquiry.
### Product Inquiry 2
* **Product Inquiry 2** (`product_inquiry_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional product inquiries or questions you have.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to contact you about your inquiry.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your inquiry.
