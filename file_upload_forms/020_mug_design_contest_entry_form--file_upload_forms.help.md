# Mug Design Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mug Design Contest Entry Form is a submission form for users to enter their mug design ideas. It helps us gather feedback and inspiration for our product design team to improve and create new products.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Submit Your Mug Design**: Enter the title of your design idea in the "Submit Your Mug Design" field.
2. **Enter Your Name**: Provide your name in the "Name" field.
3. **Enter Your Email**: Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that we will not share your email with anyone.
4. **Enter Your Phone Number (Optional)**: If you have a phone number you'd like to share, enter it in the "Phone" field. This is optional.
5. **Upload Your Design File**: Upload a file that represents your design idea.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Your Mug Design**: Enter a brief title of your design idea in this field. This is a required field.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the mug. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your submission. We promise not to share it with anyone. This is a required field.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to provide your phone number, you can enter it here. This is an optional field.
* **Design File**: Upload a file that represents your design idea. You can upload a variety of file types, including .jpg, .png, or .pdf. This is a required field.
