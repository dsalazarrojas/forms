# Client Campaign Content Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Campaign Content Upload Form is designed to collect all necessary information and assets for an upcoming campaign launch. It helps the campaign team to review and confirm the details accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your campaign's name, target launch date, and all relevant details.
- Select the campaign channels you wish to use.
- Provide a detailed description of the assets included in the campaign.
- Specify whether the campaign copy/text is final and approved or requires agency input.
- Decide if custom tracking links are required.
- Choose your primary success metric (KPI).
- Confirm whether assets have been uploaded to the client portal or provide a cloud link.
- Specify who authorized the submission.
- Select the date of submission.
- Verify that all required fields are filled out before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Campaign Content Submission --** (`campaign_intro`): This is a placeholder for you to provide any additional comments or information about your campaign submission.
- **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`): Enter your campaign's title.
- **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the name of your client.
- **Target Launch Date** (`launch_date`): Enter the date when your campaign is expected to launch.
- **-- Campaign Channels --** (`channels_header`): Select all intended channels for your campaign.
- **Campaign Channels** (`targeted_channels`): Choose the channels you selected in the previous field.
- **-- Asset Details --** (`asset_details_header`): Describe the assets included in your campaign submission.
- **Description of Assets Provided** (`asset_inventory_list`): Provide a detailed list of assets included in your campaign.
- **Is campaign copy/text included in the upload?** (`copy_status`): Specify if the campaign copy/text is final and approved or needs agency input.
- **Do you require custom tracking (UTM) links created?** (`tracking_links_needed`): Decide if you need custom tracking links.
- **Primary Success Metric (KPI)** (`primary_kpi`): Choose your primary success metric for the campaign.
- **-- Secure Submission --** (`upload_method_header`): Select whether you uploaded assets to the client portal or if a cloud link is provided.
- **Have you uploaded assets to the client portal?** (`asset_portal_upload_confirm`): Confirm the upload method used.
- **Backup Cloud Link (if applicable)** (`cloud_link_backup`): If you used a cloud link, enter the link here.
- **Authorized By (Name)** (`submission_authorized_by`): Specify who authorized the submission.
- **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields accurately to avoid errors and duplication of efforts.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary details are included.
- If you have any questions, please reach out to your account manager for assistance.
