# 3D Material Surface Data Input Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 3D Material Surface Data Input Form is designed to collect essential data for 3D material surface measurement and evaluation. This form helps to gather information about the operator, the measurement process, and the 3D scan file link.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Full name**, **Email address**, and **Order date** in the respective fields. These are required fields.
2. Provide the **Item or product name** you are measuring, and enter the **Quantity** of items.
3. Enter the **Unit price** per item in your local currency.
4. Select your preferred **Delivery method** from the options provided.
5. If you are not picking up or delivering locally, fill in your **Delivery address**.
6. If known, enter the **Requested delivery date**.
7. Enter the **Measurement date** and **Measurement time** when the surface measurement was performed.
8. Choose the **Measurement method** used for the scan (e.g., Laser Scanning, Structured Light, etc.).
9. Select the **Calibration status** of the measurement device.
10. Enter the **Surface roughness Ra** and **Rz** values measured in micrometers (µm).
11. Upload the link to your **3D scan file** stored in a cloud storage.
12. Finally, rate the **Surface quality** from 1 (worst) to 10 (best) and provide any **Quality notes** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid corporate or personal email address.
* **Order date**: Enter the date when the measurement order was placed.
* **Item or product name**: Enter the official name of the product or component being measured.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of items being measured.
* **Unit price**: Enter the price per item in your local currency.
* **Preferred delivery method**: Choose the most convenient delivery option (Standard shipping, Express shipping, Pickup, or Local delivery).
* **Delivery address**: If not picking up or delivering locally, enter the address to deliver the items.
* **Requested delivery date**: Enter the date when delivery is expected, if known.
* **Measurement date**: Enter the date when the surface measurement was performed.
* **Measurement time**: Enter the time of day when the measurement was taken.
* **Measurement method**: Select the technique used for the scan (e.g., Laser Scanning, Structured Light, etc.).
* **Calibration status**: Select the status of the measurement device calibration (Calibrated, Not Calibrated, or Pending).
* **Surface roughness Ra**: Enter the surface roughness measured in micrometers (µm).
* **Rz value**: Enter the surface roughness measured in micrometers (µm).
* **3D Scan File Link**: Upload the link to the 3D scan file stored in a cloud storage.
* **Surface quality rating**: Rate the surface quality from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Quality notes**: Enter any additional observations or concerns about the surface quality.
