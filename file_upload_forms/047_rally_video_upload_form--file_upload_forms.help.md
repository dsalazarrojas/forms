# Rally Video Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rally Video Upload Form is a tool for uploading videos related to rallies. It helps gather essential information about the videos, such as the video content, duration, and associated event details. This form is crucial for ensuring that the uploaded videos are correctly attributed to the relevant events and rallies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Upload your video file.
2.  Provide the duration of the video in hours and minutes.
3.  Enter the event ID associated with the video.
4.  Enter the name of the event where the video was recorded.
5.  Enter the rally ID associated with the video.
6.  Enter the name of the rally where the video was recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Rally Video Upload** (`file_uploads_1`, text, required: false):
    *   This field is where you can browse or drag-and-drop a video file from your computer for upload. Please ensure you have selected the correct file before submitting.
*   **Video** (`rally_video_upload_form_file_2`, text, required: false):
    *   Enter a descriptive title for the video, ideally the name of the video or a brief description of its content.
*   **Duration** (`video_duration`, number, required: false):
    *   Provide the duration of the video in hours and minutes. For example, if the video is 1 hour and 30 minutes long, enter "01:30" in this field.
*   **Event ID** (`event_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the unique ID associated with the event where the video was recorded.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false):
    *   Enter the name of the event where the video was recorded. This helps in identifying the event to which the video belongs.
*   **Rally ID** (`rally_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the unique ID associated with the rally where the video was recorded.
*   **Rally Name** (`rally_name`, text, required: false):
    *   Enter the name of the rally where the video was recorded. This helps in correctly attributing the video to the relevant rally.
