## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Upload any supporting documents required for the facade renovation, as indicated on the form.
2.  Enter the property owner's name and contact information, including email and phone number.
3.  Enter the property manager's name and contact information.
4.  Enter the contractor's name and contact information.
5.  Select the declaration date and upload any additional supporting documents.
6.  Enter the approval status and select the relevant approval reason.
7.  Enter any remarks or additional comments.
8.  Upload any other supporting documents.
9.  Enter the approval number, declaration type, and declaration ID.
10. Enter any additional comments or remarks.
