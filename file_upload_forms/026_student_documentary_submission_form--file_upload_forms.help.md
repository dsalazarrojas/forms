# Student Documentary Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to submit their documentary entries, including essays, research papers, projects, and presentations. It captures information about the submission date, file upload, student name, and contact details of the student and their educator.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the submission date by selecting the date when you submitted your documentary entry.
2. Upload your file by selecting the file you wish to submit. This can be a document, image, or other file type.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or other identifying document.
4. Provide a link to your documentary entry if it is publicly available.
5. Enter your school name as it appears on your school ID or other identifying document.
6. Select the category of the competition your documentary entry is for (e.g., Computer Science, Math, History, etc.).
7. Provide details about your assignment, including any relevant information about the submission.
8. Select the type of submission you are making (e.g., Essay, Research Paper, Project, Presentation).
9. Enter your email address and your educator's email address for contact purposes.
10. Enter your educator's name and phone number for contact purposes.
11. Enter your school's email address and phone number for contact purposes.
12. Provide a note about your submission, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Submit Date
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: false)
	+ This field captures the date when you submitted your documentary entry.

### File
* **File** (`file`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for uploading a file related to your submission.

### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering your name as it appears on your student ID or other identifying document.

### Documentary Link
* **Documentary Link** (`documentary_link`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering a publicly available link to your documentary entry.

### School Name
