# Water Photography Submission Form - Help Guide
 
## Purpose
The Water Photography Submission Form is used to submit water photography content for review and consideration for publication. The form captures essential details about the photo, including its description, upload, and metadata, as well as information about the photographer and their preferences.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Form Title**: Enter a brief title for your water photography submission.
2. **Photo Description**: Provide a descriptive text about your photo.
3. **Upload Photo**: Upload your water photography image.
4. **Photographer Details**: Enter your name and preferred contact information (email, phone number).
5. **Submission Details**: Enter the date and time you are submitting the photo.
6. **Additional Settings**: Choose whether your photo belongs to a specific **Category** and select any **Tags** that apply.
7. **Gallery Information**: Enter the name of the gallery where your photo will be featured.
8. **Submission and Review Status**: Choose the status of your submission (Active or Inactive) and the status of the review (Active or Inactive).
9. **Review Comments**: Add any comments for the reviewer.
10. **Review Details**: Enter the name and contact information (email, phone number) of the reviewer.
11. **Metadata**: Enter the ID of the category or gallery associated with your photo.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
 
* **Form Title** (Required): Enter a brief title for your submission to be displayed in the review and publication process.
 
* **Photo Description** (Optional): Write a descriptive text about your photo.
 
* **Upload Photo** (Optional): This is where you will upload your water photography image.
 
* **Photographer** (Optional): Enter your name and preferred contact information (email, phone number).
 
* **E-mail** (Optional): Enter your email address.
 
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number.
 
* **Submission Date** (Optional): Enter the date you are submitting your photo.
 
* **Submission Time** (Optional): Enter the time you are submitting your photo.
 
* **Photo Credit** (Optional): Enter the name of the credit for your photo.
 
* **Category** (Optional): Choose a category for your photo to help with the review and publication process.
 
* **Tags** (Optional): Select any tags that apply to your photo.
 
* **Gallery Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the gallery where your photo will be featured.
 
* **Photo URL** (Optional): Enter the URL of your photo.
 
* **Submission Status** (Optional): Choose the status of your submission (Active or Inactive).
 
* **Review Status** (Optional): Choose the status of the review (Active or Inactive).
 
* **Review Comments** (Optional): Add any comments for the reviewer.
 
* **Reviewer** (Optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
 
* **Reviewer E-mail** (Optional): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
 
* **Reviewer Phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
 
* **Category ID** (Optional): Enter the ID of the category associated with your photo.
 
* **Gallery ID** (Optional): Enter the ID of the gallery where your photo will be featured.
 
* **Photo Credit ID** (Optional): Enter the ID of the credit for your photo.
 
Note: This form is designed to capture all necessary information for submitting your water photography content. Please fill out each section accurately and completely for a smooth and efficient review and publication process.
