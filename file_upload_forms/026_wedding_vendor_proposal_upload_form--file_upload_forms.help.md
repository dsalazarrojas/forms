# Wedding Vendor Proposal Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Vendor Proposal Upload Form is designed to help wedding vendors create a proposal for potential clients. This form will collect information about the vendor's contact details, event details, and uploaded files.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Upload Proposal" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your name and contact details in the "Contact" field.
3. Select the type of vendor you are (e.g., Photographer, Caterer, Florist).
4. Enter the details about your event (e.g., date, time, venue).
5. Upload your proposal file and any other relevant documents.
6. Enter your contact person and their details.
7. Enter your email and phone number.
8. Add any additional message or comments about your proposal.
9. Review your proposal and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Proposal**: Upload your proposal file (.pdf, .docx, .jpg, etc.) to submit your proposal to the potential client.
* **Contact**: Enter your name, title (if applicable), and company or organization name. 
* **Vendor Type**: Select the type of vendor you are (e.g., Photographer, Caterer, Florist).
* **Contact**: Select whether you have a contact person or not.
* **Vendor Type**: Select whether you have the type of vendor you are (e.g., Photographer, Caterer, Florist).
* **Venue**: Enter the venue or location of your event.
* **Date of Event**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time of Event**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Details**: Enter any additional details about your event.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name, title, and contact information.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Message**: Add any additional comments or message about your proposal.
* **Upload File**: Upload any additional files related to your proposal (e.g., images, videos). 

Note: The "Vendor Type" field is a dropdown menu where you can select the type of vendor you are. Please select one option from the list. 

Please make sure to complete all required fields to ensure that your proposal is complete and accurate.
