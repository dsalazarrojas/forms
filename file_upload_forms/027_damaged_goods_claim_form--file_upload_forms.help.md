# damaged-goods-claim-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to submit a claim for a damaged or defective product. Please complete it thoroughly to ensure that your issue is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "customer-info" page.
2. Provide detailed information about the product in question, including its type, size, and serial number, in the "product-info" page.
3. Describe the damage or defect you are claiming, including when you received the product and what happened.
4. Click "Submit" to send your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer-info** (`customer_info`, text, required):
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID or government-issued ID.
	+ Provide your email address and phone number for us to contact you.
* **product-info** (`product_info`, text, required):
	+ Describe the product you are claiming to be damaged or defective, including its type, size, and serial number.
	+ If applicable, attach any supporting images or documents that show the issue.
* **claim-info** (`claim_info`, text, required):
	+ Describe the damage or defect you are experiencing, including when you first received the product and any steps you have taken to attempt to resolve the issue.
	+ Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your concern.

## Tips

- Please be as clear and concise as possible when providing information.
- Attach any relevant images or documents to support your claim.
- If you have already contacted us regarding this issue, please do not re-enter the same information.
- We will review your submission and contact you if we need more information or to confirm receipt of your claim.
