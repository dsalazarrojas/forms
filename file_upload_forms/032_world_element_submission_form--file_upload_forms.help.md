# World Element Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about a new world element they would like to submit. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details about the element, including its idea, description, and any relevant attachments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **World Building Idea** field with a brief description of your proposal for a new world element.
2. Enter a **Description** of the element in the field below.
3. If you have an accompanying file, such as a document or image, select **File Attachment** and upload it.
4. Enter your **Element Submitter** email address to help us contact you about your submission.
5. Provide your **Contact Info** for us to get in touch with you about your submission.
6. Add any **Additional Info** about your element in the field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **World Building Idea** (Submit Form: 1)
* This field is for a short description of your world element proposal. Please keep it brief but descriptive.

### **Description** (Submit Form: 2)
* This field is for a more detailed description of the element you are proposing. Please try to keep it concise and within the provided limit.

### **File Attachment** (Submit Form: 4)
* If you have a file that would help us better understand your proposal, such as an image or document, this field allows you to attach it.

### **Element Submitter** (Submit Form: 5)
* Enter your email address so we can contact you about your submission. We'll use this to get in touch with you to follow up on your element proposal.

### **Contact Info** (Submit Form: 6)
* Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you if we need more information about your element proposal.

### **Additional Info** (Submit Form: 7)
* This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about your element proposal that didn't fit in the previous fields.
