# Album Concept Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps artists and bands submit their album concepts to labels, managers, or other industry professionals. It gathers information about the artist's identity, project details, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, fill out the form with the following sections:

1. **Artist and Project Identity**
	* Enter your artist name or band name in the field labeled "Artist or Band Name".
	* Provide the name of the contact person (manager/lead artist) in the field labeled "Contact Person (Manager/Lead Artist)".
	* Enter your contact email address in the field labeled "Contact Email".
2. **Project Details**
	* Enter a working title for your project in the field labeled "Project Working Title".
	* Choose the primary genre of your music in the field labeled "Primary Genre" (e.g., Pop, Rock, Hip Hop/Rap, etc.).
	* Select the type of release you're aiming for (e.g., Full Length Album, EP, Single Project, Mixtape/Concept Piece).
3. **Concept and Marketing**
	* Describe the theme, mood, and story behind your project in the field labeled "Concept Summary".
	* Explain who your target audience is in the field labeled "Target Audience Description".
4. **Assets and Timeline**
	* Provide a link to demo recordings related to your project in the field labeled "Link to Demo Recordings" (ensure permissions are open).
	* Choose the current recording status of your project in the field labeled "Current Recording Status" (e.g., Concept Only, Early Demos, etc.).
5. **Additional Information**
	* Provide any additional comments or information that you think is relevant to your project in the field labeled "Additional Information for Label/Management".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name**: Enter your artist name or band name as it appears on your official website, social media, or promotional materials.
* **Contact Person (Manager/Lead Artist)**: Provide the name and title of the contact person who will handle communication related to your project.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address that will be used for communication and updates.
* **Project Working Title**: Enter a working title for your project. This can be a temporary or working title, but should be a clear representation of your project.
* **Primary Genre**: Choose the genre that best describes your music style (e.g., Pop, Rock, Hip Hop/Rap, etc.).
* **Type of Release**: Select the type of release you're aiming for (e.g., Full Length Album, EP, Single Project, Mixtape/Concept Piece).
* **Concept Summary**: Briefly describe the theme, mood, and story behind your project. Be as detailed or concise as needed.
* **Target Audience Description**: Explain who you're creating music for. Describe the age range, demographics, or preferences of your target audience.
* **Link to Demo Recordings**: Provide a public link to demo recordings related to your project. Ensure permissions are open for listening.
* **Current Recording Status**: Choose the current status of your project recording progress (e.g., Concept Only, Early Demos, etc.).
* **Desired Release Date**: Enter the desired release date for your project, if known.
* **Budget Expectations**: Indicate your expected budget for marketing and distribution (optional).
* **Additional Information for Label/Management**: Provide any additional comments or information that you think is relevant to your project.

By following this guide, artists and bands can ensure they submit their album concepts in a clear and concise manner, increasing the chances of getting noticed by labels, managers, or other industry professionals.
