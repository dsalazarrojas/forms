# Plant Variety Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Plant Variety Release Form is a document used to request the release of a plant variety. This form is meant to collect information from the release requester, who is the person or organization requesting the release of the plant variety. This information will be used to process and review the release request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the status of your request: Do you wish to release this plant variety?
2. Provide the name of the plant variety you are releasing.
3. Enter the date of the release authorization (if applicable).
4. Enter the name of the person or organization making the release request.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your organization or company name.
8. Enter your street address.
9. Enter your city.
10. Enter your state/province.
11. Enter your zip/ postal code.
12. Enter your country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **release_request** (`release_request`, select_one, required: false): This field allows you to indicate whether you wish to release the plant variety.
* **variety_name** (`variety_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the plant variety you are releasing.
* **release_authorization_date** (`release_authorization_date`, date, required: false): If you have already received release authorization, enter the date of the authorization. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **release_requester_name** (`release_requester_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name or the name of the organization making the release request.
* **release_requester_phone** (`release_requester_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the organization making the release request.
* **release_requester_email** (`release_requester_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address or the email address of the organization making the release request.
* **release_requester_organization** (`release_requester_organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **release_requester_address** (`release_requester_address`, text, required: false): Enter your street address.
* **release_requester_city** (`release_requester_city`, text, required: false): Enter your city.
* **release_requester_state** (`release_requester_state`, text, required: false): Enter your state or province.
* **release_requester_zip** (`release_requester_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **release_requester_country** (`release_requester_country`, text, required: false): Enter your country.
