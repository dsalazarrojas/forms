# digital_art_submission_use_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for artists to provide their details and consent for the use of their digital artwork.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Artist Details field with your name and other relevant information.
2. Select the correct usage rights for your artwork (e.g. public domain, limited rights).
3. Write a statement explaining your artwork (optional).
4. Provide details about your artwork (optional).
5. Check the box to agree or disagree with the usage terms.
6. Enter your contact details (optional).
7. Upload your digital artwork (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Details** (`artist_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and other relevant information about yourself.
* **Usage Rights** (`usage_rights`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the usage rights for your artwork, which can be All Rights Reserved, Limited Rights, or Public Domain.
* **Artist Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: false): This field is for you to add your signature, but it's not required.
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, `text`, required: false): Write a statement about your artwork, if you'd like.
* **Artwork Details** (`artwork_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide more information about your artwork, if needed.
* **Usage Consent** (`usage_consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Check the box to agree or disagree with the usage terms.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, if you'd like.
* **Upload Artwork** (`upload_artwork`, `text`, required: false): Upload your digital artwork, if applicable.
