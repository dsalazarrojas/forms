# professional_portfolio_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your professional portfolio, which includes your first name, last name, contact number, email address, a description of your work, and a file upload for any additional materials you'd like to share. Please review this guide carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. (Note: This field should be in the format "username@example.com")
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your work and experiences in the "Description" field.
6. Upload any files you'd like to share as part of your portfolio in the "Upload File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address in the format "username@example.com".
- **Contact Number** (Text, Not Required): Enter your contact number if you'd like to be reached directly.
- **Upload File** (Text, Not Required): Upload any files you'd like to share as part of your portfolio, such as a resume or a link to a personal website.
- **Description** (Text, Not Required): Provide a brief description of your work and experiences.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your email address is accurate and formatted correctly.
- If you're uploading a file, please ensure it's in a supported format (e.g., PDF, JPEG, etc.).
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
