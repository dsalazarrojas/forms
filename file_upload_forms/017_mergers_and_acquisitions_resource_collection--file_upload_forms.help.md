# mergers_and_acquisitions_resource_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for mergers and acquisitions resource collection from companies and its contributors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company details in the "Company Details" section.
2. Upload the required documents in the "Upload Documents" section.
3. Provide details about the contributors in the "Contributor Details" section.
4. Add any additional information in the "Additional Info" section.
5. Confirm the submission by typing it in the "Confirm" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Details (Company Details, text, required: false)
Enter the basic information about the company involved in the merger or acquisition process. This may include the company name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Upload Documents (Upload Documents, text, required: false)
Upload any necessary documents related to the merger or acquisition process. This could be contracts, reports, or any other relevant documents.

### Contributor Details (Contributor Details, text, required: false)
Provide details about the contributors involved in the merger or acquisition process. This may include their names, roles, and contact information.

### Additional Info (Additional Info, text, required: false)
Add any additional information that is not covered in the previous sections. This could be any comments, notes, or other relevant details about the merger or acquisition process.

### Confirm (Confirm, text, required: false)
Type in the word "CONFIRM" to confirm the submission of the merger or acquisition information. This is a required field to ensure that the submission is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* Be thorough in providing details in each section to ensure accuracy.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors or duplication of work.
