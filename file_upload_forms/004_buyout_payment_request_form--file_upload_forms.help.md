# Buyout Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for a buyout payment when an employee is leaving the company or when a contract is terminated. Fill out this form to ensure a smooth and accurate buyout process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the requestor information**:
	* Please enter your full name and employee ID number as it appears on your badge or in your employee record.
2. **Select your department**:
	* Choose the department that your role is associated with (e.g. Finance, Operations, etc.).
3. **Enter contract or project reference number**:
	* Enter the contract or project reference number associated with your buyout.
4. **Choose the type of buyout**:
	* Select the type of buyout (e.g. Contract Early Termination, Vacation Buyback, etc.).
5. **Enter the requested amount**:
	* Enter the gross amount before taxes for the requested buyout.
6. **Choose the desired payout date**:
	* Select a date that is subject to your company's payroll cycles.
7. **Verify documentation submission**:
	* Confirm if all required supporting documents have been uploaded.
8. **Enter approving manager's name**:
	* Enter the name of your approving manager.
9. **Enter any additional notes**:
	* If you need to provide any additional information for the finance team, use this section.
10. **Submit and agree**:
	* Review and agree that the provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Requestor**: This field is for your name as it appears on your badge or in your employee record.
* **Employee ID Number**: Enter the number associated with your employee ID.
* **Department**: Please select the department that your role is associated with.
* **Contract or Project Reference Number**: Enter the contract or project reference number for your buyout.
* **Type of Buyout**: Select the type of buyout from the provided options (e.g. Contract Early Termination, Vacation Buyback, etc.).
* **Total Requested Buyout Amount**: Enter the gross amount before taxes for the requested buyout.
* **Desired Payout Date**: Select a date that is subject to your company's payroll cycles.
* **Have all supporting documents been uploaded?**: Confirm if all required documents have been submitted with your request.
* **Documentation Upload Notice**: This is a reminder to ensure that your file upload includes the signed agreement and calculation worksheet.
* **Approving Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager who approved this request.
* **Additional Notes for Finance Team**: If you need to provide any additional information for the finance team, use this field.
* **I certify that the information provided is accurate**: Confirm that the information you provided is accurate and complete.
