# Architecture Site Survey Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from surveyors about architectural site surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (Surveyor Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Survey Date, Site Address, General Site Conditions, and Survey Type).
2. Provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to the survey in the General Site Conditions field.
3. Choose the type of survey that best describes your survey from the drop-down list.
4. Upload any relevant documents or images in the Supporting Documents, Site Photos or Images, Survey Measurements or Data, Utility Maps, and Additional Documentation fields.
5. Submit the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surveyor Name** (`surveyor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the surveyor conducting this survey.
* **Email Address** (`surveyor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`surveyor_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the survey was conducted.
* **Site Address** (`site_address`, text, required): Enter the full address or property name.
* **Parcel Number or ID** (`parcel_number`, text, optional): Enter the parcel number or ID if available.
* **Site Area (Square Feet)** (`site_area_sf`, number, optional): Enter the total lot size in square feet.
* **Type of Survey** (`survey_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of survey that best describes your survey:
	+ Boundary survey
	+ Topographic survey
	+ Condition assessment
	+ Site analysis
	+ Hazard assessment
	+ Other
* **General Site Conditions** (`general_conditions`, text, required): Describe the general site conditions in detail.
* **Existing Structures or Features** (`existing_structures`, text, optional): List any existing structures or features on the site.
* **Site Constraints or Issues** (`site_constraints`, text, optional): List any site constraints or issues that were found during the survey.
* **Surveyor Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for consideration.
* **Supporting Documents** (`survey_plan`, text, required): Upload a PDF, CAD, or image file of the survey plan or drawing.
* **Site Photos or Images** (`site_photos`, text, optional): Upload photos or images of the site conditions.
* **Survey Measurements or Data** (`survey_measurements`, text, optional): Upload a spreadsheet or document file with measurements or data collected during the survey.
* **Utility Maps** (`utility_maps`, text, optional): Upload maps or diagrams of underground utilities or services.
* **Additional Documentation** (`additional_documentation`, text, optional): Upload any additional documentation, such as zoning information or environmental reports.
* **Section Survey Findings** (`findings`, text, optional): Describe any findings or observations made during the survey.
* **Section Survey Findings** (`findings`, text, optional): This field is not visible in the form.

Note: This form is designed to be as clear and concise as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
