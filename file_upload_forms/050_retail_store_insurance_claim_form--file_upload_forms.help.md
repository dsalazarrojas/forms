# Retail Store Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Store Insurance Claim Form is used to report incidents and submit claims to the store's insurance provider. The form is designed to collect information necessary for processing your insurance claim, including incident details, store information, and claimant contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3.  Review your submissions before submitting the form.
4.  Attach any supporting documentation as necessary.
