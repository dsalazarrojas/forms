# Artist Video Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists and performers to submit their videos to the platform for review and consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your artist name and contact information in the "Artist Information" section.
2. Provide your video metadata, including title, description, type, and duration in the "Video Information" section.
3. Choose the type of video you are submitting and confirm that you own the rights to it in the "Rights and Submission" section.
4. Upload your video file or provide a link to it on YouTube or Vimeo in the "Video Upload" section.
5. Confirm that you agree to the submission terms in the "Submission Agreement" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Performer Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required): Enter your professional name as an artist or performer.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Website or Portfolio URL** (`artist_website`, `text`, optional): Enter a link to your professional website or portfolio.
* **Primary Genre or Style** (`primary_genre`, `text`, required): Choose your primary artistic discipline (e.g., music, dance, visual art, etc.).
* **Artist Biography** (`artist_bio`, `text`, required): Enter a professional bio, 100-150 words in length, describing your background and experience.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_active`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years you have been performing or creating as an artist.
* **Social Media or Streaming Links** (`social_media`, `text`, optional): Enter links to your social media profiles or streaming platforms.
* **Video Title** (`video_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the video you are submitting.
* **Video Description** (`video_description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the content of your video.
* **Type of Video** (`video_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of content your video represents (e.g., music performance, music video, etc.).
* **Video Duration** (`video_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the length of the video in minutes.
* **Upload Your Video File** (`video_file_upload`, `text`, required): Upload your video file in MP4, MOV, AVI, or other video format.
* **Or Provide YouTube Link** (`youtube_link`, `text`, optional): Enter a link to the video on YouTube.
* **Or Provide Vimeo Link** (`vimeo_link`, `text`, optional): Enter a link to the video on Vimeo.
* **Date Video Was Filmed** (`filming_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the video was recorded.
* **Video Quality** (`video_quality`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the resolution and quality of the video (e.g., HD, standard, etc.).
* **I Confirm I Hold Copyright to This Video** (`copyright_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you own the rights to this video content.
* **I Agree to the Video Submission Terms** (`submission_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to the submission guidelines and terms.
