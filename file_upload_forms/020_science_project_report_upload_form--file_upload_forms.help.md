# Science Project Report Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Project Report Upload Form is used to upload reports about science projects. This form collects information about the project category, science fair participation, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your science project.
2. Choose the relevant options for your project's science fair participation.
3. Select the year of the science fair you participated in.
4. Enter your school's name.
5. Enter your teacher's name (if applicable).
6. Enter your student's name.
7. Enter your student's school year.
8. Upload a file related to the project (e.g., a report or presentation).
9. Provide a brief description of the project.
10. Add any relevant tags or keywords for the project.
11. Finally, confirm the project's status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one): Select the category for your science project.
* **Science Fair Participation** (`science_fair_participation`, select_multiple): Choose the options that apply to your project's science fair participation.
* **Science Fair Year** (`science_fair_year`, select_one): Select the year you participated in the science fair.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text): Enter your school's name.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text): Enter your teacher's name if applicable.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text): Enter your student's name.
* **Student School Year** (`student_school_year`, text): Enter your student's school year.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text): Upload a file related to the project, such as a report or presentation.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Project Category** (`project_category`, text): Add any relevant tags or keywords for the project.
* **Project Tags** (`project_tags`, text): Add any relevant tags or keywords for the project.
* **Project Keywords** (`project_keywords`, text): Add any relevant keywords for the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, text): Confirm the project's status.
