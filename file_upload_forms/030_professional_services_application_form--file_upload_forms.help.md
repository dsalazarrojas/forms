# Professional Services Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for professional services applications, including resumes, portfolios, and skills assessments. It is intended to be used by individuals or companies applying for professional services, such as consulting services, coaching, or mentorship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Attach relevant files, such as resumes, portfolios, or certifications, as required.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resume** (`file_1`, `text`, required: false): Please attach a scanned copy of your resume, highlighting your relevant experience and qualifications.
* **Portfolio** (`file_2`, `text`, required: false): Attach a scanned copy of your portfolio, showcasing your work and projects.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `date`, required: false): Please select a date to confirm your availability for professional services.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required: false): Please select a time slot to schedule a potential meeting or call.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the skills that you have or are willing to learn, such as Yes/No options for skills like "Data Analysis", "Project Management", etc.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of professional services you are applying for, such as "Consulting" or "Coaching".
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your contact information, including phone number, email, or address.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options to confirm your application, such as "Yes" for "I confirm that the information provided is accurate".
* **Signature** (`signature`, `date`, required: false): Please attach a scanned copy of your signature, or date and sign this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Attach relevant files, such as resumes and portfolios, as required.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
