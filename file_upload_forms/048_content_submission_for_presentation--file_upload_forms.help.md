# Content Submission For Presentation - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for presenters to submit their content and information for the event. Please fill in the form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in your personal details, including your Presenter Full Name, Email, Contact Number, Job Title, and Organization Name.
2. Provide the required fields for your presentation, such as Title, Presentation Type, Duration, Audience, Learning Objectives, Key Takeaways, and Presentation Format.
3. Upload any necessary files, if requested, and specify the format they are in.
4. Specify any audio-visual equipment or special requirements for your presentation.
5. Add any additional notes or information about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Presenter Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name.
* **Presenter Email (2)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number (3)**: Enter your contact number, if available.
* **Job Title (4)**: Enter your job title.
* **Organization Name (5)**: Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Submission Date (6)**: Enter the date you submit this content.
* **Presentation Title (7)**: Enter the title of your presentation.
* **Presentation Type (8)**: Select the type of presentation you are submitting, such as Keynote Speech or Workshop.
* **Presentation Topic (9)**: Enter a brief description of your presentation.
* **Presentation Abstract (10)**: Enter a brief description of your presentation.
* **Presentation Duration (11)**: Select the duration of your presentation, such as 15 minutes or 60 minutes.
* **Target Audience (12)**: Select who your presentation is for, such as Beginners or Executives.
* **Learning Objectives (13)**: Enter what attendees will learn from your presentation.
* **Key Takeaways (14)**: Enter the main takeaways from your presentation.
* **Presentation Format (15)**: Select how you will present, such as In Person or Virtual Live.
* **Slides Submitted (16)**: Indicate if you have uploaded your slides.
* **File Format (17)**: If you have uploaded files, specify the format.
* **Audio-Visual Requirements (18)**: Select any necessary audio-visual equipment.
* **Special Requirements (19)**: Enter any special requirements for your presentation.
* **Presenter Bio (20)**: Enter a short biography.
* **Headshot Uploaded (21)**: Indicate if you have uploaded your headshot.
* **Social Media Handles (22)**: Enter your social media handles.
* **Previous Presentations (23)**: Enter any relevant presentation experience.
* **Additional Notes (24)**: Enter any other information for the organizers.

By following this guide, you should be able to submit your content accurately and efficiently. If you are still unsure about any field, please review this guide and contact the organizers if you need further clarification.
