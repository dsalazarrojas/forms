# podcast_audio_submission_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help podcasters submit audio files and metadata about their episodes. It is intended to collect information about each episode, including the title, tags, and URL, as well as upload a corresponding audio file.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for your podcast episode.
2. Select relevant tags for your episode.
3. Enter the URL where your episode can be found.
4. Upload your audio file.
5. Describe your file in the text field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Episode Title** (`episode_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your podcast episode.
- **Upload Audio File** (`upload_audio_file`, text, optional): Upload your audio file.
- **File Description** (`file_description`, text, optional): Describe your audio file in this text field.
- **Tags** (`episode_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select relevant tags for your episode from the dropdown list.
- **Episode URL** (`episode_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL where your episode can be found.
- **Audio Transcript** (`audio_transcript`, text, optional): If you have an audio transcript for your episode, enter it here.
- **Upload Host Audio** (`upload_host_audio`, text, optional): This field is not used in the form, so you can ignore it.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete the form and submit your episode.
- **File** (`upload_file`, text, optional): This field is used to upload a file, but it is not clear what type of file it expects. Please use the **Upload Audio File** field instead.
