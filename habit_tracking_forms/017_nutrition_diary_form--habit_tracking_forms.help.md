# Nutrition Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Diary Form is a personal health tracker designed to help users monitor and maintain a balanced lifestyle. By recording daily habits, food intake, and other relevant information, users can gain insights into their health patterns and make informed decisions for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Nutrition Diary Form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form on a daily basis, at the same time each day.
* Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* Be thorough in your answers, leaving no field blank if possible.
* Review and verify your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Intake** (`food_intakes`, `text`, required: false): Record your food intake for the day, including any meals, snacks, or drinks consumed.
* **Daily Habits** (`daily_habits`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether you have engaged in any of the following daily habits:
	+ Yes/No: (e.g., exercise, meditation, screen time)
* **Nutrition Diets** (`nutrition_diets`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the diet you follow, if any:
	+ Vegetarian Diet
	+ Keto Diet
	+ Omnivore Diet
* **Water Intake** (`water_intake`, `number`, required: false): Record the total amount of water consumed during the day (e.g., 8 oz, 16 oz).
* **Sleep Hours** (`sleep_hours`, `number`, required: false): Report the total hours of sleep you got during the night.
* **Exercise** (`exercise`, `text`, required: false): Describe any physical activity or exercise done during the day.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): List any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): Record any medications taken during the day.
* **Supplements** (`supplements`, `text`, required: false): Record any supplements consumed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or observations about your day.

Tips:

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Try to follow the same format and structure each time you fill out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with a healthcare professional or registered dietitian for guidance.
* Review your entries regularly to identify patterns and areas for improvement.
