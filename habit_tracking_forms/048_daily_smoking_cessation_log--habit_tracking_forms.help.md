# Daily Smoking Cessation Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals track and monitor their daily smoking cessation progress. By filling out this form, you will be able to record information about your smoking habits, including the number of cigarettes consumed, your current cravings level, triggers that prompt you to smoke, coping strategies used to manage cravings, your emotional state, and the time of cigarette consumption. This data will help you and your healthcare provider better understand your smoking habits and make informed decisions about your treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant information for each field and submit your answers. If a field is not applicable or not relevant to your situation, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cigarettes Consumed**: Enter the number of cigarettes you have consumed on this day. This will help you monitor your progress in quitting or reducing your smoking habit.
* **Cravings Level**: Select the level of cravings you are experiencing today. Choose from High, Low, or select the level that best describes your current condition.
* **Triggers**: List any situations or triggers that made you want to smoke today. This could be a specific location, person, or activity.
* **Coping Strategies**: Describe any coping strategies you have used to manage your cravings today. This could include deep breathing exercises, talking to a friend, or any other activity that helped you resist the urge to smoke.
* **Mood**: Check the box if you are feeling stressed, anxious, or otherwise affected emotionally today. This will help us understand how your smoking habit affects your mental state.
* **Time Cigarettes Consumed**: Enter the time of day when you last consumed cigarettes. This will help us track your smoking patterns throughout the day.
* **Date**: Enter the date for the data you are logging today. This will help us understand your progress over time.
