# Daily Activity Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Activity Log Form is used for tracking daily activities, events, or tasks completed by a user. It collects data on various aspects of a user's daily activities for monitoring, reporting, or auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your daily activity.
3. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete to avoid any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Activity Log Form**
  This is the title of the form, which will be used as a heading for your activity log entry.

* **Start Date**
  Enter the date your activity started. This is an optional field, but it's crucial for tracking the duration of your activity.

* **End Date**
  Enter the date your activity ended. This is also an optional field, but it helps in determining the duration of your activity.

* **Start Time**
  Enter the time your activity started. This field is optional and is used in conjunction with the "start date" field for more precise tracking.

* **End Time**
  Enter the time your activity ended. This field is optional and is used in conjunction with the "end date" field for more precise tracking.

* **Activity Type**
  Select the type of activity you completed. This field helps filter activities based on the type of work or category they belong to.

* **Activity Name**
  Enter a brief description of your activity. This field is optional but helps in tracking specific activities.

* **Notes**
  Add any additional comments or notes related to your activity. This field is optional and helps in providing more context to your activity.

* **Category**
  Select a category for your activity. This field helps filter activities based on the category they belong to.

* **Sub Category**
  Select subcategories for your activity. This field allows further organization and filtering of activities based on subcategories.

* **Tags**
  Select relevant tags for your activity. This field enables users to filter activities based on tags they assign.

* **User ID**
  This field is not relevant for most users.

* **Created At**
  This field is not relevant for most users.

* **Updated At**
  This field is not relevant for most users.

* **Deleted At**
  This field is not relevant for most users.

* **Deleted User ID**
  This field is not relevant for most users.

* **Assigned Tool**
  Enter any tools or resources assigned to the activity. This field is optional and is used for referencing any tools used during the activity.

Note: Fields with no practical explanation or purpose will be marked with "Not applicable" or "Not relevant" to avoid confusion and ensure clarity.
