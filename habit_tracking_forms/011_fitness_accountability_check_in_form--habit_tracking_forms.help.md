# Fitness Accountability Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track their progress in staying fit and healthy. It's meant to be filled out after each workout session, allowing users to provide details about their current workout plan, the days they've worked out, exercises completed, weight used, time spent exercising, and any additional notes they'd like to record. This form aims to provide accountability and encouragement for users to stick to their fitness goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your <strong>first name</strong> in the first field.
2. Enter your email address in the second field.
3. For each workout session, choose the days you've worked out from the options provided in the third field.
4. Describe your current <strong>workout plan</strong> in the fourth field.
5. Select the number of <strong>sets and reps</strong> you completed from the options in the fifth field.
6. Enter the weight you <strong>used</strong> for your workout, as a whole number.
7. Choose the unit of measurement for the weight you used from the options provided in the seventh field.
8. Enter the <strong>time spent exercising</strong> in the eighth field.
9. Add any <strong>notes</strong> about your workout session in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to help us better understand who's submitting the form.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with your account.
- **Current Workout Plan** (`current_workout_plan`, text, optional): Describe your current workout plan, including any details about the exercises, sets, reps, and weight used.
- **What days did you work out?** (`workout_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the days you've worked out during the current week.
- **Exercises Completed** (`exercises`, text, optional): List the exercises you completed during the current session.
- **Sets Completed** (`sets_rep`, select_multiple, optional): Select the number of sets and reps you completed for each exercise.
- **Weight Used** (`weight_used`, number, optional): Enter the weight you used for the exercises, as a whole number.
- **What unit of measurement used?** (`weight_used_unit`, select_one, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the weight you used (e.g., KILOGRAMS, GRAMS, LBS, POUNDS, KG, OUNCE).
- **Time Spent Exercising** (`time_spent_exercising`, time, optional): Enter the time you spent exercising, in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your workout session.
