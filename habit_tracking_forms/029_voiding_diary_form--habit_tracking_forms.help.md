# Voiding Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voiding Diary Form is a tool to track urinary habits and fluid intake over a 24-hour period. It's designed to collect data on urination times, volumes, and frequency, as well as comments and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form once per day.
2. Enter the date in the "Voiding Diary Date" field.
3. Enter your urination times in the following fields:
	* Morning Urination Time
	* Afternoon Urination Time
	* Evening Urination Time
4. Enter your urination volumes in the following fields:
	* Morning Urination Volume
	* Afternoon Urination Volume
	* Evening Urination Volume
5. Enter your total liquid intake in the following fields:
	* Total Liquid Intake
	* Total Urination Frequency
6. Enter any comments or notes about your urinary habits in the "Voiding Diary Comments" field.
7. Enter your contact information in the following fields:
	* Phone
	* Email
