> # Daily Subconscious Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Subconscious Tracker Form is a tool designed to help users monitor and analyze their daily thoughts, habits, and experiences. It is meant to be filled out daily, providing a comprehensive snapshot of an individual's mental and physical state.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Daily Subconscious Tracker Form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to understand what it represents and how it applies to your daily life.
2. Answer every question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the select fields (e.g., Vivid Dreams, Tags) to categorize your experiences or thoughts.
4. For fields with multiple options (e.g., Tags), select all relevant tags that apply.
5. If you have additional thoughts or experiences that don't fit into the provided categories, use the "Notes" field for further elaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Thoughts** (`daily_thoughts`, text, required): Describe your thoughts, concerns, or worries from the day. Be as detailed as possible, including any notable events or experiences that affected your mental state.
* **Vivid Dreams** (`vivid_dreams`, select_one, required): Choose whether you experienced vivid dreams, or if you had a normal night's sleep, or maybe something in between. Select "Maybe" if you didn't sleep at all or if your sleep quality was exceptional.
* **Anxious Thoughts** (`anxious_thoughts`, note, required): Write down any anxious thoughts or concerns you experienced throughout the day. Try to be specific and objective, focusing on the thoughts rather than emotions.
* **Daily Activities** (`daily_activities`, text, required): Record your daily activities, including any notable events, meetings, or interactions. This will help you track your daily habits and routines.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, number, required): Rate your sleep quality on a scale (e.g., 1-10), considering factors like duration, comfort, and restfulness.
* **Wake Time** (`wake_time`, time, required): If you woke up early or late, record the time you typically woke up and went to bed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional thoughts, insights, or reflections that didn't fit into the other categories.
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant tags that apply to your experiences, such as stress levels, emotions, or activities.
* **Other tags** (`tags_other`, text, required): If none of the provided tags fit your experiences, enter any additional tags that you feel are relevant.
