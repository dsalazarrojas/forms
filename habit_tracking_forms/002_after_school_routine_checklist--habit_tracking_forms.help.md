# After School Routine Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track a student's daily routine activities after school, which includes homework completion, time spent on screen, reading, physical activity, and notes on notable events or behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student name" field, which is a required field.
2. Select the correct grade level of the student.
3. Input the date of the routine entry.
4. Indicate whether homework was completed, and if not, provide the reason.
5. List any snack and meals eaten, if applicable.
6. Enter the total minutes of screen time spent after school.
7. Enter the minutes spent reading or studying.
8. Record any physical activities performed.
9. Add any additional notes or events that are notable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name to ensure accurate identification.
* **Grade level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Choose the student's grade level from the provided options.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Input the date of the routine entry for accurate tracking.
* **Homework completed** (`homework_completed`, select_one, required): Mark whether homework was completed, or select "Partially" if not finished and explain the reason.
* **Snack and meal eaten** (`snack_and_meal`, text, optional): List any food and drinks consumed after school, if applicable.
* **Screen time minutes** (`screen_time_minutes`, number, optional): Enter the total minutes spent on screen after school.
* **Reading time minutes** (`reading_time_minutes`, number, optional): Record the minutes spent reading or studying.
* **Physical activity** (`physical_activity`, text, optional): Describe any physical activity performed after school.
* **Additional notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Use this field for recording notable events or behaviors that require explanation.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling in the "Snack and meal eaten" field.
- If "Homework completed" is "False", provide a reason for not completing homework.
- Use "Partially" for homework that was not fully completed due to some reason.
- Be specific and accurate when recording "Screen time minutes" and "Reading time minutes".
