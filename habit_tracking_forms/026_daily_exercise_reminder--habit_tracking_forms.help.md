# Daily Exercise Reminder - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Daily Exercise Reminder** form is designed to help users track their daily exercise activities. This form is intended to capture relevant details such as date of exercise, type of exercise, workout duration, intensity, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select the date you performed the exercise from the date field.
2. Next, choose the type of exercise you performed from the **exercise_type** field. You can select one or more options from the provided list.
3. Enter the duration of your workout in the **workout_duration** field. This field expects a numerical value.
4. If applicable, enter the intensity of your exercise in the **exercise_intensity** field.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about your workout in the **notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **exercise_date**: This field is where you select the date you performed the exercise. Please choose the correct date from the calendar or enter manually.
* **exercise_type**: Choose from the options: **Option 1, Option 2, Option 3**. This field is optional, but selecting one or more options helps to categorize and track different types of exercises.
* **workout_duration**: Enter the duration of your workout in numerical format (e.g., 30 minutes). This field is optional.
* **exercise_intensity**: This field is a text field where you can describe the intensity of your exercise (e.g., light, moderate, intense).
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your workout in this field.

## Tips
* Ensure you select the correct date for your exercise.
* Be specific when selecting the exercise type for accurate tracking.
* Enter numerical values for workout duration.
* Provide a brief description for exercise intensity when applicable.
* Keep notes brief but informative to help with future reference.
