Sober Progress Tracking Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals track their progress in maintaining sobriety, a state of being free from addiction. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured and convenient way to record and monitor your sobriety milestones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start date of your sobriety journey.
2. Enter the number of days you have been sober.
3. Describe your current mood.
4. Share any challenges you are facing.
5. Set a goal for your sobriety journey.
6. Choose a target date for achieving your sobriety goal.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Sobriety Start Date**
(Sobriety Start Date, date, required: false): The date when you started your sobriety journey.

### 2. **Days Sober**
(Days Sober, number, required: false): Enter the number of days you have been sober.

### 3. **Mood**
(Mood, text, required: false): Briefly describe your current emotional state.

### 4. **Challenge**
(Challenge, text, required: false): If you are facing any challenges, describe them briefly.

### 5. **Goal**
(Goal, text, required: false): Set a goal for your sobriety journey.

### 6. **Sobriety Goal Date**
(Sobriety Goal Date, date, required: false): Choose a target date for achieving your sobriety goal.

### 7. **Notes**
(Notes, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or thoughts related to your sobriety journey.

**Tips**

* This form is a personal progress tracker, so feel free to fill it out regularly.
* Be honest and accurate when recording your progress.
* Review and reflect on your entries regularly to stay on track with your sobriety goal.
