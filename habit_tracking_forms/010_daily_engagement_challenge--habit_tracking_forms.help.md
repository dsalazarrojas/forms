# dailyEngagementChallenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dailyEngagementChallenge form is designed to be a daily or periodic tool to help users track their engagement with specific challenges. It's a straightforward tool that collects information about the type of challenge, the date, and any notes or reflections the user may have about their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the type of challenge you're undertaking from the "challengeType" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date of the challenge or engagement.
3. In the "coachNotes" field, write your thoughts, insights, or reflections about your experience with the challenge.
4. Click "Submit" to save your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Challenge Type**: (Challenge 1/Challenge 2, select_one): Select the type of challenge you're undertaking. This field helps the form track your progress and identify patterns in your challenges.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the challenge or engagement. This helps in tracking your progress over time and identifying trends.
* **Coach Notes** (text): Write your thoughts, insights, or reflections about your experience with the challenge. This field is for personal notes and is not required, but it can be helpful for your own growth and improvement.
* **Introduction** (note): This field is optional and can be used for any personal notes or information you want to add to your entry.
* **Daily Reflection** (text): This field is optional and can be used to add any additional thoughts or insights you want to note about your challenge or engagement.
