# Workout Routine Details Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Workout Routine Details Form" is designed to collect and store information about a specific workout routine. This form is used to document and track various aspects of the workout, including the name, type, duration, date, description, notes, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Workout Name**: Enter a brief and descriptive name for the workout routine. This field is not required.
2. **Routine Type**: Choose a category for the workout routine from the provided options. This field is required.
3. **Duration**: Enter the duration of the workout routine. This field is not required.
4. **Date**: Select the date for the workout routine. This field is required.
5. **Description**: Write a brief description of the workout routine. This field is not required.
6. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the workout routine. This field is not required.
7. **Work Out Location**: Choose the location where the workout was performed from the provided options. This field is required.
8. **Tags**: Select any relevant tags for the workout routine. This field is not required.
9. **Completed**: Indicate whether the workout routine is completed or not. This field is required.
10. **Deleted**: Indicate whether the workout routine is deleted or not. This field is not required.
11. **Submitter**: Enter the email of the user who submitted the form. This field is required.
12. **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form being submitted. This field is not required.
13. **Form Updated At**: Enter the date and time the form was last updated. This field is not required.
14. **Form Updated By**: Enter the name of the user who last updated the form. This field is not required.
15. **Deleted By**: Enter the name of the user who deleted the form. This field is not required.
16. **Form Exported At**: Enter the date and time the form was exported. This field is not required.
17. **Form Exported By**: Enter the name of the user who exported the form. This field is not required.
18. **Created At**: Enter the date and time the form was created. This field is not required.
19. **Created By**: Enter the name of the user who created the form. This field is not required.
20. **Modified At**: Enter the date and time the form was last modified. This field is not required.
21. **Modified By**: Enter the name of the user who last modified the form. This field is not required.
22. **Tags**: Select any relevant tags for the form. This field is not required.
23. **Deleted At**: Enter the date the form was deleted. This field is not required.
24. **Deleted By**: Enter the name of the user who deleted the form. This field is not required.
25. **Deleted Reason**: Enter the reason for deleting the form. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and detailed information to ensure the form can be easily referenced later.
* Use the tags feature to categorize and filter workouts by relevant criteria.
