# Anal Training Progress Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Anal Training Progress Tracker is a tool used to track the progress of an individual's anal training sessions. It helps to monitor the duration, difficulty level, and overall effectiveness of the training, as well as record notes and goals for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the session date from the calendar provided.
2. Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
3. Rate the difficulty of the session on a scale from 1 to 5.
4. Provide any additional notes on the progress made during the session.
5. Set a goal for the next session.
6. If applicable, enter the name of the coach or supervisor who led the session.
7. Select how you felt before and after the session (e.g. Low, Neutral, High).
8. Submit the form to save your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session date** (`date`, Required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Duration in minutes** (`duration_minutes`, Required): Enter the length of the session in minutes.
* **Difficulty rating** (`difficulty_rating`, Optional): Rate the session's difficulty from 1 to 5.
* **Notes on progress** (`notes`, Optional): Record any observations or next steps for future sessions.
* **Next session goal** (`next_goal`, Optional): Set a target for the next session.
* **Coach name** (`coach_name`, Optional): If applicable, enter the name of the coach or supervisor who led the session.
* **Mood before** (`mood_before`, Optional): Select how you felt before the session (Low, Neutral, High).
* **Mood after** (`mood_after`, Optional): Select how you felt after the session (Low, Neutral, High).
