# gut_health_daily_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track an individual's gut health daily log, providing a comprehensive overview of their digestive health status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Daily Digestive Health Check-In** (Daily Digestive Health Check-In, select_multiple): Select how you feel about your digestive health on a given day.
*   **Daily Routine** (Daily Routine, text): Enter your daily routine in the text field provided.
*   **Bowel Movements** (Bowel Movements, number): Record the number of bowel movements you experience per day.
*   **Abdominal Pain** (Abdominal Pain, text): If you are experiencing abdominal pain, describe it in as much detail as possible in the text field provided.
*   **Bloating** (Bloating, select_one): Check the boxes that apply to your experience with bloating.
*   **Gas and Bloating** (Gas and Bloating, select_multiple): Select from the list of options provided to describe any experiences with gas and bloating.
*   **Digestive Discomfort** (Digestive Discomfort, select_multiple): Select from the list of options provided to describe any digestive discomfort you are experiencing.
*   **Medications** (Medications, text): List any medications you are currently taking in the text field provided.
*   **Notes** (Notes, note): Add any additional notes about your digestive health in the text field provided.
*   **Date** (Date, date): Enter the date you are completing the form.
*   **Time** (Time, time): Enter the time you are completing the form.
