## Customized Skincare Routine - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customized Skincare Routine form is designed to collect information about your skincare habits and preferences. It will help us provide you with personalized skincare recommendations and advice.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, select the applicable options from the dropdown menus and text fields.
- Fill out all required fields, marked as such.
- You can skip fields if they do not apply to your situation.
- If you need help with a specific field, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customized Skincare** (`routine_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you have a customized skincare routine or not.
	+ If you have a customized skincare routine, select 'Yes'.
	+ If you do not have a skincare routine, select 'No'.
* **Selected Products** (`products`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have selected products for your skincare routine, list them here.
	+ Select all the products you currently use for your skincare routine.
* **Duration** (`routine_duration`, number, required: false): If applicable, please provide the duration of your skincare routine in minutes.
	+ This field is intended to capture the length of time you dedicate to your skincare routine.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how often you perform your skincare routine.
	+ Select the frequency of your routine, e.g., Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, select_one, required: false): Select the time of day you perform your skincare routine.
	+ Choose the time frame when you perform your routine, e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): If you have additional information about your skincare routine, add it here.
	+ This is an optional text field for any extra details you'd like to share.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted about your skincare routine, provide your email address here.
	+ This is an optional field for users to provide their email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted about your skincare routine, provide your phone number here.
	+ This is an optional field for users to provide their phone number.
