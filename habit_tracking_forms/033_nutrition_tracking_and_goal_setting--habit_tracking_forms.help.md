# Nutrition Tracking And Goal Setting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your daily and long-term progress towards your nutritional goals. It provides a comprehensive view of your daily habits, including food intake, exercise, and mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. For each field, provide a brief description or entry that represents your daily habits or progress. You can fill out the form at any time, and you can review your previous entries at any point.

* **Daily Activity** (daily_activity): List any notable activities you've done today.

* **Weekly Progress** (weekly_progress): Track your weekly progress towards your goals.
