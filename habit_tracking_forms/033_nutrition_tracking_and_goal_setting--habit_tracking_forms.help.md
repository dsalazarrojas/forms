# Nutrition Tracking And Goal Setting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your daily and long-term progress towards your nutritional goals. It provides a comprehensive view of your daily habits, including food intake, exercise, and mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. For each field, provide a brief description or entry that represents your daily habits or progress. You can fill out the form at any time, and you can review your previous entries at any point.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daily Food** (daily_food): Enter a brief description of your daily food intake.
* **Daily Water** (daily_water): Record the amount of water you've consumed today.
* **Exercise Log** (exercise_log): Describe your exercise routine for the day.
* **Daily Activity** (daily_activity): List any notable activities you've done today.
* **Goals** (goals): Write down your short-term and long-term nutrition goals.
* **Daily Progress** (daily_progress): Record your daily progress towards your goals.
* **Client Reflections** (client_reflections): Share any thoughts or insights about your progress.
* **Daily Goals** (daily_goals): Describe your daily goals for nutrition.
* **Exercise Frequency** (exercise_frequency): Record the frequency of your exercise routine.
* **Daily Weight** (daily_weight): Weigh yourself and record your weight for the day.
* **Weekly Progress** (weekly_progress): Track your weekly progress towards your goals.
* **Monthly Progress** (monthly_progress): Record your monthly progress.
* **Client Comments** (client_comments): Share any comments or feedback about your progress.
* **Daily Mind Body** (daily_mind_body): Describe your mental and physical state today.
* **Daily Mood** (daily_mood): Report your current mood.
* **Daily Energy** (daily_energy): Record your energy levels today.
* **Daily Sleep** (daily_sleep): Describe your sleep quality and duration.
* **Daily Stress** (daily_stress): Rate your stress levels today.
* **Daily Stress Level** (daily_stress_level): Describe your stress levels today.
* **Exercise Duration** (exercise_duration): Record the duration of your exercise routine.
* **Exercise Intensity** (exercise_intensity): Describe the intensity of your exercise routine.
* **Daily Hydration** (daily_hydration): Record the amount of water you've consumed today.
* **Exercise Type** (exercise_type): List the types of exercise you've done today.
* **Daily Caloric Intake** (daily_caloric_intake): Report your daily caloric intake.
* **Daily Sleep Quality** (daily_sleep_quality): Describe the quality of your sleep.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use the form regularly to track your progress.
* Review and reflect on your entries to identify areas for improvement.
