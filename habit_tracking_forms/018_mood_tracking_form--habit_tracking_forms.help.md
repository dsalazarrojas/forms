# Mood Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mood Tracking Form is designed to collect information about your mood and related aspects to help you better understand and manage your mental health. This form is meant to be completed daily to track your emotional state, physical sensations, and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Click on the form and select each day's date for which you'd like to log your mood and related aspects.
2.  Enter your mood score, ranging from 1 to 10, to indicate how you feel on a scale of happiness.
3.  List any emotions you're experiencing, such as happy, sad, or anxious.
4.  If you're feeling emotions that aren't listed, describe them in the "other_emotions" field.
5.  Describe any physical sensations you're experiencing.
6.  Write down your thoughts, if any.
7.  List any coping mechanisms you're using to manage your emotions.
8.  Provide a comment about your mood score if you'd like.
9.  Report your sleep quality, on a scale of 1 to 10.
10.  Enter the duration of your sleep, in hours.
11.  Select the time you woke up.
12.  Choose your physical activity level for the day.
13.  Indicate if you took any medication.
14.  Save or submit your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **mood_form_data**: This is a note field for any additional comments or information about your mood.
* **date**: Select the date you'd like to log your mood and related aspects. This is where you'll select the date for which you'd like to track your mood.
* **mood_score**: Enter a score from 1 to 10 to indicate your happiness level.
* **feelings**: List any emotions you're experiencing, such as happy, sad, or anxious. Choose one or more from the list.
* **other_emotions**: If you're feeling emotions not listed, describe them here.
* **physical_sensations**: Describe any physical sensations you're experiencing.
* **thoughts**: Write down any thoughts you're having.
* **coping_mechanisms**: List any strategies you're using to manage your emotions.
* **mood_score_comment**: Provide a comment about your mood score if you'd like.
* **sleep_quality**: Report your sleep quality on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **sleep_duration**: Enter the duration of your sleep, in hours.
* **wake_up_time**: Select the time you woke up.
* **physical_activity**: Choose your physical activity level for the day.
* **medication_taken**: Indicate if you took any medication.
