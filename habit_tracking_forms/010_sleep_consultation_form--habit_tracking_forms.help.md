# Sleep Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track and report their sleep patterns and quality. It is a self-assessment tool to gather personal feedback and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field. This will depend on the specific instructions provided by your sleep consultant or the specific requirements of your program.
2. In the "Sleep Patterns" field, enter a brief description of your typical sleep patterns, including the times you go to bed and wake up, and any unusual sleep-related events.
3. In the "Sleep Quality" field, describe your overall sleep quality, using a scale of 1-5 or any other scale provided by your consultant.
4. In the "Sleep Consultant Rating" field, rate your satisfaction with the sleep consulting process, from 1-5 or any other scale provided by your consultant.
5. In the "Sleep Consultant Recommendations" field, describe any recommendations or suggestions made by your sleep consultant, or any changes you plan to implement based on their feedback.
6. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field, as this will be used for further analysis and feedback.
7. In the "Output File" field, enter a brief file name or description for the output file, which will contain the data collected from this form.
8. If additional "Sleep Patterns" fields are provided, repeat the process of entering information about your typical sleep patterns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sleep Patterns**: Enter a brief description of your typical sleep patterns, including the times you go to bed and wake up, and any unusual sleep-related events.
* **Sleep Quality**: Describe your overall sleep quality, using a scale of 1-5 or any other scale provided by your sleep consultant.
* **Sleep Consultant Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the sleep consulting process, from 1-5 or any other scale provided by your consultant.
* **Sleep Consultant Recommendations**: Describe any recommendations or suggestions made by your sleep consultant, or any changes you plan to implement based on their feedback.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you by your sleep consultant, which will be used for further analysis and feedback.
* **Output File**: Enter a brief file name or description for the output file, which will contain the data collected from this form.
* **Sleep Patterns (2)**: Repeat the process of entering information about your typical sleep patterns.
* **Sleep Patterns (3)**: Repeat the process of entering information about your typical sleep patterns.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not completing this form multiple times for the same day/period, as this may result in inconsistent and incomplete data.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your sleep consultant for further guidance.
