# impulse_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track and record instances of impulsive behavior, providing a comprehensive record of the impulsive behavior, its triggers, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the specific impulse you want to log, choosing from the options provided.
2. Describe the trigger or situation that led to the impulsive behavior.
3. Describe the response or outcome of the impulsive behavior.
4. Choose the type of trigger (physical, emotional, or cognitive).
5. Choose the location where the trigger occurred.
6. Select the time of day the impulsive behavior occurred.
7. Enter the date of the impulsive behavior.
8. Rate the frequency of the impulsive behavior on a scale from 0-5.
9. Rate the intensity or difficulty of the impulsive behavior on a scale from 0-5.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about the impulsive behavior.
11. Enter any comments from a coach or mental health professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Impulse Log** (`Impulse 1`, `Impulse 2`, `Impulse 3`, etc.): 
	+ Select the specific impulse you want to log, choosing from the options provided.
* **Trigger**:
	+ Describe the situation or stimulus that led to the impulsive behavior.
* **Emotion**:
	+ Enter a brief description of the emotion felt during or after the impulsive behavior.
* **Response**:
	+ Describe the outcome or result of the impulsive behavior.
* **Trigger Type** (Physical, Emotional, Cognitive):
	+ Choose the type of trigger that led to the impulsive behavior.
* **Trigger Location**:
	+ Describe the location where the trigger occurred.
* **Time of Day**:
	+ Enter the time of day the impulsive behavior occurred.
* **Date**:
	+ Enter the date of the impulsive behavior.
* **Frequency**:
	+ Rate the frequency of the impulsive behavior on a scale from 0-5.
* **Rating**:
	+ Rate the intensity or difficulty of the impulsive behavior on a scale from 0-5.
* **Rating Scale** (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5):
	+ Select the rating for the intensity or difficulty of the impulsive behavior.
* **Note**:
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the impulsive behavior.
* **Assigned Tool**:
	+ Enter any assigned tool or resources used to address the impulsive behavior.
* **Comments**:
	+ Enter comments from a coach or mental health professional.
* **Review Date**:
	+ Enter the date when the impulse behavior was reviewed or checked.

Note:
Please complete each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible, and use the provided options or fields as a guide.
This form is meant to be a personal and individualized tool for tracking and understanding impulses.
It is not a substitute for professional medical or mental health advice.
