# Running Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Running Log Form is designed to track an individual's running activities, including details of their first run, workout date, distance, pace, duration, calories burned, and various other metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, users should fill out the required and optional fields as follows:

*   Enter details of your first run, including date and location.
*   Provide information about your most recent workout date.
*   Record the distance of your runs, including the longest and shortest distances covered.
*   Enter your age and height to track your progress in relation to these biometric factors.
*   Note the average distance and number of runs you complete each day.
*   Connect your account to apps like MyFitnessPal or Fitbit to sync your data and track your runs in those platforms.
*   Decide whether to connect your account with apps or not.
*   Enter your phone number to allow for further contact.
*   Enter your name, age, height, weight, and country of residence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Run Details** (`first_run_details`, text, optional): Enter details of your first run, including date and location.
*   **Workout Date** (`workout_date`, date, optional): Provide information about your most recent workout date.
*   **Distance** (`distance`, number, optional): Record the distance of your runs, including the longest and shortest distances covered.
*   **Pace** (`pace`, number, optional): Enter your pace to track your progress.
*   **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your run to track your progress.
*   **Calories Burned** (`calories_burned`, number, optional): Enter the number of calories burned during your run.
*   **Workout Type** (`workout_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of workout you completed (Running, Strength Training, Yoga, Other).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Leave any additional notes about your run.
*   **Apps Connected** (`apps_connected`, select_multiple, optional): Connect your account with apps like MyFitnessPal or Fitbit to sync your data.
*   **Connect with Apps** (`connect_with_apps`, select_one, optional): Decide whether to connect your account with apps or not.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email to allow for further contact.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to allow for further contact.
*   **User ID** (`user_id`, number, optional): Enter your ID number.
*   **Name** (`user_name`, number, optional): Enter your name.
*   **Age** (`user_age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
*   **Height** (`user_height`, number, optional): Enter your height.
*   **Weight** (`user_weight`, number, optional): Enter your weight.
*   **Country** (`user_country`, number, optional): Enter your country of residence.
*   **First Run Date** (`first_run_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your first run.
*   **Last Run Date** (`last_run_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your most recent run.
*   **Total Runs** (`total_runs`, number, optional): Note the total number of runs you have completed.
*   **Average Runs per Day** (`avg_runs_per_day`, number, optional): Note the average distance and number of runs you complete each day.
*   **Longest Run** (`longest_run`, number, optional): Record the longest distance you have run.
*   **Shortest Run** (`shortest_run`, number, optional): Record the shortest distance you have run.
