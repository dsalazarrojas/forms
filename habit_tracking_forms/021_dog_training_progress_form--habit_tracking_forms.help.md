# Dog Training Progress Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Progress Form is a tool for tracking and monitoring a dog's training progress. This form helps dog owners or trainers keep a record of the dog's behavior, schedule, and training milestones, making it easier to evaluate the effectiveness of the training program and make adjustments as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your dog's information, including their name and your name as the owner/trainer.
2. Choose the dates you want to start and end tracking your dog's training progress with the start and end date fields.
3. Describe your dog's training schedule, including any activities or sessions that are relevant to their progress.
4. Track your dog's progress over time using the habit tracker field, where you can write a short note about their behavior, training, or any notable events.
5. Submit the form to review and update your dog's training progress regularly.
