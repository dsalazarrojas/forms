# Dog Training Progress Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Progress Form is a tool for tracking and monitoring a dog's training progress. This form helps dog owners or trainers keep a record of the dog's behavior, schedule, and training milestones, making it easier to evaluate the effectiveness of the training program and make adjustments as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your dog's information, including their name and your name as the owner/trainer.
2. Choose the dates you want to start and end tracking your dog's training progress with the start and end date fields.
3. Describe your dog's training schedule, including any activities or sessions that are relevant to their progress.
4. Track your dog's progress over time using the habit tracker field, where you can write a short note about their behavior, training, or any notable events.
5. Submit the form to review and update your dog's training progress regularly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Habit Tracker** (`habit_tracker`, text, optional): A field for tracking and recording notes about your dog's behavior and progress.
* **Training Schedule** (`training_schedule`, date, optional): A field for noting down your dog's training schedule, including any relevant activities or sessions.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): A field for selecting the start date of your training progress tracking.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): A field for selecting the end date of your training progress tracking.
* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, optional): A field for inputting your dog's name.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): A field for inputting your name as the owner or trainer of the dog.
 
## Tips
* Use this form regularly to track your dog's progress and make adjustments to their training program as needed.
* Keep the notes in the habit tracker field concise and clear, so it's easy to review and update their progress over time.
* Make sure to select the correct start and end dates for your training progress tracking.
