# Mobile Game Daily Check In Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect daily data from players about their mobile game activities. It is meant to help game developers or administrators track player engagement and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Player ID**, a unique identifier for your player account.
2. Enter the **Game Title**, the name of the mobile game you played.
3. Select the **Date Played**, the date you played the game.
4. Enter the **Time Spent**, the amount of time you spent playing the game.
5. Enter your current **Progress**, a measure of your in-game status.
6. Enter your current **Level**, your player level in the game.
7. Enter your **Score**, your score in the game.
8. Enter any additional **Notes** about your game experience.
9. Enter your **Player Comments**, any comments or thoughts about your gaming experience.
10. Enter any **Game Comments** about the game itself, its mechanics, or gameplay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player ID**: Enter your unique player ID associated with your player account.
* **Game Title**: Enter the name of the mobile game you played.
* **Date Played**: Select the date you played the game.
* **Time Spent**: Enter the amount of time you spent playing the game.
* **Progress**: Enter your current in-game progress.
* **Level**: Enter your current player level in the game.
* **Score**: Enter your score in the game.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your game experience.
* **Player Comments**: Enter your thoughts or comments about your gaming experience.
* **Game Comments**: Enter comments or thoughts about the game itself.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate tracking.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact your game administrator or support team.
- If you have any suggestions for game improvement, use the Player Comments or Game Comments fields.
