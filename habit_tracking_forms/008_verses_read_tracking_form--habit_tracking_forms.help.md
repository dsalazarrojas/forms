# Verses Read Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Verses Read Tracking Form is a tool to help you monitor your personal reading habits, set goals, and track your progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the title of the form with the title of the verse(s) you're tracking.
2. Enter the number of verses you have read in the corresponding time frame (e.g., day, week, etc.).
3. If you want to record a reflection, add your thoughts or notes about what you read.
4. Enter the date you read the verse(s).
5. Estimate the time you spent reading the verse(s).
6. Specify when you plan to read the next verse.
7. Choose how often you plan to read or have read the verse(s) (e.g., daily, every 3 days, etc.).
8. Select any goals you've set for reading (e.g., completing a specific number of verses in a week).
9. Enter the progress you've made towards your goal.
10. Enter the total number of verses you've read so far.
11. Set a goal for how often you plan to read (e.g., once a week, etc.).
12. Enter a number for your total verse read goal.
13. Enter an estimate of the average number of verses you read per day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Verses Read Tracking Form** (Verse Read Tracking Form, text, required): Enter the title of the verse(s) you're tracking.
* **verses_read** (verses_read, number, optional): Enter the number of verses you have read.
* **reflection** (reflection, note, optional): If you want to record a reflection, add your thoughts or notes about what you read.
* **date_read** (date_read, date, optional): Enter the date you read the verse(s).
* **time_spent_reading** (time_spent_reading, time, optional): Estimate the time you spent reading the verse(s).
* **next_verse** (next_verse, text, optional): Specify when you plan to read the next verse.
* **verse_read_frequency** (verse_read_frequency, select_one, optional): Choose how often you plan to read or have read the verse(s) (e.g., daily, every 3 days, etc.).
* **goals** (goals, select_multiple, optional): Select any goals you've set for reading (e.g., completing a specific number of verses in a week).
* **progress** (progress, number, optional): Enter the progress you've made towards your goal.
* **total_verse_read** (total_verse_read, number, optional): Enter the total number of verses you've read so far.
* **reading_frequency_goal** (reading_frequency_goal, select_one, optional): Set a goal for how often you plan to read (e.g., once a week, etc.).
* **total_verse_read_goal** (total_verse_read_goal, number, optional): Enter a number for your total verse read goal.
* **average_verse_read_per_day** (average_verse_read_per_day, number, optional): Enter an estimate of the average number of verses you read per day.
