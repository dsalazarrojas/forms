# Daily Food Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a user's daily food intake. This includes their weight, food items consumed, the amount and unit of the food consumed, the time of day, any notes, email, phone number, and a daily form notes section. This information is intended for personal or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Enter your current weight in the "What is your current weight" field, if applicable.
3. Select the food items you consumed from the drop-down list in the "Select food items" field. You can select multiple items.
4. Enter the amount of each food item consumed in the "Daily Food Intake" field.
5. Select the unit of the food consumed (e.g. Cups, Pieces, Ounces) from the "Select unit" field.
6. Enter the time of day you consumed the food in the "Time of day" field.
7. Add any additional notes about your food intake in the "Any notes?" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you want to be contacted for follow-up.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if you want to be contacted.
10. Add any additional notes about your food intake in the "Daily Food Intake Form Notes" field.
11. Finally, select the date and time of when you're submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current weight (1)**: Enter your current weight if you have it. This is an optional field.
* **Select food items (2)**: Choose the food items you consumed today from the list. You can select multiple items.
* **Daily Food Intake (3)**: Enter the amount of each food item consumed.
* **Select unit (4)**: Select the unit of the food consumed (e.g. Cups, Pieces, Ounces).
* **Time of day (5)**: Enter the time of day you consumed the food.
* **Any notes? (6)**: Add any additional notes about your food intake.
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted.
* **Phone Number (8)**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Daily Food Intake Form Notes (9)**: Add any additional notes about your food intake.
* **Date (10)**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time (11)**: Enter the time you are submitting this form.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when reporting your food intake.
* Select all food items that apply to your daily consumption.
* Use the correct unit for the food consumed.
* Enter a valid email address and phone number if you want to be contacted.
* Use this form for personal or research purposes only.
