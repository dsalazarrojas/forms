# Child Habit Tracker Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help parents or guardians set up a customized habit tracking plan for their child, taking into account various factors such as habit frequency, tracking methods, reminders, and potential challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Setup Date**: Enter the date when you started setting up the habit tracker.
	* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Enter the legal first name of the parent or guardian.
	* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Enter the legal last name of the parent or guardian.
	* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the primary email address for contact.
	* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the primary phone number for contact.
	* **Child First Name**: Enter the legal first name of the child.
	* **Child Last Name**: Enter the legal last name of the child.
	* **Child Age**: Enter the current age of the child in years.
	* **Habit Category**: Select the category of habit you want to track (e.g., daily routines, hygiene habits, etc.).
	* **Primary Habit Goal**: Describe the main habit you want to track.
	* **Habit Frequency**: Select how often the habit should be performed (e.g., multiple times per day, once daily, etc.).
	* **Tracking Start Date**: Enter the date when habit tracking should begin.
	* **Tracking End Date** (optional): Enter the date when habit tracking should end.
	* **Reward System**: Select if you plan to use a reward system for habit completion.
	* **Tracking Method Preference**: Select how you prefer to track habits (e.g., digital app, printable chart, etc.).
	* **Reminder Preferences**: Select how you want to receive reminders (e.g., email, text message, etc.).
	* **Who Will Track the Habit**: Select all who will be responsible for tracking the habit.
	* **Previous Habit Tracking Attempts**: Select if you've tried tracking habits before.
	* **Additional Goals or Habits to Track**: List any additional habits you want to track.

2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Potential Challenges**: List any potential challenges you anticipate in tracking the habit.
	* **Support Needed**: List any support or resources that would help you succeed.
	* **Additional Goals or Habits to Track**: List any additional habits you want to track.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant habit categories and goals to ensure accurate tracking.
* Double-check all entered information for accuracy.
* Be specific and clear when describing the primary habit goal.
* Select a suitable reward system that motivates the child.
* Choose a tracking method that aligns with your preferences.
* Set realistic reminder preferences that work best for the child and yourself.
