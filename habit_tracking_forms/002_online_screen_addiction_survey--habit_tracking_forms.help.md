# Online Screen Addiction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's level of addiction to various screens (phone, computer, social media, email, etc.). The survey consists of questions that gauge the frequency and frequency of usage of different screens, as well as a score that calculates a total addiction level.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your responses for each question from the provided options.
2. For select multiple questions, select all that apply.
3. Enter your responses for numerical questions in the required format (e.g., 1-5 or minutes/hours).
4. Submit the form once you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screen Addiction**: This is a numerical question where you should select the number that best describes your screen addiction level.
* **Harmful Behaviors**: Select all the behaviors that you consider harmful or problematic related to your screen usage.
* **Screen Time**: Enter the amount of time you spend on screens daily.
* **Screen Frequency**: Select how often you use screens daily.
* **Phone Addiction**: Enter your phone addiction level.
* **Social Media**: Select if you are addicted to social media.
* **Email Addiction**: Enter your email addiction level.
* **Video Games**: Select all the video games you play regularly.
* **Social Connections**: Select all the social connections you maintain through screens.
* **Email Frequency**: Select how often you use email.
* **Phone Frequency**: Select how often you use your phone.
* **Social Frequency**: Select how often you interact with others through screens.
* **Video Games Frequency**: Select how often you play video games.
* **Email Screen Time**: Select how much time you spend on email.
* **Phone Screen Time**: Select how much time you spend on phone.
* **Social Screen Time**: Select how much time you spend on social media.
* **Video Screen Time**: Select how much time you spend on video games.
* **Screen Break**: Select how often you take a screen break.
* **Screen Addiction Frequency**: Select how often you feel addicted to screens.
* **Email Addiction Frequency**: Select how often you feel addicted to email.
* **Phone Addiction Frequency**: Select how often you feel addicted to phone.
* **Video Games Addiction Frequency**: Select how often you feel addicted to video games.
* **Social Addiction Frequency**: Select how often you feel addicted to social media.
* **Social Media Addiction Frequency**: Select how often you feel addicted to social media.
* **Screen Addiction Score**: This is a calculated score that represents your total addiction level.

Note: The survey questions are designed to be as clear and concise as possible. However, if you have any difficulties or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
