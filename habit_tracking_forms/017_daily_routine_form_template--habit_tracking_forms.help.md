# Daily Routine Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Routine Form Template is designed to help you track and reflect on your daily habits and behaviors. It's a tool to help you identify areas for improvement and create a more consistent and healthy daily routine.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions and fill in the fields as accurately as possible.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this will help you get a clear picture of your daily habits.
3. If a field is not applicable to you, simply leave it blank.
4. Review and update your answers regularly to track changes and progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning Routine** (morning_routine)
	+ This field is for you to describe your morning routine, including what you do from waking up to starting your day. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Prophets** (prophets)
	+ In this field, select either "Yes" or "No" if you believe in prophets or spiritual guides.
* **Sleep Time** (sleep_time)
	+ This field is for you to enter the time you go to sleep each night.
* **Exercise Time** (exercise_time)
	+ Here, you can enter the time you dedicate to exercise each day.
* **Wake Up Time** (wake_up_time)
	+ Enter the time you wake up each morning.
* **Notes** (notes)
	+ This is a text field for you to add any additional comments or notes about your daily routine.
* **Email** (email)
	+ Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for any reason related to your daily routine.
* **Phone** (phone)
	+ This field is for you to enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted.
* **Morning Routine 2** (morning_routine_2)
	+ This field is similar to the Morning Routine field, but it's a second part of your morning routine.
* **Exercise Routine** (exercise_routine)
	+ Describe your exercise routine here, including any regular activities you do.
* **Routine Goals** (routine_goals)
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" if you have goals for your daily routine.
* **Daily Activities** (daily_activities)
	+ Indicate which daily activities you regularly do, such as work, hobbies, or other tasks.
* **Sleep Pattern** (sleep_pattern)
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" if you follow a regular sleep pattern.
* **Daily Routine Frequency** (daily_routine_frequency)
	+ Indicate how frequently you follow your daily routine.
* **Morning Routine 3** (morning_routine_3)
	+ This field is similar to the Morning Routine field, but it's a third part of your morning routine.
* **Routine Frequency** (routine_frequency)
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" if you have a regular routine frequency.

## Tips

* Be consistent in filling out the form and reviewing it regularly.
* Use this form as a tool to identify areas for improvement and create a more consistent and healthy daily routine.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
