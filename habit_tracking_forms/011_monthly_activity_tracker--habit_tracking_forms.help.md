## monthly_activity_tracker - Help Guide

### Purpose

The monthly_activity_tracker form is a tool for recording and tracking individual activities and progress towards monthly goals. It helps users to maintain a record of their activities, their duration, and reflections, which can be reviewed by the team.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Monthly Activity Goal" field to specify the goal you want to achieve in the month.
2. Enter the "Activity Date" in the format of a date (month/day/year).
3. For each activity, choose whether it was a "Yes" or "No" in the "Activity Type" field, indicating whether the activity was completed or not.
4. Enter the start and end time of the activity if it was completed.
5. Optionally, enter the duration of the activity in minutes.
6. Enter a brief description of the activity.
7. Reflect on the activity by providing a reflection in the text field.
8. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes for team review.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monthly Activity Goal** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the goal you want to achieve in the month.
* **Activity Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the activity in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 01/01/2024).
* **Activity Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose "Yes" if the activity was completed, and "No" otherwise.
* **Start Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the start time of the activity if it was completed.
* **End Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the end time of the activity if it was completed.
* **Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the activity in minutes if it was completed.
* **Description** (Text, Required): Provide a brief description of the activity.
* **Reflection** (Text, Required): Reflect on the activity by providing a brief text response.
* **Team Review** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes for team review.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the activity.
