# Daily Routine Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your daily habits, including your wake-up time, exercise time, sleep time, and food intake. By filling out this form, you'll get a clear picture of your daily routines and can make adjustments as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding options for each field. For time fields (wake-up time, exercise time, sleep time), choose the correct time of day. For food fields (breakfast, lunch, dinner), type in the specific foods you had for each meal. Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Routines**: 
* **Wake-up Time**: Enter the time when you wake up in the morning. 
* **Exercise Time**: Enter the time when you exercise, such as going to the gym or engaging in physical activity. 
* **Sleep Time**: Enter the time when you go to sleep at night. 
* **Breakfast Food**: Type in the food you had for breakfast. 
* **Lunch Food**: Type in the food you had for lunch. 
* **Dinner Food**: Type in the food you had for dinner. 
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your day. 

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form. 
* Make sure to fill out all required fields. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
