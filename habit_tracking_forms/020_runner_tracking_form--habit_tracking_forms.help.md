# Runner Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track a runner's activity, collecting information about the date, location, distance, type, and other details of a run. The form should be completed for every run, walk, or other form of physical activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the date of your run in the `run_date` field. This will record the date of your physical activity.
2. In the `runner_location` field, enter the location where your run took place.
3. In the `run_distance` field, enter the distance you covered during your run. This can be a whole number value (e.g., 5, 10, 20).
4. Select the type of physical activity you performed by choosing from the options "Run", "Walk", or "Cycle".
5. Optionally, add any notes about your run in the `run_notes` field.
6. Enter any additional notes about the run in the `notes` field.
7. If you have a phone number, enter it in the `runner_phone` field for future reference.
8. Enter your email address in the `runner_email` field, this will help us contact you if we need more information.
9. If you have a specific tool assigned to you for tracking, enter it in the `assigned_tool` field.
10. Finally, enter any unique identifier for the form in the `form_ids` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **run_date** (Date): Enter the date of your run.
* **runner_location** (Text): Enter the location where your run took place.
* **run_distance** (Number): Enter the distance you covered during your run.
* **run_type** (Select One): Select the type of physical activity you performed (Run, Walk, or Cycle).
* **run_notes** (Text): Optionally, add any notes about your run.
* **notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about the run.
* **runner_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for future reference.
* **runner_email** (Email): Enter your email address for future reference.
* **assigned_tool** (Text): If you have a specific tool assigned to you for tracking, enter it here.
* **form_ids** (Text): Enter any unique identifier for the form.
* **form_version** (Text): This field is not used in the current form configuration, but it may be used in future updates.
