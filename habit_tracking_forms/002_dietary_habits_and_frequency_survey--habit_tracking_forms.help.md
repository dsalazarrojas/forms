# Dietary Habits And Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on an individual's dietary habits, including frequency of eating certain types of food, food allergies, time spent on food preparation, and frequency of eating out.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the food types that apply to you.
2. Indicate your frequency of eating each selected food type.
3. Select your food allergy status.
4. Choose your favorite food.
5. Select your eating frequency.
6. Answer the questions as accurately as possible, and make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner Frequency** (`section_2`, `select_multiple`): Select all the types of food you frequently eat for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
* **Food Allergies** (`section_3`, `select_multiple`): Check the box if you have any food allergies.
* **Types of Food Eaten** (`section_4`, `select_multiple`): Select all the types of food you frequently eat.
* **Fruit and Vegetable Frequency** (`section_5`, `select_one`): Select how often you eat fruits and vegetables.
* **Meat and Fish Frequency** (`section_6`, `select_one`): Select how often you eat meat and fish.
* **Time of Eating** (`section_7`, `select_one`): Select the time of day when you usually eat.
* **Time Spent on Food Preparation** (`section_8`, `number`): Enter the average time you spend preparing food each day.
* **Eating Out Frequency** (`section_9`, `select_one`): Select how often you eat out.
* **Leftovers Frequency** (`section_10`, `select_one`): Select how often you eat leftovers.
* **Favorite Food** (`section_11`, `text`): Enter your favorite food.
* **Eating Frequency** (`section_12`, `select_one`): Select how often you eat.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any food allergies, please select the "I have food allergies" option.
