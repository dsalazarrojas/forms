# Mobility Allowance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobility Allowance Request Form is a form designed to facilitate employees' requests for mobility allowance when traveling for business-related activities. This guide will walk you through the form's structure and what to expect in each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Request Details** field with a clear and concise description of your travel purpose and requirements.
2. Select the relevant **Date Of Travel** for your trip.
3. If applicable, select the correct **Destination Airport** for your travel.
4. Indicate whether you will be traveling for business or personal reasons in the **Travel Type** field.
5. Choose the travel documents you need to present for your trip.
6. Enter detailed travel specifics in the **Travel Details** field, including any necessary information about your travel plans.
7. Provide an estimate of the travel cost in the **Travel Cost** field.
8. Enter the start and end dates of your travel in the **Travel Start Date** and **Travel End Date** fields.
9. Select the frequency of your travel, if applicable.
10. Indicate your current **Travel Status** and provide a reason for the status in the **Travel Status Reason** field.
11. Enter the date of the last travel status update.
12. If you are the travel requester, select 'Yes' in the **Travel Requester** field.
13. Select the department that the travel is for in the **Department** field.
14. Fill in your name in the **Travel Requester Name** field.
15. Enter your email address in the **Travel Requester Email** field.
16. Enter your phone number in the **Travel Requester Phone** field.
17. Select your job title in the **Travel Requester Job Title** field.
