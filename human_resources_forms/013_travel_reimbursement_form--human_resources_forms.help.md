# Travel Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture travel reimbursement information from employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you are authorized to complete this form.
2. Fill in the travel details, including date, flight information, and destination.
3. Provide a reason for your travel.
4. Enter the expense amount and currency.
5. If required, upload supporting documents.
6. Add any additional comments.
7. Enter your email and phone number.
8. Sign the form with your signature.
9. If necessary, request manager approval.
10. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional): 
  Enter a brief description of your travel.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): 
  Enter the start and end dates of your travel.
* **Flight Information** (`flight_info`, text, optional): 
  Enter your flight itinerary or reservation number.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, select_one, optional): 
  Select the destination of your travel from the provided options.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, optional): 
  Provide a brief explanation for your travel.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, optional): 
  Enter the total expense amount for your travel.
* **Currency** (`currency`, select_one, optional): 
  Select the currency of your expense amount.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): 
  Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): 
  Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): 
  Add any additional comments or notes about your travel.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, note, optional): 
  Upload any supporting documents for your travel.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): 
  Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): 
  Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): 
  Sign this form with your digital signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, optional): 
  If necessary, your manager will sign this form.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, date, optional): 
  Date and time the manager approved your request.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select the status of your travel request: approved, pending, or rejected.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use the correct currency for your expense amount.
* Upload any necessary supporting documents.
* Sign the form accurately with your digital signature.
* If necessary, wait for manager approval before submitting the form.
* Double-check your form for accuracy before submitting.
