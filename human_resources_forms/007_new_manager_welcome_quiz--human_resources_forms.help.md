# New Manager Welcome Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Manager Welcome Quiz is a form designed to help the HR department onboard new managers into the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on the form, making sure to provide accurate and detailed information.
2. Select the correct department and department 2 (if applicable) from the drop-down menus.
3. Enter your name and name of direct supervisor in the text fields.
4. Enter your job title and years of experience in the text fields.
5. Select your completed training status in the text field.
6. Choose a date of completion from the calendar.
7. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Manager Welcome Quiz** (`title`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the form. It is not a field that needs to be filled out.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, optional): This is the department of the new manager. Select the relevant department from the drop-down menu.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the new manager.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, select the relevant department 2 from the drop-down menu.
* **Name of Direct Supervisor** (`name_of_direct_supervisor`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the direct supervisor.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the job title of the new manager.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, optional): Enter the years of experience of the new manager.
* **How Much Training Completed** (`how_much_training_completed`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of training completed.
* **Training Completed** (`training_completed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of training completed.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`, `date`, optional): Choose a date of completion from the calendar.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure of any information, please contact the HR department for assistance.
