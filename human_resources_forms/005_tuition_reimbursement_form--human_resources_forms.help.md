# Tuition Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help employees manage their tuition reimbursement requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee details in the Employee Details field.
2. Enter the Tuition Amount you want to reimburse.
3. Select the Start Date and End Date of the tuition period.
4. Enter the Reimbursed Amount, if you have provided the Reimbursement Rate.
5. Select the Reimbursement Rate (10% per semester, 15% per semester, or 20% per semester) and fill in Other Reimbursement Rate if you have chosen Other.
6. Select the Reason for Reimbursement (Tuition fees, Room and board, Books and supplies, or Other).
7. Fill in the Notes field with any additional information you feel is important for the reimbursement team.
8. Enter your Email address and Phone number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details**: <name>, <id 1>: Enter your employee ID number to help the reimbursement team identify your account.
* **Tuition Amount** (id 2): Enter the amount of tuition you want to reimburse, in a number format.
* **Start Date** (id 3): Enter the date when you started your tuition period. This could be the first day of the semester or any other date relevant to your tuition.
* **End Date** (id 4): Enter the date when you ended your tuition period. This could be the last day of the semester or any other date relevant to your tuition.
* **Reimbursed Amount** (id 5): Enter the amount you want to get reimbursed. This field is optional and should be filled in if you have chosen a Reimbursement Rate.
* **Reimbursement Rate** (id 6): Select one of the offered rates to indicate how much of your tuition will be reimbursed:
	+ 10% per semester
	+ 15% per semester
	+ 20% per semester
* **Other Reimbursement Rate** (id 7): If you have chosen Other as your Reimbursement Rate, enter the rate you want in this field.
* **Reason for Reimbursement** (id 8): Select the reason why you need the reimbursement:
	+ Tuition fees
	+ Room and board
	+ Books and supplies
	+ Other
* **Notes** (id 9): Add any additional notes you feel are important for the reimbursement team to process your request.
* **Email** (id 10): Enter your email address for the reimbursement team to contact you.
* **Phone** (id 11): Enter your phone number for the reimbursement team to contact you.
