# Employee Reasonable Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reasonable Accommodation Request Form is used to request and document reasonable accommodations for employees with disabilities or medical conditions that require special assistance or modifications in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details and needs.
- Make sure to upload a medical document if requested.
- Sign the form with your own signature as the employee.
- Ask your manager to review and sign the form as the approver.
- Date and time stamp the form when you submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (1, text, required): Enter a brief title for your accommodation request, such as "Request for Reasonable Accommodation for [Disability/Condition]".
- **Employee Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel records.
- **Job Title** (3, text, required): Enter your current job title as it appears in your personnel records.
- **Reason for Accommodation** (4, text, required): Describe the medical condition or disability that requires the accommodation, including the impact on your job performance or daily activities.
- **Accommodation Type** (5, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the accommodation is requested for a disability or medical condition (Yes), or if no accommodation is required (No).
- **Accommodation Details** (6, text, optional): Provide additional details about the accommodation you require, such as specific equipment or modifications needed.
- **Medical Document Upload** (7, note, required): Upload a medical document from your healthcare provider that supports your accommodation request.
- **Employee Signature** (8, text, required): Sign your name as the employee making the accommodation request.
- **Manager Signature** (9, text, required): Sign your manager's name as the approver of the accommodation request.
- **Date** (10, date, required): Enter the date you submit the form.
- **Time** (11, time, required): Enter the time you submit the form.
- **Form Submitted By** (12, text, optional): Indicate whether the form was submitted by you as the employee (Yes), or if someone else submitted it on your behalf (No).
- **Form Submitted To** (13, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the form was submitted to HR (Yes), or to another department (No).
- **Form Submitted Date** (14, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.

Please note that the fields marked as "optional" are not mandatory but may be necessary for further processing or review.
