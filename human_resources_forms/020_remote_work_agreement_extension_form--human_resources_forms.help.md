# Remote Work Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend or update an existing remote work agreement between an employee, their manager, and team members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields accurately and completely to ensure that the remote work agreement is extended or updated correctly.
2. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid mistakes.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee** (`remote_work_agreement_extension_form`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the employee's name and title. Please enter your name and job title as it appears in the company's system.

### 2. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false)
* This field is for your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.

### 3. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false)
* This field is for your job title as it appears in the company's system.

### 4. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false)
* This field is for your manager's name. Please enter their name as it appears in the company's system.

### 5. **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required: false)
* This field is for your manager's email address. Please ensure that it's entered correctly to ensure that your manager receives notifications.

### 6. **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required: false)
* This field allows you to select multiple team members involved in your remote work arrangement. Choose the relevant team members from the options provided.

### 7. **Work Type** (`work_type`, select_one, required: false)
* This field determines the type of work arrangement you have. Choose the option that best describes your work arrangement from the options provided.

### 8. **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false)
* This field is for the start date of your remote work arrangement. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 9. **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false)
* This field is for the end date of your remote work arrangement. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 10. **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the location where you'll be working remotely. Please enter the location where you'll be working.

### 11. **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required: false)
* This field is for your manager's phone number. Please ensure that it's entered correctly to ensure that your manager receives notifications.

### 12. **Manager Department** (`manager_department`, text, required: false)
* This field is for your manager's department. Please enter their department as it appears in the company's system.

### 13. **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, required: false)
* This field determines the type of remote work agreement you're extending or updating. Choose the option that best describes the agreement from the options provided.

### 14. **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false)
* This field is for any additional notes or comments about your remote work arrangement. Please use this field to provide any relevant information that may be necessary for the extension or update.

### 15. **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required: false)
* This field indicates the status of your remote work agreement extension or update. Choose the option that best describes the current status from the options provided.

Please note that this form is subject to change, and fields may be added or removed as needed. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for clarification.
