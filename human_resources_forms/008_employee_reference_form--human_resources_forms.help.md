# Employee Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reference Form is used to collect essential information about an employee that can be used as a reference for future checks. This form helps our team to understand the employee's background, experience, and work history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's **First name**, which is the first and primary name given to them at birth.
2. Enter the employee's **Last name**, which is the surname used to identify them.
3. Type the **Company** where the employee works or has worked.
4. Fill in the **Job title** of the employee, which describes their position within the company.
5. Enter the **Years at company** field with the number of years the employee has been with the current or previous company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter the employee's first name. This is usually the name they were given at birth.
* **Last name**: Enter the employee's last name. This is usually the surname used to identify them in official documents and records.
* **Company**: Type the name of the company where the employee works or has worked. This could be the current company or a previous employer.
* **Job title**: Describe the employee's position or role within the company. This helps to understand their responsibilities and areas of expertise.
* **Years at company**: Enter the number of years the employee has been with the current or previous company. This helps to track their work history.
