# Workplace Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Incident Form is used to gather information about workplace incidents, including the details surrounding the incident, the actions taken, and the resolution. This form is intended to help ensure that workplace incidents are properly documented, investigated, and addressed to maintain a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*). These fields are necessary for accurate and complete reporting.
2. Enter your employee's name, employee number, and incident details in the respective fields.
3. Select the correct incident type and location from the dropdown menus.
4. Enter the incident status and outcome.
5. Fill in any additional comments or notes as necessary.
6. Review and submit the form for review and investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Accident Date** (`id: 2`, `type: date`, `required: true`): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time In** (`id: 3`, `type: time`, `required: true`): Enter the start time of the incident.
* **Time Out** (`id: 4`, `type: time`, `required: true`): Enter the end time of the incident.
* **Break In** (`id: 5`, `type: time`, `required: false`): Enter the break time, if applicable.
* **Incident Description** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Location** (`id: 7`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`id: 8`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: true`): Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **Status** (`id: 9`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the status of the incident.
* **Employee Name** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the employee's name involved in the incident.
* **Employee Number** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the employee's number associated with the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Supervisor** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the incident supervisor.
* **Incident Manager** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the incident manager.
* **Incident Investigator** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the incident investigator.
* **Review Status** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the review status of the incident.
* **Closed** (`id: 17`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select whether the incident is closed or not.
* **Reason** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the reason for the incident.
* **Outcome** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the outcome of the incident.
* **Comments** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any comments from the supervisor.
* **Investigator Comments** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any comments from the investigator.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Manager Comments** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Submitted By** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the incident supervisor or manager for guidance.
* This form is intended to support and enhance existing reporting and investigation procedures, not replace them.
* Please review and submit the form for review and investigation to ensure accurate and timely resolution of the incident.
