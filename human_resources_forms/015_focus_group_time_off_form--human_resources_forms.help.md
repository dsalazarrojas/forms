# Focus Group Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on time-off or time-off-related matters from focus groups. It's an essential tool for gathering data on focus group time off, which can help organizations make informed decisions and improve their operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button on the top-right corner of the form.
2.  Enter your responses to the questions provided on each page.
3.  Make sure to fill in all the fields, as marked with an asterisk (\*).
4.  Review your responses before submitting the form.
5.  Click the "Submit" button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Focus Group Time Off** (`focus_group_time_off_1`, text, optional): This field is for entering a brief description of the time-off or time-off-related matter you want to report.
*   **Focus Group Time Off** (`focus_group_time_off_1`, text, optional): This field is for entering additional details about the time-off or time-off-related matter you want to report.
*   **Focus Group Time Off** (`focus_group_time_off_2`, text, optional): This field is for entering further information about the time-off or time-off-related matter you want to report.
*   **Focus Group Time Off** (`focus_group_time_off_3`, text, optional): This field is for entering more specifics about the time-off or time-off-related matter you want to report.
*   **Focus Group Time Off** (`focus_group_time_off_4`, text, optional): This field is for entering more details about the time-off or time-off-related matter you want to report.
*   **Focus Group Time Off** (`focus_group_time_off_5`, text, optional): This field is for entering even more details about the time-off or time-off-related matter you want to report.
*   **Focus Group Time Off** (`focus_group_time_off_6`, text, optional): This field is for entering final details about the time-off or time-off-related matter you want to report.
