# Job Pre Employment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor and record the progress of new hires through the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct job pre-employment checklist for the new hire (Pre-hire Tasks).
2. Indicate whether or not you have completed the pre-hire tasks (Pre-hire Tasks).
3. Select whether or not you have uploaded any supporting documents (Supporting Documents).
4. Choose the onboarding status (Onboarding Readiness).
5. Add any additional notes about the job offer details (Job Offer Details).
6. Specify the status of the job pre-employment checklist (Job Pre-employment Checklist Status).
7. Indicate whether or not the supporting documents have been uploaded (Supporting Documents Uploaded).
8. Enter the date the form was submitted (Date Submitted).
9. Specify the person who submitted the form (Submitted By).
10. Confirm whether or not the form was submitted on time (Submitted On).
11. Set the status of the job pre-employment checklist (Job Pre-employment Checklist Status).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre-hire Tasks**: Indicate the pre-hire tasks that need to be completed before the new hire can start working.
* **Supporting Documents**: Select whether or not you have uploaded any supporting documents required for the new hire.
* **Onboarding Readiness**: Choose the onboarding status of the new hire, which can be "Yes" or "No".
* **Job Offer Details**: Add any additional notes about the job offer details, but this field is not required.
* **Job Pre-employment Checklist Notes**: Add any additional notes about the job pre-employment checklist.
* **Supporting Documents Uploaded**: Indicate whether or not you have uploaded the required supporting documents.
* **Job Pre-employment Status**: Choose the status of the job pre-employment checklist, which can be "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By**: Specify the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On**: Indicate whether or not the form was submitted on time.
* **Job Pre-employment Checklist Status**: Set the status of the job pre-employment checklist, which can be "Active" or "Inactive".
