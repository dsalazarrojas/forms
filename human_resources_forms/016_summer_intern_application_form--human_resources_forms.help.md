# Summer Intern Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for the summer internship program. It is intended for students and recent graduates who are interested in applying for a summer internship opportunity. Please fill out this form to demonstrate your interest in the program and provide required information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Be as accurate as possible with the information provided.
* Ensure that your email address is correctly formatted.
* For the "Availability" field, select either "Full-time" or "Part-time" to indicate your preferred work schedule.
* For the "Major or Field of Study" field, enter your relevant coursework or academic background.
* For the "Start Date", "End Date", and "Duration" fields, enter the dates in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* For the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, enter the times in the format 12:00 AM - 12:00 PM.
* For the "Period" field, select either "Summer" or "Fall" to indicate the semester you prefer to start the internship.
* For the "Resume" field, attach your resume in a PDF or Word document format.
* For the "Transcripts" field, attach your transcripts in a PDF format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (Date, Optional): Enter your expected graduation date if you are currently enrolled in a degree program.
* **Availability** (Select One, Required): Select either "Full-time" or "Part-time" to indicate your preferred work schedule.
* **Major or Field of Study** (Text, Optional): Enter your major or field of study that aligns with the internship opportunity.
* **Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of your preferred internship period.
* **End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of your preferred internship period.
* **Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the length of your desired internship period in weeks.
* **Start Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the start time of your preferred work hours.
* **End Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the end time of your preferred work hours.
* **Period** (Select One, Required): Select either "Summer" or "Fall" to indicate the semester you prefer to start the internship.
* **References** (Note, Optional): Provide any relevant information about your academic or professional references.
* **Resume** (Text, Optional): Attach your resume in a PDF or Word document format.
* **Transcripts** (Text, Optional): Attach your transcripts in a PDF format.
