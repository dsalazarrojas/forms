# Sales Person Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for sales people to apply for sales positions within the organization. It collects information about your personal details, work experience, and availability so that we can review and contact you for potential job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) with your accurate and truthful information.
2. Choose your preferences from the select options for "Job Position", "Education Level", "Work Experience", and "Language".
3. Select your available time, date, and hours from the respective fields.
4. Attach your resume to the "Resume" field.
5. Click "Submit" to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* **First Name** (`text`, *) - Enter your first name.

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name** (`text`, *) - Enter your last name.

### Email (3)
* **Email** (`email`, *) - Enter your email address.

### Phone Number (4)
* **Phone Number** (`text`, *) - Enter your phone number.

### Job Position (5)
* **Job Position** (`select_one`, *) - Choose your desired job position from the list.

### Resume (6)
* **Resume** (`text`, *) - Attach your resume to demonstrate your experience and qualifications.

### Work Experience (7)
* **Work Experience** (`select_multiple`, *) - Choose your work experience from the list.

### Education Level (8)
* **Education Level** (`select_one`, *) - Choose your education level from the list.

### Available Time (9)
* **Available Time** (`time`, optional) - Select your available time in 24-hour format, e.g., 09:00-17:00.

### Available Date (10)
* **Available Date** (`date`, optional) - Select your available date.

### Available Days (11)
* **Available Days** (`text`, optional) - Enter your available days.

### Skills (12)
* **Skills** (`text`, optional) - List your skills.

### Language (13)
* **Language** (`select_one`, optional) - Choose your language from the list.

### Nationality (14)
* **Nationality** (`select_one`, optional) - Choose your nationality from the list.

### Available Hours (15)
* **Available Hours** (`select_multiple`, *) - Choose your available hours from the list.

### Job Description (16)
* **Job Description** (`text`, *) - Describe your job experience and qualifications.

### Job Location (17)
* **Job Location** (`text`, *) - Enter your job location.

### Salary Range (18)
* **Salary Range** (`text`, optional) - Enter your expected salary range.

### Available Salary (19)
* **Available Salary** (`text`, optional) - Enter your available salary.

### Job Type (20)
* **Job Type** (`select_one`, optional) - Choose your job type from the list.

### Employment Status (21)
* **Employment Status** (`select_one`, *) - Indicate your employment status.

### Education Institution (22)
* **Education Institution** (`text`, optional) - Enter your education institution.

### Graduate Degree (23)
* **Graduate Degree** (`select_one`, optional) - Indicate if you have a graduate degree.

### Job Status (24)
* **Job Status** (`select_one`, *) - Indicate your job status.

### Submitted By (25)
* **Submitted By** (`text`, optional) - Enter your name as submitted by the user.
