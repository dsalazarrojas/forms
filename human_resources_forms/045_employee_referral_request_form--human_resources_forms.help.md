# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about job postings and job openings, including job titles, job types, departments, job levels, job categories, job status, employee referrals, email, phone numbers, notes, submitted by information, submitted dates and times, job descriptions, job requirements, and job openings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the job posting, including job title, job description, job requirements, and job opening details.
2. Enter the employee name, job title, job open date, job level, job category, job type, and job status.
3. Select the department and job requirements from the provided options.
4. Enter the employee referral information, if applicable.
5. Enter the email and phone number of the submitted by person.
6. Enter the submitted date and time.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the job posting.
8. Enter the job description, job requirements, and job opening details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (`employee_name`, text, required)**: Enter the name of the employee who is submitting the job posting.
* **Job Title (`job_title`, text, required)**: Enter the title of the job posting.
* **Job Open Date (`job_open_date`, date, required)**: Enter the date the job is open.
* **Job Level (`job_level`, number, required)**: Enter the level of the job posting.
* **Job Type (`job_type`, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of job posting (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract).
* **Department (`department`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the department where the job is located.
* **Job Category (`job_category`, select_one, optional)**: Select the category of the job posting.
* **Job Status (`job_status`, select_one, optional)**: Select the status of the job posting (e.g., available, filled).
* **Employee Referral (`employee_referral`, select_multiple, optional)**: Enter any employee who referred the job posting.
* **Email (`email`, email, required)**: Enter the email of the submitted by person.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of the submitted by person.
* **Note (`note`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the job posting.
* **Submitted By (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional)**: Select the person who submitted the job posting.
* **Submitted Date (`submitted_date`, date, required)**: Enter the date the job posting was submitted.
* **Submitted Time (`submitted_time`, time, optional)**: Enter the time the job posting was submitted.
* **Submitted By Phone (`submitted_by_phone`, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the job posting.
* **Submitted By Email (`submitted_by_email`, email, optional)**: Enter the email of the person who submitted the job posting.
* **Submitted Date and Time (`submitted_date_time`, text, optional)**: Enter the date and time the job posting was submitted.
* **Job Description (`job_description`, text, required)**: Enter a brief description of the job posting.
* **Job Requirements (`job_requirements`, text, required)**: Enter any job requirements or qualifications.
* **Job Requirements List (`job_requirements_list`, text, required)**: Enter a list of job requirements or qualifications.
* **Job Requirements Important (`job_requirements_important`, text, optional)**: Enter any important job requirements or qualifications.
* **Job Opening (`job_opening`, text, required)**: Enter any details about the job opening.
* **Employee Suggest (`employee_suggest`, text, optional)**: Enter any employee suggestions or recommendations.
* **Comments (`comments`, text, required)**: Enter any comments or notes about the job posting.
