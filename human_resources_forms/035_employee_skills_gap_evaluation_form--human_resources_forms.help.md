# Employee Skills Gap Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Skills Gap Evaluation Form is a tool used by HR departments to evaluate the skills gaps of employees within the organization. It helps identify areas where employees need training or support to perform their jobs effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's job title.
2. Enter the employee's name as it appears on their ID card.
3. Enter the manager's name as it appears on their ID card.
4. Select the relevant job family for the employee's role.
5. Choose the job functions that are relevant to the employee's role.
6. List specific skills or areas of knowledge needed for the employee's job.
7. Select the type of training required for the skills gaps identified.
8. Check the status of the training for each skill gap.
9. Enter the date of the evaluation.
10. Choose the time of the evaluation.
11. Enter the employee's ID.
12. Select the employee's status.
13. Select the job status.
14. Enter the date of the job status change.
15. Select the manager's status.
16. Enter the date of the manager status change.
17. Select the job function status.
18. Enter the date of the job function status change.
19. Select the employee's performance level.
20. Select the job's performance level.
21. Enter the date of the last performance evaluation.
22. Choose the frequency of performance evaluations.
23. Select the time for the next evaluation.
24. Select the frequency of evaluations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title as it appears on their ID card.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their ID card.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the manager's name as it appears on their ID card.
* **Job Family**: Select the relevant job family for the employee's role.
* **Job Functions**: Choose the job functions that are relevant to the employee's role.
* **Skills Needed**: List specific skills or areas of knowledge needed for the employee's job.
* **Training Needed**: Select the type of training required for the skills gaps identified.
* **Training Status**: Check the status of the training for each skill gap.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID.
* **Employee Status**: Select the employee's status (Active, Inactive, Retired, or Other).
* **Job Status**: Select the job status (Active, Inactive, Open, or Other).
* **Job Status Date**: Enter the date of the job status change.
* **Manager ID**: Enter the manager's ID.
* **Manager Status**: Select the manager's status (Active, Inactive, Open, or Other).
* **Manager Status Date**: Enter the date of the manager status change.
* **Job Function Status**: Select the job function status (Active, Inactive, Open, or Other).
* **Job Function Status Date**: Enter the date of the job function status change.
* **Employee Performance**: Select the employee's performance level (Satisfactory, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory).
* **Job Performance**: Select the job's performance level (Satisfactory, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory).
* **Job Performance Date**: Enter the date of the last performance evaluation.
* **Performance Evaluation Cycle**: Choose the frequency of performance evaluations (Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **Next Evaluation**: Select the time for the next evaluation (30, 60, 90 days, etc.).
* **Evaluation Frequency**: Select the frequency of evaluations (Quarterly, Semiannually, Annually).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check the job family, job functions, and training needed for each employee.
* Select the correct status and performance levels for each employee and job.
* Use the evaluation date and time for each evaluation session.
* Use the next evaluation time to schedule future evaluations.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure it reflects the current status of each employee and job.
