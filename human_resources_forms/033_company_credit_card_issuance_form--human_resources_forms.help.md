# Company Credit Card Issuance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a company credit card for business use. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, employee ID, email, phone number, department, job title, manager's name, and email.
2. Select the type of card request (New Card, Replacement Card, Limit Increase, Additional Card).
3. Choose the type of card you need (Standard Corporate Card, Gold Corporate Card, Platinum Corporate Card, Purchasing Card (P-Card), Travel Card, Executive Card).
4. Estimate your expected monthly spend.
5. Select your primary use of the card (e.g. Travel expenses, Client entertainment, Office supplies, etc.).
6. Select all relevant expense categories for your card use.
7. If you have had a corporate card before, indicate if there were any issues with the previous card.
8. Choose how you would like the card to be delivered (e.g. Mail to home address, Mail to office, Pick up at HR, etc.).
9. Enter your home address if the card will be mailed to your home.
10. Enter the name as it should appear on the card.
11. Agree to the card policy, personal use, timely reporting of expenses, card security, and consequences of misuse.
12. If you have a manager, they will be prompted to review and approve your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Enter your first and last name.
* **Employee ID Number** (2): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your company email.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your contact number.
* **Department** (5): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (6): Enter your job title.
* **Manager/Supervisor Name** (7): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (8): Enter your manager's email.
* **Hire Date** (9): Enter the date you were hired.
* **Employment Status** (10): Select your employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Temporary).
* **Request Type** (11): Choose the type of card request (New Card, Replacement Card, Limit Increase, Additional Card).
* **Type of Card Requested** (12): Choose the type of card you need (Standard Corporate Card, Gold Corporate Card, Platinum Corporate Card, Purchasing Card (P-Card), Travel Card, Executive Card).
* **Business Justification** (13): Enter why you need a corporate card.
* **Expected Monthly Spend** (14): Estimate your expected monthly spend.
* **Requested Credit Limit** (15): Enter the requested credit limit in dollars.
* **Primary Use of Card** (16): Select your primary use of the card (e.g. Travel expenses, Client entertainment, Office supplies, etc.).
* **Expense Categories** (17): Select all relevant expense categories for your card use (e.g. Airfare, Hotel, Car rental, etc.).
* **Have you had a corporate card before?** (18): Indicate if you have had a corporate card before.
* **If yes, were there any issues with previous card?** (19): Enter any issues with the previous card.
* **How should the card be delivered?** (20): Choose how you would like the card to be delivered (e.g. Mail to home address, Mail to office, Pick up at HR, etc.).
* **Home Address** (21): Enter your home address if the card will be mailed to your home.
* **Name as it should appear on card** (22): Enter the name as it should appear on the card.
* **I have read and understand the corporate card policy** (23): Agree to the card policy.
* **I understand the card is for business use only** (24): Agree that the card is for business use only.
* **I agree to submit expense reports within required timeframe** (25): Agree to timely report expenses.
* **I agree to keep the card secure and report loss immediately** (26): Agree to keep the card secure and report loss immediately.
* **I agree to keep all receipts and documentation** (27): Agree to keep all receipts and documentation.
* **I understand the consequences of card misuse** (28): Agree to the consequences of card misuse.
* **Manager Approval** (29): If you have a manager, they will review and approve your request.
* **Manager Approval Status** (30): Select the manager's approval status (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Manager Comments** (31): Enter any manager comments.
* **Finance Review** (32): This section is for finance review.
* **Finance Approval** (33): Select the finance approval status (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Card Issued Date** (34): Enter the date the card was issued.
* **Card Last Four Digits** (35): Enter the last four digits of the card number.
* **Employee Signature** (36): Sign the form electronically.
* **Request Date** (37): Enter the date of the request.
