# Employee Engagement Survey Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a survey from your manager to gather feedback on your performance and engagement within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required information, and a survey will be generated and shared with your manager. Please note that only the required fields are marked as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title to help your manager understand your role within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Select the department you belong to for more accurate feedback.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name for direct feedback.
* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`, text, optional): List 3-5 questions you would like to ask in the survey, e.g., "What can I improve on?" or "What are my strengths and weaknesses?"
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your work email address to ensure accurate communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your work phone number for easy contact.
* **Date Last Survey** (`date_last_survey`, date, optional): If you have previously submitted a survey, provide the date of your last one for context.
* **Time Last Survey** (`time_last_survey`, time, optional): If you have previously submitted a survey, provide the time of your last one for context.

## Tips
* Make sure to list specific, actionable questions that are open-ended and allow for in-depth feedback.
* Keep your questions concise and clear.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your manager or HR representative for guidance.
