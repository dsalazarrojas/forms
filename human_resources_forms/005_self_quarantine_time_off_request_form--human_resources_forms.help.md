# Self-Quarantine Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Self-Quarantine Time Off Request Form, a formal request for employees to take time off due to self-quarantine or family members who require isolation.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please note that this form requires approval from your supervisor or HR representative before it can be submitted.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID card or in our HR system.
- **Start Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date you would like to take your time off from.
- **End Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date you would like to return to work.
- **Reason** (Select One, Required): Choose the reason for your time off:
    - **Self Isolating**: You or an immediate family member has been diagnosed with or is at risk of contracting a contagious illness.
    - **Family Member Isolating**: A family member is being self-quarantined or isolated due to health concerns.
    - **Other** (Please provide a specific reason for your time off in this option)
- **Supervisor Name** (Text, Optional): If you would like to provide your supervisor's name, please do so. This will ensure that your supervisor receives the notification of your time off request.
- **Manager Email** (Email, Optional): If you have a specific email address for your supervisor, please provide it. This is not required but can aid in faster communication.
- **Manager Phone** (Text, Optional): If you have a direct phone number for your supervisor, please provide it. This is not required but can aid in faster communication.
- **Manager Comments** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns for your supervisor, please provide them here.
- **Employee Comments** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.
- **Submitted By** (Text, Required): This field will automatically be populated with your name.
- **Submitted At** (Time, Optional): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time you submitted the form.
- **Updated By** (Text, Optional): This field will automatically be populated with the name of the person who last updated the form.
- **Updated At** (Time, Optional): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time of the last update.

Please note that while some fields may seem optional, providing accurate and complete information in all fields can aid in a smoother approval and communication process.
