# Dog Policy Update Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request an update to the existing dog policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" next to "dog_policy_update_request" to indicate whether you are requesting an update to the current dog policy.
2. Enter the name of the employee requesting the update in the "employee_requesting" field.
3. Enter the name of the new pet (if applicable) in the "new_pet_name" field.
4. Provide a reason for the request in the "reason_for_request" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" next to "manager_approval" to indicate whether the manager's approval is required for the update.
6. If the manager's approval is required, enter their name in the "manager_name" field.
7. If the manager's approval is required, enter their email address in the "manager_email" field.
8. Enter any additional dog details in the "dog_details" field.
9. Select the department affected by the update from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dog_policy_update_request**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you are requesting an update to the current dog policy, or "No" if you are not requesting an update.
* **employee_requesting**: Enter the name of the employee requesting the update.
* **new_pet_name**: Enter the name of the new pet (if applicable).
* **reason_for_request**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason behind your request.
* **manager_approval**: Select "Yes" to indicate that your manager's approval is required for the update, or "No" if it is not required.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of your manager if their approval is required.
* **manager_email**: Enter the email address of your manager if their approval is required.
* **dog_details**: Enter any additional details about the dog, such as its size, breed, or health issues.
* **department**: Select the department that will be affected by the update to the dog policy.
