# employee_advocate_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_advocate_time_off_form is used to record and track employee time off requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. If a field is optional, provide the relevant information if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Time off Request** (`time_off_request`, number, required): Enter the ID of the time off request being logged.
- **Date of Departure** (`date_of_departure`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee will be leaving for the time off.
- **Date of Return** (`date_of_return`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee will return from the time off.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee requesting the time off.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the employee requesting the time off.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the employee requesting the time off.
- **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, text, optional): Enter the manager's approval or rejection of the time off request.
- **Time off Reason** (`time_off_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the employee's time off request.
- **Time off Start** (`time_off_start`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the employee's time off.
- **Time off End** (`time_off_end`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the employee's time off.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your HR representative for clarification.
