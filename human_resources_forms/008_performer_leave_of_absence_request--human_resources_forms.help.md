# Performer Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to support Performers in requesting a leave of absence for various reasons, including medical, personal, or other types of leaves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the reason for your leave from the list of options provided.
2. Enter your leave start and end dates.
3. Specify the duration of your leave.
4. Provide contact information for yourself and any other relevant contacts.
5. Enter any additional details or comments.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your request for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **leave_start_date**: Enter the date from which you would like to start your leave.
* **leave_end_date**: Enter the date on which you would like to end your leave.
* **leave_duration**: Enter the number of days or hours you plan to take for your leave.
* **reason_for_leave**: Choose the reason for your leave from the provided options, such as Medical, Personal, Pandemic, etc.
* **contact_name**: Enter the name of the contact person for your leave.
* **contact_email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact_note**: Enter any additional information for the contact person.
* **performer_details**: Enter any additional details about your leave.
* **performer_name**: Enter your name as the Performer.
* **performer_email**: Enter your email address.
* **performer_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **performer_note**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **leave_status**: Select the current status of your leave request (Approved, Declined, Pending, Rejected).
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments about your leave request.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of the manager or person responsible for your leave.
* **manager_email**: Enter the email address of the manager.
* **manager_phone**: Enter the phone number of the manager.
* **manager_note**: Enter any additional information about the manager.
* **approver_name**: Enter the name of the person approving your leave.
* **approver_email**: Enter the email address of the approver.
* **approver_phone**: Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **approver_note**: Enter any additional information about the approver.
* **submit_button**: Click this button to send your leave request for approval.
