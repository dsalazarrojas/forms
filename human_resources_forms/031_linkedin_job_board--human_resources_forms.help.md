# linkedin_job_board - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LinkedIn job board form is used to collect and share job posting information within a company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Company Name** in the first field. This will help identify the company posting the job.
2. Provide the **Job Title** in the second field.
3. Select the **Employment Type** from the options provided: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Freelance, or Intern.
4. Choose one or multiple **Job Categories** from the available options: IT, Software Development, Marketing, Finance.
5. Enter the **Salary Range** for the job.
6. Add a **Job Description** that summarizes the job posting.
7. Specify **Job Requirements**, including any necessary skills or qualifications.
8. List the **Benefits** offered by the company.
9. Enter the **Location** where the job is based.
10. Provide **Contact Information** for applicants to reach out to.
11. Select the **Job Status**: Open, Filled, or Closed.
12. Enter the **Date Posted** and **Time Posted** for the job posting.
13. Set an **Expiration Date** and **Expiration Time** for the job posting.
14. Choose the **Assigned To**: HR, Manager, or Team Lead.
15. Select a **Posted By** user or department.
16. Add any additional **Notes** about the job posting.
17. Choose who the job is **Assigned To**: HR, Manager, or Team Lead.
18. Set a **Due Date** for the job completion.
19. Enter the **Created By** user or department.
20. Set the **Created Date** for the job posting.
21. Enter the **Last Updated** date for the job posting.
22. Enter the **Updated By** user or department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the company posting the job.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief title for the job.
* **Employment Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of employment, such as Full-time, Part-time, etc.
* **Job Category** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or multiple categories for the job posting.
* **Salary Range** (Number, Optional): Enter the salary range for the job.
* **Job Description** (Text, Optional): Summarize the job posting in a few sentences.
* **Job Requirements** (Text, Optional): List the necessary skills or qualifications for the job.
* **Benefits** (Select Multiple, Optional): List the benefits offered by the company.
* **Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where the job is based.
* **Contact Info** (Text, Optional): Provide contact information for applicants to reach out to.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address for contact information.
* **Date Posted** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the job was posted.
* **Time Posted** (Time, Optional): Enter the time the job was posted.
* **Expiration Date** (Date, Optional): Set an expiration date for the job posting.
* **Expiration Time** (Time, Optional): Set an expiration time for the job posting.
* **Job Status** (Select One, Optional): Select whether the job is Open, Filled, or Closed.
* **Posted By** (Text, Optional): Select the user or department that posted the job.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the job posting.
* **Assigned To** (Select One, Optional): Select who the job is assigned to: HR, Manager, or Team Lead.
* **Due Date** (Date, Optional): Enter a due date for the job completion.
* **Created By** (Text, Optional): Enter the user or department that created the job posting.
* **Created Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the job was created.
* **Last Updated** (Date, Optional): Enter the last updated date for the job posting.
* **Updated By** (Text, Optional): Enter the user or department that updated the job posting.
