# Retail Employee Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Employee Schedule Request Form is used to request changes to your retail work schedule. This form helps ensure that all schedule changes are accurately recorded and communicated to management and other employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. On the "Schedule Changes" page, provide a brief description of the schedule change you are requesting.
3. Select the "Start Date" of the schedule change.
4. Select the "End Date" of the schedule change.
5. Enter the number of hours you will be working on the specific dates.
6. Choose your availability from the dropdown menu.
7. Add any additional notes about your request.
8. Enter your email address and contact number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Changes**: Describe the specific schedule change you are requesting. Please be as detailed as possible.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required.
* **Start Date**: Select the date you would like your schedule change to start on.
	+ Type: Date field.
	+ Required.
* **End Date**: Select the date your schedule change will end on.
	+ Type: Date field.
	+ Required.
* **Work Hours**: Enter the number of hours you will be working on the specific dates.
	+ Type: Number field.
	+ Required.
* **Availability**: Choose when you are available to work (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend).
	+ Type: Dropdown menu.
	+ Required.
* **Notes**: Add any additional details or context to your request.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Optional.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Type: Email field.
	+ Required.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required.
