# Recruitment Team Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Team Accommodation Request Form is a tool used to collect information about team members' accommodation needs. It helps in keeping track of these requests and their current status, ensuring that accommodation arrangements are made efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by selecting the **Job Title** for which you are requesting accommodation. If you're unsure, please check with your team lead or manager for guidance.
2. Choose the **Accommodation Type** that suits your needs from the available options (Hotel, Hostel, Other).
3. Enter your **Arrival Date**, when you plan to arrive for the job assignment.
4. If needed, select your **Start Time** and **End Time** for your job assignment.
5. Specify the **Duration** of your assignment, in days.
6. If you've made a booking for a prophet, enter their name under **Prophet 1**, **Prophet 2**, and **Prophet 3** fields.
7. Provide any additional **Accommodation Details** if necessary.
8. Add any **Comments** about your accommodation request.
9. Finally, fill in the **Submitted By** field with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job for which you are requesting accommodation.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the type of accommodation you need (Hotel, Hostel, Other).
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you plan to arrive for the job assignment.
* **Start Time**: If applicable, enter the time you plan to start your job assignment.
* **End Time**: If applicable, enter the time you plan to end your job assignment.
* **Duration**: Enter the number of days you will be on the job assignment.
* **Prophet 1/2/3**: If you've made a booking for a prophet, enter their name here.
* **Accommodation Details**: Provide any additional details about your accommodation needs.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about your accommodation request.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name, as this form is submitted on your behalf.
