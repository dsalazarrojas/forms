# HR Onboarding Process Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Onboarding Process Audit Form is used to ensure that new employees are properly onboarded into the company and to identify potential areas for improvement in the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used to contact you for any further information.
3. Provide your Date of Birth.
4. Enter your Manager's Name and Department.
5. If applicable, indicate the date you joined the team.
6. If applicable, provide your Supervisor's Name.
7. Enter the date you completed the onboarding process.
8. Enter your Employee ID.
9. If applicable, enter your Job Title, Job Classification, and Job Level.
10. Select your Team Member Status.
11. Select your Job Classification Type (Management, Non-Exempt, Exempt).
12. If applicable, select your Job Grade Type (Non-Exempt, Exempt, Overtime).
13. Enter your Job Family.
14. If applicable, enter your Supervisor's Name 2.
15. Enter your Team.
16. Add any additional Notes about your onboarding experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is spelled on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is spelled on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your Email address, which will be used to contact you for any further information.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your direct manager's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department.
* **Team Member Since** (`team_member_since`, time, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date you joined the team.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the onboarding process.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique Employee ID.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, text, required/optional): Enter your job classification.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, number, required/optional): Enter your job level.
* **Team Member Status** (`team_member_status`, select_one, required): Select your team member status (Active, Inactive, Resigned).
* **Job Classification Type** (`job_classification_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your job classification type (Management, Non-Exempt, Exempt).
* **Job Grade Type** (`job_grade_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your job grade type (Non-Exempt, Exempt, Overtime).
* **Job Family** (`job_family`, text, required/optional): Enter your job family.
* **Supervisor Name 2** (`supervisor_name_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's name 2.
* **Team** (`team`, text, required/optional): Enter your team.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your onboarding experience.

Note: The fields marked as "required" or "required/optional" are mandatory and should be filled in by you. If you are unsure about any field, please contact HR department for clarification.
