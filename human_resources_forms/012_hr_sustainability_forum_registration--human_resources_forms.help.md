# HR Sustainability Forum Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the HR Sustainability Forum. We will use this information to plan and tailor the sessions according to your interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- You may skip fields that are not relevant to your interests.
- Review your submission for errors before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. User Input
* **User Input** (`<text>`, **optional**):
	+ Please provide a brief description of your interests and needs for the HR Sustainability Forum.

### 2. Name
* **Name** (`<text>`, **optional**):
	+ Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the forum.

### 3. Email
* **Email** (`<email>`, **optional**):
	+ Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding the forum.

### 4. Phone
* **Phone** (`<text>`, **optional**):
	+ Enter your phone number for any potential follow-up questions or session reminders.

### 5. Session Interest
* **Session Interest** (`<select_one>`, **optional**):
	+ Choose one or more sessions that interest you from the available options.

### 6. Date
* **Date** (`<date>`, **optional**):
	+ Select the date you are interested in attending the forum.

### 7. Time
* **Time** (`<time>`, **optional**):
	+ Choose the time slot you are interested in attending the forum.

### 8. Comments
* **Comments** (`<note>`, **optional**):
	+ Share any additional comments or questions you may have regarding the forum sessions.

### 9. Confirm Email
* **Confirm Email** (`<email>`, **optional**):
	+ Re-enter your email address to confirm its accuracy.

### 10. Confirm Phone
* **Confirm Phone** (`<text>`, **optional**):
	+ Re-enter your phone number for confirmation purposes.

Note that fields marked as **optional** are not required for submission, but we recommend filling them out for a more complete user experience.
