# Film Production Family Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help crew members request a family leave of absence for various reasons. It allows you to provide necessary information for your production team and other relevant parties to ensure a smooth transition of work during your absence.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled in. You can refer to the field-by-field explanation for more information on what each field means.
2. Submit the form to your production manager or other relevant parties for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Leave Request** (`film_production_family_leave_request`): This is the main reason for your family leave request. Please provide a clear and concise description of the reason for your absence.
* **Crew Member Name** (`crew_member_name`): This is your name as a crew member, which is required for identification purposes.
* **Family Member Relationship** (`family_member_relationship`): Please select how you are related to the family member(s) involved in the reason for your leave.
	+ Mother
	+ Father
	+ Spouse
	+ Parent
	+ Child
	+ Brother/Sister
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`): This is the start date of your family leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`): This is the end date of your family leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`): Please provide more details about the reason for your absence.
* **Production Location** (`production_location`): If you are working on a specific production, please provide its location.
* **Department** (`department`): This is the department you belong to within the production.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): This is your contact number that production and other relevant parties can reach you at.
* **Email** (`email`): This is your email address that production and other relevant parties can contact you at.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`): If you need to provide any additional information for the production team, please use this field.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`): Please select the status of your manager's approval.
	+ Approved
	+ Declined
	+ Pending
* **Production Manager Approval** (`production_manager_approval`): This is the status of your production manager's approval.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Human Resources Approval** (`human_resources_approval`): This is the status of your human resources department's approval.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): This is the date you submitted this form.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`): This is the time you submitted this form.
* **Production Schedule** (`production_schedule`): If you have a production schedule, please provide it.
* **Crew Schedule** (`crew_schedule`): If you have a crew schedule, please provide it.
* **Location** (`location`): This is the location where you are currently working or will be working during your family leave.
* **Crew ID** (`crew_id`): This is your unique crew ID.
* **Crew Name** (`crew_name`): This is your crew name.
* **Crew Number** (`crew_number`): This is your crew number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): This is the start date of your family leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): This is the end date of your family leave.
* **Production Notes** (`production_notes`): Please provide any additional production notes if necessary.
