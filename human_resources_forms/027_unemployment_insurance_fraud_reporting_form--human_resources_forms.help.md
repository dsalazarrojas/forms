# Unemployment Insurance Fraud Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting unemployment insurance fraud incidents. It is used by HR or other authorized personnel to collect facts about the incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

### Reporting Information
Please provide a brief description of the fraud incident, including any relevant details such as date, location, and circumstances.

### Suspect Information
Provide information about the individual or entity suspected of committing the fraud. This can include their name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Employer Information
Enter the name and contact information of the employer involved in the fraud incident.

### Employee Information
Enter the name and contact information of the employee affected by the fraud incident.

### Type of Fraud
Select the type of fraud that occurred, such as Bankruptcy, Embezzlement, Identity theft, or Misrepresentation.

### Date, Time, and Location of Fraud
Enter the date, time, and location of the fraud incident.

### Description of Fraud
Provide a detailed description of the fraud incident, including any relevant details.

### Supporting Information
Enter any additional information that supports the claim of fraud, such as witness statements or supporting documentation.

### Submission Information
Click "Submit" to complete the form and submit your report.

### Witness Information (Optional)
If a witness is available, provide their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please complete the form with as much detail as possible to support your claim.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your HR representative for guidance.
