# Unemployment Insurance Fraud Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting unemployment insurance fraud incidents. It is used by HR or other authorized personnel to collect facts about the incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

### Reporting Information
Please provide a brief description of the fraud incident, including any relevant details such as date, location, and circumstances.

### Suspect Information
Provide information about the individual or entity suspected of committing the fraud. This can include their name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Employer Information
Enter the name and contact information of the employer involved in the fraud incident.

### Employee Information
Enter the name and contact information of the employee affected by the fraud incident.

### Type of Fraud
Select the type of fraud that occurred, such as Bankruptcy, Embezzlement, Identity theft, or Misrepresentation.

### Date, Time, and Location of Fraud
Enter the date, time, and location of the fraud incident.

### Description of Fraud
Provide a detailed description of the fraud incident, including any relevant details.

### Supporting Information
Enter any additional information that supports the claim of fraud, such as witness statements or supporting documentation.

### Submission Information
Click "Submit" to complete the form and submit your report.

### Witness Information (Optional)
If a witness is available, provide their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please complete the form with as much detail as possible to support your claim.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporting Form** (Reporting Form, text, required): Provide a brief description of the fraud incident.
* **Suspect Information** (Suspect Information, text, required): Provide information about the individual or entity suspected of committing the fraud.
* **Employer Name** (Employer Name, text, required): Enter the name of the employer involved in the fraud incident.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter the name of the employee affected by the fraud incident.
* **Type of Fraud** (Type of Fraud, select_one, required): Select the type of fraud that occurred.
* **Date of Fraud** (Date of Fraud, date, required): Enter the date of the fraud incident.
* **Time of Fraud** (Time of Fraud, time, required): Enter the time of the fraud incident.
* **Location of Fraud** (Location of Fraud, text, required): Enter the location of the fraud incident.
* **Description of Fraud** (Description of Fraud, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the fraud incident.
* **Supporting Information** (Supporting Information, text, required): Enter any additional information that supports the claim of fraud.
* **Submit** (Submit, note, required): Click "Submit" to complete the form and submit your report.
* **Employer Phone** (Employer Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employer involved in the fraud incident.
* **Employer Email** (Employer Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the employer involved in the fraud incident.
* **Employee Email** (Employee Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the employee affected by the fraud incident.
* **Employer Address** (Employer Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the employer involved in the fraud incident.
* **Employee Address** (Employee Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the employee affected by the fraud incident.
* **Witness Information** (Witness Information, text, optional): If a witness is available, provide their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Witness Name** (Witness Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness.
* **Witness Phone** (Witness Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the witness.
* **Witness Email** (Witness Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the witness.
