# return_to_work_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The return to work form is used to collect information from employees returning to their work station after an extended absence. This form is a crucial tool for HR and management to track employee returns, ensuring a smooth transition back to work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the Company Name field.
2. Select the Date you are returning to work from the calendar.
3. Choose one of the reasons for returning from the Reason Returning field.
4. Enter the contact person's email address in the Contact Person field.
5. Enter the contact person's phone number in the Contact Phone field.
6. Provide a brief description of your reason for returning in the Reason for Returning field.
7. Fill out any additional comments or feedback in the Comments fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or department.
* **Date Returning**: Select the date you are returning to work from the calendar.
* **Reason Returning**: Select one of the following reasons:
	+ Due to office re-opening
	+ Personal reasons
	+ Medical reasons
* **Contact Person**: Enter the email address of the person you would like to be contacted about your return.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person, without any leading or trailing spaces.
* **Reason for Returning**: Provide a brief description of your reason for returning to work.
* **Comments**: Fill out any additional comments or feedback as needed.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth transition back to work.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your HR or management representative for guidance.
