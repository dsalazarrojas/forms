# Employment Support Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about job seekers who are in need of employment support services. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all relevant details about the job seeker are captured accurately and consistently, so that the employment support service can provide personalized assistance effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your job seeker details, including your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
2. Select the types of support you need, such as Job Search Assistance, Resume Building, and Interview Preparation.
3. If applicable, provide your education and work experience information.
4. Specify your employment status, English level, and any other needs you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Seeker Details** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a job seeker.
* **Support Needs** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the types of support you need, such as Job Search Assistance, Resume Building, and Interview Preparation.
* **Education Background** (`3`, `number`, optional): If applicable, provide your level of education, using the numbers provided in the options.
* **Work Experience** (`4`, `number`, optional): If applicable, provide your work experience level, using the numbers provided in the options.
* **Employment Status** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Specify your current employment status, such as Unemployed, Part Time, or Full Time.
* **English Level** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your English proficiency level, such as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **English Level 2** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the previous one. If the intention is to capture additional information, consider merging it with the previous field or removing it.
* **Other Needs 1-18** (`8-19`, `text`, optional): If you have any other needs or requirements that are not listed, please describe them here.

Note: If the intention is to ask about specific skills or qualifications, consider replacing the `Other Needs` fields with more descriptive options. For example, "List your relevant skills" or "Specify your desired job qualifications".
