# Labor Rights Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labor Rights Compliance Declaration Form is a document that helps employees declare their understanding and commitment to adhere to labor laws and regulations. This form is used to ensure that employees are aware of their rights and duties under these laws.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Select the relevant labor law versions that apply to you.
3.  Enter the date of completion.
4.  Provide your manager's name and contact information.
5.  Write a declaration text that explains your understanding of labor laws and regulations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **department_id** (`department_id`, number, required: false): Enter your department ID or equivalent identifier.
* **Labor Law Version** (`labor_law_version_id`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all labor law versions that apply to you. You can select one or multiple versions.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required: false): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Declaration Text** (`declaration_text`, text, required: false): Write a brief text explaining your understanding of labor laws and regulations.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
* Update this form regularly to reflect any changes in your employment status or work environment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR representative.
