# Peer Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Peer Review Form is designed to gather feedback and comments from reviewers on a particular submission. This form helps to ensure that the submitted work is reviewed thoroughly and provides an accurate assessment of its quality and status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the reviewer in the "Reviewer Name" field.
2. Choose the date of the review in the "Review Date" field.
3. Rate the submission out of 5 in the "Rating" field.
4. If you have any comments or feedback, enter them in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Reviewer Email" field.
6. Choose the date of the submission in the "Submission Date" field.
7. Select the status of the submission from the options provided in the "Submission Status" field.
8. Select the work being reviewed from the options provided in the "Work Reviewed" field.
9. Leave the "Reviewer Signature" field empty, as this is an optional field not required for the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer Name (reviewer_name)**: Enter your name as the reviewer for the submission.
* **Review Date (review_date)**: Choose the date when you completed the review.
* **Rating (rating)**: Rate the submission out of 5.
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any additional comments or feedback for the submission.
* **Reviewer Email (reviewer_email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)**: Enter the date when the submission was made.
* **Submission Status (submission_status)**: Select the status of the submission (pending, approved, or rejected).
* **Work Reviewed (work_reviewed)**: Choose the work being reviewed from the provided options.
* **Reviewer Signature (reviewer_signature)**: Leave this field empty, as this is an optional field not required for the review process.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out this form only once for each submission.
* Be thorough and honest in your rating and comments.
* Review the submission carefully before providing feedback.
* Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion or questions.
