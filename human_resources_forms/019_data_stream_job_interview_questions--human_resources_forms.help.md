# Data Stream Job Interview Questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Stream Job Interview Questions form is designed to collect information about a candidate's job interview, including their contact details, social media links, and answers to specific questions. This form is used to gather relevant data for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's contact details, including their name, job title, company, contact number, and email.
2. Provide links to the candidate's social media profiles (LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub, and Stack Overflow).
3. Answer the questions posed by the form, choosing from the provided options or typing in your response.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required: false): Enter the candidate's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the candidate.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company the candidate works for or has worked for.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the candidate's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the candidate's LinkedIn profile.
* **Twitter** (`twitter`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the candidate's Twitter profile.
* **GitHub** (`github`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the candidate's GitHub profile.
* **Stack Overflow** (`stack_overflow`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the candidate's Stack Overflow profile.
* **Personal Website** (`personal_website`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the candidate's personal website.
* **LinkedIn URL** (`linkedin_url`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the candidate's LinkedIn profile (this can be the same as the previous field).
