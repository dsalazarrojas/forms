# Department EXIT Termination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from departing employees to help with departmental administrative and personnel management tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as follows:
1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter the Date of Termination (in date format, e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Describe your Reason for Departure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it is written on your official documents, such as identification or ID cards.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents, such as identification or ID cards.
- **Date Termination** (`date_termination`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when your departmental position was terminated (in date format, e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
- **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the reason for your departure, providing as much detail as necessary. This will help with understanding the circumstances of your exit.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out as accurately as possible to facilitate smooth processing of your exit information.
- If any field is left blank, please provide a clear note or explanation as to why it's left blank, if possible.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
