# Outdoor Dining Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Dining Application Form is designed to collect information from employees for outdoor dining purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Reservation Time field with the date and time you wish to make a reservation.
2. Provide the Reservation Date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the Form of Payment you would like to use for your reservation.
4. Enter the Establishment Name and its address in the text fields provided.
5. Indicate who completed the form, whether it's a Manager or Assistant.
6. Add any additional Notes about your reservation, if needed.
7. Enter your Contact Number for the establishment to contact you.
8. Provide your Email address so the establishment can respond to you.
9. Enter any additional comments or details about your request.
10. Add your Signature, if required.
11. Fill in your Form Submitter Name, Title, and Department (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Time** (time, required: false):
	+ Enter the date and time you would like to make your reservation.
* **Reservation Date** (date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you would like to make your reservation (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Form of Payment** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the payment method you wish to use for your reservation.
* **Establishment Name** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the establishment you wish to dine at.
* **Establishment Address** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter the address of the establishment you wish to dine at.
* **Completed By** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Indicate who completed this form, either a Manager or Assistant.
* **Notes** (note, required: false):
	+ Add any additional notes about your reservation, if needed.
* **Contact Number** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact number for the establishment to reach you.
* **Email** (email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address so the establishment can respond to you.
* **Outdoor Dining Details** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional details about your outdoor dining request.
* **Additional Comments** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Signature** (text, required: false):
	+ Add your signature, if required.
* **Form Submitter Name** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Form Submitter Title** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter your title.
* **Form Submitter Department** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter your department (if required).

Note: This form is intended to be filled out by employees for outdoor dining purposes, so all fields are optional unless otherwise specified.
