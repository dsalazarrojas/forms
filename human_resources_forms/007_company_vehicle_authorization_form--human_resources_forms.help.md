# Company Vehicle Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize company vehicle use for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the "Driver Full Name" field.
2. Enter your company-issued Employee ID Number.
3. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown.
4. Enter your valid driver's license number and expiration date, ensuring it's not within the next 3 months.
5. Select the main reason you need a company vehicle for "Primary Purpose of Vehicle Use".
6. Choose the type of vehicle you require for "Type of Vehicle Authorized".
7. Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm adherence to the company fleet safety policy.
8. Enter the name of your approving manager.
9. Finally, submit the form with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Driver Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
- **Employee ID Number**: Enter your company-issued identification number for verification purposes.
- **Department**: Choose the department you are currently working in to ensure proper vehicle assignment.
- **Driver License Number**: Enter your valid driver's license number.
- **License Expiration Date**: Enter your driver's license expiration date, ensuring it's not within the next 3 months.
- **Primary Purpose of Vehicle Use**: Select the main reason you require a company vehicle, such as "Client Meetings" or "Delivery / Logistics".
- **Type of Vehicle Authorized**: Choose the category of vehicle you require, like "Sedan / Hatchback" or "Light Truck / Van".
- **I have read and agree to the Company Fleet Safety Policy**: Confirm that you understand and will comply with company safety regulations.
- **Approving Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager who has authorized this request.
- **Date of Authorization**: Fill in the current date to finalize the authorization.
