# exit_interview_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exit interview form is used to document the details of an employee's departure from the company. This form is meant to gather relevant information about the employee's new hire date, exit date, reasons for leaving, feedback comments, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information about the employee's departure, including their new hire date, exit date, and reasons for leaving.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
3. Proofread the form to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Date**: <label> (1) (`new_hire_date_input`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the employee was hired.
* **Exit Date**: <label> (2) (`exit_date_input`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the employee's employment with the company ended.
* **Reason for Departure**: <label> (3) (`reason_for_departure_input`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly explain the reasons for the employee's departure.
* **Feedback Comments**: <label> (4) (`feedback_comments_input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any feedback comments or notes about the employee's time at the company.
* **Employee Name**: <label> (5) (`employee_name_input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the departing employee.
* **Email**: <label> (6) (`email_input`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone Number**: <label> (7) (`phone_number_input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Supervisor Name**: <label> (8) (`supervisor_name_input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor or manager.
* **Next Steps**: <label> (9) (`next_steps_input`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any next steps or follow-up actions regarding the employee's departure.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* Proofread the form for spelling and formatting errors.
* Attach any supporting documents or evidence, if necessary.
* Store the form in a secure location, as it contains sensitive employee information.
