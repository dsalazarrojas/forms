> ## Transportation Worker Injury Recovery Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to document and manage the Transportation Worker Injury Recovery Leave of Absence (TWI-RA) process, which allows employees to take time off to recover from work-related injuries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the fields that require completion.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions for each field.
3. Review and sign the form to confirm that it has been completed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Employee Information** (`id: 2`)
* This field is for the employee's personal identification details.

### **Injury Details** (`id: 3`)
* This field is for the employee to describe the details of their work-related injury.

### **Medical Report** (`id: 4`)
* This field is for the employee to attach a medical report from their healthcare provider.

### **Return To Work** (`id: 5`)
* This field is for the employee to describe their return-to-work plan.

### **Supervisor Confirmation** (`id: 6`)
* This field is for the employee's supervisor to confirm their understanding of the employee's return-to-work plan.

### **Medical Provider** (`id: 7`)
* This field is to confirm if the employee's injury was treated by a medical provider.

### **Date Of Injury** (`id: 8`)
* This field is for the employee to provide the date of the injury.

### **Time Of Injury** (`id: 9`)
* This field is for the employee to provide the time of the injury.

### **Medical Certification** (`id: 10`)
* This field is for the employee to provide any medical certification related to their injury.

### **Status** (`id: 13`)
* This field is for the employee to provide an update on their status regarding their recovery.

### **Status** (`id: 17`)
* This field is for the employee to provide an update on their status regarding their recovery.

### **Number of Days Off Requested** (`id: 16`)
* This field is for the employee to specify the number of days off they are requesting.

### **Medical Note** (`id: 18`)
* This field is for the employee to provide any additional medical notes related to their injury.

### **Comments** (`id: 19`)
* This field is for the employee to provide any comments or additional information related to their recovery.

### **Signature** (`id: 20`)
* This field is for the employee to sign and date the form.

### **Date Submitted** (`id: 22`)
* This field is for the automatic date the form was submitted.

### **Employee ID** (`id: 23`)
* This field is for the employee's identification number.

### **Supervisor ID** (`id: 24`)
* This field is for the supervisor's identification number.

### **Manager ID** (`id: 25`)
* This field is for the manager's identification number.
