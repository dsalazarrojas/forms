# Performance Management Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from candidates for the Performance Management Fellowship program. This information will be used to assess the fit and suitability of each candidate for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your candidate details, including your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Fill out the contact details section with your current contact information.
3. In the fellowship details section, describe your current role, experience, and goals related to the fellowship program.
4. Attach any necessary documents or supporting materials that demonstrate your qualifications and relevance to the program.
5. Review and submit the form once you are satisfied with the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details**: This section is for you to provide your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Details**: This section is for you to provide your current contact information, including your email address, phone number, and physical address. This will help us contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Fellowship Details**: This section is for you to describe your current role, experience, and goals related to the fellowship program. Be as specific as possible about your qualifications and how they align with the program's objectives.
* **Documents**: This section is for you to attach any necessary documents or supporting materials that demonstrate your qualifications and relevance to the program. These can include, but are not limited to, your resume, cover letter, and any relevant certificates or awards.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the form or the fellowship program, please contact form@gic.mx.
* Attach any relevant documents or supporting materials that demonstrate your qualifications and relevance to the program.
* Review and submit the form once you are satisfied with the information provided.
