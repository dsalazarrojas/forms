# Agent Commission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Commission Form is a document that calculates and records commission payments for agents. It's used to track and process commission payments for agents, ensuring accurate and timely payment. This form is a critical part of our sales management process, providing transparency and accountability for both agents and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Agent name** in the "Agent name" field (2). This is the name associated with the commission payment.
2. Provide the **Internal identifier** for the employee in the "Employee ID" field (3). If applicable, please enter the internal ID for the employee.
3. Select the **Commission period start date** in the "Commission period start" field (4) and end date in the "Commission period end" field (5). These dates will be used to calculate the commission payment.
4. Enter the **Gross sales amount** in the "Gross sales amount" field (6). This should include all sales made during the selected period.
5. Enter the **Commission rate percentage** in the "Commission rate percent" field (7). This is the percentage applied to the gross sales amount.
6. Calculate the **Commission amount** by using the formula: Commission amount = (Gross sales amount \* Commission rate percent). You can find this value automatically populated in the "Commission amount" field (8).
7. Choose the **Payment method** from the available options (9). You can select from Bank transfer, Check, or Payroll as the payment method for the agent.
8. Enter the **Name** of the approver in the "Approver name" field (10). This is the manager's name who is approving the commission payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent name (2)**: Enter the name associated with the commission payment.
* **Employee ID (3)**: Provide the internal identifier for the employee if applicable.
* **Commission period start (4)**: Select the start date for the commission period in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Commission period end (5)**: Select the end date for the commission period in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Gross sales amount (6)**: Enter the total sales amount for the selected period.
* **Commission rate percent (7)**: Enter the commission percentage applied to the gross sales amount.
* **Commission amount (8)**: This is automatically calculated based on the formula: (Gross sales amount \* Commission rate percent).
* **Payment method (9)**: Choose from Bank transfer, Check, or Payroll as the payment method for the agent.
* **Approver name (10)**: Enter the manager's name who is approving the commission payment.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have all necessary information before filling out the form to avoid errors and discrepancies.
- Double-check the dates entered for the commission period to ensure accuracy.
- Review the calculated commission amount to ensure it's correct before submitting the form.
