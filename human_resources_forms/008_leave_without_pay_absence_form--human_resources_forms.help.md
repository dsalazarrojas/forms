# Leave Without Pay Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence without pay from work. It is intended for employees to request a leave for a specific period of time, including the date of absence and the reason for the leave. This form is usually completed by employees to notify their supervisor or HR department of their intention to be away from work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your request details in the **Request Details** field.
2. Select the **Date Of Absence** for which you will be absent.
3. Provide a brief description of the **Reason For Absence** in the text box.
4. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of your absence in the respective fields.
5. Enter the **Number Of Days** you will be absent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): A brief description of your leave request, including any relevant details or supporting information.
* **Date Of Absence** (`date_of_absence`, date, optional): The date for which you will be absent.
* **Reason For Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, optional): A brief description of the reason for your leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, time, optional): The start date of your absence.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, time, optional): The end date of your absence.
* **Number Of Days** (`number_of_days`, number, optional): The number of days you will be absent.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information in the **Request Details** field.
* Be specific about the **Date Of Absence** and **Start Date** and **End Date**.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or HR department for guidance.

Note: This help guide is intended for end-users and assumes that the form is filled out in a web-based interface. The instructions are based on the provided YAML fields and aim to provide a clear and concise explanation of each field's purpose.
