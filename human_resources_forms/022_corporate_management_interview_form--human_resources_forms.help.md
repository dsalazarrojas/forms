# corporate_management_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate_management_interview_form is a tool used to gather feedback and insights from employees about the company's management style and culture. This form helps HR or management understand employee satisfaction, engagement, and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
2. Provide specific examples whenever possible.
3. Be as detailed as you can be in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you value most about our company culture?** (`employee_values`, text, required)
	+ This question asks about your most valued aspects of our company culture.
* **How would you rate the company's communication style?** (`communication_style`, number, required)
	+ This question asks you to rate the company's communication style, with a focus on how well the company keeps you informed and involves you in decision-making processes.
* **What are your main concerns about the company's current management style?** (`concerns`, text, required)
	+ This question invites you to share any concerns or issues you've experienced with the current management style.
* **Can you describe a recent project you managed that you're particularly proud of?** (`recent_project`, text, required)
	+ Share a specific example of a project you led or were involved in, and explain what made you proud of the outcome.
* **How do you rate the company's support for employee development and growth?** (`support`, number, required)
	+ Rate the company's support for your career development and growth opportunities.
* **What are the company's biggest strengths and weaknesses from your perspective?** (`strengths_weaknesses`, text, required)
	+ Share your insights on the company's strengths and weaknesses, providing specific examples if possible.
* **Can you walk us through a recent challenging situation you faced at work and how you overcame it?** (`challenging_situation`, text, required)
	+ Describe a situation that was difficult for you, and explain how you handled it and what you learned from it.
* **What do you think the company needs to improve on to better support employee well-being?** (`recognition`, text, required)
	+ Share your thoughts on areas where the company could improve to support employee well-being.
* **How do you rate the company's work-life balance support?** (`work_life_balance`, number, required)
	+ Rate the company's support for maintaining a healthy balance between work and personal life.
* **Can you suggest new ideas for company events or activities that would boost employee morale?** (`ideas`, text, required)
	+ Offer suggestions for company events or activities that you think would improve employee morale.
* **What is your favorite company policy or initiative and why do you value it?** (`favorite_policy`, text, required)
	+ Share your favorite policy or initiative and explain why it's valuable to you.
* **Can you tell us about a time when you felt recognized and valued as an employee?** (`events`, text, required)
	+ Describe a specific time when you felt truly valued and recognized as an employee.

Note: Please answer each question in a thorough and honest manner, providing specific examples whenever possible.
