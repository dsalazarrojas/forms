# employee_exit_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The employee exit form is a crucial tool for the HR or personnel department to record employee departures, ensuring accurate records and a smooth transition. It captures essential details about the employee's exit, which is vital for future reference and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Enter the Company Name: This field is an optional field where you can input the company name. 
3. Enter the Employee ID: This field is used to input the employee's unique identifier for reference purposes.
4. Reason for Departure: This field is optional, where you can provide the reason for the employee's departure.
5. Date of Departure: Click on the date picker and select the date when the employee left the company.
6. Time of Departure: Click on the time picker and select the time when the employee left the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the company where the employee was last employed.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Optional): Input the employee's unique identifier for reference purposes.
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, text, Optional): Provide the reason for the employee's departure, if applicable.
* **Date of Departure** (`date_of_departure`, date, Optional): Select the date when the employee left the company.
* **Time of Departure** (`time_of_departure`, time, Optional): Select the time when the employee left the company.
