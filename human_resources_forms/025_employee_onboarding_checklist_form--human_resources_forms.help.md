# Employee Onboarding Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Checklist Form is used to gather essential information about new hires in the company, making the onboarding process more efficient and accurate.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for general information questions.
3. Enter relevant text or date fields for job details, contact information, supervisor and manager information, and emergency contacts.
4. Choose dates for job start and end dates.
