# Gamma Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gamma Test form is a general-purpose test form used for various human resources purposes. It is designed to gather information from individuals and has multiple pages with different field types.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the first page, labeled "First Page".
2. Fill in the text field as prompted.
3. Click on the second page, labeled "Second Page".
4. Enter a number value for the "Second Page" field.
5. Click on the third page, labeled "Email".
6. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
7. Click on the subsequent pages, which will have the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
8. For the "Select One" field, choose one of the two options: "Yes" or "No".
9. For the "Select Multiple" field, choose multiple options from the list.
10. Click on the page with the "Date" field.
11. Enter a date value in the "Date" format.
12. Click on the page with the "Time" field.
13. Enter a time value in the "Time" format.
14. Finally, click on the "Note" page and enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (`gamma_test_form_1`, `text`, Optional): Enter any text as prompted.
* **Second Page** (`gamma_test_form_2`, `number`, Optional): Enter a numerical value.
* **Email** (`gamma_test_form_3`, `email`, Optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Select One** (`gamma_test_form_4`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose one of the two options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Select Multiple** (`gamma_test_form_5`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Date** (`gamma_test_form_6`, `date`, Optional): Enter a date value in the "Date" format.
* **Time** (`gamma_test_form_7`, `time`, Optional): Enter a time value in the "Time" format.
* **Note** (`gamma_test_form_8`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the relevant help guide or contact the relevant authority.
* Be as accurate and truthful as possible when filling in the fields.
