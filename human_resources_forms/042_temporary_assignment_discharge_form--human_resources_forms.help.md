# Temporary Assignment Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to formally end an assignment for an employee. It is a necessary step in the process of managing employee assignments and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a brief title for the form in the **Form Title** field.
2. Enter the **Assignment Id** of the assignment being discharged.
3. Enter the **Employee Id** of the employee being discharged.
4. Provide a brief explanation of the reason for discharging the assignment in the **Reason** field.
5. Enter the date when the assignment was discharged in the **Date** field.
6. Add any additional comments about the discharge in the **Comments** field.
7. Confirm that the form has been submitted correctly by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Submit** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, not required): A brief title for the form, used to identify the purpose of the submission.
* **Assignment Id** (`assignment_id`, number, not required): The unique identifier for the assignment being discharged.
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, not required): The unique identifier for the employee being discharged.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, not required): A brief explanation of the reason for discharging the assignment.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): The date when the assignment was discharged.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Additional comments about the discharge of the assignment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, not required): Confirm that the form has been submitted correctly by selecting **Yes** or **No**.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary fields are complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or manager for guidance.
