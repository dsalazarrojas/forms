# Strategic Workforce Planning Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Workforce Planning Contact Form is designed to facilitate communication and information gathering from users within the Human Resources department for workforce planning purposes. This form will help to centralize and standardize the request process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and managed effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out your name, last name, and email address on page 1 of the form.
2. On the second page, provide detailed information about your request type and request details.
3. Make sure to select the correct qualification and request types as per your requirements.
4. Enter a contact name, email, and phone number for follow-up and response purposes.
5. Provide a contact title and department to help us identify the correct contact person and team.
6. Review and complete all the required fields accurately before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Message** (`message`): This field is not applicable as it seems to be redundant with other fields, but if you want to leave a message, it's a good place to do so.
* **First Page** (`first_page_break`): This field seems to be a placeholder for a page break; please ignore it.
* **Second Page** (`second_page`): This field is also a placeholder for a page break; please ignore it.
* **Qualification** (`qualification`): Select 'Yes' if you have a relevant qualification or 'No' otherwise.
* **Other Qualification** (`qualification_other`): If you have a qualification not listed, describe it here.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`): Select all request types that apply to your request.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`): Provide detailed information about your request.
* **Request Details 2** (`request_details_2`): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Request Details 3** (`request_details_3`): Enter further details about your request.
* **Request Details 4** (`request_details_4`): Enter more details about your request.
* **Request Details 5** (`request_details_5`): Enter even more details about your request.
* **Request Details 6** (`request_details_6`): Enter final details about your request.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`): Enter the department of the contact person.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field or section, don't hesitate to ask for clarification or assistance from the Human Resources team.
