# Time Clock Adjustment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Time Clock Adjustment Request" form is used to make changes to your recorded working hours. This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your Employee ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by your HR department.
2.  Enter your Employee Name as it appears in the HR system.
3.  Select the date from which you want the adjustment to start.
4.  Choose the date until which the adjustment is valid.
5.  Choose the type of adjustment you are requesting, such as Time Off or Pay Adjustment.
6.  Provide a brief reason for your adjustment request.
7.  Select the approval status from your manager(s) who will review your request.
8.  (Optional) Add any comments to support your request.
9.  Enter your name as the submitter of the request.
10.  Choose the date and time you are submitting the request.
11.  Review the current status of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee ID** (1, Employee ID, Required): Enter your unique employee ID assigned to you by the HR department.
*   **Employee Name** (2, Employee Name, Required): Enter your name as it appears in the HR system.
*   **Start Date** (3, Start Date, Required): Enter the date from which you want the adjustment to start.
*   **End Date** (4, End Date, Required): Choose the date until which the adjustment is valid.
*   **Adjustment Type** (5, Adjustment Type, Required): Select the type of adjustment you are requesting, such as Time Off or Pay Adjustment.
*   **Reason** (6, Reason, Required): Enter a brief description of your request.
*   **Manager Approval** (7, Manager Approval, Required): Select the approval status from your manager(s) that will review your request.
*   **Comments** (8, Comments, Optional): (Optional) Provide any additional information to support your request.
*   **Submitter** (9, Submitter, Required): Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
*   **Submit Date** (10, Submit Date, Optional): If required, enter the date and time of submission.
*   **Status** (12, Status, Optional): Review the status of your request, which may be Approved, Pending, or Declined.

## Tips
*   Make sure you have a valid reason for your adjustment request.
*   Ensure that you select the correct adjustment type and dates for your request.
*   Be accurate when providing your employee name and ID.
*   Clearly articulate your reason for the request.
*   Be considerate of your manager's time when requesting a manager approval.
*   Review your request before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
