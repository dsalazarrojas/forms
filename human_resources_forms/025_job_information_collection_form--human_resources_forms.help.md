# Job Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Information Collection Form is used to gather essential details about a job opportunity to share with potential candidates and for internal HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the job title, job type, job description, job category, job status, job level, and job location.
2. Select the job type (full-time or part-time) and job category (sales, marketing, IT, finance, human resources).
3. Choose the job status (active or inactive) and job level (high or low).
4. Enter the years of experience and work experience.
5. Select the relevant skills and job location type (remote or on-site).
6. Ensure all required fields are filled in and the form is complete before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job opening.
* **Job Type**: Select either full-time or part-time to indicate the type of work arrangement.
* **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of the job duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Category**: Select all applicable categories (e.g., Sales, Marketing, IT) that apply to the job.
* **Job Status**: Select the status of the job as either Active or Inactive.
* **Job Level**: Choose the level of the job as High, Low or other level not listed here.
* **Work Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience required for the job.
* **Education Level**: Select the highest level of education required for the job (e.g., High School, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree).
* **Skills**: Enter the skills required for the job.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location of the job.
* **Job Location Type**: Select the type of location (Remote or On-site).
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience required for the job.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Use accurate and concise language when filling in the fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your HR representative for guidance.
