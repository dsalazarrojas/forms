# Sexual Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sexual Harassment Complaint Form is designed to collect information about incidents of sexual harassment that have occurred within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure you have a clear understanding of the questions and fields.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about a particular question, please consult with a supervisor or HR representative.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details (Page 1)** (`incident_details`, `text`, required: false): This is a free text field where you can describe the details of the incident.
* **Description Of Harassment (Page 1)** (`description_of_harassment`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a detailed description of the harassment you experienced.
* **Perpetrator Name (Page 1)** (`perpetrator_name`, `text`, required: false): If you know the name of the person involved, please provide it.
* **Date Of Occurrence (Page 1)** (`date_of_occurrence`, `date`, required: false): Provide the date the incident occurred.
* **Time Of Occurrence (Page 1)** (`time_of_occurrence`, `time`, required: false): Provide the time the incident occurred.
* **Location Of Harassment (Page 1)** (`location_of_harassment`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Witnesses (Page 1)** (`witnesses`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If there were witnesses to the incident, please list their names.
* **Action Taken (Page 1)** (`action_taken`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the action taken after the incident.
* **Resolution Outcome (Page 1)** (`resolution_outcome`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outcome of the resolution.
* **Comments (Page 1)** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or concerns you have.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about what to include, please contact a supervisor or HR representative.
* All responses will be kept confidential.
