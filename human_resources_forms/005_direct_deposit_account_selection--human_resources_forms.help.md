# Direct Deposit Account Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to select and set up or manage a direct deposit account for an employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employer's name in the **employer_name** field.
2. Enter the account number in the **account_number** field.
3. Enter the routing number in the **routing_number** field.
4. Select the **account_type** from the list of options provided.
5. Enter the account name in the **account_name** field.
6. Enter the bank name in the **bank_name** field.
7. Enter the account ending in the **account_ending** field.
8. If applicable, select **Direct Deposit** as "Yes" and fill in the relevant fields for **account_owner**, **account_owner_contact**, **account_owner_phone**, and **account_owner_email**.
9. Select the assigned tool from the list in the **assigned_tool** field.
10. Optionally enter the name of the assigned tool in the **assigned_tool_name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter the employer's name to associate with this direct deposit account.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter the account number to complete the direct deposit setup.
* **Routing Number** (`routing_number`, text, required): Enter the routing number for the direct deposit account.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, text, required): Select the type of account from the provided options.
* **Account Name** (`account_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the account being used for direct deposit.
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the bank associated with this direct deposit account.
* **Account Ending** (`account_ending`, text, required): Enter the ending of the account number for the direct deposit account.
* **Direct Deposit** (`direct_deposit`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to enable direct deposit for this account. If selected, enter the relevant information for **account_owner**, **account_owner_contact**, **account_owner_phone**, and **account_owner_email**.
* **Bank Account Ending** (`bank_account_ending`, text, optional): Enter the ending of the bank account number.
* **Account Owner** (`account_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the account owner.
* **Contact** (`account_owner_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the account owner.
* **Phone** (`account_owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the account owner.
* **Email** (`account_owner_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of the account owner.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned for this direct deposit account.
* **Assigned Tool Name** (`assigned_tool_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned tool.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to avoid duplicate or incorrect direct deposit setups.
* If you are unsure or need help, you can contact the HR team for assistance.
* Make sure to select "Yes" for **Direct Deposit** only if you intend to use this setup for direct deposit.
* If "Yes" for **Direct Deposit** is selected, ensure that you have all required information for **account_owner**, **account_owner_contact**, **account_owner_phone**, and **account_owner_email** fields.
