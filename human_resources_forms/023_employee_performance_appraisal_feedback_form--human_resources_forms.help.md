# Employee Performance Appraisal Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Performance Appraisal Feedback Form is a tool used by managers to gather and document feedback on an employee's performance. This feedback is used to evaluate an employee's strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and create a development plan to help them grow professionally.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the manager providing this feedback.
2.  **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee being appraised.
3.  **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the department the employee works in from the dropdown list.
4.  **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title of the employee.
5.  **Feedback Questions** (`Feedback Questions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the specific areas in which you want to provide feedback, such as leadership and initiative, communication, or adaptability and flexibility.
6.  **Employee Rating** (`Employee Rating`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the employee's performance in each selected area, using a numerical value.
7.  **Performance Level** (`Performance Level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Evaluate the overall performance level of the employee, choosing from "Below Expectations", "Meets Expectations", or "Exceeds Expectations".
8.  **Development Plan** (`Development Plan`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any specific goals or action items for the employee to focus on in the next evaluation period.

## Tips
* Be specific and objective when providing feedback.
* Focus on facts and examples rather than opinions or personal biases.
* Ensure that your feedback is respectful and professional.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
