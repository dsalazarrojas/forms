# Performance Review Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Review Training Form is designed to gather information about an employee's performance and training needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, you will find a space to enter basic information about the employee.
2. The second page will ask you to provide performance data and job satisfaction information.
3. On the third page, you can specify any training needs or requests.
4. Finally, you can include any additional comments or notes.
5. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information (`employee_info`):** Enter the employee's name, job title, and any other relevant details.
* **Performance Data (`performance_data`):** Provide a brief description of the employee's performance during the review period.
* **Training Needs (`training_needs`):** Specify any training needs or requests for the employee.
* **Job Satisfaction (`job_satisfaction`):** Rate your level of satisfaction with the job, using the following scale: [1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied"].
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`):** Enter any additional comments, notes, or feedback.
