# Succession Planning Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Succession Planning Needs Assessment Form is a tool used by human resources departments to identify and assess the succession needs of an employee. This form is used to gather information about an employee's job role, job function, and succession planning needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your job title from the list provided.
2. Enter your employee name.
3. Choose your job function from the list provided.
4. Select the skills and knowledge areas you require for your job.
5. Choose your career aspirations (advancement, growth, or development).
6. Select your job title (e.g., CEO, Manager, Coordinator).
7. Choose your job family (e.g., Operations, Finance, Marketing).
8. Select your reporting manager (e.g., CEO, Manager, Coordinator).
9. Choose your department (e.g., Operations, Finance, Marketing).
10. Select your job level (e.g., Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
11. Choose your job band (e.g., Band 1, Band 2, Band 3).
12. Select your job category (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
13. Choose your job type (e.g., Type 1, Type 2, Type 3).
14. Select your job status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Probation).
15. Choose your job grade (e.g., Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3).
16. Select your job family size (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
17. Choose your job family type (e.g., Type 1, Type 2, Type 3).
18. Enter your job role.
19. Enter your succession plan.
20. Enter your succession plan start date.
21. Enter your succession plan end date.
22. Enter your reporting manager's name.
23. Enter your reporting manager's position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your job title**: This field is used to select your current job title. Please choose the one that best describes your role within the organization.
* **Employee Name**: This field is used to input your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID card or other official documents.
* **Job function**: This field is used to select your job function. Please choose the one that best describes your role within the organization.
* **Succession Planning Needs**: This field is used to select the areas of succession planning you require. Please choose all that apply.
* **Skills and Knowledge**: This field is used to select the skills and knowledge areas you require for your job.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
