# Culture Fit Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culture Fit Interview Questionnaire is a tool to help assess a candidate's cultural fit and suitability for a role within the company. It is used by HR teams and hiring managers to gather relevant information about the candidate and the role. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "Questions" section, which is where you will enter any questions or comments about the candidate. 
2. In the "Job Title" field, enter the title of the job the candidate is applying for. 
3. In the "Job Description" field, enter a brief description of the job and its requirements. 
4. In the "Company Name" field, enter the name of the company the candidate is applying to. 
5. In the "Candidate Name" field, enter the name of the candidate. 
6. In the "Contact Email" field, enter the candidate's email address. 
7. In the "Contact Phone" field, enter the candidate's phone number. 
8. In the "Preferred Communication Channel" field, select the method of communication that the candidate prefers. 
9. In the "Availability" field, enter the date and time the candidate is available for the interview. 
10. In the "Notice Period" section, select the unit of time for the notice period (weeks, months, years). 
11. In the "Notice Period Frequency" section, select the frequency of the notice period (daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually). 
12. In the "Notice Period Start Date" field, enter the date when the notice period starts. 
13. In the "Notice Period End Date" field, enter the date when the notice period ends. 
14. In the "Notice Period Start Time" fields, enter the start and end time of the notice period. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Questions** (text, required): This is where you will enter any questions or comments about the candidate. 
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the job the candidate is applying for. 
* **Job Description** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the job and its requirements. 
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the company the candidate is applying to. 
* **Candidate Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the candidate. 
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter the candidate's email address. 
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the candidate's phone number. 
* **Preferred Communication Channel** (select multiple, optional): Select the method of communication that the candidate prefers. 
* **Availability** (date, required): Enter the date and time the candidate is available for the interview. 
* **Notice Period Unit** (select one, optional): Select the unit of time for the notice period (weeks, months, years). 
* **Notice Period Frequency** (select multiple, optional): Select the frequency of the notice period (daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually). 
* **Notice Period Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the date when the notice period starts. 
* **Notice Period End Date** (date, optional): Enter the date when the notice period ends. 
* **Notice Period Start Time** (time, optional): Enter the start time of the notice period. 
* **Notice Period End Time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of the notice period.
