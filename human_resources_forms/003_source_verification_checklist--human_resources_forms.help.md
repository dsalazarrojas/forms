# Source Verification Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the source of an employee, ensuring that their background, employment, and identity information is accurate and trustworthy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you answer every question.
2. If a field is not applicable, select "No" or leave it blank.
3. If you are unsure about any information, contact your manager or HR representative for guidance.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all pages and fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Check** (`Background Check`, select_multiple, **required**): Select "Yes" if you have undergone a background check, and "No" otherwise.
* **Employment Verification** (`Employment Verification`, select_one, **required**): Select "Yes" if your employment has been verified, and "No" otherwise.
* **Identity Verification** (`Identity Verification`, select_one, **required**): Select "Yes" if your identity has been verified, and "No" otherwise.
* **Address Verification** (`Address Verification`, select_multiple, **required**): Select "Yes" if your address has been verified, and "No" otherwise.
* **Social Security Number** (`Social Security Number`, text, **required**): Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment Start Date** (`Employment Start Date`, date, **required**): Enter your employment start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment End Date** (`Employment End Date`, date, **required**): Enter your employment end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, **required**): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Name (if applicable)** (`Manager Name`, text, **required**): Enter your manager's name if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, **required**): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, **required**): Enter your department.
* **Manager Contact** (`Manager Contact`, text, **required**): Enter your manager's contact information.
* **Company** (`Company`, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" if you are a company representative.
* **Manager Employment Status** (`Manager Employment Status`, select_one, **optional**): Select "Active" if your manager is actively employed, and "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Manager Employment Start Date** (`Manager Employment Start Date`, date, **optional**): Enter your manager's employment start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Manager Employment End Date** (`Manager Employment End Date`, date, **optional**): Enter your manager's employment end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Manager Job Title** (`Manager Job Title`, text, **optional**): Enter your manager's job title.
* **Manager Manager** (`Manager Manager`, text, **optional**): Enter your manager's manager's name if applicable.
* **Employment Type** (`Employment Type`, text, **optional**): Enter your employment type (e.g. full-time, part-time, contract).
* **Manager Employment Type** (`Manager Employment Type`, text, **optional**): Enter your manager's employment type.
* **Background Check Result** (`Background Check Result`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select "Yes" if your background check result is positive, and "No" otherwise.
* **Employment Verification Result** (`Employment Verification Result`, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" if your employment verification result is positive, and "No" otherwise.
* **Identity Verification Result** (`Identity Verification Result`, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" if your identity verification result is positive, and "No" otherwise.
* **Address Verification Result** (`Address Verification Result`, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" if your address verification result is positive, and "No" otherwise.
