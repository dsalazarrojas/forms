# Workplace Code Of Conduct Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Code Of Conduct Revision Request Form is used to collect feedback and suggestions from employees on the company's code of conduct. This form helps in maintaining a fair and respectful work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your suggestion or feedback in the "suggestion" field.
4. Select the relevant category for your submission, e.g., "Code of Conduct", "Policy", "Procedure", "Process", or "Protocol".
5. Select the relevant department, e.g., "HR", "Management", "Marketing", "Operations", or "Research".
6. Select the location of your submission, e.g., "New York", "Los Angeles", "Chicago", "London", or "Paris".
7. If you are requesting a specific version of the policy, enter it in the "policy_version" field.
8. If your submission is time-sensitive, select the "submission_date" field and enter the date you are making the submission.
9. If the reviewer has added any comments, read and consider them carefully before proceeding.
10. Select the status of your submission: "Open", "In Progress", or "Completed".
11. Enter any additional comments or notes you have in the "submission_notes" field.
12. If your submission has been reviewed and approved, select "Approved" in the "approved" field. If your submission has been denied, select "Denied" and enter a reason in the "reason_denied" field.
13. Select the reviewer who reviewed your submission.
14. Finally, select the status of your submission: "Open", "In Progress", or "Completed".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: Your name is required. Please enter your full name as it appears on your badge.
* **email**: Your email address is required. Please enter your work email address.
* **suggestion**: This is the field where you can enter your suggestion or feedback on the company's code of conduct.
* **category**: Select the category that best describes your submission. This will help the reviewer categorize and prioritize your submission.
* **department**: Select the department that your submission belongs to. This will help the reviewer know the context of your submission.
* **location**: Select the location where your submission is relevant. This will help the reviewer know the scope of your submission.
* **policy_version**: If you are requesting a specific version of the policy, please enter it here.
* **submission_date**: If your submission is time-sensitive, please select the date you are making the submission.
* **reviewer_comments**: This field is for the reviewer to add comments or feedback on your submission.
* **approved**: If your submission has been approved, select "Approved". If your submission has been denied, select "Denied" and enter a reason in the "reason_denied" field.
* **reason_denied**: If your submission has been denied, please enter a reason for the denial.
* **reviewer**: Enter the name of the reviewer who reviewed your submission.
* **submission_status**: Select the status of your submission: "Open", "In Progress", or "Completed".
* **submission_notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have on your submission.
* **form_submission_date**: This field is automatically populated with the date of your submission.
