# Employee Clock In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Clock In Form is a tool used to record employee clock-in activities. It captures information such as date, start and end times, location, and comments, which can be used for attendance, time-tracking, or other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required before filling out this form.
2. Fill out each field according to the explanations provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clock In** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter the clock-in number, which is used to identify your clock-in activity.
* **Date** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter the date you clocked in.
* **Start Time** (`3`, `time`, required): Enter the time you started your workday.
* **End Time** (`4`, `time`, required): Enter the time you ended your workday.
* **Break Start Time** (`5`, `time`, optional): If you take a break during your workday, enter the time you started your break.
* **Break End Time** (`6`, `time`, optional): If you take a break, enter the time you ended your break.
* **Location** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where you clocked in.
* **Comments** (`8`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your clock-in activity, such as any relevant events or issues that occurred during your workday.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to maintain record quality.
* If unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* If you have any concerns or need further assistance, please contact your HR representative or relevant personnel.
