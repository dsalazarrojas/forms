# Workplace Drug Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to acknowledge an employee's understanding of the Workplace Drug Policy, which is a document that outlines the company's guidelines and rules regarding drug and alcohol use in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your status as a staff member.
2. Enter the date you read the policy.
3. Enter the date you signed the acknowledgment.
4. Enter your staff name.
5. Enter your job title.
6. Enter your department.
7. Check the box to indicate whether you have a supervisor.
8. Check the box to indicate your acknowledgement of the policy.
9. Sign the form with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Staff Member
* **Staff Member** (`select_one`): Select 'True' if you are a staff member, 'False' otherwise.

### Date Read
* **Date Read** (`date`): Enter the date you read the policy, in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Date Signed
* **Date Signed** (`date`): Enter the date you signed the acknowledgment, in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Staff Name
* **Staff Name** (`text`): Enter your full name as a staff member.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`text`): Enter your job title as a staff member.

### Department
* **Department** (`text`): Enter your department as a staff member.

### Supervisor
* **Supervisor** (`select_multiple`): Check the box if you have a supervisor, 'False' otherwise.

### Acknowledgement
* **Acknowledgement** (`select_multiple`): Check the box to indicate your acknowledgement of the policy, 'Agree' for agreement or 'Disagree' otherwise.

### Signed By
* **Signed By** (`text`): Enter your name as the staff member signing the form.

## Tips
* Ensure you have read and understood the policy before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the policy, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form should not be filled out by anyone who has already acknowledged the policy in the past.
