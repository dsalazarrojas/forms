# Remote Work Arrangement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for a remote work arrangement. It is intended to gather information and approval for employees who are requesting to work remotely.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, please ensure that you have discussed your remote work arrangement request with your manager and followed the correct procedures and received necessary approvals.
- Select the "Request Form" page and provide a brief and clear explanation of your remote work arrangement request in the field provided.
- Enter the date on which you received approval for your remote work arrangement in the "Approval Date" field.
- If applicable, enter the time on which you received approval for your remote work arrangement in the "Approval Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form (<name>, <type>, required/optional>):** 
This is a text field where you should provide a clear and concise explanation of your remote work arrangement request.
* **Approval Date (<name>, <type>, required/optional>):** 
This field is where you should enter the date on which you received approval for your remote work arrangement.
* **Approval Time (<name>, <type>, required/optional>):** 
This field is where you should enter the time on which you received approval for your remote work arrangement. Note that this field is optional and is only required if approval was received at a specific time.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information in the form to avoid delays in processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative or manager for guidance.
