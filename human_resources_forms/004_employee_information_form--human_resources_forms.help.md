# employee_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_information_form is a data collection tool for employees to provide their personal and professional information for internal use within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to fill out this form.
2. Fill out the form with your personal and professional details.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Id** (1, number, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **First Name** (2, text, required): Provide your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Job Title** (3, text, optional): Enter your job title or role within the organization.
* **Department** (4, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to or work with.
* **Date Hired** (5, date, optional): Enter the date you started working with the organization.
* **Email** (6, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (7, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Manager** (8, text, optional): Enter the name and title of your direct manager.
* **Location** (9, text, optional): Enter the location where you work from.
* **Salary** (10, number, optional): Enter your annual salary.

## Tips
* Please ensure the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your HR department for assistance.
* If you have already submitted this form previously, there is no need to fill it out again.
