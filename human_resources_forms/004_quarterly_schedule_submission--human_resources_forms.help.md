# Quarterly Schedule Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Quarterly Schedule Submission" form is a collection of fields that gather information from employees about their quarterly schedules, plans, and other relevant details. This form helps management track employee productivity and identify potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field as required or optional.
2. Ensure you fill in all required fields.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Quarterly Schedules (id: 1)
* **Quarterly Schedules** (Select multiple, optional): Choose the schedules you have completed this quarter. This field allows you to select multiple schedules as needed.
### Weekly Schedules (id: 2)
* **Weekly Schedules** (Select one, optional): Choose if you have any weekly schedules this quarter. This field is for reporting weekly schedules, and you can choose only one.
### Risk Notes (id: 3)
* **Risk Notes** (Note, optional): Enter any risk notes if you have any concerns or issues this quarter. This field is for documenting potential risks or challenges.
### Quarterly Plans (id: 4)
* **Quarterly Plans** (Select multiple, optional): Choose the plans you have completed this quarter. Like the schedules field, you can select multiple plans as needed.
### Employee (id: 5)
* **Employee** (Select one, optional): Identify yourself as an employee, but please note this field is currently not relevant based on the provided data. You can choose "Yes" or "No".
### Department (id: 6)
* **Department** (Select one, optional): Identify your department, but please note this field is not currently used in this form. You can choose "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or management team.
