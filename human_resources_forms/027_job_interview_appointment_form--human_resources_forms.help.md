# job_interview_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and manage job interviews by capturing candidate information, interview details, and other essential data.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the **Schedule** page to select the date and time of the interview.
2. Move on to the **Candidate Information** page, where you can enter the candidate's name and any other relevant details.
3. On the **Interview Details** page, provide a brief description of the interview.
4. Proceed to the **Interview Schedule** page, where you can select the interview time and date.
5. The **Interviewer Information** page requires you to decide whether you want to invite multiple interviewers or not.
6. Enter the email address and subject of the email to be sent to the candidate on the **Email Sent** page.
7. Finally, fill out the **Notes** and **Form Submitted** pages to add any additional comments or confirm that the form has been submitted.
