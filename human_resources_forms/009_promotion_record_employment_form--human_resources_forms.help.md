# promotion_record_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotion record employment form is designed to document and track promotions within the organization, ensuring that all relevant information is accurately recorded and readily accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the previous section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the employee for the promotion.
*   **Date of Promotion**: Enter the date of the promotion.
*   **Time of Promotion**: Enter the time of the promotion.
*   **Promotion Reason**: Describe the reason for the promotion.
*   **Job Title Change**: Select the job title changes resulting from the promotion.
*   **Pay Rate Change**: Enter the pay rate change (if applicable).
*   **Manager Signature**: Enter your signature as the manager, confirming the promotion details.
*   **Manager Name**: Enter your name as the manager.
*   **Manager Date**: Enter the date of the manager's approval.
*   **Manager Time**: Enter the time of the manager's approval.
*   **Employee Signature**: Enter the signature of the employee being promoted.
*   **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being promoted.
*   **Employee Date**: Enter the date of the employee's signature.
*   **Employee Time**: Enter the time of the employee's signature.
*   **Approved By**: Select the person or entity that approved the promotion.
*   **Approved Date**: Enter the date of the approval.
*   **Approved Time**: Enter the time of the approval.
*   **Approved By Signature**: Enter the signature of the person or entity that approved the promotion.
