# Pre Job Technical Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Job Technical Assessment form is designed to collect essential information from job applicants to help us better understand their skills, experience, and availability for the position. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and honestly, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, email address, and contact number. These fields are required and will help us reach out to you about your application.
2. Select the technical skills you possess from the given options. You can choose multiple skills if you have experience with them.
3. Choose the programming language you are most proficient in from the given options.
4. Provide your level of experience in years. This will help us understand your level of expertise.
5. Select your availability for the position. Please choose a period when you are available to work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name (Text, Required)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address that you check regularly. This is how we will communicate with you about your application.
* **contact number (Text, Required)**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **technical skills (Select Multiple, Required)**: Select all the technical skills you have experience with from the given options. This will help us understand your technical capabilities.
* **programming languages (Select One, Required)**: Choose the programming language you are most proficient in from the given options.
* **experience (Number, Required)**: Enter the number of years you have experience with your chosen technical skills.
* **available date (Date, Optional)**: If you are available to start work soon, select the date when you can start. If not, leave this field blank.
* **availability (Select One, Required)**: Choose the period when you are available to work from the given options.
