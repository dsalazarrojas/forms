# Workplace Accident Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant information to fill in each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "true" in the form) to provide the necessary information.
3. If you are providing witness statements, ensure that each witness provides a distinct and relevant account of the incident.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
5. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Information** (Page 1)
	+ **Incident Information**: Provide a brief description of the incident, including the circumstances surrounding it.
* **Employee Data** (Page 1)
	+ **Employee Data**: Enter the employee's name and employee ID number (if applicable).
* **Incident Date** (Page 1)
	+ **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Page 1)
	+ **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (Page 1)
	+ **Incident Location**: Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (Page 1)
	+ **Incident Details**: Provide a detailed account of the incident, including any relevant events leading up to and following the incident.
* **Supervisor Signature** (Page 1)
	+ **Supervisor Signature**: The supervisor responsible for the employee must provide their signature to confirm their involvement in the incident.
* **Employee Signature** (Page 1)
	+ **Employee Signature**: The employee involved in the incident must provide their signature to confirm their involvement and agreement with the report.
* **Witness Information** (Page 2)
	+ **Witness 1 Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the witness who observed or was involved in the incident.
	+ **Witness 1 Relation**: If applicable, describe the relationship between the witness and the employee involved in the incident.
	+ **Witness 1 Statement**: If applicable, enter a statement from the witness describing their experience and observations of the incident.
* **Witness 2-3 Information** (Page 2)
	+ **Witness 2/Witness 3 Name**: If applicable, enter the name(s) of additional witness(es).
	+ **Witness 2/Witness 3 Relation**: If applicable, describe the relationship between the witness and the employee involved in the incident.
	+ **Witness 2/Witness 3 Statement**: If applicable, enter a statement from the witness describing their experience and observations of the incident.

## Tips

* Ensure that all information is accurate and thorough.
* Use clear and concise language when providing witness statements.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification from the relevant personnel.
* Please submit the form promptly to facilitate timely investigation and resolution of the incident.
