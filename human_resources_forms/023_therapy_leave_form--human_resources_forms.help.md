# Therapy Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a therapy session. Complete this form to provide necessary information for your manager's review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details in the relevant fields.
2. Provide the dates for your therapy sessions.
3. Specify the duration of your leave.
4. Enter your manager's name and email (if applicable).
5. Submit your form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input**: This field is not required but provides a text description of your leave request.
* **employee_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID or payroll records.
* **employee_email**: Enter your work email address.
* **therapy_session_date**: Enter the date of your therapy session.
* **therapy_session_time**: Enter the time of your therapy session.
* **duration_hours**: Enter the duration of your leave in hours.
* **purpose_of_leave**: Briefly explain the reason for your therapy session.
* **start_date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **end_date**: Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **status**: Select the status of your leave request (pending, approved, denied).
* **reason_for_denial**: If your leave request is denied, enter the reason for denial.
* **manager_name**: Enter your manager's name (if applicable).
* **manager_email**: Enter your manager's email (if applicable).
* **department**: Enter your department or team.
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments or context about your leave request.
* **submitter**: Enter your name as the submitter of this form.
* **manager_approvals**: Select whether your manager has approved or denied your leave request.
* **form_id**: This field is auto-generated and should not be filled by the user.
* **assigned_to**: Enter the person assigned to handle your leave request (if applicable).
* **created_by**: This field is auto-generated and should not be filled by the user.
* **created_date**: This field is auto-generated and should not be filled by the user.
* **updated_by**: This field is auto-generated and should not be filled by the user.
* **updated_date**: This field is auto-generated and should not be filled by the user.
* **last_updated_by**: This field is auto-generated and should not be filled by the user.
* **last_updated_date**: This field is auto-generated and should not be filled by the user.
