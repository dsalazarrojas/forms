# Manager Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Evaluation Form is used to evaluate an employee's performance by a manager who directly supervises them. This form helps managers assess an employee's strengths and weaknesses, providing a quick and simple way to document their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete the form as soon as possible after each performance period**. This will help to create a timely and accurate record of an employee's performance.
2. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully, and provide specific examples or feedback where possible.
3. **Use the provided fields to rate the employee's skills and performance**. Use the rating system to indicate how well the employee has met expectations.
4. **Submit the completed form to the relevant department or HR representative**. They will review and store the evaluation for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name** (<type>: text): This field is used to enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Evaluation start date** (<type>: date): This field is used to enter the date on which this evaluation started.
* **Evaluation end date** (<type>: date): This field is used to enter the date on which this evaluation ended.
* **Supervisor name** (<type>: text): This field is used to enter the name of the supervisor who filled out this form.
* **Overall rating** (<type>: number): This field is used to give an overall rating of the employee's performance (1-10).
* **Communication skills** (<type>: number): This field is used to rate the employee's communication skills.
* **Teamwork skills** (<type>: number): This field is used to rate the employee's teamwork skills.
* **Problem solving** (<type>: number): This field is used to rate the employee's problem-solving skills.
* **Adaptability** (<type>: number): This field is used to rate the employee's adaptability and flexibility.
* **Initiative taking** (<type>: number): This field is used to rate the employee's initiative and willingness to take on new challenges.
