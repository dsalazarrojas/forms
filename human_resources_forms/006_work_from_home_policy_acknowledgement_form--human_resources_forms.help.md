# Work From Home Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work From Home Policy Acknowledgement Form is used by employees to confirm their understanding of the work from home policy and their agreement to adhere to its guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and guidelines carefully.
2. Fill in the required fields (employee_name, supervisor_acknowledgement, and supervisor_acknowledgement_date).
3. Sign the form with your employee_signature and employee_acknowledgement_date.
4. Have your supervisor sign the form with supervisor_signature and supervisor_acknowledgement_date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Work Policy** (`remote_work_policy`):
	+ This field is where you can enter a brief description of your work from home policy.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`):
	+ This field is where you can enter a confirmation that you have read and understand the policy.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`):
	+ This field is where you can enter your name as an employee.
* **Supervisor Acknowledgement** (`supervisor_acknowledgement`):
	+ This field is where your supervisor can enter their acknowledgement of your understanding of the policy.
* **Manager Acknowledgement** (`manager_acknowledgement`):
	+ This field is not applicable (it seems to be a duplicate field).
* **Employee Understanding** (`employee_understanding`):
	+ This field is not applicable (it seems to be a duplicate field).
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`):
	+ This field is where you can sign the form to confirm your understanding of the policy.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`):
	+ This field is where your supervisor can sign the form to confirm their acknowledgement.
* **Supervisor Acknowledgement Date** (`supervisor_acknowledgement_date`):
	+ This field is where your supervisor can enter the date they acknowledged your understanding of the policy.
* **Employee Acknowledgement Date** (`employee_acknowledgement_date`):
	+ This field is where you can enter the date you acknowledged your understanding of the policy.
* **Supervisor Acknowledgement Date** (`supervisor_acknowledgement_date`):
	+ This field is not applicable (it seems to be a duplicate field).
