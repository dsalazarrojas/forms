# Group Interview Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Interview Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the performance of a group of individuals in an interview setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the job title of the participant you are evaluating.
2. Enter the name of the participant.
3. Enter the position title of the participant.
4. Select the date and time of the interview.
5. Enter an evaluation score between 1 and 5.
6. Enter an evaluation comment for the participant.
7. Select a recommendation for the participant (Strongly recommended, Weakly recommended, or Not recommended).
8. Select the job status of the participant (Active, Inactive, or Not Applicable).
9. Enter the contact information (name, email, phone number, and notes) of the participant's contact person.
10. Enter any additional comments or questions for the participant.
11. Select an evaluation score between 1 and 5 for the participant's skills or behavior.
12. Enter an evaluation comment for the participant's skills or behavior.
13. Select a job description for the participant.
14. Enter an interviewer's comment or rating for the participant.
15. Enter any job evaluation comments or rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the participant.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Position Title**: Enter the position title of the participant.
* **Interview Date**: Select the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time**: Select the time of the interview.
* **Evaluation Score**: Enter an evaluation score between 1 and 5.
* **Evaluation Comment**: Enter an evaluation comment for the participant.
* **Recommendation**: Select a recommendation for the participant (Strongly recommended, Weakly recommended, or Not recommended).
* **Job Status**: Select the job status of the participant (Active, Inactive, or Not Applicable).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the participant's contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the participant's contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant's contact person.
* **Contact Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the participant's contact person.
* **Interviewers Comments**: Enter any comments or questions for the participant.
* **Additional Interview Questions**: Enter any additional questions or comments for the participant.
* **Evaluation Score Scale**: Enter an evaluation score between 1 and 5.
* **Evaluation Scale Comment**: Enter an evaluation comment for the participant's skills or behavior.
* **Job Description**: Enter a job description for the participant.
* **Interviewers Rating**: Enter an interviewers rating or comment for the participant.
* **Job Evaluation**: Enter any job evaluation comments or rating.
* **Evaluation Time**: Select the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date**: Select the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Score 2**: Enter an evaluation score between 1 and 5.
* **Evaluation Comment 2**: Enter an evaluation comment for the participant's skills or behavior.
* **Evaluation Score 3**: This field is not used in this form.
* **Evaluation Comment 3**: This field is not used in this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form as soon as possible after the interview while the details are still fresh in your mind.
* Use the evaluation score and comment to provide an objective evaluation of the participant's performance.
* Use the recommendation and job status fields to provide a clear and consistent assessment of the participant's performance.
* Use the contact information fields to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
* Use the interviewers comments and additional questions fields to provide any additional context or information about the participant.
* Use the job description field to provide a clear and concise job description for the participant.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
