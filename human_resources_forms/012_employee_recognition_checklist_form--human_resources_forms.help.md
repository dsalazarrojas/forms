# Employee Recognition Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Checklist Form is designed to track and document employee recognition and rewards. This form collects information about employees, their recognition details, and related assignments and submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter employee information**: Fill in the employee's name, title, and email address.
2. **Provide recognition details**: Enter the reason for recognition, recognition date, recognition time, and type of recognition.
3. **Assign tasks**: Select the assigned tool, date, time, and status for the recognition.
4. **Submit**: Click "Submit" to save your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Employee Information

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's title or position.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the manager's name.

### Page 2: Recognition Information

* **Reason for Recognition** (`reason_for_recognition`, text, required): Enter the reason for the employee recognition.
* **Recognition Date** (`recognition_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the recognition.
* **Recognition Time** (`recognition_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the recognition.
* **Type of Recognition** (`recognition_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of recognition.

### Page 3: Assignment Information

* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to the employee.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required): Enter the date of assignment.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, time, required): Enter the time of assignment.
* **Assigned Status** (`assigned_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of assignment.
* **Assigned Comment** (`assigned_comment`, text, optional): Enter a comment about the assignment.
* **Assigned To Manager Comment** (`assigned_to_manager_comment`, text, optional): Enter a comment for the manager.

### Page 4: Submission Information

* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted Status** (`submitted_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of submission.
* **Submitted Comment** (`submitted_comment`, text, optional): Enter a comment about submission.
* **Submitted To Manager Comment** (`submitted_to_manager_comment`, text, optional): Enter a comment for the manager.

### Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check all information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you need help, refer to your manager or HR department.
