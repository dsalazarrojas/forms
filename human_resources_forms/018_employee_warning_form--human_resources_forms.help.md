# Employee Warning Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and record employee warnings, providing a clear and structured format for collecting and tracking information about these incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the reason for the warning from the provided options.
2. Enter the reason for the warning in the text field.
3. Enter the date of the warning using the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter the time of the warning (optional).
5. Enter the employee's name.
6. Select the employee's department from the provided options.
7. Enter the supervisor's name.
8. Enter the warning details.
9. Select the recommended action from the provided options.
10. Select the next step from the provided options.
11. Enter the employee's response.
12. Enter the manager's response.
13. Enter the human resources manager's response.
14. Enter the employee's acknowledgement.
15. Enter the employee's signature.
16. Enter the manager's acknowledgement.
17. Enter the manager's signature.
18. Enter the human resources manager's signature.
19. Enter the date of the warning.
20. Enter the employee's department.
21. Enter the employee's name.
22. Enter the manager's name.
23. Enter the human resources manager's name.
24. Enter the submitted by.

* **Employee Response** (`employee_response`, text): Enter the employee's response.
* **Manager Response** (`manager_response`, text): Enter the manager's response.
* **Human Resources Manager Response** (`human_resources_manager_response`, text): Enter the human resources manager's response.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct reason for the warning from the provided options.
* Enter the date of the warning in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* Enter the employee's name and department correctly.
* Make sure to enter the manager's name and signature correctly.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
