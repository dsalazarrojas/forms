# Employee Idea Submission Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate and provide feedback on employee-submitted ideas. It is used to assess the feasibility, impact, and alignment of each idea with the company's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the submission date when the idea was originally submitted.
2. Enter the employee's name who submitted the idea.
3. Enter the employee's job title.
4. Select the department associated with the idea.
5. Specify who the idea was submitted to (e.g. supervisor, manager, etc.).
6. Provide feedback on the idea.
7. Rate the idea using the provided scale.
8. Select the current status of the idea.
9. Choose the category or categories the idea falls under.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Date**: The date when the idea was originally submitted. Use the calendar icon to select the date.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who submitted the idea.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee who submitted the idea.
* **Department**: Enter the department associated with the idea.
* **Submitted To**: Specify who the idea was submitted to (e.g. supervisor, manager, etc.).
* **Feedback**: Provide feedback on the idea, including any comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Rating**: Rate the idea using the provided scale.
* **Idea Status**: Select the current status of the idea. Options include Active, Inactive, Approved, Rejected, On Hold.
* **Idea Category**: Choose one or more categories that the idea falls under. Options include General, Product Development, Marketing, Finance, IT.
* **Submitted Time**: (Optional) Enter the time when the idea was originally submitted. 
* **Feedback Date**: (Optional) Enter the date when feedback was provided. 

## Tips

* Ensure that each idea is submitted with accurate and relevant information.
* Use clear and concise language when providing feedback and rating.
* Select the most relevant categories for each idea.
* Use the "Active" status for ideas that are currently being pursued or implemented.
* Use the "On Hold" status for ideas that are not currently being pursued but may be revisited in the future.
