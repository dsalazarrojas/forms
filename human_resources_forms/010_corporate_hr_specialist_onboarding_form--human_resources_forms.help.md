# corporate_hr_specialist_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate HR specialist onboarding form is a tool for HR specialists to collect and store essential information about new employees. This form helps HR professionals gather necessary details to effectively onboard and integrate new staff into the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
*   Ensure that you have permission to share the information with HR.
*   Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the new employee, including their name and contact details.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Fill in the name of the person being onboarded.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide the email address of the new employee or the person being onboarded.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the new employee or the person being onboarded.
*   **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required: false): Fill in the date the new employee started working with the company.
*   **Manager Info** (`manager_info`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of the new employee's manager.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the new employee will be in the 'Yes' or 'No' department.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether the job description is 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required: false): Enter the new employee's ID number.

## Tips
Make sure to double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form. This ensures that HR has complete and up-to-date information to effectively onboard new employees.
