# Veterinary Employee Self-Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **Veterinary Employee Self-Assessment Form** is designed to help veterinary employees evaluate their performance and set goals for professional development. This form is used to:

*   Provide an opportunity for employees to reflect on their performance and identify areas of improvement.
*   Help employees set realistic and achievable goals for their professional growth.
*   Facilitate open communication between employees and their managers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Answer each question thoughtfully and honestly.** This form is a personal reflection of your performance and goals, so be sure to provide detailed and accurate information.
2.  **Complete each page carefully.** Review each section thoroughly to ensure you've provided all necessary information.
3.  **Submit your completed form.** Once you've finished, submit the form to your manager for review and guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Performance Rating** (Page 1): Evaluate your overall performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
*   **Strengths** (Page 1): List your key strengths and areas of expertise in your role as a veterinary employee.
*   **Professional Development Goals** (Page 1): Identify specific goals you aim to achieve in your career, such as learning new skills or taking on additional responsibilities.
*   **Performance Comments** (Page 1): Provide context and explanations for your performance rating.
*   **Feedback to Manager** (Page 2): Share any feedback or concerns you have for your manager.
*   **Performance Rating 2** (Page 2): Evaluate your performance on the same scale as before.
*   **Strengths 2** (Page 2): List any additional strengths or areas of expertise you've developed since the last assessment.
*   **Professional Development Goals 2** (Page 2): Update your goals from the previous assessment, or add new ones.
*   **Performance Comments 2** (Page 2): Provide context and explanations for your current performance.
*   **Feedback to Manager 2** (Page 2): Share any new feedback or concerns you have for your manager.

Note: This form is designed to be completed every 6-12 months to track your progress and growth as a veterinary employee. Be sure to review and update your answers accordingly.
