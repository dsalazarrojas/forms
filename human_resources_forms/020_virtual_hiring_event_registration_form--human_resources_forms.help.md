# Virtual Hiring Event Registration Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose

The Virtual Hiring Event Registration Form is designed to collect event and job details for virtual hiring events. This form helps the event organizers to gather necessary information from users and schedule events efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, select the appropriate options using the dropdown menus.
3. Enter relevant information for the text fields, such as event and job descriptions.
4. Make sure to agree to the user agreement by selecting "Yes" in the corresponding field.
5. Finally, submit your registration by selecting "Yes" in the "submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. User Information
* **user_information**: Enter your name and other relevant contact information.
* **contact_details**: Provide your email address and other contact details (optional).

### 2. Event Information
* **event_information**: Enter the event details, such as event type and location.
* **event_start_date**: Enter the start date of the event.
* **event_end_date**: Enter the end date of the event.
* **event_location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **event_type**: Select "Yes" if the event is a virtual event.
* **event_details**: Enter any additional event details (optional).

### 3. Job Information
* **job_details**: Enter the job title, type, and level.
* **job_start_date**: Enter the start date of the job.
* **job_end_date**: Enter the end date of the job.
* **job_status**: Select the status of the job (Active or Inactive).
* **job_type**: Select "Yes" if the job is virtual.
* **job_level**: Select the job level (High or Low).
* **job_salary_range**: Enter the job salary range.
* **job_description**: Enter the job description.
* **job_requirements**: Enter the job requirements.
* **job_benefits**: Enter any benefits offered by the job (optional).
* **job_notes**: Enter any additional job notes (optional).

### 4. Job Preferences
* **job_preferences**: Select your preferences for the job, including virtual and non-virtual options.
* **job_availability**: Select your availability for the job.
* **job_shift**: Select your shift preferences for the job.

### 5. Submit
* **submit**: Confirm your event registration by selecting "Yes".
