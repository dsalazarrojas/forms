# Scenario Based Technical Interview Question Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scenario Based Technical Interview Question Form is a tool designed to help interviewers and evaluators assess a candidate's technical skills and abilities. It gathers information about the candidate's name, rating of their performance in a given scenario, and any comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's name in the "Candidate Name" field.
2. Read the scenario presented and rate your performance in it using the "Rating" field.
3. In the "Rating Scale" field, select the numerical value that best represents your rating.
4. Use the "Comment" field to provide any additional thoughts or explanations you may have about your rating.
5. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate being assessed.
* **Scenario** (`scenario`, text, optional): Read and understand the scenario provided. This field is for reference only and not to be filled by the candidate.
* **Rating** (`question_rating`, number, optional): Rate your performance in the scenario on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the numerical value that best represents your rating from 1 to 5.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional thoughts or explanations you may have about your rating.
