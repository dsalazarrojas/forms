## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store job-related information. It is likely used in the human resources department for job postings, employee onboarding, or performance evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the job title in the **Job Title** field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the job in the **Job Description** field.
3. Enter any specific requirements for the job in the **Job Requirements** field.
4. Select the job type from the options provided in the **Job Type** field.
5. Enter the job location in the **Job Location** field.
6. Enter the start date of the job in the **Job Start Date** field.
7. Enter the end date of the job in the **Job End Date** field.
8. Enter the salary of the job in the **Job Salary** field.
9. Select the job status from the options provided in the **Job Status** field.
10. Select the job category from the options provided in the **Job Category** field.
11. Enter the priority of the job in the **Job Priority** field.
12. Enter any additional notes about the job in the **Job Notes** field.
13. Enter the email of the job owner in the **Job Email** field.
14. Enter the phone number of the job owner in the **Job Phone** field.
15. Enter the name of the job applicant in the **Job Applicant** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the job.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the job.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, required): Enter any specific requirements for the job.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of job from the options provided.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the job.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the job.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, text, required): Enter the salary of the job.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the job from the options provided.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category of the job from the options provided.
* **Job Priority** (`job_priority`, number, optional): Enter the priority of the job.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the job.
* **Job Email** (`job_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the job owner.
* **Job Phone** (`job_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the job owner.
* **Job Applicant** (`job_applicant`, text, optional): Enter the name of the job applicant.

Note: The fields marked as `optional` can be left blank if not applicable. 

Tips:
