# Inventory Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Specialist Referral Form is used to refer an employee to an Inventory Specialist for assistance with inventory-related issues or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To refer an employee to an Inventory Specialist, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's **Contact Information** on the first page.
2. Provide the employee's **Employee Name**.
3. Enter the employee's **Email** address.
4. Enter the employee's **Phone** number.
5. Specify the employee's **Department**.
6. Enter the employee's **Job Title**.
7. Provide the **Referral Source** (e.g., who referred the employee to the Inventory Specialist).
8. Optionally, enter a brief **Description** of the reason for referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is used to store contact information for the user who is referring the employee. 
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): The name of the employee being referred.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The employee's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The employee's phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department that the employee belongs to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): The employee's job title.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, text, optional): The person or service that referred the employee to the Inventory Specialist.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A brief description of the reason for referral.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are using this form for its intended purpose.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing the employee's contact information and description.
* If you are unsure about how to complete this form, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
