# texas_crime_prevention_agency_recruitment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Texas Crime Prevention Agency Recruitment Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in joining the Texas Crime Prevention Agency as a law enforcement officer. This form helps to capture essential details about the applicant, including their contact information, employment status, job preferences, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your current employment status from the "employment_status" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your desired job title in the "job_title" field.
7. Indicate whether you prefer to work on a shift or not in the "shift_preferred" dropdown menu.
8. Select your preferred work schedule from the "work_schedule" dropdown menu.
9. Choose whether you prefer a "Yes" or "No" job type in the "job_type" dropdown menu.
10. Indicate whether you work in a department or not in the "department" dropdown menu.
11. Enter your supervisor's name in the "supervisor" field.
12. Enter your emergency contact's first name in the "emergency_contact_first_name" field.
13. Enter your emergency contact's last name in the "emergency_contact_last_name" field.
14. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "emergency_contact_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name, text, required: false): Please enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (last_name, text, required: false): Please enter your last name.
- **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Employment Status** (employment_status, select_multiple, required: false): Select your current employment status.
  - Choose "Active" if you are currently employed.
  - Choose "Inactive" if you are not employed.
- **Job Title** (job_title, text, required: false): Enter your desired job title.
- **Shift Preferred** (shift_preferred, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you prefer to work on a shift or not.
  - Choose "Yes" if you prefer to work on a shift.
  - Choose "No" if you do not prefer to work on a shift.
- **Work Schedule** (work_schedule, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred work schedule.
  - Choose "Yes" if you prefer a specific schedule.
  - Choose "No" if you do not have a preference.
- **Job Type** (job_type, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you prefer a job or not.
  - Choose "Yes" if you are interested in a job.
  - Choose "No" if you are not interested in a job.
- **Department** (department, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you work in a department or not.
  - Choose "Yes" if you work in a department.
  - Choose "No" if you do not work in a department.
- **Supervisor** (supervisor, text, required: false): Enter your supervisor's name.
- **Emergency Contact First Name** (emergency_contact_first_name, text, required: false): Enter your emergency contact's first name.
- **Emergency Contact Last Name** (emergency_contact_last_name, text, required: false): Enter your emergency contact's last name.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
