# Early Departure Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to assist you in filling out the Early Departure Time Off Form, which is used to request and manage employee departures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your employee name, start time, and duration of the departure in the respective fields.
*   Provide a valid reason for your departure in the "Reason For Departure" field.
*   Select the person you would like to approve your request from the "Approval" field.
*   Add any comments or notes you would like to include in the "Comments" field.
*   Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
*   Confirm that you have submitted your request by selecting "Yes" in the "Submit" field.
*   Fill in the name of the person submitting the request in the "Submitter" field.
*   Finally, select a date for the departure in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Request Early Departure** (Text Field): This field is for the employee to briefly describe their reason for requesting early departure.
*   **Employee Name** (Text Field): This is where you enter your name as an employee.
*   **Start Time** (Time Field): Enter the time you plan to leave work.
*   **Duration** (Number Field): Enter the length of time you plan to be away.
*   **Reason For Departure** (Text Field): Give a valid reason for your early departure.
*   **Approval** (Select One): Select the person who you would like to approve your request from the options provided.
*   **Approved By** (Text Field): This field will be filled out by the person who approves your request.
*   **Comments** (Text Area): Add any additional comments or notes related to your request.
*   **Email** (Email Field): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (Text Field): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Submit** (Select One): Confirm that you have submitted your request by selecting "Yes".
*   **Submitter** (Text Field): Fill in the name of the person submitting the request.
*   **Date** (Date Field): Select the date you plan to depart.
