# Temporary Work Contract Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temporary Work Contract Extension Form is used to extend the contract of an employee who is currently working on a temporary or project-based contract. This form is intended to provide a formal way to request an extension to the contract end date for the employee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Details**: Please enter the employee's details in this field.
2. **Why are you extending the contract?**: Provide a reason for extending the contract in this field.
3. **Contract Extension Date**: Enter the new contract end date.
4. **Manager Approval**: Select the manager's approval status for the contract extension.
5. **Manager Name**: Enter the manager's name who is approving the contract extension.
6. **Manager Email**: Enter the manager's email address.
7. **Manager Phone**: Enter the manager's phone number.
8. **Manager Signature**: This field is not required but is a good place to record the manager's signature for approval.
9. **Employee Signature**: This field is not required but is a good place to record the employee's signature for commitment.
10. **Department Approval**: Select the department's approval status for the contract extension.
11. **Approval Date**: Enter the date of the approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details (employee_details)**: Enter the employee's name and ID.
* **Why are you extending the contract? (contract_extension_reason)**: Provide a reason for extending the contract in this field. This should be a detailed explanation of why the contract needs to be extended.
* **Contract Extension Date (contract_extension_date)**: Enter the new contract end date.
* **Manager Approval (manager_approval)**: Select the manager's approval status for the contract extension. Options are Approve or Not Approve.
* **Manager Name (manager_name)**: Enter the manager's name who is approving the contract extension.
* **Manager Email (manager_email)**: Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone (manager_phone)**: Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Signature (manager_signature)**: This field is not required but is a good place to record the manager's signature for approval.
* **Employee Signature (employee_signature)**: This field is not required but is a good place to record the employee's signature for commitment.
* **Department Approval (department_approval)**: Select the department's approval status for the contract extension. Options are Approve or Not Approve.
* **Approval Date (approval_date)**: Enter the date of the approval.

## Tips
* Ensure that only one form is submitted for each employee contract extension.
* Do not submit duplicate forms for the same contract extension.
* If any required fields are not completed, the form will not be accepted.
* Make sure to enter accurate and correct information in all fields.
