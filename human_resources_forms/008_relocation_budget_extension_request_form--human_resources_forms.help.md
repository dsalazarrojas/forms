# Relocation Budget Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow employees to request an extension of their relocation budget due to various reasons such as extended travel or additional expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and position.
2. Indicate whether you are requesting a budget extension.
3. Choose the department you are currently working in.
4. Select the location(s) you are traveling to.
5. Enter the date you plan to relocate.
6. Enter the date you plan to return.
7. Provide a brief explanation for your relocation request.
8. If applicable, provide any additional travel details.
9. Choose whether you will be taking time off.
10. Enter the requested funds.
11. If necessary, provide any additional information to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or employee records.
* **Position**: Indicate if you are requesting an extension to your current position.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are requesting a position extension.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not requesting a position extension.
* **Department**: Enter your current department name.
* **Location**: Select all locations you plan to visit or travel to.
* **Relocation Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your relocation.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return from your relocation.
* **Purpose**: Provide a brief explanation of your relocation request.
* **Travel Details**: If you have any additional travel details, provide them here.
* **Time Off**: Check 'Yes' if you will be taking time off for your relocation.
* **Funds Requested**: Enter the amount of funds you are requesting.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any additional information to support your request, provide it here.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Review the information provided before submitting the form.
- Double-check for any errors or missing information.
- Review the company's relocation policies and procedures to ensure you are in compliance.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant departments for clarification.
