# Conflict Resolution Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conflict Resolution Checklist Form is designed to help facilitators and HR representatives track progress, identify areas of agreement, and develop solutions to resolve conflicts between employees in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are in the correct context and have all necessary information and resources before filling out the form.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately to the best of your ability.
3. Refer to the provided hints for guidance on what each field means and what information is expected.
4. Review and update the form regularly to track progress and adjust your approach as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Date** (`form_date`): Enter the date when you started the conflict resolution process.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`): Enter the name of the person facilitating the resolution process.
* **Facilitator Role** (`facilitator_role`): Select the role of the facilitator (e.g., HR Representative, Manager, External Mediator, Team Lead, or Other).
* **Employee One Name** (`employee_one_name`): Enter the name of the employee involved in the conflict.
* **Employee One Department** (`employee_one_department`): Enter the department of the employee involved in the conflict.
* **Employee Two Name** (`employee_two_name`): Enter the name of the other employee involved in the conflict.
* **Employee Two Department** (`employee_two_department`): Enter the department of the other employee involved in the conflict.
* **Summary of Conflict** (`conflict_summary`): Briefly describe the conflict issue.
* **Duration of Conflict** (`conflict_duration`): Select the duration of the conflict (Less than 1 week, 1 to 4 weeks, 1 to 3 months, 3 to 6 months, or More than 6 months).
* **Previous Resolution Attempts** (`previous_attempts`): Indicate if there have been prior attempts to resolve the conflict (True or False).
* **Previous Attempts Details** (`previous_attempts_details`): Describe what was tried before.
* **Ground Rules Established** (`ground_rules_established`): Indicate if communication ground rules have been set (True or False).
* **Ground Rules** (`ground_rules_list`): List the agreed-upon ground rules.
* **Each Party Has Been Heard** (`each_party_heard`): Indicate if each party has shared their perspective (True or False).
* **Pertences Documented** (`perspectives_documented`): Indicate if all viewpoints have been recorded (True or False).
* **Common Ground Identified** (`common_ground_identified`): Select if areas of agreement have been found (True, False, or Partially).
* **Common Ground Details** (`common_ground_details`): Describe areas of agreement.
* **Underlying Issues Identified** (`underlying_issues`): Indicate if root causes have been explored (True, False, or In Progress).
* **Underlying Issues** (`underlying_issues_list`): List the root causes identified.
* **Solutions Brainstormed** (`solutions_brainstormed`): Indicate if potential solutions have been discussed (True or False).
* **Proposed Solutions** (`solutions_list`): List potential solutions discussed.
* **Action Plan Created** (`action_plan_created`): Indicate if a resolution action plan has been developed (True or False).
* **Action Plan Details** (`action_plan_details`): Describe the agreed-upon action steps.
* **Responsibilities Assigned** (`responsibilities_assigned`): Indicate if tasks have been assigned to specific people (True or False).
* **Timeline Established** (`timeline_established`): Indicate if there is a timeline for implementation (True or False).
* **Implementation Timeline** (`implementation_timeline`): Describe the implementation timeline.
* **Follow-Up Meeting Scheduled** (`follow_up_scheduled`): Indicate if a follow-up session is planned (True or False).
* **Follow-Up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date of the follow-up meeting.
* **Agreement Reached** (`agreement_reached`): Select if parties have reached mutual agreement (Yes - Full Agreement, Yes - Partial Agreement, No - Continue Mediation, or No - Escalate).
* **Employee One Signature** (`employee_one_signature`): Enter the name of the first employee.
* **Employee Two Signature** (`employee_two_signature`): Enter the name of the second employee.
* **Facilitator Signature** (`facilitator_signature`): Enter the name of the facilitator.

## Tips

* Ensure you are using clear and concise language when completing this form.
* Refer to the provided hints for guidance on each field.
* Review and update the form regularly to track progress and adjust your approach as needed.
* Use this form as a tool to facilitate effective conflict resolution, not as a substitute for in-depth mediation or counseling sessions.
