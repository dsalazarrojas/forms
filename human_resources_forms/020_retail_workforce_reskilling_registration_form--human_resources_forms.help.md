# retail_workforce_reskilling_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information for retail workforce reskilling programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the reskilling program you are interested in from the "Reskilling Program" field.
2. Provide your preferences in the "Reskilling Preferences" field (optional).
3. Enter your availability in the "Availability" field in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Select your preferred workshift in the "Preferred Workshift" field (optional).
5. Enter the location where you would like to receive reskilling training in the "Reskilling Location" field (optional).
6. Indicate how often you would like to receive reskilling training in the "Reskilling Frequency" field (optional).
7. Enter the start date of the reskilling program in the "Start Date" field (optional).
8. Enter the end date of the reskilling program in the "End Date" field (optional).
9. Enter the number of training hours you are willing to commit in the "Training Hours" field (optional).
10. Select where you would like to receive reskilling training in the "Training Location" field (optional).
11. Enter your preferred time for reskilling training in the "Preferred Time" field (optional).
12. Indicate the status of your reskilling in the "Reskilling Status" field (optional).
13. Enter any additional notes about your reskilling in the "Notes" field (optional).
14. Enter your employee phone number in the "Employee Phone" field (optional).
15. Enter your employee email in the "Employee Email" field (optional).
16. Enter your supervisor's phone number in the "Supervisor Phone" field (optional).
17. Enter your supervisor's email in the "Supervisor Email" field (optional).
18. Enter your manager's phone number in the "Manager Phone" field (optional).
19. Enter your manager's email in the "Manager Email" field (optional).
20. Enter your department head's phone number in the "Department Head Phone" field (optional).
21. Enter your department head's email in the "Department Head Email" field (optional).
22. Enter your store manager's phone number in the "Store Manager Phone" field (optional).
23. Enter your store manager's email in the "Store Manager Email" field (optional).
24. Enter your district manager's phone number in the "District Manager Phone" field (optional).
25. Enter your district manager's email in the "District Manager Email" field (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure your reskilling preferences are accurate and up-to-date.
* Enter your contact information accurately to ensure we can get in touch with you about your reskilling status.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the reskilling program, contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
