# Employee Accident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Accident Report Form is used to document incidents involving employees that have resulted in an injury or near-miss. This form helps ensure that incidents are properly reported and investigated, and provides a record of the event for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the **Report Form** page and enter a brief description of the incident.
2. Enter the **Date of Accident** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the **Time of Accident** in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
4. Select the **Location of Accident** from the provided options.
5. Provide a detailed **Description** of what happened.
6. List any **Witnesses** involved, if applicable. Select multiple witnesses if needed.
7. Enter the **Employee Name**, **Employee Title**, and **Employee Phone Number**.
8. If a witness was present, enter their **Witness Name**, **Witness Title**, and **Witness Phone Number**.
9. Enter the **Supervisor Name** who completed this report.
10. Enter the **Witness Signature** and **Supervisor Signature** to confirm the report.
11. Enter the **Report Date** and **Report Time**.
12. Enter the **Reported By** (the person submitting the report).
13. Enter the **Incident Number**, which may be provided by the supervisor or HR.
14. Enter a brief **Action Taken** in response to the incident.
15. Add any additional **Notes** regarding the incident.
16. Attach any relevant **Attachment** files, such as photos or videos.
17. Select the status of the report as **Report Completed** and choose the **Reported To** (e.g. HR, supervisor, etc.).
18. Enter any **Comments** about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form**: A brief description of the incident.
	+ Please enter a concise summary of what happened.
	+ Keep it brief, about 1-2 sentences.
* **Date of Accident**: Date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
	+ Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Accident**: Time in HH:MM AM/PM format.
	+ Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location of Accident**: Select from the provided options.
	+ Choose the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description**: A detailed description of what happened.
	+ Please provide as much information as possible about the incident.
* **Witnesses**: List any witnesses involved in the incident.
	+ Select all witnesses who were present.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name involved in the incident.
	+ This is the name of the employee who was involved in the incident.
* **Employee Title**: Enter the employee's title.
	+ This is the employee's job title.
* **Employee Phone Number**: Enter the employee's phone number.
	+ This is the employee's contact number.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the witness's name.
	+ This is the name of the witness.
* **Witness Title**: Enter the witness's title.
	+ This is the witness's job title.
* **Witness Phone Number**: Enter the witness's phone number.
	+ This is the witness's contact number.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the supervisor's name.
	+ This is the supervisor's name who completed this report.
* **Witness Signature**: Signature of the witness.
	+ Please sign to confirm.
* **Supervisor Signature**: Signature of the supervisor.
	+ Please sign to confirm.
* **Report Date**: Date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
	+ Enter the date the report was completed.
* **Report Time**: Time in HH:MM AM/PM format.
	+ Enter the time the report was completed.
* **Reported By**: Name of the person submitting the report.
	+ This is the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Incident Number**: Enter the incident number.
	+ This may be provided by the supervisor or HR.
* **Action Taken**: Briefly describe the action taken in response to the incident.
	+ Please provide a short description of the action.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the incident.
	+ Please use this field to add any additional information.
* **Attachment**: Attach any relevant files.
	+ Please attach any photos, videos, or other files related to the incident.
* **Report Completed**: Select the status of the report.
	+ Choose if the report is completed or not.
* **Reported To**: Select who the report was reported to.
	+ Choose the person or department the report was sent to.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the incident.
	+ Please use this field to add any additional information.
* **Attachment**: Attach any additional files.
	+ Please attach any additional files related to the incident.
