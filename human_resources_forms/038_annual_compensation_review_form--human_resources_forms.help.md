# Annual Compensation Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for the annual compensation review process, used by employers to evaluate and adjust an employee's salary based on their performance and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your employee information, including name, employee ID, department, and current position title.
2. Select your employment start date and the years of service you have with the company.
3. Enter your current annual salary.
4. If applicable, select the date of your last salary increase and the percentage of the increase.
5. Rate your overall performance and the achievement of your annual goals.
6. Indicate if you are satisfied with your benefits package.
7. If you are requesting a salary increase, propose a new annual salary and provide justification for the increase.
8. Select your bonus or incentive eligibility.
9. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID badge or in HR records.
* **Employee ID** (text): Enter your employee ID number as assigned by the company.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter your company email address.
* **Department** (text): Select your department or team name.
* **Current Position Title** (text): Enter your current job title or role.
* **Supervisor or Manager Name** (text): Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
* **Employment Start Date** (date): Select the date you started working with the company.
* **Years of Service** (number): Enter the number of years you have been working with the company.
* **Current Annual Salary** (number): Enter your current annual salary.
* **Employment History Header**: This is a note section to provide context on your employment history.
* **Date of Last Salary Increase** (date): Select the date of your last salary increase, if applicable.
* **Last Increase Percentage** (number): Enter the percentage of your last salary increase, if applicable.
* **Performance Rating** (select_one): Rate your overall performance as:
	+ Exceeds Expectations
	+ Meets Expectations
	+ Approaching Expectations
	+ Below Expectations
	+ Unsatisfactory
* **Achievement of Annual Goals** (select_one): Select if you:
	+ Exceeded all goals
	+ Met all goals
	+ Met most goals
	+ Met some goals
	+ Did not meet goals
* **Skills Development and Training** (text): Describe any training or skills gained during the past year.
* **Compensation Review Header**: This is a note section to provide context on your compensation review.
* **Proposed New Annual Salary** (number): Enter a proposed new annual salary, if you are requesting a salary increase.
* **Justification for Increase** (text): Provide a brief explanation for the proposed salary increase.
* **Bonus or Incentive Eligibility** (select_one): Select if you are:
	+ Yes, eligible for bonus
	+ Yes, eligible for incentives
	+ Both eligible
	+ No bonuses or incentives
	+ Review needed
* **Benefits Satisfaction** (select_one): Rate your satisfaction with benefits as:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (text): Add any additional comments or notes you would like to include in the review.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are requesting a salary increase, make sure to provide a clear justification for the increase.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled and your information is accurate.
