# Union Grievance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Union Grievance Intake Form is a tool for union members or staff to submit their concerns or complaints regarding the union or their employer. This form collects relevant information to help the union administration understand and respond to the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, including all relevant details about your grievance.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: Union Grievance Intake Form** (`union_grievance_intake_form_page_1`, text, optional):
	+ This is the main page for describing your grievance. Please provide a clear and concise summary of your issue.
* **Page 2** (`union_grievance_intake_form_page_2`, text, optional):
	+ This page is for providing more detailed information about your grievance. You can add any supporting documentation or additional context as needed.
* **Page 3** (`union_grievance_intake_form_page_3`, text, optional):
	+ You can use this page to add any further comments, questions, or requests related to your grievance.
	
## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any supporting documentation, such as evidence or relevant emails, you can attach them to your submission.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, you can contact your union administration for assistance.
