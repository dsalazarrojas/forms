# Accident Recovery Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Accident Recovery Leave Form" is designed to collect information from employees who have experienced an accident and are requesting leave for recovery. This form helps HR managers and supervisors to track and manage employee leaves due to accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Enter the employee's information, including their name, ID, department, position title, and supervisor's name.
3. Describe the accident that occurred, including the date, location, and type of accident.
4. Provide detailed information about the employee's recovery progress, including their current medical condition and any work restrictions or accommodations needed upon return.
5. Specify the expected duration of the leave and the type of leave being requested.
6. Indicate whether medical clearance is required before returning to work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (1): Enter the full name of the employee requesting leave.
* **Employee ID** (2): Enter the employee's identification number.
* **Department** (3): Enter the department where the employee works.
* **Position Title** (4): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Supervisor Name** (5): Enter the name of the employee's direct supervisor.
* **Accident Date** (6): Enter the date when the accident occurred.
* **Accident Location** (7): Enter the location where the accident occurred.
* **Accident Description** (8): Briefly describe how the accident occurred.
* **Accident Type** (9): Select the type of accident that occurred.
* **Injuries Sustained** (10): Describe the injuries sustained in the accident.
* **Treating Physician** (11): Enter the name of the physician treating the employee (optional).
* **Medical Facility** (12): Enter the name of the hospital or clinic where treatment was received (optional).
* **Leave Start Date** (13): Enter the date when the leave will begin.
* **Anticipated Return Date** (14): Enter the expected date of return to work.
* **Leave Duration** (15): Enter the expected duration of the leave.
* **Leave Type** (16): Select the type of leave being requested.
* **Work Restrictions** (17): Describe any work restrictions when returning (optional).
* **Accommodation Needs** (18): Describe any accommodations needed upon return (optional).
* **Medical Clearance Required** (19): Indicate whether medical clearance is required before returning to work.
* **Contact During Leave** (20): Enter the best way to contact the employee during leave (optional).

Note: Fields marked as optional (e.g., 11, 12, 17, 18, 20) can be skipped if not applicable.
