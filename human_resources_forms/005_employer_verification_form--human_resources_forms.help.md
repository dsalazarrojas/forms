# employer_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employer verification form is used to collect and verify employee work history information. This form is essential for HR and administrative purposes to ensure accurate employment records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the employee you are verifying.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (text, required): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **employee_title** (text, required): Enter the employee's job title as it appears on their business cards or official records.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/12/1999).
* **social_security_number** (text, required): Enter the employee's social security number. Ensure you have the correct format (e.g., XXX-XX-XXXX).
* **employer_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the current or previous employer as it appears on official records or business cards.
* **job_title** (text, required): Enter the employee's job title at the time of verification.
* **job_description** (text, optional): Provide a brief job description or summary of the employee's work responsibilities.
* **employment_start_date** (date, required): Enter the date the employee started working at the current or previous employer.
* **employment_end_date** (date, optional): Enter the date the employee ended their employment. If the employee is still working, leave this field blank.
* **reason_for_leaving** (text, optional): If the employee is no longer with the company, provide the reason for their departure.
* **verification_notes** (note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the employee's verification status.
* **background_check_status** (select, optional): Choose the status of the background check from the provided options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.

Note: If you are unsure about any field, it is recommended to contact the HR department or the employee directly for clarification. Ensure you fill out the form with accurate information to avoid any discrepancies.
