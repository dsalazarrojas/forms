# employee_drug_testing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_drug_testing_consent_form is a document that ensures employees understand and agree to undergo drug testing as part of the organization's program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Sign the form to indicate your consent to be tested.
3. Ensure you understand the testing process and your rights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Drug Testing Consent** (1): This is a note field that serves as an introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Employee Name** (2): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (3): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee Job Title** (4): Enter your current job title or position within the organization.
* **Department** (5): Enter your department or work location within the organization.
* **Testing Type** (6): Select the type of testing you will undergo:
	+ Random Urine Testing
	+ Hair Testing
	+ Blood Testing
	+ Breath Testing
	+ Oral Fluid Testing
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Test Frequency** (7): Select the frequency of the testing:
	+ Upon hiring
	+ Random testing
	+ Quarterly
	+ Semi-annual
	+ Annual
* **Reason for Testing** (8): This field is optional and should only be filled out if you select "Other (please specify)" for testing type. Enter a brief explanation for why you need to be tested under this type.
* **Test Frequency Location** (9): Select the location of the testing:
	+ On-site
	+ At a medical facility
	+ At home (in the presence of a healthcare professional)
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Test Frequency Duration** (10): If applicable, enter the length of time between tests.
* **Signed** (11): Check "Yes" if you have read and understand the form, and are willing to participate in the testing process. Check "No" otherwise.
* **Date Signed** (12): The date you sign the form, indicating your consent to be tested.
