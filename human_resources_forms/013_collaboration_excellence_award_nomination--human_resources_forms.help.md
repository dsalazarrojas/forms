# Collaboration Excellence Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collaboration Excellence Award is a recognition program that acknowledges and rewards exceptional collaboration and teamwork within the organization. This form is used to nominate individuals or teams that have demonstrated outstanding collaboration and teamwork.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your information as the nominator.
2. Select the award category and nominee type that best fits the nominee's work.
3. Provide the nominee's contact information and details about their department and job title.
4. Describe the project or initiative that demonstrates the collaboration and teamwork.
5. Provide specific examples of the collaboration and teamwork demonstrated, including challenges overcome and impact achieved.
6. Select the relevant collaboration behaviors and business outcomes achieved.
7. Add any additional comments or information.
8. Confirm your relationship with the nominee and your preference for confidentiality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Full Name** (`nominator_name`, `text`, required): Your name.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required): Your email address.
* **Your Department** (`nominator_department`, `text`, required): The department you belong to.
* **Your Job Title** (`nominator_title`, `text`, required): Your position.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, `date`, required): Date of submission.
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the award category that best fits the nomination.
* **Nominee Type** (`nominee_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of nominee (Individual Employee, Team, Department, etc.).
* **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required): The name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, `email`, optional): The email of the nominee (if applicable).
* **Nominee Department** (`nominee_department`, `text`, required): The department of the nominee.
* **Nominee Job Title** (`nominee_title`, `text`, required): The position of the nominee.
* **Team Members if Team Nomination** (`team_members`, `text`, optional): List all team members if nominating a team.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, `select_one`, required): Select how long the nominee has been with the organization.
* **Project or Initiative Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): The name of the project or initiative that demonstrates the collaboration.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Describe the project.
* **Example of Outstanding Collaboration** (`collaboration_example`, `text`, required): Describe specific collaborative actions.
* **Impact of Collaboration** (`impact_description`, `text`, required): Describe the result of the collaboration.
* **Challenges Overcome** (`challenges_overcome`, `text`, required): Describe the obstacles faced.
* **Stakeholders or Departments Involved** (`stakeholders_involved`, `text`, required): List the departments or stakeholders involved.
* **Collaboration Behaviors Demonstrated** (`collaboration_behaviors`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all collaboration behaviors demonstrated.
* **Measurable Outcomes Achieved** (`measurable_outcomes`, `text`, required): Describe the measurable outcomes achieved.
* **Business Impact** (`business_impact`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the business outcomes achieved.
* **Nomination Statement** (`nomination_statement`, `text`, required): Explain why the nominee deserves the award.
* **Supporting Documents Available** (`supporting_documents`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have additional documents available.
* **Has Nominee Been Nominated Before** (`previous_nominations`, `select_one`, optional): Check if the nominee has been nominated before.
* **Additional Witness Statements** (`witness_statements`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional witness statements.
* **Your Relationship to Nominee** (`nominator_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select your relationship with the nominee.
* **Should Nominee Know About This Nomination** (`confidentiality_preference`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the nominee should be informed about the nomination.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any other comments or information.
* **Direct Supervisor** (`direct_supervisor`, `text`, optional): Optional field for direct supervisor.
* **Peer** (`peer`, `text`, optional): Optional field for peer.
* **Direct Report** (`direct_report`, `text`, optional): Optional field for direct report.
* **Cross-Functional Partner** (`cross_functional_partner`, `text`, optional): Optional field for cross-functional partner.
* **Client or Customer** (`client_or_customer`, `text`, optional): Optional field for client or customer.
* **Other** (`other`, `text`, optional): Optional field for other.
