# software_developer_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for software developers to apply for a position within the company. It's essential to fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that we have the necessary information to assess your qualifications and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready before starting.
2. Fill out the contact information (page 1).
3. Enter your experience and qualifications (pages 2-4).
4. Attach your resume (page 7).
5. Provide information on your education and previous workplace (pages 11-12).
6. Enter your expected start and end dates, as well as any other relevant details (pages 12-13).
7. Fill out any additional sections as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your application.

### Page 2: Candidate Experience
* **Years of Experience** (`candidate_experience`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your years of experience in software development.

### Page 3: Languages
* **Languages** (`languages`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the programming languages you're proficient in, such as JavaScript, Python, Java, C++, C#, C, etc.

### Page 4: Availability
* **Available to Relocate** (`available_to_relocate`, `text`, required/optional): Check if you're willing to relocate for the position.
* **Desired Salary** (`desired_salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your desired salary for the position.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your expected notice period.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required/optional): Attach your resume in PDF format.

### Page 5: Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the skills you're proficient in, such as HTML, CSS, SQL, Node.js, JavaScript, React, UI/UX, Git, SVN, Agile, Scrum, etc.

### Page 6: Certifications
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, required/optional): List any relevant certifications.

### Page 7: Previous Workplace
* **Previous Workplace** (`previous_workplace`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your previous workplace, such as Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Facebook, Apple, IBM, LinkedIn, or Dell.

### Page 8: Education
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your education background.

### Page 9: Expected Dates
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you expect to start if you're selected for the position.
* **Expected End Date** (`expected_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you expect to end if you're selected for the position.

### Page 10: Additional Details
* **Current Salary** (`current_salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your current salary.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your expected notice period.
* **Notice Salary** (`notice_salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the notice salary.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you're available to start if you're selected for the position.
* **Notice** (`notice`, `text`, required/optional): Select your notice period.

### Page 11: Additional Section
* **Available to Relocate** (`available_to_relocate`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of Page 3, Available to Relocate.

### Page 12: Additional Section
* **Previous Workplace** (`previous_workplace`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of Page 7, Previous Workplace.

### Page 13: Additional Section
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of Page 8, Education.

### Page 14: Additional Section
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of Page 10, Expected Start Date.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `number`, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of Page 10, Notice Period.
* **Notice Salary** (`notice_salary`, `number`, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of Page 10, Notice Salary.
* **Notice** (`notice`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of Page 10, Notice.
