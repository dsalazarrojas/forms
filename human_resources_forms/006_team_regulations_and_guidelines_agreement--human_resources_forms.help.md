# Team Regulations And Guidelines Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to establish a clear understanding of the team's regulations, guidelines, and expectations, promoting a positive and productive work environment. It should be reviewed and adapted to fit your organization's specific needs and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page and section carefully.
2. Fill out the corresponding page(s) with your thoughts, ideas, and feedback.
3. Review the entire form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Team Regulations** (`team_regulations`, text, optional): This section is for listing any regulations or policies specific to your team or organization.
- **Guidelines** (`guidelines`, text, optional): This section is for outlining any guidelines that team members agree to follow for effective collaboration and communication.
- **Expected Benefits** (`expected_benefits`, text, optional): Describe the benefits you hope to achieve as a team, such as improved morale, productivity, or collaboration.
- **Responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, text, optional): List the roles and duties each team member is expected to fulfill.
- **Reporting Incidents** (`reporting_incidents`, text, optional): Outline the process for reporting any incidents, such as accidents, injuries, or security breaches.
- **Code Of Conduct** (`code_of_conduct`, text, optional): This section is for establishing a code of conduct that promotes respect, trust, and fairness within the team.
- **Code Of Conduct Purpose** (`code_of_conduct_purpose`, text, optional): Explain the purpose of the code of conduct and its importance in maintaining a positive work environment.
- **Code Of Conduct Expectations** (`code_of_conduct_expectations`, text, optional): Describe the expectations team members have for each other's conduct, such as respect, honesty, and fairness.
- **Termination** (`termination`, text, optional): This section is for outlining the procedures for termination of team members, including any necessary formalities or protocols.
- **Employee Termination** (`employee_termination`, text, optional): Describe the specific procedures for terminating team members, including any applicable laws or regulations.
- **Termination Procedure** (`termination_procedure`, text, optional): Outline the detailed steps for termination, including any necessary formalities or notifications.
- **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Schedule regular reviews to ensure the team is meeting its goals and expectations.
- **Performance** (`performance`, text, optional): Track the performance of team members, including any metrics or criteria for evaluation.
- **Performance Reviews** (`performance_reviews`, text, optional): Describe the process for regular performance reviews, including any necessary feedback or evaluations.
- **Disciplinary Action** (`disciplinary_action`, text, optional): This section is for outlining the procedures for disciplinary actions, including any necessary formalities or notifications.

## Tips
- Make sure to discuss and agree on the terms and conditions with all team members before submitting the form.
- Review and update the form regularly to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
- Store the form in a secure location, such as a shared drive or team folder, for easy access and reference.
