# Applicant Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Applicant Reference Form is used to gather feedback from referees about a candidate's qualifications and character. This form is completed by people who have worked with or supervised the candidate, such as former supervisors, colleagues, or teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Referee name, which is the name of the person providing the reference.
2. Select the Relation to applicant, which describes your relationship with the candidate.
3. Enter the Applicant name, which is the full name of the candidate.
4. If you have known the candidate for a duration, enter the time you have known them.
5. Evaluate the candidate's Reliability on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
6. Provide any Strengths you have observed about the candidate.
7. If you have any Areas for development, provide constructive feedback.
8. Indicate whether you would rehire or recommend the candidate.
9. Enter your Referee contact information, including phone or email.
10. If you have any Additional comments, please provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referee name** (`referee_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person providing the reference.
* **Relation to applicant** (`referee_relation`, text, required): Select the type of relationship you have with the candidate, such as supervisor, colleague, teacher, etc.
* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the candidate.
* **Duration known** (`duration_known`, text, optional): If you have known the candidate for a duration, enter the time you have known them.
* **Reliability rating** (`reliability_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the candidate's reliability on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional): Provide any key strengths you have observed about the candidate.
* **Areas for development** (`areas_for_development`, text, optional): If you have any constructive feedback, provide it here.
* **Would you rehire or recommend** (`would_rehire`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would rehire or recommend the candidate.
* **Referee contact information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including phone or email.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional remarks or comments you may have about the candidate.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when providing information about the candidate.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to the recruiter for clarification.
- Providing honest and constructive feedback is crucial in helping the recruiter make informed decisions.
