# employment_eligibility_verification_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The employment eligibility verification form is a tool used to verify the employment status of an individual. This form is used to gather information about an employee's employment history, including their name, Social Security number, date of birth, hire date, address, and other relevant details. The form helps employers and HR personnel to verify the employee's eligibility for benefits, tax credits, and other employment-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's Social Security number in the "Employee SSN" field.
3. Enter the employee's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the employee's hire date in the "Hire Date" field.
5. Enter the employee's address in the "Employee Address" field.
6. Enter the name of the employer in the "Employer Name" field.
7. Enter the company address of the employer in the "Company Address" field.
8. Enter the date of the last verification in the "Date of Last Verification" field.
9. Enter the name of the manager in the "Manager Name" field.
10. Enter the manager's email in the "Manager Email" field.
11. Enter the verification date in the "Verification Date" field.
12. Select the reason for termination in the "Reason for Termination" field.
13. Select the reason for separation in the "Reason for Separation" field.
14. Enter the last verification result in the "Last Verification Result" field.
15. Enter the reason for termination (if separated) in the "Reason for Termination (if Separated)" field.
16. Enter the reason for separation (if separated) in the "Reason for Separation (if Separated)" field.
17. Enter the employee ID number in the "Employee ID Number" field.
18. Enter the reason for termination (if employer) in the "Reason for Termination (for Employer)" field.
19. Enter the reason for separation (if employer) in the "Reason for Separation (for Employer)" field.
20. Enter the reason for termination (if employee) in the "Reason for Termination (for Employee)" field.
21. Enter the reason for separation (if employee) in the "Reason for Separation (for Employee)" field.
22. Enter a last verification note in the "Last Verification Note" field (optional).
23. Enter the last verification date in the "Last Verification Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being verified.
* **Employee SSN** (Employee SSN, number, required): Enter the employee's Social Security number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter the employee's date of birth.
* **Hire Date** (Hire Date, date, required): Enter the employee's hire date.
* **Employee Address** (Employee Address, text, required): Enter the employee's address.
* **Employer Name** (Employer Name, text, required): Enter the name of the employer.
* **Company Address** (Company Address, text, required): Enter the company address of the employer.
* **Date of Last Verification** (Date of Last Verification, date, required): Enter the date of the last verification.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, required): Enter the name of the manager.
* **Manager Email** (Manager Email, email, required): Enter the manager's email.
* **Verification Date** (Verification Date, date, required): Enter the verification date.
* **Reason for Termination** (Reason for Termination, text, required): Select the reason for termination.
* **Reason for Separation** (Reason for Separation, text, required): Select the reason for separation.
* **Last Verification Result** (Last Verification Result, select multiple, required): Select the last verification result.
* **Reason for Termination (if Separated)** (Reason for Termination (if Separated), text, required): Enter the reason for termination (if separated).
* **Reason for Separation (if Separated)** (Reason for Separation (if Separated), text, required): Enter the reason for separation (if separated).
* **Employee ID Number** (Employee ID Number, text, required): Enter the employee ID number.
* **Reason for Termination (if Employer)** (Reason for Termination (for Employer), text, required): Enter the reason for termination (if employer).
* **Reason for Separation (if Employer)** (Reason for Separation (for Employer), text, required): Enter the reason for separation (if employer).
* **Reason for Termination (if Employee)** (Reason for Termination (for Employee), text, required): Enter the reason for termination (if employee).
* **Reason for Separation (if Employee)** (Reason for Separation (for Employee), text, required): Enter the reason for separation (if employee).
* **Last Verification Note** (Last Verification Note, note, required): Enter a last verification note.
* **Last Verification Date** (Last Verification Date, date, required): Enter the last verification date.
