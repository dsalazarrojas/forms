# Pulse Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic information and opinions about your work environment, which will help us understand your experiences and improve our work processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Job Title, if applicable, in the **Job Title** field.
3. Select your department from the dropdown list in the **Department** field.
4. Enter your work email in the **Email** field.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Select your work environment from the dropdown list in the **Work Environment** field.
7. Enter your open time in the format `hh:mm` (e.g., 08:00) in the **Open Time** field.
8. Enter your close time in the format `hh:mm` (e.g., 17:00) in the **Close Time** field.
9. Enter the number of hours you work per week in the **Work Hours** field.
10. Enter your annual salary in the **Salary** field.
11. Provide any comments about your work experience in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title**: If applicable, enter your job title within the company. 
* **Department**: Select your department from the dropdown list: Engineering, Sales, HR, Operations, or Marketing.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Environment**: Select your work environment: Pleasant, Average, or Not Pleasant.
* **Open Time**: Enter your open time in the format `hh:mm`.
* **Close Time**: Enter your close time in the format `hh:mm`.
* **Work Hours**: Enter the number of hours you work per week. 
* **Salary**: Enter your annual salary.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments about your work experience.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form.
* **Cancel**: Click this button to cancel your submission and return to the previous page.
