# Team Member Data Management Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect Team Member Data Management Consent from employees, allowing them to manage their personal data and consent to various data handling practices by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and make sure to answer truthfully.
2. Answer all required fields to ensure accurate data management.
3. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Consent** (`team_member_consent`, text, required): This field is for the employee to express their consent to data management practices.
* **Personal Data Handling** (`personal_data_handling`, text, required): This field is to describe how the organization will handle the employee's personal data.
* **Employee Data Management** (`employee_data_management`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of data management the employee consents to, such as 'Team Members' or 'Employee Directory'.
* **Data Submission** (`data_submission`, text, optional): This field is for additional information about data submission.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): This field is for the date the employee consents to data management.
* **Submission Tracking** (`submission_tracking`, time, optional): This field is for tracking the submission of the form.
* **Employee Data** (`employee_data`, select_multiple, required): Choose which types of data the employee allows to be managed, such as 'Email' or 'Phone'.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is for additional contact information. 

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully and answer truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our human resources department.
* Submit the form when finished to ensure accurate data management.
