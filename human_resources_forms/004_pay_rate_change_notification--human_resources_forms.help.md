# Pay Rate Change Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to notify HR of a change in their pay rate. It is used to communicate the new rate and the reason for the change.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Employee ID** field and enter the 6-digit code assigned to you by HR.
2. Select the **Manager ID** field and enter the 6-digit code assigned to your manager by HR.
3. In the **Reason for Change** field, briefly explain the reason for your new pay rate request.
4. Enter your **New Pay Rate** in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID to associate the pay rate change request with your personnel records. This field is not required for submission but is essential for processing.
* **Manager ID**: Enter the ID assigned to your manager by HR. This allows HR to notify your supervisor of the pay rate change request.
* **Reason for Change**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting a pay rate change. This helps HR understand the justification for the request.
* **New Pay Rate**: Enter the new pay rate you are requesting, as a numeric value.
