# Weekly Status Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the weekly status report from employees, which is essential for monitoring and managing work progress, employee performance, and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Employee" field.
2. Select your department from the "Department" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the hours you worked during the week in the "Work Hours" field.
5. Enter any relevant notes about your work in the "Notes" field.
6. Choose your weekly status (Weekly, Good, Fair, or Poor) in the "Weekly Status" field.
7. Enter your comments, if any, in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which will be used for further communication.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which will be used for urgent matters.
10. Enter the time you finished submitting the report in the "Submit Time" field.
11. Enter the date you finished submitting the report in the "Submit Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Select the department you belong to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as per your designation.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total hours you worked during the week.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any relevant notes about your work, such as challenges faced or milestones achieved.
* **Weekly Status** (`weekly_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your status for the week, using the following options:
	+ Weekly: You completed all your tasks and responsibilities on time.
	+ Good: You made significant progress, but faced some challenges.
	+ Fair: You faced some challenges, but made some progress.
	+ Poor: You did not meet your expectations, and faced significant challenges.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for urgent matters.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you finished submitting the report.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you finished submitting the report.
