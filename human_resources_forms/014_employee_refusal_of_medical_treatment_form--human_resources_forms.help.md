# Employee Refusal Of Medical Treatment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture an employee's refusal of medical treatment due to personal or philosophical reasons. The employee will acknowledge the risks involved and provide details of the treatment being refused.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and understand the implications of refusing medical treatment.
* Answer all questions and select the relevant options.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your refusal of medical treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Refusal Of Medical Treatment Form**:
  * (text, text, optional)
  * This is the main content section where you will describe your refusal of medical treatment.
* **Medical Treatment Details**:
  * (text, text, optional)
  * Please provide the specific details of the medical treatment you are refusing.
* **Employee Acknowledgement**:
  * (select_one, I understand the risks of refusal of medical treatment, I do not understand the risks of refusal of medical treatment, optional)
  * Select one of the options to indicate your understanding of the risks involved.
* **Employer Acknowledgement**:
  * (select_multiple, I am the line manager for this employee, I am not the line manager for this employee, optional)
  * Select one or both options to confirm your role in the employee's medical care.
* **Comments**:
  * (text, text, optional)
  * If you have any additional comments or explanations, please provide them here.
* **Date Of Refusal**:
  * (date, date, optional)
  * Enter the date you are refusing medical treatment.
* **Time Of Refusal**:
  * (time, time, optional)
  * Enter the time you are refusing medical treatment.
* **Employee Signature**:
  * (text, text, optional)
  * Sign your name to confirm your refusal of medical treatment. 

Note: Please make sure to keep a record of this form and store it securely with HR department.
