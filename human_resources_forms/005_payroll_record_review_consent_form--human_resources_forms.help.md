# Payroll Record Review Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Record Review Consent Form is a mandatory document that helps maintain transparency and accuracy in payroll management by obtaining employee consent to review their payroll records.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the type of review that applies to you:
    *   Manager: Review by your immediate manager
    *   HR: Review by HR representative
    *   Both: Review by both your manager and HR representative
2.  Enter your review date and time:
    *   Enter the date and time when you want to have your review
3.  Enter any comments or notes:
    *   You can use this field for any additional comments or notes related to your review
4.  Select your reviewer:
    *   Manager: Review by your immediate manager
    *   HR: Review by HR representative
    *   Both: Review by both your manager and HR representative
5.  Enter your name and department:
    *   Enter your name as it appears on your payroll record
    *   Enter your department as it appears on your payroll record
6.  Sign below:
    *   This is your opportunity to sign below to confirm your consent for the review

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Review Type** (`review_1`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of review you want to have. This is a mandatory field to ensure that the payroll records are reviewed as per your preference.
*   **Review Date** (`date_1`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you want to have your review. This is a required field to ensure that the review is conducted on the agreed-upon date.
*   **Review Time** (`time_1`, `time`, required): Enter the time when you want to have your review. This is a required field to ensure that the review is conducted at the agreed-upon time.
*   **Note** (`note_1`, `note`, required): You can use this field for any additional comments or notes related to your review.
*   **Reviewer's Comments** (`review_2`, `text`, required): This field is for additional comments from the reviewer.
*   **Reviewed By** (`review_3`, `select_one`, required): Select the person reviewing your payroll records.
*   **Employee** (`employee_1`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your payroll record.
*   **Department** (`department_1`, `text`, optional): Enter your department as it appears on your payroll record.
*   **Signature** (`signature_1`, `text`, required): Sign below to confirm your consent for the review.
*   **Date Signed** (`date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you signed the consent form.
*   **Time Signed** (`time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when you signed the consent form.
*   **Additional Note** (`note_2`, `note`, optional): You can use this field for any additional comments or notes related to your review.
