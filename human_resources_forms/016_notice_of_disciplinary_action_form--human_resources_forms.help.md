# notice_of_disciplinary_action_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document a notice of disciplinary action taken by an organization. It is used to gather information about an incident or behavior that has led to a disciplinary action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Action Taken** field, and choose the type of disciplinary action taken (e.g., Verbal Warning, Written Warning, Termination).
2. Enter the **Date of Incident** and **Date of Action** fields, which should represent the date of the incident that led to the disciplinary action and the date the action was taken, respectively.
3. Enter the **Employee Name** and **Job Title** fields with the relevant information about the employee involved.
4. Provide any additional **Notes** about the incident or the disciplinary action taken.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Action Taken**: The type of disciplinary action taken (e.g., Verbal Warning, Written Warning, Termination).
* **Date of Incident**: The date of the incident that led to the disciplinary action.
* **Date of Action**: The date the disciplinary action was taken.
* **Employee Name**: The name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Job Title**: The job title of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Disciplinary Action**: Select one of the options listed to indicate the type of disciplinary action taken.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or context about the incident or the disciplinary action taken.
## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Check for similar incidents already documented before filling out this form.
* Save a copy of the completed form for future reference.
