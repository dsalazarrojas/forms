# Employee Growth Path Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Growth Path Pilot Application Form is a tool designed to help employees explore their growth paths and career aspirations within the organization. It's a self-assessment form that will help you, as an employee, to reflect on your interests, motivations, and goals, and to provide insights to management and HR teams to support your career development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill out the form in the following pages:
	* Page 1: Application Form (Page 1)
	* Page 2: Role and Tenure (Page 2)
	* Page 3: Motivation Details (Page 3)
2. Answer each question honestly, using the provided options or writing your own response if no option is available.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information, as it will help to get a better understanding of your career aspirations and development needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Application Form (Page 1)**
* **employee_name** (required): Your name as an employee.
* **phone** (required): Your contact phone number.
* **email** (required): Your work email.

### **Role and Tenure (Page 2)**
* **role** (required): Choose whether you have a key role in your current team. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
* **tenure** (required): Please enter your years of service to the organization.
* **motivation_details** (required): Choose your motivation for applying to this program. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.

### **Motivation Details (Page 3)**
* **motivation** (required): What motivated your interest in this growth path pilot program? Provide a brief description.
* **what_motivated_your_interest** (optional): If you have any additional information that motivated your interest in this program, please provide it here.

### **Submit Button**
* **submit_button**: Please review and submit your application form carefully.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language when answering questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to management or HR teams.
* Please submit the form only once, and not repeat or save it for later submission.
