# Vacation And Sick Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation And Sick Leave Form is used to request time off from work due to a vacation or illness. This form allows employees to submit a request for leave, including start and end dates, a reason for the request, and a status update from the manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the first page of the form, titled "vacation_and_sick_leave_form_1".
2. Enter your employee ID number in the "employee_id" field.
3. Select the start and end dates for your leave using the calendar or entering the dates manually.
4. Enter a reason for your leave in the "reason" field.
5. Choose the status of your leave from the dropdown menu (approved, pending, or denied).
6. Add any additional comments from your manager in the "manager_note" field.
7. Choose the employees who will cover your duties while you're away in the "submitter" field (this can be multiple employees).
8. Enter your manager's email address in the "submitter_email" field.
9. Enter your manager's phone number in the "submitter_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id** (number): Enter your employee ID number, as assigned to you by HR.
* **start_date** (date): Select the start date of your leave, using the calendar or entering the date manually.
* **end_date** (date): Select the end date of your leave, using the calendar or entering the date manually.
* **reason** (text): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting leave.
* **status** (select_one): Choose the status of your leave from the dropdown menu: approved, pending, or denied.
* **manager_note** (text): Add any additional comments from your manager regarding your leave.
* **submitter** (select_multiple): Choose the employees who will cover your duties while you're away.
* **submitter_email** (email): Enter your manager's email address, so they can be contacted regarding your leave.
* **submitter_phone** (text): Enter your manager's phone number, so they can be contacted regarding your leave.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide all required information to ensure your leave request is processed correctly.
* Check the status of your leave regularly to see if it has been approved or denied.
* If you are requesting multiple days off, you can enter the dates manually in the start and end date fields.
