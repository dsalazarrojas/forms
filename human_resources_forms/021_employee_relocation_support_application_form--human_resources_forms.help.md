# Employee Relocation Support Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help employees submit a formal request for relocation support when they are transferring to a new location or office. This form gathers essential information for processing the request, ensuring a smooth transition for both the employee and the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and title as they appear on your employee ID card.
2. Provide your current location and the new location you are requesting to be relocated to.
3. Enter the start and end dates for your relocation.
4. Explain the reason for your move in the provided text field.
5. Select your manager's approval status from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter your manager's name, email, and phone number.
7. Fill in the rest of the required fields with your company's information, including the name, address, email, phone number, and website.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes in the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it is currently assigned to you.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, text, required): Enter the current location where you are working.
* **New Location** (`new_location`, text, required): Enter the new location where you will be transferring to.
* **Reason For Move** (`reason_for_move`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for your relocation request.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your new location.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end your current location.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, number, required): Select "Approved" from the dropdown menu to confirm your manager's approval.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, select_one, required): Select "Approved" from the dropdown menu to confirm your manager's approval status.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you will be working in at the new location.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team at the new location.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, required): Enter the name of your team lead at the new location.
* **Team Contact** (`team_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for your team at the new location.
* **Relocation Request Date** (`relocation_request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this relocation request.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you will be working for at the new location.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the company you will be working for at the new location.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the company you will be working for at the new location.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the company you will be working for at the new location.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required): Enter the website of the company you will be working for at the new location.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, text, required): Enter the name of the company representative at the new location.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your relocation request.

Please ensure you have filled out all fields accurately and completely before submitting this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or the HR department for assistance.
