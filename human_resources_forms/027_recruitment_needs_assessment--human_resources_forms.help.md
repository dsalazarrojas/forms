# Recruitment Needs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Needs Assessment form is a crucial tool used to document and assess the current and future recruitment needs of an organization. It helps in identifying gaps in talent acquisition, budget allocation, and timelines for various job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the job category and location from the provided options.
2. Enter the job title, job type, and job level as per the job requirements.
3. Provide the budget allocation for the recruitment process, which includes the total budget and breakdown of allocated expenses.
4. Choose the hiring manager responsible for the recruitment process.
5. Select the recruitment agency(s) to be involved in the process (if applicable).
6. Enter the job specifications, including job description, qualifications, and salary details.
7. Set the job posting date and time (if applicable).
8. Determine the job duration and job status.
9. Specify the job type (full-time, part-time, or contract).
10. Finally, review and submit the form for further assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (Job title, text, required): Enter the title of the job opening.
* **Job Category** (Job category, select_one, required): Select the category of the job (Sales, Marketing, Development, etc.).
* **Job Location** (Job location, text, required): Enter the location where the job is based.
* **Job Status** (Job status, select_one, required): Choose the status of the job (Open, Closed, Pending).
* **Job Priority** (Job priority, select_one, required): Select the priority of the job (High, Medium, Low).
* **Job Level** (Job level, select_one, required): Choose the level of the job (Entry level, Managerial, Executive).
* **Job Educational Requirement** (Job educational requirement, text, optional): Enter any specific educational requirements for the job.
* **Job Salary** (Job salary, number, required): Enter the salary for the job.
* **Job Currency** (Job currency, select_one, required): Choose the currency for the job (USD, EUR, GBP).
* **Job Posting Date** (Job posting, date, optional): Enter the date and time the job was posted (if applicable).
* **Job Posting Time** (Job posting time, time, optional): Enter the time the job was posted (if applicable).
* **Job Duration** (Job duration, number, required): Enter the duration of the job.
* **Job Type** (Job type, select_one, required): Choose the type of the job (Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Hiring Manager** (Hiring manager, select_one, required): Select the manager responsible for the recruitment process.
* **Recruitment Agency** (Recruitment agency, select_multiple, optional): Choose the recruitment agency(s) to be involved in the process.
* **Role Requirements** (Role requirements, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job role.
* **Budget Allocation** (Budget allocation, number, required): Enter the total budget for the recruitment process.
* **Timelines** (Timelines, select_multiple, required): Select the timelines for the recruitment process (Week 1, Week 2, Week 3, Week 4).
* **Recruitment Process** (Recruitment process, text, optional): Enter any specific details about the recruitment process.
* **Submission Date** (Submission date, date, required): Enter the date the form is being submitted.
* **Job Notes** (Job notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the job.
