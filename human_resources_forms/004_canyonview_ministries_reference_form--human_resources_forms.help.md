# Canyonview Ministries Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from someone providing a reference for an applicant. The purpose of this form is to help the reference provider provide a detailed account of their relationship with the applicant, including the duration of their relationship, how they know the applicant, and their evaluation of the applicant's qualifications and fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information to allow the applicant to contact you for reference.
2. Describe your relationship to the applicant, including how you know them and the duration of your relationship.
3. Evaluate the applicant's work quality, reliability, teamwork ability, communication skills, and other relevant qualities.
4. Confirm that the reference is accurate and truthful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reference Information**: This field is a note field where you can provide any additional information about yourself and your relationship with the applicant.
* **Your Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official ID or business card.
* **Your Title/Position**: Please enter your current job title or position.
* **Organization Name**: Please enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Your Email**: Please enter your professional email address.
* **Your Phone Number**: Please enter your best contact number.
* **Relationship to Applicant**: Please select how you know the applicant from the options provided.
* **How Long Have You Known Them**: Please select the duration of your relationship with the applicant.
* **Applicant Information**: This field is a note field where you can provide any additional information about the applicant.
* **Applicant Name**: Please enter the full name of the applicant.
* **Position Applied For**: Please enter the position the applicant is applying for.
* **Evaluation Questions**: This field is a note field where you can provide answers to the evaluation questions.
* **Work Quality Rating**: Please rate the applicant's work quality from the options provided.
* **Reliability Rating**: Please rate the applicant's reliability from the options provided.
* **Teamwork Ability**: Please rate the applicant's teamwork ability from the options provided.
* **Communication Skills**: Please rate the applicant's communication skills from the options provided.
* **Spiritual Maturity**: Please rate the applicant's spiritual maturity and character from the options provided.
* **Leadership Potential**: Please rate the applicant's leadership potential from the options provided.
* **Greatest Strengths**: Please enter the applicant's top 3 strengths.
* **Areas for Growth**: This field is optional and allows you to suggest areas where the applicant could improve.
* **Would You Rehire/Recommend**: Please select whether you would work with the applicant again.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments about the applicant.
* **Confirmation**: Please confirm that the reference is accurate and truthful.
* **Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date.
