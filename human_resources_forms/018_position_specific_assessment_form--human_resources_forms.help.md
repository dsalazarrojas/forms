# Position Specific Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Position Specific Assessment Form is designed to collect feedback or evaluations on specific positions within an organization to assess an employee or candidate's performance or fit for a particular role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's details, including their name, in the Candidate Details field.
2. Select the relevant role requirements that the candidate possesses.
3. Choose one or multiple evaluation criteria that are relevant to the assessment.
4. Enter a score using the Assessment Scale field to express your assessment.
5. Add any relevant notes about the candidate's performance or fit.
6. Enter the candidate's email address.
7. Enter the candidate's phone number.
8. Select the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (`Candidate Details`, text, required/optional): Enter the candidate's name and any other relevant information.
* **Role Requirements** (`Role Requirements`, select multiple, required/optional): Select all the role requirements that the candidate possesses, such as Leadership Skills, Collaboration, and Communication.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`Evaluation Criteria`, select one, required/optional): Choose one evaluation criteria that is most relevant to the assessment, such as Leadership Skills, Collaboration, or Communication.
* **Assessment Scale** (`Assessment Scale`, number, required/optional): Enter a score between 1 and 10 to express your assessment.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the candidate's performance or fit.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required/optional): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required/optional): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Submission Date Time** (`Submission Date Time`, date, required/optional): Select the date and time of submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully to provide a fair assessment.
* Use relevant evaluation criteria to ensure that the assessment is specific to the position.
* Enter the correct email address and phone number to ensure that the candidate can be contacted.
* Select the correct submission date and time to record the assessment accurately.
