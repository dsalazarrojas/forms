# ethics_compliance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ethics compliance declaration form is a crucial tool for employees to confirm their understanding and adherence to the company's ethics and compliance policies. This form will be used to collect information about each employee's involvement in any activities related to these policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information in the "General Information" section.
2. Enter your employee details in the "Employee Details" section.
3. Make a declaration in the "Declaration" section by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
4. Enter the date of submission in the "Submission" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (text)**: Enter your name, job title, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Employee Details (text)**: This is where you should enter your employee ID or any other unique identifier used to track your activities within the company.
* **Declaration (select_multiple)**: This field is a confirmation of your understanding and adherence to the company's ethics and compliance policies. Select "Yes" if you are aware and confirm your compliance, and "No" if you are not.
* **Submission (date)**: Enter the date you are submitting this form. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are completing this form accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- This form is a requirement for all employees, so please submit it promptly.

Note: This help guide is for internal use only, and the form is not intended to be used for external purposes.
