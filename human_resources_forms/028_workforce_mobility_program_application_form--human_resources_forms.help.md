# Workforce Mobility Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Mobility Program Application Form is designed to collect information about an employee's mobility program application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your employee name in the field provided.
2. Enter your job title in the field provided.
3. Indicate whether you have a reporting manager by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
4. Select your department from the dropdown list.
5. Enter your job function in the field provided.
6. Indicate whether you are part of a job family by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
7. Select your job group from the dropdown list.
8. Enter your job code in the field provided.
9. Enter your start date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
10. Enter your end date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
11. Indicate the reason for your leave by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
12. Enter your approval date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
13. Select your job status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
14. Select your approval status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
15. Enter any comments about your application in the 'Comments' field.
16. Enter your email address in the format 'email@example.com'.
17. Enter your mobile number in the format '123-456-7890'.
18. Enter your work number in the format '123-456-7890'.
19. Enter your reporting line manager's email address in the format 'email@example.com'.
20. Enter your reporting line manager's mobile number in the format '123-456-7890'.
21. Enter your reporting line manager's work number in the format '123-456-7890'.
22. Select your reporting line manager by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
23. Enter your reporting line in the format '123-456'.
24. Enter the date of your submission in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
