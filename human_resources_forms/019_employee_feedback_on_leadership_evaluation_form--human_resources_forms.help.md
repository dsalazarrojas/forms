# Employee Feedback On Leadership Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their leader's effectiveness in leading the team, their communication skills, and other relevant aspects. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to provide valuable insights on your leader's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the employee being evaluated in the "Employee Name" field.
2.  In the "Feedback On Leadership Effectiveness" field, provide a brief written feedback on your leader's performance.
3.  Select the level of effectiveness and communication skills you observe in your leader:
    •   Effective/Ineffective/Neutral
    •   Positive/Negative/Neutral
    •   Highly/Moderately/Not Very/ Somewhat Not Effective
    •   Empowering/Micromanaging/Neutral
4.  For some specific aspects of leadership, select all the characteristics that best describe your leader's behavior:
    •   Empowering
    •   Micromanaging
5.  In the "Leadership Skills" field, please enter a numerical value from 1-10 that represents your assessment of your leader's overall leadership skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
*   **Feedback On Leadership Effectiveness**: Provide a brief written feedback on your leader's performance.
*   **Communication Skills**: Select the level of effectiveness you observe in your leader. This can be Effective, Ineffective, or Neutral.
*   **Team Dynamics**: Select the level of team dynamics you observe in your leader. This can be Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
*   **Leadership Effectiveness**: Select the level of leadership effectiveness you observe in your leader. This can be Highly Effective, Moderately Effective, Not Very Effective, or Somewhat Not Effective.
*   **Leadership Style**: Select all the characteristics that best describe your leader's behavior. This can be Empowering, Micromanaging, or Neutral.
*   **Leadership Skills**: Enter a numerical value from 1-10 that represents your assessment of your leader's overall leadership skills.
*   **Communication Effectiveness**: This field is not currently active and should be ignored.

## Tips
Please keep your feedback honest and objective, and avoid giving false positives or negatives. Your input will help us better understand our leaders' performance and make informed decisions for future leadership development.
