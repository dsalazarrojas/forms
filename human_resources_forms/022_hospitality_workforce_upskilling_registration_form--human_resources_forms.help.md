# Hospitality Workforce Upskilling Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Workforce Upskilling Registration Form is designed to gather essential details from hospitality workers for upskilling training sessions. This form will help us better understand their preferences and needs, allowing us to tailor the training programs to meet their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
2. Please ensure that all required fields (marked as 'required') are completed before submitting.
3. Choose relevant options for 'Training Details', 'Training Location', and 'Assigned Tool' fields, as appropriate.
4. Enter your name, email, and date in the required fields.
5. Provide your preferred training location and time.
6. Add any additional notes or comments, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your official email address associated with your work account.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Provide your primary phone number for communication purposes.
* **Training Info** (`Training Info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your training preferences or requirements.
* **Training Details** (`Training Details`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more relevant training options from the list provided.
* **Training Location** (`Training Location`, select_one, required): Choose a suitable training location that best suits your needs.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session you are registering for.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Choose the preferred time of the training session you are registering for.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your training preferences or requirements.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, optional): Enter any specific tools that you may be assigned for the upskilling process. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any misunderstandings.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
* This form will be reviewed and processed by the HR department.
