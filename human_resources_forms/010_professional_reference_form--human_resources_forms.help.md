# Professional Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from professional references for job applicants, including their name, contact details, company, years of service, last contact date, and any additional comments. This form helps to streamline the hiring process by allowing applicants to provide their own references and making it easier for hiring managers to assess their qualifications and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as it appears on your professional reference's business card or ID.
2.  Enter your professional email address associated with your reference.
3.  Enter your professional title or position held at the company.
4.  Enter the number of years you have worked with your reference.
5.  Enter the date of your last contact with your reference.
6.  Provide any additional comments or information about your reference.
7.  Select "I agree" if you agree to the terms of the form, "I disagree" if you do not, or "Maybe" if you are unsure.
8.  If you selected "I disagree" or "Maybe", please provide your reasons for not agreeing to the terms.
9.  Enter any additional comments or notes about your reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **reference_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID. (Text, required: false)
* **reference_email**: Enter your professional email address. (Email, required: false)
* **reference_title**: Enter your professional title or position held at the company. (Text, required: false)
* **years_of_service**: Enter the number of years you have worked with your reference. (Number, required: false)
* **last_contacted**: Enter the date of your last contact with your reference. (Time, required: false)
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your reference. (Text, required: false)
* **agree_terms**: Select "I agree" if you agree to the terms of the form, "I disagree" if you do not, or "Maybe" if you are unsure. (Select One, required: false)
* **agree_terms_other**: If you selected "I disagree" or "Maybe", please provide your reasons for not agreeing to the terms. (Text, required: false)
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your reference. (Note, required: false)
* **signature**: Enter your signature. (Text, required: false)
