# employee-assistance-program-approval-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee-assistance-program-approval-form is a form designed to help employees request support or assistance from the organization's employee assistance program. This form will help you gather necessary information to evaluate and process your assistance request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
2. Enter your job title.
3. Enter the name of your department.
4. Describe the assistance you need from the program.
5. Choose the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee-name** (employee-name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **job-title** (job-title, text, optional): Enter your job title for reference purposes.
* **department** (department, text, optional): Enter the name of your department for reference purposes.
* **assistance-request** (assistance-request, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the assistance you need from the program.
* **employee-assistance-program-approval-form-submission** (employee-assistance-program-approval-form-submission, date, optional): Choose the date of submission.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for help.
- Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date to ensure smooth processing of your assistance request.
- You can submit the form only once per assistance request, so please be concise with your description of the need for assistance.
- The form will be reviewed and processed according to the organization's employee assistance program policies and procedures.
