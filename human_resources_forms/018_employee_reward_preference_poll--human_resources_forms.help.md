# Employee Reward Preference Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect employee preferences for rewards and recognition programs, helping HR create targeted and effective reward programs that cater to individual employee needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category for the reward preference poll from the "Category" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the categories and preferences that apply to you from the "Employee Preferences" select multiple field.
3. Select a type of reward from the "Reward Programs" select field.
4. Choose how frequently you would like to receive rewards from the "Frequency of Rewards" select multiple field.
5. If applicable, enter a specific reward amount in the "Reward Amount" field.
6. Provide any additional comments or preferences in the "Employee Preference 1" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, not required): Select a category for the reward preference poll, such as "Human Resources Forms" or "Other Forms".
* **Employee Preferences** (`employee_preferences`, select_multiple, not required): Choose one or more categories that apply to you, such as "Team", "Organization", "Department", "Job Title", or "Location".
* **Reward Programs** (`reward_programs`, select_one, not required): Select a type of reward program you would like to receive, such as "Monetary Incentives" or "Time Off".
* **Frequency of Rewards** (`frequency_of_rewards`, select_multiple, not required): Choose how frequently you would like to receive rewards, such as "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or "Quarterly".
* **Reward Amount** (`reward_amount`, number, not required): If applicable, enter a specific reward amount you would like to receive.
* **Employee Preference 1** (`employee_preferences_1`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments or preferences for rewards and recognition programs.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when selecting your preferences to help HR create effective reward programs.
- Use this form for creating or updating employee reward programs, and only fill it out by authorized HR personnel.
- If you have any concerns or questions about rewards and recognition programs, please contact your HR representative.
