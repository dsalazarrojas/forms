# Internal Candidate Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document insights, observations, and ratings from interviewers about a candidate's performance during internal job interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have completed both rounds of the interview.
2. Select the relevant page (First round or Second round) corresponding to the interview you have just completed.
3. Fill out the form fields as required for the selected page.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First round questions asked** (`first_round_questions`, `select_multiple`): List all questions asked during the first round of the interview.
* **Second round questions asked** (`second_round_questions`, `select_multiple`): List all questions asked during the second round of the interview.
* **Overall impression of the candidate** (`overall_impression`, `select_one`): Choose how the candidate's performance made you feel, selecting from Very positive, Positive, or Negative.
* **Feedback comments** (`feedback_comments`, `text`): Enter comments about the candidate's performance, if any.
* **Rating after first round** (`first_round_rating`, `select_one`): Rate the candidate after the first round, using numbers 1 through 5.
* **Rating after second round** (`second_round_rating`, `select_one`): Rate the candidate after the second round, using numbers 1 through 5.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`): Rate the candidate overall, using numbers 1 through 5.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Candidate name** (`candidate_name`, `text`): Enter the candidate's name.
* **Team member name** (`team_member_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the team member who conducted the interview.
* **Team member comments** (`team_member_comments`, `text`): Enter comments from the team member who conducted the interview.
* **Manager comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`): Enter comments from the manager who conducted the interview.
* **Manager name** (`manager_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the manager who conducted the interview.
* **Manager title** (`manager_title`, `text`): Enter the title of the manager who conducted the interview.
* **Manager department** (`manager_department`, `text`): Enter the department of the manager who conducted the interview.
* **Manager job title** (`manager_job_title`, `text`): Enter the job title of the manager who conducted the interview.
* **Manager email** (`manager_email`, `email`): Enter the email of the manager who conducted the interview.
* **Manager phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`): Enter the phone of the manager who conducted the interview.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`): Select the team member this form was assigned to, from chatjimmy or chatbobby.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`): Select the name of the person who submitted this form, from chatjimmy or chatbobby.
* **Submitted date** (`submitted_date`, `date`): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted time** (`submitted_time`, `time`): Enter the time this form was submitted.

Note: If you have any doubts or questions about any field, refer to the provided hints for guidance.
