# Payroll Account Validation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Account Validation Consent Form is designed to validate an employee's payroll account information, ensuring that the employee's account is up-to-date and accurate. This form is used to confirm the employee's consent to validate their payroll account, including their account details, employment status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your payroll account details**: Select the correct account type and enter your account number, name, and bank information.
2. **Enter your employee information**: Select your job position, department, job title, employment status, and other relevant details.
3. **Enter your manager's information**: Select your manager's name, title, and contact information.
4. **Confirm your consent**: Sign and date the form to confirm your consent to validate your payroll account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payroll Account**: Select the type of account you have (e.g., 'Account Number:', 'Account Name:', 'Bank Account Type:', 'Bank Name:', 'Routing Number:', or 'Tax ID Number:').
* **Employee Signature**: This field is for the employee to sign and date the form, confirming their consent to validate their payroll account.
* **Manager Signature**: This field is for the manager to sign and date the form, confirming their consent to validate the employee's payroll account.
* **Date**: Enter the current date to confirm the date of the form completion.
* **Manager Date**: Enter the date on which the manager signs the form.
* **Manager Name**: Select the name of the manager from the provided options.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Title**: Select the title of your manager from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Job Position**: Select your job position from the provided options.
* **Department**: Select your department from the provided options.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title from the provided options.
* **Employment Status**: Select your employment status from the provided options.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager ID**: Enter your manager's ID number.
* **Manager ID 2**: Enter your manager's second ID number.
* **Manager ID 3**: Enter your manager's third ID number.
* **Payroll Start Date**: Enter the start date of your employment.
* **Payroll End Date**: Enter the end date of your employment.
* **Manager Title 2**: Select your manager's title from the provided options.
