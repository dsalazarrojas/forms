# employee_success_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee success program application form is designed to help employees achieve their career goals and overcome specific challenges. It will be used to collect data on various aspects of an employee's job, skills, language proficiency, and employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details, including your name and contact information.
2. Provide information about your current job position and job title.
3. Describe your work experience and job duties.
4. Select your relevant skills, language proficiency, and indicate if you have referees or not.
5. Provide information about your education and employment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 - Employee Details

* **Employee Details**: A text field to enter your employee details. Please provide your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Contact Info**: A text field to enter your contact information. Please include your email address and phone number where we can reach you.
* **Job Position**: A text field to enter your current job position.
* **Job Title**: A text field to enter your job title.

### Page 2 - Additional Information

* **Work Experience**: A text field to describe your work experience. Please provide a brief summary of your relevant work experience.
* **Job Duties**: A note field to describe your job duties. Please provide a detailed description of your job duties.
* **Skills**: A dropdown menu to select your relevant skills. Please choose all that apply.
* **Languages**: A multi-select menu to select the languages you are proficient in. Please choose all that apply.
* **Referees**: A multi-select menu to indicate if you have referees or not. Please select all that apply.
* **Education**: A multi-select menu to indicate if you have a degree or certification. Please select all that apply.
* **Employment Status**: A dropdown menu to select your current employment status. Please choose between Active or Inactive.
* **Employment Type**: A dropdown menu to indicate if you are full-time or part-time. Please choose between Yes or No.

Note: Please ensure to review each field carefully and answer truthfully to get the most out of the employee success program. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the HR department.
