# Team Member Recognition Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Recognition Vote form is a tool used to allow team members to nominate and vote on team members for recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and make sure you understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Select the team members you would like to recognize by choosing "Yes" from the "Nomination" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the type of recognition you think the nominated team member deserves.
4. If you want to provide additional comments or details about your nomination, you can do so in the "Nomination Message" field.
5. Rate the nominated team member's impact on the team, using the "Impact Rating" field.
6. Optionally, rate your manager's rating of the nominated team member, using the "Manager Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination** (Select multiple): Choose the team members you would like to recognize. Selecting "Yes" will nominate the team member for recognition.
* **Nominee Id** (Select one): Not applicable for this form. Please ignore.
* **Nomination Impact Rating** (Number): Rate the nominated team member's impact on the team. Use numbers to rate their contribution.
* **Recognition Type** (Select multiple): Choose the type of recognition you think the nominated team member deserves.
* **Team Member Id** (Select one): Not applicable for this form. Please ignore.
* **Nomination Message** (Text): Provide additional comments or details about your nomination, if desired.
* **Manager Rating** (Number): Rate your manager's rating of the nominated team member, if applicable.
