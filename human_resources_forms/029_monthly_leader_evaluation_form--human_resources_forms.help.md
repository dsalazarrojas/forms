# Monthly Leader Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Leader Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess a team leader's performance during the evaluation period. The form is completed by the manager and provides a fair and structured evaluation of the leader's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, log in to your account and access the form. If you are having trouble finding it, consult with your HR department.
2.  Enter your name and the name of the leader you are evaluating in the respective fields.
3.  Review the performance indicators and select the ones that the leader has met or exceeded in the past month. Select all applicable indicators.
4.  Identify areas where the leader needs improvement in the past month. Select all applicable areas.
5.  Rate the leader's overall performance based on your feedback.
6.  Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the leader's growth and development.
7.  Finally, enter the date and time you completed the form and the IDs of the leader and manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form Title
*   **Monthly Leader Evaluation Form**: This is the title of the form that will be filled out by the manager to provide feedback on the leader's performance.

### Manager Name
*   **Manager name**: This is the field where the manager enters their name.

### Employee Name
*   **Employee name**: This is the field where the manager enters the name of the leader being evaluated.

### Performance Indicators
*   **Select the performance indicators that the leader has met or exceeded in the past month.**: This is a select multiple field where the manager selects the performance indicators that the leader has met or exceeded in the past month.
    *   High: The leader's performance was excellent and exceeded expectations.
    *   Medium: The leader's performance was good but not excellent.
    *   Low: The leader's performance was below expectations.

### Areas of Improvement
*   **Select the areas where the leader needs improvement in the past month.**: This is a select multiple field where the manager selects the areas where the leader needs improvement in the past month.
    *   Time Management: The leader struggles with managing their time effectively.
    *   Communication: The leader struggles with communicating effectively with team members.
    *   Team Work: The leader struggles with working effectively with team members.
    *   Leadership: The leader struggles with leading the team effectively.
    *   Other: Select this option if the leader's area of improvement is not listed above.

### Overall Evaluation
*   **Rate the leader's overall performance based on your feedback.**: This is a select one field where the manager rates the leader's overall performance based on their feedback.
    *   Excellent: The leader's performance was excellent and exceeded expectations.
    *   Good: The leader's performance was good but not excellent.
    *   Fair: The leader's performance was fair but not good.
    *   Poor: The leader's performance was poor and below expectations.

### Additional Comments
*   **Provide any additional comments or suggestions.**: This is the field where the manager can provide any additional comments or suggestions for the leader's growth and development.

### Date Submitted
*   **Date the feedback was submitted.**: This is the field where the manager enters the date and time they completed the form.

### Employee ID
*   **Employee ID number**: This is the field where the manager enters the leader's ID number.

### Manager ID
*   **Manager ID number**: This is the field where the manager enters their ID number.

### Evaluation Period
*   **Evaluate the leader during this period.**: This is the field where the manager selects the evaluation period in which they are providing feedback.
