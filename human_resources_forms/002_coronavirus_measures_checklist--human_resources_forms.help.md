# coronavirus_measures_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The coronavirus measures checklist is a form designed to collect information about the measures taken to prevent the spread of the coronavirus. It is intended for individuals who have been in contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19 or have traveled to a country with a high risk of transmission.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all relevant questions.
2. Enter your symptoms, if you are experiencing any, in the "symptoms" field.
3. Report your temperature, if you have had it measured recently, in the "temperature" field.
4. Describe your travel history, including any recent travel to countries with a high risk of transmission, in the "travel_history" field.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms you are experiencing, such as cough, fever, or shortness of breath.
* **Temperature**: Report your body temperature, if you have had it measured recently. This is not a requirement if you do not have access to a thermometer.
* **Travel_history**: Describe any recent travel, including countries visited, dates of travel, and modes of transportation used. This includes both domestic and international travel. 

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any question or field, contact your healthcare provider or public health official for guidance.
* This form is intended for individuals who have been in contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19 or have traveled to a country with a high risk of transmission. If you have not had direct contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19 or have not traveled to a high-risk country, you may not need to complete this form.
