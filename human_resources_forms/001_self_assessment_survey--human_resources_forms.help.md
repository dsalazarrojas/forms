# Self Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Self Assessment Survey is a form designed to gather information from employees about their self-assessment, including their department, contact details, and rating. This form helps managers and administrators evaluate employee performance and provide a comprehensive view of employee development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Self Assessment Survey, follow these steps:

1. Provide your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select your department from the list of available options.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Rate your performance on a scale of 1-5 in the "Rating" field.
6. Provide any comments about your self-assessment in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter the date and time of your assessment in the "Assessment Date and Time" field.
8. Finally, select "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your employee name. Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your department from the list of available options.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments** (`comment`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your performance.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your self-assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your self-assessment.
* **Assessment Date and Time** (`assessment_date_time`, `date`, required: false): Enter both the date and time of your self-assessment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form.
  Please note that some fields are optional and only required if specified.
