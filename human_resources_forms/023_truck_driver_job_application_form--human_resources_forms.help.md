# Truck Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Truck Driver Job Application Form is designed to collect essential information from applicants interested in becoming truck drivers. This form will be used to assess the qualifications and suitability of potential truck drivers for our company's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2. Double-check your email address and phone number for correct spelling.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. If you don't have a valid license number, please provide your driver's license status (e.g., "None" or "In progress").
5. For the "Experience years" field, please enter the number of years you have been driving a truck.
6. Select "Yes" if you are available to drive and "No" otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your personal email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social security number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Your social security number or a valid reason for not having one.
* **License number** (`license_number`, text, required): Your valid truck driver's license number. If you don't have a license, please provide your driver's license status.
* **Experience years** (`experience_years`, number, required): The number of years you have been driving a truck.
* **Available to drive** (`available_to_drive`, select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you are available to drive, "No" otherwise.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Any additional information you'd like to share about your application.
