# Workplace Change Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Change Update Form is used to document and manage changes to employee work details. Fill out this form to update information about a change of location, department, or manager, and get manager approval for the changes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form sections one by one. Each section is labeled with a page number and section name.
- Fill in the required information, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
- Leave blank any sections that do not apply to your specific case.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New location**: This is where you enter the new location of the workplace. Enter the exact address, floor, and room number if necessary.
* **Reason for change**: Briefly describe the reason for the change, such as a move to a different team or a change in department.
* **Manager Approval**: Select the manager who should approve this change. Options are Approve, Reject, and Pending.
* **Updated By**: Choose who made this update. Options are Employee, Manager, or System.
* **Updated On**: Enter the date the update was made.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about the change that is not covered in other sections.
* **New manager**: If changing the manager of an employee, select the new manager from the list.
* **Old location**: If the workplace is being moved, enter the old location.
* **Old department**: If the department is being changed, enter the old department name.
* **New department**: If the department is being changed, enter the new department name.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your HR handbook or contact the HR team.
- Submit the form once all information is complete and accurate.
- After submitting, ensure that the manager receives the form for approval and that the update is recorded in the HR system.
