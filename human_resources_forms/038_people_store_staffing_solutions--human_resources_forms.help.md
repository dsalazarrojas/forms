# People Store Staffing Solutions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide staffing information for a store, including address, contact details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store's address in the **store_address** field.
2. Select one or multiple **staffing_location** options that apply to your store.
3. Provide any additional notes about the staffing location in the **staffing_location_note** field.
4. Enter the name of the contact person in the **contact_name** field.
5. Enter the contact person's email address in the **contact_email** field.
6. Enter the contact person's phone number in the **contact_phone** field.
7. Select a date for the staffing solution in the **date** field.
8. Select a time for the staffing solution in the **time** field.
9. Choose how many staff will be working at the store in the **store_staffed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the store's address.
* **staffing_location** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or multiple locations where your store will be staffed.
* **staffing_location_note** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional notes about the staffing location.
* **contact_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Select a date for the staffing solution.
* **time** (Time, Optional): Select a time for the staffing solution.
* **store_staffed** (Select One, Optional): Choose how many staff will be working at the store.
