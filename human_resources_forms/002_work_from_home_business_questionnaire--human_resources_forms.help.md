# Work From Home Business Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees regarding their work from home business arrangements. It is used by HR to ensure accurate documentation and to facilitate effective management of work from home operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, as requested fields are marked as required.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for non-required fields.
3. Double-check your responses for spelling and grammar errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Work From Home Request** (`work_from_home_request`, text, required): Describe the reason for your work from home request, including specific dates and times you plan to work from home.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel records.
- **Department** (`department`, text, required): Identify the department you belong to for work from home purposes.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title as it appears on your personnel records.
- **Work From Home Date** (`work_from_home_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to work from home.
- **Work From Home Time** (`work_from_home_time`, time, required): Specify the time you will be working from home.
- **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): If you will be working from a different location, enter the address.
- **Work Equipment** (`work_equipment`, text, optional): If you will be working from home and using company equipment, describe the equipment you will be using.
- **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): If you will be working from home, enter your work phone number for emergencies.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and contact information.
- **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Work Address** (`work_address`, text, optional): If you will be working from a different location, enter the address.
- **Work Email** (`work_email`, text, optional): Enter your work email address for communication.
- **Work Notes** (`work_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information that will help HR facilitate your work from home arrangement.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
- Double-check your responses for spelling and grammar errors before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns regarding this form, contact your HR representative for assistance.
