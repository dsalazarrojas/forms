# Work From Home Job Opportunity - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Work From Home Job Opportunity" form is used to allow employees or job applicants to express their interest in job opportunities that can be completed from home. This form gathers necessary information about the job opportunity, such as category, description, pay rate, payment terms, application link, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Category of the job opportunity from the available options.
2. Enter a brief Description of the job opportunity.
3. Provide the Pay Rate for the job opportunity.
4. Enter the Payment Terms for the job opportunity.
5. Add the Application Link for the job opportunity.
6. Click "Apply" to submit your interest in the job opportunity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Work From Home Job Opportunity** (`job_opportunity`, text, Required/Optional): Enter a brief title of the job opportunity that describes the work-from-home position.
- **Category** (`category`, select_one, Required/Optional): Choose a category for the job opportunity from the available options. This will help categorize the opportunity and make it easier for others to find.
  - Available options: Human Resources Forms, Other Categories
- **Description** (`description`, text, Required/Optional): Enter a brief description of the job opportunity. This should be a short and concise summary of the work-from-home position.
- **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, number, Required/Optional): Enter the pay rate for the job opportunity. This is the compensation offered for the work-from-home position.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the payment terms for the job opportunity. This should include any relevant payment information, such as frequency and method of payment.
- **Application Link** (`application_link`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the link to the application form or website for the job opportunity.
- **Apply** (`apply`, text, Required/Optional): Click "Apply" to submit your interest in the job opportunity.
