# performance_management_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "performance_management_registration_form" is a vital tool used to collect and manage employee performance data for performance evaluations and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Details**: Enter the employee's details, including their name, department, location, and employee ID.
2. Select the evaluation period and frequency that applies to the employee.
3. Choose the type of evaluation required (performance, development, or both).
4. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
5. Provide any additional evaluation comments or notes.
6. Enter the employee's email address and phone number for communication purposes.
7. Select the department and location where the employee is based.
8. Enter the employee's supervisor's name and title.
9. Enter the name of the evaluator or reviewer.
10. Set the status of the evaluation (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
11. Enter any additional evaluation comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (Employee ID, Employee Name): Provide your employee's ID and name for accurate identification and registration.
* **Supervisor Details** (Supervisor Name, Supervisor Title): Enter your supervisor's name and title to ensure proper chain of command and accountability.
* **Evaluation Period** (1, 2, 3): Select the evaluation period that applies to the employee (1, 2, or 3).
* **Evaluation Frequency** (Quarterly, Semiannually, Annually): Choose the frequency of evaluations required for the employee.
* **Evaluation Type** (Performance, Development, Both): Select the type of evaluation required (performance, development, or both).
* **Evaluation Date** (Date): Enter the date of the evaluation for accurate record-keeping.
* **Evaluation Time** (Time): Enter the time of the evaluation for precise scheduling.
* **Evaluation Note** (Note): Provide any additional comments or notes for the evaluation.
* **Feedback** (Feedback): Enter any feedback or comments for the employee.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the employee's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter the employee's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Department** (Department): Select the department where the employee is based.
* **Location** (Location): Select the location where the employee is based.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID): Enter the employee's ID for accurate identification.
* **Supervisor Name** (Supervisor Name): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Title** (Supervisor Title): Enter the supervisor's title.
* **Reviewer Name** (Reviewer Name): Enter the name of the evaluator or reviewer.
* **Evaluator** (Evaluator): Enter the name of the evaluator or reviewer.
* **Evaluation Status** (Evaluation Status): Set the status of the evaluation (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Evaluation Comments** (Evaluation Comments): Enter any additional comments or notes for the evaluation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid errors and duplication.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, consult with the HR department or performance management team for guidance.
