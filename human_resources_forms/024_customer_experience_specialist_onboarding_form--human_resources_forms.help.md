# Customer Experience Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about new Customer Experience Specialists onboarding into the organization. This includes personal details, job information, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First name and Last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. If you have been hired before, enter your Date of Hire.
4. Select your relevant training requirements from the list.
5. Provide emergency contact information for the person responsible for emergency contact, including their name and relationship to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name, as you would like it to be displayed on company records and communications.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name, as you would like it to be displayed on company records and communications.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title, which will help us understand your role within the organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address, which will be used for internal communications and records.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, date, optional): If you have been hired before, please enter the date you were hired.
* **Training requirements** (`training_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Select the training courses relevant to your role as a Customer Experience Specialist.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your HR representative.
- Once completed, please submit the form to initiate the onboarding process.
