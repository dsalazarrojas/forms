# Simple Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Job Application Form is used by candidates to provide their job history and qualifications when applying for a job within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, please provide the required information for each field.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Provide your contact phone number.
4. Describe your educational background and graduation date.
5. For each reference, provide their name, relation, and phone number (if applicable).
6. Enter the name of the job you are applying for and the company you worked for.
7. Enter your start and end dates for the job.
8. Enter your job title or role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your work or personal email address. Ensure it's the address you check regularly.
* **Phone**: Provide your work or personal phone number.
* **Education**: Describe your highest level of education completed (e.g., high school, degree, diploma).
* **Graduation Date**: Enter the date you completed your highest level of education (optional).
* **Reference 1 Name**: Enter the name of your first professional reference.
* **Reference 1 Relation**: Describe your relation to the reference (e.g., supervisor, colleague).
* **Reference 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your first reference (optional).
* **Reference 2 Name**: Enter the name of your second professional reference (optional).
* **Reference 2 Relation**: Describe your relation to the second reference (e.g., supervisor, colleague).
* **Reference 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your second reference (optional).
* **Job Name**: Enter the name of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Company**: Enter the name of the company you worked for during the job.
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the date you started the job.
* **Job End Date**: Enter the date you ended the job (if applicable).
* **Job Role**: Enter your job title or role during the job.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your HR representative for assistance.
