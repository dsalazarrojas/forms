# inspector_estimator_interview_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from an Inspector Estimator about their interview with a client. The form is typically used to capture details about the client's First Name, Last Name, contact information, job title, work experience, knowledge base, interview date and time, and specific questions related to the ISO 14001 standard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your Job Title in the Job Title field.
5. Describe your Work Experience in the Work Experience field.
6. Enter your Knowledge Base in the Knowledge Base field.
7. Select the Interview Date from the calendar and choose the Interview Time from the dropdown menu.
8. Answer the question "What is the name of the first step in the ISO 14001 standard?" in the Question 1 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title as it is known to you.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your work experience, including any relevant certifications, training, or experience you have.
* **Knowledge Base** (`knowledge_base`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the knowledge base you use for your work as an inspector estimator.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the interview from the calendar.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time of the interview from the dropdown menu.
* **What is the name of the first step in the ISO 14001 standard?** (`question1`, `text`, required/optional): Answer the question with the name of the first step in the ISO 14001 standard.
