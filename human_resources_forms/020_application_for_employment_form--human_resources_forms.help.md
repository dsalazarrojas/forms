# Application For Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Application for Employment Form is a tool for individuals to provide relevant details about their qualifications when applying for a job position within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the **Applicant Name** field.
2. Enter your preferred email address for communication in the **Contact Email** field.
3. If you wish to provide your phone number for contact, enter it in the **Phone Number** field (not required, but recommended for communication).
4. Select the position you are applying for from the **Position Applied For** dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred type of employment from the **Employment Type** dropdown menu.
6. If you have any education, please enter it in the **Education** field (not required, but recommended).
7. If you have any relevant work experience, provide a brief summary in the **Work Experience Summary** field (not required, but recommended).
8. If you'd like to provide references, enter their names and contact information in the **References** field (not required, but recommended).
9. Indicate the filename of your uploaded resume in the **Resume Filename** field (not required, but recommended).
10. Type your full name as an electronic signature in the **Electronic Signature** field, which is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: <i class="fa fa-user"></i> Your full legal name as per your identification documents.
* **Contact Email**: <i class="fa fa-envelope"></i> Your preferred email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: <i class="fa fa-phone"></i> Your contact phone number (not required, but recommended).
* **Position Applied For**: <i class="fa fa-briefcase"></i> Select the position you are applying for from the provided options.
* **Employment Type**: <i class="fa fa-building"></i> Choose your preferred type of employment from the options provided:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Contract
	+ Temporary
* **Education**: <i class="fa fa-grad-cap"></i> If you have any education, enter it here.
* **Work Experience Summary**: <i class="fa fa-briefcase"></i> Briefly describe your relevant work experience.
* **References**: <i class="fa fa-users"></i> Enter names and contact information for two references.
* **Resume Filename**: <i class="fa fa-file-text"></i> Indicate the filename of your uploaded resume.
* **Electronic Signature**: <i class="fa fa-id-card"></i> Type your full name as an electronic signature.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "required") before submitting the form.
- Use a clear and concise format for your answers to ensure easy reading.
- Use proper grammar and spelling for all entries.
- Make sure to attach your resume according to the filename indicated.
