# Employee Resource Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Resource Access Request Form is a tool used to request access to company resources, such as offices, equipment, or other assets, for work purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including the Employee Name, Resource Access Date, and Resource Access Time.
2. Select the requested resources and access type from the dropdown menus.
3. Add any additional comments or information as needed.
4. Have your manager review and approve your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee identification badge.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager who will be approving your request.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address where they can be reached for further communication.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number where they can be reached for further communication.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, required): This field is for any additional comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Resource Access Date** (`resource_access_date`, date, required): Choose the date you need access to the resource.
* **Resource Access Time** (`resource_access_time`, time, required): Choose the time you need access to the resource.
* **Resource Access Type** (`resource_access_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of access you need, such as "Access" or "View Only".
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of access you need, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Access Duration** (`access_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long you need access to the resource, such as "Month", "Month to Month", or "One Time Only".
* **Requested Resources** (`requested_resources`, select_multiple, required): Select the specific resources you need access to.
* **Resource Access Notes** (`resource_access_notes`, note, required): Add any additional information about your resource access request.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Choose "Approve" or "Reject" to indicate your manager's decision.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, required): This field is for any additional comments or feedback from your manager.

Note: All required fields are marked as "required" in the form, indicating that they must be filled out before submitting the form.
