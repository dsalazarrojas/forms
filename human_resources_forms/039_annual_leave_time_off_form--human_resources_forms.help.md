# Annual Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Leave Time Off Form is a form used to request annual leave from work. It's essential to use this form to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly and in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name, employee ID, and email address.
2. Select your department and position title from the available options.
3. Enter your manager's name, who will be notified about your leave request.
4. Specify the year for which you are requesting leave.
5. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Annual Leave, Vacation, etc.).
6. Enter the start and end dates of your leave.
7. Specify the total number of business days you will be taking.
8. Enter the date you will return to work.
9. If applicable, provide your current leave balance, balance after your request, and any additional comments.
10. Specify how you can be contacted during your leave and provide an emergency contact method.
11. If necessary, provide a work handover plan and a list of pending tasks.
12. Enter an out-of-office message for your team to respond to automated emails or calls.
13. If your manager has approved or denied your request, indicate the status and provide their name and approval date (if approved).
14. Finally, confirm the HR status (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. This is a required field.
- **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number. This is not required but helps to identify you as an employee.
- **Email Address**: Enter your work email address. This is the primary method of communication for your leave request.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number. This will help your manager and HR team contact you during your leave.
- **Department**: Select your department from the available options. This helps to categorize your leave request.
- **Position Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your official documents.
- **Manager Name**: Enter your direct supervisor's name. They will be notified about your leave request.
- **Hire Date**: Enter the date you were hired. This is required for tracking purposes.
- **Leave Year**: Select the year for which you are requesting leave. You can only select one year.
- **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave you are requesting. Select from the available options.
- **Start Date**: Enter the date your leave begins.
- **End Date**: Enter the date you will return to work.
- **Total Days Requested**: Enter the number of business days you will be taking.
- **Return Date**: Enter the date you will return to work after your leave.
- **Current Leave Balance**: Enter your current leave balance. This is not required but helps to track your balance.
- **Balance After Request**: Enter your leave balance after your request is processed.
- **Reason for Leave**: Enter an optional reason for your leave request.
- **Contact During Leave**: Select how you can be contacted during your leave. You can choose to be available, have limited availability, be unavailable, or have an emergency contact method.
- **Emergency Contact Method**: Enter your emergency contact method. This is only required if you selected to be unavailable.
- **Work Handover Plan**: Enter a plan for who will cover your work while you're on leave. This is not required but helps to ensure a smooth transition.
- **Pending Tasks**: List any pending tasks or work that needs to be completed before your leave.
- **Out of Office Message**: Enter an out-of-office message that will be sent to your team when you're not available.
- **Manager Approval**: Select the status of your manager's approval.
- **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager who approved or denied your request.
- **Approval Date**: Enter the date of your manager's approval or denial.
- **HR Approval**: Select the status of your HR's approval.
- **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your leave request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form correctly to ensure your leave request is processed in a timely manner.
- If you need to request other types of leave, please use the corresponding form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR department for assistance.
