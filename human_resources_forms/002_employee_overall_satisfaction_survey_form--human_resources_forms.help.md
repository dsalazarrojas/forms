# Employee Overall Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to measure overall employee satisfaction. The goal is to gauge employees' happiness and understanding of the company's mission, as well as their willingness to recommend the company to others. This information will help management understand areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance employee experience and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2.  Select an option for each field that best describes your feelings or thoughts.
3.  Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4.  If a field is not required, it is optional and you can skip it if you prefer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How happy are you?** (`overall_happiness`, `number`, optional)
	+ This question asks how satisfied you are with your overall experience working here. Select a number that best describes your happiness, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Do you understand our mission?** (`mission_understanding`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ This question asks if you have a clear understanding of the company's mission. Select one of the options that best describes your understanding, with each option providing a different level of understanding.
* **Would you recommend us to someone?** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ This question asks if you would recommend our company to someone. Select one of the options that best describes your willingness to recommend, with each option indicating a different level of recommendation.

## Tips
* Take your time to answer each question carefully and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, select the one that best fits your feelings or thoughts.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
