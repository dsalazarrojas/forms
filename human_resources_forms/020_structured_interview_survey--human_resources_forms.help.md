# Structured Interview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Structured Interview Survey is a tool used to collect feedback and information from interviewers about candidates during the structured interview process. This data helps to evaluate the candidate's suitability for the position and provides valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the candidate information and fill in the necessary fields, if any.
2. Answer each question in the survey, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the relevant answer options for question 3 (What were the strengths of the candidate?).
4. Enter the final candidate score in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the candidate, including their name, and any other relevant details that you would like to add.
* **What did the candidate say about company culture?** (`question_1`, text, required/optional): Describe what the candidate mentioned regarding company culture during the interview.
* **How well was the candidate prepared?** (`question_2`, number, required/optional): Rate the candidate's preparation level on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **What were the strengths of the candidate?** (`question_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the strengths that you observed in the candidate (Yes/No).
* **Final candidate score** (`candidate_score`, number, required/optional): Enter the final score for the candidate, based on your assessment.

## Tips
* Be as objective and honest as possible when answering each question.
* Select all relevant strengths for question 3, even if they are minor.
* Use the final score to help evaluate the candidate's overall performance.
