# job_citizen_self_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **Job Citizen Self-Assessment Form** is designed to collect information about an individual's current and expected job details. This form helps assess the individual's current job status, job experience, education, and career goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the given_name field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the last_name field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the email field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the phone field.
5. Enter your **Work Phone Number** (if applicable) in the work_phone field.
6. Enter your **Job Title** in the job_title field.
7. Enter your **Current Company** in the company field.
8. Enter your **Current Salary** in the current_salary field.
9. Enter your **Expected Salary** in the expected_salary field.
10. Select your **Work Experience** (in years) from the select_multiple options.
11. Enter your **Degree** (e.g., BSc, BEng, BCom, etc.) in the degree field.
12. Enter the name of your **School/University** in the school_name field.
13. Enter your **Major** (if applicable) in the major field.
14. Enter the date of your **Graduation Date** in the graduation_date field.
15. Enter the name of your **University** in the university_name field.
16. Select your **Diploma** (e.g., BSc, BEng, BCom, etc.) from the select_one options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you regularly use.
* **Phone**: Enter your personal phone number that you want to be contacted on.
* **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number (if applicable).
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title or expected job title.
* **Company**: Enter your current or expected company name.
* **Current Salary**: Enter your current annual salary.
* **Expected Salary**: Enter your expected annual salary.
* **Work Experience**: Select your current work experience in years.
* **Degree**: Enter your completed degree (e.g., BSc, BEng, BCom, etc.).
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school or university.
* **Major**: Enter your major (if applicable).
* **Graduation Date**: Enter the date of your graduation.
* **University Name**: Enter the name of your university.
* **Diploma**: Select your completed diploma (e.g., BSc, BEng, BCom, etc.).
