# Character Reference Letter Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Character Reference Letter Form is designed to gather professional feedback from individuals who have had a significant relationship with the applicant. This form is used to provide an unbiased evaluation of the applicant's character, integrity, and work ethic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are in a position to provide an honest and fair evaluation of the applicant.
2. Ensure the applicant's identity and professional context is provided.
3. Provide accurate and truthful answers for each section.
4. Be specific and concise in your responses.
5. Proofread your form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Author Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Current Job Title**: Provide your current job title or academic position.
* **Organization or Institution Name**: Enter the name of your current or previous organization or institution.
* **Contact Email for Author**: Enter a contact email address for follow-up and verification purposes.
* **Applicant Full Name (Subject)**: Enter the name of the applicant for whom this letter is being written.
* **Recipient Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization or institution receiving the letter.
* **Position or Program Applied For**: Select the most accurate description of the position or program the applicant applied for.
* **Nature of Relationship Description**: Briefly describe how you know the applicant.
* **Length of Relationship (Years)**: Enter the duration of your relationship with the applicant.
* **Frequency of Interaction Type**: Select how often you interact with the applicant.
* **Summary of Applicant's Integrity**: Provide a brief summary of the applicant's honesty and moral character.
* **Summary of Applicant's Reliability**: Provide a brief summary of the applicant's consistency and responsibility.
* **Summary of Applicant's Collaboration**: Provide a brief summary of the applicant's ability to work with others.
* **Overall Recommendation Status**: Select whether you recommend the applicant for the role.
* **Confirmation of Letter Accuracy**: Choose to confirm that you are providing honest and fair feedback.
* **Date of Submission**: The current date will be populated automatically.
* **Additional Comments or Information**: Add any other relevant context or information about the applicant.
* **Would you like to be contacted for a follow-up?**: Indicate whether you would like to be contacted for further discussion or verification.
