# Strategic HR Advisory Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic HR Advisory Program Registration form is designed to register a new HR advisory program, providing essential information about the program's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Program Name** field with the name of the program you want to register.
2. Enter your **Name** and **Email** to identify yourself as the contact person for the program.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for easier communication.
4. Select the **Focus Areas** relevant to the program (Yes/No).
5. State your **Goals** for the program.
6. Choose the **Enrollment Method** (Program Enrollment, Referral, or Other).
7. If you selected "Other" for Enrollment Method, fill in the **Other Enrollment Method** field with a brief description.
8. Select the type of program you are registering (HR Advisory Program, Business Advisory Program, or Both).
9. Enter the expected **Participant Count** for the program.
10. Choose the **Start Date** for the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you want to register. This is the title of the program that will be visible to others.
* **Your Name**: Provide your name as the contact person for the program.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Focus Areas**: Select all relevant focus areas for the program (Yes for areas that apply, No for areas that do not).
* **Goals**: Describe the goals and objectives of the program.
* **Enrollment Method**: Choose how participants will be enrolled in the program (Program Enrollment, Referral, or Other).
	+ If you selected "Other", describe the enrollment method in the **Other Enrollment Method** field.
* **Other Enrollment Method**: Provide a brief description of the enrollment method you selected (if not Program Enrollment or Referral).
* **Program Type**: Select the type of program you are registering (HR Advisory Program, Business Advisory Program, or Both).
* **Participant Count**: Enter the expected number of participants for the program.
* **Start Date**: Choose the start date for the program.

## Tips
* Make sure to review existing records before registering a new program to avoid duplication.
* Fill in all required fields to ensure accurate registration.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact your HR department for assistance.
