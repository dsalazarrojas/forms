# Digital Skills Self Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you assess your digital skills and knowledge in the field of digital media. It's meant to be completed by employees who are being evaluated for their technical abilities and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Choose the correct options for each question based on your current level of digital skills and knowledge.
3. Make sure to provide accurate information for each field.
4. If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
5. Fill out the form completely and submit it to your HR representative for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1
* **first_question**: Select the answer based on your current level of experience:
  + **Yes** (I have experience): Have you worked on a project that involved digital media production?
  + **No** (No experience)

### Page 2
* **last_project_experience**: Indicate your last project experience:
  + (Date of last project)

### Page 3
* **email**: Enter your work email:
  + (e.g., john.doe@company.com)

### Page 4
* **phone**: Enter your work phone number:
  + (e.g., 123-456-7890)

### Page 5
* **note**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your digital skills and knowledge:
  + (e.g., "I have experience with video editing and graphic design.")

### Page 6
* **skills_surveyed**: Select all areas you have experience with:
  + **Yes**: Have you worked on projects involving 3D modeling, animation, and visual effects?
  + **No**: Have you not worked on projects involving 3D modeling, animation, and visual effects?

### Page 7
* **knowledge_areas**: Select all knowledge areas you have experience with:
  + **Yes**: Do you have experience with programming languages like Python, JavaScript, or C++?
  + **No**: Do you not have experience with programming languages like Python, JavaScript, or C++?

### Page 8
* **performance**: Describe your performance in the last project you worked on:
  + (e.g., "I successfully led a team to complete a project under tight deadlines.")

### Page 9
* **skills_and_knowledge_areas**: Select all areas you have experience with:
  + **Yes**: Have you worked on projects involving data analysis, machine learning, and AI?
  + **No**: Have you not worked on projects involving data analysis, machine learning, and AI?

### Page 10
* **skills_knowledge_performance**: Select all areas you have experience with:
  + **Yes**: Have you worked on projects involving project management, time management, and communication skills?
  + **No**: Have you not worked on projects involving project management, time management, and communication skills?
