# Undertime Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request time off from work. It allows employees to submit their requests for leave, and the manager is able to approve or decline the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
2. Select the date and time you wish to be off from work:
	* **Date of Departure**: Select the date you would like to be off.
	* **Time of Departure**: Select the time you would like to start your leave.
3. Provide additional details about your request:
	* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
	* **Reason for Undertime**: Enter a brief explanation for why you are requesting time off.
	* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or details related to your request.
4. Confirm your manager's involvement:
	* **Assigned To**: Select "Yes" if you would like your manager to be notified of your request.
	* **Manager Approval**: Select "Yes" if you would like your manager to approve or decline your request.
	* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
	* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
	* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
	* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from your manager.
5. Submit your request: Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, click the "Submit" button to complete your request.

* Please ensure that you have checked the company's leave policy before submitting your request.
