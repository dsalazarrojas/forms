### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the job opening and make sure you are a good fit.
2. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* Pages 1-2: Provide information about the job you are applying for.
	* Page 3: Provide specific details and requirements about the job posting.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

#### Page 1: Job Application

* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text): A brief summary of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text): A more detailed description of the job you are applying for.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, text): Upload your resume to provide more information about your background and experience.
* **Job Application** (`Job Application`, text): A brief summary of your interest in the job.

#### Page 2

* **Company** (`Company`, text): The company you are applying to.
* **Location** (`Location`, text): The location of the company.
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, select_one): Choose the type of job you are applying for (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Job Level** (`Job Level`, select_multiple): Choose the level of the job you are applying for (Manager, Supervisor, Analyst, Developer).
* **Available Start Date** (`Available Start Date`, date): The date you are available to start the job.
* **Available End Date** (`Available End Date`, date): The date you are available to end the job.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, select_one): Choose the status of the job you are applying for (Active, Inactive).
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email): Your contact email for the job posting.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text): Your contact phone number for the job posting.

#### Page 3

* **Job Reference** (`Job Reference`, select_multiple): Choose whether the job requires a reference (Yes, No).
* **Job Reference Note** (`Job Reference Note`, text): A note about the job reference.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, text): A list of your skills that are relevant to the job posting.
* **Languages** (`Languages`, text): A list of languages you are proficient in.
* **Job Description Other** (`Job Description Other`, text): Additional information about the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (`Job Requirements`, text): A list of specific requirements for the job posting.
* **Education** (`Education`, text): A description of your education background.
* **Job Reference Other** (`Job Reference Other`, text): A note about the job reference.
* **Job Type Other** (`Job Type Other`, text): Additional information about the job type.
* **Job Level Other** (`Job Level Other`, text): Additional information about the job level.
* **Years of Experience** (`Years of Experience`, number): Your years of experience in the field.
* **Available End Date** (`Available End Date`, date): The date you are available to end the job.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, select_one): Choose the status of the job you are applying for (Active, Inactive).

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check the job posting for any specific requirements or details that may be missing.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the hiring manager for clarification.
