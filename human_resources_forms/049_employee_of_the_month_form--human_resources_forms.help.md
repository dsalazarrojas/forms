# Employee of the Month Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee of the Month form is used to recognize a standout employee for exceptional performance and contributions to the organization. This form is designed to capture key information about the employee of the month, including the department they belong to, the reason for the award, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and approvals before filling out the form.
2. Fill in the employee's ID number.
3. Select the department the employee belongs to from the options provided.
4. Select the manager's name from the options provided.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the award period.
6. Enter the reason for the employee's award.
7. Enter any additional comments from the employee and their manager.
8. Select the employee's month of award from the options provided.
9. Enter the year of the award.
10. Select any relevant awards or recognition for the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID** (employee_id, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the employee being recognized.
- **Department** (department, select_one, required): Select the department the employee belongs to.
- **Manager** (manager, select_one, required): Select the manager's name for the employee.
- **Start Date** (start_date, date, required): Enter the start date of the award period.
- **End Date** (end_date, date, required): Enter the end date of the award period.
- **Reason for Award** (reason_for_award, text, required): Enter the reason for the employee's award.
- **Employee Comments** (employee_comments, text, required): Enter any additional comments from the employee.
- **Manager Comments** (manager_comments, text, required): Enter any additional comments from the manager.
- **Awards or Recognition** (employee_of_the_month_award, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant awards or recognition for the employee.
- **Month of Award** (month_of_year, select_multiple, required): Select the month of the award.
- **Year of Award** (year_of_award, number, required): Enter the year of the award.
- **Employee of the Month Award** (employee_of_the_month_award, select_multiple, required): Select the award type.
