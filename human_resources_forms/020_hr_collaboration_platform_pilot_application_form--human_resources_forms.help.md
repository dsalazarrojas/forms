# hr_collaboration_platform_pilot_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who want to be part of a collaboration platform pilot application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name accurately and correctly.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name if you wish, or leave this field blank if you prefer.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address accurately and correctly.
- **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter your company's name accurately and correctly.
- **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your job title accurately and correctly.
- **Department** (Department, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options (Yes or No) that best describes your department.
- **Team Size** (Team Size, number, required): Enter the size of your team.
- **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start using the collaboration platform.
- **End Date** (End Date, date, required): Enter the date you expect to finish using the collaboration platform.
- **Time Period** (Time Period, time, required): Choose the time period you plan to spend on the platform.
- **Pilot Details** (Pilot Details, text, optional): If you have any additional details, you can add them in this field.
- **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): You can add any comments or explanations in this field.
- **Job Description** (Job Description, text, optional): Enter your job description, if required.
- **Job Requirements** (Job Requirements, text, optional): Enter your job requirements, if required.
- **Job Qualifications** (Job Qualifications, text, optional): Enter your job qualifications, if required.
