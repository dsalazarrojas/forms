# Entry Level Remote Assembly Line Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entry Level Remote Assembly Line Worker Application Form is a general application form for individuals interested in applying for an entry-level remote assembly line worker position. This form is designed to collect relevant information about the applicant's employment status, contact details, work experience, education, skills, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required to fill out the form accurately.
2. Select the correct option for your current employment status from the "employment status" field.
3. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
4. Provide your job title and a brief description of your work history.
5. List your relevant education and skills.
6. If applicable, provide information about your emergency contact person and their relationship with you.
7. Confirm whether you have an emergency contact person and indicate their phone number.
8. Upload your resume if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **employment_status**
* **employment_status** (select one): Select "Active" if you are currently employed, "Inactive" if you are not employed.
### **name**
* **name** (text): Enter your full name.
### **email**
* **email** (email): Enter your email address.
### **phone**
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
### **address**
* **address** (text): Enter your address.
### **job_title**
* **job_title** (text): Enter your job title or the job title you are applying for.
### **work_history**
* **work_history** (text): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
### **education**
* **education** (text): List any relevant education or training you have received.
### **skills**
* **skills** (text): List any relevant skills you possess.
### **certifications**
* **certifications** (text): If applicable, list any relevant certifications you hold.
### **languages**
* **languages** (text): If applicable, list any languages you are proficient in.
### **job_references**
* **job_references** (text): If applicable, provide information about your previous job references.
### **job_objectives**
* **job_objectives** (text): If applicable, provide a brief description of your job objectives.
### **emergency_contact**
* **emergency_contact** (text): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
### **emergency_relationship**
* **emergency_relationship** (select one): Indicate your relationship with your emergency contact person (e.g., family member, friend, colleague).
### **emergency_phone**
* **emergency_phone** (text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
### **emergency_phone_2**
* **emergency_phone_2** (text): If applicable, describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
### **employment_status_2**
* **employment_status_2** (select one): Select "Active" if you are currently employed, "Inactive" if you are not employed.
### **employment_status_3**

### **employment_status_4**

### **employment_status_5**
* **employment_status_5** (select one): Select "Active" if you are currently employed, "Inactive" if you are not employed.
### **employment_status_6**
* **employment_status_6** (select one): Select "Active" if you are currently employed, "Inactive" if you are not employed.
### **upload_resume**
* **upload_resume** (note): Upload your resume as a supporting document.
