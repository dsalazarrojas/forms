# pre_interview_form_pen_brothers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-interview form for Pen Brothers is designed to collect essential information from candidates. This form helps the hiring team to evaluate and process candidate applications efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your name, title, and other relevant information.
2. Enter your contact information, such as email and phone number.
3. Briefly describe your job application, including the position you're applying for and how you heard about the job.
4. List your educational background and qualifications.
5. List your relevant skills and experience.
6. Provide a brief overview of your job history.
7. Mention any referees or references you'd like to provide.
8. Share your availability for an interview or further discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (Personal Details)**: Enter your basic personal information, such as name, title, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)**: Provide your email address and/or phone number so the hiring team can get in touch with you.
* **Job App (Job App)**: Briefly describe the job you're applying for and how you heard about it. This could be a job ad, a referral, or a job fair.
* **Education (Education)**: List your educational background, including any relevant degrees, diplomas, or certifications.
* **Skills (Skills)**: Mention any relevant skills you have that align with the job requirements.
* **Experience (Experience)**: Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, company names, and duration.
* **Job History (Job History)**: Provide a brief overview of your job history, including any gaps or significant career breaks.
* **Availability (Availability)**: Share your availability for an interview or further discussions, such as a specific date or time range.
* **Referees (Referees)**: If you have any professional referees or references, please list their names and contact information.
