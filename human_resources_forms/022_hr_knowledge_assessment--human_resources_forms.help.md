# hr_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "hr_knowledge_assessment" form is used to collect information from employees about their knowledge, experiences, and performance within the organization. This form is typically used in performance evaluations or knowledge assessments to help HR personnel or managers evaluate employee skills and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have all the required information before starting to fill out the form.
2. For each field, provide accurate and honest information about your experiences and knowledge.
3. Use the provided options for select fields (e.g., job type, employee status) to select the most applicable option.
4. If a field is not required, you can still provide information if you have it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official records or identification.
* **Last Name** (`2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official records or identification.
* **Email** (`3`, email, required): Enter your official email address used for communication within the organization.
* **Date of Birth** (`4`, date, not required): If you want to provide your date of birth, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Hire** (`5`, date, not required): If you want to provide your date of hire, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment Status** (`6`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ active
	+ inactive
	+ other
* **Reason for Leaving** (`7`, text, not required): If you have left or plan to leave the company, please provide a brief explanation for your reason.
* **Job Title** (`8`, text, not required): If you have a job title, please enter it.
* **Job Description** (`9`, text, not required): If you have a job description, please enter it.
* **Department** (`10`, text, not required): If you are aware of your department, please enter it.
* **Manager Name** (`11`, text, not required): If you have a manager's name, please enter it.
* **Employee Status** (`12`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ full-time
	+ part-time
	+ freelance
	+ other
* **Job Type** (`13`, select_one, not required): Select one of the following options:
	+ full-time
	+ part-time
	+ freelance
	+ contract
* **Job Level** (`14`, text, not required): If you are aware of your job level, please enter it.
* **Years in Job** (`15`, number, not required): If you have been in your current job for a certain number of years, please enter it.
* **Years of Service** (`16`, number, not required): If you have been working for the company for a certain number of years, please enter it.
* **Notice Period** (`17`, text, not required): If you have a notice period, please enter it.
* **Reason for Leaving Company** (`18`, text, not required): If you have left or plan to leave the company, please provide a brief explanation for your reason.
* **Last Performance Rating** (`19`, select_one, not required): Select one of the following options:
	+ satisfactory
	+ meets expectations
	+ below meets expectations
	+ does not meet expectations
* **Reason for Leaving Last Company** (`20`, text, not required): If you have left or plan to leave a previous company, please provide a brief explanation for your reason.
* **What You're Looking for in Your Next Job** (`21`, text, not required): If you are looking for something specific in your next job, please enter it.
* **Why Leaving Current Job** (`22`, text, not required): If you have left or plan to leave your current job, please provide a brief explanation for your reason.
* **Last Employer** (`23`, text, not required): If you have previously worked for another company, please enter the company name.
* **Last Job Title** (`24`, text, not required): If you have a job title from your last employer, please enter it.
* **Last Job Level** (`25`, text, not required): If you are aware of your last job level, please enter it.
