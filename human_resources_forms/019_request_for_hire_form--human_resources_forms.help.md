# Request For Hire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Hire Form is used by hiring managers to provide information about the type of candidate they are looking for to fill an open position within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please leave them blank and move on to the next.
- If you need help with any of the fields, please reach out to the hiring team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Posting** (`job_posting`, text, optional): Please describe the position you are trying to fill.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the position you are looking to fill.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of candidate you are looking for (Software Developer, Data Scientist, or Other).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location for the position (e.g. city, state, or region).
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe the level of experience you are looking for.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, optional): Enter any specific qualifications or certifications required for the position.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter any educational background required for the position.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number, or that of the person who can answer questions about the position.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, or that of the person who can answer questions about the position.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the position, including responsibilities and duties.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to reach out to the hiring team.
