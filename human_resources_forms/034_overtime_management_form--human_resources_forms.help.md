# Overtime Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Overtime Management Form is used to manage overtime requests submitted by employees. It allows employees to request extra working hours and provides a framework for managers to approve or reject these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a valid employee Id.
2. Select the date for which you're requesting overtime.
3. Enter the number of hours you're requesting.
4. Enter your manager's Id (if applicable).
5. Select the type of overtime you're requesting (Regular or Emergency).
6. Choose the start and end times for your overtime period.
7. Enter the reason for your request (Business, Personal, or Other).
8. Select the status of your request (New, In Progress, Approved, or Rejected).
9. Enter any comments or feedback about your request.
10. Specify the type of overtime you are requesting (Regular or Emergency).
9. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Id** (Required: false): Enter your employee ID, a unique identifier assigned to you by the system.
* **date** (Required: false): Select the date for which you are requesting overtime.
* **hours** (Required: false): Enter the number of hours you are requesting for overtime.
* **Manager Id** (Required: false): Enter your manager's ID, a unique identifier assigned to them by the system.
* **Approval** (Required: false): Select whether your manager has approved or rejected your request.
* **Comment** (Required: false): Enter any comments or feedback about your request.
* **Request Reason** (Required: false): Select the reason for your request (Business, Personal, or Other).
* **Overtime Type** (Required: false): Select the type of overtime you are requesting (Regular or Emergency).
* **Start Time** (Required: false): Choose the start time for your overtime period.
* **End Time** (Required: false): Choose the end time for your overtime period.
* **Form Status** (Required: false): Select the status of your request (New, In Progress, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Select an option that best fits your situation**
