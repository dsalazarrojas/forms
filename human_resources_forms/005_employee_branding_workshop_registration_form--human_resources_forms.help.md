# Employee Branding Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering employees for the Employee Branding Workshop. Please use it to provide your information and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the `employee-name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the `phone` field.
4. Select a date for the workshop (optional).
5. Choose a time slot for the workshop (optional).
6. Add any additional notes about your registration (optional).
7. For the "select" fields, choose one or multiple options from the list. Note: These fields are for preferences or interests.
