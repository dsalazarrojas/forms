# Employee Job Shadow Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Job Shadow Program Application Form is a tool used to facilitate the application process for the Job Shadow Program. The form helps employees provide necessary information about themselves and their job shadow request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Info section with your name, employee ID, and other relevant details.
2. In the Job Details section, provide information about the job you are interested in shadowing.
3. In the Contact Info section, enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
4. In the Availability section, mention your availability for job shadowing.
5. In the Comments section, add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
6. In the Job Shadow Request section, specify your job shadow request.
7. In the Submission Info section, provide any necessary submission information.
8. In the Job Shadow Details section, provide details about the job you are interested in shadowing.
9. In the Job Shadow Sponsor section, identify the sponsor of your job shadow request.
10. In the Job Shadow Status section, track the status of your job shadow request.
11. In the Job Shadow Sponsorship Status section, track the status of your job shadow sponsorship request.
12. In the Submission Status section, track the status of your submission.
13. In the Job Shadow Sponsorship Request section, specify your job shadow sponsorship request.
14. In the Job Shadow Sponsor 2 section, identify the sponsor of your job shadow request.
15. In the Job Shadow Sponsor Status section, track the status of your job shadow sponsorship request.
16. In the Form Submitted section, confirm that you have submitted your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info (Text)**: This field is where you provide your personal details, including your name and employee ID.
* **Job Details (Text)**: In this section, you should provide information about the job you are interested in shadowing, including the job title, department, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info (Text)**: Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other way we can contact you.
* **Availability (Text)**: Mention your availability for job shadowing, including the dates and times you are available.
* **Comments (Text)**: Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share about your job shadow request.
* **Job Shadow Request (Text)**: Specify your job shadow request, including the job title, department, and any other relevant details.
* **Submission Info (Text)**: Provide any necessary submission information, including your submission date and time.
* **Job Shadow Details (Text)**: Provide details about the job you are interested in shadowing, including the job title, department, and any other relevant details.
* **Job Shadow Sponsor (Text)**: Identify the sponsor of your job shadow request.
* **Job Shadow Status (Text)**: Track the status of your job shadow request.
* **Job Shadow Sponsorship Status (Text)**: Track the status of your job shadow sponsorship request.
* **Submission Status (Text)**: Track the status of your submission.
