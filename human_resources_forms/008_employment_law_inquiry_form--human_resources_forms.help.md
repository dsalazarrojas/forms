# employment_law_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employment_law_inquiry_form" is used to submit new or follow-up employment law inquiries that require HR attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Contact Information" section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Select the type of employment you are inquiring about in the "Employment Type" section.
3.  Indicate your current employment status in the "Employment Status" section.
4.  Choose the area of employment law you are inquiring about in the "Employment Law Area" section.
5.  Briefly describe the employment issue you are experiencing in the "Employment Issue" section.
6.  Select your preferred contact method in the "Contact Method" section.
7.  Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (1, text, required): Enter your name and contact details so we can get back to you.
- **Employment Type** (2, select_one, required): Specify the type of employment you are inquiring about (e.g., 'Yes' for employment law inquiry).
- **Employment Status** (3, select_multiple, required): Indicate your current employment status (e.g., Employed, Unemployed, Student).
- **Select the employment status you are inquiring about** (4, select_multiple, required): Choose the status related to your employment law inquiry.
- **Select the employment law area you are inquiring about** (6, select_one, required): Choose the area of employment law you want to inquire about (e.g., Employment Contracts, Employment Benefits).
- **Briefly describe the employment issue you are experiencing** (8, text, required): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are facing.
- **Select your preferred contact method** (10, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Phone, Email, Video).
- **Please provide your name** (12, text, required): Enter your name for us to address you properly.
- **Please provide your email address** (14, text, required): Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
- **Please provide your phone number** (16, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to reach you.
- **Additional Comments** (18, text, required): Add any further details about your inquiry.
- **Who to assign this inquiry to** (20, text, required): Specify who from the HR team should handle your inquiry.
