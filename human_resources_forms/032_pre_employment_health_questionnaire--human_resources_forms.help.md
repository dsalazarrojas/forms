> # pre_employment_health_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-employment health questionnaire is designed to collect information about your health history, which will be used to assess your fitness for employment with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Please provide accurate and truthful information about your health status.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask the person assisting you for clarification.
4. Once completed, please submit the form to the person assisting you.

* **Personal Identification (Personal Identification)** (`personal_identification`, text, required): Please provide your date of birth, social security number, or any other government-issued ID number.
