# Online Staff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Staff Registration Form is used to register new staff members or update existing information. Please fill out this form to create or update your staff record.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). The required fields are: *Name*, *Contact Number*, *Job Title*, *Work Experience*, and *Job Status*.
2. Select the correct job title and category for your position.
3. Provide your contact details.
4. Fill out any other fields that you think are relevant to your role or position.
5. Review and submit the form before saving your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): This is your full name. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): This is your phone number or any other contact information you would like to provide.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): This is your work address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required): This is the title of your position. Please select one of the available options:
	+ Software Developer
	+ Data Scientist
	+ Operations Manager
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to indicate your level of experience in your position:
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-6 years
	+ 7-10 years
	+ 11+ years
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `note`, not required): This is a text area for you to describe your job duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `note`, not required): This is a text area for you to describe the job requirements and expectations of your position.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, `note`, not required): This is a text area for you to describe your skills and qualifications.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `time`, not required): This is a field for you to enter your typical work hours.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, `number`, not required): This is a field for you to enter your annual salary.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, not required): This is a field to indicate your current job status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Staff Category** (`staff_category`, `select_one`, not required): This is a field to select your staff category:
	+ Management
	+ Technical
	+ Support
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, not required): This is a field to select the tool assigned to you:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ Other
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, not required): This is a text area for you to upload or paste your resume.

**Tips:**
* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
* Once you have completed the form, please review it carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
