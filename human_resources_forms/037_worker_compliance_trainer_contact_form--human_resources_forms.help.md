# Worker Compliance Trainer Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Compliance Trainer Contact Form is used to collect information from employees who need to report compliance or non-compliance issues with the company's policies and procedures.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. (Optional) Provide additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
5. Select the "Type of Complaint" from the provided options (Compliance, Non-compliance, or Other).
6. Click "Submit" to send the form. (Note: This form is not meant for continuous submissions, so only select "Submit" when you're finished.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your contact.
* **Type of Complaint** (select_one, required): Categorize your complaint as:
	+ **Compliance**: For issues related to company policies and procedures being followed correctly.
	+ **Non-compliance**: For issues related to company policies and procedures not being followed.
	+ **Other**: For any other type of complaint not listed above.
* **Submit** (text, not required): This button is not meant for continuous submissions. Only click "Submit" when you're finished providing information. 

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Select the correct category for your "Type of Complaint".
* If you have any other comments or notes, use the "Note" field to add them.
* Remember to enter your phone number correctly, including the area code.
