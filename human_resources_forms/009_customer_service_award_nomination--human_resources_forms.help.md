# Customer Service Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to submit a nomination for the customer service award. The goal is to acknowledge and reward employees who have demonstrated excellent customer service skills and have made significant contributions to their team and the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee you would like to nominate and enter their employee ID.
2. Provide the employee's name, manager's name, nomination date, and nomination reason.
3. Select the category that best describes the employee's achievements.
4. Enter your name, email, phone number, and department.
5. Provide a brief justification for your nomination.
6. Select the appropriate review status for the nomination.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (1, number, required): Enter the employee's ID number to associate with the nomination.
* **Employee Name** (2, text, required): Enter the employee's name who is being nominated.
* **Manager Name** (3, text, required): Enter the manager's name of the employee being nominated.
* **Nomination Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date when the employee's achievements were recognized.
* **Nomination Reason** (5, text, required): Describe the specific reasons for the nomination, including specific examples or incidents that demonstrate the employee's skills.
* **Category** (6, select one, required): Select the category that best describes the employee's achievements, such as Customer Service or Team Player.
* **Submitter Name** (7, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Email** (8, email, required): Enter your email address as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Phone** (9, text, required): Enter your phone number as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Department** (10, text, required): Enter the department of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Position** (11, text, required): Enter the position of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submitter Comments** (12, note, required): Provide any additional comments or justifications for the nomination.
* **Employee Comments** (13, note, optional): The employee being nominated may have additional comments or thoughts about their achievements.
* **Employee Department** (14, text, optional): Enter the department of the employee being nominated.
* **Employee Job Title** (15, text, optional): Enter the job title of the employee being nominated.
* **Employee Phone** (16, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee being nominated.
* **Manager Email** (17, email, optional): Enter the email address of the manager of the employee being nominated.
* **Manager Phone** (18, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the manager of the employee being nominated.
* **Manager Job Title** (19, text, optional): Enter the job title of the manager of the employee being nominated.
* **Manager Department** (20, text, optional): Enter the department of the manager of the employee being nominated.
* **Employee Achievement** (21, text, optional): Describe the specific achievement or skill the employee demonstrated.
* **I agree to the Terms of Service** (22, select one, optional): Select the terms of service that apply to the nomination.
* **Submitter Agree** (23, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or justifications for the nomination.
* **Review Status** (24, select multiple, optional): Select the status of the nomination, such as pending, approved, or rejected.
* **Submitter Notes** (25, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the nomination.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified as optional.
