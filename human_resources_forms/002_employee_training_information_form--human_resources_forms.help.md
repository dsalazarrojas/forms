# Employee Training Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track employee training information, including trainee details, training dates, locations, and completion status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a trainee from the list of employees who have undergone training.
2. Enter the trainee's email address.
3. Enter the training date.
4. Enter the training location.
5. Enter the training hours.
6. Enter the training duration (in hours).
7. Enter the supervisor's name.
8. Enter the supervisor's email address.
9. Select whether or not the trainee received training on the selected topics.
10. Enter employee comments.
11. Enter supervisor comments.
12. Select whether or not the training is completed.
13. Enter the date the training was completed.
14. Enter the trainee's phone number.
15. Enter the supervisor's phone number.
16. Enter any training notes.
17. Enter the trainee's address.
18. Enter the supervisor's address.
19. Select the training status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Information**: Enter a brief description of the training received.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Trainee Name**: Enter the name of the trainee who underwent the training.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Trainee Email**: Enter the email address of the trainee.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: No
* **Training Date**: Enter the date the training took place.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: No
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training took place.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Training Hours**: Enter the number of hours the trainee was trained.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Training Duration**: Enter the length of the training (in hours).
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor who oversaw the training.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email address of the supervisor.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: No
* **Training Topics**: Select the topics that were covered during the training.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Employee Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the trainee.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Supervisor Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the supervisor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Training Completed**: Select whether or not the training was completed.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
* **Training Completed Date**: Enter the date the training was completed.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: No
* **Trainee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the trainee.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supervisor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Training Notes**: Enter any notes about the training.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: No
* **Trainee Address**: Enter the address of the trainee.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Supervisor Address**: Enter the address of the supervisor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Training Status**: Select the status of the training (Active or Inactive).
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and is intended to be a general guide for end-users. If you need further clarification or have questions, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
