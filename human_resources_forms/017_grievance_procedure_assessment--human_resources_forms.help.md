# grievance_procedure_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grievance Procedure Assessment form is designed to help employees report and document grievances in a structured and systematic way. This form is meant to assist in the investigation and resolution of workplace issues, promoting a fair and respectful work environment. It is not a formal complaint resolution tool, but rather a tool to aid in the assessment and documentation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your employee name in the designated field.
2. Describe the grievance in the grievance description field.
3. Select the type of grievance you are reporting (Harassment or Other).
4. Choose the date the grievance occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Grievance Description** (`grievance_description`, text, required): Describe the incident or issue you are reporting. Be as specific as possible to help with the investigation.
* **Grievance Type** (`grievance_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of grievance you are reporting. If unsure, choose 'Other' and provide more details in the description field.
* **Grievance Date** (`grievance_date`, date, required): Enter the date the grievance occurred.
