# Employee Retention Grant Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Retention Grant Application is a form designed to help businesses that are experiencing difficulties in retaining their employees. The form will collect relevant information to assess the business's eligibility and the effectiveness of the retention strategies they employ.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the grant details to ensure you have a clear understanding of what is being applied for.
2. Provide information on the business and its current situation.
3. Offer insights into the retention strategies being employed by the business.
4. Calculate and enter the grant amount that is being requested.
5. Enter your contact information to ensure that you can be reached for any further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Details** (`grant_details`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the Employee Retention Grant Application, including the reason for applying and the expected outcomes.
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, optional): Enter the company's official name, address, and other relevant details that can help us understand the business context.
* **Staff Data** (`staff_data`, text, optional): Provide an overview of the current employee data, including numbers and details about turnover, retention, and other relevant metrics.
* **Retention Strategy** (`retention_strategy`, text, optional): Describe the specific strategies being employed by the business to retain employees, including any notable successes or areas of improvement.
* **Impact Assessment** (`impact_assessment`, text, optional): Outline the expected impact of the retention strategies on employee retention, including any projected financial benefits or improvements in employee satisfaction.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, number, optional): Enter the specific grant amount being requested, which will be determined by the assessment of the business's eligibility and the quality of its retention strategies.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details to ensure that we can efficiently communicate with you.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the following business types: [type1], [type2], [type3], which is necessary to ensure that we process your application correctly.
