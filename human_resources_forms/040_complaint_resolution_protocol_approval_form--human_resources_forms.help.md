# Complaint Resolution Protocol Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Complaint Resolution Protocol Approval Form is a critical document that ensures the approval and implementation of new or updated complaint resolution protocols. It gathers necessary information from the submitter and provides a clear understanding of the protocol to the approver. The form is designed to facilitate a smooth approval process while minimizing any potential risks or challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Submission Date**: Enter the date when the protocol was submitted for approval.
2. **Submitter Name**: Enter your name and title to ensure the approver knows who submitted the protocol.
3. **Submitter Email**: Enter your contact email to facilitate easy communication with the approver.
4. **Submitter Department**: Select the department associated with the submitted protocol.
5. **Protocol Name**: Enter the name of the protocol, which should be clear and concise.
6. **Protocol Version**: Enter the version number of the protocol.
7. **Protocol Type**: Select the type of protocol from the options provided, such as New, Updated, Revised, Emergency, or Temporary.
8. **Protocol Scope**: Define the scope of the protocol, such as All complaint types, Specific complaint category, Specific department, Specific location, or Specific customer segment.
9. **Protocol Description**: Provide a brief overview of the protocol.
10. **Protocol Objectives**: Clearly state the objectives of the protocol.
11. **Affected Processes**: Identify the processes affected by the protocol.
12. **Affected Roles**: Identify the roles that will be impacted by the protocol.
13. **Training Required**: Select whether training is needed and, if so, how extensive.
14. **Training Plan**: If training is required, describe the plan to deliver it.
15. **Resources Required**: Specify the resources needed to implement the protocol.
16. **Budget Impact**: Estimate the cost implications of implementing the protocol.
17. **Budget Amount**: If applicable, provide an estimated cost.
18. **Technology Changes**: Determine if any system modifications are needed.
19. **Implementation Timeline**: Provide the timeline for implementation.
20. **Proposed Effective Date**: Enter the date when the protocol will go into effect.
21. **Success Metrics**: Define how success will be measured.
22. **Risk Assessment**: Identify the risks associated with the protocol.
23. **Mitigation Strategies**: Describe how these risks will be mitigated.
24. **Stakeholder Consultation**: Identify who was consulted during the development of the protocol.
25. **Stakeholder Support**: Evaluate the level of support from stakeholders.
26. **Compliance Review**: Confirm if compliance has reviewed the protocol.
27. **Legal Review**: Confirm if legal has reviewed the protocol.
28. **Approval Status**: Select the current status of the approval process.
29. **Approver Comments**: Add any feedback from the approver.
30. **Approver Name**: Enter the name of the approver.
31. **Approver Signature**: Type the name of the approver as a signature.
32. **Approval Date**: Enter the date the protocol was approved.
