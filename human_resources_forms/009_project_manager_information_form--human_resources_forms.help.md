# Project Manager Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Manager Information Form is used to collect information about project managers for internal purposes. This form helps the organization keep track of their contact details, experience, and project-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Project Manager Information Form" link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required fields, starting with your First Name, Last Name, Job Title, and Email.
3. Provide your contact information, such as your Phone number and Address.
4. Select your level of experience (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral Degree).
5. Indicate your certifications related to project management (e.g., Project Management, Project Control, Project Planning).
6. Choose your skills relevant to project management (e.g., Project Management, Project Control, Project Planning).
7. Add any references or references that can vouch for your experience.
8. Enter the project details, including Project Name and Project Status.
9. Finally, enter any Manager information, including their Name and Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text): Enter your last name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): Enter your job title.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your work phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text): Enter your work address.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date): Enter the project start date if applicable.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date): Enter the project end date if applicable.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number): Choose your level of experience (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral Degree).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text): Provide a brief description of your job.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple): Select any project management skills you have (e.g., Project Management, Project Control, Project Planning).
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple): Choose any project management certifications you have (e.g., Project Management, Project Control, Project Planning).
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple): Select your highest level of education (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral Degree).
* **References** (`references`, text): Enter any references or references that can vouch for your experience.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text): Enter the project name.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text): Enter the manager's name.
* **Team** (`team`, text): Enter the team's name.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one): Choose the project status (e.g., Active, Inactive, or On Hold).
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text): Enter the project location.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text): Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Project Manager Notes** (`project_manager_notes`, text): Enter any notes about the project manager.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can fill them out as you see fit. This form is for internal use, and the information will be used for organizational purposes.
