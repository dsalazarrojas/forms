# No THC Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain acknowledgement from participants in a study or project about their understanding of the terms and conditions related to THC (Tetrahydrocannabinol). The form helps to ensure that participants are aware of and agree to the terms, and provides a record of their acknowledgement and the date.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Enter the employee's position in the **Employee Position** field.
3. Sign in the **Supervisor Signature** field with your official signature.
4. Sign in the **Employee Signature** field with your official signature.
5. Enter the current date in the **Date** field.
6. Enter the date of the supervisor's acknowledgement in the **Supervisor Date** field.
7. Select **Yes** or **No** in the **Participant Acknowledgement** field to indicate whether you understand the terms and conditions.
8. Enter the date of your acknowledgement in the **Participant Acknowledgement Date** field.
9. Select **Yes** or **No** in the **Supervisor Acknowledgement** field to indicate whether you have explained the terms and conditions to the participant.
10. Enter the date of your acknowledgement in the **Supervisor Acknowledgement Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (text)**: Enter the name of the participant in this field.
* **Employee Position (text)**: Enter the position of the participant in this field.
* **Supervisor Signature (text)**: Sign in this field with your official signature as a supervisor.
* **Employee Signature (text)**: Sign in this field with your official signature as a participant.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the current date in this field.
* **Supervisor Date (date)**: Enter the date when you, as supervisor, acknowledged the participant's understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Acknowledgement (select_one)**: Select **Yes** if you understand the terms and conditions, or **No** otherwise.
* **Participant Acknowledgement Date (date)**: Enter the date of your acknowledgement.
* **Supervisor Acknowledgement (select_one)**: Select **Yes** if you have explained the terms and conditions to the participant, or **No** otherwise.
* **Supervisor Acknowledgement Date (date)**: Enter the date of your acknowledgement as supervisor.
