# Employee Recommendation Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to recommend an employee for a job opening. Please provide as much detail as possible to help the candidate get the best possible outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the job title of the recommended employee from the available options.
2. Enter the ID number of the job opening for which the employee is being recommended.
3. Select the department where the employee will work.
4. Choose the status of the job opening.
5. Enter the name of the recommended candidate.
6. Provide any additional information about the employee that you think would be helpful for the hiring process.
7. If applicable, provide the date and time the job was posted.
8. If applicable, provide the name of the candidate, their position, and their email address.
9. Enter any notes about the job opening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, required, true): Select the job title of the employee being recommended from the available options.
* **Job Opening ID** (`job_opening_id`, required, true): Enter the ID number of the job opening for which the employee is being recommended.
* **Department** (`department`, required, true): Select the department where the employee will work.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, required, true): Choose the status of the job opening.
* **Recommended Employee** (`employee_recommended`, required, true): Enter the name of the recommended employee.
* **Job Opening Date** (`job_opening_date`, required, false): If applicable, enter the date the job was posted.
* **Job Posting Date/Time** (`job_posting_date`, required, false): If applicable, enter the date and time the job was posted.
* **Recommended Candidate Name** (`recommended_candidate_name`, required, false): If applicable, enter the name of the candidate.
* **Recommended Candidate Position** (`recommended_candidate_position`, required, false): If applicable, enter the position of the candidate.
* **Recommended Candidate Email** (`recommended_candidate_email`, required, false): If applicable, enter the email address of the candidate.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, required, false): If applicable, enter the name of the manager.
* **Team Leader Name** (`team_leader_name`, required, false): If applicable, enter the name of the team leader.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, required, false): If applicable, enter a brief description of the job.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, required, false): If applicable, enter the category of the job.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, required, false): If applicable, enter the level of the job.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, required, false): If applicable, enter the type of the job.
* **Job Family** (`job_family`, required, false): If applicable, enter the family of the job.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, required, false): If applicable, enter the location of the job.
* **Job Tags** (`job_tags`, required, false): If applicable, enter tags for the job.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, required, false): If applicable, enter the requirements of the job.
* **Job Requirements Notes** (`job_requirements_notes`, required, false): If applicable, enter any additional notes about the job requirements.
* **Job Opening Type** (`job_opening_type`, required, false): If applicable, enter the type of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Note** (`job_opening_note`, required, false): If applicable, enter any additional notes about the job opening.
