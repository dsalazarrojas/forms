# executiveDirectorCandidateApplication - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Director Candidate Application form is a comprehensive application form designed to collect information about candidates applying for the executive director position. This form helps the hiring committee to assess the candidate's qualifications, skills, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the **Candidate Name**, **Contact Information**, and **Resume** fields.
2. Select your **Work Experience** and **Leadership Experience** in the respective fields.
3. Choose your **Relevant Skills** and **Education** levels that align with the job requirements.
4. Indicate your **Work Availability** and **Notice Period** in case you are not already employed.
5. Fill in your **Job Title**, **Job Function**, and **Job Location** if applicable.
6. Enter your **Years of Experience** and **Salary Range** if required.
7. Choose your **Reason for Leaving** if you have left a previous job.
8. Finally, submit your **Cover Letter** and **References**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidatename`, Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Job Title** (`jobtitle`, Text, Required): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Work Experience** (`workexperience`, Text, Required): Describe your work experience relevant to the executive director position.
* **Contact Information** (`contactinformation`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your contact information type (Nonprofit, Government, Private).
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Resume** (`resume`, Text, Required): Attach your resume or paste a link to it.
* **Cover Letter** (`coverletter`, Text, Required): Attach your cover letter or paste a link to it.
* **References** (`references`, Text, Required): Provide references for your application.
* **Relevant Skills** (`skills`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the skills relevant to the executive director position (Leadership, Communication, Strategic Planning).
* **Education** (`education`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your education level (Bachelor, Master, PhD).
* **Leadership Experience** (`leadershipexperience`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your leadership experience type (Nonprofit, Government, Private).
* **Work Availability** (`workavailability`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your work availability (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Notice Period** (`noticeperiod`, Select One, Required): Choose the notice period (30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 120 days).
* **Employment Status** (`employmentstatus`, Select One, Required): Choose your employment status (Active, Resigned, Retired).
* **Notice Period Days** (`noticeperioddays`, Number, Required): Enter the number of days for the notice period.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reasonforleaving`, Text, Required): Enter your reason for leaving a previous job.
* **Work Type** (`worktype`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the work type (Nonprofit, Government, Private).
* **Job Type** (`jobtype`, Text, Required): Enter your job type.
* **Job Level** (`joblevel`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your job level (Entry-level, Mid-level, Senior).
* **Job Function** (`jobfunction`, Text, Required): Enter your job function.
* **Job Location** (`joblocation`, Text, Required): Enter your job location.
* **Years of Experience** (`yearsofexperience`, Number, Required): Enter your years of experience.
* **Job Title** (`jobtitle`, Text, Required): Enter your job title.
* **Salary Range** (`salaryrange`, Number, Required): Enter your desired salary range.
