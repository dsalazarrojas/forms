# executive_director_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The executive director evaluation form is used to collect feedback and performance data on the executive director. This data will be used to assess their overall performance and provide guidance for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
4. Select your job title from the list in the "Job Title" field.
5. Select your department from the list in the "Department" field.
6. Rate the Executive Director's performance on a scale of 1-5 in the "Performance Rating" field.
7. Provide feedback on the Executive Director's performance in the "Feedback" field.
8. Rate the Executive Director's overall performance on a scale of 1-5 in the "Overall Rating" field.
9. Enter any additional comments about the Executive Director's performance in the "Comments" field.
10. Select the date and time you submitted this form in the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the Executive Director's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the Executive Director's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the Executive Director's email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Select the Executive Director's job title from the list.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department the Executive Director belongs to from the list.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, required): Rate the Executive Director's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Provide feedback on the Executive Director's performance.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate the Executive Director's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the Executive Director's performance.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Select the date you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required): Select the time you submitted this form.
