# Early Retirement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Early Retirement Form" is used to submit a notice of early retirement for an employee. Please fill in the form carefully and accurately, as your information will be reviewed and verified by our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all the required fields. Required fields are marked as `true` in the form.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your responses for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Why You Are Leaving
* **why_you_are_leaving** (`retirement_reason`, text, **optional**): Briefly explain why you are submitting an early retirement notice. This will help us understand your reason for leaving the company.

### When Do You Expect To Quit
* **when_do_you_expect_to_quit** (`expected_quit_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date you expect to quit the company. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### How Much Notice
* **how_much_notice** (`notice_period`, number, **optional**): Enter the number of days' notice you are providing. Please note that this will be verified by the company.

### Manager Confirmation
* **manager_confirmation** (`manager_notification`, select_one, **optional**): Select the manager(s) you would like to notify about your early retirement. Please choose from the provided options.

### What Plans Do You Have After Leaving
* **what_plans_do_you_have_after_leaving** (`plan_after_leaving`, text, **optional**): Describe your plans after leaving the company. This will help us understand your future plans and any potential next steps.

### Contact Information
* **contact_information** (`contact_info`, text, **optional**): Enter any contact information you would like to provide, such as your email address or phone number.

### Email
* **email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Comments
* **comments** (`comments`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments or information you think would be helpful for our team.

### Manager Comments
* **manager_comments** (`manager_comments`, note, **optional**): Manager can add any additional comments or information about your departure.

Note: The manager will receive a notification of your early retirement submission, which will prompt them to review and confirm your information.
