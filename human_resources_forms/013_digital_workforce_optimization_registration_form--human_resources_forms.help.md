# Digital Workforce Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and manage digital workforce data for optimization purposes. It captures essential details such as contact information, work location, job title, and other relevant data. The form is designed to ensure accurate and detailed information is captured for efficient workforce management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields. These fields are required and should be filled in with your full name.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the **Email** field. Although not required, it's recommended to enter a valid email address for future communication.
3. Provide your **Phone** number in the **Phone** field. This is not a required field but can be useful for contact purposes.
4. Enter your **Job Title** in the **Job Title** field. This is a required field and should reflect your current job position.
5. Select your **Work Location** from the available options in the **Work Location** field. This field is required and should reflect the city or location where you work.
6. If applicable, select your **Assigned Tool** from the available options in the **Assigned Tool** field. This field is required and should reflect the tool or software you are using for work.
7. Fill in your **Submitted By** field with your name. This field is required and should reflect the person submitting this form.
8. Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** fields with the date and time you submitted this form. These fields are required and should reflect the actual submission date and time.
9. Select the **Submission Status** from the available options in the **Submission Status** field. This field is required and should reflect the current status of your submission.
10. Select the **Manager Name** field with your supervisor's name. This field is required and should reflect the person who has approved or rejected this submission.
11. Enter your supervisor's **Manager Email** address in the **Manager Email** field. This field is not required but can be useful for communication purposes.
12. Enter your supervisor's **Manager Phone** number in the **Manager Phone** field. This field is not required but can be useful for contact purposes.
13. Select the **Manager Submission Date** and **Manager Submission Time** fields with the date and time your supervisor submitted this form. These fields are required and should reflect the actual submission date and time.
14. Select the **Manager Submission Status** from the available options in the **Manager Submission Status** field. This field is required and should reflect the current status of your supervisor's submission.
15. Select the **Team** you are working on from the available options in the **Team** field. This field is required and should reflect the team you are a part of.
16. In the **Manager Note** field, enter any additional comments or instructions provided by your supervisor. This field is not required but can be useful for reference purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for future communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job position.
* **Work Location**: Select the city or location where you work.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool or software you are using for work (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (optional).
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or instructions.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person submitting this form.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Submission Timezone**: Not currently supported in this field.
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of your submission from the available options.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your supervisor's email address (optional).
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your supervisor's phone number (optional).
* **Submitted By Manager**: Enter your supervisor's name as the person submitting this form.
* **Manager Submission Date**: Enter the date your supervisor submitted this form.
* **Manager Submission Time**: Enter the time your supervisor submitted this form.
* **Manager Submission Status**: Select the status of your supervisor's submission from the available options.
* **Team**: Select the team you are working on from the available options.
* **Manager Note**: Enter any additional comments or instructions provided by your supervisor.
