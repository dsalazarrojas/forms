# New Manager Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Manager Onboarding Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information from new managers to ensure a smooth transition and effective onboarding process. This form helps to understand their background, preferences, and expectations, which is essential for creating a productive and comfortable work environment. Please note that this form is required for all new managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. For questions that require text input, provide a brief but detailed response.
3. For questions that require selecting an option, choose one or more relevant answers.
4. Do not leave any questions blank, as all answers are required.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Question** (`new_manager_onboarding_questionnaire_1`, text, required): Provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, job title, and department.
* **Second Question** (`new_manager_onboarding_questionnaire_2`, text, required): Share your expectations from the company, including what you hope to achieve in your new role.
* **Third Question** (`new_manager_onboarding_questionnaire_3`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: I am excited to meet my new team and start working with them immediately.
	+ Option 2: I am a bit nervous about meeting the team and would like some guidance on how to get started.
	+ Option 3: I am not sure what to expect from the team and would like some additional information before starting.
* **Fourth Question** (`new_manager_onboarding_questionnaire_4`, text, required): Describe your previous experience and background, including any relevant skills or qualifications.
* **Fifth Question** (`new_manager_onboarding_questionnaire_5`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Option 1: I am familiar with our company's policies and procedures.
	+ Option 2: I would like to learn more about our company's policies and procedures.
* **Sixth Question** (`new_manager_onboarding_questionnaire_6`, date, optional): If applicable, provide the date you would like to start your new role. If not, you can leave this question blank.
* **Seventh Question** (`new_manager_onboarding_questionnaire_7`, time, optional): If you have a preferred work schedule, please indicate your available work hours. If not, you can leave this question blank.
* **Eighth Question** (`new_manager_onboarding_questionnaire_8`, text, required): Summarize your goals and priorities for your new role, including any specific objectives or targets you would like to achieve.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this information will help us tailor the onboarding process to your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR representative or supervisor.
* Please submit the form as soon as possible to ensure a timely and effective onboarding process.
