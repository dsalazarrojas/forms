# Global Workforce Development Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for the Global Workforce Development Program. It will guide you through the necessary steps to provide your information and submit your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name, in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Participant Contact Information" section.
3. Write a brief description of your career goals in the "Career Goals" section.
4. Indicate your eligibility status in the "Eligibility" section.
5. Provide information about your training provider in the "Training Provider Information" section.
6. Enter your training provider's name, email, and phone number.
7. Submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (text, required/optional): Enter your name to identify yourself as a participant.

### Participant Contact Information
* **Participant Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Participant Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### Career Goals
* **Career Goals** (note, required/optional): Describe your short-term and long-term career goals.

### Eligibility
* **Eligibility** (select_one, required/optional): Check if you are eligible for the program by selecting "Eligible". If unsure, choose "Needs Further Review".

### Training Provider Information
* **Training Provider Information** (text, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about your training provider.
* **Training Provider Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your training provider.
* **Training Provider Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your training provider's email address.
* **Training Provider Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your training provider's phone number.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* Make sure to provide all required information to avoid delays in processing your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
