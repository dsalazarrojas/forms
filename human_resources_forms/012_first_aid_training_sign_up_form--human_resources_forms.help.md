# First Aid Training Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "First Aid Training Sign Up Form" is used to collect information from users who are interested in attending a first aid training class. This form is designed to gather essential details such as class interest, training venue, and contact information to facilitate the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select your interest in attending the first aid class.
* Choose your preferred training venue.
* Select your desired date and time for the training.
* Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* Provide any additional comments or message if needed.
* Confirm your interest in first aid class and training type.
* Specify your preferred training location.
* Confirm your training venue preferences.
* Enter the date and time you would like to sign up for the training.
* Provide your contact name, company, and phone number.
* Finally, confirm your signature and indicate your interest in attending the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your first aid class interest?** (`first_aid_class`, select_one, required)
	+ This field asks you to indicate your interest in attending the first aid class.
* **Training venue** (`training_venue`, select_multiple, required)
	+ In this field, you can select one or multiple preferred training venues.
* **What date** (`training_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter your desired date for the training.
* **What time** (`training_time`, time, required)
	+ Enter your desired time for the training.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required)
	+ Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Name** (`signup_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required)
	+ Enter your company name for contact purposes.
* **Contact** (`contact_phone`, select_one, required)
	+ Indicate if you would like to be contacted about the training.
* **Message** (`signup_message`, text, required)
	+ If you have any comments or messages, enter them here.
* **First aid class interest** (`first_aid_class_interest`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Indicate your interest in different types of first aid classes.
* **Training type** (`training_type`, select_one, required)
	+ Confirm your interest in the training type.
* **Training location** (`training_location`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Specify your preferred training location.
* **Training venue** (`training_venue2`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Confirm your training venue preferences.
* **Signup date** (`signup_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date you would like to sign up for the training.
* **Signup time** (`signup_time`, time, required)
	+ Enter the time you would like to sign up for the training.
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your contact name.
* **Contact company** (`contact_company`, text, required)
	+ Enter your company name for contact purposes.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required)
	+ Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone2`, text, required)
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required)
	+ Confirm your interest in attending the training.

Please ensure that you fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure that your training needs are met.
