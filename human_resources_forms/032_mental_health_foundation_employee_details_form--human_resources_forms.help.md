# mental_health_foundation_employee_details_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health foundation employee details form is used to collect and store employee information for administrative and HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the employee details page.
2.  On this page, enter the employee's details.
3.  Proceed to the contact info page to enter the employee's contact information.
4.  On this page, enter the employee's contact details.
5.  Continue to the job title page to enter the employee's job title and type.
6.  On this page, select the employee's job title and type.
7.  Proceed to the department and job grade page to enter the employee's department and job grade.
8.  On this page, select the employee's department and job grade.
9.  Continue to the manager information page to enter the manager's name, email, and phone number.
10. On this page, enter the manager's contact details.
11. Proceed to the emergency contact page to enter the employee's emergency contact information.
12. On this page, select the emergency contact.
13. Enter the emergency contact phone number and relation.
14. Proceed to the skills and job description page to enter the employee's skills and job description.
15. On this page, select the skills and enter the job description.
16. Continue to the work hours and location page to enter the employee's work hours and location.
17. On this page, enter the work hours and location.
18. Proceed to the job status and employee ID page to enter the employee's job status and ID.
19. On this page, select the job status and enter the employee ID.
20. Continue to the hire date and date of birth page to enter the employee's hire date and date of birth.
21. On this page, enter the hire date and date of birth.
22. Finally, review all the information you have entered to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details**: Enter the employee's name, job title, and other relevant details.
	+ **employee_details**: This field is used to capture the employee's name.
	+ **title**: This field is used to capture the employee's job title.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the employee's contact information.
	+ **contact_info**: This field is used to capture the employee's contact details.
* **Job Title and Type**: Enter the employee's job title and type.
	+ **job_title**: This field is used to capture the employee's job title.
	+ **job_type**: This field is used to capture the employee's job type.
* **Department and Job Grade**: Select the employee's department and job grade.
	+ **department**: This field is used to capture the employee's department.
	+ **job_grade**: This field is used to capture the employee's job grade.
* **Manager Information**: Enter the manager's contact details.
	+ **manager_name**: This field is used to capture the manager's name.
	+ **manager_email**: This field is used to capture the manager's email.
	+ **manager_phone**: This field is used to capture the manager's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select the emergency contact.
	+ **emergency_contact**: This field is used to capture the emergency contact.
* **Skills and Job Description**: Select the employee's skills and enter their job description.
	+ **skills**: This field is used to capture the employee's skills.
	+ **job_description**: This field is used to capture the employee's job description.
* **Work Hours and Location**: Enter the employee's work hours and location.
	+ **work_hours**: This field is used to capture the employee's work hours.
	+ **work_location**: This field is used to capture the employee's location.
* **Job Status and ID**: Select the employee's job status and enter their ID.
	+ **job_status**: This field is used to capture the employee's job status.
	+ **employee_id**: This field is used to capture the employee's ID.
* **Hire Date and Date of Birth**: Enter the employee's hire date and date of birth.
	+ **date_of_hire**: This field is used to capture the employee's hire date.
	+ **date_of_birth**: This field is used to capture the employee's date of birth.
