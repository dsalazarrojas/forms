# New Hire Onboarding Status Control Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track and control the status of new hires during their onboarding process. This form should be used to record the progress of new employees, ensuring they are accurately categorized and up to date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the current status of the new hire from the "New Hire Status" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the name of the new hire's department in the "department" field.
3. Enter the name of the new hire's manager in the "Manager" field.
4. Select the hire date of the new hire from the calendar picker.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Status** (`Active`, `Onboarding`, `New Hire`, `Completed`, `In Progress`, `Cancelled`): Select the current status of the new hire from the dropdown menu. This field is used to categorize the new hire's progress in the onboarding process.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the new hire's department. This field helps to identify the specific team or division the new hire belongs to.
* **Manager** (`Manager`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the new hire's manager. This field provides essential information for communication and supervision.
* **Hire Date** (`Hire Date`, `date`, optional): Select the date the new hire was hired from the calendar picker. This field helps in tracking the start date of the onboarding process.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling this form for new hires who are currently in the onboarding process or have recently completed it.
* Double-check the information you provide to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* If a new hire has already been terminated, do not use this form.
* If a new hire's information is already recorded, do not duplicate the entry.
