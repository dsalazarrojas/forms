# executive_staff_onboarding_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect onboarding information from executive staff members, including contact and professional details. It is crucial to fill this form accurately to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details.
3. Double-check your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your contact information, including your name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Executive Staff** (`executive_staff`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select your position within the executive staff, such as Executive Director, Regional Director, or Department Head.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, which will be used as your primary contact method.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title within the organization.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your job role and responsibilities.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Specify your department within the organization.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required: false): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your supervisor's phone number.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`reporting_manager_email`, email, required: false): Enter your reporting manager's email address.
* **Reporting Manager Phone** (`reporting_manager_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your reporting manager's phone number.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.
- If you have any existing onboarding records, review them before filling out this form.
