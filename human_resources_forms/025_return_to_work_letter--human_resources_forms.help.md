# Return To Work Letter - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's return to work after a leave of absence. It is a formal letter that outlines the employee's return date, reason for returning, and any other relevant details. The form is designed to be completed by the employee and submitted to management for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your employer's details**: In this field, please provide your current or previous employer's details, including their name, address, and phone number.
2. **Enter your employee details**: In this field, please provide your personal details, including your name, employee ID, and job title.
3. **Reason for returning**: Briefly explain why you are returning to work after the leave of absence.
4. **Date of return**: Enter the date on which you plan to return to work.
5. **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understand the terms.
6. **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you plan to report back to work.
7. **Address**: In this field, please provide your return address, which is the address where you will be returning to work.
8. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number so management can reach you with any questions or instructions.
9. **Additional details**: Please provide any other relevant information about your return to work.

* **Employer Details** (`employer_details`, text, optional): Enter your current or previous employer's name, address, and phone number.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Enter your personal details, including your name, employee ID, and job title.
