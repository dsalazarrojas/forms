# Employee Reliability And Dependability Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Reliability and Dependability Performance Evaluation" form is a tool used to assess an employee's overall performance and reliability in their role. This form helps managers evaluate an employee's strengths and weaknesses, providing feedback and guidance for growth and improvement. The evaluation includes ratings and open-ended feedback sections to help managers provide concrete and constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the employee's overall performance using the "Rate overall performance" field.
2. Provide detailed feedback on the employee's performance in the "Provide detailed feedback" field.
3. Rate the employee's reliability and dependability using the "Rate reliability" and "Rate dependability" fields, respectively.
4. Provide feedback on the employee's reliability and dependability in the "Provide feedback" fields.
5. Enter any manager feedback in the "Manager feedback" field.
6. Enter the evaluation date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Enter the employee's email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the employee's phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Enter the employee's ID in the "Employee ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rate overall performance** (`rating_1`, Number, Required: false): Rate the employee's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Provide detailed feedback** (`feedback_1`, Text, Required: false): Provide specific feedback on the employee's performance, such as strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Rate reliability** (`rating_2`, Number, Required: false): Rate the employee's reliability on a scale of 1-5.
* **Provide feedback** (`feedback_2`, Text, Required: false): Provide specific feedback on the employee's reliability, such as strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Rate dependability** (`rating_3`, Number, Required: false): Rate the employee's dependability on a scale of 1-5.
* **Provide feedback** (`feedback_3`, Text, Required: false): Provide specific feedback on the employee's dependability, such as strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Manager feedback** (`manager_feedback`, Text, Required: false): Enter any feedback or guidance from the manager's perspective.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Required: false): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: false): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required: false): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Required: false): Enter the employee's ID number.

Note: All fields are optional, and managers can choose to fill out as many or as few fields as they deem necessary for a thorough evaluation.
