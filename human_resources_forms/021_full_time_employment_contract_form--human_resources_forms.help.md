# Full Time Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employers and employees document the terms of a full-time employment contract. It ensures that all necessary information is collected for the contract, making it easier to manage and understand the employment agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in the job description, work schedule, and job title fields. These fields should provide an overview of the job duties and requirements.
2. Select the employment type from the available options. This will define the type of employment relationship, such as salaried or hourly.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the employment contract. These dates are crucial for both parties in understanding the contract duration.
4. Enter the compensation details, which should be a numerical value representing the salary or wages.
5. Finally, provide any additional benefits that come with the employment contract, in the benefits field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): This field is for a brief description of the job duties and requirements. Please enter a short paragraph summarizing the key responsibilities and expectations of the role.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, text, required): Describe the typical work hours and schedule for this position, including any variations or exceptions.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the formal title of the job, which should match the job description provided.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of employment relationship, such as salaried or hourly.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the employment contract, which marks the beginning of the employment period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the employment contract, which marks the end of the employment period.
* **Compensation** (`compensation`, number, required): Enter the monetary value of the compensation, in the format of the currency used in your organization.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, text, optional): Provide any additional benefits that come with this employment contract, such as health insurance or paid time off.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely, as this form is a legally binding agreement.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that all information remains current and relevant.
* Keep a record of all previous employment contracts for future reference and record-keeping purposes.
