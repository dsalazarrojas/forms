# Janitorial Staff Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the onboarding process of new janitorial staff members. This includes personal details, employment status, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field (field 1).
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field (field 2).
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field (field 3).
5. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field (field 4).
6. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field (field 5).
7. Select your role from the provided options in the "Role" field (field 6).
8. Enter your Supervisor's Name in the "Supervisor Name" field (field 7).
9. Enter your Department in the "Department" field (field 8).
10. Enter the name of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact" field (field 9).
11. Enter your relationship to the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field (field 10).
12. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field (field 11).
13. Enter the email address of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Email" field (field 12).
14. If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their phone number in the "Alternate Emergency Contact Phone" field (field 13).
15. If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their relationship to you in the "Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship" field (field 14).
16. If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their email address in the "Alternate Emergency Contact Email" field (field 15).
17. If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their employment type in the "Alternate Emergency Contact Employment Type" field (field 16).
18. If applicable, select your employment status from the provided options in the "Employment Status" field (field 17).
19. If your employment status is other than the provided options, enter the details in the "Employment Status Other" field (field 18).
20. If you have additional comments for your supervisor, enter them in the "Supervisor Comments" field (field 19).
21. If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their relationship to you in the "Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship" field (field 20).
22. If you have an alternate supervisor, enter their name in the "Supervisor Name Other" field (field 21).
23. If you have an alternate emergency contact phone, enter the phone number in the "Alternate Emergency Contact Phone" field (field 22).
24. If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their email address in the "Alternate Emergency Contact Email" field (field 23).
25. If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter any additional comments in the "Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship Other" field (field 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address that you would like to have on file for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Enter your phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Role (6)**: Select your role within the janitorial staff from the provided options.
* **Supervisor Name (7)**: Enter your supervisor's name as provided by your HR department or management.
* **Department (8)**: Enter the department you are a part of.
* **Emergency Contact (9)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (10)**: Enter your relationship to the emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone (11)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email (12)**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Phone (13)**: If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their phone number.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship (14)**: If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their relationship to you.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Email (15)**: If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their email address.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Employment Type (16)**: If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their employment type (e.g., colleague, friend, etc.).
* **Employment Status (17)**: Select your current employment status from the provided options.
* **Employment Status Other (18)**: If your employment status is other than the provided options, enter the details in this field.
* **Supervisor Comments (19)**: If you have any additional comments for your supervisor, enter them in this field.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship (20)**: If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their relationship to you.
* **Supervisor Name Other (21)**: If you have an alternate supervisor, enter their name in this field.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Phone (22)**: If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their phone number.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Email (23)**: If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter their email address.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship Other (24)**: If you have an alternate emergency contact person, enter any additional comments in this field.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your HR department or management for guidance.
* This form is a crucial part of the onboarding process. Make sure to review it carefully before submitting it.
* The provided options for "Employment Status" are as follows:
	+ Inactive
	+ Active
	+ Terminated
	+ Retired
	+ Resigned
	+ Resigned - On Leave
