# HR Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their client details, contact person, email, job details, contact details, job requirements, and other miscellaneous details. The HR department will use this information to better understand each client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have not already filled out a similar form or are already registered with us.
2. Start by filling out the **Client Details** field, which is required. 
3. You can then move on to filling out the **Contact Person** field, which is not required.
4. Next, provide your **Email** address in the **Email** field, this field is not required.
5. Fill out the **Job Details** field, which is required.
6. Provide any additional **Contact Details** in the **Contact Details** field, this field is not required.
7. Specify the **Job Requirements** in the **Job Requirements** field, this field is required.
8. Finally, you can add any additional **Other Details** in the **Other Details** field, this field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details** (Client Details, Text, Required): This field is for the client to provide their company name, address, or any other relevant details.
- **Contact Person** (Contact Person, Text, Optional): This field is for the client to provide the name and position of the contact person.
- **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): This field is for the client to provide their email address.
- **Job Details** (Job Details, Text, Required): This field is for the client to provide details about the job they are hiring for.
- **Contact Details** (Contact Details, Text, Optional): This field is for the client to provide any additional contact details they want to share.
- **Job Requirements** (Job Requirements, Text, Required): This field is for the client to specify the requirements and qualifications for the job.
- **Other Details** (Other Details, Text, Optional): This field is for the client to provide any additional information they think is relevant to share with the HR department.
