# Policy Acceptance Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Acceptance Declaration is a legal document used to signify that an individual or entity has accepted a policy or set of rules, regulations, or conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name as the client.
2. If applicable, fill in your attorney's name and contact information.
3. Select the type of policy you are accepting (term, whole life, or annuity).
4. Assess the risk associated with the policy (high, medium, or low).
5. Date and sign the form to confirm your acceptance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Acceptance Declaration** (`policy_acceptance_declaration`): This is the main section of the form where you will type in your acceptance of the policy.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Fill in your name as the client who is accepting the policy.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`): If you are represented by an attorney, fill in their name and contact information.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Date and sign the form to confirm your acceptance of the policy.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`): Fill in the policy number for reference.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`): Select the type of policy you are accepting (term, whole life, or annuity).
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`): Assess the risk associated with the policy by selecting high, medium, or low.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill in all required fields, including your signature.
* Review the policy terms and conditions before signing the form.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
