# Disciplinary Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for documenting disciplinary communications within an organization, including incidents, actions taken, and follow-up information. It helps maintain a clear and detailed record of the process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2.  Choose the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields, respectively.
3.  Select the disciplinary action to be taken in the "Disciplinary Action" field.
4.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
5.  Describe the action taken in the "Action Taken" field.
6.  Enter the date, time, and location of the follow-up (if applicable) in the "Follow-up Date," "Follow-up Time," and "Follow-up Location" fields.
7.  Add follow-up notes in the "Follow-up Notes" field.
8.  Enter the date and time of the action taken in the "Action Taken Date" and "Action Taken Time" fields.
9.  Enter the reporting manager's name, title, and contact information in the "Reporting Manager," "Reporting Manager Title," "Reporting Manager Phone," and "Reporting Manager Location" fields.
10.  Add notes from the reporting manager in the "Reporting Manager Notes" field.
11.  Enter the name and contact information of the reporting officer in the "Reporting Officer," "Reporting Officer Title," "Reporting Officer Phone," and "Reporting Officer Location" fields.
12.  Add notes from the reporting officer in the "Reporting Officer Notes" field.
13.  Enter the submitted-by information (name, email, and phone) in the "Submitted By," "Submitted By Email," and "Submitted By Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Disciplinary Action**: Select the type of disciplinary action to be taken (e.g., Verbal Warning, Written Warning, Suspension).
* **Incident Details**: Describe the incident in detail.
* **Action Taken**: Describe the action taken as a result of the incident.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the follow-up time (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Location**: Enter the follow-up location (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Notes**: Add any additional notes about the follow-up.
* **Action Taken Date**: Enter the date the action was taken.
* **Action Taken Time**: Enter the time the action was taken.
* **Reporting Manager**: Enter the name of the reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Title**: Enter the title of the reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Location**: Enter the location of the reporting manager.
