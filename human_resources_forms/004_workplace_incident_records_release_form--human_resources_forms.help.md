# workplace_incident_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track incidents that have occurred in the workplace, including details about the incident, the date it occurred, and the individual who reported it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the incident details, including a brief description of what happened.
2. Enter the date the incident occurred.
3. Provide the name and position of the individual who reported the incident.
4. Enter the email address and phone number of the individual who reported the incident.
5. Specify the nature of the incident (e.g., "Physical injury", "Property damage", etc.).
6. Describe the cause of the incident.
7. Describe the effect of the incident (e.g., "Minor injury", "No injury", etc.).
8. Specify the method used to prevent the incident from happening again.
9. Provide the outcome of the incident (e.g., "Resolved", "Pending", etc.).
10. Specify the investigation status (e.g., "Completed", "In progress", etc.).
11. Specify the closure status (e.g., "Closed", "Pending", etc.).
12. Enter any comments or notes about the incident.
13. If the incident has been reviewed, enter the name of the reviewer and the date of review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident details** (1): A brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident date** (2): The date the incident occurred.
* **Reported by** (3): The name and position of the individual who reported the incident.
* **Incident location** (4): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident nature** (5): The type of incident that occurred (e.g., "Physical injury", "Property damage", etc.).
* **Incident description** (6): A more detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident cause** (7): The reason why the incident occurred.
* **Incident effect** (8): The impact or consequence of the incident (e.g., "Minor injury", "No injury", etc.).
* **Incident prevention method** (9): The steps taken to prevent the incident from happening again.
* **Incident outcome** (10): The result or resolution of the incident (e.g., "Resolved", "Pending", etc.).
* **Incident investigation status** (11): The current status of the investigation (e.g., "Completed", "In progress", etc.).
* **Incident closure status** (12): The current status of the incident closure process (e.g., "Closed", "Pending", etc.).
* **Incident comments** (13): Any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Reviewed by** (14): The name of the individual who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewed date** (15): The date the incident was reviewed.

Note: This form must be completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the workplace incident records are up-to-date and reliable. Any discrepancies or missing information may affect the effectiveness of the incident tracking process.
