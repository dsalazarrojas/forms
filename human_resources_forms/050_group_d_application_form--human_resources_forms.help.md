# Group D Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Group D Application Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information from applicants for a Group D application. This form is used to evaluate candidates for a specific job or position within the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each field thoroughly and accurately**. This will ensure that your application is complete and can be reviewed efficiently.
2. **Use the same formatting and notation** as used in the form to help with consistency.
3. **Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, email, **required**): Enter your official email address.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of job you are applying for (Job A, Job B, or Job C).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, **required**): Provide a brief description of your qualifications and experience for the selected job type.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, **required**): Enter your work hours, including start and end times.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, **required**): The date you are submitting your application.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, select_multiple, **optional**): If applicable, select 'Yes' if you have uploaded files as part of your application.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments or feedback you would like to provide about your application.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **required**): Select 'Yes' if you are aware of the specific tool assigned to you for this application.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, **required**): Select the current status of your application (Pending, Rejected, or Approved).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the person submitting the application.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, **optional**): The date and time you submitted the application (not required but recommended for tracking).
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, **optional**): If your application has been approved, enter the name of the person who approved it.
* **Approved At** (`approved_at`, date, **optional**): The date and time your application was approved (not required but recommended for tracking).
