# Personal Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Leave Form is a tool for employees to submit leave requests to their manager or HR. This form is used to gather information about the leave request, including the start and end dates, reason, and duration of the leave. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out the Title field
Please enter a brief title for your leave request in this field. This will help your manager or HR understand the purpose of your request.

### Step 2: Select the start date and time of the leave
Select the start date and time of the leave in the corresponding fields. Make sure to enter the correct date and time in the correct format.

### Step 3: Enter the reason for the leave
Please enter a brief reason for your leave request in this field. Be as specific as possible to help your manager or HR understand why you are requesting leave.

### Step 4: Select the duration of the leave
Select the number of days you plan to take for the leave. Make sure to enter a valid number of days.

### Step 5: Select the frequency of the leave
Select whether this is a one-time leave or a recurring request. If you're not sure, just select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if it's a one-time or recurring leave.

### Step 6: Indicate if you will be taking multiple leave days during this period
If you plan to take multiple leave days, select 'True', otherwise select 'False'.

### Step 7: Enter the start and end dates of the leave period
Enter the start and end dates of the leave period in the correct format. If you've selected to take multiple leave days, enter the dates for each day you plan to take off.

### Step 8: Enter the end time of the leave
Enter the end time of the leave. Make sure to enter the correct time in the correct format.

### Step 9: Enter any additional comments or requests (optional)
If you have any additional comments or requests regarding the leave, please enter them here. This is an optional field, so you can skip it if you don't have anything to add.

### Step 10: Enter your phone number and email address (optional)
If you'd like to be contacted regarding your leave request, please enter your phone number and email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly.
* Double-check the accuracy of your input, especially dates and times.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR for clarification.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (1)**: Enter a brief title for the leave request.
* **Date (2)**: Enter the start date of the leave.
* **Time (3)**: Enter the start time of the leave.
* **Reason (4)**: Enter a brief reason for the leave request.
* **Duration (5)**: Enter the number of days for the leave.
* **Frequency (6)**: Select whether this is a one-time or recurring leave.
* **Multiple Leave Days (7)**: Indicate if you will be taking multiple leave days during this period.
* **Start Date (8)**: Enter the start date of the leave period.
* **End Date (9)**: Enter the end date of the leave period.
* **End Time (10)**: Enter the end time of the leave.
* **Notes (11)**: Enter any additional comments or requests regarding the leave.
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (13)**: Enter your email address.
