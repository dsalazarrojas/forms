# public_affairs_specialist_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants interested in applying for a public affairs specialist position. Please fill out this form to provide your information and attach any relevant job attachments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Public Affairs Specialist Application Form" page.
2. Fill out the form fields as per the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional):
  This field is for specifying your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional):
  Select the department you are applying to.
* **Job Summary** (`job_summary`, text, optional):
  Provide a brief summary of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional):
  Describe your job responsibilities and duties.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, optional):
  List your relevant qualifications and skills for the position.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, select_multiple, optional):
  Indicate if you have a yes or no answer regarding your education background.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select your relevant work experience for the position.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select any relevant certifications for the position.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, text, optional):
  Attach your resume as a supporting document.
* **Job Attachments** (`job_attachments`, text, optional):
  Attach any additional supporting documents for the job application.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields.
- Be concise and relevant in your answers.
- Proofread your form for errors before submission.
- You can attach multiple resume and job attachments if necessary.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant department for clarification.
