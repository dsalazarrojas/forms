# Leadership Development Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's leadership development journey, including their job title, years of experience, job function, and job satisfaction. The form will help to identify areas of improvement and provide insights for leadership development programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your job title and years of experience from the dropdown menus.
3. Choose your job function from the options provided.
4. Select your level of job satisfaction from the options provided.
5. Select your team size and work location from the dropdown menus.
6. Choose your job status and work status from the options provided.
7. Select your job level and work type from the dropdown menus.
8. Enter your job title and job description in the text fields.
9. Finally, submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, email, **Required**): Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, **Optional**): Enter your job title as it appears on your official documents.
* **Years of Experience** (years_of_experience, number, **Optional**): Enter the number of years you have been in your current or previous role.
* **Job Function** (job_function, select_one, **Required**): Choose your current job function from the options provided.
* **Salary Range** (salary_range, number, **Optional**): Enter your current salary range (not required for this form).
* **Education Level** (education_level, select_multiple, **Required**): Choose your highest level of education from the options provided.
* **Skills** (skills, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose the skills you have developed or are looking to develop.
* **Job Satisfaction** (job_satisfaction, select_multiple, **Required**): Choose your level of job satisfaction from the options provided.
* **Job Expectations** (job_expectations, text, **Optional**): Enter your current or future job expectations.
* **Job Description** (job_description, text, **Optional**): Enter a brief description of your current or future job role.
* **Performance Management** (performance_management, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose your current performance management style from the options provided.
* **Job Type** (job_type, select_one, **Optional**): Choose your job type from the options provided.
* **Work Schedule** (work_schedule, time, **Optional**): Enter your current or future work schedule.
* **Team Size** (team_size, select_one, **Required**): Choose the size of your team from the options provided.
* **Work Location** (work_location, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose your current or future work location from the options provided.
* **Job Status** (job_status, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose your current or future job status from the options provided.
* **Job Status 2** (job_status_2, select_one, **Optional**): Choose your current or future job status from the options provided.
* **Work Status** (work_status, select_one, **Optional**): Choose your current or future work status from the options provided.
* **Job Level** (job_level, select_one, **Optional**): Choose your current or future job level from the options provided.
* **Work Type** (work_type, select_one, **Optional**): Choose your current or future work type from the options provided.
* **Work Hours** (work_hours, number, **Optional**): Enter your current or future work hours.
* **Job Title 2** (job_title_2, text, **Optional**): Enter your current or future job title.
* **Job Description 2** (job_description_2, text, **Optional**): Enter a brief description of your current or future job role.
