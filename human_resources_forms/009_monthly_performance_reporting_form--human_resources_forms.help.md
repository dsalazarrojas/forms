# Monthly Performance Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Performance Reporting Form is a tool used to collect and track employee performance and progress on a monthly basis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the employee ID to identify the employee being reported on.
2. Check the box to indicate whether the team lead has been involved in the reporting process.
3. Provide an update on any recent employee updates, such as changes in role or responsibilities.
4. Select whether progress has been made on current tasks and projects.
5. Describe any outcomes or results achieved in the reporting period.
6. Check if you have had performance conversations with the employee recently.
7. Select any ongoing conversations that require attention.
8. Enter the date of the performance conversation(s).
9. Enter the time of the performance conversation(s).
10. Check the submission status of the form.
11. Confirm whether the form was submitted by the employee.
12. Confirm who the form was submitted to.
13. Confirm the method used to submit the form.
14. Enter the date the form was submitted.
15. Enter the time the form was submitted.
16. Provide any additional comments from the manager.
17. Provide any comments from the employee.
18. Enter the form ID for identification purposes.
19. Enter the name of the tool assigned to the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id (1)**: Enter the ID number of the employee being reported on.
* **team_lead (2)**: Check this box to indicate whether the team lead has been involved in the reporting process.
* **employee_updates (3)**: Provide a brief update on any recent changes in the employee's role or responsibilities.
* **progress_tracking (4)**: Select whether progress has been made on current tasks and projects.
* **outcomes_documentation (5)**: Describe any significant outcomes or results achieved in the reporting period.
* **performance_conversations (6)**: Check this box if you have had performance conversations with the employee recently.
* **ongoing_conversations (7)**: Select any ongoing conversations that require attention.
* **ongoing_conversations_date (8)**: Enter the date of the performance conversation(s).
* **ongoing_conversations_time (9)**: Enter the time of the performance conversation(s).
* **manager_comments (10)**: Provide any additional comments from the manager.
* **employee_comments (11)**: Provide any comments from the employee.
* **submission_date (12)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **submission_time (13)**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **submission_status (14)**: Check this box to confirm the submission status of the form.
* **submitted_by (15)**: Check this box to confirm whether the form was submitted by the employee.
* **submitted_to (16)**: Check this box to confirm who the form was submitted to.
* **submitted_via (17)**: Check this box to confirm the method used to submit the form.
* **submitted_on (18)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **submitted_at (19)**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **submitted_by_title (20)**: Enter the title of the person who submitted the form.
