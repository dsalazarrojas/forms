# Lab Data Analyst Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lab Data Analyst Onboarding Form" is designed to onboard new staff members in a lab setting, providing essential information to help them get started with their new role. This form collects details about the new staff member's role, internal acknowledgments, onboarding status, lab setup, research teams, role name, job title, contact information (email and phone), and role identification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and answer each question carefully, ensuring accuracy and relevance to your role and situation.
2. Select the correct options for each field, using the provided dropdown menus or text fields as needed.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role Setup** (`role_setup`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to indicate whether you have set up your role or not. Please select "Yes" if you have completed the necessary setup, or "No" if you haven't.
* **Internal Acknowledgments** (`internal_acknowledgments`, text, required: false): In this field, please type out any internal acknowledgments or agreements you have made, such as training sessions or compliance requirements.
* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the status of your onboarding process by selecting "Yes" if you're fully onboarded or "No" if you still need further assistance.
* **Lab Setup** (`lab_setup`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed your lab setup, if applicable.
* **Research Teams** (`research_teams`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the research teams you are working with or affiliated with in the lab.
* **Role Name** (`role_name`, text, required: false): Enter your role name, which is used to identify your position within the lab.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Provide your job title, which helps to clarify your responsibilities and expectations.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, which is used for communication and contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, which is used for communication and contact purposes.
* **Role ID** (`role_id`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you have a role ID or not by selecting "Yes" or "No".
