# Employee Absenteeism Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Absenteeism Form" is used by employees to report their absence from work. This form helps the management to understand the reason for absence and manage attendance and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide a brief description of what happened on your absence (Field 1).
2. Choose the reason for your absence from the list (Field 7).
3. If any other reason not listed, provide it in Field 8.
4. Specify the date and time you will return to work (Field 3).
5. Mention how long were you absent for (Field 4).
6. Provide your contact number (Field 5).
7. Mention your email address (Field 6).
8. You may also add any additional comments in Field 9.
9. You may also provide any comments from your manager in Field 10.
10. Finally, please fill in the other fields as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What happened on your absence (Field 1)**: Describe the events that led to your absence.
* **Why did you absent from work (Field 2)**: Choose the reason for your absence from the list.
* **When will you be able to return to work (Field 3)**: Specify the date and time you will return to work.
* **How long were you absent for (Field 4)**: Provide the duration of your absence.
* **Contact number (Field 5)**: Enter your contact number so that your manager can reach out to you.
* **Email address (Field 6)**: Enter your email address so that your manager can reach out to you.
* **Reason for Absence** (Field 7): Choose the reason for your absence from the list.
* **Reason for Absence - Others** (Field 8): If the reason for your absence is not listed, provide it here.
* **Comments** (Field 9): You may add any comments or notes related to your absence.
* **Manager Comments** (Field 10): Your manager may add any comments or notes related to your absence.
* **Date** (Field 11): Enter the date.
