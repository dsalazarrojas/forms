# Job Opportunity Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Opportunity Questionnaire is a form used to gather information about job opportunities within the company. This form helps to ensure that all relevant details about a job opening are captured accurately and consistently. The data collected will be used to support internal hiring processes and help in identifying potential candidates for job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the job opportunity.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with \*.
3. Choose the correct options for each select fields.
4. Enter the job title and description accurately.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter a brief and descriptive title of the job opportunity. This will help identify the position and attract potential candidates.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of job (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Internship) that best describes the opportunity.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant categories for the job opportunity (Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, Software Development, or Other).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of the job (Entry Level, Mid-level, or Senior) that best matches the opportunity.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the job opportunity, if applicable.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the job opportunity, if known.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the job opportunity, if known.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job opportunity, highlighting the main responsibilities and duties.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, text, optional): Enter more details about the job responsibilities and requirements.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any specific requirements or skills needed for the job opportunity.
* **Candidate Resume** (`candidate_resume`, note, optional): If applicable, attach or link to a resume or CV of the candidate.
* **Role Preferences** (`role_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any specific preferences or considerations for the role opportunity.
