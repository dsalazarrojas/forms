# request_for_approval_and_verification_of_absence - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for submitting a request for approval and verification of an employee's absence. Use this form to notify the management about an absence, provide details about the reason for the absence, and obtain approval or denial from a manager.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure that you have all necessary information ready before completing the form.
3. Be clear and concise in your responses to ensure accurate understanding.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee whose absence you want to request approval for.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the employee's absence.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee will be absent from.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee will return to work.
* **Duration** (`duration`, text, required): Specify the number of days or hours the employee will be absent.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Choose between "Approval" or "Deny" to indicate the manager's decision.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide additional comments or context for the manager's decision.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): This field is not typically required, but if you want to specify the date the approval was given, you can fill it out.
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Mark as "True" if the employee has confirmed their absence, or "False" if they haven't.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): If the manager has additional comments, they can be provided here.

## Tips

* Be sure to review and double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the employee's manager has all necessary information to make an informed decision.
* If the manager denies the request, please address their concerns and resubmit the form.
