# job_training_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job_training_referral_form is used to request job training referrals from the system. This form helps internal staff to refer candidates to job openings within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's information in the "Candidate information" section.
2. Fill out the job details, including the job title, description, location, job type, and category in the "Job details" section.
3. Select the relevant training programs for the candidate in the "Training programs" section.
4. Choose the training status and enter the start and end dates, if applicable.
5. Fill out the candidate's contact details, including name, ID, and salary information, in the "Candidate information" section.
6. Enter the contact person's details, including name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate information**: Enter the name and ID of the candidate you are referring.
* **Job details**: Enter the title, description, location, type, and category of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Training programs**: Select one or more training programs that the candidate is enrolled in or interested in.
* **Training status**: Choose the current status of the candidate's training. Options are "Pending", "In Progress", "Complete", and "Not Started".
* **Training start date**: Enter the start date of the candidate's training, if applicable.
* **Training end date**: Enter the end date of the candidate's training, if applicable.
* **Training duration**: Enter the duration of the candidate's training, in days or months.
* **Candidate name**: Enter the name of the candidate being referred.
* **Candidate ID**: Enter the ID number of the candidate being referred.
* **Job title**: Enter the title of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job description**: Enter a brief description of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job location**: Enter the location of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job type**: Enter the type of job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job category**: Enter the category of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job status**: Enter the status of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job opening type**: Enter the type of job opening the candidate is applying for.
* **Job opening status**: Enter the status of the job opening the candidate is applying for.
* **Candidate salary**: Enter the salary of the candidate.
* **Candidate salary range**: Enter the salary range of the candidate.
* **Job salary**: Enter the salary of the job.
* **Job salary range**: Enter the salary range of the job.
* **Contact person**: Enter the name of the contact person for the referral.
* **Contact email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips

* Always double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
* Use the correct format for dates and numbers.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the relevant department or team.
