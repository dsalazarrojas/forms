# employee_job_description_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed information about a job description for an employee, including their department, job title, working hours, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

To get started, make sure you have the relevant information about the employee's job ready. The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields that correspond to different aspects of the job description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department** (`id: 1`): Choose the department that the employee belongs to, such as Human Resources or Finance.
* **Job Title** (`id: 2`): Enter the job title of the employee, such as Marketing Manager or Software Developer.
* **Photo** (`id: 3`): Select the employees who are part of the team, for example Employee 1, Employee 2, and Employee 3.
* **Working Hours** (`id: 4`): Enter the working hours of the employee, using 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00-17:00).
* **Job Summary** (`id: 5`): Enter a brief summary of the job description, highlighting the main responsibilities and duties.
* **Reporting Manager** (`id: 6`): Choose the manager who reports to, such as Manager 1, Manager 2, or Manager 3.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`id: 7`): Enter the specific tasks and duties that the employee is responsible for.
* **Working Conditions** (`id: 8`): Select the working conditions that apply to the job, such as Hot, Cold, or Wet.
* **Job Classification** (`id: 9`): Choose the classification of the job, such as Class A, Class B, or Class C.
* **Reporting Team** (`id: 10`): Select the team that reports to, such as Team 1, Team 2, or Team 3.
* **Reporting Team Lead** (`id: 11`): Choose the team lead who reports to, such as Team 1 Lead, Team 2 Lead, or Team 3 Lead.
* **Department Head** (`id: 12`): Choose the head of the department that the employee belongs to, such as Department Head 1, Department Head 2, or Department Head 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if it's not marked as "required" in the form.
* Use the options provided in the "select" and "select_multiple" fields. If an option is not listed, please contact the form administrators to add it.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter, as it will be used to create an official job description.
* If you're unsure about any field or option, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
