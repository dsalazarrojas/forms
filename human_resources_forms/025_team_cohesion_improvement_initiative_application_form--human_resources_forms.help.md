# Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative Application Form is designed to help teams submit applications for initiatives aimed at improving team cohesion within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the relevant program from the dropdown menu under "Program".
* Provide a brief description of the initiative under "Program Description".
* Enter the start and end dates for the initiative.
* Enter the name of the team that will be impacted by the initiative.
* Enter a brief description of the team.
* Identify the team lead responsible for the initiative.
* List the team members who will be working on the initiative.
* Choose the status of the initiative from the dropdown menu.
* Optionally, provide additional comments or attachments related to the initiative.
* Identify the organizer and reviewer of the initiative.
* Choose whether the initiative has been reviewed.
* If reviewed, enter the date and name of the last reviewer.
* Enter any additional comments from the last review.
* Identify whether the initiative has additional attachments.
* Choose the current status of the initiative.
* Enter the next action planned for the initiative.
* If the next action involves reviewing, enter the next due date for the review.
* Identify the next reviewer.
* Enter any additional comments for the next review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program**: Select the relevant program from the dropdown menu.
* **Program Description**: Enter a brief description of the initiative (e.g., 150-200 words).
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when the initiative is scheduled to start.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when the initiative is scheduled to end.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team that will be impacted by the initiative.
* **Team Description**: Enter a brief description of the team.
* **Team Lead**: Identify the person responsible for leading the team.
* **Employees**: Select the team members who will be working on the initiative.
* **Status**: Choose the status of the initiative from the dropdown menu.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the initiative.
* **Attachments**: Optionally, upload attachments related to the initiative.
* **Additional Attachments**: Identify whether the initiative has additional attachments.
* **Organizer**: Identify the person responsible for organizing the initiative.
* **Reviewer**: Identify the person responsible for reviewing the initiative.
* **Reviewed**: Choose whether the initiative has been reviewed.
* **Last Review**: Enter the date when the initiative was last reviewed.
* **Last Reviewer**: Enter the name of the last reviewer.
* **Last Comments**: Enter any comments or observations from the last review.
* **Last Attachments**: Identify whether the initiative has last attachments.
* **Last Status**: Choose the status of the initiative from the dropdown menu.
* **Next Action**: Enter the next action planned for the initiative.
* **Next Due Date**: Enter the next due date for the review or action.
* **Next Reviewer**: Identify the person responsible for reviewing the initiative.
* **Next Comments**: Enter any comments or observations for the next review.

## Tips

* Be as brief and concise as possible in providing descriptions and comments.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use this form for new initiatives only, not for initiatives that are already active or under review.
* Save a copy of the form for future reference.
