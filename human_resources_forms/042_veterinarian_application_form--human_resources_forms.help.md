# Veterinarian Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who wish to apply for a position as a veterinarian. It will help us assess their qualifications, experience, and interests in the field of veterinary care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name in the `candidate_name` field.
2. Enter your address in the `address` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
5. Describe your experience as a veterinarian in the `veterinarian_experience` field.
6. Share your educational background relevant to veterinary science in the `education` field.
7. List any references who can vouch for your experience and skills.
8. Provide contact information for your emergency contact person in the `emergency_contact` field.
9. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact person in the `emergency_relationship` field.
10. Answer "Yes" or "No" in the `veterinarian_license` field, indicating whether you hold a veterinarian license or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your application.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your current address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can reach you easily.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your work or personal email address for us to contact you.
* **Veterinarian Experience** (`veterinarian_experience`, text, required: false): Describe your experience as a veterinarian, including any relevant certifications or training.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required: false): Share your educational background relevant to veterinary science, including degrees earned or courses taken.
* **References** (`references`, text, required: false): List any professionals who can vouch for your experience and skills as a veterinarian.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required: false): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Veterinarian License** (`veterinarian_license`, select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you hold a veterinarian license, "No" otherwise.
