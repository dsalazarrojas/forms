# Employee Training Needs Identification Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Training Needs Identification Evaluation Form is used to identify and evaluate the training needs of employees in the organization. It helps in providing them with relevant training and development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the employee's details in the `employee_info` field.
2.  Select the skills needed by the employee from the `skills_needed` dropdown list.
3.  Select the areas where the employee requires training and development, such as technical skills, soft skills, and knowledge gaps.
4.  Enter the date and time needed for training, if applicable.
5.  Provide any additional notes about the training needs of the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_info** (Text): Enter the employee's name, job title, and other relevant details.
* **skills_needed** (Select One): Select the skills needed by the employee, such as "Yes" for "Technical Skills" or "No" for "Soft Skills".
* **training_needs_identification** (Select Multiple): Select the areas where the employee requires training and development, such as "Yes" for "Technical Skills" or "Yes" for "Soft Skills".
* **training_needs_identification_2** (Select Multiple): Select other areas where the employee requires training and development.
* **training_needs_evaluation** (Text): Provide any additional comments or notes about the training needs of the employee.
* **development_needs** (Number): Enter the number of training hours needed per week for the employee.
* **notes** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the training needs of the employee.
* **email** (Email): Enter the employee's email address, if available.
* **phone** (Text): Enter the employee's phone number, if available.
* **training_needs_date** (Date): Enter the date needed for training, if applicable.
* **training_needs_time** (Time): Enter the time needed for training, if applicable.
* **training_needs_1** (Select One): Select whether the employee needs training for "Yes" or "No".
* **training_needs_2** (Select One): Select whether the employee needs training for "Yes" or "No".
