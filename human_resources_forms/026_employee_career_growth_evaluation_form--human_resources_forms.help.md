# Employee Career Growth Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help employees evaluate their career growth and development within the company. It is intended to assess their current job satisfaction, career aspirations, and performance expectations, as well as their career development progress. This form will help employees identify areas where they would like to grow professionally and personally, and will guide managers and HR staff in providing support and resources to help employees achieve their goals.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of six pages: Introduction, Career Development, Job Satisfaction, Career Aspirations, Job Fit, and Performance Expectations. Each page requires text input. Please answer the questions on each page and submit the form once you have completed all the pages.
