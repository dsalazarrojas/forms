# Accrued Leave Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage leave absences for employees, ensuring accurate tracking of their accrued leave balances. It helps to maintain transparency and consistency in leave approvals and management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's information, including their name, employee ID, and department.
2. Select the type of leave (e.g., Vacation, Sick leave, Personal leave, or Compensatory time).
3. Enter the start and end dates of the leave absence.
4. Specify the total number of days being used.
5. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of leave.
6. If necessary, provide contact information for urgent contact during the leave.
7. Obtain manager approval, which can be Approved, Denied, or Pending.
8. If manager approval is Pending, include any comments for the manager's review.
9. Sign the form electronically by typing your full name.
10. Finally, select a submission date for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name**: Full name as recorded in HR, used to identify the employee.
* **Employee ID**: Internal HR identifier, if applicable.
* **Department**: Department or team name where the employee belongs to.
* **Leave type**: Select the type of leave (e.g., Vacation, Sick leave, Personal leave, or Compensatory time).
* **Leave start date**: First day of absence.
* **Leave end date**: Last day of absence.
* **Total days requested**: Number of accrued days being used.
* **Reason for leave**: Brief explanation of the absence reason.
* **Contact during leave**: Phone or email for urgent contact during the leave (optional).
* **Manager approval**: Status of manager approval (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Manager comments**: Any comments from the manager regarding approval.
* **Employee signature**: Type your full name to sign the form electronically.
* **Submission date**: Date the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in to ensure proper form submission.
* Review and double-check the dates and details to ensure accuracy.
* If manager approval is Pending, please follow up with your manager to confirm the status.
* Keep the reason for leave brief but informative to help managers understand the request.
* Sign the form electronically with your full name to validate your submission.
