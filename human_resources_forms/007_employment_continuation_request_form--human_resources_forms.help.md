# Employment Continuation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Continuation Request Form is used to gather information from employees who are departing from the organization. This form is essential for the HR department to process the employee's departure efficiently and ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal details:
	* Enter your **employee_name** (required).
	* Enter your **job_title** (required).
	* Enter your **department** (required).
2. Provide employment details:
	* Enter the **date_employed** (number, required).
	* Select the **reasons_for_leaving** (select multiple, required) that apply to your situation.
3. Add supporting documents:
	* If applicable, attach **supporting_documents** (note, not required).
4. Confirm your contact information:
	* Enter your **email** (required).
	* Enter your **phone** (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department where you are currently employed.
* **date_employed** (`date_employed`, number, required): Enter the date you were employed by the organization.
* **reasons_for_leaving** (`reasons_for_leaving`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable reasons for your departure.
	+ Redundancy (no longer needed): Check if your work duties are redundant and no longer necessary.
	+ Retirement: You are leaving due to retirement.
	+ Resignation: You are leaving due to a resignation.
	+ Health issues: You are leaving due to health reasons.
	+ Bullying: You are leaving due to bullying in the workplace.
	+ Family reasons: You are leaving due to family-related reasons.
	+ Other (specify below): Select this option and provide a reason in the text field below.
* **supporting_documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, not required): Attach any supporting documents that may be required for your departure.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it is listed on your personnel file.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **reason_for_leaving_1-8** (`reason_for_leaving_1-8`, text, not required): These fields are for additional reasons for leaving; please provide any other reasons that apply to your situation.
* **reasons_for_why_2** (`reasons_for_why_2`, select_multiple, not required): Similar to **reasons_for_leaving**, select all applicable reasons for your departure.
* **reason_for_leaving_2-7** (`reason_for_leaving_2-7`, text, not required): Similar to **reason_for_leaving**, provide any additional reasons for leaving.
* **reason_for_leaving_8** (`reason_for_leaving_8`, text, not required): Another field for additional reasons for leaving.
