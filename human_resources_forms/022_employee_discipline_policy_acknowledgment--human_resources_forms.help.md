# Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for HR personnel to document and acknowledge employee discipline policies in the organization. The primary goal is to ensure that employees are aware of and understand the company's policies and procedures related to employee conduct and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the company's employee discipline policy and have not completed this form previously.
2. Review the form carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
3. Enter the date and time you acknowledged the policy.
4. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the acknowledgment.
5. If you've signed the form, indicate that you've done so and enter the date and time you signed it.
6. Enter any contact information (email or phone number) if applicable.
7. Confirm that you've read and understood the policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Form** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_form`, text, required): This field is the main body of the form where you should enter the details of the policy you've acknowledged, including any relevant notes or comments.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Date** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you acknowledged the policy. This field is optional, but recommended for record-keeping purposes.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Time** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you acknowledged the policy. This field is optional, but recommended for record-keeping purposes.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Note** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the acknowledgment.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Email** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to provide a point of contact for any follow-up discussions.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Phone** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to provide a point of contact for any follow-up discussions.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Form Signed** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_form_signed`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether or not you've signed the form. If yes, enter the date and time you signed it in the following fields.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Form Signed Date** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_form_signed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the form. This field is only required if you've indicated that you've signed the form.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Form Signed Time** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_form_signed_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you signed the form. This field is only required if you've indicated that you've signed the form.
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Form Signed Note** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_form_signed_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments regarding signing the form.

## Tips
- Ensure you've reviewed the policy carefully before filling in this form.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative or manager.
