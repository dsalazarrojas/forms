# Staff Longevity And Attrition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand staff satisfaction and retention within the organization. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement to maintain a positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question truthfully, based on your experience and observations.
2. Please complete all questions that apply to you.
3. You can skip questions that do not relate to your experience or situation.
4. Take as much time as you need to complete the survey, but feel free to submit your answers when finished.
