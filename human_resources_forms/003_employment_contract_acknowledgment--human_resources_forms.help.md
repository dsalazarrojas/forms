# employment_contract_acknowledgment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for employees to acknowledge that they have received and reviewed an employment contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have reviewed and understand the employment contract.
2. Fill in the date you acknowledged the contract on the "acknowledgment Date" field.
3. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, required: false): This field is where you will briefly describe or summarize the key terms of your employment contract. Please be as concise as possible.
* **Acknowledgment Date** (`acknowledgment_date`, date, required: false): This field is where you will input the date you acknowledged the employment contract. Please use the correct date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
 
## Tips
* Make sure to review the employment contract carefully before submitting this form.
* Keep the form up to date with your current employment contract information.
* Only submit this form once for each employment contract; do not submit new forms for any changes to your contract.
