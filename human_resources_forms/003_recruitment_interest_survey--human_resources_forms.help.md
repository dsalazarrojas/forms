# Recruitment Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Interest Survey is a form designed to collect information from potential applicants about their interest in job openings. By completing this form, you'll help our team understand your qualifications and preferences, making it easier for us to match you with suitable job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First and Last Name in the "First and Last Name" field.
2. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Provide your contact email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
5. Select the type of job you're interested in (Full Time, Part Time, Contract, Temporary, or Intern) in the "Job Type" field.
6. Choose your level of experience (Entry-Level, Mid Level, Senior Level, or Executive Level) in the "Job Level" field.
7. Enter your preferred location in the "Location" field.
8. Select your available job start date in the "Job Start Date" field.
9. Enter your preferred job end date in the "Job End Date" field.
10. Specify your desired salary range in the "Salary Range" field.
11. Describe your job in the "Job Description" field.
12. List any job requirements in the "Job Requirements" field.
13. List your ideal skills in the "Ideal Skills" field.
14. Specify any required skills in the "Required Skills" field.
15. Describe your work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
16. Describe your education background in the "Education" field.
17. Choose the days you are available to work in the "Availability" field.
18. Select your preferred work schedule in the "Work Schedule" field.
19. Choose where you prefer to work (Office, Remote, or Both) in the "Work Location" field.
20. Select your preferred work environment (Team Lead, Manager, or Team Member) in the "Work Environment" field.
21. Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Text) in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
22. Select your language proficiency (English, Spanish, or French) in the "Language" field.
23. If you have a resume, attach it in the "Resume Attachment" field.
24. If you have a resume online, provide the URL in the "Resume URL" field.
25. Add any other relevant information in the "Other Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First and Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the job you're interested in.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Job Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of job you're interested in (Full Time, Part Time, Contract, Temporary, or Intern).
* **Job Level** (Select One, Optional): Choose your level of experience (Entry-Level, Mid Level, Senior Level, or Executive Level).
* **Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where you'd like to work.
* **Job Start Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you'd like to start working.
* **Job End Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you'd like your job to end.
* **Salary Range** (Text, Optional): Specify your desired salary range.
* **Job Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the job you're interested in.
* **Job Requirements** (Text, Optional): List any specific requirements for the job.
* **Ideal Skills** (Text, Optional): List any ideal skills you have for the job.
* **Required Skills** (Text, Optional): List any required skills for the job.
* **Work Experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your work experience.
* **Education** (Text, Optional): Describe your education background.
* **Availability** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the days you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Work Schedule** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your preferred work schedule (8am-5pm, 5pm-12am, Other, 24/7).
* **Work Location** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose where you prefer to work (Office, Remote, Both).
* **Work Environment** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your preferred work environment (Team Lead, Manager, Team Member).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Language** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your language proficiency (English, Spanish, French).
* **Resume Attachment** (Note, Optional): If you have a resume, attach it here.
* **Resume URL** (Text, Optional): If you have a resume online, provide the URL.
* **Other Information** (Text, Optional): Add any other relevant information about yourself.
