# orientation-policy-acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about employees, including their demographic details and acceptance of the orientation policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your **Email** address to ensure communication with you.
3. Enter your **Job Title** to help us understand your role in the organization.
4. Select the date you **Started** working with the organization.
5. If applicable, select the **Orientation Policy** you have accepted.
6. Enter your **Manager's Name** (if applicable).
7. Choose your **Employee Status** from the provided options.
8. Once you have filled out all the required fields, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your company email address for internal communication.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title within the company.
* **Date Started** (`date_started`, date, required): Select the date you started working with the organization.
* **Accepted Orientation Policy** (`accepted_policy`, select_one, optional): Select the orientation policy you have accepted, if applicable.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name if you have a direct supervisor.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose your current employee status from the provided options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate processing.
* If you're unsure about the orientation policy, please consult with your manager or HR representative.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
