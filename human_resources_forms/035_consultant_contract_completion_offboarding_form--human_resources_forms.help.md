# Consultant Contract Completion Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a consultant's contract completion and offboarding process. It helps track the consultant's progress, ensures all tasks are completed, and confirms the transfer of knowledge and assets.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by reviewing the offboarding overview section, which is simply a note area to provide a brief description of the offboarding process.
2.  In the second section, enter the consultant's name in the format "Last name, First name" as it appears on their official records.
3.  In the contract end date section, enter the date the contract officially ended.
4.  In the deliverables completed section, select 'True' if all the contract deliverables are finished or 'False' otherwise.
5.  If applicable, confirm the submission of the final report by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the final report submitted section.
6.  In the knowledge transfer done section, confirm if the knowledge transfer sessions were completed by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
7.  In the return of assets section, enter any company assets returned by the consultant, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Consultant name**: (`consultant_name`, text, required): This field is where you enter the consultant's full name as it is recorded in official documents.
*   **Contract end date**: (`contract_end_date`, date, required): This is the date when the consultant's contract officially ended.
*   **Deliverables completed**: (`deliverables_completed`, select_one, required): Check 'True' if all the consultant's deliverables have been finished, or 'False' otherwise.
*   **Final report submitted**: (`final_report_submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm if the final report of the consultant has been submitted by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
*   **Knowledge transfer done**: (`knowledge_transfer_done`, select_one, required): Confirm if the knowledge transfer sessions with the consultant have been completed by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
*   **Return of assets**: (`return_of_assets`, text, optional): If any company assets were returned by the consultant, enter a description of what was returned in this field.
*   **Offboarding Overview**: (`section_offboarding_overview`, note, not required): This field is simply a place to provide a brief description of the offboarding process.
