# Performance Review Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Review Report Form is a tool for managers to provide feedback and guidance to employees on their performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and your name (as the manager) in the respective fields.
2. Select the review date and evaluation date using the date picker.
3. Choose the evaluation period from the dropdown menu (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
4. Provide a brief description of the employee's strengths.
5. Identify areas where the employee needs improvement.
6. Outline the employee's goals.
7. Add any additional comments.
8. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the manager.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the performance review.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Select the date the review was evaluated.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, select_one, optional): Choose the evaluation period (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional): Describe the employee's strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Identify areas where the employee needs improvement.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Outline the employee's goals.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Sign and date the form.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Sign your name as the manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide constructive feedback and guidance to help the employee grow.
* Use this form as a starting point and adapt it to your organization's specific needs and requirements.
* Keep the feedback focused on specific behaviors and actions, rather than general traits or characteristics.
