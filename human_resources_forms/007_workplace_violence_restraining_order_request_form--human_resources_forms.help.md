# Workplace Violence Restraining Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and report incidents of workplace violence, allowing HR to take necessary steps to protect employees and maintain a safe work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the following fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Review your company's policy on workplace violence before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`workplace_violence_restraining_order_request_form`, text, required/optional): Describe the incident that occurred, including any details that are relevant to the request for a restraining order.
* **Reporting Person** (`reporting_person`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name and title/position.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Please provide a detailed description of what happened, including any witnesses or injuries.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, required/optional): If there were any witnesses, please provide their names and contact information.
* **Protective Action Requested** (`protective_action_requested`, select_one, true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Requesting Protection
	+ No Protection Requested
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): If there's anything else you'd like to mention, please share it here.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, date, required/optional): If you are requesting protection, please have a witness sign and date this form. If no witness is available, please explain why not.

Note: This form is not intended to be a comprehensive report, but rather a quick and concise way to document incidents of workplace violence. Please refer to your company's policy on workplace violence for further guidance.
