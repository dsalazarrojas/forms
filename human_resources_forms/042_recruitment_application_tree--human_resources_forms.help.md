# Recruitment Application Tree - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Application Tree form is a tool designed to gather information from applicants for a position within the company. It is a comprehensive and organized way to collect personal, educational, and professional details necessary for evaluating applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the fields on the Main Page, which includes a brief personal message.
2. Proceed to the Personal Details page and provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
3. On the Education Background page, choose whether you have a degree or not.
4. The Work Experience page is where you can list your work experience in the required format.
5. In the Skills page, you can add any relevant skills you possess.
6. Finally, upload any necessary documents and click the Submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (`title`, text, optional): This is the first page where you can write a brief introduction or message to explain your application.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Enter your personal details such as name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have completed a degree or not.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, optional): List your work experience in a structured format, including job title, company name, and duration of employment.
* **Skills** (`skills`, note, optional): Add any relevant skills you possess, including software, programming languages, and tools.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, optional): Upload any necessary documents such as a resume, cover letter, or references.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Review your form before clicking the submit button to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
