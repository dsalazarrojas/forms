## Corporate Well Being Innovation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Corporate Well Being Innovation Application Form is used by employees to submit proposals for new corporate well-being initiatives. This form helps gather necessary information about the project, its timeline, and the team members involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    *   Fill out each section of the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
    *   Make sure to review and submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.
    *   If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to your department or team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Details
    *   **Employee Details (employee_details)**: 
        *   A text field to enter your name and any other identifying information as an employee.
### 2. Project Proposal
    *   **Project Proposal (project_proposal)**: 
        *   A text field to briefly describe your project proposal.
### 3. Contact Info
    *   **Contact Info (contact_info)**: 
        *   A text field to enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
### 4. Wellness Project Details
    *   **Wellness Project Details (wellness_project_details)**: 
        *   A text field to enter your wellness project proposal details.
### 5. Project Budget
    *   **Project Budget (project_budget)**: 
        *   A text field to enter the budget required for your project.
### 6. Submission Date
    *   **Submission Date (submission_date)**: 
        *   A date field to enter the date you plan to submit your project.
### 7. Project Duration
    *   **Project Duration (project_duration)**: 
        *   A number field to enter the duration of your project.
### 8. Team Leads
    *   **Team Leads (team_leads)**: 
        *   A select multiple field with options "Jim" and "John" to select team leads for your project.
