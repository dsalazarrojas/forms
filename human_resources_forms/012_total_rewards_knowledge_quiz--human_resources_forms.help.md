# Total Rewards Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Total Rewards Knowledge Quiz is designed to evaluate an individual's knowledge about company total rewards in the areas of compensation, benefits, and recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each page of questions as accurately as possible.
2. On the "compensation" page, select one or more of the options that you feel accurately describe your current compensation package.
3. On the "benefits" page, select the option that best aligns with your current benefits package.
4. On the "recognition" page, select whether you currently receive recognition as part of your total rewards package.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Compensation** (Select One/Many):
  Select the level of your current compensation package: High, Medium, or Low.

- **Benefits** (Select One):
  Select the range of your current benefits package. This could be $1000-2000, $2000-4000, or $4000-6000.

- **Recognition** (Select One/Many):
  Indicate whether you currently receive recognition as part of your total rewards package: 'True' or 'False'.
