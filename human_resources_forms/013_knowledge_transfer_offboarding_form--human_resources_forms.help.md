# knowledge_transfer_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The knowledge transfer offboarding form is a critical step in the offboarding process, which helps to ensure that the departing employee's knowledge and responsibilities are transferred to the right team members and that the company's continuity and security are maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your User Information page with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your Email Address in the format "john.doe@example.com".
3. Provide your Phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
4. Select the Project Lead responsible for your projects from the dropdown menu.
5. Check the Knowledge Domains that are relevant to the projects you were working on.
6. Enter the Start Date and End Date of your tenure with the company.
7. Provide a brief Reason for Departure to help the next team understand the circumstances of your departure.
8. Write down any Next Steps that the next team should take in your absence.
9. Add any relevant Knowledge Transfer Notes to ensure a smooth transition of your knowledge and responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user`, `text`, required): Enter your full name and any other relevant details.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your company email address in the format "john.doe@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, `select_one`, required): Select the Project Lead responsible for your projects from the dropdown menu.
* **Knowledge Domains** (`knowledge_domains`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the Knowledge Domains that are relevant to the projects you were working on.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started your tenure with the company.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you ended your tenure with the company.
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, `text`, required): Provide a brief reason for your departure to help the next team understand the circumstances.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required): Write down any Next Steps that the next team should take in your absence.
* **Knowledge Transfer Notes** (`knowledge_transfer_notes`, `text`, required): Add any relevant Knowledge Transfer Notes to ensure a smooth transition of your knowledge and responsibilities.
* **Knowledge Transfer Plan** (`knowledge_transfer_plan`, `text`, required): Describe the plan for transferring your knowledge and responsibilities to the next team.
