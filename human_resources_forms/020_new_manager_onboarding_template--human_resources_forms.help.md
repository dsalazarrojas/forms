# New Manager Onboarding Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Manager Onboarding Template is a form used to gather essential information for new managers, ensuring a smooth transition to their new role. It helps HR and other teams understand the new manager's needs and preferences, facilitating their integration into the company's systems and processes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category for which you are onboarding (e.g., HR, Operations, Finance, or Other).
2. Enter a brief description of your new role or the reason for your onboarding.
3. Choose the tool you would like to be assigned for your onboarding process (e.g., chatjimmy).
4. Select the manager preferences that best fit your work style (e.g., Preference 1, Preference 2, or Preference 3).
5. Enter your work email address.
6. Enter your work phone number.
7. Select a start date for your onboarding process.
8. (Optional) Enter a specific start time for your onboarding process.
9. Leave any additional comments or notes about your onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select the department or team you will be working with (e.g., HR, Operations, Finance, or Other).
* **Description**: Briefly describe your new role or the reason for your onboarding.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool you would like to be assigned for your onboarding process (e.g., chatjimmy).
* **Manager Preferences**: Select the manager preferences that best fit your work style (e.g., Preference 1, Preference 2, or Preference 3).
* **Email**: Enter your work email address to enable communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number for easy contact.
* **Date**: Select the start date for your onboarding process.
* **Time**: (Optional) Enter a specific start time for your onboarding process, if desired.
* **Note**: Leave any additional comments or notes about your onboarding process.
