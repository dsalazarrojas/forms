# Care Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this care questionnaire is to gather information about the patient's current condition, medical history, and social well-being to provide a comprehensive assessment for care planning and decision-making. This form is designed for care providers, caregivers, or family members to gather valuable insights into the patient's physical, emotional, and social needs, and to identify potential concerns or needs that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the patient's date of birth from the calendar.
3. Enter your name and relationship to the patient in the "Your Name" field.
4. Choose your relationship to the patient from the dropdown menu.
5. Select the date you completed the questionnaire.
6. Answer the questions about your interaction with the patient, including how long you've known them, how often you have contact with them, and how you rate their physical well-being.
7. Report any changes you've observed in the patient's physical health and mobility concerns.
8. Select all activities of daily living that the patient requires assistance with.
9. Rate the patient's social well-being and identify activities they participate in.
10. Indicate the size of their support network and identify signs of loneliness or isolation.
11. Rate the patient's psychological well-being and identify any mood observations.
12. Note any cognitive concerns or behavioral changes you've observed.
13. Indicate how well the patient copes with stress.
14. Identify any safety concerns or home environment issues.
15. List any care needs that have been identified.
16. Choose the urgency of the patient's care needs.
17. Add any additional observations or concerns you have about the patient.
18. Finally, indicate your willingness to be involved in care planning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's full name, as they would like to be addressed.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Select the patient's date of birth from the calendar.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the person completing the questionnaire.
* **Your Relationship to Patient** (`respondent_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Choose your relationship to the patient from the dropdown menu.
* **Date Completed** (`questionnaire_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you completed the questionnaire.
* **How Long Have You Known the Patient** (`how_long_known`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long you've known the patient from the dropdown menu.
* **How Often Do You Have Contact** (`contact_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you have contact with the patient from the dropdown menu.
* **How Would You Rate Their Physical Well-being** (`physical_wellbeing_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the patient's physical well-being from the dropdown menu.
* **Have You Noticed Physical Health Changes** (`physical_health_changes`, `select_one`, required): Report any changes you've observed in the patient's physical health.
* **Mobility Concerns** (`mobility_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all activities the patient has difficulty with.
* **Assistance Needed With Daily Activities** (`activities_of_daily_living`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all activities the patient needs help with.
* **How Would You Rate Their Social Well-being** (`social_wellbeing_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the patient's social well-being from the dropdown menu.
* **Social Activities Participated In** (`social_activities`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify social activities the patient participates in.
* **Size of Support Network** (`support_network`, `select_one`, required): Choose the size of the patient's support network from the dropdown menu.
* **Signs of Loneliness or Isolation** (`loneliness_indicators`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all signs of loneliness or isolation you've observed.
* **How Would You Rate Their Psychological Well-being** (`psychological_wellbeing_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the patient's psychological well-being from the dropdown menu.
* **Mood Observations** (`mood_observations`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all mood observations you've noted.
* **Cognitive Concerns** (`cognitive_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all cognitive concerns you've observed.
* **Behavioral Changes** (`behavioral_changes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all behavioral changes you've observed.
* **How Well Do They Cope With Stress** (`stress_coping`, `select_one`, required): Rate how well the patient copes with stress.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all safety concerns you've observed.
* **Home Environment Assessment** (`home_environment`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current state of the patient's home environment from the dropdown menu.
* **Care Needs Identified** (`care_needs_identified`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all care needs you've identified.
* **Urgency of Care Needs** (`urgency_of_needs`, `select_one`, required): Choose the urgency of the patient's care needs from the dropdown menu.
* **Known Patient Preferences** (`patient_preferences`, `text`, optional): Enter any known patient preferences, routines, or needs.
* **Additional Observations or Concerns** (`additional_observations`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information or concerns.
* **Are You Willing to Be Involved in Care Planning** (`willing_to_help`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to be involved in the patient's care planning.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
* This form is for your use and should not be shared without permission.
* We appreciate your input and contributions to the patient's care planning.
