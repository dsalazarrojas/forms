# Peer Support Program Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Support Program Time Off Form is used to request time off for peer support activities. This form is intended to be filled out by employees who are participating in peer support programs and need to take time off from their work schedules to attend to these activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you would like to start your peer support activity.
2. Enter the start time of your peer support activity.
3. Enter the date you would like to end your peer support activity.
4. Enter the end time of your peer support activity.
5. Select the type of peer support activity you will be participating in (e.g. "peer support activity 1" or "peer support activity 2").
6. Select any applicable supervisor approvals required for your activity (e.g. "supervisor approval 1", "supervisor approval 2", etc.).
7. Select any applicable manager approvals required for your activity (e.g. "manager approval 1", "manager approval 2", etc.).
8. Enter your name as it appears on company records.
9. Enter a brief reason for your request, if necessary.
10. Enter any additional comments or details about your request.
11. Enter any comments from your supervisor.
12. Enter any comments from your manager.
