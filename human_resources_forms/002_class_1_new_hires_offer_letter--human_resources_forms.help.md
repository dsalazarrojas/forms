# Class 1 New Hires Offer Letter - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a Class 1 New Hire Offer Letter, a document that outlines the job offer details for a new employee. The form typically includes information such as the candidate's name, job title, start date, employment type, compensation, and acceptance of the offer.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Required Information
Before filling out the form, ensure you have all the necessary information ready:
* Candidate's full name
* Their email address
* Job title offered
* Proposed start date
* Employment type (full-time, part-time, contract, or temporary)
* Brief description of salary or hourly rate
* Reporting manager's name (if applicable)
* Background checks and other conditions of employment (if applicable)
* Candidate's signature date (if you're providing a formal offer letter)

### Step 2: Fill in the Form
Complete each field with the gathered information:
* **Candidate full name**: Enter the candidate's full legal name.
* **Candidate email address**: Enter the candidate's preferred contact email.
* **Position title offered**: Enter the job title included in the offer.
* **Proposed start date**: Enter the expected first day of employment.
* **Employment type**: Select the employment arrangement (full time, part time, contract, or temporary).
* **Compensation summary**: Briefly describe the salary or hourly rate offered.
* **Reporting manager name**: If applicable, enter the direct manager's or supervisor's name.
* **Conditions of employment**: If applicable, enter any background checks or other conditions.
* **Acceptance of offer**: Confirm the candidate's acceptance of the offer (Accept, Decline, or Request changes).
* **Signature date**: Date the candidate signs the offer (if you're providing a formal offer letter).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Candidate full name** (Required)
Enter the candidate's full legal name, as it appears on their identification.

### 2. **Candidate email address** (Required)
Use this field to enter the candidate's preferred contact email.

### 3. **Position title offered** (Required)
Enter the job title included in the offer.

### 4. **Proposed start date** (Required)
Enter the expected first day of employment.

### 5. **Employment type** (Required)
Select the employment arrangement:
* **Full time**: Regular, standard employment.
* **Part time**: Flexible or reduced hours.
* **Contract**: A fixed-term employment agreement.
* **Temporary**: Short-term or project-based employment.

### 6. **Compensation summary** (Required)
Briefly describe the salary or hourly rate offered.

### 7. **Reporting manager name** (Optional)
If applicable, enter the direct manager's or supervisor's name.

### 8. **Conditions of employment** (Optional)
If applicable, enter any background checks or other conditions.

### 9. **Acceptance of offer** (Required)
Confirm the candidate's acceptance of the offer:
* **Accept**: The candidate accepts the offer.
* **Decline**: The candidate declines the offer.
* **Request changes**: The candidate requests modifications to the offer.

### 10. **Signature date** (Optional)
Date the candidate signs the offer (if you're providing a formal offer letter).

## Tips
* Always verify the accuracy of the information before presenting the form to the candidate.
* Ensure you have the necessary approvals and signatures before sending the form to the candidate.
* Keep the form up to date as needed, especially if employment conditions change.
