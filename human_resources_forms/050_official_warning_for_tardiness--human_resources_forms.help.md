# official_warning_for_tardiness - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and concise way for employees to document and address tardiness issues in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select all the tardiness issues that apply to the employee.
2. Enter the employee's name and issue date.
3. Provide a brief description of the reason for the warning.
4. Describe the warning details.
5. Sign the form to confirm that you have issued the warning.
6. Choose the type of warning (Formal or Informal) that best suits the situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tardiness Issues (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the issues that apply to the employee, such as Issue 1, Issue 2, Issue 3, Issue 4, or Issue 5.
* **employee_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the employee who is being issued the warning.
* **start_date (Date)**: Enter the date when the employee's tardiness issues started.
* **end_date (Date)**: Enter the date when the employee's tardiness issues ended (if applicable).
* **Issue Description (Text)**: Provide a brief description of the reason for the warning.
* **warning_reason (Text)**: Describe the details of the warning.
* **warning_details (Text)**: Provide more details about the warning.
* **Signature (Note)**: Sign your name as the manager or supervisor who issued the warning.
* **Issue Date (Date)**: Enter the date when the warning was issued.
* **Issued By (Text)**: Enter your name as the manager or supervisor who issued the warning.
* **warning_type (Select One)**: Choose the type of warning that best suits the situation (Formal or Informal).

## Tips
* Make sure to select all the relevant tardiness issues that apply to the employee.
* Provide clear and concise descriptions for the reason for the warning and warning details.
* Sign the form to confirm that you have issued the warning.
* Choose the correct type of warning based on the severity of the issue.
