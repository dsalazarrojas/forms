# monthly_technician_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly technician evaluation form is used to gather feedback on a technician's performance, identify areas for improvement, and track progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Complete each section of the form thoroughly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Comments** (1):
	+ A text field for the manager to leave comments about the technician's performance.
	+ Please provide specific, constructive comments to help the technician improve.
* **Technician Name** (2):
	+ A text field to enter the technician's name.
	+ Enter the technician's name as it appears on their record.
* **Rating** (3):
	+ A numeric field to rate the technician's performance (e.g., 1-5).
	+ Rate the technician's performance based on your evaluation.
* **Comments** (4):
	+ A text field for additional comments on the technician's performance.
	+ Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your comments.
* **Strengths** (5):
	+ A multiple-choice field to select the technician's strengths.
	+ Select one or more of the following options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Areas for Improvement** (6):
	+ A text field for suggestions or areas where the technician can improve.
	+ Please provide specific, actionable suggestions for improvement.
* **Action Planned** (7):
	+ A text field to outline planned actions for improvement.
	+ Describe the actions you plan to take to help the technician improve.
* **Follow-up** (8):
	+ A date field to schedule follow-up discussions or evaluations.
	+ Enter the date for the next evaluation or discussion.
* **Manager Signature** (9):
	+ A text field for the manager's signature.
	+ Enter your signature to confirm completion of the form.

## Tips
- Be honest and constructive in your evaluation and comments.
- Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your comments.
- Schedule follow-up evaluations or discussions as needed.
- Use this form to track progress over time and make data-driven decisions.
