# Seasonal Work Adjustment Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Work Adjustment Time Off Form is used for requesting adjustments to work schedules during seasonal periods, typically when there is a decrease in work demand or when employees need to adjust their work hours to better align with their work needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your seasonal work adjustment request in the "Description" field.
2. Select the correct category for your request from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your employee name and email address in the "Employee Name" and "Employee Email" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the date you are requesting the adjustment in the "Request Date" field.
5. Enter the start and end dates of your requested adjustment in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
6. Select the status of your request from the "Status" dropdown menu.
7. Enter any additional comments or information about your request in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter the reason for your request in the "Request Reason" field.
9. Enter any additional information about your request in the "Additional Request Info" field.
10. Enter the name and email address of the person who will be approving your request in the "Approver" and "Approver Email" fields.
11. Enter the name and email address of the person who submitted the request in the "Requestor" and "Requestor Email" fields.
12. Enter the department or team you are requesting the adjustment for in the "Department" field.
13. Enter your current job title in the "Job Title" field.
14. Enter a request reason code (if applicable) in the "Request Reason Code" field.
15. Select the type of person submitting the request from the "Requestor Type" dropdown menu.
16. Enter the location where you will be working during the adjustment in the "Location" field.
