# Workplace Harassment Report Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Harassment Report Intake Form is a platform for employees to submit reports of incidents of workplace harassment. This form is designed to collect accurate and detailed information about the incidents, which will be reviewed and addressed by HR to ensure a safe and respectful work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and a description of the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
2. Specify whether you witnessed the incident or not, and if you did, how much of it you witnessed.
3. Indicate whether the harasser was an employee or non-employee.
4. Choose a resolution status for the incident.
5. Indicate whether you witnessed the harasser or not, and if you did, how much of it you witnessed.
6. Specify whether you witnessed others witness the incident or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Harassment Report** (`workplace_harassment_report`, text, optional): This is the main section for your report. Please provide a detailed description of the incident, including as much information as possible.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your official personnel records.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date the incident occurred. If the date is unknown, please indicate "Unknown".
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Please enter the time the incident occurred. If the time is unknown, please indicate "Unknown".
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Harasser** (`harasser`, text, optional): Please specify the name of the harasser.
* **Witness** (`witness`, text, optional): If you witnessed the incident, please provide a description of what you witnessed.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select, optional): Please select one of the options provided for the resolution status of the incident.
* **Witnessed** (`witness_witnessed`, select_multiple, optional): If you witnessed the incident, please indicate how much of the incident you witnessed.
* **Witnessed Harasser** (`witness_witnessed_harasser`, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate whether you witnessed the harasser.
* **Employee Witnessed** (`employee_witnessed`, select_multiple, optional): If you witnessed other employees, please indicate how many you witnessed.

Please ensure you answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, as the information provided will be reviewed and addressed by HR to ensure a safe and respectful work environment.
