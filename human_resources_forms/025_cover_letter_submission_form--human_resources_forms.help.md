# cover_letter_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "cover_letter_submission_form" is a form used to collect information for cover letter submissions, typically for job applications. It captures essential details about the applicant, including contact information, job-specific data, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field.
5. Describe your **Job Description** in the "Job Description" field.
6. Upload your **Resume** in the "Resume" field.
7. Write a brief **Cover Letter** in the "Cover Letter" field.
8. Enter any **Job Reference** if applicable.
9. Provide the **Company** you are applying to, in the "Company" field.
10. Specify the **Location** of the job posting, in the "Location" field.
11. Select the **Submit Date** you are submitting the application by.
12. Enter the details about who **Submitted By**, in the "Submitted By" field.
13. Enter the **Department** you are applying to, in the "Department" field.
14. Specify the **Job Type**, in the "Job Type" field.
15. Enter the **Job Status**, in the "Job Status" field.
16. Enter the **Job Category**, in the "Job Category" field.
17. Enter the **Job Level**, in the "Job Level" field.
18. Specify the **Job Family**, in the "Job Family" field.
19. Enter the **Job Function**, in the "Job Function" field.
20. Enter the **Job Location**, in the "Job Location" field.
21. Enter the **Job Posting ID**, in the "Job Posting ID" field.
22. Enter the **Job Position**, in the "Job Position" field.
23. Enter the **Job Title ID**, in the "Job Title ID" field.
24. Enter the **Job Description ID**, in the "Job Description ID" field.
25. If necessary, provide any **Additional Information** about your application in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Describe the job title you are applying for, or mention the job title you have held previously if applicable.

### Job Description
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, required): Briefly describe the job description or the position you are applying for.

### Resume
* **Resume** (`Resume`, text, required): Upload your resume as a document, and ensure it is in a format that can be read by the hiring team.

### Cover Letter
* **Cover Letter** (`Cover Letter`, text, required): Write a brief cover letter explaining why you are the best fit for the job and what makes you an ideal candidate.

### Job Reference
* **Job Reference** (`Job Reference`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any job references that can be contacted by the hiring team.

### Company
* **Company** (`Company`, text, required): Specify the company you are applying to, if different from the one you are uploading a resume for.

### Location
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required): Enter the location of the job posting, if different from your current location.

### Submit Date
* **Submit Date** (`Submit Date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you are submitting your application.

### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the application.

### Department
* **Department** (`Department`, text, required): Specify the department you are applying to.

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, text, required): Enter the type of job you are applying for.

### Job Status
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, text, required): Describe your job status, for example, full-time, part-time, or freelance.

### Job Category
* **Job Category** (`Job Category`, text, required): Enter the category of the job you are applying for.

### Job Level
* **Job Level** (`Job Level`, text, required): Enter the level of the job you are applying for.

### Job Family
* **Job Family** (`Job Family`, text, required): Specify the job family you are applying for.

### Job Function
* **Job Function** (`Job Function`, text, required): Describe the job function you are applying for.

### Job Location
* **Job Location** (`Job Location`, text, required): Enter the location of the job posting.

### Job Posting ID
* **Job Posting ID** (`Job Posting ID`, text, required): Enter the job posting ID.

### Job Position
* **Job Position** (`Job Position`, text, required): Enter the job position you are applying for.

### Job Title ID
* **Job Title ID** (`Job Title ID`, text, required): Enter the job title ID.

### Job Description ID
* **Job Description ID** (`Job Description ID`, text, required): Enter the job description ID.

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, note, optional): If necessary, provide any additional information about your application that you feel is relevant.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting.
* Use a valid email address.
* Proofread your resume and cover letter for errors.
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Contact the hiring team if you have any questions or concerns.
* Submit the application before the specified submit date.
* Be clear and concise in your responses.
* Proofread the form for errors before submitting.
