# Chronic Condition Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off for chronic conditions. Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that your workplace is aware of your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employee Information**
	* `first_name`: Enter your first name.
	* `last_name`: Enter your last name.
	* `employee_id`: Enter your employee ID.
	* `department`: Enter your department.
	* `manager_name`: Enter your direct manager's name.
	* `contact_info`: Enter your contact phone number.
2. **Time Off Details**
	* `start_date`: Enter the start date of your time off.
	* `end_date`: Enter the end date of your time off.
	* `duration_days`: Enter the total number of days you will be absent.
	* `time_off_type`: Select the type of time off you are requesting (Full day, Half day, Hourly leave, Intermtent, etc.).
	* `medical_section`: This section is used for medical information and should only be filled out if necessary.
	* `condition_name`: Enter the name of your chronic condition.
	* `condition_type`: Select the type of your chronic condition (Cardiovascular, Respiratory, Metabolic, Autoimmune, Neurological, Mental Health, Musculoskeletal, Other).
	* `medical_certification`: Select whether you have attached a medical certification (True, False, Will be provided).
	* `ongoing_treatment`: Select whether you are currently undergoing regular appointments (Yes, regular appointments), a one-time appointment (Yes, one-time appointment), no ongoing absences (No, episodic flare), or other.
	* `appointment_frequency`: Select the estimated frequency of absences (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, As needed).
	* `work_accommodation`: This section is used to collect additional accommodations information and should only be filled out if necessary.
	* `accommodation_needed`: Select whether you need any work accommodations (True, False, Under review).
	* `accommodation_details`: Enter the details of the accommodations you need.
	* `remote_work`: Select whether you can work remotely during this period (Yes, fully remote, Yes, partial remote, False, Not applicable).
	* `additional_notes`: Enter any additional information or special circumstances.
	* `confidentiality`: Select whether you understand that this information is confidential (Yes, I agree, False).

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
