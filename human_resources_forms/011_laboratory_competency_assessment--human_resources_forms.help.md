# Laboratory Competency Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Laboratory Competency Assessment" form is a self-assessment tool used by laboratory personnel to evaluate their competency in various aspects of laboratory procedures, safety, and regulations. This form helps to verify that laboratory staff has the necessary knowledge and skills to perform their job duties safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions honestly and accurately based on your current knowledge and experience.
* For each question, select the answer that best reflects your understanding and competency in that area.
* Make sure to complete all mandatory fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
* If you are unsure or do not know the answer to a question, please mark the corresponding field as "No" or "I don't know".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Understanding (1)** (`number`, required): This question assesses your overall understanding of laboratory procedures and policies.
* **Laboratory Safety and Security (2)** (`select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" if you are aware of and comply with laboratory safety and security protocols.
* **Blood Borestream Infection Control (3)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable:
	+ "Yes" if you are aware of and follow blood-borestream infection control procedures.
* **Chemical Handling (4)** (`select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" if you have received training on chemical handling and follow procedures.
* **Blood Sample Handling (5)** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select all applicable:
	+ "Yes" if you are aware of and follow blood sample handling procedures.
* **Laboratory Equipment (6)** (`date`, not required): This field is not relevant to the form. Please ignore.
* **Quality Control and Improvement (7)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable:
	+ "Good" if you follow quality control and improvement procedures.
* **Regulatory Compliance (8)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable:
	+ "Yes" if you are aware of and comply with regulatory requirements.
* **General Laboratory Instrumentation (9)** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select all applicable:
	+ "Yes" if you are familiar with and properly use general laboratory instrumentation.
* **Safety Procedures (10)** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select all applicable:
	+ "Yes" if you follow safety procedures.
*Note: Please ensure that you have received appropriate training and authorization before selecting "Yes" for these fields. If you are unsure or do not know the answer, please mark the corresponding field as "No" or "I don't know". 

Please complete the form accurately and honestly, and return it to your supervisor or designated personnel for review and feedback.
