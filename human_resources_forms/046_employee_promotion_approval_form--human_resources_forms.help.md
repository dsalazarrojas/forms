# Employee Promotion Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Promotion Approval Form is a crucial document used to officially approve employee promotions. It helps in streamlining the promotion process, ensuring that all necessary information is captured and that both the employee and manager are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the employee's name, job title, and department to identify the employee and their current role.
*   Indicate if the promotion is a job title change by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the job title field.
*   Choose the department where the employee will be working after the promotion.
*   Select the reporting manager for the promoted employee.
*   Enter the effective date of the promotion.
*   Choose the promotion type (e.g., promotion, demotion, or no change).
*   Provide a brief reason for the promotion (optional).
*   Indicate if the employee promotion committee is involved (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
*   Enter the date of the employee's promotion (if applicable).
*   Sign and date the form as the manager to approve the promotion.
*   Sign and date the form as the employee to acknowledge the promotion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name (1)** (`Employee Name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the employee being promoted.
*   **Job Title (2)** (`Job Title`, `text`, optional): Enter the current job title of the employee.
*   **Department (3)** (`Department`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department where the employee will be working after the promotion.
*   **Reporting Manager (4)** (`Reporting Manager`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the reporting manager for the promoted employee.
*   **Job Title Change (5)** (`Job Title Change`, `text`, optional): Check 'Yes' if the promotion involves a job title change; otherwise, check 'No'.
*   **Effective Date (6)** (`Effective Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the promotion will take effect.
*   **Promotion Type (7)** (`Promotion Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether this promotion is a promotion, demotion, or no change.
*   **Reason for Promotion (8)** (`Reason for Promotion`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the promotion (e.g., performance, seniority).
*   **Employee Promotion Committee (9)** (`Employee Promotion Committee`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if the employee promotion committee is involved in the promotion decision.
*   **Employee Promotion Date (10)** (`Employee Promotion Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the employee's promotion will take place (if different from the effective date).
*   **Manager Signature (11)** (`Manager Signature`, `text`, optional): Sign and date this field as the manager to approve the promotion.
*   **Date Approved (12)** (`Date Approved`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you, as the manager, approve the promotion.
*   **Employee Signature (13)** (`Employee Signature`, `text`, optional): Sign and date this field as the employee to acknowledge the promotion.

Note: This form requires the approval of both the employee and the manager, ensuring a smooth and official promotion process.
