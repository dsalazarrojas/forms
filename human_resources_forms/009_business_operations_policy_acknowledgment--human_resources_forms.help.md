# Business Operations Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm that employees have read and understand the various policies that govern business operations within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through the form and read each policy section carefully.
2. For each policy, select "Yes, I acknowledge" if you have read and understand the policy.
3. Enter your employment details, including your first name, last name, employee ID, email address, department, job title, and employment start date.
4. Confirm that you have received copies of all company policies.
5. Confirm that all your questions have been answered.
6. Enter the current date to confirm your acknowledgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (employee_first_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (employee_last_name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or government-issued ID.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id, text, required): Enter your unique employee identifier.
* **Email Address** (employee_email, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Department** (department, text, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Employment Start Date** (start_date, date, required): Enter the date you began employment with the company.
* **I have read and understand the Code of Conduct** (code_of_conduct, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the Code of Conduct policy.
* **I have read and understand the Confidentiality Policy** (confidentiality_policy, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the Confidentiality Policy.
* **I have read and understand the Anti-Harassment Policy** (anti_harassment_policy, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the Anti-Harassment Policy.
* **I have read and understand the Data Security Policy** (data_security_policy, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the Data Security Policy.
* **I have read and understand the Attendance Policy** (attendance_policy, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the Attendance Policy.
* **I have read and understand the Health and Safety Policy** (health_safety_policy, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the Health and Safety Policy.
* **I confirm that I have received copies of all company policies** (policies_received, select_one, required): Confirm that you have received copies of all company policies.
* **All my questions have been answered** (questions_answered, select_one, required): Confirm that all your questions have been answered.
* **Date of Acknowledgment** (acknowledgment_date, date, required): Enter the current date to confirm your acknowledgment.
