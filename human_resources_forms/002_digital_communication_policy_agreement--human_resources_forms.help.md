# Digital Communication Policy Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect agreement from individuals about the company's digital communication policy. It ensures that users understand and agree to the terms of the policy, which outlines the types of digital communications they will receive and their options for opting out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and agree to the terms outlined in the policy. The form is divided into the following sections:

- **Last Name** (`digital_communication_policy_agreement_last_name`, text, required): Please fill in your last name as it appears on your documentation.
- **Email** (`digital_communication_policy_agreement_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address where you would like to receive digital communications from the company.
- **Phone** (`digital_communication_policy_agreement_phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number where you would like to receive digital communications from the company.

- **I agree to receive digital communications from the company** (`digital_communication_policy_agreement_agreement_2`, note, required): Please select this option to indicate that you agree to receive digital communications from the company.
- **I understand that I have the right to opt-out of digital communications** (`digital_communication_policy_agreement_agreement_3`, note, required): Please read and acknowledge that you have the right to opt-out of digital communications.
- **I understand the purpose and scope of digital communications** (`digital_communication_policy_agreement_agreement_4`, note, required): Please read and agree to the purpose and scope of the digital communication policy.

- **I understand that digital communications may be sent to my personal email** (`digital_communication_policy_agreement_agreement_6`, note, required): Please read and agree that digital communications may be sent to your personal email.
- **I understand that digital communications may be sent to my phone** (`digital_communication_policy_agreement_agreement_7`, note, required): Please read and agree that digital communications may be sent to your phone.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and agree to the terms of the digital communication policy.
- Ensure that you provide accurate email and phone information to receive digital communications from the company.
