# HR Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate the review process for employee concerns or issues. It is intended for use by HR staff and managers to request reviews of specific concerns or issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have permission from the relevant manager to submit a review request.
2. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure all required and optional fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (1, Text, Optional): The name of the employee who the concern belongs to.
* **Role** (2, Select One, Optional): Whether the concern is an active or inactive issue.
* **Department** (3, Select One, Optional): The department the concern or issue belongs to.
* **Issue Description** (4, Text, Optional): A brief description of the concern or issue.
* **Supporting Details** (5, Text, Optional): Additional details that support the concern or issue.
* **Concern Priority** (6, Select One, Optional): The level of priority for the concern or issue.
* **Concern Type** (7, Select One, Optional): The type of concern or issue.
* **Concern Status** (8, Select One, Optional): The status of the concern or issue.
* **Concern Details** (9, Text, Optional): Additional details about the concern or issue.
* **Concern Date** (10, Date, Optional): The date the concern or issue was raised.
* **Concern Time** (11, Time, Optional): The time the concern or issue was raised.
* **Manager Name** (12, Text, Optional): The manager's name associated with the concern or issue.
* **Action Required** (13, Text, Optional): Any specific actions required for the review.
* **Review Date** (14, Date, Optional): The date the review is scheduled for.
* **Review Time** (15, Time, Optional): The time the review is scheduled for.
* **Routing Info** (16, Text, Optional): Any additional routing information for the review.
* **Comments** (17, Text, Optional): Any comments about the concern or issue.
* **Submitted By** (18, Text, Optional): The person who submitted the review request.
* **Submitted On** (19, Date, Optional): The date and time the review request was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (20, Time, Optional): The time the review request was submitted.
* **Submitted Date** (21, Date, Optional): The date the review request was submitted.
* **Updated By** (22, Text, Optional): The person who updated the review request.
* **Updated On** (23, Date, Optional): The date and time the review request was updated.
* **Updated Time** (24, Time, Optional): The time the review request was updated.
* **Updated Date** (25, Date, Optional): The date the review request was updated.
