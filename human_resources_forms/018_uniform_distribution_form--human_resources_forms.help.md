# Uniform Distribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Distribution Form is used to record uniform distribution data for staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the staff member's ID in the "staff member id" field.
2. Enter the staff member's name in the "staff name" field.
3. Select the staff member's department from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter the date of the uniform distribution in the "date" field.
5. Enter the number of uniforms being distributed in the "number of uniforms" field.
6. Select the size of the uniform being distributed in the "size" field.
7. If necessary, enter any additional remarks about the distribution.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Member ID** (staff_member_id, number, required: false): Enter the unique ID of the staff member receiving the uniform.
* **Staff Name** (staff_name, text, required: false): Enter the staff member's name.
* **Department** (department, select_one, required: false): Select the department of the staff member from the drop-down menu.
* **Date** (date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the uniform distribution in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Number of Uniforms** (number_of_uniforms, number, required: false): Enter the number of uniforms being distributed.
* **Size** (size, select_one, required: false): Select the size of the uniform being distributed from the drop-down menu.
* **Quantity Distributed** (quantity_distributed, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of uniforms being distributed.
* **Remarks** (remarks, text, required: false): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the distribution.
