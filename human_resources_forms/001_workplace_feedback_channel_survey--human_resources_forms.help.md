# workplace_feedback_channel_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workplace feedback channel survey is a tool designed to collect feedback from employees on their experiences and preferences regarding the feedback channel. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences to improve the channel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out this form for legitimate feedback purposes.
2. Fill in your Employee ID.
3. Provide your Name.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Select your preferred feedback channel (Phone, Email, Chat, or Video Call).
6. Type in your Comment (optional).
7. Specify your Contact Request (Date).
8. Select your Time of Day.
9. Enter your Manager's ID.
10. Select your Department (HR, IT, or Finance).
11. Choose your Feedback Frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never).
12. Enter your Employee Department (optional).
13. Select your preferred Channel (Phone, Email, Chat, Video Call, or In Person).
14. Enter your Manager's Name.
15. Answer the Feedback Request question (Yes or No).
16. Enter the number of follow-up needed.
17. Select the last Follow-up Frequency (Once, Twice, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never).
18. Select the channel used for the last follow-up (Phone, Email, Chat, or Video Call).
19. Enter the Time of the last Follow-up (optional).
20. Enter your Manager's Contact information (Name and Department).
21. Select the channel used for the last contact (Phone, Email, Chat, or Video Call).
22. Enter the Time of the last contact (optional).
23. Enter your Manager's Department.
24. Enter the channel used for the last contact (optional).
25. Enter the location of the last contact (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (1, number, required): Your unique employee ID number.
* **Name** (2, text, required): Your full name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Your official email address.
* **Feedback Channel** (4, select_one, required): Select your preferred channel for receiving feedback (Phone, Email, Chat, or Video Call).
* **Comment** (5, note, optional): Type in any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Contact Request** (6, date, required): Specify your contact request date.
* **Time Of Day** (7, time, optional): Enter your current time of day.
* **Manager ID** (8, number, required): Your manager's unique ID number.
* **Department** (9, select_multiple, required): Select your department (HR, IT, or Finance).
* **Feedback Frequency** (10, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to receive feedback (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never).
* **Employee Department** (11, text, optional): Your department name (optional).
* **Channel Preference** (12, select_multiple, optional): Select all the channels you prefer to use for feedback (Phone, Email, Chat, Video Call, In Person).
* **Manager Name** (13, text, required): Enter your manager's full name.
* **Feedback Request** (14, select_one, required): Answer whether you would like feedback or not (Yes or No).
* **Follow Up** (15, number, required): The number of follow-ups you need.
* **Last Follow Up** (16, date, optional): Enter the date of your last follow-up.
* **Follow Up Frequency** (17, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to receive follow-ups (Once, Twice, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never).
* **Channel Of Last Follow Up** (18, select_multiple, required): Select the channel used for your last follow-up (Phone, Email, Chat, or Video Call).
* **Last Follow Up Time** (19, time, optional): Enter the time of your last follow-up.
* **Manager Contact** (20, text, required): Enter your manager's contact information (Name and Department).
* **Channel Preference Last Contact** (21, select_multiple, required): Select the channel used for your last contact (Phone, Email, Chat, Video Call, or In Person).
* **Last Contact Time** (22, time, optional): Enter the time of your last contact.
* **Manager Department** (23, text, required): Enter your manager's department.
* **Last Contact Channel** (24, text, optional): Enter the channel used for your last contact.
* **Last Contact Location** (25, text, optional): Enter the location of your last contact.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective when providing feedback.
* Make sure to provide complete and accurate information.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the Comment section.
* If you need any follow-up, select the number of follow-ups you need in the Follow Up field.
* If you have any specific request for follow-up, specify the channel used and frequency in the Follow Up Frequency and Channel Of Last Follow Up fields.
* If you have any specific request for last contact, specify the channel used and location in the Last Contact Channel and Last Contact Location fields.
