# International Training Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request international training leave of absence. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

* To begin, fill in your employee name.
* Select your job title.
* Enter the training date you plan to attend.
* Provide the location of the training.
* Enter the date you expect to return.
* If applicable, select the travel dates if you are traveling for the training.
* Enter the training hours and duration.
* Choose the type of training you will be attending.
* Enter any relevant training details.
* If you are traveling for the training, provide the travel cost.
* Select how your supervisor and HR should be notified about your request.
* Choose the status of your request (e.g., approved, pending, rejected).
* Enter any approval notes from your supervisor or HR.
* If applicable, provide dates for leave start and end.
* Select the type of leave you are requesting.
* Enter the start and end times for your leave.
* Add any comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee-name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge or payroll records.
* **Job Title** (`job-title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears in your employee profile.
* **Training Date** (`training-date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training you wish to attend.
* **Training Location** (`training-location`, text, required): Enter the location of the training.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor-name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor-email`, email, required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor-phone`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Training Hours** (`training-hours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours of training you will attend.
* **Training Duration** (`training-duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days or weeks of training you will attend.
* **Training Type** (`training-type`, select_one, required): Select the type of training you will be attending (e.g., workshop, conference, seminar).
* **Training Details** (`training-details`, note, required): Enter any relevant details about the training.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel-dates`, date, required): If you are traveling for the training, enter the travel dates.
* **Return Date** (`return-date`, date, required): Enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Travel Cost** (`travel-cost`, number, required): If you are traveling for the training, enter the estimated travel cost.
* **Supervisor Approve** (`supervisor-approve`, select_one, required): Select how your supervisor should be notified about your request.
* **HR Approve** (`hr-approve`, select_one, required): Select how HR should be notified about your request.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of your request (e.g., approved, pending, rejected).
* **Approval Notes** (`approval-notes`, text, required): Enter any approval notes from your supervisor or HR.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave-start-date`, date, required): If applicable, enter the date you will start your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave-end-date`, date, required): If applicable, enter the date you will end your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave-type`, select_one, required): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, sick leave, bereavement leave).
* **Leave Start Time** (`leave-start-time`, time, required): If applicable, enter the start time of your leave.
* **Leave End Time** (`leave-end-time`, time, required): If applicable, enter the end time of your leave.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about your request.
