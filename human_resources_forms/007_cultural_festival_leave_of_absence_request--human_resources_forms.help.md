# Cultural Festival Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a cultural festival leave of absence from work. You can use it to provide necessary details about your absence, including your contact information, the specifics of your leave, and any supporting documents you may need to attach.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form on the first page with your contact information.
2. On the second page, provide detailed information about your leave, including the festival you will be attending and the dates you will be absent.
3. If necessary, attach any supporting documents that may be required by your employer.
4. Add any comments you would like to include with your request.
5. Submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`Name`, `Email`, `Phone`, `Job Title`): This section is used to provide your name, contact email, phone number, and job title, so we can reach you and your supervisor for any questions or concerns.
* **Leave Details** (`Leave Dates`, `Festival Name`, `Reason for Leave`): This section is used to provide the exact dates of your leave, the name of the festival you will be attending, and a brief description of your reason for taking leave.
* **Supporting Document** (`Document Title`, `File Upload`): If you have any supporting documents that may be necessary for your leave request, such as a letter from your supervisor or a festival brochure, you can attach them here.
* **Approval Details** (`Approval Status`, `Approval Date`, `Supervisor's Name`): This section is used to indicate the status of your leave request and the date it was approved or rejected. The supervisor's name will be displayed once your request has been approved.
* **Comments** (`Comments`): This field is optional and can be used to add any additional comments or information you would like to include with your request.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to include all required information and attach any necessary supporting documents.
* Submit your form for approval once you have completed it.
* Your supervisor will review and process your request efficiently once you have submitted it.
