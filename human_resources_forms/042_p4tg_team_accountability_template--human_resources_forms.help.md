# P4tg Team Accountability Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "P4tg Team Accountability Template" is a tool to track team members' accountabilities, allowing you to monitor progress, report, and evaluate team performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required information: Employee Name and Job Title.
2. Indicate whether the team member is a 'Team Member' or not.
3. Select relevant departments where the team member reports.
4. Choose a valid date for 'Date' and 'Accountability Date'.
5. For 'Accountable To' and 'Accountable By', select 'Yes' if applicable.
6. Select the status of 'Accountabilities Status' according to your team member's situation (Active or Inactive).
7. Add any additional comments (if necessary).
8. Provide your contact information (Email and Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the team member responsible for the accountability. (Type: Text)
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the team member. (Type: Text)
* **Team Member**: Indicate whether the team member is part of the team ('Yes' or 'No'). (Type: Select One)
* **Department**: Enter the department where the team member reports. (Type: Text)
* **Date**: Enter the date for the accountability (format: DD/MM/YY). (Type: Date)
* **Time**: Enter the time for the accountability (format: HH:MM AM/PM). (Type: Time)
* **Accountability Date**: Enter the date for the accountability (format: DD/MM/YY). (Type: Date)
* **Accountable To**: Indicate if the team member is accountable to someone ('Yes' or 'No'). (Type: Select One)
* **Accountable By**: Indicate if the team member is accountable by someone ('Yes' or 'No'). (Type: Select One)
* **Accountabilities Status**: Select the status of the team member's accountabilities (Active or Inactive). (Type: Select Multiple)
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes. (Type: Note)
* **Email**: Enter the team member's email address. (Type: Email)
* **Phone**: Enter the team member's phone number. (Type: Text)

By following this guide, users should be able to effectively use the "P4tg Team Accountability Template" to track and monitor team member accountabilities.
