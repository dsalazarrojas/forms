# Recruitment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for the recruitment process. It will guide users through the steps to create a complete and accurate requisition form.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the Requisition Type:
    *   Choose the type of employment you are requesting: Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contract.
2.  Enter the Requisition Date:
    *   Enter the date when the job opening was first posted or when you plan to start the recruitment process.
3.  Add a Requisition Description:
    *   Provide a brief description of the job opening, including any essential qualifications or requirements.
4.  Enter Job Title:
    *   Enter the title of the job opening.
5.  Select the Department:
    *   Choose the department that this job opening will be a part of.
6.  Enter Job Location:
    *   Enter the location where the job will be performed.
7.  Select the Job Status:
    *   Choose the status of the job opening: Active or Inactive.
8.  Select Job Type:
    *   Choose the type of job: Full-time, Part-time, or Internship.
9.  Choose Job Level:
    *   Choose the level of the job: Executive, Mid-level, or Entry-level.
10. Enter Salary Range:
    *   (Optional) Enter the salary range for the position.
11. Choose Employment Type:
    *   Choose the type of employment: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Internship.
12. Select Education Level:
    *   Choose the required education level for the position (Bachelor, Master, or PhD).
13. Enter Years of Experience:
    *   (Optional) Enter the number of years of experience required for the position.
14. Select Job Opening:
    *   Choose the time frame for the job opening: Immediate, Within 3 weeks, Within 2 months, or Within 3 months.
15. Enter Contact Person:
    *   Enter the name of the contact person.
16. Enter Contact Email:
    *   Enter the email address of the contact person.
17. Enter Contact Phone:
    *   (Optional) Enter the phone number of the contact person.
18. Enter Job Description:
    *   Provide a detailed description of the job opening.
19. Enter Job Requirements:
    *   List essential requirements for the job opening.
20. Enter Closing Date:
    *   Enter the date to close the job opening.
21. Enter Work Location:
    *   Enter the location where the job will be performed.
22. Select Job Type 2:
    *   Choose the type of job: Office, Home-based, or On-Site.
23. Select Job Type 3:
    *   (Optional) Choose the type of job: Office, Office/Remote, or Office/Remote/On-site.
24. Select Job Type 4:
    *   Choose the type of job: Office/Remote, Office/On-site, or Home-based.
25. Enter Notes:
    *   (Optional) Add any additional notes about the job opening.
