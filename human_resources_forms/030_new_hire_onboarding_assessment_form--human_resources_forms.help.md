# New Hire Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Assessment Form is designed to collect essential information about new hires, including their personal details, job status, and work schedule. This form helps facilitate a smooth onboarding process and ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date information about our new hires.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "New Hire Onboarding Form Data" section, which is the first page of the form.
2. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Select your department from the options provided in the "Department" field.
6. Choose your job status from the options provided in the "Job Status" field:
	* Active
	* Inactive
	* On Leave of Absence
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
8. Enter your hire date in the "Hire Date" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
9. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Check the box if you have a supervisor in the "Supervisor" field:
	* Yes
	* No
12. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name in the "Emergency Contact" field.
13. Select the relationship of your emergency contact from the options provided in the "Emergency Relationship" field:
	* Spouse
	* Partner
	* None
14. Enter the contact information for your emergency contact, including their phone number in the "Emergency Phone" field.
15. Enter the address of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Address" field.
16. Enter your employee number in the "Employee Number" field.
17. Enter the date and time you're expected to work in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
18. Select your location from the options provided (not specified in the form structure, so we'll assume this field is meant to be a text input).
19. Enter your shift start time in the "Shift Start" field.
20. Enter your shift end time in the "Shift End" field.
21. Enter the number of breaks you take during your shift in the "Break Time" field.
22. Enter the length of each break in the "Break Length" field.
23. Enter the total number of hours you work in the "Work Hours" field.
24. Add any additional notes about your onboarding process in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Onboarding Form Data**: This is the first page of the form, which asks for general information about you as a new hire.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title here.
* **Department**: Select your department from the options provided.

	+ Active

* **Hire Date**: Enter your hire date in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Email**: Enter your email address here.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number here.
* **Supervisor**: Check the box if you have a supervisor.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name here.
* **Emergency Relationship**: Select the relationship of your emergency contact from the options provided:
	+ Spouse
	+ Partner
	+ None
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Employee Number**: Enter your employee number here.
* **Date**: Enter the date you're expected to work.
* **Time**: Enter the time you're expected to work.
* **Location**: Enter your work location here (note: this field is not provided in the form structure).
* **Shift Start**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Break Time**: Enter the number of breaks you take during your shift.
* **Break Length**: Enter the length of each break.
* **Work Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you work.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your onboarding process here.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* This form is not meant to be a comprehensive employee database, but rather a brief assessment to gather necessary information for administrative purposes.
* Please review and update your information as needed to ensure accuracy and up-to-date information.
