# Performance Metrics Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Metrics Training Form is a tool used to collect and track employee performance metrics training needs and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief title that describes the training session or assessment.
2. Provide a **Form Description** that outlines the purpose and scope of the training.
3. Select the relevant **Training Needs** from the provided options (Training in the past 6 months, Training in 6-12 months, or Training in 1-2 years).
4. Enter the **Performance Metric 1**, **Performance Metric 2**, and **Performance Metric 3** values, which represent specific metrics to be achieved or improved.
5. Set the **Assessment Date** to the date when the performance was last assessed.
6. Choose the **Form Category** from the provided options (Human Resources Forms or Other Forms).
7. Select the **Training Frequency** from the options (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
8. Choose who the form is assigned to (chatjimmy or Other).
9. Indicate if the form was **Form Submitted By** (Yes or No).
10. Set the **Form Status** to one of the provided options (Approved, In Progress, Declined, or Draft).
11. Set the **Form Status Date** to the date when the form status was last updated.
12. Enter the **Form Version** number.
13. Set the **Form Updated At** and **Form Created At** timestamps to the last update and creation dates, respectively.
14. Set the **Form Modified At**, **Form Completed At**, and **Form Completed By** timestamps to the dates and completion status, respectively.
15. Indicate if the form was **Form Reviewed By** and **Form Reviewed At**, and if it was **Form Reopened By** and **Form Reopened At**.
16. Set the **Form Deleted At** date to when the form was deleted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: A brief title that describes the training session or assessment.
* **Form Description**: A brief description of the purpose and scope of the training.
* **Training Needs**: Select the relevant training needs to indicate the type and duration of training required.
* **Performance Metric 1**: Enter a value representing a specific metric to be achieved or improved.
* **Performance Metric 2**: Enter a value representing another specific metric to be achieved or improved.
* **Performance Metric 3**: Enter a value representing another specific metric to be achieved or improved.
* **Assessment Date**: The date when the performance was last assessed.
* **Form Category**: Choose the category of the form (Human Resources Forms or Other Forms).
* **Training Frequency**: Select the frequency of the training (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Form Assigned To**: Choose who the form is assigned to (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Form Submitted By**: Indicate if the form was submitted by someone (Yes or No).
* **Form Status**: Set the status of the form to one of the provided options (Approved, In Progress, Declined, or Draft).
* **Form Status Date**: The date when the form status was last updated.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version number of the form.
* **Form Updated At**: The timestamp of the last update.
* **Form Created At**: The timestamp of the creation date.
* **Form Modified At**: The timestamp of the last modification.
* **Form Completed At**: The timestamp of the completion date.
* **Form Completed By**: Indicate if the form was completed by someone (Yes or No).
* **Form Reviewed By**: Indicate if the form was reviewed by someone (Yes or No).
* **Form Reviewed At**: The timestamp of the last review.
* **Form Reopened By**: Indicate if the form was reopened by someone (Yes or No).
* **Form Reopened At**: The timestamp of the last re-opening.
* **Form Deleted At**: The timestamp of the deletion date.

## Tips

* Double-check that you have the necessary permissions to access and use this form.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, as indicated by the red asterisk (*).
* If you're unsure about any field or option, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Save your progress regularly to avoid losing data due to form updates or changes.
