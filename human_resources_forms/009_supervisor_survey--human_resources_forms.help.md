# Supervisor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information from supervisors for administrative purposes. Please ensure that you are filling out this form as a supervisor and not as an employee. If you're unsure, please contact your HR department for clarification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

*   Click on the "Submit" button once you have completed all the required fields.
*   Enter your manager's title in the `manager title` field. This is where you can enter your current title in the organization.
*   Enter your job title in the `job title` field.
*   Select your department from the `department` dropdown list. Choose the department you are currently a part of.
*   Select any applicable job functions from the `job function` dropdown list. This will help us understand your role better.
*   Enter your manager's name in the `manager name` field.
*   Enter your manager's department in the `manager department` field.
*   Enter the date of the event or meeting you are attending in the `event date` field.
*   Enter the time of the event or meeting in the `event time` field.
*   Enter the location of the event or meeting in the `location` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **manager_title**: Enter your current title in the organization.
*   **job_title**: Enter your job title in the organization.
*   **department**: Select the department you are currently part of.
*   **job_function**: Select any applicable job functions that you perform.
*   **manager_name**: Enter your manager's name.
*   **manager_department**: Enter your manager's department.
*   **event_date**: Enter the date of the event or meeting you are attending.
*   **event_time**: Enter the time of the event or meeting.
*   **location**: Enter the location of the event or meeting.

Note: This form is for administrative purposes only. Please ensure that you are filling it out correctly and not duplicating any other form. If you're unsure, please contact your HR department for clarification. 

Tips: 

*   Fill out the form clearly and accurately. 
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
