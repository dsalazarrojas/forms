# Expense Allowance Adjustment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to request adjustments to their expense allowance. Employees can use this form to request increases, decreases, or reverts to the original amount of their expense allowance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Requesting An Adjustment** (form_1): Enter a brief description of the reason you are requesting an adjustment to your expense allowance.
2. **Adjustment Type** (form_2): Select the type of adjustment you are requesting, which can be an "Increase", "Decrease", or "Revert" to the original allowance amount.
3. **Adjustment Reason** (form_3): Provide a clear and concise reason for the requested adjustment. This should include details about why the change is necessary and how it relates to your work or expenses.
4. **Adjustment Amount** (form_4): If your request is for an increase, enter the proposed new amount. If your request is for a decrease, enter the proposed new amount. If your request is to revert, enter 0.
5. **Adjusted Allowance** (form_5): Enter the new, adjusted expense allowance amount based on your proposal.
6. **Current Allowance** (form_6): Enter your current, unadjusted expense allowance amount.
7. **Review Comments** (form_7): Enter any additional comments or observations that you think would be helpful for the reviewer.
8. **Reviewer Comments** (form_8): This field is reserved for the reviewer to add comments and feedback for the employee.
9. **Approved** (form_9): Select whether your request is approved or rejected.
10. **Review Date** (form_10): This field is automatically populated by the system and is not required to be filled out by the user. It will display the date the form was submitted or updated.
