# speaker_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The speaker application form is used to collect information about the speaker's details, including their name, email, speaking experience, availability, bio, topics, event type, event location, event dates, and speaker fee. This form is essential for the speaker selection process.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
- Make sure to fill out the form in sections, one page at a time.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, optional): A brief description about yourself.
	* This field is used to identify the speaker and will be used for communication purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
	* This field is used to contact you for further communication and information.
- **Speaker Experience** (`speaker_experience`, text, optional): A brief description of your experience as a speaker.
	* This field is used to evaluate your qualifications and suitability for the event.
- **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): The dates you are available to speak.
	* This field is used to determine your availability and schedule.
- **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`, text, optional): A brief description about yourself and your expertise.
	* This field is used to provide context about the speaker and their qualifications.
- **Speaker Topics** (`speaker_topics`, select_multiple, optional): List the topics you are available to speak about.
	* This field is used to determine the speaker's areas of expertise.
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): The type of event you are interested in speaking at.
	* This field is used to narrow down the speaker selection process.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): The location of the event.
	* This field is used to determine the speaker's availability for a particular location.
- **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, date, optional): The dates of the event.
	* This field is used to ensure the speaker's availability for the event.
- **Speaker Fee** (`speaker_fee`, number, optional): The amount you are willing to receive as a speaker fee.
	* This field is used to determine the speaker's fee expectations.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
- Double-check the form for any errors before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
