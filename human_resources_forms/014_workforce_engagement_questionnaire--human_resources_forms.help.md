# Workforce Engagement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employee engagement within the organization, including demographic data, job satisfaction, and relationships with managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each page carefully and answer all required questions. Some pages may have additional optional questions.
* Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Use the provided options for select fields, and type in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Demographic Data (required)**: Please select your gender identity from the options provided.
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-Binary
	+ Prefer Not To Say
	+ Other (please specify in the text field)
* **How would you rate your relationship with your manager? (required)**: Please select from the options:
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Communication (optional)**: Please provide your thoughts on communication within the organization. This is an open text field for you to share your feelings and suggestions.
* **Work life balance (optional)**: Please provide a numerical value (e.g., 1-10) to indicate how well you balance your work and personal life. 
* **Overall Satisfaction (optional)**: Please provide a numerical value (e.g., 1-10) to indicate your overall satisfaction with your job.
* **Feedback (optional)**: Please provide any additional thoughts, suggestions, or comments you have about your job or work environment. This is an open text field.
* **Job Satisfaction (optional)**: Please provide a numerical value (e.g., 1-10) to indicate your level of satisfaction with your job.
* **Introduction (optional)**: Please review this page to ensure you understand the purpose of the form and the types of questions it contains.
* **Note**: This is a note field. You can type any additional information or comments, but it is not a required field.

## Tips

* Please answer all required questions.
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
