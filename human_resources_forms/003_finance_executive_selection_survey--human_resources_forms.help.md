# Finance Executive Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on potential candidates for finance executive positions in an organization. It helps HR teams or management to evaluate and select the best candidates for these roles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with the details of the potential candidate.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and clear information about the candidate.
3. Select the role of the candidate from the options provided in the "Role" field.
4. You can skip the other text fields if not applicable.
5. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Finance Executive Selection Survey**: This field is a placeholder text field for the finance executive selection survey.
* **Role**: This field is used to select the role of the finance executive from the provided options.
	+ Options:
		- Chief Financial Officer
		- Financial Analyst
		- Financial Manager
* **Finance Executive Selection Survey 2**: This field is another text field for additional information about the finance executive.
* **Finance Executive Selection Survey 3**: This field is similar to Finance Executive Selection Survey 2, for gathering more details about the candidate.
* **Finance Executive Selection Survey 4**: Another text field for more information about the finance executive.
* **Finance Executive Selection Survey 5**: This field is similar to Finance Executive Selection Survey 4, for gathering more details about the candidate.
* **Finance Executive Selection Survey 6**: This is the last text field for any additional comments or information about the finance executive.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and clear information in the text fields.
* If you're unsure about the role of the candidate, select one of the options from the "Role" field.
* You can skip the text fields if not applicable.
* Please fill out the form completely to help the selection process.
