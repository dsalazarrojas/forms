# Q1 Feedback Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, you can simply select the desired option for each field. The form is self-explanatory, and you will be able to easily navigate through the different sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Feedback** (`q1_feedback_form`, Select Multiple, not required):
  Please select 'Yes' if you were satisfied with your experience, or 'No' if you were not. You can also select multiple 'Yes' if you experienced a mix of both 'Yes' and 'No' scenarios.
  
- **Overall Rating** (`q1_feedback_form_client_feedback`, Number, not required):
  Please rate your overall experience out of 5. A higher number indicates a more positive experience.

- **Comments** (`q1_feedback_form_comments`, Note, not required):
  Please provide any additional comments regarding your experience. This is an optional field where you can add any extra information that you feel is relevant to your feedback.

- **Client Email** (`q1_feedback_form_email`, Email, not required):
  Please enter your email address so we can respond to your feedback and keep you updated on any follow-up questions or actions taken.

- **Client Phone** (`q1_feedback_form_phone`, Text, not required):
  Please enter your phone number for us to follow up with you regarding your feedback.

- **Submit** (`q1_feedback_form_submit`, Text, not required):
  Once you're finished with your feedback, click this button to submit your response.
