# employment_follow_up_invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment_follow_up_invitation form is used to document follow-up activities related to employment opportunities or candidate interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Invitation" field and enter a brief description of the follow-up activity.
2. For the "Meeting Date" field, enter the date of the meeting or interaction.
3. In the "Meeting Time" field, select the time of the meeting or interaction.
4. In the "Purpose of Follow Up" field, describe the reason for the follow-up activity.
5. For the "Meeting Attendees" field, select all applicable attendees, such as "Yes" for those who will be attending.
6. Enter the location of the meeting or interaction in the "Meeting Location" field.
7. In the "Availability" field, indicate if the employee or candidate is available for the meeting.
8. Enter a brief message to invite the employee or candidate in the "Invite Message" field.
9. If necessary, enter the URL of a link to the meeting or invitation in the "Invite Link" field.
10. In the "Follow Up Comments" field, enter any additional comments or notes about the meeting or interaction.
11. Select the status of the follow-up activity from the options: "pending", "in progress", or "done".
12. In the "Follow Up Notes" field, enter any additional comments or notes about the follow-up activity.
13. For the "Employee or Candidate" field, enter the name of the employee or candidate being followed up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Invitation** (`title`, `text`, required): A brief description of the follow-up activity.
* **Meeting Date** (`follow_up_meeting_date`, `date`, required): The date of the meeting or interaction.
* **Meeting Time** (`follow_up_meeting_time`, `time`, required): The time of the meeting or interaction.
* **Purpose of Follow Up** (`purpose_of_follow_up`, `text`, required): A description of the reason for the follow-up activity.
* **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable attendees, such as "Yes" for those who will be attending.
* **Meeting Location** (`follow_up_meeting_location`, `text`, required): The location of the meeting or interaction.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if the employee or candidate is available for the meeting.
* **Invite Message** (`invite_message`, `text`, required): A brief message to invite the employee or candidate.
* **Invite Link** (`invite_link`, `text`, required): If necessary, enter the URL of a link to the meeting or invitation.
* **Follow Up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, `text`, required): Additional comments or notes about the meeting or interaction.
* **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, required): The status of the follow-up activity from the options: "pending", "in progress", or "done".
* **Follow Up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, `text`, required): Additional comments or notes about the follow-up activity.
* **Employee or Candidate** (`employee_or_candidate_name`, `text`, required): The name of the employee or candidate being followed up.

Note: All fields are required to be filled up for accurate and comprehensive information.
