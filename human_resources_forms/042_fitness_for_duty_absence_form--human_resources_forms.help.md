This form is used to report an employee's absence from work due to a Fitness For Duty (FFD) reason. A FFD is a situation where an employee is unable to perform their duties due to a specific circumstance that is outside their control, such as a medical emergency or a personal issue. This form is used to document the absence and provide relevant information to management and HR.

When completing this form, please ensure that you have a valid reason for your absence that is related to a FFD. If you are unsure about the purpose of this form or have any questions, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.

To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

# Fitness For Duty Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness For Duty Absence Form is used to report an employee's absence from work due to a Fitness For Duty (FFD) reason.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the employee for whom you are reporting the absence.
2. Enter a valid reason for the absence in the "Reason" field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the absence.
4. If applicable, enter the number of hours absent.
5. Add any additional comments if necessary.
6. Enter the tool used to access this form (e.g. computer, tablet, or mobile device).
7. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (Select One):
  Choose the employee for whom you are reporting the absence.
* **Reason** (Text):
  Enter a valid reason for the absence that falls under a Fitness For Duty category.
  Examples include:
    * Medical emergency
    * Family bereavement
    * Jury duty
* **Absence Start Date** (Date):
  Enter the date the absence started.
* **Absence End Date** (Date):
  Enter the date the absence ended.
* **Absence Hours** (Number):
  If applicable, enter the number of hours absent.
* **Comments** (Text):
  Add any additional comments or details about the absence.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text):
  This field is not required but can be used to indicate which tool was used to access this form.
* **Submitted By** (Text):
  This field is not required but can be used to indicate who submitted the form.
  
## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid reason for the absence that falls under a Fitness For Duty category.
* Ensure that the start and end dates of the absence are accurate.
* If applicable, enter the correct number of hours absent.
* Use this form only for genuine absences due to Fitness For Duty reasons.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are complete.
