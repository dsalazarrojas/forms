# exit_interview_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's exit from the company, ensuring a smooth transition of information and resources. It captures essential details about the employee's departure, including their personal details, authorization, and reasons for leaving.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by completing the employee details section, which includes basic information such as name, job title, and department.
2. In the authorization details section, provide the required approval from the relevant parties, typically HR or management.
3. In the exit interview details section, write a brief description of the reason for the employee's departure.
4. Finally, answer the exit interview questions, which will help identify areas for improvement and provide valuable feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's name, job title, and department in this section.
* **Authorization Details** (`authorization_details`, text, required/optional): Provide the approval from the relevant parties, such as HR or management, in this section.
* **Exit Interview Details** (`exit_interview_details`, text, required/optional): Write a brief description of the reason for the employee's departure.
* **Exit Interview Questions** (`exit_interview_questions`, text, required/optional): Answer the questions provided in this section, which will help identify areas for improvement and provide valuable feedback.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out this form accurately and with the necessary approvals.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to prevent errors or omissions.
- If you're unsure about any field or section, consult with the HR department for guidance.
