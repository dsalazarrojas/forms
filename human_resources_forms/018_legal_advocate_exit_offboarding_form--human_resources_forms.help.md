# Legal Advocate Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the offboarding process of a Legal Advocate, ensuring a smooth transition of responsibilities and maintaining a record of the reasons for departure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee Information section with the employee's details, such as their name and ID number.
2. Provide Manager Information by selecting whether the manager is aware of the offboarding process and if they have provided their contact information.
3. Specify the Reason for Departure, which could be a short explanation for the employee's departure.
4. Enter the Last Day Worked date, which is the date the employee's last day of work.
5. Select the Outplacement Support option that best represents the support the employee is receiving or will receive during the offboarding process.
6. Add any additional Comments that may be relevant to the offboarding process.
7. Enter any relevant Legal Advocate Information that may be helpful for the offboarding process.
8. Finally, sign and date the form with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information (Text)**: This field is used to enter the employee's name and ID number.
* **Manager Information (Select Multiple)**: This field is used to indicate whether the manager is aware of the offboarding process and if they have provided their contact information.
	+ 'Yes': The manager is aware of the offboarding process.
	+ 'No': The manager is not aware of the offboarding process.
* **Reason for Departure (Text)**: This field is used to specify the reason for the employee's departure.
* **Last Day Worked (Date)**: This field is used to enter the date of the employee's last day of work.
* **Outplacement Support (Select One)**: This field is used to select the type of support the employee is receiving or will receive during the offboarding process.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Comments (Text)**: This field is used to add any additional comments relevant to the offboarding process.
* **Legal Advocate Info (Text)**: This field is used to enter any relevant information about the Legal Advocate's departure.
* **Signature (Note)**: This field is used to sign and date the form, indicating completion and approval.
