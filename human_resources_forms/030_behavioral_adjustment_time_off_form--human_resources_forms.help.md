# Behavioral Adjustment Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting time off for Behavioral Adjustments, which may include mental health, stress, wellness, or personal reasons. It is meant to help employees provide necessary information for their manager and HR department to review and approve or deny the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name, Employee ID, and position.
2. Select your department and manager's name.
3. Choose the type of time off you are requesting.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your leave.
5. Specify the total number of days or partial days you are requesting.
6. Provide a brief explanation for your request, if comfortable sharing.
7. Check if this is a recurring request and specify the schedule.
8. Identify who will cover your work and any important handoff notes.
9. Specify how you will be available during your leave.
10. Decide if you need to provide medical documentation.
11. Indicate if this is your first or subsequent request.
12. Finally, confirm and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Enter your full legal name.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `true`
  
- **Employee ID**: Enter your employee identification number.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `true`

- **Department**: Select your department from the provided options.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `true`

- **Position or Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `true`

- **Manager or Supervisor Name**: Enter your direct supervisor's name.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `true`

- **Manager Email**: Enter your supervisor's email address.
  - Type: `email`
  - Required: `true`

- **Request Date**: Enter the current date.
  - Type: `date`
  - Required: `true`

- **Type of Time Off**: Select the type of leave you are requesting.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `true`

- **Start Date**: Enter the date your leave begins.
  - Type: `date`
  - Required: `true`

- **Start Time**: Enter the start time if this is a partial day.
  - Type: `time`
  - Required: `false`

- **End Date**: Enter the date you will return to work.
  - Type: `date`
  - Required: `true`

- **End Time**: Enter the end time if this is a partial day.
  - Type: `time`
  - Required: `false`

- **Total Days Requested**: Enter the number of days or partial days you are requesting.
  - Type: `number`
  - Required: `true`

- **Reason for Request**: Provide a brief explanation for your request, if comfortable sharing.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `false`

- **Is This a Recurring Request**: Check if this is a recurring request and specify the schedule.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `true`

- **Recurrence Schedule**: If this is a recurring request, enter the schedule.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `false`

- **Who Will Handle Your Work**: Identify who will cover your responsibilities.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `false`

- **Handoff Notes**: Provide important information for coverage.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `false`

- **Urgent Tasks During Absence**: Enter any critical items to address during your absence.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `false`

- **Availability During Leave**: Specify how you will be available during your leave.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `true`

- **Documentation Provided**: Decide if you need to provide medical documentation.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `true`

- **Previous Requests This Year**: Indicate how many similar requests you have made recently.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `true`

- **Employee Signature**: Confirm and submit your request.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `true`

- **Manager Approval**: For manager use only, select to approve or deny the request.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `false`

- **Manager Comments**: For manager use only, enter any comments.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `false`

- **HR Approval**: For HR use only, select to approve or deny the request.
  - Type: `select_one`
  - Required: `false`

- **HR Comments**: For HR use only, enter any comments.
  - Type: `text`
  - Required: `false`

- Ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- If you are unsure about any field, reach out to your manager or HR department for guidance.
