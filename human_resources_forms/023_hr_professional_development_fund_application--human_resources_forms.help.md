# HR Professional Development Fund Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides instructions on how to complete the HR Professional Development Fund Application form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the General Info section with your personal details.
2. Enter your Employee ID number in the Employee Info field.
3. Provide a short description of your proposed project in the Education Info field.
4. Enter a detailed description of your project in the Education Details field.
5. Select whether you are requesting funding in the Funding Request field.
6. Enter the total amount of funding you are requesting in the Funding Amount field.
7. Enter your manager's name and department in the Manager Info field.
8. Select the status of your application in the Approval Status field.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
10. Enter your email address in the Email field.
11. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
12. Enter the date and time you are submitting your application in the Date and Time fields.
13. Click the Submit button to submit your application.
14. Click the Cancel button to cancel your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info**: Enter your name, email, and department in this field.
* **Employee Info**: Enter your Employee ID number.
* **Education Info**: Provide a short description of your proposed project.
* **Education Details**: Enter a detailed description of your project.
* **Funding Request**: Select 'Yes' if you are requesting funding, 'No' otherwise.
* **Funding Amount**: Enter the total amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Manager Info**: Enter your manager's name and department.
* **Approval Status**: Select the status of your application (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting your application.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are submitting your application.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your application.
* **Cancel**: Click this button to cancel your application.

Note: Please ensure you have reviewed the company's professional development fund policy before submitting your application.
