# Leaders Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leaders Nomination Form is used to collect information about exceptional team members who have demonstrated outstanding skills, strengths, and qualities that can aid in the growth and improvement of the team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Leader's Name**: Enter the name of the leader you would like to nominate.
2. **Leadership Position**: Enter the leader's current leadership position within the organization.
3. **Leader's Expertise**: Describe the leader's expertise and qualifications.
4. **Nomination Justification**: Provide a detailed justification for the nomination.
5. **Leader's Contact Info**: Enter the leader's contact information.
6. **Nomination Reasons**: List the reasons why the leader is being nominated.
7. **Form Submission Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
8. **Form Submission Time**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
9. **Leader's Strengths**: (Optional) List the leader's strengths and qualifications.
10. **Leader's Areas of Improvement**: (Optional) Describe areas where the leader needs improvement.
11. **Nomination Review**: Evaluate the nomination status as Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
12. **Form Submission**: Evaluate the form submission status as Submitted, Rejected, or Pending.
13. **Nomination Type**: Select one of the available types (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3, Type 4).
14. **Nomination Status**: Select one of the available statuses (Active, Inactive, Pending).
15. **Leader's Bio**: Provide a brief description of the leader's background and experience.
16. **Leader's Nomination Form**: Indicate whether the leader has been nominated before.
17. **Leader's Leadership Position**: (Optional) Enter the leader's leadership position.
18. **Leader's Expertise Area**: (Optional) Describe the leader's expertise area.
19. **Form Submission Review**: Evaluate the form submission status as Reviewed, Unreviewed, or Rejected.
20. **Form Submission Type**: Select one of the available types (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3, Type 4).
21. **Leader's Contact Info**: Enter the leader's contact information.
22. **Form Submission Page 1**: (Optional) Enter any additional information related to the form submission.
23. **Leader's Organizational Position**: (Optional) Enter the leader's organizational position.
24. **Form Submission Page 2**: (Optional) Indicate whether you have any additional information to add.
25. **Leaders Nominated**: Confirm that the leader has been nominated.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the leader being nominated is not currently being nominated for the same award or recognition.
* Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Review the form thoroughly before submission to avoid any errors or omissions.
* Use the provided space to describe the leader's expertise and areas of improvement.
* Select the correct nomination status and type.
* Enter the leader's contact information accurately.
