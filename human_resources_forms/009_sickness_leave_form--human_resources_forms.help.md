# Sickness Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request sickness leave for an employee. It is designed to collect information about an employee's absence due to sickness, including the start and end dates of the leave, number of days absent, reason for absence, and other relevant details. The form is used by HR to track and manage employee absences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start and end dates of your absence due to sickness in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields.
2. Indicate the number of days you will be absent by entering a number in the "days_absent" field.
3. Provide a reason for your absence in the "reason" field.
4. Enter any additional comments or information about your absence in the "comments" field.
5. Select whether or not your supervisor has approved your leave.
6. Choose whether or not you are being assigned to a different person while on leave.
7. Select whether or not your leave has been approved by HR.
8. Select the status of your employee sickness benefits, which can be either Active or Inactive.
9. Indicate whether or not you have used any leave credits for this absence.
10. Enter your current total leave credits and your remaining leave credits.
11. Select the status of your leave balance, which can be either Active or Inactive.
12. Select whether or not your payroll status is Active or Inactive.
13. Finally, select whether or not a payroll action has been taken due to your absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sickness_leave_form**: The main form title.
* **start_date**: The date you will start your absence due to sickness. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **end_date**: The date you will end your absence due to sickness. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **days_absent**: The number of days you will be absent due to sickness.
* **reason**: The reason for your absence due to sickness.
* **comments**: Any additional information or comments about your absence.
* **supervisor_approval**: Whether or not your supervisor has approved your leave. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **assigned_to**: Whether or not you are being assigned to a different person while on leave. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **approved_by**: Whether or not your leave has been approved by HR. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **approved_by_user**: A list of users who have approved your leave. Select "Yes" or "No" for each user.
* **sickness_status**: The status of your employee sickness benefits, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **leave_credits_used**: Whether or not you have used any leave credits for this absence. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **remaining_leave_credits**: Your current remaining leave credits.
* **total_leave_credits**: Your total leave credits available.
* **leave_balance**: Your current leave balance.
* **leave_balance_status**: The status of your leave balance, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **payroll_status**: Your payroll status, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **payroll_action**: Whether or not a payroll action has been taken due to your absence. Select "Yes" or "No".
