# Trade Skill Enhancement Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage requests for a Trade Skill Enhancement Leave of Absence (TSEL) in the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review the company's leave policy before submitting the form.
3. Discuss your request with your manager and HR representative before submitting the form.
4. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and approvals before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade Skill Enhancement Leave Of Absence Form** (`form_header`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Please enter the email of your manager.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional): Please enter your employee ID.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Please enter your job title.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the type of leave you are requesting (Trade Skill Enhancement Leave of Absence or Other).
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the start date of your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the end date of your leave.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the return date after your leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required/optional): Please enter the reason for your leave.
* **Manager Comments** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_manager_comments`, text, required/optional): Please enter any comments from your manager.
* **Approver Comments** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_approver_comments`, text, required/optional): Please enter any comments from the approver.
* **Employee Comments** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_employees_comments`, text, required/optional): Please enter any comments from you.
* **Approver** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_approver`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the approver (Manager, HR, or Other).
* **Reviewer** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_reviewer`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the reviewer (Manager, HR, or Other).
* **Form Status** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_form_status`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the form status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Form Submission Status** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_form_submission_status`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the form submission status (Draft, Published, or Archived).
* **Form Submission Date** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_submit_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date of submission.
* **Form Submission Time** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_submit_time`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time of submission.
* **Approver Name** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_approver_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the approver.
* **Reviewer Name** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_reviewer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Employee Name** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_employee_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the employee.
* **Approver Email** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_approver_email`, email, required/optional): Please enter the email of the approver.
* **Reviewer Email** (`trade_skill_enhancement_leave_form_reviewer_email`, email, required/optional): Please enter the email of the reviewer.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed the company's leave policy before submitting the form.
* Please discuss your request with your manager and HR representative before submitting the form.
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and approvals before submitting the form.
