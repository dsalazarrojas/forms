# Employment Verification Affidavit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Verification Affidavit Form is designed to verify an employee's employment details, including contact information, rental and loan history, background information, and employer details. This form helps ensure accuracy and consistency in employment verification processes, ensuring that employees' personal and professional records are up-to-date and secure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the employee's details, including any available contact information (email, phone number, or address).
2. Select the relevant employer from the options provided.
3. Provide verification information, including the date of the verification.
4. Sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name, job title, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, required): Choose the employee's contact method(s) for follow-up (Email, Phone, or Address).
* **Rental Info** (`rental_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant rental history or property ownership information.
* **Loan Info** (`loan_info`, text, optional): Enter any loan or credit history relevant to the employee.
* **Background Info** (`background_info`, text, optional): Disclose any relevant background information, such as work experience or education.
* **Employer Details** (`employer_details`, select_one, required): Select the company that the employee works for from the provided options (Company A, Company B, Company C).
* **Verification Info** (`verification_info`, date, required): Enter the date of the verification.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Tips
* Double-check the employee's details for accuracy.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Use the correct date for verification.
* Sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
