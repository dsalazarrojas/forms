# leave_time_off_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "leave_time_off_request_form" is used by employees to submit a leave request. It is essential that this form is filled out accurately to ensure that their leave request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the employee information fields.
2. Choose the type of leave requested (e.g., Vacation, Sick Leave, etc.).
3. Enter the start and end dates of the leave period.
4. Specify the duration of the leave (number of days).
5. Enter the reason for the leave.
6. Select the manager's approval.
7. Add any comments for the manager to review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Employee Information** (Employee Details)
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, optional): Enter your employee ID or name to be identified as the leave requester.

### **Leave Type** (Leave Type)
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Vacation, Sick Leave, etc.).

### **Start Date** (Start Date)
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you plan to start your leave.

### **End Date** (End Date)
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you plan to end your leave.

### **Duration** (Duration)
* **Duration** (`duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you are requesting for leave.

### **Reason** (Reason)
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your reason for the leave.

### **Manager Approval** (Manager Approval)
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_one`, required): Select your manager's approval for the leave.

### **Manager Comments** (Manager Comments)
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments for your manager to review.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
