# Marketing Intern Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document the details of the interview process with marketing candidates. It ensures that all necessary information is captured consistently, eliminating confusion and miscommunication between team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the candidate's details such as address, email, phone number, work experience, and qualifications.
2. Select the candidate's availability (available, not available, or flexible).
3. Choose the preferred contact method (phone, email, or in person).
4. Select the candidate's status (interview scheduled, completed, not scheduled, or not completed).
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the candidate.
6. Provide the candidate's resume and additional comments about the candidate.
7. Answer the interview questions and provide the candidate's comments.
8. Record the interview date, time, location, and type.
9. Specify the interviewers involved.
10. Mark the review as completed and provide any review comments.
11. Determine the final status of the candidate (interviewed, not interviewed, or not applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Candidate Details (required: false)
* **Candidate Details**: Fill in the candidate's name, address, email, and phone number.
### 2. Address (required: true)
* **Address**: Enter the candidate's home address.
### 3. Email (required: true)
* **Email**: Enter the candidate's email address.
### 4. Phone Number (required: true)
* **Phone Number**: Enter the candidate's phone number.
### 5. Work Experience (required: true)
* **Work Experience**: Describe the candidate's work experience in a brief paragraph.
### 6. Qualifications (required: true)
* **Qualifications**: List the candidate's relevant qualifications and skills.
### 7. Skills (required: true)
* **Skills**: List the candidate's skills that align with the job requirements.
### 8. Available Start Date (required: true)
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date the candidate is available to start work.
### 9. End Date (required: true)
* **End Date**: Enter the date the candidate expects to end their internship.
### 10. Availability (required: false)
* **Availability**: Select whether the candidate is available, not available, or flexible.
### 11. Preferred Contact Method (required: false)
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose the method the candidate prefers to be contacted (phone, email, or in person).
### 12. Candidate Status (required: false)
* **Candidate Status**: Select whether the candidate's interview is scheduled, completed, not scheduled, or not completed.
### 13. Notes (required: true)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments about the candidate.
### 14. References (required: true)
* **References**: Enter the candidate's previous supervisors or references.
### 15. Additional Comments (required: true)
* **Additional Comments**: Add any further comments about the candidate.
### 16. Resume (required: true)
* **Resume**: Upload the candidate's resume.
### 17. Interview Questions (required: true)
* **Interview Questions**: Answer the interview questions in a brief paragraph.
### 18. Candidate Comments (required: true)
* **Candidate Comments**: The candidate's comments about the interview process.
### 19. Reviewer Comments (required: true)
* **Reviewer Comments**: Add any comments from the reviewer about the candidate.
### 20. Interview Date (required: true)
* **Interview Date**: Enter the date of the interview.
### 21. Interview Time (required: true)
* **Interview Time**: Enter the time of the interview.
### 22. Interview Location (required: true)
* **Interview Location**: Enter the location of the interview.
### 23. Interviewers (required: false)
* **Interviewers**: Choose the interviewers involved in the interview process.
### 24. Review Completed (required: false)
* **Review Completed**: Mark whether the review is completed or not.
### 25. Review Comments (required: true)
* **Review Comments**: Add any comments about the review process.
### 26. Final Status (required: false)
* **Final Status**: Determine the final status of the candidate (interviewed, not interviewed, or not applicable).
### 27. Interview Type (required: false)
* **Interview Type**: Select the type of interview (phone, in-person, or video).

Tips:
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Use the same format for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the HR department for clarification.
* The data captured in this form will be used for reference and evaluation purposes.
* Please be objective and fair when reviewing and commenting on the candidate.
