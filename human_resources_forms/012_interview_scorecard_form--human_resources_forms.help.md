# Interview Scorecard Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Scorecard Form is used to record and store data regarding an interview conducted for a candidate. This form is designed to provide a comprehensive and fair evaluation of the candidate's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Candidate Details** section with the candidate's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
2.  In the **Evaluation Details** section, provide a brief description of the interview process and any notable events or issues that arose.
3.  In the **Skills Assessment** section, choose the relevant option that best describes the candidate's skills or performance during the interview.
4.  If there are any additional comments or observations about the candidate, you can add them in the **Additional Comments** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, required): This section collects basic information about the candidate, such as their name, date of birth, and any other relevant data.
- **Evaluation Details** (`evaluation_details`, text, required): This section is for providing an account of the interview process and any notable events or issues that arose during the evaluation.
- **Skills Assessment** (`skills_assessment`, select_one, required): This field asks you to choose an option that best describes the candidate's skills or performance during the interview.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any further comments or observations about the candidate, you can add them here. 

## Tips
- Fill out this form for each candidate's interview separately to ensure accurate and consistent data.
- Be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers, especially for the **Evaluation Details** and **Additional Comments** sections.
- If you're unsure about any option in the **Skills Assessment** section, you can choose the option that best fits the candidate's skills or performance.
