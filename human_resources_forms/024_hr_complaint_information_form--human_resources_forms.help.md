# HR Complaint Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Complaint Information Form is used to collect and document employee complaints, allowing the HR department to understand and address concerns in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible, using the fields provided.
2. Ensure that all required fields (marked as `required`) are completed.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please contact your HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint information**: A space to describe the issue or problem that you are experiencing. Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Complaint type**: Select the type of complaint you are making. Choose from the options:
	+ HR Policy
	+ Work Environment
	+ Other
* **Description of complaint**: Enter a detailed description of the issue, including relevant dates, locations, and any other relevant details.
* **Location of complaint**: Select all relevant locations where the issue occurred. Choose from the options:
	+ Office
	+ Remote
* **Employee name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager name**: Enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Department**: Select all relevant departments involved. Choose from the options:
	+ HR
	+ Management
* **Date**: Enter the date the issue occurred or started to occur.
