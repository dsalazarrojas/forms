# Logistics Stress Relief Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logistics Stress Relief Leave Of Absence Form is a confidential process for requesting time off due to stress-related reasons that impact your work performance. Please fill this form to submit your request for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee ID and start date.
2. Specify the duration of your leave (if known).
3. Select the reason for your leave (e.g., "Stress-related reasons").
4. Choose the approval manager who will review your request.
5. Provide any additional notes about your leave (if necessary).
6. Enter your contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Logistics Stress Relief Leave Request**: This field is a text box where you can briefly describe your stress relief leave request.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID to identify yourself in the system.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your leave (if known).
* **Approval Status**: Select the status of your leave request (Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled). 
This is a mandatory field and indicates the current approval state of your request.
* **Reason For Leave**: Enter the reason for your stress relief leave request.
* **Approval Manager**: Choose the manager who will be reviewing your request.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your leave.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your leave.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your leave.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of your chosen approval manager.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have discussed and agreed on this request with your manager before submitting this form.
- If you're unsure about any information, contact your HR department for clarification.
- Your manager will receive an email notification once you submit this form.
- If you need to make changes to your request, please update the form and re-submit it.
