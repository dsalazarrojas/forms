# Employee Time Punch Correction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Time Punch Correction Form is used to correct inaccuracies or mistakes made in an employee's time punches. This form is meant to be filled out by employees to report discrepancies in their time records and provide a reason for the correction. This form should be used only for genuine corrections, not for changes to regular work hours or unauthorized leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Make sure you are the employee who made the original time punch.
*   Select the date of the time punch you would like to correct.
*   Enter the correct time for the punch you are trying to correct.
*   Indicate if your supervisor approved the correction by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
*   Enter a brief reason for the correction, if necessary.
*   Add any comments from your manager regarding the correction, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **date** (Required): Enter the date of the time punch you would like to correct. This field is a date picker that allows you to select a date in the format mm/dd/yy.
*   **time** (Required): Enter the correct time for the punch you are trying to correct.
*   **supervisor** (Optional): This field indicates if your supervisor has approved the correction. If they have, select "Yes" from the dropdown list; if not, select "No".
*   **reason** (Optional): Enter a brief explanation for the correction. This could be due to an error on your part, a miscalculation, or other reasons.
*   **manager_comments** (Optional): Add any comments from your manager regarding the correction, if applicable.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
*   Only use this form for genuine corrections, not for unauthorized changes to your work hours.
*   If you're unsure about what to enter in a field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
*   Submit the form once you have completed it and reviewed it for accuracy.
*   Note that corrections are subject to approval from your supervisor and HR.
