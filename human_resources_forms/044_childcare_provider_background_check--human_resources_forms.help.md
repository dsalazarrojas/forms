# Childcare Provider Background Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from childcare providers to conduct a thorough background check. The information collected will be used to verify the applicant's identity, employment history, and any potential criminal history. This form is a mandatory requirement for all childcare providers applying for a position at our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details, including your application date, name, email address, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code.
2.  Provide your date of birth, place of birth, social security number, and driver's license number and state.
3.  Select your position applied for and provide details about your previous childcare experience.
4.  If you have previous addresses, please provide them and specify how long you have lived at your current address.
5.  List any relevant certifications you hold.
6.  Indicate your current certification status for CPR and First Aid.
7.  Answer questions about your criminal history and child abuse registry check.
8.  Provide contact information for two references.
9.  Consent to a background check.
10.  Sign and date the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Date**: Date of background check application.
* **Applicant First Name**: Legal first name of applicant.
* **Applicant Last Name**: Legal last name of applicant.
* **Applicant Suffix**: Suffix (Jr., Sr., III, etc.).
* **Applicant Email**: Primary email address.
* **Applicant Phone**: Primary phone number.
* **Current Address**: Full street address.
* **City**: City of residence.
* **State**: State or province.
* **Zip Code**: Postal or zip code.
* **Years at Address**: How long have you lived at this address?
*   Please select the number of years you have lived at your current address.
*   For example, "Less than 1 year" or "More than 10 years".
* **Previous Address**: Previous address if at current address less than 5 years.
*   If you have not lived at your current address for more than 5 years, please provide your previous address.
*   This is optional, but please be honest about your previous addresses.
* **Date of Birth**: Please provide your birth date.
* **Place of Birth**: City and state or country of birth.
* **Social Security Number**: Your social security number.
* **Driver License Number**: Your driver license number.
*   Please note that you must have a valid driver's license to work as a childcare provider.
* **Driver License State**: State that issued your license.
*   Please ensure that you have a valid driver's license from a state that is accepted by our institution.
* **Position Applied For**: What position are you applying for?
*   Select one of the following options:
*     Lead teacher
*     Assistant teacher
*     Teacher aide
*     Daycare provider
*     Program director
*     Administrative staff
*     Substitute
*     Other
* **Previous Childcare Experience**: Do you have previous childcare experience?
*   Yes, 5+ years
*   Yes, 3 to 5 years
*   Yes, 1 to 3 years
*   Yes, less than 1 year
*   'False'
*   If you select "False", please proceed to the next field.
* **Experience Details**: Describe your previous childcare experience.
*   This is an optional field, but please provide details about your experience.
*   If you have any certifications or qualifications, please list them here.
* **Certifications Held**: List any relevant certifications.
*   This is an optional field, but please list any relevant certifications you hold.
*   If you have any certifications or qualifications, please list them here.
* **CPR and First Aid Certified**: Are you CPR and First Aid certified?
*   Select one of the following options:
*     Yes, current certification
*     Yes, but expired
*     No, but willing to obtain
*     'False'
*     If you are not certified, please indicate that you are willing to obtain certification.
* **Criminal History**: Have you ever been convicted of a crime?
*   Select one of the following options:
*     'True'
*     'False'
*   If you select "True", please provide details in the next field.
* **Criminal History Details**: If you have a criminal history, please provide details.
*   This is an optional field, but please provide details about your criminal history.
*   If you have any questions, please ask your reference or HR representative.
*   Please be honest about your criminal history.
* **Child Abuse Registry Check**: Have you ever been listed on a child abuse registry?
*   Select one of the following options:
*     'True'
*     'False'
*   If you select "True", please provide details in the next field.
* **Child Abuse Registry Check Details**: If you have a child abuse registry check, please provide details.
*   This is an optional field, but please provide details about your child abuse registry check.
*   If you have any questions, please ask your reference or HR representative.
*   Please be honest about your child abuse registry check.
* **Reference 1 Name**: Full name of first reference.
*   Please provide the full name of your first reference.
*   This is a required field.
* **Reference 1 Relationship**: Relationship to you.
*   Please provide the relationship to the first reference.
*   For example, "Mother" or "Colleague".
*   This is a required field.
* **Reference 1 Phone**: Phone number for first reference.
*   Please provide the phone number for your first reference.
*   This is a required field.
*   If your reference is not available to take calls, please ask them to leave a voice mail.
*   If you need to change your phone number, please let us know.
*   We will only call your reference to confirm the information provided.
*   If you need to change your phone number, please let us know.
*   Please be honest about your reference's availability.
* **Reference 1 Email**: Email for first reference.
*   Please provide the email for your first reference.
*   This is a required field.
*   If your reference is not available to receive emails, please ask them to create a new email address.
*   We will only email your reference to confirm the information provided.
*   If you need to change your email, please let us know.
*   Please be honest about your reference's email availability.
* **Reference 2 Name**: Full name of second reference.
*   Please provide the full name of your second reference.
*   This is a required field.
*   If you have not worked with your second reference before, please explain how you know them.
*   We will only contact your second reference to confirm the information provided.
*   If you need to change your second reference, please let us know.
*   Please be honest about your second reference's relationship to you.
*   Please provide the phone number for your second reference.
*   This is a required field.
*   If your second reference is not available to take calls, please ask them to leave a voice mail.
*   If you need to change your phone number, please let us know.
*   We will only call your second reference to confirm the information provided.
*   If you need to change your phone number, please let us know.
*   Please be honest about your second reference's availability.
*   Please provide the email for your second reference.
*   This is a required field.
*   If your second reference is not available to receive emails, please ask them to create a new email address.
*   We will only email your second reference to confirm the information provided.
*   If you need to change your email, please let us know.
*   Please be honest about your second reference's email availability.
* **Consent for Background Check**: Do you consent to a background check?
*   Select one of the following options:
*     Yes, I consent
*     No, I do not consent
*   Please select "Yes, I consent" if you have no objections to a background check.
*   Please select "No, I do not consent" if you have any concerns or objections.
*   Please be honest about your consent.
* **Applicant Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.
*   Please type your full name as it appears on your ID.
*   This is a required field.
*   Please be honest about your signature.
*   **Signature Date**: Date of electronic signature.
*   Please provide the date of your electronic signature.
*   This is a required field.
*   Please be honest about your signature date.

## Tips

*   Please fill in all required fields carefully and accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your reference or HR representative.
*   Please be honest about your personal and professional history.
*   Please ensure that you have valid driver's license and social security number.
*   Please ensure that you have a valid email address and phone number for your references.
*   Please be honest about your criminal history.
*   Please be honest about your child abuse registry check.
*   Please sign and date the form with your full name.
*   Please print the form and sign it in front of a notary if you prefer to sign in person.
*   Please be aware that we will only contact your references to confirm the information provided.
*   We will only call or email your references with your consent.
