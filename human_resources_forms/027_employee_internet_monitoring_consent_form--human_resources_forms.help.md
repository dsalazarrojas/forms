The form is used to obtain consent from employees to monitor their internet usage at work. This is a one-page form with three fields: employee internet monitoring consent, category, and description. Monitoring internet usage is meant to help the organization ensure that employees are not accessing non-work related sites during work hours. The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from employees so that the organization can legally monitor their internet activity, if they choose to do so. This form does not require employee consent, but it's a requirement for the organization to monitor employee internet activity.

# employee_internet_monitoring_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee internet monitoring consent form is used to obtain consent from employees to monitor their internet usage at work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the purpose of the form and make sure you understand what it's for.
2.  Check the boxes for categories of internet usage you are willing to allow to be monitored.
3.  In the description field, explain the categories you've chosen and any other relevant details about your internet usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_internet_monitoring_consent** (`employee_internet_monitoring_consent`, note, false): This field is a general statement where you can write down your consent to be monitored for internet usage at work.
* **category** (`category`, select_multiple, false): Select all categories of internet usage you're willing to allow to be monitored. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Yes (allow monitoring)
	+ No (do not allow monitoring)
* **description** (`description`, text, false): In this field, please explain the categories you've chosen and any other relevant details about your internet usage.
