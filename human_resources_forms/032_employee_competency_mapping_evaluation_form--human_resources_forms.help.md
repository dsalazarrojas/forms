# Employee Competency Mapping Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate an employee's performance, skills, and competencies, and to provide a framework for their development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information in each field to provide a comprehensive picture of your performance.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
3. If you are unsure about any information, contact your manager for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employee Name (1)**: The name of the employee being evaluated. Please enter the full name as it appears in personnel records.
  * This field is optional.
- **Job Title (2)**: The title of the employee's job or position.
  * This field is optional.
- **Department (3)**: The department to which the employee belongs. Please select from the provided options.
  * Options: Human Resources, Marketing, Sales, Other.
  * This field is optional.
- **Reporting Manager (4)**: The name of the employee's reporting manager.
  * This field is optional.
- **Job Function (5)**: A brief description of the employee's job function or role.
  * This field is optional.
- **Evaluation Date (6)**: The date of the evaluation.
  * This field is optional.
- **Competency Area (7)**: The area of competency the employee is being evaluated on. 
  * This field is optional.
- **Evaluation Method (8)**: A brief description of the evaluation method used. 
  * This field is optional.
- **Evaluation Frequency (9)**: The frequency at which the evaluation is performed. Please select from the provided options.
  * Options: Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, Other.
  * This field is optional.
- **Evaluation Comments (10)**: Any additional comments from the evaluator on the evaluation process.
  * This field is optional.
- **Employee Status (11)**: The current status of the employee. Please select from the provided options.
  * Options: Active, Inactive, On Leave, Other.
  * This field is optional.
- **Job Performance Rating (12)**: A numerical rating of the employee's job performance.
  * This field is optional.
- **Overall Risk Level (13)**: A level of risk associated with the employee's performance.
  * Options: Low, Medium, High.
  * This field is optional.
- **Competency Level (14)**: The level of competency the employee has demonstrated. Please select from the provided options.
  * Options: Basic, Proficient, Advanced, Mastery.
  * This field is optional.
- **Employee Awareness (15)**: The level of awareness the employee has about their performance.
  * Options: High, Medium, Low.
  * This field is optional.
- **Training Recommendation (16)**: Recommendations for training or development the employee needs.
  * Options: Required, Nice to Have, Not Required.
  * This field is optional.
- **Evaluation Rating (17)**: A numerical rating of the employee's performance.
  * This field is optional.
- **Overall Satisfaction (18)**: A numerical rating of the employee's overall satisfaction.
  * This field is optional.
- **Employee Comments (19)**: Any additional comments from the employee.
  * This field is optional.
- **Manager Comments (20)**: Additional comments from the manager.
  * This field is optional.
- **Training Status (21)**: The status of the employee's training or development plans.
  * Options: Active, Complete, Pending.
  * This field is optional.
