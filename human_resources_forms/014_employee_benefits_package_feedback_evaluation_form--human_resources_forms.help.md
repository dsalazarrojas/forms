# Employee Benefits Package Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees on their current benefits package. This feedback will help the organization to make informed decisions about the benefits offered to its employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the department you belong to from the "Department" dropdown list.
3. Choose which benefits you are currently using or have used from the "Benefits Offered" dropdown list. You can select multiple benefits.
4. Enter your comments and feedback in the "Comments" field, if you have any.
5. Finally, select a date for your "Overall Experience" field, which should reflect your overall satisfaction with the current benefits package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as an employee of the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to from the list. This will help us to better understand the benefits needs and preferences of different departments.
* **Benefits Offered** (`benefits_offered`, select_multiple, required): Choose the benefits you are currently using or have used from the list. You can select multiple benefits. This will give us a clear idea of which benefits are being used and which ones are not.
* **Package Overview** (`section_one`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your current benefits package. This will help us to understand your overall experience and satisfaction with the package.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the benefits package.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, date, required): Select a date that reflects your overall satisfaction with the current benefits package. Please note that this field is a date field and should be formatted as a date (e.g., 2022-01-01). 

## Tips
Please be as detailed and honest as possible when providing feedback. Your input is invaluable to us and will help us to improve the benefits package for all employees. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
