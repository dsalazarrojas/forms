# Cash Disbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Disbursement Form is used to document and track disbursements made to individuals or organizations. It ensures that all disbursements are properly recorded and verified in accordance with company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin the disbursement form.
2. Fill out the required fields:
   - **Disbursement Date**: Enter the date of the disbursement in MM/DD/YYYY format.
   - **Form Number**: Enter a unique reference number for this disbursement.
   - **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization receiving the disbursement.
   - **Organization Address**: Enter the address of the organization receiving the disbursement.
   - **Payee Name**: Enter the name of the individual or entity receiving the payment.
   - **Payee Type**: Choose the type of entity receiving the payment, such as Employee, Vendor, or Organization.
   - **Payee Address**: Enter the address of the entity receiving the payment.
   - **Payee Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the entity receiving the payment (optional).
   - **Tax ID or SSN**: Enter the tax ID or SSN of the entity receiving the payment (optional).
   - **Amount in Words**: Spell out the payment amount.
   - **Amount in Numbers**: Enter the dollar amount of the disbursement.
   - **Method of Payment**: Choose the payment method, such as Check, Cash, or Credit Card.
   - **Check Number (if applicable)**: Enter the check number (if a check was used).
   - **Purpose of Disbursement**: Choose the purpose of the disbursement, such as Employee Salary or Wages.
   - **Detailed Description**: Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for the payment.
   - **Account Code or Cost Center**: Enter the account code or cost center associated with the disbursement.
   - **Approved By**: Enter the name and signature of the person approving the disbursement.
   - **Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disbursement Date**: The date of the disbursement.
* **Form Number**: A unique reference number for this disbursement.
* **Organization Name**: The name of the organization receiving the payment.
* **Organization Address**: The address of the organization receiving the payment.
* **Payee Name**: The name of the individual or entity receiving the payment.
* **Payee Type**: The type of entity receiving the payment, such as Employee, Vendor, or Organization.
* **Payee Address**: The address of the entity receiving the payment.
* **Payee Phone Number**: The phone number of the entity receiving the payment (optional).
* **Tax ID or SSN**: The tax ID or SSN of the entity receiving the payment (optional).
* **Amount in Words**: Spell out the payment amount.
* **Amount in Numbers**: The dollar amount of the disbursement.
* **Method of Payment**: The method used to make the payment, such as Check, Cash, or Credit Card.
* **Check Number (if applicable)**: The check number (if a check was used).
* **Purpose of Disbursement**: The reason for the payment, such as Employee Salary or Wages.
* **Detailed Description**: A detailed explanation of the reason for the payment.
* **Account Code or Cost Center**: The account code or cost center associated with the disbursement.
* **Approved By**: The name and signature of the person approving the disbursement.
* **Approval Date**: The date of approval in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure of any field or have questions, please contact the accounting department.
* This form is intended for authorized personnel only; please do not use for personal or internal purposes.
