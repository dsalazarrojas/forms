# Company Learning And Development Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Learning And Development Audit Form is a tool used to gather feedback from employees on the effectiveness of our company's Learning and Development programs. This form helps us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our L&D strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name and department to help us associate your feedback with your role and team.
2. List the training programs you have recently attended, including dates.
3. Rate the effectiveness of our training programs, selecting one of the following options:
	* Very effective
	* Effective
	* Neutral
	* Ineffective
4. Identify any skills gaps you have observed within your department or the company.
5. Provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvement to our L&D programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name, or remain anonymous if you prefer.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Select your department to help us associate your feedback with your team.
* **Training programs attended** (`training_programs_attended`, text, optional): List recent training programs you have attended, including dates.
* **Training effectiveness** (`training_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of our training programs. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very effective
	+ Effective
	+ Neutral
	+ Ineffective
* **Skills gap** (`skills_gap`, text, optional): Identify any skills gaps you have observed within your department or the company.
* **Recommendations for improvement** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvement to our L&D programs.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns about our L&D programs, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation in this audit.
