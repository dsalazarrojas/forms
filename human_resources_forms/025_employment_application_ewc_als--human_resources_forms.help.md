# Employment Application EWC/ALS - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for employment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your main information (name, title, contact details).
2. Select 'Yes' if you have work experience, otherwise 'No'.
3. Provide your education background.
4. Upload or type out your resume.
5. Select 'Yes' if you have any job references, otherwise 'No'.
6. Provide details for any job references you select.
7. Select relevant job skills.
8. Indicate your current employment status.
9. Provide any job history relevant to the role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information** (`title`, `name`, `contactInformation`, `phone`, `email`): Provide personal details to contact you.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`): If you have any relevant work experience, select 'Yes' and provide details in the `Work History` field.
* **Education** (`education`): Provide information about your education background.
* **Resume** (`resume`): Upload or type out your resume.
* **References** (`references`): If you have any job references, provide their name and contact information.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`): Select relevant job skills you possess.
* **Job History** (`work_history`): Provide details about your past work experiences.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Indicate your current employment status.
* **Job History** (`job_history`): Provide any job history relevant to the role.
* **Contact Information** (`contactInformation`): Provide personal contact details.
* **Job References** (`job_references`): If you have any job references, provide their name and contact information.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth and efficient hiring process.
- Make sure to attach a clear and concise resume that includes relevant work experience, education, and skills.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the hiring team.
