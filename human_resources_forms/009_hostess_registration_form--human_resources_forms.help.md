# Hostess Registration Form - Help Guide
 
## Purpose
The Hostess Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who plan to host events. This form is used to gather essential details such as contact, address, and employment information to help us process event hosting requests efficiently. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately. 
  - Ensure all fields are filled out even if not required. 
  - Use the provided options for select fields such as job title and job type. 
  - Double-check your email for correct format before submitting. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and other relevant contact details so we can reach you for any updates or questions.
  - Format should be in free text, e.g. "John Doe, (123) 456-7890"
  - Ensure accuracy in this field as it will be used for future communications.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide your full address, including any applicable suite or unit numbers, to ensure accurate mail delivery and event coordination.
  - Format should be in free text, e.g. "123 Main St, Apt 101, New York, NY 10001"
  - Enter a valid address to ensure proper delivery of event-related materials.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in a standard format, e.g. "(123) 456-7890".
  - Please be available for future calls or messages regarding the event.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your work email address for professional communication.
  - Format should be in standard email format, e.g. "johndoe@example.com"
  - Use your work email to ensure professional updates on event hosting status.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided job title options to categorize your role within the company.
  - This will help us better understand your position and provide more targeted support.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable job types for your role within the company.
  - This will also aid in providing more precise event hosting support and resources.
 
## Tips
- Double-check your information before submission to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- If you have any issues or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will review and process your submission within the next 24-48 hours.
 
Note: Please ensure to use the provided format for contact information fields for accurate and efficient processing.
