# long_term_disability_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Long Term Disability Leave Form is used to document an employee's need for long-term disability leave. This form is used to provide necessary information for the company to process the leave request and ensure compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. **Enter your Job Title**: Provide your current job title within the company.
3. **Select your Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
4. **Provide Date of Disability**: Enter the date when your disability began or expected to begin.
5. **Reason for Disability**: Briefly describe the reason for your long-term disability.
6. **Doctor's Signature**: The doctor's signature should be from your treating doctor.
7. **Doctor's Name**: Enter your treating doctor's name as it appears on their license and ID.
8. **Doctor's Phone**: Enter your treating doctor's phone number.
9. **Doctor's License**: Enter your treating doctor's license number.
10. **Doctor's Address**: Enter your treating doctor's address.
11. **Doctor's Office Phone**: Enter your treating doctor's office phone number.
12. **Employee Relation**: Check the box to indicate the nature of your relationship with your doctor.
13. **Doctor's Relation**: Check the box to indicate the nature of your doctor's relationship with you.
14. **Doctor's Specialty**: Enter your doctor's specialty.
15. **Date Return to Work**: Enter the date you expect to return to work.
16. **Number of Days Off**: Enter the number of days you expect to be off.
17. **Doctor's Note**: The doctor's note should be from your treating doctor and should include the expected length of leave.
18. **Employee's Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
19. **Company Representative**: Enter the name and email of the company representative who reviewed and approved your leave.
20. **Company Rep Signature**: Sign to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.
21. **Company Rep Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company representative.
22. **Department Name**: Enter the name of your department.
23. **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
24. **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email.
25. **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
