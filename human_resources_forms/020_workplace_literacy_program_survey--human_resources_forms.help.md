# Workplace Literacy Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your educational background, work experience, and other relevant details to help us better understand and improve our workplace literacy programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and job title as required.
2. Provide your years of experience with the company and your current education level.
3. Select your highest level of education and the field of education you've pursued.
4. Answer questions about your work schedule and job function.
5. If you have additional job titles, education levels, or work schedules, please answer these questions as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel file.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title as per your personnel file.
* **Years with Company** (`years_with_company`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of years you've worked with the company.
* **Years in Education** (`years_in_education`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you've completed in your education.
* **Literacy Level** (`literacy_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Highest Degree Earned** (`highest_degree`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the highest degree you've earned.
* **In Progress** (`education_in_progress`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any degrees or certifications you're currently pursuing.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your current level of education.
* **Education Field** (`education_field`, `text`, required): Enter the field or discipline you're studying or have studied in.
* **Work with Literacy Program** (`work_with_literacy_program`, `text`, optional): If you've worked with our literacy program before, please enter a brief description.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, required): Enter your current job function.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your current department.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter your current location.
* **Shift** (`shift`, `text`, required): Enter your current shift.
* **Hours per Week** (`hours_per_week`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, `text`, optional): If you have an additional job title, enter it here.
* **Education Level 2** (`education_level_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you've earned additional degrees, select them here.
* **Work with Literacy Program 2** (`work_with_literacy_program_2`, `text`, optional): If you've worked with our literacy program before, enter a brief description.
* **Job Function 2** (`job_function_2`, `text`, optional): If you have an additional job function, enter it here.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, `text`, optional): If you have an additional department, enter it here.
* **Location 2** (`location_2`, `text`, optional): If you have an additional location, enter it here.
* **Shift 2** (`shift_2`, `text`, optional): If you work additional shifts, enter them here.
