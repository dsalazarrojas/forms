# Employee Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quick and easy way for employees to check in after a meeting or event. It helps the HR department to track attendance and gather feedback from employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form on the first day of the week (Monday) to ensure that all attendees are accounted for.
2. Select the correct options for "Department", "Team Lead", "Manager", and "Department Lead" based on your role and attendance.
3. Provide your feedback and comments in the "Feedback" and "Comments" fields.
4. If applicable, select the correct feedback category and provide your feedback in the "Feedback Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`): A text field where you can provide any feedback about the meeting or event.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`): Date of the meeting or event you attended.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`): Time of the arrival at the meeting or event.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Your name as an employee.
* **Department** (`department`): Select your department from the list.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`): Select if you are a team lead.
* **Manager** (`manager`): Select if you have a manager.
* **Department Lead** (`department_lead`): Select if you are a department lead.
* **Feedback Category** (`feedback_category`): Select a category for your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): A text field where you can provide detailed feedback about the meeting or event.
* **Comments** (`feedback_comments`): Additional comments about the meeting or event.
