# Profile Builder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Profile Builder Form is designed to collect information about your profile, ensuring that your HR profile is up-to-date and accurate. This form is used to gather essential details about your contact information, work experience, and skills, which are vital for profile completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with accurate and complete information about your profile.
2. Ensure you answer all the required fields, as indicated by the "Required" label.
3. Review your form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Profile Builder Form**: This is the title of the form, which you are currently filling out.
* **Contact Details (1)**: This field is used to input your contact details, such as name and other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Address (2)**: Enter your contact address to ensure that your profile has the correct address associated with it.
* **Contact Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Contact Email (4)**: Enter your email address for easy communication.
* **Profile Picture (5)**: If you have a profile picture, please upload it here.
* **Work Experience (6)**: Enter your work experience to help us understand your background.
* **Job Title (7)**: Enter your current or previous job title for reference.
* **Job Description (8)**: Provide a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Skills (9)**: Select the relevant skills you have, such as "Yes" or "No", to showcase your abilities.
* **Profile Completion Date (10)**: Enter the date you completed this profile for tracking purposes.
* **Profile Completion Time (11)**: Enter the time you completed this profile.
* **Profile Completion Note (12)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your profile completion.
* **Chat Message (13)**: This field is not currently used, but please enter any message you'd like to leave for our team.
* **Chat Message (14)**: This field is not currently used, but please ignore it for now.
