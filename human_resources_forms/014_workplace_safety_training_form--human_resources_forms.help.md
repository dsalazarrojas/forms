# Workplace Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Training Form is used to record and track employee training sessions, including dates, providers, locations, topics, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the training session in the **Session Date** field.
2. Select the type of training session from the **Training Session** dropdown menu.
3. Enter the employee's name and employee ID number.
4. If applicable, select the provider of the training session from the **Training Provider** dropdown menu.
5. Choose the location of the training session from the **Training Location** dropdown menu.
6. Select the topic(s) of the training session from the **Training Topic** dropdown menu (multiple selections are allowed).
7. Optionally, add any additional comments from the employee in the **Employee Comments** field.
8. Select the current status of the training session from the **Training Status** dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Date (id: 1)**: Enter the date of the training session in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Training Session (id: 2)**: Choose the type of training session you are completing this form for. This is a required field.
* **Employee Name (id: 3)**: Enter the name of the employee who attended the training session. This is a required field.
* **Employee ID (id: 4)**: Enter the ID number of the employee who attended the training session. This is a required field.
* **Training Provider (id: 5)**: Select the provider of the training session if applicable. This field is not required.
* **Training Location (id: 6)**: Choose the location where the training session was held. This field is not required.
* **Training Topic (id: 7)**: Select the topic(s) of the training session. Multiple topics can be selected. This field is not required.
* **Employee Comments (id: 8)**: Optionally, add any comments or feedback from the employee about the training session.
* **Training Status (id: 9)**: Select the current status of the training session from the dropdown menu. This is a required field.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and comprehensively.
* Use the form for a specific type of training session, such as safety or emergency procedures.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
