# Employee Annual Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Annual Performance Evaluation Form is designed to help managers and supervisors evaluate and provide feedback on an employee's goals, job satisfaction, and areas for improvement. This form is a crucial tool for employees to reflect on their performance and identify areas for growth and development within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the provided form and ensure you have a clear understanding of the questions and prompts.
2. For each field, provide a detailed and honest evaluation of the employee's performance.
3. Use the space provided for each question to type in your answers.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields (goals_achieved and areas_for_improvement are not required fields but employee_performance is a required field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Performance (`employee_performance`, required)
* A description of the employee's overall performance, including strengths, weaknesses, and areas of improvement.

### Goals Achieved (`goals_achieved`, optional)
* A list of specific goals achieved by the employee during the evaluation period. Be as detailed as possible in describing what was accomplished and how it positively impacted the organization.

### Areas for Improvement (`areas_for_improvement`, optional)
* A list of areas where the employee may need to improve or develop new skills to enhance their performance and career growth.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific when evaluating the employee's performance.
* Use examples or specific instances to support your evaluation and feedback.
* Ensure you keep the tone of your feedback constructive and helpful, focusing on improvement areas rather than criticism.
* Proofread your answers for accuracy and clarity before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
