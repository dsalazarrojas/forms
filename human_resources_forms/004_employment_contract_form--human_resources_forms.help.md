# Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Contract Form is a standardized document used to establish the terms and conditions of employment for new hires. This form captures essential information about the employee, including contact details, job title, and contract term.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee's personal details:
	* First Name: Enter the employee's first name.
	* Last Name: Enter the employee's last name.
	* Email: Enter the employee's email address.
	* Date: Enter the date of employment.
	* Time: Enter the time of employment.
2. Confirm the contract term:
	* Select the contract term from the available options: One Month, Three Months, Six Months, Twelve Months, or Month-to-Month.
3. Enter company details:
	* Job Title: Enter the employee's job title.
	* Company Address: Enter the company's physical address.
	* Company Phone: Enter the company's phone number.
	* Company Email: Enter the company's email address.
4. Enter employee details:
	* Employee ID: Enter a unique identifier for the employee.
5. Sign off:
	* Employee Signature: Sign and date the contract as the employee.
	* Company Signature: Sign and date the contract as the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the employee's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the employee's email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Date**: Enter the date of employment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time**: Enter the time of employment in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the contract as the employee.
* **Contract Term**: Select the contract term from the available options: One Month, Three Months, Six Months, Twelve Months, or Month-to-Month.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Company Address**: Enter the company's physical address.
* **Company Phone**: Enter the company's phone number.
* **Company Email**: Enter the company's email address.
* **Employee ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the employee.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date the contract as the employee.
* **Company Signature**: Sign and date the contract as the company. 

## Tips
- Please review the form carefully to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
- Ensure the employee signs and dates their contract.
- Review the contract terms and conditions with the employee before completion.
- Have both the employee and company sign and date the contract.
