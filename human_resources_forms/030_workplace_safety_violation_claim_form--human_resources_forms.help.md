# Workplace Safety Violation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Violation Claim Form is a tool for employees to report incidents or near-miss events that occurred while on the job. This form helps the relevant departments to take necessary actions to prevent similar incidents from happening in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions in a clear and concise manner.
2. Provide as much detail as possible about the incident or near-miss event.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.
4. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure that it is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reported Violation** (`reported_violation`): Describe the violation that occurred. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of what happened.
* **Violation Date** (`violation_date`): Enter the date on which the violation occurred. If you are unsure, please use a date range (e.g., "between January 1st and January 15th").
* **Violation Time** (`violation_time`): Enter the time on which the violation occurred. If you are unsure, please use a time range (e.g., "between 08:00 and 12:00").
* **Location** (`location`): Provide the location where the violation occurred. This could be a specific room, floor, or area of the building.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when reporting the violation.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact the relevant department for guidance.
* This form is for the sole purpose of reporting workplace safety violations, do not use it for any other purpose.
