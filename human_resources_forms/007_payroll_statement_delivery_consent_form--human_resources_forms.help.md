# Payroll Statement Delivery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to consent to the electronic delivery of their payroll statements. It is a crucial document that helps us understand your preferences for statement delivery, ensuring that you receive your statements in the manner you desire.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Details**: Enter your basic details, such as name and contact information, in this field.
2. **Consent**: Choose how you want to receive your electronic payroll statements. You can select from the available options to ensure your statements are delivered to you in a way that suits your needs.
3. **Delivery Preference**: Select the preferred method of delivery for your electronic payroll statements.
4. **Payroll Statements**: If you want to receive statements from a specific start and end date, provide them in the respective fields.
5. **Delivery Preference Frequency**: Choose how often you want to receive your statements, from the options provided.
6. **Electronic Payroll Statements**: Select the options for the type of electronic statements you want to receive.
7. **Email**: Enter your email address where you want to receive your statements.
8. **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted in case of any issues.
9. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions you have for the HR department.
10. **Employee Signature**: Select your preferred signature option.
11. **HR Signature**: Select the HR signature option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employee ID, name, and other relevant details.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you consent to receive electronic payroll statements.
* **Delivery Preference** (`delivery_preference`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more methods for delivering your electronic payroll statements. 
* **Payroll Statements** (`payroll_statements`, `date`, required/optional): Provide the start and end dates of the payroll statements you want to receive.
* **Payroll Statements Frequency** (`payroll_statements_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose how often you want to receive your payroll statements.
* **Electronic Payroll Statements** (`electronic_payroll_statements`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the types of electronic statements you want to receive.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address where you want to receive your electronic statements.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the HR department.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred signature option.
* **HR Signature** (`hr_signature`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the HR signature option.
