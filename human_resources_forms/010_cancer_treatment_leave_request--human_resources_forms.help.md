# Cancer Treatment Leave Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cancer Treatment Leave Request form is used to document an employee's request for leave due to cancer treatment. This form helps ensure that the employee's needs are met and that their rights are protected.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Your Personal Details
1. **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`): Enter your company ID number.
3. **Employee Email Address** (`employee_email`): Enter your work email address.

### Step 2: Job and Department Information
1. **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your current job title.
2. **Department** (`department`): Select your department from the options provided.
3. **Supervisor's Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.

### Step 3: Leave Details
1. **Leave Type** (`leave_type`): Select the type of leave requested (e.g., Medical leave - FMLA, Short-term disability, etc.).
2. **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`): Select the reason for your leave request.
3. **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`): Enter the first day of your leave.
4. **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`): Enter the planned return to work date.
5. **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`): Select the expected duration of your leave.
6. **Is Leave Intermittent?** (`intermittent_leave`): If you need to take leave periodically, select "Yes" and provide details in the next field.
7. **Intermittent Schedule Details** (`intermittent_schedule`): Enter the details of your intermittent schedule if applicable.
8. **Medical Certification Provided** (`medical_certification`): Indicate whether you have obtained medical certification from a doctor.
9. **Treating Physician Name** (`physician_name`): Enter the name of your treating physician.

### Step 4: Accommodations and Support
1. **Work Handover Plan** (`work_handover_plan`): Describe how your work will be covered during your leave.
2. **Colleague Coverage** (`colleague_coverage`): Identify who will cover your role while you are on leave.
3. **Remote Work Possible?** (`remote_work_possible`): Indicate whether you can work from home during your leave.
4. **Flexible Schedule Requested?** (`flexible_schedule_request`): If you need flexible hours or a compressed workweek, select "Yes".
5. **Benefits Continuation Questions** (`benefits_continuation`): Confirm that you understand your benefits during leave.
6. **Accrued Leave to Use** (`accrued_leave_used`): Select the types of leave you are using (e.g., vacation, sick leave, etc.).
7. **Contact Preferences During Leave** (`contact_during_leave`): Choose how you would like to be contacted during your leave.
8. **Anticipated Return to Work Accommodations** (`return_to_work_accommodations`): If you need accommodations upon return, select "Yes" and provide details.
9. **HR Contact Acknowledgment** (`hr_contact_acknowledgment`): Confirm that you have HR contact information or request it if needed.
10. **Employee Signature Date** (`employee_signature_date`): Enter the date of your request.
11. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any other relevant information.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have discussed your leave request with your supervisor before submitting this form.
* Review your company's policies and procedures regarding leave to understand your rights and responsibilities.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure that your needs are met and that your rights are protected.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative for guidance.
