# Retail Employee Burnout Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help retail employees request and document their leave of absence (LOA) for burnout. A LOA is a period of time off work due to exhaustion, depression, or other mental health concerns that are not work-related. This form will help the store management team assess and address the employee's situation, ensuring a smooth transition and support for the employee during their time off.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your store location from the `Store Location` field.
2. Enter your supervisor's name in the `Manager Name` field.
3. Provide a detailed explanation for your leave of absence in the `Reason for Leave` field. Be sure to include any relevant medical information or other context that may help the management team understand your situation.
4. Choose a start date for your LOA in the `Start Date` field.
5. Choose an end date for your LOA in the `End Date` field.
6. Enter your name in the `Employee Name` field.
7. If applicable, indicate the status of your manager's approval in the `Manager Approval Status` field.
8. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share in the `Employee Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Location**: Select your store location from the provided options.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor or person in charge of your work duties.
* **Reason for Leave**: Provide a detailed explanation for your leave of absence. This will help the management team understand your situation.
* **Start Date**: Choose the date you'd like to start your leave of absence.
* **End Date**: Choose the date you'd like to end your leave of absence.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employment records.
* **Manager Approval Status**: If your manager has reviewed your form, select their approval status. If they have not reviewed it yet, please select "Pending."
* **Employee Comments**: Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with the management team.
