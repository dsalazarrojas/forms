# Sick Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sick Leave Form is a tool for employees and managers to manage and process sick leaves within the organization. It captures essential details for approval, tracking, and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.** Ensure that all required fields are completed.
2. **Enter the employee's ID, name, email, phone number, and department.** This information helps in identifying and contacting the employee.
3. **Enter the start and end dates of the leave.** This includes the exact date the leave begins and ends.
4. **Provide a valid reason for the leave.** Be specific and clear about the reason for the sick leave.
5. **Get manager confirmation and approval.** Ensure that the manager checks the form and approves or denies the leave.
6. **Add any additional notes or comments.** This section is for managers to include any relevant comments or notes about the leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, **required**): Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, **required**): Enter the exact date the leave begins.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, **required**): Enter the exact date the leave ends.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, **required**): Provide a valid reason for the leave. Be specific and clear.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, select_one, **required**): Confirm whether the manager approves or denies the leave.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, **optional**): If denied, add a comment explaining the reason.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, **required**): Sign and date the form with your employee's signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, **required**): Sign and date the form with your manager's signature.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, **optional**): Add any relevant comments or notes.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, **required**): Choose whether the leave is active or inactive.
* **Approval Note** (`approval_note`, note, **optional**): Add any additional notes about the approval.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date the leave was approved.
* **Reason Denied** (`reason_denied`, text, **optional**): If denied, provide a reason for the denial.
* **Denied Note** (`denied_note`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments about the denial.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, **required**): Choose the active or inactive status of the leave.
* **Status Note** (`status_note`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments about the leave status.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **required**): Enter the employee's name.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, **required**): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, **required**): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, **required**): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, **required**): Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about the leave.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- Double-check dates and times for consistency.
- Provide clear and specific reasons for leave and denial.
- Be respectful and professional in all interactions and comments.
