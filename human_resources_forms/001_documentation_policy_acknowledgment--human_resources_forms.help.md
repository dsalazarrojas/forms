# Documentation Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to acknowledge their documentation policy, allowing for the accurate tracking and record-keeping of regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the title of the policy being documented in the "Documentation Title" field. This field should match the exact wording of the policy title.
2. Provide your employee ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by the organization.
3. Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
4. Enter the ID of your direct supervisor or manager, which is also a unique identifier assigned to them by the organization.
5. Select the date on which the policy documentation was completed.
6. Select the status of the policy documentation review, which can be "Complete", "In Progress", or "Not Complete".
7. (Optional) Enter any comments or notes about the policy documentation.
8. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Documentation Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the policy being documented.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): This is your unique employee ID assigned by the organization. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of your direct supervisor or manager, which is also a unique identifier assigned to them by the organization.
* **Documentation Date** (`documentation_date`, date, required): Select the date on which the policy documentation was completed.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select one, required): Select "Complete" if the documentation has been reviewed and is complete, "In Progress" if it is currently being reviewed, or "Not Complete" if it still needs review.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the policy documentation. This field is for your internal reference and does not affect the official completion status of the form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, time, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgment of the policy documentation.
