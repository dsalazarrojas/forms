# Employee Benefits Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on employee benefits package, including importance, effectiveness, and overall satisfaction. Your input will help the organization better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question with your honest opinion.
- For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best fit your opinion.
- For text fields, provide your thoughts and feedback in as much detail as you like.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **benefits_package** (`Select one: Yes/No`): This question asks whether you think the employee benefits package is important to you or not.
* **health_insurance** (`Select multiple: Yes/No`): Here you can indicate which types of health insurance you currently have or want to have as part of your benefits package.
* **dental_insurance** (`Select multiple: Yes/No`): Similarly, for dental insurance.
* **disability_insurance** (`Select multiple: Yes/No`): For disability insurance.
* **life_insurance** (`Select multiple: Yes/No`): For life insurance.
* **vision_vesting** (`Date`): Please enter the date for your retirement plan vesting or when you plan to retire.
* **retirement_plan** (`Select one: Yes/No`): Here you can indicate if you think the retirement plan is important to you.
* **other_benefits** (`Select multiple: Yes/No`): Check the benefits you think are missing or not important to you.
* **benefits_suggestions** (`Text`): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the benefits package.
* **open_text1** (`Text`): Additional comments or suggestions.
* **open_text2** (`Text`): More comments or suggestions.
* **benefits_satisfaction** (`Select one: Satisfied/Dissatisfied`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the benefits package.
* **benefits_importance** (`Select one: Very Important/Not Important`): Rate how important the benefits package is to you.
* **comments** (`Text`): Provide any other comments or concerns.
* **open_text3** (`Text`): More open-ended text field.
* **open_text4** (`Text`): Yet another open-ended text field.
* **benefits_satisfaction_level** (`Select one: Satisfied/Dissatisfied`): Rate your satisfaction with the benefits package.
* **benefits_satisfaction_important** (`Select one: Satisfied/Dissatisfied`): Similar to previous question, rate your satisfaction with the benefits package.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`Select one: Satisfied/Dissatisfied`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the organization's benefits package.
* **open_text5** (`Text`): Additional comments or suggestions.
* **open_text6** (`Text`): More comments or suggestions.
* **open_text7** (`Text`): Yet another open-ended text field.
* **open_text8** (`Text`): Final open-ended text field.
