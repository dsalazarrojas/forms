# Employee Getting To Know You Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees to help us get to know them better as individuals and understand their work habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each field.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your manager or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Introduction** (`employee_introduction`, text, optional):
  This is a free-text field where you can introduce yourself briefly. Please provide a short bio that includes your job title, department, and any relevant work experience.

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional):
  This field is for you to provide your current job title. Please enter your official job title as it appears on your business card or email signature.

* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional):
  Indicate if you have team members you work closely with on a regular basis. Select all that apply.

* **Preferred Communication Channels** (`preferred_communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional):
  Indicate your preferred method of communication. Select all that apply.

* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, time, optional):
  Enter your regular working hours, including start time, end time, and any breaks.

* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional):
  Provide the location where you work, including any relevant details (e.g., office, home, or remote).

* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional):
  Enter the total number of hours you work per week.

* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional):
  This is a free-text field for you to describe your job responsibilities and tasks. Provide a brief overview of your role and duties.

**Tips**
Please keep in mind that this form is for internal reference only. Your responses will not be shared with anyone outside the company.
