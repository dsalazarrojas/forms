# hr_policy_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `hr_policy_communication_form` is used for submitting requests or notifications related to HR policies, such as time off or leave of absence, between employees and their managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Select the appropriate options for each field.
3. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_fields\*\* (text)**: This is the main body of the form where you can type in your request or notification. Please be as detailed as possible so your manager has all the necessary information.
*   **policy_title\*\* (text)**: This field allows you to specify the title or subject of your policy-related request or notification.
*   **policy_content\*\* (text)**: This field is for providing more detailed information about your request or notification.
*   **acknowledgment_1\*\* (select_one)**: This field is to acknowledge that you have received the policy information. Please select "Yes" if you have read and understood the policy.
*   **employee_name\*\* (text)**: This field is for your name and contact information.
*   **employee_email\*\* (email)**: This field is for your work email address.
*   **acknowledgment_2\*\* (select_multiple)**: This field is to acknowledge that your manager has read and understood your request or notification. Please select "Yes" for each relevant acknowledgment.
*   **manager_name\*\* (text)**: This field is for your manager's name and contact information.
*   **manager_email\*\* (email)**: This field is for your manager's email address.
*   **manager_phone\*\* (text)**: This field is for your manager's phone number.
*   **policy_date\*\* (date)**: This field is for the date the policy was introduced or affects.
*   **policy_time\*\* (time)**: This field is for the time the policy was introduced or affects.
*   **policy_note\*\* (note)**: This field is for any additional notes or comments related to the policy.
