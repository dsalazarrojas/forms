# Uniform Policy Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge uniform policy compliance by employees. It helps track and record employee acknowledgement of company policies related to uniforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your title in the "Title" field.
4. Enter your Date Of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Enter your Department in the "Department" field.
6. Enter your Role in the "Role" field.
7. Select whether you have acknowledged the uniform policy in the "Acknowledged" field.
8. If you are an employee, enter your Employee Id in the "Employee Id" field.
9. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
- **Title**: Enter your job title or position.
- **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1990).
- **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
- **Role**: Enter your role or position in the organization.
- **Acknowledged**: Select 'True' if you have acknowledged the uniform policy, 'False' otherwise.
- **Employee Id**: If you are an employee, enter your employee ID number.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
