# Incident Resolution Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and resolve incidents that require attention within the organization. It's essential to accurately report incidents to ensure that they are properly addressed, and the necessary steps are taken to prevent them in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure that you are filling out a new incident, not one already documented.
2.  Select the correct "Incident Type" from the options provided, which will help to categorize the incident and guide the resolution process.
3.  Enter the date of the incident using the "incident_date" field. Ensure the date format is correct (MM/DD/YYYY).
4.  Enter the start and end dates of the incident in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields, respectively. These fields should be formatted as MM/DD/YYYY.
5.  Briefly describe the reason for the incident in the "reason" field.
6.  Provide the location where the incident occurred in the "incident_location" field.
7.  Describe the incident in the "incident_description" field, including any relevant details.
8.  Select the current status of the incident using the "incident_status" field. This will help track the progress of the resolution process.
9.  Get confirmation from your manager using the "Manager Confirmation" field. This will ensure that the incident is properly escalated.
10.  Get confirmation from the employee involved using the "Employee Confirmation" field.
11.  Add any additional notes about the incident in the "notes" field.
12.  Enter your name and the date you submitted the form in the "Form Submitted By" and "Form Submitted Date" fields, respectively.
13.  Enter the name and date of the person who last updated the form in the "Last Updated By" and "Last Updated Date" fields, respectively.
14.  If applicable, select the tool used to resolve the incident in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type**: This is the category that the incident falls under. Choose from the options provided to help guide the resolution process.
* **incident_date**: Enter the date the incident occurred. Ensure the date format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the incident. This should be formatted as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the incident. This should be formatted as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **reason**: Briefly describe the reason for the incident.
* **incident_location**: Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_description**: Describe the incident, including any relevant details.
* **incident_status**: Select the current status of the incident to help track the resolution process.
* **Manager Confirmation**: Get confirmation from your manager to ensure the incident is properly escalated.
* **Employee Confirmation**: Get confirmation from the employee involved to ensure their involvement and understanding.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes about the incident.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter your name.
* **Form Submitted Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Last Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Last Updated Date**: Enter the date the form was last updated, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, select the tool used to resolve the incident.
* **Form ID**: This field is automatically generated and not editable.
