# Consultancy Research Analyst Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a candidate's research skills, experience, and overall fit for the role of a consultancy research analyst. It is intended for use by interviewers to evaluate a candidate's suitability for the position and provide a recommendation for hiring or further evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's name in the **Candidate name** field.
2. Select the date of the interview from the calendar in the **Interview date** field.
3. Enter the name of the person conducting the interview in the **Interviewer name** field.
4. Rate the candidate's research skills in the **Research skills rating** field using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
5. List any relevant tools or methodologies the candidate has experience with in the **Technical skills** field.
6. Add any comments about the candidate's case study evaluation in the **Case study evaluation notes** field.
7. Choose a recommendation from the options provided in the **Overall recommendation** field (Strong hire, Hire, Consider, or Do not hire).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate name** (`text`, required): Enter the full legal name of the candidate being interviewed.
* **Interview date** (`date`, required): Select the date of the interview.
* **Interviewer name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the interview.
* **Research skills rating** (`select_one`, required): Rate the candidate's research skills as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Technical skills** (`text`, optional): List any relevant tools or methodologies the candidate has experience with.
* **Case study evaluation notes** (`text`, optional): Add any comments about the candidate's case study evaluation.
* **Overall recommendation** (`select_one`, required): Choose a recommendation for the candidate from the options provided.
  * Strong hire: The candidate is an excellent fit for the role and is recommended for hire.
  * Hire: The candidate is a good fit for the role and is recommended for hiring.
  * Consider: The candidate is not a strong fit, but has some potential and should be considered for further evaluation.
  * Do not hire: The candidate is not a fit for the role and is not recommended for hiring.
* **Tips**:
  * Be specific and accurate when rating the candidate's research skills and providing feedback.
  * Ensure that you are rating the candidate based on their actual skills and experience, not on your personal opinion.
  * Use the **Case study evaluation notes** field to provide detailed comments and feedback about the candidate's case study.
  * Choose the most accurate recommendation from the options provided in the **Overall recommendation** field.
