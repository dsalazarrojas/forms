# Injury Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an incident that occurred at the workplace, including injuries, illnesses, and near misses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct employee name from the list provided.
2. Enter the date the incident occurred using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour clock format (HH:MM AM/PM).
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
5. Select Yes or No for the injury type.
6. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
7. Provide a detailed description of the incident cause.
8. Select Yes or No for whether the employer conducted an investigation.
9. Select Active or Inactive for the investigation status.
10. Enter the date the investigation was completed, if applicable.
11. Enter the employee's ID number.
12. Provide the name of the person who reported the incident.
13. Select Yes or No for whether the employee's occupational health and safety officer is involved.
14. Enter the name of the supervisor.
15. Enter the title of the person who reported the incident.
16. Enter the name of the person who completed the investigation form.
17. Select Yes or No for whether the form was completed by the correct person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (1)**: Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date (2)**: Enter the date the incident occurred using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time (3)**: Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour clock format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Incident Description (4)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Injury Type (5)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if an injury occurred during the incident.
* **Injury Location (6)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Cause (7)**: Provide a detailed description of the cause of the incident.
* **Incident Employer Conducted Investigation (8)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if the employer conducted an investigation.
* **Investigation Status (9)**: Select Active or Inactive to indicate the current status of the investigation.
* **Investigation Completed Date (10)**: Enter the date the investigation was completed, if applicable.
* **Employee ID (11)**: Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Incident Reported By (12)**: Provide the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Employee Occupational Health and Safety Officer (13)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if the employee's occupational health and safety officer is involved.
* **Supervisor Name (14)**: Enter the name of the supervisor involved in the incident.
* **Reporting Person Title (15)**: Enter the title of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reporting Person Name (16)**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Investigation Form Completed By (17)**: Select Yes or No to indicate if the form was completed by the correct person.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to complete this form.
* Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
