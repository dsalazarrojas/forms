# MesquiteServicesEmploymentApplicationNonDot - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MesquiteServicesEmploymentApplicationNonDot form is a tool for candidates to apply for job openings within the Mesquite Services organization. It collects information about the candidate's employment status, job preferences, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the `name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Enter your address in the `address` field.
5. Select your job title in the `job title` field.
6. Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for in the `job description` field.
7. Enter your available start date in the `available start date` field.
8. Enter your available end date in the `available end date` field.
9. Select your job type (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract) in the `job type` field.
10. Select your employment status (Active, Non-active, or Retired) in the `employment status` field.
11. Enter your job location in the `job location` field.
12. List your relevant job skills in the `job skills` field.
13. Enter the number of hours you are available to work in the `available work hours` field.
14. Enter your job education in the `job education` field.
15. Provide a brief description of your job experience in the `job experience` field.
16. List any job references you would like to provide in the `job references` field.
17. Enter a brief description of your job availability in the `job availability` field.
18. Attach your resume in the `resume` field.
19. Attach a cover letter in the `cover letter` field.
20. If you have any other relevant information, you can enter it in the `other` field.
21. If you have any additional notes, you can enter them in the `notes` field.
22. You can also enter your job ID and job category/subcategory in the `job id` and `job category` fields, but these are optional.
