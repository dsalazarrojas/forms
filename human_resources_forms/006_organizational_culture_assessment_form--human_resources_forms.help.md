# Organizational Culture Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the organizational culture within a department or team, providing insights into the values, communication styles, and overall job satisfaction of its members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the answers that best describe your experiences and perceptions within your department or team. For multiple-choice questions, choose the option that best fits your response. You can choose one or multiple answers depending on the question type.
