# Recruitment Agency Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Agency Interview Form is designed to collect information from recruitment agencies about their interactions with candidates. This form will help us understand the agency's contact details, the job the candidate applied for, and the candidate's qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (Name, Email, and Phone number) under the "Contact Information" section.
2. Specify the email address of the recruitment agency.
3. Mention the job the candidate applied for.
4. Indicate the candidate's availability to work.
5. List the candidate's relevant skills.
6. Describe the candidate's work experience.
7. Mention any education the candidate holds.
8. List any job or professional references.
9. Provide any other relevant references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (1. Contact Information)
* **Name (text)**: Enter your name as the representative of the recruitment agency.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address of your agency.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.

### Contact Information (2. Contact Email)
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the recruitment agency.

### Contact Information (3. Contact Phone)
* **Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of your agency.

### Contact Information (4. Contact Name)
* **Name (text)**: Enter the name of the representative of the recruitment agency.

### Job Applied For (5. Job Applied For)
* **Job Applied For (text)**: Enter the job title the candidate applied for.

### Availability (6. Availability)
* **Availability (select_multiple)**: Select the candidate's availability to work (Yes or No).

### Skills (7. Skills)
* **Skills (text)**: List the candidate's relevant skills.

### Work Experience (8. Work Experience)
* **Work Experience (text)**: Describe the candidate's work experience.

### Education (9. Education)
* **Education (text)**: Mention any relevant education the candidate holds.

### Job References (10. Job References)
* **Job References (text)**: List any job or professional references.

### Other References (11. Other References)
* **Other References (text)**: Provide any other relevant references.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to submit this form.
* Check that you are not submitting duplicate reports for the same candidate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
