# Talent Acquisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Acquisition Form is a tool used to collect information from job applicants. It helps our team assess their qualifications and fit for available positions. This form is designed to gather essential details that will aid in the hiring process.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email Address and Contact Number for contact purposes.
3. If applicable, provide a link to your Resume for us to review.
4. Specify the Job Title and Job Description of the position you are applying for.
5. Choose the Job Status, Job Category, and Job Type that best match your qualifications.
6. Select the Work Location that suits your needs.
7. Fill in the dates for Job Posting, Job Expiration, and Job Open-To dates.
8. If you have any specific dates for Job Close-To, please provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known on official documents or as you prefer to be addressed.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known on official documents or as you prefer to be addressed.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for communication from us.
- **Phone**: Enter your contact number for us to reach out for further discussion.
- **Resume Link**: If you have a resume, please provide a link to it for our review.
- **Job Title**: Enter the title of the position you are applying for.
- **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job role you are applying for.
- **Job Status**: Select the status of the job you are applying for (Open, Closed, or Pending).
- **Job Category**: Select the category that best fits your job application (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, or Intern).
- **Job Type**: Select the type that best fits your job application (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, or Intern).
- **Work Location**: Select where you would be willing to work (Onsite, Remote, or Hybrid).
- **Job Posting Date**: Enter the date when you became aware of the job posting.
- **Job Expiration Date**: Enter the date when the job will expire or be closed.
- **Job Open To**: Enter the date when the job will be open to applications.
- **Job Close To**: Enter the date when the job will be closed.
- **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number for us to reach out for further discussion.
- **Job Link**: If you have a link to the job posting, please provide it for our review.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
- We appreciate your attention to detail and prompt response.
