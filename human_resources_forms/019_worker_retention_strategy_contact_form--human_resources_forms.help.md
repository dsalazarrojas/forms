# Worker Retention Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by workers to contact management or HR department with their concerns, suggestions, or ideas about the company's worker retention policies and practices. Please fill out the form with as much detail as possible to help us better understand your needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Open the form and select the "Contact Form" page.
- Fill out the "Feedback" field with your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is required.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is required.
- Select your department from the list of options. This is required.
- Enter your position title in the "Position" field. This is required.
- Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field. This is required.
- If you have any additional comments or information, you can type them in the "Comments" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Form** (`worker_retention_strategy_contact_form`, text, required/optional): Enter your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the company's worker retention policies and practices. 
- **Feedback** (`worker_retention_strategy_feedback`, text, required): Enter your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns in this field. This is a required field.
- **Email** (`worker_retention_strategy_email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This is the address we will use to contact you if needed.
- **Phone** (`worker_retention_strategy_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number. This is required for us to contact you if needed.
- **Department** (`worker_retention_strategy_department`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more departments where you work. Options are "Yes" and "No".
- **Position** (`worker_retention_strategy_position`, text, required): Enter your position title.
- **Job Title** (`worker_retention_strategy_job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
- **Comments** (`worker_retention_strategy_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, you can type them in this field.
