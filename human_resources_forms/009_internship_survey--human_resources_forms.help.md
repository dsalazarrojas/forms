# Internship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from interns about their experiences in their current or previous internship positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and email.
2. Answer the questions about your work schedule, work experience, job satisfaction, and other relevant aspects.
3. Select the options that best fit your response for each question.
4. Once you have filled out the entire form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name**: This is the field for you to enter your name.
* **What is your email**: This is the field for you to enter your email address.
* **Job title**: This field is for you to enter your job title or position.
* **Work schedule**: This is the field for you to describe your work schedule.
* **Work experience**: This field is for you to describe your work experience.
* **Job satisfaction**: This question asks how satisfied you are with your job, with options to select "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Communication**: This question asks about your communication with colleagues, with options to select "Yes" or "No".
* **Work environment**: This question asks about your work environment, with options to select "Yes" or "No".
* **Job Satisfaction 2**: This question is similar to "Job satisfaction", but with different options.
* **Company image**: This question asks how the company's image is perceived by you, with options to select "Yes" or "No".
* **Work Schedule 2**: This question asks about your work schedule, similar to "Work schedule", but with different options.
* **Work Experience 2**: This question asks about your work experience, similar to "Work experience", but with different options.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Please submit the form only once, do not repeat the process if you have already completed the form.
