# Day Worker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about day workers for scheduling and coordination purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and quickly, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the first and second fields, respectively.
2. Provide a valid email address in the email field.
3. Input your phone number in the phone number field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the date of birth field, using the standard date format of MM/DD/YY.
5. Select one or multiple availability periods in the availability field.
6. Indicate your work experience in the work experience field by selecting one of the options.
7. Choose your availability time in the time field, if applicable.
8. Select your job type from the options in the job type field.
9. Input your job title and job description in the job title and job description fields, respectively.
10. Optionally, provide any additional notes in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple periods when you are available to work, such as Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_one, optional): Indicate your work experience by selecting one of the options, such as Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years.
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, time, optional): If applicable, choose your availability time, which will help in scheduling your work assignments.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select your job type from the options, which can be Casual, Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contract.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Input your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of your job, which will help in understanding your role.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If assigned a specific tool, input the name of the tool.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Optionally, provide any additional notes about your work or schedule.
