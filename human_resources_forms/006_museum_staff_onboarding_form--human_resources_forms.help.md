# Museum Staff Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Staff Onboarding Form is a tool used to gather essential information about new staff members, including their emergency contact details, employment details, and job-specific information. This form is required for all new staff members and helps the HR department to create accurate records and facilitate smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the New Staff Member Information page with your name, contact information, and a brief description of your job responsibilities.
2.  On the Emergency Contact Information page, provide details about your emergency contact, including their name, relationship, and contact information.
3.  On the Employment Details page, enter your employment details, including job title, department, and reporting manager.
4.  On the Relationship page, select the relationship you have with your emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Child, etc.).
5.  On the Relationship Type page, choose the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact (e.g., Primary, Secondary, etc.).
6.  On the Email page, enter your official email address.
7.  On the Phone page, enter your contact phone number.
8.  On the Job Title page, enter your official job title.
9.  On the Department page, enter your department name.
10.  On the Reporting Manager page, enter the name of your reporting manager.
11.  On the Reporting Manager Email page, enter your reporting manager's email address (if required).
11.  On the Reporting Manager Phone page, enter your reporting manager's phone number (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Staff Member Information (text)** (`new_staff_member_info`, required/true): This section is for you to provide your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and a brief description of your job responsibilities.
*   **Emergency Contact Information (text)** (`emergency_contact_info`, required/false): This section is for you to provide details about your emergency contact, including their name and relationship with you.
*   **Relationship (select_one)** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required/true): Select the relationship you have with your emergency contact from the available options (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Child, Friend, Other).
*   **Relationship Type (select_multiple)** (`contact_relationship_type`, required/true): Choose the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact from the available options (e.g., Primary, Secondary, etc.).
*   **Email (email)** (`email`, required/true): Enter your official email address.
*   **Phone (text)** (`phone`, required/true): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Job Title (text)** (`job_title`, required/true): Enter your official job title.
*   **Department (text)** (`department`, required/true): Enter your department name.
*   **Reporting Manager (text)** (`reporting_manager`, required/true): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
*   **Reporting Manager Email (email)** (`reporting_manager_email`, required/false): Enter your reporting manager's email address (if required).
*   **Reporting Manager Phone (text)** (`reporting_manager_phone`, required/false): Enter your reporting manager's phone number (if required).
