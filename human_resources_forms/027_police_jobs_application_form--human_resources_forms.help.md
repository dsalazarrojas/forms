# Police Jobs Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Jobs Application Form is designed to collect relevant details from applicants applying for police jobs. The form will help the hiring process to efficiently evaluate the applicant's suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your contact information, including your first name, last name, address, phone number, and email address.
3. Provide your job title and experience in years.
4. Select your available dates for an interview or meeting.
5. Choose your preferred police officer type (Officer, Detective, or Chief of Police).
6. Select your preferred shift schedules (Morning, Afternoon, Night, or Weekend).
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (text, optional)
Your first name.

### 2. **Last Name** (text, optional)
Your last name.

### 3. **Address** (text, optional)
Your address for contact and reference purposes.

### 4. **Phone** (text, optional)
Your phone number for contact and notification purposes.

### 5. **Email** (email, optional)
Your email address for contact and notification purposes.

### 6. **Job Title** (text, optional)
The title of the job you are applying for (e.g., Patrol Officer, Detective, etc.).

### 7. **Experience Years** (number, optional)
The number of years you have experience in the police force or relevant field.

### 8. **Available Dates** (date, optional)
The dates you are available for an interview or meeting.

### 9. **Police Officer Type** (select_one, optional)
Choose your preferred police officer type: Officer, Detective, or Chief of Police.

### 10. **Shift Schedules** (select_multiple, optional)
Select your preferred shift schedules: Morning, Afternoon, Night, or Weekend.

### 11. **Comments** (note, optional)
Any additional comments or notes about your application.

Note: Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
