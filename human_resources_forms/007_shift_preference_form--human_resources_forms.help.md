# shift_preference_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a shift preference submission from employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Employee Name** and **Employee ID** for identification purposes.
2. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** of your shift preference.
3. Indicate your **Shift Time** preference by selecting one of the options (Yes/No).
4. Choose your **Shift Date** preference by selecting one of the dates available in the dropdown.
5. Select your **Shift Preferences** by choosing one or multiple options from the list (Yes/No).
6. Indicate your **Preferred Shift** preference by selecting one of the options (Yes/No).
7. If needed, provide more details about your **Shift Request** and **Shift Request Details**.
8. If your shift request requires approval from a manager, indicate their approval by selecting **Manager Approvals**.
9. If necessary, add any **Notes** about your shift request.
10. Select the current **Shift Status** of the shift you are requesting (Active/Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Select the start date of your shift preference.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Select the end date of your shift preference.
* **Shift Time** (`shift_time`, select_one, required: false): Choose your shift time preference by selecting one of the options (Yes/No).
* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date, required: false): Select your preferred shift date from the available dates.
* **Shift Preferences** (`shift_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your shift preferences by selecting one or multiple options from the list (Yes/No).
* **Preferred Shift** (`preferred_shift`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your preferred shift status by selecting one of the options (Yes/No).
* **Shift Requester** (`shift_requester`, text, required: false): Provide more details about the shift you are requesting.
* **Shift Request** (`shift_request`, text, required: false): Provide more details about your shift request.
* **Shift Request Details** (`shift_request_details`, text, required: false): Add any additional details about your shift request.
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate manager approval by selecting one or multiple options from the list (Yes/No).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes about your shift request.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required: false): Enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
* **Shift Status** (`shift_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current shift status (Active/Inactive).

## Tips
- Ensure the **Shift Date** and **Start Date** and **End Date** fields are filled in correctly to avoid any issues with shift preference submission.
- Make sure to select the correct options for **Manager Approvals** to ensure proper processing of your shift request.
- Use the **Notes** field to add any additional information that may be helpful for HR personnel processing your shift request.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your HR representative for assistance.
