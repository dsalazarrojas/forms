# Interview Questions For House Cleaners Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from house cleaners, including their background, job experience, and availability, to support the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what is optional.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, using your best judgment when answering questions.
3. Ensure that you have answered all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Check** (`background_check`, note, optional): This field is a free-text area for you to provide any relevant background information about yourself, such as previous work experience or references.
* **Job Summary** (`job_summary`, text, optional): Describe your job experience as a house cleaner. Include details about your job responsibilities and accomplishments.
* **House Rules** (`house_rules`, note, optional): This field is for you to note any specific house rules or requirements that you need to be aware of when working in a particular household.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, note, optional): Describe your preferred work environment, such as whether you prefer working in small or large homes, or if you have experience with pets or allergies.
* **Housekeeper Experience** (`housekeeper_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of experience as a house keeper, such as "Yes" if you have experience or "No" if you do not.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): Provide your availability for work, including dates when you are available and when you are not.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
* **Days Available** (`days_available`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you are available to work per week.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to increase your chances of being considered for the position.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- Please do not leave any required fields blank.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
