# quality_of_life_july_19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee feedback and concerns related to their quality of life in the workplace. It aims to provide a platform for employees to express their thoughts and suggestions on how to improve their work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee name in the "employee name" field.
2. Provide feedback in the "feedback" field.
3. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "date submitted" field.
4. Enter the employee's email address in the "employee email" field.
5. Enter the employee's phone number in the "employee phone" field.
6. Enter the supervisor's name in the "supervisor name" field.
7. Enter the supervisor's email address in the "supervisor email" field.
8. Enter the supervisor's phone number in the "supervisor phone" field.
9. Enter the employee's team name in the "team" field.
10. Choose the "submitted by" field as the person filling out the form.
11. Choose the "assigned to" field as the person who will follow up on the issue.
12. Select the "status" field as the current status of the issue (e.g., Active, Inactive).
13. Enter any additional comments in the "comments" field.
14. Select the "follow up status" field as the status of the follow-up (Active, Inactive).
15. Enter any follow-up comments in the "follow up comments" field.
16. Enter the resolution of the issue in the "resolution" field.
17. Enter any next steps in the "next steps" field.
18. Choose the date and time for the follow-up in the "follow up date" and "follow up time" fields.
19. Choose the person who closed the issue in the "closed by" field.
20. Enter the date the issue was closed in the "closed date" field.
21. Enter the time the issue was closed in the "closed time" field.
22. Enter the closed status of the issue in the "closed status" field.
23. Enter any closed comments in the "closed comments" field.
