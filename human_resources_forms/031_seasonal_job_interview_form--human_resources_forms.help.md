# Seasonal Job Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Job Interview Form is used to collect information from potential seasonal job applicants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select your preferred **Job Title** from the available options.
4. Choose your desired **Job Type** from the options provided (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
5. Enter your **Start Date** for the job.
6. Enter your **End Date** for the job.
7. Select your expected **Work Hours** for the job.
8. Choose your desired **Salary Range** from the provided options.
9. Enter your **Address** for contact purposes.
10. Enter your **Contact Email** for communication.
11. Enter your **Contact Phone** number for further communication.
12. Choose your **Availability** for the interview from the available options.
13. Add any **Notes** about your application or experience.
14. Attach your **Resume** for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title**: Select the job title that best describes your role or position.
* **Job Type**: Choose the type of job you're applying for (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, etc.).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date for the job, following the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **End Date**: Enter the end date for the job, following the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Work Hours**: Enter the number of hours you expect to work per week.
* **Salary Range**: Choose the salary range you're expecting for the job.
* **Address**: Enter your residential address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Availability**: Choose the days you're available for the interview.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant information about your application or experience.
* **Resume**: Attach your resume for review.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY) for Start Date and End Date fields.
* Enter accurate information in the Salary Range field to ensure a fair evaluation.
* Use a valid email address and phone number for contact information.
* Be as specific as possible in the Availability field to ensure we can schedule the interview accordingly.

This help guide explains how to fill out the Seasonal Job Interview Form. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
