# Request For Salary Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Request For Salary Change Form" is a self-service form for employees to submit their salary change requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your employee ID from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a detailed reason for your salary change request in the text field.
3. Choose the percentage of your desired salary change from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the actual dollar amount of the change in the numeric field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, select_one, required): Please select your employee ID from the dropdown menu to associate your request with your personnel data.

### Reason for Request
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting a salary change. Please make sure to include any relevant details or supporting materials, such as performance reviews or market research.

### Percentage of Change
* **Percentage of Change** (`salary_change_percentage`, select_multiple, required): Please select the percentage of your desired salary change from 10%, 20%, or 30%.

### Actual Dollar Amount
* **Actual Dollar Amount** (`salary_change_amount`, number, required): Please enter the actual dollar amount of the change, rounded to the nearest dollar.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the company's salary change policy before submitting your request.
* Ensure that you have all necessary documentation or supporting materials ready to be attached to your submission.
* This form is not meant for submitting multiple requests at once. Each request will be reviewed and processed separately.
