# Virtual Interview Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Interview Registration Form is used to collect information from candidates applying for virtual interview positions within the organization. This form helps to streamline the interview process and ensure that all necessary details are collected from each candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you for further communication.
3. Provide your Job Title and Years of Experience in the text fields.
4. Select your Job Level, Job Category, and Job Type from the dropdown menus.
5. Attach your Resume and Job Description, Job Requirements, and Job Location in the text fields.
6. Select the Job Status and Job Start Date in the date picker.
7. Fill in the Job End Date, Job Status 2, and Job Start Time in the text fields.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field is used to capture your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field is used to capture your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further interview stages.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Please enter your job title.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter your years of experience in this role.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, required): Choose from the dropdown menu to indicate your level (Junior or Senior).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu (Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Product Manager).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Choose from the dropdown menu (Full-time or Part-time).
* **Resume** (`resume`, note, required): Attach your resume as a document.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your desired job.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, note, required): Attach a brief description of the job you're applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, note, required): Attach a list of requirements for the job you're applying for.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you're applying for.
* **Job Type 2** (`job_type2`, select_one, required): Choose from the dropdown menu (Full-time, Part-time, Remote).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Choose from the dropdown menu (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Select the job start date from the calendar picker.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the job end date (optional).
* **Job Status 2** (`job_status2`, select_one, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu (Active or Inactive).
* **Resume 2** (`resume2`, note, optional): Attach an additional resume (if necessary).
* **Job Start Time** (`job_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the job start time (if applicable).
* **Job End Time** (`job_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the job end time (if applicable).
* **Job Location 2** (`job_location2`, text, optional): Enter the job location 2 (if applicable).
* **Job Description 2** (`job_description2`, note, optional): Attach a brief description 2 of the job (if applicable).
* **Job Requirements 2** (`job_requirements2`, note, optional): Attach a list of requirements 2 for the job (if applicable).
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, required): Enter your submission to confirm your registration.
