# Workplace Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about workplace harassment complaints. Please fill out the form to report any incidents of harassment that have occurred in the workplace. Your complaint will be reviewed and addressed according to the company's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if the incident you are reporting is a harassment complaint.
2. If it is a complaint, answer "Yes" to the "Complaint Type" field.
3. Describe what happened in the "What happened" field.
4. Specify when and where the incident occurred in the "When did it happen" and "At what time did it happen" fields.
5. If the perpetrator's identity is known, provide their name in the "Who was the perpetrator" field.
6. Answer "Yes" if there were any witnesses to the incident and provide their names in the "Who were they?" field.
7. If you have any witness statements, enter them in the "What did they say" field.
8. If the issue was resolved, answer "Yes" to the "Was the issue resolved" field.
9. If the issue was not resolved, provide details of what happened next in the "What happened next?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Type**: Check "Yes" if you are reporting a workplace harassment complaint.
* **What happened**: Describe in detail what happened, including any relevant facts and circumstances.
* **When did it happen**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **At what time did it happen**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Who was the perpetrator**: If you know the name of the person who was involved in the incident, enter it here.
* **Was there a witness**: Check "Yes" if there were any witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness List 2**: Check "Yes" if there were additional witnesses.
* **Who were they?**: Enter the names of any witnesses.
* **What did they say**: If you have any witness statements, enter them here.
* **Witness Names 2**: Check "Yes" if there are additional witnesses.
* **What happened next**: Provide details of what happened after the incident was reported and addressed.
* **Was the issue resolved**: Check "Yes" if the issue was resolved according to your understanding.
* **Resolution 2**: Check "Yes" if the issue was resolved in any other way.
* **What happened next?**: Provide details of what happened after the incident was reported and addressed.
