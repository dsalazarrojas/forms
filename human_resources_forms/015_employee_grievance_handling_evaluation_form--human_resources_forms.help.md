# Employee Grievance Handling Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Grievance Handling Evaluation Form is designed to gather information about a grievance or issue reported by an employee. The form is used to document the grievance, its resolution, and feedback from both the employee and their supervisor. It helps in evaluating the effectiveness of the resolution mechanism and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the introduction page with a brief description of the grievance.
2. Select the category and subcategory of the grievance from the dropdown menu.
3. Describe the grievance in detail, including its date and time.
4. Provide feedback on the resolution mechanism that was used to resolve the grievance.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the resolution (Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied).
6. Rate your overall rating of the employee's performance (a numerical value).
7. Suggest any improvements or changes to the employee's work or behavior.
8. Rate your satisfaction with the supervisor's handling of the grievance (a numerical value).
9. Suggest any improvements or changes to the supervisor's behavior or approach.
10. Indicate the department and team where the issue occurred.
11. Select the shift that was affected by the grievance.
12. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a space to briefly describe the grievance or issue.
* **grievance_type** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the category and subcategory of the grievance from the dropdown menu.
* **grievance_description** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe the grievance in detail, including its date and time.
* **grievance_date** (`4`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the grievance occurred.
* **grievance_time** (`5`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when the grievance occurred.
* **grievance_details** (`6`, `note`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the grievance.
* **resolution_mechanism** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Describe the resolution mechanism that was used to resolve the grievance.
* **feedback** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the resolution mechanism (Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied).
* **employee_rating** (`9`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the employee's performance (a numerical value).
* **employee_suggestion** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any improvements or changes to the employee's work or behavior.
* **supervisor_rating** (`11`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the supervisor's handling of the grievance (a numerical value).
* **supervisor_suggestion** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any improvements or changes to the supervisor's behavior or approach.
* **department** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the department where the grievance occurred.
* **team** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the team where the grievance occurred.
* **shift** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the shift that was affected by the grievance.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
- This form is confidential and will only be shared with authorized personnel.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
