# Talent Pool Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Pool Registration Form is used to collect and store information about candidates interested in job opportunities. This form helps employers and hiring managers to gather and manage candidate details for potential job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the candidate's details, such as name, contact information, and job details.
2. Provide information about your experience, skills, and availability.
3. Upload any relevant documents, such as a resume and portfolio, if applicable.
4. Add any additional information that you think is relevant to your candidacy.
5. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`): Enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Provide your contact information, such as email, phone number, and address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`): Describe the job you are applying for or the type of position you are looking for.
* **Candidate Experience** (`candidate_experience`): Share your relevant work experience, including job titles, dates, and accomplishments.
* **Available to Work** (`available_to_work`): Indicate your availability to start work.
* **Skills** (`skills`): List any relevant skills you have, including languages, tools, or software.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`): Provide your current work status, such as employed or looking for a job.
* **Resume** (`resume`): Upload your resume if you have one.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`): Upload your portfolio if you have one.
* **References** (`references`): Provide contact information for professional references.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about yourself.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or questions.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`): Upload any other relevant documents.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`): List any files you have uploaded.
* **Saved Documents** (`saved_documents`): List any saved documents.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Add any additional information that you think is relevant.
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`): Upload your resume.
* **Upload Portfolio** (`upload_portfolio`): Upload your portfolio.
* **Additional Files** (`additional_files`): Upload any additional files.
* **Uploaded Files 1** (`uploaded_files_1`): List any files you have uploaded.
* **Uploaded Files 2** (`uploaded_files_2`): List any files you have uploaded.
* **Saved Files** (`saved_files`): List any saved files.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill in as much information as possible to help us get to know you better.
