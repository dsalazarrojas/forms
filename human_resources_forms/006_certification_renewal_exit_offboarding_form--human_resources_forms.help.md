# Certification Renewal Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Certification Renewal Exit Offboarding Form is used to document the departure of an employee, ensure a smooth offboarding process, and confirm their current certifications and training status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to provide accurate information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If any tasks are assigned to you during the offboarding process, confirm that they have been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the departing employee's full name as it appears on their personnel file.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the departing employee's internal staff identifier (if applicable).
* **Last day of work** (`last_day_of_work`, date, required): Enter the employee's final working date.
* **Certifications active** (`certifications_active`, text, optional): If applicable, list the certifications the employee currently holds.
* **Renewal required** (`renewal_required`, select_one, required): Indicate whether renewal is required during offboarding.
* **Tasks completed** (`tasks_completed`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm the completion of assigned offboarding tasks. Select all that apply:
	+ Equipment returned
	+ Access revoked
	+ Knowledge transfer completed
	+ Final payroll processed
* **HR contact** (`hr_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the HR representative handling offboarding.
* **Exit Feedback** (`exit_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any optional comments about the reason for departure or offboarding process.
* **Confirmation signature** (`confirmation_signature`, text, required): The departing employee or their proxy should sign this form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

Note: Please review and submit this form carefully before finalizing the offboarding process.
