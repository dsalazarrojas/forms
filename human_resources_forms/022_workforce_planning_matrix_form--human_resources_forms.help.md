# Workforce Planning Matrix Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for workforce planning and matrix analysis to collect information from team members and stakeholders about workforce needs, priorities, and assigned tools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a department from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the job family related to your role.
3. Indicate workforce needs and priorities using the dropdown menus.
4. Enter additional notes or comments as required.
5. Enter a date and time for submission (this is optional).
6. Enter a contact email and phone number (this is optional).
7. Enter tools assigned to this project (up to 8 fields available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Select a Department
* **Select a Department**: Select the department for which you are planning workforce needs and priorities. Choose from the given options.
### 2. Select a Job Family
* **Select a Job Family**: Select the job family related to your role. Choose from the given options.
### 3. What Workforce Needs
* **What workforce needs**: Indicate whether workforce needs are high, medium, or low for this department. Choose from the given options.
### 4. What Workforce Needs?
* **What workforce needs?**: Indicate what specific workforce needs exist for this department. Choose from the given options.
### 5. What Other Workforce Needs?
* **What other workforce needs?**: Enter any additional workforce needs that are not listed in the options.
### 6. Priority 1
* **Priority 1**: Enter your highest priority number (a whole number).
### 7. Priority 2
* **Priority 2**: Enter your second-highest priority number (a whole number).
### 8. Priority 3
* **Priority 3**: Enter your third-highest priority number (a whole number).
### 9. Comments
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about this project.
### 10. Date
* **Date**: Enter a date for submission (not required).
### 11. Time
* **Time**: Enter the time for submission (not required).
### 12. Contact Email
* **Contact Email**: Enter a contact email address (not required).
### 13. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a contact phone number (not required).
### 14. Next Steps
* **Next Steps**: Enter any additional next steps or follow-up actions.
### 15. Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to this project (not required).
### 16. Other Tool
* **Other tool**: Enter any other tools assigned to this project (not required).
### 17. Tool 1
* **Tool 1**: Enter tool 1 (not required).
### 18. Tool 2
