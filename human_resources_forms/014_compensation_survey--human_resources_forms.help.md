# Compensation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Compensation Survey is a tool to help us understand what matters most to you regarding your compensation and benefits. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about employee compensation and benefits programs.

## How To Complete This Form

* To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
	+ Scroll down each page of questions
	+ Read each question carefully and understand what is being asked
	+ Enter your answers accurately and completely
	+ Submit your form when finished

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Compensation Survey --**:
	+ This is the title of the form, and it's not a question to answer.
* **employee name**:
	+ Enter your name, or leave it blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.
* **current salary band**:
	+ Select the band that best describes your current salary range.
* **satisfaction rating**:
	+ Rate your level of satisfaction, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **most important benefit**:
	+ Choose the benefit that is most important to you.
* **additional comments**:
	+ If you have any comments or suggestions regarding compensation and benefits, please enter them here.
* **willing to be contacted for follow up**:
	+ If you're willing, indicate if you'd like to be contacted by HR for further discussion.
* **submission date**:
	+ Enter the date you're submitting this form.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact HR.
* We appreciate your participation and input.
