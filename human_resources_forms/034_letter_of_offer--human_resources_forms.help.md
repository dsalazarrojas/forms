# Letter Of Offer - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Letter Of Offer form is used to document the terms of an offer to an employee, including job title, department, and offer amount, among other details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring that each field is accurately and completely filled in.
- If a field is not relevant to your specific situation, you can leave it blank.
- Ensure that you have the necessary information and approvals before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Offer Letter Details** (`offer_letter_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the offer, such as the purpose of the offer and any specific details that need to be included.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the employee to whom the offer is being extended.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the position being offered.
* **Date Of Issue** (`date_of_issue`, date, required): Enter the date on which the offer was issued.
* **Date Of Acceptance** (`date_of_acceptance`, date, required): Enter the date on which the employee accepted the offer.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the job duties and responsibilities of the position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department to which the employee will report.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): Enter the specific position title for which the employee will be working.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the manager responsible for the employee's position (only required if the position involves a manager).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter the employee's ID number or personnel number.
* **Offer Type** (`offer_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of offer being made (Offer, Rehire, or Promotion).
* **Offer Amount** (`offer_amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount of the offer.
* **Offer Date** (`offer_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the offer is valid.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the offer expires or is valid until (only required if the offer has an expiration date).
* **Offer Letter Signed** (`offer_letter_signed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the offer letter has been signed by the employee.
