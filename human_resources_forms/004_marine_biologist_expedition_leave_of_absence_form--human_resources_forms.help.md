# Marine Biologist Expedition Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a leave of absence for a marine biologist to participate in an expedition. Please fill out this form to request a leave of absence for your upcoming expedition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields (name, email, phone, start date, and end date).
3. If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the 'name' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expedition Leave Form**:
	+ (`expedition_leave_form`, `text`, required): This field is where you enter the main purpose of your leave (e.g., "Marine Biologist Expedition to study coral reef conservation").
* **Name**:
	+ (`name`, `text`, optional): This field is where you enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**:
	+ (`email`, `email`, required): This field is where you enter your official email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ (`phone`, `text`, optional): This field is where you enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Start Date**:
	+ (`start_date`, `date`, required): This field is where you enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date**:
	+ (`end_date`, `date`, required): This field is where you enter the end date of your leave.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* Submit the form well in advance of your leave to ensure timely processing.
* This form is intended for marine biologist expeditions only, please do not use for other types of leaves.
