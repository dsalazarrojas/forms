# saor_performance_appraisal_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "saor_performance_appraisal_template" form is used to facilitate a performance appraisal process for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For the "Performance Appraisal Date" field, enter the actual date of the performance appraisal.
3. Enter the name of the employee's manager in the "Manager Name" field.
4. Select the overall performance rating for the employee from the "Overall Rating" field.
5. Fill in the "Employee Name" field with the name of the employee being appraised.
6. Enter any evaluation comments in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
7. Choose the strengths of the employee that you want to mention in the "Strengths" field.
8. Identify areas of growth for the employee in the "Areas of Growth" field.
9. Provide areas for improvement for the employee in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
10. Outline development goals for the employee in the "Development Goals" field.
11. Specify the next steps or actions for the employee to take in the "Next Steps" field.
12. Enter any overall suggestion or feedback for the employee in the "Overall Suggestion" field.
