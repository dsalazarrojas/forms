# Employee Handover Documentation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the handover of employee responsibilities and tasks from one person to another. It is designed to ensure a smooth transition and minimize disruptions during the handover process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all required fields.
2. Select the correct handover status from the options provided.
3. Enter the relevant dates and times according to the required format.
4. Provide any additional handover notes as necessary.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Handover**: Enter a brief description of the handover, including the employee's role and the specific tasks being handed over.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being handed over.
* **Role**: Select the employee's role during the handover process. This will typically be their current job title.
* **Date Of Handover**: Enter the date the handover occurred or is scheduled to occur. This should be in a date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time Of Handover**: Enter the time the handover occurred or is scheduled to occur. This should be in a 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Handover Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the handover process, including any concerns or issues that need to be addressed.
* **Handover Date**: Enter the date the handover process was completed. This is the date the handover was finalized.
* **Handover Time**: Enter the time the handover process was completed. This should be in a 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Handover Status**: Select the status of the handover process. This will indicate whether the handover is completed, in progress, or not started.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager responsible for the handover process.
* **Manager Name 2**: Enter the name of the second manager responsible for the handover process.
* **Manager Name 3**: Enter the name of the third manager responsible for the handover process.
* **Team Leader Name**: Enter the name of the team leader responsible for the handover process.
* **Team Member Name**: Enter the name of the team member responsible for the handover process.

* **Handover Type**: Enter a brief description of the handover type (e.g. "Project Handover", "Task Handover", etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all information entered before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the information required, please consult with your team or manager.
* This form is designed to be completed by the person handing over their responsibilities, but any other relevant personnel may also complete it if necessary.
* Please keep the handover documentation up-to-date and accurate to ensure a smooth transition of work.
