# Employee Team Collaboration Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from team members on their experiences and observations within the team collaboration process. It will help identify areas of strength and weakness in team collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Overall Satisfaction with Team Collaboration" field and rate your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
2. In the "Collaboration Feedback" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the team collaboration process.
3. Review each of the remaining fields and answer the questions as honestly as possible. You can provide a short paragraph or sentence for each question.
   * **What skills did you observe in your team members?** (Collaboration skills)
   * **How do you feel about communication within your team?** (Feedback on communication)
   * **What challenges do you experience in collaborating with your team?** (Collaboration challenges)
   * **What tools have you used for collaboration?** (Collaboration tools)
   * **What environment fosters the best collaboration?** (Collaboration environment)
4. If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please add them to the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do your team members work together?** (`how_do_your_team_members_work_together`)
	+ This field is for you to describe the overall dynamic of your team members' interactions.
* **What skills did you observe in your team members?** (`collaboration_skills`)
	+ This field is for you to identify any skills or behaviors you have observed in your team members that promote collaboration.
* **How do you feel about communication within your team?** (`feedback_on_communication`)
	+ This field is for you to express your feelings about the communication within your team.
* **What tools have you used for collaboration?** (`collaboration_tools`)
	+ This field is for you to list any collaboration tools you have used in your team.
* **What challenges do you experience in collaborating with your team?** (`collaboration_challenges`)
	+ This field is for you to describe any challenges you face while working with your team.
* **How do you feel about time management within your team?** (`feedback_on_time_management`)
	+ This field is for you to express your feelings about time management within your team.
* **What advice would you give to improve collaboration?** (`collaboration_advice`)
	+ This field is for you to provide suggestions on how to improve team collaboration.
* **What environment fosters the best collaboration?** (`collaboration_environment`)
	+ This field is for you to describe the environment that promotes the best collaboration.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Team Collaboration** (`overall_satisfaction`)
	+ This field is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with team collaboration on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`)
	+ This field is for you to provide any other comments or feedback about the team collaboration process.

## Tips

* Be honest and specific when answering the questions.
* Provide examples or concrete experiences to support your answers.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please add them to the "Additional Feedback" field.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
