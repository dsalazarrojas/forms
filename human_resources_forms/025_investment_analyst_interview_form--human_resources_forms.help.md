# Investment Analyst Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Analyst Interview Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from interviewers on the performance of investment analyst candidates. This form helps assess the candidate's skills, provides space for comments and recommendations, and ensures that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Candidate Name** field with the name of the candidate being interviewed.
2. Enter the **Interviewer**'s name in the corresponding field.
3. Select the date of the interview.
4. Enter a rating (1-5) for the candidate's performance in the **Rating** field.
5. Provide technical feedback in the **Technical Feedback** field (optional).
6. Provide behavioral feedback in the **Behavioral Feedback** field (optional).
7. Offer a recommendation for the candidate's future prospects in the **Recommendation** field (optional).
8. Enter any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field (optional).
9. If applicable, select up to three **Technical Skills** that the candidate has demonstrated.
10. If applicable, select up to three **Behavioral Skills** that the candidate has demonstrated.
11. Enter the candidate's **Email** address.
12. Enter the candidate's phone number.
13. If applicable, estimate the **Time Spent** during the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Candidate Name (text, required: false)
 Enter the name of the candidate being interviewed.

### Interviewer (text, required: true)
Enter your name as the interviewer.

### Date (date, required: true)
Select the date of the interview.

### Rating (number, required: true)
Enter a rating (1-5) for the candidate's performance.

### Technical Feedback (text, required: false)
Provide any technical feedback or comments about the candidate's performance (optional).

### Behavioral Feedback (text, required: false)
Provide any behavioral feedback or comments about the candidate's performance (optional).

### Recommendation (text, required: false)
Offer a recommendation for the candidate's future prospects (optional).

### Additional Comments (text, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or feedback about the candidate's performance (optional).

### Rating 2 (number, required: false)
Enter another rating (1-5) for the candidate's performance (optional).

### Rating 3 (number, required: false)
Enter yet another rating (1-5) for the candidate's performance (optional).

### Technical Skills (select multiple, required: false)
Select up to three technical skills that the candidate demonstrated during the interview.

### Behavioral Skills (select multiple, required: false)
Select up to three behavioral skills that the candidate demonstrated during the interview.

### Email (email, required: true)
Enter the candidate's email address.

### Phone (text, required: true)
Enter the candidate's phone number.

### Time Spent (time, required: false)
Estimate the time spent during the interview (optional).

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in to complete the form.
*   Be concise and specific when providing feedback and ratings.
*   Use the **Technical Skills** and **Behavioral Skills** fields to highlight the candidate's strengths.
*   Enter accurate contact information (email and phone number) for follow-up purposes.
*   Consider providing feedback and ratings for each skill or section where applicable.
*   Enter a rating and feedback for each rating field (if applicable).
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
