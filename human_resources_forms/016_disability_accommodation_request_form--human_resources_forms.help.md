# Disability Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting disability accommodations at work. It is used to submit requests for modifications or adjustments to your workspace to accommodate your needs as a disabled employee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of accommodation you are requesting.
2. Provide your employee information.
3. Choose the accommodation type(s) you need.
4. Choose where you would like to work (if applicable).
5. Select the dates you would like the accommodation to start and end.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.
7. Finally, confirm the status of your accommodation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of accommodation you are requesting, such as "Physical Accommodation" or "Mental Health Accommodation".
* **Employee Information** (Text, Optional): This field is for providing your employee information, such as your name, department, or job title.
* **Accommodation Type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the specific accommodation type(s) you need, such as "Modified Work Schedule" or "Modified Workstation".
* **Work Location** (Select One, Optional): If applicable, choose where you would like to work, such as "Home Office" or "Shared Space".
* **Date of Request** (Date, Optional): Select the date you are making the accommodation request.
* **Start Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you would like the accommodation to start.
* **End Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you would like the accommodation to end.
* **Employee Notes** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Accommodation Status** (Select One, Optional): Confirm the status of your accommodation request, such as "Approved" or "Denied".
