# Employee Grooming Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Grooming Assessment Form is used to evaluate employees' adherence to company grooming standards and policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. The form is divided into several sections, which are explained in detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Policies** (`company_policies`, text, required): Please provide a brief description or examples of any company policies you think are relevant to the assessment. This can include anything from dress code to personal hygiene practices.
* **Employee Grooming Standards** (`employee_grooming_standards`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the employee is meeting the expected standards, or 'No' if not.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the employee being assessed.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select the department of the employee being assessed. This may be relevant to specific policies or standards for that department.
* **Date Assessed** (`date_assessed`, date, required): Enter the date the assessment was completed.
* **Time Assessed** (`time_assessed`, time, required): Enter the time the assessment was completed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback regarding the assessment.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address of the employee being assessed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the employee being assessed.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter the employee ID of the employee being assessed.
* **Employee ID Hints** (`employee_id_hints`, text, required): Enter any hints or comments that may aid in identification of the employee being assessed.
* (Fields 13-24 are not used in this template, please ignore)

## Tips

* Make sure to be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
* Review the employee's current state of grooming and policy adherence before completing the form.
* Use examples or descriptions to illustrate points where the employee may not be meeting standards.
* Provide feedback and guidance on areas for improvement.
