# Discrimination Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discrimination Report Form is a document used to report incidents of discrimination within the organization. This form helps to gather information about the incident and provide a formal channel for reporting such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Choose the type of report you are filing (e.g., "Harassment", "Retaliation", etc.)
* Enter the date of the incident
* Provide the time of the incident
* Enter your name as the person reporting the incident
* Enter the name of the individual or group to whom the report is being made (e.g., manager, supervisor, HR representative)
* Provide a brief description of the incident
* Enter the location of the incident
* Provide details about the incident
* List any witnesses to the incident
* Describe the witness's relationship to you (e.g., coworker, supervisor, customer)
* Select the status of the incident (e.g., "Open", "Closed", etc.)
* Enter the department to which the report is being made
* Outline the plan to resolve the incident
* Select the status of the resolution plan
* Enter the name of the officer or representative to whom the report is being made

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Type** (`Report Type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of report you are filing (e.g., "Harassment", "Retaliation", etc.).
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Report Time** (`report_time`, time, not required): Provide the time of the incident.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, not required): Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported To** (`reported_to`, text, not required): Enter the name of the individual or group to whom the report is being made (e.g., manager, supervisor, HR representative).
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, not required): Provide details about the incident.
* **Witness Names** (`witness_names`, text, not required): List any witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Relationship** (`witness_relationship`, text, not required): Describe the witness's relationship to you (e.g., coworker, supervisor, customer).
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the incident (e.g., "Open", "Closed", etc.).
* **Reported To Department** (`reported_to_department`, text, not required): Enter the department to which the report is being made.
* **Resolution Plan** (`resolution_plan`, text, not required): Outline the plan to resolve the incident.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the resolution plan (e.g., "In Progress", "Completed", etc.).
* **Reported To Officer** (`reported_to_officer`, text, not required): Enter the name of the officer or representative to whom the report is being made.
