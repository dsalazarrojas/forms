# Employee Engagement Platform Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from employees on the Employee Engagement Platform, which helps the company identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance employee satisfaction and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form honestly and thoroughly, providing detailed and specific feedback where possible.
- Answer each question based on your own experiences and opinions as an employee.
- You may choose to provide answers for the optional fields, but it's not mandatory.
- You can select one or multiple options for the Feedback questions field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Score (1)**:
	* A numerical score that represents your overall feedback on the Employee Engagement Platform. Enter a number between 0 and 100.
* **Satisfaction (2)**:
	* A numerical score that indicates your level of satisfaction with the Employee Engagement Platform. Enter a number between 0 and 100.
* **Engagement (3)**:
	* A numerical score that reflects your engagement level with the Employee Engagement Platform. Enter a number between 0 and 100.
* **Communication (4)**:
	+ Select one of the options: Very good, Good, Neutral, Bad, or Very Bad.
	+ This question helps gauge the effectiveness of internal communication within the company.
* **Feedback Questions (5)**:
	+ Select one or multiple options from the list of questions.
	+ Provide your honest feedback on the questions below:
		+ Q1: How do you feel about the overall company culture?
		+ Q2: How do you rate the manager's communication style?
		+ Q3: How do you rate the company's communication style?
		+ Q4: How do you rate the company's feedback style?
		+ Q5: How do you rate the company's feedback style?

* **Date (6)**:
	* Enter the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time (7)**:
	+ This field is likely not relevant in most cases.
	+ Leave it empty or fill out if you think it's necessary.
* **Note (8)**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
	+ This field is optional but encouraged for more detailed input.
