# HR Professional Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Professional Certification Registration Form is a form used to collect information from HR professionals to verify their certification and expertise in the field. This form helps in maintaining a database of certified HR professionals for future reference and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Choose the correct answer for the certification status.
4. Select multiple options for your areas of interest.
5. Fill in the Company details if you are representing a company.
6. Enter your first and last name as required by the employer.
7. Choose the correct answers for company-related questions.
8. Fill in your email address and phone number for company contact purposes.
9. Choose the correct answers for the company-specific questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address for contact purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.

### Certification
* **Certification** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Choose your certification status from the provided options.

### Interests
* **Interests** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options from the list of areas you are interested in.

### Company Information
* **Company First Name** (`first_name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Company Last Name** (`last_name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the company you represent.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Company Date** (`date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of establishment of the company.
* **Company Time** (`time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of establishment of the company.
* **Notes** (`note_2`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the company.

### Contact Information
* **First Name 3** (`first_name_3`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name 3** (`last_name_3`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for contact purposes.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Select One 4** (`select_one_4`, `select_one`, required): Choose your certification status from the provided options.
* **Select Multiple 4** (`select_multiple_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options from the list of areas you are interested in.

### Additional Information
* **Select One 3** (`select_one_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your certification status from the provided options.
* **Location** (`select_multiple_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options from the list of areas you are interested in.
* **Notes** (`note_2`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your certification.
