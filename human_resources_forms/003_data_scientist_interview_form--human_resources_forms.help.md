# data_scientist_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from data scientists for potential job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field according to your qualifications and availability.
2. Be honest and accurate when entering your information.
3. Make sure to review all fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Attach or copy and paste your resume or a brief description of your experience.
* **Data Science Experience** (`data_science_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years of experience you have in data science.
* **Programming Languages** (`programming_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Select all programming languages you are proficient in (e.g. Python, R, SQL, Julia).
* **Programming Languages List** (`programming_languages_list`, text, optional): Enter any other programming languages you are proficient in that are not listed.
* **Data Science Skills** (`data_science_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all data science skills you possess (e.g. Data Visualization, Machine Learning, Data Mining, Statistical Analysis).
* **Data Science Skills List** (`data_science_skills_list`, text, optional): Enter any other data science skills you possess that are not listed.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Describe your education background.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your work experience relevant to data science.
* **Project Experience** (`project_experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant project experience you have.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select all certifications you hold (e.g. Certified Data Scientist, Certified Data Architect).
* **Certifications List** (`certifications_list`, text, optional): Enter any other certifications you hold that are not listed.
* **References** (`references`, select_one, optional): Select the type of reference you would like to provide (e.g. Peer, Manager, Mentor).
* **References List** (`references_list`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your selected reference.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date you are looking for.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date you are looking for.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, time, optional): Enter the amount of hours you are willing to work per week.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter your desired salary.
* **Salary Currency** (`salary_currency`, select_one, optional): Select the currency of your desired salary (e.g. USD, EUR, JPY, CNY).
* **Salary Frequency** (`salary_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you would like to receive your salary (e.g. Monthly, Yearly, Hourly, Daily).
* **Salary Frequency Other** (`salary_frequency_other`, text, optional): Enter any other details about your desired salary frequency.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Double-check all fields before submitting.
* Make sure to proofread your responses for spelling and grammar errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
