# Training Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Training Leave Time Off Form is used to track and manage employee training activities, leaves, and related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the relevant details for your training activity.
2. Ensure all required fields (indicated by a star (*) if marked as such in the field explanation below) have accurate and complete information.
3. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Leave Reason** (`training_leave_reason`): Enter the purpose or justification for the training activity.
* **Training Leave Dates** (`training_leave_dates`): Provide the start and end dates of the training activity. Please use the date format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`): Enter the location of the training activity.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`): Describe the type of training or activity (e.g., workshop, conference, online course).
* **Training Agenda** (`training_agenda`): Briefly outline the agenda or schedule of the training activity.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name, if you are the employee submitting the form.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`): If applicable, enter the name of your reporting manager, if different from your immediate supervisor.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`): Enter your department name, if applicable.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`): Select the current status of the training activity (Active, Pending, Declined).
* **Approval Level** (`approval_level`): Select the levels that are authorized to approve the training activity (Manager, HR, Director).
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`): Enter the number of hours devoted to the training activity.
* **Training Attendance** (`training_attendance`): Enter the number of attendees, if applicable.
* **Training Cost** (`training_cost`): Enter any associated cost with the training activity.
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`): Provide additional comments or notes regarding the training activity.
* **Training Approvals** (`training_approvals`): Select the individuals or groups authorized to approve the training activity (Manager, Director, HR, Employee, Department, Department Manager).
* **Training Form Submitted By** (`training_form_submitted_by`): Select the individual or group that submitted the form (John, Jane, Bob, Alice, Jim).
* **Training Form Submitted Date** (`training_form_submitted_date`): Enter the date the form was submitted in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.

## Tips
- Ensure all dates are in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- Use the correct format for numbers (e.g., 2 for numbers, not 'two').
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.

Note that the form is designed to be completed with the most relevant and accurate information possible. If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
