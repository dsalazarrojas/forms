# mid_year_performance_review_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mid-year performance review form is a tool for evaluating employee performance, identifying areas for growth, setting future goals, and documenting feedback and action items. It's a crucial part of the performance review process, ensuring a fair and thorough assessment of an employee's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Gather all necessary information**: Before starting the form, ensure you have all the required information and approvals from your manager or HR department.
2. **Fill out the employee information section**: Complete the employee's name, job title, and employee ID in the "Employee Information" section.
3. **Rate the employee's performance**: Enter a numerical score (1-5) in the "Current Performance Score" section, representing the employee's overall performance.
4. **Identify strengths and development areas**: In the "Strengths" and "Development Areas" sections, describe the employee's strengths and areas where they need to improve.
5. **Set future goals**: Outline the employee's goals for the next six months in the "Goals for the next 6 months" section.
6. **Provide recommendations for professional development**: Suggest areas where the employee can improve or develop their skills in the "Recommendations for Professional Development" section.
7. **Exchange feedback**: Share your feedback and observations with the employee in the "Feedback from Employee" and "Feedback from Manager" sections.
8. **Create action items**: List any action items or tasks that the manager needs to take or assign to the employee in the "Action Items for the Manager" section.
9. **Schedule the next review**: Enter the date for the next performance review in the "Next Review Date" section.
10. **Submit the form**: Review and submit the form, ensuring that all sections are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Information

* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their personnel record.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title as it appears in the organization's records.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number as it appears on their personnel record.

### Current Performance Score (1-5)

* Rate the employee's overall performance on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Exceeds Expectations".

### Strengths

* Describe the employee's strengths, including their technical skills, soft skills, and areas where they excel.

### Development Areas

* Describe the areas where the employee needs to improve or develop their skills.

### Goals for the next 6 months

* Outline the employee's goals and objectives for the next six months, including specific targets and deadlines.

### Recommendations for Professional Development

* Suggest areas where the employee can improve or develop their skills, including training, education, or mentorship opportunities.

### Feedback from Employee

* Provide feedback and observations about the employee's performance from their perspective.

### Feedback from Manager

* Provide feedback and observations about the employee's performance from your perspective as their manager.

### Action Items

* List any action items or tasks that you need to take or assign to the employee.

### Next Review Date

* Enter the date for the next performance review, ideally six months from the current review date.

### Overall Performance Review Status

* Select the overall status of the performance review as "Complete", "Incomplete", or "Pending".
