# Crisis Response Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information for a crisis response leave, which is a temporary leave of absence from work due to an unexpected or emergency situation affecting an individual or their loved ones. This form will guide you through the steps to provide necessary details for your leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out your emergency contact information, if required.
- Provide the reason for your leave, if required.
- Select the start and end dates of your leave.
- Choose the duration of your leave from the options.
- Select the type of leave you are requesting.
- Choose whether your emergency status, employee status, and manager status are active or inactive, if relevant.
- Add any additional notes about your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (emergency_contact_info)** (`emergency_contact_info`, Required/Optional): Enter your contact information if your emergency contact is not the primary contact for your leave request.
* **Reason for Leave (reason_for_leave)** (`reason_for_leave`, Optional): Briefly describe the reason for your leave, which will help us understand the nature of your request.
* **Start Date (start_date)** (`start_date`, Optional): Enter the date when you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date (end_date)** (`end_date`, Optional): Enter the date when you plan to return from your leave.
* **Return Date (return_date)** (`return_date`, Optional): Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
* **Leave Duration (leave_duration)** (`leave_duration`, Required): Select the duration of your leave from 1-2 days, 3-5 days, or More than 5 days.
* **Leave Type (leave_type)** (`leave_type`, Required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting, which can be Emergency, Personal, Family, or Other.
* **Emergency Status (emergency_status)** (`emergency_status`, Optional): If you have an emergency contact, indicate whether this contact is active or inactive.
* **Employee Status (employee_status)** (`employee_status`, Optional): Check whether you are an active or inactive employee during this leave period.
* **Manager Status (manager_status)** (`manager_status`, Optional): Check whether your manager is active or inactive during this leave period.
* **Notes (notes)** (`notes`, Optional): Add any additional details or comments about your leave request.
