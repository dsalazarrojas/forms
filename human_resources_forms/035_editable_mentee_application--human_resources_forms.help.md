# Editable Mentee Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Editable Mentee Application form is designed to provide mentors with more information about the mentee's background and skills to have a more informed conversation during their mentorship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Enter the mentee's information in the designated fields.
3. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: First Name (id: 1)
• **First Name**: Enter the mentee's first name as it is typically written on official documents or records.
### Page 1: Last Name (id: 2)
• **Last Name**: Enter the mentee's last name as it is typically written on official documents or records.
### Page 1: Email (id: 3)
• **Email**: Enter the mentee's email address. This will be used for communication purposes with their mentor.
### Page 1: Phone Number (id: 4)
• **Phone Number**: Enter the mentee's phone number for communication purposes with their mentor.
### Page 1: Date of Birth (id: 5)
• **Date of Birth**: Enter the mentee's date of birth for age verification.
### Page 1: Select 1 (id: 6)
• **select 1**: Select one of the options for a specific reason related to the mentee's background or skills. Please choose the option that best describes you.
### Page 1: Select 2 (id: 7)
• **Select 2**: Select all that apply from the options for a specific reason related to the mentee's background or skills. Please choose the options that best describe you.
### Page 2: Time (id: 8)
• **Time**: Enter the mentee's preferred time for communication with their mentor. Please be as specific as possible.
### Page 2: Note (id: 9)
• **Note**: This field is for any additional information the mentee wants to share with their mentor. Please be brief and clear in your description.
### Page 2: Select 3 (id: 10)
• **Select 3**: Select one of the options for a specific reason related to the mentee's background or skills. Please choose the option that best describes you.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* The information collected will only be used for the purpose of the mentorship.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your mentor for clarification.
