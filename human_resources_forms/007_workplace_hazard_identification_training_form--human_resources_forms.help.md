# Workplace Hazard Identification Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Hazard Identification Training Form is designed to collect information about workplace hazards, their identification, and procedures for reporting and controlling them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the text field "Workplace Hazard Form Input" with relevant information about the hazard.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Workplace Hazard Identification" to confirm whether you have identified a hazard.
3. Input the reporting procedure for the identified hazard in the "Reporting Process" field.
4. Describe the control measures in place to mitigate or remove the hazard in the "Workplace Hazard Control Measures" field.
5. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Information" field.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Reporting Procedure" to indicate if there is a procedure in place.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Reporting Frequency" to indicate if reporting is done regularly.
8. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Reporting Responsible Person" to specify if a person is responsible for reporting.
9. Input the method of reporting the hazard in the "Reporting Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Hazard Form Input**: A text field where you can input any relevant information about the hazard.
* **Workplace Hazard Identification**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm whether you have identified a workplace hazard.
* **Reporting Process**: Input the procedure for reporting the hazard.
* **Workplace Hazard Control Measures**: Describe the control measures in place to mitigate or remove the hazard.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Input any emergency contact information related to the hazard.
* **Reporting Procedure**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if there is a procedure in place for reporting hazards.
* **Reporting Frequency**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if reporting is done regularly.
* **Reporting Responsible Person**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to specify if a person is responsible for reporting.
* **Reporting Method**: Input the method of reporting the hazard.

## Tips
* Always input accurate and clear information.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have followed the reporting procedure and frequency for your workplace hazard.
