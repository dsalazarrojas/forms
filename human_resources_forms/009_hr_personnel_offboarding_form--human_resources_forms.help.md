# HR Personnel Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Personnel Offboarding Form is used to document an employee's departure from the company, including their last day worked, reason for leaving, and any company property returns. This form helps maintain accurate records and facilitates a smooth transition for both the departing employee and the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Provide the employee's job title.
3. State the reason for the employee's departure in the "reason_for_leave" field.
4. Enter the date the employee last worked for the company in the "last_day_worked" field.
5. Indicate whether the employee will return any company property in the "company_property_returns" field. Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
6. Provide any relevant contact information for the departing employee in the "contact_info" field.
7. Describe the next steps the organization needs to take for a smooth transition in the "next_steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who is offboarding.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title while they were with the company.
* **Reason for Leave**: State a brief reason for the employee's departure (e.g., resignation, retirement, termination).
* **Last Day Worked**: Enter the date the employee last worked for the company.
* **Company Property Returns**: Indicate whether the employee will return any company property (e.g., laptop, phone, equipment).
* **Contact Info**: Provide any relevant contact information for the departing employee (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps the organization needs to take for a smooth transition (e.g., updating HR systems, transferring equipment, or coordinating with other teams).

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth transition for both the employee and the organization.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the HR department for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
