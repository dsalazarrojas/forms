# Best HR Management Tool Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best HR Management Tool Nomination Form is a tool for identifying and evaluating potential HR management tools for consideration in our HR software repository.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your legal full name and email address in the Personal Information section.
2. Select a relevant date for your nomination.
3. Choose the primary selection that best applies to your nomination.
4. Provide any additional information about your nomination, if necessary.
5. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
6. Estimate the value of your nomination, if applicable.
7. Enter a preferred time for scheduling, if necessary.
8. Add any final comments or remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`Full name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name, as it will be used for contact and reference purposes.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for contact and communication.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, `text`, optional): Include your country code if you are outside of our local region.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Select the date relevant to your nomination.
* **Primary selection** (`Primary selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best applies to your nomination from the available options.
* **Quantity or rating** (`Quantity or rating`, `number`, optional): Enter a whole number for quantity or rating, if applicable.
* **Additional details** (`Additional details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about your nomination.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any of the options that apply to your nomination.
* **Estimated value** (`Estimated value`, `number`, optional): Use decimal format when providing an estimated value, if applicable.
* **Preferred time** (`Preferred time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time for scheduling, if necessary.
* **Final comments or notes** (`Final comments or notes`, `text`, optional): Add any final remarks or comments about your nomination.
