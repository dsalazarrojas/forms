# Performance Review Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to check in with their performance review progress. It is essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth performance review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct pages that apply to your performance review by choosing "Yes" from the list of options.
2. On "Page 2", select a date for your performance review progress check-in.
3. On "Page 3", provide feedback about your performance review progress in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1
* **Page 1** (`Select Multiple`, **Yes/No**): This page allows you to select which pages of the performance review you are currently working on. Please choose "Yes" for each page that applies to your progress.

### Page 2
* **Page 2** (`Date`, **Required**): Select a date for your performance review progress check-in. This is a required field, so please make sure to select a valid date.

### Page 3
* **Page 3** (`Text`, **Required**): Provide feedback about your performance review progress in this text field. Please keep your feedback concise and accurate.
