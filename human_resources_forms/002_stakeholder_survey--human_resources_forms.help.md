# Stakeholder Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from stakeholders on areas of improvement for a project, team, or organization. It helps to gather information on their perceptions and suggestions on what can be improved for better performance and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the stakeholder in the `stakeholder_name` field.
2. Select the department that this feedback belongs to in the `department` field (if applicable).
3. Answer the first question `What are the most important areas of improvement?` by selecting one of the options (Leadership and management, Communication and collaboration, Performance and productivity, or Other).
4. If you have any other areas of improvement not mentioned, please select them in the `Are there any other areas of improvement that are not mentioned in the above questions?` field.
5. Enter any other areas of improvement not mentioned in the previous question in the `Name 2` field.
6. Enter the stakeholder's email address in the `email` field.
7. Enter the stakeholder's phone number in the `phone` field.
8. Enter the date in the `date` field.
9. Enter the time in the `time` field.
10. Answer the third question `How likely are you to provide feedback on these areas of improvement?` by selecting High or Low.
11. Answer the fourth question `How likely are you to suggest solutions for these areas of improvement?` by selecting High or Low.
12. Enter your name in the `Name 3` field.
13. Enter your phone number in the `Phone 2` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stakeholder Survey** (Note): This is an introduction page to the form.
* **stakeholder_name** (Text): Enter the name of the stakeholder.
* **department** (Text): Select the department that this feedback belongs to.
* **What are the most important areas of improvement?** (Select One): Choose one of the areas of improvement.
* **Are there any other areas of improvement that are not mentioned in the above questions?** (Select Multiple): Select any other areas of improvement not mentioned in the previous question.
* **Name 2** (Text): Enter any other areas of improvement not mentioned in the previous question.
* **email** (Email): Enter the stakeholder's email address.
* **phone** (Text): Enter the stakeholder's phone number.
* **date** (Date): Enter the date.
* **time** (Time): Enter the time.
* **How likely are you to provide feedback on these areas of improvement?** (Select One): Choose how likely you are to provide feedback.
* **How likely are you to suggest solutions for these areas of improvement?** (Select One): Choose how likely you are to suggest solutions.
* **Name 3** (Text): Enter your name.
* **Phone 2** (Text): Enter your phone number.

Please ensure all fields are filled correctly to provide accurate feedback and suggestions for improvement.
