# Employee Emergency Contact Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update or replace an employee's emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the employee's emergency contact.
2. Select the relationship between the employee and the emergency contact.
3. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
4. Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
5. Enter the address of the emergency contact.
6. Enter the city of the emergency contact.
7. Enter the state of the emergency contact.
8. Enter the country of the emergency contact.
9. Enter the zip code of the emergency contact.
10. Select how you know the employee (optional).
