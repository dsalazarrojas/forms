# Employee Competency Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Competency Verification Form Template is a tool used by Human Resources to gather information from employees regarding their job title, years of experience, job type, education details, skills and qualifications, job references, job satisfaction, date of birth, and email.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter your job title as it is currently used in the company.
- Provide a clear and concise description of your current job responsibilities.
- Enter your years of experience in the industry or company.
- Select the category that best describes your job type.
- Provide detailed information about your education and qualifications.
- List any relevant skills and qualifications you possess.
- Specify the names and titles of individuals who can vouch for your work performance.
- Rate your job satisfaction from 1 to 5.
- Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- Enter your current email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title as it is used in the company.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of your current job responsibilities.
- **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter your years of experience in the industry or company.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your job type. Options include: Human Resources, Marketing, Sales, Finance, Operations.
- **Education Details** (`education_details`, text, optional): Provide detailed information about your education and qualifications.
- **Skills and Qualifications** (`skills_and_qualifications`, text, optional): List any relevant skills and qualifications you possess.
- **Job References** (`job_references`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the names and titles of individuals who can vouch for your work performance. Options include: Manager, Supervisor, Colleague, Client, None.
- **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your job satisfaction from 1 to 5.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your current email address.

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest in your responses.
- Use clear and concise language for job title, job description, education details, skills and qualifications, and job references.
- If unsure about a field, refer to your personnel file or HR representative for guidance.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
