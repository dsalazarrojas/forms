# IT Industry Crisis Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the process of requesting and managing leave of absence in the IT industry. It's essential to fill this form correctly to ensure that leave requests are processed promptly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and approvals before submitting the form.
2. Select the correct leave type (Emergency, Family, Personal, Medical, or Vacation).
3. Enter the accurate start and end dates of your leave period.
4. Provide a valid reason for your leave.
5. Enter your manager's name and contact information.
6. Specify your department, position, and location (if applicable).
7. Confirm your leave duration.
8. Ensure that your manager approves or declines the leave request.
9. Add any additional comments or comments from the manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, **required**): Enter your employee ID as a number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date when your leave will start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date when your leave will end.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, **required**): Briefly describe the reason for your leave.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your manager's name as it appears on their official records.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, specify your department.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, specify your position.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, specify the company you are currently working for.
* **Manager Department** (`manager_department`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, specify your manager's department.
* **Manager Position** (`manager_position`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, specify your manager's position.
* **Company Location** (`company_location`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, specify the company location.
* **Employee Location** (`employee_location`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, specify your location.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, `number`, **required**): Enter the duration of your leave in days or hours.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Emergency, Family, Personal, Medical, or Vacation).
* **Leave Start Time** (`leave_start_time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the start time of your leave.
* **Leave End Time** (`leave_end_time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the end time of your leave.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm your current status (Active, Inactive, Leave of Absence, Resigned, or Terminated).
* **Leave Requestor** (`leave_requestor`, `select_one`, **required**): Select who is requesting the leave (IT Team, Manager, Self, or HR).
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, specify the team lead's name.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, `select_one`, **optional**): Specify your manager's approval status (Approved, Declined, Pending, or Not Applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or comments from your manager.
