# Background Investigation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Background Investigation Form is used to gather comprehensive and sensitive information from job applicants for high-security positions or comprehensive screenings. It is essential to ensure that all fields are filled accurately and honestly to facilitate a smooth and efficient hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your full name, including any suffixes (e.g., Jr., Sr., or II).
2. Enter your primary phone number that we may use to contact you or verify information.
3. For fields marked as `required`, ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearances before proceeding.
4. Be honest and accurate when providing your personal identifiers, educational institutions, professional employment history, and other relevant information.
5. If you have any doubts or uncertainties, consult with the designated personnel for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subject Full Name HR**: Enter your full name, including any suffixes (e.g., Jr., Sr., or II).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number that we may use to contact you or verify information.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number, which is a required field for database searches.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth, which is a required field for age verification purposes.
* **Driver's License Number and State**: Enter your driver's license number and state of issuance.
* **Full List of Previous Residences**: Provide a comprehensive list of your previous residences, including dates of residency.
* **Educational Institutions and Degrees**: List all your educational institutions attended and degrees earned.
* **Professional Employment History (Last 7 Years)**: Provide a summary of your professional employment history for the last 7 years, including company, title, and supervisor.
* **List Three Character References**: Enter the names, phone numbers, and email addresses of three character references.
* **Criminal History Disclosure**: Briefly describe any convictions or pending cases.
* **Credit Check Authorization**: Select 'I authorize' if you consent to a consumer credit report for employment purposes; otherwise, choose 'Not applicable.'
* **Full Authorization for Background Investigation**: Choose 'I consent and certify' to confirm that all provided information is accurate; otherwise, select 'Other.'
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date of your consent for the background investigation.

Note: Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearances before completing the form, and be honest and accurate when providing information. If you're unsure about any field, consult with the designated personnel for guidance.
