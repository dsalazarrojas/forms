# security_employment_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Security Employment Application Form" is a form designed to collect information from users applying for security employment. This form is used to gather user-provided data for the purpose of evaluating their eligibility for security roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Select the correct options for the skills and security clearance fields.
3. Upload your resume as requested.
4. Review your completed form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant user information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, such as phone number and email.
* **Employment Details** (`employment_details`, text, optional): Enter your current employment details, if applicable.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your work experience, if applicable.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant skills you have.
* **Security Clearance** (`security_clearance`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have security clearance or not.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Enter the names of your references.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, text, optional): Upload your resume as requested.
* **Employment Certification** (`employment_certification`, text, optional): Enter your employment certification information, if applicable.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter your educational qualifications, if applicable.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional personal details you feel are relevant.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click the submit button to complete your application.
