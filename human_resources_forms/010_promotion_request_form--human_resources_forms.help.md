# promotion_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotion request form is used by employees to submit a formal request for promotion. This form gathers information about the employee and the promotion they are seeking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant personal details as instructed.
* **Promotion Details** (`promotion_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the promotion you are requesting, including any relevant job responsibilities and requirements.
* **New Job Title** (`new_job_title`, `select_one`, optional): Select your proposed new job title from the list provided.
* **Supervisor Details** (`supervisor_details`, `text`, optional): If you have a supervisor, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about yourself and your promotion request.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are eligible for a promotion before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the information required, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your promotion request is processed correctly.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence, please attach them separately.
* Once submitted, this form cannot be edited or deleted. If you need to make changes, please contact the form owner.
* Please review the submission guidelines for more information on what to expect after submitting this form.
