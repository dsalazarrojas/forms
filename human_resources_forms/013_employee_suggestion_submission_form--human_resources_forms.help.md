# Employee Suggestion Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect suggestions from employees on ways to improve the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Form" field to enter your suggestion.
2. Select the category for your suggestion (Internal Improvement or External Improvement).
3. Provide a detailed description of your suggestion in the "Suggestion Details" field.
4. Choose the priority level of your suggestion (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Select who this suggestion is for (Manager, Supervisor, or Department).
6. Choose the department where the suggestion is relevant (Human Resources, Operations, or IT).
7. Submit your suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form** (`employee_suggestion_submission_form`): This is the main field where you can enter your suggestion.
	+ Please provide a clear and concise description of your idea.
	+ Use this field to explain your suggestion in as much detail as possible.
* **Suggested by** (`suggested_by`): This field is used to indicate who submitted the suggestion.
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your name tag or ID badge.
* **Submission Category** (`suggestion_category`): This field categorizes the type of suggestion.
	+ Select Internal Improvement or External Improvement to indicate the nature of your suggestion.
* **Suggestion Details** (`suggestion_details`): This field provides a detailed explanation of your suggestion.
	+ Please provide as much detail as possible to help the organization understand your idea.
* **Suggested Priority** (`suggested_priority`): This field indicates the level of urgency and importance of your suggestion.
	+ Select High, Medium, or Low to help the organization prioritize your submission.
* **Suggested by Employee** (`suggested_by_employee`): This field indicates the status of the employee who submitted the suggestion.
	+ Select Manager, Supervisor, or Employee to indicate who submitted the suggestion.
* **Suggested by Department** (`suggested_by_department`): This field indicates the department where the suggestion is relevant.
	+ Select Human Resources, Operations, or IT to indicate which department the suggestion is for.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): This field indicates the date the suggestion was submitted.
	+ This field will be automatically populated once you submit your suggestion.
* **Suggested Time** (`suggested_time`): This field indicates the time the suggestion was submitted.
	+ This field will be automatically populated once you submit your suggestion.
* **Submitted For** (`submitted_for`): This field indicates the status of the suggestion.
	+ Select Manager, Supervisor, or Department to indicate where the suggestion is submitted.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): This field indicates the status of the suggestion.
	+ Select Pending, Approved, or Rejected to indicate the status of your submission.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise submissions to ensure they are taken into account.
* Do not submit duplicate suggestions on the same issue.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the submission process, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
