# job_role_overview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job role overview form is used to capture detailed information about a job role, including its description, responsibilities, expectations, and various attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the job role.
2. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin.
3. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
4. Once you've completed all the fields, click "Submit" to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the job role.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, `text`, required): List the key responsibilities of the job role.
* **Job Expectations** (`job_expectations`, `text`, required): Describe the expectations of the job role.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`, required): Enter the physical location where the job role is performed.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of job (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Intern).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the job (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of the job (e.g. Junior, Senior, Manager).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the category of the job (e.g. IT, Marketing, Sales).
* **Job Family** (`job_family`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the family of the job (e.g. IT-Software, Marketing-Advertising).
* **Job Subcategory** (`job_subcategory`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the subcategory of the job (e.g. IT-Software-Development).
* **Job Tags** (`job_tags`, `select_multiple`, required): Select relevant tags for the job (e.g. #jobopening, #jobsearch).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the job.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the job.
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours worked per week.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, `number`, required): Enter the salary of the job.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required): Enter the physical location of the job.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the job.
* **Job Contact** (`job_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information for the job.
* **Job Email** (`job_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address for the job.
* **Job Phone** (`job_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number for the job.
* **Job URL** (`job_url`, `text`, optional): Enter a URL associated with the job.
* **Job Employer** (`job_employer`, `text`, required): Enter the employer of the job.
* **Job Code** (`job_code`, `text`, required): Enter a unique code for the job.
* **Job ID** (`job_id`, `text`, required): Enter a unique ID for the job.
