# Confidential Project Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Confidential Project Assignment Form is used to assign an employee to a new project, ensuring that they have a clear understanding of the project's details, confidentiality level, and their specific role and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your employee name, ID, email, and phone number.
2. Select the department you belong to.
3. Enter your job title.
4. Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
5. Enter the email of your direct supervisor.
6. Select the project name and internal reference code.
7. Choose the confidentiality level of the project.
8. Set the start and end dates of the project assignment, if applicable.
9. Specify your role and responsibilities on the project.
10. Allocate the percentage of time you will spend on the project.
11. List any team members assigned to the project.
12. Select the systems and areas you will need access to.
13. Specify any special clearances or security requirements.
14. Confirm if a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) is required.
15. Disclose any potential or actual conflicts of interest.
16. If applicable, describe any conflict details.
17. Specify any resources or equipment you will need.
18. Confirm if you have budget authority for the project.
19. Specify the frequency and format of project reporting requirements.
20. List any major project deliverables or milestones.
21. Confirm that you understand your confidentiality obligations.
22. Accept or decline the project assignment.
23. Sign and confirm your acceptance of the project assignment.
24. Have your direct supervisor approve and confirm the project assignment.
25. Enter the date of approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Your complete name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Your staff number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Your work email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Your contact number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Your position.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Direct supervisor.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Supervisor email.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Official project title.
* **Project Code** (`project_code`, text, required): Internal reference.
* **Confidentiality Level** (`confidentiality_level`, select_one, required): This defines the project's confidentiality level, which can be public, internal use only, confidential, highly confidential, or restricted.
* **Assignment Start Date** (`assignment_start_date`, date, required): The date when you begin working on the project.
* **Assignment End Date** (`assignment_end_date`, date, optional): The expected completion date, if applicable.
* **Your Role on Project** (`role_on_project`, text, required): Your specific role and responsibilities on the project.
* **Time Allocation** (`time_allocation`, select_one, required): The percentage of time you will spend on the project.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): Other assignees on the project.
* **Access Required** (`access_required`, select_multiple, required): The systems and areas you will need access to.
* **Special Clearances Needed** (`special_clearances`, text, optional): Security requirements.
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement** (`nda_required`, select_one, required): Confirm if an NDA is required.
* **Conflict of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, select_one, required): Disclose any potential or actual conflicts of interest.
* **Conflict Details** (`conflict_details`, text, optional): Describe any conflict details, if applicable.
* **Resources Needed** (`resources_needed`, text, optional): Equipment or tools you will need.
* **Budget Authorization** (`budget_authorization`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have budget authority for the project.
* **Reporting Requirements** (`reporting_requirements`, text, optional): Frequency and format of project reporting requirements.
* **Key Milestones** (`milestones`, text, optional): Major project deliverables or milestones.
* **Confidentiality Acknowledgment** (`confidentiality_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your confidentiality obligations.
* **Project Assignment Acceptance** (`acceptance_statement`, select_one, required): Accept or decline the project assignment.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, select_one, required): Confirm your acceptance of the project assignment.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Supervisor confirmation.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Date approved.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before assigning an employee to a project.
* If you are unsure about any project details, please check with your manager or HR representative.
* This form is a critical document that helps ensure the employee understands their role, responsibilities, and confidentiality obligations.
* Please carefully review each field before filling out the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
