# Apply For A Job Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Apply For A Job Form is designed for applicants to submit their application for a job opening. This form collects the necessary information for us to review and consider the applicant's qualifications for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Details section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your mailing address, city, and state or province.
3. Enter your ZIP or postal code.
4. Choose your employment type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Internship).
5. Enter your years of relevant work experience.
6. Provide information about your previous employer, job title, and reason for leaving.
7. List your key skills and qualifications.
8. Select your highest level of education (High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, Doctoral Degree, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Enter your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter your primary phone number where we can contact you.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, Text, Required): Provide your mailing address for us to send any necessary documents.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State/Province** (`state`, Text, Required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`postal_code`, Text, Required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, Text, Required): Select the specific job position you are applying for.
* **Job Posting ID** (`job_posting_id`, Text, Optional): If you know the job posting ID, please enter it here.
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter your expected salary range or any specific salary requirements.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose your employment type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Internship).
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (`years_of_experience`, Number, Required): Enter your years of relevant work experience.
* **Previous Employer** (`previous_employer`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter your previous employer's name or company.
* **Previous Job Title** (`previous_job_title`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter your previous job title.
* **Reason for Leaving Previous Position** (`reason_for_leaving`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the reason you left your previous position.
* **Key Skills and Qualifications** (`key_skills`, Text, Required): List your key skills and qualifications that align with the job requirements.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`highest_education`, Select One, Required): Select your highest level of education (High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, Doctoral Degree, or Other).
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter your field of study or major.
* **Relevant Certifications or Licenses** (`certifications`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Be specific when listing your key skills and qualifications.
- Make sure to choose the correct employment type that aligns with the job requirements.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
