# Support Staff Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate and assess the performance and development of support staff members within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct job title of the support staff member from the provided options.
2. Fill in the name of the support staff member.
3. Enter the date of their last reviewed performance evaluation.
4. Provide performance comments about the support staff member's performance.
5. List the strengths and weaknesses of the support staff member.
6. Select the performance ratings (Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Needs Improvement) and provide recommendations for improvement.
7. Enter the date of the next review and the reviewer's name.
8. Fill in the name of the reviewer who filled out this form.
9. (Optional) Fill in the reviewer's title and department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`support_staff`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct job title of the support staff member from the provided options.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Fill in the name of the support staff member.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the support staff member was hired.
* **Last Reviewed** (`last_reviewed`, `date`, required): Enter the date of their last reviewed performance evaluation.
* **Performance Comments** (`performance_comments`, `note`, required): Provide performance comments about the support staff member's performance.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, required): List the strengths of the support staff member.
* **Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, `text`, required): List the weaknesses of the support staff member.
* **Performance Ratings** (`performance_ratings`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the performance ratings (Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Needs Improvement).
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required): Provide recommendations for improvement.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required): Enter the next steps for the support staff member to improve.
* **Date of Next Review** (`date_of_next_review`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the next review.
* **Review Cycle** (`review_cycle`, `number`, required): Enter the review cycle (not currently used in this form).
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required): Fill in the name of the reviewer who filled out this form.
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`, `text`, required): Fill in the title of the reviewer who filled out this form.
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`, `text`, required): (Optional) Fill in the department of the reviewer who filled out this form.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
- Select the correct performance ratings and provide accurate recommendations.
- Reviewer name, title, and department are required for auditing and accountability purposes.
- Date of last reviewed, date of next review, and review cycle are optional fields.
