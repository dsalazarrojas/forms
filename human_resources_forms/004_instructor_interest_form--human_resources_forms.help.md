# Instructor Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instructor Interest Form is designed to collect information from instructors about their background, qualifications, and interests. This data will help organizers understand their needs and preferences for participating in various programs or training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following fields:
	* Name: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* Email: Your contact email address.
	* Location: Your current location (city, state, country).
	* Job Title: Your current or desired job title.
	* Years of Experience: Your number of years of experience in the field.
	* Education Level: Your highest education level achieved.
	* Date of Birth: Your date of birth.
2. Choose from the following fields (optional):
	* Job Title 2: Your secondary job title.
	* Years of Experience 2: Your number of years of experience in your secondary job title.
	* Education Level 2: Your highest education level achieved in your secondary field.
	* Date of Birth 2: Your date of birth.
	* Location 2: Your location.
	* Category: A brief description of your category (e.g., "Military", "Training", etc.)
3. Provide any additional information you'd like to share in the "Note" fields.
4. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required and optional fields.
