# Hospitality Overseas Family Leave Of Absance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Overseas Family Leave Of Absance Form is designed to gather information from employees on their family leave of absence plans while working in a hospitality setting overseas. This form helps HR departments track and manage employee leave requests, ensuring compliance with company policies and local regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and your family member's name in the respective fields.
2. Choose the correct country where you will be taking leave.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
4. Provide the reason for your leave and the duration of your leave.
5. If necessary, add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Family Member's Name** (`family_member_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the family member taking leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, optional): Select the country where you will be taking leave from the list.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for your leave (e.g., family emergency, medical, personal, or business reason).
* **Duration of Leaving** (`duration_of_leaving`, text, required): Enter the length of your planned leave.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional information or comments regarding your leave.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is not currently used.
