# Individual Accomplishment Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees evaluate their individual accomplishments and progress, and to provide a platform for feedback and goal setting. It is meant to be completed by the employee in collaboration with their supervisor or manager.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Page**: This page is the starting point for completing the form.
2. **Accomplishments**: On this page, select the accomplishments you have achieved, such as completing a project, meeting a deadline, or receiving recognition.
3. **Goals**: On this page, select the goals you have set for yourself, such as improving a skill, taking on additional responsibilities, or pursuing further education.
4. **Prophet's Name**, **Prophet's Email**, and **Prophet's Phone**: These pages are for providing contact information for the employee's supervisor or manager.
5. **Evaluation**: On this page, select whether your accomplishments and progress meet your expectations or not.
6. **Development Plan**: On this page, select whether you have a development plan in place to improve your skills and performance.
7. **Closing**: On this page, select whether you have closed out the current cycle of accomplishments and goals.
8. **Development Plan**: On this page, provide a brief description of your development plan.
9. **Closing Comments**: On this page, add any additional comments or feedback about your accomplishments and progress.
10. **Signed**: On this page, select whether you have signed off on your accomplishments and progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (First Page)**: This is the first page of the form where you can start filling out the form.
* **Accomplishments (Accomplishments)**: Select all the accomplishments you have achieved, such as completing a project, meeting a deadline, or receiving recognition.
* **Accomplishment 1 (Accomplishment 1)**: This field is for listing one of your accomplishments.
* **Accomplishment 2 (Accomplishment 2)**: This field is for listing another one of your accomplishments.
* **Goals (Goals)**: Select the goals you have set for yourself, such as improving a skill, taking on additional responsibilities, or pursuing further education.
* **Prophet's Name (Prophet's Name)**: This field is for providing the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Prophet's Email (Prophet's Email)**: This field is for providing the email of your supervisor or manager.
* **Prophet's Phone (Prophet's Phone)**: This field is for providing the phone number of your supervisor or manager.
* **Evaluation (Evaluation)**: Select whether your accomplishments and progress meet your expectations or not.
* **Overall Evaluation (Overall Evaluation)**: Select whether your accomplishments and progress meet your expectations or not (this field should only be filled out if Evaluation is also selected as "Yes").
* **Development Plan (Development Plan)**: Select whether you have a development plan in place to improve your skills and performance.
* **Development Plan (Development Plan)**: This field is for providing a brief description of your development plan.
* **Section 3 (Evaluation)**: This field is for evaluating your progress.
* **Section 4 (Development Plan)**: This field is for providing a development plan.
* **Section 5 (Closing)**: This field is for finalizing your accomplishments and progress.
* **Closing Comments (Closing Comments)**: This field is for adding any additional comments or feedback.
* **Signed (Signed)**: Select whether you have signed off on your accomplishments and progress.
