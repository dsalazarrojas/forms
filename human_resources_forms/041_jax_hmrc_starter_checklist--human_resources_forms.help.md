# jax_hmrc_starter_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The jax_hmrc_starter_checklist form is used to gather employee information for HR purposes. It is a simple and efficient tool to capture essential details about an employee's employment status, job title, department, and reporting line.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by filling out the employee details section, including job title, department, and manager's information. Then, proceed to the employment type and dates section, where you can select the type of employment and enter the start and end dates. Finally, complete the reporting line section with the necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to Jax HMRC Starter Checklist** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form and is used to provide a brief introduction to the form.
* **Employee Details** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's name and other relevant details.
* **Employment Type** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of employment (permanent, contract, or temporary) for the employee.
* **Employment Start Date** (`4`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the employee's employment.
* **Employment End Date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the employee's employment.
* **Job Title** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Department** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's department.
* **Manager Name** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`9`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Job Title** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the manager's job title.
* **Team** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's team name.
* **Role** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's role.
* **Reporting Line** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's reporting line information.
* **Reporting Line Manager** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reporting line manager's name.
* **Reporting Line Manager Email** (`16`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the reporting line manager's email address.
* **Reporting Line Manager Phone** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reporting line manager's phone number.
* **Reporting Line Manager Job Title** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reporting line manager's job title.
* **Reporting Line Manager Team** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reporting line manager's team name.
* **Reporting Line Manager Role** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reporting line manager's role.
* **Location** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's location.
* **Reporting Location** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reporting location information.
* **Reporting Location Manager** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reporting location manager's name.
* **Reporting Location Manager Email** (`24`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the reporting location manager's email address.
* **Reporting Location Manager Phone** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reporting location manager's phone number.
