**New Hire Onboarding Form - Help Guide**
=====================================

## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Form is designed to collect necessary information from new hires to facilitate the onboarding process. This form helps HR teams gather essential details about new employees, ensuring a smooth transition into the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. Take your time to fill in all the necessary fields.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the HR team.
