# hiring_procedure_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and document employee hiring procedures to ensure consistency and accuracy in the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select the appropriate options for each field and provide the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Hiring Request
* **New Hiring Request** (`New`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes the nature of the hiring request. Options are: New, Existing, or Unknown.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the job being hired for, e.g., "Software Engineer".

### Job Description
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the job role and responsibilities.

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the job type that best fits the job, e.g., Management, Sales, Marketing, Operations, or IT.

### Job Location
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the job will be based, e.g., "New York" or "Remote".

### Job Level
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the job level that best fits the job, e.g., Entry, Manager, or Director.

### Job Status
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the job, e.g., Active, Inactive.

### Job Category
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the category of the job, e.g., Full-time, Part-time, or Contract.

### Job Priority
* **Job Priority** (`job_priority`, `number`, required: false): Enter the priority level of the job, e.g., 1, 2, or 3.

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the job will start.

### End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the job will end.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure accurate tracking of the hiring procedure.
- Enter job-specific information for a detailed documentation of the hiring process.
- Review and update the form regularly to ensure consistency and accuracy.
