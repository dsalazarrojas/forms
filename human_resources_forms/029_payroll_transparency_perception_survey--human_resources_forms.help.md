# Payroll Transparency Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback and perceptions on our company's payroll practices. Your responses will help us better understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly, and try to provide specific examples or details whenever possible.
2. You may skip questions that are not applicable to your situation.
3. Use the options provided as a guide, but feel free to elaborate in the "Comments" field if you need more space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Description**: A brief description of your company will help us understand the context of your role.
* **Job Title**: Please enter your current job title as it appears on your payroll records.
* **Salary**: Enter your current annual salary.
* **Pay Period**: Select the frequency with which you are paid (e.g., bi-weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Pay Frequency**: Choose how often you receive pay (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Benefits**: Indicate which benefits you receive (e.g., health insurance, retirement plan, etc.).
* **Benefits Frequency**: Select how often you receive benefits (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of your job role and responsibilities.
* **Communication**: Rate your level of communication with your manager (e.g., regular check-ins, clear expectations, etc.).
* **Trust**: Indicate how much trust you have in your company's leadership (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Transparency**: Rate the transparency of your company's communication about pay and benefits (e.g., clear, unclear, etc.).
* **Satisfaction**: Please add any comments on your satisfaction with your job and company.
* **Email**: If you have any comments or questions, you can include your email address here.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number, in case we need to contact you for more information.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Job Title Supervisor**: Enter your supervisor's job title.
* **Job Title Department**: Enter your supervisor's department.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Department**: Enter your supervisor's department.
* **Job Location**: Enter your job location.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to add them in the "Comments" field.
