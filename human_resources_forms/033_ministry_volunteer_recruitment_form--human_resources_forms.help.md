# Ministry Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ministry Volunteer Recruitment Form is designed to gather information about potential volunteers for specific roles within the ministry. This form will help the ministry assess the suitability of volunteers for different roles and ensure that the right volunteers are matched with the right opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Volunteer Info** field with a brief description of your volunteer experience and qualifications.
2. Enter your **Contact Details** in the **Contact Details** field, including your email address and phone number.
3. Select your **Availability** by choosing from the options **Yes** or **No**.
4. Enter your **Availability Dates** if you are available to volunteer on specific dates.
5. Select your **Volunteer Type** by choosing from the options **Volunteer**, **Ministry**, or **Group**.
6. Enter your **Volunteer Hours** if you are willing to volunteer a certain number of hours per week.
7. Enter your **Volunteer Frequency** if you are willing to volunteer a certain number of times per week.
8. Enter your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** in the respective fields.
9. Select your **Volunteer Interest** by choosing from the options **General**, **Ministry**, **Community**, **Outreach**, or **Other**.
10. Select your **Volunteer Skills** by choosing from the options **Communication**, **Leadership**, **Organisational**, **Time Management**, or **Other**.
11. Select your **Volunteer References** by choosing from the options **Previous Volunteer Experience**, **Education**, **Work Experience**, **Social Media**, or **Other**.
12. Enter your **Volunteer Agreement** in the respective field.
13. Enter your **Volunteer Agreement Terms** in the respective field.
14. Enter **Volunteer Info Notes** in the **Volunteer Info Notes** field if you have any additional information to share.
15. Sign and date your **Volunteer Agreement Signature** and **Volunteer Agreement Date**.
16. Enter your **Volunteer Agreement Time** if required.
17. Enter your **Volunteer Agreement Organisation** in the respective field.
18. Enter your **Volunteer Agreement Position** in the respective field.
19. Enter your **Volunteer Agreement Organisation Address** in the respective field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Info**: A brief description of your volunteer experience and qualifications.
* **Contact Details**: Your email address and phone number.
* **Availability**: Select **Yes** if you are available to volunteer, **No** if you are not.
* **Availability Dates**: Dates on which you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Type**: Select **Volunteer** if you are a general volunteer, **Ministry** if you are a ministry volunteer, or **Group** if you are a group volunteer.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Number of hours you are willing to volunteer per week.
* **Volunteer Frequency**: Number of times you are willing to volunteer per week.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Volunteer Interest**: Select your area of interest, e.g., **General**, **Ministry**, **Community**, **Outreach**, or **Other**.
* **Volunteer Skills**: Select your skills, e.g., **Communication**, **Leadership**, **Organisational**, **Time Management**, or **Other**.
* **Volunteer References**: Select your references, e.g., **Previous Volunteer Experience**, **Education**, **Work Experience**, **Social Media**, or **Other**.
* **Volunteer Agreement**: A statement of your agreement to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Agreement Terms**: Terms of your agreement to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Info Notes**: Additional information you want to share about yourself.
* **Volunteer Agreement Signature**: Your signature.
* **Volunteer Agreement Date**: Date you signed the agreement.
* **Volunteer Agreement Time**: Time you signed the agreement.
* **Volunteer Agreement Organisation**: Organisation you are volunteering with.
* **Volunteer Agreement Position**: Position you are volunteering in.
* **Volunteer Agreement Organisation Address**: Address of the organisation you are volunteering with.
