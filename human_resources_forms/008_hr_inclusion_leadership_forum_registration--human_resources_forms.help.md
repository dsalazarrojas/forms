# HR Inclusion Leadership Forum Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register users for the HR Inclusion Leadership Forum, ensuring that all necessary information is collected to make the experience as inclusive and engaging as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Registration Details** page with your title, name, and any other relevant details as requested.
2. Move on to the **Contact Information** page and provide your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
3. On the **Session Details** page, enter the session information as requested, such as session dates and times.
4. In the **Session Attendance** page, confirm your session attendance.
5. If you have any **Accessibility Needs**, let us know so we can provide a comfortable and inclusive experience for you.
6. Finally, if you have any **Additional Comments**, feel free to include them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (text, optional):
	+ Enter your title and name as you would like to be referred to in the forum.
* **Contact Information** (text, optional):
	+ Enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Session Details** (text, optional):
	+ Enter the session dates and times as requested, to ensure you know when to attend.
* **Session Attendance** (text, optional):
	+ Confirm your session attendance by typing in your name.
* **Accessibility Needs** (text, optional):
	+ Specify your accessibility needs, such as accommodations or requirements.
* **Additional Comments** (note, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
* If you need any assistance with completing this form, don't worry - we're here to help!
