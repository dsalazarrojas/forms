# Severance Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Severance Agreement Release Form is a document used to formalize the terms of an employee's severance package and release of all claims. This form is essential for ensuring a smooth transition for both the employee and the employer, as it clarifies the agreement and protects both parties from potential legal disputes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Employee Information**: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. **Enter Agreement Details**: Fill in the Severance Amount, Notice Period, Last Day of Work, Termination Effective Date, and Agreement Sign Date.
3. **Sign and Date**: Sign and date the Release of All Claims and Agreement sections.
4. **Verify Agreement**: Ensure both the employee and employer have read and agree to the terms of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company or organization employing the individual.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): The date on which this agreement is signed.
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, `text`, optional): A brief explanation for the employee's departure, if applicable.
* **Severance Amount** (`severance_amount`, `number`, required): The monetary amount paid to the employee as part of the severance package.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `number`, required): The number of days or weeks given to the employee before the last day of work.
* **Last Day of Work** (`last_day_of_work`, `date`, required): The date on which the employee's employment terminates.
* **Termination Effective Date** (`termination_effective_date`, `date`, required): The date on which the employee's employment actually terminates.
* **Release of All Claims** (`release_of_all_claims`, `text`, optional): A statement releasing the employee from any potential claims against the employer.
* **Agreement Sign Date** (`agreement_sign_date`, `date`, required): The date on which both the employee and employer sign this agreement.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required): The employee's signature indicating their acceptance of the agreement.
* **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`, `text`, required): The employer's signature indicating their acceptance of the agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid disputes and confusion.
* Review the agreement with the employee to ensure they understand its terms.
* Keep a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
