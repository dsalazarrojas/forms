# Employment Offer And Policy Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and acknowledge employment offers and related policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the details of the employment offer.
2. Enter the date and time of the offer.
3. Enter the date and time of the start of the job.
4. Enter the job position.
5. Enter the contact information for the employee (email and phone number).
6. Acknowledge receipt of the offer.
7. Enter the date and time of acknowledgment.
8. Enter any additional notes about the employment offer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **offer_details**: A text field for entering any details about the employment offer.
- **offer_date**: A date field for entering the date of the employment offer.
- **offer_expires_in**: A numeric field for entering the number of days or hours until the employment offer expires.
- **job_position**: A text field for entering the job position.
- **job_start_date**: A date field for entering the start date of the job.
- **job_start_time**: A time field for entering the start time of the job.
- **policy_acknowledgment**: A text field for entering an acknowledgment of the employment offer policy.
- **acknowledgment_date**: A date field for entering the date of acknowledgment.
- **acknowledgment_time**: A time field for entering the time of acknowledgment.
- **email**: A field for entering the employee's email address.
- **phone**: A field for entering the employee's phone number.
- **notes**: A text field for entering any additional notes about the employment offer.
