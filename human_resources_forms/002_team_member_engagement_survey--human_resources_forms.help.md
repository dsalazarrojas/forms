# Team Member Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help team members express their opinions and feelings about their work, job satisfaction, and overall experience with the team and company. It's a tool for the management to better understand team members' engagement and satisfaction levels.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer all questions to the best of your knowledge.
2.  Select one option per question from the available choices.
3.  You can select multiple options when asked about areas of satisfaction or performance.
4.  Don't worry about the length of your answers; the more honest, the better!
5.  If a question is not applicable to you, just leave it blank or select "Not Applicable."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **how_do_you_feel_about_your_job**: This question asks about your overall job satisfaction. Please select the option that best describes your feelings about your job.
    *   **Option 1**: I am happy and satisfied with my job.
    *   **Option 2**: I am neutral, it's just a job.
    *   **Option 3**: I am unhappy and dissatisfied with my job.
*   **who_is_your_manager**: Select the name of your manager.
*   **how_would_you_like_to_provide_feedback**: This question is optional, and you can provide feedback if you have any suggestions or comments.
*   **what_do_you_like_about_your_job**: Select all the areas you are satisfied with.
*   **what_are_your_primary_job_duties**: Please type in your primary job duties.
*   **do_you_have_ambition_for_promotion**: Select if you have any ambition for promotion.
*   **what_is_your_job_satisfaction_level**: This question asks about your overall job satisfaction.
*   **would_you_like_to_provide_feedback_to_management**: Select if you want to give feedback to management.
*   **how_do_you_rate_your_job_performance**: Rate your job performance level.
*   **what_areas_of_your_job_make_you_satisfied**: Select all the areas that satisfy you in your job.
*   **what_areas_of_your_job_do_you_perform_well**: Select all the areas that you perform well in.
*   **do_you_believe_your_manager_is_affective_a_good_job**: Select if you believe your manager is effective in their job.
*   **how_do_you_rate_your_team**: Rate your team's performance level.
*   **how_do_you_rate_your_company**: Rate your company's performance level.
*   **how_do_you_rate_the_communication**: Rate the communication level in your team.
*   **what_is_your_opinion_about_the_company_culture**: Select all the aspects of company culture you are satisfied with.
*   **what_is_your_opinion_about_the_team_culture**: Select all the aspects of team culture you are satisfied with.
*   **what_is_your_job_satisfaction_level_2**: This question asks about your overall job satisfaction again.
*   **how_do_you_rate_your_job_performance_level**: Rate your job performance level.
*   **would_you_like_to_provide_feedback_to_your_team**: Select if you want to give feedback to your team.
*   **how_do_you_rate_your_company_culture_level**: Rate your company's culture level.
*   **do_you_have_ambition_for_promotion**: Select if you have any ambition for promotion.
*   **do_you_have_ambition_for_the_team**: Select if you have any ambition for the team.
*   **do_you_believe_your_manager_has_ambition**: Select if you believe your manager has ambition.
