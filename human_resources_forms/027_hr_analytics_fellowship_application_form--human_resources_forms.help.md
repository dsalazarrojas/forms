# HR Analytics Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Analytics Fellowship Application Form is designed to collect relevant information from potential applicants to support the evaluation and selection process for the HR Analytics Fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in all the required fields (indicated by a red asterisk*) with accurate and relevant information.
- Use standard format for dates (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
- If a field is not applicable or not relevant to your situation, please use "N/A" (Not Applicable) or "Not Relevant".
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the HR team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (<name>, <type: text>, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (<name>, <type: text>, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (<name>, <type: email>, required: false): Please enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (<name>, <type: text>, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Role** (<name>, <type: text>, required: false): Please enter your role or job title.
* **Experience** (<name>, <type: text>, required: false): Please describe your relevant work experience.
* **Education** (<name>, <type: text>, required: false): Please list your educational background.
* **Availability** (<name>, <type: time>, required: false): Please enter your availability in the format HH:MM (e.g., 08:00-17:00).
* **Start Date** (<name>, <type: date, required: false): Please enter the start date of your availability.
* **End Date** (<name>, <type: date, required: false): Please enter the end date of your availability.
* **Reason for Leaving** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please explain why you are leaving your current position.
* **Skills** (<name>, <type: select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the skills you possess.
* **Availability Details** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please provide more details about your availability.
* **Availability Time** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter more details about your availability.
* **Work Location** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter the location where you work.
* **Work Type** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter the type of work you do.
* **Work Shift** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter the shift of your work.
* **Availability Shift** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter your availability shift.
* **Work Hours** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter the hours you work.
* **Days Available** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter the days you are available.
* **Start Time** (<name>, <type: time, required: false): Please enter the start time of your work.
* **End Time** (<name>, <type: time, required: false): Please enter the end time of your work.
* **Work Type Details** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter more details about your work type.
* **Work Location Details** (<name>, <type: text, required: false): Please enter more details about your work location.
* **Availability Notes** (<name>, <type: note, required: false): Please use this field to add any additional information about your availability.

Please note that the fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory but it's still a good idea to fill them in for the completeness and accuracy of your application.

This guide is a starting point for the applicant to understand what information is required for the HR Analytics Fellowship Application Form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please reach out to the HR team.
