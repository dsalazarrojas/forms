# on_boarding_employee_details - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect onboarding details of an employee, including personal and employment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the onboarding process.
2. Fill out each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Onboarding Employee Details**: This is a placeholder field that describes the form. Please ignore it.
* **employee_name**: <u>Enter the name of the employee being onboarded.</u>
* **employee_email**: <u>Enter the employee's email address.</u>
* **employee_phone**: <u>Enter the employee's phone number.</u>
* **employee_job_title**: <u>Select the job title of the employee.</u>
* **employee_department**: <u>Select all departments the employee belongs to.</u>
* **employee_hire_date**: <u>Enter the date the employee was hired.</u>
* **employee_salary**: <u>Enter the employee's salary.</u>
* **employee_note**: <u>Add any additional comments or notes about the employee.</u>
* **onboarding_status**: <u>Select the employee's current onboarding status.</u>
* **onboarding_date**: <u>Enter the date the employee is scheduled for onboarding.</u>
* **onboarding_time**: <u>Enter the time of the onboarding process.</u>

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or contact the relevant personnel.
