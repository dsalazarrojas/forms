# emergency_contact_information_update_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request updates to emergency contact information for a specific employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the "Update Request" page.
2. Enter a brief description of the update request in the "Update Request" field.
3. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
4. On the "Contact Information" page, enter the contact information for the employee's emergency contact, including:
	* Contact Name
	* Contact Relationship
	* Primary Contact Name
	* Primary Contact Phone Number
	* Primary Contact Email
	* Secondary Contact Name (if applicable)
	* Secondary Contact Phone Number (if applicable)
	* Secondary Contact Email (if applicable)
5. Select the correct relationship between the emergency contact and the employee.
6. Choose the update reason from the "Update By" field.
7. Enter the date and time the update was made.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Update Request** (`Update Request`, `note`, optional): A brief description of the update request.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, optional): The name of the emergency contact.
* **Contact Relationship** (`Contact Relationship`, `text`, optional): The relationship between the emergency contact and the employee.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`Primary Contact Name`, `text`, optional): The name of the primary emergency contact.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`Primary Contact Phone Number`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the primary emergency contact.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`Primary Contact Email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the primary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Contact Name** (`Secondary Contact Name`, `text`, optional): The name of the secondary emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Secondary Contact Phone Number** (`Secondary Contact Phone Number`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the secondary emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Secondary Contact Email** (`Secondary Contact Email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the secondary emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Contact Relationship Description** (`Contact Relationship Description`, `note`, optional): A description of the relationship between the emergency contact and the employee.
* **Update By** (`Update By`, `select_one`, optional): The reason for the update, which should be one of the following options: Emergency Contact Information Update Request or Other.
* **Updated By** (`Updated By`, `select_one`, optional): The person who updated the emergency contact information, which should be one of the following options: HR or IT or Other.
* **Date Updated** (`Date Updated`, `date`, optional): The date the emergency contact information was updated.
* **Time Updated** (`Time Updated`, `time`, optional): The time the emergency contact information was updated.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Additional comments or notes about the update.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, `text`, optional): The relationship between the emergency contact and the employee.
* **Emergency Contact Status** (`Emergency Contact Status`, `select_one`, optional): The status of the emergency contact, which should be one of the following options: Pending, Approved, Denied, or Other.
* **Emergency Contact Status Reason** (`Emergency Contact Status Reason`, `text`, optional): The reason for the emergency contact status.
* **Emergency Contact Status Date** (`Emergency Contact Status Date`, `date`, optional): The date the emergency contact status was updated.
* **Emergency Contact Status Time** (`Emergency Contact Status Time`, `time`, optional): The time the emergency contact status was updated.
* **Emergency Contact Status Notes** (`Emergency Contact Status Notes`, `note`, optional): Additional comments or notes about the emergency contact status.
* **Emergency Contact Status Updated By** (`Emergency Contact Status Updated By`, `select_one`, optional): The person who updated the emergency contact status, which should be one of the following options: HR or IT or Other.
* **Emergency Contact Status Updated By Who** (`Emergency Contact Status Updated By Who`, `text`, optional): The person who updated the emergency contact status.
