# Landscaping Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential employees who wish to apply for landscaping positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Personal Information" section with your name, contact details, and job preferences.
2. In the "Family Information" section, provide contact details for a parent or guardian if required, or emergency contact information if not.
3. Ensure you have attached or uploaded a valid Resume.
4. Select "True" to agree to the Consent terms.
5. Review each section carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Applied Position** (`applied_position`, select_one, required): Choose the position you're applying for (Landscaper, Landscape Technician, or Groundskeeper).
* **Previous Employment** (`previous_employment`, note, optional): If applicable, describe your previous employment experience.
* **Job Questions** (`job_questions`, note, optional): Add any additional job-related questions or comments.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, required): Select the date you're available to start work.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Attach or upload your Resume.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Agree to the Consent terms (True or False).
* **First Name Parent** (`first_name_parent`, text, optional): Enter your parent's first name (if required).
* **Last Name Parent** (`last_name_parent`, text, optional): Enter your parent's last name (if required).
* **Relationship Prophets** (`relationship_prophets`, note, optional): If you have a parent or guardian, describe your relationship.
* **Parent Age** (`parent_age`, number, optional): If you have a parent or guardian, enter their age.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a parent or guardian, enter their phone number.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): If you have a parent or guardian, enter their email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you don't have a parent or guardian, provide emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (`emergency_phone_number`, text, optional): If you don't have a parent or guardian, enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): If you don't have a parent or guardian, enter their email address.
* **Job Questions Parent** (`job_questions_parent`, note, optional): If you have a parent or guardian, add any job-related questions or comments.
* **Available Start Date Parent** (`available_start_date_parent`, date, optional): If you have a parent or guardian, select the available start date.
* **Resume Parent** (`resume_parent`, text, optional): If you have a parent or guardian, attach or upload their Resume.
* **Consent Parent** (`consent_parent`, select_one, required): If you have a parent or guardian, agree to the Consent terms (True or False).
* **Job References** (`job_references`, note, optional): Provide any job references or recommendations.
* **References Name** (`references_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your reference or recommender.
