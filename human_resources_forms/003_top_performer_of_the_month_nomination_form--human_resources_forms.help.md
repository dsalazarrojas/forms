# Top Performer Of The Month Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to identify and recognize the outstanding efforts and contributions of employees within a company or team. The form helps to acknowledge the exceptional performance, leadership, or teamwork of individuals and maintains a record of past top performers for future planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential for the form to function correctly.
2.  Complete the nomination form with the necessary information, including the employee's name, team name, and details about their outstanding performance.
3.  Provide the nomination date to ensure the correct timing of the nomination.
4.  Enter any additional remarks or comments to support the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can enter any details about the nomination.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
* **Employee Nickname** (`employee_nickname`, text, optional): Enter any nickname or alias for the employee being nominated.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team the employee is part of.
* **Nominee's Name** (`nominee_s_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person nominating the employee.
* **Nominee's Title** (`nominee_s_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person nominating the employee.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the nomination in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Nomination Remarks** (`nomination_remarks`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or reasons for nominating the employee.
