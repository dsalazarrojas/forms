# Daily Task, Time And Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Task, Time And Ticket Form is used to record and track daily tasks, time spent, and tickets submitted by users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `user_input` field with your name or ID.
2. Enter the `start_time` and `end_time` fields with the start and end times of your task(s).
3. Enter the `time_spent` field with the amount of time spent on your task.
4. Enter the `task` field with a brief description of your task.
5. Enter the `leave` field with any leave information (if applicable).
6. Select either `Yes` or `No` in the `ticket` field if you submitted a ticket.
7. Enter any additional `details` about your task.
8. Enter any `notes` regarding your task.
9. Enter your `email` and `phone` (if you want to be contacted for further discussion).
10. Select whether your task was assigned to you (`Yes` or `No`), who it was assigned to (`Chatjimmy` or `Other`), or which team it belonged to (`Yes` or `No`).
11. Enter the date your task was `created_by` and whether it was `updated_by`.
12. Select the status of your task (`Active` or `Inactive`).
13. Optionally, select whether you submitted any `comments` or if the task is `Inactive`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This field is for your name or ID. Enter the name or ID that you want to be associated with your task.
* **Start Time**: This field is for entering the start time of your task. Use the date format to enter the start time.
* **End Time**: This field is for entering the end time of your task. Use the date format to enter the end time.
* **Time Spent**: This field is for entering the amount of time spent on your task.
* **Task**: This field is for a brief description of your task. Enter a short summary of what you did.
* **Leave**: This field is for any leave information you may have taken. Enter the type and duration of leave, if applicable.
* **Ticket**: This field is for indicating whether you submitted a ticket. Select `Yes` if you did, and `No` if you did not.
* **Details**: This field is for providing any additional information about your task. Enter any relevant details.
* **Notes**: This field is for any notes regarding your task.
* **Email**: This field is for entering your email address. This can be used for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Phone**: This field is for entering your phone number. This can be used for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Assigned To**: This field is for indicating whether your task was assigned to you (`Yes` or `No`). Select `Yes` if it was assigned to you, and `No` if it was not.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is for selecting which tool was assigned to you for the task. You can select `chatjimmy` or `Other`.
* **Assigned Team**: This field is for indicating whether your task belongs to a team (`Yes` or `No`). Select `Yes` if it does, and `No` if it does not.
* **Created By**: This field is for entering the date your task was created by. Use the date format.
* **Updated By**: This field is for indicating whether your task was updated by someone else (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Updated Date**: This field is for entering the date your task was updated. Use the date format.
* **Comments**: This field is for indicating whether you submitted any comments on your task (`Yes` or `No`). Select `Yes` if you did, and `No` if you did not.
* **Status**: This field is for selecting the status of your task (`Active` or `Inactive`). Select `Active` if your task is still ongoing, and `Inactive` if it is not.
