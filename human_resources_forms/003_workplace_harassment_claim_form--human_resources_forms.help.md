# Workplace Harassment Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about incidents of workplace harassment, allowing our management team to investigate and address them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the incident you are reporting.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident.
3. Indicate whether a perpetrator was involved and if they are still working for the company.
4. List any witnesses to the incident.
5. Indicate if any action has been taken regarding the incident.
6. Enter any details about the incident that you feel are relevant.
7. Provide any relevant information about the employee involved.
8. Enter any relevant contact information.
9. Attach any supporting documentation (e.g., photos, videos, or witness statements).
10. Indicate the current status of the case and whether an investigator has been assigned.
11. Indicate if the case is closed and, if so, the date it was closed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date): Fill in the date of the incident you are reporting. This will help our management team understand when the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident** (`description_of_incident`, text): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident. Be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise.
* **Perpetrator** (`perpetrator`, select multiple): Indicate whether there was a perpetrator involved in the incident and, if so, check 'Yes' next to their name. You can also indicate if the perpetrator is still working for the company.
* **Witness List** (`witness_list`, select multiple): List any witnesses who saw or heard about the incident. You can also indicate if they are still working for the company.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, select one): Indicate if any action has been taken regarding the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text): Enter any details about the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text): Enter any additional details that you feel are relevant to the incident.
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text): Provide any relevant information about the employee involved in the incident.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text): Enter any relevant contact information for the employee involved.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, note): Attach any supporting documentation, such as photos or witness statements, that may help our management team investigate the incident.
* **Case Status** (`case_status`, select one): Indicate the current status of the case, such as whether it's Active, Inactive, or closed.
* **Investigator** (`investigator`, select one): Indicate if an investigator has been assigned to the case.
* **Closure Date** (`closure_date`, date): If the case is closed, enter the date it was closed.

Note: Please ensure that you complete this form accurately and truthfully, as it will help us to efficiently and effectively address the incident.
