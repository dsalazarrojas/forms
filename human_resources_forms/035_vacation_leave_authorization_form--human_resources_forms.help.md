# Vacation Leave Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Leave Authorization Form is used to request time off from work due to vacation, illness, or other personal reasons. It is a mandatory form to be filled out by employees when they need to take leave from their work schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Employee ID
	* Approval Request From
	* Dates Requested
	* Duration Requested (if applicable)
2. If not required, you can leave the optional fields empty.
3. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID card or company records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your official job title.
* **Dates Requested** (`dates_requested`, date, optional): Enter the start and end dates of your requested leave.
* **Duration Requested** (`duration_requested`, number, optional): Enter the number of days or weeks of leave you are requesting.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of your department (if you have one).
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation of why you are taking leave.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number in case your manager or HR needs to reach you while you're away.
* **Approval Request From** (`approval_request_from`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Department Manager
	+ Team Lead
	+ HR Manager
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your leave request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing your leave request.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to your manager.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult your HR representative or manager for guidance.
