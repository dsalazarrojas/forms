# Employee Sentiment Summary Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Sentiment Summary Request Form is a tool used by management to gather feedback from employees on their overall sentiment towards their work environment. The form aims to help management understand the current state of employee satisfaction and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2.  Start with the first question on "What is your role in the company?" which asks for a brief description of your job function and responsibilities.
3.  On the next question, rate your likelihood to recommend the company to a friend on a scale of 1-5.
4.  Then, respond to the question "How often do you feel appreciated or recognized by your manager or colleagues?" with a numerical value representing your frequency of feeling appreciated.
5.  The following question, "What is the main reason you feel this way?", is an opportunity to provide a detailed explanation for your sentiment.
6.  After that, suggest "What can we do to improve the employee sentiment?" with any ideas or suggestions you may have.
7.  Finally, provide an estimate of your current workload and the number of hours you work per week.
