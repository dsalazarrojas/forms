# operations_lead_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "operations_lead_application_form" form is used to collect and store information about job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the First Name and Last Name of the job applicant or contact person.
2. Enter the Job Title and Job Description of the position being posted.
3. Select the Job Type from the provided options.
4. Enter the Job Reference, if applicable.
5. Upload the Resume of the applicant or contact person, if applicable.
6. Select the Job Status (Full Time, Part Time, or Contract).
7. Enter the Location and Job ID for the position.
8. Select the Job Hours and Job Salary, if applicable.
9. Enter the Job Wage, if applicable.
10. Enter the Job Notes and Job Requirements, if applicable.
11. Select the Job Skills required for the position, from the provided options.
12. Enter the Job Experience Required, if applicable.
13. Select the Job Education Level, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the first name of the job applicant or contact person.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the last name of the job applicant or contact person.
* **Job Title** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the title of the job being posted.
* **Job Description** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter a brief description of the job duties and responsibilities.
* **Work Phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the work phone number of the job applicant or contact person.
* **Work Phone Extension** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the work phone extension of the job applicant or contact person.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the job applicant or contact person.
* **Job Reference** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter a reference or ID for the job posting.
* **Resume** (Text, Required/Optional): Upload the resume of the job applicant or contact person.
* **Job Type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of job from the provided options (Accounting, Finance, Human Resources, Marketing, Operations, Sales).
* **Work Experience** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the work experience of the job applicant or contact person, if applicable.
* **Job Status** (Select One, Required): Select the job status (Full Time, Part Time, Contract).
* **Location** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the location where the job will be performed.
* **Job ID** (Text, Required): Enter a unique ID for the job posting.
* **Job Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the job date, if applicable.
* **Work Start Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the start date of the job, if applicable.
* **Work End Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the end date of the job, if applicable.
* **Job Hours** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the number of hours worked for the job, if applicable.
* **Job Wage** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the job wage, if applicable.
* **Job Salary** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the job salary, if applicable.
* **Job Notes** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional notes about the job.
* **Job Requirements** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the requirements for the job.
* **Job Skills** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select the skills required for the job from the provided options.
* **Job Experience Required** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the experience required for the job, if applicable.
* **Job Education Level** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the education level required for the job, if applicable.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and must be completed to save the form.
