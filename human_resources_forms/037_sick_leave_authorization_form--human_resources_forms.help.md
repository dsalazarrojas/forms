# Sick Leave Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a systematic way for employees to request and process sick leave. It is designed to help HR efficiently collect and evaluate leave requests, ensuring timely decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Guidelines
1.  Ensure you are filling out this form in the correct context (internal use only).
2.  Review the form carefully and fill out all required fields accurately.
3.  Provide all necessary details to facilitate proper review and decision-making.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Sick Leave Request**
*   **Sick Leave Request (sick_leave_request)**: Enter a detailed description of your reason for taking sick leave.
*   **Employee ID (employee_id)**: Enter your employee ID number to facilitate processing and tracking of your leave request.
*   **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee record.
*   **Start Date (start_date)**: Enter the date you plan to begin your sick leave in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **End Date (end_date)**: Enter the date you plan to return to work in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Reason for Absence (reason_for_absence)**: Describe your reason for taking sick leave. Provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Return Date (return_date)**: Enter the date you plan to return to work in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Manager Approval (manager_approval)**: Select whether your manager has approved your sick leave request.
*   **Comments (comments)**: If you have any additional comments or notes regarding your leave request, enter them here.

## Tips
*   Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure you have all necessary details before submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
