# Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exit survey is a form designed to gather information about your reasons for leaving the company. This information will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement to better support future employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the exit survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select the main reason for your departure from the "What was the main reason you are leaving our company?" field.
3. Provide the date you decided to leave the company.
4. If you have any feedback or suggestions, you can provide them in the "Feedback" field.
5. If you were approached for an exit survey by HR, please select "True".
6. Enter your manager's name and your contact information (name, email, phone number, and office extension).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the main reason you are leaving our company?** (`exit_reason`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the main reason why you are leaving the company from the provided options.
* **When did you decide to leave the company?** (`exit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you decided to leave the company.
* **What is the most important reason for your departure?** (`reason_for_leaving`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation of the most important reason for your departure.
* **Do you have any feedback or suggestions for the company?** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts on how we can improve.
* **Have you been approached for an exit survey by HR?** (`exit_survey`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "True" if you were approached for an exit survey by HR.
* **What is your manager's name?** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your manager's name.
* **Your name?** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Your work email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your work email.
* **Your work phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
* **Office extension** (`contact_extension`, `text`, required: false): Enter your office extension.
* **Any other comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any other comments or thoughts.

Please provide your feedback and thoughts honestly, as they will be used to improve our company's policies and procedures.
