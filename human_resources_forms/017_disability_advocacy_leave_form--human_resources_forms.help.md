# disability_advocacy_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees request and track disability-related leaves of absence. A disability leave can be taken for various reasons, including medical reasons or family and medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field, marked as *required*.
2. Select the type of leave you are taking (e.g., Medical Leave, Disability Leave, or Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) Leave).
3. Provide your expected dates of leave.
4. Choose whether you want your manager to be notified of your leave.
5. Enter any additional comments you'd like to provide for your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your leave.
* **Type of leave** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of leave you are taking from the options provided (Medical Leave, Disability Leave, or Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) Leave).
* **Dates of Leave** (`dates_of_leave`, text, optional): Enter the dates you expect to be out of work.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, note, optional): Enter a brief explanation for your leave, if desired.
* **Manager Notification** (`manager_notification`, select_one, optional): Check *Yes* if you want your manager to be notified of your leave.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or concerns you'd like to share with your manager.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, select_one, optional): Select who you are submitting the form on behalf of (Manager or HR Representative).

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR representative or manager for guidance.
* This form is designed to help you efficiently request and track your leave, but it is not a substitute for any HR or company policies or procedures.
