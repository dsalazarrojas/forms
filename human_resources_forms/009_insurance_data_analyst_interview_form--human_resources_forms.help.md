# Insurance Data Analyst Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates during the interview process for an Insurance Data Analyst position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your job location
2. Enter your job title
3. Provide a brief job description
4. List your job requirements
5. Enter your name
6. Provide your contact information
7. Share your insurance experience
8. Enter your educational background
9. Share your work experience
10. Select your job type
11. Provide your work experience 2
12. Select your job type 2
13. Enter your job location 2
14. List your insurance certifications
15. Select your job type 3
16. Enter your job location 3
17. Enter your insurance company
18. Provide a brief job description 2
19. Enter your job title 2
20. Select your job type 4
21. Enter your job location 4
22. Provide a brief job description 3
23. Enter your job title 3
24. Enter the submission date

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you work or have worked as an Insurance Data Analyst.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as an Insurance Data Analyst.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your job as an Insurance Data Analyst.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, optional): List your job requirements as an Insurance Data Analyst.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
* **Insurance Experience** (`insurance_experiences`, text, optional): Share your insurance experience and relevant work history.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter your educational background.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Share your work experience as an Insurance Data Analyst.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): Select your job type (e.g., full-time, part-time, freelance, etc.).
* **Work Experience 2** (`work_experience_2`, text, optional): Share your work experience 2.
* **Job Type 2** (`job_type_2`, text, optional): Select your job type 2.
* **Job Location 2** (`job_location_2`, text, optional): Enter your job location 2.
* **Insurance Certifications** (`insurance_certifications`, text, optional): List your insurance certifications.
* **Job Type 3** (`job_type_3`, text, optional): Select your job type 3.
* **Job Location 3** (`job_location_3`, text, optional): Enter your job location 3.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, optional): Enter the insurance company you work for or have worked for.
* **Job Description 2** (`job_description_2`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your job 2.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter your job title 2.
* **Job Type 4** (`job_type_4`, text, optional): Select your job type 4.
* **Job Location 4** (`job_location_4`, text, optional): Enter your job location 4.
* **Job Description 3** (`job_description_3`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your job 3.
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, text, optional): Enter your job title 3.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
