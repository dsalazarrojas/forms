# Employee Network Formation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee network formation application information from applicants. It is essential to complete this form accurately and carefully to ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your work email address in the email field.
3. Select your role in the role field (e.g., Account Manager, Department Manager, Team Lead, or CEO).
4. Choose your team(s) you would like to join (optional).
5. Enter the date you would like to join (in YYYY-MM-YYYY format).
6. Select your available time (24-hour format).
7. Choose your location(s) (optional).
8. Add any additional notes or information you think is relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name. This is a required field and should be filled out accurately.
* **LastName** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name. This is a required field and should be filled out accurately.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address. This is a required field and should be filled out accurately.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the company (e.g., Account Manager, Department Manager, Team Lead, or CEO). This is a required field.
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the team(s) you would like to join. You can select multiple teams if necessary.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you would like to join. This field is optional.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter a date in YYYY-MM-YYYY format. This is a required field.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select your available time in 24-hour format. This is a required field.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the location(s) you would like to be located. You can select multiple locations if necessary.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or information you think is relevant to your application. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or HR representative.
* This form is designed to be easy to use and understand. If you need help or clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
