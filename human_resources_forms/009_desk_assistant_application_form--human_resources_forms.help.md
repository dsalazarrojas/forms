# Desk Assistant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Desk Assistant Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from applicants to evaluate their suitability for the Desk Assistant position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the 'Submit' button to begin the application process.
2. Fill in the Applicant Information page with your name, title, and other details as required.
3. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) in the Contact Information page.
4. In the Work Experience page, describe your work experience relevant to the position.
5. In the Availability page, specify your available work schedule.
6. In the Job Skills page, list the top 5 skills you are familiar with, separated by commas.
7. In the Education page, provide your educational background.
8. In the Qualifications page, list any relevant qualifications or certifications.
9. In the References page, list two professional references with their contact information.
10. Attach a valid resume in the Resume page.
11. Review your application and click the 'Submit' button to submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information**:
  * This page is for you to provide your name, title, and other basic details.
* **Contact Information**:
  * Enter a valid email address for correspondence and a correct phone number for communication.
* **Work Experience**:
  * Describe your previous work experience relevant to the position, including job title, company, and dates of employment.
* **Availability**:
  * Specify your available work schedule, including days and hours of availability.
* **Job Skills**:
  * List the top 5 skills you are familiar with, separated by commas. (e.g. 'Yes, No, Yes')
* **Education**:
  * Provide your educational background, including degree and institution.
* **Qualifications**:
  * List any relevant qualifications or certifications.
* **References**:
  * List two professional references with their names, job titles, and contact information.
* **Resume**:
  * Attach a valid resume for the hiring team to review.
* **Submit**:
  * Review your application and click the 'Submit' button to submit it.
