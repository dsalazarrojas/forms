# Sales Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Onboarding Assessment Form is a critical tool used to assess new sales reps and ensure they are adequately prepared for the sales process. It helps the sales team to better understand the new sales reps' background and experience, and to provide them with necessary information to guide them in their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required information in each field. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your official email address that you use for work or other official purposes.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your work phone number or another contact number that you are available to receive calls from.
* **Years of Experience** (number, optional): If you have sales experience, enter the number of years you have been working in sales.
* **Sales Manager Name** (text, optional): If you have a sales manager, enter their name. This will help the sales team to contact them directly for further assistance.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
* Double-check that all the required fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please contact your sales manager or HR representative for assistance.
