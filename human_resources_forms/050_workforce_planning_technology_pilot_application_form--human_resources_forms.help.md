# Workforce Planning Technology Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employees' work status, skills, experience, and other relevant details in support of a workforce planning technology pilot application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and complete information.
* Fill out each section of the form according to the instructions below.
* Submit the form when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info**: Provide your basic contact information.
  * This includes your name, which is not displayed in the provided schema.
  * Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Contact Info**: Select one or more of the contact options.
  * This section is not clearly defined, but it seems to be related to the contact information. 
  * Please select one or more of the provided options.
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status.
  * This field indicates your current status in the company.
  * Options are: Active, Not Active, On Leave.
* **Skills**: Enter your relevant skills.
  * This field is for listing your skills and qualifications.
  * Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your work or experience.
  * This field is for any additional comments or notes related to your work.
  * Please keep this field concise.
* **Work Experience**: Enter your relevant work experience.
  * This field is for listing your work experience in years.
  * Please provide the number of years you have been working in the industry.
* **Years Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in the industry.
  * This field is similar to "Work Experience", but it only requires a number.
  * Please enter the exact number of years.
* **Education**: Select one or more of the education levels.
  * This field is for listing your highest education level.
  * Options are: High school, Bachelor, Master.
* **Level of Education**: Select your highest level of education.
  * This field is similar to "Education", but it only requires a selection.
  * Options are: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary.
* **Email**: Enter your official company email address.
  * This field is for your official company email address.
  * Please enter it as it appears on your company email.
* **Phone**: Enter your office or mobile phone number.
  * This field is for your contact phone number.
  * Please enter the number without any extensions.
* **Job Role**: Select your job title.
  * This field is for your current or desired job title.
  * Options are: Sales, Marketing, Finance.
* **Job Status**: Select the status of your job.
  * This field is similar to "Work Status".
  * Options are: Open, Closed, In Progress.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to.
  * This field is for your current department.
  * Options are: HR, Marketing, Sales.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
  * This field is for your direct manager's name.
  * Please enter their official name.
* **Manager Title**: Enter your manager's title.
  * This field is for their official title.
  * Please enter it as it appears on their official title.
* **Reporting Manager**: Enter your reporting manager's name.
  * This field is for your reporting manager's name.
  * Please enter their official name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
  * This field is for your current job title.
  * Please enter it as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Category**: Enter the category of your job.
  * This field is for describing your job category.
  * Please be as specific as possible.
* **Location**: Select the location where you work.
  * This field is for the location of your workplace.
  * Options are: HQ, Regional, National.
* **Shift**: Select your working shift.
  * This field is for your working shift.
  * Options are: Morning, Afternoon, Night.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter your shift start time.
  * This field is for the start time of your shift.
  * Please enter it in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Shift End Time**: Enter your shift end time.
  * This field is for the end time of your shift.
  * Please enter it in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Break Length**: Enter the length of your breaks.
  * This field is for the total length of your breaks.
  * Please enter the number of minutes.
* **Shift Length**: Enter the length of your shifts.
  * This field is for the total length of your shifts.
  * Please enter the number of hours.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid delays in the approval process.
* Ensure that the form is not submitted multiple times as this may cause confusion and duplication of effort.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
