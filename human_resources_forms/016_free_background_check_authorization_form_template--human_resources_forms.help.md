# Free Background Check Authorization Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Background Check Authorization Form Template is a document used to collect essential information from individuals, typically employees or job applicants, as part of background checks or other HR-related processes. It serves to gather accurate and comprehensive data, which is vital for making informed decisions or for verifying employment eligibility. This form template is designed to be user-friendly, ensuring that it's easy to understand and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select "Yes" or "No" as required for the "employee" field.** This field is used to indicate whether the individual in question is an employee.
2. **Enter the date of birth for the first employee (or select "Other" if applicable)**. This field is used to capture the date of birth, which is an essential piece of identifying information.
3. **Enter the address associated with the employee (if applicable)**. This field is used to collect the residential or work address.
4. **Enter the employee's number (if applicable)**. This field is intended for any employee ID or number.
5. **Enter the employee number two (if applicable)**. This field is for secondary employee identification.
6. **Enter the date of birth for the second employee (if applicable)**. Similar to the first field, this captures the date of birth for the second employee.
7. **Enter the address for the second employee (if applicable)**. This field is used to collect the residential or work address for the second employee.
8. **Select "Yes" or "No" or "Other" for the "select" field.** This field allows for multiple choices or responses.
9. **Add any additional notes or comments.** These fields are for any supplementary information or comments that need to be included with the background check or employment verification process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **employee**: This field is a select option, asking whether the individual is an employee. Please choose "Yes" or "No" as applicable.
- **date_of_birth**: Enter the date of birth for the employee, or select "Other" if the situation doesn't fit the standard options.
- **address**: Enter the residential or work address for the employee.
- **employee_number**: Enter any employee ID or number for identification purposes.
- **employee_number_two**: Enter a second employee ID or number if applicable.
- **date_of_birth_two**: Enter the date of birth for the second employee, following the same format as the first field.
- **address_two**: Enter the address for the second employee.
- **date_of_birth_three**: Enter the date of birth for the third employee (if applicable).
- **date_of_birth_four**: Enter the date of birth for the fourth employee (if applicable).
- **date_of_birth_2**: Enter the date of birth for the second employee with an alternate label.
- **date_of_birth_two_2**: Enter the date of birth for the third employee with an alternate label.
- **date_of_birth_three_2**: Enter the date of birth for the fourth employee (if applicable) with an alternate label.
- **select**: Choose "Yes", "No", or "Other" for multiple selections or comments.
- **note**: Add any additional comments or information related to the background check or employment verification process.
- **note_two**, **note_three**, **note_four**, **note_five**, **note_six**, **note_seven**, **note_eight**: These fields are for any additional comments or information that need to be included with the process.

**Tips**
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately to maintain the integrity and effectiveness of the background check or employment verification process.
- If you're unsure about any field or have questions, please review the form carefully or consult with your HR representative.
- Please do not hesitate to add any additional comments or information in the note fields to provide more context to the process.
