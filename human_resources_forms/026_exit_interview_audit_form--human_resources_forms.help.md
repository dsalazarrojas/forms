# exit interview audit form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exit interview audit form is designed to collect feedback and information from employees who are leaving the company. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and ensures a smooth transition for the departing employee. The form is a crucial tool for HR and management to understand the reasons behind an employee's departure and to make necessary changes to improve employee satisfaction and retention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your Department from the provided options in the "Department" field.
6. Enter your Job Position in the "Job Position" field.
7. Enter your Manager's Name in the "Manager Name" field.
8. Describe the reason for your departure in the "Reason for Departure" field.
9. Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5 in the "Job Satisfaction" field.
10. Rate your team satisfaction on a scale of 1-5 in the "Team Satisfaction" field.
11. Rate your communication with your manager and colleagues on a scale of 1-5 in the "Communication" field.
12. Enter your feedback and comments in the "Feedback" field.
13. Enter any suggestions you have for improving the company in the "Suggestions" field.
14. If applicable, enter your exit date in the "Exit Date" field.
15. Enter your last date attended in the "Last Date Attended" field.
16. Check the box to indicate if your manager was aware of your departure in the "Was Manager Aware" field.
17. Check the box to indicate if the exit interview was completed in the "Exit Interview Completed" field.
18. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (Text, Required): Select your department from the provided options.
* **Job Position** (Text, Required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Manager Name** (Text, Required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Reason for Departure** (Text, Required): Describe the reason for your departure.
* **Job Satisfaction** (Number, Required): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Team Satisfaction** (Number, Required): Rate your team satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication** (Number, Required): Rate your communication with your manager and colleagues on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback** (Text, Required): Enter your feedback and comments about your experience.
* **Suggestions** (Text, Required): Enter any suggestions you have for improving the company.
* **Exit Date** (Date, Optional): If applicable, enter your exit date.
* **Last Date Attended** (Date, Required): Enter your last date attended.
* **Was Manager Aware** (Select One, Required): Check the box to indicate if your manager was aware of your departure.
* **Exit Interview Completed** (Select One, Required): Check the box to indicate if the exit interview was completed.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
