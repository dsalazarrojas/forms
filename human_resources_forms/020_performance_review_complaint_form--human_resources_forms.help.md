# Performance Review Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for submitting complaints or issues related to performance reviews within your organization. It allows you to provide a description of the complaint, specify the category and department involved, and provide any additional information that may be relevant to resolving the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name for identification purposes.
2. Enter your email address for contact information.
3. Describe the complaint or issue you are experiencing.
4. Select the category and department related to your complaint.
5. Choose your supervisor for reporting purposes.
6. If applicable, select the date when the complaint occurred and when you would like to review the issue.
7. Select the status of your complaint.
8. Add any additional notes or comments.
9. Fill in the tool used to track the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text)**: Your first name for identification purposes. This is a required field.
* **Last Name (text)**: Your last name for identification purposes. This is a required field.
* **Email (email)**: Your email address for contact information. This field is optional.
* **Complaint Description (text)**: Describe the complaint or issue you are experiencing. This is a required field.
* **Category (select_one)**: Choose the category related to your complaint. Options are:
	+ Performance Review
	+ Evaluation
	+ Feedback
* **Department (select_multiple)**: Select the department related to your complaint. Options are:
	+ Human Resources
	+ Finance
	+ Operations
* **Supervisor (select_one)**: Choose your supervisor for reporting purposes. Options are:
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Joe
* **Complaint Date (date)**: If applicable, select the date when the complaint occurred.
* **Review Date (date)**: If applicable, select the date when you would like to review the issue.
* **Complaint Status (select_one)**: Select the status of your complaint. Options are:
	+ Active
	+ Resolved
* **Notes (text)**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool (text)**: Fill in the tool used to track the complaint.
* **Created By (text)**: This field is for internal use only.
* **Updated By (text)**: This field is for internal use only.
* **Created At (date)**: This field is for internal use only and indicates when the complaint was created.
* **Updated At (date)**: This field is for internal use only and indicates when the complaint was last updated.
* **Deleted At (date)**: This field is for internal use only and indicates when the complaint was deleted.
