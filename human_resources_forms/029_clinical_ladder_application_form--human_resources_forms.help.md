# Clinical Ladder Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for Clinical staff to submit an application to advance their career level. The form is used to gather information about the applicant's qualifications, experience, and achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options for each field as needed.
3. Provide detailed and clear responses to all questions.
4. Attach any additional supporting documents or evidence as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID or government-issued ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Current Clinical Level** (`current_level`, select_one, required): Select the current clinical level you are currently at.
* **Desired Clinical Level** (`desired_level`, select_one, required): Select the clinical level you desire to advance to.
* **Department or Unit** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department or unit you are currently working in.
* **Date of Hire** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter your date of hire.
* **-- Professional Portfolio --** (`section_portfolio`, note, optional): This section is optional, but if you have any relevant professional certifications, publications, or presentations, please include them here.
* **Summary of clinical achievements in the past 12 months** (`clinical_achievements`, text, required): Describe your significant clinical achievements and contributions in the past 12 months.
* **List of active professional certifications held** (`professional_certifications`, text, optional): This section is optional, but if you have any current professional certifications, please list them here.
* **Continuing Education Hours completed this calendar year** (`ce_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of CE hours you have completed this year.
* **-- Leadership and Contribution --** (`section_contribution`, note, optional): This section is optional, but if you have any leadership or contribution activities, please describe them here.
* **Committees or project teams you lead or participate in** (`committee_participation`, text, optional): List any committees or project teams you are leading or participating in.
* **Description of peer review or mentorship activities** (`peer_mentorship`, text, optional): Describe any peer review or mentorship activities you have participated in.
* **-- Manager Support --** (`section_support`, note, optional): This section is optional, but if your manager has any comments or recommendations, please include them here.
* **Immediate Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your immediate manager.
* **I have discussed this application with my direct supervisor** (`supervisor_discussion`, select_one, required): Select whether you have discussed this application with your direct supervisor.
* **Applicant Signature - Type Full Name** (`digital_signature`, text, required): Sign your name with your full name as it appears on your ID.
