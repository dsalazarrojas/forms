# Staff Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect and manage staff member information in the human resources system. The data collected will help to track staff details, role, department, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're filling out the correct information for the relevant staff member.
2. Select the role(s) and department that apply to the staff member.
3. Enter the staff member's job title, manager's information, and other relevant details.
4. Enter the staff member's contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Select the hire and termination dates, if applicable.
6. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the staff member's user ID.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more roles that apply to the staff member. This will help categorize the staff member in the system.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Select the staff member's department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the staff member's job title.
* **Manager Info** (`manager_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the manager's information, if applicable.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the manager's name, if applicable.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the manager's title, if applicable.
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the staff member.
* **Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the staff member's name.
* **Title** (`employee_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the staff member's title.
* **Reporting Manager Info** (`reporting_manager_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the reporting manager's information.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, required/optional): Enter the reporting manager's name.
* **Reporting Manager Title** (`reporting_manager_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the reporting manager's title.
* **Email** (`employee_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the staff member's email address.
* **Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the staff member's phone number.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the staff member's hire date, if applicable.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the staff member's termination date, if applicable.
* **Last Eval Date** (`last_eval_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the last evaluation, if applicable.
