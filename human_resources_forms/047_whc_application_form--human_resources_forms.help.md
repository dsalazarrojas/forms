This guide is intended to help users fill out the Whc Application Form, a critical component of the human resources management process. This form is used to collect important information about job applicants, ensuring that the hiring process is efficient and effective. Before starting, please ensure that you have not already submitted a Whc Application Form for this position; duplicating efforts can lead to confusion and unnecessary work for the hiring team.

## Purpose
The Whc Application Form is designed to capture essential details about job applicants, including their personal details, work history, and job requirements. The goal is to gather relevant information to assess their suitability for the position and facilitate the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting a blank form from the application portal or download it as a template.
2. Fill out the personal details section:
	* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your work history:
	* Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
	* Enter your department in the "Department" field (if applicable).
	* Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities in the "Job Description" field (if applicable).
4. Select your reporting manager's name in the "Reporting Manager" field.
5. Enter your reporting manager's email address in the "Reporting Manager Email" field (if applicable).
6. Enter your work start and end dates (if applicable) in the "Work Start Date" and "Work End Date" fields, respectively.
7. Enter your salary details in the "Salary" field.
8. Select your years of work experience from the options provided in the "Work Experience" field.
9. If you are currently available to start, enter the date in the "Available to start" field.
10. If applicable, select your availability to start in the "Available to start time" field.
11. Finally, select your current job status from the options provided in the "Job Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name. This is the first name you were given at birth or the name you go by.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname or family name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use or can be reached at.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date you were born. This is not required, but it can help in verifying your age and employment history.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or desired job title. This can help the hiring team understand your work responsibilities and level.
* **Department**: Enter your current or desired department. If you are not sure, leave this field blank.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities. This can help the hiring team understand your work history and experience.
* **Reporting Manager**: Enter the name of your current or desired reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email**: Enter the email address of your current or desired reporting manager.
* **Work Start Date**: Enter the date you started your current or previous job. This helps in verifying your employment history.
* **Work End Date**: Enter the date you ended your current or previous job. This is optional and can help in verifying your employment history.
* **Salary**: Enter your current or desired salary. This can help in setting expectations and compensation.
* **Benefits**: Enter any benefits you are eligible for or have received, such as health insurance, retirement plans, etc.
* **Work Experience**: Select the number of years you have worked in the industry or in similar roles.
* **Available to start**: Enter the date you are available to start the new position. If you are currently employed, enter the date you can start after your current employment ends.
* **Available to start time**: If you are currently available to start, enter the time of day you can start.
* **Job Status**: Select the status of your current job, which can be Active, Inactive, On vacation, or On leave.

Remember to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your application is properly processed and considered.
