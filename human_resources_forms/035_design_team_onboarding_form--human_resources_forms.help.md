# Design Team Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Design Team Onboarding Form is a tool used to collect relevant information about new hires joining the Design Team, helping to streamline the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the new hire's details in the "New Hire Details" section.
2. Enter the new hire's contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Select the new hire's job title from the options provided in the "Job Title" section.
4. Specify the new hire's work location from the options provided in the "Work Location" section.
5. Enter a brief description of the new hire's job in the "Job Description" section.
6. Select the preferred communication method for the new hire in the "Preferred Communication Method" section.
7. Enter the new hire's time zone in the "Time Zone" section.
8. Update the onboarding status in the "Onboarding Status" section.
9. Enter the manager's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding sections.
10. Enter the team lead's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding sections.
11. Assign the new hire to a team member in the "Assigned To" section.
12. Finally, confirm the dates for when the onboarding process was created and updated in the "Created At" and "Updated At" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Hire Details
* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the new hire's name, employee ID, or any other relevant details.

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the new hire's phone number, email, and any other relevant contact details.

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the new hire's job title from the options provided:
	+ Design Team Lead
	+ Team Member
	+ Intern

* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the new hire's work location from the options provided:
	+ Remote
	+ Office
	+ Hybrid

* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `note`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the new hire's job.

* **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the preferred communication method for the new hire:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Slack

* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the new hire's time zone.

* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Update the onboarding status from the options provided:
	+ Completed
	+ In Progress
	+ Not Started

* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the manager's name.

* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the manager's email.

* **Manager's Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the manager's phone number.

* **Team Lead's Name** (`team_lead_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the team lead's name.

* **Team Lead's Email** (`team_lead_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the team lead's email.

* **Team Lead's Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the team lead's phone number.

* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the team member the new hire will be assigned to from the options provided:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ Other

* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, required: false): Confirm the date the onboarding process was created.

* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, required: false): Confirm the date the onboarding process was updated.
