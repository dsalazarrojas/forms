# Employee Disciplinary Action Follow Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the status and follow-up information of disciplinary actions taken against employees. It helps in maintaining accurate records and tracking progress towards resolving the issues raised.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Action Status** from the provided options to indicate the current status of the disciplinary action.
2. Enter the **Action Date** if the action was taken on a specific date.
3. If applicable, enter the **Last Conducted** date to indicate the last time the action was conducted.
4. Provide the **Last Contact** date if you've had any contact with the employee regarding the disciplinary action.
5. In the **Notes** field, describe any additional information or context related to the action.
6. Choose the **Outcome** of the disciplinary action from the provided options.
7. In the **Comments** field, provide any comments or feedback from the employee or other parties involved.
8. Select **Outcomes Follow Up Required** if the disciplinary action requires follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Action Status** (Select One): Choose the current status of the disciplinary action, whether it's pending, completed, or ongoing.
* **Action Date** (Date): Enter the date when the disciplinary action was taken.
* **Last Conducted** (Date): If the action is ongoing, provide the last date it was conducted.
* **Last Contact** (Date): Provide the date of the last contact with the employee about the action.
* **Notes** (Text): Add any additional context or details related to the action.
* **Outcome** (Select Multiple): Choose the outcome of the disciplinary action.
* **Comments** (Text): Provide any comments or feedback from the employee or other parties involved.
* **Outcomes Follow Up Required** (Select Multiple): Check if the disciplinary action requires follow-up to ensure progress is being made.
