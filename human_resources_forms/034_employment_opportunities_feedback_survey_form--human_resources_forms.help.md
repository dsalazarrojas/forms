# Employment Opportunities Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employment Opportunities Feedback Survey Form is a tool used to gather feedback from individuals who have applied for or responded to a job posting. By completing this form, you will help the HR team understand how job seekers discover job postings, their level of interest in the role, what they know about the company, and their experience with the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Introduction
There is no information to fill in this page, it is a note to provide context and background information about the form.

### 2. How did you find out about this job
Please select all the ways you found out about this job. This could include social media, job boards, a company's website, or a friend or colleague.

### 3. How would you rate your interest in this role
Please select your level of interest in the role from the following options:
* Very high
* Somewhat high
* Not very high
* Not at all

### 4. What do you know about the company
Please select all the ways you know about the company. This could include from a website, a friend or colleague, or a job ad.

### 5. What did you like about the hiring process
Please describe any positive experiences you had during the hiring process.

### 6. What did you dislike about the hiring process
Please describe any negative experiences you had during the hiring process.

### 7. How can we improve the hiring process
Please provide any suggestions on how the hiring process could be improved.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide as much detail as you can when completing this form.
- If you have any additional feedback or comments, please include them in the "Other" section.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the HR team at [email or contact information].
