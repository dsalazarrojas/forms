# Employee Retention Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather crucial information about new employees joining the employee retention team. This includes basic personal details such as their name and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field, ensuring it is a valid email format.
4. If applicable, select your job title from the available options or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you have a specific job title, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in your details accurately to ensure proper onboarding and team communication.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
- If you have any additional information or comments, please feel free to add them in the space provided, although it is not a formal requirement.
