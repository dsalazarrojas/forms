# Christmas Gift List Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Christmas Gift List Form is used to collect information about an employee's preferences for Christmas gifts, including their interests, budget, and any specific items they would like or avoid. This information will be used to select a suitable gift for each employee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the employee information sections (First Name, Last Name, Employee ID, Department) with your relevant details.
2. Select one or more interest categories that best describe your interests.
3. Choose your preferred gift type (physical gift, gift card, experience, combination, or flexible).
4. Indicate your budget range preference.
5. List up to 5 specific items you would like or dislike.
6. If you have a preferred brand or retailer, list it here.
7. If you have any restrictions or preferences, list them here.
8. If you have any items to avoid, list them here.
9. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, list them here.
10. If you have a size preference for clothing or shoes, list it here.
11. If you would prefer a donation to a charity instead, choose 'True' and indicate the preferred charity type.
12. You can add any additional notes or special requests here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Employee Information --**: Please fill out your name, employee ID, and department for identification purposes.
  - **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
  - **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
  - **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number for identification purposes.
  - **Department**: Select your department for identification purposes.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - (This field is required for communication purposes.)
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
  - (This field is optional and only if you want to be contacted for further details.)
- **-- Gift Preferences --**: This section contains multiple fields for gift preferences.
  - **Select all that apply**: Choose one or more interest categories that best describe your interests.
  - **Preferred Gift Type**: Choose your preferred gift type (physical gift, gift card, experience, combination, or flexible).
  - **Budget Range Preference**: Select your preferred budget range.
  - **List 3-5 items**: List up to 5 specific items you would like or dislike.
  - **Preferred Brands or Retailers**: If you have a preferred brand or retailer, list it here.
  - **Restrictions & Preferences**: List any restrictions or preferences you may have.
  - **Items to Avoid**: If you have items you would like to avoid, list them here.
  - **Allergies or Sensitivities**: List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
  - **Size Information (if applicable)**: If you have a size preference for clothing or shoes, list it here.
  - **Would you prefer a donation to charity instead?**: If you would prefer a donation, choose 'True' and select the preferred charity type.
  - **Preferred Charity Type**: If you would prefer a donation, select the preferred charity type.
  - **Additional Notes or Special Requests**: Add any additional notes or special requests here.
