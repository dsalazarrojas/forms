# sales_trainer_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sales_trainer_interview_form" is a form used to gather information about a sales trainer candidate. It is used by hiring managers or recruiters to assess a candidate's qualifications and suitability for a sales trainer position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the candidate's name in the "name" field.
* Enter the number of years of experience the candidate has in a sales role in the "years_of_experience" field.
* Enter the name of the candidate's previous company in the "previous_company" field.
* Select the next steps you would like to discuss with the candidate in the "next_steps" field (e.g., "Next Step 1", "Next Step 2", etc.).
* Enter the date of the interview in the "interview_date" field.
* Enter the time of the interview in the "interview_time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the candidate's full name.
- **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years the candidate has been working in a sales role.
- **Previous Company** (`previous_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the candidate's previous company.
  Note: This will help you keep track of who the candidate worked with and what they accomplished.
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select multiple, required): Select the next steps you would like to discuss with the candidate.
  Note: You can select multiple steps to discuss with the candidate in this order.
- **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the interview.
  Note: Use the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD) to ensure accuracy.
- **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the interview.
  Note: Use the correct time format (HH:MM) to ensure accuracy.
