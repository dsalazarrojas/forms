# Employee Change Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Change Form is a tool used by HR personnel to update employee records. It is used to track and maintain accurate employee information, including status changes. This form should only be used when there is a need to make a change to an employee's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Locate the Employee ID field and enter the employee's identification number.
2. Select the status of the employee from the dropdown menu (Active, Inactive, Terminated, or Retired).
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reason for the change in the Reason for Change field.
4. Enter the effective date for the change in the Change Effective Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required: false): Enter the employee's identification number to ensure accuracy in tracking changes.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the employee from the available options (Active, Inactive, Terminated, or Retired).
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, required: false): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the reason behind the status change.
* **Change Effective Date** (`change_effective_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the change will take effect.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid errors.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form to ensure that it is complete and correct.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact the HR department for assistance.
