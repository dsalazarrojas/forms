# Employee Benefits Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Employee Benefits Knowledge Quiz" form is a self-assessment tool designed to test your understanding of various employee benefits offered by the organization. It consists of multiple pages, each focusing on a different type of benefit. The form will ask you to answer questions about whether you are aware of the benefits or not.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate through the form by clicking on the "Back" button or submit your answers by clicking on the "Submit" button at the end of the form.
2. For each page, read the question carefully and select the corresponding answer.
3. Make sure to answer all questions on each page before proceeding to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro Page (1)** (Note, Optional): This is the introduction page, where you will get a brief overview of the form.
* **Health Insurance (2)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Answer "Yes" if you are aware of the health insurance benefit, or "No" if you are not.
* **Retirement Plans (3)** (Select One, Optional): Answer "Yes" if you are aware of the retirement plans benefit, or "No" if you are not.
* **Time Off (4)** (Select One, Optional): Answer "Yes" if you are aware of the time off benefit, or "No" if you are not.
* **Benefits (5)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Answer "Yes" if you have access to any of the benefits, or "No" if you don't have access.
* **Entitlements (6)** (Select One, Optional): Answer "Yes" if you are aware of the entitlements benefit, or "No" if you are not.
* **Quiz (7)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Answer "Yes" if you have completed any of the quizzes, or "No" if you haven't.
* **Summary (8)** (Note, Optional): This is a summary page, where your answers will be displayed.
* **Submit (9)** (Text, Optional): Click this button to submit your answers.
* **Back (10)** (Text, Optional): Click this button to go back to the previous page.

Note: All questions are optional, so you can answer them at your own pace.
