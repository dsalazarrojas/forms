# employee_productivity_research_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their productivity, research interests, and availability for volunteer work. The data collected will be used for research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section of the form carefully.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information for each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info (id: 1)**: Enter your name, department, and job title to help us better understand your role.
* **Availability (id: 2)**: Describe your availability for research projects, including your ideal work hours and any time constraints.
* **Study Interests (id: 3)**: List your study interests, such as topics or areas you'd like to explore further.
* **Volunteer (id: 4)**: Describe your current or past volunteer work experience, including relevant skills or projects.
* **Availability 2 (id: 5)**: Provide more details about your availability for research projects, if different from your initial response.
* **Study Info (id: 6)**: Share any relevant study information, such as research papers or courses you've completed.
* **Comments (id: 7)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your research interests or availability.
* **Submit (id: 8)**: Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We appreciate your participation in this research and thank you for your time.
