# Maternity Leave Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maternity Leave Application Form Template is designed for employees to apply for maternity leave. It captures essential information about the employee, their leave duration, and the status of their leave application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with the required information.
2.  Ensure you have attached all necessary supporting documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required): This section is for entering your employee details.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or other official documentation.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your manager has approved your maternity leave application.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Please select the department you belong to for work purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Please enter your current job title.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you wish to commence your maternity leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you expect to return to work after your maternity leave.
* **Maternity Leave Status** (`maternity_leave_status`, select_multiple, required): Please select if your maternity leave status is Active or Inactive.
* **Reason for Maternity Leave** (`reason_for_maternity_leave`, select_multiple, required): Please select the reason for your maternity leave, indicating if it is "Yes" or "No".
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Please enter the start date of your maternity leave.
* **Return to Work Date** (`return_to_work_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you expect to return to work after your maternity leave.
* **Total Number of Days Off** (`total_number_of_days_off`, number, required): Please enter the total number of days you will be taking for your maternity leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select if your maternity leave is "Yes" or "No".
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_multiple, required): Please select if your maternity leave application status is Active or Inactive.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Please enter any additional comments about your maternity leave application.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, select_one, optional): Please select if you have any attachments to submit with your application.

Note: Ensure that you have attached all necessary supporting documents before submitting the form.
