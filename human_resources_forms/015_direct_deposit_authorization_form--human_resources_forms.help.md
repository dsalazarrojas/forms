# Direct Deposit Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize direct deposits from your paychecks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your personal details in the Employee Name field.
2. Provide your company's information in the Company Name field.
3. Enter the bank account number where you want the direct deposit to be made in the Direct Deposit Bank Account Number field.
4. If you have already authorized someone to sign on your behalf, you can enter their name in the Authorized Signature Employee Name field.
5. If you want to specify the date you authorized someone to sign on your behalf, you can enter it in the Authorized Signature Date field.
6. You can add your signature in the Authorized Signature field. This is where the HR representative will sign to verify your authorization.
7. You can also add the name of the person who authorized the signature in the Authorized By field.
8. Finally, enter the date you authorized the direct deposit in the Date Authored field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Direct Deposit Bank Account Number** (Direct Deposit Bank Account Number, text, required): Enter the bank account number where you want the direct deposit to be made. This is an example: XXXXXXXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Authorized Signature Date** (Authorized Signature Date, date, optional): If you have already authorized someone to sign on your behalf, enter the date you authorized them to do so. This is optional and only needed if you have delegated someone to sign for you.
* **Authorized Signature** (Authorized Signature, text, required): This is where you or your authorized representative will add their signature to verify your authorization.
* **Authorized Signature Employee Name** (Authorized Signature Employee Name, text, optional): If you have authorized someone to sign on your behalf, enter their name here.
* **Date Authored** (Date Authored, date, optional): Enter the date you authorized the direct deposit.
* **Authorized By** (Authorized By, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who authorized the signature.

Note: Only the required fields (Employee Name, Company Name, and Direct Deposit Bank Account Number) are necessary to complete the form. The other fields are optional but can provide additional information for HR verification purposes.
