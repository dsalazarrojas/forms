# Curriculum Development Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for Curriculum Development purposes. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the leave request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select a start date and end date for your leave of absence.
2. Provide a reason for your leave request.
3. Indicate whether your team lead and manager have approved your leave request.
4. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Vacation, Sick, or Personal).
5. Add any additional comments about your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (Date, required): Enter the date you would like to start your leave of absence. Please ensure that this date is accurate and realistic, as it will be used to process your leave request.
* **End Date** (Date, required): Enter the date you would like to end your leave of absence. Again, please ensure that this date is accurate and realistic.
* **Reason** (Text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave request. This will help your team lead and manager understand your request and process it efficiently.
* **Team Lead Approve** (Select Multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if your team lead has approved your leave request, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Manager Approve** (Select Multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved your leave request, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Leave Type** (Select One, required): Select the type of leave you are requesting (Vacation, Sick, or Personal). This will help your team lead and manager understand the nature of your leave request.
* **Comments** (Text, required): Add any additional comments about your leave request. This is an opportunity to provide further information that may be helpful in processing your request.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your leave request is processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
