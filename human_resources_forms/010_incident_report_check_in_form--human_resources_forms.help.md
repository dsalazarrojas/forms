# incident_report_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather information about incidents that have occurred within the organization. It is used for reporting and tracking incidents, including their location, description, status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
3. Select the location where the incident occurred from the "Incident Location" field.
4. Choose the type of incident from the "Incident Type" field.
5. Select the status of the incident from the "Incident Status" field.
6. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
7. Identify the employee who reported the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
8. Specify the department involved in the incident in the "Incident Department" field.
9. Indicate the priority level of the incident from the "Incident Priority" field.
10. Enter any additional notes regarding the incident in the "Incident Notes" field.
11. Provide a detailed resolution or outcome of the incident in the "Incident Resolution" field.
12. Specify if a follow-up is required in the "Incident Follow Up" field.
13. Indicate if the incident is closed in the "Incident Closed" field.
14. Specify if the incident has been reviewed in the "Incident Reviewed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Details (`report_details`):** This field is not explicitly defined but can be a general description of the incident.
* **Incident Location (`incident_location`):** Enter the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description (`incident_description`):** Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Type (`incident_type`):** Select the type of incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Status (`incident_status`):** Choose the current status of the incident.
* **Incident Date (`incident_date`):** Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time (`incident_time`):** Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Reporter (`incident_reporter`):** Identify the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Department (`incident_department`):** Specify the department involved in the incident.
* **Incident Priority (`incident_priority`):** Select the priority level of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Category (`incident_category`):** Not applicable, not required field.
* **Incident Notes (`incident_notes`):** Enter any additional information or context regarding the incident.
* **Incident Resolution (`incident_resolution`):** Provide the outcome or resolution of the incident.
* **Incident Follow Up (`incident_follow_up`):** Specify if a follow-up is required.
* **Incident Closed (`incident_closed`):** Indicate if the incident is closed.
* **Incident Reviewed (`incident_reviewed`):** Specify if the incident has been reviewed.
