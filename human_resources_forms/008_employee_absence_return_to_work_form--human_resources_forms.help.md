# Employee Absence Return To Work Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Absence Return To Work Form is used to document an employee's return to work after an absence. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure correct leave history records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all necessary information with you before starting.
*   Read each field explanation below to understand what information is required for each field.
*   Fill out the form carefully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Physicians Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of the physician who has certified that the employee is fit to return to work. Please enter the physician's name as it appears on their medical license.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is for the physician's signature, which confirms that the employee is fit to return to work. You are not required to fill out this field, but it's recommended for official records.
*   **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, text, optional): This field is for the employee's unique identification number. Please enter the number as it appears on the company's HR system.
*   **Absence Reason** (`absence_reason`, text, optional): This field is for the reason of the employee's absence. Please provide a brief description of the reason for the absence.
*   **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date the employee is expected to return to work. Please ensure this date is accurate to maintain correct leave history.
*   **Physician Signature Date** (`physician_signature_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date the physician signed the return-to-work form. This is for official records and should be completed by the physician.
*   **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, select_one, optional): This field is for the physician to confirm that the employee is fit to return to work. Select 'Yes' if the physician agrees, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for the employee's status. Select 'Active' if the employee is back on the job and 'Inactive' otherwise.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any field, please check with your HR representative or supervisor.
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to maintain correct records.
- If you're filling this form for another employee, please ensure you have the necessary permissions to access that employee's information.
