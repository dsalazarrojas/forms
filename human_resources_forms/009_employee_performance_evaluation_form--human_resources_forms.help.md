# employee_performance_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Performance Evaluation Form is used to assess an employee's performance over a specific period. It helps supervisors and managers to evaluate an employee's progress towards goals, identify areas for improvement, and provide feedback and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the evaluation period from the options provided in the "evaluation_frequency" field.
2. Evaluate the employee's performance by selecting the goals they have met in the "goals_met" field.
3. Enter a performance score in the "performance_score" field, using a scale provided by your organization.
4. Provide comments on the employee's performance in the "performance_comments" field.
5. Enter the evaluation date in the "evaluation_date" field.
6. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
7. Enter your own name and title in the "supervisor_name" and "job_title" fields, respectively.
8. Select the approval status in the "approval_status" field.
9. Enter the submission date in the "submission_date" field.
10. Optionally, add any additional comments in the "supervisor_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **evaluation_period**: Select the period of time over which you are evaluating the employee's performance. For example, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually.
* **goals_met**: Select all the goals that the employee has met during the evaluation period.
* **performance_score**: Enter a score representing the employee's overall performance, using the scale provided by your organization.
* **comments**: Add any comments or feedback you have on the employee's performance.
* **evaluation_date**: Enter the date on which this performance evaluation is being submitted.
* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **supervisor_name**: Enter your name and title.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title.
* **approval_status**: Select the approval status of the form, which can be Approved, Not Approved, or Pending.
* **performance_comments**: Add any comments on the employee's performance.
* **supervisor_comments**: Add any comments on the employee's performance from your perspective.
* **form_version**: Enter the version of the form you are using.
* **form_status**: Select the status of the form, which can be Active, Inactive, or Draft.
* **submission_date**: Enter the date on which the form was submitted.
* **submitted_by**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **form_id**: Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **department_id**: Enter the ID of the department the employee belongs to.
* **job_id**: Enter the ID of the job the employee holds.
* **evaluation_frequency**: Select the frequency of performance evaluations, which can be Quarterly, Semi-annually, or Annually.
* **evaluation_due_date**: Enter the date by which the evaluation is due.
* **employee_number**: Enter the employee number.
* **performance_rating**: Enter a rating of the employee's performance, using the scale provided by your organization.
* **evaluation_frequency**: Select the frequency of performance evaluations, which can be Quarterly, Semi-annually, or Annually.
* **form_submitter**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **department**: Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title.
