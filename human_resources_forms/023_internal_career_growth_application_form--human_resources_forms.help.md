# Internal Career Growth Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Career Growth Application Form is a tool for employees and job seekers to submit their applications for career growth and development opportunities within the company. This form helps capture essential information about the applicant, their background, skills, and preferences for career advancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form header** (page 1): Enter your name and email address to start the application process.
2. **Provide contact information** (page 2): Enter your phone number and department you're interested in.
3. **Describe your background** (page 3-4):
	* Upload a resume or CV to provide detailed information about your education, work experience, and skills.
	* Enter your motivation for applying for a career growth opportunity.
	* List your skills relevant to the job.
4. **Specify your job preferences** (page 4-5):
	* Select the department(s) you're interested in.
	* Choose your preferred role(s).
	* Enter any additional information about your experience.
5. **Provide employment details** (page 6-7):
	* Enter your current job title.
	* List previous jobs you've held.
	* Provide references (name and contact details).
6. **Schedule the start date** (page 8):
	* Enter your expected start date.
7. **Submit your application** (page 9):
	* Click on the "Submit" button to finalize your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
• **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
• **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
• **Department**: Choose the department you're interested in growing your career within (HR, Finance, Marketing, Sales, or Support).
• **Role**: Select one or more roles you're interested in (Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Software Engineer, Product Manager, or Business Analyst).
• **Resume**: Upload a resume or CV that includes details about your education, work experience, and skills.
• **Motivation**: Enter your reasons for wanting to grow your career within the company.
• **Skills List**: List your skills that are relevant to the job.
• **Expected Start Date**: Enter your expected start date for the new role.
• **Current Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
• **Previous Jobs**: List previous jobs you've held.
• **References**: Provide references (name and contact details).
• **Education**: Enter details about your educational background.
• **Skills**: Choose relevant skills you have (Communication, Leadership, Teamwork, Problem Solving, Time Management, Data Analysis, or Project Management).
• **Experience**: Enter any additional information about your experience that's not on your resume.
• **Notice Period**: If you have a fixed notice period, enter it.
• **Submit**: Click on this button to finalize your application.
