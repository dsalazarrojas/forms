# Role Closure Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Closure Confirmation Request Form is used to request and manage role closure processes for HR and IT teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for role closure from the list of options provided.
2. Enter the effective date of the role closure.
3. Provide a brief description of the reason for role closure.
4. Choose the managers and/or IT personnel who need to approve the role closure.
5. Enter any comments or notes relevant to the role closure.
6. Confirm the closure of the role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Role Closure Requested By** (`role_closure_requested_by`): 
  * Select the name of the person requesting the role closure.
  * Options: HR-Manager, IT-Manager, Manager
  * Required: No

- **Reason For Closure** (`reason_for_closure`): 
  * Enter a brief description of the reason for role closure.
  * Required: No

- **Effective Date** (`effective_date`): 
  * Enter the date when the role will be closed.
  * Format: MM/DD/YYYY
  * Required: No

- **Reason For Closure Description** (`reason_for_closure_description`): 
  * Enter a detailed description of the reason for role closure.
  * Required: No

- **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`): 
  * Select multiple managers who need to approve the role closure.
  * Options: approver1, approver2, approver3
  * Required: No

- **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`): 
  * Enter any comments or notes from the managers.
  * Required: No

- **IT Approvals** (`it_approvals`): 
  * Select multiple IT personnel who need to approve the role closure.
  * Options: approver1, approver2, approver3
  * Required: No

- **IT Comments** (`it_comments`): 
  * Enter any comments or notes from IT personnel.
  * Required: No

- **HR Approval** (`hr_approval`): 
  * Select the HR personnel who need to approve the role closure.
  * Options: approver1, approver2, approver3
  * Required: No

- **Closure Date** (`closure_date`): 
  * Enter the date when the role will be officially closed.
  * Format: MM/DD/YYYY
  * Required: No

- **Closure Reason** (`closure_reason`): 
  * Select the reason for role closure from the list of options provided.
  * Options: reason1, reason2, reason3
  * Required: No

- **Confirmation** (`confirmation`): 
  * Enter a confirmation message to confirm the role closure.
  * Required: No

- **Additional Notes** (`notes`): 
  * Enter any other relevant notes.
  * Required: No

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct reason for role closure.
- Ensure that all necessary approvals are captured.
- Enter any relevant comments or notes.
- Confirm the role closure to complete the process.
