# Employee Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluation on an employee's performance, providing a structured approach to assess an employee's strengths and areas for improvement. This form is used to help employees grow professionally and improve their job performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Rate the employee's performance using the appraisal score section.
3. Identify areas of improvement and provide an action plan to address them.
4. Provide feedback and comments from both the employee and the manager.
5. Select the overall assessment for the employee's performance.
6. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Employee Details
* **Employee Information** (`employee_appraisal_form_page_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's name, email, and phone number to associate with this appraisal.
* **Appraisal Score** (appraisal_score, number, required/optional): Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Performance Metrics** (employee_appraisal_form_page_2, text, required/optional): Describe the employee's key performance metrics, such as sales figures, project completion rates, etc.
* **Skills and Knowledge** (skills_and_knowledge, text, required/optional): Note the employee's skills and knowledge areas of strength and improvement.
* **Areas of Improvement** (areas_of_improvement, text, required/optional): Identify areas where the employee needs to improve and provide an action plan to address them.
* **Action Plan** (action_plan, text, required/optional): Describe the steps the employee will take to improve in the identified areas.
* **Employee Feedback** (employee_feedback, text, required/optional): Provide feedback from the employee on their performance and areas of improvement.
* **Manager Feedback** (manager_feedback, text, required/optional): Provide feedback from the manager on the employee's performance and areas of improvement.
* **Date of Appraisal** (date_of_appraisal, date, required/optional): Record the date of the appraisal.
* **Time of Appraisal** (time_of_appraisal, time, required/optional): Record the time of the appraisal.

### Page 2: Overall Assessment
* **Overall Assessment** (overall_assessment, select_one, required): Select the overall assessment of the employee's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Areas of Improvement 2** (areas_of_improvement_2, text, required/optional): Identify additional areas of improvement and provide an action plan to address them.
* **Action Plan 2** (action_plan_2, text, required/optional): Describe the steps the employee will take to improve in the identified areas.
* **Manager Feedback 2** (manager_feedback_2, text, required/optional): Provide feedback from the manager on the employee's performance and areas of improvement.
* **Date of Appraisal 2** (date_of_appraisal_2, date, required/optional): Record the date of the appraisal.
* **Time of Appraisal 2** (time_of_appraisal_2, time, required/optional): Record the time of the appraisal.
* **Email 2** (email_2, email, required/optional): Record the employee's email address.
* **Phone 2** (phone_2, text, required/optional): Record the employee's phone number.

### Page 3: Notes and Submission
* **Overall Assessment 2** (overall_assessment_2, select_one, required): Select the overall assessment of the employee's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Notes** (notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the appraisal.
* **Submit** (submit, text, required/optional): Click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.
