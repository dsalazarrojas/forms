# Retail Sales Assistant Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for Retail Sales Assistant Interview, a form designed to gather data about the store's sales assistants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the store name field (`store_name`) with the name of the store where the sales assistant works.
2. Check the `shift_schedules` field to indicate whether the sales assistant has shift schedules or not. Select all the options that apply.
3. Enter the sales assistant's availability in the `availability` field.
4. If necessary, provide additional availability details in the `availability_2` field.
5. Answer 'yes' or 'no' to indicate the sales assistant's experience in the `experience` field.
6. If necessary, provide more details about the sales assistant's experience in the `experience_2`, `experience_3`, `experience_4`, `experience_5`, `experience_6`, `experience_7`, `experience_8`, `experience_9`, `experience_10` fields.
7. Use the `candidate_info` field for any additional information about the candidate.
8. Enter the sales assistant's contact information in the `contact_info` field, including name, address, and phone number.
9. Use the `contact_info_2` field for any additional contact information.
10. Enter the sales assistant's email address in the `email` field.
