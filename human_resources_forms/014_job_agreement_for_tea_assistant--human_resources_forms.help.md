# Job Agreement For Tea Assistant - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the job agreement details for a tea assistant, including their name, job title, start and end dates, hourly wage, payment frequency, and termination details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields, which include:
	* Tea assistant name
	* Job title
	* Start date
	* End date
	* Hourly wage
	* Payment frequency
2. You can then select the payment frequency for the tea assistant, which can be weekly, biweekly, monthly, or bi-monthly.
3. If the tea assistant will be working irregular hours, you can enter the number of hours they will work.
4. The tea assistant can take breaks at any time, and you can specify the number of breaks they will take.
5. Describe the duties and responsibilities of the tea assistant.
6. Select the notice type for termination, which can be Regular, Probationary, or Terminable.
7. Enter the notice period in days.
8. Specify the payment terms for termination.
9. Enter the amount of termination payment.
10. Select the payment frequency for termination.
11. Enter the number of hours to be paid for termination.
12. Specify any paid holidays.
13. Enter the number of holidays worked by the tea assistant.
14. If the tea assistant is terminated for a reason not listed, you can enter the reason here.
15. Finally, add any employer notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tea assistant name** (`tea_assistant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tea assistant.
* **job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the tea assistant.
* **start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the tea assistant's employment.
* **end date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the tea assistant's employment.
* **hourly wage** (`hourly_wage`, number, required): Enter the hourly wage of the tea assistant.
* **pay frequency** (`pay_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the payment frequency for the tea assistant (weekly, biweekly, monthly, or bi-monthly).
* **pay frequency** (`pay_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the payment frequency for termination (weekly, biweekly, monthly, or bi-monthly).
* **hours worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours the tea assistant will work.
* **breaks** (`breaks`, number, optional): Enter the number of breaks the tea assistant takes.
* **duties** (`duties`, text, optional): Describe the duties and responsibilities of the tea assistant.
* **responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, text, optional): Describe the responsibilities of the tea assistant.
* **termination date** (`termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the termination date of the tea assistant.
* **termination reason** (`termination_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for termination.
* **notice period** (`notice_period`, text, optional): Enter the notice period in days.
* **notice type** (`notice_type`, select_one, optional): Select the notice type for termination (Regular, Probationary, or Terminable).
* **notice days** (`notice_days`, number, optional): Enter the notice days.
* **payment terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Specify the payment terms for termination.
* **termination payment** (`termination_payment`, number, optional): Enter the amount of termination payment.
* **payment frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the payment frequency for termination (weekly, biweekly, monthly, or bi-monthly).
* **hours to pay** (`hours_to_pay`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours to be paid for termination.
* **paid holidays** (`paid_holidays`, text, optional): Specify any paid holidays.
* **holidays worked** (`holidays_worked`, number, optional): Enter the number of holidays worked.
* **termination reason other** (`termination_reason_other`, text, optional): Enter a reason for termination not listed.
* **employer notes** (`employer_notes`, text, optional): Add any employer notes.
