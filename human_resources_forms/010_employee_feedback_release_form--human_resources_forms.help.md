# Employee Feedback Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees for a specific project or initiative. It is intended to be used internally within an organization to gather opinions, suggestions, and ideas from employees on how to improve a particular project or department. This form helps to ensure that all employee feedback is captured and considered in the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your employee name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select your department from the dropdown list.
3. Provide any suggestions or ideas you may have for improvement.
4. Add any additional comments about your feedback.
5. If you are a team leader or manager, enter your name and department in the respective fields.
6. If applicable, approve or reject the feedback in the manager signoff section.
7. Finally, select the status of the feedback (Open, In Progress, Closed) and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as an employee.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the list (Human Resources, Marketing, IT).
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, required): Provide any suggestions or ideas you may have for improvement.
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`, text, required): Add any additional comments about your feedback.
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text, optional): If you are a team leader, enter your name.
* **Department Lead** (`department_lead`, select_one, optional): If you are a team leader, select your department.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, optional): If you are a team leader, enter your email address.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, optional): If you are a manager, add any comments about the feedback.
* **Manager Signoff** (`manager_signoff`, select_one, optional): If you are a manager, approve or reject the feedback.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the feedback (Open, In Progress, Closed).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you are a team leader or manager, ensure that you are authorized to access this form.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide or ask your supervisor for assistance.
