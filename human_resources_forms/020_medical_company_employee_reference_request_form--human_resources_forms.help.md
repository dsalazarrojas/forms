# Medical Company Employee Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about an existing employee's work history and performance from a medical company employee reference. This information is typically used as part of the hiring process for new employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the `Employee Name` field.
2. Enter the company name of the employee's current or previous employer in the `Company Name` field.
3. Enter the employee's ID number in the `Employee ID` field (if applicable).
4. Enter the employee's job title in the `Job Title` field.
5. Select the department the employee worked in from the `Department` field (e.g. Sales, Marketing, Finance, HR).
6. Enter the employee's job start and end dates in the `Job Start Date` and `Job End Date` fields, respectively.
7. Enter the name of the employee's manager in the `Manager Name` field.
8. Enter the employee's current status (e.g. "Active" or "Retired") in the `Employee Status` field.
9. Enter the employee's email address in the `Email` field.
10. Enter the employee's phone number in the `Phone` field.
11. Enter the name of the reference person in the `Reference Name` field.
12. Enter the title of the reference person in the `Reference Title` field.
13. Enter the name of the reference company in the `Reference Company` field.
14. Enter the description of the reference relationship (e.g. "Current Manager", "Previous Supervisor") in the `Reference Relationship` field.
15. Enter the phone number of the reference person in the `Reference Phone` field.
16. Enter the email address of the reference person in the `Reference Email` field.
17. Enter any additional notes or comments in the `Note` field.
18. Once you have completed all the fields, click the `Submit` button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being referenced.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee's current or previous employer.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the employee's ID number (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department the employee worked in (e.g. Sales, Marketing, Finance, HR).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Enter the employee's job start date.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required): Enter the employee's job end date.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, text, optional): Enter the employee's current status (e.g. "Active" or "Retired").
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reference person.
* **Reference Title** (`reference_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the reference person.
* **Reference Company** (`reference_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reference company.
* **Reference Relationship** (`reference_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the description of the reference relationship (e.g. "Current Manager", "Previous Supervisor").
* **Reference Phone** (`reference_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reference person.
* **Reference Email** (`reference_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the reference person.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the reference person.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the `Submit` button to send the form.
