# Seasonal Travel Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Travel Time Off Form is used to request time off for seasonal travel, allowing employees to plan and coordinate their leave in advance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Employee Name and Employee ID Number fields with your correct details.
2. Select the Seasonal Travel Start and Seasonal Travel End Dates for your trip.
3. Enter the Reason For Seasonal Travel in the designated field.
4. Provide the Destination and Seasonal Travel Days and Seasonal Travel Days of Week for your trip (if applicable).
5. Select the Seasonal Travel Type (Business, Personal, or Both) and the Seasonal Travel Travel Status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
6. Choose the Seasonal Travel Requesting Officer (Manager, Director, or Both).
7. Optionally, enter any Seasonal Travel Comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id_number`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number as found on your ID card.
* **Seasonal Travel Start Date** (`seasonal_travel_start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of your seasonal travel.
* **Seasonal Travel End Date** (`seasonal_travel_end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of your seasonal travel.
* **Reason For Seasonal Travel** (`reason_for_seasonal_travel`, text, required): Describe the purpose of your seasonal travel (e.g., Christmas, New Year, Summer Break).
* **Destination** (`destination`, text, optional): Enter the destination of your seasonal travel (if applicable).
* **Seasonal Travel Days** (`seasonal_travel_days`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you will be away for your seasonal travel (if applicable).
* **Seasonal Travel Days of Week** (`seasonal_travel_days_of_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you will be away during the week (if applicable).
* **Seasonal Travel Type** (`seasonal_travel_travel_type`, select_one, required): Select whether your travel is for Business, Personal, or Both purposes.
* **Seasonal Travel Requesting Officer** (`seasonal_travel_requesting_officer`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a Manager, Director, or Both requesting the time off.
* **Seasonal Travel Travel Status** (`seasonal_travel_travel_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your travel request (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Seasonal Travel Comments** (`seasonal_travel_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your seasonal travel request.
