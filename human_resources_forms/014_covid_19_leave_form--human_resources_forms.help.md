# COVID 19 Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Leave Form is designed to collect information from employees who need to take leave due to COVID-19 related reasons. This form helps track and manage leave requests, ensuring that employees have the necessary documentation and arrangements for their leave, and that their work is covered while they are away.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct type of leave from the options provided in the "Type of Leave" field.
2. Choose a valid reason for your leave from the "Reason for Leave" field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your leave in the "Leave Start Date" and "Anticipated End Date" fields.
4. If you have any medical documentation, select "True" in the "Do you have medical documentation?" field and provide details in the "Type of Documentation" field.
5. Arrange work coverage by selecting "True" in the "Have you arranged coverage for your work?" field and providing details in the "Coverage Details" field.
6. Finally, acknowledge that you have read and understand the COVID-19 leave policy by selecting "True" in the "I have read and understand the COVID-19 leave policy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (Employee Name): Enter your full name as it is recorded in our system.
- **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number as it is recorded in our system.
- **Department**: Enter your department name.
- **Job Position**: Enter your job position.
- **Direct Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct manager.
- **Type of Leave**: Select the type of leave you are requesting (COVID-19 quarantine, COVID-19 sick leave, Caregiver leave, etc.).
- **Reason for Leave**: Select the reason for your leave (Diagnosed with COVID-19, Exposed to COVID-19, Caring for family member, etc.).
- **Additional Details**: Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your leave request.
- **Leave Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
- **Anticipated End Date**: Enter the date you anticipate returning to work.
- **Estimated Duration - Days**: Enter the number of days you plan to take for your leave.
- **Do you have medical documentation?**: Select "True" if you have medical documentation, "False" otherwise.
- **Type of Documentation**: Select the type of documentation you have (Positive COVID-19 test, Doctor note, Vaccine appointment confirmation, etc.).
- **Have you arranged coverage for your work?**: Select "True" if you have arranged coverage for your work, "False" otherwise.
- **Coverage Details**: Provide details on who will cover your work and what tasks they will handle.
- **I have read and understand the COVID-19 leave policy**: Select "True" to acknowledge that you have read and understand the policy.
- **Request Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
- **Submission Date**: This will be automatically filled in based on when you submit the form.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure smooth processing of your leave request.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR department for guidance.
