# Position Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Position Request Form is used to collect information from employees requesting a position change or reassignment within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the current job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Enter the name of your current reporting manager in the "Reporting Manager Name" field.
4. Enter the title of your current reporting manager in the "Reporting Manager Title" field.
5. Enter the reporting unit or department you currently belong to in the "Reporting Unit" field.
6. Enter the date you are currently reporting to your manager in the "Reporting Date" field.
7. Provide a brief explanation for your position request in the "Reason for Position Request" field.
8. Enter the proposed job title for the new position in the "Proposed Job Title" field.
9. Enter the name of your proposed reporting manager in the "Proposed Reporting Manager" field.
10. Enter the proposed reporting date for the new position in the "Proposed Reporting Date" field.
11. Add any additional comments or notes about your position request in the "Comments" field.
12. If you have any attachments or supporting documents related to your position request, upload them in the "Attachments" field.

* **Reason for Position Request** (`name: reason_for_position_request`): Provide a brief explanation for your position request.
* **Proposed Job Title** (`name: proposed_job_title`): Enter the proposed job title for the new position.
* **Proposed Reporting Manager** (`name: proposed_reporting_manager`): Enter the name of your proposed reporting manager.
* **Proposed Reporting Date** (`name: proposed_reporting_date`): Enter the proposed reporting date for the new position.
