# Payroll Correction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Correction Request Form is a tool for employees to report errors in their pay. This form helps to identify and correct discrepancies in paychecks, ensuring accuracy and fairness in the payroll process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the employee's name as it appears on their paycheck.
2.  Enter the employee's ID number as it appears in the company's database.
3.  Select the pay date of the paycheck that contains the error.
4.  Choose the type of pay error from the dropdown menu (e.g., overpayment, underpayment, or payroll error).
5.  Enter the amount of the error.
6.  Provide a brief explanation of the error in the "Error Reason" field.
7.  Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
8.  Enter your email address.
9.  Enter your phone number.
10.  Add any additional comments or notes about the error.
11.  Select any relevant categories or tags (e.g., Payroll, HR, Management).
12.  Choose the status of the request (e.g., Open, Resolved, In Progress, Closed).
13.  Enter the due date and time for the resolution of the error.
14.  Enter the location where the error was detected.
15.  Assign the request to a specific user.
16.  Finally, click the submit button to send the form to the relevant department or personnel for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (1)**: Enter the name of the employee whose pay is being corrected.
* **Employee ID (2)**: Enter the ID number of the employee as it appears in the company's database.
* **Pay Date (3)**: Enter the date of the paycheck that contains the error.
* **Pay Error Type (4)**: Select the type of pay error from the dropdown menu (e.g., overpayment, underpayment, or payroll error).
* **Error Amount (5)**: Enter the amount of the error.
* **Error Reason (6)**: Provide a brief explanation of the error in this field.
* **Requested by (7)**: Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Submission Date (8)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time (9)**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submission Location (10)**: Enter the location where the form was submitted.
* **Assigned to (11)**: Assign the request to a specific user.
* **Assigned User (12)**: Enter the name of the user assigned to the request.
* **Status (13)**: Choose the status of the request (e.g., Open, Resolved, In Progress, Closed).
* **Due Date (14)**: Enter the date by which the error should be resolved.
* **Due Time (15)**: Enter the time by which the error should be resolved.
* **Due Location (16)**: Enter the location where the error should be resolved.
* **Comments Count (17)**: Enter the number of comments on the request.
* **Email (18)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (19)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Category (20)**: Select any relevant categories or tags (e.g., Payroll, HR, Management).
* **Tags (21)**: Select any additional tags relevant to the request.
* **Comments (22)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the error.
* **Submitted by (23)**: Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted Date (24)**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time (25)**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Location (26)**: Enter the location where the form was submitted.
* **Assigned to (27)**: Assign the request to a specific user.
* **Status (28)**: Choose the status of the request (e.g., Open, Resolved, In Progress, Closed).
