# Consulting Professional Development Employment Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess your professional development in the consulting industry. It helps to identify your strengths, areas for improvement, and career interests. The information gathered from this form will be used to support your career growth and development within the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information. Make sure to answer all the questions carefully and honestly.
2. Use the provided hints for guidance on what the fields mean.
3. Save your progress and come back if you need more time to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (employee_first_name, text, required):** Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name (employee_last_name, text, required):** Enter your last name here.
* **Email Address (employee_email, email, required):** Enter your work email address here.
* **Employee ID (employee_id, text, optional):** If you have an Employee ID, enter it here (optional).
* **Current Position (current_position, text, required):** Enter your current job title here.
* **Department (department, text, required):** Enter your department or practice area here.
* **Hire Date (hire_date, date, required):** Enter the date you joined the company here.
* **Current Level (current_level, select_one, required):** Select your current career level from the options provided:
	+ Analyst
	+ Associate
	+ Senior Associate
	+ Manager
	+ Senior Manager
	+ Director
	+ Principal
	+ Partner
* **Years in Consulting (years_in_consulting, number, required):** Enter the total years of consulting experience here.
* **Current Performance Rating (performance_rating_current, select_one, required):** Select your most recent performance review rating from the options provided:
	+ Exceeds Expectations
	+ Meets Expectations
	+ Developing
	+ Below Expectations
	+ Not Yet Rated
* **Core Competencies (core_competencies, select_multiple, required):** Select your primary strengths from the options provided:
	+ Strategic Thinking
	+ Client Management
	+ Problem Solving
	+ Data Analysis
	+ Project Management
	+ Team Leadership
	+ Communication
	+ Business Development
	+ Technical Skills
	+ Industry Knowledge
* **Areas for Development (development_areas, select_multiple, required):** Select areas you want to improve from the options provided:
	+ Leadership Skills
	+ Presentation Skills
	+ Client Relationship Building
	+ Technical Expertise
	+ Industry Knowledge
	+ Business Acumen
	+ Time Management
	+ Delegation
	+ Conflict Resolution
	+ Negotiation
* **Career Interests (career_interests, select_multiple, required):** Select areas that interest you for growth from the options provided:
	+ People Management
	+ Technical Specialist
	+ Business Development
	+ Subject Matter Expert
	+ Project Leadership
	+ Industry Specialist
	+ Executive Track
	+ Other
* **Short-Term Career Goals (short_term_goals, text, required):** Enter your goals for the next 6-12 months here.
* **Long-Term Career Goals (long_term_goals, text, required):** Enter your goals for the next 2-5 years here.
* **Training Completed This Year (training_completed, text, optional):** If you have completed any training programs this year, list them here (optional).
* **Desired Training Programs (desired_training, text, required):** Enter the training programs you would like to have in the future here.
* **Certification Goals (certification_goals, text, optional):** If you plan to pursue any certifications, list them here (optional).
* **Mentorship Interest (mentorship_interest, select_one, optional):** Select your mentorship preference from the options provided:
	+ Want a Mentor
	+ Want to Be a Mentor
	+ Want Both
	+ Not Interested
* **Project Type Preferences (project_preferences, text, optional):** Enter the types of projects that interest you here.
* **Industry Preferences (industry_preferences, text, optional):** Enter the preferred industries you would like to work in here.
* **Work Location Preference (work_location_preference, select_one, optional):** Select your preferred working arrangement from the options provided:
	+ Office Based
	+ Remote
	+ Hybrid
	+ Client Site
	+ Flexible
* **Travel Willingness (travel_willingness, select_one, required):** Select how much travel you are willing to do from the options provided:
	+ Extensive - 80%+
	+ Moderate - 50-80%
	+ Limited - 25-50%
	+ Minimal - Under 25%
	+ No Travel
* **Do You Feel Ready for Promotion (promotion_readiness, select_one, required):** Select if you feel ready for advancement from the options provided:
	+ Yes, Ready Now
	+ Yes, Within 6 Months
	+ Yes, Within 1 Year
	+ Not Yet
	+ Unsure
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments, text, optional):** Enter any other information you would like to share here.
* **Assessment Date (assessment_date, date, required):** Enter the date of this assessment here.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to save your progress and come back if you need more time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
