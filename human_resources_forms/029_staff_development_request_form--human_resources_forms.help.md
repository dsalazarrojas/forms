# Staff Development Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Development Request Form is used by staff members to request support for personal or professional growth opportunities, such as training or education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the title of the development opportunity you are requesting.
2. Provide a detailed description of the request.
3. Select the category of development opportunity (e.g. Human Resources, Other).
4. Enter the name of your manager.
5. Enter the department you are requesting development for.
6. Enter the date you require the development opportunity.
7. Enter the date the development opportunity will be offered.
8. Enter the name of the person requesting the development opportunity.
9. Enter the type of development opportunity (e.g. training, education).
10. Indicate whether a budget has been approved for the development opportunity.
11. Add any notes about the request.
12. Enter the name of the person who submitted the request.
13. Enter the name of the person to whom the request was submitted.
14. Enter the date the request was submitted.
15. Indicate whether the request has been approved.
16. Enter any comments about the request.
17. Enter the form ID.
18. Select the status of the request (e.g. Open, Completed).
19. Enter the time the request was submitted.
20. Enter the type of training or development opportunity.
21. Enter the number of training hours requested.
22. Enter the location of the training or development opportunity.
23. Enter the venue of the training or development opportunity.
24. Enter the provider of the training or development opportunity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (staff_development_request): Enter the title of the development opportunity you are requesting. This should be a brief summary of the request.
* **Description** (description): Provide a detailed description of the development opportunity you are requesting. This should include any relevant information about the opportunity, such as the date it will be offered, the location, and any other relevant details.
* **Category** (category): Select the category of development opportunity (e.g. Human Resources, Other). This will help us understand the type of opportunity you are requesting.
* **Manager's Name** (manager_name): Enter the name of your manager. This will help us understand who is aware of your development request.
* **Department** (department): Enter the department you are requesting development for. This should be the department that will be impacted by the development opportunity.
* **Date Required** (date_required): Enter the date you require the development opportunity. This should be the date by which you need the opportunity.
* **Date Offered** (date_offered): Enter the date the development opportunity will be offered. This should be the date the opportunity will become available.
* **Requestor's Name** (training_requestor): Enter the name of the person requesting the development opportunity. This should be your name.
* **Type of Development Opportunity** (training_requested): Enter the type of development opportunity (e.g. training, education). This should be a clear description of the opportunity.
* **Budget Approved** (budget_approved): Indicate whether a budget has been approved for the development opportunity. This will help us understand the status of your request.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any notes about your request. This is an opportunity to provide additional information about your request.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by): Enter the name of the person who submitted the request. This should be your name.
* **Submitted To** (submitted_to): Enter the name of the person to whom the request was submitted. This should be your manager's name.
* **Submitted Date** (submitted_date): Enter the date the request was submitted. This will help us track the history of your request.
* **Approved By** (approved_by): Indicate whether the request has been approved. This will help us understand the status of your request.
* **Approved Date** (approved_date): Enter the date the request was approved. This will help us track the history of your request.
* **Comments** (comments): Enter any comments about your request. This is an opportunity to provide additional information about your request.
* **Form ID** (form_id): Enter the form ID. This will help us track the unique identifier for your request.
* **Status** (status): Select the status of the request (e.g. Open, Completed). This will help us understand the current status of your request.
* **Submitted Time** (submitted_time): Enter the time the request was submitted.
* **Training Type** (training_type): Enter the type of training or development opportunity. This should be a clear description of the opportunity.
* **Training Hours** (training_hours): Enter the number of training hours requested.
* **Training Location** (training_location): Enter the location of the training or development opportunity.
* **Training Venue** (training_venue): Enter the venue of the training or development opportunity.
* **Training Provider** (training_provider): Enter the provider of the training or development opportunity.
