# Employee Integration Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register employees for the integration workshop, a collaboration event among team members from different departments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter your email address and phone number correctly.
3. Select the correct date and time for the session you wish to attend.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Your full name as it appears on your employee ID card.
  - This is a required field to identify you as an employee.
- **Email**: Your company email address.
  - Please enter your official company email.
- **Phone**: Your work phone number.
  - This field should contain your work phone number for easy contact.
- **Session Date**: The date of the workshop.
  - Choose the date you wish to attend.
- **Session Time**: The time of the session.
  - Pick the time slot you wish to join. 

## Tips
* Make sure to register only once to avoid duplicate entries.
* If you need to register multiple employees, fill out one form per employee.
* If you have any difficulties, contact your HR representative for assistance.
