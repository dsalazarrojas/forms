# Employee Transfer Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the employee transfer process by gathering necessary information from the employee and their manager to approve and process the transfer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following details about the transfer:
	* Employee's name
	* Your name as the manager
	* Transfer start date
	* Transfer end date
	* Reason for transfer
2. Select the current and desired department of the employee
3. Update the employee's status to one of the following: Active, Inactive, or Resigned

Note: Make sure all required fields are completed as marked by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Transfer Request
* **Transfer Request** (`transfer_request_form`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can briefly describe the reason for the transfer.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the employee's name.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering your name as the manager.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the transfer is to start.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the transfer is to end.
* **department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the current and desired department of the employee.
* **reason_for_transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the transfer.
* **employee_status** (`employee_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current status and desired status of the employee from the options: Active, Inactive, or Resigned.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth transfer process.
* If the employee's status is to be changed, make sure to select the correct options for both current and desired status.
* If any issues arise during the transfer process, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
