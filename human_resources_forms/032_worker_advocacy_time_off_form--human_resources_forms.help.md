**Worker Advocacy Time Off Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Worker Advocacy Time Off Form is a tool used by employees to request time off from work. This form is designed to gather information about the reason for the time off, job title, supervisor name, department, and other relevant details to help manage and process time off requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Request**: Provide a brief description of your request for time off.
2. **Select Start and End Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of your planned time off.
3. **Enter Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
4. **Enter Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
5. **Enter Department**: Enter the name of the department you belong to.
6. **Specify Reason for Time Off**: Provide a clear explanation for why you are requesting time off.
7. **Optional: Time-Off Hours**: If you want to specify the exact number of hours you need off, enter the number of hours here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Time Off** (text, required): A brief description of your request for time off.
- **Start Date** (date, not required): The start date of your planned time off.
- **End Date** (date, not required): The end date of your planned time off.
- **Job Title** (text, not required): Your current job title.
- **Supervisor Name** (text, not required): The name of your supervisor.
- **Department** (text, not required): The name of the department you belong to.
- **Reason for Time Off** (text, not required): A clear explanation for why you are requesting time off.
- **Approved Date** (date, not required): The date the time off request was approved (not applicable when submitting a new request).
- **Time-Off Hours** (number, not required): The number of hours you need off.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth processing of your time off request.
- If your request is for an extended period, please provide specific dates or indicate the number of days or weeks off.
