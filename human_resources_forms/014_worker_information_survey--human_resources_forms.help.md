# Worker Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and maintain accurate and up-to-date information about employees. It's essential to fill out this form correctly to ensure that HR personnel have a comprehensive view of each employee's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the First Name field with the employee's first name.
2. Fill out the Last Name field with the employee's last name.
3. Enter the employee's Email address.
4. Enter the employee's Job Title.
5. Enter the employee's Department.
6. Select the employee's employment status from the drop-down menu.
7. Enter the employee's Manager's name.
8. Enter a brief description of the employee's job.
9. Enter the name of the employee's Reporting Manager.
10. Enter the employee's Job Grade.
11. Enter the employee's Salary.
12. Enter the employee's benefits details.
13. Enter the employee's Job Status.
14. Enter the employee's termination date (if applicable).
15. Enter the employee's Employee ID.
16. Enter the employee's Work Location.
17. Enter the employee's Job Classification.
18. Enter the employee's Job Category.
19. Enter the employee's Job Level.
20. Enter the employee's Job Family.
21. Enter the employee's work hours.
22. Enter the employee's vacation days.
23. Enter the name and relationship of the employee's emergency contact.
24. Select the relationship between the employee and their emergency contact from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the employee's job title.
* **Department** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's department.
* **Hire Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the employee was hired.
* **Employment Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the employee's employment status (Active, Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Manager Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Job Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the employee's job.
* **Reporting Manager** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee's reporting manager.
* **Job Grade** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's job grade.
* **Salary** (Number, Optional): Enter the employee's salary.
* **Benefits** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's benefits details.
* **Job Status** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's job status.
* **Termination Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the employee's employment was terminated (if applicable).
* **Employee ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's employee ID number.
* **Work Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's work location.
* **Job Classification** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's job classification.
* **Job Category** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's job category.
* **Job Level** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's job level.
* **Job Family** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's job family.
* **Work Hours** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's work hours.
* **Vacation Days** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's available vacation days.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Select One, Optional): Select the relationship between the employee and their emergency contact (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, Other).
