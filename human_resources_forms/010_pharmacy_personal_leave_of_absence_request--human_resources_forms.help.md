# Pharmacy Personal Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy Personal Leave Of Absence Request form is used to submit a request for a personal leave of absence from work. This form is intended for employees who need to take a leave of absence for personal reasons, such as illness, family emergency, or vacation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you are submitting a leave request.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your employee identification card.
3. Enter your employee ID number.
4. Select the start date and start time of your leave.
5. Select the end date and end time of your leave.
6. Enter the reason for your leave.
7. Select the duration of your leave from 1-3 days, 4-5 days, 6-7 days, 8-14 days, or 15 or more days.
8. Select a date to return to work.
9. Enter your contact number and email address.
10. Enter your department and supervisor's name.
11. Select the approval status of your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Request** (`Submit Request`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Confirm that you are submitting a leave request.
* **Full Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee identification card.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you would like to start your leave.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you would like to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you would like to end your leave.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you would like to end your leave.
* **Reason For Leave** (`Reason For Leave`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reason for your leave.
* **Leave Duration** (`Leave Duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of your leave from 1-3 days, 4-5 days, 6-7 days, 8-14 days, or 15 or more days.
* **Return To Work Date** (`Return To Work Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date to return to work.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (`Department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department name.
* **Supervisor Name** (`Supervisor Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Approval Status** (`Approval Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the approval status of your leave request.
