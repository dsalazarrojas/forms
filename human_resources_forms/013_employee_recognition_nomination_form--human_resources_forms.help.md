# Employee Recognition Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The **Employee Recognition Nomination Form** is used to nominate an employee for recognition or an award within the organization. This form helps to acknowledge and celebrate outstanding achievements and contributions made by employees, promoting a positive work environment and employee engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **employee's name** who is being nominated.
2. Provide a brief description of the **nominee's name** and their qualifications.
3. Write a clear and specific **Nomination Message** describing the nominee's actions or accomplishments that demonstrate their outstanding contribution to the organization.
4. Select the **Recognition Type** that best fits the nominee's achievement (e.g., Outstanding Service, Leadership, Teamwork, Innovation, or Other).
5. Choose a **Recognition Date** when the achievement was recognized or the award ceremony took place.
6. Optionally, describe the **Recognition Achievement** in more detail.
7. Enter the **nominee's title**, **department**, and **job title** (if applicable).
8. Enter the **nominee's location** (if applicable).
9. Select the **Employee Nominator's name**, **title**, **department**, and **job title** (if applicable).
10. Optionally, provide the **Employee Nominator's location** (if applicable).
11. Review and submit your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name**: The name of the employee being nominated.
* **nominee_name**: Enter the name of the person being recognized.
* **nomination_message**: Provide a clear and specific description of the nominee's actions or accomplishments.
* **nominee_title**: The title of the person being recognized (if applicable).
* **nominee_department**: The department the nominee belongs to (if applicable).
* **nominee_job_title**: The job title of the person being recognized (if applicable).
* **nominee_location**: The location of the nominee (if applicable).
* **employee_nominator_name**: The name of the employee who is nominating.
* **employee_nominator_title**: The title of the employee who is nominating (if applicable).
* **employee_nominator_department**: The department the nominator belongs to (if applicable).
* **employee_nominator_job_title**: The job title of the employee who is nominating (if applicable).
* **employee_nominator_location**: The location of the nominator (if applicable).
* **recognition_type**: Select the type of recognition that best fits the nominee's achievement.
* **recognition_date**: The date when the achievement was recognized or the award ceremony took place.
* **recognition_achievement**: A more detailed description of the nominee's recognition or award.
* **submit**: Submit your nomination.

## Tips

* Be specific and detailed when filling out the nomination message and recognition achievement fields.
* Ensure that the recognition type and date are accurate and relevant to the nomination.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your HR representative for guidance.
* Review and submit your nomination carefully to ensure accuracy.
