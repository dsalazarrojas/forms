# interview_readiness_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the "interview readiness quiz" of an individual. The purpose of the form is to measure how prepared and confident one feels about participating in a mock interview or assessment, and what their strengths and weaknesses are in certain areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page carefully.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, and select the best response that reflects your feelings and experiences.
3. For the multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply to you.
4. For the text fields, type in your thoughts and experiences.
5. Finally, review your answers and make sure they accurately reflect your feelings and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: This page serves as a general introduction to the form. Please read it carefully and take a moment to understand the purpose of the form.

	+ **Somewhat Disagreed**: I feel somewhat unconfident.
	+ **Strongly Disagreed**: I do not feel confident at all.
