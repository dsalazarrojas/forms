# Client Exit Transition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Exit Transition Form is a tool used to document the reasons behind a client's decision to end their services with our company. This form helps us understand their experience, identify areas for improvement, and maintain a record of their exit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. If you are unsure about any field, use the available options or describe your answer in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Your legal first name
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Your legal last name
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Your primary contact email
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Best contact number
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Your business name
* **Account Manager Name** (`account_manager`, text, optional): Your primary contact
* **Service Type Being Discontinued** (`service_type`, select_one, required): What service you are ending (e.g., Consulting Services, Software Subscription, etc.)
* **Client Since Date** (`client_since_date`, date, required): When did you start with us?
* **Desired Exit Date** (`exit_date`, date, required): When do you want to end services?
* **Primary Reason for Leaving** (`exit_reason_primary`, select_one, required): Main reason for departure (e.g., Cost or Pricing, Service Quality Issues, etc.)
* **Please Elaborate on Your Reason** (`exit_reason_details`, text, optional): More details about your decision
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_overall`, select_one, required): Your overall experience (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
* **Service Quality Rating** (`service_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Quality of services provided (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Was it worth the cost? (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value)
* **Customer Support Rating** (`support_rating`, select_one, required): Quality of support received (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Are You Switching to a Competitor?** (`alternative_provider`, select_one, optional): If you are switching to a different provider
* **Competitor Name** (`competitor_name`, text, optional): If you are comfortable sharing
* **Competitor Advantage** (`competitor_advantage`, text, optional): What the competitor offers that we do not
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggestions for improvement
* **Outstanding Issues** (`outstanding_issues`, text, optional): Unresolved problems
* **Data Transfer Needed** (`data_transfer_needed`, select_one, required): Do you need data transferred? (Yes, Please Transfer, Yes, I Will Download, No, You May Delete, Not Sure)
* **Preferred Data Transfer Method** (`data_transfer_method`, select_one, optional): If applicable, method for data transfer (Email, Secure Download, Physical Drive, Direct Transfer to New Provider, Not Applicable)
* **I Understand I Will Receive a Final Invoice** (`final_invoice_understanding`, select_one, required): Billing acknowledgment
* **Would You Consider Returning as a Client?** (`return_to_us`, select_one, optional): Future possibility
* **Would You Recommend Us to Others?** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, optional): Despite leaving
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any final thoughts
* **Would You Like to Participate in an Exit Interview?** (`exit_interview_requested`, select_one, required): Optional discussion
* **Form Completion Date** (`form_completion_date`, date, required): Date of form completion
