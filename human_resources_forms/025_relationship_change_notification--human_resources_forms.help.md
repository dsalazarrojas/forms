# Relationship Change Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relationship Change Notification form is used to inform relevant parties about changes to relationships, such as updates to relationship status, dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Relationship Status** in the "Relationship Status" field. This is where you'll indicate the current status of the relationship.
2.  Enter the **Effective Date** in the "Effective Date" field. This is the date when the relationship change took effect.
3.  Add a **Document Link** to provide more context or information about the change.
4.  Provide a **Reason For Change** in the "Reason For Change" field, if necessary.
5.  Enter the **Updated By** field with your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
6.  Enter the **Updated On** date and time to indicate when the form was completed.
7.  Enter the **Updated At** timestamp to capture the exact time of completion.
8.  Add any additional context in the **Related To** field, if applicable.
9.  Enter the **Organization** name, if relevant.
10. Enter the **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone** for the person completing the form.
11. Enter the **Document Description** to provide a brief description of the document associated with the relationship change.
12. Enter the **Document Date** when the document was created or updated.
13. Enter the **Document Type** to classify the document type associated with the relationship change.
14. Enter the **Document Link** to link to the document or additional information.
15. Enter the **Updated By User** to indicate who updated the form.
16. Enter the **Updated By Date** and **Updated At Time** to track when the form was last updated.
17. Enter the **Updated At Timestamp** to capture the exact timestamp of the last update.
18. Enter the **Relationship Change Date**, **Relationship Change Time**, and **Relationship Change Timestamp** to capture the date and time of the relationship change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Relationship Status**: Enter the current status of the relationship, such as "Active" or "Not Active".
*   **Effective Date**: Enter the date when the relationship change took effect.
*   **Document Link**: Add a link to a document or additional information about the relationship change.
*   **Reason For Change**: Provide a brief explanation for the relationship change, if necessary.
*   **Updated By**: Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
*   **Updated On**: Enter the date and time when the form was completed.
*   **Updated At**: Enter the timestamp when the form was completed.
*   **Related To**: Add any additional context or information about the relationship change.
*   **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization related to the relationship change.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the person completing the form.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Document Description**: Provide a brief description of the document associated with the relationship change.
*   **Document Date**: Enter the date when the document was created or updated.
*   **Document Type**: Enter the type of document associated with the relationship change.
*   **Document Link**: Enter the link to the document or additional information.
*   **Updated By User**: Enter your username or ID.
*   **Updated By Date**: Enter the date when the form was last updated.
*   **Updated At Time**: Enter the time when the form was last updated.
*   **Updated At Timestamp**: Enter the timestamp when the form was last updated.
*   **Relationship Change Date**: Enter the date of the relationship change.
*   **Relationship Change Time**: Enter the time of the relationship change.
*   **Relationship Change Timestamp**: Enter the timestamp of the relationship change.

Note: The fields that are not relevant to your use case can be removed or filled out with "N/A".
