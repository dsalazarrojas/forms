# HR Policy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Feedback Form is designed to gather information from employees about existing HR policies. This feedback helps the HR department to identify areas for improvement, understand employee concerns, and develop more effective policies that benefit everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have a valid reason to provide feedback. If you're unsure, check with your HR representative or department head.
2.  Fill in your **Employee ID** with your employee number.
3.  Select the relevant **Department** where the policy applies.
4.  Provide the name of the **Policy Name** you're giving feedback on.
5.  Enter your **Feedback** about the policy, using the space provided.
6.  Optionally, add any additional **Comments** you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required/optional: false): Enter your unique employee number to associate your feedback with your personnel record.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional: false): Choose the department related to the policy you're giving feedback on.
* **Policy Name** (`policy_name`, text, required/optional: false): Specify the name of the policy you're reviewing.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required/optional: false): Briefly describe your thoughts, opinions, or suggestions related to the policy.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional: false): Add any additional, more in-depth information you'd like to share about your feedback.
