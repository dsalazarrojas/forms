# Career Development Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Development Audit Form is a tool used to gather information about an employee's career development progress. It helps identify areas for growth and improvement, and provides a plan for future development. This form is intended for HR professionals and managers to assess an employee's current role, completed trainings, performance metrics, development needs, and recommended actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee name field with the name of the employee being audited.
2. Enter the Employee's current job title in the Current role field.
3. List the trainings completed by the employee in the Completed trainings field.
4. Identify key performance metrics and results in the Performance metrics field.
5. Specify areas for skill growth in the Development needs field.
6. Suggest a development plan in the Recommended actions field.
7. (Optional) Enter a timeline for implementing the recommended actions in the Timeline for development field.
8. Add any feedback or comments from the manager in the Manager comments field (optional).
9. Fill in the Name of auditor in the Audit completed by field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name**: The name of the employee being audited, as provided in the hint.
* **Current role**: The employee's current job title, which should be a brief description of their current position.
* **Completed trainings**: A list of trainings completed by the employee, which can be helpful for future development planning.
* **Performance metrics**: Key performance metrics and results that demonstrate the employee's progress, such as numbers or percentages.
* **Development needs**: Areas where the employee needs improvement or growth, which can be specific skills or knowledge gaps.
* **Recommended actions**: A suggested plan for the employee's development, which can include training, mentorship, or projects.
* **Timeline for development**: An optional field for specifying when to implement the recommended actions (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Manager comments**: Feedback or comments from the manager, which can be helpful for providing additional context or insights.
* **Audit completed by**: The name of the person completing the audit, which can help track responsibility and ownership.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use specific language when providing feedback or comments from managers.
* Consider attaching relevant documentation or supporting materials when submitting the form.
* Review and update the form regularly to track progress and adjust development plans as needed. 

---

Please note that the fields marked as "hint: null" do not have a specific explanation. 

This guide is intended to be a general reference, and might need to be adapted based on your specific organization's policies and practices.
