# Workplace Safety And Concerns Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for employees to report any safety or concerns they may have about their work environment. This includes any concerns related to the spread of COVID-19 or other safety-related issues. By filling out this form, employees can report their concerns and ensure that their well-being and that of their colleagues is protected.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select the "Employee Concerns" page.
3. If you have any employee concerns, type them in the "Description" field.
4. If you have any contact information, enter it in the "Contact Info" field.
5. If you have any information about the date and time of concern, enter it in the respective fields.
6. If you have any additional information about your department, select the correct department from the dropdown list.
7. Select any relevant concern types from the "Concern Type" list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Concerns (Description)** (`description`, `text`, optional): Use this field to describe your safety or concern in as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, optional): If you have any contact information such as a phone number or email, enter it in this field. This will help your colleagues or managers reach out to you if they need more information about your concern.
* **Date Of Concern** (`date_of_concern`, `date`, optional): If you know the date of the concern, enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Concern** (`time_of_concern`, `time`, optional): If you know the time of the concern, enter it in the format HH:MM.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): If you want to report a concern on behalf of another employee, enter their name here.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, optional): Select the department that the concern is related to. This will help your colleagues or managers identify the department involved.
* **Concern Type** (`concern_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any relevant concern types from the dropdown list. This will help your colleagues or managers categorize and address your concern.
