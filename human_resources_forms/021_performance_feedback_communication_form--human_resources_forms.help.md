# performance_feedback_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance Feedback Communication Form is a tool designed to facilitate open communication between employees and their supervisors about performance. This form helps to gather feedback, identify areas for improvement, and track progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Employee Feedback: Share your thoughts and insights about your performance.
	* Supervisor Feedback: Share your thoughts and insights about the employee's performance.
	* Communication Channel: Choose how you would like to communicate with your supervisor (Email, Phone, In-person, or Video Conference).
	* Expected Outcome: Provide a numerical value indicating what you expected to happen as a result of the feedback.
	* Actual Outcome: Provide a numerical value indicating what actually happened as a result of the feedback.
	* Date Completed: Enter the date the feedback was completed.
2. Select your Performance Rating:
	* Rating from 1 to 5, depending on how you rate your performance.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, required):
	* This is the space for you to write your thoughts and insights about your performance. Try to be as specific as possible and provide concrete examples.
* **Supervisor Feedback** (`supervisor_feedback`, text, required):
	* This is the space for your supervisor to write their thoughts and insights about your performance. Again, try to be specific and provide concrete examples.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, optional):
	* If you have any specific steps you're taking to improve your performance, list them here. This can help your supervisor track your progress.
* **Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`, select_one, required):
	* Choose how you would like to communicate with your supervisor. Options include:
		+ Email
		+ Phone
		+ In-person
		+ Video Conference
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, number, required):
	* Provide a numerical value indicating what you expected to happen as a result of the feedback. This can be a specific score, a description, or any other relevant information.
* **Actual Outcome** (`actual_outcome`, number, required):
	* Provide a numerical value indicating what actually happened as a result of the feedback. This can be a specific score, a description, or any other relevant information.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required):
	* Enter the date when you completed the feedback.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, required):
	* Rate your performance on a scale of 1 to 5, where:
		+ 1: Needs Improvement
		+ 2: Below Average
		+ 3: Average
		+ 4: Above Average
		+ 5: Excellent
