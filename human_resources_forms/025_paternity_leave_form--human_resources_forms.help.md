# Paternity Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paternity Leave Form is a document used to apply for paternity leave. It's a necessary step for new fathers to take time off from work to care for their newborn or adopted child. This form helps the management and HR team to process the leave request and ensure a smooth transition for the employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee ID, which is your unique identifier in the company.
2. Select the Start Date when you'd like to begin your paternity leave.
3. Choose the End Date when you expect to return to work.
4. Provide the Reason for Leave, which should be a brief explanation of why you're applying for paternity leave.
5. Indicate the Duration of Leave, which is the number of days you'd like to take off.
6. Enter the Expected Return Date, which is the date you expect to return to work after your leave.
7. If your manager has already approved or declined your request, check the Manager Confirmation field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Father Application (#1)**: This field is not required, but it's a label for the form title.
* **Employee ID (#2)** (`Employee ID`, `number`, required): This field is used to identify you as an employee in the company. Please enter your unique employee ID.
* **Start Date (#3)** (`date`, required): Choose the date when you'd like to begin your paternity leave. This is the date when your leave will start.
* **End Date (#4)** (`date`, required): Choose the date when you expect to return to work after your leave. This is the date when your leave will end.
* **Reason for Leave (#5)** (`text`, required): Provide a brief explanation of why you're applying for paternity leave. This could be a personal reason, medical reason, or any other valid reason.
* **Duration of Leave (#6)** (`number`, required): Enter the number of days you'd like to take off for paternity leave. This will help us determine the exact duration of your leave.
* **Expected Return Date (#7)** (`date`, required): Enter the date you expect to return to work after your leave. This will help us schedule your return to work smoothly.
* **Manager Confirmation (#8)** (`select_multiple`, optional): If your manager has already approved or declined your request, select the corresponding option. This helps us track the status of your leave request.
