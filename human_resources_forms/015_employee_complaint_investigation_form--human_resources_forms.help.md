<em>employee_complaint_investigation_form</em> - Help Guide

## Purpose
The <em>employee_complaint_investigation_form</em> is designed to gather information and support the investigation of employee complaints within the organization. This form helps to collect relevant details to understand the issue, identify potential causes, and guide further action to resolve the complaint.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by providing a brief description of your complaint on page 1 of the form. This will help to give context to your issue.
2. On page 2, please provide a detailed description of the complaint, including any relevant dates, times, and circumstances surrounding the incident.
3. On page 3, you may provide any additional comments or information that you believe will be helpful to the investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_complaint_investigation_form_page_1 (Employee Complaint Investigation Form)**
    (`employee_complaint_investigation_form`, text, required: false)
    This is the first page of the form where you can enter a brief description of your complaint.
* **employee_complaint_description (Employee Complaint Description)**
    (`employee_complaint_description`, text, required: false)
    On this page, provide a detailed description of your complaint, including any dates, times, and circumstances surrounding the incident.
* **employee_complaint_investigation_form_page_3 (Employee Complaint Investigation Form - Page 3)**
    (`employee_complaint_investigation_form_page_3`, text, required: false)
    This page is intended for any additional comments or information that you believe will be helpful to the investigation. 

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your complaint, as this will help the investigator to better understand the issue.
* If you have any supporting evidence or documentation related to the complaint, please attach it to your submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the process, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
* Remember that all complaints are treated with confidentiality and will be handled in accordance with organizational policies and procedures.
