# Candidate Experience Survey (Offered) - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from candidates about their experience during the offer stage of the recruitment process. It helps us understand their satisfaction with the offer, communication from the recruitment team, and overall impression of the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct answer option for each question.
3. Provide detailed elaboration when asked to elaborate on your choice (optional).
4. Submit the form once you've completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Position Offered** (`offered_role_title`, text, required): Please describe the job title you were offered.
* **Date of Offer** (`offer_letter_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you received the official offer.
* **Did you accept the offer** (`offer_acceptance_status`, select_one, required): Select whether you accepted or declined the offer. If declined, please explain your reason.
* **Overall experience from application to offer** (`end_to_end_experience`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience during the recruitment process.
* **Quality of the offer package explanation** (`package_explanation_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how well the offer package was explained to you.
* **Timeliness of the offer after the final interview** (`offer_timeliness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the wait time between the final interview and offer receipt.
* **Primary reason for your decision** (`main_decision_reason`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason for accepting or declining the offer.
* **How would you rate the communication from HR during the offer stage** (`hr_communication_rating`, number, required): Rate the communication from HR using a 1 to 5 stars scale.
* **Was the recruitment team responsive to your questions** (`team_responsiveness`, select_one, required): Select whether you found the recruitment team responsive or not.
* **Overall satisfaction with the recruiter** (`recruiter_satisfaction_score`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the recruiter using a 1 to 10 scale.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_timestamp`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.

Please note that all fields are required unless otherwise specified. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use the corresponding fields to provide more information.
