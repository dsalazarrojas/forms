# employee_wellness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employee wellness and satisfaction with their work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and concisely.
3. Select the options that best describe your current physical and mental health, participation in wellness programs, and job satisfaction.
4. Enter your email address for communication purposes only.
5. Enter your phone number for communication purposes only.
6. Identify your manager's involvement and support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional):
  This is a brief overview of the form's purpose and scope. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Physical Health** (`Physical Health`, `select_one`, optional):
  This field assesses your self-reported level of physical health. Select the option that best describes your current physical health:
  - Excellent: You are in excellent physical health.
  - Good: You are in good physical health, but not perfect.
  - Fair: You have some physical health concerns.
  - Poor: Your physical health is a concern.
* **Mental Health** (`Mental Health`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  This field asks about your self-reported level of mental health. Select all the options that best describe your current mental health:
  - Excellent: You are in excellent mental health.
  - Good: You are in good mental health, but not perfect.
  - Fair: You have some mental health concerns.
  - Poor: Your mental health is a concern.
* **Wellness Programs** (`Wellness Programs`, `select_one`, optional):
  This field assesses your participation in available wellness programs. Select the option that best describes your current participation:
  - 'True': You are actively participating in wellness programs.
  - 'False': You are not participating.
  - Maybe: You are unsure or have not participated.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`Job Satisfaction`, `number`, optional):
  This field asks about your current job satisfaction. Enter a numerical value between 0 and 10, where 0 is not at all satisfied and 10 is extremely satisfied.
* **Recommendations** (`Recommendations`, `text`, optional):
  This field is for you to provide any additional recommendations or suggestions for improving your wellness and work experience.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional):
  Enter your email address for communication purposes only. This will help us contact you with any updates or follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional):
  Enter your phone number for communication purposes only. This will help us contact you with any updates or follow-up questions.
* **Manager** (`Manager`, `select_one`, optional):
  Identify your manager's involvement and support in your wellness and work experience. Select the option that best describes their level of involvement:
  - 'True': Your manager is actively involved and supportive.
  - 'False': Your manager is not involved.
  - Maybe: Your manager's involvement is uncertain or lacking.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Select the options that best fit your current situation.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult your manager or HR representative.
* Please do not duplicate any previous form submissions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us at [contact email or phone number].
