# Retirement Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from employees regarding their retirement plans and benefits. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth transition during this significant life event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your Employee Name**: Please fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Enter your Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
3. **Enter your Social Security Number**: Type your complete social security number without any hyphens or dashes.
4. **Select your Retirement Date**: Choose the date when you plan to retire.
5. **Enter your Reason for Retirement**: Briefly describe the reason why you are retiring.
6. **Choose your Benefits Start Date**: Select the date when your benefits will start.
7. **Choose your Retirement Plan**: Select one of the available retirement plans: Plan A, Plan B, or Plan C.
8. **Enter your Pay Out**: Please enter the amount of pay out you are eligible for.
9. **Mark your Retirement Status**: Select the status of your retirement: Active, Pending, or In Progress.
10. **Add any additional Notes**: Use this field to provide any additional information about your retirement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Provide your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, number, optional): Type your complete social security number without any hyphens or dashes.
* **Retirement Date** (`retirement_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when you plan to retire.
* **Reason for Retirement** (`reason_for_retirement`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason why you are retiring.
* **Benefits Start Date** (`benefits_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date when your benefits will start.
* **Retirement Plan** (`retirement_plan`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available retirement plans: Plan A, Plan B, or Plan C.
* **Pay Out** (`pay_out`, number, optional): Enter the amount of pay out you are eligible for.
* **Retirement Status** (`retirement_status`, select_multiple, optional): Mark your retirement status as Active, Pending, or In Progress.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information about your retirement.
