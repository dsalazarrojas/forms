# worker_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback and opinions from workers on various aspects of their work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your company name in the **Company Name** field.
2. Select your role in the **Role** field (Team Leader, Manager, Employee, or Other).
3. Select your department from the list in the **Department Name** field (Finance and Accounting, HR, Marketing and Communications, or Sales and Marketing).
4. Answer the open-ended questions 1-20 with your thoughts and opinions.
5. Enter your company email in the **Company Email** field.
6. Enter your email in the **Worker Email** field.
7. Optional: Enter your department phone number in the **Department Phone** field.
