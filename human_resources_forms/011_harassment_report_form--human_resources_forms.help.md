# Harassment Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Harassment Report Form is designed to collect information about workplace harassment incidents, ensuring that each case is properly documented and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Report Date and Report Time fields with the actual dates and times of the incident.
2. Select the Harasser (the person responsible for the harassment) from the list of options.
3. Select the Victim from the list of options.
4. Provide a detailed Description of the incident.
5. Choose the Location where the incident occurred.
6. Select the Resolution Method(s) used to resolve the issue.
7. Choose the Reporting Person.
8. Select the Reportee.
9. Choose the Resolution Status.
10. Fill in the Follow-up Date and Follow-up Time fields with the scheduled follow-up dates and times.
11. Select the Follow-up Status and provide Follow-up Notes if needed.
12. Provide any additional Resolution Notes or Follow-up Notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (1) (date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Report Time** (2) (time): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Harasser** (3) (select one): Choose the person responsible for the harassment.
* **Victim** (4) (select one): Choose the person being harassed.
* **Description** (5) (text): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Location** (6) (select one): Choose the location where the incident occurred.
* **Resolution Method** (7) (select multiple): Choose the method(s) used to resolve the issue.
* **Reporting Person** (8) (select one): Choose the person who reported the incident.
* **Reportee** (9) (select one): Choose the person who was reported on.
* **Resolution Status** (10) (select multiple): Choose the status of the resolution.
* **Follow-up Date** (11) (date): Enter the scheduled follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Time** (12) (time): Enter the scheduled follow-up time.
* **Follow-up Status** (13) (select multiple): Choose the status of the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Notes** (14) (note): Provide any additional notes about the follow-up.
* **Resolution Notes** (15) (note): Provide any additional resolution notes.
* **Follow-up Contact** (16) (select one): Choose the person to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Resolution Notes 2** (17) (note): Provide any additional resolution notes.
* **Follow-up Date 2** (18) (date): Enter the scheduled follow-up date 2.
* **Follow-up Time 2** (19) (time): Enter the scheduled follow-up time 2.
* **Follow-up Status 2** (20) (select multiple): Choose the status of the follow-up 2.
* **Follow-up Notes 2** (21) (note): Provide any additional follow-up notes 2.
* **Resolution Notes 3** (22) (note): Provide any additional resolution notes 3.
* **Resolution Notes 4** (23) (note): Provide any additional resolution notes 4.
* **Follow-up Status 3** (24) (select multiple): Choose the status of the follow-up 3.
* **Follow-up Notes 3** (25) (note): Provide any additional follow-up notes 3.
