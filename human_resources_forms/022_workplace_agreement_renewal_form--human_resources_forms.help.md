This guide will walk users through the Workplace Agreement Renewal Form, explaining its purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.

### Purpose
The Workplace Agreement Renewal Form is intended for employees to renew their work agreements, which can include job title, department, and other relevant information. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are up-to-date and accurate for employee records and administrative purposes.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Enter your Employee Name.
	* Choose your Job Title.
	* Select your Department from the available options.
	* Choose your Renewal Status.
2. Optional fields:
	* Provide an additional Email if necessary.
	* If your Job Title has multiple roles, select all relevant titles from the options provided (Job Title 1, Job Title 2).
	* Enter any Notes for additional context.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required):
	+ This field is for your name as an employee.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required):
	+ Enter your current job title.
* **Job Title 1** (Job Title 1, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select any additional job titles you hold, if applicable.
* **Department** (Department, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the department you belong to.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional):
	+ Enter your work email address, if different from your primary email.
* **Email 2** (Email 2, email, optional):
	+ This is a duplicate field for your work email address.
* **Job Title 1** (Job Title 1, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field is for selecting multiple job titles you may hold.
* **Date Renewal** (Date Renewal, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of your renewal, if applicable.
* **Time Renewal** (Time Renewal, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of your renewal, if applicable.
* **Renewal Status** (Renewal Status, select_one, optional):
	+ Select your current status for the renewal process (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional):
	+ Enter additional notes or context about your renewal.
