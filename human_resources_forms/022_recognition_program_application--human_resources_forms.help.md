# Recognition Program Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recognition Program Application is a form used to collect information about individuals who are being recognized for their contributions and achievements within the organization. This form helps to ensure that the recognition process is fair, consistent, and transparent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you have uploaded the necessary supporting documents, if applicable.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`1`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`2`, Email, Optional): Enter your work email address, if you have one.
* **Phone** (`3`, Text, Optional): Your work or personal phone number.
* **Organization** (`4`, Text, Required): The name of your current or former workplace.
* **Job Title** (`5`, Text, Optional): Your position or title within your organization.
* **Years of Service** (`6`, Number, Required): The number of years you have worked for your organization.
* **Achievements** (`7`, Text, Optional): A brief description of your achievements or contributions.
* **Supporting Documents** (`8`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check if you have any supporting documents that can be verified for your recognition.
	+ Options: Yes or No
* **Date of Award or Recognition** (`9`, Date, Required): The date when the recognition was received or will be received.
* **Description** (`10`, Note, Required): A brief description of why you are being recognized.
* **Submit** (`11`, Text, Optional): Click to submit your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have answered all required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Supporting documents should be uploaded as attachments, if applicable.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
