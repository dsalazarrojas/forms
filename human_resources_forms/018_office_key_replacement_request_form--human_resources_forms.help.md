# Office Key Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Office Key Replacement Request Form is a document designed to collect information and track office key replacement requests within the organization. This form helps ensure that all requests are properly documented, reviewed, and addressed in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the office where the key replacement is needed.
2. Provide a brief reason for the replacement request.
3. Enter the date and time of submission (optional).
4. Enter your contact phone number and email address.
5. Identify the assigned user responsible for handling the request.
6. Provide additional notes for the assigned user (optional).
7. Select the status of the request (Pending, In Progress, or Completed).
8. Enter any status notes (optional).
9. Identify the reviewer who will review the request.
10. Enter any review comments (optional).
11. Enter the cost of office replacement.
12. Enter the cost of submission.
13. Identify the user who submitted the request.
14. Enter any submitted-by notes (optional).
15. Enter the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office** (`office_id`): Select the office where the key replacement is needed.
* **Reason For Replacement Request** (`reason_for_replacement_request`): Enter a brief reason for the key replacement request.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of submission (optional).
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of submission (optional).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`): Provide additional contact information or notes (optional).
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Identify the user assigned to handle the request.
* **Assigned User Notes** (`assigned_user_note`): Enter any additional notes for the assigned user (optional).
* **Status** (`status`): Select the status of the request (Pending, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Status Notes** (`status_note`): Enter any status notes (optional).
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Identify the reviewer who will review the request.
* **Reviewer Name Notes** (`reviewer_name_note`): Enter any review notes (optional).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Select the submission status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Submission Status Notes** (`submission_status_notes`): Enter any submission status notes (optional).
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`): Enter any review comments (optional).
* **Office Replacement Cost** (`office_replacement_cost`): Enter the cost of office replacement.
* **Submission Cost** (`submission_cost`): Enter the cost of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Identify the user who submitted the request.
* **Submitted By Notes** (`submitted_by_note`): Enter any submitted-by notes (optional).
* **Submission Date Time** (`submission_date_time`): Enter the date and time of submission.
