# Employee Information Security Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Information Security Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about employees and their security-related needs, awareness, and training requirements. This data will be used to ensure that employees are informed and trained on the necessary security measures for their roles, contributing to a secure work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information and not duplicating any existing submissions.
2. Complete all required fields (if any).
3. Fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_info** (employee_info, text, not required):
	+ This field is for providing any general employee information that may be relevant to their security clearance.
* **security_awareness** (security_awareness, text, not required):
	+ This field is for employees to provide any additional information about their security awareness and concerns.
* **training_needs** (training_needs, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Choose the relevant security training options that apply to you.
* **tracking_trends** (tracking_trends, text, not required):
	+ This field is for any information on security trends or observations related to your role or department.
* **employee_id** (employee_id, text, not required):
	+ Enter your employee identification number, if applicable.
* **team_id** (team_id, text, not required):
	+ Enter your team identification number, if applicable.
* **submitted_date** (submitted_date, date, not required):
	+ The date you are submitting this form.
* **form_submission_date** (form_submission_date, date, not required):
	+ The date this form was submitted.
* **assigned_tool** (assigned_tool, text, not required):
	+ Enter any relevant tool assigned to you for security purposes.
* **submitted_by** (submitted_by, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose the name of the employee submitting this form.
	+ Options: John Doe or Jane Doe.
