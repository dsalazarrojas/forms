# NISD Phone Reference Questionnaire L - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NISD Phone Reference Questionnaire L is used to collect phone reference information from users. This form is meant to be completed by users who are currently or were previously employed in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Identify Yourself
- Enter your unique identifier, "user_id". This is not a required field, but it's a good idea to fill it out to help us reference your entry later.

### Step 2: Enter Your Contact Information
- Enter your email address in the format of "username@domain.com". If you have any other contact information, such as your company email address, please include that as well.
  **Note:** This field is not required, but it's a good idea to fill it out if you have a professional email address.

### Step 3: Enter Your Phone Number
- Enter your phone number in the format of "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX.XXX.XXXX". This can be a work phone number or a personal phone number. Please make sure to include the country code if you're calling from outside the country.

### Step 4: Enter Your Date of Birth
- Enter your date of birth in the format of "MM/DD/YYYY" or "DD MMM YYYY". This is optional, but it helps us reference your employment history.

### Step 5: Enter Your Date of Joining
- Enter your date of joining in the format of "MM/DD/YYYY" or "DD MMM YYYY". This is optional, but it helps us reference your employment history. Please note that this field is not related to the date of birth.

### Step 6: Enter Your Time of Joining
- Enter your time of joining in the format of "HH:MM" or "HH:MM AM". This is not related to the date of joining and is also optional.
  **Note:** This field is meant to capture the time of day you started your job or your role, not the time of day you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_id** (Number, Optional): A unique identifier that helps us reference your entry later.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address in the format of "username@domain.com". If you have any other contact information, please include that as well.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number in the format of "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX.XXX.XXXX". This can be a work phone number or a personal phone number. Please make sure to include the country code if you're calling from outside the country.
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of "MM/DD/YYYY" or "DD MMM YYYY". This is optional, but it helps us reference your employment history.
* **date_of_joining** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of joining in the format of "MM/DD/YYYY" or "DD MMM YYYY". This is optional, but it helps us reference your employment history. Please note that this field is not related to the date of birth.
* **time_of_joining** (Time, Optional): Enter your time of joining in the format of "HH:MM" or "HH:MM AM". This is not related to the date of joining and is also optional.
  **Note:** This field is meant to capture the time of day you started your job or your role, not the time of day you submitted this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the fields that are relevant to your situation.
- If you're having trouble filling out a particular field, please contact us for assistance.
- You can save your progress and come back to the form later, but make sure to submit it when you're finished.
