# Postdoctoral Scholar Hire Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Postdoctoral Scholar Hire Notification Form is used to notify the university of a postdoctoral scholar hire, providing essential information for tracking and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the job title and job opening title in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the department, job category, and job type for the postdoctoral scholar.
3. Enter the date of the job start date, employment start date, and position effective date.
4. Indicate whether the job classification is Yes or No and select the job status as Active or Inactive.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Opening Title (<label: job_opening_title>)
* Enter the title of the job opening for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Department (<label: department)
* Enter the department that the postdoctoral scholar will be affiliated with.

### Job Category (<label: job_category)
* Enter the category of the job for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Job Type (<label: job_type)
* Enter the type of job for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Job Title (<label: job_title)
* Enter the title of the job for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Departmental Review Committee (<label: departmental_review_committee)
* Enter the departmental review committee that approved the hire.

### Hiring Unit (<label: hiring_unit)
* Enter the hiring unit responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Job Start Date (<label: job_start_date)
* Enter the date the job started for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Job End Date (<label: job_end_date)
* Enter the date the job ended for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Employment Start Date (<label: employment_start_date)
* Enter the date the employment started for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Employment End Date (<label: employment_end_date)
* Enter the date the employment ended for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Pay Basis (<label: pay_basis)
* Enter the pay basis for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Pay Basis Details (<label: pay_basis_details)
* Provide additional information about the pay basis for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Position Effective Date (<label: position_effective_date)
* Enter the date the position became effective for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Job Classification (<label: job_classification)
* Indicate whether the job classification is Yes or No.

### Postdoctoral Scholar (<label: postdoctoral_scholar>
* Enter any additional information about the postdoctoral scholar.

### Faculty Sponsor (<label: faculty_sponsor)
* Enter the faculty sponsor responsible for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Research Group (<label: research_group)
* Enter the research group the postdoctoral scholar will be affiliated with.

### Supervisor (<label: supervisor)
* Enter the supervisor for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Research Area (<label: research_area)
* Enter the research area for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Job Type (<label: job_type>)
* Enter the type of job for the postdoctoral scholar.

### Job Status (<label: job_status)
* Select the job status as Active or Inactive.

### Job Status Date (<label: job_status_date)
* Enter the date the job status became effective.

### Note (<label: note>)
* Enter any additional notes or comments about the postdoctoral scholar.
