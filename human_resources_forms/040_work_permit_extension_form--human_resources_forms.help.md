# Work Permit Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Permit Extension Form is a document used to request an extension of a work permit. It is essential for employees who need to continue working on their current project beyond the original deadline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Application Form section, providing a clear and concise explanation for your request.
2. In the Work Details section, provide information about your current project, including the project's nature and any relevant context.
3. In the Employee Information section, provide your details, including your name, job title, and employee ID.
4. In the Employee Signature section, indicate whether you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the extension.
5. In the Reason for Rejection section, provide a brief explanation if your request is being rejected. This is only required if your request is rejected.
6. In the Comments section, provide any additional comments or information that may be relevant to your request.
7. Finally, click on the Submit button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`extension_application`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide a clear and concise explanation for your request. Please include any relevant details that support your reason for requesting an extension.
* **Work Details** (`work_details`, text, optional): In this section, please describe your current project, including its nature and any relevant context.
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, optional): Here, you can input your name, job title, and employee ID for reference purposes.
* **Employee Signature** (`signature`, select_one, optional): Please indicate whether you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the extension by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Reason for Rejection** (`reason_for_rejection`, text, optional): If your request is being rejected, please provide a brief explanation for the rejection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to your request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your form once you have filled out all the required sections.
