# Crunch Time Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crunch Time Leave Of Absence Request form is used to submit and manage requests for leave of absence from work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: `critical_delivery_periods`, `leave_type`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `duration`, `reason_for_leave`, `manager_approval_status`, and `manager_id`.
2. Select the correct `critical_delivery_periods` and `leave_type` options.
3. Enter the correct start and end dates for the leave period.
4. Choose the correct duration and reason for leave.
5. Select the correct coverage options (Team Member or Other).
6. Enter the employee's details (name, title, department, employee ID, manager ID, and team ID).
7. If necessary, add a comment for the manager to approve or reject the request.
8. Review the form and submit it when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Critical Delivery Periods** (<type: select_one>)
Select the type of delivery period that best describes the reason for your leave request.

*   Day off
*   Short term
*   Long term

### 2. **Leave Type** (<type: select_multiple>)
Select the type of leave you are requesting.

*   Vacation
*   Sick
*   Family and medical
*   Other

### 3. **Start Date** (<type: date>)
Enter the start date of your leave period.

### 4. **End Date** (<type: date>)
Enter the end date of your leave period.

### 5. **Duration** (<type: number>)
Enter the number of days or hours of your leave period.

### 6. **Reason for Leave** (<type: text>)
Provide a brief explanation for the reason of your leave.

### 7. **Manager Approval Status** (<type: select_one>)
Select the status of your request.

*   Approved
*   Rejected
*   Pending

### 8. **Manager Comment** (<type: note>)
Add any comments or instructions for the manager to approve or reject the request (optional).

### 9. **Coverage** (<type: select_multiple>)
Select the team members who will cover your duties during your leave.

*   Team Member
*   Other

### 10. **Coverage Members** (<type: select_multiple>)
Select the team members who will be covering your duties during your leave (if other coverage is selected).

### 11. **Manager Email** (<type: email>)
Enter the manager's email address.

### 12. **Manager Phone** (<type: text>)
Enter the manager's phone number (optional).

### 13. **Employee Name** (<type: text>)
Enter your name.

### 14. **Department** (<type: select_multiple>)
Select your department.

*   Yes
*   No

### 15. **Employee Title** (<type: text>)
Enter your job title.

### 16. **Employee ID** (<type: text>)
Enter your employee ID.

### 17. **Manager ID** (<type: text>)
Enter your manager's ID.

### 18. **Team ID** (<type: text>)
Enter your team ID.

### 19. **Manager Approvals** (<type: select_multiple>)
Select the manager's approval status.

*   Yes
*   No

### 20. **Team Lead Approvals** (<type: select_multiple>)
Select the team lead's approval status (optional).

### 21. **Employee Manager Relationship** (<type: select_multiple>)
Select the status of your relationship with your manager.

*   Yes
*   No

### 22. **Request Details** (<type: text>)
Add any additional details for the manager to review (optional).

### 23. **Approval Date** (<type: date>)
Enter the date of the manager's approval (if approved).

### 24. **Submitted At** (<type: date>)
Enter the date the request was submitted.

This guide is designed to help you complete the Crunch Time Leave Of Absence Request form accurately and efficiently. Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting.
