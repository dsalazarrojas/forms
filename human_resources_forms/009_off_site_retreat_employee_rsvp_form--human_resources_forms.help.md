# off_site_retreat_employee_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The off-site retreat employee RSVP form is designed to collect information from employees regarding their attendance and preferences for an off-site retreat. This form is essential for the organizers to plan and arrange the retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form header with your employee information.
2. Select your attendance status for the retreat.
3. Specify your arrival and departure times if you plan to attend the retreat.
4. Choose your accommodation preferences, including whether you need accommodation and which type of accommodation you prefer.
5. Select your meal preferences.
6. Choose whether you will be attending the retreat.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
8. Provide your contact email and phone number in case the organizers need to contact you.
9. Enter any relevant notes for the organizers.
10. Finally, select the date and time you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee name and other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact details in case the organizers need to contact you.
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether you will be attending the retreat.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your expected arrival time for the retreat.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your expected departure time for the retreat.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`accommodation_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether you need accommodation and which type of accommodation you prefer.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you need accommodation.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Meal Preferences** (`meal_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your meal preferences.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Meal** (`meal`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you will be having a meal.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you will be attending the retreat.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the organizers.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email in case the organizers need to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number in case the organizers need to contact you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes for the organizers.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date, required/optional): Select the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted Time** (`form_submitted_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you submitted the form.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the organizers can plan and arrange the retreat properly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organizers.
* Make sure to check the form for any errors before submitting it.
