# FMLA Notice Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The FMLA Notice Submission Form is a document used to collect information related to Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) submissions. This form is used by employees and managers to provide essential details about leave requests, which helps the HR department process these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your specific leave request.
2. Ensure you answer all the questions accurately and completely.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (1):
	+ This field is for your name, which will be used to identify you as the employee making the leave request.
* **Notice Date** (2):
	+ Please enter the date you provided notice for your leave request.
* **End Date** (3):
	+ Enter the date you expect to return to work after your leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (4):
	+ Describe your reason for requesting leave in this field. This will help the HR department understand your situation.
* **Expected Return Date** (5):
	+ Enter the date you expect to return to work after your leave.
* **Manager's Name** (6):
	+ This field is for your manager's name, which will be used to verify your leave request.
* **Manager's Email** (7):
	+ Enter your manager's email address.
* **Employee's Salary** (8):
	+ Please enter your current salary.
* **Job Title** (9):
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Number of Work Hours Lost** (10):
	+ Calculate and enter the total number of work hours you expect to lose during your leave.
* **Pay Period Lost** (11):
	+ Calculate and enter the number of pay periods you expect to lose during your leave.
* **Employee Notice Period** (12):
	+ Enter the number of days notice you provided to your manager.
* **Manager's Notice Period** (13):
	+ Enter the number of days notice your manager provided to the HR department.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to the HR department.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the HR department for assistance.
