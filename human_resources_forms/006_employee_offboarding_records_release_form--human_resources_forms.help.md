# Employee Offboarding Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of an employee's offboarding process, ensuring accurate and up-to-date employee records. It is a crucial part of maintaining a smooth transition of responsibilities and resources after an employee's departure from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the employee's offboarding checklist and ensure that this form is not already filled out.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information about the employee's offboarding details.
3. Complete the form by filling in the relevant fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Offboarding Date** (`offboarding_date`, date, optional):
	+ A date indicating when the employee offboarded from the company.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate whether the manager has approved the employee's offboarding.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the employee's ID from the provided options.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the department where the employee was working.
* **Reason for Separation** (`reason_for_separation`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the employee's offboarding.
* **Outdate** (`outdate`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate whether the employee's last day of work was on a specific date.
* **Out Time** (`outtime`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the time of day when the employee offboarded.
* **Out Location** (`outlocation`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location where the employee offboarded from.
* **Out Reason** (`outreason`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the employee's offboarding.
* **In Location** (`inlocation`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location where the employee will be returning to. 

Please note that this form is optional in the sense that some of the fields are not required. However, it is essential to complete it thoroughly and accurately for maintaining precise employee records.
