# Weekly Work Shift Summary - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Weekly Work Shift Summary form is designed to collect information from employees about their work shifts for a given week. This form helps track employees' working hours, breaks, and overtime, allowing the organization to monitor and ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Employee Name field with your name as it appears on your official records.
2. Enter the Date of the work shift in the Date field.
3. Enter the Start Time and End Time of your work shift in the respective fields.
4. Provide the Duration of your work shift in the Duration field.
5. If necessary, add any additional Shift Details in the Shift Details field.
6. Select the correct option for Overtime to indicate whether you worked overtime.
7. Indicate whether you took a Lunch Break in the Lunch Break field.
8. Select multiple options for Meal Breaks if you took more than one meal break during your shift.
9. Choose the type of shift you worked (Regular, Overtime, or Vacation) in the Shift Type field.
10. Provide any Additional Info about your shift if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Required/Optional) - Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Date** (Optional) - Enter the date of the work shift.
* **Start Time** (Optional) - Enter the start time of your work shift.
* **End Time** (Optional) - Enter the end time of your work shift.
* **Duration** (Optional) - Provide the duration of your work shift.
* **Shift Details** (Optional) - Add any additional details about your shift.
* **Overtime** (Required) - Select "Yes" if you worked overtime, "No" otherwise.
* **Lunch Break** (Required) - Select "Yes" if you took a lunch break, "No" otherwise.
* **Meal Breaks** (Required) - Select multiple options for meal breaks you took during your shift.
* **Shift Type** (Required) - Choose the type of shift you worked (Regular, Overtime, or Vacation).
* **Additional Info** (Optional) - Provide any additional information about your shift.
