# Post-Surgery-Recovery-Leave-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and process employee's post-surgery recovery leave. It is essential that employees only fill out this form after receiving medical clearance from their doctor.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Post-Surgery-Recovery-Leave-Form**: This is the main title of the form.
* **Employee-Name**: Please fill out your full name as it appears on your employee records.
* **Employee-ID**: Enter your employee ID number as it appears in the HR system.
* **Surgery-Date**: Provide the date of your surgery.
* **Procedure-Details**: Briefly describe the details of your surgery, including the type of surgery and any relevant medical information.
* **Surgeon-Name**: If applicable, fill out the name of the surgeon who performed your surgery.
* **Hospital-Name**: If applicable, fill out the name of the hospital where you had your surgery.
* **Doctor-Name**: Enter the name of your doctor who provided your medical clearance for return to work.
* **Doctor-Phone**: Enter the phone number of your doctor for follow-up contact.
* **Doctor-Contact**: Enter the email or contact method of your doctor for follow-up contact.
* **Post-Surgery-Rehabilitation-Plan**: Briefly describe the post-surgery rehabilitation plan provided by your doctor.
* **Return-To-Work-Date**: Provide the date you expect to return to work after your recovery.
* **Medical-Certificate**: If you have been cleared to return to work, answer 'Yes'. If you have not been cleared, answer 'No'.
* **Medical-Certificate-2**: This field should only be filled out by your doctor or HR department. Please do not fill out this field.
* **Doctor-Certificate-2**: This field should only be filled out by your doctor. Please do not fill out this field.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and honestly.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your HR department for clarification.
