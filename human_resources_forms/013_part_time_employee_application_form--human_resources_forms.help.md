# Part Time Employee Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential part-time employees, including their personal details, work experience, availability, and job preferences. This form will help the relevant department assess their suitability for part-time positions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the "personal_info" field.
2. Share your work experience in the "work_experience" field.
3. Select your availability for work hours in the "availability" field.
4. Indicate your job preference in the "job_preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required: false)
Enter your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false)
Describe your previous work experience, highlighting relevant skills and positions held.

### **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the days you are available to work from Monday to Sunday.

### **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, select_one, required: true)
Choose your preferred type of employment: Part-time, Full-time, or Freelance.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete all required fields to avoid submission errors.
- Double-check your availability and job preferences to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the relevant department.
