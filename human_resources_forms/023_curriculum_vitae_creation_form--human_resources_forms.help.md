# Curriculum Vitae Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you create a comprehensive and professional Curriculum Vitae (CV) that showcases your experience, skills, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Fill out your **Job Title** and **Job Start Date** to describe your current or past work experience.
3. Provide a brief **Job Description** of your job role and responsibilities.
4. Add your **Education** background to demonstrate your qualifications.
5. Enter your **Employer** name and **Location** of your work place.
6. Select the type of **Job Type** you have (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Freelance).
7. Indicate your **Work Status** (Active, Inactive, Retired, or Deceased).
8. Add your **Email** and **Phone** contact details for communication.
9. If you have **Work Experience**, enter the number of years you have worked in your current or past role.
10. Click the "Submit" button to create your CV.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`<last_name>`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Job Title** (`<job_title>`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Start Date** (`<job_start_date>`, `date`, optional): Enter your job start date (if applicable).
* **Job End Date** (`<job_end_date>`, `date`, optional): Enter your job end date (if applicable).
* **Job Description** (`<job_description>`, `text`, optional): Describe your job role and responsibilities.
* **Education** (`<education>`, `text`, optional): Provide your educational background.
* **Employer** (`<employer>`, `text`, optional): Enter your employer's name.
* **Location** (`<location>`, `text`, optional): Enter your work location.
* **Job Type** (`<job_type>`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of job you have (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Freelance).
* **Work Status** (`<work_status>`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your work status (Active, Inactive, Retired, or Deceased).
* **Email** (`<email>`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`<phone>`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Work Experience** (`<work_experience>`, `number`, optional): If you have work experience, enter the number of years.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out your form to create a comprehensive CV.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us!
