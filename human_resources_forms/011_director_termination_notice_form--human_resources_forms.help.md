# Director Termination Notice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Director Termination Notice Form is used to document the termination of a director's position. It is a critical part of the company's human resources process and ensures that all necessary information is collected and reported accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the director's director termination date in the "Director Termination Date" field. This field is not required, but it is recommended that you include it for record-keeping purposes.
2. Select the reason for termination from the list of options provided in the "Reason for Termination" field. This field is not required, but it's essential to select a valid reason for proper reporting and record-keeping.
3. If the reason for termination is not listed in the options, please type it in the "Other Reason for Termination" field. This field is not required but is useful for recording additional information.
4. Enter the notice period in the "Notice Period" field. This field is not required, but it's recommended that you include it for record-keeping purposes.
5. Enter the last day the director worked in the "Last Day Worked" field. This field is not required, but it's essential to include it for proper calculation of benefits and pay.
6. If the reason for termination is not listed, please enter the reason in the "Other Reason for Termination" field. This field is not required but is useful for recording additional information.
7. Select the type of notice from the list of options provided in the "Notice Type" field. This field is not required, but it's essential to select a valid type for proper reporting and record-keeping.
8. Enter the reason for termination type in the "Reason for Termination Type" field. This field is not required, but it's recommended that you include it for proper reporting and record-keeping.
9. Enter the termination effective date in the "Termination Effective Date" field. This field is not required, but it's essential to include it for proper calculation of benefits and pay.
10. Enter the reason for termination reason in the "Reason for Termination Reason" field. This field is not required, but it's recommended that you include it for proper reporting and record-keeping.
11. Enter the reason for termination reason type in the "Reason for Termination Reason Type" field. This field is not required, but it's recommended that you include it for proper reporting and record-keeping.
12. Enter the termination cause in the "Termination Cause" field. This field is not required, but it's recommended that you include it for proper reporting and record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Director Termination Date** (`director_termination_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the director's position was terminated.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, Select One, Optional): Select a valid reason for termination from the list of options provided. If the reason is not listed, type it in the "Other Reason for Termination" field.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, Number, Optional): Enter the notice period given to the director before termination.
* **Last Day Worked** (`last_day_worked`, Date, Optional): Enter the last day the director worked in the company.
* **Other Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination_other`, Text, Optional): Enter any other reason for termination that is not listed in the options.
* **Notice Type** (`notice_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select a valid type of notice from the list of options provided.
* **Reason for Termination Type** (`reason_for_termination_type`, Text, Optional): Enter the type of reason for termination.
* **Termination Effective Date** (`termination_effective_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the effective date of termination.
* **Reason for Termination Reason** (`reason_for_termination_reason`, Text, Optional): Enter the reason for termination.
* **Reason for Termination Reason Type** (`reason_for_termination_reason_type`, Text, Optional): Enter the reason for termination type.
* **Termination Cause** (`termination_cause`, Text, Optional): Enter the cause of termination.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, it's best to consult with your supervisor or HR department.
* Complete the form only for legitimate reasons and avoid duplicating effort with other forms or procedures.
