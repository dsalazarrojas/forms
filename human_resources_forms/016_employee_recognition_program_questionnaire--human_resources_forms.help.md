# Employee Recognition Program Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and nominations from employees for their peers who have contributed to their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields. Required fields are marked as such in the form.
2. Enter your nomination in the "Nomination Form" field.
3. Provide recognition details in the "Recognition Details" field.
4. Enter the recipient's information in the "Recipient Information" field.
5. Confirm your submission in the "Confirmation" field.
6. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Click "Submit" to submit your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form (required)**: Enter a brief description of the employee's contribution or achievement that you would like to recognize.
* **Recognition Details (required)**: Provide details on why this recognition is being given and what specific actions the employee has taken to contribute to their work.
* **Recipient Information (required)**: Enter the recipient's name, title, and department to whom the recognition is being given.
* **Confirmation (required)**: Confirm that you have completed this form accurately and truthfully.
* **Additional Comments (optional)**: Provide any additional information you would like to include with your nomination.
