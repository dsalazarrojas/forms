# Family Reunion Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Reunion Leave Of Absence Request form is used to submit a formal request for a family reunion-related absence from work. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured, and your manager is informed of your planned absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID: This is a unique identifier assigned to you by HR. Enter your employee ID to ensure that your manager receives your request accurately.
2. Select your Start Date: Enter the date you plan to start your family reunion leave.
3. Select your End Date: Enter the date you plan to return to work after your family reunion leave.
4. Provide a Reason For Leave: Briefly explain why you are requesting family leave. This helps your manager understand your situation.
5. List Family Members Attending: If applicable, provide a list of family members who will be attending the family reunion.
6. Specify the Duration Of Leave: Enter the number of days you will be taking as leave.
7. Select Manager Review: Choose whether you want your manager to review and approve your request.
8. Add any Comments: If you have any additional comments or details, please include them here.
9. Select your Employee Status: Choose your current employee status (e.g., active, on leave, etc.).
10. Select Form Submission Status: This is an automated field and should be selected as "Submitted".

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Your unique employee ID.

* **Duration Of Leave** (`duration_of_leave`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you will be taking as leave.
* **Manager Review** (`manager_review`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you want your manager to review and approve your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or details, please include them here.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your current employee status (e.g., active, on leave, etc.).

* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `email`, required): This field is automatically populated with your email address.
