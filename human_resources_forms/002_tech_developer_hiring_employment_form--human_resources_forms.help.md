# Tech Developer Hiring Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about your application for a tech developer position, including your skills, experience, education, and work preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Application Form**: This section is for your basic contact information. Please provide your name, phone number, and email address.
2. **Resume**: Upload your resume, which should include your work experience, skills, and education.
3. **Availability**: Indicate your availability to start working immediately, and also specify your expected start and end dates, if applicable.
4. **Experience**: Provide a brief description of your previous work experience in the field of tech development.
5. **Skills**: Choose the skills you possess that are relevant to the tech developer role.
6. **Salary and Benefits**: Specify your desired salary range and choose the benefits you're interested in, such as health insurance, pension plan, and so on.
7. **Job Details**: Select the job title, work location, and job type you're interested in.
