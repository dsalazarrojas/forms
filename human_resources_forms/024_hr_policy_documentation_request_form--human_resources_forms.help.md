# HR Policy Documentation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request documentation of new or updated policies within the company. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the policy is properly documented and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the type of policy you are requesting (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
3. Choose the date you are requesting the policy (if applicable).
4. Provide a brief description of the purpose of the policy.
5. Select the department and team that the policy applies to.
6. Enter the name and email of the person requesting the policy (requestor).
7. Enter the name and email of the department head and manager.
8. Select the status of the document (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
9. Select the status of the request (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
10. Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
11. If you are attaching any documentation, provide the URL.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Your name as an employee.
* **Employee Email**: Your company email address.
* **Document Type**: Select 'Yes' if you are requesting a new policy, or 'No' if you are updating an existing policy.
* **Request Date**: The date you are requesting the policy (if applicable).
* **Purpose**: A brief description of the purpose of the policy.
* **Department**: The department that the policy applies to.
* **Team**: The team that the policy applies to.
* **Requestor Name**: The name of the person requesting the policy.
* **Requestor Email**: The email address of the person requesting the policy.
* **Department Head**: The name of the department head.
* **Department Head Email**: The email address of the department head.
* **Manager Name**: The name of your manager.
* **Manager Email**: The email address of your manager.
* **Document Status**: Select the status of the document (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Request Status**: Select the status of the request (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Attachment**: If you are attaching any documentation, provide the URL.
* **Attachment URL**: The URL of the attached documentation.
* **Request Form**: Select 'Yes' if you are submitting the form, or 'No' if you are not.
