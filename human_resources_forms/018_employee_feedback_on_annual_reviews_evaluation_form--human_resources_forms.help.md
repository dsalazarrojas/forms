# Employee Feedback On Annual Reviews Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the employee's annual review process. This feedback will help the management to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to better support employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Click on the link to the form or access it through your HR department.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
3. Select a rating scale for the overall experience.
4. Write any additional comments if you feel they are necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Section**: This is the introduction to the form where you will find a brief explanation of the form purpose and instructions.
* **Feedback Questions**: Please rate the following questions using the number scale:
	+ 1 = Not Satisfied
	+ 2 = Satisfied
	+ 3 = Very Satisfied
* **Questions and Answers**: Write your honest and constructive feedback on the following questions:
	+ What did we do well?
	+ What could we improve on?
* **Rating Scale**: Rate your overall experience with the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the following options:
	+ A number scale (1-5)
* **Comments**: Write any additional comments you feel are necessary to provide context or clarify your feedback.
* **Additional Comments**: Write any further comments or suggestions you have.
