# Employee Guarantee Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess and record employee information, including employee name, department, position, employment date, job title, and employment status. The form helps to ensure that employee records are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter the employee name**: Type the name of the employee in the `employee_name` field.
2. **Select the department**: Choose the department where the employee works from the `department` field.
3. **Enter the employee position**: Type the specific position or role of the employee in the `employee_position` field.
4. **Enter the employment date**: If applicable, enter the date the employee started working with the `employment_date` field. 
5. **Enter the job title**: Type the job title of the employee in the `job_title` field.
6. **Select the employment status**: Choose the employment status of the employee from the `employment_status` field.
7. **Enter the manager's name**: Type the name of the employee's manager in the `manager_name` field.
8. **Enter the manager's email**: Enter the email address of the employee's manager in the `manager_email` field.
9. **Optional: Enter a brief description of the job title and responsibilities**: If you want to provide more information about the job title and responsibilities, type it in the `job_title_description` field.
10. **Optional: List the job title and responsibilities**: If you want to list the specific job title and responsibilities, select the options from the `job_title_responsibilities_list` field.
11. **Select the employment type**: Choose the type of employment (e.g., regular full-time, regular part-time, temp full-time, etc.) from the `employment_type` field.
12. **Enter the employment start date**: If applicable, enter the date the employee started working with the `employment_start_date` field.
13. **Optional: Enter the employment end date**: If the employee is on leave or has ended their employment, enter the date with the `employment_end_date` field.
14. **Confirm guarantee**: Select 'True' or 'False' to confirm or deny the employee's guarantee status.
15. **Optional: Enter manager's comments**: Provide any additional comments or information from the manager in the `manager_comment` field.

* **employee_name**: The name of the employee.
* **department**: The department where the employee works.
* **employee_position**: The specific position or role of the employee.
