# Work Location Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request authorization for an employee to work at a specific location. This form is used by employees to submit their work location requests, which are then reviewed and approved by their manager and team lead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including the work location, start date, end date, manager comments, status, and assigned to fields.
2. Select the correct manager and team lead approvals.
3. Enter the assigned date, due date, completed date, and completed by fields.
4. Choose the correct work hours and work days.
5. Select the correct work location ID, manager name, team lead name, employee name, department, job title, employee department, employee job title, and employee manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Location Request**: Enter the specific location where you would like to work.
* **Manager Approvals**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that your manager has approved your request.
* **Team Lead Approvals**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that your team lead has approved your request.
* **Work Location**: Enter the date you would like to start and end working at the specified location.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start working at the specified location.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like to end working at the specified location.
* **Team Lead Signature**: This field is not required, but it's recommended that the team lead signs off on the form to confirm their approval.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any additional comments or concerns from your manager.
* **Status**: Select 'Active' to indicate that your request is active, or 'Inactive' to indicate that your request is inactive.
* **Assigned To**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have been assigned to work at the specified location.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date you were assigned to work at the specified location.
* **Due Date**: Enter the date by which you need to complete the work at the specified location.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the date you completed the work at the specified location.
* **Completed By**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have completed the work at the specified location.
* **Work Hours**: Choose the days of the week and hours of the day you will be working at the specified location.
* **Work Days**: Choose the days of the week you will be working at the specified location.
* **Work Location ID**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have a work location ID.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **Team Lead Name**: Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name.
* **Department**: Choose the department you belong to.
* **Job Title**: Choose your job title.
* **Employee Department**: Choose the department you belong to.
* **Employee Job Title**: Choose your job title.
* **Employee Manager**: Choose your manager.
* **Tips**

Note: This form is used to track and manage work location requests, so make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
