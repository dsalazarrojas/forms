# Sample Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sample Review Form" is used for managers or supervisors to conduct regular check-ins with employees, focusing on their performance in a specific area.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Select the correct rating for the specific performance area being reviewed.
3. Provide a valid date for the review.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sample Review Form** (`sample_review_form`, `text`, **Required**): This is the title of the form, which should be completed with a brief description of the performance area being reviewed.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, **Required**): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Performance Area** (`performance_area`, `text`, **Required**): Describe the specific performance area being reviewed, such as a project, task, or team.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your performance or the performance area being reviewed.
* **Score** (`score`, `number`, **Required**): Rate your performance in the specific area, on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose a rating between 1 and 5 that best reflects your performance in the specific area.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date of the review.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **Optional**): Enter the time of the review, if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
