# Job Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Recommendation Form is used to collect information about an employee's job status, enabling us to better understand their work history and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Employee" question, indicating whether the information being provided is for a current employee or not.
2. Enter the job title the employee is or was working in.
3. Specify the duration of the employee's job.
4. Provide the start and end dates of the employee's job tenure.
5. Enter any relevant work experience the employee has, such as skills or accomplishments.
6. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Job Type" question, indicating the type of job the employee is or was working in.
7. Select the current or past status of the employee ("Active" or "Inactive").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (Select One): A flag indicating if the information being provided is about a current employee. This will help us differentiate between active and inactive employment records.
* **Job Title**: A brief description of the job the employee is or was working in.
* **Job Duration**: The length of time the employee spent in the job, measured in months or years.
* **Job Start Date**: The date the employee started their job.
* **Job End Date**: The date the employee ended their job (if applicable).
* **Work Experience**: A field to capture any relevant work experience, such as skills or achievements.
* **Job Type**: A flag indicating the type of job the employee is or was working in.
* **Employee Status** (Select Multiple): The current or past status of the employee ("Active" or "Inactive"). 

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form, especially for the "Work Experience" field.
* If the employee is currently working, please ensure you are not duplicating efforts by checking for existing job recommendations.
* If the employee is no longer working, please try to provide as much detail as possible for the job start and end dates.
