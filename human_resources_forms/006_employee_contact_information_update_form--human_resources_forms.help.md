# Employee Contact Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to update employee contact information in our system. This information includes first and last names, email addresses, phone numbers, and job titles.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as per your official records.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per your official records.
3. **Email**: Enter your official work email address.
4. **Job Title**: Enter your official job title.
5. **Department**: Enter your department name.
6. **Work Phone**: Enter your official work phone number.
7. **Work Phone Extension**: Enter your work phone extension if applicable.
8. **Cell Phone**: Enter your official cell phone number.
9. **Job Title Ext**: Enter any additional information about your job title.
10. **Work E-mail**: Enter the email address of your manager.
11. **Work E-mail Extension**: Enter the email extension for your manager's email address.
12. **Department Ext**: Enter any additional information about your department.
13. **Manager First Name**: Enter the first name of your manager.
14. **Manager Last Name**: Enter the last name of your manager.
15. **Manager E-mail**: Enter your manager's official email address.
16. **Manager Cell Phone**: Enter your manager's official cell phone number.
17. **Reporting Manager First Name**: Enter the first name of your reporting manager.
18. **Reporting Manager Last Name**: Enter the last name of your reporting manager.
19. **Reporting Manager E-mail**: Enter the email address of your reporting manager.
20. **Reporting Manager Cell Phone**: Enter the cell phone number of your reporting manager.
21. **Manager Title**: Enter your manager's official title.
22. **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's official phone number.
23. **Manager Phone Ext**: Enter your manager's phone extension if applicable.
24. **Manager Job Title**: Enter your manager's job title.
25. **Manager Department**: Enter your manager's department name.
