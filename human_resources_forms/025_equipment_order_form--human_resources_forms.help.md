# Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Order Form is designed to collect information about equipment ordered by users in a business setting. It helps track and manage equipment orders, including the type of equipment, description, order date, delivery date, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Equipment Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the equipment type in the **equipment_type** field. This field should be filled with the specific type or category of the equipment being ordered.
2. In the **equipment_description** field, provide a brief description of the equipment being ordered. This will help the relevant department or personnel understand the purpose and specifics of the ordered equipment.
3. In the **requested_by** field, enter the name of the person or department that requested the equipment.
4. Enter the **order_date** and **delivery_date** for the ordered equipment. This will help track the timeline of the order.
5. Select the **status** of the order from the options provided (pending, approved, or declined).
6. If necessary, provide additional information about the reason for the **status** chosen in the **reason** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **equipment_type**: Enter the type or category of the equipment being ordered.
* **equipment_description**: Provide a brief description of the equipment.
* **requested_by**: Enter the name of the person or department that requested the equipment.
* **order_date**: Enter the date the equipment was ordered.
* **delivery_date**: Enter the expected delivery date for the equipment.
* **status**: Select the current status of the order (pending, approved, or declined).
* **reason**: If necessary, provide a brief explanation for the chosen status.
