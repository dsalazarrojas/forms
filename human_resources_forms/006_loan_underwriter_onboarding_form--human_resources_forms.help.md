# Loan Underwriter Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Underwriter Onboarding Form is designed to gather essential information about new loan underwriters, facilitating a quick and efficient onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Loan Underwriter Onboarding Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, job title, and other relevant details in the "Loan Underwriter Details" field.
2. Describe your educational background and relevant degrees in the "Education" field.
3. List your relevant certifications, licenses, and qualifications in the "Qualifications" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Underwriter Details**
  (`Loan Underwriter Details`, text, **required**): This field requires you to enter your name, job title, or any other relevant details about yourself as a loan underwriter.
* **Education**
  (`Education`, text, **required**): Describe your educational background, including any relevant degrees, institutions, or courses related to your role as a loan underwriter.
* **Qualifications**
  (`Qualifications`, text, **required**): List your relevant certifications, licenses, or qualifications that align with your role and responsibilities as a loan underwriter.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly to facilitate a smooth onboarding process.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, refer to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
