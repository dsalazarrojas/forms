# Applicant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Applicant Consent Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your preferences for data processing and collection. This form is used to obtain your explicit consent for various activities related to your job application, such as contact, background checks, and reference checks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. In the "Consent to collect and process personal data" field, select "I consent" if you agree to data collection and processing.
5. In the "Consent for reference checks" field, choose "I consent" if you're okay with being contacted by listed references.
6. For "Consent for background checks", select "I consent" if you allow background checks.
7. In "Electronic signature", type your full name.
8. Select the date when you provided consent.
9. Optionally, add any additional comments or restrictions in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
- **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Provide your contact email address.
- **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your best phone number to reach you.
- **Consent to collect and process personal data** (`consent_data_processing`, select_one, required): Select "I consent" to allow data processing.
- **Consent for reference checks** (`consent_reference_check`, select_one, required): Choose "I consent" to be contacted by listed references.
- **Consent for background checks** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I consent" for background checks.
- **Electronic signature** (`consent_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
- **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Select the date when you provided consent.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or restrictions.
