# HR Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Audit Form is used to collect and document key information about employees, including their personal details, job information, performance, benefits, and termination status. This form helps HR managers and administrators to perform audits and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the employee's **Employee Name** in the `Employee Name` field.
2. Select the employee's **Department** from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the employee's **Employee ID** number.
4. Enter the employee's **Job Title**.
5. Select the employee's **Job Title Level** (Manager or Non-Manager).
6. Select the employee's **Reporting Manager** from the dropdown list.
7. Enter the employee's **Date of Birth** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
8. Enter the employee's **Date of Hire** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
9. Enter the employee's **Last Review Date** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
10. Select the employee's **Last Review Result** (Pass or Fail).
11. Enter any **Last Review Comments**.
12. Enter the employee's **Performance Comments**.
13. Enter the employee's **Salary**.
14. Enter the employee's **Bonus**.
15. Select the employee's **Benefits** (Health, Dental, Vision, 401(k), Life).
16. Enter the employee's **Benefits Start Date** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
17. Enter the employee's **Benefits End Date** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
18. Enter the employee's **Termination Date** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY), if applicable.
19. Enter the employee's **Termination Reason**.
20. Enter any **Termination Comments**.
21. Enter the employee's **Job Change Date** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY), if applicable.
22. Enter the employee's **Job Change Reason**.
23. Select the employee's **Training** (Mandatory or Optional).
24. Enter the employee's **Training Start Date** in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY), if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's full name.
* **Department**: Select the department the employee belongs to (HR, Finance, Operations).
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Job Title Level**: Select whether the employee is a Manager or Non-Manager.
* **Reporting Manager**: Select the manager who reports directly to this employee.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the employee's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Hire**: Enter the employee's date of hire in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Last Review Date**: Enter the date of the last performance review in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Last Review Result**: Select the outcome of the last performance review (Pass or Fail).
* **Last Review Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the last performance review.
* **Performance Comments**: Enter any comments about the employee's performance.
* **Salary**: Enter the employee's current salary.
* **Bonus**: Enter the employee's current bonus.
* **Benefits**: Select the benefits the employee is currently receiving (Health, Dental, Vision, 401(k), Life).
* **Benefits Start Date**: Enter the date when the employee started receiving benefits in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Benefits End Date**: Enter the date when the employee stopped receiving benefits in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date of termination, if applicable.
* **Termination Reason**: Enter the reason for termination.
* **Termination Comments**: Enter any comments about the termination.
* **Job Change Date**: Enter the date of the last job change, if applicable.
* **Job Change Reason**: Enter the reason for the last job change.
* **Training**: Select whether the employee is currently undergoing training (Mandatory or Optional).
* **Training Start Date**: Enter the date when the employee started training in the format MM/DD/YYYY, if applicable.
