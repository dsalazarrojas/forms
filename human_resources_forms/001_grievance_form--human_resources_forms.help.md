# grievance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grievance_form is a critical tool used to document and track employee grievances in the workplace. It helps ensure that all complaints, issues, or concerns are addressed promptly and professionally. This guide will walk you through the process of completing this form to provide accurate and comprehensive information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the grievance_form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the **Introduction** section, which is a brief overview of the grievance or concern.
2.  In the **Grievance Statement** section, provide a detailed description of the issue or problem.
3.  Choose the **Grievance Category** from the available options to help categorize the grievance.
4.  Specify whether this grievance has **Supporting Documents** by checking 'Yes' or 'No'.
5.  Indicate whether the grievance has been **Resolved** or not.
6.  Choose the **Grievance Resolution Date** if it applies.
7.  Identify the **Grievance Respondent**, who is the person responding to the grievance.
8.  Specify the **Grievance Respondent's Role**.
9.  Enter the **Grievance Responder's Contact** information.
10.  Enter the **Grievance Resolution Contact** information.
11.  Add any **Grievance Comments** if necessary.
12.  Check if the **Grievance Form was Submitted**.
13.  Enter the **Grievance Date Submitted** if necessary.
14.  Identify the **Grievance Updated By**.
15.  Enter the **Grievance Updated On** if necessary.
16.  Check if the **Grievance was Closed**.
17.  Enter the **Grievance Closed By**.
18.  Enter the **Grievance Closed On** if necessary.
19.  Specify the **Grievance Closed Status**.
20.  Check if the **Grievance Form was Submitted By**.
21.  Enter the **Grievance Last Updated**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief description of the grievance or concern.
* **Grievance Statement**: A detailed description of the grievance or concern.
* **Grievance Category**: A selection from the available options to help categorize the grievance.
* **Supporting Documents**: Check 'Yes' if there are documents supporting the grievance, 'No' otherwise.
* **Grievance Resolution**: Check if the grievance has been resolved.
* **Grievance Resolution Date**: Enter the date when the grievance was resolved.
* **Grievance Respondent**: The person responding to the grievance.
* **Grievance Respondent's Role**: The role of the grievance respondent.
* **Grievance Responder's Contact**: The contact information of the grievance responder.
* **Grievance Resolution Contact**: The contact information of the grievance resolution.
* **Grievance Comments**: Any additional comments related to the grievance.
* **Grievance Form was Submitted**: Check if the form was submitted.
* **Grievance Date Submitted**: The date the form was submitted.
* **Grievance Updated By**: The person who updated the form.
* **Grievance Updated On**: The date the form was updated.
* **Grievance Closed**: Check if the grievance was closed.
* **Grievance Closed By**: The person who closed the grievance.
* **Grievance Closed On**: The date the grievance was closed.
* **Grievance Closed Status**: The status of the grievance after it was closed.
* **Grievance Form was Submitted By**: Check if the form was submitted by the current user.
* **Grievance Last Updated**: The last date the form was updated.
