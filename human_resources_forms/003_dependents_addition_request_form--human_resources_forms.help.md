# Dependents Addition Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dependents Addition Request Form is used to provide information about an employee's dependents, such as their name, type, and contact details. This form is typically completed by employees to update their HR records and ensure their benefits or insurance information is accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully and accurately to ensure your dependent's information is up-to-date.
2. Select the correct relationship type for each dependent.
3. Enter the date of birth, address, and employment status of each dependent.
4. If the dependent has a manager, select whether relationship manager approval is required or not.
5. Enter any comments from the manager for review.
6. Select the review status of the form (In Progress, Complete, or Not Required).
7. Enter any review notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dependent Name**: Enter the full name of the dependent, e.g. John Doe.
* **Dependent Type**: Select the type of dependent (e.g. spouse, child, partner, etc.).
* **Dependent Date of Birth**: Enter the dependent's date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g. 01/01/1990).
* **Dependent Relationship Status**: Select the relationship status (e.g. Single, Common Law, Married, Divorced).
* **Dependent Employment Status**: Select the employment status of the dependent (e.g. Employed, Student, Retired, Unemployed).
* **Dependent Income Range**: Select the income range of the dependent (e.g. Less than $20,000, $20,000-$30,000, etc.).
* **Dependent Age**: Enter the age of the dependent.
* **Dependent Address**: Enter the dependent's address.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name.
* **Manager Review**: Enter any comments from your manager.
* **Manager Signature**: Enter your manager's signature.
* **Date**: Enter the date in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Title**: Enter your manager's title.
* **Manager Department**: Enter your manager's department.
* **Manager Job Title**: Enter your manager's job title.
* **Manager Location**: Enter your manager's location.
* **Dependent Relationship Manager Approval**: Select whether relationship manager approval is required or not.
* **Dependent Relationship Manager Comment**: Enter any comments from the relationship manager.
* **Employee Relationship Manager Approval**: Select whether employee relationship manager approval is required or not.
* **Employee Relationship Manager Comment**: Enter any comments from the employee relationship manager.
* **Date of Review**: Enter the date of review in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Review Status**: Select the review status (e.g. In Progress, Complete, Not Required).
* **Review Notes**: Enter any review notes.

Note: This form is typically completed by employees to update their HR records and ensure their benefits or insurance information is accurate.
