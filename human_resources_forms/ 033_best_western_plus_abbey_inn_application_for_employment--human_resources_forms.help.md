# Best Western Plus Abbey Inn Application For Employment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and relevant information from applicants for employment opportunities at Best Western Plus Abbey Inn. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to help our team assess your qualifications and suitability for available roles.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your Full name in the "Full name" field.
2.  Enter a valid email address in the "Email address" field for contact purposes.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field, including your country code if applicable.
4.  Select the relevant date for your application in the "Date" field.
5.  Choose the option that best applies to your primary selection in the "Primary selection" field.
6.  Provide a whole number for quantity or rating in the "Quantity or rating" field, if required.
7.  Add any additional relevant details in the "Additional details" field.
8.  Select the options that apply to your agreement in the "Agreement" field.
9.  Enter an estimated value in the "Estimated value" field, if required, using decimal format.
10. Enter a preferred time for scheduling in the "Preferred time" field, if required.
11. Add any final comments or notes in the "Final comments or notes" field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name for identification purposes.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes, used by our team to communicate with you.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Include your country code if you are outside the local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, `date`, required): Select the relevant date for your application.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best applies to your qualifications or preferences.
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, `number`, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating, if applicable.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Add any additional relevant information about your qualifications or preferences.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the options that apply to your agreement, including I agree, I need follow up, or Not applicable.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, optional): Enter an estimated value in decimal format, if required.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time for scheduling, if required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any final comments or notes about your application.
