# Notification Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to notify the team or organization of an employee's absence from work, including the dates of absence and the reason for the absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct dates for your shift and reason for absence.
2. Fill in the shift time, start date, and end date fields with the corresponding information.
3. Provide a clear and concise reason for your absence in the Reason for Absence field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date, optional)
* This field is where you enter the date of your shift that you will be absent from.

### **Shift Time** (`shift_time`, time, optional)
* This field is where you enter the time of your shift when you will be absent.

### **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, optional)
* This field is where you provide a clear and concise reason for your absence.

### **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional)
* This field is where you enter the start date of your absence.

### **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional)
* This field is where you enter the end date of your absence.
