# repatriation_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for repatriation planning, a process of returning employees working overseas back to their home country.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the job title field with the employee's current job title.
2. Select the job classification of the employee from the provided options.
3. Enter the return date of the employee.
4. Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
5. Enter the contact person's email address.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the repatriation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's current job title.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, select_one, required/optional): Select the job classification of the employee from the provided options.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the employee is expected to return to their home country.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who will be handling the repatriation process.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the repatriation process.
