The "Employee Casual Cook Registration" form is designed for employees to register their interest and availability for casual cooking events or shifts within the company. This form helps the organizers of these events understand who can participate and assign shifts accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Fill out the General Information
- Please provide your name and any other relevant general information in the designated field.

### Step 2: Select Your Availability
- Choose when you are available to work. This will help us understand when you can participate in casual cooking events.

### Step 3: Select Your Cuisine Experience
- Pick the types of cuisines you have experience with, such as Asian, Italian, or Vegetarian.

### Step 4: Choose Your Shifts
- Select the time slots you are willing to work, such as Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner shifts.

### Step 5: Provide Emergency Contact Info (Optional)
- If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their details. This is a helpful contact in case of any last-minute changes to your availability.
