Retail Staff Maternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Staff Maternity Leave Of Absence Form is a required form for any retail staff member requesting maternity leave of absence from their work. This form is used to inform management about the staff member's absence and to document necessary information for their return to work after maternity leave.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your name from the employee dropdown menu.
2. Enter your start date of maternity leave in the Start Date field.
3. Enter your end date of maternity leave in the End Date field.
4. If applicable, provide a reason for your leave in the Reason For Leave field.
5. Select your manager's name from the Manager dropdown menu.
6. Enter your contact email in the Contact Email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
8. Add any additional notes or information in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, select_one, required): Select your name from the dropdown menu to identify yourself as the staff member requesting maternity leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are starting your maternity leave. This is usually the date when you will be leaving your work and not returning to work until after your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are expected to return to work after your maternity leave.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a reason for your maternity leave. Please be as brief as possible.
* **Manager** (`manager_name`, select_multiple, required): Select your manager's name from the dropdown menu to notify them about your absence.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address so that your manager can reach you for any updates or information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case your manager needs to contact you for any urgent matters.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your maternity leave in this field.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out before submitting the form.
