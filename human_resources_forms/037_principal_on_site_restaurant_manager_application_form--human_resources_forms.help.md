# Principal On Site Restaurant Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Principal On-Site Restaurant Manager Application Form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for a Principal On-Site Restaurant Manager position. This form will help you provide a clear overview of your application, including your contact details, work history, qualifications, and language skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the contact details section.
2. Select your current work place and employment duration (if applicable).
3. Choose your job title from the provided options or specify it if not listed.
4. Select your availability and work hours (if applicable).
5. Provide a brief description of your previous work experience and the restaurants you've worked at.
6. Specify your job title at your previous restaurant (if applicable).
7. Choose your language skills (if applicable).
8. Provide a brief description of your qualifications and education (if applicable).
9. Select any relevant certifications you hold (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title 
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to enter a brief title for your application.

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: true): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.

* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `number`, required: true): Enter your phone number for us to get in touch with you.

* **Current Work Place** (`current_work_place`, `text`, required: false): If you're not currently working, please leave this field blank. Otherwise, enter the name of your current work place.

* **Employment Duration** (`employment_duration`, `number`, required: false): If you've been employed for less than a year, please leave this field blank. Otherwise, enter the number of months or years you've been employed.

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the job title that best describes your role. Options include:
	+ Waiter/Server
	+ Kitchen Staff
	+ Host/Hostess
	+ Bartender
	+ Chef/Cook
	+ Restaurant Manager
	+ Other (please specify)

* **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required: false): If you have any restrictions on your availability, please let us know.

* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `time`, required: false): If you have a specific schedule, please let us know.

* **Previous Work Experience** (`previous_work_experience`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your previous work experience in the restaurant industry.

* **Previous Restaurants Worked** (`previous_restaurants_worked`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any restaurants where you've previously worked.

* **Job Title at Restaurant** (`job_title_at_restaurant`, `text`, required: false): If you had a job title at your previous restaurant, please specify it.

* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any languages you're proficient in.
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ German
	+ Other (please specify)

* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, `text`, required: false): If you have any relevant certifications or qualifications, please describe them.

* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): If you have any relevant education or training, please describe it.

* **Certifications** (`certification`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any relevant certifications you hold.
	+ Food Safety Certification
	+ Service Certification
	+ Other (please specify)

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields (marked as required).
* If you're unsure about any field, leave it blank or specify "N/A".
* You can attach any supporting documents or materials with your application.
* We'll review your application and get in touch with you if we need more information.
