# Project Management Interview Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Project Management Interview Schedule form is used to collect and manage schedules for project management interviews, including interview dates, times, and other relevant details. This information is crucial for coordinating with candidates and ensuring that everyone is on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Provide a brief description of the interview purpose in the `title` field.
3. For each interview, fill out the corresponding page.
4. Fill out the following fields for each interview:
	* `candidate_name`: Enter the candidate's name.
	* `interview_date`: Enter the date of the interview.
	* `interview_time`: Enter the time of the interview (optional).
	* `interview_location`: Enter the location of the interview.
	* `interviewer_name`: Enter the name of the interviewer.
	* `interview_type`: Select the type of interview (e.g., "Phone", "In-person", etc.).
	* `follow_up_info`: Provide any follow-up information or instructions for the next meeting.
	* `schedule_status`: Update the interview status (e.g., "Completed", "Rescheduled", etc.).
5. For optional fields:
	* `contact_number`: Enter a contact number for further communication.
	* `email`: Enter a contact email.
	* `notes`: Add any additional notes for the interview.
	* `schedule_date`: Enter the date of the scheduled meeting.
	* `schedule_time`: Enter the time of the scheduled meeting.
	* `schedule_duration`: Enter the duration of the meeting.
	* `schedule_frequency`: Select the frequency of the scheduled meeting (e.g., "Weekly", "Monthly", etc.).
	* `schedule_note`: Add any additional notes for the scheduled meeting.
