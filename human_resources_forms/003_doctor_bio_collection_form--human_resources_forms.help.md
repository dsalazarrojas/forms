# Doctor Bio Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Bio Collection Form is used to collect and store information about a doctor's background, qualifications, and credentials. This form helps maintain accurate and up-to-date records for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the medical practice name, if applicable.
2. Provide the doctor's last name.
3. Enter the doctor's first name.
4. Indicate the doctor's medical degree.
5. Specify the doctor's specialty.
6. Enter the doctor's phone number.
7. Provide the doctor's email address.
8. Enter the doctor's National Provider Identifier (NPI) number.
9. Enter the doctor's medical license number.
10. Upload a photo of the doctor (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Practice Name** (`medical_practice_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical practice where the doctor works.
* **Doctor Last Name** (`doctor_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's last name.
* **Doctor First Name** (`doctor_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's first name.
* **Medical Degree** (`medical_degree`, text, optional): Specify the doctor's medical degree (e.g., MD, DO, etc.).
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, text, optional): Indicate the doctor's area of specialty (e.g., cardiology, pediatrics, etc.).
* **Doctor Phone Number** (`doctor_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, optional): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Doctor NPI** (`doctor_npi`, number, optional): Enter the doctor's National Provider Identifier (NPI) number.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's medical license number.
* **Doctor Photo** (`doctor_photo`, note, optional): Upload a photo of the doctor (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant department or contact us for guidance.
* If you need to update or remove any information, please use the "Edit" feature or contact us for assistance.
