# Tax Equalization Inquiry Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to request an inquiry on tax equalization for employees. This form helps gather information from employees who have questions or concerns about their tax-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of tax-related inquiry you are making (e.g., Tax Refund, Tax Credit, or Tax Exemption) from the "Inquiry Type" field.
2. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the employee's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the tax year for which you are making the inquiry (e.g., 2022, 2023, etc.) from the "Tax Year" field.
5. Enter the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
6. Optionally, enter the date of the inquiry in the "Date of Inquiry" field.
7. Provide a detailed description of your tax-related inquiry in the "Inquiry Details" field.
8. (Optional) Assign the inquiry to a specific user by entering their name in the "Assigned User" field.
9. Select the status of the inquiry (e.g., Pending, Completed, or Declined) from the "Status" field.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about the inquiry in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name who the tax-related inquiry is about.
- **Inquiry Type**: Select the type of tax-related inquiry you are making (e.g., Tax Refund, Tax Credit, or Tax Exemption).
  • Tax Refund: Request to receive a refund on tax already paid.
  • Tax Credit: Request to apply for a tax credit.
  • Tax Exemption: Request to be exempt from tax.
- **Email**: Enter the employee's email address associated with their account.
- **Tax Year**: Select the tax year for which you are making the inquiry (e.g., 2022, 2023, etc.).
- **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number.
- **Date of Inquiry**: Optionally, enter the date of the tax-related inquiry.
- **Inquiry Details**: Provide a detailed description of your tax-related inquiry.
- **Assigned User**: Optionally, assign the inquiry to a specific user by entering their name.
- **Status**: Select the status of the inquiry (e.g., Pending, Completed, or Declined).
  • Pending: The inquiry is pending review and response.
  • Completed: The inquiry has been completed and resolved.
  • Declined: The inquiry has been declined or rejected.
- **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the inquiry.
