# Observation Sheet For HR Practices - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Observation Sheet for HR Practices is used to document and track employee performance observations. HR representatives or designated individuals fill out this form to observe an employee's practices and behaviors, providing a detailed record of their findings and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **practice name** (id: 1) of the area or task being observed.
2. Select the **observed by** (id: 2) person who conducted the observation (HR Manager, Department Head, or Team Lead).
3. Enter the **date observed** (id: 3) when the observation took place.
4. Enter the **time observed** (id: 4) when the observation took place.
5. Identify the **observed area** (id: 5) where the observation occurred.
6. Add any **notes** (id: 6) regarding the observation, if necessary.
7. Evaluate the **evaluation** (id: 7) of the observed area using a numerical scale.
8. Enter the **score** (id: 8) based on the evaluation.
9. Provide a **action plan** (id: 9) for improvement or next steps.
10. Schedule the **next review** (id: 10) for future observation.
11. Set the **follow-up** (id: 11) date for future review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **practice name** (id: 1): Enter the name of the area or task being observed.
* **observed by** (id: 2): Select the person who conducted the observation (HR Manager, Department Head, or Team Lead).
* **date observed** (id: 3): Enter the date when the observation took place.
* **time observed** (id: 4): Enter the time when the observation took place.
* **observed area** (id: 5): Enter the location or department where the observation occurred.
* **notes** (id: 6): Add any additional comments or observations about the employee's performance.
* **evaluation** (id: 7): Evaluate the performance based on a numerical scale, indicating areas for improvement.
* **score** (id: 8): Enter a score based on the evaluation.
* **action plan** (id: 9): Provide a plan of action for the employee to improve their performance.
* **next review** (id: 10): Schedule the next review or observation for future evaluation.
* **follow up** (id: 11): Set the date for future review and follow-up.

Tips:
* Please ensure that all fields are completed thoroughly and accurately.
* Be specific and objective when evaluating and scoring.
* Keep the **action plan** concise and actionable.
* Schedule the **next review** and **follow-up** dates to ensure regular progress tracking.
* If additional **notes** are added, keep them relevant and focused on the observed area.
