# Employee Data Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Data Record Form is used to collect and update employee information in a standardized format. This form helps ensure that all relevant data is recorded accurately and consistently, making it easier to manage and analyze employee information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields (First Name, Last Name, and Email) as accurately as possible.
2. If you need to select a job role, manager, or job status, browse through the options and select the appropriate choice.
3. For date fields, use the calendar picker to select the correct date or type in the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Finally, review all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's first name as it is currently known to us.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's last name as it is currently known to us.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the employee's work email address.
- **Job Role** (`job_role`, text, optional): Select the employee's primary job role within the company.
- **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, select_one, optional): Choose the employee's reporting manager from the list of options.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title as it is currently known to us.
- **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select all departments the employee is a part of.
- **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the employee's current job status from the list of options.
- **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee was hired.
- **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee's contract was terminated.
- **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the employee's termination.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
- **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of the employee's manager.
- **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the employee's manager.

## Tips
- Always use the most up-to-date information available.
- Be accurate and precise when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with HR or the employee directly.
- This form is meant to be filled out for both new hires and existing employees.
