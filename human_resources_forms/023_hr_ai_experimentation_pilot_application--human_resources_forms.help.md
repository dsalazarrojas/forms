# hr_ai_experimentation_pilot_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from HR teams for AI experimentation pilot applications. It helps HR leaders to understand the goals, expected benefits, and resource needs for a successful pilot. The form is used to collect relevant data for the HR AI experimentation pilot application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the proposal details**: In the first page, provide a brief description of your proposal.
2. **List your goals**: In the second page, specify the goals you want to achieve with the pilot application.
3. **Explain expected benefits**: In the third page, describe the expected benefits of the pilot application.
4. **List resource needs**: In the fourth page, specify the resources required to support the pilot application.
5. **Choose your submission options**: In the last page, select the submission option that best suits your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposal Details** (`proposal_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your proposal.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required: false): Specify the goals you want to achieve with the pilot application.
* **Expected Benefits** (`expected_benefits`, `text`, required: false): Describe the expected benefits of the pilot application.
* **Resource Needs** (`resource_needs`, `text`, required: false): Specify the resources required to support the pilot application.
* **Submission Form** (`submission_form`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the submission option that best suits your application. Options include:
	+ HR AI Experimentation Pilot Application
	+ AI-Experimentation-Pilot-Application-Form
	+ Form-for-HR-AI-Experimentation-Pilot-Application

## Tips
* Be specific and clear in your responses to ensure a smooth review process.
* Ensure that you have all necessary resources listed for the pilot application.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting.
