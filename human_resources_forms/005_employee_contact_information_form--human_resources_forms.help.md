# Employee Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Contact Information Form is used to collect and store essential contact information for employees. This form is typically used by HR or management to update or obtain contact information for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your current contact information, including First Name, Last Name, Email, Work Phone, and Cell Phone.
2. If an emergency contact is required, enter their First Name and Last Name, as well as their phone number.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient communication and emergency contact in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification or documentation.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification or documentation.
* **Email**: Provide your official work email address for internal and external correspondence.
* **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number, including the area code and extension (if applicable).
* **Cell Phone**: Enter your cell phone number, including the area code.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
