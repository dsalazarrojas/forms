# Work Hours Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Hours Complaint Form is used to report work-related issues or concerns regarding work hours, such as clocking errors, pay disputes, or time-off request problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a complaint, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information.
2. Enter a brief description of the issue you are experiencing.
3. Select the tool or person responsible for the issue.
4. Choose who you would like to submit your complaint to (Manager, HR, or both).
5. Provide any additional comments or context about the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Hours** (required, text): Enter a clear and concise description of your work hours complaint. This should include the dates, times, and specific issues you're experiencing.
* **Reason** (required, text): Please provide a brief explanation of why you're submitting this complaint. This helps us understand your concerns and provide a more effective resolution.
* **Comments** (optional, text): If you have any additional information or context about the issue, you can include it here.
* **Date** (required, date): Please enter the date of when you clocked in or out and when the issue occurred.
* **Time** (required, time): Enter the time of when you clocked in or out and when the issue occurred.
* **Assigned Tool** (required, select one): Choose the tool or system that was used to clock in or out.
* **Submitted By** (required, select multiple): Choose who you would like to submit your complaint to (Manager, HR, or both).

## Tips
* Please be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about who to submit your complaint to, please choose both Manager and HR.
* If you have any additional documentation or evidence, please attach it to the form or provide it separately.
