# employee_survey_data_analytics_fellowship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data from employees about their job details, experiences, and skills, which will be used for analytics and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the required fields:
	* **Form Details (1)**: Briefly describe the form you are filling out.
2. Answer the optional fields:
	* **Job Title (2)**: Check if you have a job title.
	* **Name (3)**: Enter your name.
	* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address.
	* **Resume (5)**: Attach your resume (optional).
	* **Experience (6)**: Describe your relevant experience.
	* **Skills (7)**: Select the skills you possess.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details (1)** (text): Briefly describe the form you are filling out.
* **Job Title (2)** (select_one, required: false): Check if you have a job title.
* **Name (3)** (text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email (4)** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Resume (5)** (note, required: false): Attach your resume if you have one.
* **Experience (6)** (text, required: false): Describe your relevant experience.
* **Skills (7)** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the skills you possess.
