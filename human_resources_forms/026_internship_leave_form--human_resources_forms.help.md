# Internship Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an official internship leave from your organization. It will help your supervisor to review and approve or reject your leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Start Date** field with the date you would like to begin your internship leave.
2. Fill in the **End Date** field with the date you would like to end your internship leave.
3. Enter the number of **Days Requested** for your leave in the corresponding field.
4. Provide a clear **Reason for Leave** explaining why you are requesting the leave.
5. Enter your **Supervisor Name** and **Email Address** so that your supervisor can contact you for approval or any further information.
6. If you have any **Notes** regarding your leave request, you can enter them in the **Notes** field.
7. Finally, select the **Approval Status** to inform your supervisor of the outcome of your leave request.
8. Once you have filled in all the necessary fields, submit the form for your supervisor to review and process your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date**: The date you would like to begin your internship leave.
* **End Date**: The date you would like to end your internship leave.
* **Days Requested**: The number of days you are requesting for your leave.
* **Reason for Leave**: A clear explanation of why you are requesting the leave.
* **Supervisor Name**: The name of your supervisor who will review and approve or reject your leave request.
* **Supervisor Email Address**: The email address of your supervisor.
* **Approval Date**: The date when your supervisor will approve or reject your leave request.
* **Approval Status**: This field will inform your supervisor of the outcome of your leave request.
* **Notes**: Any additional notes or information you would like to provide regarding your leave request.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where your supervisor can contact you for further information.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where your supervisor can contact you for further information.
* **Location**: The location where you will be on leave.
* **Return Date**: The date you will return to work after your internship leave.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number is optional and should be used to provide an alternative contact method for your supervisor. 

Note: Please ensure that you fill in all the necessary fields to ensure that your supervisor can review and process your leave request efficiently.
