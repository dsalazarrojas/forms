# sample_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sample_job_application_form is a general job application form used to collect information from candidates applying for job openings. This form is designed to gather essential details about the applicant, such as name, contact information, job title, and employment history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Job Title
	* Employment Start Date
	* Employment End Date
2. Select the correct Job Type from the options provided.
3. If applicable, upload a file to support your application.
4. Review your form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Job Title**: The title of the job you're applying for.
* **Job Type**: The type of job you're applying for. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Employment Start Date**: The date you began your current or previous employment.
* **Employment End Date**: The date your current or previous employment ended.
* **File Uploader**: If you have any supporting documents, please upload them here. This field is optional.
 

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the hiring team for clarification.
* Please upload only relevant files that support your application.
