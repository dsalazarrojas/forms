# intern_shift_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "intern_shift_report_form" is a simple report for interns to document their shifts. This form helps track and record important details about their work, including date, time, and tasks completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your shift in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" for the "intern_shift_date" field.
2. Select your start time for the shift in the "start time" field.
3. In the "tasks completed today" field, list the tasks you completed during your shift. Be as detailed as possible to help with future reference.
4. If you have any additional notes or comments about your shift, enter them in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intern Shift Date** (intern_shift_date, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date of your shift in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Start Time** (start time, time, required: false)
	+ Select your start time for the shift.
* **Tasks Completed Today** (tasks completed today, text, required: false)
	+ List the tasks you completed during your shift, being as detailed as possible to help with future reference.
* **Notes** (notes, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your shift, if needed. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely, as this helps us understand your work and performance better.
* You can add any additional details or comments about your shift that are not mentioned on this form. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant person in your department.
