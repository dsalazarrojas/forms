# Succession Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Succession Planning Form is designed to document the key information about the succession plan, including the current and potential future employees involved, and the current status of the form.
## How To Complete This Form
### General Guidance
- Fill out the form in order to provide a clear succession plan, especially for employees who are leaving or retiring.
- Ensure all required fields are completed, marked with an asterisk (\*).
- The form is designed to handle multiple key employees and their descriptions.

### Step 1: General Information
1. \* **Succession Planning Form Name**: Enter a unique and descriptive name for the succession plan.
2. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company this succession plan applies to.
### Step 2: Current and Future Employees
3. \* **Current Employee Name**: Enter the name of the current employee whose role is being planned for succession.
4. **Key Employees**: Select whether or not this succession plan applies to key employees.
    - Select "Yes" if this succession plan applies to key employees.
    - Select "No" otherwise.
5. **Key Employees (optional)**: If key employees were selected, list their names and descriptions in the respective fields.

### Step 3: Dates and Status
6. **Succession Planning Form Start Date**: Select the date when this succession plan started.
7. **Succession Planning Form End Date**: Select the date when this succession plan is expected to end.
8. **Succession Planning Form Status**: Select the status of this succession plan:
    - "Active"
    - "Inactive"
### Step 4: Additional Information
9. **Succession Planning Form Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to the succession plan.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible to ensure a smooth handover.
- Review this form regularly to update the key employees and their descriptions as needed.
- Ensure this form is stored and accessible for future reference.
