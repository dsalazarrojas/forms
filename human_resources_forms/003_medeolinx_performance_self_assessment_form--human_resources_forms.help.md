# MedeoLinx Performance Self-Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MedeoLinx Performance Self-Assessment Form is a tool for employees to evaluate their performance, set goals, and identify areas for development. This form is used by managers and HR teams to assess employee progress and development needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the correct evaluation period for which you are providing self-assessment feedback.
2.  Choose your Performance Level:
	*   Low: You are not meeting your performance targets, and your performance is below expectations.
	*   Medium: You are meeting some performance targets, but there is room for improvement.
	*   High: You are consistently meeting your performance targets, and your performance is above expectations.
3.  Choose your Performance Category:
	*   Excellent: You are consistently meeting and often exceeding performance targets.
	*   Good: You are meeting most performance targets, but some areas for improvement exist.
	*   Fair: You are not meeting some performance targets, and there is room for improvement.
	*   Needs Improvement: Your performance is below expectations, and you require significant improvement.
4.  Enter your Employee Name.
5.  Enter your Manager's Name (if applicable).
6.  Enter your Performance Rating (in percentage or numeric format).
7.  Enter the Review Frequency (e.g., Every 3 months, Every 6 months, Every 12 months).
8.  Enter Performance Comments (any notable achievements or areas for improvement).
9.  Enter Recommended Actions (actions you plan to take to address areas for improvement).
10. Enter your Overall Rating (a numerical value from 1-5 or percentage value).
11.  Enter the Evaluation Period (the date range for which you are being evaluated).
12.  Enter the Evaluation Date (the date of your self-assessment).
13.  Enter the Performance Level for which you are being evaluated.
14.  Choose the Performance Category for which you are being evaluated.
15.  Enter your Manager's Rating (if applicable).
16.  Enter your Employee Rating (if applicable).
17.  Enter your Self-Assessment Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Performance Level Rating** (1: Performance Level Rating): Choose the appropriate performance level that best describes your performance during the evaluation period.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel records.
*   **Manager Name** (Manager Name): Enter the name of your manager (if applicable).
*   **Performance Rating** (Performance Rating): Enter your performance rating, which can be a numerical value or a percentage value.
*   **Review Frequency** (Review Frequency): Select the frequency of performance reviews (e.g., Every 3 months, Every 6 months, Every 12 months).
*   **Performance Comments** (Performance Comments): Enter any notable achievements or areas for improvement.
*   **Recommended Actions** (Recommended Actions): List actions you plan to take to address areas for improvement.
*   **Overall Rating** (Overall Rating): Enter a numerical value from 1 to 5 or percentage value to evaluate your overall performance.
*   **Evaluation Period** (Evaluation Period): Enter the date range for which you are being evaluated.
*   **Evaluation Date** (Evaluation Date): Enter the date of your self-assessment.
*   **Performance Level** (Performance Level): Select the performance level for which you are being evaluated.
*   **Performance Category** (Performance Category): Choose the performance category that best describes your performance during the evaluation period.
*   **Recommendation** (Recommendation): Enter your recommendation for your performance and development.
*   **Employee Rating** (Employee Rating): Enter a numerical value to evaluate your performance (if applicable).
*   **Manager Rating** (Manager Rating): Enter a numerical value to evaluate your performance (if applicable).
*   **Self-Assessment** (Self-Assessment): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have for your performance.
*   **Date** (Date): Enter the date of your self-assessment.
*   **Time** (Time): Enter the time of your self-assessment.
