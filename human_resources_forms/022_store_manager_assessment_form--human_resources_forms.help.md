# Store Manager Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help HR evaluate the performance of store managers, providing a structured way to gather feedback and insights.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form for each store manager that needs to be assessed.
2.  Answer each question thoroughly, providing concrete details and examples when possible.
3.  Use the select_one field for the overall rating to indicate your assessment of the store manager's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the store manager being assessed.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Identify the specific department this store manager is responsible for.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Specify the store manager's job title.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): If applicable, provide the store manager's years of experience in their role.
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, text, optional): Share any observations, concerns, or areas for improvement related to the store manager's performance.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor, to reflect your overall assessment of the store manager's performance.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional): List the store manager's strengths or areas where they excel.
* **Overall Rating Explanation** (Implicit): The Overall Rating field has four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor. Choose the option that best reflects your assessment of the store manager's performance.

## Tips

*   Be thorough and honest when filling out this form.
*   Consider providing concrete examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the form's purpose and the context in which it's being used.
