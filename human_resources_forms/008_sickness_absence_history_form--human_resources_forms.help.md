# sickness_absence_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track an employee's sickness absence history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's ID in the **employee_id** field.
2. Enter the start date of the absence in the **start_date** field.
3. Enter the end date of the absence in the **end_date** field.
4. Enter the reason for the absence in the **reason** field.
5. Select the type of sickness in the **sickness_type** field.
6. Enter the duration of the absence in the **duration** field.
7. Choose any supporting documents relevant to the absence in the **supporting_documents** field.
8. Provide any additional comments or information related to the absence in the **comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **employee_id** (employee_id, number, required: false)
* This field is used to identify the employee who was absent. Enter the employee's ID number.

### **start_date** (start_date, date, required: false)
* This field is used to record the start date of the absence.

### **end_date** (end_date, date, required: false)
* This field is used to record the end date of the absence.

### **reason** (reason, text, required: false)
* This field is used to describe the reason for the absence.

### **sickness_type** (sickness_type, select_one, required: false)
* This field is used to select the type of sickness. Choose one of the available options.

### **duration** (duration, number, required: false)
* This field is used to record the duration of the absence.

### **supporting_documents** (supporting_documents, select_multiple, required: false)
* This field is used to select any supporting documents related to the absence. Choose one or more of the available options.

### **comments** (comments, text, required: false)
* This field is used to provide any additional comments or information related to the absence.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and consistently to avoid errors in processing.
* Only complete the form for an employee if it is necessary to record their sickness absence history.
* Use the supporting documents field to attach relevant files or images to the absence record.
* Keep the comments field for any additional information or context that may be relevant to the absence.
