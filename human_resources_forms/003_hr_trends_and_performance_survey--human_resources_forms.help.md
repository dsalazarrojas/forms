# hr_trends_and_performance_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hr_trends_and_performance_survey form is designed to gather insights from employees on various aspects of their work experience, including employee satisfaction, engagement, communication, performance, and more. This form will help the management team to understand trends and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your honest and accurate feedback on each question.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, but briefly.
3. If you need help or have any questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, text, required: false): Please provide your level of satisfaction with your job. You can type a brief description of what you are satisfied or dissatisfied with.
* **Engagement** (`engagement`, text, required: false): Describe your level of engagement with your work.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on the communication between you and your manager/ team members.
* **Performance** (`performance`, text, required: false): Provide feedback on your own performance and how you measure up to your goals.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, text, required: false): Describe your overall satisfaction with your job and company.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Leave any comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Demographics 1** (`demographics_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all that apply to describe your demographics:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Demographics 2** (`demographics_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all that apply to describe your demographics:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Manager Performance** (`manager_performance`, select_one, required: false): Rate your manager's performance:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, select_one, required: false): Rate your team's leadership:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Organizational Performance** (`organizational_performance`, select_one, required: false): Rate the overall performance of the organization:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **Manager Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your manager's satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **Engagement Score** (`engagement_score`, number, required: false): Rate your engagement on a scale of 1-10.
* **Time Score** (`time_score`, number, required: false): Rate your time management on a scale of 1-10.
* **Performance Score** (`performance_score`, number, required: false): Rate your performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Date 1** (`date_1`, date, required: false): Please provide the date of your last performance review.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, required: false): Please provide the date of your next performance review.
* **Time 1** (`time_1`, time, required: false): Please provide the time you started your job.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, required: false): Please provide the time you are expected to end your job.
* **Note 1** (`note_1`, note, required: false): Please provide any notes or comments on your performance.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please provide your phone number.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your manager or HR representative for clarification.
* This form is meant to be a tool for constructive feedback and improvement, so please keep your comments professional and respectful.
