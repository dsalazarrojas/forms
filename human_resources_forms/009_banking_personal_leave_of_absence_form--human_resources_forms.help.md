# Banking Personal Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request and track personal leave of absence for employees in the company. It is essential for proper documentation and approval of leave of absence to avoid any conflicts or misunderstandings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3.  Review and understand the company's leave policies and procedures before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name (1)** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): This is your full legal name.
*   **Employee ID (2)** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): This is your unique employee identifier.
*   **Department (3)** (`department`, `text`, required): This is your department or division.
*   **Direct Manager Name (4)** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of your immediate supervisor.
*   **Type of Leave (5)** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for your leave (Medical Leave, Personal Leave, Family Emergency, Maternity Leave, Paternity Leave, Sabbatical, Other).
*   **Leave Start Date (6)** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, required): This is the first day of your absence.
*   **Anticipated Return Date (7)** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, required): This is the expected date of return to work.
*   **Total Duration (8)** (`leave_duration`, `number`, required): This is how many working days you will be absent.
*   **Reason for Leave (9)** (`leave_reason`, `text`, required): A detailed explanation of the leave request.
*   **Contact Information While Away (10)** (`contact_during_absence`, `text`, optional): Provide a phone number or email for emergencies.
*   **Emergency Contact Name (11)** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): This is the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (12)** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): This is the emergency contact phone number.
*   **Work Handover Status (13)** (`work_handover_status`, `select_one`, required): This is the status of your work handover (Handover Completed, In Progress, Not Started, No Handover Needed).
*   **Coverage Plan Details (14)** (`coverage_plan`, `text`, required): This is who will cover your responsibilities.
*   **List Any Pending Projects or Tasks (15)** (`pending_projects`, `text`, optional): Describe critical items needing attention.
*   **Client Account Handover (16)** (`client_accounts`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have client accounts needing transition (Yes, No).
*   **Client Account Details (17)** (`client_details`, `text`, optional): List client names and assigned coverage.
*   **Current Leave Balance (18)** (`leave_balance`, `number`, optional): This is your current accrued leave days.
*   **Leave Type - Paid or Unpaid (19)** (`paid_unpaid`, `select_one`, required): This is whether this leave is paid or unpaid (Paid Leave, Unpaid Leave, Partial Paid).
*   **Health Insurance During Leave (20)** (`insurance_continuation`, `select_one`, required): Select whether health insurance will continue (Yes, No, Need to Verify).
*   **Manager Approval Status (21)** (`manager_approval_requested`, `select_one`, required): This is the status of your manager's approval (Pending, Approved, Denied, Under Review).
*   **HR Review Date (22)** (`hr_review_date`, `date`, optional): This is when HR will review your request.
*   **Additional Notes or Comments (23)** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): This is any other relevant information you want to include.
