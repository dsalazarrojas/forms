# staff_burnout_prevention_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help staff members identify their own burnout prevention needs. It is intended to be filled out by staff members who are experiencing high levels of stress or burnout.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Indicate your current work hours in the "Work Hours" field.
4. Select all the stress factors that you are currently experiencing (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night Shift, Weekend).
5. Rate your burnout score on a scale from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
6. Select the support services you have received (if any) from the options provided.
7. Rate the support score you have received on a scale from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
8. Select your current well-being scale on a scale from Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
9. Enter any comments you have about your work experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title, e.g., "Software Engineer" or "Sales Manager".
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter your current work hours per week.
* **Stress Factors** (`stress_factors`, select_multiple, required): Select all the stress factors you are currently experiencing. 
* **Burnout Score** (`burnout_score`, number, required): Rate your burnout score on a scale from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Support Services** (`support_services`, select_one, optional): Select the support services you have received, if any. 
* **Support Score** (`support_score`, number, optional): Rate the support score you have received on a scale from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Well-being Scale** (`well_being_scale`, select_one, required): Select your current well-being scale on a scale from Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments about your work experience.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
* Please select all the stress factors you are currently experiencing.
* If you are not sure about your burnout score or support score, rate it based on your perception.
* If you have received support services, rate your support score accordingly.
* Select your well-being scale based on your current situation.
* Enter any comments about your work experience, if you have any.
