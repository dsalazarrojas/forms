# supervision_activity_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Supervision Activity Log" form is used by supervisors to record and track their interactions with employees, such as performance evaluations, training, or employee development activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
3. Choose the date of the supervision activity in the "Date" field.
4. Select the type of activity performed (e.g., "Yes" or "No") in the "Activity Type" field.
5. Add any additional comments about the activity in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the employee being supervised.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your name as the supervisor.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional: false): Choose the date of the supervision activity.
* **Activity Type** (`activity_type`, select_multiple, required/optional: false): Select the type of activity performed, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional: false): Provide any additional comments about the activity.
