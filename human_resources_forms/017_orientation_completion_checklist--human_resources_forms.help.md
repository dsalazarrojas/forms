# Orientation Completion Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orientation Completion Checklist is a form used to track an employee's orientation process. It captures essential information about the employee's experience, ensuring that they have a smooth transition into the company.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the employee's name and job title.
3. Provide their email address (if applicable).
4. Note the orientation date and time.
5. Specify the location of the orientation.
6. Indicate whether the employee attended the orientation.
7. Add any comments about the orientation experience.
8. Enter a unique employee ID.
9. Select the department the employee belongs to.
10. Optionally, provide the name of the school the employee attended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address (if available).
* **Orientation Date** (`orientation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the orientation.
* **Orientation Time** (`orientation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the orientation (if applicable).
* **Orientation Location** (`orientation_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the orientation.
* **Orientation Supervisor** (`orientation_supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor (if applicable).
* **Attended Orientation** (`attended_orientation`, select_one, required): Check 'Yes' if the employee attended the orientation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about the orientation experience.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter a unique ID for the employee.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select the department the employee belongs to.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the employee attended (if applicable).
