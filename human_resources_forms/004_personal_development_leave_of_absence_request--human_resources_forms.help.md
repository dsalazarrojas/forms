# Personal Development Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a personal development leave of absence for a specific period of time. It is meant to be filled out by the employee and submitted to their supervisor for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select your employee ID from the dropdown menu.
2.  Choose the start and end dates of your leave period.
3.  Select the relevant reasons for your leave from the dropdown menu for manager's review.
4.  Add any additional comments regarding your leave request.
5.  Choose the submission status of your form.
6.  Fill out the form ID if required.
7.  Select the date and time you submitted the form.
8.  Choose the person who submitted the form.
9.  Select the person who created the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your Employee ID from the dropdown menu to identify yourself. 
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of your leave period.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of your leave period.
*   **Manager Review** (`manager_review`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the reasons for your manager's review.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments about your leave request.
*   **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the status of your form submission.
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your Form ID if required.
*   **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the person who submitted the form.
*   **Created By** (`created_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the person who created the form.

## Tips
*   Before filling out this form, ensure that you have already discussed your leave request with your manager or supervisor.
*   Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to help your manager make an informed decision about your leave request.
*   If you are unsure about any part of this form, feel free to ask your manager or supervisor for assistance.
*   If you are submitting this form on behalf of another employee, please ensure that the employee you are representing has given you permission to do so and provide their name and ID.
