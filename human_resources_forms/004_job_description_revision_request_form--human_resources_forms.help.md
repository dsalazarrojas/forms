# Job Description Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request revisions to an existing job description. It is a crucial process in maintaining accurate and up-to-date job information.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the job status of the job description you are revising.
2. Enter the current job title of the job description.
3. Enter the new job title of the job description.
4. If necessary, select the effective date of the job description revision.
5. Provide the reason for the request in the "Reason for Request" field.
6. If applicable, select the type of job (full-time, part-time, contract).
7. Enter the job family of the job description.
8. Enter the name of the person requesting the job revision.
9. Enter the department of the person requesting the job revision.
10. Enter the email address of the person requesting the job revision.
11. Enter the phone number of the person requesting the job revision.
12. Enter the name of the person reviewing the job revision.
13. Enter the department of the person reviewing the job revision.
14. Enter the email address of the person reviewing the job revision.
15. Enter the phone number of the person reviewing the job revision.
16. Enter the name of the person approving the job revision.
17. Enter the department of the person approving the job revision.
18. Enter the email address of the person approving the job revision.
19. Enter the phone number of the person approving the job revision.
20. Enter any notes from the person requesting the job revision.
21. Enter any notes from the person reviewing the job revision.
22. Enter any notes from the person approving the job revision.
23. Enter any comments from the person approving the job revision.
24. Confirm that the form has been completed and the date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Job Status**: Select the current status of the job, which can be Open, Closed, or Pending.
* **Current Job Title**: Enter the current title of the job.
* **New Job Title**: Enter the new title of the job.
* **Effective Date**: If the job is being revised, enter the effective date of the change.
* **Reason for Request**: Provide a clear reason for requesting the job revision.
* **Job Type**: If applicable, select the type of job, which can be Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contract.
* **Job Family**: Enter the job family of the job description.
* **Requester Name**: Enter the name of the person requesting the job revision.
* **Requester Department**: Enter the department of the person requesting the job revision.
* **Requester Email**: Enter the email address of the person requesting the job revision.
* **Requester Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person requesting the job revision.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the job revision.
* **Reviewer Department**: Enter the department of the person reviewing the job revision.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email address of the person reviewing the job revision.
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person reviewing the job revision.
* **Approver Name**: Enter the name of the person approving the job revision.
* **Approver Department**: Enter the department of the person approving the job revision.
* **Approver Email**: Enter the email address of the person approving the job revision.
* **Approver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person approving the job revision.
* **Requester Notes**: Enter any notes from the person requesting the job revision.
* **Reviewer Notes**: Enter any notes from the person reviewing the job revision.
* **Approver Notes**: Enter any notes from the person approving the job revision.
* **Approver Comments**: Enter any comments from the person approving the job revision.
* **Form Completed**: Confirm that the form has been completed and the date.
