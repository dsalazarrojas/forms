# store_employee_termination_offboarding_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The store_employee_termination_offboarding_form is used to document the termination or offboarding of an employee. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is collected and that the employee's departure from the company is properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Termination Date**: Enter the date of the employee's termination.
2. Enter the **Reason**: Explain the reason for the employee's termination.
3. Enter the **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager responsible for the termination.
4. Enter the **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the manager responsible for the termination.
5. Enter the **Employee ID**: Enter the ID number of the employee being terminated.
6. Select the **Termination Status**: Choose the current status of the employee (Terminated, Active, Pending, or Resigned).
7. Enter the **Reason for Leaving**: Explain the reason for the employee's leaving.
8. Enter the **Notice Period**: Enter the number of days notice given to the employee.
9. Enter the **Last Day of Work**: Enter the last day the employee worked.
10. Check if **Benefits Terminated**: Check if the employee's benefits were terminated.
11. Enter the **Separation Allowance**: Enter the amount of separation allowance paid to the employee.
12. Select the **Outplacement Services** used: Choose the outplacement services used for the employee (Career Counseling, Resume Review, Job Placement, or Outplacement Services).
13. Select the **Exit Interview**: Choose the type of exit interview conducted (Manager, Employee, or Both).
14. Enter the **Final Separation Allowance**: Enter the amount of final separation allowance paid to the employee.
15. Enter the **Last Day of Work Date**: Enter the date of the last day the employee worked.
16. Enter the **Last Payroll Date**: Enter the date of the last payroll.
17. Enter the **Notice Period Date**: Enter the date the notice period started.
18. Enter the **Separation Allowance Date**: Enter the date the separation allowance was paid.
19. Enter the **Benefits Start Date**: Enter the date benefits started.
20. Enter the **Benefits End Date**: Enter the date benefits ended.
21. Enter the **Last Check Date**: Enter the date of the last check.
22. Check if **Outplacement Services Used**: Check if outplacement services were used for the employee.
23. Enter the **Manager Name 2**: Enter the name of the manager who handled the termination.
24. Enter the **Manager Email 2**: Enter the email address of the manager who handled the termination.
25. Enter the **Manager Title 2**: Enter the title of the manager who handled the termination.
