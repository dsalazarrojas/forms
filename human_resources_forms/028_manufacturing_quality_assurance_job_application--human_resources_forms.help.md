# Manufacturing Quality Assurance Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Manufacturing Quality Assurance Job Application form is designed to gather essential information from candidates applying for the Manufacturing Quality Assurance job position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form structure**: The form is divided into two sections: Candidate Information and Job Application Details.
2. **Provide complete and accurate information**: Make sure to fill out all required fields and double-check your responses for accuracy.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've completed the form, review your answers for accuracy and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Candidate Information
* **Candidate Information (id: 1)**: A text field to enter your name, title, and contact details.

### Page 2: Work Experience
* **Work Experience (id: 2)**: Select one or multiple options that describe your work experience related to the job:
	+ Option 1: Relevant experience
	+ Option 2: Limited experience
	+ Option 3: No experience

### Page 3: Job Applicant
* **Job Applicant (id: 3)**: Select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: New Hire
	+ Option 2: Internal Promotion
	+ Option 3: External Hire

### Page 4: Contact Information
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (id: 5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job Role (id: 6)**: Enter your current job role or position.
* **Job Location (id: 7)**: Enter the location of your current job.
* **Job Education (id: 8)**: Enter your educational background and relevant courses taken.
* **Job Skills (id: 9)**: Enter your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Job Certifications (id: 10)**: Enter any relevant certifications or licenses held.
* **Job References (id: 11)**: Enter names and contact details of professional references.
* **Job Application Comments (id: 12)**: Provide any additional comments or explanations related to your application.

### Page 5: Job Application Details
* **Job Salary (id: 13)**: Enter your expected salary or wage.
* **Job Availability (id: 14)**: Enter your availability to start work.
* **Job Start Date (id: 15)**: Enter the date you're available to start work.
* **Job End Date (id: 16)**: Enter the date you're expected to end in your current position.
* **Job Status (id: 17)**: Select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Available
	+ Option 2: Not Available
	+ Option 3: Other
* **Job Status Comments (id: 18)**: Provide any additional comments or explanations related to your job status.
* **Job Interviewer Notes (id: 19)**: Enter any notes or feedback from the interviewer.
* **Job Rejection Reason (id: 20)**: If your application was rejected, select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Incomplete Application
	+ Option 2: Not a good fit
	+ Option 3: Other
