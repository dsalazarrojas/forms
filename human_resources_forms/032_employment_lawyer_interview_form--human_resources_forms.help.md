# employment_lawyer_interview_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information from candidates during an employment lawyer interview. It's essential to provide honest and accurate answers to all the questions to ensure a thorough evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your candidate information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide a detailed account of your work history, including job titles, durations, and responsibilities.
3. Describe your education background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
4. List your job skills, including technical skills and soft skills.
5. Share your salary history, including previous salaries and any notable salary milestones.
6. Provide information about your employment lawyer experience, including any relevant certifications or licenses.
7. Answer any additional inquiries as per the employment lawyer's request.
8. List your references, including names, job titles, and contact information.
9. Mention any certifications or special skills you possess.
10. Specify the languages you are proficient in.
11. Provide links to your social media profiles (if applicable).
12. Indicate your current work status (employed, unemployed, etc.).
13. Specify your job status (full-time, part-time, etc.).
14. Indicate your employment status (employed, self-employed, etc.).
15. Specify the employment type (full-time, part-time, etc.).
16. Provide information about your employment lawyer's role and any other relevant details.
17. Add any notes or comments from the employment lawyer.
18. Finally, provide the date and time of the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`, `text`, **required: false**): This section is to capture the candidate's basic information, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant information. Please fill in your details accurately and honestly.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide your contact information, including email, phone numbers, and social media profiles.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe your work experience, including job titles, durations, and responsibilities.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, **required: false**): Share your work history, including job titles, durations, and responsibilities.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, `text`, **required: false**): List your technical and soft skills, including programming languages, tools, and software proficiency.
* **Salary History** (`salary_history`, `text`, **required: false**): Share your previous salary history, including salary ranges and any notable salary milestones.
* **Employment Lawyer Information** (`employment_lawyer_information`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide information about the employment lawyer's role and any other relevant details.
* **Additional Inquiries** (`additional_inquiries`, `text`, **required: false**): Answer any additional questions or inquires from the employment lawyer.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, **required: false**): List your references, including names, job titles, and contact information.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, **required: false**): Mention any certifications or special skills you possess.
* **Work Certifications** (`work_certifications`, `text`, **required: false**): Share any work-related certifications or special skills.
* **Languages** (`languages`, `text`, **required: false**): Specify the languages you are proficient in.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide links to your social media profiles (if applicable).
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, `text`, **required: false**): Indicate your current work status (employed, unemployed, etc.).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `text`, **required: false**): Specify your job status (full-time, part-time, etc.).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `text`, **required: false**): Indicate your employment status (employed, self-employed, etc.).
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `text`, **required: false**): Specify the employment type (full-time, part-time, etc.).
* **Employment Lawyer Info** (`employment_lawyer_info`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide information about the employment lawyer's role and any other relevant details.
* **Employment Lawyer Notes** (`employment_lawyer_notes`, `text`, **required: false**): Add any notes or comments from the employment lawyer.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, `date`, **required: false**): Specify the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, `time`, **required: false**): Specify the time of the interview.
* **Interviewers** (`interviewers`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): Indicate if you will be interviewed by multiple people.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, **required: false**): Indicate if you submitted by yourself or another person.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, **required: false**): Specify the date you submitted this form.

Note: This guide assumes the form is completed by a human. If a field is marked as **required: true**, the corresponding field should be filled in accurately. If a field is **required: false**, it is optional but still essential for providing the best possible information about the candidate.
