# onboarding_signature_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from new hires as part of the onboarding process. It helps HR teams gather necessary details for employee onboarding, including job titles, hire dates, and policy acknowledgments. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct policy acknowledgments for your new hire.
3. Provide the correct date of hire for your new hire.
4. Enter your job title.
5. Enter your manager's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Details**: 
	+ Enter a brief description of the new hire's role and responsibilities.
* **Policy Acknowledgments**: 
	+ Select one or more policies that apply to your new hire. This is an important step in ensuring compliance and understanding company policies.
* **Date of Hire**: 
	+ Enter the date the new hire started working for the company. This helps with tracking employee start dates and benefits eligibility.
* **Job Title**: 
	+ Enter your job title as it appears in the company's HR system.
* **Manager's Name**: 
	+ Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure proper onboarding documentation.
* Double-check policy acknowledgments to ensure you've selected the correct policies.
* Enter the correct date of hire to avoid delays in benefits and other HR processes.
