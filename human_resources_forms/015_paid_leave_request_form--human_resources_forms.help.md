# Paid Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request paid leave from the company for various reasons such as vacation, sick leave, bereavement, or other personal reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Vacation, Sick Leave, Bereavement, etc.)
2. Enter the start and end dates of your leave
3. Enter the number of days you plan to take off
4. Provide a brief explanation of the purpose of your leave
5. If you are requesting leave for an emergency reason, provide your contact phone number and email
6. If you are an approver, review and provide comments and signature as required

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leave Type**: Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Vacation, Sick Leave, Bereavement, etc.)
  * This field is used to indicate the reason for your leave request.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your leave
  * This field is used to indicate the date from which you will begin your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your leave
  * This field is used to indicate the date on which you will end your leave.
* **Number of Days Off**: Enter the number of days you plan to take off
  * This field is used to indicate the total number of days you plan to take off during your leave.
* **Purpose of Leave**: Provide a brief explanation of the purpose of your leave
  * This field is used to provide additional context about your leave request.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (if requesting emergency leave)
  * This field is used to provide a way for the approver to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address (if requesting emergency leave)
  * This field is used to provide a way for the approver to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Submitter's Info**: This field is intended for you to provide any additional information about your request
  * This field is used to provide any additional context or details about your leave request.
* **Approver's Name**: This field is for the approver to enter their name
  * This field is used to indicate who has reviewed and approved your request.
* **Approver's Signature**: This field is for the approver to enter their signature
  * This field is used to indicate that the approver has reviewed and approved your request.
* **Approver's Comment**: This field is for the approver to provide comments on your request
  * This field is used to provide additional feedback or comments on your leave request.
* **Approver's Sign Date**: This field is for the approver to enter the date they approved your request
  * This field is used to indicate when the approver reviewed and approved your request.
* **Approver's Sign Date/Time**: This field is for the approver to enter the date and time they approved your request
  * This field is used to provide a record of when the approver reviewed and approved your request.
* **Approver's Info**: This field is for the approver to enter any additional information about their approval
  * This field is used to provide any additional context or details about their approval.
* **Approver's Signature Image**: This field is intended for the approver to upload a digital image of their signature
  * This field is used to provide a visual record of the approver's signature.
