# job_description_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for job description approvals. It includes details such as job status, approval date, approved personnel, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out this form, begin by selecting the job status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- Enter the title and department of the job.
- Select the person(s) who approved the job.
- Enter the date of approval.
- Optionally, add any additional notes or comments.
- Enter the email address of the person who approved the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title**: (`job_title`, `text`, required) Enter the title of the job. This is an important field that helps identify the role within the organization.
* **Department**: (`department`, `text`, required) Enter the department where this job belongs.
* **Job Status**: (`job_status`, `select_one`, required) Choose Active, Inactive, or Pending for the current status of the job.
* **Approval Date**: (`approval_date`, `date`, required) Enter the date when the job was approved.
* **Approved By**: (`approved_by`, `text`, required) Enter the name of the person who approved the job.
* **Notes**: (`notes`, `note`, optional) Add any additional comments or notes about the job.
* **Approver**: (`approver`, `select_multiple`, optional) Select the person(s) who approved the job. You can choose one or multiple approvers.
* **Approver Email**: (`approver_email`, `email`, optional) Enter the email address of the person(s) who approved the job.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct job status to accurately represent the current state of the job.
- Enter the correct department to associate the job with the correct team or unit.
- Be specific when entering the approval date to ensure accurate tracking of the approval process.
- Use the "Approver" field to capture the name(s) of the person who approved the job, if applicable.
- Use the "Approver Email" field to capture the email address of the person who approved the job, if applicable.
- Use the "Notes" field for any additional context or information about the job that doesn't fit into other fields.
