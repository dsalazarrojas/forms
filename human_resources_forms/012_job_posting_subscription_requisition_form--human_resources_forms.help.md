# Job Posting Subscription Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a job posting subscription requisition to the management team for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (marked with an asterisk * below) and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for job category, job type, job level, and job status.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the job posting.
4. Enter any additional job notes or comments.
5. Upload any relevant job attachments.
6. Select the user who will be posting the job.
7. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Posting Subscription Requisition Form** (`job_posting_subscription_requisition_form`): This is the main title of the form.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`): Select the category of the job posting.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the title of the job posting.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`): Select the type of job posting (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Intern).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`): Select the level of the job posting (e.g. Entry-level, Mid-level, Senior-level).
* **Job Family** (`job_family`): Enter the family of the job posting.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`): Select the status of the job posting (e.g. New, Renewal, Extension).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`): Enter the start date of the job posting.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`): Enter the end date of the job posting.
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`): Enter the number of hours worked per week.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`): Enter a detailed description of the job posting.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`): Enter the location of the job posting.
* **Job Contact** (`job_contact`): Enter the contact information for the job posting.
* **Job Email** (`job_email`): Enter the email address for the job posting.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the job posting.
* **Job Attachments** (`job_attachments`): Upload any relevant attachments for the job posting.
* **Job Comments** (`job_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the job posting.
* **Job Requisition Date** (`job_requisition_date`): Enter the date the job requisition was submitted.
* **Job Posting User** (`job_posting_user`): Select the user who posted the job requisition.
* **Job Reviewed By** (`job_reviewed_by`): Select the user who reviewed the job requisition.
* **Job Approved By** (`job_approved_by`): Select the user who approved the job requisition.
* **Job Rejected By** (`job_rejected_by`): Select the user who rejected the job requisition.
* **Job Status History** (`job_status_history`): Enter any historical status changes for the job posting.
* **Job Status Notes** (`job_status_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments on job status changes.
* **Job Status Notes History** (`job_status_notes_history`): Enter any historical notes or comments on job status changes.
