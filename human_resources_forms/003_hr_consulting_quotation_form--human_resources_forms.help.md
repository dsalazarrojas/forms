# HR Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Consulting Quotation Form is a tool used to collect relevant information from clients to create an accurate quotation for HR consulting services. Please use this form to gather the required details to ensure a smooth consulting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the field "Client Name."
2. Select the type of service you require from the "Service type" dropdown menu. Currently, the available options are "HR Consulting" and "Recruitment."
3. Provide a detailed description of the services you need in the field "Service Description."
4. Enter the date of the consultation in the "Quotation Date" field and the time of the consultation in the "Quotation Time" field.
5. Enter the total price for the consultation services in the "Quotation Price" field.
6. Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted) and phone number in the fields "Email" and "Phone."
7. If applicable, enter any additional information in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name
• **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the client's name to help us understand who the quotation is for.

### Service Type
• **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose between "HR Consulting" and "Recruitment" to specify the type of service you need.

### Service Description
• **Service Description** (`service_description`, `text`, **required**): A detailed description of the services you require will help us provide an accurate quotation.

### Quotation Date
• **Quotation Date** (`quotation_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the consultation. Please use the format dd/mm/yyyy.

### Quotation Time
• **Quotation Time** (`quotation_time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time of the consultation. This will help us ensure that we can schedule the service accurately.

### Quotation Price
• **Quotation Price** (`quotation_price`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total price for the services you require.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, `email`, **optional**): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted regarding the quotation or future services.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information or to schedule the consultation.

### Address
• **Address** (`address`, `text`, **optional**): If you wish to provide your address for future contact or meetings, enter it here.

### Message
• **Message** (`message`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any additional information or special requests, enter it here.
