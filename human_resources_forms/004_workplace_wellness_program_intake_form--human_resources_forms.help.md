# Workplace Wellbeing Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from employees who are joining or have joined the workplace wellness program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your employee name, job title, department, and contact information.
2. Provide your manager's contact information, including their name and contact details.
3. Enter your date of birth and job start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your current job title as it is displayed on your job description or your business cards.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Provide your department name, as mentioned in your job description or your business cards.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's name who you report to.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Fill in your personal contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your work email address.
* **Manager Contact Phone** (`manager_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Fill in your manager's contact phone number.
* **Manager Contact Email** (`manager_contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your manager's work email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Provide your date of birth in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter your job start date in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have filled in all the required and optional fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your input for any errors before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your HR representative.
