# Independent Contractor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Independent Contractor Information Form is designed to collect essential details about your independent contractor engagement with the organization. This form is used to track and manage your contract, payment, and tax information.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete each page of the form according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Info** (Contract Info, text, required): Enter a brief description of the contract.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required): Provide your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Tax Id** (Tax Id, text, required): Enter your Tax ID number (EIN or SSN).
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, text, required): Describe the payment terms and conditions.
* **Contract Start Date** (Contract Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of the contract in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Contract End Date** (Contract End Date, date, required): Enter the end date of the contract in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Payment Rate** (Payment Rate, select_one, optional): Choose your payment rate type from the options provided (Fixed Rate, Hourly Rate, Daily Rate).
* **Payment Schedule** (Payment Schedule, select_one, optional): Select your payment frequency from the options provided (Net 15, Net 30).
* **Work Schedule** (Work Schedule, select_one, optional): Choose your work schedule from the options provided (Full Time, Part Time).
* **Work Hours** (Work Hours, number, optional): Enter the number of hours worked.
* **Work Start Time** (Work Start Time, time, optional): Enter the start time of work in 12:00 AM/PM format.
* **Work End Time** (Work End Time, time, optional): Enter the end time of work in 12:00 AM/PM format.
* **Work Days** (Work Days, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you work from the provided list (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Payment Terms Note** (Payment Terms Note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations about the payment terms.
* **Payment Frequency** (Payment Frequency, select_one, optional): Choose your payment frequency from the options provided (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Semi-Monthly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, optional): Select your payment method from the options provided (Check, Bank Transfer, PayPal).
* **Tax Id Type** (Tax Id Type, select_one, optional): Choose your Tax ID type from the options provided (EIN, SSN).
* **Contract Status** (Contract Status, select_one, optional): Select your contract status from the options provided (Active, Inactive).
* **Payment Status** (Payment Status, select_one, optional): Select your payment status from the options provided (Paid, Unpaid, Pending).
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations about your contract or payment.
* **Tax Address** (Tax Address, text, optional): Enter your tax address.
* **Tax Name** (Tax Name, text, optional): Enter your tax name.
* **Tax EIN** (Tax EIN, text, optional): Enter your Tax EIN number.
* **Tax SSN** (Tax SSN, text, optional): Enter your Tax SSN number.
