# Employee Benefits Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Benefits Program Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from employees on their current benefits and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible, based on your personal experiences with the employee benefits program.
2. Select the benefits that are currently offered by the organization.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the benefits on a scale of 1-10.
4. Provide feedback and suggestions for improvements.
5. Select the category that best describes your response.
6. Enter your name and email address (if you wish to be contacted for further clarification).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Benefits Program Evaluation Form** (`employee_benefits_program_evaluation_form`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, please enter your name if you want to be identified.
* **Benefits Offered** (`benefits_offered`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all benefits that are currently offered by the organization.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the benefits on a scale of 1-10.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, required: false): Provide feedback and suggestions for improvements.
* **Benefits Improvement Plan** (`benefits_improvement_plan`, text, required: false): Describe your suggestions for improvements to the benefits program.
* **Category** (`employee_benefits_program_evaluation_form_category`, select_one, required: true): Select the category that best describes your response.
* **Description** (`employee_benefits_program_evaluation_form_description`, note, required: false): Please enter a brief description of your response.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for further clarification).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted for further clarification).
* **Assigned Tool** (`employee_benefits_program_evaluation_form_assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the tool assigned to complete this form.
* **Output File** (`employee_benefits_program_evaluation_form_output_file`, text, required: false): Enter the output file name.
* **Form IDs** (`employee_benefits_program_evaluation_form_form_ids`, text, required: false): Enter the IDs of this form.
