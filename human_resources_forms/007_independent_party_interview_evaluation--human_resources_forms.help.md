# Independent Party Interview Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Independent Party Interview Evaluation form is used to assess the performance of an independent party in an interview, gathering information on the candidate's skills, experience, and overall fit for a role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have reviewed the candidate's performance during the interview.
2. Fill in the candidate's information, including their rating and comments.
3. Select the candidate's status and form completion status.
4. Choose the completed by field accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the candidate's details, such as name and job title.
* **Candidate Rating** (`candidate_rating`, number, optional)
	+ Rate the candidate's performance out of some unspecified scale, likely 1-10 or 1-5.
	+ Note: A rating system may be used to evaluate the candidate's performance.
* **Candidate Interview Date** (`candidate_interview_date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the location of the interview.
* **Candidate Interviewer** (`candidate_interviewer`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of the person who conducted the interview.
* **Candidate Recommendation** (`candidate_recommendation`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select the candidate's recommendation (First Choice, Second Choice, Third Choice).
	+ Note: This field indicates the order of preference for the candidate.
* **Candidate Comments** (`candidate_comments`, note, optional)
	+ Enter any additional comments about the candidate.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Candidate Phone** (`candidate_phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Recruiter Comments** (`recruiter_comments`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any additional comments from the recruiter.
* **Candidate Status** (`candidate_status`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the candidate's status (Status 1, Status 2, Status 3).
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, select_one, required)
	+ Select whether the form is completed or incomplete.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, select_one, required)
	+ Select whether the candidate's performance was completed by you or not. 

## Tips
- Ensure you have thoroughly reviewed the candidate's performance before completing this form.
- Enter accurate and truthful information in all fields.
- Review and update this form regularly to ensure accurate candidate evaluations.
