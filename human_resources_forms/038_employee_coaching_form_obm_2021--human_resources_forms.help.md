# Employee Coaching Form OBM 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Coaching Form OBM 2021 is designed to be a tool for managers or HR staff to collect relevant information from employees to facilitate effective coaching.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start with **Employee Data** (page 1). You will need to provide your name, and optionally you can add any additional information that you would like to share with your coach.
2.  Select the relevant **Contact Info** (page 2) to ensure that your coach can reach you.
3.  Choose the **Coaching Topics** (page 3) that you would like to focus on during the coaching sessions.
4.  Provide the **Coaching Notes** (page 4) that will help your coach understand your goals and challenges.
5.  Select the **Action Items** (page 5) that you commit to complete.
6.  Describe the **Item Details** (page 6) for each action item.
7.  Choose the **Assigned Tool** (page 7) that you will use to complete your action items.
8.  Select the **Coaching Status** (page 8) of your action items.
9.  Provide the **Next Steps** (page 9) for your coach to guide you further.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Employee Data** (Page 1)
*   **Employee Data**: This is a field for you to provide any relevant information about yourself.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false

### **Contact Info** (Page 2)
*   **Contact Info**: Please provide your contact information so that your coach can reach you.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: true

### **Coaching Topics** (Page 3)
*   **Coaching Topics**: Select all the topics you would like to focus on during the coaching sessions.
    *   Type: select_multiple
    *   Required: true

### **Coaching Notes** (Page 4)
*   **Coaching Notes**: Write down any notes or information that your coach should know about you.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false

### **Action Items** (Page 5)
*   **Action Items**: Choose all the action items you commit to complete.
    *   Type: select_one
    *   Required: true

### **Item Details** (Page 6)
*   **Item Details**: Describe the details of each action item you selected.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false

### **Follow-up** (Page 7)
*   **Follow-up**: This field is not currently used in this form.
    *   Type: date
    *   Required: false

### **Assigned Tool** (Page 8)
*   **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool that you will use to complete your action items.
    *   Type: select_one
    *   Required: true

### **Coaching Status** (Page 9)
*   **Coaching Status**: Indicate the status of your action items.
    *   Type: select_one
    *   Required: true

### **Next Steps** (Page 10)
*   **Next Steps**: Provide any further actions or next steps you need your coach to consider for you.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false

## Tips
Remember to provide as much detail as possible and be honest with yourself and your coach. This will help ensure that you get the most out of the coaching process.
