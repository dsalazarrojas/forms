# employee_wellness_leave_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The employee_wellness_leave_form is a form used to request time off, such as vacation, sick leave, or other personal leave, for an employee. This form helps to keep track of employee leave requests and ensures that the necessary information is collected for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee ID number.
2. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
3. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Personal, Family, or Company).
4. Provide a brief reason for your leave.
5. Select your department and job title (if applicable).
6. Enter your team lead's name and contact information (if applicable).
7. Enter your manager's name and contact information (if applicable).
8. Add any additional comments or notes.
9. Enter your email address and phone number.
10. Select the status of your leave (Pending, Approved, or Deny).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id** (Number): Enter your employee ID number.
* **leave_start_date** (Date): Select the start date of your leave.
* **leave_end_date** (Date): Select the end date of your leave.
* **reason_for_leave** (Text): Briefly explain the reason for your leave.
* **employee_department** (Select One): Choose your department from the list (Finance, Marketing, Sales, IT).
* **employee_job_title** (Select Multiple): Select your job title(s) from the list (Manager, Assistant Manager, Team Lead).
* **team_lead_name** (Text): Enter the name of your team lead.
* **manager_name** (Text): Enter the name of your manager.
* **team_name** (Text): Enter the name of your team.
* **leave_type** (Select One): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Personal, Family, Company).
* **comment** (Text): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **employee_email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **employee_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **employee_extension** (Text): Enter your extension number.
* **manager_email** (Email): Enter your manager's email address.
* **manager_phone** (Text): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **team_manager_email** (Email): Enter your team manager's email address.
* **team_manager_phone** (Text): Enter your team manager's phone number.
* **employee_notes** (Text): Add any additional notes from you.
* **manager_notes** (Text): Add any additional notes from your manager.
* **team_notes** (Text): Add any additional notes from your team.
* **date_requested** (Date): Enter the date you requested the leave.
* **date_approved** (Date): Enter the date your leave was approved.
* **date_returned** (Date): Enter the date you returned from leave.
* **status** (Select One): Select the status of your leave (Pending, Approved, Deny).
