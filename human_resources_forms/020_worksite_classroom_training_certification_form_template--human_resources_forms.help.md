# Worksites Classroom Training Certification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record information about the completion of a worksite classroom training certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title, briefly describing the training session.
2. Select the category of the training session.
3. Provide a detailed description of the training session.
4. Enter the name of the assessor who conducted the training session.
5. Enter the location of the training session.
6. Enter the date the training session was conducted.
7. Enter the time the training session started.
8. Record the trainee's signature to indicate completion.
9. Record the assessor's signature to indicate completion and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Worksite Classrooms Training Certification Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the training session.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more categories for the training session.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the training session.
* **Assessor Name** (`form_assessor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the assessor who conducted the training session.
* **Location** (`form_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the training session was conducted.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the training session was conducted.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the training session.
* **Trainee Signed** (`form_trainee_signed`, note, required: false): Record the trainee's signature to indicate completion.
* **Assessor Signature** (`form_assessor_signature`, note, required: false): Record the assessor's signature to indicate completion and accuracy.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- Use the correct category for the training session.
- Keep the description concise and clear for easy reference.
- Record both the trainee's and assessor's signatures for completion verification.
