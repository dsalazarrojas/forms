# HR Compliance Workshop Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Compliance Workshop Absence Form is used to record and track attendance and reasons for absence from a HR Compliance Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields:
	- **Workshop Date**: This is the date the workshop was scheduled to take place.
	- **Start Time**: This is the start time of the workshop.
	- **End Time**: This is the end time of the workshop.
	- **Absence Reason**: Provide a brief reason for being absent.
	- **Attendance Status**: Select whether you were Absent, Present, or Late.
	- **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
	- **Department**: Select the department you belong to.
	- **Email**: Enter your email address.
	- **Submission Status**: Select whether this submission is True or False (e.g. submitted or not).
2. Fill out optional fields:
	- **Attendance Status Note**: If you have additional comments regarding your attendance status, you can provide them here.
	- **Job Title**: If you are a manager or have a specific job title, you can provide it here.
	- **Phone**: If you have a phone number you'd like to provide, you can do so here.
	- **Reviewer**: If you have a reviewer assigned to you, you can provide their information here.
	- **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of your reviewer.
	- **Reviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of your reviewer.
	- **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email address of your reviewer.
	- **Approver**: If you have an approver assigned to you, you can provide their information here.
	- **Approver Name**: Enter the name of your approver.
	- **Approver Phone**: Enter the phone number of your approver.
	- **Approver Email**: Enter the email address of your approver.
	- **Submitter**: Enter your submitter information if not already provided.
