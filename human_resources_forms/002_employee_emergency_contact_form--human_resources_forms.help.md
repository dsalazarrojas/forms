# Employee Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Emergency Contact Form is used to gather information about an employee's emergency contact details. This form is designed to help us stay in touch with employees' emergency contacts in case of any unexpected situations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the employee's details, including their name and employee ID.
2. Provide contact details for the employee's emergency contact, such as their phone number and email address.
3. Specify the relationship between the employee and their emergency contact (e.g., primary or alternate contact).
4. Select the department and job title of the employee.
5. Set the status of the emergency contact (active or inactive).
6. Add any additional notes about the employee's status and emergency contact.
7. Click the "Submit" button to save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department the employee belongs to (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Customer Support).
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the employee (active or inactive).
* **Employee Status Notes** (`employee_status_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the employee's status.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the emergency contact is primary or alternate.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Status** (`emergency_contact_status`, select_one, optional): Set the status of the emergency contact (active or inactive).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Notes** (`emergency_contact_relationship_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the relationship between the employee and emergency contact.
