# Employee Handbook Compliance Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for employees to submit their time off requests, which are then reviewed and approved by HR.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your first name, last name, and employee ID.
2. Provide the reason for your time off and the dates of absence.
3. Enter the start and end times of your time off.
4. Enter the total hours of your time off.
5. Select the status of your time off (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
6. Enter the submitted by and submitted on dates.
7. If approved, enter the approved by and approved on dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID assigned to you by HR.
* **Reason for Time Off**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for your time off request.
* **Date of Absence**: Enter the start and end dates of your time off.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your time off in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your time off in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Time Off Hours**: Enter the total hours of your time off.
* **Time Off Status**: Select the status of your time off (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Submitted By**: Enter the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Approved By**: Enter the person approving the request.
* **Approved On**: Enter the date the request was approved.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments for the request.
* **Time Off Hours Approved**: Enter the total hours approved.
* **Time Off Status Approved**: Select the status of the approved time off (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Submitted By Approved**: Enter the person approving the request.
* **Submitted On Approved**: Enter the date the request was approved.
* **Approved By Approved**: Enter the person reapproving the request.
* **Comments Approved**: Add any additional comments for the reapproval.
* **Time Off Hours Approved Approved**: Enter the total hours reapproved.
* **Time Off Status Approved Approved**: Select the status of the reapproved time off (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Submitted By Approved Approved**: Enter the person reapproving the request.
* **Submitted On Approved Approved**: Enter the date the request was reapproved.
* **Approved By Approved Approved**: Enter the person reapproving the request for the second time.
* **Comments Approved Approved**: Add any additional comments for the second reapproval.
* **Time Off Hours Approved Approved Approved**: Enter the total hours reapproved.
* **Time Off Status Approved Approved**: Select the status of the second reapproved time off (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
