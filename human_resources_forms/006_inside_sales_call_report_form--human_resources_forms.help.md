# inside_sales_call_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inside Sales Call Report Form is designed to collect information about daily calls made by inside sales teams to evaluate sales performance and provide management insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form for every call made during the reporting period.
* Answer each field as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Calls** (`page_1`, text, optional): Briefly describe the call(s) made during the reporting period.
* **Contact** (`page_2`, select_multiple, optional): Check if the call involved a "Yes" or "No" contact. 
* **Date** (`page_3`, date, optional): Enter the date the call was made.
* **Time** (`page_4`, time, optional): Enter the time the call was made.
* **Call Duration** (`page_5`, number, optional): Record the duration of the call in minutes.
* **Notes** (`page_6`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the call.
* **Email** (`page_8`, email, optional): Record the email address associated with the call.
* **Phone** (`page_8`, text, optional): Record the phone number associated with the call.
* **Category** (`page_9`, select_one, optional): Check if the call was a "Yes" or "No" category call.
* **Salesperson** (`page_10`, text, optional): Record the name of the salesperson involved in the call.
* **Manager** (`page_11`, text, optional): Record the name of the manager involved in the call.
* **Region** (`page_12`, text, optional): Record the region associated with the call.
* **Product** (`page_13`, text, optional): Record the product associated with the call.
* **Customer** (`page_14`, text, optional): Record the customer involved in the call.
* **Account Manager** (`page_15`, text, optional): Record the account manager involved in the call.
* **Account Executive** (`page_16`, text, optional): Record the account executive involved in the call.
* **Sales Director** (`page_17`, text, optional): Record the sales director involved in the call.
* **Product Line** (`page_18`, text, optional): Record the product line associated with the call.
* **Product Family** (`page_19`, text, optional): Record the product family associated with the call.
* **Product Group** (`page_20`, text, optional): Record the product group associated with the call.
* **Product** (`page_21`, text, optional): (Duplicate of `page_21`, please do not fill)
* **Territory** (`page_22`, text, optional): Record the territory associated with the call.
* **District** (`page_23`, text, optional): Record the district associated with the call.
* **Division** (`page_24`, text, optional): Record the division associated with the call.
* **Region** (`page_25`, text, optional): (Duplicate of `page_25`, please do not fill)

Note: Fields marked as optional are not required but should be filled out as accurately as possible.
