# Hotel Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about a hotel's daily operations, including the start and end times of shifts, total hours worked, and descriptions of situations encountered during the shift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the shift start hour at the top of the page.
2. Enter the shift end hour at the bottom of the page.
3. Type in the total hours worked in the middle of the page.
4. Describe any notable situations that occurred during the shift in the text areas provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shift Start Hour** (`shift_start_hour`, `number`, required): Enter the start time of your shift, using the 24-hour clock.
* **Shift End Hour** (`shift_end_hour`, `number`, required): Enter the end time of your shift, using the 24-hour clock.
* **Total Hours Worked** (`total_hours_worked`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours you worked during the shift.
* **Situations Encountered** (`situations`, `text`, required): Write a brief description of any notable situations that occurred during the shift.
* **Situation 1** (`situation_1`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the first situation that occurred during the shift.
* **Situation 2** (`situation_2`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the second situation that occurred during the shift.
* **Situation 3** (`situation_3`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the third situation that occurred during the shift.
* **Situation 4** (`situation_4`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the fourth situation that occurred during the shift.
* **Situation 5** (`situation_5`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the fifth situation that occurred during the shift.
* **Situation 6** (`situation_6`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the sixth situation that occurred during the shift.
* **Situation 7** (`situation_7`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the seventh situation that occurred during the shift.
* **Situation 8** (`situation_8`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the eighth situation that occurred during the shift.
* **Activity Summary** (`activity_summary`, `text`, required): Write a brief summary of your activities and observations during the shift.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter the correct start and end times for your shift.
* Use the provided text areas to describe each situation in detail.
* Be as thorough as possible when providing descriptions of situations.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, include them in the activity summary.
