# Background Verification Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain authorization from applicants for background verification checks as part of the hiring process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Select all the checks you are authorizing from the "Scope of Verification" field.
3. Answer the "Conviction History" question truthfully.
4. Provide a detailed explanation of any convictions.
5. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Other Names Used** (`other_names`, text, optional): List any other names you have been known by, including maiden names, aliases, or nicknames.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Enter your Social Security number in the XXX-XX-XXXX format.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current full street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your current residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your current residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Drivers License Number** (`drivers_license_number`, text, required): Enter your driver's license or state ID number.
* **Issuing State** (`drivers_license_state`, text, required): Enter the state that issued your driver's license or state ID.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, text, required): Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are applying to.
* **Scope of Verification** (`verification_scope`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of checks you are authorizing the company to conduct.
* **Seven Year Residence History** (`seven_year_history`, text, required): List all addresses where you have lived for the past 7 years.
* **Conviction History** (`conviction_history_question`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have been convicted of a felony or misdemeanor.
* **Explanation of Convictions** (`conviction_explanation`, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of any convictions.
* **Authorization Statement** (`authorization_statement`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you authorize the company to conduct background verification checks.
* **FCRA Disclosure Acknowledgment** (`fcra_disclosure`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have read and understood your FCRA rights.
* **Adverse Action Consent** (`adverse_action_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you understand that adverse action may result from this check.
* **Request Copy of Report** (`report_copy_request`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you want to receive a copy of the report.
* **Authorization Duration** (`authorization_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long the authorization is valid for.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Sign your name digitally or type it out.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date the authorization.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Each applicant must fill out this form separately.
* This form is intended for background verification purposes only.
* The company will use the information provided to conduct background checks and may take adverse action if the information reveals a disqualifying factor.
