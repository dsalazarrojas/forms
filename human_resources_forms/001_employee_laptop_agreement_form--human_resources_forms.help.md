# employee_laptop_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_laptop_agreement_form" is a form used for capturing employee laptop usage information, including details such as employee name, department, laptop model, and agreement date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the employee's department from the list of options in the "Department" field.
3. Enter the laptop model in the "Laptop Model" field.
4. Select the agreement date using the date picker in the "Agreement Date" field.
5. Enter the number of days the laptop will be used in the "Usage Period" field.
6. Enter the employee's email address in the "Employee Email" field.
7. Enter the department manager's email address in the "Department Manager Email" field.
8. Select the department manager's title from the list of options in the "Department Manager Title" field.
9. Select the status of the agreement from the list of options in the "Agreement Status" field.
10. Enter the laptop serial number in the "Laptop Serial Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (user_name)**: Enter the name of the employee who will be using the laptop.
* **Department (department)**: Select the department the employee belongs to from the list of options.
* **Laptop Model (laptop_model)**: Enter the model of the laptop being used.
* **Agreement Date (agreement_date)**: Select the date the agreement was made or is valid from.
* **Usage Period (usage_period)**: Enter the number of days the laptop will be used by the employee.
* **Employee Email (employee_email)**: Enter the email address of the employee who will be using the laptop.
* **Department Manager Email (department_manager_email)**: Enter the email address of the department manager responsible for the employee.
* **Department (department)**: Select the department the employee and manager belong to from the list of options.
* **Employee Title (employee_title)**: Enter the title of the employee who will be using the laptop.
* **Manager Title (manager_title)**: Enter the title of the department manager responsible for the employee.
* **Agreement Status (agreement_status)**: Select the status of the agreement from the list of options.
* **Laptop Serial Number (laptop_serial_number)**: Enter the serial number of the laptop being used.
