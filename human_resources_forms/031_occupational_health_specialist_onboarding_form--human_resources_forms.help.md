# Occupational Health Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Occupational Health Specialist Onboarding Form is used to collect information about new hires and their job details. This form is intended to help the HR department to process new hire data efficiently and accurately. Please ensure that you are filling out this form for the correct purpose and not duplicating efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the New Hire Information page with the following details:
	* Enter the new hire's name and any other relevant information.
2. Move on to the Name page and enter the new hire's name.
3. Proceed to the Email page and enter the new hire's email address.
4. On the Phone page, enter the new hire's phone number.
5. On the Job Title page, enter the new hire's job title.
6. Go to the Hire Date page and select the date of hire using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
7. Move on to the Supervisor Name page and enter the supervisor's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Hire Information
* **New Hire Information** (`new_hire_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the new hire, such as their job description or any special considerations.

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the new hire's name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the new hire's email address in the format example@example.com.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the new hire's phone number.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the new hire's job title.

### Hire Date
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, optional): Select the date of hire using the MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Supervisor Name
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the supervisor's name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays in the onboarding process.
