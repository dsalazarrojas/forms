# Cross Training Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Training Record Form is used to document the details of cross-training sessions, which are essential for employees to gain new skills and knowledge to support their growth and development within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for the "Cross Training Session" field, which indicates whether the employee has participated in a cross-training session or not.
2. Fill out the "Employee Name" field with the name of the employee being cross-trained.
3. Provide the "Training Date" of the cross-training session, which is the date when the session was held.
4. Enter the "Training Session Date" which is the date when the cross-training session took place.
5. Choose the "Assessment Result" field, which is the outcome of the cross-training session.
6. Provide any additional "Assessment Notes" about the session.
7. Fill out the "Trainer Name" field with the name of the person who conducted the session.
8. Enter the "Trainer Phone Number" of the person who conducted the session.
9. Fill out the "Assessor Name" field with the name of the person who assessed the session.
10. Enter the "Assessor Phone Number" of the person who assessed the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cross Training Session** (`cross_training_session`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the employee has participated in a cross-training session or not.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being cross-trained.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Provide the date when the cross-training session was held.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the cross-training session took place.
* **Assessment Result** (`assessment_result`, select_one, required): Choose the outcome of the cross-training session.
* **Assessment Notes** (`assessment_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes about the session.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who conducted the session.
* **Trainer Phone Number** (`trainer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who conducted the session.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who assessed the session.
* **Assessor Phone Number** (`assessor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who assessed the session.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly and provide as much detail as possible in optional fields.
* Before filling out the form, check if you are not already aware of the results of a similar session and if the training session's details are not already recorded elsewhere.
* Double-check the accuracy of the details you enter in the form to ensure that they are correct and up-to-date.
