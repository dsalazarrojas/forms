# emergency_contact_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect emergency contact information for participants in an event, program, or project. The collected data is for the purpose of emergency contact and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can reach your emergency contacts in case of an emergency.
* For Participant's Emergency Contact 1, fill in the name, relationship, phone number, email, and address.
* For Participant's Another Emergency Contact, fill in the name, relationship, phone number, email, and address if it's applicable.
* Ensure that you're providing up-to-date contact information for both emergency contacts.
* Click submit once you've filled in all required fields for both emergency contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant's Emergency Contact 1 (emergency_contact_info_1)** (`Participant's Emergency Contact 1`, text, required)
	+ This field is for the first emergency contact's name. Enter the full name of the person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to Participant 1 (participant_relationship_1)** (`Relationship to Participant 1`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose how the emergency contact is related to the participant (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact 1 Phone (contact_phone_1)** (`Emergency Contact 1 Phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter the phone number of the first emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Email (contact_email_1)** (`Emergency Contact 1 Email`, email, required)
	+ Enter the email address of the first emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Address (contact_address_1)** (`Emergency Contact 1 Address`, text, required)
	+ Enter the address of the first emergency contact.
* **Another Emergency Contact (emergency_contact_info_2)** (Another Emergency Contact, text, optional)
	+ Fill in the name of the second emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Relationship to Participant 2 (participant_relationship_2)** (`Relationship to Participant 2`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose how the second emergency contact is related to the participant (e.g., Mother, Father, Grandparent, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone (contact_phone_2)** (`Emergency Contact 2 Phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Email (contact_email_2)** (`Emergency Contact 2 Email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter the email address of the second emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Address (contact_address_2)** (`Emergency Contact 2 Address`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the address of the second emergency contact, if applicable.
