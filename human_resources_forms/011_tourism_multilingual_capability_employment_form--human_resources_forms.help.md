# Tourism Multilingual Capability Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a job applicant's personal details, language proficiencies, employment history, and availability for work in the tourism industry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
	* Email: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
	* Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	* Native Language: Select 'Yes' if you are fluent in your native language, 'No' otherwise.
2. Please provide information about your language proficiencies:
	* Language Proficiencies: Select the languages you are proficient in (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
	* Language Proficiency Level: Select the level of your language proficiency (e.g., High, Low).
3. Describe your employment history:
	* Employment History: Enter your employment history in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
	* Employment Status: Select 'Active' if you are currently employed, 'Inactive' if not.
4. Provide details about your job position and work schedule:
	* Job Title: Enter your current or desired job title.
	* Job Description: Describe your job position and responsibilities.
	* Job Location: Select 'Yes' if you are available to work in the location specified, 'No' otherwise.
	* Work Experience: Select 'Yes' if you have work experience in the tourism industry, 'No' otherwise.
5. Confirm your availability:
	* Available to Work: Select a date and time when you are available to start work.
	* Work Schedule: Select your work schedule (e.g., full-time, part-time).
	* Available Days: Select the days you are available to work.
	* Availability: Select a date and time when you are available to start work.
	* Availability 2: Select another date and time when you are available to start work.
	* Availability 3: Select a time when you are available to start work.
6. Submit your application:
	* Submission: Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your application.
