# professional_growth_opportunities_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your job satisfaction, job satisfaction rating, skills needed, and training needs to help in your professional growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please proceed to the next page and fill out the fields according to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Satisfaction**: This field is for you to describe your overall job satisfaction. Please provide a brief answer in the text box below. 
* **Job Satisfaction Rating**: This field is for you to rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Skills Needed**: This field is for you to select the skills you feel are necessary for your professional growth and development. Please choose one or more from the options below.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Training Needs**: This field is for you to describe any training or development needs you have. Please provide a brief answer in the text box below.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- If you have filled out this form previously, please ensure you are not duplicating your responses.
