# Resume Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resume Questionnaire form is a tool used to gather information about your work experience, skills, and career goals for the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts and fill in the relevant fields. You can refer to the field-by-field explanations for more information on each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe your previous work experience. This is a text field where you can type in any relevant work experience you have.

### Job Titles
* **Job Titles** (`job_titles`, select_multiple, optional): List all your previous job titles from the provided options. You can select multiple titles that apply to your work experience.

### Education
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): List all your degrees and institutions attended from the provided options.

### Career Goals
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, optional): Briefly describe your short-term and long-term career goals. This is a text field where you can type in any relevant career objectives.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): List your relevant skills from the provided options. You can select multiple skills that apply to your work experience.

### Work Environment
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of work environment that best describes your preferences. Please select from the provided options.

### Job Locations
* **Job Locations** (`job_locations`, select_multiple, optional): List all your past or current job locations from the provided options.

### Work Dates
* **Work Dates** (`work_dates`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the dates of your previous employment. Please format as `mm/dd/yyyy`.

### Resume Attachments
* **Resume Attachments** (`resume_attachments`, text, optional): You can upload or attach any relevant resume attachments here.

### Resume Comments
* **Resume Comments** (`resume_comments`, note, optional): You can add any comments or notes about your resume here.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your professional phone number.

### Resume Language
* **Resume Language** (`resume_language`, select_multiple, optional): List any languages you are proficient in from the provided options.
