# Employee Pay Rate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Pay Rate Form is used to document and track employee pay rates for employees within the organization. This form is intended to provide a structured format for collecting and maintaining accurate and up-to-date pay rate information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the employee details page and enter the employee's details.
2. Select the pay period (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
3. Select the pay frequency (Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
4. Enter the pay rate and currency.
5. Enter the employee's salary and currency.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes regarding the job title, pay rate, employee salary, pay period, pay frequency, salary currency, pay rate currency, employee salary, pay rate date, pay rate time, or employee pay rate form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (employee_pay_rate_form): Enter the employee's details, such as name and contact information.
* **Job Title** (job_title): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Pay Rate** (pay_rate): Enter the employee's pay rate.
* **Pay Period** (pay_period): Select the pay period (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Pay Frequency** (pay_frequency): Select the pay frequency (Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
* **Salary Currency** (salary_currency): Enter the currency of the employee's salary.
* **Pay Rate Currency** (pay_rate_currency): Enter the currency of the employee's pay rate.
* **Employee Salary** (employee_salary): Enter the employee's salary.
* **Pay Rate Date** (pay_rate_date): Optional field for entering a specific date for the pay rate.
* **Pay Rate Time** (pay_rate_time): Optional field for entering a specific time for the pay rate.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional notes regarding the employee's pay rate or other relevant information.
* **Email** (email): Optional field for entering the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (phone): Optional field for entering the employee's phone number.
* **Job Title Note** (job_title_note): Add any additional notes regarding the job title.
* **Employee Pay Rate Note** (employee_pay_rate_note): Add any additional notes regarding the employee's pay rate.
* **Employee Salary Note** (employee_salary_note): Add any additional notes regarding the employee's salary.
* **Pay Rate Note** (pay_rate_note): Add any additional notes regarding the pay rate.
* **Pay Period Note** (pay_period_note): Add any additional notes regarding the pay period.
* **Pay Frequency Note** (pay_frequency_note): Add any additional notes regarding the pay frequency.
* **Salary Currency Note** (salary_currency_note): Add any additional notes regarding the salary currency.
* **Pay Rate Currency Note** (pay_rate_currency_note): Add any additional notes regarding the pay rate currency.
* **Employee Salary Note** (employee_salary_note): Add any additional notes regarding the employee's salary.
* **Pay Rate Date Note** (pay_rate_date_note): Add any additional notes regarding the pay rate date.
* **Pay Rate Time Note** (pay_rate_time_note): Add any additional notes regarding the pay rate time.
* **Employee Pay Rate Form Note** (employee_pay_rate_form_note): Add any additional notes regarding the employee pay rate form.
