# Leave of Absence Request for Estate Affairs - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a leave of absence to manage their estate affairs, such as dealing with a death in the family, a change in marital status, or other significant life events.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you're only submitting a single request for estate affairs per form instance.
* Click on the "Employee Details" page.
* Fill in the required information, including your name, job title, and other relevant details.
* Click on the "Estate Details" page.
* Provide a brief description of the estate affair you're dealing with, such as a death in the family or a change in marital status.
* Click on the "Absence Details" page.
* Enter the dates of your requested absence, including the start and end dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details
**Name** (`employee_details`, text, required): This is where you'll enter your name as it appears on your official ID or in HR records.
*   This field is mandatory.
### Estate Details
**Description** (`estate_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the estate affair you're dealing with. This could be due to a death in the family, a change in marital status, or another significant life event.
*   This field is optional.
### Absence Details
**Start Date** (`absence_details`, date, required) and **End Date** (`absence_details`, date, required): Enter the dates for your requested absence, including the start and end dates.
*   These fields are mandatory.
*   Please ensure the start date is the earliest possible date and the end date is the latest possible date.
*   If the start date is in the past, please ensure you've already completed any necessary steps and submitted this form as soon as possible.

## Tips
*   Make sure to submit this form only once for each request for estate affairs.
*   Be as concise and clear as possible in the fields provided.
*   If you're unsure about any part of the process, contact your HR representative for guidance.
