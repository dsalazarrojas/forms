# Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Evaluation Form is designed to assess the progress, achievements, and areas for improvement of an individual or team. This form is used to provide a systematic and structured evaluation process for HR or management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in the title of the form, which is the same as the form's name.
2.  Proceed to the first section, where you will select the relevant options for each question using the dropdown menus.
3.  For each section, choose the option that best fits the individual's or team's performance.
4.  Fill in any additional information in the text fields as needed.
5.  Finally, review the form and make sure to submit it once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Performance Evaluation Form
*   **Title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is the same as the form's name. Please fill it in accurately.
*   **section_1** (`performance_evaluation_form_section1`, `select_one`, required/true): This section asks you to choose one of the options provided. Select the option that best matches the individual's or team's performance during this period.
*   **section_2** (`performance_evaluation_form_section2`, `select_multiple`, optional/false): Similar to section 1, this section also asks for a selection of options. You may choose multiple options if needed.
*   **section_3** (`performance_evaluation_form_section3`, `date`, optional/false): This section is for providing a specific date related to the individual's or team's performance.
*   **section_4** (`performance_evaluation_form_section4`, `time`, optional/false): This section is for providing a time related to the individual's or team's performance.
*   **section_5** (`performance_evaluation_form_section5`, `select_one`, optional/false): Another selection section, similar to section 1.
*   **section_6** (`performance_evaluation_form_section6`, `select_multiple`, optional/false): Select multiple options for this section if needed.
*   **section_7** (`performance_evaluation_form_section7`, `text`, optional/false): Fill in any additional information in this text field.
*   **section_8** (`performance_evaluation_form_section8`, `note`, optional/false): This section is for leaving any additional notes or comments.
*   **section_9** (`performance_evaluation_form_section9`, `email`, optional/false): This section is not intended for use, as there are no options for email addresses.
*   **section_10** (`performance_evaluation_form_section10`, `text`, optional/false): Fill in any additional information in this text field.

## Tips
*   Be honest and objective when completing the form.
*   Review the form before submitting to ensure accuracy.
*   Do not reuse or copy previous evaluations.
*   Submit the form once for each evaluation period.
*   If you need immediate feedback or support, please reach out to your manager or HR representative.
