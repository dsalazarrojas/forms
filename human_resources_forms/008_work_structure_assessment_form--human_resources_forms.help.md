## Purpose

The work_structure_assessment_form is designed to evaluate the work structure, employee role awareness, and collaboration dynamics within an organization. It's a critical tool for HR personnel to analyze departmental performance and make data-driven decisions about company policies and processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each field carefully, and fill in the required information. Note that some fields are not required, but it's still essential to complete them for a comprehensive assessment.
2.  Ensure you understand the labels and options for each field to complete them accurately.
3.  Review the form multiple times to ensure you've filled in all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Work Structure** (`work_structure`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to input your thoughts about the work structure within your department or team. Please provide a brief description of your current work setup, including the number of people, roles, and responsibilities.
*   **Role Awareness** (`role_awareness`, `text`, required: false): In this field, describe how well you understand your role and responsibilities within the team or department. This is your opportunity to demonstrate your self-awareness and willingness to take on tasks and challenges.
*   **Work Structure Score** (`work_structure_score`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the work structure on a scale of 1 to 5. This will help HR identify areas of improvement and potential hotspots for policy changes.
*   **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from the list of collaboration options that best describe your team's dynamics. Select all that apply:
    -   **Collaboration and Communication**
    -   **Conflict Resolution**
    -   **Problem Solving**
    -   **Teamwork and Trust**
    -   **Other** (if you have something else in mind)
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you have about the work structure and collaboration dynamics within your team or department. This is your chance to voice your opinions and contribute to the overall assessment.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): Provide your employee ID number for HR to track your responses.
*   **Team Size** (`team_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your team's size from the given options:
    -   **1-5**
    -   **6-10**
    -   **11-15**
    -   **16-20**
    -   **21 or more**
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your job type from the following options:
    -   **Management**
    -   **Operations**
    -   **Sales**
    -   **Development**
    -   **Other**
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the department you belong to from the list:
    -   **Human Resources**
    -   **Marketing**
    -   **Sales**
    -   **Development**
    -   **Other**
*   **Job Function** (`job_function`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your primary job function from the options:
    -   **Data Scientist**
    -   **Software Engineer**
    -   **Marketing Manager**
    -   **Sales Manager**
    -   **Other**
*   **Work Structure Score Range** (`work_structure_score_range`, `text`, required: false): This field is currently not used, so do not fill it in.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about the work structure and collaboration dynamics within your team or department.
