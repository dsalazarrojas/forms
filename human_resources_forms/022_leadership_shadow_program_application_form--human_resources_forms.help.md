# Leadership Shadow Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Shadow Program Application Form is a form designed for employees to apply for the Leadership Shadow Program. This program allows staff members to work closely with senior leaders, gain experience, build relationships, and develop leadership skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. If you don't have an email address, you can leave this field blank.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the program interest areas that interest you by checking the relevant boxes in the "Program Interest Area" field.
5. Write a short essay to provide additional context about why you're interested in the Leadership Shadow Program in the "Essay" field. This is optional.
6. Enter your availability date in the "Date" field.
7. Choose a time slot for the program in the "Time" field. This field is optional.
8. Enter the name of the program you're interested in in the "Program Name" field.
9. Provide any additional information about your application in the "Additional Information" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address. If you don't have an email address, you can leave this field blank.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Program Interest Area** (select multiple, required): Select the program interest areas that interest you.
* **Essay** (note, optional): Write a short essay to provide additional context about your interest in the Leadership Shadow Program.
* **Date** (date, required): Choose the date when you'll be available for the program.
* **Time** (time, optional): Choose a time slot for the program. This field is optional.
* **Program Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the program you're interested in.
* **Additional Information** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about your application.
