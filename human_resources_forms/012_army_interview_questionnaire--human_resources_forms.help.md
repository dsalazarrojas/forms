# Army Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Army Interview Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about an individual's personal and professional background, qualifications, and motivations for joining the Army.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use the provided options for dropdown fields, or type your answer in the text fields.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact the Army for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Candidate Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Current Address** (Current Address, text, required): Provide your current address, including your street address, apartment number (if applicable), city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Contact Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during the day and evening.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Citizenship Status** (Citizenship Status, select one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ U.S. citizen
	+ Permanent resident
	+ Other
* **Military Position or Career Field Interested** (Military Position or Career Field Interested, text, required): Enter the specific position or career field within the Army that you are interested in.
* **Why are you interested in this position** (Why are you interested in this position, text, required): Explain why you are interested in this position, including any relevant experience or qualifications.
* **Knowledge about the Army and this position** (Knowledge about the Army and this position, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the Army and this position that you would like to share.
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (Highest Level of Education Completed, select one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ High school diploma
	+ Some college
	+ Associate degree
	+ Bachelor degree
	+ Graduate degree
* **Relevant Skills or Qualifications** (Relevant Skills or Qualifications, text, required): List any relevant skills or qualifications you possess that would make you a strong candidate for this position.
* **Previous Military Experience** (Previous military experience, text, optional): If you have previous military experience, please provide details about your service, including any relevant training or certifications.
* **Technical or Specialized Skills** (Technical or Specialized Skills, text, optional): List any technical or specialized skills you possess that would be valuable in this position.
* **Current Health Status** (Current Health Status, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your service, please provide details.
* **Current Physical Fitness Level** (Current Physical Fitness Level, select one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Willingness to Relocate** (Willingness to relocate, select one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ With conditions
* **Availability to Start** (Availability to Start, date, required): Enter your availability to start in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Any criminal convictions or legal issues** (Any criminal convictions or legal issues, select one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ False
	+ True
* **What motivates you to serve in the Army** (What motivates you to serve in the Army, text, required): Explain what motivates you to serve in the Army, including any personal or professional reasons.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Army for clarification.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
