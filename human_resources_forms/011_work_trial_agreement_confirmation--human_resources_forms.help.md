# work_trial_agreement_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to confirm the details of a work trial agreement between a participant and a recruiter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the participant details section (fields 1-5) and ensure that the participant's information is accurate.
2. Fill in the work trial start and end dates (fields 6-7) according to the agreed-upon terms.
3. In the consent to terms section (field 8), select the option indicating that the participant has read and understood the terms and conditions.
4. Sign and date the agreement in the participant signature section (field 9).
5. Review the recruiter details section (fields 10-15) and ensure that the recruiter's information is accurate.
6. Sign and date the agreement in the recruiter signature section (field 16).
7. Review the employment program details section (fields 17-19) and ensure that the employment program information is accurate.
8. Fill in the trial placement start and end dates (fields 18-19) according to the agreed-upon terms.
9. Sign and date the agreement in the employment program signature section (field 20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (Field 1)**: Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Title (Field 2)**: Enter the participant's job title.
* **Participant Company (Field 3)**: Enter the participant's company name.
* **Participant Email (Field 4)**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone (Field 5)**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address (Field 6)**: Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Notes (Field 7)**: Add any additional notes or comments for the participant.
* **Consent to Terms (Field 8)**: Select "I have read and understand the Terms and Conditions" to confirm understanding.
* **Signatures (Field 9)**: Sign and date the participant's signature.
* **Recruiter Details (Field 10)**: Enter the recruiter's name.
* **Recruiter Title (Field 11)**: Enter the recruiter's job title.
* **Recruiter Company (Field 12)**: Enter the recruiter's company name.
* **Recruiter Email (Field 13)**: Enter the recruiter's email address.
* **Recruiter Phone (Field 14)**: Enter the recruiter's phone number.
* **Recruiter Notes (Field 15)**: Add any additional notes or comments for the recruiter.
* **Employment Program Details (Field 16)**: Enter the employment program name.
* **Trial Placement Start Date (Field 17)**: Enter the agreed-upon trial placement start date.
* **Trial Placement End Date (Field 18)**: Enter the agreed-upon trial placement end date.
* **Consent Signed (Field 19)**: Select "Yes" to confirm that the participant has read and signed the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Signature (Field 20)**: Sign and date the participant's signature.
* **Recruiter Signature (Field 21)**: Sign and date the recruiter's signature.
* **Employment Program Signature (Field 22)**: Sign and date the employment program signature.
* **Participant Email (Field 23)**: Enter or re-enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone (Field 24)**: Enter or re-enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address (Field 25)**: Enter or re-enter the participant's address.

Please note that some fields are not required, but it's essential to fill them out if you have the necessary information. If you're unsure, please contact the relevant parties or the form administrator for guidance.
