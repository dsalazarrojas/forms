# Disaster Recovery Plan Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track employee absences in the context of disaster recovery planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the employee's information, including their Employee ID and name.
* Choose the reason for the absence from the provided options.
* Enter the start and end dates of the absence.
* Specify the number of days and hours the employee was absent.
* Enter the contact information, including phone number and email.
* Provide the location and any comments or notes about the absence.
* Update the employee's status and indicate any emergency contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the employee who is absent.
  * Type: `number`
  * Required
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who is absent.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required
* **Absence Reason**: Choose the reason for the employee's absence from the provided options.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the employee's absence started.
  * Type: `date`
  * Required
* **End Date**: Enter the date the employee's absence ended.
  * Type: `date`
  * Required
* **Days Absent**: Specify the number of days the employee was absent.
  * Type: `number`
  * Required
* **Hours Absent**: Specify the number of hours the employee was absent.
  * Type: `number`
  * Required
* **Contact Number**: Enter the employee's contact phone number.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required
* **Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
  * Type: `email`
  * Required
* **Location**: Enter the location where the employee was absent.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or notes about the absence.
  * Type: `note`
  * Optional
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
  * Type: `text`
  * Optional
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
  * Type: `text`
  * Optional
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
  * Type: `email`
  * Optional
* **Employee Status**: Choose the current status of the employee from the provided options.
  * Type: `select_multiple`
  * Required
* **Disaster Recovery Group**: Enter the name of the disaster recovery group the employee belongs to.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required
* **Disaster Recovery Location**: Enter the location of the disaster recovery group.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required
* **Disaster Recovery Status**: Choose the status of the disaster recovery group from the provided options.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required
* **Disaster Recovery Notes**: Provide any notes or comments about the disaster recovery group.
  * Type: `note`
  * Required
* **Date of Last Status Change**: Enter the date of the last status change.
  * Type: `date`
  * Required
* **Time of Last Status Change**: Enter the time of the last status change.
  * Type: `time`
  * Required
* **Location of Last Status Change**: Enter the location of the last status change.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required
* **Status Update**: Enter a comment or note about the status update.
  * Type: `note`
  * Required
* **Comments of Last Status Change**: Provide any comments or notes about the last status change.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required
* **Last Status Change By**: Enter the name of the person who made the last status change.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required
