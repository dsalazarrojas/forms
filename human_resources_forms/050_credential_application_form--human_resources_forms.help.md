# Credential Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees to apply for credentials, which are used to verify their employment status and work history. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID, which is a unique identifier for each employee.
2. Provide your First Name and Last Name accurately to ensure that we can verify your identity.
3. Enter your Email Address to ensure that we can contact you for any further information or updates.
4. Enter your Date of Birth, which is required for age verification purposes.
5. Describe your work history, including the companies you have worked with and your job roles.
6. Enter your current Job Title and the Department you are working in.
7. Enter your Job Function, which describes your role within the company.
8. If applicable, select your Job Status (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance) and enter your Work Phone number and Work Email.
9. Enter your Work Address, which is your work location.
10. If applicable, enter your Home Phone number and Home Email.
11. Enter your Home Address, which is your residential location.
12. Enter your Social Security Number, which is required for government-issued credentials.
13. If applicable, upload your Resume, which is a supporting document for your application.
14. Enter your Certification Date, which is the date you obtained the relevant certification.
15. Enter your License Expiration Date, which is the date your license expires.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter your unique employee ID to identify yourself.
* **First Name (2)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name (3)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter your official work email address.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Work History (6)**: Describe your previous work experience, including companies you've worked with and job roles.
* **Job Title (7)**: Enter your current job title.
* **Department (8)**: Select your department from the list.
* **Job Function (9)**: Enter a brief description of your role within the company.
* **Job Status (10)**: Select your job status, if applicable (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance).
* **Work Phone (11)**: Enter your work phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Work Email (12)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Work Address (13)**: Enter your work address.
* **Home Phone (14)**: Enter your home phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Home Email (15)**: Enter your home email address.
* **Home Address (16)**: Enter your home address.
* **Social Security Number (17)**: Enter your Social Security Number, as required for government-issued credentials.
* **Resume (18)**: Upload a copy of your resume as supporting documentation.
* **Certification Date (19)**: Enter the date you obtained the relevant certification.
* **License Expiration Date (20)**: Enter the date your license expires.
* **Work Experience (21)**: Describe your current and past work experience in detail.

Note: Fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled out for your application to be processed.
