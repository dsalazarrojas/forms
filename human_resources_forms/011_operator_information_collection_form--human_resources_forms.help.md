# Operator Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect operator information for various purposes, such as record-keeping, reporting, and communication. Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information about the operator to ensure that their status and schedule are properly reflected in our records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the operator's name in the `Operator Name` field.
2. Provide a valid contact email address in the `Contact Email` field.
3. Enter the operator's phone number in the `Operator Phone` field.
4. Describe the operator's job title in the `Operator Job Title` field.
5. Specify the operator's location in the `Operator Location` field.
6. Select whether the operator has a shift or not. If they do, indicate the start and end times of their shift in the `Operator Shift Start Time` and `Operator Shift End Time` fields respectively.
7. If necessary, specify the operator's safety training status in the `Operator Safety Training` field. Select the types of safety training they have received or indicate if they need to take other safety training.
8. Provide the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency in the `Operator Emergency Contact Name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Operator Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the operator's name as it appears on their identification or personnel records.
- **Contact Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the operator's contact email address for communication and record-keeping purposes.
- **Operator Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the operator's phone number for emergency contact and communication.
- **Operator Job Title** (text, required/optional): Describe the operator's job title or position within the organization.
- **Operator Location** (text, required/optional): Specify the location where the operator is based or will be operating.
- **Operator Shift** (select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the operator has a shift, 'No' otherwise.
- **Operator Shift Start Time** (time, required/optional): If the operator has a shift, enter the start time of their shift.
- **Operator Shift End Time** (time, required/optional): If the operator has a shift, enter the end time of their shift.
- **Operator Safety Training** (select_multiple, required/optional): If applicable, select the types of safety training the operator has received or needs to take.
- **Operator Emergency Contact Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
