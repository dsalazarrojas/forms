# Outside Sales Representative Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates applying for Outside Sales Representative positions within our company. The form is used to gather personal details, sales experience, and preferred job characteristics. Ensure you provide accurate and honest information to increase the chances of being considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number to create a profile.
2. Enter your Address in the format: Address 1 (Line 1), Address 2 (Line 2), City, State, and Zip.
3. If you have sales experience, select the relevant job level, job status, job type, and sales region that best fit your situation.
4. Enter your desired salary and experience information as accurately as possible.
5. Choose the most suitable Job Title and Job Duties that describe your current or desired role.
6. Finally, submit the form, ensuring all required and optional fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address 1 (Address 1)**: Enter your current address line 1.
* **Address 2 (Address 2)**: Enter your current address line 2.
* **City (City)**: Enter your city.
* **State (State)**: Enter your state.
* **Zip (Zip)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Work Phone (Work Phone)**: Enter your work phone number, if applicable.
* **Experience Years (Experience Years)**: Enter your years of sales experience.
* **Experience Months (Experience Months)**: Enter the months you have been working in sales (if you've had a non-traditional schedule, estimate the months).
* **Sales Percentage (Sales Percentage)**: Enter your current sales percentage (e.g., 10, 20, 30).
* **Desired Salary (Desired Salary)**: Enter your desired salary range (e.g., $50,000 - $75,000).
* **Job Title (Job Title)**: Select a job title that best describes your current or desired role.
* **Company (Company)**: Enter the name of your current or previous company.
* **Job Duties (Job Duties)**: Describe your current or desired job duties.
* **Sales Experience (Sales Experience)**: Select the level of sales experience you have (e.g., Senior Sales Representative, Sales Manager).
* **Job Level (Job Level)**: Select the level of job you're looking for (e.g., Senior, Entry-Level, Mid-Level).
* **Sales Region (Sales Region)**: Enter your current or desired sales region.
* **Job Function (Job Function)**: Describe your current or desired job function.
* **Sales Territory (Sales Territory)**: Enter your current or desired sales territory.
* **Job Status (Job Status)**: Select the type of job you're looking for (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Job Type (Job Type)**: Select the type of job you're looking for (e.g., Commissioned, Non-commissioned, Commission-based).
* **Submitted By (Submitted By)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the application.
