# HR Disciplinary Panel Hearing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Disciplinary Panel Hearing Request Form is used for requesting a hearing for an employee who may have committed a breach of company policies or procedures. The form helps to document the incident, gather information, and facilitate the hearing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide the employee's name and details of the incident in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the **Incident Date** field.
3. Specify the time of the incident in the **Time of Incident** field (optional).
4. Provide the location of the incident in the **Location** field.
5. Enter the manager's name in the **Manager's Name** field.
6. Explain the **Reason for Hearing**, stating the specific policy or procedure breached.
7. Provide **Witness Details** if relevant (optional).
8. Upload any supporting **Evidence** related to the incident (optional).
9. Select the disciplinary action to be taken from the **Disciplinary Action** dropdown menu.
10. Choose the outcome of the hearing from the **Outcome** dropdown menu (optional).
11. Add any additional **Comments** regarding the incident or hearing process (optional).
12. Enter the date the request was received in the **Date Received** field (optional).
13. Enter the date the disciplinary action was issued in the **Date Issued** field (optional).
14. Indicate if the employee has completed an **Employee Awareness Form** (optional).
15. The employee must sign the form with their **Employee Signature**.
16. The manager must sign the form with their **Manager Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date (Optional)**: Enter the date of the incident, if known.
* **Time of Incident (Optional)**: Enter the time of the incident, if known.
* **Location (Required)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Manager's Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Reason for Hearing (Required)**: Explain the specific policy or procedure breached.
* **Witness Details (Optional)**: Provide any details of witnesses, if relevant.
* **Evidence (Optional)**: Upload any supporting evidence related to the incident.
* **Disciplinary Action (Optional)**: Select the disciplinary action to be taken (e.g., Verbal Warning, Written Warning, etc.).
* **Outcome (Optional)**: Choose the outcome of the hearing (e.g., Approved, Denied, etc.).
* **Comments (Optional)**: Add any additional comments regarding the incident or hearing process.
* **Date Received (Optional)**: Enter the date the request was received.
* **Date Issued (Optional)**: Enter the date the disciplinary action was issued.
* **Employee Awareness Form (Optional)**: Indicate if the employee has completed an Employee Awareness Form.
* **Employee Signature (Required)**: The employee must sign the form to indicate they have read and understand the content.
* **Manager Signature (Required)**: The manager must sign the form to indicate they have reviewed and verified the content.
