# Managerial-Training-Absence-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Managerial-Training-Absence-Form is used to record a manager's absence during a training session. This form is meant to be filled out by the manager who is absent, providing details about their status, reason for absence, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Choose the correct options for each field, taking into consideration the specific circumstances of your absence.
- Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure they are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, required: false)
Enter the title of the training session for which you are reporting your absence.
### 2. **Trainee ID** (`trainee_id`, number, required: false)
Enter the ID of the trainee you are responsible for during the training session.
### 3. **Start Date** (`startDate`, text, required: false)
Enter the start date of the training session.
### 4. **End Date** (`endDate`, text, required: false)
Enter the end date of the training session.
### 5. **Reason** (`Reason`, select_one, required: false)
Select the reason for your absence. Choose from the options provided:
- Yes
- No
### 6. **Manager ID** (`managerId`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select whether or not you are the manager during the training session. Choose from the options:
- Yes
- No
### 7. **Trainee Name** (`traineeName`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the trainee you are responsible for during the training session.
### 8. **Trainee Email** (`traineeEmail`, email, required: false)
Enter the email of the trainee you are responsible for during the training session.
### 9. **Trainee Phone** (`traineePhone`, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the trainee you are responsible for during the training session.
### 10. **Training Location** (`trainingLocation`, text, required: false)
Enter the location of the training session.
### 11. **Training Duration** (`trainingDuration`, number, required: false)
Enter the duration of the training session.
### 12. **Attendance Status** (`attendanceStatus`, select_one, required: false)
Select the attendance status of the trainee. Choose from the options:
- Active
- Inactive
