# Team Culture Lab Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Culture Lab Application Form is a tool used to collect information from team members about their team structure, size, and composition. This form helps us to understand team dynamics and identify areas for improvement in team culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Date of Birth)
2. Select your department and position in the team
3. Choose your interest in various programs (Team Building, Culture Building, Communication, Team Culture, Innovation)
4. Select your availability for meetings (e.g., Monday to Friday, Tuesday to Thursday)
5. Add any additional comments
6. Agree to terms and conditions
7. Specify your team size and project lead's details (if applicable)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Please enter your first name as it is on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Please enter your last name as it is on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required: false`): Please enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `required: false`): Please enter your work phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `required: false`): Please enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, `required: false`): Please select your department from the available options.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, `required: false`): Please select your position in the team from the available options.
* **Program Interest** (`program_interest`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Please select your interest in Team Building, Culture Building, Communication, Team Culture, or Innovation.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, `required: true`): Please select your available days for meetings from the provided options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, `required: false`): Please use this field to add any additional comments or information.
* **Agree to terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Please select 'True' to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Please enter your team name.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `select_multiple`, `required: true`): Please select your team size from the provided options.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Please enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, `required: false`): Please enter your manager's email address.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, `text`, `required: false`): Please select your project lead from the available options.

## Tips

* Please fill in your personal details accurately and truthfully.
* Make sure to select your available days for meetings correctly.
* Agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
