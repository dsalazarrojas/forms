# Data Scientist Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in applying for a data scientist position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details such as first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Attach a link to your resume.
3. Write a cover letter introducing yourself and your qualifications.
4. List your relevant skills, research interests, and research area.
5. Choose your level of education (bachelor, master, or doctorate).
6. Fill in your experience.
7. Fill in your research area of interest.
8. Optionally, you can fill in additional fields such as research contribution and research methodology.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Resume** (`resume_link`, text, optional): Attach a link to your resume.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, optional): Write a cover letter introducing yourself and your qualifications.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): List your relevant skills (e.g., Machine Learning, Data Science, Statistics).
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Fill in your experience.
* **Education** (`education`, select_one, required): Choose your level of education (bachelor, master, or doctorate).
* **Research interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, optional): List your research interests (e.g., Machine Learning, Data Science, Statistics).
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, select_one, required): Choose your research area (e.g., Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Data Science, Statistics).
* **Research Methodology** (`research_methodology`, text, optional): Fill in your research methodology.
* **Research Contribution** (`research_contribution`, text, optional): Fill in your research contribution.
* **GitHub** (`github`, text, optional): Attach a link to your GitHub profile.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, optional): Attach a link to your LinkedIn profile.
* **Twitter** (`twitter`, text, optional): Attach a link to your Twitter profile.
* **Research Paper** (`research_paper`, text, optional): Attach a link to a research paper.
* **Research Paper Link** (`research_paper_link`, text, optional): Attach a link to a research paper.
* **Certification** (`certification`, text, optional): Fill in your certification.
* **Certification Link** (`certification_link`, text, optional): Attach a link to a certification.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional): Attach a link to your portfolio.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Attach a link to your portfolio.
* **GitHub Profile** (`github_profile`, text, optional): Attach a link to your GitHub profile.
* **LinkedIn Profile** (`linkedin_profile`, text, optional): Attach a link to your LinkedIn profile.
* **Research Contribution** (`research_contribution`, text, optional): Fill in your research contribution.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Fill in any additional comments.
