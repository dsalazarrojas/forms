# hr_management_software_subscription_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to manage and track software subscriptions for employees within the HR management software.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access the form.
2. Enter the following information:
   * Company Name
   * Job Title
   * Email
   * Phone Number
   * Subscription Date
   * Subscription End Date
   * Software Version
   * License Agreement
   * Billing Cycle (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually)
   * Payment Method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other)
   * Billing Address
   * Billing City
   * Billing State

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the company associated with the subscription.
* **Job Title** (text, required: false): Enter the title of the employee's job.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter the email address of the employee associated with the subscription.
* **Phone Number** (text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the employee associated with the subscription.
* **Subscription Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date when the software subscription started.
* **Subscription End Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date when the software subscription will end.
* **Software Version** (text, required: false): Enter the version of the software used.
* **License Agreement** (note, required: false): This is a field for any additional notes or comments related to the subscription.
* **Billing Cycle** (select_one, required: false): Select the billing cycle for the subscription (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the method of payment for the subscription (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Billing Address** (text, required: false): Enter the address associated with the billing information.
* **Billing City** (text, required: false): Enter the city associated with the billing information.
* **Billing State** (select_one, required: false): Select the state associated with the billing information.
