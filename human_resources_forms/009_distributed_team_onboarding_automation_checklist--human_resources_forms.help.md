# Distributed Team Onboarding Automation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distributed Team Onboarding Automation Checklist is a form designed to collect information from team leads about their team's setup, which will aid in the onboarding process for automation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field and its purpose.
2. Fill in the fields accurately and thoroughly, taking care to fill in all required fields.
3. Double-check your responses for completeness and accuracy.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Team Lead Details** (`team_lead_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the team lead's details, such as name, role, or any additional information that might be relevant to the onboarding process.
- **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team lead who will be in charge of communication and coordination during onboarding.
- **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the team lead where they can be reached for queries and updates.
- **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, optional): Provide a contact number for the team lead, in case you need to reach out to them directly.
- **Team Lead Setup** (`team_lead_setup`, note, optional): This field is for providing any further setup details about the team lead's setup, which will aid in the onboarding process.

Note: If you're unsure about any field, please contact the onboarding team for assistance.
