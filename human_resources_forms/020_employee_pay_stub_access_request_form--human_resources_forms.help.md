# Employee Pay Stub Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow employees to request access to their electronic pay stubs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the period for which you wish to access your pay stub.
2. Choose your delivery preferences for the pay stub.
3. Select the pay period you wish to access.
4. Enter your name and ID as required by your company's HR department.
5. Optionally, enter your department.
6. If your request status is active, you can leave the supervisor approval field blank.
7. Select the request status you wish to have for your pay stub.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Period Selection** (Period Selection, Select Multiple, Optional): 
  Choose the period for which you wish to access your pay stub. This field will allow you to select one or more periods from a list of available options.
  - If you choose multiple periods, you will be able to access all selected periods.
  - If you choose only one period, you will only be able to access that period.
- **Delivery Preferences** (Delivery Preferences, Select One, Optional): 
  Choose how you wish to receive your pay stub. This field will allow you to select one option from the list of available options.
  - If you choose to receive your pay stub via email, it will be delivered to your email address.
  - If you choose to receive your pay stub in print, it will be delivered to your office.
  - If you choose to not receive your pay stub, it will not be delivered to you.
- **Pay Period** (Pay Period, Select One, Optional): 
  Choose the pay period for which you wish to access your pay stub. This field will allow you to select one option from the list of available options.
  - If you choose an active pay period, you will be able to access your pay stub.
  - If you choose an inactive pay period, you will not be able to access your pay stub.
- **Request Date** (Request Date, Date, Optional): 
  Enter the date on which you are making your request. This field will allow you to enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - This field is optional, but it will help the HR department to track your request.
- **Employee Name** (Employee Name, Text, Optional): 
  Enter your name as it appears on your pay stub. This field will allow you to enter your name in plain text.
  - This field is optional, but it will help the HR department to verify your identity.
- **Employee ID** (Employee ID, Text, Optional): 
  Enter your employee ID as assigned by your company's HR department. This field will allow you to enter your ID in plain text.
  - This field is optional, but it will help the HR department to verify your identity.
- **Department** (Department, Text, Optional): 
  Enter the department in which you work. This field will allow you to enter your department in plain text.
  - This field is optional, but it will help the HR department to track your department.
- **Supervisor Approval** (Supervisor Approval, Date, Optional): 
  If your pay stub is currently inactive, you will be required to enter the date on which you received your supervisor's approval. This field will allow you to enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - If your pay stub is currently active, you can leave this field blank.
- **Request Status** (Request Status, Select One, Optional): 
  Choose the status of your request. This field will allow you to select one option from the list of available options.
  - If you choose active, your pay stub will be made available to you.
  - If you choose inactive, your pay stub will not be made available to you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have checked with your supervisor before filling out this form.
- If you have any issues with your pay stub, please contact the HR department.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can consult with your supervisor or HR department.
