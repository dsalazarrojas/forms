# skills_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skills_inventory_form is used to document an individual's skills, experience, and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct information.
