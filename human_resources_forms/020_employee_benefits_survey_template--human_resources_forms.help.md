# Employee Benefits Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees about their benefits, job details, and contact information. It is intended to be used by HR departments to gather data and track changes in employee benefits and job status.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer each question to the best of your ability
* Ensure that all required fields are completed
* Review your answers for accuracy before submitting

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Benefits** (1): Please select the number of employee benefits you have received.
* **Manager Name** (2): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Title** (3): Enter the title of your manager.
* **Job Title** (4): Enter your current job title.
* **Employee Type** (5): Select the type of employee you are. (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Contract)
* **Benefits Received** (6): Please select the number of benefits you have received.
* **Benefits Satisfied** (7): Select the benefits that have been satisfied. (e.g. Health insurance, Retirement Plan)
* **Comments** (8): Please enter any additional comments or notes about your benefits.
* **Email** (9): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (10): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Start Date** (11): Enter the date you started your job.
* **Job End Date** (12): Enter the date you ended your job. (If you are still employed, enter "Not applicable")
* **Hours Per Week** (13): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Pay Rate** (14): Enter your pay rate.
* **Benefits Employer** (15): Enter the name of your benefits employer.
* **Benefits Plan** (16): Enter the name of your benefits plan.
* **Benefits Plan Employer** (17): Enter the name of the employer of your benefits plan.
* **Benefits Plan Type** (18): Enter the type of benefits plan you have. (e.g. Health, Dental, Vision)
* **Benefits Employer Address** (19): Enter the address of your benefits employer.
* **Manager Name Email** (20): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Name Phone** (21): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Job Title Changes** (22): Enter any changes to your job title.
* **Job Type** (23): Enter your job type. (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Contract)
* **Job Type Changes** (24): Enter any changes to your job type.
* **Job Description** (25): Enter a brief description of your job.

Note: This form is for general use and should not be used for reporting benefits or hours worked. If you need to report benefits or hours worked, please use a different form.
