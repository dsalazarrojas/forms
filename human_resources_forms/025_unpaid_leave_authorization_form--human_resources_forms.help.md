# Unpaid Leave Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request and document an employee's unpaid leave, ensuring that all relevant information is captured and approved by their manager.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your employee information, including your name and any other required details.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your requested leave.
3. Specify the number of hours you are requesting for your leave.
4. Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
5. If your manager has approved your request, they will sign and date their approval.
6. If your request is denied, your manager will provide a reason for the denial and you will need to revisit your request.
7. Once your manager has reviewed and signed your request, you will need to sign and date the form to confirm you have received their approval.
8. Finally, your manager will add any additional comments or information regarding your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): This field is where you enter your name, ensuring that your manager knows who is requesting the leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of your requested leave, using the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Required): Enter the end date of your requested leave, using the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Hours of Leave** (`hours_of_leave`, Number, Required): Specify the number of hours you are requesting for your leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, Text, Required): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request, including the purpose and details of your requested time off.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, Text, Optional): If your manager approves your request, they will sign here to confirm their approval.
* **Date Manager Signed** (`date_manager_signed`, Date, Optional): If your manager approves your request, they will date this field to confirm when they signed the form.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, Text, Optional): Your manager can add any additional comments or information regarding your leave request.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, Text, Optional): Once you have received your manager's approval, you will sign here to confirm you have received and understood their approval.
* **Date Employee Signed** (`date_employee_signed`, Date, Optional): Date and sign this field to confirm you have received and understood your manager's approval.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, Select One, Optional): If your manager has approved or denied your request, select the corresponding status.
* **Reason for Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, Text, Optional): If your request is denied, your manager will provide a reason for the denial.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Your manager can add any additional comments or information regarding your leave request.
