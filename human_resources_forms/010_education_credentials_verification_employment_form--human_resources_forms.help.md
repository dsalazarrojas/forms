# Education Credentials Verification Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to verify the education credentials of a candidate for employment purposes. It is meant to gather information about the candidate's level of education, the institution they attended, and their employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Provide your current and previous employment details if applicable.
3. Ensure that your education details are up-to-date and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Verification**: This is the first step in the form. Please answer this question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
	+ **Level of Education** (`education_level`): This field asks for the level of education you have completed (e.g., High School, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, etc.). Please select the option that best represents your educational background.
	+ **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`): If you have completed any level of education, please provide the date you completed it.
	+ **Institution Name** (`institution_name`): If you attended an educational institution, please provide the name of the institution.
	+ **Institution Location** (`institution_location`): If you attended an educational institution, please provide its location.
	+ **Major Field of Study** (`major_field_of_study`): If you are asked about a major field of study, please provide the relevant information.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Please select the option that best describes your current employment status.
	+ **Employed** (`Employed`): If you are currently working full-time or part-time.
	+ **Unemployed** (`Unemployed`): If you are not employed at the moment.
	+ **Other** (`Other`): If you are in an employment status not listed above.
* **Employment Details**:
	+ **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`): If you are employed, please provide the date you started working.
	+ **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`): If you are no longer employed, please provide the date you stopped working.
	+ **Current Employer** (`current_employer`): If you are employed, please provide the name of your current employer.
	+ **Previous Employer** (`previous_employer`): If you have previously been employed, please provide the name of your previous employer.
* **Education Verification Consent** (`education_verification_consent`): Please read and sign this section to confirm that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips

* Ensure that all the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* This form is designed to support employment verification processes. Do not use it as a substitute for the official employment application process.
