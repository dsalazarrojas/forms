# Employee Earnings Overview - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Earnings Overview form is used to summarize and verify an employee's earnings data, including gross pay, net pay, tax withholding, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form for any required fields (denoted by *) and ensure they are filled out before proceeding.
2. Enter the employee's gross pay information in the Gross Pay field.
3. Enter the employee's net pay information in the Net Pay field.
4. Select the employee's tax withholding status in the Tax Withholding field.
5. Indicate the employee's benefits status in the Benefits field.
6. Enter any relevant employee notes in the Employee Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Main Page
* **Main Page**: This page serves as an introduction to the form and is not a required field.

### Employee Compensation Information
* **Employee Compensation Information** (`compensation_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ This field is not required and is not applicable to this form, as it appears to be a placeholder for a potential future field. However, if the field were populated, it would allow users to select the employee's compensation options (e.g., hourly, salary, commission).

### Gross Pay
* **Gross Pay** (`gross_pay`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Enter the employee's gross pay, which is their total earnings before tax withholding and other deductions.

### Net Pay
* **Net Pay** (`net_pay`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Enter the employee's net pay, which is their total earnings after tax withholding and other deductions.

### Tax Withholding
* **Tax Withholding** (`tax_withholding`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Enter the percentage of tax withheld from the employee's gross pay.

### Benefits
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select whether the employee receives benefits (e.g., health, dental, vision).

### Employee Notes
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, `note`, required: false):
	+ Enter any relevant notes or comments about the employee's earnings data.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your HR or payroll guidelines for clarification.
