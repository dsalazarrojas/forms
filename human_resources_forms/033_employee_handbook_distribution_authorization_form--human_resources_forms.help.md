# employee_handbook_distribution_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize and track the distribution of the employee handbook to employees. It helps to ensure that employees have access to the necessary information and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your employee details: Enter your name, employee ID number, department, job title, and work location.
2.  Confirm your authorization: Check the "Employee Name" field to confirm that you are the authorized personnel.
3.  Provide your manager's details: Enter your manager's name, ID number, title, phone number, email, and address.
4.  Add company details: Enter the company name, phone number, and email address.
5.  Add any manager notes or company notes: Enter any relevant notes or comments that you want to include.
6.  Submit the form: Once you have filled in all the required fields, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Handbook Distribution**: Enter the date you received the employee handbook.
*   **Authorization Date**: Enter the date you authorized the distribution of the employee handbook.
*   **Employee Name**: Check to confirm that you are the authorized personnel.
*   **Department**: Select your department.
*   **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
*   **Location**: Select your work location.
*   **Manager Signature Date**: Enter the date the manager signed the authorization.
*   **Employee Signature**: Enter the date you signed the authorization.
*   **Manager Name**: Check to confirm that you have the manager's name.
*   **Check Employee Name**: Check to confirm that you are the employee.
*   **Manager Title**: Enter the manager's title.
*   **Company Name**: Enter the company name.
*   **Employee ID Number**: Enter your employee ID number.
*   **Manager ID Number**: Enter the manager's ID number.
*   **Company Address**: Enter the company address.
*   **Manager Address**: Enter the manager's address.
*   **Manager Phone**: Enter the manager's phone number.
*   **Manager Email**: Enter the manager's email address.
*   **Company Phone**: Enter the company phone number.
*   **Company Email**: Enter the company email address.
*   **Manager Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the manager.
*   **Company Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the company.
*   **Employee Department**: Select your department.
*   **Employee Job Title**: Enter your job title.
*   **Employee Work Location**: Select your work location.
