# performance-review-tool-requisition-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information and documentation for the performance review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For select_one fields, select the most relevant option.
3. For date fields, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. For time fields, enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
5. For email fields, enter a valid email address.
6. Upload any required attachments, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (<label> Title, <type> text, <required> true): Enter the title of the performance review.
* **First Name** (<label> First Name, <type> text, <required> false): Enter the first name of the person initiating the performance review.
* **Last Name** (<label> Last Name, <type> text, <required> false): Enter the last name of the person initiating the performance review.
* **Department** (<label> Department, <type> select_one, <required> false): Select the department that the person initiating the performance review belongs to.
* **Team** (<label> Team, <type> select_one, <required> false): Select whether the person initiating the performance review is part of a team.
* **Manager ID** (<label> Manager ID, <type> text, <required> true): Enter the ID of the person's manager.
* **Employee ID** (<label> Employee ID, <type> text, <required> false): Enter the ID of the person initiating the performance review.
* **Requisition Reason** (<label> Requisition Reason, <type> text, <required> true): Enter the reason for the performance review.
* **Performance Period** (<label> Performance Period, <type> select_one, <required> false): Select the performance period for which the review is being done.
* **Review Date** (<label> Review Date, <type> date, <required> true): Enter the date of the review.
* **Submitted By** (<label> Submitted By, <type> select_one, <required> false): Select who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date** (<label> Submission Date, <type> date, <required> true): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (<label> Submitted Time, <type> time, <required> false): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By Full Name** (<label> Submitted By Full Name, <type> text, <required> false): Enter the full name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Title** (<label> Submitted By Title, <type> text, <required> false): Enter the title of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Email** (<label> Submitted By Email, <type> email, <required> false): Enter the email of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Phone** (<label> Submitted By Phone, <type> text, <required> false): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Reviewer ID** (<label> Reviewer ID, <type> text, <required> false): Enter the ID of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (<label> Reviewer Title, <type> text, <required> true): Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (<label> Reviewer Email, <type> email, <required> true): Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Remark** (<label> Reviewer Remark, <type> text, <required> true): Enter any remark made by the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Attachment** (<label> Reviewer Attachment, <type> text, <required> false): Upload any attachment made by the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Comment** (<label> Reviewer Comment, <type> text, <required> false): Enter any comment made by the reviewer.
* **Submission Status** (<label> Submission Status, <type> select_one, <required> true): Select the status of the submission.
* **Assigned To ID** (<label> Assigned To ID, <type> text, <required> false): Enter the ID of the person assigned to.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Upload any required attachments, if applicable.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
