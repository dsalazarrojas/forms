# employee_exit_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_exit_interview_form" is a crucial tool for gathering information about departing employees. Its primary function is to collect reasons for leaving, gather feedback and suggestions, and obtain contact details for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company location in the "Company Location" field.
2. Provide a clear and brief description of the reason for your departure in the "Reason for Leaving" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the date you joined the company in the "Date Joined" field.
5. Write down your manager's name in the "Manager's Name" field.
6. Share any comments or suggestions you have in the "Comments" field (optional).
7. If you have any feedback for the company, select the appropriate options in the "Feedback" field.
8. If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Write down the name of the person to contact for further information in the "Contact Name" field.
11. Enter the contact's position in the "Contact Position" field.
12. If you have a contact email, enter it in the "Contact Email" field.
13. Enter the contact's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
14. Finally, provide any additional comments about your departure in the "Comments on Leaving" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Location
* **Company Location** (`Company Location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you are based.
### Reason for Leaving
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of why you are leaving the company.
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
### Date Joined
* **Date Joined** (`date_joined`, date, required): Enter the date you joined the company.
### Manager's Name
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Write down your manager's name.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have.
### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Provide feedback on your experience with the company.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact for further information.
### Contact Position
* **Contact Position** (`contact_position`, text, optional): Enter the contact's position.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact's email address.
### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact's phone number.
### Comments on Leaving
* **Comments on Leaving** (`comments_on_leaving`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your departure.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not applicable.
