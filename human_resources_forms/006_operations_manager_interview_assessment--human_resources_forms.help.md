# Operations Manager Interview Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operations Manager Interview Assessment is a form designed to collect information about a candidate's qualifications and fit for the Operations Manager position. This form is meant to be completed by the candidate after they have passed other screening processes and are being assessed for this specific role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Candidate field with the name of the person being assessed.
2. Enter the Date of the assessment in the Date field.
3. In the Manager field, provide the name and title of the person conducting the assessment.
4. In the Feedback Score field, rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Exceeds Expectations".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate** (`1`, `number`, required: `false`): Fill in the name of the candidate being assessed.
* **Date** (`2`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Manager** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide the name and title of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Feedback Score** (`4`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Exceeds Expectations".

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, ask for clarification from the person conducting the assessment.
- Remember that this form is meant to be a guide, not a comprehensive evaluation. The assessment is meant to provide more in-depth information about the candidate's fit for the role.
