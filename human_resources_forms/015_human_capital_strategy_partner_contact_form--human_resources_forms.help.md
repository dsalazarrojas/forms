# Human Capital Strategy Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Capital Strategy Partner Contact Form is a tool for capturing information from partners who are working with Human Capital Strategy. This form is used to collect relevant details about these partners, including their name, category, description, and other contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form title to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Category** (category, required/optional): Select the relevant category for this form.
* **Description** (text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of this form.
* **Form ID** (number, required/optional): Enter a unique form ID.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the tool assigned to this form.
* **Form File** (text, required/optional): Upload a file related to this form.
* **Output File** (text, required/optional): Upload an output file related to this form.
* **Form Category 2** (text, required/optional): Select the category for this form.
* **Form Category 3** (text, required/optional): Select the category for this form.
* **Form Category 4** (text, required/optional): Select the category for this form.
* **Form Category 5** (text, required/optional): Select the category for this form.
* **Notes** (note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about this form.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the partner's phone number.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the partner's email address.
* **Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date for this form.
* **Time** (time, required/optional): Select the time for this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
- Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and instructions above.
