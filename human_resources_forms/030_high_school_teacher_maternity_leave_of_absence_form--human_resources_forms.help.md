# High School Teacher Maternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather details about a high school teacher's request for maternity leave of absence. It's crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and compliant process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic teacher information on page 1, "Teacher Information".
2. On page 2, "Leave Request", provide a brief description of your leave request.
3. On page 3, "Leave Start Date" and "Leave End Date", specify the dates you wish to be away from work.
4. In "Reason For Leave", provide a clear explanation for your leave request.
5. If applicable, enter your expected return date in "Expected Return Date".
6. If you have a doctor's note, attach it in "Doctor Note".
7. If you have a doctor's note, provide the doctor's name, address, email, phone number, and license number in "Doctor Name", "Doctor Address", "Doctor Email", "Doctor Phone", and "Doctor License".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Information** (`teacher_info`, text, required: false): This page captures basic teacher information, such as your name, which will be used to verify your identity.
* **Leave Request** (`leave_request`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your leave request, including the reason for your absence.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required: false): Specify the start date of your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required: false): Specify the end date of your leave.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required: false): Clearly state the reason for your leave request.
* **Expected Return Date** (`expected_return_date`, date, required: false): If known, provide your expected return date to facilitate planning and arrangements.
* **Doctor Note** (`doctor_note`, note, required: false): Attach any relevant doctor's notes or medical certificates related to your leave.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required: false): If you have a doctor's note, enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor Address** (`doctor_address`, text, required: false): If you have a doctor's note, enter the doctor's address.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, required: false): If you have a doctor's note, enter the doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required: false): If you have a doctor's note, enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor License** (`doctor_license`, text, required: false): If you have a doctor's note, enter the doctor's license number.
* **Leave Request Reason** (`leave_request_reason`, text, required: false): Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave request. 

## Tips
* Please complete this form accurately and completely to ensure that all necessary information is captured.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or department head.
