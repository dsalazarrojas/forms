# Weekly Schedule And Bonus Options - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekly Schedule And Bonus Options form is designed to collect information about an employee's schedule, contact details, and bonus preferences. This form helps to ensure that employees are aware of their schedule and bonus opportunities, and that management has accurate information for payroll and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Employee Schedule" field, which should be a brief description of your work schedule for the week.
2. In the "Contact Details" field, provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
3. In the "Bonus Preferences" field, select your preferred bonus options, such as whether you would like to receive bonuses and how frequently you would like them to be paid.
4. Enter your availability in the "Availability" field, which should be a date indicating when you are available to work.
5. If applicable, provide a bonus amount in the "Bonus Amount" field.
6. In the "Bonus Frequency" field, select how frequently you would like to receive bonuses.
7. If you have a preferred bonus payment date, enter it in the "Bonus Payment Date" field.
8. Finally, provide any additional contact information, including an alternate email and phone number, in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Schedule** (employee_schedule, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your work schedule for the week.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, optional): Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you efficiently.
* **Bonus Preferences** (bonus_preferences, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you would like to receive bonuses and how frequently you would like them to be paid. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Availability** (availability, date, optional): Enter a date indicating when you are available to work. This is helpful for scheduling purposes.
* **Bonus Amount** (bonus_amount, number, optional): If you would like to receive bonuses, enter the amount you would like to receive.
* **Bonus Frequency** (bonus_frequency, select_one, optional): Select how frequently you would like to receive bonuses. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Bonus Payment Date** (bonus_payment_date, date, optional): If you have a preferred bonus payment date, enter it here.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you efficiently.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you efficiently.
* **Alternate Contact Email** (contact_alternate_email, email, optional): If you have an alternate email address, enter it here.
* **Alternate Contact Phone** (contact_alternate_phone, text, optional): If you have an alternate phone number, enter it here.
* **Note** (note, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, enter it here.
