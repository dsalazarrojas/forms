# Staff Apparel Size Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Staff Apparel Size Collection" form is used to collect information about employee clothing sizes for HR purposes, including uniforms, promotional items, and other staff clothing needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select your size for each type of staff apparel:
  * **T-shirt Small**: Choose your size from the dropdown options.
  * **T-shirt Large**: Choose your size from the dropdown options.
  * **Hooded Jacket Small**: Choose your size from the dropdown options.
  * **Hooded Jacket Large**: Choose your size from the dropdown options.
  * **Pant Small**: Choose your size from the dropdown options.
  * **Pant Large**: Choose your size from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their personnel record.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the employee's official job title.
* **T-shirt Small** (number, optional): Select the number that corresponds with your T-shirt size.
* **T-shirt Large** (number, optional): Select the number that corresponds with your T-shirt size.
* **Hooded Jacket Small** (number, optional): Select the number that corresponds with your hooded jacket size.
* **Hooded Jacket Large** (number, optional): Select the number that corresponds with your hooded jacket size.
* **Pant Small** (number, optional): Select the number that corresponds with your pant size.
* **Pant Large** (number, optional): Select the number that corresponds with your pant size.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are accurate when entering your sizes, as this will help the administration team order the correct sizes for your staff apparel.
* If you are unsure of any of your sizes, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
