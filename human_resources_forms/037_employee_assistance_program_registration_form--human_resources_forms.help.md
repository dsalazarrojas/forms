# Employee Assistance Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Assistance Program Registration Form is a crucial tool for the Employee Assistance Program team to understand employee needs and provide relevant support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the fields and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Provide accurate and honest responses.
3. Fill in the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (*).
4. Select relevant options from the dropdown menus.
5. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* **First Name** (text, First Name, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name** (text, Last Name, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.

### Job Title (3)
* **Job Title** (text, Job Title, required/optional): Enter your current job title or the job title of your position.

### Department (4)
* **Department** (text, Department, required/optional): Enter the name of your department.

### Email (5)
* **Email** (email, Email, required/optional): Enter your work email address.

### Phone (6)
* **Phone** (text, Phone, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.

### Employee Type (7)
* **Employee Type** (select_one, Employee Type, required/optional): Select your employee type from the dropdown menu:
	+ Clerk
	+ Manager
	+ Team Lead
	+ Employee

### Other Employee Type (8)
* **Other Employee Type** (text, Other Employee Type, required/optional): If "Other" is selected in the previous field, provide a brief description of your other employee type.

### Date of Birth (9)
* **Date of Birth** (date, Date of Birth, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Hire Date (10)
* **Hire Date** (date, Hire Date, required/optional): Enter your hire date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Manager Name (11)
* **Manager Name** (text, Manager Name, required/optional): Enter your manager's name.

### Manager Title (12)
* **Manager Title** (text, Manager Title, required/optional): Enter your manager's title.

### Team Leads (13)
* **Team Leads** (select_multiple, Team Leads, required/optional): Select your team leads from the dropdown menu:
	+ Team Lead 1
	+ Team Lead 2
	+ Team Lead 3

### Job Function (14)
* **Job Function** (text, Job Function, required/optional): Enter your job function or position.

### Other Job Function (15)
* **Other Job Function** (text, Other Job Function, required/optional): If "Other" is selected in the previous field, provide a brief description of your other job function.

### Work Location (16)
* **Work Location** (text, Work Location, required/optional): Enter your work location.

### Other Work Location (17)
* **Other Work Location** (text, Other Work Location, required/optional): If "Other" is selected in the previous field, provide a brief description of your other work location.

### Work Start Date (18)
* **Work Start Date** (date, Work Start Date, required/optional): Enter your work start date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Work End Date (19)
* **Work End Date** (date, Work End Date, required/optional): Enter your work end date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Work Status (20)
* **Work Status** (text, Work Status, required/optional): Enter your current work status.

### Other Work Status (21)
* **Other Work Status** (text, Other Work Status, required/optional): If "Other" is selected in the previous field, provide a brief description of your other work status.

### Emergency Contact (22)
* **Emergency Contact** (select_one, Emergency Contact, required/optional): Select whether you have an emergency contact from the dropdown menu:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Other Emergency Contact (23)
* **Other Emergency Contact** (text, Other Emergency Contact, required/optional): If "Yes" is selected in the previous field, enter the name of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Relationship (24)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (select_one, Emergency Contact Relationship, required/optional): Select the relationship of your emergency contact from the dropdown menu:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Other Emergency Contact Relationship (25)
* **Other Emergency Contact Relationship** (text, Other Emergency Contact Relationship, required/optional): If "Yes" is selected in the previous field, enter a brief description of your emergency contact relationship.
