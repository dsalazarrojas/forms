# Employee Warning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document a formal warning to an employee regarding a potential issue or concern with their job performance or conduct. This form will be used to initiate a formal warning process, which may lead to subsequent disciplinary actions or corrective measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the date of the warning.
3. Provide a clear and concise reason for the warning.
4. Enter the employee's ID number.
5. Enter the name of the manager who assigned the warning.
6. Select the warning level (High or Low).
7. Enter any comments or additional information related to the warning.
8. Select the warning date.
9. If applicable, select the employee's status (Active or Inactive).
10. If applicable, select the type of warning (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
11. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (required): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last name** (required): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Date of warning** (required): Enter the date of the warning.
* **Reason for warning** (required): Provide a clear and concise reason for the warning.
* **Employee ID** (required): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Manager's name** (required): Enter the name of the manager who assigned the warning.
* **Warning level** (required): Select the level of warning (High or Low).
* **Comments** (optional): Enter any additional comments or information related to the warning.
* **Warning date** (required): Enter the date of the warning.
* **Employee status** (optional): If the employee is Inactive, select this option.
* **Warning type** (optional): If applicable, select the type of warning (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Tips

* Ensure that the reason for the warning is clearly documented.
* Keep the form up-to-date to track any progress or changes related to the warning.
* If additional information is required or if the employee's status changes, update the form accordingly.
