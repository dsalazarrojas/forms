# store_manager - Help Guide
## Purpose
The store_manager form is a simple collection of fields meant to gather information about employees and stores.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's basic details such as name, job title, and date.
2. For some fields, you have options to select from. Choose the correct response based on the options provided.
3. Enter or select store-related information such as store name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's name in this field.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's job title in this field.
* **Job Title List** (`job_title_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate whether or not the employee has a job title.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, select_one, optional): Select from the options provided to confirm or deny the employee's job title.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date in this field in the desired format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time in this field in the 24-hour format.
* **Employee Information 2** (`store_manager_form_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the employee.
* **Employee Name 2** (`employee_name_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the employee.
* **Store Information** (`store_manager_form_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the store.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the store.

Note: Some fields are optional, so you do not need to fill them in if you do not have the information or it is not applicable.
