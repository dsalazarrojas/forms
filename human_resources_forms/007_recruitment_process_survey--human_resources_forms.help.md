# Recruitment Process Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on the recruitment process used by the organization. It will help recruiters, hiring managers, and other stakeholders refine the process to better serve job applicants and improve the overall hiring experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Select the best option that applies to your experience from the available options.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application Process**: Describe your experience with the job application process.
  • Type: Text
  • Purpose: Collecting feedback on the job application process.
* **Candidate Experience**: Select all the experiences you had with the candidate during the recruitment process.
  • Type: Multiple selection (Select one, two or more options)
  • Purpose: Understanding the candidate's experience during the recruitment process.
* **Communication Frequency**: Enter the frequency of communication with the candidate during the recruitment process.
  • Type: Number
  • Purpose: Understanding how often communication is maintained with the candidate.
* **The Interview Process**: Select the type of interview process used.
  • Type: Single selection (Select one option)
  • Purpose: Understanding the type of interview process used.
* **Job Offer and Onboarding**: Enter the duration of the job offer and onboarding process.
  • Type: Time
  • Purpose: Understanding the duration of the job offer and onboarding process.
* **Candidate Satisfaction**: Select all the ways in which you were satisfied with the candidate during the recruitment process.
  • Type: Multiple selection (Select one, two or more options)
  • Purpose: Understanding the candidate's level of satisfaction with the recruitment process.
* **Communication Channels**: Select the communication channel used.
  • Type: Single selection (Select one option)
  • Purpose: Understanding the communication channel used.
* **Job Ad Channels**: Select all the channels used for job advertisements.
  • Type: Multiple selection (Select one, two or more options)
  • Purpose: Understanding the channels used for job advertisements.
* **Job Ad Duration**: Enter the duration of the job advertisement.
  • Type: Text
  • Purpose: Understanding the duration of the job advertisement.
* **Job Interview**: Select all the types of interviews conducted.
  • Type: Multiple selection (Select one, two or more options)
  • Purpose: Understanding the types of interviews conducted.
* **Job Evaluation**: Enter the duration of the job evaluation process.
  • Type: Time
  • Purpose: Understanding the duration of the job evaluation process.
* **Onboarding**: Select the type of onboarding process used.
  • Type: Single selection (Select one option)
  • Purpose: Understanding the type of onboarding process used.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the text box provided.
