# human_resources_optimization_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The human_resources_optimization_contact_form is used to collect information from customers or users regarding their human resources optimization needs. This helps us understand their requirements and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the size of your company from the list (Small, Medium, or Large).
2. Enter your First Name.
3. Enter your Last Name.
4. Provide your Email address.
5. Enter your Phone Number.
6. Check if you need HR Priorities (True or False).
7. Add any additional Notes you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Size (Company Size)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the size of your company from the list. This will help us understand your organization's structure and needs.
* **First Name (First Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **HR Priorities (HR Priorities)** (`select_one`, required: false): Check this box if you need HR priorities.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.
