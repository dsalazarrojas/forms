# Contractor Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off for contractors. Please fill out the form below to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contractor information, including your full name, ID number, and email address.
2. Provide your phone number and department or project assignment.
3. Select the type of time off you are requesting (e.g. Vacation, Personal Day, etc.).
4. Enter the dates of your time off, including the start and end dates.
5. Specify how many days you are requesting for time off.
6. List your current projects or assignments and any pending deadlines during your absence.
7. Explain how your work will be covered during your time off.
8. Identify the colleague who will be briefed on your work and covering for you.
9. Indicate your availability during your time off, including if you can be reached by phone, email, or if you are unavailable.
10. Specify your preferred method of emergency contact.
11. Review the section on previous time off requests and indicate if you have any previous requests pending or approved.
12. Enter your current time off balance (if known).
13. Acknowledge that you understand the time off policy.
14. Indicate if this request is urgent.
15. Add any additional notes or comments.
16. Finally, date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contractor Full Name** (`contractor_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contractor ID Number** (`contractor_id`): Enter your ID number or employee/contractor identification number.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department or Project** (`department`): Enter your current department or project assignment.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Type of Time Off** (`time_off_type`): Select the type of leave requested (e.g. Vacation, Personal Day, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the first day of your time off.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the last day of your time off.
* **Return to Work Date** (`return_date`): Enter the date you will resume work.
* **Total Days Requested** (`total_days`): Enter the number of days being requested.
* **Current Projects or Assignments** (`current_projects`): List your active projects or assignments.
* **Pending Deadlines During Absence** (`pending_deadlines`): List any deadlines during your absence.
* **How Will Your Work Be Covered** (`coverage_plan`): Explain how your work will be covered during your time off.
* **Colleague Briefed On Your Work** (`colleague_briefing`): Enter the name of the colleague who will be briefed on your work.
* **Availability During Time Off** (`available_by_phone`, `available_by_email`): Select how you can be reached during your time off.
* **Preferred Emergency Contact Method** (`emergency_contact_method`): Select your preferred method of emergency contact (e.g. phone call, text message, etc.).
* **Previous Time Off Taken This Year** (`previous_time_off_this_year`): Enter the number of days already taken this year.
* **Accrued Time Off Balance** (`accrued_time_off_balance`): Enter your current balance (if known).
* **Request Acknowledgement** (`understand_policy`): Acknowledge that you understand the time off policy.
* **Is This Request Urgent?** (`request_urgent`): Indicate if this request is urgent.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`): Add any other relevant information.
* **Request Submission Date** (`request_date`): Date and time of submission.

## Tips
Please review the time off policy carefully before submitting this form. Ensure that you have not already submitted a similar request for the same time period. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
