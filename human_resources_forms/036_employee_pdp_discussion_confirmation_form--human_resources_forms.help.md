# Employee PDP Discussion Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm and document discussions about employee performance, status, or other matters with management. It helps to track and verify the occurrence of such discussions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the options for "employee_pdp_discussion_confirmation_form" field carefully and select the correct option that best describes the nature of the discussion you had with your manager.
2. Enter your name in the "employee_name" field.
3. Enter your manager's name in the "manager_name" field.
4. Choose the date of the discussion from the calendar in the "discussion_date" field.
5. Select the time of the discussion in the "discussion_time" field.
6. Enter the duration of the meeting in the "meeting_duration" field.
7. Provide the location of the discussion in the "meeting_location" field.
8. Select the time of the meeting from the "meeting_time" field.
9. Enter the date of the meeting in the "meeting_date" field.
10. Describe the discussion or any other details that were discussed in the "meeting_details" field.
11. Select your status as an employee regarding your performance or any other issues discussed in the "employee_status" field.
12. Select your manager's status as an employee regarding your performance or other issues discussed in the "manager_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_pdp_discussion_confirmation_form** (Select Multiple): This field confirms the type of discussion you had with your manager. Please select "Yes" if you had a discussion, "No" otherwise.
* **employee_name** (Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **manager_name** (Text): Enter your manager's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **discussion_date** (Date): Choose the date you had the discussion with your manager.
* **discussion_time** (Time): Choose the time you had the discussion with your manager.
* **meeting_duration** (Number): Enter the duration of the meeting or discussion (in minutes or hours).
* **meeting_location** (Text): Enter the location where you had the discussion with your manager.
* **meeting_time** (Time): Select the time of the meeting (if the discussion took place during a meeting).
* **meeting_date** (Date): Enter the date of the meeting (if the discussion took place during a meeting).
* **meeting_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about the discussion or meeting that were not already covered.
* **employee_status** (Select One): Select your current status regarding your performance or issues discussed (Active, Pending, or Inactive).
* **manager_status** (Select One): Select your manager's current status regarding your performance or issues discussed (Active, Pending, or Inactive).
