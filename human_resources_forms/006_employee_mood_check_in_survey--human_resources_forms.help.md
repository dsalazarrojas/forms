# Employee Mood Check In Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Mood Check In Survey is a form designed to help employees express their emotions and provide feedback on their current state. It is likely used by HR department to monitor employee well-being and identify potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer the questions with the options provided, or write in your own response if none of the provided options match your feelings.
3. Make sure to provide any comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Survey
* **Employee Survey** (`employee_survey`, text, optional): This field is where you can write a short description of your current workday, including any challenges or concerns you're facing.

### 2. Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Fill in your name to help HR track your feedback. 

### 3. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so HR can contact you if they need more information or follow up on your feedback.

### 4. Department
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which department you belong to. This will help HR track feedback from different departments.

### 5. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Fill in your job title to help HR understand your role and responsibilities.

### 6. Mood
* **Mood** (`mood`, select_one, optional): Choose how you're feeling today:
	+ Very Happy
	+ Somewhat Happy
	+ Somewhat Sad
	+ Very Sad

### 7. Suggestions
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions on how to improve your work experience or address any issues you're facing, write them down here.

### 8. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, write them down here.

### 9. Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you're filling out the form.

### 10. Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you're filling out the form.

### 11. Submitted
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you've submitted the form or not:
	+ Yes
	+ No

Please make sure to complete the form honestly and thoroughly, and feel free to ask HR if you have any questions or need further clarification.
