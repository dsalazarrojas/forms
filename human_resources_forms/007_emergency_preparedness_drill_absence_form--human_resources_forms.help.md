# Emergency Preparedness Drill Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Emergency Preparedness Drill Absence Form is used to report your status and provide necessary information for drill organizers and emergency responders. Please fill out this form if you are unable to participate in the emergency preparedness drill.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and last name in the First name and Last name fields.
2. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and phone number in the Emergency Contact Name and Emergency Contact Phone fields.
3. Select the Absence Reason for your absence from the options provided.
4. Enter the Start Time and End Time of your intended absence from the drill.
5. If applicable, provide any comments or additional information about your absence.
6. Check the Form submitted field to indicate that you have completed the form.
7. Review the other fields for any additional information that might be relevant to your absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Absence** (Date): Enter the date you plan to be absent from the drill.
* **Start Time** (Time): Enter the start time of your absence.
* **End Time** (Time): Enter the end time of your absence.
* **Absence Reason** (Text): Select the reason for your absence.
* **Contact Method** (Text): Enter how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (Text): Explain the relation between you and your emergency contact.
* **Participant status** (Text): Indicate your status as a participant in the drill.
* **Drill location** (Text): Enter the drill location.
* **Drill date** (Date): Enter the date of the drill.
* **Drill time** (Time): Enter the time of the drill.
* **Absence type** (Text): Select the type of your absence.
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments about your absence.
* **Form submitted** (Text): Indicate that this is your completed form.
* **Participant name** (Text): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Drill status** (Text): Select the status of the drill.
* **Drill participants** (Text): Enter any additional drill participants.
* **Drill participant status** (Text): Select the status of drill participants.
* **Participant ID** (Text): Enter your participant ID.
* **Participant email** (Email): Enter your participant email address.
* **Drill supervisor** (Text): Enter the name of the drill supervisor.
* **Form assigned** (Text): Indicate that this form was assigned to you.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
* If you need to report your absence, please fill out this form to ensure that drill organizers and emergency responders have the necessary information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the drill organizers or emergency responders.
