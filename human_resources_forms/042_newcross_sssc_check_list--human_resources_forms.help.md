# Newcross SSSC Check List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employees for human resources or personnel management purposes. It is likely used for administrative tasks such as checking employee data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Provide the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Enter the employee's department in the "Department" field.
4. Enter the employee's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter the employee's job start date in the "Job Start Date" field.
6. Enter the employee's manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
7. Enter the employee's manager's email in the "Manager EMail" field.
8. Choose the employee's type from the "Employee Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, optional): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title or position.
* **Department** (Department, text, optional): Enter the employee's department or team.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Enter the employee's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Job Start Date** (Job Start Date, date, optional): Enter the employee's job start date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, optional): Enter the manager's full name.
* **Manager EMail** (Manager EMail, email, optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Employee Type** (Employee Type, text, optional): Choose the employee type (e.g., full-time, part-time, contractor, etc.).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- Check with the employee if necessary to verify their information.
- This form is for internal use only, so do not share with anyone outside of your organization.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the HR department.
