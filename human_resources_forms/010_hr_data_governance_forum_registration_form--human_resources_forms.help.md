# HR Data Governance Forum Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Data Governance Forum Registration Form is a required form for attendees who want to participate in the HR Data Governance Forum. This form collects relevant information about attendees, including their event type, date, time, location, contact information, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are not already registered for this event to avoid duplicate submissions.
2. Select the correct event type from the dropdown list.
3. Fill out the event date and time according to your preference.
4. Enter the location of the event.
5. If you want to add a speaker, enter their information in the corresponding field.
6. Check "Yes" or "No" for the attendee interest question.
7. Enter any additional comments about your interest in the event.
8. Fill out your name, company, job title, contact number, and contact email accurately.
9. Confirm your registration by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the registration confirmation question.
10. Confirm your attendee agreement by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the attendee agreement question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of event you are interested in (Yes or No).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the event. The format should be in YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the event. The format should be in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Speaker** (`speaker`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and information of the speaker (if applicable).
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the speaker (if applicable).
* **Attendee Interest** (`attendee_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check "Yes" or "No" for the attendee interest question.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments about your interest in the event.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name accurately.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company accurately.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title accurately.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number accurately.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email accurately.
* **Registration Confirmation** (`registration_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your registration by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Attendee Agreement** (`attendee_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your attendee agreement by selecting "Yes" or "No".
