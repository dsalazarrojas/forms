# Team Member Directory Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Directory Submission form is used to gather essential details about each team member to be included in the team directory. Please submit a new form for each team member who needs to be included in the directory.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Enter the team member's name, first name, last name, email address, contact phone number, and role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`, text, required): Enter the team member's name.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the team member's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the team member's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the team member's email address. Ensure it is formatted as a standard email address (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the team member's contact phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the team member's role or function within the team.

## Tips
* Ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date to guarantee that you are correctly listed in the team directory. 
* If you have any issues or corrections regarding your submission, please contact the team lead or administrator for assistance.
