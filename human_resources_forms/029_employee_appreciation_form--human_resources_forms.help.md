# Employee Appreciation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Appreciation Form is used to gather information on employee recognition or rewards for their hard work and contributions. It helps maintain a formal record of employee appreciation for future reference and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the employee's unique ID to ensure the appreciation is directed to the correct individual.
2. Provide a brief description of the event or reason for the appreciation.
3. Select the type of appreciation (e.g., Employee of the Month, Employee of the Year, Job Well Done, Great Job).
4. Enter the appreciation amount.
5. Choose the appreciation type (e.g., Cash, Gift Card, Public Recognition, Private Recognition).
6. Enter the appreciation date.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the appreciation.
8. Enter the employee's name, manager's name, and supervisor's name.
9. Select the employee's department and division.
10. Enter the employee's job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee's unique identifier.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the appreciation event (if applicable).
* **Event** (`event`, text, optional): Briefly describe the event or reason for the appreciation.
* **Appreciation** (`appreciation`, select_one, optional): Select the type of appreciation (e.g., Employee of the Month, Employee of the Year).
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter a brief message to the employee.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the manager's name.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the employee's department.
* **Division** (`division`, text, required): Enter the employee's division.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Appreciation Amount** (`appreciation_amount`, number, required): Enter the appreciation amount.
* **Appreciation Type** (`appreciation_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of appreciation (e.g., Cash, Gift Card, Public Recognition, Private Recognition).
* **Appreciation Date** (`appreciation_date`, date, required): Enter the appreciation date.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the appreciation.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, optional): Select the employee's status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Retired, On Leave).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's email (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number (if applicable).
