# Workplace Climate Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Climate Audit Form is designed to measure the overall climate and satisfaction of employees in the workplace. It is a tool to help management understand the sentiment and feelings of employees regarding their work environment, climate, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question honestly, to the best of your ability.
2. Select the appropriate answer for each question from the available options.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Comments" field.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Climate Audit Form** (Climate Survey 2, select_one): 
   Indicates whether you have taken the climate survey.
* **Climate Satisfaction** (Climate Satisfaction, select_multiple): 
   Select how satisfied you are with the climate in the workplace.
* **Workplace Climate** (Work Place Climate, select_one): 
   Indicates whether the work place climate is satisfactory.
* **Overall Climate Satisfaction** (Overall Climate Satisfaction, select_one): 
   Select how satisfied you are with overall climate.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction (2), number): 
   Rate your overall satisfaction level.
* **Workplace Climate Score** (Workplace Climate Score 2, number): 
   Rate the workplace climate score.
* **Work Phone** (Work Phone, text): 
   Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Phone 2** (Work Phone 2, text): 
   Enter your work phone number for reference.
* **Email** (Email, text): 
   Enter your email address.
* **Email 2** (Email 2, text): 
   Enter your email address for reference.
* **Date** (Date, date): 
   Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (Time, time): 
   Enter the time in HH:MM format.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (Employee Satisfaction, text): 
   Enter any comments or feedback about employee satisfaction.
* **Comments** (Comments, text): 
   Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Suggestions** (Suggestions, text): 
   Enter any suggestions or feedback for improvement.
* **Work Environment Page** (Work Environment Page, text): 
   Describe your work environment.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, select_one): 
   Rate your overall satisfaction level.
* **Workplace Climate Index** (Work Climate Index, number): 
   Rate the workplace climate index.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text): 
   Enter any additional feedback or comments.
* **Workplace Climate Score** (Workplace Climate Score, number): 
   Rate the workplace climate score.
* **Climate Survey** (Climate Survey, select_one): 
   Indicates whether you have taken the climate survey.
* **Notes** (Notes, text): 
   Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Overall Satisfaction (2)** (Overall Satisfaction (2), number): 
   Rate your overall satisfaction level.
* **Climate Survey 2** (Climate Survey 2, select_one): 
   Indicates whether you have taken the climate survey.
* **Work Place Page** (Work Place Page, text): 
   Describe your work place page.
