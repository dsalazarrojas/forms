# Accident Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Accident Incident Form is used to report and document workplace accidents and incidents. This form helps us identify the cause and severity of the incident, allowing us to take necessary steps to prevent future occurrences and ensure a safe work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter your employee information, including your name, ID number, department, job title, and supervisor's name.
3.  Provide the details of the incident, including date, time, location, and type of incident.
4.  Specify the body part(s) injured and the severity of the injury.
5.  Describe any first aid administered and whether medical treatment was sought.
6.  List any witnesses and equipment involved in the incident.
7.  Identify any contributing factors that may have led to the incident.
8.  Describe any immediate corrective actions taken.
9.  Enter the date this report was filed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel document.
*   **Employee ID Number (3)**: Enter your official employee ID number.
*   **Department (4)**: Enter the department where the incident occurred.
*   **Job Title (5)**: Enter your current job title.
*   **Supervisor Name (6)**: Enter the name of your supervisor or person in charge.
*   **Phone Number (7)**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
*   **Date of Incident (9)**: Enter the date of the incident in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Time of Incident (10)**: Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour format (e.g., 1:00 PM).
*   **Location Where Incident Occurred (11)**: Be specific about the location, including building, floor, and area.
*   **Type of Incident (12)**: Select the type of incident from the provided options.
*   **Detailed Description of What Happened (13)**: Describe the events leading up to and following the incident.
*   **Body Part Injured (14)**: Select all body parts involved in the incident.
*   **Severity of Injury (15)**: Select the severity of the injury from the provided options.
*   **Was First Aid Administered (17)**: Select whether first aid was administered.
*   **Was Professional Medical Treatment Sought (18)**: Select whether professional medical treatment was sought.
*   **Names of Witnesses (if any) (19)**: List the names and contact information of any witnesses.
*   **Equipment or Machinery Involved (20)**: List any equipment or machinery involved in the incident.
*   **Contributing Factors (21)**: Select all contributing factors that led to the incident.
*   **Immediate Corrective Actions Taken (22)**: Describe any immediate actions taken to prevent recurrence.
*   **Date This Report Was Filed (23)**: Enter the date this report was filed in MM/DD/YYYY format.
