# remote_work_readiness_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The remote_work_readiness_assessment form is designed to evaluate an employee's readiness to work from home. This form collects information about the employee's work habits, location, and manager's preferences to assess their suitability for remote work arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information, including name, department, job title, work email, phone number, and alternate phone number.
2. Answer questions about your work location, work from home status, and shift type.
3. Evaluate your own work readiness score and level, and provide any additional notes.
4. Enter your manager's information, including name, email, and phone number.
5. Review and submit the form to complete the assessment.
