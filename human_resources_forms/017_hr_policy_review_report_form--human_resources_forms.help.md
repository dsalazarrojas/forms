# hr-policy-review-report-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track information for policy reviews within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee ID number of the employee associated with the policy.
2. Select the department associated with the policy.
3. Enter the name of the policy being reviewed.
4. Select the category of the policy.
5. Enter a brief description of the policy.
6. Assign a rating (on a scale) to the policy.
7. Enter any comments related to the policy.
8. Select the status of the policy's approval (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending).
9. Enter the date of the policy review.
10. Enter the name(s) of the reviewer(s) associated with the policy review.
11. Select the employee(s) contact(s) associated with the policy.
12. Select the status of the manager's approval (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending).
13. Select the status of the policy review (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
14. Enter any comments related to the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the Employee ID number of the employee associated with the policy.
* **Department** (`department_id`, text, optional): Select the department associated with the policy.
* **Policy Name** (`policy_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the policy being reviewed.
* **Category** (`policy_category`, text, optional): Select the category of the policy.
* **Description** (`policy_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the policy.
* **Ratings** (`policy_ratings`, number, optional): Assign a rating (on a scale) to the policy.
* **Comments** (`policy_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments related to the policy.
* **Approval Status** (`policy_approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the policy's approval (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending).
* **Review Date** (`policy_review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the policy review.
* **Reviewer** (`policy_reviewer`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) of the reviewer(s) associated with the policy review.
* **Employee Contacts** (`employee_contacts`, select_multiple, optional): Select the employee(s) contact(s) associated with the policy.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the manager's approval (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending).
* **Review Status** (`policy_review_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the policy review (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments related to the review.
