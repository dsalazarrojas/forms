# Extended Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting an extended leave of absence from work. You will be asked to provide details about the leave duration, type, and reasons for your request. Please ensure you fill this form accurately and completely to facilitate a smooth processing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your Employee Name
* Enter your Job Title
* Select the Start Date of your leave
* Select the End Date of your leave
* Provide a Reason for your leave
* Choose your Approval Level (High, Medium, Low)
* Choose the Manager(s) for approval (Manager 1, Manager 2, Manager 3)
* Select whether this is a one-time request or a recurring request (Request Type)
* Enter the number of extended leave days

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (Employee Name)** 
  (Text field)
  Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or payroll records.
* **Job Title (Job Title)**
  (Text field)
  Enter your official job title, as recorded in the company directory.
* **Start Date (Start Date)**
  (Date field)
  Enter the date from which you will be taking your extended leave.
* **End Date (End Date)**
  (Date field)
  Enter the date until which you will be taking your extended leave.
* **Reason (Reason)**
  (Note field)
  Provide a brief explanation for your leave request, including any relevant details or supporting documentation.
* **Approval Level (Approval Level)**
  (Select One field)
  Choose the level of approval required for your request, with options:
  + High
  + Medium
  + Low
* **Manager Approval (Manager Approval)**
  (Select Multiple field)
  Select the manager(s) who will review and approve your request.
  + Manager 1
  + Manager 2
  + Manager 3
* **Request Type (Request Type)**
  (Select One field)
  Indicate whether this is a one-time request or a recurring request.
  + 'Yes'
  + 'No'
* **Extended Leave Days (Extended Leave Days)**
  (Number field)
  Enter the number of days for which you are requesting extended leave. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Be realistic with your requested dates and extended leave days.
- Clearly explain the reason for your leave request, including any supporting documentation.
- Double-check that your Manager Approval selections match your Approval Level choice.
- Review and submit your form carefully before submission.
