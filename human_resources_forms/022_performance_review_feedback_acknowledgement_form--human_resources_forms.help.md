# Performance Review Feedback Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Review Feedback Acknowledgement Form is a crucial document that confirms your understanding and acceptance of your performance review. It's a way to verify that you've received and reviewed your performance review results and to acknowledge your feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review your performance review document carefully and ensure that you understand your feedback and ratings.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Select the correct Department that corresponds to your role or position.
4. Optionally, provide the Date of Last Review if you'd like to include it.
5. Provide any feedback you'd like to share with your reviewer in the Feedback field.
6. Select the Review Outcome as per your performance review results.
7. If you have any additional comments, please include them in the Comments field.
8. Enter your Employee ID and Reviewer ID for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you belong to, which corresponds to your role or position.
* **Date of Last Review** (`date_of_last_review`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last performance review, if available.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any feedback you'd like to share with your reviewer.
* **Review Outcome** (`review_outcome`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the outcome of your performance review as per your performance review results.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments you'd like to include.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter your Employee ID for record-keeping purposes.
* **Reviewer ID** (`reviewer_id`, `text`, required): Enter your Reviewer ID for record-keeping purposes.
