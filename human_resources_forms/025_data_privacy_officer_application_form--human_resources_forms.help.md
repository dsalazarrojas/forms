# data_privacy_officer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The data_privacy_officer_application_form is a form designed to collect information from individuals applying for a data privacy officer role. This form is reviewed by the HR department and is used to assess your qualifications and experience for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form information section (Form Information) with a brief introduction about yourself.
2. Enter your name and email address in the Name and Email fields.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, contact number, and preferred contact method (Contact).
4. Select your availability for the role (Availability).
5. Enter your job preferences, including job title, job location, job category, job type, job status, and job description (Job Details).
6. Provide your job start and end dates, as well as your hourly rate and annual salary/bonus (Job Requirements).
7. Click Submit to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Form Information** (Form Information, text, required: false)
This field is a brief section where you can provide additional information about yourself and your application.

### 2. **Name** (Name, text, required: true)
Please enter your full name.

### 3. **Email** (Email, email, required: true)
Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.

### 4. **Contact Information** (Contact Information, note, required: false)
This field is for additional contact information. Please provide any relevant details here.

### 5. **Experience** (experience, note, required: false)
This field is for you to provide any relevant experience you have. Please be brief and concise.

### 6. **Resume** (Resume, text, required: true)
Please attach your resume in PDF format for review.

### 7. **References** (References, note, required: false)
This field is for you to provide any additional references you have. Please provide their names and contact information.

### 8. **Submit** (Submit, select_one, required: true)
Select Submit to submit your application.

### 9. **Name** (Name, text, required: false)
This field is not used in the application process. Please disregard.

### 10. **Role** (Role, text, required: true)

### 12. **Contact** (Contact, select_one, required: true)

### 13. **Availability** (Availability, select_multiple, required: true)

### 14. **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required: false)

### 15. **Job Location** (Job Location, text, required: false)
