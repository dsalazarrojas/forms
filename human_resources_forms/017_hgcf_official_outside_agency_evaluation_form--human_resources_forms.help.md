## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the referral source of the agency.
2. Select the agency you are collaborating with.
3. Select the evaluation date.
4. Select the start and end times of the evaluation.
5. Select the duration of the evaluation.
6. Enter any additional notes about the evaluation.
7. Enter contact information for the agency or collaborator.
8. Select the relationship between HGCF and the collaborator.
9. Select the method of evaluation.
10. Enter the outcome of the evaluation.
11. If necessary, select the second agency you are collaborating with and enter any other agency information.
12. Submit the form.
