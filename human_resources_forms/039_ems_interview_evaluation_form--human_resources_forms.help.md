# EMS Interview Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EMS Interview Evaluation Form is used by HR personnel to evaluate the performance of candidates during an interview. This form is essential for documenting and tracking interview processes, ensuring a standardized and fair evaluation of candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are filling this form for the correct candidate and interview.
2. Fill in the candidate's information in the **Candidate Information** section.
3. Briefly describe the interview process in the **Interview Process** section.
4. In the **Evaluation Scale** section, select the appropriate score for the candidate's performance.
5. Take a moment to write down your evaluation and notes about the candidate in the **Evaluation Notes** section.
6. Provide your professional recommendation for the candidate in the **Recommendation** section.
7. Add any additional comments about the candidate in the **Comments** section.
8. Score the candidate's performance using the provided scale in the **Score** section.
9. Enter the date and time of the interview in the **Date Of Interview** section.
10. In the **Interview Panel** section, select the member(s) of the panel that conducted the interview.
11. Finally, assign a weightage to the candidate's score in the **Candidate Score Weightage** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information**: Enter the candidate's name and any other relevant information.
* **Interview Process**: Briefly describe the interview process, including any challenges or unusual circumstances that may have affected the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Scale**: Select an integer from 1 to 5 to indicate the candidate's performance.
*   1: Unsatisfactory
*   2: Below Expectation
*   3: Average
*   4: Above Expectation
*   5: Outstanding
* **Evaluation Notes**: Write down your evaluation and notes about the candidate.
*   This field is optional, but it's an opportunity to provide more context and information about the candidate's performance.
*   Keep it concise and professional.
* **Recommendation**: Provide your professional recommendation for the candidate.
*   This field is optional, but it's an opportunity to provide a clear and concise recommendation.
*   Keep it brief and to the point.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the candidate.
*   This field is optional, but it's an opportunity to provide more information about the candidate's performance.
*   Keep it concise and professional.
* **Score**: Score the candidate's performance using the provided scale.
*   This field is required.
* **Date Of Interview**: Enter the date of the interview.
*   This field is required.
* **Time Of Interview**: Enter the time of the interview.
*   This field is required.
* **Interview Panel**: Select the member(s) of the panel that conducted the interview.
*   This field is required.
* **Candidate Score Weightage**: Assign a weightage to the candidate's score.
*   This field is required.
*   This field is optional, but it's an opportunity to provide more information about the candidate's performance.
*   Keep it concise and professional.
