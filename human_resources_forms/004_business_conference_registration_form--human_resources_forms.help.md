# Business Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Business Conference Registration Form, which is used to register attendees for the conference. The form collects essential information from the registrants, including their contact details, conference participation preferences, and accommodation and transportation needs. It ensures that the attendees can efficiently and accurately register for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Registration Type" from the options provided, which indicates the type of registration package you require.
2. Provide your "First Name", "Last Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" in the respective fields. Ensure that your email address is accurate, as we will use it for updates and confirmation.
3. Enter your "Job Title" if applicable.
4. Select your "Industry" from the provided options (e.g., Technology, Finance, Healthcare, etc.).
5. Choose the days you will attend by selecting all applicable options in the "Attending Days" field.
6. If you have any dietary restrictions, select all that apply in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
7. Enter your "Additional Dietary Notes" if needed.
8. If you require hotel accommodation, select "Yes" in the "Do you need hotel accommodation?" field.
9. Provide your "Arrival Date" and "Departure Date" if you have not already done so.
10. Select your transportation needs, if any.
11. Read and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" by selecting "Yes". Please ensure you have read and understood the terms before completing the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address, which we will use for updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which can be used for communication.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position if applicable.
* **Company Name**: Enter your employer or organization name.
* **Industry**: Select your industry from the provided options.
* **Attending Days**: Select all days you will participate in the conference.
* **Track Preference**: Provide any preferred track or session(s).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select all dietary restrictions that apply to you.
* **Additional Dietary Notes**: Enter any additional dietary notes or preferences not listed.
* **Hotel Needed**: Select "Yes" if you require accommodation.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter your estimated arrival date.
* **Departure Date**: Enter your estimated departure date.
* **Transportation needs**: Select "Yes" if you require airport transportation.
* **Registration Type**: Select your registration package (e.g., Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, etc.).
* **Early Bird Eligible**: Select "Yes" if you qualify for early bird pricing.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the conference.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Review your form before submitting to avoid errors or missing information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conference organizers for assistance.
* The conference organizers will use the provided information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.
