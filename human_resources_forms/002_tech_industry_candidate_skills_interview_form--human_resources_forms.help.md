# Tech Industry Candidate Skills Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a candidate's skills and experience for hiring purposes. It helps us to assess a candidate's suitability for our tech industry job openings. Please fill out this form to provide us with accurate and up-to-date information about your background.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Indicate whether you have completed a Bachelor Degree.
3. Indicate whether you have completed a Masters Degree.
4. Select the skills you possess from the given options.
5. Enter your Phone Number, which is a required field.
6. Enter your Email Address, which is a required field.
7. (Optional) Provide a brief description of your work experience.
8. (Optional) Enter your Address, which is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Skills** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the skills you possess from the given options. Some examples of skills that we are looking for include Programming, Soft Skills, etc.
* **Experience** (`4`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
* **Bachelor Degree** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have completed a Bachelor Degree. Select 'True' if you have, and 'False' if you have not.
* **Masters Degree** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have completed a Masters Degree. Select 'True' if you have, and 'False' if you have not.
* **Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, required): Please enter your valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Email** (`8`, `text`, required): Please enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Address** (`9`, `text`, optional): You can provide your address if you want, but it is not required.
