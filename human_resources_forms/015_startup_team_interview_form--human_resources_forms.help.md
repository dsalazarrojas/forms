# Startup Team Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Team Interview Form is a tool used to gather information from potential team members. It is a comprehensive assessment that helps us understand your skills, experience, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. You can use the provided options as reference for the select fields.
4. Use the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD) for the available date and time field.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Required)**: This section is meant to gather basic information about you. Please provide your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details (Required)**: This section is for providing your phone number, LinkedIn or other professional social media profiles, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Education History (Required)**: This section is meant to gather information about your educational background. Please select all relevant options that apply to you.
* **Work Experience (Required)**: This section is for providing information about your previous work experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Skills Required (Required)**: Select all the skills that you feel you possess, especially those relevant to the startup's industry or field.
* **Education Background (Required)**: Select all relevant education levels that apply to you.
* **Previous Employment (Required)**: Provide detailed information about your previous employment, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Work History (Required)**: Please provide detailed information about your work history, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Available Date and Time (Required)**: Please enter the date and time you are available to start working.
* **Availability (Required)**: Select your availability status, either Full time or Part time.
* **Work Status (Required)**: Select all relevant work status options that apply to you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
