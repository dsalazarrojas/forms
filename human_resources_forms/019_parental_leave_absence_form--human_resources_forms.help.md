# Parental Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Leave Absence Form is a required form that needs to be completed by employees who wish to take parental leave. This form helps to document the employee's absence and ensures that their leave is properly recorded and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (\*). The form is not required, but it is recommended that you complete it in full to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Enter your employee name and reason for leave in the "employee" and "reason" fields, respectively.
* Enter the start and end dates of your leave in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields, respectively. Use the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Enter the duration of your leave in the "duration" field. This should be a number that represents the number of days you will be away from work.
* If your leave is not a standard parental leave period, select "Yes" in the "frequency" field. If you have a different frequency, enter your alternative frequency in the "other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee** (`employee`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel file.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a brief description of why you are taking parental leave.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will start your leave. Use the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will end your leave. Use the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days you will be away from work.
*   **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if your leave is a standard parental leave period. If your leave is not a standard parental leave period, select "No" and enter your alternative frequency in the "other" field.
*   **Other** (`other`, text, required if "No" is selected in "Frequency"): Enter the frequency of your leave if it is not a standard parental leave period.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, consult your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is used to document your parental leave absence.
