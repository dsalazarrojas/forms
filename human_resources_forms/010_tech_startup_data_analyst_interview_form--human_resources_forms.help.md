# Tech Startup Data Analyst Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Startup Data Analyst Interview Form is used to gather information from potential candidates for a data analyst position in a tech startup. This form helps the hiring team to evaluate candidates based on their experience, skills, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe your job title and job level in the "Job Title" and "Job Level" fields.
5. Specify your work experience in years and months in the "Work Experience" field.
6. Choose your education level from the available options in the "Education Level" field.
7. Indicate if you can speak more than one language by selecting all applicable languages in the "Languages Spoken" field.
8. Choose the programming languages you are proficient in from the "Programming Languages" field.
9. Enter your available start date and earliest start date in the "Available Start Date" and "Earliest Start Date" fields.
10. If applicable, enter the earliest end date and reason for leaving in the "Earliest End Date" and "Earliest End Date Reason" fields.
11. Specify your availability in the "Availability" field.
12. Choose your role from the available options in the "Role" field.
13. Enter your expected salary and salary range in the "Expected Salary" and "Salary Range" fields.
14. Specify your salary currency in the "Salary Currency" field.
15. Describe your job type and status in the "Job Type" and "Job Status" fields.
16. Choose your job category from the available options in the "Job Category" field.
17. Enter your job sector and location in the "Job Sector" and "Job Location" fields.
18. Upload your resume in the "Resume" field.

* **Earliest Start Date**: Enter your earliest start date.
* **Earliest End Date**: Enter your earliest end date. If this date is not applicable, enter "N/A".
* **Earliest End Date Reason**: If you have an earliest end date, enter the reason for it.
* **Availability**: Specify your availability.
* **Role**: Choose your role.
* **Expected Salary**: Enter your expected salary.
* **Salary Range**: Enter your salary range.
* **Salary Currency**: Enter your salary currency.
* **Job Type**: Describe your job type.
* **Job Status**: Describe your job status.
