# Workplace Bullying Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Bullying Complaint Form is a platform for employees to report incidents of workplace bullying. This form is designed to ensure that incidents are reported and addressed in a timely and confidential manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, click on the "Start" button and begin filling out the fields as they appear.
2.  Fill out each field as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Bullying Incident**: This field is for a brief description of the incident. Please provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Date of Incident**: This field is for the date the bullying incident occurred. You can enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Workplace Location**: Please select the location where the incident happened from the provided options.
*   **Description of Incident**: This is an optional field for providing more detailed information about what happened.
*   **Perpetrator**: If you know the person involved in the incident, choose the option that best describes them. Please select all that apply.
*   **Witness**: If someone witnessed the incident, you may describe them here. Please be as specific as possible.
*   **Action Taken**: If you've taken any action to address the situation, select all that apply.
*   **Outcome**: Please select the outcome of the action taken.
*   **Employee ID**: This is your employee ID number.
*   **Department**: Please select the department where the incident occurred.
*   **Manager's Name**: If your manager was involved or aware of the incident, please provide their name.
*   **Manager's Contact**: If you wish to contact your manager about this incident, select the relevant option.
*   **Support Team**: Select the support team that you would like to handle the case.
*   **Date Reported**: This field is for when you submitted the report.
*   **Time Reported**: Enter the time you submitted the report.
*   **Reported By**: This is your name.
*   **Department ID**: This is your department ID number.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter the number where you can be reached.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Notes**: Please provide any additional comments or details about the incident.
*   **Form Status**: Please select the status of your report (e.g., "In Progress," "Resolved," etc.).
*   **Submitted By**: This field is automatically populated by the system.
*   **Submitted On**: This field will be filled out by the system.
*   **Submitted Time**: This field will also be automatically filled out.
*   **Submitted By User**: This field will be automatically populated by the system.

## Tips

*   Always report incidents in a clear and concise manner.
*   Ensure that your report is thorough but not excessively detailed.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please refer to this guide or contact your HR department for assistance.
*   All reports are reviewed and investigated by the designated support team.
*   Reports are handled in the strictest confidence and in line with company policies.
