# Project Manager Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from applicants for a project manager position. The form is intended to gather details about the applicant's background, experience, and qualifications for the project manager role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any other relevant information in the "Applicant Information" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Describe your relevant work experience in the "Work Experience" section.
4. List your educational background in the "Education History" section.
5. List the projects you have worked on in the "Projects Worked" section.
6. List your relevant skills in the "Skills List" section.
7. Indicate your availability to start the position.
8. Upload your resume.
9. Upload your portfolio.
10. Provide references (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information**: Enter your full name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your email address, phone number, and any other contact details you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates.
* **Education History**: List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Projects Worked**: List the projects you have worked on, including project names, dates, and roles.
* **Skills List**: List your relevant skills, including programming languages, tools, and certifications.
* **Availability**: Indicate your availability to start the position, including start date and notice period.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Portfolio**: Upload your portfolio, including relevant work samples and examples.
* **References**: Provide references, including names, job titles, and contact information.

## Tips

* Please upload your resume and portfolio in a clear and readable format.
* Make sure to proofread your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the contact email: form@gic.mx.
