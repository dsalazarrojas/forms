# new_employee_equipment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The new_employee_equipment_form is designed to gather information about the equipment needed by new employees for their work. The form is divided into multiple pages with various fields that require completion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete each page of the form, paying attention to required fields (marked as required: true).
3. Enter your name and department on new_employees_equipment_form_page_19 and new_employees_equipment_form_page_24.
4. Enter your department on new_employees_equipment_form_page_4 and new_employees_equipment_form_page_24.
5. Indicate whether the equipment is new or used on new_employees_equipment_form_page_5, new_employees_equipment_form_page_12, new_employees_equipment_form_page_14, new_employees_equipment_form_page_21, new_employees_equipment_form_page_23.
6. Select multiple equipment options on new_employees_equipment_form_page_6, new_employees_equipment_form_page_11, new_employees_equipment_form_page_13, new_employees_equipment_form_page_15, new_employees_equipment_form_page_22.
7. Enter the date the equipment is needed on new_employees_equipment_form_page_3 and new_employees_equipment_form_page_7.
8. Enter notes on new_employees_equipment_form_page_9 and new_employees_equipment_form_page_16.
9. Enter the employee's email on new_employees_equipment_form_page_10 and new_employees_equipment_form_page_17.
10. Select the correct options for time on new_employees_equipment_form_page_8.
11. Enter the equipment's description and quantity on new_employees_equipment_form_page_2.
12. Enter the cost of the equipment on new_employees_equipment_form_page_2.
13. Enter the correct options for equipment on new_employees_equipment_form_page_18, new_employees_equipment_form_page_22.
14. Enter the correct options for new_employees_equipment_form_page_21, new_employees_equipment_form_page_23.
15. Enter the equipment's type on new_employees_equipment_form_page_1.
16. Enter the correct options for new_employees_equipment_form_page_6.
17. Enter the correct options for new_employees_equipment_form_page_13.
18. Enter the correct options for new_employees_equipment_form_page_15.
19. Enter the correct options for new_employees_equipment_form_page_22.
20. Review your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_1): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification. 
* **Department** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_4): Enter the name of your department.
* **Job Title** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_1): Enter your official job title.
* **Equipment Type** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_1): Enter the type of equipment you need.
* **Equipment Description** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_2): Enter a detailed description of the equipment you need.
* **Quantity** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_2): Enter the number of equipment units you need.
* **Cost** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_2): Enter the cost of the equipment.
* **New/Used** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_5, new_employees_equipment_form_page_12, new_employees_equipment_form_page_14, new_employees_equipment_form_page_21, new_employees_equipment_form_page_23): Select whether the equipment is new or used.
* **Date Needed** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_3, new_employees_equipment_form_page_7): Enter the date you need the equipment.
* **Multiple Options** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_6, new_employees_equipment_form_page_11, new_employees_equipment_form_page_13, new_employees_equipment_form_page_15, new_employees_equipment_form_page_22): Select the options that apply to your equipment needs.
* **Notes** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_9, new_employees_equipment_form_page_16): Enter any additional information that will help us understand your needs.
* **Email** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_10, new_employees_equipment_form_page_17): Enter your official email address.
* **Equipment Options** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_18, new_employees_equipment_form_page_22): Select the equipment options that apply to your needs.
* **New/Used** (new_employees_equipment_form_page_21, new_employees_equipment_form_page_23): Select whether the equipment is new or used.

## Tips

* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Check for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.
* Be specific when entering the equipment type, equipment description, and equipment options. 
* Enter your department and employee name accurately.
* Be aware that the form is for new employees; if you have questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative. 
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact your department or HR representative for assistance.
