# seasonal_worker_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information regarding a seasonal worker's time off. It is used to gather details such as the worker's period of work, start and end dates, reason for time off, manager's confirmation, team leader's confirmation, employee's status, and leave type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the seasonal worker's name and work period in the respective fields.
2. Select the start and end dates of the time off period.
3. Provide a reason for the time off.
4. Confirm whether the manager and team leader are aware of the time off.
5. Select the status of the employee during the time off period (Active or Inactive).
6. Choose the type of leave (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **seasonal_worker**: Enter the seasonal worker's name.
* **work_period**: Enter the worker's work period.
* **start_date**: Select the start date of the time off period using the date picker input field.
* **end_date**: Select the end date of the time off period using the date picker input field.
* **reason**: Enter the reason for the time off.
* **manager**: Confirm if the manager is aware of the time off (Yes or No).
* **team**: Confirm if the team leader is aware of the time off (Yes or No).
* **employee**: Select which employees are on time off (Yes or No).
* **type**: Choose the type of leave (Yes or No).
* **status**: Select the status of the employee during the time off period (Active or Inactive).
