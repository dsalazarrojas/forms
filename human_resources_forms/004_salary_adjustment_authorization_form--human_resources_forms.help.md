# Salary Adjustment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salary Adjustment Authorization Form is used to request a salary adjustment, which is an increase or decrease in an employee's salary. This form is typically used for legitimate requests and may be required by HR or management for processing such requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your company's name.
*   Provide your name as it appears on your official records.
*   Enter your position or job title.
*   Briefly explain the reason for the requested salary adjustment.
*   Enter the exact amount of the adjustment you are requesting.
*   Enter the date when the adjustment will take effect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name where you work.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Type your official name as it appears on your records.
*   **Position** (`position`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
*   **Reason for Adjustment** (`reason_for_adjustment`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation for the requested salary adjustment.
*   **Adjustment Amount** (`adjustment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the exact amount of the salary adjustment you are requesting.
*   **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the adjustment will take effect.

## Tips

*   Be honest and provide a valid reason for the requested salary adjustment.
*   Ensure the amount you request is reasonable and follows company policies.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Submitting duplicate or illegitimate requests may result in delays or denial of your request.
