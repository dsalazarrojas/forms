# Staff Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Appraisal Form is a performance evaluation tool used to assess an employee's job performance, objectives, and growth areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Upload Job Description**: Please upload a copy of your job description, which will serve as a reference for your performance evaluation.
2. **Objectives**: Describe your job objectives and targets for the evaluation period.
3. **Evaluation**: Provide a detailed evaluation of your performance, highlighting areas of strength and weakness.
4. **Tasks**: List any notable tasks you completed during the evaluation period.
5. **Achievements**: Describe any outstanding achievements or accomplishments that demonstrate your growth and contributions.
6. **Failures**: Identify any areas where you fell short of your objectives or targets.
7. **Expectations**: Outline your expectations for the upcoming evaluation period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Job Description**: Upload a copy of your job description, which should include your job title, responsibilities, and key performance indicators.
* **Objectives**: Describe your job objectives and targets for the evaluation period, including specific, measurable, and achievable goals.
* **Evaluation**: Provide a detailed evaluation of your performance, using the following format: "Strengths: [list of strengths], Weaknesses: [list of weaknesses], Opportunities: [list of opportunities], and Threats: [list of threats]".
* **Tasks**: List any notable tasks you completed during the evaluation period, including projects, initiatives, or special assignments.
* **Achievements**: Describe any outstanding achievements or accomplishments that demonstrate your growth and contributions, such as awards, certifications, or promotions.
* **Failures**: Identify any areas where you fell short of your objectives or targets, including lessons learned and areas for improvement.
* **Expectations**: Outline your expectations for the upcoming evaluation period, including specific, measurable, and achievable goals.
