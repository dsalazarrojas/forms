# interview_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "interview_questionnaire" form is used to collect information from candidates during an interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the form accurately and completely.
3. Provide all required fields (indicated by a * in the field label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Personal details
* **Personal details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): This section is for providing personal details about the candidate.
* **Contact details**
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This section is for providing contact information such as email and phone numbers.
* **Answers**
* **Answers** (`answers`, text, optional): This section is for providing answers to questions about the candidate's motivation and suitability for a job.
* **Motivation**
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, optional): This section is for explaining the candidate's motivation for applying for the job.
* **Suitability**
* **Suitability** (`suitability`, text, optional): This section is for discussing the candidate's suitability for the job.
* **Name**
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This section is for providing the candidate's name.
* **Surname**
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, optional): This section is for providing the candidate's surname.
* **Email**
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This section is for providing the candidate's email address.
* **Phone**
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This section is for providing the candidate's phone number.
* **Date of birth**
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This section is for providing the candidate's date of birth.
* **Job role**
* **Job role** (`job_role`, text, optional): This section is for selecting the job role the candidate is applying for.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that you have provided all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your interviewer for clarification.
