# worker_feedback_summary_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **worker_feedback_summary_request_form** is designed to collect feedback from workers on specific topics, allowing managers and administrators to gather valuable information and insights. This form is typically used to facilitate employee feedback, performance evaluations, or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the purpose of this form**: Choose the reason for requesting feedback from the worker. You can select either "For employee feedback" or "For performance evaluation".
2. **Choose how the information will be used**: Select how the collected feedback will be used, such as "To improve employee experience" or "To identify areas for improvement".
3. **Specify the best time to send the form**: Choose the best time for sending the form, considering the worker's schedule and availability.
4. **Choose how to contact the worker**: Select the preferred method of contact, such as email or phone call.
5. **Specify how the worker can be contacted**: Enter the contact method of choice.
6. **Rate the form usefulness**: Evaluate whether the form is helpful or not.
7. **Additional comments**: Enter any additional thoughts or suggestions the worker may have.
8. **Form title and description**: Enter a title and description of the form, as required.
9. **Form category and ID**: Enter the category and ID of the form, as required.
10. **Form assigned tool and version**: Enter the tool and version of the form, as required.
11. **Form output file and modified date**: Enter the output file and modified date of the form, as required.
12. **Form modified by and at**: Enter the person who modified the form and the date and time it was modified, as required.
13. **Form created date and by**: Enter the date and time the form was created and the person who created it, as required.
14. **Form created by user**: Enter the user who created the form, as required.
