# Casual Day Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help employees participate in Casual Day events within the company. It gathers information about each participant's employee details, dress code understanding, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Employee ID** in the number field.
3. Select your **Department** from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your **Email Address** in the email field.
5. Select the **Casual Day Date** of the event from the calendar.
6. Confirm your **Participation Status** by selecting "Yes", "No", or "Maybe" from the dropdown menu.
7. Confirm that you understand the **Dress Code** by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
8. If you're participating, select the **Outfit Theme** from the dropdown menu (if applicable).
9. If you're bringing snacks to share, select "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
10. If you're bringing snacks, provide more details in the text field.
11. If you have any **Dietary Restrictions**, enter them in the text field.
12. If you'd like to **Volunteer to Help Organize** future events, select "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
13. Confirm your **Photo Consent** by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
14. If you have any **Suggestions for Future Events**, enter them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`): Select your department from the dropdown menu.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your company email address.
* **Casual Day Date** (`event_date`): Select the date of the Casual Day event from the calendar.
* **Will You Participate** (`participation_status`): Confirm your participation status by selecting "Yes", "No", or "Maybe" from the dropdown menu.
* **Will You Participate** (`participation_status`) explanation: Confirm your participation status.
* **Dress Code Acknowledgment** (`dress_code_acknowledgment`): Confirm that you understand the dress code by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
* **Outfit Theme** (`outfit_theme`): If participating, select the outfit theme from the dropdown menu.
* **Will You Bring Snacks** (`bringing_snacks`): If you're bringing snacks, confirm by selecting "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
* **Snack Details** (`snack_details`): If you're bringing snacks, provide more details in the text field.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them in the text field.
* **Volunteer to Help Organize** (`volunteer_help`): If you'd like to help organize future events, confirm by selecting "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
* **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`): Confirm your photo consent by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
* **Suggestions for Future Events** (`feedback_suggestions`): If you have any suggestions for future events, enter them in the text field.
