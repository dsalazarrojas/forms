# Employment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Intake Form is a document used to collect personal and employment-related information about an individual. It's intended to be completed by current or potential employees for HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all necessary information about yourself before starting the form.
- Start by entering your personal details, including your first, middle, and last names.
- Provide your date of birth and age (if applicable).
- Select your current education level and the institution you attended.
- Specify your work history, including your employment status (active, passive, or un-employed) and the type of employment you have (W2, contract, or freelance).
- Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
- Optionally, provide your job title and work experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (personal_info)**: This field is used to capture your first name.
* **Middle Name (middle_name)**: This field is used to capture your middle name.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: This field is used to capture your last name.
* **Date Of Birth (date_of_birth)**: This field is used to capture your date of birth. Please enter it in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age (age)**: This field is used to capture your age (if applicable).
* **Education Level (education_level)**: This field is used to select your highest education level attained. Select from the available options: High School, Bachelor, Master, Doctoral, or Post-Graduate.
* **Education Institution (education_institution)**: This field is used to capture the name of the institution where you attained your highest education level.
* **Work History (work_history)**: This field is used to select your current employment status and type. You can select multiple options from the available choices: Intern, Part-time, Full-time, Active, Passive, Un-employed, W2, Contract, or Freelance.
* **Address (address)**: This field is used to capture your contact address.
* **Email (email)**: This field is used to capture your email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: This field is used to capture your phone number.
* **Employment Status (employment_status)**: This field is used to select your current employment status. Select from the available options: Active, Passive, or Un-employed.
* **Employment Type (employment_type)**: This field is used to select your type of employment. Choose from the available options: W2, Contract, or Freelance.
* **Job Title (job_title)**: This field is used to capture your job title (if applicable).
* **Work Experience (work_experience)**: This field is used to capture any relevant work experience you have had (if applicable).
