# Employee Happiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Happiness Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from employees about their job satisfaction, happiness, and overall well-being. This data will help management identify areas of improvement and implement policies to create a positive work environment and increase employee engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information, including your name and department.
2. Provide your manager's name.
3. Select your job title and job description.
4. Choose your job grade (A, B, or C).
5. Enter your job status (Active or Inactive).
6. Select your work location and job type (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
7. Choose your job level (Junior, Senior, or Manager).
8. Select your team and team member (if applicable).
9. Provide your job title 2 and job status 2.
10. Enter your job description 2 (if applicable).
11. Enter your job title 3 and job status 3.
12. Select your job type (Yes or No).
13. Choose your work status (Active or Inactive).
14. Select your job level (Junior, Senior, or Manager).
15. Select your team (if applicable).
16. Enter your job type 2 (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Text): Enter your name and department.
* **Manager Name** (Text): Provide your manager's name.
* **Department** (Select One): Choose your department (Marketing, Sales, or Human Resources).
* **Employee Name** (Text): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (Select Multiple): Select your job titles (Yes or No).
* **Job Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Job Grade** (Select One): Choose your job grade (A, B, or C).
* **Prophets** (Text): Not applicable.
* **Work Location** (Select Multiple): Select your work location (Yes or No).
* **Job Level** (Select One): Choose your job level (Junior, Senior, or Manager).
* **Work Type** (Select One): Choose your job type (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
* **Satisfaction** (Number): Not applicable.
* **Happiness** (Select Multiple): Select your happiness level (Yes or No).
* **Job Type** (Select One): Not applicable.
* **Work Status** (Select Multiple): Select your work status (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Status** (Select One): Choose your job status (Active or Inactive).
* **Team** (Text): Enter your team name.
* **Team Member** (Text): Not applicable.
* **Job Title 2** (Select One): Not applicable.
* **Job Status 2** (Select One): Not applicable.
* **Prophets 2** (Text): Not applicable.
* **Job Title 3** (Select One): Not applicable.
* **Job Status 3** (Select One): Not applicable.
