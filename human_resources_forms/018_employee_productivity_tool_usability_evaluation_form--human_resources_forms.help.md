# Employee Productivity Tool Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the usability of various tools within the organization, focusing on aspects such as ease of use, feasibility, and suitability. It helps identify areas for improvement and ensures that tools are meeting the needs of employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the tool being evaluated (Human Resources, Information Technology, or Marketing).
2. Enter the title of the tool being evaluated.
3. Provide a brief description of the tool.
4. Rate the tool's usability on a scale of 1-5.
5. Rate the tool's ease of use, feasibility, and suitability.
6. Indicate whether you have submitted the form or not.
7. Enter your name and department.
8. Select the departmental status of the tool (Human Resources, Information Technology, or Marketing).
9. Enter your employee rating of the tool.
10. Enter your overall rating of the tool.
11. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category**: The category of the tool being evaluated (Human Resources, Information Technology, or Marketing).
* **title**: The title of the tool being evaluated.
* **description**: A brief description of the tool.
* **tool_rating**: Rate the tool's usability on a scale of 1-5.
* **tool_ease_of_use**: Rate the tool's ease of use on a scale of 1-5.
* **tool_feasibility**: Rate the tool's feasibility on a scale of 1-5.
* **tool_suitability**: Rate the tool's suitability on a scale of 1-5.
* **submission**: Indicate whether you have submitted the form or not.
* **name**: Enter your name and department.
* **departmental**: Select the departmental status of the tool (Human Resources, Information Technology, or Marketing).
* **form_status**: Select the submission status of the form (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
* **employee_rating**: Enter your employee rating of the tool.
* **rating**: Enter your overall rating of the tool.
* **form_comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when rating the tool's usability and ease of use.
* Use the provided options for each field, as they are designed to help us understand your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Form Comments" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the provided options or ask your supervisor for clarification.
