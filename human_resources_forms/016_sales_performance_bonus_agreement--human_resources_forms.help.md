Sales Performance Bonus Agreement - Help Guide
=====================================

### Purpose

This form is used to document and track sales performance bonuses for sales representatives. It captures information about the company, sales team, sales representative, and bonus achievement details. The purpose of this form is to standardize the process of calculating and recording sales bonuses for each representative, ensuring accuracy and transparency in the bonus calculation process.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company" field.
2. Select the sales team for the representative in the "Sales Team" field.
3. Enter the sales representative's name and ID in the "Sales Representative Name" and "Sales Representative ID" fields.
4. Set the bonus achievement criteria and amount in the "Bonus Criteria" field.
5. Choose the sales representative's performance period (e.g., Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4) from the dropdown menu.
6. If applicable, enter the currency of the bonus achievement in the "Bonus Achievement Currency" field.
7. Repeat steps 3-6 for each sales representative listed in the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (1, text, required): Enter the company name.
* **Sales Team** (2, text, required): Select the sales team for the representative.
* **Sales Representative Name** (3, text, required): Enter the sales representative's name.
* **Sales Representative ID** (4, text, required): Enter the sales representative's ID.
* **Bonus Criteria** (5, text, optional): Enter the bonus achievement criteria.
* **Bonus Achievement** (7, text, required): Enter the bonus achievement amount.
* **Bonus Achievement Percentage** (8, number, required): Enter the bonus achievement percentage.
* **Bonus Achievement Currency** (9, text, optional): Enter the bonus achievement currency (e.g., USD, EUR).
* **Sales Representative Manager** (11, text, optional): Enter the sales representative's manager's name.
* **Sales Representative Manager** (15, text, optional): Enter the second sales representative's manager's name.
* **Performance Period** (10, select_one, required): Choose the sales representative's performance period.
* **Performance Period** (17, select_one, optional): Choose the second sales representative's performance period.
* **Bonus Achievement Currency** (12, text, optional): Enter the bonus achievement currency for the second representative.
* **Bonus Achievement Currency** (14, text, optional): Enter the bonus achievement currency for the third representative.
* **Sales Representative Name** (13, text, optional): Enter the second sales representative's name.
* **Sales Representative ID** (16, text, optional): Enter the second sales representative's ID.
* **Bonus Criteria** (19, text, optional): Enter the bonus criteria for the second representative.
* **Bonus Criteria** (20, text, optional): Enter the bonus criteria for the third representative.
* **Sales Representative ID** (23, text, optional): Enter the second sales representative's ID.
* **Sales Representative Manager** (22, text, optional): Enter the second sales representative's manager's name.
* **Performance Period** (24, select_one, optional): Choose the performance period for the third representative.
* **Bonus Achievement Currency** (25, text, optional): Enter the bonus achievement currency for the third representative.
