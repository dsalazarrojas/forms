# Online Exit Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. It's a crucial tool for understanding the reasons behind their departure and for improving the work environment and policies of the organization. This form helps in recording the employee's experience, feedback, and suggestions for future development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your details, including your first name, last name, email address, and other relevant information.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for departing the company.
3. Mention your job title, company, and the manager with whom you worked directly.
4. Specify how long you were employed by the company.
5. If applicable, enter the last date of your employment with the company.
6. Share any comments you have about your experience and any suggestions you might have.
7. Finally, provide any feedback or comments to the manager or anyone else who might be involved in the exit process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, Optional): Enter your first name to complete your details in the form.
* **Last Name** (text, Optional): Enter your last name to complete your details in the form.
* **Email Address** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address, which will help us get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Reason For Departing** (text, Optional): Provide a clear and concise reason for leaving the company. This is an opportunity for you to express your thoughts and feelings about your time with the company.
* **Job Title** (text, Optional): Enter your job title or the title you held during your employment with the company.
* **Company** (text, Optional): Enter the name of the company you worked for.
* **Manager Name** (text, Optional): Mention the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Reason For Leaving** (text, Optional): Provide more details about your reason for leaving, if you feel they are not already covered in your "Reason For Departing" field.
* **What Would Have Kept** (text, Optional): This field is for any suggestions or ideas you might have about what could have kept you with the company longer.
* **How Long Employed** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of years or months you were employed by the company.
* **Last Date Of Employment** (Date, Optional): If applicable, enter the last date you worked for the company.
* **Manager Comments** (text, Optional): This field is for any comments or feedback from your manager during the exit process.
* **Employee Comments** (text, Optional): Share any thoughts, suggestions, or feedback about your experience with the company.
* **Exit Interview Conducted By** (text, Optional): If applicable, enter the person or team that conducted the exit interview with you.

**Tips**

- Please be as honest and clear as possible when filling out the form.
- Use proper grammar and spelling for better understanding.
- If you have any attachments or additional information that could be relevant, feel free to share them with the exit interview team.
- The information collected will be kept confidential and used for the purpose of improving the company and its work environment.
