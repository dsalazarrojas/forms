# employee_referral_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about an employee referred by another employee for internal job openings or promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the fields with the correct information about the employee being referred and the referring employee.
2. Ensure the employee's name and job title are accurate.
3. Choose the correct options for the referred date and manager's referred date.
