# Mental Health Treatment Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Treatment Time Off Form is used to report a mental health treatment time off request. It ensures that employees have the necessary documentation to support their claim for time off related to mental health treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a detailed description of your mental health treatment in the mental health treatment request field.
2. Select the mental health condition that best describes your treatment.
3. Enter the number of days you are requesting for your treatment in the treatment duration field.
4. Select the start and end dates for your treatment in the start and end date fields.
5. Enter the name of your doctor or mental health professional in the doctor's name field.
6. Provide the location where you received your mental health treatment in the treatment location field.
7. In the treatment plan details field, please provide any additional information about your treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mental Health Treatment Request** (`mental_health_treatment_request`, `text`, optional): Please provide a detailed description of your mental health treatment.
* **Mental Health Condition** (`mental_health_condition`, `select_one`, required): Select the mental health condition that best describes your treatment. This field is required because it helps to ensure that your request is properly processed.
* **Treatment Duration** (`treatment_duration_days`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of days you are requesting for your treatment. Please note that this is optional and will be used for planning purposes only.
* **Treatment Start Date** (`treatment_start_date`, `date`, optional): Select the start date of your mental health treatment. This field is optional but helps to ensure that your request is properly recorded.
* **Treatment End Date** (`treatment_end_date`, `date`, optional): Select the end date of your mental health treatment. This field is optional but helps to ensure that your request is properly recorded.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your doctor or mental health professional.
* **Treatment Location** (`treatment_location`, `text`, optional): Provide the location where you received your mental health treatment.
* **Treatment Plan Details** (`mental_health_treatment_plan`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information about your treatment plan. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have completed this form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact your HR representative.
* If you have already submitted this form for the same reason in the past, please do not submit again as it will be considered as duplication.
