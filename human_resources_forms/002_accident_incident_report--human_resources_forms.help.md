# Accident Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accident Incident Report form is used to document and track workplace accidents or incidents. It gathers information about the incident, its severity, and recommended follow-up actions to prevent recurrence.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Date of incident**: Enter the date when the incident occurred. Please use the format: dd/mm/yyyy.
2. **Time of incident**: Enter the approximate time of the incident, or leave blank if unknown.
3. **Location of incident**: Provide the building and area or address where the incident occurred.
4. **Person reporting the incident**: Enter your full name as the person reporting the incident.
5. **Reporter contact information**: Provide your phone number or email for follow-up purposes.
6. **Persons involved in incident**: List the names and roles of all individuals involved in the incident.
7. **Witness information**: Provide names and contact details of any witnesses.
8. **Description of event**: Give a clear and factual description of what happened during the incident.
9. **Injuries sustained**: Describe any injuries sustained during the incident, specifying affected body parts.
10. **Immediate actions taken**: Record any first aid, emergency response, or notifications made during or after the incident.
11. **Property damage description**: Describe any property or equipment damaged during the incident.
12. **Reported to authorities**: Indicate if external authorities (e.g., police, fire department) were notified.
13. **Incident severity rating**: Choose the best description of the incident's severity from Minor, Moderate, Serious, or Critical.
14. **Follow-up actions required**: Suggest any recommended actions to prevent similar incidents in the future.
15. **Supervisor comments**: Include any comments or guidance from your supervisor or HR.
16. **Reporter signature**: Type your full name as an electronic signature.
