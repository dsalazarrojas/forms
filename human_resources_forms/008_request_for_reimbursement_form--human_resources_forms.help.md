# Request For Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Request For Reimbursement Form is used by employees to submit requests for reimbursement for work-related expenses. To be eligible for reimbursement, the expense must be approved by management and meet company policy guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **Employee Name** in the "Employee Name" field. This field is required to ensure that the request is attributed to the correct person.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This field is required to ensure that you can be contacted regarding your reimbursement request.
3. Select your **Department** from the options provided in the "Department" field.
4. Enter the **Date** of the expense in the "Date" field.
5. Provide a detailed **Description** of the expense in the "Description" field.
6. Enter the **Expense Amount** in the "Expense Amount" field.
7. Select the **Expense Type** from the options provided (Travel, Training, or Miscellaneous) in the "Expense Type" field. This field is required to ensure that the expense is properly categorized.
8. If applicable, attach **Receipts** for the expense in the "Receipts" field.
9. Enter the **Submit Date** of the form in the "Submit Date" field.
10. Select the **Status** of your reimbursement request from the options provided (Pending, Approved, or Rejected) in the "Status" field. This field is required to ensure that the request is properly tracked.
11. Select who **Submitted By** you from the options provided (chatjimmy, other) in the "Submitted By" field. This field is required to ensure that the request is attributed to the correct person.
12. Add any **Comments** regarding your reimbursement request in the "Comments" field.
13. If applicable, add **Comments** regarding the approval status of your request in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Select your department from the options provided.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the expense.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the expense.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the expense.
* **Expense Type** (`expense_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of expense (Travel, Training, or Miscellaneous).
* **Receipts** (`receipts`, text, optional): Attach receipts for the expense.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the request.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your reimbursement request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by_user`, select_one, required): Select who submitted the request (chatjimmy, other).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments regarding your reimbursement request.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): Add any comments from the person who approved your request.
* **Reimbursed By** (`reimbursed_by`, text, optional): Add any comments from the person who reimbursed your request.
* **Reimbursement Date** (`reimbursement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the reimbursement.
* **Amount Reimbursed** (`amount_reimbursed`, number, optional): Enter the amount reimbursed.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the reimbursement.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the form (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, text, optional): Upload files regarding your request.
* **Uploaded Receipts** (`uploaded_receipts`, text, optional): Upload receipts for the expense.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments regarding the form status.

Note: All fields are case sensitive, so ensure that you enter the correct capitalization for your name and email address.
