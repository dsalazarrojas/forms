# Employee End Of Day Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee End of Day Report is a daily form that helps track employee progress and productivity by recording tasks completed and any notes from the day. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure your work is tracked and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form at the end of each day, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (this is a required field).
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (this is a required field).
6. Select your department from the provided options in the "Department" field (this is a required field).
7. Enter your current job title in the "Job Title" field (this is a required field).
8. Briefly describe the tasks you completed in the "Task Completed" field (this is a required field).
9. If you have any additional notes or comments, use the "Notes" field to record them (this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your valid email address to help track communication and updates.
* **Phone (Text, Required)**: Enter your phone number for contact and reference purposes.
* **Department (Text, Required)**: Select your department from the provided options to help track team progress and resources.
* **Job Title (Text, Required)**: Enter your current job title to help track employee hierarchy and roles.
* **Task Completed (Text, Required)**: Briefly describe the tasks you completed during the day, including any accomplishments or challenges you faced.
* **Notes (Text, Optional)**: Record any additional notes or comments about your day, such as successes, challenges, or areas for improvement.
