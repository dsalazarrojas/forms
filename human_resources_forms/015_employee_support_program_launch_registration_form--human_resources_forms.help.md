# Employee Support Program Launch Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Support Program Launch Registration Form is used to register for the Employee Support Program. This form captures essential information about the employee, their job title, and program details. It also requires the approval of the approver and reviewer, ensuring that the employee's application is reviewed and validated before being activated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the correct job title from the options provided.
3. Choose whether the team member is 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
4. Enter the program name.
5. Select the start and end dates of the program.
6. Select the program status as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
7. Provide any comments if necessary.
8. Enter the approver's and reviewer's name, email, and phone number.
9. Select the approver and reviewer status as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
10. Select the submission status as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your official phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Select your official job title from the options provided.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`): Choose whether you have team members ('Yes' or 'No') that require program support.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`): Enter the name of the program you are registering for.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`): Select the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`): Select the end date of the program.
* **Program Status** (`program_status`): Select the status of the program as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments if necessary.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name.
* **Approver** (`approver`): Enter the approver's name.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`): Enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`): Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`): Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`): Enter the reviewer's email address.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`): Enter the reviewer's phone number.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`): Provide any comments from the reviewer.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`): Provide any comments from the approver.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`): Provide any comments from the submitter.
* **Approver Status** (`approver_status`): Select the status of the approver as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Reviewer Status** (`reviewer_status`): Select the status of the reviewer as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Select the submission status as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

Please ensure that you complete all the required fields to ensure successful submission and review of your application.
