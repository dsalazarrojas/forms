## Employment Verification Form For Mortgage - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Verification Form for Mortgage is used to provide lenders with accurate and up-to-date employment information to process a mortgage application. This form is typically used by employees to verify their employment status and provide information about their job title, employer, and employment dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your Social Security Number (SSN) as it appears on your identification.
3. Enter your employer's name as it appears on your paystub or letterhead.
4. Enter your job title as it appears on your paystub or letterhead.
5. Enter the dates you have been employed by your current employer.
6. Enter your current work address as it appears on your paystub or letterhead.
7. Select your current employment status from the options provided.
8. If terminated from your current employment, enter the reason for termination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Employee SSN**: Please enter your Social Security Number (SSN) as it appears on your identification.
* **Employer Name**: Please enter your employer's name as it appears on your paystub or letterhead.
* **Job Title**: Please enter your job title as it appears on your paystub or letterhead.
* **Dates of Employment**: Please enter the dates you have been employed by your current employer.
* **Employee Address**: Please enter your current work address as it appears on your paystub or letterhead.
* **Employment Status**: Please select your current employment status from the options provided:
	+ **Active**: You are currently employed.
	+ **Inactive**: You are no longer employed but have not been terminated.
	+ **Terminated**: You have been terminated from your employment.
* **Reason for Termination**: If you have been terminated, please enter the reason for termination.
