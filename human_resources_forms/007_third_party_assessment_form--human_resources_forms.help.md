# Third Party Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from third parties who have interacted with employees or clients. It is intended to be used by assessors or raters who are asked to evaluate the performance of another individual, known as the ratee.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you are a valid rater and are authorized to complete this assessment.
3. Do not share your answers with other raters.
4. Complete each section of the form according to the instructions provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Rater** (`name_of_rater`, text, required): Please enter the name of the rater completing this form.
* **Position of Rater** (`position_of_rater`, text, required): Please enter the position or title of the rater completing this form.
* **Name of Ratee** (`name_of_ratee`, text, required): Please enter the name of the individual being assessed, known as the ratee.
* **Position of Ratee** (`position_of_ratee`, text, required): Please enter the position or title of the ratee being assessed.
* **Rater Rating** (`rater_rating`, number, required): Please enter a numerical rating from 1 to 5, indicating your overall assessment of the ratee's performance.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Please select a rating scale from 1 to 5, indicating your overall assessment of the ratee's performance.
* **Rater Comments** (`rater_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments you have about the ratee's performance.
* **Rating Date** (`rating_date`, date, required): Please enter the date on which the assessment was completed.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, required): Please enter the amount of time spent interacting with the ratee, in hours.
* **Rater Phone** (`rater_phone`, text, optional): Please enter a phone number where the rater can be reached.
* **Rater Email** (`rater_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of the rater.
* **Ratee Email** (`ratee_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of the ratee being assessed.
* **Ratee Phone** (`ratee_phone`, text, optional): Please enter a phone number for the ratee.
* **Rating Frequency** (`rating_frequency`, select_one, required): Please select how often you have interacted with the ratee, from 1 to 6 times.
* **Rating Comments** (`rating_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments you have about the ratee's performance.
* **Rater Signature** (`rater_signature`, text, optional): Please provide a signature from the rater.
* **Raters Agreement** (`raters_agreement`, text, optional): Please agree or disagree with the terms of the assessment.
* **Raters Disagreement** (`raters_disagreement`, text, optional): Please specify any reasons for disagreement with the terms of the assessment.
* **Ratee Signature** (`ratee_signature`, text, optional): Please obtain a signature from the ratee.
* **Ratee Agreement** (`ratee_agreement`, text, optional): Please obtain agreement from the ratee.
* **Ratee Disagreement** (`ratee_disagreement`, text, optional): Please obtain reasons for disagreement from the ratee.
* **Rater 1 Comment** (`rater_1_comment`, text, optional): Please provide a comment from the first rater.
* **Rater 2 Comment** (`rater_2_comment`, text, optional): Please provide a comment from the second rater.
* **Rater 3 Comment** (`rater_3_comment`, text, optional): Please provide a comment from the third rater.
* **Rating Agreement** (`rating_agreement`, text, optional): Please agree or disagree with the final rating.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from HR or management.
* Do not share your answers with other raters or ratees.
* Please complete the form as soon as possible after interacting with the ratee.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact HR or management.
