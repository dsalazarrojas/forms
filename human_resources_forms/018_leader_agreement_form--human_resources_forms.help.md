# Leader Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leader Agreement Form is a standard document for new leaders or employees to provide their details, commitments, and other essential information. It ensures a smooth onboarding process and clarifies their role and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Leader Agreement Form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the New Leader Details section, providing your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2.  In the Leader Commitments section, list your key commitments and priorities for the role.
3.  In the Responsibilities section, describe your main responsibilities and tasks.
4.  Choose the evaluation criteria that apply to your performance (optional).
5.  Provide your signature, date, and time (if applicable).
6.  Enter your official email address.
