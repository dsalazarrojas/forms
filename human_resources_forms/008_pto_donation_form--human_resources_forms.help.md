# PTO Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you donate your available PTO hours to a colleague in need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, email, and the name and email of the colleague you want to donate to.
2. Enter the amount of PTO hours you want to donate.
3. Provide a reason for the donation (e.g., "Support for a family emergency").
4. Enter your department and job title for reference.
5. Submit the form and await approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the person donating the PTO hours.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Donation Amount** (`amount`, number, not required): Enter the number of PTO hours you want to donate.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the colleague you want to donate the PTO hours to.
* **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the colleague you want to donate the PTO hours to.
* **Reason for Donation** (`reason`, text, not required): Enter a brief explanation for the donation.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Approval Message** (`approval_message`, text, not required): This field is for the person approving the donation to enter a message.
* **Rejection Message** (`rejection_message`, text, not required): This field is for the person rejecting the donation to enter a message.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the donation (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, not required): Enter the department of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, not required): Enter the job title of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Job Function** (`submitter_job_function`, text, not required): Enter the job function of the person submitting the form.
* **Recipient Department** (`recipient_department`, text, not required): Enter the department of the colleague you want to donate the PTO hours to.
