# mou_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mou_request form is a crucial tool for the union to gather information about employees and their work preferences for Management of Union (MOU) purposes. It helps the union understand employees' needs and make informed decisions to improve work conditions and employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your Employee MOU Request form carefully, making sure to select the right options for each field.
2.  Provide accurate information, especially for fields like Date of Birth, Employee ID, and Contact Info.
3.  If you have a supervisor or manager, ensure to select the correct options for their presence and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee MOU Request** (`employee_mou_request`, `text`, required: false): This field is the main description of your request or concern. Please write your specific request or concern regarding the union here.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification or as you remember it. Ensure it is in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **Department** (`select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you want to be part of the union, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **Job Title** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you hold this job title, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **Supervisor** (`select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have a supervisor, and select their response.
*   **Manager** (`select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have a manager, and select their response.
*   **Location** (`text`, required: false): Enter the location where you work or are available to work.
*   **Employee ID** (`text`, required: false): Enter your Employee ID number as it appears on your identification.
*   **Job Classification** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the correct classification for your role or job type.
*   **Shift** (`time`, required: false): Enter your working shift or available shift in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*   **Contact Info** (`text`, required: false): Add any other contact information that may be helpful for the union to contact you.

## Tips

*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification, ask your HR representative for assistance.
*   Remember that this form is for your union, and your responses will help improve your work experience. 

Please review and complete the form carefully, ensuring all fields have correct information. If unsure, don't hesitate to ask your HR representative for support.
