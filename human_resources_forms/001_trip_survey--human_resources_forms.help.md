# Trip Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trip Survey form is designed to collect information about a trip, including travel details, dates, duration, transportation, and other relevant details. This form is to be filled out by travelers to provide accurate and helpful information for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Trip Survey form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form as instructed below.
2. Enter your travel information in the corresponding fields.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Destination** (`travel_details`): Enter the location of your travel destination.
* **Trip Dates** (`travel_dates`): Enter the start and end dates of your trip in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Trip Duration** (`travel_duration`): Enter the number of days for your trip. This field is required.
* **Transport** (`mode_of_transportation`): Select one of the following options: Train, Car, Bus, or Plane.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`): Select all that apply from the options provided: Business, Personal, or Family. (This field is optional)
* **Travel Currency** (`travel_currency`): Select one of the following options: USD, EUR, or JPY. (This field is required)
* **Other Currency** (`travel_currency_other`): If your travel currency is not listed, enter the name of the currency you are using.
* **Other Currency Description** (`travel_currency_other_description`): Provide a brief description of the other currency you selected.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select all that apply from the options provided: chatjimmy or Other. (This field is optional)
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your trip.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the form's creator or support team for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, and your information will be used to improve travel-related processes and services.
