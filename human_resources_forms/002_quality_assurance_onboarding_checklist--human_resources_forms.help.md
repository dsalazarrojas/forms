### Quality Assurance Onboarding Checklist - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The Quality Assurance Onboarding Checklist is a form designed to ensure new hires or existing employees have the necessary knowledge and skills to perform their quality assurance roles and responsibilities effectively. This form is used during the onboarding process to gather information about the employee's readiness and identify potential knowledge gaps.

#### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the steps from the quality assurance process that you are ready to take on. You can select multiple options by checking the boxes next to the steps.
2. Choose one of the options provided for the "New Hire Ready" field.
3. In the "Internal Processes" field, provide a brief description of your understanding of our internal processes and how they affect your role.
4. If you have any comments or notes about your readiness, please type them in the "Submission Notes" field.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **QA Steps 1** (`qa_steps_1`, Select Multiple, Required): This field allows you to indicate which steps from the quality assurance process you are ready to take on. Please select all the steps that apply to your role.
* **New Hire Ready** (`new_hire_ready`, Select One, Required): This field asks you to choose one of the options provided to indicate your level of readiness to take on new hire responsibilities.
* **Internal Processes** (`internal_processes`, Text, Optional): In this field, provide a brief description of your understanding of our internal processes and how they affect your role. This will help us assess your knowledge and identify any potential knowledge gaps.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, Note, Optional): If you have any comments or notes about your readiness, please type them in this field.

#### Tips
- Please be honest about your knowledge and skills gaps.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the quality assurance team.
- This form is a one-time requirement during the onboarding process, and it's not intended to be used for ongoing monitoring or evaluation purposes.
