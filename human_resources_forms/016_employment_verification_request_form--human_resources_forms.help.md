# Employment Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Verification Request Form is used to request verification of employment details for an employee. It provides an overview of the employee's job title, department, and employment status, as well as the reason for the request. This form is used to gather information for external agencies or services that require employment verification.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the employee's details, such as their job title, department, and employment status.
*   Select the reason for the request, such as for external agencies or services.
*   Sign and date the form as the authorized representative.
*   Add any comments or notes as necessary.
*   Submit the form to the designated person or agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company for which the employee works.
*   **Employee Title**: Choose the employee's job title from the provided options (Director, Manager, Employee).
*   **Employee Name**: Select the name of the employee being requested.
*   **Employee Start Date**: Enter the date the employee started working for the company.
*   **Department**: Enter the department the employee works in.
*   **Job Title**: Enter the employee's current job title.
*   **Employment Status**: Choose the current employment status of the employee (Active, Inactive, Unknown).
*   **Reason For Request**: Enter the reason why the employment verification is being requested.
*   **Authorized Signature**: This field is for the authorized representative to sign and date the request.
*   **Signature Date**: The date of the authorized representative's signature.
*   **Submitter Name**: The name of the person submitting the request.
*   **Submitter Title**: The title of the person submitting the request.
*   **Submitter Signature**: The signature of the person submitting the request.
*   **Submitter Date**: The date the request was submitted.
*   **Verification Requester**: The name of the person or agency requesting verification.
*   **Verification Date**: The date the verification request was made.
*   **Request Status**: Choose the current status of the request (Pending, Processing, Complete, Cancelled).
*   **Request Type**: Choose the type of request (Employment Verification, Background Check, Other).
*   **Verification Method**: Choose the method of verification (Email, Postal Mail, In-Person).
*   **Verification Status**: Choose the current status of the verification (Complete, Partial, Not Verified).
*   **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes can be added here.
*   **Submitted By**: The name of the person submitting the request.
*   **Submitted Date**: The date the request was submitted.
*   **Updated By**: The name of the person updating the request.
*   **Updated Date**: The date the request was updated.
