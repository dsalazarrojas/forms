# pre-employment-screening-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-employment screening consent form is a document that you are required to fill out as part of the hiring process. It is used to gather information about your background, employment history, and other personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background checks**: This is a background check to see if you are who you claim to be.
	+ This section is used to verify your identity and check your background.
	+ Please answer "Yes" if you have had a background check within the last 6 years.
	+ Please answer "No" if you have not had a background check within the last 6 years.
* **Consent to Share Information**: This is a section where you give consent to share your personal information with the company.
	+ Please check "Yes" if you are willing to share your information with the company.
	+ Please check "No" if you do not wish to share your information with the company.
* **Background Checks Within 6 Years**: This is a background check to see if you are who you claim to be.
	+ Please answer "Yes" if you have had a background check within the last 6 years.
	+ Please answer "No" if you have not had a background check within the last 6 years.
* **Date of Birth**: This is your date of birth.
	+ Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: This is your address.
	+ Please enter your current address.
* **Social Security Number**: This is your social security number.
	+ Please enter your social security number.
* **Driver's License Number**: This is your driver's license number.
	+ Please enter your driver's license number.
* **Education and Work History**: This is a section where you provide information about your education and work history.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your education and work experience.
* **Employment History**: This is a section where you provide information about your previous employment.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your employment history.
* **Criminal Convictions**: This is a section where you disclose any criminal convictions.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your criminal convictions.
* **Military Service**: This is a section where you provide information about your military service.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your military service.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: This is a section where you provide information about your emergency contact.
	+ Please provide the name, relationship, phone number, address, and email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact**: This is a section where you select which contact information you are willing to share.
	+ Please select "Name", "Relationship", "Phone", "Address", or "Email" to share with the company.
* **Emergency Telephone Number**: This is the phone number of your emergency contact.
	+ Please enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Address**: This is the address of your emergency contact.
	+ Please enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Medical Conditions**: This is a section where you disclose any medical conditions.
	+ Please provide a brief description of any medical conditions.
* **Medical Provider Information**: This is a section where you provide information about your medical provider.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your medical provider information.
* **Emergency Contact E-mail**: This is the email of your emergency contact.
	+ Please enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Date of Last Day of Work**: This is the date you last worked.
	+ Please enter the date you last worked.
* **Termination Date**: This is the date you were terminated.
	+ Please enter the date you were terminated.
* **Employment Start Date**: This is the date you started your current employment.
	+ Please enter the date you started your current employment.
* **Reason for Leaving**: This is a section where you explain why you left your previous employment.
	+ Please provide a brief description of why you left your previous employment.
* **Job Title**: This is your current job title.
	+ Please enter your current job title.
* **Department**: This is the department you work in.
	+ Please enter the department you work in.
* **Supervisor Name**: This is the name of your supervisor.
	+ Please enter the name of your supervisor.
