# Retail Employee Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Employee Onboarding Form is used to collect necessary information from new retail employees to facilitate their onboarding process and ensure a smooth transition into their new role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately for each new retail employee.
2. Answer each question carefully, ensuring that the information provided is true and up-to-date.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the new retail employee.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the new retail employee.
* **Store Number** (`store_number`, text, required): Enter the store number where the new retail employee will be working.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the new retail employee started their job.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the new retail employee's manager.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, select_one, required): Select the store where the new retail employee will be working from the available options.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required): Select the roles or responsibilities of the new retail employee from the available options.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, required): Enter the store's phone number where the new retail employee will be working.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the new retail employee's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select the relationship between the new retail employee and their emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the new retail employee's emergency contact.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, number, required): Enter a unique employee number for the new retail employee.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the new retail employee.
* **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, select_multiple, required): Select the job performance level of the new retail employee from the available options.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the new retail employee's manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the new retail employee's manager.
* **Shift Start** (`shift_start`, date, required): Enter the date the new retail employee's shift started.
* **Shift End** (`shift_end`, date, required): Enter the date the new retail employee's shift ended.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it for approval.
