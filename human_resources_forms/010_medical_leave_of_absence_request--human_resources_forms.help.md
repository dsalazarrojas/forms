# Medical Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a standardized process for employees to submit medical leave requests. This form collects employee and manager information, and is used to track and process employee requests for medical leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are submitting the form for a genuine medical reason and that you have not already submitted a form for the same reason.
2. Fill in your employee ID and name.
3. Enter your email address and phone number.
4. Enter your manager's ID and name.
5. Enter your manager's email address and phone number.
6. Enter the name and phone number of your doctor.
7. Enter your doctor's license number.
8. Enter the specialty or department your doctor is from.
9. Provide a brief reason for your request.
10. Enter the start and end dates for your leave.
11. Specify the duration of your leave.
12. Check the box if you are requesting leave.
13. Check the boxes for your status (Active or Inactive).
14. Add any additional notes.
15. Sign and date your form.
16. Have your manager sign and date their form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your ID number as assigned by HR or your department.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card or HR records.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your manager's ID number as assigned by HR or your department.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your manager's full name as it appears on their ID card or HR records.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your manager's work email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your manager's work phone number.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your doctor's name as they appear on their ID card or medical credentials.
* **Doctor License** (`doctor_license`, `text`, required: false): Enter your doctor's license number as issued by the relevant medical authority.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, `text`, required: false): Enter the medical group or department your doctor is from.
* **Medical Group ID** (`medical_group_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID number of the medical group or department your doctor is from.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, `text`, required: false): Enter your doctor's specialty or department.
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, `text`, required: false): Enter any medical certification you hold.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for the reason you are requesting leave.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date for your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date for your leave.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `text`, required: false): Specify the duration of your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Check the box if you are requesting leave.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes for your status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required: false): Have you sign and date your form.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `text`, required: false): Have your manager sign and date their form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you submit this form with a valid reason for your leave.
* Please ensure that you sign and date your form.
* Please have your manager sign and date their form.
* Please keep your form in a secure location to protect employee information.
* Please review this form carefully to ensure all information is complete and accurate.
