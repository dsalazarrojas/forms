# Executive Role Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Role Employment Form is a document that captures key information about executive roles within your organization. It is used by the HR team to track and maintain up-to-date executive role data.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you are filling out this form for your own executive role employment.
- If you are filling this form on behalf of another person, please fill it out with their information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* First Name: Enter your first name (e.g., John)

### Email
* Email: Enter your email address associated with your executive role (e.g., [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com))

### Job Title
* Job Title: Enter your executive role title (e.g., CEO)

### Resume
* Resume: A link or uploaded file with your resume or CV.

### Job description
* Job description: Briefly describe your role and responsibilities (e.g., "Oversee the overall strategy and direction of the company.")

### Years of Experience
* Years of Experience: Enter the number of years you have been in your current executive role.

### Work Status
* Work Status: Select the status of your employment (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, Other)

### Available to start
* Available to start: Enter the date you are available to start in your new role or position.

### Skills
* Skills: List relevant skills and qualifications that you possess.

Note: Fields with a type of "text" can be used for longer-form answers or descriptions.
