# lifestyle survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive lifestyle survey designed to gather detailed information about an individual's personal and professional life. It is likely used for a specific recruitment or hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Answer the Personal History question to describe any relevant personal experiences.
4. Select your Family Background, Education, Work Experience, and Employment Status from the provided options.
5. Answer the References question to confirm availability of references.
6. Select your Employment Status, Job Type, Job Title, and Years In Job for your current employment.
7. Answer the Job Satisfaction, Work Life Balance, and Career Growth questions to gauge your job satisfaction and growth prospects.
8. Repeat steps 4-7 for the second section of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your work or personal email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Personal History** (5, note, required): Describe any personal experiences or history that may be relevant to your professional life.
* **Family Background** (6, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have family responsibilities.
* **Education** (7, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Work Experience** (8, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your work experience.
* **References** (9, select_one, required): Select 'Available' or 'Unavaiable' to confirm availability of references.
* **Employment Status** (10, select_one, required): Select your current employment status.
* **Job Type** (11, select_multiple, required): Select your job type.
* **Job Title** (14, select_one, required): Select your current job title.
* **Years In Job** (15, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been in your current job.
* **Job Satisfaction** (16, number, required): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale.
* **Work Life Balance** (17, number, required): Rate your work life balance on a scale.
* **Career Growth** (18, number, required): Rate your career growth on a scale.
* **Job Type** (19, select_multiple, required): Select your job type.
* **Job Status** (20, select_one, required): Select your job status.
* **Employment Status 2** (21, select_one, required): Select your employment status.
* **Job Title 2** (22, select_one, required): Select your job title.
* **Years In Job 2** (23, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been in your second job.
* **Job Satisfaction 2** (24, number, required): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale.
* **Work Life Balance 2** (25, number, required): Rate your work life balance on a scale.
* **Career Growth 2** (26, number, required): Rate your career growth on a scale.
* **Education 2** (27, select_multiple, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **References 2** (28, select_one, required): Select 'Available' or 'Unavaiable' to confirm availability of references.
