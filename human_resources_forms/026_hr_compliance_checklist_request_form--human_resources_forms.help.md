# HR Compliance Checklist Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Compliance Checklist Request Form is used to track the HR compliance status of employees. This form helps HR personnel to review and approve the compliance status of employees, ensuring that the company is in compliance with various regulations and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Employee ID** field with the employee's ID number.
2.  Enter the **Company Name** where the employee works.
3.  Select the **Compliance Date** when the employee was last checked for compliance.
4.  Enter the **Policy Version** number.
5.  Enter the **Regulation Number**.
6.  Enter the **Regulation Title**.
7.  Select the **Compliance Status** from the options provided.
8.  Enter any **Compliance Notes** related to the employee's compliance status.
9.  If necessary, enter the **Reviewed By** and **Approved By** fields with the names of the personnel who reviewed and approved the compliance status.
10.  Enter the **Submitter** name and date.
11.  Enter the **Review Date** and **Approved Date** if applicable.
12.  Enter any **Comments** about the employee's compliance status.
13.  Finally, sign the **Signature** field with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Company Name**: Enter the company name where the employee works.
* **Compliance Date**: Select the date when the employee was last checked for compliance.
* **Policy Version**: Enter the policy version number.
* **Regulation Number**: Enter the regulation number.
* **Regulation Title**: Enter the regulation title.
* **Compliance Status**: Select the compliance status from the options provided.
	+ Options: In Compliance, Out of Compliance
* **Compliance Notes**: Enter any notes related to the employee's compliance status.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the employee's compliance status.
* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person who approved the employee's compliance status.
* **Prophet**: Enter the name of the prophet (this field is not clear, and its purpose is unknown, so we recommend not filling it out).
* **HR Department**: Enter the name of the HR department (this field is optional and not clearly defined, so we recommend not filling it out).
* **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date when the form was reviewed.
* **Reviewed By 2**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form (this field is not clearly defined, so we recommend not filling it out).
* **Approved By 2**: Enter the name of the person who approved the form (this field is not clearly defined, so we recommend not filling it out).
* **Approved Date**: Enter the date when the form was approved (this field is not clearly defined, so we recommend not filling it out).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments about the employee's compliance status.
* **Signature**: Sign the form with your name.
