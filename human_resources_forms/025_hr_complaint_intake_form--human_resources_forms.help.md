# HR Complaint Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Complaint Intake Form is a tool for employees to report and document complaints related to workplace issues such as bullying, harassment, and other forms of discrimination. This form is designed to help employees feel safe and supported while addressing sensitive topics, and to aid HR in efficiently resolving these complaints.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary permissions to collect sensitive information from colleagues.
2. Fill in the required fields and attach any supporting documentation as needed.
3. Sign and date your response to confirm your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Info
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing your contact information and any relevant details about your role or position within the organization.

### Complaint Type
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following categories:
	+ Bullying
	+ Harassment
	+ Racism
	+ Sexual Harassment
	+ Retaliation
	+ Age Discrimination
	+ Disability Discrimination

### Complaint Details
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a detailed description of your complaint, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant information.

### Supporting Documents
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents that are relevant to your complaint, such as witness statements, photographs, or emails.

### Resolution Action
* **Resolution Action** (`resolution_action`, text, optional): This field is for suggesting a course of action you would like to take to resolve your complaint.

### Employee Signature
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date this form to confirm that you are the person submitting the complaint.

### Manager Signature
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, optional): Your manager will sign and date this form to confirm they have received your complaint and will review it.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your complaint.
* Attach relevant documentation and evidence to support your complaint.
* Be respectful and professional in your communication.
* Ensure you have all necessary permissions to collect sensitive information from colleagues.
* Follow your organization's HR policies and procedures for reporting and resolving complaints.
