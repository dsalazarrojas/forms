# team_bonding_initiative_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team bonding initiative application form is designed to gather information about team members' preferences, requirements, and availability for team bonding activities. This helps team leaders plan and organize activities that cater to everyone's needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in sequence, one page at a time.
2. Provide accurate and honest information about your preferences and availability.
3. Please answer each question carefully, and do not leave any fields blank unless specified as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Team Bonding Initiative Application
* **Team Bonding Initiative Application** (page 1, `page_1`:): A text field where you can provide a brief introduction or any additional information about your team bonding initiative application.
* **Your Role in the Team** (page 2, `page_2`): 
  * **Team Member**: You are a regular team member.
  * **Team Lead**: You are a team lead or leader.
  * **Manager**: You are a manager.
  * **Other (please specify)**: You have a different role in the team (please specify).
* **Department/ Area of Focus** (page 3, `page_3`): Select the department or area you primarily focus on.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Application Form Page 4** (page 4, `page_4`): A note field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Available Dates** (page 5, `page_5`): 
  * Monday: You are available on Monday.
  * Tuesday: You are available on Tuesday.
  * Wednesday: You are available on Wednesday.
  * Thursday: You are available on Thursday.
  * Friday: You are available on Friday.
  * Saturday: You are available on Saturday.
  * Sunday: You are available on Sunday.
* **Previous Team Bonding Activities** (page 6, `page_6`): Enter the number of previous team bonding activities you have participated in.
* **Preferred Activities** (page 7, `page_7`): 
  * **Team Sports**: You are interested in team sports.
  * **Group Games**: You are interested in group games.
  * **Escape Room**: You are interested in escape room activities.
  * **Scavenger Hunt**: You are interested in scavenger hunt activities.
  * **Creative Workshop**: You are interested in creative workshops.
  * **Outdoor Adventure**: You are interested in outdoor adventures.
* **Availability** (page 8, `page_8`): Select your availability for team bonding activities.
* **Communication Preferences** (page 9, `page_9`): 
  * **Phone**: You prefer to communicate via phone.
  * **Email**: You prefer to communicate via email.
  * **Text**: You prefer to communicate via text.

* **Emergency Contact** (page 10, `page_10`): A note field where you can provide your emergency contact information.
* **Multi-Department Team** (page 11, `page_11`): Select if you are available to work with a team with members from different departments.
* **Previous Team Building Activities** (page 12, `page_12`): Check if you have participated in team building activities.
* **Interested Activities** (page 13, `page_13`): 
  * **Team-building Workshop**: You are interested in team-building workshops.
  * **Group Discussion**: You are interested in group discussions.
  * **Creative Projects**: You are interested in creative projects.
  * **Volunteering**: You are interested in volunteering.
  * **Team Sports**: You are interested in team sports.
  * **Team Game Night**: You are interested in team game nights.
* **Medical Conditions** (page 14, `page_14`): Check if there are any medical conditions or disabilities the team should be aware of.
* **Interested Activities** (page 15, `page_15`): Not present in original form.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your team lead or HR representative.
* This form is for team bonding initiative applications only, and any other use is not recommended.
