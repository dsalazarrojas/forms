# Holiday Recognition Awards Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit nominations for holiday recognition awards. This form is used to provide information about the achievements of employees who have made significant contributions during a holiday season or other specific time frame.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that the employee you are nominating is a real person and has a valid email address.
2. Fill in the fields with the required information:
   - Nomination Form: Provide a brief explanation or description of the employee's achievements.
   - Recognition Reason: Explain the specific reason why the employee deserves recognition.
   - Employee Name: Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
   - Employee Email: Enter the email address of the employee being nominated.
   - Nominator Name: Enter your name and title (if applicable).
   - Nominator Email: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with any questions or follow-ups.
   - Manager Name: Enter the name of the employee's manager (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form (Text)**: Briefly describe the employee's achievements and reasons for nomination.
* **Recognition Reason (Text)**: Explain why you think the employee deserves recognition, e.g., exceptional work, teamwork, leadership, etc.
* **Employee Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
* **Employee Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the employee being nominated. Please ensure this is a valid email address.
* **Nominator Name (Text)**: Enter your name and title (if applicable).
* **Nominator Email (Email)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with any questions or follow-ups.
* **Manager Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the employee's manager (if applicable).

## Tips
* Be specific when explaining the reasons for recognition, and provide concrete examples or anecdotes if possible.
* Ensure all email addresses entered are valid and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any part of this form, please ask for clarification before submitting.
