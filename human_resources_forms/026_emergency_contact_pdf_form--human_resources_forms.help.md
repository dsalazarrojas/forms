# emergency_contact_pdf_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect emergency contact information for residents in a facility or organization. Emergency contacts are individuals who can be reached in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Resident Name** field with the resident's name.
2. Provide the name of the emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
3. Choose the relationship between the resident and the emergency contact from the **Relationship** dropdown menu.
4. If the relationship is not listed, provide a description in the **Other (specify)** field.
5. Select all applicable methods of contact for the emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact** dropdown menu.
6. If the emergency contact has a preferred contact method that is not listed, provide the details in the **Other (specify)** field.
7. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the **Emergency Contact Email** field.
8. Provide the emergency contact's address in the **Emergency Contact Address** field.
9. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Name**: The name of the resident who is submitting this form.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of the individual who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship**: The relationship between the resident and the emergency contact, such as mother, father, etc.
	+ Select from the dropdown menu and if not found, use the **Other (specify)** field to describe the relationship.
* **Other (specify)**: A field for providing a description of the relationship if it is not listed in the dropdown menu.
* **Emergency Contact**: The methods through which the emergency contact can be reached, such as home, work, etc.
	+ Select all applicable options and if not found, use the **Other (specify)** field to describe the method.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: The email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: The address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact**: The methods through which the emergency contact can be reached, such as home, work, etc.
	+ Select all applicable options and if not found, use the **Other (specify)** field to describe the method.

Note: Some fields are optional but it is recommended to fill them out for completeness.
