# Remote Work Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Consent Form is used to obtain the employee's consent and details of their remote work arrangement. This form is required for all employees who will be working remotely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email, phone number, and manager's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose your work arrangement (On-site, Remote, Hybrid) from the "Work Arrangement" dropdown list.
3. Enter your work hours and work location.
4. If applicable, provide additional details about your work arrangement.
5. Check the "Remote Work Consent" box if you agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Select the "Agreement" options to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
7. Enter the date and time you are signing the form.
8. Sign the form with your signature and date it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Work Arrangement** (`work_arrangement`, select_one, required): Choose your work arrangement (On-site, Remote, Hybrid).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, required): Enter your work hours (e.g., 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you will be working (e.g., home, coffee shop, co-working space).
* **Work Arrangement Details** (`work_arrangement_details`, text, optional): Provide additional details about your work arrangement, if applicable.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, date, optional): Enter the date of your work schedule (not required for most cases).
* **Performance Expectations** (`performance_expectations`, text, optional): Enter your performance expectations, if applicable.
* **Remote Work Consent** (`remote_work_consent`, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate your consent to remote work.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are signing the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are signing the form.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, required): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required): Sign the form with your manager's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Enter the time you signed the form.
