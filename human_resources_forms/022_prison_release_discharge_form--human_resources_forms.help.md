# Prison Release Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Prison Release Discharge Form" is a document used to collect essential information from individuals being released from prison or custody. This form helps verify personal details, address, and release information, which is vital for updating records and facilitating a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Date Of Birth** accurately and truthfully.
2. Enter your **Social Security Number** without any errors.
3. Provide your **Address** and **City** where you can be contacted after release.
4. Select your **State** of residence from the list.
5. Enter your **Zip Code** for mail delivery.
6. List your **Phone Number** for emergency contact purposes.
7. Enter the **Date Of Release** for official records.
8. Provide details about the **Crime** you committed.
9. Specify the **Crime Type** if applicable.
10. Describe the **Crime Details** in as much detail as possible.
11. List the court where you were held, the **Facility Name**, and its **Location**.
12. Mention any **Address 2** if you have one.
13. Provide a brief description of your personal **Prophets** if any.
14. Enter the **Release Date** for tracking purposes.
15. Specify the **Release Status** as requested by your release counselor.
16. Write any **Release Notes** or instructions from your release counselor.
17. Enter the name and contact information of your assigned **Release Counselor**.
18. Enter the notes from your **Release Counselor** if any.
19. Enter the **Discharge Date** for official records.
20. Briefly explain the reason for your **Discharge Reason**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your given name at birth or any other name you use.
* **Last Name**: Your surname or family name.
* **Date Of Birth**: Your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number**: Your government-issued identification number.
* **Address**: Your current address where you can be contacted.
* **City**: The city where you reside.
* **State**: Your state of residence. Choose from the list: CA, FL, NY, TX, IL, OH, WA, CO, MD, PA.
* **Zip**: Your zip code for mail delivery.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Date Of Release**: The date you are being released.
* **Crime**: Briefly describe the crime you committed.
* **Crime Type**: If applicable, specify the type of crime.
* **Crime Details**: Provide as much detail as possible about the crime.
* **Court**: The court where you were held.
* **Facility**: The name of the facility where you were held.
* **Facility Location**: The location of the facility where you were held.
* **Address 2**: If you have a secondary address, please enter it.
* **Prophets**: Any religious leaders or figures who support you.
* **Release Date**: The date you are to be released.
* **Release Status**: Specify the release status as requested by your release counselor.
* **Release Notes**: Any instructions or notes from your release counselor.
* **Release Counselor**: The name and contact information of your assigned release counselor.
* **Release Counselor Notes**: Any notes from your release counselor.
* **Discharge Date**: The date you are discharged from the facility.
* **Discharge Reason**: Briefly explain the reason for your discharge.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your release counselor.
* Ensure to review and sign the form before submission.
* Failure to complete the form may lead to delays in your release process.
* If you have any difficulties in filling out the form, contact your facility's administrative office.
