# Payroll Tax Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Payroll Tax Process Survey is designed to gather information from HR teams regarding their current payroll tax processes. The goal is to help teams evaluate their processes, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights to enhance compliance and efficiency.
