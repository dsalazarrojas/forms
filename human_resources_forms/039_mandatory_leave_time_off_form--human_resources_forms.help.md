# Mandatory Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mandatory Leave Time Off Form is used to submit a request for paid or unpaid leave of absence from work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your employee information and details of your leave request.
2. Select your employee status (Active, Inactive, On Leave of Absence, or On Maternity Leave) in the "Employee Status" field.
3. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Sick Leave, Family Leave, Bereavement Leave, Other Leave, or Annual Leave).
4. Enter the start and end dates of your leave period.
5. Provide the reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
6. Enter your expected return date.
7. Select the approval status of your leave request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
8. Enter the department head's approval date and name (if applicable).
9. Enter your leave requester's name, phone number, and email (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
- **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's name.
- **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your leave period.
- **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your leave period.
- **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your reason for leave.
- **Expected Return Date** (`expected_return_date`, date, optional): Enter your expected return date.
- **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, required): Select your current status.
- **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your leave period.
- **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of leave you are requesting.
- **Leave Start Time** (`leave_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your leave period.
- **Leave End Time** (`leave_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your leave period.
- **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your leave request.
- **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your manager's approval.
- **Approval By** (`approval_by`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your leave request.
- **Department Head Approval** (`department_head_approval`, select_one, required): Select the approval status from the department head.
- **Department Head Approval Date** (`department_head_approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your department head's approval.
- **Department Head Approval By** (`department_head_approval_by`, text, optional): Enter your department head's name.
- **Leave Requester** (`leave_requester`, text, optional): Enter your name.
- **Leave Requester Phone** (`leave_requester_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
