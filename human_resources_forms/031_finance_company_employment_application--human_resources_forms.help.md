# Finance Company Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finance Company Employment Application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in working at a finance company. The form gathers details about the applicant, their contact information, work history, and financial background to assist the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed. Start by filling out the user_info section, which includes general information about yourself. Then, move on to the contact_info section, where you can provide your contact details. Next, the employment_history section is where you can describe your previous work experience. Finally, in the finance_info section, you can provide any relevant financial information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **user_info** (user_info, text, optional): This section is where you can provide your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
- **contact_info** (contact_info, text, optional): Here, you can enter your contact information, such as your phone number and physical address.
- **employment_history** (employment_history, text, optional): Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
- **finance_info** (finance_info, text, optional): In this section, you can provide any relevant financial information about yourself, such as savings, investments, or other assets.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can upload them separately and provide the relevant file name and link in this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
