# part-time-employment-contract-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document part-time employment contracts for employees, capturing essential details such as job status, job type, compensation, and termination conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* Enter your Employee Name.
	* Choose your Job Title.
	* Select your Job Status (Active or Inactive).
	* Indicate if you have Yes or No contract (Job Type).
	* Provide your Start Date and End Date for the contract.
	* Enter your Salary or hourly rate.
	* Answer the Pay Frequency question (Yes or No).
	* Indicate if the contract has a contract length (Yes or No).
	* Enter the job requirements.
	* Describe your work schedule.
	* List any benefits you'll receive (e.g., health insurance, paid time off).
	* Specify your compensation details (e.g., salary, bonuses).
	* Enter your emergency contact information (name, phone number, address).
	* List any medical coverage you'll have (e.g., health insurance, medical expenses).
	* Specify the termination conditions for the contract.
	* Enter the termination notice period.
	* List the reasons for termination.
	* Describe the consequences of termination.
	* Indicate if there's a review period for the contract (Yes or No).
	* Choose the performance review frequency (Yes or No).
	* List the performance requirements.
	* Specify the performance review frequency.
	* Enter the performance review period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position you'll be holding.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select Active if you're currently employed or Inactive if you're not.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Answer Yes if you have a contract, No if you don't.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your contract begins.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your contract ends.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter your annual or hourly salary or rate.
* **Pay Frequency** (`pay_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select Yes if you're paid regularly or No if your pay is irregular.
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`, select_one, optional): Choose Yes if your contract has a specific length or No if it doesn't.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, optional): List any requirements or responsibilities you have for your job.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, text, optional): Describe your work schedule, including working hours and days.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, text, optional): List any benefits you'll be receiving (e.g., health insurance, paid time off).
* **Compensation** (`compensation`, text, optional): Specify your salary or compensation details (e.g., bonuses, benefits).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and address.
* **Medical Coverage** (`medical_coverage`, text, optional): List any medical coverage you'll have (e.g., health insurance, medical expenses).
* **Termination Conditions** (`termination_conditions`, text, optional): Specify the conditions for terminating your contract.
* **Termination Notice Period** (`termination_notice_period`, text, optional): Enter the notice period for termination.
* **Termination Reasons** (`termination_reasons`, text, optional): List the reasons for termination.
* **Termination Consequences** (`termination_consequences`, text, optional): Describe the consequences of termination.
* **Review Period** (`review_period`, select_one, optional): Choose Yes if there's a review period or No if there isn't.
* **Performance Reviews** (`performance_reviews`, select_multiple, optional): Select Yes if you'll have performance reviews or No if you won't.
* **Performance Requirements** (`performance_requirements`, text, optional): List the performance requirements for your job.
* **Performance Review Frequency** (`performance_review_freq`, select_one, optional): Choose Yes if you'll have performance reviews or No if you won't.
* **Performance Review Period** (`performance_review_period`, text, optional): Enter the review period.
* **Termination Notice Period Notice** (`termination_notice_period_notice`, text, optional): Enter any additional notice for termination.
* **Review Period** (`review_period`, select_one, optional): This field is a duplicate of the previous Review Period field and can be ignored.

Note that this form has 25 fields, but some fields might be optional depending on your specific employment contract. Ensure you complete all required fields accurately.
