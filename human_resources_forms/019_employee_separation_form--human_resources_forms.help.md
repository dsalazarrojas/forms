# Employee Separation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Separation Form is used to document the details surrounding an employee's separation from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the employee's name in the field provided.
2.  Select a valid reason for the employee's separation, if applicable.
3.  Choose the date of the employee's separation.
4.  Identify the department associated with the employee's separation.
5.  List the roles associated with the employee's separation.
6.  Sign and date the form, indicating your approval or acknowledgment of the separation details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1.  **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, Required: False)
*   This field is where you should enter the name of the employee who is separating.

### 2.  **Reason for Separation** (`reason_for_separation`, `text`, Required: False)
*   Here, you can enter the reason why the employee is separating from the company. This might include details on the reason for the separation, if it's voluntary or involuntary, and any other relevant information.

### 3.  **Date of Separation** (`date_of_separation`, `date`, Required: False)
*   Enter the date when the employee will be separating from the company. This date is used to establish a clear timeline for the separation process.

### 4.  **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, Required: False)
*   Choose the department that the employee belongs to or will no longer be a part of after the separation.

### 5.  **Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, Required: False)
*   List the roles that the employee held while at the company. This helps to clarify their responsibilities and any outstanding tasks or projects related to their separation.

### 6.  **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `note`, Required: False)
*   The employee should sign this field to acknowledge that they have reviewed and understood the separation details.

### 7.  **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `note`, Required: False)
*   The employee's direct manager should sign this field to acknowledge their involvement and approval of the separation details.

### 8.  **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, `note`, Required: False)
*   The employee's supervisor should sign this field to provide additional context and confirmation on the employee's separation details.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered for each field.
- Ensure that all required fields, if applicable, are completed before proceeding.
- Keep in mind that the employee's separation details will be reviewed and processed accordingly.
