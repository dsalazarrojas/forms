# Employee Sustainability Ambassador Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Sustainability Ambassador Application Form is designed to collect information about employees who want to participate in the employee ambassador program. This form helps to verify the accuracy of submitted data and prevent duplicate applications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the fields carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information.
2. Fill in your applicant information (page 1).
3. Provide your contact information (page 2).
4. Indicate your work hours and work location (page 3).
5. Select your department ID and enter any relevant manager information (page 4).
6. Choose how you would prefer to be contacted by your manager (page 5).
7. Add any additional employee information and job details (page 6-7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information (page 1):** Please provide your name, job title, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (page 2):** Enter your name and contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Work Hours (page 3):** Enter your standard work hours (e.g., 8-5, M-F).
* **Work Location (page 3):** Enter your work location (e.g., floor number, building).
* **Department ID (page 4):** If you're unsure, check with your manager or department head.
* **Manager Name (page 4):** Enter your direct manager's name.
* **Manager Email (page 4):** Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Title (page 4):** Enter your manager's title (if you know).
* **Manager Contact Method (page 5):** Choose how you prefer to be contacted by your manager:
	+ Mobile
	+ Office
	+ Email
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Employee Information (page 6):** Add any relevant details about yourself.
* **Job Title (page 6):** Enter your current job title.
* **Job Location (page 6):** Enter your current job location.
* **Job Start Date (page 7):** Enter your start date on the job.
