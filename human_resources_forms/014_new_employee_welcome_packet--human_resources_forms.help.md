# New Employee Welcome Packet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Employee Welcome Packet form is designed to collect essential information about new employees during their onboarding process. It is a critical document for HR to accurately record new employee details and facilitate smooth integration into the organization's systems and processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly:

1. Provide accurate and complete information about yourself.
2. For required fields, ensure you have the necessary details and permissions before proceeding.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: <label> (`Job Title`, `text`, `required`: `true`): Enter your official job title as recognized by the company.
* **Department**: <label> (`Department`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Select the department you belong to.
* **Job Location**: <label> (`Job Location`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Choose the location where you will be working.
* **Start Date**: <label> (`Start Date`, `date`, `required`: `true`): Enter your expected start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact**: <label> (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact**: <label> (`Relationship with Emergency Contact`, `text`, `required`: `true`): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., family member, spouse, roommate).
* **Phone**: <label> (`Phone`, `text`, `required`: `true`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Manager Name**: <label> (`Manager Name`, `text`, `required`: `true`): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Department**: <label> (`Manager Department`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Select your manager's department.
* **Manager Shift**: <label> (`Manager Shift`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Choose your manager's shift.
* **Manager Information**: <label> (`Manager Information`, `text`, `required`: `true`): Provide any additional information about your manager that is relevant to this form.

Note: If a field is required but you are unsure about the required information, please check with HR or your manager before proceeding.
