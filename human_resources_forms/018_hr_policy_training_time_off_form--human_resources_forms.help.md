# HR Policy Training Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Training Time Off Form is used to record and track time off requests made by employees. This form helps HR managers and administrators to manage and approve time off requests in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the days you wish to take off from the list provided in the "Request Time Off" field. You can choose multiple days if needed.
2. Enter the date when you will be taking the time off in the "Time Off Date" field.
3. Enter the time from when you will start your time off in the "From Time" field.
4. Enter the time to when you will finish your time off in the "To Time" field.
5. Provide a brief reason for your time off in the "Reason for Time Off" field.
6. Enter your manager's approval status in the "Manager Approval Status" field. This will indicate if your manager has approved or declined your request.
7. Enter any comments or notes from your manager in the "Manager Comment" field.
8. Enter your employee name, ID, department, location, and job title in the respective fields.
9. Enter your manager's name and email address in the respective fields.
10. Finally, select whether your manager has approved or declined your request in the "Manager Approval" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Time Off** (Select multiple): Choose the days you wish to take off. You can select multiple days from the list provided.
* **Time Off Date**: Enter the date when you will be taking the time off.
* **From Time**: Enter the time when you will start your time off.
* **To Time**: Enter the time when you will finish your time off.
* **Reason for Time Off**: Provide a brief reason for taking time off.
* **Manager Approval Status**: Indicate whether your manager has approved or declined your request.
* **Manager Comment**: Enter any comments or notes from your manager.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your employee name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Location**: Enter your location.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Approval**: Select whether your manager has approved or declined your request.
* **Approved Date**: Enter the date when your manager has approved your request.
* **Declined Date**: Enter the date when your manager has declined your request.
* **Request Details**: Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments or notes from your manager.
* **Status**: Select the status of your request (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments or notes from your manager.

Please note that some fields may not be required, depending on the specific circumstances of your time off request.
