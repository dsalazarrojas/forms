# UX Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects informed consent from research participants and researchers to ensure transparency and accountability in UX research activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Fill out the required fields marked as "required".
3. Ensure you understand the purpose, scope, and risks involved in the research study.
4. Select the correct options for each field, if applicable.
5. Sign the form, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, `required: false`): This field is used to collect general information about the participant or researcher.
* **Research Details** (`research_details`, `text`, `required: false`): In this field, describe the research study's purpose, scope, and any relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your contact information, such as email, phone number, or any other relevant contact method.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, `required: false`): This field is used to indicate your consent to participate or conduct research under the conditions outlined.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, `required: false`): This is a text field for signing the form, if required.
* **Participant First Name** (`participant_first_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the participant's first name.
* **Participant Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the participant's last name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, `required: false`): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Research Study Name** (`research_study_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the research study.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, `date`, `required: false`): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Select the participant's gender.
* **Researcher DPI** (`research_dpi`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the researcher's DPI (Data Protection Information).
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the researcher's name.
* **Researcher Phone** (`researcher_phone`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the researcher's phone number.
* **Researcher Email** (`researcher_email`, `email`, `required: false`): Enter the researcher's email address.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Select your consent to participate.
* **Participant DPI** (`participant_dpi`, `number`, `required: false`): Enter the participant's DPI (Data Protection Information).
* **Researcher Consent** (`researcher_consent`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Select your consent to conduct research.
* **Participant First Name 2** (`participant_first_name_2`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the first name of the participant or researcher.
* **Participant Last Name 2** (`participant_last_name_2`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the last name of the participant or researcher.
* **Participant Email 2** (`participant_email_2`, `email`, `required: false`): Enter the email address of the participant or researcher.
* **Participant Phone 2** (`participant_phone_2`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number of the participant or researcher.

## Tips

* Review the form carefully before filling it out.
* Ensure you understand the purpose, scope, and risks involved in the research study.
* Select the correct options for each field, if applicable.
* Sign the form, if required.
* If you're unsure about any field or information, please ask your research lead or contact person for clarification.
