# Employee Clothing Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employees' clothing preferences and work environment, aiming to create a comfortable and productive work atmosphere.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Company Abbreviation` field with your company's abbreviation or short name.
2. Select all applicable clothing types you wear to work from the `Clothing Type` field.
3. Choose your preferred clothing type from the `Preferred Clothing` field.
4. Choose your company culture from the `Company Culture` field, indicating whether your workplace is formal or casual.
5. In the `Work Environment` field, describe your work environment, if applicable.
6. Leave any additional feedback in the `Feedback` field if you have any comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Abbreviation**: A field to fill in your company's short name or abbreviation.
* **Gender Identity**: A field to describe your gender identity, if you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Clothing Type**: A selection field where you can choose all applicable clothing types you wear to work, such as Short sleeve, Long sleeve, Dress, or Jeans.
* **Preferred Clothing**: A field to choose your preferred clothing type, similar to the `Clothing Type` field but with a single selection.
* **Company Culture**: A field to choose whether your workplace is formal or casual.
* **Work Environment**: A field to describe your work environment, if applicable.
* **Feedback**: A field to leave any additional feedback or comments.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide accurate information to help create a better work atmosphere for everyone.
* If you're unsure about any question, you can always leave it blank or ask your supervisor for guidance.
* This form is meant to be a helpful tool for creating a more comfortable workplace, so please feel free to share your thoughts and suggestions.
