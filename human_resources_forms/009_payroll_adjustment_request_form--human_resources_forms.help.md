# Payroll Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request adjustments to their payroll. Adjustments may include correcting mistakes on previous paychecks or requesting extra pay for specific periods.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Information section with your name and relevant details.
2. In the Pay Adjustments section, describe the pay adjustment you are requesting.
3. Select the date you are submitting the form.
4. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the Comments section.
5. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Information (employee_info)
• **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your paystub or ID card.
### Pay Adjustments (pay_adjustments)
• **Pay Adjustments** (text, required): Describe the specific change you are requesting, such as correcting a mistake on a previous paycheck or requesting extra pay for a specific period.
### Submission Date (submission_date)
• **Submission Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
### Comments (comments)
• **Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional information you want to provide context for your request.
### Submitted By (submitted_by)
• **Submitted By** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide clear and concise information in the Pay Adjustments section to help the payroll team process your request efficiently.
- Use the Comments section to add any additional context or supporting documents that may be helpful for the payroll team to process your request.
- If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact the payroll team for assistance.
