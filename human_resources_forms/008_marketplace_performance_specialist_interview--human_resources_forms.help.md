# Marketplace Performance Specialist Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Performance Specialist Interview form is designed to gather information about a candidate's background, expertise, and availability for an interview. It is intended for use in the hiring process for Marketplace Performance Specialist roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling out the "Candidate Details" page (Page 1), which requires you to provide basic information about the candidate, such as their name and other contact details.
* Next, move to the "Marketplace Expertise" page (Page 2), where you will be asked to select one or more areas of expertise that the candidate has experience in, such as "E-commerce", "Marketplace", or "Product Management".
* On the "Interview Details" page (Page 3), you will be asked to provide any additional details relevant to the interview, such as any specific requirements or questions you'd like to ask the candidate.
* On the "Schedule Interview" page (Page 4), you will be asked to select one or more times for the interview, such as "10:00 AM", "11:00 AM", "12:00 PM", or "1:00 PM".
* Optionally, you can add any additional "Notes" (Page 5) about the candidate or interview.
* Finally, on the "Submitted By" page (Page 6), you will be asked to select who submitted the form, such as "HR Representative", "Manager", or "Team Lead".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (Page 1, `name`: text, required/optional):
	+ This field is for providing basic information about the candidate, such as their name and other contact details.
* **Marketplace Expertise** (Page 2, `name`: select_one, required/optional):
	+ This field allows you to select one or more areas of expertise that the candidate has experience in. Choose from the options: E-commerce, Marketplace, or Product Management.
* **Interview Details** (Page 3, `name`: text, required/optional):
	+ This field is for providing any additional details relevant to the interview, such as specific requirements or questions you'd like to ask the candidate.
* **Schedule Interview** (Page 4, `name`: select_multiple, required/optional):
	+ This field allows you to select one or more times for the interview, such as 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, or 1:00 PM.
* **Notes** (Page 5, `name`: note, required/optional):
	+ This field is for adding any additional notes or comments about the candidate or interview.
* **Submitted By** (Page 6, `name`: select_one, required/optional):
	+ This field is for indicating who submitted the form, such as "HR Representative", "Manager", or "Team Lead".
