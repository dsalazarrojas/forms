# Worker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for registering new workers in the organization's database.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Please enter the worker's first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Please enter the worker's last name.
* **Email** (email): Please enter the worker's email address.
* **Phone Number** (text): Please enter the worker's phone number.
* **Job Title** (text): Please enter the worker's job title.
* **Job Title (Other)** (text): If the worker has a job title that's not listed in the options, please enter it here.
* **Job Status** (select_one): Choose the worker's current job status from the list (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Job Status (Other)** (text): If the worker's job status is not listed in the options, please enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Please enter the worker's date of birth.
* **Hire Date** (date): Please enter the worker's hire date.
* **Supervisor Name** (text): Please enter the supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (email): Please enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (text): Please enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Department** (text): Please enter the department the worker belongs to.
* **Department (Other)** (text): If the worker belongs to a department that's not listed, please enter it here.
* **Manager Name** (text): Please enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (email): Please enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (text): Please enter the manager's phone number.
* **Employee Number** (text): Please enter the worker's employee number.
* **Notes** (note): Please enter any additional notes about the worker.
* **Other Notes** (note): If you have any other notes about the worker, please enter them here.
* **Job Classification** (select_multiple): Choose the worker's job classification from the list (Management, Finance, HR, Marketing).
* **Job Classification (Other)** (text): If the worker's job classification is not listed, please enter it here.
* **Termination Date** (date): Please enter the worker's termination date.
* **Termination Reason** (text): Please enter the reason for the worker's termination.

Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please contact the relevant department or HR representative for assistance.
