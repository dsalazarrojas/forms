# HR Officer Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Officer Job Application form is a tool for candidates to provide their details and qualifications for consideration for an HR Officer position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Candidate Name** in the first field. This is your full name as it appears on official documents.
2. Select the **Position Applying For** from the list provided. Please choose the position you are applying for: HR Officer, HR Manager, or Team Lead.
3. Enter your **Email** address. This is how we will contact you for further information or to invite you to an interview.
4. Provide your **Company** name, if you are currently working or have worked with a company.
5. Enter your **Job Title** if you have one.
6. Indicate the **Job Duration** you have held the position.
7. Describe your **Work Organisation** in a few words.
8. List your **Qualifications** relevant to the HR Officer position.
9. Describe your **Education**, including any relevant courses or degrees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name**: This is your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Position Applying For**: Choose the position you are applying for: HR Officer, HR Manager, or Team Lead.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is how we will contact you for further information or to invite you to an interview.
* **Company**: If you are currently working or have worked with a company, enter the name.
* **Job Title**: If you have a job title, enter it here.
* **Job Duration**: Indicate how long you have held this position.
* **Work Organisation**: Briefly describe your work organisation in a few words.
* **Qualifications**: List relevant qualifications, skills, or certifications that align with the HR Officer position.
* **Education**: Describe your education, including any relevant courses or degrees.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you may attach them to your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and effort in filling out this form.
