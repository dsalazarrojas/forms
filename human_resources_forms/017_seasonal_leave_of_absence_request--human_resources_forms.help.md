# seasonal_leave_of_absence_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Leave Request form is used to request seasonal leave from HR department. This form is required to be filled out by employees to submit a request for seasonal leave. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your Seasonal Leave Request information in the text area provided.
*   Select the Season you are taking leave for from the drop-down list.
*   Enter the Start and End Dates of your leave.
*   Choose the reason for your leave from the text area provided.
*   If applicable, enter your Team Lead's name and Manager's name in the text areas provided.
*   Enter your name and email to ensure that the form is submitted to the right person.
*   Select the type of leave you are taking from the drop-down list.
*   Enter the date of approval and choose the status of your leave request from the drop-down list.
*   Optionally, add any notes or comments you wish to include.
*   Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Leave Request**: This is the main text area where you can enter your seasonal leave request information.
*  **Season**: Select the season you are taking leave for from the drop-down list. This can be Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter.
*  **Start Date**: Enter the date you will start your leave.
*  **End Date**: Enter the date you will end your leave.
*  **Reason for Leave**: Enter the reason you are taking leave for.
*  **Team Lead**: If you have a Team Lead, enter their name.
*  **Manager Name**: If you have a Manager, enter their name.
*  **Employee Name**: Enter your name.
*  **Employee Email**: Enter your email address.
*  **Department**: Enter your department.
*  **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
*  **Leave Type**: Select the type of leave you are taking. This can be Sick leave, Vacation, or Family Leave.
*  **Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval.
*  **Approval Status**: Select the status of your leave request. This can be Pending, Approved, or Declined.
*  **Notes**: Add any notes or comments you want to include.
*  **Form Submitter**: Enter the person who submitted the form.
*  **Form Submitter Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
*  **Form Submitter Time**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
*  **Last Updated**: Enter the last updated date.
*  **Form Submitter Location**: Enter the location the form was submitted from.
*  **Form Submitter User Agent**: Enter the user agent who submitted the form.
*  **Form Submission Status**: Select the status of your form submission. This can be Draft, Submitted, Approved, or Declined.
*  **Form Submission Status Details**: Enter any details about the form submission status.
*  **Form Submission Comments**: Enter any comments about the form submission.
*  **Form Submission User Agent**: Enter the user agent who submitted the form.
