# Proworks Recrutment Services Medical Staff Telephonic Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from medical staff for Proworks Recruitment Services. It is intended to collect demographic and professional details for potential candidates or existing employees in the medical field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your date of birth in the "date of birth" field.
6. Enter your job title in the "job title" field.
7. Provide your work phone number in the "work phone" field.
8. Enter your personal email in the "personal email" field.
9. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "emergency contact name" field.
10. Describe your relationship with your emergency contact in the "emergency contact relationship" field.
11. Enter your address in the "address" field.
12. Enter your city in the "city" field.
13. Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
14. Enter your state in the "state" field.
15. Enter your country in the "country" field.
16. Enter the number of years you have worked in the medical field in the "work years worked" field.
17. Enter the number of years you have worked at Proworks in the "years worked here" field.
18. Indicate if you have a medical degree by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "highest degree" field.
19. Enter the name of your highest degree in the "degree name" field.
20. Enter the field of your highest degree in the "degree field" field.
21. Enter the name of the institution where you obtained your highest degree in the "degree institution" field.
22. Enter the country where you obtained your highest degree in the "degree country" field.
23. Enter the date you obtained your highest degree in the "degree date" field.
24. Enter your medical license number in the "medical license" field.
25. Enter your medical license number in the "license number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **last name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **date of birth** (5, date, required/optional): Select your date of birth.
* **job title** (6, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **work phone** (7, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **personal email** (8, email, required/optional): Enter your personal email address.
* **emergency contact name** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **emergency contact relationship** (10, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **address** (11, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **city** (12, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **zip** (13, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **state** (14, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **country** (15, text, required/optional): Enter your country.
* **work years worked** (16, number, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in the medical field.
* **years worked here** (17, number, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have worked at Proworks.
* **highest degree** (18, select one, required/optional): Indicate if you have a medical degree.
* **degree name** (19, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your highest degree.
* **degree field** (20, text, required/optional): Enter the field of your highest degree.
* **degree institution** (21, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the institution where you obtained your highest degree.
* **degree country** (22, text, required/optional): Enter the country where you obtained your highest degree.
* **degree date** (23, date, required/optional): Enter the date you obtained your highest degree.
* **medical license** (24, text, required/optional): Enter your medical license number.
* **license number** (25, text, required/optional): Enter your medical license number.
