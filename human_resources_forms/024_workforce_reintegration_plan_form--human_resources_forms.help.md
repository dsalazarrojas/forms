# Workforce Reintegration Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Reintegration Plan Form is designed to facilitate the return-to-work process for employees who are ready to come back to work after an absence due to illness, injury, or other reasons. This form helps HR staff and managers to gather essential information about the employee's return-to-work details, accommodation requests, training needs, and other relevant factors. Completing this form ensures that the employee's needs are addressed and that a smooth transition back to work can be achieved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Return to Work Details" page and enter the necessary information in the required fields.
2. On the "Accommodation Requests" page, select the accommodations that the employee needs, such as "Accommodation 1" or "Accommodation 2".
3. On the "Training Needs" page, select the training sessions the employee requires to get back to work efficiently.
4. Enter the date and time the employee plans to return to work on the "Return to Work Date" and "Return to Work Time" pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Return to Work Details**
* (`return_to_work_details`, text, required/optional): This is the main content field where you can enter the employee's return to work details, such as the reasons for their return, their work schedule, and any other relevant information.

### **Accommodation Requests**
* (`accommodation_requests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more accommodations that the employee requires to facilitate their return to work, such as "Accommodation 1" or "Accommodation 2".

### **Training Needs**
* (`training_needs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more training sessions that the employee needs to get back to work efficiently, such as "Training 1" or "Training 2".

### **Return to Work Date**
* (`return_to_work_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the employee plans to return to work.

### **Return to Work Time**
* (`return_to_work_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the employee plans to return to work.

### **Manager Comments**
* (`manager_comments`, note, required/optional): This is the space for the manager to add any comments or notes about the employee's return to work plan.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your HR guidelines or contact HR support for guidance.
* This form is meant to facilitate communication and collaboration; feel free to add any relevant information that will help the HR team better understand the employee's needs.
