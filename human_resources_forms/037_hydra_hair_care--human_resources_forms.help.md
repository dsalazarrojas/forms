# Hydra Hair Care - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about Hydra Hair Care employees, including company details, job title, job level, job type, benefits, and contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the company name where you work.
2. Fill in your job title (CEO, CTO, CIO, etc.).
3. Select your job level (Junior, Senior, Lead, etc.).
4. Choose whether you are a Full-time, Part-time, or Contract employee.
5. Enter the date of your job status change (if applicable).
6. Select your job type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
7. Enter your work hours (e.g., 9am-5pm).
8. Enter your salary.
9. Select any benefits you are entitled to (Health Insurance, Dental Insurance, Vision Insurance, etc.).
10. Enter any details about your benefits.
11. Enter a brief job description.
12. Enter the name and contact information of your contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company where you work.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required): Choose your job title from the list (CEO, CTO, CIO, etc.).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job level (Junior, Senior, Lead, etc.).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, date, optional): Enter the date of your job status change.
* **Job Status Date** (`job_status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your job status change (if different from job_status).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started working.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you ended your job.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, optional): Enter your work hours (e.g., 9am-5pm).
* **Work Hours Ending** (`work_hours_ending`, time, optional): Enter your work hours ending (if different from work hours).
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter your salary.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, optional): Select any benefits you are entitled to (Health Insurance, Dental Insurance, Vision Insurance, etc.).
* **Benefits Details** (`benefits_details`, note, optional): Enter any details about your benefits.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of your contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of your contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your contact person.
* **Job Status Change** (`job_status_change`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reason for your job status change (Hire, Promote, Transfer, Termination, etc.).
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reason for your leave (Vacation, Sick Leave, Medical Leave, etc.).
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you ended your leave.
* **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for your leave.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether your manager has approved your job status change.
