# Company Selection For Interviews - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help companies get a clear understanding of candidates' preferences and qualifications for interviews.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and attachments before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form in a logical and sequential manner, starting from the candidate details section.
3. Be as accurate and honest as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as per your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that you frequently use for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number to facilitate easy communication.
* **Position Applied For**: Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Application ID**: If you have one, enter the ID provided by the company or recruiter.
* **-- Company Preferences --**:
  * **First Choice Company**: Enter the top preference company for your interview.
  * **Why is this your first choice?**: Briefly explain why this company is your top preference.
  * **Second Choice Company**: Enter the backup preference company.
  * **Third Choice Company**: Enter the third preference company.
  * **Are you open to other companies?**: Choose if you are open to other companies or not.
* **-- Interview Preferences --**:
  * **Preferred Interview Format**: Choose the format of the interview (in-person, video call, phone, or either).
  * **Preferred Interview Location**: If in-person, enter the preferred location (if not, select 'Not specified').
  * **General Availability**: Choose the days you are available for interviews (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or weekend).
  * **Specific Dates Available**: If any, enter the date range of your availability.
  * **Dates You Are NOT Available**: If any, enter the date range of your unavailability.
* **-- Logistics --**:
  * **Are you willing to relocate?**: Choose if you are willing to relocate or not.
  * **If yes, which locations?**: If willing to relocate, enter the preferred locations.
  * **Are you willing to travel for interviews?**: Choose the travel distance you are willing to cover (any distance, within region, up to 2 hours, or local only).
  * **Who will cover travel expenses?**: Choose who will cover travel expenses (company, self, split, or not applicable).
* **-- Compensation Expectations --**:
  * **Expected Salary Range**: Enter the expected salary range.
  * **Is your salary expectation negotiable?**: Choose if your salary expectation is negotiable or not.
  * **Which benefits are most important to you?**: Choose the top benefits that matter most to you (health, dental, vision, 401(k), paid time off, remote work, professional development, stock options, or bonus structure).
* **-- Additional Information --**:
  * **Have you attached your resume?**: Choose if you have attached your resume or not.
  * **Have you attached a cover letter?**: Choose if you have attached a cover letter or not.
  * **Can you provide references?**: Choose if you are willing to provide references or not.
  * **Are you authorized to work in this country?**: Choose if you are authorized or not.
  * **If applicable, what is your visa status?**: If applicable, enter your visa status.
  * **Any additional comments or questions?**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
  * **How did you hear about this opportunity?**: Choose how you heard about this opportunity (company website, job board, social media, referral, career fair, recruiter, or other).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the current date.

By following these steps and explanations, you will be able to fill out this form accurately and comprehensively, providing companies with valuable information to make informed decisions.
