# Market Analysis Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for a Market Analysis project. The form is used to capture information about the employee's leave request, including the start and end dates of the leave, the reason for the leave, and the status of the leave within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name and ID.
2. Select the start and end dates of the leave.
3. Enter the reason for the leave.
4. Select the status of the leave within the organization (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
5. Select the status of the leave within the team (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
6. Select the status of the market analytics (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
7. Enter any additional comments.
8. Enter the manager's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Leave Start Date** (Leave Start Date, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the leave.
* **Leave End Date** (Leave End Date, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the leave.
* **Reason For Leave** (Reason For Leave, Text, Required): Enter the reason for the leave, e.g., project deadline, personal emergency, etc.
* **Manager Review Status** (Manager Review Status, Select Multiple, Required): Select the current status of the leave within the organization:
	+ Approved: The leave has been approved.
	+ Denied: The leave has been denied.
	+ Pending: The leave is still under review.
* **Team Lead Review Status** (Team Lead Review Status, Select Multiple, Required): Select the current status of the leave within the team:
	+ Approved: The leave has been approved by the team lead.
	+ Denied: The leave has been denied by the team lead.
	+ Pending: The leave is still under review by the team lead.
* **Market Analytics Status** (Market Analytics Status, Select Multiple, Required): Select the current status of the market analytics:
	+ Completed: The market analytics have been completed.
	+ In Progress: The market analytics are currently being worked on.
	+ Not Started: The market analytics have not been started yet.
* **Market Analytics Data** (Market Analytics Data, Text, Required): Enter any market analytics data relevant to the leave.
* **Comments** (Comments, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the leave.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, Number, Required): Enter the employee's ID.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the manager approving or denying the leave.
* **Manager Comments** (Manager Comments, Text, Optional): Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Team Lead Name** (Team Lead Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the team lead approving or denying the leave.
* **Team Lead Comments** (Team Lead Comments, Text, Optional): Enter any comments from the team lead.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be clear and concise in your comments and answers.
* Double-check the status of the leave within the organization and the team before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your manager or team lead for guidance.
