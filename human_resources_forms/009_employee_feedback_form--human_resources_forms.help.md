# employee_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees about their job and work environment. It helps to identify areas of improvement and areas of strength, which can inform management decisions and contribute to a positive work culture.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form honestly, sharing your thoughts and feelings about your job and work environment.
- For multi-select questions, select all that apply.
- For text fields, provide detailed answers.
- Please answer all questions, but you do not have to answer all of them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_feedback_form_1**: This is an introduction page with no fields to fill out.
* **what_do_you_like_about_your_job**: Select all the things you like about your job.
* **what_do_you_dislike_about_your_job**: Select all the things you dislike about your job.
* **what_would_you_like_to_improve_about_your_job**: Write your thoughts on what you would like to improve about your job.
* **what_is_one_thing_that_would_improve_your_job**: Select one thing that would improve your job.
* **what_is_one_thing_you_are_proud_of_in_your_work**: Select all the things you are proud of in your work.
* **do_you_feel_supported_in_your_job**: Select if you feel supported in your job.
* **what_are_your_goals_and_objectives**: Select all the things that align with your goals and objectives.
* **what_is_the_most_difficult_challenge_you_have_faced**: Write about the most difficult challenge you have faced.
* **how_do_you_feel_about_your_manager**: Select all the things you feel about your manager.
* **how_do_you_feel_about_your_work_environment**: Select how you feel about your work environment.
* **do_you_feel_inspired_by_your_work**: Select if you feel inspired by your work.
* **what_are_the_top_3_things_you_like_about_your_job**: Select all the top 3 things you like about your job.
* **what_are_the_top_3_things_you_dislike_about_your_job**: Select all the top 3 things you dislike about your job.
* **what_are_your_long_term_goals**: Write about your long-term goals.
* **do_you_feel_supported_by_your_colleagues**: Select if you feel supported by your colleagues.
* **how_do_you_feel_about_your_salary**: Select how you feel about your salary.
* **do_you_feel_your_ideas_are_valued**: Select if you feel your ideas are valued.
* **do_you_feel_confident_in_your_skills**: Select if you feel confident in your skills.
* **do_you_feel_included_in_your_job**: Select if you feel included in your job.
* **do_you_feel_motivated_by_your_job**: Select if you feel motivated by your job.
* **do_you_feel_stressful_at_work**: Select if you feel stressful at work.
* **do_you_feel_overworked**: Select if you feel overworked.
* **do_you_feel_underutilized**: Select if you feel underutilized.
* **employee_comments**: This is a text field for any additional comments or feedback you have.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by employees, and the answers are anonymous. All answers will be kept confidential and used to inform management decisions.
