# Mental Health Evaluation Form For Supervisors - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Evaluation Form For Supervisors is designed to help you assess the mental well-being of your team members. This form asks questions to gather information about your team members' performance, mental health issues, and your feedback as a supervisor. The form is intended to be a starting point for further discussions and support, not a definitive diagnosis or evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On each page, you will be asked to provide information about a specific topic.
2. Please answer each question as thoroughly and honestly as possible.
3. You can use the "Employee ID" or "Name" field to identify which team member you are evaluating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Employee Performance
* **Employee ID** (Text, Not Required): Enter the ID or name of the team member you are evaluating.
* **Employee Performance** (Text, Not Required): Describe the team member's overall performance, including their strengths and challenges. This should include specific examples or situations where their performance has improved or deteriorated.

### Page 2: Mental Health Issues
* **Mental Health Issues** (Text, Not Required): If you are aware of any mental health concerns or issues affecting the team member, describe them briefly. This could include any relevant medical conditions, diagnoses, or symptoms.

### Page 3: Supervisor Feedback
* **Supervisor Feedback** (Text, Not Required): Share any relevant feedback or observations about the team member's performance or behavior that could impact their mental health. This might include positive feedback, constructive criticism, or suggestions for improvement.

## Tips
- Be sincere and honest in your answers, as these will help identify areas for growth and support.
- If you are concerned about a team member's mental health, encourage them to seek professional help from a qualified mental health professional.
- Remember that this form is a sensitive topic; approach it with empathy and respect for each team member's well-being.
