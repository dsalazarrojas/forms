# PtoRolloverOrPayoutRequestForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PtoRolloverOrPayoutRequestForm is used to request a paid time off (PTO) or a payout for a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you have discussed and been approved for a PTO or payout with your manager before submitting this form.
2. Select the correct PTO or payout option from the list.
3. Enter the Start Date and End Date for PTO requests.
4. Enter the Total PTO Hours, Pay Period, and other details as required.
5. Add any additional Notes or comments.
6. Enter your Email and Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pto Submission**: Enter a brief description of the PTO or payout request.
* **Payout Option**: Select only one option from the list to specify the type of payout.
	+ Cash
	+ Rollover
* **Reason for PTO**: Briefly explain the reason for your PTO request.
* **Start Date**: The date from which you would like your PTO to start.
* **End Date**: The date until which you would like your PTO to end.
* **Total PTO Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you are requesting as PTO.
* **Pay Period**: Specify the pay period for which you are requesting PTO or payout.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Email**: Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
