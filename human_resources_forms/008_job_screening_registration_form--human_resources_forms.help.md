# job_screening_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register job openings and collect information from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the candidate details section with the required information.
2. Select the relevant options for the resume section.
3. Choose the skills that best match the job requirements.
4. Enter the work experience, education, and contact details of the job applicant.
5. Provide a note if you have any additional information about the job.
6. Fill in the job details section with the required information.
7. Finally, submit the form to register the job screening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (Page 1, Required)
	+ This section requires the basic details of the applicant, such as name, email, and job title.
* **Resume** (Page 1, Required)
	+ Please select the type of resume that best matches the job requirements.
* **Skills** (Page 2, Not required)
	+ Choose the skills that the applicant possesses to match the job requirements.
* **Work Experience** (Page 2, Required)
	+ Enter the work experience of the applicant, including start and end dates.
* **Education** (Page 3, Required)
	+ Enter the education details of the applicant, including degree and institution.
* **Contact Preferences** (Page 3, Not required)
	+ Enter the contact preferences of the applicant, including email and phone number.
* **Note** (Page 3, Not required)
	+ Add any additional note about the applicant.
* **Submitted By** (Page 4, Required)
	+ Enter the date the job screening was submitted.
* **Job Title** (Page 4, Required)
	+ Select the job title that best matches the job requirements.
* **Job Category** (Page 4, Required)
	+ Select the job category that best matches the job requirements.
* **Job Type** (Page 4, Required)
	+ Select the job type that best matches the job requirements.
* **Job Status** (Page 4, Required)
	+ Select the job status that best matches the job requirements.
* **Job Location** (Page 5, Required)
	+ Enter the location of the job.
* **Job Description** (Page 5, Required)
	+ Enter a brief description of the job requirements.
* **Job Salary** (Page 6, Required)
	+ Enter the salary range for the job.
* **Job Requirements** (Page 6, Required)
	+ Enter the job requirements.
* **Job Opening Date** (Page 6, Required)
	+ Enter the date the job was opened.
* **Job Closing Date** (Page 6, Required)
	+ Enter the date the job will be closed.
* **Job Applicant Name** (Page 7, Required)
	+ Enter the name of the job applicant.
* **Job Applicant Position** (Page 7, Required)
	+ Enter the position of the job applicant.
* **Job Applicant Email** (Page 7, Required)
	+ Enter the email of the job applicant.
* **Job Applicant Phone** (Page 7, Required)
	+ Enter the phone number of the job applicant.
* **Job Applicant Note** (Page 8, Not required)
	+ Add any additional note about the job applicant.
