# Diversity & Inclusion Advisory Panel Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Panel Application form is designed to collect information from employees interested in joining the panel. The panel's purpose is to provide advice on matters related to diversity and inclusion within the organization. The form helps to ensure that a diverse range of perspectives are represented on the panel and that the right people are selected for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "Required".
3. Provide accurate and truthful information about yourself and your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official records.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address that you would like to be reached out to, please enter it here. This field is optional, but it will help us contact you if we need to.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number that you would like to be reached out to, please enter it here. This field is optional, but it will help us contact you if we need to.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Please enter your current job title within the organization.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): If you would like to specify which department you work in, please enter it here. This field is optional.
- **Diversity Statement** (`diversity_statement`, `text`, required): Please write a brief diversity statement explaining why you are a good fit for the panel. This should be a 2-3 paragraph statement.
- **Role in Panel** (`role_in_panel`, `select_one`, required): Please select "Yes" if you are interested in joining the panel, and "No" if you are not.
- **Experience** (`experience`, `time`, optional): If you have any experience that you would like to mention in terms of your availability, please select the relevant time slot.
- **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all relevant time slots that you are available for panel meetings.
- **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, not required): Clicking this button will submit your application. Make sure to review your answers before submitting it.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields truthfully and accurately.
- Please ensure that you have the necessary experience and permission to contribute to the panel's discussions.
- Be specific in your diversity statement and mention any relevant experiences that would make you a good fit for the panel.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to HR or the panel administrators.
