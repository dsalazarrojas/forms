# simple_job_application_form_red_and_responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from job applicants, including their position name, contact email, drag and drop resume, and a brief description of the job they're applying for. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Position Name** in the "Position Name" field.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a resume, you can **Drag and Drop** it into the "Drag and Drop Resume" field.
4. In the "Job Description" field, type a brief description of the job you are applying for.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Position Name**:
    A field to enter your desired position name.
    *Required*
- **candidate_name**:
    A field to enter your name.
    *Required*
- **Email**:
    A field to enter your email address.
    *Optional*
- **Drag and Drop Resume**:
    A field to upload your resume.
    *Required*
    *Please note: This field only accepts file upload, not text*
- **Job Title**:
    A field to select the job title you're applying for.
    *Optional*
    *Note: This field allows you to select one job type from the options below*
    *Options: IT Support, Software Developer, Marketing Specialist*
- **Job Type**:
    A field to select the job type you're interested in.
    *Optional*
    *Note: This field allows you to select multiple job types from the options below*
    *Options: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Freelance*
- **Job Description**:
    A field to type a brief description of the job you are applying for.
    *Required*
    *Please note: This field only allows text entry* 
- **Submit**:
    A button to submit your application.
