# Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Application Form is used to collect information from candidates applying for jobs. It is a required form for all new job applicants and should not be used for internal job postings or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the job title that best matches your application from the "Job Title" dropdown menu.
5. Write a brief summary of your qualifications and experience in the "Job Description" field.
6. Upload your resume in the "Resume Upload" field.
7. Enter the date you are applying for the job in the "Apply Date" field.
8. Enter the time you are available to start in the "Apply Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your personal or professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Job Title**: Select the job title that best matches your qualifications and experience.
* **Job Description**: Briefly describe your experience and qualifications that make you a good fit for the job.
* **Resume Upload**: Upload your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Apply Date**: Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **Apply Time**: Enter the time you are available to start working, following the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
