# internship_leave_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internship leave application form is used for employees on internship to request leave for a specific period. This form is to be filled out with accurate and complete information to ensure proper processing and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields (marked as `*`).
2.  Review each field carefully and ensure accuracy.
3.  Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **applicant_name** (`applicant_name`, text, *): This is your name as an employee on internship.
    Enter your name as it appears on your official company ID or personnel file.
*   **start_date** (`leave_start_date`, date, *): The start date of your leave request.
    Enter the date you would like your leave to begin.
*   **end_date** (`leave_end_date`, date, *): The end date of your leave request.
    Enter the date you would like your leave to end.
*   **reason_for_leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, *): The reason for your leave request.
    Enter a clear and concise explanation of your leave request.
*   **supervisor_approval** (`supervisor_approval_status`, select_one, *): The status of your supervisor's approval.
    Select one of the following options: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
*   **comments** (`comments`, note, ): This is an optional field for your comments.
    You can add any additional information that may be helpful for your supervisor.
*   **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, text, ): This field is automatically populated by the system and is for tracking purposes.
    Do not fill this field out manually.
*   **start_time** (`leave_start_time`, time, ): The start time of your leave request.
    Enter the time you would like your leave to start.
*   **end_time** (`leave_end_time`, time, ): The end time of your leave request.
    Enter the time you would like your leave to end.
*   **duration** (`leave_duration`, number, *): The duration of your leave request in hours.
    Enter the number of hours you would like your leave to last.
*   **type** (`leave_type`, select_one, *): The type of leave you are requesting.
    Select one of the following options: Vacation, Sick Leave, or Emergency Leave.
