# Employee Recognition Program Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Program Approval Form is used to request approval for employee recognition programs, which provide rewards or incentives to employees for their contributions to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Organization Name, Manager Name, Employee Name, Date of Recognition, Reason for Recognition, and Approval Status.
2. Select the Recognition Type (Monetary or Non-Monetary).
3. If you are selecting Monetary, enter the Monetary Value.
4. Enter the Employee ID (if available).
5. Enter the Manager's Email and Phone Number (if available).
6. Enter any additional Manager Comments (if necessary).
7. Select the Submit Date and Time (when the request was submitted).
8. Select the name of the person who submitted the request (Submitter's Name).
9. Select the Approver Name, Title, and Approval Status.
10. Enter the Approver Comments (if necessary).
11. Enter the Notes (if necessary).
12. Select the Approver who approved the request (Approved By).
13. Select the date and time the request was approved (Approved Date and Time).
14. Enter the Approved Location and Department (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required, text): The name of the organization submitting the recognition request.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, required, text): The name of the manager who will be approving the recognition request.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required, text): The name of the employee being recognized.
* **Date of Recognition** (`date_of_recognition`, required, date): The date the employee's achievement was recognized.
* **Reason for Recognition** (`reason_for_recognition`, required, text): A brief description of the reason for the recognition.
* **Recognition Type** (`recognition_type`, optional, select_one): The type of recognition the employee received, such as Monetary or Non-Monetary.
* **Monetary Value** (`monetary_value`, optional, number): The value of the monetary recognition (if Monetary was selected).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, optional, number): The employee's ID number (if available).
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, optional, email): The manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, optional, text): The manager's phone number.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, optional, text): Any additional comments from the manager.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, required, date): The date the recognition request was submitted.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, required, select_one): The name of the person who submitted the request, John or Jane.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, optional, text): The name of the person who approved the recognition request (if different from the submitter's name).
* **Approver Title** (`approver_title`, optional, text): The title of the person who approved the recognition request (if different from the manager's title).
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, required, select_one): The status of the recognition request, Approved or Rejected.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, optional, text): Any additional comments from the approver.
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional, text): Any additional notes about the recognition request.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, required, select_one): The title of the person who approved the recognition request, Manager or Director.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, optional, date): The date the recognition request was approved.
* **Approved Time** (`approved_time`, optional, time): The time the recognition request was approved.
* **Approved Location** (`approved_location`, optional, text): The location where the recognition request was approved.
* **Approved Department** (`approved_department`, optional, text): The department where the recognition request was approved.
* **Submitted by Name** (`submitted_by_name`, optional, text): The name of the person who submitted the request (same as the submitter's name).
