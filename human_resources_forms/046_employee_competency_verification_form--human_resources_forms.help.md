# Employee Competency Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by the HR department to verify the competencies of employees to ensure consistency and prevent non-duplication of work within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the `user_id` to identify the employee.
2. Select the employee's `Job Title` from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the number of `Years Of Experience` the employee has.
4. Choose the relevant `Skills` the employee possesses.
5. Select the employee's `Education` background.
6. Enter the employee's `Experience` in years and months.
7. Select the `Verification Date` and `Verification Time` when the employee's competencies were verified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID (1)** (`user_id`, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the unique identifier assigned to the employee in the organization.
* **Job Title (2)** (`job_title`, text, required: false)
	+ Select the title of the job or position the employee is currently holding or has held.
* **Years Of Experience (3)** (`years_of_experience`, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the number of years the employee has been working in the organization or in the relevant field.
* **Relevant Skills (4)** (`relevant_skills`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the skills that the employee possesses and are relevant to their job or position.
* **Education (5)** (`education`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the education background of the employee (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Diploma).
* **Experience (6)** (`experience`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the years and months of experience the employee has in their job or relevant field.
* **Verification Date (7)** (`verification_date`, date, required: false)
	+ Select the date when the employee's competencies were verified.
* **Verification Time (8)** (`verification_time`, time, required: false)
	+ Select the time when the employee's competencies were verified.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the data entered is accurate and up-to-date.
- If the employee's skills or education have changed since the last verification, please update the relevant fields accordingly.
- This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with external parties without permission from HR department.
