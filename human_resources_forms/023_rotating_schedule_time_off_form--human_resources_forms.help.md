# rotating_schedule_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rotating Schedule Time Off form is designed to allow employees to request time off for a specific period. This form collects information about the employee's name, start and end dates of the time off period, and the reason for the absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling in your name as it appears on your employee ID.
*   Enter the start and end dates of the time off period you are requesting.
*   Type in a clear and concise reason for your absence, such as "Medical appointment" or "Family vacation".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Your full name as it appears on your employee ID. Please enter your name in the format "Last name, First Name" (e.g., "Smith, John").
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you would like your time off to start.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you would like your time off to end.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, Text, Required): Provide a clear and concise reason for your absence. Please keep your explanation brief but informative.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
*   Check that you have not already submitted a time off request for the same period before submitting this form.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, consult with your HR representative.
