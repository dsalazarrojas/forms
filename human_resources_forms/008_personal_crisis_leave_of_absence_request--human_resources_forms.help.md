# Personal Crisis Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a personal crisis leave of absence due to serious personal or family matters. The form is used to notify the HR department and the employee's manager of the request, which will then review and process the request accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your requested absence in the "Date of Absence Start" and "Date of Absence End" fields, respectively.
3. Choose the reason for your leave from the "Reason For Leave" dropdown menu.
4. Add any manager comments in the "Manager Comments" field.
5. If applicable, select the additional information you would like to share with your manager in the "Employee Comments" field.
6. Finally, click submit to send your request for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID**: (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your unique employee number or ID to identify yourself in the system.
* **Date of Absence Start**: (`date_of_absence_start`, date, required): Select the start date of your requested absence.
* **Date of Absence End**: (`date_of_absence_end`, date, required): Select the end date of your requested absence.
* **Reason For Leave**: (`reason_for_leave`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your leave from the provided options.
* **Manager Comments**: (`manager_comments`, note, required): Add any comments or information you would like to share with your manager.
* **Employee Comments**: (`employee_comments`, select_multiple, required): If applicable, select any additional information you would like to share with your manager.
* **Approval Status**: (`approval_status`, select_one, required): This field will be automatically populated once your manager reviews and approves your request.
* **Approved By**: (`approved_by`, text, required): This field will be automatically populated with the name of the person who approved your request.
* **Prophets**: (`employee_comments`, select_multiple, required): This field appears to be a duplicate of the "Employee Comments" field. Please select any additional information you would like to share with your manager.
