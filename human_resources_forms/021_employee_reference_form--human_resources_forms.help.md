# Employee Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reference Form is used to provide information about your work history and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review your work history to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Application ID**: This field is not required but can be useful for referencing your application. Enter your candidate application ID if you have one.
* **Reference Name**: Please enter the name of the person who can vouch for your work experience.
* **Reference Title**: Please enter the title or position of the person who can vouch for your work experience.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the person who can vouch for your work experience. This will help them receive notifications and instructions.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who can vouch for your work experience. This will help them receive notifications and instructions.
* **Reference Since**: Please enter the date you last worked with the reference person.
* **Comment**: Please provide any additional comments or information about your work experience with the reference person.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not required and can be used to specify any tools or software used during your work experience.

Please note that the form requires at least three fields: Reference Name, Reference Title, and Email. Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting.
