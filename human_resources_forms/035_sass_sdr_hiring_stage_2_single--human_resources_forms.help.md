# sass_sdr_hiring_stage_2_single - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sass_sdr_hiring_stage_2_single form is used to collect more detailed information about a candidate for the SDR hiring process. This form is designed to be completed by the hiring team or a designated person in the organization and is used to gather essential data for the evaluation and decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Employee Info** page with the candidate's details, including their name and other relevant information.
2. On the **Question ID** page, enter the unique ID assigned to the question asked during the hiring process.
3. On the **Question Name** page, enter a brief description or title of the question.
4. On the **Question Type** page, select whether the question is "Yes" or "No".
5. On the **Question Type ID** page, enter the ID of the question type.
6. On the **Question Text**, **Question Video URL**, **Question Audio URL**, **Question Audio Transcript**, and **Question Video Transcript** pages, enter the text or URL associated with the question.
7. On the **Question Audio File**, **Question Video File**, **Question Audio Transcript File**, and **Question Video Transcript File** pages, enter the file associated with the question.
8. On the **Question Audio URL**, **Question Video URL**, **Question Audio File URL**, and **Question Video File URL** pages, enter the URL or file associated with the question.
9. On the **Question Audio**, **Question Video**, **Question Audio File Type**, and **Question Video File Type** pages, enter the audio or video associated with the question.
10. On the **Question Transcript**, **Question Audio Transcript File**, and **Question Video Transcript File** pages, enter the transcript associated with the question.
10. Review and submit the form for evaluation and decision-making.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info**: Enter the candidate's name and other relevant information.
* **Question ID**: Enter the unique ID assigned to the question asked during the hiring process.
* **Question Name**: Enter a brief description or title of the question.
* **Question Type**: Select whether the question is "Yes" or "No".
* **Question Type ID**: Enter the ID of the question type.
* **Question Text**: Enter the text associated with the question.
* **Question Audio URL**: Enter the URL of the question's audio file.
* **Question Video URL**: Enter the URL of the question's video file.
* **Question Audio Transcript**: Enter the transcript associated with the question's audio file.
* **Question Video Transcript**: Enter the transcript associated with the question's video file.
* **Question Audio File**: Enter the file associated with the question's audio.
* **Question Video File**: Enter the file associated with the question's video.
* **Question Audio File Type**: Select whether the question's audio file is "Yes" or "No".
* **Question Video File Type**: Select whether the question's video file is "Yes" or "No".
* **Question Audio**: Enter the audio associated with the question.
* **Question Video**: Enter the video associated with the question.
* **Question Audio Transcript File**: Enter the file associated with the question's audio transcript.
* **Question Video Transcript File**: Enter the file associated with the question's video transcript.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have reviewed the previous stage of the hiring process before proceeding with this form.
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the candidate's information is correctly evaluated.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the hiring team or a designated person in the organization.
