# Biology's Staff Hire/Recruitment Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect essential details for hiring and recruitment purposes within the Biology department. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately to ensure that all relevant stakeholders are notified and informed about new staff additions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the **Department Head Name**, which is the individual responsible for the department where the new staff member will be working.
2. Next, specify the **Proposed Position Title** for the new staff member.
3. Select the **Position Type** that best describes the role of the new staff member (Faculty, Research Staff, Administrative, or Technical Support).
4. Choose the **Primary Funding Source** that will be financing the new position (Department Budget, Grant Funding, University Endowment, or External Sponsorship).
5. If applicable, select the **Recruitment Urgency** level, which indicates the importance of filling the position (Immediate - High Priority, Planned - Next Quarter, or Future Need).
6. Describe the **Key Responsibilities and Duties** of the new staff member, including their core responsibilities and tasks.
7. Provide the **Expected Salary Range - Minimum** and **Expected Salary Range - Maximum** for the new staff member.
8. Select the **Internal Approvals** required for the new position (if applicable).
9. Enter the **Date of Notification** when the form is submitted.
10. Enter the **Contact Person for HR Coordination**, who will be responsible for further processing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department Head Name**: Enter the name of the individual responsible for the department where the new staff member will be working.
* **Proposed Position Title**: Enter the title of the new position, which should be clear and concise.
* **Position Type**: Choose the category that best describes the new staff member's role within the department (Faculty, Research Staff, Administrative, or Technical Support).
* **Primary Funding Source**: Select the main funding source for the new position (Department Budget, Grant Funding, University Endowment, or External Sponsorship).
* **Recruitment Urgency**: Indicate the urgency level of filling the position (Immediate - High Priority, Planned - Next Quarter, or Future Need).
* **Key Responsibilities and Duties**: Describe the main tasks and responsibilities of the new staff member, including any specific duties or tasks they will be expected to perform.
* **Expected Salary Range - Minimum** and **Expected Salary Range - Maximum**: Enter the expected annual salary range for the new staff member.
* **Internal Approvals**: If required, select any internal approvals needed for the new position.
* **Date of Notification**: Enter the date when the form is submitted.
* **Contact Person for HR Coordination**: Enter the contact person responsible for further processing the form.
