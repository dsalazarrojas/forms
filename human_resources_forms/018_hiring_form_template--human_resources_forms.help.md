# Hiring Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiring Form Template is a tool used to gather information about job applicants and support the hiring process. It provides a structured way to collect and store data about job postings and candidates.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form fields and understand the type of information they collect.
*   Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the job posting and applicant.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

Here's a breakdown of each field and its purpose:

*   **Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the job applicant or job title.
*   **Email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the applicant or the contact email for the job posting.
*   **Job Title** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title or position name.
*   **Job Description** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a description of the job responsibilities and requirements.
*   **Job Type** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the types of job categories, such as full-time, part-time, or remote.
*   **Location** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the job is based.
*   **Experience** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the years of experience required for the job.
*   **Salary** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the salary range for the job.
*   **Skills** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the skills required for the job.
*   **Education** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the educational background required for the job.
*   **Resume** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Upload or link to the applicant's resume.
*   **Portfolio** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Upload or link to the applicant's portfolio.
*   **LinkedIn** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the applicant's LinkedIn profile link.
*   **GitHub** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the applicant's GitHub profile link.
*   **Twitter** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the applicant's Twitter profile link.
*   **Instagram** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the applicant's Instagram profile link.
*   **Website** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the applicant's personal or professional website link.
*   **Phone** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the applicant or the contact phone number for the job posting.
*   **Address** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the physical address of the job location.
*   **Date of Birth** (`20`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the applicant.
*   **Date of Application** (`21`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the applicant applied for the job.
*   **Date of Hire** (`22`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the applicant was hired.
*   **Job Status** (`23`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current job status of the applicant.
*   **Job Type** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the job type, such as full-time, part-time, or remote.
*   **Job Level** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the job level, such as entry level, mid-level, or senior level.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete all required fields.
*   Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
*   Review the form before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
*   Use the form consistently to maintain data integrity and simplify the hiring process.
