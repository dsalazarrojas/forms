# Employee File Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request access to employee files. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Submit the form to the designated approver (usually HR or IT).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter your employee number, which can be found on your employee ID badge or in the company directory.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge or in the company directory.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the departments you are a member of. This may include your primary department, as well as any secondary departments you are a part of.
	+ Options: Accounting and Finance
	+ Operations
	+ Engineering
	+ Sales and Marketing
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are requesting access to the file. This is not required, but will help us track when your request was made.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief explanation for why you are requesting access to the file. This will help the approver understand the purpose of your request.
* **File Number** (`file_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the file number of the file you are requesting access to. This can usually be found on the file's label or in the file's metadata.
