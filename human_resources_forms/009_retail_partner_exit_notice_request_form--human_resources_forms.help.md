# Retail Partner Exit Notice Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a notice period for a retail partner, which is a formal process for the partner to exit the partnership. This form helps in documenting the partner's notice period, payment terms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the partner type from the given options (e.g., Partner, Manager, Store Manager, etc.)
2. Enter the date when the partner wants to exit the retail partnership.
3. Choose the reason for exiting the partnership from the given options (e.g., Unsatisfactory performance, Personal reasons, etc.).
4. Select the notice period (e.g., 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, etc.).
5. Enter the amount of notice to be paid to the partner.
6. Choose the payment term for the notice period (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Bi-monthly, etc.).
7. Select the payment schedule for the notice period (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Bi-monthly, etc.).
8. Choose the payment frequency for the notice period (e.g., Every 4 weeks, Every 6 weeks, etc.).
9. Enter the value of the payment frequency (e.g., number of weeks, etc.).
10. Enter the start date of the payment schedule.
11. Enter the end date of the payment schedule.
12. Select the frequency of payment (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Bi-monthly, etc.).
13. Choose the method of payment (e.g., Check, EFT, Wire Transfer, etc.).
14. Select the bank account for payment (e.g., Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Chase, etc.).
15. Enter the last payment date.
16. Enter the last payment amount.
17. Enter the next payment date.
18. Enter the next payment amount.
19. Select the payment status (e.g., Paid, Unpaid, Partially paid, Overdue, etc.).
20. Enter the partner's name.
21. Enter the manager's name.
22. Enter the termination date.
23. Enter the reason for termination.
24. Select the termination status (e.g., Active, Terminated, On Leave, etc.).

Note: All fields are optional except for the partner type which is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Type** (required): Select the type of partner requesting the notice period.
* **Retail Partner Exit Date** (optional): Enter the date when the partner wants to exit the partnership.
* **Reason for Exit** (optional): Choose the reason for exiting the partnership.
* **Notice Period** (optional): Select the notice period (e.g., 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, etc.).
* **Notice Amount** (optional): Enter the amount of notice to be paid to the partner.
* **Notice Payment Term** (optional): Choose the payment term for the notice period (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Bi-monthly, etc.).
* **Notice Payment Schedule** (optional): Select the payment schedule for the notice period (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Bi-monthly, etc.).
* **Notice Payment Frequency** (optional): Choose the payment frequency for the notice period (e.g., Every 4 weeks, Every 6 weeks, etc.).
* **Notice Payment Frequency Value** (optional): Enter the value of the payment frequency (e.g., number of weeks, etc.).
* **Notice Payment Start Date** (optional): Enter the start date of the payment schedule.
* **Notice Payment End Date** (optional): Enter the end date of the payment schedule.
* **Payment Frequency** (optional): Select the frequency of payment (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Bi-monthly, etc.).
* **Payment Method** (optional): Choose the method of payment (e.g., Check, EFT, Wire Transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Bank Account** (optional): Select the bank account for payment (e.g., Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Chase, etc.).
* **Last Payment Date** (optional): Enter the last payment date.
* **Last Payment Amount** (optional): Enter the last payment amount.
* **Next Payment Date** (optional): Enter the next payment date.
* **Next Payment Amount** (optional): Enter the next payment amount.
* **Payment Status** (optional): Select the payment status (e.g., Paid, Unpaid, Partially paid, Overdue, etc.).
* **Partner Name** (optional): Enter the name of the partner.
* **Manager Name** (optional): Enter the name of the manager.
* **Termination Date** (optional): Enter the date of termination.
* **Reason for Termination** (optional): Enter the reason for termination.
* **Termination Status** (optional): Select the termination status (e.g., Active, Terminated, On Leave, etc.).

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct partner type to ensure accurate processing of the form.
* Enter accurate payment details to avoid delays in the payment process.
* Review all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
