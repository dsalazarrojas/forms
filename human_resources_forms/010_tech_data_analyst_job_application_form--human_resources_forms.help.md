# Tech Data Analyst Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Data Analyst Job Application Form is designed to collect information from candidates interested in applying for a Tech Data Analyst job position. This form helps our team assess the candidate's qualifications, experience, and availability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Upload your Resume in the Resume field.
5. Select your availability dates in the Availability fields.
6. Select your experience and skills in the Experience and Skills fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (text)
• **First Name**: Your first name (required)

### Last Name (text)
• **Last Name**: Your last name (required)

### Email (email)
• **Email**: Your email address (required)

### Phone (text)
• **Phone**: Your phone number (required)

### Resume (text)
• **Resume**: Attach your resume or CV to demonstrate your qualifications (required)

### Resume type (text)
• **Resume type**: Select the type of resume you are attaching (not required)

### Availability (date)
• **Availability**: Select the date you are available to start (required)

### Availability 1 (date), Availability 2 (date), Availability 3 (date), Availability 4 (date), Availability 5 (date)
• **Availability 1, 2, 3, 4, 5**: Select the additional dates you are available (required)

### Skills (select_multiple)
• **Skills**: Select your technical skills (e.g., programming languages, tools)

### Experience (select_multiple)
• **Experience 1, 2, 3**: Select the relevant experience for the position (required)

### Education (select_multiple)
• **Education**: Select your level of education (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, PhD)

### Job Type (select_one)
• **Job Type**: Select the type of job you are applying for (required)

### Job Title (text)
• **Job Title**: Enter the job title you are applying for (required)

### Location (select_one)
• **Location**: Select the location you are available to work at (not required)

### Job Description (text)
• **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job you are applying for (required)

### Salary (select_one)
• **Salary**: Select the salary you are expecting for the position (not required)

### Availability 1 (date), Availability 2 (date), Availability 3 (date), Availability 4 (date), Availability 5 (date)
• **Availability 1, 2, 3, 4, 5**: Select the additional dates you are available (required)

### Skills 1 (text), Skills 2 (text)
• **Skills 1, 2**: Enter your additional skills (optional)

### Experience 1 (select_multiple), Experience 2 (select_multiple), Experience 3 (select_multiple)
• **Experience 1, 2, 3**: Select the additional relevant experience for the position (required)

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Attach a clear and concise resume or CV.
* Provide specific details about your availability and experience.
* Proofread your application before submitting it.
