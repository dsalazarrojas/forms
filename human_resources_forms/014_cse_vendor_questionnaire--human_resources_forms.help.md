# CSE Vendor Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Legal Name or Entity Name**
(required)
The full legal name or entity name as it appears on tax documents.

### 2. **Taxpayer Identification Number - SSN or EIN**
(required)
Enter the Taxpayer Identification Number, SSN (Social Security Number), or EIN (Employer Identification Number).

### 3. **Contact Email Address**
(required)
Enter the contact email address of the vendor.

### 4. **Business Mailing Address**
(required)
Enter the business mailing address of the vendor.

### 5. **Description of Services Provided**
(required)
Provide a specific description of the services to be performed by the vendor.

### 6. **Expected Start Date**
(required)
Enter the expected start date for the services to be provided.

### 7. **Expected End Date** (if applicable)
(optional)
If applicable, enter the expected end date for the services to be provided.

### 8. **Do you perform services for other entities?**
(required)
Select 'True' if the vendor performs services for other entities, 'False' otherwise.

### 9. **Is your business incorporated?**
(required)
Select 'True' if the vendor's business is incorporated, 'False' otherwise.

### 10. **Are you related to any MICA employees?**
(required)
Select 'True' if the vendor is related to any MICA employees, 'False' otherwise.

### 11. **Agreed Compensation Rate**
(required)
Enter the agreed-upon compensation rate for the services to be provided.

### 12. **Total Not-to-Exceed Amount**
(required)
Enter the total not-to-exceed amount for the services to be provided.

### 13. **Date of Submission**
(required)
Enter the date on which this questionnaire was submitted.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* Provide specific and detailed information about the services to be provided.
* Ensure the agreed-upon compensation rate and total not-to-exceed amount are accurate and reasonable.
* If you are related to any MICA employees, disclose this information to avoid any potential conflicts of interest.
