# Payroll Portal Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Portal Access Request Form is a request form used to ask for access to the payroll portal for employees. This form is used to submit a formal request for access to payroll information and is typically submitted by employees who need access to specific payroll areas for their job responsibilities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee information section to provide the necessary details about the employee who is making the request.
2. Choose the requested access level in the "Requested Access" section to specify what type of access is needed.
3. Enter a reason for the request in the "Request Reason" section to help the approver understand the purpose of the request.
4. Provide the approver's information, including their email address, in the "Approver Information" section.
5. Include the approver's phone number and name in the "Approver Phone" and "Submitting Manager" sections.
6. The "Approving Manager" section is for the approving manager's name.
7. In the "Comments" section, include any additional information that may be helpful for the approver.
8. Choose a date for the request in the "Date Requested" section to indicate when the access is needed.
9. Choose a date for the approval in the "Date Approved" section once the request has been approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information (form_1)**: Enter the employee's name and other relevant details.
* **Job Title (form_2)**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Requested Access (form_3)**: Select the type of access needed, such as "Accounting" or "Payroll".
* **Request Reason (form_4)**: Explain why the access is being requested.
* **Approver Information (form_6)**: Enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone (form_7)**: Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Submitting Manager (form_8)**: Enter the submitting manager's name.
* **Approving Manager (form_9)**: Enter the approving manager's name.
* **Comments (form_10)**: Include any additional comments that may be helpful for the approver.
* **Date Requested (form_11)**: Choose the date when the access is needed.
* **Date Approved (form_12)**: Choose the date when the access is approved.
