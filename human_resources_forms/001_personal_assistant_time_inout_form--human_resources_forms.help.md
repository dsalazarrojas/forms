# Personal Assistant Time InOut Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Assistant Time InOut Form is used to track the working hours rendered by employees to clients or customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the Time In and Time Out details for the task you worked on. Make sure to enter the correct date and time you started and finished working on the task.
2. Enter the Employee ID of the person who rendered the hours.
3. Enter the hours rendered for the task.
4. If required, enter the hours billed for the task.
5. Choose the Submit or Cancel option to complete or cancel the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time In** (`time_in`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you started working on the task.
* **Date In** (`date_in`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you started working on the task.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee who rendered the hours.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you finished working on the task.
* **Date Out** (`date_out`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you finished working on the task.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the Employee ID of the person who rendered the hours.
* **Hours Rendered** (`hours_rendered`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours rendered for the task.
* **Hours Billed** (`hours_billed`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours billed for the task.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Submit" to complete and save the form, or "Cancel" to discard the changes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any comments about the task or working hours.
* **Form Assigned To** (`form_assigned_to`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the form assigned to (optional).
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required: false): Enter the category of the task or working hours (optional).
* **Category Name** (`category_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the category (optional).
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, `text`, required: false): Enter the category of the form (optional).
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, required: false): Displayed the name of the person who created the form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, `text`, required: false): Displayed the version of the form.
* **Modified By** (`modified_by`, `text`, required: false): Displayed the name of the person who modified the form.
* **Modified Date** (`modified_date`, `date`, required: false): Displayed the date the form was modified.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, `date`, required: false): Displayed the date the form was created.
* **Modified Time** (`modified_time`, `time`, required: false): Displayed the time the form was modified.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required: false): Displayed the ID of the form.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): Displayed the name of the user.
* **Date Time** (`date_time`, `date`, required: false): Displayed the date and time the form was created.
