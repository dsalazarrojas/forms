# professional_development_leave_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Professional Development Leave Request" form is used to request professional development leave from an employee. This form helps HR or management evaluate and approve or deny such requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "yes" or "no" to indicate if you're requesting professional development leave. If you're requesting something else, select "Other" and provide details in the "comments" field.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
3. Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel record.
4. Select the start and end dates of your leave period.
5. Select any relevant training courses you'll be attending during your leave.
6. Choose whether your manager has approved your request or select "Other" if it's pending.
7. Provide any comments or additional information regarding your request.
8. Your manager can add comments or feedback regarding your request.
9. Enter the tool or resource assigned to you for your professional development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Professional Development Leave** (`yes,no,Other`, required): Indicate if you're requesting professional development leave. If "Other", please provide details in the "comments" field.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select the start date of your leave period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Select the end date of your leave period.
* **Training Course** (`training_course`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any relevant training courses you'll be attending during your leave.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether your manager has approved your request or select "Other" if it's pending.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Provide any comments or additional information regarding your request.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `note`, required): Your manager can add comments or feedback regarding your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool or resource assigned to you for your professional development.
