# Workstation Ergonomic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the ergonomic comfort and safety of a workstation, ensuring that it meets the needs and expectations of employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Workstation Ergonomic Assessment Form**: Enter a brief description of the workstation, if necessary.
2. **Employee**: Enter the name of the employee using the workstation.
3. **Supervisor**: Enter the name of the supervisor or manager responsible for the employee's workstation.
4. **Workstation ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the workstation.
5. **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the ergonomic assessment.
6. **Ergonomic Score**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the workstation meets ergonomic standards.
7. **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the workstation's ergonomic comfort and safety.
8. **Recommendations**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the workstation requires any ergonomic recommendations.
9. **Workstation Ergonomic Rating**: Select 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor' to rate the overall ergonomic comfort and safety of the workstation.
10. **Ergonomic Risk**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the workstation poses any ergonomic risks.
11. **Action Plans**: Enter any planned actions or changes to improve the workstation's ergonomic comfort and safety.
12. **Risks**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each identified risk factor to indicate its severity.
13. **Adjustments**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each identified adjustment factor to indicate its severity.
14. **Ergonomic Risk Factors**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each identified ergonomic risk factor to indicate its severity.
15. **Corrective Actions**: Enter any corrective actions or changes to address any identified risks or comfort issues.
