# New Shift Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Shift Request Form is used to collect information from employees for a new shift request. This form is used by managers or administrators to process the request and ensure that the employee's shift is scheduled correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name as it appears on your paycheck in the field "Employee Name".
2. Enter the start time of the new shift in the field "Shift Start Time".
3. Enter the end time of the new shift in the field "Shift End Time".
4. Enter the date of the new shift in the field "Shift Date".
5. Enter the duration of the new shift in the field "Shift Duration".
6. If you have any comments or notes about the new shift, enter them in the field "Comment".
7. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the field "Will you work the shift?".
8. If you have any conflicts or requests for the new shift, enter them in the field "Conflicts or Requests".
9. If you would like to pick up additional shifts, enter them in the field "Additional Shifts".
10. If you have any conflicts or requests for the additional shifts, enter them in the field "New Shift Request Form 10".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your paycheck.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of the new shift.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of the new shift.
* **Shift Date**: Enter the date of the new shift.
* **Shift Duration**: Enter the duration of the new shift in hours.
* **Comment**: Enter any comments or notes about the new shift. (Optional)
* **Will you work the shift?**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you will work the shift.
* **Conflicts or Requests**: Enter any conflicts or requests you have for the new shift. (Optional)
* **Additional Shifts**: Enter any additional shifts you would like to pick up. (Optional)
* **New Shift Request Form 10**: Enter any conflicts or requests you have for the additional shifts. (Optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your shift request is processed correctly.
* If you have previously submitted a shift request, please do not submit another request.
* Please enter the correct date of the new shift to ensure that your shift is scheduled correctly.
