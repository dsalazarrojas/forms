# Incident Injury Reporting Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The incident injury reporting form template is a comprehensive document used for reporting incidents, injuries, or near misses within an organization. Its purpose is to gather detailed information about an incident, including its causes, consequences, and the subsequent actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by describing the incident in the **incident_date** field, providing the date it occurred.
2.  Enter the time of the incident in the **incident_time** field.
3.  Specify the location where the incident took place in the **incident_location** field.
4.  Provide the employer's information in the **incident_employer_info** field.
5.  Write a detailed description of the incident in the **incident_description** field.
6.  Select the type of injury that occurred in the **incident_injury_type** field.
7.  Indicate which part of the body was injured in the **incident_injured_part** field.
8.  Choose the status of the incident from the options provided in the **incident_status** field.
9.  Record the response from the employer in the **incident_employer_response** field.
10. Enter the name of the safety officer in charge in the **incident_safety_officer** field.
11.  Select the reporting status of the incident from the options in the **incident_reporting_status** field.
12. Enter the date when the incident was reported in the **incident_reporting_date** field.
13. Enter the time when the incident was reported in the **incident_reporting_time** field.
14. Write a reason for reporting the incident in the **incident_reporting_reason** field.
15.  Add any additional comments or notes about the incident in the **incident_notes** field.
16. If necessary, provide the name and contact information of the employer who reported the incident in the **incident_injury_reporting_form_template_employer** field.
17. Enter the name of the employee who reported the incident in the **incident_employer** field.
18. Specify the name of the supervisor or manager in charge in the **incident_supervisor** field.
19. List the employees involved in the incident in the **incident_employees_involved** field.
20. Identify the employees who were injured in the incident in the **incident_injured_employees** field.
21. Enter the response from the supervisor in charge in the **incident_supervisor_response** field.
22. Describe any safety meetings that were held regarding the incident in the **incident_safety_meeting** field.
23. Schedule any follow-up meetings related to the incident in the **incident_followup_meeting** field.
24. Choose the status of the incident review in the **incident_review_status** field.
25. Enter the date of the incident review in the **incident_review_date** field.
