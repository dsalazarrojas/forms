# Security Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for security-related job positions. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to increase your chances of being considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Double-check your contact information to ensure it is up-to-date.
3. Be honest when answering the "Background Check" question.
4. Provide clear and concise answers to the "Job History" and "Employment History" questions.
5. Attach any supporting documents or references as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (contact_info)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your active and accessible email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Provide a phone number and/or any other contact information you wish to share.
* **Job Title (job_title)**: Enter the job title you are applying for, as listed in the job description.
* **Job History (job_history)**: Provide a clear and concise description of your previous job experiences, including dates and job titles.
* **Military Service (military_service)**: If you have any military experience, describe it briefly.
* **Background Check (background_check)**: Indicate if you are willing to undergo a background check. Please answer truthfully.
* **Employment History (employment_history)**: List your previous employment history, including company names, job titles, and dates of employment.
* **Education (education)**: List your relevant education and training, including degrees and institutions.
* **References (references)**: Provide the names and contact information of at least two professional references.
* **Criminal Background (criminal_background)**: Indicate if you have any past convictions or pending charges.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers.
* Attach any supporting documents or references as required.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
