# Employee Internal Coaching Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture your interest in internal coaching, a process to help you improve your performance, achieve your career goals, and enhance your growth within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If a field is required (marked as "Yes" in the form), it is mandatory to fill out.
3. You can select multiple options in the "Coaching Interest" field by holding Ctrl + Click (Windows) or Cmd + Click (Mac) on the desired options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Application**:
	* Enter a brief description of why you want to apply for internal coaching.
	* Please provide a maximum of 1000 characters.
* **Employment Status**:
	+ Select your current employment status:
		- Active: Currently employed with the company.
		- Terminated: Your employment has ended.
		- Retired: You have retired from the company.
		- Resigned: You have left the company voluntarily.
		- Leave of Absence: You are on an approved leave of absence.
* **Employment Start Date**:
	* Enter your start date of employment with the company in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Job Title**:
	* Enter your current job title or position.
* **Coaching Interest**:
	+ Select all interests that apply:
		- Leadership Development: Improvement in leadership skills.
		- Career Advancement: Career growth and progression.
		- Personal Growth: Personal development and self-improvement.
		- Career Transition: Transitioning to a new role or career.
		- Well-being: Improvement in well-being and work-life balance.
* **Department**:
	* Enter your department name or team.
* **Supervisor Name**:
	* Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Coach Name**:
	* Enter the name of your proposed internal coach.
* **Training Goals**:
	+ Enter your coaching goals and objectives.
* **Application Status**:
	+ Select your application status:
		- In Progress: Your application is still being processed.
		- Completed: Your internal coaching has been completed.
		- Declined: Your application was declined or not approved.
* **Coaching Location**:
	* Enter your preferred coaching location (e.g., office, off-site, remote).
* **Preferred Coaching Location**:
	+ Enter your preferred coaching location.
* **Coaching Start Date**:
	* Enter the date you would like to start internal coaching in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

Please note that some fields are not required but are included for your convenience and to help provide more information. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department or internal coaching program administrators.
