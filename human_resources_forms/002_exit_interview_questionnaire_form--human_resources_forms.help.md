# Exit Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exit Interview Questionnaire Form is designed to gather feedback from departing employees to help the company understand their experiences and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as accurately as possible, based on your experience while working at the company.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all the options that apply to your experience.
3. For the "comments" field, provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### company_formation
* **How was your overall experience at this company?** (Text field)
	+ Please provide a brief description of your overall experience at the company.
### employee_type
* **What type of job were you in while here?** (Select one option)
	+ Choose the category that best describes your job position while working at the company.
	+ Options: Manager, Non-Mgr, Other
### job_satisfaction
* **How was your level of job satisfaction while here?** (Number field)
	+ Rate your level of job satisfaction on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
### job_security
* **How secure did you feel in your position?** (Number field)
	+ Rate your level of job security on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
### manager_satisfaction
* **How would you rate your manager's performance?** (Select multiple options)
	+ Choose all the options that apply to your experience with your manager.
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
### work_life_balance
* **How would you rate your work-life balance while at this company?** (Time field)
	+ Rate your work-life balance on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
### salary
* **What was your salary while here?** (Date field)
	+ Please provide your salary information while working at the company.
### reason_for_departure
* **What is the reason for your departure?** (Select multiple options)
	+ Choose all the options that apply to your reason for leaving.
	+ Options: Retirement, Other, Career advancement, Burnout, Health issues, Family issues, Lack of challenge, Other (please specify)
### comments
* **Comments or suggestions for improvement**
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the company.
### contact_info
* **Is there anything we should contact you regarding this form?** (Email field)
	+ If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please provide your email address.
 
 ## Tips
 Please provide accurate and honest information to help the company improve and grow.
