# Flexible Work Schedule Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Flexible Work Schedule Approval Form is designed to enable employees to request adjustments to their work schedule, allowing for better work-life balance and increased flexibility. This form is used by employees to submit their schedule changes to their managers for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Employee Request section with your requested work schedule changes.
2. Select the relevant manager approvals for your changes from the Manager Approvals section.
3. Input the dates you will be working on your new schedule in the Dates Worked section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Request** (1/Employee Request/text/Optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the changes you are requesting to your work schedule.
* **Manager Approvals** (2/Manager Approvals/select_multiple/Optional): Choose the managers who will be reviewing and approving or declining your schedule changes. You may select one or more of the options.
* **Dates Worked** (3/Dates Worked/date/Optional): Enter the dates you will be working on your new schedule. Ensure to format the dates in the correct format (month/day/year).
