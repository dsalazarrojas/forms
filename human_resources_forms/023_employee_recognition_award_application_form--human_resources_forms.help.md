# Employee Recognition Award Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Award Application Form is used to nominate colleagues for their outstanding work and efforts. This form helps the nominator to provide a detailed description of the nominee's achievements and contributions to the company, along with any supporting documents or attachments that can be provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the name of the employee you are nominating.
2. Describe the employee's exceptional contributions and achievements that warrant recognition.
3. Upload any supporting attachments that can help demonstrate the nominee's work and its impact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nomination
* **Nomination**: Enter the name of the employee you are nominating. Please enter the employee's full name as it appears on their personnel records.
* **Department Details**: Describe the department or team where the employee's outstanding work took place.
* **Description**: Describe the specific achievements and contributions of the nominee that demonstrate their commitment and dedication to the company.
* **Upload Attachments**: You can attach relevant documents or images that support the nominee's achievements, such as certificates or project reports. This can help to provide additional evidence of the nominee's work and its impact.

### Tips
* Make sure to include specific examples or dates when describing the nominee's achievements.
* If the nominee has made significant contributions to multiple projects or teams, please describe each separately.
* Remember to keep the description clear and concise, focusing on the key aspects of the nominee's work that demonstrate their value to the company.
* Ensure that the attachments are relevant and directly support the nominee's achievements.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
