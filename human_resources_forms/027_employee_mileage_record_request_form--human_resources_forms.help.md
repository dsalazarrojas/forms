# employee_mileage_record_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record employee mileage for business-related travel, allowing the company to track and manage expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the Employee.
2. Select the Start Date and End Date of your business trip.
3. Enter the Distance traveled during the trip.
4. Provide a brief description of the Purpose of your trip.
5. If applicable, select the Assigned Tool used during the trip (e.g., "chatjimmy" is likely a company-provided tool).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: Enter your name as an employee of the company.
* **Start Date**: Select the date when your business trip started.
* **End Date**: Select the date when your business trip ended.
* **Distance**: Enter the total distance traveled during your trip in kilometers or miles.
* **Purpose**: Provide a brief description of the purpose or goal of your trip.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you used a company-provided tool during your trip, select the name of the tool (e.g., "chatjimmy") from the list or choose "Other" if it's not listed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately to ensure proper tracking and reimbursement.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your manager or HR representative for clarification.
