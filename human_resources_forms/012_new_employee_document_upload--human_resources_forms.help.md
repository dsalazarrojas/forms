# new_employee_document_upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to upload new employee documents for new employees, which helps the HR team to keep track of the documents required for onboarding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the status of the new uploads for the new employee.
2. Upload the resume for the new employee.
3. Enter the proof of address for the new employee.
4. Upload any onboarding documents for the new employee.
5. Choose the type of document being uploaded.
6. Set the date and time of upload.
7. Choose the department of the new employee.
8. Enter the job title of the new employee.
9. Enter the employee ID of the new employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_uploads**: This field allows you to select the status of the new uploads for the new employee. You can choose between "Yes" and "No" to indicate whether new uploads are required.
* **resume**: Upload the resume for the new employee.
* **proof_of_address**: Enter the proof of address for the new employee.
* **onboarding_documents**: Upload any onboarding documents for the new employee. You can choose between "Yes" and "No" to indicate whether onboarding documents are required.
* **upload_date**: Enter the date on which the document was uploaded.
* **upload_time**: Enter the time on which the document was uploaded.
* **document_type**: Choose the type of document being uploaded.
* **department**: Enter the department of the new employee.
* **job_title**: Enter the job title of the new employee.
* **employee_id**: Enter the employee ID of the new employee.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct status for new uploads for the new employee.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the HR team for assistance.
