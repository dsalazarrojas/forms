# executive_mentor_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Mentor Recommendation Form is used to recommend an executive mentor for a project or program. This form helps to gather essential information about the mentor, including their name, organization, contact details, and the nature of their relationship with the project or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information. The fields marked as "required" are essential for processing the recommendation.
2. Double-check that all required fields have been filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Mentor Name** (`mentor_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the executive mentor being recommended.

### **Organization** (`organization`, text, required)
Enter the name of the organization or institution the mentor is affiliated with.

### **Title** (`title`, text, optional)
Enter the title or position of the executive mentor.

### **Email** (`email`, email, required)
Enter the email address of the executive mentor.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
Enter the phone number of the executive mentor.

### **Relationship** (`mentor_relationship`, text, required)
Describe the nature of the executive mentor's relationship with the project or program.

### **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required)
Enter the start date of the executive mentor's involvement with the project or program.

### **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required)
Enter the end date of the executive mentor's involvement with the project or program.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the executive mentor's details are correctly recorded.
- If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the project or program coordinator for guidance.
