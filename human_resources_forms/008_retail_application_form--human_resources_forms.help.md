# Retail Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Application Form is used to capture information from retail employees to create or update their job records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your store name, which is the name of the retail store where you work.
2. Provide your name and employee number.
3. Choose your position and available work hours (full time or part time, seasonal, or other).
4. Enter your contact number and email address.
5. List your emergency contact's name and contact number, including their relationship to you.
6. Select the shift schedule you will be working (AM, PM, or all day).
7. Choose your job type and status (active or inactive).
8. Enter your job type ID and job status ID.
9. List your store ID and employee ID if applicable.
10. Set your available hours start time, store opening time, work break time, and work end time.
11. Select the days of the week you will be working.
12. List your job description.
13. Set your shift schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, required): Enter the name of the retail store where you work.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required): Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Position** (`position`, required): Select your position within the store (e.g., sales associate, manager, etc.).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, required): Select the date you started working at the store.
* **Available Work Hours** (`available_work_hours`, required): Select your available work hours (full time, part time, seasonal, etc.).
* **Store Manager Name** (`store_manager`, required): Enter the name of your store manager.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Employee** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Select how you are related to the employee (parent, spouse, sibling, or other).
* **Available Hours Start** (`available_hours_start`, required): Set your available hours start time.
* **Store Opening Time** (`store_opening_time`, required): Set the store opening time.
* **Store Closing Time** (`store_closing_time`, required): Set the store closing time.
* **Work Break Time** (`work_break_time`, required): Set your work break time.
* **Work End Time** (`work_end_time`, required): Set your work end time.
* **Days of Week** (`days_of_week`, required): Select the days of the week you will be working.
* **Shift Schedule** (`shift_schedule`, required): Select your shift schedule (AM, PM, or all day).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, required): Enter your job type (e.g., sales associate, manager, etc.).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, optional): Enter a brief job description.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, required): Select your job status (active or inactive).
* **Job Type ID** (`job_type_id`, required): Enter your job type ID.
* **Job Status ID** (`job_status_id`, optional): Enter your job status ID.
* **Store ID** (`store_id`, optional): Enter your store ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, optional): Enter your employee ID.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
