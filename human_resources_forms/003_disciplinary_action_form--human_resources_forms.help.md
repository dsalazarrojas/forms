# Disciplinary Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Action Form is used to document and record the reason and outcome of a disciplinary action taken against an employee. This form helps to ensure that all disciplinary actions are properly documented and communicated to the employee, and that any necessary steps are taken to resolve the issue and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **To complete this form, find the Employee section and enter the ID number of the employee involved in the incident.**
2. **Enter the reason for the disciplinary action in the Reason for Disciplinary Action section. This is an opportunity to clearly and concisely explain the incident and the reason for the action.**
3. **Enter the Date of Incident, which is the date when the incident occurred.**
4. **In the Disciplinary Action Taken section, select all the actions that were taken against the employee.**
5. **Provide any additional notes or information about the incident in the Notes section.**
6. **The Manager Comments section is an opportunity for the manager to provide additional feedback or context about the incident.**
7. **Finally, complete the form by providing the Employee Signature and Manager Signature, which serves as verification that the employee and manager have reviewed and acknowledged the form.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID number of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Reason for Disciplinary Action** (`reason_for_disciplinary_action`, text, optional): A clear and concise explanation of the reason for the disciplinary action.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, optional): The date when the incident occurred.
* **Disciplinary Action Taken** (`disciplinary_action_taken`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the actions taken against the employee.
