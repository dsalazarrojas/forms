# Applicant Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Applicant Screening Form is a tool used to collect and evaluate applicant information for various positions. This form helps hiring managers or screening teams quickly and accurately gather necessary details about each applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Personal Information**: Fill in your first name, last name, and email address.
2. **Contact Information**: Provide your phone number and current address.
3. **Work Experience**: If applicable, share information about your previous employer, job duration, and reason for leaving.
4. **References**: Enter the names, positions, and contact information of at least one professional reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current home address.
* **Position Applying For**: Specify the job position you are applying for.
* **Date of Birth** (Optional): Enter your date of birth if applicable.
* **Marital Status** (Optional): Select your current marital status.
* **Number of Dependents** (Optional): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Family member names and relationships** (Optional): List the names and relationships of your family members.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **How long have you worked in this field?** (Optional): Briefly describe your work experience in the field.
* **Previous Employer** (Optional): Enter the name of your previous employer.
* **Reason for leaving previous job** (Optional): Explain why you left your previous job.
* **Reference 1 - Name**: Enter the name of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 - Position**: Enter the position or title of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 - Phone**: Enter the phone number of your first reference.
* **Reference 2 - Name**: Enter the name of your second reference.
* **Reference 2 - Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of your second reference.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about a field, use your best judgment and describe the answer as clearly as possible.
* Remember to proofread your form before submission for any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
