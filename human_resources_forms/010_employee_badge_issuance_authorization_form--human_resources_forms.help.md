# Employee Badge Issuance Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request authorization for employee badges, which are digital badges that represent an employee's role, department, or permissions within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read the guide carefully**: Before you start filling out the form, take a few minutes to read this guide to understand each field's purpose.
2. **Fill out your details**: Complete the fields with your employee information as prompted.
3. **Choose your manager approval options**: Select the department(s) your manager belongs to that are relevant to your badge request.
4. **Confirm your manager's name**: Enter your manager's name to associate them with their respective department.
5. **Submit the form**: Once you've completed all the fields, submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details**
  * (Employee Details): This field collects your employee information. Enter your details as prompted.

* **Manager Approval**
  * (Manager Approval): Select the department your manager belongs to that is relevant to your badge request. You can choose multiple departments if applicable.
  * Options: HR Department, IT Department, Finance Department

* **Manager Name**
  * (Manager Name): Enter your manager's name to associate them with their respective department.
  * (Note: This field is optional but recommended for accurate processing.)

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct department(s) for your manager's approval.
*   If you're unsure about any field or its purpose, refer to this guide for clarification.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
