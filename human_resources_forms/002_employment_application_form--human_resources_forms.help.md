# employment_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The employment application form is a form used to collect essential information about job applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first text field. This is a required field, even though it is optional, as it will help you to start the form.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second text field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third text field. This should be a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your **Phone** in the fourth text field.
5. Enter your **Job Title** in the fifth text field. This is the title of the position you are applying for.
6. Enter your **Work Experience** in the sixth text field. This is a description of your work history.
7. Enter your **Education** in the seventh text field. This is a description of your educational background.
8. Select your **Skills** in the eighth field, either "Yes" if you have the skill or "No" if you do not.
9. Enter your **References** in the ninth text field. This is the name and contact information of at least one professional reference.
10. Click the **Upload Resume** button and select your resume file.
11. Click the **Upload Cover Letter** button and select your cover letter file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, name, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, name, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (text, phone, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (text, name, required): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Work Experience** (text, name, required): Enter a brief description of your work history.
* **Education** (text, name, required): Enter a description of your educational background.
* **Skills** (select multiple, name, required): Select all skills you have from the options provided.
* **References** (text, name, required): Enter the name and contact information of at least one professional reference.
* **Resume** (text, file, required): Click the "Upload Resume" button and select your resume file.
* **Cover Letter** (text, file, required): Click the "Upload Cover Letter" button and select your cover letter file.
