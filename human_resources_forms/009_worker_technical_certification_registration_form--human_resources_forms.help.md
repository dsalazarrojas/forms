# Worker Technical Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Technical Certification Registration Form is used to register a worker's technical certifications. This form is used to collect information about the worker's certifications, including the type, status, and duration of the certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's data in the "Employee Data" field.
2. Enter the contact information for the employee and their employer in the "Contact Information" field and "Employer Information" field, respectively.
3. Select the relevant certifications from the "Certifications" field.
4. Choose the "Certification Type" and "Certification Status" for the selected certification.
5. Enter the start and end dates and times for the certification, as well as the certification duration.
6. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Data
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employee registering the certification.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the employee.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Employee Data** (`employee_data`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about the employee.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter any contact information for the employee, such as an address or a message.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### Certification Information
* **Certification Information** (`certification_info`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about the certification.
* **Certifications** (`certification_list`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant certifications from the list.
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of certification.
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the certification.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the certification was granted.
* **Certification Time** (`certification_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the certification was granted.
* **Certification Duration** (`certification_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the certification.
* **Certification Number** (`certification_number`, `number`, required): Enter a unique number for the certification.
* **Certification Name** (`certification_name`, `text`, required): Enter a name for the certification.

### Employer Information
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employee's employer.
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the employer.
* **Certification Start Date** (`certification_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the certification.
* **Certification Start Time** (`certification_start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the certification.
* **Certification End Date** (`certification_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the certification.
* **Certification End Time** (`certification_end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the certification.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority for guidance.
