# Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's employment history and job preferences. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to process the employment application correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal details, including your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
2. Provide your contact information, such as Email, Contact Number, and Work Phone.
3. Fill in your job-related details, such as Job Title, Start Date, and End Date.
4. Select your job status, job type, and employment status from the provided options.
5. Choose your job role, employment type, and job category from the provided options.
6. Specify your work hours and work address (if applicable).
7. Review your application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your personal email address.
* **Job Title** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Date of Birth** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start Date** (`6`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your employment.
* **End Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your employment (if applicable).
* **Contact Number** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Work Phone** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter your work phone number (if applicable).
* **Work Email** (`10`, `email`, optional): Enter your work email address (if applicable).
* **Position** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter your current or desired job position.
* **Department** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the department where you work or would like to work in.
* **Job Status** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select your current job status (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Type** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have a job (Yes or No).
* **Job Role** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Select whether this is a full-time or part-time job (Yes or No).
* **Employment Status** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Employment Type** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have other employment (Yes or No).
* **Job Category** (`18`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable job categories.
* **Job Level** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Select your job level (High or Low).
* **Work Type** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether your job is full-time or part-time (Yes or No).
* **Work Shift** (`21`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable work shifts.
* **Other Employment Type** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter any other employment type not listed (if applicable).
* **Job Family** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the job family or category (if applicable).
* **Work Address** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter your work address (if applicable).
* **Work Hours** (`25`, `time`, optional): Enter your work hours (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check all fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the HR department for assistance.
- Ensure that you're filling out this form for the correct purpose and that the information you provide is not a duplicate of any existing employment application.
