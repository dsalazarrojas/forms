# Disciplinary Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Disciplinary Process Feedback Survey" form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with the disciplinary process within their organization. This feedback helps identify areas of improvement and informs management decisions to enhance the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your personal experience with the disciplinary process.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended fields.
4. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your company ID or personnel record.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): If you have a manager associated with your disciplinary process, please enter their name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): If you have a manager's email address, please enter it.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a manager's phone number, please enter it.
* **Reporting Officer Name** (`reporting_officer_name`, text, required/optional): If you have a reporting officer's name, please enter it.
* **Reporting Officer Email** (`reporting_officer_email`, email, required/optional): If you have a reporting officer's email address, please enter it.
* **Reporting Officer Phone** (`reporting_officer_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a reporting officer's phone number, please enter it.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Please enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Please enter your job title.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Please enter the location where you work.
* **Date of Occurrence** (`date_of_occurrence`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date the disciplinary procedure took place.
* **Time of Occurrence** (`time_of_occurrence`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time the disciplinary procedure took place.
* **Date of Discussion** (`date_of_discussion`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date the outcome of the disciplinary procedure was discussed.
* **Time of Discussion** (`time_of_discussion`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time the outcome of the disciplinary procedure was discussed.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, text, required/optional): Please describe the outcome of the disciplinary procedure.
* **Outcome Date** (`outcome_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date the outcome of the disciplinary procedure was implemented.
* **Outcome Time** (`outcome_time`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time the outcome of the disciplinary procedure was implemented.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Please provide your feedback on the disciplinary procedure.
* **How would you rate the process of your disciplinary procedure?** (`feedback`, select_one, required/optional): Please select how you rate the fairness of your experience with the disciplinary procedure.
* **What was your experience like with the disciplinary procedure?** (`feedback_description`, text, required/optional): Please describe your experience with the disciplinary procedure.
* **What could be improved?** (`improvement`, text, required/optional): Please suggest areas for improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments, please provide them here.
