# tech_company_employee_admission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect accurate information from new employees for the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the employee's details section, which includes:
	* Job Title: Select the employee's job title from the dropdown menu.
	* Date of Birth: Enter the employee's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	* Email: Enter the employee's email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter the employee's phone number.
	* Onboarding Date: Enter the date the employee is expected to start working.
	* Onboarding Location: Select the location where the employee will be working (Virtual or In-office).
2. Fill out the manager's details section, which includes:
	* Manager Name: Enter the manager's name.
	* Manager Email: Enter the manager's email address.
	* Manager Phone: Enter the manager's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, required): Select the employee's job title from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, required): Enter the date the employee is expected to start working.
* **Onboarding Location** (`onboarding_location`, required): Select the location where the employee will be working (Virtual or In-office).
* **Department** (`department`, required): Select the department the employee will be working in (HR or Marketing).
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, required): Select the current onboarding status (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, required): Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, required): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, required): Enter the manager's phone number.
