# Panel Interview Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Panel Interview Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from panelists and candidates after an interview. This feedback will help the interview committee to evaluate and improve their interviewing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading the form carefully to understand what is required.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
3. Fill in all the required fields, marked as required.
4. Use the available options or text fields to provide detailed feedback.
5. Ensure that you submit all the fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This field is a note that introduces the form and explains its purpose.
* **Interviewer Name** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter the name of the interviewer who conducted the interview.
* **Panelist Score** (`3`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate the panelist's performance from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Feedback to Panelist** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide feedback to the panelist on their performance and how they can improve.
* **Feedback to Candidate** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide feedback to the candidate on their performance and how they can improve.
* **Panelist Skills** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Evaluate the panelist's skills as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Not Evaluated
* **Candidate Skills** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Evaluate the candidate's skills as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Not Evaluated
* **Overall Experience** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Rate the overall experience of the panelist and candidate as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Not Evaluated
* **Feedback for Improvement** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide specific feedback on how the panelist and candidate can improve.
* **Recommendations for Improvement** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide recommendations on how the panelist and candidate can improve.
* **Rating** (`11`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate the candidate's performance from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.

Note: All fields marked as required are mandatory fields and must be filled out.
