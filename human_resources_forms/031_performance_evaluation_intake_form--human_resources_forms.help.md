# performance_evaluation_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "performance_evaluation_intake_form" is used for capturing performance evaluations from managers. This form is designed to collect feedback on an employee's performance, including strengths and weaknesses, areas for improvement, and action items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
4. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Select the "Feedback Type" from the dropdown menu:
	* Performance: This type of feedback is typically about an employee's accomplishments and strengths.
	* Development: This type of feedback is about an employee's growth and development opportunities.
	* Salary: This type of feedback is about an employee's salary and compensation.
7. Enter your evaluation date in the "Evaluation Date" field.
8. Optionally, enter any additional comments in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
9. Enter your performance rating in the "Performance Rating" field.
10. Enter areas of improvement in the "Areas of Improvement" field.
11. Enter action items for the employee in the "Action Items" field.
12. Enter any review notes from your manager in the "Review Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Employee ID** (text): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Manager Name** (text): Enter your manager's name.
* **Job Title** (text): Enter your job title.
* **Feedback Type** (select_one): Select the type of feedback you want to provide (Performance, Development, or Salary).
* **Evaluation Date** (date): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comments** (text): Optionally, enter any additional comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Performance Rating** (text): Enter your performance rating, which is a subjective measure of your performance.
* **Areas of Improvement** (text): Enter any areas where you need improvement.
* **Action Items** (text): Enter any action items or tasks that your manager wants you to complete.
* **Review Notes** (text): Enter any review notes from your manager.
