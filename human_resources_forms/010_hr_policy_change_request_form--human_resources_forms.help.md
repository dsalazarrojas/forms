# HR Policy Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Change Request Form is used for requesting changes to existing or new organizational policies. The form gathers information about the policy change request, including the employee who submitted the request, the proposed changes, and the reviewers and approvers assigned to review and approve the change.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and ID number.
2. **Policy Change** (`policy_change`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the policy change request.
3. **Policy Description** (`policy_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the impact of the policy change on employees and the organization.
4. **Impact Analysis** (`impact_analysis`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed analysis of the policy change.
5. **Proposed Changes** (`proposed_changes`, text, required/optional): List the proposed changes to the policy.
6. **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the policy change will take effect.
7. **Reviewer Name 1** (`reviewer_name`, select_multiple, required): Select up to two reviewers to review and comment on the policy change.
8. **Reviewers' Comments** (`reviewer_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter comments and feedback from the selected reviewers.
9. **Approver Name 1** (`approver_name`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to two approvers to approve or reject the policy change.
10. **Approvers' Comments** (`approver_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter comments and feedback from the selected approvers.
11. **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, select_multiple, required): Enter your name.
12. **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
13. **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
14. **Reviewer 2 Name** (`reviewer_2_name`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to two reviewers to review and comment on the policy change.
