# Staff Coverage Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by staff members to request staff coverage during a specific shift.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the name of your manager in the "Manager Name" field.
2.  Enter the date of the shift you are requesting coverage for in the "Shift Date" field.
3.  Enter the start and end times of your shift in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
4.  Select "Approved" or "Declined" in the "Team Leader" field, depending on your team leader's approval status.
5.  Enter your request details in the "Request Details" field.
6.  Select the status of your request in the "Coverage Approvals" field.
7.  If necessary, enter any comments or feedback in the "Manager Comments" field.
8.  If necessary, enter any comments or feedback in the "Team Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, required): This field is for the staff member to enter their manager's name.
*   **Shift Date** (`Shift Date`, date, required): This field is for the staff member to enter the date of the shift they are requesting coverage for.
*   **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, required): This field is for the staff member to enter the start time of their shift.
*   **End Time** (`End Time`, time, required): This field is for the staff member to enter the end time of their shift.
*   **Team Leader** (`Team Leader`, text, required): This field is for the staff member to select "Approved" or "Declined" for the team leader's approval status.
*   **Request Details** (`Request Details`, text, required): This field is for the staff member to enter the details of their request.
*   **Coverage Approvals** (`Coverage Approvals`, select_one, required): This field is for the staff member to select the status of their request.
*   **Manager Comments** (`Manager Comments`, note, optional): This field is for the staff member to enter any comments or feedback for their manager.
*   **Team Approval** (`Team Approval`, select_multiple, required): This field is for the manager or team leader to select "Approved" or "Declined" for the staff member's request.
*   **Approval Date** (`Approval Date`, date, required): This field is for the manager or team leader to enter the date they approved or declined the request.
*   **Request Status** (`Request Status`, select_one, required): This field is for the staff member to select the status of their request.
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): This field is for the staff member to enter any comments or feedback.
*   **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, optional): This field is for the staff member to enter their name.
*   **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date, optional): This field is for the staff member to enter the date they submitted the form.
*   **Manager Approval** (`Manager Approval`, select_one, required): This field is for the manager to select "Approved" or "Declined" for the staff member's request.
*   **Team Leader Approval** (`Team Leader Approval`, select_one, required): This field is for the team leader to select "Approved" or "Declined" for the staff member's request.
*   **Approval Status** (`Approval Status`, select_one, required): This field is for the staff member to select the status of their request.
*   **Comments By Manager** (`Comments By Manager`, note, optional): This field is for the manager to enter any comments or feedback.
*   **Approval Date Manager** (`Approval Date Manager`, date, required): This field is for the manager to enter the date they approved or declined the request.
*   **Team Comments** (`Team Comments`, note, optional): This field is for the team leader to enter any comments or feedback.
*   **Submission Date Manager** (`Submission Date Manager`, date, optional): This field is for the manager or team leader to enter the date they submitted the form.
*   **Team Approval Date** (`Team Approval Date`, date, required): This field is for the manager or team leader to enter the date they approved or declined the request.
*   **Team Approval Status** (`Team Approval Status`, select_one, required): This field is for the staff member to select the status of their request.
*   **Manager Submission Date** (`Manager Submission Date`, date, required): This field is for the manager or team leader to enter the date they submitted the form.
*   **Staff Comments** (`Staff Comments`, note, optional): This field is for the staff member to enter any additional comments or feedback.
*   **Manager Approval Date Manager** (`Manager Approval Date Manager`, date, optional): This field is for the manager to enter the date they approved or declined the request.

## Tips

*   Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Make sure to select the correct status for each field.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer back to the form's purpose and instructions.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or team leader.
*   Double-check your form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
