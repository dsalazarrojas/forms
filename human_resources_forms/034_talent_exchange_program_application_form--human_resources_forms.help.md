# Talent Exchange Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Talent Exchange Program Application Form is designed to collect information about an individual's exchange program, including personal details and rotation preferences. Completing this form will help us process your exchange request efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) after the field label.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Start Date and End Date accurately.
3. Select the correct Team Lead from the provided options.
4. Choose the preferred Rotation Location from the list of available options.
5. Provide a clear reason for your rotation request.
6. Enter the Rotation Duration if applicable.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about your rotation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name* (Text):** Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name* (Text):** Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email* (Email):** Enter your official email address that you use to communicate with your team.
* **Phone* (Text):** Enter your primary phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Start Date* (Date):** Enter the date you plan to start your rotation.
* **End Date* (Date):** Enter the date you plan to end your rotation.
* **Rotation Duration* (Text):** Enter the duration of your rotation in the format of "X months" or "X weeks".
* **Team Lead* (Select One):** Select the team lead that will be responsible for your rotation.
* **Rotation Location* (Select Multiple):** Select all locations where you plan to be during your rotation.
* **Rotation Reason* (Text):** Enter a clear and concise reason for your rotation request.
* **Rotation Comment (Optional):** Enter any additional comments or notes about your rotation.
