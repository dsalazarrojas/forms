# Medical Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide medical clearance for individuals who need to undergo a medical examination or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose the relevant options for the medical clearance questions.
3. Ensure that the issuer signs and dates the form to confirm that the individual has undergone the necessary medical examination or treatment.
4. Fill in any additional fields as necessary.
