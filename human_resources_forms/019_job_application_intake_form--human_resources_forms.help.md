# job-application-intake-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new job posting for a job candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category and subcategory of the job posting.
2. Enter the job title, job description, and other relevant details.
3. Provide the candidate's contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
4. Upload the candidate's resume.
5. Select the job posting status and source.
6. Enter the dates the job was posted and closed.
7. Choose the reviewer and provide their contact information.
8. Add any additional comments or review notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Job Posting** (text): Enter a brief description of the new job posting.
* **Job Posting Category** (select_one): Select the category of the job posting (e.g., Accounting, Marketing, Finance, etc.).
* **Job Posting Subcategory** (select_multiple): Select multiple subcategories for the job posting (e.g., Accounting, Financial, Management, Marketing, Operations, etc.).
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the job posting.
* **Job Description** (text, required): Enter a detailed description of the job posting.
* **Job Type** (select_one): Select the type of job posting (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
* **Job Posting Status** (select_one): Select the current status of the job posting (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Job Posting Source** (select_one): Select where the job posting originated (e.g., Social Media, Referral, Career Website, Job Board).
* **Job Posting Date Posted** (date, required): Enter the date the job posting was posted.
* **Job Posting Date Closed** (date): Enter the date the job posting was closed.
* **Candidate Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the job candidate.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Address** (text): Enter the candidate's address.
* **Availability** (text): Enter the candidate's availability.
* **Resume** (text, required): Upload the candidate's resume.
* **Social Links** (text): Enter any social media links for the candidate.
* **Manager Name** (text): Enter the name of the manager who hired the candidate.
* **Manager Email** (email): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (text): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Reviewer** (select_one): Select who reviewed the candidate (e.g., HR, Team Lead, Manager).
* **Reviewer Name** (text): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (email): Enter the reviewer's email address.
* **Reviewer Phone** (text): Enter the reviewer's phone number.
* **Reviewer Comments** (note): Add any additional comments from the reviewer.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out thoroughly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the HR department for clarification.
* This form will help streamline the hiring process and ensure that all necessary information is collected in one place.
