# office_organization_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from employees on the office organization, including space and resource satisfaction. This feedback will help office managers to understand the needs and concerns of employees and make data-driven decisions for office space and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address as it appears on your employee record.
2.  Select your Department from the provided list.
3.  Provide feedback on your overall satisfaction with the office space and resources using the select_multiple field.
4.  Enter your feedback details in the text field.
5.  Input the date and time you are providing feedback.
6.  Rate your satisfaction with the office space and resources on a scale of 1-5.
7.  Optionally, provide any suggestions or comments in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your employee record.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your employee record.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your employee record.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, optional): Select your department from the list provided.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply for your overall feedback on the office space and resources.
*   **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about your feedback.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are providing feedback.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are providing feedback.
*   **Space Satisfaction** (`space_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the office space on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Resources Satisfaction** (`resources_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the office resources on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Space Rating** (`space_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the office space on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Resources Rating** (`resources_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the office resources on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide any suggestions for improvement.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide any additional comments.

## Tips

*   Please provide honest and constructive feedback.
*   Make sure to rate your satisfaction with each field on a scale of 1-5.
*   Provide specific examples or details when providing feedback.
