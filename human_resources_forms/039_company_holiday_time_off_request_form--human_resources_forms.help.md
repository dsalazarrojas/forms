# Company Holiday Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for employees to request time off for holidays, providing essential details for manager approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name as it appears on company records.
2.  If you know your internal employee ID, please enter it. This is an optional field.
3.  Select the start date of your requested leave.
4.  Select the end date of your requested leave.
5.  Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Paid time off, Unpaid leave, Comp time, or Other).
6.  Provide a brief reason for your time off (optional).
7.  Enter the name of the manager who will be approving your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee full name** (`Employee full name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name as it is recorded in the company's records.
*   **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, `text`, **optional**): If known, enter your internal employee ID for reference.
*   **Leave start date** (`Leave start date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the first day of your requested leave.
*   **Leave end date** (`Leave end date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the last day of your requested leave.
*   **Leave type** (`Leave type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the type of leave you are requesting. Options include Paid time off, Unpaid leave, Comp time, and Other.
*   **Reason for time off** (`Reason for time off`, `text`, **optional**): Provide a brief reason for your leave (optional).
*   **Manager for approval** (`Manager for approval`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of your manager who will be reviewing your request.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you submit your request in advance to allow for sufficient review and approval time.
*   Double-check your entered information for accuracy before submitting.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR department.
