# E Learning Specialist Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Learning Specialist Interview Form is a tool used by HR personnel to document the process of assigning or promoting E-Learning Specialists within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options from the "eLearningSpecialistInterviewForm" section to indicate whether the candidate is a suitable E-Learning Specialist.
2. Fill in the "Job Title" field with the current job title of the candidate.
3. Provide a brief description of the candidate's job responsibilities in the "JobDescription" field.
4. Check the "ElearningCourses" field to indicate the candidate's level of expertise in E-Learning courses.
5. Enter the candidate's name in the "CandidateName" field.
6. Enter the candidate's contact email address in the "ContactEmail" field.
7. Enter the candidate's contact phone number in the "ContactPhone" field.
8. Briefly describe the candidate's E-Learning expertise in the "ElearningExpertise" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **eLearningSpecialistInterviewForm (Select Multiple)**: 
  This field allows you to select all the E-Learning roles that the candidate is suitable for. Please choose the roles that best match the candidate's skills and experience.
* **Job Title (Text)**: 
  Enter the current job title of the candidate. This will help HR to assess the candidate's current role and responsibilities.
* **Job Description (Text)**: 
  Provide a brief description of the candidate's job responsibilities. This will help HR to understand the candidate's current position within the organization.
* **ElearningCourses (Select One)**: 
  Indicate the level of the candidate's expertise in E-Learning courses. Please select the option that best describes the candidate's level of expertise.
* **CandidateName (Text)**: 
  Enter the full name of the candidate. This will help HR to identify the candidate and document their information accurately.
* **ContactEmail (Email)**: 
  Enter the candidate's contact email address. This will enable HR to communicate with the candidate and gather more information.
* **ContactPhone (Text)**: 
  Enter the candidate's contact phone number. This will help HR to contact the candidate and discuss their qualifications.
* **ElearningExpertise (Text)**: 
  Briefly describe the candidate's E-Learning expertise. Please include any relevant training, certifications, or experience the candidate has in E-Learning.
