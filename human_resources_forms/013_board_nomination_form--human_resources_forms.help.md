# Board Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for nominating candidates to join the board. It is used to collect details about both the nominator and the nominee, including their names, positions, email, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and position in the "Nominator Information" section.
2. Enter the nominee's name, position, and email in the "Nominee Information" section.
3. Select the proposed role for the nominee from the "Proposed Board Role" dropdown menu.
4. Enter a brief description of the nominee's qualifications in the "Nominee Qualifications" field.
5. Select the skills the nominee brings to the board in the "Nominee Skills" section.
6. Add any additional comments or support for the nomination in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).
7. Enter the date of the nomination in the "Nomination Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, **required**): Enter your name as a current employee of the company.
* **Nominator Position** (`nominator_position`, text, **required**): Enter your position within the company.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, **required**): Enter your company email address.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Department** (`nominee_department`, text, **required**): Enter the department of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Years at Company** (`tenure_years`, number, **required**): Enter the number of years the nominee has been with the company.
* **Proposed Board Role** (`proposed_role`, select_one, **required**): Select the proposed role for the nominee from the dropdown menu:
	+ Member at Large
	+ Secretary
	+ Treasurer
	+ Vice-Chair
	+ Advisor
* **Nominee Qualifications** (`nominee_qualifications`, text, **required**): Briefly describe the nominee's qualifications for the board position.
* **Nominee Skills** (`nominee_skills`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the skills the nominee brings to the board:
	+ Strategic Planning
	+ Financial Oversight
	+ Leadership
	+ Industry Expertise
	+ Community Relations
* **Additional Comments** (`nomination_support`, text, **optional**): Add any additional comments or support for the nomination.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the nomination.

Please ensure that you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
