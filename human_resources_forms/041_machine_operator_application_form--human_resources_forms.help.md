# machine_operator_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The machine operator application form is used to apply for jobs or positions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Employment Status** (`<employment_status>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Education Background** (`<education_background>`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your education background.
* **Machine Experience** (`<machine_experience>`, `note`, required/optional): Share any relevant machine experience you have.
* **References** (`<references>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Provide names and contact information of two professional references.
* **Skills** (`<skills>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the skills you have that match the job requirements.
* **Availability** (`<availability>`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your availability in hours per week.
* **Salary** (`<salary>`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your desired salary range.
* **Job Title** (`<job_title>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Location** (`<job_location>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the job you're applying for.
* **Notice Period** (`<notice_period>`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your available notice period.
* **Job Type** (`<job_type>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of job you are applying for.
* **Employment Type** (`<employment_type>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of employment you're looking for.
* **Job Category** (`<job_category>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the category of the job you're applying for.
* **Machine Operator Type** (`<machine_operator_type>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of machine operator you are.
* **Machines** (`<machine>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the machines you have experience with.
* **Supervisor** (`<supervisor>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your supervisor(s) for the job application.
* **Shift** (`<shift>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the shift you are available to work.
* **Work Hours** (`<work_hours>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the hours you are available to work per week.
* **Work Days** (`<work_days>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the days you are available to work.
* **Start Date** (`<start_date>`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your start date for the job.
* **End Date** (`<end_date>`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your end date for the job.
* **Employment Start Date** (`<employment_start_date>`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your employment start date.
* **Employment End Date** (`<employment_end_date>`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your employment end date.
* **Job ID** (`<job_id>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job ID you are applying for.

Note: The required fields are indicated with `required: true` in the form design.
