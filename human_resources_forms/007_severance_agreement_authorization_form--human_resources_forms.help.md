# Severance Agreement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Severance Agreement Authorization Form. This form is used to document the details of an employee's severance agreement, including their personal information, severance terms, and agreement status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the employee's severance agreement.
2. Enter the employee's details, such as their name, ID, and email.
3. Enter the manager's details, including their name and email.
4. Enter the date of the last change, severance amount, last date of pay, and termination date.
5. Select the reason for termination and agreement status.
6. Enter the notice period, pay in lieu, and pay out details.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the severance agreement.
8. Select the last pay period and agree to the severance agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Date of Last Change** (`date_of_last_change`, date, required): Enter the date of the last change.
* **Severance Amount** (`severance_amount`, number, required): Enter the severance amount.
* **Last Date of Pay** (`last_date_of_pay`, date, required): Enter the last date of pay.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required): Enter the termination date.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, select_one, required): Select the reason for termination from the provided options.
* **Severance Agreement Status** (`severance_agreement_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the agreement status from the provided options.
* **Last Date of Pay Period** (`last_date_of_pay_period`, time, required): Enter the last date of pay period.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, required): Enter the notice period.
* **Pay In Lieu** (`pay_in_lieu`, number, required): Enter the pay in lieu.
* **Notice Period Start Date** (`notice_period_start_date`, date, required): Enter the notice period start date.
* **Notice Period End Date** (`notice_period_end_date`, date, required): Enter the notice period end date.
* **Pay Period End Date** (`pay_period_end_date`, date, required): Enter the pay period end date.
* **Severance Agreement End Date** (`severance_agreement_end_date`, date, required): Enter the severance agreement end date.
* **Severance Agreement Start Date** (`severance_agreement_start_date`, date, required): Enter the severance agreement start date.
* **Last Pay Period** (`last_pay_period`, select_one, required): Select the last pay period from the provided options.
* **Severance Agreement** (`severance_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Agree or disagree to the severance agreement.
* **Termination Date 2** (`termination_date_2`, date, required): Enter the termination date 2.
* **Reason for Termination 2** (`reason_for_termination_2`, select_one, required): Select the reason for termination 2 from the provided options.
* **Severance Agreement Status 2** (`severance_agreement_status_2`, select_multiple, required): Select the agreement status 2 from the provided options.
* **Pay Out** (`pay_out`, number, required): Enter the pay out.
