# dropbox_hr_file_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit employee files, such as contracts, non-disclosure agreements, and termination agreements, for processing and storage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Employee Email" field.
4. Select the type of document you are submitting from the "Document Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter a brief description of the document in the "Document" field.
6. Select the date of your signature in the "Signature" field.
7. If applicable, provide additional documents in the "Document 1" and "Document 2" fields.
8. Confirm that you have reviewed and accepted the submitted documents by selecting "Yes" in the "Submit" field.
9. Optionally, enter any review comments in the "Review Comments" field.
10. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (Text, Required)**: Please enter your full employee name.
* **Employee ID (Text, Required)**: Enter your employee ID number for identification purposes.
* **Employee Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Document Type (Select One, Required)**: Select from the options provided:
	+ Contract
	+ Non-Disclosure Agreement
	+ Termination Agreement
* **Document (Text, Required)**: Enter a brief description of the document being submitted.
* **Signature (Date, Required)**: Select the date you sign this form.
* **Document 1 (Text, Optional)**: If applicable, provide additional documents or information related to this submission.
* **Document 2 (Text, Optional)**: If applicable, provide additional documents or information related to this submission.
* **Submit (Select One, Required)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and accepted the submitted documents by selecting "Yes".
* **Review Comments (Text, Optional)**: Enter any comments or feedback regarding this submission.
* **Submitter Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as the submitter of this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check the "Document Type" selection to ensure correct categorization.
* If you have any questions or need guidance, contact your HR representative.
