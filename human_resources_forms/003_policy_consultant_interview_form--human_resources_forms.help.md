# Policy Consultant Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from the policy consultant for an interview with a candidate. Ensure that you have the necessary authority to fill out this form, and provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
- For fields with multiple options, select the one that best fits your information.
- If you don't see an option that fits your situation, you can use the "Other (specify)" field to provide more details.
- Enter dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY for date fields and HH:MM for time fields.
- Use the "Location" field to select the location of the interview.
- Use the "Other (specify)" field for any other location that is not listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Consultant Information** (`policy_consultant_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your role and responsibilities as a policy consultant.
* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the candidate, such as their job title or position.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide the contact details of the policy consultant, including name, phone number, and email address.
* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`, text, optional): Describe the interview details, including date, time, and location.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, select_one, optional): Select Yes or No to indicate whether the policy consultant has the job position.
* **Job Position (Other)** (`job_position_other`, text, optional): If the policy consultant doesn't have the job position, provide more details about their job title.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Select High or Low to indicate the job level of the policy consultant.
* **Job Level (Other)** (`job_level_other`, text, optional): If the policy consultant's job level is not listed, provide more details about their job level.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the policy consultant.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the policy consultant's manager.
* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the policy consultant's manager.
* **Manager's Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the policy consultant's manager.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the interview.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the location of the interview, or use the "Other (specify)" field to provide more details.
* **Location (Other)** (`location_other`, text, optional): If the location of the interview is not listed, provide more details about the location.
* **Interview Duration** (`interview_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the interview.
* **Policy Consultant Name** (`policy_consultant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the policy consultant.
* **Policy Consultant Phone** (`policy_consultant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the policy consultant.
* **Policy Consultant Email** (`policy_consultant_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the policy consultant.
* **Policy Consultant Notes** (`policy_consultant_notes`, note, optional): Provide any notes or comments about the policy consultant.
* **Candidate Comments** (`candidate_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the candidate.
* **Candidate References** (`candidate_references`, text, optional): Provide any references or contacts for the candidate.
* **Policy Consultant Comments** (`policy_consultant_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the policy consultant.
* **Candidate Availability** (`candidate_availability`, date, optional): Enter the availability of the candidate.
* **Policy Consultant Availability** (`policy_consultant_availability`, date, optional): Enter the availability of the policy consultant.
