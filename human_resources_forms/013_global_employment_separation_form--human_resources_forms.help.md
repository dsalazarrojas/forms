# Global Employment Separation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Employment Separation Form is a document used to record an employee's separation from the company. It helps in maintaining an accurate and up-to-date record of the separation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions to fill out the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Provide accurate and detailed information in the text fields.
4.  Choose the correct options for the select fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Offboarding Date**: Enter the date when the employee will be offboarded from the company. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank if the company doesn't require a specific offboarding date.
*   **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager who will be responsible for the separation process. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank or check with your supervisor/HR department to confirm the correct manager.
*   **Reason for Separation**: Provide a clear and concise reason for the employee's separation. This could be due to resignation, retirement, termination, or other valid reasons. If you're unsure, double-check with the employee and your supervisor/HR department.
*   **Last Day of Work**: Enter the date when the employee's last day of work will be. This field is required if the company has a specific last day of work policy.
*   **Reason for Separation Details**: Provide more details about the reason for the employee's separation, if needed. This could include any additional information or context relevant to the separation process.
*   **Separation Status**: Choose the correct status for the employee's separation from the given options. For example, "Active, Inactive", "Terminated", or "Retired".
*   **Last Date of Work Status**: Choose the status of the last date of work from the given options. For example, "Yes", "No", or other options depending on company policies.
*   **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the employee's manager who will be handling the separation process. This field is optional.
*   **Employee ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the employee being separated. This field is optional.
*   **Separation Date**: Enter the date of the employee's separation. This field is not required if the offboarding date is provided.
*   **Manager Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the employee's manager who will be handling the separation process. This field is optional.
*   **Reason for Termination**: Choose the reason for the employee's termination from the given options. For example, "Poor performance", "Redundancy", "Retirement", or other valid options.
*   **Separation Type**: Choose the type of separation from the given options. For example, "Voluntary", "Involuntary", "Retirement", or other valid options.

## Tips
*   Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
*   Ensure you have all necessary permissions and approvals before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field or option, consult with your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
