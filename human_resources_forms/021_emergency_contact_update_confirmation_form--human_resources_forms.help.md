# Emergency Contact Update Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect and update the emergency contact information of an individual. It should be completed by authorized personnel to ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the updated emergency contact information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are accurate.
3. Click the "Submit" button to save the changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relation** (`relation`, text, optional): Enter the relationship of the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Relation Type** (`relation_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of relationship (e.g., mother, father, etc.).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the emergency contact's address.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the emergency contact's address.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter the ZIP/Postal code of the emergency contact's address.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly to avoid errors.
* If a field is left blank, it will be marked as invalid.
* You can only update the fields that have changed, and not fill in all fields if you're unsure.
