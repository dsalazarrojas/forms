# IT Administrator Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Administrator Information Form is a tool used to collect and store information about IT administrators. This form helps to gather essential details about their roles, responsibilities, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Select your department from the available options.
5. Enter your work location in the "Location" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
9. Enter your time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field.
10. Add any relevant notes about yourself in the "IT Administrator Notes" field.
11. Select your job type from the list of options in the "Job Type" field.
12. Select the skills you possess from the list of options in the "Skills" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name, the name given to you at birth.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name, the name of your family or surname.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Describe your role as an IT administrator.

### Department
* **Department** (text, optional): Choose the department where you work.

### Location
* **Location** (text, optional): Enter your work location.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (number, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Email
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your official work email address.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Time of Birth
* **Time of Birth** (time, optional): Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM:SS.

### IT Administrator Notes
* **IT Administrator Notes** (note, optional): Add any relevant notes about yourself or your work.

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (select_one, optional): Choose your role as an IT administrator from the options provided:
	+ IT Manager
	+ Software Engineer
	+ Network Administrator

### Skills
* **Skills** (select_multiple, optional): Select the skills you possess:
	+ Programming
	+ Network Administration
	+ IT Management
