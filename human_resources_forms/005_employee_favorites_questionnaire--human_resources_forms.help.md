The purpose of this form is to gather information about an employee's preferences and interests, such as their favorite food, drink, color, and hobby. This data will be used to build a more personal and engaging work environment for the employee.

This form is designed to be filled out once, so there's no need to complete it multiple times. Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and honestly, as the information collected will be used to tailor company events and activities to your interests.

## Employee Favorites Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your preferences and interests to create a more personal and engaging work environment for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your preferences and interests. If you're unsure about any of the options, just choose the one that best fits your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label>Favorite Food</label>

	+ Apple

	+ Ice cream
	+ Donuts

### <label>Recommend</label>
* **Recommend** (`recommend`, select_one, required: false): Do you think you'd like to recommend us to a friend or family member? Choose from the options below:
	+ True
	+ False

### <label>Favorite Drink</label>

## Tips
* Please answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
