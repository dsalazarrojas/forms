# Employment Income Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to verify employment income for an individual, allowing financial institutions or other businesses to assess creditworthiness or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employee Information** (`employee`): Enter the employee's name.
2. **Employment Date** (`employee_employment_date`): Enter the employee's employment start date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. **Employee Last Name** (`employee_last_name`): Enter the employee's last name.
4. **Employee First Name** (`employee_first_name`): Enter the employee's first name.
5. **Employee Address** (`employee_address`): Enter the employee's address.
6. **Salary Start Date** (`employee_salary_start_date`): Enter the employee's salary start date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
7. **Salary End Date** (`employee_salary_end_date`): Enter the employee's salary end date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
8. **Employer Name** (`employer_name`): Enter the name of the employee's current or previous employer.
9. **Employer Address** (`employer_address`): Enter the employer's address.
10. **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`): Enter the employer's phone number.
11. **Employer Email** (`employer_email`): Enter the employer's email address.
12. **Certification Status** (`certification_issued`): Select one of the following:
	* Certified
	* Not Certified
	* Unknown
13. **Certification Date** (`certification_date`): Enter the date of certification if applicable.
14. **Termination Date** (`termination_date`): Enter the date of termination if applicable.
15. **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`): Enter a brief explanation for termination if applicable.
16. **Certification Issuer** (`certification_issuer`): Enter the name of the certification issuer if applicable.
17. **Verification Note** (`verification_note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the verification process.
18. **Employer Confirmation** (`employer_confirmation`): Select one of the following:
	* True
	* False
19. **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`): Select one of the following:
	* None
	* Other
20. **Verification Status** (`verification_status`): Select one of the following:
	* Verified
	* Unverified
	* Pending
21. **Employee Status** (`employee_status`): Select one of the following:
	* None
	* Other
22. **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`): Enter the employee's date of death if applicable.
23. **Time of Death** (`time_of_death`): Enter the time of death if applicable.
24. **Place of Death** (`place_of_death`): Enter the place of death if applicable.
25. **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`): Enter the employer's signature.
26. **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Enter the employee's signature.
27. **Verification Date** (`verification_date`): Enter the date of verification.
28. **Verification Time** (`verification_time`): Enter the time of verification.
29. **Verification Employee** (`verification_employee`): Enter the name of the person who verified the employment details.
30. **Verification Employee Signature** (`verification_employee_signature`): Enter the signature of the person who verified the employment details.
