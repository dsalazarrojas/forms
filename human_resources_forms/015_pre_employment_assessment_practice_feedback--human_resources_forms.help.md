# Pre Employment Assessment Practice Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with the practice assessment, helping us to improve it for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions that you feel are relevant to your experience.
2. Be as honest and detailed as possible in your answers.
3. You can skip any questions if you don't feel they apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think about the practice assessment?** (Text field, Optional)
    This field is where you can share your thoughts about the practice assessment, including any positive or negative aspects you encountered.
* **How would you rate the practice assessment?** (Select One field, Optional)
    This field offers a rating scale of Good, Fair. Please choose the one that best matches your experience with the practice assessment.
* **What was your overall experience with the practice assessment?** (Text field, Optional)
    Share your overall impressions and thoughts about the practice assessment, including any challenges you faced and how it impacted your experience. 

## Tips
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the first field.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions, and avoid generic responses.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
