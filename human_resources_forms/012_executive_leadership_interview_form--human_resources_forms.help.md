# Executive Leadership Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Leadership Interview Form is a tool used to document and evaluate the performance of executive candidates during an interview. It helps to gather information about the candidate's background, job title, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the candidate's name and job title in the corresponding fields.
2.  Select the interview panel responsible for conducting the interview from the provided options.
3.  Evaluate the candidate's performance using the score field.
4.  Provide any additional comments or recommendations in the designated fields.
5.  Record the date and start/end time of the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate Info** (`candidate_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the candidate's name and any additional information about the candidate.
*   **Interview Panel** (`interview_panel`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the panel responsible for conducting the interview. Options: 
    *   HR Panel
    *   Hiring Manager
    *   Other
*   **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the candidate's name.
*   **Candidate Job Title** (`candidate_job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the candidate's job title.
*   **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, `number`, required: false): Evaluate the candidate's performance using a score.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback or comments about the candidate.
*   **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required: false): Provide any recommendations for the candidate.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations about the candidate.
*   **Date of Interview** (`date_of_interview`, `date`, required: false): Record the date of the interview.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Record the start time of the interview.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Record the end time of the interview.
