# Misconduct Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents of misconduct within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Report Misconduct" field with a clear and concise description of the incident that has occurred.
2. Provide the date of the incident in the "Date of Incident" field. This field should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the time of the incident in the "Time of Incident" field. This field should be in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4. Describe the location of the incident in the "Location of Incident" field.
5. If you were a witness, provide your identity and any relevant information about what you saw in the "Your Identity" field.
6. If you are not a witness, you can leave this field blank.
7. In the "Witness Details" field, provide any additional information you have about the incident.
8. In the "Complaint Details" field, provide a detailed account of the incident, including any supporting evidence or documentation.
9. In the "Resolution Outcome" field, describe the outcome of the incident, if known.
10. In the "Action Taken" field, describe the steps you have taken or plan to take in response to the incident.
11. In the "Action Planned" field, describe any further actions you plan to take.
12. If applicable, provide the date and time for the follow-up in the "Follow Up Date" and "Follow Up Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Misconduct** (`report_misconduct`, text, optional): Describe the incident that has occurred.
* **Date of Incident** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Incident** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Location of Incident** (`location`, text, optional): Describe the location of the incident.
* **Your Identity** (`reporter_identity`, text, optional): If you are a witness, provide your identity.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the incident.
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, optional): Describe the incident in detail, including any supporting evidence or documentation.
* **Resolution Outcome** (`resolution_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the outcome of the incident, if known.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): Describe the steps you have taken or plan to take in response to the incident.
* **Action Planned** (`action_planned`, text, optional): Describe any further actions you plan to take.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): If applicable, provide the date for the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): If applicable, provide the time for the follow-up.
