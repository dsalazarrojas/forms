# emergency_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Absence Form is used by employees to report their unexpected or sudden absence from work due to emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **emergency_absence_form** field with a brief description of the situation.
2. Specify the **reason_for_absence** and explain the reason for your absence.
3. Enter the **start_date** and **end_date** of your absence.
4. If applicable, select the **assigned_to** person who is to be contacted in case of an emergency.
5. Enter the **assigned_to_first_name**, **assigned_to_last_name**, and **assigned_to_email** for the contact person.
6. If the contact person is not a supervisor, enter the **emergency_contact_person**, **emergency_contact_relation**, and **emergency_contact_number** for the contact person.
7. Enter the **supervisor** and their corresponding **supervisor_first_name**, **supervisor_last_name**, and **supervisor_email** and **supervisor_phone** if applicable.
8. Choose the **emergency_contact_method** to be used in case of an emergency (e.g., phone, email, or other).
9. Enter the **emergency_contact_method_other** if other than the standard methods are chosen.
10. Enter the **supervisor_contact_method** and **supervisor_contact_method_other** if applicable.
11. Fill out the **comments** field with any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency_absence_form**: This field is where you enter a brief description of your absence.
* **reason_for_absence**: Provide a clear explanation of the reason for your absence.
* **start_date**: Enter the date when you began your absence.
* **end_date**: Enter the date when you expect to return to work.
* **assigned_to**: This field is where you select the person to contact in case of an emergency. Typically, this should be a supervisor.
* **assigned_to_first_name**, **assigned_to_last_name**, and **assigned_to_email**: Enter the contact person's name and email address.
* **emergency_contact_person**: Enter the person to contact in case of an emergency (if different from the assigned contact person).
* **emergency_contact_relation**: Explain the relation between you and the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_number**: Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Enter the type of relation you have with the emergency contact person.
* **supervisor**: This field is where you select the supervisor to contact in case of an emergency.
* **supervisor_first_name, supervisor_last_name, supervisor_email, and supervisor_phone**: Enter the supervisor's name and contact information.
* **emergency_contact_method**: Choose the preferred contact method to use in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_method_other**: Enter other contact method if not listed in the previous option.
* **supervisor_contact_method**: Choose the contact method for the supervisor.
* **supervisor_contact_method_other**: Enter other contact method for the supervisor.
* **form_comments**: Add any additional comments or information related to your absence.
