# Job Offer And Role Terms Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a job offer, including job title, description, terms, and other relevant details. It is intended to be used by HR professionals, recruiters, or anyone involved in the hiring process to document and track job offers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the job offer details, such as job title, description, and location.
2. Select the job type (full-time, part-time, or intern) and category (permanent, temporary, or contract).
3. Choose the employee type (full-time, part-time, or temporary) and level (high, mid, or low).
4. Select the job status (active, pending, or reopened).
5. Indicate the job level frequency (daily, weekly, or monthly).
6. Optionally, provide additional details about the job, such as job level, employee status, and job category range.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the job offer.

### 2. Job Description
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the job offer.

### 3. Job Terms
* **Job Terms** (`job_terms`, `text`, optional): Enter any special terms or conditions of the job offer.

### 4. Role Responsibilities
* **Role Responsiblities** (`role_responsibilities`, `text`, optional): Describe the responsibilities of the job.

### 5. Role Terms
* **Role Terms** (`role_terms`, `text`, optional): Enter any special terms or conditions related to the role.

### 6. Job Start Date
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the job is expected to start.

### 7. Job End Date
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the job is expected to end.

### 8. Salary
* **Salary** (`salary`, `text`, optional): Enter the salary for the job offer.

### 9. Job Location
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the job is offered.

### 10. Job Status
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the job (active, pending, or reopened).

### 11. Job Type
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of job (full-time, part-time, or intern).

### 12. Job Category
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category of the job (permanent, temporary, or contract).

### 13. Employee Type
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of employee (full-time, part-time, or temporary).

### 14. Job Level
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of the job (high, mid, or low).

### 15. Job Level Description
* **Job Level Description** (`job_level_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the job level.

### 16. Employee Status
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the status of the employee (active, inactive, or retired).

### 17. Job Level Range
* **Job Level Range** (`job_level_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the range of the job level (low, mid, or high).

### 18. Job Type Range
* **Job Type Range** (`job_type_range`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the range of the job type (low, mid, or high).

### 19. Job Category Range
* **Job Category Range** (`job_category_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the range of the job category (low, mid, or high).

### 20. Employee Level Range
* **Employee Level Range** (`employee_level_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the range of the employee level (low, mid, or high).

### 21. Job Status Range
* **Job Status Range** (`job_status_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the range of the job status (active, pending, or reopened).

### 22. Job Level Frequency
* **Job Level Frequency** (`job_level_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency of the job level (daily, weekly, or monthly).

### 23. Job Type Frequency
* **Job Type Frequency** (`job_type_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the frequency of the job type (high, medium, or low).

### 24. Job Location
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the job is offered.
