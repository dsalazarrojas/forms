# employee_motivation_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee motivation training form is designed to provide an opportunity for employees to share their ideas, thoughts, and feedback related to employee motivation and training. This form is meant to be used by employees to express their needs and desires for growth and development within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the field provided.
2. Enter your Email address in the field provided.
3. Enter your Phone number in the field provided.
4. Enter your Manager's Name in the field provided.
5. Enter your Employee ID in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee who is submitting the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the employee's email address. This will be used to communicate with the employee.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the employee's phone number. This will be used to contact the employee if needed.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required: false): Enter the employee's ID number.
