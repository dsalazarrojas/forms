# driving_record_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to an employee's driver's license and employment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Id**, which is your unique identifier within the organization.
2. Enter your **Driver Id**, which is used to track your driving record.
3. Enter your **License Number**, which is the number printed on your driver's license.
4. Enter the **Date Of Issue**, which is the date your driver's license was issued.
5. Enter the **Date Of Birth**, which is your date of birth.
6. Select your **License Class**, which describes the type of license you hold (e.g., Class 1, Class 2, etc.).
7. Choose your **License Status**, which indicates whether your license is active, inactive, or suspended.
8. Enter your **Employer**, which is your current employer's name.
9. Enter your **Address**, which is your physical address.
10. Enter your **Department**, which is the department you work in.
11. Enter your **Manager**, which is your direct supervisor's name.
12. Enter your **Manager Phone**, which is your manager's contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id** (1, required/optional): This is your unique identifier within the organization.
* **Driver Id** (2, required/optional): This is used to track your driving record.
* **License Number** (3, required/optional): This is the number printed on your driver's license.
* **Date Of Issue** (4, required/optional): This is the date your driver's license was issued.
* **Date Of Birth** (5, required/optional): This is your date of birth.
* **License Class** (6, required/optional): This describes the type of license you hold (e.g., Class 1, Class 2, etc.).
* **License Status** (7, required/optional): This indicates whether your license is active, inactive, or suspended.
* **Employer** (8, required/optional): This is your current employer's name.
* **Address** (9, required/optional): This is your physical address.
* **Department** (10, required/optional): This is the department you work in.
* **Manager** (11, required/optional): This is your direct supervisor's name.
* **Manager Phone** (12, required/optional): This is your manager's contact phone number.
