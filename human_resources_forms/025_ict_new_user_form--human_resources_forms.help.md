# ICT New User Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICT New User Form is designed to collect essential information about new team members, including their personal details, job roles, and team affiliations. This form will help us ensure a smooth onboarding process and proper integration into our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as 'required') with accurate and complete information.
3. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you don't have the information or if it's not relevant to your situation.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address. This must be a valid email address that you use to communicate with us.
* **Job Title** (4): Enter a brief description of your job role or position within the company.
* **Team** (5): Please select all the teams you are a part of. You can choose from 'Yes', 'No', or select multiple teams.
* **Job Type** (6): Choose whether you are 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your job type.
* **Job Level** (7): Select whether your job level is 'High', 'Low', or any other option that best describes your role.
* **Reporting Manager** (8): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Manager Name** (9): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (10): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (11): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Job Description** (12): Briefly describe your job role and responsibilities.
* **Skills** (13): Enter any relevant skills you possess.
* **Requirements** (14): List any specific requirements or qualifications needed for your job role.
* **Department** (15): Enter the department you belong to or the department you work with.
* **Reporting Department** (16): Choose whether you are 'Yes' or 'No' to the question about your reporting department.

## Tips
* Please double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to integrating you into our team.
