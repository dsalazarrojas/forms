# Teaching Job Application For JIS - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Teaching Job Application Form is a tool for individuals who are interested in teaching positions within JIS. It collects relevant information from applicants, which will be reviewed to determine their qualifications and suitability for available teaching positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a genuine reason for completing the form.
2. Review this help guide to understand each field and its purpose.
3. Fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information (1)
* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing your personal details such as name, address, and other relevant information.

### Contact Information (2)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact details, including phone number and email address, to ensure we can reach you for further communication.

### Marital Status (3)
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your marital status, which is optional but may be required for some positions or internal purposes.

### Medical Conditions (4)
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any medical conditions or disabilities, please list them here for our consideration.

### Position (5)
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, required: false): Describe the specific teaching position you are interested in or would be a suitable fit for.

### Submit (6)
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to send your application. Make sure to review your entries carefully before submitting.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx. 
- Review your entries before submitting to avoid mistakes.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please ask.
- Thank you for your interest in joining our team!
