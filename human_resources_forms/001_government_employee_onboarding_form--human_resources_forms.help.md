# Government Employee Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from new government employees during the onboarding process. The form helps to gather details about the employee's personal and emergency contacts, as well as their employment history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review each field carefully** to ensure you are providing accurate and complete information.
2. **Complete all required fields**, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the field label.
3. **Enter your information clearly and accurately** in the provided fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a brief description or comment about the new hire.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Child, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required: false): Enter your hire date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
