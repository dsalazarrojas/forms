# Employee Time Off Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for employees to request time off for various reasons such as vacation, sick leave, or family leave.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and employee ID for identification purposes.
2. Select the reason for your time off (e.g., vacation, sick leave, family leave).
3. Enter the start and end dates of your time off period.
4. Enter your manager's name and contact information for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time Off Type**: 
	+ Select the type of time off you are requesting, such as "Vacation", "Sick Leave", or "Family Leave".
* **Reason for Time Off**: 
	+ Enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting time off.
* **Start Date**:
	+ Enter the date when you want to start your time off.
* **End Date**:
	+ Enter the date when you want to end your time off.
* **Employee Name**: 
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID.
* **Employee ID**: 
	+ Enter your unique employee ID for identification purposes.
* **Job Title**: 
	+ Enter your job title as it appears on your company ID.
* **Department**: 
	+ Enter your department as it appears on your company ID.
* **Manager Name**: 
	+ Enter your manager's name as it appears on their company ID.
* **Manager Email**: 
	+ Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: 
	+ Enter your manager's phone number.
