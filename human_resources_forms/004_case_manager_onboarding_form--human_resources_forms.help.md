# Case Manager Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Case Manager Onboarding Form, ensuring you have a comprehensive understanding of each field and what information is required or optional.

## How To Complete This Form
### Before Starting
- Please complete this form with up-to-date and accurate information about yourself.
- This form is designed to be completed by case managers who are new to the role.
- The information collected will help us ensure that you have the necessary training and support to effectively perform your duties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required):
  This field is for your legal name as it appears on your ID. Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID.

* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required):
  Enter your employment start date, which will help us track your onboarding progress and ensure you receive the necessary training and support.

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional):
  If you have an internal identifier, please provide it. This will help us keep your records accurate and up-to-date.

* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`, required):
  Enter the office or region where you will be working. This information will help us ensure that you receive the necessary support and resources for your role.

* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required):
  Please provide the name of your direct manager or supervisor. This will help us communicate with the right person for any questions or concerns you may have.

* **Caseload Limit** (`caseload_limit`, `number`, optional):
  This is the recommended maximum number of active cases you can manage. Please note that this is a guideline rather than a strict limit.

* **Required Training Completed** (`required_training_completed`, `select_multiple`, required):
  Select the trainings you have already completed from the list provided. This will help us ensure that you have the necessary knowledge and skills to perform your duties.

* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional):
  Please provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact. This information will only be used in case of an emergency.

* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional):
  If you have any onboarding concerns or requests, please share them in this field. This will help us provide you with the necessary support and resources.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We are committed to making your onboarding experience smooth and efficient.
