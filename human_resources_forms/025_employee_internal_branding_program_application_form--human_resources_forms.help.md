# Employee Internal Branding Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Internal Branding Program Application Form is used for employees to apply for internal branding initiatives, such as workshops, training sessions, and other activities. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the required fields with the requested information, such as your Employee Name, Email, and Job Title.
2.  Choose your Department and select which Internal Branding Program you wish to apply for.
3.  Provide a brief explanation for why you want to apply for the program and any expected outcomes.
4.  If your application is approved, enter the date and time you would like to be approved.
5.  Enter your Manager's approval status and any comments or notes.
6.  Finally, sign the form by typing your name and entering your Employee ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
*   **Email**: Enter your company email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your application.
*   **Department**: Check the box to indicate whether you are in a department that participates in internal branding initiatives.
*   **Manager**: Enter the name of your direct manager.
*   **Role**: Enter your job title or role within the company.
*   **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
*   **Team**: Enter the name of your team or department.
*   **Location**: Enter your current work location.
*   **Internal Branding Program**: Select the internal branding program you wish to apply for. Choose from the options provided.
*   **Why Apply**: Enter a brief explanation for why you want to participate in the program.
*   **Benefits**: Enter any benefits you hope to gain from participating in the program.
*   **Expected Outcome**: Enter any expected outcomes or results from participating in the program.
*   **Submission Date**: Enter the date you would like to be approved for the program.
*   **Submission Time**: Enter the time you would like to be approved for the program.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments regarding your application.
*   **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your application.
*   **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID number.
*   **Internal Branding Officer**: Enter the name of the internal branding officer who will be reviewing your application.
*   **Manager Approval**: Select whether your manager has approved or denied your application.
*   **Date Approved**: Enter the date your manager approved your application.
*   **Time Approved**: Enter the time your manager approved your application.
*   **Notes Approved**: Enter any additional information or comments from your manager.
*   **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from your manager.
*   **Employee Response**: Enter any response or comment from you regarding your manager's feedback.
*   **Approval Status**: Select the status of your application, whether approved, denied, or pending.
