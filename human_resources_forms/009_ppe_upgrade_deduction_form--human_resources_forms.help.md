# PPE Upgrade Deduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an upgrade or deduction on Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for an employee. It helps track and manage PPE upgrades or deductions for various types of equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and department in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the supervisor's name and department.
3. Select the type of equipment being upgraded or deducted (e.g. Safety Equipment, Tools, Clothing, or Accessories).
4. Check the employee's approval for the upgrade or deduction.
5. Check the supervisor's approval for the upgrade or deduction.
6. Enter the date and time of the upgrade or deduction.
7. Describe the assigned tool and category.
8. Provide a description of the form and the equipment being upgraded or deducted.
9. Enter the assigned tool, user, and assigned to fields.
10. Select the form category, type, and status.
11. Enter the assigned date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of this form instance.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee requesting the upgrade or deduction.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the employee requesting the upgrade or deduction.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor who will review and approve the upgrade or deduction.
* **Supervisor Department** (`supervisor_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the supervisor who will review and approve the upgrade or deduction.
* **Employee Approval** (`employee_approval`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the employee approves or conditionally approves the upgrade or deduction.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the supervisor approves or conditionally approves the upgrade or deduction.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the upgrade or deduction.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the upgrade or deduction.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool being upgraded or deducted.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter the category of the tool being upgraded or deducted.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the tool and upgrade or deduction.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of this form instance.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool being assigned to the employee.
* **User** (`user`, text, optional): Enter the user who will be using the assigned tool.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the person assigned to use the tool.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, text, optional): Enter the person assigning the tool.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, optional): Select the category of this form.
* **Form Type** (`form_type`, text, optional): Select the type of this form.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, text, optional): Select the status of this form.
* **Form Status 2** (`form_status_2`, text, optional): Select the secondary status of this form.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was assigned.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was assigned.

Note: Some fields may be optional, but please ensure you have the necessary approvals and permissions before filling out the form.
