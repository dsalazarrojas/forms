# HR Process Intelligence Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Process Intelligence Pilot Application Form is a tool used to gather information from applicants participating in an HR Process Intelligence Pilot program. The form is designed to assess the applicant's qualifications and determine their fit for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in the Applicant Details section.
2. Provide information about the applicant's background and experience.
3. In the HR Challenges section, describe any challenges the applicant is currently facing in their role.
4. In the Goals section, outline the applicant's professional goals.
5. In the Goals 2 section, provide any additional goals or information that may be relevant to the application.
6. In the Additional Information section, provide any additional context or information about the applicant.
7. In the Email and Phone fields, provide contact information for the applicant.
8. In the Notes section, provide any additional comments or notes about the applicant.
9. Finally, click the Submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Details
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, Required): This field is used to enter the applicant's background and experience.

### HR Challenges
* **HR Challenges** (`hr_challenges`, text, Required): This field is used to describe any challenges the applicant is currently facing in their role.

### Goals
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, Required): This field is used to outline the applicant's professional goals.

### Goals 2
* **Goals 2** (`goals_2`, text, Required): This field is used to provide any additional goals or information that may be relevant to the application.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, Required): This field is used to provide any additional context or information about the applicant.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): This field is used to enter the applicant's email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required): This field is used to enter the applicant's phone number.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, Required): This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes about the applicant.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, Required): This field is used to complete and submit the form.

### Cancel
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, Required): This field is used to cancel the form submission.

Note: The required fields are marked as required. It is recommended that all fields are filled in thoroughly to ensure a complete and accurate application.
