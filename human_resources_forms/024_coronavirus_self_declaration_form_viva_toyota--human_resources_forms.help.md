# Coronavirus Self Declaration Form VIVA TOYOTA - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a confidential and internal document for Viva Toyota employees to declare their health status and report on any symptoms or potential exposure to the coronavirus. It helps the company to track and manage the health and safety of employees during the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and what information is required.
2. Answer all the questions honestly and accurately, using the provided options and text fields as instructed.
3. If you are unsure about any question or section, please ask your HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, employee ID, and any other relevant information.
* **Employee Suppliers and Customers** (`employee_suppliers_and_customers`, `text`, required: false): List the suppliers and customers you have come into contact with recently.
* **Employee Contact Info** (`employee_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information, including phone number, email, and address.
* **Employee Work Status** (`employee_work_status`, `text`, required: false): Report your current work status, including whether you are working from home or in the office.
* **Employee Symptoms** (`employee_symptoms`, `note`, required: false): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Employee Quarantine Status** (`employee_quarantine_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer whether you are currently in quarantine or isolation due to COVID-19 exposure.
* **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of your suppliers.
* **Supplier Suppliers and Customers** (`supplier_suppliers_and_customers`, `text`, required: false): List the suppliers and customers you have come into contact with recently.
* **Supplier Contact Info** (`supplier_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide the contact information of your suppliers.
* **Supplier Contact Method** (`supplier_contact_method`, `text`, required: false): Describe how you communicate with your suppliers.
* **Supplier Status** (`supplier_status`, `text`, required: false): Report your current supplier status, including whether they are working from home or in the office.
* **Customer Contact Info** (`customer_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of your customers.
* **Customer Contact Method** (`customer_contact_method`, `text`, required: false): Describe how you communicate with your customers.
* **Customer Status** (`customer_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer whether your customers are currently active or inactive.
* **Customer Relationship Info** (`customer_relationship_info`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship with your customers.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Employee Age** (`employee_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Employee Work Duration** (`employee_work_duration`, `number`, required: false): Report the duration of your current employment.
* **Employee Symptom Info** (`employee_symptom_info`, `text`, required: false): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Employee Symptom List** (`employee_symptom_list`, `text`, required: false): List any recent symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Employee Quarantine Date** (`employee_quarantine_date`, `date`, required: false): Report the date you are currently in quarantine or isolation due to COVID-19 exposure.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your supplier.
* **Supplier Age** (`supplier_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter the age of your supplier.
* **Supplier Work Duration** (`supplier_work_duration`, `number`, required: false): Report the duration of your supplier's employment.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question or section, please ask your HR representative for guidance.
* This form is confidential and will only be used for employee management and safety purposes.
