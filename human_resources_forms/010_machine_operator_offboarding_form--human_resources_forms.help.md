# machine_operator_offboarding_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The machine operator offboarding form is a tool used by managers to provide necessary information for ending an employee's employment with the company. This form helps to formally document the offboarding process, ensuring a smooth transition for both the departing employee and the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee's Name
2. Enter the Manager's Name
3. Select the Department the employee belongs to
4. Enter the Job Title of the employee
5. Select the Start Date of employment
6. Select the End Date of employment
7. Provide the Reason for Offboarding
8. Add any comments or notes regarding the offboarding process
9. Enter the Employee's Email
10. Enter the Employee's Phone number
11. Enter the Employee's Address
12. Enter any details about equipment or property that needs to be returned or handled

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being offboarded.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager responsible for the offboarding process.
* **Department**: Select the department the employee belongs to (e.g., Machine Operator, Engineering, Operations, or HR).
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee being offboarded.
* **Start Date**: Select the date the employee started working with the company.
* **End Date**: Select the date the employee's employment will officially end.
* **Reason for Offboarding**: Provide a brief explanation for why the employee's employment is being ended.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the offboarding process.
* **Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the employee's address.
* **Equipment**: Enter any details about equipment or property that needs to be returned or handled.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth transition.
* Be professional and respectful when completing this form.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact HR or your supervisor for guidance.
