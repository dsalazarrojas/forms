# Cricket Coach Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from candidates applying for the position of Cricket Coach.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your professional email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Describe your coaching experience in the "Coaching Experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Coaching Experience** (`experience`, text, required): Describe your coaching experience, including any relevant qualifications, training, or achievements.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your answers are accurate and relevant to the position.
* Use a professional email address and phone number.
* If you're unsure about any information, please contact the HR department for clarification.
* Duplicate applications may be automatically filtered out, so ensure your application is unique.
