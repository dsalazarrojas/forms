# Employee Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Health Screening Questionnaire is designed to collect general information about employees, including their medical history, contact information, and emergency contact details. This form is intended to support employees' health and wellbeing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with your name and other relevant details.
2. Provide any medical history or health concerns in the Medical Information section.
3. Enter your contact information in the Contact Information section.
4. If you have any emergency contact information, provide it in the Emergency Information section.
5. Review and submit the form once you've completed all the required sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, Required: False)
This section is for providing your basic details such as your name and any other relevant information that may be necessary for your health screening.

### **Medical Information** (`Medical Information`, `text`, Required: False)
Please provide any medical history or health concerns that you think may be relevant to your health screening. This could include any allergies, medical conditions, or previous surgeries.

### **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, Required: False)
Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email. This will help us stay in touch with you and ensure that we can reach you in case of an emergency.

### **Emergency Information** (`Emergency Information`, `text`, Required: False)
If you have any emergency contact information, such as a friend or family member who can be reached in case of an emergency, please provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Use clear and concise language in the text boxes.
- If you're unsure about any section or field, refer to your organizational policies and procedures.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is complete and accurate.
