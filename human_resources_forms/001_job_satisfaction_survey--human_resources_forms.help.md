# Job Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help management understand the job satisfaction of employees and identify areas for improvement. The form is designed to gather feedback from employees on various aspects of their job, including their overall job satisfaction, manager support, manager communication, department, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your feelings and experiences.
3. Do not leave any questions blank, as this will not provide a clear picture of your job satisfaction.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Feedback (Employee Survey)**: This is a text field where you can provide any feedback on your overall job satisfaction. Please type in a few sentences to give us an understanding of your feelings and experiences.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Please select a number that best matches your job satisfaction. This will help us understand how you feel about your job overall.
* **Manager Support**: Select how strongly you feel about your manager's support. Please choose from the following options: Strongly Support, Somewhat Support, Neutral, Somewhat Neutral, Not Support, Not at all.
* **Manager Communication**: Select how often you feel your manager communicates with you. Please choose from the following options: Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never.
* **Department**: Please select the department you belong to. This will help us understand which department has the most impact on your job satisfaction.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them in here.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Please select a number that best matches your overall job satisfaction. This will help us understand your overall feelings about your job.
* **Job Satisfaction Level**: Please select how high or low you rate your job satisfaction. Please choose from the following options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Contact Number**: This field is optional and can be left blank. If you want to provide any additional contact information, you can type it in here.
* **Manager Rating**: This field is optional and can be left blank. If you want to rate your manager, please provide a number between 1 and 10.
