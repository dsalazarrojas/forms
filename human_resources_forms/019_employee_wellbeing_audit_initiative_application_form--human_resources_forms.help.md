# Employee Wellbeing Audit Initiative Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Employee Wellbeing Audit Initiative Application Form" is designed to support employee well-being initiatives within an organization. This form helps HR representatives collect valuable information and assess employees' mental health, well-being, and work-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including the audit initiative application date, time, location, and any additional comments.
2. Indicate whether you are involved in the wellbeing initiative (Yes/No).
3. Confirm if your manager is involved in the wellbeing initiative (Yes/No).
4. Choose the type of submission (Yes/No).
5. Confirm the submission version.
6. Confirm the submission organization.
7. Confirm the submission confirmation.
