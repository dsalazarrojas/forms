# New Hire Employment Package Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Employment Package Form is used to create a comprehensive package for new hires, including their personal details, job information, and employment contract details. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is captured and that the new hire is properly onboarded.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the employee's start date and job title.
2. Select the job classification (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Temporary) and provide a brief explanation if necessary.
3. Enter the contact person's email and phone number.
4. Choose the job status (Active or Inactive) and provide a job start date expiration if necessary.
5. Select the employment type (e.g. Contract, Full-time, etc.).
6. Provide any additional details for the employment contract, if necessary.
7. Fill in the contact person's other details, if necessary.
8. Provide any additional details for the job classification, if necessary.
9. Enter the job title other details, if necessary.
10. Fill in the contact address, if necessary.
11. Provide any additional details for the job start date, if necessary.
12. Provide any additional details for the job end date, if necessary.
13. Choose the job status expiration, if necessary.
14. Provide any additional details for the employment contract, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Start Date**: Enter the employee's start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the new hire.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the new hire.
* **Job Classification**: Select the job classification for the new hire (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Temporary).
* **Job Classification Explanation**: Provide a brief explanation for the job classification selection.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Job Title Description**: Enter a brief description of the job title.
* **Job Start Date Expiration**: Enter the expiration date for the job start date (if applicable).
* **Job End Date**: Enter the end date for the job (if applicable).
* **Job Status**: Select the job status (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Status Expiration**: Enter the expiration date for the job status (if applicable).
* **Employment Type**: Select the employment type (e.g. Contract, Full-time, etc.).
* **Employment Type Other**: Provide additional details for the employment type.
* **Job Classification Other Details**: Provide any additional details for the job classification.
* **Job Title Other Details**: Provide any additional details for the job title.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the contact address of the contact person.
* **Job Start Date Other Details**: Provide any additional details for the job start date.
* **Job End Date Other Details**: Provide any additional details for the job end date.
* **Contact Person Other Details**: Provide any additional details for the contact person.
* **Job Classification Other Details**: Provide any additional details for the job classification.
* **Employment Contract Details**: Provide any additional details for the employment contract.
* **Employment Contract Details Other**: Provide any additional details for the employment contract.
* **Contact Person Other Details**: Provide any additional details for the contact person.
* **Job Title Other Details**: Provide any additional details for the job title.

# Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
* The form is designed to be completed by the HR team or the hiring manager, but please ensure that all necessary approvals are in place before submitting.
