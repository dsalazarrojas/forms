## staff_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request changes to staff schedules, job titles, or other HR-related matters. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required for each field.
2. Choose the correct options for each field, taking into consideration the specific details of your request.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any delays in processing your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **staff_type** (Employee/Contractor, select_one): Select the type of staff member you are requesting changes to (e.g., Employee or Contractor).
* **job_title** (text): Enter the job title you are requesting changes to.
* **department** (text): Enter the department associated with the job title.
* **shift** (select_multiple, e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend, Holidays): Select the shifts you are requesting changes to.
* **start_date** (date): Enter the start date of the requested schedule change (optional).
* **end_date** (date): Enter the end date of the requested schedule change (optional).
* **requested_by** (text): Enter your name, as the person requesting the change.
* **manager_note** (text): Enter any additional information or comments for the manager's review (optional).
* **contact_number** (text): Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication (optional).
* **email** (email): Enter your email address for any follow-up communication (optional).
* **department_contact** (text): Enter the contact person's name for the department (optional).
* **department_manager** (text): Enter the name of the department manager (optional).
* **hr_contact** (text): Enter the name of the HR contact person (optional).
* **approved_by** (HR/Manager, select_one): Select who approved the request (e.g., HR or Manager).
* **denied_reason** (text): Enter the reason for denial of the request (optional).
* **approved_date** (date): Enter the date of approval (optional).
* **denied_date** (date): Enter the date of denial (optional).
* **submitted_by** (text): Enter your name, as the person submitting the request.
* **submitted_date** (date): Enter the date you submitted the request.
* **updated_by** (text): Enter the name of the person who last updated the request.
* **updated_date** (date): Enter the date the request was last updated.
* **approved_status** (Approved/Denied/Pending, select_multiple): Select the status of the request (e.g., Approved, Denied, or Pending).
