# Law Firm Associate Paternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a paternity leave of absence for an associate due to the birth or adoption of a child. It is used to notify the associate's manager and HR department of the associate's intention to take a leave of absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the associate's name in the "Associate Name" field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the leave in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
3. Enter the reason for the leave in the "Reason For Leave" field.
4. Enter the expected return date in the "Expected Return Date" field.
5. The manager must confirm their approval of the leave by selecting "Approved" in the "Manager Approval" field.
6. The manager must also sign the form in the "Manager Signature" field.
7. The associate must sign the form in the "Employee Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Associate Name** (Text, Required): The name of the associate requesting the leave.
- **Start Date** (Date, Required): The date the leave is expected to begin.
- **End Date** (Date, Required): The date the leave is expected to end.
- **Reason For Leave** (Text, Required): The reason the associate is requesting the leave (e.g. birth of child, adoption of child).
- **Expected Return Date** (Date, Required): The date the associate is expected to return to work.
- **Manager Name** (Text, Required): The name of the manager approving or denying the leave.
- **Manager Email** (Email, Required): The email address of the manager approving or denying the leave.
- **Manager Phone** (Text, Required): The phone number of the manager approving or denying the leave.
- **Manager Department** (Text, Required): The department of the manager approving or denying the leave.
- **Employee Signature** (Note, Required): The associate's signature confirming they have completed the form.
- **Manager Signature** (Note, Required): The manager's signature confirming they have approved or denied the leave.
- **Manager Approval** (Select One, Required): The manager's approval or denial of the leave (e.g. Approved, Denied, Pending).
- **Company Manager Signature** (Note, Required): The signature of the company manager confirming they have approved or denied the leave.
- **Company Manager Approval** (Select One, Required): The company manager's approval or denial of the leave (e.g. Approved, Denied, Pending).

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Ensure the manager's approval is obtained before leaving.
- The form should not be used for other types of leaves.
