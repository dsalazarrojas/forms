# employee_travel_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for employee travel, providing a structured way to gather necessary information and approvals before the trip takes place.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields with the required information.
2. Enter the travel dates in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Provide a brief description of the travel purpose.
4. Enter the destination of the trip.
5. Enter the duration of the trip in days.
6. Enter the employee's name.
7. Select the department for the employee.
8. Choose the supervisor and manager approval options.
9. Enter any additional travel reason (optional).
10. Add any comments or notes about the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required: false): Enter the travel dates in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the purpose or reason for the trip.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required: false): Enter the location(s) visited during the trip.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the trip in days.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Select the department for the employee.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether to Approve or Denied the travel request.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether to Approve or Deny the travel request.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, required: false): Enter any additional reason for the travel.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the trip.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the necessary approvals from both the supervisor and manager before submitting the form.
- Double-check that you have entered all required fields accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant department or supervisor for clarification.
