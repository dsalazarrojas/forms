# Curriculum Developer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for new curriculum developers to provide essential information about themselves and their onboarding expectations. It will help the institution's team understand your background, qualifications, and what you need to get started with your new role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Details section with your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. In the Onboarding Expectations section, write down your expectations and requirements for the onboarding process.
3. Choose your Education Level and Education Field from the provided options.
4. Enter the name and location of your previous education institution.
5. Date of completion for your previous education.
6. Enter any additional details in the Onboarding Data Fields, if applicable.
7. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Details** (`User Details`, text, required): Please provide your name, email address, and any other contact information you'd like to share with the institution.
### **Onboarding Expectations** (`Onboarding Expectations`, text, optional): Write down your expectations and requirements for the onboarding process.
### **Education Level** (`Education Level`, select_multiple, required): Choose your level of education completed (Option 3, Option 4, etc.).
### **Education Field** (`Education Field`, select_multiple, optional): Select the field of education you completed (Option 5, Option 6, etc.).
### **Education Institution** (`Education Institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of your previous education institution.
### **Education Date** (`Education Date`, date, required): Enter the date of completion for your previous education.
### **Education Location** (`Education Location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your previous education institution.
### **Onboarding Data Field 1** (`Onboarding Data Field 1`, text, optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your onboarding.
### **Onboarding Data Field 2** (`Onboarding Data Field 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your onboarding.
### **Onboarding Data Field 3** (`Onboarding Data Field 3`, text, optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your onboarding.

## Tips
