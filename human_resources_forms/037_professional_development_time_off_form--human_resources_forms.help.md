# professional_development_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist in the processing of professional development time off requests. It helps in capturing essential details from the employee, ensuring that all necessary information is provided for smooth review and approval procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Request" field with a brief description of your professional development time off request.
2. Provide the name of the training course you would like to attend or participate in.
3. In the "Review and Approval" field, enter any additional comments or details that may be relevant to your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Request** (`User Request`, text, required/optional: false): Enter a brief description of your professional development time off request. This field is optional, but providing a description will help in understanding your needs.
* **Training Course** (`Training Course`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the training course you would like to attend or participate in.
* **Review and Approval** (`Review and Approval`, text, required/optional: false): Enter any additional comments or details that may be relevant to your request.
