# entry-level-accountant-interview-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about entry-level accountants, including job title, contact information, availability, work schedule, salary range, experience, skills, education, references, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Job Title field with the candidate's current or desired job title.
2. Enter the candidate's contact information, including their email address.
3. Select the candidate's availability (morning, afternoon, or evening).
4. Choose the candidate's work schedule (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
5. Enter the candidate's salary range.
6. Select the candidate's experience level (0-2 years, 2-5 years, or 5-10 years).
7. Choose the candidate's skills (Microsoft Office, Google Suite, QuickBooks).
8. Select the candidate's level of education (High School, Bachelor's, Master's).
9. Indicate whether the candidate has available references.
10. Enter any additional comments or information in the Resume, License, Notes, and Other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Title**: Enter the candidate's current or desired job title.
- **Contact Info**: Enter the candidate's email address.
- **Phone**: Enter the candidate's phone number.
- **Availability**: Select the candidate's availability (morning, afternoon, or evening).
- **Availability 1**: Select a specific time for the candidate's first availability slot (e.g., 9:00 AM).
- **Availability 2**: Select a specific time for the candidate's second availability slot (e.g., 3:00 PM).
- **Work Schedule**: Choose the days the candidate is available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
- **Salary Range**: Enter the candidate's expected or current salary range.
- **Experience**: Select the candidate's experience level (0-2 years, 2-5 years, or 5-10 years).
- **Skills**: Choose the candidate's relevant skills (Microsoft Office, Google Suite, QuickBooks).
- **Education**: Select the candidate's level of education (High School, Bachelor's, Master's).
- **References**: Indicate whether the candidate has available references.
- **Resume**: Enter any additional comments or information about the candidate's resume.
- **License**: Enter any additional comments or information about the candidate's license.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the candidate.
- **Other**: Enter any additional comments or information about the candidate.
- **Other Info 1**, **Other Info 2**, **Other Info 3**, **Other Info 4**, **Other Info 5**, **Other Info 6**, **Other Info 7**: These fields are for additional information about the candidate, if necessary.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have the candidate's permission to access and share their personal data.
- This form is not a replacement for a thorough interview process, but rather a supplementary tool.
- Enter as much or as little information as you need for each field, depending on the candidate's responses and your assessment needs.
