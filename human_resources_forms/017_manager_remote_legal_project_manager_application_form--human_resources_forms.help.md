# Manager Remote Legal Project Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help the selection committee quickly and accurately gather the necessary information for the project management position. This form ensures that we get a comprehensive view of your skills, experience and availability, making the selection process easier and faster.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the instructions below to fill out this form accurately and efficiently:

1. Fill out each field with the requested information. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the selection committee for help.
2. Double-check that you've completed all required fields before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you've attached your resume and cover letter as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Project Manager Application Form**
*   This is the title of the form and not a field to fill out.
*   Please ignore this field.

### 2. **Applicant Name**
*   This field is for your name as an applicant.
*   Please fill out your full name as you'd like to be addressed.

### 3. **Email**
*   This field is for your email address.
*   Please enter your professional email address that the selection committee can use to contact you.

### 4. **Phone Number**
*   This field is for your phone number.
*   Please enter your work or personal phone number that the selection committee can use to contact you.

### 5. **Resume Upload**
*   This field is for uploading your resume.
*   Please attach your resume in a PDF or Word document format.
*   Your resume should include your work experience, skills, and qualifications.

### 6. **Cover Letter Upload**
*   This field is for uploading your cover letter.
*   Please attach your cover letter in a PDF or Word document format.
*   Your cover letter should explain why you're a good fit for the project management position.

### 7. **Project Description**
*   This field is for describing the project you're applying for.
*   Please provide a brief description of the project and your role in it.

### 8. **Work Experience**
*   This field is for describing your work experience.
*   Please provide a brief description of your relevant work experience.

### 9. **Skills**
*   This field is for listing your skills.
*   Please list your relevant skills that align with the project requirements.

### 10. **Availability**
*   This field is for selecting your availability.
*   Please select your dates of availability for the project.

### 11. **Notice Period**
*   This field is for selecting your notice period.
*   Please select your notice period if you're currently employed.

### 12. **Employment Agreement**
*   This field is for accepting or declining the employment agreement.
*   Please read the agreement carefully before accepting or declining.

### 13. **Salary Requirements**
*   This field is for entering your salary requirements.
*   Please enter a number representing your salary requirements.
