# Employee Work Life Balance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate an employee's work-life balance. It is used to assess their overall balance between work and personal life, including communication, support, and engagement. This form helps management and HR teams to understand each employee's current state and make informed decisions to support their well-being and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the questions asked.
2. Answer each question honestly, taking your time to think about your current work-life balance.
3. For select questions (Work Life Balance, Communication, Support, and Engagement), choose the answer that best reflects your current situation.
4. For numerical score questions (Work Life Balance Score, Communication Score, Support Score, and Engagement Score), enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest score.
5. In the Work Life Balance Comments section, add any additional comments or feedback about your current work-life balance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ This is an introduction page to the form. Please read it carefully and proceed to the next page.
* **Work Life Balance**:
	+ This question asks about your current work-life balance. Choose "Yes" if you feel you have a good balance between work and personal life, and "No" if you feel you don't.
* **Communication**:
	+ This question asks about your experience of communication with your team and management. Choose all that apply:
		- Yes - You feel well-informed and supported by your team and management.
		- No - You don't feel well-informed and supported by your team and management.
		- Other (please specify) - If you have any other comments or feelings about your communication experience.
* **Support**:
	+ This question asks about your experience of support from your team and management. Choose all that apply:
		- Yes - You feel well-supported by your team and management.
		- No - You don't feel well-supported by your team and management.
		- Other (please specify) - If you have any other comments or feelings about your support experience.
* **Engagement**:
	+ This question asks about your level of engagement in your work. Choose all that apply:
		- Yes - You feel highly engaged and motivated in your work.
		- No - You don't feel highly engaged and motivated in your work.
		- Other (please specify) - If you have any other comments or feelings about your engagement experience.
* **Work Life Balance Score**, **Communication Score**, **Support Score**, and **Engagement Score**:
	+ These questions ask for a numerical score between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest score, to evaluate your current work-life balance.
* **Work Life Balance Comments**:
	+ This is an optional text field for you to add any additional comments or feedback about your current work-life balance.
* **Overall Score**:
	+ This is the final score that represents your overall work-life balance based on your answers to the previous questions.
* **Overall Score** is a calculated score that represents your overall work-life balance.

## Tips
- Take your time to answer each question honestly.
- If you're unsure about any question, please ask your HR representative for guidance.
- Remember that this form is a confidential assessment, and your feedback is valuable to us.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by employees, and its purpose is to support employee well-being and productivity.
