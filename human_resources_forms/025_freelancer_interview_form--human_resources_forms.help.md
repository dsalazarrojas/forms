# Freelancer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freelancer Interview Form is designed to collect essential information from freelancers to help us assess their qualifications and suitability for potential collaborations or projects. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the General Info section with your basic details.
2. Provide your work experience in the Work Experience field.
3. List your educational background in the Education field.
4. Answer the questions in the Availability field.
5. Complete the Contact Info section with your contact information.
6. Select your skills in the Skills field.
7. Indicate your proficiency in languages in the Languages field.
8. List any references that can vouch for your work in the References field.
9. Share your portfolio links or samples in the Portfolio field.
10. If you have any work samples, please upload them in the Work Sample field.
11. If you have other skills that you'd like to highlight, list them in the Other Skills field.
12. If you have other language proficiency, list them in the Other Languages field.
13. If you have other references that can vouch for your work, list them in the Other References field.
14. If you have additional work experience, list it in the Work Experience 2 field.
15. If you have more portfolios, list them in the Portfolio 2 field.
16. If you have another education background, list it in the Education 2 field.
17. If you have another availability, list it in the Availability 2 field.
18. If you have another contact information, list it in the Contact Info 2 field.
19. Select your language proficiency for any other languages you speak in the Languages 3 field.
20. Select your skills in the Skills 3 field.
21. List your work experience in the Work Experience 3 field.
22. List your portfolios in the Portfolio 3 field.
23. List your education in the Education 3 field.
24. List your availability in the Availability 3 field.
25. List your contact information in the Contact Info 2 field.
