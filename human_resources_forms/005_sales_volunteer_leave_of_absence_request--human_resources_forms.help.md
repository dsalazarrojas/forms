# Sales Volunteer Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence as a sales volunteer. It helps track and manage sales team members' temporary non-paid leave requests to ensure smooth operations and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email in the "email" field.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields respectively.
4. Provide a reason for your leave in the "reason" field.
5. Select the approval status from your manager in the "manager_approval" field.
6. Select the approval status from the sales manager in the "sales_manager_approval" field.
7. If necessary, enter comments from your manager in the "manager_comments" field.
8. If necessary, enter comments from the sales manager in the "sales_manager_comments" field.
9. Enter your sales manager's name in the "sales_manager" field.
10. Enter your manager's name in the "manager" field.
11. Enter your team in the "team" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your full name.
* **email**: Enter your work email address.
* **start_date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **end_date**: Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **reason**: Enter a brief reason for your leave.
* **manager_approval**: Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved your leave, 'No' if they have rejected it, or 'Pending' if they have not approved or rejected it yet.
* **sales_manager_approval**: Select 'Yes' if the sales manager has approved your leave, 'No' if they have rejected it, or 'Pending' if they have not approved or rejected it yet.
* **comments**: If your manager has any comments, enter them here.
* **sales_manager_comments**: If the sales manager has any comments, enter them here.
* **sales_manager**: Enter the name of your sales manager.
* **manager**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **team**: Enter the name of your team.
* **sales_volunteer_leave_of_absence_request_status**: Select the current status of your leave request, which can be 'Pending', 'Approved', 'Rejected', or 'Cancelled'.
