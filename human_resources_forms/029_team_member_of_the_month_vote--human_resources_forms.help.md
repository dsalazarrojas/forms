# Team Member Of The Month Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect votes for a team member of the month. This form allows team members to submit their vote for a team member who has made significant contributions to the team. The selected team member will be recognized and appreciated for their efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the vote, if desired.
2. Select a category for the vote (e.g., "Team Member of the Month").
3. Provide a brief description of why the team member should be selected.
4. Choose the team member you'd like to vote for from the list of options.
5. Select the type of vote you'd like to cast (e.g., "Vote" or "Nomination").
6. Enter the date you'd like to cast your vote on.
7. Provide a reason for your vote, if desired.
8. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
9. Enter your phone number, if desired.
10. Add any comments or suggestions you'd like to make.
11. Click "Submit" to submit your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (form_title, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the vote, if desired.
* **Category** (category, text, optional): Select a category for the vote (e.g., "Team Member of the Month").
* **Description** (description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of why the team member should be selected.
* **Candidate** (candidate, select_one, optional): Choose the team member you'd like to vote for from the list of options.
* **Vote Type** (vote_type, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of vote you'd like to cast (e.g., "Vote" or "Nomination").
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to cast your vote on.
* **Reason** (reason, text, optional): Provide a reason for your vote, if desired.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if desired.
* **Comments** (comments, note, optional): Add any comments or suggestions you'd like to make.
* **Submit** (submit, text, optional): Click "Submit" to submit your vote.

## Tips
* Please be fair and transparent in your vote.
* Make sure to provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
* Keep your comments or suggestions brief and clear.
* Submit only one vote per user.
