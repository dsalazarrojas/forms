# Background Verification Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to verify information provided by applicants, such as employment history, education, and personal details, to ensure accuracy and consistency across all applicants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full legal name (page 1).
2. Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (page 2).
3. Provide your Social Security Number in the format XXX-XX-XXXX (page 3).
4. Enter your current address, including street and apartment number (page 4).
5. Select your city of residence (page 5).
6. Choose the state or province you reside in (page 6).
7. Enter your ZIP code (page 7).
8. Provide your contact phone number (page 8).
9. Enter your primary email address (page 9).
10. Select your current position or role (page 10).
11. Enter the name of your current or previous organization (page 11).
12. Select the purpose of this declaration (page 12).
13. Declare that all information provided is accurate (page 13).
14. Answer whether you have any criminal convictions (page 14).
15. If you have a criminal conviction, provide details (page 15). Note: This field is not required if you answered "no" to the previous question.
16. Select whether there are gaps in your employment history (page 16).
17. If you have employment gaps, explain them (page 17). Note: This field is not required if you answered "no" to the previous question.
18. Consent to education verification (page 18).
19. Consent to employment verification (page 19).
20. Consent to contact with references (page 20).
21. Answer whether you are involved in any pending legal matters (page 21).
22. If you are involved in a legal matter, provide details (page 22). Note: This field is not required if you answered "no" to the previous question.
23. Declare that you understand the consequences of providing false information (page 23).
24. Sign and date your declaration (page 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (page 1): Enter your first, middle, and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (page 2): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (page 3): Enter your Social Security Number in the format XXX-XX-XXXX.
* **Current Address** (page 4): Enter your full street address, including apartment number.
* **City** (page 5): Select your city of residence.
* **State** (page 6): Select the state or province you reside in.
* **ZIP Code** (page 7): Enter your postal code.
* **Phone Number** (page 8): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (page 9): Enter your primary email address.
* **Position or Role** (page 10): Select your current position or role.
* **Organization Name** (page 11): Enter the name of your current or previous organization.
* **Purpose of Declaration** (page 12): Select the reason for this declaration. 
* **Information Accuracy Declaration** (page 13): Declare that all information provided is accurate.
* **Criminal History Declaration** (page 14): Answer whether you have any criminal convictions.
* **Criminal History Details** (page 15): If you answered "yes" to the previous question, provide details of your criminal conviction.
* **Employment Gaps Declaration** (page 16): Answer whether there are gaps in your employment history.
* **Employment Gap Explanation** (page 17): If you answered "yes" to the previous question, explain the gaps in your employment history.
* **Education Verification Consent** (page 18): Consent to verification of your education.
* **Employment Verification Consent** (page 19): Consent to verification of your employment.
* **Reference Verification Consent** (page 20): Consent to contact with your references.
* **Pending Legal Proceedings** (page 21): Answer whether you are involved in any pending legal matters.
* **Legal Proceedings Details** (page 22): If you are involved in a legal matter, provide details.
* **False Information Consequences** (page 23): Declare that you understand the consequences of providing false information.
* **Declarant Signature** (page 24): Sign your name digitally or type it in the required format.
* **Date Signed** (page 25): Date of declaration.

## Tips

* Please take your time to carefully review each field to ensure accurate completion.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or contact person.
