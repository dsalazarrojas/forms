# Staff Certification Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about staff members' certifications, education, and experience. Please fill out the form with your current status to help with evaluation and assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all relevant sections.
2. Enter your correct name and title for accurate identification.
3. Provide your employee ID for reference purposes.
4. Select your highest level of education that you have achieved.
5. Enter the field of study that you specialized in.
6. Indicate if you have any certifications.
7. Enter the expiration date of any certification you have.
8. Enter your years of work experience.
9. Describe your relevant field of experience.
10. Indicate if you have attended any training sessions.
11. Select if you have relevant certifications.
12. Select the method of evaluation to be used for your assessment.
13. Enter the frequency of evaluation, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Title**: Enter your current job title for reference purposes.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID for accurate reference purposes.
* **Highest Level of Education**: Select your highest level of education achieved (High School, Some College, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral).
* **Field of Study**: Enter the field of study you specialized in.
* **Has Certification**: Indicate if you have any certifications.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the date of expiration of any certification you have.
* **Years of Work Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your field.
* **Field of Experience**: Describe your relevant field of experience.
* **Attended Training**: Indicate if you have attended any training sessions.
* **Relevant Certifications**: Select if you have relevant certifications.
* **Evaluation Method**: Select the evaluation method to be used for your assessment (Peer, Managerial, Self-Evaluation).
* **Evaluation Frequency**: Enter the frequency of evaluation (if applicable).

Please note that all fields are required unless specified as optional. Please fill out the form completely and accurately for evaluation purposes.
