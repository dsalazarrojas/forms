# Employee Transfer And Pay Rate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Transfer And Pay Rate Survey" form is used to collect information about employees' job titles, pay frequencies, transfer dates, and reasons for transfer. This form helps managers and HR teams to update employee records and ensure accurate tracking of employee data.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee for whom you are updating information using the dropdown menu under "Employee Details".
2. Enter or select the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Choose the employee's pay frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) using the "Pay Frequency" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the employee's transfer date in the "Transfer Date" field.
5. Enter the employee's manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
6. Select the employee's department from the available options.
7. Enter a brief description of the employee's job responsibilities in the "Job Description" field.
8. Enter a list of the employee's job responsibilities in the "Job Responsibilities" field.
9. Enter the reason for the employee's transfer in the "Reason for Transfer" field.
10. Select the transfer type (internal or external) using the "Transfer Type" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details**: Select the employee you are updating information for from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Title**: Enter or select the employee's current job title.
* **Job Category**: This field is currently not being used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Job Location**: This field is currently not being used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Pay Frequency**: Choose how often the employee is paid (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly).
* **Pay Rate**: Enter the employee's current pay rate.
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date of the employee's transfer.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the manager's name who will be overseeing the employee.
* **Department**: Select the employee's department.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the employee's job.
* **Job Responsibilities**: Enter a list of the employee's job responsibilities.
* **Reason for Transfer**: Enter a reason for the employee's transfer.
* **Transfer Type**: Select whether the transfer is internal or external.

# Tips
* Please ensure that you are using the correct employee's information and that the data is accurate.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the employee or relevant department manager.
