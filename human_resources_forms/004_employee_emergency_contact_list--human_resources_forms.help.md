# Employee Emergency Contact List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about emergency contacts for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information, including name, relationship, address, phone numbers, and email addresses, for the emergency contact.
2. Make sure to enter all required information for each contact.
3. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (1)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name (2)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship (3)**: Select the relationship between the employee and the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, child).
* **Address (4)**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Email (6)**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Phone 2 (7)**: Enter an additional phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship 2 (8)**: Select the relationship between the employee and the second emergency contact.
* **Address 2 (9)**: Enter the address of the second emergency contact.
* **Phone 3 (10)**: Enter another phone number of the second emergency contact.
* **Email 2 (11)**: Enter the email address of the second emergency contact.
* **Phone 4 (12)**: Enter an additional phone number of the second emergency contact.
* **Relationship 3 (13)**: Select the relationship between the employee and the third emergency contact.
* **Address 3 (14)**: Enter the address of the third emergency contact.
* **Phone 5 (15)**: Enter a phone number of the third emergency contact.
* **Relationship 4 (16)**: Select the relationship between the employee and the fourth emergency contact.
* **Address 4 (17)**: Enter the address of the fourth emergency contact.
* **Phone 6 (18)**: Enter another phone number of the fourth emergency contact.
* **Address 5 (19)**: Enter the address of the fifth emergency contact.
* **Contact Phone 1 (20)**: Enter the phone number of the first additional contact.
* **Contact Phone 2 (21)**: Enter the phone number of the second additional contact.
* **Contact Phone 3 (22)**: Enter the phone number of the third additional contact.
* **Contact Phone 4 (23)**: Enter the phone number of the fourth additional contact.
* **Contact Phone 5 (24)**: Enter the phone number of the fifth additional contact.
* **Contact Information 2 (25)**: Enter any additional contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
