# Software Conference Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to submit a leave of absence request for attending a software conference. It captures necessary details to process the request, including conference information, employee details, dates of absence, reason for leave, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Conference Details** section with the name and any other relevant details of the conference you wish to attend.
2. Provide your **Employee Details** in the designated fields.
3. Specify the **Request Start Date** and **Request End Date** when you plan to be away from work.
4. Select a valid reason for your **Reason For Leave** from the provided options.
5. Optionally, provide any additional **Organizer Details** if necessary.
6. The **Approval Status** field will be automatically populated based on the review process.
7. Finally, add any additional **Notes** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Conference Details** (Conference Name, text, required): Please enter the name of the conference you wish to attend.
- **Employee Details** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your employee name and any other relevant details.
- **Request Start Date** (Date, required): Choose the date you plan to start your leave from work.
- **Request End Date** (Date, required): Choose the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
- **Reason For Leave** (Text, required): Select a valid reason for your leave from the provided options.
- **Organizer Details** (Text, optional): If necessary, provide additional details about the conference organizer.
- **Approval Status** (Select One, required): The status will be automatically populated based on the review process.
- **Notes** (Note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your leave request.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly to facilitate smooth processing.
- If you have already submitted a request for the same conference, please do not resubmit.
- Please follow company policies and procedures for requesting leave of absence.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
