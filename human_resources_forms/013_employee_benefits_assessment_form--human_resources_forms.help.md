# Employee Benefits Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Benefits Assessment Form is designed to gather employee feedback on various aspects of their job and work environment. This form aims to help the organization identify areas of improvement and drive positive changes in the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly based on your current work experience.
2. Select your preferences from the options for each field.
3. Provide a numerical rating for job satisfaction (2).
4. Complete all sections and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with your job. Choose from: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Benefits Packages**: Select the benefits packages that you are using. Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Commute Options**: Select the commute options that are available to you. Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Work Environment**: Select the characteristics of your work environment that you find favorable. Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Team Dynamics**: Select the team dynamics that you experience. Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Company Culture**: Select the aspects of the company culture that you find positive. Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Management**: Select the management style that you prefer. Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Communication**: Select the level of communication that you experience. Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Job Satisfaction (2)**: Rate your job satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **Benefits Packages Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the benefits packages. Choose from: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Commute Improvement**: Select if commute options are being improved. Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Job Value Alignment**: Select how well your job aligns with your personal values. Choose from: Yes, No.

## Tips
* Be honest in your feedback to help us improve.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, choose the one that best fits your current experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the text box (not provided in this form).
