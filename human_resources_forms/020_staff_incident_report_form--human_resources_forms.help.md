# Staff Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for staff members to report incidents that have occurred in the workplace. It is used to document and track incidents in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field, but do not worry about formatting or grammar.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
4. Save your progress as you go, and don't worry if you need to make changes later.
5. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Report** (`staff_incident_report`): Enter a brief description of the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the floor/building/unit, etc.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Enter a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details such as circumstances, participants, and consequences.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`): Enter the cause or reason for the incident, if known.
* **Witness List** (`witness_list`): Select any witnesses who may have seen the incident, and provide their names and contact information.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`): Select the outcome of the incident, from the options provided.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`): Enter your name, as the staff member reporting the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`): Select the status of the incident, from the options provided.
* **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`): Enter a detailed description of how the incident was or will be resolved.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about the incident, if needed.
* **Incident Reporter Email** (`incident_reporter_email`): Enter your email address, as the staff member reporting the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`): Enter your phone number, as the staff member reporting the incident.
* **Escalation Date** (`incident_escalation_date`): Enter the date the incident was escalated, if applicable.
* **Escalation Location** (`incident_escalation_location`): Enter the location where the incident was escalated, if applicable.
* **Escalation Description** (`incident_escalation_description`): Enter a detailed description of what happened during the escalation, if applicable.
* **Escalation Cause** (`incident_escalation_cause`): Enter the cause or reason for the escalation, if known.
* **Witness List (Escalation)** (`witness_escalation_list`): Select any witnesses who may have seen the escalation, and provide their names and contact information.
* **Escalation Outcome** (`escalation_outcome`): Select the outcome of the escalation, from the options provided.
* **Escalation Reporter** (`escalation_reporter`): Enter the name of the staff member who reported the escalation.
* **Escalation Status** (`escalation_status`): Select the status of the escalation, from the options provided.
* **Escalation Resolution** (`escalation_resolution`): Enter a detailed description of how the escalation was or will be resolved.
* **Comments (Escalation)** (`comments_escalation`): Enter any additional comments or information about the escalation, if needed.
* **Incident Reporter Manager Name** (`incident_reporter_manager_name`): Enter the name of your manager, as the staff member reporting the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Manager Email** (`incident_reporter_manager_email`): Enter your manager's email address, as the staff member reporting the incident.

## Tips
- Make sure to save your progress as you go.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- Remember that this form is for reporting incidents, not complaints or grievances.
- Be as detailed and specific as possible when providing information.
- Do not worry if you need to make changes or corrections later.
