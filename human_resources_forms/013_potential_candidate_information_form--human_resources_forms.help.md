# Potential Candidate Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential candidates applying for a job or position within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Carefully read each question and provide accurate information about your qualifications and experience.
*   Enter your details in the required fields. Note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
*   Ensure that all the information provided is true and accurate.
*   Review your form before submitting it to ensure that you have completed it correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Job Application**: Please enter a brief description of the job or position you are applying for.
*   **Candidate Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience and how it relates to the job or position you are applying for.
*   **Education Background**: Please provide information about your educational background, including any relevant courses or training.
*   **Job History**: Provide a detailed account of your previous work experience, including job titles, employers, and dates of employment.
*   **Job References**: Please provide the names and contact information of professional references.
*   **Salary Range**: If applicable, please specify the salary range you are expecting or willing to accept.
*   **Available Start Date**: If applicable, please enter the date you are available to start work.
*   **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.

## Tips

*   If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please do not hesitate to contact HR personnel for clarification.
*   Ensure that you have attached any required documentation or supporting materials as per the job posting.
*   Please proofread your form before submitting it to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
