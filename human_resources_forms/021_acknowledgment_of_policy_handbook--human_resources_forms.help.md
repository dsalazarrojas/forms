# Acknowledgment Of Policy Handbook - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain acknowledgment from employees on company policies outlined in the policy handbook. It's a critical process to maintain compliance and reduce the risk of policy violations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, employee ID, department, and job title.
2. Provide the version of the policy handbook you are acknowledging.
3. Specify the date you received the policy handbook.
4. Choose whether you have read and understand the policies outlined in the handbook.
5. Declare your commitment to comply with all policies in the handbook.
6. Indicate if you have questions about any policies.
7. Confirm that you understand the consequences of policy violations.
8. Sign and date the acknowledgment.
9. If necessary, provide the name of an HR representative who witnessed your acknowledgment.
10. Add any additional comments about the policy handbook.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as an employee acknowledging the policy handbook.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department where you work.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current position title.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you were hired.
* **Policy Handbook Version** (`handbook_version`, text, required): Enter the version of the policy handbook you are acknowledging.
* **Date Received Handbook** (`handbook_receipt_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you received the policy handbook.
* **Acknowledgment Statement** (`acknowledgment_statement`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose "Yes, I acknowledge and understand" if you have read and understand the policies.
	+ Choose "No, I have not read the handbook" if you have not read the policies.
* **Commitment to Compliance** (`compliance_commitment`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose "Yes, I commit to compliance" if you promise to follow all policies.
	+ Choose "No, I cannot commit to compliance" if you cannot follow all policies.
* **Do You Have Questions About Any Policies** (`questions_about_policies`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose "Yes, I have questions" if you have any questions about policies.
	+ Choose "No, I have no questions" if you do not have any questions.
* **Understanding of Policy Violation Consequences** (`policy_violation_understanding`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose "Yes, I understand consequences" if you understand that policy violations may result in disciplinary action.
	+ Choose "No, I do not understand consequences" if you do not understand the consequences of policy violations.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date of acknowledgment and signature.
* **Witness Name (HR Representative)** (`witness_name`, text, optional): If you need to provide the name of an HR representative who witnessed your acknowledgment.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): You can add any additional comments about the policy handbook here.
