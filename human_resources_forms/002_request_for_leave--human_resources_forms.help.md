# Request For Leave - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Leave form is designed for employees to submit leave requests to their HR department. This form captures essential information about the leave, such as dates, duration, and status, to help HR process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your leave application.
2. If applicable, select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., sick leave, vacation, etc.).
3. Enter the duration of your leave in days.
4. Specify the leave dates (from and to).
5. Select the status of your leave (e.g., active, inactive).
6. Provide any additional comments or reasons for your leave.
7. If necessary, provide additional information for custom leave types, reporting managers, or other specific fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting your leave request.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you have a reporting manager for this leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., sick leave, vacation, etc.).
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your leave reason.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of days for your leave.
* **Leave Date From** (`leave_date_from`, `date`, required: false): Select the start date of your leave.
* **Leave Date To** (`leave_date_to`, `date`, required: false): Select the end date of your leave.
* **Leave Status** (`leave_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of your leave (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or reasons for your leave.
* **Leave Days** (`leave_days`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of leave days.
* **Leave Type Other** (`leave_type_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional leave type information.
* **Reporting Manager Other** (`reporting_manager_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional reporting manager information.
* **Leave From Other** (`leave_from_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional leave from information.
* **Leave To Other** (`leave_to_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional leave to information.
* **Leave Status Other** (`leave_status_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional leave status information.
* **Leave Reason Other** (`leave_reason_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional leave reason information.
* **Leave Duration Other** (`leave_duration_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional leave duration information.
* **Leave Days Other** (`leave_days_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional leave days information.
* **Comments Other** (`comments_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments information.

Note: If you have any issues or need help, please contact the HR department for assistance.
