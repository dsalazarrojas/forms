# occupational_health_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The occupational health absence form is used to report and record occupational health and safety incidents, absences, and related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a legitimate reason for absence related to employee health and well-being.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the employee's identification number to associate the form with their personnel record.
- **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, required/optional): Describe the reason for the absence, including any relevant details or circumstances.
- **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, text, required/optional): Attach a medical certificate or supporting document, if required or recommended by your organization's policies.
- **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required/optional): If a medical certificate is attached, enter the name of the doctor who issued it, for verification purposes.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy and completeness of your input for each field.
- Review and understand your organization's policies on occupational health and safety before filling out this form.
- Ensure you have any necessary supporting documentation or certificates before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
