# Employee Resource Group Creation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new Employee Resource Group (ERG) for employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Role Title in the field labeled "Role Title".
4. Enter your Department in the field labeled "Department".
5. Enter your Job Title in the field labeled "Job Title".
6. Select your reporting level for the new ERG in the field labeled "Reporting Level 1" and "Reporting Level 2".
7. Optionally, select additional reporting levels for the new ERG in the field labeled "Reporting Level 3".
8. Enter the Date of the new ERG in the field labeled "Date Field".
9. Enter the Time of the new ERG in the field labeled "Time Field".
10. Enter any additional notes about the new ERG in the field labeled "Note".
11. Enter your Email in the field labeled "Email".
12. Enter your Phone number in the field labeled "Phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Role Title** (`role_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your role title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Reporting Level 1** (`reporting_level_1`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select your reporting level 1 for the new ERG. Choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Reporting Level 2** (`reporting_level_2`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your reporting level 2 for the new ERG. Choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Reporting Level 3** (`reporting_level_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Optionally, select additional reporting levels for the new ERG. Choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Date Field** (`date_field`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the new ERG.
* **Time Field** (`time_field`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the new ERG.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the new ERG.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
