# Employee Referral Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about job openings and referrals from HR teams. It will help in creating a comprehensive and organized record of job openings, making it easier for the HR team to manage job postings and employee referrals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These are the fields that are necessary to complete the form and cannot be left blank.
2. Fill out the optional fields if you have the relevant information available. While these fields are not required, having accurate and up-to-date information will help in creating a complete and organized record of job openings.
3. Ensure that you fill in the correct information for each field. If you are unsure about a field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (1, text, required): Enter the job title of the job opening.
* **First Name** (2, text, required): Enter the first name of the employee or candidate.
* **Last Name** (3, text, required): Enter the last name of the employee or candidate.
* **Email** (4, email, required): Enter the email address of the employee or candidate.
* **Phone** (5, text, required): Enter the phone number of the employee or candidate.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the employee or candidate. (Note: This field is optional, but please fill it out if you have the information available.)
* **Department** (7, text, optional): Enter the department where the job opening is located.
* **Job Title 1** (8, select_one, optional): Select whether this job title is applicable to the job opening.
* **Job Title 2** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable job titles for the job opening.
* **Job Title 3** (10, select_one, optional): Select whether this job title is applicable to the job opening.
* **Job Level** (11, select_one, optional): Select the job level of the job opening (High or Low).
* **Salary Range** (12, number, optional): Enter the salary range for the job opening.
* **Job Status** (13, select_one, optional): Select the job status (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Type** (14, select_one, optional): Select the job type (Yes or No).
* **Job Category** (15, select_one, optional): Select the job category (Yes or No).
* **Job Description** (16, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job opening.
* **Job Location** (17, text, optional): Enter the location where the job opening is located.
* **Job URL** (18, text, optional): Enter the URL of the job posting or posting site.
* **Job Date Open** (19, date, optional): Enter the date the job opening was posted.
* **Job Status Note** (20, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the job status.
* **Job Requisition** (21, text, optional): Enter the job requisition number.
* **Job Urgency** (22, number, optional): Enter the job urgency level (e.g., 1-5).
* **Job Level Note** (23, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the job level.
* **Job Description Note** (24, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the job description.
* **Job Requisition Note** (25, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the job requisition.
