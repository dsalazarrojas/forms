# Store Employee Orientation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Employee Orientation Registration Form is designed to capture essential employee details, ensuring accurate and complete information for HR and management use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Employee Number, which is your unique identifier within the company.
2.  Enter your Employee Name, which is your full name as it appears on your ID or government-issued identification.
3.  Select your Employee Status, which indicates your current employment status (active, inactive, or pending).
4.  Enter your Email address, which is your primary contact information.
5.  Enter your Phone number, which is your work-related contact number.
6.  Choose your Employee Category (Full-time or Part-time) and Type (Regular or Temporary).
7.  Choose your Employee Type Category (Hourly or Salaried).
8.  Enter your Shift Start and Shift End times, which indicates your work hours.
9.  Enter your Work Date, which indicates the date you will be working.
10. Enter any additional notes regarding your employment details, if necessary.
11. Review and submit the form once all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or government-issued identification.
* **Employee Number**: Select your unique identifier within the company from the available options.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store you will be working at. 
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birth date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title or role within the company.
* **Department**: Enter the department you will be working in. 
* **Shift Start**: Enter the start time of your shift in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Shift End**: Enter the end time of your shift in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Work Date**: Enter the date you will be working in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Employee Status**: Select your current employment status (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes regarding your employment details.
* **Email**: Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your work-related phone number.
* **Employee Category**: Choose whether you are a Full-time or Part-time employee.
* **Employee Type**: Choose whether you are a Regular or Temporary employee.
* **Employee Type Category**: Choose whether you are Hourly or Salaried employee.
* **Store ID**: Enter the ID of the store you will be working at. 
* **Manager ID**: Enter the ID of your manager. 
* **Supervisor ID**: Enter the ID of your supervisor. 
* **Store Branch**: Enter the branch of the store you will be working at. 
* **Store Department**: Enter the department you will be working in. 
* **Date Hired**: Enter the date you were hired. 
* **Date Terminated**: Enter the date you were terminated. 
* **Date Returned**: Enter the date you returned to work after being terminated. 
* **Date Review**: Enter the date of your last performance review.
