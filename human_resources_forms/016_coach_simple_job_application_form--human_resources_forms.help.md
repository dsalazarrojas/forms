# Coach Simple Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive guide for applicants to provide necessary information for the job application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Applicant name** in the "Applicant name" field. This is your full name that will be used for communication and official records.
2. Provide your **Email address** in the "Email address" field. This is the contact email where we will reach out for further information or to schedule an interview.
3. If applicable, enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone number" field. This will help us contact you for any additional questions or to confirm your application.
4. Indicate the **Position applied for** by typing the job title you are interested in.
5. Enter your **Availability start date** (optional) in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. Provide a brief **Relevant experience** summary in the "Relevant experience" field. This is your opportunity to showcase your skills and experiences related to the job.
7. Attach or share a link to your **Resume** in the "Resume link or attachment reference" field. This will help us review your qualifications.
8. Provide contact information for your **References** (optional) in the "References" field.
9. Choose to **Consent to background check** by selecting either "True" or "False". This is a mandatory step to confirm your permission to undergo a background check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name (Required)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email address (Required)**: Provide your contact email for further communication.
* **Phone number (Optional)**: Enter your mobile phone number for easy contact.
* **Position applied for (Required)**: Type the job title you are interested in applying for.
* **Availability start date (Optional)**: Enter your available start date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Relevant experience (Required)**: Provide a brief summary of your relevant skills and experiences.
* **Resume link or attachment reference (Optional)**: Attach or share a link to your resume for review.
* **References (Optional)**: Provide contact information for your professional references.
* **Consent to background check (Required)**: Select "True" to consent to a background check, or "False" to decline.
