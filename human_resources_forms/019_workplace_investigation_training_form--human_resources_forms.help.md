# Workplace Investigation Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information about participants in a workplace investigation training program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the participant's details in the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, note, optional): Enter any relevant details about the incident or investigation.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, note, optional): Provide any feedback about the training program.
* **Participant Supervisor Name** (`participant_supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's supervisor's name.
* **Participant Supervisor Phone** (`participant_supervisor_phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's supervisor's phone number.
* **Participant Supervisor Email** (`participant_supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's supervisor's email address.
* **Participant Supervisor Occupation** (`participant_supervisor_occupation`, text, required): Enter the participant's supervisor's occupation.
* **Participant Supervisor Location** (`participant_supervisor_location`, text, optional): Enter the participant's supervisor's work location.
* **Participant Supervisor Start Date** (`participant_supervisor_start_date`, date, required): Enter the participant's supervisor's start date.
* **Participant Supervisor End Date** (`participant_supervisor_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the participant's supervisor's end date.
* **Participant Supervisor Shift** (`participant_supervisor_shift`, text, optional): Enter the participant's supervisor's work shift.
* **Participant Organizer** (`participant_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the participant's organizer's name.
* **Participant Organizer Phone** (`participant_organizer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the participant's organizer's phone number.
* **Participant Start Date** (`participant_start_date`, date, required): Enter the participant's start date.
* **Participant End Date** (`participant_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the participant's end date.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are essential for the form to be considered complete.
