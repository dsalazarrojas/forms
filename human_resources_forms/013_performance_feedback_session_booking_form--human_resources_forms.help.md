# Performance Feedback Session Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Feedback Session Booking Form is a tool used to schedule performance feedback sessions between employees and their superiors. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded for such sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to schedule a meeting.
2. Choose a date for the session using the calendar picker.
3. Select a time for the session using the time picker.
4. Enter the duration of the session.
5. Enter the name of the employee receiving feedback.
6. Enter the email address of the employee receiving feedback.
7. Optionally, enter the topic and location of the meeting.
8. Enter any additional meeting notes.
9. Enter your name and email address as the person booking the session.
10. Confirm your phone number and department (if applicable) as the person booking the session.
11. Finally, confirm the date and time of the session and add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Meeting**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to schedule a meeting.
* **Date Of Session**: Choose a date for the session using the calendar picker.
* **Time Of Session**: Select a time for the session using the time picker.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee receiving feedback.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the email address of the employee receiving feedback.
* **Meeting Topic**: Optionally, enter the topic of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location**: Optionally, enter the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes**: Enter any additional meeting notes.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person booking the session.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the person booking the session.
* **Submitter Phone**: Optionally, enter your phone number as the person booking the session.
* **Submitter Department**: Optionally, enter your department as the person booking the session.
* **Submitter Job Title**: Optionally, enter your job title as the person booking the session.
* **Submitter Position**: Optionally, enter your position as the person booking the session.
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the booking.
* **Submitter Time**: Select the time you are submitting the booking.
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any additional comments.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes.
* **Other Comments**: Optionally, enter any other comments.
* **Other Phone**: Optionally, enter an other phone number.
* **Other Phone Ext**: Optionally, enter the extension for the other phone number.
* **Other Email**: Optionally, enter the email address of the other person.
* **Other Department**: Optionally, enter the department of the other person.
* **Submitter Other Phone**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to book for someone else.
* **Submitter Other Phone Ext**: Optionally, enter the extension for the other phone number.
* **Submitter Other Email**: Optionally, enter the email address of the other person.
* **Submitter Other Department**: Optionally, enter the department of the other person.
