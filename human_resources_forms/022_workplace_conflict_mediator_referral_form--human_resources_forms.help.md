# Workplace Conflict Mediator Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Conflict Mediator Referral Form is a tool used by employees and their supervisors to report and refer workplace conflicts to mediation services. This form captures essential information about the conflict, including the employee's name, department, and conflict type, as well as the conflict's severity, duration, and frequency. The form also allows for the reporting of the conflict's resolution outcome and the employee's supervisor's name and department.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and department.
2. Select the conflict type from the provided options.
3. Provide a brief description of the conflict.
4. Choose the conflict's severity, duration, and frequency.
5. Select the resolution outcome and the mediation services needed.
6. Enter the employee's supervisor's name and department.
7. Add any comments from the employee and their supervisor.
8. Select the submitted by options for the employee and their supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee involved in the conflict.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department of the employee involved in the conflict.
* **Conflict Type** (`conflict_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of conflict (Performance, Personal, or Other).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the conflict.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, select_multiple, optional): Select the position of the employee involved in the conflict (Manager, Non-Manager).
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the employee involved in the conflict (Active, Retired, or Other).
* **Conflict Severity** (`conflict_severity`, select_one, optional): Select the severity of the conflict (Minor, Moderate, or Severe).
* **Resolution Outcome** (`resolution_outcome`, select_multiple, optional): Select the resolution outcome (Success, Partial Success, or None).
* **Employee Supervisor** (`employee_supervisor`, select_multiple, optional): Select the supervisor of the employee involved in the conflict (Manager, Other).
* **Conflict Duration** (`conflict_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of the conflict (Less than a month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, or 6+ months).
* **Conflict Frequency** (`conflict_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of the conflict (Single Incident, Recurring).
* **Conflict Cause** (`conflict_cause`, select_multiple, optional): Select the cause of the conflict (Communication Issues, Trust Issues, Performance Issues, or Other).
* **Mediation Services** (`meditation_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the mediation services needed (Individual Coaching, Team Coaching, or None).
* **Employee Referral Date** (`employee_referral_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee referred the conflict to mediation.
* **Manager Referral Date** (`manager_referral_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the manager referred the conflict to mediation.
* **Conflict Resolution Date** (`conflict_resolution_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the conflict was resolved.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments from the employee involved in the conflict.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments from the supervisor involved in the conflict.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments about the conflict.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the conflict to mediation (Manager, Other).
* **Manager Name** (`submitted_by_manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the manager who submitted the conflict.
* **Submitted Department** (`submitted_by_department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department of the manager who submitted the conflict (Human Resources, Other).
* **Submitted By Position** (`submitted_by_position`, select_multiple, optional): Select the position of the manager who submitted the conflict (Manager, Other).
* **Submitted By Supervisor** (`submitted_by_supervisor`, select_multiple, optional): Select the supervisor position of the manager who submitted the conflict (Manager, Other).
* **Employee Name** (`submitted_by_employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee who submitted the conflict.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to include all necessary information.
* Double-check the submitted information before submitting it.
* Ensure that the conflict is not already being resolved through other channels.
* If unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your HR representative.
