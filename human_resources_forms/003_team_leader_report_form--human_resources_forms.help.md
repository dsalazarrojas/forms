# Team Leader Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Leader Report Form is used to gather information from team leaders about their team's performance and progress. The form is meant to be completed by team leaders on a regular basis, typically at the end of each reporting period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the reporting period for which you are submitting the report.
2. Select the status of your project (Active or Inactive).
3. Provide an update on the progress of your project.
4. Describe any challenges your team is facing.
5. Outline the key actions you plan to take to address these challenges.
6. Submit the form to provide a comprehensive report on your team's status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, date, required): Enter the reporting period for which you are submitting the report. This is the time period for which you are providing an update on your team's status.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your project (Active or Inactive). This indicates whether your project is currently active or inactive.
* **Project Progress** (`project_progress`, text, required): Provide a detailed update on the progress of your project. Describe any significant milestones achieved or challenges encountered.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, required): Describe any challenges your team is facing. Be as specific as possible to help identify areas that require attention.
* **Key Actions** (`key_actions`, text, required): Outline the key actions you plan to take to address the challenges described in the previous field.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether your team has any team members who are currently experiencing challenges or require assistance.
* **Team Leader Report** (`team_leaders_report`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the team leader report.
