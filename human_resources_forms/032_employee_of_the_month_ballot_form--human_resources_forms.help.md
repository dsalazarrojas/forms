# Employee Of The Month Ballot Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Of The Month Ballot Form is used to nominate employees for recognition and rewards.

## How To Complete This Form
To successfully complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Determine if the employee has already been recognized as Employee of the Month in the past year. If yes, do not submit this form.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" to whether the employee is a strong candidate for Employee of the Month.
3. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field. Please ensure that the name matches the employee's official name.
4. Select the employee's department from the available options.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the employee's tenure in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively. This could be the start and end dates of their employment, project, or other relevant period.
6. Provide a brief reason for the nomination in the "Reason For Nomination" field.
7. Enter the nominee's email address in the "Nominee Email" field.
8. Enter the nominee's phone number in the "Nominee Phone" field.
9. Select the date of the nomination in the "Nomination Date" field.
10. Select the date of the submission in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination** (`select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" if the employee is a strong candidate for Employee of the Month.
* **Employee Name** (`text`, required): Enter the employee's official name.
* **Department** (`text`, optional): Select the employee's department.
* **Start Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the start date of the employee's tenure.
* **End Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the end date of the employee's tenure.
* **Reason For Nomination** (`text`, optional): Provide a brief reason for the nomination.
* **Nominee Email** (`email`, optional): Enter the nominee's email address.
* **Nominee Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the nominee's phone number.
* **Nomination Date** (`date`, optional): Select the date of the nomination.
* **Submission Date** (`date`, optional): Select the date of the submission.
