# Human Resources Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting personal and professional details of individuals applying for a job position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field. Note that this is not a required field, but please enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Enter your Company Name in the "Company Name" field.
7. Enter your Job Reference Number in the "Job Reference Number" field.
8. Upload your Resume in the "Resume" field.
9. Enter your Skills in the "Skills" field.
10. Enter your Work Experience in the "Work Experience" field.
11. Enter your Education in the "Education" field.
12. Enter your References in the "References" field.
13. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
14. Enter your Nationality in the "Nationality" field.
15. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
16. Enter your City in the "City" field.
17. Enter your Country in the "Country" field.
18. Enter your Post Code in the "Post Code" field.
19. Enter your Language in the "Language" field.
20. Enter how you found us in the "How did you find us?" field.
21. Enter your Job Position in the "Job Position" field.
22. Enter your Job Salary in the "Job Salary" field.
23. Enter your Job Start Date in the "Job Start Date" field.
24. Enter your Job End Date in the "Job End Date" field.
25. Enter your Job Description in the "Job Description" field.
