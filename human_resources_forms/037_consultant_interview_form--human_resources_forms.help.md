# Consultant Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultant Interview Form is a tool used to gather essential information about a candidate during an interview, focusing on evaluating their competencies, cultural fit, and overall suitability for a role or project. This form helps interviewers make informed decisions by providing a structured framework for assessing key qualities and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Start by filling out the basic information section**, which includes:
    *   Candidate name: Enter the full name of the candidate, as provided on their resume or application.
    *   Interview date: Enter the date of the interview, ensuring accuracy for reference purposes.
    *   Interviewer: Enter your own name for accurate identification.
2.  Proceed to the competencies section and briefly outline the candidate's scores, focusing on their performance across core competencies.
3.  Provide notes on the candidate's cultural fit and how they align with the team's values, highlighting both strengths and areas for improvement.
4.  In the final section, select the strongest recommendation for the candidate, considering their overall performance and fit:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the full name of the candidate to ensure accurate identification.
*   **Interview date** (`interview_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date of the interview to reference later on.
*   **Interviewer** (`interviewer`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your own name to identify yourself as the interviewer.
*   **Competency scores summary** (`competency_scores`, `text`, **Optional**): Briefly describe the candidate's performance across key competencies, providing a summary of their strengths and areas for improvement.
*   **Cultural fit assessment** (`cultural_fit`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide notes on how the candidate fits with the team's values and culture, highlighting both positive and negative aspects.
*   **Final decision** (`final_decision`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the strongest recommendation for the candidate, considering their overall performance and fit:

## Tips
-   Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely to get the most out of this form.
-   Focus on providing a clear and concise summary in the competency scores section to help with future reference.
-   Keep cultural fit notes brief but informative to aid in understanding the candidate's alignment with the team.
-   Choose the strongest recommendation based on the candidate's performance and cultural fit.
