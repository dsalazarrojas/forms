# faculty_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for faculty job applications, collecting essential information about the applicant's identity, qualifications, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, making sure to provide all required information.
2. Start with the first page, which includes fields for personal details like first name, middle initial, last name, email, and phone number.
3. Continue to the subsequent pages, which cover various aspects of your academic and professional background, such as job title, department, academic title, and research interests.
4. Provide details about your education, work experience, teaching experience, and language proficiency.
5. Add any relevant supporting documents like a CV, resume, or portfolio.
6. List any references and indicate their willingness to provide information about you.
7. Complete any remaining fields, including research areas, teaching experience, and academic appointments.
8. Review your application carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Middle Initial** (`middle_initial`, text, required): Enter your middle name's initial (e.g., "J.D." for John David).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title you're applying for.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you're interested in.
* **Academic Title** (`academic_title`, text, required): Enter your academic title (e.g., Dr., Prof., etc.).
* **CV** (`cv`, text, required): Attach a copy of your CV.
* **References** (`references`, select_multiple, required): Check "Yes" if you have references available, or "No" if you don't.
* **Education** (`education`, select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you have the necessary education, or "No" if you don't.
* **Research Areas** (`research_areas`, text, required): Describe your research interests and expertise.
* **Teaching Experience** (`teaching_experience`, text, required): Outline your teaching experience and qualifications.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Detail your relevant work experience.
* **Language** (`language`, text, required): Specify your language skills (e.g., fluency in English, etc.).
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach a copy of your resume.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, required): Share your portfolio or link to it.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required): List your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **References List** (`references_list`, text, required): Provide a list of your references with their contact information.
* **Education List** (`education_list`, text, required): List your educational background.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, text, required): Describe your research interests and areas of focus.
* **Research Areas Expertise** (`research_areas_expertise`, text, required): Specify your areas of research expertise.
* **Academic Appointments** (`academic_appointments`, text, required): List any academic appointments or honors you've received.
* **Other** (`other`, text, required): Add any additional information about yourself.
