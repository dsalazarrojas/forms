# Stock Options Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to declare and manage stock options, which are a type of equity compensation granted to employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by ensuring that you have all necessary information about your stock options ready.
2. On Page 1, enter your Employee ID number.
3. Select the type of option you are declaring: Stock Options, Stock Options - Restricted, or Stock Options - Vested.
4. Enter the number of stock options you have.
5. Enter the option price.
6. Enter the grant date.
7. Enter the vest date.
8. Enter the last vest date.
9. Select the option status: Active or Inactive.
10. Add any additional comments or explanations in the notes field.
11. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Option Type (2)**: Select the type of option you are declaring: Stock Options, Stock Options - Restricted, or Stock Options - Vested.
* **Number of Options (3)**: Enter the total number of stock options you have.
* **Option Price (4)**: Enter the price of the stock options.
* **Grant Date (5)**: Enter the date when you were granted the stock options.
* **Vest Date (6)**: Enter the date when your stock options vested (became eligible for exercise).
* **Last Vest Date (7)**: Enter the date when your stock options last vested.
* **Option Status (8)**: Select the status of your stock options: Active or Inactive.
* **Notes (9)**: Add any additional comments or explanations about your stock options.
* **Email (10)**: Enter your email address (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your HR representative for clarification.
