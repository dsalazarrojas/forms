# Employee Wellbeing Tool Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Wellbeing Tool Requisition Form is designed to help employees request wellbeing tools and resources that support their mental and emotional health and wellbeing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including the assigned wellbeing tool you are requesting.
2. Provide any additional comments or information about your request in the comments section.
3. Submit the form to the wellbeing team for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Wellbeing Tool Requisition Form**: This is the main form that requests the wellbeing tool you need. Please select from the options provided.
* **Assigned Wellbeing Tool**: This field is required and allows you to choose a wellbeing tool that is suitable for your needs. Please select one of the options listed.
* **Employee Wellbeing Tool Requisition Form Comments**: This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments or information about your wellbeing needs.

## Tips

* Please be specific about your wellbeing needs and the tool you are requesting.
* If you are unsure about which wellbeing tool to choose, contact the wellbeing team for guidance.
* We will respond to your wellbeing tool request as soon as possible, but please allow up to 5 working days for processing.
