# Exit Interview Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from departing employees about their reasons for leaving the company, last day of work, and other relevant details. This information will help the HR department to gather feedback and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the name of your supervisor in the "Supervisor Name" field.
5. Enter the name of your manager in the "Manager Name" field.
6. Provide a brief explanation of your reason for leaving in the "Reason for Leaving" field.
7. Enter your last date of work in the "Last Date of Work" field.
8. Enter the day you last worked in the "Last Day of Week" field.
9. Add any additional comments about your experience in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
11. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
12. Enter your team in the "Team" field.
13. Enter the name of your reporting manager in the "Reporting Manager" field.
14. Enter a brief description of your job in the "Job Description" field.
15. Enter your job classification in the "Job Classification" field.
16. Enter your job family in the "Job Family" field.
17. Enter your job level in the "Job Level" field.
18. Enter your job grade in the "Job Grade" field.
19. Enter your job category in the "Job Category" field.
20. Enter a brief description of your job function in the "Job Function" field.
21. Enter a brief description of your job family in the "Job Family Description" field.
22. Enter your job grade/level in the "Job Grade/Level" field.
23. Enter your job title code in the "Job Title Code" field.
24. Enter a brief description of your job category in the "Job Category Description" field.
25. Enter a brief description of your job classification in the "Job Classification Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Required): Enter your job title as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the manager who supervised you during your tenure.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, Text, Required): Provide a brief explanation of why you are leaving the company.
* **Last Date of Work** (`last_date_of_work`, Date, Required): Enter the date you last worked for the company.
* **Last Day of Week** (`last_day_of_week`, Time, Required): Enter the day of the week you last worked for the company.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your department as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Team** (`team`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your team as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the manager who reported to you.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your job role and responsibilities.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, Text, Required): Enter your job classification as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Job Family** (`job_family`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your job family as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, Text, Required): Enter your job level as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Job Grade** (`job_grade`, Text, Required): Enter your job grade as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your job category as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your job function.
* **Job Family Description** (`job_family_description`, Note, Required): Enter a brief description of your job family.
* **Job Grade/Level** (`job_grade_level`, Text, Required): Enter your job grade and level.
* **Job Title Code** (`job_title_code`, Text, Required): Enter the job title code as it is written in the company's HR system.
* **Job Category Description** (`job_category_description`, Note, Required): Enter a brief description of your job category.
* **Job Classification Description** (`job_classification_description`, Note, Required): Enter a brief description of your job classification.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and honest information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
* Please complete the form as thoroughly as possible to provide valuable feedback to the company.
