# Volunteer Database - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Database form is designed to collect and store essential information about volunteers. This information includes personal details, contact information, and volunteer-related data. The form is intended to be used in conjunction with other systems for comprehensive volunteer management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Volunteer Database form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the volunteer before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any) and provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.
3. Review the information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Database Form** (`volunteer_database_form`, text, required/optional: optional)
	+ A brief description of the form, providing context for the subsequent fields.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter the volunteer's full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter the volunteer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter the volunteer's phone number.
* **Volunteer Date** (`volunteer_date`, date, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter the date the volunteer is available to work.
* **Volunteer Time** (`volunteer_time`, time, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter the specific time the volunteer is available to work.
* **Volunteer Duration** (`volunteer_duration`, text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter the duration of the volunteer's available time slot.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional: optional)
	+ Add any additional comments or information about the volunteer's availability.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional: optional)
	+ Select the tool assigned to the volunteer from the options provided.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter a category or label to further describe the volunteer's information.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter the department or team the volunteer belongs to.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure accurate volunteer management.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors or omissions.
* The form is not intended to be a comprehensive volunteer management system, so use it in conjunction with other systems for more in-depth management.
