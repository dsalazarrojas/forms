# Retail Onboarding Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Onboarding Quiz is a form designed to collect information about the candidate's retail experience, skills, and focus areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions based on your genuine experiences and skills.
2. Choose the options that best match your experiences and skills.
3. Fill in any text fields with your honest answers.
4. Review your answers before submitting.
