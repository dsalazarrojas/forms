# Assistant Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assistant Declaration Form is a critical document that ensures transparency and clarity on new assignments within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form) carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for assignment duration and type.
3. Provide a detailed description of the new assignment, including key responsibilities and performance expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (employee_first_name): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (employee_last_name): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): Enter your personnel identification number.
* **Email Address** (email_address): Enter your work email address.
* **Department** (department): Enter your department or division within the organization.
* **Current Position** (current_position): Enter your current job title.
* **Supervisor Name** (supervisor_name): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Declaration Date** (declaration_date): Enter the date of this declaration.
* **New Assignment Title** (new_assignment_title): Enter the title of your new role or assignment.
* **Assignment Effective Date** (assignment_effective_date): Enter the date when your new assignment starts.
* **Assignment Duration** (assignment_duration): Select the duration of your new assignment (Permanent, Temporary (6 months), Temporary (1 year), Contract Based, or Project Based).
* **Type of Assignment** (assignment_type): Select the category of your new assignment (Full-time Role, Part-time Role, Additional Responsibility, Acting Role, Relief Position, Volunteer Assignment, or Other).
* **Assignment Description** (assignment_description): Enter a detailed description of your new assignment.
* **Key Responsibilities** (key_responsibilities): Enter the main duties and tasks of your new assignment.
* **Required Qualifications** (required_qualifications): (Optional) Enter the skills and experience required for this new assignment.
* **Salary or Compensation Adjustment** (salary_compensation): (Optional) Enter any changes to your pay or compensation.
* **Reporting Structure** (reporting_structure): Enter who you will report to in your new assignment.
* **Performance Expectations** (performance_expectations): Enter your goals and success metrics for this new assignment.
* **Training or Development Required** (training_development): (Optional) Enter any training or development needed for this new assignment.
* **Support and Resources Available** (support_resources): Enter the support and resources that will be provided for this new assignment.
* **I Acknowledge Understanding** (acknowledge_understanding): Confirm that you understand the terms of your new assignment.
* **I Accept this Assignment** (acknowledge_acceptance): Confirm that you accept this new assignment.
* **I Agree to Conditions** (acknowledge_conditions): Confirm that you agree to the conditions stated above.
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature): Sign your name as a declaration of your understanding and acceptance.
* **Supervisor Signature** (supervisor_signature): Sign as a supervisor to acknowledge your understanding and acceptance.
