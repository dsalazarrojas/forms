# workforce_pipeline_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workforce_pipeline_intake_form is a data collection form designed to gather information about candidates applying to various job or program opportunities. Its primary purpose is to streamline the intake process, making it easier to manage and track candidate data throughout the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's personal details, such as name, contact information, and availability.
2. Provide information about the program or job the candidate is applying for, including job preferences and job notes.
3. Describe the candidate's skills, experience, and education.
4. List any relevant certifications the candidate holds.
5. Specify the candidate's desired work arrangement (e.g., full-time, part-time, remote).
6. Finally, submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details (candidate_details)** (`candidate_details`, text, not required): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Program Details (program_details)** (`program_details`, text, not required): Enter the program or job title you're applying for, including any relevant details.
* **Job Details (job_details)** (`job_details`, text, not required): Enter any relevant job-specific information, such as job description or requirements.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, or other relevant details.
* **Skills (skills)** (`skills`, text, not required): List your relevant skills, qualifications, or areas of expertise.
* **Experience (experience)** (`experience`, text, not required): Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates.
* **Education (education)** (`education`, text, not required): List your relevant education background, including degrees, institutions, and dates.
* **Certifications (certifications)** (`certifications`, text, not required): List any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
* **Work Arrangement (work_arrangement)** (`work_arrangement`, text, not required): Specify your preferred work arrangement (e.g., full-time, part-time, remote).
* **Availability (availability)** (`availability`, text, not required): Indicate your availability for interviews, meetings, or other work-related activities.
* **Job Preferences (job_preferences)** (`job_preferences`, text, not required): Enter any specific job preferences or requirements you have.
* **Job Notes (job_notes)** (`job_notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional job-specific information or comments.
