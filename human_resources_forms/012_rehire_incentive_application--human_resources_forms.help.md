# Rehire Incentive Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehire Incentive Application form is used to request rehire for an employee after they have left the company. This form helps to track the reason for rehire, category, date of rehire, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for rehire and your manager's approval.
2. Fill out the 'Reason for Rehire' field with a brief description of your reason.
3. Select the relevant 'Category' for your rehire request.
4. Enter the 'Date of Rehire' in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Fill out the 'Job Title' field if required.
6. Provide a brief description in the 'Manager Comments' field if needed.
7. If approved, fill out the 'Rehire Incentive Amount' field.
8. Select 'Approved' in the 'Rehire Incentive Approval' field.
9. If approved, enter the 'Rehire Incentive Approval Date'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rehire Incentive Application** (`rehire_incentive_application_1`, text, required: false): This is the main title of the form.
* **Category** (`rehire_incentive_application_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more relevant categories for your rehire request. Options: HR, Recruiting, Finance.
* **Date of Rehire** (`rehire_incentive_application_3`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you expect to rejoin the company in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Date of Rehire** (`rehire_incentive_application_4`, date, required: false): This field is a duplicate of the 'Date of Rehire' field. Please ensure that you fill out only one of these fields.
* **Job Title** (`rehire_incentive_application_5`, text, required: false): If required, enter your job title after rehire.
* **Reason for Rehire** (`rehire_incentive_application_6`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your reason for rehire.
* **Manager Comments** (`rehire_incentive_application_7`, text, required: false): If needed, provide a brief description in the 'Manager Comments' field.
* **Rehire Incentive Amount** (`rehire_incentive_application_8`, number, required: false): If approved, fill out this field with the amount of rehire incentive.
* **Rehire Incentive Approval** (`rehire_incentive_application_9`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Approved' or 'Not Approved' for rehire incentive approval. Options: Approved, Not Approved.
* **Rehire Incentive Approval Date** (`rehire_incentive_application_10`, date, required: false): If approved, enter the date of rehire incentive approval in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
