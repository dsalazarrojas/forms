# Employee Exit Interview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an employee's exit interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (1): This field is used to identify the employee completing the form.
* **Reason for Leaving** (2): Provide a brief description of why you are leaving the company.
* **New Company** (3): If you have joined a new company, please enter the name of the new company.
* **Last Date Worked** (4): Enter the date you last worked for the company.
* **Last Title** (5): Answer whether you held a title at your last employment with the company.
* **Last Salary Range** (6): If you answer yes to the last title, enter the corresponding salary range for that title.
* **New Salary Range** (7): If you are currently employed, enter your current salary range.
* **New Title** (8): Enter your current job title.
* **Last Manager Name** (9): Enter the name of your last manager.
* **Last Manager Phone** (10): Enter the phone number of your last manager.
* **Last Termination Date** (11): Enter the date of your last termination.
* **Reason for Termination** (12): If you were terminated, provide a brief description of the reason for termination.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and honest information in this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your HR representative for guidance.
