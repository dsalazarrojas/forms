# team_leaders_feedback_form_generic_monitoring - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by team leaders to provide feedback on the performance and behavior of team members in a generic monitoring context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the team member you are providing feedback for in the **employee** field.
2. Type your feedback regarding the team member's performance and behavior in the **feedback** field.
3. Select the date on which you took note of the feedback in the **date** field.
4. If applicable, specify the time of day when you took note of the feedback in the **time** field.
5. If you are providing feedback from someone else, please mention their name in the **feedback_from** field.
6. If you are providing feedback to someone else, please mention their name in the **feedback_to** field.
7. You may choose to rate the team member's performance on a scale of 1 to 5 in the **rating** field, although this field is optional.
8. Finally, include any additional comments regarding the feedback in the **comment** field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee** (`name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the team member you are providing feedback for.
* **feedback** (`name`, `text`, `required`): Provide your feedback regarding the team member's performance and behavior.
* **date** (`name`, `date`, `required`): Select the date on which you took note of the feedback.
* **time** (`name`, `time`, `required`): If applicable, specify the time of day when you took note of the feedback.
* **feedback_from** (`name`, `text`, `required`): If you are providing feedback from someone else, mention their name here.
* **feedback_to** (`name`, `text`, `required`): If you are providing feedback to someone else, mention their name here.
* **rating** (`name`, `number`, `optional`): You may choose to rate the team member's performance on a scale of 1 to 5, although this field is optional.
* **comment** (`name`, `text`, `optional`): Include any additional comments regarding the feedback, if necessary.
