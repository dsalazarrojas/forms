# Part Time Job Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Part Time Job Declaration Form is used to declare any part-time jobs that an employee has outside of their primary employment with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read each field carefully and answer all required fields.
2. If you have a part-time job, select "Yes" under "outside jobs".
3. If you do not have a part-time job, select "No".
4. If you select "Yes" under "outside jobs", enter the name of your part-time job under "job name".
5. Select the type of your part-time job under "outside job type".
6. Indicate how frequently you work at your part-time job under "job frequency".
7. Enter the start and end dates of your part-time job under "job start date" and "job end date", respectively.
8. Enter the location of your part-time job under "job location".
9. If applicable, enter the number of hours worked at your part-time job under "outside job hours".
10. If applicable, enter a brief description of your part-time job duties under "job duties".
11. If applicable, enter a brief description of your part-time job duties under "job duties description".
12. If your part-time job has terminated, enter the date it terminated under "job termination date". If your part-time job terminated, select "Yes" under "outside jobs" and enter a brief reason under "job termination reason".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Outside jobs** (`outside_jobs`, select_multiple, required): 
This field asks if you have any part-time jobs outside of your primary employment with the company.
* **Job name** (`job_name`, text, required): 
Enter the name of your part-time job.
* **Outside job type** (`outside_job_type`, select_one, required): 
Select the type of your part-time job. 
* **Job frequency** (`job_frequency`, select_multiple, required): 
Indicate how frequently you work at your part-time job.
* **Job hours** (`job_hours`, text, optional): 
Enter the number of hours worked at your part-time job.
* **Job start date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): 
Enter the start date of your part-time job.
* **Job end date** (`job_end_date`, date, required): 
Enter the end date of your part-time job.
* **Job location** (`job_location`, text, required): 
Enter the location of your part-time job.
* **Outside job hours** (`outside_job_hours`, text, optional): 
Enter the number of hours worked at your part-time job.
* **Job duties** (`job_duties`, text, optional): 
Enter a brief description of your part-time job duties.
* **Job duties description** (`job_duties_description`, text, optional): 
Enter a brief description of your part-time job duties.
* **Job termination date** (`job_termination_date`, date, optional): 
Enter the date your part-time job terminated.
* **Job termination reason** (`job_termination_reason`, text, optional): 
Enter a brief reason why your part-time job terminated.
