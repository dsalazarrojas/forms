# Product Manager Leave of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about a Product Manager's leave of absence, including start and end dates, reason for leave, status, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee field with your employee ID or name.
2. Enter your Job Title and Job Group in the respective fields.
3. Choose the From Date and To Date of your leave of absence.
4. Provide a clear Reason for your leave.
5. Update your Status to Active or Inactive, depending on your current status.
6. Choose whether your Manager has given approval for this leave (Yes or No).
7. Select the Approval Status of this leave (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (`Employee`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID or name to help us identify you in our system.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, optional): Enter your job title to help us understand your role within the company.
* **Job Group** (`Job Group`, text, optional): Enter your job group to help us understand your team or department.
* **From Date** (`From Date`, date, optional): Choose the date you plan to start your leave on.
* **To Date** (`To Date`, date, optional): Choose the date you plan to end your leave on.
* **Reason** (`Reason`, text, optional): Provide a clear reason for your leave of absence.
* **Status** (`Status`, select multiple, optional): Update your status to Active or Inactive, depending on your current status.
* **Manager** (`Manager`, select one, optional): Choose whether your manager has given approval for this leave (Yes or No).
* **Approval Status** (`Approval Status`, select one, optional): Select the approval status of this leave (Active or Inactive).
