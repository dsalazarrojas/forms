# Workforce Size Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Size Assessment Form is used to gather information about the workforce size and composition of a team within an organization. This form helps HR or departmental teams to assess and document the size and status of their teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide the job title and description of the team in the "Job Title" and "Job Description" fields.
3. Enter the headcount of the team members in the "Headcount" field.
4. Enter the name of the manager in charge of the team in the "Manager" field.
5. Enter the email address of the manager in charge of the team in the "Manager Email" field.
6. Enter the phone number of the manager in charge of the team in the "Phone" field.
7. Indicate if the team is led by a team lead by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Team Lead" field.
8. List the team members in the "Team Members" field by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each member.
9. Enter the size of the team in the "Team Size" field.
10. Indicate the type of team in the "Team Type" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
11. Indicate the status of the team in the "Team Status" field by selecting "Active" or "Inactive".
12. Enter the email address of the team lead in the "Team Lead Email" field.
13. Enter the phone number of the team lead in the "Team Lead Phone" field.
14. Enter the count of team members in the "Team Members Count" field.
15. Enter the name of team members in the "Team Members Name" field.
16. Enter the email addresses of team members in the "Team Members Email" field.
17. Enter the phone numbers of team members in the "Team Members Phone" field.
18. Enter the job titles of team members in the "Team Members Job Title" field.
19. Enter the headcount of team members in the "Team Members Headcount" field.
20. Enter the department of team members in the "Team Members Department" field.
21. Enter the name of the manager of team members in the "Team Members Manager" field.
22. Enter the email address of team members' managers in the "Team Members Manager Email" field.
23. Enter the phone number of team members' managers in the "Team Members Manager Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): A title for the form that will be used to reference this team's data.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: true): The title of the job that this team is responsible for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): A brief description of the job that this team is responsible for.
* **Headcount** (`headcount`, number, required: true): The number of team members in this team.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): The department that this team belongs to.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, required: true): The name of the manager in charge of this team.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required: true): The email address of the manager in charge of this team.
* **Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required: true): The phone number of the manager in charge of this team.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, required: true): Indicate if the team is led by a team lead by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required: true): List the team members by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each member.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required: true): The size of the team.
* **Team Type** (`team_type`, select_one, required: true): Indicate the type of team by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Team Status** (`team_status`, select_one, required: true): Indicate the status of the team by selecting "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, required: true): The email address of the team lead.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, required: true): The phone number of the team lead.
* **Team Members Count** (`team_members_count`, number, required: true): The count of team members.
* **Team Members Name** (`team_members_name`, text, required: true): The name of team members.
* **Team Members Email** (`team_members_email`, text, required: false): A list of email addresses of team members.
* **Team Members Phone** (`team_members_phone`, text, required: false): A list of phone numbers of team members.
* **Team Members Job Title** (`team_members_job_title`, text, required: false): A list of job titles of team members.
* **Team Members Headcount** (`team_members_headcount`, number, required: false): The headcount of team members.
* **Team Members Department** (`team_members_department`, text, required: false): The department of team members.
* **Team Members Manager** (`team_members_manager`, text, required: false): The name of the manager of team members.
* **Team Members Manager Email** (`team_members_manager_email`, email, required: false): The email address of team members' managers.
* **Team Members Manager Phone** (`team_members_manager_phone`, text, required: false): The phone number of team members' managers.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as required.
* Double-check that the form title is clear and descriptive.
* List team members with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that the team lead and manager information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the team status to ensure it accurately reflects the current state of the team.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR or departmental team for guidance.
