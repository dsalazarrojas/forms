# separation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The separation form is a document used to record the separation of an employee from the organization. It captures essential details about the employee's departure, including their name, reason for leaving, department, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the employee's name in the `Employee Name` field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the `Employee ID` field (if applicable).
3. Specify the reason for the employee's departure in the `Reason for Leaving` field.
4. Indicate whether the employee's department is affected by their separation (Yes/No) in the `Department` field.
5. Obtain the manager's signature in the `Manager Signature` field.
6. Get the employee's signature in the `Employee Signature` field.
7. Enter the date of the employee's separation in the `Date of Separation` field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the `Notes` field (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their ID card or personnel record.
* **Employee ID** (number, optional): Enter the employee's ID number if applicable to the organization's record-keeping practices.
* **Reason for Leaving** (text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for the employee's departure. This will help in maintaining a record of departures for future reference.
* **Department** (select one, optional): Indicate whether the employee's department will be affected by their separation. Select "Yes" if the department is affected, and "No" otherwise.
* **Manager Signature** (text, required): Obtain a signature from the manager who oversees the employee's area of work. This ensures the manager acknowledges the employee's departure.
* **Employee Signature** (text, required): Obtain a signature from the employee themselves, confirming their understanding of the separation and agreement to the terms.
* **Date of Separation** (date, required): Enter the date on which the employee is separating from the organization.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the separation if necessary. This can include details about the reasons for the departure or any special circumstances surrounding the employee's separation.
