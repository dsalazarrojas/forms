# Procurement Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Procurement Authorization Form is used to request the purchase of items or services within the organization. It serves as a verification step to ensure that all necessary procedures have been followed and approvals have been obtained before initiating a procurement process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct category for your request from the drop-down menu.
2. Provide a detailed description of the items or services you are seeking to purchase.
3. Enter the name of the person requesting the purchase.
4. Enter the date of the purchase (if applicable, as this field is not required).
5. Enter the purchase amount.
6. Select the individual who has approved this purchase.
7. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes regarding the purchase request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (Form Title, Text, Required): Enter a brief title for your purchase request.
* **Category** (Category, Select One, Required): Select the relevant category for your purchase request, such as "Human Resources Forms" or "Procurement Forms".
* **Description** (Description, Text, Required): Provide a detailed description of the items or services you are seeking to purchase.
* **Purchase Requestor** (Purchase Requestor, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person making the purchase request.
* **Date of Purchase** (Date of Purchase, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the purchase, if applicable.
* **Purchase Amount** (Purchase Amount, Number, Required): Enter the total amount of the purchase.
* **Approved By** (Approved By, Select One, Required): Select the individual who has approved this purchase, such as "Procurement Manager" or "Procurement Coordinator".
* **Comments** (Comments, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the purchase request.
