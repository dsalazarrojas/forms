# Personal Travel Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Personal Travel Leave Of Absence Request" form is used to submit a formal request for a leave of absence for personal travel. This form helps the HR department track and process your leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee name.
2. Enter your Leave from date and To date.
3. Provide a brief Reason for leave.
4. Specify the Number of days off you will be taking.
5. Enter your Travel location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or your official company documents.
* **Leave from date** (`leave_from_date`, Date, Required): Choose the date you will be leaving for your leave. This date should match your actual travel plans.
* **To date** (`to_date`, Date, Required): Choose the date you will be returning from your leave. This date should match your actual travel plans.
* **Reason for leave** (`reason_for_leave`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave, e.g., "Family vacation," "Personal appointment," etc.
* **Number of days off** (`number_of_days_off`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of days you will be taking off. This will help the HR team schedule your work coverage.
* **Travel location** (`travel_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location(s) you will be visiting during your leave.
