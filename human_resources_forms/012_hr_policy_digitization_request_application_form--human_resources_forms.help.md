# HR Policy Digitization Request Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assist employees and staff in submitting requests for digitization of HR policies. The purpose of this form is to help HR teams to quickly identify and prioritize requests, making it easier to digitize policies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review your request and make sure it is clear and concise.
2. Decide on the type of document you are requesting to be digitized (e.g., policy, procedure, guidelines, etc.).
3. Select the priority level of your request (high, medium, low).
4. Enter your email and phone number so that the HR team can contact you for further clarification or information.
5. Provide a description of the document or policy you are requesting to be digitized.
6. Enter your department and job title to help HR teams identify your location and role.
7. If necessary, provide the employee ID and policy ID numbers related to the document or policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Please select "Yes" if you wish to submit a request, and "No" if you are not ready to submit a request at this time.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select one or more document types you are requesting to be digitized (e.g., policy, procedure, guidelines, etc.).
* **Description** (`request_description`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of the document or policy you are requesting to be digitized.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the level of priority you assign to your request (high, medium, low).
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address so that the HR team can contact you for further clarification or information.
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number so that the HR team can contact you for further clarification or information.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your employee ID number.
* **Policy ID** (`policy_id`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the policy ID number related to the document or policy.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select your department.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Employee ID** (`submitter_employee_id`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your employee ID number.
* **Submitter Policy ID** (`submitter_policy_id`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the policy ID number related to the document or policy.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all fields that are required or relevant to your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the HR team for guidance.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting your request.
* The HR team will review and prioritize your request based on your input.
