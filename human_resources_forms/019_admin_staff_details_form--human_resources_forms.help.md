# Admin Staff Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Admin Staff Details Form is designed to collect and store detailed information about admin staff members, which will be used for HR-related purposes such as staff management, performance evaluations, and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Admin Staff Details Form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are filling out the form for an admin staff member.
2.  Enter the staff member's legal name in the "Full Name" field.
3.  Enter the staff member's unique employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
4.  Enter the staff member's professional email address in the "Email Address" field.
5.  Enter the staff member's contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6.  Enter the staff member's current residential address in the "Home Address" field.
7.  Enter the staff member's city of residence in the "City" field.
8.  Enter the staff member's state or province of residence in the "State/Province" field.
9.  Enter the postal code of the staff member's residence in the "ZIP/Postal Code" field.
10.  Enter the staff member's country of residence in the "Country" field.
11.  Enter the staff member's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
12.  Select the staff member's current job title in the "Position Title" field.
13.  Select the department the staff member works in from the "Department" options.
14.  Select the type of employment arrangement the staff member has (e.g., full-time, part-time, etc.) in the "Employment Type" field.
15.  Enter the date the staff member was hired in the "Hire Date" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
16.  Enter the name of the staff member's direct supervisor in the "Manager/Supervisor Name" field.
17.  Enter the email address of the staff member's direct supervisor in the "Manager/Supervisor Email" field (optional).
18.  Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
19.  Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
20.  Enter the relationship between the staff member and the emergency contact person in the "Relationship to Emergency Contact" field.
21.  Select the highest level of education completed by the staff member in the "Education Level" field.
22.  Enter the number of years of previous experience the staff member has.
23.  Enter any special skills or qualifications relevant to the position in the "Special Skills or Qualifications" field.
24.  Enter any professional certifications the staff member holds in the "Professional Certifications" field.
25.  Upload a professional photo of the staff member (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`staff_full_name`, text, required): Enter the staff member's legal name.
* **Employee ID** (`staff_employee_id`, text, required): Enter the staff member's unique employee ID.
* **Email Address** (`staff_email`, email, required): Enter the staff member's professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`staff_phone`, text, required): Enter the staff member's contact phone number.
* **Home Address** (`staff_address`, text, required): Enter the staff member's current residential address.
* **City** (`staff_city`, text, required): Enter the staff member's city of residence.
* **State/Province** (`staff_state`, text, required): Enter the staff member's state or province of residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`staff_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the staff member's residence.
* **Country** (`staff_country`, text, required): Enter the staff member's country of residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the staff member's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Select the staff member's current job title.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department the staff member works in.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of employment arrangement the staff member has.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter the date the staff member was hired in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Manager/Supervisor Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member's direct supervisor.
* **Manager/Supervisor Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the staff member's direct supervisor.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship between the staff member and the emergency contact person.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select the highest level of education completed by the staff member.
* **Years of Previous Experience** (`previous_experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years of previous experience the staff member has.
* **Special Skills or Qualifications** (`special_skills`, text, optional): Enter any special skills or qualifications relevant to the position.
* **Professional Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): Enter any professional certifications the staff member holds.
* **Photo Upload** (`staff_photo_upload`, text, optional): Upload a professional photo of the staff member (optional).

## Tips

* Ensure you are filling out the form for an admin staff member to prevent duplication or misuse.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information for the staff member.
* Use the format YYYY-MM-DD for date fields.
* Use the correct format for email addresses (e.g., `username@example.com`).
* Select the correct department and employment type from the available options.
* Use the correct format for phone numbers (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
