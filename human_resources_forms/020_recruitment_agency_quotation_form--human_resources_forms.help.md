# Recruitment Agency Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for recruitment agencies to quote services to clients, providing a comprehensive summary of their offer, including terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your agency's name and any relevant contact information.
2. Provide a brief description of the service you're quoting.
3. Specify the client's name and any other relevant details.
4. Enter the address where the service will be rendered.
5. Add any additional service details as necessary.
6. Select the service start and end dates.
7. Determine the service charge and payment terms.
8. Confirm whether the payment terms have been agreed upon.
9. Sign the form to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter your agency's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their business card or letterhead.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the service you're offering. This could include details about what the service entails, any relevant experience you have, or any other information that might influence the client's decision.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address where the service will be performed.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the primary contact person for this service.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for the client to contact you.
* **Quotation** (`quotation`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise description of your offer, including any specific details about what's included and what's not.
* **Service Charge** (`service_charge`, number, required): Enter the total service charge for the client to consider.
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time when the service will start.
* **Service End Date** (`service_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time when the service will end.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Add any special payment details or requirements.
* **Payment Terms Agreed** (`payment_terms_agreed`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" to confirm that the payment terms have been agreed upon with the client.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Include any relevant terms and conditions for the service, such as liability, warranties, or guarantees.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm that you've reviewed and agreed to the terms with the client.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to provide a fair and comprehensive quote to the client. Review the terms and conditions carefully to avoid any misunderstandings.
