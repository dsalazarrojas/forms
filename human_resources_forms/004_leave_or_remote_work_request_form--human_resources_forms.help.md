# Leave Or Remote Work Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Leave Or Remote Work Request Form" is designed to be used by employees to request leave or remote work arrangements. This form will help the HR department to process and manage these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Employee Details" field with your name and employee ID (if applicable).
2. Enter your "Remote Work Start Date" and "Remote Work End Date" to specify the duration of your remote work arrangement.
3. Provide a brief "Reason For Remote Work" to explain your request.
4. If applicable, select the "Leave Type" that best describes your reason for requesting leave.
5. Enter your "Remote Work Location" where you will be working remotely (if different from the office).
6. Enter your "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" if you would like the HR department to contact you directly.
7. Select your "Department" from the dropdown list to ensure that your request is directed to the correct team.
8. Indicate whether your request requires "Manager Approval" (yes or no).
9. Provide an "Approval Reason" if you would like to add any additional context for your manager's consideration.
10. Enter your "Manager Name" and their contact information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details
• **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, optional): This field is where you will enter your name and employee ID (if applicable).

### Remote Work Start Date
• **Remote Work Start Date** (`remote_work_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you will start your remote work arrangement.

### Remote Work End Date
• **Remote Work End Date** (`remote_work_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you will end your remote work arrangement.

### Reason For Remote Work
• **Reason For Remote Work** (`reason_for_remote_work`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your remote work request.

### Leave Type
• **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of leave you are requesting (Sick Leave, Vacation Leave, Business Leave, or Other).

### Remote Work Location
• **Remote Work Location** (`remote_work_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where you will be working remotely (if different from the office).

### Contact Email
• **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where the HR department can contact you directly.

### Contact Phone
• **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where the HR department can contact you directly.

### Department
• **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, optional): Select your department from the dropdown list to ensure that your request is directed to the correct team.

### Manager Approval
• **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether your request requires manager approval (yes or no).

### Approval Reason
• **Approval Reason** (`approval_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide an additional context for your manager's consideration.

### Manager Name
• **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's name and their contact information (if applicable).

### Note
Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative directly.
