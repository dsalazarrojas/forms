## Farewell Gift Contribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farewell Gift Contribution Form is designed to collect user information and contribution details for gift-giving purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Details** section with your personal information.
2. Provide your contribution details in the **Contribution Details** section.
3. Enter your payment details in the **Payment Details** section (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details (User Details)**: This field is a required field where you should enter your personal name, address, and contact information.
* **Contribution Details (Contribution Details)**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any contribution details you'd like to share.
* **Payment Details (Payment Details)**: This field is also optional and is used to record payment information if you are making a contribution.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid any issues with the gift-giving process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
