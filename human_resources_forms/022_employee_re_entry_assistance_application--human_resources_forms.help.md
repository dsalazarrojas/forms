# Employee Re Entry Assistance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist employees in understanding how to fill out the Employee Re Entry Assistance Application form. The form is a crucial tool for HR staff to gather essential information for employee re-entry processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Fill out each field as described in the field-by-field explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department where the employee will be re-entered. This field helps ensure that the employee is re-assigned to the correct team. Please choose from the available options:
	+ Accounting
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales

(Note: This field is required, so please choose one of the provided options.)

* **Reason** (`Reason`, `text`, optional): Describe the reason for the employee's re-entry. This could be for a promotion, transfer, or other work-related purposes.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's contact information, such as phone number or email address.
* **Manager** (`Manager`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's manager's name and title.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like the employee to be re-entered.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you would like the employee to be re-entered.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `note`, optional): This is the final step. Once all required fields are completed, click "Submit" to submit your application.
