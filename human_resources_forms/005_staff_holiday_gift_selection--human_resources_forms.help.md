# Staff Holiday Gift Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Holiday Gift Selection form is used by HR teams to collect information about employee preferences for holiday gifts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each field carefully and answer the questions according to your preferences.
- Select the options that best match your preferences for each field.
- Make sure to provide all required information to ensure accurate processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Preferences (Text)** (`employee_preferences`, text, optional)
    This field is an open-ended text field where you can express any holiday preferences you have.
* **Delivery Method (Select One)** (`delivery_method`, select one, optional)
    Select the method of delivery for your holiday gift. Options include:
        + In-person delivery
        + Mail delivery
        + Both options
        + No delivery
* **Gift Confirmation (Select One)** (`gift_confirmation`, select one, optional)
    Confirm your holiday gift choice. Options include:
        + True
        + False
* **Type of Gift (Select One)** (`gift_type`, select one, optional)
    Select the type of gift you would like to receive. Options include:
        + Gift Card
        + Physical Gift
        + Both options
        + No gift
* **Gift Details (Text)** (`gift_details`, text, optional)
    If you have a specific gift in mind, describe it here.
* **Delivery Information (Text)** (`delivery_info`, text, optional)
    Provide any additional information about your delivery preferences, such as a preferred delivery address.
* **Contact Info (Text)** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
    Provide your contact information so that HR can reach out to you if needed.
* **Gift Delivery Info (Text)** (`gift_delivery_info`, text, optional)
    If you have any specific delivery instructions or requirements, provide them here.
* **Employee Confirmation (Select Multiple)** (`employee_confirmation`, select multiple, optional)
    Confirm your status regarding receipt of your gift. Options include:
        + I confirm I will receive my gift
        + Not yet
        + Unable to confirm

# Tips
- Complete the form carefully and truthfully to ensure accurate information is collected.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR team.
- Make sure to review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
