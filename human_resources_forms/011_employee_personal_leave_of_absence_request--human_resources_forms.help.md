# Employee Personal Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a personal leave of absence for an employee. This form is used to gather information about the employee's leave dates, reason, and supervisor comments. It will be reviewed by designated managers for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Employee Name, Job Title, and Department.
2. Choose the start and end dates for the leave of absence.
3. Select the reason for the leave in the text field.
4. If needed, provide supervisor comments.
5. Select the managers who should approve the leave of absence.
6. Click the submit button to send the request to the designated managers for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date from which the leave of absence will start.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of day when the leave of absence will start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date on which the leave of absence will end.
* **Reason** (`reason`, Text, Optional): Provide the reason for the leave of absence.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter the job title of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Enter the department of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any comments that the supervisor wishes to convey to the manager.
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the managers who should approve the leave of absence.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, Select One, Optional): Select the manager who approved the leave of absence.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you need to provide additional comments or information, use the supervisor comments field.
* If you are unsure about which managers should approve the leave of absence, consult with HR or your supervisor.
