# Bereavement Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request bereavement leave for personal or family member's death or significant loss.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please enter your full name as per your official documents.
2. Enter a valid email address that can be used for contact purposes.
3. If you are outside the local region, please include your country code when entering your phone number.
4. Select the relevant date for your bereavement leave.
5. Choose the option that best applies to your situation in the primary selection field.
6. If required, provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
7. Enter any additional relevant information in the additional details field.
8. Select all that apply for the agreement field.
9. If necessary, enter an estimated value in decimal format.
10. Enter a preferred time for your scheduled leave if required.
11. Provide any final comments or notes in the closing remarks field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Enter your official full name as per your identification documents.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, optional): If outside the local region, please include your country code.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the relevant date for your bereavement leave.
* **Primary selection** (Primary selection, select_one, required): Choose the option that best applies to your situation.
* **Quantity or rating** (Quantity or rating, number, optional): If required, provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (Additional details, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information.
* **Agreement** (Agreement, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply.
* **Estimated value** (Estimated value, number, optional): If necessary, enter an estimated value in decimal format.
* **Preferred time** (Preferred time, time, optional): Enter a preferred time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (Final comments or notes, text, optional): Provide any final comments or notes.
