# Executive Team Hiring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps the hiring team gather information about the ideal candidate profile for executive team positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the company size that best describes the ideal team size.
2. Choose the job title(s) that match the position you're looking to fill.
3. Select the job level of the position you're looking to fill.
4. Enter the number of years of experience you're looking for in the candidate.
5. Select the educational level of the candidate.
6. Indicate whether the job title is preferred or not.
7. Choose the department(s) where the position will be located.
8. Select the location(s) where the position will be located.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Size** (`Small`, `Medium`, `Large`): Choose the size of the company you're looking to hire for.
* **Job Title** (`CEO`, `Manager`, `Director`, `Officer`): Select all job titles that match the position you're looking to fill.
* **Job Level** (`Executive`, `Manager`, `Director`): Choose the level of the job you're looking to fill.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience you're looking for in the candidate.
* **Education** (`High School`, `Bachelor`, `Master`, `Doctorate`): Select all educational levels that match the candidate's qualifications.
* **Job Title Preferred** (`Yes`, `No`): Indicate whether the job title is a preference or not.
* **Department** (`Sales`, `Marketing`, `Finance`, `Operations`): Select all departments where the position will be located.
* **Location** (`New York`, `Los Angeles`, `Chicago`, `San Francisco`): Select all locations where the position will be located.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out this form to help the hiring team find the best fit for the position.
- Make sure to select all relevant job titles, departments, and locations to increase the chances of finding a suitable candidate.
