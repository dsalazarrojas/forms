# Quality Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Manager Application Form is used to collect information from candidates applying to be a Quality Manager. This form helps us evaluate their suitability for the role and ensures that we receive the necessary details for the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and contact information in the relevant fields.
2.  Select your employment status, available to move, and highest education level.
3.  Describe your work experience, previous work, and any relevant skills you possess.
4.  Indicate your proficiency in languages and certifications.
5.  Confirm your current work status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Job title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title.
* **Employment status** (Text, Optional): Select your current employment status.
* **Available to move** (Text, Optional): Indicate if you are available to move for work.
* **Highest education** (Text, Optional): Describe your highest level of education.
* **Work experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your work experience.
* **Previous work** (Text, Optional): Describe your previous work experience.
* **Skills** (Select multiple, Required): Select the skills you possess.
* **Languages** (Select multiple, Optional): Indicate your proficiency in languages.
* **Certifications** (Select multiple, Optional): Indicate if you have any relevant certifications.
* **Work status** (Text, Optional): Confirm your current work status.

**Tips**

* Ensure you fill in all required fields to avoid delays in the hiring process.
* Be honest and accurate when providing your information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
