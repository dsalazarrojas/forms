# Missed Punch Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Missed Punch Form is used to record and document instances when a worker has missed a scheduled punch, either due to being away from their station, personal reasons, or technical issues. This form helps to provide accurate records and ensure compliance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form with the correct details.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, Number, Required): This field is used to identify the worker associated with the missed punch.
* **date** (`date`, Date, Required): The date on which the punch was missed.
* **punch_time** (`punch_time`, Time, Required): The scheduled punch time that was missed.
* **missed_hours** (`missed_hours`, Number, Required): The number of hours missed during the punched time.
* **reason** (`reason`, Text, Required): A brief explanation of why the punch was missed.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about the purpose or requirements of this form, refer to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Ensure to provide accurate and detailed information when filling out the form.
- If you've already reported the missed punch, do not submit this form again.
