# Performance Review Meeting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Review Meeting Request Form is used by managers to request a performance review meeting with their team members. This form helps to clarify the meeting's purpose, date, time, location, and duration, ensuring both parties are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not you would like to request a performance review meeting.
2. If you selected "Yes", choose the desired date for the meeting.
3. Choose the desired time for the meeting (AM/PM format).
4. Select the topic(s) you would like to cover during the meeting.
5. Choose the team member(s) you would like to have attend the meeting.
6. Select the location for the meeting (Remote, Office, On-site).
7. Enter the meeting duration in minutes.
8. Choose "Submit" to send the request to the team member or "Cancel" to discard the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Review Meeting
* **Request Review Meeting** (`review_request`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether or not you would like to request a performance review meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the desired date for the meeting in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required): Choose the desired time for the meeting in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Review Topic** (`review_topic`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more topics you would like to review during the meeting.
* **Reviewers** (`reviewers`, select_multiple, required): Choose the team member(s) you would like to have attend the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, select_one, required): Select the location for the meeting (Remote, Office, On-site).
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, required): Enter the meeting duration in minutes.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, select_one, required): Choose "Submit" to send the request to the team member or "Cancel" to discard the request.
