# Performance Evaluation Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Evaluation Slot Booking Form is a tool used by managers or reviewers to assess the performance of employees in their assigned slots. This form is designed to gather relevant information to facilitate fair and structured performance evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and access rights to complete this form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
3. Complete all sections as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`Employee Name`, `Title/Position`, `Employee ID`): This section requires your employee's name, job title, and employee ID. Please enter this information accurately to ensure the form is associated with the correct person.
* **Slot Details** (`Slot Number`, `Slot Description`, `Slot Duration`): Here, you will provide details about the employee's performance slot, including the slot number, a brief description, and duration.
* **Reviewer Information** (`Reviewer Name`, `Reviewer Title/Position`, `Reviewer ID`): Enter your own name, job title, and reviewer ID to ensure the form is attributed to you.
* **Review Details** (`Review Date`, `Review Time`, `Slot Status`): Provide the date and time of the review, as well as the current status of the slot (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
* **Reviewer Comments** (`Comments`, `Rating`, `Recommendation`): Leave feedback and ratings for the employee's performance, and include any recommendation for improvement.
* **Form Actions** (`Actions`, `Submit`, `Reset`): These options allow you to either submit or reset your filled-out form. Please note that the form does not require a 'Reset' option, as the form does not automatically reset, but rather, this field is for internal documentation and development purposes only.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when providing feedback and ratings.
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
