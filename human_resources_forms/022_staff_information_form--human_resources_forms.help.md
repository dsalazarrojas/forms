# Staff Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store staff information, including contact details and employment status. It helps HR personnel to maintain accurate records and track staff data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a general description of the staff information form.
2. Fill out each field with the relevant details:
	* Enter your staff title.
	* Provide your contact information, including your home, work, or other phone numbers (if applicable).
	* Enter your email address.
3. Select your employment status (active, inactive, on leave, or terminated).
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **staff_information_form**: This field is a text area where you can enter a brief description of your role or position within the organization.
* **contact_information**: This field is a text area where you can enter your contact details, such as your name and address (if your work address is different from your home address, you can select "work" or "other" as the contact address).
* **job_title**: Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **employment_status**: Select your current employment status (active, inactive, on leave, or terminated) to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* **contact_address**: Select the type of address you wish to provide (home, work, or other) to help HR personnel differentiate between your contact addresses.
* **work_phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **other_phone**: Enter any other phone number you wish to provide (e.g., cell phone number).
* **email_address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **other_phone**: Enter any other phone number you wish to provide (e.g., personal phone number).
* **email_address**: Enter your email address.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all fields accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
