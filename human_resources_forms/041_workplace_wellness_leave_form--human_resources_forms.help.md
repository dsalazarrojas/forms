### Workplace Wellness Leave Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The "Workplace Wellness Leave Form" is used by employees to request and record their own leave absences. This form helps us track employee availability and better understand their work hours.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee ID to access your leave history.
2.  Select the date you would like to leave work (e.g., 2024-02-27).
3.  Choose a date for your return to work.
4.  Describe the reason for your leave.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Required): Enter your unique employee ID to access your leave history.
*   **Date Of Leaving** (`date_of_leaving`, Date, Optional): Select the date you would like to leave work.
*   **Returning Date** (`returning_date`, Date, Optional): Choose a date for your return to work.
*   **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, Text, Optional): Describe the reason for your leave. This will help us better understand your absence.

### Tips

*   Please ensure that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Make sure to submit your form before your leave dates.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
