# Security Awareness Training Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track employee completion of security awareness training.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your company email in the Email field.
4. Select your department from the Department field, if applicable.
5. Answer whether you have completed the security awareness training by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Training Completed field.
6. Enter the date you completed the training in the Date Completed field.
7. Confirm the date you completed the training in the Confirm Date field.
8. (Optional) Enter any comments about the training in the Comments field.
9. (Optional) Provide feedback about the training in the Training Feedback field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Select your department from the list, if applicable.
* **Completed** (`completed`, select_one, required): Select whether you have completed the security awareness training (Yes/No).
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the training.
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, date, required): Confirm the date you completed the training.
* **Training Completed** (`training_completed`, select_one, required): Select whether you have completed the security awareness training (Yes/No).
* **Date Training Completed** (`date_training_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the training, if not the same as Date Completed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the training.
* **Training Feedback** (`training_feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback about the training.
