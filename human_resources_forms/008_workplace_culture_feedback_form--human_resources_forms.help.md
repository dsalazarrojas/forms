# Workplace Culture Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Workplace Culture Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their perception of the current workplace culture. This feedback will be used to help the organization identify areas that need improvement and make informed decisions to create a more positive and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your true feelings and experiences.
2. You may select only one option for multiple-choice questions (e.g., "Engagement level").
3. You may select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections (e.g., "Work life balance").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, Number, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job.
* **Engagement level** (`engagement_level`, Select One, Optional): How do you feel about your job? Select one of the following options:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, Number, Optional): Rate your job satisfaction. (Note: This field will not be used in this guide as it's currently empty and doesn't seem to have any options.)
* **Work life balance** (`work_life_balance`, Select Multiple, Required): Rate your work-life balance. Select all that apply:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Communication channels** (`communication_channels`, Text, Optional): How do you typically communicate with your team and manager? Please provide a brief description.
* **Feedback frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): How often do you receive feedback? Select all that apply:
	+ Often
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, Text, Optional): What would you suggest to improve our workplace culture? Please provide a brief description.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please feel free to share them.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide your genuine feedback to help us improve the workplace culture.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask your manager or HR representative for guidance.
* Your feedback will be treated confidentially and used to make positive changes to our workplace culture.
