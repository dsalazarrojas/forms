# Employee Productivity Innovation Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for an Employee Productivity Innovation Grant. The grant is designed to support employees in their projects and initiatives that promote innovation and productivity within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the project details, including the project start and end dates, expected outcomes, and funding needs.
2. Enter the grant amount you are requesting.
3. Provide information about yourself as the applicant, including your name, job title, department, and contact email and phone number.
4. Check if you are willing to agree to the submission terms.
5. Select the project status and type.
6. Enter the project budget.
7. Fill out any additional information about the project, such as notes and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to provide information about the project, including its start and end dates.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can describe the expected outcomes of your project.
* **Funding Needs** (`3`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total funding needed for your project.
* **Grant Amount** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter the grant amount you are requesting.
* **Applicant Info** (`5`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will provide information about yourself as the applicant.
* **Contact Info** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact email and phone number.
* **Submission Terms** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if you agree to submit your application electronically.
* **Project Start Date** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`9`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Status** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your project.
* **Project Type** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`12`, `number`, required: false): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Contact Email** (`13`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Project Notes** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your project.
* **Applicant Name** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Project Location** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of your project.

Note: Some fields are not required, so you can fill them out or leave them blank depending on your project requirements.
