# Performance Management Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Management Reflection Form is a tool for employees to reflect on their performance and progress. It helps employees and their managers identify strengths, areas for improvement, and next steps for growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your employee information in the "Employee Information" section.
2. In the "Review Information" section, describe your current performance and progress towards your goals.
3. In the "Reflection" section, describe your thoughts on your performance and progress, and identify areas where you could improve.
4. In the "Next Steps" section, outline your plans for improvement and growth.
5. Finally, in the "Review Date" section, suggest a date for when you would like to have your performance reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Information
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, required/optional): This section is for you to provide your basic information, such as your name, job title, and department.

### Review Information
* **Review Information** (`review_info`, text, required/optional): In this section, please describe your current performance and progress towards your goals. Be as specific as possible, and include any accomplishments or successes you've had.

### Reflection
* **Reflection** (`reflection`, text, required/optional): In this section, think about your strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement. What did you do well, and what could you do better? Be honest and self-reflective.

### Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required/optional): Outline your plans for improvement and growth. What specific actions will you take, and when? Be as concrete as possible.

### Review Date
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, text, required/optional): Suggest a date for when you would like to have your performance reviewed. This will help your manager to schedule a review at a convenient time for both of you.

## Tips
* Be honest and self-reflective in your responses.
* Be specific and concrete in your answers.
* Use this form as a tool for growth and development, not just to complete a task.
