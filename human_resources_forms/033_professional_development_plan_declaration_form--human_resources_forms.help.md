# Professional Development Plan Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Development Plan Declaration Form is used to declare and document an employee's professional development goals and progress. This form is intended to support career growth and development within the company. To ensure that you are filling out the correct form, please note that this form is specific to your department and job title.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct department from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter your name, job title, and list your short-term and long-term goals.
3. Briefly explain your objectives and progress toward achieving your goals.
4. Complete all required fields and review the form for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Department** (select_one, optional): Select your department from the drop-down menu.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Goals** (text, optional): List your short-term and long-term goals.
* **Objective** (text, optional): Briefly explain your objectives.
* **Progress** (text, optional): Describe your progress toward your goals.

## Tips
* Make sure to be specific and concrete when listing your goals and objectives.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are complete and accurate.
