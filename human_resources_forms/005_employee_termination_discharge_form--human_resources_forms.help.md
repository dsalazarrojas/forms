# employee_termination_discharge_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the termination or separation of an employee from the company. It captures the necessary information to understand the reason for the termination, the notice period, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee Name in the corresponding field.
2. Select the reason for separation or termination from the options provided or describe it in the text field.
3. Enter the Date of Separation or Termination.
4. If the reason for termination is not listed, provide a detailed description of the reason in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee being terminated or separated.
* **reason_for_separation** (`Reason for Separation`, text, required: false): Select the reason for the employee's separation or termination from the options provided or describe it in the text field.
* **Date Of Separation** (`Date Of Separation`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the employee's separation or termination took effect.
* **Reason For Termination** (`Reason For Termination`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more reasons for termination from the options provided.
	+ **Termination for Cause**: The employee was terminated due to a reason specified in the company's policies or laws.
	+ **Termination by Employee**: The employee chose to leave the company or resigned.
	+ **Termination by Management**: The employee's position or role was eliminated or reorganized.
* **Reason For Termination Other** (`Reason For Termination Other`, text, required: false): If the reason for termination is not listed, provide a detailed description of the reason in this text field.
* **Reason For Termination Other Details** (`Reason For Termination Other Details`, text, required: false): Enter additional details about the reason for termination if necessary.
* **Notice Period End Date** (`Notice Period End Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the notice period ends, if applicable.
