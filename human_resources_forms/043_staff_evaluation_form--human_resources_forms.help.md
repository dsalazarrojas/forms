# Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the performance of staff members and provide feedback on their strengths and areas for improvement. This form is typically used by supervisors to assess an employee's performance during a specific evaluation period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each staff member you need to evaluate.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting the evaluation.
3. Be thorough and honest when providing feedback and ratings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **staff_evaluation_form** (`staff_evaluation_form`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the evaluation.
* **staff_name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member being evaluated.
* **evaluation_date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **evaluation_period** (`evaluation_period`, text, optional): Enter the evaluation period (e.g., "Q1 2022").
* **key_areas** (`key_areas`, text, optional): List the key areas that the staff member was evaluated on.
* **overall_rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate the staff member's overall performance (e.g., 1-5).
* **strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional): List the staff member's strengths and areas of improvement.
* **areas_for_improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): List specific areas where the staff member needs improvement.
* **recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide recommendations for the staff member's growth and development.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Additional feedback for the staff member.
* **supervisor_name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor completing the evaluation.
* **supervisor_email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the supervisor completing the evaluation.
* **supervisor_phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the supervisor completing the evaluation.
* **evaluation_completed** (`evaluation_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **evaluation_ending_date** (`evaluation_ending_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
* **evaluation_start_date** (`evaluation_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
* **evaluation_start_time** (`evaluation_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the evaluation period.
* **evaluation_end_time** (`evaluation_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the evaluation period.
* **evaluation_duration** (`evaluation_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the evaluation period.
* **evaluation_status** (`evaluation_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the evaluation (e.g., "incomplete", "complete").
* **form_submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the form was submitted (e.g., "True", "False").

Note: Some fields may not be required, but providing accurate and complete information is essential for a thorough evaluation. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting the evaluation.
