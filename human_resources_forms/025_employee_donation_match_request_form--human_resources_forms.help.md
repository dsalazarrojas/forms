# Employee Donation Match Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a donation match for an employee's charitable contribution. The form is intended to be completed by employees who want to donate to a charitable organization and request that the company matches their donation. This form will be reviewed by HR to determine if the donation match is eligible for company matching funds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company information, including your name and job title.
2. Enter your name as an employee making the donation request.
3. Describe the charitable organization and the purpose of your donation.
4. Enter the amount you are donating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as the employee making the donation request.
* **Donation Match Request** (`donation_match_request`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the charitable organization and the purpose of your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount you are donating. Please include cents in your response if necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to include all required information to ensure your donation match request is reviewed promptly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact HR for assistance.
* Note that not all donations may be eligible for matching funds, and HR will review each request on a case-by-case basis.
