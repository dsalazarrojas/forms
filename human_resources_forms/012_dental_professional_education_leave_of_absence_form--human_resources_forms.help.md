# Dental Professional Education Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Professional Education Leave Of Absence Form is used to request a leave of absence for educational purposes, such as pursuing further studies or attending conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all the mandatory fields marked as required ( Education Start Date, Education End Date, Reason, Approval Manager, Approval Status, Approval Date, and Approved By).
2. Select the appropriate options for Education Start Date, Education End Date, Approval Status, and Approved By.
3. Enter the reason for your leave in the Reason field.
4. Provide the URL of your education institution in the Education Institution URL field if required.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for Approval Manager and Approved By fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Leave Form** (page_one, text, not required): This field is a text area where you can enter any additional information about your leave of absence.
* **Education Start Date** (education_start_date, date, required): Enter the start date of your educational leave.
* **Education End Date** (education_end_date, date, required): Enter the end date of your educational leave.
* **Reason** (reason, text, required): Enter the reason for your educational leave.
* **Approval Manager** (approval_manager, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if your leave has been approved, "No" otherwise.
* **Education Institution** (education_institution, text, not required): Enter the name of your education institution.
* **Education Institution URL** (education_institution_url, text, not required): Enter the URL of your education institution.
* **Approval Status** (approval_status, select_one, required): Select "Active" if your leave has been approved, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Comments** (comments, text, not required): Enter any additional comments about your leave.
* **Approval Date** (approval_date, date, required): Enter the date of approval for your leave.
* **Approved By** (approved_by, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you have been approved, "No" otherwise.
* **Denial Reason** (denial_reason, text, not required): Enter the reason for denial if your leave is denied.
* **Denied By** (denied_by, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who denied your leave.
