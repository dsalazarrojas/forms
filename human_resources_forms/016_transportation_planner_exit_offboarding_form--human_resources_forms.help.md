# Transportation Planner Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Planner Exit Offboarding Form is designed to gather feedback and insights from departing transportation planners to improve the planning process and provide a smooth transition for the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your manager or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What Is Your Reason?
* **What is your reason?** (`what_is_your_reason`, Text, Required): This field is where you can provide a brief explanation for your exit or offboarding from the organization. Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your reasons.

### 2. What Can Improve Planning?
* **What can improve planning?** (`what_can_improve_planning`, Text, Required): This field is where you can suggest improvements or ideas to enhance the planning process. Your input will help us develop a better planning process for future transportation projects.

### 3. Comments and Suggestions
* **Comments and Suggestions** (`comments_and_suggestions`, Text, Required): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions to help us improve the planning process.

### 4. Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, Text, Required): This field is where you can outline the next steps you would like the organization to take or consider when you are offboarding. Your input will help ensure a smooth transition.
