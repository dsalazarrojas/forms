# Disaster Response Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disaster Response Leave Form is used to report a request for leave due to an emergency or other unforeseen circumstances that requires immediate attention. This form helps to provide accurate and timely information to the relevant authorities and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of emergency that applies to your situation.
2.  Enter the date you are requesting leave for.
3.  Choose the time you need to take off from work.
4.  Specify the number of days you will be away from work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Emergency Type**: Select the type of emergency that applies to your situation. Options include:
    *   Disaster: This applies to emergencies such as natural disasters, accidents, or unexpected events that require immediate attention.
    *   Weather: This applies to emergencies caused by severe weather conditions, such as storms or hurricanes.
    *   Medical: This applies to emergencies related to health issues or medical conditions that require attention.
    *   Family: This applies to emergencies involving family members or loved ones.
    *   Other: Select this option if your emergency does not fit into any of the above categories.
*   **Request Date**: Enter the date when you are planning to take leave. Please use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Request Time**: Choose the time you need to take off from work. Select the time slot that best suits your needs.
*   **Duration**: Specify the number of days you will be away from work. Please note that this will be used to calculate your leave period.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
*   This form is for reporting emergencies only; please use it for other types of leave or absence requests.
