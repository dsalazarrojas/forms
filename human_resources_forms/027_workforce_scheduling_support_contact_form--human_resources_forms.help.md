# Workforce Scheduling Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather general information from users who need support with workforce scheduling. It helps the support team to understand the user's concerns and provide timely assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. You can skip optional fields if you don't have any additional information to provide.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your concern or question regarding workforce scheduling.
* **Schedule Inquiry** (`schedule_inquiry`, text, optional): If you have a specific schedule inquiry, please provide more details here.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, note, optional): If you have any additional context or information that would help us better understand your concern, please provide it here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to submit your request:
	+ **Submit**: This will send your request to the support team for review.
	+ **Save and Continue Editing**: This will save your progress, but you can still make changes before resubmitting.
	+ **Save as Draft**: This will save your request, but it will not be reviewed until you resubmit it.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in the "General Information" field.
* If you have multiple concerns, you can submit multiple forms.
* The support team will contact you within 24 hours to discuss your request.
