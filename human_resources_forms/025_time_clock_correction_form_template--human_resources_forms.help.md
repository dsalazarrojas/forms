# Time Clock Correction Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to correct time clock entries for employees. It is used to identify and correct errors or discrepancies in employee time entries, and to ensure that all corrections are properly documented and approved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the start and end times of the shift that needs to be corrected in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
3. Enter a brief explanation for the correction in the "Reason for correction" field.
4. Select the date of the correction in the "Correction Date" field.
5. Enter the amount of time to be added or subtracted from the time clock entry in the "Correction Amount" field.
6. Enter your manager's name and signature in the "Manager Name" and "Manager Signature" fields, respectively.
7. Enter your own signature in the "Employee Signature" field.
8. Enter the date that the form was submitted in the "Date Submitted" field.
9. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
10. If applicable, enter the name of the person who approved the form in the "Approved By" field.
11. If applicable, enter the date the form was approved in the "Approved Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name_snippet`, `text`, required): Enter your employee ID or name as it appears on your time clock card.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `number`, required): Enter the start time of the shift that needs to be corrected.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `number`, required): Enter the end time of the shift that needs to be corrected.
* **Reason for correction** (`reason_for_correction`, `text`, required): Enter a brief explanation for the correction.
* **Correction Date** (`correction_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the correction.
* **Correction Amount** (`correction_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of time to be added or subtracted from the time clock entry.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `note`, required): Enter your manager's signature to approve the correction.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your manager's name, but it is not required.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your signature to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the correction.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required): Enter the date that the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `text`, not required): If the form was approved, enter the name of the person who approved it.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, `date`, not required): If the form was approved, enter the date that it was approved.

## Tips

* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you are having trouble with your time clock entry, contact your HR or payroll representative for assistance.
