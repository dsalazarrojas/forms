# Employee Handover Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Handover Form" is a document used to facilitate the transfer of an employee from one department or team to another.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
2.  Make sure to fill in the correct information in each field as prompted.
3.  Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name (`Employee Name`)**: Enter the name of the employee being transferred.
*   **Date (`Date`)**: Enter the date of the transfer.
*   **Time (`Time`)**: Enter the time of the transfer.
*   **Department (`Department`)**: Enter the department of the employee being transferred.
*   **Asset Description (`Asset Description`)**: Enter a brief description of any assets being transferred.
*   **Asset Transfer Details**: Enter any relevant information about the assets being transferred.
*   **Transfer Confirmation (`Employee Transfer Confirmation`)**: Select whether the transfer is completed.
*   **Team Confirmation (`Team Confirmation`)**: Select whether the team has confirmed the transfer.
*   **Manager Confirmation (`Manager Confirmation`)**: Select whether the manager has confirmed the transfer.
*   **Manager Signature (`Manager Signature`)**: Enter the manager's signature.
*   **Employee Signature (`Employee Signature`)**: Enter the employee's signature.
