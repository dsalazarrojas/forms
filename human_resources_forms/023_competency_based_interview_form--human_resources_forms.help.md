# Competency Based Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Competency Based Interview Form is designed to assess a candidate's competencies through a structured interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name_int`, `text`, required): Fill in the candidate's full name.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name_int`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the evaluation.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_interviewed_for`, `text`, required): Specify the position the candidate is being assessed against.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_completion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the interview took place.
* **Describe the candidate's example for Adaptability** (`adaptability_example`, `text`, required): Use the STAR method to describe a situation where the candidate demonstrated adaptability.
* **Adaptability Rating** (`adaptability_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the candidate's adaptability based on the provided example (1-5).
* **Describe the candidate's example for Leadership** (`leadership_example`, `text`, required): Use the STAR method to describe a situation where the candidate demonstrated leadership.
* **Leadership Rating** (`leadership_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the candidate's leadership based on the provided example (1-5).
* **Overall Hiring Recommendation** (`hire_recommendation_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose a hiring status (Strong Hire, Hire, Hire with Reservations, Do Not Hire).
* **Comments on Cultural Alignment** (`cultural_fit_comments`, `text`, required): Provide comments on the candidate's cultural fit with the organization.
* **Adaptability** (`competency_adaptability_section`, `note`, required): This section assesses the candidate's ability to adjust to changing environments and tasks.
* **Leadership / Influence** (`competency_leadership_section`, `note`, required): This section evaluates the candidate's ability to influence positive outcomes.
