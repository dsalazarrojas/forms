# Corrective Training Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge receipt of corrective training provided to employees in the organization. It ensures that the training has been received and understood by the trainee, and that the training facilitator has completed the required training hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the form is only completed once after each training session.
2. Enter the assigned trained date in the "Assigned Trained Date" field. This date should be the date when the corrective training was provided.
3. Enter the name of the training facilitator who provided the corrective training in the "Training Facilitator" field.
4. Enter the name of the trainee who received the corrective training in the "Trainee Name" field.
5. Enter the total number of training hours required in the "Training Hours" field.
6. Enter the number of training hours completed in the "Training Hours Completed" field.
7. Select the status of the training in the "Training Status" field. Options include "Not Completed", "Completed", and "Partially Completed".
8. Enter any comments regarding the training status in the "Training Status Comments" field.
9. If necessary, enter the follow-up date in the "Follow-up Date" field.
10. If necessary, enter the next follow-up date in the "Next Follow-up Date" field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the training session in the "Notes" field.
12. Enter the name of the person who assigned the corrective training in the "Assigned Trained By" field.
13. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form in the "Reviewer Name" field.
14. Enter the title of the person who reviewed the form in the "Reviewer Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Corrective Training Acknowledgment Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Assigned Trained Date**: The date when the corrective training was provided.
*	**Training Facilitator**: The name of the person who provided the corrective training.
*	**Trainee Name**: The name of the trainee who received the corrective training.
*	**Training Hours**: The total number of training hours required.
*	**Training Hours Completed**: The number of training hours completed.
*	**Training Status**: Select "Not Completed", "Completed", or "Partially Completed" to indicate the status of the training.
*	**Training Status Comments**: Any comments regarding the training status.
*	**Follow-up Date**: The date for follow-up on the corrective training.
*	**Next Follow-up Date**: The date for next follow-up on the corrective training.
*	**Notes**: Any additional notes about the training session.
*	**Assigned Trained By**: The name of the person who assigned the corrective training.
*	**Reviewer**: The name of the person who reviewed the form.
*	**Reviewer Name**: The name of the person who reviewed the form.
*	**Reviewer Title**: The title of the person who reviewed the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the form is only completed once after each training session.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the HR department for assistance.
- This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with external parties.
