# Team Member Payment Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to update information related to team member payments or status changes. Please use it to accurately and efficiently modify the relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Team Member** field with the name or ID of the team member you are updating.
2. In the **Pay Change** field, enter any changes to the team member's payment details.
3. Select a date in the **Effective Date** field for when the change will take effect.
4. Choose the relevant status of the team member in the **Team Member Status** field (Active, Inactive, or On Leave).
5. Select the payment method for the team member in the **Pay Method** field.
6. In the **Reason** field, briefly explain the reason for the change.
7. Choose the manager's name who will review this form in the **Manager Review** field.
8. In the **Manager Comments** field, add any additional comments or notes from the reviewing manager.
9. In the **Manager Signature** field, have the reviewing manager sign off on the changes.
10. Finally, in the **Team Member Signature** field, have the team member whose data is being updated sign off on the changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member**: Enter the name or ID of the team member you are updating.
* **Pay Change**: Describe any changes to the team member's payment details, such as rate increases or decreases.
* **Effective Date**: Choose a date when the change will take effect.
* **Reason**: Briefly explain the reason for the change.
* **Team Member Status**: Select the status of the team member (Active, Inactive, or On Leave).
* **Pay Method**: Select the payment method used for the team member (Bank Draft, Bank Transfer, Payroll, or Other).
* **Manager Review**: Choose the manager's name who will review this form.
* **Manager Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes from the reviewing manager.
* **Manager Signature**: Have the reviewing manager sign off on the changes.
* **Team Member Signature**: Have the team member whose data is being updated sign off on the changes.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately and clearly fill in all required fields.
* If the form requires manager approval, be prepared to address any comments or concerns from them.
* Ensure all fields are signed off on the changes by both the team member and the manager.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
