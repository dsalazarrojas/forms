# easy_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "easy_job_application_form" is a tool for candidates to provide their application details for a specific job position. It helps our hiring team to efficiently review and evaluate candidates for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact details (fields 1-3).
2. Select your employment status (field 4) from the options provided.
3. Provide your available start date (field 7).
4. Enter your street address and city (fields 8-9).
5. Enter your zip code (field 11).
6. Enter a contact number (field 12).
7. Upload any required documents (fields 13-15) if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (2, text, required): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Desired Position** (4, text, required): Enter the job position you are applying for.
* **Employment Status** (5, select multiple, required): Select one or more of the provided options to indicate your current employment status (Active, Inactive, Looking).
* **Available Start Date** (7, date, required): Enter your preferred start date for the new position.
* **Street** (8, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (9, text, required): Enter the city of your address.
* **Zip** (11, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Contact Number** (12, text, required): Enter the phone number of a contact person or your emergency contact.
* **Document 1** (13, text, required): Upload any required documents as specified by the job posting.
* **Document 2** (14, text, required): Upload any additional required documents as specified by the job posting.
* **Document 3** (15, text, required): Upload any further required documents as specified by the job posting.
