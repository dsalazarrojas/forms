# Employee Loan Application Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Loan Application Request Form is used by employees to request a loan from their company. This form is used to gather necessary information for the loan request, which is then reviewed and processed by the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the desired loan term from the "Loan Term" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the loan amount in the "Loan Amount" field.
4. Select the loan start and end dates from the calendar pop-up calendars.
5. Enter any additional comments about the loan in the "Loan Comments" field.
6. Finally, submit the form to request the loan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee Loan Application Request**: Enter a brief description of the loan request (this field is not actually displayed on the form, so we're just explaining its purpose).
* **Manager Approval**: Select "Approve" to indicate that your manager has reviewed and approved the loan, or "Deny" if the loan is not approved.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of money you are requesting as a loan.
* **Loan Term**: Select the duration of the loan from the dropdown menu.
* **Loan Rate**: Enter the interest rate for the loan.
* **Loan Start Date**: Select the date when the loan is expected to start.
* **Loan End Date**: Select the date when the loan is expected to end.
* **Loan Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the loan, such as its purpose or any conditions.
* **Form Submission Date**: This field is automatically filled in and cannot be edited by the user. It represents the date when the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the loan request, please contact your manager or HR department.
* This form is for internal use only, and all information entered will be reviewed by the relevant authorities.
