# Newborn Care Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newborn Care Leave Of Absence Request form is used to request a leave of absence for new parental care duties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the Employee Request field with a brief description of your reason for requesting the leave.
2. Enter the Employee Request Date field with the start date of your desired leave.
3. Select your Manager Approval for your request in the Manager Approval field.
4. Enter the Manager Approval Date field with the date your manager approved or denied your request.
5. Select your People Operations Approval for your request in the People Operations Approval field.
6. Enter the People Operations Approval Date field with the date your People Operations team approved or denied your request.
7. Enter any Manager Comments regarding your request in the Manager Comments field (optional).
8. Enter any Employees Comments regarding your request in the Employees Comments field (optional).
9. Select the Approval Status field with the current status of your request.
10. Enter the Approval Status Date field with the date your request was approved or denied.
11. Enter a Reason for Rejection if your request was denied in the Reason for Rejection field (optional).
12. Enter the Reason for Rejection Date field with the date your request was denied (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Request** (`employee_request`, text, required): This is the brief description of your reason for requesting the leave.
* **Employee Request Date** (`employee_request_date_start`, date, required): This is the start date of your desired leave.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' to approve, 'No' to deny your request.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date your manager approved or denied your request.
* **People Operations Approval** (`people_operations_approval`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' to approve, 'No' to deny your request.
* **People Operations Approval Date** (`people_operations_approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date your People Operations team approved or denied your request.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments your manager may have regarding your request.
* **Employees Comments** (`employees_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments you may have regarding your request.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Approval Status Date** (`approval_status_date`, date, required): Enter the date your request was approved or denied.
* **Reason for Rejection** (`reason_for_rejection`, text, optional): Enter the reason why your request was denied.
* **Reason for Rejection Date** (`reason_for_rejection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your request was denied.
