# Data Entry Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Entry Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate the proficiency of data entry operators in various aspects of data entry, including accuracy, typing speed, and completed entries within a specified time frame.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the First Name field. This field is used to identify you.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the Last Name field. This field is also used for identification purposes.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the Email field. This field is used for communication and record-keeping.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the Phone field. This field is for contact purposes.
5. Enter your **Typing Speed** in the Typing Speed field. This field is used to measure how fast you can type.
6. Enter your **Accuracy** in the Accuracy field. This is a measure of how precise you are with your typing.
7. Enter the number of **Completed Entries** you've made within a specified time frame in the Completed Entries field. This field is used to measure your productivity.
8. Enter your **Average WPM (Words Per Minute)** in the Average WPM field. This field is used to calculate your average typing speed.
9. Enter the number of **Errors** you've made within a specified time frame in the Errors field. This field is used to measure your accuracy.
10. Enter your **Accuracy Percentage** in the Accuracy Percentage field. This is a measure of how precise you are with your typing, expressed as a percentage.
11. Enter the duration of time it took you to complete the tasks in the **Time** field. This field is used to measure your efficiency.
12. Enter the number of **Completed 10** entries you've made within 10 minutes in the Completed 10 field. This field is used to measure your productivity within the first 10 minutes.
13. Enter the number of **Completed 30** entries you've made within 30 minutes in the Completed 30 field. This field is used to measure your productivity within the first 30 minutes.
14. Enter the number of **Errors 10** you've made within 10 minutes in the Errors 10 field. This field is used to measure your accuracy within the first 10 minutes.
15. Enter the number of **Errors 30** you've made within 30 minutes in the Errors 30 field. This field is used to measure your accuracy within the first 30 minutes.
16. Enter the number of **Completed 60** entries you've made within 60 minutes in the Completed 60 field. This field is used to measure your productivity within 60 minutes.
17. Enter the number of **Errors 60** you've made within 60 minutes in the Errors 60 field. This field is used to measure your accuracy within 60 minutes.
18. Enter the duration of time it took you to complete 60 minutes of tasks in the **Time 60** field. This field is used to measure your efficiency within 60 minutes.
19. Enter your **Average WPM 10** within 10 minutes in the Average WPM 10 field. This field is used to calculate your average typing speed within the first 10 minutes.
20. Enter your **Average WPM 30** within 30 minutes in the Average WPM 30 field. This field is used to calculate your average typing speed within the first 30 minutes.
21. Enter your **Average WPM 60** within 60 minutes in the Average WPM 60 field. This field is used to calculate your average typing speed within 60 minutes.
22. Enter your **Accuracy 10** within 10 minutes in the Accuracy 10 field. This field is used to measure your accuracy within the first 10 minutes.
23. Enter your **Accuracy 30** within 30 minutes in the Accuracy 30 field. This field is used to measure your accuracy within the first 30 minutes.
24. Enter your **Accuracy 60** within 60 minutes in the Accuracy 60 field. This field is used to measure your accuracy within 60 minutes.
25. Enter the total duration of time it took you to complete all tasks in the **Total Time** field. This field is used to measure your overall efficiency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself. This field is required.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname to identify yourself. This field is required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and record-keeping. This field is required.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This field is not required.
* **Typing Speed**: Enter how fast you can type in words per minute. This field is not required.
* **Accuracy**: Enter how precise you are with your typing. This field is not required.
* **Completed Entries**: Enter the number of entries you've made within a specified time frame. This field is not required.
* **Average WPM**: Enter your average typing speed in words per minute. This field is not required.
* **Errors**: Enter the number of errors you've made within a specified time frame. This field is not required.
* **Accuracy Percentage**: Enter how precise you are with your typing, expressed as a percentage. This field is not required.
* **Time**: Enter the duration of time it took you to complete tasks. This field is not required.
* **Completed 10/30/60**: Enter the number of entries you've made within 10/30/60 minutes. This field is not required.
* **Errors 10/30/60**: Enter the number of errors you've made within 10/30/60 minutes. This field is not required.
* **Total Time**: Enter the total duration of time it took you to complete all tasks. This field is not required.
* **Average WPM 10/30/60**: Enter your average typing speed in words per minute within 10/30/60 minutes. This field is not required.
* **Accuracy 10/30/60**: Enter how precise you are with your typing within 10/30/60 minutes. This field is not required.
