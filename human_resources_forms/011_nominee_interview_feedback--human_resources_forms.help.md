# Nominee Interview Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the performance of candidates in an interview setting. It aims to provide assessors with a structured way to evaluate and compare candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the candidate's information and the interview context.
2. Select the relevant ratings for each question.
3. Provide detailed comments on the candidate's performance.
4. Add any additional comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Interview Ratings
* **Interview Ratings** (`interview_ratings`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the candidate's performance in the interview on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfactory" and 5 is "Excellent".

### Candidate Comments
* **Candidate Comments** (`candidate_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any specific comments or concerns you have about the candidate's performance.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information that may be helpful to the candidate, such as their strengths or areas for improvement.

## Tips
* Be specific and objective when providing feedback.
* Keep comments concise and focused on the candidate's performance.
* Ensure that feedback is constructive and actionable.
* Review and submit the form only after completing all relevant fields.
