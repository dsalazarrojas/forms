# Simple Annual Performance Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the completion of annual performance reviews. It helps employees and supervisors evaluate performance over the past year, identify areas of improvement, and outline goals and recommendations for future growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form sections and filling out the required information:
	* Employee name and review year.
	* Select relevant performance areas and levels.
	* Provide strengths, objectives, goals, and areas for improvement.
	* Include supervisor feedback and recommendations.
	* Date and sign the form.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Proofread your responses for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee name** (`employee name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
* **review year** (`review year`, `number`, **required**): Enter the year the performance review is for. This will help track performance trends over time.
* **performance area** (`performance area`, `text`, **optional**): Identify specific areas where the employee's performance excelled or requires improvement.
* **performance level** (`performance level`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the level of performance achieved in each identified area (e.g., High, Low).
* **goals** (`goals`, `text`, **optional**): Outline specific goals and objectives achieved during the review period.
* **objectives** (`objectives`, `text`, **optional**): Describe the specific tasks or projects where the employee demonstrated exceptional performance.
* **strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, **optional**): List the employee's notable strengths and competencies.
* **areas for improvement** (`areas for improvement`, `text`, **optional**): Identify areas where the employee may need to improve.
* **recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, **optional**): Provide feedback and suggestions for the employee's professional growth.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, **optional**): Include any additional comments or feedback for the employee's improvement.
* **signature date** (`signature date`, `date`, **optional**): Date the supervisor's signature, which can be done by selecting a date or typing one in. This serves as the official date of the review.
* **supervisor signature** (`supervisor signature`, `text`, **optional**): This is where the supervisor signs to acknowledge and approve the review.
* **employee signature** (`employee signature`, `text`, **optional**): This is where the employee acknowledges their agreement with the review.
 
# Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Use the "performance level" field to provide a balanced assessment of the employee's performance.
- Use the "strengths" field to identify specific skills or traits that excel.
- Proofread your responses to ensure accuracy and clarity.
