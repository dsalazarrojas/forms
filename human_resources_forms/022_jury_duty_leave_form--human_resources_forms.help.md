# jury_duty_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting jury duty leave from work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (1): Select the employee who is requesting jury duty leave.
* **Jury Duty Date** (2): Enter the date the employee is scheduled to serve on the jury.
* **Return Date** (3): Enter the date the employee expects to return to work after serving on the jury.
* **Reason for Leave** (4): If the employee has a specific reason for the leave, enter it here.
* **Manager Approval** (5): Select whether the manager's approval is required for the leave.
* **Comments** (6): Include any additional comments or information about the leave request.
* **Employee Status** (7): Choose the employee's current status.
* **Company Policy** (8): Select the company policy that applies to the leave request.
