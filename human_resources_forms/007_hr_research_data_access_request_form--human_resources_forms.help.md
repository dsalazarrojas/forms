# HR Research Data Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting access to an employee's research data. This data includes information about their work projects, department, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's ID number.
2. Enter the employee's name.
3. Enter the department they belong to.
4. Enter the title of the employee's job.
5. Select whether the project is related to "Prophets".
6. Enter the start date of the project.
7. Enter the end date of the project.
8. Choose the duration of the project.
9. Select the access type.
10. Enter the start and end dates of access.
11. Fill in the name of the reviewer.
12. Enter the name of the approver.
13. Enter any additional notes about the request.
14. Enter the submitter's email.
15. Enter the submitter's phone number.
16. Enter the submitter's department.
17. Enter the submitter's title.
18. (Optional) Enter any additional notes about the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (required): Enter the ID number of the employee who is involved in the research project.
* **employee name** (optional): Enter the name of the employee who is involved in the research project.
* **department** (optional): Enter the department where the employee belongs.
* **job title** (optional): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **research project** (optional): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Prophets** (required): Select whether the project is related to "Prophets" or not.
* **start date** (required): Enter the start date of the project.
* **end date** (required): Enter the end date of the project.
* **duration** (required): Choose the duration of the project in months.
* **access type** (required): Select the type of access you need.
* **access start** (required): Enter the date when access starts.
* **access end** (required): Enter the date when access ends.
* **reviewer** (required): Enter the name of the person reviewing the request.
* **approver** (required): Enter the name of the person approving the request.
* **notes** (optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the request.
* **email** (required): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **phone** (optional): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **submitter** (required): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **submitter email** (required): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **submitter phone** (optional): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **submitter department** (required): Enter the department of the submitter.
* **submitter title** (required): Enter the title of the submitter.
* **submitter note** (optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the submitter.
* **submitter acknowledgment** (optional): Enter any acknowledgment or confirmation of the request from the submitter.
