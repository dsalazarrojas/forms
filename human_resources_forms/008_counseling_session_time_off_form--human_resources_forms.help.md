# Counseling Session Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request time off for a counseling session. It helps employees notify their manager about their need for time off and allows the manager to approve or deny this request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have the necessary information ready before submitting it.
3. Attach any supporting documentation if required.
4. Submit the form to your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID, passport, or other official documents.
* **Date Requested** (`date_requested`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you would like to take the time off for.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the start time of the counseling session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the end time of the counseling session.
* **Reason for Time Off** (`reason_for_time_off`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe the reason for taking the time off for the counseling session.
* **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the counselor you will be meeting with.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the options to indicate your manager's approval status:
		+ Approve
		+ Deny
		+ Pending
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or notes about the time off request.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in all fields to ensure a smooth approval process.
- If you have any supporting documentation, attach it to the form.
- If your manager has questions or needs more information, they will contact you.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with your manager or HR representative.
