# Scrum Master Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scrum Master Interview Form is a tool used to collect information from candidates for a Scrum Master position. It helps the hiring team to evaluate and compare candidates' qualifications, experiences, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2.  Provide your Job Title and Job Level.
3.  Select your Scrum Master Role, experience, and education.
4.  List your languages, skills, and tools you are familiar with.
5.  Specify your work status, notice period, and notice start and end dates.
6.  Choose your work type and job type.
7.  Enter your salary and notice salary information.
8.  Choose your currency.
9.  Attach your resume (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`<email>`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Job Title** (`<text>`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
*   **Job Level** (`<job_level>`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your job level.
*   **Scrum Master Role** (`<scrum_master_role>`, `select_one`, required): Select your Scrum Master role.
*   **Experience** (`<experience>`, `number`, required): Enter your experience in years.
*   **Education** (`<education>`, `text`, required): Enter your educational background.
*   **Certification** (`<certification>`, `text`, optional): Enter your certification information.
*   **Languages** (`<languages>`, `text`, required): List the languages you are proficient in.
*   **Skills** (`<skills>`, `text`, required): List your technical skills.
*   **Tools** (`<tools>`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the tools you are familiar with.
*   **Work Status** (`<work_status>`, `select_one`, required): Choose your work status.
*   **Notice Period** (`<notice_period>`, `date`, required): Enter your notice period in weeks.
*   **Notice Start** (`<notice_start>`, `date`, required): Enter your notice start date.
*   **Notice End** (`<notice_end>`, `date`, optional): Enter your notice end date.
*   **Availability** (`<availability>`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your availability.
*   **Work Type** (`<work_type>`, `select_one`, required): Choose your work type.
*   **Job Type** (`<job_type>`, `select_one`, required): Choose your job type.
*   **Salary** (`<salary>`, `number`, required): Enter your salary information.
*   **Desired Salary** (`<desired_salary>`, `number`, optional): Enter your desired salary information.
*   **Notice Salary** (`<notice_salary>`, `number`, required): Enter your notice salary information.
*   **Notice Bonus** (`<notice_bonus>`, `number`, optional): Enter your notice bonus information.
*   **Currency** (`<currency>`, `select_one`, required): Choose your currency.
*   **Resume** (`<resume>`, `text`, optional): Attach your resume.
