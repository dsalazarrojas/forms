# Scrum Master Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scrum Master Interview Form is a tool used to collect information from candidates for a Scrum Master position. It helps the hiring team to evaluate and compare candidates' qualifications, experiences, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2.  Provide your Job Title and Job Level.
3.  Select your Scrum Master Role, experience, and education.
4.  List your languages, skills, and tools you are familiar with.
5.  Specify your work status, notice period, and notice start and end dates.
6.  Choose your work type and job type.
7.  Enter your salary and notice salary information.
8.  Choose your currency.
9.  Attach your resume (optional).
