# Employment Verification Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the accuracy of an employee's employment history. It should only be filled out by employees who have been asked to do so by HR or management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your employment history.
2. Ensure that you include any employment gaps or breaks in the "Reason for Leave of Absence" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the fields asking about your employment status.
4. Enter the date of your last day of work in the "Date of Last Employment" field.
5. Provide any other relevant employment-related information in the "Additional Employment Details" field.
