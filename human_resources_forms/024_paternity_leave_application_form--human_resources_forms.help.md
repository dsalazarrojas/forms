# Paternity Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an employee's paternity leave request. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name and Employee ID accurately.
2. Select the correct Department from the list of available options.
3. If applicable, enter the Leave Start Date and Leave End Date.
4. Provide a clear Reason For Leave.
5. Enter the Number Of Days you require for leave.
6. Select the Manager Approval Status (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
7. If your manager has provided a comment, enter it in the Manager Comment field.
8. Sign and date the Employee Signature and Manager Signature fields.
9. Enter the Date of Submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your Employee ID number.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, optional): If you are requesting leave, enter the date you will start your leave. If not, leave blank.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, optional): If you are requesting leave, enter the date you will end your leave. If not, leave blank.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Explain why you are requesting leave due to paternity.
* **Number Of Days** (`number_of_days`, number, required): Enter the number of days you are requesting for leave.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your manager's approval (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, optional): If your manager has provided a comment, enter it here.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, required): Sign and date this field to confirm you submitted the form.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required): Your manager signs this field to confirm they reviewed and approved/ rejected your leave request.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select your department from the list of available options.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to your HR department for guidance.
- Please note that this form is for official use only and is subject to change.
