# Operator Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of an operator, providing a structured framework to assess their strengths and weaknesses. It is used by supervisors or those in positions of authority to guide operators in their development and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the operator's name in the "Operator Name" field.
2. Assess the operator's performance by entering a rating (1-5) in the "Rating" field.
3. Enter the date of the performance evaluation in the "Date" field.
4. Provide specific comments in the "Comments" field, detailing the operator's strengths and weaknesses.
5. Offer recommendations for future improvement in the "Recommendations" field.
6. Select the department the operator belongs to in the "Department" field.
7. Enter the supervisor's name who is completing the evaluation in the "Supervisor" field.
8. Enter the date of the next performance evaluation meeting in the "Meeting Date" field.
9. Enter the date of the next scheduled meeting in the "Next Meeting" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the operator being evaluated.
* **Rating** (`performance_rating`, Number, Optional): Assess the operator's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Date** (`performance_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the performance evaluation.
* **Comments** (`performance_comments`, Text, Optional): Provide specific comments on the operator's performance.
* **Recommendations** (`performance_recommendations`, Text, Optional): Offer suggestions for future improvement.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Select the department the operator belongs to.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the supervisor's name completing the evaluation.
* **Meeting Date** (`performance_meeting_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the performance evaluation meeting.
* **Next Meeting** (`next_meeting_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled meeting.
