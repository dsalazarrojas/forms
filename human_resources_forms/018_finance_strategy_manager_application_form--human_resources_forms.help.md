# finance_strategy_manager_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for the Finance Strategy Manager position. It is not a comprehensive assessment, but rather a means to gather relevant data to help with the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each page of the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Make sure to provide specific details for each field, especially for fields that require it.
- Select the correct options for fields with select_one or select_multiple options.
- If you need more space or clarification for a field, feel free to attach additional documentation or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **application_form** (`text`, Optional): 
  This is a brief overview of your application. Please provide a short description of your qualifications and experience related to the Finance Strategy Manager position.

- **contact_information** (`text`, Optional): 
  Please provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.

- **finance_strategy_manager_experience** (`text`, Optional): 
  Describe your experience in finance strategy management, including any relevant certifications, education, or projects you've worked on.

- **finance_strategy_management_certifications** (`text`, Optional): 
  List any certifications or credentials you have related to finance strategy management.

- **finance_strategy_manager_education** (`select_one`, Optional):
  Select from the provided options to indicate your educational background.

- **finance_strategy_manager_references** (`text`, Optional): 
  Provide contact information for at least two professional references.

- **finance_strategy_manager_availability** (`text`, Optional): 
  Describe your availability for work, including any constraints or limitations.

- **finance_strategy_manager_salary_range** (`select_one`, Optional):
  Select from the provided options to indicate your desired salary range.

- **finance_strategy_manager_job_type** (`select_one`, Optional):
  Select from the provided options to indicate your job type.

- **finance_strategy_manager_job_level** (`text`, Optional): 
  Describe your current or desired job level.

- **finance_strategy_manager_english_proficiency** (`text`, Optional): 
  Self-describe your English proficiency level.

- **finance_strategy_manager_american_english_proficiency** (`text`, Optional): 
  Self-describe your American English proficiency level.

- **finance_strategy_manager_proficiency_test_score** (`text`, Optional):

- **finance_strategy_manager_proficiency_test_date** (`date`, Optional):

- **finance_strategy_manager_proficiency_test_score** (`number`, Optional):
