# Compensation And Rewards Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compensation And Rewards Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from employees on their compensation and rewards program. This form is intended to gather anonymous and non-anonymous feedback from employees to help the HR team understand what they do well and where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  The first page is a note that serves as a header and introduction to the form.
2.  On the second page, please enter your employee name if you feel comfortable sharing it. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.
3.  On the third page, select your primary department from the options provided.
4.  On the fourth page, enter your role or job title. This can be brief, just a few words that describe your position.
5.  On the next page, rate your overall satisfaction with your compensation, on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
6.  On the following page, select all that apply for the most important rewards to you. You can choose one or more options.
7.  On the next page, provide any feedback you have on the transparency of pay decisions.
8.  On the following page, share any suggestions you have for improving the rewards program.
9.  On the last page, indicate whether you are willing to discuss your feedback with HR.
10. On the final page, please enter the date you submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Employee Feedback --**: This is a header that serves as an introduction to the form.
*   **employee name**: Enter your employee name if you prefer to share it. If you wish to remain anonymous, leave this field blank.
*   **department**: Select your primary department from the options provided.
*   **role or job title**: Enter a brief title that describes your position.
*   **overall satisfaction with compensation**: Rate your satisfaction with your compensation, on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
*   **most important rewards**: Select all that apply from the options provided. You can choose one or more options.
*   **feedback on transparency of pay decisions**: Provide any feedback you have on pay decisions.
*   **recommend changes to rewards program**: Share any suggestions you have for improving the rewards program.
*   **willing to discuss with HR**: Indicate whether you are willing to discuss your feedback with HR.
*   **submission date**: Enter the date you submit your response.
