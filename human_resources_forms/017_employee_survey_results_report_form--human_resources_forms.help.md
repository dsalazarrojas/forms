# Employee Survey Results Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee survey results, which are used to gather feedback and suggestions from employees. The form is not mandatory, but it's recommended to be completed by all employees to ensure a complete and representative picture of the team's sentiments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the page corresponding to your demographic information (Page 4: Demographics).
2. Choose your demographic category from the options provided (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
3. On Page 2 (Satisfaction), provide your feedback on your overall satisfaction with your job.
4. On Page 3 (Suggestions), write any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement.
5. Fill in your Age (Page 5) and Salary (Page 6) information.
6. On Page 7 (Date), select the date you were last surveyed.
7. On Page 8 (Time), select the time you usually work.
8. On Page 9 (Email), provide your work email address.
9. On Page 10 (Phone), enter your work phone number.
10. On Page 11 (Page 11), add any additional comments or notes you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demographics** (Page 4, select_one): Choose the category that best describes you.
	+ This field asks you to select your demographic category, which will help us understand your perspective on the survey results.
* **Satisfaction** (Page 2, text): Write your overall satisfaction with your job.
	+ This field is where you can provide your honest feedback on your job satisfaction.
* **Suggestions** (Page 3, text): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement.
	+ This field is for writing down any ideas you have for improving our work environment or processes.
* **Age** (Page 5, number): Enter your age.
	+ This field requires you to enter your age for demographic purposes.
* **Salary** (Page 6, number): Enter your salary range.
	+ This field asks you to enter your salary range for demographic purposes.
* **Date** (Page 7, date): Select the date you were last surveyed.
	+ This field is for selecting the date you were last surveyed, so we can track your feedback over time.
* **Time** (Page 8, time): Select the time you usually work.
	+ This field is for selecting the time you usually work, so we can understand your schedule better.
* **Email** (Page 9, email): Enter your work email address.
	+ This field is where you can enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (Page 10, text): Enter your work phone number.
	+ This field is for entering your work phone number.
* **Additional comments** (Page 11, note): Add any additional comments or notes you have.
	+ This field is for adding any extra thoughts or comments you want to share with us.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any issues or problems with the form, feel free to reach out to us.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, please share them with us in the Suggestions field.
