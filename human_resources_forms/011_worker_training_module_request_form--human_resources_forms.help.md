# worker_training_module_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The worker_training_module_request_form is a tool used to collect requests from staff members for access to training modules. It provides an efficient way to gather essential information about the modules they need and the dates they prefer for the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant modules you are interested in from the module choices list.
2. Provide a brief explanation for your module request in the module request field.
3. Specify your preferred date for the training in the preferred date field.
4. Add any additional information about your training requirements in the training requirements field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Module Request** (`module_request`, Text, Optional): This field allows you to provide a brief explanation for your module request.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, Date, Optional): Please select a date when you would like to receive the training. Be sure to choose a date that works for you and allows for sufficient time for preparation and the training process to take place.
* **Training Requirements** (`training_requirements`, Text, Optional): In this field, you can provide any additional information about your training needs or requirements.
* **Module Choices** (`module_choices`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the modules you are interested in for the training process. This will help us ensure that you get the right training for your needs.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Optional): Click this button to send your request for the training module.
