## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees request a creative break leave of absence, which is a period of time off work to focus on personal projects, attend to family matters, or pursue other activities not directly related to work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Requester Information** (`requester_info`): Enter your name and other personal details to identify yourself as the person requesting the leave.
*   **Date of Absence** (`date_of_absence`): Specify the date when you would like to start and end your break.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of your absence.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of your absence.
*   **Number of Days Absence** (`days_absence`): Indicate the number of days you will be absent.
*   **Purpose of Absence** (`purpose`): Explain the reason for your absence.
*   **Manager Information** (`manager_info`): Enter your manager's name and other relevant details.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter your manager's name.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your manager's email address.
*   **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): Optionally, enter your manager's phone number.
*   **Approval Status** (`approval_status`): Select the current status of your request (Approved, Denied, Pending).
*   **Approval Comments** (`approval_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback from your manager regarding your request.
*   **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or details about your request.
*   **Created By** (`created_by`): Display the name of the person who created the request.
*   **Created At** (`created_at`): Display the date the request was created.
*   **Updated At** (`updated_at`): Display the date the request was last updated.
*   **Approved By** (`approved_by`): Display the name of the person who approved or denied the request.
*   **Approved At** (`approved_at`): Display the date the request was approved or denied.
