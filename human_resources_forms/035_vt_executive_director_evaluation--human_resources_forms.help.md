# vt_executive_director_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of an executive director, providing a structured way to assess their leadership, communication skills, and overall effectiveness. The form is designed to be completed by evaluators who have worked closely with the executive director and have first-hand knowledge of their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title** field with a brief title of the evaluation.
2. Evaluate the executive director's **Leadership Evaluation** by selecting from the options provided.
3. Assess their **Communication Skills** by selecting from the options provided.
4. Rate their **Overall Rating** by selecting from the options provided.
5. Provide **Recommendations** for the executive director's development by selecting from the options provided.
6. Enter any additional **Comments** about the executive director's performance.
7. Select a **Date** for the evaluation.
8. If applicable, enter any additional **Leader Comments** about the executive director's performance.
9. Provide a final **Executive Evaluation** rating by selecting from the options provided.
10. Enter any **Additional Info** about the executive director's performance.
11. (Optional) Enter your **Phone Number**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (`evaluation_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the evaluation.
- **Leadership Evaluation** (`leadership_evaluation`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the executive director's leadership skills by selecting from the options provided.
  * Highly Satisfactory
  * Somewhat Satisfactory
  * Unsatisfactory
  * Very Unsatisfactory
- **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, required): Assess the executive director's communication skills by selecting from the options provided.
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Poor
- **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the executive director's overall performance by selecting from the options provided.
  * Highly Satisfactory
  * Somewhat Satisfactory
  * Unsatisfactory
  * Very Unsatisfactory
- **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, select_multiple, required): Provide recommendations for the executive director's development by selecting from the options provided.
  * Highly Recommended
  * Somewhat Recommended
  * Not Recommended
  * Not Applicable
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the executive director's performance.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the evaluation.
- **Leader Comments** (`leader_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the leader about the executive director's performance.
- **Executive Evaluation** (`executive_evaluation`, select_one, required): Provide a final rating for the executive director's overall performance by selecting from the options provided.
  * Highly Satisfactory
  * Somewhat Satisfactory
  * Unsatisfactory
  * Very Unsatisfactory
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the executive director's performance.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for future contact.

Note: All fields are required for a successful evaluation, except for **Form Title** and **Comments** and **Leader Comments** and **Additional Info** and **Phone** which are optional.
