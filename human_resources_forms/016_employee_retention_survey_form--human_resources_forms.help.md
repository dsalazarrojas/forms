# employee_retention_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Retention Survey Form is designed to measure employee satisfaction and retention. It is used to collect feedback on the work environment, engagement, and overall job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your feelings and experiences.
3. Make sure to fill out the form completely, as some fields are required.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introductory paragraph`, not required): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that describe your current job satisfaction. For example, if you are satisfied, dissatisfied, and neutral, select all three.
* **Engagement** (`select_one`, optional): Select one option that best describes your level of engagement at work. You can choose 'Yes', indicating you are engaged, or 'No', indicating you are not.
* **Open-Ended Question 1** (`text`, optional): Please answer this question in as much detail as possible. What is one thing that you like about working for our company?
* **Open-Ended Question 2** (`text`, optional): Please answer this question in as much detail as possible. What is one thing that you dislike about working for our company?
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Employee Name** (`text`, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Department** (`select_one`, optional): Select the department you belong to.
* **Manager Name** (`text`, optional): Please enter your manager's name.
* **Comments** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your current work situation. For example, if you have had a positive experience, select 'Yes'.
* **Manager Comments** (`select_one`, optional): Select one option that best describes your manager's comments about you. You can choose 'Yes', indicating they are positive, or 'No', indicating they are not.

This guide is intended to be a general help document, and should be reviewed by an experienced form designer/technical writer before being released to end-users. The goal is to provide a clear, concise, and practical explanation of the form fields. If a field label is weak or unclear, an effort has been made to explain the best-effort intent without inventing unsupported business logic.
