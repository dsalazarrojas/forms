# Remote Work Ergonomic Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Ergonomic Assessment form is designed to help employees working from home identify potential ergonomic issues in their workspace setup.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring to answer all required fields. If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Home Office Setup (Field 1)
* **Home Office Setup**: A text field to describe your home office setup. Please provide a brief explanation of your workspace, including any necessary details such as room size, chair height, and monitor placement.

### Employee Id (Field 2)
* **Employee Id (Number)**: This field is used to record your employee ID. This is a required field for HR and administrative purposes.

### Supervisor (Field 3)
* **Supervisor (Select):** This field is used to determine whether you have a supervisor who will be evaluating your workspace. Choose 'Yes' if you have a supervisor, or 'No' if you don't.

### Employee Workstation (Field 4)
* **Employee Workstation (Select Multiple):** Check all the options that apply to your workspace:
	+ Yes: You work on a shared workstation with others.
	+ No: You work on a dedicated workstation alone.

### Workstation Setup (Field 5)
* **Workstation Setup (Select):** Check whether your workstation is:
	+ Yes: Set up for optimal ergonomic comfort.
	+ No: Not set up for optimal ergonomic comfort.

### Chair Height (Field 6)
* **Chair Height (Number):** This field is used to record your chair height in inches. Please enter the correct height for accurate assessment.

### Distance Between Monitors (Field 7)
* **Distance Between Monitors (Number):** This field is used to record the distance between your monitors in inches. Please enter the correct distance for accurate assessment.

### Keyboard Location (Field 8)
* **Keyboard Location (Select):** Check whether your keyboard is:
	+ Yes: Located at the correct ergonomic height for your body.
	+ No: Not located at the correct height for your body.
