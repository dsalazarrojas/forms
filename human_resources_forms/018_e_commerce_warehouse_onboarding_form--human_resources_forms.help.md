# e-commerce_warehouse_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from new hires for the e-commerce warehouse, including their personal details, emergency contact information, and shift scheduling preferences. This information is crucial for ensuring the smooth onboarding process and providing a safe and efficient work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you've understood the purpose and requirements for each field.
2. Fill out the New Hire Details section with your personal information.
3. Select your role within the e-commerce warehouse (Stock Manager, Floor Stocker, or Inventory Receiver).
4. Choose your shift preferences (Morning, Afternoon, or Overnight Shift).
5. Read and agree to the safety guidelines.
6. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
7. Enter your contact information and manager's contact information.
8. Choose your preferred break times and shift duration.
9. Enter the start and end dates of your shift.
10. Indicate whether you are available to work.
11. Add any additional notes or emergency information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Details (1)** (`id: 1, type: text`): Enter your name, email, and any other personal details.
* **Role (2)** (`id: 2, type: select_multiple`): Choose one or multiple roles from the options provided (Stock Manager, Floor Stocker, Inventory Receiver).
* **Shift Preferences (3)** (`id: 3, type: select_one`): Choose your preferred shift time from the options provided (Morning, Afternoon, Overnight Shift).
* **Safety Acknowledgments (4)** (`id: 4, type: note`): Read and agree to the safety guidelines.
* **Emergency Contact (5)** (`id: 5, type: email`): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Info (6)** (`id: 6, type: text`): Enter your contact information (e.g., address, email).
* **Phone (7)** (`id: 7, type: number`): Enter a phone number that can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone (8)** (`id: 8, type: number`): Enter an alternate phone number for emergency contact.
* **Manager Name (9)** (`id: 9, type: text`): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Emergency Contact Email (10)** (`id: 10, type: email`): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Manager Phone (11)** (`id: 11, type: text`): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Shift Schedule (12)** (`id: 12, type: date`): Enter the start and end dates of your shift.
* **Shift Start Time (13)** (`id: 13, type: time`): Choose your preferred start time for your shift.
* **Shift End Time (14)** (`id: 14, type: time`): Choose your preferred end time for your shift.
* **Break Times (15)** (`id: 15, type: select_one`): Choose your preferred break time from the options provided (30 or 60 minutes).

* **Manager Phone (Alternate) (20)** (`id: 20, type: text`): Enter an alternate phone number for your manager.

* **Shift Note (24)** (`id: 24, type: text`): Enter any additional notes about your shift.
* **Emergency Info (25)** (`id: 25, type: note`): In the event of an emergency, enter what you would do.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you've filled out all required fields.
* Review the safety guidelines and agree to them before proceeding.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your manager or HR representative for clarification.
