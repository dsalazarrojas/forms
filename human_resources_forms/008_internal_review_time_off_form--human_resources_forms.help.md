# Internal Review Time-Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Review Time-Off Form is a tool used to track and record employee time-off requests and approvals. This form is used by managers and employees to submit, review, and manage leave requests for various reasons, including vacations, medical emergencies, or other personal matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" from the "Submit Form" dropdown menu if the leave request is approved, or "No" if it is declined.
2. If the leave request is approved, select the "Reason For Leave" field to provide a brief description of the reason for the time-off.
3. Enter the "Date Start Offered" and "Date End Offered" fields to specify the start and end dates of the leave period.
4. If the leave is due to a medical reason, provide a detailed description of the medical reason in the "Reason For Medical Leave" field.
5. Upload any supporting "Doctor Note" if necessary.
6. Select the "Manager Approvals" field to indicate the manager's approval status, which can be "Approve", "Decline", or "No Action".
7. Enter the "Employee Signature" and "Manager Signature" fields to acknowledge the submission and approval of the leave request.
8. Enter the "Date Reviewed" and "Review Date" fields to track the review dates.
9. Select the "Status" field to indicate the current status of the leave request, which can be "Active" or "Inactive".
10. Enter any "Comments" to provide additional context or notes.
11. Enter the "Employee Name", "Employee Email", "Employee Phone", "Manager Name", "Manager Email", and "Manager Phone" fields to provide contact information for both parties.
12. Enter the "Department" and "Job Title" fields to provide context about the employee's role and team.
13. Enter any "Manager Review Comments" and "Employee Review Comments" to provide additional feedback or context.
14. Enter the "Manager Approved Date" field to track the manager's approval date.
15. Finally, enter the "Manager Rejection Reason" field if the manager declines the leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Form** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" to approve the leave request or "No" to decline it.
* **Reason For Leave** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the reason for the time-off.
* **Date Start Offered** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the leave period.
* **Date End Offered** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the leave period.
* **Reason For Medical Leave** (Text, Optional): Provide a clear and detailed description of the medical leave reason.
* **Doctor Note** (Note, Optional): Upload any supporting doctor's note.
* **Manager Approvals** (Select One, Optional): Select "Approve", "Decline", or "No Action" to indicate the manager's approval status.
* **Employee Signature** (Text, Optional): Acknowledge the submission of the leave request.
* **Manager Signature** (Text, Optional): Acknowledge the manager's approval or declination of the leave request.
* **Date Reviewed** (Date, Optional): Track the review dates.
* **Review Date** (Date, Optional): Track the review dates.
* **Status** (Select One, Required): Indicate the current status of the leave request, which can be "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide additional context or notes.
* **Employee Name** (Text, Optional): Provide the employee's name.
* **Employee Email** (Email, Required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Employee Phone** (Text, Optional): Provide the employee's phone number.
* **Manager Name** (Text, Optional): Provide the manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (Email, Required): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (Text, Optional): Provide the manager's phone number.
* **Department** (Text, Optional): Provide the department the employee belongs to.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Provide the employee's job title.
* **Manager Review Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any manager review comments.
* **Employee Review Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any employee review comments.
* **Manager Approved Date** (Date, Optional): Track the manager's approval date.
* **Manager Rejection Reason** (Text, Required): Enter the reason for manager's declination.
