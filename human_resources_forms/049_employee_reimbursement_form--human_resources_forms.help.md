# Employee Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reimbursement Form is used to collect and track employee requests for reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information ready, including form ID, employee status, and any other requested fields.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Form ID
* **Form ID (form_id)**: A unique identifier for this employee's reimbursement form. This will help us track and reference this form in the future.

### 2. Employee
* **Employee (employee)**: Select the relevant employee status. This field ensures that we have the correct employee information for the reimbursement process.

### 3. Date of Service
* **Date of Service (date_of_service)**: The date when the expense was incurred for the employee. This helps us determine the time of the reimbursement request.

### 4. Amount Requested
* **Amount Requested (amount_requested)**: The total amount the employee is requesting for reimbursement. This should be entered accurately to ensure proper tracking and processing.

### 5. Status
* **Status (status)**: The status of the reimbursement request. You can choose between 'Active' or 'Inactive' based on the current status of the request.

### 6. Approved Amount
* **Approved Amount (approved_amount)**: The amount approved by management for reimbursement. This will be filled out automatically after approval.

### 7. Approved Date
* **Approved Date (approved_date)**: The date when the reimbursement was approved. This will be filled out automatically after approval.

### 8. Comments
* **Comments (comments)**: Any additional comments regarding the reimbursement request. This can be used to clarify or provide context for the request.

### 9. Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: Any specific tool or resource assigned to the employee for the reimbursement request. This can be relevant in certain cases.

### 10. Created By
* **Created By (created_by)**: The person who initially created this reimbursement form. This will be filled out automatically.

### 11. Updated By
* **Updated By (updated_by)**: The person who last updated this reimbursement form. This will be filled out automatically.

### 12. Created At
* **Created At (created_at)**: The timestamp when the form was created. This will be filled out automatically.

Note: The form fields with 'required: false' can be left blank, and they will not affect the form submission.
