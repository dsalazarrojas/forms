# Template Employment Screening - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Template Employment Screening form is used to collect essential information about employees, including their personal details, job position, and employment status. This form is essential for HR and management teams to gather accurate and consistent data for administrative and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure that you are authorized to access and submit this form.
2. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and any specific guidance provided.
3. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the relevant text fields.
4. Select your Position Applied from the dropdown menu.
5. Select your Job Title and Job Level from the respective dropdown menus, ensuring that you choose the most relevant options.
6. Select your Department from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose your Work Status from the dropdown menu.
8. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your work email address that you use to communicate with the company.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your work or personal phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date Hired (5)** (`date_hired`, date, required: false): Enter the date you were hired if applicable.
* **Position Applied (6)** (`position_applied`, select_one, required: true): Select the position you are applying or are currently holding within the company.
* **Job Title (7)** (`job_title`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable job titles that you hold or are applying for.
* **Job Level (8)** (`job_level`, select_one, required: true): Select your job level (Junior, Senior, Lead).
* **Department (9)** (`department`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable departments where you work or are applying for.
* **Work Status (10)** (`work_status`, select_one, required: true): Select your current work status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract).

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate and consistent information to avoid errors or duplication of effort.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed once for each new employee or employment status change.
