# video_editor_job_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The video editor job application form is a tool used to collect information from candidates interested in working as a video editor. This form helps the hiring team to evaluate and assess the qualifications of potential applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Job Title and contact information.
2. Add a brief description of your video editing experience and any notable projects you have worked on.
3. Select your relevant skills and education level.
4. Provide your availability for the position, including the start date you are available to start working.
5. Enter your desired salary.
6. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their information, including their relation to you and a brief description of your relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current or desired job title.

### Resume
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required): Attach a brief description of your experience and qualifications.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.

### Video Samples
* **Video Samples** (`video_samples`, `text`, required): Attach examples of your video editing work, if applicable.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your relevant video editing skills, including:
	+ Video Editing
	+ Video Production
	+ Visual Effects
	+ Color Grading
	+ Sound Design

### Previous Work Experience
* **Previous Work Experience** (`previous_work_experience`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your experience as a video editor, including any notable projects you have worked on.

### Education
* **Education** (`education`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your education level:
	+ Bachelor
	+ Associate
	+ Master
	+ Phd

### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required): Enter your start date of availability for the position.

### Desired Salary
* **Desired Salary** (`desired_salary`, `number`, required): Enter your desired salary for the position.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, required): Select your emergency contact from the options:
	+ Mother
	+ Father
	+ Spouse
	+ Child
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select your relation to your emergency contact:
	+ Spouse
	+ Partner
	+ Child
	+ Parent
	+ Friend
	+ Other
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Description** (`emergency_contact_relationship_description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Since** (`emergency_contact_relationship_since`, `text`, required): Describe how long you've known your emergency contact.
