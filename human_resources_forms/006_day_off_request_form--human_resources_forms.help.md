# Day Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a day off from work. It is intended for employees to submit their leave requests and for managers to approve or decline these requests. The form is a simple, digital way to manage employee leave requests, reducing paperwork and increasing efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Start Date" field with the date you would like your leave to start.
3. Fill out the "End Date" field with the date you would like your leave to end.
4. In the "Reason for Leaving" field, briefly explain why you are requesting a day off. This is optional, but it can help your manager understand your request.
5. Select your "Employee Signature" from the dropdown menu. This confirms that you are the person submitting the request.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Approved" field to indicate if your manager has approved or declined your request.
7. If you have any additional comments, you can type them in the "Comments" field.
8. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to send your request to your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date**: The date when you would like your leave to start.
* **End Date**: The date when you would like your leave to end.
* **Reason for Leaving**: A brief explanation of why you are requesting a day off.
* **Employee Signature**: Select your name from the dropdown menu to confirm that you are the person submitting the request.
* **Manager Signature**: Select your manager's name from the dropdown menu to confirm that they have reviewed and approved or declined your request.
* **Approved**: Check "Yes" if your manager has approved your request, or "No" if they have declined it.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes that you would like to include with your request.
