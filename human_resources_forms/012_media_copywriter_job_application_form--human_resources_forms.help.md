# Media Copywriter Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information for the Media Copywriter position. Please fill out this form with your personal details, professional experience, and supporting materials to increase your chances of getting noticed by our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by a *) in each section.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Paste a link to your portfolio in the "Portfolio" field (optional).
6. Select the "Job Title" you are applying for.
7. Briefly describe your relevant experience in the "Experience" field.
8. Enter a link to your portfolio in the "Portfolio Link" field (optional).
9. Attach your resume as a file in the "Resume" field.
10. If you have a portfolio preview, paste the link in the "Portfolio Preview" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Portfolio** (note): This field is for optional, written explanations or descriptions of your portfolio.
* **Job Title** (text): Select the job title you are applying for.
* **Experience** (text): Briefly describe your relevant experience in the field of Media Copywriter.
* **Portfolio Link** (text): Enter a link to your portfolio.
* **Resume** (text): Attach your resume as a file.
* **Portfolio Preview** (note): This field is for optional, brief descriptions of your portfolio preview.
* **Submit Resume** (text): This field is used to submit your resume as a file.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach your resume as a file for the "Resume" field.
* Enter a brief description of your portfolio, including any relevant links, for the "Portfolio Preview" field.
* Ensure your portfolio link and email address are accurate.
* Double-check your resume before submitting it.
