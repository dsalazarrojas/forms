# City Wide Shift Bid Participation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "City Wide Shift Bid Participation" form is used to collect information from employees about their willingness to work on different shifts, their availability constraints, and other relevant details. This form helps management allocate work shifts more fairly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee name and internal ID.
2. Select your department from the options provided.
3. List your preferred shifts or times.
4. If applicable, specify any dates or times you cannot work.
5. If you have seniority, enter the number of years you have served.
6. If you are willing to trade shifts, select 'True'.
7. If you need to specify any trade preferences or conditions, provide the details in the text box.
8. If pre-approval from your manager is required, select 'True'.
9. Finally, select the current date as the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name**: This field is required and should be entered in full, as in your official records.
* **Employee ID**: This field is also required and should be entered as your internal identifier.
* **Department**: Select your department from the options provided to associate your availability with the correct team.
* **Preferred shifts**: List the shifts or times you are most willing to work, separated by commas or on separate lines.
* **Availability constraints**: If there are specific dates or times you cannot work, list them here.
* **Years of service for seniority**: If you have served the city for over 10 years, enter the number of years here.
* **Willingness to trade shifts**: Select 'True' if you are willing to trade shifts, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Trade preferences details**: If you are willing to trade shifts, provide any specific conditions or preferences here.
* **Manager approval needed**: If pre-approval from your manager is required, select 'True'. Otherwise, select 'False'.
* **Submission date**: Select the current date to indicate when you submitted your bid.
