# Staff Application Form Medical - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Application Form Medical is used to collect medical information from staff members to ensure accurate and up-to-date records in the staff database. This form is designed to gather information in a clear and concise manner to prevent any potential health risks and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the Staff Application Form Medical link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form by typing in your first name and last name.
3. Provide your date of birth.
4. Enter any relevant medical history.
5. If applicable, input your medical license information, including license number, issue date, and expiration date.
6. Answer whether you have any medical specialties or not.
7. Confirm whether you have any medical boards or not.
8. Specify whether you have completed any medical training or not.
9. Input emergency contact first and last name.
10. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
11. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Staff Application Form Medical 1, Text, Required): This field is used to capture your first name.
* **Last Name** (Staff Application Form Medical 2, Text, Required): This field is used to capture your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Staff Application Form Medical 3, Date, Required): This field is used to record your date of birth.
* **Medical History** (Staff Application Form Medical 4, Text, Optional): Provide any relevant medical history or conditions you have.
* **Medical License Number** (Staff Application Form Medical 5, Text, Optional): If you have a medical license, input the number here.
* **Medical License Issue Date** (Staff Application Form Medical 6, Date, Optional): If applicable, input the date your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Expiration Date** (Staff Application Form Medical 7, Date, Optional): If applicable, input the date your medical license is set to expire.
* **Medical Specialties** (Staff Application Form Medical 8, Select Multiple, Optional): Check whether you have any medical specialties.
* **Medical Boards** (Staff Application Form Medical 9, Select Multiple, Optional): Check whether you have any medical boards.
* **Medical Training** (Staff Application Form Medical 10, Select Multiple, Optional): Check whether you have completed any medical training.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (Staff Application Form Medical 11, Text, Optional): Input the first name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (Staff Application Form Medical 12, Text, Optional): Input the last name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Staff Application Form Medical 13, Text, Optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.

Note: The fields marked as "Optional" can be filled out if the information is available and applicable to you. 

Tips:
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is used solely for staff medical records and will be kept confidential.
