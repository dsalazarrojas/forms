# Employee Annual Review Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Annual Review Feedback Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather feedback from supervisors or managers on an employee's performance over the past year. This form is typically used during the annual review process to evaluate an employee's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement. It helps supervisors to provide constructive feedback and recommendations for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter your name as the manager or supervisor in the "manager_name" field.
3. Select the feedback topics you want to discuss in the "feedback_questions" field. You can choose one or multiple options depending on your needs.
4. Choose the date of the review in the "employee_review_date" field. This should be the date of the actual review meeting.
5. Rate the employee's performance in the "review_rating" field. Choose between Outstanding, Average, or Below Average.
6. In the "feedback" field, provide a detailed description of the employee's strengths and weaknesses, as well as areas for improvement.
7. Select the areas of development for the employee in the "development_areas" field. Choose one or multiple options depending on your needs.
8. If applicable, enter any action plans or suggestions for improvement in the "action_plans" field.
9. Enter the date for the next review in the "next_review_date" field.
10. If applicable, select the person to whom you will assign the feedback to in the "assigned_to" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (Text): This field is where you enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
* **manager_name** (Text): This field is where you enter your name as the manager or supervisor.
* **feedback_questions** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select one or multiple topics for feedback, such as "Feedback on job performance" or "Other".
* **employee_review_date** (Date): This field is where you enter the date of the actual review meeting.
* **review_rating** (Select One): This field is where you rate the employee's performance, choosing between Outstanding, Average, or Below Average.
* **feedback** (Text): This is the main field for providing feedback on the employee's performance, including strengths and weaknesses.
* **development_areas** (Select Multiple): This field is where you select areas of development for the employee, such as Communication, Leadership, or Time Management.
* **action_plans** (Text): This field is for entering any action plans or suggestions for improvement.
* **next_review_date** (Date): This field is where you enter the date for the next review.
* **assigned_to** (Select One): This field is where you select the person to whom you will assign the feedback to, such as a team lead or HR representative.
