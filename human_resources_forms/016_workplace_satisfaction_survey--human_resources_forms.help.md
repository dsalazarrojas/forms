# Workplace Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Satisfaction Survey is a tool used by HR departments to gauge employee satisfaction and engagement in the workplace. It's designed to gather data on various aspects of work environment, management, and overall job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options that best fit your experiences and feelings.
3. Fill out all the questions that are applicable to you.
4. Make sure to provide your name and contact information (email) at the end of the form, if requested.
