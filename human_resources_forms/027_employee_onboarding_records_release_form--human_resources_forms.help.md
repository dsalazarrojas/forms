# Employee Onboarding Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track new hire information for employee onboarding processes. It helps HR departments to collect and store essential details about new employees, including their personal and employment-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "New Hires" page.
2. Review the fields below for each page and provide the required information.
3. Complete the form by filling in all the fields.
4. Submit the form once you've filled in all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hires** (<label> New Hires, <type> note, <required> false): This page is for recording new hire information. You can add notes about new hires, but it's not required.
* **Employee Details** (<label> Employee Details, <type> text, <required> false): Provide the new employee's name, employee ID, job title, or any other relevant details you'd like to record.
* **Emergency Contact** (<label> Emergency Contact, <type> text, <required> false): List the new employee's emergency contact information, such as name, phone number, and relationship.
* **Employment Details** (<label> Employment Details, <type> text, <required> false): Record the new employee's employment information, such as job description, start date, or salary.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all the required information accurately and completely.
* This form is for internal use only, so ensure that sensitive or confidential information is not shared outside of your organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department.
