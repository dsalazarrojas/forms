# Caregiver Termination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the termination of a caregiver's employment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the caregiver's name in the "Caregiver name" field.
2. Enter the caregiver's internal identifier if available in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select the date when the caregiver's employment was terminated in the "Termination date" field.
4. Choose the primary reason for the termination from the drop-down list in the "Termination reason" field.
5. Enter the name of the person completing this form in the "Manager or supervisor name" field.
6. Enter the final scheduled work date for the caregiver in the "Last working day" field.
7. Enter the number of weeks of notice provided in the "Notice period weeks" field.
8. Select if an exit interview was conducted for the caregiver in the "Exit interview conducted" field.
9. If necessary, enter any additional comments about the termination in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver name**: Enter the full legal name of the caregiver being terminated.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the internal identifier for the caregiver if available.
* **Termination date**: Select the date when the caregiver's employment was terminated.
* **Termination reason**: Select the primary reason for the termination from the following options: Resignation, Performance, Misconduct, Redundancy, or Other.
* **Manager or supervisor name**: Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Last working day**: Enter the final scheduled work date for the caregiver.
* **Notice period weeks**: Enter the number of weeks of notice provided.
* **Exit interview conducted**: Select if an exit interview was conducted for the caregiver.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any other relevant details about the termination.
