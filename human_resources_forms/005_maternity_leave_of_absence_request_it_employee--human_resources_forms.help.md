# Maternity Leave Of Absence Request (IT Employee) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for IT department employees who are new mothers and need to request maternity leave. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the employee's leave request, including the duration, start date, and end date of their leave. The IT department has specific rules and regulations regarding maternity leave, so please ensure you have reviewed and understood these before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Employee Information page with your details, including your name and ID number.
2. Move on to the It Employment Details page, where you will provide information about your employment status and department.
3. On the Leave Request page, provide a brief explanation of your leave request, including the dates you are requesting for your leave.
4. If you are requesting for coverage during your leave, provide details on the Coverage Details page.
5. Finally, submit the form for manager approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information (Employee Information)**: 
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will enter your details, including your name and ID number. Please make sure to enter the correct information to ensure that your request is processed accurately.
* **Employment Details (It Employment Details)**: 
	+ On this page, you will provide information about your current employment status and department. Please ensure that you have accurate information to avoid any delays in processing your request.
* **Leave Request (Leave Request)**: 
	+ Here, you will provide a brief explanation of your leave request, including the dates you are requesting for your leave. Please make sure to include all the necessary details to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* **Timeline (Timeline)**: 
	+ This page is not currently used in this form, so please do not fill it out.
* **Coverage Details (Coverage Details)**: 
	+ If you are requesting for coverage during your leave, provide details on this page. Please note that this is an optional field, so you can skip it if not required.
* **Manager Approval (Manager Approval)**: 
	+ This field is not currently used, but it is required. Please do not fill it out, as it is a placeholder for manager approval.
