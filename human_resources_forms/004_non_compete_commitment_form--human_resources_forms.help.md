# Non Compete Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Compete Commitment Form is a document that ensures employees understand and agree to the company's non-compete clause, which is essential to prevent engaging in activities that could harm the company's business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are not duplicating the effort by checking if you have already submitted a non-compete commitment form for the same purpose.
2. Refer to your employment contract or other relevant documents for details on the company's non-compete policy and scope.
3. Complete the form with the required information, including:
	* Employer's name and title.
	* Employee's start date.
	* Non-compete period.
	* Non-compete scope (if applicable).
	* Acknowledgement date.
	* Signed by the employee.
4. Get the necessary signatures from the employee and a manager or supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employer**: The name of the person or entity that hired you as an employee.
* **Employee Title**: The title of your position within the company.
* **Employee Start Date**: The date you started working for the company.
* **Non Compete Period**: The time period during which you are committed not to compete with the company.
* **Non Compete Scope**: A brief description of the areas or activities you are committed not to engage in.
* **Acknowledgement Date**: The date you signed this non-compete commitment form.
* **Signed By**: The name and title of the person signing this form.
* **Employee Signature**: Your signature, confirming your commitment not to compete with the company.
* **Manager Signature**: The signature of the manager or supervisor, confirming their understanding of your commitment.
