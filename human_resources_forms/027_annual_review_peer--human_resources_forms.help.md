# Annual Review (Peer) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a colleague's performance from their peers, focusing on their strengths, areas for improvement, and overall quality of work. It's intended for use in office settings where teamwork is involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your honest feedback.
2. Choose the relevant options for each field based on your experience working with the colleague.
3. Provide specific examples to support your feedback, if possible.
4. Leave additional feedback or suggestions in the "Additional Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name (Peer Reviewer)**: Enter your full name as a peer reviewer.
* **Your Email**: Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the colleague being reviewed.
* **Employee Position**: Enter the job title or position of the colleague being reviewed.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the colleague works.
* **How long have you worked with this colleague**: Choose the duration of your working relationship with the colleague.
* **Nature of working relationship**: Choose the frequency of your interaction with the colleague.
* **Overall peer performance rating**: Rate the colleague's performance based on your experience.
* **Quality of work produced**: Rate the quality of work produced by the colleague.
* **Reliability and follow-through**: Rate the colleague's ability to follow through on commitments.
* **Collaboration and teamwork**: Rate the colleague's ability to work with others.
* **Communication and professionalism**: Rate the colleague's communication and professionalism.
* **Will to help others**: Rate the colleague's willingness to help others.
* **Problem-solving and initiative**: Rate the colleague's ability to take initiative and solve problems.
* **Attitude and demeanor**: Rate the colleague's attitude and demeanor.
* **Technical skills and expertise**: Rate the colleague's technical skills and expertise.
* **This colleague's key strengths**: Enter the colleague's key strengths or areas where they excel.
* **Areas for improvement**: Enter areas where the colleague needs to improve.
* **Specific examples**: Provide specific examples that support your feedback, if possible.
* **Suggestion for better collaboration**: Provide suggestions on how you can work more effectively together.
* **Would you want to work with them again**: Rate your desire to work with the colleague again.
* **Additional feedback**: Leave any additional feedback or suggestions for the colleague.

Note: The "Specific examples" and "Suggestion for better collaboration" fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
