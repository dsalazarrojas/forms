# Museum Curator Acquisition Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the Leave Of Absence request process for Museum Curators. It helps curators to provide required information, and enables the management to track the requests and approve or deny them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter your curator name in the `Curator Name` field.
2. Select the `Start Date` for your leave of absence.
3. Select the `End Date` for your leave of absence.
4. Provide the reason for your leave of absence in the `Reason For Leave` field.
5. (Optional) If you wish, please provide a coverage plan in the `Coverage Plan` field.
6. You can select the `Approval Status` from the given options (Approved, Denied, Pending).
7. You can enter any comments or additional information in the `Comments` field. 
8. Enter your curator's email address in the `Curator Email` field.
9. Enter your curator's phone number in the `Curator Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Curator Name** (`curator_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as a curator.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the date when you start your leave of absence.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the date when you expect to end your leave of absence.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Please describe the reason for your leave of absence.
* **Coverage Plan** (`coverage_plan`, text, optional): Please provide a plan for covering your duties while you are away.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the approval status (Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Please enter any additional comments or information regarding your leave of absence.
* **Curator Email** (`curator_email`, email, required): Please enter your curator's email address.
* **Curator Phone** (`curator_phone`, text, required): Please enter your curator's phone number.
