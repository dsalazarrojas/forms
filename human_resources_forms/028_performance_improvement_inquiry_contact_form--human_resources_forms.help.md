# Performance Improvement Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Improvement Inquiry Contact Form is used to capture and document performance improvement inquiries and concerns from users. This form will help us better understand the issues and opportunities for improvement within departments or teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief description of your performance concern or improvement opportunity.
2.  Select the urgency level of your concern, choosing between Urgent, Normal, or Low.
3.  Choose the person you would like to contact regarding your concern.
4.  Enter the department that is related to your concern.
5.  Add your contact email address.
6.  Enter your contact phone number.
7.  Provide any additional comments you may have (optional).
7.  If you have any relevant attachments, please upload them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Improvement Inquiry Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text, optional): A brief description of your performance concern or opportunity.
* **Performance Concerns** (`performance_concerns`, text, required): A detailed description of your performance concern.
* **Urgency** (`urgency_priority`, select_one, required): The level of urgency you associate with your concern, with options for Urgent, Normal, or Low.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, date, required): The date you would like to be contacted to follow up on your concern.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): The name of the person you would like to contact regarding your concern.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): The department related to your concern.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Your phone number for communication.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, text, optional): If you have any relevant attachments, you can upload them here.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields for your concern to be properly documented and followed up on.
