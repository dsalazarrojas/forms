# Department Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Department Information Form is designed to collect details about departments within an organization. This form is intended for internal use, such as HR or administrative purposes, to gather information about each department, including its name, description, and leadership contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the department's name in the "Department Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the department in the "Department Description" field.
3. Enter the department head's contact information, including phone number and email.
4. Add any additional notes or details about the department in the "Department Notes" field.
5. Complete all required fields to submit the form successfully.

### Department Name <small>(text, required: false)</small>

### Department Description <small>(text, required: false)</small>

### Department Head Manager Phone <small>(text, required: false)</small>

### Department Head Manager Email <small>(text, required: false)</small>
Enter the department head's manager's email address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use a brief and concise description of the department to provide a clear understanding of its purpose and function.
* Use the department head's contact information to ensure that they can be reached easily.
* Add any additional notes or details about the department in the "Department Notes" field to provide context and clarity.
