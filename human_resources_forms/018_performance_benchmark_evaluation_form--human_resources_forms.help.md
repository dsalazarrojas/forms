# Performance Benchmark Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Benchmark Evaluation Form is used to evaluate an employee's performance in a specific time period. It is a crucial tool for HR personnel to assess an employee's productivity, efficiency, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the performance evaluation form and ensure you understand the criteria being evaluated.
2. Fill out the form with your performance data for the specified time period.
3. Complete the form accurately and honestly, providing relevant details for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Benchmark** (`Performance Benchmark`, `number`, required: false): Enter the numerical value of your performance benchmark, if applicable.
* **Job Position** (`Job Position`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job position or role.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Department** (`Department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department or team you belong to.
* **Performance Benchmark Date** (`Performance Benchmark Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the performance benchmark.
* **Performance Benchmark Time** (`Performance Benchmark Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the performance benchmark.
* **Evaluation Benchmark** (`Evaluation Benchmark`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the evaluation benchmark that applies to you.
	+ Options: option 1, option 2, option 3
* **Performance Benchmark Score** (`Performance Benchmark Score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the score or rating for your performance benchmark.
* **Performance Benchmark Notes** (`Performance Benchmark Notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant notes about your performance.
* **Evaluation Benchmark Frequency** (`Evaluation Benchmark Frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the evaluation frequency that applies to your position.
	+ Options: option 1, option 2, option 3
