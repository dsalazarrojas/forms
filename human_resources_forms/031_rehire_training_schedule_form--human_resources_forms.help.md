# rehire_training_schedule_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and schedule a rehire training for an employee. It allows users to collect and store information about the training, including the schedule date, time, type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled in. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the schedule date in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/20/2023).
3. Enter the schedule time in the format of hour:minute am/pm (e.g., 01:30 pm).
4. Choose the type of training by selecting one of the available options.
5. Enter the duration of the training in numbers (e.g., 2 hours).
6. Choose the status of the training by selecting one of the available options.
7. Enter the start date of the training in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/20/2023).
8. Enter the end date of the training in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/20/2023).
9. Enter the location of the training (e.g., conference room).
10. Enter the manager's name.
11. Enter the manager's email.
12. Enter the manager's phone number.
13. Enter the employee's name.
14. Enter the employee's department.
15. Enter the employee's job title.
16. Enter the employee's supervisor.
17. Enter any additional notes about the training.
18. Enter any notes about the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **schedule_date** (ID: 2, type: date, required: false): Enter the date of the training in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/20/2023).
* **schedule_time** (ID: 3, type: time, required: false): Enter the time of the training in the format of hour:minute am/pm (e.g., 01:30 pm).
* **training_type** (ID: 4, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the type of training that the employee will be attending.
* **training_duration** (ID: 5, type: number, required: false): Enter the duration of the training in numbers (e.g., 2 hours).
* **training_status** (ID: 6, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the training by selecting one of the available options.
* **training_start_date** (ID: 7, type: date, required: false): Enter the start date of the training in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/20/2023).
* **training_end_date** (ID: 8, type: date, required: false): Enter the end date of the training in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/20/2023).
* **training_location** (ID: 9, type: text, required: false): Enter the location of the training (e.g., conference room).
* **manager_name** (ID: 10, type: text, required: false): Enter the manager's name.
* **manager_email** (ID: 11, type: email, required: false): Enter the manager's email.
* **manager_phone** (ID: 12, type: text, required: false): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **employee_name** (ID: 13, type: text, required: false): Enter the employee's name.
* **employee_department** (ID: 14, type: text, required: false): Enter the employee's department.
* **employee_job_title** (ID: 15, type: text, required: false): Enter the employee's job title.
* **employee_supervisor** (ID: 16, type: text, required: false): Enter the employee's supervisor.
* **notes** (ID: 17, type: text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the training.
* **training_notes** (ID: 18, type: text, required: false): Enter any notes about the training.
* **approval_status** (ID: 19, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the approval status of the training by selecting one of the available options.
