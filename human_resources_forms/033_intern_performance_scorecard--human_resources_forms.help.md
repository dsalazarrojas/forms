# Intern Performance Scorecard - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Performance Scorecard is a form used to assess the performance of interns in a company, providing feedback on their skills, teamwork, punctuality, communication, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the intern's work experience and performance during their tenure.
2. Answer each question based on your assessment, taking into account your observations and feedback received.
3. Select the corresponding options for each question, considering the intern's strengths and areas for improvement.
4. Save your progress frequently to avoid losing your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Overall Rating** (Intern Rating, number, Optional)
Overall performance rating of the intern.

### **Skills Used** (Skills Used, select_one, Optional)
Select one or multiple skills that the intern has demonstrated proficiency in, such as Programming, Data Analysis, Project Management, Communication, Time Management.

### **Communication** (Communication, select_multiple, Optional)
Rate the intern's communication skills, selecting Strong, Satisfactory, or Weak, or select N/A if not applicable.

### **Teamwork** (Teamwork, select_one, Optional)
Rate the intern's ability to work with others, selecting Strong, Satisfactory, or Weak, or select N/A if not applicable.

### **Punctuality** (Punctuality, select_one, Optional)
Rate the intern's punctuality, selecting Strong, Satisfactory, or Weak, or select N/A if not applicable.

### **Feedback Given** (Feedback Given, number, Optional)
Enter the number of times you have given feedback to this intern.

### **Feedback Received** (Feedback Received, number, Optional)
Enter the number of times this intern has received feedback from you.

### **Performance Rating** (Performance Rating, select_one, Optional)
Rating of the intern's overall performance, selecting 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
