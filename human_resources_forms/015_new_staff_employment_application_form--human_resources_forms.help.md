# New Staff Employment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Staff Employment Application Form is used to collect information about a new staff member's employment status, job details, and contact information. It is essential for the HR department to accurately track and categorize employee data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the correct form for the correct position.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
3. Review and verify the accuracy of your input before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of your job position. This could be a unique identifier for your role within the company, e.g., Software Engineer, Marketing Manager, or Graphic Designer.

### 2. First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.

### 3. Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.

### 4. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address. Ensure it is a valid email format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).

### 5. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number without any special characters, e.g., `1234567890`.

### 6. Job Description
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your job role or responsibilities.

### 7. Job Status
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of your job position: Active, Inactive, or Pending.

### 8. Work Status
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your work status from the list: Full Time, Part Time, or Contractor. If you're a freelancer, select Part Time or Freelancer.

### 9. Job Type
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of job you're applying for: Full Time, Part Time, or Freelance.

### 10. Department
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the department you're working in: Sales, Marketing, or Customer Service.

### 11. Job Level
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of your job role: Junior, Senior, or Lead.

### 12. Job Category
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the category or industry you're working in: Software Development, Sales, or Marketing.

### 13. Salary
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your annual salary in the format of a numerical value.

### 14. Date Hired
* **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you were hired in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 15. Employment Status
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your current employment status: Active, Terminated, or Furloughed.

### 16. Work Hours
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you work in a standard working week.

### 17. Work Date
* **Work Date** (`work_date`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your usual start time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

### 18. Job Title 2
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of your secondary job position if applicable.

### 19. Job Status 2
* **Job Status 2** (`job_status_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the status of your secondary job position if applicable.

### 20. Department 2
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department of your secondary job position if applicable.

### 21. Job Level 2
* **Job Level 2** (`job_level_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of your secondary job position: Junior, Senior, or Lead.

### 22. Job Category 2
* **Job Category 2** (`job_category_2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the category or industry of your secondary job position: Software Development, Sales, or Marketing.

### 23. Employment Status 2
* **Employment Status 2** (`employment_status_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your current employment status for your secondary job position: Active, Terminated, or Furloughed.

### 24. Work Hours 2
* **Work Hours 2** (`work_hours_2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you work for your secondary job position.

### 25. Work Date 2
* **Work Date 2** (`work_date_2`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your usual start time for your secondary job position.

### 26. Job Title 3
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of your tertiary job position if applicable.

### 27. Job Status 3
* **Job Status 3** (`job_status_3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the status of your tertiary job position if applicable.

### 28. Department 3
* **Department 3** (`department_3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department of your tertiary job position if applicable.

### 29. Job Level 3
* **Job Level 3** (`job_level_3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of your tertiary job position: Junior, Senior, or Lead.

## Tips
Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely. This form serves as a central repository for employee data and is crucial for HR department operations. Review and verify your input before submission. If you encounter any issues or need further assistance, contact your HR representative.
