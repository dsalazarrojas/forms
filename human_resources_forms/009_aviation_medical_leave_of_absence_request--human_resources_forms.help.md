# Aviation Medical Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aviation Medical Leave Of Absence Request form is a mandatory form used to collect necessary information from employees requesting a medical leave of absence. This form helps the HR department process the leave request efficiently and ensures that all necessary details are gathered to facilitate a smooth and effective leave process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2.  Enter your employee ID number as issued by your employer.
3.  Enter your current job title.
4.  Select your department or division from the list provided.
5.  Enter your home base or station location.
6.  Enter your work or personal email address.
7.  Enter your best contact phone number during the leave period.
8.  Enter the date you were hired.
9.  Select the type of medical leave you are requesting (e.g., Personal medical condition, Family medical leave (FMLA), etc.).
10. Enter the first day of your leave.
11. Enter the expected date of return to work.
12. Enter the total number of calendar days requested.
13. If you need time off occasionally, select the type of intermittent leave and provide details if applicable.
14. Check if you have attached medical documentation or if you will provide it within 15 days.
15. Enter the name of your treating physician.
16. Enter your healthcare provider's contact information (phone number).
17. (Optional) Enter the name of the hospital or clinic where you received treatment.
18. Indicate any expected work restrictions upon return.
19. Select the current status of your FAA medical certificate.
20. Check if you wish to continue benefits during the leave period.
21. Select your preferred method of contact during the leave period.
22. Enter your immediate supervisor's name.
23. (Optional) Enter brief notes on pending work or responsibilities.
24. Check that you certify the accuracy of the information provided.
25. Enter the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Employee ID Number**: Enter the ID number issued by your employer.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current position.
* **Department**: Select your department or division from the provided list.
* **Base Location**: Enter your home base or station location.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact phone number during the leave period.
* **Date of Hire**: Enter the date you were hired.
* **Type of Leave**: Select the type of medical leave you are requesting.
* **Leave Start Date**: Enter the first day of your leave.
* **Expected Return Date**: Enter the date when you plan to return to work.
* **Total Days Requested**: Enter the total number of calendar days you are requesting.
* **Intermittent Leave**: Select if you need occasional time off.
* **Intermittent Schedule Details**: If you selected intermittent leave, describe the details of your schedule.
* **Medical Certification Attached**: Check if you have attached medical documentation or if you will provide it within 15 days.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of your treating physician.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone**: Enter your healthcare provider's contact phone number.
* **Medical Facility or Clinic**: (Optional) Enter the name of the hospital or clinic where you received treatment.
* **Expected Work Restrictions**: Indicate any restrictions you may have upon return to work.
* **FAA Medical Certificate Status**: Select the current status of your FAA medical certificate.
* **Benefits Continuation**: Check if you wish to continue benefits during the leave period.
* **Contact Preference**: Select your preferred method of contact during the leave period.
* **Immediate Supervisor Name**: Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Work Handover Notes**: (Optional) Enter brief notes on pending work or responsibilities.
* **I Certify This Information Is Accurate**: Check to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date of submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are requesting intermittent leave, please provide details on your schedule.
* Be sure to attach any required medical documentation.
* Double-check your contact preference during the leave period to ensure you are reached effectively.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your HR department for clarification.
