# manager_relationship_insight_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand the manager-employee relationship, including communication, feedback, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, taking into account your current or previous manager.
2. If you have had multiple managers, answer based on your most recent experience.
3. Be specific and provide concrete examples when answering open-ended questions.
4. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name as you know it.
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, optional): Describe any feedback you have received from your manager, whether positive or negative.
* **How would you describe your relationship with your manager?** (`Manager Relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with your manager, e.g., friendly, professional, or distant.
* **How would you describe your manager's communication style?** (`Communication Style`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Open
	+ Closed
	+ Collaborative
* **How frequently do you meet with your manager?** (`Frequency of Meetings`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **How often do you give feedback to your manager?** (`Feedback Frequency`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **How would you describe your manager's support?** (`Manager Support`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **How often does your manager offer help when you need it?** (`Manager Help`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Always
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **How valuable is the feedback you receive from your manager?** (`Feedback Value`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Very Valuable
	+ Somewhat Valuable
	+ Not Very Valuable
	+ Not At All Valuable
* **Overall Satisfaction with Management** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select one of the following:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Open-ended Comments** (`Open-ended Comments`, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your manager.
* **Open-ended Comments about Manager** (`Open-ended Comments about Manager`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your manager.

## Tips
* Please answer each question based on your experiences and perceptions.
* Be specific and provide concrete examples when answering open-ended questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us.
