# Disciplinary Meeting Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to invite employees to attend a disciplinary meeting. The meeting may be related to a specific issue or concern that affects the employee's performance, behavior, or conduct in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Meeting Details" section with any relevant information about the meeting, such as its purpose, location, and date.
2. Enter the name of the employee who will be attending the meeting.
3. Select a date for the meeting using the date picker.
4. Choose a time for the meeting using the time picker.
5. Enter the location of the meeting.
6. Provide a brief explanation for the reason behind the meeting.
7. Outline any agenda items that will be discussed during the meeting.
8. Add any additional meeting notes that are relevant to the discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details** (text, Meeting Details, required: false): Enter a brief description of the meeting, including its purpose and any other relevant details.
* **Employee Name** (text, Employee Name, required: false): Enter the name of the employee who will be attending the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (date, Meeting Date, required: false): Select the date of the meeting using the date picker.
* **Meeting Time** (time, Meeting Time, required: false): Choose the time of the meeting using the time picker.
* **Meeting Location** (text, Meeting Location, required: false): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Reason for Meeting** (text, Reason for Meeting, required: false): Enter a brief explanation for the reason behind the meeting.
* **Meeting Agenda** (text, Meeting Agenda, required: false): Outline any agenda items that will be discussed during the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes** (text, Meeting Notes, required: false): Add any additional meeting notes that are relevant to the discussion.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear when filling in the meeting details to ensure that the employee understands the purpose and expectations of the meeting.
* Double-check the date and time to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* Proofread the form for any spelling or formatting errors before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
