# Family Emergency Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Emergency Leave Form is a document that employees can use to request leave from work due to a family emergency. This form is used to collect relevant information about the leave request, and ensure that all necessary details are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Emergency Leave Request** field with a brief description of the family emergency.
2. Select the **Start Date** when you plan to begin your leave from work.
3. Select the **End Date** when you plan to return to work after your leave.
4. Provide a **Reason** for the leave, if applicable.
5. Enter your contact **Name**, **Phone**, and **Email** for emergency purposes.
6. If required, enter the name of the **Emergency Contact** for your family member.
7. Select the **Approval Status** for your leave request from the options below:
	* Approved
	* Denied
	* Pending
8. Add any **Comments** you would like to include with your request.
9. Select the **Return Date** when you expect to return to work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Leave Request** (text): A brief description of the family emergency that requires leave.
* **Start Date** (date): The date when you plan to begin your leave from work.
* **End Date** (date): The date when you plan to return to work after your leave.
* **Reason** (text): A brief explanation of the reason for the leave, if applicable.
* **Contact Name** (text): Your name for emergency contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (email): Your email address for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact** (text): The name of the emergency contact for your family member.
* **Return Date** (date): The date when you expect to return to work.
* **Approval Status** (select_one): Select one of the following options:
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
	+ Pending
* **Comments** (text): Any additional comments you would like to include with your request.
* **Approval Date** (date): The date when your leave request was approved or denied.
