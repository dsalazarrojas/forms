# Job Applicant Reference Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Applicant Reference Report Form is used to collect information about a job applicant's previous work experience from their employer. This form helps to verify the applicant's work history and gather details about their job role, supervisor, and work dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name of the applicant's previous employer.
2. Enter the job title of the applicant's role while working at the company.
3. Enter the start date of the applicant's employment at the company (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
4. Enter the end date of the applicant's employment at the company (in YYYY-MM-DD). Leave this field blank if the applicant is still currently employed.
5. Enter the name and email of the applicant's direct supervisor.
6. Enter the phone number of the applicant's direct supervisor.
7. Enter the job title of the applicant's direct supervisor.
8. Enter the name of the applicant's direct supervisor's supervisor (if applicable).
9. Select the type of reference that best describes your relationship with the applicant (e.g., performance, professional, character).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Name** (Employer Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company that the applicant worked for.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter the job title held by the applicant while working at the company.
* **Job Start Date** (Job Start Date, date, required): Enter the date the applicant started working at the company (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Job End Date** (Job End Date, date, optional): Enter the date the applicant ended working at the company (in YYYY-MM-DD format). Leave this field blank if the applicant is still currently employed.
* **Supervisor Name** (Supervisor Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the applicant's direct supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (Supervisor Email, email, optional): Enter the email of the applicant's direct supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (Supervisor Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the applicant's direct supervisor.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (Supervisor Job Title, text, optional): Enter the job title of the applicant's direct supervisor.
* **Supervisor Supervisor Name** (Supervisor Supervisor Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the applicant's direct supervisor's supervisor (if applicable).
* **Reference Type** (Reference Type, text, required): Select the type of reference that best describes your relationship with the applicant (e.g., performance, professional, character).
