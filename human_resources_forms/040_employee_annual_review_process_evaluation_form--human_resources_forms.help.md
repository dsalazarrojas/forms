# employee_annual_review_process_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee annual review process evaluation form is designed to help managers evaluate and document an employee's performance over the course of the year. This form is typically used to assess an employee's progress, identify areas of strength and weakness, and provide constructive feedback for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field carefully and thoroughly. Make sure to use your own experience and observations when answering each question to provide an honest and accurate assessment of the employee's performance. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Header** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field is not applicable to the form as there are no options to select.
  - **employee_name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
- **department** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the employee is in the department, or 'No' if they are not.
- **job_title** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's job title as it may have changed since the last evaluation.
- **performance_areas** (`5`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant notes about the employee's performance in this area.
  - **skills** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable skills that the employee possesses (e.g. 'Yes' to indicate possession of a skill, 'No' otherwise).
  - **areas_for_improvement** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all areas where the employee needs improvement (e.g. 'Yes' to indicate a need for improvement in an area).
  - **feedback** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the employee's performance.
  - **Signature** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter your signature to confirm that you have completed the evaluation form.
  - **Date Completed** (`10`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which this evaluation was completed.

## Tips
- Be thorough and honest when completing this form to ensure an accurate and fair evaluation.
- Make sure to keep the evaluation focused on the employee's progress and performance over the past year.
- If the employee has changed positions or responsibilities, be sure to note this in the 'job_title' field.
- Use the 'performance_areas' field to provide any relevant notes about the employee's performance.
- The 'skills' and 'areas_for_improvement' fields allow you to select multiple options; select 'Yes' for skills the employee possesses and 'No' for areas where they need improvement.
- The 'feedback' field is a place to provide any additional comments or suggestions for the employee's growth and improvement.
