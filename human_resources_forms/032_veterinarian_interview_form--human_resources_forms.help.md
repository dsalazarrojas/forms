# Veterinarian Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinarian Interview Form is a tool used to gather information from veterinarians applying for a job or updating their employment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the "First name" and "Last name" fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Indicate your relevant work experience in the "Experience" field.
5. Check the box next to each certification that applies to you in the "Certifications" field.
6. Select the dates you are available to work in the "Availability" field.
7. Choose your level of education completed in the "Education" field.
8. Describe any previous work you have done in the "Previous work" field.
9. Name the company you previously worked for in the "Previous company" field.
10. Enter your previous salary range in the "Previous salary" field.
11. Enter your desired salary range in the "Desired salary" field.
12. Select the time you are available to work in the "Availability 2" field.
13. Choose the skills you possess from the "Skills" field.
14. Select the languages you are proficient in from the "Languages" field.
15. Briefly describe your work experience in the "Work experience" field.
16. Choose the number of hours you are available to work in the "Work hours" field.
17. Enter your job position, title, and category in the "Job position", "Job title", and "Job category" fields.
18. Select your job status, type, and location from the "Job status", "Job type", "Job location" fields.
19. Briefly describe your job responsibilities in the "Job description" field.
20. List any specific requirements for the job in the "Job requirements" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience as a veterinarian.
* **Certifications**: Check the box next to each certification you hold.
* **Availability**: Select the dates you are available to work.
* **Education**: Choose the level of education you have completed (High School, Associate's, Bachelor's, or Postgraduate).
* **Previous work**: Briefly describe any previous work experience you have.
* **Previous company**: Name the company you previously worked for.
* **Previous salary**: Enter your previous salary range.
* **Desired salary**: Enter your desired salary range.
* **Availability 2**: Select the time you are available to work (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, etc.).
* **Skills**: Choose the skills you possess (Computer, Communication, Leadership, Time management).
* **Languages**: Select the languages you are proficient in (English, Spanish, French, German).
* **Work experience**: Briefly describe your work experience.
* **Work hours**: Choose the number of hours you are available to work (8-16, Full-time, Part-time, Seasonal).
* **Job position**: Enter your job title.
* **Job title**: Enter your job title.
* **Job category**: Choose the category of your job (Sales, Marketing, Management, Administration).
* **Job status**: Select your job status (Active, Inactive, On leave).
* **Job type**: Choose the type of job you are applying for (Full-time, Part-time, Temporary).
* **Job location**: Select the location of your job (Remote, Office, Home).
* **Job description**: Briefly describe your job responsibilities.
* **Job requirements**: List any specific requirements for the job.
