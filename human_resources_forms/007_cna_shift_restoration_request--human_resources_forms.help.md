# CNA Shift Restoration Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request the restoration of a shift for Certified Nursing Assistants (CNAs) who have lost their shift due to various reasons. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about your shift restoration request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your **Employee Name**, **Employee ID**, and **Department** in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your **Current Employment Status** and **Shift Type Before Loss** from the provided options.
3. Enter the **Reason for Shift Loss** and select the **Date Shift Was Lost**.
4. Enter the **Requested Restoration Date** and select your preferred **Shift Type** for restoration.
5. If applicable, answer if you are **Flexible with Shift Times** and enter any **Availability Restrictions**.
6. Enter any **Position Availability Information** and **Certifications** you hold.
7. Answer if you have **Any Availability Restrictions** and if your **Supervisor is Supportive of Restoration**.
8. Enter your **Current Supervisor Name** and their **Supervisor Recommendation**.
9. Finally, enter the **Request Submission Date** and provide any **Additional Information** about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or personnel record.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee identification number or code.
* **Department**: Type the name of the nursing unit or department you are currently working in.
* **Current Employment Status**: Select your current employment status: Full-time, Part-time, Per Diem, Temporary, or Leave of Absence.
* **Shift Type Before Loss**: Select the type of shift you were working before it was lost: Day Shift, Evening Shift, Night Shift, Rotating Shifts, or Weekends Only.
* **Reason for Shift Loss**: Enter a brief description of why your shift was reduced or eliminated.
* **Date Shift Was Lost**: Enter the date when your shift was changed or eliminated.
* **Requested Restoration Date**: Enter the date when you would like your shift restored.
* **Preferred Shift Type**: Select the type of shift you would like to have restored to.
* **Are you flexible with shift times?**: Answer if you are open to flexible shift times, somewhat flexible, limited flexibility, or not flexible at all.
* **Availability Restrictions**: Enter any specific times or days you cannot work.
* **Position Availability Information**: Enter any positions or shifts you are aware of that may be available.
* **Certifications**: Select any applicable certifications, such as CNA Certification, BLS/CPR, ACLS, PALS, or Other.
* **Any Availability Restrictions**: Enter any times or days you cannot work.
* **Current Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your current supervisor.
* **Supervisor Recommendation**: Select your supervisor's recommendation: Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, or Pending Review.
* **Request Submission Date**: Enter the date this form is submitted.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any other relevant details about your shift restoration request.
