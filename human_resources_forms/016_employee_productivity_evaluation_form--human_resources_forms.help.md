# Employee Productivity Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of employees, providing a comprehensive assessment of their skills, work ethic, and overall contribution to the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide feedback on each section. You can choose from the options provided or type in your own comments. Make sure to select the relevant sections that apply to the employee you are evaluating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee Performance**
* **(Employee Performance, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to provide a brief description of the employee's performance over the evaluation period.
### 2. **Work Quality**
* **(Work Quality, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to comment on the employee's work quality, including any strengths or weaknesses in their work habits or output.
### 3. **Collaboration Skills**
* **(Collaboration Skills, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to evaluate the employee's ability to work collaboratively with others, including their communication and teamwork skills.
### 4. **Communication Skills**
* **(Communication Skills, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to rate the employee's communication skills, including their ability to clearly and effectively convey ideas and information.
### 5. **Time Management**
* **(Time Management, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to evaluate the employee's ability to manage their time effectively, including meeting deadlines and prioritizing tasks.
### 6. **Problem Solving**
* **(Problem Solving, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to comment on the employee's ability to solve problems and think critically.
### 7. **Adaptability**
* **(Adaptability, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to evaluate the employee's ability to adapt to change and new situations.
### 8. **Initiative**
* **(Initiative, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to comment on the employee's willingness to take initiative and go above and beyond in their role.
### 9. **Feedback**
* **(Feedback, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to provide feedback and comments on the employee's performance, work habits, and areas for improvement.
### 10. **Employee Rating**
* **(Employee Rating, Number, Optional)**: This field is used to rate the employee's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
### 11. **Employee Comments**
* **(Employee Comments, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback on the employee's performance.
### 12. **Team Collaboration**
* **(Team Collaboration, Select One, Optional)**: This field is used to select from a rating for the employee's ability to work collaboratively with their team, with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
### 13. **Manager Name**
* **(Manager Name, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to input the name of the evaluating manager.
### 14. **Department**
* **(Department, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to input the department of the employee being evaluated.
### 15. **Employee Title**
* **(Employee Title, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to input the title of the employee being evaluated.
### 16. **Employee Name**
* **(Employee Name, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to input the name of the employee being evaluated.
### 17. **Manager Title**
* **(Manager Title, Text, Optional)**: This field is used to input the title of the manager providing the evaluation.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide specific examples and concrete feedback for the employee's performance.
* Keep your comments and feedback professional and constructive.
* Use this form as a tool to help the employee grow and improve, not to critique or belittle them.
