# Workplace Culture Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Culture Evaluation Form is designed to collect information from employees to help evaluate and improve workplace culture. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to share your thoughts and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the department you belong to from the "Department" options.
3. Enter your current job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Select your primary work location from the "Work Location" options.
5. If applicable, select any additional locations where you may work (if not primary location).
6. If you have an alternative job classification, select it from the "Job Classification" options.
7. Enter the number of years you've been with the organization in the "Years of Service" field.
8. If applicable, enter the name of your direct manager.
9. In the "Feedback" field, share your thoughts on your experiences and any feedback you have about the workplace culture.
10. In the "Suggestions" field, provide any suggestions you have for improving the workplace culture.
11. In the "Comments" field, add any additional comments or thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as an employee of the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department you belong to, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select your primary work location, and if applicable, any additional locations where you may work.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, select_multiple, optional): If you have an alternative job classification, select it.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you've been with the organization.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences about the workplace culture.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the workplace culture.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you have.
