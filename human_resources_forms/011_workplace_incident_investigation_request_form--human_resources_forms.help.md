# workplace_incident_investigation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about workplace incidents for investigation and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have permission from HR or management to complete this form.
2. If you are unsure about the form's purpose, refer to the form's purpose description above.
3. Complete each field as follows:
	* Enter the employee's name as it appears on their record.
	* Enter the employee's ID number (if applicable).
	* Enter the employee's job title.
	* Enter the date and time of the incident (in the format "MM/DD/YY HH:MM AM/PM").
	* Enter the location where the incident occurred.
	* Enter a detailed description of what happened.
	* If applicable, enter the name and title of the person(s) who were injured.
	* Enter the name and title of the person conducting the investigation.
	* Enter the date of the investigation.
	* Select the status of the investigation from the options provided.
	* Select the action to be taken following the investigation (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the ID number of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date and time of the incident (in the format "MM/DD/YY HH:MM AM/PM").
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred (in the format "HH:MM AM/PM").
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Injured Parties**: If applicable, enter the name and title of the person(s) who were injured.
* **Investigator**: Enter the name and title of the person conducting the investigation.
* **Investigation Date**: Enter the date of the investigation.
* **Investigation Status**: Select the status of the investigation from the options provided.
* **Follow Up Action**: Select the action to be taken following the investigation (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible in each field to ensure thorough investigation and resolution of the incident.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose description above.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact HR or management.
