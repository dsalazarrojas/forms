# Interview Schedule Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for scheduling interviews, ensuring a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the application name, email, and phone number (if available).
3. Select the preferred contact method (phone call, email, or in-person).
4. Provide additional comments if necessary.
5. Confirm the interview schedule by selecting 'True' and inputting the confirmed date and time.
6. Input the confirmed location and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Name** (`interview_schedule_form_application_name`, `text`, *): Enter the name of the applicant.
- **Email** (`interview_schedule_form_email`, `email`, *): Enter the email address of the applicant.
- **Phone Number** (`interview_schedule_form_phone_number`, `text`, ): Provide the phone number of the applicant (optional).
- **Location** (`interview_schedule_form_location`, `text`, *): Enter the location where the interview will take place.
- **Date** (`interview_schedule_form_date`, `date`, *): Select the date of the interview.
- **Time** (`interview_schedule_form_time`, `time`, *): Select the time of the interview.
- **Additional Comments** (`interview_schedule_form_additional_comments`, `note`, ): Provide any additional comments about the interview.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`interview_schedule_form_preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, *): Select the preferred contact method (phone call, email, or in-person).
- **Interviewer** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer`, `text`, ): Enter the name of the interviewer (optional).
- **Interviewer Email** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer_email`, `email`, ): Provide the email address of the interviewer (optional).
- **Interviewer Phone** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer_phone`, `text`, ): Provide the phone number of the interviewer (optional).
- **Interviewer Location** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer_location`, `text`, ): Enter the location of the interviewer (optional).
- **Interviewer Availability** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer_availability`, `select_multiple`, *): Select the availability of the interviewer (morning, afternoon, or evening).
- **Confirmed** (`interview_schedule_form_confirmed`, `select_one`, *): Select 'True' to confirm the interview schedule.
- **Confirmed Date** (`interview_schedule_form_confirmed_date`, `date`, ): Select the confirmed date of the interview (if confirmed).
- **Confirmed Time** (`interview_schedule_form_confirmed_time`, `time`, ): Select the confirmed time of the interview (if confirmed).
- **Confirmed Location** (`interview_schedule_form_confirmed_location`, `text`, ): Enter the confirmed location of the interview (if confirmed).
- **Confirmed Comments** (`interview_schedule_form_confirmed_comments`, `note`, ): Provide any confirmed comments about the interview.
