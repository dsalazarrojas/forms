# Generic Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect general information about a job applicant. It is designed to be used for various job openings within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

- Start by selecting the appropriate job opening from the dropdown menu.
- Fill out the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
- Make sure to attach any necessary documents that support your application.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Application Details
* **Application Details** (`application_details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any additional information about your application. Please use this field to explain why you are a good fit for the job and what makes you a strong candidate.

### Personal Information
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name here.

### Contact Information
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address here.

### Work Information
* **Work experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your work experience and how it relates to the job.
* **Work history** (`work_history`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a list of your previous work experiences.
* **Job applied for** (`job_applied_for`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have applied for this job before.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your qualifications and how they relate to the job.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your educational background.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, required/optional): List your relevant skills.

### Status and Employment Type
* **Work status** (`work_status`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your current work status.
* **Employment type** (`employment_type`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employment type.
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your salary expectations.
* **Job start date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would start working.
* **Job end date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would stop working.
* **Work hours** (`work_hours`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your work hours.
* **Work years** (`work_years`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have worked.

### Employment Details
* **Work address** (`work_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current work address.
* **Job notes** (`job_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your application.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a list of professional references.
* **Job application** (`job_application`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in applying for this job.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you submitted your application.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth hiring process.
