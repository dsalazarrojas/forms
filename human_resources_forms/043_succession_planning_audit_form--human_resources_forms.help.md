# Succession Planning Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Succession Planning Audit Form is designed to facilitate the process of evaluating an employee's readiness for leadership succession. This form helps identify key performance areas and transition planning status, ensuring a smooth transition of responsibilities and maintaining continuity within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form in the designated order of pages, starting with page 1.
2. Enter the organization's name in the "organization" field on page 1.
3. Rate the employee's readiness for succession by selecting a number between 1 and 100 in the "employee_readiness_rating" field on page 2.
4. Choose the current transition planning status from the options provided in the "transition_planning_status" field on page 2.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the employee's succession plan in the "notes" field on page 3.
6. Enter the email address of the employee in the "email" field on page 4.
7. Enter the phone number of the employee in the "phone" field on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Succession Planning Audit Form** (`succession_planning_audit_form`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the organization.
* **Employee Readiness Rating** (`employee_readiness_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the employee's readiness for succession on a scale of 1-100.
* **Transition Planning Status** (`transition_planning_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current transition planning status from the options provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the employee's succession plan.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the employee.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely for each field.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- This form is intended to be used for internal purposes only.
