# HR Process Optimization Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for partners interested in optimizing their human resources (HR) processes. It helps us understand your needs and goals, allowing us to provide tailored guidance and support. 

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, please provide all required information. If you have any trouble, our team is here to assist you.

### **Company Details** (`company_details`, `text`, required)
Please provide a brief description of your company.

### **HR Focus Areas** (`hr_focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select all HR focus areas that interest you for partnership:
* 'Yes' (check box is already there, indicating you're selecting this area) and 'No' (uncheck box, indicating not selecting this area)

### **Partnership Goals** (`partnership_goals`, `text`, required)
Describe your goals for HR process optimization and how our partnership can help you achieve them.

### **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required)
Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get back to you.
