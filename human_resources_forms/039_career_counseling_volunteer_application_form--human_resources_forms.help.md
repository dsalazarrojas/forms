# Career Counseling Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from volunteers interested in participating in career counseling activities. Please submit your application to be considered for volunteer placement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have only one application submitted per individual.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
3. Provide honest and relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required)

Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required)

Enter your contact email address where you can be reached for communication.

### 3. **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional)

Provide your best phone number where you can be reached for communication.

### 4. **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required)

Enter the days and times you are available to work.

### 5. **Relevant Experience** (`relevant_experience`, `text`, optional)

Describe your coaching or mentoring experience, if any.

### 6. **References** (`references`, `text`, required)

Provide names and contact information for two professional references.

### 7. **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, `select_one`, required)

Indicate your consent for a background check.

### 8. **Role Preference** (`role_preference`, `select_one`, optional)

Choose the type of volunteer work you prefer (e.g., One on one mentoring, Group workshops, Event support).

### 9. **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional)

Share anything else you think would be helpful for your volunteer placement.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out your application.
* Use the provided hints to guide your answers.
* Double-check your application before submission.

Please submit your application to be considered for volunteer placement.
