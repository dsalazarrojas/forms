# Salary Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request authorization for salary changes for an employee. Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid any delays in the processing of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are authorized to make the changes requested.
2. Complete the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
3. Verify the accuracy of the information entered.
4. Submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the employee whose salary is being changed.
* **Salary Amount** (`salary_amount`, Number, Required): Enter the new salary amount for the employee.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date when the new salary will take effect.
* **Salary Type** (`salary_type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of salary change being requested (e.g. 'Yes' for an increase, 'No' for a decrease).
* **Bonus Amount** (`bonus_amount`, Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the bonus amount for the employee.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, Date, Optional): If approved, enter the date of approval.
