# Certification And Credential Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track information on employee's certifications and credentials. It is intended for employees to provide accurate and up-to-date information about their certifications, which can be reviewed and verified by HR.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Date of checklist completion: Enter the date and time you are completing this checklist.
*   Employee Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
*   Employee ID: Enter your official employee identification number.
*   Department: Enter your department or division.
*   Position Title: Enter your current job title.
*   Supervisor Name: Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
*   Certification 1: If you have any certifications, enter the name of the first one.
*   Certification 1 Status: Select the current status of your first certification (Active, Pending, Expired, In progress, Not required).
*   Certification 1 Issue Date: Enter the date your first certification was issued.
*   Certification 1 Expiration Date: Enter the date your first certification expires.
*   Certification 1 Issuing Body: Enter the organization that issued your first certification.
*   Certification 2: If you have any other certifications, enter the name of the second one.
*   Certification 2 Status: Select the current status of your second certification (Active, Pending, Expired, In progress, Not required).
*   Certification 2 Issue Date: Enter the date your second certification was issued.
*   Certification 2 Expiration Date: Enter the date your second certification expires.
*   Certification 2 Issuing Body: Enter the organization that issued your second certification.
*   Certification 3: If you have any other certifications, enter the name of the third one.
*   Certification 3 Status: Select the current status of your third certification (Active, Pending, Expired, In progress, Not required).
*   Certification 3 Issue Date: Enter the date your third certification was issued.
*   Certification 3 Expiration Date: Enter the date your third certification expires.
*   Certification 3 Issuing Body: Enter the organization that issued your third certification.
*   Supporting Documents Uploaded: Select if all supporting documents have been uploaded.
*   Renewal Reminders: Select if you want to receive renewal reminders.
*   Verification Status: Select if your credentials have been verified.
*   HR Notes: Add any additional notes from the HR department.
*   Checklist Completed By: Enter your name as the person completing the checklist.
*   Completion Signature: Enter your full name as a signature.
