# Intermittent Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an intermittent leave of absence from work. It is used to provide information about the request and to track the status of the leave.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant details about your request (Request Details) from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record (Employee Name).
3. Choose the department you belong to (Department).
4. Enter the date you plan to start your leave (Start Date).
5. Enter the date you plan to end your leave (End Date).
6. Provide a brief reason for your leave (Reason for Leave).
7. Enter the date you plan to return to work (Return Date).
8. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Leave Type).
9. Enter the number of hours you will be absent for (Leave Hours).
10. Select the status of your leave (Leave Status).
11. Add any additional comments or notes about your leave (Notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details (Select Multiple)** (`request_details`): Select one or more options from the list to indicate the details of your request.
* **Employee Name (Text)** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Department (Select One)** (`department`): Choose the department you belong to.
* **Start Date (Date)** (`start_date`): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date (Date)** (`end_date`): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Reason for Leave (Text)** (`reason_for_leave`): Provide a brief reason for your leave.
* **Return Date (Date)** (`return_date`): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Leave Type (Select One)** (`leave_type`): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Medical, Vacation, Personal).
* **Leave Hours (Number)** (`leave_hours`): Enter the number of hours you will be absent for.
* **Leave Status (Select Multiple)** (`leave_status`): Select the status of your leave (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Notes (Note)** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about your leave.
