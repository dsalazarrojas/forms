# Medical Science Liaison Interview Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate a candidate's performance during a Medical Science Liaison interview. This form assesses their communication, collaboration, technical, problem-solving, and professional skills, as well as their overall fit for a potential position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the title of the form, which is already pre-filled as "Medical Science Liaison Interview Evaluation Form".
2. Enter the name of the candidate who was interviewed.
3. Choose the date on which the interview took place.
4. Enter the name of the evaluator who conducted the interview.
5. Rate the candidate's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
6. Rate the candidate's communication skills on a scale of 1-5.
7. Rate the candidate's collaboration skills on a scale of 1-5.
8. Rate the candidate's technical skills on a scale of 1-5.
9. Rate the candidate's problem-solving skills on a scale of 1-5.
10. Rate the candidate's professionalism on a scale of 1-5.
11. Rate the candidate's attitude on a scale of 1-5.
12. Rate the candidate's appearance on a scale of 1-5.
13. Provide any additional comments about the candidate.
14. Select all positions for which the candidate is recommended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical Science Liaison Interview** (`title`): The title of this form is pre-filled, so this field is not editable.
- **Candidate** (`candidate_name`): Enter the name of the candidate who was interviewed.
- **Interview Date** (`interview_date`): Choose the date on which the interview took place.
- **Evaluator** (`evaluator_name`): Enter the name of the evaluator who conducted the interview.
- **Overall Score** (`overall_score`): Rate the candidate's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
- **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`): Rate the candidate's communication skills on a scale of 1-5.
- **Collaboration Skills** (`collaboration_skills`): Rate the candidate's collaboration skills on a scale of 1-5.
- **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`): Rate the candidate's technical skills on a scale of 1-5.
- **Problem Solving Skills** (`problem_solving_skills`): Rate the candidate's problem-solving skills on a scale of 1-5.
- **Professionalism** (`professionalism`): Rate the candidate's professionalism on a scale of 1-5.
- **Attitude** (`attitude`): Rate the candidate's attitude on a scale of 1-5.
- **Appearance** (`appearance`): Rate the candidate's appearance on a scale of 1-5.
- **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments about the candidate.
- **Recommended for Position** (`recommended_for_position`): Select all positions for which the candidate is recommended.

## Tips
*   Please rate each field on a scale of 1-5.
*   Be as specific as possible when providing comments about the candidate.
*   Select only positions for which the candidate is truly recommended.
