# Office Employee Safety Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Employee Safety Check In Form is used by employees to report their current status and location, allowing management to track employee whereabouts for safety and security purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Name in the corresponding field.
2. Select your current Office Location using the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your current status from the available options (Onsite, Remote, or Other).
4. If necessary, provide your emergency contact information.
5. Add any comments in the comments field, if you need to share something important.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name in this field. This will help management track your identity.
* **Office Location**: Select where you are currently located - either at the Office, Home, or working remotely.
* **Status**: Choose your current status - Onsite if you are physically present at the office, Remote if you are working from elsewhere, or Other if your status is different from the above options.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency. This is a mandatory field.
* **Comments**: Use this field to add any additional information you want to share with management. This field is optional.
* **Tips**:
    * Please ensure you are truthful and accurate when filling out this form to maintain a safe working environment.
    * If you have any concerns or questions, reach out to management or HR for assistance.
