# employment_status_clarification_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to clarify an employee's employment status. This includes updating job titles, roles, or other employment-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill in the required fields with accurate information about yourself. 
    - Please enter your Employee Id number in the "Employee Id" field.
    - Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2.  If applicable, select your current employment status from the list provided in the "Current Status" field.
3.  Select all applicable employment types in the "Employment Type" field.
4.  Enter the start date of your employment in the "Start Date" field.
5.  If applicable, enter the end date of your employment in the "End Date" field.
6.  If applicable, enter the reason for your leave or termination in the "Reason for Leave" or "Reason for Termination" field.
7.  Enter any manager comments in the "Manager Comments" field.
8.  Enter any HR comments in the "HR Comments" field.
9.  Add your signature in the "Signature" field.
10. Enter the current date in the "Date" field.
11. Enter the current time in the "Time" field.
12. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
13. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
14. Enter your address in the "Address" field. (Optional)
15. Enter your department and job title in the "Department" and "Job Title" field. (Optional)
16. Enter your location in the "Location" field. (Optional)
17. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field. (Optional)
18. Enter the reason for termination or resignation or retirement in the "Reason for Termination", "Reason for Resignation", or "Reason for Retirement" field.  (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Id (1)**: Please enter your employee ID number.
*   **Employee Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card or in the company's HR system.
*   **Manager Name (3)**: If you are an employee, enter your manager's name, or if you are a manager, enter your direct report's name.
*   **Current Status (4)**: Select one of the following status options:
    +   Active: employed and actively working
    +   Inactive: not actively working but still employed
    +   On Leave: on a leave of absence
    +   Terminated: no longer employed
    +   Retired: retired and no longer actively working
    +   Resigned: resigned but not terminated
    +   On Probation: under probationary status
*   **Employment Type (5)**: Select all applicable employment types.
    +   Full-time
    +   Part-time
    +   Temporary
    +   Contract
    +   Intern
    +   Student
    +   Other
*   **Start Date (6)**: Enter the date of your employment start date.
*   **End Date (7)**: Enter the date of your employment end date, if applicable.
*   **Reason for Leave (8)**: Enter the reason for your leave, if applicable.
*   **Manager Comments (9)**: Enter any comments from your manager.
*   **HR Comments (10)**: Enter any comments from HR.
*   **Signature (11)**: Add your signature to confirm the information provided.
*   **Date (12)**: Enter the current date.
*   **Time (13)**: Enter the current time.
*   **Email (14)**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone (15)**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Address (16)**: Enter your current address. (Optional)
*   **Department (17)**: Enter your department. (Optional)
*   **Job Title (18)**: Enter your job title. (Optional)
*   **Location (19)**: Enter your current location. (Optional)
*   **Notes (20)**: Add any additional notes.
*   **Reason for Termination (21)**: Enter the reason for your termination. (Optional)
*   **Reason for Resignation (22)**: Enter the reason for your resignation. (Optional)
*   **Reason for Retirement (23)**: Enter the reason for your retirement. (Optional)
*   **Reason for Probation (24)**: Enter the reason for your probation. (Optional)
*   **Reason for Reinstatement (25)**: Enter the reason for your reinstatement. (Optional)

**Tips**

*   Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.
*   Review the form before submission to ensure completeness.
*   If you have any doubts or issues with this form, please contact your HR department.
*   If you have any additional information, you can enter it in the "Notes" field.
