# The Spot Employee Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spot Employee Survey Form is designed to collect employee information and feedback on various categories, including demographics, work shifts, and feedback on specific topics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide honest and clear responses to all questions.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
