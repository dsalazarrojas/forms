# Parental Leave Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Leave Authorization Form is a form used by employees to request a period of parental leave. This form is used to submit a formal request for leave and includes all necessary information for processing the request.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your employee ID, reason for leave, and start date.
2.  Enter the end date and number of days you plan to take off.
3.  Check the boxes for supervisor and manager approval.
4.  Include any additional comments if necessary.
5.  Finally, enter your employee's name, job title, department, and manager/supervisor's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Id** (1): Enter your employee ID number.
- **Reason For Leave** (2): Please specify the reason for your leave, e.g., maternity/paternity, adoption, or other related reason.
- **Start Date** (3): Enter the start date of your leave.
- **End Date** (4): Enter the end date of your leave.
- **Number Of Days** (5): Enter the number of days you plan to take off for your leave.
- **Supervisor Approval** (6): Check "Yes" if your supervisor has approved your leave request.
- **Manager Approval** (7): Check "Yes" if your manager has approved your leave request.
- **Employee Signature** (8): Sign and date the form to confirm your request.
- **Supervisor Signature** (9): Sign and date the form to confirm their approval.
- **Manager Signature** (10): Sign and date the form to confirm their approval.
- **Approval Date** (11): Enter the date your leave request was approved.
- **Comments** (12): Enter any additional comments or explanations from the approvers.
- **Employee Name** (13): Enter your employee's name.
- **Job Title** (14): Enter your employee's job title.
- **Department** (15): Enter your employee's department.
- **Manager Name** (16): Enter your manager's name.
- **Supervisor Name** (17): Enter your supervisor's name.
- **Employee Id** (18): Enter your employee ID number for reference.
- **Supervisor Id** (19): Enter your supervisor ID number for reference.
- **Manager Id** (20): Enter your manager ID number for reference.
- **Leave Type** (21): Check "Yes" if you are requesting a parental leave.
- **Leave Start Date** (22): Enter the start date of your leave.
- **Leave End Date** (23): Enter the end date of your leave.
- **Leave Days Requested** (24): Enter the number of leave days you are requesting.
- **Leave Days Approved** (25): Enter the number of leave days approved for you.
