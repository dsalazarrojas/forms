# Editor Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Editor Application Form Template is a tool designed to help editors apply for projects with our channel.

## How To Complete This Form

- To start, make sure you have all necessary information ready before filling out the form.
- Go through each section and fill in the required fields.
- Be accurate and honest when filling out the form, as we take the submitted information into consideration for project assignments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Channel URL** (editor_channel_url, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter your channel's URL so we can review your content.
* **What is your experience with video editing?** (editor_experience, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select from: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
	+ Please choose the level of your experience with video editing.
* **What are your skills?** (editor_skills, text, required: false): This field is for you to list any skills you have that might be relevant to the project.
* **What's your YouTube channel URL?** (youtube_channel, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter your personal YouTube channel URL.
* **Why do you want to edit my videos?** (why_edit, text, required: false): Briefly explain why you are interested in editing videos.
* **What's your availability for projects?** (availability, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select from: Full-time, Part-time, As needed
	+ Please choose the best option that describes your availability for projects.
* **What is your rate request?** (rate_request, number, required: false): This field is for you to enter your requested rate for the project.
* **What is your portfolio?** (portfolio_items, text, required: false): Share any relevant work you have done in the past.
* **What equipment do you have?** (equipment, text, required: false): This field is for you to list any equipment you have access to.
* **How can I contact you?** (contact_information, text, required: false): Provide your email address or any other contact information.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, required: false): This is for any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select from: Me, Assistant
	+ Please choose who submitted the form (you or an assistant).
* **Submitted On** (submitted_on, date, required: false): This field is for the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted to** (submitted_to, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select from: Channel Owner, Assistant
	+ Please choose who the form was submitted to.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select from: ChatJIMMY, Other
	+ Please choose the tool you were assigned to use.

## Tips

- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- We will review your application carefully and may contact you for further questions.
- Please do not submit the same application multiple times.
