# Merit Increase Justification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merit Increase Justification Form is used to document and track salary increase requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee's name and job title in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a detailed reason for the salary increase request in the "Reason For Increase" field.
3. Enter the amount of the salary increase (as a percentage or dollar amount) in the "Increase Amount" field.
4. Enter a detailed justification for the salary increase request in the "Justification" field.
5. Provide your manager's approval or denial in the "Manager Approval" field.
6. If necessary, add any comments or feedback from your manager in the "Manager Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Reason For Increase**: Provide a detailed reason for the salary increase request. This should include specific examples or metrics that demonstrate the employee's value to the company.
* **Increase Amount**: Enter a valid percentage or dollar amount for the salary increase.
* **Justification**: Provide a detailed justification for the salary increase request. This should include specific examples or metrics that demonstrate the employee's value to the company.
* **Manager Approval**: Choose "Approved" if you are granting the salary increase, or "Denied" if you are not granting the increase.
* **Manager Comments**: If you have any comments or feedback for the employee, enter them here.
* **Form Footer**: This field is for internal use only and does not require input from the user.

# Tips

* Make sure to review the company's merit increase policy before filling out this form.
* Ensure that the request is not a duplicate.
* Be detailed and specific when providing reasons and justifications for the salary increase request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with HR or your supervisor for clarification.
