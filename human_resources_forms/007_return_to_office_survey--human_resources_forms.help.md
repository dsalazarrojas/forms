# return_to_office_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The return_to_office_survey form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from employees on returning to the office. This will help management understand the feasibility and potential benefits of remote work arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the frequency at which you travel to the office.
2. Choose the reasons you would like to return to the office (if any).
3. Select your work situation (Remote, Hybrid, or In-office).
4. Select the impact you think returning to the office would have on your productivity, culture, and job satisfaction (if any).
5. If applicable, estimate the number of days you would like to return to the office.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
7. Enter your email address and phone number (if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you travel to the office. This will help us understand how often employees are currently visiting the office.
	+ Always
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Never
* **Reasons for Returning**: If you would like to return to the office, choose the reasons that apply to you. This could be for commute time, work-life balance, social interaction, job security, or personal purpose.
	+ Commute time
	+ Work life balance
	+ Social interaction
	+ Job security
	+ Personal purpose
* **Return to Office**: If you have a specific number of days you would like to return to the office, enter it here.
* **Work Situation**: Select your current work situation.
	+ Remote
	+ Hybrid
	+ In office
* **Impact of Returning**: If you think returning to the office would have an impact on your productivity, culture, or job satisfaction, select the relevant option(s).
	+ Increased productivity
	+ Improved culture
	+ Increased satisfaction
	+ Decreased turnover
	+ Improved communication
* **Return on Investment**: If you have an estimate of the number of days you would like to return to the office, enter it here.
* **Comments**: Share any additional thoughts or suggestions on returning to the office.
* **Open-Ended Comments**: Provide any additional open-ended comments or suggestions.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your feedback and suggestions.
