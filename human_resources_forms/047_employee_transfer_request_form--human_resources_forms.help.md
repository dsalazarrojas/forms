# Employee Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Transfer Request Form is used to submit a formal request to transfer between departments. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is gathered and recorded for approval and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee ID.
2.  Select your current department.
3.  Select your desired new department.
4.  Enter a brief reason for the transfer request.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments.
6.  Enter your email address.
7.  Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID to identify you within the company.
*   **Current Department** (`current_department`, select_one, optional): Select your current department from the list provided.
*   **New Department** (`new_department`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the departments you would like to transfer to.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for your transfer request.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your request.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding this request.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your request.

## Tips
*   Be specific and clear in your transfer request reason.
*   Ensure you have necessary approvals and follow-up steps before submitting this form.
*   Complete the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to avoid delays or rejections.
