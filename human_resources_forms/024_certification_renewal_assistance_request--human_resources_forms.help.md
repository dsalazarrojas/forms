# Certification Renewal Assistance Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request assistance with certification renewal. It is intended for individuals who need guidance, support, or resources to complete the certification renewal process. The form helps us understand the details of the certification and the level of support required to facilitate the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can provide you with the necessary assistance.
* Make sure to provide all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (1, *Required*): The date on which you are requesting assistance with certification renewal.
* **Requester Name** (2, *Required*): Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (3, *Required*): The email address to which we should contact you for updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (4, *Required*): Your phone number for us to contact you, if necessary.
* **Employee ID** (5, *Optional*): Your employee identification number, if applicable.
* **Department** (6, *Optional*): The department you belong to, if applicable.
* **Certification Name** (7, *Required*): The name of the certification for which you are requesting assistance.
* **Certification Number** (8, *Optional*): The certification identification number, if known.
* **Original Issue Date** (9, *Optional*): The date when the certification was originally issued.
* **Expiration Date** (10, *Required*): The date when the certification expires.
* **Issuing Organization** (11, *Required*): The organization that issued the certification.
* **Renewal Deadline** (12, *Optional*): The deadline for renewal, if set.
* **Type of Assistance Needed** (13, *Required*): Select all the types of assistance you need with renewal.
* **CEU Credits Completed** (14, *Optional*): The number of continuing education units completed, if applicable.
* **CEU Credits Required** (15, *Optional*): The number of credits required for renewal, if known.
* **Renewal Fee Status** (16, *Required*): Indicate whether the renewal fee has been paid or not.
* **Employer Sponsorship** (17, *Required*): Indicate whether your employer is sponsoring the renewal or not.
* **Urgency Level** (18, *Required*): Indicate how urgent the renewal is, based on the expiration date.
* **Previous Renewals** (19, *Optional*): Indicate how many times you have renewed this certification previously.
* **Barriers to Renewal** (20, *Optional*): Describe any challenges or barriers you are facing with the renewal process.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (21, *Required*): Choose how you would like to be contacted for updates and information.
* **Additional Notes** (22, *Optional*): Provide any other relevant information about your renewal request.
* **Requester Signature** (23, *Required*): Your full name, as a signature, confirming the accuracy of the submitted form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in the form, as it will help us provide you with the necessary assistance.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.
* Our team will review and process your request as quickly and efficiently as possible.
