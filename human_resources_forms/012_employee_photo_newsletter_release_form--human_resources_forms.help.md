# employee_photo_newsletter_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to release their photo for use in printed and digital newsletters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you have read and understood the photo release form.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your official email address for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Department**: Enter your current department.
* **Photo Release**: Choose 'Yes' if you agree to release your photo for printed and digital newsletters.
* **Consent to Use Photo**: Choose 'Yes' for either printed newsletters, digital newsletters, or both, specifying which.
* **Photo Description**: Describe where and when your photo was taken.
* **Photo Location**: Enter the location where your photo was taken.
* **Photo Orientation**: Enter a brief description of the photo orientation (e.g., 'front facing', 'side view', etc.).
* **Photo Date Taken**: Enter the date when your photo was taken in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Photo Taken By**: Enter the name or initials of the person who took your photo.

Please submit the form once you have filled out all required fields, and make sure to review it carefully to avoid any errors.
