# Employee Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Engagement Survey is a tool used to measure employee engagement and satisfaction within the organization. It's designed to collect feedback from employees on various aspects of their work and work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best reflect your feelings and experiences.
3. Provide brief descriptions for open-ended questions.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is the first question of the survey. It's a welcome message that sets the tone for the rest of the form.
* **How many years have you been with the company?** (`tenure`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Answer the number of years you've worked for the company.
* **How satisfied are you with your job?** (`job_satisfaction`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Answer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is 'very dissatisfied' and 5 is 'very satisfied'.
* **How often do you feel empowered to make decisions?** (`decision_making`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select one option - Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Almost Always.
* **What are the most significant challenges you face at work?** (`work_challenges`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Provide a brief description of the challenges you face at work.
* **How many hours do you usually work per week?** (`work_hours`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the number of hours you usually work per week.
* **Which of the following benefits do you value most?** (`benefits`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select all the benefits that you value most. Options include Health Insurance, Retirement Plan, Paid Time Off, Stock Options, and Other (please specify).
* **How would you rate your manager's leadership style?** (`manager_leadership`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select one option - Autocratic, Democratic, Laissez-Faire, Participative, Transformational.
* **Are you satisfied with your work-life balance?** (`work_life_balance`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select one option - Yes, No.
* **What would you like to see improved in the workplace?** (`open_ended_feedback`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Provide a brief description of what you'd like to see improved in the workplace.
* **How likely are you to recommend this organization to a friend or family member?** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select one option - Very Likely, Not Likely, Not Likely at all.
* **What is your preferred mode of communication with your team?** (`communication_method`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select all the modes of communication that you prefer. Options include Email, Phone Call, Text Message, Instant Messaging, and Other (please specify).
* **How would you rate the company culture?** (`company_culture`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select one option - Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative.
* **Thank you for participating in this survey. Your input is greatly appreciated.** (`conclusion`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is the final question of the survey.
