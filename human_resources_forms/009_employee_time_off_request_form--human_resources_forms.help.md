# Employee Time Off Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Employee Time Off Request Form is a tool for employees to request time off from work. This form helps to track and manage time off requests, including the type of time off, duration, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your request information.
2. Choose the correct time off type from the options provided.
3. Select the start and end dates of your time off.
4. Specify the duration of your time off (in hours, half-days, or full days).
5. Select any applicable benefits related to your time off.
6. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) for manager's reference.
7. Enter your manager's name and email for manager approval.
8. Choose the approval status and comments for manager approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee Time Off Request** (`employee_time_off_request_form_page_1`, text, **required**: no)
* This is the main text area for you to provide a brief description of your time off request.

### 2. **Time Off Type** (`time_off_type`, select_one, **required**: no)
* Select the type of time off you are requesting (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

### 3. **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, **required**: no)
* Enter the start date of your time off.

### 4. **End Date** (`end_date`, date, **required**: no)
* Enter the end date of your time off.

### 5. **Time Off Duration** (`time_off_duration`, number, **required**: no)
* Enter the duration of your time off in a numerical value.

### 6. **Time Off Duration Units** (`time_off_duration_units`, select_one, **required**: no)
* Select the unit of time for your time off duration (e.g., hour, half-day, or full-day).

### 7. **Time Off Benefits** (`time_off_benefits`, select_multiple, **required**: no)
* Select any applicable benefits related to your time off.

### 8. **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, **required**: no)
* Enter your email address for manager's reference.

### 9. **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, **required**: no)
* Enter your phone number for manager's reference.

### 10. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, **required**: no)
* Enter your manager's name.

### 11. **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, **required**: no)
* Enter your manager's email.

### 12. **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, **required**: no)
* Choose the approval status of your time off request (e.g., approved, pending, or rejected).

### 13. **Comments** (`comments`, note, **required**: no)
* Provide any comments or explanations for your time off request.

### 14. **Approval Comments** (`approval_comments`, note, **required**: no)
* Enter any comments or explanations from your manager.

### 16. **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date, **required**: no)
