# Diversity And Inclusion Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diversity And Inclusion Report Form is a tool for employees to share their experiences, thoughts, and feelings about diversity and inclusion practices within the organization. It helps the HR department to track and analyze the progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to submit a report.
2.  Start by filling out the form with your personal details: first name, last name, email, job title, and department.
3.  Select your department from the dropdown menu to categorize your report.
4.  Describe your experiences and thoughts about diversity and inclusion practices in the provided text fields.
5.  Enter the date and time you submitted the form for record-keeping purposes.
6.  Optionally, provide any additional comments or feedback.
7.  Finally, assign the report to a team member or manager for review and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name, for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name, for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your job title, to provide context for your report.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the provided menu to categorize your report.
* **Diversity Practices** (`diversity_practices`, text, optional): Describe your experiences and thoughts about diversity and inclusion practices.
* **Experiences** (`experiences`, text, required): Share your experiences and thoughts about diversity and inclusion practices.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form for record-keeping purposes.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form for record-keeping purposes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the team member or manager to whom you would like to assign the report for review and follow-up.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your report carefully before submitting it.
* Double-check that you have filled out all the required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask HR for clarification.
* Submitting multiple reports with the same information can lead to confusion and duplication of effort.
