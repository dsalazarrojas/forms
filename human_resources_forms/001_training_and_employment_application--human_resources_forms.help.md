# Training And Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants who are interested in training or employment opportunities with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide a brief summary of your employment history, including any relevant work experience.
3. List your skills and qualifications that are relevant to the training or employment opportunity.
4. Fill out the contact information 2 section with any additional details or contact information.
5. Select your current employment status (active, inactive, or unknown).
6. Enter your employment start date and end date (if applicable).
7. Enter your employment start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ Provide a brief summary of your work experience, including any relevant details.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ List your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_information_2`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ Enter any additional details or contact information.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, `optional`):
	+ Describe your work experience, including job responsibilities and achievements.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, `optional`):
	+ Enter the date you started your current or previous job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, `optional`):
	+ Enter the date you ended your previous job (if applicable).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, `optional`):
	+ Select active, inactive, or unknown to indicate your current employment status.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, `date`, `optional`):
	+ Enter the date you started your current or previous job.
* **Employment End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, `optional`):
	+ Enter the date you ended your previous job (if applicable).
