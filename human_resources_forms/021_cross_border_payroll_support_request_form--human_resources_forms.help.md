# Cross Border Payroll Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request support for cross-border payroll processing issues. Please use this form to submit your requests and a member of our payroll support team will be notified and will assist you in resolving the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Requestor Name.
2. Select your Department.
3. Enter your Location.
4. Enter your Employee Number.
5. Select the Request Date.
6. Provide a brief description of your request in the Request Description field.
7. Attach any supporting documents if necessary.
8. Specify if the request is a priority.
9. Choose who the Payroll Support Requester is.
10. Select who the Requestee is.
11. Choose who the request is assigned to.
12. Specify the status of the request.
13. Provide any additional comments.
14. Enter the Form ID (if available).
15. Enter the Created By field (if available).
16. Enter the Created At field (if available).
17. Enter the Updated By field (if available).
18. Enter the Updated At field (if available).
19. Enter the Submitted By field (if available).
20. Enter the Submitted At field (if available).
21. Enter the Assigned By field (if available).
22. Select who the request was submitted to.
23. Enter any additional comments.
24. Enter the Assigned To Name field (if available).
25. Enter the Status 2 field (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name** (1): Enter your full name, as you are the individual submitting the request.
* **Department** (2): Select your department to help the payroll support team identify your team.
* **Location** (3): Enter your work location to help the payroll support team locate the issue.
* **Employee Number** (4): Enter your employee ID number for reference purposes.
* **Request Date** (5): Enter the date of the issue or the desired resolution date.
* **Request Description** (6): Enter a brief description of the issue you are experiencing or the resolution you are seeking.
* **Supporting Documents** (7): Attach any relevant documents that support your request, such as screenshots or error logs.
* **Priority** (8): Check this box if your request is time-sensitive or requires immediate attention.
* **Payroll Support Requester** (9): Choose who the request is for, whether it's for a team member or yourself.
* **Requestee** (10): Select who the request is for, whether it's for a team member or yourself.
* **Assigned To** (11): Choose who the request is assigned to.
* **Status** (12): Specify the status of the request, whether it's active or inactive.
* **Comments** (13): Enter any additional comments or information about the request.
* **Form ID** (14): Enter the unique identifier of the form, if available.
* **Created By** (15): Enter the person who created the request.
* **Created At** (16): Enter the date and time the request was created.
* **Updated By** (17): Enter the person who last updated the request.
* **Updated At** (18): Enter the date and time the request was last updated.
* **Submitted By** (19): Enter the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitted At** (20): Enter the date and time the request was submitted.
* **Assigned By** (21): Enter the person who assigned the request.
* **Submitted To** (22): Select who the request was submitted to.
* **Comments 2** (23): Enter any additional comments.
* **Assigned To Name** (24): Enter the name of the person assigned to the request.
* **Status 2** (25): Specify the status of the request, whether it's active or inactive.
