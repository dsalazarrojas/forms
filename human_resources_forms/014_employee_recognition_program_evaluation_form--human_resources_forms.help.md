# employee_recognition_program_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee recognition program evaluation form is used to assess the effectiveness of the employee recognition program and identify areas for improvement. This form is designed to be completed by managers and HR representatives to gather data on the program's performance and make informed decisions to enhance it.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer based on factual information.
- Review all sections before starting.
- Make sure to answer all questions that apply to your organization.
- Be honest and accurate in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the employee ID of the person who will be completing the form.
  - This field is not required and is only for internal identification purposes.
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the person completing the form.
  - This field is optional, but it can be useful for tracking who completed the form.
- **Department ID** (`department_id`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the department ID of the person completing the form.
  - This field is not required and is only for internal identification purposes.
- **Recognition Initiatives** (`recognition_initiatives`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all initiatives that are currently offered by your company.
  - Check the options and select all the initiatives that are being implemented in your organization.
- **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the specific goals you want to achieve with your recognition program.
  - Be as specific as possible when describing the goals of the program.
- **Frequency Of Recognition** (`frequency_of_recognition`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose how often the manager/leader provides recognition.
  - Select the frequency that best describes your organization's recognition process.
- **Recognition Methods** (`recognition_methods`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the methods used for recognition.
  - Check the options and choose all the methods being used in your organization.
- **Recognition Channels** (`recognition_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the channels used for recognition.
  - Check the options and choose all the channels being used in your organization.
- **Recognition Types** (`recognition_types`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the types of recognition given.
  - Check the options and choose all the types of recognition being given in your organization.
- **Recognition Benefits** (`recognition_benefits`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the benefits of recognition.
  - Be as specific as possible when describing the benefits of recognition.
- **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose how often you evaluate recognition initiatives.
  - Select the frequency that best describes your organization's evaluation process.
- **Evaluation Methods** (`evaluation_methods`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the methods used for evaluation.
  - Check the options and choose all the methods being used in your organization.
- **Evaluation Benefits** (`evaluation_benefits`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the benefits of evaluation.
  - Be as specific as possible when describing the benefits of evaluation.
- **Employee Response** (`employee_response`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the response from employees.
  - Select the response that best describes employee feedback.
- **Manager Involvement** (`manager_involvement`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the level of involvement of managers.
  - Select the level of involvement that best describes your organization's manager involvement.
- **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the level of satisfaction of employees.
