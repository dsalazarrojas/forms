# Team Member Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from team members about each other's performance, skills, and contributions to the team. It helps in evaluating team members' strengths and weaknesses, and provides valuable insights for team development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus or enter your answers in the text fields.
3. Make sure to fill out all mandatory fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. If you are unsure about any question, ask your team lead or HR representative for clarification.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluate Employees** (`evaluate_employees`, `select_one`, required): This question asks you to decide whether the team member's performance is satisfactory or not. Choose "Yes" if you think they are doing well, and "No" if there are concerns.
* **Rate Employees** (`rate_employees`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate the team member's performance on a scale. Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate how well you think they are performing.
* **Share Results** (`share_results`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all the ways you want to share the results of this evaluation with the team member. You can choose "Yes" for each method you'd like to use.
* **Get Feedback** (`get_feedback`, `note`, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback about the team member. It's optional, but it can be helpful for the team member to understand your perspective.
* **Share Online** (`share_online`, `select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" if you want to share the results online, and "No" otherwise.
* **Project Functions** (`project_functions`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the project functions the team member is responsible for.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Provide a short description of the project the team member is working on.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the status of the project the team member is working on. Choose "Active" if it's ongoing, and "Inactive" if it's completed or on hold.
