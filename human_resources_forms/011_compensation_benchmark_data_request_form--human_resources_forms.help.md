# Compensation Benchmark Data Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compensation Benchmark Data Request Form is a tool used to collect compensation data from clients or partners for the purpose of benchmarking. It helps us gather essential information from you to facilitate the data collection and reporting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following pages:

1. **Request Details** - Provide context for your benchmarking request in the designated space.
2. Fill in your contact information:
	* **Requester Name**: This is your name and contact information for follow-up purposes.
	* **Requester Email**: Your email address, where you can be reached for further questions or clarification.
3. Confirm your organization's details:
	* **Organization Size**: The number of employees in your organization.
4. Specify the roles or job families you wish to benchmark:
	* **Roles or Job Families Requested**: List the key roles or job families you'd like to have benchmarked.
5. Define the scope of your request:
	* **Geographic Scope**: Select the scope of your request, such as Local, National, Regional, or Global.
6. Choose your preferred data format:
	* **Data Format**: Select whether you'd like the data in CSV, Excel, or a Summary Report.
7. Set a deadline for the data:
	* **Deadline**: If you have a specific date when the benchmarking is needed, please provide it.
8. Add any additional notes:
	* **Additional Notes**: Provide any extra details that might help us assist with your request.
9. Confirm manager approval:
	* **Manager Approval**: Confirm that you've obtained approval from your manager for this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name**: Your name and contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Requester Email**: Your email address, where you can be reached for further questions or clarification.
* **Organization Size**: The number of employees in your organization.
* **Roles or Job Families Requested**: List the key roles or job families you'd like to have benchmarked.
* **Geographic Scope**: The scope of your request, covering Local, National, Regional, or Global areas.
* **Data Format**: Your preferred data format, such as CSV, Excel, or Summary Report.
* **Deadline**: If you have a specific date when the benchmarking is needed, please provide it.
* **Additional Notes**: Optional details to help us assist with your request.
* **Manager Approval**: Confirm that you've obtained approval from your manager for this request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have manager approval before submitting this form.
* Make sure to provide accurate information to avoid delays in the data collection process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
