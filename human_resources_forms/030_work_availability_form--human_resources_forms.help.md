# Work Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Availability Form is designed to gather information about an employee's work schedule, availability, and other relevant details to facilitate team coordination and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's details in the "employee" field.
2. Select a date for their work availability in the "work availability" field.
3. Indicate the number of hours they work per week in the "hours per week" field.
4. Provide additional comments or details about their work availability in the "work availability details" field.
5. Select the managers with whom they would like to concurrence in the "manager concurrence" field.
6. Enter any comments from their manager in the "manager comments" field.
7. Select the managers with whom they have concurred in the "employee concurrence" field.
8. Enter any comments they would like to submit to their manager in the "employee comments" field.
9. Select the manager's approval status in the "manager approval" field.
9. Enter any comments from their manager regarding their approval in the "manager comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee** (employee, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who is filling out the form.
* **work availability** (work availability, date, required): Select a date for the employee's work availability.
* **hours per week** (hours per week, number, required): Indicate the number of hours the employee works per week.
* **work availability details** (work availability details, text, required): Provide any additional comments or details about the employee's work availability.
* **manager concurrence** (manager concurrence, select_one, required): Select the manager(s) with whom the employee has concurred.
* **manager comments** (manager comments, text, required): Enter any comments from the manager.
* **employee concurrence** (employee concurrence, select_multiple, required): Select the manager(s) with whom the employee has concurred.
* **employee comments** (employee comments, text, required): Enter any comments from the employee.
* **manager approval** (manager approval, select_one, required): Select the manager's approval status.
* **manager comment** (manager comment, text, required): Enter any comments from the manager regarding their approval.
* **manager comment** (manager comment, text, not required): Enter any additional comments from the manager.
* **manager comments** (manager comments, text, not required): This field is a duplicate of the "manager comment" field.
