# Employee Assistance Program Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) is a confidential support service provided to employees for mental health and wellness concerns. This form allows employees to request access to the EAP services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
2. Select your department from the drop-down list.
3. Provide a brief reason for requesting EAP services.
4. Indicate whether your manager has approved the request.
5. Choose the EAP Counselor you would like to be assigned to you.
6. Submit the form to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee identification number.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your employee ID.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list:
	+ HR
	+ Finance
	+ Marketing
* **Reason for Request**: Explain briefly why you are requesting EAP services.
* **Manager Approval**: Indicate whether your manager has approved the request. Please select "Yes" if they have approved, and "No" if they have not.
* **EAP Counselor**: Choose the EAP Counselor you would like to be assigned to you:
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Doe

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR representative for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the EAP services, contact the EAP counselor assigned to you.
