# CV Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CV Form Template is a tool used to gather relevant information about an individual's skills, experience, and qualifications for internal job openings within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Be as specific as possible in the description of your current role, skills, and education.
3. Ensure that your upload link (if applicable) is a working link or a clear reference to a file attachment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant full name**: A required field where you should enter your legal name.
- **Contact email**: A required field where you should provide your preferred email for contact purposes.
- **Phone number**: An optional field where you can enter your phone number, including the country code.
- **Current employer**: An optional field where you can enter the name of your current or previous employer.
- **Role summary**: A required field where you should provide a brief description of your current role.
- **Skills list**: A required field where you should list your primary technical and soft skills.
- **Education and training**: A required field where you should list your highest qualifications.
- **Upload link**: An optional field where you can upload a link to an external file or reference a file attachment.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
