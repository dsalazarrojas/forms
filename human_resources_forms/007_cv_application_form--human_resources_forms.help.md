# CV Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CV Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants to fill out a Curriculum Vitae (CV) application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the Personal Information section with your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Current Address, City, State/Province, and Zip/Postal Code.
* Select the Job Title and Department you are applying for.
* Choose your Current Employment Status and Years of Work Experience.
* List your Previous Positions, including company names and dates.
* Select your Highest Degree Attained.
* Choose the Field of Study for your highest degree.
* List your Key Skills.
* Upload your CV in PDF, DOC, or DOCX format.
* If you want to, write a Cover Letter to introduce yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State/Province**: Enter your state or province.
* **Zip/Postal Code**: Enter your zip or postal code.
### Position Applied For
* **Job Title**: Select the job title you are applying for.
* **Department**: Choose the department you are applying for.
### Work Experience
* **Current Employment Status**: Choose your current employment status.
* **Years of Work Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked.
* **List Your Previous Positions**: List your previous positions, including company names and dates.
### Education
* **Highest Degree Attained**: Select your highest degree attained.
* **Field of Study**: Enter the field of study for your highest degree.
* **University/Institution Name**: Enter the name of the university or institution where you attained your highest degree.
### Skills
* **List Your Key Skills**: List your key skills separated by commas.
### CV Upload
* **Upload Your CV**: Upload your CV in PDF, DOC, or DOCX format.
### Cover Letter
* **Cover Letter**: This is optional, if you want to introduce yourself and explain why you are a good fit for the position.

Note: Please make sure you have a clear and concise summary of your work experience, education, and skills before filling out this form.
