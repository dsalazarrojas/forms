# Sick Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a sick leave from work due to illness or other medical reasons. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your employee name on the first page.
2.  Enter the start and end dates of your sick leave on the second page.
3.  Specify the duration of your leave on the third page.
4.  Provide a valid reason for your absence on the fourth page.
5.  Add any additional comments or notes for your manager's reference on the fifth page.
6.  Select the required manager approval for your sick leave on the sixth page.
7.  Select any necessary HR approvals on the seventh page.
8.  Provide any additional comments or notes for your manager's reference on the eighth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, optional): The date you plan to begin your sick leave. If you're requesting an urgent absence, please provide this information as soon as possible.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, optional): The date you plan to return to work. Please ensure this date is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Duration** (`duration`, optional): The number of days you expect to be on sick leave. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Reason** (`reason`, optional): Please provide a valid reason for your absence, such as doctor's note or personal medical reasons.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for your manager's reference.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, optional): Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved your sick leave request, 'No' if your manager has rejected your request, or 'Pending' if your manager requires more information.
* **HR Approvals** (`hr_approvals`, optional): Select all necessary HR approvals for your sick leave. Please refer to your HR representative for guidance on required approvals.
* **Manager Note 2** (`manager_note_2`, optional): Add any further comments or notes for your manager's reference.

**Tips**
- Ensure you complete each page in sequence and provide accurate information for each field.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult your HR representative or refer back to this guide.
- Please provide valid reasons for your absence to ensure efficient processing of your sick leave request.
