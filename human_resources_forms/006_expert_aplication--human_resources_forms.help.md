# Expert Aplication - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expert Application form is designed to collect information from experts applying for a job position within the company. This form is used by HR department to gather relevant data for recruitment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** if you have one.
3. Select your **Job Title** from the dropdown list.
4. Enter your **Job Title Id** if you have one.
5. Provide a link to your **Resume URL** if you have one.
6. Choose your relevant **Skills** from the dropdown list.
7. Write a brief **Bio** to introduce yourself and your expertise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one. This can be used for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Select your current or desired job title from the list.
* **Job Title Id** (`job_title_id`, text, optional): Enter your job title ID if you have one. This is not a standard field and may not be used for anything.
* **Resume URL** (`resume_url`, text, optional): Provide a link to your resume if you have one. This can be used for review purposes.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the skills you possess from the list.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, optional): Write a brief description of yourself and your expertise.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use your professional email address.
- Keep your **Bio** brief and concise.
- Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy.
