# performance_bonus_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a performance bonus for an employee. It is intended to be completed by employees to submit their bonus requests for review and approval. The form ensures that the employee provides all necessary details for their bonus request, including their department, bonus type, and reason for the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. For the `employee_id` field, enter your employee ID as provided by HR.
3. Select your department from the drop-down options in the `employee_department` field.
4. Choose the type of bonus you are requesting from the options in the `bonus_type` field.
5. Select all applicable bonus types from the options in the `prophets` field.
6. Enter the amount of the bonus you are requesting in the `bonus_amount` field.
7. Provide a brief reason for your bonus request in the `reason` field.
8. Enter the date you submitted your request in the `date_submitted` field.
9. Enter the date your request was approved in the `date_approved` field.
10. If applicable, enter the name of the person who approved your request in the `approved_by` field.
11. Add any comments about your bonus request in the `comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee id** (`employee_id`, number, *): Enter your employee ID as provided by HR.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, *): Select your department from the drop-down options.
* **Bonus Type** (`bonus_type`, select_one, *): Choose the type of bonus you are requesting.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_multiple, *): Select all applicable bonus types.
* **Bonus Amount** (`bonus_amount`, number, *): Enter the amount of the bonus you are requesting.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, *): Provide a brief reason for your bonus request.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, *): Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, date, *): Enter the date your request was approved.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, ): If applicable, enter the name of the person who approved your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, ): Add any comments about your bonus request.
