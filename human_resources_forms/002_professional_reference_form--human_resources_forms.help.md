# Professional Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Reference Form is used to gather information about a job applicant's professional references, which will be used to evaluate their candidacy for a job opening.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand what is being asked.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
3. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and honest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company where you had the professional relationship.
* **Professional Reference** (`professional_reference`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you would like to provide a professional reference, or "No" if not.
* **Professional Relationship** (`professional_relationship`, select_one, optional): If you have checked "Yes" for Professional Reference, select the type of relationship you have with the reference (e.g., supervisor, colleague, mentor).
* **Professional Reference Email** (`professional_reference_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the professional reference.
* **Professional Reference Phone** (`professional_reference_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the professional reference.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): You can include any additional information about your professional relationship that may be helpful.
* **Professional Reference Comment** (`professional_reference_comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the professional reference.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): If you have provided a professional reference, rate their level of supportiveness (e.g., 1-5).

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any issues while filling out the form, please contact the hiring team for assistance.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you have not missed any important information.
