# Online Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Job Application Form is designed to collect information from job applicants for internal HR management purposes. This form helps track job applications, including contact details, work experience, education, and job references.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields as required, indicated by the asterisk (*) icon.
2. Enter your job title in the <job_title> field.
3. Describe your current job position in the <job_description> field.
4. Select your location from the <job_location> dropdown menu.
5. Enter your contact email in the <contact_email> field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the <contact_phone> field.
7. Attach your resume in the <resume> field.
8. Select the start and end dates of your job in the <job_start_date> and <job_end_date> fields, respectively.
9. Select the status of your job in the <job_status> field.
10. List your skills and experiences in the <job_skills> and <work_experience> fields, respectively.
11. Select your education level in the <education> dropdown menu.
12. Select your available work hours in the <work_hours> field.
13. Select your references for the job in the <job_reference1> to <job_reference10> fields.
14. Provide any additional notes about your application in the <job_note> field.
15. Select the status of your job application in the <job_application_status> field.
