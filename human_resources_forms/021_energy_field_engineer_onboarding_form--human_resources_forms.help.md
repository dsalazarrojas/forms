# Energy Field Engineer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Field Engineer Onboarding Form is a mandatory form for all new hires in the energy field. It is used to gather necessary information for onboarding and ensure that new employees have received the necessary training, certifications, and orientation to begin their work safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name, email, and work hours.
2. Acknowledge that you have received all necessary training and certifications for your position.
3. Provide your emergency contact information, including their name, phone number, relationship, and address.
4. Update your onboarding status and complete date if applicable.
5. Add any additional comments or notes from your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, text, required: false): Please fill in your personal details, including your name and email.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Safety Acknowledgments** (`safety_acknowledgments`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ I have received all of the necessary training and certifications for this position.
	+ I have completed all required safety courses.
	+ I have reviewed and understand the safety policies and procedures.
	+ I have received my safety orientation.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **First Name of Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of your emergency contact person.
* **Last Name of Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of your emergency contact person.
* **Phone Number of Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Address of Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, text, required: false): Fill in your work hours.
* **Onboarding Information** (`onboarding_information`, text, required: false): Provide any additional onboarding information.
* **Onboarding Start Date** (`onboarding_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you started your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding End Date** (`onboarding_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed your onboarding process.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, note, required: false): Allow your supervisor to leave comments or notes about your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Not Started
	+ In Progress
	+ Complete
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Complete Date** (`onboarding_complete_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed your onboarding process.
* **Supervisor Approvals** (`supervisor_approvals`, text, required: false): Allow your supervisor to leave comments or approvals about your onboarding process.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of your supervisor.
* **Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, note, required: false): Allow your supervisor to leave comments or notes about your onboarding process.
* **Status** (`supervisor_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Not Started
	+ In Progress
	+ Complete
* **Complete Date** (`supervisor_complete_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed your onboarding process.

## Tips
Please make sure to review each field carefully and answer all required fields before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative or supervisor.
