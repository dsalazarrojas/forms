# 360 Degree Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive evaluation of an employee's performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed the employee's work history, performance data, and any relevant feedback from colleagues or peers.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Section 1: Employee Information**
		+ Enter the employee's full name.
		+ Enter their work email address.
		+ Enter their department.
		+ Enter their job title.
		+ Enter the name of their immediate supervisor.
	* **Section 2: Performance Assessment**
		+ Rate their communication skills.
		+ Evaluate their teamwork and collaboration.
		+ Evaluate their problem-solving ability.
		+ Evaluate their leadership potential.
		+ Evaluate their time management.
		+ Evaluate their technical competency.
		+ Evaluate their innovation and creativity.
		+ Evaluate their attendance and punctuality.
	* **Section 3: Development and Growth**
		+ Select all areas where the employee needs improvement.
		+ Identify their key strengths.
		+ Set specific goals for their development.
		+ Specify their training or development needs.
3. In the "Additional Evaluator Comments" field, provide any other observations or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name of employee being evaluated**: This field is required to ensure accurate identification of the employee being evaluated.
* **Employee email address**: This field is used to contact the employee for any additional information or feedback.
* **Department**: This field indicates the employee's current department assignment.
* **Job title**: This field shows the employee's current position title.
* **Immediate supervisor name**: This field is used to identify the employee's direct manager.
* **Evaluation period start date**: This field is used to indicate the beginning of the review period.
* **Evaluation period end date**: This field indicates the end of the review period.
* **Years at company**: This field shows the employee's total tenure in years.
* **Communication skills rating**: This field is used to evaluate the employee's verbal and written communication effectiveness.
* **Teamwork and collaboration**: This field evaluates the employee's ability to work effectively with others.
* **Problem-solving ability**: This field assesses the employee's approach to identifying and resolving issues.
* **Leadership potential**: This field evaluates the employee's demonstration of leadership qualities.
* **Time management and organization**: This field assesses the employee's ability to prioritize and meet deadlines.
* **Technical competency**: This field evaluates the employee's knowledge and application of job-specific skills.
* **Innovation and creativity**: This field assesses the employee's ability to bring new ideas and approaches to work.
* **Attendance and punctuality**: This field evaluates the employee's reliability in showing up on time.
* **Areas for development (select all that apply)**: This field allows you to identify skills or competencies where the employee needs improvement.
* **Key strengths identified**: This field is used to note the employee's areas of excellence.
* **Specific improvement goals for next period**: This field allows you to set realistic goals for the employee's development.
* **Training or development needs**: This field specifies the training or development opportunities beneficial to the employee.
* **Overall performance rating**: This field rates the employee's overall performance.
* **Promotion recommendation**: This field assesses whether the employee is ready for advancement.
* **Additional evaluator comments**: This field provides space for any other observations or feedback.

## Tips
When completing this form, please be as objective and unbiased as possible. Focus on specific examples and behaviors, rather than general characteristics. Also, ensure that you have sufficient time to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
