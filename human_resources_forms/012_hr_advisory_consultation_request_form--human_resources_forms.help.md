# hr_advisory_consultation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hr_advisory_consultation_request_form is designed for employees to request time slots for meetings, training, or other HR-related activities. This form helps the HR department to manage employee availability and schedule appointments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First name** in the First name field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the Last name field.
3. Provide your **Email** in the Email field, following the standard email format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field, including the country code and any other relevant details.
5. Choose an appropriate **Appointment time** for your meeting or consultation from the provided time slots.
6. In the **Key details** field, provide any additional information about your consultation or meeting that would be helpful for the HR department to know.
7. Select your **Preferred time** for the meeting or consultation from the options provided. Note that you can choose multiple time slots if needed.
8. (Optional) Select additional time slots or options from the **Preferred time 2** to **Preferred time 19** fields as needed, following the same instructions as step 7.
