# Anti Harassment Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare your compliance with the organization's anti-harassment policy. It ensures that you understand the policy's requirements and agree to treat others with respect and dignity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully before starting.
2. For each field, choose the correct answer based on your understanding and experience.
3. Provide truthful and honest answers.
4. Save your work and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID as issued by the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to within the organization.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Direct Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of employment you have with the organization:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Seasonal
	+ Temporary
	+ Contract
* **Employment Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started working with the organization.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are declaring your compliance with this policy.
* **I have received and read the anti-harassment policy** (`policy_receipt_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - received and read
	+ Yes - reviewed summary
	+ No - have not received
	+ In process
* **I understand the anti-harassment policy** (`policy_understanding`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Completely understand
	+ Mostly understand
	+ Partially understand
	+ Do not understand
	+ Have questions
* **I understand what conduct is prohibited** (`prohibited_conduct_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - clearly understand
	+ Yes - generally understand
	+ Somewhat understand
	+ No - do not understand
* **I know how to report harassment or violations** (`reporting_procedures_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - clearly understand process
	+ Yes - generally understand
	+ Somewhat understand
	+ No - do not know procedure
* **I understand no retaliation for good faith reports** (`non_retaliation_understanding`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - fully understand
	+ Yes - generally understand
	+ Somewhat understand
	+ No - do not understand
* **I commit to treating others with respect and dignity** (`conduct_commitment`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Somewhat agree
	+ Do not agree
* **I acknowledge harassment is unacceptable and harmful** (`harassment_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - strongly agree
	+ Yes - agree
	+ Somewhat agree
	+ Disagree
* **I understand my responsibility to report violations** (`reporting_responsibility`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - clearly understand
	+ Yes - generally understand
	+ Somewhat understand
	+ No - do not understand
* **I understand my role as a bystander** (`bystander_responsibility`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - clearly understand
	+ Yes - generally understand
	+ Somewhat understand
	+ No - do not understand
* **Have I prior harassment or misconduct violations?** (`prior_violations`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Correct - no violations
	+ Not applicable - new employee
	+ Yes - prior violations exist
	+ Unsure
* **Details of prior violations** (`violation_details`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes - prior violations exist", enter the details of prior violations.
* **Do I have current workplace harassment concerns?** (`workplace_concerns`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Correct - no current concerns
	+ I have concerns
	+ I am unsure
* **Details of workplace concerns** (`concerns_details`, text, optional): If you selected "I have concerns", enter the details of workplace concerns.
* **I declare my compliance with this policy** (`policy_compliance_declaration`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ I comply and agree
	+ I have questions
	+ I do not agree
* **I have completed required anti-harassment training** (`training_completion`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes - completed
	+ Scheduled to complete
	+ Not yet required
	+ No - have not completed

## Tips

* Answer honestly and with care when completing this form.
* Review and ensure you understand each field before submitting your declaration.
* If you have questions or concerns, contact your Human Resources department.
* Save your work and submit the form when complete.
