# Strike Participation Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Strike Participation Time Off Form is used to record employee participation in strikes or other work stoppages. This form helps to track the duration and reason for the strike, as well as any relevant details about the strike's location and management's involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the start date of the strike in the "Strike Start Date" field.
3. Choose the end date of the strike in the "Strike End Date" field.
4. Enter the duration of the strike in the "Strike Duration" field. This can be expressed as a number of hours or days.
5. Enter a brief description of the reason for leaving or participating in the strike in the "Reason for Leaving" field.
6. Provide the location of the strike in the "Strike Location" field.
7. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
8. Enter the manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
9. Enter the manager's phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
10. Enter the employee's phone number in the "Employee Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the employee participating in the strike.
* **Strike Start Date** (date, required): Select the date when the strike began.
* **Strike End Date** (date, required): Select the date when the strike ended.
* **Strike Duration** (number, required): Enter the duration of the strike in hours or days.
* **Reason for Leaving** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for participating in the strike.
* **Strike Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the strike took place.
* **Manager Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the manager involved in the strike.
* **Manager Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the manager.
* **Manager Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the manager.
* **Employee Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the employee participating in the strike.
