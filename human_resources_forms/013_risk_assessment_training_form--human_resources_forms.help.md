# risk_assessment_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The risk assessment training form is designed to assess employee risk management training status. It is used to gather information on the risk management level, risk areas of concern, risk frequency, and training hours completed by employees to ensure they are adequately prepared to handle risks in their workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the respective fields.
2. Select the risk management level that best describes your current level of risk management knowledge.
3. Choose the risk areas that you are concerned with (e.g., physical, environmental, or cyber risks).
4. Select how often you are exposed to risks (e.g., often, occasionally, or rarely).
5. Enter your training hours completed.
6. Enter the date you completed the training.
7. Enter your assessment score out of 10.
8. Enter any comments or feedback on your assessment.
9. Select the status of your assessment (e.g., complete or incomplete).
10. Enter any comments on your assessment status.
11. Select the status of your training hours (e.g., complete, partially complete, or not started).
12. Enter any comments on your training hours status.
13. Enter the version of the risk assessment form you are using.
14. Enter the date the risk assessment form was last updated.
15. Enter who created the risk assessment form.
16. Enter who last updated the risk assessment form.
17. Enter the date the risk assessment form was last updated.
18. Enter any additional comments on the risk assessment form version.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Risk Manager Title** (`risk_manager_title`, required): Enter your title or position.
* **Risk Management Level** (`risk_management_level`, required): Select the level of risk management knowledge you have (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Risk Management Area** (`risk_management_area`, required): Select the areas of risk you are concerned with (e.g., physical, environmental, cyber).
* **Risk Management Frequency** (`risk_management_frequency`, required): Select how often you are exposed to risks (e.g., often, occasionally, rarely).
* **Risk Area Of Concern** (`risk_area_of_concern`, required): Select the area of risk you are concerned with (e.g., physical, environmental, cyber).
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, required): Enter a brief description of the risk you are concerned with.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, required): Enter a numerical value to describe your level of risk (e.g., 1-10).
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, required): Enter the number of hours of risk management training you have completed.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, required): Enter the date you completed the training.
* **Assessment Score** (`assessment_score`, required): Enter your assessment score out of 10.
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback on your assessment.
* **Assessment Status** (`assessment_status`, required): Select the status of your assessment (e.g., complete or incomplete).
* **Assessment Comment** (`assessment_comment`, optional): Enter any comments on your assessment status.
* **Training Hours Status** (`training_hours_status`, required): Select the status of your training hours (e.g., complete, partially complete, not started).
* **Training Hours Note** (`training_hours_note`, optional): Enter any notes on your training hours status.
* **Training Hours Comment** (`training_hours_comment`, optional): Enter any comments on your training hours status.
* **Risk Assessment Form Version** (`risk_assessment_form_version`, required): Enter the version of the risk assessment form you are using.
* **Risk Assessment Form Last Updated** (`risk_assessment_form_last_updated`, required): Enter the date the risk assessment form was last updated.
* **Risk Assessment Form Created By** (`risk_assessment_form_created_by`, optional): Enter who created the risk assessment form.
* **Risk Assessment Form Last Updated By** (`risk_assessment_form_last_updated_by`, optional): Enter who last updated the risk assessment form.
* **Risk Assessment Form Updated On** (`risk_assessment_form_updated_on`, required): Enter the date the risk assessment form was last updated.
* **Risk Assessment Form Version Note** (`risk_assessment_form_version_note`, optional): Enter any additional comments on the risk assessment form version.
* **Risk Assessment Form Version Last Updated** (`risk_assessment_form_version_last_updated`, optional): Enter the date the risk assessment form version was last updated.
* **Risk Assessment Form Version Created By** (`risk_assessment_form_version_created_by`, optional): Enter who created the risk assessment form version.
