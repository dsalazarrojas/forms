# Clinical Inventory Checklist And Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinical Inventory Checklist And Agreement Form is used to track clinical inventory and confirm employee agreement on equipment usage and care. This form helps to ensure that employees are aware of the equipment assigned to them, understand their responsibility for its care, and acknowledge the need to return equipment upon request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary equipment with you and are ready to confirm your agreement on its usage and care.
2. Fill in your employee details: `Employee Name` and `Employee ID`.
3. Select your department and position from the provided options.
4. Confirm the `Inventory Date` for the equipment being checked.
5. Indicate the `Inventory Type` for the equipment being checked (e.g., new hire, quarterly inventory, annual audit, etc.).
6. Answer the questions about each piece of equipment assigned to you, marking whether it is assigned, already possessed, not required, or requested.
7. If equipment is assigned, describe its `Device Serial Number` and list the `Keys and Access Cards` assigned with you.
8. Acknowledge the `Uniform Allowance Processed` for the current month or year.
9. Document any additional equipment assigned to you in the `Additional Equipment Assigned` field.
10. Record any `Equipment Condition Notes` about existing damage or issues.
11. Confirm any `Items Needing Replacement` due to damage or wear.
12. Sign and date the `Employee Agreement Statement` to acknowledge receipt and proper usage of equipment.
13. Confirm your understanding of `I Understand My Responsibility for Equipment Care` and `I Understand Equipment Must Be Returned Upon Request`.
14. Sign your name and date the `Employee Signature` field.
15. Sign and date the `Supervisor Signature` field to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel documentation.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number as it appears on your personnel documentation.
* **Department**: Select your department from the provided options.
* **Position or Role**: Enter your position or role in the department.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Inventory Date**: Enter the date of the current inventory check.
* **Inventory Type**: Select the type of inventory being checked (e.g., new hire, quarterly, annual, etc.).
* **Stethoscope**: Answer whether the stethoscope is assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **Stethoscope Condition**: If assigned, describe the condition of the stethoscope.
* **Blood Pressure Cuff**: Answer whether the blood pressure cuff is assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **Penlight**: Answer whether the penlight is assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **Scissors or Trauma Shears**: Answer whether the scissors or trauma shears are assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **Watch with Second Hand**: Answer whether the watch with second hand is assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **Badge Holder and ID**: Answer whether the badge holder and ID are assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **PPE Kit**: Answer whether the PPE kit is assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **Communication Device**: Answer whether the communication device is assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **Laptop or Tablet**: Answer whether the laptop or tablet is assigned, already have, not required, or requested.
* **Device Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the assigned equipment if applicable.
* **Keys and Access Cards**: List any keys and access cards assigned with you.
* **Uniform Allowance Processed**: Confirm whether the uniform allowance has been processed for the current month or year.
* **Uniform Sizes**: Enter your uniform sizes if applicable.
* **Additional Equipment Assigned**: List any other equipment assigned to you.
* **Equipment Condition Notes**: Document any existing damage or issues with the equipment.
* **Items Needing Replacement**: Confirm any items that need replacement due to damage or wear.
* **Employee Agreement Statement**: Sign and date the employee agreement statement to acknowledge receipt and proper usage of equipment.
* **I Understand My Responsibility for Equipment Care**: Confirm that you understand your responsibility for equipment care.
* **I Understand Equipment Must Be Returned Upon Request**: Confirm that you understand that equipment must be returned upon request.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign your name as it appears on your personnel documentation.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the employee signature.
* **Supervisor Signature**: Sign and date your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Signature Date**: Enter the date of the supervisor's signature.

Note that this form should be reviewed and signed by both the employee and supervisor to ensure understanding and agreement on equipment usage and care.
