# Annual Review (Direct Report Feedback) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on their direct report's performance and management style. This feedback will be used to identify strengths and areas for improvement and to create a positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the prompts and select the appropriate options for each question. Make sure to be specific and honest in your feedback, and focus on examples and behaviors exhibited by your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Your Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required): Please enter your work email address.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your direct report's manager or supervisor.
* **Your Position Title** (`position_title`, `text`, required): Please enter your current role or position title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Please enter your department or work area.
* **How long have you worked under this manager** (`time_under_manager`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you have worked under this manager, using one of the following options:
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **Overall, how would you rate your manager** (`overall_manager_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your manager's performance using one of the following options:
	+ Excellent - significantly exceeds expectations
	+ Good - meets expectations very well
	+ Satisfactory - meets basic expectations
	+ Needs improvement - below expectations
	+ Poor - significantly below expectations
* **How effective is your manager at communication** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Select how effective you find your manager's communication, using one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs improvement
	+ Poor
* **Does your manager set clear expectations** (`clear_expectations`, `select_one`, required): Select how clearly you think your manager sets expectations, using one of the following options:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **Quality of feedback received from manager** (`feedback_quality`, `select_one`, required): Select the quality of feedback you receive from your manager, using one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs improvement
	+ Poor
* **How often do you receive feedback** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you receive feedback from your manager, using one of the following options:
	+ Regularly - at least monthly
	+ Occasionally - quarterly
	+ Rarely - only annually
	+ Never
	+ Too frequently
* **Manager recognizes and appreciates my work** (`recognition_appreciation`, `select_one`, required): Select how much you agree that your manager recognizes and appreciates your work, using one of the following options:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **Manager supports my career development** (`career_development`, `select_one`, required): Select how much you agree that your manager supports your career development, using one of the following options:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **How much have you grown professionally under this manager** (`professional_growth`, `select_one`, required): Select how much you have grown professionally under this manager, using one of the following options:
	+ Significantly
	+ Moderately
	+ Somewhat
	+ Not much
	+ Declined
* **I trust my manager and value their integrity** (`trust_integrity`, `select_one`, required): Select how much you trust your manager and value their integrity, using one of the following options:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **Manager supports me during challenges** (`support_in_challenges`, `select_one`, required): Select how much you agree that your manager supports you during challenges, using one of the following options:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **Manager's greatest strengths** (`manager_strengths`, `text`, required): Please enter your manager's greatest strengths.
* **Areas for your manager's improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, required): Please enter areas where your manager needs to improve.
* **Specific examples of effective management** (`specific_examples`, `text`, optional): Provide specific examples of effective management behaviors or situations where your manager was particularly helpful.
* **Manager creates a positive work environment** (`workplace_environment`, `select_one`, required): Select how much you agree that your manager creates a positive work environment, using one of the following options:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **Likelihood to stay under this manager** (`retention_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Select how likely you are to stay under this manager, using one of the following options:
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Undecided
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very unlikely
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
