# HR Digital Training Academy Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees who are interested in participating in the HR Digital Training Academy. It will help the academy to better understand their training requirements and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with accurate and clear information. Make sure to provide your correct first name, last name, and email address. You can also add a phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
- Select the training interest that best suits your needs from the list of options.
- Choose the training programs you are interested in. You can select multiple programs if you are interested in more than one.
- If you have any additional comments or information to provide, use the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Training Interest** (`training_interest`, select_one, required): Select the training interest that best suits your needs. You can choose only one interest from the list of options.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_multiple, required): Select the training programs you are interested in. You can select multiple programs from the list of options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or information to provide, use this field.
