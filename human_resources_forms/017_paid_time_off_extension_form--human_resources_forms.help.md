# Paid Time Off Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paid Time Off Extension Form is used by employees to request an extension of a previously approved paid time off period. This form helps employees to provide the necessary information to process their request and ensure it aligns with company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Determine whether you need to request an extension to a previously approved paid time off period.
2. Select the option "Yes" from the "Employee Requesting Extension" field if this is the case.
3. Provide the reason for the extension request in the "Reason For Extension" field.
4. Select the start date and end date for the extension period in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
5. Indicate the number of days you are requesting for the extension period in the "Number of Days" field.
6. Select "Yes" from the "Manager Approval" field if you have obtained approval from your manager.
7. Select "Yes" from the "Employee Confirmation" field if you confirm that you have obtained all necessary department approvals.
8. Select all departments that you have obtained approval from in the "Department Approvals" field.
9. Select "Yes" from the "Manager Confirmation" field if your manager has confirmed the extension.
10. Enter the date of the manager's approval in the "Date Of Approval" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Requesting Extension** (`employee_requesting_extension`, Select One, Required):
    This field asks whether you are requesting an extension to a previously approved paid time off period.
* **Reason For Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, Text, Required):
    This field is where you can provide the reason for requesting an extension to your paid time off period.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Required):
    Enter the desired start date of the extended paid time off period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Required):
    Enter the desired end date of the extended paid time off period.
* **Number of Days** (`number_of_days`, Number, Required):
    Enter the number of days you are requesting for the extension period.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, Select One, Required):
    Select "Yes" if you have obtained approval from your manager for the extension.
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, Select One, Required):
    Select "Yes" if you have obtained all necessary department approvals for the extension.
* **Department Approvals** (`department_approvals`, Select Multiple, Required):
    Select all departments that you have obtained approval from for the extension.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, Select One, Required):
    Select "Yes" if your manager has confirmed the extension.
* **Date Of Approval** (`date_of_approval`, Date, Required):
    Enter the date when your manager approved the extension.
