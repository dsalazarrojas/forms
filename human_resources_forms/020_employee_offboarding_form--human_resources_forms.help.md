# employee_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees when they are offboarding from the company. This information will help the HR department to process the employee's departure and ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee information, including your name and contact details.
2. Select the reason for your offboarding, such as Termination, Job change, or Other.
3. Enter the date of your last working day.
4. Enter the date of your employment start.
5. Select the manager's information, if applicable.
6. Choose the company equipment you are returning, such as Laptop, Phone, or Other.
7. Choose the company property you are returning, such as Desk, Chair, or Other.
8. Select your benefits status, if applicable.
9. Enter the date of your benefits end.
10. Enter the date of your benefits start.
11. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (Page 1):
	+ **Employee Name** (Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
	+ **Employee Email** (Text): Enter your company email address.
* **Offboarding Reason** (Page 2):
	+ **Termination** (Select One): If you are leaving due to termination, select this option.
	+ **Job Change** (Select One): If you are leaving for a job change, select this option.
	+ **Contract Ended** (Select One): If your contract ended, select this option.
	+ **Other** (Select One): If your reason for leaving is not listed above, select this option.
* **Date Last Worked** (Page 2):
	+ Enter the date of your last working day in MM/dd/yyyy format.
* **Date Employment Began** (Page 2):
	+ Enter the date of your employment start in MM/dd/yyyy format.
* **Manager Information** (Page 3):
	+ **John Doe** (Select Multiple): If your manager is John Doe, select this option.
	+ **Jane Doe** (Select Multiple): If your manager is Jane Doe, select this option.
	+ **Other** (Select Multiple): If your manager is not listed above, select this option.
* **Company Equipment** (Page 3):
	+ **Laptop** (Select Multiple): If you are returning a company laptop, select this option.
	+ **Phone** (Select Multiple): If you are returning a company phone, select this option.
	+ **Other** (Select Multiple): If you are returning other company equipment, select this option.
* **Company Property** (Page 4):
	+ **Desk** (Select Multiple): If you are returning a company desk, select this option.
	+ **Chair** (Select Multiple): If you are returning a company chair, select this option.
	+ **Other** (Select Multiple): If you are returning other company property, select this option.
* **Benefits Status** (Page 5):
	+ **Active** (Select Multiple): If your benefits are active, select this option.
	+ **Inactive** (Select Multiple): If your benefits are inactive, select this option.
	+ **Unknown** (Select Multiple): If you are not sure about your benefits status, select this option.
* **Benefits End Date** (Page 6):
	+ Enter the date of your benefits end in MM/dd/yyyy format.
* **Benefits Start Date** (Page 6):
	+ Enter the date of your benefits start in MM/dd/yyyy format.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the HR department for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the HR department directly.
