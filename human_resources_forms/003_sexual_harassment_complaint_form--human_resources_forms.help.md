# Sexual Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents of sexual harassment in the workplace. It is intended to be used by employees to report and document incidents in a confidential and safe environment. The information collected through this form will be used to investigate and address the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date" field.
3. Indicate who reported the incident (you or a manager) in the "Reported By" field.
4. Provide the name of the perpetrator in the "Perpetrator's Name" field.
5. Enter the perpetrator's title or position in the "Perpetrator's Title" field.
6. Describe the location where the incident occurred in the "Location" field.
7. List any witnesses in the "Witness List" field.
8. Indicate to whom the incident was reported in the "Reported To" field.
9. If applicable, provide additional witness information in the "Witness 1", "Witness 2", and "Witness 3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Please provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details such as what happened, where it happened, and any witnesses involved.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Employee: You, as the employee experiencing the incident.
	+ Manager: Your manager or supervisor.
	+ Other: If you select this option, please provide the name of the person you reported the incident to in the "Reported To" field.
* **Perpetrator's Name** (`perpetrator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the incident.
* **Perpetrator's Title** (`perpetrator_title`, text, required): Enter the title or position of the perpetrator.
* **Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Witness List** (`witness_list`, text, required): List any individuals who witnessed the incident.
* **Reported To** (`reported_to`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ HR: Report the incident to the Human Resources department.
	+ Manager: Report the incident to your manager or supervisor.
	+ Other: If you select this option, please enter the name of the person you reported the incident to.
* **Witness 1**, **Witness 2**, **Witness 3** (`witness_1`, `witness_2`, `witness_3`, text, required): If there were multiple witnesses, enter their names and any relevant details about their roles or involvement in the incident.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form.
