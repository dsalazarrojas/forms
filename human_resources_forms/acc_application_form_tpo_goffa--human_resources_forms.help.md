# ACC Application Form (TPO Goffa) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ACC Application Form (TPO Goffa) is used to collect information about your personal and professional details, as well as feedback about your experience with the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your full name, email address, phone number, department, job title, and date of hire.
3. Select the correct options for your current skills, remote work status, and satisfaction with the ACC Application Form (TPO Goffa).
4. Rate your work-life balance and provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, \*): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, \*): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, ): Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Department** (`department`, text, \*): Enter the department you are working in, e.g., Marketing, Finance, Engineering.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, \*): Enter your official position title.
* **Date of hire** (`hire_date`, date, ): Enter the date you were hired in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Years at organization** (`years_employed`, integer, ): Enter the number of years you have been working at the organization.
* **Current annual salary** (`salary`, decimal, ): Enter your current annual salary in USD.
* **Typical work hours per week** (`work_hours_per_week`, integer, ): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Primary work location** (`work_location`, text, ): Enter the city and office name where you work.
* **Are you a remote worker?** (`is_remote_worker`, select_one, ): Select 'True' if you work from home.
* **Remote work days per week** (`remote_work_days_per_week`, integer, ): Enter the number of days you work remotely per week.
* **Current professional skills** (`current_skills`, select_multiple, ): Select all the skills you possess from the list.
* **Section header: Feedback & Satisfaction** (`section_header_feedback`, note, ): This section is for feedback and satisfaction questions.
* **How satisfied are you with the ACC Application Form (TPO Goffa)?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, \*): Choose the option that best describes your satisfaction.
* **How would you rate communication from management?** (`management_rating`, select_one, \*): Choose the option that best describes your experience with management communication.
* **Do you feel supported in professional development?** (`growth_opportunities`, select_one, \*): Choose the option that best describes your agreement.
* **Rate your work-life balance** (`work_life_balance`, integer, \*): Rate your work-life balance from 1 (very poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **What could improve your experience at work?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, ): Provide specific suggestions or ideas to improve your experience.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, ): Any other feedback you wish to share.
