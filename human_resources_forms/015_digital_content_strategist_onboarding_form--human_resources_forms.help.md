# Digital Content Strategist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to support the onboarding process for a Digital Content Strategist. The collected information will be used to ensure a smooth onboarding process for the individual being on-boarded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For the `Team Leads` field, select all applicable team leads involved in the project.
3. For the `Content Strategist Readiness` field, choose the level of readiness from 1 to 3.
4. In the `Readiness Comments` field, provide any additional comments or explanations for the readiness level chosen.
5. In the `Project Notes` field, provide any relevant project details.
6. In the `Manager Name`, `Manager Email`, and `Team Notes` fields, provide the necessary information about the project's manager and team.
7. Choose the correct `Onboarding Status` from the options provided.
8. Enter the `Onboarding Comments` in the dedicated field.
9. Set the `Review Date`, `Review Time`, `Next Review Date`, and `Next Review Time` fields according to the project schedule.
10. Finally, set the `Onboarding Date` based on the project's onboarding start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the first name of the Digital Content Strategist being on-boarded.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the last name of the Digital Content Strategist being on-boarded.

### Email
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the Digital Content Strategist being on-boarded.

### Phone
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the Digital Content Strategist being on-boarded.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (Text, Required): Enter the job title of the Digital Content Strategist being on-boarded.

### Agency
* **Agency** (Text, Required): Enter the agency that the Digital Content Strategist works for.

### Department
* **Department** (Text, Required): Enter the department of the Digital Content Strategist.

### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the manager of the Digital Content Strategist being on-boarded.

### Manager Email
* **Manager Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the manager of the Digital Content Strategist being on-boarded.

### Team Leads
* **Team Leads** (Multiple Selection, Required): Select all team leads involved in the project.

### Project Details
* **Project Details** (Text, Required): Enter any relevant project details.

### Content Strategist Readiness
* **Content Strategist Readiness** (Selection, Required): Choose the level of readiness from 1 to 3.

### Readiness Comments

### Team Notes
