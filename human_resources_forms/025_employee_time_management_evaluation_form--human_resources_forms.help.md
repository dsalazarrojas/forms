# Employee Time Management Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Time Management Evaluation Form is used to collect information about an employee's work hours, overtime, and time management skills from their manager. This form helps managers evaluate an employee's work performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the number of work hours the employee worked last week and last month in the "Work Hours Last Week" and "Work Hours Last Month" fields.
3. Select the overtime hours worked last week and last month from the dropdown menus.
4. Rate the employee's work hours last week from "High", "Medium", or "Low" in the "Work Hours Last Week Highest" and "Work Hours Last Week Lowest" dropdown menus.
5. Select the employee's time management skills level from "High", "Medium", or "Low" in the "Time Management Skills" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the employee's productivity level as a number.
7. Enter any additional comments in the "Evaluation Form Comments" field.
8. Enter the employee manager's name, title, and email in the respective fields.
9. Select the form completion status and submit date in the "Form Completed By" and "Date Submitted" fields.
10. Enter the manager's phone number and extension in the "Manager Phone" and "Manager Extension" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Work Hours Last Week** (`work_hours_last_week`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours the employee worked last week.
* **Work Hours Last Month** (`work_hours_last_month`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours the employee worked last month.
* **Overtime Last Week** (`overtime_last_week`, `number`, required): Enter the number of overtime hours worked last week.
* **Overtime Last Month** (`overtime_last_month`, `number`, required): Enter the number of overtime hours worked last month.
* **Work Hours Last Week Highest** (`work_hours_last_week_highest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the highest and lowest work hours the employee worked last week.
* **Work Hours Last Week Lowest** (`work_hours_last_week_lowest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the lowest work hours the employee worked last week.
* **Work Hours Last Week Average** (`work_hours_last_week_average`, `number`, required): Enter the average work hours the employee worked last week.
* **Time Management Skills** (`time_management_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select the employee's time management skills level.
* **Productivity** (`productivity`, `number`, required): Enter the employee's productivity level.
* **Evaluation Form Comments** (`evaluation_form_comments`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Employee Manager Name** (`employee_manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee manager's name.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's name.
* **Form Completed By** (`form_completed_by`, `select_one`, required): Select who completed the form.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required): Select the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Enter who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `time`, required): Select the time the form was submitted.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's title.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required): Enter the manager's email.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Extension** (`manager_extension`, `number`, required): Enter the manager's extension.
* **Manager Title 1** (`manager_title_1`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's title.
* **Manager Title 2** (`manager_title_2`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's title.
