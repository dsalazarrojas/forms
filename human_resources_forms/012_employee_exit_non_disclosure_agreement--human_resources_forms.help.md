# Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Exit Non Disclosure Agreement is a form designed to ensure a departing employee understands their non-disclosure obligations after leaving the company. This form is used to gather acknowledgments and confirmations from the employee, formalizing their agreement to maintain confidentiality.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please provide the following information:
	* Your employee details in the "Employee Details" field (page 1)
	* A statement acknowledging your non-disclosure obligations in the "Non Disclosure Acknowledgments" field (page 2)
	* The date you sign this agreement in the "Signature Date" field (page 3)
	* A signature from your manager in the "Manager Signature" field (page 4)
	* A signature from HR in the "HR Signature" field (page 5)
	* The date you confirm your non-disclosure acknowledgments in the "Acknowledgments Date" field (page 6)
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details (Employee Details, Text, Optional)**: Please provide your name, job title, and any other relevant information as per company policy.
* **Non Disclosure Acknowledgments (Non Disclosure Acknowledgments, Text, Optional)**: Acknowledge that you understand and agree to maintain confidentiality of company information and trade secrets after your departure.
* **Signature Date (Signature Date, Date, Optional)**: Enter the date you sign this agreement.
* **Manager Signature (Manager Signature, Text, Optional)**: A signature from your manager confirming their understanding of your non-disclosure obligations.
* **HR Signature (HR Signature, Text, Optional)**: A signature from HR confirming their understanding of your non-disclosure acknowledgments.
* **Acknowledgments Date (Acknowledgments Date, Date, Optional)**: The date you confirm your non-disclosure acknowledgments.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully and sign it only after you have understood the terms of the agreement.
* Ensure your manager and HR review and sign the form as well.
* Keep the signed form for your records, as it serves as proof of your agreement to maintain confidentiality.
