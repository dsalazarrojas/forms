# Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance assessment form is used to evaluate an employee's progress over a specified period, providing a structured and objective basis for identifying strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the performance criteria that the employee has demonstrated over the assessment period. Choose one or multiple criteria from the provided list.
2.  Choose the assessment period over which the employee's performance will be evaluated.
3.  Assess the employee's performance using the selected criteria, selecting either "Satisfactory" or "Needs Improvement."
4.  If necessary, provide feedback to the manager and/or Head of Department (HOD) with specific comments.
5.  Add any additional comments about the employee's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Performance Criteria** (`performance_criteria`, select_one): This field allows you to select the specific performance criteria that the employee has demonstrated over the assessment period. Choose one or multiple criteria from the list to evaluate the employee's performance.
*   **Assessment Period** (`assessment_period`, select_one): Select the period over which the employee's performance will be evaluated.
*   **Employee Performance** (`employee_performance`, select_multiple): Evaluate the employee's performance using the selected criteria, choosing "Satisfactory" or "Needs Improvement."
*   **Feedback to Manager** (`feedback_to_manager`, text): Provide feedback to the employee's manager with specific comments about their performance.
*   **Feedback to HOD** (`feedback_to_hod`, text): Provide feedback to the HOD with specific comments about the employee's performance.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Add any additional comments about the employee's performance that are not covered by the other fields.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the most relevant performance criteria to ensure a fair evaluation.
*   Clearly provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your assessment of the employee's performance.
*   Be objective and constructive in your feedback, focusing on specific behaviors or actions rather than general traits.
*   Use clear and concise language when providing feedback and additional comments.
*   Review and revise your assessment and feedback regularly to ensure it is accurate and fair.
