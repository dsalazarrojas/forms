# Facebook Ad Marketer Recruitment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from business owners about their Facebook ad marketing requirements, including their budget allocation, marketing goals, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (fields marked as required).
3. For select options, choose the best fit for your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Owners Criteria**: This field is optional and allows you to describe your business's specific criteria for selecting ad marketers. Please provide any relevant information that might help us understand your business's needs.
* **Budget**: This field is optional and allows you to input a budget for your ad marketing efforts. Please note that this is not a required field.
* **Ad Budget**: This field is optional and allows you to input a specific ad budget. Please note that this is not a required field.
* **Ad Marketing Budget**: This field is optional and allows you to input a specific ad marketing budget. Please note that this is not a required field.
* **Ad Marketing Goals**: Choose one or multiple goals for your ad marketing efforts (Increase sales, Increase brand awareness, Increase website traffic).
* **Desired Duration**: Choose one or multiple desired duration for your ad marketing efforts (Short term, Medium term, Long term).
* **Ad Frequency**: Choose one or multiple ad frequency for your ad marketing efforts (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Ad Budget Allocation**: Choose one or multiple ad budget allocation for your ad marketing efforts (Search, Social Media, Email).
* **Target Audience**: Choose one or multiple target audience for your ad marketing efforts (Demographics, Interests, Behaviors).
* **Ad Marketing Channels**: Choose one or multiple ad marketing channels for your ad marketing efforts (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
* **Ad Frequency**: This field is optional and represents the ad frequency (date) for your ad marketing efforts. Please note that this is not a required field.
* **Ad Budget Freqeuency**: This field is optional and represents the ad budget frequency (time) for your ad marketing efforts. Please note that this is not a required field.
* **Ad Budget Freqeuency Days**: This field is optional and represents the ad budget frequency days for your ad marketing efforts. Please note that this is not a required field.
* **Ad Start Date**: This field is optional and represents the start date for your ad marketing efforts. Please note that this is not a required field.
* **Ad End Date**: This field is optional and represents the end date for your ad marketing efforts. Please note that this is not a required field.
* **Notes**: This field is optional and allows you to input any additional notes or comments about your ad marketing efforts.
* **Email**: This field is optional and allows you to input your email address.

Please note that all fields marked as optional are not required, and you can choose to fill them out if they are relevant to your ad marketing requirements.
