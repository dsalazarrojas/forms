# Employee Christmas Gift Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Employee Christmas Gift Form is used to collect information about an employee's gift and bonus preferences for the Christmas season. It helps us to ensure that employees receive a gift that suits their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the requested information for the employee.
2. Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately, as this information will be used to determine the employee's gift and bonus preferences.
3. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their company ID card or personnel file.
* **Gift Suggestion** (`gift_suggestion`, optional): If you have a specific gift idea in mind, please type it in here. This will help us to better understand the employee's preferences.
* **Department** (`department`, required): Enter the employee's department name as it appears on their company ID card or personnel file.
* **Bonus Preferred** (`bonus_preferred`, optional): If the employee has a preferred bonus frequency, please select one of the options below.
* **Gift Category** (`gift_category`, required): Choose a gift category that suits the employee's interests, such as "Monetary", "Gift Card", "Gift Basket", or "Other".
* **Gift Value** (`gift_value`, required): Enter the amount or value of the gift you would like to give.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter the employee's email address as it appears on their company ID card or personnel file.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter the employee's phone number as it appears on their company ID card or personnel file.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter the employee's date of birth.
* **Holiday Hours Worked** (`holiday_hours_worked`, required): Choose the number of holiday hours the employee has worked this season.
* **Bonus Frequency** (`bonus_frequency`, required): Choose how often the employee would like to receive bonuses.
* **Note** (`note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about the employee, please type them in here.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, required): Choose who submitted this form, either the HR Manager, Team Leader, or the Employee.
* **Form Submitted Date** (`form_submitted_date`, required): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted Time** (`form_submitted_time`, required): Enter the time this form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted Hour** (`form_submitted_hour`, required): Enter the hour this form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted Second** (`form_submitted_second`, required): Enter the second this form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted AM/PM** (`form_submitted_am_pm`, required): Enter AM or PM for the time submitted.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, required): Choose the employee's status, either Active, Inactive, or Retired.
* **Departmental Bonus** (`departmental_bonus`, required): Enter the amount of the departmental bonus.
* **Departmental Bonus Frequency** (`departmental_bonus_frequency`, required): Choose how often the departmental bonus is paid.
* **Departmental Bonus Amount** (`departmental_bonus_amount`, required): Enter the amount of the departmental bonus.
* **Form Submitted By Abbreviation** (`form_submitted_by_abbreviation`, optional): If you want to enter an abbreviation for the person who submitted this form, please type it in.
* **Form Submitted By Title** (`form_submitted_by_title`, optional): If you want to enter a title for the person who submitted this form, please type it in.
