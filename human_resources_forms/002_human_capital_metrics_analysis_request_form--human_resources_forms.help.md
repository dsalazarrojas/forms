# Human Capital Metrics Analysis Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request human capital metrics analysis for an employee. It gathers data about the employee's performance and provides insights to aid in making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill in the employee's ID, which is a unique number assigned to each employee.
*   Select the department where the employee belongs to ensure that the analysis is done correctly.
*   Fill in the specific metrics required for the analysis:
    *   Number of employees in the team.
    *   Average salary of the team.
    *   Percentage of employees on leave.
    *   Date and time of this analysis.
    *   Email address and phone number of the employee.
    *   Number of years of service.
    *   Number of certifications achieved.
    *   Number of awards received.
    *   Overall rating of the employee.
*   Add any additional notes about the employee, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is the employee ID?** (`employee_id`, number, required): This is a unique number assigned to each employee. Please ensure it's accurate and correctly filled out.
*   **What is the department the employee belongs to?** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select the department where the employee works to ensure the analysis is done correctly.
*   **What is the number of employees in the team?** (`metric_1`, number, required): This field is used to gather information about the team size.
*   **What is the average salary of the team?** (`metric_2`, number, required): This field is used to gather information about the average salary of the team.
*   **What is the percentage of employees on leave?** (`metric_3`, number, required): This field is used to gather information about the number of employees on leave.
*   **What is the date of this analysis?** (`date`, date, required): This field is used to track when the analysis was done.
*   **What is the time of this analysis?** (`time`, time, required): This field is used to track the time of the analysis.
*   **What is the email address of the employee?** (`email`, email, required): This field is used for communication purposes.
*   **What is the phone number of the employee?** (`phone`, text, required): This field is used for communication purposes.
*   **What is the number of years of service?** (`metric_7`, number, required): This field is used to gather information about the employee's years of service.
*   **What is the number of certifications achieved?** (`metric_8`, number, required): This field is used to gather information about the certifications achieved by the employee.
*   **What is the number of awards received?** (`metric_9`, number, required): This field is used to gather information about the awards received by the employee.
*   **What is the overall rating of the employee?** (`metric_10`, number, required): This field is used to gather information about the employee's overall performance.
*   **Is there any additional note about the employee?** (`note`, note, required): This field is used to add any additional information about the employee.

## Tips

*   Always ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out this form.
*   Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
*   If you are unsure about any part of this form, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
