# Religious Exemption For Covid Vaccine Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a religious exemption from the COVID-19 vaccination. If you are an employee who, due to religious reasons, cannot receive the COVID-19 vaccine, use this form to submit your exemption request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
2. For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank if you do not have the information or if they are not relevant to your exemption request.
3. Make sure to sign the form with your employee signature, supervisor signature, and religious leader signature (if applicable) to confirm that you have submitted this form voluntarily and without coercion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`1`): This field is required and should be filled with your employee ID number.
* **Reason For Exemption** (`2`): Please provide a clear and concise reason for requesting a religious exemption from the COVID-19 vaccination.
* **Exemption Date** (`3`): If you have a specific date when you would like to be exempt from the vaccination, please provide it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Religious Organization** (`4`): If you are a member of a religious organization, please provide its name and address (if applicable).
* **Employee Signature** (`5`): This is your official signature as an employee, confirming that you have submitted this form voluntarily.
* **Date Of Birth** (`6`): This field is not required but can be useful for verification purposes.
* **Employee Phone** (`7`): This field is not required but can be useful for communication purposes.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`8`): This is your supervisor's signature, confirming that they are aware of your exemption request and have reviewed the form.
* **Religious Leader Signature** (`9`): This is the signature of your religious leader, confirming their agreement with your exemption request (if applicable).
* **Reason For Refusal** (`10`): Please provide a clear and concise reason for refusing the COVID-19 vaccination, if applicable.
* **Religious Belief Statement** (`11`): If you have a specific statement regarding your religious beliefs that support your exemption request, please provide it.
* **Exemption Request Date** (`12`): This field is not required but can be useful for tracking purposes.
* **Religious Discrimination Statement** (`13`): This field is not required but can be useful for addressing any potential concerns about discrimination.
* **Employee Agreement** (`14`): Please confirm that you understand and agree with the terms of your exemption.
* **Religious Discrimination Concern** (`15`): Please confirm that you do not have any concerns about religious discrimination.
* **Religious Leader Agreement** (`16`): Please confirm that your religious leader agrees with your exemption request.
* **Religious Organization Address** (`17`): This field is not required but can be useful for verification purposes.
* **Date Request Received** (`18`): This field is automatically populated with the date you submit this form.
* **Employee Name** (`19`): This is your name as an employee.
* **Employee ID Number** (`20`): This is your employee ID number.
* **Supervisor Name** (`21`): This is your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor ID Number** (`22`): This is your supervisor's ID number.
* **Religious Leader Name** (`23`): If you are requesting a religious exemption, please provide your religious leader's name.
* **Religious Leader ID Number** (`24`): If you are requesting a religious exemption, please provide your religious leader's ID number.
* **Date Completed** (`25`): This field is automatically populated with the date you submit this form.

Please note that this form is subject to review and approval by your supervisor and religious leader (if applicable). Your exemption request will be processed based on your provided information and the relevant company policies and procedures.
