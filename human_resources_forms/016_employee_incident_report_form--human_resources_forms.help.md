# Employee Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that may have caused harm, injury, or near-miss situations. It collects information about the employee's name, a description of the incident, the type of incident, the date and time it occurred, the location where it happened, and the name and contact information of the supervisor involved.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form completely and accurately, making sure all required fields are completed.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- Please note that the fields marked as 'required' are necessary to submit the form.
- If you need to report an incident that occurred in the past, you can complete the form with the relevant information.
- If the incident is ongoing, please provide an update once it is resolved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge or official records.

### Incident Description
* **incident_description** (`incident_description`, `text`, optional): Describe what happened during the incident. Be as detailed as possible, including any witnesses or contributing factors.

### Report Type
* **Report Type** (`report_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of incident that best describes what happened:
	+ Hazard
	+ Near Miss
	+ Incident

### Incident Date
* **incident_date** (`incident_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.

### Incident Time
* **incident_time** (`incident_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.

### Incident Location
* **incident_location** (`incident_location`, `text`, optional): Describe the location where the incident happened, including any relevant details about the location.

### Supervisor Name
* **supervisor_name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor or the person responsible for reporting incidents.

### Supervisor Email
* **supervisor_email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your supervisor or the person responsible for reporting incidents.
Note: Ensure that the email address is in a standard format (e.g. [email@domain.com](mailto:email@domain.com)).

Please submit the form once you have completed all the required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
