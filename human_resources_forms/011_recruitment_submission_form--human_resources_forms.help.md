# Recruitment Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Submission Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about job openings, including job title, description, work experience, education, and other relevant details. This form helps HR or recruitment teams streamline the hiring process and ensure consistency in the data collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their purpose.
2. Enter your information in the required fields, marked with a **(Required)** symbol.
3. For select-one fields, choose the relevant option that best describes your job posting.
4. For text fields, enter your response in the provided text box.
5. Ensure that you've reviewed and verified the information before submitting the form.

## Tips

* Ensure that you've reviewed and verified the information before submitting the form.
* Avoid submitting duplicate entries for the same job posting.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the HR or recruitment team for clarification.
* If you've made an error in the form, do not hesitate to request a correction or deletion of the submission.
