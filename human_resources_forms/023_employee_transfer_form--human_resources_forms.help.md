# Employee Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate employee transfers, allowing for the collection of relevant information and ensuring that all stakeholders are informed throughout the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Select the department the employee is currently in in the **Department Name** field.
3. Enter the date of the transfer in the **Transfer Date** field.
4. Provide a reason for the transfer in the **Reason For Transfer** field.
5. If the transfer is to another department within the organization, select **Internal** in the **Transfer Type** field. If the transfer is to an external organization, select **External**.
6. Select whether the manager approves or rejects the transfer in the **Manager Approval** field.
7. Enter any comments from the manager in the **Manager Comments** field.
8. Enter the department ID of the new department in the **New Department ID** field.
9. Select the current status of the employee in the **Employee Status** field.
10. Select the status of the transfer in the **Transfer Status** field.
11. Enter the date of the second transfer in the **Transfer Date 2** field.
12. Enter a reason for the second transfer in the **Transfer Reason** field.
13. Enter any comments from the manager in the **Manager Notes** field.
14. Enter any notes for the department in the **Department Notes** field.
15. Enter a reason for the change in the **Reason For Change** field.
16. Enter the date of the change in the **Date Of Change** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being transferred.
* **Department Name**: Select the department the employee is currently in.
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Reason For Transfer**: Provide a reason for the transfer.
* **Transfer Type**: Select whether the transfer is internal or external.
* **Manager Approval**: Select whether the manager approves or rejects the transfer.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from the manager.
* **New Department Name**: Enter the name of the new department.
* **New Department ID**: Enter the ID of the new department.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the ID of the employee.
* **Manager Notes**: Enter any notes from the manager.
* **Department Notes**: Enter any notes for the department.
* **Reason For Change**: Enter a reason for the change.
* **Date Of Change**: Enter the date of the change.
