# Workplace Culture Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Culture Monitoring Form is a tool used to gather insights on the workplace culture within an organization. It is designed to collect feedback and perceptions from employees on various aspects of their work environment, including work environment, communication, feedback, team dynamics, management, diversity and inclusion, employee satisfaction, and team satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand what each question is asking.
2. Select the appropriate page from the form and begin answering the questions.
3. For each question, provide a brief but thorough answer in the text field provided.
4. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
5. Save and submit your responses when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Work Environment (Page 1)
**Work Environment** (required): Describe your physical workspace, including the noise level, temperature, and overall comfort.

### 2. Work Style (Page 1)
**Work Style** (required): Describe the overall pace and tone of work in your department or team.

### 3. Communication (Page 1)
**Communication** (required): Describe the communication style of your supervisor, colleagues, and overall team, including frequency, style, and effectiveness.

### 4. Feedback (Page 1)
**Feedback** (required): Describe how often you receive feedback from your supervisor or colleagues, and what kind of feedback you receive (positive, constructive, or negative).

### 5. Comments (Page 1)
**Comments** (required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your work environment or team.

### 6. Team Dynamics (Page 2)
**Team Dynamics** (required): Describe the overall dynamics and relationships within your team, including collaboration, conflict, and teamwork.

### 7. Management (Page 2)
**Management** (required): Describe the style and effectiveness of your supervisor or manager, including decision-making, leadership, and communication.

### 8. Diversity and Inclusion (Page 2)
**Diversity and Inclusion** (required): Describe the level of diversity and inclusion within your team or department, including opportunities for growth and development.

### 9. Employee Satisfaction (Page 3)
**Employee Satisfaction** (optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your work on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.

### 10. Employee Satisfaction Scale (Page 3)
**Satisfaction Level** (optional): Rate your satisfaction with specific aspects of your work on a scale of 1-5, such as job content, job satisfaction, and organizational commitment.

### 11. Team Satisfaction (Page 3)
**Team Satisfaction** (optional): Rate your satisfaction with your team on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.

### 12. Comments (Page 3)
**Comments** (optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your team or work environment.
