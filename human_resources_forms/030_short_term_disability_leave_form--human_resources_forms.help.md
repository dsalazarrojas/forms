# Short Term Disability Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Term Disability Leave Form is used to record employee absences due to health reasons, allowing for tracking and management of such absences within the HR system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User ID to associate the leave request with your employee record.
2. Select the start date of your leave.
3. Select the end date of your leave.
4. Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
5. Provide a brief reason for your leave.
6. Attach any relevant medical documents.
7. Enter your contact number for follow-up.
8. Enter your email for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Id** (1, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID to associate the leave request with your employee record.
* **Start Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date when you start taking the leave.
* **End Date** (3, date, required): Enter the date when you end taking the leave.
* **Return To Work Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date when you plan to return to work.
* **Reason For Leave** (5, text, required): Provide a brief reason for your leave request.
* **Medical Documents** (6, note, required): Attach relevant medical documents to support your leave request.
* **Contact Number** (7, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): Enter your email for follow-up.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach any required medical documents to support your leave request.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access the HR system and submit this form.
* If you're unsure about any of these requirements, contact your HR representative for guidance.
