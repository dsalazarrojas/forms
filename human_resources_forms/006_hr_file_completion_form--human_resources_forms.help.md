**HR File Completion Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information related to employee HR files for completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the "Employee Details" section.
2. Indicate whether you have emergency contacts listed in the "Emergency Contacts" section.
3. Specify whether you have any documents attached to your file in the "Documents" section.
4. Acknowledge the relevant policies in the "Policy Acknowledgments" section.
5. Upload any required files in the "File Upload" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details**: This section is for providing your personal details, such as your name and other identifying information.
* **Emergency Contacts**: Please indicate if you have emergency contacts listed. Select "Yes" if you have emergency contacts, and "No" otherwise.
* **Documents**: Check "Yes" if you have any documents attached to your file, and "No" otherwise.
* **Policy Acknowledgments**: Review and acknowledge the relevant policies by selecting the correct options.
* **File Upload**: This section allows you to upload any files required to complete your employee's personnel file.
