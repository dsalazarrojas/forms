# Ethical Labor Practices Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ethical Labor Practices Audit Form is designed to assess an organization's labor practices from an ethical standpoint. This form will help you identify and evaluate areas of improvement in your organization's labor policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have all the necessary information and details ready before starting the form.
2. Review the form pages and questions carefully before answering.
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
4. For each field, provide a clear and concise response that accurately reflects your organization's practices.
5. If you are unsure about a particular question, feel free to provide additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Field 1)
**General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, optional): This field is intended to gather basic contact and company information to help identify and contact the respondent for further clarification or follow-up questions.

### Contact Details (Field 2)
**Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing contact information so that we can reach out to you for any questions or follow-up regarding your responses.

### Labor Practices (Field 3)
**Labor Practices** (`Labor Practices`, `text`, optional): This field is designed to gather information about your organization's labor practices, such as hiring and firing policies, pay and benefits, and working conditions.

### Work Practices (Field 4)
**Work Practices** (`Work Practices`, `text`, optional): This field is to gather more specific information about your work practices, such as work hours, workloads, and break times.

### Pay and Benefits (Field 5)
**Pay and Benefits** (`Pay and Benefits`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing information about your organization's pay and benefits policies, including wages, bonuses, and time off.

### Time Off (Field 6)
**Time Off** (`Time Off`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about your organization's paid time off policies, including vacation time, sick leave, and bereavement leave.

### Benefits (Field 7)
**Benefits** (`Benefits`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about the benefits your organization offers, including health insurance, retirement plans, and other perks.

### Reporting (Field 8)
**Reporting** (`Reporting`, `text`, optional): This field is for gathering information about your organization's reporting practices, including reporting times, frequency, and methods.

### Additional Notes (Field 9)
**Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, `note`, optional): This field is for providing any additional information that you feel is necessary or relevant to your responses.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
- If you are unsure about a particular question, feel free to provide additional notes.
- This form is confidential and for internal use only, so you can answer freely without fear of repercussions.
- If you need help or have further questions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
