# Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to request parental leave from work. It allows employees to provide their contact information and details about their leave request, including start and end dates, reason for the leave, and manager approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
5. Select your manager's approval in the "Manager Approval" field.
6. If your manager has left a comment, read and respond to it in the "Manager Comment" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes about your leave request in the "Employee Comment" field.
8. Choose how you would like to submit your form (Submit, Save as Draft, or Cancel).
9. Select the output format for your form (CSV, Excel, PDF, or JSON).
10. Choose whether you would like to receive an email notification about your form submission.
11. Enter your notification email address in the "Notification Email Address" field.
12. Enter your output file name and path in the "Output File" and "Output Path" fields.
13. Select the tool assigned to manage your form submission (Chatjimmy or Other).
14. Enter any additional notes or comments about your form submission in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or HR records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your official job title as assigned by HR.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your leave in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, text, required/optional): Enter the end date of your leave in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your leave request.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required/optional): Select your manager's approval status (Manager 1, Manager 2, or Manager 3).
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, note, required/optional): Read and respond to your manager's comment about your leave request.
* **Employee Comment** (`employee_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to submit your form (Submit, Save as Draft, or Cancel).
* **Output Format** (`output_format`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the output format for your form (CSV, Excel, PDF, or JSON).
* **Email Notification** (`email_notification`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you would like to receive an email notification about your form submission.
* **Notification Email Address** (`notification_email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you would like to receive notifications.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and path of the output file.
* **Output Filename** (`output_filename`, text, required/optional): Enter the output file name.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to manage your form submission (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your form submission.
* **Output Path** (`output_path`, text, required/optional): Enter the path of the output file.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address as the submitter.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* Review your form submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
* If you experience any issues with form submission, contact the IT department for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your HR representative for guidance.
