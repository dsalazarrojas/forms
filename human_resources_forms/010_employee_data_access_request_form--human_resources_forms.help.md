# employee_data_access_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee data access request form is used by employees to request access to their own employment history data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are requesting the correct data.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id_label (Required: false)**: Enter your employee ID number to identify yourself in our system.
* **employee_name_label (Required: false)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee record.
* **department_label (Required: false)**: Select your department from the list to ensure accurate data is retrieved.
	+ Options: "Department of Human Resources", "Accounting", "IT", "Marketing"
* **job_title_label (Required: false)**: Enter your job title.
* **date_of_birth_label (Required: false)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **employment_start_date_label (Required: false)**: Enter the date you started working with us.
* **employee_phone_label (Required: false)**: Enter your work phone number.
* **employee_email_label (Required: false)**: Enter your work email address.
* **job_title_2_label (Required: false)**: Enter any additional job titles that may be relevant to your data request.
* **job_title_3_label (Required: false)**: Enter any additional job titles that may be relevant to your data request.
* **job_title_4_label (Required: false)**: Enter any additional job titles that may be relevant to your data request.
* **manager_name_label (Required: false)**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **manager_phone_label (Required: false)**: Enter the phone number of your manager.
