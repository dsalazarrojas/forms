# Organizational Culture Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Organizational Culture Survey is designed to gather honest feedback from employees about the company's culture, values, and policies. This feedback is essential for the company to understand its strengths and weaknesses and make informed decisions to improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. For questions that require a numerical rating (communication style, leadership style, company culture, manager support, and years with company), choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
3. For questions that require a select_one option (decision-making process, manager support), choose one option from the list provided.
4. For questions that require a text answer (introduction, what the company's core values are, policy changes, role, department), provide detailed and specific answers.
5. Finally, confirm your submission by selecting "True" from the submit dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Write a brief introduction to help us understand your perspective and provide context for your answers.
* **What are the company's core values?**: Think about the most important principles that guide the company's behavior and write a short description.
* **How would you rate the company's communication style?**: Rate on a scale of 1 to 5 how well the company shares information with employees.
* **What do you think is the most significant factor in the company's decision-making process?**: Choose one of the options provided.
* **How would you rate the company's leadership style?**: Rate on a scale of 1 to 5 how well leaders motivate and support employees.
* **How do you rate the overall culture of the company?**: Rate on a scale of 1 to 5 how well the company promotes a positive work environment.
* **What changes would you like to see in the company's current policies?** (Optional): Be honest and provide specific examples.
* **How would you rate the level of support from your manager?** (Optional): Rate on a scale of 1 to 5 how well your manager listens to your concerns.
* **How many days do you take sick leave per year?** (Optional): Only include days actually taken, not expected or available.
* **What is your role in the company?** (Optional): Provide your job title and department.
* **How many years have you been with the company?** (Optional): Only include years actually working at the company.
* **What department do you belong to?** (Optional): Provide your department name.
* **Submit**: Confirm your submission.

## Tips
Remember to be honest and provide specific examples where possible. Your feedback is crucial for the company's improvement, and your input will help shape the organization's policies and culture.
