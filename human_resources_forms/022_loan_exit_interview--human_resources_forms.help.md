# loan_exit_interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The loan exit interview form is used to collect information from employees who are leaving the company. This includes loan details, contact information, repayment options, and a summary of the employee's experience. This form helps the HR department to gather valuable insights and feedback from exiting employees to improve the company's loan policies and processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on each page as thoroughly as possible.
2. Review each page carefully before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Loan Details** (`loan_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the loan, including the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment terms.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name, email address, and phone number so that the HR department can contact you if needed.
* **Repayment Option** (`repayment_option`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options to indicate how you would like to repay the loan, such as in full or through installments.
* **Summary** (`summary`, `note`, required: false): Enter a brief summary of your experience with the company's loan program, including any positive or negative feedback you may have.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the HR department for assistance.
* The information collected from this form will be kept confidential and used to improve the company's loan policies and processes.
