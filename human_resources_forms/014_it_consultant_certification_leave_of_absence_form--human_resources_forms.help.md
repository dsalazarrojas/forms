# IT Consultant Certification Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence for IT consultants who need to take time off for certification purposes. It ensures that the necessary approvals and notifications are in place for both the manager and team manager.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth leave request process.
- Review each field carefully and provide all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Certification Type**: Select the type of certification you are pursuing (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your work email.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation of your reason for leave.
* **Manager Approval**: Select the approval status from your manager (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Team Manager Approval**: Select the approval status from your team manager (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Approval Status**: Select the current approval status of your request (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your completed form for processing.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing of your leave request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
