# Home Office Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Office Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about your work-from-home arrangement for evaluation and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each field represents.
2. For the "User Input" section, select the options that best describe your work-from-home arrangements.
3. Fill in your name and contact information in the subsequent sections.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`Yes`, `No`, multiple-select, optional): Check the boxes that apply to your work-from-home arrangement, such as "Yes" for "I have a dedicated workspace at home" or "No" for "I work from multiple locations."

### Name
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name in the text field. This is a field for your personal identification.

### Email
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you're providing accurate information to help your employer make informed decisions about your work-from-home arrangements.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
