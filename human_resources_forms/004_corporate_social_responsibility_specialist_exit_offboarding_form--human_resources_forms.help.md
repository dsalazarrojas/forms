# corporate_social_responsibility_specialist_exit_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track the exit offboarding process of a corporate social responsibility specialist. It collects information about the specialist's departure, including their title, category, description, assigned tool, and output file.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the form.
2. Select the category of the form (e.g., human resources forms).
3. Provide a brief description of the form.
4. Enter the title of the specialist that is being offboarded.
5. Select the category of the specialist (e.g., human resources forms).
6. Enter a description of the specialist's role.
7. Enter the tool assigned to the specialist.
8. Enter the IDs of any forms associated with the offboarding process.
9. Upload the output file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **corporate_social_responsibility_specialist_exit_offboarding_form**: This is the title of the form. Enter a descriptive name for the form. (Text, required: false)
* **human_resources_forms**: This is the category of the form. Select "human resources forms" to categorize the form under human resources. (Text, required: false)
* **description**: Provide a brief description of the form, explaining its purpose and scope. (Text, required: false)
* **title**: Enter the title of the specialist being offboarded. (Text, required: false)
* **category**: Select the category of the specialist being offboarded. This should match the category of the form. (Text, required: false)
* **description**: Provide a brief description of the specialist's role and responsibilities. (Text, required: false)
* **assigned_tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the specialist. (Text, required: false)
* **form_ids**: Enter the IDs of any forms associated with the offboarding process. (Number, required: false)
* **output_file**: Upload the output file generated by the offboarding process. (Text, required: false)

Note: The required field status of each field indicates whether the field is required or optional. If a field is not required, it is not mandatory to fill it out, but it's recommended to provide as much information as possible for a complete and accurate offboarding process.
