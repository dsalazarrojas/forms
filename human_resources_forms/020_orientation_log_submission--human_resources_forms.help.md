# Orientation Log Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record participant details, orientation sessions, and feedback for HR tracking and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Details**: Enter your name and email address as required.
2. **Orientation Type**: Select the type of orientation you attended (e.g., "orientation type 1", "orientation type 2", etc.).
3. **Topics Covered**: Briefly describe the topics covered during the orientation session.
4. **Feedback**: Share any feedback or comments about the orientation session.
5. **Participant Status**: Select all the statuses that apply (e.g., "status 1", "status 2", etc.).
6. **DID NOT Attend**: If you did not attend the orientation, select "Yes" to indicate this.
7. **DID NOT Receive**: If you did not receive any materials or information during the orientation, select "Yes" to indicate this.
8. **Participant Comments**: Share any additional comments or remarks about the orientation session.
9. **Orientation Supervisor**: Enter the name of the supervisor or person who led the orientation session.
10. **Orientation Date**: Enter the date of the orientation session.
11. **Orientation Time**: Enter the time of the orientation session.
12. **Orientation Duration**: Enter the duration of the orientation session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details**: Enter your name and email address as required.
  * **Name**: A text field to input your name.
  * **Email**: A field to input your email address.
- **Orientation Type**: Select the type of orientation you attended from the provided options.
- **Topics Covered**: Briefly describe the topics covered during the orientation session.
- **Feedback**: Share any feedback or comments about the orientation session.
- **Participant Status**: Select all the statuses that apply (e.g., "status 1", "status 2", etc.).
  * Note: This field allows you to select multiple statuses.
- **DID NOT Attend**: If you did not attend the orientation, select "Yes" to indicate this.
- **DID NOT Receive**: If you did not receive any materials or information during the orientation, select "Yes" to indicate this.
  * Note: This field is a multiple-choice selection.
- **Participant Comments**: Share any additional comments or remarks about the orientation session.
- **Orientation Supervisor**: Enter the name of the supervisor or person who led the orientation session.
- **Orientation Date**: Enter the date of the orientation session.
  * Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Orientation Time**: Enter the time of the orientation session.
  * Format: HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Orientation Duration**: Enter the duration of the orientation session (in minutes).
- **Participant Did Not Attend**: If you did not attend the orientation, enter "Yes" in this field.
- **Participant Did Not Receive**: If you did not receive any materials or information during the orientation, enter "Yes" in this field.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate information in all fields to ensure correct recording and tracking of participant engagement.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant HR representative for guidance.
* Complete the form in a timely manner to ensure it's recorded correctly.
