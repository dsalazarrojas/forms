# Pay Increase Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pay Increase Form is used to request a pay increase for an employee. This form requires an employee's information, the type of pay increase requested, the amount of the increase, and other relevant details. The form helps to streamline the pay increase process and ensures that all necessary information is captured for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant employee from the list of available options.
2. Choose the type of pay increase requested (e.g., "Yes" for a pay increase and "No" for no pay increase).
3. Enter the amount of the pay increase (if applicable).
4. Enter the effective date of the pay increase (if applicable).
5. Provide a justification letter for the pay increase (if applicable).
6. Indicate whether the employee's approval status is Active or Inactive.
7. Choose whether the decision was made by "Yes" or "No" (for example, whether an approval was made).
8. (Optional) Attach a note with any further justification or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee** (Select One): Choose the employee to whom the pay increase is being requested.
* **increase_type** (Select One): Choose "Yes" to request a pay increase, or "No" if no pay increase is requested.
* **amount** (Text): Enter the amount of the pay increase (if applicable).
* **effective_date** (Date): Enter the date when the pay increase will take effect (if applicable).
* **justification_letter** (Text): Provide a justification letter for the pay increase (if applicable).
* **approval_status** (Select One): Choose whether the employee's approval status is Active or Inactive.
* **decision_made_by** (Select One): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether an approval was made.
* **signature_decision** (Note): (Optional) Attach a note with any further justification or comments.
* **approved_by** (Text): Enter the name of the person approving the pay increase (if applicable).
* **approved_date** (Date): Enter the date of the approval (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct employee from the list to ensure accurate processing of the request.
* If no pay increase is requested, select "No" for the increase_type field.
* Enter the amount and effective date only if the pay increase is approved.
* Use the justification letter to provide a clear and concise reason for the pay increase.
* Attach any additional comments or justifications for the decision made.
* Ensure that the approval status is accurately reflected for each employee.
