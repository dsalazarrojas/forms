# Independent Contractor Classification Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form helps businesses or organizations classify independent contractors based on their contracts, including company details, contract type, status, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information about the independent contractor.
2. For each field, be as accurate as possible based on the available information.
3. Review and edit the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Company Details (Page 1)

* **Company Name** (`company_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the independent contractor's company or business.
* **Contract Details** (`contract_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the contract between the client and the independent contractor.
* **Contractors Information** (`contractor_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the independent contractor, such as their name, address, phone number, or email.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any information about the payment structure or terms of the contract, such as payment frequency or amount.

### Contract and Payment (Page 2)

* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the contract began, if applicable.
* **Contract End** (`contract_end`, text, required/optional): Enter the date the contract ended, if applicable.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of contract (e.g., Hourly, Project, Retainer, Commission, Percentage, Flat Rate, or Unknown).
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of the contract (e.g., Active, Inactive, Unknown, Cancelled, Suspended).

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, leave it blank or ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
