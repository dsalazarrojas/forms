# Talent Acquisition Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about new talent acquisition specialists joining the company. It ensures that all required details are captured accurately to facilitate smooth integration and effective communication within the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field provided.
2. Enter your Last Name in the field provided.
3. Enter your Email address in the field provided.
4. Enter your Phone number in the field provided.
5. Enter your Job Title in the field provided.
6. Enter your Reporting Manager's name in the field provided.
7. Select the team members you are working with from the list provided (optional).
8. Enter your Start Date in the field provided.
9. Enter your Onboarding Date (if applicable).
10. Enter your Job Location in the field provided.
11. Enter a detailed description of your Job in the field provided.
12. Select the Job Category from the list provided.
13. Select the Job Status from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, Required):** Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, Required):** Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email, email, Required):** Enter your company email address or the email address associated with your professional online presence.
* **Phone (Phone, text, Required):** Enter your phone number to ensure accurate communication.
* **Job Title (Job Title, text, Required):** Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Reporting Manager (Reporting Manager, text, Required):** Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Team Members (Team Members, select_multiple, Optional):** Select the team members you will be working with from the provided list.
* **Start Date (Start Date, date, Required):** Enter the date you started your role or plan to start.
* **Onboarding Date (Onboarding Date, date, Optional):** Enter the date of your onboarding, if applicable.
* **Job Location (Job Location, text, Required):** Enter the location where you will be performing your job duties.
* **Job Description (Job Description, text, Required):** Enter a detailed description of your job role and responsibilities.
* **Job Category (Job Category, text, Required):** Enter the job category or department you belong to.
* **Job Status (Job Status, select_one, Required):** Select the current status of your job, either Active or Inactive.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in onboarding.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to your reporting manager or HR department for clarification.
- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access the provided team members list.
- This form is designed to be completed in a short and concise manner, so please answer each field as accurately as possible.
