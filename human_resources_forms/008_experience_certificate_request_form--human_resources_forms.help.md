# Experience Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experience Certificate Request Form is used to request an experience certificate for an employee, which can be used for various purposes such as job certification, education, or other official documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee Name in the first field.
2. Enter the Employee ID in the second field.
3. Select the Department from the options provided in the third field.
4. Enter the Job Title in the fourth field.
5. Enter the Date of Joining in the fifth field.
6. Enter the Reason for Request in the sixth field.
7. Enter the Manager Name in the seventh field.
8. Enter the Manager Email in the eighth field.
9. Enter the Manager Phone in the ninth field.
10. Enter the Employee Address in the tenth field.
11. Select the Certificate Type from the options provided in the eleventh field.
12. Enter the Certificate Details in the twelfth field.
13. Enter the Issued Date in the thirteenth field.
14. Enter the Certificate Number in the fourteenth field.
15. Sign the Employee Signature field in the fifteenth field.
16. Sign the Manager Signature field in the sixteenth field.
17. Finally, enter the Date Signed in the seventeenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (<name>)
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card. (text, required)

### Employee ID (<id>)
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number or any other unique identifier assigned to you. (text, required)

### Department (<name>)
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the options provided. (select_one, not required)

### Job Title (<name>)
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or the position you hold. (text, not required)

### Date of Joining (<name>)
* **Date of Joining**: Enter the date you joined the company. (date, not required)

### Reason for Request (<name>)
* **Reason for Request**: Enter the purpose for which you are requesting the experience certificate. (text, not required)

### Manager Name (<name>)
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor or team leader. (text, not required)

### Manager Email (<name>)
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of your supervisor or team leader. (email, not required)

### Manager Phone (<name>)
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of your supervisor or team leader. (text, not required)

### Employee Address (<name>)
* **Employee Address**: Enter your current address or the address where you can be contacted. (text, not required)

### Certificate Type (<name>)
* **Certificate Type**: Select the type of certificate you require from the options provided. (select_multiple, not required)

### Certificate Details (<name>)
* **Certificate Details**: Enter any additional information about the certificate you require. (text, not required)

### Issued Date (<name>)
* **Issued Date**: Enter the date when you would like the certificate to be issued. (date, not required)

### Certificate Number (<name>)
* **Certificate Number**: Enter the certificate number you have been assigned or any other unique identifier. (text, not required)

### Employee Signature (<name>)
* **Employee Signature**: Sign your name here to confirm that the information is correct. (text, not required)

### Manager Signature (<name>)
* **Manager Signature**: Sign your name here to confirm that the information is correct. (text, not required)

### Date Signed (<name>)
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date when you signed this form. (date, not required)
