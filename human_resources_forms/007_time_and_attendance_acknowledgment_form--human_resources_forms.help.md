# Time And Attendance Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Time And Attendance Acknowledgment Form, used by employees and their managers to record and acknowledge an employee's attendance and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form when necessary to confirm your presence or absence.
2. Select the correct acknowledgment status: Active or Inactive.
3. Enter your employee name.
4. Enter your manager's name.
5. Enter the name of your department.
6. Enter the name of the location where you are working.
7. Confirm whether the information provided is correct.
8. Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **acknowledgment_date** (`acknowledgment_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your absence or presence.
* **acknowledgment_time** (`acknowledgment_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your absence or presence.
* **acknowledgment_status** (`acknowledgment_status`, select one, required/optional): Select whether your status is Active or Inactive.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as an employee.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **department_name** (`department_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **location_name** (`location_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the location where you are working.
* **confirmation** (`confirmation`, select multiple, required/optional): Confirm that the information provided is correct.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes if necessary.
