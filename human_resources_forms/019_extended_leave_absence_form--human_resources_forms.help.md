# Extended Leave Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request an extended leave of absence from work. It helps you submit a leave request to your organization's HR team, providing necessary details for approval and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Specify the reason for your absence in the "reason for absence" field.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave using the "start date" and "end date" fields.
4. Enter the duration of your leave in the "leave duration" field.
5. Choose your employee type (Full-time, Part-time, or Contractor) from the "Employee Type" dropdown menu.
6. Select the status of your leave (Open, Awaiting Approval, Approved, or Declined) from the "leave status" dropdown menu.
7. Choose the approval manager (HR Manager, Department Head, Team Lead, or Supervisor) from the "Approval Manager" dropdown menu.
8. Select the department and location of your team from the "Department" and "Location" dropdown menus.
9. Select your team from the "Team" dropdown menu.
10. Add any comments or notes about your leave in the "comments" field.
11. Upload any attachments related to your leave (e.g., doctor's note).
12. Enter the date and time of approval for your leave using the "approval date" and "approval time" fields.
13. Select the approval status of your leave (Open, Awaiting Approval, Approved, or Declined) from the "approval status" dropdown menu.
14. Enter your employee ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID card or personnel file.
* **reason for absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave of absence.
* **start date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to start your leave.
* **end date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to end your leave.
* **leave duration** (`leave_duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of days or hours you plan to be absent.
* **leave start time** (`leave_start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of your leave.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your employee type (Full-time, Part-time, or Contractor).
* **leave status** (`leave_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of your leave (Open, Awaiting Approval, Approved, or Declined).
* **Approval Manager** (`approval_manager`, select_one, optional): Choose the person who will approve or decline your leave request.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department where you work.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the location where you are based.
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, optional): Select your team.
* **comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments about your leave.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your leave.
* **attachments** (`attachments`, note, optional): Upload any files related to your leave.
* **approval date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of approval for your leave.
* **approval time** (`approval_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of approval for your leave.
* **approval status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of approval for your leave (Open, Awaiting Approval, Approved, or Declined).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
