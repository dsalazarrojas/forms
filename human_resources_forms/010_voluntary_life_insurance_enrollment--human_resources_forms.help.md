# Voluntary Life Insurance Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll in the company's voluntary life insurance program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details, including your first name, last name, employee ID, and date of hire.
2. Select up to 3 plans to enroll in by choosing from the available options.
3. Enter your beneficiary's information, including type, name, relationship, address, phone number, and email.
4. Enter your employee's name, title, department, and date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID.
* **Plan Selections**: Select up to 3 plans to enroll in by choosing from the available options.
	+ Each plan selection will provide you with life insurance coverage.
* **Beneficiary Type**: Choose the type of beneficiary (Spouse, Child, Parent, Other).
* **Beneficiary Name**: Enter your beneficiary's name.
* **Relationship**: Enter the relationship between you and your beneficiary (e.g., spouse, child, parent).
* **Beneficiary Address**: Enter your beneficiary's address.
* **Beneficiary Phone Number**: Enter your beneficiary's phone number.
* **Beneficiary Email**: Enter your beneficiary's email.
* **Plan 1 Name**: Enter the name of Plan 1.
* **Plan 1 Rate**: Enter the rate for Plan 1.
* **Plan 2 Name**: Enter the name of Plan 2.
* **Plan 2 Rate**: Enter the rate for Plan 2.
* **Plan 3 Name**: Enter the name of Plan 3.
* **Plan 3 Rate**: Enter the rate for Plan 3.
* **Plan Selection**: Select the plan you want to enroll in by choosing from the available options.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Employees Hire Date**: Enter your date of hire.
* **Time of Death**: Enter the time of death (not applicable for life insurance enrollment).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your employee name.
* **Employee Title**: Enter your employee title.
* **Employee Department**: Enter your employee department.
* **Employee Department**: Enter your employee department (this field seems to be a duplicate, please review the YAML to verify).

**Tips**

* Make sure to select up to 3 plans to enroll in for life insurance coverage.
* Ensure all beneficiary information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your enrollment selections carefully before submitting the form.
