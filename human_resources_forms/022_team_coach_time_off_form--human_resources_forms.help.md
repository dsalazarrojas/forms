# Team Coach Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by team coaches to request time off for their employees. It helps track and manage the team coach's availability and work schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Employee" field and enter the ID of the employee for whom you are requesting time off.
2. Fill in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields with the dates of the requested time off.
3. In the "Reason" field, enter a brief explanation for the time off request.
4. Select "Approved By" to indicate if the time off request has been approved or denied.
5. In the "Team Coach Status" field, choose the status of the team coach (Active or Inactive).
6. In the "Team Coach Status Approved" field, select if the team coach status has been approved.
7. In the "Team Coach Status Denied" field, select if the team coach status has been denied.
8. In the "Team Coach Status Pending" field, select if the team coach status is pending.
9. In the "Team Coach Status Approved By" field, select who approved the team coach status.
10. In the "Team Coach Status Denied By" field, select who denied the team coach status.
11. In the "Team Coach Status Pending By" field, select who is pending the team coach status.
12. In the "Team Coach Status Comments" field, enter any comments regarding the team coach status.
