# Organization Culture Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organization Culture Feedback Questionnaire is a tool for employees to provide feedback on various aspects of their work environment. This form will help management understand employees' perspectives and identify areas for improvement in the organization's culture and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the following options for each question:
	* Select "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" for questions about employee satisfaction and work engagement.
	* Choose "Yes" or "No" for questions about organizational policies and practices.
	* Select "Good" or "Poor" for questions about feedback quality.
	* Answer on a scale from 1 to 5 or choose from available options for questions about feedback frequency and other numerical questions.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
