# Development Plan Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather acknowledgments from employees and managers regarding development plans within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing your name, job title, department, and project name.
- Confirm your acknowledgement status (seen and agreed, not seen, or refused).
- If you have any comments, enter them in the corresponding field.
- Sign the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it is spelled.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title as it is assigned to you.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department as it is assigned to you.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the project you are involved in.
* **Development Plan** (`development_plan`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant details about the development plan.
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you acknowledged the development plan.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have seen and agreed to the development plan.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's name if they confirm the plan.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your manager's email address if you provide their name.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature to complete the acknowledgement.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's signature if they confirm the plan.
* **Department Head Confirmation** (`department_head_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have seen and agreed to the development plan.
* **Department Head Name** (`department_head_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the department head's name if they confirm the plan.
* **Department Head Email** (`department_head_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the department head's email address if you provide their name.
* **Employee Acknowledgement Status** (`employee_acknowledgement_status`, select_one, required): Confirm your acknowledgement status (seen and agreed, not seen, or refused).
* **Employee Acknowledgement Comments** (`employee_acknowledgement_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments you have regarding the development plan.
* **Manager Acknowledgement Status** (`manager_acknowledgement_status`, select_one, required): Confirm your manager's acknowledgement status (seen and agreed, not seen, or refused).
* **Manager Acknowledgement Comments** (`manager_acknowledgement_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments your manager has regarding the development plan.
* **Department Head Acknowledgement Status** (`department_head_acknowledgement_status`, select_one, required): Confirm your department head's acknowledgement status (seen and agreed, not seen, or refused).
* **Department Head Acknowledgement Comments** (`department_head_acknowledgement_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments the department head has regarding the development plan.
