# Employee Absence Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify the team and manager about an employee's absence from work. It ensures that all necessary information is captured for efficient processing and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the start and end dates of your absence.
- Enter the reason for your absence in the "Reason for absence" field.
- If required, select your team, department, and assigned tool.
- Enter your employee name, title, and manager's name, email, and phone number.
- If applicable, enter the manager's comments and employee notes.
- Select the approval status and approval date/time.
- Finally, select the employee's absence status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_date**: The start date of your absence. Select a date from the calendar picker.
* **end_date**: The end date of your absence. Select a date from the calendar picker.
* **reason**: The reason for your absence. Enter a brief description of your absence.
* **status**: The current status of your absence request. Select from "Approved", "Rejected", "Pending".
* **assigned_tool**: The tool or equipment assigned to you for your absence. Select from "chatjimmy" or "Other".
* **employee_name**: Your employee name.
* **employee_title**: Your employee title.
* **manager_name**: Your manager's name.
* **manager_email**: Your manager's email.
* **manager_phone**: Your manager's phone number.
* **reason_for_absence**: The reason for your absence. Enter a brief description.
* **department**: The department you belong to. Select from "HR", "Finance", "IT".
* **team**: The team you belong to. Select from "Team A", "Team B", "Team C".
* **manager_comment**: Your manager's comments.
* **approval_date**: The date of approval.
* **approval_time**: The time of approval.
* **approved_by**: The name of the person who approved your request.
* **approval_status**: The current status of your request. Select from "Approved", "Rejected", "Pending".
* **employee_note**: Your comments.
* **manager_note**: Your manager's comments.
* **manager_approval**: The manager's approval status.
* **employee_absence**: The end date of your absence.
* **manager_approval_date**: The date of manager approval.
* **manager_note2**: Your manager's additional comments.
* **manager_note3**: Your manager's additional comments.
* **manager_approval_date**: The date of manager approval.
* **manager_note2**: Your manager's additional comments.
* **manager_note3**: Your manager's additional comments.

Note: This form requires the following fields to be filled out: start_date, end_date, reason, status, employee_name, employee_title, manager_name, manager_email, manager_phone, reason_for_absence, approval_date, approval_time, approved_by, approval_status, employee_absence, manager_approval_date.
