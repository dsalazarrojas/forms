# senior_executive_panel_interview_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Executive Panel Interview Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from senior executive panel members after an interview. This form helps to assess each candidate's performance and provide areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Candidate's Details: This includes any information about the candidate, such as name, role, or any other relevant details.
2. Identify the Panel Reviewer: Enter the name or ID of the panel member who conducted the interview.
3. Evaluate the Candidate: Provide a numerical evaluation of the candidate's performance, with values ranging from 1 (Poor) to 3 (Excellent).
4. Add Feedback: Select one of the provided feedback options (Excellent, Fair, Poor) to provide a brief assessment of the candidate's performance.
5. Provide Additional Comments: Leave any additional comments or notes about the candidate's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details**: Enter any relevant information about the candidate, such as name, role, or any other details that might be helpful in the feedback process.
* **Panel Reviewer**: Enter the name or ID of the panel member who conducted the interview, so the feedback can be associated with the correct reviewer.
* **Evaluation**: Provide a numerical evaluation of the candidate's performance, with values ranging from 1 (Poor) to 3 (Excellent).
* **Feedback**: Select one of the provided feedback options (Excellent, Fair, Poor) to give a brief assessment of the candidate's performance.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the candidate's performance that might be helpful in the feedback process.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete feedback.
- Use the evaluation and feedback fields to provide a balanced and fair assessment of the candidate's performance.
- Be specific and provide concrete examples when leaving comments to help the candidate improve.
