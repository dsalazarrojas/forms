# Employee Overtime Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Overtime Survey is a crucial tool for gathering insights into employees' experiences with rendering overtime. Your input will help management identify areas where overtime procedures can be improved to better support you and your colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Choose the most accurate option for each question from the dropdown menus.
3. If you choose the "Other" option, please specify your answer in the free-text field provided.
4. Do not skip any questions, as your input is valuable for providing actionable insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What was the primary reason you rendered overtime?**
    (Required)
    This question helps us understand why you needed to work extra hours. Please select the reason that best describes your situation.
    - Overtime was requested by my manager
    - Had an unexpected task or emergency
    - Was not feeling well or had a family emergency
    - Other (please specify)

- **How many hours did you render overtime?**
    (Required)
    This question aims to help us understand the scope of overtime rendered. Enter the number of hours you worked extra.
    (No specific guidance is provided for this field due to the nature of the question)

- **Do you have any suggestions for how we can improve our overtime procedures?**
    (Optional)
    Share your thoughts on how we can make working overtime less burdensome and more efficient. Please type your suggestions in this text field.

- **What do you think is the biggest obstacle to rendering overtime?**
    (Required)
    This question helps us identify potential areas where we can improve. Choose the option that you think is the biggest obstacle.
    - Lack of resources
    - Lack of staffing
    - Other (please specify)

- **What are some possible solutions to improve overtime procedures?**
    (Required)
    Share your ideas on how we can address the obstacles you mentioned earlier. Please select all that apply.
    - Provide more resources
    - Improve staffing levels
    - Other (please specify)

- **Have you experienced any burnout or fatigue from rendering overtime?**
    (Optional)
    This question helps us understand the impact of overtime on your well-being. Please select one of the options.
    - Yes
    - No

- **How many hours do you think you will render overtime in the next quarter?**
    (Optional)
    This question is meant to help us forecast and plan for future overtime needs. Enter your estimated hours.

- **Do you have any other comments or suggestions about rendering overtime?**
    (Optional)
    Share any additional thoughts or insights that didn't fit in the previous questions. Type your comments here.
