# hr_legal_compliance_forum_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the HR Legal Compliance Forum. The information collected will be used to follow up on your registration and share event updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Event Details, including the event type, topics, and dates.
2. Provide your Attendee Info, including your job title, department, role, and company.
3. Enter your Contact Info, including your email address, phone number, and time zone.
4. Choose your Email Type (e.g., Work, Personal, Other, or None).
5. Enter any additional information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event you are registering for.
* **Attendee Info**: Enter your job title, department, role, and company.
* **Event Preferences**: Enter any additional information about the event you would like to share.
* **Consent**: We will use your info to follow up on your registration and share event updates.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address, phone number, and time zone.
* **Email Type**: Choose the type of email address you have (e.g., Work, Personal, Other, None).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Role**: Enter your role.
* **Company**: Enter your company.
* **Job Function**: Enter your job function.
* **Reporting Manager**: Enter your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Team**: Enter your reporting team.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your work.
* **Timezone**: Choose the time zone you are in (e.g., America/New York, America/Los Angeles, ...).
* **Time Zone**: Choose the time zone you are in (e.g., America/New York, America/Los Angeles, ...).
* **Event Dates**: Enter the dates of the event.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the event.
* **Recurrence**: Enter any recurrence information about the event.
* **Event Type**: Enter the type of event.
* **Event Topics**: Enter the topics of the event.
* **Company**: Enter the company you work for.
