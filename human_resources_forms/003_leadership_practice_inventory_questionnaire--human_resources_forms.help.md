# Leadership Practice Inventory Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leadership Practice Inventory Questionnaire is designed to help you assess your leadership style and practices. It is a self-assessment tool to identify areas where you excel and areas where you need improvement. This form will help you develop a plan for personal and professional growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Use the options provided as a guide, but feel free to elaborate or provide additional information if needed.
3. Be honest about your strengths and weaknesses.
4. Review your answers and reflect on areas where you need improvement.
5. Use the insights gained from this form to develop a plan for growth and improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What is your leadership style?** (`leadership_practice_inventory_questionnaire_page_1`, select_one, required)
    Select one of the following options:
    - Autocratic: A style of leadership where the leader makes all the decisions without input from others.
    - Democratic: A style of leadership where the leader involves others in the decision-making process.
    - Laissez-faire: A style of leadership where the leader gives a lot of autonomy to team members.
