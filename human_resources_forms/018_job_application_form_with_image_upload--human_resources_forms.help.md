# Job Application Form With Image Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Application Form With Image Upload is used to collect information from job applicants for internal recruitment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Contact Email, Job Title, Job Description, and either Resume, Job Image, or both.
2. Ensure the Resume and/or Job Image fields are filled with the correct file formats. Resume should be in a Word document (.docx) or text format, and Job Image should be a valid image format (JPG, PNG, or GIF).
3. Enter your contact phone number and job location details.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resume** (`resume_text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your resume, or upload your resume as a text file.
* **Resume Image** (`resume_image`, `text`, required/optional): Upload a resume as an image file (JPG, PNG, or GIF). 
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Image** (`job_image`, `text`, required/optional): Upload an image to represent your job.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, required/optional): Click this button to submit your application.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the correct file formats for Resume and Job Image fields before submitting.
* Double-check your contact email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you are having trouble uploading files, check your internet connection and file size limits.
