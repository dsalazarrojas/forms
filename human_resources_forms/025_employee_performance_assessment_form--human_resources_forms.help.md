# Employee Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Performance Assessment Form is used to collect feedback from managers on employee performance, goals, and areas of improvement. This form helps managers provide regular and constructive feedback to employees, promoting growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your assessment of the employee's performance.
2. Provide clear and concise comments for each field, if required.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Review** (`manager_review`, text, optional): This field is used for the manager to provide a brief review of the employee's performance.
* **Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): This field is used for the manager to provide additional comments or feedback on the employee's performance.
* **Self Review** (`self_review`, text, optional): This field is used for the employee to reflect on their own performance and goals.
* **Performance Evaluation** (`employee_evaluation`, number, optional): This field is used for the manager to evaluate the employee's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Skills Assessment** (`skills_assessment`, select_one, optional): This field is used for the manager to assess the employee's skills, with options: Strong, Weak, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Areas For Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used for the manager to identify areas where the employee needs improvement, with options: Communication, Leadership, Teamwork, Problem Solving, Time Management, Adaptability, Collaboration, Initiative.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, optional): This field is used for the manager to rate the employee's performance, with options: Outstanding, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, Poor.
* **Evaluation Cycle** (`evaluation_cycle`, number, optional): This field is used to track the evaluation cycle number.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the review was completed.

## Tips

* Be specific and constructive with your feedback, focusing on behaviors and performance rather than personal traits.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Use the skills assessment and areas for improvement fields to provide specific and actionable feedback.
* Use the performance rating field to provide a clear and concise summary of the employee's performance.
* Keep the comments field concise and focused on key points.
* Use the date reviewed field to track progress and consistency.
