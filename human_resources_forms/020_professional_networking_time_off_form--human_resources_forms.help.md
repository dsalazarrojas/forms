# Professional Networking Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Professional Networking Time Off Form" is a mandatory form for employees to request time off for professional networking events. It helps track and approve employee requests, ensuring that all relevant details are recorded and verified.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and event details, including event name, date, start and end time, and location.
2. Provide a brief description of the event, highlighting its business relevance.
3. Select your current status (Active, On Leave, On Vacation, or Other) to ensure your manager and HR have an accurate picture of your work schedule.
4. Choose the manager and HR approval status (Approve, Reject, or Approve with Notes) to facilitate timely approval and feedback.
5. Select your department (Human Resources, Marketing, Sales, or Other) to help with tracking and reporting.
6. Enter your manager's name, email, and phone number (if applicable) for easy contact and follow-up.
7. Enter your email and phone number for easy contact and follow-up.
8. (Optional) Provide a reason for your leave, date, time, duration, business impact, and manager comments and HR comments for additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Event Name**: Provide a clear and concise name of the event, workshop, or conference.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time**: Choose the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: Select the end time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Provide the location of the event, if applicable.
* **Event Description**: Briefly describe the event, highlighting its business relevance and importance.
* **Business Relevance**: Explain how the event aligns with your job role and department.
* **Employee Status**: Select your current status to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* **Manager Approval**: Choose the manager's approval status (Approve, Reject, or Approve with Notes).
* **HR Approval**: Choose the HR approval status (Approve, Reject, or Approve with Notes).
* **Department**: Select your department for easy tracking and reporting.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name for easy contact and follow-up.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email for easy contact and follow-up.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number (if applicable).
* **Employee Email**: Enter your email for easy contact and follow-up.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact and follow-up.
* **Reason for Leave**: Provide a brief reason for your leave (if applicable).
* **Date**: Enter the date of your leave (if applicable).
* **Time**: Choose the time of your leave (if applicable).
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your leave (if applicable).
* **Business Impact**: Explain the business impact of your event or leave (if applicable).
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from your manager about your leave (if applicable).
* **HR Comments**: Enter any comments from HR about your leave (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your leave (if applicable).
