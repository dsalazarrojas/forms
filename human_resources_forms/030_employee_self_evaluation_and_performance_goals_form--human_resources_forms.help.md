# employee_self_evaluation_and_performance_goals_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee self-assessments and performance goals for review cycles. It helps managers and HR teams standardize data collection and support productive performance conversations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the review cycle you are evaluating: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are currently in a review cycle.
2. Enter your manager's name (if applicable).
3. Enter your name (employee name).
4. Enter your job title.
5. Set performance goals.
6. Write a self-assessment.
7. Schedule your next review date and time (optional).
8. Add any additional notes (optional).
9. Enter your email address (optional).
10. Enter your phone number (optional).
11. Select the category (this will default to 'Human Resources Forms').

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Review Cycle** (review_cycle, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are currently in a review cycle. This is a way to indicate your current status. You can choose between 'Yes' and 'No'.
- **Manager Name** (managerName, select_multiple, required: false): Enter your manager's name. You might need to select multiple managers if applicable.
- **Employee Name** (employeeName, text, required: false): Enter your name as the employee.
- **Job Title** (jobTitle, text, required: false): Enter your job title as it appears in HR records.
- **Performance Goals** (performanceGoals, text, required: false): Set your performance goals for the review cycle. Be specific and clear with what you want to achieve.
- **Self-Assessment** (selfAssessment, text, required: false): Write a self-assessment for yourself. Describe your progress, successes, and areas of improvement.
- **Next Review Date** (nextReviewDate, date, required: false): Schedule your next review date if you know it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
- **Next Review Time** (nextReviewTime, time, required: false): Set your next review time if you know it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
- **Notes** (notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes you'd like to share with your manager.
- **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if you'd like to receive updates or notifications).
- **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to receive calls or reminders).
- **Category** (category, text, required: false): This field is automatically populated with 'Human Resources Forms'. You can leave it as is.
- **Description** (description, text, required: false): This field is meant to provide context. Leave it as is or use it for additional information.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when filling out your self-assessment and performance goals.
- Make sure to schedule your next review date and time accurately.
- Use the 'Notes' field for any additional comments or reminders.
- Ensure your email and phone number are up to date to receive important updates.
