# Corporate Employee Presence Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for corporate employees to record their daily presence and absence.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your personal details:
	* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Type your full name as it is recorded in the company's records.
	* **Location** (`location`): Indicate where you are currently located (e.g., office, field, or working from home).
	* **Department** (`department`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm whether you are working in your usual department or not.
	* **Date** (`date`): Select the date of your current day.
	* **Time** (`time`): Select the time of your current work hours.
2. Confirm your attendance status:
	* **Location** (`location`): Confirm your location by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each of the locations listed.
	* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm whether you are the one submitting this form or someone else on your behalf.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes (optional, `notes`): If you have any specific information you'd like to add for reference, you can include it in this section.
4. Enter your contact information (optional):
	* **Email** (`email`): Enter your company-issued email address.
	* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
4. Review and submit:
	* Ensure all required fields are complete before submitting the form.
5. Submit the form to save your record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (`employee_name`)**: 
	+ This field is required and should reflect the name used in your company's HR records.
* **Department (`department`)**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you are working in your usual department.
	+ Answer "No" otherwise.
* **Location (`location`)**: 
	+ Select "Yes" if you are currently at your usual location or working remotely.
	+ Select "No" if you are not at any of the listed locations.
* **Date (`date`)**: 
	+ Select the date of your current workday.
* **Time (`time`)**: 
	+ Select the time of your current work hours.
* **Location (select_multiple)**: 
	+ This field confirms your current location for multiple spots.
* **Submitted By (`submitted_by`)**: 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you are submitting this form on your own behalf.
	+ Answer "No" if someone else is submitting this form on your behalf.
* **Notes (`notes`)**: 
	+ This field is optional and for any additional comments or notes you might need to add.
* **Email (`email`)**: 
	+ Enter your company-issued email address (if you need to add contact information).
* **Phone (`phone`)**: 
	+ Enter your contact phone number (if you need to add contact information).
* **Form ID (`form_id`)**: 
	+ This is an automatically generated ID for internal purposes.
