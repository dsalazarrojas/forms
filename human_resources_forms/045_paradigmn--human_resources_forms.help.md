# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employees in the organization. It can be used to update employee records, track progress, or for other internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the job title of the employee in the "Job Title" field.
2. Type the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the department the employee belongs to in the "Department" field.
4. Select the correct "Prophets" option from the dropdown list.
5. Enter the employee's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the employee's phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select a date from the calendar for the "Date" field.
8. Enter the time in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Optional): Type the employee's name.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, Select One, Optional): Select one of the two options, "John" or "Jane".
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Select a date from the calendar.
* **Time** (`time`, Text, Optional): Enter the time of the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Use the correct formatting for dates and times (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD for dates and 24-hour format for times).
- If a field is optional, only enter information if necessary.
