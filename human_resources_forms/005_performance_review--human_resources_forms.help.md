# Performance Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Review form is used to evaluate an employee's progress and growth during a specific period. It helps reviewers provide constructive feedback and guidance for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Employee Name field with the correct name of the employee being reviewed.
2. Choose a review period from the options provided in the Review Period field.
3. Enter the employee's title in the Employee Title field.
4. Enter your evaluation of the employee's performance in the Performance Rating field.
5. Provide comments and feedback in the Comments field.
6. Choose your level of agreement with the employee's performance in the Rating Scale field.
7. Provide recommendations for improvement in the Recommendations field.
8. Enter any action items that need to be addressed in the Action Items field.
9. Select the date of the last evaluation in the Evaluation Date field.
10. Choose whether you have signed off on this review in the Manager Signoff field.
11. Enter your name in the Reviewer Name field.
12. Choose a due date for this review in the Review Due Date field.
13. Indicate whether this review is completed in the Completed field.
14. If completed, enter the date of completion in the Completed Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
* **Review Period**: Select the period over which you are evaluating the employee's performance.
* **Employee Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Performance Rating**: Evaluate the employee's performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments**: Provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* **Rating Scale**: Choose your level of agreement with the employee's performance.
* **Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for improvement.
* **Action Items**: Enter any action items that need to be addressed.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the last evaluation.
* **Manager Signoff**: Choose whether you have signed off on this review.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Review Due Date**: Choose the due date for this review.
* **Completed**: Indicate whether this review is completed.
* **Completed Date**: If completed, enter the date of completion.
