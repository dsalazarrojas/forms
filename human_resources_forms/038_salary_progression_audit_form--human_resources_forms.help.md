# Salary Progression Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Salary Progression Audit Form is used to track and document the progression of an employee's salary over time. This form helps to ensure that employee salary progression is accurately and consistently recorded, and that all relevant details are captured for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's details (Form 1: Employee Details)
   Fill in the employee's name, job title, department, and any other relevant information.

2. Enter the job title and department of the employee (Form 2: Job Title)
   Select the employee's job title and department from the provided options.

3. Enter the hire date of the employee (Form 4: Hire Date)
   Enter the date the employee was hired.

4. Enter the salary amount of the employee (Form 5: Salary Amount)
   Enter the current salary of the employee.

5. Select whether the employee's salary has progressed (Form 7: Progression Status)
   Select 'Yes' if the employee's salary has progressed, 'No' otherwise.

6. Provide a reason for salary progression or denial (Form 8: Reason for Progression, Form 19: Reason for Denial)
   Enter a reason for why the employee's salary has progressed or been denied.

7. Get an employee signature (Form 9: Employee Signature)
   The employee must sign to confirm they have seen and agree to the form details.

8. Get a manager's signature (Form 10: Manager Signature)
   The manager must sign to confirm they have reviewed and agree to the form details.

9. Enter the date the form was submitted (Form 11: Date of Submission)
   Enter the date the form was submitted.

10. Enter the date the form was approved or denied (Form 16: Date Approved, Form 20: Date Denied)
    Enter the date the form was approved or denied.

11. Select the review status of the form (Form 24: Review Status)
    Select 'Active' if the form is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.

12. Enter the date the form was reviewed (Form 25: Date Reviewed)
    Enter the date the form was last reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (Form 1, Text, Required): Enter the employee's name, job title, department, and any other relevant information.
* **Job Title** (Form 2, Text, Required): Select the employee's job title.
* **Department** (Form 3, Text, Required): Select the employee's department.
* **Hire Date** (Form 4, Date, Required): Enter the date the employee was hired.
* **Salary Amount** (Form 5, Number, Required): Enter the current salary of the employee.
* **Progression Date** (Form 6, Date, Required): Enter the date the employee's salary was last progressed.
* **Progression Status** (Form 7, Select One, Required): Select 'Yes' if the employee's salary has progressed, 'No' otherwise.
* **Reason for Progression** (Form 8, Text, Required): Enter a reason for why the employee's salary has progressed.
* **Employee Signature** (Form 9, Text, Required): The employee must sign to confirm they have seen and agree to the form details.
* **Manager Signature** (Form 10, Text, Required): The manager must sign to confirm they have reviewed and agree to the form details.
* **Date of Submission** (Form 11, Date, Required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Reason for Non-Progression** (Form 12, Text, Required): Enter a reason for why the employee's salary has not progressed.
* **Additional Comments** (Form 13, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the employee's salary progression.
* **Departmental Approval** (Form 14, Select Multiple, Required): Select whether the department has approved the form.
* **Additional Comments** (Form 15, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the form.
* **Date Approved** (Form 16, Date, Required): Enter the date the form was approved.
* **Date Updated** (Form 17, Date, Required): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Form Status** (Form 18, Text, Optional): Enter the status of the form.
* **Reason for Denial** (Form 19, Text, Required): Enter a reason for why the form was denied.
* **Date Denied** (Form 20, Date, Required): Enter the date the form was denied.
* **Manager Reason** (Form 21, Text, Optional): Enter a reason from the manager for denial or progression.
* **Additional Comments** (Form 22, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the form.
* **Date of Rejection** (Form 23, Date, Optional): Enter the date the form was rejected.
* **Review Status** (Form 24, Select One, Required): Select 'Active' if the form is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Date Reviewed** (Form 25, Date, Required): Enter the date the form was last reviewed.
