# job_safety_observation_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Safety Observation Form is a tool for capturing and documenting safety observations during a job. It helps ensure that work sites are safe and that employees are aware of potential hazards and risks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete the form for each observation, using the provided fields to gather necessary information.
2. Be sure to select the correct options for "Employee(s) Involved" and "Employees Involved List" for each observation.
3. Enter the accurate date and time of the observation.
4. Describe the location of the observation in detail.
5. Enter a brief description of what was observed.
6. Include any recommendations for improvement or further action.
7. Sign and date the form as the observer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Safety Observation Form** (`1`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the safety observation.
* **Employee(s) Involved** (`2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all employees involved in the observation. 
* **Employees** (`3`, select_multiple, optional): Another way to select employees involved in the observation.
* **Observation Date** (`4`, date, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Observation Time** (`5`, time, required): Enter the time of the observation.
* **Location** (`6`, text, required): Enter the location of the observation in detail.
* **Description** (`7`, text, required): Briefly describe what was observed.
* **Recommendations** (`8`, text, required): Include any recommendations for improvement or further action.
* **Employees Involved List** (`9`, select_multiple, optional): Another way to select employees involved in the observation.
* **Employer Signature** (`10`, note, required): Sign and date the form as the observer's employer.
* **Employer Name** (`11`, text, required): Enter the employer's name.
* **Employer Title** (`12`, text, required): Enter the employer's title.
* **Employer Department** (`13`, text, required): Enter the employer's department.
* **Employer Position** (`14`, text, required): Enter the employer's position.
* **Employee Signature** (`15`, note, required): Sign and date the form as the employee.
* **Employee Name** (`16`, text, required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Employee Title** (`17`, text, required): Enter the employee's title.
* **Employee Department** (`18`, text, required): Enter the employee's department.
* **Employee Position** (`19`, text, required): Enter the employee's position.
* **Observer Name** (`20`, text, required): Enter the observer's name.
* **Observer Title** (`21`, text, required): Enter the observer's title.
* **Observer Department** (`22`, text, required): Enter the observer's department.
* **Observer Position** (`23`, text, required): Enter the observer's position.
* **Observer** (`24`, text, required): Enter the observer's name (this field is intended for the observer's name).
* **Additional Comments** (`25`, text, optional): Include any additional comments or notes.

Please note that some fields may not be required for each observation, but are still important for accurate record-keeping.
