# Employee Performance Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Performance Appraisal Form" is used to evaluate an employee's performance over a specific period, focusing on their strengths, areas for improvement, and overall contribution to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the necessary information in each field, making sure to answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review the fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure you've included all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Performance Section** (`employee_performance_section`, Note, Required: No): This field is used to capture any notable events, achievements, or challenges related to the employee's performance over the evaluation period.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor who is performing the evaluation.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, Select One, Required: No): Indicate if the supervisor has performed an evaluation for this employee in the past (`Yes`/`No`).
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Completed Date** (`date_completed`, Date, Required: No): Enter the date when the evaluation is completed.
* **Overall Rubric Score** (`overall_rubric_score`, Number, Required: No): Enter a numerical score out of a maximum of 10 to represent the employee's overall performance.
* **Key Performance Areas** (`key_performance_areas`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Indicate which key performance areas the employee has performed well or not (`Yes`/`No`).
* **Performance Comments** (`performance_comments`, Text, Required: No): Add any comments or observations about the employee's performance, including strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Next Review Date** (`next_review_date`, Date, Required: No): Enter the date when the next evaluation is scheduled.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, Select One, Required: No): Select a performance rating (`Good`/`Fair`) to represent the employee's overall performance.
* **Employee Rating** (`employee_rating`, Select One, Required: No): Select an employee rating (`Good`/`Fair`) to represent the employee's overall performance.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, Text, Required: No): Enter any additional notes or comments about the rating.
* **Rating Notes** (`rating_notes`, Text, Required: No): Add any further comments or observations about the performance rating.

## Tips
- Be thorough and honest when filling out this form to provide accurate and fair feedback.
- Ensure that the form is completed within the required timeframe to maintain consistency and fairness in employee evaluations.
