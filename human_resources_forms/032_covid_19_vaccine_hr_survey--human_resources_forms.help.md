# COVID 19 Vaccine HR Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the COVID-19 Vaccine HR Survey form. The form is designed to gather information from employees about their interest in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine, their preferences and concerns, and their logistical needs for vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your Employee Name and ID Number in the designated fields.
2. Enter your Work Email Address.
3. Select your Department or Division from the provided options.
4. Enter your Job Title or Position.
5. Answer the following questions regarding your interest in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine:
	* Are you interested in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine? (Yes, definitely, Yes, probably, Unsure, Probably not, Definitely not)
	* How soon would you like to be vaccinated? (As soon as possible, Within 1-2 weeks, Within 1 month, Flexible timeline, No time preference)
6. Select your preferred vaccine type:
	* Pfizer
	* Moderna
	* Johnson and Johnson
	* No preference
	* Unsure
7. Answer the following questions regarding your health and risk assessment:
	* Age Group: (18-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60-69, 70+)
	* Work Location Type: (Office, Remote, Hybrid, Field/On-site, Healthcare facility)
	* Consider yourself at high risk for severe COVID-19? (True, False, Unsure)
	* Have underlying health conditions? (True, False, Prefer not to say)
	* Live with vulnerable or high-risk individuals? (True, False)
8. Check all that apply regarding your main concerns about vaccination:
	* Safety concerns
	* Side effects
	* Allergies
	* Effectiveness
	* Personal choice
	* Religious reasons
	* Medical contraindications
	* Lack of information
	* Cost
	* Other
9. If you have any additional concerns or questions about vaccination, please provide more information in the text box provided.
10. Select your preferred location for vaccination:
	* On-site at workplace
	* Nearby clinic
	* Pharmacy
	* Hospital
	* Healthcare provider office
	* Mobile clinic
	* Home or remote
11. Choose your preferred time for vaccination:
	* Morning
	* Afternoon
	* Evening
	* Flexible
	* During work hours
	* Outside work hours
12. Are you willing to use work time for vaccination appointment? (True, False, Unsure)
13. How long do you expect to take off after vaccination? (Same day return to work, 1 day off, 2-3 days off, Unsure)
14. If you have any additional questions or comments, please provide in the text box below.
15. Finally, are you willing to be contacted with vaccination information? (True, False)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee ID Number**: Enter your employee ID number as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Work Email Address**: Enter your company email address.
* **Department/Division**: Select the department or division you belong to.
* **Job Title/Position**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Are you interested in receiving the COVID-19 vaccine**: Check if you are interested in getting vaccinated.
* **How soon would you like to be vaccinated**: Select your preferred timeline for vaccination.
* **Vaccine Type Preference**: Select your preferred vaccine type.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group.
* **Work Location Type**: Select your work location type.
* **Consider yourself at high risk for severe COVID-19**: Check if you consider yourself at high risk for severe COVID-19.
* **Have underlying health conditions**: Check if you have any underlying health conditions.
* **Live with vulnerable or high-risk individuals**: Check if you live with vulnerable or high-risk individuals.
* **Main Concerns About Vaccination**: Check all that apply.
* **Main Concerns**: Provide more details about your concerns.
* **Vaccination Location**: Select your preferred location for vaccination.
* **Preferred Time for Vaccination**: Select your preferred time for vaccination.
* **Willing to use work time for vaccination appointment**: Check if you are willing to use work time for vaccination appointment.
* **Expected Recovery Time after Vaccination**: Select your expected recovery time after vaccination.
* **Additional Questions or Comments**: Provide any additional questions or comments.
* **May we contact you with vaccination information**: Check if you are willing to be contacted with vaccination information.
