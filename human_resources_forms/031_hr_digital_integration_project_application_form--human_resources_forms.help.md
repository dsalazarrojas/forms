# hr_digital_integration_project_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Digital Integration Project Application Form is used to collect information about ongoing and completed projects from employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below.

1. Select the project details page and fill in the required information (Project Details).
2. Move on to the project timeline and provide the relevant details.
3. If you have any supporting documents, upload them under the Supporting Documents section.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the project has a project lead and if the team members are involved.
5. Enter the email and phone number of the project lead and team members.
6. Finally, select the project status (Active or Inactive) and click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: <project_id> (<title>, <description>): Enter the project title and description to provide a brief overview of the project.
* **Time Line**: <project timeline>: Fill in the project timeline to provide details on the project's start and end dates.
* **Supporting Documents**: <upload supporting documents>: Upload any relevant documents such as project reports, meeting notes, and photos.
* **Project Lead**: Does the project have a lead? (Yes/No): Select "Yes" if the project has a designated lead and "No" otherwise.
* **Team Members**: Are team members involved? (Yes/No): Select "Yes" if the project team members are involved and "No" otherwise.
* **Email**: <email address>: Enter the email address of the project lead and team members.
* **Phone Number**: <phone number>: Enter the phone number of the project lead and team members.
* **Project Status**: <project status> (Active/Inactive): Select the current project status, whether it's Active or Inactive.
