# AA Employee Equipment Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AA Employee Equipment Agreement is a form used to establish an official agreement between the employee and the company regarding company-owned or company-provided equipment. This form is used to collect information about the equipment, such as type, brand, serial number, and expected return date. It also outlines the employee's responsibilities and liabilities for the equipment, including reporting any damage or loss and using it only for authorized business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the information required.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Select your current department.
5. Enter your job title.
6. If you were hired, enter your date of hire.
7. Sign the form to acknowledge your agreement.
8. For the equipment details section, select the category and brand/model of the equipment assigned.
9. Enter the serial number of the equipment.
10. Assess the condition of the equipment at the time of receipt.
11. Enter the approximate market value of the equipment.
12. Enter the date the equipment was assigned to you.
13. If applicable, enter the expected return date.
14. Read and agree to the responsibilities and acknowledgments section.
15. Sign your manager's name to approve the equipment assignment.
16. Date and sign the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: <Full name> (Required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address**: <Email address> (Required): Enter your company email address.
* **Department**: <Department> (Required): Select your current department assignment.
* **Job title**: <Job title> (Required): Enter your current position title.
* **Date of hire**: <Date of hire> (Optional): If you were hired, enter the date you started employment.
* **Employee signature**: <Employee signature> (Required): Sign to acknowledge agreement.
* **Type of equipment assigned**: <Equipment type> (Required): Select the category of the equipment (e.g., Laptop Computer, Desktop Computer, etc.).
* **Brand/Model**: <Brand/Model> (Required): Enter the manufacturer and model number of the equipment.
* **Serial number**: <Serial number> (Required): Enter the unique identifier for the device.
* **Condition of equipment at time of receipt**: <Condition> (Required): Assess the physical state of the equipment (e.g., New, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Equipment value (USD)**: <Equipment value> (Required): Enter the approximate current market value of the equipment.
* **Date assigned**: <Date assigned> (Required): Enter the date the equipment was issued to you.
* **Expected return date**: <Expected return date> (Optional): If applicable, enter the expected return date of the equipment.
* **Responsibilities and Acknowledgments**:
  * **I understand I am responsible for loss or damage due to negligence**: <Yes/No> (Required): Select "Yes, I understand" to agree to this responsibility.
  * **I agree to use equipment only for authorized business purposes**: <Yes/No> (Required): Select "Yes, I agree" to this responsibility.
  * **I will comply with all company security policies**: <Yes/No> (Required): Select "Yes, I agree" to this responsibility.
* **Manager name**: <Manager name> (Required): Enter your supervisor's name approving this equipment assignment.
* **Manager signature**: <Manager signature> (Required): Sign to approve equipment assignment.
* **Date of agreement**: <Date of agreement> (Required): Date this agreement was signed.

Note: This form is a sample and should be reviewed and adapted according to your company's specific policies and procedures.

# Tips
* Review the company's equipment policies and procedures before filling out this form.
* Ensure that you understand your responsibilities and liabilities for the equipment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, consult with your supervisor or HR department.
