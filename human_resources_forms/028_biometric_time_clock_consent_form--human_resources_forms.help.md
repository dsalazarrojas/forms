# Biometric Time Clock Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to obtain your consent for the use of biometric data collection, storage, and usage for the biometric time clock system.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your personal details, such as your name, employee ID, and department.
2. Choose the biometric type (e.g., fingerprint, facial recognition, hand geometry, palm print, iris scan, voice pattern) and purpose (e.g., time and attendance tracking, access control, payroll processing) that will be used for data collection.
3. Select the data storage location (e.g., on-premises secure server, encrypted cloud storage, local device storage, third-party secure facility) and data retention period (e.g., duration of employment plus 3 years, duration of employment plus 5 years, indefinitely until requested deletion).
4. Provide your consent for biometric data collection, usage, and sharing, and acknowledge your understanding of your privacy rights, data security, and withdrawal rights.
5. If you choose not to provide biometric data, select an alternative method (e.g., traditional time card, PIN entry system, mobile app) for time tracking.
6. Confirm that you have read and understood the form, had opportunity to ask questions, and that your questions have been addressed.
7. Sign the form electronically to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`Employee Information`, note, optional): This section is for your personal details.
* **First Name** (`employee_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`employee_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or division.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter your date of hire.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, required): Enter your company email.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work contact number.
* **Biometric System Information** (`biometric_system_information`, note, optional): Learn about the biometric time clock system.
* **System Description** (`system_description`, note, optional): Description of the biometric system.
* **Biometric Type Used** (`biometric_type`, select_one, required): Choose the biometric type that will be used (e.g., fingerprint, facial recognition, hand geometry).
* **Purpose of Data Collection** (`purpose_of_collection`, select_one, required): Choose the purpose of data collection (e.g., time and attendance tracking, access control, payroll processing).
* **Data Storage Location** (`data_storage_location`, select_one, required): Choose where your data will be stored (e.g., on-premises secure server, encrypted cloud storage, local device storage).
* **Data Retention Period** (`data_retention_period`, select_one, required): Choose the data retention period (e.g., duration of employment plus 3 years, duration of employment plus 5 years, indefinitely until requested deletion).
* **Consent Declaration** (`consent_declaration`, note, optional): Provide your consent.
* **Voluntary Consent** (`voluntary_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm if your consent is voluntary.
* **Data Usage Consent** (`data_usage_consent`, select_one, required): Choose how your data may be used (e.g., time tracking only, time tracking and access control).
* **Data Sharing Consent** (`data_sharing_consent`, select_one, required): Choose if your data can be shared (e.g., with authorized vendors, as required by law).
* **Privacy Rights Understanding** (`privacy_rights_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of your privacy rights.
* **Data Security Understanding** (`data_security_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of data security measures.
* **Withdrawal Rights** (`withdrawal_rights`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of your right to withdraw consent.
* **Alternative Methods** (`alternative_methods`, select_one, required): If not providing biometric data, choose an alternative method (e.g., traditional time card, PIN entry system, mobile app).
* **Alternative Method Selected** (`alternative_method_selected`, select_one, optional): If choosing an alternative method, select it here.
* **Employee Acknowledgments** (`employee_acknowledgments`, note, optional): Acknowledge your understanding.
* **Accuracy Acknowledgment** (`accuracy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Questions Addressed** (`questions_addressed`, select_one, required): Confirm that your questions have been addressed.
* **Remaining Questions** (`remaining_questions`, text, optional): If you have any remaining questions.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent electronically.
* **Date of Consent** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your electronic signature.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of an HR representative or witness.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, select_one, optional): Confirm the witness's signature.
* **Witness Date** (`witness_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the witness's signature.
* **Employer Acknowledgment** (`employer_acknowledgment`, select_one, optional): Confirm that the employer has received and accepted your consent.
* **HR Contact Name** (`hr_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of an HR representative for questions.
* **HR Contact Email** (`hr_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of an HR representative for questions.
* **HR Contact Phone** (`hr_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of an HR representative for questions.
