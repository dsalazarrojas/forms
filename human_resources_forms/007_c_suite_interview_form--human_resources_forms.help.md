# C-Suite Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The C-Suite Interview Form is a comprehensive document used to assess and evaluate executive candidates during the hiring process. It ensures a structured and consistent evaluation process for all candidates, providing a clear and concise summary of their strengths, weaknesses, and alignment with company values.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the candidate's name and position applied for.
2. Evaluate each candidate's competencies, including strategic thinking, leadership style, financial acumen, and alignment with corporate values.
3. Record your observations and key strengths and weaknesses for each candidate.
4. Provide a final recommendation and indicate the recommended next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name**: Enter the candidate's name in this field.
* **Position Applied For**: Select the position the candidate is applying for from the list below:
	+ Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
	+ Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
	+ Chief Operating Officer (COO)
	+ Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
	+ Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
* **Interviewer Name**: Enter your name as the person conducting the interview.
* **Date of Interview**: Select the date of the interview from the calendar or enter the date manually.
* **Strategic Thinking and Vision**: Evaluate the candidate's ability to think strategically and plan for the future:
	+ 1 - Unsatisfactory
	+ 2 - Below Average
	+ 3 - Average
	+ 4 - Above Average
	+ 5 - Exceptional
* **Primary Leadership Style**: Assess the candidate's leadership style:
	+ Transformational
	+ Democratic
	+ Laissez-faire
	+ Transactional
	+ Servant Leadership
* **Financial Acumen and Resource Management**: Evaluate the candidate's ability to manage financials and resources:
	+ Weak
	+ Proficient
	+ Strong
* **Alignment with Corporate Values**: Assess the candidate's alignment with corporate values:
	+ No Alignment
	+ Partial Alignment
	+ Full Alignment
* **Previous Executive Experience (Years)**: Record the number of years the candidate has spent in executive roles.
* **Key Strengths and Observations**: Describe the candidate's key strengths and any observations or areas of concern.
* **Final Recommendation**: Provide a final recommendation for the candidate:
	+ Strongly Recommend
	+ Recommend
	+ Recommend with Reservations
	+ Do Not Recommend
* **Recommended Next Steps**: Suggest any next steps for the candidate, e.g., second interview, background check.

This guide will help you complete the C-Suite Interview Form accurately and effectively, ensuring a structured and informed evaluation process for all executive candidates.
