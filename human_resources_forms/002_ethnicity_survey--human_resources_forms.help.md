# Ethnicity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethnicity Survey is a form used to collect demographic data from users, including their ethnicity, language preference, and other personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your ethnicity from the provided options.
2. Choose your language preference from the options provided.
3. Enter any additional information you wish to provide in the "Additional information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ethnicity** (`id: 2`, `label: Ethnicity`, `type: select_one`): Select your ethnicity from the options provided:
	+ African American
	+ Asian American
	+ Caucasian
	+ Hispanic/Latino
	+ Native American
	+ Other
* **Language preference** (`id: 3`, `label: Language preference`, `type: select_multiple`): Choose your language preference from the options provided:
	+ Spanish
	+ Mandarin
	+ English
	+ Other
* **Additional information** (`id: 6`, `label: Additional information`, `type: note`): Enter any additional information you wish to provide.
* **Email** (`id: 15`, `label: User Email`, `type: email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 16`, `label: User Phone`, `type: text`): Enter your phone number.
* **User Address** (`id: 17`, `label: User Address`, `type: text`): Enter your user address.
* **User Category** (`id: 24`, `label: User Category`, `type: text`): Enter a category related to your user.
* **User Category 2** (`id: 25`, `label: User Category 2`, `type: select_multiple`): Choose a category for your user:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
