# workplace_culture_suggestion_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "workplace_culture_suggestion_request_form" is a platform for employees to share their ideas and suggestions about how to improve our workplace culture. This form helps collect and track suggestions from employees, ensuring that their input is heard and acted upon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in your name and email address accurately, as these will be used to identify you and contact you for follow-ups.
2. Share your suggestion in the "Employee Suggestion" field.
3. Choose the department that your suggestion is relevant to.
4. Select the type of suggestion it is (e.g., "suggestion type 1", "suggestion type 2", "suggestion type 3").
5. Choose how your suggestion will impact the organization ("suggestion impact 1", "suggestion impact 2").
6. Select the status of your suggestion ("suggestion status 1", "suggestion status 2").
7. If relevant, provide the date and time for follow-up.
8. Provide any follow-up comments if necessary.
9. Finally, select the review status ("review status 1", "review status 2") and choose a date and time for the review.
