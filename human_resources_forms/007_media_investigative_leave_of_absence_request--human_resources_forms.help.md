# Media Investigative Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a media investigative leave of absence for an employee. The form must be completed by the employee and their manager, and is used to track and approve or decline requests for leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and email address.
2. Enter the employee's manager's email address and name.
3. Select the start and end dates of the leave.
4. Enter the reason for the leave.
5. Choose the department and leave type.
6. Specify the number of days requested.
7. Choose the manager's confirmation (Approved, Declined, or On Hold).
8. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Investigative Leave Of Absence Request**: This is the title of the form.
* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee requesting the leave.
* **employee_email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **employee_email**: Enter the manager's email address.
* **manager_name**: Enter the manager's name.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the leave in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the leave in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **reason**: Enter a brief description of the reason for the leave.
* **department**: Enter the department where the employee works.
* **leave_type**: Choose the type of leave (e.g. vacation, sick leave, etc.).
* **days_requested**: Enter the number of days requested for the leave.
* **manager_confirmation**: Select how the manager wants to confirm the leave (Approved, Declined, or On Hold).
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments about the leave request.
