# hr_tuition_assistance_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Tuition Assistance Application Form is a document used to process employee requests for tuition assistance. This form is for employees who wish to receive financial support for educational courses, training, or other educational expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and job title.
2. Select your department from the provided options.
3. Specify the course you are applying for.
4. Enter your start and end dates for the course.
5. Enter the number of hours you will work while taking the course.
6. Enter your expected rate of pay for working during the course.
7. Enter the total tuition amount for the course.
8. Select the personnel who will approve your application.
9. Enter the date of approval for your application.
10. Enter your email address so that your application can be sent to the HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (required): Enter your current job title within the company.
* **Department** (required): Select the department where you are currently working.
* **Course** (required): Enter the name and details of the course you are applying for.
* **Start Date** (required): Enter the date you plan to start the course.
* **End Date** (required): Enter the date you plan to end the course.
* **Hours Worked** (required): Enter the number of hours you will be working during the course.
* **Rate** (required): Enter your expected rate of pay for working during the course.
* **Tuition Amount** (required): Enter the total tuition amount for the course.
* **Approved By** (required): Select the person who will approve your application (HR Manager, Director, or Assistant Manager).
* **Approval Date** (required): Enter the date of approval for your application.
* **Submitted By** (required): Enter your email address so that your application can be sent to the HR department.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in processing your application. Also, please note that approval and approval dates are subject to change based on company policies and procedures.
