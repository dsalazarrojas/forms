# Payroll Statement Copy Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Statement Copy Request Form is used to request a copy of an employee's payroll statement. This form is designed to provide a convenient way for employees to request a copy of their payroll statement in a secure and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select the employee's department from the "Department" field.
4. Enter the employee's manager's name and email in the "Manager Name" and "Manager Email" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the employee's phone number and address in the "Employee Phone" and "Employee Address" fields, respectively.
6. If applicable, select the reason for the request from the "Reason for Request" field.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the payroll period.
8. Sign the form with your digital signature in the "Signature" field.
9. Select the submitter (employee or manager) and enter the date and time of submission in the "Submitter" and "Date Submitted" fields.
10. Enter the employee's job title, job code, and job family.
11. Enter the form ID, user ID, and form version.
12. Enter the timestamp of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the full name of the employee.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Department**: Select the employee's department.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the employee's manager.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Employee Address**: Enter the employee's address.
* **Reason for Request**: Select all that apply for the reason of the request.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the payroll period.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the payroll period.
* **Signature**: Sign the form with your digital signature.
* **Submitter**: Select whether the employee or manager is submitting the form.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID.
* **User ID**: Enter the user ID.
* **Form Version**: Enter the form version.
* **Timestamp**: Enter the timestamp of submission.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes regarding the request.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Address**: Enter the manager's address.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Job Code**: Enter the employee's job code.
* **Job Group**: Enter the employee's job group.
* **Job Family**: Enter the employee's job family.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary authorization to request a copy of the employee's payroll statement.
* Double-check the employee's information to ensure accuracy.
* Submit the form to the relevant department for processing.
* Keep a record of the form submission for future reference.
