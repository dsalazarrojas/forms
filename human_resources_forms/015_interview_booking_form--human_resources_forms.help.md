# interview_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interview_booking_form is used to book an interview, capturing relevant details such as the interviewee's name, email, phone number, preferred time slot, and other essential information. This form is designed to streamline the interview process and make it more efficient for both the interviewer and the interviewee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the interview_booking_form link to access the form.
*   Select your response to the fields as prompted, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
*   Ensure you fill in all required fields (marked as *Required*).
*   Review your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here's what each field is for:

*   **Interview Time** (Page 1, id: 1): Select whether an interview is required at this time or not.
*   **Name** (Page 1, id: 2): Enter your name for the interview.
*   **Email** (Page 1, id: 3): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (Page 1, id: 4): Provide your phone number for further communication.
*   **Preferred Time** (Page 1, id: 5): Specify your preferred time slot for the interview. This is optional.
*   **Time Slot** (Page 1, id: 6): Select your preferred time slot for the interview. This is also optional.
*   **Date** (Page 1, id: 7): Enter the date of the interview.
*   **Time** (Page 1, id: 8): Specify the time of the interview.
*   **Note** (Page 1, id: 9): Add any additional comments or information about the interview.
*   **Submitted By** (Page 1, id: 10): Indicate whether this form was submitted by you or someone else.
*   **Submitted At** (Page 1, id: 11): Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
*   **Submitted Time** (Page 1, id: 12): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
*   **Submitted Note** (Page 1, id: 13): Add any additional comments about the submission process.
*   **Assigned Tool** (Page 1, id: 14): Specify whether a specific tool was assigned for the interview.
*   **Assigned User** (Page 1, id: 15): Select whether a specific user was assigned for the interview.

## Tips

*   Always ensure you fill in all required fields for accurate completion of the form.
*   Double-check your responses to avoid errors or confusion.
*   If you have any issues or questions, reach out to your administrator or support team for assistance.
