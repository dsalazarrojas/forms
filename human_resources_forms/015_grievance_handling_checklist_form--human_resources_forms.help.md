# grievance_handling_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grievance Handling Checklist Form is used to document and address grievances that employees may have against their workplace or colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all pages and fields.
2. Answer each field according to your best knowledge and understanding.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance from your supervisor or HR representative.
