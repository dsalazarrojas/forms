# Reduced Hours Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reduced Hours Agreement Form is used to document an agreement between an employee and their manager regarding reduced hours of work. It ensures that all necessary details are accurately documented and communicated between parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Reduced Hours Agreement Form, follow these steps:
- Start by selecting the agreement period during which the employee's hours will be reduced.
- Enter the employee's name as it is recorded in the HR system.
- Enter your manager's name as it is recorded in the HR system.
- Enter the employee's ID number as recorded in the HR system.
- Select the status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- Choose the start and end dates of the agreement period.
- Enter the total hours worked during the agreement period.
- Add any approval notes if necessary.
- Select the date of approval.
- Finally, have both the employee and manager sign the form, ensuring that they acknowledge the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **agreement_period** (1, date, required): Enter the start date of the agreement period. This date should mark the beginning of the time when the employee's reduced hours are in effect.
* **employee_name** (2, text, required): Enter the employee's name as it is recorded in the HR system.
* **manager_name** (3, text, required): Enter your manager's name as it is recorded in the HR system.
* **employee_id** (4, number, required): Enter the employee's ID number as recorded in the HR system.
* **agreement_status** (5, select_one, required): Select the status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Pending) to reflect the current state of the agreement.
* **start_date** (6, date, required): Enter the start date of the agreement period. This date marks the beginning of the time when the employee's reduced hours are in effect.
* **end_date** (7, date, required): Enter the end date of the agreement period. This date marks the end of the time when the employee's reduced hours are in effect.
* **hours_worked** (8, number, required): Enter the total number of hours worked during the agreement period.
* **approval_note** (9, note, required): Add any notes related to the approval process.
* **approval_date** (10, date, required): Enter the date of approval.
* **manager_signature** (11, text, required): Have your manager sign this section to acknowledge and approve the agreement.
* **employee_signature** (12, text, required): Have the employee sign this section to confirm their understanding of the agreement terms.
