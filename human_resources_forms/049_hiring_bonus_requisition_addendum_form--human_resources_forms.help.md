# hiring_bonus_requisition_addendum_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hiring bonus requisition addendum form is used to request a bonus for an employee in relation to their job performance or other relevant factors. This form should only be submitted by authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's ID number.
2. Enter the job ID number.
3. Select the bonus amount.
4. Select the bonus type (Yes/No).
5. Select the bonus frequency (Single, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, At Hire).
6. Enter the start date of the bonus period.
7. Enter the end date of the bonus period.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments.
9. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
10. Select the date and time the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id** (Employee Id, number, Required): Enter the employee's ID number assigned by the system.
* **Job Id** (Job Id, number, Optional): Enter the ID number of the job the employee is assigned to.
* **Bonus Amount** (Bonus Amount, number, Optional): Enter the amount of the bonus to be awarded.
* **Bonus Type** (Bonus Type, select_one, Optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the bonus is applicable, 'No' otherwise.
* **Bonus Frequency** (Bonus Frequency, select_one, Optional): Select the frequency of the bonus payments (Single, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, At Hire).
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, Optional): Enter the start date of the bonus period.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, Optional): Enter the end date of the bonus period.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the bonus.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, text, Optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (Submitted Date, time, Optional): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Make sure to check the form validity before submitting it to ensure that the bonus requisition is valid.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative before submitting the form.
