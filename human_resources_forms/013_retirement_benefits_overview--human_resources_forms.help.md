# Retirement Benefits Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retirement Benefits Overview form is used to gather and present information on employee retirement benefits.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form's purpose and relevance to your specific benefit package or employee.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to required and optional fields.
3.  Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes to the benefit package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Benefit Summary (benefits_summary)** (`text`, Optional): Enter a brief summary of the benefit package.
*   **Retirement Age (retirement_age)** (`number`, Optional): Enter the age at which the employee can retire.
*   **Benefit Options (benefit_options)** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Select all applicable benefits from the available options (Yes/No).
*   **Benefit Cost (benefits_cost)** (`number`, Optional): Enter the associated cost for the benefit package.
*   **Benefit Details (benefit_details)** (`text`, Optional): Enter any additional details about the benefit package.
