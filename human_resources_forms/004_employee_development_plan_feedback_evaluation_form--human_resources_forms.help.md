# employee_development_plan_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee development plan feedback evaluation form is a tool used by HR or management to assess an employee's development plan and provide guidance on their career growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the employee's development plan to understand their goals and objectives.
2. Answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Use the select options to provide feedback on the employee's development plan.
4. Provide detailed feedback in the text fields.
5. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Development Plan Rating (id: 2)**: Rate the employee's development plan on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not satisfactory" and 5 is "Excellent". This rating will help assess the effectiveness of the employee's development plan.
* **Employee Development Plan Comment (id: 3)**: Provide a brief comment on the employee's development plan. This is an opportunity to provide specific feedback on their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Employee Development Plan Suggestions (id: 4)**: Suggest areas for improvement or recommendations for the employee's development plan.
* **Employee Development Plan Actions (id: 5)**: Indicate whether the employee's development plan is actively being followed or not.
* **Employee Development Plan Status (id: 6)**: Select the current status of the employee's development plan, either Active or Inactive.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up (id: 7)**: Select a date for the next follow-up meeting or review.
* **Employee Development Plan Due Date (id: 8)**: Select the due date for the next review or evaluation.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Date (id: 9)**: Select the date for the next follow-up meeting or review.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Time (id: 10)**: Select the time for the next follow-up meeting or review.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder (id: 11)**: Select a time for the next follow-up reminder.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Frequency (id: 12)**: Select the frequency of the follow-up reminders, either Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Send To (id: 17)**: Select who to send the follow-up reminder to, either Manager, Employee, Team, Department, or Company.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Send To (id: 18)**: Select who to send the follow-up reminder to, either Manager or Employee.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Send To (id: 19)**: Select who to send the follow-up reminder to, either Team or Employee.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Send To (id: 20)**: Select who to send the follow-up reminder to, either Department or Employee.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Send To (id: 21)**: Select who to send the follow-up reminder to, either Company or Employee.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Send To (id: 22)**: Select who to send the follow-up reminder to, either Other or Employee.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Message (id: 24)**: Provide a message for the follow-up reminder.
* **Employee Development Plan Follow-up Reminder Message 1 (id: 25)**: Provide a message for the follow-up reminder.
