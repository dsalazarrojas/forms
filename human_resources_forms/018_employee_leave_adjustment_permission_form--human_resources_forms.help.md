## Purpose
The Employee Leave Adjustment Permission Form is used to request an adjustment to an existing leave schedule or to make new leave requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the leave adjustment request (Human Resources Forms or Other Forms).
2. Briefly describe the reason for the leave adjustment in the description field.
3. Choose the date and time of the leave adjustment (if applicable).
4. Enter the employee's ID number.
5. Enter the name and email of the employee's manager.
