# energy_efficiency_specialist_exit_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the offboarding process of energy efficiency specialists who are leaving the company. It is intended to collect necessary information to ensure a smooth transition and to help the company learn from the experience of the departing specialist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information, including feedback and comments.
3. Submit the form to HR personnel for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Offboarding Process**: This field is where you can provide a brief description of the offboarding process that was followed.
* **Feedback**: Please select all that apply to indicate whether you received feedback from your manager or colleagues. This field is not required.
* **Specialist ID**: Enter your employee ID number as assigned by HR.
* **Specialist Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your HR record.
* **Reason for Departure**: If you are leaving the company, please select the reason for your departure.
* **Date of Departure**: Enter the date you will be leaving the company.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager.

Note: The required fields are **Specialist ID**, **Specialist Name**, and **Date of Departure**. All other fields are optional and may be completed if you feel they are necessary.
