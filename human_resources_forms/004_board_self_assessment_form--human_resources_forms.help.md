# Board Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Self Assessment Form is a tool for board members to evaluate their own performance, understanding, and perception of the board's effectiveness. This form helps to identify areas of strength and weakness, and to provide suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and position on the board.
2. Answer each question honestly, using the provided options (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
3. Describe your strengths and areas for personal development in the text fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as a board member.
- **Your Position on the Board** (`board_position`, `text`, required): Please enter your title or role on the board.
- **Years of Service on Board** (`tenure_years`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of years you have served on the board.
- **I have a clear understanding of my role** (`role_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate your understanding of your role.
- **My responsibilities are clearly defined** (`responsibility_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate your understanding of your responsibilities.
- **Delegation within the board is effective** (`delegation_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate your perception of delegation within the board.
- **Board members collaborate effectively** (`collaboration_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Rate your perception of collaboration among board members.
- **Communication is open and transparent** (`communication_openness`, `select_one`, required): Rate your perception of board communication.
- **I fulfill my board duties effectively** (`role_fulfillment`, `select_one`, required): Rate your self-awareness of your performance in your role.
- **I have the necessary skills and knowledge for my board position** (`competency_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your self-awareness of your skills and knowledge.
- **Board and staff work well together** (`staff_collaboration`, `select_one`, required): Rate your perception of collaboration between the board and staff.
- **I understand the organization's governance structure** (`governance_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Rate your understanding of the organization's governance structure.
- **Conflicts are handled constructively** (`conflict_resolution`, `select_one`, required): Rate your perception of how conflicts are handled within the board.
- **Board decisions are well-reasoned** (`decision_making_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate your perception of board decision-making processes.
- **My work aligns with the organization's strategy** (`strategic_alignment`, `select_one`, required): Rate your perception of your alignment with the organization's strategy.
- **What are your key strengths?** (`strengths_assessment`, `text`, optional): Please describe your contributions to the board.
- **What skills would you like to improve?** (`development_areas`, `text`, optional): Please describe areas where you would like to develop your skills.
- **Suggestions to improve board effectiveness** (`board_improvements`, `text`, optional): Please provide suggestions to improve board effectiveness.
- **I am confident in the board's leadership** (`confidence_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your confidence in the board's leadership.
- **I am committed to continuing my service** (`commitment_continuation`, `select_one`, required): Rate your commitment to continuing your service on the board.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Take your time to complete each question carefully.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.
- The answers will be used for internal evaluation and improvement purposes only.
