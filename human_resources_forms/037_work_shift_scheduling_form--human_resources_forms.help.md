# Work Shift Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Shift Scheduling Form is used to collect information about employee availability and preferences for specific shifts. This data is used to create a schedule that suits the needs of both employees and the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Date
	* Start Time
	* End Time
	* Employee ID
2. Select the days of the week you are available to work from the Weekly Availability field.
3. Choose the shifts you are available to work from the Shifts Wanted field.
4. Select your preferred shift from the Preferred Shift field.
5. Identify any potential conflicts with your availability from the Conflict Availability field.
6. Add any comments or notes about your availability or shifts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you are available to work.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the start and end time of your shift.
* **Employee ID** (`number`): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Start Time** (`time`): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **End Time** (`time`): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Weekly Availability** (`select_one`): Select the days of the week you are available to work.
*   * Monday
*   * Tuesday
*   * Wednesday
*   * Thursday
*   * Friday
*   * Saturday
*   * Sunday
* **Shifts Wanted** (`select_multiple`): Choose the shifts you are available to work.
*   * Shift 1
*   * Shift 2
*   * Shift 3
* **Preferred Shift** (`select_one`): Select your preferred shift.
*   * Shift 1
*   * Shift 2
*   * Shift 3
* **Conflict Availability** (`select_multiple`): Identify any potential conflicts with your availability.
*   * Monday
*   * Tuesday
*   * Wednesday
*   * Thursday
*   * Friday
*   * Saturday
*   * Sunday
* **Conflicts** (`note`): Add any comments or notes about your availability or shifts.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`note`): Add any comments or notes from your supervisor.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your selections carefully to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR or management staff.
* This form is used to gather information for scheduling purposes only.
