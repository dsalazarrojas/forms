# HR Analytics Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's request for HR analytics consultation, including their inquiry details, analytics needs, preferred date and time, and any follow-up details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "inquiry_details" field with a brief description of the employee's request or inquiry.
2. In the "analytics_needs" field, specify what kind of analytics support you're looking for.
3. Select a preferred date for the consultation in the "preferred_date" field.
4. Choose a preferred time for the consultation in the "preferred_time" field.
5. If you need any additional information or follow-up actions, provide them in the "follow_up_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inquiry_details** (Inquiry Details, text, optional): This field is where you'll provide a brief description of the employee's request or inquiry. For example, "Request for employee engagement analysis" or "Concern about performance metrics."
* **analytics_needs** (Analytics Needs, text, optional): Here, you'll specify what kind of analytics support you're looking for. For instance, "Trend analysis" or "Data visualization."
* **preferred_date** (Preferred Date, date, optional): Select a date for the consultation that works for you. This will help us schedule the consultation at your preferred time.
* **preferred_time** (Preferred Time, time, optional): Choose a time for the consultation that suits your schedule. Keep in mind that we'll do our best to accommodate your preferred time.
* **follow_up_details** (Follow-up Details, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information or follow-up actions that you need us to take care of.
