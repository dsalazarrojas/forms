# Employee Handbooks Revision Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from employees on the current state of the employee handbook. It is a crucial part of our organization's policy review process, allowing us to stay up-to-date with the latest changes and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Submission reason" field with a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting a revision.
2.  Select all applicable sections from the "Employee handbook section" dropdown that you would like to propose revisions for.
3.  In the "Proposed revisions" field, provide a list of specific changes you would like to make to the selected sections.
4.  Enter your name in the "Name" field for identification purposes.
5.  Finally, provide any additional comments or information that you would like to share in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission reason**: <code>Submission reason</code> (Text, Optional)
	+ Please provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting a revision to the employee handbook.
* **Proposed revisions**: <code>Proposed revisions</code> (Select Multiple, Required)
	+ Choose all sections you would like to propose revisions for. Selecting "Yes" will indicate that you would like to make changes to that section.
* **Employee handbook section**: <code>Employee handbook section</code> (Select One, Optional)
	+ Choose one section that you would like to propose revisions for. This will help us identify the specific area that needs updating.
* **Name**: <code>Name</code> (Text, Optional)
	+ Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Comments**: <code>Comments</code> (Text, Required)
	+ Share any additional comments or information that you would like to provide about your proposed revisions.

## Tips
* Be specific and clear when proposing revisions to ensure that your suggestions are actionable.
* Only select sections that you have thoroughly reviewed and are familiar with.
* Keep your comments concise and focused on the proposed revisions.

Note: The fields marked as "Required" will be checked by the system, and the form will not be submitted without filling out these fields.
