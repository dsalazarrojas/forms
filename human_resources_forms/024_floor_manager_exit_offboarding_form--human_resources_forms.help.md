# Floor Manager Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Floor Manager Exit Offboarding Form is a tool used to collect information and feedback from floor managers as they exit their position, facilitating a seamless transition of tasks and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. Provide detailed responses to each question, as they will be used to create a comprehensive offboarding plan.
3. Be sure to submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General info** (`General info`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    This section is for providing basic details about your exit from the company.
- **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    Share any feedback or comments about your experience as a floor manager, including areas for improvement and suggestions for the future.
- **Transition plan** (`Transition plan`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    Describe the steps and tasks that need to be completed to ensure a smooth transition of your responsibilities and duties.
- **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    Suggest any improvements or changes that could be made to the floor management process or team.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure that your colleagues and team members can properly take over your tasks and responsibilities.
- Proofread your answers carefully to ensure that they are accurate and complete.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative or team lead for assistance.
