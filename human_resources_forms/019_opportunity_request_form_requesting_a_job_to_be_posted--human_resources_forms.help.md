# Opportunity Request Form (requesting A Job To Be Posted) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opportunity Request Form is used to gather essential information about a job posting to be created within the company.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the new job posting in the **Job Title** field. This title will be the main identifying feature of the job posting.
2.  Provide a brief description of the job in the **Job Description** field. This description should accurately reflect the job's main responsibilities and expectations.
3.  Select the **Job Type** from the provided options (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, or Temporary) that best describes the nature of the job.
4.  Enter any specific requirements or qualifications needed for the job in the **Job Requirements** field.
5.  Identify the contact person responsible for the job posting in the **Contact Person** field.
6.  Specify the department to which the job belongs in the **Department** field.
7.  Choose a **Job Posting Date** for when you want the job to be posted.
8.  Select a **Job Close Date** for when you want the job posting to be removed.
9.  Enter the work location for the job in the **Work Location** field.
10. Choose the work hours for the job from the provided options (8-5, 10-7, or Flexible).
11. Select the contact method (Email, Phone, or Phone and Email) for the contact person in the **Contact Method** field.
12. Enter the contact phone number for the contact person in the **Contact Phone** field.
13. Enter the contact email address for the contact person in the **Contact Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a clear and concise title that accurately describes the job position.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the job, including its main responsibilities and expectations.
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of job that best fits its nature.
*   **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `text`, required: false): Add any specific requirements or qualifications needed for the job posting.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Identify the contact person responsible for the job posting.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Specify the department to which the job belongs.
*   **Job Posting Date** (`job_posting_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date for when you want the job to be posted.
*   **Job Close Date** (`job_close_date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for when you want the job posting to be removed.
*   **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the work will be performed.
*   **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the work hours for the job from the provided options.
*   **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the method of contact for the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number for the contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email address for the contact person.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Double-check that the job posting does not duplicate an existing one.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields or have further questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your departmental manager or the person in charge of job postings.
