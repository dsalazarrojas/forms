# Retirement And Health Savings Contributions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to employee requests for changes to their retirement and health plan contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have all the necessary information to complete the form accurately. This includes any relevant documentation or records from previous contributions. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative for assistance.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your retirement plan in the "Retirement Plan" field.
2. Enter the amount you would like to contribute to your health savings plan in the "Health Savings Contribution" field.
3. If you would like to contribute to the health flex plan, enter the amount in the "Health Flex Contribution" field.
4. Enter the date you would like to make changes to your contributions in the "Date of Change" field.
5. Enter the effective date for your health flex plan changes in the "Health Flex Effective Date" field.
6. Enter the effective date for your retirement plan changes in the "Retirement Plan Effective Date" field.
7. If you have a reason for your request, enter it in the "Request Reason" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Retirement Plan
* **Retirement Plan** (`retirement_plan`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current or proposed retirement plan.

### Health Savings Contribution
* **Health Savings Contribution** (`health_savings_contribution`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you would like to contribute to your health savings plan.

### Health Flex Contribution
* **Health Flex Contribution** (`health_flex_contribution`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you would like to contribute to your health flex plan. This field is only required if you are contributing to the health flex plan.

### Date of Change
* **Date of Change** (`date_of_change`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to make changes to your contributions.

### Health Flex Effective Date
* **Health Flex Effective Date** (`health_flex_effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the effective date for your health flex plan changes.

### Retirement Plan Effective Date
* **Retirement Plan Effective Date** (`retirement_plan_effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the effective date for your retirement plan changes.

### Request Reason
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, `text`, optional): Enter a reason for your request, if applicable. This information will be used for reference purposes only.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information and documentation before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
