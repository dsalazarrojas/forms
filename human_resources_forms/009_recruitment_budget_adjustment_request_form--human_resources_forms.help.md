# Recruitment Budget Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Budget Adjustment Request Form is used to submit requests for changes to the recruitment budget associated with a specific job title.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the reason for your request in the "Reason for Request" field.
2. Enter the amount of the budget adjustment in the "Budget Adjustment Amount" field.
3. Enter the new budget for the job title in the "New Budget" field.
4. Enter a brief description of the adjustment in the "Adjustment Description" field.
5. Enter your name and department in the "Submitter" and "Submitter Department" fields.
6. Enter your position and email address in the "Submitter Position" and "Submitter Email" fields.
7. Select the status of your request from the "Review Status" field.
8. Enter any comments for the reviewer in the "Review Comments" field.
9. Enter the name and department of the reviewer in the "Reviewer" and "Reviewer Department" fields.
10. Enter the email address and phone number of the reviewer in the "Reviewer Email" and "Reviewer Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (recruitment_budget_adjustment_form):
	+ A brief title for the form.
* **Job Title** (job_title):
	+ Enter the job title associated with the budget adjustment request.
* **Reason for Request** (reason_for_request):
	+ A brief description of the reason for the budget adjustment request.
* **Budget Adjustment Amount** (budget_adjustment_amount):
	+ The amount of the budget adjustment.
* **New Budget** (new_budget):
	+ The new budget for the job title.
* **Adjustment Description** (adjustment_description):
	+ A brief description of the adjustment.
* **Submitter** (submitter):

	+ Your department.

	+ Comments for the reviewer.

	+ The name of the reviewer.

	+ The department of the reviewer.

	+ The email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (reviewer_phone):
	+ The phone number of the reviewer.

## Tips
- Be accurate and detailed when filling out this form.
- Ensure that your request is well-justified and reasonable.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Duplicate requests will be reviewed carefully to avoid unnecessary adjustments.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact your department administrator.
