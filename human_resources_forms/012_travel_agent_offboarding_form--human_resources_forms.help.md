# Travel Agent Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document an employee's travel details and reason for departure when offboarding from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your employee information, including your name, manager's name, and other optional details.
2. Select your travel destination from the options provided.
3. Provide the reason for your departure from the company.
4. Enter your travel dates (both departure and return).
5. If applicable, provide your travel agent's name and email.
6. Add any additional comments or observations about your offboarding in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information (Employee Info)** (`employee_info`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is required and is where you should enter your name.
* **Manager Info (Manager Info)** (`manager_info`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is required and is where you should enter your manager's name.
* **Travel Details (Travel Details)** (`travel_details`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is required and is where you should enter any details about your travel.
* **Reason for Departure (Reason for Departure)** (`reason_for_departure`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Travel Destination (Travel Destination)** (`travel_destination`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose one destination from the options provided.
* **Travel Date (Travel Date)** (`travel_date`, `date`, required):
	+ Enter your travel date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Return Date (Return Date)** (`return_date`, `date`, required):
	+ Enter your return date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Travel Agent (Travel Agent)** (`travel_agent`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your travel agent's name if applicable.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address if applicable.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, `text`, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or observations about your offboarding.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Select all relevant reasons for departure.
* Enter your travel dates accurately.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department.
