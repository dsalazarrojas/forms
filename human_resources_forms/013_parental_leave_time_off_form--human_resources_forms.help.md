# Parental Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request parental leave for an employee. It captures necessary information to process the leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name.
2. Enter the employee's job title.
3. Select the start and end dates of the leave.
4. Choose the reason for the leave (parental leave for adoption, parental leave for pregnancy, or other).
5. Select the pay type (paid, unpaid, or unavailable).
6. Enter the number of days of leave.
7. (Optional) Enter the leave starting date and time.
8. Enter the pay amount (if paid leave).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their HR records.
  * Required field.
- **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title as it appears on their HR records.
  * Required field.
- **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the leave in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
  * Required field.
- **End Date**: Enter the end date of the leave in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
  * Required field.
- **Reason for Leave**: Select the reason for the leave from the dropdown menu.
  * Required field.
  * Please select one of the provided options.
- **Number of Days**: Enter the number of days the employee will be taking for leave.
  * Optional field.
  * If not filled, the system will default to a standard number of days for the selected reason.
- **Pay Type**: Select the pay type from the dropdown menu.
  * Required field.
  * Please select one of the provided options.
- **Leave Starting Date**: Enter the time of day the leave starts.
  * Optional field.
  * Format: HH:MM (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
- **Pay**: Enter the pay amount if the leave is paid.
  * Optional field.
  * Format: Number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understood the company's parental leave policy before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
* This form will be reviewed and verified by HR before approval.
