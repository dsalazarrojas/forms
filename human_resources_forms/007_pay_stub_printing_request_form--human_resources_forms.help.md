# Pay Stub Printing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request printing of their pay stubs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your pay rate from the options provided in the "Pay Rate" field.
5. Indicate how frequently you want your pay stubs printed by selecting an option in the "Pay Frequency" field.
6. Schedule the printing of your pay stubs by entering a date in the "Delivery Date" field and a time in the "Delivery Time" field.
7. Choose your department from the options provided in the "Department" field.
8. Enter the name of your manager in the "Manager Name" field.
9. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
10. Enter your manager's phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
11. Add any additional notes for your manager in the "Departmental Note" field.
12. Enter the name of the payroll manager in the "Payroll Manager Name" field.
13. Enter the date and time you are submitting the request in the "Request Date" and "Request Time" fields.
14. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
15. Indicate your employee status by selecting an option in the "Employee Status" field.
16. Track the status of your submission by selecting an option in the "Form Submission Status" field.
17. Record the time of submission in the "Form Submission Time" field.
18. Indicate whether the departmental manager is aware of the request by selecting an option in the "Departmental Manager" field.
19. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
20. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
21. Add any additional departmental notes in the "Departmental Note 2" field.
22. Track the status of submission by selecting an option in the "Submission Status" field.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are submitting this form for your own pay stub printing request.
* Please ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information for your manager and departmental notes.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your manager or payroll manager.
