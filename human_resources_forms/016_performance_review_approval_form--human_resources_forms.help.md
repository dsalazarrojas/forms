# Performance Review Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Review Approval Form is a document used to review and approve employee performance and progress. This form is designed to help managers and reviewers track and evaluate employee performance, identify areas of improvement, and provide feedback and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for the performance review period you are evaluating (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
2. Choose the frequency of reviews (Annually, Semi-Annually, Quarterly, or Bi-Annually).
3. Select the submission status of the performance review (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
4. Enter the manager's name and email.
5. Enter the manager's phone number (optional).
6. Select the performance rating of the employee (Excellent, Good, Fair).
7. Select the approval status of the performance review (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
8. Enter the approval date (optional).
9. Add any reviewer comments or notes.
10. Add any manager comments or notes.
11. Select the review form status (In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled).
12. Add any review form comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Form** (`review_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the performance review.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the manager responsible for the performance review.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the manager responsible for the performance review.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the manager responsible for the performance review.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_multiple, required/true): Select the performance rating of the employee (Excellent, Good, Fair).
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required/true): Select the approval status of the performance review (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of approval (optional).
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, note, required/optional): Enter the signature of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Note** (`reviewer_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments from the reviewer.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required/optional): Enter the signature of the manager.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments from the manager.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of submission (optional).
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of submission (optional).
* **Submission By** (`submission_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the person who submitted the performance review.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about the submission.
* **Review Form Status** (`review_form_status`, select_one, required/true): Select the status of the performance review (In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled).
* **Review Form Comments** (`review_form_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes about the performance review.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, select_one, required/true): Select the frequency of performance reviews (Annually, Semi-Annually, Quarterly, or Bi-Annually).
* **Review Period** (`review_period`, select_one, required/true): Select the performance review period (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required/true): Select the status of the submission (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of submission (optional).
* **Submission By** (`submission_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the person who submitted the performance review.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about the submission.

Tips:

* Make sure to select the correct review period and frequency for accurate tracking and evaluation.
* Ensure that the submission status is accurate to reflect the current state of the performance review.
* Enter any necessary comments or notes to provide feedback and guidance to the employee.
* Review and review the performance review form carefully to ensure all necessary information is provided.
* Update the status of the performance review form regularly to reflect any changes or updates.
