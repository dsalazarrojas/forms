# Phone Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phone Interview form is a tool used by HR (Human Resources) to collect information about a candidate's contact details for phone interviews or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in your **phone_interview_form** (id: 1), which is a brief description of your phone interview.
3. Enter your **first_name** (id: 2), which is your first name.
4. Enter your **last_name** (id: 3), which is your last name.
5. Select your **position** (id: 4) from the dropdown menu, which indicates your role in the company.
6. Enter your **email** (id: 5) address, which is your personal or professional email.
7. Enter your **phone_number** (id: 6), which is your phone number.
8. Choose a valid **date** (id: 7) for your phone interview from the calendar picker.
9. Enter the **time** (id: 8) of your phone interview, which should be in 24-hour format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Phone Interview Form (id: 1)**: A brief description of your phone interview.
* **First Name (id: 2)**: Your personal first name.
* **Last Name (id: 3)**: Your personal last name.
* **Position (id: 4)**: Your role in the company. Options: 
  - Job candidate
  - Job manager
  - Job director
* **Email (id: 5)**: Your personal or professional email address.
* **Phone Number (id: 6)**: Your phone number.
* **Date (id: 7)**: A valid date for your phone interview. Use the calendar picker to select a date.
* **Time (id: 8)**: The time of your phone interview, in 24-hour format.
