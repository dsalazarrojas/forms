# remote_employee_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The remote_employee_offboarding_form is used to collect information about an employee's departure from the company. This form helps to document the reason for leaving, last day of service, and other important details to facilitate a successful offboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's ID number.
2. Select the job title of the departing employee.
3. Choose the reason for the employee's departure.
4. Enter the last date of service.
5. Specify the notice period.
6. Select whether the employee will be receiving benefits.
7. Indicate whether the employee has a travel request pending.
8. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id**: Enter the employee's ID number.
* **job_title**: Select the job title of the departing employee.
* **reason_for_departure**: Choose the reason for the employee's departure (Yes/No).
* **last_date_of_service**: Enter the last date of service.
* **notice_period**: Specify the notice period.
* **benefits**: Select whether the employee will be receiving benefits (Yes/No).
* **last_date_of_travel_request**: Indicate whether the employee has a travel request pending (Yes/No).
* **_prophets**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding prophets.
* **_action_items**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding action items.
* **_offboarding_status**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding offboarding status.
* **_employee_status_update**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding employee status update.
* **_next_steps**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding next steps.
* **_offboarding_notes**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding offboarding process.
* **_action_items_follow_up**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding action items follow-up.
* **_rehire**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding rehire.
* **_rehire_status**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding rehire status.
* **_next_steps_for_employees**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding next steps for employees.
* **_offboarding_status_updates**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding offboarding status updates.
* **_offboarding_process**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding offboarding process.
* **_final_checklist**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding final checklist.
* **_exit_survey**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding exit survey.
* **_action_items_1**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding action items 1.
* **_action_items_2**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding action items 2.

Note: Please fill in the fields as necessary and add any additional comments or notes as required.
