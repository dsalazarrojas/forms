# Temporary Staffing Authorization Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request temporary staffing authorization from management for a specific job role, shift, and time period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field:
	* **Job Role**: Enter the job title or role for which you are requesting temporary staffing.
	* **Duration**: Enter the number of days or hours you need the temporary staff for.
	* **Shift Start Date**: Enter the date when you want the temporary staff to start working.
	* **Shift End Date**: Enter the date when you want the temporary staff to end working.
	* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time of day when you want the temporary staff to start working.
	* **Shift End Time**: Enter the time of day when you want the temporary staff to end working.
	* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person you can contact for further questions or concerns.
	* **Shift Details**: Enter any additional information about the shift, such as location or specific duties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Role** (`Job Role`, text, required): Enter the job title or role for which you are requesting temporary staffing.
* **Duration** (`Duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days or hours you need the temporary staff for.
* **Shift Start Date** (`Shift Start Date`, date, required): Enter the date when you want the temporary staff to start working.
* **Shift End Date** (`Shift End Date`, date, required): Enter the date when you want the temporary staff to end working.
* **Shift Start Time** (`Shift Start Time`, time, required): Enter the time of day when you want the temporary staff to start working.
* **Shift End Time** (`Shift End Time`, time, required): Enter the time of day when you want the temporary staff to end working.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person you can contact for further questions or concerns.
* **Shift Details** (`Shift Details`, note, required): Enter any additional information about the shift, such as location or specific duties.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to ensure a smooth temporary staffing process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the person listed as the Contact Person.
* This form will be reviewed and approved by management before temporary staffing can be arranged.
* Please note that this form may be subject to change or modification as needed.
* If you are requesting temporary staffing for an unexpected or emergency situation, please include as much detail as possible to help facilitate a prompt response.
