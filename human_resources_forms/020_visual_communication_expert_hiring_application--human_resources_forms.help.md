# Visual Communication Expert Hiring Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for experts in visual communication to apply for potential work opportunities. If you are interested in hiring a visual communication expert or have a specific project in mind that requires visual communication expertise, this is the place to find the right person for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide as much information as possible to showcase your expertise and availability. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address where you can be reached for further communication. (Required)
* **Resume**: Select one or more options to share with us, or describe your portfolio in the field below. (Optional)
* **Portfolio**: If you select "Other", please provide a brief description of your portfolio in this field. (Optional)
* **Portfolio Link**: Enter the link to your portfolio website, Behance profile, or other relevant online platform. (Required)
* **LinkedIn**: Enter your LinkedIn profile URL if you have one. (Optional)
* **Behance**: Enter your Behance profile URL if you have one. (Optional)
* **Other (Specify below)**: If you have a portfolio that doesn't fit the options above, describe it briefly. (Optional)
* **Type of Portfolio**: Select the type of portfolio that best describes your work. (Optional)
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have spent working in the field of visual communication. (Required)
* **Most Recent Job Title**: Enter your current or most recent job title. (Optional)
* **Education**: Enter any relevant education or training you have received. (Optional)
* **Availability**: Select the days you are available to work. (Required)
* **Rate**: Enter your hourly rate or desired hourly rate. (Optional)
* **Portfolio Description**: Provide a brief description of your portfolio and the kind of projects you have worked on. (Optional)
* **Availability Notes**: If you have any availability notes or special requests, enter them here. (Optional)
* **Work Hours**: Enter the hours you are available to work per day. (Required)
* **Frequency**: Select how frequently you are available to work. (Optional)
* **Available Days**: Select the days you are available to work. (Required)
* **Availability Other (Specify below)**: If you have any other availability that isn't listed, describe it briefly. (Optional)
* **Availability Other Frequency**: If you have any other availability that isn't listed, describe how frequently you are available. (Optional)
