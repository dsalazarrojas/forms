# Restaurant Manager Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Manager Interview Form is designed to collect data about a restaurant manager interview, including restaurant details, candidate information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you're not duplicating any existing records.
2. Fill out the form by entering the requested information in each field.
3. Be precise and accurate when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Restaurant Details
* **Restaurant Name (1)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the restaurant where the manager works or will be working.
* **Candidate Name (2)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the candidate being interviewed.
* **Contact Number (3)** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact number of the candidate or the restaurant.
* **Position Title (4)** (Text, Optional): Enter the position title of the candidate.

### Page 2: Candidate Information
* **Work Experience (5)** (Text, Optional): Describe the candidate's work experience related to the position.
* **Education Background (6)** (Text, Optional): Enter the candidate's education background, including degrees or certifications.
* **Available Start Date (7)** (Date, Optional): If applicable, enter the date when the candidate is expected to start work.
* **Expected Finish Date (8)** (Date, Optional): If applicable, enter the date when the candidate is expected to finish work.

### Page 3: Additional Details
* **Salary Requirement (9)** (Text, Optional): Describe the candidate's salary requirement for the position.
* **References (10)** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant references or comments about the candidate.
 
## Tips
- Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any information, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
- If you're unable to complete the form, contact the relevant personnel for help.
