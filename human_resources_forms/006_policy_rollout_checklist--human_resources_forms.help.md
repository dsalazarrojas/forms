# Policy Rollout Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document information related to the rollout of policies within the organization. It helps to track the progress and status of policy implementation, ensuring a smooth and error-free process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the policy guidelines and understand the requirements for implementation.
2. Select the assignee(s) involved in the policy rollout (Employee, Manager, or HR Representative).
3. Provide a clear and concise description of the policy rollout.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Rollout Checklist** (`policy_rollout_checklist_page_3`, text, required: false): 
  This field is the main area for describing the policy rollout. Please provide a detailed account of the policy, including its purpose, scope, and any relevant background information.
* **Assignee Information** (`assignee_info_page_2`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose one or more assignees who are involved in the policy rollout. Select all applicable assignees, such as Employees, Managers, or HR Representatives.
  - Example: Select all employees who will be affected by the policy change, or choose specific managers who need to review and implement the policy.
* **Policy Rollout Checklist** (`policy_rollout_checklist_page_1`, text, required: false): 
  Use this field to record any notes or comments about the policy rollout, such as action items, deadlines, or follow-up tasks.
  - Example: Document any specific actions or next steps required for successful policy implementation.

## Tips
- Make sure to clearly describe the policy and its purpose in the Policy Rollout Checklist field.
- Be as specific as possible when selecting assignees, ensuring that all relevant individuals are included.
- Use the Policy Rollout Checklist field to track progress and note any issues or concerns that arise during the policy rollout.
