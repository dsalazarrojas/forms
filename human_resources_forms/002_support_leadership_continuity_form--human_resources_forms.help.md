# Support Leadership Continuity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Leadership Continuity Form is used to identify and document potential successors to key leadership positions and the resources needed to ensure leadership continuity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you are completing it for the correct purpose.
2. Answer each question in the provided fields.
3. If you are unsure about any question or field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
4. Submit the completed form to the designated recipient for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Succession Status**: A brief description of the current status of leadership succession for this position. This field is optional, but can be useful for tracking progress.
* **Potential Successors**: Select all applicable successors from the dropdown list. This field is not required, but can help identify potential candidates for leadership roles.
* **Resource Needs**: Provide a list of resources currently available to support the position, as well as any additional resources that will be needed for a smooth transition. This field is not required, but can help ensure leadership continuity.
* **Current Leadership**: A brief description of the current leadership holding this position. This field is not required, but can be useful for historical context.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is intended to be used for succession planning and leadership handoffs.
