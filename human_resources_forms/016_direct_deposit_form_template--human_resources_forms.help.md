# Direct Deposit Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for direct deposit processing. It captures details about the employee, employer, and direct deposit settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the form by entering the employee's details in the "Form Data Section" (page 1).
2. Select the employer's information in the "Employer Information" section (page 2).
3. Choose the authorized payee in the "Authorized Payee Information" section (page 3).
4. Select the employees to be included in the direct deposit (page 4).
5. Set the direct deposit frequency (page 5).
6. Enter the direct deposit amount (page 6).
7. Choose the direct deposit method (page 7).
8. Add any additional information (page 8).
9. Sign and date the form in the "Employee Signature Section" (page 9).
10. Sign and date the form in the "Employer Signature Section" (page 10).
11. Review and sign the form in the "Authorized Signature 2 Section" (page 11).
12. Review and sign the form in the "Authorized Signature 3 Section" (page 12).
13. Review and sign the form in the "Authorized Signature 4 Section" (page 13).
14. Review and sign the form in the "Authorized Signature 5 Section" (page 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data Section (1)**: Enter the employee's details, such as first name, last name, and address.
* **Employer Information (2)**: Select the employer's information for the employee.
* **Authorized Payee Information (3)**: Select the authorized payee for the direct deposit.
* **Employees Section (4)**: Select the employees to be included in the direct deposit.
* **Payee Section (5)**: This field is not applicable.
* **Direct Deposit Frequency Section (6)**: Set the direct deposit frequency.
* **Direct Deposit Amount Section (7)**: Enter the direct deposit amount.
* **Direct Deposit Method Section (8)**: Choose the direct deposit method.
* **Additional Information Section (9)**: Add any additional information about the direct deposit.
* **Employee Signature Section (10)**: Sign and date the form by the employee.
* **Employer Signature Section (11)**: Sign and date the form by the employer.
* **Authorized Signature 2 Section (12)**: Review and sign the form by the authorized representative.
* **Authorized Signature 3 Section (13)**: Review and sign the form by the authorized representative.
* **Authorized Signature 4 Section (14)**: Review and sign the form by the authorized representative.
* **Authorized Signature 5 Section (15)**: Review and sign the form by the authorized representative.
* **Review 1 Section (16)**: Review the form by the employee.
* **Authorized Signature 6 Section (17)**: This field is not applicable.
* **Review 2 Section (18)**: Review the form by the employer.
* **Authorized Signature 7 Section (19)**: This field is not applicable.
* **Review 3 Section (20)**: Review the form by the authorized representative.
* **Authorized Signature 8 Section (21)**: This field is not applicable.
* **Review 4 Section (22)**: Review the form by the authorized representative.
* **Authorized Signature 9 Section (23)**: This field is not applicable.
* **Review 5 Section (24)**: Review the form by the authorized representative.
* **Authorized Signature 10 Section (25)**: This field is not applicable.

Note: Some fields are not applicable, but are included for completeness.
