# Case Study Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for applicants to submit their case studies for job applications. It ensures that the submission process is completed efficiently and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Submission ID Number**: Enter the reference number of the case study submission.
2. **Submission Date**: Enter the date of the case study submission.
3. **Applicant Information**: Enter your personal details, including your full name, email address, phone number, and address.
4. **Case Study Information**: Enter the title, topic, and due date of the case study.
5. **Submission Format**: Select the format of your case study submission (e.g., PDF document, Word document, etc.).
6. **Files Attached**: Specify whether you have attached all relevant files or not.
7. **List of File Names**: If you have attached files, list their names.
8. **Executive Summary**: Provide a brief overview of your case study.
9. **Problem Statement**: Explain the problem you addressed in the case study.
10. **Methodology**: Describe the approach you took to complete the case study.
11. **Key Findings**: Highlight the main discoveries you made during the case study.
12. **Recommendations**: Propose solutions to the problem you addressed.
13. **Implementation Plan**: Outline how you would implement your proposed solutions.
14. **Expected Outcomes**: Project the results of your case study.
15. **Challenges Encountered**: Describe any difficulties you faced during the case study.
16. **Assumptions Made**: Explain the assumptions you made during the case study.
17. **Data Sources**: List the information sources you used.
18. **Tools or Frameworks Used**: Describe the tools or frameworks you employed.
19. **Original Work Declaration**: Confirm that your case study is your original work.
20. **Authenticity Confirmation**: Confirm that your case study is authentic and not copied from others.
21. **AI Tools Usage**: Disclose whether you used AI tools and how.
22. **Confidentiality Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to keep the assignment confidential.
23. **Availability for Presentation**: Specify whether you are available to present your case study.
24. **Presentation Dates**: Provide your availability for presentation, if applicable.
25. **Questions or Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments.
