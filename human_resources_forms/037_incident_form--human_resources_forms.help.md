# Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Form is a structured template for documenting and tracking incidents within your organization. It helps to gather relevant information and facilitate review and closure of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Incident Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the incident date field with the date of the incident.
2. Select the incident type from the dropdown menu to categorize the incident.
3. Enter the location of the incident in the text field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the description field.
5. Select the status of the incident from the multi-select field.
6. Add any relevant notes for investigation in the incident investigation field.
7. Choose the outcome of the incident from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter the reviewer's name or ID in the incident reviewer field.
9. Enter the date of the review in the incident review date field.
10. If necessary, enter the date the incident was closed.
