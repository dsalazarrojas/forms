# Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Form is a structured template for documenting and tracking incidents within your organization. It helps to gather relevant information and facilitate review and closure of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Incident Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the incident date field with the date of the incident.
2. Select the incident type from the dropdown menu to categorize the incident.
3. Enter the location of the incident in the text field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the description field.
5. Select the status of the incident from the multi-select field.
6. Add any relevant notes for investigation in the incident investigation field.
7. Choose the outcome of the incident from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter the reviewer's name or ID in the incident reviewer field.
9. Enter the date of the review in the incident review date field.
10. If necessary, enter the date the incident was closed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Date**: A date field to record the date of the incident. 
  - Type: date 
  - Required: false 

- **Incident Type**: A dropdown selection field to categorize the incident. 
  - Type: select_one 
  - Required: true 

- **Incident Location**: A text field to record the location of the incident. 
  - Type: text 
  - Required: false 

- **Incident Description**: A text field to provide a detailed description of the incident. 
  - Type: text 
  - Required: false 

- **Incident Status**: A multi-select field to track the status of the incident. 
  - Type: select_multiple 
  - Required: true 

- **Incident Investigation**: A note field to record notes related to the incident investigation. 
  - Type: note 
  - Required: false 

- **Incident Outcome**: A dropdown selection field to track the outcome of the incident. 
  - Type: select_one 
  - Required: false 

- **Incident Reviewer**: A text field to record the reviewer's name or ID. 
  - Type: text 
  - Required: true 

- **Incident Review Date**: A date field to record the date of the review. 
  - Type: date 
  - Required: false 

- **Incident Close Date**: A date field to record the date the incident was closed. 
  - Type: date 
  - Required: false 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check the incident type and status to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
- Use the incident investigation field to capture relevant notes and details.
- Review and close the incident according to your organization's procedures and guidelines.
