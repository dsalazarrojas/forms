# Compensatory Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for requesting compensatory time off (comp time) from your accrued balance. Compensatory time is a benefit provided to employees as a form of compensation for work performed beyond regular working hours. The form is intended to be filled out by employees who have worked extra hours and wish to request time off as compensation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure you have enough comp time balance to cover the requested hours.
3.  Check the "I understand that comp time must be used within the fiscal year" agreement box.
4.  Verify the form fields for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name_ct`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your employment documents or ID.
*   **Department** (`department_ct`, text, required): Provide the name of your department as it is listed in your employee directory or as you know it.
*   **Approving Manager** (`manager_name_ct`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct manager who authorized the original overtime hours.
*   **Date Overtime was Worked** (`date_overtime_worked`, date, required): Provide the specific date the extra hours were performed.
*   **Number of Overtime Hours Worked** (`overtime_hours_worked`, number, required): Enter the total hours worked beyond regular working hours (e.g., 4.5).
*   **Reason for Overtime** (`reason_for_overtime`, text, required): Briefly describe the project or task that required the extra hours.
*   **Proposed Date for Comp Time Off** (`proposed_comp_time_date`, date, required): Choose a date when you wish to take your compensatory time off.
*   **Total Hours of Comp Time Requested** (`comp_time_hours_requested`, number, required): Enter the total hours of comp time you are requesting, which should not exceed the hours worked.
*   **Current Accrued Comp Time Balance** (`current_comp_balance`, number, optional): If known, enter your existing balance before this request.
*   **I understand that comp time must be used within the fiscal year** (`policy_agreement_ct`, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm your understanding of HR policy.
*   **Date of Submission** (`ct_request_submission_date`, date, required): The date the form is being submitted.

Note: Please ensure to review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the request.
