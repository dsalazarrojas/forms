# Human System Pressure Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human System Pressure Assessment is a form designed to evaluate the current state of pressure and satisfaction levels among employees. It is used to identify areas that need improvement and provide insights for creating a more supportive and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, based on your current experience and feelings.
2. For select_one fields, choose the answer that best describes your situation.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all that apply.
4. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer.
5. If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the current state of workplace pressure?** (`workplace_pressure`, text): Describe the current state of pressure in the workplace.
* **Select the current pressure level** (`work_pressure_level`, select_one): Choose from High, Medium, Low.
* **How long has this pressure been present?** (`work_pressure_duration`, text): Describe how long the pressure has been present.
* **How many team members report feeling pressure?** (`team_pressure`, select_multiple): Choose from Many, Few, None.
* **How many managers report feeling pressure?** (`manager_pressure`, select_multiple): Choose from Many, Few, None.
* **Do you have a healthy work-life balance?** (`work_life_balance`, select_one): Choose from True, False.
* **How often do you receive employee support?** (`employee_support`, select_one): Choose from Often, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **How do you handle work-family conflicts?** (`work_family_life`, text): Describe how you handle work-family conflicts.
* **Are you experiencing any health issues due to work pressure?** (`work_health`, select_one): Choose from True, False.
* **What stress management techniques are you using?** (`stress_management`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply (Mindfulness, Exercise, Meditation).
* **How often do you receive manager support?** (`manager_support`, select_one): Choose from Often, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **What causes pressure at work?** (`employee_pressure_sources`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply (Workload, Workload/Deadlines, Workload/Deadlines and Work-life balance, Workload/Deadlines and Work-life balance, Other).
* **What is the most stressful work environment aspect?** (`work_environment`, text): Describe the most stressful work environment aspect.
* **What causes team dysfunction?** (`team_dysfunction`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply (Communication problems, Conflicting interests, Other).
* **Are you satisfied with work schedules?** (`work_schedules`, select_one): Choose from Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Is your work space comfortable?** (`work_space`, select_one): Choose from Very comfortable, Somewhat comfortable, Neutral, Somewhat uncomfortable, Very uncomfortable.
* **How would you rate team leadership?** (`team_leadership`, select_one): Choose from Very effective, Somewhat effective, Neutral, Somewhat ineffective, Very ineffective.
* **Is your work equipment adequate?** (`work_equipment`, select_one): Choose from Very adequate, Somewhat adequate, Neutral, Somewhat inadequate, Very inadequate.
* **Is work-life balance adequate?** (`work_life_balance_adequate`, select_one): Choose from Very adequate, Somewhat adequate, Neutral, Somewhat inadequate, Very inadequate.
* **What causes team dysfunction?** (`team_dysfunction_causes`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply (Communication problems, Conflicting interests, Other).
* **Satisfaction with work schedules?** (`work_schedules_satisfaction`, select_one): Choose from Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **How satisfied are you with your current role?** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one): Choose from Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **How do you reduce work pressure?** (`work_pressure_reduction`, select_multiple): Choose all that apply (Mindfulness, Exercise, Meditation).
* **How satisfied are you with the work environment?** (`work_environment_satisfaction`, select_one): Choose from Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Is employee satisfaction adequate?** (`employee_satisfaction_adequate`, select_one): Choose from Very adequate, Somewhat adequate, Neutral, Somewhat inadequate, Very inadequate.
