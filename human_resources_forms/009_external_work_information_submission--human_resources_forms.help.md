# external_work_information_submission - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about external work activities for HR-related purposes. This includes details about external work locations, agencies, and job-related information. The form aims to gather accurate and comprehensive data to support HR operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with accurate and complete data.
3. Double-check your input for errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **External Work Activity**: A brief description of the external work activity or project.
* **Employer**: The name of the external organization or agency you worked with.
* **Job Title**: Your job title or position during the external work activity.
* **Employment Type**: The type of employment or contract you had with the external agency (e.g., internship, contract, etc.).
* **Job Classification**: A classification or category of the external work activity (e.g., administrative, technical, etc.).
* **Supervisor**: The name of your supervisor or manager during the external work activity.
* **Department**: The department or team you worked with at the external agency.
* **Job Start Date**: The date you started working for the external agency.
* **Job End Date**: The date you ended working for the external agency.
* **Pay Rate**: Your hourly or salary rate for the external work activity.
* **External Agency**: The name of the external agency or organization you worked with.
* **External Agency Contact**: The contact person or representative of the external agency.
* **External Agency Address**: The address of the external agency.
* **External Work Location**: The location where you worked for the external agency.
* **External Agency Phone**: The phone number of the external agency.
* **External Work Location Map**: A note field for any additional information about the work location.
* **External Work Location Map URL**: The URL of a map or location-specific resource.
* **External Agency Email**: The email address of the external agency.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of your input, especially dates and contact information.
* Ensure that you've completed all required fields, marked as required.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.
