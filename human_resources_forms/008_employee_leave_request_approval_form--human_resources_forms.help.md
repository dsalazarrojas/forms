# Employee Leave Request Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Leave Request Approval Form" is used to submit a request for time off from work, including vacation, sick leave, or other types of absences. This form helps streamline the request process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and reviewed by the approver before the leave is approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you would like to start your leave in the "leave_start_date" field.
2. Enter the date you would like to end your leave in the "leave_end_date" field.
3. Provide a detailed reason for your leave in the "reason_for_leave" field.
4. If applicable, enter the pay rate and total pay request in the "pay_rate" and "total_pay_request" fields.
5. Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, sick leave, or other) in the "leave_type" field.
6. Select the status of your approval (e.g., pending, other) in the "approval_status" field.
7. Enter any comments or notes regarding your leave request in the "comments" field.
8. Enter the name of the approver who will review your request in the "approver_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **submit_request**: Date field to select when you would like to submit your leave request.
* **leave_start_date**: Date field to enter the start date of your leave.
* **leave_end_date**: Date field to enter the end date of your leave.
* **reason_for_leave**: Text field to provide a detailed reason for your leave.
* **pay_rate**: Number field to enter your pay rate (if applicable).
* **total_pay_request**: Number field to enter the total pay request (if applicable).
* **leave_duration**: Number field to enter the duration of your leave (if applicable).
* **leave_type**: Select field to select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., vacation, sick leave, or other).
* **approval_status**: Select field to select the status of your approval (e.g., pending, other).
* **comments**: Text field to enter any comments or notes regarding your leave request.
* **approver_name**: Text field to enter the name of the approver who will review your request.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide all required information to ensure your leave request is properly reviewed and processed.
* Double-check the dates and pay rates to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or approver.
