# Trade Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a trade reference from a professional contact. The reference will be used for future job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name
2. Fill in your last name
3. Enter your date of birth
4. Enter your job title
5. Enter your work start date
6. Enter your work end date (if applicable)
7. Enter your current work location
8. Enter the name of your manager
9. Enter the job title of your manager
10. Enter your contact number
11. Enter your email address
12. Enter the name of the reference
13. Enter the relationship with the reference (e.g., work colleague, mentor, etc.)
14. Enter the start date of the reference
15. Enter the end date of the reference (if applicable)
16. Enter the location of the reference
17. Enter the job title of the reference
18. Enter the reason for the reference
19. Enter any additional reference notes
20. Select the person or department that this reference will be assigned to (at least one must be selected)
21. Select all relevant individuals or departments that this reference will be assigned to (at least one must be selected)
22. Ensure that you submit the form and select the correct submitted date

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current or previous job title.
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started your current or previous job.
* **Work End Date** (`work_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you ended your current or previous job (if applicable).
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your current or previous workplace.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of your manager or supervisor.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reference provider.
* **Reference Relationship** (`reference_relationship`, text, optional): Enter your relationship with the reference provider (e.g., work colleague, mentor, etc.).
* **Reference Start Date** (`reference_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the reference.
* **Reference End Date** (`reference_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the reference (if applicable).
* **Reference Location** (`reference_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the reference.
* **Reference Job Title** (`reference_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the reference.
* **Reason for Reference** (`reason_for_reference`, text, optional): Enter the reason why you are requesting the reference.
* **Reference Notes** (`reference_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the reference.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name or alias.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name or alias of the person who last updated this form.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, optional): Enter the date this form was last updated.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person or department that this reference will be assigned to.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant individuals or departments that this reference will be assigned to.
