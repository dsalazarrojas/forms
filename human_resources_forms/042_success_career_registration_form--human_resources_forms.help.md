# success_career_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Success Career Registration Form is designed to register users for career advancement opportunities within the organization. This form will help HR teams to better understand the users' career aspirations and goals, and provide relevant career advancement opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Input field with your career aspirations and goals.
2. Select one or multiple options from the Select One and Select Multiple dropdowns.
3. Enter your desired Date and Time for career advancement opportunities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (text): This field is where you can enter your career aspirations and goals.
* **Select One** (select_one): Please select one option that best describes your career aspirations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple): Please select multiple options that best describe your career aspirations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date** (date): Enter your desired Date for career advancement opportunities.
* **Time** (time): Enter your desired Time for career advancement opportunities.
