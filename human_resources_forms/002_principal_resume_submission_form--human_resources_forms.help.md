# Principal Resume Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Principal Resume Submission Form is a tool used to create a professional and comprehensive resume for principals to showcase their background, experience, and qualifications. This form will help you to provide a clear and concise overview of your work and educational experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the sections below with your relevant information. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible to make your resume stand out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Principal Details** (`principal_details`, text, optional): This section is for providing your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that you would like to include in your resume.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload a copy of your resume, or paste the text of your resume directly into this field.
* **School Experience** (`school_experience`, text, optional): List your experience working in schools, including the name of the school, your role, and dates of employment.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): List your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and dates of graduation.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): List your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible to make your resume stand out.
* Proofread your resume for any errors before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
