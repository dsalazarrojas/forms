# work_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect detailed information about an employee's work schedule, job title, department, and other relevant work-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling out the required fields. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
2. Enter your job title in the field labeled "Job Title."
3. Select your job grade from the provided options.
4. Choose your department from the list of options.
5. Enter your manager's name and email address.
6. Select your job status, if applicable.
7. Enter your job description, job requirements, and job category.
8. Enter your job code, job level, and job class.
9. Enter your work start and end dates, if applicable.
10. Enter your work hours, if applicable.
11. Enter your work type (e.g., full-time, part-time, or freelance).
12. Select your work shift, if applicable.
13. Enter your job title code and job category code.
14. Enter your work location and work location address.
15. Choose your department, if applicable.
