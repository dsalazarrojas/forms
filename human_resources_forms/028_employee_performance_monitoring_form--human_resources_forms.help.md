# Employee Performance Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Performance Monitoring Form is used to evaluate employee performance, provide feedback and guidance, and document progress for individual employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have confirmation from your supervisor or HR department that the form has been assigned to you for the correct employee.
2. Review the form instructions and understand the purpose and requirements.
3. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing constructive feedback and guidance for the employee's performance.
4. Sign and date the form when completed to indicate approval or rejection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info** (`employee_performance_monitoring_form_employee_info`): Enter the employee's basic information. This field is optional.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the employee's name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter the employee's ID number. This field is optional.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Performance Review Period** (`performance_review_period`): Select the performance review period (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4) that this form pertains to. If not listed, enter 'Other' and provide the date in the next field.
* **Other (if not listed)** (`performance_review_period_other`): Enter the performance review period if not listed above.
* **Performance Score** (`performance_score`): Enter the employee's performance score.
* **Feedback Given** (`feedback_given`): Enter any feedback given to the employee.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`): Enter the evaluation criteria used for this performance review.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`): Enter any comments regarding the evaluation.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`): Enter any action plan provided to the employee.
* **Next Review Date** (`next_review_date`): Enter the date of the next performance review.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`): Sign and date this form to indicate approval or rejection.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign and date this form to indicate agreement or rejection.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`): Enter any notes from the employee.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`): Enter any notes from the manager.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Enter the date of approval.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`): Select the approval status (Approved or Rejected).
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`): Enter any manager comments.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`): Select the form status (Draft, Submitted, Approved, or Rejected).
