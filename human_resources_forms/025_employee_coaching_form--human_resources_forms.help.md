# Employee Coaching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Coaching Form is designed to help employees and managers record and track coaching sessions for employees. This form can be used to schedule and document coaching sessions, including identifying coaching goals and objectives, and whether the sessions are part of a formal coaching program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the fields as necessary for each coaching session.
2. Review and confirm the accuracy of the information entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Coaching Session Form** (`employee_coaching_session_form`): Please enter a brief description of the coaching session.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Coaching Sessions** (`coaching_sessions`): Select all the coaching sessions this form applies to.
	+ This field is optional and allows you to indicate which coaching sessions this form applies to.
* **Coaching Session Date** (`coaching_session_date`): Enter the date for the coaching session.
	+ This field is optional and allows you to specify the date of the coaching session.
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goals_objective`): Enter a note describing the goals and objectives for the coaching session.
	+ This field is optional and allows you to record the specific goals and objectives for the session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field as necessary for each coaching session.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* If a coaching session does not have a specific date, use the "no date" option.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your HR department for assistance.
