# Medical Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request medical leave time off from work. It is intended for employees to submit a request for leave due to medical reasons.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a category for your leave request (e.g., "Human Resources Forms").
2. Provide a brief description of your reason for requesting leave.
3. Enter your employee name.
4. Specify your medical condition (if applicable).
5. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
6. Provide any additional medical information as required.
7. Track the status of your request with your manager and the date of their approval or denial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Human Resources Forms, Other): Choose a category for your leave request.
* **Description** (text): Briefly describe your reason for requesting leave.
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Medical Condition** (text): If applicable, specify your medical condition.
* **From Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to start your leave.
* **To Date** (date): Enter the date you would like your leave to end.
* **Reason for Leave** (text): Enter a brief description of your reason for requesting leave.
* **Start Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to start your leave. If this date is not the same as the "From Date", please enter the date you actually started your leave.
* **End Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to end your leave. If this date is not the same as the "To Date", please enter the date you actually ended your leave.
* **Medical Supplemental Info** (text): Enter any additional medical information you feel is necessary.
* **Manager Approval Status** (Approved, Pending, Denied): Track the status of your request with your manager.
* **Manager Approval Date** (date): Record the date of your manager's approval or denial.

## Tips

* Be as clear and concise as possible when entering your information.
* If you have any medical documentation or evidence supporting your leave request, please attach it to your submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative or manager.
* Please ensure that you are entering the correct dates, as these will be used to track your leave.
