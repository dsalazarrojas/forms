# Leadership Transformation Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leadership Transformation Impact Survey is a tool used to assess the effectiveness of the leadership team in driving positive change and impact within the organization. The survey aims to measure the perception of employees on the current state of leadership and provide insights on areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct answer for each question based on your experience and perception.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Take care to provide clear and concise answers in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to explain the purpose and context of the survey. Please provide a short overview of the survey and its importance.
* **Employee Performance** (`employee performance`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Yes: The leadership team has positively impacted employee performance.
	+ No: The leadership team has not impacted employee performance.
* **Leadership Skills** (`leadership skills`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Effective communication and delegation.
	+ Option 2: Strong problem-solving and decision-making.
	+ Option 3: Emotional intelligence and empathy.
* **Employee Engagement** (`employee engagement`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Yes: Employees feel engaged and motivated.
	+ No: Employees feel disengaged and unmotivated.
* **Training Effectiveness** (`training effectiveness`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Relevant and timely training programs.
	+ Option 2: Access to ongoing education and professional development.
	+ Option 3: Personalized feedback and coaching.
* **Impact on Employees** (`impact on employees`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Yes: The leadership team has positively impacted employees.
	+ No: The leadership team has not impacted employees.
* **Impact on Organization** (`impact on organization`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Yes: The leadership team has positively impacted the organization.
	+ No: The leadership team has not impacted the organization.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional feedback`, note, required: false): This is a text field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Employee Prophets** (`employee prophets`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Yes: Employees feel valued and empowered.
	+ No: Employees do not feel valued and empowered.
* **Leadership Transformation** (`leadership transformation`, note, required: false): This is a text field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
