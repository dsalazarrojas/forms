# Workforce Inclusion Strategy Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees identify and request support for workforce inclusion, promoting a more inclusive and welcoming work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Describe the organization's current state in the **Organizational Context** field, providing a clear picture of the situation.
2. List the workforce inclusion challenges you are facing in the **Workforce Inclusion Challenges** field.
3. Specify the support needs you require to address these challenges in the **Support Needs** field.
4. Indicate the organizational capacity to address these challenges in the **Organizational Capacity** field.
5. Select all that apply to indicate leadership support in the **Leadership Support** field.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the **Feedback** field.
7. List specific dates for the review and resolution of these challenges in the **Review Dates** field.
8. Specify specific times for the review and resolution of these challenges in the **Review Times** field.
9. Enter your email address in the **Email** field so that we can contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizational Context** (Text, Required: False): Describe the organization's current state, including any relevant background information.
* **Workforce Inclusion Challenges** (Text, Required: False): List specific challenges you are facing related to workforce inclusion.
* **Support Needs** (Text, Required: False): Specify the support you require to address these challenges.
* **Organizational Capacity** (Number, Required: False): Indicate the organization's capacity to address these challenges.
* **Team Capacity** (Number, Required: False): Indicate the team's capacity to address these challenges.
* **Leadership Support** (Select One, Required: False): Select all that apply to indicate leadership support.
	+ Yes: Leadership is fully supportive.
	+ No: Leadership is not supportive.
* **Communication** (Text, Required: False): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Feedback** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Check all that apply to indicate areas where feedback is needed.
	+ Yes: Feedback is needed in this area.
	+ No: Feedback is not needed in this area.
* **Next Steps** (Note, Required: False): List specific next steps or actions to be taken.
* **Review Dates** (Date, Required: False): List specific dates for the review and resolution of these challenges.
* **Review Times** (Time, Required: False): List specific times for the review and resolution of these challenges.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when describing the challenges and support needs.
- Make sure to check the "Feedback" field to indicate areas where feedback is needed.
- Use the "Next Steps" field to provide a clear plan of action.
- Make sure to enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or requests for clarification.
