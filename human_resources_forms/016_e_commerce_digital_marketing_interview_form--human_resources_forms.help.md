# E Commerce Digital Marketing Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a candidate applying for an e-commerce digital marketing role.

## How To Complete This Form
See above steps for completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`page_1`, `text`, required/optional): This page is for providing personal details about you.
* **Work Experience** (`page_2`, `text`, required/optional): Share your relevant work experience, highlighting your achievements and skills.
* **Qualifications** (`page_3`, `text`, required/optional): Share any relevant certifications, courses, or training programs you have completed.
* **References** (`page_4`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about your professional references.
* **Upload CV/Resume** (`page_5`, `text`, required/optional): Please upload an updated version of your CV or resume.
* **Testimonial** (`page_6`, `text`, required/optional): Share a brief testimonial about your previous work or experience.
* **Email** (`page_7`, `email`, required/optional): Provide your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`page_8`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your work phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`page_9`, `date`, required/optional): Share your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`page_10`, `time`, required/optional): Share your time of birth.
* **E-Commerce Knowledge** (`page_11`, `text`, required/optional): Share your experience and knowledge in e-commerce.
* **Social Media** (`page_12`, `select_one`, required/true): Select all relevant social media channels you are active on.
* **E-Commerce Channels** (`page_13`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Select all e-commerce channels you use.
* **Product Categories** (`page_14`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Select product categories you have experience with.
* **Job Expectations** (`page_15`, `text`, required/optional): Share your expectations and goals for this position.
* **Why do you love E-commerce?** (`page_16`, `text`, required/optional): Share a brief description of your passion for e-commerce.
* **Why do you want to work with us?** (`page_17`, `text`, required/optional): Share why you are interested in working with our company.
* **What is your current salary expectation?** (`page_18`, `number`, required/optional): Share your current salary expectation.
* **How much do you charge per hour?** (`page_19`, `number`, required/optional): Share your current hourly rate.
* **Do you have any other job offers?** (`page_20`, `select_one`, required/true): Select if you have any other job offers.
* **What makes you unique?** (`page_21`, `text`, required/optional): Share a brief description of what makes you stand out.
* **How do you like to stay up-to-date with e-commerce trends?** (`page_22`, `text`, required/optional): Share how you stay informed about e-commerce trends.
* **Upload a picture of yourself** (`page_23`, `text`, required/optional): Upload a high-quality picture of yourself.
* **Upload a video file** (`page_24`, `text`, required/optional): Upload a high-quality video file.
* **Upload any other file** (`page_25`, `text`, required/optional): Upload any other high-quality file.
