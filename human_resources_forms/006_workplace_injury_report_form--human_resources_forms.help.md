# workplace_injury_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report workplace injuries and their details. It's essential to fill in all required fields carefully and accurately to ensure the incident is properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (*) on the form. These fields are essential for a complete and accurate reporting of the incident.
2.  Fill in the date and time of the incident, as well as its location.
3.  Briefly describe what happened, including any injuries sustained and their nature.
4.  Identify the type and severity of the injuries.
5.  If there were any witnesses to the incident, provide their names and contact information.
6.  Record any statements or comments made by the witnesses.
7.  Select the type of injuries sustained, ensuring all relevant options are chosen.
8.  Briefly describe the injuries, ensuring accuracy and clarity.
9.  Explain the cause of the incident, if known.
10. Note any preventative actions taken in response to the incident.
11.  Identify the person who reported the incident and their contact information.
12.  Mention the supervisor's name and contact information.
13.  Provide the employee's name and contact information, if applicable.
14.  Record the manager's name and contact information.
15.  Specify the department affected by the incident.
16.  Identify the person who reported the incident, including their name and contact information.
17.  Add any additional information about the incident.
18.  Attach any relevant files or documentation related to the incident.
19.  Enter any comments or remarks about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workplace Injury Form**: This is the main form that guides you through the reporting process.
*   **Incident Date**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Incident Location**: Specify the location where the incident happened.
*   **Incident Description**: Briefly describe what transpired.
*   **Injury Type**: Choose all relevant injury options.
*   **Injury Severity**: Select the severity of the injuries sustained.
*   **Witness Names**: If there were any witnesses, list their names.
*   **Witness Contact Information**: Enter their contact information.
*   **Witness Statement**: Record any statements made by the witnesses.
*   **Incident Injuries Sustained**: List the injuries sustained during the incident, choosing all applicable options.
*   **Incident Injuries Description**: Describe the injuries in detail, being as accurate and clear as possible.
*   **Incident Cause**: Explain the cause of the incident, if known.
*   **Preventative Actions Taken**: Note any actions taken to prevent similar incidents.
*   **Incident Reporter**: Identify the person who reported the incident.
*   **Incident Supervisor**: Mention the supervisor's name and contact information.
*   **Incident Employee**: Provide the employee's name and contact information, if applicable.
*   **Incident Manager**: Record the manager's name and contact information.
*   **Reported To**: Mention who the incident was reported to.
*   **Incident Department**: Identify the department affected by the incident.
*   **Reported By**: Identify the person who reported the incident.
*   **Incident Notes**: Add any additional comments or remarks about the incident.
*   **Additional Info**: Include any further details or information about the incident.
*   **Attachments**: Attach any relevant files or documentation related to the incident.
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments or remarks about the incident.
