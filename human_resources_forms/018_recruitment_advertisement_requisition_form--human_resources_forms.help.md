# Recruitment Advertisement Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for creating a job posting for a new or existing job opening. This form helps to ensure consistency and prevent duplication of job postings across different job boards and platforms.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all required fields (indicated by *) and any optional fields that you need to provide specific information about the job opening.
* Make sure to select the correct employment type (full-time, part-time, contractors, or consultants) and work location (select all relevant locations where the job can be performed).
* For the job category, select all relevant categories that the job pertains to.
* Enter the contact email and contact phone number of the person or department responsible for inquiries about the job opening.
* If applicable, enter the job start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title of the job opening.
* **Job description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the job duties and responsibilities.
* **Employment type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of employment (full-time, part-time, contractors, or consultants) that this job opening is for.
* **Work location** (`work_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all locations where the job can be performed.
* **Job category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all categories that the job pertains to.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the person or department responsible for inquiries.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the person or department responsible for inquiries.
* **Job start date** (`job_start_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date when the job will start.
