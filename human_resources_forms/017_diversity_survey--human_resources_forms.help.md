# Diversity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This diversity survey aims to gather feedback on your experiences with diversity and inclusion in the workplace. Your responses will help us understand areas where we excel and areas where we can improve to create a more inclusive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select an answer for each question that best describes your experience or feelings.
2. If you don't see an option that matches your experience, choose "None of the above" or "Something else" and provide a brief explanation.
3. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
4. Take a few minutes to complete the survey, and feel free to provide any additional comments or suggestions in the final section.
