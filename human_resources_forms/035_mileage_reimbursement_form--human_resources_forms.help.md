# Mileage Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage mileage reimbursement claims for company travels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the travel dates
2. Enter the start and end time of the travel
3. Enter the mileage amount
4. Enter the reimbursement amount
5. Provide a reason for the travel
6. Enter the business purpose of the travel
7. Add any expense details
8. Enter the submission details (submitted by, submitted date, updated by, updated date, created by, created date)
9. Choose the approval status and reimbursement status
10. Add any comments or notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Dates**: Enter the dates of the travel (e.g. 2022-01-01 to 2022-01-03).
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the travel (in 24-hour format, e.g. 08:00).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the travel (in 24-hour format, e.g. 17:00).
* **Mileage Amount**: Enter the mileage amount (e.g. 50).
* **Reimbursement Amount**: Enter the reimbursement amount (e.g. 100).
* **Reason for Travel**: Provide a brief explanation for the travel (e.g. Business meeting).
* **Business Purpose**: Enter the business purpose of the travel (e.g. Client meeting).
* **Expense Details**: Add any additional details about expenses incurred during the travel (e.g. Meals, accommodation).
* **Submitted By**: Enter your email address (e.g. [your-email@company.com]).
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted (automatically populated).
* **Updated By**: Enter the person who last updated the form (e.g. your-name@company.com).
* **Updated Date**: Enter the date the form was last updated (automatically populated).
* **Created By**: Enter the person who created the form (e.g. another-email@company.com).
* **Created Date**: Enter the date the form was created (automatically populated).
* **Assigned To**: Check if the form is assigned to someone (Yes/No).
* **Assigned By**: Check if someone is assigned to work on the form (Yes/No).
* **Form Comments**: Add any comments or notes for the form.
* **Submission ID**: Check if the submission is approved (Yes/No).
* **Approval Status**: Choose the approval status (Active/Inactive).
* **Reimbursement Status**: Choose the reimbursement status (Active/Inactive).
* **Form Status**: Choose the form status (Approved/Rejected).
* **Form Notes**: Add any notes or comments for the form.
