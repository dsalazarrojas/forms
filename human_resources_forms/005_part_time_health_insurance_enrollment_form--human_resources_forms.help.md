# Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from part-time employees for health insurance enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
* Provide personal details about yourself as requested in the "Employee Information" page.
* If you have dependents, please fill out the "Dependent Information" page with their details.
* Check the "Benefits Enrollment" page to select your insurance benefits options.
* Fill out your "Contact Information" page with your address and other contact details.
* Sign the "Agreement" page to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.
* Date and time stamp the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`Employee Information`, text, required): Please provide your full name, job title, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Dependent Information** (`Dependent Information`, text, required): If you have dependents, please list their names, ages, and relationships to you.
* **Benefits Enrollment** (`Benefits Enrollment`, text, required): Select your preferred health insurance benefits options, such as medical, dental, or vision coverage.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Please fill out your address, phone number, and email address.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required): Sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, text, required): Read and sign the agreement to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.
* **I agree to the above agreement.** (`Agreement Text`, note, required): Please read and acknowledge that you have read and understood the agreement.
* **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`, date, required): Date and timestamp the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed** (`Time Signed`, time, required): Date and timestamp the time you signed the form.
