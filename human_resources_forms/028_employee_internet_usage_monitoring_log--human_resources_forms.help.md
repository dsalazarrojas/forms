# Employee Internet Usage Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Internet Usage Monitoring Log form is used to track and monitor employee internet usage on company time. It is a mandatory form that should be completed daily for each employee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have access to the form and the necessary information for the employee whose internet usage you are logging.
2. Fill out the form on the exact date and time of the employee's internet usage.
3. Enter the employee's name, job title, and other relevant details.
4. Enter the start and end time of the internet usage.
5. Enter the website(s) visited, including the reason for the visit.
6. Record the duration of the internet usage in minutes.
7. Provide any additional notes about the internet usage.
8. Click the "submit" button to save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee whose internet usage you are logging.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the employee, if applicable.
* **Internet Usage Date** (`internet_usage_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the employee's internet usage.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the employee's internet usage.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the employee's internet usage.
* **Website Visited** (`website_visited`, text, optional): Enter the website(s) visited by the employee during the internet usage.
* **Reason Visited** (`reason_visited`, text, optional): Enter the reason for visiting the website(s) visited.
* **Duration (minutes)** (`duration_minutes`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the internet usage in minutes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the internet usage.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
