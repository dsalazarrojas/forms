# Feedback On Transition Process - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Feedback On Transition Process" form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experiences with the transition process within the organization. This helps us to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to ensure a smoother transition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the workplace or educational setting you were in before the transition.
2. Select all the types of transitions you went through during this process.
3. Provide your feedback on your experience.
4. Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).
5. Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted via phone).
6. Add any additional comments you may have about your experience.
7. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace or Educational Setting** (`workplace_educational_setting`, select_one, required):
  This field asks you to specify where you were before the transition. Choose the option that best describes your previous workplace or educational setting.
* **Type of Transition** (`transition_type`, select_multiple, required):
  This field asks you to select all the types of transitions you went through during this process. Choose all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
  This field is where you can provide detailed feedback about your experience with the transition process. Be as specific as possible to help us understand what we can improve.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
  If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive additional information, enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional):
  If you want to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Comments** (`comment`, note, optional):
  This field is where you can add any additional comments or details about your experience with the transition process.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional):
  Click this button to send your feedback to us.
