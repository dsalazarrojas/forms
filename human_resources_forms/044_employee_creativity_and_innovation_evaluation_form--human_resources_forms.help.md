# Employee Creativity And Innovation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an employee's creativity and innovation abilities, which are essential components of their job performance. Your evaluation will help your supervisor or manager understand your creative potential and identify areas for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the required information and data needed for the evaluation.
2.  Review the form and understand the purpose and context of each question.
3.  Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
4.  Submit your completed form to your supervisor or manager for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee Creativity** (`employee_creativity`, number, optional)
*   A numeric indicator of your creative potential, with higher numbers representing more creative employees.

### 2. **Innovation Contributions** (`innovation_contributions`, text, optional)
*   Describe any innovations or ideas you've contributed to the company, such as new products, processes, or services.

### 3. **Creativity Skills** (`creativity_skills`, select_one, optional)
*   Choose one of the following options that best describe your creative skills:
    *   Innovation
    *   Creativity
    *   Collaboration

### 4. **Innovative Contributions** (`innovative_contributions`, select_multiple, optional)
*   Select all the options that apply to your innovative contributions:
    *   Innovation
    *   Creativity
    *   Teamwork

### 5. **Innovation Capacity** (`innovation_capacity`, select_one, optional)
*   Choose one of the following options to indicate your innovation capacity:
    *   High
    *   Medium
    *   Low

### 6. **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, optional)
*   Select the scale that best describes your evaluation:
    *   Scale 1
    *   Scale 2
    *   Scale 3

### 7. **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional)
*   Enter the date of your evaluation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 8. **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional)
*   Enter the time of your evaluation in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### 9. **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_multiple, optional)
*   Select all the options that apply to your evaluation frequency:
    *   High
    *   Medium
    *   Low

Note to users: If you're unsure about any of the fields or the form's purpose, please reach out to your supervisor or manager for clarification and guidance. They'll be happy to assist you in completing the form correctly.
