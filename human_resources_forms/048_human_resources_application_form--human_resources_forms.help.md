# Human Resources Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Resources Application Form is a tool to collect information from applicants to help you evaluate and consider their application for available positions. The form gathers general information about the applicant, including their personal details, employment history, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name and Last Name**: Enter your full first and last names as they appear on your identification documents. This will help us verify your identity.
2. **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly check. This will be our primary contact method for communication.
3. **Job Title and Job Type**: Select the job title and type that match your current or desired role. This will help us understand your career goals.
4. **Job Description**: Briefly describe your job experience and relevant skills. This will help us evaluate your qualifications.
5. **Work Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your current or desired field. This will help us understand your level of expertise.
6. **Education**: Select all relevant educational levels you have achieved. This will help us assess your academic background.
7. **Skills**: List any relevant skills you possess that are not listed in the other fields. This will help us understand your strengths.
8. **Certifications**: If you have any relevant certifications, list them here.
9. **Work Status**: Indicate your current employment status. This will help us understand your availability.
10. **Job Start and End Dates**: If applicable, enter your job start and end dates. This will help us understand your employment history.
11. **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number where we can reach you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the job type that matches your current or desired role (Full-time, Part-time, Temporary).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your job experience and relevant skills.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in your current or desired field.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant educational levels you have achieved (High School, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Ph.D., Other).
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List any relevant skills you possess that are not listed in the other fields.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): If you have any relevant certifications, list them here.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate your current employment status (Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Other).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter your job start date.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter your job end date.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number where we can reach you.
