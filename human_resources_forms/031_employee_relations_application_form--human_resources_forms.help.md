# employee-relations-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee-relations-application-form is used to capture information from employees regarding their employee relations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your concern or request.
2. Provide a brief description of your issue in the "Grievance" field.
3. Choose the relevant category for your issue in the "Category" field.
4. Select the type of request or grievance in the "Request Type" field.
5. Enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
6. Provide the relevant department and manager's name in the respective fields.
7. If applicable, provide the location where the issue occurred.
8. Choose the date and time of the incident.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or manager.
- Ensure that you are submitting this form for a genuine reason.
