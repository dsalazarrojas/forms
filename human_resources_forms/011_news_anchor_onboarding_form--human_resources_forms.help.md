# News Anchor Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and professional information for new employees joining our team, ensuring a smooth onboarding process and efficient setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the first section.
2. Provide your professional contact information and relationship status.
3. Attach any relevant documents (resume, contract, etc.).
4. Add any additional information or context that will help us understand your role and expectations.
5. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, `required`): Enter your professional email address.
* **Name** (`4`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name you wish to be known by in the newsroom.
* **Relationship** (`5`, `text`, `required`): Describe your relationship with the newsroom (e.g., anchor, reporter, producer).
* **Phone** (`6`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Resume** (`7`, `text`, `optional`): Attach your resume or a brief professional summary.
* **Contract** (`8`, `text`, `optional`): Attach your employment contract or agreement.
* **Other Document** (`9`, `text`, `optional`): Attach any other relevant documents (e.g., ID, diplomas).
* **First Name** (`10`, `text`, `required`): This field is a duplicate of field 1, please enter your first name again.
* **Last Name** (`11`, `text`, `required`): This field is a duplicate of field 2, please enter your last name again.
* **Additional Info** (`12`, `text`, `optional`): Share any additional information or context that will help us understand your role and expectations.
* **Newsroom Info** (`13`, `text`, `optional`): Share information about your newsroom responsibilities and expectations.
* **Manager Info** (`14`, `text`, `optional`): Provide information about your direct manager or supervisor.
* **First Name** (`15`, `text`, `required`): This field is another duplicate of field 1, please enter your first name one last time.
* **Last Name** (`16`, `text`, `required`): This field is another duplicate of field 2, please enter your last name one last time.
