# Résumé 'Tune Up' Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Résumé 'Tune Up' Questionnaire" form is designed to gather relevant information about an employee's work profile. By filling out this form, you will be able to update or maintain your employee records accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Job Title (id: 1)
   - Type in your current job title in the provided text field.

2. Enter your Job Description (id: 2)
   - Type in a brief description of your job, in the provided text field.

3. Enter your Company Name (id: 3)
   - Type in your current company name in the provided text field.

4. Select your Job Type (id: 4)
   - Choose your job type from the provided options: Job Title, Job Title 2, Job Title 3, Job Title 4, Job Title 5.

5. Select your Education Level (id: 5)
   - Choose your education level from the provided options: High School, Bachelor, Associate, Master, Doctorate.

6. Enter your Work Experience (id: 6)
   - Type in the number of years you have worked in your current position.

7. Select your Job Status (id: 7)
   - Choose your job status from the provided options: Active, Inactive, Pending.

8. Enter your Years of Experience (id: 8)
   - Type in the total number of years you have worked.

9. Select your Expected Employment Type (id: 9)
   - Choose your expected employment type from the provided options: Full Time, Part-Time, Freelance.

10. Enter your Contact Email (id: 10)
    - Type in your current contact email in the provided email field.

11. Enter your Contact Phone (id: 11)
    - Type in your current contact phone number in the provided text field.

12. Enter your Contact Address (id: 12)
    - Type in your current contact address in the provided text field.

13. Select your Work Hours (id: 13)
    - Choose your work hours from the time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (id: 1, text): Enter your current job title in this field.
* **Job Description** (id: 2, text): Type in a brief description of your job.
* **Company Name** (id: 3, text): Type in your current company name.
* **Job Type** (id: 4, select_one): Choose your job type from the options provided.
* **Education Level** (id: 5, select_multiple): Select all the education levels that apply to you.
* **Work Experience** (id: 6, number): Type in the number of years you have worked.
* **Job Status** (id: 7, select_one): Choose your job status from the options provided.
* **Years of Experience** (id: 8, number): Type in the total number of years you have worked.
* **Expected Employment Type** (id: 9, select_multiple): Choose the expected employment type that applies to you.
* **Contact Email** (id: 10, email): Type in your current contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 11, text): Type in your current contact phone number.
* **Contact Address** (id: 12, text): Type in your current contact address.
* **Work Hours** (id: 13, time): Choose your work hours from the time field.
