# professional_development_specialist_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about new hires, including their training needs, job title, manager's contact information, and other relevant details. This form is a vital tool for the onboarding process, ensuring that new hires receive the necessary training and support to excel in their roles.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your new hire information, including your job title, manager's contact information, and any specific training needs you may have.
2. Answer the training needs question to inform your manager about the type of training you require.
3. Select your training status (Completed or Not Completed) to help track your training progress.
4. Enter your email address so your manager can contact you.
5. Enter your phone number to enable easy communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Info** (new_hire_info, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your basic new hire information.
* **Training Needs** (training_needs, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the training needs that apply to you, such as "Training for new hire" or "Ongoing training for new hire".
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required: false): Enter your job title as it appears on your business card or email signature.
* **Manager Info** (manager_info, text, required: false): Provide your manager's name and title (e.g., John Doe - Team Lead) or their email address.
* **Training Status** (training_status, select_one, required: false): Select "Completed" if you have finished all required training, "Not Completed" if you have not started or finished training, or "Unknown" if you are unsure.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address so your manager can contact you about training and other important matters.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to enable easy communication between you and your manager.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all the necessary information accurately and completely to facilitate your onboarding process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or HR representative for assistance.
