# Salary Slip Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salary Slip Overview Form is used to collect and display essential information about an employee's salary, including their name, job title, and salary amount. This form helps to streamline the process of managing employee compensation data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's ID in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select the employee's job title from the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the employee's salary amount in the "Salary" field.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the salary period in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the salary in the "Note" field.
7. Select the finance team involved in managing the employee's salary from the "Finance Team" field.
8. Select any HR team members involved in managing the employee's salary from the "HR Team" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their personnel record.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the employee's unique ID number as assigned by HR or payroll systems.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Select the employee's job title or role within the organization.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter the employee's monthly salary amount.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee's salary period began.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee's salary period ended.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the salary.
* **Finance Team** (`finance_team`, select_one, optional): Select the finance team responsible for managing the employee's salary. Options include:
	+ Accounting
	+ Finance
	+ HR
* **HR Team** (`hr_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select any HR team members involved in managing the employee's salary. Options include:
	+ HR Manager
	+ HR Assistant
