# Resource Management Training Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resource Management Training Acknowledgement form is used to record attendance, topics covered, and feedback from both trainers and participants in a resource management training session.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select your attendance in the "Attendance" field. If you attended the training session, choose "Yes". Otherwise, choose "No".
2.  Select the relevant topics from the "Topics" field. This field is optional, so feel free to choose all the topics that apply.
3.  Sign above the "Trainers Signature" field with your name as the trainer to acknowledge that you have delivered the training.
4.  Sign above the "Participants Signature" field with your name as the participant to acknowledge your attendance.
5.  In the "Trainers Feedback" field, enter your feedback or suggestions for the training session.
6.  In the "Participants Feedback" field, enter your feedback or suggestions for the training session.
7.  Select the date of the training session in the "Training Session Date" field.
8.  Select the end date of the training session in the "End Date" field.
9.  Enter the start time of the training session in the "Training Start Time" field.
10.  Enter the end time of the training session in the "End Time" field.
11.  Enter the duration of the training session in the "Training Duration" field.
12.  Select the attendance of all participants in the "Attendance" field. If all participants attended, choose "Yes". Otherwise, choose "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendance** (`resource_management_training_acknowledgement`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your attendance in this field. If you attended the training session, choose "Yes".
*   **Topics** (`training_topics`, select_one, required: false): Select the topics that were covered in the training session. This field is optional.
*   **Trainers Signature** (`trainer_signature`, text, required: false): Sign above this field with your name as the trainer to acknowledge that you have delivered the training.
*   **Participants Signature** (`attendee_signature`, text, required: false): Sign above this field with your name as the participant to acknowledge your attendance.
*   **Trainers Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Enter your feedback or suggestions for the training session.
*   **Participants Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Enter your feedback or suggestions for the training session.
*   **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the training session.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Select the end date of the training session.
*   **Training Start Time** (`training_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the training session.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the training session.
*   **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the training session.
*   **Attendance** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the attendance of all participants. If all participants attended, choose "Yes".

## Tips

*   Make sure to sign both the "Trainers Signature" and "Participants Signature" fields with your name.
*   Enter your feedback or suggestions in the "Trainers Feedback" and "Participants Feedback" fields.
*   Ensure that you have completed all the required fields before saving your response.
