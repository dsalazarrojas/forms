# Shift Realignment Time Off Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a realignment of your shifts. Please submit this form to request a change in your scheduled shift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") completely.
2. Select "Yes" to submit your request.
3. Optionally, provide any additional comments for your manager's review.
4. If your manager reviews and approves your request, they may leave comments for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time Off Request** (`time_off_request`, text, required):
	+ Enter your request for shift realignment, including details of which shifts you would like to realign.
* **Select One Time Off Type** (`select_one_time_off_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the reason for your request from the available options.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date you would like your shift realignment to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date you would like your shift realignment to end.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required):
	+ Enter the reason for your shift realignment request.
* **Manager Review** (`manager_review`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose how you would like your manager to review your request.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select "Yes" to submit your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments for your manager's review.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional):
	+ If your manager reviews and approves your request, they may leave comments here.
