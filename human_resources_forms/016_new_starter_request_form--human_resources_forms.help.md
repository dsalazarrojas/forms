# New Stater Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Stater Request Form is used to submit a request for new stater status to start a new role in the company. This form requires information about the employee's new company name, job title, manager's name, department, email, phone number, start date, and any notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your new company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter your new job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
4. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, following the format `example@example.com`.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, including the country code if necessary.
7. Enter the date you are expected to start in the "Date of Starting" field.
8. Enter the date you are expected to start in the "Date of Starting" field (this field has the same name as the previous one but has a different purpose).
9. Leave the "Assigned Tool" field blank if you are not assigned a tool, or enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
10. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (1)**: Enter your new company name.
* **Job Title (2)**: Enter your new job title.
* **Manager Name (3)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Department (4)**: Enter your department.
* **Email (5)**: Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number, including the country code if necessary.
* **Date of Starting (7)**: Enter the date you are expected to start.
* **Date of Starting (8)**: Enter the date you are expected to start (this field is identical to field 7 but has a different purpose).
* **Assigned Tool (9)**: Leave blank if not assigned a tool, or enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Note (10)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your new role. 

## Tips
- Double-check the fields for any errors before submitting.
- Use a valid email address and phone number formats.
- Ensure the start date and date of starting fields are correctly formatted dates.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your manager or HR representative.
