# Medical Leave Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Medical Leave Extension Form is used to request an extension of an already approved medical leave. It is essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form to understand the required fields and their purpose.
2.  Enter your employee ID or name in the **Employee Name** field for identification purposes.
3.  Choose the start and end dates of your proposed extended leave period using the **Leave Start Date** and **Leave End Date** fields.
4.  Attach a **Medical Certificate** from a licensed medical professional, which confirms your medical condition and reason for the leave extension.
5.  Enter the date you plan to **Return To Work** after your leave extension.
6.  If required, enter the **Manager Name** and **Manager Email** of the person in charge of your leave approval process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name**: Enter your employee ID or name for identification purposes.
*   **Leave Start Date**: Choose the date you want your leave to start.
*   **Leave End Date**: Choose the date you want your leave to end. This date should be the same as or later than the previously approved end date.
*   **Medical Certificate**: Attach a valid **Medical Certificate** from a licensed medical professional. This is a mandatory requirement for a medical leave extension.
*   **Return To Work Date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave extension.
*   **Manager Name**: If required, enter the name of your immediate manager or supervisor who needs to sign off on this leave extension.
*   **Manager Email**: If required, enter the email address of your manager or supervisor for notification purposes.
