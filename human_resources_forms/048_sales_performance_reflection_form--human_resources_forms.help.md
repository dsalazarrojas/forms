# Sales Performance Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your sales performance and strategies during the current period. This will help our management team to understand your strengths and weaknesses and make informed decisions to improve our sales processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading the introduction to understand the context and expectations for the form.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing detailed explanations for each field.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. Once you have completed the form, review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `intro`, `Introduction`, note, not required): A brief description of the purpose and expectations for the form.
* **Reflect on your sales activities this period** (`2`, `reflection_activities`, `text`, not required): Share your thoughts on the sales activities you have performed during this period. This could include successes, challenges, and areas for improvement.
* **What were the sales numbers for this period?** (`3`, `sales_num`, `number`, required): Provide the sales numbers for the current period.
* **Sales Num2** (`4`, `sales_num2`, `number`, not required): This field is not clearly labeled, but it is expected to be another sales-related number. Please provide any relevant information.
* **What is your current sales strategy?** (`5`, `sales_strategy`, `text`, not required): Describe your current sales strategy, including any goals, targets, or approaches.
* **Sales Strategy2** (`6`, `sales_strategy2`, `text`, not required): Similar to the previous field, this field is not clearly labeled. Please provide any additional insights on your sales strategy.
* **How do you manage your sales team?** (`7`, `sales_team_management`, `text`, not required): Share your approach to managing your sales team, including any best practices or challenges.
* **Do you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement?** (`8`, `feedback_and_suggestions`, `text`, not required): Provide any comments or ideas for improving sales performance or processes.
* **Are you a sales manager?** (`9`, `sales_manager_role`, `select_one`, not required): Answer with "Yes" or "No".
* **What are your responsibilities as a sales manager?** (`10`, `sales_manager_responsibilities`, `text`, not required): Describe your responsibilities as a sales manager, including any specific tasks or expectations.
* **Do you have any feedback for your sales manager?** (`11`, `sales_manager_feedback`, `text`, not required): Share any comments or suggestions for your sales manager.
* **How would you rate your sales performance this period?** (`12`, `sales_performance`, `number`, not required): Rate your sales performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback And Suggestions2** (`13`, `feedback_and_suggestions2`, `text`, not required): This field is not clearly labeled, but it is similar to the previous "Do you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement?" field. Please provide any additional comments or ideas.
* **Sales Manager Feedback2** (`14`, `sales_manager_feedback2`, `text`, not required): Similar to the previous field, this field is not clearly labeled. Please provide any feedback for your sales manager.
* **Sales Manager Responsibilities2** (`15`, `sales_manager_responsibilities2`, `text`, not required): Describe your responsibilities as a sales manager.
* **Sales Manager Role2** (`16`, `sales_manager_role2`, `select_one`, not required): Answer with "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving your sales performance?** (`17`, `sales_performance_improvement`, `text`, not required): Provide any ideas for improving sales performance.
* **Sales Manager Responsibilities3** (`18`, `sales_manager_responsibilities3`, `text`, not required): Describe your responsibilities as a sales manager.
* **Would you like to receive a performance review?** (`19`, `sales_performance_review`, `select_one`, not required): Answer with "Yes" or "No".
