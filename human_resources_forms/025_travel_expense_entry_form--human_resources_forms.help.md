# travel_expense_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_expense_entry_form is designed for employees to accurately report their business-related travel expenses, ensuring accurate tracking and potential reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing any **additional travel remarks or comments** on the **page_1**. Although this field is not required, it's a good opportunity to include any extra information you feel is necessary.
2. Move to **travel_details**, where you can enter a short description of your trip. Although this field is optional, it's another chance to offer additional context about your travel.
3. Select your **travel_dates** by choosing a date for your trip's start date and end date.
4. Indicate the **travel_destination**, which is the primary location or destination of your journey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **page_1 (page_1)** (`text`, optional): This field is a space for any additional travel-related comments or remarks you might have. You can enter anything you feel is relevant for us to know about your trip.
- **travel_details (travel_details)** (`text`, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of your travel. It's an optional but useful field for further contextualizing your expenses.
- **travel_dates (travel_dates)** (`date`, required): This is where you'll enter the start and end dates of your travel. Please ensure you input the correct dates for accurate tracking.
- **travel_destination (travel_destination)** (`text`, required): Here, you'll indicate the main destination or location of your trip. This is a required field, so please fill it out accurately.

## Tips
Remember, the more information you provide in these fields, the easier it is for us to process and reimburse your expenses accurately. If you have any trouble, feel free to reach out to us for support.
