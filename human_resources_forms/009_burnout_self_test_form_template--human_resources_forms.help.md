# Burnout Self Test Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your risk of burnout, a state of emotional, mental, and physical exhaustion caused by prolonged stress and overwork. It is a self-assessment tool that will guide you through a series of questions to help you identify your level of burnout.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select the options that best match your experiences and feelings. The form is divided into sections, and each section is designed to help you understand different aspects of burnout. You can complete the form at your own pace, and it should take approximately 10-15 minutes to finish.

## Tips

* Please be honest and answer based on your current experiences and feelings.
* This form is for your personal assessment only, and your responses will not affect your work performance or any other consequences.
* You can complete the form at your own pace, and it should take approximately 10-15 minutes to finish.
* If you feel overwhelmed or need help, please seek support from your colleagues, supervisor, or HR representative.
