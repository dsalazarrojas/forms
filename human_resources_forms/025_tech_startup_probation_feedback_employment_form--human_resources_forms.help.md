# tech_startup_probation_feedback_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information on the probationary employment period for employees in a tech startup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are authorized to access and use this form.
2. Select the date when the probationary period started for the employee.
3. Enter the employee's name.
4. Enter the manager’s name.
5. Provide the job title of the employee.
6. Add any performance feedback for the employee.
7. Specify the date when the probation period ended.
8. Summarize the evaluation outcomes.
9. Add any recommendations for the employee.
10. Enter your comments as the manager.
11. Enter any comments from the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Probation Period Start Date** (`probation_period_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the employee's probation period started.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the manager.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Performance Feedback** (`performance_feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback on the employee's performance during the probation period.
* **Probation Period End Date** (`probation_period_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the probation period ended.
* **Evaluation Outcomes** (`evaluation_outcomes`, note, required/optional): Describe the outcomes of the evaluation.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required/optional): Make any recommendations for the employee.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter your comments as the manager.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the employee.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- Use clear and concise language when providing feedback and comments.
- If a field is not relevant, you can leave it blank.
