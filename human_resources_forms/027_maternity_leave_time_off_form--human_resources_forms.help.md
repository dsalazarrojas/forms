# Maternity Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maternity Leave Time Off Form is a request to take time off for maternity leave. It is used by employees to notify their manager and HR of their upcoming leave dates and related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields carefully.
2. Ensure you have read and understood the company's maternity leave policy.
3. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
4. Avoid missing any sections, as this may cause delays in processing your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee (1)**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Supervisor (2)**: Enter the ID number of your supervisor.
* **Manager (3)**: Enter the ID number of your manager.
* **Start Date (4)**: Enter the date you plan to start your maternity leave. Use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **End Date (5)**: Enter the date you plan to return to work after your maternity leave. Use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Pay Period (6)**: Enter the pay period for which you are requesting leave.
* **Reason (7)**: Enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting maternity leave. This will help the manager understand the circumstances.
* **Approval Status (8)**: Select the status of your request (Waiting on Manager, Approved, or Declined).
* **Notes (9)**: Add any additional comments or details to support your request.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough in your answers.
- Avoid missing any required fields.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact HR or your manager for clarification.
