# Employee Retirement Plan Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees about the company's retirement plan. It aims to collect their opinions and suggestions on the plan's effectiveness, and to understand their thoughts on how it can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's name as it is recorded in our records.
2. Provide a brief description of the employee's current retirement plan.
3. Share your thoughts and opinions on the retirement plan's effectiveness.
4. If applicable, enter the date of your retirement.
5. Review and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name**: Enter your name as it is recorded in our records.
* **retirement_plan**: Describe your current retirement plan in a few words.
* **feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions on the retirement plan's effectiveness. Be as detailed as you'd like.
* **date_of_retirement**: If you have already retired, enter the date you retired. If not yet retired, leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Be honest and open with your feedback.
* Provide specific examples if possible.
* If you have any suggestions for improving the retirement plan, feel free to share them.
* Ensure that you answer all the fields that are marked as required (if any).
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
