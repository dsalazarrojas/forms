# Employee Equipment Issue Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about equipment issues reported by employees. The purpose of this form is to ensure that facilities or maintenance teams receive accurate and timely information to address equipment-related concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date the issue occurred in the **Issue Date** field.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue in the **Issue Description** field.
3. Enter the name of the equipment affected by the issue in the **Equipment Name** field.
4. Enter your name in the **Employee Name** field.
5. If applicable, select the department where the issue occurred from the **Department** field.
6. If applicable, select the type of issue from the **Issue Type** field.
7. If you have an image that can help resolve the issue, you can upload it in the **Issue Image** field.
8. Add any additional comments about the issue in the **Issue Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issue Date**: Enter the date the issue occurred. This will help the facilities or maintenance team track the timeline of the issue.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a brief description of the issue, including any symptoms or problems you experienced. This will help the facilities or maintenance team understand the nature of the issue.
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the equipment affected by the issue. This will help the facilities or maintenance team identify the specific equipment that needs attention.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name and ID number (if applicable) to ensure that your issue is attributed to you.
* **Department**: If you are unsure of the department where the issue occurred, you can select "Unknown" or "Not sure." However, if you know the department, please select it from the list provided.
* **Issue Type**: Select the type of issue that occurred, such as "Broken," "Faulty," or "Missing."
* **Issue Image**: If you have an image that can help resolve the issue, please upload it here. This can include photos or screenshots that illustrate the problem.
* **Issue Comment**: Add any additional comments or information about the issue that may help the facilities or maintenance team resolve it.
