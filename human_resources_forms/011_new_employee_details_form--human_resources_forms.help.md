# New Employee Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Employee Details Form is a simple form designed to collect essential information about new employees. This form helps the HR department to keep track of employee details and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the employee's details in the relevant fields.
* Select the job title and job type that best describe your role in the company.
* Choose the department where you will be working.
* Indicate your job status by selecting all applicable options.
* If you are working under a manager or team, provide their name (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details (employee_details)**: This is the main section where you can input your employee details, such as your name and identification number.
* **Job Title (job_title)**: Select the job title that accurately represents your role in the company. This will help the HR department to understand your responsibilities and duties.
* **Hire Date (hire_date)**: This is the date when you were hired by the company. Please input your start date in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/2024).
* **Job Type (job_type)**: Choose the type of job that best fits your role, such as Admin, Manager, Team Member, or Temporary.
* **Job Status (job_status)**: Select all applicable statuses that describe your current work situation. This can include Active, Inactive, Pending, or On Leave.
* **Department (department)**: This is the department where you will be working. If you are not sure, you can check with your HR representative.
* **Manager's Name (manager_name)**: If you have a direct manager, please provide their name. This is not a required field if you are a manager or team member.
* **Reporting Manager's Name (reporting_manager)**: If you work under a team or group, please provide the name of your reporting manager. This is not a required field.
