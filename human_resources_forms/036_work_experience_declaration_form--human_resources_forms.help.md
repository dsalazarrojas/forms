## Tips

* **Job Title (`job_title`)**: Enter the exact job title you held while working at that company.
* **Company (`company`)**: Type the name of the company where you worked.
* **Start Date (`start_date`)**: Select the date you started working at that company.
* **End Date (`end_date`)**: If you are no longer working at that company, select the date you stopped working.
* **Job Description (`job_description`)**: Briefly describe your job responsibilities and duties.

Note: The fields are not explicitly labeled as "required" or "optional" but based on the YAML schema, it seems that all fields are optional.
