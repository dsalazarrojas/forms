# Employee Knowledge Sharing Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Knowledge Sharing Evaluation Form is used to assess an employee's knowledge sharing behavior in the company. This form helps to evaluate how well an employee has shared their knowledge, ideas, and expertise with their colleagues, and what can be improved to encourage more sharing. The form is used to provide feedback and guidance to employees on their knowledge sharing practices, helping them to become better contributors to the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the Evaluation Questions page. For each question, select the most accurate answer.
2. On the Rating Score page, select the score that best reflects your opinion on the employee's knowledge sharing behavior.
3. On the Additional Comments page, provide any additional feedback or comments about the employee's knowledge sharing practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation Questions**: This is the first page where you will be asked to evaluate the employee's knowledge sharing behavior. Select the option that best answers each question.
	+ Question 1: What specific knowledge or expertise has the employee shared with their colleagues?
	+ Question 2: Has the employee actively contributed to team projects or initiatives?
	+ Question 3: Has the employee provided guidance or mentorship to less experienced colleagues?
* **Rating Score**: On this page, rate the employee's knowledge sharing behavior on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments**: This is the final page where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the employee's knowledge sharing practices. This is an opportunity to highlight strengths and areas for improvement.
