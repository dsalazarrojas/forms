# Paid Clock Time Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Paid Clock Time Form is a tool used to collect and manage employee timeclocked hours for a specific project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the project you are logging time for.
2. Enter the clock hours you worked for the project.
3. Enter the start and end times and dates for your clock in and clock out.
4. Add any notes about your timeclocked hours.
5. Select if the project is assigned to anyone.
6. Enter any comments about your timeclocked hours.
7. Enter your email and phone number.
8. Select your clock in and out status.
9. Select the type of clock (e.g., yes or no).
10. Enter the hours worked for the project.
11. Select the pay type (e.g., yes or no).
12. Enter the pay rate for the project.
13. Enter the pay rate currency.
14. Enter the pay rate currency symbol.
15. Select the clock rounding options (e.g., yes or no).
16. Select the clock rounding precision.
17. Select the clock rounding type.
18. Enter the break in and out times.
19. Enter the break duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clock Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you worked on the project.
* **Project**: Select the project you are logging time for.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your clock in for the project.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your clock out for the project.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you started clocking in for the project.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you ended clocking out for the project.
* **Notes**: Add any notes about your timeclocked hours.
* **Assigned To**: Select if the project is assigned to anyone.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments about your timeclocked hours.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Clock In**: Select if you are currently clocked in.
* **Clock Out**: Select if you are currently clocked out.
* **Clock Type**: Select the type of clock (e.g., yes or no).
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the total hours worked for the project.
* **Pay Type**: Select the pay type (e.g., yes or no).
* **Pay Rate**: Enter the pay rate for the project.
* **Pay Rate Currency**: Enter the pay rate currency.
* **Pay Rate Currency Symbol**: Enter the pay rate currency symbol.
* **Clock Rounding**: Select the clock rounding options (e.g., yes or no).
* **Clock Rounding Precision**: Select the clock rounding precision.
* **Clock Rounding Type**: Select the clock rounding type.
* **Break In**: Enter the start time of your break.
* **Break Out**: Enter the end time of your break.
* **Break Duration**: Enter the duration of your break.
