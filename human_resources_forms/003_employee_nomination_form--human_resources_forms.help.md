# employee_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee nomination form is used to gather information and reasons for nominating an employee for internal recognition or awards. This form helps the nomination committee understand the employee's contributions, skills, and qualities that make them deserving of recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
* Enter the employee's email address in the "Email" field.
* Add the employee's phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Select the department the employee belongs to from the "Department" dropdown list.
* Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
* Choose the reason for nomination in the "Reason for Nomination" field.
* Add any additional comments or notes about the employee's qualifications in the "Notes" field.
* Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department the employee belongs to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee was hired.
* **Reason for Nomination** (`reason_for_nomination`, text, optional): Choose a reason for nominating the employee (e.g., exceptional service, outstanding performance, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the employee's qualifications.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit the form.
