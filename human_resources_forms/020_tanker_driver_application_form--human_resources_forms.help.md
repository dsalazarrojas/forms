# Tanker Driver Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tanker drivers to submit their applications or updates. It helps us gather essential information about you, including your experience, contact details, and driving history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct tanker vehicle type from the options provided.
3. Ensure your contact details are valid and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tanker Driver Information** (`tanker_driver_info`, `text`, required): This field is for you to provide a brief description about yourself, including your experience and qualifications as a tanker driver.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Please provide your current contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): This field is for you to provide your current address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required): Describe your work experience as a tanker driver, including the types of vehicles you've driven and any notable achievements.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, `number`, required): Enter your valid driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of tanker vehicle you have experience with from the options provided.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you've worked as a tanker driver.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, `number`, required): Enter your current salary range.
* **Driver License Status** (`driver_license_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your driver's license status from the options provided.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, `select_multiple`, required): Check 'Yes' if you have a valid medical certificate; otherwise, select 'No'.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all required fields accurately and honestly.
* Double-check your contact information and driver's license status.
* Please provide up-to-date information about your medical certificate, if applicable.
