# Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Leave Application Form is a tool used to collect information from users regarding their leave requests. This form gathers details about the user, their contact information, leave specifics, and other relevant details. This form helps the administration to process leave applications efficiently and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by filling in your personal information in the **User Information** section.
2. Provide your contact details in the **Contact Information** section.
3. Specify your leave details in the **Leave Information** section.
4. Select the type of leave you are applying for in the **Type of Leave** section.
5. Enter the dates of your leave in the **Start Date** and **End Date** sections.
6. If necessary, enter your leave duration and reason for leave in the **Leave Duration** and **Reason for Leave** sections.
7. Enter your department and job title if applicable.
8. Provide your contact number and email address.
9. Enter your return date if you are planning to return to work during your leave.
10. Finally, provide any travel details if your leave involves travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your name and other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, including your contact number and email address.
* **Leave Information**: Provide details about your leave, including the dates and duration of your leave.
* **Type of Leave**: Select the type of leave you are applying for.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Leave Duration**: Enter the duration of your leave if necessary.
* **Reason for Leave**: Provide a brief explanation for your leave.
* **Department**: Enter your department if applicable.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title if applicable.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Travel Details**: Enter any travel details if your leave involves travel.
* **Status**: Select the status of your leave application.
* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person who approved your leave.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or notes about your leave application.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your leave application.
