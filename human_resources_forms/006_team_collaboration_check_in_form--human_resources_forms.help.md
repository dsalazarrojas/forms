# Team Collaboration Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Collaboration Check In Form is a tool used by team members to provide information about their project. This form helps team leads and managers understand project context and dynamics, facilitating better collaboration and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the project name and description in the respective fields.
3. Provide the team size and dynamics in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the project you are working on.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required/optional: false): Briefly describe the project in a few sentences.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the number of team members currently working on the project.
* **Team Dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, `note`, required/optional: false): Provide any relevant information about your team's dynamics and communication styles.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your team's status is correctly reflected.
* You can always refer back to this form if you need to update your project information.
