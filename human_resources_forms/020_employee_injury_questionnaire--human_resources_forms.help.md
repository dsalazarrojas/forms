# Employee Injury Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report injuries that occur to employees while on the job. This information will be used to help management track and analyze workplace injuries, identify potential hazards, and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information, making sure to enter accurate and complete details about the injury.
2. Please make sure to fill out the form for each injury that occurs.
3. If a field is not applicable or is not known, it's not necessary to enter anything.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who was injured.
* **Injury Date**: Enter the date the injury occurred.
* **Area of Work**: Describe the area where the injury took place.
* **Injury Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened and how the injury occurred.
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of the employer where the employee was working at the time of the injury.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the employee's supervisor at the time of the injury.
* **Witness Names**: List the names of any witnesses who saw the injury occur.
* **Other Witness Names**: List any other witness names that are not included in the previous field.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number, if applicable.
* **Supervisor ID**: Enter the supervisor's ID number, if applicable.
* **Employer ID**: Enter the employer's ID number, if applicable.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date the injury was reported.
* **Reported By**: Enter the name of the person or department who reported the injury.
