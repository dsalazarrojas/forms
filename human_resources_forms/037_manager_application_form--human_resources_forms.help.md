# Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for a manager position within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **Manager Name** field (page 1, field 1).
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field (page 1, field 2).
3. Describe your job experience in the **Job Experience** field (page 1, field 3).
4. List your relevant job qualifications in the **Job Qualifications** field (page 1, field 4).
5. Upload a copy of your resume in the **Resume Uploader** field (page 1, field 5).
6. Select your relevant skills from the **Skills** field (page 1, field 6). You can choose one or multiple skills from the list.
7. Choose your current job title from the **Job Title** field (page 1, field 7).
8. Select up to 3 references from the **References** field (page 1, field 8).
9. Add any additional comments in the **Comments** field (page 1, field 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Name** (Page 1, Field 1, `manager_name`): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your application.
* **Email** (Page 1, Field 2, `email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Job Experience** (Page 1, Field 3, `experience`): Describe your previous work experience in the field.
* **Job Qualifications** (Page 1, Field 4, `qualifications`): List any relevant certifications, training, or education that aligns with the manager position.
* **Resume Uploader** (Page 1, Field 5, `resume`): Attach a copy of your resume or CV.
* **Skills** (Page 1, Field 6, `skills`): Select one or multiple skills that you possess, such as Accounting, Data Analysis, Financial Planning, Marketing, Project Management, or Time Management.
* **Job Title** (Page 1, Field 7, `job_role`): Choose your current job title from the list.
* **References** (Page 1, Field 8, `manager_references`): Select up to 3 individuals who can vouch for your work experience and skills.
* **Comments** (Page 1, Field 9, `manager_comments`): Add any additional information you would like to provide about your application.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (none of the fields are marked as required, but ensure you complete all fields relevant to your application).
- Attach a clear and concise resume or CV.
- Use the skills list as a guide to select the relevant skills you possess. 
- Choose up to 3 references who can vouch for your work experience and skills.
