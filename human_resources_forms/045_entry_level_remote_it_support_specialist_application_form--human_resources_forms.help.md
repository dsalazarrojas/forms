# entry_level_remote_it_support_specialist_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who are applying for an entry-level remote IT support specialist position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Personal Details" page with your name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. If applicable, provide your job title, work experience, and education.
3. Choose your area of specialization by selecting one of the available options from the "Skills" dropdown menu.
4. Select your preferred job type (full-time, freelance, or intern) and job status (full-time or part-time).
5. Choose your preferred job shift (day or night) and job schedule (Monday to Friday or Saturday and Sunday).
6. Select your available hours of work (8am-16pm, 10am-18pm, or 12am-20pm).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Details
* **Personal Details** (`title`, `text`, required): This is the introduction page where you can provide your personal details such as name, email, phone number, and date of birth.

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide a phone number where you can be reached.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Resume
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required): Upload or paste a link to your resume or CV.

### Job Title (Optional)
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific job title, you can provide it here.

### Work Experience (Optional)
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your previous work experience.

### Education (Optional)
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, optional): List your educational background and relevant courses.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the available options:
	+ IT support specialist
	+ Help desk specialist
	+ Network administrator
	+ Cloud specialist
	+ Database administrator

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the available options:
	+ Full-time
	+ Freelance
	+ Intern

### Job Status
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the available options:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time

### Job Shift
* **Job Shift** (`job_shift`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the available options:
	+ Day
	+ Night

### Job Schedule
* **Job Schedule** (`job_schedule`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the available options:
	+ Monday to Friday
	+ Saturday and Sunday

### Available Hours
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the available options:
	+ 8am-16pm
	+ 10am-18pm
	+ 12am-20pm

Please ensure you fill out all required fields to complete your application.
