# Tech Team Lead Annual Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Team Lead Annual Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance of a team lead within the tech team, gathering feedback from peers and the manager to help identify areas of improvement and set goals for the upcoming year.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure that you are logged in with your valid credentials before accessing the form.
2.  The form is divided into sections, each focusing on different aspects of the team lead's performance.
3.  Please answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk \*.
4.  For fields that are not required, feel free to add any relevant information.
5.  Review and verify your responses before submitting the form.
6.  Once submitted, the form will be reviewed and feedback will be provided to help the team lead grow and improve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Lead Name (\***):** A field where you will enter your team lead's name.
*   **Manager Name (\***):** A field where you will enter your name as the manager of the team lead.
*   **Evaluation Time (Optional):** The time it took to complete the evaluation form. This field is optional and not required.
*   **Evaluation Date (\***):** The date of the evaluation.
*   **Feedback from Peers (\***):** Enter feedback from your peers, providing constructive comments on the team lead's performance.
*   **Feedback from Manager (\***):** Enter feedback from your manager, providing guidance on areas of improvement and strengths.
*   **Goals Met (\***):** Answer if the team lead met their goals for the past year. Choose either 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Areas of Improvement (Optional):** A field to enter areas where the team lead could improve.
*   **Suggestions (Optional):** A field where you can enter suggestions for the team lead's development.
*   **Evaluation Comments (\***):** A field where you can provide any final comments on the team lead's performance.
*   **Overall Rating (\***):** Rate the team lead's overall performance. Choose either 'Good' or 'Fair'.
*   **Evaluation Form Completion Time (Optional):** The time it took to complete the form. This field is optional.
*   **Review Cycle (Optional):** Enter any information regarding the review cycle.
*   **Next Steps (Optional):** A field where you can enter any next steps or actions planned for the team lead.
*   **Feedback on Next Steps (Optional):** Enter feedback on the next steps or actions planned for the team lead.
7.  Once you have completed the form, review and submit it for review and feedback.
