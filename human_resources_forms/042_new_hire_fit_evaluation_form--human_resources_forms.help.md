# New Hire Fit Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Fit Evaluation Form is a tool used by managers to evaluate an employee's fit for the company, team, and job. This form helps gather information on the employee's skills, experience, and expectations, providing valuable insights for a smooth onboarding process and a strong start in their new role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the relevant fields according to your thoughts on the employee's fit for the company, team, and job.
2. For fields with dropdown options, choose the one that best describes the employee's fit.
3. Use the text fields to elaborate on the employee's strengths, weaknesses, and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company values** (`Company values`, text, optional): Please describe the company's values and how they align with the employee's own values and expectations.
* **Job requirements** (`Job requirements`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes the employee's fit for the job requirements. Options: Strong work ethic, Teamwork skills, Problem-solving skills.
* **Fit for company culture** (`Fit for company culture`, text, optional): Explain how the employee's personality and work style align with the company culture.
* **Fit for team** (`Fit for team`, text, optional): Describe the employee's ability to work collaboratively with the team and how they will contribute to team dynamics.
* **Fit for job** (`Fit for job`, text, optional): Outline the employee's skills and experience that make them a strong fit for the job.
* **Fit for manager** (`Fit for manager`, text, optional): Describe the employee's ability to work effectively with the manager and how they will respond to feedback and guidance.
* **Fit for workplace** (`Fit for workplace`, text, optional): Discuss the employee's ability to adapt to the company's work environment and how they will perform in different situations.
* **Onboarding process** (`Onboarding process`, text, optional): Outline the steps the employee needs to take during the onboarding process to ensure a smooth transition into their new role.
* **Training program** (`Training program`, text, optional): Describe the type of training or mentorship the employee may need to receive to excel in their new role.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any feedback or concerns you may have about the employee's fit for the company, team, or job.
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the employee's fit that didn't fit in the other fields.
