# Work Permit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Permit Form is designed to collect and manage employee work permits, ensuring compliance with applicable regulations and policies. This form will guide you through the required fields for a seamless process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee's name in the first field, **Employee Name**.
2. In the second field, **Employee ID**, enter your employee's ID number.
3. Choose the correct **Work Permit Type** from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the **Expiration Date** of the current permit.
5. Provide a reason for the **Renewal Reason**.
6. Enter the **New Permit Start Date**.
7. Enter the **Current Permit End Date**.
8. (Optional) Enter the **Supervisor Approval Date**, **Manager Approval Date**, and **HR Review Date**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Please enter the name of the employee for whom you are filling this form.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the unique ID number of the employee.
* **Work Permit Type**: Choose the correct permit type from the available options to ensure accurate classification.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the date when the current permit expires.
* **Renewal Reason**: Provide the reason for renewing the permit, if applicable.
* **New Permit Start Date**: Enter the start date of the new permit.
* **Current Permit End Date**: Enter the end date of the current permit.
* **Supervisor Approval Date** (Optional): Enter the date of supervisor approval.
* **Manager Approval Date** (Optional): Enter the date of manager approval.
* **HR Review Date** (Optional): Enter the date of HR review.

## Tips
Remember to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly, ensuring compliance with applicable regulations and company policies. If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank or mark it as "N/A" where necessary. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
