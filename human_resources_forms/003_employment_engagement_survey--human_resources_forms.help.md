# employment_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on overall happiness, satisfaction levels, and perceptions of employees in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answers to each question, and be honest about your feelings.
2. Choose all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Write any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall happiness** (Select multiple options):
	+ Choose from: Very happy, Somewhat happy, Neutral, Somewhat unhappy, Very unhappy
	+ This question helps us understand how satisfied you are with your overall experience in the workplace.
* **Satisfaction level** (Select one option):
	+ Choose from: Highly satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied
	+ This question gauges your level of satisfaction with your job.
* **Perception** (Select multiple options):
	+ Choose from: Very positive, Somewhat positive, Neutral, Somewhat negative, Very negative
	+ This question explores your perceptions of the workplace atmosphere.
* **Comments**:
	+ Write any additional thoughts, suggestions, or feedback you have.
	+ Be as brief or as detailed as you like.
* **Overall happiness 2** (Select multiple options):
	+ This is another question to gauge overall happiness, similar to the first one.
	+ Choose from: Very happy, Somewhat happy, Neutral, Somewhat unhappy, Very unhappy

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide your genuine feedback.
- Select all that apply for each question.
- If you have any further comments or questions, feel free to write them down in the "Comments" field.
