# Doctrine And Philosophy Of Ministry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the doctrine and philosophy of a ministry. It will help staff members provide input on the ministry's direction and focus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of the doctrine and philosophy of the ministry in the "Form Title" field.
2. Select the category of the doctrine and philosophy from the options provided in the "Form Category" field.
3. Enter a brief description of the doctrine and philosophy in the "Form Description" field.
4. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the doctrine and philosophy in the "Form Email" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the doctrine and philosophy in the "Form Phone" field.
6. Select a date for the doctrine and philosophy in the "Form Date" field.
7. Select a time for the doctrine and philosophy in the "Form Time" field.
8. Enter any additional notes in the "Form Note" field.
9. Select the submit and cancel buttons to complete or cancel the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief title of the doctrine and philosophy of the ministry.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category of the doctrine and philosophy from the options provided.
	+ Options: HR, Finance, Other
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the doctrine and philosophy of the ministry.
* **Form Email** (`form_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the doctrine and philosophy.
* **Form Phone** (`form_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the doctrine and philosophy.
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the doctrine and philosophy.
* **Form Time** (`form_time`, `time`, required: false): Select a time for the doctrine and philosophy.
* **Form Note** (`form_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the doctrine and philosophy.
* **Form Submit Button** (`form_submit_button`, `text`, required: false): Select this button to complete the form.
* **Form Cancel Button** (`form_cancel_button`, `text`, required: false): Select this button to cancel the form.
* **Form Required Note** (`form_required_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes required for the doctrine and philosophy.
* **Form Required Label** (`form_required_label`, `text`, required: false): Enter a label for the required notes.
* **Form Required Hint** (`form_required_hint`, `text`, required: false): Enter a hint for the required notes.
* **Form Required Type** (`form_required_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of the required notes from the options provided.
* **Form Required Option** (`form_required_option`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options for the required notes.
* **Form Required Text** (`form_required_text`, `text`, required: false): Enter text for the required notes.
* **Form Required Hint** (`form_required_hint`, `text`, required: false): Enter a hint for the required text.
* **Form Required Note** (`form_required_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional required notes.
* **Form Required Email** (`form_required_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the required notes.
* **Form Required Phone** (`form_required_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the required notes.
* **Form Required Date** (`form_required_date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the required notes.
* **Form Required Time** (`form_required_time`, `time`, required: false): Select a time for the required notes.
* **Form Required Submit Button** (`form_required_submit_button`, `text`, required: false): Select this button to complete the required notes.
* **Form Required Cancel Button** (`form_required_cancel_button`, `text`, required: false): Select this button to cancel the required notes.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and edit the doctrine and philosophy of the ministry before completing this form. If you are unsure, please check with your supervisor or the ministry's leadership.
