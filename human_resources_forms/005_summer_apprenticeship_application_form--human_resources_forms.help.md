# Summer Apprenticeship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are interested in participating in a summer internship program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you are filling out the form on your own behalf, unless you are submitting a group application.
3. If you are submitting a group application, please ensure that all members of the group have filled out the form correctly.
4. Please provide your correct contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you.
