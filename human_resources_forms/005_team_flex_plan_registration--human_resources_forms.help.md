# team_flex_plan_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The team_flex_plan_registration form is designed to collect user details and other relevant information for team management and planning purposes. It's a crucial tool to help teams function effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2.  Fill out the form with the required information.
3.  Enter your user details in the "User Details" field.
4.  Select the team leader from the "Team Leaders" dropdown list.
5.  Provide shift details in the "Shift Details" field.
6.  Enter the start date in the "Start Date" field.
7.  Enter the end date in the "End Date" field.
8.  Select the status from the "Status" dropdown list.
9.  Add any relevant notes in the "Notes" field.
10.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12.  Provide manager details in the "Manager Details" field.
13.  Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Details** (`User Details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant user details.
*   **Team Leaders** (`Team Leaders`, select_one, required/optional): Select one team leader from the dropdown list.
*   **Shift Details** (`Shift Details`, text, required/optional): Provide shift details such as shift start and end times, breaks, and other relevant information.
*   **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the team flex plan.
*   **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the team flex plan.
*   **Status** (`Status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of the team flex plan from the dropdown list (Active, Inactive, Pending).
*   **Notes** (`Notes`, text, required/optional): Add any relevant notes about the team flex plan.
*   **Email** (`Email`, text, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Manager Details** (`Manager Details`, text, required/optional): Provide manager details such as name, email, and phone number.
*   **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required/optional): Click on this button to submit the form.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have all the necessary information and permissions before filling out the form.
*   Be accurate and precise while filling out the form to ensure that the data collected is useful for its intended purpose.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
