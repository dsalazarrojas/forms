# Health Compliance Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Compliance Employment Form is a document used to collect and record employee health information, ensuring that their health and medical requirements are met and monitored.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the Employee ID in the designated field.
2. Provide the Job Title, Department, and Job Status relevant to the employee.
3. Select the Job Type (Full-time, Part-time, or Intern).
4. Upload the Medical Certificate (if required).
5. Provide the Workplace Readiness and Date of the Medical Certificate.
6. Confirm Medical Clearance (True or False).
7. Add any relevant Medical Clearance Notes.
8. Sign the Employee Signature and Date.
9. Sign the Employer Signature and Date.
10. Enter the Date Submitted and Date Received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (Number)**: Enter your unique Employee ID number.
* **Job Title**: Provide the title of your job or position in the company.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Job Status**: Select the current status of your job (On the Job, Furloughed, Terminated, or Resigned).
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you hold (Full-time, Part-time, or Intern).
* **Health Attestation (Date)**: Provide the date of your health attestation.
* **Health Atteestation (Note)**: Add any additional health attestation notes.
* **Medical Certificate (Text)**: Upload your medical certificate (if required).
* **Workplace Readiness (Date)**: Provide the date of your workplace readiness.
* **Medical Clearance (Select One)**: Confirm whether your medical clearance is True or False.
* **Medical Clearance Notes (Note)**: Add any additional medical clearance notes.
* **Employee Signature (Text)**: Sign your name as the employee.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Employer Signature (Text)**: Sign the name of the employer representative.
* **Date Submitted (Date)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Date Received (Date)**: Enter the date of receipt.
* **Medical Certificate Status (Select Multiple)**: Select the status of your medical certificate (Not Required, Required, or Review Required).
* **Medical Certificate Upload (Text)**: Upload your medical certificate.
* **Workplace Readiness Notes (Note)**: Add any additional workplace readiness notes.
* **Medical Clearance Notes (Note)**: Add any additional medical clearance notes.
* **Employee Health Notes (Note)**: Add any additional employee health notes.
* **Date Completed (Date)**: Enter the date completed.
* **Date Reviewed (Date)**: Enter the date reviewed.
* **Date Approved (Date)**: Enter the date approved.
* **Date Declined (Date)**: Enter the date declined.
