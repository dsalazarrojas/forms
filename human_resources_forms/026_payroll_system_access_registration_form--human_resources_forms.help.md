# Payroll System Access Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for HR personnel and system administrators to register new employees or update existing ones in the Payroll System. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your employee information accurately.
2. Enter your email address for verification and communication purposes.
3. Provide your current job title or position (prophets).
4. Choose your access level(s) by selecting from the list of options. Please note that some options may not be available or may be subject to approval. 
5. Select the approvers for your access level (approvals). This is a multi-select field, so select as many approvers as applicable.
6. Add any additional notes about your request or current status (notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (1, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or in our HR system.
* **Email** (2, email, required/optional): Enter your official email address for payroll system notifications and communication purposes.
* **Prophets** (3, text, required/optional): Enter your current job title or position in the company.
* **Access Levels** (4, select_one, required/optional): Choose your highest level of access you need for the Payroll System. Please note that some options may not be available or may be subject to approval. You may choose one or multiple access levels.

* **Approvals** (5, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the approvers for your access level(s). You must select one or multiple approvers depending on your needs.

* **Notes** (6, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your request or current status.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have questions or need assistance, please contact your HR or payroll department.
