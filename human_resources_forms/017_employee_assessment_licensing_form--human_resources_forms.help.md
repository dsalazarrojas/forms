# employee_assessment_licensing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's assessment for licensing purposes, including job title, job function, license type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the employee's job title and job function in the respective fields.
3. Select the correct license type from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the employee's name and ID, if applicable.
5. Record the assessment date, if required.
6. Add any comments or review comments, if necessary.
7. Complete the form by entering the license number and assessment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Licensing Requirements**: This field is not applicable in this form.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title, e.g., "Software Engineer" or "Marketing Manager".
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name, as it appears on their identification or in your organization's records.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the employee started with the company, if required for licensure purposes.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the employee's license will expire or end, if applicable.
* **Employee Status**: Select the current status of the employee, whether Active or Inactive.
* **Job Function**: Select the employee's job function, either Manager or Employee.
* **License Type**: Select the type of license the employee possesses, e.g., Type1 or Type2.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date the employee's assessment was conducted, if required.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the employee's assessment.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number, if applicable.
* **Manager Signature**: The manager's signature, indicating approval of the assessment.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Employee Signature**: The employee's signature, indicating completion of the assessment.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the review.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date the review was conducted.
* **Review Type**: Select the type of review, either Positive or Negative.
* **License Number**: Enter the license number assigned to the employee.
* **Assessment Status**: Select the status of the assessment, whether Complete or Incomplete.
* **Comments Reviewer**: Add any comments from the reviewer.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the date the assessment was completed.
* **Review Completed**: Enter the date the review was completed.
* **License Expiration**: Enter the date the license will expire, if applicable.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submission.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Store the completed form in a secure location, such as a digital archive.
* Review and update the form as needed to ensure it remains relevant to your organization's requirements.
