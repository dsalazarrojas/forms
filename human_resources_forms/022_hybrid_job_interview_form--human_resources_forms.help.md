# Hybrid Job Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Job Interview Form is designed to collect information from candidates during the interview process. This form aims to gather essential details about the candidate, interview context, and any feedback or comments provided by the interviewer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Candidate Information section with the applicant's details.
2. Provide the interview information in the corresponding section.
3. Share any feedback or comments in the Feedback Information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Candidate Information
- **Candidate Information** (`Candidate Information`, `text`, optional): Enter the candidate's name, email address, and any other relevant details you want to capture.

### Interview Information
- **Interview Information** (`Interview Information`, `text`, optional): Share the context and details about the interview, such as the date, time, and location.

### Feedback Information
- **Feedback Information** (`Feedback Information`, `text`, optional): Record any comments or feedback from the candidate, including suggestions or areas for improvement.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form before completing it to ensure you have all the necessary information.
- Enter accurate and legible handwriting in the text fields.
- Save and print a copy of the form for future reference, if needed.
