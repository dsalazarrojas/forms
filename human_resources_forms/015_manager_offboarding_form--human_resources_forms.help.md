# Manager Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Offboarding Form is used to document the offboarding process of a manager, capturing key information and details about their departure, and ensuring a smooth transition and accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter the required information into the designated fields, including manager name, job title, start and end dates, reason for departure, and other relevant details.
3. Select the appropriate options for benefits terminated, assets returned, knowledge transferred, equipment returned, company resources, property returned, vehicle returned, and other assets returned.
4. Sign and date the form as required, and submit it for review and completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the manager's name as it appears on their official records or as they are commonly known.
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the manager's job title as it is currently assigned to them or as it will be after their departure.
### Manager Email
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the manager's work email address as it appears on their official records or as it is currently active.
### Manager Phone
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the manager's work phone number as it appears on their official records or as it is currently active.
### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the manager's employment started, or the date they began their role as a manager.
### End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the manager's employment ended, or the date they will leave their role as a manager.
### Reason for Departure
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, text, required/optional): Select the reason for the manager's departure as it applies to their situation.
### Notice Period
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, required/optional): Select the notice period provided to the manager before their departure.
### Benefits Terminated
* **Benefits Terminated** (`benefits_terminated`, select multiple, required/optional): Select all the benefits that will be terminated for the manager.
### Assets Returned
* **Assets Returned** (`assets_returned`, select one, required/optional): Select whether all assets have been returned or not.
### Knowledge Transferred
* **Knowledge Transferred** (`knowledge_transferred`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the level of knowledge transferred to other team members or colleagues.
### Equipment Returned
* **Equipment Returned** (`equipment_returned`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the equipment that has been returned or not.
### Company Resources
* **Company Resources** (`company_resources`, select one, required/optional): Select whether company resources have been returned or not.
### Property Returned
* **Property Returned** (`property_returned`, select one, required/optional): Select whether company property has been returned or not.
### Vehicle Returned
* **Vehicle Returned** (`vehicle_returned`, select one, required/optional): Select whether company vehicle has been returned or not.
### Other Assets Returned
* **Other Assets Returned** (`other_assets_returned`, select one, required/optional): Select whether other company assets have been returned or not.
### Manager Signature
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the manager's signature as proof of completion and acknowledgment.
### HR Signature
* **HR Signature** (`hr_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the HR representative's signature as proof of completion and acknowledgment.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments that may be relevant to the manager's offboarding process.
### Completed
* **Completed** (`completed`, select one, required/optional): Select whether the offboarding process has been completed or not.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for review and completion.
* Double-check the manager's contact information for accuracy.
* Verify that all relevant assets and equipment have been returned or accounted for.
* Sign and date the form as required for official documentation and completion.
