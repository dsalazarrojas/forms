# Editorial Staff Employment Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about new editorial staff members. This includes details such as job title, department, job grade, new hire status, job start and end dates, job type, job hours, contact information, and emergency contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "New Hire Details" section with the employee's name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the correct job classification and title from the available options.
3. Enter the department and job grade for the new hire.
4. Choose the correct new hire status and job start date.
5. Select the job type and enter the job hours.
6. Provide the manager's name, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
7. Enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, and email address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, text, required): Please enter the employee's name, email address, and phone number.
* **Acknowledgement Details** (`acknowledgement_details`, text, required): Please provide any acknowledgement or agreement details.
* **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, text, required): Please enter any other details relevant to the agreement.
* **New Hire Information** (`new_hire_information`, text, required): Please enter any relevant information about the new hire.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Please enter a brief description of the job.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, select_one, required): Please select the correct job classification from the available options.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Please enter the job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Please enter the department the new hire will be working in.
* **Job Grade** (`job_grade`, select_one, required): Please select the correct job grade from the available options.
* **New Hire Status** (`new_hire_status`, select_one, required): Please select the correct new hire status from the available options.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Please enter the job start date.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required): Please enter the job end date (if applicable).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select the correct job type from the available options.
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`, number, required): Please enter the number of job hours.
* **New Hire Email** (`new_hire_email`, email, required): Please enter the new hire's email address.
* **New Hire Phone** (`new_hire_phone`, text, required): Please enter the new hire's phone number.
* **Manager Details** (`manager_details`, text, required): Please enter the manager's name, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Please enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, and email address (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Please enter the relationship between the emergency contact and the new hire.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Please enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Please enter the emergency contact's address.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure completeness and accuracy. 
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
