# Employee Image Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Image Release Consent Form is used to obtain consent from employees to release their images, audio, or video recordings for official purposes such as HR and communications projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and implications of releasing your image, audio, or video recordings.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for Form Control to indicate whether you are authorized to release the image, audio, or video recording.
3. Enter your employee ID or name in the employee_name field.
4. Choose the department you belong to in the department field (if applicable).
5. Select the job title you hold in the job_title field (if applicable).
6. Choose the team you wish to release the image to in the release_to field.
7. Indicate whether you wish to release the image for official purposes in the release_for field.
8. Sign and date the form by typing your name and the current date in the signature, date_signed, and form_completed_by fields.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes in the comments field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Control** (Form Control, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to indicate whether you are authorized to release the image, audio, or video recording. Select "Yes" to indicate you are authorized, and "No" to indicate you are not.
- **employee_photo** (employee_photo, text, required): This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form.
- **date_of_event** (date_of_event, date, required): This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form.
- **release_to** (release_to, select_one, required): This field allows you to choose the team you wish to release the image to. Select "HR and Communications Team" or "Marketing and Communications Team" or "Management" or "Security and IT".
- **release_for** (release_for, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to indicate whether you wish to release the image for official purposes. Select "Yes" or "No".
- **audio_video_photo_release** (audio_video_photo_release, text, required): This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form.
- **consent_signature** (consent_signature, text, required): This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form.
- **date_signed** (date_signed, date, required): This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form.
- **employee_name** (employee_name, text, required): This field is used to enter your employee ID or name.
- **department** (department, select_one, required): This field allows you to choose your department. Select "Yes" or "No" (if not applicable).
- **job_title** (job_title, select_one, required): This field allows you to choose your job title. Select "Yes" or "No" (if not applicable).
- **signature** (signature, text, required): This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form.
- **comments** (comments, note, required): This field is not applicable as it's not present in the form.
- **form_completed_by** (form_completed_by, text, required): This field is used to enter your name to confirm you have completed the form.
- **date_completed** (date_completed, date, required): This field is used to enter the current date you completed the form.

Note: Some fields are not present in this form, but were included in the original YAML structure.
