# Urban Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Leave Request Form is used to request a leave of absence from work. This form helps employees provide necessary information about their leave, allowing for a smoother and more efficient request process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, follow these steps:
	* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to submit this form.
	* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly with your personal and leave details.
	* Review your answers carefully to ensure they are correct and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details (`employee_details`, Text, Required: false):** This field is where you enter your name, employee ID, or any other relevant identification information.
* **Leave Type (`leave_type`, Select, Required: false):** Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are requesting leave.
* **Start Date (`start_date`, Date, Required: false):** Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date (`end_date`, Date, Required: false):** Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason (`reason`, Text, Required: false):** Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave.
* **Contact Number (`contact_number`, Text, Required: false):** Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **Email (`email`, Email, Required: false):** Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Manager (`manager`, Select, Required: false):** Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your manager has approved this leave.
* **Additional Tips:** 
  * Ensure your manager's approval is confirmed before submitting this form.
  * Review and edit your answers carefully before submission.
