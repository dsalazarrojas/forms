The Workforce Diversity Survey Form is designed to collect information about an employee's demographics, job details, and other relevant data. This data will be used to assess and improve the diversity, equity, and inclusion of our workforce. Please note that this information will be used to identify potential biases and gaps in our hiring and promotion processes, and to make informed decisions to address these issues.

To complete the form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Survey Information**: Start by providing a brief description of your job title, job category, and job location.
2. **Demographic Information**: Answer questions about your demographics, such as your age, birthdate, country of residence, language, and native country.
3. **Job Details**: Answer questions about your job details, such as years worked, job level, and employment status.
4. **Employee Data**: Provide your employee ID, name, gender, disability status, and veteran status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Information**:
	+ **Job Title**: A brief description of your job title.
	+ **Job Category**: A brief description of the category your job falls under.
	+ **Job Location**: A brief description of the location where you work.
* **Demographic Information**:
	+ **Employee ID**: A unique ID for you as an employee.
	+ **Employee Name**: Your first and last name.
	+ **Employee Gender**: Your gender (male, female, non-binary, etc.).
	+ **Employee Age**: Your age.
	+ **Employee Birthdate**: Your birthdate.
	+ **Employee Ethnicity**: Select all that apply to describe your ethnicity.
	+ **Employee Race**: Select all that apply to describe your race.
	+ **Employee Country of Residence**: Your country of residence.
	+ **Employee Language**: Your native language.
	+ **Employee Native Country**: Your country of origin.
* **Job Details**:
	+ **Job Years Worked**: The number of years you have worked in your current job.
	+ **Job Level**: A brief description of your job level (e.g., entry-level, senior-level, etc.).
	+ **Job Employment Status**: Your current employment status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
	+ **Job Tenure**: The number of years you have been employed with the company.
* **Employee Data**:
	+ **Employee Disability Status**: Select your current disability status (Active or Inactive).
	+ **Employee Veteran Status**: Select your current veteran status (Active or Inactive).

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
* This form is confidential and will be used to improve the diversity and inclusion of our workforce.
