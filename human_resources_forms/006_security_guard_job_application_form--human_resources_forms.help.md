# Security Guard Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in working as security guards. The information gathered will be used to evaluate the applicant's suitability for the security guard position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details section with your personal information.
2. Provide your work experience in the work experience sections.
3. Select your languages and skills that you possess.
4. Fill out the emergency contact section with your next of kin's information.
5. Provide any relevant medical certifications or clearances.
6. Fill out the remaining sections with any additional information that is relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`): This section is where you can provide a brief description of yourself.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Provide your current contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_details`): This is where you can list your next of kin or emergency contact person and their contact information.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Provide a brief description of your employment status, including your current job and any previous jobs.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`): This section is where you can provide detailed descriptions of your work experience.
* **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`): Select the types of jobs you are interested in or qualified for.
* **References** (`references`): Provide the names and contact information of people who can vouch for your work ethic and skills.
* **Education** (`education`): Provide a description of your educational background.
* **Languages** (`languages`): Select the languages you are proficient in.
* **Availability** (`availability`): Provide your availability for work and any relevant schedules.
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`): Provide any medical clearances or certifications you have.
* **Skills** (`skills`): Select the skills you possess that are relevant to the security guard position.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`): If you have any emergency services experience, provide a brief description.
* **Physical Description** (`physical_description`): Provide a brief description of your physical appearance.
* **Criminal History** (`criminal_history`): Provide any relevant information about your criminal history.
* **License and Certifications** (`license_and_certifications`): Provide any relevant licenses or certifications you hold.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`): Provide any medical certifications you hold.
* **Next of Kin** (`next_of_kin`): Provide the name and contact information of your next of kin.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`): Provide the address of your emergency contact.
* **Work Experience 1** (`work_experience_1`): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
* **Work Experience 2** (`work_experience_2`): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
* **Job References 1** (`job_references_1`): Provide the name and contact information of a job reference.
* **Job References 2** (`job_references_2`): Provide the name and contact information of a job reference.

Note: Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure your application is evaluated fairly.
