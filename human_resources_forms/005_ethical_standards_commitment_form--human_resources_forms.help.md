# Ethical Standards Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethical Standards Commitment Form is a document that requires employees to acknowledge and commit to the company's Ethical Standards. It's a vital step in the onboarding process to ensure a seamless integration into the company culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please provide your Employee Name in the space provided.
2. Enter your Manager's Name, who is responsible for overseeing your work.
3. Select 'Yes' if you agree to abide by the company's Ethical Standards.
4. Enter the date you signed this form, using the date picker provided.
5. Write a brief declaration stating your understanding and commitment to the Ethical Standards.
6. Write a brief declaration stating your understanding and commitment to the Stakeholder Declaration.
7. Enter the date of the review of this form, using the date picker provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your official employee name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager who is responsible for overseeing your work.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by_signature`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you agree to abide by the company's Ethical Standards. This confirms your commitment to upholding the company's values and principles.
* **Dated By** (`dated_by_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign this form, using the date picker to ensure accuracy.
* **Staff Declaration** (`staff_declaration`, text, required): Write a brief statement explaining your understanding and commitment to the Ethical Standards.
* **Stakeholder Declaration** (`stakeholder_declaration`, text, required): Write a brief statement explaining your understanding and commitment to the Stakeholder Declaration.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the review of this form, using the date picker to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask the relevant team for clarification.
- Remember that this form is a commitment to uphold the company's Ethical Standards, so please be honest and thorough in your responses.
