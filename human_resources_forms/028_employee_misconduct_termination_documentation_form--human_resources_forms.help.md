# Employee Misconduct Termination Documentation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Misconduct Termination Documentation Form is used to document the termination of an employee's employment due to misconduct. This form is a crucial document that helps maintain accurate records and ensures compliance with company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's details: Provide the employee's number, name, and department in the respective fields.
2. Select the reason for termination: Choose the reason for termination from the given options or provide a custom reason if it's not listed.
3. Describe the misconduct: Provide a detailed description of the misconduct committed by the employee.
4. Specify the termination dates: Enter the termination dates, including the start and end dates of termination.
5. Provide additional details: Add any other relevant details, such as the manager's and supervisor's names, department, and job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's number or ID.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's name.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of termination.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the reason for termination from the given options or provide a custom reason.
* **Misconduct Description** (`misconduct_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the misconduct committed by the employee.
* **Termination Type** (`termination_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of termination from the given options.
* **Reason for Termination Other** (`reason_for_termination_other`, `text`, required/optional): If the reason for termination is not listed, provide a custom reason.
* **Termination Date End Date** (`termination_date_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of termination.
* **Termination Date Start Date** (`termination_date_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of termination.
* **Termination Date End Date 2** (`termination_date_end_date_2`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the second end date of termination.
* **Reason For Termination Other 2** (`reason_for_termination_other_2`, `text`, required/optional): If the reason for termination is not listed, provide a custom reason.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the manager's name.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title.
* **Termination Date Start** (`termination_date_start`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of termination.
* **Reason For Termination Start** (`reason_for_termination_start`, `text`, required/optional): If the reason for termination is not listed, provide a custom reason.
* **Misconduct Description Other** (`misconduct_description_other`, `text`, required/optional): If the misconduct description is not listed, provide a custom description.
* **Termination Date End** (`termination_date_end`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of termination.
* **Supervisor Name 2** (`supervisor_name_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Reason For Termination End** (`reason_for_termination_end`, `text`, required/optional): If the reason for termination is not listed, provide a custom reason.
* **Manager Name 2** (`manager_name_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the manager's name.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to complete this form.
* Double-check the dates and reasons for termination before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is a crucial document that helps maintain accurate records and ensures compliance with company policies and procedures.
