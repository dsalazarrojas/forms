# Employee Timesheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a standard template for tracking employee work hours for each workweek. It helps in maintaining accurate records and facilitates the calculation of hours worked and billed, as well as other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant workweek (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
2.  Enter the start date and time of your workday.
3.  Enter the end date and time of your workday.
4.  Calculate and enter the hours you worked during the week (including breaks).
5.  Calculate and enter the hours billed for the pay period.
6.  If applicable, record the time spent on breaks, as well as the payment rate for the pay period.
7.  Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the workweek.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workweek**: Select the week you are logging hours for (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
    *   Type: Text
*   **start\_date**: Enter the date when your work began on this day.
    *   Type: Date
*   **start\_time**: Enter the time when you began working on this day.
    *   Type: Time
*   **end\_date**: Enter the date when you completed work on this day.
    *   Type: Date
*   **end\_time**: Enter the time when you finished work on this day.
    *   Type: Time
*   **hours\_worked**: Enter the total hours worked during this week, including breaks.
    *   Type: Number
*   **hours\_billed**: Enter the hours billed for this pay period.
    *   Type: Number
*   **break\_time**: Enter the total time spent on breaks during this week.
    *   Type: Time
*   **pay\_rate**: Enter the payment rate for this pay period.
    *   Type: Number
*   **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the workweek.
    *   Type: Note
