# Employee Wellness Program Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a waiver from employees participating in the Employee Wellness Program. The waiver is required to be filled out by the employee to acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to the program terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the Employee Wellness Program you are participating in.
2. Select the date you wish to participate in the program.
3. Enter your employee name.
4. Enter your employee email address.
5. Enter your employee phone number.
6. Select either 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have read, understand, and agree to the program terms and conditions.
7. Enter your agreement to the program terms, as per your waiver selection.
8. Enter your signature, as proof of your waiver and agreement.
9. Select the date you have signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Wellness Program Name** (program_name, text, required): This field is to enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Program Date** (program_date, date, required): This field is to select a date for the program participation. Make sure to pick the correct date for your participation.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required): This field is to enter your full name as an employee.
* **Employee Email** (employee_email, email, required): This field is to enter your employee email address, used for communication.
* **Employee Phone** (employee_phone, text, required): This field is to enter your employee phone number.
* **Employee Waiver** (employee_waiver, select_one, required): Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the program terms and conditions. If you select 'False', you will not be able to proceed.
* **Program Terms** (program_terms, text, required): This field is to enter your explanation for your waiver selection. Please explain why you are selecting 'False' for your waiver.
* **Program Agreement** (program_agreement, text, required): This field is to enter your agreement to the program terms and conditions.
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature, text, required): This field is to enter your signature, as proof of your waiver and agreement.
* **Date Signed** (date_signed, date, required): This field is to select the date you have signed the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before proceeding.
- Select 'True' for the waiver only if you have fully read, understand, and agree to the program terms and conditions.
- Enter your signature in the 'Employee Signature' field.
