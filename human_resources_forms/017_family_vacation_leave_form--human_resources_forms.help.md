# Family Vacation Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Vacation Leave Form is used to request time off for family-related reasons, such as vacations or bereavements. This form is meant for employees to submit their leave requests to their manager or HR department for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your **Start Date** and **End Date** of your leave in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
3. Specify the **Number of Days** you plan to take off.
4. Provide a **Reason for Leave** in the "Reason for Leave" field.
5. Select your **Manager Approval** in the "Manager Approval" field.
6. Choose your **Employee Status** (e.g., Active, Active on Leave) in the "Employee Status" field.
7. Select the **Leave Type** (e.g., Vacation, Bereavement, Sick Leave) in the "Leave Type" field.
8. Select your **Department** (e.g., HR, IT, Finance, Marketing) in the "Department" field.
9. Optionally, enter your **Employee Email** and **Employee Phone** in the "Employee Email" and "Employee Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official employee record.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Number of Days**: Enter the number of days you plan to take off.
* **Reason for Leave**: Enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting leave (e.g., vacation, bereavement, family emergency).
* **Manager Approval**: Select the manager who will be approving your leave request.
* **Employee Status**: Choose your current status as an employee (e.g., Active, Active on Leave).
* **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Vacation, Bereavement, Sick Leave).
* **Department**: Select your department (e.g., HR, IT, Finance, Marketing).
* **Employee Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave (optional).

Note: The **Manager Approval** field is required to be filled out, as it indicates that the manager has reviewed and approved your request.
