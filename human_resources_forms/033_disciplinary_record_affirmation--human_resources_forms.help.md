# Disciplinary Record Affirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and record disciplinary actions taken against employees, ensuring consistency and accuracy in maintaining a fair and transparent work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee ID of the employee involved in the incident.
2. Select the date of the disciplinary incident.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
4. Choose the disciplinary action taken (if any).
5. Enter the reason for the disciplinary action (optional).
6. Enter any witness statements or comments relevant to the incident (optional).
7. Select the outcome of the incident (e.g., Employee Acknowledged Incidents, Incident Resolved, Incident Not Resolved).
8. Enter a detailed description of the incident outcome (optional).
9. Sign and date the form as the employee involved in the incident.
10. Sign and date the form as the manager or supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee ID (1)
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required: false): Enter the employee's identification number or ID.

### Disciplinary Incident Date (2)
* **Disciplinary Incident Date** (`disciplinarity_incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.

### Incident Description (3)
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the facts and circumstances surrounding it.

### Disciplinary Action Taken (4)
* **Disciplinary Action Taken** (`disciplinary_action_taken`, select_one, required: false): Select the disciplinary action taken, if any, from the following options:
	+ Verbal Warning
	+ Written Warning
	+ Suspension
	+ Termination

### Disciplinary Action Reason (5)
* **Disciplinary Action Reason** (`disciplinary_action_reason`, text, required: false): Enter the reason for the disciplinary action taken (if any).

### Witness Statements (6)
* **Witness Statements** (`witness_statements`, text, required: false): Enter any witness statements or comments relevant to the incident.

### Incident Outcome (7)
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, select_one, required: false): Select the outcome of the incident from the following options:
	+ Employee Acknowledged Incidents
	+ Incident Resolved
	+ Incident Not Resolved

### Incident Outcome Details (8)
* **Incident Outcome Details** (`incident_outcome_details`, text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the incident outcome (if any).

### Employee Signature (9)
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date the form as the employee involved in the incident.

### Manager Signature (10)
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date the form as the manager or supervisor.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Review and update the form as necessary to reflect changes in company policies and procedures.
- Store the completed form securely and in compliance with relevant data protection regulations.
