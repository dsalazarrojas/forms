# job_description_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job_description_form is a collection of fields that helps job applicants provide a detailed description of their skills, experience, and qualifications for a job posting. This form is designed to be completed by job seekers and is intended to be reviewed by HR professionals or hiring managers to assess candidate suitability for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the job posting and understand the requirements and expectations of the position.
2. Answer each field accurately and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Details
* **Job Details** (`JobDetails`, text, no): Enter a brief description of your relevant work or academic experience, including any notable projects or achievements that demonstrate your skills and qualifications.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`JobTitle`, text, no): Enter your current or desired job title, as it relates to the position you are applying for.

### Job Summary
* **Job Summary** (`JobSummary`, text, no): Provide a concise summary of your experience, skills, or education that aligns with the job posting.

### Job Description
* **Job Description** (`JobDescription`, text, no): Enter a detailed description of your experience, skills, or education that aligns with the job posting.

### Job Requirements
* **Job Requirements** (`JobRequirements`, text, no): List any specific requirements or qualifications you have that align with the job posting, such as certifications or software proficiency.

### Job Educational Background
* **Job Educational Background** (`JobEducationalBackground`, text, no): Describe your educational background, including degrees, certifications, or other relevant qualifications.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the job posting carefully and answer each field accurately and honestly.
* Use specific examples to demonstrate your skills and qualifications.
* Proofread your answers carefully to ensure they are complete and free of errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
