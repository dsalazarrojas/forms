# hr-impact-measurement-research-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Impact Measurement Research Application Form is designed to collect data on research projects that measure the impact of human resources initiatives. This form will help researchers provide detailed information about their projects, including project details, funding status, and impact assessment. By completing this form, researchers can help the institution track and evaluate research projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your project (number, e.g., "HR123").
2. **Title**: Enter a concise and descriptive title for your research project.
3. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of your research project, including its purpose and goals.
4. **Research Proposal**: Optionally, provide a brief research proposal outlining your project's objectives and methodology.
5. **Supporting Docs**: Select any relevant documents that support your research proposal, such as a resume or letter of recommendation.
6. **Impact Details**: Select the type of impact you expect from your research, such as Financial or HR metrics.
7. **Project Status**: Enter the current status of your project, in date format.
8. **Project Duration**: Enter the expected duration of your project.
9. **Start Date**: Enter the planned start date of your project.
10. **End Date**: Enter the planned end date of your project.
11. **Funding Status**: Select the funding status of your project, such as Fully Funded, Partially Funded, or Not Funded.
12. **Funding Source**: Select the source of your funding, such as Government, Private, or Other (please provide details).
13. **Funding Amount**: Enter the amount of funding you have or will receive.
14. **Research Methodology**: Describe the methodology you will use to conduct your research.
15. **Research Outcomes**: Describe the expected outcomes of your research.
16. **Research Conclusions**: Summarize your research conclusions.
17. **Project Contacts**: Provide any relevant project contacts, such as email or phone numbers.
18. **Research Justification**: Explain why your research is important and how it will contribute to the institution.
19. **Supporting Docs**: Select any additional supporting documents, such as a resume or letter of recommendation.
20. **Impact Assessment**: Provide a detailed assessment of the impact of your research.
21. **Impact Assessment Ratio**: Enter the ratio of the impact of your research.
22. **Impact Assessment Status**: Select the status of your impact assessment, such as Completed, Ongoing, or Not Started.
23. **Impact Assessment Date**: Enter the date of your impact assessment.
24. **Impact Assessment Duration**: Enter the duration of your impact assessment.
25. **Impact Assessment Funding**: Select the funding status of your impact assessment, such as Fully Funded, Partially Funded, or Not Funded.
26. **Impact Assessment Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your impact assessment.
27. **Review Status**: Select the status of your review, such as Completed, Ongoing, or Not Started.
