# Agricultural Worker Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Worker Onboarding Form is used to collect essential information from potential workers and verify their eligibility for employment on the farm.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button to start the onboarding process.
- Fill out each section of the form as instructed.
- Ensure all required fields are filled in according to the instructions provided.
- Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button to confirm accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`worker_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`worker_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Middle Name** (`middle_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your middle name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Social Security Number** (`social_security`, `text`, required): Enter your Social Security Number for employment verification purposes.
- **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current mailing address.
- **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
- **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
- **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary contact number.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
- **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, `select_one`, required): Select the job title or role you are applying for from the provided options.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter your expected start date for the job.
- **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of employment you are applying for from the provided options.
- **Previous Agricultural Experience** (`previous_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of years you have worked in agriculture from the provided options.
- **Experience Details** (`experience_details`, `text`, optional): Describe your previous experience in agriculture if necessary.
- **Equipment Certifications** (`equipment_certification`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all certifications you hold related to equipment operation.
- **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all languages you are proficient in.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact.
- **Work Authorization Status** (`work_authorization`, `select_one`, required): Select your current work authorization status from the provided options.
- **Transportation Method** (`transportation`, `select_one`, required): Select how you plan to get to work from the provided options.
- **Housing Assistance Needed** (`housing_needed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you need housing assistance.
- **Relevant Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, `text`, optional): Describe any health conditions the farm should be aware of for safety purposes.
- **PPE Size** (`ppe_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the size of your personal protective equipment from the provided options.
- **Safety Training Completed** (`training_completed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have completed farm safety training within the last year.
- **Drug Screen Consent** (`drug_screen_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent for drug screening.
- **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent for a background check.
- **Worker Signature** (`worker_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your digital signature for verification.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your digital signature.
- **Assigned Supervisor** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your assigned supervisor.
- **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your current onboarding status from the provided options.

Please note that all required fields must be filled out for a complete submission. Ensure you review your form carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
