# Health and Safety Officer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health and Safety Officer Application Form is used to collect information from applicants for the Health and Safety Officer position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone in the respective fields.
2.  Select your Job Title and Level of Experience to help us understand your work background and authority.
3.  Provide your Work Experience to describe your relevant work history.
4.  Enter your Education details, including any relevant certifications or diplomas.
5.  List any Certifications and Licenses you hold, and upload relevant documents if required.
6.  For Emergency Contact details, provide the name, relationship, phone number, and email of a contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
7.  If you have a secondary emergency contact, provide their details as well.
8.  Upload your Resume and any required certifications or licenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your work email or any other contact email.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number or any other contact phone number.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Job Title**: Please select your current or last job title you have held.
* **Job Level**: Select your level of experience and authority.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience in the space provided.
* **Education**: List any relevant education, diplomas, or certifications.
* **Certifications**: List any certifications or licenses relevant to the Health and Safety Officer position.
* **Work Authority**: Describe your level of authority and responsibilities in your previous roles.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the secondary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Upload Resume**: Upload your Resume in a file format (.pdf, .docx, .rtf, etc.).
* **Upload License**: Upload your License in a file format (.pdf, .docx, .rtf, etc.).
* **Upload Certifications**: Upload your Certifications in a file format (.pdf, .docx, .rtf, etc.).
* **Upload Other Certifications**: Upload other certifications not listed in the previous field.
* **Upload Other Certifications**: This field is used for any additional certifications not listed above.
