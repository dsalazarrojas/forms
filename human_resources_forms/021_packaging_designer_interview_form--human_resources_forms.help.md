# Packaging Designer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packaging Designer Interview Form is designed to collect information about an applicant's personal details, contact information, availability to work, expected salary, and previous work experience. This form helps us to accurately assess the applicant's qualifications and preferences for the Packaging Designer position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer all the fields, but you can leave blank any field that is not relevant to you.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* If a field is not applicable to you, please provide an explanation or a brief comment to clarify your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): 
Please provide your name and any other relevant details that you feel are necessary for us to know.

### 2. **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): 
Enter your current email address and phone number where we can contact you.

### 3. **Availability to Work** (`available_to_work`, number, optional): 
Please select a number that best represents your availability to work, where 1-2 years is 1-2 years of work experience and so on.

### 4. **Salary Expectation** (`salary_expectation`, number, optional): 
Please select your expected salary range.

### 5. **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, optional): 
Please select all applicable years of work experience from the options provided.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about a specific field, please do not hesitate to reach out for clarification.
* Remember to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and efficient process.
