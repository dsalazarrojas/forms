# hr_policy_compliance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Compliance Declaration Form is designed to gather information from employees about their understanding and agreement with company HR policies. This form is essential for maintaining compliance and should be filled out by all employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

- Fill in the required information in each field accurately and thoroughly.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **I Understand** (`i_understand`, text, optional): This field is a text box for you to type in your understanding of the HR policies. Please provide a brief statement of your understanding of the policies.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, number, optional): This field is for you to enter your policy number, if applicable. If not, please leave it blank.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you sign this declaration.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your employee email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your employee phone number.
* **I Agree** (`i_agree`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you understand and agree with the HR policies, 'False' if you do not, or 'maybe' if you're unsure.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please type them in this field.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.
- Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
