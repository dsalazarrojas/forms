# Verbal Warning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Verbal Warning Form is a tool used to document and formalize verbal warnings given to employees. It helps ensure that both the manager and the employee are on the same page regarding the incident, and it provides a clear record of what happened.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form immediately after the verbal warning is given to the employee.
2. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date of Incident" field.
5. Describe the incident in detail in the "Incident Description" field.
6. Briefly describe the actions taken as a result of the incident in the "Action Taken" field.
7. Provide a space for the employee to respond or comment on the incident in the "Employee Response" field.
8. Have the manager sign the form in the "Manager Signature" field to indicate that the verbal warning was given.
9. Have the employee sign the form in the "Employee Signature" field to acknowledge that the verbal warning was received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who received the verbal warning.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee who received the verbal warning.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Briefly describe what happened during the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Action Taken**: Describe any actions you took as a result of the incident.
* **Employee Response**: Provide space for the employee to comment or respond to the incident.
* **Manager Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that the verbal warning was given.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign the form to acknowledge that the verbal warning was received.

## Tips
* Always keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
* Ensure that the form is completed in a timely manner after the incident occurs.
* Review the form with the employee to ensure it accurately reflects the incident and any actions taken.
