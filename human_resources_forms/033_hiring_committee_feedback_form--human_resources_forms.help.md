# hiring_committee_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hiring_committee_feedback_form is used to collect feedback from hiring committees about candidates. It helps committees provide subjective evaluations and comments about a candidate's background, skills, and performance during an interview or evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form for each candidate**: Complete one form per candidate.
2. **Enter contact details**: Provide your name and contact information.
3. **Enter candidate data**: Enter the candidate's name and any relevant details.
4. **Evaluate the candidate**: Answer the Evaluation Scale questions.
5. **Score the candidate**: Provide scores 1-4 for the candidate's performance.
6. **Enter evaluation results**: Answer Yes or No for each Evaluation question.
7. **Add comments**: Provide any additional comments about the candidate.
8. **Add recommendations**: Provide any recommendations for the candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (Text, optional)**: Enter your name and contact information so that the candidate can reach out to you for more information.
* **Candidate Data (Text, optional)**: Enter the candidate's name and any relevant details, such as their position or department they applied for.
* **Evaluation Scale (Number, optional)**: This scale helps us evaluate the candidate's performance based on their background, skills, and performance during an interview or evaluation process.
* **Score 1 (Number, optional)**: Provide a score for the candidate's performance based on their background.
* **Score 2 (Number, optional)**: Provide a score for the candidate's performance based on their skills.
* **Score 3 (Number, optional)**: Provide a score for the candidate's performance based on their performance during the interview or evaluation process.
* **Score 4 (Number, optional)**: Provide a score for the candidate's overall performance.
* **Evaluation 1 (Select One, optional)**: Answer Yes or No to whether the candidate's background matches their job description.
* **Evaluation 2 (Select One, optional)**: Answer Yes or No to whether the candidate's skills match their job requirements.
* **Comments (Text, optional)**: Provide any additional comments about the candidate, such as their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Recommendations (Text, optional)**: Provide any recommendations for the candidate, such as next steps or areas for improvement.
