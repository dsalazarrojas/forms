# Leave Request Form (Spain) - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leave Request Form (Spain) is a form designed for employees to request leaves of absence for personal or business-related reasons. This form is used to collect information from employees about their leave requests, including start and end dates, purpose of the leave, and other relevant details. This guide will walk you through each field of the form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Leave Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information for each field. Fields with a red asterisk (\*) are required.
2. Enter your employee name in the `employee_name` field.
3. Select the start date of your leave in the `start_date` field.
4. Select the end date of your leave in the `end_date` field.
5. Enter the purpose of your leave in the `leave_purpose` field.
6. Choose the status of your leave from the options in the `status` field.
7. Select the department you belong to from the options in the `department` field.
8. Choose your team leaders from the options in the `team_leader` field.
9. Enter any additional comments about your leave in the `comments` field.
10. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
11. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
12. Select the tool assigned to you for this form from the options in the `assigned_tool` field.
13. Choose the person who created this form from the options in the `created_by` field.
14. Choose the person who updated this form from the options in the `updated_by` field.
15. Select the date you submitted this form in the `submitted_date` field (this will be automatically populated).
16. Enter the number of comments on this form in the `comments_count` field (this will be automatically populated).
17. Enter the ID of this form in the `form_id` field (this will be automatically populated).
17. Enter the name of this form in the `form_name` field (this will be automatically populated).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **employee_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your employee name.
*   **start_date** (Date, Optional): Select the start date of your leave.
*   **end_date** (Date, Optional): Select the end date of your leave.
*   **leave_purpose** (Text, Optional): Enter the purpose of your leave.
*   **status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the status of your leave (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
*   **department** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the department you belong to.
*   **team_leader** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your team leaders.
*   **comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments about your leave.
*   **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
*   **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **assigned_tool** (Select One, Optional): Select the tool assigned to you for this form (Jotform, Google Forms, or Microsoft Forms).
*   **created_by** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the person who created this form.
*   **updated_by** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the person who updated this form.
*   **submitted_date** (Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated and should not be changed.
*   **comments_count** (Number, Optional): This field is automatically populated and should not be changed.
*   **form_id** (Text, Optional): This field is automatically populated and should not be changed.
*   **form_name** (Text, Optional): This field is automatically populated and should not be changed.
