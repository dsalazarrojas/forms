# Soft Skills Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soft Skills Evaluation Form is designed to assess an employee's skills in areas such as communication, teamwork, time management, and leadership.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please review the employee's job description and requirements to understand their specific role and responsibilities.
2.  Go through each of the sections in the form carefully and answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
3.  Provide clear and concise answers to the questions, avoiding any unnecessary comments or details.
4.  Ensure that you have considered all the necessary skills to be evaluated and have provided feedback accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Soft Skills Evaluation Form** (`soft_skills_evaluation_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main heading of the form, providing a brief summary of the evaluation.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide information about the employee's job title, department, or other relevant details.
* **Skills Assessment** (`skills_assessment`, text, required/optional): Provide a general evaluation of the employee's overall performance in areas such as communication, teamwork, and leadership.
* **Skills to Evaluate** (`skills_to_evaluate`, select_multiple, required/optional): List the specific skills that are to be evaluated for this employee. Please select all relevant skills from the list.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the scale of evaluation for each skill, where 1 indicates "Not Evaluated" and 5 indicates "Excellent".
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, required/optional): Enter a score for each skill, based on the evaluation scale chosen. Please ensure that the score is accurate and fair.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for the employee's improvement.
* **Employee Rating** (`employee_rating`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the overall rating of the employee's performance, where Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor are the available options.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions from the manager's perspective.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required/optional): Please have the employee sign this form to acknowledge receipt of the evaluation.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required/optional): Please have the manager sign this form to confirm the evaluation.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the employee and manager signed the evaluation form.

## Tips

* Please keep the evaluation process fair, consistent, and objective.
* Ensure that the evaluation is based on the employee's actual performance and not on assumptions or biases.
* Provide feedback that is specific, actionable, and respectful.
* Review and discuss the evaluation with the employee to clarify any concerns or questions.
* Store the evaluation form in a secure location for future reference.
