# Unadvertised Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for internal job applications for unadvertised positions within the company.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the advertised role(s) that you are applying for (if applicable).
2. Provide your contact details (name, email, phone number).
3. Share any relevant application details (e.g., why you are a good fit for the role).
4. List any relevant skills and certifications that you have.
5. Add any additional information you would like to share with the hiring manager.
6. If you are the person who found the unadvertised job opening, please indicate if this role is unadvertised or not.
7. Share any job references (if applicable).
8. Provide the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Advertised Role** (`advertised_role`, select_multiple, required: false): 
Please select the role(s) that you are applying for from the list of advertised roles provided. If no roles are selected, please indicate that you are applying for a different role.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): 
Please provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Application Details** (`application_details`, text, required: false): 
Share any relevant information about your application, such as why you are a good fit for the role.
* **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, text, required: false): 
If applicable, share any specific preferences or requirements you have for the job (e.g., work schedule, location).
* **Skills and Certifications** (`skills_and_certifications`, text, required: false): 
List any relevant skills or certifications you have that align with the role requirements.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): 
Add any additional information you would like to share with the hiring manager.
* **Unadvertised Role** (`unadvertised_role`, select_one, required: false): 
If you found the job opening, please indicate if it was unadvertised or not.
* **Submitter Info** (`submitter_info`, text, required: false): 
Please provide your name and role within the company (e.g., Team Lead, Software Engineer).
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: false): 
Please provide the date of submission.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required: false): 
Please provide the time of submission.
* **Job References** (`job_references`, text, required: false): 
Share any relevant job references or work experience.
## Tips

* Please ensure that you are submitting your application for an unadvertised job opening within the company.
* If you are not sure about a field, please ask your manager or HR representative for assistance.
* Please submit your application in a timely manner to ensure that it is reviewed and processed promptly.
