# exit_interview_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exit interview form is used to collect information from employees who are leaving the company to capture details about their job title, job description, reporting manager's response, department, job status, reason for leaving, and next move.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
2. Describe your job in the "Job Description" field.
3. Provide your Reporting Manager's name and response in the "Reporting Manager" and "Reporting Manager Response" fields, respectively.
4. Select your Department from the dropdown list.
5. Choose your Job Status from the dropdown list.
6. Indicate whether you have a Next Move date selected (if applicable).
7. Select all that apply from the "Reason For Leave" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your job responsibilities and role in the company.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your Reporting Manager's name.
* **Reporting Manager Response** (`reporting_manager_response`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your Reporting Manager's response, if any.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your department from the dropdown list.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your job status from the list (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Intern).
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply from the list of reasons for leaving (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Next Move** (`next_move`, `date`, required/optional): If you have a next move selected, enter the date.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your Reporting Manager or HR representative.
* The data collected from this form will help in understanding the reasons for employee departures and improve the company's policies and procedures.
