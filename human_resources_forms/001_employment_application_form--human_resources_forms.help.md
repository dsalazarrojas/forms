# Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment application form is used to collect information from job seekers who are interested in applying for a job opening at the organization. This form is designed to help us understand your qualifications, experience, and availability for potential employment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2. Describe your work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
3. Indicate your education background in the "Education" field.
4. Select your job type in the "Job Type" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Upload your resume in the "Resume" field.
7. Enter your desired salary range in the "Salary" field.
8. Select your available start date in the "Available Start Date" field.
9. Indicate your current work status in the "Work Status" field.
10. Select your job status in the "Job Status" field.
11. Describe your work experience in the "Work Experience 2" field (if applicable).
12. Indicate your education background in the "Education 2" field (if applicable).
13. Enter your available end date in the "Available End Date 2" field (if applicable).
14. Select your job status in the "Job Status 2" field (if applicable).
15. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info 2" field (if applicable).
16. Describe your work history in the "Work History" field (if applicable).
17. Enter your available hours in the "Available Hours" field (if applicable).
18. Enter your job title in the "Job Title 2" field (if applicable).
19. Describe your education background in the "Education 3" field (if applicable).
20. Enter your available days in the "Available Days" field (if applicable).
21. Enter your job title in the "Job Title 3" field (if applicable).
22. Select your work status in the "Work Status 2" field (if applicable).
23. Select your job status in the "Job Status 3" field (if applicable).
24. Enter your available end date in the "Available End Date 2" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can enter your name, email, and phone number for us to contact you.

### Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, industries, and relevant skills.

### Education
* **Education** (`education`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate your educational background by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option. 

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of job you are applying for.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your current or desired job title.

### Resume
* **Resume** (`resume`, `note`, optional): Upload your resume to support your application.

### Salary
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, optional): Enter your desired salary range in dollars.

### Available Start Date
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, `date`, optional): Select your available start date.

### Work Status
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your current work status.

### Job Status
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your job status.

### Work Experience 2
* **Work Experience 2** (`work_experience_2`, `text`, optional): Describe your additional work experience.

### Education 2
* **Education 2** (`education_2`, `text`, optional): Indicate your additional education background.

### Available End Date
* **Available End Date** (`available_end_date`, `date`, optional): Select your available end date.

### Job Status 2
* **Job Status 2** (`job_status_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select your job status.

### Contact Info 2
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your additional contact information.

### Work History
* **Work History** (`work_history`, `text`, optional): Describe your work history.

### Available Hours
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter your available hours.

### Job Title 2
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your additional job title.

### Education 3
* **Education 3** (`education_3`, `text`, optional): Indicate your additional education background.

### Available Days
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, `number`, optional): Enter your available days.

### Job Title 3
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, `text`, optional): Enter your additional job title.

### Work Status 2
* **Work Status 2** (`work_status_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select your work status.

### Job Status 3
* **Job Status 3** (`job_status_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select your job status.

### Available End Date 2
* **Available End Date 2** (`available_end_date_2`, `date`, optional): Select your available end date.
