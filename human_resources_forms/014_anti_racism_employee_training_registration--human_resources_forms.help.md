# Anti Racism Employee Training Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees to register for anti-racism employee training. The purpose of this training is to promote diversity, equity, and inclusion in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure you have all required information before proceeding.
2. Complete each section of the form thoroughly, making sure to select all relevant options and provide accurate information.
3. Submit the form once you have finished filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Full Name (1)** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your employee ID or HR records.
* **Employee ID Number (2)** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter your unique employee identification number.
* **Work Email Address (3)** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number for any updates or follow-ups.
* **Department (5)** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your current department or division within the company.
* **Job Title (6)** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Manager or Supervisor Name (7)** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Work Location (8)** (`location`, `select_one`, required): Choose where you work, either at the headquarters, a regional office, remotely, or a hybrid model.
* **Employment Type (9)** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment type, whether full-time, part-time, contract, temporary, or internship.
* **Hire Date (10)** (`hire_date`, `date`, required): Enter your start date of employment.
* **Have you completed DEI training before? (11)** (`previous_training`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have completed diversity, equity, and inclusion training before, within the past year, or never.
* **What motivates you to attend? (12)** (`training_motivation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all reasons why you want to attend the training, such as it being required by your employer, personal growth, or improving workplace culture.
* **Preferred Session Format (13)** (`session_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer to attend the training, whether in-person, virtual live, self-paced online, or hybrid.
* **Preferred Training Date (14)** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Select a preferred date for the training.
* **Preferred Time (15)** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required): Choose a preferred time for the training, either morning, afternoon, evening, or flexible.
* **Accessibility Needs (16)** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any necessary accommodations for the training, such as wheelchair access, sign language interpreter, or large print materials.
* **Accommodation Details (17)** (`accommodation_details`, `text`, optional): If you selected any accommodations, please provide more details.
* **Dietary Restrictions (18)** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have dietary restrictions, select them for in-person sessions with catering.
* **Comfort Level with Group Discussions (19)** (`discussion_comfort`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how comfortable you are sharing in group discussions, from very comfortable to preferring not to share.
* **Specific Topics of Interest (20)** (`specific_topics`, `select_multiple`, required): Select topics related to anti-racism and inclusion that you would like to focus on.
* **Concerns or Questions Before Training (21)** (`concerns_or_questions`, `text`, optional): If you have any concerns or questions, please provide them.
* **Manager Approval Status (22)** (`manager_approval`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if your manager has approved your training registration.
* **Time Commitment Acknowledged (23)** (`time_commitment_acknowledged`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the time commitment required for the training.
* **Confidentiality Agreement (24)** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you agree to keep discussions confidential.
* **Registration Date (25)** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): The date your registration is submitted.

## Tips
* Review and double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any concerns or questions, reach out to your HR department or training administrator.
