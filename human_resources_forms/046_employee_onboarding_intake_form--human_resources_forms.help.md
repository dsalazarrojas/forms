# Employee Onboarding Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from new employees, ensuring a smooth and efficient onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Select your Hire Date from the date picker calendar.
4. Choose your Job Title from the dropdown menu.
5. Select your Department from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter your Job Code, Supervisor ID, and Reporting Manager ID numbers (if applicable).
7. Provide the Email and Phone numbers for your Reporting Manager and Direct Reporter (if applicable).
8. Enter the Name and Email of your Reporting Manager.
9. Enter the Name and Phone number of your Direct Reporter (if applicable).
10. Enter your Manager's Name, Phone number, and Email.
11. Select your Team Lead ID and enter their Name and Phone number (if applicable).
12. Enter the Email of your Team Lead (if applicable).
13. Select the Training Start Date for any scheduled training programs (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Select the date you were hired.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Choose your job title from the list provided.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select all departments you belong to.
* **Job Code** (`job_code`, number, optional): Enter your job code number (if applicable).
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, number, optional): Enter your supervisor's ID number (if applicable).
* **Reporting Manager ID** (`reporting_manager_id`, number, optional): Enter your reporting manager's ID number (if applicable).
* **Reporting Manager Name** (`reporting_manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's name.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`reporting_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your reporting manager's email address.
* **Reporting Manager Phone** (`reporting_manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's phone number.
* **Direct Reporter ID** (`direct_reporter_id`, number, optional): Enter your direct reporter's ID number (if applicable).
* **Direct Reporter Name** (`direct_reporter_name`, text, optional): Enter your direct reporter's name.
* **Direct Reporter Email** (`direct_reporter_email`, email, optional): Enter your direct reporter's email address.
* **Direct Reporter Phone** (`direct_reporter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your direct reporter's phone number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Enter your manager's title.
* **Team Lead ID** (`team_lead_id`, number, optional): Enter your team lead's ID number (if applicable).
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text, optional): Enter your team lead's name.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, optional): Enter your team lead's phone number.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, optional): Enter your team lead's email address.
* **Training Start Date** (`training_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date for any scheduled training programs (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your HR representative for guidance.
* This form will only be reviewed once submitted.
