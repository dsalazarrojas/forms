# Hiring Personality Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiring Personality Assessment Questionnaire is a tool used by hiring managers and recruiters to gather information about a candidate's personality and skills. The form is designed to provide an objective and structured way of assessing a candidate's strengths and weaknesses, helping to make more informed decisions during the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the candidate.
2. Answer the confidence level question to express how confident you are in your answers.
3. Answer questions about handling conflicts, task prioritization, overcoming challenges, communication skills, leadership style, and stress management.
4. Indicate who will be your contact person for this candidate.
5. Select the next steps for the candidate (interview, screening, hire, reject, or no action).
6. Add any additional notes about the candidate.
7. Upload any relevant attachments or documentation about the candidate.
8. Select the next steps for additional evaluation (interview, screening, hire, reject, or no action).
9. Upload any additional attachments or documentation about the candidate.
10. Upload the candidate's resume and cover letter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Candidate Information**: Please enter the candidate's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **2. Confidence Level**: Please indicate how confident you are in your answers on this form, on a scale of 1 (not confident) to 10 (very confident).
* **3. Handling Conflicts**: Describe the candidate's approach to handling conflicts or disagreements in a team environment.
* **4. Task Prioritization**: Describe the candidate's approach to prioritizing tasks and managing their workload.
* **5. Overcoming Challenges**: Describe the candidate's approach to overcoming obstacles or difficulties in their work.
* **6. Communication Skills**: Rate the candidate's communication skills on a scale of 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **7. Leadership Style**: Describe the candidate's leadership style and approach.
* **8. Team Project**: Describe the candidate's experience working on team projects.
* **9. Stress Management**: Describe the candidate's approach to managing stress and pressure.
* **10. Contact Person**: Select the person who will be your contact person for this candidate.
* **11. Email**: Enter the candidate's email address.
* **12. Next Steps**: Select the next steps for the candidate (interview, screening, hire, reject, or no action).
* **13. Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the candidate.
* **14. Attachments**: Upload any relevant attachments or documentation about the candidate.
* **15. Next Steps 2**: Select the next steps for additional evaluation (interview, screening, hire, reject, or no action).
* **16. Attachments 2**: Upload any additional attachments or documentation about the candidate.
* **17. Resume**: Upload the candidate's resume.
* **18. Cover Letter**: Upload the candidate's cover letter.

## Tips

* Please be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Use specific examples to illustrate your answers.
* Keep your answers concise and to the point.
* Use proper formatting and attachment upload procedures to ensure clear and easy access to the submitted materials.
