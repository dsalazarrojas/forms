# hr_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Complaint Form is a tool to document and address employee complaints or grievances within the organization. It ensures that all complaints are handled fairly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Describe the complaint or grievance you are experiencing in the "Complaint" field.
2. Specify the type of grievance you are experiencing from the options provided ("Harassment", "Discrimination", or "Other").
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Grievance Description" field.
4. Include any relevant details about the issue in the "Grievance Details" field.
5. Select the desired resolution for the complaint from the options provided ("Resolve internally", "Escalate to manager", or "Escalate to HR").
6. Provide your contact information in the "Employee Contact" field.
7. If applicable, provide your manager's contact information in the "Manager Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint (note)**: A detailed description of the complaint or grievance you are experiencing.
* **Grievance (text)**: A specific type of complaint, choose from "Harassment", "Discrimination", or "Other".
* **Grievance Type (select_one)**: A description of the complaint, choose from "Harassment", "Discrimination", or "Other".
* **Grievance Description (text)**: A detailed description of the issue, including as much information as possible.
* **Grievance Details (text)**: Additional details about the issue, such as relevant dates, times, or locations.
* **Desired Resolution (select_one)**: The preferred way to handle the complaint, choose from "Resolve internally", "Escalate to manager", or "Escalate to HR".
* **Employee Contact (text)**: Your name, email, and phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Manager Contact (text)**: The name, email, and phone number of your supervisor or manager for follow-up communication.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the complaint or grievance.
* Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact your HR representative for guidance.
