## Employee Verification Appointment Scheduler - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to assist HR or administrative staff in scheduling employee verification meetings.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Go to the designated section for Form Input and enter your employee ID or a brief description of your request.
* Enter your Employee Name in the designated field.
* In the Employee ID field, enter your employee ID or a brief description of your ID number.
* In the Meeting Time field, select the time slot that suits you best for the verification meeting.
* In the Meeting Date field, select the date for the verification meeting.
