## Employee One On One Meeting Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
### Tips

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting to complete the form.
2. If you are unsure about a particular field, please refer to this guide for further clarification.
3. Double-check that you have saved your progress before navigating away from the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee One On One Meeting Form**: This is the main field for entering the title of the meeting. You can enter a brief description or title of the meeting.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager who met with the employee.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who participated in the meeting.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting in 24-hour format.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Notes**: Enter notes from the meeting, including any important points discussed or agreed upon.
* **Performance Goals**: Enter any performance goals or objectives discussed during the meeting.
* **Development Goals**: Enter any development goals or objectives discussed during the meeting.
* **Employee Attendance**: Check the box if the employee attended the meeting.
* **Manager Attendance**: Check the box if the manager attended the meeting.
* **Manager Comment**: Enter any comments or feedback from the manager after the meeting.
* **Employee Comment**: Enter any comments or feedback from the employee after the meeting.
