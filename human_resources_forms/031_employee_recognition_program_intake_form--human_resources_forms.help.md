# Employee Recognition Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the Employee Recognition Program, which recognizes and rewards employees' outstanding contributions, achievements, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the employees you wish to nominate by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each employee.
2. Enter the Employee Name(s) of the nominated employee(s).
3. Choose the type of award you wish to recognize: Achievement Award, Merit Award, or Service Award.
4. Provide a brief description of the reason for recognition.
5. Enter the Employee Department of the nominated employee(s).
6. Select the date when the recognition will be given.
7. Enter your Manager's Name.
8. Enter your Manager's Title.
9. Enter the Employee's Title.
10. Enter the Employee's Email.
11. Enter your Manager's Email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Nominations**: Select one or multiple employees you wish to nominate for recognition.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee(s) being nominated.
* **Award Type**: Choose from the three available award types: Achievement Award, Merit Award, or Service Award. This selection will help determine the type of recognition that will be given.
* **Reason For Recognition**: Provide a brief description of the reason why the employee(s) is/are being recognized. This should include specific details about their outstanding contribution, achievement, or service.
* **Employee Department**: Enter the department of the nominated employee(s) to help with communication and coordination for the recognition process.
* **Recognition Date**: Select the date when the recognition will be given. This will help with scheduling and planning.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your name as the manager of the nominated employee(s).
* **Manager Title**: Enter your title as the manager of the nominated employee(s).
* **Employee Title**: Enter the employee's title as the nominated employee(s).
* **Employee Email**: Enter the email of the nominated employee(s) to ensure they are informed about the recognition.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your email as the manager of the nominated employee(s) to ensure you are informed about the recognition process and any updates.
