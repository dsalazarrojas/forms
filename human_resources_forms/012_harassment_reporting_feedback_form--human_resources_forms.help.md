# Harassment Reporting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Harassment Reporting Feedback Form is a tool for employees to report incidents of harassment that they have experienced or witnessed within the organization. This form collects necessary information to facilitate a thorough investigation and ensure that appropriate action is taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have already checked if a similar incident has been reported before.
2.  Fill in the form with accurate and detailed information.
3.  For fields with options, choose the correct answer that best describes the incident.
4.  Make sure to report only one incident at a time. If you've experienced multiple incidents, please fill out the form multiple times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Report Details (1)**: Briefly describe the incident, including any relevant details such as date, time, location, and the individuals involved.
*   **Incident Location (2)**: Select the location where the incident occurred, such as your workspace, office building, or a specific area.
*   **Reporting Officer (3)**: Check 'Yes' if the reporting officer knows who is handling your case or if you've assigned them to handle it. 'No' if you're unsure or if you want someone else to handle it.
*   **Incident Description (4)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant context or details.
*   **Reporting Method (5)**: Choose how you reported the incident, such as in-person, via email, or through this form.
*   **Reporting Date (6)**: Enter the date you reported the incident (or the date it occurred, if you're reporting retrospectively).
*   **Reporting Time (7)**: Enter the time you reported the incident (or the time it occurred, if you're reporting retrospectively).
*   **Reporting Status (8)**: Select 'Active' if the case is ongoing or 'Inactive' if it's been resolved or closed.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide.
- If you have trouble accessing the form or have questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
