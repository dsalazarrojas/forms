# Worker Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Compensation Claim Form is a document used to report and claim for compensation due to work-related injuries or accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before starting.
2. Provide your employee name.
3. Enter the date of the injury or accident.
4. Describe the injury or accident in detail.
5. Specify your current employee status and type.
6. Enter the hours worked at the time of the injury or accident (if applicable).
7. Provide your supervisor's name (if applicable).
8. Sign and date the Claimant Declaration section as a statement of fact.
9. Enter your employer's name and signature.
10. Declare the claim type.
11. Specify the location of the accident (if applicable).
12. Provide witness details (if applicable).
13. Sign the Claimant Signature section as proof of authenticity.
14. Sign the Employee Signature section as proof of submission.
15. Sign the Supervisor Signature section as proof of receipt.
16. Sign the Employer Signature section as proof of acknowledgement.
17. Date the Claimant Report Date.
17. Sign the Witness Signature section as proof of witness declaration (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel record.
* **Date Of Injury** (`date_of_injury`, date, optional): Enter the date of the injury or accident.
* **Description Of Injury** (`description_of_injury`, text, optional): Describe the injury or accident in as much detail as possible.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, optional): Specify your current employee status: Active or Inactive.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, select_one, optional): Specify your type of employment: Yes or No.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the hours worked at the time of the injury or accident (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's name (if applicable).
* **Claimant Declaration** (`claimant_declaration`, note, optional): This section is for you to sign and date as a statement of fact.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter your employer's name.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, optional): Declare the claim type: Yes or No.
* **Accident Location** (`accident_location`, text, optional): Specify the location of the accident (if applicable).
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, optional): Provide witness details (if applicable).
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date this section as proof of authenticity.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): Sign this section as proof of submission.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, optional): Sign this section as proof of receipt.
* **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`, text, optional): Sign this section as proof of acknowledgement.
* **Claimant Report Date** (`claimant_report_date`, date, optional): Date the Claimant Report Date.

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID or personnel record number.
* **Claimant Contact** (`claimant_contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up.
