# Achievements And Warnings Record - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Achievements And Warnings Record" form is essential for HR departments, managers, or supervisors to document employee milestones, incidents, and performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Provide Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears in the HR system.
2.  **Enter Employee ID**: Enter the employee's unique identifier or personnel number.
3.  **Select Record Type**: Choose the nature of this entry (Achievement, Performance Warning, Behavioral Warning, or Policy Violation).
4.  **Date of Incident or Achievement**: Enter the date of the incident or achievement (e.g., date of sales milestone or warning issuance).
5.  **Title of Record**: Enter a brief title or description of the record (e.g., "Sales Milestone Q3" or "Attendance Warning").
6.  **Detailed Description**: Provide specific facts and context about the incident or achievement.
7.  **Severity Level**: Choose the severity level of the incident or achievement (Low, Moderate, High, or Critical).
8.  **Witnesses Involved**: Select all individuals who witnessed the incident or were involved (e.g., Manager, HR Representative, Peer, or External Party).
9.  **Action Taken or Reward Given**: Describe any actions taken or rewards given to the employee (e.g., written warning or bonus).
10. **Follow-up Date Required**: If necessary, enter the date when follow-up actions or reviews are required.
11. **Manager Signature**: Enter your name to sign the form.
12. **Employee Acknowledgment Signature**: Enter the employee's name to confirm receipt of the record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears in the HR system.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter the employee's unique identifier or personnel number.
*   **Record Type** (`record_type`, select\_one, required): Select the nature of this entry (Achievement, Performance Warning, Behavioral Warning, or Policy Violation).
*   **Date of Incident or Achievement** (`date_of_incident_or_achievement`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident or achievement (e.g., date of sales milestone or warning issuance).
*   **Title of Record** (`title_of_record`, text, required): Enter a brief title or description of the record (e.g., "Sales Milestone Q3" or "Attendance Warning").
*   **Detailed Description** (`detailed_description`, text, required): Provide specific facts and context about the incident or achievement.
*   **Severity Level** (`severity_level`, select\_one, required): Choose the severity level of the incident or achievement (Low, Moderate, High, or Critical).
*   **Witnesses Involved** (`witnesses_involved`, select\_multiple, optional): Select all individuals who witnessed the incident or were involved (e.g., Manager, HR Representative, Peer, or External Party).
*   **Action Taken or Reward Given** (`action_taken_or_reward_given`, text, required): Describe any actions taken or rewards given to the employee (e.g., written warning or bonus).
*   **Follow-up Date Required** (`follow_up_date_required`, date, optional): If necessary, enter the date when follow-up actions or reviews are required.
*   **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): Enter your name to sign the form.
*   **Employee Acknowledgment Signature** (`employee_acknowledgment_signature`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name to confirm receipt of the record.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and concise when filling out each field.
*   Use clear and descriptive language for the Title of Record and Detailed Description.
*   Select all relevant witnesses involved in the incident or achievement.
*   If necessary, set a follow-up date for further actions or reviews.
*   Obtain the employee's signature for all required and optional fields.
*   Review and validate the completed form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
