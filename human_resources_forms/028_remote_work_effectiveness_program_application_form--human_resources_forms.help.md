# Remote Work Effectiveness Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant details from employees who wish to participate in the Remote Work Effectiveness Program. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately to help the program's administrators assess your eligibility and provide a seamless experience for you and your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
2.  Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant employee details as requested.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current work status from the options provided (On Site, Off Site).
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of work you perform (Full Time, Part Time, Freelance).
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you work or perform your job duties.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Manager's Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your current job responsibilities.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Ensure accurate and up-to-date information is entered to avoid any potential issues or delays in the evaluation process.
