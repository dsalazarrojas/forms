# Performance Review Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Review Reimbursement Claim Form is used by employees to submit reimbursement claims related to their performance review expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Employee Name to identify yourself.
2. Enter the reimbursement_date of when you incurred the expense.
3. Select the expenses you incurred during the performance review period using the "Expense Type" field.
4. Enter the Expense Amount of the expenses you selected.
5. Provide a detailed description of the expenses in the "Expense Details" field.
6. Enter the Submission Date when you are submitting the claim.
7. Enter your Manager Name who reviewed your performance.
8. Select the Status of your claim as Active or Inactive.
9. Add any additional Comments about your claim.
10. Enter the name of the person who Approved your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **reimbursement_date** (2): Enter the date when you incurred the expense.
* **Expense Type** (3): Select all the types of expenses that you have incurred during the performance review period. (Note: "Yes" for expenses that were incurred and "No" for those that were not.)
* **Expense Amount** (4): Enter the amount of the expenses you incurred.
* **Expense Details** (5): Enter a detailed description of each expense, including receipts and supporting documents.
* **Submission Date** (6): Enter the date when you are submitting this claim.
* **Manager Name** (7): Enter the name of your manager who reviewed your performance.
* **Status** (8): Select the status of your claim as Active (being reviewed) or Inactive (not being reviewed).
* **Comments** (9): Add any additional comments about your claim that may be helpful for the reviewer.
* **Approved By** (10): Enter the name of the person who approved your claim.
