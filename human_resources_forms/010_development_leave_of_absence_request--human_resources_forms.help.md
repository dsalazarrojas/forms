# Development Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Development Leave of Absence Request form is used by employees to submit requests for leave of absence from work due to various reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Start Date** field with the date from which you will be absent.
2. Fill in the **End Date** field with the date until which you will be absent.
3. Select the reason for your absence from the **Reason For Absence** dropdown menu.
   - **Vacation**: For leave due to vacation or personal time off.
   - **Sick**: For leave due to illness or medical reasons.
   - **Bereavement**: For leave due to the passing of a family member or close friend.
   - **Family Event**: For leave due to family events or emergencies.
   - **Other**: For any other reason not listed above.
4. Enter the number of **Hours Absent** you will be absent in a 24-hour format.
5. (Optional) If you have a specific **Start Time** and **End Time**, enter them in their respective fields.
6. Select the type of **Leave Type** you are requesting:
   - **Vacation**: For planned vacation or personal time off.
   - **Bereavement**: For leave due to the passing of a family member or close friend.
   - **Other**: For any other reason not listed above.
7. Select up to three team members who will cover your responsibilities during your absence:
   - **John**
   - **Jane**
   - **Alice**
8. Select the **Approval Status** of your request:
   - **Approved**: Your leave request has been approved.
   - **Denied**: Your leave request has been denied.
   - **In Progress**: Your leave request is still being reviewed.
9. (Optional) Enter any **Comments** regarding your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date**: Enter the date from which you will be absent.
* **End Date**: Enter the date until which you will be absent.
* **Reason For Absence**: Select the reason for your absence.
* **Hours Absent**: Enter the number of hours you will be absent in a 24-hour format.
* **Start Time**: (Optional) Enter your start time if you will be absent at a specific time.
* **End Time**: (Optional) Enter your end time if you will be absent at a specific time.
* **Leave Type**: Select the type of leave you are requesting.
* **Coverage**: Select up to three team members who will cover your responsibilities during your absence.
* **Approval Status**: Select the current status of your leave request.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments regarding your leave request.
