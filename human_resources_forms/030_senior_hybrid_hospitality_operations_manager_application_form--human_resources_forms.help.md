# Senior Hybrid Hospitality Operations Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Hybrid Hospitality Operations Manager Application Form is used to collect information from applicants for a senior hybrid hospitality operations manager position. This form helps evaluate applicants' eligibility for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as First name, Last name, and Email.
2. Provide your work experience, Previous manager, Education, and Previous employer.
3. Specify your Job title, Work hours, and Date of birth.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Provide information about your Previous position, Manager name, Job description, and Previous work location.
6. Input your Work experience start date and end date.
7. Mention your Notice period.
8. Enter your Previous salary.
9. Input your Notice period start and end dates.
10. Sign with your Manager signature, date, and Employee signature.
11. Date and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional):
	+ Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Work experience** (`work_experience`, Text, Optional):
	+ Briefly describe your work experience in the hospitality industry.
* **Previous manager** (`previous_manager`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the name of your previous manager, if any.
* **Education** (`education`, Text, Optional):
	+ Mention your educational background relevant to the position.
* **Previous employer** (`previous_employer`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the name of your previous employer, if any.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your current or previous job title in the hospitality industry.
* **Work hours** (`work_hours`, Text, Optional):
	+ Specify your typical work hours or shifts.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter a valid phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Previous position** (`previous_position`, Text, Optional):
	+ Briefly describe your previous position or role.
* **Manager name** (`manager_name`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the name of your current or previous manager, if any.
* **Job description** (`job_description`, Text, Optional):
	+ Describe your job responsibilities and duties in the hospitality industry.
* **Previous work location** (`previous_work_location`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the location of your previous work, if any.
* **Work experience start date** (`work_experience_start_date`, Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the start date of your work experience.
* **Work experience end date** (`work_experience_end_date`, Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the end date of your work experience, if any.
* **Notice period** (`notice_period`, Text, Optional):
	+ Specify your notice period for resignation or termination.
* **Previous salary** (`previous_salary`, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your previous salary or wage, if any.
* **Notice period start date** (`notice_period_start_date`, Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the start date of your notice period.
* **Notice period end date** (`notice_period_end_date`, Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the end date of your notice period.
* **Manager signature** (`manager_signature`, Text, Optional):
	+ Sign with the manager's name and date.
* **Manager date** (`manager_date`, Date, Optional):
	+ Date and sign the form with the manager's name.
* **Employee signature** (`employee_signature`, Text, Optional):
	+ Sign with the employee's name.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional):
	+ Date and sign the form with the employee's name.
