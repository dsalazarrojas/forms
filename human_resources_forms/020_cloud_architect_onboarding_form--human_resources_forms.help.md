# Cloud Architect Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from new employees to facilitate their onboarding process. It's crucial for setting up access, training, and other necessary processes for a smooth transition into your new role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Fill out the form accurately and consistently.
3. Ensure that you complete all required fields.
4. Refer to the field explanations below for specific guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Your assigned employee ID number.
* **Work Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Your work email address.
* **Work Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Your work phone number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): The first day of your employment.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Your job title as assigned by the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): The department you belong to.
* **Direct Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): The name of your direct manager.
* **Office Location** (`office_location`, `text`, required): Your primary office location.
* **Laptop Configuration Required?** (`laptop_required`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you require a laptop, 'No' otherwise.
* **Preferred Operating System** (`operating_system`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred operating system.
* **Cloud Platforms Requiring Access** (`software_access`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all cloud platforms you need access to.
* **VPN Access Required?** (`vpn_required`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you require VPN access, 'No' otherwise.
* **Training Needed for Cloud Platforms** (`onboarding_training`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all trainings needed for cloud platforms.
* **Certification Goals for First Year** (`certification_goals`, `text`, required): Enter your certification goals for the first year.
* **Documentation Items Received** (`documentation_received`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all documentation items you've received (e.g. employee handbook, IT policies, etc.).
* **Background Check Cleared?** (`background_check`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if your background check is cleared, 'No' otherwise.
* **NDA and Confidentiality Agreement Signed?** (`nda_signed`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you've signed the agreement, 'No' otherwise.

This guide is here to help ensure that you fill out the form correctly and consistently, providing the necessary information for your onboarding process. Please take the time to review each field carefully and enter accurate information.
