# Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from potential drivers, which will be used to assess your eligibility for the role. Please ensure you fill it out accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First name and Last name in the first and second sections.
2. Enter a valid Email address in the third section.
3. Enter your Phone number in the fourth section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as you'd like to be referred to.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as you'd like to be referred to.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address associated with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure you're providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for correctness.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact us to clarify.
