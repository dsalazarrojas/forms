# salon_application_of_employment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for salon employees to submit their application for employment. Please fill out this form to let us know about your contact details, job preferences, and other relevant work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure that all required and optional fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
2. **Provide clear and concise answers**: Answer each question with clear and concise information, avoiding any unnecessary details.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the provided job options that best fit your desired position within the salon.
	+ Options: Manager, Hairdresser, Hair stylist, Receptionist, Barber, Cosmetologist, Hair designer, Hair model
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, optional):
	+ Indicate the number of years of relevant experience in the salon industry.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional):
	+ Provide a date when you can start working, or any other date that is relevant to your availability.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate your current employment status, which may be Employed, Self employed, Unemployed, Retired, Student, or Other.
* **Education** (`education`, note, optional):
	+ Provide any relevant information about your education.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, note, optional):
	+ Provide any relevant information about your work experience in the salon industry.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional):
	+ List any relevant skills you have that are beneficial for the position.
* **References** (`references`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate if you have any professional references. Select 'Yes' if you do.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, optional):
	+ Upload any relevant documents that you'd like to share with the hiring team.

Note: This form is for informational purposes and is not a binding contract or employment offer.
