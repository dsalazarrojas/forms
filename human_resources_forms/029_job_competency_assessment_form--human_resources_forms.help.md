# job_competency_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job competency assessment form is used to evaluate and record the performance and competencies of an employee in their job role. It helps supervisors and HR to assess and monitor performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the job position being assessed.
2. Provide a brief description of the job responsibilities.
3. List any key observations or concerns related to the job performance.
4. Select the rating scale for the employee's overall performance.
5. Indicate the areas where the employee has demonstrated competence (Yes/No).
6. If desired, add any additional comments or notes.
7. Enter your email and phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the job position being assessed.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Describe the key responsibilities and duties of the job position.
* **Job Observations** (`job_observations`, text, optional): Note any observations or concerns related to the job performance (e.g., areas for improvement).
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, text, required): List the key responsibilities and duties of the job position.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, required): Select a rating scale that best describes the employee's overall performance (Good or Fair).
* **Performance Level** (`performance_level`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value to indicate the employee's performance level. Please note that this field is not required but can provide additional feedback.
* **Competency Areas** (`competency_areas`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which areas where the employee has demonstrated competence (Yes/No).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the employee's performance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
