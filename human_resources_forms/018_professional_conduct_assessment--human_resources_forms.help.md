# Professional Conduct Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Professional Conduct Assessment form is used to document and evaluate employee behavior in the workplace. This form helps identify and address any misconduct or incidents, ensuring that employees understand their role in maintaining a professional and respectful work environment.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and answer each question accurately and truthfully.
2.  Ensure you have all necessary information before completing the form.
3.  Enter your information in the required fields.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department where the employee works.
* **Behavior Date** (`behavior_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the behavior occurred.
* **Behavior Time** (`behavior_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the behavior occurred.
* **Behavior Location** (`behavior_location`, text, optional): Describe the location where the behavior occurred.
* **Behavior Details** (`behavior_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the behavior.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Witnesses** (`witnesses`, text, optional): List any witnesses who observed the behavior or incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): Describe any action taken as a result of the behavior or incident.
* **Behavior Outcome** (`behavior_outcome`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Positive**: The behavior was acceptable and met expectations.
	+ **Negative**: The behavior was unacceptable and did not meet expectations.
	+ **Neutral**: The behavior was neutral or neither positive nor negative.
* **Behavior Outcome Details** (`behavior_outcome_details`, text, optional): Provide further details on the behavior outcome.
* **Recommended Action** (`recommended_action`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Counseling**
	+ **Training**
	+ **Verbal Warning**
	+ **Written Warning**
	+ **Termination**
* **Recommended Action Details** (`recommended_action_details`, text, optional): Provide further details on the recommended action.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Manager**
	+ **HR**
	+ **Supervisor**
	+ **Team Lead**
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`, select_one, optional):
	+ **HR**
	+ **Finance**
	+ **Marketing**
* **Reviewer Location** (`reviewer_location`, text, optional): Describe the location of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`reviewer_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's signature.
* **Reviewer Date** (`reviewer_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Reviewer Time** (`reviewer_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the review was completed.
