# Store Manager Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about store managers and their performance. It's intended to be used in human resources management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*).
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Manager Interview Form Page 1**: This page is for you to provide general store information.
	+ **Store Name** (id: 1): Enter the name of your store.
	+ **Store Address** (id: 2): Enter your store's physical address.
	+ **Store Phone Number** (id: 3): Enter your store's phone number.
* **Store Manager Interview Form Page 2**: This page is for you to provide information about your team.
	+ **Store Manager Name** (id: 4): Enter your name as the store manager.
	+ **Years of Experience** (id: 5): Enter the number of years you've been working as a store manager.
	+ **Team Size** (id: 6): Enter the number of employees in your store.
* **Store Manager Interview Form Page 3**: This page is for you to evaluate your store's performance.
	+ **Store Performance** (id: 7): Enter a brief description of your store's current performance.
	+ **Sales Goal Achievement** (id: 8): Enter the percentage of your sales goal achieved.
* **Store Manager Interview Form Page 4**: This page is for you to provide financial information.
	+ **Average Sale Price** (id: 9): Enter the average sale price of items sold at your store.
	+ **Total Sales** (id: 10): Enter your store's total sales.
* **Store Manager Interview Form Page 5**: This page is for you to evaluate customer satisfaction.
	+ **Customer Satisfaction** (id: 11): Enter a brief description of customer satisfaction at your store.
	+ **Customer Complaints** (id: 12): Enter the number of customer complaints.
* **Store Manager Interview Form Page 6**: This page is for you to evaluate employee satisfaction.
	+ **Employee Satisfaction** (id: 13): Enter a brief description of employee satisfaction at your store.
	+ **Employee Turnover Rate** (id: 14): Enter the number of employees who have left the store.

Remember, this form is a guide for you to provide accurate and detailed information about your store. Make sure to answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
