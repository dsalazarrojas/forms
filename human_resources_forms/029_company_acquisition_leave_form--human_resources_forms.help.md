# Company Acquisition Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from employees who are leaving the company as a result of an acquisition. The form is used to ensure a smooth transition and to provide necessary details for the acquiring company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your employee information, including your full name, employee ID, email, phone number, department, job title, manager's name and email, original hire date, and the name of the acquiring company.
2. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Personal Leave, Family Leave, etc.).
3. Enter the dates for your leave start and expected return dates.
4. Specify the duration of your leave.
5. Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
6. Answer questions about the acquisition's impact on your leave decision.
7. Specify your current work status and the last day of work.
8. Confirm if you have any pending projects or responsibilities.
9. Describe any project handover arrangements.
10. Indicate if you have any company property to return.
11. List any company property that needs to be returned.
12. Choose how you would like to receive information about benefits continuation during your leave.
13. Specify if you would like HR to contact you during your leave.
14. Indicate if you intend to return to the company after the acquisition transition.
15. If yes, specify any conditions for your return.
16. Choose if you would like to meet with HR before your leave.
17. Add any additional comments or concerns.
18. Sign the form electronically.
19. Enter the submission date.
20. (Optional) Complete the manager approval section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Employee Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Employee ID Number** (Employee ID, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Email Address** (Email, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Manager/Supervisor Name** (Manager Name, text, required): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Manager Email** (Manager Email, email, required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Original Hire Date** (Hire Date, date, required): Enter the date you were hired.
* **Name of Acquiring Company** (Acquiring Company, text, required): Enter the name of the acquiring company.
* **Acquisition Announcement Date** (Acquisition Date, date, required): Enter the date the acquisition was announced.
* **Expected Acquisition Effective Date** (Acquisition Effective Date, date, required): Enter the expected date of acquisition completion.
* **Type of Leave Requested** (Leave Type, select_one, required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Personal Leave, Family Leave, etc.).
* **Leave Start Date** (Leave Start Date, date, required): Enter the date your leave starts.
* **Expected Return Date** (Return Date, date, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Total Duration of Leave** (Leave Duration, select_one, required): Choose the length of your leave (e.g. Less than 1 week, 1-2 weeks, etc.).
* **Reason for Leave** (Reason for Leave, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **How is the acquisition influencing your leave decision?** (Acquisition Impact, text, required): Answer questions about the acquisition's impact on your leave decision.
* **Current Work Status** (Current Status, select_one, required): Choose your current work status (e.g. Currently working, On existing leave, etc.).
* **Last Day Before Leave** (Last Day, date, required): Enter the last day of work.
* **Do you have pending projects or responsibilities?** (Pending Projects, select_one, required): Confirm if you have any pending projects or responsibilities.
* **Have you arranged for project handover?** (Project Handover, select_one, required): Describe your project handover arrangements.
* **Do you have company property to return?** (Company Property, select_one, required): Confirm if you have any company property to return.
* **List company property to be returned** (Property List, text, required): List any company property that needs to be returned.
* **How would you like to receive information about benefits continuation during your leave?** (Benefits Continuation, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive information about benefits continuation during your leave.
* **May HR contact you during your leave if needed?** (Contact During Leave, select_one, required): Specify if you would like HR to contact you during your leave.
* **Do you intend to return after the acquisition transition?** (Return Intention, select_one, required): Indicate if you intend to return to the company after the acquisition transition.
* **If yes, are there any conditions for your return?** (Return Conditions, text, required): Specify any conditions for your return.
* **Would you like to meet with HR before leave?** (HR Meeting, select_one, required): Choose if you would like to meet with HR before your leave.
* **Any additional comments or concerns?** (Additional Comments, text, required): Add any additional comments or concerns.
* **Employee Signature** (Employee Signature, text, required): Sign the form electronically.
* **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required): Enter the submission date.
* **Manager Approval (to be completed by manager)** (Manager Approval, text, required): Complete this section if you are a manager.
* **HR Review Status** (HR Review Status, select_one, required): Leave this field blank for HR use only. 

Please note that this guide only addresses the fields for the employee to fill out. If you are a manager, please refer to the manager approval section.
