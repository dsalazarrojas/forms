# intern_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from interns who have completed their internships to improve the programs and make them more effective for future interns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2.  For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best describes your experience.
3.  For text questions, provide a brief but clear explanation of your thoughts and feelings.
4.  Please be specific with your feedback, as it will help the organization improve the programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What were your intern programs?
*   **What were your intern programs** (`intern_programs`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks you to select all the programs you participated in during your internship. Choose all the programs that apply to you.

### 2. What was your job description?
*   **What was your job description** (`job_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your job responsibilities and tasks. Try to be as specific as possible.

### 3. What was your team?
*   **What was your team** (`team`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the team you worked with during your internship. This will help us understand who you interacted with during your program.

### 4. What skills were you using?
*   **What skills were you using** (`skills_used`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the skills you used during your internship. This will help us understand your work experience and areas where you might need improvement.

### 5. How satisfied were you with your job?
*   **How satisfied were you with your job** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how satisfied you were with your job during the internship. Be honest and specific with your answer.

### 6. How satisfied were you with your intern experience?
*   **How satisfied were you with your intern experience** (`intern_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how satisfied you were with your overall intern experience. This will help us understand your overall experience and suggest areas for improvement.

### 7. Please provide feedback
*   **Please provide feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): This is an open-ended question for you to share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience. Provide specific examples or suggestions for improvement, if possible.
