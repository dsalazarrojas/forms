# Team Leadership Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the leadership style, dynamics, and member influence within a team. It helps evaluate how a team works together, identifies strengths and challenges, and determines the most influential team member.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the team's leadership style from the available options (Autocratic, Democratic, or Laissez-faire).
2. Choose the ways in which team members collaborate with each other (Communication, Collaboration, and Adaptability).
3. List the members of the team.
4. Choose the leadership style that best fits this team.
5. Select the ways in which team members work together (Communication, Collaboration, and Adaptability).
6. Provide a brief description of the team's strengths and challenges in the "Strengths and Challenges" field.
7. Select the member of the team who you think is the most influential.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the team's leadership style?** (Select One, required): Choose how the team makes decisions and works together. This field helps determine how the team's leadership style affects its overall performance.
* **How do team members collaborate with each other?** (Select Multiple, required): Select all the ways in which team members work together, such as through communication, collaboration, or adaptability.
* **List the team members** (Select Multiple, required): Enter the names of all team members who are part of this team.
* **What leadership style works best for this team?** (Select One, required): Choose the leadership style that fits this team the best, such as Autocratic, Democratic, or Laissez-faire.
* **How do team members work together?** (Select Multiple, required): Select all the ways in which team members work together, such as through communication, collaboration, or adaptability.
* **What are the team's strengths and challenges?** (Text, required): Provide a brief description of the team's strengths and challenges.
* **Who do you think is the most influential team member?** (Select Multiple, required): Select the team member who you think is the most influential in leading the team.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all team members listed before submitting the form.
* Be honest and accurate when describing the team's strengths and challenges.
* Select the leadership style that best fits the team's actual performance.
