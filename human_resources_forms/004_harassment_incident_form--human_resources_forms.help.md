# Harassment Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document incidents of harassment in the workplace. Human resources personnel will use the information gathered here to investigate and address the incident.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **Incident Details**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including the events leading up to and after the incident.
2. **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred. If you're unsure, you can use a calendar or date picker tool to select the correct date.
3. **Date of Reporting**: Enter the date you are reporting the incident. This is not the same as the date of the incident.
4. **Incident Location**: Provide the location where the incident occurred.
5. **Incident Description**: Please provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: This field is for providing a detailed description of the incident. Please include as much information as possible, such as the events leading up to the incident, the actions taken, and any outcomes or resolutions.
* **Date of Incident**: This field is for entering the date the incident occurred. If you're unsure, you can use a calendar or date picker tool to select the correct date.
* **Date of Reporting**: This field is for entering the date you are reporting the incident, which may be the same or different from the date of the incident.
* **Incident Location**: This field is for providing the location where the incident occurred. This could be a specific room, floor, or building within the workplace.
* **Incident Description**: This field is for providing a detailed description of what happened during the incident. Please be as specific as possible, including any relevant details such as names, dates, times, and circumstances.
