# Short Term Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for recording short term absences from work, typically for a day or a short period of time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct dates for your absence.
2. Enter a valid reason for your absence.
3. Indicate if your manager's approval is required.
4. Add any comments if necessary.
5. Enter your email and phone number.
6. Specify if a tool has been assigned to you for your absence.
7. If necessary, add additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prophets** (1, date, optional): Enter the date you will be absent from work.
* **end_date** (2, date, optional): Enter the date you will return to work.
* **reason_for_absence** (3, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for your absence.
* **manager_approvals** (4, select_one, optional): Indicate if manager approval is required for your absence.
* **manager_approvals_2** (5, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if multiple manager approvals are required.
* **comments** (6, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your absence.
* **email** (7, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (8, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **assigned_tool** (9, select_one, optional): Specify if a tool has been assigned to you for your absence.
* **comments_2** (10, note, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **submitted_by** (11, select_one, optional): Indicate if you submitted this form.
* **submitted_at** (12, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **start_time** (13, time, optional): Specify the start time of your absence.
* **end_time** (14, time, optional): Specify the end time of your absence.
* **duration** (15, number, optional): Enter the duration of your absence.
* **submitted** (16, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have submitted this form.
* **comments_3** (17, note, optional): Add any additional comments.</textarea>

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be completed or left blank if not applicable.
