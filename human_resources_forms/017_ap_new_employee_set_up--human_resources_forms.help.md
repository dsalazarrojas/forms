# AP New Employee Set Up - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a comprehensive setup for new employees, designed to capture all essential information for HR and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

- Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
- Follow the instructions provided for each field for accurate and consistent completion.
- Take your time, as the form may have multiple pages or fields that require attention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Employee Full Name)
	+ A text field for entering the complete legal name of the employee.
	+ Ensure that the name is written as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Work Email Address** (Employee Email Address)
	+ A field for entering the official email address assigned to the employee.
	+ Use the company-provided email address as specified by HR or IT.
* **Phone Number** (Employee Phone Number)
	+ A text field for entering the primary contact number of the employee.
	+ Ensure that the number is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID)
	+ A text field for entering the assigned employee identification number.
	+ This can be a unique ID provided by the company or as specified by HR.
* **Position Title** (Position Title)
	+ A text field for entering the official job title of the employee.
	+ Use the title as specified by HR or the company's organizational structure.
* **Department** (Department)
	+ A select field with options: Accounting, Finance, Operations, Sales, Marketing, HR, IT, Management, Other
	+ Choose the department that the employee will be working in.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name)
	+ A text field for entering the name of the direct supervisor.
	+ Ensure that the manager's name is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Employment Start Date** (Employment Start Date)
	+ A date field for entering the employee's first day of employment.
	+ Use the date in the format specified by the company.
* **Employment Type** (Employment Type)
	+ A select field with options: Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Temporary, Internship
	+ Choose the type of employment arrangement the employee has.
* **Salary/Hourly Rate** (Salary/Hourly Rate)
	+ A number field for entering the agreed compensation amount.
	+ Ensure that the amount is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name)
	+ A text field for entering the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
	+ Ensure that the name is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone)
	+ A text field for entering the phone number of the emergency contact.
	+ Ensure that the number is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Direct Deposit Account Number** (Direct Deposit Account Number)
	+ A text field for entering the bank account number for payroll deposits.
	+ This field is optional and not required for all employees.
* **Direct Deposit Routing Number** (Direct Deposit Routing Number)
	+ A text field for entering the routing number for the bank account.
	+ This field is optional and not required for all employees.
* **Benefits Enrollment Selection** (Benefits Enrollment Selection)
	+ A select_multiple field with options: Health Insurance, Dental Insurance, Vision Insurance, Life Insurance, Retirement Plan, Flexible Spending Account, None
	+ Choose the benefits to which the employee would like to enroll.
* **System Access Requests** (System Access Requests)
	+ A select_multiple field with options: Email System, Accounting Software, CRM, File Server, VPN Access, Admin Portal, Other
	+ Choose the systems to which the employee needs access.
