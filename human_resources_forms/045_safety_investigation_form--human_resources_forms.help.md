# Safety Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Investigation Form is used to document and investigate incidents that occurred within the workplace. This form helps to gather essential information about the incident, including the date, description, factors leading up to the incident, contributing factors, and any corrective actions taken. The data collected from this form is used to identify potential hazards and take steps to prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the date of the incident in the **Incident Date** field.
2. Describe the incident in the **Incident Description** field.
3. Indicate whether **Factors Leading Up** were involved in the incident. Select all applicable options from the list.
4. Identify any **Contributing Factors** that contributed to the incident. Select all applicable options from the list.
5. Describe any **Corrective Actions Taken** to address the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Factors Leading Up** (`factor_leading_up`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the factors leading up to the incident were involved.
	+ Select 'No' if the factors leading up to the incident were not involved.
* **Contributing Factors** (`contributing_factors`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the contributing factors contributed to the incident.
	+ Select 'No' if the contributing factors did not contribute to the incident.
* **Corrective Actions Taken** (`corrective_actions_taken`, text, required: false): Describe any corrective actions taken to address the incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure that the investigation is conducted effectively.
* If unsure about any field, please consult with a supervisor or safety officer.
* Please note that the information collected from this form is confidential and will not be shared with anyone without proper authorization.
