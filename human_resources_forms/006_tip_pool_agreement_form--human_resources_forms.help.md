# Tip Pool Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tip Pool Agreement Form is designed for employees to agree on how tips earned from customers are shared among team members. This form helps to formalize the agreement among team members, ensuring everyone is on the same page regarding tip-sharing policies and methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the `Tip Pool Agreement` page and fill in the text field.
2. On the `Tip Sharing Policies` page, you can describe the specific policies for sharing tips.
3. On the `Distribution Methods` page, select the methods by which tips will be distributed (Manual or Automated).
4. If you're signing on behalf of the employer, mark that you've signed the agreement in the `Signed Tip Pool Agreement` page.
5. Confirm whether the document is stored or not in the `Tip Pool Agreement Document Stored` page.
6. Enter your name in the `Signed By` field.
7. Enter the date you signed the agreement in the `Date Signed` field.
8. Optionally, enter the time you signed the agreement in the `Time Signed` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tip Pool Agreement**: Fill in the text box with a description of the agreement, including the rules and expectations for sharing tips. This field is optional.
* **Tip Sharing Policies**: Describe the specific policies for sharing tips, including any rules or guidelines that must be followed. This field is optional.
* **Distribution Methods**: Select the methods by which tips will be distributed. You can choose between Manual or Automated. This field is optional.
* **Signed Tip Pool Agreement**: If you're signing on behalf of the employer, mark that you've signed the agreement. This field is optional and only for employer representatives.
* **Tip Pool Agreement Document Stored**: Select whether the document is stored or not. This field is optional.
* **Signed By**: Enter your name in this field if you're signing on behalf of the employer. This field is optional and only for employer representatives.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the agreement. This field is optional.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed the agreement. This field is optional.
