# DHOOM Sales Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for sales orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information for your sales order.
2. Make sure to input accurate and complete data for each field.
3. If you're unsure about a field, ask a sales representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name here.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name here.

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address here. Please include your street name, house number, and zip code for accurate location.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address here. This will be used for communication and updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number here for sales representatives to contact you.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for your sales order.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your sales order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate processing.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact a sales representative for assistance.
