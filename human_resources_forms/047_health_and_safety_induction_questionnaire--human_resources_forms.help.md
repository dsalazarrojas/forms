# Health and Safety Induction Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their health and safety procedures, emergency contacts, and other relevant details to ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee ID, name, and department.
2. Provide your job title and the location of safety equipment.
3. Choose the training you have completed (if applicable).
4. Answer questions about emergency procedures, fire procedures, and first aid kit locations.
5. Complete all fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID (Employee ID, text, required)**: Enter your employee ID number for identification purposes.
- **Name (name, text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge.
- **Department (department, text, required)**: Enter the department where you work.
- **Job Title (job_title, text, required)**: Enter your job title.
- **Safety Procedures (safety_procedures, text, required)**: Describe your understanding of our workplace safety procedures.
- **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact, text, required)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
- **Onboarding Date (onboarding_date, date, required)**: Enter the date you started working in our organization.
- **Training Completed (training_completed, select_one, optional)**: Choose the training you have completed if applicable.
- **Safety Equipment (safety_equipment, text, required)**: Describe the location of safety equipment in your workspace.
- **Fire Procedures (fire_procedures, text, required)**: Describe your understanding of fire procedures in our workplace.
- **First Aid Kit Location (first_aid_kit_location, text, required)**: Describe the location of first aid kits in your workplace.
- **Emergency Exits (emergency_exits, text, required)**: Describe the location of emergency exits in your workplace.
- **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact_name, text, required)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Safety Equipment Location (safety_equipment_location, text, required)**: Describe the location of safety equipment in your workplace.
- **Fire Fighting Equipment (fire_fighting_equipment, text, required)**: Describe the location of fire fighting equipment in your workplace.
- **Evacuation Procedure (evacuation_procedure, text, required)**: Describe the procedure to follow during an evacuation.
- **Fire Alarm Procedure (fire_alarm_procedure, text, required)**: Describe the procedure to follow when the fire alarm sounds.
- **Emergency Contact Procedure (emergency_contact_procedure, text, required)**: Describe the procedure to contact emergency services.
- **Fire Safety Procedure (fire_safety_procedure, text, required)**: Describe your understanding of fire safety procedures in our workplace.
- **Emergency Exit Location (emergency_exit_location, text, required)**: Describe the location of emergency exits in your workplace.
- **Emergency Exit Number (emergency_exit_number, text, required)**: Enter the number of emergency exits in your workplace.
- **First Aid Kit Supplies (first_aid_kit_supplies, text, required)**: Describe the supplies available in first aid kits in your workplace.
- **Emergency Contact Number (emergency_contact_number, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

## Tips
Please answer all questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you are unsure about any information, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
