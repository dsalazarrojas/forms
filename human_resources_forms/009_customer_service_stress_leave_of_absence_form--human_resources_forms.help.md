# Customer Service Stress Leave Of Absance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Customer Service Stress Leave Of Absance Form" is used to document and track employee stress leave requests within the Customer Service department. It helps managers and team leads make informed decisions and ensures all necessary information is gathered before granting or denying stress leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*) with accurate information.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Stress Leave ID** (`customer_service_stress_leave_id`, number, *Required*): Enter a unique identifier for the stress leave request.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, *Required*): Enter the name of the employee requesting stress leave.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, *Required*): Enter the name of the manager who will review and approve the stress leave request.
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text, *Optional*): Enter the name of the team lead who will review and approve the stress leave request.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Yes" to indicate the stress leave request is related to the Customer Service department.
* **Date of Absence Start** (`date_of_absence_start`, date, *Required*): Enter the date the employee wants to start their stress leave.
* **Date of Absence End** (`date_of_absence_end`, date, *Required*): Enter the date the employee wants to end their stress leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, *Required*): Enter a brief description of the reason for the stress leave.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Yes" to indicate the manager has approved the stress leave request.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, *Optional*): Enter any comments or feedback from the manager.
* **Team Lead Approval** (`team_lead_approval`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Yes" to indicate the team lead has approved the stress leave request.
* **Team Lead Comment** (`team_lead_comment`, text, *Optional*): Enter any comments or feedback from the team lead.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the stress leave request.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Yes" to indicate the form was submitted by the employee or manager.
* **Form Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, *Required*): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Yes" to indicate the form was updated.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, date, *Required*): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, *Optional*): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Team Lead Notes** (`team_lead_notes`, text, *Optional*): Enter any comments or feedback from the team lead.
* **Customer Service Team Lead** (`customer_service_team_lead`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Yes" to indicate the team lead is responsible for reviewing and approving the stress leave request.
* **Customer Service Manager** (`customer_service_manager`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Yes" to indicate the manager is responsible for reviewing and approving the stress leave request.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the employee.
* **Manager Status** (`manager_status`, select_one, *Required*): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the manager.
* **Customer Service Stress Leave ID 2** (`customer_service_stress_leave_id_2`, number, *Optional*): Enter a secondary identifier for the stress leave request.
