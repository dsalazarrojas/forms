# Informal Staff Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Informal Staff Review Form is designed to facilitate regular check-ins and progress tracking for employees. This form allows managers to evaluate staff performance, provide positive feedback, identify areas for improvement, and offer recommendations for growth. The form is intended to be a collaborative tool for constructive feedback and development, promoting a healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Staff Member Name**: Enter the name of the staff member being reviewed.
2. **Manager Name**: Enter your name and title, as the reviewing manager.
3. **Performance Areas Evaluated**: Describe the specific performance areas or goals that were assessed during this review.
4. **Positive Comments**: Provide genuine and specific praise for the staff member's work, accomplishments, or behaviors.
5. **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas where the staff member can improve or develop further, providing actionable suggestions or recommendations.
6. **Recommendations**: Offer recommendations for the staff member to work on, focusing on growth and development.
7. **Overall Feedback**: Summarize your overall impression of the staff member's performance and progress.
8. **Date of Review**: Enter the date of the review, as it's an important record-keeping element.
9. **Review Conducted By**: Enter your name and title, as the reviewing manager.
10. **Reviewer Comments**: Provide any additional comments, concerns, or observations about the review process or staff member.
