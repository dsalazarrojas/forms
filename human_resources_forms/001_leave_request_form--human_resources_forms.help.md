# Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Leave Request Form" is a crucial tool for employees to communicate their leave requests to the management. The form helps to streamline the leave process, ensuring timely processing of employee leaves.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID to associate your leave request with your personnel record.
- Fill in your **Employee Name**: Provide your name as it appears on your personnel record.
- Select **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Leave, Holiday, or Sick).
- Choose **Start Date** and **End Date**: Enter the dates of your leave request.
- Provide a brief **Reason for Leave**: Explain the reason for your leave request.
- Select **Department**: Choose the department you belong to.
- Leave **Leave Status**: This field is not mandatory; however, please select "Active" or "Inactive" if your leave status changes due to your leave request.
- Enter any **Comments** regarding your leave request.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, required: false): Enter your employee ID to associate your leave request with your personnel record.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required: false): Provide your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Leave, Holiday, or Sick).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, required: false): Enter the date you want your leave to begin.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, required: false): Enter the date your leave will end.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, required: false): Explain the reason for your leave request.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose the department you belong to.
* **Leave Status** (`leave_status`, select_one): Select "Active" or "Inactive" if your leave status changes due to your leave request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information about your leave request.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, text, required: false): Attach any supporting documents or files to your leave request.
## Tips
* Ensure your leave request is accurate and complete to avoid delays in processing.
* Attach any supporting documents or files to your leave request to ensure smooth processing.
* If your leave status changes due to your leave request, select "Active" or "Inactive" accordingly.
