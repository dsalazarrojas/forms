# per_diem_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a per diem reimbursement for travel-related expenses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the start and end dates of the travel period.
2. Enter the destination of the travel, such as a city or country.
3. Specify the travel dates.
4. Enter your travel start and end time, if applicable.
5. Choose the per diem rate for your travel (Good or Fair).
6. Enter the total amount reimbursable for the per diem rate.
7. Add any comments or notes about your travel expenses.
8. Specify the location of the travel.
9. Provide a reason for the travel.
10. Enter the total expense amount for the trip.
11. Choose whether you have expenses for this trip (Yes or No).
12. Specify whether your travel frequency (Yes or No).
13. Choose the mileage rate for your travel (Good or Fair).
14. Enter the total amount reimbursable for mileage.
15. Choose the meal per diem rate for your travel (Good or Fair).
16. Enter the total amount reimbursable for meal per diem.
17. Choose the hotel rate for your travel (Good or Fair).
18. Enter the total amount reimbursable for hotel expenses.
19. Choose the transportation rate for your travel (Good or Fair).
20. Enter the total amount reimbursable for transportation expenses.
21. Choose the lodging rate for your travel (Good or Fair).
22. Enter the total amount reimbursable for lodging expenses.
23. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_date**: The start date of the travel period. This field is required.
* **end_date**: The end date of the travel period. This field is required.
* **travel_destination**: The destination of the travel, such as a city or country. This field is required.
* **travel_dates**: The dates of the travel. This field is required.
* **start_time**: The start time of the travel, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **end_time**: The end time of the travel, if applicable. This field is optional.

* **travel_reason**: Provide a reason for the travel. This field is required.
* **expense_amount**: Enter the total expense amount for the trip. This field is required.
* **expense_type**: Choose whether you have expenses for this trip (Yes or No). This field is required.
* **travel_frequency**: Specify whether your travel frequency (Yes or No). This field is required.
* **mileage_rate**: Choose the mileage rate for your travel (Good or Fair). This field is required.
* **mileage_amount**: Enter the total amount reimbursable for mileage. This field is required.
* **meal_per_diem_rate**: Choose the meal per diem rate for your travel (Good or Fair). This field is required.
* **meal_per_diem_amount**: Enter the total amount reimbursable for meal per diem. This field is required.
* **hotel_rate**: Choose the hotel rate for your travel (Good or Fair). This field is required.
* **hotel_amount**: Enter the total amount reimbursable for hotel expenses. This field is required.
* **transportation_rate**: Choose the transportation rate for your travel (Good or Fair). This field is required.
