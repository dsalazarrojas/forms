# Healthcare Medical Assistant Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from candidates who are interested in working as a healthcare medical assistant. This form is used to gather relevant details about the candidate, including their general information, contact details, availability, medical certifications, work experience, and education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **General Information** section with your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your **Contact Details**, including your phone number and address.
3. Specify your **Availability**, including your preferred work schedule and any other relevant details.
4. List your **Medical Certifications**, selecting from the available options, such as BLS, ACLS, ACLS/PALS, and ACLS/PALS Combo.
5. Describe your **Work Experience**, including any relevant job titles, work hours, and job responsibilities.
6. Enter your **Education**, including the name of your educational institution and your relevant degrees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information (1)**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details that you would like to provide.
- **Contact Details (2)**: Enter your phone number and address to ensure we can contact you about your application.
- **Availability (3)**: Specify your preferred work schedule, including any days or shifts you are available to work.
- **Medical Certifications (4)**: Select all relevant medical certifications you hold, such as BLS, ACLS, ACLS/PALS, and ACLS/PALS Combo.
  Note: This field is not required, but it's recommended to select all relevant certifications you hold.
- **Work Experience (5)**: Describe your previous work experience as a healthcare medical assistant, including job titles, work hours, and job responsibilities.
- **Education (6)**: Enter the name of your educational institution and any relevant degrees you have completed.
  Note: This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide accurate information about your education.
