# Work Authorization Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the details of an employee's work authorization release. This includes information about the employee's job title, department, and job status, as well as the reason for termination and notice period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the **Form Title** field.
2. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
3. Enter the employee's ID in the **Employee ID** field.
4. Select the manager's name from the list of options in the **Manager Name** field.
5. Enter the start date of the employee's work authorization in the **Work Start Date** field.
6. Enter the end date of the employee's work authorization in the **Work End Date** field.
7. Enter the employee's job title in the **Job Title** field.
8. Select the employee's department from the list of options in the **Department** field.
9. Select the employee's job status from the list of options in the **Job Status** field.
10. Enter the reason for termination in the **Reason for Termination** field.
11. Enter the pay period in the **Pay Period** field.
12. Enter the notice period in the **Notice Period** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title
* **Form Title**: Enter the title of the form, which should be a unique identifier for the work authorization release.
### Employee Name
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being released from work authorization.
### Employee ID
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number, if applicable.
### Manager Name
* **Manager Name**: Select the name of the manager who will be responsible for the employee's work authorization release.
### Work Start Date
* **Work Start Date**: Enter the date when the employee's work authorization started.
### Work End Date
* **Work End Date**: Enter the date when the employee's work authorization ended or is expected to end.
### Job Title
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title during the period of their work authorization.
### Department
* **Department**: Select the department where the employee worked during the period of their work authorization.
### Job Status
* **Job Status**: Select the status of the employee's job during the period of their work authorization (Active or Inactive).
### Reason for Termination
* **Reason for Termination**: Enter the reason for the employee's work authorization release.
### Notice Period
* **Notice Period**: Enter the notice period provided to the employee before their work authorization ended.
### Pay Period
* **Pay Period**: Enter the pay period or payment schedule for the employee during their work authorization release.
### Notice Period
* **Notice Period**: Enter the amount of time the employee had to take off from work before their work authorization ended.
(There are two Notice Period fields, so this entry should be ignored or deleted.)
