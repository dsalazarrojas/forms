# workplace_accommodation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees submit requests for workplace accommodations due to medical conditions, disabilities, or other personal circumstances. It will be reviewed and approved by the HR department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, please enter a clear and concise description of your accommodation request.
2. On the second page, select the type of accommodation you are requesting (e.g., flexible work schedule, wheelchair ramp, etc.).
3. On the third page, provide any additional information that would be helpful for the HR department to consider your request.
4. On the fourth page, sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form**: (`workplace_accommodation_request_form_page_1`, text, required): This is the main section where you can describe your accommodation request in as much detail as possible. Please be specific about your needs and preferences.
* **Accommodation Type**: (`workplace_accommodation_request_form_page_2`, text, required): Choose the type of accommodation you are requesting from the list of options provided.
* **Additional Information**: (`workplace_accommodation_request_form_page_3`, text, required): If there's any other information that you think would be helpful for us to know, please provide it here. This could include any medical documentation or support requirements.
* **Employee Signature**: (`workplace_accommodation_request_form_page_4`, text, required): Please sign and date this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
