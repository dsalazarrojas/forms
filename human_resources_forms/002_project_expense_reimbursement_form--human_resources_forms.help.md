# Project Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Expense Reimbursement Form is used to request reimbursement for expenses related to a specific project. This form helps track and manage project expenses and ensures that they are accurately accounted for in the organization's financial records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the expense (e.g., Human Resources, Other).
2. Provide a brief description of the expense.
3. Choose the department responsible for the expense (e.g., Accounting, Finance, HR).
4. Enter the expense amount.
5. Select the date of the expense.
6. Enter your name and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Human Resources Forms, Other`, required):
    * This field allows you to select the type of expense you are submitting (e.g., travel, training, etc.). You can choose from two options: Human Resources Forms and Other.
* **Description** (`text`, optional):
    * Please provide a brief description of the expense, including details about what it was for (e.g., "Travel for team meeting").
* **Department** (`Accounting, Finance, HR`, required):
    * Choose the department responsible for the expense (e.g., Accounting, Finance, Human Resources).
* **Expense Amount** (`number`, optional):
    * Enter the exact amount of the expense.
* **Date** (`date`, optional):
    * Select the date the expense was incurred.
* **Submitter** (`text`, optional):
    * Enter your name as the person submitting the expense report.

## Tips
* Ensure that you select the correct category and department to ensure accurate expense tracking.
* Double-check the expense amount and date to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the department responsible for expenses.
* You can submit multiple expenses in one form, but each expense must be listed separately.
