# Employee Exit Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Exit Communication Form is used to gather information about an employee's departure from the company, including the reason for their exit, feedback, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's ID number in the "employee_id" field.
2. In the "reason_for_departure" field, enter a brief description of why the employee is leaving the company.
3. If you have any feedback about their time with the company, enter it in the "feedback" field.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvements that the company could make, enter them in the "suggestions_for_improvement" field.
5. If you have any open-ended questions or comments, enter them in the "open_ended_questions" field.
6. In the "next_steps" field, describe what the employee plans to do next.
7. Repeat the employee's ID number in the "employee_id" field for verification purposes.
8. Finally, enter the date of their last day of work in the "exit_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee's ID number to identify them in our system.
* **reason_for_departure** (`reason_for_departure`, text, optional): Explain why the employee is leaving the company.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback you have about their time with the company.
* **suggestions_for_improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements that the company could make.
* **open_ended_questions** (`open_ended_questions`, text, optional): Share any open-ended questions or comments.
* **next_steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Describe the employee's plans for next steps.
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Repeat the employee's ID number for verification purposes.
* **exit_date** (`exit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of their last day of work.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you're filling out this form for the correct employee.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, reach out to HR or management for guidance.
