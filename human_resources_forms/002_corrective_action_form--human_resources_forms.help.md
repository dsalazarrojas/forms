# Corrective Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corrective Action Form is used to document and track corrective actions taken in response to issues or incidents that have occurred in the workplace. This form helps ensure that all necessary steps are taken to address the issue, and that the outcome and next steps are properly documented and communicated to relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the Corrective Action Status of the issue or incident that has occurred (Corrective Action Open, Corrective Action In Progress, or Corrective Action Completed).
2. Enter the date of when the corrective action was taken.
3. Enter the employee(s) affected by the corrective action.
4. Enter a brief description of the corrective action taken.
5. Enter the number of employees affected by the corrective action.
6. (Optional) Enter the actions taken as a result of the corrective action (e.g. additional training, disciplinary action, etc.).
7. (Optional) Enter any outcome or results of the corrective action.
8. (Optional) Enter any recommendations or suggestions for future reference.
9. (Optional) Enter any next steps planned or recommended.
10. (Optional) Enter the name and title of the person who reviewed the corrective action.
11. (Optional) Enter the date of when the corrective action was reviewed.
12. (Optional) Enter the date of when the corrective action was closed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Corrective Action Status** (`corrective_action_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current status of the corrective action from the dropdown menu.
  - This field helps track the progress of the corrective action and ensures that it is properly documented.
- **Corrective Action Date** (`corrective_action_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the corrective action was taken.
  - This field helps to establish a timeline for the corrective action and ensures that it is properly recorded.
- **Employee(s) Affected** (`corrective_action_employees_affected`, `number`, required): Enter the number of employees affected by the corrective action.
  - This field helps to identify the scope of the issue or incident.
- **Corrective Action Description** (`corrective_action_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the corrective action taken.
  - This field provides a clear and concise description of the action taken to address the issue or incident.
- **Actions Taken** (`corrective_action_actions_taken`, `text`, optional): Enter any actions taken as a result of the corrective action.
  - This field is for documenting any additional actions taken to address the issue or incident.
- **Outcome** (`corrective_action_outcome`, `text`, optional): Enter any outcome or results of the corrective action.
  - This field is for documenting any outcome or results of the corrective action.
- **Recommendations** (`corrective_action_recommendations`, `text`, optional): Enter any recommendations or suggestions for future reference.
  - This field is for documenting any suggestions or recommendations resulting from the corrective action.
- **Next Steps** (`corrective_action_next_steps`, `text`, optional): Enter any next steps planned or recommended.
  - This field helps to outline a plan for future action and ensures that the corrective action is continued or completed.
- **Reviewer's Name and Title** (`corrective_action_reviewed_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and title of the person who reviewed the corrective action.
  - This field helps to assign accountability and responsibility for reviewing the corrective action.
- **Review Date** (`corrective_action_reviewed_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of when the corrective action was reviewed.
  - This field helps to establish a timeline for the review process.
- **Closed Date** (`corrective_action_closed_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of when the corrective action was closed.
  - This field helps to mark the completion of the corrective action.
 
Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled out before submission.
