To avoid duplication and ensure relevance, this form is designed to collect essential information from employees for their workplace readiness, particularly in situations related to health-related incidents or absences. It helps in assessing the employee's ability to work safely and efficiently during and after such incidents. This form is crucial in facilitating effective communication and decision-making among HR, supervisors, and other relevant parties. 

# workplace_readiness_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Readiness Declaration Form is a tool to help ensure a safe and healthy work environment. It is essential for employees to complete this form to declare their current health status, work readiness, and other relevant information to facilitate a smooth return to work process.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Employee Information
Complete the Employee Information section by filling in the required fields. This includes entering your name, which is a critical piece of information to help identify you in the return-to-work process.

### Step 2: Enter Contact Information
Complete the Contact Information section by providing your contact details. This will help the relevant authorities to get in touch with you for any follow-up or further clarification.

### Step 3: Declare Health Status
In the Health Declaration section, select one of the provided options to declare your current health status. This will help assess your readiness to return to work.

### Step 4: Enter Health Risk Level
If your health status is not fit and able to work, please enter the level of health risk you are willing to disclose. This will help us understand the nature of the risk.

### Step 5: Enter Safety Declaration
Provide the required information in the Safety Declaration section, which will help us understand your work readiness from a safety perspective.

### Step 6: Enter Workplace Safety Information (If Applicable)
If you are declaring not ready to work or partially ready to work, enter the required information regarding your safety readiness.

### Step 7: Enter Return-to-Work Date (If Applicable)
If you are ready to return to work, please provide your expected date of return.

### Step 8: Follow Up Information (If Applicable)
Enter your follow-up contact information, including the name, phone number, and visit date for follow-up visits.

### Step 9: Medical Certification and Test Information (If Applicable)
Indicate whether you require medical certification or testing and if the results are required.

### Step 10: Emergency Contact (If Applicable)
Enter your emergency contact's information, including their name, email, and phone number.

### Step 11: Medical Provider Details (If Applicable)
Enter your medical provider's details, including their name, contact phone number, and address.

### Step 12: Follow Up Contact Information (If Applicable)
Enter follow-up contact information, including their name, phone number, and visit date.

### Step 13: Supervisor Information (If Applicable)
Enter your supervisor's information, including their name, phone number, and email.

### Step 14: Submitter Information (If Applicable)
Enter your submitter's information, including their name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, required): Enter your personal details to help identify you in the return-to-work process.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact details to enable communication for follow-up or further clarification.
* **Health Declaration** (`health_declaration`, select_one, required): Declare your current health status: I am fit and able to work, I am not fit and able to work, or I am not sure if I am fit and able to work.
* **Health Risk** (`health_risk`, number, required): Enter the level of health risk you are willing to disclose if you are not fit and able to work.
* **Work Readiness** (`work_readiness`, select_one, required): Declare your work readiness: I am ready to work, I am not ready to work, or I am partially ready to work.
* **Safety Declaration** (`safety_declaration`, text, required): Enter your safety declaration, which will help understand your work readiness from a safety perspective.
* **Workplace Safety** (`workplace_safety`, time, required): If you are not ready to work or partially ready to work, enter the required information regarding your safety readiness.
* **Return To Work Date** (`return_to_work_date`, date, required): If you are ready to return to work, enter your expected date of return.
* **Follow Up Visit** (`follow_up_visit`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you require follow-up visits, including true, false, or not required.
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you require medical certification, true, false, or not required.
* **Medical Test** (`medical_test`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you require medical testing, required, not required, or not applicable.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you require a medical record, required, not required, or not applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, email, required): Enter your emergency contact's email and phone number.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, select_one, required): Indicate your medical provider's status: primary, secondary, or not applicable.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, text, required): Enter your medical provider's name.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, text, required): Enter your medical provider's contact phone number.
* **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, text, required): Enter your medical provider's address.
* **Follow Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, required): Enter your follow-up contact's name.
* **Follow Up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, text, required): Enter your follow-up contact's phone number.
* **Follow Up Visit Date** (`follow_up_visit_date`, date, required): Enter your follow-up visit date.
* **Follow Up Note** (`follow_up_note`, text, required): Enter your follow-up note.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter your supervisor's email.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter the submitter's name.
