# HR Process Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The HR Process Experience Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback from employees and managers on the human resources processes used within the organization. This survey aims to collect data on how these processes are perceived and identify areas that require improvement. The information gathered will help the HR department refine their processes to better serve the needs of employees and the organization as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct department and job title relevant to your experience.
2. Choose the areas of the HR process you have experience with.
3. Rate your overall experience with the selected process.
4. Add any comments or observations you have about your experience.
5. Choose who submitted the form and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID to be associated with your survey response. This helps the HR department track your feedback and any follow-up actions.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department you are associated with, such as HR, Accounting, Engineering, or Sales.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title to provide context to your feedback.
* **Process Areas** (`process_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the areas of the HR process you have experience with, such as Payroll, Recruitment, or Employee Development.
* **Experience Rating** (`experience_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the selected process on a scale (not specified, so assume it's a 1-5 scale).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or observations you have about your experience. This can be a good opportunity to provide specific feedback or suggestions.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Choose who submitted the form, whether it's HR, a Manager, or an Employee.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the form.
