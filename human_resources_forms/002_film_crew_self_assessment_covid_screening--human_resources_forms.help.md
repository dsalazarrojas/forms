# Film Crew Self Assessment COVID Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Crew Self Assessment COVID Screening form helps film crew members assess their personal COVID-19 risk level and provide a self-assessment of their status. This information is used for internal monitoring and management of COVID-19 risks within the film industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name.
2.  Fill in your Last Name.
3.  Enter your Email address (for contact purposes only).
4.  Enter your Phone number.
5.  Answer questions 1-6 regarding your COVID-19 risk status (these questions are optional).
6.  Select your overall Risk Level.
7.  Provide any additional comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Required**): This is your first name, which should be used for identification purposes only.
    *   Please provide your first name in this field.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **Required**): This is your last name, which is used for identification purposes only.
    *   Please provide your last name in this field.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): This field is for contact purposes only and is used to reach you if needed.
    *   Please enter a valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): This field is for contact purposes only and is used to reach you if needed.
    *   Please enter a valid phone number.
*   **Question 1**, **Question 2**, **Question 3**, **Question 4**, and **Question 5**, **Question 6** (`risk_assessment_question1`, `risk_assessment_question2`, `risk_assessment_question3`, `risk_assessment_question4`, `risk_assessment_question5`, `risk_assessment_question6`, note, **Optional**): These questions are designed to assess your COVID-19 risk status. Please answer them as accurately as possible.
    *   Please provide any relevant information to answer these questions.
*   **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the best description of your current COVID-19 risk level.
    *   Select one of the options provided.
*   **Comments** (`risk_comments`, text, **Optional**): If you have any further information or comments regarding your risk status, please provide it here.
    *   Please enter any additional comments or information that may be relevant to your risk status. 

Note: Please be honest when answering these questions. This form is used for the purpose of monitoring and managing COVID-19 risks within the industry.
