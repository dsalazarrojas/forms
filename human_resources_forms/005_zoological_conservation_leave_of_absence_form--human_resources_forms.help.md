# zoological_conserveion_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The zoological_conserveion_leave_of_absence_form is used for requesting a leave of absence for a Zoological Conservation employee. This form is required for all employees who need to take a leave of absence for any reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Enter your employee name and job title accurately.
3. Select the start and end dates of your requested leave.
4. Provide a reason for your leave (if applicable).
5. Enter your manager's name, email, and phone number.
6. Enter the approver's name, email, phone number, and department.
7. Add any additional notes (if necessary).
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title within the Zoological Conservation department.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like your leave to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your leave will end.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request (if applicable).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter the department you are requesting the leave from.
* **Approver** (`approver`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who will be approving your leave.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approver Department** (`approver_department`, `text`, required): Enter the approver's department.
* **Approver Notes** (`approver_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes for the approver (if necessary).
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your completed form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, optional): Click this button to cancel your form submission and start over.
* **Save Draft** (`save_draft`, `text`, optional): Save your form as a draft for later completion.
* **Print Preview** (`print_preview`, `text`, optional): View a print preview of your completed form.
* **Export Data** (`export_data`, `text`, optional): Export your form data to a file.
* **Export PDF** (`export_pdf`, `text`, optional): Export your form as a PDF file.
* **Approver Email Label** (`approver_email_label`, `text`, optional): This field is not used in the form.
* **Approver Phone Label** (`approver_phone_label`, `text`, optional): This field is not used in the form.
* **Approver Department Label** (`approver_department_label`, `text`, optional): This field is not used in the form.
