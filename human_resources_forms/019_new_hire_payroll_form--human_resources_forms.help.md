# New Hire Payroll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Payroll Form is used to collect and track essential information about new employees for the company's payroll purposes. This form will be reviewed and processed internally by the HR department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information about the new employee.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing.
3. Double-check the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter the new employee's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the new employee's job title or position in the company.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the new employee was hired.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, number, optional): Enter the new employee's monthly pay rate.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, text, optional): Describe any benefits or allowances the new employee is entitled to.
* **Tax Status** (`tax_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'active' if the new employee is subject to taxes, 'inactive' if not.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the new employee's employment may be terminated.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the new employee.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, optional): Enter the new employee's Social Security number.
* **SSN Copy** (`ssn_copy`, text, optional): Please enter a copy of the Social Security number for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the HR department for guidance.
* Submit the form only after it is complete and accurate to avoid delays.
