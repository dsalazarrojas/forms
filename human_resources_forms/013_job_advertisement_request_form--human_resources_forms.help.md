# Job Advertisement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new job advertisement for your department's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the necessary information.
2. Make sure to enter a contact person who will be responsible for answering questions and providing further details about the job.
3. Select the department and job type relevant to the position.
4. Provide a clear and concise job title and description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Advertisement Request**: Enter a brief summary of the job advertisement, including a description of the position and its responsibilities. (Required)
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for answering questions and providing further details about the job. (Required)
* **Job Title**: Enter the official title of the job, including any relevant certifications or qualifications. (Required)
* **Department**: Select the department responsible for the job, such as IT, HR, or Marketing. (Required)
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job, such as Full Time, Part Time, or Temp/Contract. (Required)
* **Job Description**: Enter a detailed description of the job and its responsibilities. (Required)

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to proofread your submission for any errors or typos.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the HR department for assistance.
