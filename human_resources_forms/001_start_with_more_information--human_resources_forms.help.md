# start_with_more_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "start_with_more_information" form is used to collect more detailed information about employees, which is then used for HR-related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Employee ID
 Enter the Employee ID to identify the employee in question.

### Step 2: Enter Date of Birth
Enter the date of birth of the employee.

### Step 3: Enter Phone Number (Optional)
 Enter the phone number of the employee (if applicable).

### Step 4: Enter Email (Optional)
 Enter the email of the employee (if applicable).

### Step 5: Enter Details
Enter any additional details about the employee, such as job responsibilities or performance notes.

### Step 6: Select Services Offered
Select which services the employee is offering (e.g. yes or no).

### Step 7: Enter Criteria
Enter any criteria relevant to the employee, such as performance metrics or job requirements.

### Step 8: Enter Notes (Optional)
Enter any notes or comments about the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee ID (1, number, required)
The Employee ID is used to identify the employee in question.

*   *Employee ID*: Enter the unique ID assigned to the employee by HR.

### Date of Birth (2, date, required)
Enter the date of birth of the employee.

*   *Date of Birth*: Enter the date of birth of the employee in the MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Phone Number (3, text, optional)
Enter the phone number of the employee, if applicable.

*   *Phone Number*: Enter the phone number of the employee, e.g. (123) 456-7890.

### Email (4, email, optional)
Enter the email of the employee, if applicable.

*   *Email*: Enter the email address of the employee, e.g. jane.doe@example.com.

### Details (5, text, required)
Enter any additional details about the employee, such as job responsibilities or performance notes.

*   *Details*: Enter any relevant information about the employee.

### Services Offered (6, select_multiple, required)
Select which services the employee is offering.

*   *Services Offered*: Select all applicable services, e.g. Yes, Yes, No.

### Criteria (7, text, required)
Enter any criteria relevant to the employee, such as performance metrics or job requirements.

*   *Criteria*: Enter any relevant criteria about the employee.

### Notes (8, note, optional)
Enter any notes or comments about the employee.

*   *Notes*: Enter any notes or comments about the employee.
