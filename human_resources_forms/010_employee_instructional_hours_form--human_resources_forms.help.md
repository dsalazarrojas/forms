# Employee Instructional Hours Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Instructional Hours Form is used to track and record instructional hours completed by employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the Employee Instructional Hours Form.
2. Select the "employee_information" page.
3. Enter your employee name in the field provided.
4. Select the "instructional_hours" page.
5. Enter your instructional hours completed in the field provided.
6. Click on "submit_and_reset" to submit and reset the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_information`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.
* **Instructional Hours** (`instructional_hours`, text, required): Enter the total number of instructional hours completed. Ensure the hours are accurately recorded.
* **Submit and Reset** (`submit_and_reset`, text, required): Click this button to submit your data and reset the form for future use.
