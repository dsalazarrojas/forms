# simple_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The simple_job_application_form is a form designed to collect basic job application information from candidates. This form is used for internal job openings and is meant to be completed by individuals interested in applying for a job at our organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:
1. Personal Info: Provide your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Educational Background: Share your highest level of education completed and any relevant certifications.
3. Reference Info: List at least one reference with their name, job title, and contact information.
4. Job Interest: Select the job category that best fits your qualifications.
5. Additional Comments: If you have any additional information you'd like to share, such as previous experience or skills.
6. Email: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
7. Phone: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
8. File Attachment 1: If you have any additional documents or certificates, you can attach them here.
9. File Attachment 2: If you have any other documents or certificates, you can attach them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, optional): A field to provide your personal details such as name and contact information.
* **Educational Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): A field to share your highest level of education completed and any relevant certifications.
* **Reference Info** (`reference_info`, text, optional): A field to list at least one reference with their name, job title, and contact information.
* **Job Interest** (`job_interest`, select_one, optional): A field to select the job category that best fits your qualifications.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): A field to share any additional information you'd like to share, such as previous experience or skills.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): A field to enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): A field to enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **File Attachment 1** (`file_attachment`, text, optional): A field to attach any additional documents or certificates you'd like to share.
* **File Attachment 2** (`file_attachment_2`, text, optional): A field to attach any other documents or certificates you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled and accurate.
* Be as clear and concise as possible in your responses.
* If you have any attachments, please make sure they are in a valid format (e.g., PDF, Docx).
* We recommend proofreading your application before submitting to ensure it's error-free.
