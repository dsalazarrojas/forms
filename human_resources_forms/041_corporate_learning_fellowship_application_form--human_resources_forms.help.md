# Corporate Learning Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees who wish to apply for the Corporate Learning Fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill out the required fields with your accurate and true information.
2. Ensure you have permission from your manager or supervisor before submitting this form.
3. Review each field carefully and ensure you have provided the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, false): Please enter your name as it is listed on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, text, false): Enter your valid email address for further communication.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, false): Type your current job title as it is listed on your official records.
* **Department** (`department`, text, false): Enter the name of the department you belong to in the company.
* **Degree** (`degree`, text, false): If applicable, please specify your degree or academic credential relevant to your job.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, false): Enter the field of study related to your degree.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, false): Enter the name of the institution where you received your degree.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, duplicate, false): This field is intended to record your job title at another organization you have worked with. Please enter it here if relevant.
* **Employer** (`employer`, text, false): Type the name of the previous employer if you have previous work experience outside of the company.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, false): Enter the date you started working for your previous employer, if applicable.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, false): Enter the date you ended your employment with your previous employer, if applicable.
* **Skill** (`skill`, select multiple, false): Select all relevant skills you have from the list provided.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, false): Enter the number of years you have been working in the relevant field.

## Tips
* Please ensure the accuracy of the information provided.
* Use the format MM/DD/YYYY for dates.
* Select only relevant skills from the options provided.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, please contact your manager or HR department for assistance.
