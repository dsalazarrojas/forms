# Job Offer Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to approve job offers. It gathers all the necessary information to ensure a smooth and efficient job offer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to fill out this form.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Select the correct options for job type, job status, job level, job classification, job shift, and job workweek.
4. Enter the employee's name, email, and phone number.
5. Enter the job date, start date, end date, start time, and end time.
6. Enter the job location and job hours.
7. Enter the job education and job experience.
8. Enter any relevant job notes.
9. Confirm submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter the title of the job offer.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter a brief description of the job offer.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of job offer. Options are New Hire, Promotions, and Transfers.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter the department the job offer is for.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the current status of the job offer. Options are Pending Approval, Approved, and On Hold.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of the job. Options are Executive, Management, Non-Executive, and Non-Management.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, `select_one`, required): Select the classification of the job. Options are Exempt, Non-Exempt, and Overtime Eligible.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter the name of the employee.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required): This field is used to enter the email of the employee.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter the phone number of the employee.
* **Job Date** (`job_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to enter the date of the job offer.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to enter the start date of the job offer.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to enter the end date of the job offer.
* **Job Start Time** (`job_start_time`, `time`, required): This field is used to enter the start time of the job offer.
* **Job End Time** (`job_end_time`, `time`, required): This field is used to enter the end time of the job offer.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter the location of the job offer.
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`, `number`, required): This field is used to enter the number of hours worked for the job offer.
* **Job Shift** (`job_shift`, `select_one`, required): Select the shift of the job offer. Options are Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night.
* **Job Workweek** (`job_workweek`, `select_one`, required): Select the workweek of the job offer. Options are 5/7, 4/7, 3/7, 2/7, 1/7.
* **Job Education** (`job_education`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter any relevant education for the job offer.
* **Job Experience** (`job_experience`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter any relevant experience for the job offer.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter any relevant skills for the job offer.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, `note`, required): This field is used to enter any additional notes about the job offer.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that the submission is correct.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but it's recommended to fill them out whenever possible to ensure accurate and complete information.
