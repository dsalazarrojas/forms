# Cooperative Leadership Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cooperative leadership skills assessment form is designed to evaluate the skills and abilities of a team leader or leader in a cooperative environment. This form helps to identify strengths and weaknesses in areas such as team leadership, self-leadership, collaboration, communication, conflict resolution, and leadership capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant skills and characteristics that best describe your team leader or leader's abilities.
2. Provide additional comments or observations about your assessment in the "Assessment Notes" field.
3. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Results" field.
4. Outline the next steps and actions for improvement in the "Next Steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Leader Skills (1)**: Select the skills that best describe the team leader or leader's abilities, such as "Leader Development", "Team Collaboration", "Conflict Resolution", "Adaptability", "Communication", and "Strategic Planning".
* **Self Leadership Skills (2)**: Select all the skills that you consider relevant to the leader's self-leadership abilities, such as "Emotional Intelligence", "Empathy", "Adaptability", "Self-Awareness", and "Resilience".
* **Collaboration Skills (3)**: Enter a brief description of the team leader or leader's collaboration skills, such as their ability to work with others, share ideas, and build relationships.
* **Communication Skills (4)**: Enter a brief description of the team leader or leader's communication skills, such as their ability to listen actively, articulate ideas clearly, and facilitate open communication.
* **Conflict Resolution Skills (5)**: Enter a brief description of the team leader or leader's conflict resolution skills, such as their ability to resolve disputes, mediate conflicts, and find common ground.
* **Leadership Capability Scale (6)**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to assess the team leader or leader's overall leadership capabilities, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Team Capacity (7)**: Enter a brief description of the team's capacity, such as their ability to take on new challenges, adapt to change, and work effectively.
* **Team Capacity Assessment (8)**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to assess the team's capacity, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Team Capacity is Important (9)**: Select the level of importance of the team capacity, such as "Very Important", "Important", "Neutral", "Not Very Important", or "Not At All Important".
* **Team Capacity Impact (10)**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to assess the impact of the team's capacity, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Leadership Capability Scale Assessment (11)**: Enter a brief description of the team leader or leader's leadership capability scale, such as their ability to lead effectively.
* **Assessment Notes (12)**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the assessment.
* **Assessment Results (13)**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Next Steps (14)**: Outline the next steps and actions for improvement.
