# Fire Medic Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Medic Application Form is used to collect the necessary information from individuals applying to be a fire medic. This form is crucial for the fire department's hiring process and ensures that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are:
   * First Name
   * Last Name
   * Address
   * Fire Department
   * Date of Birth
   * Employment Agreement
   * Background Check
   * Emergency Contact Name (if applicable)
   * Emergency Contact Phone (if applicable)
   * Emergency Contact Relation (if applicable)
   * Emergency Contact Message (if applicable)
   * Availability
   * Shift Preference (if applicable)
2. Enter your email address in the Email field if you want to be contacted for further information.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone field for contact purposes.
4. Select your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relation from the corresponding fields.
5. Enter a message for your emergency contact if necessary.
6. If you have a medical certification, enter it in the Medical Certification field.
7. If you have firefighter experience, enter it in the Firefighter Experience field.
8. If you have an education background, enter it in the Education Background field.
9. List any languages you speak in the Languages Spoken field.
10. List any skills and certifications you have in the Skills and Certifications field.
11. Provide references in the References field (if necessary).
12. Enter your job history in the Job History field (if necessary).
13. Select "True" or "False" for Employment Agreement.
14. Select "True" or "False" for Background Check.
15. Select "Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Fire Department** (`fire_department`, text, required): Enter the name of the fire department you are applying to.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, select_one, required): Select the relation of your emergency contact (Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Spouse).
* **Emergency Contact Message** (`emergency_contact_message`, text, optional): Enter a message for your emergency contact (if necessary).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, text, optional): Enter your medical certification.
* **Firefighter Experience** (`firefighter_experience`, text, optional): Enter your firefighter experience.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): Enter your education background.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, text, optional): List any languages you speak.
* **Skills and Certifications** (`skills_and_certifications`, text, optional): List any skills and certifications you have.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide references (if necessary).
* **Job History** (`job_history`, text, optional): Enter your job history (if necessary).
* **Employment Agreement** (`employment_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "True" or "False" for employment agreement.
* **Background Check** (`background_check`, select_multiple, required): Select "True" or "False" for background check.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, text, optional): Enter any emergency services you can provide.
* **Certification** (`certification`, text, optional): Enter any certifications you have.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Enter your availability.
* **Shift Preference** (`shift_preference`, text, optional): Enter your shift preference.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select "Submit" to submit your application or "Decline" to decline your application.
