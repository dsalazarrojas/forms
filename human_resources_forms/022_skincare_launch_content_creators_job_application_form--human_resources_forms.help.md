# Skincare Launch Content Creators Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants who are interested in launching a career as a content creator for a skincare brand.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information about yourself.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, even if they are not marked as such.
3. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info**: Please fill out this field with a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Experience**: Please provide a number representing your level of experience in the field of skincare or content creation.
* **Social Media**: Choose the social media platforms where you have a presence:
	+ Instagram
	+ TikTok
	+ YouTube
* **Portfolio**: Please provide a link to your portfolio or a brief description of your work.
* **Availability**: Please provide your availability for a new role in a suitable format (e.g., "full-time", "part-time", "freelance").
* **Contact Info**: Please fill out this field with your contact information.
* **Email**: Please fill out this field with your email address.
* **Phone**: Please fill out this field with your phone number.
* **Note**: Please use this field for any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Submit**: Choose "submit" to send your application, or "cancel" to discard your progress.
* **Date**: Please select a date when you are available to start a new role.
* **Category**: Choose a category that best describes your area of expertise:
	+ Skincare
	+ Beauty
	+ Wellness
* **Description**: Please provide a brief description of your work and experience.
* **Experience Years**: Please provide a number representing your level of experience in the field of skincare or content creation.
* **Education**: Choose the level of education you have completed:
	+ High School
	+ College
	+ University
* **Location**: Please fill out this field with your location.
* **Job Type**: Choose a job type that best describes your desired work arrangement:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Freelance
* **Language**: Please fill out this field with your language proficiency.
* **Expected Start Date**: Please select a date when you are expected to start a new role.
* **Expected Start Time**: Please select a time when you are expected to start a new role.
* **Salary**: Please provide a number representing your expected salary.
* **Salary Frequency**: Choose a frequency for your expected salary:
	+ Hourly
	+ Monthly
	+ Yearly
* **Resume**: Please upload your resume as a PDF or link to it online.
* **Portfolio Link**: Please provide a link to your portfolio or a brief description of your work.
* **Note**: Please use this field for any additional information you'd like to share.
