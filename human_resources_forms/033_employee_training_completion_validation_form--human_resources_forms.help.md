# employee-training-completion-validation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee-training-completion-validation-form" is a form used to collect information on an employee's training completion status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are authorized to access and complete this form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training-completion-form** (`employee_training_completion_validation_form`, `text`, **required**): This is the header of the form, providing a clear title of what the form is about.
* **Training Course Name** (`training_course_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the training course you have completed.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your employee name for identification purposes.
* **Training Completion Date** (`training_completion_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you completed the training course.
* **Training Completion Status** (`training_completion_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose from the options "Completed" or "Not Completed" to indicate your training completion status.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, **optional**): Provide any additional notes or comments about your training completion status.
* **Email Verified** (`email_verified`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose whether or not your email has been verified. ("True" or "False")
* **Training Completion Certification Verified** (`training_completion_certification_verified`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose whether or not your training completion certification has been verified. ("True" or "False")

## Tips
* Please ensure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department for assistance.
* If you have already completed this form, please do not attempt to fill it out again as it may cause duplication of records.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form.
* Make sure to update your training completion status as soon as you complete a training course.
