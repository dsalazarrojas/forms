# Work Authorization Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to renew and update the work authorization status of employees who have been granted work authorization in the past. This form helps HR track and manage the status of employee work authorizations, ensuring that they are up-to-date and valid.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's information, including their employee ID, name, and manager's contact details.
2. Select the current work authorization status (Active or Inactive).
3. Enter the reason for the leave (if applicable).
4. Choose the start and end dates of the leave (if applicable).
5. Submit the form to renew the employee's work authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_id`, `text`, Optional): Enter the employee's unique ID number. If you're unsure of the ID, please contact HR to confirm.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, Optional): Provide the full name of the employee.
* **Manager Information** (`manager_id`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's ID number. If you're unsure of the ID, please contact HR to confirm.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, Optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Work Authorization Status** (`work_authorization_status`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the current work authorization status:
	+ Active: The employee is currently authorized to work.
	+ Inactive: The employee is not currently authorized to work.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_leave`, `text`, Optional): Provide a brief reason for the employee's leave (if applicable).
* **Start Date** (`date_leave_start`, `date`, Optional): Enter the start date of the leave (if applicable).
* **End Date** (`date_leave_end`, `date`, Optional): Enter the end date of the leave (if applicable).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, Optional): Re-enter the employee's ID number for confirmation.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, `text`, Optional): Re-enter the manager's ID number for confirmation.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, Optional): Re-enter the manager's email address for confirmation.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, Optional): Re-enter the manager's phone number for confirmation.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the information before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact HR for clarification.
- Ensure that the employee's work authorization status is correctly selected.
- This form is not meant to create new work authorizations, but to renew existing ones.
- If the employee's work authorization status needs to be changed, please use the corresponding form for that purpose.
