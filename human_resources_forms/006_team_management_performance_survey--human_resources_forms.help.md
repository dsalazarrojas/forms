# Team Management Performance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Management Performance Survey is designed to collect feedback and performance data from team members. This form is used to gather information on team performance, suggestions, and feedback from employees, which will help in making informed decisions for team management and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This is the initial page where you will find a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
2. Select the **employee** who you are evaluating (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2).
3. Provide your **feedback** about the employee's performance in the text field.
4. Suggest **additional feedback** or comments in the note field.
5. Select the **evaluation frequency** for the performance evaluation (e.g., Option 9, Option 10, or Option 11).
6. Choose the **team name** where the employee is part of (e.g., Option 5, Option 6, or Option 8).
7. Select the **role** of the employee (e.g., a text field for their job title).
8. Choose the **department** the employee is part of (e.g., a text field).
9. Select the **location** where the employee works (e.g., a text field).
10. Enter the **email** of the employee (e.g., their work email).
11. Enter the **phone number** of the employee (e.g., their work phone number).
12. Enter any **comments** about the employee's performance in the note field.
13. Rate the employee's performance using the **performance scales** (e.g., Option 18, Option 19, Option 20, Option 21, Option 22, Option 23, Option 24, or Option 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee**: Select the employee being evaluated (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2).
* **feedback**: Provide feedback about the employee's performance in this text field.
* **suggestions**: Provide any suggestions for improvement in this note field.
* **evaluation_frequency**: Select the frequency of the performance evaluation (e.g., Option 9, Option 10, or Option 11).
* **team_name**: Choose the team the employee is part of (e.g., Option 5, Option 6, or Option 8).
* **role**: Enter the employee's role or job title in this text field.
* **department**: Choose the department the employee is part of (e.g., a text field).
* **location**: Enter the location where the employee works (e.g., a text field).
* **email**: Enter the employee's work email in this field.
* **phone**: Enter the employee's work phone number in this field.
* **comments**: Enter any comments about the employee's performance in this note field.
* **performance_rating**: Rate the employee's performance using the performance scales (e.g., Option 18, Option 19, Option 20, Option 21, Option 22, Option 23, Option 24, or Option 25).
