# Worker Skills Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request upgrades to a worker's skills or position within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Employee ID: Enter the unique identifier of the employee requesting the upgrade.
	* Training Request: Describe the specific skills or position you are requesting.
	* Manager Name: Enter the name of your direct manager.
2. Optional fields:
	* Preferred Format: Select "Yes" if you prefer a specific format for the training or upgrade request. If not, leave this field blank.
	* Manager Approval: Select the managers who will be approving your request. You can choose multiple managers if needed.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID as assigned by the company.
- **Training Request** (`training_request`, text, required): Describe the specific skills or position you are requesting. Be as detailed as possible so that your manager can understand your requirements.
- **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, select, optional): If you have a preferred format for the skills or position upgrade, select "Yes". If you don't have a preference, leave this field blank.
- **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select, optional): Select the managers who will be approving your request. You can choose multiple managers if needed.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct manager.
- **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, optional): If your request has been approved, enter the date it was approved. If not, leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult your manager or HR department for clarification.
* Be specific and detailed in your training request to ensure your manager understands your requirements.
