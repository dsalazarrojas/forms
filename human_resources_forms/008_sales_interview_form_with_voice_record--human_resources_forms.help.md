# sales_interview_form_with_voice_record - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from candidates during the sales interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information for the sales interview.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Review and save the form for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the candidate being interviewed.
* **Candidate Audio Response** (`candidate_audio_response`, `time`, required: false): Record or attach a sound file of the candidate's audio response during the interview.
* **Recruiter Name** (`recruiter_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the recruiter who conducted the interview.
* **Recruiter Department** (`recruiter_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department that the recruiter works in.
* **Recruiter Location** (`recruiter_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the recruiter.
* **Candidate Phone** (`candidate_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the candidate.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the email address of the candidate.
* **Recruiter Note** (`recruiter_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any notes or comments from the recruiter about the interview.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields accurately to get the most out of this form.
* Use sound files for the candidate's audio response to provide more detailed information.
* Review and save the form for future reference.
