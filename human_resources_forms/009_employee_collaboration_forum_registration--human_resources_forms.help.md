# Employee Collaboration Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee collaboration forum is a platform where employees can share information, ideas, and collaborate on various projects and initiatives. This form is the first step in registering for the forum.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Employee Registration** (`title`, required): Select your participation status by checking "Yes" or "No" in the Employee Registration field.
2. **Department** (`department`, required): Select the department you belong to from the list provided.
3. **Interests** (`interests`, required): In the Interests field, type a brief description of your areas of interest or expertise.
4. **Expectations** (`expectations`, required): In the Expectations field, type a brief description of what you hope to achieve or gain from participating in the collaboration forum.
5. **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your willingness to participate in the collaboration forum.
6. **Participant Details Department** (`participant_details_department`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not you want your department to be included in the participant details.
7. **Participant Details Interests** (`participant_details_interests`, required): In the Participant Details Interests field, type a brief description of your interests or expertise.
8. **Participant Details Expectations** (`participant_details_expectations`, required): In the Participant Details Expectations field, type a brief description of what you hope to achieve or gain from participating in the collaboration forum.
9. **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your communication preferences.
10. **Participant Details Communication Preferences** (`participant_details_communication_preferences`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your willingness to share your communication preferences with other participants.
11. **Participant Details Communication Channel** (`participant_details_communication_channel`, required): Select your preferred communication channel (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
12. **Participant Details Communication Frequency** (`participant_details_communication_frequency`, required): Select how often you would like to communicate (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
13. **Participant Details Communication Time** (`participant_details_communication_time`, required): Select the best time of day for communication (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
14. **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Provide any additional information or notes you would like to share.
15. **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, optional): Provide any additional information or notes you would like to share.
