# retail_shift_employee_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail shift employee attendance form is used to track the attendance of retail shift employees. It captures essential information, including employee details, attendance information, and manager confirmations, to ensure accurate time tracking and proper attendance management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's information on page 1, including their name, and any other required details.
2. Provide attendance information on page 2, including any relevant details about the employee's shift.
3. Add any additional notes on page 3, if necessary.
4. On page 4, select one or more manager confirmations for the employee's attendance.
5. On page 5, enter the manager's name who approved the employee's attendance.
6. On page 6, select one option for the employee's confirmation.
7. Finally, sign the employee's signature on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info** (employee_info, text, optional): This field collects the employee's basic details, such as their name. Please fill in the employee's name accurately to ensure correct identification.
* **Attendance Info** (attendance_info, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the employee's shift, like the shift start and end times.
* **Note** (note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the employee's attendance, if necessary.
* **Manager Confirmation** (manager_confirmation, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options from the list to confirm the employee's attendance. This field is intended for the manager to verify the employee's presence and any other relevant attendance details.
* **Manager Name** (manager_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the manager who approved the employee's attendance.
* **Employee Confirmation** (employee_confirmation, select_one, optional): Select one option for the employee's confirmation. This field is likely for the employee to confirm their own attendance.
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature, text, optional): Sign and date the form to indicate the employee's confirmation of their attendance. Please ensure the signature is legible and clear.
