# Policy Violation Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Violation Incident Form is used to report policy violations and incidents, providing a structured format for capturing relevant information and facilitating the investigation and resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to start completing it.
2. Select the date of the incident.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
4. Select the status of the incident.
5. Enter the reporting officer's name.
6. Enter the reporting officer's details (name, email, phone number, address, department, job title, and ID).
7. Select the reason for the incident.
8. Select multiple reasons for the incident, if necessary.
9. Enter any additional comments from the reporting officer.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, required): The date of the incident being reported.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, optional): A detailed description of the incident, including the circumstances and any relevant details.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, optional): The status of the incident (Active, Inactive, Closed).
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer_name`, optional): The name of the officer who is reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Officer Details** (`reporting_officer_details`, optional): Additional details of the reporting officer (name, email, phone number, address, department, job title, and ID).
* **Reporting Officer Email** (`reporting_officer_email`, optional): The email of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Phone** (`reporting_officer_phone`, optional): The phone number of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Address** (`reporting_officer_address`, optional): The address of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Department** (`reporting_officer_department`, optional): The department of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Job Title** (`reporting_officer_job_title`, optional): The job title of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer ID** (`reporting_officer_id`, optional): The ID of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Notes** (`reporting_officer_notes`, optional): Additional comments from the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Date** (`reporting_officer_date`, optional): The date of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Time** (`reporting_officer_time`, optional): The time of the reporting officer.
* **Incident Reporting Reason** (`incident_reporting_reason`, optional): The reason for reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Officer Reason** (`reporting_officer_reason`, optional): The reason for reporting the incident.
* **Select Multiple Reasons** (`reporting_officer_reasons`, optional): Select multiple reasons for the incident (Policy Violation, Incident Report, Other).
* **Incident Reporting Officer Notes** (`incident_reporting_officer_notes`, optional): Additional comments from the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Email 2** (`reporting_officer_email_2`, optional): The email of the second reporting officer (if necessary).
* **Reporting Officer Phone 2** (`reporting_officer_phone_2`, optional): The phone number of the second reporting officer (if necessary).
* **Reporting Officer Address 2** (`reporting_officer_address_2`, optional): The address of the second reporting officer (if necessary).
* **Reporting Officer Department 2** (`reporting_officer_department_2`, optional): The department of the second reporting officer (if necessary).
* **Reporting Officer Job Title 2** (`reporting_officer_job_title_2`, optional): The job title of the second reporting officer (if necessary).
* **Reporting Officer ID 2** (`reporting_officer_id_2`, optional): The ID of the second reporting officer (if necessary).
* **Reporting Officer Notes 2** (`reporting_officer_notes_2`, optional): Additional comments from the second reporting officer (if necessary).

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that the reporting officer's details are accurate and up-to-date.
* Select the correct reasons for the incident and multiple reasons if necessary.
* Provide additional comments from the reporting officer, if applicable.
* Review and submit the form carefully before finalizing it.
