# Team Member Follow Up Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from team members on their performance after a follow-up meeting. The feedback provided will help identify areas of strength and weakness, and inform decisions on next steps and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing specific examples or details when possible.
3. Make sure to save your progress regularly to avoid losing your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Follow Up Evaluation** (`title`, required/optional: `optional`): This is the title of the form.
* **First Page** (`team_member_follow_up_evaluation_first_page`, type: `note`, required: `false`): This page is for any additional information or notes about the team member's performance.
* **Performance** (`performance`, type: `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ **Yes**: The team member has made significant progress or achieved notable success.
	+ **No**: The team member has not made significant progress or has faced significant challenges.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, type: `text`, required: `false`): This field is for suggestions or ideas on next steps or actions to be taken by the team member.

## Tips
* Be specific and provide concrete examples when answering questions.
* Focus on providing actionable feedback that can be used to support the team member's growth and development.
* If you are unsure about a particular question or area, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for guidance.
