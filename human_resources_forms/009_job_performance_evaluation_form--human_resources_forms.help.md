# Job Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Performance Evaluation Form is used to evaluate an employee's performance. It is completed by a reviewer, typically a manager or supervisor, to assess the employee's job performance during a specific evaluation period. The form helps to identify areas of strength and weakness, provide feedback, and track progress towards goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter the employee's job title in the "job_title" field.
3. Select the employee's performance level from the "performance_level" field.
4. Enter the evaluation period in the "evaluation_period" field.
5. Provide comments about the employee's performance in the "evaluation_comments" field.
6. Enter your name and title in the "manager_name" and "reviewer_title" fields.
7. Enter the review date in the "review_date" field.
8. Enter the employee's performance level in the "employee_performance_level" field.
9. Enter the evaluation date in the "evaluation_date" field.
10. Enter the job performance level in the "job_performance_level" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (text): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **job_title** (text): Enter the job title of the employee being evaluated.
* **performance_level** (select one): Select the level of performance for the employee, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **evaluation_period** (date): Enter the period in which the evaluation is taking place.
* **evaluation_comments** (note): Provide feedback and comments about the employee's performance.
* **submit_button** (text): Not a field to fill out, but a button to submit the form.
* **email** (email): Not used in this form.
* **phone** (text): Not used in this form.
* **manager_name** (text): Enter your name as the manager or reviewer.
* **reviewer_name** (text): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **reviewer_title** (text): Enter your title as the reviewer.
* **review_date** (time): Not used in this form.
* **employee_performance_level** (text): Enter the employee's performance level.
* **evaluation_date** (date): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **job_performance_level** (text): Enter the job performance level.
* **employee_id** (number): Not used in this form.
* **department_id** (number): Not used in this form.
* **manager_id** (number): Not used in this form.
* **reviewer_id** (number): Not used in this form.
* **evaluation_period_id** (number): Not used in this form.
* **review_date_id** (number): Not used in this form.
* **job_performance_level_id** (number): Not used in this form.
* **employee_performance_level_id** (number): Not used in this form.
