# Leadership Development Program Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to release from the Leadership Development Program. It is used to gather necessary details for the release process and ensure a smooth transition for the participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and authorization to complete the form.
2. Select the "Participant Details" page and fill in the required fields.
3. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
4. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
5. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
6. If necessary, add any additional details about the program in the "Program Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the participant, as it is on their official documents or ID.

### Participant Phone
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): Enter the participant's phone number, including the country code (if applicable).

### Participant Email
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, not required): Enter the participant's email address.

### Program Details
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, not required): If there is any relevant information about the participant's program, such as the program's title, dates, or status, please add it here.
