# International Employment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document agreements between employees and their managers on international employment contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the field labeled "Employee Name".
2. Enter the employee's email address in the field labeled "Email".
3. Enter the manager's name in the field labeled "Manager Name".
4. Select the job title for the employee in the field labeled "Job Title".
5. Choose the length of the employment term by selecting a value for "Employment Term" (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
6. Enter the date the contract was signed in the field labeled "Contract Sign Date".
7. Enter the date the contract will end in the field labeled "Contract End Date".
8. Enter your signature in the field labeled "Employee Sign".
9. Enter your manager's signature in the field labeled "Manager Sign".
10. Choose whether the contract is currently active or inactive in the field labeled "Contract Status" (Active or Inactive).
11. Select the review status of the contract in the field labeled "Review Status" (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who signed the contract.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Job Title**: Select a job title that describes the employee's role.
* **Employment Term**: Choose the length of the employment term (e.g., 1 month, 2 months, 3 months, etc.).
* **Contract Sign Date**: Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the date the contract will end.
* **Employee Sign**: This field is for the employee to sign and date the contract.
* **Manager Sign**: This field is for the manager to sign and date the contract.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date the contract will be reviewed.
* **Contract Status**: Select whether the contract is currently active or inactive.
* **Contract Status Choices**: This field is for notes or explanations regarding the contract status.
* **Review Status**: Select whether the contract is currently under review or not.
* **Review Status Choices**: This field is for notes or explanations regarding the review status.
