# Employee Raise Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's raise request, capturing information from both the employee and their manager to process the raise request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's details such as name, phone, department, job title, and email.
2. Enter the raise amount and reason for the raise.
3. Provide the date and time of the raise request.
4. Enter the manager's name and email.
5. Sign and comment (optional) as the manager.
6. The final approval status can be selected by both employee and manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee's Name** (`employee_raise_form_employees_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employee requesting the raise.
* **Employee's Phone** (`employee_raise_form_employees_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Employee's Department** (`employee_raise_form_employees_department`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's department.
* **Employee's Job Title** (`employee_raise_form_employees_job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Employee's Email** (`employee_raise_form_employees_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Raise Amount** (`employee_raise_form_raise_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the raise amount.
* **Reason for Raise** (`employee_raise_form_reason_for_raise`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for the raise.
* **Date of Raise** (`employee_raise_form_date_of_raise`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the raise.
* **Time of Raise** (`employee_raise_form_time_of_raise`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the raise.
* **Departmental Review** (`employee_raise_form_departmental_review`, `text`, optional): Enter the departmental review.
* **Manager's Name** (`employee_raise_form_manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager's Signature** (`employee_raise_form_manager_signature`, `note`, optional): Sign as the manager.
* **Manager's Email** (`employee_raise_form_manager_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Employee's Comments** (`employee_raise_form_employees_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's comments.
* **Manager's Comments** (`employee_raise_form_manager_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter the manager's comments.
* **Employee's Approval** (`employee_raise_form_employees_approval`, `select_one`, optional): Select the employee's approval status.
* **Manager's Approval** (`employee_raise_form_manager_approval`, `select_one`, optional): Select the manager's approval status.
* **Final Approval** (`employee_raise_form_final_approval`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the final approval status.
