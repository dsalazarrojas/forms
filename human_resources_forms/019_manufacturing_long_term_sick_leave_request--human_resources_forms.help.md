# Manufacturing Long Term Sick Leave Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Long Term Sick Leave Request form is used to submit a request for long term sick leave. This form is used to request a prolonged period of leave due to medical or personal reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Employee** (`Employee`, `text`, required)
		+ Enter the name of the employee requesting the leave.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Job Title** (`job title`, `text`, required)
		+ Enter the job title of the employee requesting the leave.
3. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Start Date** (`start date`, `date`, required)
		+ Enter the start date of the leave period.
	* **End Date** (`end date`, `date`, required)
		+ Enter the end date of the leave period.
4. Select a **Leave Type** (`leave type`, `select_one`, required)
		+ Choose the type of leave being requested (Medical, Personal, Family, or Vacation).
5. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required)
		+ Enter a brief explanation of the reason for the leave.
6. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
	* **Doctor Name** (`doctor`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter the name of the doctor who has certified the leave.
	* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor phone`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter the phone number of the doctor who has certified the leave.
	* **Doctor Address** (`doctor address`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter the address of the doctor who has certified the leave.
	* **Hospital Name** (`hospital`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter the name of the hospital where the doctor is based.
	* **Hospital Address** (`hospital address`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter the address of the hospital where the doctor is based.
	* **Doctor License** (`doctor license`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter the medical license number of the doctor who has certified the leave.
6. If applicable, select whether the **Medical Certificate** (`medical certificate`, `select_multiple`, required) has been uploaded.
7. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required)
		+ Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
8. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Approver** (`approver`, `text`, required)
		+ Enter the name of the person approving the form.
9. Select the **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required)
	* Choose the status of the leave request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
10. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Comments Approved** (`comments approved`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter any comments or feedback from the approver.
	* **Approver Comments** (`approver comments`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter any comments or feedback from the submitter.
	* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter comments`, `text`, optional)
		+ Enter any comments or feedback from the submitter.
11. If applicable, select the **Approval Date** (`approval date`, `date`, optional) and **Approval Time** (`approval time`, `time`, optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee**: Enter the name of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the leave period.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the leave period.
* **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave being requested (Medical, Personal, Family, or Vacation).
* **Reason**: Enter a brief explanation of the reason for the leave.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who has certified the leave.
* **Doctor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the doctor who has certified the leave.
* **Doctor Address**: Enter the address of the doctor who has certified the leave.
* **Hospital Name**: Enter the name of the hospital where the doctor is based.
* **Hospital Address**: Enter the address of the hospital where the doctor is based.
* **Doctor License**: Enter the medical license number of the doctor who has certified the leave.
* **Medical Certificate**: Choose whether the medical certificate has been uploaded.
* **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Approver**: Enter the name of the person approving the form.
* **Status**: Choose the status of the leave request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Comments Approved**: Enter any comments or feedback from the approver.
* **Approver Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the submitter.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval.
* **Approval Time**: Enter the time of approval.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative for guidance.
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling in the form to ensure a smooth approval process.
* Attach any supporting documents, such as medical certificates, as required.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the approver or submitter for assistance.
