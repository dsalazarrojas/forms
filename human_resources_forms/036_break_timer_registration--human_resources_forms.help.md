# Break Timer Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employee break times and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have reviewed and understand the break policies before submitting this form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
3. Enter your employee name, email address, and employee ID number.
4. Select your department and direct manager.
5. Choose your work schedule.
6. Select your preferred break times and duration.
7. Choose how you would like to be reminded of breaks.
8. Select the timing of the reminders.
9. Indicate what you usually do during your breaks.
10. If you have wellness goals, select them.
11. If you need any accommodations, choose them.
12. Set your start date for the break schedule.
13. Acknowledge understanding of break policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name (1)** (Employee Full Name, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address (2)** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Employee ID (3)** (Employee ID, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department (4)** (Department, select_one, required): Select your department or team.
* **Manager Name (5)** (Manager Name, text, required): Enter your direct manager's name.
* **Work Schedule (6)** (Work Schedule, select_one, required): Choose your regular schedule.
* **Preferred Break Times (7)** (Preferred Break Times, select_multiple, required): Select when you prefer to take breaks.
* **Preferred Break Duration (8)** (Preferred Break Duration, select_one, required): Choose the length of your breaks.
* **Breaks Per Day (9)** (Breaks Per Day, select_one, required): Select how many breaks you take per day.
* **Reminder Method (10)** (Reminder Method, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be reminded of breaks.
* **Reminder Timing (11)** (Reminder Timing, select_one, required): Select when reminders should be sent.
* **Typical Break Activities (12)** (Typical Break Activities, select_multiple, required): Indicate what you usually do during breaks.
* **Wellness Goals (13)** (Wellness Goals, select_one, optional): Select if you have wellness goals.
* **Team Coordination (14)** (Team Coordination, select_one, required): Choose if you'd like to coordinate breaks with your team.
* **Accessibility Needs (15)** (Accessibility Needs, select_one, required): Select if you need any accommodations.
* **Registration Start Date (16)** (Registration Start Date, date, required): Choose when you'd like the break schedule to start.
* **Acknowledgment (17)** (Acknowledgment, select_one, required): Confirm understanding of break policies.

## Tips
- Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
- Review and understand the break policies before submitting.
- This form is only for employees to fill out.
