# Employee Self Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Self Appraisal Form is a tool for employees to evaluate and provide feedback on their own performance. This form is designed to help employees take ownership of their goals and objectives, and for managers to track and discuss performance with employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided.
2. Fill out all required fields.
3. Review and edit your responses as needed.
4. Submit the form for review by your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Rating
* **Employee Rating** (`employee_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate yourself from 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest, based on your overall performance over the past period.
### Employee Comments
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide a written explanation of your performance and areas for improvement.
### Manager Comments
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, required/optional): If you are a manager, use this field to provide feedback and comments on the employee's performance.
### Performance Area Comments
* **Performance Area Comments** (`performance_area_comments`, text, required/optional): Discuss the employee's performance in specific areas such as sales, customer service, or project management.
### Performance Area Rating
* **Performance Area Rating** (`performance_area_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the employee's performance in each area from 1-5.
### Job Performance Rating
* **Job Performance Rating** (`job_performance_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall job performance from 1-5.
### Job Comments
* **Job Comments** (`job_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide a written explanation of your job performance and areas for improvement.
### Performance Goal Rating
* **Performance Goal Rating** (`performance_goal_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your progress towards your performance goals from 1-5.
