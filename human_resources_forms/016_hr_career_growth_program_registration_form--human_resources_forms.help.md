# HR Career Growth Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Career Growth Program Registration Form is designed to help employees register for the company's career growth program, which aims to support their professional development and growth within the organization. Completing this form will help the HR department understand each employee's goals and objectives, making it easier to tailor support and resources to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Scroll through the form pages and fields below to understand the sections and fields that require your input.
2.  Fill out the employee details page (Page 1) with your name, job title, department, and any other relevant employee information.
3.  On the program goals page (Page 2), describe your goals and objectives for the career growth program. Be specific about what you hope to achieve through this program.
4.  On the approvals page (Page 3), provide any necessary approvals or signatures from your manager or HR representative.
5.  If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them on the notes page (Page 4).
6.  Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Details (Page 1)**: Enter your name, job title, and department to help the HR department understand your identity and role within the company.
*   **Program Goals (Page 2)**: Describe your goals and objectives for the career growth program. Be specific about what you hope to achieve through this program.
*   **Approvals (Page 3)**: If necessary, provide any approvals or signatures from your manager or HR representative. This will help the HR department verify your registration and goals.
*   **Notes (Page 4)**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have about your registration or goals.
*   **Submit (Page 5)**: Click this button to complete and submit your registration form.
