# PTO Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTO Reimbursement Form is used to process requests for Paid Time Off (PTO) reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already submitted a PTO claim for the same period.
2. Enter your Employee ID in the designated field.
3. Select the start and end dates of your PTO period.
4. Enter the number of days you took off.
5. Provide a brief reason for taking the PTO.
6. Select the type of reimbursement you are eligible for (PTO or Vacation).
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments.
8. Submit the form for review and processing by your HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID to identify you in the system.
* **Start Date** (`pto_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started your PTO period.
* **End Date** (`pto_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you ended your PTO period.
* **Days Off** (`days_off`, number, required): Enter the total number of days you took off.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for taking the PTO.
* **Reimbursement Type** (`reimbursement_type`, select one, required): Select either PTO or Vacation as the type of reimbursement you are eligible for.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your PTO claim, if necessary.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name for record-keeping purposes.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form, if necessary.
