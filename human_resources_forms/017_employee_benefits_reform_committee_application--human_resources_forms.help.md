# Employee Benefits Reform Committee Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from employees who are interested in joining the Employee Benefits Reform Committee. The committee aims to review and propose changes to the company's employee benefits package. By filling out this form, you will be considered for membership on the committee.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and clear information about your contact details, work experience, and any additional notes or comments.
- Ensure that you have permission from your manager or HR department to join the committee.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the committee chair or HR department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: This field is used to collect your name, title, and contact information so that we can get in touch with you about your application.
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
	+ Enter your title or job position.
	+ Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you about your application.
* **Experience**: This field is used to collect information about your relevant work experience. Please provide a brief description of your experience and how it relates to the committee's purpose.
* **Acknowledgment**: In this field, please acknowledge that you understand the committee's purpose and that you are interested in joining the committee.
* **Notes**: This field is optional, but please use it to provide any additional comments or information that you think would be helpful to the committee.
* **Agreement**: This field is also optional, but please indicate your willingness to commit to attending committee meetings and contributing to the discussion.

## Tips

- Make sure to be honest and clear in your answers.
- Be respectful and professional in your communication.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate your interest in joining the committee, and we will review your application carefully.
