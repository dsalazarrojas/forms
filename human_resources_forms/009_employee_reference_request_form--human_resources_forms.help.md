# employee_reference_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_reference_request_form is used to document an employee's job history, details of their previous employment, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Ensure you are completing the form for a valid reason and not for external sharing.
- Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
- Enter your job title and date employed.
- Enter your previous employer's name and job description.
- Enter your supervisor's name, job title, phone number, and email address.
- Enter the supervisor's company and job title.
- Enter the supervisor's start and end dates.
- Enter the reason for termination (if applicable).
- Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (3): Enter your current or previous job title.
* **Date Employed** (4): Enter the date you started working at your current or previous job.
* **Previous Employer** (5): Enter the name of your previous employer.
* **Job Description** (6): Enter a brief description of your job title and responsibilities.
* **Supervisor Name** (7): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (8): Enter the job title of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (9): Enter the phone number of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (10): Enter the email address of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Company** (11): Enter the name of your supervisor's company.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (12): Enter the job title of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Supervisor** (13): Enter the name of your supervisor's supervisor (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Address** (14): Enter the address of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Start Date** (15): Enter the start date of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor End Date** (16): Enter the end date of your supervisor.
* **Reason for Termination** (17): Enter the reason for termination (if applicable).
* **Comments** (18): Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are correctly filled in to avoid incomplete or incorrect information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your HR or management representative for clarification.
- If you have any issues or concerns while completing this form, please contact your HR or management representative.
