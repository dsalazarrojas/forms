# Insurance Agency Resignation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record an employee's resignation from an insurance agency. It is typically completed by the employee to provide official notice of their departure and to specify the reason for their resignation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your resignation date.
2. Provide your name and reason for resigning.
3. Enter your last day of work and the notice period (if applicable).
4. If your reason for resigning is "Other", please specify the reason.
5. Enter your manager's name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resignation Date** (`resignation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you officially intend to resign from the insurance agency.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as an employee of the agency.
* **Reason For Resigning** (`reason_for_resigning`, text, optional): Select the reason for your resignation from the pre-defined list. If your reason is not listed, you can choose "Other" and provide a specific reason.
* **Last Day of Work** (`last_day_of_work`, number, optional): Enter the date of your last day of work in MM/DD format.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you are providing as notice period.
* **Other (please specify)** (`reason_for_resigning_other`, text, optional): If your reason for resigning is not listed, enter a brief description of your reason.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth transition of your responsibilities.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for guidance.
* This form will be reviewed and processed by the HR department.
