# HR Operational Excellence Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Operational Excellence Conference Registration form is used to collect information from attendees, sponsors, and speakers for the HR Operational Excellence Conference. This form is necessary for registration and planning purposes, and it helps the conference organizers to manage attendee, sponsor, and speaker data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all the required information before starting the registration process.
2. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
3. Select your participation type by choosing one or more of the options under "What type of participation are you registering for?".
4. If you have any special needs or requests for accommodations, select the corresponding option under "Do you have any special needs or requests for accommodations?".
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field.
7. Provide a detailed description of your participant details in the "Please provide a detailed description of your participant details." field.
8. If you have any special needs or requests for accommodations, select the corresponding option under "Special Needs 2".
9. Check if this is your first time attending the conference under "Is this your first time attending the conference?".
10. Describe your previous experience with the HR Operational Excellence Conference in the "Please describe your previous experience with the HR Operational Excellence Conference." field.
11. Check if you are interested in serving on a conference committee or helping with logistics under "Are you interested in serving on a conference committee or helping with logistics?".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name?**: This field is a text field where you enter your name.
- **What type of participation are you registering for?**: This is a selection of multiple options, choose all that apply. The options are:
  - **Attendee**: You are registering as an attendee.
  - **Sponsor**: You are registering as a sponsor.
  - **Speaker**: You are registering as a speaker.
- **Do you have any special needs or requests for accommodations?**: This field is a selection of multiple options, choose all that apply. The options are:
  - **Yes**
  - **No**
- **Email**: This field is an email address where you enter your email.
- **What is your phone number?**: This field is a text field where you enter your phone number.
- **Please provide a detailed description of your participant details**: This field is a text box where you provide a detailed description of your participant details.
- **Special Needs 2**: This is a selection of multiple options, choose all that apply. The options are:
  - **Yes**
  - **No**
- **Is this your first time attending the conference?**: This is a selection of one option, choose one. The options are:
  - **Yes**
  - **No**
- **Please describe your previous experience with the HR Operational Excellence Conference**: This field is a text box where you describe your previous experience with the HR Operational Excellence Conference.
- **Are you interested in serving on a conference committee or helping with logistics?**: This is a selection of one option, choose one. The options are:
  - **Yes**
  - **No**
