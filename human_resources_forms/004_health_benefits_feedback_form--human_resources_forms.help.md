# health_benefits_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health benefits feedback form is designed to collect feedback from employees on
the quality of the health benefits provided by the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide a short description of your overall satisfaction with the
    current health benefits provided by the company.
2.  If you have any issues or suggestions related to the health
    benefits program, please describe them in the "Additional Feedback"
    field.
3.  Optionally, select all the ways you prefer to communicate with the
    benefits administration team (e.g., phone, email, chat, in-person).
4.  If applicable, select the status of your benefits administration
    team (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, temporary).
5.  Finally, if you have any other feedback or suggestions, provide
    them in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Benefits Feedback**: This field is for you to describe your
  thoughts about the current health benefits provided by the company.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This field helps us understand your
  satisfaction level with the health benefits. Please select the option
  that best represents your feelings.
* **Benefits Program**: This field is for you to describe any issues or
  suggestions related to the health benefits program.
* **Communication**: This field allows you to choose how you would like
  to communicate with the benefits administration team.
* **Benefits Admin**: This field is for you to select the status of your
  benefits administration team (if applicable).
* **Feedback**: This field is for any other feedback or
  suggestions you may have.
* **Additional Feedback**: This field is for any additional
  comments or suggestions you would like to provide.
* **Name**: This field is optional and for you to provide your
  name for the purpose of follow-up communication.
