# Remote Work Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Survey form is designed to collect information about remote work policies and practices within your organization. This data will help HR professionals and department administrators make informed decisions about updates and adjustments to these policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your remote work survey in the "Remote Work Survey" field.
2. Select your company's current work from home policy from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief description of your company's work from home policy.
4. Choose the syncing options that best fit your company's workflow:
	* Sync with Google Drive
	* Sync with Dropbox
	* Sync with Box
	* (Select all that apply)
5. Choose whether or not your company has experienced policy changes related to remote work:
	* Yes
	* No
	* Maybe
6. Select the frequency of policy changes:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Bi-Weekly
7. Enter the date of your last policy change (if applicable).
8. Briefly describe any policy changes made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Remote Work Survey Title (Text):** Enter a title for your remote work survey to help identify the purpose and scope of the survey. This title will be used for reporting and analysis.
* **Work From Home Policy (Select Multiple):** Select your company's current work from home policy. This option will help us understand your company's stance on remote work.
* **Work From Home Policy Description (Text):** Provide a brief description of your company's work from home policy. This will help us understand the details of your policy.
* **Sync Responses (Select One):** Choose the syncing option that best fits your company's workflow:
	+ Sync with Google Drive
	+ Sync with Dropbox
	+ Sync with Box
* **Sync with App (Select Multiple):** Select all the syncing options that apply to your company's workflow.
* **Policy Changes (Select One):** Select whether or not your company has experienced policy changes related to remote work:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Maybe
* **Policy Change Frequency (Select One):** Select the frequency of policy changes:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-Weekly
* **Policy Change Date (Date):** Enter the date of your last policy change (if applicable).
* **Policy Change Description (Text):** Briefly describe any policy changes made.
* **Work From Home Frequency (Text):** Enter the frequency of work from home arrangements for your employees.
