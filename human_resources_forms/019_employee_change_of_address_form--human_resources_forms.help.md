# Employee Change Of Address Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees update their address information, ensuring that your organization has the most accurate and up-to-date data about them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your company name in the provided field.
2. Enter the date you changed your address in the `Date Of Change` field.
3. Enter your old address details, including your old city, state, and country.
4. Enter your new address details, including your new city, state, and country.
5. Enter your name as it is currently recorded in your employee database.
6. Provide your employee ID for identification purposes.
7. Specify the reason for the change of address.
8. Sign the form with your employee signature, confirming the accuracy of the information provided.
9. Finally, an approver must sign the form, confirming they have reviewed the change of address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Company Name**: Enter the name of your company where you are employed.
* **Date Of Change**: Enter the date you actually changed your address.
* **Old Address**: This is where you used to live before you moved.
	+ **Old City**: Enter the city where you used to live.
	+ **Old State**: Enter the state where you used to live.
	+ **Old Country**: Enter the country where you used to live.
* **New Address**: This is where you currently live.
	+ **New City**: Enter the city where you currently live.
	+ **New State**: Enter the state where you currently live.
	+ **New Country**: Enter the country where you currently live.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it is currently recorded in your employee database.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
* **Reason For Change**: Specify the reason you are changing your address.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign this form with your signature, confirming the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Approver Signature**: An approver must sign this form, confirming they have reviewed the change of address.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any issues with your benefits, communication, and other administrative tasks.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your HR department for assistance.
- Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
