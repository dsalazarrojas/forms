# employee_exit_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees who are leaving the company, facilitating a smooth and accurate departure process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Job Title Before Departure**, **Reason for Departure**, **Manager Name**, **Manager Phone**, and **Manager Email**. These fields are marked as required, indicating that they need to be filled out.
2. If applicable, select your previous department and location from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your employee number and date information (Date of Prophets and Date Joined).
4. If necessary, provide additional comments or notes.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`<text>`, `required`): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`<text>`, `required`): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title Before Departure** (`<text>`, `required`): Enter your job title before leaving the company.
* **Reason for Departure** (`<text>`, `required`): Explain the reason for your departure.
* **Manager Name** (`<text>`, `required`): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Phone** (`<text>`, `required`): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`<email>`, `required`): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Department** (`<select_multiple>`, `optional`): Select your current department (e.g., Human Resources, Finance, Marketing, Sales, Support).
* **Location** (`<select_multiple>`, `optional`): Select your current location (e.g., HQ, Remote, Office, Home).
* **Supervisor Name** (`<text>`, `optional`): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`<text>`, `optional`): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (`<text>`, `optional`): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Date of Prophets** (`<date>`, `required`): Enter the date of your departure.
* **Date Joined** (`<date>`, `required`): Enter the date you joined the company.
* **Employee Number** (`<text>`, `required`): Enter your employee number.
* **Notes** (`<text>`, `optional`): Add any additional comments or notes about your departure.
* **Comments** (`<text>`, `optional`): Add any additional comments about your departure.
* **Previous Department** (`<text>`, `optional`): Enter your previous department.
* **Previous Location** (`<text>`, `optional`): Enter your previous location.
* **Job Title Before** (`<text>`, `optional`): Enter your previous job title.

## Tips

- Double-check the required fields to ensure they are filled out accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR representative or manager for guidance.
- This form is designed to be filled out by employees; however, if you need assistance, please ask a colleague or manager for help.
