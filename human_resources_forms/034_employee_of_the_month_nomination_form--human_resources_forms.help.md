# Employee Of The Month Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Of The Month Nomination Form is used to recognize and reward employees who have made significant contributions to the company. This form is intended to identify and celebrate the hard work and dedication of our team members. To ensure accurate and fair nominations, please make sure to nominate only once per month and for a specific employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select "Nomination Form" page.
2.  Fill in the required fields ( Employee Name, Nomination Date, Nomination Reason, Submitter Email, and Nominee Email, and Nominee Phone).
3.  Enter the Employee Name of the person you wish to nominate.
4.  Choose the Nomination Date of the nomination.
5.  Provide a brief description of why the employee should be nominated (Nomination Reason).
6.  Enter the Nominee's Email address and Phone number to contact them with the nomination.
7.  Enter your own Email address as the Submitter.
8.  Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### * Nomination Form (Page)
*  **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form_1`, text, required: false): This is the first page of the form where you select the Nomination Form option.
*  **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee you wish to nominate.
*  **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date of the nomination.
*  **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required: false): Describe in a few sentences why the employee should be nominated.
*  **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, required: false): Enter the nominee's email address.
*  **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the nominee's phone number.
*  **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as the person submitting the nomination.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Be specific and provide clear reasons for nominating the employee.
*   Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting.
