# Profit Sharing Scheme Enrollment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to enable employees to enroll in the company's profit-sharing scheme, which distributes a portion of the company's profits to its employees as a form of compensation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read through the form carefully and ensure you understand each section and field.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. For optional fields, please only provide relevant information if applicable.
4. Double-check your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details (Page 1)** (`employee_details`, text, required/optional): Provide your name, job title, and other relevant details about yourself as an employee.
* **Preferred Profit Shares (Page 1)** (`preferred_profit_shares`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your preferred profit shares option, either "Yes" or "No". This will determine your enrollment status in the profit-sharing scheme.
* **Consent (Page 1)** (`consent`, note, required/optional): Please provide your consent to participate in the profit-sharing scheme. This is a one-time agreement, and you will be able to opt-out at any time.
* **Email (Page 1)** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your company email address for further communication regarding the profit-sharing scheme.
* **Phone Number (Page 1)** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number for any urgent contact related to the profit-sharing scheme.
* **Date of Birth (Page 1)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth to help us understand your eligibility for the profit-sharing scheme.
* **Time Worked (Page 1)** (`time_worked`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you have worked with the company to demonstrate your work experience.
* **Job Title (Page 1)** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title to help us understand your role within the company.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your input carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your HR representative for clarification.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any delays or issues with your enrollment.
