# Emotional Intelligence Test Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is a tool for assessing emotional intelligence in individuals or groups, covering emotional awareness, empathy, and behavioral aspects. It's designed to be used in personal or group settings for development, coaching, or training.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, please follow these steps:

*   Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
*   Select the options that best describe your emotions, behaviors, or thoughts.
*   Take as much time as needed to provide thoughtful and accurate answers.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the administrator or facilitator for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: False): This section is an introduction to the test. Please read it carefully and take a moment to understand the purpose and context of the assessment.
*   **Emotional Awareness** (`Emotional Awareness`, `select_multiple`, required: False): This section asks about your ability to recognize and understand your own emotions. Select the options that best describe your emotional state.
*   **Recognition of Emotions** (`Recognition of Emotions`, `select_one`, required: False): This question is about your ability to recognize and acknowledge the emotions of others. Choose the emotion that you most often notice or experience in social situations.
*   **Empathy** (`Empathy`, `select_multiple`, required: False): This section assesses your ability to understand and share the feelings of others. Choose the options that best describe your empathetic behaviors.
*   **Open Ended Questions** (`Open Ended Questions`, `select_multiple`, required: False): This section involves asking questions related to your feelings and behaviors. Select the options that apply to you, and be prepared to provide more details if necessary.
*   **Self Reporting** (`Self Reporting`, `number`, required: False): This section is about assessing your self-awareness. Please enter a number that best describes how well you understand yourself.
*   **Behavioral Questions** (`Behavioral Questions`, `select_one`, required: False): This question is about your ability to ask questions about others' behaviors. Choose the option that best describes your actions.
*   **Conclusion** (`Conclusion`, `note`, required: False): This is the final section. Take a moment to review your answers and reflect on your emotional intelligence.
## Tips

*   Please answer honestly and thoughtfully.
*   Be kind to yourself and others.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
*   This assessment is meant to be a tool for growth and development. Use it to identify areas for improvement and work on becoming a more emotionally intelligent person.
