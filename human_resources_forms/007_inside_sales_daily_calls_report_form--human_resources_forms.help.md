# Inside Sales Daily Calls Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the sales calls made by inside sales representatives on a daily basis. It is used to track and monitor call activities, identify trends, and provide insights for improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID (user) in the **user** field.
2. Enter the number of call tasks completed in the **call_tasks** field.
3. Enter the number of reach-outs made in the **reach_outs** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User (user)** (number, required): Enter your employee ID as a numeric value.
* **Call Tasks (call_tasks)** (number, optional): Enter the number of call tasks completed, e.g., 10.
* **Reach-outs (reach_outs)** (number, optional): Enter the number of reach-outs made, e.g., 5. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly for better reporting and analysis.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your sales manager.
