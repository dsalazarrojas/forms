# Corporate Retreat Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about participants attending a corporate retreat. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all participants have their accommodation preferences and contact information recorded for the event organizers. This form is typically filled out by participants in advance of the retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the participant's name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select the participant's accommodation preferences from the options provided.
3. Enter the start date and arrival time for the participant.
4. Provide any additional location information if necessary.
5. Enter the contact name and email address of the participant's emergency contact.
6. If the participant is representing a company, enter the company name and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (1)
* **Participant Name**: A field to enter the participant's name.
*   Type: Text
*   Required: Not required but recommended for accurate identification.

### Participant Email (3)
*   **Participant Email**: A field to enter the participant's email address.
*   Type: Email
*   Required: Not required but recommended for communication purposes.

### Company Name (4)
*   **Company Name**: A field to enter the company name of the participant.
*   Type: Text
*   Required: Not required but recommended for company information purposes.

### Accommodation Preferences (5)
*   **Accommodation Preferences**: Select multiple accommodation options that best suit the participant's needs.
*   Type: Select Multiple
*   Required: Not required but recommended for accommodation arrangements.

### Start Date (6)
*   **Start Date**: A field to enter the participant's start date for the retreat.
*   Type: Date
*   Required: Not required but recommended for scheduling purposes.

### Arrival Time (7)

*   Required: Not required but recommended for scheduling purposes.

### Location (8)

*   Type: Text
*   Required: Not required but recommended for location information.

### Additional Location Info (9)
*   **Additional Location Info**: A field to enter any additional location information.
*   Type: Text
*   Required: Not required but recommended for location details.

### Contact Name (10)
*   **Contact Name**: A field to enter the emergency contact's name.
*   Type: Text
*   Required: Not required but recommended for emergency contact purposes.

### Contact Email (11)

*   Type: Email

### Company Name (12)
