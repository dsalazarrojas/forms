# Work From Home Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees request to work from home on a specific day. It allows employees to provide their details, reasons for requesting to work from home, and other relevant information that will be reviewed by the approved staff member.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your personal details on the first page: **Employee Info**. Enter your employee ID or name and other relevant information.
- On the second page, provide information about your home office: **Home Office Info**.
- On the third page, provide a clear reason for your work from home request: **Request Reason**. Make sure to include as much detail as possible.
- On the fourth page, enter your expected work hours: **Work Hours**. Please be accurate in your calculation as this will affect your productivity and our team's availability.
- On the final page, an approved staff member will enter their approval status: **Approved By**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Info (<employee_info>)
*   **Employee Info** (text, not required): Enter your employee ID or name and any other relevant information that may be requested by the approved staff member.
### Home Office Info (<home_office_info>)
*   **Home Office Info** (text, not required): Provide details about your home office, such as your address, contact information, and any other relevant details that will help the approved staff member in their decision.
### Request Reason (<request_reason>)
*   **Request Reason** (text, not required): Provide a clear and concise reason for your request to work from home. This could be due to a family emergency, doctor's appointment, or any other valid reason.
### Work Hours (<work_hours>)
*   **Work Hours** (number, not required): Enter the number of hours you will be working from home. This will help the approved staff member in their decision and ensure that we are able to provide the necessary resources and support.
### Approved By (<approved_by>)
*   **Approved By** (text, not required): This field will be populated by the approved staff member once they have reviewed and approved your request. Please do not edit this field.
