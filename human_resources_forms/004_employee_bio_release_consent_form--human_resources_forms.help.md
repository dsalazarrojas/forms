# employee_bio_release_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The employee_bio_release_consent_form is designed to collect information and consent from employees for creating their employee bio. This form is used to gather details such as employee information, bio, consent, date of birth, and photo.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Employee Information** section with your details as requested.
2. Write a brief description of yourself in the **Employee Bio** section.
3. Choose your consent preference in the **Consent** section.
4. Enter your date of birth in the **Date Of Birth** section.
5. Upload a photo of yourself in the **Employee Photo** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (employee_info):
	+ A text field for entering your name and any other relevant details.
* **Employee Bio** (employee_bio):
	+ A text field for describing yourself in a brief and professional manner.
* **Consent** (consent):
	+ Select one of the three options to indicate your consent for how your bio is used.
	+ Options are: Label 1, Label 2, and Label 3.
* **Date Of Birth** (date_of_birth):
	+ A date field for entering your date of birth.
* **Employee Photo** (employee_photo):
	+ A text field for uploading your photo.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling in your employee information.
* Keep your employee bio professional and respectful.
* Choose your consent preference carefully, considering how your bio will be used.
* Ensure your date of birth is entered correctly.
* Upload a clear and recent photo of yourself.
