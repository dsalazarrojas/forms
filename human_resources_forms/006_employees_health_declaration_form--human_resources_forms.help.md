# Employees Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document employee health information, medical history, and contact details to ensure a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Employee Information** - Provide your basic employee information, including your name and ID number.
2.  **Contact Information** - Enter your address, phone number, and email address for contact purposes.
3.  **Health Status** - Report your current health status, including any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are taking.
4.  **Medical Condition** - List any medical conditions you are aware of.
5.  **Medication List** - Report any medications you are currently taking.
6.  **Allergies** - List any allergies you have.
7.  **Medical History** - Report any significant medical events or treatments in your history.
8.  **Emergency Contacts** - Provide contact information for your emergency contacts.
9.  **Medical Insurance** - Report your medical insurance information.
10. **Employee Signature** - Sign the form to confirm you have completed it accurately.

12. **Reviewer Signature** - A reviewer (usually HR or management) will sign this section to confirm they have reviewed the form.
13. **Date Reviewed** - Record the date the reviewer signed the form.
14. **Reviewer Information** - Additional information about the reviewer.
15. **Medical Certificate** - A medical professional may use this section to provide medical certifications or clearances.
