# New Hire Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Information Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential details about new hires, facilitating a smooth onboarding process and ensuring that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the new hire's information, including their name, job title, job description, job category, and other relevant details.
2. Select the job status (Active or Inactive) and the start date of the new hire.
3. Choose the reporting manager's name, their title, and their email address.
4. Provide the new hire's employee ID, job code, and other relevant departmental details.
5. Enter the date the new hire started (Hire Date) and the date they will cease to be employed (Termination Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: Enter the new hire's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Job Title**: Enter the new hire's job title as it appears in the company's organizational structure.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the new hire's job role and responsibilities.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Job Category**: Select the category of the new hire's job (Active or Inactive).
  - Type: Select One, Required: No
- **Reporting Manager**: Enter the name of the new hire's direct reporting manager.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Job Status**: Select the status of the new hire's job (Active or Inactive).
  - Type: Select One, Required: No
- **Start Date**: Enter the date the new hire started their employment.
  - Type: Date, Required: No
- **End Date**: Enter the date the new hire will cease to be employed.
  - Type: Date, Required: No
- **Salary**: Enter the new hire's salary.
  - Type: Number, Required: No
- **Location**: Enter the location where the new hire will work.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Department**: Enter the department of the new hire's job.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Job Type**: Enter the type of job the new hire will be performing.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Job ID**: Enter a unique ID for the new hire's job.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Hire Date**: Enter the date the new hire started their employment.
  - Type: Date, Required: No
- **Termination Date**: Enter the date the new hire will cease to be employed.
  - Type: Date, Required: No
- **Employee ID**: Enter the unique ID of the new hire.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Job Code**: Enter a unique code for the new hire's job.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Manager's Name**: Enter the name of the new hire's direct reporting manager.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Manager's Title**: Enter the title of the new hire's direct reporting manager.
  - Type: Text, Required: No
- **Manager's Email**: Enter the email address of the new hire's direct reporting manager.
  - Type: Email, Required: No
