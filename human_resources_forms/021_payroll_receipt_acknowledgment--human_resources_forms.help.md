# Payroll Receipt Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge receipt of pay for the specified pay period. It ensures that employees accurately receive their compensation and provides a means to track and confirm pay receipt.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select the correct date of receipt in the "Date" field.
4. Choose the correct time of day in the "Time of Day" field (optional).
5. Select the pay period for which you are acknowledging receipt in the "Pay Period" field.
6. Enter the pay amount received in the "Pay Amount" field.
7. Select the date of pay in the "Pay Date" field.
8. Enter your acknowledgment of receipt in the "Acknowledgment" field.
9. Confirm that you have received your pay for the selected period in the "Confirm Pay" field.
10. Confirm that the pay period is correct in the "Confirm Pay Period" field.
11. Add any additional comments about the pay receipt (optional).
12. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
13. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
14. Select your department in the "Department" field (optional).
15. Confirm whether you have a manager who has been notified of your pay receipt in the "Manager" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you received your pay.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): If relevant, select the time you received your pay.
* **Pay Period** (`pay_period`, select_one, required): Select the pay period for which you are acknowledging receipt.
* **Pay Amount** (`pay_amount`, number, required): Enter the pay amount received.
* **Pay Date** (`pay_date`, date, required): Select the pay date for which you received your pay.
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, note, required): Enter a brief acknowledgment of receipt of pay.
* **Confirm Pay** (`confirm_pay`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you have received your pay for the selected period.
* **Confirm Pay Period** (`confirm_pay_period`, select_multiple, required): Confirm the pay period for which you are acknowledging receipt.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the pay receipt.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select your department.
* **Manager** (`manager`, select_multiple, required): Confirm whether you have a manager who has been notified of your pay receipt.
