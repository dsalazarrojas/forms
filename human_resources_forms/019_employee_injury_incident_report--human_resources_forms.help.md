# Employee Injury Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Injury Incident Report form is used to document and report incidents that have occurred during work hours or work-related activities. This form helps in maintaining a record of workplace injuries, which is essential for ensuring the health and well-being of employees. It also aids in the prompt and proper handling of reported incidents, facilitating timely and effective support for the affected employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Report Info**: This section is for providing general information about the incident, such as date, time, and location.
2. **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant circumstances or events that led to the injury.
3. **Incident Cause**: If known, specify the cause of the injury, such as a specific accident, equipment failure, or other factors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Info**
	+ **Report Info**: Enter a brief description of the incident, including date, time, and location.
* **Incident Description**
	+ **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details or circumstances.
* **Incident Cause**
	+ **Incident Cause**: If you know the cause of the injury, enter a brief explanation of what led to the incident. 

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* If the incident is still under investigation, do not speculate or guess the cause.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
