# Recruiter Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users on the effectiveness of a recruiter. It is meant to be filled out by the user after a job interview or a meeting with the recruiter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form title with a brief description of the recruiter interaction.
2. Enter the job title and job description to give context to the feedback.
3. Select the category of the job that the recruiter was assisting with.
4. Enter the job ID if available.
5. Enter the name of the recruiter you interacted with.
6. Rate the recruiter's performance with a score between 1-5 (where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest).
7. Provide any additional feedback on the recruiter's performance.
8. Decide if you would recommend working with the recruiter again.
9. Choose whether or not you submitted this form.
10. Enter the date you submitted this form.
11. If applicable, indicate which tool was used for this interaction (this is optional).
12. Choose the category for this form (this is for internal use).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: Enter a brief description of the recruiter interaction (e.g., "Meeting with John for Sales Position").
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job the recruiter was assisting with.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job the recruiter was assisting with.
* **Job Category**: Enter the category of the job the recruiter was assisting with (e.g., Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Job ID**: Enter the ID of the job the recruiter was assisting with (if available).
* **Recruiter Name**: Enter the name of the recruiter you interacted with.
* **Feedback Score**: Rate the recruiter's performance with a score between 1-5 (where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest).
* **Feedback Text**: Provide any additional feedback on the recruiter's performance.
* **Recommend to Work with**: Decide if you would recommend working with the recruiter again.
* **Submitted by**: Check if you submitted this form.
* **Submitted on**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for this form (this is for internal use).
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, indicate which tool was used for this interaction (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **Form Category**: Choose the category for this form (e.g., recruiter performance, etc.).
