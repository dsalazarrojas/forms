## Education Verification Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Education Verification Form Template is a document used by employers to verify the education details of potential employees. This form helps ensure that the provided information is accurate and complete, and can be used as a reference for employment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name and any relevant information on the "Education Verification Form" page.
2. Provide a brief description of the employee's education and qualifications on the "Description" page.
3. Fill out the employee's contact information on the "Contact Information" page.
4. Answer questions related to the employee's education on the "Education Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Verification Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which should be filled in with the title of the education verification form.
* **Description** (`form_description`, note, optional): This field is for a brief description of the employee's education and qualifications.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Email** (`employer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your company or organization.
* **Phone** (`employer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your company or organization.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee being verified.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the employee being verified.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee being verified.
* **School** (`employee_school`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of school attended by the employee:
	+ Yes: The employee attended this type of school.
	+ No: The employee did not attend this type of school.
* **Degree** (`employee_degree`, select_one, optional): Select the type of degree earned by the employee:
	+ Yes: The employee earned this type of degree.
	+ No: The employee did not earn this type of degree.
* **Graduation Date** (`employee_grad_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee graduated.
* **Graduation Month** (`employee_grad_month`, select_one, optional): Select the month the employee graduated:
	+ January
	+ February
	+ March
	+ April
	+ May
	+ June
	+ July
	+ August
	+ September
	+ October
	+ November
	+ December
* **Graduation Year** (`employee_grad_year`, number, optional): Enter the year the employee graduated.
* **Start Date** (`employee_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the employee's education.
* **End Date** (`employee_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the employee's education.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use the provided examples to help guide your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
