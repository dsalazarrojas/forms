# hotel_staff_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel staff onboarding form is designed to gather information about new employees in the hotel industry. It is typically completed by the employee's manager or HR representative during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please review each section carefully and fill in the required information for your new employee.
- Ensure that you complete all fields accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info (1)** (`Employee Info`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to capture the employee's personal details. Please enter the employee's name and any other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact (2)** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): This section is used to gather information about the employee's emergency contact. Please enter the name and any other relevant details.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact (3)** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the relationship of the emergency contact to the employee, such as John (father), Jane (mother), etc.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other (4)** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, `text`, optional): This field is used to describe any other relationship the emergency contact may have with the employee.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (5)** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email (6)** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, optional): Please enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address (7)** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, optional): This field is used to gather the emergency contact's address.
* **Manager Info (8)** (`manager_info`, `text`, optional): This section is used to capture the manager's personal details. Please enter the manager's name and any other relevant information.
* **Manager Name (9)** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Phone (10)** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email (11)** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): Please enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Relationship (12)** (`manager_relationship`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the relationship of the manager to the employee, such as Direct (e.g., team lead), Indirect (e.g., department head).
* **Manager Relationship Other (13)** (`manager_relationship_other`, `text`, optional): This field is used to describe any other relationship the manager may have with the employee.
* **Emergency Contact Note (14)** (`emergency_contact_note`, `note`, optional): Please enter any note about the emergency contact.
* **Manager Note (15)** (`manager_note`, `note`, optional): Please enter any note about the manager.
* **Employee Signature (16)** (`employee_signature`, `text`, optional): Please sign this form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Manager Signature (17)** (`manager_signature`, `text`, optional): The manager should sign this form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Date of Joining (18)** (`date_of_joining`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date the employee joined the hotel.
* **Date of Termination (19)** (`date_of_termination`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date the employee terminated.
* **Department (20)** (`department`, `text`, optional): Please enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **Job Title (21)** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Please enter the job title of the employee.
* **Employee ID (22)** (`employee_id`, `number`, optional): Please enter the employee ID.
* **Employee Status (23)** (`employee_status`, `text`, optional): Please enter the employee's status.
* **Employee Notes (24)** (`employee_notes`, `note`, optional): Please enter any notes about the employee.
