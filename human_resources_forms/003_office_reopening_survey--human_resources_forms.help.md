# office_reopening_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from employees about their plans for when the office reopens after a certain period of closure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your office location in the "office_location" field.
2. Select the date you plan to return to the office in the "reopening_date" field.
3. Enter the time you plan to return to the office in the "reopening_time" field.
4. In the "reasons_for_reopening" field, provide a brief explanation for why you plan to return to the office.
5. Select your plans for attending in the "will_attend" field. You can choose to attend, not attend, or are undecided.
6. If you are not planning to attend, provide a brief explanation in the "reasons_not_attending" field.
7. Select your plans for commuting in the "will_commute" field. You can choose to commute, not commute, or are undecided.
8. Select your plans for returning to the office in the "will_return_to_office" field. You can choose to return, not return, or are undecided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **office_location**: Enter your office location (e.g., office address or a brief description of the location).
* **reopening_date**: Select the date you plan to return to the office after it reopens.
* **reopening_time**: Enter the time you plan to return to the office after it reopens.
* **reasons_for_reopening**: Provide a brief explanation for why you plan to return to the office after it reopens.
* **will_attend**: Select your plans for attending the office after it reopens. You can choose to attend, not attend, or are undecided.
* **reasons_not_attending**: If you selected not to attend, provide a brief explanation for why you won't be returning to the office.
* **will_commute**: Select your plans for commuting to the office after it reopens. You can choose to commute, not commute, or are undecided.
* **will_return_to_office**: Select your plans for returning to the office after it reopens. You can choose to return, not return, or are undecided.
