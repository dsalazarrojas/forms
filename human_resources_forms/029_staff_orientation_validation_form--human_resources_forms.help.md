# staff_orientation_validation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information and validate the orientation process of new staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) for new employee information, contact numbers, job title, and date signed.
2. Select the relevant options for department, employee type, training status, and employee signature.
3. Enter the evaluation date and time.
4. Write any additional comments or feedback in the comments field.
5. Sign and date the form with both the employee's and supervisor's signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Employee Information** (`new_employees_form`, text, required*: This field is for entering new employee details.
* **Contact Numbers** (`contact_numbers`, text, required*: Enter the new employee's contact numbers.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required*: Enter the new employee's job title.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required*: Enter the new employee's supervisor's name.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department the new employee belongs to.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the new employee's job.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the new employee is an employee or not.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, optional): Enter any details about the new employee's training program.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the new employee is active or inactive in the training program.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the new employee's evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the new employee's evaluation.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the new employee.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the new employee.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the new employee has signed the form.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the supervisor has signed the form.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was signed.
