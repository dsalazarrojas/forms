# internal_mentorship_assignment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Mentorship Assignment Request Form is designed to request the creation of a mentorship relationship between an employee and their supervisor. This form is used to facilitate the pairing of employees with mentors, allowing for guidance, support, and development opportunities. It is crucial to ensure that the provided information is accurate and complete for a successful mentorship relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information, including your name, contact details, and manager's information.
2. Specify the start and end dates of the mentorship period.
3. Choose your mentor's name and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official personnel record.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official personnel record.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for internal communication.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or team where you work.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Manager First Name** (`manager_first_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's first name.
* **Manager Last Name** (`manager_last_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's last name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Job Title** (`manager_job_title`, text, required): Enter your manager's job title.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of the mentorship period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of the mentorship period.
* **Mentor First Name** (`mentor_first_name`, text, required): Enter your chosen mentor's first name.
* **Mentor Last Name** (`mentor_last_name`, text, required): Enter your chosen mentor's last name.
* **Mentor Title** (`mentor_title`, text, required): Enter your chosen mentor's job title.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the mentorship.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your manager's information for accuracy.
* Choose a mentor who is not in a conflict of interest, such as a direct supervisor.
* Be clear about the duration of the mentorship period.
* Be considerate of your chosen mentor's workload and availability.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
