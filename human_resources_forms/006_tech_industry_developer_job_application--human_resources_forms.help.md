# Tech Industry Developer Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Tech Industry Developer Job Application form is designed for developers who are interested in applying for tech industry job positions. It gathers information about your personal details, work experience, education, skills, and any relevant certifications or work examples.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as required.
2. Answer the questions and provide relevant information.
3. Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Application Form

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can enter any additional comments or information about your application.
### Personal Info

* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can enter your personal details, such as your name and address.
### Contact Info

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can enter your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
### Experience

* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can describe your work experience and relevant projects.
### Education

* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can enter your educational background and relevant courses.
### Work Examples

* **Work Examples** (`work_examples`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can provide examples of your work and relevant projects.
### Skills

* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): A field where you can select from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your relevant skills. Please select all that apply.
### Certifications

* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can enter any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
### Resume

* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can attach your resume or CV.
### Cover Letter

* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, `text`, required: false): A text field where you can attach a cover letter or introduction to your application.
