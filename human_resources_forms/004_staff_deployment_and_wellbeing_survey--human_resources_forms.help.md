# Staff Deployment And Wellbeing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees report their current status, overall wellbeing, and assess their job satisfaction and engagement levels. The information gathered from this form will be used to identify areas where support is needed and to provide targeted resources to help staff members thrive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the status of your current deployment from the list provided.
2. Select your overall wellbeing from the list of options.
3. If desired, provide more details on your job satisfaction.
4. Rate your current engagement level.
5. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Deployment Status** (`deployment_status`, select_multiple, Not Required):
    This field allows you to choose which of the following you are currently:
    - **Active**: Currently deployed and working.
    - **On Leave**: Currently on leave of absence.
    - **Resigned**: No longer employed with our organization.
* **Overall Wellbeing** (`overall_wellbeing`, select_one, Not Required):
    This field assesses how you are feeling overall.
    - **Poor**: I am feeling extremely unhappy or unwell.
    - **Fair**: I am feeling somewhat unhappy or unwell.
    - **Good**: I am feeling relatively happy or well.
    - **Excellent**: I am feeling very happy or well.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, text, Not Required):
    Use this field to elaborate on your level of job satisfaction, e.g. "Very dissatisfied", "Neutral", "Highly Satisfied".
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, number, Not Required):
    Rate your current engagement level (1-5), where 1 is "not engaged" and 5 is "fully engaged".
