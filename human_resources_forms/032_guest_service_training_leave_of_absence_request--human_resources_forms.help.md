# Guest Service Training Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow employees to request leave of absence for guest service training. It is used to ensure that training schedules are coordinated in advance and do not conflict with other employee training requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Leave Request` field with a brief description of your training request.
2. Enter the `start date` of your training in the `training start date` field.
3. Enter the `end date` of your training in the `training end date` field.
4. Select the type of training you will be taking in the `training type` field. You can choose one or more options from the list.
5. Select the approval status for your request in the `supervisor approval` field. You can choose one of the available options.
6. Select the department where you work in the `department` field. You can choose one option from the list.
7. Enter your name in the `employee` field.
8. Enter your employee ID in the `employee ID` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Leave Request (Required)
* **Leave Request**: A brief description of your training request.

### Training Start Date (Optional)
* **Training Start Date**: The date you will be starting your training on. This is the date that the training will start and you will be on leave.

### Training End Date (Optional)
* **Training End Date**: The date you will be ending your training on. This is the date that the training will end and you will return to work.

### Training Type (Optional)
* **Training Type**: The type of training you will be taking. You can select one or more options from the list.

### Supervisor Approval (Optional)
* **Supervisor Approval**: The status of your supervisor's approval for your training request. You can select one of the available options.

### Department (Optional)
* **Department**: The department where you work. You can select one option from the list.

### Employee (Optional)
* **Employee**: Your name.

### Employee ID (Optional)
* **Employee ID**: Your employee ID.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is only for guest service training leave of absence requests. Do not use this form for other types of leave requests.
