# Court Appearance Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request leave from an employee to attend a court proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Select the start and end dates of the leave period in the `start_date` and `end_date` fields.
3. Provide a brief description of the reason for the leave in the `purpose` field.
4. Include your employee's signature in the `employee_signature` field.
5. If necessary, add any further comments from your manager in the `manager_comment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (`employee_name`)**: Enter the name of the employee requesting the leave.
* **Start Date (`start_date`)**: Enter the date from which the employee will be absent from work due to the court appearance.
* **End Date (`end_date`)**: Enter the date until which the employee will be absent from work due to the court appearance.
* **Purpose (`purpose`)**: Briefly describe the reason for the leave, such as an upcoming court date or a related work commitment.
* **Employee Signature (`employee_signature`)**: Add your employee's signature to confirm their request for leave.
* **Manager Comment (`manager_comment`)**: If necessary, add further comments or approval notes from your manager.
Note: Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with the leave request process.
