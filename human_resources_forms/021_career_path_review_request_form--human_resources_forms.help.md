# Career Path Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assist employees in requesting a career path review with their supervisor or HR representative. This process helps employees reflect on their career goals, identify areas for development, and receive guidance and support to advance in their careers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information, and be as detailed as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your official work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Include your work phone number, including the area code.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your current department or team.
* **Current Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Direct Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your direct manager's name.
* **Years in Current Position** (`tenure_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been in your current position.
* **Years with Company** (`years_company`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Date of Review Request** (`review_request_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like to schedule the review.
* **Reason for Requesting Career Path Review** (`review_reason`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided reasons:
	+ Considering promotion
	+ Looking for development opportunities
	+ Career transition planning
	+ Performance discussion
	+ Annual development planning
	+ Skill development goals
	+ Other reason
* **Your Career Aspirations** (`career_aspirations`, text, required): Describe where you see your career going in the future.
* **Short-Term Goals (Next 12 Months)** (`short_term_goals`, text, required): List specific goals you want to accomplish in the next 12 months.
* **Long-Term Career Goals (3-5 Years)** (`long_term_goals`, text, required): Describe your long-term career goals.
* **Your Key Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): List your key strengths and skills.
* **Areas for Development** (`development_areas`, text, required): Identify areas where you would like to improve or develop new skills.
* **Skills Needed for Career Goals** (`skill_gaps`, text, optional): List any new skills or competencies you feel are required for your career advancement.
* **Training or Development Interests** (`training_interests`, text, optional): Describe any training or development programs you would like to participate in.
* **Do You Feel Ready for Advancement?** (`advancement_readiness`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Yes - ready for promotion
	+ Mostly - need some development
	+ Somewhat - significant development needed
	+ No - need more experience
* **Obstacles or Challenges to Progress** (`obstacles_challenges`, text, optional): Describe any obstacles or challenges that prevent you from advancing in your career.
* **Interest in Mentoring or Coaching** (`mentor_coach_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Yes - very interested
	+ Yes - somewhat interested
	+ Neutral - open to it
	+ No - not interested
* **Open to Lateral Moves or Role Changes?** (`lateralMove_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Yes - interested in different roles
	+ Somewhat - open to exploring
	+ Prefer vertical advancement
	+ No - want to stay in current role
* **Expected Outcomes from This Review** (`expected_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe what you hope to achieve from this review process.
* **Preferred Focus Areas for Review** (`preferred_review_focus`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable areas for review:
	+ Career progression path
	+ Skills and competencies
	+ Training and development needs
	+ Compensation and benefits
	+ Work-life balance
	+ Role responsibilities
	+ Team dynamics
	+ Performance evaluation
	+ Leadership potential
	+ Educational opportunities
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments you would like to share with the reviewer.

## Tips
- Be honest and realistic when answering the questions.
- Provide specific examples and details when possible.
- Proofread your answers for grammar and spelling errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
