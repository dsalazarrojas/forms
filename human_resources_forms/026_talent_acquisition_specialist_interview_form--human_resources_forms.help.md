# talent-acquisition-specialist-interview-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates about their skills, experience, and contact details specifically for specialist roles. Ensure that you only use this form for specialist talent acquisition purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your basic candidate information.
- Provide your contact details.
- Describe your work experience relevant to the specialist role.
- Select the skills you possess that match the specialist role requirements.
- Write your feedback and comments about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter your basic candidate information, including your name and any other relevant details. Please be as concise as possible while providing accurate and clear information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact details, including email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information. Ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and correct.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relevant work experience, highlighting any skills, projects, or achievements that align with the specialist role requirements. Be specific about your roles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the skills you possess from the list provided. Only select the skills that match the specialist role requirements. Ensure that you only select skills you are proficient in.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): Write your feedback and comments about your application, expressing your interest in the role and highlighting your relevant skills and experience. This section is your chance to stand out and showcase your personality.
