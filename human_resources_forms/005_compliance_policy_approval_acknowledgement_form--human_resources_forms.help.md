# Compliance Policy Approval Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect compliance policy approval acknowledgements from employees to ensure that they have received, read, and understood the policy requirements and their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields. Start by entering your full name as it appears in payroll, followed by your employee ID number. Then, select your department from the provided options. Provide the name of your manager or supervisor. In the policy information section, confirm the specific policy being acknowledged. Enter the date you received the policy. Check all that apply to confirm your understanding of the policy. If you have any questions or clarifications, you can provide them in the questions or clarifications field. You can also indicate if you have completed the required training for this policy. Finally, confirm your digital signature and provide any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears in payroll.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your unique identifier assigned by the organization.
* **Department** (`organization_department`, select_one, required): Select your primary department from the options.
* **Manager or Supervisor Name** (`manager_supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Policy Information** (`policy_information_section`, note, optional): Confirm the specific policy being acknowledged.
* **Policy Title and Version** (`policy_title_and_version`, text, required): Enter the policy title and version as listed in the policy document.
* **Date Policy Received** (`date_policy_received`, date, required): Enter the date you received the policy.
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement_statement`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply to confirm your understanding of the policy.
	+ I have received a copy of the policy.
	+ I have read and understood the policy requirements.
	+ I agree to comply with all terms and conditions.
	+ I understand that non-compliance may result in disciplinary action.
* **Questions or Clarifications** (`questions_clarifications`, text, optional): If you have any questions or clarifications, provide them here.
* **Training Completion** (`training_completion`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have completed the required training for this policy.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Training Not Required
* **Date of Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, required): Enter the current date of submission.
* **Digital Signature Confirmation** (`signature_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Confirm that this electronic submission serves as your official acknowledgement.
	+ I Confirm
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments you may have.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department.
