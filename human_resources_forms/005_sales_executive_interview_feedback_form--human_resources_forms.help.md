# Sales Executive Interview Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Executive Interview Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback and evaluation results from the sales executive interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Open the form and select the first page, "sales_executive_interview_feedback_form".
2.  Complete the required fields: "Candidate Name", "Interviewers Name", "Feedback Questions", "Recommendation", and "Final Recommendation".
3.  Enter the candidate's name as it appears on their business card or ID.
4.  Type the interviewers' name as it appears on their business card or ID.
5.  Provide a detailed response to the "Feedback Questions" field, including any relevant notes or comments.
6.  Enter the final recommendation, which should be based on the candidate's overall performance.
7.  Complete the overall score by selecting the corresponding number.
8.  If applicable, list the areas of improvement and provide a brief description.
9.  Complete the action plan by describing any steps you plan to take based on the evaluation.
10.  Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, required, text): Enter the candidate's name as it appears on their business card or ID.
* **Candidate Title** (`candidate_title`, optional, text): Enter the candidate's title or position.
* **Interviewers Name** (`interviewers_name`, required, text): Type the interviewers' name as it appears on their business card or ID.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, optional, number): This field is not currently used in the application, but may be used in the future for additional analysis.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, optional, date): Enter the date of the evaluation, if applicable.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, optional, time): Enter the time of the evaluation, if applicable.
* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`, required, text): Provide a detailed response to these questions, including any relevant notes or comments.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, required, text): Enter the final recommendation based on the candidate's overall performance.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, required, number): Select the corresponding number to represent the overall score.
* **Areas of Improvement** (`areas_of_improvement`, optional, text): If applicable, list the areas of improvement and provide a brief description.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, optional, text): Describe any steps you plan to take based on the evaluation.
* **Final Recommendation** (`final_recommendation`, required, text): Enter the final recommendation for the candidate.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, optional, date): Enter the date of submission, if applicable.

Note: Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and consistently to provide a clear and comprehensive evaluation.
