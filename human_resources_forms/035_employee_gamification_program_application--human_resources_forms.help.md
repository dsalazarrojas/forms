# Employee Gamification Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Gamification Program Application form is designed to help employees register for the Employee Gamification Program. This program aims to enhance employee engagement and motivation through a points-based system, recognizing and rewarding employees for their achievements and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Role.
2. Select your Team Lead and Department.
3. Choose your interests and preferences, such as gaming, sports, reading, and more.
4. Choose your availability and start and end times.
5. Confirm your consent to participate in the program.
6. Add any additional comments or comments.
7. Review and submit your application.
