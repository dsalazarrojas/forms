# HR Performance Coaching Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Performance Coaching Camp Registration Form is used to register employees for performance coaching sessions. It ensures that both the employee and the manager are on the same page regarding the coaching process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the employee's name and job title.
* **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the available goals for the coaching session.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the manager's approval status for the coaching session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the coaching session.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submission Date and Time** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Submission Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the manager's name from the available options.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the manager's title from the available options.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's name.
* **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the employee.
* **Coaching Notes** (`coaching_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the coaching session.
* **Coaching Schedule** (`coaching_schedule`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for selecting a date for the coaching session.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the coach's name.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the coach's phone number.
* **Coach Title** (`coach_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the coach's title.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the coach's email address.
* **Coach Notes** (`coach_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the coach.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.
* **Manager Approval Time** (`manager_approval_time`, `time`, required/optional): This field is automatically populated by the system.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Select the correct options for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the HR department for assistance.
