# HR Analytics Panel Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Analytics Panel Registration Form is designed to collect information from HR teams, managers, and other stakeholders for performance analysis, team management, and other HR-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out the form**: Begin by providing your participant's name and date of birth.
2. **Enter your participant's information**: Fill out the required and optional fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the participant.
3. **Review and submit**: Once you've filled out all the necessary fields, review your submission carefully and click on the submit button to save your changes.
