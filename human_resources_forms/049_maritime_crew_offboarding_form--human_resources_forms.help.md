# Maritime Crew Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maritime Crew Offboarding Form is designed to gather essential information from departing crew members, ensuring a seamless transition of knowledge and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Fill out the form with the requested details about your departure.
3. Select the applicable options for Onboarding Form Status and Training Program(s).
4. Enter your feedback and exit reasons for the offboarding process.
5. Provide your next contact information for future reference.
6. Submit the form to finalize your offboarding details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name, job title, and any other relevant employee information.
- **Exit Reasons** (`exit_reasons`, text, optional): Describe the reason(s) for your departure from the company, including any relevant circumstances or challenges you faced.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your feedback about your experience working with the company, including any suggestions for improvement.
- **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, text, optional): Enter the date you expect to leave the company.
- **Next Contact** (`next_contact`, text, optional): Provide your next contact information, including phone number, email, or address.
- **Onboarding Form Status** (`onboarding_form`, select_one, optional): Indicate which onboarding forms you have completed.
  * Onboarding Form 1
  * Onboarding Form 2
  * Onboarding Form 3
  * Onboarding Form 4
  * Onboarding Form 5
  * Onboarding Form 6
- **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_multiple, optional): Select any training programs you have participated in.
  * Training Program 1
  * Training Program 2
  * Training Program 3

## Tips
- Ensure you're providing accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate a smooth transition.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or your supervisor for clarification.
- The form will be reviewed and acted upon upon submission.
