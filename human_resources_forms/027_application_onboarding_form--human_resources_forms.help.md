# Application Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Application Onboarding Form is a crucial document that enables us to collect essential information from applicants for the purpose of evaluating their qualifications and suitability for a position. This form is used to gather details about the applicant's personal information, application details, and employment requirements. Please ensure that you have all the necessary documentation and information before filling out this form to ensure that we can process your application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out your personal information, which includes your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number. These fields are marked as required, so please ensure you fill them out accurately.
2.  Move on to the Application Details section, where you will be asked to provide information about the position you are applying for, the Application Date, and the Job Posting ID (if applicable).
3.  Next, select the type of employment you are applying for: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Temporary, or Internship.
4.  Indicate your available start date, which is a required field to ensure that we can schedule an interview or start your employment as soon as possible.
5.  Please indicate whether you have attached a resume and a cover letter, and also whether you have provided references or plan to provide them later.
6.  The Background Information and Consents sections are not required but are optional sections where you can provide additional details about your background and agreements.
7.  Finally, please indicate your consent to a background check and agree to the confidentiality agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
  - **Type**: text
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please ensure it matches your official documents.
  
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
  - **Type**: text
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please ensure it matches your official documents.

- **Email Address**: Enter your work or personal email address where you can be reached.
  - **Type**: email
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please ensure it is a valid email address.

- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
  - **Type**: text
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please ensure it is a valid phone number.

- **Position Applied For**: Describe the job you are applying for and why you are a suitable candidate.
  - **Type**: text
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please be specific about the job and your qualifications.

- **Application Date**: Enter the date of your application.
  - **Type**: date
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please use the exact date of your application.

- **Job Posting ID**: Enter the ID of the job posting you are applying for.
  - **Type**: text
  - **Required**: No
  - **Hint**: Only required for internal job postings.

- **Employment Type**: Choose the type of employment you are applying for: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Temporary, or Internship.
  - **Type**: select_one
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please select the correct type of employment.

- **Available Start Date**: Enter your available start date for the position.
  - **Type**: date
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please be realistic about your start date.

- **Resume attached**: Indicate whether you have attached a resume.
  - **Type**: select_one
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please ensure it is a valid attachment.

- **Cover letter attached**: Indicate whether you have attached a cover letter.
  - **Type**: select_one
  - **Required**: No
  - **Hint**: Only if applicable.

- **References provided**: Indicate whether you have provided references or will provide them later.
  - **Type**: select_one
  - **Required**: No
  - **Hint**: Only if applicable.

- **Background Information**: Provide any additional background information that will aid in your evaluation.
  - **Type**: text
  - **Required**: No
  - **Hint**: Only if applicable.

- **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of relevant work years you have.
  - **Type**: number
  - **Required**: No
  - **Hint**: Only if applicable.

- **Education Level**: Select your highest education level: High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, or Doctoral Degree.
  - **Type**: select_one
  - **Required**: No
  - **Hint**: Only if applicable.

- **Certifications**: Enter any relevant certifications you may have.
  - **Type**: text
  - **Required**: No
  - **Hint**: Only if applicable.

- **Consent and Agreements**: This section is not required but is a place to provide any additional agreements or information.
  - **Type**: text
  - **Required**: No
  - **Hint**: Only if applicable.

- **Background Check Consent**: Indicate your consent to a background check.
  - **Type**: select_one
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please consider the implications.

- **Confidentiality Agreement**: Indicate your agreement to the confidentiality agreement.
  - **Type**: select_one
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Hint**: Please read and agree to the agreement.
