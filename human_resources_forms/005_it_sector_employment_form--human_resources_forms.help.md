# IT Sector Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IT Sector Employment Form is a data collection tool used to gather employment information from job applicants in the IT sector.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can get in touch with you.
3. Enter your phone number to facilitate communication.
4. Select the job position you are applying for.
5. Choose whether you are a job applicant or not.
6. If you are a job applicant, attach a resume that includes your relevant experience and qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Email** (contact_email, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Job Applicant** (job_applicant, select_one, optional):
	+ Select "John Doe" if you are applying for this position.
	+ Select "Other" if you are not applying for this position.
* **Job Position** (job_position, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select "Yes" if you are applying for this position.
	+ Select "No" if you are not applying for this position.
* **Resume** (resume, note, optional):
	+ Attach a resume that includes your relevant experience and qualifications.
	+ This field is not required, but it is recommended to increase your chances of being considered for the position.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are completing this form for a valid employment opportunity.
* If you have previously submitted a form for the same position or a different position in the IT sector, please ensure that you are not duplicating efforts.
* Attach a clear and concise resume that showcases your relevant experience and qualifications.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, contact the HR department for clarification.
