> # Food Service Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of an employee in the food service department. It collects feedback on their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the employee's first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter the employee's last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
* Provide feedback on the employee's performance in the "Feedback" field.
* Enter the name of the employee's supervisor in the "Supervisor Name" field.
* Enter the date and time the form was submitted in the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Feedback** (Note, Optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the employee's performance.
* **Supervisor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor.
* **Date Submitted** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (Time, Optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
