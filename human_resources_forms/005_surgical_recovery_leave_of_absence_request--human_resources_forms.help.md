# Surgical Recovery Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence due to surgical recovery. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and efficient leave process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the **Employee Name** field.
2.  Provide the date of your surgery in the **Date Of Surgery** field.
3.  Indicate your expected return-to-work date in the **Return To Work Date** field.
4.  Select whether your manager has **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Pending** your leave request in the **Manager Approval** field.
5.  Describe the **Reason For Leave** in the text field.
6.  Attach a note from your doctor in the **Doctor's Note** field.
7.  Outline your return-to-work plans in the **Return To Work Plans** field.
8.  If desired, add any additional comments in the **HR Comment** field.
9.  Ensure that your leave request has been **Approved By** a manager or HR representative on a specific date, if applicable.
10.  Enter the date and time when you **Submitted** this form.
11.  Finally, add any additional **Comments** regarding your leave request, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name** (Text, required): This field is used to identify the employee requesting a leave of absence.
*   **Date Of Surgery** (Date, required): Enter the date of your surgery to facilitate accurate tracking of your recovery period.
*   **Return To Work Date** (Date, required): Indicate your expected return-to-work date to ensure a smooth transition back to work.
*   **Manager Approval** (Select One, required): Select one of the options to indicate your manager's approval status: **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Pending**.
*   **Reason For Leave** (Text, required): Explain the reason for your leave request to provide context.
*   **Doctor's Note** (Text, required): Attach a note from your doctor to support your leave request.
*   **Return To Work Plans** (Text, required): Describe your plans for returning to work after your recovery.
*   **HR Comment** (Text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from HR regarding your leave request.
*   **Approved By** (Date, optional): If applicable, enter the date your leave request was approved by a manager or HR representative.
*   **Submitted By** (Text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
*   **Submitted On** (Time, required): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
*   **Comments** (Text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your leave request.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
*   Attach relevant documentation, such as a doctor's note, to support your leave request.
*   Clearly outline your return-to-work plans.
*   Be respectful and concise in your communication.
