# Recruitment Plan Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recruitment Plan Approval Form is a document that captures key information about a job posting and ensures that it has been reviewed and approved by a designated approver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee ID, job title, job type, job level, department, and salary fields with the relevant information.
2. Provide a detailed job description to help the approver understand the requirements of the job.
3. Specify the job posting and close dates, if applicable.
4. Choose the job status and any additional notes if needed.
5. Fill in the approver's name, email, phone number, and signature.
6. Date and time stamps will be automatically populated upon submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID**: Unique identifier for the employee. 
- **Job Title**: The title of the job posting.
- **Job Type**: Type of job posting, select one from "Manager," "Engineer," or "Other."
- **Job Level**: Level of the job posting, select one or multiple from "Senior," "Junior," or "Entry-Level."
- **Department**: The department that the job is posted under. 
- **Salary**: The salary for the job posting.
- **Job Description**: A detailed description of the job posting.
- **Job Posting Date**: Date when the job was posted. 
- **Job Close Date**: Date when the job was closed. 
- **Job Status**: Status of the job posting, select one from "Open" or "Closed."
- **Notes**: Additional notes about the job posting. 
- **Approver Name**: Name of the person approving the job posting. 
- **Approver Email**: Email of the person approving the job posting. 
- **Approver Phone**: Phone number of the person approving the job posting. 
- **Approver Signature**: Signature of the person approving the job posting. 
- **Date Approved**: Date when the job was approved. 
- **Date Rejected**: Date when the job was rejected. 
- **Reason for Rejection**: Reason for rejecting the job posting. 
- **Comments**: Comments from the approver. 
- **Approver Comments**: Comments from the person who approved the job posting. 
- **Approved By**: Name of the person who approved the job posting. 
- **Approved Date**: Date when the job was approved. 
- **Submitted By**: Name of the person who submitted the job posting. 
- **Submitted Date**: Date when the job was submitted. 
- **Submitted Time**: Time when the job was submitted. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Double-check the information provided for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
