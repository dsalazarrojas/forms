# Junior Associate Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior Associate Evaluation Form is used to assess a Junior Associate's performance, communication, and teamwork skills, providing feedback for their growth and development within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the introduction section, providing a brief context about the individual being assessed and the purpose of the evaluation.
* Answer the performance question with a numerical value indicating how well the Junior Associate has demonstrated specific skills or abilities during the evaluation period.
* In the communication field, provide a description of the Junior Associate's verbal and written communication skills, including any notable strengths or areas for improvement.
* In the teamwork field, describe the Junior Associate's ability to work collaboratively and effectively with others, including any notable strengths or areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a brief overview of the context and purpose of the evaluation.
* **Performance** (`performance`, number, optional):
	+ Answer with a numerical value between 1-10, where 1 is "far below expectations" and 10 is "exceeds expectations" for the Junior Associate's performance during the evaluation period.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the Junior Associate's verbal and written communication skills, including any notable strengths or areas for improvement, such as clarity, tone, and ability to effectively convey ideas.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the Junior Associate's ability to work collaboratively and effectively with others, including any notable strengths or areas for improvement, such as collaboration, problem-solving, and adaptability.
