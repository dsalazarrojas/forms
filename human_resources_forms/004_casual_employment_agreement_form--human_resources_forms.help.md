# Casual Employment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casual Employment Agreement Form is a comprehensive document used for capturing essential information about employees, including their personal details, employment terms, and financial information. This form helps the organization to maintain accurate records and communicate effectively with employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have all the required information before starting the form.
2. Enter the employee's personal details, including their full name, address, email, phone number, and tax file number.
3. Provide the job title, department assignment, and expected work schedule.
4. Fill in the hourly rate and pay frequency details.
5. Confirm whether leave loading is applicable and specify the superannuation fund and member number (if applicable).
6. Enter the bank details, including the bank name, BSB number, and account number.
7. Provide emergency contact information, including the name and phone number.
8. Sign and date the agreement acknowledgment, indicating that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the employee's legal name.
*   **Employee Address** (`employee_address`): Enter the current residential address of the employee.
*   **Email Address** (`employee_email`): Enter the employee's personal email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`employee_phone`): Enter the employee's contact phone number.
*   **Position Title** (`position_title`): Enter the job title for this role.
*   **Department** (`department`): Select the department assignment for the employee (e.g., Human Resources, Sales, Marketing, Operations, IT, Finance, Customer Service, or Other).
*   **Employment Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the first day of employment.
*   **Hourly Rate** (`hourly_rate`): Enter the agreed hourly wage.
*   **Expected Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`): Describe the typical days and hours of work.
*   **Minimum Hours Per Week** (`minimum_hours`): Enter the guaranteed minimum hours if applicable (leave blank if not applicable).
*   **Maximum Hours Per Week** (`maximum_hours`): Enter the maximum allowable hours (leave blank if not applicable).
*   **Pay Frequency** (`pay_frequency`): Select how often the employee will be paid (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Semi-monthly, Monthly).
*   **Leave Loading Applicable** (`leave_loading`): Confirm whether leave loading is included in the casual rate (e.g., True or False).
*   **Superannuation Fund** (`superannuation`): Enter the name of the superannuation fund (if applicable).
*   **Superannuation Member Number** (`superannuation_number`): Enter the employee's superannuation member number (if applicable).
*   **Tax File Number** (`tax_file_number`): Enter the employee's tax file number.
*   **Bank Name** (`bank_name`): Enter the name of the bank.
*   **BSB Number** (`bsb_number`): Enter the bank branch's BSB number.
*   **Account Number** (`account_number`): Enter the bank account number.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the full name of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
*   **Agreement Acknowledgment** (`agreement_acknowledgment`): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes - I Agree" or "No - I Do Not Agree".
*   **Signature Date** (`employee_signature_date`): Date the agreement acknowledgment.

## Tips

Please ensure that all fields are accurate and completed before submission. If unsure about any field, please consult with HR or the relevant department.
