# Employee Reference Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a current or former employee's work performance, qualifications, and job history from their supervisor or HR contact. The purpose of this form is to help verify an employee's employment history and ensure that they are a good fit for a new role. This form should be completed by a supervisor or HR representative who has a professional relationship with the employee in question.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Employee Reference Check Form" page.
2. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
3. Enter the employee's job title in the "job_title" field.
4. Enter the employee's former company name in the "company_name" field.
5. Enter the supervisor's name in the "supervisor_name" field.
6. Enter the supervisor's phone number in the "supervisor_phone" field.
7. Enter the supervisor's email address in the "supervisor_email" field.
8. Enter the dates the employee worked for the supervisor in "supervisor_worked_at" field.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "supervisor_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their ID card or official documents.
* **Job Title (job_title)**: Enter the employee's job title while they worked at the referenced company.
* **Company Name (company_name)**: Enter the name of the company where the employee worked.
* **Supervisor's Name (supervisor_name)**: Enter the full name of the supervisor who will be providing the reference.
* **Supervisor's Phone (supervisor_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the supervisor who will be providing the reference.
* **Supervisor's Email (supervisor_email)**: Enter the email address of the supervisor who will be providing the reference.
* **Worked At (supervisor_worked_at)**: Enter the dates the employee worked for the supervisor.
* **Supervisor's Notes (supervisor_notes)**: Enter any additional comments or information from the supervisor that may be relevant to the reference check.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the employee's name as it appears on their ID card or official documents.
* Double-check the supervisor's contact information to ensure it is accurate.
* Be thorough in providing any additional notes or comments from the supervisor.
* This form should only be completed by a supervisor or HR representative who has a professional relationship with the employee in question.
