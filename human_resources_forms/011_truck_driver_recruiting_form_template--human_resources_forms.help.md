# truck_driver_recruiting_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The truck_driver_recruiting_form_template is a form used to collect information from potential truck drivers for recruitment purposes. It helps us understand their qualifications, experience, and other relevant details to assess their suitability for truck driving roles within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and professional details.
2. Provide accurate information about your experience and driving history.
3. Specify your vehicle preference and available work schedule.
4. Add emergency contact information, including their name and phone number.
5. Select your relevant skills from the dropdown list.
6. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your full name and contact details, including your email address and phone number.
* **Experience** (2, text, required/optional): Describe your relevant work experience, highlighting your experience with truck driving-related tasks.
* **Driving History** (3, text, required/optional): Share your driving history, including any relevant training, licenses, or certifications.
* **Vehicle Preference** (4, text, required/optional): Specify the type of vehicle you are comfortable driving (e.g., tractor trailer, delivery truck, etc.).
* **References** (5, text, required/optional): Provide references from previous employers or colleagues who can vouch for your skills and experience.
* **Contact Information** (6, text, required/optional): Enter your contact details, including your address, email, and phone number.
* **Availability** (7, number, required/optional): Specify your available work schedule, including days and hours you are available to work.
* **Emergency Contact** (8, text, required/optional): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Information** (10, text, required/optional): Provide any additional emergency information, such as their address or relationship to you.
* **Relevant Skills** (11, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant skills you possess, such as Class A, B, or C CDL.
