# Pre Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants before an interview, allowing the HR department to assess the applicant's qualifications and prepare for the interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details as requested.
2. For questions that are not marked as required, you can choose to leave them blank.
3. If you have any work experience, select the relevant options for "Work experience".
4. For questions that have multiple options, select the one that best describes your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.

### Last name
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.

### Date of birth
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### Work experience
* **Work experience** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all relevant work experiences you have.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'

### Job title
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current job title.

### Notice period
* **Notice period** (`notice_period`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of days' notice you are willing to give if you were to leave your job.

### Job satisfaction
* **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): How would you rate your job satisfaction?
	+ Options: 'Very High', 'High', 'Medium', 'Low'

### Salary
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your current salary.

	+ Options: 'Very High', 'High', 'Medium', 'Low'
