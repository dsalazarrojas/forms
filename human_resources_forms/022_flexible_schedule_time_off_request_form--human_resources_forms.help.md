# Flexible Schedule Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off from work, such as vacation or sick leave. It is designed to be completed by employees to provide necessary details for their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Flexible Schedule Time Off Request Form" link or button to access the form.
2. Select the start and end dates of your requested time off.
3. Provide schedule details, such as any specific work arrangements needed during your time off.
4. Indicate if you need to work at specific times during your time off.
5. Select whether your work schedule will be flexible or not.
6. Provide a reason for your request, if applicable.
7. If necessary, indicate if you need a manager review.
8. Select an approval status (Active or Inactive) and provide any comments, if needed.
9. Finally, enter your employee ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_date** (date, required): Enter the start date of your requested time off.
* **end_date** (date, required): Enter the end date of your requested time off.
* **schedule_details** (text, optional): Provide any specific work arrangements you need during your time off, such as meetings or tasks that need to be completed.
* **work_arrangements** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you need to work during your time off (Yes or No).
* **start_time** (time, optional): Enter the start time of your work during your time off.
* **end_time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of your work during your time off.

* **approval_status** (select_one, optional): Select the approval status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **comments** (text, optional): Provide any comments regarding your request, if needed.
* **employee_id** (number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
