# Creative Design Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information from individuals interested in the Creative Design Manager position. Please provide accurate and complete details about your contact information, work experience, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully:

1. Fill in your contact details, such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Provide your phone number (optional).
3. Enter your portfolio link (optional).
4. Select your desired position from the options listed.
5. Provide a brief description of your work experience, education, and availability.
6. If applicable, specify your start and end dates of previous jobs or projects.
7. Enter the number of hours you are willing to work and your desired pay rate.
8. Specify your notice period and a brief description of your availability.
9. Enter your expected salary and experience level.
10. List your key skills and work type.
11. If necessary, provide a job reference.
12. Enter a brief description of your work experience, availability, notice period, and pay rate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your professional email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, `text`, required: false): Upload a link to your online portfolio or website, showcasing your creative work (optional).
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required: false): Attach or upload your resume (optional).
* **Desired Position** (`position`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the position you are applying for.
	+ Options: Creative Director, Art Director, Graphic Designer, User Experience Designer, Other.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your previous work experience.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false): Describe your educational background (optional).
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, `text`, required: false): Enter the link to your online portfolio or website (optional).
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you are available to start.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of your last job or project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of your last job or project.
* **Hours of Work** (`hours_of_work`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter your desired pay rate per hour.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of days or weeks of notice you require before leaving a job.
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, required: false): Enter your desired salary.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `number`, required: false): Enter your years of experience.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, required: false): List your key skills and qualifications.
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, `text`, required: false): Describe the type of work you are applying for.
* **Job Reference** (`job_reference`, `text`, required: false): Provide a job reference for verification purposes (optional).
* **Work Experience Description** (`work_experience_description`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your work experience.
* **Availability Description** (`availability_description`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your availability.
* **Notice Period Description** (`notice_period_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the notice period you require.
* **Pay Rate Description** (`pay_rate_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe your pay rate and expectations.
