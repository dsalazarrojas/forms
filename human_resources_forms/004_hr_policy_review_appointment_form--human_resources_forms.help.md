# hR Policy Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hR Policy Review Appointment Form is designed to gather information about employees, their meeting schedules, and policy updates. This form helps HR teams manage and review meetings efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the User ID of the employee you are scheduling a meeting with.
2. Enter the Meeting Date and Meeting Time for the scheduled meeting.
3. Choose the Meeting Status from the dropdown menu to indicate whether the meeting is Available, Not Available, Rescheduled, or Cancelled.
4. If the meeting is Rescheduled, select a new Reschedule Date and Reschedule Time.
5. Leave Comments 2 (optional) for any additional notes or information about the meeting.
6. Select the Assigned Employee(s) from the dropdown menu, who will be responsible for attending the meeting.
7. Enter any Comments (optional) to provide further details about the meeting.
7. Enter the Employee Name of the person attending the meeting (optional).
8. Enter the Manager Name of the person coordinating the meeting (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID (1)** (`user_id`, required): Enter the unique ID of the employee you are scheduling a meeting with.
* **Meeting Date (2)** (`meeting_date`, required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Meeting Time (3)** (`meeting_time`, required): Enter the time of the meeting in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Policy Update (4)** (`policy_update`, optional): Enter any updates to company policies that need to be discussed during the meeting.
* **Comments (5)** (`comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Employee Name (6)** (`employee_name`, optional): Enter the name of the employee attending the meeting.
* **Manager Name (7)** (`manager_name`, optional): Enter the name of the manager coordinating the meeting.
* **Meeting Status (8)** (`meeting_status`, required): Select the status of the meeting from the dropdown menu: Available, Not Available, Rescheduled, or Cancelled.
* **Reschedule Date (9)** (`reschedule_date`, optional): If the meeting is Rescheduled, enter the new date of the meeting.
* **Reschedule Time (10)** (`reschedule_time`, optional): If the meeting is Rescheduled, enter the new time of the meeting.
* **Comments 2 (11)** (`comments2`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Assigned Employee (12)** (`assigned_employee`, required): Select the employee(s) assigned to attend the meeting from the dropdown menu.
