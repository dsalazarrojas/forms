# IT Employee Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new IT employees to help them get settled into their role efficiently. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address where you can be reached by the IT department.
2. Select the onboarding tasks that you've completed from the list.
3. If you've completed tasks not listed, type them in the "Other Onboarding Tasks" field.
4. Enter your supervisor's name and contact details if they are not already known to us.
5. Choose your department and team from the dropdown menu.
6. If required, select your shifts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Address (`email_address`):** Enter your company-issued email address that you'll be using for work-related communications.
* **Onboarding Tasks (`onboarding_tasks`):** Select the tasks that you've completed as part of the onboarding process. You can choose one or multiple tasks.
* **Other Onboarding Tasks (`onboarding_tasks_other`):** If there are any other tasks that you feel are relevant to your onboarding process, list them here.
* **Supervisor Name (`supervisor_name`):** Enter your supervisor's name. If you're unsure, you can check with the IT department.
* **Supervisor Email (`supervisor_email`):** Enter your supervisor's email address. If you're unsure, you can check with the IT department.
* **Supervisor Phone (`supervisor_phone`):** Enter your supervisor's phone number. If you're unsure, you can check with the IT department.
* **Onboarding Date (`onboarding_date`):** Enter the date you began your onboarding process. If you're unsure, use the current date as a placeholder.
* **IT Department (`it_department`):** Choose the department you belong to from the dropdown menu.
* **IT Contact (`it_contact`):** If you need to reach out to the IT department, enter their name and contact information here.
* **IT Manager (`it_manager`):** Enter the name of your IT manager. If you're unsure, check with the IT department.
* **Team (`team`):** Choose the team you belong to from the dropdown menu.
* **Shift (`shift`):** If you're on a regular shift, select it from the dropdown menu. If you're unsure, check with your supervisor. 

## Tips
* Please double-check the accuracy of the information you provide to ensure a smooth onboarding experience.
* If you're unsure about any field or have completed onboarding tasks not listed, contact the IT department for assistance. 
* This form should not be used for external purposes; it's for internal use within the company's IT department.
