# Promotion Justification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotion Justification Form is used by employees to provide a clear and concise justification and evidence to support their request for promotion. The form is designed to guide employees in preparing a well-structured and compelling submission that meets the organization's promotion criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Employee Data, including your name and ID number.
2. Provide a clear and concise justification reason for your promotion request.
3. Attach any relevant evidence that supports your promotion request.
4. Specify the name and date of your manager who will be reviewing your promotion request.
5. Specify the date and time you are submitting the request.
6. Optionally, you can add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Data**:
  * Enter your Employee ID and other required data.

- **Employee ID**:
  * Enter your unique employee ID number as assigned by the organization.

- **Justification Reason**:
  * Provide a clear and concise explanation for your promotion request, including any relevant details or circumstances that support your request.

- **Evidence**:
  * Attach any relevant evidence, such as reports, certificates, or documents, that support your promotion request.

- **Manager Name**:
  * Enter the name of your manager who will be reviewing your promotion request.

- **Reviewer Name**:
  * Enter the name of your reviewer who will be evaluating your promotion request.

- **Reviewer Date**:
  * Enter the date your reviewer will be reviewing your promotion request.

- **Reviewer Time**:
  * Enter the time your reviewer will be reviewing your promotion request.

- **Notes**:
  * Provide any additional comments or details that may be helpful for your reviewer.

- **Submitter Name**:
  * Enter your name as the submitter of this promotion request.

- **Submitter Date**:
  * Enter the date you are submitting this promotion request.

Tips:

* Please ensure that your justification and evidence are accurate and truthful.
* Please proofread your submission carefully before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any part of this form, please seek guidance from your manager or HR representative.
