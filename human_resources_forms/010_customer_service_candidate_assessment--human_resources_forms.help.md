# Customer Service Candidate Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Candidate Assessment form is used to evaluate candidates for customer service roles within the organization. This form is completed by candidates and assessors (hiring managers or other authorized personnel) to collect and document candidate information, qualifications, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Candidate Details page with your name, email, and any other required information.
2. Provide your contact information on the Contact Information page.
3. Share your work experience and availability on the Work Experience page.
4. Indicate your skills and references on the Skills and References pages.
5. Add any additional comments or notes as required.
6. Submit your assessment form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, required): Please enter your name and any other required information to begin the assessment process.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and other relevant contact details.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Describe your previous work experience related to customer service roles.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required): Specify your availability for the position, including any constraints or limitations.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select the skills you possess that are relevant to the customer service role.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide the names and contact information of at least two professional references.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes that are relevant to your application.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, text, required): Indicate the status of your submission, such as "completed" or "in progress".
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted your assessment form.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, text, required): Indicate the status of your review, such as "completed" or "in progress".
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, select_multiple, optional): Select the comments that apply to your assessment.
* **Hiring Manager Comments** (`hiring_manager_comments`, select_multiple, optional): Select the comments that apply to your assessment.
* **Final Decision** (`final_decision`, text, required): Indicate the final decision regarding your application.
* **Candidate Status** (`candidate_status`, text, required): Specify your current status as a candidate.
* **Hiring Manager Status** (`hiring_manager_status`, text, required): Indicate the status of the hiring manager's review.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required): Add any additional information that is relevant to your assessment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Add any notes or comments from the hiring manager or assessor.
* **Interview Questions** (`interview_questions`, text, required): Record the interview questions that were asked.
* **Candidate Answers** (`candidate_answers`, text, required): Record the answers provided by the candidate.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, text, required): Add your evaluation of the assessment process.
* **Interview Schedule** (`interview_schedule`, text, required): Schedule an interview with the hiring manager or assessor.
* **Interview Attendant** (`interview_attendant`, text, required): Indicate the person or team that attended the interview.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text, required): Specify the location of the interview.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, text, required): Record the time of submission.
* **Candidate Rating** (`candidate_rating`, text, required): Indicate your overall rating of the candidate.
