# Employee Digital Resources Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees regarding their access and understanding of digital resources within the organization. It's used to document employee acknowledgment of policies and procedures related to digital resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal information and acknowledgment of digital resources policies.
2. Select the correct options for the fields as instructed by your supervisor or HR representative.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (`department_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your department or team name.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your team name.
* **Assigned By Name** (`assigned_by_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who assigned you digital resources.
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options regarding your acknowledgment of digital resources policies.
* **Digital Resources Acknowledgement Date** (`employee_acknowledgement_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you acknowledged digital resources policies.
* **Digital Resources Acknowledgement Time** (`digital_resources_acknowledgement_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you acknowledged digital resources policies.
* **Assigned Digital Resources** (`assigned_digital_resources`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the digital resources you were assigned.
* **Assigned Digital Resources Date** (`assigned_digital_resources_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you were assigned digital resources.
* **Assigned Digital Resources Time** (`assigned_digital_resources_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you were assigned digital resources.
* **IT Acknowledgement** (`it_acknowledgement`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options regarding your acknowledgment of IT policies.
* **IT Acknowledgement Date** (`it_acknowledgement_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you acknowledged IT policies.
* **IT Acknowledgement Time** (`it_acknowledgement_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you acknowledged IT policies.
* **Jotform Data Collection** (`jotform_data_collection`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the options regarding Jotform data collection policies.
* **Jotform Data Collection Date** (`jotform_data_collection_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you acknowledged Jotform data collection policies.
* **Jotform Data Collection Time** (`jotform_collection_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you acknowledged Jotform data collection policies.
* **Tracking Across Teams** (`tracking_across_teams`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the options regarding digital resources tracking across teams.
* **Tracking Across Teams Date** (`tracking_across_teams_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you acknowledged tracking across teams policies.
* **Tracking Across Teams Time** (`tracking_across_teams_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you acknowledged tracking across teams policies.
* **Form Completion Date** (`form_completion_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Form Completion Time** (`form_completion_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you completed this form.
