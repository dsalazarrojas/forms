# Social Media Policy Acceptance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the purpose and completion of the Social Media Policy Acceptance Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name in the "Enter Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Enter Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Enter Phone" field.
4. Select a date on the "Enter Date" field.
5. Select a time on the "Enter Time" field.
6. Select one option on the "Select One" field.
7. Select multiple options on the "Select Multiple" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your record.
* **Enter Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Enter Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Enter Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Enter Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date from the calendar.
* **Enter Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the list.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as you can to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* Double-check the information you provide to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
