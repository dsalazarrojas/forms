# Creative Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Leave Of Absence Request form is used to request and track creative leave of absence for employees. It is a mandatory form for all employees who need to take a creative leave of absence.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill in your employee's name in the Employee Name field.
* Enter the start date of your leave in the Start Date field.
* Enter the end date of your leave in the End Date field.
* Enter the reason for your leave in the Reason field.
* Enter your manager's name in the Manager Name field.
* Enter your manager's email in the Manager Email field.
* Enter your manager's phone number in the Manager Phone field.
* Select the approval status (Awaiting Approval, Approved, or Denied) in the Approval Status field.
* Enter any comments or notes about the request in the Comments field.
* Enter your name in the Submitter Name field.
* Enter your email in the Submitter Email field.
* Enter your phone number in the Submitter Phone field.
* Enter your title in the Submitter Title field.
* Fill in the rest of the fields as necessary for tracking and recording purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your employee's name.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for your leave.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required): Enter your manager's email.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your team lead's name.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your team lead's email.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your team lead's phone number.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the approval status (Awaiting Approval, Approved, or Denied).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or notes about the request.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, `text`, required): Enter your title.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when your manager approved the request.
* **Team Lead Approval Date** (`team_lead_approval_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when your team lead approved the request.
* **Submitter Approval Date** (`submitter_approval_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you approved the request.
* **Approval Request Details** (`approval_request_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about the approval request.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments from your manager.
* **Team Lead Comments** (`team_lead_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments from your team lead.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments from you.
* **Approval Status Update** (`approval_status_update`, `text`, optional): Enter any updates about the approval status.
* **Approval Request ID** (`approval_request_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the approval request.

Note: The fields with "required" or "optional" label will be filled accordingly by the user.
