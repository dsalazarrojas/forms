# Creative Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Leave Of Absence Request form is used to request and track creative leave of absence for employees. It is a mandatory form for all employees who need to take a creative leave of absence.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill in your employee's name in the Employee Name field.
* Enter the start date of your leave in the Start Date field.
* Enter the end date of your leave in the End Date field.
* Enter the reason for your leave in the Reason field.
* Enter your manager's name in the Manager Name field.
* Enter your manager's email in the Manager Email field.
* Enter your manager's phone number in the Manager Phone field.
* Select the approval status (Awaiting Approval, Approved, or Denied) in the Approval Status field.
* Enter any comments or notes about the request in the Comments field.
* Enter your name in the Submitter Name field.
* Enter your email in the Submitter Email field.
* Enter your phone number in the Submitter Phone field.
* Enter your title in the Submitter Title field.
* Fill in the rest of the fields as necessary for tracking and recording purposes.

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your employee's name.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your leave.

* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or notes about the request.
