# HR Professional Collaboration Circle Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Professional Collaboration Circle is a platform for HR professionals to connect, share knowledge, and collaborate on best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select your role within your organization (e.g. HR Manager, Recruiter, etc.).
6. Select your department (e.g. HR, Marketing, etc.).
7. Select whether you are a team member or not.
8. Select your job functions (multiple selections are allowed).
9. Select your job title.
10. Provide a brief description of your job.
11. Choose your job level (e.g. High, Low).
12. Submit the form.
