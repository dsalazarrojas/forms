# Manager of the Year Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager of the Year Survey is an anonymous feedback mechanism that allows employees to provide feedback on their manager's performance and leadership skills. The survey is designed to help identify the manager with the highest ratings in the department, which will receive the Manager of the Year award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category that best describes the purpose of your feedback (Human Resources Forms or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of your manager's performance and leadership skills.
3. Choose the skills you would like to comment on, such as Performance, Leadership, or specific areas like Communication, Teamwork, etc.
4. Provide feedback on those selected skills, such as strengths and areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Manager Of The Year Survey Name** (`manager_of_the_year_survey_name`, text, required: false): This is the title of the survey that will be displayed on the certificate for the winner. Please provide a title that captures the essence of your manager's work and leadership style.
- **category** (text, select one, required: false): Choose whether this feedback is related to Human Resources Forms or something else.
- **description** (text, text, required: false): Briefly describe your manager's work and leadership style.
- **Manager of the Year Survey Title** (`manager_of_the_year_survey_title`, text, required: false): This is an optional title that can be used to further describe your manager's work and leadership style.
- **Manager of the Year Survey Description** (`manager_of_the_year_survey_description`, text, required: false): This is an optional description that can be used to provide more details about your manager's work and leadership style.
- **Performance and Leadership Skills** (select multiple, required: false): Select the Performance and Leadership skills that you would like to comment on (e.g., Communication, Leadership, Teamwork).
- **Feedback on Performance** (select multiple, required: false): Provide feedback on the selected Performance skills, such as strengths and areas for improvement.
- **Feedback on Leadership** (select multiple, required: false): Provide feedback on the selected Leadership skills, such as strengths and areas for improvement.
