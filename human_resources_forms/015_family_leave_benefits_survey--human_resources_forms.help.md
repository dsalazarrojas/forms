# Family Leave Benefits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand employee awareness, usage, and satisfaction with family leave benefits offered by the company. The goal is to gather data that will help HR to make informed decisions about improving the leave benefits policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply regarding your awareness of the Family Leave Act.
2. Choose whether you use the company's family leave benefits.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the current family leave benefits offered by the company.
4. Provide feedback about your experience with the family leave benefits.
5. Suggest any improvements to the current family leave benefits.
6. Confirm if your department offers leave benefits.
7. Choose if the company offers leave benefits.
8. Confirm if your supervisor offers leave benefits.
9. Select all that apply regarding the job boards used by the company.
10. Provide any company policies related to leave benefits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Awareness** (Select all that apply): Please choose all the laws or policies that you are aware of regarding family leave.
* **Usage** (Select one): Choose whether you use the company's family leave benefits.
* **Satisfaction** (Select all that apply): Rate your satisfaction with the current family leave benefits.
* **Feedback** (Select one): Provide any feedback about your experience with the family leave benefits.
* **Suggestions** (Text): Suggest any improvements to the current family leave benefits.
* **Departmental Leave Benefits** (Select all that apply): Confirm if your department offers leave benefits.
* **Company Leave Benefits** (Select one): Confirm if the company offers leave benefits.
* **Supervisor Leave Benefits** (Select one): Confirm if your supervisor offers leave benefits.
* **Job Boards** (Select all that apply): Select all the job boards used by the company.
* **Company Policies** (Text): Provide any company policies related to leave benefits.
