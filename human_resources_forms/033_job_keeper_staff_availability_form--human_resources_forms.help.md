# Job Keeper Staff Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Keeper Staff Availability Form is designed to gather information about staff members' availability and job details. This form is used to report the number of hours worked, job status, and other relevant details to support team coordination and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form pages and ensure you understand the questions being asked.
2.  Enter your job title, department, and job type as relevant.
3.  Indicate your job status, whether you are employed or not.
4.  Report your available hours, including the number of hours worked and any breaks taken.
5.  Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job title** (`job_keeper_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Department** (`job_keeper_department`, text, optional): Indicate your department or area of work within the organization.
* **Job Type** (`job_keeper_job_type`, text, optional): Specify your job type or role within the organization.
* **Job Status** (`job_keeper_job_status`, text, optional): Report your current job status, e.g., employed, not employed, etc.
* **Available Hours** (`job_keeper_staff_availability_form_page_3`, text, optional): Provide the number of hours you are available to work in a given period, including any breaks or time off.
* **Job Keeper Staff Availability Form Page 1** (`job_keeper_staff_availability`, text, optional): Report any other relevant information about your availability, if desired.
* **Hidden Talents** (`job_keeper_hidden_talents_form_page_2`, text, optional): Mention any hidden skills or talents that might be useful for your role.
