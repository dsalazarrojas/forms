# Payroll Disbursement Channel Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Disbursement Channel Application Form is used to capture employee information for payroll disbursements. This form is essential for ensuring accurate and timely disbursement of employee wages.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information for the employee.
2. Review the fields carefully to ensure you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, Optional): Enter the employee's ID number (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, Required): Choose the payment method to use for the employee's wages (Cash, Direct Deposit, Check, or ACH).
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the bank where the employee's wages will be deposited.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, `number`, Required): Enter the employee's account number at the selected bank.
* **Routing Number** (`routing_number`, `text`, Optional): Enter the employee's routing number at the selected bank.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the type of account (Savings or Checking).
* **Tax Status** (`tax_status`, `select_one`, Required): Indicate the employee's tax status (True or False).
* **W2 Form** (`w2_form`, `date`, Optional): If applicable, enter the date of the employee's W2 form.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the start date of the employee's payroll disbursement.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the end date of the employee's payroll disbursement.
* **Bank Address** (`bank_address`, `text`, Required): Enter the bank's address.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, `text`, Required): Enter the employee's address.
* **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, `text`, Optional): Enter the employee's tax ID (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the employee's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, Optional): Enter the relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's email.
* **Payroll Disbursement Channel** (`payroll_disbursement_channel`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the payroll disbursement channel (Online or In Person).
* **Payroll Disbursement Frequency** (`payroll_disbursement_frequency`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose the disbursement frequency (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payroll Disbursement Start Date** (`payroll_disbursement_start_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the start date of the payroll disbursement.
* **Payroll Disbursement End Date** (`payroll_disbursement_end_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the end date of the payroll disbursement.
* **Payroll Frequency Ending** (`payroll_frequency_ending`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the disbursement frequency ending (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payroll Disbursement Status** (`payroll_disbursement_status`, `select_one`, Required): Indicate the disbursement status (Active or Inactive).

**Tips**

* Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submission.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the payroll department for assistance.
