# New Employee Email Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about new employees, including their personal and professional details. It helps in maintaining up-to-date records and facilitates communication with the HR team.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
- Go to the form and select the 'New Employee Creation Form' option.
- Fill in the required details as prompted.
- Review the information before submitting.
- Submit the form to the HR team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name**
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the employee's first name.

### 2. **Last Name**
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the employee's last name.

### 3. **Email**
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required/Optional): Enter the employee's email address.

### 4. **Company**
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the company the employee belongs to.

### 5. **Job Title**
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the employee's job title.

### 6. **Employee Number**
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the employee's unique number.

### 7. **Department**
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Select all the departments the employee belongs to. Possible options are: Department A, Department B, Department C.

### 8. **Job Grade**
* **Job Grade** (`job_grade`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the employee's grade. Possible options are: Grade A, Grade B, Grade C.

### 9. **Date of Birth**
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 10. **Date of Hire**
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter the employee's date of hire in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 11. **Phone**
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter the employee's contact phone number.

### 12. **Notes**
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the employee.
