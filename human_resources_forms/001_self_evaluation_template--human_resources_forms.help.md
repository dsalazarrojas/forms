# self_evaluation_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self_evaluation_template form is used to collect self-evaluation data from employees. This data is used by HR departments to monitor and assess employee performance, progress, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your employee information:
	* Employee: Enter your name as an employee.
	* Manager: Enter your direct manager's name.
2. Select the relevant dates:
	* Date Start: Enter the start date of your evaluation period.
	* Date End: Enter the end date of your evaluation period.
3. Evaluate your performance:
	* Performance Area: Select 'Yes' if you met your performance goals in this area, 'No' otherwise.
	* Goals Met: Select 'Yes' if you met your goals, 'No' otherwise.
	* Feedback Received: Select all the types of feedback you received (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
4. Choose your evaluation method:
	* Evaluation Method: Select 'Yes' if this is the method of evaluation used, 'No' otherwise.
5. Add a rating:
	* Rating: Rate your performance as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
6. Add any additional comments:
	* Comment: Enter any comments or feedback you may have.
7. Enter employee and manager details (if required):
	* Employee ID: Enter your employee ID (if required).
	* Employee Email: Enter your employee email (if required).
	* Manager ID: Enter your manager's ID (if required).
	* Manager Email: Enter your manager's email (if required).
