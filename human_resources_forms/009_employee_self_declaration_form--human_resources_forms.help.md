# Employee Self Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an employee's details for HR or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Job Title, Job Type, and Relationship with Employee.
2. Fill in any optional fields, such as Job Location, Department, and Emergency Contact information, as relevant and accurate.
3. Select the correct dates of employment, shift start and end dates, work hours, and job status.
4. Enter any additional work notes in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title**: (`form_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options to describe your job type.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number, if available.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team name.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you work.
* **Shift Start Date** (`shift_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your shift, if applicable.
* **Shift End Date** (`shift_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your shift, if applicable.
* **Relationship with Employee** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options to describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, optional): Enter your work hours, if applicable.
* **Work Weekdays** (`work_weekdays`, number, optional): Enter the number of work weekdays.
* **Work Days Start** (`work_days_start`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your work days.
* **Work Days End** (`work_days_end`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your work days.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options to describe your job status.
* **Work Notes** (`work_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional work notes or comments.
