# vacation_leave_time_off_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vacation_leave_time_off_form" is used to submit requests for time off or vacations. It is intended for employees to submit their requests and for managers to review and approve or deny them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the "vacation_request" page.
2. Enter your vacation request in the "vacation_request" field.
3. Select the type of time off request in the "time_off_request" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates for your leave in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields.
5. Set the leave start and end time in the "leave_start_time" and "leave_end_time" fields.
6. Select your work status (Active or Inactive) in the "work_status" field.
7. Select your manager's approval status (Yes or No) in the "manager_approval" field.
8. Enter any comments from your manager in the "manager_comment" field.
9. Enter the date of your manager's approval in the "manager_approval_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vacation Request** (Note, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your time off request.
- **Start Date** (Date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your leave.
- **End Date** (Date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your leave.
- **Time Off Request** (Select One, required): Select the type of time off request (Full-time employee, Part-time employee, Both full-time and part-time employees).
- **Leave Start Time** (Date, required/optional): Enter the start time of your leave.
- **Leave End Time** (Date, required/optional): Enter the end time of your leave.
- **Work Status** (Select Multiple, required/optional): Select your work status (Active or Inactive).
- **Manager Approval** (Select Multiple, required/optional): Select your manager's approval status (Yes or No).
- **Manager Comment** (Note, required/optional): Enter any comments from your manager.
- **Manager Approval Date** (Date, required/optional): Enter the date of your manager's approval.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your manager for clarification.
* Make sure to submit the form before the start date to ensure timely review and approval.
