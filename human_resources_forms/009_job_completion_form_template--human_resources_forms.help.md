# Job Completion Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The job completion form template is used to capture information about completed jobs, including job titles, start and end dates, hours worked, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in the job title.
3. Enter the job start and end dates accurately.
4. Indicate whether the job was assigned or not.
5. Enter the number of hours worked for the job.
6. Add any relevant job notes.
7. Select the correct status (Active or Inactive) of the job.
8. Confirm whether the form has been submitted or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Job Title (Job title)
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required): Enter the title of the job you have completed.
### 2. Assigned To (Assigned to)
* **Assigned to** (assigned_to, select_one, required): Indicate whether the job was assigned to an individual or not. Select "Yes" if the job was assigned, otherwise select "No".
### 3. Job Start Date (Job start date)
* **Job Start Date** (job_start_date, date, required): Enter the date you started working on the job.
### 4. Job End Date (Job end date)
* **Job End Date** (job_end_date, date, required): Enter the date you completed the job.
### 5. Job Hours (Job hours)
* **Job Hours** (job_hours, number, required): Enter the total number of hours you worked on the job.
### 6. Job Notes (Job notes)
* **Job Notes** (job_notes, text, required): Add any relevant notes or comments about the job.
### 7. Employer (Employer)
* **Employer** (employer, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you were working for an employer during this job, otherwise select "No".
### 8. Job Status (Job status)
* **Job Status** (job_status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the job as "Active" or "Inactive".
### 9. Form Submitted (Form submitted)
* **Form Submitted** (form_submitted, select_one, required): Indicate whether the form has been submitted or not. Select "Yes" if the form has been submitted, otherwise select "No".
