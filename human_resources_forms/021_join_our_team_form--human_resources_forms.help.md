# Join Our Team Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Join Our Team Form is designed to collect applications from candidates interested in joining your organization. This form allows candidates to provide necessary information about themselves, their qualifications, and experience. It's essential to fill out this form to be considered for job openings within your company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the job title you are applying for in the **Job Application** field.
2. Describe your relevant experience and qualifications for the job in the **Job Description** field.
3. Attach your Resume, which will be reviewed as part of the application process.
4. Write a brief introduction in the **Cover Letter** field, explaining why you're a great fit for the job and our company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application** (application_info): Enter the job title of the position you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (job_description): Provide a concise summary of your relevant experience and qualifications for the job. This will help us understand how your skills and background align with the position.
* **Resume** (resume): Attach a copy of your resume, which will be reviewed as part of the application process.
* **Cover Letter** (cover_letter): Write a brief introduction explaining why you're a great fit for the job and our company. This is your chance to showcase your interest and enthusiasm for the position.
