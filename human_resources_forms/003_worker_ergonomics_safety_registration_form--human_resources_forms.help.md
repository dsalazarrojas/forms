# Worker Ergonomics Safety Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and fill it out completely and accurately.
2. Enter your worker's information in the designated fields.
3. Select the relevant options for job classification, shift pattern, and work start/finish times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`worker_ergonomics_safety_registration_form`, text, required: false): This field is for entering any additional comments or remarks.
* **Personal Information** (`worker_ergonomics_safety_registration_form_2`, text, required: false): This field is for entering personal information not already covered in other fields.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the worker's job title.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required: false): Enter the worker's work location.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, select_one, required: false): Select the job classification that best suits your worker's job. Choose from the available options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, text, required: false): Enter your worker's standard working hours.
* **Shift Pattern** (`shift_pattern`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable shift patterns for your worker. Choose from:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Work Start Time** (`work_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter your worker's start time.
* **Work Finish Time** (`work_finish_time`, time, required: false): Enter your worker's finish time.
* **Job Classification Other** (`job_classification_other`, text, required: false): If not already covered in other fields, enter any additional job classification information here.
* **Work Location Other** (`work_location_other`, text, required: false): If not already covered in other fields, enter any additional work location information here.
* **Job Title Other** (`job_title_other`, text, required: false): If not already covered in other fields, enter any additional job title information here.
