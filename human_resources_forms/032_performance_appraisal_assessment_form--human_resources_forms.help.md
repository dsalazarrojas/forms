# Performance Appraisal Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Appraisal Assessment Form is designed to help employees evaluate their own performance, providing a structured approach to self-assessment and review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the provided field.
2. Select your Job Title from the available options.
3. Choose your Reporting Manager from the list of available managers.
4. Select one or more Performance Areas that you have demonstrated during your tenure.
5. Describe the Performance Criteria you have met or improved upon.
6. Rate your Overall Performance as Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement.
7. Enter the Date of your last evaluation.
8. Enter the Date of your next evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee Name**
* Label: Employee Name
* Type: Text Input
* Required: False
* Explanation: Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.

### 2. **Job Title**
* Label: Job Title
* Type: Text Input
* Required: False
* Explanation: Provide your current job title as it is reflected in your official records.

### 3. **Reporting Manager**
* Label: Reporting Manager
* Type: Select One
* Required: False
* Explanation: Choose the manager who supervises your work from the available options.

### 4. **Performance Areas**
* Label: Performance Areas
* Type: Select Multiple
* Required: False
* Explanation: Select one or more areas where you have demonstrated strong performance during your tenure. Options include:
	+ Communication
	+ Teamwork
	+ Adaptability
	+ Time Management
	+ Leadership
	+ Initiative
	+ Problem Solving

### 5. **Performance Criteria**
* Label: Performance Criteria
* Type: Text Input
* Required: False
* Explanation: Describe any notable performance criteria you have met or improved upon.

### 6. **Overall Performance**
* Label: Overall Performance
* Type: Select One
* Required: False
* Explanation: Rate your performance as Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement.

### 7. **Date Last Evaluation**
* Label: Date Last Evaluation
* Type: Date Input
* Required: False
* Explanation: Enter the date of your last performance evaluation.

### 8. **Date Next Evaluation**
* Label: Date Next Evaluation
* Type: Date Input
* Required: False
* Explanation: Enter the date of your next scheduled performance evaluation.
