# Cost Of Living Adjustment Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a cost of living adjustment. Please use this form to submit a formal request for a cost of living adjustment due to changes in the cost of living in your area.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your employee information in the "Employee Information" field.
2. Select the type of request you are submitting ("Request Type").
3. Describe the reason for your request in the "Reason" field.
4. Enter the request amount and effective date if necessary.
5. Enter your manager's confirmation and confirmation date, if applicable.
6. Enter your confirmation and confirmation date, if applicable.
7. Select your department and location, if applicable.
8. Enter any additional comment about your request.
9. Select the status of your manager's confirmation and your confirmation, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (employee, text, required): Please enter your name and ID number to identify yourself as the requestor.
* **Request Type** (request_type, select_one, required): Select the type of request you are submitting. This will help us to process your request more efficiently.
* **Reason** (reason, text, required): Please describe the reason for your request. This will help us to understand your needs and process your request.
* **Request Amount** (request_amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the adjustment you are requesting. If you are unsure about the amount, please provide an estimate or a range.
* **Effective Date** (effective_date, date, required): Enter the date when you would like the adjustment to be effective. This will help us to process your request accordingly.
* **Manager Confirmation** (manager_confirmation, text, required): Please enter your manager's confirmation of your request. This is a requirement for us to process your request.
* **Manager Confirmation Date** (manager_confirmation_date, date, required): Enter the date when your manager confirmed your request. This is a requirement for us to process your request.
* **Employee Confirmation** (employee_confirmation, text, required): Please enter your confirmation of your request. This is a requirement for us to process your request.
* **Employee Confirmation Date** (employee_confirmation_date, date, required): Enter the date when you confirmed your request. This is a requirement for us to process your request.
* **Department** (department, select_one, required): Select your department from the options provided. This will help us to process your request more efficiently.
* **Location** (location, text, required): Enter the location where you live. This will help us to understand your needs and process your request.
* **Comment** (comment, text, required): Please enter any additional comment or information about your request.
* **Manager Confirmation Status** (manager_confirmation_status, select_one, required): Select the status of your manager's confirmation. This will help us to know how to proceed with your request.
* **Employee Confirmation Status** (employee_confirmation_status, select_one, required): Select the status of your confirmation. This will help us to know how to proceed with your request.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the HR department for assistance.
* Please note that this form is used to process a cost of living adjustment request. If you are requesting a different type of adjustment, please use a different form.
