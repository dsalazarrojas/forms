# Food Service Exit Interview Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Service Exit Interview Employment Form is a tool to gather feedback from employees who are leaving or have left the food service employment. The data collected from this form will help the company to understand the reasons behind employee departures, improve job satisfaction, provide better growth opportunities, and foster a positive company culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please select the reasons why you are leaving the food service employment.
2. Choose the reasons why you were employed in the food service.
3. Select the description of the company culture you experienced.
4. Choose how you experienced the relationship with your manager.
5. Select your job satisfaction level.
6. Choose the growth opportunities you experienced while being employed in the food service.
7. Select the company values you experienced while being employed.
8. Finally, provide your recommendation for the food service company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Service Exit Interview** (page 1):
	+ This is an introductory note to explain the purpose of the form.
* **reason_for_leaving** (page 2):
	+ Please select all the reasons why you are leaving the food service employment.
	+ You may choose one or more options.
* **why_employed** (page 3):
	+ Please select the description of why you were employed in the food service.
	+ You may choose one option.
* **company_culture** (page 4):
	+ Please select the description of the company culture you experienced.
	+ You may choose one option.
* **manager_relationship** (page 5):
	+ Please select all the ways you experienced the relationship with your manager.
	+ You may choose one or more options.
* **job_satisfaction** (page 6):
	+ Please select all the ways you experienced your job satisfaction level.
	+ You may choose one or more options.
* **growth_opportunities** (page 7):
	+ Please select all the growth opportunities you experienced while being employed in the food service.
	+ You may choose one or more options.
* **company_values** (page 8):
	+ Please select all the company values you experienced while being employed in the food service.
	+ You may choose one or more options.
* **recommendation** (page 9):
	+ Please provide your recommendation for the food service company.
* **Form End** (page 10):
	+ This is a note to complete the form.

## Tips
* Answer truthfully and thoroughly to provide the most useful information to the company.
* You may select one or more options for each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form's administrators.
