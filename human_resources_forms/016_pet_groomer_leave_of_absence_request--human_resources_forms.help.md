# Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Request" form is used to request a leave of absence for pet groomers. This form helps you inform your manager and HR department about your leave of absence, allowing them to review and process your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting the reason for your leave from the options provided.
2. Enter the start date and end date for your leave.
3. Select the status of your leave from the options provided.
4. Add any additional comments or information for your manager's attention.
5. Finally, select the covering staff member(s) that will take care of your duties while you're on leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Request Form**: This is the main form that requests a leave of absence for pet groomers.
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Details**: Select the reason for your leave from the options provided (e.g., "Request", "Leave", "Other").
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Reason**: Select the reason for your leave (e.g., "Vacation", "Sick", "Other").
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence End Date**: Enter the date your leave will end.
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Duration**: Enter the duration of your leave.
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Status**: Select the status of your leave from the options provided (e.g., "Approved", "Denied", "Pending").
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Manager Comment**: Add any additional comments or information for your manager's attention.
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Covering Staff**: Select the covering staff member(s) that will take care of your duties while you're on leave.
* **Pet Groomer Leave Of Absence Covering Staff Availability**: Select the availability of the covering staff member(s) (e.g., "Available", "Unavailable").
