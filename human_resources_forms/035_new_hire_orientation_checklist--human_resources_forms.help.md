# New Hire Orientation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Orientation Checklist is a form designed to collect information about new employees to facilitate a smooth onboarding process. This form is meant to gather details about the new hire's job, schedule, and benefits eligibility, among other things.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your new hire information, including job title and department, to help us get you started.
* Enter your job start date to ensure we capture the correct date of your employment.
* Describe your work schedule, including your hours of work and any time off you may have.
* Enter your supervisor's name and email address so we can reach out to them for assistance.
* Provide a brief description of your job, highlighting key responsibilities and expectations.
* Check your benefits eligibility and indicate your status (e.g., full-time, part-time, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Information**
  (new_hire_information, text, required/optional):
    * This field is used to capture your name, address, and any other relevant information about your employment.
* **Job Title**
  (job_title, text, required/optional):
    * Enter your job title as per your job description.
* **Department**
  (department, text, required/optional):
    * Enter the department you belong to in the company.
* **Job Start Date**
  (job_start_date, date, required/optional):
    * Enter the date you started your new job.
* **Work Schedule**
  (work_schedule, text, required/optional):
    * Describe your work schedule, including your hours of work and any time off you may have.
* **Supervisor Name**
  (supervisor_name, text, required/optional):
    * Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email**
  (supervisor_email, email, required/optional):
    * Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Job Description**
  (job_description, text, required/optional):
    * Provide a brief description of your job, highlighting key responsibilities and expectations.
* **Benefits Eligibility**
  (benefits_eligibility, text, required/optional):
    * Check your benefits eligibility to see what you're entitled to.
* **Job Status**
  (job_status, text, required/optional):
    * Indicate your job status, e.g., full-time, part-time, etc.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your HR representative for assistance.
* Make sure to review and understand your benefits eligibility to know what you're entitled to.
