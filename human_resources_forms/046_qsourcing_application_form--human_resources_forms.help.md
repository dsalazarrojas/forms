# Qsourcing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide individuals through the Qsourcing application process, providing a clear and simple way to submit their job application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the basic contact information fields, including User Name, phone number, and email.
2. Select the Job Title and Job Type that match your current position or interest.
3. Choose the Job Category and Job Level that best fit your skills and experience.
4. Select the Job Type 2 and Job Status that apply to your situation.
5. Optionally, provide more details about your Work Experience, Job Description, and Job Category 2.
6. Attach your Resume and Application Comments if needed.
7. Finally, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your application.
- **Job Title (2)**: Select the current or desired Job Title that best fits your position.
- **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Job Application Date (5)**: Enter the date you are submitting your application.
- **Phone Number (6)**: Enter your phone number.
- **Email (7)**: Enter your email address.
- **Address (8)**: Enter your address.
- **Resume (9)**: Attach your Resume or CV.
- **Job Description (10)**: Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
- **Job Category (11)**: Select the Category that best fits your job.
- **Job Level (12)**: Select the Level that best fits your experience.
- **Job Type 2 (13)**: Select the Type 2 that best fits your job requirements.
- **Work Experience (14)**: Provide details about your work experience.
- **Job Application Status (15)**: Select the status of your application.
- **Application Comments (16)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
- **Job Status (17)**: Select the current status of your job.
- **Job Category 2 (18)**: Select the Category 2 that best fits your job.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your application before submitting it to ensure all required and optional fields are completed accurately.
- Attach a professional Resume or CV to showcase your skills and experience.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the Job Description field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
