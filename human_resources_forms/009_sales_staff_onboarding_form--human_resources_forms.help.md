# sales_staff_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The sales staff onboarding form is used to onboard new sales staff members to the company's sales team. It collects essential data for their profile, sales channels, and reporting relationships.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the personal details: First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Provide your sales title, company, and manager's name for reference and reporting purposes.
3. Specify your sales channel, sales region, and sales product for which you are responsible.
4. If you have a reporting manager, please provide their name, email, and phone number for proper reporting and escalation purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your personal name as a sales staff member.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as a sales staff member.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid work email for reference and reporting purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your sales title.
* **Company** (Company, text, optional): Enter the name of your current company for reference.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, optional): Enter the name of your direct manager for reporting purposes.
* **Manager Email** (Manager Email, email, optional): Enter the email of your direct manager for reporting purposes.
* **Manager Phone** (Manager Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your direct manager for reporting purposes.
* **Sales Channel** (Sales Channel, text, required): Enter the sales channel you are responsible for (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
* **Sales Region** (Sales Region, text, required): Enter your sales region.
* **Sales Product** (Sales Product, text, required): Enter the sales product you are responsible for.
* **Sales Team** (Sales Team, text, required): Enter your sales team for which you are responsible.
* **Reporting Manager** (Reporting Manager, text, optional): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (Reporting Manager Email, email, optional): Enter the email of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Phone** (Reporting Manager Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Title** (Reporting Manager Title, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's title for reference.
* **Reporting Manager Company** (Reporting Manager Company, text, optional): Enter the reporting manager's company for reference.
* **Reporting Manager Sales Channel** (Reporting Manager Sales Channel, text, optional): Enter the sales channel of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Sales Region** (Reporting Manager Sales Region, text, optional): Enter the sales region of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Sales Product** (Reporting Manager Sales Product, text, optional): Enter the sales product of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Sales Team** (Reporting Manager Sales Team, text, optional): Enter the sales team of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Territory** (Reporting Manager Territory, text, optional): Enter the territory of your reporting manager.
* **Other (please describe)** (Other, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details about your reporting manager.
* **Territory** (Territory, text, required): Enter the territory you are responsible for.
* **Reporting Manager Other (please describe)** (Reporting Manager Other, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details about your reporting manager.
