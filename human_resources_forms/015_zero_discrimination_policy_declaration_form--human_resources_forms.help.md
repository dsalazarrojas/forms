# Zero Discrimination Policy Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your personal experiences with discrimination, demographic characteristics, and language and country of origin. Your input will help us assess the effectiveness of our anti-discrimination measures and policies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and title.
2. Enter your organization's name and address.
3. Provide your participant's name and contact information (email, phone, and date of birth).
4. Select your participant's sex, race/ethnicity, and gender identity.
5. Indicate your LBGTIQ identity and disability status.
6. Select your languages spoken.
7. Select your country of birth and country of residence.
8. Specify the length of time you've lived in your current country of residence.
9. Indicate if you've ever moved from your birth country to another country.
10. Indicate if you've ever moved from one city to another.
11. Indicate if you've ever moved from one state to another.
12. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Zero Discrimination Policy Declaration Form** (`title`, required): Enter the title of this form as it appears in your institution's policies.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, required): Enter the address of your institution.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, required): Enter your title or position within your institution.
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, required): Enter the department where you work.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, required): Enter your official phone number.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participant Sex** (`participant_sex`, required): Select your sex from the provided options.
* **Participant Race/Ethnicity** (`participant_race_ethnicity`, required): Select your race/ethnicity from the provided options.
* **Participant Gender Identity** (`participant_gender_identity`, required): Select your gender identity from the provided options.
* **Participant LBGTIQ Identity** (`participant_lbgtiq_identity`, required): Select your LBGTIQ identity from the provided options.
* **Participant Disability Status** (`participant_disability_status`, required): Select your disability status from the provided options.
* **Participant Discrimination Status** (`participant_discrimination_status`, required): Select your discrimination status from the provided options.
* **Participant Language** (`participant_language`, required): Select your languages spoken from the provided options.
* **Participant Native Language** (`participant_native_language`, required): Select your native language from the provided options.
* **Participant Birth Country** (`participant_birth_country`, required): Select your country of birth from the provided options.
* **Participant Country of Residence** (`participant_country_of_residence`, required): Select your country of residence from the provided options.
* **Participant Residence Country Since** (`participant_residence_country_since`, required): Select the length of time you've lived in your current country of residence from the provided options.
* **Participant Ever Moved Country** (`participant_ever_moved_country`, required): Indicate if you've ever moved from your birth country to another country from the provided options.
* **Participant Ever Moved City** (`participant_ever_moved_city`, required): Indicate if you've ever moved from one city to another from the provided options.
* **Participant Ever Moved State** (`participant_ever_moved_state`, required): Indicate if you've ever moved from one state to another from the provided options.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, optional): Sign and date this form to confirm your declaration.
