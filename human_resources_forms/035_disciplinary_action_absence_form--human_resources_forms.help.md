# Disciplinary Action Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Action Absence Form is used to document and record disciplinary actions taken against employees for absences. This form captures details from both the manager and the employee to ensure a clear understanding of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Comments from Manager**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the reason for the absence.
2. **Enter Response from Employee**: Ask the employee to respond to the incident, providing their understanding of what happened.
3. **Enter Comments from Employee**: Allow the employee to provide their comments on the incident, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the incident from the manager's perspective.
* **Employee Response** (`employee_response`, `text`, required): Ask the employee to respond to the incident, providing their understanding of what happened.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, `text`, required): Allow the employee to provide their comments on the incident, if any.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise when filling out each field.
- Ensure that your comments accurately reflect your understanding of the incident.
- If the employee does not respond or provide comments, please note this in the corresponding field.
