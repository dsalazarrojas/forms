# Job Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess your job satisfaction and provide feedback on your work environment and company values.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll down to each page and select the corresponding rating for each field.
2. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can add them in the Open Comments field.
3. Finally, click submit to submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Satisfaction** (Page 1)
  * A number between 1 and 5 that represents your overall satisfaction with your job. The higher the number, the more satisfied you are.
* **Work Life** (Page 1)
  * A number between 1 and 5 that represents your satisfaction with your work life balance. The higher the number, the more balanced you feel in your work and personal life.
* **Engagement Level** (Page 2)
  * A text field where you can describe your level of engagement with your job. For example, "High" if you're very engaged, or "Low" if you're not very engaged.
* **Manager Satisfaction** (Page 2)
  * A text field where you can describe your satisfaction with your manager. For example, "Very Satisfied" if you're happy with your manager, or "Not Satisfied" if you're not.
* **Work Life Satisfaction** (Page 3)
  * A text field where you can describe your satisfaction with your work life balance. For example, "Very Satisfied" if you feel balanced between work and personal life, or "Not Satisfied" if you feel out of balance.
* **Work Environment** (Page 3)
  * A text field where you can describe your satisfaction with your work environment. For example, "Very Good" if you like your workspace, or "Not Good" if you don't.
* **Communication** (Page 4)
  * A text field where you can describe your satisfaction with communication in your team or department. For example, "Very Good" if you like the way your team communicates, or "Not Good" if you don't.
* **Support** (Page 4)
  * A text field where you can describe your satisfaction with the support you receive from your team or department. For example, "Very Good" if you feel supported, or "Not Good" if you don't.
* **Growth Opportunities** (Page 5)
  * A text field where you can describe your opportunities for growth and development in your job. For example, "Many" if you feel there are plenty of opportunities, or "Few" if you don't see many.
* **Team Spirit** (Page 5)
  * A text field where you can describe your satisfaction with your team's spirit and collaboration. For example, "Very Good" if you like your team's dynamic, or "Not Good" if you don't.
* **Company Values** (Page 6)
  * A text field where you can describe your satisfaction with your company values. For example, "Very Good" if you align with the company values, or "Not Good" if you don't.
* **Job Satisfaction Rating** (Page 7)
