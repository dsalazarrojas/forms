# Medical Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical staff application information for processing employment or affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please fill in your **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email** details accurately.
2. Provide your **Phone** number to contact you.
3. Select your **License type**, **Education**, **Languages**, **Licensed countries**, and **Licenses** relevant to your medical profession.
4. If applicable, fill in your **Experience**, **Licenses issued**, **Start date**, and **End date**.
5. Provide a clear **Reason** for your application.
6. Include any additional **Comments** you would like to add to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`medical_staff_application_form_first_name`, text, required): Your given name.
* **Last name** (`medical_staff_application_form_last_name`, text, required): Your family name.
* **Email** (`medical_staff_application_form_email`, email, required): A contact email address.
* **Phone** (`medical_staff_application_form_phone`, text, optional): A contact phone number.
* **License type** (`medical_staff_application_form_license_type`, select_one, optional): The type of medical license you hold.
* **Education** (`medical_staff_application_form_education`, select_multiple, optional): Your medical education background.
* **Languages** (`medical_staff_application_form_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Languages you are proficient in for medical practice.
* **Licensed countries** (`medical_staff_application_form_licensed_countries`, select_multiple, optional): Countries where you hold a medical license.
* **Licenses** (`medical_staff_application_form_licenses`, select_multiple, optional): Licenses you hold.
* **Experience** (`medical_staff_application_form_experience`, time, optional): Your medical work experience duration.
* **Licenses issued** (`medical_staff_application_form_licenses_issued`, date, optional): Date when you were issued a medical license.
* **Start date** (`medical_staff_application_form_start_date`, date, optional): Date you began working as a medical professional.
* **End date** (`medical_staff_application_form_end_date`, date, optional): Date you ended your medical practice or work.
* **Reason** (`medical_staff_application_form_reason`, text, optional): A reason for your application.
* **Comments** (`medical_staff_application_form_comments`, text, optional): Additional comments for your application.

Please note that all fields marked as "optional" can be filled in if relevant, but are not required for the application to be considered complete.
