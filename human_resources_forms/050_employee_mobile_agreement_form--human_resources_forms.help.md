# Employee Mobile Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Mobile Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of employee mobile usage. It ensures that employees understand their responsibilities and expectations when using company-provided mobile devices. This form should only be completed by employees who require a mobile device for work purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant option from the dropdown menu for the employee mobile agreement form.
2. Enter your agreement date in the format of MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
3. Type your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
4. Enter the name of the company that you are representing.
5. Enter your job title or role within the company.
6. Select the status of your agreement, such as Active, Inactive, or Resigned.
7. Sign your name in the designated box, indicating your acceptance of the terms.
8. Enter your email address, used for official communications.
9. Enter your phone number, used for contact purposes.
10. Enter the date of your signature in the format of MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
11. Enter the date of termination of your agreement in the format of MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_mobile_agreement_form**: <br>
	+ Select the option that best describes your mobile agreement.
* **agreement_date**: <br>
	+ Enter the date on which you agree to the terms of the mobile usage policy.
* **employee_name**: <br>
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **company_name**: <br>
	+ Enter the name of the company that you represent.
* **employee_title**: <br>
	+ Enter your job title or role within the company.
* **agreement_status**: <br>
	+ Select the status of your mobile agreement, such as Active, Inactive, or Resigned.
* **signature**: <br>
	+ Sign your name, indicating your acceptance of the terms and conditions of the mobile usage policy.
* **email**: <br>
	+ Enter your official email address, used for company communications.
* **phone_number**: <br>
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **date_of_signature**: <br>
	+ Enter the date on which you signed this agreement.
* **date_of_termination**: <br>
	+ Enter the date on which your mobile agreement will end.

## Tips

* Please review the terms and conditions of the mobile usage policy before signing the agreement.
* Ensure that you have read and understood all the terms before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
