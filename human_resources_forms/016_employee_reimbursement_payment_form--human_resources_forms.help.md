# employee_reimbursement_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request reimbursement payment from employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you want to request reimbursement for 'Yes' or 'No'. If you select 'Yes', you will be presented with additional fields to complete.
2. Enter the date of the expense in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Enter your employee ID number.
4. Select the type of expense you are requesting reimbursement for from the options provided.
5. Enter the amount of the expense.
6. Choose how you would like to receive your payment from the options provided.
7. Enter the email address of the person who will be submitting the request.
8. Enter the email address of the person who will be receiving the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_reimbursement_payment_form_fields**: This is a multiple-choice question asking whether you are requesting reimbursement. Select 'Yes' to proceed with the form.
* **date**: Enter the date of the expense in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **employee_id**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **expense_type**: Select the type of expense you are requesting reimbursement for from the options provided.
* **amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **payment_method**: Choose how you would like to receive your payment from the options provided.
* **submitted_by**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **submitted_to**: Enter the email address of the person receiving the request.
