# Flexible Working Arrangement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flexible Working Arrangement Form is used to document the arrangement made between an employee and their manager to adjust their working arrangement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee and manager involved in the arrangement.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the arrangement.
3. Specify the flexible time and flexible days of the arrangement.
4. Record the approvals and comments from both employee and manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Work Arrangement** (`work_arrangement`): Enter a short description of the arrangement.
- **Work Arrangement Status** (`work_arrangement_status`): Enter the status of the arrangement (e.g., approved, denied, pending).
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of the manager involved in the arrangement.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the name of the employee involved in the arrangement.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter the employee ID number.
- **Manager ID** (`manager_id`): Enter the manager ID number.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the arrangement.
- **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of the arrangement.
- **Flexible Time** (`flexible_time`): Enter the flexible time arrangement.
- **Flexible Days** (`flexible_days`): Select the days when the employee is flexible.
- **Work Arrangement Status Comment** (`work_arrangement_status_comment`): Enter any comments regarding the status of the arrangement.
- **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`): Enter any comments from the manager.
- **Employee Comment** (`employee_comment`): Enter any comments from the employee.
- **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Enter the date when the arrangement was approved or denied.
- **Approved By** (`approved_by`): Indicate whether the arrangement was approved or denied.
- **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`): Select the managers who approved the arrangement.
- **Manager Approvals List** (`manager_approvals_list`): List the managers who approved the arrangement.
- **Employee Approvals** (`employee_approvals`): Indicate whether the employee approved the arrangement.
- **Employee Approvals List** (`employee_approvals_list`): List the employees who approved the arrangement.
