# Employee Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Availability Form is used to record and update an employee's availability for work shifts. This form helps ensure that employees can be contacted and managed efficiently during work hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and any other relevant details.
2.  Select your start and end dates for work shifts.
3.  Choose your start and end times for work shifts.
4.  Select the days you are available to work.
5.  Add any additional details such as shift length, break length, and work hours.
6.  If you have a manager, enter their name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Your name, as recorded in our system.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Start Date**: The date you are available to start work.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: Yes
* **End Date**: The date you are available to end work.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Start Time**: The time you are available to start work.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: Yes
* **End Time**: The time you are available to end work.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: Yes
* **Available Days**: Select the days you are available to work.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
	+ Required: No
* **Start Time (AM/PM)**: Choose AM or PM for your start time.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: AM, PM
	+ Required: No
* **End Time (AM/PM)**: Choose AM or PM for your end time.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: AM, PM
	+ Required: No
* **Other**: Enter any other details about your availability.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Shift Length**: Enter the length of your shift.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Break Length**: Enter the length of your break.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Work Hours**: Enter the number of work hours.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Break**: Choose your break duration.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: None, 30 minutes, 60 minutes, 90 minutes
	+ Required: No
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: No
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager.
