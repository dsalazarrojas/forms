# Maternity Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Maternity Leave Form is designed to collect essential information from employees regarding their maternity leave. It helps track and record employee's leave request, which is used to process their leave correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your employee information, including your name and job title.
2. Provide your start and end dates for the maternity leave.
3. Enter the reason for your leave (e.g., pregnancy, childbirth, etc.).
4. Add any comments or additional information you'd like to provide.
5. Your supervisor's input is also required to provide additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card or company records.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor's name.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your maternity leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end your maternity leave.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name or location.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your maternity leave (e.g., pregnancy, childbirth, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
* **Employee Input** (`employee_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information from the employee.

**Tips**

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department or supervisor.
