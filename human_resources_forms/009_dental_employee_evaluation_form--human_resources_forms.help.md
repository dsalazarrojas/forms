# Dental Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dental Employee Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect feedback and evaluations from supervisors or colleagues on the performance of dental employees. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and provide constructive feedback for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for the evaluation (e.g., Human Resources Forms, Other).
2. Enter a brief description of the evaluation or feedback you are providing.
3. Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
4. Enter the employee's ID number.
5. Enter the employee's email address.
6. Choose the date of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Form Title** (Note): A brief title for the evaluation.
*   Please enter a concise title that summarizes the evaluation.

### 2. **Category** (Select One)
*   Choose the category that best fits the purpose of the evaluation (e.g., Human Resources Forms, Other).

### 3. **Description** (Text)
*   Enter a brief description of the evaluation or feedback you are providing. This field is optional, but it helps to provide context for the evaluation.

### 4. **Employee Name** (Text)
*   Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.

### 5. **Employee ID** (Number)
*   Enter the employee's ID number.

### 6. **Employee Email** (Email)
*   Enter the employee's email address.

### 7. **Date** (Date)
*   Choose the date of the evaluation.
*   Make sure to use a standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy).
