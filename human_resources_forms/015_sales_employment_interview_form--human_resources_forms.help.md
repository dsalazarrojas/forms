# Sales Employment Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Employment Interview Form is used to collect information about employment history and work experience during a sales employment interview. It is a critical part of the hiring process to get a clear picture of the candidate's employment history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each sales employment interview, ensuring that you do not fill out a duplicate form.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information.
3. If you have already completed this form, do not fill it out again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Employment Form** (`sales_employment_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you will write a brief description of your employment history.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the job title you are applying for. Please choose the title that best matches your experience.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to indicate your current job status. Please select both Active and Inactive if you are currently employed and have been employed in the past.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your relevant work experience. Please provide a brief description of your work experience, including the company name, job title, and dates of employment.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select your employment type. Please choose the options that best fit your employment situation.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): This field is used to report your current salary or the highest salary you have earned in the past. Please enter a numerical value for your salary.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): This field is used to report your current or past work location. Please enter a brief description of the location where you worked.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): This field is used to report the start date of your current or past employment. Please enter the start date of your job using the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): This field is used to report the end date of your current or past employment. Please enter the end date of your job using the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to indicate your current employment status. Please select both Active and Inactive if you are currently employed and have been employed in the past.
