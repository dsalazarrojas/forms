# Relocation Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relocation Leave Form is used to process employee relocations, such as when an employee is moving to a new location or returning to their previous location. This form ensures that all relevant details are captured and approved by relevant departments before the employee's relocation is finalized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Details section with your employee's information.
2. Provide a valid reason for your relocation in the Reason for Relocation field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your relocation.
4. Specify the reason for your leave in the Reason for Leave field.
5. Enter the location you will be moving to or returning to.
6. If applicable, enter the department you will be associated with.
7. Enter the location you will return to after your leave.
8. If applicable, provide a reason for your return.
9. Enter any relocation costs associated with your move.
10. Enter the date you will return to work.
11. Select the status of your relocation (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
12. Add any additional notes about your relocation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee`, text, required): Enter your employee ID or name.
* **Reason for Relocation** (`reason_for_relocation`, text, not required): Provide a brief reason for your relocation.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you are starting your relocation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you will end your relocation.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, not required): Specify the reason for your leave.
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter the location you will be moving to or returning to.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required): If applicable, enter the department you will be associated with.
* **Return Location** (`return_location`, text, not required): Enter the location you will return to after your leave.
* **Reason for Return** (`reason_for_return`, text, not required): If applicable, provide a reason for your return.
* **Relocation Cost** (`relocation_cost`, number, not required): Enter any relocation costs associated with your move.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you will return to work.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of your relocation (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about your relocation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Not applicable in this context.

Note: This form may vary in its requirements depending on company policies and specific use cases.
