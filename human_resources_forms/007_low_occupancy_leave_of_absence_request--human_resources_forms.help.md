# Low Occupancy Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Low Occupancy Leave Of Absence Request form is used to document and track employee leave of absence requests. This form helps to ensure that necessary information is collected and that the request is properly communicated to relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the employee's name and other relevant details on page 1.
2.  Enter the start and end dates of the leave period on pages 2 and 3.
3.  Select the type of leave requested and confirm if the manager and department are aware of the request on pages 4 and 6.
4.  Provide a brief reason for the leave on page 7.
5.  Set the status of the leave request on page 8.
6.  Finally, confirm the submitter and submission date on pages 9 and 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee requesting the LOA.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the leave is expected to begin.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the leave is expected to end.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of leave being requested (e.g., medical, family, etc.).
* **Manager Awareness** (`manager`, select_one, optional): Select if the manager is aware of the request.
* **Department Awareness** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select if the department is aware of the request.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the leave request.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Set the status of the leave request (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date the request was submitted.
*   **Manager** (`manager`, select_one, required): Confirm if the manager is aware of the leave request.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Confirm if the department is aware of the leave request. 

## Tips

*   Be sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any potential issues or delays.
*   Ensure that the manager and department are aware of the request to facilitate smooth communication and processing.
*   Consult with the HR department or supervisor if you are unsure about any part of the process.
*   Follow up on the status of the request to ensure it is being processed promptly.
