# Skill Based Hiring Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skill Based Hiring Evaluation Form is designed to help managers and evaluators assess a candidate's skills and performance in a systematic way.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the candidate's information: Enter the candidate's name, email, phone number, and department in the respective fields.
2. Select the job title and job level of the candidate from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the skills the candidate possesses from the "Required Skills" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the candidate's technical skills from the "Technical Skills" dropdown menu.
5. Select the evaluation date and time.
6. Enter any additional comments or rating reasons.
7. Evaluate the candidate's overall performance and score.
8. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Candidate**: This field is used to enter the candidate's name.
• **Candidate Skills**: Enter the candidate's skills that match the job requirements.
• **Job Title**: Select the job title of the candidate.
• **Job Level**: Select the job level of the candidate.
• **Required Skills**: Choose the skills required for the job from the dropdown menu.
• **Technical Skills**: Enter any additional technical skills the candidate possesses.
• **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
• **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation.
• **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or reasons for the evaluation.
• **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the candidate.
• **Email**: Enter the candidate's email address.
• **Phone**: Enter the candidate's phone number.
• **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
• **Department**: Enter the department of the candidate.
• **Location**: Enter the location of the candidate.
• **Rating**: Evaluate the candidate's performance.
• **Rating Reason**: Enter the reason for the rating.
• **Additional Rating**: Evaluate the candidate's performance for any additional skills.
• **Additional Rating Reason**: Enter the reason for the additional rating.
• **Evaluation Score**: Evaluate the candidate's overall performance.
• **Evaluation Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the candidate.
• **Next Steps**: Enter any next steps or recommendations.
• **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm the evaluation.
• **Date Signed**: Enter the date signed.
• **Time Signed**: Enter the time signed.
