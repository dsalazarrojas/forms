# Termination Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of an employee's termination from the company. It is used to gather information about the termination process, including the date, reason, and any relevant termination records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Termination Date: This is the date on which the employee's termination took effect.
2. Enter the Reason for Termination: This is the reason why the employee's termination was processed. Please select from one of the available options.
3. Select the Termination Records: This is a list of relevant termination records that are related to the employee's termination. Please select from one of the available options.
4. Enter the Signature Date: This is the date on which the employee signed the form.
5. Enter the Employee Signature: This is the employee's signature, indicating their agreement to the termination details.
6. Enter the Date Termination Record Last Modified: This is the date on which the termination records were last updated or modified.
7. Enter the Last Updated By: This is the person who last updated or modified the termination records.
8. Enter the Last Update Date: This is the date on which the termination records were last updated or modified.
9. Enter the Last Updated By Employee: This is the employee who last updated or modified the termination records, if applicable.
10. Enter the Created By: This is the person who created the termination records.
11. Enter the Created Date: This is the date on which the termination records were created.
12. Enter the Form ID: This is a unique identifier for the form.
13. Enter the Form Title: This is the title of the form.
14. Enter the User ID: This is the ID of the user who is accessing or editing the form.
15. Enter the Employee ID: This is the ID of the employee being terminated.
16. Enter the Employee Name: This is the name of the employee being terminated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required): The date on which the employee's termination took effect.
- **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, optional): The reason why the employee's termination was processed.
- **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination_2`, text, optional): A secondary reason for termination, if applicable.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): The ID of the employee being terminated.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): The name of the employee being terminated.
- **Termination Records** (`termination_records`, select_one, optional): A list of relevant termination records related to the employee's termination.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): The date on which the employee signed the form.
- **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, optional): The employee's signature, indicating their agreement to the termination details.
- **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, optional): The manager's signature, indicating their approval of the termination details.
- **Date Termination Record Last Modified** (`date_termination_record_last_modified`, date, optional): The date on which the termination records were last updated or modified.
- **Last Updated By** (`last_updated_by`, text, optional): The person who last updated or modified the termination records.
- **Last Update Date** (`last_update_date`, date, optional): The date on which the termination records were last updated or modified.
- **Last Updated By Employee** (`last_updated_by_employee`, text, optional): The employee who last updated or modified the termination records, if applicable.
- **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): The person who created the termination records.
- **Created Date** (`created_date`, date, optional): The date on which the termination records were created.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): A unique identifier for the form.
- **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): The title of the form.
- **User ID** (`user_id`, text, optional): The ID of the user who is accessing or editing the form.
- **Last Updated By Employee** (`last_updated_by_employee`, text, optional): The employee who last updated or modified the termination records, if applicable.
