# Recruiting Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document job opening information for HR to use when creating a new job posting.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please ensure you have all the necessary information about the job and the company's hiring process. This form will help you create a comprehensive job posting.

1. Start by entering the job title and type.
2. Fill out the job requirements, available time, and days.
3. Enter the work location.
4. Select the job status (Active, Expired, or Archived).
5. If required, fill out the salary, benefits, and job description.
6. Add any additional information about the job in the "Job Requirements" field.
7. Repeat the steps above for any additional job openings (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`Job Title 2`, text, not required): Enter the title of the job.
* **Job Requirements** (`Job Requirements 2`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about the job requirements.
* **Available Time** (`Available Time 2`, time, not required): Select the available time for the job.
* **Available Days** (`Available Days 2`, text, not required): Select the available days for the job.
* **Work Location** (`Work Location 2`, text, not required): Enter the location where the job will be performed.
* **Work Type** (`Work Type 2`, text, not required): Enter the type of work required for the job.
* **Salary** (`Salary 2`, number, not required): Enter the salary for the job.
* **Benefits** (`Benefits 2`, text, not required): Enter any benefits the job may offer.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status 2`, select_one, required): Select the status of the job (Active, Expired, or Archived).
* **Recruiter** (`Recruiter 2`, text, not required): Enter the name of the recruiter.
* **Created At** (`Created At 2`, date, not required): Enter the date the job was created.
* **Updated At** (`Updated At 2`, date, not required): Enter the date the job was last updated.

Note: Some fields may have additional options or specific instructions, please refer to the job posting guidelines for more information.
