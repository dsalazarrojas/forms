# Offline Payment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request offline payments from the company. It is designed for employees who wish to receive compensation in a way that does not involve online banking or digital payment platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by ensuring you have all the necessary information before beginning the form.
2.  Enter your order number in the provided field.
3.  Enter your salary reference number.
4.  Select the payment type from the options provided.
5.  Enter the amount to be paid.
6.  Enter the date you would like the payment to be made.
7.  Optionally, enter the time of the payment.
8.  Verify that all required and optional fields are completed correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Number** (`Order Number`, `text`, optional): Enter the order number associated with your request for offline payment.
*   **Salary Reference Number** (`Salary Reference Number`, `text`, optional): Enter your salary reference number for record-keeping purposes.
*   **Payment Type** (`Payment Type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of payment you prefer from the options provided.
*   **Amount** (`Amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you wish to receive as payment.
*   **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like the payment to be made.
*   **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you would like the payment to be made (Note: This is optional and only required if you specified a date)

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about this form or the offline payment process, please contact your HR representative.
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed correctly to avoid delays in processing your request.
