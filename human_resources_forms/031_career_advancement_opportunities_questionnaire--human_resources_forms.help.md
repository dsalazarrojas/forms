# Career Advancement Opportunities Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the employee identify career advancement opportunities within the organization. It aims to gather information about the employee's current role, career interests, training needs, and readiness for promotion. This will help the organization to better understand the employee's career aspirations and provide guidance on potential growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Employee name" field.
2. Select your department from the list provided.
3. Enter your current job title in the "Current role" field.
4. Provide an approximate number of years you have been in your current role.
5. Choose the areas of interest that you would like to explore for career advancement (e.g., Leadership, Technical skills, Project management, Cross-functional mobility).
6. Indicate what training or development opportunities would help you advance your career.
7. Self-assess your readiness for promotion by selecting one of the options provided.
8. If there are any perceived obstacles to career advancement, please describe them in the "Barriers to advancement" field.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments or relevant information in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge or official documents.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select the team or unit you belong to.
* **Current role** (`current_role`, text, required): Enter your current job title as it appears on your official job description.
* **Years in current role** (`years_in_role`, number, optional): Approximate number of years you have been in your current role.
* **Career interest areas** (`career_interest_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Choose areas you are interested in exploring for career advancement, such as Leadership, Technical skills, Project management, or Cross-functional mobility.
* **Training needs** (`training_needs`, text, optional): Describe what training or development opportunities would help you advance your career.
* **Readiness for promotion** (`readiness_for_promotion`, select_one, required): Self-assess your readiness for promotion by selecting one of the options provided.
* **Barriers to advancement** (`barriers_to_advancement`, text, optional): If you perceive any obstacles to career advancement, describe them here.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or relevant information that will help the organization understand your career aspirations.
