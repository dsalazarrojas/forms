# Workplace Inclusion Program Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Inclusion Program Absence Form is a tool used to report and track employee absences in the workplace. This form is part of the company's human resources policies and is used to ensure accurate and consistent absence reporting.

## How to Complete This Form

### General Instructions
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including employee details, absence type, reason for absence, start and end dates, start and end times, workplace location, and employee signature.
2. Review and confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
3. Submit the form for review and approval.
