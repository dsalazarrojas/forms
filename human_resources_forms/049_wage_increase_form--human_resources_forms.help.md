## Purpose
The Wage Increase Form is designed to request and track wage increases for employees. To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Employee Information

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, required): Enter the unique ID of the employee for whom you are requesting the wage increase. This ID can usually be found on the employee's personnel file or in the HR system.
* **Reason for Increase** (`reason_for_increase`, required): Enter a valid reason for requesting the wage increase. Be specific and clear about why the employee deserves a raise.
* **Percentage Increase** (`percentage_increase`, required): Enter the percentage of the wage increase.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, required): Enter the date on which the wage increase will take effect.
