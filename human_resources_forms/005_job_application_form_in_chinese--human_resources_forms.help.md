# Job Application Form In Chinese - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit their job applications in a professional and organized manner. It collects essential information about the applicant, including personal details, education background, work experience, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Attach your Resume and job description in the Resume and Job Description fields.
3. Select your preferred Job Title, Job Type (Part-time, Full-time, or Internship), and Job Location (Beijing, Shanghai, or Shenzhen).
4. Choose your Native Language and Level of English proficiency.
5. Describe your Education Background and Work Experience in the text fields.
6. Select your preferred Availability Date and Available Location (if applicable).
7. Choose your Job Status (Open, Closed, or In Progress) and Job Reference (Interview, Test, or Both).
8. If required, select your Job Application Fee and Job Application Method (Online, Offline, or Both).
9. Finally, check the Job Application Status (New, In Progress, Approved, or Rejected) and select your preferred Assigned Tool (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Resume** (Text): Attach your resume or CV.
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter your desired job title.
* **Job Type** (Select One): Select your preferred job type (Part-time, Full-time, or Internship).
* **Job Location** (Select Multiple): Select your preferred job location (Beijing, Shanghai, or Shenzhen).
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth (if required).
* **Native Language** (Select One): Select your native language (English, Mandarin, Cantonese, or Japanese).
* **Level of English** (Select Multiple): Select your level of English proficiency (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, or None).
* **Education Background** (Text): Describe your education background.
* **Work Experience** (Text): Describe your work experience.
* **Job Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirement** (Text): Enter the job requirements or qualifications.
* **Salary Range** (Text): Enter your expected salary range.
* **Available Date** (Date): Enter your available date (if required).
* **Available Time** (Time): Enter your available time (if required).
* **Available Location** (Select Multiple): Select your available location (Beijing, Shanghai, or Shenzhen).
* **Job Status** (Select One): Select your job status (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
* **Job Reference** (Select One): Select your preferred job reference (Interview, Test, or Both).
* **Job Application Fee** (Number): Enter the application fee (if required).
* **Job Application Method** (Select One): Select your preferred job application method (Online, Offline, or Both).
* **Job Application Note** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Job Application Status** (Select One): Select your job application status (New, In Progress, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Assigned Tool** (Text): Enter the assigned tool (if required).

Tips:

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the hiring manager.
