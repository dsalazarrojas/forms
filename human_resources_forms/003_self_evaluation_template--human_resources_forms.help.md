# self_evaluation_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The self_evaluation_template form is used to evaluate an employee's performance and progress in the workplace. It helps gather information about an employee's strengths, areas for improvement, and development plans, as well as collects feedback and ratings from the evaluator.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the basic information about the employee on the first page.
2. Scroll through the subsequent pages and provide feedback, ratings, and comments as required.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): This field is used to input the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the department the employee belongs to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the employee being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, text, required): Enter the evaluation period for the employee.
* **Goals** (`goals`, note, not required): Provide any specific goals or objectives the employee has set.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, not required): Mention any notable strengths or accomplishments the employee has demonstrated.
* **Areas For Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, note, not required): Identify areas where the employee needs improvement or development.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Add any additional feedback or comments for the employee.
* **Development Plans** (`development_plans`, text, not required): Outline any development plans or training recommendations for the employee.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments about the employee's performance.
* **Evaluator Comments** (`evaluator_comments`, note, not required): Add any comments or feedback you'd like to include.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, number, not required): Rate the employee on a scale of 1-5 on one of the evaluation criteria.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, number, not required): Rate the employee on a scale of 1-5 on another evaluation criterion.
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, number, not required): Rate the employee on a scale of 1-5 on another evaluation criterion.
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`, number, not required): Rate the employee on a scale of 1-5 on another evaluation criterion.
* **Rating 5** (`rating_5`, number, not required): Rate the employee on a scale of 1-5 on another evaluation criterion.
* **Self Assessment Rating** (`self_assessment_rating`, number, not required): Rate the employee's self-assessment of their performance.
* **Manager Rating** (`manager_rating`, number, not required): Rate the employee on a scale of 1-5 by the manager.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Provide honest and constructive feedback to help the employee grow.
* Keep the form concise and to the point.
* Use the ratings and comments to identify areas for improvement and development.
