# team_member_of_the_month_voting_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member of the Month voting form is designed to allow team members to vote for their colleagues they deem deserving of recognition for their outstanding work and behavior. This recognition program aims to foster a positive work environment and encourage teamwork.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team member whose outstanding performance you wish to acknowledge.
2. Choose the month for which you are voting (e.g., January, February, etc.).
3. Provide a brief comment to justify your vote (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Of The Month**: This field is where you should enter the name of the team member you are voting for.
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the team member you are voting for.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the team member you are voting for.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the team member you are voting for (this will be used to notify them of your vote).
* **Department**: Enter the department of the team member you are voting for.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the team member you are voting for.
* **Vote For**: Select "Yes" if you believe the team member deserves recognition, "No" if not.
* **Comment**: Provide a brief comment to justify your vote (optional).
* **Month**: Select the month for which you are voting for the team member (e.g., January, February, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to only vote for team members who are in good standing and have been with the company for at least six months.
* Be specific with your comments, providing reasons why you believe the team member deserves recognition.
* Only select "Yes" if you genuinely believe the team member deserves recognition.
