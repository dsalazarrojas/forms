# union_leader_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The union leader information form is used to collect data about the current union leader. This form is essential for maintaining accurate and up-to-date information about the union's key representative. Please fill out this form with the most recent and accurate information about the current union leader.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the name of the current union leader in the "union_leader" field.
2. Select the correct role of the union leader from the dropdown options in the "role" field.
3. Enter the email address of the union leader in the "contact_email" field.
4. Enter the phone number of the union leader in the "contact_phone" field.
5. Enter the address of the union leader in the "contact_address" field.
6. Enter a brief bio or description of the union leader in the "contact_bio" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **union_leader** (text, required): Enter the name of the current union leader.
* **role** (select_one, required): Select the role of the union leader from the options: "Union President", "Union Secretary", "Union Treasurer", or "Other".
* **contact_name** (text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the union leader (e.g., name).
* **contact_email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the union leader.
* **contact_phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the union leader.
* **contact_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the union leader.
* **contact_bio** (text, optional): Enter a brief description or bio of the union leader.
