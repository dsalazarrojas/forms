# Expatriate Assignment Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expatriate Assignment Confirmation Form is used to gather information about an employee's assignment details and to confirm their acceptance of terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal employee details, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Enter the details of your assignment, including the location, duration, and purpose of your trip.
3. Accept or decline the terms and conditions of your assignment by selecting one of the two options in the "Terms Acceptance" field.
4. Confirm your acceptance or declination of the assignment by selecting one of the two options in the "Employee Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details (`employee_details`)
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information to be used for communication purposes.

### Assignment Details (`assignment_details`)
* **Assignment Details** (`assignment_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the details of your assignment, including the location, duration, and purpose of your trip.

### Terms Acceptance (`terms_acceptance`)
* **Terms Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Accept or decline the terms and conditions of your assignment by selecting one of the two options: "Yes, I accept the terms" or "No, I do not accept the terms".

### Employee Confirmation (`employee_confirmation`)
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm your acceptance or declination of the assignment by selecting one of the two options: "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Make sure to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all necessary steps are taken to facilitate a smooth and successful assignment.
* If you are unsure about any of the information requested, please contact your HR or management team for assistance.
