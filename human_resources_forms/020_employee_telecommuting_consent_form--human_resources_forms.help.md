# Employee Telecommuting Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Telecommuting Consent Form is a document that employees must complete to express their consent to work from home or remotely. This form ensures that employees understand the company's policies and requirements for telecommuting, as well as their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Company Name.
2. Enter your Employee Name.
3. Provide your Manager's Name (if applicable).
4. Select your Work Start Date.
5. Select your Work End Date.
6. Choose the Prophets Allowed options that apply to you.
7. Choose the Remote Work Days that apply to you.
8. Enter your Remote Work Hours (if applicable).
9. Provide your Work Location (e.g., home address).
10. Enter your Work Location Address (if applicable).
11. Enter any additional Work Location Notes (if applicable).
12. Select whether you will be working from home (True/False).
13. Choose your Travel Reimbursement options (if applicable).
14. Select the Work Location Restrictions that apply to you.
15. Enter any Work Location Restrictions Notes (if applicable).
16. Enter your Emergency Contact Name (if applicable).
17. Enter your Emergency Contact Phone (if applicable).
18. Enter your Emergency Contact Email (if applicable).
19. Sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.
20. Sign the form as the employee, indicating your Manager's Name and Title. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as an employee.
* **Manager Name**: Provide the name of your supervisor or manager (if applicable).
* **Work Start Date**: Select the date you will start working from home or remotely.
* **Work End Date**: Select the date you will stop working from home or remotely.
* **Prophets Allowed**: Select the options that apply to you (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.).
* **Remote Work Days**: Choose the days you will work from home or remotely.
* **Remote Work Hours**: Enter the hours you will work from home or remotely (if applicable).
* **Work Location**: Enter the address or location where you will work from home or remotely.
* **Work Location Address**: Enter the address or location details (if applicable).
* **Work Location Notes**: Enter any additional information about your work location (if applicable).
* **Work From Home Arrangement**: Select whether you will be working from home (True/False).
* **Travel Reimbursement**: Choose the options that apply to you (if applicable).
* **Work Location Restrictions**: Select the restrictions that apply to you.
* **Work Location Restrictions Notes**: Enter any additional information about the restrictions (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the contact person or person you would like to be notified in case of an emergency (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person or person you would like to be notified in case of an emergency (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or person you would like to be notified in case of an emergency (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or person you would like to be notified in case of an emergency (if applicable).
* **Employee Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Manager Signature**: Sign the form as the manager or supervisor, indicating their title and date.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Manager Title**: Enter the title of your supervisor or manager.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of your supervisor or manager.
