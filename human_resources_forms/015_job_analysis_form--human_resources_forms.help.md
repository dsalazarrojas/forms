# Job Analysis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Analysis Form is used to document and analyze job details for Human Resources purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields and provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.
2. Fill out the form for recently updated or active jobs only.
3. Ensure you have all necessary permissions and authority to fill out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter the job title, e.g., "Software Developer," "Marketing Manager," etc.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the job's responsibilities and duties.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ Select "Yes" if the job is active and being performed.
	+ Select "No" if the job is inactive or has been discontinued.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ Select multiple categories that best describe the job, e.g., "Software Development," "Marketing and Sales."
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ Select "Active" for jobs that are currently being performed.
	+ Select "Inactive" for jobs that have been discontinued or ended.
* **Job Status Note** (`job_status_note`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the job's status.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, not required): Enter the job's location, e.g., "Remote," "Office," etc.
* **Job Termination Reason** (`job_termination_reason`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ Select "Yes" if the job was terminated due to a specific reason.
	+ Select "No" if the job is ongoing or active.

## Tips
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure up-to-date job records.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Only fill out this form for active and recently updated jobs to avoid duplication of efforts.
