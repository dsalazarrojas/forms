# remote_worker_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for remote workers to apply for work opportunities. It collects personal and professional information to help evaluate the applicant's qualifications and fit for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide accurate and detailed information in each field, including your personal details, work experience, skills, and emergency contact information.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for your work style, availability, and employment status.
4. Upload your resume if required.
5. Review your application before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, not required): This is the first field where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Application Information** (`application_info`, text, not required): This field is not required, but you can use it to provide any additional information about your application.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, not required): Describe your previous work experience, including job roles and responsibilities.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, not required): List your educational background, including degrees and institutions attended.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select multiple, required): Select the skills you have that are relevant to the job opportunity.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, not required): If you have any specific availability constraints, such as availability to start work, please indicate it here.
* **Work Style** (`work_style`, select one, required): Choose the work style that best describes your work habits.
* **Communication** (`communication`, note, not required): This field is not required, but you can use it to provide any relevant communication information.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, not required): If required, please upload your resume here.
* **References** (`references`, select multiple, required): Select any relevant references that can vouch for your work experience and skills.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, not required): Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, not required): If required, please provide detailed information about your emergency contact.
