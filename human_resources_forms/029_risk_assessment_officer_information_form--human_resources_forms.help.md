# Risk Assessment Officer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Assessment Officer Information Form is designed to collect personal details of Risk Assessment Officers, including first name, last name, email, phone number, role, department, location, and date hired.
## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Fill Out Required Fields
Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as they are indicated.
### Step 2: Fill Out Optional Fields
Fill out the optional fields as needed with accurate and up-to-date information.
### Step 3: Review and Submit
Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure they are correct.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official documentation.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official documentation.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your official contact phone number.
* **Role**: Enter your official position or job title.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Location**: Enter your physical work location.
* **Date Hired**: Enter the date you were hired in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
