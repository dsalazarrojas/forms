# Fitness Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fitness Reimbursement Form is used to process fitness reimbursement claims from employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Required): Enter your job title as it appears in your employee directory.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Required): Enter your department as it appears in your employee directory.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, Number, Required): Enter your manager's unique ID number.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, Date, Required): Enter the date you were hired in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Date of Departure** (`date_of_departure`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you left the company. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, Select, Required): Select your current status as an active, inactive, or terminated employee.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all types that apply to your employment (Full Time, Part Time, or Contract).
* **Date of Request** (`date_of_request`, Date, Required): Enter the date you submitted this reimbursement request in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Requested Amount** (`requested_amount`, Number, Required): Enter the total amount you are requesting for reimbursement.
* **Reimbursement Type** (`reimbursement_type`, Select, Required): Select the type of reimbursement you are requesting (Gym Member, Class Member, or Membership).
* **Reimbursement Date** (`reimbursement_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date the reimbursement is valid from the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, Note, Required): Sign the form to confirm your information is accurate.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, Note, Required): Sign the form to approve or deny the reimbursement request.

Tips:
* Ensure all dates are in the correct format.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* Do not submit duplicate requests.
