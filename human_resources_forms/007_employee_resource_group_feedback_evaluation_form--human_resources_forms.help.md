# employee_resource_group_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experiences and thoughts about their resource group. The feedback provided will help identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for the team and department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in your first name and last name in their respective fields.
* Select your department from the provided options (IT, Finance, HR).
* Provide your job title.
* Answer the feedback questions (4 questions).
* Enter your email address.
* Select the date and time of the feedback submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Department**: Select your department from the options provided (IT, Finance, HR).
* **Job title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Feedback question 1**: Answer the first feedback question.
* **Feedback answer 1**: Enter your response to the first feedback question.
* **Feedback question 2**: Answer the second feedback question.
* **Feedback answer 2**: Enter your response to the second feedback question.
* **Feedback question 3**: Answer the third feedback question.
* **Feedback answer 3**: Enter your response to the third feedback question.
* **Feedback question 4**: Answer the fourth feedback question.
* **Feedback answer 4**: Enter your response to the fourth feedback question.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Date**: Select the date of the feedback submission.
* **Time**: Select the time of the feedback submission.
* **Departmental diversity focus**: Enter your department's diversity focus.
* **Job function**: Enter your job function.
* **Job function additional info**: Enter any additional information about your job function.
* **Team name**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Team lead**: Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Departmental diversity focus 2**: Enter your department's diversity focus 2.
* **Job function additional info 2**: Enter any additional information about your job function 2.
* **Job function additional info 3**: Enter any additional information about your job function 3.
* **Job function additional info 4**: Enter any additional information about your job function 4.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Provide clear and concise feedback for each question.
* Use proper spelling and grammar.
* Make sure to select the correct department and email address.
