# part_time_employee_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for part-time employees to provide their exit details, such as exit date, reason for leaving, and notice period, to ensure a smooth transition process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's details on page 1, including their name and any other relevant information.
2. Select the exit date on page 2.
3. Choose a reason for leaving on page 3.
4. Choose the next step for the employee's departure on page 4.
5. Enter the job title and job type on page 5.
6. Select the notice period for the employee's departure on page 6.
7. Enter the last day of work on page 7.
8. Select the termination reason on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the part-time employee, such as their name and contact details.
* **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, `date`, required/false): Select the date the part-time employee will be leaving the organization.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, `text`, required/false): Choose a reason for the part-time employee's departure. This will help the HR team understand the circumstances surrounding their leaving.
* **Next Step** (`next_step`, `select_one`, required/false): Select the next step in the transition process, such as providing further support or finalizing paperwork.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/false): Enter the part-time employee's job title.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required/false): Choose the type of job the part-time employee held.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `number`, required/false): Enter the number of days or weeks notice the part-time employee provided.
* **Last Day of Work** (`last_day_of_work`, `date`, required/false): Enter the last day the part-time employee worked.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Select all relevant reasons for the part-time employee's termination.
