# employee_conflict_resolution_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee conflict resolution evaluation form is a tool used to document and evaluate workplace conflicts. It allows employees to report and describe conflicts, provide information about the resolution outcome, and document any actions taken. This form helps to ensure that all conflicts are properly documented and that the resolution process is tracked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee involved in the conflict from the list of options.
2. Choose the date when the conflict occurred.
3. Describe the conflict in detail.
4. Indicate whether a resolution was attempted and whether it was successful.
5. Choose the resolution outcome from the available options.
6. Provide any comments about the resolution outcome.
7. If the conflict was resolved by someone other than an HR Manager, select that person from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Involved** (`employee_involved`, select_one, required): Choose the employee who was directly involved in the conflict. This can be a manager, team member, team leader, or another employee.
* **Other Employee (if not manager, team member, team leader)** (`employee_involved_other`, text, optional): If the employee involved is not a manager, team member, or team leader, enter their name or title here.
* **Date Conflicts Occurred** (`date_conflict_occurred`, date, required): Enter the date when the conflict occurred.
* **Describe Conflict** (`conflict_description`, text, required): Describe the conflict in detail, including any relevant context or background information.
* **Yes/No** (`resolution_attempted`, select_one, required): Check whether a resolution was attempted and whether it was successful. If a resolution was attempted, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Resolution Outcome** (`resolution_outcome`, select_multiple, required): Choose the resolution outcome from the available options. This includes "Conflicts Resolved", "Conflicts Not Resolved", or "Pending".
* **Resolution Comments** (`resolution_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments about the resolution outcome.
* **Actions Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): Describe any actions taken to resolve the conflict.
* **Conflict Resolution Form Completed** (`conflict_resolution_form_completed`, time, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was completed.
* **Completed By** (`conflict_resolution_form_completed_by`, select_one, optional): If the conflict resolution form was completed by someone other than an HR Manager, select their name from the list of options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the conflict.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the conflict and the resolution outcome.
* If the conflict was resolved by someone other than an HR Manager, make sure to select their name from the list of options.
