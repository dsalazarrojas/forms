# Performance Improvement Plan Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of an employee's departure from the company as they exit the performance improvement plan. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose the reason for exiting the performance improvement plan from the options provided.
2. Enter the employee's name and ID as they are recorded in the system.
3. Select the status of the performance improvement plan (Active, Inactive, or Removed).
4. Enter the date the employee is off-boarded.
5. Provide a brief explanation for why the employee is off-boarded.
6. Describe the next steps that should be taken.
7. Enter the final offboarding date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **performance_improvement_plan_exit_offboarding_form** (`Select One`, Optional): Choose the reason for exiting the performance improvement plan from the options provided. This option allows you to select the specific reason for the employee's departure.
* **employee_name** (`Text`, Optional): Enter the name of the employee as recorded in the system.
* **employee_id** (`Number`, Optional): Enter the ID of the employee as recorded in the system.
* **performance_improvement_plan_status** (`Select Multiple`, Optional): Select the status of the performance improvement plan (Active, Inactive, or Removed). This option allows you to update the status of the employee's performance improvement plan.
* **date_off_boarded** (`Date`, Optional): Enter the date the employee is off-boarded.
* **reason_off_boarded** (`Text`, Optional): Provide a brief explanation for why the employee is off-boarded. This is a free-text field where you can enter as much or as little information as needed.
* **next_steps** (`Text`, Optional): Describe the next steps that should be taken after the employee is off-boarded. This is a free-text field where you can enter as much or as little information as needed.
* **exit_date** (`Date`, Optional): Enter the final offboarding date. 

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure it is accurate and complete. 
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative. 

Note: The required fields are indicated by the word "Required" in the YAML configuration. If you are unsure about any of the required fields, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
