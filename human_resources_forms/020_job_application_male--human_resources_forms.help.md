# Job Application Male - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Application Male form is used to collect information about job applications for male candidates. This form helps to keep track of job postings and applicant data for HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form for every job application
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job being applied for.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the job.
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, select, required): Choose the type of job: Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, or Temporary.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, select multiple, required): Select all relevant statuses for the job: Active, Closed, or Archived.
* **Date Hired** (`Date Hired`, date, optional): Enter the date the job was hired for if applicable.
* **Hire Date** (`Hire Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the job was hired on if applicable.
* **Salary** (`Salary`, number, optional): Enter the salary for the job if applicable.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information for the applicant.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the applicant if applicable.
* **Job Application** (`Job Application`, text, optional): Enter any additional job application information.
* **Job Application Comments** (`Job Application Comments`, note, optional): Add any comments about the job application.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information
- If you're unsure about a field, you can ask for clarification or skip it for now and add it later
