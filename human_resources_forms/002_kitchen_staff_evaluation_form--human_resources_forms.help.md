# Kitchen Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Staff Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation data from kitchen staff members during their regular performance evaluations. This information will help the kitchen staff improve their skills, receive constructive feedback, and track their progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Enter the staff member's name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Please type in the full name of the kitchen staff member being evaluated.
2. **Enter the supervisor's name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Please type in the full name of the supervisor who evaluated the kitchen staff member.
3. **Provide feedback given** (`feedback_given`, text, optional): Please provide any feedback or comments about the staff member's performance.
4. **Enter the evaluation date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Please select the date of the evaluation.
5. **Enter the evaluation time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Please select the time of the evaluation.
6. **Provide overall rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Please provide a numerical rating from 1 to 5 to indicate the staff member's overall performance.
7. **Enter feedback received** (`feedback_received`, text, optional): Please provide any feedback or comments received by the staff member.
8. **Enter supervisor signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, required): Please sign with your name as the supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Staff Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the kitchen staff member being evaluated.
- **Supervisor Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor who evaluated the staff member.
- **Feedback Given** (text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback on the staff member's performance.
- **Evaluation Date** (date, optional): Select the date of the evaluation.
- **Evaluation Time** (time, optional): Select the time of the evaluation.
- **Overall Rating** (number, optional): Provide a numerical rating from 1 to 5 to indicate the staff member's overall performance.
- **Feedback Received** (text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments received by the staff member.
- **Supervisor Signature** (text, required): Sign with your name as the supervisor.
