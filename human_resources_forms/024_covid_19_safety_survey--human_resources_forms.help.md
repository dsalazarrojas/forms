# covid_19_safety_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the COVID-19 safety data of employees. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to help our organization ensure a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee ID** (ID number) in the designated field.
2. Enter your **Name** as it appears on your official records.
3. Provide your **Age** in numbers.
4. Enter your **Contact Number** (phone number or email).
5. Describe any **Symptoms** you are experiencing, if any.
6. Share your **Medical History**, including any pre-existing conditions or allergies.
7. Confirm if you have received the **Vaccine Administered**, and if so, specify the **Dosage** administered.
8. Report any **Reaction** you experienced after the vaccination.
9. Confirm if you have been **Hospitalized** due to COVID-19-related symptoms or reactions.
10. Describe your **Medical Attention**, including any medical care or treatment received.
11. Provide the reason for your **Medical Attention** (e.g., symptoms, vaccine reaction, etc.).
12. Confirm if you have taken any **Medical Care**, and if so, specify who provided it.
13. Schedule a **Follow Up**, including the date and time for the follow-up.
14. Specify the **Symptoms After Vaccine** you are experiencing, if any.
15. Describe your **Medical History After Vaccine**, including any new or changed symptoms.
16. Confirm the **Medical Care Provided** after the vaccine, including any medication or treatment administered.
17. Confirm if you have had any **Contact With Doctor**, and specify if it's yes or no.
18. Provide the **Contact With Doctor** details, including their name and contact information.
19. Confirm if you have had a **Medical Attendant**, and specify if it's yes or no.
20. Confirm if you have had **Medical Attendance**, and specify if it's yes or no.
21. Confirm if you have had a **Medical Attendant 2**, and specify if it's yes or no.
22. Confirm if you have had **Medical Attendance 2**, and specify if it's yes or no.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID**: Your unique ID number assigned by the organization.
* **Name**: Your official name as it appears on your records.
* **Age**: Your age in numbers.
* **Contact Number**: Your phone number or email address for contact purposes.
* **Symptoms**: Describe any COVID-19-related symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Medical History**: Share your pre-existing medical conditions or allergies.
* **Vaccine Administered**: Confirm if you have received the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Dosage**: If you have received the vaccine, specify the dosage administered.
* **Reaction**: Describe any reaction or side effects you experienced after the vaccination.
* **Hospitalization**: Confirm if you have been hospitalized due to COVID-19-related symptoms or reactions.
* **Medical Attention**: Describe any medical care or treatment you have received.
* **Medical Attention Reason**: Provide the reason for your medical attention (e.g., symptoms, vaccine reaction, etc.).
* **Medical Care Taken**: Confirm if you have taken any medical care or treatment.
* **Medical Care Taker**: Specify who provided the medical care or treatment.
* **Follow Up**: Schedule a follow-up appointment or check-in.
* **Follow Up Time**: Specify the date and time for the follow-up.
* **Symptoms After Vaccine**: Describe any new or changed symptoms after the vaccination.
* **Medical History After Vaccine**: Share any new or changed medical history after the vaccination.
* **Medical Care Provided**: Confirm if you have received any medical care or treatment after the vaccination.
* **Contact With Doctor**: Confirm if you have had contact with a doctor.
* **Medical Attendants Contact**: Provide the contact information for the medical attendant.
* **Medical Attendant**: Confirm if you have had a medical attendant.
* **Medical Attendance**: Confirm if you have had medical attendance.
* **Medical Attendant 2**: Confirm if you have had a medical attendant 2.
* **Medical Attendance 2**: Confirm if you have had medical attendance 2.

## Tips
* Please ensure you answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or your HR department for clarification.
* Keep in mind that the data collected will be used to provide personalized guidance and support to employees who may be experiencing symptoms or have concerns about their health.
