# Interview Notes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information from interviews, including candidate information, assessments, and feedback. It helps in documenting the process and providing valuable insights for future references.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the interview details in the "Interview Details" field.
2. Enter the candidate's information in the "Candidate Info" field.
3. Record any assessment or observations made during the interview in the "Assessment" field.
4. Share any feedback or comments about the candidate in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interview Details** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the interview, including the date, time, and location.
* **Candidate Info** (2, text, optional): Provide basic information about the candidate, such as name, role, and experience.
* **Assessment** (3, text, optional): Record any assessments or observations made during the interview, including comments and concerns.
* **Feedback** (4, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments about the candidate, including areas of improvement and suggestions.
