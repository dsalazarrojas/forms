# Employee Discipline Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Discipline Form is a tool used to document and address employee incidents. This form is to be used only once for each incident, and not to repeat the incident in other forms or reports.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Employee Incident Report" section and start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each section completely, providing all required information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Incident Report** (Employee Incident Report, note, optional): This is the first section of the form where you will document the basic details of the incident. Please ensure that this section is filled out completely for each incident.
* **Incident Date** (Incident Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident in "YYYY-MM-DD" format.
* **Incident Time** (Incident Time, time, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred in "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, number, optional): Enter the employee's unique identification number.
* **Description** (Description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of what happened, including any relevant details about what the employee did wrong.
* **Action Taken** (Action Taken, text, optional): Enter the steps taken to address the incident.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language to describe the incident.
* Proofread carefully before submitting the form.
