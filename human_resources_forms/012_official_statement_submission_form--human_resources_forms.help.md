# Official Statement Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Official Statement Submission Form is a tool for employees to submit official statements, which can be internal reviews, HR matters, or incident documentation. This form helps to standardize the submission process, making it easier for management or HR to review and process submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the type of statement you are submitting (Internal Review, HR Matter, or Incident Documentation) from the "Statement Type" dropdown menu.
3. If required, select a date for "Submission Date" and "Submission Updated At" fields.
4. Provide a clear and concise statement in the "Statement" field.
5. If applicable, add any supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.
6. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the statement in the "Statement Reviewed By" field.
7. Select the current status of the submission (Draft, Pending, or Approved) from the "Submission Status" dropdown menu.
8. Add any relevant comments or feedback in the "Submission Comment" field.
9. If necessary, enter the name of the person who submitted the statement in the "Submitted By" field.
10. Select the date of submission from the "Submitted On" field.
11. Enter the current timestamp of the submission in the "Submission Updated At Timestamp" field.
12. If necessary, enter the name of the person who last updated the submission in the "Submission Last Updated By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your company ID or badge.
* **Statement Type** (`statement_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of statement you are submitting (Internal Review, HR Matter, or Incident Documentation).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the statement.
* **Statement** (`statement`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise statement, explaining the reason for submission and any supporting information.
* **Reason For Submission** (`reason_for_submission`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for submitting the statement.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents that support your statement.
* **Statement Reviewed By** (`statement_reviewed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reviewed your statement.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the submission (Draft, Pending, or Approved).
* **Submission Comment** (`submission_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback for the reviewer.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the statement.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Updated At** (`submission_updated_at`, date, optional): Enter the timestamp of the last update.
* **Submission Updated By** (`submission_updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the submission.
* **Submission Updated By User** (`submission_updated_by_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the submission.
* **Submission Updated At Timestamp** (`submission_updated_at_timestamp`, text, optional): Enter the timestamp of the last update.
* **Submission Last Updated By** (`submission_last_updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the submission.
