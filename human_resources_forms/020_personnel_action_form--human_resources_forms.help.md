# personnel_action_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "personnel_action_form" is used to document and track personnel actions within the organization. It helps ensure accuracy and consistency in personnel management decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee whose action you are documenting.
2. Choose the action date and time.
3. Provide a brief description of the action taken.
4. Indicate the current status of the employee (Active or Inactive).
5. Specify the current status of the job (Active or Inactive).
6. Provide a reason for the action taken (optional).
7. Choose the status of the job details (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **employee** (`employee`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the employee whose action you are documenting.
- **action_date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the action taken.
- **action_time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the action taken.
- **job_details** (`text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the job details.
- **action_taken** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the action taken.
- **reason** (`text`, optional): Provide a reason for the action taken.
- **employee_status** (`select_one`, optional): Select the current status of the employee (Active or Inactive).
- **job_status** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the current status of the job (Active or Inactive).

## Tips
* Always ensure that the employee information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* Clearly and concisely describe the action taken and its reason.
* Use the correct status for employee and job details.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting.
