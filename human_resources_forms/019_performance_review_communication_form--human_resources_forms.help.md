# Performance Review Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Review Communication Form is used to gather feedback and suggestions from reviewers to provide to employees during performance reviews. This form helps in documenting the review process, providing a structured way to communicate feedback and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the review period and employee's name.
2. Select the areas where feedback is required (Feedback Areas).
3. Rate the employee's performance overall.
4. Provide detailed feedback and suggestions.
5. Schedule the next review date and time.
6. Select the status of the review (Completed, In Progress, Pending).
7. Sign off with the reviewer's name and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Review Period
* **Review Period** (`review_period`, `text`, required: false): Enter the review period in text format (e.g., Q1 2022).

### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's full name.

### Reviewer Name
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reviewer's name.

### Reviewer Title
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reviewer's title.

### Feedback Areas
* **Feedback Areas** (`feedback_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the areas where feedback is required (e.g., communication, teamwork, leadership).

### Overall Rating
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the employee's performance overall (Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Improvement).

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide detailed feedback about the employee's performance.

### Action Items
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, `text`, required: false): Record action items that need to be addressed by the employee.

### Next Review Date
* **Next Review Date** (`next_review_date`, `date`, required: false): Schedule the next review date.

### Next Review Time
* **Next Review Time** (`next_review_time`, `time`, required: false): Schedule the next review time.

### Review Status
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the review (Completed, In Progress, Pending).

### Reviewed By
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the person reviewing the employee.

### Reviewer Comments
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reviewer's comments.

### Reviewer Suggestions
* **Reviewer Suggestions** (`reviewer_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Enter suggestions from the reviewer.

### Reviewed Comments
* **Reviewed Comments** (`reviewed_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter comments from the person being reviewed.

### Reviewed Rating
* **Reviewed Rating** (`reviewed_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the employee's performance based on the reviewer's comments.

### Reviewed Suggestions
* **Reviewed Suggestions** (`reviewed_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Enter suggestions from the person being reviewed.

### Reviewed Actions
* **Reviewed Actions** (`reviewed_actions`, `text`, required: false): Enter actions taken by the employee based on the review.

### Reviewer Signature
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign off with the reviewer's name and title.

### Reviewer Name and Title
* **Reviewer Name and Title** (`reviewer_name_and_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reviewer's name and title.

### Reviewed Name
* **Reviewed Name** (`reviewed_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's name.

### Reviewed Title
* **Reviewed Title** (`reviewed_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's title.

### Reviewed Department
* **Reviewed Department** (`reviewed_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's department.

### Reviewer Department
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reviewer's department.
