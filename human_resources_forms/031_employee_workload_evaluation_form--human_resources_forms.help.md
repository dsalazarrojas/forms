# employee_workload_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on employee workload from managers to evaluate and adjust tasks and resources accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the employee ID in the `employee_id` field. This is the unique identifier assigned to the employee.
2.  Enter the ID of the manager who will be reviewing the employee's workload in the `manager_id` field.
3.  Enter the total number of tasks assigned to the employee in the `task_count` field.
4.  Select the employee's satisfaction level with their current workload from the options: Excellent, Good, or Poor, in the `satisfaction_level` field.
5.  Add any additional comments or feedback in the `comment` field.
6.  Enter the date when the employee's workload was last evaluated in the `date_last_evaluated` field (optional).
7.  Enter the time when the employee's workload was last evaluated in the `time_last_evaluated` field (optional).
8.  Add any additional notes about the employee's workload in the `notes` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, Number, Required): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the employee.
* **manager_id** (`manager_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the ID of the manager who will be reviewing the employee's workload.
* **task_count** (`task_count`, Number, Optional): Enter the total number of tasks assigned to the employee.
* **satisfaction_level** (`satisfaction_level`, Select One, Optional): Select the employee's satisfaction level with their current workload from the options: Excellent, Good, or Poor.
* **comment** (`comment`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the employee's workload.
* **date_last_evaluated** (`date_last_evaluated`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the employee's workload was last evaluated.
* **time_last_evaluated** (`time_last_evaluated`, Time, Optional): Enter the time when the employee's workload was last evaluated.
* **notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the employee's workload.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as this form is used to assess the workload of employees.
* All fields are optional, so please fill out only the fields that are relevant to the situation.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please reach out to your manager or HR department.
