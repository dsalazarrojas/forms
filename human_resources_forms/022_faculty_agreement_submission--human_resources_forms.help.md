# Faculty Agreement Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Agreement Submission form is used to collect information related to faculty agreements, including contract terms, dates, and statuses. This form is designed to help ensure accurate and efficient data entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
2. Enter the faculty agreement submission form details.
3. Select the relevant options for the faculty agreement submission form.
4. Enter the faculty agreement submission date and time.
5. Enter the faculty agreement submission email and phone number.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.
7. Select the faculty agreement submission license.
8. Select the type of contract.
9. Enter the faculty agreement submission duration.
10. Enter the start and end dates of the faculty agreement.
11. Select the contract term.
12. Enter the contract term duration.
13. Select the status of the faculty agreement.
14. Enter the renewal date.
15. Select the renewal status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract**: Enter the contract details. This field is optional.
* **Acknowledgement**: Enter any acknowledgement related to the faculty agreement. This field is optional.
* **Details**: Select the relevant details related to the faculty agreement. Options include Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the faculty agreement submission. This field is optional.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the faculty agreement submission. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the faculty member. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the faculty member. This field is optional.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to the faculty agreement. This field is optional.
* **License**: Enter the license number or details. This field is optional.
* **Contract Type**: Select the type of contract. Options include Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of the contract. This field is optional.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the faculty agreement. This field is optional.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the faculty agreement. This field is optional.
* **Contract Term**: Enter the contract term details. This field is optional.
* **Contract Term Duration**: Enter the contract term duration. This field is optional.
* **Status**: Select the status of the faculty agreement. Options include Active and Inactive.
* **Renewal**: Enter the renewal date. This field is optional.
* **Contract Review**: Enter any contract review notes or comments. This field is optional.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the agreement date. This field is optional.
* **Agreement Status**: Select the agreement status. Options include Active and Inactive.
* **Agreement Term**: Enter the agreement term details. This field is optional.
* **Agreement Term Duration**: Enter the agreement term duration. This field is optional.
* **Renewal Date**: Enter the renewal date. This field is optional.
* **Renewal Status**: Select the renewal status. Options include Active and Inactive.
