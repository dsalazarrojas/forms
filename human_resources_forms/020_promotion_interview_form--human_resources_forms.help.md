# promotion_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotion interview form is used to collect information about candidates for promotion and to ensure a fair and thorough evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the candidate's name and job title.
2. Provide a brief description of the reasons for promoting the candidate.
3. List the candidate's qualifications and skills that make them suitable for promotion.
4. Record the details of the interview, including any observations or notes.
5. Evaluate the candidate's overall performance using the provided scale.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the candidate.
7. Enter the candidate's email address and contact number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Info** (`candidate_info`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's name and job title.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's current job title.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's full name.
* **Reason for Promotion** (`reason_for_promotion`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the reasons for promoting the candidate.
* **Qualifications** (`candidate_qualifications`, text, optional): List the candidate's qualifications and skills that make them suitable for promotion.
* **Skills** (`candidate_skills`, text, optional): List the candidate's skills that make them suitable for promotion.
* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`, text, optional): Record the details of the interview, including any observations or notes.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, select_one, required): Evaluate the candidate's overall performance using the provided scale (Very Good, Good, Not Good).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the candidate.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's contact number.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have a valid reason for promoting the candidate and that this reason is not already documented elsewhere.
- Complete all fields carefully and thoroughly.
- Use the evaluation scale to provide an objective assessment of the candidate's performance.
