# Employee Records Update Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to update employee records. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the employee's profile is current and reflects their latest status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the employee's current information to ensure accuracy before making any changes.
2. Enter the employee's updated details in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the correct options for department, job status, and job start date (if applicable).
4. Add any relevant notes about the employee's status.
5. Verify that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the unique ID assigned to the employee. This field is used to identify the employee in our records.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their official documentation (e.g. passport, ID card, etc.).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter the employee's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, optional): Select the department the employee belongs to from the list:
	+ Marketing
	+ HR
	+ IT
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's job title as it appears in our records.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the employee's email address used for internal communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's phone number for internal communication.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant information about the employee's status, such as reason for absence or leave.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of the employee from:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ On Leave
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the employee started their job in MM/DD/YYYY format, or leave this field blank if not applicable.

## Tips
Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any discrepancies in our records. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the employee's official documentation or contact HR for clarification.
