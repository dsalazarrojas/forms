# Religious Observation Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Religious Observation Time Off Form is used to request time off for a valid religious observation. This form ensures that your request is properly documented and submitted for approval.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by selecting the "Request Submission" page.
2. Fill in your religious observation details such as the type of observation and dates.
3. Provide a valid reason for your request.
4. If necessary, add any comments.
5. Submit your request for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Submission** (`request_submission`, text, required): This is the first page of the form where you can provide the details of your request.
- **Religious Observation** (`religious_observation`, select_one, required): Select the type of religious observation you are requesting time off for (e.g. Baptist, Catholic, etc.).
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will start your religious observation.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date your religious observation will end.
- **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days you will be taking off.
- **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a valid reason for your request.
- **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): If your request is approved, this field will be filled in automatically.
- **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, optional): If your request is approved, this field will be filled in automatically.
- **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): This field will be filled in automatically based on your request outcome.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your request, if necessary.
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): This field will be filled in automatically with your name or ID.
- **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field will be filled in automatically and displays the date your request was submitted.
