# Performance Evaluation Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for employees and managers to provide input on performance evaluation strategies for team members. It allows them to share their thoughts, goals, and expectations for the upcoming evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details as the Employee.
2. Provide your email and phone number.
3. Select your department.
4. Choose the performance review date and frequency.
5. Write a brief note about your performance evaluation strategy.
6. Enter the submitter's details (name, email, phone, and department).
7. Add any additional notes for the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official records. 
* **Email**: Enter your work email address that we use for official communications.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number that we use for official contacts.
* **Department**: Select your current department from the options provided (Human Resources, IT, Finance).
* **Performance Review Date**: Choose the date of your next performance review.
* **Performance Review Frequency**: Select how often you would like to have performance reviews (Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually).
* **Performance Evaluation Strategy**: Write a brief note about your goals and expectations for the performance evaluation.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting this form (e.g., your manager).
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Department**: Select the department of the person submitting this form (Human Resources, IT, Finance).
* **Submitter Notes**: Add any additional comments or context for the submitter.
