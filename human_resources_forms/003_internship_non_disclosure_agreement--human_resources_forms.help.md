# Internship Non Disclosure Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain an Internship Non Disclosure Agreement (NDA) from the intern, ensuring they understand and agree to maintain confidentiality of company information and intellectual property during their internship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the Introduction field carefully and understand the importance of maintaining confidentiality.
2. Select the Company Information field(s) that apply to you.
3. Provide your agreement to the terms of the agreement in the Agreement field.
4. Acknowledge that you will maintain confidentiality in the Acknowledgement field.
5. Sign and date the form in the Date Signed field.
6. Sign your name in the Signature field.
7. Enter your name in the Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (Introduction): This is a brief introduction to the form. Read it carefully to understand the purpose and importance of the form.
- **Company Information** (Company Information): Select all the applicable company information fields that pertain to your internship.
  - **Option 1**: This option is not clearly defined, but it may refer to company information such as projects, departments, or teams you'll be working with.
  - **Option 2**: This option is also not clearly defined, but it may refer to company information such as policies, procedures, or protocols.
  - **Option 3**: This option is not clearly defined, but it may refer to company information such as company-wide projects or initiatives.
- **Agreement** (Agreement): This is a free-text field where you can provide a statement of understanding and agreement to the terms of the agreement.
- **Acknowledgement** (Acknowledgement): Select "Yes" to acknowledge that you will maintain confidentiality, or "No" if you are unsure or cannot agree to the terms.
- **Date Signed** (Date Signed): This field is for the date you sign the form, which is a date field where you can enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Signature** (Signature): This is the physical signature of the intern, indicating that they have read and agreed to the terms of the agreement.
- **Name** (Name): This field is for the intern's name, which will be used for identification purposes only.
