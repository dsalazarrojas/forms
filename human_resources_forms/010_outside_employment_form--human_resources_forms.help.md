# Outside Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outside Employment Form is used to collect information about employees who are leaving the company or taking a new job. This form helps the HR department to properly document the employee's transition and maintain an up-to-date employee database.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting to complete this form, make sure you have all the necessary information ready. The form is divided into different sections, each of which has its own set of fields. Please fill out the form section by section, and make sure to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **new employee data** (`Yes/No`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are filling this form to update your current employment data. If you are leaving the company, select "No". This field is used to indicate whether you are submitting new employment data or not.
- **new employment date** (`Date`, required/optional): If you are selecting "Yes" in the "new employee data" field, please enter your new employment date. This field is used to record the date when you started your new job or position.
- **new start date** (`Date`, required/optional): If you are selecting "Yes" in the "new employee data" field, please enter your new start date. This field is used to record the date when you started your new job or position.
- **new location** (`Text`, required/optional): If you are selecting "Yes" in the "new employee data" field, please enter your new location. This field is used to record the location of your new job or position.
- **reason** (`Text`, required/optional): Please enter a brief description of your reason for leaving the company or taking a new job. This field is used to provide context about your transition.
- **manager name** (`Text`, required/optional): If you are leaving the company, please enter the name of your manager. This field is used to record the name of your supervisor or manager.
- **new position** (`Text`, required/optional): If you are selecting "Yes" in the "new employee data" field, please enter your new job title or position. This field is used to record your new job title or position.
- **old position** (`Text`, required/optional): If you are leaving the company, please enter your old job title or position. This field is used to record your previous job title or position.
- **reason leaving** (`Text`, required/optional): If you are leaving the company, please enter a brief description of your reason for leaving. This field is used to provide context about your transition.
- **employee type** (`Text`, required/optional): Please enter your employee type. This field is used to record your employee type.

**Tips**
Before filling out this form, make sure to review all the fields carefully and provide accurate information. If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact the HR department for assistance.
