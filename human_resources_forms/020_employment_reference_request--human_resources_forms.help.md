# employment_reference_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment_reference_request form is designed to gather information about an employee's employment reference request. The form helps the HR department to collect relevant details from the employee and their referee (previous employer or supervisor). The form is used to facilitate a smooth employment verification process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details in the "employee_details" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "contact_information" field.
3. Enter the referee's details in the "referee_details" field.
4. Select the current status of the request in the "request_status" field.
5. If necessary, provide a reason for the request in the "reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_details**: This field is for the employee to provide their personal details, such as name, job title, and employment dates.
* **contact_information**: Enter your contact information so that the HR department can reach out to you if needed.
* **referee_details**: Enter the details of your previous employer or supervisor, including their name, job title, and contact information.
* **request_status**: Select the current status of the request, whether it's "active" or "inactive".
* **reason**: If you need to provide a reason for the request, please enter it here.
