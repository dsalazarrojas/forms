# Fan Engagement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your engagement with our sports team. Please answer the following questions to provide us with valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite sports team.
2. Tell us how often you interact with us on social media.
3. Enter your email address associated with your social media handle (this is required to verify your identity).
4. Enter your name as it appears on your social media profile.
5. Choose your favorite way to engage with our team.
6. If applicable, share additional comments about your engagement experience.
7. Let us know if you've attended any of our events.
8. Select the time of day you usually interact with our team.
9. Rate your overall experience with our team on a scale of 1-5.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Provide your birthday in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite sports team?** (`favorite_team`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select your favorite sports team from the list below to show us your team spirit.
* **How do you currently interact with our team on social media?** (`social_media_interaction`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Choose how often you interact with us on social media.
* **Email address associated with your social media handle** (`email`, `email`, required: true)
	+ Enter the email address associated with your social media handle to verify your identity.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your social media profile.
* **Favorite way to engage with our team** (`engagement_channel`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select your favorite way to engage with our team.
* **Additional comments about your engagement experience** (`engagement_experience`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Tell us more about your engagement experience with our team.
* **Have you ever attended any of our events?** (`event_attendance`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if you've attended any of our events.
* **Time of day you usually interact with our team** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Select the time of day you usually interact with our team.
* **Rate your overall experience with our team** (`experience_rating`, `number`, required: true)
	+ Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Birthday** (`birthday`, `date`, required: true)
	+ Enter your birthday in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
