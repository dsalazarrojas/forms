# Schedule Your Interview Bell - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Schedule Your Interview Bell" form is a tool for individuals to schedule an interview with a representative. The form helps them choose a suitable time, date, day, location, and select an interviewer, with the option to provide additional notes or contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select a time that suits you best from the available options (00:00, 08:00, 07:20, 09:60, 12:00)
*   Choose a date for the interview (2020-01-01, 2020-01-02, 2020-01-03)
*   Select a day of the week for the interview (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday)
*   Choose a location for the interview (Room A, Room B, Room C)
*   Select the interviewer you'd like to meet with (Jim, Bob, Jane)
*   Provide any additional notes or comments about the interview
*   Enter your email address for contact information
*   Choose one or more phone numbers for contact information

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Select Time**: Choose a time slot that suits you best for the interview. This is a one-time selection from the available options.
*   **Select Date**: Pick a date for the interview from the provided options.
*   **Select Day**: Select a day of the week for the interview. This will help us determine the best schedule for you.
*   **Select Location**: Choose a location for the interview from the available rooms.
*   **Select Interviewer**: Choose the representative you'd like to meet with.
*   **Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the interview. This is an optional field.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information. This is an optional field.
*   **Select Phone**: Choose one or more phone numbers for contact information. This is an optional field.

## Tips
*   Be as specific as possible when selecting your time, date, and day for the interview.
*   Ensure that your selected interviewer is available for the chosen time and date.
*   If you have any special requirements or requests, please provide them in the note field.
*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
