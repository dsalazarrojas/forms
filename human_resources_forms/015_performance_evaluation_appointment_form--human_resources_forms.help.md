# performance_evaluation_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance Evaluation Appointment Form is used to document and evaluate employee performance. This form is completed by employees and their managers to provide a formal assessment of an employee's performance, goals, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Page 1: Performance Evaluation Form**
   * This page is a general note about the performance evaluation form.
2. **Page 2: Employee Information**
   * Enter the employee's name in the **employee_name** field. This is the person being evaluated.
3. **Page 3: Manager Information**
   * Enter the name of the manager responsible for evaluating the employee in the **manager_name** field.
4. **Page 4: Evaluation Details**
   * Choose the date of the evaluation in the **evaluation_date** field.
   * Choose the time of the evaluation in the **evaluation_time** field.
5. **Page 5: Evaluation Metrics and Score**
   * Enter the performance metrics and score related to the employee's performance in the **performance_metrics** and **evaluation_score** fields, respectively.
6. **Page 6: Feedback**
   * Enter any feedback or suggestions for improvement in the **feedback** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **manager_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the manager responsible for evaluating the employee.
* **evaluation_date** (Date, Optional): Choose the date of the evaluation.
* **evaluation_time** (Time, Optional): Choose the time of the evaluation.
* **performance_metrics** (Number, Optional): Enter performance metrics or data points related to the employee's performance.
* **evaluation_score** (Number, Optional): Enter a score or grade for the employee's performance.
* **feedback** (Text, Optional): Enter any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
