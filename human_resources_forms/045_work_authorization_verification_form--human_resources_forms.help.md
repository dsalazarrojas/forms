# Work Authorization Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for verifying work authorizations for employees in various situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): The full name of the employee being verified.
	* **Start Date** (`employment_start_date`): The date the employee started working.
	* **End Date** (`employment_end_date`): The date the employee ended working (if applicable).
	* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_employment`): The reason the employee left their previous employment (if applicable).
	* **Work Authorization Type** (`work_authorization_type`): Select the type of work authorization the employee has (e.g., "Authorization for Work").
	* **Address** (`employee_address`): The employee's address.
	* **City** (`employee_city`): The city where the employee lives.
	* **State** (`employee_state`): Confirm if the employee is from the state (yes/no).
	* **Country** (`employee_country`): The country where the employee lives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the employee being verified.
* **Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee started working in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee ended working (if applicable) in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_employment`, text, optional): Explain the reason why the employee left their previous employment (if applicable).
* **Work Authorization Type** (`work_authorization_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of work authorization the employee has from the provided options.
* **Address** (`employee_address`, text, optional): Enter the employee's full address.
* **City** (`employee_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the employee lives.
* **State** (`employee_state`, select_one, optional): Confirm if the employee is from the state (yes/no).
* **Country** (`employee_country`, text, optional): Enter the country where the employee lives.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form with accurate information to avoid errors.
* If the employee's work authorization type is not listed, select the "Other" option and provide a brief explanation in the "Reason for Leaving" field.
