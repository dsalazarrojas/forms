# Work Life Balance Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to acknowledge an employee's understanding and commitment to maintaining a healthy work-life balance. It helps to gather information about their work environment, policies, and personal experiences, which can inform HR initiatives to support employee well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete this form to acknowledge your understanding of and commitment to maintaining a healthy work-life balance.
2.  Review the list of policies provided and select all that have been reviewed.
3.  Rate your experience with each policy on a scale of 1-5.
4.  Provide any additional feedback about your work-life balance experience.
5.  Enter your manager's name and your employee ID for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false)
    Enter your name as it appears on your personnel document.
* **Has Reviewed Policies** (`policy_review`, select_multiple, required: true)
    Select all policies that you have reviewed:
        *   Has reviewed all policies
        *   Not reviewed all policies
* **Policy Rating** (`policy_rating`, number, required: true)
    Rate your experience with each policy on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false)
    Provide any additional comments or concerns about your work-life balance experience.
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, note, required: false)
    Please write a brief statement acknowledging your understanding and commitment to maintaining a healthy work-life balance.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false)
    Enter the name of your supervisor or manager for reference.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required: false)
    Enter your employee ID for reference.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: true)
    Enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
