# CASA Supervisor Weekly Progress Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CASA Supervisor Weekly Progress Report is a template designed to track staff productivity and challenges. It's completed by site supervisors to provide a weekly snapshot of staff performance, identify areas for improvement, and facilitate constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the reporting site location.
2. Identify the staff member under review and provide their name.
3. List the key tasks completed this week.
4. Evaluate the percentage of weekly goals achieved.
5. Document any primary challenges encountered.
6. Describe the support provided to the staff member this week.
7. Identify any training or development needs for the staff member.
8. Outline top priorities for next week.
9. Report any attendance or punctuality issues.
10. Rate the staff member's overall weekly performance.
11. Provide additional comments or feedback.
12. Confirm that you've discussed this report with the staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Supervisor Name** (Supervisor Name, text, required)
Enter your name and title as the supervisor.

### 2. **Reporting Site Location** (Reporting Site Location, select_one, required)
Choose the location where the report is being submitted from.

### 3. **Week Ending Date (Friday)** (Week Ending Date (Friday), date, required)
Enter the last day of the week (typically Friday) the report covers.

### 4. **Staff Member Name** (Staff Member Name, text, required)
Enter the name of the staff member being reported on.

### 5. **Key Tasks Completed This Week** (Key Tasks Completed This Week, text, required)
List the main achievements or projects finalized this week.

### 6. **Percentage of Weekly Goals Achieved** (Percentage of Weekly Goals Achieved, select_one, required)
Choose the percentage of goals achieved from 0-100%.

### 7. **Primary Challenges Encountered** (Primary Challenges Encountered, text, required)
Describe any obstacles that hindered progress.

### 8. **Support Provided by Supervisor** (Support Provided by Supervisor, text, required)
Describe how you assisted the staff member this week.

### 9. **Identified Training or Development Needs** (Identified Training or Development Needs, text, required)
List any training or development needs identified for the staff member.

### 10. **Top Priorities for Next Week** (Top Priorities for Next Week, text, required)
Outline the staff member's top priorities for the next week.

### 11. **Were There Any Attendance or Punctuality Issues?** (Were there any attendance or punctuality issues?, select_one, required)
Choose from the given options regarding attendance or punctuality issues.

### 12. **Overall Weekly Performance Rating** (Overall Weekly Performance Rating, select_one, required)
Choose from the options (Unsatisfactory, Satisfactory, Commendable, Outstanding) based on the staff member's performance.

### 13. **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, required)
Provide any additional comments or feedback for the staff member.

### 14. **I have discussed this report with the staff member** (I have discussed this report with the staff member, select_one, required)
Choose 'True' if you've discussed the report with the staff member, 'No (planned for next week)' otherwise.
