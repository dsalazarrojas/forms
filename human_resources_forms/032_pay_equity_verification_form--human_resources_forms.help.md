# Pay Equity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pay Equity Verification Form is used to collect information about employees' job roles, compensation packages, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Page 1

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your pay stubs and benefits documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it is currently listed in the company directory.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department that you currently work in. 
*   Select all departments that apply.

### Page 2

* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have a job category or not. This is typically determined by your job description.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter your current salary as it appears on your pay stubs.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Pay Period** (`pay_period`, select_one, optional): Select the pay period that you are currently in. This is usually every two weeks or bi-weekly.
* **Pay Basis** (`pay_basis`, select_one, optional): Select the pay basis that you are currently paid on, e.g., hourly, weekly, etc.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, optional): Select all benefits that you receive.

### Page 3

* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are classified as exempt or non-exempt.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please reach out to your HR representative for clarification.
* This form is meant to be completed by employees in various roles and departments within the company.
* All employees are encouraged to complete the form to help ensure pay equity.
