# Headcount Forecasting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request headcount forecasting for various departments and teams.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the submit option (1) to confirm that you are submitting a request.
2. Enter the start date (2) and end date (3) of the forecast period.
3. Select the role (4) that applies to you:
	* Manager: I am the manager of the team.
	* Team Member: I am a team member.
	* Other: Select this option if you are submitting on behalf of someone else.
4. Enter your name (5) and contact phone number (11) and email (12) for follow-up.
5. Enter the team size (6) and duration (9) of the forecast period.
6. Select the start time (7) and end time (8) of the first day of the forecast period.
7. Enter the notes (10) for the forecast.
8. Select the department (14) and location (15) of the forecast.
9. Enter the team lead's name (16), title (18), and contact information (20).
10. Enter the manager's name (17), title (19), and contact information (21).
11. Select the department title (21) and contact information (22).
12. Enter the manager's manager name (23), team lead's manager name (24), and department manager name (25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Submit Request**: Confirm that you are submitting a request.
* **2. Start Date**: Enter the date when the forecast period starts.
* **3. End Date**: Enter the date when the forecast period ends.
* **4. Role**: Select your role in the team.
	+ **Manager**: You are the manager of the team.
	+ **Team Member**: You are a team member.
	+ **Other**: Select this option if you are submitting on behalf of someone else.
* **5. Submitter Name**: Enter your name for follow-up.
* **6. Team Size**: Enter the size of the team for the forecast period.
* **7. Start Time**: Enter the start time of the first day of the forecast period.
* **8. End Time**: Enter the end time of the first day of the forecast period.
* **9. Duration**: Enter the duration of the forecast period.
* **10. Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the forecast.
* **11. Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up.
* **12. Contact Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up.
* **13. Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager.
* **14. Department**: Select the department of the forecast.
* **15. Location**: Enter the location of the forecast.
* **16. Team Lead**: Enter the name of the team lead.
* **17. Manager Title**: Enter the title of the manager.
* **18. Team Lead Title**: Enter the title of the team lead.
* **19. Department Title**: Enter the title of the department.
* **20. Team Lead Contact**: Enter the contact information of the team lead.
* **21. Manager Contact**: Enter the contact information of the manager.
* **22. Department Contact**: Enter the contact information of the department.
* **23. Manager Manager**: Enter the name of the manager's manager.
* **24. Team Lead Manager**: Enter the name of the team lead's manager.
* **25. Department Manager**: Enter the name of the department manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure the success of the forecast.
* Use the notes field for any additional information that may help with the forecast.
* If you are unsure of any information, please contact your manager or team lead for guidance.
