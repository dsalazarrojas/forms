# HR Interview Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from candidates during the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's contact information on pages 1-3.
2. Describe the candidate's work history, skills, references, and education on pages 4-7.
3. Provide details about previous employment, salary request, expected salary, benefits, and availability on pages 8-12.
4. Select the interviewer(s) on page 18.
5. Add any comments or notes on page 19.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name (page 1)**: Enter the candidate's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Job Title (page 2)**: Select the job title for which the candidate is applying.
* **Contact Info (page 3)**: Enter the candidate's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Work History (page 4)**: Describe the candidate's work history, including previous jobs, positions, and dates of employment.
* **Skills (page 5)**: List the candidate's relevant skills and qualifications.
* **References (page 6)**: Provide the names and contact information of the candidate's professional references.
* **Education (page 7)**: Describe the candidate's educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Work Experience (page 8)**: Describe the candidate's work experience, including job titles, dates, and achievements.
* **Previous Employment (page 9)**: Describe the candidate's previous employment, including job titles, dates, and reasons for leaving.
* **Salary Request (page 10)**: Indicate the candidate's expected salary range.
* **Expected Salary (page 11)**: Describe the candidate's expected salary range.
* **Benefits (page 12)**: List the benefits the candidate is expecting from the employer.
* **Availability (page 13)**: Indicate the candidate's availability for an interview.
* **Employment Start Date (page 14)**: Enter the date the candidate is available to start work.
* **Employment End Date (page 15)**: Enter the date the candidate's previous employment ended.
* **Interview Date (page 16)**: Indicate the date of the interview.
* **Interview Location (page 17)**: Enter the location of the interview.
* **Interviewers (page 18)**: Select the interviewer(s) who will be conducting the interview.
* **Comments (page 19)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the candidate.

Note: The fields marked as required are essential to complete the form. Please ensure to fill them accurately.
