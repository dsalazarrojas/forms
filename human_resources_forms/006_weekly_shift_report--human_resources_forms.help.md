# weekly_shift_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly shift report form is used to collect information from employees about their weekly shifts, including start and end dates, shift days, hours worked, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Select the week starting date using the `week_starting_date` field.
3. Choose the days you worked during the week using the `shift_days` field.
4. Enter the number of hours you worked in the `hours_worked` field.
5. Enter your start and end times using the `shift_start_time` and `shift_end_time` fields.
6. Add any comments about your shifts in the `comments` field.
7. Enter your employee ID in the `employee_id` field.
8. Enter your manager's ID in the `manager_id` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **week_starting_date** (date, required/optional): Select the date when your week started.
* **shift_days** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the days you worked during the week.
	+ Available options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
* **hours_worked** (number, required/optional): Enter the total hours you worked during the week.
* **shift_start_time** (time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **shift_end_time** (time, required/optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **comments** (note, required/optional): Add any comments about your shifts.
* **employee_id** (text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID.
* **manager_id** (text, required/optional): Enter your manager's ID.
