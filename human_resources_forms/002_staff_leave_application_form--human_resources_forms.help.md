# Staff Leave Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Staff Leave Application Form is used for employees to formally request leave from work. This form is required for all leave types, including annual leave, sick leave, and bereavement leave. It helps facilitate the leave approval process and ensures that all necessary details are captured for record-keeping and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all the required information.
2. Select the correct reporting officer from the list of options provided.
3. Ensure that all fields are correctly filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date from which you would like to start your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you would like to return to work after your leave.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Provide a clear reason for your leave.
* **Duration Of Leave** (`duration_of_leave`, number, optional): Enter the total number of days you would like to take for your leave.
* **Number Of Days** (`number_of_days`, number, optional): Enter the actual number of working days you would like to take for your leave.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Mention the department you belong to.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, select_one, optional): Select the reporting officer to whom you report.
