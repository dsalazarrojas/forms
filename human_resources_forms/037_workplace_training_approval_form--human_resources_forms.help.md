# workplace_training_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workplace training approval form is used to request and track approval for training activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that you only submit one request per form.
3. Provide all necessary details, such as training type, location, duration, start and end dates, and other relevant information.
4. Make sure to check the "Training Type" field to ensure you are selecting the correct type of training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Training Request** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`)
The Training Request field is the initial description of the training activity you are requesting.

### 2. **Training Details** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`)
This field is for providing more details about the training activity, such as the objective and expected outcomes.

### 3. **Training Duration** (`3`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter the duration of the training in hours.

### 4. **Training Start Date** (`4`, `date`, required: `false`)
Enter the start date of the training.

### 5. **Training End Date** (`5`, `date`, required: `false`)
Enter the end date of the training.

### 6. **Approval Status** (`6`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Select the current status of your training request. Options are: Pending, Approved, Rejected.

### 7. **Training Approver** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Identify the person who approved or rejected your training request. Options are: Yes, No.

### 8. **Training Type** (`8`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Select the type of training you are requesting. Options are: On-the-Job, Outside of Work, Classroom.

### 9. **Training Cost** (`9`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter the cost associated with the training.

### 10. **Training Location** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`)
Enter the location of the training.

### 11. **Training Attendance** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
Indicate if you attended the training. Options are: Attended, Not Attended, No Answer.

### 12. **Training Notes** (`12`, `text`, required: `false`)
Add any additional notes about the training activity.

### 13. **Submitted By** (`13`, `email`, required: `false`)
Enter the email of the person who submitted the training request.

### 14. **Submitted On** (`14`, `date`, required: `false`)
Enter the date when the training request was submitted.

### 15. **Updated By** (`15`, `email`, required: `false`)
Enter the email of the person who last updated the training request.

### 16. **Updated On** (`16`, `date`, required: `false`)
Enter the date when the training request was last updated.

### 17. **Created By** (`17`, `email`, required: `false`)
Enter the email of the person who created the training request.

### 18. **Created On** (`18`, `date`, required: `false`)
Enter the date when the training request was created.

### 19. **Approval By** (`19`, `email`, required: `false`)
Enter the email of the person who approved or rejected the training request.

### 20. **Approved Date** (`20`, `date`, required: `false`)
Enter the date when the training request was approved or rejected.

### 21. **Approval Status** (`21`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Select the status of your approval after the review process. Options are: Pending, Approved, Rejected.

### 22. **Training Type 2** (`22`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Select the second type of training. Options are: Yes, No.

### 23. **Training Type 3** (`23`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Select the third type of training. Options are: Yes, No.

### 24. **Training Type 4** (`24`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Select the fourth type of training. Options are: Yes, No.

### 25. **Training Type 5** (`25`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Select the fifth type of training. Options are: Yes, No.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and thorough information.
- Check your training request carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure that you are selecting the correct training type and status.
- If you need to update your training request, make sure to include all the necessary information.
