# Union Representative Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the reason for a union representative's exit from the company. It is a required form for union representatives who are leaving the company, and it helps HR and union representative management teams maintain accurate records and ensure a smooth transition for the next representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for the union representative's exit. The options available are:
	* Union Agreement
	* Voluntarily
	* Other (select this option if the reason for exit is not listed above)
2. If you selected "Other" as the reason for exit, please provide the specific reason in the "other_reason" field.
3. Enter the name of the union representative.
4. Enter the title of the union representative.
5. Select the date when the union representative's exit will be effective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **union_representative_exit_reason** (`Select Multiple (Union Agreement, Voluntarily, Other)`, Required/Optional): Choose the reason for the union representative's exit from the available options.
* **other_reason** (`Text`, Optional): If you selected "Other" as the reason for exit, provide a detailed description of the reason in this text field.
* **union_representative_name** (`Text`, Optional): Enter the name of the union representative.
* **union_representative_title** (`Text`, Optional): Enter the title of the union representative.
* **effective_date** (`Date`, Optional): Enter the date when the union representative's exit will be effective.
* **union_representative_contact** (`Text`, Optional): Enter the union representative's contact information for further communication.
* **union_representative_notes** (`Note`, Optional): Add any additional notes about the union representative's exit.
