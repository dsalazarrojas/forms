# Information Technology Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from candidates applying for a position in Information Technology.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields: Candidate Name, Application Date (if applicable), Email Address, Contact Info, Resume Upload, and Availability.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant answer that best describes your situation.
3. Upload your resume in the specified format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`Candidate Name`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Application Date** (`Application Date`, optional): If you have a specific application date in mind, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Position Sought** (`Position Sought`, optional): Select one or more positions you are interested in applying for from the list, or choose "Other" if you do not find a match.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, optional): Please enter the number of years you have relevant work experience.
* **Education** (`Education`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have completed relevant education or training for the position, or "No" otherwise.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have the required skills for the position, or "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, required): Please enter your contact information, including your phone number and any other relevant details.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, required): Please enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Resume Upload** (`Resume Upload`, required): Attach your resume in the specified format.
* **Personal Statement** (`Personal Statement`, optional): Please provide a brief statement about yourself and why you are a good fit for the position.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, required): Please enter your availability to start, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Referees** (`Referees`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have referees who can vouch for your experience, or "No" otherwise.
* **Referee 1 Name** (`Referee 1 Name`, optional): Please enter the name of your referee 1.
* **Referee 1 Phone** (`Referee 1 Phone`, optional): Please enter the phone number of your referee 1.
* **Referee 1 Email** (`Referee 1 Email`, optional): Please enter the email address of your referee 1.
* **Referee 2 Name** (`Referee 2 Name`, optional): Please enter the name of your referee 2.
* **Referee 2 Phone** (`Referee 2 Phone`, optional): Please enter the phone number of your referee 2.
* **Referee 2 Email** (`Referee 2 Email`, optional): Please enter the email address of your referee 2.
* **Referee 3 Name** (`Referee 3 Name`, optional): Please enter the name of your referee 3.
* **Referee 3 Phone** (`Referee 3 Phone`, optional): Please enter the phone number of your referee 3.
* **Referee 3 Email** (`Referee 3 Email`, optional): Please enter the email address of your referee 3.
* **Referee 4 Name** (`Referee 4 Name`, optional): Please enter the name of your referee 4.
* **Referee 4 Phone** (`Referee 4 Phone`, optional): Please enter the phone number of your referee 4.
* **Referee 4 Email** (`Referee 4 Email`, optional): Please enter the email address of your referee 4.
* **Referee 5 Name** (`Referee 5 Name`, optional): Please enter the name of your referee 5.
* **Referee 5 Phone** (`Referee 5 Phone`, optional): Please enter the phone number of your referee 5.
* **Referee 5 Email** (`Referee 5 Email`, optional): Please enter the email address of your referee 5.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Use the correct format for dates and contact information.
- Attach your resume in the specified format.
- If you have any issues while filling out the form, please contact the hiring team.
