# Employee Non Solicitation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain an employee's acknowledgment of their understanding and agreement to the non-solicitation terms and conditions of their employment. This form is essential for HR or management to ensure employees comprehend their obligations and limitations when it comes to non-solicitation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the non-solicitation terms and conditions provided.
2. Acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms.
3. Date and sign the acknowledgment section to confirm your understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non-solicitation Terms** (`non_solicitation_terms`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description or summary of your company's non-solicitation terms and conditions. This section should outline what is not allowed during your employment, such as soliciting clients, customers, or employees for a competing business.
* **Acknowledgment Date** (`acknowledgment_date`, date, optional):
	+ Provide the date you acknowledged and understood the non-solicitation terms and conditions.
* **Acknowledgment Signature** (`acknowledgment_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Sign to confirm that you have read and understood the non-solicitation terms and conditions.
