# Employee Retention Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Retention Interview Form is designed to collect information from departing employees on their reasons for leaving the organization. This form helps management understand the reasons behind the employee's departure and can aid in making informed decisions for future employee retention and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the Employee Retention Questionnaire with your reasons for leaving the company.
2. Rate your satisfaction score out of 10 (if applicable).
3. Select the reason for your departure from the provided options.
4. Answer the open-ended question (if you feel it's necessary to provide additional information).
5. Optionally, provide your email and phone number for follow-up contact (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Retention Questionnaire** (`employee_retention_questionnaire`, text, required/optional): This field is an open-ended text area where you can provide a detailed reason for your departure. Please be as specific and honest as possible.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`, number, required/optional): If you feel it's applicable, rate your overall satisfaction with your job and the company on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Retention Reason** (`retention_reason`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided reasons for your departure. Please choose the one that best describes your situation.
* **Open-ended Question** (`open_ended_question`, note, required/optional): If you feel there's more to share about your reasons for leaving, use this field to provide additional information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted for a follow-up conversation, provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone for a follow-up conversation, provide your phone number.
