# covid-19-daily-screening-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Covid-19 Daily Screening Form is a critical tool to track and monitor the spread of the virus within the organization. It helps in maintaining accurate records of employee symptoms, test results, vaccination status, and travel history. This information will aid in early detection and response to potential outbreaks.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your employee name, contact number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2.  Select the correct date and time for when you were screened.
3.  Provide an account of any symptoms you're experiencing, if applicable.
4.  Report your travel history, including any international or interstate travel.
5.  Specify your exposure risk, if you've been exposed to anyone suspected of having COVID-19.
6.  List your medical history if it's relevant.
7.  Report your test results (if tested positive, pending, or negative).
8.  If applicable, report your vaccination status and details.
9.  Indicate your next follow-up appointment or date.
10. Submit the form after reviewing and confirming all the details.
11. Finally, submit the form, and your submitted date will be automatically recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Fill in your name as an employee of the company.
    *   This field is mandatory to track your identity and affiliation with the organization.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number for further communication.
    *   This field is not mandatory but will help in contacting you for follow-ups or further information.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email for official communication purposes.
    *   Although not mandatory, this helps in official communication and reduces the risk of miscommunication.
*   **Date Screened** (`date_screened`, date, optional): Select the date you were screened.
    *   This field will help in tracking when you were screened and aid in monitoring the spread of the virus.
*   **Time Screened** (`time_screened`, time, optional): Report the time you were screened.
    *   Time is an essential factor in tracking the spread of the virus.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms you're experiencing.
    *   If you're feeling unwell, please report your symptoms accurately.
*   **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, optional): Report any international or interstate travel.
    *   Accurate travel history will aid in monitoring potential risks.
*   **Exposure Risk** (`exposure_risk`, text, optional): Report if you've been exposed to anyone suspected of having COVID-19.
    *   This helps in identifying potential risks.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If applicable, report any pre-existing medical conditions.
    *   Your medical history is essential in understanding any pre-existing medical conditions.
*   **Test Results** (`test_results`, select\_one, optional): Report your test results (positive, negative, or pending).
    *   Accurate test results help in monitoring and controlling the spread of the virus.
*   **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, optional): Report the date you were tested.
    *   This field is crucial in understanding your test status.
*   **Test Time** (`test_time`, time, optional): Report the time you were tested.
    *   Time is an essential factor in monitoring the test results.
*   **Next Follow-up** (`next_follow_up`, date, optional): Report the date and time of your next follow-up appointment.
    *   This field helps in scheduling follow-ups and keeping track of your health.
*   **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Report the time of your next follow-up.
    *   Accurate follow-up time is important in monitoring your health.
*   **Medical Supplies** (`medical_supplies`, text, optional): Report any medical supplies or equipment you have.
    *   This field helps in identifying potential needs.
*   **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select\_one, optional): Report your vaccination status.
    *   Accurate vaccination status is crucial for public health monitoring.
*   **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, optional): Report the date you received your vaccination.
    *   This field helps in tracking your vaccination status.
*   **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, time, optional): Report the time you received your vaccination.
    *   Time is an essential factor in monitoring your vaccination status.
*   **Next Vaccination** (`next_vaccination`, date, optional): Report the date of your next vaccination appointment.
    *   This field helps in scheduling your next vaccination.
*   **Vaccination Schedule** (`vaccination_schedule`, text, optional): Report your vaccination schedule.
    *   Accurate vaccination schedule will aid in monitoring your vaccination progress.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any other information that might be helpful for your follow-up appointments.
    *   This field is optional but helps in keeping track of your health status.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Confirm all details before submitting the form.
    *   Review all the details before submitting the form.
*   **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Automatically recorded when you submit the form.
    *   This field is automatically populated upon form submission.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Automatically recorded when you submit the form.
    *   This field is automatically populated upon form submission.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill in all relevant fields accurately.
*   Report all symptoms and medical conditions truthfully.
*   Keep your contact information up to date for smooth communication.
*   Follow up on your vaccination status regularly for public health monitoring.
*   Review the form thoroughly before submission to avoid errors.
*   Be honest and accurate with your symptoms, medical history, and vaccination status for the betterment of public health.
