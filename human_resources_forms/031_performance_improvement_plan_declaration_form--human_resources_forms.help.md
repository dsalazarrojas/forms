# performance_improvement_plan_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance improvement plan declaration form is designed to help employees document and track their performance improvement plans, allowing HR departments to monitor progress and provide support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select the date you are declaring your performance improvement plan in the "Performance Improvement Plan Declaration Date" field.
4. Describe your performance improvement goal in the "Performance Improvement Goal Description" field.
5. List the action steps you plan to take to achieve your goal in the "Action Steps" field.
6. Enter the expected completion date for your performance improvement plan in the "Expected Completion Date" field.
7. Enter the actual completion date for your performance improvement plan in the "Actual Completion Date" field (if applicable).
8. Choose the status of your performance improvement plan (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started) in the "Status" field.
9. Enter the date of your review in the "Review Date" field.
10. Optionally, provide any review comments from your manager or reviewer in the "Reviewer Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your employee name to identify yourself.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title to provide context to your performance improvement plan.
* **Performance Improvement Plan Declaration Date** (`performance_improvement_plan_declaration_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are declaring your performance improvement plan.
* **Performance Improvement Goal** (`performance_improvement_goal`, text, optional): Describe your performance improvement goal to clarify your objectives.
* **Performance Improvement Goal Description** (`performance_improvement_goal_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your performance improvement goal.
* **Action Steps** (`action_steps`, text, optional): List the specific actions you plan to take to achieve your performance improvement goal.
* **Expected Completion Date** (`expected_completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date by which you expect to complete your performance improvement plan.
* **Actual Completion Date** (`actual_completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the actual date you completed your performance improvement plan (if applicable).
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your performance improvement plan (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your review.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments from your manager or reviewer regarding your performance improvement plan.

## Tips

* Be specific and detailed when describing your performance improvement goal and action steps.
* Ensure you enter accurate dates for your expected and actual completion dates.
* Update your status regularly to reflect your progress.
* Use this form to track and report on your performance improvement plans.
* Review and discuss your performance improvement plan with your manager or reviewer as needed.
