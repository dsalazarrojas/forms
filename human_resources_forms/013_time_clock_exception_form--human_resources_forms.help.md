# Time Clock Exception Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Clock Exception Form is used to request exceptions to an employee's time clock, which are used to track employee attendance and time off.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the exception you are submitting.
2. Enter the start and end times for the exception.
3. Choose the type of exception you are submitting (on-the-job injury, off-the-job injury, illness, or other).
4. Enter any additional comments or details about the exception.
5. If you are an employee, select the employee ID associated with your account.
6. If you are a manager, select the manager ID associated with your account.
7. Enter your email address for submitted by and submit to fields.
8. Enter the date the request was submitted.
9. Enter the date the request was approved or rejected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exception Date** (`exception_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the exception you are submitting.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the exception.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the exception.
* **Reason** (`reason`, note, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for the exception.
* **Exception Type** (`exception_type`, select one, required): Choose the type of exception you are submitting.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID associated with your account.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID associated with your account.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person or department submitting the request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the exception.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Approved On** (`approved_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the request was approved or rejected, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure you are submitting the correct exception type and date of the exception.
* Enter as much detail as possible in the Reason field to help the approver understand the request.
* Use the correct IDs associated with your account to submit the request.
* Enter accurate dates and times to ensure accurate tracking.
