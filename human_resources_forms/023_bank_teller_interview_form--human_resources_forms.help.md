# Bank Teller Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Bank Teller Interview Form is a comprehensive form used by Human Resources (HR) staff to collect information about a candidate's qualifications, skills, and experience in the banking industry. This form is a crucial tool for assessing a candidate's suitability for banking positions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form with accurate and honest information**. Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
2. **Be truthful**. The information provided will be used to assess your qualifications for the position.
3. **Use the correct units of measurement**. For example, when answering the "Years of Banking Experience" question, use whole numbers (e.g., 5, 10, 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Full Name** (`candidate_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`candidate_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`candidate_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address in the format: Street, City, State, ZIP.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, select_one, required): Select the position you are applying for from the options: Head Teller, Teller, Senior Teller, Part-Time Teller, or Float Teller.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who conducted the interview.
* **Years of Banking Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in banking.
* **Previous Bank Experience** (`previous_bank_experience`, text, optional): If you have relevant experience, describe your previous experience in banking.
* **Cash Handling Experience** (`cash_handling_experience`, select_one, required): Select your level of experience with cash handling from the options: Extensive, Moderate, Limited, or None.
* **Customer Service Skills** (`customer_service_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your customer service skills as: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your communication skills as: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Attention to Detail** (`attention_to_detail_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your attention to detail as: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Numerical Ability** (`numerical_ability_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your numerical ability as: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your technical skills as: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Integrity and Ethics** (`integrity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your integrity and ethics as: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Teamwork Ability** (`teamwork_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your teamwork ability as: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required): Select your availability as: Full-Time, Part-Time, Flexible Schedule, or Weekends Only.
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date_availability`, date, required): Enter the date you are available to start.
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, text, optional): If you have salary expectations, enter them in the format: $ [amount].
* **Certifications or Licenses** (`certification_licenses`, text, optional): If you have any relevant certifications or licenses, describe them.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select if you consent to a background check as: Yes, I consent, or False.
* **Drug Test Consent** (`drug_test_consent`, select_one, required): Select if you consent to a drug test as: Yes, I consent, or False.
* **Interviewer Recommendation** (`interviewer_recommendation`, select_one, required): Select the interviewer's recommendation as: Highly Recommend, Recommend, Recommend with Reservations, or Do Not Recommend.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, enter them here.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* Use the correct units of measurement when answering questions.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask the interviewer for clarification.
* Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
