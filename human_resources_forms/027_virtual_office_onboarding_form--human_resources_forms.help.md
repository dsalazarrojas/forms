# Virtual Office Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Office Onboarding Form is used to collect information about your company, its services, and representatives. This form helps us understand your company's needs and provide the best possible services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide company information such as name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Add your company's contact details, including representatives' names and their roles.
3. Specify your company's service details and services.
4. Choose your office's location, operating hours, and days.
5. Select the start and end dates of your service.
6. Choose your billing cycle and frequency.
7. Specify the billing amount.
8. Add your company's social media handles (Facebook, Instagram).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information** (Page 1)
	+ **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
	+ **Company Address**: Enter your company's address.
* **Contact Details** (Page 1)
	+ **Contact Name**: Enter the contact person's name.
	+ **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email.
* **Company Service Details** (Page 2)
	+ **Service Details**: Enter your company's service details.
* **Company Representatives** (Page 2)
	+ **Representatives**: List your company's representatives.
* **Service Administrator** (Page 3)
	+ **Service Administrator**: Choose the person in charge of the service.
* **Company Administrators** (Page 3)
	+ **Company Administrators**: Choose who has access to the company's administration.
* **Office Location** (Page 4)
	+ **Office Location**: Specify your office location.
* **Company Services** (Page 4)
	+ **Company Services**: Specify your company services.
* **Company Services Details** (Page 5)
	+ **Company Services Details**: Specify your company's services details.
* **Office Hours** (Page 5)
	+ **Office Hours**: Specify your office hours.
* **Office Days** (Page 6)
	+ **Office Days**: Specify which days your office is open.
* **Service Start Date** (Page 6)
	+ **Service Start Date**: Enter the start date of your service.
* **Service End Date** (Page 7)
	+ **Service End Date**: Enter the end date of your service.
* **Billing Cycle** (Page 7)
	+ **Billing Cycle**: Choose your billing cycle.
* **Billing Frequency** (Page 8)
	+ **Billing Frequency**: Choose your billing frequency.
* **Billing Amount** (Page 8)
	+ **Billing Amount**: Enter the billing amount.
* **Company Email** (Page 9)
	+ **Company Email**: Enter your company's email.
* **Company Phone** (Page 9)
	+ **Company Phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Website** (Page 10)
	+ **Company Website**: Enter your company's website.
* **Company Facebook** (Page 10)
	+ **Company Facebook**: Enter your company's Facebook handle.
* **Company Instagram** (Page 11)
	+ **Company Instagram**: Enter your company's Instagram handle.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
