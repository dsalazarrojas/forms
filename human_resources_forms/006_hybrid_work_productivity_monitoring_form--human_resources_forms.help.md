# hybrid_work_productivity_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hybrid_work_productivity_monitoring_form is used to collect user information for productivity monitoring and hybrid work purposes. It is essential to fill this form accurately to help improve work processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review each field for correct completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID (1)** (`User ID`, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Last Day of Work (2)** (`Last Day of Work`, date, required): Enter the date you last worked.
* **Work Hours (3)** (`Work Hours`, time, required): Enter your regular work hours or schedule.
* **Note (4)** (`Note`, note, required): Add any important notes or comments about your work.
* **Email (5)** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone (6)** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Communication Preference (7)** (`Communication Preference`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to communicate with your team or management:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
* **Work Style (8)** (`Work Style`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred work style:
	+ In-person meetings
	+ Virtual meetings
	+ Both
* **Note 2 (9)** (`Note 2`, note, required): Add any additional important notes or comments.
* **Email 2 (10)** (`Email 2`, email, required): Enter your personal email address.
* **Phone 2 (11)** (`Phone 2`, text, required): Enter your personal mobile phone number.
* **Work Preference (12)** (`Work Preference`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred work style:
	+ Flexibility
	+ Structure
	+ Both
* **Note 3 (13)** (`Note 3`, note, required): Add any final important notes or comments.
