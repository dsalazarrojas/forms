# Staff Movement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The staff movement form is used to record the movement of staff members from one site to another.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the staff member you are moving from and to (site_1 to site_2).
2. Check if the staff member is leaving or staying at the current site (site_1).
3. Select the site the staff member is moving to (site_2).
4. Enter the date and time the staff member is leaving the current site (date and time).
5. Enter the date and time the staff member starts at the new site (effective_date and effective_time).
6. Enter a reason for the staff member's movement (reason_for_movement).
7. Select the tools assigned to the staff member at the current site (assigned_tool).
8. Select the location of the staff member at the current site (location).
9. Select the sites where the staff member will be working at the new location (site).
10. Select any other sites where the staff member will be working at the new location (site_1_1, site_2_1, site_3_1, site_4_1, site_5_1, site_6_1, site_7_1, site_8_1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. site_1**: Select the site you are moving from.*
* **2. site_1_label**: This is the label for the site you are moving from.*
* **3. site_2**: Select the site you are moving to.*
* **4. date**: Enter the date the staff member will leave the current site.*
* **5. time**: Enter the time the staff member will leave the current site.*
* **6. reason_for_movement**: Enter a reason for the staff member's movement.*
* **7. site_2_1**: Select sites where the staff member will be working at the new location.*

Note: Some fields are optional and only required if specified in the form's configuration.
