# Maternity Leave Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for maternity leave absence requests to be processed by the human resources department. It captures relevant information to evaluate and approve or deny the leave request.

## How To Complete This Form

* Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before proceeding.
* Enter your employee ID in the field "Employee ID" and click on "Next" to move on to the next step.
* If you have already worked on this form, you can resume from where you left off using the "Resume" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (`employee_id`, text, required):** Enter your employee ID to identify yourself in the system. This is a unique identifier that helps the HR department process your request.
* **Last Day Worked (`prophets_last_day_worked`, date, optional):** If you have already stopped working, enter the date you last worked. If you are still working, you can leave this field blank.
* **Start Date (`start_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date you plan to start your maternity leave. If you are not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **End Date (`end_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date you plan to return to work after your maternity leave. If you are not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Reason for Leave (`reason_for_leave`, text, required):** Briefly explain the reason for your maternity leave request. This will help the HR department evaluate your request.
* **Return Date (`return_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date you plan to return to work after your maternity leave. If you are not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Manager Approval (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional):** Select "Approve" or "Deny" to indicate the manager's initial decision on your leave request. This is not required for the HR department to evaluate your request.
* **Note (`note`, note, optional):** Add any additional comments or notes related to your request. This will be reviewed by the HR department to further evaluate your request.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional):** Enter your email address to receive updates on your leave request status.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional):** Enter your phone number to receive phone calls or texts from the HR department regarding your leave request.
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional):** Select the tool(s) assigned to you for this leave request. This will help track progress and communication.
* **Assigned Tool ID (`assigned_tool_id`, number, optional):** Enter the ID of the tool assigned to you for this leave request. This is not required but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Hr Approve (`hr_approve`, select_one, optional):** Select "Approve" or "Deny" to indicate the HR department's final decision on your leave request. This is not required but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Notes (`notes`, select_multiple, optional):** Select any notes related to your request, such as "Note 1", "Note 2", or "Note 3". This will be reviewed by the HR department for further evaluation.
* **Comments (`comments`, text, required):** Add any additional comments or details regarding your maternity leave request. This is a required field to ensure a thorough evaluation of your request.
