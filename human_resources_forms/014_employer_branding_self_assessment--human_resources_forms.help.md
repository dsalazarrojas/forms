# Employer Branding Self-Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employer Branding Self-Assessment form is a tool for employees to provide their opinions and thoughts about the company's employer brand. The form is designed to help internal teams understand employee perceptions and gather insights for potential improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read each question carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. Provide as much detail as you can in the text boxes.
3. Select the relevant options for the select questions.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note section where you can provide any background information about your thoughts and opinions about the company's employer brand.
* **What are your current thoughts about the company's employer brand?**: This question is meant to elicit your general opinions and feelings about the company's employer brand. Please answer as honestly as possible.
* **How would you rate the company's employer brand right now?**: This question is meant to help us understand how you perceive the company's employer brand currently. Please select the option that best aligns with your views.
* **What are three key strengths of the company's employer brand?**: This question is asking you to identify the aspects of the company's employer brand that you feel are particularly strong or positive. Please select up to three strengths from the options provided.
* **What are three key areas for improvement?**: This question is meant to help us understand where the company's employer brand may be lacking or could be improved. Please select up to three areas for improvement from the options provided.
* **Contact Information**: This section is for you to provide your contact information, such as your email address, so we can follow up with you about this survey.
* **What is your role in the company?**: This question is to help us understand your position or role within the company.
* **What is your department?**: This question is meant to help us understand your organizational context within the company.
* **Which of the following best describes your work relationship with this company?**: This question is to help us understand your relationship with the company. Please select the option that best describes your situation.
* **How do you prefer to be contacted about this survey?**: This question is to help us reach out to you for any follow-up questions or discussions. Please select the option that best suits your preferences.
* **Do you have any other comments about this survey or the company's employer brand?**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments you may have about the survey or the company's employer brand.
