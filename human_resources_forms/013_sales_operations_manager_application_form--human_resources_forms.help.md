# Sales Operations Manager Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for sales operations managers to submit applications for job openings within the company. The form gathers detailed information about the candidate, including their personal and professional experience, skills, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), for your personal information, including your name, email address, phone number, and job title.
2. Select the relevant experience and job preferences from the provided options for "Work Experience", "Available Days", "Preferred Time", and "Job Type".
3. Enter a detailed description of your job requirements, skills, and responsibilities in the "Job Description", "Job Requirements", "Job Skills", and "Job Responsibilities" fields.
4. Attach your resume and cover letter to demonstrate your qualifications.
5. Finally, provide information about your job references and the job's status, location, and work hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, *): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, *): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, *): Enter your work phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, *): Enter your current job title.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, *): Select the number of years of work experience you have.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, `select_one`, *): Choose the days you are available to work.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, *): Enter your preferred work hours.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, *): Enter a detailed description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `text`, *): Enter a detailed description of the job requirements.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, `text`, *): Enter your job skills and qualifications.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, `text`, *): Enter your job responsibilities and tasks.
* **Job Educations** (`job_educations`, `text`, *): Enter your educational background.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, *): Attach your resume.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, `text`, *): Attach a cover letter.
* **Job References** (`job_references`, `text`, *): Enter information about your job references.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **Expected End Date** (`expected_end_date`, `date`, *): Enter the expected end date of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, *): Choose the status of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, *): Enter the location of the job you are applying for.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, *): Enter the number of work hours you are willing to work.
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, *): Enter your expected salary.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, *): Choose the type of job you are applying for (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, *): Choose the category of the job you are applying for (Sales, Marketing, Finance).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, *): Enter a detailed description of the job you are applying for.
