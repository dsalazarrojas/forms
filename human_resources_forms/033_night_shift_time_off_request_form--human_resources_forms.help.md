# Night Shift Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to facilitate employee requests for time off during night shifts. It collects necessary information from the employee, manager, and shift details to process time off requests accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee information:
	* Ensure that you, the employee, enter your employee ID or name.
	* Provide your email address.
2. Enter your shift time off details:
	* Choose the start and end dates for your requested time off.
	* Select the start and end times for your shift.
	* Specify the number of hours you are requesting for your time off.
3. Provide your reason for requesting time off.
4. Select your shift type (Night Shift or Day Shift).
5. Enter your manager's information:
	* Enter your manager's name.
	* Enter your manager's email address.
	* Enter your manager's phone number.
6. Get approval from your manager:
	* Your manager will review your request and select either 'Approved' or 'Denied'.
7. Confirm your manager's approval:
	* Your manager will select either 'True' or 'False' to confirm approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: This field is for your name.
* **Employee Email**: This field is for your email address.
* **Start Date**: This is the date you are requesting to start your time off.
* **End Date**: This is the date you are requesting to end your time off.
* **Start Time**: This is the time you are requesting to start your shift.
* **End Time**: This is the time you are requesting to end your shift.
* **Hours Request**: This field is for the number of hours you are requesting for your time off.
* **Reason**: This field is for you to enter your reason for requesting time off.
* **Manager Name**: This field is for your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: This field is for your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: This field is for your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Approval Date**: This field is for the date your manager's approval or denial is made.
* **Manager Approval Time**: This field is for the time your manager's approval or denial is made.
* **Shift Type**: This field is for you to select the type of shift (Night Shift or Day Shift) you are requesting time off for.
* **Shift Hours**: This field is for the number of hours you are requesting for your shift.
* **Shift Minutes**: This field is not applicable, but it is included for completeness and future reference.
* **Shift Notes**: This is a field for any additional notes you or your manager may have about your shift.
* **Manager Comment**: This field is for your manager to add any comments about your request.
* **Employee Agrees**: This field is for you to confirm that you agree with your manager's approval or denial.
* **Manager Agrees**: This field is for your manager to confirm that they agree with their own approval or denial.
* **Submitted By**: This field is for you to enter your employee ID or name.

Note: This guide assumes a basic understanding of the form's purpose and functionality. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR representative. 

Note: The form includes fields for both day and night shifts; however, the shift type field will determine which fields are relevant.
