# Nursing Family Event Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nursing Family Event Leave Of Absance Form" is used for employees to request a leave of absence due to a family event or emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Approved, Unpaid, or Paid) on the "Leave type" field.
2. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide the reason for your leave in the "Reason" field.
4. Enter the date you will be leaving from and the date you will be returning to in the "Date from" and "Date to" fields, respectively.
5. If applicable, select your leave type (Approved, Unpaid, or Paid) for the "Leave type" field.
6. Check your manager's and team's approval in the "Manager approval" and "Team approval" fields, respectively.
7. Add any comments in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the "Submitter email" and "Submitter phone" fields, respectively.
9. Select your department and role in the "Submitter department" and "Submitter role" fields, respectively.
10. Select your position in the "Submitter position" field.
11. Choose your location in the "Submitter location" field.
12. Enter the date you are submitting this request in the "Submitter date" field.
13. Select the time you are submitting this request in the "Submitter time" field.
14. Add any additional comments for your submission in the "Submitter comments" field.
15. Review and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Submitter agree" field.
16. Select the terms and conditions you agree to in the "Submitter terms" field.
17. Finally, submit the form by clicking "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form**: This is the main form page.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Reason**: Provide the reason for your leave.
* **Date from**: Enter the date you will be leaving from.
* **Date to**: Enter the date you will be returning to.
* **Leave type**: Select the type of leave you are requesting (Approved, Unpaid, or Paid).
* **Manager approval**: Check your manager's approval.
* **Team approval**: Check your team's approval.
* **Comments**: Add any comments.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name.
* **Submitter email**: Enter your email.
* **Submitter phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter department**: Select your department.
* **Submitter role**: Select your role.
* **Submitter position**: Select your position.
* **Submitter location**: Select your location.
* **Submitter date**: Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Submitter time**: Enter the time you are submitting this request.
* **Submitter comments**: Add any additional comments.
* **Submitter agree**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submitter terms**: Select the terms and conditions you agree to.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review your submission carefully before clicking "Submit".
* Ensure that you have the necessary approvals from your manager and team.
* Review the terms and conditions before agreeing to them.
