# material_cutting_control_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material cutting control form is used to collect information related to material cutting operations. It is designed to ensure the quality of materials being cut, and the correct completion of this form is essential for maintaining control and consistency in production processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you have the necessary information.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully.
3. If unsure about any field, ask for assistance or clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Machine Number** (`page2`, `number`, required): Enter the unique number assigned to the machine used for cutting operations. This helps track and identify specific machines and their corresponding production runs.
- **Operator** (`page3`, `text`, required): Enter the name and/or ID of the operator performing the cutting operation.
- **Cutting Material** (`page4`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of material being cut (e.g., Cutting Material 1, Cutting Material 2, or Cutting Material 3).
- **Quality Check** (`page5`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all relevant options that apply to the quality of the cut material (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
- **Cutting Method** (`page6`, `select_one`, required): Select the method used for cutting operations (e.g., Method 1, Method 2, or Method 3).
- **Cutting Time** (`page7`, `time`, optional): Enter the time spent on the cutting operation if applicable.
- **Cut Sign Off** (`page8`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all relevant options that apply to the cut material (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
- **Notes** (`page9`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional relevant information or comments about the cutting operation.
