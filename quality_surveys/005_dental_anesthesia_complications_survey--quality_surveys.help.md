# Dental Anesthesia Complications Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Anesthesia Complications Survey is designed to collect data on complications that may occur during dental anesthesia procedures. This data will help improve the quality of care and inform future treatment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.**
2. **Ensure you are filling out the form for the intended purpose only.**
3. **Do not use the form for duplicate reporting.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record or as they are known to you.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age as recorded in their medical record.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, optional): Choose the patient's gender from the options provided.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant allergies, medical conditions, or previous complications.
* **Procedure Information** (`procedure_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the procedure that was performed, including any relevant medical notes or comments.
* **Complications Occurred** (`complications_occurred`, select_one, optional): Indicate if complications occurred during the procedure, choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
* **Complications Description** (`complications_description`, text, optional): Describe the complications that occurred, including any signs or symptoms exhibited by the patient.
* **Anesthesia Type** (`anesthesia_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate if anesthesia was used during the procedure, choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
* **Complication Type** (`complication_type`, select_multiple, optional): Identify any complications that occurred during the procedure, choosing from the options provided.
* **Reaction Description** (`reaction_description`, text, optional): Describe the patient's reaction or response to the anesthesia or procedure.
* **Treatment Outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, select_one, optional): Indicate the outcome of the treatment, choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
* **Follow-up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, optional): Indicate if follow-up is required, choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the procedure or patient.
