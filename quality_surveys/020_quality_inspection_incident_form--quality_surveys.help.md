# Quality Inspection Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents occurring during quality inspections. It will help identify root causes and guide corrective actions to improve quality processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Quality Inspection Incident Form, follow these steps:

* Read each field carefully and fill in the required information.
* For each field, provide as much detail as possible.
* If a field is not applicable or does not apply, mark it as such.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (text, required/optional): Describe the incident that occurred during the quality inspection. Include as much detail as possible.
* **Incident Date/Time** (date, required/optional): Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (text, required/optional): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (select multiple, required/optional): Choose all applicable categories for the incident, such as Quality Control, Production, Other.
* **Incident Description** (text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Root Cause** (text, required/optional): Identify the root cause of the incident.
* **Corrective Actions** (text, required/optional): Describe the actions taken or planned to address the incident.
* **Incident Resolved** (select one, required/optional): Mark whether the incident was resolved (True), not resolved (False), or not yet (Not Yet).
