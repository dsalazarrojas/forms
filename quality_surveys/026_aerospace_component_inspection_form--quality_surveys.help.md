# Aerospace Component Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Aerospace Component Inspection Form is a crucial tool for maintenance personnel to document the inspection results of aerospace components. This form ensures that the component's condition is accurately reported, enabling informed decisions about its acceptance, rejection, or repair.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Inspector ID` field with your employee ID, as this will associate you with the inspection results.
2. Enter the `Date of Inspection` and `Time of Inspection` using the correct date and time format.
3. For `Component Information`, enter the `Part Number`, `Serial Number`, and `Component Name / Description` accurately.
4. Check the `Visual Condition` field and select the appropriate option based on your observation.
5. Answer the `Dimensional Check` question by selecting `Pass` if the measurements are within tolerance or `Fail` if they are not.
6. If applicable, provide the `NDT Results` outcome.
7. Enter any `Defect Description` if issues were found during the inspection.
8. Select the final disposition of the component by choosing one of the options: `Accepted (Serviceable)`, `Rejected (Scrap)`, `Hold for Review (MRB)`, or `Repair Required`.
9. Finally, sign off by selecting `Signed` to confirm that the inspection is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`): Enter your employee ID to associate you with the inspection results.
* **Date of Inspection** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format.
* **Time of Inspection** (`inspection_time`): Enter the time of the inspection in the correct format.
* **Component Information** (`component_info_header`): This section contains essential details about the component.
  * **Part Number** (`part_number`): Enter the alphanumeric code for the component.
  * **Serial Number** (`serial_number`): Enter the unique identifier for the component.
  * **Component Name / Description** (`component_name`): Enter a brief description of the component.
* **Visual Condition** (`visual_condition`): Check if any visible defects are present.
* **Dimensional Check** (`dimensional_check`): Select if the measurements are within tolerance or not.
* **NDT Results** (`ndt_results`): Enter the outcome of any non-destructive testing.
* **Defect Description** (`defect_description`): Provide details about any defects found.
* **Final Disposition** (`final_disposition`): Select the final outcome for the component.
* **Digital Signature** (`signature`): Confirm that the inspection is accurate by selecting `Signed`.
