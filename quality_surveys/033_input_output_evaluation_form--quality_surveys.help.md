# ประกพมลนปนพประกประกนปนพยปม - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect ratings and feedback on quality surveys. It's crucial to fill it out accurately to provide valuable insights and improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out each page and field according to your observations and ratings.
2. For fields with rating options (e.g., very good, satisfactory, unsatisfactory), select the most appropriate rating based on your experience.
3. For fields with numerical ratings (e.g., input consistency, process consistency), provide a numerical rating from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Input Consistency
* **input consistency** (`input consistency`, number, optional): Rate the consistency of the input values from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### Page 2: Output Quality
* **output quality** (`output quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the output from:
	+ very good
	+ satisfactory
	+ unsatisfactory
	+ very unsatisfactory

### Page 3: Output Effectiveness
* **output effectiveness** (`output effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the output from:
	+ very good
	+ good
	+ unsatisfactory
	+ very unsatisfactory

### Page 4: Process Consistency
* **process consistency** (`process consistency`, number, optional): Rate the consistency of the process from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### Page 5: Process Effectiveness
* **process effectiveness** (`process effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the process from:
	+ very good
	+ good
	+ unsatisfactory
	+ very unsatisfactory

### Page 6: Time to Complete
* **time to complete** (`time to complete`, time, optional): Rate the time it takes to complete the task from:
	+ hours
	+ minutes
	+ seconds

### Page 7: Time to Complete Variance
* **time to complete variance** (`time to complete variance`, number, optional): Rate the variance of the time to complete the task from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### Page 8: Time to Complete Variation
* **time to complete variation** (`time to complete variation`, number, optional): Rate the variation of the time to complete the task from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### Page 9: Output Variance
* **output variance** (`output variance`, number, optional): Rate the variance of the output from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

## Tips
* Please fill out each page and field according to your actual experience and observations.
* If you're unsure about a particular section, try to make an educated guess or consult with others who have completed the task.
