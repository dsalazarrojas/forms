# Public Transit Service Quality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Transit Service Quality Audit Form is designed to collect feedback from passengers on the frequency and quality of public transit services. This form is meant to be filled out by passengers who have used the public transit service in question.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Route Id of the service you have used.
2. Optionally, enter a brief description of the route you took.
3. Enter the date of the audit.
4. Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
5. Enter the name of the transit agency providing the service.
6. Select the frequency of service you have used (e.g., 6 times per week, 3-5 times per week, less than 3 times per week).
7. Rate the quality of service for 8 different criteria.
8. Add any additional notes about your experience.
9. Select your level of satisfaction with the service (very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Route Id** (Route Id, text, required): Enter the number or code of the route you took. This helps us identify which service you are providing feedback on.
* **Route Description** (route_description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the route you took.
* **Audit Date** (audit_date, date, required): Enter the date of the audit. This date will be recorded as the date the audit was completed.
* **Auditor Name** (auditor_name, text, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Auditor Email** (auditor_email, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Agency Name** (agency_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the agency providing the service.
* **Route Frequency** (route_frequency, select_one, required): Select the frequency of service you have used (e.g., 6 times per week, 3-5 times per week, less than 3 times per week).
* **Evaluation 1-8** (evaluation_1-8, time, optional): Rate the quality of service for 8 different criteria (e.g., reliability, cleanliness, communication, etc.).
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your experience.
* **Feedback** (feedback, select_multiple, required): Select your level of satisfaction with the service (very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).

**Tips**: Please be as specific and honest as possible when filling out this form. Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve the public transit service. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Notes" field.
