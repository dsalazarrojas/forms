## Forest Fire Prevention Awareness Survey - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is designed to assess an individual's or organization's awareness and understanding of forest fire prevention and safety measures. It collects information on various aspects of forest fire prevention, including risk assessment, prevention behaviors, risk factors, and follow-up actions. This data will help us better understand the current state of forest fire prevention and identify areas for improvement.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Select the appropriate option for each question based on your understanding.
2. Fill in the text fields with your answer or description.
3. If a field is required, you must complete it.
4. You can skip the "Contact Info" field if you don't want to provide your contact information.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Risk** (`fire_risk`, select_one, required): Choose the level of fire risk in your area:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
* **Fire Prevention** (`fire_prevention`, select_multiple, required): Select all the prevention methods you engage in:
	+ a
	+ b
	+ c
* **Fire Safety** (`fire_safety`, select_one, required): Choose your answer for fire safety:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Prevention Behaviors** (`prevention_behaviors`, text, required): Describe some of the prevention behaviors you engage in.
* **Risk Behaviors** (`risk_behaviors`, select_multiple, required): Select all the risk behaviors you engage in:
	+ a
	+ b
	+ c
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, date, required): Enter the date of your last risk assessment.
* **Prevention Behaviors Detail** (`prevention_behaviors_detail`, text, required): Describe your prevention behaviors in more detail.
* **Prevention Plan** (`prevention_plan`, select_one, required): Choose your prevention plan:
	+ a
	+ b
	+ c
* **Prevention Plan Detail** (`prevention_plan_detail`, text, required): Describe your prevention plan.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, note, required): Enter any follow-up actions you have taken or plan to take.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter your contact information (email address).
* **Follow Up Detail** (`follow_up_detail`, text, required): Describe your follow-up actions in more detail.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to follow up.
* **Prevention Plan Implementation** (`prevention_plan_implementation`, select_multiple, required): Select all the methods for implementing your prevention plan:
	+ a
	+ b
	+ c
* **Prevention Plan Implementation Detail** (`prevention_plan_implementation_detail`, text, required): Describe how you plan to implement your prevention plan.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your prevention plan or practices.
