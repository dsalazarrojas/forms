# Quality Improvement Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Quality Improvement Checklist is a form designed to help you identify areas for improvement and gather feedback on the quality of our services. This form is used to collect information on various aspects of our work, allowing us to make data-driven decisions to enhance our quality of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, and provide your feedback on the quality of our services.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with your honest feedback.
3. For fields that are not required, you can provide additional comments or insights if you feel they are relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_1_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_1`, `text`, required): This is the first page of the checklist. Please write your thoughts and comments about the quality of our services on this page.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_2_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_2`, `number`, required): On this page, rate the quality of our services on a scale of 1-10.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_3_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_3`, `number`, required): Similarly, rate the quality of our services on this page.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_4_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_4`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the two options: Good or Poor.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_5_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_5`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or multiple of the options: Good or Poor.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_6_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_6`, `date`, required): Select a date related to your feedback.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_7_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_7`, `time`, required): Enter a time related to your feedback.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_8_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_8`, `note`, required): Provide additional comments or insights on this page.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_9_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_9`, `email`, required): Enter an email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_10_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_10`, `text`, required): Fill in this field with your further feedback.
* **quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_11_label** (`quality_improvement_checklist_form_page_11`, `text`, required): Fill in this field with any additional comments or insights.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We value your input and appreciate your help in improving our services.
