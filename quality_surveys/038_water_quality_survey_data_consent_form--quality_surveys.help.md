# Water Quality Survey Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Quality Survey Data Consent Form is designed to collect aggregate data from users on their experiences and concerns with water quality. The data collected will help authorities make informed decisions on water management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about yourself.
2. Enter your contact details if you'd like to be contacted.
3. Indicate your source of water (e.g., well, municipal, rainwater).
4. Check if you give permission for your data to be used for research purposes.
5. Enter a contact email address if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Provide any additional information you'd like to share about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (general_info)**: Enter your name, address, etc.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Water Source (water_source)**: Select the source of your water from the provided options. This will help us understand your specific situation.
* **Permission for Water Quality Research (permission)**: Check the box to give permission for your data to be used for research purposes.
* **Consent (consent)**: Enter a brief statement stating that you agree to share your information.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
* **Additional Information (additional_info)**: Provide any additional context or comments about your experience.
