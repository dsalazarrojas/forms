# Animal Product Quality Assurance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Product Quality Assurance Survey is a form used to collect feedback on animal products, focusing on their packaging, appearance, consistency, and overall quality. This data will aid in identifying potential issues and ensure that the products meet safety and quality standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, including the respondent's name, company name, role, email address, product category, product name, batch or lot number (if applicable), and the date of evaluation.
2.  Select the rating for the packaging quality, appearance, and other product attributes as per your observations.
3.  Indicate whether any defects were found in the product and describe them in the provided space.
4.  Suggest corrective actions for the found defects, considering the potential impact on customers and whether retesting is necessary.
5.  Provide additional comments if you have any further observations.
6.  Finally, provide your name for review documentation purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, which will be used for internal tracking and reference purposes.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Type the name of the organization you are representing.
- **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, text, required): Identify your job title or position within the organization.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email for follow-up and discussion purposes.
- **Product Category Evaluated** (`product_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the animal product you are evaluating.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the specific name of the product being evaluated.
- **Batch or Lot Number** (`batch_number`, text, optional): Provide the batch or lot number if applicable for tracking and reference.
- **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of evaluation for accurate tracking.
- **Packaging Quality Rating** (`packaging_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the packaging based on your observations.
- **Product Appearance** (`product_appearance`, select_one, required): Assess the product's appearance.
- **Product Consistency** (`product_consistency`, select_one, required): Evaluate the uniformity of the product across samples.
- **Label Accuracy** (`label_accuracy`, select_one, required): Check if the label on the product matches the product's characteristics.
- **Safety Compliance** (`safety_compliance`, select_one, required): Determine if the product meets safety standards.
- **Overall Quality Score** (`quality_score`, select_one, required): Assign an overall quality score out of five.
- **Were Any Defects Found** (`defects_found`, select_one, required): Indicate if any defects were discovered.
- **Defect Description** (`defect_description`, text, optional): Describe the defects found, if any.
- **Corrective Action Recommended** (`corrective_action`, select_multiple, required): Suggest actions to correct the defects, considering the potential customer impact and retesting needs.
- **Potential Customer Impact** (`customer_impact`, select_one, required): Assess the potential impact on customers based on the defects found.
- **Is Retesting Required** (`retest_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if retesting is necessary.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any further observations or comments.
- **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_signature`, text, required): Enter your name for internal tracking and documentation.
