# Product Durability Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about the durability of our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the questions carefully and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Product Durability Feedback** (Text Field): A text field for general feedback about the product's durability.
*   **Product Rating** (Number Field): A numerical value to rate the product's durability from 1-5.
*   **Satisfaction Score** (Number Field): A numerical value to rate your overall satisfaction with the product.
*   **Improvement Areas** (Multiple-Select Field): A multiple-select field to identify areas where the product can be improved.
*   **Product Condition** (Select Field): A select field to rate the product's condition.
*   **Durability Issues** (Text Field): A text field to describe any issues you've encountered with the product's durability.
*   **Comments** (Text Field): A text field for any additional comments or suggestions.
*   **Email** (Email Field): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
*   **Phone Number** (Text Field): A text field for your phone number (optional).
*   **Product Type** (Text Field): A text field for the product's type.
*   **Product Model** (Text Field): A text field for the product's model.
*   **Purchase Date** (Date Field): A date field for the date you purchased the product.
*   **Purchase Price** (Number Field): A numerical value to enter the product's purchase price.
