# Fruit Juice Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fruit Juice Quality Assessment form is designed to collect data on the quality attributes of fruit juice products. This form helps producers and quality control teams to evaluate the sensory and physical characteristics of the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of assessment from the "Quality Assessments" drop-down menu.
2. Select the sensory attributes that apply to the product being assessed in the "Sensory Tests" section.
3. Provide notes on "Color", "Flavor", "Aroma", and "Taste" for detailed quality assessment.
4. Evaluate the sweetness, acidity, flavor intensity, texture, and storage conditions of the product.
5. Select the applicable fruit types, production process, packaging materials, and packaging location.
6. Enter the expiration date, production date, batch number, product name, packaging weight, storage location, and other relevant details.
7. Provide a quality rating and any additional comments.
