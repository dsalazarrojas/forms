# Audit Documentation Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audit Documentation Quality Survey is a tool used to assess the quality of audit documentation in various departments and roles. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and provide recommendations for better documentation practices. The survey is designed to be completed by individuals involved in the audit process to provide their honest feedback on the documentation's completeness, accuracy, clarity, organization, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name and email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions).
2. Select your department and role in the audit process.
3. Choose the date of the survey.
4. Rate the quality of documentation in various aspects, such as completeness, accuracy, clarity, organization, and overall quality.
5. Identify which tools are used for documentation.
6. Suggest areas that need improvement and provide recommendations.
7. Rate the overall quality of documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is your name, which will be used for identification purposes. You can leave it blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required/optional): This is your email address, which will be used for follow-up questions. You can leave it blank if you prefer not to be contacted.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the provided options (Internal Audit, External Audit, Quality Assurance, Compliance, Risk Management, or Other).
* **Role in Audit** (`role_in_audit`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role in the audit process from the provided options (Audit Partner, Audit Manager, Senior Auditor, Staff Auditor, Reviewer, Client, or Other).
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the survey.
* **Audit Period** (`audit_period`, `text`, required): Enter the period of the audit that is being reviewed.
* **Documentation Completeness** (`documentation_completeness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the completeness of audit documentation (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Documentation Accuracy** (`documentation_accuracy`, `select_one`, required): Rate the accuracy of audit documentation (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Documentation Clarity** (`documentation_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of audit documentation (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Documentation Organization** (`documentation_organization`, `select_one`, required): Rate the organization of audit documentation (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Workpaper Quality** (`workpaper_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of workpapers (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Evidence Sufficiency** (`evidence_sufficiency`, `select_one`, required): Rate the sufficiency of audit evidence (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Evidence Appropriateness** (`evidence_appropriateness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the appropriateness of audit evidence (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Compliance with Standards** (`compliance_with_standards`, `select_one`, required): Rate compliance with auditing standards (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Review Notes Resolution** (`review_notes_resolution`, `select_one`, required): Rate how well review notes are resolved (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Documentation Timeliness** (`documentation_timeliness`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether documentation is completed on time (Always, Usually, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never).
* **Template Effectiveness** (`template_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of documentation templates (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Software Tools Used** (`software_tools_used`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose which software tools are used for documentation (Excel, Word, Audit-specific software, Cloud-based tools, Paper-based, or Other).
* **Areas Needing Improvement** (`areas_needing_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify areas that need improvement (Completeness, Accuracy, Clarity, Organization, Timeliness, Standardization, Technology, or Other).
* **Improvement Recommendations** (`improvement_recommendations`, `text`, optional): Provide any recommendations for improvement.
* **Overall Quality Rating** (`overall_quality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall quality of documentation (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Take a moment to review each field before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
