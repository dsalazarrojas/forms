# Quality Control Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers or users about any quality control complaints they may have regarding products or services. The goal is to gather details about the issue, including the type of complaint, product information, and any relevant dates or images.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): A short title that describes the complaint. This is not required but can provide context for the complaint.
2. **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the complaint. This is not required but can provide additional context.
3. **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, select one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
4. **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): The name of the product or service related to the complaint.
5. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Your contact information, including name, email, or phone number.
6. **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, optional): A link or URL to an image related to the complaint.
7. **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, date, optional): The date the complaint occurred.
8. **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, optional): A detailed description of the issue you're experiencing.
9. **Signature** (`signature`, date, optional): The date you're signing this complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: Enter a brief title that describes the complaint.
* **Complaint Type**: Choose the type of complaint you're experiencing. This helps us understand the nature of the issue.
* **Product Name**: If the complaint is related to a product, please enter the name of the product.
* **Contact Info**: Please provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for further clarification.
* **Product Image**: If you have an image that illustrates the issue, you can provide a link to it.
* **Complaint Date**: Enter the date the complaint occurred.
* **Complaint Details**: Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any steps you took or actions you performed.
* **Signature**: Please sign the complaint with the current date.

## Tips
* Make sure to submit unique complaints to help us track and address issues effectively.
* Be as descriptive as possible in the **Complaint Details** field to provide us with sufficient information.
* If you have any additional information or clarification, you can attach it to the complaint.
