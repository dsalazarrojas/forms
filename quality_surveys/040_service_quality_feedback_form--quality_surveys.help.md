# Service Quality Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience with our service. Your input will help us improve and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form only once per interaction with the service.
2. Select the "quality-surveys" option from the category dropdown menu that best describes your experience.
3. Enter your thoughts and comments in the "service-quality-feedback-form" field.
4. Enter your email address (if you'd like to receive a follow-up or if we need to contact you).
5. Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted directly).
6. Choose the date you interacted with the service.
7. If needed, select the time you interacted with the service.
8. If applicable, describe a major pain point or issue you experienced with the service in the "pain-point-1" and "pain-point-2" fields.
9. Finally, hit submit to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Quality Feedback Form (Required/Optional)**: Enter a brief description of your experience with the service, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Category (Select One)**: Choose the quality level of your experience. If you've had a positive interaction, you can select "Good". If you feel the service didn't meet expectations, you can choose "Poor".
* **Email (Optional)**: Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a follow-up or if we need to contact you for any additional information.
* **Phone (Optional)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly.
* **Date (Optional)**: Choose the date you interacted with the service.
* **Time (Optional)**: Select the time you interacted with the service.
* **Pain Point 1 (Optional)**: If you experienced any issues or problems with the service, describe them here.
* **Pain Point 2 (Optional)**: If there were additional issues or problems, describe them here.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Use the provided options for the category and date fields.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, contact us at [your contact info].
