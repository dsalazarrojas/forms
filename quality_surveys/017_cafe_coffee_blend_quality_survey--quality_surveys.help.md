# Cafe Coffee Blend Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cafe Coffee Blend Quality Survey is designed to help us understand your experience with our coffee blend. Your feedback will help us improve the quality and taste of our coffee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly to give us a complete picture of your experience.
2. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please feel free to share them with us.
3. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional Comments" field to share your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date you visited our cafe.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, select_one, required): Choose the time of day you visited our cafe:
	+ Morning before 10am
	+ Mid-morning 10am-12pm
	+ Afternoon 12pm-4pm
	+ Evening after 4pm
* **Coffee Blend Sampled** (`blend_sampled`, select_one, required): Select the coffee blend you tried:
	+ House Blend
	+ Morning Roast
	+ Espresso Blend
	+ Single Origin Ethiopia
	+ Single Origin Colombia
	+ Dark Roast
	+ Decaf Blend
	+ Seasonal Special
* **Flavor Rating** (`flavor_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the flavor of the coffee:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Aroma Rating** (`aroma_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the aroma of the coffee:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Strength Rating** (`strength_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the strength of the coffee:
	+ Too weak
	+ Slightly weak
	+ Just right
	+ Slightly strong
	+ Too strong
* **Acidity Rating** (`acidity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the acidity level of the coffee:
	+ Too low
	+ Low
	+ Just right
	+ High
	+ Too high
* **Smoothness Rating** (`smoothness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the smoothness of the coffee:
	+ Very smooth
	+ Smooth
	+ Average
	+ Rough
	+ Very rough
* **Temperature Rating** (`temperature_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the temperature of the coffee:
	+ Too cold
	+ Slightly cool
	+ Just right
	+ Slightly hot
	+ Too hot
* **Value for Price** (`value_for_price`, select_one, required): Rate the value of the coffee for its price:
	+ Excellent value
	+ Good value
	+ Fair value
	+ Poor value
	+ Overpriced
* **Would You Purchase Again** (`would_purchase_again`, select_one, required): Would you purchase this blend again:
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Not sure
	+ Probably not
	+ Definitely not
* **Flavor Notes Detected** (`flavor_notes_detected`, select_multiple, optional): Select any flavors you detected in the coffee:
	+ Chocolate
	+ Caramel
	+ Nutty
	+ Fruity
	+ Floral
	+ Spicy
	+ Earthy
	+ Smoky
	+ None
* **Preferred Brewing Method** (`preferred_brewing_method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred brewing method:
	+ Drip
	+ French Press
	+ Pour Over
	+ Espresso
	+ Cold Brew
	+ AeroPress
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the coffee:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for improvement for this blend.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about the coffee.
