# Residential Lighting Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from homeowners about their experiences with residential lighting. Your responses will help us understand what types of lighting are being used, how bright they are, and how they affect energy usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of residence you are filling this form for.
2. Choose the types of lighting you have in your home.
3. Rate the brightness of your lights.
4. Report your average energy usage (measured in kWh or watts).
5. Estimate how much time you spend using these lights.
6. Choose how much energy savings you have noticed.
7. Select the energy efficiency rating of your lights.
8. Share any additional comments or feedback.
9. Provide your email address for any follow-up contact (optional).
10. Enter your name (optional).
11. Enter your zip code (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. **Home Type**
* **Home Type (Home Type)**: Select the type of residence you are filling this form for (House, Condo, Townhouse).
### 3. **Lighting Type**
* **Lighting Type (Lighting Type)**: Choose the types of lighting you have in your home (LED, Incandescent, Halogen, Fluorescent).
### 4. **Brightness Level**
* **Brightness Level (Brightness Level)**: Rate the brightness of your lights on a scale.
### 5. **Energy Usage**
* **Energy Usage (Energy Usage)**: Report your average energy usage for these lights (measured in kWh or watts).
### 6. **Time Spent Using Lights**
* **Time Spent Using Lights (Time Wanted)**: Estimate how much time you spend using these lights.
### 7. **Energy Savings**
* **Energy Savings (Energy Savings)**: Choose how much energy savings you have noticed (High, Medium, Low).
### 8. **Energy Efficiency**
* **Energy Efficiency (Energy Efficiency)**: Select the energy efficiency rating of your lights (Energy Star, Non-Energy Star).
### 9. **Comments**
* **Comments (Comments)**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your residential lighting.
### 10. **Email**
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for any follow-up contact (optional).
### 11. **Name**
* **Name (Name)**: Enter your name for verification purposes (optional).
### 12. **Zip Code**
* **Zip Code (Zip)**: Enter your zip code for location verification (optional).
