# SNF Measurement Data Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SNF Measurement Data Survey is designed to collect information about your experiences and interactions with the product or service. This information will help the organization understand user behavior and preferences, enabling improvements to be made to enhance your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have provided all required fields.
3. Take your time and answer each question carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`what_is_your_name`, text, required): This field asks for your name, which will help us understand who is providing the feedback.
* **How old are you** (`how_old_are_you`, number, required): Please enter your age to help us understand your demographic information.
* **What is your email** (`what_is_your_email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `abc@example.com`).
* **What is your phone** (`what_is_your_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format (123) 456-1234.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you visited the product or service.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time you visited the product or service.
* **SNF** (`snf`, number, required): Please enter the SNF value related to your interaction with the product or service.
* **SNF** (`snf`, select_one, required): Select one of the available SNF options related to your experience with the product or service.
* **What is your address** (`what_is_your_address`, text, required): Enter your address to help us understand your location.
* **How often do you visit** (`how_often_do_you_visit`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you visit the product or service.
* **What is your age** (`what_is_your_age`, text, optional): This field is not required, but please enter your age if you feel it's relevant.
* **How do you feel about this form** (`how_do_you_feel_about_this_form`, select_one, optional): Please select how you feel about this form.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **More comments** (`more_comments`, note, optional): This field is not required, but please add any additional information if you feel it's necessary.
