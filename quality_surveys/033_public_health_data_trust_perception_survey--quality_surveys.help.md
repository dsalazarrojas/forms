# Public Health Data Trust Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your perception of public health data. It's crucial that you answer honestly, as the data collected will help us better understand and improve data handling practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. For select multiple or select one fields, choose one or more of the available options.
3. If a field is marked as required, you must answer it.
4. Once you've filled out all the sections, select "submit" to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section introduces the purpose and importance of the survey.
* **awareness_question**: This question assesses your level of awareness about data handling practices. Please answer with the option that best reflects your understanding.
* **trust_scale**: This question measures your perception of data trust. Answer on a scale (we assume it's a number input).
* **concerns_scale**: This question gauges your concerns about data handling. Please answer with the option that best reflects your current concerns.
* **data_practices**: This section asks for your open-ended thoughts on data handling practices.
* **survey_preferences**: This question evaluates your interest in future surveys. Please answer with the options that apply to you.
* **contact_info**: This section is for providing contact information, specifically an email address for follow-up questions or concerns. You can answer with any email address you have. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you'd like.
* **submit**: This is the final step, where you'll let us know if you're ready to submit your answers. Please select "Yes" if you've completed the form honestly and are ready to submit.

**Tips**

* Answer all required fields.
* You may leave the contact info field blank if you're uncomfortable providing it.
* If you have any questions, contact us using the email address provided on the introduction page.
