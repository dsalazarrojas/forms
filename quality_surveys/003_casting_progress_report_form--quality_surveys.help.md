# Casting Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casting Progress Report Form is a tool used to track and evaluate the casting process. It helps casting directors and production managers to document their progress, identify challenges, and make informed decisions. The form is used to provide regular updates on the casting status, major milestones achieved, and quality assessments of the casting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the report date in the "Report Date" field. This date should reflect the period of the report.
2. Provide the production name in the "Production Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the reporting officer in the "Reporting Officer" field.
4. Select the casting status from the options provided in the "Casting Status" field.
5. Enter the number of roles to be cast in the "Total Roles to Cast" field.
6. Enter the number of roles filled in the "Roles Filled" field.
7. Enter the overall progress percentage in the "Overall Progress Percentage" field.
8. Provide a quality assessment of the casting process in the "Quality Assessment" field.
9. Enter any open issues or concerns in the "Open Issues or Concerns" field.
10. Outline the next steps in the "Next Steps" field.
11. If necessary, indicate whether any timeline adjustments are needed in the "Timeline Adjustments Needed?" field.
12. Enter the name of the supervisor or producer who approved the report in the "Approved By" field (if applicable).
13. Enter the approval date in the "Approval Date" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the report, reflecting the period being covered.
* **Production Name** (`production_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the production being reported on.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person reporting on the casting progress.
* **Casting Status** (`casting_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current status of the casting process from the provided options.
* **Total Roles to Cast** (`total_roles_to_cast`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of roles to be cast.
* **Roles Filled** (`roles_filled`, `number`, required): Enter the number of roles filled so far.
* **Overall Progress Percentage** (`overall_progress_percentage`, `number`, required): Enter the overall progress percentage of the casting process.
* **Quality Assessment** (`quality_assessment`, `select_one`, required): Assess the quality of the casting process using the provided options.
* **Open Issues or Concerns** (`open_issues`, `text`, required): Enter any open issues or concerns related to the casting process.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required): Outline the planned activities for the next reporting period.
* **Timeline Adjustments Needed?** (`timeline_adjustments`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether any timeline adjustments are needed.
* **Approved By** (`approval_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the supervisor or producer who approved the report (if applicable).
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of approval (if applicable).
