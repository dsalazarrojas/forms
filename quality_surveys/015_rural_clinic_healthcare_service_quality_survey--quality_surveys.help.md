# rural_clinic_healthcare_service_quality_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from patients about their experience at a rural clinic, to help the clinic understand areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the clinic visit date from your memory to help us understand which visit this feedback is about.
2. Choose the doctor who assisted you during your visit.
3. Choose the nurse who assisted you during your visit.
4. Estimate the waiting time (in minutes) you experienced during your visit.
5. Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
6. Choose whether you plan to return to this clinic for future visits.
7. Choose whether or not you would recommend this clinic to others.
8. Choose the level of cleanliness of the clinic.
9. Choose the level of communication between the doctor and nurse.
10. Choose whether the waiting time was sufficient for your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Clinic Visit** (Clinic Visit, text, optional): Provide the date of your visit to the clinic.
- **Doctor** (Doctor, select_one, optional): Choose the doctor who assisted you during your visit. 
  * Option 1: [Name]
  * Option 2: [Name]
  * Option 3: [Name]
- **Nurse** (Nurse, select_multiple, optional): Choose the nurses who assisted you during your visit. 
  * Option 1: [Name]
  * Option 2: [Name]
  * Option 3: [Name]
- **Waiting Time** (Waiting Time, time, optional): Estimate the waiting time in minutes.
- **Overall Experience** (Overall Experience, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
- **Will Return** (Will Return, select_one, optional): Choose whether you plan to return to this clinic for future visits.
  * Option 1: Yes
  * Option 2: No
  * Option 3: Maybe
- **Recommend** (Recommend, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would recommend this clinic to others.
  * Option 1: Yes
  * Option 2: No
  * Option 3: Maybe
- **Clinic Cleanliness** (Clinic Cleanliness, select_multiple, optional): Choose the level of cleanliness of the clinic.
  * Option 1: Very Clean
  * Option 2: Clean
  * Option 3: Not Very Clean
- **Doctor-Nurse Communication** (Doctor-Nurse Communication, select_multiple, optional): Choose the level of communication between the doctor and nurse.
  * Option 1: Very Good
  * Option 2: Good
  * Option 3: Not Good
- **Waiting Time Sufficient** (Waiting Time Sufficient, select_one, optional): Choose whether the waiting time was sufficient.
  * Option 1: Yes
  * Option 2: No
  * Option 3: Maybe

**Tips**

* Please fill in the form as accurately and truthfully as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them in the comments section (not shown in this guide).
* If you have any questions, please contact the clinic administration.
