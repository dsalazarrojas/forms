# Defect Type Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Defect Type Selection Survey form is designed to collect information about defects, such as whether they exist, their type, date, time, description, location, and any patterns or characteristics. This form helps to identify and categorize defects for further analysis and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully, and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your knowledge and understanding.
3. Enter any relevant details in the text fields, such as defect location or description.
4. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Defect Type Selection Form** (`id: 1`, `select_one`, required/optional)
Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether defects exist on the system or device.

### **Defect Type** (`id: 2`, `select_one`, required/optional)
Choose the type of defect, such as "Yes" or "No", to indicate the nature of the defect.

### **Defect Date** (`id: 3`, `date`, required/optional)
Enter the date on which the defect was detected or occurred.

### **Defect Time** (`id: 4`, `time`, required/optional)
Enter the time at which the defect was detected or occurred.

### **Defect Description** (`id: 5`, `note`, required/optional)
Provide a detailed description of the defect, including any relevant details or symptoms.

### **Team Member** (`id: 6`, `text`, required/optional)
Enter the team member or personnel involved in the defect detection or resolution process.

### **Defect Location** (`id: 7`, `text`, required/optional)
Specify the location where the defect was detected or occurred.

### **Defect Pattern** (`id: 8`, `text`, required/optional)
Enter any patterns or characteristics of the defect that may be relevant for analysis or resolution.
