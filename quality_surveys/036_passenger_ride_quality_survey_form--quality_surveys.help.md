# Passenger Ride Quality Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from passengers about their experience during their trip. Your input will help us understand how our service meets or falls short of your expectations, which will guide our efforts to improve quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your category of travel (e.g., category 1, category 2, category 3)
2. Provide details about your trip (e.g., trip details 1, trip details 2, trip details 3)
3. Rate your satisfaction with your experience (using a number)
4. Rate your overall experience (using a number)
5. Add any comments about your experience (optional)
6. Enter your email address (optional)
7. Enter your phone number (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category (category)**: Please select the category that best fits your trip type.
* **trip_details (trip details)**: Choose the details that best describe your trip.
* **satisfaction (satisfaction)**: Rate your level of satisfaction with your experience, using the number that best represents it.
* **overall_experience (overall experience)**: Rate your overall experience, using the number that best represents it.
* **comments (comments)**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience (optional).
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address, if you'd like to receive follow-up about your feedback (optional).
* **phone_number (phone number)**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback (optional).
