# CAPA Quality Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CAPA (Corrective Action Preventative) Quality Inspection Form is used to document and report any findings or issues encountered during a quality inspection. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to help identify and address potential problems and ensure compliance with quality standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the inspection date and the department or work center being inspected. Ensure that the date is accurate and that you are selecting the correct department or work center from the list.
2. Select the type of inspection that was conducted (e.g., Routine Walkthrough, Scheduled Audit, etc.).
3. Identify the primary category of the finding (e.g., Safety Violation, Process Deviation, etc.).
4. Choose the severity level of the non-conformance (e.g., Minor, Major, Critical).
5. Provide detailed observations and evidence of the finding, including any photos or documentation that support your observations.
6. Answer the question regarding immediate correction performed, including any actions taken to address the issue.
7. Indicate whether a formal CAPA process is required for the finding (based on the severity level).
8. Provide the scheduled follow-up date (if applicable).
9. Add any general inspector notes, including any additional information about the finding.
10. Confirm whether photos of the finding are available.
11. Check if the department supervisor was notified (Yes, during inspection, Yes, post-inspection, or No).
12. Select the final inspection status (e.g., Pass, Conditional Pass, Fail - Action Required).
