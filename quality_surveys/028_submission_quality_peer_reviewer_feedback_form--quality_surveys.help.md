# submission_quality_peer_reviewer_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from peer reviewers on the quality of a submission, allowing them to express their overall impression, provide specific comments, and offer suggestions for improvement. It is intended to help the submitter and the community evaluate and refine their contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide a comprehensive review, please follow these steps:

1. 1. What is your overall impression of the submission? - This field is a free-text area where you can express your initial thoughts on the submission.
2. 2. Please provide your comments on the submission. - This field is for detailed comments and feedback on the submission.
3. 3. Do you agree or disagree with the submission? - Select the response that best reflects your stance.
4. 4. What specific suggestions do you have for the submission? - Provide any ideas or suggestions that could improve the submission.
5. 5. What is the quality score you give this submission? - Choose one of the available options to rate the submission's quality.
6. 6. Do you have any additional comments for the submission? - This field is for any further thoughts or feedback.
7. 7. Do you agree or disagree with the submission? (second instance) - This question is intended to confirm or contradict your previous response.
8. 8. What specific improvements do you suggest for the submission? - Provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
9. 9. What is your rating for the submission quality? - Choose one of the available options to rate the submission's quality.
10. 10. Can you suggest any references or resources for the submission? - This field is for any relevant references or resources.
11. 11. Do you have any other comments or suggestions? - This field is for any other feedback or comments.
12. 12. Any other comments or suggestions? - Another field for any last-minute feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is your overall impression of the submission?** (text) - This field allows you to write your immediate thoughts on the submission. Be as specific or general as you like.
* **2. Please provide your comments on the submission.** (text) - Use this field for detailed feedback, including any praise or constructive criticism.
* **3. Do you agree or disagree with the submission?** (select_one) - Choose the response that reflects your stance, from Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree.
* **4. What specific suggestions do you have for the submission?** (text) - This field is for any ideas or suggestions that could help improve the submission.
* **5. What is the quality score you give this submission?** (select_one) - Choose one of the available options to rate the submission's quality, from Excellent to Poor.
* **6. Do you have any additional comments for the submission?** (text) - This field is for any further feedback or comments.
* **7. Do you agree or disagree with the submission? (second instance)** (select_one) - Confirm or contradict your previous response.
* **8. What specific improvements do you suggest for the submission?** (text) - Provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* **9. What is your rating for the submission quality?** (select_one) - Choose one of the available options to rate the submission's quality.
* **10. Can you suggest any references or resources for the submission?** (text) - This field is for any relevant references or resources.
* **11. Do you have any other comments or suggestions?** (text) - This field is for any other feedback or comments.
* **12. Any other comments or suggestions?** (text) - Another field for any last-minute feedback or suggestions.
