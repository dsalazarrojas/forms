# Adhesive Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to evaluate the performance of an adhesive product. It will ask you to provide feedback on various aspects of the adhesive, such as its ease of application, durability, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your experience with the adhesive.
2. Select the most accurate option for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the text boxes.
4. Ensure that your responses are specific and relevant to the adhesive being evaluated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (`Evaluator Name`, text, required): This is your name, which will help us associate the survey data with your identity.
* **Evaluator Email** (`Evaluator Email`, email, optional): Your email will be used for follow-up contact and to send updates about the product development.
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, date, required): The date of the evaluation is essential for tracking the timeline and consistency of the data.
* **Adhesive Product Name** (`Adhesive Product Name`, text, required): Provide the name of the adhesive being evaluated.
* **Batch/Lot Number** (`Batch/Lot Number`, text, required): Enter the batch or lot number of the adhesive sample.
* **Substrate Type** (`Substrate Type`, select_one, required): Choose the material that the adhesive was applied to (e.g., Wood, Metal, Plastic, etc.).
* **Application Temperature (°F)** (`Application Temperature (°F)`, number, optional): The temperature during application.
* **Bond Strength Rating** (`Bond Strength Rating`, select_one, required): Rate the strength of the bond formed (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Cure Time Rating** (`Cure Time Rating`, select_one, required): Rate the curing time (Too Fast, Appropriate, Too Slow, Did Not Cure).
* **Viscosity Rating** (`Viscosity Rating`, select_one, required): Rate the viscosity (Too Thick, Appropriate, Too Thin, Difficult to Apply).
* **Coverage Rate** (`Coverage Rate`, select_one, optional): Rate how much area the adhesive covers per unit (Above expectations, Met expectations, Below expectations, Unknown).
* **Durability Rating** (`Durability Rating`, select_one, required): Rate the bond's durability over time (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Temperature Resistance** (`Temperature Resistance`, select_one, required): Rate the bond's ability to withstand temperature changes (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Water/Moisture Resistance** (`Water/Moisture Resistance`, select_one, required): Rate the bond's ability to withstand water/moisture (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Chemical Resistance** (`Chemical Resistance`, select_one, required): Rate the bond's ability to withstand chemicals (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Ease of Application** (`Ease of Application`, select_one, required): Rate how easy it was to apply the adhesive (Very Easy, Easy, Moderate, Difficult, Very Difficult).
* **Ease of Cleanup** (`Ease of Cleanup`, select_one, required): Rate how easy it was to clean up after application (Very Easy, Easy, Moderate, Difficult, Very Difficult).
* **Odor Rating** (`Odor Rating`, select_one, required): Rate the strength of the adhesive's odor (No odor, Mild, Moderate, Strong, Overpowering).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the adhesive (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`Recommendation Likelihood`, number, required): Choose a number indicating how likely you are to recommend this adhesive (1-10).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`Suggestions for Improvement`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improving the adhesive's performance.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments about the adhesive.
