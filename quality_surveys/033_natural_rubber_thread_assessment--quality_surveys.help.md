# Natural Rubber Thread Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Natural Rubber Thread Assessment form is designed to evaluate the quality of a natural rubber thread. It assesses various aspects of the thread, such as defects and rating systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them accurately based on your assessment.
2. For each question, select the relevant answer from the options provided.
3. If a question is not applicable, mark the "Other" option and provide a brief explanation.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to provide some basic information about the thread.
* **Defect Rating**: This question asks you to rate the thread's quality based on the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, and Very Poor.
* **Defect 1**: This question asks you to select multiple defects that are present in the thread. You can choose one or more of the options: Bulge, Tangle, Knot, Slub, and Other.
* **Page 2**: This is the second page of the form where you will be asked to provide more information about the thread.
* **Defect 2**: This question asks you to rate the thread's quality based on the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, and Very Poor.
* **Defect 3**: This question asks you to select multiple defects that are present in the thread. You can choose one or more of the options: Bulge, Tangle, Knot, Slub, and Other.
* **Signed Evaluation**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or observations about the thread.
* **Page 3**: This is the third page of the form where you will be asked to provide more information about the thread.
* ... (Continue this pattern for the remaining pages)

Note: The form is quite long, with 20 pages in total. Please review each page carefully and provide accurate answers based on your assessment.
