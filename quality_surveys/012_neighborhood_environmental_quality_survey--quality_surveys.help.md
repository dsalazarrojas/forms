# Neighborhood Environmental Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from residents about their neighborhood's environmental quality. It aims to gather data on various aspects of the neighborhood, including demographics and resident experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your zip code in the "What is your zip code?" field.
2. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field (optional).
5. Select a date from the calendar to answer "When did you move into this neighborhood?".
6. In the "Have you noticed any problems or concerns with your neighborhood?" field, provide a brief description of any issues you have encountered.
7. Enter a description of the problems or concerns you have with your neighborhood in the "What are the problems or concerns you have about your neighborhood?" field.
8. Enter the number of children under 12 living in your house in the "How many children under the age of 12 live in your house?" field.
9. Enter the total square footage of your home in the "What is the total square footage of your home?" field (optional).
10. Enter the number of stories in your house in the "How many stories are in your house?" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your zip code?** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your zip code in the format "XXXXX" or "XXXXX-XXXX".
* **What is your name?** (`2`, `text`, required: true):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **What is your email address?** (`3`, `email`, required: true):
	+ Enter a valid email address that you use to contact you.
* **What is your phone number?** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
* **When did you move into this neighborhood?** (`5`, `date`, required: true):
	+ Select a date from the calendar that corresponds to the date you moved into the neighborhood.
* **Have you noticed any problems or concerns with your neighborhood?** (`6`, `text`, required: true):
	+ Provide a brief description of any issues you have experienced in the neighborhood.
* **What are the problems or concerns you have about your neighborhood?** (`7`, `text`, required: true):
	+ Describe any issues you have with the neighborhood in detail.
* **How many children under 12 live in your house?** (`8`, `number`, required: true):
	+ Enter the number of children under 12 living in your household.
* **What is the total square footage of your home?** (`9`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Enter the total square footage of your home in square feet.
* **How many stories are in your house?** (`10`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of stories in your house, including any additional levels such as basements or attics.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and honest information to help us better understand your neighborhood's environmental quality.
- If you have any problems or concerns, please describe them in as much detail as possible to help us understand the issues you face.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact us.
