# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey is a tool to gather information about your understanding of climate policies and their impact on youth. Your input will help us understand the current state of climate policy awareness and perception among young people.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions on each page. You can move to the next page by clicking on the "Next" button. When you have finished, click on the "Submit" button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: This page is a placeholder page and not required for the user to fill in. Please do not fill in anything in this field.
* **youth-climate-policy-understanding-survey**: This field is the title of the survey, which is not editable.
* **climate_change_concern**: This field asks about your concern for climate change, which refers to how worried you are about the current climate situation.
* **climate_policies**: This field asks about your understanding of climate policies, including their current implementation and effectiveness.
* **policy_implementation**: This field asks about the policies you believe are being implemented to address climate change.
* **policy_effectiveness**: This field asks about your opinion on how effective you think climate policies are in addressing climate change.
* **survey_involvement**: This field asks about your level of involvement in the policy-making process related to climate change.
* **policy_urgency**: This field asks about your opinion on how urgent you think climate action is.
* **demographic_data**: This field collects demographic information, such as age and sex, which will help us understand who is responding to the survey.
* **page_two**: This page is a placeholder page and not required for the user to fill in. Please do not fill in anything in this field.
* **page_three**: This page is a placeholder page and not required for the user to fill in. Please do not fill in anything in this field.
* **survey_completion**: This field asks you to confirm you have completed the survey.
* **survey_completion_date**: This field is a date field where you can choose the date of completion of the survey.
