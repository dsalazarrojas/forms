# Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track the accuracy of prescription fulfillment. It allows users to record and report discrepancies between prescribed and actual orders, including the number of correct and incorrect orders, and the reasons for discrepancies. The form is used to facilitate communication and action planning within the team, ensuring timely and effective resolution of errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Report Date**, which is the date on which the report is submitted.
2. Enter the **Number of Prophets**, which is the number of orders that had discrepancies.
3. Enter the **Correct Orders** and **Incorrect Orders**, which represent the number of correct and incorrect orders, respectively.
4. Enter the **Reasons for Prophets Discrepancy**, which are the explanations for the discrepancies.
5. Select an **Action Planned**, which indicates the plan for resolution. Options are:
	* None
	* Awaiting
	* Planned
	* In Progress
	* Completed
6. Select an **Action Implemented**, which indicates the action taken to resolve the discrepancy. Options are:
	* None
	* Awaiting
	* Planned
	* In Progress
	* Completed
7. Select an **Action Effective**, which indicates the effectiveness of the action taken. Options are:
	* None
	* Awaiting
	* Planned
	* In Progress
	* Completed
8. Select a **Reason for Prophets Implementation**, which explains why the action was taken. Options are:
	* None
	* Awaiting
	* Planned
	* In Progress
	* Completed
9. Enter the **Team Lead**, **Team Member**, **Location**, **Pharmacist**, and **Technician**, which are the individuals responsible for the discrepancy and their roles.
10. Enter any additional **Comments**, which are notes or explanations for the discrepancy.
11. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the action taken to resolve the discrepancy.
12. Enter any additional **Comments**, which are final notes or explanations for the discrepancy.
13. If necessary, enter the **Action Date** and **Action Time**, which are the date and time when the action was taken.
14. Finally, select a **Team Lead 2**, **Team Member 2**, **Location 2**, and **Pharmacist 2**, which are additional team members or individuals involved in the resolution, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Date**: The date on which the report is submitted.
* **Prophets**: The number of orders that had discrepancies.
* **Correct Orders**: The number of correct orders.
* **Incorrect Orders**: The number of incorrect orders.
* **Reasons for Prophets Discrepancy**: The explanations for the discrepancies.
* **Action Planned**: The plan for resolution.
* **Action Implemented**: The action taken to resolve the discrepancy.
* **Action Effective**: The effectiveness of the action taken.
* **Reason for Prophets Implementation**: The explanation for the action taken.
* **Team Lead**: The individual responsible for the discrepancy.
* **Team Member**: The individual responsible for the discrepancy.
* **Location**: The location where the discrepancy occurred.
* **Pharmacist**: The pharmacist involved in the discrepancy.
* **Technician**: The technician involved in the discrepancy.
* **Other**: Additional information about the discrepancy.
* **Comments**: Notes or explanations for the discrepancy.
* **Date**: The date of the action taken to resolve the discrepancy.
* **Time**: The time of the action taken to resolve the discrepancy.
* **Action Date**: The date when the action was taken.
* **Action Time**: The time when the action was taken.
* **Team Lead 2**: Additional team lead information.
* **Team Member 2**: Additional team member information.
* **Location 2**: Additional location information.
* **Pharmacist 2**: Additional pharmacist information.
* **Technician 2**: Additional technician information.

Note: This help guide is for internal use within the organization, but it is assumed that the users are familiar with standard business practices and terminology.
