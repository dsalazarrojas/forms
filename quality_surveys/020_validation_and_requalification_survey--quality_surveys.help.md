# Validation And Requalification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Validation and Requalification Survey is used to collect data on the quality control and assurance process, including qualification and requalification periods, changes made, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction (field 1) to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Select the relevant organizations or departments involved in the quality control and assurance process (field 2).
3. Answer questions about the qualification and requalification periods (fields 6, 7, 12, and 13).
4. Record any changes made during these periods (fields 3, 4, 5, and 16).
5. Update the status of the product or service (field 11).
6. Enter the relevant dates and times (fields 6, 7, 12, 13, 14, and 15).
7. Add any additional comments or notes (field 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (field 1, text): This is a brief introduction or instruction for the form. It helps to set the context and purpose of the survey.
* **organization** (field 2, select_multiple): Select one or more organizations or departments involved in the quality control and assurance process.
* **qualification** (field 3, select_one): Select the qualification period for the product or service.
* **changes** (field 4, select_one): Select any changes made during the qualification period.
* **review** (field 5, select_one): Select the review period for the product or service.
* **qualification period** (field 6, date): Enter the qualification period for the product or service.
* **requalification period** (field 7, date): Enter the requalification period for the product or service.
* **email** (field 8, email): Enter the email address for contact or reference.
* **note** (field 9, note): Add any additional comments or notes about the product or service.
* **Qualification** (field 10, select_one): Select the qualification status for the product or service.
* **status** (field 11, select_one): Select the current status of the product or service.
* **qualification date** (field 12, date): Enter the date of the qualification period.
* **requalification date** (field 13, date): Enter the date of the requalification period.
* **qualification time** (field 14, time): Enter the time of the qualification period.
* **requalification time** (field 15, time): Enter the time of the requalification period.
* **changes** (field 16, select_multiple): Select any changes made during the requalification period.
* **cha** (field 17, select_multiple): This field is not used in the provided form, but it may have been left as an unused placeholder.
