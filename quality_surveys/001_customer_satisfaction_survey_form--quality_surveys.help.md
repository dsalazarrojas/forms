# Customer Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from customers on their overall satisfaction and experience with your product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your honest experience.
2. Select the options that best fit your feelings or responses.
3. Fill in any text fields with your thoughts or comments.
4. Submit the form once you have answered all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Survey Form** (`customer_survey_form`, text, optional): This field is a text box where you can write any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Please select how you feel about your overall experience with our product or service:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, note, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with Question 1 (not displayed).
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, text, optional): Answer Question 2 with your thoughts or comments.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply for Question 3:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Question 4** (`question_4`, time, optional): Answer Question 4 with the amount of time you used our service.
* **Question 5** (`question_5`, date, optional): Answer Question 5 with the date when you last used our service.
* **Question 6** (`question_6`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted about your feedback).
* **Question 7** (`question_7`, select_one, optional): Answer Question 7:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Question 8** (`question_8`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply for Question 8:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Question 9** (`question_9`, note, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with Question 9 (not displayed).
* **Question 10** (`question_10`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply for Question 10:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Very Dissatisfied

## Tips
- Be honest in your responses.
- Take your time to answer each question carefully.
- If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please use the text fields to provide them.
- We value your feedback and appreciate your time.
