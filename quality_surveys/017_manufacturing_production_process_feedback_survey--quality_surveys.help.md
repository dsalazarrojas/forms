# Manufacturing Production Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from production team members about the manufacturing production process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Production Team**: Enter your name and title, so we can understand who provided this feedback.
2. **What production process is this feedback about?**: Select the process you are providing feedback on. Options are:
	* Assembly
	* Inspection
	* Other (please specify)
3. **Safety Concerns**: If you have concerns about safety, provide a brief description of the incident.
4. **Training Needs**: Check all that apply:
	* On-the-job training
	* Classroom training
	* Both

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Team**: Your name and title, which helps us know who provided this feedback.
* **What production process is this feedback about?**: This field will help us understand which production process you are providing feedback on.
* **Safety Concerns**: This field is for brief descriptions of safety incidents. If you need to report an accident or incident, this is where to provide details.
* **Training Needs**: This field will help us determine what type of training or support is needed to ensure you or your team are proficient in the production process. Select all that apply, as needed.
