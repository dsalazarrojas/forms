# Catalyst Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

This form is designed to help you evaluate the performance of a catalyst in a specific application. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the catalyst's effectiveness, safety, and overall performance. Please fill out the form carefully, and if you have any doubts or concerns, you can ask for help.

This form should only be filled out once for each catalyst evaluation. If you need to evaluate multiple catalysts, you will need to fill out this form separately for each one.

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the performance of a catalyst in a specific application. The information collected will help us improve our understanding of catalyst behavior and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Respondent Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your email address as it appears on your business card or profile.
3. **Department**: Select your department from the options provided:
	* Research and Development
	* Quality Control
	* Process Engineering
	* Production
	* Management
	* Other
4. **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date on which the evaluation was conducted.
5. **Catalyst ID or Batch Number**: Enter the identification number for the catalyst evaluated.
6. **Catalyst Type**: Select the type of catalyst being evaluated:
	* Homogeneous
	* Heterogeneous
	* Enzymatic
	* Photocatalyst
	* Electrocatalyst
	* Other
7. **Application Area**: Select the area where the catalyst was used:
	* Petrochemical
	* Pharmaceutical
	* Environmental
	* Energy Production
	* Food Processing
	* Other
8. **Reaction Temperature**: Enter the temperature in Celsius at which the reaction was conducted.
9. **Reaction Pressure**: Enter the pressure in bar or atm at which the reaction was conducted (optional).
10. **Conversion Rate**: Enter the percentage conversion achieved.
11. **Selectivity**: Enter the product selectivity percentage.
12. **Yield**: Enter the overall yield percentage.
13. **Catalytic Activity Rating**: Rate the catalyst's activity:
	* Excellent
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
14. **Stability Rating**: Rate the catalyst's stability:
	* Excellent
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
15. **Deactivation Observed**: Select if deactivation was noticed:
	* True
	* False
	* Uncertain
16. **Regeneration Possible**: Select if the catalyst can be regenerated:
	* True
	* False
	* Not Tested
17. **Number of Cycles Completed**: Enter the number of reaction cycles conducted (optional).
18. **Cost Effectiveness**: Rate the cost effectiveness:
	* Very Cost Effective
	* Cost Effective
	* Neutral
	* Expensive
	* Very Expensive
19. **Safety Concerns**: Enter any safety issues observed.
20. **Environmental Impact**: Enter any environmental considerations.
21. **Would You Recommend This Catalyst**: Select if you would recommend the catalyst for similar applications:
	* Definitely Yes
	* Probably Yes
	* Neutral
	* Probably No
	* Definitely No
22. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully, as the information collected will be used to improve our understanding of catalyst behavior and performance.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask for help.
* This form should only be filled out once for each catalyst evaluation.
* If you need to evaluate multiple catalysts, fill out this form separately for each one.
