# Furniture Production Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the quality and production process of a furniture production project. It will help assessors to identify areas of improvement and provide feedback to stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start, navigate through the pages of the form by clicking on the "Next" or "Previous" buttons.
*   For each page, read the label and description of the fields carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
*   Ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the project, including its purpose, scope, and objectives. Please be concise and clear in your answer.
*   **Production Process** (`production_process`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable production processes used in the project, such as "Production", "Manufacturing", and "Assembly".
*   **Quality Standards** (`quality_standards`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable quality standards applied to the project, such as "ISO 9001", "ISO 14001", and "ASME".
*   **Quality Measures** (`quality_measures`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing the quality measures achieved during the project.
*   **Production Capacity** (`production_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the total production capacity of the project.
*   **Project Status** (`project_status`, select one, optional): Select the current status of the project, such as "Active", "Inactive", or "Completed".
*   **Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected or actual completion date of the project.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the project.

## Tips

*   Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
*   Please note that this form is meant to be a guide, and your answers will help assessors provide valuable feedback to stakeholders.
