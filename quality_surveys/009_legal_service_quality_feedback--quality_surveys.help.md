# Legal Service Quality Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the quality of our legal services. Your input will help us understand how we can improve and better serve you and others in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the main reason for your legal service request (Category).
2. Indicate whether you had a positive, negative, or mixed experience with our legal service.
3. Confirm whether your legal case was resolved.
4. Answer if you would recommend our service to others.
5. Enter the date you received our service.
6. Optionally, provide any additional comments about our service.
7. Select a contact person (if applicable).
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Legal Consultation, Legal Representation, Other): Select the main reason for your legal service request.
* **Did you have a positive experience with our legal service?** (Positive, Negative, Mixed): Indicate the quality of your experience.
* **Was your legal case resolved?** (True, False): Confirm the outcome of your case.
* **Would you recommend our legal service to others?** (True, False): Share your recommendation.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date you received our service.
* **Comments about our service**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Contact Person** (Yes, No): Indicate if a contact person was involved.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
