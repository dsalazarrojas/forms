# Digital Assessment Feedback Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality of a digital assessment, aiming to identify areas of improvement for future assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully about your overall impression of the assessment.
2. Rate the difficulty level of the assessment on a scale of 1-5.
3. Choose the relevance of the questions, selecting one or more options that best fit your experience.
4. Rate the clarity of the instructions on a scale of 1-5.
5. Share any suggestions you have for improving the assessment.
6. Provide any additional feedback you have about the assessment.
7. Rate the overall quality of the assessment on a scale of 1-5.
8. Indicate whether you think the assessment meets the learning objectives.
9. Choose how likely you are to use the assessment in future classes on a scale of 1-5.
10. Optionally, add any additional feedback or comments about the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your overall impression of this assessment?** (`question_1`): This question helps us understand your initial reaction to the assessment.
* **How would you rate the difficulty level of this assessment?** (`question_2`): This question helps us gauge how challenging the assessment was for you.
* **How would you rate the relevance of the questions?** (`question_3`): This question assesses how well the questions align with the learning objectives.
* **How would you rate the clarity of the instructions?** (`question_4`): This question checks if the instructions were clear and easy to follow.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving this assessment?** (`question_5`): Share any ideas you have for making the assessment better.
* **Is there anything else you would like to say about this assessment?** (`question_6`): Add any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of this assessment?** (`question_7`): This question rates your overall satisfaction with the assessment.
* **Do you think this assessment meets the learning objectives?** (`question_8`): This question confirms if the assessment aligns with its intended goals.
* **How likely are you to use this assessment in future classes?** (`question_9`): This question gauges your willingness to reuse the assessment in the future.
* **Do you have any other feedback about this assessment?** (`question_9`): This question allows for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

Tips:
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the space provided.
* If you need more guidance on how to answer any question, don't hesitate to ask.
