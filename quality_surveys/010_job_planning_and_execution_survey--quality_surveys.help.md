# Job Planning And Execution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Planning And Execution Survey is designed to collect data on project details, team performance, and customer interactions. This information helps us improve our services and enhance overall project management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, ensure that you provide the most up-to-date information.
3. If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank or note that it's not applicable in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): This field is used to record the name of the project you are working on.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of job from the list (Project, Maintenance, Service).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the job started.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the job finished.
* **Actual Start Date** (`actual_start_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the actual date when the job started.
* **Actual End Date** (`actual_end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the actual date when the job finished.
* **Planned Start Date** (`planned_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the planned start date of the job.
* **Planned End Date** (`planned_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the planned end date of the job.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Select all team members involved in the project from the list.
* **Team Members Notes** (`team_members_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes about team members.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, optional): Select the team lead from the list.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, select_one, optional): Select the project manager from the list.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the job took place.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes about the job.
* **Actual Hours** (`actual_hours`, number, optional): Enter the actual number of hours worked.
* **Actual Costs** (`actual_costs`, number, optional): Enter the actual costs incurred.
* **Actual Revenue** (`actual_revenue`, number, optional): Enter the actual revenue generated.
* **Planned Hours** (`planned_hours`, number, optional): Enter the planned number of hours for the job.
* **Planned Costs** (`planned_costs`, number, optional): Enter the planned costs for the job.
* **Planned Revenue** (`planned_revenue`, number, optional): Enter the planned revenue for the job.
* **Job Outcome** (`job_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the outcome of the job.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback about the job.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
