# Surgical Precision Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the precision of surgical procedures and gather information on quality control measures, feedback availability, and resource utilization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Review each field carefully before proceeding to the next section.
3. Use numbers to indicate frequency or rating when applicable.
4. Select the correct option for peer review frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Surgical Precision Evaluation (`surgical_precision_evaluation_survey`, text, required: false)
This field is where you'll describe your surgical procedures and how precise they are. Provide a brief explanation of your surgical precision methods and control measures.

### 2. Quality Improvement (`quality_improvement`, text, required: false)
Here, explain how you improve the quality of your surgical procedures. Describe any new methods or techniques you've incorporated.

### 3. Peer Review (`peer_review`, text, required: false)
In this field, describe how frequently you have peer reviews of your surgical procedures.

### 4. Data Collection (`data_collection`, text, required: false)
Explain how you collect data for your surgical procedures. Describe your methods and tools used.

### 5. Ongoing Quality Control (`ongoing_quality_control`, text, required: false)
Here, describe the quality control measures you have in place for your surgical procedures.

### 6. Feedback Availability (`feedback_availability`, text, required: false)
Describe how feedback is available for your surgical procedures. How do you solicit feedback, and from whom?

### 7. Resource Availability (`resource_availability`, text, required: false)
Explain how resources are available for your surgical procedures. Describe the availability of equipment, personnel, and other resources.

### 8. Quality Rating (`quality_rating`, number, required: false)
Rate the quality of your surgical procedures on a scale of 1-5. 1 is the lowest, and 5 is the highest.

### 9. Feedback Frequency (`feedback_frequency`, number, required: false)
Rate how frequently feedback is provided for your surgical procedures. Use the scale of 1-5. 1 is rarely, and 5 is always.

### 10. Quality Control Measures (`quality_control_measures`, text, required: false)
Describe the quality control measures you have in place for your surgical procedures.

### 11. Peer Review Frequency (`peer_review_frequency`, select one, required: true)
Select the frequency of peer review from the following options:
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
	+ Always

### 12. Data Collection Methods (`data_collection_methods`, text, required: false)
Explain the methods and tools you use to collect data for your surgical procedures.

### 13. Ongoing Quality Control Rating (`ongoing_quality_control_rating`, number, required: false)
Rate the effectiveness of your ongoing quality control measures. Use the scale of 1-5. 1 is the lowest, and 5 is the highest.

### 14. Feedback Availability Rating (`feedback_availability_rating`, number, required: false)
Rate the availability of feedback for your surgical procedures. Use the scale of 1-5. 1 is the lowest, and 5 is the highest.

## Tips
* Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding.
* Use numbers to rate quality and feedback frequency.
