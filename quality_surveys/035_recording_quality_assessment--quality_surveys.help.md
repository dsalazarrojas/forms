# Recording Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the quality of a recording, allowing users to evaluate the audio or video content on a scale of excellent, good, fair, or poor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the assessment for each field based on your opinion.
2. Choose one of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) that best describes your assessment.
3. If you have specific feedback or comments, you can type them in the "Additional Comments" field (field 4).

	+ Fair: The recording has noticeable issues, but is still usable.
	+ Poor: The recording is of very poor quality.
