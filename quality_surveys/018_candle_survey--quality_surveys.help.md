# Candle Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from customers about their candle purchasing experience. It is used to collect data on customer satisfaction, preferences, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Answer questions about the type of candle you purchased, its scent, size, and burn time.
3. Rate the quality of the candle, including the scent, burn, and wick performance.
4. Evaluate the value for money and overall satisfaction with the purchase.
5. Share your likelihood of purchasing again and recommending the candle to others.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Date of Purchase** (`purchase_date`, date, required): The date you purchased the candle.
* **Type of Candle Purchased** (`candle_type`, select, required): The type of candle you purchased (Jar, Pillar, Votive, etc.).
* **Scent or Fragrance** (`candle_scent`, text, required): The scent or fragrance of the candle you purchased.
* **Candle Size** (`candle_size`, select, required): The size of the candle you purchased (Small, Medium, Large, etc.).
* **Where Did You Purchase** (`purchase_location`, select, required): The location where you purchased the candle (Online store, Physical retail store, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select, required): Your overall satisfaction with the purchase (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, etc.).
* **Scent Quality Rating** (`scent_quality_rating`, select, required): Your rating of the fragrance (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Scent Strength** (`scent_strength`, select, required): The strength of the scent when burning (Too strong, Slightly strong, Just right, Slightly weak, Too weak).
* **Scent Longevity** (`scent_longevity`, select, required): How long the scent lasts when burning (Entire burn time, Most of burn time, Half of burn time, Briefly, No scent when burning).
* **Burn Quality** (`burn_quality`, select, required): The quality of the burn (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Total Burn Time** (`burn_time`, select, required): The length of time the candle lasted (As expected, Longer than expected, Shorter than expected, Much shorter than expected).
* **Wax Quality Rating** (`wax_quality_rating`, select, required): Your rating of the wax quality (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Wick Performance** (`wick_performance`, select, required): The performance of the wick (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Container or Jar Quality** (`container_quality`, select, optional): The quality of the container or jar (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Packaging Rating** (`packaging_rating`, select, required): The quality of the packaging (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Did Product Arrive Damaged** (`packaging_condition`, select, optional): Whether the product arrived in perfect condition (No - perfect condition, Minor damage, Moderate damage, Severe damage, Did not ship).
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select, required): Your evaluation of the value for money (Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Poor value, Very poor value).
* **Would You Purchase Again** (`would_purchase_again`, select, required): Your likelihood of purchasing the candle again (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Would You Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, select, required): Your likelihood of recommending the candle to others (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **What Do You Like Most** (`favorite_aspect`, text, optional): The aspect of the candle that you like most.
* **What Could Be Improved** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Suggestions for improvement.
* **What Scents Would You Like to See** (`desired_scents`, text, optional): Future product ideas.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other feedback or comments.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): The date you completed the survey.
