# Quality Assurance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Assurance Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and assessments on the quality of a specific process, product, or service. This form helps identify areas of improvement and tracks the progression of quality over time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all mandatory questions, marked as required, and answer any additional questions that you feel are relevant.
2. Ensure that you are answering accurately and truthfully based on your experience or observation.
3. If a question does not apply to your situation, please select "N/A" or provide a clear explanation.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quality Assurance Evaluation Form** (`question_1`): This is a text field where you can provide a brief description of the process, product, or service being evaluated.
* **quality_assurance_level** (`question_2`): This question requires you to rate the quality level of the process, product, or service. Choose from the options "Good" or "Poor" based on your assessment.
* **Quality Assurance Score** (`question_3`): If applicable, please provide a numerical value to score the quality of the process, product, or service. This is an optional field.
* **Quality Assurance Comments** (`question_4`): If you have any comments or observations about the process, product, or service, please provide them here. This field is optional.
* **Quality Assurance Evaluation Date** (`question_5`): Please select the date of the evaluation or assessment.
* **Quality Assurance Time** (`question_6`): Please select the time of the evaluation or assessment.
* **quality_assurance_rating** (`question_7`): This question is similar to `question_2` and requires you to rate the quality level of the process, product, or service. However, this field is optional.
* **Contact Information** (`question_8`): If you'd like to be contacted for further clarification or feedback, please provide your contact information (email or phone number).
* **Quality Assurance Description** (`question_9`): If you have any additional comments or observations about the process, product, or service, please provide them here. This field is optional.
* **Quality Assurance Additional Comments** (`question_10`): If you have any further comments or observations, please provide them here. This field is optional.
* **Quality Assurance Form Date** (`question_11`): Please select the date the form was filled out.
* **Quality Assurance Form Time** (`question_12`): Please select the time the form was filled out.

Note: Please ensure that all mandatory questions are answered before submitting the form.
