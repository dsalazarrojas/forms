# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey is a self-assessment tool for employees to express their opinions and concerns about potential or ongoing foreign conflicts that may impact their work or organization. This form is meant to be completed by employees who are interested in exploring their awareness and perceptions of these conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully before starting. This will help you understand the questions and their context.
2.  Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3.  Use the provided options for the multiple-choice questions to select your response.
4.  If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
5.  Complete the form in one session, as saving and resuming is not supported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey** (`foreign_conflict_awareness_survey`, text, optional):
    This is the title of the form that will be displayed on the page.
*   **Information Sources** (`information_sources`, select_multiple, optional):
    This question asks how you gather information about potential or ongoing foreign conflicts.
    Select all the sources that apply to you from the options provided.
*   **Perceptions of Foreign Conflict** (`perceptions_of_foreign_conflict`, select_one, optional):
    This question asks about your perception of foreign conflicts.
    Select only one response from the options provided.
*   **Public Awareness** (`public_awareness`, number, optional):
    This question asks how much you think the public is aware of foreign conflicts.
    Enter a number between 0 and 100 to indicate your level of awareness.
*   **Drag and Drop Interface** (`drag_and_drop_interface`, text, optional):
    This is an experimental section of the form that may help in the future to provide a drag-and-drop interface.
    This field is currently not active and should not be filled.
*   **No Code Form Builder** (`no_code_form_builder`, text, optional):
    This is another experimental section of the form that may help in the future to provide a no-code form builder.
    This field is currently not active and should not be filled.
