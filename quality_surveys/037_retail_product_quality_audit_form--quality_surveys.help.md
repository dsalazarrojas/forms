# Retail Product Quality Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retail Product Quality Audit Form is a tool used to collect data for retail product quality audits. Its purpose is to assess the quality of retail products, identify areas for improvement, and provide detailed feedback to help retailers make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being audited.
2. **Product Description**: Provide a brief description of the product.
3. **Category**: Select the category the product belongs to.
4. **Audit Date**: Enter the date of the audit.
5. **Time Start**: Enter the start time of the audit.
6. **Time End**: Enter the end time of the audit.
7. **Location**: Enter the location of the audit.
8. **Quality Check 1**: Select the quality of the product for the first check.
9. **Quality Check 2**: Select the quality of the product for the second check.
10. **Quality Check 3**: Select the quality of the product for the third check.
11. **Observation**: Enter any notable observations about the product.
12. **Note**: Enter any additional comments about the product.
13. **Email**: Enter your email address (not required).
14. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (not required).
15. **Assigned To**: Enter the person(s) responsible for the product.
16. **Assigned By**: Enter who assigned the audit (not required).
17. **Review**: Enter the person responsible for the review (not required).
18. **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review (not required).
19. **Time Reviewed**: Enter the time of the review (not required).
20. **Quality Score**: Enter a numerical score to represent the product's quality (not required).
21. **Product Status**: Enter the status of the product.
22. **Category Status**: Enter the status of the category.
23. **Location Status**: Enter the status of the location.
24. **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the product.
