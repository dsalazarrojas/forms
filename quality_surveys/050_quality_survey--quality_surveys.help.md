# Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to measure the satisfaction of customers after a purchase or during a service or delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please carefully read the instructions and questions on each page.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to provide answers for all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction** (page 1, `introduction`, optional)
*   `Introduction` is a note field that provides an overview of the purpose of the form.

### **How satisfied are you with your purchase?** (page 2, `customer_satisfaction`, optional)
*   **Very Satisfied**: You are extremely happy with the purchase.
*   **Somewhat Satisfied**: You are somewhat happy with the purchase.
*   **Not Satisfied**: You are not happy with the purchase.
*   **Not At All Satisfied**: You are extremely unhappy with the purchase.

### **What did we get right?** (page 3, `customer_satisfaction_what_did_we_get_right`, optional)
*   **The product met my expectations**: The product performed as expected.
*   **The product exceeded my expectations**: The product performed better than expected.
*   **The product did not meet my expectations**: The product did not perform as expected.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and thoughtful answers to each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* If you have any other comments or feedback, please use the "Note" field to provide them.
