# gender_violence_prevention_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about experiences and perceptions of gender-based violence in the community. It is intended for individuals who have personally experienced or witnessed gender-based violence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the pages that make up the form, each labeled as "Page 1", "Page 2", "Page 3", etc.
2.  Fill out the required fields, marked with a red asterisk \*.
3.  Leave blank fields that are not applicable to you.
4.  Use the provided options for select fields, such as "Male", "Female", or "Other" for contact information.
5.  Type in any additional information that you think is relevant to the question.
6.  Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.
