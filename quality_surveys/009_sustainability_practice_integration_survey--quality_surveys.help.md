# Sustainability Practice Integration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess the current state of sustainability practices within your organization, specifically in regards to environmental, social, and economic aspects. It will help us understand the level of awareness and influence of these practices within your operations, which can inform future improvements and initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on each page as accurately as possible.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best describe your current state.
3. For number fields, enter a numerical value between 0 and 100, where 0 means none and 100 means fully integrated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization ID**: Please select "Yes" if your organization has a unique ID for sustainability practices, or "No" if not.
* **Sustainability Practices Awareness**: Rate your level of awareness of sustainability practices, where 0 means none and 100 means fully aware.
* **Operations Awareness**: Select the level of awareness of operations-related sustainability practices within your organization.
	+ Aware: fully aware of operations-related sustainability practices
	+ Limited: somewhat aware, but with some knowledge gaps
	+ Unaware: not aware at all
* **Environmental Awareness**: Select the level of awareness of environmental-related sustainability practices within your organization.
	+ Aware: fully aware of environmental-related sustainability practices
	+ Limited: somewhat aware, but with some knowledge gaps
	+ Unaware: not aware at all
* **Social Awareness**: Select the level of awareness of social-related sustainability practices within your organization.
	+ Aware: fully aware of social-related sustainability practices
	+ Limited: somewhat aware, but with some knowledge gaps
	+ Unaware: not aware at all
* **Economic Awareness**: Select the level of awareness of economic-related sustainability practices within your organization.
	+ Aware: fully aware of economic-related sustainability practices
	+ Limited: somewhat aware, but with some knowledge gaps
	+ Unaware: not aware at all
* **Operations Influence**: Rate the influence of operations on sustainability practices within your organization.
* **Environmental Influence**: Rate the influence of environmental practices on sustainability practices within your organization.
* **Social Influence**: Rate the influence of social practices on sustainability practices within your organization.
* **Economic Influence**: Rate the influence of economic practices on sustainability practices within your organization.
* **Operations Influence 2**: Rate the influence of operations on sustainability practices within your organization (if different from previous question).
* **Operations Influence Score**: This field is for rating the overall influence of operations on sustainability practices within your organization.
* **Environmental Practices**: Select the types of environmental practices currently implemented within your organization.
	+ Present: fully implemented
	+ Absent: not implemented
* **Social Practices**: Select the types of social practices currently implemented within your organization.
	+ Present: fully implemented
	+ Absent: not implemented
* **Economic Practices**: Select the types of economic practices currently implemented within your organization.
	+ Present: fully implemented
	+ Absent: not implemented
* **Operations Practices**: Select the types of operations-related sustainability practices currently implemented within your organization.
	+ Present: fully implemented
	+ Absent: not implemented
* **Environment Score**: Rate the overall score for environmental practices within your organization.
* **Social Score**: Rate the overall score for social practices within your organization.
* **Economic Score**: Rate the overall score for economic practices within your organization.
* **Operations Score**: Rate the overall score for operations practices within your organization.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately based on your current state.
- If you're unsure about any question, it's best to choose the option that best describes your current state.
- You can ask for help if you need clarification on any question.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
