# Quality System Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality System Self Assessment form is designed to collect feedback from quality system personnel on the effectiveness of the quality system. This feedback is essential for identifying areas of improvement and implementing necessary changes to enhance the quality system's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address (if required).
2. Answer the department questions, selecting all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Enter the date and time of your last quality system update (if applicable).
4. Add comments for each page to provide further information and insights on the quality system.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your first name to identify yourself as the quality system reviewer.

### **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required)
Enter your last name to identify yourself as the quality system reviewer.

### **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
Enter your email address to allow for future communication and follow-up.

### **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select all applicable departments that have been impacted by the quality system. You can choose from the following options:
 * Yes
 * No

### **Date Last Update** (`date_last_update`, `date`, optional)
Enter the date of your last quality system update. This will help track the progress of the quality system.

### **Time Last Update** (`time_last_update`, `time`, optional)
Enter the time of your last quality system update. This will help track the timing of the quality system updates.

### **First Page Comments** (`first_page_comments`, `note`, optional)
Provide additional comments on the first page of the form to offer further insights and information.

### **Department Comments** (`department_comments`, `note`, optional)
Provide additional comments on the departmental aspects of the quality system.

### **Performance Comments** (`performance_comments`, `note`, optional)
Provide comments on the performance of the quality system, including areas of improvement and suggestions for enhancement.

### **Process Change Comments** (`process_change_comments`, `note`, optional)
Provide comments on changes to the quality system processes.

### **Improvement Comments** (`improvement_comments`, `note`, optional)
Provide comments on areas of improvement for the quality system.
