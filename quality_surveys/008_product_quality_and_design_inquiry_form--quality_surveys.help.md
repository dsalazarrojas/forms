# Product Quality And Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and inquiries on product quality and design from customers. It helps the product development team to understand customer needs and expectations, ensuring that the product meets quality and design standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the product you are inquiring about from the dropdown menu on **Page 1: Product Information**.
2. If you have any comments or concerns about the product quality, select your rating on **Page 2: Product Quality**.
3. Provide any specific dates or events related to your inquiry on **Page 3: Date**.
4. If you have any time-sensitive concerns, provide the time on **Page 4: Time**.
5. Choose your rating for the product design on **Page 5: Product Design**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Information** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_1`, Select One, Required): This field is a dropdown menu where you select the product you are inquiring about.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_5`, Select Multiple, Required): Rate the product quality as **Excellent**, **Good**, or **Fair**.
* **Date** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_6`, Date, Required): Provide the date related to your inquiry, for example, the date you received the product or when you first used it.
* **Time** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_7`, Time, Required): If you have any time-sensitive concerns, provide the time of day or the exact moment related to your inquiry.
* **Product Design** (`product_quality_and_design_inquiry_form_8`, Select One, Required): Rate the product design as **A**, **B**, or **C**.
  (Note: This is a subjective evaluation and the ratings are arbitrary and subjective. Please choose the answer that best matches your perception of the product's design.)

Note: This form is not intended to be completed by employees or partners. Please do not submit feedback if you are not a customer.
