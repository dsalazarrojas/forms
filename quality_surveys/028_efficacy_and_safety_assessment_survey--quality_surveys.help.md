# Efficacy And Safety Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Efficacy And Safety Assessment Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our product, including their overall satisfaction, recommendations, and areas for improvement. This survey helps us to identify potential product issues, understand customer needs, and make data-driven decisions to enhance our product's efficacy and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, as they will help us understand your experience and improve our product.
2.  For questions with options, select the one that best describes your experience or opinion.
3.  For questions that ask for a numerical rating, choose a value between 1 and 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
4.  For the "How did you contact us regarding the issue with our product?" question, select the option that best describes how you reached out to us.
5.  For the "How would you rate the overall quality of our product support?" question, choose a numerical value between 1 and 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
6.  For the "Would you like to be contacted by us regarding any follow-up questions or issues?" question, select the option that best describes your preference.
7.  For the "What time of day do you usually contact us?" question, choose the time that best matches your typical schedule.
8.  For the "Which channel did you contact us through (phone, email, or web chat)?" question, select the option that best describes how you contacted us.
