# Product Quality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers on the quality of a product, helping us understand what we do well and where we can improve. Your input is valuable in shaping our product development and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field below. If you need any help or clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Quality Survey** (`1`, `note`, optional): This is the title of the survey, and not a field to fill out. Just for reference.
* **How would you rate the product?** (`2`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the product, with `5` being the highest and `1` being the lowest.
* **Quality Characteristics** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
	+ Select any quality characteristics that you associate with the product, such as quality1, quality2, or quality3.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`4`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with your overall experience, with `5` being the highest and `1` being the lowest.
* **Which of the following features are you using?** (`5`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
	+ Select any of the features that you are using, such as feature1, feature2, or feature3.
* **Additional Comments** (`6`, `text`, optional): 
	+ Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the product.
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, optional): 
	+ Enter your email address so we can follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`8`, `text`, optional): 
	+ Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **How would you rate the product?** (`9`, `number`, optional): 
	+ Enter a numeric rating from 1-5 for the product. Note that this field seems to be a duplicate of field 2, please choose one rating or ignore this field.
* **Category** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): 
	+ Choose the category of the product, such as Cat A, Cat B, or Cat C.
* **Subcategory** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): 
	+ Choose the subcategory of the product, such as Sub A, Sub B, or Sub C.

## Tips

* Please answer as honestly as possible to help us better understand your experience with the product.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
