# Housing Accessibility Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from residents about their experiences with housing accessibility. It will help property managers or owners identify areas for improvement and inform future planning decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Select all the accessibility features that apply to your experience with your current housing (page 1).
2. Choose the primary barrier you face when accessing your housing (page 3).
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with your housing's accessibility (page 5).
4. Select the type of housing you currently live in (page 6).
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Housing Accessibility Features**: On page 1, select all the accessibility features that you have found to be available in your housing. These features could include things like wheelchair accessibility, accessible showers, grab bars, and emergency alarm systems. Be as specific as possible when selecting the features that are relevant to your experience.
* **Barrier**: On page 3, select the primary barrier you face when accessing your housing. This could be something like a narrow door, a step, or a lack of accessible parking. Select only one option that best describes your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: On page 5, provide a brief description of your overall satisfaction with your housing's accessibility. This could be a positive or negative feedback, and be as specific as possible in your response.
* **Current Housing**: On page 6, select the type of housing you currently live in. This could be a house, apartment, condominium, or townhouse.
* **Additional Comments**: On page 7, provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with your housing's accessibility. Be as specific as possible and provide any details that may be helpful for future planning and improvement.
