# Service Reliability Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer feedback on the reliability of our services. It will help us identify areas where our services are not meeting customer expectations, allowing us to make improvements and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields. Make sure to provide detailed responses for fields where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **satisfaction** (Number): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service, using a scale of 0 to 100.
* **service_disruption** (Select One): Has our service experienced any disruptions recently? Choose 'Yes' if yes, 'No' if no, or 'Not Applicable' if this question does not apply to your experience.
* **service_disruption_details** (Text): If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, please describe the disruption you experienced.
* **satisfaction_score** (Number): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service, using a scale of 1 to 10.
* **service_disruption_score** (Number): Rate your satisfaction with our service, excluding any disruptions, using a scale of 0 to 100.
* **support_team_response** (Text): Share any comments about how our support team has responded to you.
* **additional_comments** (Text): Share any other comments or concerns you may have about our service.
* **service_reliability_form** (Text): This field is currently not used for inputting data. It's just a label and not a field for customer to fill in.

Note: Fields with 'required: false' are optional.
