# Educational Workshop Quality Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide is designed to help you complete the Educational Workshop Quality Assessment form accurately and thoroughly. The form is used to collect feedback on educational workshops, enabling workshop organizers to improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

This section explains the steps to complete the form:

1.  Please fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2.  If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the text fields for that purpose.
3.  Rate your experience by selecting the corresponding option for each question.
4.  For questions with multiple options, select only one that best describes your experience.
5.  Be honest and provide feedback that is helpful for future workshop improvements.
6.  Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields, marked as (*) in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the workshop you attended.
* **Facilitator** (`facilitator`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the facilitator of the workshop.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall experience from 1-5.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, text, required/optional): Describe the length of the session.
* **Content** (`content`, select_one, required/optional): Was the content of the workshop helpful?
* **Organization** (`organization`, select_one, required/optional): Was the workshop organized?
* **Interaction** (`interaction`, select_one, required/optional): Was there enough interaction between trainers and attendees?
* **Materials** (`materials`, select_multiple, required/optional): Which materials were available during the workshop?
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_one, required/optional): Was time management good?
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, required/optional): Was communication clear?
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the workshop.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required/optional): Do you have any recommendations for future sessions?
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required/optional): This field is not used and can be omitted.
* **Rating 2** (`rating2`, number, required/optional): This field is not used and can be omitted.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for future contact.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Describe the date of the workshop.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required/optional): Describe the time of the workshop.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Comments 2** (`comments2`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required/optional): Describe the length of the workshop.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, required/optional): Describe the start time of the workshop.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, required/optional): Describe the end time of the workshop.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Describe the date of the workshop.
