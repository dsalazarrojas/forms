# Quality Calibration Review Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form

*   To start the request, fill out the "Calibration Request" text field with a brief description of the calibration request.
*   Enter your name and department in the "Quality Requester Name" and "Requester Department" fields.
*   Select the level of calibration review required from the "Calibration Level" dropdown.
*   Select the category of the review from the "Category" dropdown.
*   Enter the date and time the review is requested in the "Request Date" and "Request Time" fields.
*   Add any additional details about the review in the "Request Details" text field.
*   Enter your email address in the "Requester Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Requester Phone" field.
*   If the review is completed, select "True" in the "Review Closed" field.
*   If the review is approved or rejected, select "Approved" or "Rejected" in the "Review Status" field.
*   Enter the review details and comments in the "Review Details" and "Review Comments" text fields.
*   Enter the reviewer's name and department in the "Reviewer Name" and "Reviewer Department" fields.
*   Enter the reviewer's phone number and email address in the "Reviewer Phone" and "Reviewer Email" fields.
*   Enter the reviewer's position in the "Reviewer Position" field.
*   Enter the reviewer's company in the "Reviewer Company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Calibration Request** (`quality_calibration_request`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the calibration request.
*   **Quality Requester Name** (`quality_requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the quality requester.
*   **Quality Calibration Level** (`quality_calibration_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of calibration review required.
*   **Category** (`quality_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the review.
*   **Request Date** (`quality_request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the review request.
*   **Request Time** (`quality_request_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the review request.
*   **Request Details** (`quality_request_details`, `text`, optional): Add any additional details about the review.
*   **Requester Email** (`quality_requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Requester Phone** (`quality_requester_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Requester Department** (`quality_requester_department`, `text`, required): Enter your department.
*   **Requester Title** (`quality_requester_title`, `text`, required): Enter your title.
*   **Requester Position** (`quality_requester_position`, `text`, required): Enter your position.
*   **Requester Company** (`quality_requester_company`, `text`, required): Enter your company.
*   **Review Date** (`quality_review_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the review.
*   **Review Time** (`quality_review_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the review.
*   **Review Details** (`quality_review_details`, `text`, optional): Enter the review details.
*   **Review Comments** (`quality_review_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the review.
*   **Review Status** (`quality_review_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the review.
*   **Review Closed** (`quality_review_closed`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the review is closed.
*   **Reviewer Name** (`quality_reviewer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the reviewer's name.
*   **Reviewer Phone** (`quality_reviewer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the reviewer's phone number.
*   **Reviewer Department** (`quality_reviewer_department`, `text`, optional): Enter the reviewer's department.
*   **Reviewer Position** (`quality_reviewer_position`, `text`, optional): Enter the reviewer's position.
*   **Reviewer Company** (`quality_reviewer_company`, `text`, optional): Enter the reviewer's company.
*   **Reviewer Email** (`quality_reviewer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the reviewer's email address.
