# Home Delivery Service Quality Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Delivery Service Quality Survey Form is designed to collect your feedback on our home delivery service. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill out the form with your feedback on your recent home delivery experience.
* Answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, use the "Additional Feedback" field.
* Once you've filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: This is the first page of the form where you can enter your feedback about your home delivery experience.
	+ **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of your recent home delivery.
	+ **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Rating 2 - 4**: Rate specific aspects of your experience (e.g., delivery time, quality of products, etc.).
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Email**: If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Additional Feedback**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your feedback.

Note: If you have any further feedback or comments after completing the form, please don't hesitate to contact us directly. We appreciate your input and value your feedback.
