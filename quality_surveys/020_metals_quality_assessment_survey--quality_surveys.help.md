# Metals Quality Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metals Quality Assessment Survey is a form designed to collect information about the physical properties of metals samples. The form is intended to be completed by experts in the field of metallurgy to quickly evaluate and categorize metals samples.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the sample ID number of the metal sample you wish to assess.
2. Choose the type of the sample (Yes or No).
3. Enter the weight of the sample in grams or kilograms.
4. Select the color of the sample (Yes or No).
5. Choose the shape of the sample (Yes or No).
6. Determine the hardness of the sample (Yes or No).
7. Enter the density of the sample in g/cm³.
8. Enter the melting point of the sample in degrees Celsius.
9. Select the element present in the sample (Yes or No).
10. Determine the atomic weight of the sample (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sample ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the metal sample.
* **Sample Type**: Choose "Yes" if the sample is of a metal type.
* **Sample Weight**: Enter the weight of the sample in grams or kilograms.
* **Sample Color**: Choose "Yes" if the sample has a distinct color.
* **Sample Shape**: Choose "Yes" if the sample is in its natural shape.
* **Hardness**: Choose "Yes" if the sample is hard.
* **Density**: Enter the density of the sample in g/cm³.
* **Melting Point**: Enter the melting point of the sample in degrees Celsius.
* **Element**: Choose "Yes" if the element is present in the sample.
* **Atomic Weight**: Choose "Yes" if the atomic weight of the sample is significant.
