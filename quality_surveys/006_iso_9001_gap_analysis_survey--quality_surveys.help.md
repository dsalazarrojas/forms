# iso_9001_gap_analysis_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The ISO 9001 Gap Analysis Survey form is designed to help organizations identify areas where they need to improve to meet the requirements of ISO 9001, a quality management standard. The survey covers various aspects of the standard and asks questions to gauge your current processes, systems, and practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the option that best represents your current state.
3. For text and number fields, enter your answer in the space provided.
4. Submit your completed form to review and identify areas of improvement.
