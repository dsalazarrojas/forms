# software_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect software satisfaction feedback from users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide answers to the questions based on your personal experience and opinion.
2. For the "overall_satisfaction_score" field, select a value that best represents your satisfaction level.
3. In "software_satisfaction_1" to "software_satisfaction_6", select all the options that apply to your experience.
4. For "date", "time", and "email" fields, enter your relevant contact information.
5. In "note" fields, provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **software_satisfaction**: Provide a brief description of your experience with the software.
  - A text field where you can type your thoughts and opinions.
- **overall_satisfaction_score**: Select a score that best represents your overall satisfaction with the software.
  - A scale from "very unlikely" to "very likely".
- **software_satisfaction_1**: Select all the options that apply to your experience with the software's features or functionality.
