# product_quality_score_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of a product. Your input will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
3. Rate the product's quality on a scale of 1-5.
4. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **product_name** (Product Name, Text, Optional)
Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
### 2. **product_rating** (Product Rating, Number, Optional)
Rate the product's quality on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
### 3. **product_comment** (Product Comment, Text, Optional)
If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the product, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please rate the product's quality based on your honest opinion.
* If you have any feedback, be as specific as possible to help us improve our products.
* You can leave any additional comments to provide more context for your rating.
