# Supplier Quality Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Quality Assessment Form is designed to gather information about a supplier's quality, reliability, communication, and other relevant factors. This form helps us assess the supplier's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the supplier information section with the supplier's name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Provide information about the product, including product name, quality level, and delivery speed.
3. Evaluate the supplier's quality, reliability, and communication by selecting the corresponding options or entering a brief description in the comments field.
4. Rate the supplier's overall performance with the supplier rating options.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the supplier.
6. Select the date and time of the assessment.
7. Finally, review and submit the form to record the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the supplier company.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the product being assessed.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, `text`, not required): Briefly describe the supplier's contact information.
* **Product Data** (`product_data`, `text`, not required): Provide additional information about the product, if necessary.
* **Quality Score** (`quality_score`, `number`, not required): Rate the supplier's quality on a numerical scale.
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, `select_one`, not required): Select the frequency of deliveries from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Reliability** (`reliability`, `text`, not required): Enter a description of the supplier's reliability.
* **Quality Control** (`quality_control`, `select_one`, not required): Select the quality control level from the options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, not required): Enter a description of the supplier's communication.
* **Supplier Rating** (`supplier_rating`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the overall performance rating from the options: Excellent, Good, Needs Improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the supplier.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, not required): Add any notes or reminders about the supplier.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Select the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): Select the time of the assessment.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter the supplier's physical address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, not required): Enter the city of the supplier's location.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, not required): Enter the state or province of the supplier's location.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, not required): Enter the supplier's zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, not required): Enter the country of the supplier's location.
* **Rating Quality Level** (`quality_level`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the quality level from the options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Delivery Speed** (`delivery_speed`, `select_one`, not required): Select the delivery speed from the options: Fast, Slow.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, not required): Enter the price of the product or service.
