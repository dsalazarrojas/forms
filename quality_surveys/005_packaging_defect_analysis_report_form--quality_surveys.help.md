# Packaging Defect Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about packaging defects, which is used to analyze and address the issues to ensure high-quality packaging. Please provide detailed information as much as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct section and page corresponding to the information you want to provide.
2. Fill in the required fields with the accurate information.
3. Use the dropdown menus for selecting the defect type and location.
4. Enter the date and time when the defect was discovered.
5. Fill in the Reporting Officer field with the name of the person reporting the defect.
6. Sign and date the Initial Signature field.
7. Enter the final comments about the defect in the Final Comments field.
8. Sign and date the Final Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Section 1 - Initial Information** (`packaging_form_page_1`, text, optional): This section contains general information about the packaging defect. Enter the information here.
* **Initial Comments** (`initial_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments you would like to share about the packaging defect.
* **Section 2 - Packaging Defects** (`packaging_form_page_2`, text, optional): This section is for providing additional details about the packaging defect.
* **Section 3 - Analysis** (`packaging_form_page_3`, text, optional): Enter any analysis or observations about the packaging defect.
* **Defect Type** (`defect_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of defect from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: Bump, Crease, Dimple, Kiss, Rise, Set, Sink, Slant, Warp
* **Defect Location** (`defect_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the location of the defect from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: Bottom, Left, Middle, Right, Top
* **Defect Date** (`defect_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the defect was discovered.
* **Defect Time** (`defect_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the defect was discovered.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person reporting the defect.
* **Initial Signature** (`initial_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the Initial Signature field.
* **Final Comments** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Enter the final comments about the packaging defect.
* **Final Signature** (`final_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the Final Signature field.
* **Section 4 - Final Information** (`packaging_form_page_4`, text, optional): This section is for providing final information about the packaging defect.
