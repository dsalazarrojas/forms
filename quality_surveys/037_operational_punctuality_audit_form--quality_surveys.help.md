# Operational Punctuality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather data on operational punctuality audits, which helps in tracking and evaluating the performance of operational activities. It ensures accurate and thorough investigation of delays and their effects on the process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're filling out the correct information.
2. Enter the start time of the delay accurately.
3. Provide a brief explanation of the delay reason.
4. Indicate the delay duration in hours or minutes.
5. Select the operational unit responsible for the delay.
6. Assess the impact of the delay on the process.
7. Identify the root cause of the delay.
8. Document any actions taken to address the delay.
9. Add any comments or observations relevant to the delay.
10. Choose the current operational status.
11. Select the review status.
12. Enter the person who reviewed the delay.
13. Enter the date and time of the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Time**: This field is used to report the start time of the delay. Enter the exact time when the delay occurred.
* **Delay Reason**: This field is for providing a brief explanation of why the delay occurred.
* **Delay Duration**: Enter the length of the delay in hours or minutes.
* **Operational Unit**: Select the department or team responsible for the delayed operation.
* **Delay Impact**: Assess the severity of the delay's impact on the process. Choose one of the options: None, Minor, Moderate, Major, or Severe.
* **Root Cause**: This field is for describing the reason or underlying cause of the delay.
* **Actions Taken**: Document any actions taken to address or rectify the delay.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations relevant to the delay.
* **Operational Status**: Choose the current status of the operational activity.
* **Review Status**: Select whether the delay has been reviewed, approved, or rejected.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the delay.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date when the delay was reviewed.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review.

Note: All fields are optional but required for a complete and accurate audit.
