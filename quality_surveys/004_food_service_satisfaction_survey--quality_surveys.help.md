# Food Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a satisfaction survey designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our food service. It helps us understand their level of satisfaction with various aspects of our service, such as food quality, service quality, cleanliness, and value for money. The data collected from this form will be used to identify areas for improvement and enhance customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select all the options that apply to your experience. If you don't see a specific option, choose "Other" and provide your own feedback in the text field below.
* Fill in the free-text fields (satisfaction level, service quality, recommendations) with your thoughts and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Service Satisfaction** (select multiple):
	+ This field asks about your overall satisfaction with our food service. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Satisfaction Level** (text):
	+ Please describe your level of satisfaction with our food service in this field. For example, you can write "very satisfied", "somewhat satisfied", "neutral", etc.
* **Food Quality** (select one):
	+ This field asks about the quality of the food we serve. Select "None" if you did not experience any issue, or "Other" if you want to provide your own feedback.
* **Service Quality** (text):
	+ Please describe your experience with our service staff. Were they friendly, efficient, and helpful? Share your thoughts here.
* **Cleanliness** (select one):
	+ This field asks about the cleanliness of our premises. Select "None" if you did not experience any issue, or "Other" if you want to provide your own feedback.
* **Ambiance** (text):
	+ Please describe your experience with the ambiance of our premises. Was it comfortable, welcoming, or something else? Share your thoughts here.
* **Value for Money** (select one):
	+ This field asks about the value you got from our service compared to what you expected. Select "None" if you did not experience any issue, or "Other" if you want to provide your own feedback.
* **Recommendations** (text):
	+ Do you have any suggestions or recommendations for us to improve our food service? Share your thoughts here.
