# Toy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toy Survey is a form designed to gather feedback and ratings on toys from end-users. This data will help us improve the design and functionality of toys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of toy you have interacted with (Action, Educational, or Toy).
2. Rate the toy's quality on a scale.
3. Provide any comments or feedback about the toy.
4. Enter your contact information, including name and email.
5. Add any additional notes you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Toy Category** (`toy_category`): Select the type of toy you have interacted with from the options provided (Action, Educational, or Toy).
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate the toy's quality on a scale.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any comments or feedback about the toy. Please keep your comments concise and to the point.
* **Category Rating** (`category_rating`): This field is not currently being used, and you can ignore it.
* **Contact Information** (`contact`): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes you would like to share about the toy.
