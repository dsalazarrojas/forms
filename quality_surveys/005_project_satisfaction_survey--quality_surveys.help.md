# Project Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your project experience. Your responses will help us understand your perspective and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start.
2. Select the category that best describes your project experience (if applicable).
3. Answer the questions according to your thoughts and opinions.
4. Click on "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Project)** (`project_category`, optional): Select the category that best describes your project experience. This can be a project you are currently working on or one that is completed. 
* **What do you think about the project's objectives?** (`question_3`, optional): Share your thoughts on how the project's objectives align with your expectations and goals. 
* **How would you rate the communication between your team leader and you?** (`question_4`, optional): Rate the effectiveness of communication in your team on a scale of 1-10. 
* **Rate your job satisfaction** (`question_5`, optional): Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale of 1-10. 
* **How satisfied are you with your job overall?** (`question_6`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-10. 
* **What do you think about the company's resources?** (`question_7`, optional): Choose the best description of your experience with company resources (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor). 
* **Would you recommend this company to a friend?** (`question_8`, optional): Indicate whether you would recommend the company to a friend or colleague. 
* **Why didn't you like this experience?** (`question_9`, optional): Share any concerns or issues you faced during the project. 
* **Do you have any suggestions or comments?** (`question_10`, optional): Provide any suggestions or comments you have about the project or company.
