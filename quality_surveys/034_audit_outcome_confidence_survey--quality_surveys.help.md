### Audit Outcome Confidence Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to measure the confidence of stakeholders in the outcomes of an audit. It assesses various aspects of the audit process, including the clarity of findings, the professionalism of auditors, and the overall quality of the audit. The survey aims to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the audit in providing valuable insights and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up).
2. Select your relationship to the audit (e.g., Board Member, Senior Management, etc.).
3. Provide the date of the survey and the name of the audit.
4. Rate your understanding of the audit outcomes, the clarity of findings, and the confidence in findings.
5. Evaluate the appropriateness and actionability of recommendations.
6. Assess the adequacy of management's response and the likelihood of recommendations being implemented.
7. Predict the expected impact of the audit.
8. Rate the overall quality of the audit, the professionalism of auditors, and the effectiveness of communications.
9. Provide any concerns or questions about the audit outcomes.
10. Add any additional feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): Provide your name as a respondent.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Stakeholder Type** (`stakeholder_type`, select_one, Required): Select your relationship to the audit (e.g., Board Member, Senior Management, etc.).
* **Department** (`department`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, Required): Enter the date of this survey.
* **Audit Name** (`audit_name`, text, Required): Enter the name or reference of the audit.
* **Audit Completion Date** (`audit_completion_date`, date, Required): Enter the date when the audit was completed.
* **Outcome Understanding** (`outcome_understanding`, select_one, Required): Rate how well you understand the audit outcomes (e.g., Very well, Well, etc.).
* **Findings Clarity** (`findings_clarity`, select_one, Required): Rate the clarity of the audit findings (e.g., Very clear, Clear, etc.).
* **Confidence in Findings** (`confidence_in_findings`, select_one, Required): Rate your confidence in the audit findings (e.g., Very confident, Confident, etc.).
* **Recommendations Appropriateness** (`recommendations_appropriateness`, select_one, Required): Rate the appropriateness of recommendations (e.g., Very appropriate, Appropriate, etc.).
* **Actionability of Recommendations** (`actionability_of_recommendations`, select_one, Required): Rate the actionability of recommendations (e.g., Very actionable, Actionable, etc.).
* **Management Response Adequacy** (`management_response_adequacy`, select_one, Required): Rate the adequacy of management's response (e.g., Very adequate, Adequate, etc.).
* **Implementation Likelihood** (`implementation_likelihood`, select_one, Required): Rate the likelihood of recommendations being implemented (e.g., Very likely, Likely, etc.).
* **Expected Impact** (`expected_impact`, select_one, Required): Predict the expected impact of the audit (e.g., Significant positive impact, Moderate positive impact, etc.).
* **Audit Quality Rating** (`audit_quality_rating`, select_one, Required): Rate the overall quality of the audit (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, etc.).
* **Auditor Professionalism** (`auditor_professionalism`, select_one, Required): Rate the professionalism of auditors (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, etc.).
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, Required): Rate the effectiveness of audit communications (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, etc.).
* **Concerns or Questions** (`concerns_or_questions`, text, Optional): Enter any concerns or questions about the audit outcomes.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, Optional): Enter any additional feedback.
* **Overall Confidence** (`overall_confidence`, select_one, Required): Rate your overall confidence in audit outcomes (e.g., Very confident, Confident, etc.).
