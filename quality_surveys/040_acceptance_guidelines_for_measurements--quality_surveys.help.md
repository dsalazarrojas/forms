# Acceptance Guidelines For Measurements - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide employees in their acceptance and acknowledgment of measurement procedures and criteria in their daily work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and employee ID number in the relevant fields.
2. Select your department from the provided options.
3. Enter the date of your training or review on the measurement procedures.
4. Choose the type of measurement procedure you are reviewing (e.g. Dimensional measurements, Weight and mass, etc.).
5. List specific instruments or tools used in the measurement.
6. Select all the acceptance criteria that apply to your review (e.g. Tolerance limits, Calibration standards, etc.).
7. Indicate your level of confidence in your understanding of the measurement procedures.
8. If you have any questions or concerns, please provide them in the text field.
9. If this is not your first training, indicate how long ago you received the training.
10. Finally, confirm that you have reviewed and understand the acceptance guidelines for measurements applicable to your role and commit to following them consistently in your daily work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee full name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel card.
* **Email address (3)**: Enter your work email address as it appears in the company directory.
* **Employee ID number (4)**: Enter your unique ID number assigned to you by the company.
* **Department (5)**: Select the department where you work.
* **Supervisor name (6)**: Enter the name of your supervisor or the person who trained you on the measurement procedures.
* **Date of training or review (7)**: Enter the date you received the training or review on the measurement procedures.
* **-- Measurement Procedures Reviewed -- (8)**: This field is not required, but you can add any notes or comments about the measurement procedures reviewed.
* **Type of measurement procedure reviewed (9)**: Select the type of measurement procedure you are reviewing (e.g. Dimensional measurements, Weight and mass, etc.).
* **Measurement equipment covered (10)**: List specific instruments or tools used in the measurement.
* **Which acceptance criteria were reviewed (11)**: Select all the acceptance criteria that apply to your review (e.g. Tolerance limits, Calibration standards, etc.).
* **How confident are you in your understanding (12)**: Indicate your level of confidence in your understanding of the measurement procedures.
* **Do you have any questions or concerns (13)**: If you have any questions or concerns, please provide them in this text field.
* **Have you received training on these procedures before (14)**: If you have received training on these procedures before, indicate how long ago you received the training.
* **I acknowledge that I have reviewed and understand the acceptance guidelines for measurements applicable to my role (15)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the acceptance guidelines for measurements applicable to your role.
* **I commit to following these guidelines consistently in my daily work (16)**: Confirm that you commit to following the acceptance guidelines for measurements consistently in your daily work.
* **Date of acknowledgment (17)**: Enter the date you confirmed that you have reviewed and understand the acceptance guidelines for measurements applicable to your role.
