# Child Marriage Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a survey on awareness and attitudes towards child marriage. It is designed to gather information from individuals about their understanding of the issue, its causes and consequences, and potential solutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions based on your genuine understanding and experiences.
2. Select only one option for each question.
3. If you are unsure or do not know the answer, please select the corresponding option.
4. Please be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`): This is the date you are completing this form.
* **Respondent Age** (`respondent_age`): Please enter your age in years.
* **Respondent Gender** (`respondent_gender`): Select your gender identity.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`): Please select your highest level of education completed.
* **Geographic Location** (`geographic_location`): Select the type of area you live in.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`): Please select your current marital status.
* **Do You Have Children** (`has_children`): Do you have any children?
* **Awareness of Child Marriage** (`heard_of_child_marriage`): Please select your level of awareness of child marriage.
* **Perceived Prevalence** (`child_marriage_prevalence`): Select how common you think child marriage is in your community.
* **Knowledge of Legal Marriage Age** (`child_marriage_legal_age`): Do you know the legal minimum age for marriage in your country?
* **Perceived Causes of Child Marriage** (`child_marriage_causes`): Select all the reasons you think contribute to child marriage.
* **Perceived Consequences of Child Marriage** (`child_marriage_consequences`): Select all the consequences you think result from child marriage.
* **Support for Ending Child Marriage** (`support_ending_child_marriage`): Please select your level of support for efforts to end child marriage.
* **Most Effective Interventions** (`effective_interventions`): Select all the interventions you think would be most effective in reducing child marriage.
* **Personal Knowledge of Affected Individuals** (`know_affected_individuals`): Do you personally know anyone who was married as a child?
* **Willingness to Advocate** (`willing_to_advocate`): Would you be willing to advocate against child marriage?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Please add any additional thoughts or comments on child marriage.
