# Facility Cleaning Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Cleaning Quality Survey is designed to assess the cleanliness standards and maintenance of a facility. This survey helps identify areas for improvement and ensures that the facility meets the required cleanliness standards. The survey is used to collect feedback from users about the cleanliness of the facility, including the frequency and quality of cleaning, and the overall rating of the facility's cleanliness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Rate the cleanliness of the facility based on your experience.
3. Provide specific comments or feedback about any areas that need improvement.
4. Select the frequency with which the cleaning staff visits the facility and the facility inspector visits.
5. Rate the quality of the facility's cleanliness and the frequency of the facility inspections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility**: Enter the name of the facility that you are rating.
* **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the overall cleanliness of the area on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Comments on Cleanliness**: Provide specific comments or feedback about the cleanliness of the area.
* **Cleaning Frequency**: Select the frequency with which you think the cleaning staff cleans the area:
	+ **Daily**
	+ **Weekly**
	+ **Bi-Weekly**
	+ **Monthly**
	+ **Rarely**
* **Cleanliness Quality**: Rate the quality of the cleaning on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Cleanliness Standards**: Select the cleanliness standards in the area:
	+ **High**
	+ **Medium**
	+ **Low**
* **Cleanliness Issues**: Select any issues that you have noticed with the cleanliness of the area:
	+ **Dirty floors**
	+ **Dirty restrooms**
	+ **Dirty trash**
	+ **Dirty break room**
	+ **Other**
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the facility's cleanliness.
* **Cleanliness Rating Score**: This field will automatically calculate a cleanliness rating based on your input.
* **Facility Inspector**: Check if the facility has an inspector.
* **Facility Inspector Frequency**: Select the frequency with which the facility inspector visits:
	+ **Daily**
	+ **Weekly**
	+ **Bi-Weekly**
	+ **Monthly**
	+ **Rarely**
* **Facility Inspector Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback about the facility inspector.
* **Cleanliness Inspection**: Enter the date of the last cleanliness inspection.
* **Facility Inspector**: Enter the name of the facility inspector.
