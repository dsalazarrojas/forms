# Building Compliance Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Compliance Efficiency Survey is designed to assess the efficiency of compliance processes within a building or organization. This survey helps identify areas of improvement, bottlenecks, and challenges in the compliance workflow, communication, and tools and systems used.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Respondents are asked to provide their personal information, including their name, title, and department or area of responsibility.
2. They will be asked about their years of compliance experience.
3. Respondents will then assess the efficiency of various aspects of the compliance process, including overall efficiency, clarity of process documentation, workflow standardization, and communication flow between teams.
4. They will also be asked about the tools and systems used for compliance, the effectiveness of those tools, and the integration between systems.
5. To identify bottlenecks and challenges, respondents will select main workflow bottlenecks and provide information on the most time-consuming activities and frequency of compliance errors.
6. Finally, respondents will provide their top priorities for improvement, opportunities for automation, training needs for efficiency, resources needed for improvement, and capacity to implement improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (`name`, `text`, **required**: true):
    * Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Your name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, **required**: true):
    * Enter your name as it is currently displayed on your official documents.
* **Your title or role** (`role_title`, `text`, **required**: true):
    * Enter your title or role within the organization, such as Compliance Officer or Building Manager.
* **Department or area of responsibility** (`department_area`, `text`, **required**: true):
    * Enter the department or area where you work, such as Operations or Facilities.
* **Years of compliance experience** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Select the number of years you have been working in compliance.
    * Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **Overall efficiency of compliance processes** (`overall_efficiency`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Assess the overall efficiency of the compliance process.
    * Very efficient, Efficient, Neutral, Inefficient, or Very inefficient.
* **Clarity of process documentation** (`process_documentation`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Assess the clarity of process documentation.
    * Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, or No documentation.
* **Workflow standardization across building** (`workflow_standardization`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Assess the standardization of workflow across the building.
    * Fully standardized, Mostly standardized, Partially standardized, Minimally standardized, or Not standardized.
* **Communication flow between teams** (`communication_flow`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Assess the communication flow between teams.
    * Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Tools and systems used for compliance** (`tools_used`, `select_multiple`, **required**: true):
    * Select the tools and systems you use for compliance, such as Spreadsheets, Specialized compliance software, Building management system, Document management, Email, Manual processes, Custom database, or Other.
* **Effectiveness of current tools** (`tool_effectiveness`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Assess the effectiveness of the current tools.
    * Very effective, Effective, Somewhat effective, Not very effective, or Not effective.
* **Integration between systems** (`system_integration`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Assess the integration between systems.
    * Fully integrated, Mostly integrated, Partially integrated, Minimal integration, or No integration.
* **Accessibility of compliance data** (`data_accessibility`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Assess the accessibility of compliance data.
    * Very accessible, Accessible, Somewhat accessible, Difficult to access, or Not accessible.
* **Main workflow bottlenecks** (`main_bottlenecks`, `select_multiple`, **required**: true):
    * Identify the main workflow bottlenecks.
    * Select multiple options, such as Data entry and manual work, System limitations, Communication delays, Approval processes, Document organization, Resource constraints, Training gaps, Unclear procedures, System integration issues, or Other.
* **Most time-consuming activities** (`time_consumption`, `text`, **required**: true):
    * Describe the most time-consuming activities in compliance.
* **Frequency of compliance errors** (`error_frequency`, `select_one`, **required**: true):
    * Assess the frequency of compliance errors.
    * Rarely - Less than once per month, Occasionally - Monthly, Frequently - Weekly, Very frequently - Daily.
* **Primary causes of errors** (`error_causes`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false):
    * Identify the primary causes of errors.
    * Select multiple options, such as Manual entry mistakes, Unclear procedures, Miscommunication, System limitations, Training gaps, Outdated information, or Resource limitations.
* **Top improvement priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, `select_multiple`, **required**: true):
    * Prioritize the top improvements.
    * Select multiple options, such as Automation of processes, Better system integration, Improved documentation, Staff training, New tools/software, Process redesign, Better communication, or Real-time dashboards.
* **Opportunities for automation** (`automation_opportunities`, `text`, **required**: false):
    * Describe opportunities for automation.
* **Training needs for efficiency** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false):
    * Identify the training needs for efficiency.
    * Select multiple options, such as Tool training, Process training, System updates, Best practices, New software, Compliance updates, or Other.
* **Resources needed for improvement** (`resource_requirements`, `text`, **required**: false):
    * Describe the resources needed for improvement.
* **Capacity to implement improvements** (`implementation_capacity`, `select_one`, **required**: false):
    * Assess your capacity to implement improvements.
    * High capacity, Moderate capacity, Limited capacity, or Very limited capacity.
* **Additional feedback or suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, **required**: false):
    * Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and based on your experience.
* If you have any further feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the text field at the end.
* If you need more information or clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

This guide should provide clear and concrete explanations to help users complete the form accurately. Note that some fields may require multiple responses.
