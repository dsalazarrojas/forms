# Life Cycle Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Cycle Assessment Survey is a structured form designed to gather detailed information about a project, product, or process's life cycle. This survey is intended to help teams and stakeholders track and analyze the evolution of the life cycle, identify key events, and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully and fill out the corresponding sections with the requested information.
2. Use the available options for fields with dropdown menus (e.g., Company, Product, Process).
3. Fill out text fields with the most accurate and concise information available.
4. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: A brief description of the life cycle, setting the context for the survey.
* **Company (2)**: Select the company involved in the life cycle (e.g., A, B, C).
* **Product/Process (3)**: Select all relevant products or processes involved in the life cycle (e.g., A, B, C). 
* **Process (4)**: A short description of the process involved in the life cycle.
* **Material (5)**: A description of the material used in the life cycle (e.g., materials, resources, etc.).
* **Location (6)**: The location where the life cycle occurred (e.g., city, country, region).
* **Start Date (7)**: The start date of the life cycle (format: month/day/year).
* **Start Time (8)**: The start time of the life cycle (format: 12-hour clock).
* **End Date (9)**: The end date of the life cycle (format: month/day/year).
* **End Time (10)**: The end time of the life cycle (format: 12-hour clock).
* **Hours (11)**: The total hours spent in the life cycle.
* **Minutes (12)**: The total minutes spent in the life cycle.
* **Total Cost (13)**: The total cost associated with the life cycle (format: numerical value).

Note: All dates and times should be filled out in the format specified, and hours, minutes, and total cost values should be entered in the required format. If unsure, refer back to this guide for clarification. 

**Tips**

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the survey.
* Double-check the fields for accuracy and relevance.
* If you need further assistance, contact your supervisor or survey administrator. 

Note: I've assumed that the "options" fields are for select_one and select_multiple, hence the explanations for "Company" and "Product/Process" are provided accordingly. If the "options" fields are meant for other types of input (e.g., text), please clarify and I will adjust the explanations. 

If you need further assistance, feel free to ask.
