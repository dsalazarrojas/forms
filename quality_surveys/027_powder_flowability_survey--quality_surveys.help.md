# Powder Flowability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Powder Flowability Survey is used to collect data on the flowability of a powder, including its flowability test, flowability scale, and handling properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information for the powder being surveyed.
2. Answer the flowability test and flowability scale questions with the appropriate numbers or select the relevant options.
3. Provide details about the handling properties of the powder in the text fields.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Flowability Test** (`flowability_test`): This field is used to collect a description of the flowability test performed on the powder.
* **Flowability Scale** (`flowability_scale`): This field asks you to rate the flowability of the powder on a scale of 1-5.
* **Flowability Value Range** (`flowability_value_range`): This field allows you to enter a numerical range that describes the powder's flowability.
* **Handling** (`handling`): This field is used to collect details about the powder's handling properties, such as how it flows or settles.
* **Flowability Score** (`flowability_score`): This field asks you to rate the flowability of the powder on a scale of 1-5.
* **Flowability Scale 2** (`flowability_scale_2`): This field is similar to the flowability scale, but is used to collect additional information about the powder's flowability.
* **Handling 2** (`handling_2`): This field is used to collect details about the powder's handling properties, such as how it flows or settles.
* **Flowability Score 2** (`flowability_score_2`): This field asks you to rate the flowability of the powder on a scale of 1-5.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `notes_2`, `notes_3`): These fields are used to collect additional comments or notes about the powder's flowability.
* **Handling 3-13** (`handling_3`-`handling_13`): These fields are used to collect details about the powder's handling properties, such as how it flows or settles.
* **Flowability Test 2-3** (`flowability_test_2`, `flowability_test_3`): These fields are used to collect a description of the flowability test performed on the powder.
* **Flowability Score 3** (`flowability_score_3`): This field asks you to rate the flowability of the powder on a scale of 1-5.

Please note that the fields marked as required must be filled out. The other fields are optional and can be filled out if additional information is available.
