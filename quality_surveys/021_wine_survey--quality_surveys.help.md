# Wine Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on wine consumption habits and preferences. It helps us understand whether individuals purchase wine, the frequency of wine consumption, and the quality of wine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on the main page, which asks about your wine purchase and consumption habits.
2. Select your responses to each question carefully, as they will help us understand your preferences and habits.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, which are marked as "required".
4. Once you have completed all the questions, review your responses for accuracy.
