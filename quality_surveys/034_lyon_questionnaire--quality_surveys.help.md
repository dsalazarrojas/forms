# lyon_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lyon Questionnaire is a form designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about their experience with Lyon. This form helps us understand your satisfaction, rating, and any other feedback or comments you might have. This information will be used to improve the quality of our services and provide better experiences for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them according to your experience with Lyon.
2. Make sure to answer all questions completely and honestly.
3. If you have any feedback or comments, please use the "any_other_feedback" field.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (1) (`introduction`, `note`): This field is a brief description of the introduction page.
* **How would you rate Lyon?** (2) (`how_would_you_rate_lyon`, `number`): Please rate your overall satisfaction with Lyon on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **How satisfied are you with your visit to Lyon?** (3) (`how_satisfied_with_your_visit_to_lyon`, `number`): Rate your satisfaction with your visit to Lyon on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Would you recommend Lyon to a friend?** (4) (`recommend_lyon_to_a_friend`, `select_one`): Choose whether you would recommend Lyon to a friend or not.
* **Email** (5) (`email`, `email`): Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for future surveys or feedback.
* **Your contact information** (6) (`your_contact_info`, `text`): Enter your contact information if you would like us to contact you for any further discussion or feedback.
* **Any other feedback?** (7) (`any_other_feedback`, `text`): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you might have.
* **Date** (8) (`date`, `date`): This field is not currently used in the form, please do not worry about it.
* **Time** (9) (`time`, `time`): This field is not currently used in the form, please do not worry about it.
* **Phone** (10) (`phone`, `text`): This field is not currently used in the form, please do not worry about it.
* **Email** (11) (`email_2`, `email`): This field is a duplicate of the email field above, please do not worry about it.
* **Contact information** (12) (`contact_info_2`, `text`): This field is a duplicate of the contact information field above, please do not worry about it.
* **Feedback** (13) (`feedback_2`, `text`): This field is a duplicate of the feedback field above, please do not worry about it.
* **How would you rate Lyon?** (14) (`rating_2`, `number`): This field is a duplicate of the rating field above, please do not worry about it.
* **How satisfied are you with your visit to Lyon?** (15) (`satisfaction_2`, `number`): This field is a duplicate of the satisfaction field above, please do not worry about it.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please feel free to contact us.
