# Health Communication Equity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your experiences with health communication and equity issues. It's used for research or analysis purposes to identify areas where health communication can be improved or to develop targeted interventions to address health inequities.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read the form carefully and understand each question before answering.
- Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
- Complete all required sections before submitting the form.
- For questions with multiple choice options, select all that apply.
- For questions with single choice options, select only one option.
