# Building Energy Transition Readiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the readiness of a building for energy transition, which involves transitioning from traditional energy sources to more sustainable and efficient ones. This survey aims to gather information about the building's current energy systems, its energy-saving potential, and its feasibility for renewable energy adoption. The goal is to identify areas of improvement and opportunities for energy efficiency and savings.

## How To Complete This Form

* Start by filling in the "Survey Date" field with the date this assessment is conducted.
* Fill in the "Property Name" field with the name of the building.
* Enter the "Full Address" field with the full address of the building.
* If available, fill in the "Total Square Footage" field with the building's total square footage.
* Answer the questions about the current heating and cooling systems, building insulation, window conditions, and air sealing needs to provide insight into the building's current energy efficiency.
* Indicate your interest in renewable energy and the approximate budget available for energy transition projects.
* Select the preferred timeline for implementing energy transition changes.
* If applicable, list your key concerns about the energy transition process.
* Finally, add any additional notes or questions relevant to the energy transition assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date (1)** (`survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date this assessment is conducted.
* **Property Name (2)** (`property_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the building.
* **Property Address (3)** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the building.
* **Total Square Footage (4)** (`total_square_feet`, number, optional): Enter the building's total square footage.
* **Building Age (5)** (`building_age`, number, optional): Enter the years since construction.
* **Current Heating System (6)** (`current_heating_system`, select_one, required): Select the type of heating system currently in use (Natural Gas Furnace, Electric Heat, Oil Heat, Geothermal, Unknown).
* **Current Heating System Efficiency (7)** (`heating_efficiency`, select_one, required): Indicate how well the current heating system is working (High Efficiency, Good, Fair, Poor, Unknown).
* **Current Cooling System (8)** (`cooling_system`, select_one, required): Select the type of cooling system currently in use (Central AC, Heat Pump, Window Units, None, Unknown).
* **Building Insulation Status (9)** (`insulation_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the current state of the building's insulation (Well Insulated, Aduquately Insulated, Poor Insulation, Unknown).
* **Window Condition (10)** (`window_condition`, select_one, required): Select the condition of the windows (Modern Double/Triple Pane, Older Double Pane, Single Pane, Mixed).
* **Air Sealing and Weatherization Needs (11)** (`air_sealing_needs`, select_one, required): Select the current status of air sealing and weatherization (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Interest in Renewable Energy (12)** (`renewable_energy_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate your interest in renewable energy (Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, Not Sure, Not Interested).
* **Building Feasibility for Renewables (13)** (`renewable_feasibility`, select_one, optional): Select the feasibility of renewables for the building (Highly Feasible, Feasible, Limited Feasibility, Not Feasible).
* **Approximate Budget Available (14)** (`budget_available`, select_one, optional): Enter the approximate budget available for energy transition projects (Under 50000, 50000 - 100000, 100000 - 250000, 250000 - 500000, Over 500000, TBD).
* **Preferred Timeline for Transition (15)** (`timeline_preference`, select_one, required): Select the preferred timeline for implementing energy transition changes (Immediate, Within 1 Year, 1-3 Years, 3-5 Years, No Specific Timeline).
* **Key Concerns About Energy Transition (16)** (`key_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): List the key concerns about energy transition (Cost, Disruption, Technology, Funding, ROI).
* **Additional Notes or Questions (17)** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or questions relevant to the energy transition assessment.
