# Clean Water Project Impact Study - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to measure the impact of a clean water project on the community, particularly in terms of water access, health, and economic benefits. It aims to gather data on changes in water sources, time spent on water collection, waterborne illnesses, and overall satisfaction with the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide information about your project, including its name, ID number, location, and completion date.
2. Answer questions about your household demographics, such as household size, number of children under 18, and primary occupation.
3. Describe your main water source before and after the project, as well as your travel distance and time spent collecting water.
4. Report on waterborne illnesses and common illnesses experienced by your family before and after the project.
5. Assess your overall health improvement and change in medical expenses since the project.
6. Answer questions about your satisfaction with the water quality and maintenance of the project.
7. Evaluate the impact of the project on your time, income, and children's school attendance.
8. Describe any additional benefits or challenges you have experienced since the project.
9. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for future projects.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name (3)**: Please enter the name of the clean water project.
* **Project ID Number (4)**: Enter the unique project identifier assigned to the project.
* **Project Location (4)**: Type the name of the village, town, or community where the project is located.
* **District or Region (5)**: Enter the administrative district where the project is located.
* **Country (6)**: Type the country where the project is located.
* **Project Completion Date (7)**: Enter the completion date of the project.
* **Survey Date (8)**: Type the date of this impact survey.
* **Surveyor Name (9)**: Enter the name of the person conducting the survey.
* **Implementing Organization (10)**: Type the name of the organization that implemented the project.
* **Household Size (12)**: Choose your household size from the given options.
* **Number of Children Under 18 (13)**: Select the number of children under 18 in your household.
* **Primary Occupation (14)**: Choose your primary source of livelihood from the given options.
* **Main Water Source Before Project (16)**: Select where you got your water from before the project.
* **Distance to Water Source Before (17)**: Choose how far you traveled for water before the project.
* **Time Spent Collecting Water Before (18)**: Select how long it took to collect water before the project.
* **Main Water Source After Project (19)**: Select your main water source after the project.
* **Distance to Water Source Now (20)**: Choose how far you travel for water now.
* **Time Spent Collecting Water Now (21)**: Select how long it takes to collect water now.
* **Water Availability (22)**: Choose how available clean water is year-round.
* **Waterborne Illnesses Before Project (24)**: Select how often your family members got sick from water before the project.
* **Waterborne Illnesses After Project (25)**: Select how often your family members get sick from water now.
* **Common Water-Related Illnesses Experienced (26)**: Choose all the illnesses that applied to your family before the project.
* **Overall Health Improvement (27)**: Select how much your family's health has improved since the project.
* **Change in Medical Expenses (28)**: Choose how medical expenses have changed since the project.
* **Water Taste (30)**: Select how you rate the taste of the water.
* **Water Clarity (31)**: Choose how clear the water is.
* **Water Odor (32)**: Select whether the water has any odor.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Project (33)**: Choose how satisfied you are with the project.
* **Project Maintenance and Sustainability (34)**: Select how well the project is maintained.
* **Community Training Received (35)**: Choose whether you received any training on the project.
* **How Is Saved Time Now Used (37)**: Select all the activities you do with the time saved from water collection.
* **Impact on Household Income (38)**: Choose how the project has affected your income.
* **Impact on Children School Attendance (39)**: Select how school attendance has changed.
* **Impact on Women and Girls (40)**: Type how the project has affected women and girls specifically.
* **Biggest Benefit from Project (42)**: Type the most important benefit you have experienced from the project.
* **Challenges Remaining (43)**: Type any remaining water-related challenges you face.
* **Recommendations for Future Projects (44)**: Type any suggestions for future projects.
* **Would You Recommend This Type of Project (45)**: Select whether you would recommend this type of project to other communities.
* **Additional Comments (46)**: Provide any additional feedback or stories you have.
