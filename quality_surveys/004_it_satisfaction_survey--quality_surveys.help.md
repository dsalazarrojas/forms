# IT Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather information about your experience and perceptions regarding IT services. Your feedback will help us understand how we're doing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide your valuable input, simply go through the following steps:

* Start by selecting the first page.
* Answer all the questions on each page according to your honest thoughts and feelings.
* You can save your progress and return later if needed.
* Once you've finished, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (text)** (`title`, not required):

    Describe the first page of the IT Satisfaction Survey. 
* **Page 2 (page_2, not required)**:
    This is the second page of the survey.
* **Page 3 (page_3, not required)**:

* **Page 4 (page_4, not required)**:

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any specific examples or anecdotes, please feel free to share them.
* If you've already completed this survey, do not submit multiple responses.
* If you have access to IT services performance metrics, do not complete this form.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* We appreciate your willingness to share your thoughts and suggestions, which will help us improve our IT services.
