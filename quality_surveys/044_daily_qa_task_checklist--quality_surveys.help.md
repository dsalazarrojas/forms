# daily_qa_task_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily QA task checklist is a critical tool for quality assurance teams to document and track daily tasks, issues, and follow-ups. This form helps teams stay organized and ensures that all tasks are accurately reported and tracked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill out the required fields. Start by selecting the correct department from the drop-down menu. Then, select the tasks you are responsible for completing by checking the boxes. Enter the date and time of submission, and any relevant issue details. Finally, provide an email and phone number for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the drop-down menu. This will help the team track which department is responsible for which tasks.
	+ Example: QA Department 1
* **Task 1 (task_1)**: Select all tasks you are responsible for completing by checking the boxes. This is a critical field that ensures tasks are accurately reported.
	+ Example: Task 1a, Task 1b
* **Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form. This will help track when tasks were completed.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Time**: Enter the time you submitted the form. This will help track when tasks were completed.
	+ Format: HH:MM AM/PM
* **Issue**: If you have any issues or concerns, type them in this field. This will help the team track any problems that arise.
	+ Example: "Error in task 1a"
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication. This will ensure the team can reach out to you for any necessary updates.
	+ Format: user@example.com
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication. This will ensure the team can reach out to you for any urgent updates.
	+ Format: XXX-XXX-XXXX
* **Submission**: Enter a brief description of your submission. This will help the team understand what was submitted.
	+ Example: "Completed task 1a"
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form. This will help track when submissions were made.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Review Date**: Enter the date you reviewed the form. This will help track when reviews were made.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Issue Status**: Select the status of the issue by choosing from the drop-down menu. This will help track the progress of issues.
	+ Example: Not Done
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date you followed up on the issue. This will help track the progress of issues.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Follow-up By**: Enter the name and date of who followed up on the issue. This will help track who followed up on issues.
	+ Example: "John Doe, 02/20/2024"
