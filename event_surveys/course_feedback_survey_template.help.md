# course_feedback_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from course attendees to help organizers improve future course offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for the "Which sessions did you find most valuable?" question.
3. Enter your honest opinion for the "What was the most impactful takeaway from the course?" question.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied were you with the course overall?**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale from 1 (Very dissatisfied) to 5 (Very satisfied).
* **Which sessions did you find most valuable?**: Select all the sessions that you found most valuable or interesting. These can be:
	+ Introduction
	+ Core Concepts
	+ Practical Workshop
	+ Advanced Topics
	+ Q&A Panel
* **Was the course event well organized?**: Answer this question with one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Somewhat
* **How likely are you to attend a similar course again?**: Rate your likelihood of attending a similar course again on a scale from 1 (Not likely) to 4 (Very likely).
* **What was the most impactful takeaway from the course?**: Provide a brief description of the most impactful thing you took away from the course.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?**: Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have about the course event.
