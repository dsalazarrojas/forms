# event_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on a recent event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the question about your overall satisfaction with the event.
2.  Choose the event aspects or sessions that you found most valuable.
3.  Indicate whether the event was well-organized and managed.
4.  Express your likelihood of attending a similar event in the future.
5.  Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How satisfied were you with the event overall?
*   **Very satisfied/ Somewhat satisfied/ Neutral/ Somewhat dissatisfied/ Very dissatisfied**: This question helps us understand your general impression of the event. Choose the option that best reflects your level of satisfaction.
### Which event aspects or sessions did you find most valuable?
*   **Keynote speeches, Breakout sessions, Networking opportunities, Workshops, Exhibitor booths, Q&A panels**: Select all that apply, as this question is designed to help us identify the most engaging and useful aspects of the event.
### Was the event well-organized and managed?
*   **true/ false**: This question helps us assess the event's logistics and organization.
### How likely are you to attend a similar event in the future?
*   **Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely**: Choose the option that best reflects your likelihood of attending a similar event.
### Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for improvement?
*   **Text field**: Share your thoughts and ideas on how we can improve future events.
