# post_event_feedback_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from event attendees to help event organizers improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your true feelings and experiences about the event.
2. Choose your responses based on the options provided for each question.
3. Fill in any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How satisfied were you with the event overall?
* **Very satisfied** (`Very satisfied`, single, required): This option indicates that you were extremely satisfied with the event.
* **Somewhat satisfied** (`Somewhat satisfied`, single, required): This option indicates that you were somewhat satisfied with the event.
* **Neutral** (`Neutral`, single, required): This option indicates that you were neither satisfied nor dissatisfied with the event.
* **Somewhat dissatisfied** (`Somewhat dissatisfied`, single, required): This option indicates that you were somewhat dissatisfied with the event.
* **Very dissatisfied** (`Very dissatisfied`, single, required): This option indicates that you were extremely dissatisfied with the event.

### Which sessions or presentations did you find most valuable?
* **Keynote Speech** (`Keynote Speech`, multiple, optional): This option indicates that the keynote speech was the most valuable session for you.
* **Panel Discussion** (`Panel Discussion`, multiple, optional): This option indicates that the panel discussion was the most valuable session for you.
* **Workshop A** (`Workshop A`, multiple, optional): This option indicates that workshop A was the most valuable session for you.
* **Networking Session** (`Networking Session`, multiple, optional): This option indicates that the networking session was the most valuable session for you.
* **Product Demo** (`Product Demo`, multiple, optional): This option indicates that the product demo was the most valuable session for you.

### Was the event well organized and managed?
* **Yes** (`true`, single, required): This option indicates that the event was well organized and managed.
* **No** (`false`, single, required): This option indicates that the event was not well organized and managed.

### How likely are you to attend a similar event in the future?
* **Very likely** (`Very likely`, single, required): This option indicates that you are extremely likely to attend a similar event in the future.
* **Somewhat likely** (`Somewhat likely`, single, required): This option indicates that you are somewhat likely to attend a similar event in the future.
* **Not likely** (`Not likely`, single, required): This option indicates that you are not likely to attend a similar event in the future.

### Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for us?
* **Text field** (`text`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
