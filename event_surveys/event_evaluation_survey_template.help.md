# event_evaluation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event evaluation survey template is designed to gather feedback from event attendees to help improve future events. It aims to assess overall satisfaction, identify valuable sessions, evaluate event organization, and determine the likelihood of future attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply answer the questions to the best of your ability, and feel free to add any additional comments or feedback in the text field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`How satisfied were you with the event overall?`): Please select one of the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied, to express your level of satisfaction with the event.
* **Valuable Sessions/Aspects** (`Which sessions or aspects did you find most valuable?`): Share your thoughts on the sessions or aspects that resonated with you the most. You can type in as much or as little as you'd like.
* **Organization** (`Was the event well organized?`): Choose one of the options: True, False, to indicate whether you felt the event was well-organized or not.
* **Future Attendance** (`How likely are you to attend a similar event in the future?`): Select one of the options: Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not likely, to express your likelihood of attending a similar event in the future.
* **Additional Comments/Feedback** (`Do you have any additional comments or feedback?`): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you might have. This is an open-ended field where you can type in as much or as little as you'd like.
