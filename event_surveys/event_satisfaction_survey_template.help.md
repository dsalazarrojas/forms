# event_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from event attendees on their experience at our recent event. This feedback will help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Overall, how satisfied were you with the event?" by selecting one of the five options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
2. Rate the quality of the presentations and speakers by selecting one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
3. Identify the most valuable aspects of the event by selecting all that apply from the options: Presentations, Networking opportunities, Workshops/Breakout sessions, Venue/Logistics, or Information provided.
4. Answer "Did you feel the event was engaging and informative?" by selecting one of the three options: true, false, or Somewhat.
5. Provide any suggestions on what could be improved for future events.
6. Offer any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how satisfied were you with the event?** (`satisfied`, `single`, required): This question asks about your overall experience at the event. Select the option that best describes how you felt.
* **How would you rate the quality of the presentations and speakers?** (`rate`, `single`, required): This question asks about the quality of the presentations and speakers at the event. Select the option that best describes your opinion.
* **Which aspects of the event did you find most valuable?** (`value`, `multiple`, optional): This question asks you to select all that apply from the options provided. Think about what made the event valuable to you.
* **Did you feel the event was engaging and informative?** (`informative`, `single`, required): This question asks about your overall opinion on the event's engagement and information quality. Select one of the three options: true, false, or Somewhat.
* **What could be improved for future events?** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): This field is for any suggestions or ideas you have to improve future events. Be specific and provide constructive feedback.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have. Feel free to share as much or as little as you like.

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere in your answers to provide valuable feedback.
- Select all that apply for the "Which aspects of the event did you find most valuable?" question.
- Use clear and concise language in your answers to provide the most helpful feedback.
