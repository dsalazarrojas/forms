# pre_event_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre_event_survey_template form is designed to collect feedback and expectations from event attendees before the event takes place.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select your satisfaction with event information**: Choose your level of satisfaction from the options:
	* Very satisfied
	* Somewhat satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Somewhat dissatisfied
	* Very dissatisfied
2. **Choose your favorite sessions**: Select all the sessions or topics that you are looking forward to:
	* Keynote speeches
	* Interactive workshops
	* Networking sessions
	* Panel discussions
	* Product demos
3. **Assess the registration process**: Choose how easy you found the event registration process:
	* Very easy
	* Easy
	* Neutral
	* Difficult
	* Very difficult
4. **Identify your primary goal**: Choose your primary goal for attending the event:
	* Networking
	* Learning new skills
	* Gaining industry insights
	* Discovering new products
	* Career development
5. **Provide any questions or concerns**: If you have any questions or concerns, type them in the text field.
6. **Additional information**: If there's any other information you'd like to share with us prior to the event, please type it in this text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with the event information?**: This question gauges your level of satisfaction with the event information provided, helping us to refine the content and presentation.
* **Which sessions or topics are you looking forward to?**: Choose all the sessions or topics that interest you, as this helps us tailor the content and schedule.
* **How easy was the event registration process?**: This feedback helps us to identify areas for improvement in the registration process.
* **What is your primary goal for attending this event?**: Your primary goal will help us understand your expectations and tailor the content and activities accordingly.
* **Do you have any questions or concerns?**: If you have any questions or concerns, please provide them in this field.
* **What additional information would be helpful for you?**: If you think there's anything else we should consider, let us know in this field.

## Tips
* Please answer questions based on your actual experience and expectations.
* You can select more than one option in the "Choose your favorite sessions" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please keep them concise and clear.
* If you have any additional information to share, please be as specific as possible.
