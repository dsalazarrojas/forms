# event_feedback_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Feedback Form aims to gather your thoughts and opinions on your recent experience with an event. Your feedback will help us make informed decisions to enhance the event experience for you and other attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide your feedback, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How satisfied were you with the overall event experience?" by selecting one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
2. Choose all the aspects of the event that you found most valuable from the list: Keynote speeches, Interactive workshops, Networking opportunities, Panel discussions, Product demos/exhibits, or Social activities.
3. Answer the question "Was the event well-organized and easy to navigate?" by selecting one of the three options: true, false, or Partially.
4. Rate how likely you are to recommend this event to a colleague or friend by selecting one of the three options: Very likely, Somewhat likely, or Not likely.
5. Evaluate the event venue and facilities by selecting one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
6. Finally, add any additional comments or suggestions you have for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied were you with the overall event experience?** (`event_satisfaction`, single, required):
  Please select how satisfied you were with the overall event experience.
* **Which aspects of the event did you find most valuable?** (`event_aspects`, multiple, required):
  Choose all the aspects of the event that you found most valuable. These may include Keynote speeches, Interactive workshops, Networking opportunities, Panel discussions, Product demos/exhibits, or Social activities.
* **Was the event well-organized and easy to navigate?** (`event_organization`, single, required):
  Answer whether the event was well-organized and easy to navigate.
* **How likely are you to recommend this event to a colleague or friend?** (`recommendation`, single, required):
  Select how likely you are to recommend this event to a colleague or friend.
* **How would you rate the event venue and facilities?** (`venue_facilities`, single, required):
  Evaluate the event venue and facilities by selecting one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`comments`, text, optional):
  Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving future events.
