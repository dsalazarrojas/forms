# Braintree Online Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Braintree Online Donation Form is designed to collect donations from individuals for a specific cause or organization. This form is used to gather information about the donor, including their contact details, donation preferences, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information as requested.
2. Review and verify the accuracy of your details before submitting.
3. Select your desired donation amount and frequency.
4. Choose your preferred donation type and fund designation.
5. If donating in honor or memory, provide the honoree's name.
6. Check the boxes for notification and receipt delivery preferences.
7. Select your payment method and provide the required payment details.
8. If you have a campaign code, enter it.
9. Review and agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.
10. Sign the form with your full legal name.
