# Dispute Resolution Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors about their contributions to the Dispute Resolution Fund. The goal is to ensure that all donors receive a proper receipt of their donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the donor's name in the "Donor name" field.
2. Enter the donor's email address in the "Donor email" field, as this is where the receipt will be sent.
3. Enter the donation amount in the "Donation amount" field, using only numeric values.
4. Select the payment method from the options provided in the "Payment method" field.
5. If applicable, choose the allocation preference for the donation from the options in the "Allocation preference" field.
6. Decide whether to remain anonymous in the "Make donation anonymous" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual or organization making the donation.
* **Donor email** (`donor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address where the receipt for the donation will be sent.
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the donation using only numeric values.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method through which the donation will be made (Credit card, Bank transfer, Check).
* **Allocation preference** (`allocation_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose where the funds should be used within the Dispute Resolution Fund (Mediation services, Legal aid support, Community education, General fund). If left blank, the donation will go into the General fund.
* **Make donation anonymous** (`anonymous`, select_one, optional): Decide whether the donation should be publicly displayed or remain anonymous.
