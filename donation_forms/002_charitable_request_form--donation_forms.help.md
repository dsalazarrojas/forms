# Charitable Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from organizations requesting charitable donations from us.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including Organization Legal Name, Contact Person Name, Contact Email Address, Contact Phone Number, Tax ID (EIN), Type of Donation Requested, Requested Amount or Value ($), Purpose of the Donation, Event Date (if applicable), and Date of Request.
2. If you have any additional documentation to support your request, please provide a link to it under the "Link to Supporting Documentation" field.
3. If you would like to offer recognition or marketing benefits for the donation, select all applicable options under "Recognition or Marketing Offered".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Legal Name** (`organization_legal_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this request.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your organization's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your organization's contact phone number.
* **Tax ID (EIN)** (`tax_id_ein`, text, required): Enter your organization's Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN).
* **Type of Donation Requested** (`donation_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of donation you are requesting (Monetary, In-Kind Product, Services, or Gift Certificates).
* **Requested Amount or Value ($)** (`requested_amount_value`, number, required): Enter the amount or value of the donation you are requesting.
* **Purpose of the Donation** (`donation_purpose`, text, required): Explain how the donation will be used.
* **Event Date (if applicable)** (`event_date`, date, optional): If this donation is for a specific event, enter the date of the event.
* **Is this donation tax write-off eligible** (`tax_write_off_eligible`, select_one, required): Choose whether this donation is eligible for tax write-off.
* **Recognition or Marketing Offered** (`recognition_offered`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any recognition or marketing benefits you would like to offer for the donation (Logo on Website, Event Signage, Social Media Post, Newsletter Mention, or None).
* **Link to Supporting Documentation** (`additional_documentation`, text, optional): Provide a link to any additional documentation that supports your request.
* **Date of Request** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
