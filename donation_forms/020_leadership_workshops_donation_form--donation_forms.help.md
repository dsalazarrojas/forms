# Leadership Workshops Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from customers for leadership workshops. It is a secure and simple way to contribute to the organization's efforts in providing valuable training and resources to individuals and groups.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read and carefully follow each step below to ensure accurate completion of your donation.
- Review the form fields to understand their purpose.
- Enter your valid email address.
- Select your preferred donation amount.
- Specify the purpose of your donation by choosing one or multiple options.
- Provide any additional information you would like to be considered.
- Click the submit button to finalize your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Leadership Workshops Donation Form**: This is the title of the form.
- **Unique ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your donation record. If left blank, a unique ID will be generated.
- **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for correspondence. This must be your actual email address and not a generic contact email.
- **Phone Number**: This field is not required but can be helpful for contact.
- **Donation Amount**: Specify the amount you would like to donate. This is a numerical value.
- **Purpose**: Choose the purpose of your donation from the available options.
- **Additional Information**: Add any additional details that you would like to be considered for your donation.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and completeness of all fields before submission.
- If you have any difficulties in filling out the form, contact our support for assistance.
- All donations will be acknowledged and handled confidentially.
