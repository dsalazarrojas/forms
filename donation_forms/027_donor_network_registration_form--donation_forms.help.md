# Donor Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Network Registration Form is designed to collect essential information about our donors, allowing us to update our donor database and provide personalized services to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Donor Name** in the field labeled "Donor Name". This is the name we will use to identify you in our database.
2. Enter your **Email** in the field labeled "Email". This is the email address we will use to contact you and keep you informed about our services.
3. Enter your **Address** in the field labeled "Address". This is the physical address we will use to deliver any mail or packages to you.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the field labeled "Phone". This is the phone number we will use to contact you in case of emergencies or important updates.
5. Select your **Date of Birth** in the field labeled "Date of Birth". This is the date we will use to determine your eligibility for certain services.
6. Select your **Date of Registration** in the field labeled "Date of Registration". This is the date you signed up for our donor network.
7. If applicable, select your **Organization** in the field labeled "Organization". This is the organization you are associated with.
8. Select your **Role** in the field labeled "Role". This option is required to ensure we understand your relationship with our organization.
9. If applicable, select one or more **Segments** in the field labeled "Segment". This option allows you to identify yourself as part of a specific community or group.
10. Enter any additional **Notes** in the field labeled "Note". This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information you'd like us to know.
11. Confirm your **Submission** in the field labeled "Submission Confirmation". This confirms that you have reviewed and understand all the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Name**: Please enter your name as it should be displayed in our database.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true
- **Email**: Please enter the email address we will use to contact you.
  • Type: email
  • Required: true
- **Address**: Please enter your physical address.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true
- **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
  • Type: text
  • Required: true
- **Date of Birth**: Please select your date of birth.
  • Type: date
  • Required: true
- **Date of Registration**: Please select the date you registered for our donor network.
  • Type: date
  • Required: true
- **Organization**: If you are associated with an organization, please select it from the options provided. This field is optional.
  • Type: text
  • Required: false
- **Role**: Please select your role in our donor network.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true
- **Segment**: If you are part of a specific community or group, please select one or more segments from the options provided. This field is optional.
  • Type: select_multiple
  • Required: false
- **Note**: This field is optional and allows you to provide additional information about yourself.
  • Type: note
  • Required: false
- **Confirmation**: This confirms that you have reviewed and understand all the information provided.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: true
- **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to our terms and conditions.
  • Type: note
  • Required: false
- **Submission Confirmation**: This confirms that you have submitted your information correctly.
  • Type: note
  • Required: false
