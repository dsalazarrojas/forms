# Performing Arts Auditorium Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations from patrons for the Performing Arts Auditorium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select your donation type from the "Donation Type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Amount" field.
5. Choose the frequency of your donation from the "Frequency" dropdown menu.
6. If you are making a recurring donation, enter the start date and end date in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields respectively.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
8. Provide any additional message for the Performing Arts Auditorium in the "Message" field.
9. Confirm how you would like to receive acknowledgement of your donation by selecting the relevant options from the "Acknowledgement" dropdown menu.
10. If you have any message for the donor, enter it in the "Supporter Message" field.
11. Finally, enter the name of the tool you would like to be assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
- **Donation Type (select_one)**: Choose between One-time or Recurring donation. 
  - **One-time**: A single lump sum payment.
  - **Recurring**: Ongoing donations at a regular interval.
- **Amount (number)**: Enter the amount of your donation.
- **Frequency (select_multiple)**: Choose between Daily, Weekly, or Monthly frequency.
  - **Daily**: Every day.
  - **Weekly**: Every week.
  - **Monthly**: Every month.
- **Start Date (date)**: Enter the date when you want the recurring donation to start.
- **End Date (date)**: Enter the date when you want the recurring donation to end.
- **Address (text)**: Enter your mailing address for communication and sending of any goods.
- **Message (note)**: Provide any additional message or note about your donation.
- **Acknowledgement (select_multiple)**: Choose how you would like to be acknowledged for your donation.
  - **Email**: Receive an email acknowledgement.
  - **Phone**: Receive a phone call acknowledgement.
  - **Letter**: Receive a physical letter acknowledgement.
- **Supporter Message (text)**: Enter any message you would like to convey to the donor.
- **Assigned Tool (text)**: Enter the name of the tool you would like to be assigned to you.
