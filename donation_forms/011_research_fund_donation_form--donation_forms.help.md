# Research Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a research fund donation from users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
- Enter the gift amount you would like to donate in the "Gift Amount" field.
- Select the currency of your donation from the options listed in the "Currency" field.
- Enter the date of your donation in the "Donation Date" field.
- Confirm the donation amount you entered in the "Donation Amount" field.
- Choose whether your donation is recurring or not by selecting an option in the "Recurring Donation" field.
- Select your preferred payment method from the options in the "Payment Method" field.
- Provide any additional terms of payment in the "Payment Terms" field.
- Select the status of your donation from the options in the "Payment Status" field.
- Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name (donor_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
- **E-mail (email)**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates and confirmation of your donation.
- **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
- **Gift Amount (gift_amount)**: Enter the amount you would like to donate, this is the amount that will be reflected in the research fund's balance.
- **Currency (currency)**: Select the currency of your donation from the options provided, this is important for accurate accounting and processing.
- **Donation Date (donation_date)**: Enter the date of your donation, this helps in tracking the history of donations.
- **Donation Amount (donation_amount)**: Confirm the amount you entered earlier, this ensures accuracy in accounting.
- **Recurring Donation (recurring_donation)**: Choose whether your donation is recurring or not, this will affect future notifications and updates.
- **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided, this will determine the processing and handling of your donation.
- **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**: Enter any additional terms of payment, this can include instructions or details about your donation.
- **Payment Status (payment_status)**: Select the status of your donation from the options provided, this helps in tracking and processing your donation.
- **Submit (submit)**: Click this button to complete and submit your form.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing of your donation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your support for the research fund.
