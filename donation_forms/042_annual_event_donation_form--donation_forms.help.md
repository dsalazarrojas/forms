# Annual Event Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Event Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors about their donations to our organization. This form helps us to track and acknowledge your generous support for our annual events. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can process your donation correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details, making sure to complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
2. Select the correct options for each field, paying attention to the hints and explanations provided for each field.
3. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, *):* Enter your full name as it should be displayed publicly.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, *):* Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, ): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, *):* Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, `select_one`, *):* Select the type of donation you are providing (e.g., Monetary, In-Kind, Service or Volunteer Time, etc.).
* **Description of In-Kind Donation** (`in_kind_description`, `text`, ): If donating goods or services, describe what you are donating.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_one`, *):* Choose how often you want to donate (e.g., One-time, Monthly Recurring, Quarterly, etc.).
* **Duration for Recurring Donations** (`recurring_duration`, `select_one`, ): If this is a recurring donation, choose how long you want it to continue.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, *):* Identify the event this donation is for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, ): Enter the date of the event.
* **Donation Purpose** (`donation_purpose`, `select_multiple`, ): Choose how you want the funds to be used (e.g., General Event Expenses, Venue or Equipment, etc.).
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, *):* Choose how you want to pay (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, etc.).
* **Recognition Level** (`recognition_level`, `select_one`, ): Choose how you want to be recognized (e.g., Public Recognition, Acknowledgment in Program, etc.).
* **Name for Public Recognition** (`public_name`, `text`, ): If different from your legal name, enter your public name.
* **Donor Type** (`donor_type`, `select_one`, ): Select if you are donating as an individual or business.
* **Tax ID or Documentation Needed** (`tax_documentation`, `text`, ): Enter any necessary tax ID or documentation.
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, ): Enter any special requests or messages.
