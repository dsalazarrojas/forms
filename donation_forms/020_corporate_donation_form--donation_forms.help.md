# Corporate Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from corporate donors for donation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Employee Information page.
2. On the next page, select the relevant donation details.
3. Enter your Date of Birth, Company Name (if applicable), and any additional Contact Information you'd like to provide.
4. Fill out the Donation Amount and select a suitable purpose for your donation.
5. Finally, provide your email address, phone number, and any comments or purpose for your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (Employee Information, text, required): Enter your employee information.
* **Donation Details** (Donation Details, select_multiple, required): Select the donation details.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, optional): If you're representing a company, enter your company name.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **Donation Amount** (Donation Amount, number, optional): Enter the donation amount.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Provide your address.
* **Purpose** (Purpose, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your donation.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your donation.
