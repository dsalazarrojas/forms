# Local Theater Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow individuals or organizations to make donations to support local theaters in our community. Please fill out this form carefully to provide the required information and ensure that your donation is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the local theater that you would like to support in the **Theater Name** field.
2. Enter your name in the **Donor Name** field.
3. Enter the amount of money you would like to donate in the **Donation Amount** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Select the date and time you would like to make your donation in the **Date** and **Time** fields.
7. Add any additional notes about your donation in the **Note** field.
8. If you are donating to one specific theater, select it from the list in the **Select Theater** field. If you are donating to multiple theaters, select all relevant theaters in the **Select Multiple Theaters** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Theater Name** (`theater_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the local theater you would like to support.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you would like to donate. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to make your donation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to make your donation.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your donation.
* **Select Theater** (`select_theater`, select_one, required: false): Select the specific theater you are making a donation to.
* **Select Multiple Theaters** (`select_multiple_theaters`, select_multiple, required: false): If you are making a donation to multiple theaters, select all relevant theaters here.
