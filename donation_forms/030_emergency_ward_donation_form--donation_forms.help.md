# Emergency Ward Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Ward Donation Form is used to collect information from donors who want to make a donation to the Emergency Ward. Your donation will help support the Emergency Ward's mission, and we are grateful for your support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Emergency Ward Donation Form" title to access the form.
2. Fill out the form fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Ward Donation Form**: This is the title of the form and is only for reference purposes.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Phone**: This is optional and for follow-up contact purposes.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate. Please use numbers only.
* **Frequency**: Select how often you would like to donate, either once, weekly, monthly, or annually.
* **Donation Method**: Select how you would like to make your donation, either online, by mail, or in person.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your donation, either by credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
* **Payment Info**: If you select "Credit Card" as your payment method, you can enter any additional payment information here.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field for you to leave any additional comments or notes about your donation.
