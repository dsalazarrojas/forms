# fundraising_event_extension_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The fundraising event extension form is designed to gather participant information and feedback for a fundraising event. This form is likely used to collect additional details from participants and track their donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each field in the form as instructed below.
2. Make sure to review and submit the form carefully, as incorrect submissions can lead to incomplete or duplicate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, not required): This field is for providing a brief description of the participant.
* **Participant Donations** (`participant_donations`, number, not required): Enter the amount of money donated by the participant.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, not required): Share any feedback or comments about the event or the participant experience.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, not required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): Enter the participant's phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each participant.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the event organizer for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is filled out correctly.
