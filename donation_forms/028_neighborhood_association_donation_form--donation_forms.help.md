# Neighborhood Association Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Neighborhood Association Donation Form is designed to collect donations from residents and businesses to support the association's projects and initiatives. By filling out this form, you can contribute to the association's efforts and help make a positive impact in the neighborhood.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used to confirm your donation.
3. If you are a business or organization, select the correct type from the "Donor Type" field.
4. Choose how you would like to make your donation, either online or offline.
5. Enter the amount of your donation.
6. If you are donating for a specific project, provide its name and description.
7. Optionally, you can provide additional notes about your donation.
8. Confirm your email address to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to confirm your donation.
* **Business Name**: If you are a business or organization, enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Purpose**: If you are donating for a specific project, enter its name and description.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are donating for.
* **Project Description**: Provide a brief description of the project you are donating for.
* **Project Budget**: Enter the budget for the project you are donating for.
* **Donation Frequency**: If you plan to donate regularly, enter how often you plan to donate.
* **How often do you plan to donate?**: This is a helper field to confirm your donation frequency.
* **Project Status**: Select the status of the project you are donating for (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Donor Type**: Select the type of donor you are (Resident, Business, or Organization).
* **Donation Method**: Choose how you would like to make your donation (Online or Offline).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your donation.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to complete the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address to receive confirmation of your donation.
* If you are donating for a specific project, please ensure that the project is active and has a valid budget.
* If you are a business or organization, please select the correct type of donor.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Neighborhood Association office.
