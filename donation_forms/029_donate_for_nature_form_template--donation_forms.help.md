# Donate For Nature Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donation information for a nature-related cause or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Donor Information** (field 2) with your name, address, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your **Donation Amount** (field 3) in the specified format (e.g., numerical value).
3. Provide your **Credit Card Information** (field 4) including your **Card Number**, **Expiration Date**, and **Card Code**.
4. Select your **Donation Type** (field 12) to indicate the type of donation you are making.
5. Select your **Currency** (field 13) for the donation amount.
6. Enter the **Value** (field 14) of your donation amount.
7. Select the **Submit** options (field 15) to confirm your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (field 2)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details to help us understand who you are and where to send your receipt or confirmation.
* **Donation Amount (field 3)**: Enter the numerical value of your donation. Please ensure it is accurate and follows the specified format.
* **Card Information (field 4)**: Provide your credit card details to process your donation. Please enter your card number, expiration date, and card code.
* **Expiration Date (field 6)**: Enter the month and year of your credit card's expiration date.
* **Card Code (field 7)**: Enter your credit card's security code.
* **Security Code (field 8)**: Enter the three or four-digit security code on the back of your card.
* **Name (field 9)**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on any receipts or confirmations.
* **Email (field 10)**: Enter your email address to receive confirmation of your donation.
* **Phone (field 11)**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communications.
* **Donation Type (field 12)**: Select the type of donation you are making (e.g., one-time, recurring).
* **Currency (field 13)**: Select the currency of your donation amount.
* **Value (field 14)**: Enter the numerical value of your donation amount.
* **Submit (field 15)**: Confirm your donation by selecting the "Submit" option.
