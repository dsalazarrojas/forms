# Square Donate For Clean River Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals and organizations that want to support the cleaning of local rivers. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the sponsor and donor, including their contact information and the amount and purpose of the donation. This information will be used to track and acknowledge the donations, and to communicate with the sponsors and donors about their contribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Enter the sponsor's name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
4. Select a date for the donation, using the date format specified in the form (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Enter the amount donated and the purpose of the donation.
6. Add any additional messages or comments about the donation.
7. Enter your name, email, and phone number as the donor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the sponsor who is making the donation.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the sponsor.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of money donated.
* **Purpose Of Donation** (`purpose_of_donation`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of why you are making this donation.
* **Additional Message** (`additional_message`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or messages about the donation.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address as the donor.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number as the donor.
* **Date Donated** (`date_donated`, date, required/optional): Select a date for the donation, using the date format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email and phone numbers for formatting and spelling errors.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and accessible. If you need assistance or have any issues with the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
