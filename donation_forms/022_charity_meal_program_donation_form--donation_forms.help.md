# Charity Meal Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors to process their charitable donation to the meal program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your full name and email address. This will allow us to communicate with you about your donation.
2. Choose how much you would like to donate, including the frequency of your donation. You can choose from one-time, monthly, annually, or quarterly donations.
3. Select the meal program you would like to support with your donation.
4. Confirm how many meals your donation will provide.
5. Choose your preferred payment method, including credit card, debit card, bank transfer, or check.
6. If paying by card, provide the cardholder's name and the last four digits of the card number.
7. Decide if you want a tax receipt for your donation and if you want to receive program updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donor Information
* **Full Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, Required): Your complete name.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, `email`, Required): Your email address for receipt and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, `text`, Optional): Your contact number for optional use.

### Donation Details
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, Required): How much you would like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_one`, Required): Choose from one-time, monthly, annually, or quarterly donations.
* **What Does Your Donation Support** (`donation_purpose`, `select_one`, Optional): Select from General Meal Program, Breakfast Program, Lunch Program, Dinner Program, Weekend Meals, or Emergency Meals.
* **Approximate Number of Meals Funded** (`meals_provided`, `number`, Optional): Your contribution provides this many meals.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, Required): Choose from Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check.
* **Cardholder Name** (`cardholder_name`, `text`, Optional): If paying by card, provide the cardholder's name.
* **Card Last Four Digits** (`card_last_four`, `text`, Optional): The last four digits of the card number.

### Communication Preferences
* **I Want a Tax Receipt** (`receipt_request`, `select_one`, Required): For tax purposes.
* **I Want to Receive Program Updates** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, Optional): To get information about the meal program.

### Donation Date
* **Date of Donation** (`donation_date`, `date`, Required): Today's date.

Note: This help guide is a simplified explanation of the form and the fields. For specific questions or concerns, please contact the meal program organization directly.
