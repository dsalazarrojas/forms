# senior_care_initiative_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The senior care initiative donation form is used to collect information from users who want to donate to the senior care initiative. This form helps track donations, follow up with donors, and provide additional information to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Input** (field 1) with a brief description of your donation.
2. Enter the **Donation Amount** (field 2) to specify the amount you would like to donate.
3. Choose the **Frequency** (field 3) of your donation, such as Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
4. Select the **Donation Method** (field 4) you would like to use, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Check.
5. Enter your **Contact E-mail** (field 5) to allow us to follow up with you.
6. Provide your **Phone** (field 6) number so we can contact you if needed.
7. Enter your **Address** (field 7) to help us understand your location.
8. Choose the **Payment Method** (field 8) you would like to use, such as Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Check.
9. Provide any additional **Notes** (field 9) you would like to share with us.
10. Enter the **Date** (field 10) and **Time** (field 11) you would like to make the donation.
11. Enter your **Name** (field 12) and **Organization** (field 13) if you are representing an organization.
12. Enter the details for **Address 1** (field 14), **Address 2** (field 15), **City** (field 16), **State** (field 17), **Zip** (field 18), and **Country** (field 19).
13. Finally, click the **Submit** (field 20) button to submit your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (field 1, text, required: false): This field allows you to provide a brief description of your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (field 2, number, required: false): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Frequency** (field 3, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you would like to donate, such as Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Donation Method** (field 4, select_multiple, required: false): Select the method you would like to use for your donation, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Check.
* **Contact E-mail** (field 5, email, required: false): Enter your contact email so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone** (field 6, text, required: false): Provide your phone number to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Address** (field 7, text, required: false): Enter your address to help us understand your location.
* **Payment Method** (field 8, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the payment method you would like to use, such as Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Check.
* **Notes** (field 9, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes you would like to share with us.
* **Date** (field 10, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to make the donation.
* **Time** (field 11, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to make the donation.
* **Name** (field 12, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Organization** (field 13, text, required: false): Enter your organization's name if you are representing one.
* **Address 1** (field 14, text, required: false): Enter the address of your organization (if applicable).
* **Address 2** (field 15, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information.
* **City** (field 16, text, required: false): Enter the city of your location.
* **State** (field 17, text, required: false): Enter the state of your location.
* **Zip** (field 18, text, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (field 19, text, required: false): Enter the country of your location.
* **Assign Tool** (field 21, text, required: false): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
