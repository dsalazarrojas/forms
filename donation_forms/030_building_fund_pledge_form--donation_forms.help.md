# Building Fund Pledge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors to support the building project. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth and successful donation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by understanding the purpose of the form and the project.
2. Fill in your full name and email address.
3. Provide your phone number, street address, and city, state, and zip code.
4. Choose your donor category (Individual donor, Family/household, Business/corporation, etc.).
5. Select your organization name (if you're representing a business or institution).
6. Choose your membership or affiliation status (Active member, Former member, Friend/supporter, etc.).
7. Enter the total pledge amount you intend to give.
8. Choose how you'd like to make the payment (Single lump sum, Multiple installments, etc.).
9. Set the first payment amount and date.
10. Select the payment frequency (One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, etc.).
11. Choose how long you'll make payments.
12. Select your preferred payment method (Check, Credit card, Bank transfer, etc.).
13. Indicate how you'd like to be recognized (Public recognition, Recognition optional, Anonymous donation, etc.).
14. If you'd like to dedicate your pledge, choose the type of dedication and provide the name.
15. Select areas of the building project to support.
16. Provide additional comments or questions, if any.
