# payjunction_alumni_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from alumni for various purposes, allowing us to track and acknowledge their contributions to the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the date you made the donation in the "Date Donated" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donation Amount (1, number, required: false)
*   **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation.
### Name (2, text, required: false)
*   **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
### Email (3, email, required: false)
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
### Phone (4, text, required: false)
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### Date Donated (5, date, required: false)
*   **Date Donated**: Select the date you made the donation.
### Tips
*   Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure we can contact you and record your donation correctly.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
