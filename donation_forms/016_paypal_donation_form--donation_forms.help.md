# PayPal Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Donation Form is designed to collect donations from users, allowing them to specify the donation amount, frequency, method, currency, and other relevant details. This form streamlines the donation process, making it easier for users to contribute to the cause.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and select the correct donation options.
2. Fill out the required and optional fields as necessary.
3. Ensure that you have provided all necessary information before submitting the form.

* **Donation Form** (donation_form_1): This is the title of the form.
* **Amount** (donation_amount_1): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Frequency** (donation_frequency_1): Select how often you would like to donate: One-time, Recurring, or Both.
* **Frequency** (donation_frequency_2): Select how often you would like to donate (this field is optional and allows users to specify an additional frequency).
* **Method** (donation_method_1): Choose how you would like to make the donation: Credit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer.
* **Currency** (donation_currency_1): Select the currency of the donation: USD, EUR, or CAD.
* **Currency** (donation_currency_2): Select the currency of the donation (this field is optional and allows users to specify an additional currency).
