# Donation Refund Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to claim a refund for a donation made to our organization. Please fill out the form with the necessary information to process your refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. This will help us process your refund request quickly and efficiently.
2. **Submit the form to us**. Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Refund Claim Form** (`donation_claim_form`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or details about your refund request. Please provide a brief description of why you are requesting a refund.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your donation receipt or in our records.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as it is associated with your donation. This will help us contact you with updates on your refund request.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you are claiming a refund for. This should be the date of the donation or the date you are requesting a refund by.
* **Reason for Refund** (`refund_reason`, text, required: false): Describe the reason you are requesting a refund. This will help us process your request accurately.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the refund you are requesting. Please ensure this amount matches the original donation amount.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to your refund request.
* Our team will review your refund request and respond with updates on our processing.
