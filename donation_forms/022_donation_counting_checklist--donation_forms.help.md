# Donation Counting Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donation Counting Checklist is a form used to record and track donations received by an organization. This form helps to ensure accurate and up-to-date records of donations, making it easier to manage and report on donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Donor name** in the first field. This is the name of the person or organization making the donation.
2. Fill in the **Donation amount** field with the actual amount of the donation. This field is required to record the total amount received.
3. Enter the **Date of donation** field with the date the donation was received. This is a required field to ensure accurate tracking.
4. If applicable, fill in the **Time of donation** field with the time of day the donation was received. This field is optional and only required if the donation was made during a specific time.
5. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization making the donation.
- **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Fill in the total amount of the donation. Ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Date of donation** (`donation_date`, date, required): Enter the date the donation was received. This is crucial for tracking donations over time.
- **Time of donation** (`donation_time`, time, optional): If the donation was made at a specific time, fill in this field. Otherwise, leave it blank.

## Tips
- Ensure the form is filled out completely and accurately for every donation.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
- The form is designed for tracking donations, so ensure all fields are filled in as accurately as possible.
