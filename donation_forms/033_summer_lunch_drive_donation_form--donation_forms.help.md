# Summer Lunch Drive Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Lunch Drive Donation Form is designed to collect donations and relevant information from donors for the Summer Lunch Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information.

* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `text`, optional): Indicate how often you'd like to donate (e.g., one-time, recurring).
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your donation.
* **Donation Time** (`donation_time`, `time`, optional): If applicable, specify the time of your donation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about your donation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.

* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Specify the city where you're located.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code.
