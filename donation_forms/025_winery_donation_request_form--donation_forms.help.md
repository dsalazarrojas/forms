# Winery Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winery Donation Request Form is designed to collect information from wineries about their donation requests, including event details and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following fields:
	* **Event Details**: A brief description of the event where the donation will be used.
	* **Supporting Documents**: Any relevant documents or materials that support the donation request.
	* **Follow-up Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the follow-up process.
	* **Follow-up Phone**: The phone number of the person to follow up with.
	* **Follow-up Email**: The email address of the person to follow up with.
	* **Follow-up Status**: The current status of the follow-up process (New, In Progress, Done).
	* **Follow-up Followed up by**: The person who will follow up (Winery, Event Inquirer).
	* **Follow-up Date Followed up**: The date of the last follow-up.
	* **Winery Donation Amount**: The amount of the donation requested.
	* **Event Inquirer Name**: The name of the person inquiring about the donation.
	* **Event Inquirer Email**: The email address of the person inquiring about the donation.
	* **Event Inquirer Phone**: The phone number of the person inquiring about the donation.
	* **Winery Email**: The email address of the winery.
	* **Winery Phone**: The phone number of the winery.
	* **Winery Address**: The address of the winery.
	* **Event Inquirer Address**: The address of the person inquiring about the donation.
	* **Next Follow-up**: Any additional notes about the next follow-up.
