# VETERANS_HOME_UPGRADE_DONATION_FORM - Help Guide
## Purpose

The VETERANS_HOME_UPGRADE_DONATION_FORM is used to collect information about donations made to support the Veterans' Home upgrade initiative. This form helps us understand the donor's information, donation amount, payment method, and any additional comments or details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*), and optional fields with the information requested.
2. Ensure that you accurately represent the donor and donation details.
3. Use the provided options for payment method and frequency of donation to select the correct option(s).
4. Enter the correct date of the donation in the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. If you have an alternate payment method or amount, please use the "Other" options to specify this information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information**: This field is used to collect the donor's personal information.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Enter the donor's name or name of the organization.
* **Amount**: This field is used to specify the amount of the donation.
	+ Type: number
	+ Purpose: Enter the total amount of the donation.
* **Payment Method**: This field is used to select the payment method used for the donation.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: Choose from the provided options: Cash, Check, or Credit Card.
* **Frequency of Donation**: This field is used to specify if the donation is a one-time or repeating donation.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Purpose: Choose from the provided options: One-time or Repeating.
* **Date of Donation**: This field is used to enter the date of the donation.
	+ Type: date
	+ Purpose: Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to enter the donor's phone number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Purpose: Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Email**: This field is used to enter the donor's email address.
	+ Type: email
	+ Purpose: Enter the donor's email address.
* **Donation Comment**: This field is used to enter any additional comments or information about the donation.
	+ Type: note
	+ Purpose: Enter any additional comments or information about the donation.
* **Other Donation Amount**: This field is used to enter the amount of the donation if it's not specified in the "Amount" field.
	+ Type: number
	+ Purpose: Enter the alternate amount of the donation.
* **Other Payment Method**: This field is used to select an alternate payment method if it's not specified in the "Payment Method" field.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Purpose: Choose from the provided options: Cash, Check, or Credit Card.
