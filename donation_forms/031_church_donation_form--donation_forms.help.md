# Church Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Donation Form is a simple, online form designed to collect donation information from donors. It captures details such as donor contact information, donation amount, and preferred payment method. This form is intended to be used by individuals who wish to make a donation to the church, either as a one-time gift or as a recurring contribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the donor's contact information, including name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose the donation amount and frequency (one-time or recurring).
3. Select the fund designation where you would like your gift to be allocated.
4. Decide if you would like to remain anonymous or not.
5. If the gift is in memory or honor of someone, select the appropriate option.
6. Provide the name of the person being honored or remembered.
7. Choose your preferred payment method (credit/debit card, electronic check, or mailing a physical check).
8. Add any additional comments or instructions.
9. Confirm the date of the donation.
10. Finally, confirm that you understand this is a tax-deductible contribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`donor_name`): Enter the full name of the donor.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`): Enter the email address of the donor. This will be used for gift confirmation and digital receipt.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`): Enter the phone number of the donor (optional).
* **Mailing Address** (`donor_address`): Enter the mailing address of the donor. This will be used for official year-end tax statements.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`): Choose whether the gift is one-time or recurring (e.g., weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **Fund Designation** (`fund_designation`): Select the fund where you would like your gift to be allocated (e.g., General Fund, Tithes and Offerings, Building Fund, etc.).
* **Would you like to remain anonymous?** (`anonymous_giving`): Decide if you would like to remain anonymous or not.
* **Is this gift in memory or honor of someone?** (`memorial_gift`): If the gift is in memory or honor of someone, select the appropriate option.
* **Name of Honoree** (`honoree_name`): If the gift is in memory or honor of someone, enter their name.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_preference`): Choose your preferred payment method (credit/debit card, electronic check, or mailing a physical check).
* **Additional Comments or Instructions** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or instructions (optional).
* **Date of Donation** (`donation_date`): Enter the date of the donation.
* **I understand this is a tax-deductible contribution** (`tax_ack_confirmed`): Confirm that you understand this is a tax-deductible contribution.
