# Dinner Event Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dinner Event Donation Form is designed to collect information for a dinner event donation. The form is used by event organizers to gather details about the event, the donor, and the donation amount and items. This information will be used to manage donations for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including your name, event date, and email address.
2. If you are making a donation, fill out the donation amount and select the donation items you are donating.
3. If you want to confirm your donation, select "Yes" for the "Confirm Donations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself as a donor.
* **Event Details**: This field is optional, but please provide any additional information about the event if you have any.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Donation Items**: Select the items you are donating from the list of options (Apple, Banana, Carrot).
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation, if applicable.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Event Details 2**: This field is optional, but you can use it to provide any additional information about the event.
* **Confirm Donations**: Select "Yes" if you are confirming your donation, or "No" if you are not making a donation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately.
- If you are making a donation, please ensure that the donation amount and items are filled out correctly.
- Double-check your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
