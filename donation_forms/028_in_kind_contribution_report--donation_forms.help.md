# in_kind_contribution_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The in-kind contribution report form is used to collect detailed information from in-kind donors, including their name, address, contribution date, type, amount, and description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the in-kind contribution report form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name as the in-kind donor in the "In Kind Donor Name" field.
2. Enter the date of your in-kind contribution in the "In Kind Contribution Date" field.
3. Describe the type of in-kind contribution you made in the "In Kind Contribution Type" field.
4. Enter the amount of your contribution in the "In Kind Amount" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your contribution in the "In Kind Description" field.
6. Fill in your address in the "In Kind Donor Address" field.
7. Enter your city in the "In Kind Donor City" field.
8. Select your state in the "In Kind Donor State" field.
9. Enter your ZIP code in the "In Kind Donor Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **In Kind Contribution Form**: This is a section to acknowledge that you are submitting an in-kind contribution report.
* **In Kind Contribution Date**: Enter the date you made your in-kind contribution. This is a required field.
* **In Kind Donor Name**: Enter your name as the in-kind donor. This is a required field.
* **In Kind Contribution Type**: Describe the type of in-kind contribution you made, e.g., "food", "clothing", "services", etc.
* **In Kind Amount**: Enter the amount of your contribution.
* **In Kind Description**: Provide a brief description of your contribution.
* **In Kind Donor Address**: Enter your address where you made the contribution.
* **In Kind Donor City**: Enter the city where you made the contribution.
* **In Kind Donor State**: Enter the state where you made the contribution.
* **In Kind Donor Zip**: Enter your ZIP code where you made the contribution.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
* This form is for in-kind contributions made to the organization, not for any other purpose.
* If you have any questions, contact the organization for further assistance.
