# orphanage_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a donation to an orphanage. Fill out this form to provide details about the donation you'd like to make and support the orphanage you'd like to help.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the required information. Start by filling out the **Donations** field on page 1, where you'll be able to provide a brief description of the item or amount you'd like to donate. On the second page, select the **Donor** checkbox to indicate whether you are the donor or not. If you are the donor, please make sure to fill out the other donor fields. On the third page, provide the details about the **Orphanage** you'd like to support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donations** (`donation_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe the item or amount you'd like to donate. This can be a monetary donation, a product or service, or any other type of contribution you'd like to make.
* **Donor** (`donor_details`, select_multiple, optional): Check the **Donor** checkbox to indicate whether you are making the donation yourself or someone else is. If you are not the donor, please provide the name and contact information of the person making the donation.
	+ **Yes**: I am making the donation myself.
	+ **No**: Someone else is making the donation on my behalf.
* **Orphanage** (`orphanage_details`, text, optional): Provide the name and location of the orphanage you'd like to support. If you're unsure of the location, you can try searching online for the orphanage's website or contact the orphanage directly.
