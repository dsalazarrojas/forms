# credit_card_donation_forms - Help Guide

## Purpose
The credit_card_donation_forms is used to collect information from donors who wish to make a credit card donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your donor information in the **Donor Information** section.
2. Enter your credit card details in the **Credit Card Information** section.
3. Enter the **Amount Donated** to be credited to your selected fund.
4. Select the fund you wish to donate to in the **Select a Fund** section.
5. Choose how often you would like to donate in the **How often would you like to donate** section.
6. If you would like to receive emails, provide your **Donor Email** address.
7. (Optional) If you would like to be contacted, provide your **Phone Number**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information**: Enter your name, address, etc. to ensure we can reach you if necessary.
* **Credit Card Information**: Enter your credit card details to complete the donation.
* **Amount Donated**: Specify the amount you wish to donate.
* **Select a Fund**: Choose a fund to receive your donation. 
* **Select a Fund**: This field is intended to be a label for the selected fund.
* **How often would you like to donate**: Choose how often you would like to make donations.
* **Donor Email**: If you would like to receive emails about your donation and other updates, provide your email address.
* **Phone Number**: If you would like to be contacted by phone, provide your phone number.
