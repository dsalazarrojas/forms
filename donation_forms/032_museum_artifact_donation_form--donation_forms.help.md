# Museum Artifact Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals or organizations wishing to donate artifacts to the museum. Please complete this form to initiate the donation process and help the museum's collections management team evaluate your proposed donation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide the required information for each field, as indicated by the label.
2. Fill in the **Donation Details** page with a brief description of the artifact(s) you wish to donate.
3. Proceed to the **Contact Information** page and enter your contact details.
4. Enter your email address and phone number on the **Contact Information** page.
5. Enter the donor's name in the **Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Details**: A text field where you can briefly describe the artifact(s) you wish to donate.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your personal or institutional contact information to be contacted regarding your donation.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to be contacted by the museum's collections management team.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number to be contacted by the museum's collections management team.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the individual or organization making the donation.
