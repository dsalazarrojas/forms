# Play Therapy Room Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect donations for the Play Therapy Room, a space for individuals to engage in activities that support mental health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents ready before starting the form.
2. Select the correct donation method and amount to be donated.
3. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Method** (`Donation Method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method through which you made the donation, such as Cash, Check, or Online.
* **Amount Donated** (`Amount Donated`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you donated.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address associated with the donation, enter it here. This can be useful for follow-up communication or updates.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number associated with the donation, enter it here. This can be useful for further communication or follow-up questions.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the donation, if known.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the donation, if known.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the donation, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant teams or departments for clarification.
* Review the form guidelines and requirements before making a submission.
