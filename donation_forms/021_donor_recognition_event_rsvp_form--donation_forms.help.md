# Donor Recognition Event RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Recognition Event RSVP Form is designed for donors to RSVP and provide information for an upcoming Donor Recognition Event. This form allows donors to confirm their attendance and share their guest count, email address, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name as required by the donor recognition event organizers.
2. Specify the number of guests you will be bringing with you.
3. Provide your email address so we can keep you updated about the event.
4. If you have a phone number, please enter it to ensure we can contact you with any last-minute updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification or driver's license.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification or driver's license.
* **Guest Count (Number)**: Please specify the number of guests you will be bringing with you. This helps us plan for seating and accommodations.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you with any updates or information about the event.
* **Phone (Text)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can reach you if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is up-to-date and correct to receive important event updates.
* If you are unable to attend, please let us know as soon as possible so we can make the necessary adjustments.
