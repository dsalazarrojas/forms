# Fire Department Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations and feedback from the public in support of the local fire department. Your input will help the department understand community needs and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your First name and Last name in the text fields marked "First name" and "Last name" respectively.
* Provide a valid Email address in the field marked "Email" to enable us to contact you regarding your donation.
* Enter your Phone number in the field marked "Phone" to enable us to contact you for any follow-up questions or updates.
* Choose a donation amount from the field marked "Donation amount" and enter the corresponding value.
* Select the purpose of your donation from the dropdown menu marked "Purpose".
* Optionally, provide comments or additional information about your donation in the field marked "Comments".
* Click the "Submit" button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid Email address that we can use to contact you about your donation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions or updates.
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required: false): Select a donation amount that you wish to make.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, select_one, required: false): Choose the purpose of your donation from the available options: "Fire Department" or "Other".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Optionally, provide additional comments or information about your donation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click to send your completed form to us.
