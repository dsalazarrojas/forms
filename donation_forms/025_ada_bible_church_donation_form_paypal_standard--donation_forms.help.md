# Ada Bible Church Donation Form - Paypal Standard - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations from individuals supporting the Ada Bible Church through the PayPal standard platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is required for tax-deductible receipt purposes.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Enter your mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field. This is also required for tax-deductible receipt purposes.
5. Select the fund to which you would like to donate from the "Designation / Fund" options.
6. Enter the amount you wish to give in the "Donation Amount ($)" field.
7. Choose the frequency of your gift from the "Frequency" options.
8. Select your home campus from the "Home Campus" options.
9. Confirm that you understand the payment method by selecting "I Understand" from the "Proceed to PayPal for secure processing" options.
10. If you are gifting in honor or memory of someone, select the corresponding option and enter their name.
11. Add any additional comments or special instructions in the "Comments or Special Instructions" field (optional).
12. Finally, confirm that you understand this is a voluntary donation to a non-profit organization by selecting "I Agree" from the "I understand this is a voluntary donation to a non-profit organization" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address. This is required for tax-deductible receipt purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number (optional).
* **Mailing Address** (`donor_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address. This is also required for tax-deductible receipt purposes.
* **Designation / Fund** (`donation_fund`, select_one, required): Select the fund to which you would like to donate.
* **Donation Amount ($)** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to give.
* **Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of your gift.
* **Home Campus** (`campus_location`, select_one, required): Select your home campus.
* **Proceed to PayPal for secure processing** (`payment_processor_ack`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the payment method.
* **Is this gift in honor or memory of someone?** (`tribute_gift`, select_one, optional): If you are gifting in honor or memory of someone, select the corresponding option and enter their name.
* **Tribute Name** (`tribute_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person being honored or remembered.
* **Comments or Special Instructions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or special instructions.
* **I understand this is a voluntary donation to a non-profit organization** (`tax_ack`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand this is a voluntary donation to a non-profit organization.
