# donation_acknowledgement_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information about donations received by the organization, including details about the donor, the donation amount, frequency, date, and acknowledgement message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all necessary information before starting to fill it out.
* Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
* For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank or fill them out if you have additional information that is relevant to the donation.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information** (Donor Details, text, optional): Enter the donor's name and any other relevant details.
* **Donations Received** (Donation Amount, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the donation.
* **Donation Frequency** (Donation Frequency, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of options provided.
* **Donation Date** (Donation Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the donation.
* **Acknowledgement Message** (Acknowledgement Message, note, optional): Enter a message to be sent to the donor as an acknowledgement.
* **Additional Donor Notes** (Additional Donor Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about the donor.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the donor's email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Acknowledgement Sent** (Acknowledgement Sent, select one, optional): Check if the acknowledgement has been sent to the donor.

Note: The fields marked "optional" do not need to be filled out unless you have additional information that is relevant to the donation.
