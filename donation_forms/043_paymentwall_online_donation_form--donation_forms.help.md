# Paymentwall Online Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paymentwall Online Donation Form is designed for users to make online donations. This form will help you collect donation information from users in a secure and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your complete name in the "Complete Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select one or more options from the "Products" dropdown menu to indicate what you would like to donate.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Choose one or more options from the "Type of Donation" dropdown menu to specify the type of donation.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comment Box" field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit your donation information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paymentwall Online Donation Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Complete Name** (`complete_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your complete name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address. This will be used to contact you.
* **Products** (`products`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more options from the menu to specify what you would like to donate. Currently, the options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Please enter your contact number.
* **Type of Donation** (`type_of_donation`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more options from the menu to specify the type of donation. Currently, the options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Comment Box** (`comment_box`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit your donation information.
