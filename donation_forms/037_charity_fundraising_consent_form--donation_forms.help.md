# Charity Fundraising Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and consent from participants, donors, and event organizers for charity fundraising purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **participant name**, **email address**, **phone number** and **mailing address** for contact purposes.
2. Select your **date of birth** to verify your age for participation and donation purposes.
3. Choose your **participation type** (donor, fundraiser, volunteer, etc.).
4. If donating, select your desired **donation amount** and frequency (one-time or recurring).
5. Choose how you plan to **contribute** (credit card, debit card, PayPal, bank transfer, etc.).
6. If fundraising, select your **personal fundraising goal**.
7. Provide consent to use your personal data and **agree to terms and conditions**.
8. Select your preference for **public recognition** (listing your name or organization).
9. Choose whether you need a **tax receipt**.
10. Finally, provide any **additional comments**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email Address**: Contact email for updates and communications.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for any further discussion.
* **Mailing Address**: Full address where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: For age verification purposes.
* **Charity Organization**: Beneficiary organization you are supporting.
* **Fundraising Event or Campaign**: Which campaign you are participating in or supporting.
* **Type of Participation**: Your role in the fundraising event or campaign.
* **Donation Amount**: Select your desired donation amount. If not applicable, select "Not applicable".
* **Donation Frequency**: One-time or recurring donation.
* **Payment Method**: How you plan to contribute (credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Personal Fundraising Goal**: If fundraising, select your target amount.
* **Consent to Data Usage**: Agree to use your data for fundraising purposes.
* **Consent to Communications**: Choose how you want to receive updates and information from us.
* **Photo and Video Consent**: Permit use of your image for promotional purposes.
* **Public Recognition Preference**: Choose whether you want to be publicly recognized as a donor or participant.
* **Tax Receipt Needed**: Select whether you need a tax receipt.
* **Do You Agree to Terms**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the campaign.
* **Date of Consent**: Date and time of form submission.
* **Additional Comments**: Any further notes or comments about your participation.
