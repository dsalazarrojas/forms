# Tribute Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals wishing to make a donation in tribute to a specific person or event. The form is designed to gather relevant details to process the donation and ensure the donor's information is accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the donation, if any, you would like to make.
2. Enter your details, including:
	* Donor name
	* Donor email address
	* Phone number
	* Date of birth (in format `mm/dd/yyyy`)
	* Time of event (in 24-hour format `HH:MM`)
3. If you are making a donation in tribute to someone, enter their name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tribute Donation Form** (`title`): Enter a brief description of the donation, if any, you would like to make.
* **Donor Name** (`name`): Enter your name, which will be used to record your donation.
* **Donor Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your donation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`): Enter the time of the event you are making a donation for, in 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
* **Tribute Name** (`tribute_name`): If you are making a donation in tribute to a specific person, enter their name here.
* **Donor Contact Information**: These fields are optional, but if filled, we can use them to reach you if we need additional information or clarification.

### Additional Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when entering your information to ensure accurate processing of your donation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through your email address provided.
* You may enter any additional information or notes in the `Donor Note` field.
