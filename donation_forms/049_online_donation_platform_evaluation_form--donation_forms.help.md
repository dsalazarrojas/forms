# Online Donaton Platform Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Donaton Platform Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from users to help improve the platform's effectiveness and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
5. Indicate if you have made a donation on the platform (Donation Frequency).
6. Rate your overall experience with the platform (Rating).
7. Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
8. If you have any further comments, please enter them in the field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address to contact you for further discussions or to send updates about the platform.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number. This is optional and not required.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you've donated on the platform, if any.
* **Donation Frequency**: Check if you've made a donation on the platform. Please select 'Yes' if you have, 'No' otherwise.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the platform, using the options: Good, Fair.
* **Feedback**: Share any comments or suggestions about your experience with the platform.
* **Comments**: Enter any further comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience.
* **Comments (Additional)**: If you have more comments or feedback, please enter them here.
