# Nursing Scholarship Contribution Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nursing Scholarship Contribution Form is designed to receive contributions from individuals who wish to support the nursing scholarship program. This form is used to collect information about the donor, details about the contribution, and any additional comments or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Donor Information section**: Provide your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. **Provide Contribution Information**: Share details about your contribution, such as the amount and frequency of your support.
3. **Enter Payment Information**: If applicable, provide payment details to process your contribution.
4. **Share Your Comments**: Leave any additional comments or feedback about your contribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information (Donor Info)**: This field is used to collect your name and contact information, so we can get in touch with you about your contribution.
* **Contribution Information (Contribution Info)**: Share the amount and frequency of your contribution to support the nursing scholarship program.
* **Payment Information (Payment Info)**: If you are making a payment, provide the necessary details to process your contribution. This field is only required if you are making a payment.
* **Contribution Amount**: Please enter the amount you wish to contribute to the nursing scholarship program.
* **Contribution Frequency**: Indicate how often you would like to make contributions to the program.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your contribution and any updates about the program.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number in case we need to reach you about your contribution or any other matter.
* **Donor Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your contribution or the nursing scholarship program.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays in processing your contribution.
* If you are making a payment, ensure that you have the necessary funds available.
* You can always contact us if you have any questions or need further assistance filling out this form.
