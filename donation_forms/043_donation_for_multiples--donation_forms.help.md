# Donation For Multiples - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Donation For Multiples" form is designed to collect donations from donors for events or schools, allowing for flexible and adaptable donation collection.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the donation amount field with the desired amount you would like to donate.
2. Select the payment method you prefer (PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash).
3. If necessary, provide additional payment details in the payment details field.
4. Enter your name as the donor.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and thank you messages.
6. Enter your phone number for further communication.
7. Select the school facilities that you would like your donation to support (School Hall, School Field, School Laboratory, or any other facilities available).
8. Briefly describe the event or school need you would like to support with your donation.
9. Specify how your donation will be used to support the students' needs.
10. Select the purpose of your donation (Event or School).
11. Finally, click the submit button to submit your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donations Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate. Please note that this field is optional, and you can leave it blank if you don't want to specify a donation amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to make the payment (PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): If you need to provide more details about your payment method, please enter them here.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and thank you messages.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **School Facilities** (`school_facilities`, select_multiple, optional): Select the school facilities you would like your donation to support (School Hall, School Field, School Laboratory, or any other facilities available).
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the event or school need you would like to support with your donation.
* **Students Needs** (`students_needs`, text, optional): Specify how your donation will be used to support the students' needs.
* **Donation Purpose** (`donation_purpose`, select_one, optional): Select the purpose of your donation (Event or School).
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your donation.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, note, optional): A confirmation message will be displayed after submitting your donation.
* **Thank You Message** (`thank_you_message`, text, optional): A thank you message will be displayed after submitting your donation.
