# philanthropic_contribution_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare philanthropic contributions for tax purposes. It collects information about the donor's donation date, amount, and details about the gift, including the type of gift, the recipient entity, and the gift's value and frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the donation date.
2. Enter the donation amount.
3. Select your non-citizen status (US citizen, Legal Permanent Resident, or Non-US Citizen).
4. Select your residency status (Legal Permanent Resident, Conditional Resident, or Asylum).
5. Choose the type of gift (Cash, Property, Stock, or Other).
6. Enter the details of the recipient entity, including their name, address, and type.
7. Enter the donor's information (name, address, and affiliation).
8. Specify the gift's purpose and frequency (One-time, Ongoing, or Unknown).
9. Enter any additional details about the gift (description, value, frequency, and recipient entity information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Date**: The date the donation was made.
	+ Please enter the date the donation was made, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Donation Amount**: The amount of the donation.
	+ Enter the total value of the gift.
* **Non-Citizen Status**: Your status as a non-US citizen.
	+ Select US citizen, Legal Permanent Resident, or Non-US Citizen.
* **Non-Citizen Residency Status**: Your residency status as a non-citizen.
	+ Select Legal Permanent Resident, Conditional Resident, or Asylum.
* **Gift Type**: The type of gift made.
	+ Choose from Cash, Property, Stock, or Other.
* **Recipient Entity**: The entity receiving the gift.
	+ Enter the name, address, and type of the recipient entity.
* **Donor Name**: The name of the donor.
	+ Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donor Address**: The address of the donor.
	+ Enter your address.
* **Recipient Name**: The name of the recipient entity.
	+ Enter the name of the recipient entity.
* **Recipient Address**: The address of the recipient entity.
	+ Enter the address of the recipient entity.
* **Gift Value**: The value of the gift.
	+ Enter the total value of the gift.
* **Gift Description**: A description of the gift.
	+ Enter any additional details about the gift.
* **Date of Death**: The date of death (if applicable).
	+ If the gift was made in honor or memory of someone, enter their date of death.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of birth (if applicable).
	+ If the gift was made in celebration of someone's birthday, enter their date of birth.
* **Donor Relationship**: The relationship between the donor and the recipient entity.
	+ Enter any additional details about your relationship with the recipient entity.
* **Donor Affiliation**: The donor's affiliation (if applicable).
	+ Enter any additional details about your affiliation.
* **Gift Purpose**: The purpose of the gift.
	+ Choose from One-time, Ongoing, or Unknown.
* **Gift Frequency**: The frequency of the gift.
	+ Choose from One-time, Ongoing, or Unknown.
* **Gift Frequency Other Description**: Any additional details about the gift's frequency.
	+ Enter any additional details about the gift's frequency.
* **Gift Recipient Entity Type**: The type of the recipient entity.
	+ Enter the type of the recipient entity.
* **Gift Recipient Entity Name**: The name of the recipient entity.
	+ Enter the name of the recipient entity.
* **Gift Recipient Entity Address**: The address of the recipient entity.
	+ Enter the address of the recipient entity.
* **Gift Recipient Entity Nickname**: The nickname of the recipient entity (if applicable).
	+ Enter any nickname for the recipient entity.
* **Gift Recipient Entity Tax ID**: The tax ID of the recipient entity (if applicable).
	+ Enter any tax ID for the recipient entity.
* **Gift Recipient Entity EIN**: The Employer Identification Number of the recipient entity (if applicable).
	+ Enter any EIN for the recipient entity.
