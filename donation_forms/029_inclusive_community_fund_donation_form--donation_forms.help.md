# Inclusive Community Fund Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inclusive Community Fund Donation Form is a tool for collecting donations and feedback from the community. It allows users to submit their contributions and share their thoughts and comments about the project. This form helps the project team to track and manage donations, and to understand the needs and preferences of the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Donation Form** section.
2. Select the **Donations** you wish to make (you can choose one or multiple options).
3. Enter the **Amount** of the donation (this field is optional).
4. Enter your **Donor Email** (this field is optional, but it helps us get in touch with you).
5. Enter your **Donor Phone** (this field is optional, but it can be useful for follow-up communication).
6. Provide any additional **Donor Comments** you'd like to share.
7. If applicable, include **Donor Notes** (this field is for any additional information you'd like to provide).
8. Select who you are a **Form Submitter** (this field is optional, but it helps us understand who submitted the form).
9. Date and time of the **Donation Date** and **Donation Time** (these fields are optional, but they can be useful for tracking donations).
10. Finally, select the **Donation Status** (this field is optional, but it helps us track the status of your donation).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Form**: This is the main section where you can provide a brief description of your donation.
* **Donations**: Choose one or multiple options for your donation, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Amount**: The amount of your donation (if you're making an in-kind contribution, please enter 0).
* **Donor Email**: Your email address, which we'll use to get in touch with you.
* **Donor Phone**: Your phone number, which we can use for follow-up communication.
* **Donor Comments**: Any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share about your donation.
* **Donor Notes**: Any additional information you'd like to provide about your donation.
* **Form Submitter**: Who submitted the form on your behalf (e.g., staff member, volunteer, etc.).
* **Donation Date**: Date of your donation (if applicable).
* **Donation Time**: Time of your donation (if applicable).
* **Donation Status**: Status of your donation (e.g., "New", "Processed", etc.).
