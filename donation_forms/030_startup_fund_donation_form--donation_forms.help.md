# startup_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting information from potential donors who are interested in supporting a startup or business by contributing funds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **General Information**. This field is not required, and you can enter any relevant details about yourself or your organization.
2. Provide your **Name**, which is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email**, which is a required field. Please ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date.
4. Enter your **Phone** number, which is not required but can be useful for communication purposes.
5. Specify the **Donation Amount**, which is a required field to indicate how much you are willing to donate.
6. Choose how often you want to **Donation Frequency**, which is not required but can help in planning future donations. You can select One-time, Recurring, or Ongoing.
7. If you have any **Notes**, you can enter them in this field, which is not required.
8. Confirm your **Donation**, which is not required but can help in tracking your donation status.
9. Confirm your **Donation Frequency**, which is not required but can help in planning future donations.
10. If you have a specific **Assigned Tool**, you can enter it in this field, which is not required.
11. If you have a preference for the **Output File**, you can specify it in this field, which is not required.
12. Select a **Category** for your donation, which is not required.
13. Enter a **Title** for your donation, which is not required.
14. Provide a **Description** of your donation, which is not required.
15. If you know the **Form ID**, please enter it, which is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Enter any relevant details about yourself or your organization.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid and up-to-date email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to donate.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose how often you want to donate (One-time, Recurring, or Ongoing).
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or information, enter them here.
* **Confirm Donation**: Confirm that you are making a donation.
* **Confirm Frequency**: Confirm your donation frequency.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have a specific tool assigned to your donation, enter it here.
* **Output File**: If you have a preference for the output file, specify it here.
* **Category**: Select a category for your donation.
* **Title**: Enter a title for your donation.
* **Description**: Provide a description of your donation.
* **Form ID**: If you know the ID of the form, enter it here.
