# Donate For Nature - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from users for a nature-related cause. It gathers essential details such as the donor's name, email address, donation amount, currency, and payment method. The form also includes space for any additional information or a message.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field.
4. Select the currency of your donation from the "Currency" field.
5. Choose a payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
6. If you have any additional information you'd like to share, type it in the "Additional Donation Info" field.
7. Confirm your donation in the "Confirm Donation" field.
8. Optionally, you can agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agree to Terms" field.
9. Finally, submit your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name (text)**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Donation Amount (number)**: Enter the amount of your donation in the specified currency.
* **Currency (select_one)**: Select the currency of your donation (e.g., USD, EUR, or GBP).
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Choose a payment method that suits you best (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Additional Donation Info (text)**: Type any additional information you'd like to provide about your donation.
* **Confirm Donation (select_one)**: Confirm that you're willing to make a donation.
* **Agree to Terms (select_one)**: Optionally, confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Contact Us (note)**: In case of any questions or issues, feel free to contact us.
* **Confirm Donation Info (select_one)**: Confirm that you've read and understand the donation details.
* **Donation Date (date)**: Enter the date of your donation. (Note: This field is optional, but recommended for tracking purposes.)
* **Donation Time (time)**: Enter the time of your donation. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Donation Message (note)**: Optionally, type a message to accompany your donation.
* **Confirm Donation (select_one)**: Confirm that you're making a donation.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Choose how you wish to make your payment (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Donation Name (text)**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Donation Amount (number)**: Enter the amount of your donation in the specified currency.
* **Currency (select_one)**: Select the currency of your donation (e.g., USD, EUR, or GBP).
