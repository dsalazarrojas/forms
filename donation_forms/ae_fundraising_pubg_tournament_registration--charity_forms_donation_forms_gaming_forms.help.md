### AE FUNDRAISING PUBG Tournament Registration - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is for registering participants for the AE FUNDRAISING PUBG Tournament. You will be asked to provide personal and event information to help us prepare for the event.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Select the registration date from the calendar.
2. Fill in your full name and email address for confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your phone number (optional but recommended for urgent contact).
4. If you are 16 or older, provide your date of birth.
5. Choose the type of registration: Solo, Team (captain), or Spectator.
6. If you are registering as a team, enter your team name and up to 3 teammate usernames.
7. Select your experience level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional.
8. Choose the platform you will play on: PC, Mobile, or Console.
9. Set the minimum suggested donation amount (at least $5).
10. Select your preferred payment method for donation.
11. Indicate how you heard about the tournament.
12. If applicable, list any dietary restrictions.
13. Provide an emergency contact name and phone number (optional).
14. Confirm consent for photography and streaming.
15. Select the waiver agreement to acknowledge tournament rules.
16. Add any additional comments or accessibility requests.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration date**: Enter the date you plan to participate in the tournament.
* **Full name**: Enter your full legal name or preferred display name.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address where you will receive confirmation and event updates.
* **Phone number**: Your phone number for urgent contact (optional).
* **Date of birth**: If you are 16 or older, provide your date of birth for age verification.
* **Age**: Not applicable since date of birth is provided.
* **Registration type**: Choose Solo, Team (captain), or Spectator.
* **Team name**: Leave blank if you are Solo or Spectator. Enter your team name if you are registering as a team.
* **Teammate in-game names**: Enter up to 3 teammate usernames if registering as a team.
* **Experience level**: Select the option that best describes your average skill level.
* **Platform**: Choose the platform you will play on: PC, Mobile, or Console.
* **Donation amount (USD)**: Enter at least $5, or 0 to register without donating.
* **Preferred payment method**: Choose your preferred method for donating: PayPal, Bank transfer, Cash on arrival, or Other.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Select how you found out about the tournament.
* **Dietary restrictions**: List any dietary restrictions if you will be consuming provided meals.
* **Emergency contact name**: Provide a name and phone number of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Consent to be photographed/streamed**: Confirm if you consent to being photographed or streamed.
* **Waivers and agreements**: Select the option to acknowledge the tournament rules and liability waiver.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or accessibility requests.
