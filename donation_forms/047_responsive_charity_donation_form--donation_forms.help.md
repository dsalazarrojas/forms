# responsive_charity_donation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for users to make online donations to a charitable cause. It allows users to provide their name, email, phone number, and the amount they wish to donate. The information collected will be used to process the donation and may be shared with the charitable organization to facilitate communication and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Amount Donated" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate, in whole numbers only (e.g., 10, 20, 50, etc.).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your donation can be processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact us.
* Your donation will be processed securely and handled according to the charitable organization's policies.
