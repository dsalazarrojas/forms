# Environmental Campaign Sponsorship Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors of environmental campaigns. It helps us understand who is supporting each campaign and ensures that the selected organization and donation type are correctly associated with the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
   - **Select Organization**: Choose the correct organization from the list of available options.
   - **Select Donation Type**: Select the correct type of donation for your organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **organization_name**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **name**: Enter your name.
* **contact_person**: Enter the name of the contact person for your organization.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **address**: Enter your organization's address.
* **city**: Enter the city of your organization's location.
* **zip_code**: Enter your organization's zip code (only numbers, please).
* **campaign_name**: Enter the name of the campaign you are sponsoring.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of your campaign (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of your campaign (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **amount**: Enter the amount of your donation.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes about your donation.
* **select_organization**: Choose your organization from the list of available options. 
* **select_donation_type**: Select the type of donation for your organization (pledges 1, 2, or 3). 

## Tips
- Please ensure that the organization name and contact person are accurate and up-to-date.
- Make sure to select the correct donation type for your organization. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
