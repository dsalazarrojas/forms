# Corporate Donor Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register your company's information for the corporate donor program. It is intended for company representatives who want to join the program. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your company is properly registered in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please follow these steps:
  - Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
  - Make sure to submit the form after completing all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sponsor Information** (`sponsor_info`, `text`, not required): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your company and its involvement in the program.
- **Address** (`address`, `select_one`, not required): Please select one of the following options for your address: Address, Suite/Apt, City, State, or Zip. This will help us understand your company's location.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): This field is for any additional contact information you would like to provide.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your company's phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your company's email address.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, not required): This is your company's name that will be displayed in the program.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter your job title within the company.
- **Job Department** (`job_dept`, `text`, not required): Enter the department you are associated with within the company.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, not required): This is your name as an employee of the company.
- **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, `text`, not required): Enter your title or position within the company.
- **Company Logo** (`company_logo`, `text`, not required): You can enter your company's logo if you have one.

## Tips
- Please make sure to submit the form completely and accurately to ensure that your company is properly registered in the program.
- If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
