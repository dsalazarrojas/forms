# COVID Plasma Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in donating COVID-19 convalescent plasma. The goal is to gather essential details about the donor, including their personal and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are necessary for the donation process.
2.  Be accurate and honest when filling in your personal and medical history.
3.  Ensure that you have the necessary medical clearance and consent before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`donor_name`, text, \*): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, \*): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, \*): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, \*): Enter your phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
*   **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, \*): Provide your street address where you can be contacted.
*   **City, State, ZIP Code** (`city_state_zip`, text, \*): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code for contact and follow-up purposes.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, select_one): Choose your gender from the options provided.
*   **Prior COVID-19 Diagnosis** (`prior_covid_diagnosis`, select_one, \*): If you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, select 'True'. If you have not, select 'False'.
*   **Date of COVID-19 Diagnosis** (`covid_diagnosis_date`, date, ): If you have been diagnosed with COVID-19, enter the date of diagnosis in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Have You Fully Recovered from COVID-19** (`covid_recovery`, select_one, \*): If you have fully recovered from COVID-19, select 'True'. If you have not, select 'False' or 'Mostly' if you are still experiencing symptoms.
*   **Date of Recovery or End of Symptoms** (`recovered_date`, date, ): If you have fully recovered or your symptoms have ended, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Prior COVID Convalescent Plasma Donation** (`prior_plasma_donation`, select_one, \*): If you have donated COVID-19 convalescent plasma before, select 'True'. If you have not, select 'False'.
*   **How Often Have You Donated** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, ): If you have donated blood before, select how often you have done so.
*   **Blood Type** (`blood_type`, select_one, \*): Choose your blood type from the options provided.
*   **Ever Donated Blood Before** (`previous_blood_donation`, select_one, \*): If you have donated blood before, select 'True'. If you have not, select 'False'.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, ): If you have any medical conditions that might affect donation eligibility, enter them in this field.
*   **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, ): If you are currently taking any medications, list them here.
*   **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, text, ): If you have any known allergies, list them here.
*   **Currently Experiencing Illness** (`current_illness`, select_one, \*): If you are currently experiencing any illness or symptoms, select 'True'. If you are not, select 'False'.
*   **I consent to COVID plasma donation** (`consent_agreement`, select_one, \*): Select 'Yes, I consent' if you agree to donate COVID-19 plasma. Select 'No, I do not consent' if you do not agree.

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that you have the necessary medical clearance and consent before proceeding.
*   Be honest and accurate when filling in your personal and medical history.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
