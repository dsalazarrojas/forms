# Braintree Donation Template 3 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for various programs and causes. It is intended for individuals to provide their contact information, donation preferences, and payment details to support their chosen programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Review each field for clarity and correctness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`donor_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`donor_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address for donation receipt.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Address** (`donor_address`, `text`, not required): Enter your complete mailing address.
* **City** (`donor_city`, `text`, not required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`donor_state`, `text`, not required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`donor_zip`, `text`, not required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`donor_country`, `select_one`, not required): Select the country where you reside.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `select_one`, required): Select the amount you wish to donate from the provided options.
* **Custom Amount** (`custom_amount`, `number`, not required): Enter a custom donation amount if not listed in the options.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you would like to donate.
* **Donation Program** (`donation_program`, `select_one`, required): Select which program should receive your donation.
* **Tribute Donation** (`tribute_donation`, `select_one`, required): Select if this donation is in honor or memory of someone.
* **Tribute Name** (`tribute_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person being honored.
* **Tribute Notification** (`tribute_notification`, `select_one`, not required): Select if family should be notified.
* **Tribute Address** (`tribute_address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address for notification.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your employer's name for potential matching gifts.
* **Matching Gift** (`matching_gift`, `select_one`, not required): Select if your employer will match your donation.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method for payment (credit card, debit card, PayPal, or bank account).
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, `text`, not required): Enter the card number for payment.
* **Card Expiry** (`card_expiry`, `text`, not required): Enter the MM/YY of the card expiry.
* **Card CVV** (`card_cvv`, `text`, not required): Enter the security code for payment.
* **Billing Address Same** (`billing_address_same`, `select_one`, not required): Select if the billing address is the same as the above.
* **Anonymous Donation** (`anonymous_donation`, `select_one`, required): Select if you want to remain anonymous.
* **Receipt Preference** (`receipt_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to receive your receipt.
* **Newsletter Signup** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`, not required): Select if you want to receive updates on our work.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any message or dedication for your donation.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Select to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Donation Dedication** (`donation_dedication`, `text`, not required): Enter any special dedication message.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
