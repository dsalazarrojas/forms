# Radio Equipment Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Radio Equipment Donation Form is a simple form used to collect information from donors when they wish to donate their radio equipment. This information helps us process the donation efficiently and ensures that the donor receives credit for their generosity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the "Donor Info" section, including your name and any other details you'd like to provide.
2. Describe the radio equipment you are donating in the "Donation Item" field.
3. Choose a pickup date and time for the donated equipment in the "Pickup Date" and "Pickup Time" fields.
4. Provide the location where the equipment will be picked up in the "Donation Location" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Donor Email" field to help us contact you if we need more information.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Donor Phone" field to ensure we can reach you if needed.
7. Add any additional notes about the donation in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Info (Text)**: Enter your name and any other details you'd like to provide.
* **Donation Item (Text)**: Describe the radio equipment you are donating, including its model and condition.
* **Pickup Date (Date)**: Choose a date when you're available to have the equipment picked up.
* **Pickup Time (Time)**: Select a time when you're available for pickup.
* **Donation Location (Text)**: Enter the address where the equipment will be picked up.
* **Donor Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Donor Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Notes (Note)**: Add any additional comments or details about the donation.
