# software_development_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used for collecting donations for software development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Ensure the purpose of the donation is for software development.
- Fill out the form with the required and optional information as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your first name for contact purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): 
  Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name of the project you are donating to or for.
- **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required: false): 
  Enter the amount of the donation.
- **Purpose** (`purpose`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Select the purpose of the donation from the options: general donation, software development, or other.
- **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter any additional information about your donation.
- **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the contact person's name and title.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the contact information (email, phone number, etc.).
- **Message** (`message`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter any additional message for the developers.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): 
  Select the tool assigned to the project.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, required: false): 
  Enter a unique number for the form submission.
  This field is used for tracking and record-keeping purposes only.

## Tips
- Ensure that the purpose of the donation is for software development.
- Be honest about the purpose of the donation.
- Enter accurate information for contact and communication purposes.
- The assigned tool field is for tracking purposes and not for actual assignment of tools.
