# Donor Tracking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Tracking Survey is a form used to track donor information and follow-up activities. This form helps us understand how often to follow up with donors and what information we need from them to continue our efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
- Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
- For the "tracking_info_2" field, select "Yes" if the donor has provided tracking information, "No" otherwise.
- For the "frequency_2" field, select all frequencies with which you follow up with the donor (e.g., "Yes" for each frequency).
- In the "follow_up_info" field, enter any additional comments or information about the donor.
- Select "Yes" or "No" for "follow_up_method" and "follow_up_frequency" to indicate how you plan to follow up with the donor.
- Finally, enter any additional follow-up notes in the fields provided.
