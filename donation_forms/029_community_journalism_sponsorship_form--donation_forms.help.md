# Community Journalism Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect sponsorship information from individuals or organizations interested in supporting community journalism projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sponsor's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Provide the sponsorship amount or in-kind description.
3. Choose how you want to be recognized as a sponsor.
4. Select the payment method for the sponsorship.
5. Sign the sponsorship agreement, including your name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor name**: This field is required and should be filled out with the name of the company or individual sponsoring the project.
* **Contact person full name**: This field is required and should be filled out with the primary contact person's name at the sponsor's organization.
* **Contact email**: This field is required and should be filled out with the email address of the sponsor's contact person.
* **Sponsorship amount or in-kind description**: This field is required and should be filled out with the sponsorship amount or details of in-kind support.
* **Recognition preference**: Choose how you want to be recognized as a sponsor. You can select from the options: "Logo on website", "Mention in articles", "Event signage", or "Other".
* **Campaign target or project supported**: This field is optional and should be filled out if you want to specify which journalism project you are supporting.
* **Payment method**: This field is required and should be selected from the options: "Bank transfer", "Check", or "Online payment".
* **Authorized signature name**: This field is required and should be filled out with the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Date of signature**: This field is required and should be filled out with the date the sponsorship agreement was signed.
