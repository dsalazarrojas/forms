# Trail Maintenance Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations from individuals who wish to support trail maintenance efforts. Your contributions will help us maintain and improve trails for everyone to enjoy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields below. You can submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Form** (`donation_form`, text, optional): This is the main text field where you can describe your donation, e.g., "I'm donating $10 for trail maintenance".
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number, optional): Please enter the amount you are donating. You can also enter a range or a specific number, e.g., "10" or "10-20".
* **Purpose Donated** (`purpose_donated`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the purpose of your donation from the list:
	+ Trail maintenance
	+ Trail improvements
	+ Other
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you would like to donate:
	+ One Time
	+ Recurring
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted or acknowledged for your donation, please enter your name.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): If you have a email address and would like to receive updates or thank-you messages, please enter it here.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number and would like to be contacted, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit a valid email address and phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for your support!
