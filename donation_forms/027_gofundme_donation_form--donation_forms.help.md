# GoFundMe Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GoFundMe Donation Form is designed to collect donations for different causes, such as Animal Shelter, Environment, Education, Health, and Homelessness. This form allows users to select their preferred cause and payment method, providing a convenient way to support their chosen charity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the required information in the fields that follow, and make sure to select at least one cause and payment method.
2. Fill in your name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Select the amount of your donation by choosing the desired "Donation Amount".
4. Choose whether your donation is One-Time or Recurring.
5. Select your preferred payment method(s) from the available options.
6. Enter your email address for communication and updates.
7. Optionally, provide your phone number and address for further communication.
8. Enter your city, which is required for postal purposes.
9. Finally, review your selections to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to associate with your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Choose the amount you'd like to donate. You can enter a specific amount or use the default amount provided.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Select whether this is a One-Time or Recurring donation.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more payment methods you'd like to use. Select Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer options.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for further communication and updates.
* **Address** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your address for further communication and updates.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city for postal purposes.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code for postal purposes.
* **Cause** (`cause`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more causes you'd like to support. Choose from options: Animal Shelter, Environment, Education, Health, and Homelessness.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your selections before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Select at least one cause and payment method to proceed.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact us for assistance.
