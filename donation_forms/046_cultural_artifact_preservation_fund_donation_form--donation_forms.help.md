# cultural_artifact_preservation_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect donations from individuals or organizations for the preservation of cultural artifacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your organization or institution.
2. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter the amount of your donation.
5. Select a payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
6. Choose the type of donation (One-time or Ongoing).
7. Optionally, select the frequency of giving (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
8. If you have chosen to give on a frequency basis, enter the payment date and time.
9. If you have chosen to give on a frequency basis, select the gift frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
10. Enter the start date of your gift.
11. Optionally, enter any additional notes about your gift frequency.
12. If you are eligible, select whether your gift is tax-deductible.
13. Enter a tax ID number (e.g., for tax deduction purposes).
14. Select the payment terms (Immediate or Deferred).
15. Select the frequency terms (Continuous or One-Time).
16. Enter any additional details about yourself as a donor.
17. Enter the date and time of your payment.
18. Optionally, select the gift frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
