# PayPal Nonprofit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donation information from users for your nonprofit organization. It's meant for individuals who want to donate to your campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
3. Select your desired donation amount using the "Donation Amount" field.
4. Choose how often you'd like to donate using the "Donation Frequency" select_one field.
5. If you choose "Other" for the donation frequency, provide additional information in the "How often?" field.
6. Enter your campaign details, including "Campaign ID", "Campaign Name", and "Campaign URL".
7. Select the status of your donation using the "Donation Status" select_one field.
8. Accept the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" in the "Terms and Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your donation record.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you'll receive confirmation and updates about your donation.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for any reason related to this donation.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to donate (one-time, regularly, or other).
	+ If you choose "Other", provide additional information in the "How often?" field.
* **How often?**: Enter the frequency of your donation (e.g., weekly, monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **Campaign ID**: Enter the campaign ID if you know it.
* **Campaign Name**: Enter the name of the campaign you're supporting.
* **Campaign URL**: Enter the URL of the campaign page.
* **Donation Status**: Choose the status of your donation (active or inactive).
* **Terms and Conditions**: Accept the terms and conditions of your donation by selecting "Yes".

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns with your donation, contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your support to our nonprofit organization!
