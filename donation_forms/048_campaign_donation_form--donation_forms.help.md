# Campaign Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campaign Donation Form is a tool used for campaign donation purposes. It collects contact information, donation details, and demographic data from donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Donor Full Name**, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your **Email Address** for donation receipt purposes.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4. Enter your **Mailing Address**.
5. Choose a **Donation Amount** from the provided options or enter a custom amount if you select "Other".
6. Select the **Donation Frequency**, whether it's a one-time gift, monthly recurring, or quarterly recurring gift.
7. Choose a **Payment Method**, which can be credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer (ACH).
8. Check the **Acknowledgment of Contribution Limits** and certify that your donation does not exceed legal limits.
9. Confirm your **U.S. Citizen Status**, certifying that you are a U.S. citizen or lawfully admitted permanent resident.
10. Set a **Date of Donation** using the date field.
11. Opt-in for **Campaign Updates** by selecting "Yes, I want updates".
12. Provide a **Public Comment** (optional), which may be displayed on our donation wall.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Full Name (Donor Full Name)**: Enter your full name, including first and last name.
- **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter your email address for donation receipt purposes.
- **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Mailing Address (Mailing Address)**: Enter your mailing address.
- **Donation Amount (Donation Amount)**: Choose a donation amount from the options or enter a custom amount if you select "Other".
- **Donation Frequency (Donation Frequency)**: Select the frequency of your donation.
- **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Choose how you want to make the payment.
- **Acknowledgment of Contribution Limits (Acknowledgment of Contribution Limits)**: Check that your donation does not exceed legal limits.
- **U.S. Citizen Status (U.S. Citizen - Green Card Holder Certification)**: Confirm your U.S. citizenship status.
- **Date of Donation (Date of Donation)**: Set the date of your donation.
- **Campaign Updates (Opt-in for Campaign Updates)**: Opt-in for campaign updates.
- **Public Comment (Public Comment (Optional))**: Provide a public comment (optional).
