# Nonprofit Transparency Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Transparency Declaration Form is designed for non-profit organizations to submit a transparency declaration, which is a document that provides information about an organization's financial and leadership details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization Name" field.
2. Enter your organization's EIN number in the "EIN Number" field.
3. Provide your organization's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your city in the "City" field.
5. Enter your state in the "State" field.
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
7. Enter the name of your executive director in the "Executive Director" field.
8. Enter the name of your financial officer in the "Financial Officer" field.
9. Enter the name of your chief finance officer in the "Chief Finance Officer" field.
10. Select your board members from the list in the "Board Members" field.
11. Enter any conflicts of interest in the "Conflict of Interest" field.
12. Attach a financial statement in the "Financial Statement" field, if applicable.
13. Attach an annual report, if applicable.
14. Provide a transparency declaration in the "Transparency Declaration" field.
15. Finally, select the status of your review and approval in the "Review and Approval" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, optional, text): Enter the name of your non-profit organization.
* **EIN Number** (`ein_number`, optional, text): Enter your organization's Employer Identification Number (EIN) if you have one.
* **Address** (`address`, optional, text): Provide your organization's physical address.
* **City** (`city`, optional, text): Enter the city where your organization is located.
* **State** (`state`, optional, text): Enter the state where your organization is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, optional, text): Enter the zip code of your organization's location.
* **Executive Director** (`executive_director`, optional, text): Enter the name of your executive director.
* **Financial Officer** (`financial_officer`, optional, text): Enter the name of your financial officer.
* **Chief Finance Officer** (`chief_finance_officer`, optional, text): Enter the name of your chief finance officer.
* **Board Members** (`board_members`, optional, select_multiple): Select the names of your board members from the list.
* **Conflict of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, optional, text): Enter any conflicts of interest that your organization has.
* **Financial Statement** (`financial_statement`, optional, text): Attach a financial statement, if applicable.
* **Annual Report** (`annual_report`, optional, text): Attach an annual report, if applicable.
* **Transparency Declaration** (`transparency_declaration`, optional, text): Provide a statement declaring your organization's transparency.
* **Review and Approval** (`review_and_approval`, optional, select_one): Select the status of your review and approval process.
