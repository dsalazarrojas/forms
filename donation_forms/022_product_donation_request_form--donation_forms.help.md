# Product Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Donation Request Form is used to request donations of products from various organizations. This form helps in tracking and managing the donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Make sure you have permission to use this form from your authorized personnel.
* Review all fields carefully and only fill in the relevant fields that pertain to your specific needs.
* Fill in the Donatenumber, if you know your organization's unique ID.
* Fill in your name, title, and email or phone number so that the request can be directed back to you.
* Select the type of product you need to donate (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
* Select the method of pickup (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
* Select the category of the product (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
* You may be asked to provide some additional information, which may include the number of products, the unit price per product, the total amount, the pickup date, and the time of pickup. If you do not know this information, it's best to provide an estimate or leave it blank.
* You can optionally provide additional details about the product and the reasons for your request. 
* Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donatenumber (1)**: Enter your organization's unique ID number.
* **DonorName (2)**: Enter your name and title.
* **DonorType (3)**: Select the type of donor you are (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Organization (4)**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **ProductName (5)**: Enter the name of the product you need to donate.
* **Quantity (6)**: Enter the number of products you need.
* **UnitCost (7)**: Enter the unit price per product.
* **TotalAmount (8)**: Enter the total amount of the donation.
* **PickupLocation (9)**: Enter the location where you want to pick up the product.
* **PickupDate (10)**: Enter the date and time you want to pick up the product.
* **PickupTime (11)**: Enter the time you want to pick up the product.
* **Notes (12)**: Provide any additional information about the product or the donation.
* **Email (13)**: Enter your email address or phone number.
* **Phone (14)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Options (15)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any options or requirements.
* **DonorTag (16)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are a donor or not.
* **PickupMethod (17)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the method of pickup.
* **ProductCategory (18)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the category of the product.
* **OrganizationID (19)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you need help with the organization ID.
* **Status (20)**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of your request.
* **ReasonForRequest (21)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the reason for the request.
* **Notes 2 (22)**: Provide any additional information about the product or the donation.
* **Assigned (23)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if someone is assigned to help you with the request.
* **AssignedTool (24)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are using a specific tool for the request.
* **ReviewStatus (25)**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of your request's review.

Note: The fields may vary based on your specific needs and organization's requirements. This help guide is to assist you in filling out the form correctly. If you need further assistance or have questions, contact your authorized personnel.
