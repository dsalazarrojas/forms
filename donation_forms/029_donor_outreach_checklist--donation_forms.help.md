# Donor Outreach Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about donors for outreach purposes, allowing organizations to manage and track their donor relationships effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Donor Name** in the text field, ensuring that the name is spelled correctly.
2. Select the **Relationship** between the organization and the donor from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the **Email** address of the donor, if available.
4. Choose the **Contact Method** that applies to the donor (Phone, Email, Letter, or Social Media).
5. Enter any additional **Notes** about the donor.
6. Check the **Follow up** status (Follow up scheduled, Follow up completed, or Not scheduled).
7. Enter the **Last Contacted** date, if applicable.
8. Select the **Contact Frequency** (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Never).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (`donor_outreach_checklist`, Text, Required: No): Enter the name of the donor.
* **Relationship** (`donor_relationship`, Select, Required: No): This field is used to identify the relationship between the donor and the organization (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary).
* **Email** (`donor_email`, Email, Required: No): Enter the email address of the donor, if available.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Choose the contact method that applies to the donor (Phone, Email, Letter, Social Media).
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Required: No): Enter any additional notes about the donor.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up_status`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Check the follow up status (Follow up scheduled, Follow up completed, Not scheduled).
* **Last Contacted** (`last_contacted`, Date, Required: No): Enter the date of the last contacted.
* **Contact Frequency** (`frequency_of_contact`, Select, Required: No): Select the frequency of contact (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Never).
