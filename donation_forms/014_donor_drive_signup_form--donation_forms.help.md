# Donor Drive Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Drive Signup Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in supporting a specific campaign or cause. The form gathers details such as name, email, phone number, address, and other relevant data to help track and follow up with supporters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), and provide accurate information for each field.
3. Select the correct options for the fields that allow multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Drive Signup** (`donor_drive_signup_form`, Text): Enter a brief description of the campaign or cause you are interested in supporting.
- **Supporter Info** (`supporter_info`, Text): Enter a brief description of why you are interested in supporting this campaign or cause.
- **Supporter Signups** (`supporter_signups`, Select Multiple):
	+ Select all the ways you are interested in being contacted, such as Yes, No, Yes, via phone, Yes, via email, or Yes, via mail.
- **Outreach Preferences** (`outreach_preferences`, Text): Enter any additional information about your outreach preferences.
- **Name** (`name`, Text): Enter your full name.
- **Email** (`email`, Email): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, Text): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (`address`, Text): Enter your address.
- **Drive ID** (`drive_id`, Number): Enter the ID number associated with this campaign or cause.
- **Donor ID** (`donor_id`, Number): Enter your donor ID number.
- **Campaign ID** (`campaign_id`, Number): Enter the ID number associated with this campaign or cause.
- **Campaign URL** (`campaign_url`, Text): Enter the URL of the campaign or cause you are supporting.
- **Signup Date** (`signup_date`, Date): Enter the date you signed up to support this campaign or cause.
- **Signup Time** (`signup_time`, Time): Enter the time you signed up to support this campaign or cause.
- **Donor Type** (`donor_type`, Select One):
	+ Select Regular Donor, Recurring Donor, or In-Memoriam Donor based on your intentions.
- **Follow up** (`follow_up`, Select One):
	+ Select how you want to be followed up, such as No Follow up, Yes, via phone, Yes, via email, or Yes, via mail.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrator for assistance.
- This form is designed to collect information to help track and follow up with supporters.
