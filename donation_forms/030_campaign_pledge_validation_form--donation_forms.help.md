# Campaign Pledge Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and validate pledges to a campaign, ensuring accurate and timely communication with donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the donor's first name in the "Donor First Name" field.
*   Enter the donor's last name in the "Donor Last Name" field.
*   Enter the donor's email address in the "Donor Email" field.
*   Enter the donor's phone number in the "Donor Phone" field.
*   Enter the donor's street address in the "Donor Address" field.
*   Enter the city where the donor is located in the "City" field.
*   Enter the state or province where the donor is located in the "State" field.
*   Enter the donor's zip code in the "ZIP Code" field.
*   Enter the name of the campaign in the "Campaign Name" field.
*   Enter the pledge amount in the "Pledge Amount" field.
*   Select the currency of the pledge in the "Currency" field.
*   Select the type of pledge in the "Pledge Type" field.
*   Enter the start date of the pledge in the "Pledge Start Date" field.
*   Enter the end date of the pledge, if applicable, in the "Pledge End Date" field.
*   Select the preferred payment method in the "Preferred Payment Method" field.
*   Select the purpose of the pledge in the "Pledge Purpose" field.
*   If the pledge purpose is "Other", provide a description in the "If Other - Specify" field.
*   If the employer matches this gift, select "Yes - will submit" in the "Is Your Employer Matching This Gift" field.
*   Indicate whether you wish to remain anonymous in the "Do You Wish to Remain Anonymous" field.
*   Select how you would like to be acknowledged in the "How Would You Like to Be Acknowledged" field.
*   If follow-up is needed, select "Yes - schedule call" in the "Is Follow-up Needed" field.
*   If follow-up is needed, enter the date in the "Follow-up Date" field.
*   Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
