# Assistive Technology Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for assistive technology donations. It's intended for individuals or organizations that wish to donate equipment, such as wheelchairs, hearing aids, or other assistive devices, or make a monetary donation to support our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your donor information, including your name, email address, and phone number (if desired).
2.  Select the type of assistive technology you wish to donate (e.g., Wheelchair, Hearing Aid, etc.).
3.  If donating physical equipment, provide its condition (e.g., New, Like New, Good Condition, etc.).
4.  Describe the equipment you are donating, including its brand, model, and specifications.
5.  Enter the estimated value of the equipment (if applicable).
6.  Indicate the amount of your monetary donation (if applicable).
7.  Choose your preferred delivery method (e.g., Drop Off at Facility, Schedule Pickup, etc.).
8.  Select your preferred donation date.
9.  If you have a preference for who receives the donation (e.g., Children, Adults, etc.), indicate that here.
10. Indicate whether you would like a tax-deductible receipt for your donation.
11. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them.
12. Finally, indicate whether you would like to receive updates about our programs via our newsletter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donor Information** (`donor_information`): This field is for your personal contact details.
*   **Email Address** (`donor_email`): Enter your email address to receive a donation receipt and updates from our organization.
*   **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
*   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): If you are donating on behalf of an organization, please enter its name.
*   **Type of Donation** (`donation_type`): Select the type of assistive technology you are donating (e.g., Wheelchair, Hearing Aid, etc.).
*   **Condition of Equipment** (`device_condition`): If you are donating physical equipment, indicate its condition (e.g., New, Like New, Good Condition, etc.).
*   **Equipment Description** (`device_description`): Describe the equipment you are donating, including its brand, model, and specifications.
*   **Estimated Value in USD** (`estimated_value`): Approximate the value of the equipment for tax deduction purposes (if applicable).
*   **Monetary Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`): Enter the amount of your monetary donation (if applicable).
*   **Preferred Delivery Method** (`preferred_delivery`): Choose your preferred delivery method (e.g., Drop Off at Facility, Schedule Pickup, etc.).
*   **Preferred Donation Date** (`donation_date`): Select a date for the donation.
*   **Recipient Preference** (`recipient_preference`): If you have a preference for who receives the donation, indicate that here.
*   **Tax Receipt Needed** (`tax_receipt_needed`): Indicate whether you need a tax-deductible receipt for your donation.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional information you would like to share about your donation.
*   **Newsletter Signup** (`newsletter_signup`): Choose whether you would like to receive updates about our programs via our newsletter.
