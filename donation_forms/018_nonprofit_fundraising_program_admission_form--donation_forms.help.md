# Nonprofit Fundraising Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Fundraising Program Admission Form is designed to collect information from potential participants and event organizers. This form is intended for both individuals and organizations looking to participate in or organize fundraising events for the nonprofit program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and enter the requested information.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields, marked as "required".
3. If a field is optional, you may choose to enter information or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info**: Provide a brief description of the participant or participant group (e.g., "Individuals", "Organization A", etc.).
* **Contact Info**: Enter the email address of the participant or organization contact person.
* **Event Info**: Select the specific fundraising event you are interested in participating in or organizing.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the participant or organization contact person.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the participant or organization.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the participant or organization.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate to the nonprofit program.
* **Fundraising Event**: Select the specific fundraising event you are interested in participating in or organizing.
* **Participant Type**: Select the type of participant you are (e.g., individual, organization, etc.).
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant or organization representative.
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., charity, non-profit, etc.).
