# Empowerment Program Donation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for the Empowerment Program, a initiative that supports various focus areas such as training and education, micro-grants, operational support, and the general fund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the donor.
2. Provide your email address for tax receipt and communication purposes.
3. Enter the numeric amount of your donation.
4. Select the focus area for your donation from the provided options.
5. Choose the frequency of your donation (one time, monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
6. (Optional) Choose to make your donation anonymous.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor name (Donor name)** (`donor_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as the donor to the Empowerment Program.
* **Email Address (Email address)** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for tax receipt and communication purposes.
* **Donation Amount (Donation amount)** (`amount`, Number, Required): Enter the numeric amount of your donation.
* **Donation Purpose (Donation purpose)** (`donation_purpose`, Select One, Optional): Select the focus area for your donation from the options provided.
* **Recurring Donation (Set up recurring donation)** (`recurring`, Select One, Required): Choose the frequency of your donation (one time, monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
* **Anonymity (Make donation anonymous)** (`anonymous`, Select One, Optional): Choose to make your donation anonymous, and your name will not be displayed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is accurate for tax receipt and communication purposes.
* If you are setting up a recurring donation, select the frequency of your choice.
* If you choose to make your donation anonymous, your name will not be displayed.
* Double-check your donation amount to ensure it is numeric.
