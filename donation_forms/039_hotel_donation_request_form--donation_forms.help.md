# Hotel Donation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for guests or attendees to make a donation to an event held at or by the hotel. The form collects personal contact information, event details, and donation details to ensure correct donation processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (Text): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (Text): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
*   **Donation type** (Select One): Select the type of donation you are making (e.g., Hotel stay, Monetary, Goods).
*   **Event date** (Date): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event time** (Time): Enter the time of the event.
*   **Event location** (Text): Enter the location of the event.
*   **Event description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the event.
*   **Event organizer** (Text): Enter the name of the event organizer.
*   **Event organizer email** (Email): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
*   **Event organizer phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
*   **Event organizer address** (Text): Enter the physical address of the event organizer.
*   **Donation amount** (Number): Enter the amount of the donation.
*   **Donation frequency** (Select One): Select the frequency of the donation (e.g., Once, Multiple times, Ongoing).
*   **Donor notes** (Note): Add any notes or comments about the donation.
*   **Additional comments** (Text): If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.
