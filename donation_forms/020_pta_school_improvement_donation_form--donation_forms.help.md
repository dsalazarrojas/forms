# PTA School Improvement Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for donations to the PTA School Improvement program. It allows users to provide details about their donations, including the amount donated, the purpose of the donation, and any additional comments or information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the amount donated in the **Amount Donated** field.
2. Select the **Purpose of Donation** field to indicate why the donation is being made.
3. Enter your **Donor Name** and **Donor E-mail** if you are the donor.
4. If you are a PTA board member, indicate this in the **Are you a PTA board member?** field.
5. If you are a member of the PTA, enter your **PTA Position** in the **PTA Position** field.
6. If you are donating on behalf of a student, enter the student's **Student Name**, **Student Grade**, and any additional **Student Information**.
7. If you are donating on behalf of a parent, enter the parent's **Parent Name**, **Parent E-mail**, and any additional **Parent Comments**.
8. Enter any details about the **PTA Event** you are sponsoring, if applicable.
9. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Amount Donated** (amount_donated, number, required)
  * This is the amount of the donation being made.

### 2. **Purpose of Donation** (purpose_of_donation, select_one, required)
  * Select one of the following options to indicate the purpose of the donation:
    * School Improvement
    * Other
    * PTA Events

### 3. **Donor Name** (donor_name, text, optional)
  * Enter your name as the donor.

### 4. **Donor E-mail** (donor_email, email, optional)
  * Enter your email address if you are the donor.

### 5. **Donor Phone** (donor_phone, text, optional)
  * Enter your phone number if you are the donor.

### 6. **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, note, optional)
  * Enter any additional comments or information about the donation.

### 7. **Are you a PTA board member?** (pta_board_member, select_multiple, optional)
  * Check one or more of the following:
    * Yes
    * No
    * Not sure

### 8. **PTA Position** (pta_position, select_one, optional)
  * Select one of the following options if you are a member of the PTA:
    * Yes
    * No

### 9. **PTA School Name** (pta_school_name, text, optional)
  * Enter the name of the school associated with the PTA.

### 10. **Student Name** (student_name, text, optional)
  * Enter the name of the student the donation is for.

### 11. **Student Grade** (student_grade, select_one, optional)
  * Select one of the following options for the student's grade:
    * Yes
    * No

### 12. **Student Class** (student_class, text, optional)
  * Enter the student's class.

### 13. **Student Teacher** (student_teacher, text, optional)
  * Enter the student's teacher.

### 14. **Other Student Information** (student_other, text, optional)
  * Enter any other information about the student.

### 15. **Parent Name** (parent_name, text, optional)
  * Enter the parent's name if donating on their behalf.

### 16. **Parent E-mail** (parent_email, email, optional)
  * Enter the parent's email address.

### 17. **Parent Phone** (parent_phone, text, optional)
  * Enter the parent's phone number.

### 18. **Additional Parent Comments** (additional_parent_comments, note, optional)
  * Enter any additional comments about the parent or the donation.

### 19. **PTA Event Name** (pta_event_name, text, optional)
  * Enter the name of the PTA event.

### 20. **PTA Event Description** (pta_event_description, text, optional)
  * Enter a brief description of the PTA event.

### 21. **PTA Event Date** (pta_event_date, date, optional)
  * Enter the date of the PTA event.

### 22. **PTA Event Time** (pta_event_time, time, optional)
  * Enter the time of the PTA event.

### 23. **PTA Event Location** (pta_event_location, text, optional)
  * Enter the location of the PTA event.

### 24. **PTA Event Organizer** (pta_event_organizer, text, optional)
  * Enter the person organizing the PTA event.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling in the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can leave them blank or contact the PTA for guidance.
* This form is designed for tracking and management of donations. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the PTA.
