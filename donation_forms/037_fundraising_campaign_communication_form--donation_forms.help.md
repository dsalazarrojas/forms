# Fundraising Campaign Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fundraising campaign communication form is used to collect information from users for the purpose of fundraising campaigns. This form captures the user's details, contact information, and preferences for communication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Campaign Details" section with the relevant information about the campaign.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" section, including your email address and phone number.
3. Select your preferred communication method and additional comments in the "Communication Method" and "Additional Comments" sections.
4. If applicable, provide notes and comments about your interaction with the user.
5. Confirm whether follow-up is necessary and indicate your donation details and social media preferences.
6. Enter any additional contact information, such as a website, address, and language spoken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Details** (Campaign Details, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of the fundraising campaign.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, number, **required**): Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach out to you.
* **Email** (Email, email, **required**): Enter your email address so we can send you updates about the campaign.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, **required**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
* **Communication Method** (Communication Method, select_one, **not required**): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or text).
* **Preferred Method** (Preferred Method, select_multiple, **not required**): Select any additional communication methods you prefer (e.g., email, phone, or text).
* **Notes** (Notes, note, **not required**): Enter any notes or comments about your interaction with the user.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, **not required**): Enter any comments or feedback about the campaign.
* **Date** (Date, date, **not required**): Enter the date of your interaction with the user.
* **Time** (Time, time, **not required**): Enter the time of your interaction with the user.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, select_multiple, **not required**): Select any additional comments or preferences.
* **Follow Up** (Follow Up, select_multiple, **not required**): Confirm whether follow-up is necessary.
* **Donation Details** (Donation Details, select_multiple, **not required**): Indicate your donation details, if applicable.
* **Social Media** (Social Media, select_multiple, **not required**): Select your social media preferences.
* **Website** (Website, text, **not required**): Enter the website associated with your name or organization.
* **Address** (Address, text, **not required**): Enter your address for future reference.
* **City** (City, text, **not required**): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (State, text, **not required**): Enter the state where you are located.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code, text, **not required**): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (Country, text, **not required**): Enter the country where you are located.
* **Language** (Language, text, **not required**): Enter the language spoken.
* **Email2** (Email2, email, **not required**): Enter an email address for another person or organization associated with you.
* **Phone2** (Phone2, text, **not required**): Enter a phone number for another person or organization associated with you.
* **Communication Method2** (Communication Method2, select_multiple, **not required**): Select how you would like to be contacted by the second person or organization.
* **Additional Comments2** (Additional Comments2, select_multiple, **not required**): Select any additional comments or preferences for the second person or organization.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Select only one communication method for each field.
* Be specific when entering any additional comments or preferences.
* Check for any typos or errors before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
