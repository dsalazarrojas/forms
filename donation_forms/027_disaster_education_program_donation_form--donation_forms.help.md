# Disaster Education Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Education Program Donation Form is designed to collect donations for individuals and families affected by natural disasters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your Name in the text field.
3. Enter your Email address in the email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the text field.
5. Select your desired donation Amount from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose the Donation Method you prefer (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
7. If applicable, add any Additional Information about your donation.
8. Optionally, select the Category of your donation (General or Disaster Recovery).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form ID
* **Form ID** (Form ID, number, optional): This is the unique ID of the form, which helps us to track your submission. Please do not fill this field unless you are required to do so by our team.

### Name
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to in our records.

### Email
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and confirmations.

### Phone
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### Donation Amount
* **Donation Amount** (Donation Amount, number, optional): Choose the amount you would like to donate. You can select from the provided options or enter a custom amount.

### Donation Method
* **Donation Method** (Donation Method, select_one, optional): Select the method you prefer to make your donation. You can choose from Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.

### Date
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): If you are making a one-time donation, please provide the date you are making the donation. If you are making a recurring donation, please ignore this field.

### Time
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): If you are making a one-time donation, please provide the time you are making the donation. If you are making a recurring donation, please ignore this field.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, note, optional): Add any relevant details about your donation, if you would like to.

### Category
* **Category** (Category, select_multiple, optional): Select all the categories that apply to your donation. You can choose from General or Disaster Recovery.
