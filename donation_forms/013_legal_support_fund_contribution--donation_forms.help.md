# legal_support_fund_contribution - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for the legal support fund. It is meant to gather relevant information from users to facilitate the processing and tracking of donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the "legal_support_fund_contribution" form.
2. Fill in the donation details in the "Donation Details" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
6. Enter the amount of the donation in the "Amount ($)" field. This will be used to calculate the total donation amount.
7. If applicable, provide a description of your donation in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Details**: Enter a description or details about your donation, if any.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the donation receipt.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: Optional
* **Amount ($)**: Enter the amount of your donation.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: Optional
* **Description**: Provide a description or reason for your donation, if any.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Optional

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your donation, feel free to contact us at our support email.
* If you would like to track your donation, please keep your receipt for reference.
