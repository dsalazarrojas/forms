# Toys For Tots Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals or organizations wishing to make a donation to the Toys For Tots charity. It collects information about the donor, including their name, contact details, and donation specifics. Please complete the form only if you are a legitimate donor and do not need to duplicate your application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in each field according to the instructions below.
3. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name as the sponsor of the donation.
* **Address** (text, required: false): Enter your address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Zip Code** (number, required: false): Enter your postal/zip code.
* **Donation Amount** (number, required: false): Enter the monetary amount you wish to donate.
* **Purpose of Donation** (text, required: false): Describe the reason or purpose for your donation.
* **Number of Gift Cards** (text, required: false): Enter the number of gift cards you wish to purchase or donate.
* **Number of Toys Requested** (number, required: false): Enter the number of toys you wish to donate.
* **Description of Toys Requested** (text, required: false): Describe the type of toys you wish to donate.
