# Quick Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quick Donation Form is a simple, user-friendly interface for making donations to various funds. This guide will walk you through each field to help you get started.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the fund you wish to support by choosing an option from the "Select Fund" field.
2.  Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field.
3.  If you have a message or note for the organization, type it in the "Message to Organizations" field.
4.  If you have an affiliation or group affiliation, select it from the "Affiliation" dropdown.
5.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field to enable us to contact you.
6.  Optional: If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7.  Add any additional message or details in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donation Form**: A text field where you can enter a brief description of your donation or any additional information. (Optional)
- **Select Fund**: Choose which fund you wish to support with your donation. 
- **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation. Please use only numbers and decimal places for accurate calculations. 
- **Message to Organizations**: Type a message for the organization or recipient of your donation, if desired. 
- **Affiliation**: Select your affiliation or group you represent if applicable. 
- **Email**: Provide your email address to enable us to contact you, if necessary.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone. 
- **Message**: Add any additional comments or details regarding your donation.
