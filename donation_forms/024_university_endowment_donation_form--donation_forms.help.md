# University Endowment Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Endowment Donation Form is used to collect donations from individuals and organizations to support the university's endowment. Donations can be made one-time or on a recurring basis, and can include additional donations. The form captures contact information, donation amount, frequency, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your donor type (Individual Donor, Organization, or Other).
2. Enter your contact number.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Specify the donation amount.
5. Choose the donation frequency (One Time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
6. Indicate if you would like to make additional donations.
7. Select the payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Check).
8. Enter the payment date.
9. Enter the payment time.
10. Add any additional notes about your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **University Endowment Donation Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Donor Information**: Select your donor type (Individual Donor, Organization, or Other).
	+ This field determines how your donation will be acknowledged and recognized.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
	+ This field is required to ensure we can contact you after your donation.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ This field is required to ensure we can contact you after your donation.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation.
	+ This field is required to process your donation.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your donation (One Time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
	+ This field determines how often you will be making this donation.
* **Additional Donations**: Indicate if you would like to make additional donations.
	+ This field allows you to specify if you want to make one-time or recurring donations.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Check).
	+ This field determines how your donation will be processed.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of your payment.
	+ This field is required to process your donation.
* **Payment Time**: Enter the time of your payment.
	+ This field is required to process your donation.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.
	+ This field is optional and for your reference.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate to ensure we can contact you after your donation.
* Please specify the correct payment method and payment date to process your donation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
