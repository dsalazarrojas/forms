# Donation Receipt Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users requesting a donation receipt. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and any relevant contact information in the "user" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the donation you are requesting in the "donation" field.
3. Enter the amount of the donation in the "amount" field.
4. Include a description of the receipt you are requesting in the "receipt" field.
5. Enter the date of the donation in the "date" field.
6. Enter the time of the donation in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **user (Text)** (Required/Optional)
* Enter your name and any relevant contact information to help the processing team quickly identify and contact you.
### **donation (Text)** (Required/Optional)
* Briefly describe the donation you are requesting, including any relevant details.
### **amount (Number)** (Required/Optional)
* Enter the exact amount of the donation you are making.
### **receipt (Text)** (Required/Optional)
* Describe the type of receipt you are requesting, such as a physical receipt or an emailed receipt.
### **date (Date)** (Required/Optional)
* Enter the date of the donation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### **time (Time)** (Required/Optional)
* Enter the time of the donation in the format 12:00:00 PM.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any additional information or questions, please include it in the "donation" field. 
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the support team for assistance.
