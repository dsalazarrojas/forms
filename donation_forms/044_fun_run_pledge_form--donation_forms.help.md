# Fun Run Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information from supporters of a fun run event participant. The form will be used to collect contact and demographic details from the supporter, as well as any additional donation details such as payment method and amount.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Participation Status**: Check if you have a relationship with the participant. If you have a relationship, select "Yes" and fill in the relationship details in the box below.
2.  **Supporter Name**: Enter the name of the supporter who is pledging to support the participant.
3.  **Supporter Email**: Enter the email address of the supporter. This will be used to send updates and information to the supporter.
4.  **Supporter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supporter.
5.  **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount the supporter is pledging to donate.
6.  **Message to Pledgee**: Enter a personal message for the participant.
7.  **Payment Method**: Check if the supporter is paying by bank transfer, credit card, or PayPal.
8.  **Supporter Signature**: The supporter must sign their name in this field to authenticate their pledge.
9.  **Supporter Details**: Enter any additional details about the supporter.
10. **Donor/Supporter Relationship**: Check if the supporter is a donor or supporter of the participant.
11. **Donor Organisation**: If the supporter is a donor, enter the name of their organisation.
12.  **Supporter Address**: Enter the supporter's address.
13.  **Supporter City**: Enter the city of the supporter's address.
14.  **Supporter Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the supporter's address.
15.  **Supporter Country**: Enter the country of the supporter's address.
16.  **Supporter State**: Enter the state of the supporter's address.
