# youth_empowerment_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit their donations to the Youth Empowerment Fund. Please fill out the form with accurate information to help us process your donation efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all required information.
2. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide about your donation.
* **Donations Amount**: Enter the amount you'd like to donate. This field requires a numerical value.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select how often you'd like to make donations. Options are:
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Yearly
* **Donation Frequency (If Other)**: If you don't see a suitable option in the previous field, you can provide a custom frequency in this text box.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment method you'd like to use for your donation. Options are:
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
* **Payment Method**: Select how you'd like to make your payment. Options are:
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Other (please specify in the text box)
* **Billing Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your billing statement.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address.
* **Billing Phone**: Enter your billing phone number.
* **Payment Method (If Other)**: If you don't see a suitable option in the previous field, you can select "Other" and provide details in this text box.
* **Donations Date**: Select the date you'd like to make your donation.
* **Donations Time**: Select the time you'd like to make your donation.
