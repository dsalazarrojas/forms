# Support Local Agriculture Initiative - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from users to support the "Local Agriculture Initiative". It's essential to complete this form accurately to help us understand your interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the volunteer sign-up option that best suits your interests. You can choose one or more options from the list.
2. Enter the amount you would like to pledge for the initiative. You can use numerical values only.
3. Choose the donation method(s) you would like to use for your pledge. Select one or more options from the list.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Enter your phone number for further contact.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments about your participation in the initiative.
7. Select the type(s) of volunteer work you would like to be involved in. You can choose one or more options from the list.
8. Choose your preferred contact method for updates and information about the initiative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Signups** (`Volunteer Signups`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the volunteer sign-ups that interest you. Select one or more options from the list.
* **Pledge Amount** (`Pledge Amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you would like to pledge for the initiative.
* **Donation Method** (`Donation Method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the donation method that suits you best. Select one or more options from the list.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your participation in the initiative.
* **Volunteer Type** (`Volunteer Type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the volunteer type that interests you. Select one or more options from the list.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred contact method for updates and information about the initiative.
