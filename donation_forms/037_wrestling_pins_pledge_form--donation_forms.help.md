# wrestling_pins_pledge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a pledge to support a specific wrestling event or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Pledge Amount** you would like to pledge.
2.  Enter the **Pin Amount** you would like to pledge.
3.  Enter the **Wrestler Name** of the event or program you are supporting.
4.  Select the **Pledge Frequency** of your pledge (Once, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
5.  Enter the **Pledge Start Date** of your pledge.
6.  Enter the **Pledge End Date** of your pledge.
7.  Enter your **Email** address to contact you.
8.  Enter your **Phone** number to contact you.
9.  Select any **Additional Instruments** you would like to support.
10.  Enter any **Note** about your pledge.
11.  Ensure you have checked the **Required Fields** select box with all the mandatory fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pledge Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to pledge.
* **Pin Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to support.
* **Wrestler Name**: Enter the name of the wrestler or event you are supporting.
* **Pledge Frequency**: Select how often you would like to make your pledge (Once, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Pledge Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your pledge.
* **Pledge End Date**: Enter the date you would like to end your pledge.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Additional Instrument**: Select any additional instruments you would like to support.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about your pledge.
* **Required Fields**: Ensure you have selected all the mandatory fields for the form to be valid.
