# Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Form is a tool for users to provide their donation information. It is designed to be completed by individuals who wish to make a donation to a specific cause or campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Form** (`donation_form`, text, required: false)
This field is the main text box where you can provide your donation message or reason for giving. You can type in any information you'd like to share with the recipient.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required: false)
Enter the amount you'd like to donate. Please enter a numerical value in the format of a currency amount (e.g. $10, $20, etc.).
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required: false)
Select whether this is a one-time donation or if you'd like to repeat it.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the date you'd like to make your donation. Please use the calendar format (mm/dd/yyyy).
