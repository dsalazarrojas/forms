# Stripe Orphanage Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their donations to the Stripe Orphanage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the amount you wish to donate.
2. Choose your preferred payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
3. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. Provide your phone number for further communication.
5. Choose the type of donation you would like to make (General Donation, Monthly Subscription, or Recurring Donation).
6. Select the frequency of your recurring donation (Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
7. Enter your name for identification purposes.
8. Leave a message or comment about your donation (optional).
9. Confirm that you have completed your donation details correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate to the Stripe Orphanage.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to make your donation (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for the Stripe Orphanage to contact you.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Donation Type**: Select the type of donation you would like to make (General Donation, Monthly Subscription, or Recurring Donation).
* **Recurring Donation Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to make your recurring donation (Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Message**: Leave a message or comment about your donation (optional).
* **Confirm Donation**: Select 'True' to confirm that you have completed your donation details correctly.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
- If you have any concerns or issues, contact the Stripe Orphanage's support team for assistance.
