# Solar Community Center Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Community Center Donation Form is designed to collect information from potential donors about their donation preferences and details. This form is used to gather essential data to process and manage donations for the Solar Community Center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Information** on page 1, which includes your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred **Donation Amount** on page 1.
3. Select your **Project Type** on page 1, which will help us understand the purpose of your donation.
4. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** on page 1, which can be credit card, bank transfer, or PayPal.
5. Provide your **Email** and **Phone** on page 2, which will be used for communication and confirmation purposes.
6. Add any **Additional Notes** on page 2 to specify any special requests or comments.
7. Confirm your **Donation Amount** on page 2, if you have any.
8. Choose whether you want to make a one-time, ongoing, or in-kind donation by selecting your **Donation Type** on page 2.
9. Select the **Yearly** option on page 2, which will indicate if you want to make an annual donation.
10. Finally, enter the date and time you are willing to make the donation or visit us, including the **Date**, **Time**, and **Year** on page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information (Page 1)
* **User Information (Page 1)**: This field is used to collect your name, email address, and phone number for further contact and communication purposes.

### Donation Amount (Page 1)
* **Donation Amount (Page 1)**: This field is used to specify the amount you are willing to donate. Please enter a numerical value in this field.

### Project Type (Page 1)
* **Project Type (Page 1)**: This select option is used to determine the purpose of your donation. Please select one of the following options:
    + Solar Energy
    + Wind Energy
    + Other

### Payment Method (Page 1)
* **Payment Method (Page 1)**: This select option is used to determine your preferred payment method. Please select one of the following options:
    + Credit Card
    + Bank Transfer
    + PayPal

### Email (Page 2)
* **Email (Page 2)**: This field is used to collect your email address for further communication and confirmation purposes.

### Phone (Page 2)
* **Phone (Page 2)**: This field is used to collect your phone number for further communication and confirmation purposes.

### Additional Notes (Page 2)
* **Additional Notes (Page 2)**: This field is used to add any special comments or requests you have regarding your donation.

### Donation Amount (Page 2)
* **Donation Amount (Page 2)**: This field is used to confirm your donation amount. Please enter the same value as in the **Donation Amount (Page 1)** field.

### Payment Method (Page 2)
* **Payment Method (Page 2)**: This select option is used to confirm your preferred payment method. Please select one of the following options:
    + Yes

### Time (Page 2)
* **Time (Page 2)**: This field is used to specify the time you are willing to make the donation. Please enter a time value in this field.

### Date (Page 2)
* **Date (Page 2)**: This field is used to specify the date you are willing to make the donation. Please enter a date value in this field.

### Year (Page 2)
* **Year (Page 2)**: This field is used to specify the year you are willing to make the donation. Please enter a year value in this field.

### Yearly (Page 2)
* **Yearly (Page 2)**: This select option is used to determine whether you want to make an annual donation. Please select one of the following options:
    + True
    + False

### Donation Type (Page 2)
* **Donation Type (Page 2)**: This select option is used to determine the type of donation you want to make. Please select one of the following options:
    + One Time
    + Ongoing
    + In Kind
