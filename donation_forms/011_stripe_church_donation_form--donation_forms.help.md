# Stripe Church Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Church Donation Form is designed to collect information from church members for donations to the church. This form will help the church track and manage donations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Select the appropriate options for donation frequency and payment method.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please fill in your name and other relevant information.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you would like to donate, either "One Time", "Regular", or "Irregular".
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment method you would like to use, which includes "Stripe", "Bank Transfer", "PayPal", or "Other".
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to make the donation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to make the donation.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your donation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient tracking and management of donations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
