# Donor Referral Source Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience as a donor and how you refer others to our organization. The goal is to help us understand what motivates your referrals and how you interact with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best match your experience and interaction with our organization. If you have difficulty finding the right answers or have any questions, you can ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What motivated you to support our organization?**: This question helps us understand what drives your decision to support our organization. Select the option that best matches your motivation.
	+ Donor Newsletter: You received information about our organization through our newsletter.
	+ Social Media: You learned about our organization through social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.
	+ Referral from a friend or family member: A friend or family member referred you to our organization.
	+ Online search: You found our organization through an online search.
	+ Word of mouth: You heard about us through a conversation with someone else.
	+ Volunteer: You have been involved as a volunteer with our organization.
	+ Event: You learned about us through an event or meeting hosted by our organization.
	+ Radio or TV ad: You saw one of our advertisements on the radio or TV.
	+ Other: If none of the above apply.
* **Have you referred anyone to our organization before?**: If you have referred someone to our organization, select all the methods through which you made these referrals.
	+ Donor Newsletter: You referred someone through our newsletter.
	+ Social Media: You referred someone through social media.
	+ Referral from a friend or family member: You were referred by a friend or family member.
	+ Online search: You referred someone through an online search.
* **How did you hear about our organization initially?**: This question helps us understand the first point of contact with our organization. Select the method through which you initially learned about us.
	+ Donor Newsletter: You received information about us through our newsletter.
	+ Social Media: You learned about us through social media.
	+ Referral from a friend or family member: A friend or family member introduced you to our organization.
	+ Online search: You found us through an online search.
	+ Word of mouth: You heard about us through a conversation with someone else.
	+ Volunteer: You were involved with our organization as a volunteer.
	+ Event: You attended one of our events.
	+ Radio or TV ad: You saw one of our advertisements on the radio or TV.
	+ Other: If none of the above apply.
* **Have you interacted with our organization through social media?**: This question is to understand your level of engagement with our social media channels. Select all the platforms you use to interact with our organization.
	+ Facebook: You use Facebook to interact with our organization.
	+ Twitter: You use Twitter to interact with our organization.
	+ Instagram: You use Instagram to interact with our organization.
	+ YouTube: You use YouTube to interact with our organization.
	+ LinkedIn: You use LinkedIn to interact with our organization.
* **Do you have any friends or family members who support our organization?**: This question is to understand your personal network of supporters. Select true if you have friends or family members who support our organization.
* **What is your contact information for the person who referred you to our organization?**: This question is to allow us to follow up with the person who referred you for more information or to thank them for their referral.
* **How did you hear about our organization's mission or values?**: This question helps us understand what motivates you to support our organization. Select the method through which you learned about our mission or values.
	+ Donor Newsletter: You received information about our mission or values through our newsletter.
	+ Social Media: You learned about our mission or values through social media.
	+ Referral from a friend or family member: A friend or family member told you about our mission or values.
	+ Online search: You found information about our mission or values through an online search.
	+ Word of mouth: You heard about our mission or values through a conversation with someone else.
	+ Volunteer: You learned about our mission or values through your involvement as a volunteer.
	+ Event: You attended one of our events where you learned about our mission or values.
	+ Radio or TV ad: You saw one of our advertisements on the radio or TV.
	+ Other: If none of the above apply.
* **Have you ever attended an event hosted by our organization?**: This question is to understand your level of engagement with our events. Select true if you have attended one of our events.
* **How did you hear about our organization's events?**: This question is to understand the method through which you learned about our events. Select all the methods through which you heard about our events.
	+ Donor Newsletter: You received information about our events through our newsletter.
	+ Social Media: You learned about our events through social media.
	+ Referral from a friend or family member: A friend or family member told you about our events.
	+ Online search: You found information about our events through an online search.
	+ Word of mouth: You heard about our events through a conversation with someone else.
	+ Volunteer: You attended one of our events as a volunteer.
	+ Event: You attended one of our events.
	+ Radio or TV ad: You saw one of our advertisements on the radio or TV.
	+ Other: If none of the above apply.
* **Do you have any other questions or comments about our organization?**: This is an open question to allow you to provide any additional information or ask questions about our organization.
