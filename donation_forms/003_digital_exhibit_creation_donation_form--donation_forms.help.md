# digital_exhibit_creation_donation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from donors for digital exhibit creation donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your Donor Information (page 1).
- Select your preferred Donation Amount (page 2).
- Choose your donation frequency (page 3).
- Select your preferred payment method (page 4).
- Enter your Donor Name, Donor Organization, and Email (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information (Donor Name)** (`donor_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donation Amount (Amount)** (`amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Frequency of Donations (Frequency of Donations)** (`frequency_of_donation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether your donation is a one-time or recurring gift.
* **Frequency of Donations (Frequency of Donations)** (`donor_frequency`, select_one, required/true): This field seems to be a duplicate of the previous field and is not necessary for the form. You can ignore it.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/true): Choose your preferred payment method, which can be Bank Draft, Check, or Cash.
* **Donor Name (Donor Name)** (`donor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donor Organization (Donor Organization)** (`donor_organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization you represent (if applicable).
* **Donor Email (Email)** (`donor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
