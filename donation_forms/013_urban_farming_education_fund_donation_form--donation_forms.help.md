# Urban Farming Education Fund Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Urban Farming Education Fund Donation Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to make a donation to support urban farming education. The form is used to gather the required details needed to process the donation, including the donor's contact information, donation amount, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Donor Name" field. This is the name that will be associated with your donation.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the email address that will be used to contact you regarding your donation.
3.  Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donation Amount" field. This is the amount you are willing to donate to the Urban Farming Education Fund.
4.  If you are making a donation for a specific purpose, select the corresponding option in the "Purpose" field. You can choose from "Urban Farming Education Fund" or "Urban Farming Education Fund- Other" to describe the reason for your donation.
5.  Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" options. You can select from "Stripe," "Venmo," or "Other" to determine how you want to make your donation.
6.  In the "Notes" field, you can provide any additional comments or details you want to share about your donation.
7.  Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your donation and submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donor Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name that will be associated with your donation.
*   **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address that will be used to contact you regarding your donation.
*   **Donation Amount** (3, number, required): Enter the amount you are willing to donate to the Urban Farming Education Fund.
*   **Purpose** (4, select_one, optional): If you are making a donation for a specific purpose, select the corresponding option in this field. You can choose from "Urban Farming Education Fund" or "Urban Farming Education Fund- Other".
*   **Payment Method** (5, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided. You can select from "Stripe", "Venmo", or "Other".
*   **Notes** (6, note, optional): You can provide any additional comments or details you want to share about your donation.
*   **Submit** (7, text, required): Click this button to complete your donation and submit your information.
