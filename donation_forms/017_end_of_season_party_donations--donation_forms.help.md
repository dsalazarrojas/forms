# End Of Season Party Donations - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "End Of Season Party Donations" form is used to collect donations, contact information, and messages from donors for a party at the end of a season. This form helps the organizers of the party to track and acknowledge donations, and it's essential to fill it out accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, ensure you're filling out the correct form for the intended purpose and event.
2. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
3. Fill in your name and contact information for the "Donor Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the party in the "Party Date" field.
5. Indicate your donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
6. Select the type of donor (Individual, Team, or Organization) in the "Donor Type" field.
7. Provide your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
8. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
9. If you have a message for the donor, type it in the "Donor Message" field.
10. Choose the type of donation (General Donation or Fundraiser) in the "Donation Type" field.
11. If you have additional donations, enter the amount in the "Additional Donation" field.
12. Add any notes about the donation in the "Donor Notes" field.
13. Enter your donor's address in the "Donor Address" field.
14. Fill in your city in the "City" field.
15. Provide your state in the "State" field.
16. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
17. Finally, type in the name of the party in the "Party Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Date**: Enter the date of the party to be acknowledged correctly.
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name to be associated with the donation.
* **Donation Amount**: Provide the amount of the donation.
* **Donor Type**: Choose whether you are an individual, team, or organization.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that the donor can contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Donor Message**: If you have a message, type it here.
* **Donation Type**: Choose between general and fundraiser donation types.
* **Additional Donation**: If you have more to give, enter the amount here.
* **Donor Notes**: Add any additional comments about the donation.
* **Donor Address**: Enter your address for the donor record.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Provide your state.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Party Name**: Type the name of the party you are donating to.
