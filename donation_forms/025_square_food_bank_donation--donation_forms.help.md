# square_food_bank_donation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and manage information about donations made to the food bank.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form info section with a brief description of the purpose of the donation.
2. Enter the donor's information, including their name and any relevant contact details.
3. Enter the sponsor's information, if applicable.
4. Enter the date and time of the donation.
5. Enter the value of the donation.
6. Enter any additional notes about the donation.
7. Enter the donor's email and phone number for further contact and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_info** (`form_info`, text, required: false): A brief description of the purpose of the donation.
* **donor_info** (`donor_info`, text, required: false): Enter the donor's name and any relevant contact information.
* **sponsor_info** (`sponsor_info`, text, required: false): Enter the sponsor's name and any relevant contact information.
* **donation_date** (`donation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the donation.
* **donation_time** (`donation_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the donation.
* **donation_value** (`donation_value`, number, required: false): Enter the value of the donation.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the donation.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the donor's email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the donor's phone number.
* **donation_method** (`donation_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how the donation was made (cash, goods, or services).

## Tips
* Double-check the information entered in each field for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that the donor's contact information is up-to-date and correct.
* If you are unsure about any part of the process, do not hesitate to reach out to the food bank staff for assistance.
