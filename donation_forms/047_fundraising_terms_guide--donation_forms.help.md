# Fundraising Terms Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fundraising Terms Guide form is designed to gather information from potential donors about their fundraising intentions and goals. This form aims to understand the motivations behind their fundraising efforts and ensures that all users understand the terms and conditions associated with each type of fundraising.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For each field, select the option that best describes your intentions.
3. Make sure to read and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in each section.
