# historical_society_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The historical society donation form is used to collect information about donations from individuals and organizations. This data will help us better understand our donors and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**. This field is not required, but it's a good idea to include it for us to address you properly.
2. Enter your **Email** address. This is how we will contact you if we need more information or to confirm your donation.
3. Enter the **Donation Amount** you are giving. This can be any dollar amount you choose.
4. Select the **Date of Donation** when you made the donation.
5. Choose if you are an **Donor Type**, either **Individual** or **Organization**.
6. Select the **Frequency of Donations**, either **One time** or **Recurring**.
7. Choose your **Payment Method**. If you selected **Yes**, please fill in the **Other Payment Method** field to tell us how you paid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Email**: The email address where you can be reached.
* **Donation Amount**: The amount of money you are donating.
* **Date of Donation**: The date when you made the donation.
* **Donor Type**: The type of donor you are, either individual or organization.
* **Frequency of Donations**: If you donate regularly, select **Recurring**.
* **Payment Method**: Select if you paid with credit card, bank transfer, etc.
* **Other Payment Method**: If you chose **Yes** for payment method, specify the type of payment method you used.
