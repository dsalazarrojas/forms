# Fund Giving Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals and organizations submit applications for funding to non-profit organizations. It is intended to gather information about the applicant's identity, purpose, and financial details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose the correct non-profit organization from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter a brief description of the purpose and amount of the funding application.
4. Submit your application by selecting the "Submit" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Non-Profits**: Select the non-profit organization you are applying to.
* **Non-Profits Name**: Enter the name of the non-profit organization you are applying to.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the non-profit organization you are applying to.
* **Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the purpose of the funding application.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Amount (2)**: This field is a duplicate of the "Amount" field and is intended to be ignored.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the project or purpose.
* **Submit**: Choose "Submit" to complete and submit your application.
* **Continue/Cancel**: These options are not relevant to the completion of the form and are intended for navigation purposes.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form.
