# online-foundation-donation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who have decided to make a donation to the online foundation. It will capture details about the donation, including the amount, type, and method, as well as contact information for the donor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with \*.
2. For each field, enter the information as prompted.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donation Amount (\*)
* Donation Amount (`donation_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate to the foundation.

### Donation Type (\*)
* Donation Type (`donation_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of donation you would like to make.

### Name (donor_name, text, optional)
* Name (donor_name, text, optional): Enter your name.

### Email (donor_email, email, optional)
* Email (donor_email, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone (donor_phone, text, optional)
* Phone (donor_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
