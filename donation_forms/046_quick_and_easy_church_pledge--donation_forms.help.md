# quick_and_easy_church_pledge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quick_and_easy_church_pledge form helps you communicate your donation and tithing commitments with your church. This form guides you through a few simple questions to help you provide clear and accurate information about your giving.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the General Donation question, if applicable.
2.  Choose your Tithing Commitment from the available options.
3.  Enter the Amount of your General Donation or Tithing Commitment, if applicable.
4.  Indicate your Regular Tithe preference.
5.  Select the Frequency of your Regular Tithe.
6.  If you have an Other Tithing arrangement, specify its frequency.
7.  Choose any Building Fund commitments you'd like to support.
8.  If you have other Building Fund commitments, describe them in the Other (please specify) field.
9.  Confirm whether you regularly make an Offering.
10. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Donation (ID: 1)
*   **General Donation** (General Donation, text, optional): A space to provide any additional information about your general donation. If you have any comments or details about your general donation, please enter them here.

### Tithing Commitment (ID: 2)
*   **Tithing Commitment** (Tithing Commitment, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are committed to tithing (giving 10% of your income to your church) or have a different arrangement (other).

### Amount of General Donation (ID: 3)
*   **Amount of General Donation** (Amount of General Donation, number, optional): Enter the amount of your general donation.

### Amount of Tithing Commitment (ID: 4)
*   **Amount of Tithing Commitment** (Amount of Tithing Commitment, number, optional): Enter the amount of your tithing commitment.

### Regular Tithe (ID: 5)
*   **Regular Tithe** (Regular Tithe, select_one, optional): Choose whether you tithing monthly, weekly, or as needed.

### Frequency of Tithe (ID: 6)
*   **Frequency of Tithe** (Frequency of Tithe, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of your regular tithe: weekly, monthly, or as needed.

### Frequency of Other Tithing (ID: 7)
*   **Frequency of Other Tithing** (Frequency of Other Tithing, select_one, optional): If you have an Other Tithing arrangement, select its frequency: weekly, monthly, or as needed.

### Other Tithe Comments (ID: 8)
*   **Other Tithe Comments** (Other Tithe Comments, text, optional): Describe any other tithing commitments you have or would like to make.

### Building Fund (ID: 9)
*   **Building Fund** (Building Fund, select_multiple, optional): Choose any Building Fund commitments you'd like to support.

### Other (please specify) (ID: 10)
*   **Other (please specify)** (Other (please specify), text, optional): If you have other Building Fund commitments not listed, describe them here.

### Regular Offering (ID: 11)
*   **Regular Offering** (Regular Offering, select_one, optional): Confirm whether you regularly make an offering: weekly, monthly, or no. 

### Other (ID: 11)
 
There are no other fields to describe.
