# Public Garden Maintenance Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for public garden maintenance donation purposes. It allows users to provide their contact information and choose a frequency for donations and contact. This form is for public garden maintenance, so please ensure you are submitting a relevant donation request. If you have already donated or contacted the garden in the past, please do not fill out this form again.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as needed. Make sure to enter your contact information accurately and choose a donation frequency that best suits your needs. You can submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation form** (`donation_form`, text, optional): A field for any additional information about your donation request.
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate to the public garden.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Note** (`contact_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your donation.
* **Donation frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you would like to donate to the public garden. Options are Monthly, Quarterly, and Annually.
* **Contact frequency** (`contact_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you would like to be contacted by the public garden. Options are Email, Phone, and In-person.
