# Independent Film Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations to make donations to independent film projects. It collects information about the project, including its name, description, and status, as well as donation details such as amount, currency, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in the "Project Name" field with the title of the independent film project you would like to support.
2. Provide a brief description of the project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Enter the URL of the project's website or online platform in the "Project URL" field.
4. Select the current status of the project ("Project Started", "In Progress", or "Completed") in the "Project Status" field.
5. Fill in the amount donated in the "Amount Donated" field, using the format specified in the "Currency" field (USD, EUR, CAD, GBP, JPY).
6. Select the "Currency" that the donation will be made in (USD, EUR, CAD, GBP, JPY).
7. Choose the payment method for the donation (Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
8. Enter the recipient's name in the "Recipient Name" field (if applicable).
9. Fill in the recipient's address in the "Recipient Address" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the recipient's email address in the "Email" field (if applicable).
11. Enter the recipient's phone number in the "Phone" field (if applicable).
12. Add any additional message or information in the "Message" field (if applicable).
13. Select whether the donation is a one-time or recurring gift ("Yes" or "No") in the "form-1-13" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`Project Name`, text, required): Enter the title of the independent film project you would like to support.
* **Project Description** (`Project Description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Project URL** (`Project URL`, text, required): Enter the URL of the project's website or online platform.
* **Project Status** (`Project Status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the project.
* **Amount Donated** (`Amount Donated`, number, optional): Fill in the amount donated, using the format specified in the "Currency" field.
* **Currency** (`Currency`, select_one, required): Select the currency of the donation (USD, EUR, CAD, GBP, JPY).
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_multiple, required): Choose the payment method for the donation (Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Recipient Name** (`Recipient Name`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's name (if applicable).
* **Recipient Address** (`Recipient Address`, text, optional): Fill in the recipient's address (if applicable).
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the recipient's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's phone number (if applicable).
* **Message** (`Message`, note, optional): Add any additional message or information (if applicable).
* **form-1-13** (`form-1-13`, select_one, optional): Select whether the donation is a one-time or recurring gift ("Yes" or "No").
