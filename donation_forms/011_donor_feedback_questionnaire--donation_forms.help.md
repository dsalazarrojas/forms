# Donor Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from donors about their experience with a donation event. The form is part of the "donation_forms" category and is titled "Donor Feedback Questionnaire". It is intended to help event organizers and administrators understand donor satisfaction and experiences, and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Email, Phone, Name, Organization, Role, Event Details, Donation, Amount, Next Follow-up, and Email Opt-in.
2. Answer the multiple-choice questions: Donor Feedback, Feedback, and Frequency.
3. Provide any additional comments in the Comments and Suggestions fields.
4. Enter your social media presence and social media URL (if applicable).
5. Opt-in for email updates by filling in the Email Opt-in field and the Email Opt-in Message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Feedback**: A multiple-choice question asking about your satisfaction level with the event. Please select one of the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not very satisfied, Not at all satisfied.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for future updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Event Name**: (Not used in this guide)
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or thoughts about the event.
* **Suggestions**: Suggestions for future events.
* **Questions**: (Not used in this guide)
* **Feedback**: A multiple-choice question asking you to agree or disagree with certain statements.
* **Follow-up**: The next follow-up date you would like to receive.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the event.
* **Donation**: Your thoughts on the donation process.
* **Amount**: The amount of donation you made.
* **Frequency**: How often you would like to make donations. Please select one of the options: Once, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, Never.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments.
* **Next Follow-up**: The next follow-up date you would like to receive.
* **Name**: Your name.
* **Organization**: Your organization.
* **Role**: Your role or position.
* **Event Details**: Any additional event details.
* **Contact**: (Not used in this guide)
* **Social Media**: Your social media presence (e.g. Twitter, Facebook, Instagram).
* **Social Media URL**: (Not used in this guide)
* **Email Opt-in**: Opt-in for email updates.
* **Email Opt-in Message**: A message to be displayed when you opt-in for email updates.
* **Email Opt-in**: A field to enter your email address for email updates.
* **Name**: Your name (again, for verification).
* **Organization**: Your organization (again, for verification).
* **Role**: Your role or position (again, for verification).
* **Event Details**: Any additional event details (again, for verification).
* **Donation**: Your thoughts on the donation process (again, for verification).
* **Amount**: The amount of donation you made (again, for verification).
* **Follow-up**: The next follow-up date you would like to receive (again, for verification).
