# Hospital Cancer Research Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hospital Cancer Research Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors about their donation to the hospital's cancer research program. This form helps us to track the donor's details, donation frequency, amount, and other relevant information to ensure the successful continuation of our research programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the base information (field 1).
2. Provide your contact details (field 2).
3. Enter your payment details (field 3).
4. Select the payment method (field 4).
5. Choose the gift amount and frequency (fields 6 and 7).
6. Enter your donor information, including your name (field 8), email (field 9), and phone number (field 10), if required.
7. Add any additional donor notes (fields 11 and 12).
8. Select the payment frequency (field 13).
9. Choose the donation amount (field 14).
10. Select the donation frequency (field 15).
11. Enter your donor address and donation method (fields 16 and 17).
12. Enter any additional payment details (field 18).
13. Select the payment status (field 19).
14. Enter the donation date (field 20).
15. Enter your donor status (fields 21-24 and 25).
