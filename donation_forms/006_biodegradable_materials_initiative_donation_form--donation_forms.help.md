# Biodegradable Materials Initiative Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biodegradable Materials Initiative Donation Form is designed to collect information for individuals who want to support the initiative by making a donation. This form allows us to track and acknowledge donations, send receipts, and utilize the collected funds to drive research, outreach, and education efforts for biodegradable materials.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Donor Information" section and read the note for context.
2.  Enter your full name in the "Donor name" field. This is required for tax receipts.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email address" field to receive a donation confirmation.
4.  Enter the donation amount in the "Donation amount" field, using your currency.
5.  Select the "Donation type" - whether the donation is a one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annual gift.
6.  Select your "Payment method", which can be credit card, bank transfer, or check.
7.  If desired, provide an optional "Mailing address" for receipts.
8.  If you are donating in honor or memory of someone, select "Yes in honor of" or "Yes in memory of" in the "Tribute option" field.
9.  Optionally, you can provide a message or dedication for the acknowledgment in the "Message or dedication" field.
10.  Finally, read and consent to the "Consent for use of donation" section, understanding that donations support research, outreach, and education.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donor Information** (`donation_header`, note, not required): This section provides an introduction to the form and its purpose.
*   **Donor name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for tax receipt purposes.
*   **Email address** (`donor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive a donation confirmation.
*   **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of your donation in your currency.
*   **Donation type** (`donation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of donation - one time, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
*   **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method - credit card, bank transfer, check, or mobile payment.
*   **Mailing address** (`address`, text, not required): Provide an optional mailing address for receipts.
*   **Tribute option** (`tribute_option`, select_one, not required): If you are donating in honor or memory of someone, select the corresponding option.
*   **Message or dedication** (`message`, text, not required): Optionally, provide a message or dedication for the acknowledgment.
*   **Consent for use of donation** (`consent_note`, note, required): Read and consent to the use of your donation for research and outreach.
