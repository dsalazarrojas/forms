# Donation Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donation Form Template is a standardized form designed to collect donation information from individuals or organizations. This form is used to gather data for donations made to the organization, ensuring consistent and accurate recording of donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Donations" page from the form.
2. Enter your **Donor Name** in the "Donor Name" field. This is a required field and should include your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Donor Email" field, if applicable. This is optional, but helps us keep you informed about your donation status.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Donor Phone" field, if applicable. This is optional, but helps us reach you if needed.
5. Enter your **Address** in the "Donor Address" field. This is a required field and should include your full address as it appears on your identification documents.
6. Choose the amount of your **Donation Amount** from the dropdown options provided or enter a custom value. This is a required field and affects the overall donation amount.
7. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the options provided: Bank Transfer, Online Banking, or Check. This is a required field and determines the method of payment for your donation.
8. Provide any additional **Payment Information** in the "Payment Info" field, if applicable. This is optional and helps us process your donation efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Donor Name** (required)
Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Donor Email** (optional)
If you would like to stay informed about your donation status, enter your email address here.

### **Donor Phone** (optional)
If you would like us to contact you regarding your donation, enter your phone number here.

### **Donor Address** (required)
Enter your full address as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Donation Amount** (required)
Choose the amount of your donation from the provided options or enter a custom value.

### **Payment Method** (required)
Select the method of payment you prefer: Bank Transfer, Online Banking, or Check.

### **Payment Information** (optional)
Enter any additional information about your payment method if needed.
