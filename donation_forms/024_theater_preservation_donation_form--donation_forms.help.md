Theater Preservation Donation Form Help Guide
=====================================

# Theater Preservation Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
Theater Preservation Donation Form is used to collect donations and information from supporters for theater preservation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Fill Out the Form" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Select the "Donation Method" dropdown to choose the method of donation you would like to use.
3.  Enter the "Donation Amount" in the field provided, if other than the options provided.
4.  Enter the supporter's "Email", "Supporter Name", "Supporter City", "Supporter State", and "Supporter Zip" to ensure accurate tracking and communication.
5.  Choose the "Assigned User" and set the "Assigned Date" and "Assigned Time" for tracking and coordination.
6.  If necessary, fill out the "Supporter Notes" and "Donor Notes" fields for additional context.
7.  Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the donation being made.
*   **Donation Method** (`Donation Method`, select_one, required): Select the method of donation you would like to use, such as Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the supporter's email address for tracking and communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter the supporter's phone number, if available.
*   **Donation Note** (`Donation Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the donation, if necessary.
*   **Supporter Name** (`Supporter Name`, text, required): Enter the supporter's name for accurate tracking and communication.
*   **Supporter Email** (`Supporter Email`, email, required): Enter the supporter's email address for accurate tracking and communication.
*   **Supporter Phone Number** (`Supporter Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter the supporter's phone number, if available.
*   **Supporter Address** (`Supporter Address`, text, optional): Enter the supporter's address for accurate tracking and communication.
*   **Supporter City** (`Supporter City`, text, required): Enter the supporter's city for accurate tracking and communication.
*   **Supporter State** (`Supporter State`, select_one, required): Choose the supporter's state from the options provided.
*   **Supporter Zip** (`Supporter Zip`, text, required): Enter the supporter's zip code for accurate tracking and communication.
*   **Supporter Notes** (`Supporter Notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the supporter, if necessary.
*   **Assigned User** (`Assigned User`, select_one, required): Choose the user assigned to this supporter for tracking and coordination.
*   **Assigned Date** (`Assigned Date`, time, required): Select the date the supporter is assigned to.
*   **Assigned Time** (`Assigned Time`, time, required): Select the time the supporter is assigned to.
*   **Donation Amount (Other)** (`Donation Amount (Other)`, number, optional): Enter a custom donation amount, if other than the options provided.
*   **Supporter Country** (`Supporter Country`, select_one, required): Choose the supporter's country from the options provided.
*   **Supporter State (Other)** (`Supporter State (Other)`, text, optional): Enter a custom state for the supporter, if other than the options provided.
*   **Supporter City (Other)** (`Supporter City (Other)`, text, optional): Enter a custom city for the supporter, if other than the options provided.
*   **Supporter Address (Other)** (`Supporter Address (Other)`, text, optional): Enter a custom address for the supporter, if other than the options provided.
*   **Supporter Notes (Other)** (`Supporter Notes (Other)`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the supporter, if necessary.
*   **Supporter Zip (Other)** (`Supporter Zip (Other)`, text, optional): Enter a custom zip code for the supporter, if other than the options provided.
*   **Supporter Country (Other)** (`Supporter Country (Other)`, text, optional): Enter a custom country for the supporter, if other than the options provided.
