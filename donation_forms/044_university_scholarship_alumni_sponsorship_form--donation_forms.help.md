# University Scholarship Alumni Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from university alumni who are interested in sponsoring scholarships for students. The form is designed to gather details about the donor, including their contact information, sponsorship level, and areas of interest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review and agree to the terms**: Before proceeding, please review the form's terms and conditions, and ensure that you understand your responsibilities as a sponsor.
2. **Fill out the form**: Enter your information in each field, and select the relevant options for your sponsorship level and areas of interest.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your information to the university administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **University Scholarship Alumni Sponsorship Form** (`form_header`, note, not required): This is a header section that explains the purpose and context of the form.
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, email, not required): This field is used to collect general information about the donor, including their email address.
* **Name** (`donor_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email** (`donor_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, select_one, not required): Select one of the three sponsorship levels:
	+ Level 1: $100-$499
	+ Level 2: $500-$999
	+ Level 3: $1,000 or more
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to make the donation.
* **Time** (`donation_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you would like to make the donation.
* **Interests** (`donor_interests`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more areas of interest for the scholarship:
	+ Arts
	+ Science
	+ Language and Literature
	+ Business
	+ Health and Medicine
	+ History and Culture
	+ Technology
	+ Social Sciences and Humanities
	+ Other

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already sponsored a scholarship in the past.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the university administration for assistance.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the university administration at [insert contact information].
