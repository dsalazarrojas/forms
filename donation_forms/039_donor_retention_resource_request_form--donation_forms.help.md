# Donor Retention Resource Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for donors who need access to specific resources to support their work or organization. It helps us understand their requests and provide them with the necessary resources in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "first_name" and "last_name" fields.
2. Enter your organization's name in the "organization" field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the "contact_email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
5. Specify the type of resource you need in the "resource_type" field.
6. If you have selected "Other" as the resource type, provide more information about the resource in the "resource_type_other" field.
7. Select the frequency of resource you need in the "resource_request_frequency" field.
8. Select the availability of the resource in the "resource_request_availability" field.
9. Specify your resource request comments in the "resource_request_comments" field.
10. Choose how often you would like to be contacted for follow-up about the resource in the "follow_up" field.
11. Select your preferred method of communication in the "communication" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization or affiliation.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Resource Request** (`resource_request`, text, optional): Describe the specific resource you need.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your request.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of resource you need (Book, Computer, or Other).
* **Resource Type Other** (`resource_type_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other", provide more information about the resource.
* **Additional Resources** (`additional_resources`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional resources you need (Book, Computer, or Other).
* **Resource Request Frequency** (`resource_request_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of the resource you need (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Resource Request Availability** (`resource_request_availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select when the resource should be available (Now, Soon, Flexible, or Flexible (To be determined)).
* **Resource Request Comments** (`resource_request_comments`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you want to be contacted about your resource request (Yes, No).
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Yes, No).
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred method of communication (Email, Phone, Text/SMS, In-app, None).
