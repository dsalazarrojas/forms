# youth_program_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors about their donations to the youth program. It helps us process and track donations for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **form_details** field with a brief description of the donation.
2. Provide your **donor_info** in the next field.
3. Select the **donation_amount** you wish to donate.
4. Choose the **donation_frequency** for which you would like to make the donation (e.g., one-time, monthly, etc.).
5. Enter the **donation_date** when you plan to make the donation.
6. Enter the **donation_time** for the planned donation time.
7. Select the **payment_method** you prefer to make the donation (e.g., online, bank transfer, etc.).
8. Enter your **donor_email** for further communication.
9. Finally, provide your **donor_phone** number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details (<name>):** 
	+ A brief description of the donation.
* **Donor Info (<type>):** 
	+ A field to input additional information about the donor.
* **Donation Amount (<type>):** 
	+ The amount the donor wishes to donate to the program.
* **Donation Frequency (<options>):** 
	+ The frequency of the donation (e.g., one-time, monthly, etc.).
* **Donation Date (<type>):** 
	+ The date the donor plans to make the donation.
* **Donation Time (<type>):** 
	+ The time of the planned donation.
* **Payment Method (<options>):** 
	+ The method the donor prefers for payment (e.g., online, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Donor Email (<type>):** 
	+ The email of the donor for further communication.
* **Donor Phone (<type>):** 
	+ The phone number of the donor for contact purposes.
