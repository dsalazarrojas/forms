# Donor Thank You Letter Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Thank You Letter Request Form is designed to collect information from donors who have made a donation to your organization. This form is used to request personalized thank you letters for these donors, allowing you to express your gratitude and maintain a positive relationship with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Read through each field to understand what information is required and how it should be filled out.
2. **Provide Donor Information**: In the **Donor Information** field, enter the donor's details, including their name, as this is where you will sign the letter.
3. **Describe the Letter Content**: In the **Letter Details** field, please describe the content you would like to appear in the letter. This is where you can include a personal message, a specific reason for the donation, or a special occasion.
4. **Choose Delivery Options**: In the **Delivery Preferences** field, indicate how you would like to receive the letter - e.g., email, mail, or print copy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information** (`donation_form`, text, required/optional): Enter the donor's name as it should appear in the letter. This is where you will sign the letter.
* **Letter Details** (`letter_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the content you would like to appear in the letter, including any personal messages or specific reasons for the donation.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, required/optional): Choose how you would like to receive the letter, such as email, mail, or print copy.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
* The form is designed to be easy to use and understand, but if you need any assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out.
