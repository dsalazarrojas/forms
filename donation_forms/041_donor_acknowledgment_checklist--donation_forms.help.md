# Donor Acknowledgment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Acknowledgment Checklist is a form used to track and document the donation process, ensuring that all necessary steps are completed to acknowledge and thank donors for their contributions. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records and facilitating effective donor communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Donation Date** field to enter the date the donation was received.
2. Enter the **Donation Amount** field with the actual amount donated.
3. Enter the **Donor Name** field with the name of the donor.
4. Select the **Acknowledgment Date** field to enter the date the acknowledgment was sent to the donor.
5. Select the **Thank-you Sent** field to confirm if a thank-you message was sent to the donor. Choose from the options: "Thank You", "Sent", or "No Response".
6. Select the **Follow-up Sent** field to confirm if a follow-up message was sent to the donor. Choose all applicable options from the list: "Follow Up", "Sent", or "No Response".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`): Enter the date the donation was received.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`): Enter the actual amount donated.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`): Enter the name of the donor.
* **Acknowledgment Date** (`acknowledgment_date`): Enter the date the acknowledgment was sent to the donor.
* **Thank-you Sent** (`thank_you_sent`): Confirm if a thank-you message was sent to the donor. Choose from the options: "Thank You", "Sent", or "No Response".
* **Follow-up Sent** (`follow_up_sent`): Confirm if a follow-up message was sent to the donor. Choose all applicable options from the list: "Follow Up", "Sent", or "No Response".
