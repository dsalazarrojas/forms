# Virtual Tip Jar - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Tip Jar form is designed to allow you to show appreciation to service workers by providing a tip.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the information as instructed below:

1. Enter the name of the service worker you wish to provide a tip for.
2. Enter a valid service worker ID.
3. Enter the amount you wish to send as a tip.
4. Enter a message for your tip, if you'd like.
5. If you wish to receive a confirmation of your tip, enter your email address.
6. If you wish to receive a follow-up call or message regarding your tip, enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Tip Jar** (virtual_tip_jar, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the reason for your tip, if you wish.
* **Service worker name** (service_worker_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the service worker you wish to provide a tip for.
* **Service worker ID** (service_worker_id, text, required: false): Enter a valid service worker ID.
* **Amount** (amount, number, required: false): Enter the amount you wish to send as a tip.
* **Message** (message, text, required: false): Enter a message for your tip, if you'd like.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a confirmation of your tip.
* **Phone number** (phone_number, text, required: false): Enter a valid phone number if you'd like to receive a follow-up call or message regarding your tip.
