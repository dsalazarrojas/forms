# Donor Recognition Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from donors regarding their preferences for recognition and acknowledgement of their donations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Choose your preferred method of acknowledgement (e.g. phone call, email, mail, social media).
3. Select your preferred frequency of acknowledgement (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
4. Choose the method of communication for acknowledgement (e.g. phone call, email, mail, social media).
5. If you have an existing organization, please provide its details in the "Other Organisation" field.
6. If you have any other organisation that you would like to be recognized separately, please provide details in the "Other Organisation Details" field.
7. Select the tool to be assigned for recognition (e.g. chatjimmy or chatgeoff).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication regarding your recognition and acknowledgement.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This will be used for phone call acknowledgement.
* **Address**: Enter your address. This will be used for mail acknowledgement.
* **Recognition Preferences**: Choose between "Donor Name", "Organization Name", or "Both" for the type of recognition you would like to receive.
* **Social Media**: Select one or more social media platforms where you would like to be acknowledged (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn).
* **Acknowledgement**: This is a text field for any additional information you would like to provide regarding your acknowledgement preferences.
* **Acknowledgement Frequency**: Choose the frequency of acknowledgement you would like to receive (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Acknowledgement Method**: Choose the method of acknowledgement you would like to receive (e.g. phone call, email, mail, social media).
* **Other Organisation**: If you have an existing organization, please enter its details.
* **Other Organisation Details**: If you have any other organisation you would like to be recognized separately, please provide its details.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool to be assigned for recognition (e.g. chatjimmy or chatgeoff).

## Tips
* Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that your recognition preferences are met.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Once you have completed this form, your information will be used to provide you with the recognition and acknowledgement you have chosen.
