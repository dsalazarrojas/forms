# Bluepay Alumni Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information for alumni donations, allowing us to properly process and direct your contributions to the intended department or fund. Please fill out this form with accurate details to ensure your donation reaches its intended purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Bluepay Alumni Donation Form" link to access the form.
2.  Enter your information in the following fields, ensuring accuracy and completeness.
3.  Once you have filled out all the required fields, click "submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (`donor_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communication about your donation.
- **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Year of Graduation** (`graduation_year`, text, required): Indicate the year you graduated from the institution.
- **Major / Field of Study** (`major_field`, text, required): Specify the major or field of study you completed during your time at the institution.
- **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount_selection`, select_one, required): Select from the provided options or enter a custom amount if it's not listed.
- **If Other - Please enter amount** (`custom_donation_amount`, number, optional): If you selected "Other" as your donation amount, enter the exact amount here.
- **Where would you like your donation to go?** (`fund_designation`, select_one, required): Choose from the available fund designation options or specify a department for your donation.
- **Name on Card** (`cc_name`, text, required): Enter the name as it appears on your credit card.
- **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how frequently you would like to donate, from one-time to annually.

## Tips

- Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
- We appreciate your generosity and contributions to your alma mater.
