# Children Surgery Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Surgery Donation Form is a tool for individuals to contribute money to support the surgery of children in need. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide financial assistance to help make a difference in the lives of children undergoing surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address to allow us to contact you and provide updates on your donation.
2. Enter the amount you wish to donate to support children's surgery.
3. If applicable, indicate if your donation is for a specific fund or patient.
4. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Check, or Bank Transfer).
5. If you are giving in honor or memory of someone, please provide their name.
6. Decide if you want to receive updates on the program (optional).
7. Choose if you prefer to make your gift anonymous (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, required): Provide your email address to receive updates and receipts about your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate. This will support children's surgery.
* **Donation Designation** (`donation_designation`, text, optional): If you have a specific fund or patient in mind, please indicate it here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to make your payment (Credit Card, Check, or Bank Transfer).
* **Tribute Information** (`tribute_information`, text, optional): If your donation is in honor or memory of someone, please provide their name here.
* **Receive Program Updates** (`receive_updates`, select_one, optional): Decide if you want to receive updates on the program's progress.
* **Make Gift Anonymous** (`anonymous_gift`, select_one, optional): Choose if you prefer to make your gift anonymous.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the amount you enter is accurate and sufficient to support children's surgery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your contact email or phone number].
