# Thank You Gift Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about gifts received, including gift details, donor information, and other related data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**, as it will be used to acknowledge the gift and update relevant records.
2. **Provide all necessary details**, including gift details, donor information, and delivery information, to ensure accurate and timely processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Details** (`Gift Details`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the gift received.
* **Donation Information** (`Donation Information`, `text`, optional): Please provide details about the donation, such as the amount and any other relevant information.
* **Recipient Info** (`Recipient Info`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing information about the recipient of the gift.
* **Donor Details** (`Donor Details`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing information about the donor, including their contact information and any other relevant details.
* **Delivery Info** (`Delivery Info`, `text`, optional): Please provide information about the delivery of the gift, such as the date and time it was received.
* **Thank You Note** (`Thank You Note`, `text`, optional): This field is for writing a personal message to the donor, thanking them for their gift.
* **Gift Receipt** (`Gift Receipt`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing a receipt for the gift, which can be used for tax purposes or other record-keeping needs.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes about the gift.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, `date`, optional): Please select the date the gift was delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, `time`, optional): Please select the time the gift was delivered.
* **Donor E-mail** (`Donor E-mail`, `email`, optional): This field is for providing the donor's email address.
* **Donor Phone** (`Donor Phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing the donor's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the tool assigned to the gift.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`Assigned Tool 2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select the tools assigned to the gift.

Note: Required fields are marked as required: true, and the user is expected to fill them out. Optional fields can be left blank or filled out as desired.
