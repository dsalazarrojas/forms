# Epidemic Response Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Epidemic Response Fund Donation Form is used to collect donations specifically for the epidemic response fund. It is intended for individuals who wish to contribute to this cause.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information fields, including First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Enter the amount donated in the Amount Donated field.
3. Select your preferred Donation Frequency and Donation Method.
4. Fill out the address fields, including Address 1, Address 2, City, State, and Zip.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you want to donate:
	+ One-time Donation: Donate a single amount.
	+ Monthly Support: Donate a recurring monthly amount.
	+ Recurring Support: Donate a recurring amount at a set interval (not specified in this form).
* **Frequency of Donation** (`frequency_donation`, select_multiple, optional): Select how frequently you want to donate:
	+ One-time: Donate only once.
	+ Monthly: Donate on a monthly basis.
	+ Quarterly: Donate on a quarterly basis.
	+ Annually: Donate on an annual basis.
* **Donation Method** (`donation_method`, select_one, required): Select how you want to make your donation:
	+ Credit Card: Use a credit card to make your donation.
	+ Bank Draft: Use a bank draft to make your donation.
	+ ACH: Use an Automated Clearing House (ACH) transfer to make your donation.
* **Address 1** (`address1`, text, required): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, text, optional): Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

Note: Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate proper follow-up actions.
