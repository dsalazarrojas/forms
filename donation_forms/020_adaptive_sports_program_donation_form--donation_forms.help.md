# Adaptive Sports Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adaptive Sports Program Donation Form is a tool used to collect donations and information from individuals who want to support the program. The form helps the Adaptive Sports Program to process the donations, send receipts, and communicate with the donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Donor information** section (page 1).
2. Enter your **Donor name** in the field provided. This is the name that will be used for receipt purposes.
3. Enter your **Contact email** in the field provided. This is the email address that the Adaptive Sports Program will use to send receipts and updates.
4. Enter the **Donation amount** in the field provided. Please enter the amount in USD.
5. Choose your **Program designation**. This is the specific program or team you want to support with your donation.
6. If you're making a **Recurring gift**, choose the frequency of your gift (One time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
7. If you're making a **Tribute or Memorial gift**, enter the name and message for the honoree.
8. Choose how you'd like to be **Recognized**. You can choose to be recognized publicly, remain anonymous, or not have your donation published.
9. Choose your **Payment method**. You can pay using a credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
10. If you have an **Additional message** for the program, you can enter it in the provided field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor information** (page 1): This section is a general introduction to the form.
* **Donor name** (page 2): This is the name that will be used for receipt purposes. Please enter your full name.
* **Contact email** (page 2): This is the email address that the Adaptive Sports Program will use to send receipts and updates.
* **Donation amount** (page 3): Please enter the amount of your donation in USD.
* **Program designation** (page 3): This is the specific program or team you want to support with your donation.
* **Recurring gift option** (page 4): If you're making a recurring gift, choose the frequency of your gift (One time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Tribute or memorial gift information** (page 4): If you're making a tribute or memorial gift, enter the name and message for the honoree.
* **Recognition preference** (page 4): Choose how you'd like to be acknowledged. You can choose to be recognized publicly, remain anonymous, or not have your donation published.
* **Payment method** (page 5): Choose your payment method (Credit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer).
* **Additional message** (page 5): This is an optional field where you can enter a message for the program.
* **Payment method** (page 5): This is the final step in the form. Review your information and submit the form to complete the donation process.
