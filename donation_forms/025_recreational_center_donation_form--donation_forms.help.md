# Recreational Center Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recreational Center Donation Form is used to collect information from donors who want to support the Recreational Center's activities and programs. This form helps the Recreational Center to track and acknowledge donations, and to communicate with donors about their contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the amount you have donated to the Recreational Center in the "Amount Donated" field.
4. If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the program(s) you would like to support from the "Program" dropdown menu.
7. Choose how you made your donation (e.g., Cash, Check, Online) in the "Donation Method" dropdown menu.
8. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "Notes" field.
9. If assigned a tool for a specific program, enter the name of the tool in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Finally, enter any comments or remarks you would like to make in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Amount Donated** (number, required): Enter the total amount you have donated to the Recreational Center.
* **Email** (email, optional): If you have an email address, enter it so we can communicate with you about your donation.
* **Phone** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can reach you if needed.
* **Program** (select_one, optional): Select one or more programs you would like to support with your donation.
	+ Recreational Center Programs: Supports the Recreational Center's general activities.
	+ Sports and Recreation: Supports sports and fitness-related programs.
	+ Fitness and Wellness: Supports programs focused on physical fitness and well-being.
* **Donation Method** (select_multiple, optional): Choose how you made your donation.
	+ Cash: You made the donation in cash.
	+ Check: You made the donation by check.
	+ Online: You made the donation online.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about your donation.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): If assigned a tool for a specific program, enter the name of the tool.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Enter any other comments or remarks you would like to make about your donation.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields (First Name, Last Name, and Amount Donated) to complete the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Recreational Center staff.
- Your feedback and comments are appreciated and help us improve our services.
