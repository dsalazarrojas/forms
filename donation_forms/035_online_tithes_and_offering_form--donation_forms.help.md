# Online Tithes And Offering Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations, tithes, and offerings from individuals for the benefit of a religious or charitable organization. It captures relevant information such as the date and time of the donation, the amount given, and any comments or notes associated with the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide the date and time of your donation in the "date" and "time" fields.
3. Enter the amount of tithes and offering you are making.
4. Share any comments or notes about your donation in the "comments" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (text, optional): Enter a brief title or description of your donation.
* **tithes** (text, optional): Enter the amount or value of the tithes you are making.
* **offering** (text, optional): Enter the amount or value of the offering you are making.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your donation.
* **time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your donation.
* **tithes_amount** (number, optional): Enter the monetary amount of the tithes you are making.
* **offering_amount** (number, optional): Enter the monetary amount of the offering you are making.
* **comments** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your donation.
* **name** (text, optional): Enter your name.
* **form_submitted_by** (text, optional): Enter the person submitting this form (if not yourself).
