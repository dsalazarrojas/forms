# Candle Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Candle Donation Form is a digital form designed to collect information from donors about their desired candle donations. This form helps us ensure that all donations are properly recorded and acknowledged, allowing us to fulfill the donor's intentions and provide a meaningful experience for the donor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select the options as instructed. You can submit your form electronically, and we'll take care of the rest. Please make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you have completed it correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name** (`donor_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as you would like to be referenced in the candle dedication.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, Email, Required): Provide your email address to receive a confirmation of your donation and any additional updates.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for any additional communication or reminders.
* **Physical Address** (`donor_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your physical address if you would like the candle to be lit at a specific location.
* **Prayer Intent or Dedication** (`prayer_intent_dedication`, Text, Required): Briefly describe the purpose or intent behind your donation, e.g., "In Memory of...", "For the healing of...", etc.
* **Type of Candle** (`type_of_candle_choice`, Select One, Required): Choose the size of the candle to be lit based on the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`candle_quantity`, Number, Required): Specify the number of candles to be lit for your intended purpose.
* **Preferred Date for Lighting** (`candle_placement_date`, Date, Required): Select a date when you would like the candle to be lit.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions_candle`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional instructions or requests for the candle placement.
* **Donation Amount** (`total_donation_amount`, Number, Required): Enter the total amount of your donation based on your selection.
* **Payment Method** (`candle_payment_method`, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to donate, such as credit card, PayPal, or in-person.
* **Email Confirmation Preference** (`email_confirmation_pref`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether you would like to receive an email confirmation of your donation.
* **Subscribe to Parish Bulletin** (`parish_bulletin_subscription`, Select One, Required): Check if you would like to stay updated with church news and events.
* **Dedication Reading Consent** (`dedication_reading_consent`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you consent to having your dedication mentioned during mass.
* **Submission Date** (`candle_submission_timestamp`, Date, Required): The date and time when this form is submitted.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
