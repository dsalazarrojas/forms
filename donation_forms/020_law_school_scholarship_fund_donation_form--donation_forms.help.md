# Law School Scholarship Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law School Scholarship Fund Donation Form is designed to collect donations for students in need of financial aid to pursue their law studies.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your donation is processed correctly.
- Please enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address to identify yourself as a donor.
- You can choose how often you wish to donate (One Time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) and specify the payment method (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, or American Express).
- Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Amount" field.
- If you have any additional information or comments, please add a Note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
- **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you wish to pay for your donation (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, or American Express).
- **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you wish to donate (One Time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you are paying for a specific date, please enter it in this field.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you are paying for a specific time, please enter it in this field.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please add them here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have the necessary funds to cover your donation before submitting this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your email/phone number].
- Thank you for your support to the Law School Scholarship Fund!
