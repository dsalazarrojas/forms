# COVID 19 Response Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from individuals in response to the COVID-19 crisis. The information collected will be used to process donations and may be shared with relevant parties, such as the donor and our organization, to facilitate communication and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the "Donor Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be the primary method of communication with you.
3. If you would like to provide a phone number for follow-up or additional contact, you may enter it in the "Phone Number" field. This is optional.
4. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donation Amount ($)" field.
5. Select your preferred donation frequency from the options provided in the "Donation Frequency" field.
6. Choose your preferred payment method from the options in the "Payment Method" field.
7. If you would like to dedicate your donation to a specific person or occasion, you may enter a message in the "Dedication (Optional)" field.
8. Indicate your preference for recognition of your donation by selecting an option in the "Recognition Preference" field.
9. If your employer offers a matching gift program, please let us know by selecting the correct option in the "Does your employer offer a matching gift program?" field. This is optional.
10. Finally, confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy by selecting the correct option in the "I agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: If you would like to provide a phone number for follow-up or additional contact, please enter it here.
* **Donation Amount ($)**: Enter the amount you would like to donate in the specified currency.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select how often you would like to make donations:
	+ One-time
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
* **Payment Method**: Choose from the available payment options:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Stripe
	+ Apple Pay
	+ Google Pay
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address for payment processing purposes.
* **Dedication (Optional)**: If you would like to dedicate your donation to a specific person or occasion, please enter a message here.
* **Recognition Preference**: Indicate how you would like your donation acknowledged:
	+ Publish my name
	+ I wish to remain anonymous
* **Does your employer offer a matching gift program?**: If your employer offers a matching gift program, please select the correct option.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
- The information collected will be used to process your donation and facilitate communication with you.
