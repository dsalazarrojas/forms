# GIK Request Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for assistance or resources. It is meant to gather information about the requestor, their contact details, and the items or tools they need. Please note that this form is not intended for duplicate requests, and the information submitted will be reviewed and processed accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as the requestor in the "Requestor Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will ensure that we can contact you regarding your request.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the date you would like to make the request for in the "Request Date" field.
5. Describe the item or tool you are requesting in the "Description" field.
6. Specify the quantity of the item you need in the "Quantity" field.
7. Choose whether the item is of type "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu in the "Type" field.
8. If you are representing an organization, please enter the name of your organization in the "Organization Name" field.
9. Provide any additional comments or details about your request in the "Comments" field.
10. If applicable, please specify the assigned tool in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person making the request.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to make the request for.
* **Description** (`item_description`, text, optional): Describe the item or tool you are requesting.
* **Quantity** (`quantity_needed`, number, optional): Specify the quantity of the item you need.
* **Type** (`item_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the item is of type "Yes" or "No".
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of your organization.
* **Comments** (`requestor_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the assigned tool.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate so that we can reach out to you efficiently.
* Make sure to select the correct type for the item or tool you are requesting.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask and we will guide you through the process.
