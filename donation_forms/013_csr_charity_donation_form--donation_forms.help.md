# CSR Charity Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donation information from individuals and organizations for charitable purposes. It is intended for donors to specify their name, email, donation amount, donation type, and any additional details about their gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Donor name** field with the name of the person or organization donating.
2. Enter the **Donor email** to ensure that we can contact them and provide a receipt.
3. Select the **Donation amount** in the local currency.
4. Choose the **Donation type**:
	* **One time**: A single donation.
	* **Monthly recurring**: A donation made monthly.
	* **Annual recurring**: A donation made annually.
5. If you are donating to a specific **Designation or campaign**, please specify it in the designated field.
6. Choose whether you would like to **Donate anonymously** or not.
7. Select **Yes** or **No** to receive a **Tax receipt**.
8. If you would like to include a **Message to recipient**, you can enter a note in this field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor name**: The name of the person or organization donating.
* **Donor email**: The email address of the person or organization donating, which we will use to contact them and provide a donation receipt.
* **Donation amount**: The numeric value of the donation in the local currency.
* **Donation type**: The type of donation you are making, such as one-time, monthly recurring, or annual recurring.
* **Designation or campaign**: If you are donating to a specific program or campaign, please specify it here (optional).
* **Donate anonymously**: Choose **True** if you would like to remain anonymous, or **False** otherwise.
* **Tax receipt**: Select **Yes** to receive a tax receipt for your donation, or **No** otherwise.
* **Message to recipient**: An optional field to include a message to the recipient (the charity or organization).
