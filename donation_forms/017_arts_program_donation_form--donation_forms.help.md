# Arts Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for the Arts Program, a program that aims to keep the arts alive in the community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form and fill it out completely.
2. Enter your full name and email address in the fields provided.
3. Select your donation amount from the options provided.
4. Choose the frequency of your donation (one-time, monthly recurring, or annual recurring).
5. Select where you would like your gift to be directed.
6. If you would like to make your donation in honor or memory of someone, enter their name in the "Is this gift in honor or memory of someone" field.
7. If you have a company that matches donations, select "True" in the "Does your employer match donations" field.
8. Finally, select "Yes, please" to subscribe to our monthly arts newsletter, or "No, thanks" if you do not wish to receive it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the receipt.
- **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address to receive your tax-deductible receipt.
- **Donation Amount** (select_one, required): Select from the options provided or enter a custom amount if you select "Other Amount".
- **Donation Frequency** (select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to make this donation (one-time, monthly recurring, or annual recurring).
- **Where should your gift be directed** (select_one, required): Select from the options provided, such as General Fund - Greatest Need, Youth Art Scholarships, etc.
- **Is this gift in honor or memory of someone** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person this donation is in honor or memory of, e.g., In Memory of Jane Doe.
- **Does your employer match donations** (select_one, optional): Select "True" if your employer matches donations.
- **Subscribe to our monthly arts newsletter** (select_one, optional): Choose to receive our monthly newsletter or decline.
