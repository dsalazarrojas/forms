# Rehabilitation Center Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donation information from individuals interested in supporting the Rehabilitation Center. Please fill out the form accurately and securely to ensure that your donation is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details and donation preferences.
2. Choose the payment method and amount you would like to donate.
3. Select any additional options or agreements that apply to your donation.
4. Review and submit the form to complete your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rehabilitation Center Fund Donation Form**: This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill out.
* **form_1 (form_1_label)**: Please enter your name to identify yourself as a donor. (Required)
* **form_2 (donor_information)**: Enter any additional information about yourself, such as your address or contact number. (Optional)

* **form_6 (payment_amount)**: Enter the amount you would like to donate. (Optional)
