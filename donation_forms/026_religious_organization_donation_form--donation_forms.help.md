# Religious Organization Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Religious Organization Donation Form is a tool for individuals and organizations to make donations to support the religious cause. It allows donors to provide their contact information and donation details, which helps the organization manage and process donations effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Organization Name" field with the name of the organization receiving the donation.
2. Enter your "Donor Name" in the designated field.
3. Specify your "Donation Amount" in the required field.
4. Choose your preferred "Donation Frequency" from the options provided.
5. If you choose "Recurring" donation frequency, specify the frequency in the "Donation Frequency Other" field.
6. Select your preferred "Donation Method" from the available options.
7. Enter your "Donor Email" address.
8. Provide your "Donor Phone" number (optional).
9. Select your preferred "Payment Method" from the options provided.
10. Choose your "Payment Terms" from the options.
11. Specify the "Donation Date" (if applicable).
12. Choose the "Donation Time" (if applicable).
13. Add a "Donation Note" (optional).
14. Select the "Donation Status" from the options.
15. Finally, select your "Donor Country" from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the organization receiving the donation.
* **Donor Name** (text, optional): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donation Amount** (number, required): Specify the amount of your donation.
* **Donation Frequency** (select_one, optional): Choose from "One-Time" or "Recurring" options. If you choose "Recurring", please specify the frequency in "Donation Frequency Other" field.
* **Donation Frequency Other** (text, optional): Enter a frequency of your choice if you selected "Recurring" option. For example, "Monthly", "Weekly", etc.
* **Donation Method** (select_one, optional): Select from "Card Number", "Bank Transfer", or other options.
* **Donor Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Donor Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, optional): Choose from "Credit Card", "PayPal", "Direct Bank Transfer", etc.
* **Payment Terms** (select_one, optional): Select from "One-Time" or "Recurring" options.
* **Donation Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your donation.
* **Donation Time** (time, optional): Choose the time of your donation.
* **Donation Note** (note, optional): Add a note about your donation.
* **Donation Status** (select_one, optional): Choose from "Pending" or "Processed" options.
* **Donor Country** (select_one, optional): Select your country of residence.
