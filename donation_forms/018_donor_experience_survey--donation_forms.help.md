# Donor Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on your experience as a donor with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please complete each page of the form by selecting the relevant answers or entering your response in the text fields.
* You can select multiple options for the "Donation Experience" page.
* Ensure that you only answer the questions that apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Experience** (Select Multiple): 
  This question asks about your overall experience as a donor. Please select all that apply.
* **Communication** (Text): 
  Describe how well you feel the organization communicates with you, such as through email, phone, or mail.
* **Donation Amount** (Number): 
  Enter the amount you typically donate to the organization.
* **Frequency of Donating** (Text): 
  Describe how often you donate to the organization, such as daily, weekly, monthly, etc.
* **Giving History** (Text): 
  Share your history of giving with the organization, including how long you've been donating.
* **Donation Channel** (Text): 
  Describe the channel(s) you use to make your donation, such as online, in-person, etc.
* **Contact Method** (Text): 
  Describe how you prefer to be contacted by the organization, such as email, phone, mail, etc.
* **Contact Person** (Select One): 
  If you've had contact with a specific person from the organization, select "Yes" here.
* **Satisfaction** (Text): 
  Rate your overall satisfaction with the organization's services and communication.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (Text): 
  Provide any suggestions you have to help the organization improve its services and processes.
