# Children Wing Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Wing Donation Form is used to collect donations from individuals or organizations for the benefit of the Children Wing, a charitable program that supports the well-being of children.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and email address as the donor.
2. Specify the amount of your donation in the local currency.
3. Choose the purpose or program for your donation (optional).
4. Select the frequency of your recurring donation (if applicable).
5. If your donation is a tribute, specify the honoree details (if applicable).
6. Choose your preferred payment method.
7. Decide if you wish to remain anonymous (if applicable).
8. Select your preferred receipt option (email or postal mail).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (Donor Name, text, **required**): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donor Email** (Donor Email, email, **required**): Enter your email address for donation receipt.
* **Donation Amount** (Donation Amount, number, **required**): Enter the amount of your donation in the local currency.
* **Designation** (Designation, text, optional): Specify the purpose or program for your donation.
* **Recurring Donation** (Recurring Donation, select one, **required**): Choose the frequency of your recurring donation (if applicable).
* **Is this a Tribute Gift?** (Is this a Tribute Gift, select one, **optional**): Indicate if your donation is a tribute, and provide honoree details if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select one, **required**): Choose your preferred payment method (credit card, check, or bank transfer).
* **Make Donation Anonymous** (Make Donation Anonymous, select one, **optional**): Decide if you wish to remain anonymous.
* **Receipt Preference** (Receipt Preference, select one, **required**): Select your preferred receipt option (email or postal mail).

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling in your name and email address.
* Ensure that your donation amount is entered correctly in the local currency.
* If you are making a tribute gift, provide clear details about the honoree.
* Choose your preferred payment method for efficient processing.
* Select your receipt preference for easy access to your receipt.
* If you wish to remain anonymous, indicate this so we can respect your preference.
