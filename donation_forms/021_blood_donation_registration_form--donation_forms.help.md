# Blood Donation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register individuals who want to donate blood. It collects essential details to ensure the safety and efficiency of the donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as follows:

* Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
* Provide your date of birth, which is your birthdate.
* Choose your gender from the provided options.
* Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Enter your residential address, city, and state to help us identify your location.
* Provide your postal code for our records.
* If you know your blood type, select it from the options provided.
* Enter your height and weight if you have the information available.
* Indicate your previous donation experience.
* If you have a preferred donation center, enter it here.
* Choose your preferred contact method(s) for future communications.
* Finally, select a registration date for your record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Gender**: Choose your identity as male, female, or other.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for easy reach-out.
* **Residential Address**: Enter your current address where you reside.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State/Province**: Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your residential address.
* **Blood Type**: If known, choose your blood type from the options provided.
* **Height (cm)**: Enter your height if you know it.
* **Weight (kg)**: Enter your weight if you have the information available.
* **Previous Donation Experience**: Indicate if you have donated blood before.
* **Preferred Donation Center**: Enter your preferred donation center if you have one.
* **Contact Preferences**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, SMS, or Any).
* **Registration Date**: Select a date to register for your next donation session.
* **Preferred Donation Center**: This field is for future reference and not a mandatory field.
* **Contact Preferences**: This field is for selecting how you would like to be contacted for future updates. 

Note: Please ensure you have the necessary information available when filling out this form, and if you need any help or clarification, feel free to reach out to us.
