# Hospital Charity Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Charity Donation Form is a public form designed to collect information from individuals who wish to make a donation to the hospital charity. This form helps the charity team to understand donor preferences and tailor future communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name and Email.
2. Optional fields can be filled out if you wish to provide more information about your donation.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (<name>, <type>): text
Your name as it appears on your ID or passport.

### Last Name (<name>, <type>): text
Your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.

### Email (<name>, <type>): email
Your email address for communication with the charity team.

### Phone (<name>, <type>): text
Your phone number for the charity team to contact you.

### Donation Amount (<name>, <type>): number
The amount you are willing to donate to the hospital charity.

### Frequency of Donations (<name>, <type>): select_one
How often you would like to make donations to the hospital charity.

### Donation Method (<name>, <type>): select_multiple
Choose one or more methods for your donation:

*   Cash
*   Check
*   Card
*   Online

### Date Donated (<name>, <type>): date
The date of your last donation to the hospital charity.

### Time Donated (<name>, <type>): time
The time of your last donation to the hospital charity.

### Message (<name>, <type>): note
Any additional message or comments you would like to share with the charity team.

### Submit (<name>, <type>): text
Please submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary information.

## Tips
*   All fields are not required, you can skip any field you are not comfortable providing information for.
*   Please ensure that your email and phone number are up-to-date and accurate.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the charity team directly.
