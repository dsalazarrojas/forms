# Donor Onboarding Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Onboarding Program Registration Form is used to register donors to the program, ensuring they have a structured and personalized experience. The form collects information from the donor, including contact details, preferences, and donation habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact name and email address in the "Contact Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3.  Select your preferred communication method and frequency for follow-up emails or calls in the "Communication Method" field.
4.  Indicate your donation frequency and amount (if applicable) in the "Donation Frequency" and "Donation Amount" fields.
5.  Choose your preferred donation method in the "Donation Method" field.
6.  Enter any additional notes for the onboarding process in the "Notes" field.
7.  Confirm your follow-up preferences and methods in the "Follow-up Confirm" field.
8.  Select your donor status in the "Donor Status" field.
9.  Choose the date and time for the onboarding process in the "Onboarding Date" and "Onboarding Time" fields.
10. Select the date for the confirmation of the onboarding process in the "Confirmation Date" field.
11. Select the date for the follow-up process in the "Follow-up Date" field.
12. Enter your preferred follow-up time in the "Follow-up Time" field.
13. Confirm the follow-up method and frequency in the "Follow-up Confirm" field.
14. Enter your donor ID in the "Donor ID" field.
15. Finally, submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name for communication and contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or texts.
* **Donor Preferences** (`donor_preferences`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred method of communication for follow-up emails or calls.
* **Communication Choice** (`communication_choice`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred frequency of follow-up communications.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `time`, required): Indicate how frequently you intend to donate to the program.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of your donations, if applicable.
* **Donation Method** (`donation_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred method of donation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes for the onboarding process.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `note`, optional): Enter any confirmation notes after the onboarding process.
* **Donor Status** (`donor_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your donor status.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Time** (`onboarding_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time for the onboarding process.
* **Confirmation Date** (`confirmation_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for the confirmation of the onboarding process.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for the follow-up process.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time for the follow-up process.
* **Follow-up Confirm** (`follow_up_confirm`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm the follow-up method and frequency.
* **Donor ID** (`donor_id`, `text`, required): Enter your donor ID for identification purposes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): This field is automatically populated upon form submission.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
* Ensure you only fill out this form once to avoid duplication.
* Review each field carefully to provide the necessary information for your onboarding.
