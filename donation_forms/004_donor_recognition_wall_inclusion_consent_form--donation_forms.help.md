# Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent Form is a form used to gather consent from donors for their inclusion on the Donor Recognition Wall. This form is designed to inform donors of their information that will be displayed and to obtain their agreement to be included.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information, including your:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone Number (optional)
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
3. Check the "I understand and agree" checkbox to confirm that you are willing to be included on the Donor Recognition Wall.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name as you would like it to be displayed on the Donor Recognition Wall.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as you would like it to be displayed on the Donor Recognition Wall.
* **Email**: The email address that you would like to be used to contact you regarding your inclusion on the Donor Recognition Wall.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number that you would like to be used to contact you regarding your inclusion on the Donor Recognition Wall (optional).
* **I understand and agree**: Check this box to confirm that you are willing to be included on the Donor Recognition Wall and understand that your information will be displayed publicly.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
* If you would like to be removed from the Donor Recognition Wall at any time, please submit a request to us at [insert contact information].
