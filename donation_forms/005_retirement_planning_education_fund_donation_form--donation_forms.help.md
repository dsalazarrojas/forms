# Retirement Planning Education Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retirement Planning Education Fund Donation Form is a tool used to collect donations for the retirement planning education resource. By filling out this form, you will be contributing to the fund's goal of providing financial literacy and planning information to individuals in need.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please note that all fields are optional** unless specifically marked as required.
- **Start by filling out the first page** of the form, which is a general information page.
- **Proceed to the second page** where you will find fields related to donation details.
- **On the second page, select "Donation Amount"**, a numeric field where you will input the amount of your donation.
- **Next, select "Donation Frequency" and choose how often you would like to make this donation.** This field is a select one option, with choices being "Yes" or "No".
- **Select "Donation Method"** as either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preferred method of donation.
- **Enter your email address** if you would like to be contacted regarding your donation.
- **Provide your phone number** for additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Page**: This page is a general information page.
	* **First Page**: This is a text area where you can enter any additional information or comments about your donation.
- **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate to the retirement planning education fund.
- **Donation Frequency**: Select "Yes" if you would like to make this donation on a regular basis, or "No" if this is a one-time donation.
- **Donation Method**: Indicate your preferred method of donation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
- **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted regarding your donation.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
