# Small Business Fund Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Small Business Fund, which provides financial support to small businesses or entrepreneurs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name in the first_name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last_name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter a donation amount in the donation_amount field.
5. Select your donation frequency from the donation_frequency field, which is multiple-choice options.
6. For subsequent fields (gift_frequency, gift_option_1, gift_option_2, and gift_option_3), select one option from the respective fields to express your gift preferences.
7. Enter a date (if applicable) in the date field.
8. Enter a time (if applicable) in the time field.
9. Provide any additional comments in the comment field.
10. Confirm your submission by selecting an option from the confirm field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **donation_amount**: Enter a numeric value for your donation.
* **donation_frequency**: Select your preferred donation frequency.
* **gift_frequency**: Select your preferred gift frequency. This field is multiple-choice options.
* **gift_option_1**: Select one option from the gift frequency options.
* **gift_option_2**: Select one option from the gift frequency options.
* **gift_option_3**: Select one option from the gift frequency options.
* **date**: Enter a date if you wish to schedule a recurring donation.
* **time**: Enter a time for the scheduled donation (if applicable).
* **comment**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **confirm**: Confirm your submission by selecting an option from the confirm field.
