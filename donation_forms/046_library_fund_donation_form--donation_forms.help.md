# library_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations from library patrons for the library fund. It is intended for one-time submissions from library patrons and is used to gather information about the donation, such as the amount donated, payment method, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you with any updates or confirmations about your donation.
3. If you have a **Phone** number, you can enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the **Amount Donated** in the "Amount Donated" field. This should be a number, not a written description.
5. Select your **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu:
	* **Credit Card**: We accept credit card payments through our secure online system.
	* **Bank Account**: We also accept bank account transfers.
	* **Check**: You can also pay by check, but please note that we will not be able to contact you to confirm your payment.
6. Select your **Recurring Donations** frequency:
	* **Monthly**: You will be donating a fixed amount every month.
	* **Yearly**: You will be donating a fixed amount every year.
	* **One Time**: This is a one-time donation, not recurring.
7. If you have any **Notes** about your donation, you can enter them in this field.
8. Finally, enter the **Date** and **Time** of your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here, which will help us contact you about your donation.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number here, but it's not required.
* **Amount Donated**: Enter a numerical value for the amount you're donating.
* **Payment Method**: Select from the options provided (Credit Card, Bank Account, Check).
* **Recurring Donations**: Choose how often you want to make your donation (Monthly, Yearly, One Time).
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your donation.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your donation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct amount and payment method to avoid any issues with your donation.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us through our contact form or email.
* We appreciate your support for our library, and thank you for considering a donation!
