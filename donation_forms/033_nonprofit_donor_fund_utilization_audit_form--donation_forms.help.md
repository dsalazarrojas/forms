# Nonprofit Donor Fund Utilization Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the utilization of funds provided by nonprofit donors. It helps to ensure that the funds are being used transparently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nonprofit Donor Fund Utilization** (`non_profit_donor_fund_utilization`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose and goals of the fund utilization.
* **Nonprofit Donor Amount Funds** (`non_profit_donor_amount_funds`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of funds received from the non-profit donor.
* **Nonprofit Donor Cause** (`non_profit_donor_cause`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant causes or purposes that the funds will be used for, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2".
* **Nonprofit Donor Periodic Report** (`non_profit_donor_periodic_report`, date, optional): Enter the date when the next periodic report is due.
* **Nonprofit Donor Annual Report** (`non_profit_donor_annual_report`, select_one, required): Select the type of annual report required, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2".
* **Nonprofit Donor Fund Usage Reporting** (`non_profit_donor_fund_usage_reporting`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the fund usage.
* **Nonprofit Donor Comments** (`non_profit_donor_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the non-profit donor.
* **Nonprofit Donor Email** (`non_profit_donor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the non-profit donor.

**Tips**: Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities or support team.
