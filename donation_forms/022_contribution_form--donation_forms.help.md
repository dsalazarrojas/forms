# Contribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations from contributors and ensure that all required information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your personal information, including your full name, address, city, state, zip code, email, and phone number.
2.  Provide your employment information, including your employer's name and address.
3.  Specify the amount and date of your contribution.
4.  Choose the type and purpose of your contribution.
5.  Select the method of payment and any additional payment details.
6.  Confirm your eligibility to make a contribution and your understanding of federal contribution limits.
7.  Declare the source of your funds and any other relevant information.
8.  Provide the recipient's name and type.
9.  Certify that all information provided is accurate and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contributor Full Name** (`contributor_name`, text, required): Fill in your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Contributor Address** (`contributor_address`, text, required): Enter your full mailing address.
- **City** (`contributor_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
- **State** (`contributor_state`, text, required): Enter the state of your residence.
- **ZIP Code** (`contributor_zip`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
- **Email Address** (`contributor_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (`contributor_phone`, text, optional): If you want to be contacted, enter your phone number.
- **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter your employer's name as it appears on official documents.
- **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, optional): Enter your employer's business address.
- **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required): Enter your job title or profession.
- **Contribution Amount** (`contribution_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the contribution in dollars.
- **Contribution Date** (`contribution_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the contribution.
- **Type of Contribution** (`contribution_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of contribution (Monetary Donation, In-Kind Services, Fundraising Event, Bundled Contribution, or Other).
- **Purpose of Contribution** (`contribution_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose the purpose of the contribution (Campaign Support, General Fund, Specific Initiative, Event Sponsorship, or Other).
- **Method of Payment** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of payment (Check, Credit Card, Wire Transfer, Cash, In-Kind, or Other).
- **Check Number** (`check_number`, text, optional): If paying by check, enter the check number.
- **Credit Card Type** (`card_type`, select_one, optional): If paying by card, choose the type of card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, Not Applicable).
- **Are you a US citizen or permanent resident?** (`us_citizen`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are a US citizen or permanent resident.
- **Are these funds your own?** (`funds_own`, select_one, required): Declare whether these funds are your own.
- **Will you NOT be reimbursed for this contribution?** (`not_reimbursed`, select_one, required): Declare whether you will not be reimbursed for this contribution.
- **I understand federal contribution limits apply** (`federal_limit_compliance`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of federal contribution limits.
- **Source of Funds** (`source_of_funds`, select_one, required): Choose the source of the funds (Personal Savings, Salary, Investment Returns, Business Profits, Loan, Gift, or Other).
- **Source Details** (`source_details`, text, optional): If other, provide additional information about the source of funds.
- **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the recipient.
- **Recipient Type** (`recipient_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of recipient (Political Candidate, Political Action Committee, Political Party, Advocacy Group, or Other).
- **I certify that all information provided is accurate** (`information_accurate`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information provided is accurate.
- **I am at least 18 years of age** (`legal_age`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are at least 18 years of age.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of certification.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information.

Please note that some fields may be optional, but filling them out can help ensure that your contribution is accurately recorded and reported.
