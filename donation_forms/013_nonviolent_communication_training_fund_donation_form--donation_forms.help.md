# nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonviolent Communication (NVC) training fund donation form is designed to collect donations to support the NVC training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your donation amount in the "donation amount" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "contact information" field.
3. Select the type of donation you are making in the "donation type" field.
4. If making a recurring donation, select the frequency of the donation in the "donation frequency" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field (optional).
7. Add any additional comments in the "comments" field (optional).
8. Enter the date and time of the donation in the "donation date" and "donation time" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_name`): Enter the amount of your donation. This can be a one-time or recurring amount.
* **Contact Information** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_contact_information`): Provide your name, address, and other contact details for follow-up purposes.
* **Donation Type** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_donation_type`): Select the type of donation you are making, such as one-time or recurring.
* **Donation Frequency** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_donation_frequency`): If making a recurring donation, select the frequency of the donation (e.g., monthly, quarterly).
* **Email** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up and confirmation purposes.
* **Phone** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Comments** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_comments`): Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Donation Date** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_donation_date`): Enter the date of the donation (optional).
* **Donation Time** (`nonviolent_communication_training_fund_donation_form_donation_time`): Enter the time of the donation (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that the email address and phone number you provide are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are making a recurring donation, please note that we may contact you for further details or to confirm your donation frequency.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
