# Hunger Relief Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hunger Relief Donation Form is a simple form designed to collect donations for hunger relief efforts. Fill out this form to contribute to the cause and help make a difference in the lives of those struggling with food insecurity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Donation Form** field with your donation amount.
2. Select the **Hunger Relief Amount** field and choose the amount you wish to donate.
3. Choose the **Donation Currency** field, where you can select the currency of your donation (USD, EUR, or GBP).
4. For **Payment Method**, type in a payment method of your choice (e.g., PayPal, credit card, etc.).
5. Enter your **Email** address so we can keep you updated on your donation.
6. Optionally, provide your **Phone** number if you'd like to receive a call from us.
7. Finally, type in your **Name** and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Form**: Enter the amount you wish to donate to support hunger relief efforts.
* **Hunger Relief Amount**: This field is where you confirm the amount you wish to donate, making sure it matches the amount you entered in the previous field.
* **Donation Currency**: Select the currency of your donation (USD, EUR, or GBP) to help us process your contribution correctly.
* **Payment Method**: Enter your preferred payment method, such as PayPal or credit card details.
* **Email**: Provide your email address so we can keep you updated on your donation and follow up with any further information or details.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to receive a call from us to confirm your donation, enter your phone number here.
* **Name**: Finally, type in your name to associate with your donation and make it easier for us to contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct currency for your donation to avoid any processing issues.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with filling out this form, don't hesitate to contact us at your email or phone number provided.
