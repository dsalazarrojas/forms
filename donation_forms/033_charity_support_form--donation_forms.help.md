# Charity Support Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help our charity understand your support and preferences for donations. By providing the necessary information, you'll be able to contribute to our cause and stay updated on our activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, which is the name you'd like to be known by.
2. Provide your **Email Address**, so we can send you receipts and updates.
3. If you'd like, you can also enter your **Phone Number** for an optional contact point.
4. Fill in your **Mailing Address** to help us get your donations to you.
5. Choose how you would like to **Support Us** - select from one of the available options (e.g., Monetary, In-Kind, Volunteer, Sponsorship, Advocacy, Multiple Ways).
6. Enter your preferred **Donation Amount** for the chosen support type.
7. Choose how frequently you'd like to **Donate** - select from the options (e.g., One-Time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
8. If you'd like, you can specify what your **Donation Should Support** - choose from the available options (e.g., General, Specific, Emergency, Building, Donor Choice).
9. Select your areas of **Greatest Interest** - choose from the available options (e.g., Youth, Seniors, Healthcare, Education, Community, Environmental, Hunger).
10. Enter your **Motivation** for supporting this charity - share with us why you're supporting us.
11. If you'd like, you can choose how you'd like to be **Recognized** - select from the options (e.g., Public, Private, Anonymous, Do Not Recognize).
12. Let us know if you'd like to receive **Updates** - select from the options (e.g., Yes, No).
13. If you need a **Tax Receipt**, select Yes.
14. Finally, enter the **Date of Support** - this will help us track your support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 2, required): Your complete name.
* **Email Address** (id: 3, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and receipts.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4, optional): Your phone number for an optional contact point.
* **Mailing Address** (id: 5, optional): Enter your address so we can get donations to you.
* **Support Type** (id: 9, required): Choose from available options how you'd like to support us (e.g., Monetary, In-Kind, Volunteer, Sponsorship, Advocacy, Multiple Ways).
* **Donation Amount** (id: 11, required): Enter the amount of your donation in dollars.
* **Donation Frequency** (id: 12, required): Choose how often you'd like to donate (e.g., One-Time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Donation Focus** (id: 13, optional): Select what your donation should support if you have a specific program in mind.
* **Impact Interest** (id: 15, optional): Select your areas of greatest interest (e.g., Youth, Seniors, Healthcare, Education, Community, Environmental, Hunger).
* **Motivation** (id: 16, optional): Share with us why you're supporting this charity.
* **Recognition Preference** (id: 18, optional): Choose how you'd like to be recognized (e.g., Public, Private, Anonymous, Do Not Recognize).
* **Update Preference** (id: 19, required): Let us know if you'd like to receive updates (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Tax Receipt** (id: 20, required): If you need a tax receipt, select Yes.
* **Date of Support** (id: 21, required): Enter the date of your support.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are completed.
- For optional fields, feel free to provide any relevant information that helps us understand your support better.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
