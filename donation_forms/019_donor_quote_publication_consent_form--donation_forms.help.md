# Donor Quote Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from donors for the publication of their quotes or testimonials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the options for the first field, "Donor Quote Publication", and select the option that best represents your preference.
2. In the "Quote Consent" field, enter a brief description of your consent.
3. For "Quote Permission Signed", select "Yes" if you have signed the permission, and "No" otherwise.
4. If you have provided an email address, please enter it in the "Email Signature" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Donor Permission Signed" field to indicate whether you have signed the donor permission.
6. Review the options for "Donor Quote Publication Consent Form 2" and select the option that best represents your preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Quote Publication** (`Donor Quote Publication`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks you to indicate whether you consent to the publication of your quote. Select "Yes" if you are okay with it, and "No" otherwise.
* **Quote Consent** (`quote_consent`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of your consent.
* **Quote Permission Signed** (`quote_permission_signed`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to confirm whether you have signed the permission. Select "Yes" if you have signed it, and "No" otherwise.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): This field is for you to sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Email Signature** (`email_signature`, email, required/optional): If you have provided an email address, enter it here to confirm your consent.
* **Donor Permission Signed** (`donor_permission_signed`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks you to confirm whether you have signed the donor permission. Select "Yes" if you have signed it, and "No" otherwise.
* **Donor Quote Publication Consent Form 2** (`donor_quote_publication_consent_form_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks you to indicate whether you consent to the publication of your quote. Select "Yes" if you are okay with it, and "No" otherwise.
