# Reforestation Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reforestation Donation Form is used to collect information from individuals who want to make a donation to support reforestation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your donor information in the "Donor Information" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Donor Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if you have an email address).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (if you have a phone number).
5. Enter any additional project information in the "Project Information" field.
6. Enter the title of the project you are supporting in the "Project Title" field.
7. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donation Amount" field.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
9. If you would like to leave a note or message, enter it in the "Note" field.
10. Confirm your donation by selecting "True" in the "Confirm Donation" field.
11. Confirm that you have read and understood the donation terms by selecting "True" in the "Confirm Donation 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional information about yourself that you would like to share with us.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates about your donation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by our team.
* **Project Information** (`project_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the project you are supporting.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the project you are supporting.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following payment methods:
	+ Bank transfer
	+ Credit card
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional message or note about your donation.
* **Confirm Donation** (`confirm_donation`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you are making a donation by selecting "True".
* **Confirm Donation 2** (`confirm_donation2`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have read and understood the donation terms by selecting "True".

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have entered accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your donation is processed correctly.
- If you have any issues or concerns about your donation, please contact us through the provided email address.
- We appreciate your support for reforestation efforts!
