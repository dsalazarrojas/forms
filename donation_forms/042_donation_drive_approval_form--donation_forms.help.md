# Donation Drive Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for approving or requesting approval for a donation drive. A donation drive is an event where a group or individual collects donations for a specific cause or organization. The form helps to streamline the approval process for these drives, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered and reviewed before approval is granted.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Drive Name** field with the name of the donation drive you are requesting approval for.
2. Enter the **Drive Date** in the format mm/dd/yyyy for the date of the drive.
3. Enter the **Drive Location** where the drive will take place.
4. Select the **Drive Type** from the options provided (e.g. Approval for Donation Drive or Approval for Event).
5. Enter the **Approver Name**, **Approver Email**, and **Approver Phone** of the person requesting approval.
6. Select the **Approver Designation** (e.g. Manager or Director).
7. Enter any additional **Notes** for the approver to review.
8. Select the **Drive Status** (e.g. Active, Pending, or Cancelled).
9. Enter any additional **Drive Notes** for the approver to review.
10. If a second approver is required, repeat steps 5-9 for the second approver.
11. Add any additional **Approver Notes** if necessary.
12. Finally, add any **Form Notes** for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drive Name**: The name of the donation drive you are requesting approval for.
* **Drive Date**: The date of the drive.
* **Drive Location**: The location where the drive will take place.
* **Drive Type**: The type of drive (e.g. Approval for Donation Drive or Approval for Event).
* **Approver Name**: The name of the person requesting approval.
* **Approver Email**: The email of the person requesting approval.
* **Approver Phone**: The phone number of the person requesting approval.
* **Approver Designation**: The designation of the person requesting approval (e.g. Manager or Director).
* **Notes**: Any additional notes for the approver to review.
* **Drive Status**: The status of the drive (e.g. Active, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Drive Notes**: Additional notes for the drive.
* **Approver Name 2**: The name of the second approver.
* **Approver Email 2**: The email of the second approver.
* **Approver Phone 2**: The phone number of the second approver.
* **Approver Designation 2**: The designation of the second approver.
* **Notes 2**: Any additional notes for the second approver to review.
* **Drive Status 2**: The status of the drive for the second approver.
* **Drive Notes 2**: Additional notes for the second drive.
* **Approver Notes**: Any additional notes from the approver.
* **Drive Approval Level**: The level of approval required (e.g. High or Low).
* **Approver Notes 2**: Any additional notes from the second approver.
* **Form Notes**: Additional notes for the form.
