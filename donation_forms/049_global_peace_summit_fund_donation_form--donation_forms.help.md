# Global Peace Summit Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for the Global Peace Summit Fund. It is intended to be used by individuals who wish to donate to the Global Peace Summit Fund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field.
3. Select your preferred donation frequency from the "Donation Frequency" field.
4. Choose your preferred donation method from the "Donation Method" field.
5. Add any additional comments or messages in the "Comment" field.
6. Review the terms of agreement and select "Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (form_1_name)
* **Name (text, required)**: Please enter your full name.

### Donation Amount (form_2_amount)
* **Donation Amount (number, optional)**: Enter the amount you wish to donate. Please use numbers only, without any currency symbol.

### Email (form_3_email)
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted by us.

### Phone (form_4_phone)
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by us.

### Additional Comment (form_9_message)
* **Additional Comment (note, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or messages you have.

### Donation Frequency (form_7_donation_frequency)
* **Donation Frequency (select multiple, optional)**: Select one or more of the following frequencies: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.

### Donation Method (form_8_donation_method)
* **Donation Method (select one, optional)**: Choose your preferred donation method: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.

### Date (form_10_date)
* **Date (date, optional)**: Enter the date you wish to make your donation.

### Time (form_11_time)
* **Time (time, optional)**: Enter the time you wish to make your donation.

### Comment (form_12_comment)
* **Comment (text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or messages you have.

### Agree (form_13_agree)
* **Agree (select one, optional)**: Select "Option 1" or "Option 2".

### Agree Label (form_14_agree_label)
* **Agree Label (text, optional)**: This field is for internal use only.

### Agree Hint (form_15_agree_hint)
* **Agree Hint (text, optional)**: This field is for internal use only.

## Tips
* Please note that all fields are optional, except for the "Name" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your email or phone number.
