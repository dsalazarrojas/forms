# Open Educational Resources Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for open educational resources. Please fill out the form to provide your personal and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Review each field for any additional information or clarification needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Donation Amount** (`amount`, text, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you wish to make your payment (credit card, bank transfer, or check).
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, optional): If you're representing an institution, enter the name of your institution.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your donation.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (`confirm_phone_number`, text, optional): Re-enter your phone number for confirmation.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you'd like to make payments (one-time or recurring).
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you'd like to make payments (weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **Confirm Payment Schedule** (`confirm_payment_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Re-enter your payment schedule for confirmation.
* **Confirm Payment Schedule Frequency** (`confirm_payment_schedule_frequency`, select_one, optional): Re-enter how often you'd like to make payments (one-time or recurring).
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your donation.
* **Payment Method Confirmation** (`payment_method_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Re-enter how you made your payment (credit card, bank transfer, or check).
* **Confirmation Date** (`confirmation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your confirmation.
* **Amount Confirmation** (`amount_confirmation`, text, optional): Re-enter the amount of your donation.
* **Institution Email** (`institution_email`, email, optional): If you're representing an institution, enter the email of your institution.
* **Payment Method Confirmation** (`payment_method_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Re-enter how you made your payment (credit card, bank transfer, or check).
* **Institution Website** (`institution_website`, text, optional): If you're representing an institution, enter the website of your institution.
* **Confirmation Phone Number** (`confirmation_phone_number`, text, optional): Re-enter your phone number for confirmation.
* **Institution Email Confirmation** (`institution_email_confirmation`, text, optional): Re-enter the email of your institution for confirmation.
* **Institution Website Confirmation** (`institution_website_confirmation`, text, optional): Re-enter the website of your institution for confirmation.
* **Phone Number Confirmation** (`confirmation_phone_number`, text, optional): Re-enter your phone number for confirmation.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and honestly.
* Double-check your details for correctness and consistency.
* If you're representing an institution, ensure you have the necessary permissions to collect information on behalf of your institution.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
