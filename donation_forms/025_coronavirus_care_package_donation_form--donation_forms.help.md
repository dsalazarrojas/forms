# Coronavirus Care Package Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in donating care packages to people affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. The form helps to track donor details, order information, and payment details in a secure and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Donor Information** section (Field 1) with your details, including your name and contact information.
2. In the **Order Information** section (Field 2), provide details about the type and quantity of care packages you are interested in donating.
3. In the **Payment Information** section (Field 3), enter your payment method and details (if applicable).
4. Finally, in the **Donation Information** section (Field 4), specify the type and quantity of care packages you are willing to donate, and any additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (Field 1)** (`donor_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information so we can contact you to confirm your donation and provide updates.
* **Order Information (Field 2)** (`order_info`, text, required: true): Describe the type and quantity of care packages you are interested in donating (e.g., "2 care packages with food and toiletries").
* **Payment Information (Field 3)** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): If you are able to pay for shipping or other expenses related to the care packages, you can provide your payment method and details here.
* **Donation Information (Field 4)** (`donation_info`, text, required: true): Specify the type and quantity of care packages you are willing to donate, and include any additional information or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Order Information and Donation Information).
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) and we will assist you.
* Your donations will be acknowledged and used to support those in need. Thank you for your generosity!
