# Habitat Restoration Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from individuals who wish to support habitat restoration projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide information about yourself, including any relevant contact details (section: **Donor Info**).
2. Indicate the project(s) you wish to support by entering a brief description in the **Project Info** section.
3. Specify your desired donation amount using the **Donation Amount** field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided (section: **Payment Method**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Donor Info**
* **Donor Info (donor_info)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information. This field is **optional**, but providing your contact details helps ensure that we can keep you updated about your donation and any project progress. (Type: **text**, **optional**)

### **Project Info**
* **Project Info (project_info)**: Describe the habitat restoration project you wish to support in a few words. This field is **optional** and helps us understand which project your donation is for. (Type: **text**, **optional**)

### **Donation Amount**
* **Donation Amount (donation_amount)**: Enter a numeric value representing the amount you would like to donate. This field is **optional** and does not require any specific formatting. (Type: **number**, **optional**)

### **Payment Method**
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select how you would like to make your donation:
	+ **Cash**: Pay in person or by bank transfer.
	+ **Credit Card**: Pay online using a credit card.
	+ **PayPal**: Pay using your PayPal account. (Type: **select one**, **optional**)
