# Employee Donation Match Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees who wish to request matching donations from their company's Matching Gifts program. This program allows employees to donate to a charity of their choice and have their donations matched by the company. To ensure accurate and efficient processing, please complete this form carefully and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your desired donation option from the "User Input" field.
2.  Choose the non-profit organization(s) you wish to support by selecting them from the "Nonprofit" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
3.  Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (`User Input`, select_one, required): This field allows you to choose the type of donation you are requesting. Please select the option that best describes your intention. 
*   **Nonprofit** (`Nonprofit`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you select the non-profit organization(s) you wish to support. You can choose one or multiple options. Please ensure you select the correct organization(s) for your intended donation.
*   **Amount** (`Amount`, number, optional): This is where you enter the amount you wish to donate. Please enter a numerical value for the amount you are requesting.
