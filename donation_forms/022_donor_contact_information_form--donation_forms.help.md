# Donor Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from donors and gather essential details to communicate with them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address to ensure you receive updates and communications from us.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field. This is the address we will use to mail any materials or documents to you.
6. Select your preferred language from the "Preferred language" dropdown menu.
7. Select the contact methods you would like to use to communicate with you, such as Email, Phone, or Mail, in the "Contact method" field.
8. If you have a specific way you would like to be contacted, enter it in the "How did you hear about us" field.
9. If you would like to receive updates from us, select "True" in the "Would you like to receive updates" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: This is the field where you will enter your first name.
* **Last name**: This is the field where you will enter your last name.
* **Email**: This is the field where you will enter your email address. Please use a valid email address to ensure you receive updates and communications from us.
* **Phone**: This is the field where you will enter your phone number (optional). We will use this number to contact you if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Address**: This is the field where you will enter your address. We will use this address to mail any materials or documents to you.
* **Preferred language**: This is the dropdown menu where you will select your preferred language for communication.
* **Contact method**: This is the dropdown menu where you will select the contact methods you would like to use, such as Email, Phone, or Mail.
* **How did you hear about us**: This is the field where you can enter any specific way you would like to be contacted.
* **Would you like to receive updates**: This is the dropdown menu where you can select whether you would like to receive updates from us.
* **Tips**: If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us. We are here to help.
