# Outdoor Fitness Park Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors to the outdoor fitness park. By filling out this form, you will be able to submit a donation, specify the amount and frequency of your donation, and provide any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your donation.
2. Enter a phone number (optional) for us to contact you for further information.
3. Choose the amount of your donation from the options provided.
4. Select one of the options from the 'Select One' field, which will allow you to specify the type of donation you are making (e.g., equipment, supplies, services).
5. If you are donating multiple items, select all applicable options from the 'Select Multiple' field.
6. Specify the date and time of your donation (optional).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your donation.
8. Set the frequency of your donation (optional, but helps us plan for future donations).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you for further information (optional).
* **Donation Amount**: Choose the amount you are willing to donate to the outdoor fitness park. The options are:
	+ Option 1: $10
	+ Option 2: $20
	+ Option 3: $50
* **Select One**: Choose one of the options to specify the type of donation you are making:
	+ Option 1: Equipment
	+ Option 2: Supplies
	+ Option 3: Services
* **Select Multiple**: If you are donating multiple items, select all applicable options from the list:
	+ Multiple 1: Fitness equipment
	+ Multiple 2: Sports supplies
	+ Multiple 3: Event management services
* **Date**: Specify the date of your donation (optional).
* **Time**: Specify the time of your donation (optional).
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your donation (optional):
	+ Option 1: One-time
	+ Option 2: Weekly
	+ Option 3: Monthly
