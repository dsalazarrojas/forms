# Campaign Fundraising Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by organizers to declare their campaign's fundraising activities, goals, and outcomes. It gathers essential information to track and report fundraising efforts for regulatory compliance and internal record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Organizer Name** in the "Organizer Name" field. This is the name of the person filing the declaration.
2. Provide the **Organization or Campaign** name in the "Organization or Campaign" field. This is the name of the campaign or group conducting the fundraising activity.
3. Enter the **Total Amount Raised** in the "Total Amount Raised" field. This is the total amount collected during the reporting period.
4. Specify the **Fundraiser Period** in the "Fundraiser Period" field. This should cover the dates of the fundraising activity.
5. If applicable, enter the **Number of Donors** in the "Number of Donors" field. This is a count of individual donors.
6. If necessary, list the **Major Donors** in the "Major Donors" field. This is for listing the names of major donors, if applicable.
7. Describe the **Intended Use of Funds** in the "Intended Use of Funds" field. This is how the raised funds will be utilized.
8. Confirm **Compliance** with fundraising rules by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Compliance Confirmed" field.
9. Provide your **Authorized Signature Name** in the "Authorized Signature Name" field. This is the name of the person authorizing this declaration.
10. Add any additional **Additional Notes** in the "Additional Notes" field. This is for any other explanatory information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organizer Name**: Enter the name of the person filing the declaration.
- **Organization or Campaign**: Enter the name of the campaign or group conducting the fundraising activity.
- **Total Amount Raised**: Enter the total amount collected during the reporting period.
- **Fundraiser Period**: Enter the dates covering the fundraising activity.
- **Number of Donors**: If applicable, enter the count of individual donors.
- **Major Donors**: If necessary, list the names of major donors.
- **Intended Use of Funds**: Describe how the raised funds will be utilized.
- **Compliance Confirmed**: Confirm compliance with fundraising rules by selecting "True" or "False".
- **Authorized Signature Name**: Enter the name of the person authorizing this declaration.
- **Additional Notes**: Add any other explanatory information.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- Review and verify the information provided to ensure compliance with fundraising rules.
- Double-check the dates in the "Fundraiser Period" field to ensure they are correct and cover the entire activity.
- If you are listing major donors, make sure to include their names in the "Major Donors" field.
