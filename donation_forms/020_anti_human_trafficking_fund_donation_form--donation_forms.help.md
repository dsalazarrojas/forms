# Anti Human Trafficking Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Human Trafficking Fund Donation Form is designed to allow individuals to make a donation to support efforts to combat human trafficking. This form collects information necessary to process and record the donation accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, if you would like to be acknowledged for your donation. 
2. Provide your contact email address, as this will be used for donation receipt and communication.
3. Enter the amount of the donation in local currency.
4. Choose the frequency of the donation: One time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
5. Select the preferred payment method: Credit card, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Check.
6. If you prefer, you may choose to make this donation anonymous and your name and contact details will be withheld from public reports.
7. If you wish, you may include a gift message or dedication with your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Details** (`section_donation`): This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose.
* **Donor name** (`donor_name`): Enter your name, if you would like to be acknowledged for your donation. This field is optional.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email address. This will be used for donation receipt and communication. *This field is required*.
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`): Enter the amount of the donation in local currency. *This field is required*.
* **Donation frequency** (`donation_frequency`): Choose how often you would like to make this donation: One time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually. *This field is required*.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`): Select your preferred payment method: Credit card, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Check. *This field is required*.
* **Make this donation anonymous** (`anonymous_donation`): If you prefer, you can choose to make this donation anonymous, and your name and contact details will be withheld from public reports. *This field is optional*.
* **Gift message or dedication** (`gift_a_message`): If you wish, you can include a gift message or dedication with your donation. *This field is optional*.

## Tips
* Ensure that your contact email address is accurate to facilitate correct donation receipt and communication.
* Please note that making this donation anonymous means that your name and contact details will be withheld from public reports.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
