# prayer_request_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "prayer_request_form_template" form is designed to collect information from users for prayers and prayer requests. It is intended to help us better understand the needs and intentions of our users, enabling us to provide more personalized and effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Prayer Request" field, which is the main text area where you can enter your prayer request.
2. Enter your Name in the "Name" field.
3. If you have an email address, please enter it in the "Email" field. This is helpful for us to contact you back.
4. You can optionally select a "Donation Amount" if you'd like to make a donation.
5. Choose your preferred "Donation Frequency" from the options provided.
6. Select the type of "Donation Type" you'd like to make.
7. If you have a specific "Message" you'd like to share, you can do so in the "Message" field.
8. Enter the time you'd like to make your donation in the "Time" field.
9. Choose a "Date" for your donation, if you'd like to schedule it for a specific date.
10. Select a "Category" for your prayer request.
11. Optionally, you can enter a "Prayer Name" and a "Prayer Message" if you have a specific prayer request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prayer Request** (text): Enter your specific prayer request in this field.
* **Name** (text): Enter your name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you back.
* **Donation Amount** (number): Optionally, enter the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (select_one): Choose how often you'd like to make your donation. Options include "Once", "Weekly", "Monthly", etc.
* **Donation Type** (select_multiple): Select the type of donation you'd like to make. Options include "Prayer", "Candle", etc.
* **Message** (text): Enter any additional message you'd like to share with us.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of your donation.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of your donation.
* **Category** (select_one): Select a category for your prayer request. Options include "Healing", "Protection", etc.
* **Prayer Name** (text): Optionally, enter the name of your prayer.
* **Prayer Message** (text): Enter any additional message related to your prayer.
* **Candle Type** (select_multiple): Select the type of candle you'd like to light for your prayer.
* **Candle Color** (select_one): Select the color of the candle you'd like to light.
* **Candle Size** (select_one): Select the size of the candle you'd like to light.
* **Prayer Type** (select_multiple): Select the type of prayer you'd like to make. Options include "Prayer", "Candle", etc.
* **Prayer Name** (text): Optionally, enter the name of your prayer.
* **Prayer Message** (text): Enter any additional message related to your prayer.
