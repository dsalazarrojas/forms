# Rare Disease Research Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors to support rare disease research. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide crucial information to the research organization to help them better understand your intentions and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, ensure you have all the necessary information ready. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you complete the form:

1. **Donor Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
2. **Donor Title**: Choose your title (Mr, Ms, Dr, Mrs, Prof, or Rev).
3. **Donor E-mail**: Enter your valid email address. This will help us contact you with updates and requests.
4. **Donor Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
5. **Donor Address 1** and **Donor Address 2**: Enter your complete address, including street and postal code (optional).
6. **Donor Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with us (optional).
7. **Donor Type**: Choose how you want to be associated with the research (Researcher, Advocate, Family Member).
8. **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you'd like to donate.
9. **Donation Method**: Select how you'd like to donate (One time or Monthly).
10. **Frequency of Donation**: If you selected Monthly or Quarterly, choose how often you'd like to donate (Never, Monthly, or Quarterly).
11. **Donor Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to be contacted about the research (Never, Monthly, or Quarterly).
12. **Disease for Research**: Select the rare disease you'd like to support.
13. **Disease Notes**: Add any additional information about the disease (optional).
14. **Donation Date**: Enter the date you'd like to start or have started your donation (optional).
15. **Donor Birthday**: Enter your date of birth (optional).
16. **Donor Gender**: Select your gender (Male or Female).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name**: Your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Donor Title**: Your title (Mr, Ms, Dr, Mrs, Prof, or Rev).
* **Donor E-mail**: Your valid email address for communication.
* **Donor Phone**: Your phone number (optional).
* **Donor Address 1** and **Donor Address 2**: Your complete address, including street and postal code (optional).
* **Donor Notes**: Additional information you'd like to share with us (optional).
* **Donor Type**: How you want to be associated with the research (Researcher, Advocate, Family Member).
* **Donation Amount**: The amount you'd like to donate.
* **Donation Method**: How you'd like to donate (One time or Monthly).
* **Frequency of Donation**: How often you'd like to donate if you selected a recurring method.
* **Donor Frequency**: How often you'd like to be contacted about the research.
* **Disease for Research**: The rare disease you'd like to support.
* **Disease Notes**: Additional information about the disease (optional).
* **Donation Date**: The date you'd like to start or have started your donation (optional).
* **Donor Birthday**: Your date of birth (optional).
* **Donor Gender**: Your gender (Male or Female).

By following this guide, you'll be able to provide the necessary information to support rare disease research and help make a difference in the lives of those affected.
