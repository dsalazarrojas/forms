# IT Equipment Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for requesting IT equipment donations from individuals or organizations. It is designed to collect essential information to facilitate a smooth and efficient donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide detailed descriptions for each field to ensure a thorough evaluation.
3. Double-check for spelling and grammar errors before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IT Equipment Donation Request** (`form_data`, text, required): Briefly describe the IT equipment you are willing to donate, including its condition and quantity.
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, text, required): Fill in your contact details, including your name, organization (if applicable), and a valid email address.
* **IT Equipment** (`it_equipment`, text, required): Provide detailed descriptions of the donated IT equipment, including specifications, condition, and quantity.
* **Donation Details** (`donation_details`, text, required): Specify the type of donation you are offering, e.g., one-time, recurring, or ongoing.
* **Pickup or Dropoff Info** (`pickup_dropoff_info`, text, required): Provide information on how you would like to arrange for pickup or dropoff, including pickup location and preferred date and time.
* **Donation Status** (`donation_status`, text, required): Indicate the status of your donation, e.g., pending, confirmed, or declined.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, text, required): Fill in your name and department/role (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Include any additional comments or requests, if any.

Note: All fields are required for a complete submission, unless otherwise specified. Please fill them in accordingly to facilitate a thorough evaluation and efficient processing.
