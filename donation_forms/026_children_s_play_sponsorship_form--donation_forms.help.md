# Children's Play Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children's Play Sponsorship Form is used to collect information from sponsors who wish to support a play. This form helps the organization manage sponsorship relationships and ensures that all necessary information is collected in a structured way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the sponsor's information in the "Sponsor Information" section.
*   Select the sponsor type (Individual, Business, Organization, or Foundation).
*   Enter the sponsor's email address.
*   Enter the sponsor's phone number.
*   Enter the sponsor's address (optional).
*   Select the play to sponsor (e.g. Shakespeare Classics, Modern Dramas, etc.).
*   Choose the preferred performance dates.
*   Select the sponsorship level (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, or Custom Amount).
*   If choosing Custom Amount, enter the custom sponsorship amount.
*   If interested in VIP experience, select "Yes".
*   Select the preferred payment method (Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
*   Choose when you can make the donation (Immediately, Within one week, Within two weeks, or Before opening night).
*   Select any additional support you'd like to provide (Props, Costumes, Technical Equipment, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor.
*   **Sponsor Type**: Select the type of sponsor (Individual, Business, Organization, or Foundation).
*   **Email Address**: Enter the sponsor's email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the sponsor's phone number.
*   **Address**: Enter the sponsor's address (optional).
*   **Play Selection**: Select the play to sponsor (e.g. Shakespeare Classics, Modern Dramas, etc.).
*   **Preferred Performance Dates**: Choose the preferred performance dates.
*   **Sponsorship Level**: Select the sponsorship level (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, or Custom Amount).
*   **Custom Sponsorship Amount**: If choosing Custom Amount, enter the custom sponsorship amount.
*   **Recognition and Benefits**: Select how you'd like to be recognized (Program listing, Website listing, Social media mention, etc.).
*   **Number of Complimentary Tickets**: Enter the number of complimentary tickets you'd like to request.
*   **VIP Experience**: If interested, select "Yes" for a VIP experience.
*   **Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method (Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
*   **When Can You Make the Donation**: Choose when you can make the donation (Immediately, Within one week, Within two weeks, or Before opening night).
*   **Mailing Address for Tax Receipt**: Enter the mailing address for the tax receipt (optional).
*   **Additional Support**: Enter any additional support you'd like to provide (Props, Costumes, Technical Equipment, etc.).
