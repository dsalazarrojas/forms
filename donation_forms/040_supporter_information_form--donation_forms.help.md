# Supporter Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about supporters of your organization. It's used to understand their contact details, preferences, and interests. This data helps you tailor your communication and engagement strategies to better serve your supporters.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Indicate your support preferences, such as whether you prefer to be contacted by phone, email, or both.
3. Choose your preferred frequency of communication, such as how often you'd like to receive updates or requests for support.
4. Select the topics or interests you're passionate about, so we can share relevant information with you.
5. If you're a donor or supporter, indicate how frequently you'd like to donate or support.
6. Choose whether you're an active or inactive supporter.
7. Let us know your type of relationship with our organization (e.g., supporter, volunteer).
8. Provide your name if you'd like to be contacted.
9. Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
10. Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by email.
11. Provide your address if you'd like to be contacted at your home or office.
12. Leave any additional notes or comments about your support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (text): Enter your full name and other contact information.
* **Support Preferences** (select multiple): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., Support, Volunteer, Both).
* **Contact Method** (select one): Select how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Both, Not Specified).
* **Contact Frequency** (number): Indicate how often you'd like to receive updates or requests for support.
* **Supporter Interests** (select multiple): Select the topics or interests you're passionate about.
* **Donation Frequency** (number): Indicate how frequently you'd like to donate or support.
* **Supporter Preference Frequency** (select one): Choose how frequently you'd like to be contacted about your supporter preferences.
* **Supporter Status** (select multiple): Choose whether you're an active or inactive supporter.
* **Supporter Type** (select one): Select your relationship with our organization (e.g., supporter, volunteer).
* **Name** (text): Enter your name if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Email** (text): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by email.
* **Address** (text): Enter your address if you'd like to be contacted at your home or office.
* **Notes** (note): Leave any additional notes or comments about your support.

Tips:

* Please fill in all required fields (indicated by *) to ensure your information is complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Your input will help us better understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to provide more effective support.
* This form will be used to personalize your communication and engagement with our organization.
