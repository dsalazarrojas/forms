# Alumni Association Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Association Donation Form is a tool for individuals to provide personal and donation information to the association. This form helps the association to understand donors' preferences and manage donations effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide your graduation year from our institution.
3. Enter your email address where you would like to receive your receipt.
4. (Optional) Enter your phone number for further communication.
5. Select where you would like your gift to be applied.
6. Choose your donation frequency.
7. Decide if you want your donation to be anonymous.
8. If necessary, enter any additional comments regarding your gift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: <full_name> is the space to enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ This is a required field.
* **Graduation Year**: <graduation_year> is the space to enter the year you graduated from our institution.
	+ This is a required field.
* **Email Address**: <email_address> is the space to enter the email address where you would like to receive your receipt.
	+ This is a required field.
* **Phone Number**: <phone_number> is the space to enter your phone number for further communication.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Donation Amount**: <donation_amount> is the space to enter the amount of your donation in USD.
	+ This is a required field.
* **Donation Designation**: <donation_designation> is the space to select where you would like your gift to be applied.
	+ This is a required field with options General Fund, Scholarship Fund, Athletics Program, Research Initiatives, and Arts and Culture.
* **Donation Frequency**: <donation_frequency> is the space to select your donation frequency.
	+ This is a required field with options One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, and Annually.
* **Would you like this donation to be anonymous?**: <anonymous_donation> is the space to decide if you want your donation to be anonymous.
	+ This is a required field with options True and False.
* **Additional Comments**: <additional_comments> is the space for any specific instructions or comments regarding your gift.
	+ This field is optional.
