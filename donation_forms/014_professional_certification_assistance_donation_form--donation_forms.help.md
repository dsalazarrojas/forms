# Professional Certification Assistance Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a professional certification assistance donation, which helps fund the development and maintenance of tools and resources for certification programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your donor details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your name as you would like it to be displayed on the donation record.
3. Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your donation.
4. Specify the amount you would like to donate.
5. If you have a specific purpose for your donation, please enter that here.
6. If you have any additional message for us, please enter that here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Details**: This field is optional and allows you to provide additional information about yourself as a donor.
- **Donor Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the donation record.
- **Donor Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your donation.
- **Donation Amount**: Specify the amount you would like to donate.
- **Purpose of Donation**: If you have a specific purpose for your donation, please enter that here.
- **Donor Message**: If you have any additional message for us, please enter that here.
- **Certification**: Enter the name of the certification you are interested in supporting.
- **Certification Level**: Enter the level of the certification you are interested in supporting (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
- **Certification Year**: Enter the year you are interested in supporting (e.g., 2022, 2023, etc.).
- **Certification Status**: Enter the status of the certification you are interested in supporting (e.g., pending, active, expired).
- **Assigned Tool**: This field is optional and is used by administrators to assign tools and resources to certified individuals.
- **Assigned Tool ID**: This field is optional and is used by administrators to track the tool ID assigned to certified individuals.
- **Assigned Tool**: This field is likely intended to be used by administrators to assign tools and resources to certified individuals, but it is currently not well-defined.
