# Charity Partnership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to apply for partnership opportunities with our organization. It will help us understand your charity's goals, needs, and qualifications for partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
2. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
3. Use the help guide below for specific guidance on each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, **required**): Enter the legal name of your charitable organization.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, **required**): Provide the first name of the primary contact for your organization.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, **required**): Enter the last name of the primary contact for your organization.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the primary contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of the primary contact.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of organization you represent (e.g., 501c3 nonprofit, 501c4 organization, Foundation, etc.).
* **EIN or Tax ID** (`ein_number`, text, **required**): Enter your organization's Tax ID or Employer Identification Number.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, **required**): Enter the full address of your organization.
* **Website URL** (`website`, text, **required**): Enter the URL of your website.
* **Year Established** (`year_established`, number, **required**): Enter the year your organization was established.
* **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, text, **required**): Enter a brief statement about your organization's mission.
* **Type of Partnership Sought** (`partnership_type`, select_multiple, **required**): Check all applicable partnership types you're interested in (e.g., funding, program collaboration, etc.).
* **Funding Amount Requested** (`funding_amount`, select_one, **optional**): If you're seeking funding, select the range that applies to you.
* **Project or Program Description** (`project_description`, text, **required**): Describe the project or program you're proposing.
* **Target Population** (`target_population`, text, **required**): Describe the population your organization serves.
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, text, **required**): Enter the expected duration of your project or program.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, **required**): Describe the expected outcomes of your project or program.
* **Budget Overview** (`budget_overview`, text, **required**): Provide a brief financial summary of your project or program.
* **Prior Partnerships** (`prior_partnerships`, text, **optional**): List any past partnerships with our organization or similar organizations.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, **required**): Indicate whether you can submit supporting documents, such as 990 forms, etc.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, **optional**): List the names and contacts of professional references.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional information you'd like to share about your application.
