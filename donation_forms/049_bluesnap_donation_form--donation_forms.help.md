# BlueSnap Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donation information from users and allow them to specify their donation amount, frequency, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select your donation amount from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose your donation frequency and recognition preferences.
5. Select your payment method.
6. Provide any additional information as needed.
7. Review your submission before clicking "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (donor_full_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (donor_email_address): Enter your valid email address that you want us to use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (donor_phone_number): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with updates or other information.
* **Mailing Address** (donor_mailing_address): Enter your street address, including your street number and postal code.
* **City** (donor_city): Enter the city where you live.
* **State/Province** (donor_state): Enter the state or province where you live.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (donor_postal_code): Enter your postal code for mailing purposes.
* **Organization Name** (organization_name): If you are representing an organization, enter the name here.
* **Donation Amount** (donation_amount_select): Select from the dropdown menu or enter a custom amount if desired.
* **Custom Amount** (custom_donation_amount): If you selected "Custom Amount" above, enter the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Type of Fund** (fund_type): Choose where you'd like your donation to go to.
* **Donation Frequency** (donation_frequency_type): Choose how frequently you'd like to make this donation.
* **How Should We Recognize You?** (donor_recognition): Choose if you'd like to be recognized for this donation publicly or not.
* **Need Tax Documentation?** (tax_deduction_required): Choose if you need tax documentation for your donation.
* **Subscribe to Our Newsletter?** (newsletter_subscription): Choose if you'd like to stay updated on our activities.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method_selection): Choose how you'd like to make your donation.
* **Donation Date** (donation_submission_date): Choose the date you'd like to make your donation on.

Note: All required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
