# Donation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about donors, including their name, contact details, and preferred method of contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Donor Name" field with your actual name.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact" field. You can choose from "Contact via Email", "Contact via Phone", or "Contact via SMS".
4. If you have a contact number, you can enter it in the "Contact Number" field. This will help us reach out to you if we need more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donor Name
* **Donor Name**: Enter your actual name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, drivers' license). (text, required: true)
### Email
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you. (email, required: true)
### Preferred Contact
* **Preferred Contact**: Select how you would like us to contact you: Contact via Email, Contact via Phone, or Contact via SMS. (select_one, required: false)
### Contact Number
* **Contact Number**: If you have a contact number, enter it here. This will help us reach out to you if needed. (text, required: false)
