# Donor Campaign Outcome Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the outcomes and performance of a donor campaign. It helps to gather information about the campaign's success and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the outcome of the campaign from the "campaign_outcome" dropdown list.
2. Choose the performance of the campaign from the "campaign_performance" dropdown list (multiple selections are allowed).
3. Enter any feedback from the donor about their experience (in the "donor_feedback" text field).
4. Enter the name of the donor who participated in the campaign (in the "donor_name" text field).
5. Enter the email address of the donor who participated in the campaign (in the "donor_email" text field).
6. Enter the amount donated by the donor (in the "donation_amount" numeric field).
7. Enter the name of the nonprofit organization running the campaign (in the "nonprofit_name" text field).
8. Enter the start date of the campaign (in the "campaign_start_date" date field).
9. Enter the end date of the campaign (in the "campaign_end_date" date field).
10. Select the status of the form submission (in the "form_submitted" dropdown list).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Campaign Outcome**: Select the outcome of the campaign from the dropdown list. For example, if the campaign had a positive impact, select "Outcome 1". If the campaign did not have a positive impact, select "Outcome 2" or "Outcome 3".
- **Campaign Performance**: Choose the performance of the campaign by selecting multiple options from the dropdown list. For example, if the campaign had good communication with donors, select "Option 1". If the campaign had good social media engagement, select "Option 2". If the campaign had good event organization, select "Option 3".
- **Donor Feedback**: Enter any feedback from the donor about their experience with the campaign.
- **Donor Name**: Enter the name of the donor who participated in the campaign.
- **Donor Email**: Enter the email address of the donor who participated in the campaign.
- **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount donated by the donor.
- **Nonprofit Name**: Enter the name of the nonprofit organization running the campaign.
- **Campaign Start Date**: Enter the start date of the campaign.
- **Campaign End Date**: Enter the end date of the campaign.
- **Form Submitted**: Select the status of the form submission, for example, if the form was submitted correctly, select "Option 1". If the form was not submitted correctly, select "Option 2" or "Option 3".
