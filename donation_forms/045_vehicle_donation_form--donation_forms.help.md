# Vehicle Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Donation Form is used to collect and record information about vehicles being donated to an organization, including details about the vehicle, the donor, and the status of the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the required fields**: Only the fields marked as "required" must be filled in. This is to ensure accuracy and completeness of the data.
2. **Provide accurate and relevant information**: Enter accurate and relevant details about the vehicle, the donor, and the donation.
3. **Review and edit your submission**: Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the vehicle being donated.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, required/optional): Provide additional details about the vehicle, such as make, model, and year.
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact details of the person or organization donating the vehicle.
* **Donation Information** (`donation_info`, text, required/optional): Enter details about the donation, such as date and time of donation.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, required/optional): Describe the overall condition of the vehicle being donated.
* **Vehicle Title** (`vehicle_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the vehicle being donated.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the year of the vehicle being donated.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number, required/optional): Enter the mileage of the vehicle being donated.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): Enter the make of the vehicle being donated.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required/optional): Enter the model of the vehicle being donated.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or organization donating the vehicle.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the donor.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the donor.
* **Donor City** (`donor_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of the donor.
* **Donor State** (`donor_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of the donor.
* **Donor ZIP** (`donor_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the donor.
* **Donor Country** (`donor_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of the donor.
* **Donor Notes** (`donor_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the donor.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the vehicle being donated (Active or Inactive).
* **Donation Status** (`donation_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the donation (Active or Inactive).
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the donation.
* **Donation Time** (`donation_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the donation.
* **Vehicle Donation Type** (`vehicle_donation_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the vehicle is donated (Yes or No).
* **Donation Fund** (`donation_fund`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the donation is for a specific fund (Yes or No).

Note: Fields marked as "required/optional" may be filled in or left blank depending on the specific requirements of the organization. 

This guide is not exhaustive, and additional information or clarification may be needed for specific fields or situations.
