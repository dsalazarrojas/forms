# Memorial Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Memorial Donation Form is a tool to collect information for a charitable donation in memory of someone who has passed away. It is designed to help individuals or organizations collect personal and contact information from donors, as well as provide a message or dedication to honor the deceased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your name and contact information in the **Name**, **Donor Name**, and **Donor Email** fields.
3. Enter the amount of the donation in the **Amount** field.
4. Provide a message or dedication to honor the deceased in the **Message** field (optional).
5. Enter the **Honoree First Name**, **Honoree Last Name**, **Honoree Date of Birth**, and **Honoree Date of Death** fields with the correct information.
6. Select the **Honoree Relationship** field with the correct relationship to the deceased.
7. Enter your **Donation Date**.
8. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: * (Required) Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on any recognition materials.
- **Amount**: * (Required) Enter the amount of the donation.
- **Message**: (Optional) Enter a message or dedication to honor the deceased.
- **Honoree First Name**: * (Required) Enter the first name of the person being honored.
- **Honoree Last Name**: * (Required) Enter the last name of the person being honored.
- **Honoree Date of Birth**: * (Required) Enter the date of birth of the person being honored.
- **Honoree Date of Death**: * (Required) Enter the date of death of the person being honored.
- **Honoree Relationship**: * (Required) Select the relationship you have to the person being honored (e.g. family member, friend, colleague, etc.).
- **Donor Name**: * (Required) Enter your name as the person making the donation.
- **Donor Email**: * (Required) Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Donor Phone**: (Optional) Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Donation Date**: * (Required) Enter the date of the donation.
- **Submit**: * (Required) Click this button to complete and submit the form.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Please fill out all required fields accurately to ensure that the form can be submitted correctly.
