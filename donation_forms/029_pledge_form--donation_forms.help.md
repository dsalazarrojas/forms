# Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pledge Form" is designed to collect information from donors about their financial commitment to support a cause or organization. This form is typically used during events, campaigns, or other fundraising activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your "Donation Amount" in the designated field. You can type in a numeric value.
2. Provide your "Donor Name" in the text field.
3. Enter your "Email" address in the correct format (e.g., `example@domain.com`).
4. Select one of the options for "Frequency of Giving" (e.g., weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
5. If you have a "Phone" number, enter it in the designated field.
6. If you have an "Address", enter it in the text field.
7. In the "Pledge Amount in Words" field, provide a written description of your pledge amount (e.g., "One thousand dollars").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to donate. This field requires a numeric value.
* **Donor Name**: Provide your name as you would like to be contacted by us. This is a text field.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you and provide updates on the progress of the cause. This field requires a specific email format (e.g., `example@domain.com`).
* **Frequency of Giving**: Select one of the options: weekly, biweekly, or monthly, to indicate how frequently you would like to donate.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here. You can format the phone number as you prefer (e.g., `123-456-7890` or `1 234 567 890`).
* **Address**: Enter your address if you would like to be contacted by mail or to receive updates on the cause's progress. This field is optional.
* **Pledge Amount in Words**: Provide a written description of your pledge amount (e.g., "One thousand dollars"). This field is optional but helps us understand the context of your pledge.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure we can contact you successfully.
* If you change your mind about your pledge amount, you can contact us to update your pledge.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the information provided in this guide.
