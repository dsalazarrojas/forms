# report_donations - Help Guide
## Purpose
The report_donations form is designed to record and track donations received by an organization. It collects detailed information about each donation, including donor details and donation specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the donor's name (First Name and Last Name)
2. Enter the donor's email address
3. Provide the donor's phone number
4. Select the department that the donation is for
5. Choose the type of equipment donated
6. Indicate the condition of the equipment donated
7. Enter the quantity of equipment donated
8. Enter the value of the donation
9. Select the method of delivery (e.g., pickup, delivery)
10. (Optional) Provide additional information about the donor (Created By, Updated At, Assigned To, etc.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter the first name of the donor.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter the last name of the donor.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the donor.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, **required**): Select the department that the donation is for.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the type of equipment donated.
* **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, **required**): Indicate the condition of the equipment donated.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, **required**): Enter the quantity of equipment donated.
* **Value** (`value`, number, **required**): Enter the value of the donation.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, **required**): Select the method of delivery (e.g., pickup, delivery).
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, **optional**): Enter the person who created the donation entry (e.g., staff member or volunteer).
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, **optional**): Enter the date the donation entry was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, **optional**): Enter the date the donation entry was last updated.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, **optional**): Enter the person or team assigned to the donation.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, **optional**): Select the status of the donation (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, select_one, **optional**): Provide any additional comments about the donation.
* **Tags** (`tags`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional tags or keywords related to the donation.
* **Custom Attributes** (`custom_attributes`, text, **optional**): Enter any custom attributes or notes about the donation.
* **Tags 2** (`tags_2`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional tags or keywords related to the donation.
* **Assigned To 2** (`assigned_to_2`, text, **optional**): Enter the person or team assigned to the donation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional notes or comments about the donation.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional notes or comments about the donation.
* **Notes 3** (`notes_3`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional notes or comments about the donation.
