# Donation Allocation Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Allocation Complaint Form is used to report issues regarding the allocation of a donation. If you are a donor and have experienced a problem with the allocation of your donation, please fill out this form to provide detailed information about the issue.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Describe your complaint
Please provide a clear and detailed description of the problem you are experiencing with the allocation of your donation. Be as specific as possible.

### Step 2: Identify yourself
 Enter the Donor ID that is associated with the donation that is at issue.

### Step 3: Specify the type of donation
Select the type of donation that was misallocated, which may be:

* Donation
* Grant
* Other

### Step 4: Provide the amount of the donation
Enter the amount of the donation that was misallocated.

### Step 5: Provide more details about the issue
Please provide a detailed description of the issue that occurred with the allocation of the donation.

### Step 6: Explain how you would like the issue resolved
Enter a detailed description of how you would like the issue resolved.

### Step 7: Enter your name
Please enter your name as it appears on your donation records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Describe your complaint**: This field is where you can provide a detailed explanation of the problem you are experiencing with the allocation of your donation. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Donor ID**: This field is used to identify you as the donor who made the donation that is at issue. Please enter the Donor ID that is associated with your donation.
* **Complaint date**: This field is where you can enter the date of the complaint.
* **Donation type**: This field is used to specify the type of donation that was misallocated. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Donation
	+ Grant
	+ Other
* **Donation amount**: This field is where you can enter the amount of the donation that was misallocated.
* **Description of issue**: This field is where you can provide a detailed description of the issue that occurred with the allocation of the donation.
* **Description of resolution**: This field is where you can describe how you would like the issue resolved. Please provide a detailed description of the solution you are seeking.
* **Complainant name**: This field is where you can enter your name as it appears on your donation records.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are using the correct Donor ID and date to report the issue.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department or team for assistance.
* This form is not meant for general feedback or inquiry about donations, so please do not submit this form if you are seeking information about a donation that was allocated correctly.
