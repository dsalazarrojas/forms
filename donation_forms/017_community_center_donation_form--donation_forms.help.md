# Community Center Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Community Center Donation Form is designed to collect donations from individuals or organizations to support various programs and initiatives of the Community Center.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields marked as **required**. These include:
	* Donor name: Enter the name of the individual or organization making the donation.
	* Donor email: Enter the email address where the donation receipt will be sent.
	* Donation amount: Enter the numeric amount of the donation in the local currency.
	* Recurrence frequency: Choose how often you would like to make this donation (One time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
	* Payment method: Choose how you would like to make the payment (Credit card, Bank transfer, Check, or Online payment portal).
2. Fill in any optional fields as needed. These include:
	* Donor name (if different from the first field)
	* Mailing address (if you would like to receive receipts at a different address)
3. Choose the donation purpose: Select from the available options to designate where your donation will go towards (General support, Youth programs, Facility improvements, Scholarships, or Other).
4. Choose whether or not to make the donation anonymous.
5. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor name**: Enter the name of the individual or organization making the donation.
* **Donor email**: Enter the email address where the donation receipt will be sent. This will be used for communication and to provide receipts.
* **Donation amount**: Enter the numeric amount of the donation in the local currency. This field is required.
* **Donation purpose**: Choose from the available options to designate where your donation will go towards. Options include:
	+ General support: Donations for general operating expenses.
	+ Youth programs: Donations for programs and activities for youth development.
	+ Facility improvements: Donations for improvements to the Community Center facilities.
	+ Scholarships: Donations for scholarships for individuals or groups.
	+ Other: Donations for other purposes not listed here.
* **Recurring donation**: Choose how often you would like to make this donation. Options include:
	+ One time: A one-time donation.
	+ Monthly: A monthly recurring donation.
	+ Quarterly: A quarterly recurring donation.
	+ Annually: An annual recurring donation.
* **Payment method**: Choose how you would like to make the payment. Options include:
	+ Credit card: A one-time payment via credit card.
	+ Bank transfer: A one-time payment via bank transfer.
	+ Check: A one-time payment via check.
	+ Online payment portal: A one-time or recurring payment through our online portal.
* **Set up recurring donation**: Choose whether or not to set up a recurring donation. If you choose to set up a recurring donation, choose the recurrence frequency from the available options.
* **Make donation anonymous**: Choose whether or not to make the donation anonymous. If you choose to make the donation anonymous, your name will not be listed publicly.
* **Mailing address**: Enter the mailing address where you would like to receive receipts. This is optional.
* **Payment method**: Choose how you would like to make the payment. Options include:
	+ Credit card: A one-time payment via credit card.
	+ Bank transfer: A one-time payment via bank transfer.
	+ Check: A one-time payment via check.
	+ Online payment portal: A one-time or recurring payment through our online portal.
