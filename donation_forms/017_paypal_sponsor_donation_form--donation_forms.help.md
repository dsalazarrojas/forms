# paypal_sponsor_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "paypal_sponsor_donation_form" is a form designed for users to make donations to a sponsor. This form collects basic information from the donator, including their name, email, donation amount, and description, to process the donation and provide a receipt for the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Donator Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Donator Email" field.
3. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field.
4. Select the type of donation from the "Donation Type" dropdown menu (Funding or Donation).
5. Optionally, provide a description of the reason for your donation in the "Description" field.
6. Add any additional notes about your donation in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donator Name** (`donator_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you want it to appear on the donation receipt.
* **Donator Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive a donation receipt and any follow-up communication.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of donation you are making, either "Funding" or "Donation".
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of why you are making this donation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.
