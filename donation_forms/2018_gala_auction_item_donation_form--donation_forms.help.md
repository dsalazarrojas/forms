# 2018 Gala Auction Item Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for donations being made to a charity or organization during the 2018 Gala Auction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* **Full name**: Enter your first and last name.
	* **Email address**: We'll use this to send you receipts and updates.
	* **Phone number**: Provide an optional contact number for us to reach you.
2. Enter your billing address:
	* **Billing address**: Enter your street, city, state, and ZIP code.
3. Describe your donation:
	* **Item description**: Provide a brief description of the item you're donating.
	* **Item condition**: Select the best description of the item's condition (e.g., New, Like new, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Payment details:
	* **Estimated value**: Enter the estimated market value of the item.
	* **Donation type**: Select the type of donation (e.g., Physical, Digital, Service).
	* **Amount**: Enter the total amount of the donation.
	* **Payment date**: Enter the date of the payment.
	* **Payment method**: Select the payment method (e.g., Credit card, Debit card, Bank transfer, PayPal, Check, Cash).
	* **Purpose of payment**: Enter the purpose of the payment (e.g., Silent Auction).
	* **Invoice or reference number**: If applicable, enter the invoice or reference number.
5. Pickup and storage details:
	* **Pickup location**: Select your preferred pickup method (e.g., In-person pickup, Shipping, Drop box).
	* **Pickup date**: Enter the date of the pickup.
	* **Pickup time**: Enter the time of the pickup (if applicable).
	* **Special storage requirements**: If necessary, provide any special storage requirements.
6. Consent and agreement:
	* **Consent to photograph**: Select whether you consent to having the item photographed.
	* **Agree to terms**: Select that you agree to the terms and conditions.

* **Purpose of payment** (`payment_purpose`, Text, **required**): Enter the purpose of the payment (e.g., Silent Auction).
