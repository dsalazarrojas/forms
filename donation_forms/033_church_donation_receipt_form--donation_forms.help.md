# Church Donation Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document donations received by the church. It ensures accurate tracking, reporting, and transparency in financial dealings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form:

1.  Fill in the **Receipt Number**, which is an internal tracking ID.
2.  Enter the **Date Processed**, when the form is completed.
3.  Enter your **Process Name**, the name of the person processing the donation.
4.  Fill in the **Donor Full Name**, which is essential for identification and communication.
5.  If applicable, enter the **Donor ID** for donor identification.
6.  Enter the **Date of Actual Contribution**, the date the contribution was made.
7.  Enter the **Total Amount Received**, which is the total amount of the contribution.
8.  Select the **Method of Contribution**, such as Cash, Check, Credit Card, etc.
9.  If a check was used, enter the **Check Number**.
10. Select the **Fund Credited**, to which the funds will be allocated.
11. Confirm that no goods or services were provided for this gift, selecting the correct option.
12. If goods or services were provided, enter the **Value of Goods/Services Provided**.
13. Sign your name as the **Signature of Authorized Officer**.
14. Select whether to send a copy of this receipt to the donor immediately.
15. If sending a copy, enter the **Donor Email for Receipt**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Receipt Number (2)**: Internal tracking ID for donations.
    \*\*Enter a unique identifier for each donation.
*   **Date Processed (3)**: Date the form is completed.
    \*\*Date when the form is filled out.
*   **Processor Name (4)**: Name of the person processing the donation.
    \*\*Name of the person filling out the form.
*   **Donor Full Name (6)**: Required for donor identification and communication.
    \*\*Complete name of the donor.
*   **Donor ID (7)**: Optional identification for donors.
    \*\*ID number if applicable.
*   **Date of Actual Contribution (8)**: Date the contribution was made.
    \*\*Date when the donation was received.
*   **Total Amount Received (9)**: Required for financial tracking and reporting.
    \*\*Enter the total amount of the donation.
*   **Method of Contribution (10)**: Required for understanding the type of contribution.
    \*\*Select from the available options: Cash, Check, Credit Card, etc.
*   **Check Number (11)**: Optional for tracking check payments.
    \*\*Enter the check number if a check was used.
*   **Fund Credited (12)**: Required for fund allocation and tracking.
    \*\*Select from the available options: General, Building, Missions, etc.
*   **Goods/Services Disclaimer (13)**: Required for tax purposes and compliance.
    \*\*Confirm if goods or services were provided.
*   **Value of Goods/Services Provided (14)**: Optional for tax purposes.
    \*\*Enter value if goods or services were provided.
*   **Signature of Authorized Officer (15)**: Required for verification and validation.
    \*\*Sign with electronic signature.
*   **Send copy to Donor (16)**: Required for donor communication and satisfaction.
    \*\*Select whether to send a copy of the receipt immediately.
*   **Donor Email for Receipt (17)**: Optional for sending receipts to donors.
    \*\*Enter the donor's email if sending a copy.

## Tips

*   Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
*   Ensure the form is completed in a timely and organized manner.
*   Follow the instructions for the **Signature of Authorized Officer**.
*   Consider adding a comment or note for any unusual or complex situations.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   Use the **Donor Email for Receipt** to send a copy of the receipt to the donor.
