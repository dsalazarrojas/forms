# Church Donation Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document donations received by the church. It ensures accurate tracking, reporting, and transparency in financial dealings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form:

1.  Fill in the **Receipt Number**, which is an internal tracking ID.
2.  Enter the **Date Processed**, when the form is completed.
3.  Enter your **Process Name**, the name of the person processing the donation.
4.  Fill in the **Donor Full Name**, which is essential for identification and communication.
5.  If applicable, enter the **Donor ID** for donor identification.
6.  Enter the **Date of Actual Contribution**, the date the contribution was made.
7.  Enter the **Total Amount Received**, which is the total amount of the contribution.
8.  Select the **Method of Contribution**, such as Cash, Check, Credit Card, etc.
9.  If a check was used, enter the **Check Number**.
10. Select the **Fund Credited**, to which the funds will be allocated.
11. Confirm that no goods or services were provided for this gift, selecting the correct option.
12. If goods or services were provided, enter the **Value of Goods/Services Provided**.
13. Sign your name as the **Signature of Authorized Officer**.
14. Select whether to send a copy of this receipt to the donor immediately.
15. If sending a copy, enter the **Donor Email for Receipt**.
