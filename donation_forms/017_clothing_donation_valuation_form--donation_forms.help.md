The purpose of this form is to collect information about clothing donations made to an organization. This form will help the donation team assess and value the items being donated, and provide the donor with a clear understanding of the total value of their donation. This form will guide you through the necessary steps and fields required to complete a thorough and accurate clothing donation valuation.

This form consists of 25 fields that collect various details about the donation, including the donor's name, donation date, item descriptions, condition assessments, and valuation method used. The form is divided into six sections, each with a specific focus:

1. Donor Information
2. Item Descriptions
3. Condition Assessment
4. Valuation Details
5. Brand Information
6. Special Items and Valuation

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

**How To Complete This Form**

1. Fill out the Donor Information section with your full name and any applicable identification number.
2. List each clothing item with its description, type, and brand, and indicate the primary category of items being donated (e.g., everyday wear, designer, vintage, etc.).
3. Assess the condition of each item, using the provided scale, and describe any notable conditions (stains, damage, repairs).
4. Choose the valuation method used (e.g., fair market value, thrift store pricing, etc.).
5. Enter the estimated retail value or fair market value of each item.
6. List any high-end or designer brands included in the donation.
7. If applicable, list any special or valuable items.
8. Indicate whether you are eligible for a tax deduction.
9. If applicable, provide additional valuation information and comments from the appraiser.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **1. Donor Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **2. Donor ID**: If you have an identification number, please enter it here (optional but recommended).
* **3. Donation Date**: Date of the donation.
* **4. Item Descriptions**: List each clothing item with its type, brand, and size.
* **5. Total Number of Items**: How many items are being donated.
* **6. Primary Category of Items**: Choose the primary category of items being donated (e.g., everyday wear, designer, etc.).
* **7. Item-by-Item Description**: List each item with its type, brand, and size.
* **8. Overall Condition Assessment**: Assess the condition of each item using the provided scale (e.g., like new, excellent, etc.).
* **9. Condition Details**: Describe any notable conditions (stains, damage, repairs) for each item.
* **10. Overall Condition of Donation**: Choose the overall condition of the entire donation using the provided scale (e.g., like new, excellent, etc.).
* **11. Valuation Method Used**: Choose the method used to determine the value of each item (e.g., fair market value, etc.).
* **12. Estimated Retail Value**: Enter the original purchase price or equivalent value of each item.
* **13. Fair Market Value**: Enter the value of each item if it were sold today.
* **14. High-End or Designer Brands**: Indicate if any high-end or designer brands are included in the donation.
* **15. List of Notable Brands**: List any premium or recognizable brands included in the donation.
* **16. Special Items**: List any special or valuable items (e.g., wedding dress, fur coat, etc.).
* **17. Estimated Value of Special Items**: Enter the value of each special item.
* **18. Total Appraised Value**: Enter the total value of the entire donation.
* **19. Appraised by**: Choose who appraised the items (e.g., donor self-appraisal, staff assessment, etc.).
* **20. Eligible for Tax Deduction**: Indicate if you are eligible for a tax deduction.
* **21. Appraiser Notes and Comments**: Provide any additional valuation information or comments from the appraiser.

**Tips**

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the donation team.
* Review and double-check your submission before finalizing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the donation team.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is not intended to be used for any other purpose.
