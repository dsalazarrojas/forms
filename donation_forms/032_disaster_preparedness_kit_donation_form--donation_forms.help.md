# disaster_preparedness_kit_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors for disaster relief efforts. It helps us to acknowledge and appreciate your contributions, ensuring that we can deliver critical aid to those affected by disasters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your donor details, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Enter the amount you'd like to donate.
3. Provide your email address so we can keep you updated on our efforts.
4. Indicate your preferred method of communication for confirmation of your donation.
5. Select any emergency supplies you'd like to contribute, such as water, food, or first aid kits.
6. If you have any other supplies to contribute, you can specify them in the text box below.
7. Confirm how you'd like to be contacted for confirmation of your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Details** (`donor_details`): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`): Enter the amount you'd like to donate. This is a mandatory field.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on our efforts.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Emergency Supplies** (`emergency_supply`): Select the emergency supplies you'd like to contribute, such as water, food, or first aid kits.
* **Other Supplies** (`other_supplies`): If you have any other supplies to contribute, specify them in this text box.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Use this text box to provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
* **Confirmation Method** (`confirm_email`): Select how you'd like to be contacted for confirmation of your donation, such as email or text message.
* **Confirmation Method (if not listed)** (`other_confirmation_method`): If the above options don't apply, specify your preferred method of contact.
* **Donor Acknowledgement** (`donor_acknowledges_donation`): Confirm whether you acknowledge your donation, with options to select whether you're certain, unsure, or not sure.

# Tips

* Please ensure your contact information is accurate so we can reach out to you effectively.
* If you select "other" for emergency supplies, please specify what it is.
* If you select "other" for confirmation method, please specify what it is.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
