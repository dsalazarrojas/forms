# corporate_matching_gift_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate matching gift consent form is used to determine whether or not to provide matching gift benefits to employees. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected to facilitate accurate processing and eligibility determination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all fields are completed accurately and that it is not a duplicate of a previously completed form.
2. Select "Employer Information" to choose the company that will be providing the matching gift.
3. Choose the matching level for the gift.
4. Fill out "Gift Details" with any additional information about the gift.
5. Select the gift frequency (how often the gift will be given) and the gift value.
6. Enter the donor's information (name, email, phone number, and address).
7. If the gift frequency is "Other," provide a description of the frequency.
8. Confirm the corporate match amount and percentage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Information** (`company`, `type: select_multiple`): Choose the company that will be providing the matching gift.
* **Matching Level** (`match_level`, `type: select_one`): Choose the matching level for the gift (True or False).
* **Gift Details** (`gift_details`, `type: note`): Add any additional information about the gift.
* **Gift Value** (`gift_value`, `type: number`): Enter the value of the gift.
* **Gift Frequency** (`gift_frequency`, `type: select_one`): Choose how often the gift will be given (once, ongoing, or other).
* **Gift Date** (`gift_date`, `type: date`): Enter the date of the gift (not required).
* **Gift Value Frequency** (`gift_value_frequency`, `type: number`): Enter how often the gift value will change.
* **Matching Gift Employer** (`matching_gift_employer`, `type: text`): Enter the name of the company providing the matching gift (if not the same as the employer's name).
* **Gift Annually** (`gift_annually`, `type: select_one`): Choose if the gift will be given annually.
* **Gift Quaterly** (`gift_quarterly`, `type: select_one`): Choose if the gift will be given quarterly.
* **Gift Monthly** (`gift_monthly`, `type: select_one`): Choose if the gift will be given monthly.
* **Gift Weekly** (`gift_weekly`, `type: select_one`): Choose if the gift will be given weekly.
* **Gift Daily** (`gift_daily`, `type: select_one`): Choose if the gift will be given daily.
* **Gift Frequency Other** (`gift_frequency_other`, `type: text`): Enter an alternative frequency for the gift.
* **Gift Frequency Other Description** (`gift_frequency_other_description`, `type: text`): Add a description of the other frequency.
* **Matching Gift Employer Other** (`matching_gift_employer_other`, `type: text`): Enter an alternative name for the company providing the matching gift.
* **Matching Gift Employer Other Description** (`matching_gift_employer_other_description`, `type: text`): Add a description of the other matching gift employer.
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, `type: text`): Enter information about the donor.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, `type: email`): Enter the donor's email address.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, `type: text`): Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, `type: text`): Enter the donor's address.
* **Gift Frequency Other Frequency** (`gift_frequency_other_frequency`, `type: number`): Enter how often the gift frequency other will be given.
* **Gift Value Other** (`gift_value_other`, `type: number`): Enter the value of the gift other.
* **Corporate Match Amount** (`corporate_match_amount`, `type: number`): Enter the amount of the corporate match.
* **Corporate Match Percentage** (`corporate_match_percent`, `type: number`): Enter the corporate match percentage.
