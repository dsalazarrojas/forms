# pta_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pta_donation_form is a tool used to collect information from donors, allowing us to track and record donations made to our school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Donater Name** field with the donor's name.
2. Enter the **Email** of the donor in the designated field.
3. (Optional) If you have a physical address for the donor, enter it in the **Address** field.
4. Select the **Amount Donated** field and fill in the amount of the donation.
5. Choose the **Purpose of Donation** from the provided options (School Programs, School Events, or Other).
6. Enter the **Date of Donation** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Select all applicable methods of donation from the **Method of Donation** field (Cash, Check, Online Payment).
8. Sign the **Signature** field with your signature, if applicable.
9. (Optional) You can add any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field.
10. (Optional) If you want to add any additional details about the donor, enter it in the **Other Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donater Name** (`Donater Name`, text, required): Enter the donor's name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the donor's email address.
* **Address** (`Address`, note, optional): Enter any physical address for the donor (if applicable).
* **Amount Donated** (`Amount Donated`, number, required): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Purpose of Donation** (`Purpose of Donation`, select one, required): Choose the reason for the donation.
* **Date of Donation** (`Date of Donation`, date, required): Enter the date of the donation.
* **Method of Donation** (`Method of Donation`, select multiple, required): Select all applicable methods of donation.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, note, required): Sign the form with your signature (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the donation.
* **Other Details** (`Other Details`, text, optional): Enter any other details about the donor.
* **Amount Donated (USD)** (`Amount Donated (USD)`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the donation in USD (if applicable).
* **Amount Donated (CAN)** (`Amount Donated (CAN)`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the donation in CAD (if applicable).
