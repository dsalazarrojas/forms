# Donor Story Submission Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Story Submission Request Form is a platform for donors to share their stories, experiences, and motivations behind their charitable donations. This form allows donors to express their gratitude, showcase their impact, and inspire others through their personal narratives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Submit Your Story**: Fill in the "Share Your Story" field with a detailed account of your experience, including your reasons for donating and the impact of your donation.
2. **Provide Contact Information**: Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the respective fields to ensure we can respond to your submission.
3. **Add Organization Context (Optional)**: If you're affiliated with a specific organization, you can mention it in the "Organization" field.
4. **Specify Your Category (Optional)**: Choose a category that best fits your story from the options provided in the "Category" field.
5. **Sub-Category (Optional)**: Select a sub-category related to your story from the options provided in the "Sub-Category" field.
6. **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name so we can address you in our communication.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for the same reason as above.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so we can respond to you and keep you updated on our work.
* **Organization**: If you're affiliated with an organization, mention it here to provide context to your story.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication and follow-up questions.
* **Share Your Story**: This is the main field where you'll describe your experience, reasons for donating, and the impact of your donation.
* **Category**: Select a category that best fits your story from the options provided:
	+ **Donations**: General stories about donating to a cause or organization.
	+ **Volunteering**: Stories about volunteering your time or skills to a cause or organization.
	+ **Fundraising**: Stories about raising funds for a specific cause or organization.
	+ **Other**: Any other category not listed above.
* **Sub-Category**: Select a sub-category that further describes your story from the options provided:
	+ **General**: General stories that don't fit into the above categories.
	+ **Health**: Stories related to health, wellness, or medical research.
	+ **Education**: Stories related to educational institutions or programs.
	+ **Environment**: Stories related to environmental conservation or sustainability.
	+ **Animal Welfare**: Stories related to animal rights or welfare.
	+ **Other**: Any other sub-category not listed above.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your story to help our team understand its context.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your story for review and publication.
