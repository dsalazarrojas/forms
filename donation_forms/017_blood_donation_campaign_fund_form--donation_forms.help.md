# Blood Donation Campaign Fund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blood Donation Campaign Fund Form is designed to collect information from individuals and organizations interested in supporting a blood donation campaign. This form will guide you through the process of making a donation, allowing you to choose the donation method, amount, and frequency that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter your full name and email address in the required fields.
3. Choose your preferred donation method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check).
4. Select the one-time, monthly, or annual donation option that best fits your schedule.
5. Enter your donation amount from the provided options or select "Other" and enter a custom amount.
6. If making a corporate donation, please enter your company's name.
7. Indicate how you learned about the campaign and what aspect of the campaign motivated your donation.
8. Choose whether you want public recognition for your donation.
9. Select your preferred donation frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
10. If you require a tax receipt, select "Yes" or "No."
11. Choose a date for your donation.
12. Finally, review all the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted regarding your donation.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, `text`, optional): If you provide a phone number, we can contact you with further information about your donation.
* **Mailing Address** (`donor_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your mailing address if you would like to receive correspondence from us.
* **Contribution Type** (`contribution_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like to make your contribution: one-time, monthly, or annual.
* **Donation Amount** (`contribution_amount`, `select_one`, required): Select the amount of your donation from the provided options or enter a custom amount if you select "Other."
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like to make your payment: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check.
* **Corporate Donation?** (`is_corporate_donation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if this is a corporate donation.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name if making a corporate donation.
* **How did you learn about this campaign?** (`campaign_awareness`, `select_one`, optional): Share how you discovered the campaign: Social Media, Email, Website, Word of Mouth, or Advertisement.
* **What motivated you to donate?** (`campaign_goal`, `text`, optional): Share what aspect of the campaign inspired your decision to donate.
* **Public Recognition** (`public_recognition`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you'd like to be recognized for your donation: Yes, list my name, Yes, but anonymous, or No public recognition.
* **Recurring Donation Preferences** (`recurring_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select how frequently you'd like to make your donations: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Tax Receipt Needed?** (`tax_receipt_needed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you require a tax receipt.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for your donation.
* **Other Options**

Please note that some fields have optional or conditional fields that require additional information only when specific conditions are met. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your donation processing.
