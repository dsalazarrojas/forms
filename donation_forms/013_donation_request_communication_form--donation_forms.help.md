# donation_request_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The donation_request_communication_form is used for users to submit their requests for donations to the organization. This form collects basic contact information and specific details about the donation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Write a message in the "message" field, describing your donation request.
4. You may enter your name again if desired.
5. If you have a phone number, you may enter it in the "phone" field.
6. Indicate the amount of the donation you are requesting.
7. Select the frequency of the donation you would like to make from the "donation_frequency" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (text, required): Your name.
* **email** (email, required): Your email address.
* **message** (text, required): A message describing your donation request.
* **name** (text, required): Your name (again, for confirmation).
* **phone** (text, optional): Your phone number (if you have one).
* **donation_amount** (number, optional): The amount of the donation you are requesting.
* **donation_frequency** (select_one, optional): The frequency of the donation you would like to make. Options:
  * Option 1
  * Option 2
  * Option 3

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any additional information or clarification regarding your donation request, include it in the "message" field.
* Once you have submitted your form, you will receive communication from the organization regarding your donation request.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the organization directly.
