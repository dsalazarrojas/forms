# Donate For Education Using E.Check.Net - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable users to make donations to a cause or organization using E.Check.Net.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the `Donor Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Donor Email` field.
3. Enter the amount of your donation in the `Donation Amount` field.
4. Select the option for transferring funds through E.Check.Net using the `E.Check.Net` dropdown.
5. If desired, add any additional comments or notes in the `Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name (`donor_name`)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Donor Email (`donor_email`)**: Enter your email address to receive confirmation of your donation.
* **Donation Amount (`donation_amount`)**: Enter the amount of your donation.
* **E.Check.Net (`e_check`)**: Select the option for transferring funds through E.Check.Net.
* **Notes (`notes`)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your donation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in the required fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the E.Check.Net support team.
* Review your donation details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
