# eco_tourism_training_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for the eco-tourism training fund. Donors can input their name, email, phone number, and address, as well as the amount and method of their donation, and whether or not their organization is a donor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" option below the form to send in your application. This option will be visible after filling out the necessary fields.
2. Choose the category of your donation and provide a brief description of your donation.
3. Enter your donor name, email address, and phone number.
4. Enter your donation amount and select the payment method and any other relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Eco Tourism Training Fund Donation Form** (text): This field is for entering a title for your donation.
* **Category** (select_one): Choose the category of your donation from the options provided.
* **Description** (text): Provide a brief description of your donation.
* **Donor Name** (text): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Donation Amount** (number): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple): Select the method of payment for your donation from the options provided.
* **Submit** (select_one): Choose this option to submit your application.
* **Donor Organization** (select_multiple): Check if your organization is a donor.
* **Donor Designation** (text): Enter any specific details about your donation.
* **Attachment** (text): If applicable, attach any supporting documents or files.
* **Donor Organization** (select_multiple): Check if your organization is a donor. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the necessary fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any supporting documents or files, attach them to the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our team for assistance.
