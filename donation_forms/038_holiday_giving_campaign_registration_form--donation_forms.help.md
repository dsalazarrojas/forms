# Holiday Giving Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Giving Campaign Registration Form is used to collect information from donors about their charitable contributions during the holiday season. This form helps internal staff review and process donation data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Registration Form Data fields with your name and contact information.
2. Provide details about the donor and the gift they are offering.
3. Enter information about the gift, including its value and any additional details.
4. Provide your payment information, if applicable.
5. Review your entry to ensure it's accurate and complete.
6. Click the Submit button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form Data (Page 1)
* **Registration Form Data** (`<title>`, `text`, optional): This is the title of your registration form. Please enter a brief description of the purpose of your donation.
* **Donor Information** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Enter information about the donor, including their name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Donation Details** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Describe the gift you are offering, including its value and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Gift Details** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about the gift, such as its condition or any other relevant information.

### Donor Information (Page 2)
* **Donor Information** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Repeat the process of filling out Donor Information on a new page.

### Donation Details (Page 3)
* **Gift Value** (`<type>`, `number`, optional): If you are offering a monetary gift, enter the gift value. If you are offering an in-kind gift, enter the fair market value of the item.
* **Tax Receipt** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the tax receipt, if applicable.
* **Payment Information** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Enter your payment method and any additional payment details.
* **Review** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Review your entry to ensure it's accurate and complete.

### Review and Confirmation (Page 4)
* **Confirmation** (`<type>`, `text`, optional): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your entry accurately.
