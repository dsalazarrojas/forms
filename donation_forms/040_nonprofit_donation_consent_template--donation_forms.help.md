# NonProfit Donation Consent Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for non-profit organizations to collect consents from donors for donations. It helps non-profit organizations gather information about the donor's name, their non-profit organization, the cause they're supporting, and their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your non-profit organization in the **Non Profit Organization** field.
2. Describe the cause you're raising funds for in the **cause_to_support** field.
3. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Non Profit Organization**: Enter the name of your non-profit organization, e.g., "Red Cross" or "Save the Children".
* **cause_to_support**: Describe the specific cause you're raising funds for, e.g., "Hunger Relief" or "Disaster Response".
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, email address, phone number, or any other relevant contact information so we can reach out to you.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* This form is for non-profit organizations only, so please only use it for your legitimate purposes.
