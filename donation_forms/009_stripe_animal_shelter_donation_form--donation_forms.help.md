# stripe_animal_shelter_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect donations for an animal shelter. If you're interested in supporting the shelter, this form is the correct place to start.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you understand what information is required.
2. Select the correct options for each field, paying attention to required fields.
3. Enter your details accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether or not you are a user who is interested in donating to the animal shelter. This field is not required, but selecting "Yes" is the correct choice if you're filling out this form to make a donation.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it should be displayed in the donation records. This field is not required, but providing your name helps the shelter acknowledge your contribution.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address if you'd like to receive updates or information from the animal shelter. This field is not required, but can be useful for communication purposes.
* **Donate Amount** (`donate_amount`, number, required: false): Specify the amount you would like to donate to the animal shelter. You can enter any number, but it's recommended to select an amount that feels meaningful to you.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required: false): This field is currently not used in the explanation, so it can be left blank. However, if you know the payment method, you can enter it here.
* **Stripe Token** (`stripe_token`, text, required: false): This field is likely a placeholder for a Stripe payment token. It's not currently required or explained in the form context, so it can be left blank.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any issues or questions, reach out to the animal shelter directly.
* The shelter will use the information provided to process your donation and contact you if necessary.
