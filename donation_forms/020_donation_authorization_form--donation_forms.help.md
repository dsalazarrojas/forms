# Donation Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donation Authorization Form is used to collect and authorize donations for a specific purpose or project. It gathers information about the donor, the donation amount, and the payment method. The form is designed to ensure that donations are properly authorized and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the Donation Information page with the required details.
3. Proceed to the Payment Information page and provide the necessary payment method.
4. Enter a valid email address in the Donor Email field.
5. Enter a valid contribution amount in the Contribution Amount field.
6. Select the payment method from the Payment Method field.
7. Click the Submit button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Information**: Enter the details about the donation, but this field is not required.
* **Payment Information**: Enter the payment method for the donation.
* **Donor Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with the donation.
* **Contribution Amount**: Enter a valid contribution amount for the donation.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for the donation, which is a required field.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form and complete the donation process.
