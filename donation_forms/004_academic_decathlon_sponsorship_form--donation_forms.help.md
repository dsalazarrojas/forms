# Academic Decathlon Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Decathlon Sponsorship Form is used to provide sponsorship support to our initiative. This form helps us understand your sponsorship needs and preferences, ensuring efficient processing and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you provide the necessary information for the sponsorship.
2.  Select the preferred sponsorship level from the drop-down menu.
3.  Choose your preferred payment method and enter any additional details if required.
4.  If you have any additional notes or instructions, type them in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sponsor name**: *Required* - Enter the name of the individual or organization providing the sponsorship.
*   **Contact person**: *Required* - Provide the primary contact person's name for sponsorship correspondence.
*   **Contact email**: *Required* - Enter the email address for invoice and communication purposes.
*   **Sponsorship level**: *Required* - Choose the level of sponsorship you are providing (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, or Custom).
*   **Sponsorship amount**: *Required* - Enter the pledged amount in the local currency.
*   **Recognition preference**: - Choose the method of recognition for your sponsorship (Program materials, Website, Event signage, Social media, or No public recognition).
*   **Preferred payment method**: *Required* - Select the preferred payment method (Bank transfer, Check, Credit card, or Invoice).
*   **Billing address**: - Enter the address for invoicing purposes (if required).
*   **Additional notes**: - Provide any special requests or terms in the "Additional notes" field.

## Tips
- If you need to make any changes to your sponsorship details, please contact us and we will assist you.
- Please ensure that the sponsorship amount and level are clearly stated and accurate to avoid misunderstandings.
- We will follow up with you for any additional information required for the sponsorship process.
