# Community Outreach Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Outreach Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors for the purpose of processing their donations to the community outreach program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Donor full name" field. This is a required field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Donor email address" field. This will be used for donation receipts and confirmation.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Donor phone number" field. This is optional.
4. Enter the donation amount in the "Donation amount" field. Please use the currency of your local country.
5. If you wish to make a recurring donation, select the corresponding option in the "Donation frequency" field.
6. If you'd like to specify a particular project or program for your donation, enter it in the "Donation designation or program" field. This is optional.
7. Provide your mailing address in the "Mailing address" field. This is optional.
8. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment method" field. This is a required field.
9. If you'd like to receive newsletters and impact reports, select "True" in the "Opt in for acknowledgement and updates" field.
10. In the "Additional notes or instructions" field, enter any special instructions related to your donation, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor full name (Donor full name)** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Donor email address (Donor email address)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address that we will use for donation receipts and confirmation.
* **Donor phone number (Donor phone number)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for urgent contact, if available.
* **Donation amount (Donation amount)** (`number`, required): Enter the donation amount in your local currency.
* **Donation frequency (Donation frequency)** (`select_one`, required): Choose one time, monthly, quarterly, or annually for your donation.
* **Donation designation or program (Donation designation or program)** (`text`, optional): Specify the project or program for this gift or leave blank for the general fund.
* **Mailing address (Mailing address)** (`text`, optional): Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Payment method (Payment method)** (`select_one`, required): Select credit card, bank transfer, check, or online payment.
* **Opt in for acknowledgement and updates (Opt in for acknowledgement and updates)** (`select_one`, optional): Select "True" to receive newsletters and impact reports.
* **Additional notes or instructions (Additional notes or instructions)** (`text`, optional): Enter any special instructions related to your donation, if needed.
