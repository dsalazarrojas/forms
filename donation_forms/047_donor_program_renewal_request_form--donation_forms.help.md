# Donor Program Renewal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help donors renew their program memberships. It is intended for donors who wish to continue participating in a program and need to update their information or make changes to their membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are an authorized user and that your program membership is eligible for renewal.
2. Choose your donor program from the dropdown menu.
3. Select the frequency at which you would like to receive updates from the program (Quarterly, Bi-annually, or Annually).
4. Specify your preferred renewal date.
5. Enter your signature to confirm your request.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor First Name** (`donor_program_renewal_request_form_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Donor Last Name** (`donor_program_renewal_request_form_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_program_renewal_request_form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address associated with your donor program membership.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_program_renewal_request_form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number associated with your donor program membership.
* **Donor Program** (`donor_program_renewal_request_form_program`, select_one, required/optional): Select the donor program for which you wish to renew your membership.
* **Renewal Frequency** (`donor_program_renewal_request_form_renewal_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose how often you would like to receive updates from the program (Quarterly, Bi-annually, or Annually).
* **Renewal Date** (`donor_program_renewal_request_form_renewal_date`, date, required/optional): Specify the desired date for the renewal of your program membership.
* **Signature** (`donor_program_renewal_request_form_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature to confirm your request.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the donor program administrator for clarification.
* Please note that the form will be reviewed and processed by the donor program team after submission.
