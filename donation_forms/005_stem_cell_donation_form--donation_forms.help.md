# Stem Cell Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal information from individuals interested in donating stem cells for various medical applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field and its purpose.
2. Fill in your information accurately and completely, especially if a field is marked as "required."
3. Double-check your input for errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the introduction to the form, providing context and background information.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Address** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter your current address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **City** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **Zip** (`8`, `number`, optional): Enter your zip code for contact and follow-up purposes.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the responsible authorities for clarification.
* Double-check your input for errors before submitting the form.
