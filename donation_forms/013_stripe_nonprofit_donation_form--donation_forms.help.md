# Stripe Nonprofit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form enables users to donate to your nonprofit organization using Stripe as the payment processor. It supports one-time and recurring donations, as well as provides an option for gift receipts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your information (first name, last name, and email).
2. Choose your donation type (one-time or recurring).
3. Select the frequency of your donation (daily, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
4. Optionally, enter any additional information, such as a campaign or message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for donation updates and receipts.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number (not required for donation processing).
* **Donation Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Frequency** (Select One, Optional): Choose between one-time, recurring, stripe, or other options.
* **Frequency Frequency** (Select One, Required): Select the frequency of your donation (daily, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **Campaign** (Text, Optional): Enter a campaign or project name.
* **Stripe Account ID** (Text, Optional): Enter your Stripe account ID.
* **Stripe Plan ID** (Number, Optional): Enter your Stripe plan ID.
* **Stripe Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the Stripe amount.
* **Stripe Currency** (Select One, Optional): Choose between various currencies (USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, GBP, AUD, INR).
* **Stripe Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your Stripe email address.
* **Stripe Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your Stripe phone number.
* **Stripe Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your Stripe address.
* **Stripe Zip** (Text, Optional): Enter your Stripe zip code.
* **Gift Receipt** (Select One, Optional): Choose whether to receive a gift receipt.
* **Gift Receipt Frequency** (Select Multiple, Required): Select how frequently you want to receive gift receipts (monthly, quarterly, annually).
* **Gift Receipt Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your gift receipt name.
* **Gift Receipt Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your gift receipt email address.
* **Gift Receipt Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your gift receipt phone number.
* **Gift Receipt Note** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional information for your gift receipt.

## Tips
* Always review your information before submitting the form.
* For recurring donations, select the frequency and frequency frequency according to your intentions.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact your organization's support team.
