# Food Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from companies requesting food donations for their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Company Requesting Food" field and enter the name of your company.
2. Select the type of food you are requesting (Fresh Produce, Non-Perishable Food, or Both).
3. Enter the quantity of food you are requesting.
4. Enter your name and title as the authorized representative of your company.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further details.
6. Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further details.
7. If you know the date you would like to receive the food, please enter it in the "Request Date" field. If not, leave it blank.
8. Enter the address where you would like to pick up the food.
9. Click on the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Requesting Food**: Enter the name of your company that is requesting food donations.
* **Type of Food**: Select the type of food you are requesting (Fresh Produce, Non-Perishable Food, or Both).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units (e.g., pounds, kilograms, etc.) of food you are requesting.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the authorized representative of your company.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your company's email address so we can contact you for further details.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your company's phone number so we can contact you for further details.
* **Request Date**: If you know the date you would like to receive the food, enter it here. If not, leave it blank.
* **Drop off location**: Enter the address where you would like to pick up the food. 

Note: The "Request Date" field is optional, if not provided, it will be set to the current date. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any further questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Our internal review process will ensure that your request is handled efficiently and effectively.
