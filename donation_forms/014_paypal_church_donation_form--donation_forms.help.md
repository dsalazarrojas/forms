# paypal_church_donation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PayPal Church Donation Form is designed to collect user information and donation data for a church or religious institution. This form is intended for individuals who wish to support the church's activities and mission through donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **User Information**: Provide your name and any other relevant details. 
2. **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate. You can enter a numerical value. 
3. **Donation Frequency**: Select how often you would like to donate (e.g., one-time, monthly, or weekly). 
4. **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, or other). 
5. **Notes (Optional)**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them in the space provided. 
6. **Email**: Enter your email address (if you have one).
7. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for your name and any other relevant details.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like to donate (e.g., one-time, monthly, or weekly).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, or other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form. If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
