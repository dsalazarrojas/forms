# donation_authenticity_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The donation authenticity verification form is a crucial tool for ensuring the legitimacy of all donated funds. This form helps to verify the identity of the donor, the amount and method of the donation, and other relevant details. It is essential to complete this form accurately to prevent and detect potential issues, such as duplicate or fake donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the donor's name in the "Donor Name" field.
2. Enter the donor's email address in the "Donor Email" field.
3. Describe the donor's documentation in the "Donor Documentation" field.
4. Select the date of the donation in the "Donation Date" field.
5. Enter the amount of the donation in the "Donation Amount" field.
6. Select the method of the donation (e.g., Card, Check, ACH) in the "Donor Method" field.
7. Enter any additional notes about the donation in the "Donor Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization making the donation.
* **Donor Email**: Enter the email address associated with the donor's account or contact information.
* **Donor Documentation**: Provide any relevant documentation or information about the donor, such as a copy of the check or receipt.
* **Donation Date**: Select the date the donation was made.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the exact amount of the donation.
* **Donor Method**: Select the method of the donation (e.g., Card, Check, ACH).
* **Donor Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the donation.
