# Stock Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Donation Form is used by donors to report stock donations to a charitable organization. This form gathers information about the stock being donated, including its details, value, and the donor's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the stock donation details, including the stock symbol, name, class, type, and market value.
2. Provide the date you bought the stock, date you sold it, and date you donated it.
3. Enter the number of shares being donated.
4. Fill in your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stock Donation Form**: This field is the title of the form and is not to be filled in.
* **Donor Details**: This field is where you provide your name and contact information.
* **Stock Details**: This field gathers information about the stock being donated, including the stock symbol, name, class, type, and market value.
	+ **Stock Symbol**: Enter the stock symbol of the stock being donated.
	+ **Stock Name**: Enter the name of the stock being donated.
	+ **Stock Class**: Enter the class of the stock being donated (e.g., common or preferred).
	+ **Stock Type**: Enter the type of stock being donated (e.g., stock, bond, etc.).
	+ **Market Value (US)**: Enter the market value of the stock being donated in US dollars.
* **Charitable Organization**: This field is where you provide the name and address of the charitable organization receiving the donation.
* **Charitable Organization Information**: This field gathers more information about the charitable organization, including its address, city, state, postal code, and phone number.
	+ **Address**: Enter the address of the charitable organization.
	+ **City**: Enter the city of the charitable organization.
	+ **State**: Enter the state of the charitable organization.
	+ **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the charitable organization.
	+ **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the charitable organization.

Please note that the fields marked "required" are essential for the accurate and correct processing of the information provided.
