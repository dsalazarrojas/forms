# hearing_impaired_program_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from users for the Hearing Impaired Program Fund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Form** (`donation_form`, text, not required):
This is the form itself, where you can enter your donation information. Simply type your message or details about your donation.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required):
Please enter your name so we can acknowledge your donation and contact you, if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation, if necessary.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required):
Provide your phone number so we can reach you, if required.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, not required):
Specify the amount you would like to donate. Please note that you can enter a whole number value.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required):
Select one of the following payment methods:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, not required):
Enter any additional information about your payment, such as the payment date or any specific details about your transaction.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for considering making a donation to the Hearing Impaired Program Fund!
