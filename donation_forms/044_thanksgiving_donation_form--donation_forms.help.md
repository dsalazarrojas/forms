# thanksgiving_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the processing of a donation, specifically for the Thanksgiving season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your input in the **User Input** field. This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information you'd like to be remembered for the donation.
2. Enter the **Donation Amount** in the field below. This is the amount you are willing to donate for the Thanksgiving season.
3. Provide your **Email** to receive updates and confirmation of your donation.
4. Enter your **Phone** number so we can follow up with you.
5. In the **Description** field, provide any additional details about the donation, such as why you are donating or a special message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are willing to donate.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email to receive updates and confirmation of your donation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the donation.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately to process the donation correctly.
* If you have any issues while filling out the form, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
