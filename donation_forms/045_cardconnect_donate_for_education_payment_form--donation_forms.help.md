# Cardconnect Donate For Education Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardconnect Donate For Education Payment Form is a tool for individuals to make donations to support education causes. This form helps donors provide their contact information, specify their donation details, and choose their preferences for communication and recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the donor information section with your contact details.
2. Select the education cause you want to support from the options provided.
3. Specify the donation amount and frequency.
4. Choose your communication preferences.
5. Review and confirm your donation details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your active email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
- **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your active phone number where you can be reached for updates and information.
- **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the organization.
- **Street address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where you can be reached at.
- **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you can be reached at.
- **State/Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where you can be reached at.
- **Zip/Postal code** (`zip_postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter the zip or postal code of your location.
- **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter the country where you can be reached at.
- **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
- **Donation frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you want to make this donation.
  - **One-time Donation**: Make a one-time donation.
  - **Monthly Recurring**: Make a recurring monthly donation.
  - **Quarterly**: Make a quarterly donation.
  - **Annual**: Make an annual donation.
  - **Other**: Specify other frequencies in the special message box.
- **Which education cause would you like to support?** (`donation_focus`, `select_one`, required): Select the education cause that you want to support.
  - **Primary Education**: Support primary education causes.
  - **Secondary Education**: Support secondary education causes.
  - **Higher Education**: Support higher education causes.
  - **Vocational Training**: Support vocational training causes.
  - **Special Education**: Support special education causes.
  - **Rural Education**: Support rural education causes.
  - **All Programs**: Support all programs.
  - **Other**: Specify other causes in the special message box.
- **Target region for impact** (`target_region`, `select_one`, optional): Choose where you want to see the impact of your donation.
  - **Local**: Impact will be seen locally.
  - **National**: Impact will be seen nationally.
  - **International**: Impact will be seen internationally.
  - **Africa**: Impact will be seen in Africa.
  - **Asia**: Impact will be seen in Asia.
  - **South America**: Impact will be seen in South America.
  - **Middle East**: Impact will be seen in the Middle East.
  - **No Preference**: No preference specified.
- **Donor preferences** (`donor_preferences`, `note`, optional): If you have any other preferences or notes, enter them here.
- **Make this an anonymous donation?** (`anonymous_donation`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you want your donation to be anonymous.
  - **True**: Make this donation anonymous.
  - **False**: Do not make this donation anonymous.
- **Recognition preference** (`recognition_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you want to be recognized for your donation.
  - **Public Recognition**: Receive public recognition for your donation.
  - **Private Recognition**: Receive private recognition for your donation.
  - **No Recognition Needed**: No recognition is needed.
- **I want to receive impact updates and reports?** (`impact_updates`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you want to receive updates and reports about the impact of your donation.
  - **True**: Receive impact updates and reports.
  - **False**: Do not receive impact updates and reports.
- **Preferred communication method** (`communication_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you want to receive communication from us.
  - **Email**: Prefer to receive updates and information via email.
  - **Phone**: Prefer to receive updates and information via phone.
  - **Mail**: Prefer to receive updates and information via mail.
  - **Text Message**: Prefer to receive updates and information via text message.
  - **No Preference**: No preference specified.
- **Tax receipt requested?** (`tax_deductible`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you need a tax receipt for your donation.
  - **True**: Request a tax receipt.
  - **False**: Do not request a tax receipt.
- **Will your employer match this donation?** (`employer_match`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if your employer matches donations.
  - **True**: Employer will match this donation.
  - **False**: Employer will not match this donation.
  - **Not Sure**: Not sure about employer matching.
- **Special message or dedication** (`donation_message`, `text`, optional): If you want to add a special message or dedication, enter it here.
- **Any additional information or questions?** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information or questions, enter them here.
