# youth_sports_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "youth_sports_fund_donation_form" is a form intended for collecting donation information from individuals or organizations to support local youth sports programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and details ready before starting the form.
2. Fill out each field as requested, providing accurate and relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required): This field is for providing your name and contact information for us to communicate with you about your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required): Indicate the amount you wish to donate to support the youth sports programs.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you'd like to make regular donations, select from the available frequencies (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually) to set up a recurring payment schedule.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you wish to make your payment (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, optional): If you have any specific requests or comments for your donation, use this field for notes.
* **Youth Sports Programs** (`youth_sports_programs`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the youth sports programs you wish to support (Baseball, Basketball, Football, Soccer).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, use this field to provide them.

## Tips
- Ensure the information and donation amount provided are accurate and align with your goals and policies.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- If you experience any issues or have questions, contact us for support.
