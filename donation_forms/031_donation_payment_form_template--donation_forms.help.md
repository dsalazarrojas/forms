# Donation Payment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donation payment information from donors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting a category (Donation Forms or Fundraiser Forms) from the dropdown list.
2. Enter your donation amount in the Donation Amount field.
3. Choose your preferred donation frequency (One-time or Recurring) in the Donation Frequency field.
4. Fill in your name and email address in the Donor Name and Donor Email fields, respectively.
5. Provide your phone number in the Donor Phone field.
6. Add any additional notes about your donation in the Donor Note field.
7. Choose your preferred donation method (Online, Mail, or In-person) in the Donation Method field.
8. If you have any specific payment terms, specify them in the Payment Terms field.
9. Provide the expiration date of your donation in the Expiration Date field.
10. Indicate your donor status (New Donor, Returning Donor, or Donor Not Found) in the Donation Status field.
11. Fill in your address in the Donor Address field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Forms/Category** (Select One): Choose the type of donation form you are filling out.
* **Donation Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Donation Frequency** (Select One): Choose if this is a one-time or recurring donation.
* **Donor Name** (Text): Fill in your name as the donor.
* **Donor Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Donor Phone** (Text): Provide your phone number.
* **Donor Note** (Note): Add any additional notes about your donation.
* **Donation Method** (Select One): Choose how you want to make your donation (Online, Mail, or In-person).
* **Payment Terms** (Time): Specify any payment terms or conditions.
* **Expiration Date** (Date): Enter the expiration date of your donation.
* **Donation Status** (Select One): Indicate your donor status (New Donor, Returning Donor, or Donor Not Found).
* **Donor Address** (Text): Fill in your address as the donor.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your donation is processed correctly.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the fundraising team for assistance.
