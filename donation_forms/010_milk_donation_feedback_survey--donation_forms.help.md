# Milk Donation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from milk donors, which will help us identify areas of improvement in our milk donation system and processes. We want to hear about your experiences, so we can make the process better for you and other donors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. 
2. Select only the options that apply to your experiences. 
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please use the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name**: Your name as a milk donor.
* **Prophets**: If you were able to get milk from a prophet, please answer 'Yes'. If you were not, please answer 'No'. 
	+ This field is optional.
* **Rating**: How would you rate your experience with our milk donation system? 
	+ Select all that apply if you had multiple experiences.
* **Feedback**: Is there anything you'd like to add or comment about your experience with our milk donation system? 
	+ Please be as specific as possible.
* **Contact**: If you'd like to be contacted to discuss your feedback, please provide your email address.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Date**: The date of your last milk collection or interaction with our system.
* **Time**: The time of your last milk collection or interaction with our system.
* **Frequency**: If you would like to donate milk frequently, please answer 'Yes'. If not, answer 'No'.
* **How Often**: How often would you like to donate milk? 
	+ Select all that apply if you have multiple preferences.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use this field to let us know. 

Note: Please fill out as much or as little as you'd like, and we'll take it all into account when improving our milk donation system.
