# Game Server Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from users for a specific game server. To complete the form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter your email**: Please type in your email address so that we can contact you regarding your donation.
	* Email (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address you want to use for this donation.
2. **Stream ID**: Enter the ID of your stream to associate with your donation.
	* Stream ID (`stream_id`, `number`, required): Enter the ID of your stream, this will help us identify the stream you are donating for.
3. **Your name**: Provide your name so we know who is making the donation.
	* Your name (`player_name`, `text`, required): Type in your name.
4. **Amount**: Specify the amount of money you would like to donate.
	* Amount (`preferred_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
5. **Type of donation**: Select the type of donation you are making.
	* Type of donation (`type_of_donation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the options to specify the type of donation you are making.
6. **Additional information**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional context about your donation.
	* Game Server Donation Form (`form_8`, `note`, required): You can enter any additional information about your donation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email correctly to ensure we can contact you regarding your donation.
* Enter the correct stream ID to ensure your donation is associated with the right stream.
* Be as specific as possible when entering your amount and type of donation.
