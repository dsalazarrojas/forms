# Crowdfunding Reward Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crowdfunding Reward Selection form is used to collect detailed information from backers who have pledged to support a crowdfunding campaign. This form will ask for specific details about the backer's reward, shipping information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the relevant fields as prompted:

- Follow the instructions on your campaign page to determine which reward tier you have selected.
- Fill out the Backer Details field with your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
- Select your shipping information, including whether you would like to use the default shipping option or specify custom shipping.
- Choose your shipping date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Backer Details** (`reward_details`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture the backer's name and contact information. Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture any additional shipping information, such as a shipping address or any special instructions.
* **Reward Tier** (`reward_tier`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the reward tier you selected during the campaign. Select "Default" if you are receiving the default reward, or "Custom" if you are receiving a custom reward.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the shipping option that best suits your needs. You can choose to use the default shipping option or specify custom shipping. Please note that custom shipping may incur additional costs or delays.
* **E-Mail** (`backer_email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates and information about your reward.
* **Phone** (`backer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to you with any questions or issues.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, `date`, optional): Choose the date you would like to receive your reward.
* **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time that works best for you to receive your reward.

## Tips
- Please double-check that your reward tier is correct before filling out this form.
- Make sure to select the correct shipping option to avoid any additional costs or delays.
- If you have any special requests or instructions, please enter them in the Shipping Information field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
