# Relief Fund Distribution Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about relief fund distributions and ensure accurate and efficient record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Enter the Donor's ID, which is their unique identifier.
3. Choose a Donation Date for the transaction.
4. Enter the Amount of the donation.
5. Provide the Recipient's Name.
6. Enter the Recipient's Email and Phone (if applicable).
7. Add any additional Notes about the donation.
8. Record the Total Donations made so far.
9. If necessary, indicate the form was submitted by someone other than the actual Donor.
10. Enter the date the form was submitted.
11. Choose the status of the donation and the recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Id (1)**: Enter the Donor's unique identifier.
* **Donation Date (2)**: Choose the date the donation was made.
* **Amount (3)**: Enter the total amount of the donation.
* **Recipient Name (4)**: Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the donation.
* **Recipient Email (5)**: Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Recipient Phone (6)**: Enter the phone number of the recipient (if applicable).
* **Notes (7)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the donation.
* **Total Donations (8)**: Record the total amount donated so far.
* **Form Submitted By (9)**: If this form is not completed by the actual donor, enter the name or identifier of the person or organization submitting the form.
* **Form Submitted On (10)**: Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted By (11)**: Choose the name or identifier of the person or organization that submitted the form.
* **Donation Status (12)**: Select whether the donation is Active, Inactive, or Archived.
* **Recipient Status (13)**: Select whether the recipient is Active, Inactive, or Archived.
* **Notes (14)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the recipient.
* **Donation Status (14)**: Select whether the donation is Active, Inactive, or Archived.
* **Recipient Status (15)**: Select whether the recipient is Active, Inactive, or Archived.
* **Notes (16)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the recipient.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields accurately to ensure accurate record-keeping.
- If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, clearly indicate who submitted the form.
- Double-check the donation status and recipient status to ensure they accurately reflect the current situation.
- Use the form for each new donation, ensuring all donations are tracked and reported accurately.
