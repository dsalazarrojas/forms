# Zoo Animal Exhibit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about donations made to the zoo's animal exhibits, including donor contact details, donation amount, and the purpose of the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Amount Donated, Donor Signature, and Date Donated.
2. Select the animal exhibit you wish to support by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' in the Select an Animal Exhibit field.
3. Indicate your donation frequency by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' in the Select Donation Frequency field.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them in the Donation Comment field.
5. If you have any further questions or need assistance, please contact the zoo's administration team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Amount Donated**: Enter the amount you are donating to the zoo.
* **Purpose of Donation**: If you have a specific purpose for your donation, please describe it here. (Optional)
* **Select an Animal Exhibit**: Choose the animal exhibit you wish to support by selecting 'Yes' from the list.
* **Select Donation Frequency**: Indicate how often you plan to donate to the zoo by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Select Donation Frequency field.
* **Date Donated**: Enter the date you made the donation.
* **Time Donated**: Enter the time you made the donation.
* **Donation Comment**: If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them here. (Optional)
* **Donor Signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge that you are making this donation.
* **Donor Address**: Enter your physical address where you can be contacted for further information. (Optional)
* **Donor City**: Enter the city where you reside. (Optional)
* **Donor State**: Enter the state where you reside. (Optional)
* **Donor Zip**: Enter your zip code where you reside. (Optional)
* **Donor Country**: Enter the country where you reside. (Optional)
* **Donor Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information. (Optional)
* **Donor Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information. (Optional)
* **Donor Comments**: If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them here. (Optional)
* **Donation Total Donated**: Enter the total amount you are donating to the zoo. (Optional)
* **Zoo Animal Exhibit Donation**: If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them here. (Optional)
* **Select Donation Frequency 2**: Indicate how often you plan to donate to the zoo by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Select Donation Frequency 2 field.
* **Select an Animal Exhibit 2**: Choose the animal exhibit you wish to support by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Select Animal Exhibit 2 field.
* **Select Donation Frequency 3**: Indicate how often you plan to donate to the zoo by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Select Donation Frequency 3 field.
