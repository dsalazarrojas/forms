# Charity Event Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Event Report Form is used to collect information about a charity event, including details about the event, attendance, financials, and outcomes. This form helps event organizers and coordinators provide a structured and detailed report of the event, which can be useful for future planning and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the event details**: Enter the official name of the event, the date it was held, and the venue address.
2. **Enter event coordinator information**: Provide the name of the person who coordinated the event.
3. **Report submission date**: Enter the date when you are submitting this report.
4. **Attendance and financials**: Enter the expected and actual attendance numbers, as well as the total revenue raised and total expenses.
5. **Event outcomes**: Evaluate the event's success by selecting whether you met, partially met, or did not meet your goals.
6. **Key successes and challenges**: Describe what went well and what was difficult or problematic during the event.
7. **Improvement recommendations**: Suggest ways to improve future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: The official name of the event.
* **Event Date**: The date when the event was held.
* **Event Location**: The venue address where the event took place.
* **Event Coordinator Name**: The name of the person who coordinated the event.
* **Report Date**: The date when you are submitting this report.
* **Expected Attendance**: The number of people expected to attend the event.
* **Actual Attendance**: The number of people who actually attended the event.
* **Total Revenue Raised**: The amount of money raised during the event.
* **Total Expenses**: The amount of money spent organizing the event.
* **Net Proceeds**: The difference between revenue and expenses.
* **Were Event Goals Achieieved**: Evaluate the event's success by selecting whether you met, partially met, or did not meet your goals.
* **Key Successes**: Describe what went well during the event.
* **Challenges and Obstacles**: Describe what was difficult or problematic during the event.
* **Recommendations for Improvement**: Suggest ways to improve future events.
* **Media Coverage or Publicity**: Describe any press mentions or social media reach.
