# Online Learning Platform Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you donate to our online learning platform. By completing this form, you'll be able to provide your information and preferences for donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to receive updates and follow-ups from us.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** so we can contact you if needed.
4. Choose the amount you'd like to **Donation Amount**.
5. Select your preferred **Donation Frequency** from the options: **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **One-time**.
6. If you have any additional comments about your donation frequency, you can provide them in the **Frequency Note** field.
7. Enter the **Date** you'd like to donate (if any).
8. Enter the **Time** you'd like to donate (if any).
9. Select your **Time Zone** from the options: **Eastern**, **Central**, **Mountain**, or **Pacific**.
10. Enter the **Timezone Offset** (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is where you'll enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: This field is for your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Donation Amount**: Choose the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select the frequency of your donation:
	+ **Monthly**: Donate on a monthly basis.
	+ **Quarterly**: Donate on a quarterly basis.
	+ **One-time**: Donate once.
* **Frequency Note**: If you have any comments about your donation frequency, you can provide them here.
* **Date Donated**: Enter the date you'd like to donate (if any).
* **Time Donated**: Enter the time you'd like to donate (if any).
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone:
	+ **Eastern**: Eastern time zone.
	+ **Central**: Central time zone.
	+ **Mountain**: Mountain time zone.
	+ **Pacific**: Pacific time zone.
* **Timezone Offset**: Enter the offset from UTC (if any).

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [support email].
- We appreciate your donation and support for our online learning platform!
