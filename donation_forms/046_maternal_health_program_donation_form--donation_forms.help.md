# Maternal Health Program Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a donation collection tool for the Maternal Health Program. It is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to contribute to the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by entering your donation amount.
2. Next, select how frequently you would like to make your donations.
3. Choose your preferred payment method.
4. Enter your name for our records.
5. Provide your email address so we can follow up with you about your donation.
6. Finally, enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donations Amount (1)** (`donation_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the Maternal Health Program. This is a required field.
* **Frequency of Donations (2)** (`donation_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select from the available options to indicate how frequently you would like to make your donations. You can choose one or more of the following: Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly.
* **Payment Method (3)** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options: Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer.
* **Name (4)** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name for our records.
* **Email (5)** (`email`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address so we can follow up with you about your donation.
* **Phone Number (6)** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by our team.

## Tips
* Please ensure the donation amount is accurate to avoid any processing issues.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the provided email address.
* Your donation will help support the Maternal Health Program in its mission to improve maternal health outcomes.
