# Public Safety Equipment Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit information about public safety equipment donations, allowing for efficient and accurate tracking of such donations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety or skip sections not relevant to your submission.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Use the "Donate Type" field to indicate the type of donation (Safety Equipment or Other).
4. Be detailed in the "Equipment Needed" field, listing the specific type and quantity of equipment being donated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information (1)
* **User Information** (id: 1, text, required: false):
The field where you provide your name and contact details to be associated with the donation.

### Donate Type (2)
* **Donate Type** (id: 2, select_one, required: false):
Select the type of donation: Safety Equipment or Other.

### Equipment Needed (3)
* **Equipment Needed** (id: 3, text, required: false):
Describe the type and quantity of equipment being donated.

### Condition (4)
* **Condition** (id: 4, text, required: false):
Specify the condition of the equipment (New or Used).

### Donation Value (5)
* **Donation Value** (id: 5, number, required: false):
Enter the value of the donation.

### Donor Name (6)
* **Donor Name** (id: 6, text, required: false):
Provide your name to be associated with the donation.

### Email (7)
* **Email** (id: 7, email, required: false):
Enter your email address for contact.

### Phone (8)
* **Phone** (id: 8, text, required: false):
Add your phone number for contact.

### Address (9)
* **Address** (id: 9, note, required: false):
Enter your work or organization address if applicable.

### Additional Comments (10)
* **Additional Comments** (id: 10, note, required: false):
Provide any further details about the donation.

### Assigned To (11)
* **Assigned To** (id: 11, select_one, required: false):
Select who will be responsible for the equipment: Admin or Manager.

### Department (12)
* **Department** (id: 12, select_multiple, required: false):
Choose the department where the equipment is most likely to be used: Police, Fire, or Emergency Medical Services.

### Equipment Description (13)
* **Equipment Description** (id: 13, text, required: false):
Describe the equipment in detail, including its condition and any notable features.

### Equipment Image (14)
* **Equipment Image** (id: 14, text, required: false):
Upload an image of the equipment, if available.

### Equipment Location (15)
* **Equipment Location** (id: 15, text, required: false):
Specify the location of the equipment.

### Additional Equipment Image (16)
* **Additional Equipment Image** (id: 16, text, required: false):
Upload an image of any additional equipment if applicable.

### Donation Date (17)
* **Donation Date** (id: 17, date, required: false):
Enter the date of the donation.

### Donation Time (18)
* **Donation Time** (id: 18, time, required: false):
Specify the time of the donation.

### Assigned Date (19)
* **Assigned Date** (id: 19, date, required: false):
Enter the date the equipment was assigned.

### Assigned Time (20)
* **Assigned Time** (id: 20, time, required: false):
Specify the time the equipment was assigned.

### Status (21)
* **Status** (id: 21, select_one, required: false):
Mark the status of the equipment: Available or Not Available.

### Condition Status (22)
* **Condition Status** (id: 22, select_one, required: false):
Specify the condition of the equipment: New or Used.

### Comments (23)
* **Comments** (id: 23, select_multiple, required: false):
Indicate if comments are applicable: Yes or No.

### Assigned To Status (24)
* **Assigned To Status** (id: 24, select_one, required: false):
Mark the status of who the equipment is assigned to: Active or Archived.

This form will help ensure that all donations are accurately recorded and tracked according to the relevant details provided by the donor.
