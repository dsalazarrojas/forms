# Community Playground Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Playground Donation Form is designed to collect donations for the maintenance, equipment, or other expenses of a community playground. Fill out this form to provide your contact information, specify your donation details, and choose your preferred recognition and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your donor information: Enter your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify your donation details: Choose the type of donation (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually), select a designee for your donation, and enter a specific playground location if applicable.
3. Choose your payment method: Select a payment method (credit card, debit card, PayPal, check, or bank transfer).
4. Specify recognition preferences: Choose how you would like to be recognized for your donation (e.g., listed on the donor wall, online recognition, or no recognition at all).
5. Specify additional preferences: Choose whether the donation is in honor or memory of someone, and provide the name of the person if applicable.
6. Confirm your tax receipt preference: Select whether you would like to receive a tax receipt via email or not.
7. Add any additional comments or instructions.
8. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information**
	+ **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your email address for receipt and updates.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Donation Information**
	+ **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation in dollars.
	+ **Donation Frequency**: Choose the type of donation (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
	+ **Designate Your Donation**: Choose what your donation should support (e.g., general playground fund, equipment purchase, or safety improvements).
* **Payment Information**
	+ **Payment Method**: Choose a payment method (credit card, debit card, PayPal, check, or bank transfer).
	+ **Card Type**: If you've chosen a credit or debit card, select the type of card you have (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, or Discover).
* **Card Details**
	+ **Card Number**: Enter your card number (if applicable).
	+ **Expiration Date**: Enter your expiration date in MM/YY format.
	+ **CVV**: Enter your three or four-digit code.
* **Recognition and Dedications**
	+ **Recognition Preferences**: Choose how you would like to be recognized for your donation.
	+ **Dedication**: Choose whether the donation is in honor or memory of someone and provide the name if applicable.
	+ **Dedication Name**: Enter the name of the person being honored or remembered.
	+ **Employer Match**: Choose whether your employer offers matching gifts.
* **Additional Preferences**
	+ **Tax Receipt Preference**: Choose whether you'd like to receive a tax receipt via email or not.
	+ **Additional Comments**: Enter any further message or instructions.
* **Specific Playground Location**:
	+ **Enter a specific playground location** if you wish to designate a particular playground for your donation.
* **Additional Comments**
	+ **Provide any additional comments or instructions for your donation**.

Please review the form carefully and fill it out accurately to ensure that your donation is processed correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
