# Animal Shelter Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Shelter Donation Form is used for donors to make a donation to the Animal Shelter. This guide will walk you through the form's sections and fields to ensure you complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the Type of Donation and Designate Your Donation options.
3. Choose your preferred Payment Method and Card Type.
4. If you're paying by card, enter the necessary card details.
5. Confirm your donation details, and review the Donation Terms and Conditions.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name (1)**: Enter your full name or the name of your organization. 
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address for receipt and update purposes. This field is required.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Street Address (4)**: Enter your street address for tax receipt purposes (optional).
* **City (5)**: Enter your city of residence (optional).
* **State (6)**: Enter your state or province of residence (optional).
* **ZIP or Postal Code (7)**: Enter your zip or postal code (optional).
* **Type of Donation (8)**: Select the type of donation you'd like to make: One-Time, Monthly Recurring, Quarterly Recurring, Annual Recurring, Memorial or Tribute, Corporate Matching, or In-Kind Donation.
* **Donation Amount (9)**: Enter the amount of your donation in dollars. This field is required.
* **Designate Your Donation To (10)**: Choose where you'd like your gift to be used. Select Where Needed Most, Medical Care Fund, Food and Supplies, Facility Maintenance, Emergency Rescue Fund, Spay and Neuter Program, Senior Animal Care, Puppy and Kitten Program, Behavioral Rehabilitation, or Other.
* **Other Designation (11)**: If you've selected Other, provide the specific designation for your gift.
* **Is This Donation In Honor or Memory of Someone (12)**: Choose whether this donation is in honor or memory of someone. Select Yes - In Honor, Yes - In Memory, or No.
* **Honoree Name (13)**: If you've selected In Honor or In Memory, enter the name of the person being honored.
* **Should We Send Notification to Honoree Family (14)**: If you've selected Yes - In Honor or In Memory, choose whether you'd like to send notification to the honoree's family. Select Yes - Email, Yes - Mail, or No Thank You.
* **Notification Email (15)**: If you've selected Yes - Email, enter the email address to send the notification to.
* **Payment Method (16)**: Select the method of payment: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check by Mail, or Cryptocurrency.
* **Card Type (17)**: If you've selected Credit Card, Debit Card, or PayPal, choose the type of card you're using: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, or Discover.
* **Card Number (18)**: If you're paying by card, enter the card number. This field is only required if you've selected Credit Card, Debit Card, or PayPal.
* **Expiration Date (19)**: If you're paying by card, enter the expiration date in MM-YYYY format. This field is only required if you've selected Credit Card, Debit Card, or PayPal.
* **Security Code (20)**: If you're paying by card, enter the CVV on the back of the card.
* **Billing Address (21)**: If you've selected a different address for billing purposes, enter it here (optional).
* **Employer Name (22)**: If you're making a matched gift, enter your employer's name.
* **Is Your Employer Matching This Donation (23)**: Indicate whether your employer is matching this donation. Select Yes - Will Submit, Yes - Already Submitted, No, or Not Eligible.
* **Tax Receipt Preference (24)**: Choose whether you'd like to receive an email receipt or a mail receipt.
* **Would You Like to Receive Our Newsletter (25)**: Indicate whether you'd like to receive updates about our work. Select Yes Please, No Thank You.
* **How May We Contact You (26)**: Select all the ways you'd like for us to contact you.
* **Additional Comments or Questions (27)**: Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
* **I Agree to the Donation Terms and Conditions (28)**: Confirm your agreement to the donation terms and conditions. Select Yes I Agree or Other.
