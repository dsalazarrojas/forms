# Online Donation Form Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Donation Form Theme is designed to collect information for online event donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event name in the **Event name** field.
2. If desired, provide a brief description of the event in the **Event description** field.
3. Select the date of the event in the **Event date** field.
4. Choose the time of the event in the **Event time** field.
5. Enter your name in the **Donor name** field.
6. Enter your email address in the **Donor email** field.
7. Enter the donation amount in the **Donation amount** field.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the **Selected payment method** field.
9. (Optional) Provide additional notes in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are interested in.
* **Event description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the event you are interested in (optional).
* **Event date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event.
* **Donor name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Donor email** (`donor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are donating.
* **Donor phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide additional notes about your donation.
* **Selected payment method** (`selected_payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to make your donation (Bank transfer, Credit card, or Cash).
* **Payment details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your payment.
* **Payment method description** (`payment_method_description`, text, optional): Describe the payment method you selected.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the tool assigned to your account.
