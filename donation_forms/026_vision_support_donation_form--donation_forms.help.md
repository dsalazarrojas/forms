# vision_support_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vision Support Donation Form" is a form designed to collect information from individuals who wish to show their support for a particular vision or cause.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your name and email address to complete the form.
2.  Specify the donation amount, frequency, and type.
3.  Choose a payment method and confirm your donation details.
4.  Provide any additional comments or information, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (`Donor Name`, text, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **Donor Email** (`Donor Email`, email, required): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address, if applicable.
* **Gift Amount** (`Gift Amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the gift.
* **Frequency** (`Frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how frequently you would like to make this donation (e.g., "Monthly" or "One-time").
* **Gift Type** (`Gift Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of gift, whether it's "Monetary" or "In-kind".
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_multiple, required): Choose the method of payment from the options provided.
* **Confirm Donation** (`Confirm Donation`, text, required): Confirm your donation details.
* **Tax ID** (`Tax ID`, text, required): If the donation amount exceeds $5,000, you may be prompted for a tax ID number.
* **Is Tax-Deductible** (`Is Tax-Deductible`, select_one, required): Specify whether the donation is tax-deductible.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Comments 2** (`Comments 2`, note, optional): Provide more comments or information, if desired.
