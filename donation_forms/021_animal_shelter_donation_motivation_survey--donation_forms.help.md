# Animal Shelter Donation Motivation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Animal Shelter Donation Motivation Survey is designed to gather information from donors to help the shelter better understand their motivations, preferences, and expectations. This survey aims to identify areas of improvement and build a stronger relationship with donors to increase engagement and donations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your contact information (name and email address) to help us contact you for follow-up.
2. Select your donor type (Individual, Family, Corporate, Foundation, or Anonymous).
3. Choose how long you've been donating to the shelter (First Time Donor, Less than 1 Year, 1-3 Years, 3-5 Years, 5-10 Years, or Over 10 Years).
4. Indicate how often you donate (One-Time Only, Occasionally, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
5. Select your primary motivation for donation (Love of Animals, Personal Experience with Shelter, Community Support, Tax Benefits, Matching Gift Opportunity, Emotional Appeal, Friend or Family Recommendation, or Other).
6. If you have a personal connection to the shelter, select Yes - Adopted From Us, Yes - Volunteered, Yes - Worked Here, Yes - Friend or Family Adopted, or No Personal Connection).
7. Choose all that apply from the list of factors that influence your decision to donate (Success Stories, Photos of Animals, Statistics and Impact Reports, Emergency Appeals, Matching Gift Campaigns, Social Media Posts, Email Newsletters, Direct Mail, Events, Word of Mouth).
8. Select all that apply from the list of causes that matter most to you (Medical Care for Animals, Daily Operations, Emergency Rescues, Spay and Neuter Programs, Behavioral Training, Senior Animal Care, Puppy and Kitten Programs, Facility Improvements, Community Outreach, Where Needed Most).
9. Choose what determines your donation amount (Personal Budget, Specific Need or Campaign, Matching Gift Opportunity, Suggested Amounts, Emotional Response, Regular Fixed Amount).
10. Select all that apply from the list of preferred communication channels (Email, Direct Mail, Social Media, Phone Call, Text Message, Website, Events).
11. Choose what types of messages resonate with you the most (Individual Animal Stories, Shelter-Wide Impact Statistics, Urgent Emergency Appeals, Success and Happy Endings, Educational Content, Behind-the-Scenes Updates).
12. Select all that apply from the list of factors that build your trust in a charity (Financial Transparency, Impact Reports, Third-Party Ratings, Personal Experience, Recommendations, Low Overhead Costs, Clear Mission, Regular Communication).
13. If applicable, select all that apply from the list of potential barriers to your giving (Financial Constraints, Lack of Information, Too Many Requests, Concerns About Fund Usage, Prefer Other Charities, Nothing - I Give Generously, Other).
14. Choose how you'd like to be recognized (Public Recognition, Private Thank You, Newsletter Mention, No Recognition Needed, Anonymous Preferred).
15. Indicate if you've attended fundraising events (Yes - Multiple Events, Yes - One Event, No But Interested, No - Not Interested).
16. If you're interested, choose how you feel about volunteering (Yes - Currently Volunteer, Yes - Planning To, Considering It, No - Prefer to Only Donate).
17. If you're willing, consider including the animal shelter in your estate plans (Yes - Already Done, Yes - Considering, No - Not at This Time, No - Not Interested).
18. Choose your satisfaction level with how the shelter uses donations (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
19. Indicate your likelihood to continue donating (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
20. Choose all that apply from the list of factors that would make you more likely to increase your donation (More Impact Reports, Specific Project Funding, Matching Gift Opportunities, Recognition Programs, More Communication, Less Communication, Nothing - I Give What I Can, Other).
21. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the text box.
22. Finally, thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts with us!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

• **Your Name** (`donor_name`, **text**, Optional): Enter your name to help us contact you for follow-up.
• **Email Address** (`donor_email`, **email**, For follow-up if needed): Provide your email address for us to reach out to you.
• **Donor Type** (`donor_type`, **select_one**, required): Choose your type of donor (Individual, Family, Corporate, Foundation, or Anonymous).
• **How Long Have You Been Donating to Our Shelter?** (`donation_history`, **select_one**, required): Select how long you've been donating to the shelter.
• **How Often Do You Donate?** (`donation_frequency`, **select_one**, required): Choose how often you donate.
• **What Primarily Motivates Your Donations?** (`primary_motivation`, **select_one**, required): Select your primary reason for donating.
• **Do You Have a Personal Connection to Our Shelter?** (`emotional_connection`, **select_one**, required): If you have a personal connection, select the relevant option.
• **What Influences Your Decision to Donate?** (`influence_factors`, **select_multiple**, required): Select all that apply from the list of factors that influence your decision to donate.
• **Which Causes Matter Most to You?** (`preferred_cause`, **select_multiple**, required): Select all that apply from the list of causes that matter most to you.
• **What Determines Your Donation Amount?** (`donation_amount_factor`, **select_one**, required): Choose what determines your donation amount.
• **How Do You Prefer to Hear About Donation Opportunities?** (`communication_preference`, **select_multiple**, required): Select all that apply from the list of preferred communication channels.
• **What Types of Messages Resonate with You the Most?** (`message_resonance`, **select_one**, required): Choose what types of messages resonate with you the most.
• **What Builds Your Trust in a Charity?** (`trust_factors`, **select_multiple**, required): Select all that apply from the list of factors that build your trust in a charity.
• **What Might Prevent You from Donating More?** (`barrier_to_giving`, **select_multiple**, optional): If applicable, select all that apply from the list of potential barriers to your giving.
• **How Do You Prefer to Be Recognized?** (`recognition_preference`, **select_one**, required): Choose how you'd like to be recognized.
• **Have You Ever Attended Our Fundraising Events?** (`event_participation`, **select_one**, required): Indicate if you've attended fundraising events.
• **Have You Considered Volunteering?** (`volunteer_interest`, **select_one**, required): If you're interested, choose how you feel about volunteering.
• **Have You Considered Including Us in Your Estate Plans?** (`legacy_giving`, **select_one**, optional): If willing, consider including the animal shelter in your estate plans.
• **How Satisfied Are You with How We Use Donations?** (`satisfaction_level`, **select_one**, required): Choose your satisfaction level with how the shelter uses donations.
• **How Likely Are You to Continue Donating?** (`likelihood_continue`, **select_one**, required): Indicate your likelihood to continue donating.
• **What Would Make You More Likely to Increase Your Donation?** (`likelihood_increase`, **select_multiple**, optional): Select all that apply from the list of factors that would make you more likely to increase your donation.
• **Any Additional Feedback or Suggestions?** (`additional_feedback`, **text**, optional): If you have any thoughts or suggestions, please provide them here.
