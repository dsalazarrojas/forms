# Charity Event Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand your charity event requirements to provide a quote for our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (org_name, text, Required): Enter your nonprofit or charity's name.
* **Contact Person** (org_contact_person, text, Required): Identify who we should contact.
* **Email Address** (org_email, email, Required): Provide your organization's email address.
* **Phone Number** (org_phone, text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization Address** (org_address, text, Optional): Provide your organization's physical address.
* **Event Type** (event_type, select_one, Required): Choose the type of event (e.g., Gala, Fundraiser Dinner, Auction, etc.).
* **Proposed Event Date** (event_date, date, Required): Select the date of your event.
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (expected_attendance, number, Required): Enter the estimated number of attendees.
* **Venue Needed** (venue_requirement, select_one, Required): Indicate if you need help with venue selection (Yes, No, or Already Selected).
* **Catering Required** (catering_needed, select_one, Required): Choose if you need catering services (Yes, No).
* **Estimated Budget Range** (estimated_budget, number, Optional): Enter your estimated budget in dollars.
* **Services You Need a Quote For** (services_needed, select_multiple, Required): Select the services you need (e.g., Venue Rental, Catering, Audio-Visual Equipment, etc.).
* **Special Requests or Requirements** (special_requests, text, Optional): Provide any unique needs or requirements for your event.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (accessibility_requirements, text, Optional): Specify any accessibility accommodations required (e.g., wheelchair access, hearing assistance, etc.).
* **Primary Goal or Fundraising Target** (event_goal, text, Optional): Describe your primary goal or fundraising target.
* **When Do You Need This Quote** (quote_deadline, date, Required): Select the date by which you need our estimate.
* **Additional Information** (additional_information, text, Optional): Provide any other relevant details for us to know.
