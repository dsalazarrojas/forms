# Non Profit Donation Template WorldPay US - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Non Profit Donation Template WorldPay US form is designed to collect donation information from users for non-profit organizations operating in the United States. This form is intended for secure and easy donations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select the "Donation Amount" field and enter your donation amount.
2. Enter your "Full Name" in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred "Payment Method" from the available options.
6. Select the "Recurring Payment" option if you want to set up recurring payments.
7. Choose the frequency of your recurring payments from the "Frequency" field.
8. If you're setting up a recurring payment, select the start and end dates for your payments in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
9. Select your timezone in the "Timezone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Full Name** (Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication and payment notifications.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options.
* **Recurring Payment** (Select Multiple): Select this option to set up recurring payments.
* **Frequency** (Select One): Choose the frequency of your recurring payments.
* **Start Date** (Date): Select the start date for your recurring payments.
* **End Date** (Date): Select the end date for your recurring payments.
* **Timezone** (Time): Select your timezone for accurate payment scheduling.
* **End Date** (Date): Select the end date for your recurring payments.
