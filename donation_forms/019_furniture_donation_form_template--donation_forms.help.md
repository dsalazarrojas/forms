# Furniture Donation Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about furniture donations, including contact details and delivery address of the donor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** in the "Email" field (ensure it's a valid email address).
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Type in your **Address 1** in the "Address 1" field.
6. Enter any additional information in the "Address 2" field, if necessary.
7. Fill in your **City** in the "City" field.
8. Select your **State** in the "State" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given first name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
	+ Type: `email`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Address 1**: Enter your street address or the address of the donation location.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Address 2**: Enter any additional address information, such as apartment or building number.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **City**: Enter the city where the donation is located.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **State**: Select the state where the donation is located.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
