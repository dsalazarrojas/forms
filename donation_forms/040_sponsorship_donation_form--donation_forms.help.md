# Sponsorship Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track donations from sponsors. It captures essential information about the sponsor, including their name, email, and phone number, as well as the details of the donation such as the amount donated, date received, and purpose of the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the sponsorship in the "Sponsorship Donation Form" field.
2. Upload a logo for the sponsor in the "Logo" field.
3. Enter the sponsor's name in the "Sponsor Name" field.
4. Enter the sponsor's email address in the "Sponsor Email" field (if required).
5. Enter the sponsor's phone number in the "Sponsor Phone" field (if required).
6. Select the date the donation was received in the "Date Received" field.
7. Enter the amount donated in the "Amount Donated" field.
8. Provide a brief description of the purpose of the donation in the "Purpose of Donation" field (if required).
9. Choose whether to archive the submission in the "Archive Submissions" field.
10. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field (if required).
11. Enter the name of the person who submitted the donation in the "Submitted By" field (if required).
12. Select the time the submission was made in the "Submission Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsorship Donation Form** (`form_title`, text, **Required**): Enter a title for the sponsorship.
* **Upload Logo** (`logo`, text, **Required**): Upload a logo representing the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, **Optional**): Enter the sponsor's email address (if required).
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter the sponsor's phone number (if required).
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, **Required**): Select the date the donation was received.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number, **Required**): Enter the amount donated.
* **Purpose of Donation** (`purpose_donation`, text, **Optional**): Provide a brief description of the purpose of the donation (if required).
* **Archive Submissions** (`archive_submission`, select_one, **Required**): Choose whether to archive the submission.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, **Optional**): Add any additional notes about the donation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the person who submitted the donation (if required).
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, **Required**): Select the time the submission was made.
