# Military Support Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for donations to support military personnel or causes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Enter the donation amount you wish to donate.
3. Choose the payment method (e.g. Cash, Online Payment, Credit Card, or PayPal).
4. Select the date and time you wish to donate.
5. If you have any notes or comments, you can add them in the "Note" field.
6. Fill in any additional details such as Organization, Purpose, Military ID, Address, City, State, Zip, and Country.
7. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 1`): Please enter your first name for the donation records.
* **Last Name** (`id: 2`): Please enter your last name for the donation records.
* **Email** (`id: 3`): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`id: 4`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Donation Amount** (`id: 5`): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Payment Method 1** (`id: 6`): Select the payment method for your donation (e.g. Cash, Online Payment, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Payment Method 2** (`id: 7`): (This field seems redundant with "Payment Method 1", but it's likely meant for selecting multiple payment methods).
* **Date to Donate** (`id: 8`): Enter the date you wish to make the donation.
* **Time to Donate** (`id: 9`): Enter the time you wish to make the donation.
* **Note** (`id: 10`): Enter any notes or comments you have about your donation.
* **Organization** (`id: 11`): If you are associated with an organization, please provide the name of it.
* **Purpose** (`id: 12`): Enter the purpose of your donation (e.g. for a specific soldier or cause).
* **Military ID** (`id: 13`): Enter your military ID number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`id: 14`): Enter your address for delivery or mailing purposes.
* **City** (`id: 15`): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`id: 16`): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (`id: 17`): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`id: 18`): Enter the country where you reside.
* **Amount Donated** (`id: 19`): This field is likely used to record the donation amount after processing.
* **Donation Status** (`id: 20`): This field is likely used to track the status of the donation (e.g. Pending, Accepted, Declined, Cancelled, or Completed).
* **Reason Declined** (`id: 21`): Enter the reason for declining the donation (if applicable).
* **Amount Refunded** (`id: 22`): Enter the amount refunded (if applicable).
* **Refund Status** (`id: 23`): Enter the refund status (e.g. Not Refunded, Partially Refunded, Fully Refunded).
* **Refund Method** (`id: 24`): Enter the method of refund (e.g. Check, Bank Transfer, PayPal).
* **Comments** (`id: 25`): Enter any additional comments about your donation.
