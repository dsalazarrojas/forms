# Fundraising Gala Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Gala Attendance Form is a required form for all attendees to provide essential information about themselves and their guests. This form helps our team prepare for the event and ensure that everyone has a great experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your affiliation with the organization (e.g., "Volunteer", "Donor", etc.) in the "role" field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact phone" field.
6. If applicable, provide any comments about your experience or suggestions for the event in the "comments" field.
7. Indicate your preferred dress code for the event in the "event dress code" field.
8. If you have any dietary restrictions, select the relevant options in the "dietary restrictions" field. You can also specify any other dietary restrictions in the "dietary restrictions other" field if needed.
9. If you are bringing a guest, select the "guest attendee" options accordingly in the "guest attendee" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the organization or affiliation you are representing (e.g., "Volunteer", "Donor", etc.).
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select the role you will be playing at the event (e.g., "Attendee", "Volunteer", "Donor", etc.).
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions for the event, please enter them here.
* **Event dress code** (`event_dress_code`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred dress code for the event (e.g., "Formal", "Casual", "Other").
* **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., "Vegan", "Vegetarian", "Gluten-free", etc.).
* **Dietary restrictions other** (`dietary_restrictions_other`, `text`, optional): If you have any other dietary restrictions not listed, please specify them here.
* **Guest attendee** (`guest_attendee`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether you are bringing a guest (yes or no).
* **Guest attendee** (`guest_attendee`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is identical to the previous field and is included for redundancy; please select whether you are bringing a guest (yes or no). 

Note: The form is designed to be completed by each attendee, not by the person making the donation or registering the attendee.
