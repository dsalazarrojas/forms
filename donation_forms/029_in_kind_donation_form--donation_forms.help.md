# In Kind Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about in-kind donations made by individuals or organizations to the company. In-kind donations are non-monetary items, such as goods or services, provided to support a particular cause or project. This form helps us understand the details of the donation, including the amount donated, items accepted, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for the in-kind donation, in the designated field.
2. Enter the amount of the in-kind donation in the specified field.
3. List the items accepted as part of the in-kind donation in the designated field.
4. Optionally, provide any additional comments about the donation in the comments field.
5. Enter your email address in the email field.
6. Enter your phone number in the phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **In-Kind Donation Form** (`in_kind_donation_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the in-kind donation, e.g., "New Office Equipment" or "Food Drive for Homeless Shelter".
* **In Kind Donation Amount** (`in_kind_donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the dollar amount of the in-kind donation.
* **In Kind Donation Items Accepted** (`in_kind_donation_items_accepted`, text, optional): List the specific items accepted as part of the donation, e.g., "Office chairs, desks, and computers".
* **In Kind Donation Comments** (`in_kind_donation_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or details about the donation, e.g., "Donated to support our office expansion project".
* **In Kind Donation Email** (`in_kind_donation_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for verification and follow-up purposes.
* **In Kind Donation Phone** (`in_kind_donation_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for verification and follow-up purposes.
