# Farmers Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmers Support Donation Form is designed to collect information from farmers who wish to support our organization's activities through donations. This form helps us understand your support and tailor our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your First Name and Last Name to identify yourself.
* Provide your Email and Phone Number for contact purposes.
* Fill in your Address for us to communicate with you effectively.
* Specify the Amount Donated and the Purpose of Donation for our records.
* Add any Additional Donation Details if necessary.
* Choose your Donor Type from the provided options.
* If applicable, enter the name of your Donor Organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to communicate with you.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your current address for us to send any necessary information.
* **Amount Donated** (number, optional): Specify the amount you wish to donate.
* **Purpose of Donation** (text, optional): Describe the reason for your donation.
* **Additional Donation Details** (text, optional): Add any further information you'd like to share about your donation.
* **Donor Type** (select_one, optional): Choose which category you fall under:
	+ Option 1: Individual Farmer
	+ Option 2: Farming Organization
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **Donor Organization** (text, optional): If you're representing an organization, enter its name here.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We appreciate your support and look forward to assisting you.
