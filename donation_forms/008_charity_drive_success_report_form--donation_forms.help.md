# Charity Drive Success Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the success of a charity drive, providing information on key metrics such as funds raised, number of donors, and participant engagement. It is meant to be filled out by the person responsible for managing the charity drive, and it will help them provide accurate and comprehensive insights into the event's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and truthfully**, providing detailed answers to all questions.
2. **Use a single form per charity drive**, do not fill out multiple forms for the same event.
3. **Only report on a completed charity drive**, do not use this form for ongoing or future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Charity Drive**: Enter the name of the charity drive, such as "Summer Food Drive" or "Holiday Toy Drive".
* **Event Date**: Enter the date the charity drive took place.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person responsible for managing the charity drive.
* **Total Funds Raised**: Enter the total amount of funds raised during the charity drive.
* **Total Number of Donors**: Enter the total number of people who donated during the charity drive.
* **Was the fundraising goal met**: Select one of the options: "Goal Exceeded", "Goal Met", or "Goal Not Met".
* **Total Volunteer Hours Logged**: Enter the total number of volunteer hours logged during the charity drive.
* **Number of First-Time Supporters**: Enter the number of new supporters who donated during the charity drive.
* **Estimated People Reached**: Enter an estimate of how many people were reached through marketing and outreach efforts.
* **What were the top success factors**: Provide a brief description of the key factors that contributed to the charity drive's success.
* **Major Challenges Encountered**: Describe any major challenges that occurred during the charity drive.
* **Summary of Donor Feedback**: Provide a brief summary of the feedback received from donors.
* **Recommendations for Future Drives**: Suggest ways to improve future charity drives based on your experience.
* **Date of Report**: Enter the date you are submitting this report.
