# Widows’ Assistance Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Widows’ Assistance Donation Form is designed to collect information from potential donors who want to support the Widows’ Assistance program. This form helps us understand who is donating and why, so we can better utilize their support for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Donor" field with your name or the name of the organization you are representing.
2. Enter the amount you would like to donate in the "Donation Amount" field. You can use a number without any currency symbol.
3. If you have a specific purpose for your donation, describe it in the "Purpose of Donation" field. This helps us understand how you would like your contribution to be used.
4. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field. This is how we will contact you to confirm your donation and provide updates on how your support is being used.
5. Format your phone number as (123) 456-7890 in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor**: Enter your name or the name of the organization you are representing.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate without any currency symbol. This is the amount we will use to process your donation.
* **Purpose of Donation**: If you have a specific reason for your donation, describe it here. This helps us understand how to utilize your support effectively.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address to which we will send a confirmation of your donation and updates on how your support is being used.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format (123) 456-7890 to ensure accurate and easy communication.
