# Mini Grant Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mini Grant Donation Form is designed to collect information for a small-scale grant donation program. The form helps us understand the donor's name, contact details, and intended donation amount. Please complete this form to submit your interest in participating in the grant program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the submit button to start the donation request process.
2. Fill in the required fields below.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donator Name** (`donator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to appear on the grant report.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you for further instructions on the grant process.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate for the grant program.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us at [support contact].
* Review your form submission before hitting the submit button to catch any errors.
