# Donor Family Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Family Registration Form is a tool used to gather essential information about a donor family, including their contact details, donation history, and medical information. This form helps organizations to collect and manage donor family data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form thoroughly**: Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
2. **Review each section carefully**: Ensure you have provided accurate information for each field.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to save your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Family Info (page 1)**:
	+ <label>Donor Family Info</label> (Required): Provide the name and contact information of the donor family.
* **Contact Details (page 2)**:
	+ <label>Contact Details</label> (Required): Enter the contact details of the person responsible for the donor family, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Donation Info (page 3)**:
	+ <label>Donation Info</label> (Optional): Share any relevant information about the donation, such as the date, amount, and purpose.
* **Consent (page 4)**:
	+ <label>Consent</label> (Optional): Indicate whether the donor family has given consent for their information to be shared.
* **Medical Info (page 5)**:
	+ <label>Medical Info</label> (Optional): Share any relevant medical information about the donor family.
* **Medical History (page 6)**:
	+ <label>Medical History</label> (Optional): Provide any relevant medical history for the donor family.
* **Emergency Contacts (page 7)**:
	+ <label>Emergency Contacts</label> (Optional): List emergency contact information for the donor family.
* **Additional Info (page 8)**:
	+ <label>Additional Info</label> (Optional): Provide any additional information about the donor family.

## Tips

* Be as thorough as possible when filling out the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator.
