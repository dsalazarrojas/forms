# Free Health Checkup Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from donors who want to contribute to the free health checkup program. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are accurate and filled out.
2. Select the donation frequency and payment terms that best reflect your intentions.
3. Enter your email address correctly, as this will be used for future communication.
4. Provide any additional comments or information about your donation, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information** (`Donor Information`, `text`, not required): Please enter your personal details as the representative of the donation. This can include any relevant information such as your name, address, or contact number.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person making the donation, if not the same as yourself.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, not required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, `number`, not required): Select a monetary amount you would like to donate, in whole numbers only.
* **Donation Frequency** (`Donation Frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how often you would like to donate:
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ One-time
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method you would like to use:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Gift Details** (`Gift Details`, `text`, not required): If you have specific wishes or requests for how the donation should be used, enter them here.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method by which you would like to pay:
	+ Online
	+ Offline
* **Donor Comments** (`Donor Comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information about your donation, such as your motivation or the name of the person you would like to be notified about the donation.
* **Donor Info Additional** (`Donor Info Additional`, `note`, not required): This is an open field for any additional information you would like to share about your donation. 

## Tips
* Please review all fields carefully before submitting to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you have any issues with this form, contact the administration team for assistance.
* This form is intended for individual donors, if you are representing an organization, please contact us separately for further instructions.
