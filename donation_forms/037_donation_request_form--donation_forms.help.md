# Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors about their donation requests, including the amount and frequency of their donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field.
4. Choose how frequently you'd like to donate from the options provided in the "Donation Frequency" field.
5. Select your preferred payment method from the list in the "Payment Method" field.
6. If applicable, share any additional information or message in the "Additional Message" field.
7. If applicable, select your age group from the list in the "Donor Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate, without including any currency symbols or decimal points.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how frequently you'd like to donate: Monthly, Quarterly, or One-Time.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the payment method you prefer: Card, EFT, Bank Account, or Wire Transfer.
* **Additional Message** (`message`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information or message, please share it here.
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the age group you fit into: Age 18-24, Age 25-34, Age 35-44, Age 45-54, or Age 55+.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled in to submit the form.
