# music_venue_support_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and donations from users to support music venues. It helps us understand the value of your contribution and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (`user_info`)**: Enter your or the donor's name for accurate tracking and acknowledgement.
* **Donation Amount (`donation_amount`)**: Enter a numerical value to specify the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Donation Fees (`donation_fees`)**: Add any extra fees related to your donation.
* **Select A Venue (`select_venue`)**: Choose one or more venues to support, such as Venue 1, Venue 2, or Venue 3.
* **Donation Frequency (`donation_frequency`)**: Select how often you'd like to make donations, e.g., once, monthly, or quarterly.
* **Date (`date`)**: Specify the date for processing or using your donation.
* **Time (`time`)**: Enter the specific time, if required.
* **Note (`note`)**: Add any comments or notes about your donation.
* **Email (`email`)**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone (`phone`)**: Provide your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for further information.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Choose dates and times that suit your schedule and needs.
* Feel free to contact us with any questions or concerns.
