# Matching Gift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Matching Gift Form is used to collect information about matching gifts, which are donations made on behalf of the user. This form is optional but helps to track and acknowledge matching gifts when they occur.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the required information about the donor, including their name, email, and phone number.
* If the gift is a matching gift, select the corresponding matching gift type (Matching or Challenge) from the options provided.
* Enter the amount of the matching gift.
* Provide any additional information about the match request, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Request**: This is a note field to provide any additional information about the donation.
* **Donor Name**: This is the name of the person making the matching gift on behalf of the user. 
* **Donor Email**: This is the email address of the person making the matching gift.
* **Donor Phone**: This is the phone number of the person making the matching gift.
* **Matching Gift Type**: This is the type of match the donor is making (Matching or Challenge).
* **Matching Amount**: This is the amount of the matching gift.
* **Match Request**: This is any additional information the donor would like to provide about their match request.

## Tips
* Make sure to include accurate and up-to-date information about the donor and the matching gift.
* If the form is being completed in an emergency or time-sensitive situation, prioritize accuracy but do not sacrifice completeness.
* If the form is being completed by someone other than the actual donor, please ensure you have their explicit permission to collect this information.
