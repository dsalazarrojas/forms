# Save The Trees Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Save The Trees Donation Form is a user-facing form used to collect donation information from individuals who wish to support the organization's mission to save trees. This form is designed to gather essential details about the donor, including name, contact information, and donation amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the Last Name field (optional).
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
5. Enter your desired donation amount in the Donation Amount field.
6. Select the method of donation from the Donation Method field (PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
7. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can enter them in the Note field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`save_the_trees_donation_form_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`save_the_trees_donation_form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name (if applicable).
* **Email** (`save_the_trees_donation_form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`save_the_trees_donation_form_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Donation Amount** (`save_the_trees_donation_form_donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Donation Method** (`save_the_trees_donation_form_donation_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of donation (PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Note** (`save_the_trees_donation_form_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
