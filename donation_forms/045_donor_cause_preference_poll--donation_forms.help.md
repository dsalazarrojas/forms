# Donor Cause Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Donor Cause Preference Poll" form is used to collect information from donors about their preferred causes, donation frequency, and contact methods. This information will help the organization understand the preferences of their donors and tailor their communications accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the title and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Choose the cause you prefer to support from the options provided.
3. Indicate how important the selected cause is to you.
4. Select your preferred donation frequency.
5. Provide any additional comments if you have any.
6. Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
7. Select how often you want to be contacted (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
8. Provide your email address if you want to be contacted by email.
9. Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
10. Add any additional comments.
11. Choose how often you want to be contacted by email and phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Preference Poll** (`donation_form_donor_cause_preference_poll`): This is the title of the form, which is used as a header.
* **What is the cause you prefer to support** (`donor_cause`): Choose the cause you prefer to support from the options provided.
* **How important is this cause to you** (`cause_priority`): Indicate how important the selected cause is to you.
* **How often do you make donations** (`donation_frequency`): Select your preferred donation frequency.
* **Additional comments** (`cause_comment`): Provide any additional comments about the selected cause.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **How often do you want to be contacted** (`contact_method_frequency`): Select how often you want to be contacted.
* **E-mail** (`email`): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments.
* **How often do you want to be contacted by email** (`email_frequency`): Select how often you want to be contacted by email.
* **How often do you want to be contacted by phone** (`phone_frequency`): Select how often you want to be contacted by phone.

## Tips
* Please select only one cause you prefer to support.
* You can provide multiple reasons why you prefer the selected cause.
* Make sure to enter your email and phone number accurately.
* If you select to be contacted by email or phone, please ensure that the email address and phone number are up-to-date.
* You can provide additional comments if you have any other information to share.
* Select the preferred contact frequency carefully, as this will determine how often you will be contacted.
