# Burn Unit Expansion Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Burn Unit Expansion Donation Form is designed to collect donations for a burn unit expansion project. This form helps us to track donor information, donation details, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the donor information section with your full name and email address.
2. Enter the donation amount in local currency.
3. Select the type of donation (One time, Monthly pledge, or In honor of someone).
4. If the donation is in honor of someone, provide their name.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it for contact purposes.
6. Provide your billing address if needed for receipts.
7. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit card, Bank transfer, Check, or Online payment gateway).
8. Decide if you would like to receive a donation receipt.
9. Optionally, include a message to be included with the donation receipt.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor full name**: Your full name as it should appear on donation records.
* **Donor email**: Enter your email address for donation receipts and acknowledgements.
* **Donation amount**: Enter the amount of the donation in local currency.
* **Donation type**: Choose the type of donation you would like to make (One time, Monthly pledge, or In honor of someone).
* **Tribute name**: If the donation is in honor of someone, provide their name. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Billing address**: Enter your billing address for receipts if required (optional).
* **Payment method**: Select your preferred payment method (Credit card, Bank transfer, Check, or Online payment gateway).
* **Request donation receipt**: Decide if you would like to receive a donation receipt.
