# Legislative Awareness Campaign Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a donation to the legislative awareness campaign fund, ensuring accurate reflection and secure transmission of the donor's information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the Donation Form, providing as much information as you can to ensure a smooth experience.
2. Select your preferred donation amount and method from the options provided.
3. Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your donation.
4. Review your information to ensure accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Form** (`_`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us about your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate, and we will process it accordingly.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided (Cash, Card, or Bank Transfer).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your donation.
