# music_program_equipment_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Music Program Equipment Fund Donation Form is a tool for schools to provide information about their music programs and the equipment they need to enhance their music education offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you need to provide the following information:

*   Name of the school
*   Address of the school
*   Contact name
*   Contact email
*   Contact phone number
*   Music program department
*   Number of instruments needed
*   Type of equipment requested
*   Value of the donation
*   Follow-up request

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name**: A required field where you need to enter the name of the school.
*   **Organization**: A required field where you need to enter the name of the organization.
*   **Address**: A required field where you need to enter the address of the school.
*   **Email**: A required field where you need to enter a valid email address.
*   **Phone**: A required field where you need to enter the contact phone number.
*   **Description**: An optional field where you can enter additional information about the music program and the equipment requested.
*   **Music Instrument**: A non-required field where you can select one or more music instruments that are needed for the program.
*   **Donation Amount**: A required field where you need to enter the value of the donation.
*   **Donation Date**: A required field where you need to enter the date the donation is intended to be made.
*   **Instrument Request**: A non-required field where you can select whether the donation is for a specific instrument or not.
*   **School Name**: A required field where you need to enter the name of the school.
*   **School Address**: A required field where you need to enter the address of the school.
*   **Contact Name**: A required field where you need to enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email**: A required field where you need to enter a valid email address.
*   **Contact Phone**: A required field where you need to enter the contact phone number.
*   **Instrument Type**: A non-required field where you can select the type of instrument or material needed.
*   **Music Department**: A required field where you need to enter the department name.
*   **Instrument Count**: A required field where you need to enter the number of instruments needed.
*   **Equipment Request**: A non-required field where you can select one or more equipment types that are needed.
*   **Other Equipment**: A non-required field where you can enter other equipment that is not listed in the options.
*   **Donation Value**: A required field where you need to enter the value of the donation.
*   **Notes**: An optional field where you can enter additional comments or notes about the donation.
*   **Follow Up**: A non-required field where you can select whether you want to be followed up on or not.
*   **Submitted By**: A non-required field where you can select who submitted the request.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form fields. The specific requirements and constraints may vary depending on the context and organization.
