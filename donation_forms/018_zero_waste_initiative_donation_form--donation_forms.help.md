# zero_waste_initiative_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The zero_waste_initiative_donation_form is a form designed to collect donations for the zero waste initiative. This form is used to gather information about the donation amount, frequency, and other details from donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (if any) by typing in the relevant information.
2. Select the desired frequency of donation from the options.
3. Choose a project, organization, and contact from the respective options.
4. Provide any additional message or comment in the message field if needed.
5. Review the form to ensure everything is correct before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donation Amount (1)
• **Donation Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
### Donation Frequency (2)
• **Donation Frequency** (select one, optional): Select how frequently you would like to make this donation, either Monthly, One-time, or Recurring.
### Project Name (3)
• **Project Name** (text, optional): Type in the name of the project you would like to support.
### Organization Name (4)
• **Organization Name** (text, optional): Type in the name of the organization you represent.
### Contact Name (5)
• **Contact Name** (text, optional): Type in your name or the name of the person to contact.
### Email (6)
• **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
### Phone (7)
• **Phone** (text, optional): Type in your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
### Message (8)
• **Message** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your donation.
### Donation Frequency Options (9)
• **Donation Frequency** (select multiple, optional): Select multiple frequencies of donation you would like to make, such as Monthly, One-time, or Recurring.
### Project (10)
• **Project** (select one, optional): Choose a project you would like to support, such as Project A, Project B, or Project C.
### Organization (11)
• **Organization** (select one, optional): Choose an organization you represent, such as Organization A, Organization B, or Organization C.
### Contact (12)
• **Contact** (select one, optional): Choose a contact person, such as Contact A, Contact B, or Contact C.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct frequency and options to accurately reflect your donation preferences.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are correct.
