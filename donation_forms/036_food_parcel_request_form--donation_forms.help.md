# Food Parcel Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Parcel Request Form is used to collect information from users for food parcel requests. This form is designed to be completed by users who want to request food parcels.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your user name in the "user" field.
2. Specify the quantity of food you require in the "quantity" field.
3. Select the type of food parcel you need by choosing from the options in the "food_parcel_type" field.
4. Enter the date you would like to collect your food parcel in the "collection_date" field.
5. Choose the food items you would like to collect by selecting from the options in the "food_item" field.
6. Enter the location where you would like to collect your food parcel in the "collection_location" field.
7. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
9. Enter a message or comments about your food parcel request in the "message" field.
10. Specify the time you would like to collect your food parcel in the "collection_time" field.
11. Choose the time zone of the collection location in the "collection_time_zone" field.
12. Select the day of the week you would like to collect your food parcel in the "collection_day_of_week" field.
13. Specify whether this is a form submission for a new food parcel request or not in the "form_submission_id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user** (Text, Optional): Enter your user name to help us track your requests.
* **quantity** (Number, Optional): Specify the quantity of food you require.
* **food_parcel_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of food parcel you need.
* **collection_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you would like to collect your food parcel.
* **food_item** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the food items you would like to collect.
* **collection_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where you would like to collect your food parcel.
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact number.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **message** (Text, Optional): Enter a message or comments about your food parcel request.
* **collection_date_time** (Time, Optional): Specify the time you would like to collect your food parcel.
* **collection_time_zone** (Select One, Optional): Choose the time zone of the collection location.
* **collection_day_of_week** (Select One, Optional): Select the day of the week you would like to collect your food parcel.
* **collection_time** (Time, Optional): Specify the time you would like to collect your food parcel.
* **form_submission_id** (Select One, Optional): Specify whether this is a form submission for a new food parcel request or not.
* **collection_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where you would like to collect your food parcel.
