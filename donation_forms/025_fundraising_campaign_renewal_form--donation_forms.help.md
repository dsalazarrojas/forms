# fundraising_campaign_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help donors renew their existing fundraising campaigns. It collects information about the donor and the campaign details to ensure that the campaign continues without interruption.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the donor information section (ID 1-6) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Fill out the campaign details section (ID 7-10) with the campaign's title, start date, and end date.
3. Select the desired donation frequency (ID 14).
4. Choose a payment method (ID 15).
5. Fill out any additional notes or comments (ID 16).
6. Check if the campaign is still active (ID 17).
7. Fill out any other campaign details (ID 18-20).
8. Fill out the frequency start and end dates (ID 21-22).
