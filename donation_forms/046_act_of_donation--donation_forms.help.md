# Act Of Donation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Act Of Donation form is used to document the transfer of a vehicle without monetary payment, allowing for accurate and consistent recording of this type of transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the donor information, including the donor's full name and address.
2. Enter the donee information, including their full name and address.
3. Provide details about the vehicle being transferred, including make, model, vehicle year, and VIN number.
4. Specify the donation purpose (e.g. Family Transfer, Charitable Gift, or Other).
5. Sign both the donor and donee sections to confirm the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name** (`donor_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person giving the vehicle.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the person giving the vehicle.
* **Donee Full Name** (`donee_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person receiving the vehicle.
* **Donee Address** (`donee_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the person receiving the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the make of the vehicle being transferred (e.g. Ford, Toyota).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter the model of the vehicle being transferred (e.g. F-150, Camry).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required): Enter the year of the vehicle being transferred.
* **VIN Number** (`vin_number`, text, required): Enter the 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle being transferred.
* **Estimated Value at Time of Donation** (`estimated_value_at_time_of_donation`, number, optional): Enter an estimate of the vehicle's value at the time of the donation.
* **Date of Transfer** (`date_of_transfer`, date, required): Enter the date the vehicle was transferred.
* **Donation Purpose** (`donation_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of the donation (e.g. Family Transfer, Charitable Gift, or Other).
* **Donor Signature** (`donor_signature`, text, required): Sign as the donor by typing your name.
* **Donee Signature** (`donee_signature`, text, required): Sign as the donee by typing their name.
