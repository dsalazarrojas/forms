# surgical_ward_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **surgical_ward_donation_form** is a form used to collect donations for the surgical ward. The form is designed to gather general information, donation amount, and payment method from the user. This form is required for all donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information page with the required details.
2. Enter the donation amount on the next page.
3. Select the payment method from the available options.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This page collects general information about the donor, such as name, contact details, or any other relevant details. Please fill in the necessary fields.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate for the surgical ward. Please ensure it is a numerical value.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the available payment methods:
	+ **Cash**: Payment by cash at the reception desk.
	+ **Card**: Payment by credit card (e.g. Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
	+ **PayPal**: Payment through PayPal, a secure online payment system.
