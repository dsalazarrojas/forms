# Water Bottle Donation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about water bottle donations, including user and donor information, donation details, and delivery information. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your user information:
	* Enter your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the donor's information:
	* Enter the donor's name and any other relevant details.
3. Provide donation details:
	* Enter the number of bottles you need and any additional notes.
4. Select the delivery date and time:
	* Choose a date and time for delivery.
5. Enter any delivery notes:
	* Enter any additional delivery instructions or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`):
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`):
	+ Enter the donor's name and any other relevant details about them.
* **Donation Details** (`donation_details`):
	+ Enter the number of bottles you need, and provide any additional notes about the donation.
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`):
	+ Enter the date and time you need the donation to be delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`):
	+ Enter the date you need the donation to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`):
	+ Enter the time you need the donation to be delivered.
* **Quantities Needed** (`quantities_needed`):
	+ Select the number of bottles you need, from 1-10, 11-20, or 21-30 bottles.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`):
	+ Enter any additional delivery instructions or requests.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`):
	+ Enter the donor's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Donation Request** (`donation_request`):
	+ Enter any additional notes about the donation request.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`):
	+ Enter the date you are making the donation request.
* 

Tips: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for further assistance.
