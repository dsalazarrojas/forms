# Job Training Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track donations for job training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and the fields that are required.
2. Fill in the answers for each field based on your knowledge or research.
3. Submit the form to record the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Training Donation** (`job_training_donation_form_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if this job training program has been donated for, or "No" if it has not. This field is used to determine whether the program is active.
  - If you are unsure, review your internal records or discuss with the relevant team or department.
- **Recurring Donation Frequency** (`recurring_donation_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if the donation is a recurring one, or "No" if it is a one-time donation. This field is used to determine the frequency of the donation.
  - If you are unsure, check the internal agreement or contract details.
- **Donation Amount** (`job_training_donation_form_3`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended to capture the amount of the donation.
  - Please use the correct formatting (e.g., "X dollars", "Y dollars per year", etc.).
  - Leave blank if not applicable.
- **Donor Contact Information** (`job_training_donation_form_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the contact information for the donor (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
  - If there is no contact information, select "No" and describe why in the `job_training_donation_form_8` field below.
- **Donation Period** (`job_training_donation_form_5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the donation period (e.g., 1, 2, 5 years, etc.).
  - If not applicable, leave blank.
- **Donor Email** (`job_training_donation_form_6`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the donor.
  - If there is no email address, select "No" and describe why in the `job_training_donation_form_8` field below.
- **Additional Comments** (`job_training_donation_form_7`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the donation.
  - This field is for extra information that may not fit in the other fields.
- **Notes** (`job_training_donation_form_8`, `note`, required: false): If there is no donor contact information, please describe the reason in this field.
  - This field is for internal notes and comments that do not fit in other fields.
