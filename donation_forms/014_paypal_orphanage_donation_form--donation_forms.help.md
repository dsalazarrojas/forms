# PayPal Orphanage Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The PayPal Orphanage Donation Form is designed to facilitate the donation process for the orphanage. It's essential to fill out this form to provide your support to this cause. Ensure you're not making duplicate donations or have already contributed for the same purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the form with your details.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you've provided the required information.
3.  Submit the form to complete the donation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`user_input_2`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email** (`user_input_3`, email, required: false)
	+ A field to enter a valid email address for correspondence and communication.
* **Phone** (`user_input_4`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Amount** (`user_input_5`, number, required: false)
	+ A field to enter the amount you'd like to donate, which will be used for processing the donation.
* **Donor Type** (`user_input_6`, select_one, required: false)
	+ A dropdown selection to choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Donor Type Options** (`user_input_7`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ A multiple selection field to choose from options 'Type A', 'Type B', or 'Type C'.
* **Date** (`user_input_8`, date, required: false)
	+ A date field to enter the date of your donation.
* **Time** (`user_input_9`, time, required: false)
	+ A time field for scheduling or recording a specific time of your donation.
* **Note** (`user_input_10`, note, required: false)
	+ A field for entering any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Comment** (`user_input_11`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field for entering any extra comments or thoughts about your donation.

## Tips

* Ensure you've entered accurate and up-to-date information to process your donation efficiently.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
* Double-check your selections and entries before submitting the form.
