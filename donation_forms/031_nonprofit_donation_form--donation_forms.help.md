# Nonprofit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Donation Form is a tool for individuals to contribute to various campaigns and causes through an online platform. It collects essential information from donors to process their donation correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your Donor Information (page 1).
2. Select your preferred Campaign(s) (page 2).
3. Enter your Donation Amount (page 2).
4. Select your preferred Payment Method (page 3).
5. Choose your Donation Frequency (page 3).
6. Enter your Email and Phone (page 4).
7. Enter your Address (page 4).
8. Add any additional Note to the Donor (page 5).
9. Review and submit your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donor Information (Page 1)
* **Donor Information**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details.

### Campaign (Page 2)
* **Campaign**: Choose the campaign for which you want to make a donation. Selecting more than one campaign allows you to support multiple causes at once.

### Donation Amount (Page 2)
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate. Please be sure to enter a valid number, as this will be used for processing.

### Payment Information (Page 3)
* **Payment Information**: This field is not applicable for this form. Please proceed to the next step.

### Donation Frequency (Page 3)
* **Donation Frequency**: Select how frequently you'd like to make your donation: One Time, Ongoing, Quarterly, or Monthly.

### Payment Method (Page 3)
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal. Be sure to have the necessary information ready for secure processing.

### Email (Page 4)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone (Page 4)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Address (Page 4)
* **Address**: Enter your physical address for delivery or correspondence purposes.

### Note to Donor (Page 5)
* **Note to Donor**: Add any additional comments or details for the donor. This is optional, but helps us better understand your support.

### Additional Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have the necessary payment information available for secure processing.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
