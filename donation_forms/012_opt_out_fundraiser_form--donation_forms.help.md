# opt_out_fundraiser_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to record donations and opt outs for a fundraiser. It allows donors to specify their name, email, and phone number, as well as the purpose of their donation and any additional notes they may have. The form also collects a signature for the donor and may have multiple "Donor" sections for recording multiple donations from the same person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Donor Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Amount" field.
4. Select a purpose for your donation from the options provided (General Fund, Team A, Team B, Team C, or Other (Specify below)).
5. If you selected "Other (Specify below)", provide more information in the "Other (Specify)" field.
6. Sign the form by entering your signature in the "Signature (required)" field.
7. If you are donating for multiple teams, select the teams for which you are donating in the "Team" field.
8. If you are donating as a representative of another person, provide their name, email, and phone number in the "Other Donor" section.
9. Enter any additional notes for your donation in the "Note" field.
10. Review the form to ensure all required fields have been completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Opt Out Fundraiser Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Donor Name**: Your name as the person donating.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
* **Description**: A description field for any additional information you want to provide about your donation.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Donor Name**: Your name as the person donating (if different from above).
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Email**: Your email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: false
* **Amount**: The amount of your donation.
  * Type: number
  * Required: true
* **Purpose**: The purpose of your donation (General Fund, Team A, Team B, Team C, or Other (Specify below)).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
* **Other (Specify)**: If you selected "Other (Specify below)", provide more information about the purpose of your donation.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Signature (required)**: Your signature as the person donating.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
* **Date**: The date of your donation.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
* **Time**: The time of your donation.
  * Type: time
  * Required: false
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Note**: Any additional notes about your donation.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
* **Donor Note**: Any additional notes about the donor.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
* **Donor Signature**: Your signature as the person donating.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
* **Team**: The teams you are donating to (if multiple teams).
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: false
* **Other Donor Name**: The name of the person you are representing (if different from above).
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Other Donor Email**: The email of the person you are representing (if different from above).
  * Type: email
  * Required: false
* **Other Donor Phone Number**: The phone number of the person you are representing (if different from above).
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Other Donor Note**: Any additional notes about the person you are representing.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
* **Other Donor Signature**: The signature of the person you are representing.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Donor Name 2**: The name of the second person donating (if multiple people are donating).
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Donor Email 2**: The email of the second person donating (if different from above).
  * Type: email
  * Required: false
* **Donor Phone Number 2**: The phone number of the second person donating (if different from above).
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Donor Note 2**: Any additional notes about the second person donating.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
* **Donor Signature 2**: The signature of the second person donating.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
