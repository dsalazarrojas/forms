# Peer Counseling Support Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Counseling Support Donation Form is a tool to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in supporting peer counseling services. This form will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your E-mail in the "E-mail" field. Please ensure it is a valid E-mail address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your City in the "City" field.
7. Enter your State in the "State" field.
8. Enter your Zip code in the "Zip" field. Please note that this field is a numerical value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (form_first_name, text, required):** Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (form_last_name, text, required):** Enter your last name in this field.
* **E-mail (form_email, email, required):** Enter your valid E-mail address in this field.
* **Phone (form_phone, text, required):** Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Address (form_address, text, required):** Enter your address in this field.
* **City (form_city, text, required):** Enter your city in this field.
* **State (form_state, text, required):** Enter your state in this field.
* **Zip (form_zip, number, required):** Enter your zip code as a numerical value in this field.
