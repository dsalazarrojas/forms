# pledge_drive_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a pledge drive campaign, which may include donations or pledges from supporters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Ensure that you have entered accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review the form for errors before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Information** (`base_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose or context of the pledge drive.
* **Pledge Amount** (`pledge_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are pledging or donating for the cause.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your pledge or donation.
* **Submit Information** (`submit_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your submission.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have accurate and complete information in each field.
* Review the form for errors before submission.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact your organization's support team.
* If you are unsure about the purpose or relevance of this form, please review with your team before proceeding.
