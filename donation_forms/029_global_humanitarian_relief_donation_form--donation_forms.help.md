# Global Humanitarian Relief Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Humanitarian Relief Donation Form is designed to collect donations for various causes, allowing individuals to contribute to relief efforts globally. This form is used to gather information from donors, including their personal details, donation preferences, and notes. Donations can be made in different frequencies, such as one-time or monthly, and can be allocated towards water, food, shelter, or healthcare causes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your **Email address** in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Specify the **Amount** you wish to donate.
6. Choose the **Frequency** of your donation: **Monthly** or **One-time**.
7. If you select **Monthly**, choose the **Frequency (Frequency)** option for each month you wish to donate.
8. Select the **Cause** towards which you would like to donate: **Water**, **Food**, **Shelter**, or **Healthcare**.
9. Add any **Notes** you would like to include with your donation.
10. Click the **Submit** button to complete your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate in a numerical format.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how frequently you would like to donate: **Monthly** or **One-time**.
* **Frequency (Frequency)** (`frequency_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): If you selected **Monthly**, choose which months you would like to donate in this field.
* **Cause** (`cause`, select_one, optional): Select the **Cause** towards which you would like to donate: **Water**, **Food**, **Shelter**, or **Healthcare**.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your donation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete your donation and submit it for processing.
