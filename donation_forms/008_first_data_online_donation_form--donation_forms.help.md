# First Data Online Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Data Online Donation Form is a tool for users to make a donation to an organization or project. The form collects information about the donor, including their contact details, donation amount, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donations Amount** (`donations_amount`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter the amount you wish to donate. This can be a one-time or recurring donation.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, `email`, required: false): 
Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Donor Message** (`donor_message`, `note`, required: false): 
Add any additional comments or information about your donation.
* **Gift Receipt** (`gift_reciept`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose whether you would like to receive a gift receipt for your donation.
* **Date of Donation** (`donation_date`, `date`, required: false): 
Enter the date of your donation.
* **Time of Donation** (`time_of_donation`, `time`, required: false): 
Enter the time of your donation.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your address where you can be contacted.
* **Frequency of Donation** (`donation_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Choose how often you would like to make a donation. Options include One Time, Monthly, Quarterly, and Annually.
* **Frequency Description** (`frequency_description`, `note`, required: false): 
Add any additional information about your donation frequency.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the tool or project you are supporting with your donation.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose who created this form. 
* **Created By User** (`created_by_user`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the user who created this form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose who updated this form. 
* **Updated By User** (`updated_by_user`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the user who updated this form.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, required: false): 
Enter the date this form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, required: false): 
Enter the date this form was last updated.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required: false): 
Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): 
Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): 
Enter the time this form was submitted.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose the status of your submission. Options include Pending, Approved, and Declined.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the user who submitted this form.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, `note`, required: false): 
Add any additional comments about your submission.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the ID of the form you are submitting.

## Tips

* Make sure to check the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
