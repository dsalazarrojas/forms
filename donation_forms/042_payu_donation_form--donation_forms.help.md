# PayU Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayU Donation Form collects information for donation purposes, helping us keep records and follow up.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out as much information as you can to help us better understand your donation.
- You may fill out all or some of the fields, but please ensure you fill out the fields with the correct type.
- If you need to leave a field blank, please indicate that by entering a value such as "N/A" or "Not Applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This field is for your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This field is for your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Please enter a valid email address that you use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, text, optional): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Date of Donation** (`donation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you made your donation.
* **Time of Donation** (`donation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you made your donation.

## Tips
- Please fill out accurate and correct information for a successful submission.
- For select fields, please select a valid option from the choices provided.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us.
