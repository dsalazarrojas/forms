# Recurring Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or edit a recurring donation setup for automatic donations at specified intervals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your assigned tool from the list of available tools.
2. Enter the amount you want to donate on a regular basis.
3. Choose the frequency of your donations (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
4. If you want to set an end date for your recurring donations, enter it in the "End Date" field.
5. Provide a description of your recurring donation in the "Recurring donation form description" field (optional).
6. Optionally, enter your email address and phone number for further communication.
7. Add any notes about your recurring donation in the "Recurring donation form notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recurring donation** (`recurring_donation`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your recurring donation.
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you want to donate on a regular basis.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you want your recurring donations to start.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose the frequency of your donations (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **End date** (`end_date`, date, optional): If you want to set an end date for your recurring donations, enter it here.
* **Recurring donation form description** (`recurring_donation_form_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of your recurring donation.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool that this recurring donation is associated with.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Recurring donation form notes** (`recurring_donation_form_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about your recurring donation.

Note: The assigned tool is a required field, please make sure to select the correct tool for your recurring donation.
