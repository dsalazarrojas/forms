# Community Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from community members for various funds and causes. Please fill out this form to support your community and make a positive impact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Donor Information" section:
	* Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* Enter your email address where you'd like to receive your receipt.
	* Enter your phone number (optional).
	* Enter your mailing address in the format: Street, City, State, Zip.
2. Select the donation details:
	* Choose the donation amount from the available options or type in your custom amount.
	* Select how often you'd like to donate (One-time, Monthly, or Annually).
3. Choose where your donation should go:
	* Select a designated fund (General, Youth Programs, Senior Services, Community Gardens, or Emergency Relief).
4. Confirm your donation:
	* Choose whether you'd like to keep this donation anonymous or not.
	* If not, confirm that this donation is voluntary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you'd like to receive your receipt.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your address in the format: Street, City, State, Zip.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `select_one`, required): Select a donation amount from the available options or enter your custom amount.
* **Other Amount** (`custom_amount`, `number`, optional): If you selected "Other" for the donation amount, enter the amount here.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you'd like to donate (One-time, Monthly, or Annually).
* **Designated Fund** (`designated_fund`, `select_one`, required): Select a designated fund to receive your donation (General, Youth Programs, Senior Services, Community Gardens, or Emergency Relief).
* **Keep this donation anonymous** (`anonymous_donation`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you'd like to keep this donation anonymous or not.
* **Comments or Special Instructions** (`donor_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any special instructions or comments, enter them here.
* **I confirm this donation is voluntary** (`acknowledgement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that this donation is voluntary.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out to complete the form accurately.
* If you select "Other" for the donation amount, enter the custom amount in the "Other Amount" field.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
