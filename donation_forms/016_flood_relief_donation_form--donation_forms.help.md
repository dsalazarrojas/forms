# flood_relief_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by users to submit donation information for flood relief efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your donor information on page 1.
2. Provide your name on page 2.
3. Enter your email address on page 3.
4. Enter your phone number on page 4.
5. Specify the amount you would like to donate on page 5.
6. Select your preferred donation method on page 6.
7. Indicate your donation frequency on page 7.
8. Enter any additional notes on page 8.
9. If you have a different donation frequency, specify it on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (Page 1): Enter your basic information, including your name and address.
* **Name** (Page 2): Enter your name as it should be displayed on our records.
* **Email** (Page 3): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Page 4): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Amount** (Page 5): Enter the amount you would like to donate to the flood relief efforts.
* **Donation Method** (Page 6): Select how you would like to make your donation (e.g., bank transfer, credit card, etc.).
* **Donation Frequency** (Page 7): Indicate how often you would like to donate (e.g., one-time, monthly, etc.).
	+ **Yes** or **No**: Select whether you would like to make this donation a one-time or recurring donation.
* **Notes** (Page 8): Enter any additional notes or comments you have.
* **Donation Frequency Other** (Page 9): If you have a different donation frequency not listed on the previous page, describe it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to submit the form.
- Use the correct email format (e.g., example@example.com) to enter your email address.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [support email].
