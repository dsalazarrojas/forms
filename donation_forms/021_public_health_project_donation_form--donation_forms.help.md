# Public Health Project Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Health Project Donation Form is designed for individuals to provide support to projects that benefit public health initiatives. This form is used to collect information from donors, including project details, donation amounts, frequency, and payment information. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your donation is processed correctly and reaches its intended purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Project Details** field with a brief description of the project you wish to support.
2. In the **Donation Amount** field, enter the amount you wish to donate.
3. If you wish to donate at a specific **Donation Frequency**, select the options that apply to you.
4. In the **Payment Information** field, provide any relevant details about your payment method.
5. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the provided options.
6. Fill out the **Donor Information** field with any additional details you wish to provide about yourself.
7. Enter your **Address**, including your street address or postal address.
8. Provide the **City** where you reside.
9. Select your **State/Province** or enter if it's not listed.
10. Enter your **Postal Code** (zip code).
11. If you're a resident of a specific **Country**, select it from the list.
12. If applicable, enter your **Date of Birth** for identification purposes.
13. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
14. Select your preferred **Contact Method** (Email or Phone).
15. Finally, enter any additional **Additional Information** you wish to provide about your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: A brief description of the project you wish to support.
* **Project Lead**: The name of the person leading the project (not applicable for public donations).
* **Donation Amount**: The amount you wish to donate. Please ensure this matches your intended donation amount.
* **Donation Frequency**: The frequency at which you wish to donate (Yes or No).
* **Payment Information**: Any relevant details about your payment method.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (Option 1, Option 2, Cash, Cheque, or Other).
* **Donor Information**: Any additional details you wish to provide about yourself.
* **Address**: Your physical street or postal address.
* **City**: The city where you reside.
* **State/Province**: Your state or province of residence.
* **Postal Code**: Your postal or zip code.
* **Country**: The country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Method**: Your preferred contact method (Email or Phone).
* **Additional Information**: Any additional details you wish to provide about your donation.

Please note that the **Project Lead** field is intended for internal use and should not be filled by public donors. The **Donation Amount** field should match your actual donation amount. The **Payment Method** field requires a valid payment option selection to ensure smooth processing.</thinking>
