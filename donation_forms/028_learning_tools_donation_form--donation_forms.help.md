# learning-tools-donation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for donations to support education startups. It is intended for individuals who want to make a donation to support these startups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start.
2. Fill in the required fields for each page.
3. Review and submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Startup** (`education_startup`, `text`, required): Enter the name or a brief description of the education startup you want to support.
* **Nonprofit** (`nonprofit`, `select_one`, optional): Check if the education startup you are supporting is a nonprofit organization.
* **Your Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): If you are making a donation on behalf of someone else, enter their name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates about your donation.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you are making a one-time or recurring donation.
* **Gift Type** (`gift_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you are giving a monetary or in-kind donation (e.g., materials, equipment).
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you are donating. Please use numerical values only (e.g., 100, 200, etc.).
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the frequency of your donation (e.g., one-time, monthly, quarterly, semi-annually).
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, `note`, optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your donation.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact the support team.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
