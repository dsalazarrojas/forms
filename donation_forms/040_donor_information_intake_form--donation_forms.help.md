# Donor Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Information Intake Form is a collection of information about the donor, including contact details, donation preferences, and frequency of giving. This form helps the organization gather essential data to manage donor relationships and tailor communication according to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select your **Address** from the available options.
5. Choose the **Type** of address (Home, Work, or Other).
6. Enter the **Donation Amount** you wish to donate.
7. Select the **Frequency** of your donations (One Time, Ongoing, or Regular).
8. Provide any additional **Frequency Details** in the note field.
9. Select the **Frequency Interval** for your donations (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
10. Choose the **Gift Frequency** and **Donation Frequency** for your gift.
11. Select the **Donation Interval** for your donations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **donor_details**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter your contact information.
  - **name**: Your name as you wish to be known by the organization.
  - **email**: Your email address for communication.
  - **phone**: Your phone number for communication.
  - **address**: Select the address type and enter the address you reside or work at.
- **name**: This field is for your name as it appears on our records.
- **email**: Enter your email address for our organization to contact you.
- **phone**: Your phone number for our organization to contact you.
- **address**: Select the address type and enter the address you reside or work at.
- **address_type**: This field indicates the type of address you selected (Home, Work, or Other).
- **donation_amount**: The amount you wish to donate to our organization.
- **frequency**: This field determines how often you would like to make donations (One Time, Ongoing, or Regular).
- **frequency_details**: You can provide additional details about your donation frequency.
- **frequency_interval**: Select the interval for your donation frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
- **gift_date**: The date you would like to make the gift.
- **gift_frequency**: This field determines the gift frequency (One Time, Ongoing, or Regular).
- **donation_frequency**: This field determines the frequency of your donations (One Time, Ongoing, or Regular).
- **donation_interval**: Select the interval for your donation frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
