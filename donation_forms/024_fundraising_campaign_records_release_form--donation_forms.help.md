# fundraising_campaign_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fundraising_campaign_records_release_form" is designed to record participant information for a fundraising campaign. This form is used to collect details such as participant name, contact information, and campaign participation dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start on the first page. 
2. Enter the participant's name and optional email address.
3. On the second page, enter the participant's phone number, select the permission given and choose the start and end dates of participation.
4. Enter a description of the purpose of the participant's involvement in the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (text, required/optional):
  Enter the participant's name.
* **email** (email, required/optional):
  Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (text, required/optional):
  Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Permission Given** (select one, required/optional):
  Select one of the three options for the permission given.
* **Date of Participation** (date, required/optional):
  Enter the date of participation.
* **Start Date** (date, required/optional):
  Enter the start date of participation.
* **End Date** (date, required/optional):
  Enter the end date of participation.
* **Purpose** (text, required/optional):
  Enter a brief description of the participant's purpose. 

## Tips
Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field as this form is used for official records.
