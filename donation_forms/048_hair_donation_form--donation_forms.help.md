# Hair Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Donation Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to donate their hair for people in need, such as cancer patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal and contact information.
2. Provide details about the hair you want to donate, including its length and weight.
3. Choose your preferred contact method for further communication.
4. Fill out any other required or optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Donation Details** (`Donation Details`, text, required: false): Describe the hair you want to donate, including its condition and any specific requirements.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, email, or other contact method to ensure we can reach you for further communication.
* **User Information 1** (`User Information 1`, text, required: false): This field can be used to provide any additional information about yourself that's not included in the previous field.
* **Donation Type** (`Donation Type`, select multiple, required: false): Choose the type of donation you're making (e.g., Cancer, Alopecia, Other).
* **Hair Length** (`Hair Length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of the hair you want to donate.
* **Hair Weight** (`Hair Weight`, number, required: false): Enter the weight of the hair you want to donate.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select one, required: false): Choose how you want us to contact you (Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required: false): Enter your address for further communication.
* **City** (`City`, text, required: false): Enter the city where you're located.
* **State** (`State`, text, required: false): Enter the state where you're located.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, required: false): Enter your zip code for further communication.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you're submitting this form.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you're submitting this form.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your donation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* We'll use the information you provide to process and facilitate your donation.
