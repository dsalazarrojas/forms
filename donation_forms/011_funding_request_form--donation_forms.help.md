# funding_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to facilitate the funding request process by gathering necessary information from users seeking financial support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number (optional).
3. Enter your address.
4. Specify the funding amount you are requesting.
5. Choose the source of your funding (Donor, Grant, or Other).
6. If necessary, add any additional comments or questions.
7. Review the form before submitting.
