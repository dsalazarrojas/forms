# Donation Distribution Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Distribution Report Form is designed to collect information for a donation distribution report. This form helps to track the flow of donated items, ensuring accurate reporting and monitoring of donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Distribution Date**, the date when the donation was distributed.
2. Enter the **Donation Amount**, the value of the donation.
3. Provide the **Donor Name**, the name of the person or organization making the donation.
4. Select the **Donation Type** from the options: Monetary, In-Kind, or Goods.
5. Choose the **Distribution Status** from the options: Shipped, In Transit, Not Yet Shipped, or Cancelled.
6. Add any additional **Notes** about the donation.
7. Enter the **Donor Email**, the email address of the person or organization making the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Distribution Date**: Enter the date when the donation was distributed.
- **Donation Amount**: Enter the value of the donation.
- **Donor Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization making the donation.
- **Donation Type**: Select the type of donation, which can be Monetary, In-Kind, or Goods.
- **Distribution Status**: Choose the status of the donation, which can be Shipped, In Transit, Not Yet Shipped, or Cancelled.
- **Notes**: Add any additional information about the donation.
- **Donor Email**: Enter the email address of the person or organization making the donation.
