# Digital Literacy Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals making a donation to support the digital literacy program. It will help us process your donation and stay in touch with you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Donor Information section with your details.
2. Choose your preferred payment method (Card, Check, or Bank Transfer).
3. Select the frequency of your donation (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
4. Fill out the Amount Donated field if you wish to specify the amount.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Text).
6. Provide any additional notes if required.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Fill out this section with your name, address, and any other relevant details.
- **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required: `false`): Specify the amount you wish to donate. This field is optional and allows you to choose the amount you're comfortable with.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose how you'd like to pay (Card, Check, or Bank Transfer). Select one or more options as applicable.
- **Frequency of Donation** (`frequency_of_donation`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the frequency at which you'd like to donate (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
- **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is currently not used in the form; it's recommended to leave it empty or ignore it for now.
- **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, `number`, required: `false`): Fill out this field if you wish to specify the amount donated. It is optional and should be the same as the `Donation Amount` field value.
- **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Text).
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: `false`): Provide any additional information or comments you have.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted via phone.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- You can select one or more payment methods if applicable.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
