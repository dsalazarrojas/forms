# Nonprofit Organization Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to make a donation to a nonprofit organization. It is used for administrative purposes to track donor interactions and follow up on donation commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form submission page (page 1).
- Select or enter the contact information of the person making the donation (page 2).
- Enter your name and other relevant details as required or desired (page 3).
- Provide a valid email address and/or phone number for the organization to contact you (page 4 and 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, note, not required): This section serves as a placeholder for the form's introductory page.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select one, not required): Choose one of the pre-existing options provided in the list, such as "John Smith" or "Jane Doe," to select the person making the donation or the person to contact. If you do not see your name, please contact the nonprofit organization directly.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, not required): Enter your name if you are the one making the donation or the individual who will be receiving the donation.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address where the nonprofit organization can contact you for updates and follow-ups.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter a valid phone number for the nonprofit organization to contact you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in processing your donation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the nonprofit organization directly.
- If you are an existing donor or have a pre-existing relationship with the nonprofit, please note this in the relevant field for easier tracking.
