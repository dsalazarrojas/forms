# incubator_support_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the "incubator_support_donation_form" is designed to collect information about donations to support an incubator program. It gathers data from potential donors regarding their interest in participating in the program, their personal information, and the purpose of the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options in the "Donation Program" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Donor Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Specify the amount of the donation in the "Amount" field.
6. Write a brief message in the "Message" field.
7. Confirm your donation by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Donation" field.
8. Optionally, provide additional information in the "Assigned Tool" field.
9. (Optional) Upload a file related to the donation in the "Output File" field.
10. Enter the form ID for recordkeeping purposes in the "Form ID" field.
11. Select a category for the donation (optional) in the "Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Program**: Select "Yes" to indicate your interest in participating in the program, or "No" otherwise.
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name as the person making the donation.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation you are making.
* **Message**: Write a brief message about the purpose of your donation.
* **Confirm Donation**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you are making the donation, or "No" otherwise.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any additional information about the tool or equipment you are donating.
* **Output File**: Optionally, upload a file related to the donation.
* **Form ID**: Enter the ID of the form for recordkeeping purposes.
* **Category**: Optionally, select a category for your donation.
* **Output File**: 
*   This field is not actually part of the form. The correct label should be "Output File" but there is a mistake in the form schema.
