# International Development Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors for international development projects, helping to track and manage donations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in your personal details:
	* `first_name`: Enter your first name as it is.
	* `last_name`: Enter your last name as it is.
	* `email`: Enter a valid email address to contact you.
2. Specify the project details:
	* `project_type`: Select the type of project you are supporting (e.g., Agriculture, Education, Housing, Water, Health, or Other).
	* `project_status`: Indicate the status of the project (In progress, Completed, or Not Started).
3. Confirm your donation:
	* `amount`: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
	* `currency`: Select the currency for your donation.
4. Confirm your donation frequency:
	* `recurring`: Enter how often you want to donate (this field might be best described as "0" for one-time, or "X" for X times, e.g., "1" for one time, "3" for three times, etc.).
5. Add more details about you:
	* `donor_name`: Enter your name as you prefer to be addressed.
	* `donor_organization`: If you represent an organization, enter its name.
	* `donor_title`: Enter your title within the organization (if applicable).
6. Provide contact information:
	* `donor_email`: Enter a valid email address for you or your organization.
	* `phone_number`: Enter your phone number (optional).
7. Add any additional context:
	* `notes`: Enter any additional comments or context about your donation.
8. Send a message:
	* `message`: Enter any additional message or comments.

## Tips

- Ensure accurate and up-to-date information for personal and contact details.
- Be clear in specifying the project details and status.
- Use the correct currency for your donation.
