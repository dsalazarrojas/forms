# Donation Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Agreement Renewal Form is used to update and renew existing donation agreements between donors and the organization. This form ensures that the organization has the necessary information to continue or terminate the agreement as agreed upon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Donor Name" and "Phone" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you have one.
3. Select the frequency at which you wish to make gifts in the "Gift Frequency" field.
4. Choose the frequency at which you wish to make payments in the "Payment Frequency" field.
5. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donation Amount" field.
6. Select the term for which you are renewing the agreement in the "Renewal Term" field.
7. Choose the method of payment for your donation in the "Payment Method" field.
8. If necessary, enter any other gift frequency, payment frequency, donation amount, or renewal term details in the "Other (Gift Frequency)", "Other (Payment Frequency)", "Other (Donation Amount)", and "Other (Renewal Term)" fields.
9. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.
10. Confirm whether you have signed the agreement in the "Signed Agreement" field.
11. Select the type of agreement you are renewing in the "Agreement Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (Required)
	+ This field is for your name as the donor.
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone** (Required)
	+ This field is for your phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Email** (Optional)
	+ This field is for your email address.
	+ If you have an email address, please enter it here.
* **Donor Address** (Required)
	+ This field is for your mailing address.
	+ Please enter your address as it appears on your identification.
* **Gift Frequency**
	+ This field is for the frequency at which you wish to make gifts.
	+ Choose from the options: Quarterly, Semi-annually, Annually.
* **Payment Frequency**
	+ This field is for the frequency at which you wish to make payments.
	+ Choose from the options: Monthly, Quarterly, Annually.
* **Donation Amount** (Required)
	+ This field is for the amount of your donation.
	+ Please enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Renewal Term**
	+ This field is for the term for which you are renewing the agreement.
	+ Enter a number representing the term in years.
* **Payment Method**
	+ This field is for the method of payment for your donation.
	+ Choose from the options: Credit Card, Bank Draft, Bank Transfer.
* **Other (Gift Frequency)**
	+ This field is for any additional gift frequency information.
	+ Please enter any additional details regarding your gift frequency.
* **Other (Payment Frequency)**
	+ This field is for any additional payment frequency information.
	+ Please enter any additional details regarding your payment frequency.
* **Other (Donation Amount)**
	+ This field is for any additional donation amount information.
	+ Please enter any additional details regarding your donation amount.
* **Other (Renewal Term)**
	+ This field is for any additional renewal term information.
	+ Please enter any additional details regarding your renewal term.
* **Additional Comments**
	+ This field is for any additional comments or information.
	+ Please enter any additional details as needed.
* **Signed Agreement**
	+ This field is for confirming whether you have signed the agreement.
	+ Select "Signed" if you have signed the agreement, otherwise select "Not Signed".
* **Agreement Type**
	+ This field is for the type of agreement you are renewing.
	+ Choose from the options: Donor Agreement, Pledge Agreement, Grant Agreement.
