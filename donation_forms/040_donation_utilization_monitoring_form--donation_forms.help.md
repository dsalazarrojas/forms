# Donation Utilization Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and monitor donations, ensuring that all relevant details are accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the donor's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Specify the donor type (e.g., individual, organization, or other).
3. Enter the donation amount and allocation method.
4. Select the impact of the donation.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Specify the date and time of submission.
7. Identify the user who submitted and approved the form.
8. Review and update the form as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (`donation_form_data_1`): Enter the donor's basic details, such as their name and contact information.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_info_2`): Enter the name of the donor.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info_3`): Enter any additional contact information, such as a note or address.
* **Email** (`email_4`): Enter the donor's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number_5`): Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Donor Type** (`donor_type_6`): Select the type of donor (e.g., individual, organization, or other).
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount_7`): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Allocation Method** (`allocation_method_8`): Select how the donation will be allocated (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Impact** (`impact_9`): Select the impact of the donation (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Notes** (`notes_10`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the donation.
* **Date** (`date_11`): Enter the date of the donation.
* **Time** (`time_12`): Enter the time of the donation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by_13`): Identify the user who submitted the form.
* **Created By** (`created_by_14`): Identify the user who created the form.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by_15`): Identify the user who approved the form.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by_16`): Identify the user who reviewed the form.
* **Approved On** (`approved_on_17`): Enter the date when the form was approved.
* **Created On** (`created_on_18`): Enter the date when the form was created.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on_19`): Enter the date when the form was last updated.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by_20`): Identify the user who last updated the form.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at_21`): Enter the time when the form was last updated.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on_22`): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by_23`): Identify the user who submitted the form.
* **Review Status** (`review_status_24`): Select the review status of the form (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments_25`): Enter any review comments about the form.
