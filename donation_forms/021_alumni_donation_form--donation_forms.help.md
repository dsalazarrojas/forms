# Alumni Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Donation Form is designed to collect donations from University of [University Name] alumni, supporting various areas of the institution. This form helps to track donation details and ensures that donors' preferences are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by providing the required information (indicated by an asterisk*).
- Make sure to choose the correct payment method and donation category.
- Fill in any additional details if you wish to be recognized or if you're making a donation in memory or honor of someone.
- Finally, review your completed form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (required): Your full name, as you would like to be recognized.
* **Email Address** (required): Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Your contact number for any follow-up questions.
* **Year of Graduation** (required): When you graduated from University of [University Name].
* **Street Address** (optional): Your current residential address.
* **City** (optional): The city you currently reside in.
* **State/Province** (optional): The state or province where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (optional): Your postal code.
* **Country** (optional): The country where you currently reside.
* **Donation Amount** (required): The amount you would like to donate. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Donation Category** (required): Select from General Scholarship Fund, Athletic Programs, Academic Programs, Building Improvements, Student Services, Undesignated, or Other.
* **Donation in Memory of/Donation in Honor of** (optional): If you'd like to honor someone, enter their name.
* **Type of Donation** (required): Choose from Cash, Check, Securities, Property, Planned Gift, Matching Gift, or Other.
* **Does Your Employer Match Gifts?** (optional): Answer 'Yes', 'No', or 'Unsure' to indicate whether your employer matches gifts.
* **Employer Name** (optional): If your employer matches gifts, enter your company name.
* **Remain Anonymous** (optional): Keep your donation confidential.
* **Recognition Level** (optional): Select from Public Recognition, Listed with Class, Corporate Listing, or No Recognition to determine how you'd like to be recognized.
* **Payment Method** (required): Choose from Online Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, Wire Transfer, Stock Transfer, or Other.
* **Optional Message/Comments** (optional): Any additional comments or message for the school.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (optional): Receive alumni news and updates from the University of [University Name].

Please note that all optional fields are for your personal preference. Make sure to fill out the required fields accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient donation process.
