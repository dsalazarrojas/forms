# Monthly Giving Option Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Giving Option Poll form is designed to collect information from users on their preferences for making donations to our organization. This guide will walk you through each field of the form, helping you understand what each question is asking and how to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your options for each field. If a field is required, please make sure to fill it in as it is essential for our team to understand your preferences correctly. You can choose your preferred option for each field and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Monthly Giving Options
* **Monthly giving options** (`monthly_giving_option`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks you to choose between "Yes" and "No" options, indicating whether you would like to make regular monthly donations. Select the option that best reflects your intention.

### Donation Amount
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, required/optional): This field allows you to specify the amount you would like to donate. Please enter a numerical value, as this will directly impact the amount we receive from you.

### Payment Frequency
* **Payment frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field presents four frequency options: "One time", "Monthly", "Quarterly", and "Annually". Select the option that best represents your preferred donation schedule.

### Donation Frequency
* **Donation frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Here, you can select multiple options from the list to specify how often you would like to make donations. Choose "Once a month" for monthly donations, "Twice a week" for weekly donations, or "Less often" for less frequent donations.

### Contact Method
* **Contact method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): In this field, select your preferred method of contact: "Phone", "Email", "Text", or "Online". This helps us reach out to you efficiently and effectively.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): If you selected "Email" as your contact method, please enter your email address here. This is the address we'll use to communicate with you about your donations.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here. We will use this number to reach out to you about your donations.

### Online Contact
* **Online contact** (`online_contact`, `text`, required/optional): If you selected "Online" as your contact method, please enter your online contact method here. This could be a URL, social media handle, or any other online method you prefer to be contacted through.

Note: This form is meant to gather your preferences for donations, and it is essential to fill it out accurately so that we can provide the best experience for you.
