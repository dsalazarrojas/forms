# Athletes Support Fundraising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletes Support Fundraising Form is designed to support athletes or sports teams with various fundraising efforts. Donors can contribute financially to support travel, equipment, training, and medical expenses. The form is used to collect necessary information from donors for tax-deductible receipts and recognition purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Campaign Name** field with the name of the campaign you are supporting.
2. Enter the **Beneficiary Team or Athlete Name**.
3. Select the **Primary Purpose of Fundraising** from the provided options.
4. Provide your **Donor Full Name**.
5. Enter your **Email Address** for donation receipt purposes.
6. Fill in your **Mailing Address** for tax-deductible receipts.
7. If donating on behalf of a business, enter the **Organization or Company Name**.
8. Select your preferred **Donation Frequency** (one-time, monthly, or quarterly).
9. Choose your preferred **Payment Method**.
10. Decide whether you want to **remain anonymous** or be recognized.
11. If remaining anonymous, you can choose to provide a **Name for Donor Recognition**, which will be used in the annual report.
12. If this donation is in honor or memory of someone, select the appropriate option and provide the **Tribute Name**.
13. You can optionally provide a **Personal Message to the Athlete or Team**.
14. Acknowledge that your donation is non-refundable.
15. Decide whether you want to receive **future updates and newsletters** from us.
16. Select a date for your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign Name**: Enter the name of the campaign you are supporting.
* **Beneficiary Team or Athlete Name**: Enter the name of the athlete or team you are supporting.
* **Primary Purpose of Fundraising**: Select the primary reason for this fundraising effort.
* **Donor Full Name**: Enter your full name as the donor.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for donation receipts.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your physical address for tax-deductible receipts.
* **Organization or Company Name**: If donating on behalf of a business, enter the company name.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select how often you want to make this donation (one-time, monthly, or quarterly).
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation in USD.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose how you want to make your payment (credit/debit card, PayPal, bank transfer, or check by mail).
* **Would you like to remain anonymous?**: Decide whether you want to remain anonymous or be recognized.
* **Name for Donor Recognition**: If choosing to be recognized, enter your name as it should appear in the annual report.
* **Is this donation in honor or memory of someone?**: If this donation is in honor or memory of someone, select the appropriate option.
* **Tribute Name**: If this is a tribute donation, enter the name of the person being honored or remembered.
* **Personal Message to the Athlete or Team**: Optionally provide a message to the athlete or team.
* **I understand that my donation is non-refundable**: Acknowledge that your donation is non-refundable.
* **Consent to receive future campaign updates and newsletters**: Decide whether you want to receive updates and newsletters from us.
* **Date of Donation**: Select the date of your donation.
