# online_donation_transaction_data_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online donation transaction data consent form is used to collect consent from donors for the use of their data in online transactions. This form is essential for ensuring that donors are aware of how their data will be used and to obtain their explicit consent for its use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Donor Name in the "Donor Name" field.
2. Provide your Donor Email in the "Donor Email" field.
3. Enter the Transaction Amount associated with the donation.
4. Choose your preferred options for "Consent" (Allow Data Usage or Do Not Allow Data Usage).
5. Select the communication channels you prefer to be contacted through (phone, email, or post).
6. Choose which data uses you consent to (Donor Profile, Transaction Data, or Donation Amount).
7. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for future communication and data storage.
* **Donor Email**: Enter your email address that we will use to contact you for any future communication related to your donation.
* **Transaction Amount**: Enter the amount of the transaction you are making.
* **Consent**: Choose whether you consent to the use of your data (Allow Data Usage) or not (Do Not Allow Data Usage).
* **Contact Preferences**: Select the communication channels you prefer to be contacted through (phone, email, or post).
* **Data Uses**: Choose which data uses you consent to (Donor Profile, Transaction Data, or Donation Amount).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have regarding your donation or data usage.
