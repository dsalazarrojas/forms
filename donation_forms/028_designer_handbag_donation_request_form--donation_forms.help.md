# Designer Handbag Donation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help designers submit a request for a handbag donation. Please review the form carefully to ensure you complete it correctly and your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Personal Information section with your details.
2. Select the type of handbag you wish to donate.
3. Provide a description of the handbag, including its condition.
4. Select the status of the handbag.
5. Add any notes or additional information about the handbag.
6. Choose your preferred follow-up method and date.
7. Optionally, add a photo of the handbag and upload any relevant files.
8. Review your submission and ensure all fields are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information**
  (`user_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to input your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as it would appear on your donation request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Handbag Information** (`handbag_info`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the handbag you wish to donate.
* **Type** (`handbag_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of handbag you are donating.
* **Image** (`handbag_image`, `text`, optional): Upload a photo of the handbag or provide a URL where it can be found.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the handbag.
* **Condition** (`condition`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the condition of the handbag.
* **Status** (`donation_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your donation request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information about the handbag.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred follow-up method.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Select a date for follow-up contact.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitted_on`, `date`, optional): This field is automatically populated with the date of your submission.
* **Photo** (`photo_url`, `text`, optional): Upload a photo of the handbag or provide a URL where it can be found.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, `text`, optional): Upload any relevant files related to the handbag.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information to help us process your request efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.
* We will follow up with you via your preferred method once your request has been processed.
