# Donor Contact Information Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate contact information for donors, ensuring that the provided data is accurate and complete. It is intended to be used in conjunction with other systems to ensure that donor data is up-to-date and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the donor's first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter the donor's last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter the donor's email address in the proper format in the **Email** field.
4. Enter the donor's physical address in the **Address** field.
5. Enter the donor's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
6. Enter the donor's unique identifier (if applicable) in the **Donar Number** field. **Note:** This field is not required, and if you don't have a unique identifier for the donor, you can leave it blank.
7. Enter the donor's zip code in the **Zip** field.
8. Enter the donor's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, optional): Enter the donor's first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, optional): Enter the donor's last name.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the donor.
* **Address** (4, text, optional): Enter the donor's physical address.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, optional): Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Donar Number** (6, text, optional): Enter the donor's unique identifier (if applicable). **Note:** If you don't have a unique identifier for the donor, you can leave this field blank.
* **Zip** (7, text, optional): Enter the donor's zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (8, date, optional): Enter the donor's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
