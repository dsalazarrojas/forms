# Parent Feedback Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Feedback Contact Form is a tool for parents to provide feedback and suggestions on various aspects of their child's education. It helps school administrators and teachers understand the needs and concerns of the parents and respond to their questions and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of feedback you want to provide: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
2. Enter your contact number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX (e.g., 123-456-7890).
3. Enter your name as you would like it to appear in the response.
4. Choose your time zone from the options provided.
5. Select your preferred date and time for a response.
6. Choose how satisfied you are as a parent with your child's education: Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Dissatisfied.
7. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the text box.
8. Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted by the school).
9. Sign your response with a note (this will be kept confidential and only visible to the school staff).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **parent_feedback_contact_form** (Text, Optional): This is the main text field where you can type your feedback and suggestions.
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **parent_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear in the response.
* **feedback_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of feedback you want to provide: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **comment** (Text, Optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in this text box.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted by the school).
* **time_zone** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your time zone from the options provided.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Select your preferred date for a response.
* **time** (Time, Optional): Select your preferred time for a response.
* **parent_feedback** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how satisfied you are as a parent with your child's education: Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Dissatisfied.
* **parent_signature** (Note, Optional): Sign your response with a note (this will be kept confidential and only visible to the school staff).
