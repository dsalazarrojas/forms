# School Event Parent Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Event Parent Feedback Questionnaire is a survey designed to collect feedback from parents who have attended a school event. This feedback will help the school understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the requested information. Please ensure that you are honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (<name>, text, required/optional): This field is where you should enter your name as a parent. This will help the school associate your feedback with your identity.
* **Event Date** (<event_date>, date, required/optional): Please enter the date of the school event you attended. This will help the school track the specific event you are providing feedback on.
* **School Name** (<school_name>, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school you attended the event at. This will help the school understand which event you are providing feedback on.
* **Event Type** (<event_type>, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of event you attended from the options listed:
	+ School Event
	+ School Field Trip
	+ School Assembly
	+ School Festival
	+ School Camp
	+ Other (Specify)
* **Event Details** (<event_details>, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the event. This will help the school understand more about the specific event and provide more accurate feedback.
* **Parent Contact Method** (<parent_contact_method>, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose how you would prefer to be contacted for any follow-up:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text
	+ In-person
	+ Other (Specify)
* **Feedback** (<feedback>, text, required/optional): Please provide any thoughts or comments you have about the event. This will help the school understand your concerns and suggestions.
* **Suggestions** (<suggestions>, text, required/optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them here. This will help the school make informed decisions for future events.
* **Event Satisfaction** (<event_satisfaction>, number, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (<overall_satisfaction>, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the school on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback and suggestions.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please address them directly in the "Feedback" section.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the school administration.
