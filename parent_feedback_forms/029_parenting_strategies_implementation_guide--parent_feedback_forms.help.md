# Parenting Strategies Implementation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parenting Strategies Implementation Guide is a form designed to help parents track and report their implementation of various strategies in their parenting approach. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, explaining what each field is for and how to complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Open the form**: Start by navigating to the Parenting Strategies Implementation Guide page.
- **Review the form sections**: The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions and fields.
- **Complete each field**: Answer each question and fill in the corresponding field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent ID** (1, number, required/optional): This field is for identifying the parent's unique ID number. Enter the number exactly as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Strategy ID** (2, number, required/optional): This field is for the strategy ID number associated with the strategy you are implementing. If you're unsure, please ask your coach or mentor for clarification.
- **Approach ID** (3, number, required/optional): This field is for the approach ID number related to the approach you are using. Again, if you're unsure, please ask your coach or mentor for guidance.
- **Session ID** (4, number, required/optional): This field is for the session ID number corresponding to the session you attended. If you've attended multiple sessions, please list each one separately.
- **Parent Date** (5, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you implemented the strategy or approach in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Parent Time** (6, time, required/optional): Enter the time when you implemented the strategy or approach in the format HH:MM:SS.
- **Parent Note** (7, note, required/optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments you have about your parenting experience. Be as detailed as possible.
- **Email** (8, email, required/optional): Enter your email address as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Phone Number** (9, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Strategy Notes** (10, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional information or context about the strategy you are implementing.
- **Approach Notes** (11, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional information or context about the approach you are using.
- **Session Notes** (12, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional information or context about the session you attended.
- **Parent Feedback** (13, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts, comments, or observations about your parenting experience.
- **Parent Goal** (14, text, required/optional): Describe your parenting goal or objective.
- **Parent Goal** (15, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional information about your parenting goal.
