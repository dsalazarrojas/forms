# Parent Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow parents to communicate with the institution in a convenient and organized way. It covers various aspects of communication, including sending messages, providing feedback, and expressing concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your Parent Name**: Enter your name as a parent in the "Parent Name" field.
2. **Choose a Communication Type**: Select the type of communication you wish to have with the institution in the "Type" field (Parent, School, or Other).
3. **Select a Communication Method**: Choose the method(s) you prefer to communicate (Email, Phone, Text, or In-person).
4. **Enter your Message**: Type your message in the "message" field.
5. **Add Comments (Optional)**: If you have any additional comments, use the "Comments" field to provide more context.
6. **Submit Your Feedback**: If you have any feedback or suggestions, type them in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Name** (parent_name): Enter your name as a parent.
- **Teacher Name** (teacher_name): Enter the name of the teacher you are addressing.
- **Type** (communication_type): Select one of the following options:
	+ Parent: General updates or concerns.
	+ School: School-wide announcements or events.
	+ Other: Custom or specific requests.
- **Method** (communication_method): Select one or more of the following options to send your message:
	+ Email: Send a message via email.
	+ Phone: Communicate over the phone.
	+ Text: Send a text message.
	+ In-person: Meet in person to discuss.
- **Message** (message): Type a message to the teacher or institution.
- **Comments** (comments): Add any additional comments or context to your message.
- **Feedback** (feedback): Provide feedback or suggestions for improvement.
