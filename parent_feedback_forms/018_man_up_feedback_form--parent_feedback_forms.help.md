# man_up_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides instructions on how to complete the man_up_feedback_form, a form designed to gather feedback from users after events or meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Event Name in the first field.
2. Enter the Event Date in the second field.
3. Enter the Parent Name in the third field.
4. Enter your Feedback in the fourth field.
5. If you have any Suggestions, enter them in the fifth field.
6. If you have any Comments, enter them in the sixth field.
7. Enter your Contact Info in the seventh field.
8. Enter the Event Location in the eighth field.
9. Enter Event Details in the ninth field.
10. Select the Feedback Type from the options provided in the tenth field.
11. If you want to provide Feedback Level, choose the relevant option from the eleventh field.
12. Enter your Feedback Description in the twelfth field. (Optional)
13. Enter your Contact Email in the thirteenth field.
14. Enter the Event Organizer in the fourteenth field.
15. If you want to specify an Event Coordinator, enter their name in the fifteenth field. (Optional)
16. If applicable, enter the number of Attendees in the sixteenth field. (Optional)
17. Enter any Event Notes in the seventeenth field. (Optional)
18. Enter the Feedback Date in the eighteenth field.
19. Enter the Time of the event in the nineteenth field.
20. Enter the Event Start Date in the twentieth field.
21. Enter the Event End Date in the twenty-first field.
22. If applicable, enter the Event Duration in the twenty-second field. (Optional)
23. Select the Event Status from the options provided in the twenty-third field. (Optional)
24. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form in the twenty-fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback for. (Text)
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event. (Date)
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or organizer of the event. (Text)
* **Feedback**: Enter your feedback about the event. (Text)
* **Suggestions**: Enter any suggestions for future events. (Text) (Optional)
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the event. (Text) (Optional)
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information so we can get back to you. (Text)
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event. (Text)
* **Event Details**: Enter any additional details about the event. (Text)
* **Feedback Type**: Select the type of feedback you are providing (Positive, Negative, or Neutral). (Select One)
* **Feedback Level**: Choose the level of feedback you are providing (Low, Medium, High, or Very High). (Select Multiple) (Optional)
* **Feedback Description**: Enter a description of your feedback. (Text) (Optional)
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you. (Email)
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer. (Text)
* **Event Coordinator**: Enter the name of the event coordinator. (Text) (Optional)
* **Attendees**: Enter the number of attendees for the event. (Text) (Optional)
* **Event Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the event. (Text) (Optional)
* **Feedback Date**: Enter the date you are providing feedback. (Date)
* **Time**: Enter the time of the event. (Time)
* **Event Start**: Enter the start date of the event. (Date)
* **Event End**: Enter the end date of the event. (Date)
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event. (Number) (Optional)
* **Event Status**: Select the status of the event (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started). (Select One) (Optional)
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form. (Text)
