# Parent Self Efficacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Self Efficacy Survey is a research tool designed to measure the self-efficacy of parents in various domains. It is a 25-page survey that asks parents to rate their confidence in their abilities to accomplish specific tasks and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each question and provide your honest response.
2. Rate your confidence level for each question using the scale provided.
3. Answer all questions, even if you are unsure or uncomfortable with a particular topic.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the survey.
