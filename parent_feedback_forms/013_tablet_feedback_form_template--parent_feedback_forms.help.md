# Tablet Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from teachers about their experiences with tablets. It will help us understand how teachers use and perceive tablets in their classrooms, allowing us to make informed decisions to improve the tablet-usage program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the first page, you will see a text field for the "Tablet Feedback Form Template" title. This is a required field where you can enter a title for your feedback, if needed.
2. On the second page, you will see two fields: "Teacher Name" and "Teacher Email". These fields are also optional but can help us contact you directly if needed for further information.
3. To complete this form, simply type in the required fields, and click submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tablet Feedback Form Template**: This is the title field for your feedback. Enter a title that describes your feedback and help us understand what you have to say.
* **Teacher Name**: This field is optional but can be useful for us to know who gave us feedback. Enter your name if you feel comfortable sharing it with us.
* **Teacher Email**: This field is also optional but can help us contact you for further information or clarification about your feedback. Enter your email if you feel comfortable sharing it with us.

## Tips
- Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the tablet-usage program.
- If you have any technical issues with the tablets, please let us know so we can fix them promptly.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the program, we would love to hear them!
