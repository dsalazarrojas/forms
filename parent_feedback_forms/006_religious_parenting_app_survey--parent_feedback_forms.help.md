# Religious Parenting App Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Religious Parenting App Survey form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the app. The survey is used to help us understand how the app is being used, what features are working well, and what areas need improvement. Your input will help us make the app more user-friendly and effective for you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on your experience.
3. If a question asks for a specific date or time, use the format MM/DD/YYYY for the date and HH:MM AM/PM for the time.
4. For any additional comments, please provide a brief and concise response.
5. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.
