# Youth Sports Parent Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents on their experience with their child's sports team's communication methods. The information gathered will help the team identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to better meet the parents' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Read each question carefully and provide your honest feedback.
* Select your preferred method of communication for updates about your child's team.
* Rate the quality of our communication and provide suggestions for improvement.
* Answer questions about your preferences for receiving feedback, such as frequency and method.
* Let us know if you're interested in volunteering for your child's team.
