# Parent Reader Favorites Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Parent Reader Favorites Survey" is a questionnaire created to gather information about your child's reading habits, preferences, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2.  Choose the correct option for each select_one or select_multiple questions.
3.  Fill in the text fields with your child's favorite characters, books, authors, series, and other preferences.
4.  Complete all questions in this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What do you want your child to learn to read?** (Text field, optional)
    - This question helps in understanding your expectations and goals for your child's reading skills.
*   **Who is your child's favorite character?** (Select One, required)
    - Choose from: The Cat in the Hat, Alice in Wonderland, The Cat and the Fiddle, The Very Hungry Caterpillar.
    - This question helps in knowing your child's favorite characters from stories.
*   **How often does your child read?** (Select Multiple, required)
    - Choose from: Every day, Several times a week, Rarely.
    - This question helps in understanding how frequently your child reads.
*   **What time of the day does your child read?** (Select One, required)
    - Choose from: Morning, Afternoon, Evening.
    - This question helps in understanding when your child reads.
*   **What time of the day do you read with your child?** (Select One, required)
    - Choose from: Morning, Afternoon, Evening.
    - This question helps in understanding when you read with your child.
*   **What reading preferences do you have for your child?** (Text field, required)
    - Describe your child's reading preferences, such as favorite topics or subjects.
*   **How often do you read with your child?** (Select One, required)
    - Choose from: Daily, Several times a week, Rarely.
    - This question helps in understanding how often you read with your child.
*   **What materials do you use for reading with your child?** (Select Multiple, required)
    - Choose from: Books, Audiobooks, Ebooks, Newspapers.
    - This question helps in understanding what materials you use for reading with your child.
*   **What format do you prefer for reading materials?** (Select Multiple, optional)
    - Choose from: Paperback, Hardcover, Ebook, Audiobook.
    - This question helps in understanding your preferred reading material format.
*   **Where do you read with your child?** (Select Multiple, required)
    - Choose from: Home, School, Public Library, Bookstore, Parks, Online.
    - This question helps in understanding where you read with your child.
*   **What device do you use for reading?** (Select Multiple, required)
    - Choose from: Tablet, Smartphone, Ereader, Computer, Printed Book.
    - This question helps in understanding what device you use for reading.
*   **How often do you read?** (Select One, required)
    - Choose from: Daily, Several times a week, Rarely.
    - This question helps in understanding how often you read.
*   **What's your child's favorite book?** (Text field, optional)
    - Describe your child's favorite book.
*   **Who is your child's favorite author?** (Text field, optional)
    - Describe your child's favorite author.
*   **What's your child's favorite series?** (Text field, optional)
    - Describe your child's favorite series.
*   **What subject area is your child's favorite?** (Text field, optional)
    - Describe your child's favorite subject area.
*   **Are you aware of your child's reading preferences?** (Select One, required)
    - Choose from: Yes, No.
    - This question helps in understanding if you are aware of your child's reading preferences.
*   **How often do you read with your child?** (Select One, required)
    - Choose from: Every day, Several times a week, Rarely.
    - This question helps in understanding how often you read with your child.
*   **What reading habits do you have for your child?** (Text field, optional)
    - Describe your reading habits for your child.
*   **What are your concerns or challenges when reading with your child?** (Text field, optional)
    - Describe any concerns or challenges you face when reading with your child.
*   **Are there any other reading resources you use with your child?** (Text field, optional)
    - Describe any other reading resources you use with your child.
*   **Do you have any other comments about reading with your child?** (Text field, optional)
    - Add any other comments or suggestions about reading with your child.

## Tips
- Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
- If you have any further comments or suggestions, please add them in the "Other Comments" field.
- Thank you for your participation!
