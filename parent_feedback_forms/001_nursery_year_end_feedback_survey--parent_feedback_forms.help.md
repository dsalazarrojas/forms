# Nursery Year End Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from nurseries at the end of the year to help us understand their experience, teaching, and areas of improvement. Your input is crucial in shaping the future of our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email address** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone number** field (optional).
5. Select your **Experience at nursery** from the dropdown menu.
6. Select all that apply for **Teaching and care** from the multiple-choice menu.
7. Describe what we can **improve** in the text field.
8. Add any **Comments** about your experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Your first name is required for identification purposes.
* **Last name**: Your last name is required for identification purposes.
* **Email address**: Your email address is required for communication purposes. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number**: Your phone number is optional but helps us contact you if needed.
* **Experience at nursery**: Select one of the options to describe your experience at the nursery.
* **Teaching and care**: Select all that apply to describe the teaching and care aspects of the nursery.
* **What could we improve**: Describe one or more areas that we could improve at the nursery.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your experience (optional).
