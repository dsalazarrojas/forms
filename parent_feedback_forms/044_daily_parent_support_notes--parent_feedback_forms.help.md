# Daily Parent Support Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Parent Support Notes form is a tool designed to help teachers gather information from parents on a daily basis. It's a quick and easy way to keep track of parent feedback, support needs, and action plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each day you'd like to track parent feedback and support needs.
2. Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as the parent.
3. Provide your child's name and date of birth to associate the feedback with the correct student.
4. Add any additional notes or comments from yourself or your child's teacher.
5. Create an action plan to track progress and follow-up on support needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the parent or caregiver.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name to associate the feedback with the correct student.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth to track progress and identify the student.
* **Teacher Support Notes** (`teacher_support_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from your child's teacher.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, text, optional): Create a plan to track progress and follow-up on support needs.
* **Notes from Parent** (`notes_from_parent`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from yourself.
* **Parent Feedback Form** (`parent_feedback_form`, text, optional): This field is not used; you can skip it.

## Tips
* Use this form daily to keep track of parent feedback and support needs.
* Be concise and clear when providing feedback and comments.
* Create an action plan that is specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
* Review and update the form regularly to track progress.
