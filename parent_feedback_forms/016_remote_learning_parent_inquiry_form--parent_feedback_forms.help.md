# Remote Learning Parent Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents about their remote learning experiences. It will help the administration understand their concerns, feedback, and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by `*`) to ensure your inquiry is properly recorded.
2. Enter your school name.
3. Provide your contact number so we can contact you back.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Provide a brief message with your concerns, feedback, or suggestions.
6. Select a category to categorize your inquiry.
7. Enter a brief subject line for your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number so we can call or text you back.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can reply to your message.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required): Enter a brief message with your concerns, feedback, or suggestions.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select a category to help us understand your inquiry.
	+ Option 1: General
	+ Option 2: Technical
	+ Option 3: Administrative
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief subject line for your message.
