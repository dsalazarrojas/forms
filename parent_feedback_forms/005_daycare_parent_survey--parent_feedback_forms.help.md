# Daycare Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Daycare Parent Survey is designed to gather your honest feedback about your experience with our daycare center. The information you provide will help us improve our services to better meet your needs and the needs of your child.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "What do you think about your child's daycare?" text field.
3. If you have any comments or ratings, use the "Overall Experience" text field to provide a brief summary.
4. If you want to provide a rating (out of 10), use the "How would you rate the daycare?" field.
5. Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a welcome note to explain the purpose of the form and encourage you to provide your feedback.
* **Parent Information** (<parent_info>): Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **What do you think about your child's daycare?** (<feedback>): This is an open-ended question where you can share your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about our daycare services. 
* **How would you rate the daycare?** (<rating_scale>): Rate our services from 1 to 10, where 1 means "very poor" and 10 means "excellent".
* **Overall Experience** (<overall_experience>): Provide a brief summary of your overall experience with our daycare center, including any positive or negative comments you might have.
* **Tips**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them here.
