# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on what young people think and feel about climate policy and environmental protection. It will help identify the needs, concerns, and ideas of young people in relation to climate change.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address (if available).
2. Select your country of residence.
3. Choose your organization or affiliation (NGO, School, Advocacy Group, etc.).
4. Answer the questions honestly and provide detailed responses where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name should be provided to identify you as a participant.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name should be provided to identify you as a participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): If you have an email address, please provide it to allow us to send you updates and follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): If you have a phone number, please provide it to allow us to contact you for further questions.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): This will help us understand where you are coming from and the context of your responses.
* **Organization** (`organization`, select_multiple, required): Please select your affiliation type from the provided options.
* **Awareness About Policy** (`awareness_about_policy`, text, optional): If you have some knowledge about climate policy, please provide a brief description.
* **Ideas About Policy** (`ideas_about_policy`, text, optional): If you have ideas on how to address climate policy, please share them with us.
* **Attitudes Towards Policy** (`attitudes_towards_policy`, text, optional): If you have any attitudes or concerns about climate policy, please share them with us.
* **Support For Policy Ideas** (`support_for_policy_ideas`, text, optional): If you support any policy ideas, please let us know what they are.
* **Support For Policy Action** (`support_for_policy_action`, text, optional): If you support any policy action, please let us know what they are.
* **Suggestions About Climate** (`suggestions_about_climate`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions about climate, please share them with us.
* **Suggestions About Environmental Protection** (`suggestions_about_environmental_protection`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions about environmental protection, please share them with us.
* **Suggestions For Climate Policy** (`suggestions_for_climate_policy`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for climate policy, please share them with us.
* **Climate Solutions** (`climate_solutions`, text, optional): If you have any climate solutions, please let us know what they are.
* **Climate Consequences** (`climate_consequences`, text, optional): If you are aware of any climate consequences, please describe them to us.
* **Climate Emissions** (`climate_emissions`, text, optional): If you are aware of any climate emissions, please describe them to us.
* **Climate Change** (`climate_change`, text, optional): If you are aware of any climate change, please describe it to us.
* **Climate Policy** (`climate_policy`, text, optional): If you have any thoughts on climate policy, please share them with us.
* **Suggestions For Climate Protection** (`suggestions_for_climate_protection`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for climate protection, please share them with us.
* **Climate Policy Awareness** (`climate_policy_awareness`, text, optional): If you are aware of any climate policy awareness, please describe it to us.
* **Suggestions For Climate Action** (`suggestions_for_climate_action`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for climate action, please share them with us.
* **Climate Emissions Reduction** (`climate_emissions_reduction`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for climate emissions reduction, please share them with us.
