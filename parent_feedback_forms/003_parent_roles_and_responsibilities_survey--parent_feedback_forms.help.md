# Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey is designed to collect information about the parent's roles and responsibilities within a household. This survey aims to gather data on the relationships between parents, children, and guardians, as well as their interactions with teachers and the dynamics within the household.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the role of the student in the household.
2. Choose the relationship of the student to the parent/guardian.
3. Indicate how often you interact with the teacher.
4. Specify how many people are in the household.
5. Check if there is a stay-at-home parent in the household.
6. Enter the number of children in the household.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What role does the student play in their family?** (`parent_roles_and_responsibilities_1`, select_one, required/optional)
	+ Choose the correct role of the student from the provided options (Mom, Dad, Grandparent, Other).
* **What is the student's relationship to the parent/guardian?** (`parent_roles_and_responsibilities_2`, select_one, required/optional)
	+ Choose the correct relationship between the student and the parent/guardian (Child, Grandchild, Sibling, Other).
* **How often do you interact with the teacher?** (`parent_roles_and_responsibilities_3`, select_multiple, required/optional)
	+ Select all that apply for how often you interact with the teacher (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
* **How many people are in the household?** (`parent_roles_and_responsibilities_4`, number, required/optional)
	+ Enter the total number of people living in the household.
* **Is there a stay-at-home parent in the household?** (`parent_roles_and_responsibilities_5`, select_one, required/optional)
	+ Check the correct answer for whether there is a stay-at-home parent in the household (True, False).
* **How many children are there in the household?** (`parent_roles_and_responsibilities_6`, number, required/optional)
	+ Enter the number of children living in the household.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* This form is meant to be filled out by parents or guardians, not students themselves.
