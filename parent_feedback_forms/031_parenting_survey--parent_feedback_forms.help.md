# Parenting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parenting Survey is designed to gather information from parents about their family and parenting experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions honestly, and try to provide specific examples or explanations when needed.
2. For each question, take your time and think carefully before providing your answer.
3. If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Questions** (`1`, `text`, required):
	+ This section includes questions that cover general aspects of your family and parenting experiences.
	+ Please provide your thoughts and feelings about your family and parenting style.
* **Parent Feelings** (`2`, `text`, required):
	+ This section is designed to understand how you feel about your family and parenting roles.
	+ Please be honest and provide your personal feelings and emotions.
* **Parent Attitudes** (`3`, `text`, required):
	+ This section aims to understand your attitudes towards your family and parenting.
	+ Please provide your thoughts and opinions about your family relationships.
* **Children Feelings** (`4`, `text`, required):
	+ This section is designed to understand how your children feel about your family and parenting.
	+ Please provide their thoughts and feelings about their relationships with you.
* **Family Dynamics** (`5`, `text`, required):
	+ This section covers the dynamics within your family, including interactions and communication.
	+ Please provide examples or explanations of your family's dynamics.
* **Support Systems** (`6`, `text`, required):
	+ This section is designed to understand the support systems you have in place for your family.
	+ Please list or describe the resources and support you have for your family.
* **Communication Channels** (`7`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about the ways in which you communicate with your family members.
	+ Please provide examples or explanations of your communication channels.
* **Parenting Styles** (`8`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about your parenting style and approach.
	+ Please provide information about how you raise your children.
* **Family Values** (`9`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about the values you hold dear in your family.
	+ Please provide information about your family's values and beliefs.
* **Parent Knowledge** (`10`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about your knowledge and education related to parenting.
	+ Please provide information about your knowledge and skills related to parenting.
* **Parent Skills** (`11`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about your skills and abilities as a parent.
	+ Please provide information about your strengths and abilities related to parenting.
* **Parent Responsiveness** (`12`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about how responsive you are to your children's needs.
	+ Please provide information about your responsiveness to your children.
* **Family Stress** (`13`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about stress within your family.
	+ Please provide information about stressors in your family.
* **Parent Stress** (`14`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about stress you experience as a parent.
	+ Please provide information about stressors you face as a parent.
* **Social Support** (`15`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about social support you receive as a parent.
	+ Please provide information about support from family, friends, or community.
* **Family Participation** (`16`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about family participation in activities and decision-making.
	+ Please provide information about your family's level of participation.
* **Family Culture** (`17`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about the culture and traditions within your family.
	+ Please provide information about your family's cultural values and beliefs.
* **Family Organisation** (`18`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about the organisation and structure within your family.
	+ Please provide information about your family's routines and systems.
* **Parent Organisation** (`19`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is about your personal organisation and time management as a parent.
	+ Please provide information about your organisational skills and strategies.
* **Parent Organisation 2-7** (`20-25`, `text`, optional):
	+ These sections are about additional aspects of your parent organisation and time management.
	+ Please provide information about your personal organisational skills and strategies.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
* You can use the space provided for each question to write your answers.
* If you need to skip a question, please feel free to do so, but be sure to answer all required questions.
* The survey is designed to be completed by one person per family.
