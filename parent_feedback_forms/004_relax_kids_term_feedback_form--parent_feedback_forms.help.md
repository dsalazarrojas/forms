# relax_kids_term_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect feedback from parents about their child's experience with a relaxation program. The goal is to understand how effective the program has been in reducing kids' stress levels.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Feedback title**, a brief description of your feedback.
2. Type your **Session ID**, a unique identifier for the session your child attended.
3. Rate your child's experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
4. Share your **Feedback**, any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
5. Optionally, submit a **Testimonial**, a short statement about your child's experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): A brief description of your feedback.
	+ Please type a short title to help us understand your feedback.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, text, required/optional): A unique identifier for the session your child attended.
	+ Please type the session ID provided by the program.
* **Rate** (`rating`, select_one, required/optional): A score from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
	+ Rate your child's experience from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Additional comments or suggestions you may have.
	+ Please share your thoughts and suggestions to help us improve.
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, text, required/optional): A short statement about your child's experience.
	+ Share a brief testimonial about your child's experience with the program.

## Tips
- Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us understand your experience.
- The fields with a star are required.
- The fields with a star will help us improve the program and provide better service.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
