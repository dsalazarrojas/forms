# Hotel Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their stay at our hotel. This form helps us understand their satisfaction, experience, and any issues they faced during their stay. We appreciate your honest feedback, which will aid us in improving our services and provide better experiences for our future guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields according to your experience and feedback.
2. Fill out the form completely, especially the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Use descriptive answers for text fields, such as "Hotel Stay" and "Additional Comments".
4. Choose the correct options for select fields, such as "Service Quality" and "Category".
5. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Stay** (`hotel_stay`, text, optional): Please describe your stay at the hotel, including your room number and check-in date.
* **Hotel Room** (`hotel_room`, text, optional): Share your experience with your hotel room, including any issues you faced.
* **Hotel Staff** (`hotel_staff`, text, optional): Provide feedback about the hotel staff, including their friendliness and helpfulness.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with a number (1-5).
* **Recommend This Hotel** (`recommendation`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would recommend this hotel to others.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions about your stay.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact (optional).
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, number, optional): Rate the service quality with a number (1-5).
* **Room Cleanliness** (`room_cleanliness`, number, optional): Rate the cleanliness of your room with a number (1-5).
* **Value For Money** (`value_for_money`, number, optional): Rate the value for money with a number (1-5).
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, number, optional): Rate the friendliness of the hotel staff with a number (1-5).
* **Check In - Check Out** (`check_in_check_out`, time, optional): Enter your check-in and check-out dates and times.
* **Hotel Amenities** (`amenities`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which hotel amenities you used during your stay.
* **Food And Beverage** (`food_and_beverage`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the quality of food and beverage services.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose whether your stay was "Good", "Bad", or "Average".
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select which assigned tool you used during your stay.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form (not necessary).
* **Created At** (`created_at`, text, optional): This is a system-generated field and not editable.

Note: The fields marked as "select_multiple" and "select_one" have options listed below them. These options are used to rate or choose from a pre-defined list.
