# HVAC Maintenance Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a structured and organized way to document and track HVAC maintenance services. It allows you to input details about the service, including service type, dates, times, customer information, and technician notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form title. Enter a brief description of the service being provided.
2. Select the type of service being performed from the available options.
3. Enter the date of the service was performed.
4. Record the start and end times of the service.
5. Input the customer's name, email, phone number, and any additional comments.
6. If applicable, select the technician's name and enter their notes about the service.
7. Update the appointment status as needed.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about the service.
9. Finally, include a signature from both the customer and the technician (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **HVAC Maintenance Check In Form**: A brief description of the service being performed.
* **service_type** (Select One): Choose the type of service being performed (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **maintenance_date**: The date the service was performed.
* **start_time**: The start time of the service.
* **end_time**: The end time of the service.
* **customer_name**: The name of the customer.
* **customer_email**: The email address of the customer.
* **customer_phone**: The phone number of the customer.
* **technician**: The name of the technician performing the service.
* **technician_notes**: Notes from the technician about the service.
* **appointment_status**: The current status of the appointment (e.g., 'Active', 'Inactive').
* **comments**: Additional comments about the service.
* **signature**: A signature from the customer (if required).
* **required_signature**: A signature from the technician (if required).
* **form_notes**: Additional notes about the form itself or the service.
