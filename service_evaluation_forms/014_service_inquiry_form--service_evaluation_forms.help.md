# Service Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users regarding their service inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate through the form's pages using the page selectors.
2. Fill in your inquiry details on each page.
3. Ensure you provide accurate and clear information for each field.
4. Review your submissions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Inquiry Form (First page)** (`service_inquiry_form_first_page`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for any initial comments or questions you may have.
* **Service Inquiry Form (Second page)** (`service_inquiry_form_second_page`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Service Inquiry Form** (`service_inquiry_form_third_page`, note, not required):
	+ This field is a space for any additional notes or comments you may have.

## Tips

* Be clear and concise in your responses.
* Ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
