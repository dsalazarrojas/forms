# Work Order Close Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Order Close Out Form is used to capture and document the completion status of a work order, ensuring accurate and standardized information is recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the work order to ensure it is complete and accurate.
2. Fill out the form with the relevant details, including:
	* Work Order Information (Page 1)
	* Contracted Job Information (Page 2)
	* Hours Worked (Page 3)
	* Materials Used (Page 4)
	* Total Cost (Page 5)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Order Information** (`work_order_close_out_form_page`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the work order, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Contracted Job Information** (`contracted_job_information`, text, optional): Enter a description of the contracted job, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the total number of hours worked on the project.
* **Materials Used** (`materials_used`, text, optional): Enter a list of materials used during the project, if applicable.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the project.

**Tips**

* Be sure to review and update work orders only when necessary to avoid unnecessary duplication.
* Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information to ensure accurate project close-out status.
* Use the form to maintain a consistent and accurate record of project close-out status.
