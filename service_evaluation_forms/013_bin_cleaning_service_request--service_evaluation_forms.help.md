# Bin Cleaning Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information required for efficient and effective bin cleaning services. It allows customers to provide their contact details, bin cleaning requirements, and service preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all required fields filled out before submitting.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for service confirmation.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for scheduling.
5. Enter the service address in the "Service Address" field.
6. Enter the city in the "City" field.
7. Enter the ZIP code in the "ZIP Code" field.
8. Describe the type of bins that need cleaning in the "Bin Type" field.
9. Choose the size of the bins that need cleaning in the "Bin Size" field.
10. Specify the number of bins that need cleaning in the "Number of Bins" field.
11. Choose the current condition of the bins in the "Bin Condition" field.
12. Enter the last cleaning date for the bins in the "Last Cleaning Date" field.
13. Specify any special service preferences in the "Service Preferences" field.
14. Choose the type of cleaning service required in the "Service Type" field.
15. Select a preferred service date in the "Preferred Service Date" field.
16. Choose a preferred time window for the service in the "Preferred Time Window" field.
17. Specify the location of the bins on the property in the "Bin Location" field.
18. Enter any special access instructions in the "Access Instructions" field.
19. Select any additional services required in the "Additional Services" field.
20. Specify if a water source is available in the "Water Source Available" field.
21. Check if a pricing estimate is needed in the "Pricing Estimate Needed" field.
22. Enter any special requests or comments in the "Special Requests" field.
23. Agree to the service terms and conditions in the "Service Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Note):** This field is for entering your contact details.
* **First Name:** Enter your first name.
* **Last Name:** Enter your last name.
* **Email Address:** Enter your email address for service confirmation.
* **Phone Number:** Enter your phone number for scheduling.
* **Service Address:** Enter the address where you would like the bins cleaned.
* **City:** Enter the city for the service address.
* **ZIP Code:** Enter the postal code for the service address.
* **Bin Type:** Choose the type of bins that need cleaning (e.g., Trash Bin, Recycling Bin, Compost Bin, etc.).
* **Bin Size:** Choose the size of the bins that need cleaning (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large, Commercial Dumpster, etc.).
* **Number of Bins:** Specify the number of bins that need cleaning.
* **Bin Condition:** Choose the current condition of the bins (e.g., Lightly Soiled, Moderately Dirty, Heavily Soiled, Extremely Dirty, Has Pests or Maggots, etc.).
* **Last Cleaning Date:** Enter the last cleaning date for the bins.
* **Service Preferences:** Specify any special service preferences.
* **Service Type:** Choose the type of cleaning service required (e.g., One-Time Cleaning, Monthly Service, Bi-Monthly Service, Quarterly Service, Annual Deep Clean, etc.).
* **Preferred Service Date:** Select a preferred service date.
* **Preferred Time Window:** Choose a preferred time window for the service (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Any Time).
* **Bin Location:** Specify the location of the bins on the property.
* **Access Instructions:** Enter any special access instructions.
* **Additional Services:** Select any additional services required (e.g., Deodorizing Treatment, Disinfectant Spray, Pest Prevention Treatment, Bin Inspection, Photo Documentation, etc.).
* **Water Source Available:** Specify if a water source is available.
* **Pricing Estimate Needed:** Check if a pricing estimate is needed.
* **Special Requests:** Enter any special requests or comments.
* **Service Agreement:** Agree to the service terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.
- Use clear and concise language in the fields.
- Double-check the selected options for accuracy.
- If you have any doubts, please contact the service provider for clarification.
- This form is for one-time or recurring service requests only. For general inquiries, please contact the service provider directly.
