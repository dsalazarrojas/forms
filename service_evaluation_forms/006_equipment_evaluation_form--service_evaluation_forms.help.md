# Equipment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on equipment performance, type, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on each page, working from left to right.
2. You may skip questions that are not relevant to your feedback.
3. Use the provided fields to provide clear and concise comments and ratings.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Equipment Performance
* **Equipment Performance** (`equipment_performance`, note, required: false): This is a free-form text area where you can describe your overall experience with the equipment. Please provide any comments or observations you have about its performance.
### Page 2: Equipment Type
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of equipment you are evaluating. Please choose one of the options provided.
### Page 3: Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): This is a free-form text area where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the equipment.
### Page 4: Performance Rating 1
* **Performance Rating 1** (`performance_rating_1`, number, required: false): This is a numerical rating field where you can rate the equipment's performance. Please enter a whole number value between 1 and 5.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and truthful feedback to help us improve our equipment.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only; your feedback will help us make informed decisions about equipment maintenance and improvement.
