# Employer Of Record Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from users on their experience with the Employer Of Record (EOR) service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information and details.
2. Be as specific and concrete as possible when providing feedback and ratings.
3. For fields with select options, choose the most accurate and relevant option from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **EOR Service Name (id: 1)**: Enter the name of the EOR service you are evaluating.
* **Overall Rating (id: 2)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the EOR service, using the number scale provided.
* **EOR Provider (id: 3)**: Select all the EOR providers that you have interacted with during the service. This will help us understand who provided the service.
* **Feedback (id: 4)**: Provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the EOR service. This is an opportunity to share any concerns, suggestions, or praise.
* **Contact Name (id: 5)**: Enter the name of the contact person you interacted with during the service.
* **Contact Email (id: 6)**: Enter the email address of the contact person you interacted with during the service.
* **Contact Phone (id: 7)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person you interacted with during the service.
* **Service Date (id: 8)**: Enter the date when you received the service.
* **Service Time (id: 9)**: Enter the time when you received the service.
* **Satisfaction (id: 10)**: Rate your satisfaction with the service, using the number scale provided.
* **EOR Satisfaction (id: 11)**: Rate your satisfaction with the EOR service, using the number scale provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction (id: 12)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the EOR service, using the number scale provided.
* **Recommend (id: 13)**: Choose whether you would recommend the EOR service or not, from the provided options.
* **Comments (id: 14)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the EOR service.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Use specific examples or instances to support your ratings and feedback.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide them in the "Feedback" field.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
