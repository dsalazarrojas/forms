# Service And Hospitality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from customers about their experience with our services. Your input will help us understand your needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "First Page" tab and fill in the required fields: "First Name", "Last Name", and "Contact Info".
2. Select the "Second Page" tab and fill in the required fields: "Email" and "Rating Score".
3. Optional: If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the "Comment" field on the "Second Page" tab.
4. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Your last name.
* **Contact Info** (Text, Required): A valid email or phone number where you can be reached for follow-up.

### Second Page
* **Email** (Email, Required): The email address associated with your account or contact information.
* **Rating Score** (Number, Required): Choose a score between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction with the service.
* **Comment** (Text, Optional): Any additional feedback or comments you'd like to share.

### Tips
* Please fill in all required fields.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Contact Info" field.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for help or clarification.
