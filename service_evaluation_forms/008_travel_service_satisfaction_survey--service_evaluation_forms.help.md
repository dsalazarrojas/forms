## Travel Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback about their experience with a travel service. It helps the service provider to understand customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully and answer them honestly.
*   For the open-ended questions (What was the quality of service and Travel service improvement suggestions), please provide as much detail as possible.
*   For the numerical rating questions (Overall travel service satisfaction, Service rating, and Service rating 2), please select a rating from 1 to 5.
*   For the Service rating 3 question, please select all relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What was the quality of service?** (`travel_service_satisfaction_survey`, text, optional): This question asks you to describe your experience with the service. Please be specific about what happened during your visit.
*   **Overall travel service satisfaction** (`overall_travel_service_satisfaction`, number, optional): This question asks for your overall satisfaction rating of the service on a scale of 1 to 5.
*   **Travel service improvement suggestions** (`travel_service_improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): This question allows you to provide suggestions on how the service can be improved.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is your email address, which helps us to contact you if needed.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your phone number, which helps us to contact you if needed.
*   **Travel service date** (`travel_service_date`, date, optional): This question asks for the date of your visit.
*   **Travel service time** (`travel_service_time`, time, optional): This question asks for the time of your visit.
*   **Service rating** (`service_rating`, number, optional): This question asks for your rating of the service quality.
*   **Service rating 2** (`service_rating_2`, select_one, required): This question asks you to select your satisfaction rating from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5.
*   **Service rating 3** (`service_rating_3`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to select all relevant options that describe your satisfaction with the service.

## Tips

*   Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
*   If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use the space provided at the end of the form.
