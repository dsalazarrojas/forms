# hvac_service_coordination_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to HVAC service coordination surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the service details page and fill out the required fields.
2. Select the type of service being performed on the service type page.
3. Choose the priority level of the service on the priority level page.
4. If applicable, schedule a visit on the schedule visit page.
5. Add any additional service notes on the service notes page.
6. Provide your contact information on the contact name page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Details** (Text, Optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the service being performed. Please provide a brief description of the service.
* **Service Type** (Select One, Required): This field is used to select the type of service being performed. Please choose one of the available options: Air Conditioner, Furnace, or Water Heater.
* **Priority Level** (Select One, Required): This field is used to determine the priority level of the service. Please choose one of the available options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Schedule Visit** (Date, Optional): This field is used to schedule a visit for the service. Please provide a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Service Notes** (Text, Optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the service. Please provide any notes or comments about the service.
* **Contact Name** (Text, Optional): This field is used to capture your contact information. Please provide your name as the point of contact for the service.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the service team for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please take your time to ensure accuracy.
