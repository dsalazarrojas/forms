# Service Termination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Termination Request Form is a document used by service providers to request the termination of a service. It is used to communicate the service provider's decision to terminate a service and provide details on the reasons for termination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Determine if you are eligible to complete this form. This form is intended for service providers, so please ensure you have the necessary authority to submit it.
2. Review the form fields and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
3. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Termination Request Form**: This field is the title of the form and is not a field to be filled in.
* **Service Provider**: Select 'Yes' to confirm that you are a service provider and have the authority to submit this form. Selecting 'No' will result in the form not being processed.
* **Customer**: If customers are affected by the service termination, select 'Yes' to indicate this. This will help the service team understand the impact of the termination on customers.
* **Reason for Termination**: Provide a clear and concise description of the reason for service termination.
* **Service Termination Date**: Enter the date on which you wish to terminate the service. This date should be in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or information regarding the service termination.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for the service team to reach out to you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for the service team to contact you regarding the service termination.
* **Signature**: This field is intended for your electronic signature, confirming that you have submitted this form accurately and authentically.
