# HGCF Official Outside Agency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect evaluations from outside agencies, such as external partners, clients, or referrals, to assess their performance and service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the referral source of the agency from the dropdown menu.
- Enter the name of the agency being evaluated.
- If applicable, select the date and time of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Referral Source (Select Multiple)**: Choose the source of the referral, such as "Client," "Community Partner," or "Referral." This helps in tracking the origin of the agency's engagement.
- **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency being evaluated. Please ensure it is spelled correctly and matches the agency's official name.
- **Evaluation Date**: If the evaluation occurred on a specific date, select it from the calendar. If not, leave this field empty.
- **Evaluation Time**: If the evaluation was conducted at a specific time, select it from the available options. If not, leave this field empty.

## Tips
- Be precise when selecting the referral source to accurately track the agency's origin.
- Double-check the agency name to ensure it matches their official name.
- Only fill in dates and times relevant to the evaluation.
- Review the form before submission to ensure accuracy and consistency.
