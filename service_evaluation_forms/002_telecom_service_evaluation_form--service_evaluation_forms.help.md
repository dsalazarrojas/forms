# Telecom Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telecom Service Evaluation Form is designed to collect customer feedback and ratings for telecom service providers. It helps service providers understand the customers' perception of their service quality, value, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information:
	* customer_name: Enter your full name as a customer of the telecom service provider.
	* email: Enter your email address (if you have one).
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with your telecom service provider:
	* telecom_service_provider: Choose the provider you are currently using (e.g., Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, Sprint).
	* telecom_service_quality: Select the level of quality you have experienced with your service (Poor, Good, Excellent).
	* service_pricing: Rate the pricing of your service (if applicable).
	* overall_satisfaction: Choose your overall satisfaction level with your service (Poor, Satisfactory, Good, Excellent).
	* rating: Rate your experience with 0 to 5 stars.
3. Select multiple options that best fit your experience:
	* customer_features: Choose the features you use with your service (e.g., 1GB, 2-3GB, 4-7GB, 8-11GB, Other).
	* telecom_service_features: Select the features you use with your service (e.g., Voice, Data, Text, Voice + Data, etc.).
4. Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience:
	* customer_feedback: Type any comments or feedback you have about your service provider.
	* telecom_service_comments: Type any additional comments or feedback about your service.
