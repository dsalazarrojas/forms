# Technician Evaluation Form Westmin Area 22 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technician Evaluation Form Westmin Area 22 is designed to gather information from users about their experience with a technician. This form aims to collect feedback on the quality of service provided, rating of the service, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer to the question "Was the service satisfactory?" on the main page.
2. Rate your experience with the technician on the "rating" field.
3. Provide any additional feedback about your experience with the technician in the "feedback" field.
4. Enter the date of the visit in the "date_of_visit" field.
5. Select the time of the visit in the "time_of_visit" field.
6. If you want to leave any notes about the technician, you can add them in the "technician_name" field. (This field is optional.)
7. Enter the email of the technician in the "technician_email" field.
8. Describe the service provided by the technician in the "service_provided" field.
9. List the areas served by the technician in the "areas_served" field.
10. Add any comments about your experience in the "comments" field. (This field is optional.)
11. Rate the score of the technician in the "rating_score" field. (This field is optional.)
12. Select "Yes" if the technician was recommended for training, otherwise select "No" in the "recommended_for_training" field.
13. Enter the assigned tool used by the technician in the "assigned_tool" field.
14. Enter the name of the assigned manager of the technician in the "assigned_manager" field.
15. Select "Yes" if the evaluation is type was satisfactory, otherwise select "No" in the "evaluation_type" field.
16. Select "Yes" if the evaluation frequency is satisfactory, otherwise select "No" in the "evaluation_frequency" field.
17. Enter the evaluation date in the "evaluation_date" field.
18. Enter the evaluation time in the "evaluation_time" field.
19. Rate the technician's level in the "technician_level" field.
20. Enter any comments about Westmin Area 22 in the "westmin_area_22" field.
21. Enter the technician's ID in the "technician_id" field.
22. Enter the form ID in the "form_id" field.
23. Enter the technician's status in the "technician_status" field.
24. Enter any comments about the supervisor in the "supervisor_comment" field.
25. Select "Yes" if the supervisor approval is satisfactory, otherwise select "No" in the "supervisor_approval" field.
