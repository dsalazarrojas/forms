# Service Inquiry Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their service inquiries. It aims to gather essential details such as name, contact information, and service-related data to facilitate effective communication and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
* Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
* Provide your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Select the Service you are inquiring about from the "Service" dropdown menu.
* Enter your Address in the "Address" field, if necessary.
* Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
* Specify the Time you are available for the service in the "Time" field.
* Select the Date you are available for the service in the "Date" field.
* If applicable, choose the Category and Service Type relevant to your inquiry from the "Category" and "Service Type" dropdown menus.
* Lastly, provide your Contact Number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name, which is a required field.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name, which is a required field.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address, which is a required field.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Service (select_one)**: Select the service you are inquiring about from the provided options.
* **Address (text)**: Enter your address if you are inquiring about services that require physical presence.
* **Notes (note)**: Add any additional information or comments about your service inquiry.
* **Time (time)**: Specify the time you are available for the service.
* **Date (date)**: Select the date you are available for the service.
* **Category (select_multiple)**: Choose one or more categories relevant to your inquiry.
* **Service Type (select_multiple)**: Choose one or more service types relevant to your inquiry.
* **Contact Number (number)**: Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
