# General Service Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The General Service Quotation Form is used to gather information about clients for the purpose of creating a quote for services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select a date for the service in the "date" field.
6. Describe the service required in the "service_description" field.
7. Enter the price for the service in the "service_price" field.
8. Select the type of client (e.g., individual, business) in the "client_type" field.
9. Choose the client's address type (e.g., home, office) in the "client_address" field.
10. Provide any additional notes about the service in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name for reference.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name for reference.
- **Email**: Provide a valid email address for contact.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication.
- **Date**: Select a date for the service.
- **Service Description**: Describe the service you require.
- **Service Price**: Enter the price for the service.
- **Client Type**: Select the type of client you are (e.g., individual, business).
- **Client Address**: Choose the type of address (e.g., home, office).
- **Note**: Provide any additional information about the service. **This field is required.**
