# Vendor Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Service Request Form is a tool used to collect information from vendors for service requests. It helps communicate service needs and requirements to service requesters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form with your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose the type of service you need by selecting from the available options.
3. Provide a brief description of the service you need, if applicable.
4. Specify the date and time you need the service to be completed.
5. Enter any additional services you need, if applicable.
6. Choose the priority level of the service, which can be High, Medium, or Low.
7. Track the status of the service by selecting from the available options.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the service.
9. Finally, provide any other relevant information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name to be associated with this service request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address so we can reach out to you about the service.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of service you need from the available options.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, `note`, required: false): If applicable, provide a brief description of the service you need.
* **Service Request Date** (`service_request_date`, `date`, required: false): Specify the date you need the service to be completed.
* **Service Request Time** (`service_request_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you need the service to be completed.
* **Service Requester Name** (`service_requester_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the service requester.
* **Service Requester Organization** (`service_requester_organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter your organization's name.
* **Service Requester Phone** (`service_requester_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as the service requester.
* **Service Requester Email** (`service_requester_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as the service requester.
* **Additional Services Needed** (`additional_services_needed`, `note`, required: false): If applicable, provide any additional services you need.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a priority level for the service, which can be High, Medium, or Low.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Track the status of the service by selecting from the available options.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the service.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the service request.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: false): Specify the date the service request was submitted.
* **Submitted By Organization** (`submitted_by_organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the organization submitting the service request.
* **Service Request ID** (`service_request_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique ID for the service request.
* **Service Requester Note** (`service_requester_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments from the service requester.
* **Service Requester Comment** (`service_requester_comment`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments from the service requester.
* **Service Note** (`service_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the service.
* **Service Requester Organization URL** (`service_requester_organization_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter the URL of the organization submitting the service request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter the tool assigned to the service request.

Note: This guide is designed to provide a clear and concise explanation of each field, ensuring that users understand what information is required and how to fill out the form correctly.
