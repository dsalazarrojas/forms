This form is used to record and report the status of a transformer service. It provides a way to document the service report, categorize the service, and track the transformer's condition. The form can be used by technicians to submit reports on the service performed on a transformer.

# Transformer Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured format for submitting reports on transformer services. It allows service requestors and managers to document the service report, including the condition and status of the transformer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the correct option for "service_category" to indicate whether the service performed was yes or no.
2. Enter the name of the technician who performed the service in the "technician_name" field.
3. Select the "maintenance_activity" option to indicate whether the activity was yes or no.
4. If applicable, enter the maintenance date and time in "maintenance_date" and "maintenance_time" fields respectively.
5. Enter the current status of the transformer in the "transformer_status" field.
6. Enter the name of the service requestor in the "service_requestor" field.
7. Enter the location of the transformer in the "transformer_location" field.
8. Enter the current transformer capacity in the "transformer_capacity" field.
9. Enter the current transformer voltage in the "transformer_voltage" field.
10. Enter any remarks or comments in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **service_report** (1, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering a brief description of the service report.
* **service_category** (2, Select One, Optional):
  This field categorizes the service performed as yes or no.
* **technician_name** (3, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering the name of the technician who performed the service.
* **maintenance_activity** (4, Select Multiple, Optional):
  This field is for selecting the maintenance activities performed.
* **notes** (5, Note, Optional):
  This field is for entering any additional notes or comments.
* **transformer_model** (6, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering the model number of the transformer.
* **maintenance_date** (7, Date, Optional):
  This field is for entering the date of the maintenance.
* **maintenance_time** (8, Time, Optional):
  This field is for entering the time of the maintenance.
* **transformer_status** (9, Select One, Optional):
  This field is for selecting the current status of the transformer as active or inactive.
* **service_requestor** (10, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering the name of the service requestor.
* **transformer_location** (11, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering the location of the transformer.
* **transformer_capacity** (12, Number, Optional):
  This field is for entering the current capacity of the transformer.
* **transformer_voltage** (13, Number, Optional):
  This field is for entering the current voltage of the transformer.
* **amperege** (14, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering any additional information about the transformer's amperage.
* **transformer_phase** (15, Number, Optional):
  This field is for entering the current phase of the transformer.
* **transformer_service_type** (16, Select One, Optional):
  This field is for selecting whether the service performed was yes or no.
* **service_manager** (17, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering the name of the service manager.
* **transformer_condition** (18, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering any comments about the condition of the transformer.
* **maintenance_cost** (19, Number, Optional):
  This field is for entering any costs associated with the maintenance.
* **transformer_rating** (20, Select One, Optional):
  This field is for selecting the rating of the transformer as good or fair.
* **comments** (21, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering any additional comments.
* **remarks** (22, Text, Optional):
  This field is for entering any additional remarks.

Note that some fields may be hidden or removed if they do not apply to the specific transformer being serviced.
