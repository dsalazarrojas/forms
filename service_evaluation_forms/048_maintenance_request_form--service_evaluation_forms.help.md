# Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit maintenance requests for various issues in buildings, such as repairs, upgrades, or replacements. It allows building occupants or facility managers to provide detailed information about the problem and their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a maintenance request, follow these steps:

1. **User Info** (`user_info`, text, not required): Enter your name or identifier to help us identify you.
2. Choose your **Building Info** (`building_info`, select_one, required): Select the building where the issue is located from the list.
3. Enter your **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide a valid phone number or email address so we can reach you to confirm your request.
4. In the **Request Info** (`request_info`, note, not required): You can add any additional details or comments about the maintenance request.
5. Click on **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, required): Choose to submit or save the request as a draft.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (`user_info`): Enter your name or identifier to help us identify you.
- **Building Info** (`building_info`): Select the building where the issue is located from the list.
  Example: If you're in Building A, select "Building A" from the options.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Provide a valid phone number or email address so we can reach you to confirm your request.
- **Request Info** (`request_info`): You can add any additional details or comments about the maintenance request.
  Example: If it's a critical issue, please describe the impact to the building's operations.
- **Submit** (`submit_button`): Choose to submit or save the request as a draft. To submit, select "Submit" from the options, and to save as a draft, select "Save as Draft."
