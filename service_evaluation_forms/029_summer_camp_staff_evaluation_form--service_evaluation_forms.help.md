# Summer Camp Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Summer Camp Staff Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback from campers on their experience with summer camp staff. This feedback will be used to improve staff performance and the overall summer camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Camper Evaluation section with your honest feedback about your experience with the summer camp staff.
2. Enter your Camper Name so that we can reference your feedback.
3. Specify the Date of Evaluation when you last had contact with the staff member you are evaluating.
4. Rate the Performance Rating on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Exceeds Expectations".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Evaluation**: This is the main section where you can provide a written evaluation of your experience with the summer camp staff. Be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Camper Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your registration or identification badge. This is used for reference purposes only.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Please specify the date when you last had contact with the staff member you are evaluating.
* **Performance Rating**: Rate the staff member's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Exceeds Expectations".

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will help us improve the summer camp experience.
* Make sure to specify the specific staff member you are evaluating, as this will help us provide targeted feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please include them in your written evaluation.
* If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
