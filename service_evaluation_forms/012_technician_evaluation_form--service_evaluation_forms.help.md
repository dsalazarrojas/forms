# Technician Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technician Evaluation Form is a tool to evaluate a technician's performance, efficiency, and quality of service during a service call. It helps to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable feedback for the technician and the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the service call and gather all necessary information and documents related to the service call.
2.  For each field, provide a clear and concise answer to the question or statement.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out, indicated by a "required" tag.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Service Feedback (`service_feedback`, text, optional):**
    A space to provide any feedback about the technician's service, such as any issues they may have faced or anything that could be improved.

*   **Technician Performance (`technician_performance`, number, optional):**
    A rating from 1-5 to evaluate the technician's performance during the service call.

*   **Communication Skills (`communication_skills`, text, optional):**
    A space to evaluate how well the technician communicated with you, including their clarity, effectiveness, and professionalism.

*   **Problem Solving Skills (`problem_solving_skills`, text, optional):**
    A space to evaluate how well the technician handled any issues or problems that arose during the service call.

*   **Technician Availability (`technician_availability`, number, optional):**
    A rating from 1-5 to evaluate how responsive and available the technician was during the service call.

*   **Work Quality (`work_quality`, number, optional):**
    A rating from 1-5 to evaluate the overall quality of the work performed by the technician.

*   **Service Quality (`service_quality`, select_one, required):**
    A selection from the options provided to evaluate the overall quality of the service provided by the technician.

*   **Comments (`technician_evaluation_form_comments`, note, optional):**
    A space to provide any additional comments or feedback about the technician's performance.

*   **Additional Information (`technician_form_additional_info`, text, optional):**
    A space to provide any additional information about the service call or the technician's performance.

Note: The ratings for "Technician Performance", "Technician Availability", and "Work Quality" should be based on your overall satisfaction with the service call, and should reflect how well the technician met your expectations.
