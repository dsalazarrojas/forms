# Post Massage Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their massage session. The information collected will help us to improve our services and provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, but feel free to skip any questions that do not apply to your experience.
2. Choose the relevant options for each question based on your experience during the massage session.
3. Be honest and specific when providing feedback, as it will help us to understand your concerns and improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Massage Session
• **Massage Session** (`massage_session`, text, optional): This field is where you can briefly describe the massage session you experienced, including the date and time of your appointment.

### 2. Therapist
• **Therapist** (`therapist`, select_one, optional): If you were treated by a specific therapist, please select 'Yes' to indicate that you experienced a positive interaction with them. If you were not treated by a specific therapist, select 'No'.

### 3. Massage Area Worked On
• **Massage Area Worked On** (`massage_area_worked_on`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas of your body that were worked on during the massage session. 

### 4. Time of Massage Started
• **Time of Massage Started** (`time_massage_started`, time, optional): If applicable, please enter the start time of your massage session.

### 5. Time of Massage Finished
• **Time of Massage Finished** (`time_massage_finished`, time, optional): If applicable, please enter the finish time of your massage session.

### 6. Overall Satisfaction
• **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the massage session, on a scale of 1-5. The options are:
  • 1 - Excellent
  • 2 - Good
  • 3 - Neutral
  • 4 - Bad
  • 5 - Poor

### 7. Area for Improvement
• **Area for Improvement** (`area_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): If you experienced any areas for improvement during your massage session, please select all that apply. The options are:
  • Communication
  • Environment
  • Technique
  • Equipment
  • Other

### 8. Feedback
• **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any comments or suggestions for improvement. Be specific and honest in your feedback, as it will help us to understand your concerns and improve our services.
