# Non Conformance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Conformance Form is a tool used to report incidents where services have not met the expected standards. It is intended for customers to provide feedback and help improve quality of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and confirm that it is relevant to your situation.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the service did not meet your expectations.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident, including any technical support issues.
4. Select "Technical support", "Quality", or "Other" to indicate the type of issue.
5. If applicable, enter the date and time of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Conformance Form** (non_conformance_form_page_1, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field is used to confirm whether the service did not meet your expectations. Select "Yes" if the service did not meet your expectations and "No" otherwise.
* **Submit Feedback** (non_conformance_form_page_2, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of the incident, including any details about technical support issues.
* **Technical support** (non_conformance_form_page_3, text, required: false)
	+ Describe any technical support issues you experienced during the incident.
* **Quality** (non_conformance_form_page_4, select_one, required: true)
	+ Select "Technical support", "Quality", or "Other" to indicate the type of issue. This will help us understand the root cause of the problem.
* **Description** (non_conformance_form_page_5, text, required: false)
	+ Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Date** (non_conformance_form_page_6, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date of the incident, if known.
* **Time** (non_conformance_form_page_7, time, required: false)
	+ Enter the time of the incident, if known.

## Tips
* Be as brief as possible when filling out this form.
* Provide as much detail as you can about the incident.
* If unsure about any of the fields, don't hesitate to contact the relevant team or department for guidance.
