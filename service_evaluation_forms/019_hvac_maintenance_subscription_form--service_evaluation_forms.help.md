# hvac_maintenance_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hvac_maintenance_subscription_form is used to gather information from customers who want to subscribe to a maintenance plan for their HVAC system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Info" section with your name and contact information.
2. Choose your service plan (Yes or No) in the "Service Plan" section.
3. Select your payment terms (Yes or No) in the "Payment Terms" section.
4. Enter your preferred maintenance schedule in the "Maintenance Schedule" section using a date.
5. Choose your preferred technician (Yes or No) in the "Technician" section.
6. Add any additional notes about your HVAC system in the "Notes" section.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Service Plan** (`Service Plan`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you want to subscribe to a maintenance plan for your HVAC system. This will help us know if you want to continue with regular check-ups and maintenance.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are able to pay for the maintenance plan on time. This will help us know your payment schedule.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`Maintenance Schedule`, date, required: false): Choose a date for your preferred maintenance schedule. Please use a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Technician** (`Technician`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you want to work with a specific technician. If you don't choose this option, we will assign a technician to you.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional information about your HVAC system, such as any issues you have been experiencing.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
