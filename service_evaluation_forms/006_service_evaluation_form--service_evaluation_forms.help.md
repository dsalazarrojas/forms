# Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from users or participants about a particular service, such as a course or training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the fields as follows:

1. **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or as you would like to be addressed.
2. **Company**: Enter your company name or institution where you took the service.
3. **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course or service you evaluated.
4. **Telephone**: Enter your telephone number for contact purposes.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and confirmation.
6. **Facilitator Name**: Enter the name of the person or instructor who facilitated the service.
7. **Knowledge of subject matter**: Provide your thoughts on the level of knowledge you gained from the service.
8. **Suggestions for improvement**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have for improving the service.
9. **Would you recommend this course to your colleagues?**: Select one of the provided options to indicate your level of recommendation:
	* Option 1: Yes, without reservation.
	* Option 2: Yes, with some reservations.
	* Option 3: No.
10. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the service.
11. **Date**: Enter the date you completed the service (if applicable).
12. **Time**: Enter the time you completed the service (if applicable).
13. **Email Confirmation**: Enter your email address for confirmation and follow-up communication (if desired).
