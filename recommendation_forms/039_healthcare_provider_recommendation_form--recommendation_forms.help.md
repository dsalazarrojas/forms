# healthcare_provider_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare provider recommendation form is used to collect information from healthcare providers for clinical trials, research, or other medical studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the healthcare provider's name in the **Healthcare Provider Name** field.
3. Select whether the healthcare provider has a **Specialty**. If they do, choose "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose the **Department**(s) that the healthcare provider works in. You can select multiple options.
5. Enter the healthcare provider's **Email** address, as this is a required field for contact purposes.
6. Optionally, enter the healthcare provider's **Address** and **Phone** for additional contact information.
7. Add any **Notes** you'd like to share about the healthcare provider.
8. Enter the date and time of your **Last Visit** with the healthcare provider.
9. Finally, select the **Assigned Tool** that the healthcare provider has access to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient associated with this healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Specialty**: Check "Yes" if the healthcare provider has a specialty, "No" otherwise.
* **Department**: Choose the department where the healthcare provider works.
* **Medical License**: You do not need to fill in this field, but if you have information about the healthcare provider's license, enter it here.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the healthcare provider's office or location.
* **Email**: Enter the healthcare provider's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter the healthcare provider's phone number for additional contact information.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or remarks about the healthcare provider.
* **Date of Last Visit**: Enter the date of your last visit with the healthcare provider.
* **Time of Last Visit**: Enter the time of your last visit with the healthcare provider.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that the healthcare provider has access to.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- Complete this form only once for each healthcare provider.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your administrator or contact for help.
