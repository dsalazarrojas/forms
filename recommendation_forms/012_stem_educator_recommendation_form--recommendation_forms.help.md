# stem educator recommendation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stem Educator Recommendation form is used to collect information from educators to recommend students or mentees for a particular opportunity or program. This form is used to provide a personal endorsement of the student's skills and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To start, ensure that you are recommending a student or mentee for whom you have a relationship.
2.  Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
3.  Enter the student's ID (if applicable) in the **Student ID** field.
4.  Enter the student's email address in the **Student Email** field.
5.  Enter your name in the **Recommender Name** field.
6.  Enter your job title or position in the **Recommender Title** field.
7.  Enter the name of your institution in the **Recommender Institution** field.
8.  Enter your department or team in the **Recommender Department** field.
9.  Enter your contact information in the **Recommender Contact Info** field.
10. Sign your name in the **Recommender Signature** field.
11. Describe your relationship with the student in the **Recommender Relationship** field.
11. Specify how many years you have known the student in the **Years Known** field.
12. Enter a brief comment or recommendation for the student in the **Recommender Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's ID (if applicable).
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Recommender Name** (`recommender_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
*   **Recommender Title** (`recommender_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title or position.
*   **Recommender Institution** (`recommender_institution`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your institution.
*   **Recommender Department** (`recommender_department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department or team.
*   **Recommender Contact Info** (`recommender_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information.
*   **Recommender Signature** (`recommender_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign your name.
*   **Recommender Relationship** (`recommender_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with the student.
*   **Years Known** (`recommender_years_known`, text, required/optional): Specify how many years you have known the student.
*   **Recommender Comment** (`recommender_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief comment or recommendation for the student.
