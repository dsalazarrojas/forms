# Science Educator Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect recommendations from science educators who have had a close relationship with the candidate or employee.

## How To Complete This Form
* Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Ensure you fill in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your internal team members for clarification.
* Check if a similar form has been filled out for the same candidate before submitting this one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and job title, if applicable.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the reference or person who is being recommended.
* **Reference Title** (`reference_title`, select_one, required): Select the title of the reference, such as Dean/Principal, Teacher, or Director.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the institution or organization where the reference works.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the department or team where the reference works.
* **Reference Position** (`reference_position`, text, required/optional): Enter the position or role of the reference.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the institution.
* **Reference Address** (`reference_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the reference.
* **Reference Phone** (`reference_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the reference.
* **Relationship** (`reference_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select the relationship between the reference and the candidate, such as colleague, student, or supervisor.
* **How long have you known the applicant?** (`reference_since`, date, required/optional): Enter the length of time you have known the candidate.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, note, required/optional): Enter a personal recommendation or endorsement for the candidate.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature as the reference.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Name of Signer** (`name_of_signer`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and title as the reference.

Note: This field-by-field explanation is designed to guide you through the form and ensure that you are providing accurate and relevant information. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
