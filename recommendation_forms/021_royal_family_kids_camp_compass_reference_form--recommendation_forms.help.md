# royal_family_kids_camp_compass_reference_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide important information about a child's relationship with their referee or camp leader at the Royal Family's Kids Camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name and the child's name.
2. Choose the correct relationship from the list of options.
3. Select the frequency of interaction with the referee or camp leader.
4. Provide the names of any other references or contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camp Leader** (`camp_leader`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the child's camp leader.
* **Name of Referee** (`name_of_referee`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the referee or adult who will be working with your child.
* **Name of Player** (`name_of_player`, `text`, required: false): This field is not explicitly used in the form, however, it seems to be intended to gather information about the child. If this is not a required field, please remove it or provide a clear explanation about its purpose.
* **Referee Relation** (`referee_relation`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how your child is related to the referee or camp leader (Parent, Coach, Relative, Friend, Other).
* **How Long** (`how_long`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of time that your child has known the referee or camp leader. Please enter a numeric value representing the number of days, months, or years. 
* **How Often** (`how_often`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how often your child interacts with the referee or camp leader (Daily, Several times a week, Several times a month, Rarely).
* **Name of Reference 1** (`name_of_reference_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of another reference or contact for your child.
* **Name of Reference 2** (`name_of_reference_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of another reference or contact for your child.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information about your child's relationship with the referee or camp leader.
* Make sure to fill out this form for each child individually.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the camp administration.
* This form should be submitted before the start of the camp session.
