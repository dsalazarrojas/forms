# Honor Society Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Honor Society Recommendation Form is used to gather information from recommenders about a student's qualifications, abilities, and character. This information is essential for evaluating a student's eligibility for the Honor Society program. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that the student's application is considered fairly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the student whose recommendation you are providing.
2. Enter the student's name exactly as it appears on their application.
3. Rate the student's grade level (if applicable).
4. Enter the student's school name.
5. Provide your name and title (e.g., teacher, mentor, or counselor).
6. Choose your relationship with the student (e.g., Teacher, Mentor, or Counselor).
7. Indicate how long you have known the student.
8. Enter a brief essay describing the student's qualifications, abilities, and character.
9. Select the type of recommendation you are providing (e.g., High Recommendation, Conditional Recommendation, or Not Recommended).
10. Provide any additional comments about the student's application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name exactly as it appears on their application.
* **Student Grade**: If applicable, rate the student's grade level (e.g., 9-12, 11-12, etc.).
* **Student School**: Enter the student's school name.
* **Recommender Name**: Enter your name and title (e.g., teacher, mentor, or counselor).
* **Recommender Relationship**: Choose your relationship with the student (e.g., Teacher, Mentor, or Counselor).
* **Recommender Years Known**: Indicate how long you have known the student.
* **Recommender Essay**: Enter a brief essay describing the student's qualifications, abilities, and character. Please provide specific examples and anecdotes about the student's accomplishments and strengths.
* **Recommender Submission**: Select the type of recommendation you are providing (e.g., High Recommendation, Conditional Recommendation, or Not Recommended).
* **Recommender Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the student's application that will help the selection committee make an informed decision.
