# Postdoctoral Fellowship Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Postdoctoral Fellowship Recommendation Form is used to gather information from recommenders (e.g. supervisors, colleagues, or mentors) about a candidate's qualifications, achievements, and fit for a postdoctoral fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the candidate's name in the "Candidate Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the candidate's institution in the "Candidate Institution" field.
3. Enter your name and affiliation in the "Recommender Name" field.
4. Enter your title and position in the "Recommender Position" and "Recommender Position Title" fields.
5. Select the evaluation period for which you are making the recommendation from the "Evaluation Period" field.
6. Check "Yes" or "No" in the "Recommend to" field to indicate whether or not you recommend the candidate.
7. Enter your email and phone number in the "Recommender Email" and "Recommender Phone" fields.
8. Enter your department and institution in the "Recommender Department" and "Recommender Institution" fields.
9. Enter your other affiliations or positions in the "Recommender Other Affiliation" and "Recommender Other Position" fields.
10. Enter a brief evaluation comment in the "Evaluation Comment" field.
11. Enter a recommendation letter (if applicable) in the "Recommendation Letter" field.
12. Enter your signature in the "Recommender Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name (1)**: Enter the name of the candidate for whom you are providing a recommendation.
* **Candidate Institution (2)**: Enter the name of the institution where the candidate is currently or has been affiliated with.
* **Recommender Name (3)**: Enter your name as the recommender.
* **Recommender Affiliation (4)**: Enter your affiliation with the institution or organization.
* **Recommender Position (5)**: Enter your position or title.
* **Recommender Department (6)**: Enter the department where you are based.
* **Recommender Institution (7)**: Enter the name of the institution where you are based.
* **Recommender Title (8)**: Enter your title or position within the institution.
* **Evaluation Date (9)**: Select the date when you started evaluating the candidate.
* **Recommender Email (10)**: Enter your email address.
* **Recommender Phone (11)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Evaluation Score (12)**: Provide a numerical score out of 5 to assess the candidate's qualifications and fit.
* **Evaluation Comment (13)**: Enter a brief comment about the candidate's strengths and weaknesses.
* **Recommend to (14)**: Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you recommend the candidate.
* **Recommendation Letter (15)**: Attach a letter of recommendation (if applicable).
* **Recommender Signature (16)**: Enter your signature.
* **Evaluation Period (17)**: Select the period of evaluation for which you are making the recommendation (e.g. Postdoctoral, Tenure-track, Both).
* **Evaluation Basis (18)**: Enter the basis on which you are evaluating the candidate (optional).
* **Recommender Affiliation Title (19)**: Enter your title within the institution or organization.
* **Recommender Institutional Affiliation (20)**: Enter the department or division within the institution where you are based.
* **Recommender Other Affiliation (21)**: Enter any other affiliations or positions you hold.
* **Recommender Relationship (22)**: Describe your relationship with the candidate.
* **Recommender Position Title (23)**: Enter your position or title within the institution or organization.
* **Recommender Institutional Position (24)**: Enter the department or division within the institution where you are based.
* **Recommender Other Position (25)**: Enter any other positions or responsibilities you hold.

## Tips

* Please make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Only recommend candidates you have had the opportunity to evaluate and know well.
* If you are unsure about a field, please contact the evaluation committee for guidance.
