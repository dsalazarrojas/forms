# Referral Program Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Program Form is designed to collect data on client and referral information for internal use within your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Client Name** field with the name of the client you are referring.
2.  Enter the **Email** of the client.
3.  Enter the **Number** of the client.
4.  If applicable, enter the **Referral Name**, **Referral Email**, and **Referral Number** for the person making the referral.
5.  Provide any additional **Referral Comments** about the referral in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client you are referring.
- **Email** (`client_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the client.
- **Number** (`client_number`, text, required): Enter the number of the client.
- **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, text, optional): If you're reporting on a referral made by someone, enter their name here.
- **Referral Email** (`referral_email`, text, optional): If you're reporting on a referral made by someone, enter their email here.
- **Referral Number** (`referral_number`, text, optional): If you're reporting on a referral made by someone, enter their number here.
- **Referral Comments** (`referral_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the referral.

## Tips
- Fill in all required fields accurately and honestly.
- Make sure you are reporting on a real referral.
- If unsure, reach out to your supervisor or colleague before filling out the form.
