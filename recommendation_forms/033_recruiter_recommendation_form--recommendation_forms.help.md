# Recruiter Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a candidate from a recommender, who is usually someone who knows the candidate well, such as a manager or colleague. This information will be used to help make informed decisions about the candidate's fit for a job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you are completing the form as a recommender, someone who knows the candidate well.
2. Fill out the basic information about the candidate, including their first and last name, job title, and years known.
3. Provide details about your own information, including your first and last name, title, and company.
4. Answer questions about the job the candidate is being considered for, including the job title, job duties, and job category.
5. Provide your honest rating of the candidate, as well as any additional comments you may have about their qualifications and fit for the job.
6. If you have any additional comments or information that you think may be relevant, please include them in the comments section.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the candidate's information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback about the candidate's qualifications and fit for the job.
* Please be specific and detailed in your answers to ensure that the candidate's application is thoroughly evaluated.
* Please do not duplicate or copy and paste information from other sources.
* Please do not use jargon or technical terms that may be unfamiliar to others.
* Please proofread your answers for grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors.
* Please be respectful and professional in your feedback.
* Please do not use confidential or sensitive information about the candidate without their consent.
* Please do not share this form with anyone outside of the authorized personnel.
