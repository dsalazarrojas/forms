# University Research Program Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to refer colleagues, research collaborators, or students to the university research program for their research projects. This form will help us track and manage referrals for the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, such as the faculty member's and department's details.
2. Provide a brief research summary and title.
3. Select the research status and category.
4. Enter the research location, budget, and funding agency details.
5. Add any additional research collaborators.
6. Enter your own details, including your name, email, phone number, affiliation, department, and title.
7. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Referral Form**: This is the main form where you can refer a colleague, collaborator, or student to the research program. You can enter a brief description of the project.
* **Faculty Name**: Enter the name of the faculty member or researcher you are referring.
* **Faculty Title**: Enter the title of the faculty member or researcher.
* **Faculty Institution**: Enter the institution of the faculty member or researcher.
* **Faculty Department**: Enter the department of the faculty member or researcher.
* **Faculty Email**: Enter the email of the faculty member or researcher.
* **Faculty Phone**: Enter the phone number of the faculty member or researcher.
* **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department where the research will take place.
* **Department Institution**: Enter the institution where the research will take place.
* **Department Department**: Enter the department where the research will take place.
* **Department Email**: Enter the email of the department where the research will take place.
* **Department Phone**: Enter the phone number of the department where the research will take place.
* **Research Title**: Enter a brief title of the research project.
* **Research Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the research project.
* **Research Start Date**: Enter the start date of the research project.
* **Research End Date**: Enter the end date of the research project.
* **Research Status**: Select the status of the research project, either active or inactive.
* **Research Category**: Select the category of the research project, either yes or no.
* **Research Location**: Enter the location of the research project.
* **Research Budget**: Enter the budget allocated for the research project.
* **Research Funding Agency**: Enter the agency providing funding for the research project.
* **Research Funding Amount**: Enter the amount of funding allocated for the research project.
* **Research Funding Notes**: Add any additional notes about the funding.
* **Research Collaborators**: Select whether there are any research collaborators for the project.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email.
* **Submitter Affiliation**: Enter your affiliation.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Title**: Enter your title.
* **Submitter Notes**: Add any additional notes about yourself.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Ensure that you have permission from the faculty member or researcher before referring them to the program.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact us at [support email].

Note: The form is designed to be used internally for reference and record-keeping purposes, so please be careful with the information you enter.
