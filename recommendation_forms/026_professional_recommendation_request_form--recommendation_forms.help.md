# professional_recommendation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a professional recommendation from a recommender, typically an academic or professional contact. The purpose of this form is to gather information needed to request a recommendation from the recommender.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details, including your name, role, email, job title, institution, department, and relationship.
2. Enter the recommender's details, including their name, position, institution, department, and email.
3. Provide a signature or a statement of recommendation for the recommender.
4. Submit the form once all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed in the recommendation.
* **Role**: Select your current or desired role (e.g., Student, Job Seeker).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Institution**: Enter the name of the institution you are affiliated with or wish to be affiliated with.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department or area of study.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the recommender (e.g., supervisor, colleague, classmate).
* **Recommender Name**: Enter the name of the recommender.
* **Recommender Position**: Enter the title or position of the recommender.
* **Recommender Institution**: Enter the name of the institution the recommender is affiliated with.
* **Recommender Department**: Enter the name of the recommender's department or area of expertise.
* **Recommender Email**: Enter the recommender's email address.
* **Recommender Signature**: Enter a statement of recommendation from the recommender or a signature on their letterhead.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
