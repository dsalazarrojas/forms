# Cinque Terre Hotel Recommendation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your trip to Cinque Terre, including your personal details, travel dates, group size, and preferences, to recommend a suitable hotel for your stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name.
2. Provide your email address where you can be reached for communication.
3. Enter your phone number (optional but recommended for faster communication).
4. Select your country of origin.
5. Choose the arrival and departure dates for your trip to Cinque Terre.
6. Select the number of people traveling with you.
7. Specify if you're traveling with family, friends, or solo.
8. If applicable, enter the number and age of children traveling with you.
9. Choose the village in Cinque Terre you would like to stay in (optional).
10. Select the type of hotel you prefer (luxury, budget, etc.).
11. Choose the room type that suits your needs (single, double, suite, etc.).
12. Select your budget per night.
13. Choose any must-have amenities (e.g., sea view, pool, etc.).
14. Specify your primary interests (e.g., beach, hiking, etc.).
15. If necessary, indicate any mobility or dietary restrictions you have.
16. If necessary, provide any additional information or preferences not listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Email Address**: We'll use this email to send you hotel recommendations and any further communication. Please ensure it's valid and active.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number will help us speed up our communication if needed. You can leave it optional if you prefer.
* **Country of Origin**: Select your country of origin to help us understand your travel context.
* **Arrival Date in Cinque Terre**: Enter the date you plan to arrive in Cinque Terre.
* **Departure Date from Cinque Terre**: Enter the date you plan to leave Cinque Terre.
* **Total Number of Travelers**: Enter the total number of people traveling with you.
* **Traveling With**: Select how you're traveling (family, friends, solo, etc.).
* **Number of Children (if applicable)**: Enter the number of children traveling with you, if applicable.
* **Ages of Children**: If you've selected 'family' or 'with children', specify the ages of the children.
* **Preferred Village in Cinque Terre**: Choose one or more villages in Cinque Terre where you'd like to stay (if applicable).
* **Preferred Hotel Type**: Select the type of hotel that suits your needs (luxury, budget, etc.).
* **Room Type Preference**: Choose the room type that suits your needs (single, double, etc.).
* **Budget per Night (EUR)**: Enter your budget per night for the hotel.
* **Must-Have Amenities**: Select all amenities that are essential to you (sea view, pool, etc.).
* **Primary Travel Interests**: Select all interests that best describe your trip (beach, hiking, etc.).
* **Accessibility or Mobility Requirements**: Indicate if you have any mobility or accessibility needs.
* **Describe Accessibility Needs**: If you've selected 'yes', please describe your needs (wheelchair access, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences**: Specify any dietary restrictions or preferences you have (vegetarian, vegan, etc.).
* **Any Other Preferences or Special Requests**: If you have any additional preferences or requests not listed above, please specify.

Remember to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure we have a clear understanding of your needs. Thank you for helping us provide the best possible recommendations for your stay in Cinque Terre!
