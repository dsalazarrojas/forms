# Theater Director Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
Theater Director Recommendation Form is used to collect recommendations for candidates applying for a theater director position. The form should be completed by someone who knows the candidate, such as a colleague, teacher, or mentor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are recommending a candidate for a theater director position.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Recommendation
3. Provide additional information about yourself, if desired:
	* First Name
	* Job Title
	* Institution
	* Department
	* Recommender Name
	* Recommender Position
	* Recommender Institution
	* Recommender Email
	* Recommender Phone
	* Recommender Relationship
	* Years Known
	* Additional Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the candidate's first name (optional, but recommended for personal touch).
* **Last Name**: Enter the candidate's last name (required, as it's a unique identifier).
* **Email**: Enter the candidate's email address (required for contact purposes).
* **Job Title**: Enter a brief description of the candidate's job title (optional, for context).
* **Institution**: Enter the institution where the candidate works or studies (optional, for context).
* **Department**: Enter the department or section where the candidate works or studies (optional, for context).
* **Recommender Name**: Enter your name (optional, for contact purposes).
* **Recommender Position**: Enter your job title or position (optional, for context).
* **Recommender Institution**: Enter the institution where you work or study (optional, for context).
* **Recommender Email**: Enter your email address (optional, for contact purposes).
* **Recommender Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional, for contact purposes).
* **Recommender Relationship**: Briefly describe your relationship with the candidate (e.g., colleague, teacher, mentor).
* **Years Known**: Enter the number of years you've known the candidate (optional, for context).
* **Recommendation**: Write a personal recommendation for the candidate, stating their strengths, qualities, and achievements (required).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information or context that supports your recommendation (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure your recommendation is specific, sincere, and free of clichés.
* Use proper grammar and spelling when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the candidate or ask for clarification.
* If you're recommending multiple candidates, create separate forms for each.
