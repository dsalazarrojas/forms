# Restaurant Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and recommendations from customers about their dining experience at a restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name or email address so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions.
2. Rate your experience at the restaurant using the scale provided.
3. Describe your experience in the comment field.
4. Enter your phone number so we can call you to discuss your feedback.
5. Click "Submit" to send your feedback to the restaurant owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Recommendation Form**: This is the title of the form, it's here to help you identify the form and its purpose. You can't leave this field blank.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience at the restaurant on a scale of 1-5. This will help us understand your level of satisfaction.
* **Comment**: Describe your experience in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand what you liked and disliked about your visit.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number so we can call you to discuss your feedback.
