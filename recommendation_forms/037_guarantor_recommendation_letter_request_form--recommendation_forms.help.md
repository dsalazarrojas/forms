# guarantor_recommendation_letter_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The guarantor recommendation letter request form is designed to collect information from a guarantor, typically a professional or personal reference, about their relationship with the applicant and their assessment of the applicant's abilities. This form is intended to provide a written statement of endorsement or support from the guarantor, which can be useful in various contexts, such as loan applications, employment verification, or academic recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields to ensure that your submission is complete and accurate.
2. Provide detailed and specific information about the applicant, including their contact details and your relationship with them.
3. Specify the requirements for the letter, including any specific information that the guarantor needs to include or avoid.
4. Include any follow-up details, such as how you plan to contact the applicant or send the letter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Details
* **Applicant Details** (`applicants_details`, `text`, required): Please enter the applicant's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.

### Guarantor Details
* **Guarantor Details** (`guarantor_details`, `text`, required): Please enter your name, job title (if applicable), and any other relevant information about yourself as the guarantor.

### Guarantor Contact
* **Guarantor Contact** (`guarantor_contact`, `text`, required): Enter your email and/or phone number so that we can contact you to follow up.

### Letter Requirements
* **Letter Requirements** (`letter_requirements`, `text`, required): Please specify any specific requirements or requests that the letter should include, such as dates, events, or specific information to be covered.

### Follow-up Details
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about how you plan to follow up with the applicant or send the letter, such as email or postal mail.

### Submission
* **Submission** (no label, `note`, required): Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete and accurate.

## Tips
* Be clear and specific in your answers, as the letter will be used to support the applicant's application.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals to act as a guarantor.
