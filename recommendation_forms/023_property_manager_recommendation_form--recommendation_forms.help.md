# Property Manager Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Manager Recommendation Form is designed to gather information about a property and its manager. This form is used to provide a comprehensive overview of the property, including its type, address, and manager's contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property manager's name in the `property_manager_name` field.
2. Select the type of property from the `property_type` dropdown menu.
3. Provide the property address in the `property_address` field.
4. Enter the property manager's contact email in the `contact_email` field.
5. Enter the property manager's phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
6. (Optional) Enter the property manager's date of birth and the time of contact in the respective fields.
7. Add any additional notes about the property in the `note` field.
8. Provide any additional property details in the `property_details` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_manager_name** (text): Enter the name of the property manager.
* **property_type** (select_one): Select the type of property (e.g., Rental, Owner Occupied, etc.).
* **property_address** (text): Enter the address of the property.
* **contact_email** (email): Enter the contact email of the property manager.
* **contact_phone** (text): Enter the contact phone number of the property manager.
* **date_of_birth** (date): (Optional) Enter the date of birth of the property manager.
* **time_of_contact** (time): (Optional) Enter the time of contact with the property manager.
* **note** (note): Add any additional notes about the property.
* **property_details** (text): Provide any additional information about the property.
