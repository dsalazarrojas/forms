# Immigration Officer Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immigration Officer Recommendation Form is used to capture information from immigration officers and legal advisors about an individual's immigration status. This form is essential for accurate and efficient processing of the immigration case.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Name**: Enter the name of the individual about whom the recommendation is being made.
2.  **Contact Information**: Enter any relevant contact information for the immigration officer.
3.  **Officer Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience the officer has.
4.  **Officer Position**: Select the officer's position.
5.  **Officer Department**: Select the department the officer belongs to.
6.  **Officer Date First Assigned**: Enter the date the officer was first assigned.
7.  **Officer Date Last Assigned**: Enter the date the officer was last assigned.
8.  **Officer Signature**: Sign the form with your official signature.
9.  **Legal Advisor**: Select whether the legal advisor has recommended the individual.
10.  **Legal Advisor Department**: Select the department the legal advisor belongs to.
11.  **Legal Advisor Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience the legal advisor has.
12.  **Legal Advisor Signature**: Sign the form with your official signature.
13.  **Other Officer Recommendations**: Select whether there are other officer recommendations.
14.  **Other Legal Advisor Recommendations**: Select whether there are other legal advisor recommendations.
15.  **Immigration Officer Comment**: Enter any additional comments from the immigration officer.
16.  **Legal Advisor Comment**: Enter any additional comments from the legal advisor.
17.  **Officer Agreed**: Enter the hours agreed by the officer.
18.  **Legal Advisor Agreed**: Enter the hours agreed by the legal advisor.
19.  **Officer Date**: Enter the date of the officer's signature.
20.  **Legal Advisor Date**: Enter the date of the legal advisor's signature.
21.  **Officer Hours**: Enter the total hours worked by the officer.
22.  **Legal Hours**: Enter the total hours worked by the legal advisor.
23.  **Other Hours**: Enter the total hours for other recommendations (if applicable).
24.  **Officer Agreed Hours**: Enter the agreed hours for the officer.
25.  **Legal Advisor Agreed Hours**: Enter the agreed hours for the legal advisor.
