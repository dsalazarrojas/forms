# Neighborhood Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neighborhood Recommendation Form is used to collect suggestions and feedback from citizens regarding neighborhood-related matters. It helps to gather valuable input from the community, which can be used to inform decisions and improve services in the area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (if you have one).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (if applicable).
5. Write your message in the "Message" field.
6. Select the category that best describes your suggestion (e.g., "Community", "Local Government", "Business", or "Other") in the "Category" field.
7. Provide additional details about your suggestion in the "Details" field.
8. Choose any relevant neighborhoods that your suggestion pertains to (e.g., "Downtown" or "Uptown") in the "Neighborhood" field.
9. Specify when you would like to be contacted (in the past or in the future) using the "Contact" field.
10. If desired, select a follow-up date for your suggestion in the "Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it to allow us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Write a brief description of your suggestion.
* **Category** (`suggestion_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your suggestion (e.g., "Community", "Local Government", "Business", or "Other").
* **Details** (`suggestion_detail`, text, optional): Provide more details about your suggestion.
* **Neighborhood** (`neighborhood`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any relevant neighborhoods your suggestion pertains to (e.g., "Downtown" or "Uptown").
* **Contact** (`contact`, time, optional): Specify when you would like to be contacted (in the past or in the future).
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Select a date for follow-up on your suggestion.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your suggestions are specific, actionable, and relevant to the neighborhood's needs.
* Duplicate submissions may be deleted to maintain data quality.
* Providing complete and accurate information will help us to better address your issues.
