# job_placement_specialist_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide a job placement specialist's recommendation for a job candidate. It's meant to provide additional context and insight into the candidate's background and qualifications.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the job title field with the specific job that the candidate is applying for.
2. Enter the candidate's first name in the candidate first name field.
3. Enter the candidate's last name in the candidate last name field.
4. Enter the job start date and job end date if applicable.
5. Provide a description of the job in the job description field.
6. Select the job status (Active, Inactive, or Pending) to reflect the current status of the job.
7. Choose the job type (Entry Level, Mid-Level, Senior, or Intern) that best describes the job.
8. Select the type of recommendation you are providing (Academic, Professional, Reference, or Other).
9. Enter the name of the contact person providing the recommendation.
10. Enter the contact email and phone number of the contact person.
11. Finally, provide a brief description of your relationship with the candidate and how you can speak to their abilities and characteristics in the recommendation field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the specific job title that the candidate is applying for.
* **Candidate First Name** (`candidate_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the candidate's first name.
* **Candidate Last Name** (`candidate_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the candidate's last name.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the job, if applicable.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the job, if applicable.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Provide a description of the job that the candidate is applying for.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the job status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the job type (Entry Level, Mid-Level, Senior, or Intern) that best describes the job.
* **Recommendation Type** (`recommendation_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of recommendation you are providing (Academic, Professional, Reference, or Other).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person providing the recommendation.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your relationship with the candidate and how you can speak to their abilities and characteristics.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are providing a brief but detailed description of your relationship with the candidate and how you can speak to their abilities and characteristics.
* Be as specific as possible and avoid providing generic or unhelpful responses.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the hiring team for clarification.
