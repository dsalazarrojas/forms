# Academic Recognition Award Nomination Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for nominating a faculty member, staff, or student for an academic recognition award. It helps to identify and acknowledge outstanding individuals who have made significant contributions to the institution. The nomination process is used to recognize and reward those who have demonstrated exceptional service, leadership, or other qualities that have positively impacted the institution or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are authorized to submit this nomination.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
3. Provide a clear and concise explanation for each field, especially for the nomination justification and achievements summary.
4. Attach supporting documents if required or relevant.
5. Review and submit your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Academic Recognition Award Nomination** (Award Nomination Intro, page 1): A brief introduction to the nomination, explaining why you are submitting this nomination.
* **Nominator Name** (Nominator Name, field 2): Your full name as the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (Nominator Email, field 3): A valid email address where you can be contacted for any additional information.
* **Nominator Institution** (Nominator Institution, field 4): The name of the institution or organization you are affiliated with.
* **Nominee Name** (Nominee Name, field 5): The full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Institution** (Nominee Institution, field 6): The name of the institution or organization the nominee is affiliated with.
* **Nominee Position** (Nominee Position, field 7): The current position or role of the nominee.
* **Award Category** (Award Category, field 8): Select one or more of the award categories listed (e.g. Outstanding Teaching, Research Excellence, etc.).
* **Nomination Justification** (Nomination Justification, field 9): A clear and concise explanation of why the nominee is deserving of the award.
* **Summary of Achievements** (Achievements Summary, field 10): A brief summary of the nominee's key achievements.
* **Impact Description** (Impact Description, field 11): Describe the impact of the nominee's work on the institution or community.
* **Years in Field** (Years in Field, field 12): The number of years the nominee has been active in their field.
* **Previous Recognitions** (Previous Recognitions, field 13): List any previous recognitions or awards received by the nominee.
* **Supporting Documents Attached** (Supporting Documents Attached, field 14): Attach any supporting documents that are relevant to the nomination.
* **Relationship to Nominee** (Relationship to Nominee, field 15): Select how you know the nominee (e.g. colleague, supervisor, student, etc.).
* **Additional Endorsements** (Additional Endorsements, field 16): List any additional endorsements or supporting statements from others.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, field 17): Any other information that supports this nomination.

Please note that all fields marked as `required` must be filled in order to submit the nomination.
