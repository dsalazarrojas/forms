# Volunteer Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Recommendation Form is designed to collect information about an individual's recommendation of a volunteer. This form helps us to gather details about the person being recommended, their relationship with the volunteer, and the reasons for the recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

- **Read each field carefully** to ensure you have the necessary information ready.
- **Fill out the form completely** to provide as much detail as possible.
- **Save your progress** if you need to come back later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Recommendation Form (id: 1)**
  **Title**: (no label) (text, required/optional: false)
  This field is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled with the title "Volunteer Recommendation Form". You do not need to fill this out.
* **Recommendee (id: 2)**
  **Recommendee** (label: recommendee, type: select_one, required: false)
  Please select "Yes" if you are recommending this volunteer, and "No" if you are not. This will help us understand if you have a personal connection with the volunteer.
* **Recommender (id: 3)**
  **Recommender** (label: recommender, type: text, required: false)
  Please enter your name or title as the recommender. This could be your name, a supervisor, or someone who knows you well.
* **Date of Recommendation (id: 4)**
  **Date of Recommendation** (label: date_of_recommendation, type: date, required: false)
  Please enter the date you first met or started working with the volunteer.
* **Recommender Organization (id: 5)**
  **Recommender Organization** (label: recommender_organization, type: text, required: false)
  Enter the name of your organization or institution where you are recommending the volunteer from.
* **Recommended Organization (id: 6)**
  **Recommended Organization** (label: recommendee_organization, type: text, required: false)
  Enter the name of the organization or institution the volunteer is associated with.
* **Recommender Position (id: 7)**
  **Recommender Position** (label: recommender_position, type: text, required: false)
  Enter your position or role within your organization or institution.
* **Date of First Contact (id: 8)**
  **Date of First Contact** (label: date_of_first_contact, type: date, required: false)
  Please enter the date you first made contact with the volunteer.
* **Recommender Length of Time (id: 9)**
  **Recommender Length of Time** (label: recommender_length_of_time, type: text, required: false)
  Please enter the length of time you have known the volunteer.
* **Recommender Reason for Recommendation (id: 10)**
  **Reason for Recommendation** (label: recommender_reason_for_recommendation, type: text, required: false)
  Enter the reasons why you are recommending the volunteer.
* **Recommended Length of Time (id: 11)**
  **Recommended Length of Time** (label: recommendee_length_of_time, type: text, required: false)
  Enter the length of time the volunteer has been active in their role.
* **Recommended Reason for Recommendation (id: 12)**
  **Reason for Recommendation** (label: recommendee_reason_for_recommendation, type: text, required: false)
  Enter the reasons why you are recommending the volunteer.
* **Recommender Observations (id: 13)**
  **Recommender Observations** (label: recommender_observations, type: text, required: false)
  Enter any additional comments or observations about the volunteer.

## Tips

- **Be honest** and accurate when filling out this form.
- **Use complete sentences** and paragraphs to provide clear explanations.
- **Proofread** your work before submitting.
