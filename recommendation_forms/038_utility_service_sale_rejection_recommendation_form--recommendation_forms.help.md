# Utility Service Sale Rejection Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users when a utility service sale is rejected. It is designed to be completed by the user to provide a clear explanation of the rejection and any subsequent actions that should be taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Rejection Rationale**: Enter a brief description of the reason why the sale was rejected.
2. **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps that should be taken to resolve the issue or improve future sales.
3. **Sales Leader Review**: Select the sales leader that was involved in the rejection decision.
4. **Quality Team Review**: (Optional) Select one or more members of the quality team that were involved in the rejection decision.
5. **Internal Recommendation**: Provide a detailed description of any internal actions or changes that should be taken as a result of the sale rejection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Rejection Rationale
* **Rejection Rationale**: Enter a brief description of the reason why the sale was rejected. This field is required and should be completed in a few sentences or a short paragraph.

### Next Steps
* **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps that should be taken to resolve the issue or improve future sales. This field is required and should be completed in a few sentences or a short paragraph.

### Sales Leader Review
* **Sales Leader Review**: Select the sales leader that was involved in the rejection decision. This field is required and should be completed by choosing one of the following options:
	+ Sales Leader 1
	+ Sales Leader 2

### Quality Team Review
* **Quality Team Review**: (Optional) Select one or more members of the quality team that were involved in the rejection decision. This field is optional and should be completed by choosing one or more of the following options:
	+ Quality Team 1
	+ Quality Team 2
	+ Quality Team 3

### Internal Recommendation
* **Internal Recommendation**: Provide a detailed description of any internal actions or changes that should be taken as a result of the sale rejection. This field is required and should be completed in a detailed description.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be clear and concise in your explanations to ensure that the next steps and internal recommendations are well understood.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all necessary information is included.
* If you are unsure of what to enter, please contact the form administrator.
