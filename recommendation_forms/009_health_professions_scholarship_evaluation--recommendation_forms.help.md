# health_professions_scholarship_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health professions scholarship evaluation form is designed to gather information about a candidate's strengths, areas for improvement, and other relevant details. It helps reviewers evaluate the candidate's potential and provide feedback to the candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Read the introduction carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of the evaluation.
2.  Enter the applicant's information in the "Applicant Info" field.
3.  Evaluate the applicant's review score and strengths in the "Review Score" and "Strengths" fields.
4.  Identify areas for improvement in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
5.  Provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
6.  Enter the date of review in the "Date Reviewed" field.
7.  Enter your reviewer information in the "Reviewer Info" field.
8.  Sign the form in the "Signature" field.
9.  Enter your name in the "Reviewer Name" field.
10. Enter your title in the "Reviewer Title" field.
11. Enter your email in the "Reviewer Email" field.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Reviewer Phone" field.
13. Sign the form in the "Reviewer Signature" field.
14. Confirm that the review is complete in the "Review Complete" field.
15. Enter the date of submission in the "Date Submitted" field.
16. Select the areas of improvement in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Applicant Info**: Enter the applicant's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Review Score**: Evaluate the applicant's overall score out of 100.
* **Strengths**: Identify the applicant's strengths, such as excellent communication skills or problem-solving ability.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas where the applicant can improve, such as time management or teamwork skills.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the applicant.
* **Date Reviewed**: Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Reviewer Info**: Enter your name, title, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your identity.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title**: Enter your title or position.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Reviewer Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your identity as the reviewer.
* **Review Complete**: Confirm that the review is complete.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Comments**: Select areas where the applicant can improve, such as strengths, weaknesses, or other areas.
* **Review Complete**: Confirm that the review is complete.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.

Note: The fields marked as required in the YAML schema are marked with a star (*) in the help guide to indicate that they are mandatory fields.
