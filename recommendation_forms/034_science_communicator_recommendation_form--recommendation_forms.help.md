# Science Communicator Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Science Communicator Recommendation Form is designed to collect recommendations for science communicators who are doing an excellent job in their field. This form is intended for internal use within the organization to gather information about science communicators and identify outstanding individuals for recognition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the "Form 1 Label" field and enter a brief description of the science communicator you are recommending. This field should provide a clear and concise overview of the individual's work.
2. In "Form 2", select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu that best describe the science communicator's area of expertise. This field is intended to help categorize the recommended science communicator.
3. Enter the date you encountered or learned about the science communicator's work in "Form 3" (Date Label). While this field is not currently used, it's possible that it will be utilized in the future.
4. If available, enter the science communicator's email address in "Email Label" (science_communicator_email). This will help the organization's team contact them for further information.
5. Provide the science communicator's phone number, if available, in "Phone Label" (science_communicator_phone).
6. Select one option from "Select One Label" (Form 8) that best describes the science communicator's work or expertise.
7. In "Form 9" to "Form 13" (Form 9 Label to Form 13 Label), enter any additional information or comments you'd like to provide about the science communicator. These fields are optional and can be used to provide more context or details about the science communicator's work.
8. Finally, in "Form 14" (Form 14 Label), enter any additional comments or notes about the science communicator. This field is also optional and can be used to provide any further information that may be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1 Label (science_communicator_recommendation_form_1)** (`text`, `required: false`): A brief description of the science communicator's work.
* **Form 2 (science_communicator_form_2)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select one or multiple options that describe the science communicator's area of expertise.
* **Date Label (science_communicator_form_3)** (`date`, `required: false`): Enter the date you encountered or learned about the science communicator's work. (Note: This field is not currently used.)
* **Phone Label (science_communicator_phone)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the science communicator's phone number, if available.
* **Email Label (science_communicator_email)** (`email`, `required: false`): Enter the science communicator's email address, if available.
* **Select One Label (science_communicator_form_8)** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Select one option that best describes the science communicator's work or expertise.
* **Form 9 Label (science_communicator_form_9)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional information or comments about the science communicator.
* **Form 10 Label (science_communicator_form_10)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional information or comments about the science communicator.
* **Form 11 Label (science_communicator_form_11)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional information or comments about the science communicator.
* **Form 12 Label (science_communicator_form_12)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional information or comments about the science communicator.
* **Form 13 Label (science_communicator_form_13)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional information or comments about the science communicator.
* **Form 14 Label (science_communicator_form_14)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the science communicator.
