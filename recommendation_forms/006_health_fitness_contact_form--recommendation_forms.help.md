# Health & Fitness Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details from users who have health and fitness concerns or goals. The information you provide will help our team understand your needs and offer personalized guidance and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Category**: Choose "Yes" if you are seeking guidance on health and fitness.
	* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your health and fitness concern or goal.
	* **Name**: Enter your name.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Date of Birth (optional)**: Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
	* **Problematic Area**: Specify the area where you need help or guidance.
	* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your form submission (if required by your healthcare provider).
	* **Submitted**: Choose "Yes" if you are ready to submit your form.
2. If applicable, provide additional information:
	* **Advisor**: If you are seeking guidance from an advisor, choose "Yes" and provide any relevant notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you are seeking health and fitness guidance.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your health and fitness concern or goal.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (optional)**: Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Problematic Area**: Specify the area where you need help or guidance.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your form submission (if required by your healthcare provider).
* **Advisor (optional)**: If you are seeking guidance from an advisor, choose "Yes" and provide any relevant notes.
* **Advisor Notes (optional)**: If you have any additional information for the advisor, provide it here.
* **Submitted (Final Step)**: Choose "Yes" to indicate that you are ready to submit your form.
