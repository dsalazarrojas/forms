# expert_accelerator_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "expert_accelerator_contact_form" is a form designed for customers or partners to contact the organization's experts. It allows them to submit their contact information and express their interest in specific services offered by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, or mail).
3. Select the services you are interested in.
4. Provide a brief message with any additional information about your request.
5. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
* **Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Method** (select one): Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or mail).
*   **Email** (email): Enter your email address. If you are not sure about the format, it is typically the address of your email account on the format "username@domain".
*   **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number (including the country code if necessary).
*   **Message** (text): Enter a brief message or any additional information about your request.

### Services Interested In
*   **Services Interested In** (select multiple): Select one or more services you are interested in from the provided options. These are services that our organization offers.

### Program Information
*   **Program Information** (text): Enter any additional information about the services you are interested in or any other relevant details.
