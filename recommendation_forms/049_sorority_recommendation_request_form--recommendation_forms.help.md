# sorority_recommendation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from nominators for a sorority recommendation. The information collected will be used to support a nomination for a specific individual.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have a legitimate connection to the person you are nominating.
2. Complete each section of the form thoroughly and accurately.
3. Be sure to include all required and optional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person being nominated.

* **Contact Information** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter any relevant contact information for you or the nominee, such as email or phone number. This will help the sorority contact you or the nominee if needed.

* **Relationship** (id: 3, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with the person being nominated.

* **Have you known your nominee for how long?** (id: 4, text, required: false): Enter the length of time you have known your nominee.

* **Why did you nominate this individual?** (id: 5, text, required: false): Explain why you are nominating this person and what makes them suitable for the sorority.

* **Sorority Name** (id: 6, text, required: false): If you are familiar with the sorority's name or chapter, please enter it here, otherwise, leave blank.

* **Email** (id: 7, email, required: false): Enter your email address or the email address of the person being nominated.

* **Phone** (id: 8, text, required: false): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person being nominated.
