# submit_your_recommendation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `submit_your_recommendation` form is a collection tool for users to provide their thoughts, ideas, or suggestions on a particular subject.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the `submit_your_recommendation` form.
2. Fill in the required fields: Name, Email, Type of Recommendation, and accept Terms.
3. Provide a Description of your recommendation, and upload any additional files required.
4. Confirm that you have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
5. Click "Submit" to finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (`name`):** Enter your name for identification and contact purposes.
* **Email (`email`):** Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you about your submission.
* **Contact Number (`contact_number`):** If you wish to be contacted via phone, provide your contact number.
* **Type of Recommendation (`type_of_recommendation`):** Select one of the options provided to categorize your submission.
* **Description (`description`):** Write a brief description of your recommendation.
* **File 1 (`file_upload_1`), File 2 (`file_upload_2`), and File 3 (`file_upload_3`):** Upload any necessary files to support your submission.
* **accept_terms (`accept_terms`):** Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **accept_terms_policy (`accept_terms_policy`):** Select the relevant policy related to your submission, which may include either the privacy policy or terms and conditions.
* **Submit (`submit`):** Finalize your submission by clicking the "Submit" button.
