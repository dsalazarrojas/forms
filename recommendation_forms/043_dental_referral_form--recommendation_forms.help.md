# dental-referral-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental-referral-form is a tool for collecting information from dental professionals when referring a patient to another dentist or specialist for further care. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are gathered and communicated accurately, facilitating smooth and efficient referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Patient Info** section with the patient's basic details.
2. Provide a clear explanation for the **Referral Reason**.
3. Enter the relevant information about the patient's current **Dentist Info**.
4. Add any relevant **Patient Notes** that the referring dentist should be aware of.
5. Fill out the **Referral Info** with the necessary details for the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Page 1, Field 1)**:
	+ A short form to gather the patient's basic information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Referral Reason (Page 1, Field 2)**:
	+ A field where the dentist can briefly explain the reason for the referral, including the specific problem or issue that requires attention.
* **Dentist Info (Page 1, Field 3)**:
	+ A field for the referring dentist to enter their information and contact details.
* **Patient Notes (Page 1, Field 4)**:
	+ A free-text field for the referring dentist to include any relevant notes or comments about the patient.
* **Referral Info (Page 1, Field 5)**:
	+ A field where the referring dentist can enter the necessary details for the patient's referral, such as the specialist's contact information and any additional instructions.
