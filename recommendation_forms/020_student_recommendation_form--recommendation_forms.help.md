# student_recommendation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide teachers with a structured way to submit student recommendations to support student development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have first-hand knowledge of the student's abilities and potential.
2. Fill out the required fields (Student Id, Teacher Name, Student Name, Student Grade, Student Class, and Teacher Signature).
3. If applicable, provide additional information in the optional fields (Teacher Comment, Student Email, Student Phone, and Assigned Tool).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Id** (number): Enter the unique identification number of the student.
* **Teacher Name** (text): Enter your name as the recommending teacher.
* **Student Name** (text): Enter the name of the student you are recommending.
* **Student Grade** (select one): Select the student's grade level (1, 2, 3, or 4).
* **Student Class** (select multiple): Choose the student's class or level of instruction (A, B, C, or D).
* **Teacher Comment** (text): Provide any additional comments or information about the student's abilities or potential (optional).
* **Student Email** (email): Enter the student's email address (optional).
* **Student Phone** (text): Enter the student's phone number (optional).
* **Assigned Tool** (select one): Choose the tool or resource assigned to the student (optional).
* **Teacher Signature** (note): Provide your digital signature as the recommending teacher.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy and relevance when filling out this form.
* Avoid duplicating previously submitted recommendations.
* Submit this form only for students who have not yet been recommended or whose previous recommendation is no longer applicable.
