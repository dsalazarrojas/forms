# Neuroscience Training Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide recommendations for training programs in the neuroscience field. This form helps to streamline the process of selecting suitable training programs for neuroscience professionals by capturing the expertise and interests of recommenders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the recommended training program that aligns with your expertise.
2. Choose the training date that you believe is most suitable for the selected program.
3. Enter the duration of the training in days.
4. Select the facilitator who best fits the needs of the recommended program.
5. Enter the title of the program.
6. Enter the location where the program will take place.
7. Enter any additional comments about the recommended program.
8. If the recommended facilitator is not listed, enter their name.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Enter your institutional email address (if applicable).
12. Enter your institutional phone number (if applicable).
13. Enter your institutional address (if applicable).
14. Enter your institutional email address (if applicable).
15. Enter any additional comments about yourself.
16. Check the declaration and sign it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Coordinator**: Enter the name of the training coordinator for the recommended program.
* **Recommended Training Program**: Select a training program that aligns with your expertise. This will help program managers make informed decisions about which programs to offer.
* **Recommended Training Date**: Enter the date when you believe the training program should take place. This will help coordinators plan the program schedule.
* **Training Duration**: Enter the number of days the training program should last.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training will be held.
* **Recommended Training Facilitator**: Choose a facilitator who is best suited for the recommended program. Select from the list if available, or enter the name of the facilitator if not listed.
* **Training Topics**: Enter any additional topics that the recommended program should cover.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the recommended program.
* **Recommended Training Facilitator (Other)**: If the recommended facilitator is not listed, enter their name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your institutional email address (if applicable).
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your institutional phone number (if applicable).
* **Submitter Institution**: Enter your institutional name.
* **Submitter Title**: Enter your title or position.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Submitter Position**: Enter your position within the department.
* **Submitter Institutional Affiliation**: Enter your institutional affiliation.
* **Submitter Institutional Address**: Enter your institutional address (if applicable).
* **Submitter Institutional Phone**: Enter your institutional phone number (if applicable).
* **Submitter Institutional Email**: Enter your institutional email address (if applicable).
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional comments about yourself.
* **Submitter Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about yourself.
* **Submitter Declaration**: Read and sign the declaration to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
