# Product Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Recommendation Form is a tool used to collect information about a product, its description, price, and the person who recommended it. This form helps us understand what products our customers are interested in and what they think about them. Please only fill out this form for products you've actually recommended to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the title of the product** in the "Product" field. This is the name of the product you are recommending.
2. **Enter the description of the product** in the "Description" field. This helps us understand the details about the product.
3. **Enter the price of the product** in the "Price" field. Please format the price as a numerical value.
4. **Choose who recommended this product** from the "Recommended By" dropdown menu. You can choose "ChatJimmy" if you want to attribute the recommendation to our chatbot, or select "Other" if you want to enter a different name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product**: This is the name of the product you are recommending. Enter a brief name for the product.
* **Description**: This is the description of the product. Enter as much detail as you need to help us understand the product.
* **Price**: This is the price of the product. Please enter a numerical value for the price.
* **Recommended By**: This field allows you to attribute the recommendation to either "ChatJimmy" or another person. Select "ChatJimmy" to attribute the recommendation to our chatbot, or "Other" to enter a different name.
* **Product Recommendation Form**: This is the main field for entering your recommendation.
