# Publisher Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from publishers who want to recommend a book or article. Fill out this form to provide your contact details and answer a few simple questions to help facilitate the recommendation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "publisher_name" field with your name as the publisher.
3. Provide your "contact_name" and "contact_email" to receive further communication about the recommendation.
4. Enter your "contact_phone" number (optional) to ensure we can contact you about the recommendation.
5. Select your "contact_job_title" to help us understand your role within the publication.
6. Write a brief "form_comments" to let us know why you're recommending the book or article.
7. Choose whether you are the actual "publisher_name_list" by selecting "Yes" or "No".
8. Provide a "publisher_comment" if you'd like to add additional information about your recommendation.
9. Finally, review your information and click "submit" to send in your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start_of_form** (Date): 
    * This field is not a required field but helps us track when you submitted your form.
* **publisher_name** (Text):
    * Enter your name as the publisher of the recommended book or article.
* **contact_name** (Text):
    * Enter your name for the person filling out this form.
* **contact_email** (Email):
    * Enter your email address so we can contact you about the recommendation.
* **contact_phone** (Text):
    * Optional field for your phone number.
* **contact_job_title** (Text):
    * Select your job title to help us understand your role within the publication.
* **form_comments** (Text):
    * A field to provide any additional information about your recommendation.
* **publisher_name_list** (Select Multiple):
    * Choose whether you are the actual publisher by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **publisher_comment** (Text):
    * A field to provide any additional comments about your recommendation.
* **submit_button** (Select One):
    * Select "submit" to send in your form. If you're unsure, you can choose "cancel" to exit the form.

Note: This form is meant to collect information from publishers and does not require a physical submission of a publication.
