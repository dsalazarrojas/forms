# Veterinary Patient Summary And Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about a patient's visit to the veterinary clinic, including contact information, medical conditions, treatment, and follow-up schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's contact information (page 1).
2. Select the relevant medical conditions that apply to the patient (page 1).
3. Enter the medical treatment and medication the patient received (page 1).
4. Add any additional notes about the patient's visit (page 1).
5. Schedule a follow-up appointment or check-in (page 1).
6. Sign and date the form as the client and veterinarian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, address, and contact information.
* **patient_id**: Enter the unique patient ID number.
* **Client Information**: Enter the client's name and contact information.
* **Owner Name**: Select the relevant option for the client's name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the client's phone number.

### Medical Information (Page 1)
* **Medical Conditions**: Check the boxes for any medical conditions that apply to the patient.
* **Medical Treatment**: Enter the treatment provided to the patient.
* **Medication**: Select the relevant medication(s) prescribed.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the patient's visit.

### Follow Up (Page 1)
* **Follow Up**: Schedule a follow-up appointment or check-in with the client.
* **Follow Up Time**: Select the time for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Status**: Check the status of the follow-up appointment.
* **Patient Status**: Enter the patient's status after the visit.
* **Veterinarian Status**: Enter the veterinarian's status after the visit.
* **Client Status**: Enter the client's status after the visit.
* **Follow Up Status**: Check the status of the follow-up appointment.
* **Medical Status**: Enter the patient's medical status after the visit.
* **Medication Status**: Enter the patient's medication status after the visit.

### Signature and Date (Page 2)
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date as the client.
* **Veterinarian Signature**: Sign and date as the veterinarian.
* **Veterinarian Name**: Enter the veterinarian's name.
* **Veterinarian Title**: Enter the veterinarian's title.
* **Date**: Date the form.
* **Time**: Time the form was completed.
* **Veterinarian Status**: Enter the veterinarian's status after the visit.
* **Patient Status**: Enter the patient's status after the visit.
