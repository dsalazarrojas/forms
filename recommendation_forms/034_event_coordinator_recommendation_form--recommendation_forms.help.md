# event_coordinator_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event coordinator recommendation form is designed to provide an opportunity to recommend event coordinators who have done an outstanding job in coordinating events. This form helps to recognize and appreciate the hard work and dedication of event coordinators and provides valuable feedback for future event planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

- Fill in the required fields:
  - **event_coordinator_name**: Enter the name of the event coordinator you wish to recommend.
  - **event_coordinator_supervisor**: Select the supervisor of the event coordinator you are recommending.
  - **event_start_time**: Enter the start time of the event for which you are making the recommendation.
  - **event_end_time**: Enter the end time of the event for which you are making the recommendation.
  - **event_coordinator_rating**: Rate the performance of the event coordinator (1-5).
  - **event_recommendation_score**: Rate the overall performance of the event (1-5).
  - **event_recommendation_justification**: Provide a justification for your recommendation.
- You may also provide additional information in the **event_description**, **event_location**, **event_date_of_last_event**, **event_coordinator_department**, **phone**, and **email** fields if you feel it is necessary.
