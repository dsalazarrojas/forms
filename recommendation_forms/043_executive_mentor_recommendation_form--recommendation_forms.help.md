# executive_mentor_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The executive mentor recommendation form is used to gather details about the employee's background, job position, and relationship with their potential mentor.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Describe your job title and position in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the name of your company in the "Company" field.
5. Specify your department in the "Department" field.
6. If applicable, list the person you report to in the "Report To" field.
7. Choose your job function in the "Job Function" field.
8. In the "Recommendation" field, describe why you are looking for a mentor and what you hope to achieve through the mentorship program.
9. Describe your relationship with your potential mentor in the "Relationship" field.
10. Enter the start date of your desired mentorship in the "Start Date" field.
11. Enter the end date of your desired mentorship in the "End Date" field.
12. Provide the name of your potential mentor in the "Mentor Name" field.
13. Specify the title of your potential mentor in the "Mentor Title" field.
14. If applicable, describe the number of years you have been mentored in the "Years of Mentorship" field.
15. Enter the start date of your mentorship in the "Mentorship Start Date" field.
16. Enter the end date of your mentorship in the "Mentorship End Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Describe your job title and position.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Specify your department.
* **Report To** (`report_to`, text, required): If applicable, list the person you report to.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required): Choose your job function.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, text, required): Describe why you are looking for a mentor and what you hope to achieve through the mentorship program.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with your potential mentor.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your desired mentorship.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your desired mentorship.
* **Mentor Name** (`mentor_name`, text, required): Provide the name of your potential mentor.
* **Mentor Title** (`mentor_title`, text, required): Specify the title of your potential mentor.
* **Years of Mentorship** (`years_of_mentorship`, text, required): If applicable, describe the number of years you have been mentored.
* **Mentorship Start Date** (`mentorship_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your mentorship.
* **Mentorship End Date** (`mentorship_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your mentorship.
