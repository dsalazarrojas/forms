# Construction Firm Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect endorsements from individuals who have had a positive experience with a construction firm. It is intended to provide social proof for potential clients considering working with the firm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact email address in the "Recommender name" and "Recommender contact email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide the name of the firm you are recommending in the "Firm recommended" field.
3. Enter the number of years you have worked with the firm in the "Relationship duration years" field.
4. Select the firm's strengths that you value most, such as quality, timeliness, and communication, in the "Firm strengths" field.
5. Choose the overall level of your recommendation for the firm, including "Highly recommend", "Recommend", "Recommend with reservations", or "Do not recommend".
6. If you have any additional comments or remarks about the firm, you can enter them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recommender name**: Please enter your full name, which will be associated with this recommendation.
* **Recommender contact email**: Please enter your email address, which will be used for verification purposes if needed.
* **Firm recommended**: Enter the name of the construction firm you are recommending.
* **Relationship duration years**: Provide the number of years you have worked with the firm.
* **Firm strengths**: Select the strengths you value most about the firm, such as Quality, Timeliness, or Safety.
* **Overall recommendation level**: Choose the level of your recommendation, including Highly recommend, Recommend, Recommend with reservations, or Do not recommend.
* **Additional comments**: If you have any additional thoughts or comments about the firm, you can enter them here.
