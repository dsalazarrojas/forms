# Director Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Director Recommendation Form is a tool used to collect information from recommenders about their candidate for academic or professional purposes. This form helps in gathering essential details about the candidate, such as their name, title, and institutional affiliation, which are then used to support the recommendation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Candidate Information" section, which includes fields for the candidate's name, title, and institutional details.
2. In the "Recommender Information" section, provide your personal details, including your name, title, and institutional affiliation.
3. You can add a comment or note about the candidate, if needed.
4. Finally, add your signature to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`form_section_1`, text, optional): This section is where you provide the candidate's details, including their name, title, and institutional affiliation.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): This field is for the candidate's name.
* **Recommender Name** (`recommender_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name as the recommender.
* **Recommender Title** (`recommender_title`, text, optional): This field is for your title as the recommender.
* **Recommender Institution** (`recommender_institution`, text, optional): This field is for your institutional affiliation as the recommender.
* **Recommender Email** (`recommender_email`, email, optional): This field is for your email address as the recommender.
* **Recommender Phone** (`recommender_phone`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number as the recommender.
* **Recommender Comment** (`recommender_comment`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the candidate.
* **Recommender Signature** (`recommender_signature`, text, optional): This field is for your electronic signature, confirming that the information provided is accurate.
* **Recommender Institutional Title** (`recommender_institutional_title`, text, optional): This field is for your institutional title as the recommender.
* **Recommender Employer** (`recommender_employer`, text, optional): This field is for your employer as the recommender.
