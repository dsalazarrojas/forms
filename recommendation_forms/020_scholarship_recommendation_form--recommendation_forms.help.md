# Scholarship Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from recommenders for students who are applying for scholarships. The purpose of this form is to capture a student's relationship with the recommender, the nature of their relationship, and a brief recommendation for the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
*   Enter the student's email address in the **Student Email** field.
*   Enter your name in the **Recommender Name** field.
*   Enter your title in the **Recommender Title** field.
*   Enter your organization in the **Recommender Organization** field.
*   Describe your relationship with the student in the **Recommender Relationship** field.
*   Enter the number of years you have known the student in the **Years Known** field.
*   Enter your brief recommendation for the student in the **Recommendation** field.
*   Sign your name in the **Recommender Signature** field, and date it in the **Signature Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Header**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification card or other official documents.
*   **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
*   **Recommender Name**: Enter your name as the recommender.
*   **Recommender Title**: Enter your title or position.
*   **Recommender Organization**: Enter the organization you represent.
*   **Recommender Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the student, e.g., supervisor, teacher, mentor.
*   **Years Known**: Enter the number of years you have known the student.
*   **Recommendation**: Enter a brief recommendation for the student, explaining why they are a strong candidate for a scholarship.
*   **Recommender Signature**: Sign your name as the recommender.
*   **Signature Date**: Date the recommendation by signing your name above and entering the date.

Please note that this form should only be completed by a recommender who knows the student and is able to provide a genuine recommendation.
