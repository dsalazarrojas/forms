# teacher-recommendation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect teacher recommendations for students. Teachers can use this form to provide their endorsement and support for a student's development and career prospects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
2. Enter the name of the student's school.
3. Select the grade level of the student.
4. Enter the teacher's name who is submitting this recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
	+ Type: `Student Name`
	+ Optional/Required: Optional, but required for the teacher to submit this form.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the student's school.
	+ Type: `School Name`
	+ Optional/Required: Optional, but required for the teacher to submit this form.
* **Student Grade**: Select the grade level of the student.
	+ Type: `number`
	+ Optional/Required: Optional, but required for the teacher to submit this form.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your own name as the teacher who is submitting this recommendation.
	+ Type: `Teacher Name`
	+ Optional/Required: Optional, but required for the teacher to submit this form.

## Tips
- Please ensure you are submitting this form for the correct student and academic year.
- You can only submit one recommendation per student per academic year.
- If you are a teacher, only submit this form if you have directly taught the student in the relevant academic year. 

This form is intended to gather teacher recommendations from their direct students, ensuring that the recommendations are accurate, authentic, and relevant to the student's development.
