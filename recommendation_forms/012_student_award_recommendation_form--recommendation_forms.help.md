# Student Award Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Student Award Recommendation Form is used to provide detailed information about a student's qualifications, skills, and achievements. This form helps the award committee evaluate the student's suitability for an award by capturing the recommendations from educators, coaches, or mentors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the student's details, focusing on their strengths and achievements.
2. Select the correct categories and levels to accurately describe the student's qualifications.
3. Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your recommendations.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
5. Double-check your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (1, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **School Name** (2, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the student attends (if applicable).
* **Grade Level** (3, select_one, optional): Select the grade level of the student (e.g., High School, Middle School, Elementary School).
* **Recommendation Type** (4, select_one, optional): Select the type of recommendation (e.g., Academic, Athletic, Personal).
* **Date** (5, date, optional): Enter the date of the recommendation (if applicable).
* **Student Image** (6, text, optional): Upload an image of the student (if applicable).
* **Recommender Name** (7, text, optional): Enter your name as the recommender.
* **Recommender Title** (8, text, optional): Enter your title (if applicable).
* **Recommender Organization** (9, text, optional): Enter your organization or affiliation (if applicable).
* **Recommender Phone** (10, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Recommender Email** (11, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Recommender Relationship** (12, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the student (e.g., teacher, coach, mentor).
* **Recommender Comment** (13, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the student.
* **Student Award Category** (14, select_one, optional): Select the category for the award (e.g., Academic, Athletic, Artistic).
* **Student Award Type** (15, select_one, optional): Select the type of award (e.g., Academic, Artistic, Athletic, Community Service).
* **Student Award Level** (16, select_one, optional): Select the level of the award (e.g., High School, College, University, Elementary School, Middle School).
* **School Name** (17, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the student attends (if applicable).
* **School Address** (18, text, optional): Enter the school's address (if applicable).

* **Award Committee** (20, text, optional): Enter the name of the award committee (if applicable).
* **Committee Member Name** (21, text, optional): Enter the name of the committee member.
* **Committee Member Title** (22, text, optional): Enter the title of the committee member (if applicable).
* **Committee Member Organization** (23, text, optional): Enter the committee member's organization or affiliation (if applicable).
