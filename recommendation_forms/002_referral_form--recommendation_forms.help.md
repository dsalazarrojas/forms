# Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Form is used to collect information about a referee for internal purposes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the referee's name in the "Referee" field.
2. Add the referee's contact information in the "Contact" field.
3. Enter the referee's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the referee's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the referee in the "Referee Note" field.
6. Enter the referrer's contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
7. Enter the referrer's name in the "Name" field.
8. Enter the referrer's job title in the "Title" field.
9. Enter the referrer's department or role in the "Role" field.
10. Enter the department or team the referrer is from in the "Department" field.
11. Enter the address of the referrer's department or team in the "Address" field.
12. Enter the city of the referrer's department or team in the "City" field.
13. Enter the state of the referrer's department or team in the "State" field.
14. Enter the zip code of the referrer's department or team in the "Zip" field.
15. Enter any additional referral request or comments in the "Referral Request" field.
16. Enter the phone number of the referrer's department or team in the "Referee" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referee (1)**: Enter the name of the referee.
* **Referee** (2): Enter the full name of the referee.
* **Contact** (3): Enter the contact information of the referee, such as their phone number or address.
* **Email** (4): Enter the email address of the referee.
* **Phone** (5): Enter the phone number of the referee.
* **Note** (6): Add any additional notes or comments about the referee.
* **Referee Note** (7): Add any additional notes or comments about the referee.
* **Contact Information** (8): Enter the contact information of the referrer, such as their phone number or address.
* **Name** (9): Enter the name of the referrer.
* **Title** (10): Enter the job title of the referrer.
* **Role** (11): Enter the department or role of the referrer.
* **Department** (12): Enter the department or team the referrer is from.
* **Address** (13): Enter the address of the referrer's department or team.
* **City** (14): Enter the city of the referrer's department or team.
* **State** (15): Enter the state of the referrer's department or team.
* **Zip** (16): Enter the zip code of the referrer's department or team.
* **Referral Request** (17): Enter any additional referral request or comments.
* **Referee** (18): Enter the phone number of the referrer's department or team.
