# website_suggestion_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "website_suggestion_form_template" is a form designed for users to submit suggestions or feedback about the website. It helps the team identify areas that need improvement, gather user insights, and make informed decisions about the website's development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your suggestion in the Suggestion field.
4. Choose the type of suggestion you are submitting (e.g., bug, feature request, or feedback).
5. Add a brief description of your suggestion.
6. Enter the URL of the website related to your suggestion (if applicable).
7. Choose relevant category and tags for your suggestion (if applicable).
8. Select the date when you experienced the issue or noticed the suggestion (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be displayed on the suggestion.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for more information.
* **Suggestion** (3, text, required): Describe your suggestion or feedback for the website.
* **Suggestion Type** (4, select_one, required): Choose the type of suggestion you are submitting (e.g., bug, feature request, or feedback).
* **Description** (5, text, required): Provide a brief description of your suggestion or feedback.
* **Website** (6, text, required): Enter the URL of the website related to your suggestion (if applicable).
* **Category** (7, text, required): Choose a category that best describes your suggestion (e.g., UI/UX, Content, or Performance).
* **Tags** (8, text, required): Add relevant tags to help us quickly identify and filter your suggestion.
* **Date** (9, date, required): Select the date when you experienced the issue or noticed the suggestion (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you submit a clear and concise suggestion.
* If you have multiple suggestions, you can submit them separately.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on a field, feel free to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your feedback and suggestions, and they will help us improve the website.
