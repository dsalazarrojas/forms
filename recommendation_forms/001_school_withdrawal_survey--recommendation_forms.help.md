# School Withdrawal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from students who are leaving school. Your input will help the school administration understand reasons for student departures and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field "Student Name".
2. Select who filled out this form (e.g., "Student") in the field "Who Fills This Form Out".
3. Enter the grade you are currently in or last attended in the field "Grade".
4. Enter your grade level in the field "Grade Level".
5. Describe any disappointments you have experienced in the field "Disappointments".
6. Provide the reason why you're leaving school in the field "Why Are You Leaving".
7. If you have any other reasons not listed above, enter them in the field "Other Reasons".
8. Finally, enter any additional comments or thoughts about the school in the field "Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Please enter your full name as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Who Fills This Form Out**: Select who is filling out this form, e.g., "Student", "Parent", or "Teacher".
* **Grade**: Enter your current or last attended grade level.
* **Grade Level**: Enter your grade level, for example, 9th, 10th, etc.
* **Disappointments**: Share any disappointments you experienced while at school.
* **Why Are You Leaving**: Describe the reason you are leaving school.
* **Other Reasons**: If you have any other reasons not listed above, enter them here.
* **Comments**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about the school.
