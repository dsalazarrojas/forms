# Instructor Interview & Recommendation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from instructors for a Recommendation Survey. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and recommendations from instructors about their interactions with the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling out your name in the **instructor_name** field.
*   Provide your email address in the **email** field in the format you would like to receive communications from us.
*   Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
*   Select the date of your interview from the available date picker in the **interview_date** field.
*   Choose your recommendation from the available options in the **recommendation** field. You can select one or multiple options.
*   Provide any additional comments or feedback in the **additional_comments** field, if you have anything specific to share.
*   Finally, fill out your name in the **name_2** field, which will be used to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **instructor_name**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in our communications.
*   **email**: Your email address will be used to contact you with follow-up questions or information regarding your feedback.
*   **phone**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted via phone.
*   **interview_date**: Select the date of your interview from the available date picker.
*   **recommendation**: Choose one or multiple options for your recommendation.
*   **additional_comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you would like to provide.
*   **name_2**: Confirm your name to ensure your feedback is accurately attributed to you.
