# Horse Riding Instructor Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and recommendations about horse riding instructors, including their primary focus, equipment, and education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the instructor's primary focus in the "What is the horse riding instructor's primary focus" field.
2. Select the recommended riding instructors from the list.
3. Provide feedback about the instructor's teaching style and effectiveness in the "Feedback" field.
4. Rate the instructor's performance using the number rating system.
5. Enter a brief evaluation of the instructor's effectiveness.
6. Enter the date and time of the riding session.
7. Select the duration of the ride.
8. Enter the number of students present during the session.
9. Choose the instructor's education level (e.g., B.S. or Associate, B.S. or Doctorate, etc.).
10. Choose the instructor's certifications (e.g., Equine, Equine or Other, etc.).
11. Select the equipment used during the session (e.g., Horse, Other, etc.).
12. Enter any additional equipment details.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the horse riding instructor's primary focus** (1): Enter a brief description of the instructor's primary focus, such as teaching riding techniques or providing care and handling instructions.
* **Recommended Riding Instructor** (2): Select the riding instructors you have feedback for, using the options provided.
* **Feedback** (3): Provide feedback about the instructor's teaching style, including strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Instructor Rating (1-5)** (4): Rate the instructor's performance using the number rating system, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Instructor Evaluation** (5): Enter a brief evaluation of the instructor's effectiveness, such as their ability to communicate complex instructions or provide clear feedback.
* **Date of Riding** (6): Enter the date of the riding session, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Riding** (7): Enter the time of the riding session, using 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Riding Duration** (8): Enter the duration of the ride, in hours.
* **Number of Students** (9): Enter the number of students present during the session.
* **Riding Instructor Education** (10): Choose the instructor's education level, such as B.S. or Associate, B.S. or Doctorate, etc.
* **Riding Instructor Certification** (11): Choose the instructor's certifications, such as Equine, Equine or Other, etc.
* **Instructor Equipment** (12): Select the equipment used during the session, such as Horse, Other, etc.
* **Instructor Equipment 1-10** (13-23): Select the additional equipment used during the session.
* **Instructor Equipment Details** (14): Enter any additional equipment details, such as specific brands or models.

### Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and ratings.
* Ensure that you are recommending the correct instructor and providing accurate information.
* Use the correct format for entering dates and times.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
