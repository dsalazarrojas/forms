# Safety Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Suggestion Form is a tool designed to collect suggestions from employees that aim to improve workplace safety. It's essential to contribute to a safer work environment. Please ensure that you only submit suggestions that have not been previously discussed or proposed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your suggestion in the "Suggested Suggestion" field.
2. Provide your name and employee ID in the "Employee Name" and "Employee ID" fields, respectively.
3. Choose a relevant date for your submission.
4. In the "Description" field, elaborate on your safety suggestion in as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Suggested Suggestion** (text, optional): A brief description of your proposed safety improvement.
* **Employee Name** (text, optional): Your name as an employee who submitted the suggestion.
* **Employee ID** (number, optional): Your employee ID number.
* **Date** (date, optional): A specific date for tracking purposes.
* **Description** (text, optional): Elaborate on your safety suggestion to help the relevant team members understand your idea.
