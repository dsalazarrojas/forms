# Submit Website Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Submit Website Form is a simple form designed to gather essential details about a website, enabling its owner or manager to update and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your website in the **website_form** field.
2. Provide the URL of your website in the **website_url** field.
3. Add relevant keywords associated with your website in the **keywords** field.
4. If you have any additional information about the website, you can include it in the **user_data** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **website_form** (Text, Optional)
Enter the title of your website, a brief description that identifies the site and helps with search engine optimization (SEO).

### **website_url** (Text, Optional)
Provide the full URL of your website, including http:// or https://, for easy identification and accessibility.

### **user_data** (Text, Optional)
If there's any additional information you'd like to share about the website, such as its category, description, or features, you can add it here.

### **keywords** (Text, Optional)
Enter relevant keywords related to your website that can help improve its visibility on search engines, such as keywords, tags, or topics covered by the site.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate records.
- Use the correct format for the website URL (http:// or https://).
- Add relevant keywords to enhance search engine results.
- If you have more information about the website, feel free to include it in the **user_data** field.
