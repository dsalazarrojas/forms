# Workplace Mediator Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from parties involved in a workplace conflict to recommend a suitable mediator for resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring that you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete the fields with accurate and clear information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mediator Name** (`mediator_name`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to capture the name of the proposed mediator. Please enter the name of the mediator you are recommending.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to capture the contact number of the proposed mediator. Please enter the number where the mediator can be reached.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ This field is used to capture the email address of the proposed mediator. Please enter the email address of the mediator.
* **Conflict Description** (`conflict_description`, note, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide a brief description of the conflict. Please describe the situation in a clear and concise manner.
* **Resolution Plan** (`resolution_plan`, note, optional):
	+ This field is used to outline the proposed resolution plan. Please describe the proposed steps to resolve the conflict.
* **Mediator Recommendation** (`mediator_recommendation`, note, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide any additional comments or recommendations regarding the proposed mediator. Please use this space to elaborate on your recommendation.
* **Start form** (`form_start`, note, optional):
	+ This field is not used by the user, it's just a marker to indicate the beginning of the form.
* **End form** (`form_end`, note, optional):
	+ This field is not used by the user, it's just a marker to indicate the end of the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to the conflict resolution team.
