# Holiday Book Recommendation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holiday book recommendation survey is designed to help us understand what types of holiday books you're interested in reading. This information will be used to provide personalized book suggestions tailored to your tastes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your holiday book preferences (options 1-3)
2. Select one holiday theme interest (option 4-6)
3. Select up to 3 holiday theme subcategories (options 10-12)
4. Select up to 3 holiday book genres (options 19-21)
5. Write a note with any holiday book suggestions you have (option 8)
6. Provide your email address for notifications (option 9)
7. Choose how often you'd like to receive email notifications (option 10)
8. Enter a subject line for your email notifications (option 11)
9. Enter content for your email notifications (option 12)
10. Select who to send notifications to (option 13)
11. Choose a date and time to send notifications (options 14-17)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Book Preferences** (`holiday_book_recommendation_survey_page_1`, select_multiple, required): Choose up to 3 options that describe the type of holiday book you're interested in reading. Selecting up to 3 options will help us understand your interests and preferences.
* **Holiday Book Suggestions** (`holiday_book_suggestions`, select_one, required): Select one option that best describes your holiday book suggestion. This will help us provide more accurate recommendations.
* **Holiday Theme Interest** (`holiday_theme_interest`, select_one, required): Select one option that best describes your holiday theme interest. This will help us understand what type of holidays you're interested in.
* **Holiday Theme Subcategory** (`holiday_theme_subcategory`, select_multiple, required): Choose up to 3 options that best describe your holiday theme subcategory. Selecting up to 3 options will help us understand your interests and preferences.
* **Holiday Book Genres** (`holiday_genres`, select_multiple, required): Choose up to 3 options that best describe your holiday book genre interests. Selecting up to 3 options will help us understand your interests and preferences.
* **Holiday Book Suggestions Note** (`holiday_book_recommendations`, note, required): Write a note explaining your holiday book suggestions. This will help us provide more accurate recommendations.
* **Email** (`email_notification`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive holiday book recommendations.
* **Email Frequency** (`email_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you'd like to receive email notifications.
* **Email Subject** (`email_subject`, text, required): Enter a subject line for your email notifications.
* **Email Content** (`email_content`, text, required): Enter content for your email notifications.
* **Send To** (`email_send_to`, email, required): Enter the email address to send notifications to.
* **Send On** (`send_on`, date, required): Choose a date to send notifications.
* **Send Time** (`send_time`, time, optional): Choose a time to send notifications (optional).
* **Send Before/After** (`send_before` and `send_after`, time, optional): Choose a time to send notifications before/after (optional).
* **Holiday Book Recommendation Survey Page 2-7** (`holiday_book_recommendation_survey_page_2`-`holiday_book_recommendation_survey_page_7`, select_multiple and text, optional): Select up to 3 options that best describe your holiday book interests on each page, and enter any additional information in the text fields.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
