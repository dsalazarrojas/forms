# reviewed_doctors_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about medical groups and their practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the correct medical group from the options in the 'Reviewed Doctors' field.
2. Fill out the contact information, practice address, phone numbers, email addresses, website, and other relevant details for the selected medical group.
3. Ensure that the 'Medical Group Name', 'Practice Name', 'Medical Group Street', 'Medical Group City', 'Medical Group State', 'Medical Group Zip', 'Medical Group Phone', 'Medical Group Email', 'Medical Group Fax', and 'Medical Group License' fields are accurate and up-to-date.
4. Indicate whether the medical group has a license, certified, accredited, or no license.
5. Confirm that the form is complete and accurate before submitting.
