# Food Service Worker Recommendation Letter Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a written recommendation from a food service worker's supervisor or manager for the purpose of a job application or promotion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the applicant's name in the "Applicant Name" field.
2. Enter the recommender's name in the "Recommender Name" field.
3. Provide the recommender's position in the "Recommender Position" field.
4. Enter the recommender's title in the "Recommender Title" field.
5. Enter the recommender's email address in the "Recommender Email" field.
6. Enter the recommender's phone number in the "Recommender Phone" field.
7. Select the period for which the recommendation is being made in the "Reference Period" field.
8. Sign the recommender's signature in the "Recommender Signature" field.
9. Sign the applicant's name in the "Applicant Name Signature" field.
10. Date the recommender's signature in the "Recommender Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the food service worker who is requesting the recommendation.
* **Recommender Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person providing the recommendation.
* **Recommender Position** (text, required): Enter the position of the recommender in the food service organization.
* **Recommender Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the recommender (e.g. "Supervisor", "Manager").
* **Recommender Email** (email, required): Enter the recommender's email address.
* **Recommender Phone** (text, required): Enter the recommender's phone number.
* **Reference Period** (date, required): Select the period for which the recommendation is being made (e.g. "January 2022 - December 2022").
* **Recommender Signature** (text, required): Sign your name as the recommender.
* **Applicant Name Signature** (text, required): The food service worker should sign their name.
* **Recommender Signature Date** (date, required): Date the recommender's signature.
* **Recommender Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the worker (e.g. "Works well under pressure.", etc.).
* **Agree** (select, required): Select whether you agree or disagree with the request.
