# Player Coach Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Coach Recommendation Form is a tool for players, coaches, or sports team staff to provide recommendations on a specific player. This form helps to gather details about a player's skills, performance, and other relevant information to aid in their development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have first-hand knowledge or experience with the player.
2. Read each field description carefully and fill in the relevant information.
3. Complete all required fields (indicated by a star \*).
4. Submit your recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Player Name** (1, text, *): Enter the player's full name.
- **Player Position** (2, select_one): Select the player's primary position (e.g., Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, Forward).
- **Player Age** (3, number): Enter the player's age.
- **Feedback** (4, text): Describe the player's greatest strength or area of improvement.
- **Potential** (5, select_multiple): Rate the player's potential (High, Medium, Low).
- **Coach Name** (6, text): Enter your name as the coach or staff member recommending the player.
- **Coach Email** (7, email): Enter your email for communication purposes.
- **Coach Phone** (8, text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
- **Date of Birth** (9, date): Enter the player's date of birth.
- **Time of Birth** (10, time): Enter the player's time of birth.
- **Note** (11, note): Add any additional comments or notes about the player.
- **Player Performance** (12, select_multiple): Rate the player's performance (Very Good, Average, Poor).
- **Coach Performance** (13, select_multiple): Rate your coaching performance (Very Good, Average, Poor).
- **Coach Experience** (14, number): Enter your years of coaching experience.
- **Years Coached** (15, number): Enter the number of years you have coached.
- **Players Coached** (16, number): Enter the number of players you have coached.
- **Player Coached** (17, select_one): Indicate if you have coached the player before.
- **Recommended** (18, select_one): Indicate if you recommend the player.
- **Comments** (19, text): Add any additional comments or feedback.
- **Submit** (20, text): Click to submit your recommendation.
- **Player Gender** (21, select_one): Select the player's gender (Male, Female).
- **Date Submitted** (22, date): The date and time you submitted the form.
- **Coach Position** (23, select_one): Select your coaching position (Head Coach, Assistant Coach).
- **Team Name** (24, text): Enter the name of the team you are coaching or have coached.
- **Other Team** (25, select_one): Select the other team if applicable.
