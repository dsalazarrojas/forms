# Virtual Reality Content Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your demographics and preferences for virtual reality content recommendations. Your responses will help us provide tailored content suggestions based on your age, gender, content type, and other preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by confirming that you are ready to begin the form.
2. Select your gender from the options provided.
3. Enter your age (in years).
4. If you are willing to continue with the form, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
5. Choose the types of virtual reality content you are interested in.
6. Select the content types you would like to be recommended for.
7. Once you have completed the form, click "Form End" to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Start** (`form_start`, note, not required): This field indicates that you are ready to begin the form.
* **User Gender** (`user_gender`, select_multiple, not required): Please select your gender from the options provided to help us better understand your preferences.
* **User Age** (`user_age`, number, not required): Please enter your age in years to help us provide relevant content recommendations.
* **Form Continue** (`form_continue`, select_one, not required): If you are willing to continue with the form, select "Yes". If you are not, select "No".
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the types of virtual reality content you are interested in (e.g., games, educational, entertainment).
* **Recommended Content** (`recommended_content`, select_multiple, not required): Select the types of content you would like to be recommended for.
* **Form End** (`form_end`, note, not required): Once you have completed the form, click "Form End" to submit your responses.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly to ensure accurate content recommendations.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or our content recommendations, feel free to contact us.
* Your responses are kept confidential and are used solely for the purpose of providing personalized content recommendations.
