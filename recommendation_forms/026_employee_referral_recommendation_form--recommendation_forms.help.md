# Employee Referral Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Referral Recommendation Form is a tool for current employees to recommend colleagues who they believe would be a good fit for open positions within the company. This form helps to streamline the hiring process by providing managers with valuable insights from their team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Be specific about the employee's skills, qualifications, and work habits.
3. Provide clear and concise answers that will help the hiring team understand why the referred employee is a good candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (Page 1, Field 1)** (`employee_name`): Enter the name of the employee you are referring.
* **Employee Position (Page 1, Field 2)** (`employee_position`): Enter the current position of the employee you are referring.
* **Employee Department (Page 1, Field 3)** (`employee_department`): Enter the department where the employee works.
* **Employee Manager (Page 1, Field 4)** (`employee_manager`): Enter the name of the employee's current manager.
* **Referred Name (Page 1, Field 5)** (`referred_name`): Enter the name of the employee you are referring.
* **Referred Position (Page 1, Field 6)** (`referred_position`): Enter the position you believe the referred employee would be a good fit for.
* **Referred Department (Page 1, Field 7)** (`referred_department`): Enter the department where you believe the referred employee would work well.
* **Manager Name (Page 1, Field 8)** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of the manager who would be responsible for the referred employee.
* **Manager Position (Page 1, Field 9)** (`manager_position`): Enter the position of the manager who would be supervising the referred employee.
* **Manager Department (Page 1, Field 10)** (`manager_department`): Enter the department where the manager works.
* **Manager Manager (Page 1, Field 11)** (`manager_manager`): Enter the name of the manager's manager.
* **Manager Manager Position (Page 1, Field 12)** (`manager_position`): Enter the position of the manager's manager.
* **Manager Manager Department (Page 1, Field 13)** (`manager_department`): Enter the department where the manager's manager works.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide accurate information about the referred employee's skills, qualifications, and work habits.
* Avoid making assumptions or speculating about the referred employee's potential.
* Focus on the employee's strengths and qualifications that would make them a good fit for the position.
* Use clear and concise language to help the hiring team quickly understand the referred employee's qualifications.
