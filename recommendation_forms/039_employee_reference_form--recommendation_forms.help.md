# Employee Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reference Form is used to collect information about an employee's professional references for employment or promotion purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the candidate's name and position.
2.  Enter the referee's name and title (e.g., Manager, Team Lead, or other).
3.  Provide the referee's phone number and email address.
4.  Enter the referee's position and department (if applicable).
5.  Select the date when the referee started and ended working with the candidate.
6.  Enter the number of years the candidate has worked with the referee.
7.  Enter the number of years the referee has worked at the current company.
8.  Select the candidate's job title and position.
9.  Choose the candidate's current position (e.g., Manager, Team Member, or Team Lead).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (1, text, required): Enter the candidate's name.
* **Candidate Position** (2, select_one, required): Select the candidate's current or most recent position (e.g., Manager, Team Member, or Team Lead).
* **Referee Name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the person providing the reference.
* **Referee Title** (4, select_multiple, required): Select the title of the referee (e.g., CEO, Manager, Team Lead, or Other).
* **Referee Phone** (5, text, required): Enter the referee's phone number.
* **Referee Email** (6, email, required): Enter the referee's email address.
* **Referee Position** (7, text, required): Enter the referee's position or department (if applicable).
* **Candidate Position 2** (8, select_one, required): Select the candidate's current or most recent position (e.g., Manager, Team Member, or Team Lead).
* **Company Name** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the company where the candidate works.
* **Company** (10, text, required): Enter the name of the company where the candidate works.
* **Department** (11, text, optional): Enter the department where the candidate works.
* **Team** (12, text, optional): Enter the team the candidate works in.
* **Job Title** (13, text, required): Enter the candidate's job title.
* **Date of Reference** (14, date, required): Enter the date when the referee provided the reference.
* **Referee Start Date** (15, date, required): Enter the date when the referee started working with the candidate.
* **Referee End Date** (16, date, optional): Enter the date when the referee stopped working with the candidate (if applicable).
* **Years of Experience** (17, number, optional): Enter the number of years the candidate has worked with the referee.
* **Years at Current Company** (18, number, optional): Enter the number of years the candidate has worked at the current company.

**Tips**
* Be accurate when providing dates and years of experience.
* Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about a field, please contact your HR representative for clarification.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and explanations are written with the assumption that each field has a specific and clear purpose. However, if a field label is weak or unclear, the explanation will be adjusted accordingly to provide the best-effort interpretation of its purpose.
