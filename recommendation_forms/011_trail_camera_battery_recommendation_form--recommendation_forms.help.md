# Trail Camera Battery Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information to recommend a suitable battery for a trail camera based on the user's usage pattern, environment, and other factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant options for each field to provide as much detail as possible.
2. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the customer's needs.
3. Ensure that you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trail Camera Usage Pattern** (`trail_camera_usage_pattern`): Please select the frequency and duration of the trail camera's usage. This will help us to determine the power requirements for the battery.
* **Trail Camera Environment** (`trail_camera_environment`): Please select the environment where the trail camera will be used (Urban, Rural, or Both).
* **Battery Budget** (`battery_budget`): Please enter the maximum budget for the battery.
* **Number of Cameras** (`number_of_cameras`): If multiple cameras will be used, please enter the total number of cameras.
* **Camera Location** (`camera_location`): Please select the locations where the trail cameras will be installed (Forest, Mountain, or Waterfront).
* **Camera Height** (`camera_height`): If applicable, please enter the height of the camera.
* **Power Cycle** (`power_cycle`): Please select the power cycle required for the trail camera (24 Hours, 48 Hours, or 72 Hours).
* **Battery Type** (`battery_type`): Please select the type of battery required (AA, D, or 9V).
* **Recommended Battery** (`recommended_battery`): This field is not required, but please enter any other relevant information that may help us to provide a better recommendation.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Please provide any additional information that may be relevant to the customer's needs.
* **Retailer Recommendation** (`retailer_recommendation`): Please enter any additional information or recommendations that you may have for the customer.
* **Retailer Signature** (`retailer_signature`): Please enter your name and signature as the retailer.
* **Date** (`date`): Please enter the date when the form was completed.
* **Time** (`time`): Please enter the time when the form was completed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Please enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Please enter the customer's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Please enter the customer's address.
* **Note** (`note`): Please enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`): Please confirm the customer's email address.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (`confirm_phone_number`): Please confirm the customer's phone number.
