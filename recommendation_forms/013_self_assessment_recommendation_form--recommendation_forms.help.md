# Self Assessment Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Self Assessment Recommendation Form is a tool used to collect evaluations and recommendations from managers to help employees identify areas for personal and professional growth. This form is designed to provide a structured way for managers to provide constructive feedback, allowing employees to develop their skills and advance in their careers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee Name.
2. Provide the Job Title of the employee.
3. Enter the Manager Name.
4. Give a brief Employee Evaluation, if applicable.
5. Identify the employee's greatest strength(s).
6. Choose one or more areas where the employee needs development support (Development, Communication, Time Management, Leadership, or Other).
7. Suggest specific actions the employee can take to improve in the selected areas.
8. Finally, sign the form with your date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee being assessed.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job or position the employee holds.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the manager or supervisor.
* **Employee Evaluation** (`employee_evaluation`, text, optional): Provide a brief evaluation of the employee's performance, if applicable.
* **Greatest Strength** (`recommendation_1`, text, optional): Describe what you think is the employee's greatest strength or area of expertise.
* **Areas of Development** (`recommendation_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more areas where you think the employee needs development support. Choose from: Development, Communication, Time Management, Leadership, or Other.
* **Actions for Improvement** (`recommendation_3`, text, optional): Suggest specific actions the employee can take to improve in the selected areas.
* **Signature** (`signature`, date, optional): Sign the form with the date to confirm your recommendations.

## Tips
Please fill out this form as comprehensively as possible, providing specific examples and concrete suggestions for improvement. This will help the employee grow and develop their skills, ultimately benefiting their career and your team's performance. Remember, this form is a constructive tool for growth and development, not a source of criticism.
