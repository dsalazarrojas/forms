# Operations Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about operations complaints and feedback from users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title to identify the specific operation that is being reported on.
2. Provide the complainant's name and any additional relevant contact information.
3. Describe the nature of the complaint and the incident that occurred.
4. Specify the department that was involved in the operation.
5. Provide the location where the incident occurred.
6. Enter the date and time of the incident.
7. Describe any action taken to resolve the issue.
8. Specify if the issue has been resolved or is still ongoing.
9. Provide any follow-up details if necessary.
10. Enter any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title to identify the operation that is being reported on.
* **Complainant** (`complainant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the complaint.
* **Nature Of Complaint** (`nature_of_complaint`, text, required/optional): Describe the nature of the complaint or incident that occurred.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the complaint or incident.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Identify the department that was involved in the operation.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Date Of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Time Of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, required/optional): Describe any action taken to resolve the issue.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, text, required/optional): Provide any follow-up details if necessary.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, required/optional): Specify if the issue has been resolved or is still ongoing.
* **Closed Date** (`closed_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the issue was resolved.
* **Closed Time** (`closed_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the issue was resolved.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required/optional): Identify who was assigned to resolve the issue.
* **Resolution Notes** (`resolution_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes about the resolution.
* **Follow Up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any follow-up details if necessary.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the complainant.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number associated with the complainant.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.

Note: The fields marked as required/optional can be filled out at the discretion of the user, but are required for the form to be submitted.
