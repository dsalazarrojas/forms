# get_an_estimate_for_some_service_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "get_an_estimate_for_some_service_form" is a form used to collect information from customers for the purpose of obtaining an estimate for a service. This form is designed to gather details about the customer's needs and preferences, as well as the business's offerings and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Select the services you are interested in from the "Services Offered" field.
5. Provide an estimate of the cost of the service in the "Estimated Cost" field.
6. Optionally, add a description of the service in the "Description" field.
7. Select whether you would like to submit a request for the service in the "Submit Request" field.
8. Add any additional customer comments or requests in the "Customer Request" field.
9. Estimate the time required for the service in the "Estimated Time" field.
10. Select the service hours you are interested in from the "Service Hours" field.
11. Evaluate the business relationships with our company from the "Business Relationships" field.
12. Evaluate the customer relationships with our company from the "Customer Relationships" field.
13. Add any customer comments about your experience or preferences in the "Customer Comments" field.
14. Add any business comments or notes about the service in the "Business Comments" field.
15. Select whether you would like to follow up on the request in the "Follow Up" field.
16. Finally, select the next actions to be taken for this request from the "Next Actions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, required): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Services Offered** (`Services Offered`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are interested in.
* **Estimated Cost** (`Estimated Cost`, number, required): Provide an estimate of the cost of the service.
* **Description** (`Description`, note, required): Add a brief description of the service.
* **Services Offered** (`Services Offered`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are interested in.
* **Estimated Time** (`Estimated Time`, time, required): Estimate the time required for the service.
* **Service Hours** (`Service Hours`, number, required): Select the service hours you are interested in.
* **Business Relationships** (`Business Relationships`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the business relationships with our company.
* **Customer Relationships** (`Customer Relationships`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the customer relationships with our company.
* **Customer Comments** (`Customer Comments`, text, required): Add any customer comments or preferences.
* **Business Comments** (`Business Comments`, text, required): Add any business comments or notes about the service.
* **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`, select_one, required): Select whether you would like to follow up on the request.
* **Next Actions** (`Next Actions`, select_one, required): Select the next actions to be taken for this request.
