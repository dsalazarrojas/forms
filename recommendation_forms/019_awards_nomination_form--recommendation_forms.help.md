# Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Awards Nomination Form is designed to gather information for nominations submitted to an awards committee. This form helps to identify candidates that deserve recognition and reward excellence in various categories.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the award category that best fits the nominee's achievements.
2. Fill out the nominator's information: first name, last name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Enter the nominee's first name and last name.
4. Provide the nominee's email (if known).
5. Enter the nominee's organization or institution.
6. Specify the nominee's position or role.
7. Choose how you know the nominee (relationship).
8. Indicate how long you have known the nominee.
9. Write a brief summary of why the nominee deserves this award.
10. Write a detailed letter explaining why the nominee is worthy of this award.
11. List specific accomplishments and achievements of the nominee.
12. If applicable, describe leadership examples or community impact.
13. Indicate if references are available.
14. Specify if the nominee is aware of this nomination.
15. Provide any additional information the committee should know.
16. Confirm agreement to the nomination terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Award Name** (`Award Name`, select_one, required): Select the award category that best fits the nominee's achievements.
* **Nominator First Name** (`nominator_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Nominator Last Name** (`nominator_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`nominator_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Your Organization** (`nominator_organization`, text, optional): Enter your company or institution (if applicable).
* **Nominee First Name** (`nominee_first_name`, text, required): Enter the nominee's first name.
* **Nominee Last Name** (`nominee_last_name`, text, required): Enter the nominee’s last name.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, optional): Enter the nominee’s email (if known).
* **Nominee Organization** (`nominee_organization`, text, required): Enter the nominee’s company or institution.
* **Nominee Position** (`nominee_position`, text, required): Enter the nominee’s job title or role.
* **Your Relationship** (`relationship_to_nominee`, select_one, required): Choose how you know the nominee.
* **Length of Relationship** (`length_of_relationship`, select_one, required): Indicate how long you have known the nominee.
* **Nomination Summary** (`nomination_summary`, text, required): Write a brief summary of why the nominee deserves this award.
* **Detailed Nomination Letter** (`detailed_nomination`, text, required): Write a detailed letter explaining why the nominee is worthy of this award.
* **Notable Achievements** (`notable_achievements`, text, required): List specific accomplishments and achievements of the nominee.
* **Leadership Examples** (`leadership_examples`, text, optional): Describe leadership examples (if applicable).
* **Community Impact** (`community_impact`, text, optional): Describe how the nominee has impacted the community (if applicable).
* **Challenges Overcome** (`challenges_overcome`, text, optional): Indicate what challenges the nominee has overcome (if applicable).
* **Supporting Materials** (`supporting_materials`, text, optional): List any attachments or references (if applicable).
* **Are References Available** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Indicate if references are available.
* **Is Nominee Aware** (`nominee_aware`, select_one, required): Indicate if the nominee is aware of this nomination.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information the committee should know.
* **I Agree to Nomination Terms** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm agreement to the nomination terms. 
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Select today's date.
