# travel_agent_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_agent_recommendation_form is a tool used by travel agents to provide personalized recommendations to customers based on their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, you will need to provide information about yourself as a travel agent, as well as the customer's details and their travel preferences.
2.  Begin by filling out the form with the required information:
	*   **Travel Agent Info**: Enter your name.
	*   **Travel Agent Name**: Enter your name.
	*   **Travel Agent Email**: Enter your email address.
	*   **Travel Agent Phone**: Enter your phone number.
3.  Then, enter the customer's information:
	*   **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
	*   **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
	*   **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
4.  Next, choose the destination for the trip:
	*   **Destination**: Select the destination you are recommending (e.g., Paris, France, Tokyo, Japan, New York City, USA).
5.  Specify the travel dates:
	*   **Travel Dates**: Choose the seasons you want to travel (e.g., Summer, Winter, Spring, Fall).
6.  Select the activities for the trip:
	*   **Activities**: Choose the types of activities you want to do on the trip (e.g., Beach, Mountain, City, Adventure).
7.  Choose the type of travel:
	*   **Travel Type**: Select the type of travel (e.g., Relaxation, Adventure, Culture, Foodie).
8.  Choose the preferred travel style:
	*   **Preferred Travel Style**: Select the style of travel you prefer (e.g., Budget, Luxury, Family, Solo).
9.  Rate the travel agent's experience:
	*   **Travel Agent Rating**: Rate your experience as a travel agent from 1 to 5.
10.  Finally, add any additional comments:
	*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information about your recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Travel Agent Info (ID 1)**: This field is used to provide information about yourself as a travel agent.
*   **Travel Agent Name (ID 2)**: Enter your name as a travel agent.
*   **Travel Agent Email (ID 3)**: Enter your email address as a travel agent.
*   **Travel Agent Phone (ID 4)**: Enter your phone number as a travel agent.
*   **Customer Info (ID 5)**: Provide information about the customer.
*   **Customer Name (ID 6)**: Enter the customer's name.
*   **Customer Email (ID 7)**: Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Customer Phone (ID 8)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **Destination (ID 9)**: Select the destination you are recommending.
*   **Travel Dates (ID 10)**: Choose the travel dates.
*   **Activities (ID 11)**: Select the activities for the trip.
*   **Travel Type (ID 12)**: Select the type of travel.
*   **Preferred Travel Style (ID 13)**: Select the preferred travel style.
*   **Travel Agent Rating (ID 14)**: Rate your experience as a travel agent.
*   **Recommendation (ID 15)**: Enter a recommendation for the customer.
*   **Additional Comments (ID 16)**: Enter any additional information about your recommendation.
