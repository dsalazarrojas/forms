# Physical Therapist Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapist Recommendation Form is designed to collect information from healthcare professionals to support patient care and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the **patient's name** in the first field.
*   Fill in the **date of birth** of the patient in the second field.
*   Add any relevant **medical history** in the third field.
*   Provide **treatment plans** for the patient in the fourth field.
*   Explain the **referral reason** for the patient's visit in the fifth field.
*   Enter the **therapist's name** responsible for the patient's care in the sixth field.
*   Specify the **patient's condition** in the seventh field.
*   Select the **treatment plan status** (Active or Inactive) in the eighth field.
*   Enter any additional **insurance information** for the patient in the ninth field.
*   Add any further **medical history 2** information in the tenth field.
*   Specify the **physical therapy goal** for the patient in the eleventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name**: A field to enter the name of the patient.
    *   **Label: patient name**
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Date of Birth**: A field to input the patient's date of birth.
    *   **Label: date of birth**
    *   **Type: date**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Medical History**: A field to add any relevant medical history information.
    *   **Label: medical_history**
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Treatment Plans**: A field to provide treatment plans for the patient.
    *   **Label: treatment_plans**
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Referral Reason**: A field to explain the reason for the patient's visit.
    *   **Label: referral_reason**
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Therapist Name**: A field to enter the therapist's name responsible for the patient's care.
    *   **Label: therapist_name**
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Patient Condition**: A field to specify the patient's condition.
    *   **Label: patient_condition**
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Treatment Plan Status**: A field to select the status of the treatment plan (Active or Inactive).
    *   **Label: treatment_plan_status**
    *   **Type: select_one**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Insurance Info**: A field to enter any additional insurance information for the patient.
    *   **Label: insurance_info**
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Medical History 2**: A field to add any further medical history information.
    *   **Label: medical_history_2**

    *   **Required: false**

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Use clear and concise language when providing information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the healthcare professional for assistance.
*   This form is designed to be completed by healthcare professionals to support patient care and outcomes.
