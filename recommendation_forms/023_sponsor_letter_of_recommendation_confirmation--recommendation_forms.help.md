# Sponsor Letter Of Recommendation Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Letter Of Recommendation Confirmation form is used to collect and confirm sponsor letters from recommenders, ensuring that they are properly acknowledged and tracked.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address as the sponsor.
2. Enter your title and company name.
3. Select the department you are associated with.
4. Indicate your relationship with the individual submitting the letter (e.g., friend, classmate, teacher, colleague).
5. Choose the type of letter you are submitting (e.g., academic, work reference, personal).
6. Enter the date you submitted the letter.
7. Sign the letter with your handwritten signature.
8. Enter the date you signed the letter.
9. Indicate the status of the letter (e.g., received, not received).
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter your name as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email**: Enter your email address as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Title**: Enter your title or job position.
* **Sponsor Company**: Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Sponsor Department**: Enter the department or team you are associated with.
* **Sponsor Relationship**: Choose how you know the individual submitting the letter (e.g., friend, classmate, teacher, colleague).
* **Sponsor Letter Type**: Select the type of letter you are submitting (e.g., academic, work reference, personal).
* **Sponsor Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted the letter.
* **Sponsor Signature Date**: Enter the date you signed the letter.
* **Sponsor Signature**: Sign the letter with your handwritten signature.
* **Sponsor Letter Received Date**: Enter the date the letter was received.
* **Sponsor Letter Received Status**: Indicate whether the letter was received or not.
* **Submit**: Review all fields and click the "Submit" button to complete the form.


## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting.
* Ensure that the sponsor letter is properly acknowledged and confirmed.
* Keep in mind that this form is for confirmation purposes only; the actual content and authenticity of the sponsor letter must be verified separately.
