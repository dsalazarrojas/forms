# Request A Service Recommendation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to request a service recommendation from our team. It's a simple form that collects basic contact information and a brief description of the service needed. Fill out the form to get a prompt response from our team about the recommended services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field. Please use a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. In the **Service** field, provide a brief description of the service you are looking for.
6. For **Urgency**, select the level of priority you would like us to respond with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is the field to enter your first name.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To capture your first name for communication purposes.
* **Last Name**: This is the field to enter your last name.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To capture your last name for communication purposes.
* **Email**: This is the field to enter your email address.
	+ Type: Email field
	+ Purpose: To enable us to contact you back.
* **Phone**: This is the field to enter your phone number.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide an alternative means of communication.
* **Service**: This is the field to describe the service you are looking for.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To understand your requirements and provide relevant recommendations.
* **Urgency**: This is the field to select the level of priority you would like us to respond with.
	+ Type: Select One field
	+ Options: High, Medium, Low
	+ Purpose: To ensure we respond accordingly to your needs.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form completely for a prompt response.
- Use a valid email address to enable us to contact you.
- If you have any additional information or details to provide, include them in the **Service** field.
