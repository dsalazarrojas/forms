# Musician Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Musician Recommendation Form is designed to help us identify potential musicians that match our school or organization's music programs. The form asks for specific information about the musician, their music school, experience, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all the required fields (marked as required) with accurate information about the musician.
2.  Select relevant options for the musician's music school and instrument (if applicable).
3.  Provide a brief message describing the reasons for your recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Musician Form Input (Text)** (`musician_form_input`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to share about the musician.
* **Music School (Select One)** (`music_school_input`, select_one, optional): Select the music school that the musician attends or has attended. This will help us understand their background and experience.
* **Musician Name (Text)** (`musician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the musician. This is not required but can be helpful in identifying the musician.
* **Music Genre (Select Multiple)** (`music_genre`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the genres of music the musician specializes in. This will help us understand their musical style and abilities.
* **Recommendation Date (Date)** (`recommendation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are recommending the musician for. This can be any date in the past or future.
* **Musician Experience (Text)** (`musician_experience`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the musician's experience and qualifications.
* **Length of Experience (Number)** (`musician_experience_length`, number, optional): Enter the number of years the musician has been playing or performing.
* **Music Instrument (Select One)** (`music_instrument`, select_one, optional): Choose the instrument the musician plays.
* **Recommendation Message (Text)** (`recommendation_message`, text, optional): Enter a brief message explaining why you are recommending this musician and why you think they would be a good fit for our school or organization.

Please note that all fields with "optional" status should still be filled out if you have information to provide.
