# Return To Work Letter - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Return To Work Letter is a form used to document an employee's return to work after an absence. This form helps to ensure a smooth transition for the employee's return, provides a clear record of the absence, and aids in maintaining accurate personnel records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Employee ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the employee returning to work.
2. **Provide the Reason for Absence**: Briefly state the reason for the employee's absence (e.g., illness, family emergency, vacation).
3. **Specify Absence Dates**: Enter the date the employee began their absence and the date they ended their absence.
4. **Provide Additional Reason (Optional)**: If necessary, provide an additional reason for the absence.
5. **Enter Company Information**: Type in your company's name as it appears on the letterhead.
6. **Sign and Date the Form**: Both the employee and supervisor/employer must sign and date the form to confirm its authenticity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID**: <employee_id> (<type>: number, <required>: false) - Enter the unique identifier assigned to the employee.
* **Reason for Absence**: <reason_for_absence> (<type>: text, <required>: false) - Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the employee's absence.
* **Date Absence Began**: <date_absence_began> (<type>: date, <required>: false) - Enter the date the employee's absence began.
* **Date Absence Ended**: <date_absence_ended> (<type>: date, <required>: false) - Enter the date the employee's absence ended.
* **Reason for Absence 2**: <reason_for_absence_2> (<type>: text, <required>: false) - If necessary, provide an additional reason for the absence.
* **Signature**: <signature> (<type>: note, <required>: false) - This field is reserved for any additional comments or notes from the employee.
* **Company Name**: <company_name> (<type>: text, <required>: false) - Type in your company's name as it appears on the letterhead.
* **Employer Signature**: <employer_signature> (<type>: text, <required>: false) - The employer's signature and date confirm the employee's return to work.
* **Supervisor Signature**: <supervisor_signature> (<type>: text, <required>: false) - The supervisor's signature and date confirm the employee's return to work.
* **Date**: <date> (<type>: date, <required>: false) - Enter the current date to confirm the return to work date.
* **Employee Signature**: <employee_signature> (<type>: text, <required>: false) - The employee's signature confirms their return to work.
