# Return To Work Discussion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Return To Work Discussion Form is used for a manager or HR representative to discuss and document an employee's return to work plans after a leave of absence. This form helps ensure that all relevant details and agreements are captured and communicated between the employee and their manager, promoting a smooth transition back to work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Select the relevant pages to fill out based on your needs.
3. Fill out the form with the requested information, making sure to answer all required fields.
4. Submit the form for review and discussion with your manager or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`1`, `number`, required: false): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Date Of Leave** (`2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the employee plans to return to work.
* **Reason For Leave** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the reason for the leave of absence.

## Tips
- Make sure to include all relevant details and agreements in the form to ensure a successful return to work transition.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, please consult with your manager or HR representative.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
