# work_from_home_job_opportunity - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Work From Home Job Opportunity" form is used to collect information about job openings that allow employees to work from home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the job opportunity.
2. Select the category of the job opportunity.
3. Provide a brief description of the job opportunity.
4. Add more information about the job opportunity (optional).
5. Provide a website URL associated with the job opportunity (optional).
6. Add the label for the "Apply Now" button (optional).
7. Enter the phone number for the job opportunity (optional).
8. Enter the email address for the job opportunity (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work From Home Job Opportunity** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the job opportunity.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the job opportunity from the provided options.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the job opportunity.
* **More Information** (`form_description_long`, text, optional): Add more details about the job opportunity.
* **Visit Website** (`form_url`, text, optional): Provide a website URL associated with the job opportunity.
* **Apply Now** (`form_url_label`, text, optional): Enter the label for the "Apply Now" button.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for the job opportunity.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address for the job opportunity.
