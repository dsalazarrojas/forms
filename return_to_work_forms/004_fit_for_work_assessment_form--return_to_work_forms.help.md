# Fit For Work Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fit For Work Assessment Form is designed to assess an employee's readiness to return to work after a period of absence due to medical reasons. This form is intended to gather information about the employee's current health status, availability, and work schedule to ensure a smooth transition back to work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the initial assessment question: "How are you feeling today?" 
2. Describe your medical condition in the "Medical Report" field.
3. Answer the readiness question: "Do you feel ready to return to work?" 
4. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Enter the date and time you plan to return to work in the "Date of Return" and "Time of Return" fields.
6. Indicate your next steps in the "Next Steps" field.
7. Check if you need any support in the "Support" field.
8. Indicate your availability in the "Availability" field.
9. Specify your work frequency in the "Frequency of Work" field.
10. Enter your preferred work time in the "Time of Day" field (optional).
11. Enter the duration of your work in the "Duration" field (optional).
12. Provide any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How are you feeling today?**: Please provide a brief description of your current health status.
  (Text input, required)
- **Medical Report**: Please describe your medical condition and any related details.
  (Text input, required)
- **Do you feel ready to return to work?**: Indicate whether you feel ready to return to work or not.
  (Radio input, options: True, False, required)
- **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
  (Email input, required)
- **When do you plan to return to work?**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
  (Date input, required)
- **At what time do you plan to return to work?**: Enter the time you plan to return to work.
  (Time input, required)
- **Is there anything else you would like to add?**: Please provide any additional comments or information.
  (Text input, optional)
- **Do you need any support?**: Check the options if you need any support.
  (Checkbox input, options: Option A, Option B, required)
- **How frequently do you plan to work?**: Indicate your plan for work frequency.
  (Select input, options: Yes, No, Other, required)
- **What are your availability?**: Select your availability for work.
  (Select Multiple input, options: Yes, No, Other, optional)
- **At what time do you prefer to work?**: Enter your preferred work time.
  (Time input, optional)
- **How long do you plan to work?**: Enter the duration of your work.
  (Number input, optional)
- **Is there anything else you'd like to add?**: Please provide any additional comments.
  (Text input, optional)
