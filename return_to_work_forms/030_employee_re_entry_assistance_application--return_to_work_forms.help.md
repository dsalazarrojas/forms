# Employee Re Entry Assistance Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Re Entry Assistance Application is designed to assist employees who are returning to work after a period of absence. This form helps gather information from the employee and their supervisor to ensure a smooth transition back to work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Return To Work Request**: Enter your return-to-work request in this text field.
2. **Employee Id**: Enter your employee ID number.
3. **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
4. **Supervisor Name**: Enter your supervisor's name as it appears on their identification documents.
5. **Supervisor Signature**: Enter your supervisor's signature.
6. **Date Returning**: Enter the date you are returning to work.
7. **Return Date**: Enter the date you expect to return to work.
8. **Work Hours**: Enter the number of hours you expect to work on your first day back.
9. **Job Title**: Enter your job title as per your employee profile.
10. **Department**: Enter your department as per your employee profile.
11. **Location**: Enter the location where you expect to work.
12. **Work Type**: Select your work type from the options: Office, Remote, or Field.
13. **Shift**: Select one or multiple shifts from the options: Morning, Afternoon, Night, Weekend.
14. **Break Times**: Enter the number of break times you expect to take.
15. **Break Duration**: Enter the duration of each break in minutes.
16. **Break Frequency**: Enter the frequency of breaks per hour.
17. **Break Type**: Enter the type of break you expect to take.
18. **Lunch Break**: Enter the number of lunch breaks you expect to take.
19. **Lunch Duration**: Enter the duration of each lunch break in minutes.
20. **Break Status**: Enter your break status.
21. **Shift Leader**: Enter the name of your shift leader.
22. **Shift Leader Title**: Enter the title of your shift leader.
23. **Employee Status**: Select your current status from the options: Active, Inactive, Leave of Absence, Other.
24. **Reason**: Enter a brief reason for your return to work.
25. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes for your supervisor.
