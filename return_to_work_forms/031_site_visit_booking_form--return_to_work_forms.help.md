# Site Visit Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Visit Booking Form is designed to help users schedule a site visit. This form collects relevant information about the user's site visit, including date, time, location, and other personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your site visit in the `Site Visit Booking` field.
2. Choose the date of your site visit from the calendar in the `Date` field.
3. Select the time of your site visit from the available time slots in the `Time` field.
4. Type the location of your site visit in the `Location` field.
5. Enter your name in the `Name` field.
6. Type your phone number in the `Phone` field.
7. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
8. Provide any additional notes about your site visit in the `Note` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Visit Booking**: Enter a brief title for your site visit.
	+ A short text field to describe the purpose of your site visit.
* **Date**: Choose the date of your site visit using the calendar.
	+ A date field where you can select a date using the calendar.
* **Time**: Select the time of your site visit from the available options.
	+ A time field where you can choose a specific time slot.
* **Location**: Type the location of your site visit.
	+ A text field to describe the address or location of the site visit.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
	+ A text field to enter your personal name.
* **Phone**: Type your phone number.
	+ A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ An email field where you can enter your email address.
* **Note**: Provide any additional notes about your site visit.
	+ A note field where you can add any additional comments or details about your site visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your site visit is successfully booked.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure it is accurate and correct.
* If you have any issues or questions about your site visit, contact the site manager or administrator for assistance.
