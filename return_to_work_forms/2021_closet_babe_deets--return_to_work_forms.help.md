# 2021 Closet Babe Deets - Return to Work Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who are looking to return to work, specifically women who are interested in the Closet Babe program. The form is intended to help users provide details about their current and desired professional styles, wardrobe needs, and preferences for their return-to-work journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please provide your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
2. Enter your email address for program updates and scheduling purposes.
3. Provide your phone number with country code (if applicable).
4. Specify your estimated return-to-work date.
5. Select your current employment status.
6. Choose your target industry/sector.
7. Select your desired role level.
8. Describe your desired professional image.
9. Select all the style and career goals you'd like to achieve through the Closet Babe program.
10. Choose how you would describe your current professional style.
11. Specify how you'd like to be perceived professionally.
12. Select all the wardrobe areas that need the most attention.
13. Share any specific colors or styles you prefer or dislike.
14. Provide your preferred clothing size system or general size range (if applicable).
15. Indicate your comfort level with wearing heels for work.
16. Choose your estimated budget range for new wardrobe pieces.
17. Select your preferred consultation format (virtual or in-person).
18. Choose which days of the week are best for a consultation (select all).
19. Provide any additional comments or requests.
20. Finally, specify how you heard about Closet Babe Deets.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please provide your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for program updates and scheduling purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): Please provide your phone number with country code (if applicable).
* **Estimated Return-to-Work Date** (`return_to_work_date`, `date`, required): Specify when you plan to re-enter the professional environment.
* **Current Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current situation.
* **Target Industry/Sector** (`target_industry`, `text`, required): Choose your target industry/sector (e.g., Tech, Healthcare, Finance, Arts, Education).
* **Desired Role Level** (`target_role_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of position you're aiming for.
* **Desired Professional Image** (`desired_professional_image`, `text`, required): Describe how you'd like to be perceived professionally (e.g., Authoritative, Approachable, Innovative, Trustworthy).
* **Style and Career Goals** (`program_expectations`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the style and career goals you'd like to achieve through the Closet Babe program.
* **Current Professional Style** (`current_professional_style`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would describe your current professional style.
* **Wardrobe Areas** (`wardrobe_focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the wardrobe areas that need the most attention.
* **Color Palette** (`preferred_color_palette`, `text`, optional): Share any specific colors or styles you prefer or dislike.
* **Clothing Size** (`clothing_size_preference`, `text`, optional): Provide your preferred clothing size system or general size range (if applicable).
* **Comfort Level with Heels** (`comfort_level_heels`, `select_one`, required): Select how comfortable you are with wearing heels for work.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_estimation`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your estimated budget range for new wardrobe pieces.
* **Consultation Format** (`consultation_format_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred consultation format (virtual or in-person).
* **Available Days** (`preferred_consultation_days`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose which days of the week are best for a consultation (select all).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests.
* **How Heard About Closet Babe Deets** (`how_heard_about_us`, `text`, optional): Finally, specify how you heard about Closet Babe Deets.
