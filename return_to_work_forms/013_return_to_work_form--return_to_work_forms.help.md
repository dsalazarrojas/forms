# Return To Work Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Return To Work Form is used to report an employee's return to work after an absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date you returned to work in the "Date Returned To Work" field.
2. Enter your start time in the "Shift Start Time" field.
3. Enter your end time in the "Shift End Time" field.
4. Enter the reason you returned to work in the "Reason Returned To Work" field.
5. Review all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date Returned To Work**: Enter the date you actually returned to work.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time you started your shift.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the time you ended your shift.
* **Reason Returned To Work**: Enter a brief explanation of why you are returning to work.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have already completed a return to work form for the same date, do not fill out this form again.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
