# Return To Work Anxiety Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Return To Work Anxiety Self Assessment is a form designed to help employees gauge their anxiety levels and concerns about returning to work. This form will aid in identifying potential areas of support and comfort for the employee's return.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, and to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple select questions, select all that apply.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as you feel necessary.
4. If you're unsure about any question, don't worry, and just provide your best answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (1): This is a text field where you'll provide your name for contact purposes.
* **What do you feel about returning to work** (2): This question is designed to understand your general sentiment about returning to work.
* **Are you feeling anxious about returning to work** (3): Choose 'Yes' if you're feeling anxious, 'No' otherwise.
* **How anxious are you about returning to work** (4): Choose a number (1-10) that represents your level of anxiety.
* **What are your concerns about returning to work** (5): This is a multiple select question where you can choose all that apply from the options.
* **Is your anxiety due to a previous traumatic event** (6): If you've experienced a traumatic event, please choose 'Yes' if you think it's contributing to your anxiety.
* **Do you have a support plan in place** (7): Choose 'Yes' if you have a support plan, 'No' otherwise.
* **Have you talked to anyone about returning to work** (8): Select 'Yes' if you've discussed your concerns with someone.
* **Would you like to have a support plan in place** (9): Choose 'Yes' if you'd like support.
* **What kind of support do you want** (10): This is a multiple select question where you can choose all that apply.
* **How long will it take to get used to being back at work** (11): Provide the duration you think it'll take to adjust.
* **How long have you been away from the workplace** (12): Provide the duration of time you've been away.
* **Have you been away from the workplace due to anxiety** (13): Choose 'Yes' if your absence is related to anxiety.
* **Have you been away from the workplace for an extended period** (14): Choose 'Yes' if you've been away for a longer than usual period.
* **How would you rate your overall anxiety level** (15): Choose a number (1-10) that represents your current anxiety level.
* **Has your anxiety been diagnosed by a mental health professional** (16): If you've received a diagnosis, choose 'Yes', 'No' otherwise.
* **Have you sought support from colleagues** (17): Choose 'Yes' if you've received support from colleagues.
* **Have you sought support from management** (18): Choose 'Yes' if you've received support from management.
* **Have you sought support from HR** (19): Choose 'Yes' if you've received support from HR.
* **Have you sought support from an EAP (Employee Assistance Program)** (20): Choose 'Yes' if you've received support from an EAP.
* **Have you been diagnosed with any mental health condition** (21): If you have a diagnosis, choose 'Yes', 'No' otherwise.
* **What would make you feel more comfortable returning to work** (22): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or concerns.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to add about returning to work** (23): This is a text field for any additional comments or concerns.
* **How can we contact you** (24): Please provide your email address for further communication.
* **Do you have any other concerns about returning to work** (25): This is a text field for any additional comments or concerns.
