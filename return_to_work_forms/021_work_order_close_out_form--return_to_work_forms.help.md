# Work Order Close Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Order Close Out Form is used to document the completion of a work order, providing details on the close out date, reason, and costs associated with the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information to fill out the form accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information it collects.
3. Enter the correct information in each field, and save your progress as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Order Close Out Form Data (<id: 1, type: text>):** Enter a brief description or details about the work order being closed out.
* **Close Out Date (<id: 2, type: date>):** Enter the date when the work order was completed or closed out.
* **Close Out Reason (<id: 3, type: text>):** Enter the reason for closing out the work order.
* **Cost of Work (<id: 4, type: number>):** Enter the total cost associated with the work order.
* **Close Out Comments (<id: 5, type: note>):** Enter any additional comments or notes about the work order.
* **Hours Worked (<id: 6, type: number>):** Enter the total hours worked on the project.
* **Job Completion Status (<id: 7, type: select_one>):** Select the status of the job completion: Completed or Not Completed.
* **Materials Used (<id: 8, type: text>):** Enter a list or description of materials used in the project.
* **Total Cost (<id: 9, type: number>):** Enter the total cost of the project.
