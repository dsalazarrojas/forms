# Prescription Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare professionals to request a prescription for a patient. It gathers information about the patient, doctor, and medicine for prescription processing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information for the corresponding category.
2. Make sure all required fields are filled out.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.

### 2. **Patient phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the patient's contact phone number.

### 3. **Patient address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the patient's home or billing address.

### 4. **Doctor name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the doctor who prescribed the medicine.

### 5. **Medicine name** (`medicine_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the medicine being prescribed.

### 6. **Medicine dosage** (`medicine_dosage`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the dosage instructions and quantity for the medicine.

### 7. **Patient age** (`patient_age`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the patient's age.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
- This form is intended for internal use only and should not be shared with patients or unauthorized personnel.
