# Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Fulfillment Accuracy Report Form is used to report and track the accuracy of prescriptions fulfilled by pharmacies. This form helps to ensure that prescriptions are accurately filled, verified, and documented, and any issues that arise during the fulfillment process are addressed and resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the location from where the prescription was fulfilled.
2. Enter the date of submission.
3. Enter the prescription ID number.
4. Enter the patient's name.
5. Enter the pharmacist's name.
6. Select the fulfillment status.
7. Enter the accuracy rating (on a scale of 1-3).
8. Enter any comments or notes related to the fulfillment process.
9. Enter any actions taken to address any issues.
10. Enter the name of the person who took the action.
11. Enter the date the action was taken.
12. Enter any comments from the location manager.
13. Enter any comments from the regional manager.
14. Enter any comments from the regional director.
15. Select the accuracy rating scale (Option 1, 2, or 3).
16. Enter any improvement actions taken.
17. Enter the follow-up date.
18. Enter any follow-up comments.
19. Select the follow-up status.
20. Enter the name of the person who performed the follow-up.
21. Enter any follow-up comments from the location.
22. Enter any follow-up comments from the regional director.
23. Enter the name of the regional director who performed the follow-up.
24. Enter any follow-up comments from the regional manager.
25. Enter the name of the regional manager who performed the follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location** (2, Select one, required/optional): Select the location where the prescription was fulfilled. This is used to track the fulfillment process across different locations.
* **Date of Submission** (3, Date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Prescription ID** (4, Number, required/optional): Enter the ID number of the prescription that was fulfilled.
* **Patient Name** (5, Text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Pharmacist Name** (6, Text, required/optional): Enter the name of the pharmacist who filled the prescription.
* **Fulfillment Status** (7, Select one, required/optional): Select the status of the fulfillment process (e.g. "Complete", "Incomplete", "Disputed").
* **Accuracy Rating** (8, Number, required/optional): Rate the accuracy of the fulfillment process on a scale of 1-3.
* **Comments** (9, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes related to the fulfillment process.
* **Action Taken** (10, Text, required/optional): Enter any actions taken to address any issues that arose during the fulfillment process.
* **Action Taken by** (11, Text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who took the action.
* **Action Taken Date** (12, Date, required/optional): Enter the date the action was taken.
* **Location Comments** (13, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the location manager.
* **Pharmacist Comments** (14, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the pharmacist.
* **Location Manager Comments** (15, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the regional manager.
* **Regional Manager Comments** (16, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the regional director.
* **Regional Director Comments** (17, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the regional director.
* **Accuracy Rating Scale** (18, Select one, required/optional): Select the accuracy rating scale to be used.
* **Improvement Actions** (19, Text, required/optional): Enter any improvement actions taken.
* **Follow-up Date** (20, Date, required/optional): Enter the date of the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (21, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes related to the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Status** (22, Select one, required/optional): Select the status of the follow-up process (e.g. "Complete", "Incomplete", "Disputed").
* **Follow-up by** (23, Text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who performed the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (24, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the location.
* **Follow-up Location** (25, Text, required/optional): Enter the location where the follow-up took place.
* **Follow-up Pharmacist** (26, Text, required/optional): Enter the name of the pharmacist who performed the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Pharmacist Comments** (27, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the follow-up pharmacist.
* **Follow-up Regional Director Comments** (28, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the regional director.
* **Follow-up Regional Manager Comments** (29, Text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the regional manager.
* **Follow-up Regional Manager** (30, Text, required/optional): Enter the name of the regional manager who performed the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Regional Director** (31, Text, required/optional): Enter the name of the regional director who performed the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Regional Director Name** (32, Text, required/optional): Enter the name of the regional director.
