# Pharmacy Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to patient medication orders, allowing pharmacists to gather necessary details and ensure accurate communication with patients and doctors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Information**: Please enter the patient's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. **Patient Medication History**: Describe the patient's past medical history, including any allergies, sensitivities, or previous medical conditions that may impact treatment.
3. **Medication Order**: Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are ordering medication or not.
4. **Medical Condition**: Provide a brief description of the patient's current medical condition.
5. **Treatment Option**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have chosen a treatment option for the patient.
6. **Dosing Instructions**: Provide any special instructions for the patient's medication dosing.
7. **Patient Signature**: Have the patient sign the form to acknowledge they have reviewed and agree to the information provided.
8. **Doctor Signature**: Have the doctor sign the form to confirm they have ordered the medication and reviewed the information.
9. **Pharmacy Signature**: Have the pharmacist sign the form to confirm they have received and will fulfill the order.
10. **Pharmacy Name**: Enter the name of the pharmacy where the medication will be dispensed.
11. **Date**: Enter the date on which the order was received.
12. **Time**: Enter the time on which the order was received.
13. **Medication**: Provide the name of the medication being ordered.
14. **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of medication needed.
15. **Instructions**: Provide any additional instructions for the medication.
16. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient or order.
17. **Pharmacy Name 2**: Enter an alternate name for the pharmacy.
18. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the pharmacy.
19. **Email**: Enter the email address of the pharmacy.
20. **Address**: Enter the address of the pharmacy.
21. **Doctor**: Enter the name of the doctor who ordered the medication.
22. **Doctor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the doctor.
23. **Doctor Email**: Enter the email address of the doctor.
24. **Doctor Address**: Enter the address of the doctor.
25. **Date of Last Visit**: Enter the date of the patient's last visit.
