## COVID 19 Vaccine Pre Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients for pre-registration for COVID-19 vaccination. It will help us to understand the patient's details and preferences for vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Gender.
2. Provide your Email Address and Phone Number so that we can contact you for appointment confirmation and updates.
3. Enter your address details such as Street Address, City, and ZIP Code.
4. Specify your COVID-19 vaccination status, vaccine type preference, and which dose you are seeking.
5. If you have any previous vaccination history, enter the date of your last vaccine.
6. Provide your insurance information, if applicable.
7. Mention any known allergies or serious medical conditions that might affect your vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
• **First Name** (text): Please enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.

### Last Name
• **Last Name** (text): Please enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.

### Date of Birth
• **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Gender
• **Gender** (select one): Choose your gender from the options provided.

### Email Address
• **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address for appointment confirmation and updates.

### Phone Number
• **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number for appointment confirmation and updates.

### Street Address
• **Street Address** (text): Enter your street address where you can be reached for vaccination appointment.

### City
• **City** (text): Enter the city where you live.

### State
• **State** (text): Enter the state where you live.

### ZIP Code
• **ZIP Code** (text): Enter your ZIP code.

### COVID-19 Vaccination Status
• **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (select one): Choose your vaccination status from the options provided.

### Vaccine Type Preference
• **Vaccine Type Preference** (select one): Choose your preferred vaccine type from the options provided. If you don't have a preference, please select "No preference".

### Which Dose Are You Seeking
• **Which Dose Are You Seeking** (select one): Choose which dose you are seeking.

### Date of Last Vaccine
• **Date of Last Vaccine** (date): If you have had a previous vaccine, enter the date of your last vaccine.

### Insurance Provider
• **Insurance Provider** (text): If you have insurance, enter the name of your provider.

### Insurance ID Number
• **Insurance ID Number** (text): If you have insurance, enter your ID number.

### Known Allergies
• **Known Allergies** (text): Mention any known allergies, especially to vaccine components.

### Serious Medical Conditions
• **Serious Medical Conditions** (text): Mention any serious medical conditions that might affect vaccination.

### Current Medications
• **Current Medications** (text): Mention any current medications you are taking.

### Pregnant or Breastfeeding
• **Pregnant or Breastfeeding** (select one): Choose whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

Note: Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
