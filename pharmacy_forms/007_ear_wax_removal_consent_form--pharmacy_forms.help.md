# Ear Wax Removal Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ear Wax Removal Consent Form is a document that patients need to sign to indicate their consent for an ear wax removal procedure. This form helps to obtain informed consent from patients before performing the procedure, ensuring they understand the risks and benefits involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Sign the form to indicate that you have read, understood, and agreed to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required: false)
* This section is for collecting basic patient information, such as their name, date of birth, and contact details. Please provide your name, address, and contact number to help us contact you after the procedure.

### **Consent To Procedure** (`consent_to_procedure`, select_one, required: false)
* Please select 'Yes' to indicate that you consent to the ear wax removal procedure. If you select 'No', please explain your reason for declining the procedure.

### **Medical Concerns** (`medical_concerns`, text, required: false)
* Describe any medical concerns or conditions that may affect your ability to undergo the ear wax removal procedure. This includes any allergies, previous medical conditions, or medications that may impact the procedure.
