# Simple Prescription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Simple Prescription Form is designed to collect patient information, medical history, and other relevant details for prescribing medications. It is intended for healthcare professionals to gather necessary information in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Info" section.
3. For the "Medication" field, select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient is taking any medications.
4. In the "Patient History" field, select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient has any relevant medical history.
5. In the "Medical Notes" field, enter any additional medical notes or comments you want to include.
6. Enter the pharmacy's information in the "Pharmacy Info" field.
7. Enter the prescription details in the "Prescription" field.
8. Add your signature to the form using the "Signature" field.
9. Enter your name and any other relevant information in the "Submitted By" field.
10. Enter the time and date of submission in the "Submitted At" field.
11. Finally, enter any additional information about the person who submitted the form in the "Submitted By Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (patient_info, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Medication** (medication, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the patient is taking any medications, 'No' otherwise.
* **Doctor Info** (doctor_info, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's name and contact information.
* **Patient History** (patient_history, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the patient has any relevant medical history, 'No' otherwise.
* **Medical Notes** (medical_notes, text, required/optional): Enter any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Pharmacy Info** (pharmacy_info, text, required/optional): Enter the pharmacy's name, address, and contact information.
* **Prescription** (prescription, text, required/optional): Enter the prescription details, including the medication name, dosage, and duration.
* **Signature** (signature, date, required/optional): Add your signature to the form to confirm you have reviewed and approved the information.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Submitted At** (submitted_at, time, required/optional): Enter the time and date of submission.
* **Submitted By Info** (submitted_by_info, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the person who submitted the form.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use clear and concise language when entering information.
* Use your professional judgment when evaluating patient information and making medical decisions.
