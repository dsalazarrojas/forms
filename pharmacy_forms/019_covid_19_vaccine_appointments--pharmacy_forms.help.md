# COVID 19 Vaccine Appointments - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Vaccine Appointments form is used to gather information from individuals and groups to schedule vaccine appointments. This form helps to organize appointments, reduce vaccine waste, and ensure that those who need the vaccine can get it on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your full name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select a preferred pharmacy location for your appointment.
3. Choose your preferred appointment date and time.
4. Indicate the number of people requesting the appointment.
5. Select the phase group you belong to for vaccination.
6. Choose your preferred type of vaccine.
7. If you are including additional people in your appointment, fill out their information accordingly.
8. Answer questions about your current health status, including any recent COVID-19 symptoms or vaccine allergies.
9. Acknowledge that all the provided information is accurate.
