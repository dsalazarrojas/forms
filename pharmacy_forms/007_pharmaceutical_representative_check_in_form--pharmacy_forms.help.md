# Pharmaceutical Representative Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for pharmaceutical representatives to check in when arriving at a meeting or event. It captures basic information for identification and contact purposes. The form helps the event staff to track who has arrived, making it easier to manage the event and provide a smooth experience for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and any other required information (e.g., Date, Time, Check In Time).
2. Choose your Region (North, South, East, West) from the available options.
3. Add your Rep Number and Phone and Email if applicable.
4. Review your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or identification.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event or meeting you are attending.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you arrive at the event or meeting.
- **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, `time`, required): Enter the exact time you checked in.
- **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or identification.
- **Region** (`region`, `select_one`, required): Choose your regional affiliation from the options provided (North, South, East, West).
- **Territory** (`territory`, `text`, optional): Enter your specific territory or area of responsibility.
- **Rep Number** (`rep_number`, `text`, required): Enter your representative number or ID provided by your company.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the information before submission.
- If you have any trouble filling out this form, please contact the event staff for assistance.
