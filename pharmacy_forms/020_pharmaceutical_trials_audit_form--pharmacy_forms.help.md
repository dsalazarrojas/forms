# Pharmaceutical Trials Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Trials Audit Form is a tool used to document site visits and audits conducted on pharmaceutical trials. It helps to ensure that trials are conducted according to established guidelines and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Trial ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the trial, if applicable.
2. **Date of Visit**: Enter the start and end dates of the site visit.
3. **Auditor's Name**: Enter your name as the auditor of this trial site visit.
4. **Auditor's Organization**: Enter your organization or affiliation as the auditor.
5. **Auditor's Title**: Enter your title or position as the auditor.
6. **Auditor's Department**: Enter your department or office as the auditor.
7. **Auditor's Phone**: Enter your contact phone number as the auditor.
8. **Auditor's Email**: Enter your contact email as the auditor.
9. **Date of Visit Start**: Enter the start date of the site visit.
10. **Date of Visit End**: Enter the end date of the site visit.
11. **Site Visit Duration**: Enter the duration of the site visit in minutes.
12. **Site Visit Type**: Select the type of site visit (On Site, Off Site, or Remote).
13. **Site Visit Status**: Select the status of the site visit (On Site, Off Site, or Remote).
14. **Findings**: Select whether any findings or issues were noted during the site visit (Yes or No).
15. **Findings Details**: Enter a brief description of any findings or issues noted during the site visit.
