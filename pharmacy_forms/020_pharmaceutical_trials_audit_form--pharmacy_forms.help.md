# Pharmaceutical Trials Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Pharmaceutical Trials Audit Form is a tool used to document site visits and audits conducted on pharmaceutical trials.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Follow these steps to complete the form:

2. Fill in the required fields as prompted by your organization.
3. Make sure to enter accurate and clear information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Trial ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the trial.
*   **Date of Visit**: Enter the start and end dates of the site visit.
*   **Auditor's Name**: Enter your name as the auditor.
*   **Auditor's Organization**: Enter your organization or affiliation.
*   **Auditor's Title**: Enter your title or position.
*   **Auditor's Department**: Enter your department or office.
*   **Auditor's Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Auditor's Email**: Enter your contact email.
*   **Date of Visit Start**: Enter the start date of the site visit.
*   **Date of Visit End**: Enter the end date of the site visit.
*   **Site Visit Duration**: Enter the duration of the site visit in minutes.
*   **Site Visit Type**: Select the type of site visit.
*   **Site Visit Status**: Select the status of the site visit.
*   **Findings**: Select whether any findings or issues were noted during the site visit.
*   **Findings Details**: Enter a brief description of any findings or issues noted during the site visit.
