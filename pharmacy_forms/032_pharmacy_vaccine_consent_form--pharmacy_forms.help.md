# Pharmacy Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy Vaccine Consent Form is a document that captures a patient's consent for vaccination, ensuring they are aware of the risks and benefits of the vaccine and providing an opportunity for them to share any relevant medical history.
 
## How To Complete This Form
1. The patient or their guardian/parent should complete this form in the presence of a medical practitioner.
2. The form should be read and understood by the patient before signing it.
3. The patient should sign the form after understanding and agreeing to its contents.
4. This form should only be used for patients who have not previously completed a vaccination consent form for the same vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, not required): Enter your name and contact information, ensuring that it's up-to-date and accurate.
 
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, not required): Describe any medical conditions or allergies you have that may be relevant to the administration of the vaccine.
 
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required): List any allergies you have, including but not limited to:
  • Allergic to penicillin
  • Allergic to amoxicillin
* 
* **Vaccine Information** (`vaccine_info`, text, not required): Provide any other relevant information about the vaccine, such as previous experiences or reactions.
 
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you received the vaccine or the date you are scheduled to receive it.
 
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, text, not required): Specify the location where you received or will receive the vaccine.
 
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, not required): Sign the form to indicate that you understand and agree to its contents.
 
* **Medical Practitioner** (`medical_practitioner`, text, not required): Enter the name and title of the medical practitioner who provided you with the vaccine.
 
Please note that some fields are not required but are recommended for providing additional context and information.
