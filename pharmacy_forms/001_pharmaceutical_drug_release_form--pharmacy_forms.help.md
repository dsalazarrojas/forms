# Pharmaceutical Drug Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the release of a pharmaceutical drug to a patient. It captures essential information about the patient, medication, and release details to ensure accurate and safe drug administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the patient information section, which includes selecting whether the patient is "Yes" or "No" to the question.
2.  Next, fill out the release information section, where you select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" to indicate the type of release.
3.  The medication information section is where you enter the required information about the medication, including the "Pharmacist Name", "Pharmacist Phone", and "Pharmacist Email".
4.  In the clinic information section, enter the "Clinic Name" and "Clinic Phone" if applicable.
5.  The lab information section is for additional details about the lab where the drug is to be released.
6.  Enter the distributor's information in the distributor information section, including "Distributor Name", "Distributor Phone", and "Distributor Email".
7.  Add any relevant "Release Notes" in the notes section.
8.  Finally, sign the form by selecting "Yes" in the "Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Header** (`form_header`, `text`, not required): This is the header of the form where you can enter any additional information.
*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient has any information provided.
*   **Release Information** (`release_info`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" to indicate the type of release.
*   **Medication Information** (`medication_info`, `number`, not required): Enter the required information about the medication.
*   **Pharmacy Information** (`pharmacy_info`, `text`, not required): Enter any details about the pharmacy.
*   **Clinic Information** (`clinic_info`, `text`, not required): Enter any details about the clinic.
*   **Lab Information** (`lab_info`, `text`, not required): Enter any details about the lab.
*   **Distributor Information** (`distributor_info`, `text`, not required): Enter any details about the distributor.
*   **Release Notes** (`release_notes`, `note`, not required): Add any additional release notes.
*   **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, `time`, not required): This is not a relevant field for this form.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Yes" to sign the form.
*   **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient has a phone number.
*   **Clinic Phone** (`clinic_phone`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the clinic has a phone number.
*   **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of release.
*   **Release Time** (`release_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of release.
*   **Release By** (`release_by`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the person releasing the drug.
*   **Release To** (`release_to`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the person receiving the drug.
*   **Pharmacist Name** (`pharmacist_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the pharmacist.
*   **Pharmacist Phone** (`pharmacist_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the pharmacist.
*   **Pharmacist Email** (`pharmacist_email`, `text`, not required): Enter the email address of the pharmacist.
*   **Prescriber Name** (`prescriber_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the prescriber.
*   **Prescriber Phone** (`prescriber_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the prescriber.
*   **Prescriber Email** (`prescriber_email`, `text`, not required): Enter the email address of the prescriber.
*   **Prescriber Address** (`prescriber_address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of the prescriber.
*   **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional notes about the release.

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*   Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy and consistency.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
