# Immunization Consent Form CPESN Pharmacy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immunization Consent Form CPESN Pharmacy is used to collect patient information, schedule an appointment, and obtain consent for immunization. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth immunization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient Information**: Fill in the patient's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
2. **Provide Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number for communication purposes.
3. **Enter Email**: Enter the patient's email address for communication and follow-up.
4. **Provide Medical Details**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies that may affect the immunization process.
5. **Schedule Appointment**: Choose a date and time for the immunization appointment.
6. **Read and Consent**: Carefully read the immunization terms and provide consent by filling in the designated field.
7. **Choose Payment Preference**: Select the preferred method of payment for the immunization process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (Patient Info)** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact number for communication purposes.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Medical Details (Medical Details)** (`medical_details`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions or allergies that may affect the immunization process.
* **Schedule Appointment (Schedule Appointment)** (`schedule_appointment`, date, required): Choose a date and time for the immunization appointment.
* **Immunization Terms (Immunization Terms)** (`immunization_terms`, text, required): Carefully read the immunization terms and provide consent by filling in this field.
* **Payment Preference (Payment Preference)** (`payment_preference`, select_one, required): Select the preferred method of payment for the immunization process.
* **Consent (Consent)** (`consent`, text, required): Provide consent by filling in this field after carefully reading the immunization terms.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the immunization terms carefully before providing consent.
* Choose a suitable payment method that works for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the pharmacy staff for assistance.
