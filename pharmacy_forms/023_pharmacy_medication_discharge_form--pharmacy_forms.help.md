# Pharmacy Medication Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be used in a healthcare setting to obtain information from a patient before discharging them from the pharmacy. The form aims to capture relevant details about the patient's medical history, medication regimen, and any allergies or sensitivities they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and medical record number. This is the patient's identifying data that will help the pharmacy staff to access their medical records.
2.  Provide details about the patient's current medication regimen, including the name of the medication, dosage, and frequency of administration. This will help the pharmacy staff to update the patient's medical records and ensure that they are receiving the correct medication.
3.  List any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have. This is crucial information that will help the pharmacy staff to avoid any potential risks or adverse reactions.
4.  Complete the pharmacy information section by providing the patient's contact information, including their address and phone number. This will help the pharmacy staff to contact the patient if any issues arise during or after discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): This field should be filled in with the patient's name, date of birth, and medical record number. This information will help the pharmacy staff to access the patient's medical records and ensure that they are receiving the correct medication.
*   **Medication Info** (`medication_info`, text, required: false): In this field, please provide details about the patient's current medication regimen, including the name of the medication, dosage, and frequency of administration. This will help the pharmacy staff to update the patient's medical records and ensure that they are receiving the correct medication.
*   **Dosage Info** (`dosage_info`, text, required: false): This field should be filled in with the details of the patient's medication dosage, including the frequency of administration and any specific instructions from the doctor.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have. This is crucial information that will help the pharmacy staff to avoid any potential risks or adverse reactions.
*   **Pharmacy Info** (`pharmacy_info`, text, required: false): In this field, please provide the patient's contact information, including their address and phone number. This will help the pharmacy staff to contact the patient if any issues arise during or after discharge.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check the patient's information for any errors or discrepancies.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to ask the patient or the pharmacy staff for clarification.
