# restricted_antibiotic_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The restricted antibiotic request form is used to collect information from patients and healthcare staff for requesting restricted antibiotics. This form ensures that the necessary approvals and checks are in place before dispensing these medications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's ID number.
2. Provide the patient's name.
3. Enter the patient's phone number.
4. Enter the reason for the request.
5. Enter any relevant medical history.
6. Enter the doctor's name.
7. Select the doctor's title.
8. Enter the doctor's phone number.
9. Enter the name and phone number of the approver.
10. Enter the name and email of the reviewer.
11. Enter the pharmacy name.
12. Enter the pharmacy's phone number.
13. Enter the date and time of the request.
14. Enter the route to the pharmacy.
15. (Optional) Provide any additional information.
16. Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID** (`Patient ID`, `number`, required): Enter the patient's ID number, which is necessary for identifying the patient's information.
* **Patient Name** (`patient name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Phone** (`Patient Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Reason for Request** (`Reason for request`, `text`, required): Enter a brief explanation for the request.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant medical history for the patient, if applicable.
* **Doctor Name** (`Doctor Name`, `text`, required): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor Title** (`Doctor Title`, `select_one`, required): Select the doctor's title (e.g., Doctor, Resident, Fellow).
* **Doctor Phone** (`Doctor Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Approver Name** (`Approver Name`, `text`, required): Enter the approver's name.
* **Approver Phone** (`Approver Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approver Email** (`Approver Email`, `email`, required): Enter the approver's email address.
* **Reviewer Name** (`Reviewer Name`, `text`, required): Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`Reviewer Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the reviewer's phone number.
* **Reviewer Email** (`Reviewer Email`, `email`, required): Enter the reviewer's email address.
* **Pharmacy Name** (`Pharmacy Name`, `text`, required): Enter the pharmacy's name.
* **Pharmacy Phone** (`Pharmacy Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the pharmacy's phone number.
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the request.
* **Request Time** (`Request Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the request.
* **Route To Pharmacy** (`Route To Pharmacy`, `text`, required): Enter the route to the pharmacy.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the request.
* **Submit Button** (`Submit Button`, `text`, optional): Click to submit the form.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, `text`, optional): Enter the form ID, if required.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `text`, optional): Enter the assigned tool, if required.
