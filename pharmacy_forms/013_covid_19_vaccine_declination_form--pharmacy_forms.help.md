# COVID 19 Vaccine Declination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who wish to decline the COVID-19 vaccine. It is designed to gather information about their reasons for declining the vaccine and ensure they understand the risks and benefits associated with vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow the sections as they appear on screen.
*   Enter your personal details in the corresponding fields.
*   Select your choice of declination from the options provided.
*   Provide detailed information about your reason for declination.
*   Confirm your understanding of the risks and benefits of vaccination.
*   Sign and date the form to acknowledge your declination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`declination_first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`declination_last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`declination_dob`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
*   **Email Address** (`declination_email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further information.
*   **Phone Number** (`declination_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Current Address** (`declination_address`, text, **optional**): Enter your current address.
*   **I Decline the COVID-19 Vaccine at This Time** (`declination_statement`, select_one, **required**): Select the reason for declining the vaccine from the provided options.
*   **Reason for Declining the COVID-19 Vaccine** (`reason_for_declination`, select_one, **required**): Select a reason from the provided options.
*   **Please Explain Your Reason in Detail** (`detailed_reason`, text, **optional**): Provide more information about your reason for declination.
*   **I Understand the Risks of Not Vaccinating** (`understand_risks`, select_one, **required**): Confirm your understanding of the risks associated with not vaccinating.
*   **I Have Received COVID-19 Vaccine Information** (`received_information`, select_one, **required**): Confirm whether you have received information about the vaccine.
*   **I Understand the Benefits of Vaccination** (`understand_benefits`, select_one, **required**): Confirm your understanding of the benefits of vaccination.
*   **Would You Reconsider Vaccination in the Future** (`future_vaccination`, select_one, **optional**): Indicate your willingness to consider vaccination in the future.
*   **Known Medical Contraindications** (`known_contraindications`, text, **optional**): Enter any known medical conditions that may affect your vaccination decision.
*   **Previous Adverse Vaccine Reactions** (`previous_adverse_reactions`, text, **optional**): Enter any previous adverse reactions to vaccines.
*   **Current Health Conditions** (`current_health_conditions`, text, **optional**): Enter any current health conditions that may affect your vaccination decision.
*   **I Acknowledge I am Declining the COVID-19 Vaccine** (`acknowledge_declination`, select_one, **required**): Confirm your declination and accept full responsibility.
*   **Healthcare Provider Signature Required** (`healthcare_provider_signature_required`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of your healthcare provider if you have received counseling from them.
*   **Date of Declination** (`declination_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of this declination form.
*   **I Have Read and Acknowledge the Information** (`acknowledge_declination`, select_one, **required**): Confirm that you have read and understand the information on this form.
*   **I Acknowledge and Accept Full Responsibility** (`acknowledge_declination`, select_one, **required**): Confirm that you understand the risks and benefits of not vaccinating.
*   **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, text, **optional**): Enter the signature of your healthcare provider if required.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
*   Enter your contact information so that we can follow up on your declination.
*   Select the correct options for each field to ensure that your declination is processed correctly.
*   Review each section carefully before signing and dating the form.
