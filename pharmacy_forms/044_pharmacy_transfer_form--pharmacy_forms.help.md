# Pharmacy Transfer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pharmacy Transfer Form is a document used to collect and document essential information related to the transfer of patients from one pharmacy to another. This form ensures a seamless transition of care and helps healthcare professionals manage patient records effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" page.
2. Provide details on the transfer information in the "Transfer Information" page.
3. List any medications that the patient is taking in the "Medication List" page.
4. Enter the pharmacist's information in the "Pharmacist Information" page.
5. Add any additional notes about the transfer in the "Notes" page.
6. Specify the date and time of the transfer in the "Date of Transfer" and "Time of Transfer" pages.
7. Enter the patient's email address and phone number in the "Email Address" and "Phone Number" pages, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, address, and any other relevant details as needed.
- **Transfer Information** (`Transfer Information`, text, required/optional): Describe the reason for the transfer and any other relevant details.
- **Medication List** (`Medication List`, select multiple, required/optional): Select all medications the patient is currently taking.
- **Pharmacist Information** (`Pharmacist Information`, text, required/optional): Enter the name, contact number, and address of the pharmacist transferring the patient.
- **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any important notes or comments about the transfer.
- **Date of Transfer** (`Date of Transfer`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the transfer is scheduled to take place.
- **Time of Transfer** (`Time of Transfer`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the transfer is scheduled to take place.
- **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
- **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
- Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
- Use this form only when a patient's transfer is confirmed.
