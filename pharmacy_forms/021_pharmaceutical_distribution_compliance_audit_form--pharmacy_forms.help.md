# Pharmaceutical Distribution Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Distribution Compliance Audit Form is used to ensure that all pharmacy branches within a district are complying with the company's policies and procedures related to pharmaceutical distribution.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. **Distributor Information**: Enter the name of the pharmacy branch being audited.
2. **Address**: Enter the address of the pharmacy branch being audited.
3. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the pharmacy branch being audited.
4. **Email**: Enter the email address of the pharmacy branch being audited.
5. **Audit Results**: Describe the results of the audit, including any findings or observations.
6. **Operational Practices**: Describe any operational practices or procedures that were observed during the audit.
7. **Follow-up**: Describe any follow-up actions or recommendations for the pharmacy branch.
8. **Review Date**: Enter the date the audit was reviewed or completed.
9. **Review Time**: Enter the time the audit was reviewed or completed.
10. **Review By**: Enter the name of the person or team that reviewed the audit.
11. **Next Actions**: Describe any next actions or steps required for the pharmacy branch to improve their compliance status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. form_header**: This field is a form header that provides a brief introduction to the form. (Note: This field is not a required field and is for informational purposes only.)
* **2. distributor_info**: Enter the name of the pharmacy branch being audited. (Required)
* **3. address**: Enter the address of the pharmacy branch being audited. (Required)
* **4. phone**: Enter the phone number of the pharmacy branch being audited. (Required)
* **5. email**: Enter the email address of the pharmacy branch being audited. (Required)
* **6. audit_results**: Describe the results of the audit, including any findings or observations. (Required)
* **7. operational_practices**: Describe any operational practices or procedures that were observed during the audit. (Required)
* **8. follow_up**: Describe any follow-up actions or recommendations for the pharmacy branch. (Optional)
* **9. review_date**: Enter the date the audit was reviewed or completed. (Required)
* **10. review_time**: Enter the time the audit was reviewed or completed. (Required)
* **11. review_by**: Enter the name of the person or team that reviewed the audit. (Required)
* **12. next_actions**: Describe any next actions or steps required for the pharmacy branch to improve their compliance status. (Required)

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly, including all required fields.
- Double-check your answers to ensure they are correct and complete.
- If you are unsure about any field or question, refer to the company's policies and procedures for guidance.
