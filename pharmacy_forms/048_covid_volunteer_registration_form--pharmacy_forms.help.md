# COVID Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in volunteering for a pharmacy-related program during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details, including full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2.  Provide your current address and city, state, and ZIP code.
3.  Answer questions about your healthcare background, volunteer experience, and relevant skills or certifications.
4.  Indicate the type of volunteer work you are interested in (e.g., patient interaction, administrative support, etc.).
5.  Specify your availability in terms of hours per week you can dedicate.
6.  Choose a preferred start date for your volunteer work and indicate how long you can commit to the program.
7.  Answer questions about your health and safety status, including vaccination, symptoms, and recent exposure to COVID-19.
8.  Check the boxes indicating your commitment to follow all safety protocols, volunteer code of conduct, and truthfulness of the information provided.
9.  Finally, select a date for completing this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (pharma_vol_name): Enter your complete legal name.
*   **Date of Birth** (pharma_vol_dob): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
*   **Email Address** (pharma_vol_email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (pharma_vol_phone): Enter your phone number for contact.
*   **Current Address** (pharma_vol_address): Enter your street address.
*   **City, State, ZIP Code** (pharma_vol_city_state_zip): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
*   **Healthcare Background** (pharma_vol_healthcare_background): Select your healthcare background from the provided options.
*   **Relevant Skills or Certifications** (pharma_vol_relevant_skills): Enter any relevant skills or certifications you have, such as CPR or first aid certification.
*   **Volunteer Work Interested In** (pharma_vol_volunteer_interest): Select the type of volunteer work you are interested in.
*   **Hours per Week Available** (pharma_vol_hours_per_week): Select the number of hours you can dedicate per week.
*   **Preferred Start Date** (pharma_vol_start_date): Choose a date to begin your volunteer work.
*   **Commitment Duration** (pharma_vol_commitment_length): Select how long you can commit to the program.
*   **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (pharma_vol_vaccination_status): Select your vaccination status.
*   **Currently Have COVID-19 Symptoms** (pharma_vol_current_symptoms): Indicate if you have any current symptoms.
*   **Recent Exposure to COVID-19** (pharma_vol_recent_exposure): Select if you have had any recent exposure to COVID-19.
*   **Any Health Conditions Affecting Volunteering** (pharma_vol_health_conditions): Enter any health conditions that may affect your ability to volunteer.
*   **Volunteer Agreement** (pharma_vol_agreement_header): This section includes an agreement to follow all safety protocols, volunteer code of conduct, and truthfulness of the information provided.
*   **Safety Commitment** (pharma_vol_safety_commitment): Select your commitment to follow all safety protocols.
*   **Volunteer Code of Conduct** (pharma_vol_conduct_agreement): Select your agreement to the volunteer code of conduct.
*   **Truthfulness** (pharma_vol_truthfulness): Select your commitment to providing truthful information.
*   **Date of Registration** (pharma_vol_registration_date): Enter the date you are completing this form.

**Tips**

*   Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the organization.
*   This form is designed to ensure the volunteer process is smooth and safe for all involved.
*   Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   If you need help or support, do not hesitate to reach out.
