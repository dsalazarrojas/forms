# COVID 19 Vaccine Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for facilities to order COVID-19 vaccines. It collects essential information for accurate delivery and processing of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your facility's name and ID number to identify your facility.
2. Provide your contact person's name and email address for communication.
3. Enter your facility's phone number and street address.
4. Enter your facility's city, state, and ZIP code.
5. Select the type of vaccine you are ordering.
6. Enter the total number of doses ordered.
7. Enter the current cold storage capacity.
8. Select the storage temperature capability of your facility.
9. Enter the preferred delivery date when you need the vaccines.
10. If necessary, enter a different delivery address from your facility's address.
11. Estimate the number of recipients for the vaccine.
12. Select the priority status of the order.
13. Check any additional supplies needed for the order.
14. Enter any special instructions for the delivery.
15. Sign and date the order with your authorized name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (text, required): Enter your facility's name, such as Hospital, clinic, or pharmacy.
* **Facility ID Number** (text, required): Enter your facility's ID number for identification purposes.
* **Contact Person Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the order.
* **Facility Email Address** (email, required): Enter your facility's email address for communication.
* **Facility Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your facility's phone number.
* **Facility Street Address** (text, required): Enter your facility's street address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (text, required): Enter your facility's city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Order Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the order is being placed.
* **Vaccine Type Ordered** (select_one, required): Select the type of vaccine you are ordering (e.g., Pfizer-BioNTech, Moderna, Johnson & Johnson, AstraZeneca, or Mixed types).
* **Total Number of Doses** (number, required): Enter the total number of doses ordered.
* **Current Cold Storage Capacity** (number, required): Enter the number of doses your facility can store.
* **Storage Temperature Capability** (select_one, required): Select the storage temperature capability of your facility (e.g., Ultra cold (-80C to -60C), Deep freezer (-25C to -15C), Regular freezer (-15C or warmer), or Refrigerated (2-8C)).
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (date, required): Enter the date when you prefer to have the vaccines delivered.
* **Delivery Address** (text, optional): If different from your facility's address, enter the delivery address.
* **Estimated Number of Recipients** (number, optional): Estimate the number of people who will be vaccinated.
* **Priority Status** (select_one, required): Select the priority status of the order (e.g., Regular order, High priority, or Emergency request).
* **Additional Supplies Needed** (select_multiple, optional): Check any additional supplies you need for the order (e.g., Syringes and needles, Alcohol wipes, Gauze and bandages, Vaccine cards, or Information sheets).
* **Special Instructions** (text, optional): Enter any special instructions for the delivery.
* **Authorized Order Signature** (text, required): Enter your name as the authorized person for the order.
* **Authorization Date** (date, required): Enter the date of authorization for the order.
