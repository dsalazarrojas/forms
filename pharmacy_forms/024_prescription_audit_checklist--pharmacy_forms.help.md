# Prescription Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Audit Checklist is a form used to ensure that prescriptions are accurately filled and all relevant information is documented.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the patient's prescription and gather any necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prescription Id (1)**: Enter the ID of the prescription, if available.
* **Date Prophetsion (2)**: Enter the date the prescription was provided.
