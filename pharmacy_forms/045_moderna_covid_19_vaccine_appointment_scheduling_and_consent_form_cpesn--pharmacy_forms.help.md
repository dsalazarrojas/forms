# Moderna COVID-19 Vaccine Appointment Scheduling And Consent Form CPESN - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from patients for the Moderna COVID-19 vaccine administration process. It includes fields for patient contact information, appointment scheduling, medical history, and post-vaccination follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field (page 1).
3. Select an appointment date and time for the vaccine administration (pages 2-3).
4. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email (pages 4-5).
5. Provide any relevant medical information, such as medical conditions or allergies (pages 6-7).
6. Select the vaccination status (page 8).
7. Fill in the dates and times for the first and second doses, if applicable (pages 9-10).
8. Select the vaccine type, brand, and lot number for the doses (pages 11-13).
9. Confirm the administration of the first and second doses (pages 14-15).
10. Sign the form to confirm you have read and understand the information provided (page 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your name in this field (required).
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date for the vaccine administration (required).
* **Appointment Time**: Select the time for the vaccine administration (required).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up (required).
* **Email**: Enter your email for follow-up (required).
* **Medical Condition**: Provide any medical condition(s) that may be relevant to the vaccination (optional).
* **Allergies**: Select any allergies or sensitivities you have (optional).
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history information (optional).
* **Vaccination Status**: Select your current vaccination status (optional).
* **Dose 1 Date**: Enter the date for the first dose (optional).
* **Dose 1 Time**: Enter the time for the first dose (optional).
* **Dose 2 Date**: Enter the date for the second dose (optional).
* **Dose 2 Time**: Enter the time for the second dose (optional).
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for follow-up (optional).
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time for follow-up (optional).
* **Vaccine Administered**: Confirm the vaccine was administered (optional).
* **Dose 1 Vaccine**: Select the vaccine type for the first dose (optional).
* **Dose 2 Vaccine**: Select the vaccine type for the second dose (optional).
* **Vaccine Brand**: Select the vaccine brand for the doses (optional).
* **Vaccine Lot**: Select the vaccine lot number for the doses (optional).
* **Dose 1 Administered**: Confirm the first dose was administered (optional).
* **Dose 2 Administered**: Confirm the second dose was administered (optional).
* **Vaccination Site**: Enter the vaccination site (optional).
* **Vaccination Status**: Enter the vaccination status (optional).
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm you have read and understand the information provided (required).
