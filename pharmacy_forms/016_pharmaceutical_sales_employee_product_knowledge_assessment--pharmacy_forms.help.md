# Pharmaceutical Sales Employee Product Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the knowledge and skills of pharmaceutical sales employees in order to provide accurate and up-to-date product information to customers and to identify areas where the employee needs additional training or support to improve their performance and communication skills.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Sales Rep**: Type the name of the sales representative.
2. **Territory**: Type the territory covered by the sales representative.
3. **Product Name**: Type the name of the product.
4. **Product Description**: Provide a brief description of the product.
5. **Customer**: Type the customer's name.
6. **Product Knowledge**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the sales representative's current product knowledge.
7. **Training Needs**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the sales representative's current training needs.
8. **Sales Performance**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the sales representative's sales performance.
9. **Sales Tactics**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the sales representative's sales tactics.
10. **Communication Skills**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the sales representative's communication skills.
11. **Product Knowledge Date**: The date the sales representative's product knowledge was last updated or refreshed.
12. **Sales Rep Signature**: The signature of the sales representative.
13. **Customer Signature**: The signature of the customer.
14. **Product Knowledge Training Date**: The date the sales representative received training on the product.
15. **Product Knowledge Training Status**: Answer "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the sales representative's product knowledge training.

* **Sales Rep** (`sales_rep`, text, required): Type the name of the sales representative.

* **Product Knowledge** (`product_knowledge`, select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the sales representative's current product knowledge.

* **Sales Performance** (`sales_performance`, select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the sales representative's sales performance.

* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the sales representative's communication skills.
* **Product Knowledge Date** (`product_knowledge_date`, date, optional): The date the sales representative's product knowledge was last updated or refreshed.
* **Sales Rep Signature** (`sales_rep_signature`, text, required): The signature of the sales representative.
