# COVID Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure patients understand the benefits, risks, and implications of receiving the COVID-19 vaccine. By completing this form, you'll be providing informed consent for vaccination, which is a critical step in protecting your health and the community.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2.  If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider or pharmacist.
3.  Ensure you understand each question before submitting your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (pharm_patient_name): Provide your complete legal name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth** (pharm_date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format (e.g., 01-01-1990).
* **Email Address** (pharm_email): Provide your active email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (pharm_phone): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Current Address** (pharm_address): Provide your current street address, including apartment number (if applicable).
* **Pharmacy Name** (pharm_pharmacy_name): Name of the pharmacy where you will receive the vaccine.
* **Administering Pharmacist Name** (pharm_pharmacist_name): Name of the pharmacist who will administer the vaccine.
* **Vaccination Date** (pharm_vaccination_date): Enter the date you plan to receive the vaccine.
* **I Have Received COVID-19 Vaccine Information** (pharm_received_vaccine_info): Select "Yes, received information" if you have received the required information about the vaccine. If not, select accordingly.
*   **I Understand the Benefits of Vaccination** (pharm_understand_benefits): Select "Yes, understand" if you understand the benefits of vaccination. If not, select accordingly.
*   **I Understand Possible Side Effects and Risks** (pharm_understand_risks): Select "Yes, understand" if you understand the potential side effects and risks of the vaccine. If not, select accordingly.
*   **Allergies** (pharm_allergies): If you have allergies to vaccine components or medications, list them here.
*   **Current Medications** (pharm_current_medications): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Chronic Medical Conditions** (pharm_chronic_conditions): List any ongoing health conditions you have.
*   **Immunocompromised** (pharm_immunocompromised): Select "True" if you have a weakened immune system, "False" if not, or "Unsure" if unsure.
*   **Pregnancy or Nursing Status** (pharm_pregnancy_nursing): Select "Pregnant," "Breastfeeding," "Neither," or "Prefer not to say."
*   **Consent Statement**:
    +   **I Authorize COVID-19 Vaccination** (pharm_authorize_vaccination): Select "I authorize" to confirm your consent.
    +   **This Consent is Voluntary** (pharm_voluntary_consent): Select "Voluntary, not coerced" to confirm this is your free choice.
    +   **Truthful Information** (pharm_truthful_information): Select "Information is truthful" to confirm all provided information is accurate.
*   **Date of Consent** (pharm_consent_date): Enter the date you are providing consent.

Please ensure you read and understand each question carefully before submitting your responses. If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider or pharmacist for guidance.
