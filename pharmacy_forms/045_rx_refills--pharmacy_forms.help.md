# Rx Refills - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information and medical details for refill purposes in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully, providing accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to review each section before proceeding to the next.
3. Double-check for any missing or incorrect information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the relevant patient information from the provided options.
* **Patient Information Details** (`patient_information_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional details related to the patient's information.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's medical record number if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time of the patient's visit.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's email.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select any allergies the patient has from the provided options.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select any current medications the patient is taking from the provided options.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's medical condition.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's medical provider.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional medical history.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional details related to the patient's medical history.
* **Medical Record Number 2** (`medical_record_number_2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's medical record number 2 if applicable.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the patient's last visit.
* **Time of Last Visit** (`time_of_last_visit`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time of the patient's last visit.
* **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's contact number 2.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's email 2.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required: `false`): Sign and date the form with the patient's signature.
* **Medical Provider 2** (`medical_provider_2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the patient's medical provider 2.
* **Date of Refill** (`date_of_refill`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of the refill.
