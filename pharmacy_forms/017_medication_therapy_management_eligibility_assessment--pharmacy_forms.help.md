# medication_therapy_management_eligibility_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medication therapy management eligibility assessment form is a tool used to evaluate a patient's eligibility for medication therapy. This form gathers essential information from the patient, including their user details, medical history, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including any necessary details.
2. Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.
3. List your medical conditions, if applicable.
4. Indicate your current patient status.
5. Enter your medication list, if any.
6. In the medical review section, provide any additional information relevant to your case.
7. Select your review results from the available options.
8. Sign below to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: This field is used to capture your basic user details.
* **Medical History**: This field is used to gather information about your previous illnesses or conditions.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please list them in this field.
* **Patient Status**: Indicate your current patient status in this field.
* **Medication List**: If you are currently taking any medications, list them in this field.
* **Medical Review**: This field is for you to provide any additional information relevant to your case.
* **Review Results**: Select one of the available options to indicate your review results.
* **Signature**: Please sign below to confirm your submission.
* **Submit**: This field is not required but is the submit button to complete your form submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
* This form is for assessment purposes only and is not intended for commercial or medical use.
