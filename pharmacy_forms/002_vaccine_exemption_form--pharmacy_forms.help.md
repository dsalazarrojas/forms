# vaccine_exempt_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vaccine exempt form is used to document a child's exemption from vaccination due to medical or philosophical reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Parent Name and Date of Birth on the first page.
2. Provide a reason for refusal in the Reason for Refusal field.
3. If applicable, describe any medical conditions that may impact vaccination in the Medical Condition field.
4. Sign the form with a Doctor Signature, if required.
5. Sign the form with a Parent Signature.
6. If applicable, provide a School ID and School Name.
7. Finally, sign the form with a Teacher Signature, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the child's parent or guardian.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Reason for Refusal** (`reason_for_refusal`, text, required: false): Describe the reason for refusing vaccination.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): If applicable, describe any medical conditions that may impact vaccination.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form if required by a doctor.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form with your parent signature.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, text, required: false): Provide a school ID if required.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the school name if required.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form if required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a medical professional.
* Ensure that all signatures are genuine and authentic.
