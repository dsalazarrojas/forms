# Pharmaceutical Cold Chain Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document any deviations that occur during the storage and transportation of temperature-sensitive medications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the storage location where the deviation occurred (Storage Location field).
2. Describe the storage area where the deviation occurred (Storage Area field).
3. Specify the storage conditions that contributed to the deviation (Storage Conditions field).
4. Record the current temperature reading where the deviation occurred (Temperature field).
5. Enter the relative humidity reading where the deviation occurred (Humidity field).
6. Describe the deviation that occurred, including any relevant details (Deviation Description field).
7. Enter the date the deviation occurred (Deviation Date field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storage Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location where the deviation occurred, such as a warehouse, fridge, or vehicle.
* **Storage Area** (text, required/optional): Describe the specific area within the storage location where the deviation occurred, if applicable.
* **Storage Conditions** (text, required/optional): Specify the storage conditions that contributed to the deviation, such as a temperature range, humidity level, or exposure to light.
* **Temperature** (text, required/optional): Enter the current temperature reading where the deviation occurred, in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Humidity** (text, required/optional): Enter the current humidity reading where the deviation occurred, as a percentage.
* **Deviation Description** (text, required/optional): Describe the deviation that occurred, including any relevant details, such as the type of medication affected, the quantity involved, and any symptoms or effects observed.
* **Deviation Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the deviation occurred.

## Tips

* Always enter accurate and precise information to ensure a thorough investigation of the deviation.
* Use the provided fields to record all relevant details about the deviation.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or a qualified expert for guidance.
* This form is for reporting deviations that occurred during storage and transportation of temperature-sensitive medications. If you are not reporting a deviation, please do not fill out this form.
