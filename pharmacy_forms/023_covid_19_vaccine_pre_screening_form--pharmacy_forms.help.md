# COVID 19 Vaccine Pre Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information necessary for a safe and successful COVID-19 vaccination experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill in your personal information**:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name as per your official identification.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per your official identification.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
	* **Screening Date**: Enter the date of this screening.
2. **Allergy Screening**:
	* **Severe Allergy to Vaccine Components**: Choose the correct option from 'No known allergies', 'Yes, have allergies', or 'Unsure'. If you have allergies, choose 'Yes, have allergies' and describe the allergen in the 'If Yes, Please Describe the Allergy' field.
	* **Previous Severe Allergic Reaction to Any Vaccine**: Choose 'No previous reactions', 'Yes, had severe reaction', or 'Unsure' based on your experiences.
	* **If Yes, Please Describe the Previous Reaction**: Provide details about previous reactions in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **screening_patient_header**: This is a header for the patient information section.
* **screening_first_name**: Enter your first name here.
* **screening_last_name**: Enter your last name here.
* **screening_dob**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **screening_email**: Enter your valid email address here.
* **screening_phone**: Enter your phone number here.
* **screening_date**: Enter the date of this screening.
* **screening_healthcare_provider**: If you're being screened by a healthcare provider, enter their name here. If not, it's optional.
* **vaccine_component_allergy**: Answer whether you have any severe allergy to vaccine components such as 'polysorbate', 'eggs', etc.
* **allergy_details**: If you have any severe allergy, describe the allergen in this text field.
* **previous_vaccine_reaction**: Choose the option whether you've had any previous reactions to any vaccine.
* **previous_reaction_details**: If you've had reactions, enter details about the reaction here.
* **medical_screening_header**: This header is for medical conditions section.
* **current_illness**: Choose whether you're currently ill with acute illness such as fever or acute infection.
* **illness_description**: If you're ill, describe the symptoms here.
* **immune_condition**: Answer whether you're immunosuppressed or on immunosuppressive therapy.
* **bleeding_disorder**: Answer whether you have any bleeding disorders or on anticoagulation therapy.
* **vaccination_timing_header**: This is a header for the vaccination timing section.
* **received_another_covid_vaccine**: Choose whether you've received another COVID-19 vaccine in last 14 days.
* **received_another_vaccine**: Choose whether you've received another vaccine in last 14 days.
* **other_vaccine_details**: If you've received another vaccine, enter type of vaccine here.
* **screening_recommendation_header**: This is a header for screening recommendation.
* **screening_clearance**: Choose the outcome of your screening result.
* **screening_notes**: Enter any additional notes or recommendations from the provider here.

## Tips

* Be honest about your medical history and allergies for a safe vaccination experience.
* Ensure you answer all required fields accurately.
* If unsure about any field, choose 'Unsure' and discuss with your healthcare provider.
* Review and ensure all fields are accurate before submitting the form.
