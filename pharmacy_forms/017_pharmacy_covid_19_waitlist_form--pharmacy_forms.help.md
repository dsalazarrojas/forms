# Pharmacy COVID 19 Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy COVID 19 Waitlist Form is used to gather contact information and preferences from patients for the COVID-19 waitlist. This form is used to manage the waitlist and ensure that patients receive the necessary information and care in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your phone number and email address.
3. Select your date of birth.
4. Choose your preferred language.
5. Select your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, In Person).
6. Enter the name of your pharmacy.
7. Enter your address.
8. Enter your zip code.
9. Enter your medical record ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Email** (text, required/optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Select your date of birth.
* **Preferred Language** (select_one, required): Choose the language you would like to receive information in.
* **Contact Method** (select_multiple, required): Select the method you prefer to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, In Person).
* **Pharmacy Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your pharmacy for us to refer you back to.
* **Address** (text, required/optional): Enter your address for us to deliver information to.
* **Zip Code** (text, required/optional): Enter your zip code for us to deliver information to.
* **Medical Record ID** (text, required/optional): If you have a medical record ID, enter it for us to refer back to.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any doubts about the form or your contact information, contact us directly.
* If you need assistance with filling out the form, ask a staff member for help.
