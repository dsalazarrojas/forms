# fmlk_covid_19_vaccination_appointments_pharmacy_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and schedule COVID-19 vaccination appointments in a pharmacy setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed the form's purpose and understand its relevance to your role.
2. Fill in the patient's first name, last name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide the appointment date and time for the patient's scheduled vaccination.
4. Select the type of vaccine to be administered from the options provided.
5. Indicate the appointment status, which can be scheduled, confirmed, completed, or in progress.
6. Add any relevant patient notes, such as medical history or allergies.
7. Provide the provider's first name, last name, email, and phone number.
8. Add any appointment notes, such as vaccination site or administration instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the scheduled vaccination appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the scheduled vaccination appointment.
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of vaccine to be administered (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, AstraZeneca).
* **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the appointment (e.g., scheduled, confirmed, completed, in progress).
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes about the patient's medical history or allergies.
* **Provider First Name** (`provider_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the provider's first name.
* **Provider Last Name** (`provider_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the provider's last name.
* **Provider Email** (`provider_email`, email, optional): Enter the provider's email address.
* **Provider Phone Number** (`provider_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the provider's phone number.
* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Address 1** (`patient_address1`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Address 2** (`patient_address2`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address 2 (e.g., apartment number).
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the patient's city.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the patient's state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Patient Date** (`patient_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's birth or any other relevant medical date.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the patient's medical history or allergies.
* **Appointment Notes** (`appointment_notes`, note, optional): Add any appointment-specific notes.
* **Next Appointment Status** (`next_appointment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the next appointment (e.g., scheduled, confirmed, completed, in progress).
