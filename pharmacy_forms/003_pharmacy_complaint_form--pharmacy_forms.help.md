# Pharmacy Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient feedback and complaints about their experiences with a pharmacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient information, including your name and contact details.
2. Describe your complaint or concern in the "Complaint Details" field.
3. Fill in your contact information, including your address and phone number.
4. Specify the date and time of the event that triggered your complaint.
5. Enter the location where you visited the pharmacy.
6. If applicable, mention the product you used and its type (e.g., Generic, Brand, Over-the-counter, Prescription).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Patient Information)**: Enter your name and any additional details about yourself.
* **Complaint Details (Complaint Details)**: Describe your issue or problem with the pharmacy.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your address and phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date and Time (Date and Time)**: Enter the date and time of your visit to the pharmacy.
* **Location (Location)**: Enter the location where you visited the pharmacy.
* **Product Used (Product Used)**: If applicable, mention the product you used during your visit.
* **Product Type (Product Type)**: Select the type of product you used (e.g., Generic, Brand, Over-the-counter, Prescription).
* **Symptoms (Symptoms)**: Check any of the symptoms that apply to your condition (e.g., Fever, Cough, Diarrhea, Vomiting, Nausea).
* **Medical History (Medical History)**: If you have any relevant medical history, provide more details in this field.
* **Action Taken (Action Taken)**: Describe any actions you took as a result of your visit or issue.
