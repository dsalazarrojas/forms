# ultra_care_pharmacy_germantown_covid_19_vaccine_screening_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register and track COVID-19 vaccination records for patients visiting the Ultra Care Pharmacy in Germantown.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the demographic fields (First Name, Last Name, Date Of Birth, Phone, Email).
2. Provide medical history and any pre-existing conditions.
3. Enter vaccine dosage and unit information.
4. Select the type of vaccine administered.
5. Enter the details of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccine.
6. Report the date and time of vaccination.
7. Record the details of the person or department that reported the vaccination record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter the patient's first name as per their identification.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter the patient's last name as per their identification.
* **Date Of Birth** (date): Enter the patient's date of birth for medical history and age verification.
* **Phone** (text): Enter the patient's contact phone number for communication and follow-up.
* **Email** (email): Enter the patient's email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Medical History** (text): Enter the patient's medical history, including pre-existing conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures.
* **Medical Conditions** (text): Enter any additional medical conditions not captured in the medical history section.
* **Vaccine Dosage** (number): Enter the amount of vaccine administered to the patient.
* **Vaccine Dosage Unit** (text): Enter the unit of measurement for the vaccine dosage (e.g., mL, units).
* **Vaccine Type** (select_one): Select the type of vaccine administered (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson & Johnson, AstraZeneca, or Other).
* **Vaccine Type Other** (text): If the vaccine type is not listed above, enter the specific type.
* **Medical Provider Name** (text): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccine.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (text): Enter the contact phone number of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Provider Email** (email): Enter the email address of the healthcare provider.
* **Date Of Vaccination** (date): Enter the date the vaccine was administered.
* **Time Of Vaccination** (time): Enter the time the vaccine was administered.
* **Vaccine Administered By** (text): Enter the person or department that administered the vaccine.
* **Vaccine Administered By Title** (text): Enter the title of the person who administered the vaccine.
* **Vaccine Administered By Department** (text): Enter the department of the healthcare organization that administered the vaccine.
* **Date Reported** (date): Enter the date the vaccination record was reported.
* **Time Reported** (time): Enter the time the vaccination record was reported.
* **Reported By** (text): Enter the name of the person or department that reported the vaccination record.
* **Reported By Title** (text): Enter the title of the person or department that reported the vaccination record.
* **Reported By Department** (text): Enter the department of the healthcare organization that reported the vaccination record.
* **Submitted** (text): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the record.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are accurately filled to prevent errors or duplication of records.
* If unsure about any field, contact the healthcare provider for clarification.
* Verify the vaccine type and dosage information for accurate tracking and reporting.
* Report any medical conditions or allergies to ensure accurate patient care.
