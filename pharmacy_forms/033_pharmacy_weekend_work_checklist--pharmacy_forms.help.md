# Pharmacy Weekend Work Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by pharmacists to record their weekend work activities, ensuring they can track their tasks and provide a safe working environment for patients. Completing this form helps pharmacists and management keep track of their work and identify any potential issues or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page (e.g., Work Tasks, Safety, etc.) according to your work experience over the weekend.
2. Select the relevant options that apply to your work activities.
3. Enter your comments and additional information where necessary.
4. Select the date and time when you completed each task.
5. Specify who completed the task, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Tasks (weekend_workchecklist_form_1)**: Check the boxes that apply to the work tasks you performed over the weekend. This helps you and management track your activities and identify potential issues.
* **Safety (weekend_workchecklist_form_2)**: Briefly describe any safety concerns or incidents that occurred over the weekend, or any notable events that may affect patient safety.
* **Emergency Contact Info (weekend_workchecklist_form_3)**: Select the contact person to reach out to in case of an emergency. This ensures that everyone knows who to contact in case of an incident.
* **Tasks Completed (weekend_workchecklist_form_4)**: Write a brief description of the tasks you completed over the weekend, including any notable accomplishments or issues that arose.
* **Date (weekend_workchecklist_form_5)**: Enter the date when you completed each task, using the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Time (weekend_workchecklist_form_6)**: Enter the time when you completed each task, using the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Comments (weekend_workchecklist_form_7)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your work activities over the weekend.
* **Additional Comments (weekend_workchecklist_form_8)**: Enter any additional comments or details that are not covered in the other fields.
* **Tasks Left (weekend_workchecklist_form_9)**: Briefly describe any tasks left incomplete or pending over the weekend.
* **Date Completed (weekend_workchecklist_form_10)**: Enter the date when you completed each task, using the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Time Completed (weekend_workchecklist_form_11)**: Enter the time when you completed each task, using the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Completed By (weekend_workchecklist_form_12)**: Select who completed each task, in case it's not already specified in the task description.
