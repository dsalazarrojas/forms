# Moderna COVID 19 Vaccine Consent Form CPESN - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to consent for the Moderna COVID-19 vaccine. It captures essential information about the patient, including contact details, medical screening, and vaccine administration site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name, contact email, and contact phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Answer the medical screening questions about recent travel, pregnancy, and vaccination site.
3. Select the correct vaccine manufacturer and site where the vaccination will be administered.
4. If necessary, provide any additional details about the vaccine in the "Consent Details" field.
5. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address where they can be contacted for follow-up.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number where they can be contacted for follow-up.
* **Have you traveled recently?** (`vaccine_screening_1`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Yes, traveled recently
	+ Option 2: No, not recently
* **Are you pregnant?** (`vaccine_screening_2`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Yes, pregnant
	+ Option 2: No, not pregnant
* **Have you been pregnant in the last 30 days?** (`vaccine_screening_3`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Yes, been pregnant in the last 30 days
	+ Option 2: No, not pregnant in the last 30 days
* **Vaccine Details** (`vaccine_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the vaccine, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Vaccine Manufacturer** (`vaccine_manufacturer`, select_one, required): Select the correct vaccine manufacturer:
	+ Option 1: Moderna
	+ Option 2: Other (please specify)
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, select_one, required): Select the site where the vaccination will be administered:
	+ Option 1: Hospital
	+ Option 2: Clinic
	+ Option 3: Mobile Vaccination Unit
* **Consent Details** (`consent_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the consent, such as medical conditions or allergies.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the vaccination.
