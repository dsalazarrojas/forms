# COVID 19 Vaccine Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to screen patients for potential risks and contraindications associated with COVID-19 vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Full Name" field.
2. Enter your patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter your patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your patient's email address in the "Email Address" field (optional).
5. Answer the health screening questions in the "Health Screening Questions" section.
6. Answer the questions in the "Allergies" and "Current Medications" sections.
7. Answer the questions in the "Chronic Health Conditions" and "Immunocompromised" sections.
8. Answer the questions in the "Pregnancy Status" and "COVID-19 History" sections.
9. Answer the questions in the "Screening Approval" section.
10. Complete any additional information required by the pharmacist or healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (Required): Please enter your patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your patient's phone number.
* **Email Address** (Optional): Enter your patient's email address.
* **Health Screening Questions**: Please answer the following health screening questions:
	+ Are you feeling well today? 
	+ Do you have a fever?
	+ Do you have a cough?
	+ Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath?
	+ Do you have a sore throat?
	+ Loss of taste or smell?
	+ Unusual fatigue or weakness?
* **Allergies**:
	+ Allergies to any vaccine components?
	+ If yes, which components?
* **Current Medications**:
	+ Currently taking any medications?
	+ List medications
* **Chronic Health Conditions**:
	+ Chronic health conditions (e.g., Heart disease, diabetes, lung disease)?
	+ If yes, which conditions?
* **Immunocompromised**:
	+ Immunocompromised or immunosuppressed (e.g., HIV, cancer treatment)?
* **Pregnancy Status**:
	+ Are you pregnant?
	+ Are you breastfeeding?
* **COVID-19 History**:
	+ Ever tested positive for COVID-19?
	+ If yes, when?
	+ Have you fully recovered?
* **COVID-19 Vaccination History**:
	+ Prior COVID-19 vaccination?
	+ Number of doses received?
* **Screening Approval**:
	+ Approved for vaccination today?
	+ If not approved, why?
* **Pharmacist Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the pharmacist who will complete the screening.

## Tips
Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure that you are not at risk for adverse reactions or complications. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
