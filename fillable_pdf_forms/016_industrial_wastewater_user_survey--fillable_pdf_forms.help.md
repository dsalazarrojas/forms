# industrial_wastewater_user_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about industrial wastewater management practices and characteristics from industrial facility users. It's essential to provide accurate and honest answers to help the facility's management team understand their current practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your contact information in the first section.
2. In the next sections, select the relevant options for your wastewater characteristics, management practices, and destination.
3. Finally, provide your facility's address and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **UserName**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **UserPhone**: Enter your work or cell phone number where you can be reached.
* **WastewaterType**: Select the type of wastewater you generate (Discharge, Industrial wastewater, Stormwater, Septage, or Agricultural waste).
* **WastewaterCharacteristics**: Briefly describe the characteristics of your wastewater, e.g., pH, temperature, etc.
* **WasteManagementPractices**: Describe your current waste management practices, e.g., treatment, disposal, etc.
* **WasteManagementGoals**: State your goals for managing your wastewater, e.g., reducing pollution, improving water quality, etc.
* **WastewaterSources**: Describe the sources of your wastewater, e.g., cooling systems, manufacturing processes, etc.
* **WastewaterTreatment**: Describe your treatment processes, e.g., chemical treatment, biological treatment, etc.
* **WastewaterDestination**: Select all the places where you dispose of your wastewater (Discharge, Storage, Treatment, Disposal).
* **WastewaterFrequency**: Select how often you dispose of your wastewater (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your wastewater management practices or concerns.
* **IndustrialFacility**: Confirm that you are affiliated with an industrial facility (True or False).
* **FacilityType**: Select the type of facility you are working with (Manufacturing, Chemical, Mining, Construction, Other).
* **FacilityLocation**: Enter your facility's location in the format: city, state, zip, country.
* **FacilitySize**: Select the size of your facility (Small, Medium, Large, Very Large).
* **FacilityName**: Enter your facility's name.
* **ContactPerson**: Enter your name and title or position.
* **ContactEmail**: Enter your work email address.
* **ContactPhone**: Enter your work or cell phone number.
* **ContactFacility**: Enter your facility's name.
* **FacilityAddress**: Enter your facility's full address.
* **FacilityCity**: Enter your facility's city.
* **FacilityState**: Enter your facility's state (optional).
* **FacilityZip**: Enter your facility's zip code (optional).
* **FacilityCountry**: Enter your facility's country.

Note: If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us.
