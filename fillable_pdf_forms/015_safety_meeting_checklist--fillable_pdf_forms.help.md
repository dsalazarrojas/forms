# Safety Meeting Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Meeting Checklist is a tool to document and review the key aspects of a safety meeting. It helps ensure that all necessary discussions and agreements are captured and recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Safety Meeting Checklist, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Meeting Topic**, a brief description of the meeting's objective and agenda.
2. Specify the **Meeting Location**, where the meeting took place.
3. Indicate whether **Meeting Attendees** were present at the meeting.
4. Select the **Meeting Time**, using the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
5. Choose the **Safety Officer** in charge of the meeting.
6. Enter the **Safety Meeting Notes**, a detailed description of the meeting's discussion and decisions.
7. List the **Action Items**, any tasks or activities assigned to team members as a result of the meeting.
8. Schedule the **Next Meeting**, the date and time for the next scheduled meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Topic**: Enter a brief description of the meeting's objective and agenda. (Text input)
* **Meeting Location**: Specify where the meeting took place. (Text input)
* **Meeting Attendees**: Indicate whether team members were present at the meeting. (Select Multiple options: 'Yes' or 'No')
* **Meeting Time**: Use the format `HH:MM AM/PM` to specify the time of the meeting. (Time input)
* **Safety Officer**: Choose the responsible person for the meeting. (Select One option from the list)
* **Safety Meeting Notes**: Provide a detailed description of the meeting's discussion and decisions. (Text input)
* **Action Items**: List any tasks or activities assigned to team members as a result of the meeting. (Select Multiple options: 'Yes' or 'No')
* **Next Meeting**: Schedule the date and time for the next scheduled meeting. (Date input)
