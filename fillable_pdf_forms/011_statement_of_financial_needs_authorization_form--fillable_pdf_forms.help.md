# Statement Of Financial Needs Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Statement Of Financial Needs Authorization Form is used to collect information about a business or individual's financial needs, including their business ownership structure, address, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form as it appears on the first page.
2. Provide your name as the applicant or representative of the business.
3. Choose the business ownership structure (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, etc.).
4. Enter the business address, city, and state.
5. Enter the post office box or zip code for the business.
6. Enter the name of the business.
7. Enter the phone number and email address of the business.
8. Describe the financial needs of the business (e.g., funding, loans, etc.).
9. Enter any additional financial needs or information.
10. Enter your name and title to sign off on the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Statement Of Financial Needs Authorization Form** (<form_title>, text): Enter the title of this form, which should match what is shown on this page.
* **name of applicant** (<name_of_applicant>, text): Enter your name as the applicant or representative of the business.
* **business ownership** (<business_ownership, select_one): Choose the business ownership structure (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, etc.).
* **business address** (<business_address, text): Enter the address of the business.
* **city** (<business_city, text): Enter the city where the business is located.
* **state** (<business_state, text): Enter the state where the business is located.
* **post office box zip** (<business_postal_code, text): Enter the post office box or zip code for the business.
* **name of business** (<name_of_business, text): Enter the name of the business.
* **phone** (<business_phone, text): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **email** (<business_email, email): Enter the email address of the business.
* **financial needs** (<financial_needs, text): Describe the financial needs of the business (e.g., funding, loans, etc.).
* **financial needs 2** (<financial_needs_2, text): Enter any additional financial needs or information.
* **authorization** (<authorization, text): Enter the name of the person authorizing the financial needs.
* **authorized by** (<authorized_by, text): Enter the title of the person authorizing the financial needs.
* **date** (<date, date): Enter the date of this form, in MM/DD/YY format.
* **time** (<time, time): Enter the time of this form, in 12-hour format.
* **note** (<note, note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the financial needs.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields with accurate information.
* Double-check the entered information before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the form creator or administrator.
