# Client Rights Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Rights Consent Form is used to gather essential information and consent from clients before proceeding with a service or treatment. This form ensures that clients are aware of their rights, understand the risks involved, and provide informed consent for the proposed service or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's details in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the correct type of service or treatment from the options provided.
3. Read and acknowledge the provided information, selecting the correct options for each field.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Name
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification document.
*   **Type**: Text field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 2. Age
* **Age**: Confirm that you are 18 years or older. If you are not 18, please provide parental consent.
*   **Type**: Number field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 3. Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
*   **Type**: Email field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 4. Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
*   **Type**: Text field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 5. Type of Service or Treatment
*   **Type of Service or Treatment**: Select the type of service or treatment you are consenting to. Options include:
	+ Healthcare
	+ Therapeutic
	+ Legal
	+ Financial advisory
	+ Fitness
	+ Beauty services
	+ Other
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 6. Service Provider Name
*   **Service Provider Name**: Enter the name of the service provider.
*   **Type**: Text field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 7. Date of Consent
*   **Date of Consent**: Enter the date you are consenting to the service or treatment.
*   **Type**: Date field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 8. I Acknowledge That I Have Been Informed of My Rights
*   **I acknowledge that I have been informed of my rights**: Select "I agree" if you have been fully informed.
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 9. I Understand My Information Will Be Kept Confidential
*   **I understand my information will be kept confidential**: Select "I agree" if you understand your information will be kept confidential.
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 10. I Have Read and Agree to the Privacy Policy
*   **I have read and agree to the privacy policy**: Select "I agree" if you have read and understood the policy.
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 11. I Consent to the Proposed Service or Treatment
*   **I consent to the proposed service or treatment**: Select "I consent" if you agree to the service or treatment.
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 12. I Understand the Risks Involved
*   **I understand the risks involved**: Select "Yes, I understand" if you are aware of the risks involved.
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 13. Alternative Options Have Been Discussed
*   **Alternative options have been discussed**: Select "True" if alternative options have been discussed. If not applicable, select "Not applicable."
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: No.

### 14. All My Questions Have Been Answered Satisfactorily
*   **All my questions have been answered satisfactorily**: Select "True" if your questions have been answered.
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 15. Emergency Contact Name
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
*   **Type**: Text field.
*   **Required**: No.

### 16. Emergency Contact Phone
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Type**: Text field.
*   **Required**: No.

### 17. I Acknowledge That I Have Read This Form and Consent to the Service
*   **I acknowledge that I have read this form and consent to the service**: Select "I agree and consent" if you have read and understood the form.
*   **Type**: Select One field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### 18. Date Completed
*   **Date Completed**: Enter the date the form was completed.
*   **Type**: Date field.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and understand each field before completing the form.
- Answer accurately and truthfully.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and consent.
- Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
