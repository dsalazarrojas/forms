# LS54 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LS54 Form is a document used to collect essential details about an individual, including their personal details (name, address) and employment information (employer details). 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by *required* in the field description.
2. For fields with optional *hint* descriptions, fill in the information as required or relevant for your purposes.
3. Ensure you enter all requested information accurately to avoid errors or incomplete forms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, **Optional**): If applicable, enter your current or past job title.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, **Optional**): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Address** (`address`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your address for work or residence.
- **Employer ID Number** (`employer_id_number`, Text, **Optional**): If applicable, enter your employer's ID or tax ID number.
- **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your work phone number.
- **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, Text, **Optional**): If applicable, enter your employer's name.
- **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, Text, **Optional**): If applicable, enter your employer's address. 

## Tips
