# COVID 19 Daily Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Daily Health Screening Form is a tool for employees, students, or individuals to report their health status daily. This form helps identify potential COVID-19 cases, monitor health trends, and take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are in a quiet and private area to fill out this form accurately.
2. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
3. Enter your answers in the format specified for each question.
4. Save and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screening Date** (`screening_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you are completing this screening form.
* **Screening Time** (`screening_time`, Time, Optional): Indicate the time you are completing this screening form. This helps us track when you completed the form, but it is not required.
* **Name** (`person_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **ID Number** (`person_id`, Text, Optional): Enter your Employee ID, Student ID, or other identifier if you have one.
* **Organization/Department** (`organization`, Text, Required): Type the name of your workplace, school, or facility.
* **Body Temperature** (`temperature`, Number, Optional): Enter your body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Do you have a fever (100.4 degrees F or higher)?** (`fever_check`, Select One, Required): Answer 'Yes' if you have a fever above 100.4 F. Answer 'No' if you do not have a fever.
* **Do you have a new or worsening cough?** (`cough`, Select One, Required): Report 'Yes' if you have a new or worsening cough. Answer 'No' if you do not have a cough.
* **Do you have shortness of breath?** (`shortness_of_breath`, Select One, Required): Report 'Yes' if you experience difficulty breathing or chest tightness. Answer 'No' if you do not have shortness of breath.
* **Have you lost your sense of taste or smell?** (`loss_taste_smell`, Select One, Required): Answer 'Yes' if you have lost your sense of taste or smell. Report 'No' if you do not have these symptoms.
* **Do you have a sore throat?** (`sore_throat`, Select One, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you have a sore throat. Report 'No' if you do not have a sore throat.
* **Do you have body aches or muscle pain?** (`body_aches`, Select One, Optional): Report 'Yes' if you have body aches or muscle pain. Answer 'No' if you do not have these symptoms.
* **Do you have a headache?** (`headache`, Select One, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you have a headache. Report 'No' if you do not have a headache.
* **Do you feel unusual fatigue or tiredness?** (`fatigue`, Select One, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you feel unusual fatigue or tiredness. Report 'No' if you do not have these symptoms.
* **Do you have nausea or vomiting?** (`nausea_vomiting`, Select One, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you have nausea or vomiting. Report 'No' if you do not have these symptoms.
* **Do you have diarrhea?** (`diarrhea`, Select One, Optional): Answer 'Yes' if you have diarrhea. Report 'No' if you do not have diarrhea.
* **Have you been exposed to anyone with COVID-19?** (`covid_exposure`, Select One, Required): Answer 'Yes' if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days. Report 'No' exposure if you have not been exposed.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19?** (`covid_test_positive`, Select One, Required): Answer 'Yes' if you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days. Report 'No' if you have not tested positive.
* **Are you currently under quarantine or isolation?** (`quarantine_isolation`, Select One, Required): Answer 'Yes' if you are under quarantine or isolation. Report 'No' if you are not under quarantine or isolation.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, Select One, Optional): Answer as follows:
	+ Fully vaccinated: You are fully vaccinated against COVID-19.
	+ Partially vaccinated: You are partially vaccinated against COVID-19.
	+ Not vaccinated: You are not vaccinated against COVID-19.
	+ Prefer not to answer: You do not wish to disclose your vaccination status.
* **Screening Result** (`screening_outcome`, Select One, Required): Based on your answers above, report your screening result.
* **Reviewed by Health Professional** (`health_professional_review`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person reviewing your results.
* **Additional Notes or Concerns** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant health information or concerns you want to report.

## Tips
* Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact your HR representative or facility administrator.
* This form is confidential and will only be used for health surveillance and monitoring purposes.
