# Employment Verification Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Verification Release Form is a document that provides information about an employee's employment status and details, helping to verify their employment history. This form is used for administrative purposes, such as payroll processing, benefits, or employment history reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form, which is "Employment Verification Release Form".
2. Provide the employee's name as it is known.
3. Enter the employee's date of birth.
4. Enter the employee's Social Security Number.
5. Enter the employee's employee title or job title.
6. Enter the name of the company the employee is (or was) working for.
7. Enter the job title the employee holds or held.
8. Provide the start date of the employee's employment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employment Verification Release Form** (`form_title`, required/optional): Enter the title of this form to be displayed in the header or footer of the form. This field is not essential for data collection, but it helps with form identification.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required/optional): Provide the name of the employee you are verifying, as it is known in their company or records.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required/optional): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, required/optional): Enter the employee's Social Security Number.
- **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, required/optional): Enter the employee's current or former job title, such as "CEO" or "Software Developer".
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, required/optional): Provide the name of the company the employee is (or was) working for.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, required/optional): Enter the job title the employee holds or held.
- **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the employee's employment in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If any field is left blank, it may be considered a missing value and may lead to form rejection or incorrect data processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact your HR or payroll department for clarification.
- This form is meant for administrative use only and should not be used for any other purpose.
- Please fill out the form carefully and completely.
