# w-8ben - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "w-8ben" form is designed to collect information about a foreign account or entity that may be subject to Withholding Tax requirements. This includes details about the type of entity, its name, address, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Determine the type of entity you're reporting for (Foreign Trust, Corporation, Individual, Partnership, or Other) and select the corresponding option in the "foreign_status" field.
2. Enter the name of the foreign entity or account in the "name" field.
3. Provide the address of the foreign entity or account holder in the "address" field.
4. If applicable, enter the date of the account opening or the latest known date of account activity in the "date" field.
5. Enter the account number (if applicable) in the "account_number" field.
6. Enter the amount (if applicable) associated with the account in the "amount" field.
7. Specify the beneficial owner of the account, which is usually the individual or organization benefiting from the account, in the "beneficial_owner" field.
8. Identify the foreign country involved in the account in the "foreign_country" field.
9. Indicate whether the account holder is a U.S. person in the "us_person" field.
10. Provide the name of the beneficial owner if they are different from the entity itself in the "name_of_owner" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Foreign Status**: Choose the type of entity (Foreign Trust, Corporation, Individual, Partnership, or Other).
* **Name**: Enter the name of the foreign entity or account holder.
* **Address**: Provide the address of the foreign entity or account holder.
* **Date**: Enter the date of account opening or the latest known date of account activity (if applicable).
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number (if applicable).
* **Amount**: Enter the amount associated with the account (if applicable).
* **Beneficial Owner**: Specify the individual or organization benefiting from the account.
* **Foreign Country**: Identify the foreign country involved in the account.
* **U.S. Person**: Indicate whether the account holder is a U.S. person.
* **Name of Owner (if different)**: If the beneficial owner is different from the entity itself, provide their name.
