# ICIQ UI Short Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about urinary incontinence, a condition involving involuntary loss of urine. The form helps healthcare professionals and researchers gather data on the frequency, type, and severity of incontinence episodes, as well as fluid intake and bladder control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply:

1. Answer each question according to your true status.
2. Choose the best answer based on your understanding of the question.
3. Do not leave any fields blank if you can accurately answer them.
4. Review your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information (1)** (`patient_information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your patient's name, age, or any other relevant identification details.
- **Chief Complaint (2)** (`chief_complaint`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the patient's primary issue or problem.
- **Chief Complaint Date (3)** (`chief_complaint_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's chief complaint.
- **Urinary Incontinence Scales (4)** (`ui_scales`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose from a scale to describe the severity of incontinence:
  - Incontinence is not present
  - Mild
  - Moderate
  - Severe
- **Pad Worn Last 24 Hours (5)** (`pad_worn_last_24hrs`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often you used a pad in the last 24 hours:
  - Never
  - Less than 24 hours
  - 1-2 days
  - 3-7 days
  - More than 1 week
- **Fluid Intake Last 24 Hours (6)** (`fluid_intake_last_24hrs`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how much fluid you drank in the last 24 hours:
  - 0-1 L
  - 1-2 L
  - 2-3 L
  - More than 3 L
- **Bladder Control (7)** (`bladder_control`, `select_one`, required/optional): Describe your bladder control:
  - Normal
  - Abnormal
- **Pad Used Last 24 Hours (8)** (`pad_used_last_24hrs`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often you used a pad in the last 24 hours:
  - Never
  - 1-2 times
  - 3-5 times
  - More than 5 times
- **Type of Incontinence (9)** (`continence_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose from a list to describe the type of incontinence:
  - Urine leakage
  - Gas and stools
  - Fecal incontinence
  - Urine and feces
- **Pad Capacity Last 24 Hours (10)** (`pad_capacity_last_24hrs`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the capacity of the pad used in the last 24 hours:
  - 0-1 L
  - 1-2 L
  - 2-3 L
  - More than 3 L
- **Voiding Frequency (11)** (`voiding_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often you voided (went to the bathroom) in the last 24 hours:
  - Less than 1 hour
  - 1-2 hours
  - 2-4 hours
  - More than 4 hours
- **Incontinence Frequency (12)** (`continence_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often you experienced incontinence in the last 24 hours:
  - Never
  - Less than 1 day
  - 1-2 days
  - 3-5 days
  - 1 week or more
- **Volume Voided Last 24 Hours (13)** (`voided_volume_last_24hrs`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the volume of fluid voided (urinated) in the last 24 hours:
  - 0-1 mL
  - 1-2 mL
  - 2-5 mL
  - More than 5 mL
- **Fluid Intake Last 24 Hours Units (14)** (`fluid_intake_last_24hrs_units`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the unit of measurement for fluid intake:
  - L
  - mL
- **Bladder Sensation (15)** (`bladder_sensation`, `select_one`, required/optional): Describe your bladder sensation:
  - None
  - Mild
  - Moderate
  - Severe
- **Urgency (16)** (`urgency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often you experience urgency:
  - Never
  - Less than 1 hour
  - More than 1 hour
- **Frequency (17)** (`frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often you experience frequency:
  - Never
  - Less than 1 day
  - More than 1 day
- **Nocturia Frequency (18)** (`nocturia_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often you experience nocturia (waking up to urinate during the night):
  - Never
  - 1-2 times
  - 3-5 times
  - More than 5 times
- **Volume Voided Last 24 Hours Units (19)** (`voided_volume_last_24hrs_units`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the unit of measurement for voided volume:
  - mL
