# Motor Ride Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motor Ride Event Registration Form is used to register motor ride events for participants. This form captures essential details about the event, including participant information and ride specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your ride name and location details in the "Ride Name" and "Start Location" fields.
2. Choose the event type (e.g., Motorcycle, Mountain Bike, Road Bike, or Car).
3. Enter the ride date and start time in the "Ride Date" and "Start Time" fields.
4. Provide details about the number of participants and enter their contact information (email, phone number, and emergency contact details).
5. Enter the participant's details, including name, date of birth, gender, age, vehicle type, and license number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Data (Participant Data)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Ride Name (Ride Name)**: Enter the name of the ride or event.
* **Ride Date (Ride Date)**: Enter the date of the ride event.
* **Start Location (Start Location)**: Enter the location where the ride starts.
* **End Location (End Location)**: Enter the location where the ride ends.
* **Event Type (Event Type)**: Choose the type of ride event (e.g., Motorcycle, Mountain Bike, Road Bike, or Car).
* **Start Time (Start Time)**: Enter the start time of the ride event.
* **End Time (End Time)**: Enter the end time of the ride event.
* **Number of Participants (Number of Participants)**: Enter the number of participants in the ride event.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone (Emergency Phone)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email (Emergency Email)**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Participant Name (Participant Name)**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant DOB (Participant DOB)**: Enter the date of birth of the participant.
* **Participant Gender (Participant Gender)**: Choose the gender of the participant.
* **Participant Age (Participant Age)**: Enter the age of the participant.
* **Participant Vehicle (Participant Vehicle)**: Enter the type of vehicle used for the ride event.
* **Participant Vehicle Type (Participant Vehicle Type)**: Enter the type of vehicle used for the ride event.
* **Participant License Number (Participant License Number)**: Enter the license number of the participant's vehicle.
* **Participant Vehicle Registration (Participant Vehicle Registration)**: Enter the registration number of the participant's vehicle.
* **Participant Insurance (Participant Insurance)**: Enter the insurance information of the participant's vehicle.
* **Participant Vehicle Make (Participant Vehicle Make)**: Enter the make of the participant's vehicle.
