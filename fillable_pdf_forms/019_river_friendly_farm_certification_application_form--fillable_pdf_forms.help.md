# River Friendly Farm Certification Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The River Friendly Farm Certification Application Form is designed to help farmers demonstrate their commitment to sustainable agricultural practices and effective water resource management. By completing this form, you will be able to apply for certification that recognizes your farm's efforts to protect and preserve our water resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question carefully and accurately.
* Review and complete all pages of the form.
* Submit your application to the certifying agency for review and evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your farm.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your farm's address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your farm's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your farm's email address.
* **Farm Type** (`farm_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of your farm (River, Wetland, Forest, or Other).
* **Farm Size (acres)** (`farm_size`, number, required): Enter the size of your farm in acres.
* **Farm Location** (`farm_location`, text, required): Enter your farm's location.
* **Farm Water Source** (`farm_water_source`, select_multiple, required): Select the sources of water used on your farm (Groundwater, Surface Water, Pond, or Other).
* **Farm Practices** (`farm_practice`, select_one, required): Select the agricultural practices you use on your farm (Conservation Tillage, Organic, Integrated, or Other).
* **Certification Date** (`farm_certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date you certified your farm.
* **Certification Expiration Date** (`farm_certification_expiration`, date, required): Enter the date your certification expires.
* **Certifying Agency** (`farm_certifying_agency`, text, required): Enter the certifying agency that certified your farm (River, State, Federal, or Other).
* **Farm Survey** (`farm_survey`, select_one, required): Select whether you have completed a farm survey (True or False).
* **Frequency of Farm Surveys** (`farm_survey_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of farm surveys you have completed (Annually, Bi-annually, Quarterly, or Other).
* **Date of Last Survey** (`farm_survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your last farm survey.
* **Surveyor** (`farm_surveyor`, text, required): Enter the name of the surveyor who conducted your last farm survey.
* **Farm Inspection** (`farm_inspection`, select_one, required): Select whether your farm requires an inspection (Required or Not Required).
* **Frequency of Inspection** (`farm_inspection_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of farm inspections you have completed (Annually, Bi-annually, Quarterly, or Other).
* **Date of Last Inspection** (`farm_inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your last farm inspection.
* **Inspectionor** (`farm_inspectionor`, text, required): Enter the name of the inspectionor who conducted your last farm inspection.
* **Farm Certificate** (`farm_certificate`, select_one, required): Select the status of your farm certificate (Active or Expired).
* **Certificate Number** (`farm_certificate_number`, text, required): Enter your farm certificate number.
* **Certificate Expiration Date** (`farm_certificate_date`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your farm certificate.
* **Certifying Agency** (`farm_certifying_agency`, text, required): Enter the certifying agency that issued your farm certificate.

Note: Please ensure you answer all the required fields to complete the application process accurately.
