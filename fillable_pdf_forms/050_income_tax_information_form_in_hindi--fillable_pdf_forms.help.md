# Income Tax Information Form In Hindi - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect income tax information from individuals or businesses in Hindi language for tax calculation and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select the tax year you are filling the form for.
2. Select your filing status (Active or Inactive).
3. Indicate whether you reside in India or not.
4. Fill in your filing address.
5. If you have a business, select the business name and type.
6. Enter your business address.
7. Provide your tax payer name.
8. Enter your tax payer email and phone number.
9. Enter your tax payer ID number.
10. Select the type of ID you have.
11. Enter your date of birth.
12. Enter your place of birth.
13. Enter the amount of income you have.
14. Enter any tax deductible amount.
15. Provide any other sources of income.
16. Enter any other income amounts.
17. Enter any tax deductions you have.
18. Enter any tax exemptions you have.
19. Sign the form to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tax_year** (Select One): Select the tax year you are filling the form for.
* **filing status** (Select One): Indicate whether you are actively filing tax or not.
* **residence** (Select One): Indicate whether you reside in India or not.
* **filing address** (Text): Enter your filing address.
* **business name** (Select Multiple): If you have a business, select the business name.
* **business type** (Select One): If you have a business, select the type of business.
* **business address** (Text): Enter your business address.
* **tax payer name** (Text): Enter your name as the tax payer.
* **tax payer email** (Email): Enter your tax payer email.
* **tax payer phone** (Text): Enter your tax payer phone number.
* **tax payer id number** (Text): Enter your tax payer ID number.
* **tax payer id type** (Select One): Select the type of ID you have.
* **tax payer date of birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth.
* **tax payer place of birth** (Text): Enter your place of birth.
* **income amount** (Number): Enter the amount of income you have.
* **tax deductible amount** (Number): Enter any tax deductible amount.
* **other income sources** (Text): Provide any other sources of income.
* **other income amounts** (Text): Enter any other income amounts.
* **tax deductions** (Text): Enter any tax deductions you have.
* **tax exemptions** (Text): Enter any tax exemptions you have.
* **signature** (Note): Sign the form to confirm your information.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Review your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts, please consult the tax authorities or seek help from a tax expert.
