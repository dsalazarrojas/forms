# Church Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Contact Information Form is designed to collect contact information from individuals who have interacted with the church. This information will be used to help the church staff and members better understand how to communicate with them and tailor their outreach efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the primary contact information section.
	* Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
	* Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
	* Enter your **Mobile Phone Number** in the field labeled "Mobile Phone".
2. Next, provide your contact preferences.
	* Select your **Preferred way for the church to contact you** from the dropdown menu.
	* Decide whether you would like to receive our **weekly email newsletter**.
3. If applicable, provide family information.
	* Enter your **Spouse's Name** (if applicable).
	* Enter the names and ages of your **Children** (if applicable).
4. Specify your relationship with the church.
	* Select whether you are a **Member**, **Regular Attendee**, or **Visitor**.
5. Indicate your interest in being on the **Prayer List**.
	* Select whether you would like to be added to the prayer list or not.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.
	* Enter any other relevant information in the field labeled "Anything else you would like us to know?"

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Mobile Phone** (`mobile_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your mobile phone number here.
* **Home Phone** (`home_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your home phone number here (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Street Address** (`address_street`, `text`, required): Enter your street address here.
* **City** (`address_city`, `text`, required): Enter your city here.
* **State/Province** (`address_state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province here.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (`address_zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip or postal code here.
* **Preferred way for the church to contact you** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like the church to contact you (email, text message, phone call, or mailing).
* **Would you like to receive our weekly email newsletter?** (`newsletter_subscription`, `select_one`, required): Decide whether you would like to receive our weekly email newsletter.
* **Spouse's Name** (`spouse_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your spouse's name here (if applicable).
* **Children's Names and Ages** (`children_names`, `text`, optional): Enter the names and ages of your children here (if applicable).
* **Are you a member or visitor?** (`visitor_or_member`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are a member, regular attendee, or visitor.
* **Would you like to be added to our prayer list?** (`prayer_request_consent`, `select_one`, required): Decide whether you would like to be added to the prayer list.
* **Anything else you would like us to know?** (`comment_section`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.
