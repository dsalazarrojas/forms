# Zoning Permit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a zoning permit application. This form will gather data that will help determine whether a property meets the zoning regulations for a specific permit.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the information requested in each section. If you are unsure of any information, please consult with the relevant department or contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, optional): Please enter the street address of the property you are applying for a zoning permit. This could be a single-family home, apartment building, or commercial property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Please select whether the property is a single-family home, multi-family home, commercial, or other.
* **Zoning Districts** (`zoning_districts`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all zoning districts that apply to your property. Typically, this includes the zoning districts where the property's address is located.
* **Parcel Numbers** (`parcel_numbers`, text, optional): Please enter any parcel numbers associated with your property. These can be found on the property tax bill, county records, or by contacting the county assessor's office.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure of any information, please contact us for assistance.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
