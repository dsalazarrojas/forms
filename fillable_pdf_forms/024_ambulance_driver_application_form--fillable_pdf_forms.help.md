# Ambulance Driver Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ambulance Driver Application Form is designed to collect comprehensive information from applicants seeking to drive ambulances for the emergency medical services team. The form is divided into several sections that cover personal, professional, and driving history.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand all the sections and questions.
2.  Answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
3.  Provide detailed information for each section, as required.
4.  Make sure to sign and date the form at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Position Applied For**: Select the position you are applying for, either Ambulance Driver, EMT-Driver, Paramedic-Driver, Part-time Driver, Per Diem Driver, Volunteer Driver, etc.
*  **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to begin work as an ambulance driver.
*  **Desired Employment Type**: Select the type of employment you are seeking, either Full-time, Part-time, Per diem, Contract, or Volunteer.
*  **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*  **Middle Name**: Enter your middle name as it appears on your identification documents (if applicable).
*  **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*  **Name Suffix**: Enter any name suffix (Jr., Sr., etc.) as it appears on your identification documents.
*  **Street Address**: Enter your current street address.
*  **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
*  **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
*  **Zip Code**: Enter your current ZIP code.
*  **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
*  **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
*  **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (MM = month, DD = day, YYYY = year).
*  **Last Four Digits of SSN**: Enter the last four digits of your Social Security Number (for background check purposes).
*  **Driver's License Number**: Enter your valid driver's license number.
*  **License State**: Enter the state where your driver's license was issued.
*  **License Expiration Date**: Enter the date your driver's license will expire.
*  **License Class**: Select the class of your driver's license (Class A, Class B, Class C, or Other).
*  **Do You Hold a Commercial Driver's License (CDL)**: Select Yes or No.
*  **CDL Class**: Select your CDL class (if applicable).
*  **CDL Endorsements**: Select all applicable endorsements for your CDL.
*  **Traffic Violations in Past 3 Years**: Select the number of traffic violations you have had within the past three years.
*  **Describe Violations**: Describe the traffic violations you have had within the past three years.
*  **Accidents in Past 3 Years**: Select the number of accidents you have been involved in within the past three years.
*  **Describe Accidents**: Describe the accidents you have been involved in within the past three years.
*  **Has Your License Ever Been Suspended or Revoked**: Select Yes or No.
*  **Explain License Suspension**: Explain why your license has been suspended or revoked (if applicable).
*  **EMT Certification**: Select your EMT certification level (EMT-Basic, EMT-Intermediate, EMT-Paramedic, or None).
*  **EMT Certification State**: Enter the state where your EMT certification was issued (if applicable).
*  **EMT Certification Expiration**: Enter the date your EMT certification will expire (if applicable).
*  **CPR Certification**: Select your CPR certification level (Current - BLS, Current - Healthcare Provider, Expired, or None).
*  **Other Relevant Certifications**: Enter any other relevant certifications you hold.
*  **Current Employer**: Enter the name of your current employer.
*  **Current Position**: Enter your current position.
*  **Previous Employer 1**: Enter the name of your previous employer 1.
*  **Previous Position 1**: Enter your previous position 1.
*  **Dates of Employment 1**: Enter the dates of your employment with your previous employer 1.
*  **Previous Employer 2**: Enter the name of your previous employer 2.
*  **Previous Position 2**: Enter your previous position 2.
*  **Dates of Employment 2**: Enter the dates of your employment with your previous employer 2.
*  **Highest Level of Education**: Select your highest level of education completed.
*  **School Name**: Enter the name of your educational institution.
*  **Reference 1 Name**: Enter the name of your reference 1.
*  **Reference 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your reference 1.
*  **Reference 1 Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your reference 1.
*  **Reference 2 Name**: Enter the name of your reference 2.
*  **Reference 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your reference 2.
*  **Reference 2 Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your reference 2.
*  **Shift Availability**: Select all the shifts you are available to work.
*  **Willingness to Work Overtime**: Select your willingness to work overtime.
*  **Have You Ever Been Convicted of a Felony**: Select Yes or No.
*  **Explain Felony Conviction**: Explain why you were convicted of a felony (if applicable).
*  **Can You Perform Required Physical Tasks**: Select Yes or No.
*  **Can You Accommodate**: Select Yes or No.
*  **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information.
*  **How Did You Hear About This Position**: Select how you heard about this position.
*  **Applicant Signature**: Enter your full name as your signature.
*  **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of your signature in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips

*  Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have answered all questions accurately and truthfully.
*  Make sure to sign and date the form at the end.
*  Provide detailed information for each section, as required.
*  If you are unsure about any question, consult the form instructions or contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
