# International Health Certificate Exam Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Health Certificate Exam Application Form is used to collect information necessary for the application process of a medical certification exam.
## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form completely and accurately.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Info, required):
	+ This section is used to collect your personal information, such as name, email, and contact number.
	+ Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Info, required):
	+ This section is used to collect your contact information, such as address and phone number.
	+ Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, required):
	+ This section is used to collect your medical history, such as previous medical conditions.
	+ Please provide accurate and detailed information.
* **Additional Medical History** (Additional Medical History, optional):
	+ This section is used to collect any additional medical history that may be relevant to your application.
	+ Please provide accurate and detailed information.
* **Medical Certification Info** (Medical Certification Info, required):
	+ This section is used to collect information about your medical certification, such as certification type and date.
	+ Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Additional Medical Certification Info** (Additional Medical Certification Info, optional):
	+ This section is used to collect any additional information about your medical certification.
	+ Please provide accurate and detailed information.
* **Exam Information** (Exam Info, required):
	+ This section is used to collect information about the exam, such as exam type and date.
	+ Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Additional Exam Information** (Additional Exam Info, optional):
	+ This section is used to collect any additional information about the exam.

* **Exam Location** (Exam Location, required):

* **Additional Exam Location** (Additional Exam Location, optional):
	+ This section is used to collect any additional location information.
	+ Please provide accurate and detailed information.
* **Exam Type** (Exam Type, required):
	+ This section is used to collect the type of exam.

* **Exam Duration** (Exam Duration, required):
	+ This section is used to collect the duration of the exam.

* **Exam Schedule** (Exam Schedule, required):

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields with a star (\*) are filled out.
* Review the form before submission to ensure everything is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the examiners or support team.
* Ensure that you are filling out the form for a different exam or application period. If you have recently filled out the same form, it is possible that your application might not be processed.
