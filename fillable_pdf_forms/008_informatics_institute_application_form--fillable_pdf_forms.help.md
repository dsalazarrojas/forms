# Informatics Institute Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The informatics_institute_application_form is used by applicants to provide necessary information for the selection and evaluation process by the Informatics Institute reviewers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out page 1 - Application Form, providing all required and optional details as instructed.
2. Move on to page 2 - Student Info, providing details about your education and student status.
3. Continue on to page 3 - Contact Info, providing your contact and personal details.
4. Fill out page 4 - Academic History, describing your educational background.
5. Complete page 5 - Education Background, detailing your educational qualifications.
6. Complete page 6 - Employment History, describing your previous employment and job roles.
7. Fill out page 7 - Education Background 2, providing any additional educational information.
8. Select your relevant skills from page 8 - Skills, indicating your applicable skills.
9. Enter page 9 - References, detailing any references you have provided.
10. Write a declaration on page 10 - Declaration of Intent, stating your intentions and expectations.
11. Sign and date page 11 - Agreement, indicating your acceptance of the institute's terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form (Page 1)** (`Application Form`, text, optional)
    Enter your name and other application details as instructed.
* **Student Info (Page 2)** (`Student Info`, text, optional)
    Enter your student details, providing any relevant student status information.
* **Contact Info (Page 3)** (`Contact Info`, text, optional)
    Enter your contact and personal details, such as email, address, and phone number.
* **Academic History (Page 4)** (`Academic History`, text, optional)
    Describe your academic background, including any previous education or relevant courses.
* **Education Background (Page 5)** (`Education Background`, text, optional)
    Detail your educational qualifications, including degrees earned and relevant courses taken.
* **Employment History (Page 6)** (`Employment History`, text, optional)
    Describe your previous employment and job roles, including any relevant work experience.
* **Education Background 2 (Page 7)** (`Education Background 2`, text, optional)
    Provide any additional educational information that may be relevant to your application.
* **Skills (Page 8)** (`Skills`, select multiple, optional)
    Select any relevant skills you possess, such as programming languages, tools, or certifications.
* **References (Page 9)** (`References`, text, optional)
    List any references you have provided, including their contact information.
* **Declaration of Intent (Page 10)** (`Declaration of Intent`, text, optional)
    Write a declaration stating your intentions and expectations.
* **Agreement (Page 11)** (`Agreement`, date, required)
    Sign and date your agreement with the institute's terms and conditions.
