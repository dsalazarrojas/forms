# Air Conditioner HVAC Installation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide HVAC installers through a series of questions to ensure a thorough and accurate installation of air conditioners. The completed form will serve as a reference for quality control and future troubleshooting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to the instructions provided below. Make sure to carefully review each field to ensure accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Installer name (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Enter the name of the technician responsible for the installation.

### Installation date (<name>, <type>: date, required)
Enter the date the air conditioner was installed.

### Site address (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Enter the address where the installation took place.

### Unit model and serial number (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Enter the model and serial number of the installed air conditioner.

### Electrical connection checked (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Confirm whether the electrical connection was properly checked. Choose 'True' if the connection was verified and 'False' otherwise.

### Refrigerant charge verified (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Confirm whether the refrigerant charge matches the specification. Choose 'True' if it matches, 'False' if it doesn't, or 'Not applicable' if the charge is not applicable.

### Airflow test passed (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Confirm whether the airflow test was passed. Choose 'True' if it passed, 'False' otherwise.

### Condensate drain checked (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Confirm whether the condensate drain was checked. Choose 'True' if it was checked and 'False' otherwise.

### Customer notes and handover (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
If necessary, add any additional notes or instructions given to the customer during the installation.

### Installer acknowledgement (<name>, <type>: note, required)
Sign and date the form to confirm that the installation was completed according to the checklist.

## Tips
* Ensure you carefully review each field to provide accurate and complete information.
* Double-check the electrical connection to prevent damage or safety hazards.
* Verify the refrigerant charge to ensure proper unit operation.
* Test the airflow to ensure proper unit performance.
