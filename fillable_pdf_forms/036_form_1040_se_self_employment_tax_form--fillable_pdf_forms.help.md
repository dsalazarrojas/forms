# Form 1040 SE Self Employment Tax Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Form 1040 SE Self Employment Tax Form is a document used to report income from self-employment and calculate self-employment tax liability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have reviewed the relevant tax laws and regulations before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Self-Employment Income** (`self_employment_income`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is used to report the total self-employment income earned for the tax year. Please enter the total income earned from self-employment, without any deductions.
* **Expenses Deductible** (`expenses_deductible`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your business has expenses that are deductible for self-employment tax purposes. If you have deductible expenses, you may need to attach a separate schedule to report them.
* **Self-Employment Business Name** (`self_employment_business_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of your self-employment business or trade name.
* **Business Address** (`self_employment_business_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the address of your self-employment business or trade location.

## Tips

* Ensure you have accurate and complete information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your tax professional or the relevant tax authority.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors and delays.
