# Visitation Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information about visits made to facilities, including details about the visitor and visit details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by filling in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields. Ensure you enter accurate information as it will be used for record-keeping.
- Enter the date and time of your visit as prompted.
- Provide the location visited and the reason for your visit.
- Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you if needed.
- Enter your email address for further communication.
- You can add any additional notes if necessary.
- If this visit was conducted using an assigned tool, provide the tool's details.
- Finally, confirm that the form is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name for accurate record-keeping.
* **Date of Visit** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your visit for record-keeping.
* **Time of Visit** (Time, Required): Enter the time of your visit for accurate record-keeping.
* **Location Visited** (Text, Required): Please provide the location you visited.
* **Reason for Visit** (Text, Required): Describe the reason for your visit.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Required): Enter your contact number for us to reach out to you if needed.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Note** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or information about your visit.
* **Confirm** (Select One, Required): Confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): If a tool was assigned to you, please provide its details.
* **Assigned Tool User** (Text, Optional): If a tool was assigned to you, enter the user associated with it.
* **Assigned Tool Date** (Date, Optional): If a tool was assigned to you, enter the date it was assigned.
* **Assigned Tool Time** (Time, Optional): If a tool was assigned to you, enter the time it was assigned.
* **Assigned Tool Location** (Text, Optional): If a tool was assigned to you, provide the location it was assigned for.
* **Assigned Tool Note** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the assigned tool.
* **Created By** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who created this form.
* **Created Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date this form was created.
* **Created Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time this form was created.
* **Modified By** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who last modified this form.
* **Modified Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date this form was last modified.
* **Modified Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time this form was last modified.
* **Submitted By** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who submitted this form.
* **Submitted Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time this form was submitted.
