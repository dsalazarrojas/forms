# Simple Annual Performance Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Annual Performance Review Form is a digital document used to evaluate an employee's performance over a specific period of time. It helps the supervisor to provide feedback and goals for the employee to work on during the next review period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information.
3. Make sure to save your progress as you complete each section.
4. Review and finalize your form before submitting it to your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Goals**: Enter a brief description of the employee's performance goals for the review period.
* **Target**: Enter the target performance goals for the review period.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the employee's performance.
* **Performance Review**: Enter a brief performance review.
* **Recommendations**: Check if any recommendations are to be made.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments on the performance review.
* **Signed Review**: Check if the review is signed.
* **Signed Review Time**: Enter the time when the review was signed.
* **Supervisor Signed**: Check if the supervisor has signed the review.
* **Supervisor Signed Time**: Enter the time when the supervisor signed the review.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Performance Goals Status**: Select the status of the performance goals (On Track, At Risk, Not Met).
* **Performance Goals Status Comments**: Enter any comments on the performance goals status.
* **Performance Improvement Areas**: Check if any performance improvement areas are present.
* **Performance Improvement Plan**: Check if a performance improvement plan is in place.
* **Performance Improvement Plan Comments**: Enter any comments on the performance improvement plan.
* **Next Review Date**: Enter the date of the next review.
* **Next Review Time**: Enter the time of the next review.
* **Completed**: Check if the review is completed.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Employee Title**: Enter the employee's title.
* **Supervisor Title**: Enter the supervisor's title.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the supervisor's email.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the employee's email.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you should only fill out the fields that apply to your situation. If you are unsure about any specific field, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
