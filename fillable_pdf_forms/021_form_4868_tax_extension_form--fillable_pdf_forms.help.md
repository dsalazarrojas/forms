# Form 4868 Tax Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Form 4868 Tax Extension Form is used to request an extension for a tax deadline. This form is typically used by taxpayers to request an extension for tax-related purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a legitimate reason for requesting a tax extension.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Provide a reason for the extension request.
4. Specify the date you are requesting the extension for.
5. Confirm whether the extension was granted or not.
6. If denied, provide a reason for denial.
7. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Apply For A Tax Deadline Extension**: This field is the main section of the form where you should provide a brief explanation for the reason you are requesting a tax extension.
* **Your Name**: Please fill out your full name as it appears on your tax return.
* **Your Home Street Address**: Enter your home street address or the address where you can be reached.
* **Your Apartment Number/City**: Enter your apartment number or city and state where you can be reached.
* **Your State**: Select your state from the provided options.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Your Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Reason For Extension**: Explain the reason for requesting a tax extension.
* **Date Extension Requested**: Specify the date you are requesting the extension for.
* **Amount Requested**: Enter the amount of extension you are requesting.
* **Extension Granted**: Confirm whether the extension was granted or not.
* **Extension Date**: If granted, enter the new date for the tax return.
* **Tax Year**: Enter the tax year related to the extension request.
* **Amount Granted**: Enter the amount of extension granted.
* **Reason For Denial**: If denied, provide a reason for the denial.
* **Notice Date**: If denied, enter the date of the notice.
* **Notice**: If denied, enter any additional comments or notice from the tax authority.
* **Amount Paid**: Enter the amount paid for the extension.
* **Balance**: Enter the remaining balance after the extension.
* **Balance Forward**: Enter any balance that will be carried forward.
* **Extension Amount**: Enter the total extension amount.
* **Extension Date**: If granted, enter the new extension date.
* **Your Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your identity.
* **Taxpayer's Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your tax authority.
