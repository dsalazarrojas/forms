# Film Crew COVID 19 Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from film crew members regarding their COVID-19 health status. It's a requirement for all crew members before joining the production team to ensure the safety and health of everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read this form carefully before completing it.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
3. Answer all questions truthfully, as this information will be used to assess your health status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Film Crew Member Name (Required)** (`film_crew_member_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Contact Details (Optional)** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, optional): Please provide your contact details, such as phone number and email, so we can reach out to you if needed. You can select one or more of the options.
* **Medical History (Optional)** (`medical_history`, select_one, optional): If you have experienced any medical conditions in the last 2 years, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
* **Recent Contacts (Optional)** (`recent_contacts`, select_one, optional): If you have had any recent contacts with someone with COVID-19, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
* **Travel History (Optional)** (`travel_history`, select_one, optional): If you have traveled anywhere in the last 2 weeks, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
* **Review and Sign (Required)** (`review_status`, text, required): Please read and sign this form to confirm that you have filled it out accurately.
* **Additional Comments (Optional)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): You can provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful for us to know.
* **Contact Details 2 (Optional)** (`contact_details_2`, select_multiple, optional): If you are willing to share your contact details, you can select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
