# Social Emotional Learning In The Workplace Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate an employee's social emotional learning skills in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field (page 1).
2. Enter your workplace address in the "Workplace Address" field (page 1).
3. Write your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field (page 1).
4. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field (page 1).
5. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field (page 1).
6. Select your level of emotional intelligence from the "Emotional Intelligence" dropdown menu (page 2).
7. Choose your social skills from the "Social Skills" multiple-choice menu (page 2).
8. Select your emotional skills from the "Emotional Skills" multiple-choice menu (page 3).
9. Enter a note about your work environment in the "Work Environment" text field (page 3).
10. Enter the date you took the test in the "Test Date" field (page 4).
11. Enter the start and end times of the test in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields (page 4).
12. Enter the location where the test was taken in the "Location" field (page 4).
13. Enter any additional remarks or comments in the "Remarks" field (page 5).
14. Sign your supervisor's signature in the "Supervisor Signature" field (page 5).
15. Enter your supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field (page 5).
16. Sign your employee signature in the "Employee Signature" field (page 5).
17. Enter any notes about your work experience in the "Employee Notes" field (page 5).
18. Enter your re-entered employee name in the "Re-Enter Employee Name" field (page 5).
19. Enter your supervisor's notes in the "Supervisor Notes" field (page 6).
20. Enter the date you reviewed the form in the "Date Reviewed" field (page 6).
21. Enter the name of who reviewed the form in the "Reviewed By" field (page 6).
22. Enter the date the form was reviewed in the "Reviewed Date" field (page 6).
23. Enter the name of who approved the form in the "Approved By" field (page 6).
24. Enter the date the form was approved in the "Approved Date" field (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Workplace Address** (1, text, required): Enter your workplace address.
* **Manager Name** (1, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (1, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address (if applicable).
* **Employee ID** (1, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Emotional Intelligence** (2, select_one, required): Choose your level of emotional intelligence (High, Low, Medium).
* **Social Skills** (2, select_multiple, required): Choose your social skills (Teamwork, Communication, Conflict Resolution).
* **Emotional Skills** (3, select_multiple, required): Choose your emotional skills (Empathy, Motivation, Adaptability).
* **Work Environment** (3, note, required): Enter a note about your work environment.
* **Test Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date you took the test.
* **Start Time** (4, time, required): Enter the start time of the test.
* **End Time** (4, time, required): Enter the end time of the test.
* **Location** (4, text, required): Enter the location where you took the test.
* **Remarks** (5, text, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments.
* **Supervisor Signature** (5, text, required): Sign your supervisor's signature.
* **Supervisor Name** (5, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Employee Signature** (5, text, required): Sign your employee signature.
* **Employee Notes** (5, text, required): Enter any notes about your work experience.
* **Re-Enter Employee Name** (5, text, optional): Enter your name again for verification purposes.
* **Supervisor Notes** (6, text, optional): Enter any notes from your supervisor.
* **Date Reviewed** (6, date, optional): Enter the date you reviewed the form.
* **Reviewed By** (6, text, optional): Enter the name of who reviewed the form.
* **Reviewed Date** (6, date, optional): Enter the date the form was reviewed.
* **Approved By** (6, text, optional): Enter the name of who approved the form.
* **Approved Date** (6, date, optional): Enter the date the form was approved.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Enter accurate and honest answers.
* Review your form before submitting it.
* Ensure that your supervisor reviews and approves the form before submitting it.
* Use this form for educational or training purposes only.
