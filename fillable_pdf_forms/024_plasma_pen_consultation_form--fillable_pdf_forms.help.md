# Plasma Pen Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plasma Pen Consultation Form is designed to collect information about your plasma pen consultation appointment. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure we have all the necessary information to proceed with your consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
3. If you need help with any question, please ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or medications that may be relevant to your plasma pen consultation.
* **I understand that** (`consent_1`, select_one, required/optional): Please select 'True' if you understand that the information you provide will be used to facilitate your plasma pen consultation. If you are unsure, select 'unsure'.
* **I have read** (`consent_2`, select_one, required/optional): Please select 'True' if you have read and understood the content of this form. If you are unsure, select 'unsure'.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask us.
- We will review your form submission and get back to you soon.
