# City Year Event Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Year Event Proposal Form is a submission tool for event organizers to propose events to be held in the city. This form helps us understand your event's details, ensuring that we can review and respond to your proposal efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Proposer name" field.
2. Enter your organization's name, if applicable, in the "Organization" field.
3. Provide a brief working title for your event in the "Event title" field.
4. Choose a date range for your event in the "Proposed event date" field.
5. Estimate the number of attendees you expect in the "Expected attendance" field.
6. Summarize your event's activities and goals in the "Event description" field.
7. If applicable, estimate the total cost for your event in the "Budget estimate" field.
8. List any required permits or approvals needed for your event in the "Required permits or approvals" field.
9. Specify any support requested from the city for your event in the "Support requested from city" field.
10. Finally, acknowledge that you understand your proposal will be reviewed and responded to in the "Submission acknowledgement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposer name**: This field is used to collect your name and contact information. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Organization**: If you are submitting on behalf of an organization, please enter their name here. This helps us understand the affiliation with your event.
* **Event title**: Provide a brief working title for your event. This should give us an idea of what your event is about.
* **Proposed event date**: Choose a date range for your event. Please ensure it is specific and realistic.
* **Expected attendance**: Estimate the number of participants you expect for your event. This helps us understand the scale of your event.
* **Event description**: Summarize the activities and goals of your event in a few sentences. This should give us a clear understanding of what your event is about.
* **Budget estimate**: If applicable, estimate the total cost for your event. This helps us understand the financial implications of your event.
* **Required permits or approvals**: List any known permit or approval needs for your event. This ensures that we can guide you on the necessary steps.
* **Support requested from city**: Specify any support you are requesting from the city for your event. This could include staffing, venue, or equipment needs.
* **Submission acknowledgement**: Please acknowledge that you understand that your proposal will be reviewed and responded to. This confirms your understanding of our review process.
