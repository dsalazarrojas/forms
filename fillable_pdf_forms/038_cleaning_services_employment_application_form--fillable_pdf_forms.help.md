# Cleaning Services Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals seeking employment within a cleaning services organization. It is a comprehensive application form that gathers personal, contact, employment history, skills, qualifications, availability, background information, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal information, including your name, date of birth, social security number, driver's license number (if applicable), address, phone number, and email address.
2. Next, provide your employment history, including your previous cleaning experience, years of experience, and previous employment history.
3. List your relevant skills and qualifications, such as cleaning skills and equipment proficiency.
4. Confirm your availability, including your available days and times of the week.
5. If applicable, provide consent for a background check and inform us about any criminal history.
6. Finally, indicate your job preferences, including your preferred position type, wage expectations, and any additional information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security`, `text`, required): Enter your social security number in the `xxx-xx-xxxx` format.
* **Driver's License Number** (`driver_license`, `text`, optional): Enter your driver's license number (if applicable).
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Previous Cleaning Experience** (`previous_cleaning_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select `True` if you have worked in the cleaning industry before.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in the cleaning industry (if applicable).
* **Previous Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, required): List your last 3 jobs.
* **Available Days of the Week** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all days you are available to work.
* **Available Times** (`available_times`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all available times of the week.
* **Can You Travel to Different Job Sites?** (`can_travel`, `select_one`, required): Select `True` if you can travel to different job sites.
* **Do You Have Reliable Transportation?** (`transportation`, `select_one`, required): Select `True` if you have reliable transportation.
* **Consent to Background Check** (`background_check_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select `True` if you consent to a background check.
* **Do You Have Any Criminal Convictions?** (`criminal_history`, `select_one`, required): Select `True` if you have any criminal convictions.
* **If Yes, Please Explain** (`criminal_explanation`, `text`, optional): Provide a description of your criminal convictions.
* **Preferred Position Type** (`preferred_position`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of cleaning work you prefer (Residential, Commercial, Office Cleaning, or No Preference).
* **Wage Expectations** (`wage_expectations`, `text`, optional): Enter your wage expectations (if applicable).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share.
* **I Agree** (`agree_application`, `select_one`, required): Select `I Agree` to confirm that all information provided is true and accurate.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required): The date you submit this application.

Note that this guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. Please review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete completion.
