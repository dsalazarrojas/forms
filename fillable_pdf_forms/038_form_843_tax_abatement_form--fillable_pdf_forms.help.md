# Form 843 Tax Abatement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Form 843 Tax Abatement Form is designed to assist taxpayers in claiming their tax refunds in a simple and straightforward manner. This form is intended for taxpayers who are eligible for tax refunds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and documentation required for your tax claim.
2. Select the correct category for your claim (Form 1, Form 2, or Form 3).
3. Provide a clear and concise description of your claim.
4. Fill in the description text boxes with the required details.
5. Choose the desired output format for your completed form (CSV, Excel, or PDF).
6. Optionally, select the "one click download" option for easy sharing and distribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Category**
* (`category`, select_one, required: false)
Select the category for your tax claim: Form 1, Form 2, or Form 3.

### **Claim Tax Refund**
* (`description`, text, required: false)
Provide a clear and concise description of your tax claim.

### **Easy to Fill**
* (`description_text`, text, required: false)
Fill in the description text box with additional details to support your claim.

### **Saves Submissions as PDFs**
* (`description_text_2`, text, required: false)
Enter any further details to support your claim.

### **Output Format**
* (`output_file`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the output format for your completed form: CSV, Excel, or PDF.

### **One Click Download**
* (`output_file_2`, text, required: false)
Select this option to enable one-click download and sharing of your completed form.

### **Assigned Tool**
* (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false)
Not applicable in this form.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by taxpayers for tax refund claims. Please ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct purpose and category.
