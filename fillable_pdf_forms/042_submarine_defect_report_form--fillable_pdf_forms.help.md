# Submarine Defect Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Submarine Defect Report Form is used to report defects on submarines, collecting essential information to facilitate the analysis and resolution of these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Defect Date field with the date the defect was observed.
2. In the Description field, provide a brief explanation of the defect.
3. Select all applicable categories from the Category field.
4. If applicable, enter the affected Submarine and Location fields.
5. Choose the Priority of the defect (Low, Medium, or High).
6. Fill in the Resolution field with the resolution or outcome of the defect.
7. Enter the Technician's name who resolved the defect (if applicable).
8. Enter the Date of the resolution (if applicable).
9. Select the status of the affected Subsystem (Active or Inactive) for each status field.
