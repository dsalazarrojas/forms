# Animal Transport License Renewal Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for the renewal of an animal transport license, which is required for transporting animals such as pets, livestock, or other types of animals that need special permits or licenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your license.
2. Provide your current animal transport license number.
3. Enter the current expiration date of your license.
4. Enter your registered business name (if applicable).
5. Enter your business physical address (if applicable).
6. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle you are using for transport.
7. Enter the make and model of the vehicle you are using for transport.
8. Enter the date of the last vehicle inspection.
9. Describe your relevant driver qualifications (e.g., certifications, years of experience).
10. Confirm if you have provided proof of insurance (Yes or No, Will provide separately).
11. Confirm your renewal fee payment status (Paid, Pending).
12. Make a declaration of compliance with all relevant transport regulations (I declare compliance, Other).
