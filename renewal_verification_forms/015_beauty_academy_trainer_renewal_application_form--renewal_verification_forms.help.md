# Beauty Academy Trainer Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for Beauty Academy Trainers to renew their licenses and continue teaching at the Academy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately**. Please ensure that all fields are completed as required.
2. **Provide detailed and honest information** about your current status and activities as a trainer.
3. **Submit the form for review and approval** once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Current Trainer ID**: Enter your current Trainer ID number.
* **Current Status at Academy**: Select your current status at the Academy (Active Full-time, Active Part-time, On Leave, or Semi-Retired).
* **Beauty License Number**: Enter your Beauty License Number.
* **License Expiration Date**: Enter your license expiration date.
* **Current Professional Certifications**: List all active certifications you hold.
* **Continuing Education Completed**: Describe any continuing education courses you have completed.
* **Specialty Teaching Areas**: List the specialties you teach at the Academy.
* **Number of Students Trained Annually**: Enter the number of students you train each year.
* **Current Classes Taught**: List the classes you are currently teaching at the Academy.
* **Recent Student Feedback Rating**: Select the rating that reflects your recent student feedback (Below Average, Average, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **New Training Methods or Innovations Implemented**: Describe any new methods or innovations you have implemented in your training.
* **Any Disciplinary Actions in Past 2 Years**: Select whether you have had any disciplinary actions in the past 2 years (Yes - No, Yes - Minor, or Yes - Major).
* **Explain Any Disciplinary Actions**: If you have had disciplinary actions, provide a detailed explanation.
* **Acknowledge Compliance with Academy Standards**: Select whether you comply with the Academy's standards (Yes or No).
* **Renewal Period**: Select the renewal period you are requesting (1 Year, 2 Years, or 3 Years).
* **Commitment to Continue Teaching**: Select whether you are committed to continuing teaching at the Academy (Yes or No).
* **Supporting Documents Attached**: Select whether you have attached supporting documents to your application (Yes or No).
* **Additional Comments or Information**: Provide any additional comments or information about your application.

Please note that this form is required for all trainers to renew their licenses and continue teaching at the Academy.
