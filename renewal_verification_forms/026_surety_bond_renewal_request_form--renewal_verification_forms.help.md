# Surety Bond Renewal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surety Bond Renewal Request Form is designed for users to request the renewal of a surety bond. This form will be used by the surety bond administrators to collect information from customers who need to renew their bond.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Company Name, etc.)
2. Provide the necessary bond information (Surety Bond Number, Issue Date, Expiration Date, etc.)
3. Choose the Bond Type
4. Provide the reason for the bond renewal
5. Add your contact information (Contact Number, Email, Fax, etc.)
6. Add your office and street address
7. Fill in your city, state, and zip code
8. Select the Status of your bond renewal
9. Provide the Follow-up Date, Follow-up Method, and Follow-up Notes
10. Select the Follow-up Status and Assigned To (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your bond documentation.
### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your bond documentation.
### Surety Bond Number (3)
* **Surety Bond Number**: Enter your surety bond number as it appears on your bond documentation.
### Issue Date (4)
* **Issue Date**: Enter the date your surety bond was issued.
### Expiration Date (5)
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the date your surety bond is set to expire.
### Bond Amount (6)
* **Bond Amount**: Enter the amount of the bond.
### Renewal Date (7)
* **Renewal Date**: Enter the date you would like your bond to be renewed.
### Bond Type (8)
* **Bond Type**: Choose the type of bond you hold (Type 1 or Type 2).
### Renewal Reason (9)
* **Renal Reason**: Provide the reason for requesting the bond renewal.
### Company Name (10)
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name as it appears on your bond documentation.
### Contact Person (11)
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to contact with regards to the bond renewal.
### Contact Number (12)
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for the bond administrator to contact you.
### Email (13)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for the bond administrator to contact you.
### Fax (14)
* **Fax**: Enter your fax number if available.
### Office Address (15)
* **Office Address**: Enter your office address as it appears on your bond documentation.
### Street Address (16)
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address as it appears on your bond documentation.
### City (17)
* **City**: Enter the city in which your office is located.
### State (18)
* **State**: Enter the state in which your office is located.
### Zip Code (19)
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of your office.
### Status (20)
* **Status**: Select the status of your bond renewal.
### Follow-up Date (21)
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date you would like the bond administrator to follow up with you.
### Follow-up Method (22)
* **Follow-up Method**: Choose the method by which you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
### Follow-up Notes (23)
* **Follow-up Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments for the follow-up.
### Follow-up Status (24)
* **Follow-up Status**: Select the follow-up status of your bond renewal.
### Assigned To (25)
* **Assigned To**: Select the user who will be handling your bond renewal (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the bond details before submitting the form.
- Provide clear and concise information for the bond administrator to contact you effectively.
