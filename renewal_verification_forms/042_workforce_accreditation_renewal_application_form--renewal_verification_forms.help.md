# Workforce Accreditation Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Accreditation Renewal Application Form is designed to collect information from users regarding their workforce accreditation renewal application. This form gathers details about the user's job title, contact information, and license status, as well as their renewal request and details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to provide all necessary details for your renewal request.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**:
	+ This field is for your personal details.
	+ Please fill out your first name and last name.
	+ Enter your email address correctly (e.g., [example@example.com](mailto:example@example.com)).
* **First Name**:
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**:
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address correctly (e.g., [example@example.com](mailto:example@example.com)).
* **Job Title**:
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Work Email**:
	+ Enter your work email address correctly (e.g., [example@work.com](mailto:example@work.com)).
* **Work Phone**:
	+ Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Address**:
	+ Enter your work address.
* **Work City**:
	+ Enter your work city.
* **Work Postal Code**:
	+ Enter your work postal code.
* **Work Country**:
	+ Enter your work country.
* **Work Province**:
	+ Enter your work province.
* **License Number**:
	+ Enter your license number.
* **License Expiration Date**:
	+ Enter your license expiration date.
* **License Expiration Date 2**:
	+ Enter your license expiration date 2.
* **License Status**:
	+ Select your current license status (Active or Inactive).
* **License Type**:
	+ Select your license type (Full time, Part time, Contractual, or Other).
* **Contract Details**:
	+ Enter any contract details.
* **Contract Start Date**:
	+ Enter your contract start date.
* **Contract End Date**:
	+ Enter your contract end date.
* **Renewal Request**:
	+ Enter your renewal request.
* **Renewal Request Details**:
	+ Enter details about your renewal request.
* **Renewal Date**:
	+ Enter your renewal date.
* **Renewal Status**:
	+ Select your current renewal status (Active or Inactive).
* **Renewal Status Reason**:
	+ Enter the reason for your renewal status.
* **Authorized**:
	+ Select whether you are authorized (Yes or No).
* **Reviewed By**:
	+ Enter the name of the person who reviewed your information.
* **Review Date**:
	+ Enter the date you were reviewed.
* **Review Status**:
	+ Select your current review status (Active or Inactive).
* **Review Status Reason**:
	+ Enter the reason for your review status.
* **Last Reviewed**:
	+ Select whether you have been last reviewed (Yes or No).
* **Last Reviewed By**:
	+ Enter the name of the person who last reviewed your information.
* **Last Review Date**:
	+ Enter the date you were last reviewed.
* **Comments**:
	+ Enter any comments about your renewal application.
* **Renewal Notes**:
	+ Enter any notes about your renewal application.
