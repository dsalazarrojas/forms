# Roadside Assistance Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew a Roadside Assistance Membership for a vehicle and its driver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required fields with the relevant information, including membership ID, vehicle details, and driver details.
- Choose the correct payment method for the renewal.
- Confirm that the terms of the membership have been accepted.
- Enter any additional notes about the membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Membership ID**
 Membership ID (`membership_id`, number, required)

 Enter the membership ID assigned to the vehicle and its driver.

### 2. **Plan**
 Plan (`plan`, select_one, required)

 Select the membership plan that applies to the vehicle (Basic, Premium, Plus, or Ultimate).

### 3. **Expiration Date**
 Expiration Date (`expiration_date`, date, required)

 Enter the current expiration date of the membership.

### 4. **Payment Method**
 Payment Method (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required)

 Choose the method of payment for the membership renewal (credit card, bank transfer, or other).

### 5. **Terms Accepted**
 Terms accepted (`payment_terms_accepted`, select_multiple, required)

 Confirm that the terms of the membership have been accepted.

### 6. **Vehicle Type**
 Vehicle Type (`vehicle_type`, select_multiple, required)

 Select the type of vehicle covered by the membership (car, truck, motorcycle, or van).

### 7. **Vehicle Year**
 Vehicle Year (`vehicle_year`, number, required)

 Enter the year of the vehicle.

### 8. **Vehicle Make**
 Vehicle Make (`vehicle_make`, text, required)

 Enter the make of the vehicle.

### 9. **Vehicle Model**
 Vehicle Model (`vehicle_model`, text, required)

 Enter the model of the vehicle.

### 10. **Vehicle Registration**
 Vehicle Registration (`vehicle_registration`, text, required)

 Enter the registration number of the vehicle.

### 11. **Driver Name**
 Driver Name (`driver_name`, text, required)

 Enter the name of the driver.

### 12. **Driver Phone**
 Driver Phone (`driver_phone`, text, required)

 Enter the phone number of the driver.

### 13. **Driver Email**
 Driver Email (`driver_email`, email, required)

 Enter the email address of the driver.

### 14. **Driver Address**
 Driver Address (`driver_address`, text, required)

 Enter the address of the driver.

### 15. **Driver City**
 Driver City (`driver_city`, text, required)

 Enter the city of the driver.

### 16. **Driver State**
 Driver State (`driver_state`, text, required)

 Enter the state of the driver.

### 17. **Driver Zip**
 Driver Zip (`driver_zip`, text, required)

 Enter the zip code of the driver.

### 18. **Emergency Contact**
 Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, text, required)

 Enter the name of the emergency contact.

### 19. **Emergency Contact Phone**
 Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required)

 Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

### 20. **Relationship**
 Relationship (`relationship`, text, required)

 Enter the relationship between the driver and the emergency contact.

### 21. **Emergency Contact Phone 2**
 Emergency Contact Phone 2 (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, text, optional)

 Enter the phone number of the emergency contact (if applicable).

### 22. **Emergency Contact Email**
 Emergency Contact Email (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional)

 Enter the email address of the emergency contact (if applicable).

### 23. **Emergency Contact Address**
 Emergency Contact Address (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional)

 Enter the address of the emergency contact (if applicable).

### 24. **Notes**
 Notes (`notes`, note, optional)

 Enter any additional notes about the membership renewal.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
