# Life Verification Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Life Verification Submission Form" is a document used by our organization to verify the identity and demographic information of individuals. This form is used to collect personal details such as name, contact information, and address, which are essential for our internal record-keeping and verification processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your first name in the "First Name" field (field 1).
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field (field 2).
3. Fill in your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (field 3), using the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your Social Security Number (SSN) in the "Social Security Number" field (field 4).
5. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field (field 5).
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (field 6).
7. Enter your physical address in the "Address" field (field 7).
8. Enter your city in the "City" field (field 8).
9. Select your country of origin from the options in the "Country" field (field 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your surname or family name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Social Security Number** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your full Social Security Number (XXX-XX-XXXX).
* **Phone** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, including area code and extension (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Email** (6, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Address** (7, text, required/optional): Enter your physical address, including street address and apartment number (if applicable).
* **City** (8, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State** (9, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you are a US citizen, 'No' otherwise.
* **Zip** (10, text, required/optional): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Country** (11, select_one, required/optional): Select the country of your origin.
