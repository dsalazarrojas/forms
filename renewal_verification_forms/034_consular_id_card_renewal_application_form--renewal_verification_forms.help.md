# Consular ID Card Renewal Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for renewing a Consular ID Card, a type of identification card issued to citizens of another country who reside in the United States. The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information and supporting documents to facilitate the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth and efficient processing of your application.
2. Ensure that all supporting documents are uploaded correctly, as specified in the checklist.
3. Review and acknowledge the fees and logistics information carefully.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your identity and the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name (as shown on current ID)** (`applicant_full_name`): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your current Consular ID Card.
* **Date of Birth** (`applicant_date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Current Consular ID Card Number** (`current_consular_id_number`): Enter your current Consular ID Card number.
* **Current ID Expiration Date** (`current_id_expiration`): Enter your current Consular ID Card expiration date.
* **Current Residential Address (Street)** (`current_address_street`): Enter your current street address.
* **City, State, ZIP** (`current_address_city_state`): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Primary Email Address** (`applicant_email_ident`): Enter your primary email address, which will be used to send renewal status updates.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`applicant_phone_ident`): Enter your primary phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Country of Citizenship** (`country_of_citizenship_ident`): Select your country of citizenship.
* **Do you hold any other citizenships?** (`second_citizenship_status`): Select 'No' if you do not hold any other citizenships, otherwise select 'Yes - Please specify below'.
* **Specify Other Citizenships** (`other_citizenships_ident`): If you selected 'Yes' above, enter any other citizenships you hold.
* **Required Documents for Upload/Submission** (`docs_ready_checklist`): Check all documents that apply to upload or submit, as specified in the checklist.
* **Fees and Logistics** (`renewal_fee_ack_ident`): Acknowledge the renewal fee of $50.00, which is non-refundable once processed.
* **Preferred ID Card Delivery Method** (`delivery_method_pref`): Select your preferred delivery method for the renewed ID Card.
* **I certify that the information provided is true and correct** (`truthfulness_oath_ident`): Verify that the information provided is accurate.
* **Authorized By (Full Legal Name)** (`applicant_signature_name_label`): Sign your name as a legal representative.
* **Date of Application** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
