# Collection Agency License Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information required to renew a collection agency's license. The form asks for agency details, compliance status, financial information, and certification of accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting.
3. Use correct input formats for date and number fields.
4. Choose the correct option for each dropdown field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Agency Details
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, `text`, required): Enter the agency's legally registered business name.
* **Current License Number** (`agency_registration_number`, `text`, required): Enter the existing license number to be renewed.
* **Original License Issue Date** (`license_issue_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the license was initially issued.
* **Current License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the current license expiration date.
* **Principal Owner Name** (`principal_owner_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the principal owner.
* **Owner Email Address** (`owner_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the owner's email address.
* **Owner Phone Number** (`owner_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter the agency's physical location.
* **City** (`business_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the agency operates.
* **State - Province** (`business_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where the agency operates.
* **Postal Code** (`business_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the agency's postal code.
* **Country** (`business_country`, `text`, required): Enter the country where the agency operates.

### Compliance and Financial Information
* **Is Registered Office Same as Business Address** (`registered_office_same`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if the registered office is the same as the business address, or "No" if different.
* **Registered Office Address - if Different** (`registered_office_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address if the registered office is different from the business address.
* **Compliance Training Completed in Last Year** (`compliance_training_completed`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if compliance training was completed, or "No" if not.
* **Training Course Details** (`training_details`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and completion date of the compliance training course.
* **Have Complaints Been Filed Against This Agency in the Past Year** (`complaint_filed`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if complaints have been filed, or "No" if not.
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of any filed complaints.
* **Have Any Violations Been Found During Inspections** (`violations_found`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if violations have been found, or "No" if not, or "No inspections conducted" if no inspections have been conducted.
* **Violation Details** (`violation_details`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of any found violations and remedial actions taken.
* **Financial Statement Attached** (`financial_statement_attached`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if a recent financial statement is attached, or "No" if not.
* **Professional Liability Insurance - Current Status** (`insurance_coverage`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Active" if insurance coverage is active, "Expired" if expired, or "No coverage" if no coverage.
* **Insurance Coverage Amount** (`insurance_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the insurance coverage amount.
* **Renewal Fee Payment Method** (`renewal_fee_payment`, `select_one`, required): Select the method of payment for the renewal fee: "Check", "Credit Card", "Bank Transfer", or "Electronic Payment".
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes" if all information provided is true and accurate, or "No" if not.

Please note that the agency must ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date to ensure a smooth license renewal process.
