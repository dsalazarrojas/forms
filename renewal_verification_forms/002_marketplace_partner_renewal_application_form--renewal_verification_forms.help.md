# Marketplace Partner Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Partner Renewal Application Form is a crucial document that helps us verify your partnership with our marketplace. It contains the necessary information we require to process your renewal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review each field to understand what information is being requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Info** (`partner_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you provide basic information about your business or organization. Please include your company name, partner name, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to contact you or your representative. Please provide your email address, phone number, or other contact information.
* **Address Info** (`address_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you provide your business address or location.
* **Renewal Info** (`renewal_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide information about your renewal request. Please describe your needs and expectations.
* **Partner Services** (`partner_services`, text, required: false): This field is where you list the services you offer as a partner.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you provide billing information, such as your bank account number or payment terms.
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, select_multiple, required: false): In this field, please indicate whether you agree to the terms and conditions of our marketplace partnership. Choose either "Yes" or "No" to confirm your agreement.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required: false): This field is where you submit your completed form. Please click "Submit" to send your application.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have all necessary documentation and information ready before filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
