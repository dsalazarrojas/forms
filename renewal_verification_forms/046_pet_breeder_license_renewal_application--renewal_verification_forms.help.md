# Pet Breeder License Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew a pet breeder's license. It collects information on the breeder's contact details, license status, breed list, and compliance history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your breeder contact information from the options provided.
2.  List your license information, if applicable.
3.  Provide a detailed history of your compliance with our license regulations.
4.  List the animals you breed, including their relevant health certifications.
5.  Finally, confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Breeder Contact Info**: 
  - This field is used to select your contact information from the options provided.
  - Please choose the correct option that represents your breeder contact details.
* **License Info**:
  - This field is used to list any additional license information that you have.
  - Select any relevant licenses you hold.
* **Animal Info**:
  - This field is used to provide a space for you to input any notes about your animals, such as their breed, age, etc.
  - Please use this space to provide any relevant information about your animals.
* **Compliance History**:
  - This field is used to provide a detailed history of your compliance with our license regulations.
  - Please provide a thorough explanation of your compliance history, including any relevant dates and events.
* **Animals**:
  - This field is used to list the animals you breed, along with their relevant health certifications.
  - Please select all the animals you breed, along with their health certifications.
* **Breeder Acceptance**:
  - This field is used to confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions of this application.
  - Please select "I accept the terms and conditions" to confirm your acceptance.
