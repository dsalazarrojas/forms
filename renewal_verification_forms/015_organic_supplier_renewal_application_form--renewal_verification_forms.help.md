# Organic Supplier Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for internal use by a business or organization to collect and verify information from organic suppliers for renewal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your supplier's name and contact details.
2. Enter the name of the company or organization you represent.
3. Enter your address for contact and communication purposes.
4. Specify the city where your operation or business is located.
5. Select the country where your operation or business is located.
6. If applicable, list any relevant certifications or credentials.
7. Select the product categories your business offers.
8. If necessary, provide any updated details or changes regarding your business or operations.
9. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of your organic supplier.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person handling your business.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company or organization you represent.
* **Address**: Enter the address where your business is located.
* **City**: Enter the city where your operation or business is located.
* **Country**: Select the country where your operation or business is located.
	+ Options: Australia, Canada, United States
* **Certifications**: If applicable, list any relevant certifications or credentials.
* **Product Categories**: Select the product categories your business offers.
	+ Options: Category 1, Category 2, Category 3
* **Updated Details**: If necessary, provide any updated details or changes regarding your business or operations.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding your submission.
* **Product Categories**: This field is a multiple-select field, meaning you can select multiple options from the provided list.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the supplier is not already registered or verified in the system before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department or team for clarification.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need to make any changes, you can update your information by resubmitting the form.
