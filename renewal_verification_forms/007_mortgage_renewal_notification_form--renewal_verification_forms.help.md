# Mortgage Renewal Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Renewal Notification Form is used to collect and organize important information for home loan renewals, ensuring timely and accurate communication between the home owner, lender, and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Home Owner Name** in the first field, which is required to identify the homeowner.
2.  Enter the **Lender Name** in the second field, which is required to identify the lender.
3.  Enter the **Loan Amount** in the third field, which is required to understand the loan's value.
4.  Select the **Renewal Date** in the fourth field, which marks when the loan is due for renewal.
5.  Choose whether to enable **Follow-up Reminders** to keep stakeholders on track.
6.  Select a **Contact Method** (Phone, Email, or None) to choose how you want to be contacted.
7.  If you choose a contact method, enter the corresponding **Contact Number** or **Contact Email**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Home Owner Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the homeowner.
    *   This field is required to identify the homeowner.
*   **Lender Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the lender.
    *   This field is required to identify the lender.
*   **Loan Amount** (3, number, required): Enter the loan's value.
    *   This field is required to understand the loan's value.
*   **Renewal Date** (4, date, required): Select the date the loan is due for renewal.
    *   This field is required to keep track of the loan's renewal schedule.
*   **Follow-up Reminders** (5, select_one, required): Choose whether to enable follow-up reminders.
    *   Select "True" to receive reminders, "False" to turn them off.
*   **Contact Method** (6, select_one, required): Select a contact method (Phone, Email, or None).
    *   This field is required to choose how you want to be contacted.
*   **Contact Number** (7, text, optional): Enter the contact number if you chose "Phone" as the contact method.
    *   This field is optional and only visible if "Phone" is selected as the contact method.
*   **Contact Email** (8, email, optional): Enter the contact email if you chose "Email" as the contact method.
    *   This field is optional and only visible if "Email" is selected as the contact method.
*   **Note** (9, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the mortgage renewal.
    *   This field is optional for capturing any additional information.
