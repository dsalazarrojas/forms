# Resident Access Badge Renewal Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to obtain resident consent for the renewal of their access badge. This form collects necessary information required for the renewal process, including contact details, badge type, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand all the questions and fields.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.

* **Resident Access Badge Renewal Consent Form Page 1 (Page 1)** (`resident_access_badge_renewal_consent_form_page`)
	+ **Title** (`title`): Enter the title of the form, which is already filled out as "Resident Access Badge Renewal Consent Form".
* **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`resident_access_badge_renewal_consent_form_page_2`)
	+ **Contact Information** (`type: select_one`): Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the resident has provided their contact information.
