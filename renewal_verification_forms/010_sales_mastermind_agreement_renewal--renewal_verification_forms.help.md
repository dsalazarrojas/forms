# Sales Mastermind Agreement Renewal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew the Sales Mastermind Agreement between the participant and the sales rep. It is a crucial document that outlines the terms and conditions of their partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please provide the following information:
	* Fill out your name and email address in the "participant name" and "participant email" fields, respectively.
	* If you do not have a sales rep assigned to you, please indicate your preference in the "mastermind program" field.
	* Fill out your sales rep's name and email address in the "sales rep name" and "sales rep email" fields, respectively.
	* If you are renewing your term, select the start and end dates of your current term in the "term start date" and "term end date" fields, respectively.
	* Finally, indicate the desired term length for your renewed agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Mastermind Agreement Renewal Form** (`sales_mastermind_agreement_renewal_form`, text, optional):
	* This field is for the form title and will automatically be filled out for you.
* **participant name** (`participant_name`, text, not required):
	* Your name as the participant in the Sales Mastermind program.
* **participant email** (`participant_email`, email, not required):
	* Your email address associated with the Sales Mastermind program.
* **sales rep name** (`sales_rep_name`, text, not required):
	* The name of your assigned sales representative.
* **sales rep email** (`sales_rep_email`, email, not required):
	* The email address of your assigned sales representative.
* **mastermind program** (`mastermind_program`, select_one, not required):
	* If you are renewing your term, please indicate if you want to be assigned to a new sales rep. Choose 'Yes' to be assigned or 'No' to continue with your current sales rep.
* **term preference** (`term_preference`, text, optional):
	* Please provide a brief description of your term preference (e.g., month, quarter, etc.).
* **term start date** (`term_start_date`, date, not required):
	* The start date of your current term.
* **term end date** (`term_end_date`, date, not required):
	* The end date of your current term.
* **term length** (`term_length`, number, not required):
	* The length of your current term in months.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth renewal process.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your sales representative for assistance.
