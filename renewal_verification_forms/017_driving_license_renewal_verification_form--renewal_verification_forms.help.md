# driving_license_renewal_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Driving License Renewal Verification Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your driver information in the "Driver Information" section (Page 1).
2. Provide details about your vehicle in the "Vehicle Information" section (Page 2).
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section (Page 3).
4. Confirm your submission details in the "Submit Information" section (Page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Information** (`driver_info`, text, Optional): Fill in your driver's name, address, and other relevant details as prompted.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, Optional): Provide details about your vehicle, including make, model, and license plate number.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, Optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email or any other preferred contact method.
* **Submit Information** (`submit_info`, text, Optional): Confirm your submission by entering your full name and any other relevant details as required.

## Tips
Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly. Double-check for any spelling and formatting errors before submitting the form.
