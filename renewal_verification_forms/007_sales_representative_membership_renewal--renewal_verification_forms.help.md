# Sales Representative Membership Renewal - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from Sales Representatives for the purposes of membership renewal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents ready before starting to fill out the form.
2. Complete the **Sales Representative Form** on page 1:
	* Enter your name and other sales representative information in the **Sales Representative Form**.
3. On page 2:
	* Provide your **payment information** in the **payment-information** field.
4. On page 3:
	* Sign and date the **signature** field to confirm your identity and commitment to the membership program.
5. On page 4:
	* Describe any **renewal requests** you have in the **Renewal Requests** field.
6. On page 5:
	* Enter your **Sales Representative Information** in the **Sales Representative Info** field.
7. On page 6:
	* Select your current **Membership Plan** from the available options.
8. On page 7:
	* Enter your current **Renewal Status** in the **Renewal Status** field.
9. On page 8:
	* Select your current **Submission Status** in the **Submission Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Representative Form** (Page 1, sales_representative_form):
	+ This section is used to collect your basic sales representative information.
* **payment-information** (Page 2, payment_information):
	+ Please provide your payment details, such as credit card number, expiration date, etc.
* **signature** (Page 3, signature):
	+ Sign and date this field to confirm your identity and commitment to the membership program.
* **Renewal Requests** (Page 4, renewal_requests):
	+ Describe any changes or requests you have regarding your membership status.
* **Sales Representative Info** (Page 5, sales_representative_info):
	+ Enter your sales representative information, such as your job title, contact details, etc.
* **Membership Plan** (Page 6, membership_plan):
	+ Select your current membership plan from the available options.
* **Renewal Status** (Page 7, renewal_status):
	+ Enter your current membership renewal status.
* **Submission Status** (Page 8, submission_status):
	+ Select your submission status for this membership renewal.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have attached all required documents and payment details.
- If you're unsure about any field or requirement, please contact your representative for guidance.
