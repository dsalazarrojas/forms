# Smart Retail Certification Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Retail Certification Renewal Application form is designed to support the renewal of smart retail certifications granted to retailers or distributors. This form helps to collect required information for the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of certification you wish to renew: Retailer or Distributor.
2. Upload the relevant documentation (either Proof of Registration or Proof of Business Operations).
3. Enter the Date of Issue of your current certification.
4. Enter the Expiration Date of your current certification.
5. Upload a copy of the relevant documentation.
6. Confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete before submitting your renewal application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **<font size=4pt>Renewal Request</font>** (`renewal_request`, `note`, optional): This section is informational only and is used to acknowledge your intention to renew your certification. Please fill out this section to help us understand your renewal request.
* **<font size=4pt>Certification Details</font>** (`certification_details`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of certification you wish to renew: Retailer or Distributor. Please choose the correct option to ensure accurate processing of your renewal request.
* **<font size=4pt>Document Upload</font>** (`document_upload`, `text`, optional): Upload a copy of the relevant documentation (either Proof of Registration or Proof of Business Operations) that meets the required standards.
* **<font size=4pt>Document Type</font>** (`document_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of documentation you uploaded to confirm it meets the required standards. Please choose either Proof of Registration or Proof of Business Operations.
* **<font size=4pt>Date of Issue</font>** (`date_of_issue`, `date`, optional): Enter the Date of Issue of your current certification. Please use the correct date format to ensure accurate processing.
* **<font size=4pt>Expiration Date</font>** (`expiration_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the Expiration Date of your current certification. Please use the correct date format to ensure accurate processing.
* **<font size=4pt>Upload Document</font>** (`upload_document`, `text`, optional): Upload a copy of the relevant documentation for verification purposes.
* **<font size=4pt>Confirm Submission</font>** (`confirm_submission`, `note`, optional): Confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete before submitting your renewal application. Please review your entries carefully to avoid errors.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are submitting for the correct type of certification.
* Please upload a clear and legible copy of the relevant documentation.
* Verify the accuracy of the Date of Issue and Expiration Date of your current certification.
* Confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete before submitting your renewal application.
