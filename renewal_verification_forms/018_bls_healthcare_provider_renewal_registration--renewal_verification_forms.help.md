# BLS Healthcare Provider Renewal Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for healthcare providers to register for a BLS (Basic Life Support) certification renewal. The form is used to verify a provider's current license or certification number, license expiration date, healthcare role, and other relevant information. Please use this form only if you are a certified BLS provider and need to renew your certification. Do not submit this form if you are a new student or certification candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your healthcare provider name in the "Healthcare Provider Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your current license or certification number in the "Current License or Certification Number" field.
5. Enter your current license expiration date in the "Current License Expiration Date" field.
6. Select your healthcare role or credential from the dropdown menu in the "Healthcare Role or Credential" field.
7. If applicable, enter your primary facility or organization in the "Primary Facility or Organization" field.
8. Select the reason for your renewal from the dropdown menu in the "Reason for Renewal" field.
9. Select your BLS certification type from the dropdown menu in the "BLS Certification Type" field.
10. Select a preferred training session date in the "Preferred Training Session Date" field.
11. Select a preferred session time from the dropdown menu in the "Preferred Session Time" field.
12. If you prefer a different delivery method, select from the dropdown menu in the "Preferred Delivery Method" field. Note that in-person classroom is the standard method.
13. If you have completed continuing education hours since your last certification, enter the number in the "Continuing Education Hours Completed" field.
14. If your employer will sponsor your renewal, select "True" in the "Will your employer be sponsoring this renewal?" field.
15. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
16. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, enter them in the "Dietary Restrictions or Allergies" field.
17. If you have any accessibility needs, enter them in the "Accessibility Needs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Healthcare Provider Name** (provider_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (provider_email): Enter your work email address or a secondary contact email.
*   **Phone Number** (provider_phone): Enter your work or personal phone number.
*   **Current License or Certification Number** (current_license_number): Enter your current BLS provider license or certification number.
*   **Current License Expiration Date** (license_expiration_date): Enter your current BLS provider license or certification expiration date.
*   **Healthcare Role or Credential** (healthcare_role): Select your healthcare role or credential from the dropdown menu.
*   **Primary Facility or Organization** (facility_affiliation): If you are affiliated with a facility or organization, enter their name.
*   **Reason for Renewal** (renewal_reason): Select the reason for your BLS provider renewal from the dropdown menu.
*   **BLS Certification Type** (bls_certification_type): Select your BLS certification type from the dropdown menu.
*   **Preferred Training Session Date** (preferred_session_date): Select a preferred date for your BLS training session.
*   **Preferred Session Time** (preferred_session_time): Select a preferred time for your BLS training session from the dropdown menu.
*   **Preferred Delivery Method** (delivery_method): Select your preferred delivery method from the dropdown menu.
*   **Continuing Education Hours Completed** (continuing_education_hours): If you have completed continuing education hours since your last certification, enter the number.
*   **Will your employer be sponsoring this renewal?** (employer_sponsorship): If your employer will sponsor your renewal, select "True".
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (dietary_restrictions): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, enter them here.
*   **Accessibility Needs** (accessibility_needs): If you have any accessibility needs, enter them here.
