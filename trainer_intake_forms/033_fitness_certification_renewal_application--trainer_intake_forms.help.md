## Fitness Certification Renewal Application - Help Guide
### Purpose

This form is intended to collect information for renewing your fitness certification.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your certification ID, which is the ID assigned to your fitness certification.
4. Choose a renewal date for your certification.
5. Confirm your selected renewal date.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. If you do not have a first name, enter your commonly used alias.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. If you do not have a last name, enter your commonly used alias.
* **Certification ID** (`certification_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the ID assigned to your fitness certification. This is usually found on your original certification document or certificate.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date to renew your certification. You can choose any date that is within the next 6 months from today's date.
* **Confirm Renewal Date** (`confirm_renewal_date`, date, required/optional): Confirm the date you selected for renewal.

### Tips

- Please ensure the information you provide is accurate to avoid delays in processing your certification renewal.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your certification authority for guidance.
