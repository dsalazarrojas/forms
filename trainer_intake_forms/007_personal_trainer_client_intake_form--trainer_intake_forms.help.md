# Personal Trainer Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Client Intake Form is used to collect essential information from clients before they start training. This information helps trainers tailor the program to the client's needs and ensure their safety during training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the fields marked as "required" or provide a valid reason for not doing so.
2. For fields marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple", choose the most relevant option from the list.
3. Enter your information in the provided fields and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information (1)**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can reach you.
- **Client Information (2)**: Briefly describe your current physical condition, fitness level, and any previous experience with training.
- **Training Goals (3)**: Select the reason you want to train for (Weight Loss, Fat Loss, Strength Training, or Endurance Training).
- **Medical History (4)**: Describe any medical conditions, illnesses, or surgeries you have had.
- **Emergency Contact (5)**: Enter the name, phone number, and relationship to the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
- **Medical Procedures (6)**: List any recent or pending medical procedures you have had or will have.
- **Medical Conditions (7)**: Select any medical conditions you have (Medical Condition 1, Medical Condition 2, etc.).
- **Medication (8)**: List any prescription medications you are currently taking.
- **Medical History Other (9)**: Describe any additional medical history not listed above.
- **Medical Tests (10)**: List any medical tests you have had or are scheduled to have.
- **Doctor Name (11)**: Enter the name of your primary care physician.
- **Doctor Phone (12)**: Enter the contact phone number for your primary care physician.
- **Doctor Office (13)**: Enter the name and address of your primary care physician's office.
- **Doctor Date Visited (14)**: Enter the date you last visited your primary care physician.
- **Hospitalizations (15)**: List any hospitalizations or surgeries you have had.
- **Previous Injuries (16)**: Describe any previous injuries or conditions that may affect your training.
- **Allergies (17)**: List any allergies you have.
- **Medical Alerts (18)**: Enter any medical alerts or warnings you have (e.g., high blood pressure).
- **Medical Notes (19)**: Enter any additional medical information not listed above.
- **Insurance (20)**: Enter your insurance provider and plan details.
- **Insurance Provider (21)**: Enter the name and contact information of your insurance provider.
- **Doctor Specialty (22)**: Enter the specialty of your primary care physician (e.g., cardiologist).
- **Doctor Degree (23)**: Enter your primary care physician's degree (e.g., MD).
- **Doctor Certification (24)**: Enter your primary care physician's certification (e.g., board certified).
- **Medical Professional (25)**: Select any medical professionals you have consulted (Medical Professional 1, Medical Professional 2, etc.).
