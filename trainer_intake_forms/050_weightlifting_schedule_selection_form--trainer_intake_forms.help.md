# Weightlifting Schedule Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weightlifting Schedule Selection Form is designed to collect information from weightlifters to schedule their workouts. This form is used to help trainers understand each weightlifter's preferences and needs, allowing them to create a customized schedule that suits each individual.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select your preferred lifting frequency from the options provided.
2.  Choose your preferred lifting time from the options provided.
3.  Select your preferred lifting day from the options provided.
4.  Choose your preferred lifting time slot from the options provided.
5.  If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the space below.
6.  Sign and date the form to confirm your completion.
7.  Select the status of your form completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Weightlifting Schedule Selection Form** (`weightlifting_schedule_selection_form`, text): This is the title of the form.
*   **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date you would like to start your weightlifting schedule.
*   **Time** (`time`, time): Choose the time of day you would like to work out.
*   **Trainer Intake Form** (`trainer_intake_form`, text): Please provide any relevant information for your trainer.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email): Please enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number.
*   **Training Group** (`training_group`, select_multiple): Select the training group(s) you would like to join.
*   **Other Training Group** (`training_group_other`, text): If you have a training group not listed, describe it here.
*   **Training Group Capacity** (`training_group_capacity`, number): Enter the number of people in your training group.
*   **Other Training Group Capacity** (`training_group_capacity_other`, text): If your training group capacity is not numeric, describe it here.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note): Please provide any additional information for your notes section.
*   **Trainer Intake Form Notes** (`trainer_intake_form_notes`, note): Please provide any additional information for your trainer intake form notes.
*   **Preferred Lifting Frequency** (`preferred_lifting_frequency`, select_one): Choose how often you would like to lift.
*   **Preferred Lifting Time** (`preferred_lifting_time`, time): Choose the time of day you would like to work out.
*   **Preferred Lifting Day** (`preferred_lifting_day`, select_one): Select the day of the week you would like to work out.
*   **Preferred Lifting Time Slot** (`preferred_lifting_time_slot`, select_one): Choose your preferred time slot for your workout.
*   **Preferred Lifting Day Slot** (`preferred_lifting_day_slot`, select_one): Select your preferred time slot for your workout.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Please provide any additional comments or information.
*   **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text): Sign and date this section to confirm your completion of this form.
*   **Trainer Signature** (`trainer_signature`, text): Sign and date this section to confirm your completion of this form.
*   **Trainer Intake Form Notes** (`trainer_intake_form_notes`, note): Provide any additional information for your trainer intake form notes.
*   **Form Completion Status** (`form_completion_status`, select_one): Select the status of your form completion.
*   **Other Form Completion Status** (`form_completion_status_other`, text): If your form completion status is not listed, describe it here.
