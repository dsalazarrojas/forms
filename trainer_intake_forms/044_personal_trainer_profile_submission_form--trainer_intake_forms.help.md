# Personal Trainer Profile Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Profile Submission Form is a tool used by trainers to provide accurate and up-to-date information about their training qualifications, certifications, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information ready to fill out the form. This includes your name, email address, contact information, training certifications, years of experience, available days, and any additional details you would like to share.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure that all information is complete and accurate.
* Submit the form only once. If you have already submitted a form, do not resubmit unless there are changes to the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your profile.
* **Trainer Email** (`trainer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for client inquiries and appointments.
* **Training Certifications** (`training_certifications`, select multiple, optional): Select all certifications that you hold, such as Personal Training Certification, Group Fitness Certification, etc.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been training.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, number, optional): Enter the days you are available for client appointments.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your address or physical location.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for clients to contact you.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter an alternate email address for clients to contact you.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, optional): Enter a brief bio about yourself and your training style.
* **Training Focus** (`training_focus`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your training focus, such as weightlifting or cardio.
* **Trainer Image** (`trainer_image`, text, optional): Upload a profile image for your trainer profile.
* **Services** (`services`, text, optional): Enter a description of the services you provide, such as personal training or group fitness classes.
