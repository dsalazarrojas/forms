# Private Coaching Session Sign-up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for private coaching sessions. It consists of four sections: Client Details, Goals, Session Time, and Session Date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client details (name and contact information) on the first page.
2. Specify your goals for the private coaching sessions on the second page.
3. Choose your preferred session time on the third page.
4. Select a date for your private coaching session on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
* **Client Details** (<label>Client Details</label>): Please provide your name and contact information.

### Goals
* **Goals** (<label>goals</label>): Specify what you want to achieve through the coaching sessions.

### Session Time
* **Session Time** (<label>Session Time</label>): Choose from available time slots: 09:00, 600, or 660.

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (<label>Session Date</label>): Select a date for your private coaching session.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your contact information and session date to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your coach for clarification.
