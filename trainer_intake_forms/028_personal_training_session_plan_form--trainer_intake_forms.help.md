# Personal Training Session Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Session Plan Form is a crucial document that helps both the client and the trainer understand each other's expectations before starting a training session. This guide will walk you through each field of the form to ensure you complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully, understanding each field's purpose and requirement status.
2. Fill in all required fields as instructed.
3. For optional fields, include as much detail as you find relevant and practical.
4. Review your completed form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Details** (<type:text>, required:false)
* This field allows you to enter your personal details, such as name, address, and any other relevant information about you.

### 2. **Fitness Goals** (<type:text>, required:false)
* Enter a clear and concise description of what you want to achieve through this training session. This could include specific goals like "losing weight" or "increasing endurance."

### 3. **Preferred Training Days** (<type:select_one>, required:true)
* Select the days of the week you would like to conduct your training sessions. Typically, you would select one or multiple days from the options provided, as this will help us tailor your training plan accordingly.

### 4. **Preferred Time of Day** (<type:time>, required:false)
* If you have a preferred time of day for your training sessions, please select it here. This will help us schedule appointments at your preferred time if possible.

### 5. **Start Date** (<type:date>, required:false)
* This field is for the date you would like to start your training sessions. If you have no preference, you can leave this field blank.

### 6. **End Date** (<type:date>, required:false)
* Similar to the Start Date field, this is for the date you would like to end your training sessions. If you have no preference, you can leave this field blank.

### 7. **Training Sessions per Week** (<type:number>, required:false)
* This field allows you to specify how many training sessions you would like to have per week. This is crucial for planning your training schedule.

### 8. **Training Duration** (<type:number>, required:false)
* Enter the number of sessions you would like each of your training sessions to be.

### 9. **Rest Days** (<type:number>, required:false)
* If you need or prefer rest days between training sessions, specify them here. This will help us tailor your training plan around your recovery needs.

### 10. **Emergency Contact** (<type:text>, required:false)
* Please provide the name of a person we can contact in case of an emergency. This could be a family member, friend, or colleague.

### 11. **Emergency Phone** (<type:text>, required:false)
* Enter the phone number of your emergency contact. This should be a number that we can reach them at in case of an emergency.

### 12. **Emergency E-mail** (<type:email>, required:false)
* If you prefer to be contacted via email, provide this address here. This is useful for us to keep you informed about any updates or reminders.
