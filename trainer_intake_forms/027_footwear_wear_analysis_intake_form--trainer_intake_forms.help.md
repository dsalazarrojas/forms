# Footwear Wear Analysis Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's footwear wear analysis. It is meant to gather details from the patient's perspective, providing insight into their comfort, functionality, and any issues related to their shoes. Completing this form helps healthcare professionals gather accurate and relevant information to make informed decisions for proper patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information for each section.
2. Answer all required fields as they are marked.
3. Double-check your answers for clarity and completeness.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`main_page`, text, optional): This section serves as an introduction to the form. You can provide any additional information you'd like to help healthcare professionals understand your situation.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): In this field, please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant patient details.
* **Shoe Info** (`shoe_info`, text, optional): Describe the shoes you are wearing, including brand, model, size, and any other notable features.
* **Wear Analysis** (`wear_analysis`, text, optional): In this field, detail your wear and tear experiences, including any problems you've encountered, such as blisters, discomfort, or pain.
* **Gait Assessment** (`gait_assessment`, text, optional): Describe your gait (walking style), including any issues you've noticed or concerns you have.
* **Pain Report** (`pain_report`, text, optional): If you experience any pain while wearing your shoes, please describe it in detail, including location, severity, and any factors that exacerbate or alleviate it.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Add any relevant medical history, allergies, or other information that may be useful for healthcare professionals.
* **Photos** (`photos`, text, optional): If relevant, please attach photos of your shoes, feet, or any issues you've encountered while wearing them.
