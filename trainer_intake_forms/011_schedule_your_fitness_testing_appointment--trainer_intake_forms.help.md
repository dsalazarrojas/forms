# Schedule Your Fitness Testing Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you schedule a fitness testing appointment with our training staff. Please fill out the form with your information and preferences to ensure we can schedule an appointment that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Client Details** section with your name and contact information.
2. In the **Health Information** section, provide any relevant health information that our training staff should be aware of.
3. Select your preferred **Time Slots** for the appointment.
4. Choose a **Date of Visit** for the appointment.
5. If you have any **Available Dates**, indicate which ones you are available for the appointment.
6. Select the **Trainer Intake Form** that you will be filling out as part of the appointment process.
7. Add any **Notes** about your appointment in the text field.
8. Finally, provide your **Contact Number** so we can reach out to you with scheduling details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: 
  * **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded.
  * **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you with appointment details.
* **Health Information**: 
  * Provide any health information that our training staff should be aware of before the appointment.
* **Preferred Time Slots**: 
  * Choose the time slots that work best for you, and our staff will do our best to accommodate your selection.
* **Date of Visit**: 
  * Select a date for the appointment, and our staff will schedule it for you.
* **Available Dates**: 
  * Indicate which dates you are available for the appointment.
* **Available Dates 2**: 
  * Confirm which dates you are available for the appointment.
* **Trainer Intake Forms**: 
  * Select the form that you will be filling out as part of the appointment process.
* **Notes**: 
  * Add any additional notes about your appointment that you would like our staff to be aware of.
* **Contact Number**: 
  * Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you with appointment details.
