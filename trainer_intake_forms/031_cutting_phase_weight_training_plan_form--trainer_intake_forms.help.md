# cutting-phase-weight-training-plan-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users about their fitness goals, training habits, and preferences to create a personalized workout plan. Please answer the questions below to help us better understand your needs and provide you with the best possible guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately.
* Choose the options that best fit your goals and preferences.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): This is where you can provide us with your basic information, such as your name and contact details. Please be as concise as possible.
* **Goal** (`goal`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following goals:
	+ **Cutting Phase**: Focus on reducing body fat and increasing muscle mass.
	+ **Maintaining Phase**: Focus on maintaining current muscle mass and strength.
	+ **Weight Loss**: Focus on losing weight and body fat.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): If you know your current weight, please enter it here. This will help us better understand your goals and provide you with more accurate guidance.
* **Workout Frequency** (`workout_frequency`, select_one, optional): How often do you currently work out?
	+ **3 times a week**: You work out 3 times a week.
	+ **4-5 times a week**: You work out 4-5 times a week.
	+ **6 or more times a week**: You work out 6 or more times a week.
* **Training Plan** (`training_plan`, select_multiple, optional): Do you plan to do any of the following:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in creating a training plan.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in creating a training plan.
* **Workout Duration** (`workout_duration`, select_one, optional): How long do your workouts typically last?
	+ **30-60 minutes**: Your workouts last between 30-60 minutes.
	+ **60-90 minutes**: Your workouts last between 60-90 minutes.
	+ **90 minutes or more**: Your workouts last 90 minutes or more.
* **Workout Type** (`workout_type`, select_one, optional): What type of workouts are you interested in?
	+ **Weight Loss**: Focus on losing weight and body fat.
	+ **Strength**: Focus on building strength and muscle mass.
	+ **Power**: Focus on increasing power and endurance.
* **Workout Days** (`workout_days`, select_multiple, optional): Are you interested in doing any of the following:
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in doing these workouts.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in doing these workouts.
* **Target Date** (`target_date`, date, optional): If you have a target date for your goals, please enter it here (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Training Experience** (`training_experience`, number, optional): If you have any prior training experience, please enter it here.
* **Coach Preference** (`coach_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Are you interested in working with a coach?
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in working with a coach.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in working with a coach.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when providing your information.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
* This form is meant to be a starting point for creating a personalized workout plan.
