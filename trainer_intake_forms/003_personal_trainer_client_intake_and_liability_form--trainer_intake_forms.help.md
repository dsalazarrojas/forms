# Personal Trainer Client Intake And Liability Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Personal Trainer Client Intake And Liability Form is a crucial document that helps personal trainers understand their clients' needs and ensure a safe and effective training environment. By filling out this form, you'll be providing essential information about your health, emergency contact information, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct emergency contact relationship.
3. Indicate whether you have any medical clearance.
4. Provide your emergency response details, including the date, time, and location.
5. List any recent injuries or medical conditions.
6. Specify any other medication you are taking.
7. Provide any additional information about your medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Details**: Please provide a brief description of your current health status, including any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are taking.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact person (Spouse, Parent, Friend, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Other**: If you select "Other" as the emergency contact relationship, please provide the name and contact information of the person.
* **Medical Clearance**: Indicate whether you have any medical clearance or permission from your doctor to participate in physical activities.
* **Emergency Response**: Provide the date and time of your emergency response plan.
* **Emergency Response Location**: Specify the location where you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Response Location Other**: If the emergency response location is not listed, please provide a brief description of the location.
* **Consent**: Please acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the training program.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: If you select "Other" as the emergency contact relationship, please specify the relationship (e.g., sibling, caregiver, etc.).
* **Recent Injury**: List any recent injuries or medical conditions.
* **Recent Surgery**: If you have undergone recent surgery, please provide a brief description.
* **Other Medication**: If you are taking any medication not listed, please specify the name and dosage.
* **Other Medication Details**: Provide any additional information about your medication.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your trainer for clarification.
* Please keep in mind that your safety is our top priority, and we will do our best to ensure a safe and effective training environment.
