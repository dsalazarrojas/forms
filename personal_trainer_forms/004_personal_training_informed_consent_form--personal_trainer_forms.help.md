# Personal Training Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used before the start of a personal training session to inform and obtain the client's consent for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Name**: Please enter your name as it is to be used in the training records.
2. **Date**: Enter the date of the training session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. **Time Of Training Session**: Enter the start and finish time of the training session in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
4. **Location**: Choose the location where the training will take place from the provided options.
5. **Emergency Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
6. **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the emergency contact person.
7. **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
8. **Medical History**: Please enter any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in the training session.
9. **Medical Condition**: Check the box next to "Yes" if you have any medical conditions that may be relevant.
10. **Medication**: Enter any medication you are currently taking.
11. **Allergies**: Enter any allergies you have.
12. **Emergency Contact Signature**: Select the signature type for your emergency contact person.
13. **Client Signature**: Select the signature type for yourself.
14. **Trainer Signature**: Select the signature type for the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: This field is required for identification and record-keeping purposes.
* **Date**: Ensure you enter the correct date of the training session to ensure accurate records.
* **Time Of Training Session**: Enter the start and finish time of the training session to ensure you are present during the correct timeframe.
* **Location**: Choose the location where you will be training to ensure you are at the correct site.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Provide a contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Medical History**: Inform the trainer of any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate.
* **Medical Condition**: Check "Yes" if you have any medical conditions.
* **Medication**: List any medication you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: Inform the trainer of any allergies you may have.
* **Emergency Contact Signature**: Select the signature type for your emergency contact person.
* **Client Signature**: Select the signature type for yourself.
* **Trainer Signature**: Select the signature type for the trainer.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete this form accurately and honestly.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your trainer for clarification.
* This form is a mandatory requirement for all personal training sessions.
