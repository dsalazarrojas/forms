# crossfit-training-informed-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect informed consent from participants in a CrossFit training program. It is a critical document to ensure that participants are aware of the risks and benefits associated with the training and are willing to participate voluntarily.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: Read through the entire form carefully to understand the purpose and instructions.
2. Ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information about yourself.
3. Sign the form electronically or manually, depending on your preference.
4. Ensure that your trainer(s) sign(s) the form as well.
5. Review and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in the form.
