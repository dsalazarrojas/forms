# Personal Training Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge of a client from a personal training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and authorization to finalize the client's discharge.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the client.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact number, if available.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address, if available.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client's emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number, if applicable.
* **Total Sessions** (`total_sessions`, number, optional): Enter the total number of sessions the client has completed.
* **Last Session Date** (`last_session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the client's last session.
* **Last Session Notes** (`last_session_notes`, note, optional): Enter any relevant notes about the client's last session.
* **Goal Description** (`goal_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the client's goals and objectives.
* **Progress** (`progress`, select_multiple, optional): Select the client's progress status from the options provided.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Enter any recommendations or next steps for the client.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the client is being discharged from the program.
* **Discharge Reason** (`discharge_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the client's discharge.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select_one, optional): Select the next steps for the client, if applicable.
* **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or reviews about the client's discharge.

Note: The fields marked as optional are not required for submission, but are recommended for providing additional context and information about the client's discharge.
