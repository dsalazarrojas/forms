# Personal Trainer Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals interested in starting a personal training program. This form helps trainers understand the client's goals, preferences, and needs, allowing them to create a customized training plan tailored to their client's objectives and limitations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2.  Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3.  Provide your email address in the `email` field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5.  Enter your height in the `height` field.
6.  Describe your fitness goals in the `fitness_goals` field.
7.  Select your preferred training intensity level in the `training_frequency` field.
8.  Indicate your training experience in the `training_experiences` field.
9.  Specify the days you are available to train in the `available_days` field.
10. Enter the time you are available to train in the `available_time` field.
11. Provide emergency contact information in the `emergency_contact` field.
12. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the `emergency_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
*   **Height** (`height`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your height in centimeters or feet and inches.
*   **Fitness goals** (`fitness_goals`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your fitness goals, such as losing weight, gaining muscle, or improving overall health.
*   **Training frequency** (`training_frequency`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the following options:
    *   High Intensity: Intense workouts with short breaks.
    *   Low Intensity: Gentle workouts with longer breaks.
    *   Maintenance: Regular workouts with a balanced intensity.
*   **Training experiences** (`training_experiences`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Indicate your training experience:
    *   Yes: You have training experience.
    *   No: You have no training experience.
*   **Available days** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Specify the days you are available to train:
    *   Yes: You are available to train on the selected days.
    *   No: You are not available to train on the selected days.
*   **Available time** (`available_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time you are available to train, using the 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
*   **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency phone number** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
