# Virtual Workout Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Workout Payment Form is designed to collect payment details for virtual workouts. This form ensures accurate and efficient payment processing for clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out this form.
2.  Select the correct Workout Type: Choose between Virtual, In-Person, or Hybrid.
3.  Enter a valid Email address for the client.
4.  Provide the Client Name (if desired).
5.  Select the preferred Payment Method: Card, PayPal, or Stripe.
6.  Enter the payment Amount and Currency (USD, EUR, or JPY).
7.  Optionally, add a Client Message.
8.  Set the Payment Date and Time (if desired).
9.  Add any additional Notes (if necessary).
10.  Finally, confirm that the payment details are accurate and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workout Type**: Select the type of workout you are providing: Virtual, In-Person, or Hybrid.
* **Email**: Enter the client's Email address.
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name for record keeping purposes (optional).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to accept payment: Card, PayPal, or Stripe.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount due for the workout.
* **Payment Currency**: Select the currency for the payment: USD, EUR, or JPY.
* **Client Message**: Add any special instructions or comments for the client (optional).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment (if different from the current date).
* **Payment Time**: Enter the time of the payment (if different from the current time).
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes for the payment (optional).
* **Submit**: Confirm that the payment details are accurate and submit the form for processing.

Note: This form is designed to be completed for each client's payment. Ensure each client has a unique form submission.
