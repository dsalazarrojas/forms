# Private Lesson Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request private lessons with a private instructor. Please fill out this form with your details and an instructor will contact you to schedule a lesson.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of lesson you are requesting (Private or Group)
2.  Enter your preferred start date and start time
3.  Enter your preferred end time (note: lesson duration is not visible to be filled out as it is calculated automatically)
4.  Enter your student name, email, and phone number (for the instructor's records)
5.  Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to provide to the instructor
6.  Select the status of the private lesson (Scheduled, Confirmed, Rescheduled, or Cancelled)
7.  Enter any additional notes or comments about the lesson

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Private Lesson Request**: This is the main title of the form, please enter a brief description of your lesson request.
* **Lesson Type** (`lesson_type`): Select "Private" or "Group" to specify the type of lesson you are requesting. 
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the date you would like the lesson to start.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time you would like the lesson to start.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time you would like the lesson to end.
* **Lesson Duration** (`lesson_duration`): This field is automatically populated based on the start and end times you select, and is not visible to be filled out manually.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your name as it should appear on the lesson records.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter your email address so the instructor can contact you about the lesson.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): Enter your phone number so the instructor can contact you about the lesson.
* **Lesson Notes** (`lesson_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the lesson you'd like to share with the instructor.
* **Private Lesson Status** (`private_lesson_status`): Select the status of your private lesson (Scheduled, Confirmed, Rescheduled, or Cancelled).
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`): Enter the name of the instructor you are requesting a lesson with.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`): Enter the phone number of the instructor you are requesting a lesson with.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`): Enter the email address of the instructor you are requesting a lesson with.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the lesson.
