# registration_form_for_training_children - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents or guardians to register their child for a training program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name and Last Name fields with the child's name.
2. Enter the Birth Date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide the child's Grade level.
4. Enter the child's Address (optional).
5. Enter the child's phone number.
6. Enter the child’s email address.
7. Enter the emergency contact's First Name and Last Name.
8. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
9. Enter the emergency contact's email address.
10. Provide the name of the child's organization (optional).
11. Provide the child's team name (optional).
12. Provide any Medical Conditions, Medication, Allergies, and Medical Notes about the child.
13. Provide any additional notes or comments about the child.
14. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter the child's last name.
* **Birth Date** (Birth Date, date, required): Enter the child's birth date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter the child's address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the child's phone number.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the child's email address.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (Emergency Contact First Name, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (Emergency Contact Last Name, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's last name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Emergency Contact Email, email, required): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, optional): Enter the child's organization name (if applicable).
* **Team** (Team, text, optional): Enter the child's team name (if applicable).
* **Grade** (Grade, number, required): Enter the child's current grade level.
* **School** (School, text, optional): Enter the child's school name (if applicable).
* **Teacher Name** (Teacher Name, text, optional): Enter the child's teacher's name (if applicable).
* **Teacher Phone** (Teacher Phone, text, optional): Enter the child's teacher's phone number (if applicable).
* **Teacher Email** (Teacher Email, text, optional): Enter the child's teacher's email address (if applicable).
* **Guardian First Name** (Guardian First Name, text, optional): Enter the guardian's first name (if the child has a guardian other than the emergency contact).
* **Guardian Last Name** (Guardian Last Name, text, optional): Enter the guardian's last name (if the child has a guardian other than the emergency contact).
* **Guardian Phone** (Guardian Phone, text, optional): Enter the guardian's phone number (if the child has a guardian other than the emergency contact).
* **Guardian Email** (Guardian Email, text, optional): Enter the guardian's email address (if the child has a guardian other than the emergency contact).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, text, optional): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the child.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the child has.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, optional): Enter any medication the child is taking.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, optional): Enter any allergies the child has.
* **Medical Notes** (Medical Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes about the child.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the child.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click to submit the form.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check the child's details for any errors.
* Review the form before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
