# Covid 19 Pilates Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential health information from clients or individuals before participating in Pilates activities. By providing detailed responses, users can help ensure their safety and a successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form efficiently, follow these steps:
1.  Start by providing general information about yourself on page 1.
2.  Move on to page 2 and answer questions regarding Covid-19 concerns.
3.  On page 3, select any pre-existing medical conditions, recent injuries, or chronic medical issues that might affect your participation.
4.  In pages 4-6, provide contact information for emergency contacts and any other relevant medical history.
5.  On page 7, provide additional information about your medical history.
6.  Finally, confirm your contact information on page 8.
7.  Select any additional health conditions on page 9 that might impact your participation.
8.  Complete your contact information on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false):
    This is the first section of the form where you'll provide basic information such as your name, contact details, and any other relevant data the instructor needs to know about you.
* **Covid-19 Concerns** (`covid_concerns`, text, required: false):
    If you have any concerns or recent exposure to Covid-19, please mention them here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required: false):
    Select any pre-existing medical conditions, recent injuries, or chronic medical issues that you may have.
    *   **Pre-Existing Medical Conditions**:
        Please choose from the options: "Heart Conditions", "Respiratory Conditions", "Immune System Issues".
    *   **Recent Injuries**:
        Select if you've experienced any recent injuries or medical events that might affect your participation.
    *   **Chronic Medical Issues**:
        Choose from the list if you have ongoing health issues impacting your ability to participate.
*   **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false):
    Provide information about an emergency contact person or contact information in case of an incident.
*   **Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false):
    Enter the contact information for the emergency contact mentioned above.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false):
    Please provide any relevant medical history that might impact your participation in Pilates activities.
*   **Additional Medical History** (`medical_history_info`, text, required: false):
    Offer any further information about your medical history that you think is essential.
*   **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2`, text, required: false):
    Enter more contact information for another emergency contact person or alternative contact.
*   **Health Conditions** (`medical_condition_2`, select_multiple, required: false):
    Select any health conditions that might affect your participation in Pilates activities.
    *   **Heart Conditions**:
        Please choose from the list if you have any heart conditions.
    *   **Respiratory Conditions**:
        Select if you have any respiratory issues.
    *   **Immune System Issues**:
        Choose from the list if you experience issues with your immune system.
*   **Contact** (`contact`, text, required: false):
    Enter your contact information to confirm your details.
*   **Emergency Contact 3** (`emergency_contact_3`, text, required: false):
    Enter any further emergency contact information or alternative contact details.

## Tips
*   Ensure you provide detailed and accurate information to guarantee a safe and successful experience.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please ask the instructor for guidance.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors and potential issues.
