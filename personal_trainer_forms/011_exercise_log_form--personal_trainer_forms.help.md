# Exercise Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and track exercise activities. It is intended to be used by individuals who perform regular physical activities, such as athletes, fitness enthusiasts, or gym-goers. The form will help you to monitor your progress and identify patterns in your exercise routine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the exercise you performed from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the number of repetitions you completed.
3. Enter the number of sets you completed.
4. Enter the weight you lifted (if applicable).
5. Choose the date and time of your workout.
6. Add any additional notes about your workout.
7. Decide if you want to submit your workout for review or tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Exercise**: Choose the type of exercise you performed from the dropdown menu. Currently available options are:
	+ Squats
	+ Push-ups
	+ Lunges
* **Repetitions**: Enter the number of times you completed the exercise.
* **Sets**: Enter the number of groups of repetitions you completed.
* **Weight Lifted**: Enter the weight you lifted during your workout (if applicable).
* **Date**: Enter the date you performed the exercise.
* **Time**: Enter the time you spent on your workout.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about your workout, such as any challenges you faced or successes you achieved.
* **Submit**: Choose whether you want to submit your workout for review or tracking.
