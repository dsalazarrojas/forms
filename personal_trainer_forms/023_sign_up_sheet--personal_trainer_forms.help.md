# sign_up_sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
The `sign_up_sheet` form is a registration sheet for participants in an event, training session, or other activity. It collects information from potential participants, such as their name, contact details, and availability. This form helps event organizers and staff to efficiently gather the necessary data for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the `sign_up_sheet` form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name**, a text field that requires your name.
2. Enter your **Email**, a field that accepts an email address.
3. Provide your **Contact Number**, a text field for your phone number.
4. Select your **Participant Type**, which categorizes you as a Coach, Athlete, Support Staff, or Trainer.
5. Enter your **Availability**, a date field to specify your availability.
6. Select your **Start Time**, a time field to choose your start time.
7. Select your **End Time**, a time field to choose your end time.
8. Optionally provide a brief **Description** of your situation or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as a participant.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Participant Type**: Choose your role in the event, such as Coach, Athlete, Support Staff, or Trainer.
* **Availability**: Specify a date when you are available to participate in the event.
* **Start Time**: Choose your start time for the event.
* **End Time**: Choose your end time for the event.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your preferences or situation, if any.

## Tips
This form is designed to be as simple and straightforward as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers or staff.
