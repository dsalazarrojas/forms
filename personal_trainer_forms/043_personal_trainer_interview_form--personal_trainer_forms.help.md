# Personal Trainer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Interview Form is a required step in the application process for individuals interested in becoming a certified personal trainer. This form is used to collect contact information and relevant details about your background, fitness goals, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill out all the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter your first and last name in the "first_name" and "last_name" fields.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. In the "fitness_goals" field, describe your motivations for becoming a certified personal trainer and your goals for your own fitness journey.
7. In the "medical_history" field, provide any relevant medical information that may impact your ability to become a certified personal trainer, such as any medical conditions or allergies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you about your application and certification process.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you about your application and certification process.
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, text, **required**): Describe your motivations for becoming a certified personal trainer and your goals for your own fitness journey.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, **required**): Provide any relevant medical information that may impact your ability to become a certified personal trainer, such as any medical conditions or allergies.
