# Personal Trainer Review Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Personal Trainer Review Publication Consent Form is designed to collect feedback and testimonials from individuals who have interacted with a personal trainer. It gathers information on the quality of service, rating, and consent for publishing the review on various social media channels.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please complete the form in the following order:
    *   Fill out the testimonial field with your honest review of the personal trainer's services.
    *   Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with the personal trainer.
    *   Select the social media channels where you would like your review to be shared.
    *   Provide your email address and phone number for verification and publication purposes.
    *   Specify the date and time you would like your review to be published.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Testimonial**: This is the text area where you can type a detailed review of your experience with the personal trainer. Please be honest and provide feedback on what you liked and disliked.
*   **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the personal trainer's services by choosing one of the five available options (1 to 5).
*   **Can we publish your review?**: Check 'True' if you're willing to have your review published on the specified social media channels. This will allow the personal training service to share your testimonial with their audience.
*   **Which social media channels would you like your testimonial to be shared on?**: Choose the social media platforms where you would like your review to be shared. This could be Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or LinkedIn.
*   **Email Address**: Provide your email address for verification and publication purposes. This is a required field for us to share your review on the chosen social media channels.
*   **Phone Number**: Please input your phone number for any follow-up communication.
*   **Date Published**: Specify the date and time you would like your review to be published.
*   **Time Published**: Select the exact time of the day you'd like your review to be published. Note that this field is not required.
