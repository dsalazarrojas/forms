# strength_training_workout_plan_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "strength_training_workout_plan_questionnaire" form is designed to collect information about your personal details, training experience, and preferences for a strength training workout plan. This will help us create a customized plan tailored to your needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Trainer** (Text, Required: False):
	+ This field is where you can provide us with your name, title, or any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Client Name** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like us to address you.
* **Training Experience** (Number, Required: False):
	+ If you have any experience with strength training, please let us know how many years you have been training.
* **Current Fitness Level** (Number, Required: False):
	+ Please select your current fitness level, from 1 (beginner) to 5 (advanced).
* **Available Days** (Select One, Required: False):
	+ Choose the days you are available to train.
* **Available Time** (Time, Required: False):
	+ Please enter the time of day you are available to train.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Preferred Location** (Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Choose where you would like to train, either in-studio, online, or both.
* **Training Goals** (Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Select the goals you have for your strength training, such as weight loss, increased strength, etc.
* **Training Frequency** (Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Choose how often you would like to train per week.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Please enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Select the relationship you have with your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will use the information provided in this form to create a customized strength training workout plan tailored to your needs and goals.
