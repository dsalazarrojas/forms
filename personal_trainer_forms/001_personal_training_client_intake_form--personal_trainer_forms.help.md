# Personal Training Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about the client, including their personal details, medical history, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "Client First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Client Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Client Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the first name of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact First Name" field.
6. Enter the last name of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Last Name" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone Number" field.
8. Provide any relevant medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" field.
9. Specify the relationship with the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
10. Enter any additional medical information in the "Medical Information" field.
11. Select the permission level for the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone Permission" field.
12. Select the permission level for the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Email Permission" field.
13. Enter the address of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
14. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
15. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
16. Enter the date of birth of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Date of Birth" field.
17. Specify the type of relationship with the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Relationship Type" field.
18. Enter the duration of the relationship with the emergency contact person in the "Relationship Type Since" field.
19. Enter any additional information about the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Note" field.
20. Enter any additional notes about the client in the "Client Notes" field.
21. Enter any additional notes for staff members in the "Client Notes for Staff" field.
22. Provide any other medical information about the client in the "Other Medical Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client First Name** (`client_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Client Last Name** (`client_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition`, Text, Required): Provide any relevant medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Text, Required): Specify the relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Permission** (`emergency_contact_phone_permission`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the permission level for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email Permission** (`emergency_contact_email_permission`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the permission level for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, Text, Required): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Height** (`height`, Number, Required): Enter your height.
* **Weight** (`weight`, Number, Required): Enter your weight.
* **Age** (`age`, Number, Required): Enter your age.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Date of Birth** (`emergency_contact_date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter the date of birth of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type`, Select One, Required): Specify the type of relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship Type Since** (`emergency_contact_relationship_since`, Date, Required): Enter the duration of the relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, Note, Required): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact person.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, Note, Required): Enter any additional notes about the client.
* **Client Notes for Staff** (`client_notes_for_staff`, Note, Required): Enter any additional notes for staff members.
* **Client Medical History** (`client_medical_history`, Text, Required): Provide any other medical information about the client.
* **Other Medical Info** (`other_medical_info`, Text, Required): Enter any additional medical information about the client.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type`, Select One, Required): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, Text, Required): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
