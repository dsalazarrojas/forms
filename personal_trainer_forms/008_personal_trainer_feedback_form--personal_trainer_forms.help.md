# Personal Trainer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Personal Trainer Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your recent training session experience. This form will help us understand what worked well and what areas can be improved for future sessions. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your overall rating for the session from the dropdown menu:
	* **session_rating**: Rate your experience with the session.
2. Provide a brief description of what you liked about the session:
	* **session_rating_happy**: Write a short note on the positive aspects of the session.
3. Enter the content of the session:
	* **session_content**: Describe the topics covered during the session.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the session:
	* **overall_satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you were with the session.
5. Select the training style that best suits your preferences:
	* **training_style**: Choose between traditional, modern, or hybrid approaches.
6. Evaluate the trainer's attitude:
	* **trainer_attitude**: Rate the trainer's helpfulness and responsiveness.
7. Choose how you would rate the communication:
	* **communication**: Rate the trainer's communication skills.
8. Enter the session length:
	* **session_length**: Provide the length of the session in numbers.
9. Enter the session duration:
	* **session_duration**: Provide the start and end time of the session.
10. Assess the trainer's professionalism:
	* **trainer_professionalism**: Rate the trainer's professionalism.
11. Add any additional feedback or comments:
	* **feedback_notes**: Type any additional thoughts or suggestions you have.
12. Enter your email:
	* **email**: Enter your contact email address.
13. Enter your phone number:
	* **phone_number**: This field is optional.
14. Indicate if you are open to follow-up questions:
	* **followup_questions**: Check if you are willing to receive follow-up questions or not.
