# gym_trainer_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "gym_trainer_evaluation_survey" form is designed to provide valuable insights for gym management to evaluate the effectiveness of their trainers and make informed decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions based on your recent experience with the trainer you are evaluating.
- You can select one or more options from the given choices for questions that allow multiple selections.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) in the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the trainer's services.
* **Communication** (Communication, text, optional): Describe your experience with the trainer's communication style. For example, how well did they explain the exercises, listen to your needs, and keep you updated on your progress?
* **Professionalism** (Professionalism, select one, *): Select one of the options: Highly, Moderately, Not very. Indicate the level of professionalism you observed in the trainer's behavior, such as punctuality, respect for members, and adherence to the training schedule.
* **Trainer Proficiency** (Trainer Proficiency, select one, *): Select one of the options: Highly, Moderately, Not very. Assess the trainer's ability to provide quality coaching and guidance.
* **Motivation** (Motivation, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options: Highly, Moderately, Not very. Rate the trainer's ability to motivate you during the training sessions.
* **Continuous Improvement** (Continuous Improvement, text, optional): Describe any suggestions or ideas you have for the trainer to improve their services.
* **Training Style** (Training Style, select one, optional): Select one of the options: Highly, Moderately, Not very. Rate the trainer's training style, such as their ability to adapt to your learning pace and provide personalized feedback.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective when evaluating the trainer's performance.
- Focus on specific examples or incidents that illustrate your points.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them in the "Continuous Improvement" field.
- If you have any other comments or feedback, you can add them in the "Continuous Improvement" field as well.
