# Personal Trimmer contract extension form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend a Personal Trimmer contract for clients. It's essential to fill out this form when a client's contract has expired or is approaching its end date. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate and timely extensions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Trainer Concept field with a brief description of the services you plan to offer the client.
2. In the Client Concept field, describe the client's goals and objectives you will work with.
3. Enter the Contract Start Date of the original contract.
4. Enter the Contract End Date you are requesting to extend.
5. If applicable, enter the Trainer Fee for the extended period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Concept** (Trainer Concept, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to describe the services you will provide to the client. Be as detailed as possible to ensure the client understands your approach.
* **Client Concept** (Client Concept, text, required: false)
	+ In this field, describe the client's goals and objectives you will work with. This will help you and the client stay on the same page throughout the contract.
* **Contract Start Date** (Contract Start Date, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date when the original contract began. This will help us track the contract's progression.
* **Contract End Date** (Contract End Date, date, required: false)
	+ Please enter the new end date you are requesting for the contract extension.
* **Trainer Fee** (Trainer Fee, number, required: false)
	+ If you are requesting a fee for the extended period, enter the amount here. Please note that this is optional and only applicable for extended services.
