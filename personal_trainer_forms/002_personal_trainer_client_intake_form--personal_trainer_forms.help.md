# Personal Trainer Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Client Intake Form is designed to collect information from clients about their contact details, training preferences, and medical history to provide their personal trainer with a comprehensive understanding of their goals and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Contact Information**: Fill in your name, address, phone number, and email address accurately.
2. **Training Preferences**: Choose your goals and frequency of training.
3. **Medical History**: Select any medical conditions, medications, or past injuries you may have.
4. **Payment Information**: Select your payment method and frequency.
5. **Contract**: Choose the duration of your contract.
6. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information about your payment terms and frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address to ensure your trainer can contact you easily.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so your trainer can reach you by phone.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so your trainer can contact you via email.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information of a person your trainer can reach in case of an emergency.
* **Goals**: Select all the goals that apply to you, such as Fitness, Weight Loss, Strength, Performance, Wellness, or Endurance.
* **Frequency**: Select how frequently you plan to train, such as 1-2 times/week, 2-3 times/week, 4-5 times/week, 6-7 times/week, or daily.
* **Personal History**: Use this text area to provide any relevant information about your past experiences or medical history.
* **Physical Characteristics**: Use this text area to provide any relevant information about your physical characteristics, such as height, weight, or body fat percentage.
* **Medical History**: Select any medical conditions you have, such as High Blood Pressure, Allergies, Diabetes, or other conditions.
* **Medications**: Select any medications you are taking.
* **Family History**: Select any medical conditions that run in your family.
* **Past Injuries**: Select any past injuries you may have.
* **Training Equipment**: Select any training equipment you have or prefer to use.
* **Goal Duration**: Enter the duration of your training goal.
* **Goal Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your training sessions.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay, such as Bank Draft, Credit Card, or Check.
* **Payment Frequency**: Select how frequently you plan to pay, such as Monthly, Bi-Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Payment Plan**: Enter any additional information about your payment plan.
* **Contract**: Choose the duration of your contract, such as 1-Month, 3-Month, or 6-Month.
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your contract to start.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any additional terms of payment.
* **Payment Terms Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Note**: Use this text area to provide any additional information about your payment terms.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review your form before submitting it to ensure all fields are complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your personal trainer for assistance.
* This form is confidential and will only be used for the purpose of your training program.
