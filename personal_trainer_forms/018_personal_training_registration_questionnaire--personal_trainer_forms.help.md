# Personal Training Registration Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients to create a comprehensive profile for safe and effective personal training. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful training experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions for each section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`3`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`4`, `text`, required): Please enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Please enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`6`, `email`, required): Please enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, required): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`8`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Relationship** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you know your emergency contact (e.g. Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Partner, Friend, Other).
* **Medical History** (`10`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical history, please provide it.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`11`, `text`, optional): Please enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Fitness Goals** (`12`, `text`, optional): Please describe your fitness goals.
* **Height** (`13`, `number`, optional): Please enter your height in inches or cm.
* **Weight** (`14`, `number`, optional): Please enter your weight in pounds or kg.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`15`, `text`, optional): Please enter your emergency contact's city.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`16`, `text`, optional): Please enter your emergency contact's state.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (`17`, `text`, optional): Please enter your emergency contact's zip code.
* **Relationship Type** (`18`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you know your emergency contact (e.g. Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Partner, Friend, Other).
* **Height Unit** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Select the unit of your height (e.g. ft, in, cm, m, yd).
* **Weight Unit** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Select the unit of your weight (e.g. lb, kg, st, lbs, oz).
* **Phone Number Type** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of phone number you have (e.g. Home, Work, Other).
* **Relationship Type Note** (`22`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information about your relationship.
* **Fitness Experience** (`23`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your level of fitness experience (e.g. Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Height** (`24`, `time`, optional): Please enter your height in hours and minutes.
* **Weight** (`25`, `number`, optional): Please enter your weight.

Note: Please fill out all required fields to complete the form. Optional fields are for additional information only.
