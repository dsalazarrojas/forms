# dodgeball_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dodgeball Registration Form is used for the registration process of dodgeball players. It is meant for users to provide their personal and contact information, as well as medical clearance and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the User Information section (Page 1).
2. Provide your name.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Enter your phone number and email (if applicable).
5. Continue to the subsequent pages and fill in the required information for each section (e.g., dodgeball experience, emergency contact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the team roster.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Dodgeball Experience** (`dodgeball_experience`, number, optional): If you have experience playing dodgeball, please enter the number of years you have played.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and relationship of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, optional): Enter any medical information that may be relevant to your participation in the dodgeball league.
* **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_notes`, note, optional): Enter any emergency contact information or notes.
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, select one, optional): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have any medical clearance or restrictions.
* **Team** (`team`, select one, optional): Select the team you would like to be registered with.
* **Team Position** (`team_position`, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple team positions you would like to be considered for.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select one, optional): Select 'Paid' or 'Not Paid' to indicate your payment status.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of registration (New, Returning, Free).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the dodgeball league administrators.
* This form is for internal use only, and the information provided will not be shared publicly.
