# zumba_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The zumba_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from users about their Zumba class experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Instructor Name" field.
2. Select the specific Zumba class you attended from the "Zumba Class" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date you attended the class in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the time you attended the class in the "Time" field.
5. Type your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
6. Enter any additional notes you may have in the "Note" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field (we will use this to contact you if needed).
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Instructor Name** (Text): This is the field where you enter the name of your instructor.
* **Zumba Class** (Select One): Choose the specific Zumba class you attended from the list of options.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date you attended the Zumba class.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time you attended the Zumba class.
* **Feedback** (Text): This is the field where you can type your feedback about your experience.
* **Note** (Note): Enter any additional notes you may have about your experience.
* **E-mail** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
