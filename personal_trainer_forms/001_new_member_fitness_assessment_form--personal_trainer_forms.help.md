# New Member Fitness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Member Fitness Assessment Form is designed to collect personal information from new members to create a member profile and provide a better experience for the member. This form is not mandatory, but it helps the fitness center or gym tailor their services and communication to each member's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field. Please note that this must be a valid email address.
4. Fill in your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (new_member_fitness_assessment_form_first_name, text, optional):** Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Last Name (new_member_fitness_assessment_form_last_name, text, optional):** Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email (new_member_fitness_assessment_form_email, email, optional):** A valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number (new_member_fitness_assessment_form_phone_number, text, optional):** Your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Address (new_member_fitness_assessment_form_address, text, optional):** Enter your current address for us to better understand your location.

## Tips

* If you're having trouble finding your ID or driver's license, you can use any other valid identification document to fill in your first and last name.
* If you're not sure about your email address, just enter the one you use for personal emails or the one you created for our services.
* If you're not sure about your phone number, just enter the one you use for personal calls or the one you created for our services.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you with important updates and information.
* If you're not sure about your address, just enter the address where you'll be staying during your visit or the one you're currently residing at.
