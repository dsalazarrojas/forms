# Fitness Performance Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate a client's fitness performance and progress in a training program. It collects information about the client's exercise preferences, training plan, workout schedule, performance data, and progress tracking. This data will be used to provide personalized feedback and guidance to help the client achieve their fitness goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on each page.
2. Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully to ensure accurate evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Client Information**
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing general information about the client. Please include the client's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.

**Exercise Preferences**
* **Exercise Preferences** (`exercise_preferences`, `text`, required/optional): In this field, please list the client's preferred exercises, routines, or activities they enjoy doing for fitness.

**Training Plan**
* **Training Plan** (`training_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe the client's current training plan, including goals, objectives, and any specific exercises or activities included.

**Workout Schedule**
* **Workout Schedule** (`workout_schedule`, `text`, required/optional): Share the client's current workout schedule, including days, times, and frequencies.

**Performance Data**
* **Performance Data** (`performance_data`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant performance data, such as measurements, progress photos, or weight loss records.

**Progress Tracking**
* **Progress Tracking** (`progress_tracking`, `text`, required/optional): In this field, please track the client's progress, including successes, challenges, or areas for improvement.

Note: This form is for general guidance and should not be considered as a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional before starting a new exercise or training program.
