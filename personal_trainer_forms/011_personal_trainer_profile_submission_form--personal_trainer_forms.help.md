## Personal Trainer Profile Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a submission form for personal trainers to provide their profile information. It helps us better understand their qualifications, experience, and availability to match them with clients. It's essential to fill this form accurately to ensure we can effectively match you with suitable clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you've entered all required fields (marked as "required").
3. Double-check your information before submission to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you. Please use your actual email address, not a work or other contact email.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your official documents. This will be used to contact you for further information or to schedule sessions with clients.

### Bio
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe yourself, your experience, and your qualifications as a personal trainer. This will help us understand your background and match you with suitable clients.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid delays or errors in the matching process.
* If you've submitted previously, please review and update your profile to reflect any changes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
