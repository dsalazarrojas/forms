## Fitness Equipment Resource Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help fitness enthusiasts create a comprehensive guide for finding and utilizing the right fitness equipment for their goals and needs. The purpose of this form is to gather information about your fitness goals, available space and budget, and specific equipment requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start on the first page where you will be asked to describe your fitness goals. Please provide a brief description of your goals, including the types of exercises you would like to perform.
2. Proceed to the next page where you will be asked about your available space and budget. Provide the dimensions of your available space and a breakdown of your budget.
3. On the third page, answer questions about your equipment needs, including what type of equipment you will be using, and what equipment you will need.
4. Finally, provide any additional equipment you would like to recommend or have already found helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Goals** (`form_first_page`): Describe your fitness goals in a few sentences. For example, "I want to lose weight" or "I want to improve my running performance".
* **Briefly describe your fitness goals** (`form_goal_brief`): Provide a brief description of your fitness goals. This is an optional field, but it will help us better understand your goals.
* **More detailed goals and objectives** (`form_goal_specifics`): Describe the types of exercises you would like to perform, such as "weight lifting", "cardio", etc.
* **Available space dimensions** (`form_space_requirements`): Measure your available space in feet or meters. This will help us determine the best equipment for your space.
* **How much budget do you have available** (`form_budget_details`): Describe your budget for fitness equipment.
* **Available space type** (`form_space_types`): Select "Yes" if you have limited space, "No" if you have plenty of space, or "Other" if you have a unique space situation.
* **Form Space Dimensions** (`form_space_dimensions`): Describe your available space dimensions, including length, width, and height.
* **Available space orientation** (`form_space_orientation`): Describe the orientation of your available space, such as "floor", "ceiling", etc.
* **Budget Breakdown** (`form_budget_breakdown`): Provide a breakdown of your budget for fitness equipment.
* **Equipment budget breakdown** (`form_budget_breakdown_2`): Provide a breakdown of your budget for equipment.
* **How will you allocate your budget** (`form_budget_allocation`): Describe how you plan to allocate your budget for fitness equipment.
* **Equipment budget allocation** (`form_budget_allocation_2`): Describe how you plan to allocate your budget for equipment.
* **What type of equipment will you be using** (`form_goal_type`): Select "Yes" if you will be using the equipment, "No" if you won't be using it, or "Other" if you have a different type of equipment in mind.
* **Type of equipment** (`form_goal_type_2`): Select "Yes" if you will be using the equipment, "No" if you won't be using it, or "Other" if you have a different type of equipment in mind.
