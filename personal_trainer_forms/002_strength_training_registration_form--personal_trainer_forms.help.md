# Strength Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information to create a customized and safe fitness experience. Please provide accurate answers to help us get to know you better and ensure your well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the user information form (questions 1-3).
2. Choose your preferred email address.
3. Select your available training days and time.
4. Provide any medical conditions, allergies, or previous injuries that may affect your training.
5. Indicate if you have medical clearance or insurance.
6. Share your training goals and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, Text, Optional)
	+ Please enter your basic details such as name, age, and address.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional)
	+ Enter your preferred email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Optional)
	+ Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Height** (Height, Number, Optional)
	+ Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (Weight, Number, Optional)
	+ Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Strength Training Goals** (Strength Training Goals, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Choose your primary goals (Muscle Gain, Weight Loss, Endurance, Flexibility, Cardiovascular Endurance).
* **Available Days** (Available Days, Select One, Optional)
	+ Select the days you are available for training.
* **Available Time** (Available Time, Select One, Optional)
	+ Choose your preferred time for training (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide a contact name and phone number in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (Emergency Phone, Text, Optional)
	+ Enter the contact phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, Text, Optional)
	+ Share any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact your training.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, Text, Optional)
	+ List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Insurance** (Medical Insurance, Select One, Optional)
	+ Indicate if you have medical insurance.
* **Injury History** (Injury History, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Check 'Yes' if you have a history of injuries.
* **Previous Injuries** (Previous Injuries, Text, Optional)
	+ Describe any previous injuries.
* **Injury Precautions** (Injury Precautions, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide any injury-related precautions.
* **Medication** (Medication, Text, Optional)
	+ List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Clearance** (Medical Clearance, Select One, Optional)
	+ Indicate if you have received a doctor's note for medical clearance.
* **Training Goals** (Training Goals, Text, Optional)
	+ Describe your training goals.
* **Training Frequency** (Training Frequency, Text, Optional)
	+ Share your preferred training frequency.
* **Training Intensity** (Training Intensity, Text, Optional)
	+ Choose your preferred training intensity.
* **Training Type** (Training Type, Text, Optional)
	+ Select your preferred training type.
* **Training Schedule** (Training Schedule, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide your available training schedule.
* **Comments** (Comments, Text, Optional)
	+ Share any additional comments or questions.
