# Personal Trainer Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Recommendation Form is designed to be filled out by users seeking personalized guidance in their fitness journey. This form will help the personal trainer understand your needs, preferences, and availability to create a tailored workout plan just for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the text fields provided.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Select up to three fitness goals that you are aiming for.
4. Choose the days you are available for training (you can select multiple days).
5. Enter the time you are available for training (in 24-hour format, e.g., 08:00).
6. Select the best time of day you would like to be contacted by the trainer (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
7. Choose the locations where you would like to train (you can select multiple locations, e.g., Home, Studio, or Gym).
8. Select your current experience level in fitness (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
9. Choose up to seven preferred activities for your training (you can select multiple activities, e.g., Cardio, Strength, Flexibility, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your family name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted by the personal trainer.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Fitness Goal**: Select up to three goals you are aiming for (e.g., Goal 1, Goal 2, Goal 3).
* **Available Days**: Choose the days of the week you are available for training (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
* **Available Time**: Enter the time you are available for training in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Best Time to Contact**: Select the time of day you would like to be contacted by the trainer (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Preferred Location**: Choose the locations where you would like to train (e.g., Home, Studio, Gym).
* **Experience Level**: Select your current level of experience in fitness (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Preferred Activities**: Choose up to seven activities you would like to focus on during your training (e.g., Cardio, Strength, Flexibility, etc.).
