# Fitness Program Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients interested in fitness programs. It will help fitness professionals or programs determine which programs would be the best fit for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your details in the space provided.
2. Select your training preferences on the second page.
3. Provide any additional information that will help us understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: This is the first field in the form. Please provide any relevant details about yourself, such as your name, contact information, or any other information you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Training Preferences**: On this page, you will find several options for selecting your training preferences. Please choose one or multiple options that best describe your interests.
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any additional information you would like to share with us. You can provide any comments, concerns, or context about your interest in fitness programs.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions thoroughly to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, you can reach out to us using the contact information below.
* Contact: form@gic.mx
