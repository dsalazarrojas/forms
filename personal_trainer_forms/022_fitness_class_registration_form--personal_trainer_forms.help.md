# Fitness Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a fitness class program. It collects necessary information to ensure a safe and effective experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields marked with *.
2. Enter your name, email address, age, and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
3. Select your gender from the options provided.
4. For your emergency contact, enter their name, phone number, and email address.
5. Specify your medical conditions, if any.
6. Answer the questions regarding your program preferences (type, start date, and duration).
7. Indicate your consent by selecting the appropriate option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your active email address.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age in numeric format (e.g., 25).
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your program (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your program (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of program you are interested in.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Select the duration of the program.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have an emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your consent to participate in the program.
