# Personal Training Agreement And Guidelines Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect necessary information for creating a personal training agreement between the client and trainer. It includes fields for contact information, training details, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **client_name** in the field provided. This is your name that will be used for communication purposes.
2. Enter your **email** in the field provided. This will be the email address that we will use to communicate with you.
3. Enter your **contact_number** in the field provided. This is your phone number that we will use to contact you in case of emergencies.
4. Provide a detailed description of your **training_plan**, including your goals and expectations for the training sessions.
5. If applicable, specify your **emergency_contact** name and relationship to the person, as well as their **emergency_contact_method** (e.g., phone, text, email).
6. Specify your **payment_terms** and **payment_frequency**. These fields should include the payment amount and frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
7. Choose the **payment_method** you would like to use for payment (e.g., cash, bank transfer, PayPal).
8. Choose the **payment_date** for the first payment, and the **contract_start_date** and **contract_end_date** for the contract term.
9. Select the **contract_duration** for the training contract.
10. Choose the **training_frequency** for the training sessions (e.g., daily, 2-3 times a week, etc.).
11. Select the **training_time** for the training sessions (e.g., 30 minutes, 60 minutes, etc.).
12. Choose the **training_location** for the training sessions (e.g., home, in-person, online).
13. Choose the **emergency_contact_method** for emergency contacts (e.g., phone, text, email).
14. If you have any **medical_conditions** that need to be considered for the training, please provide details in this field.
15. If you have any **emergency_contact_name** and relationship, please provide details in this field.
16. If you have any **medical_notes**, please provide details in this field.
17. If you have any **payment_schedule**, please provide details in this field.
18. If you have any **training_type**, please provide details in this field.
19. Enter your **signature** in the field provided, confirming that you have read and understood this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_name**: Enter your name as it will be used for communication purposes.
* **email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **contact_number**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **training_plan**: Describe your training goals and expectations.
* **emergency_contact**: If applicable, provide the name and relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: If applicable, describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_method**: Choose the method to contact you in case of an emergency (e.g., phone, text, email).
* **payment_terms**: Specify the payment amount and frequency.
* **payment_method**: Choose the payment method (e.g., cash, bank transfer, PayPal).
* **payment_date**: Choose the payment date.
* **contract_start_date**: Choose the contract start date.
* **contract_end_date**: Choose the contract end date.
* **contract_duration**: Specify the contract duration.
* **training_frequency**: Choose the training frequency (e.g., daily, 2-3 times a week, etc.).
* **training_time**: Choose the training time (e.g., 30 minutes, 60 minutes, etc.).
* **training_location**: Choose the training location (e.g., home, in-person, online).
* **emergency_contact_name**: If applicable, provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **medical_conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, provide details in this field.
* **training_goal**: Specify your training goal.
* **signature**: Enter your signature, confirming that you have read and understood this form.
