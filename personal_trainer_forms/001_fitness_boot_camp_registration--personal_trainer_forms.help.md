# Fitness Boot Camp Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Boot Camp Registration form is used to collect information from potential participants for the Fitness Boot Camp program. This form helps the fitness center staff to manage the registration process and ensure that participants are properly registered for the program.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fitness Boot Camp Registration form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Age** in the "Age" field.
4. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your desired **Membership Type** (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly) in the "Membership Type" field.
7. Choose your preferred **Class Time** (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) in the "Class Time" field.
8. Select your **Membership Start Date** and **Membership End Date** in the respective fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Age** (Number): Enter your age.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (Date): Select your desired start date for the membership.
* **Time** (Time): Select your preferred class time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Membership Type** (Select Multiple): Select one or more membership types (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly) that suits your needs.
* **Class Time** (Select One): Choose your preferred class time from the available options.
* **Membership Start Date** (Date): Select your desired start date for the membership.
* **Membership End Date** (Date): Select your desired end date for the membership.
