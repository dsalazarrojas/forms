# leg_extension_exercise_guide_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from personal trainers regarding the leg extension exercises they perform, which might be used for research, assessment, or administrative purposes within a fitness center or gym.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
- Ensure all required fields are accurate and up-to-date.
- Enter information in the correct format (e.g., date of birth in DD/MMM format).
- If you are a participant, fill out the form as accurately and truthfully as possible.
- If you are a trainer, provide detailed and honest information about the exercises you perform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1): Enter your name as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (2): Enter your surname as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Email** (3): Enter your valid email address.
- **Contact Number** (4): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Preferred Training Day** (5): Select the day of the week you prefer to train, Monday to Sunday.
- **Preferred Training Time** (6): Choose a time that works best for you, e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening.
- **Training Goals** (7): Briefly describe your goals for the exercise, e.g., strength, flexibility, or endurance.
- **Medical Conditions** (8): List any medical conditions that affect your ability to perform the exercise.
- **Exercise Experience** (9): Select the level of your experience with exercises, e.g., beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
- **Current Workout Plan** (10): Describe your current workout routine.
- **Preferred Weight** (11): Choose your preferred weight for the exercise.
- **Training Area** (12): Describe the area you want to train, e.g., legs, arms, etc.
- **Training Type** (13): Select the type of training you prefer, e.g., strength, high-intensity interval, etc.
- **Training Intensity** (14): Choose how intense you prefer to train, e.g., low, medium, high.
- **Leg Extension Exercise** (15): Briefly describe the leg extension exercise you perform.
- **Leg Extension Technique** (16): Describe the technique you use for the leg extension exercise.
- **Leg Extension Frequency** (17): Select how frequently you perform the leg extension exercise, e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.
- **Leg Extension Duration** (18): Choose the length of time you spend on the leg extension exercise.
- **Leg Extension Description** (19): Describe the leg extension exercise in more detail.
- **Trainer Feedback** (20): Provide feedback from your trainer or instructor.
- **Participant Feedback** (21): Share feedback about your performance from a participant or colleague.
- **Additional Comments** (22): Enter any other relevant information not covered elsewhere on the form.
- **Date Of Birth** (23): Enter the date of your birth in DD/MMM format (e.g., 12/08/1999).
- **Age** (24): Enter your age as of your date of birth.
- **Submit** (25): Click the submit button to send the form for review and approval.
