# personal_training_session_recording_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from personal training clients regarding their consent for recording sessions. It aims to ensure that clients are informed and agree to the recording of their sessions, whether it's audio or video.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Info section with your details.
2. Select your preferred recording type (Audio Recordings, Video Recordings, or None) in the Recording section.
3. Choose which submissions (if any) you would like to be recorded in the Submission section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, optional): This section is for providing your basic contact information. Please fill in your name and any other relevant details as required.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): This section is for providing more specific information about the session you are attending. You can include any additional details you feel are relevant.
* **Recording** (`recording`, select_one, required): Please select whether you want your session to be recorded as Audio Recordings, Video Recordings, or None. This will determine how your session will be captured.
* **Submissions** (`submission`, select_multiple, required): Select which submissions (if any) you would like to be recorded during the session. Please choose the submissions that apply to you.

## Tips
- Make sure you have read and understood the recording policy before completing this form.
- Clearly select your preferred recording type and submissions to avoid any misunderstandings.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your trainer or contact the training organization.
