# Fitness Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Liability Waiver form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals before they participate in fitness-related activities or programs. This form helps to ensure the well-being and safety of the individual during their participation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your emergency contact information, including the name and contact details of the person responsible for reaching you in case of an emergency.
3. If applicable, describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
4. Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
5. Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
6. Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
7. If you have any general medical information, please provide it in the space below.
8. If you have any medical history, please describe it in the space provided.
9. If you have any physical conditions that may affect your participation, please describe them in the space provided.
10. If you have any allergies, please list them in the space provided.
11. If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in the space provided.
12. If you have any insurance coverage, please provide the details in the space provided.
13. If you are visiting a medical facility, please provide the details in the space provided.
14. Finally, provide your personal data and emergency services information, including phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This is the first field where you can enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the person responsible for reaching you in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship**: If you have a relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, guardian), describe it here.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **General Medical**: If you have any general medical information, such as medications or implants, please provide it here.
* **Medical History**: If you have any medical history, such as surgeries or hospitalizations, please describe it in the space provided.
* **Physical Condition**: If you have any physical conditions that may affect your participation, such as a recent injury or disability, please describe them here.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, please list them in the space provided.
* **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions, such as diabetes or heart conditions, please describe them in the space provided.
* **Insurance**: If you have any insurance coverage, please provide the details in the space provided.
* **Medical Facility**: If you are visiting a medical facility, please provide the details in the space provided.
* **Personal Data**: This field is intended for you to enter any other personal data that you feel is relevant to your participation.
* **Emergency Services**: Enter the information for the emergency services, including phone number and email address.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please ensure you fill out all the required fields accurately to ensure your safety and well-being during participation.
