# Group Fitness Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Fitness Onboarding Form is designed to collect new member information and emergency contact details to create a comprehensive member profile.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For emergency contact relationships, select the most accurate description from the dropdown menu.
3. If you have an "Other" relationship, provide additional details in the text fields.
4. Ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Member Information (id: 1, text, required):** Enter your name, username, or any other identifying information for your membership profile.
* **Emergency Contact (id: 2, text, optional):** Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact (id: 3, select_one, required):** Select the most accurate description from the dropdown menu for your emergency contact relationship.
	+ Parent
	+ Spouse
	+ Partner
	+ Other
* **Other Relationship (id: 4, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, provide a brief description of the relationship.
* **Address (id: 5, text, required):** Enter your address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Line 1 (id: 6, text, required):** Enter the first line of your address.
* **Line 2 (id: 7, text, optional):** Enter any additional address details, if applicable.
* **City (id: 8, text, required):** Enter the city where you reside.
* **State (id: 9, select_one, required):** Select the state where you reside from the dropdown menu.
	+ AL
	+ AK
	+ AZ
	+ AR
	+ CA
	+ CO
	+ CT
	+ DE
	+ FL
	+ GA
* **Country (id: 10, select_one, required):** Select whether you reside in the United States or another country.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Zip (id: 11, text, required):** Enter your zip code for postal and delivery purposes.
* **Other Emergency Contact Relationship Description (id: 12, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, provide additional details about your relationship.
* **Phone (id: 13, text, required):** Enter your phone number for contact and emergency purposes.
* **Email (id: 14, email, optional):** Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted electronically.
* **Date of Birth (id: 15, date, required):** Enter your date of birth for demographic and age verification purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (id: 16, text, optional):** Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Extension (id: 17, text, optional):** Enter any additional phone extension for your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Alternate) (id: 18, text, optional):** Enter another phone number for your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Details (id: 19, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, provide any additional details.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Notes (id: 20, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, provide any notes or comments.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Email (id: 21, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, enter the email address for your contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Phone (id: 22, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, enter another phone number for your contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Relationship (id: 23, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, describe the nature of the relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Name (id: 24, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, enter the name of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Relationship Details (id: 25, text, optional):** If you selected "Other" for the emergency contact relationship, provide any additional details about the relationship.
