### Personal Training Waiver - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The Personal Training Waiver form is used to collect essential information and obtain informed consent from clients before starting a workout program. This includes their personal details, emergency contact information, and a waiver that acknowledges they understand and accept the risks associated with physical exercise.

#### How To Complete This Form

1.  **Personal Training Waiver**: This is the first page of the form. Please type in your Personal Training Waiver information.
2.  **First Name**: Enter your first name.
3.  **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
4.  **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/12/1999).
5.  **Date of Last Medical Check-Up**: Enter the date of your last medical check-up.
6.  **Email**: Enter your email address.
7.  **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
8.  **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
9.  **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
10.  **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
11.  **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
12.  **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, sibling).
13.  **Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
14.  **City**: Enter the city of your emergency contact's address.
15.  **State**: Enter the state of your emergency contact's address.
16.  **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your emergency contact's address.
17.  **Country**: Enter the country of your emergency contact's address.
18.  **Informed Consent**: Select 'Yes' if you understand and agree to the terms of the waiver. If you select 'No', please provide a reason in the text area below.
19.  **Informed Consent Details**: If you selected 'No' for informed consent, please provide a brief explanation.
20.  **Waiver**: This is the waiver that you acknowledge the risks associated with physical exercise. Select 'Yes' if you understand and agree to the risks.
