# Fitness Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from potential clients about their contact details, health history, availability status, and training goals, as well as their acknowledgement of terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself. This will help us better understand your needs and preferences, ensuring that we can provide you with the best possible experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (7): Enter your primary email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (8): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Height** (9): Enter your height in centimeters (cm).
* **Weight** (10): Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Smoker** (11): Select 'Yes' if you are a smoker, 'No' if you are not.
* **Heart Condition** (12): Select 'Yes' if you have any heart condition, 'No' if you do not.
* **Available** (13): Select 'Yes' if you are available to start training immediately, 'No' if you are not.
* **Unavailable** (14): Select 'No' if you are unavailable to start training immediately, 'Yes' if you are.
* **Goal** (15): Select all goals that apply to your training needs, such as 'Weight Loss', 'Muscle Gain', 'Endurance', etc.
* **Frequency** (16): Select all frequency options that apply to your training needs, such as 'Daily', 'Weekly', 'Monthly', etc.
* **Duration** (17): Select all duration options that apply to your training needs, such as 'Short', 'Medium', 'Long', etc.
* **Date** (18): Enter the date you are available to start training.
* **Time** (19): Enter the time you are available to start training.
* **Terms of Service** (20): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of our services.
* **Signature** (21): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.

Please note that some fields may be optional or have specific instructions, so be sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your personal trainer for assistance.
