# Personal Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Liability Waiver form is designed to collect liability waiver information from clients participating in personal training programs. This form protects both the client and the training provider by ensuring a clear understanding of risks and liabilities involved in physical activity.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
- Review each field carefully and ask your training provider if you have any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you're having trouble filling out the form, reach out to your training provider for assistance.
- Please note that this form is for personal training programs and is not a substitute for medical clearance or other necessary certifications.
