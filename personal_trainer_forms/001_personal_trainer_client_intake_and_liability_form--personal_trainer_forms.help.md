# Personal Trainer Client Intake And Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from clients before they start personal training sessions. It helps establish a clear understanding of the client's health status, expectations, and responsibilities, ensuring a safe and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2.  Read each field carefully and make sure to understand its purpose and implications.
3.  Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`personal_trainer_client_intake_and_liability_form_1`, text, false): Please enter your name, email, and phone number to contact you.
* **Client Health Information** (`personal_trainer_client_intake_and_liability_form_2`, text, false): Provide any relevant health information, such as medical conditions, allergies, or previous injuries, that may affect your training sessions.
* **Client Agreement** (`personal_trainer_client_intake_and_liability_form_3`, select_multiple, false): Please select all that apply:
	+ In consideration of all information collected from this form, I understand that I will be working with a personal trainer who is not a healthcare professional.
	+ I do not have any physical or mental health conditions that may pose a risk during training sessions.
	+ I do not have any medical conditions that require immediate attention.
	+ I am aware that it is your responsibility to provide a safe training environment.
	+ I am aware that personal trainers are not healthcare professionals.
* **Additional Information** (`personal_trainer_client_intake_and_liability_form_4`, text, false): If you have any additional information that you feel is relevant to your training, please provide it here.
* **Client Contact Information** (`personal_trainer_client_intake_and_liability_form_5`, text, false): Enter your email and phone number so we can contact you effectively.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your personal trainer.
* It's essential to review and understand each field carefully to ensure a safe and effective training experience.
