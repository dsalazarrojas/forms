# personal-trainer-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in requesting personal training services, including personal coaching, nutrition counseling, or workout plans. It collects their contact information, training preferences, and schedule availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your training interest(s) by choosing one or more options from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the days you are interested in having a session by selecting one or more days from the list.
4. Provide your availability by selecting a date.
5. Add any additional notes about your request in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (1): Enter your full name as you would like to be contacted.
* **Contact Email** (2): Enter your email address where you would like to receive communication about your request.
* **Contact Phone** (3): Enter your phone number where you would like to be contacted.
* **Training Interest** (4): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Personal Training: One-on-one training sessions.
	+ Nutrition Counseling: Guidance on healthy eating and meal planning.
	+ Workout Plan: A customized exercise plan to reach your fitness goals.
* **Schedule Interest** (5): Select one or more days you are interested in having a session:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Availability** (6): Choose a date for your session.
* **Notes** (7): Add any additional information about your request, such as specific goals or concerns.

## Tips
Remember to be specific about your training interest and schedule availability to help us better understand your needs.
