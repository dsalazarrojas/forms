# Personal Trainer Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Renewal Form is designed to collect necessary information from personal trainers to renew their certifications and services. This form will help us update your trainer profile and ensure you receive your renewed certification and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your trainer license number in the trainer license field.
6. Describe your goals in the goals field.
7. Select your services offered (yes/no) in the services offered field.
8. Enter the payment amount in the payment amount field.
9. Select whether you offer payment type (yes/no) in the payment type field.
10. Select the payment date for renewal.
11. Select the renewal date for your certification.
12. Enter any payment terms in the payment terms field.
13. Select whether you have trainer experience (yes/no) in the trainer experience field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Renewal Details**: This field is a text field where you can enter any additional details about your renewal.
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Trainer License**: Enter your trainer license number.
- **Goals**: Describe your goals and objectives for the renewal.
- **Services Offered**: Select all services you offer, such as "Yes" for each service.
- **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for the renewal.
- **Payment Type**: Select whether you offer payment type, such as "Yes" for yes.
- **Payment Date**: Select the date for payment.
- **Renewal Date**: Select the date for renewal.
- **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions.
- **Trainer Experience**: Select whether you have trainer experience, such as "Yes" for yes.

Tips:
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us at <insert contact email or phone number>.
- Please fill out the form honestly and accurately to ensure a smooth renewal process.
