# Schedule Your Fitness Testing Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for scheduling a fitness testing appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the days you are available for the appointment by clicking on the checkboxes next to the days you prefer.
2. Once you have selected the available days, click on the "Select Time Slots" field to choose the specific time slots for the appointment.
3. Enter any additional information in the "Note" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Time Slots**: Select the days you are available for the appointment. The available days are:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Selected Time Slots**: This field is required and will list the selected time slots for the appointment.
* **Note**: This field is not required and is for providing additional information about your appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the days you are available for the appointment.
* Choose the specific time slots that suit you best.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
