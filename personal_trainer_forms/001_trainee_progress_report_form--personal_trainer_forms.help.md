# Trainee Progress Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to monitor and record the progress of trainees in their roles. It captures essential information from both the trainee and the reviewer to provide a comprehensive picture of the trainee's development and areas needing improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the trainee's name and ID in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose the trainee's current status regarding their role, such as whether they are doing well, poorly, or satisfactory.
3. Select the skills the trainee has demonstrated, such as communication, problem-solving, time management, and teamwork skills.
4. Indicate the current training status of the trainee, like whether they have completed, are in progress, or have not started.
5. Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer and trainee.
6. Select the review date and time.
7. Enter the reviewer's name and title.
8. Sign the form with both the reviewer and trainee's signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainee Name** (id: 12): Enter the name of the trainee being reported on.
* **Trainee ID** (id: 13): Enter the ID of the trainee being reported on.
* **Trainee Email** (id: 14): Enter the email address of the trainee being reported on.
* **Trainee Phone** (id: 15): Enter the phone number of the trainee being reported on.
* **Supervisor Name** (id: 16): Enter the name of the supervisor or reviewer.
* **Supervisor Title** (id: 17): Enter the title of the supervisor or reviewer.
* **General Info** (id: 1): Provide a brief description of the trainee's role and any relevant background information.
* **Goals** (id: 2): Select one of the options to describe the trainee's performance: Trainee is doing well, Trainee is doing poorly, or Trainee is doing satisfactory.
* **Performance** (id: 3): Provide an update on the trainee's performance, describing their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Skills** (id: 4): Select all applicable skills the trainee has demonstrated, such as communication, problem-solving, time management, and teamwork skills.
* **Training Status** (id: 5): Select one of the options to describe the trainee's current training status: Completed, In progress, or Not started.
* **Comments** (id: 6): Enter any feedback, suggestions, or concerns from the reviewer and trainee.
* **Next Steps** (id: 7): Provide an update on what steps the trainee should take next to improve their performance or skills.
* **Review Date** (id: 8): Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Review Time** (id: 9): Enter the time the review was completed.
* **Reviewer Name** (id: 10): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (id: 11): Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Review Status** (id: 19): Select one of the options to describe the current status of the review: Completed, In progress, or Not started.
* **Review Signature** (id: 21): Sign the form with the reviewer's signature.
* **Trainee Signature** (id: 22): Sign the form with the trainee's signature.
* **Review Version** (id: 20): Enter the version number of the form, which will automatically update each time the form is filled out.
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 25): Enter the tool or software used for the review.
* **Trainee Version Control** (id: 24): Enter the version control number of the trainee's progress report form.
* **Trainee Progress Report Form Version** (id: 20): Enter the version number of the trainee progress report form.
* **Trainee Version Control** (id: 24): Enter the version control number of the trainee's progress report form.
* **Comments** (id: 18): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
