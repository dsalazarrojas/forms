# Personal Training Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Consultation Questionnaire is designed to gather essential information from clients interested in taking part in a personal training program. This form is intended to help trainers assess each client's unique needs and tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Provide your valid email address to ensure communication and correspondence.
3. Enter your phone number in the designated text field.
4. Select your training goal(s) from the list provided.
5. Choose the days you are available for sessions.
6. Set your goal date and time for the training program.
7. Provide any relevant injury history or medical information.
8. Enter any emergency contact information (name, phone number, and relation).
9. Select your preferred payment method and frequency.
10. Enter the payment amount you are willing to pay.
11. Add any additional notes or medical information (if necessary).
12. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Training Goal**: Select one or more of the provided goals (General Health, Weight Loss, Injury Prevention) that align with your intentions for the training program.
* **Available Days**: Choose the days you are available for sessions (Monday to Sunday).
* **Goal Date**: Set your preferred start date for the training program.
* **Goal Time**: Set your preferred start time for the training program.
* **Injury History**: Provide any relevant injury history or medical information that may affect your training.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide contact information for an emergency contact (name, phone number, relation).
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relation**: Select the relation of your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother/Sister, Spouse, Child, Other).
* **Emergency Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Insurance Info**: Provide any relevant insurance information (if applicable).
* **Doctor Info**: Enter any relevant medical information from your doctor (if applicable).
* **Medication**: List any medications you are currently taking (if applicable).
* **Medical Notes**: Provide any additional medical notes or information (if necessary).
* **Training Sessions**: Select the frequency of training sessions you are willing to commit to (1x/week, 2x/week, 3x/week, 4x/week, 5x/week).
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Frequency**: Select the payment frequency you are comfortable with (Monthly, Weekly, Bi-Weekly).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount you are willing to pay per session.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or information about your training preferences.
* **Signed Up For**: Set the date you signed up for the training program (if applicable).
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement and understanding of the terms.
