# Online Fitness Coach Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from potential clients for the online fitness coach service. The form helps gather essential details about the client's medical history, emergency contacts, and fitness goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2.  Enter your Personal Trainer Form information in the first field.
3.  Provide your Medical History in the second field.
4.  List any medications you are currently taking in the third field.
5.  Specify your Fitness Goals in the fourth field.
6.  Enter your Emergency Contact's Name in the fifth field.
7.  Enter your Emergency Contact's Phone in the sixth field.
8.  Enter your Emergency Contact's Address in the seventh field.
9.  Enter your Emergency Contact's Email in the eighth field.
10. Specify the Relationship with your Alternate Emergency Contact in the ninth field.
11. Choose the Alternate Emergency Contact you want to list in the tenth field.
12. Enter the Name of the Alternate Emergency Contact in the eleventh field.
13. Enter the Phone number of the Alternate Emergency Contact in the twelfth field.
14. Enter the Address of the Alternate Emergency Contact in the thirteenth field.
15. Enter the Email of the Alternate Emergency Contact in the fourteenth field.
16. Enter any additional Relatives of the Alternate Emergency Contact in the fifteenth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Trainer Form** (`personal_trainer_form`, text, required): Please enter your personal trainer form information.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of your medical history.
*   **Health and Medications** (`health_and_medications`, text, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, text, required): Describe your fitness goals.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required): Choose your emergency contact from the list.
*   **Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
*   **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
*   **Relatives** (`emergency_contact_relatives`, text, required): List any additional relatives of your emergency contact.
*   **Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.
*   **Alternate Contact** (`emergency_contact_alternate`, select_one, required): Choose the alternate emergency contact from the list.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Name** (`emergency_contact_alternate_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your alternate emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Phone** (`emergency_contact_alternate_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your alternate emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Address** (`emergency_contact_alternate_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your alternate emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Email** (`emergency_contact_alternate_email`, email, required): Enter the email of your alternate emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Relatives** (`emergency_contact_alternate_relatives`, text, required): List any additional relatives of your alternate emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Notes** (`emergency_contact_alternate_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about your alternate emergency contact.
*   **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_alternate_relationship`, select_one, required): Specify the relationship with your alternate emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Alternate Address 2** (`emergency_contact_alternate_address_2`, text, required): Enter the address of your alternate emergency contact.

Please note that all fields with an asterisk (\*) are required to complete the form successfully.
