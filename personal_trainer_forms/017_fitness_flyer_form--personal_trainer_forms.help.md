# Fitness Flyer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect general information from customers to help us better understand their needs and preferences. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2. If a field is not applicable or not relevant to you, please leave it blank or indicate that it's not applicable.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YY).
* **Height** (`height`, number, required: false): Enter your height in inches.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender (Female, Male, Other).
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the days you are available for training (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required: false): Enter your preferred time for training.
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, note, required: false): Describe your fitness goals and objectives.
* **Available Coaches** (`available_coaches`, select_one, required: false): Check if you are available to train with a coach.
* **Available Trainers** (`available_trainers`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the trainers you are available to train with.
* **Available Locations** (`available_locations`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the locations you are available to train at (Gym, Home, Park, Online).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: true): Please provide any additional comments or information about your needs and preferences.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required: false): Check your relationship with your emergency contact person (Yes, No).
* **Emergency Contact Emergency** (`emergency_contact_emergency`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the emergency contact person's medical conditions (Heart Condition, Allergies, Medical Condition, Other).
* **Available Languages** (`available_languages`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the languages you are able to communicate in (English, Spanish, French, Chinese).
