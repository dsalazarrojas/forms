# Exercise Completion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exercise Completion Form is a document designed to collect information about an individual's participation in a physical exercise or training program. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and provides useful data for trainers and researchers to analyze and improve exercise programs.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into the following sections:

1. **Form Data Section (1)**: A text field where you can provide any additional context or notes about the exercise completion.
2. **Participant Information (2)**: 
	* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Enter your active email address. This will help us stay in touch with you about the exercise.
	* **Participant Phone (participant_phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
3. **Exercise Details (4, 6, 8)**: 
	* **Workout Date (workout_date)**: Enter the date of the exercise.
	* **Exercise Name (exercise_name)**: Enter the name of the exercise you completed.
	* **Exercise Description (exercise_description)**: Describe the exercise you completed, including any specific details.
	* **Exercise Outcomes (exercise_outcomes)**: Report any notable outcomes or results from the exercise.
4. **Additional Comments (7)**: Provide any additional comments or remarks about the exercise.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately to ensure the form is useful for trainers and researchers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
- The form can be submitted online or in person, as per your preference.
- Make sure to enter a valid email address to facilitate communication.
