This form is designed to gather information about a user's workout routine details. The purpose of this form is to collect essential data from users regarding their workout frequency, duration, start and end dates, and other relevant details. This form is crucial for personal trainers and fitness professionals to understand their clients' workout patterns and provide tailored guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for your workout routine.
2. Answer the questions about your workout details, including the frequency, duration, start and end dates, and other relevant information.
3. If applicable, provide your email and phone number for future reference.
4. Add any additional notes about your workout routine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Title** (`title`, text, required: false)

* This field is for you to enter a title for your workout routine. It's a text field where you can provide a brief name or description of your workout plan.

### 2. **Routine Details** (`routine_details`, text, required: false)

* In this field, please enter any additional details about your workout routine. This can be a general description or any specific information you'd like to share with us.

### 3. **Exercises** (`exercises`, select_multiple, required: true)

* In this field, choose if your workout includes exercises or not. This option helps us understand if you're currently doing any exercises as part of your routine.

### 4. **Workout Frequency** (`workout_frequency`, select_one, required: true)

* Please select the frequency of your workouts from the options provided: Daily, Every Other Day, 2-3 times a week, 4-5 times a week, or 6-7 times a week.

### 5. **Duration in Minutes** (`duration_in_minutes`, number, required: true)

* Enter the total duration of your workout in minutes. This is an essential piece of information for us to understand the scope of your routine.

### 6. **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: true)

* Please select the start date of your workout routine. This helps us track the start of your regimen.

### 7. **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: true)

* Choose the end date of your workout routine. This is essential for us to know when your routine concludes.

### 8. **Workout Days** (`workout_days`, select_multiple, required: true)

* Select the days of the week you work out. This helps us understand your weekly schedule.

### 9. **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false)

* If you have any additional notes or comments about your workout routine, you can add them here. This is an optional field for any extra information you'd like to share.

### 10. **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)

* If you'd like to be contacted via email for any updates or follow-ups, please enter your email address here. This is an optional field and not required for submission.

### 11. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false)

* If you'd like to be contacted via phone for any updates or follow-ups, please enter your phone number here. This is an optional field and not required for submission.

### 12. **Connect App** (`connect_app`, select_multiple, required: false)

* If you'd like to connect with us through a specific app, please select the option from the list. This is an optional field and not required for submission.
