# fitness_program_new_member_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fitness program new member registration form is a form used to register new members with the fitness program by providing their personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. If necessary, provide your birth details in the relevant fields.
8. Review and submit the form once complete.
