# Fitness Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information about an individual's physical fitness level and medical history, helping our fitness professionals assess their readiness for various fitness programs and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your details as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure you've selected the correct units for your height and weight.
3. Be honest about your exercise frequency and type.
4. If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or medications, provide the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Height**: Your height in [units selected in Height Unit field]. 
* **Weight**: Your weight in [units selected in Weight Unit field].
* **Max Heart Rate**: Your maximum heart rate in beats per minute.
* **Resting Heart Rate**: Your resting heart rate in beats per minute.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Select one or more of the options that best describe your exercise habits:
	+ Sedentary: You rarely exercise or are mostly inactive.
	+ Light: You lightly exercise (e.g., short walks or light yoga).
	+ Moderately Active: You moderately exercise (e.g., regularly walking, jogging, or cycling).
	+ Highly Active: You are very physically active.
* **Exercise Type**: Select the type of exercise you are (or usually) engaged in:
	+ Cardio: Exercises that improve heart health (e.g., jogging, swimming).
	+ Strength: Exercises that build muscle (e.g., weightlifting, bodyweight).
	+ Flexibility: Exercises that improve flexibility (e.g., yoga, stretching).
	+ Stretching: Exercises that focus on stretching.
	+ Balance: Exercises that improve balance (e.g., tai chi, balance boards).
	+ Weightlifting: Exercises that aim to improve muscle strength.
* **Medical Conditions**: Provide any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes or heart disease.
* **Medication**: If you take any medication, list them here.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, list them here.
* **Height Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for your height:
	+ ft: feet
	+ in: inches
	+ cm: centimeters
* **Weight Unit**: Select the unit of measurement for your weight:
	+ lbs: pounds
	+ kg: kilograms
	+ st: stones
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD-MM-YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD, depending on your region).
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which we'll use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which we'll use to contact you.
* **Medical History**: If you have any relevant medical history, provide it here.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask our staff for guidance.
* The information you provide will help us tailor our fitness programs to your needs and abilities.
