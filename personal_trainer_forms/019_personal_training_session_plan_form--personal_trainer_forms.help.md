# Personal Training Session Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help personal trainers create a customized plan for their clients' fitness sessions. It collects information about the client's details, fitness goals, and scheduling preferences to tailor the training plan to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details in the "Client Details" page.
2. Select one or multiple fitness goals for the client from the "Fitness Goals" dropdown menu.
3. Choose a date for the training session in the "Scheduling Preferences" page.
4. Select a preferred time for the training session in the "Preferred Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Details
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Fitness Goals
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or multiple goals for the client, such as:
	+ Goal 1
	+ Goal 2
	+ Goal 3

### Scheduling Preferences
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date for the training session.

### Preferred Time
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required: false): Select a preferred time for the training session.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the client's details are accurate and up-to-date.
- Select specific and realistic fitness goals for the client.
- Choose a date and time that are convenient for the client and the trainer.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with the client or trainer to ensure the accuracy of the information.
