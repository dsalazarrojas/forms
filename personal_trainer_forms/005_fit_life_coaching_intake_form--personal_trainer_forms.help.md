# Fit Life Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fit Life Coaching Intake Form is designed to collect personal and health information from potential clients for a fitness coaching program. The form is essential for the coach to understand the client's needs and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields marked with <font color="red">*asterisk</font> in the form.
2. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide detailed information in the corresponding fields.
4. In the contact preference field, please specify your preferred method of communication (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Fill out your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Contact Availability** (`contact_availability`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you're available during certain times of the day or have any specific schedules, please indicate them here.
* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, `text`, optional): Describe your fitness goals and what you hope to achieve with our coaching program.
* **Physical Condition** (`physical_condition`, `text`, optional): If you have any physical conditions that may impact our coaching, please provide information.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions or health concerns, please provide details.
