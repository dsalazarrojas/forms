# Fitness Coaching Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients to help fitness coaches understand their goals, health, and lifestyle habits. It will help coaches create a tailored fitness plan that suits the client's needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, height, weight, and contact information (phone number and email) in the "Client Information" section.
2. Answer the questions in the "Health History" section about your past medical history and current health status.
3. In the "Fitness Goals" section, select the options that best describe your goals for working out.
4. In the "Lifestyle Habits" section, select the frequency and type of exercise you do, and answer questions about your sleep pattern and stress level.
5. Answer the questions in the "Health and Wellbeing" section about your current medical history, medication, and nutrition plan.
6. In the "Emergency Contact" section, select the people you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
