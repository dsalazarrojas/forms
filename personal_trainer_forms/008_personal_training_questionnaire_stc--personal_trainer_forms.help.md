# Personal Training Questionnaire STC - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for personal training purposes. It consists of multiple sections to gather essential details about the client, their medical history, goals, contact information, and emergency contact details. The form is divided into eight sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the client's information. Completing this form will help the trainer to understand the client's needs and preferences, ensuring a safe and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client's **Client Details** section with their basic information.
2. In the **Medical Details** section, provide any relevant medical history or concerns that may affect their training.
3. In the **Goal Details** section, describe their fitness goals and objectives for training.
4. Enter their **Contact Details**, including their name, email, and phone number.
5. Provide contact information for their emergency contact, including their name and relationship to the client.
6. If applicable, fill out the **Medical Authorization** section with any necessary medical clearance or permissions.
7. Finally, confirm their agreement to be contacted by emergency contacts in the **Emergency Contact Agreement** section.
8. If you wish to include an emergency contact person in the client's file, fill out the **Emergency Contact Info** section with their name, phone number, and relationship to the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): This is the client's basic information, including their name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, text, optional): This section is for any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns that may affect their training.
* **Goal Details** (`goal_details`, text, required): Describe the client's fitness goals and objectives for training, such as weight loss or muscle gain.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter the client's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required): Provide contact information for the client's emergency contact, including their name and relationship to the client.
* **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorization`, text, optional): If necessary, provide any medical clearance or permissions required for the training.
* **Emergency Contact Agreement** (`emergency_contact_agreement`, text, required): Confirm the client's agreement to be contacted by emergency contacts in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): If an emergency contact person is included in the client's file, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to the client.
