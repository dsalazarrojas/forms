# Mentoring Session Attendance Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentoring Session Attendance Confirmation Form is a simple form used to confirm attendance at a mentoring session. It helps you provide feedback about your experience, and it is a crucial step in evaluating the effectiveness of our mentoring program. Please fill out this form accurately to help us improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide a brief description of the session you attended, including the date, time, and location.
3. Confirm your attendance by selecting the status of your attendance from the provided options.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to add them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (1, text, **required**): This field is used to collect your name and any other relevant contact details. Please enter your name and any other information you think is necessary for us to contact you.
* **Session Details** (2, text, **required**): Describe the session you attended, including the date, time, and location. This information will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the session and make it better in the future.
* **Attendance Confirmation** (5, select_one, **required**): Please select the status of your attendance from the provided options: Attended, Declined, or Unable to Attend. This is a crucial field to help us track attendance and evaluate the session's success.
* **Session Summary** (4, text, **optional**): This field is optional, but feel free to provide any additional comments or feedback about the session. This will help us improve the mentoring program.
* **session_participant_attendance_status** (5, select_one, **required**): Please refer to the **Attendance Confirmation** field, which explains what this field is used for.
* **form_group_6** (6, text, **required**): This field is currently empty and seems to be a placeholder. Please ignore it for now.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This form is a crucial step in evaluating the effectiveness of our mentoring program.
