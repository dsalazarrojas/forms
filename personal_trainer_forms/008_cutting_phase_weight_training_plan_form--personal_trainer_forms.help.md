# Cutting Phase Weight Training Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the client and coach regarding their progress, goals, and challenges during the cutting phase of their weight training plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the user.
2. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your phone number for further contact.
4. Describe your current training plan, goals, and any specific needs or concerns you have during this phase.
5. Indicate whether you will be following a specific training plan for each day from Day 1 to Day 10.
6. Add any additional notes about your progress, challenges, or goals.
7. Let your coach know if you need any recommendations or adjustments to your plan.
8. Set the submission date and time for your form.
9. Enter your coach's name for accountability and reference.
10. (Optional) Enter a unique form ID for further tracking or reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Name** (Text)
Enter your name as the user.

### 2. **Email** (Email)
Provide your email address for communication purposes.

### 3. **Phone** (Text)
Enter your phone number for further contact.

### 4. **Training Plan** (Note)
Describe your current training plan, goals, and any specific needs or concerns you have during this phase.

### 5-10. **Day 1-10** (Select One)
Indicate whether you will be following a specific training plan for each day from Day 1 to Day 10.

### 11. **Client Notes** (Note)
Add any additional notes about your progress, challenges, or goals.

### 12. **Coach Recommendation** (Select Multiple)
Let your coach know if you need any recommendations or adjustments to your plan.

### 13. **Submission Date** (Date)
Set the submission date for your form.

### 14. **Submission Time** (Time)
Set the submission time for your form.

### 15. **Coach Name** (Text)
Enter your coach's name for accountability and reference.

### 16. **Form ID** (Text)
(Optional) Enter a unique form ID for further tracking or reference.

Tips: Ensure all fields are filled out accurately, and do not hesitate to ask your coach for guidance or clarification if you have any doubts or concerns.
