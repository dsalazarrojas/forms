# Trainer Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from trainers to facilitate smooth communication and scheduling between trainers and clients. Please complete this form accurately to help us better understand your availability and provide the best possible services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Trainers Availability section with a brief description of your available time slots.
2. Select your preferred Schedule from the provided options.
3. Choose your Specialties from the options listed.
4. Choose your preferred Location from the options provided.
5. Provide your Availability in a specific format (e.g., day and time format).
6. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the Notes field.
7. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
8. Enter your Phone number for emergency contact.
9. (Optional) If you have any other specialties that are not listed, you can specify them in the Trainer Specialties field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainers Availability**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of your available time slots. For example, "Monday to Friday, 10am to 5pm."
* **Schedule**: A multiple selection field where you can choose your preferred schedule. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Specialties**: A single selection field where you can choose your preferred specialties. Please select only one option that best describes your expertise.
* **Location**: A single selection field where you can choose your preferred location. Please select only one option that best describes where you work.
* **Availability**: A time field where you can provide your specific availability in a format like "Monday to Friday, 10am to 5pm."
* **Notes**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or information.
* **Email**: A text field where you can enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: A text field where you can enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Trainer Specialties**: A text field where you can provide any other specialties that are not listed. This field is optional.
