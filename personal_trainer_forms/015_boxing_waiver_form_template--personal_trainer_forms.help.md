# Boxing Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and obtain their consent for participation in boxing activities. It is essential to fill it out carefully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the participant's personal information (Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, and Phone Number).
2. Indicate that you have read and understand the warnings and risks associated with boxing.
3. Confirm that you have a medical professional's clearance for participation.
4. Disclose any medical history, including heart or respiratory conditions, previous concussions, or head injuries.
5. List any current medications.
6. Release the facility and trainer from liability for any injuries or damages sustained during the activity.
7. Confirm that you understand the waiver includes ordinary negligence.
8. Indicate that you are responsible for obtaining personal insurance coverage.
9. Confirm that you fully agree to and consent to this waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **I acknowledge the inherent risks of boxing** (`risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select "I acknowledge" to indicate that you understand the inherent risks of boxing.
* **I understand serious injury or death is possible** (`serious_injury_risk`, select_one, required): Select "I understand" to acknowledge that serious injury or death is a possibility.
* **Head injuries and concussions are possible** (`head_injury_risk`, select_one, required): Select "I understand this risk" to acknowledge the possibility of head injuries and concussions.
* **I have read all warnings and understand them** (`warning_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select "I have read and understand" to confirm that you have read and understood the warnings.
* **Medical professional has cleared you for boxing** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I have clearance" to confirm that you have a doctor's approval for participation.
* **History of heart or respiratory condition** (`heart_condition`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have a heart or respiratory condition.
* **Previous concussions or head injuries** (`concussion_history`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have experienced concussions or head injuries.
* **Currently taking medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **I release the facility and trainer from liability** (`liability_release`, select_one, required): Select "I release them from liability" to confirm that you release the facility and trainer from liability.
* **I understand this includes ordinary negligence** (`negligence_release`, select_one, required): Select "I understand and agree" to confirm that you understand the comprehensive nature of this waiver.
* **I am responsible for personal insurance** (`insurance_responsible`, select_one, required): Select "I agree and will obtain coverage" to confirm that you are responsible for obtaining personal insurance.
* **I voluntarily agree to this waiver** (`final_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I fully agree and consent" to confirm that you have read and understand this waiver.
