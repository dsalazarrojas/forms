# Fitness Center Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Center Contact Form is designed to allow users to send messages or ask questions to the fitness center staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Contact Us" page.
2. Type your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Type your message in the "Message" field.
6. Review your information and click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, text, optional): This is the title of the first page of the form, which asks users to click on it to proceed.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your name for the fitness center staff to reference when responding to your inquiry.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is where you enter your email address, which the fitness center staff may use to send you a response or follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your phone number, which the fitness center staff may use to contact you.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): This field is where you type your message or question for the fitness center staff to review and respond to.

## Tips
* Make sure to include enough detail in your message to ensure the fitness center staff can provide the most accurate response.
* Be clear and concise in your communication to facilitate a timely response.
* If you're unsure about any information or have a specific concern, please don't hesitate to ask.
