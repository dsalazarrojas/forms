# Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals applying for personal trainer certification. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. Submit the completed form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): The date on which you submitted this application.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`applicant_address`, text, required): Your full mailing address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Your date of birth.
* **Certification Sought** (`certification_sought`, select_one, required): Select which certification you are applying for (e.g., Certified Personal Trainer, Certified Strength Coach, etc.).
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Your highest educational level completed (e.g., High school diploma, Associate degree, etc.).
* **Field of Study** (`education_field`, text, not required): If applicable, the major or field of study for your highest education level.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, not required): The name of the institution where you received your highest education level.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, not required): The year you graduated from your highest education level.
* **CPR AED Certification** (`cpr_certified`, select_one, required): Whether you have current CPR AED certification.
* **CPR AED Expiration Date** (`cpr_expiration`, date, not required): The date your CPR AED certification expires.
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (`relevant_experience`, select_one, required): How many years of experience you have in the fitness industry.
* **Area of Specialization** (`specialization_area`, select_one, required): Select your area of focus in the fitness industry.
* **Previous Certifications Held** (`previous_certifications`, text, not required): If applicable, list any current or previous certifications you have held.
* **Liability Insurance** (`liability_insurance`, select_one, required): Whether you have professional liability insurance.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check`, select_one, required): Whether you consent to a criminal background check.
* **Exam Delivery Preference** (`exam_preference`, select_one, required): How you would like to take the certification exam.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): The method of payment for the application fee (e.g., credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Application Fee Acknowledgment** (`application_fee`, select_one, required): You understand that the application fee is non-refundable.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, select_one, required): You have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Type your full legal name as signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): The date of signing.
