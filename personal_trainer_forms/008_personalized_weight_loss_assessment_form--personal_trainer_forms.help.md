# Personalized Weight Loss Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Weight Loss Assessment Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about your health, lifestyle, and weight loss goals. This form helps us understand your individual needs and preferences to create a tailored weight loss plan that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with personalized advice and progress updates.
3. Provide your phone number for easier communication.
4. Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Enter your height, including the unit of measurement (e.g., inches, cm, feet, meters).
6. Enter your current weight, including the unit of measurement (e.g., pounds, kilograms, ounces, stones).
7. Select your activity level, which is your average level of physical activity you engage in daily.
8. List any medical conditions you have, if applicable.
9. List any medications you are currently taking, if applicable.
10. List any allergies you have, if applicable.
11. List any previous injuries, if applicable.
12. Describe your typical diet.
13. Indicate how often you exercise.
14. Enter your average sleep duration per day.
15. Enter your wake-up time.
16. Enter your target weight.
17. Specify your weight loss goals.
18. Share your motivation behind your weight loss journey.
19. List any previous weight loss programs you have participated in.
20. List any previous coaches or trainers you have worked with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for personalized advice and progress updates.
*   **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for easier communication.
*   **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Height** (Text, Required): Enter your height, including the unit of measurement (e.g., inches, cm, feet, meters).
*   **Weight** (Text, Required): Enter your current weight, including the unit of measurement (e.g., pounds, kilograms, ounces, stones).
*   **Height Unit** (Select One, Required): Select the unit of measurement for your height (e.g., inches, cm, feet, meters).
*   **Weight Unit** (Select One, Required): Select the unit of measurement for your weight (e.g., pounds, kilograms, ounces, stones).
*   **Activity Level** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your average level of physical activity you engage in daily (e.g., sedentary, lightly active, moderately active, very active, extremely active).
*   **Medical Conditions** (Text, Optional): List any medical conditions you have, if applicable.
*   **Medications** (Text, Optional): List any medications you are currently taking, if applicable.
*   **Allergies** (Text, Optional): List any allergies you have, if applicable.
*   **Previous Injuries** (Text, Optional): List any previous injuries, if applicable.
*   **Diet** (Text, Optional): Describe your typical diet.
*   **Exercise Frequency** (Text, Optional): Indicate how often you exercise.
*   **Sleep Duration** (Text, Optional): Enter your average sleep duration per day.
*   **Wake-up Time** (Text, Optional): Enter your wake-up time.
*   **Target Weight** (Text, Optional): Enter your target weight.
*   **Weight Loss Goals** (Text, Optional): Specify your weight loss goals.
*   **Motivation** (Text, Optional): Share your motivation behind your weight loss journey.
*   **Previous Programs** (Text, Optional): List any previous weight loss programs you have participated in.
*   **Previous Coaches** (Text, Optional): List any previous coaches or trainers you have worked with.
