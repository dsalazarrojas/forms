# Personalized Fitness And Nutrition Plan Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients to create a personalized fitness and nutrition plan tailored to their specific needs and goals. Please provide accurate and detailed information to help our trainers create an effective plan for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your Client Details.
2. Describe your short-term and long-term goals.
3. Select your activity level.
4. Enter your height, weight, and age.
5. Provide any relevant health details.
6. Choose any medical conditions that may impact your workout routine (if applicable).
7. Select your desired workout frequency.
8. Choose a time commitment for your workouts.
9. Select the type of goals you'd like to achieve with your workout (e.g., general fitness, weight loss, weight gain).
10. Choose a meal plan type that suits your needs.
11. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with our trainers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: Enter your full name, phone number, and email address to ensure our trainers can contact you to discuss your plan.
* **Goals**: Describe your short-term and long-term goals, such as weight loss, muscle gain, or improved overall health.
* **Activity Level**: Select the level of physical activity you engage in daily, from sedentary (little or no exercise) to extremely active (very physically active).
* **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters or feet and inches.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Health Details**: Provide any relevant health information that may impact your workout routine.
* **Medical Condition**: Select any medical conditions that may affect your workout routine, such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, or diabetes.
* **Workout Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to work out per week.
* **Time Commitment**: Select how much time you're willing to dedicate to your workouts per session.
* **Goals For Workout**: Choose the type of goals you'd like to achieve through your workouts, such as general fitness or weight loss.
* **Meal Plan**: Select a meal plan type that suits your dietary needs, such as low carb or balanced.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with our trainers, such as your dietary restrictions or preferences.

Please provide accurate and detailed information to help our trainers create an effective plan for you. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask!
