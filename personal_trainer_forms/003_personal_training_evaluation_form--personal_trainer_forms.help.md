# Personal Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's fitness progress, goals, and feedback from their trainer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately**. Make sure to enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) correctly.
2. **Select your current progress level**: Choose the level that best describes your current fitness status.
3. **Choose your goals and objectives**: Select all the goals and objectives you are currently working towards.
4. **Provide your trainer's comments**: Share your thoughts and feedback with your trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID (1)**: A unique identifier for your entry. This will be used for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and information.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Progress Level (5)**: Select the level that describes your current level of fitness. This will help your trainer understand where you are in your training journey. Choose from:
	+ High
	+ Low
* **Goals and Objectives (6)**: Select all the goals and objectives you are working towards. Choose from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Trainer's Comments (7)**: Use this section to share any comments or feedback you have for your trainer.
