# Personal Graduation Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students, parents, teachers, and school administrators plan and track student graduation goals, progress, and information. It gathers essential details about the student's graduation date, goals, and personal statement, as well as parent and teacher input. The form aims to facilitate open communication and collaboration between all parties involved in the graduation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the student's name in the `student_name` field.
2.  Enter the student's planned graduation date in the `graduation_date` field.
3.  Describe the student's graduation plan and goals in the `graduation_plan_goals` field.
4.  Add any relevant parent information in the `parent_info` field.
5.  Add any relevant teacher input in the `teacher_info` field.
6.  Enter relevant school information in the `school_info` field.
7.  State your career goals in the `career_goals` field.
8.  Type out your personal statement in the `personal_statement` field.
9.  Sign the form with your name in the `student_signature` field.
10. Sign the form with your school's name in the `school_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name (`student_name`, text, optional):** Enter your name in this field.
*   **Graduation Date (`graduation_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date you plan to graduate.
*   **Graduation Plan Goals (`graduation_plan_goals`, text, optional):** Describe your goals and plans for after graduation.
*   **Parent Info (`parent_info`, text, optional):** Add any relevant information from your parents or guardians.
*   **Teacher Info (`teacher_info`, text, optional):** Add any comments or recommendations from your teachers.
*   **School Info (`school_info`, text, optional):** Enter any relevant information about your school.
*   **Career Goals (`career_goals`, text, optional):** Describe your career goals and aspirations after graduation.
*   **Personal Statement (`personal_statement`, note, optional):** Write a personal message to yourself or others about your goals and aspirations.
*   **Student Signature (`student_signature`, text, optional):** Sign your name to confirm your information.
*   **School Signature (`school_signature`, text, optional):** Sign your school's name to confirm their input.

## Tips
This form is designed to help guide your graduation planning process. Make sure to review and update your information regularly to ensure it stays accurate and relevant. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your school's administration or counselor.
