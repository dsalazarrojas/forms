# Student Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Complaint Form is used to report incidents or complaints that students have experienced within the school environment. This form helps school administrators and counselors understand the issues and address them promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident or complaint.
3. Ensure that you're reporting incidents that haven't been addressed by school administrators or counselors yet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Complaint Form** (Page 1, **student_complaint_form**):
	+ This is the form title, which is not editable.
* **Description** (Page 1, **description**):
	+ This field is where you can describe the incident or complaint in as much detail as possible.
	+ Please be as clear and concise as you can, including any relevant dates, times, and locations.
* **Incident Date** (Page 2, **incident_date**):
	+ This field is for the date on which the incident occurred.
	+ Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Incident Time** (Page 2, **incident_time**):
	+ This field is for the time at which the incident occurred.
	+ Use the time format HH:MM (e.g., 12:00).
* **Location** (Page 3, **location**):
	+ This field is for the location where the incident occurred.
	+ Be as specific as possible, including the exact room, building, or area.
* **Description of Incident** (Page 3, **description_of_incident**):
	+ This field is where you can provide a more detailed explanation of what happened.
	+ Include any relevant details, such as witnesses, injuries, or consequences.
* **Student Name** (Page 4, **student_name**):
	+ This field is for your name as the student reporting the incident.
* **Student ID** (Page 4, **student_id**):
	+ This field is for your student ID number.
	+ Please ensure you enter the correct number.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when reporting incidents or complaints.
* If you're unsure about what to report, contact your school counselor or administrator for guidance.
* If you've already reported an incident, please don't submit duplicate reports.
