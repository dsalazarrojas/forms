# Student Technology Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Technology Usage Survey is designed to collect information about students' technology usage, access, and learning experiences in schools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, as your responses will help us understand your technology usage and learning habits.
2. Select the options that best describe your experiences and preferences.
3. Fill out all required fields, even if you're unsure of the answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Technology Usage Survey** (`student_technology_usage_survey`, text, optional): This is a title field that serves as a prompt to remind you of the form's purpose.
- **Student Access to Technology** (`student_access_to_technology`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have access to technology in your school, such as computers, laptops, or mobile devices. If you don't have access, select 'No'.
- **Technology Familiarity** (`technology_familiarity`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of familiarity with different technologies, such as computers, mobile devices, or software applications. Select all that apply.
- **Teacher Aided Learning** (`teacher_aided_learning`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you receive help from teachers or instructors when using technology for learning.
- **Student Support for Learning** (`student_support_for_learning`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that describe how you use technology to support your learning, such as online resources, tutorials, or discussion forums.
- **Student Independence in Learning** (`student_independence_in_learning`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how much freedom you have to use technology for learning, such as choosing your own learning paths or working independently.
- **Student Self-Directed Learning** (`student_self_directed_learning`, note, optional): Describe how you use technology to direct your own learning, such as setting goals, tracking progress, or seeking resources.
- **Student Self-Learning** (`student_self_learning`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on how you use technology to learn independently, without guidance from teachers or instructors.
- **Technology Use in Classroom** (`technology_use_in_classroom`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that describe how you use technology in your classroom, such as taking notes, participating in discussions, or working on projects.
- **Device Types** (`student_device_type`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the types of devices you use for learning, such as laptops, tablets, or smartphones.
- **Device Purpose** (`student_device_purpose`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the purposes for which you use different devices, such as browsing the internet, creating documents, or playing games.
- **Teacher Training** (`teacher_training`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if your teachers or instructors have received training on technology use in the classroom.
