# Parent Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "Parent Interest Survey," is designed to gather information from parents to better understand their interests and needs. The data collected will help us understand how to tailor our support and resources to meet the needs of our parents and students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please navigate through the pages of questions. There are several pages with different types of questions.
2. For each page, follow the instructions provided for each question.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions, as they are marked as required or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Parent Interest Survey
* **Parent Interest Survey** (`Parent Interest Survey`, `text`, required/optional): This is a text box where you can provide any information about your interests or concerns.
* **Extracurriculars** (`Extracurriculars`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select whether you are interested in extracurricular activities for your child. You can choose between "Yes" or "No".
* **Teacher** (`Teacher`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any information about your child's teacher or teacher preferences.
* **Parent** (`Parent`, `text`, required/optional): Share any information about yourself as a parent or guardian.
* **Stay at home Parent** (`Stay at home Parent`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, provide any relevant information about being a stay-at-home parent.
* **School Counselor** (`School Counselor`, `text`, required/optional): Share any concerns or thoughts about the school counselor.

### Page 2: Free Parent Interest Survey
* **Free Parent Interest Survey** (`Free Parent Interest Survey`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select whether you are interested in taking the free survey again. You can choose between "Yes" or "No".
