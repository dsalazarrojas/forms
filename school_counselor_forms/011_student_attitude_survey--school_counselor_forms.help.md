# Student Attitude Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to help the school understand the attitudes and preferences of its students. This guide is designed to assist you in completing the survey and understand what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please enter your name in the box provided.
2. Select your favorite subject from the list.
3. Select your least favorite subject from the list.
4. Answer the questions regarding your favorite and least favorite teacher.
5. Answer the questions regarding your overall experience and perception of the school.
6. Select the time of day you feel most positive about school.
7. Rate your overall school experience using the scale provided.
8. Select the teacher you feel most positive about.
9. Select the teacher attribute that you feel is most positive.
10. Select your least favorite teacher and teacher attribute.
11. Answer the questions regarding your favorite and least favorite teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name here.
* **School Attitude**: Choose how you feel about your school, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Teacher Attitude**: Choose how you feel about your teachers, by selecting multiple options such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience in the school using the scale provided.
* **Favorite Subject**: Select your favorite subject from the list.
* **Least Favorite Subject**: Select your least favorite subject from the list.
* **Favorite Teacher**: Select the teacher you feel most positive about.
* **Least Favorite Teacher**: Select the teacher you feel least positive about.
* **Why Favorite Subject**: Explain why you chose your favorite subject.
* **Why Least Favorite Subject**: Explain why you chose your least favorite subject.
* **When do you feel most positive about school?**: Select the time of day when you feel most positive about school.
* **Why Favorite Teacher**: Explain why you feel positive about your favorite teacher.
* **Why Least Favorite Teacher**: Explain why you feel negative about your least favorite teacher.
* **Student Perceptions**: Choose how you perceive your school, by selecting multiple options such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Favorite Teacher Attribute**: Select the attribute of your favorite teacher that you feel is most positive.
* **Least Favorite Teacher Attribute**: Select the attribute of your least favorite teacher that you feel is most negative.
* **Teacher Attitude**: Choose how you feel about your teachers, by selecting multiple options such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Student Attitude**: Choose how you feel about your school, by selecting multiple options such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
