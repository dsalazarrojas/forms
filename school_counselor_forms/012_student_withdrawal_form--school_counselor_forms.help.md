# student_withdrawal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate a student's withdrawal from a course. It allows students to provide their reason for withdrawal, which can be used to understand their perspective and make necessary adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you need to withdraw from a course, please provide your reason for doing so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): If you have an ID number, please enter it here. If you do not have one, please leave this field blank.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Please enter the title of the course you are withdrawing from.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, text, optional): Please select one of the following reasons for withdrawal, or enter a custom reason:
	+ I am voluntarily withdrawing from the course.
	+ The course is no longer meeting my academic needs.
	+ I have found alternative accommodations.
	+ The course is not meeting my financial needs.
	+ I have personal reasons for withdrawal.
	+ Other (Specify below)
* **Reason for Withdrawal (Optional)** (`other_reason`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" above, please provide more information about your reason for withdrawal.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date on which you plan to complete your withdrawal.
* **School Administrator's Note** (`school_administrators_note`, note, optional): Any additional comments or information from the school administrator will be recorded here.
* **Student Confirmation** (`student_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Please select the reason for withdrawal from the list above, or choose "Other" if you have a different reason.
* **Additional Comments** (`other_reason`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" for the reason for withdrawal, please provide more details about your reason.

Note: All fields are optional, but please make sure to fill out as much information as possible for proper processing. Thank you!
