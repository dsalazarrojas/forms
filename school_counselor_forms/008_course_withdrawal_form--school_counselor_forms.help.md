# Course Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Withdrawal Form is used to document the withdrawal of a student from a course. This form is typically used by the student to provide a reason for withdrawal and the withdrawal date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Course Id of the student's course being withdrawn.
2. Enter the Student Id of the student being withdrawn.
3. Enter the Student's Name as it appears on their student record.
4. Provide a reason for the student's withdrawal in the Reason For Withdrawal field.
5. Enter the name of the course being withdrawn from.
6. Enter the name of the instructor of the course being withdrawn from.
7. Enter the date of withdrawal.
8. (Optional) Provide any additional notes about the withdrawal.
9. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
10. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
11. Enter the name of the assigned counselor.
12. Enter the name of the assigned tool or resource.
13. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
14. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
15. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
16. Enter any comments from the person submitting the form.
17. Enter the department of the person submitting the form.
18. Enter the role of the person submitting the form.
19. Enter the location of the person submitting the form.
20. Enter any comments from the person submitting the form.
21. Indicate if the form was submitted within the correct time zone.
22. Enter the role of the person submitting the form.
23. Enter the location of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Id**: Enter the unique identifier for the student's course being withdrawn.
* **Student Id**: Enter the unique identifier for the student being withdrawn.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their student record.
* **Reason For Withdrawal**: Provide a brief explanation for the student's withdrawal.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course being withdrawn from.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the course's instructor.
* **Withdrawal Date**: Enter the date of withdrawal.
* **Notes**: (Optional) Provide any additional comments or notes about the withdrawal.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Assigned Counselor**: Enter the name of the assigned counselor.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the assigned tool or resource.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any comments from the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter the department of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Role**: Enter the role of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Location**: Enter the location of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted At Time Zone**: Indicate if the form was submitted within the correct time zone.
* **Submitted By Role**: Enter the role of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By Location**: Enter the location of the person submitting the form.

Note: This help guide assumes that all fields are correctly filled out in the context of withdrawal of students from courses. If you are unsure about the form's purpose or fields, please review the form's context and fields carefully before proceeding.
