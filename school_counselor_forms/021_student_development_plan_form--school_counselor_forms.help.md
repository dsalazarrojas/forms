# Student Development Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Development Plan Form is used to track the progress and goals of students in a school setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Make sure to have the student's permission before sharing any information with others.
* Fill out the form accurately and carefully.
* Review and update the form regularly to keep the student's progress up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, `number`, required: true): Enter the student's number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `number`, required: true): Select the student's grade level.
* **Student English Level** (`student_english_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the student's English level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Student Math Level** (`student_math_level`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the student's Math levels (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Student Science Level** (`student_science_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the student's Science level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Student Social Level** (`student_social_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the student's Social levels (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Student Language Level** (`student_language_level`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's Language level.
* **Additional Comments** (`student_additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the student.
* **Student Goal** (`student_goal`, `note`, required: false): Enter the student's goal.
* **Action Steps** (`student_action_steps`, `text`, required: false): Enter the action steps for the student.
* **Review Date** (`student_review_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the last review.
* **Review Time** (`student_review_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the last review.
* **Reviewer Name** (`student_reviewer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the last reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (`student_reviewer_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the last reviewer.
* **Sign Date** (`student_sign_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of signing the form.
* **Sign Time** (`student_sign_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of signing the form.
* **Status** (`student_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the student (In Progress, Complete).
* **Comments** (`student_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments about the student.
* **Next Review Date** (`student_next_review_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the next review.
* **Next Review Time** (`student_next_review_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the next review.
* **Last Review Date** (`student_last_review_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the last review.
* **Last Review Time** (`student_last_review_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the last review.
* **Last Reviewer Name** (`student_last_reviewer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the last reviewer.
* **Last Reviewer Title** (`student_last_reviewer_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the last reviewer.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update the form regularly to keep the student's progress up-to-date.
* Be careful when selecting the student's levels, as they are required for accurate tracking.
* Use the "Additional Comments" field for any additional information that is not already covered in the form.
* Use the "Action Steps" field to outline the steps that the student needs to take to achieve their goal.
