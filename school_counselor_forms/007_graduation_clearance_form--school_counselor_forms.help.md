# Graduation Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Clearance Form is used to collect information from students, counselors, and schools for graduation clearance purposes. This form helps to ensure that all necessary clearances are in place before a student graduates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Graduation Clearance Form" fields, including your name, student ID, and other relevant information.
2. Select the clearance type that applies to you (e.g. Graduation Clearance, Academic Clearance).
3. Provide any necessary comments or remarks in the "Counselor Comments" and "Student Comments" fields.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your clearance.
5. Enter your graduation date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation Clearance Form (title)**: This is the title of the form.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Student (student)**: Enter your name as a student.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Student Name**: Enter your name as a student.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Counselor**: Enter the name of your counselor.
* **Graduation Clearance Form School**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Grade**: Enter your graduation grade (e.g. Freshman, Sophomore, etc.).
* **Graduation Clearance Form Major**: Select the major or field of study you are pursuing.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Status**: Select the status of your clearance (e.g. Pending, Cleared, Incomplete).
* **Graduation Clearance Form Date Cleared**: Enter the date you were cleared to graduate.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Date Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Counselor Comments**: Enter any comments from your counselor.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Student Comments**: Enter any comments from you as a student.
* **Graduation Clearance Form School Comments**: Enter any comments from your school.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Other Comments**: Enter any additional comments.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Signature**: Sign this form to confirm your clearance.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Date Signature**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Counselor Name**: Enter the name of your counselor.
* **Graduation Clearance Form School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Department Name**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Major Name**: Enter the name of your major.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Status Name**: Enter the name of your clearance status.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Counselor Title**: Enter the title of your counselor.
* **Graduation Clearance Form School Counselor**: Enter the name of your school counselor.
* **Graduation Clearance Form Other Counselor**: Enter the name of another counselor.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in.
