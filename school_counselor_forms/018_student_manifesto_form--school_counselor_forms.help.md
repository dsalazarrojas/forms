# Student Manifesto Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Manifesto Form is a document that outlines the student's goals, aspirations, and personal information, as well as their relationships with parents, guardians, and teachers. This form is designed to help students take ownership of their learning and development, and to provide guidance for school staff to support students' success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully, and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Fill in the form with the requested information, including any relevant details about your personal goals, aspirations, and relationships.
3. Select the options that best represent your current status or situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): Please take a moment to read this page before proceeding with the rest of the form. This section provides additional context and information about the Student Manifesto Form, which will help you understand its purpose and how it can support your learning and development.

### Beliefs
* **beliefs** (`beliefs`, `text`, not required): Here, you are invited to share your personal values and beliefs that drive your actions and decisions. This section is a space for you to express your thoughts and feelings about what is important to you.

### Goals
* **goals** (`goals`, `text`, not required): Describe your short-term and long-term goals, including academic and personal objectives. This will help us better understand what you aim to achieve and how we can support you.

### Aspirations
* **aspirations** (`aspirations`, `text`, not required): Share your dreams, desires, and aspirations. What would you like to achieve in the short term and long term? What are your hopes and dreams for your future?

### Personal Info
* **personal info** (`personal info`, `select one`, not required): Do you have any personal issues or concerns that you would like to share with your teacher or counselor? Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Academic Info
* **academic info** (`academic info`, `select one`, not required): Are you facing any academic difficulties or concerns? Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Contact
* **contact** (`contact`, `select one`, not required): Do you have any personal issues or concerns that you would like to discuss with your teacher or counselor? Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Emergency Contact
* **emergency contact** (`emergency contact`, `select multiple`, not required): Who would you like to be contacted in case of an emergency? Please select all relevant contacts.

### Parent Info
* **parent info** (`parent info`, `select multiple`, not required): Do you have any parents or guardians that are not listed on your emergency contact list? Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Guardian Info
* **guardian info** (`guardian info`, `select multiple`, not required): Do you have any other adults that are not listed on your emergency contact list that you would like to have support from? Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Student Info
* **student info** (`student info`, `select multiple`, not required): Do you have any information about your student status that you would like to share with your teacher or counselor? Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Teacher Info
* **teacher info** (`teacher info`, `select multiple`, not required): Do you have any information about your relationships with your teachers that you would like to share with your teacher or counselor? Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your teacher or counselor.
* The information you provide will be kept confidential and used to support your learning and development.
