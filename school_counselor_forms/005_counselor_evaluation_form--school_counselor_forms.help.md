# Counselor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counselor Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from students regarding their experience with their school counselors.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in the following order:
    1. School Counselor's name
    2. School Counselor's email and phone number
    3. Student's name, grade level and subject area of concern
    4. Rate your experience with the School Counselor (select one or multiple options)
    5. Enter the date and time of your evaluation
    6. Enter the Evaluation Date and Time for each of the School Counselor's comments sections
    7. Provide your feedback and comments about the School Counselor's service

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Counselor**: Enter the name of the school counselor who assisted you.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the School Counselor, selecting one of the options: Very satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date and time of your evaluation with the School Counselor. (Format: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS)
* **Rating 1**: Rate your experience with the School Counselor's service for this particular evaluation. (Select one option)
* **Rating 2**: Rate your experience with the School Counselor's service for this particular evaluation. (Select multiple options)
* **Rating 3**: Rate your experience with the School Counselor's service for this particular evaluation. (Select multiple options)
* **Evaluation Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about the School Counselor's service.
* **School Counselor Name**: Enter the name of the school counselor who assisted you.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
* **Student Grade**: Enter your grade level.
* **Evaluation Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of your evaluation with the School Counselor. (Format: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS)
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of your evaluation with the School Counselor. (Format: 24-hour format, HH:MM:SS)
* **Evaluation Time 1**: Enter the time of your first evaluation with the School Counselor. (Format: 24-hour format, HH:MM:SS)
* **Evaluation Time 2**: Enter the time of your second evaluation with the School Counselor. (Format: 24-hour format, HH:MM:SS)
* **School Counselor Email**: Enter the email of the school counselor who assisted you.
* **School Counselor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school counselor who assisted you.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school you attended.
* **Grade Level**: Enter your grade level.
* **Teacher**: Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Subject**: Enter the subject area of concern.
* **School Counselor Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about the School Counselor's service.
* **School Counselor Comments 1**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the School Counselor's service.
* **School Counselor Comments 2**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the School Counselor's service.

## Tips

* Be honest and as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the field explanations above.
