# Seat Belt Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seat Belt Pledge Form is designed to collect information from school administrators and students about seat belt usage on school buses. This form aims to track and verify seat belt usage practices within the school community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the user's first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter the user's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the user's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the user's pledge status in the "Pledge Status" field.
5. If applicable, enter any comments in the "Comments" field.
6. Sign the "Signature" field with a handwritten or electronic signature.
7. Select the date of the survey in the "Date" field.
8. (Optional) Enter the time of the survey in the "Time" field.
9. Enter the school's name in the "School Name" field.
10. Enter the school's email address in the "School Email" field.
11. Enter the school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
12. Select whether the school has verified the information provided in the "School Verified" field.
13. If applicable, enter the name of who verified the information in the "Verified By" field.
14. (Optional) Enter the date the school verified the information in the "School Date" field.
15. (Optional) Enter the time the school verified the information in the "School Time" field.
16. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
17. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
18. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
19. Select whether the student's information has been verified in the "Student Verified" field.
20. If applicable, enter the name of who verified the student's information in the "Verified By" field.
21. (Optional) Enter the date the student's information was verified in the "Student Date" field.
22. (Optional) Enter the time the student's information was verified in the "Student Time" field.
23. Select the "Submit Button" option to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number here.
* **Pledge Status**: Select "True" if you pledge to always wear a seat belt, "False" if you do not, or "Undecided" if you are unsure.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments about seat belt usage, enter them here.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to verify your identity.
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time**: (Optional) Enter the time you completed this form.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school you are associated with.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of your school's administration or department.
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **School Verified**: Select "True" if your school has verified this information.
* **Verified By**: (Optional) Enter the name of who verified your information.
* **School Date**: (Optional) Enter the date your school verified this information.
* **School Time**: (Optional) Enter the time your school verified this information.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student associated with this school.
* **Student Email**: Enter the email address of the student.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Verified**: Select "True" if the student's information has been verified.
* **Verified By**: (Optional) Enter the name of who verified the student's information.
* **Student Date**: (Optional) Enter the date the student's information was verified.
* **Student Time**: (Optional) Enter the time the student's information was verified.
* **Student Verified By**: (Optional) Enter the name of who verified the student's information.
* **Student Date**: (Optional) Enter the date the student's information was verified.
* **Student Time**: (Optional) Enter the time the student's information was verified.
* **Submit Button**: Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form.
