# Parent Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents regarding emergency procedures, daily schedules, health and safety policies, payment terms, billing schedules, additional fees, and signatures. It is intended to be used in educational or childcare settings, such as schools or daycare centers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each section carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
* Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to select the correct options for each field.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, text, optional): This field is for providing emergency contact information and procedures in case of an emergency.
* **Daily Schedule** (`daily_schedule`, time, optional): This field is for indicating the child's daily schedule, including pickup and drop-off times, meal times, and other relevant details.
* **Health & Safety Policies** (`health_safety_policies`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select multiple options regarding health and safety policies, such as First Aid, Epi-Pen, and Allergy Information.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): This field is for providing payment terms and conditions, such as payment methods and due dates.
* **Billing Schedule** (`billing_schedule`, text, optional): This field is for providing billing information, including due dates and payment schedules.
* **Additional Fees** (`additional_fees`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select one option regarding additional fees, such as Field Trip or Late Payment.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, select_one, optional): This field is for obtaining the parent's signature, which is required for agreement and acknowledgment.
* **Caregiver Signature** (`caregiver_signature`, select_one, optional): This field is for obtaining the caregiver's signature, which is required for agreement and acknowledgment.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, optional): This field is for confirming the agreement and acknowledgment of the parent's and caregiver's signatures.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your contact person for clarification.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm your agreement and acknowledgment.
