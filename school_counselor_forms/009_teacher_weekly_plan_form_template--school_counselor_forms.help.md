# Teacher Weekly Plan Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to help teachers plan their weekly activities, meetings, and tasks. The form is intended to be a concise and organized guide for teachers to outline their weekly goals, meetings with students, and any other important tasks they need to accomplish. The form is meant to help teachers stay on top of their tasks and ensure they are effectively managing their time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Teacher Weekly Plan Form Template** at the top of the page.
2.  Check if you have any **School Counselor Forms** that you need to complete.
3.  In the **Teacher Weekly Plan** section, add a brief description of your weekly goals and tasks.
4.  In the **Managing Schedule** section, provide any additional information about managing your schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teacher Weekly Plan Form Template (Page 1)**
    (`teacher_weekly_plan_form_template`, text, required: false): This is the main form section where you can add your weekly plan, goals, and tasks. Enter as much detail as you need to stay organized.
*   **School Counselor Forms (Page 1)**
    (`school_counselor_forms`, select_one, required: false): This section is to confirm if you have any school counselor forms that need to be completed. Select 'Yes' if you do, 'No' if you don't.
*   **Teacher Weekly Plan (Page 1)**
    (`teacher_weekly_plan`, note, required: false): Here you can add a brief description of your weekly goals and tasks. This section is meant to be a quick reference for yourself to stay on track.
*   **Managing Schedule (Page 1)**
    (`managing_schedule`, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about managing your schedule, such as appointments or special events, please add it here.

## Tips
*   Make sure to save your form regularly to avoid losing your progress.
*   Double-check the **School Counselor Forms** section to ensure you don't miss any important forms.
*   In the **Teacher Weekly Plan** section, keep your goals and tasks concise but clear.
*   In the **Managing Schedule** section, be as detailed as needed to stay organized.
*   Review your form regularly to adjust your plans and stay on track.
