# Transcript Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain authorization from a student's or their parent/guardian's to release their academic transcript.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete this form:

*   Fill in the student's name as it appears on their identification.
*   Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their school records.
*   Enter the student's email address, if applicable.
*   Enter the student's phone number, if applicable.
*   Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   Select the method by which the transcript will be authorized to be released.
*   Add a digital signature to indicate authorization.
*   Include the date that the form was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### name
*   **name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification.

### student id
*   **student id** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their school records.

### email
*   **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address, if applicable.

### phone
*   **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number, if applicable.

### date of birth
*   **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### authorization method
*   **authorization method** (`authorization_method`, select_one, required): Select the method by which the transcript will be authorized to be released. Options may include, but are not limited to, "Parent/Guardian," "Student," or "Third Party."

### signature
*   **signature** (`signature`, note, required): This section is for a digital signature, indicating that the individual is authorizing the transcript release. Please note that this field is not meant for student or guardian signatures.

### date signed
*   **date signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): This field should contain the date that the form was signed.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Incomplete or incorrect information may delay or prevent transcript release.
*   Please note that this form is for authorization purposes only and is not for other uses such as course registration or financial aid applications.
