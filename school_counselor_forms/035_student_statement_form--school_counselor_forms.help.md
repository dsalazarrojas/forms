# Student Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Statement Form is a crucial document that captures essential information about students, including their name, identification details, contact information, and any other relevant personal details. This form is used to gather accurate and up-to-date information about students, ensuring that we can provide the best possible support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information.
2. Review and verify the accuracy of the information before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you do not fill out this form multiple times for the same student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_your_name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on official documents.
* **student_id** (Number, Optional): If you have an ID number, please enter it here. This is not a required field, but it can be helpful for administrative purposes.
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **what_is_your_statement** (Text, Required): Write a brief statement about yourself, including any relevant information about your education, interests, or goals.
* **additional_information** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your educational background or personal circumstances.
* **parent_name** (Text, Optional): If you are a student with a guardian or parent, enter their name here.
* **parent_email** (Email, Optional): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **student_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **other_information** (Text, Optional): If you have any other information to share about yourself, enter it here.
* **other_information_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address associated with other information.
* **other_information_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number associated with other information.
* **other_information_other** (Select One, Optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
	+ option 3
* **other_information_other_other** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple options from the list above.
* **other_information_other_other_date** (Date, Optional): Enter a date related to other information.
* **other_information_other_other_time** (Time, Optional): Enter a time related to other information.
* **other_information_other_other_note** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about other information.
* **other_information_other_other_email** (Email, Optional): Enter an email address related to other information.
* **other_information_other_other_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter a phone number related to other information.
* **other_information_other_other_note** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about other information.
