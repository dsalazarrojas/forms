# COVID 19 School Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for schools to report their COVID-19 status, including vaccination rates, cases, and other related metrics. It helps school administrators and health officials gather data on the spread and mitigation of the virus within the school community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the official name of your school.
2. Select the date of this report.
3. Enter the name of your school district or education authority.
4. Enter the name and email address of the person completing this report.
5. Enter the phone number for report inquiries (optional).
6. Report on the vaccination status of students and staff.
7. Provide counts for students with complete and partial vaccination doses.
8. Report on the number of students with no vaccination doses.
9. Report on the number of fully and partially vaccinated staff members.
10. Report on the number of staff members with no vaccination doses.
11. Estimate the overall school vaccination rate as a percentage.
12. Report on confirmed COVID-19 cases among students and staff.
13. Select the current school closure status.
14. Indicate the percentage of classes offered remotely.
15. Select the status of your on-site testing program.
16. Select all safety measures currently implemented.
17. Provide any additional notes or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: Enter the official name of your school. This is a required field.
* **Report Date**: Select the date of this report. This is a required field.
* **School District**: Enter the name of your school district or education authority. This is a required field.
* **Reporting Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person completing this report. This is a required field.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address for report inquiries. This is a required field.
* **Contact Phone Number** (optional): Enter the phone number for report inquiries. This field is optional.
* **Vaccination Status**: Report on the vaccination status of students and staff. This field is for additional information.
* **Number of Students Fully Vaccinated**: Provide the count of students with complete vaccination series. This field is required.
* **Number of Students Partially Vaccinated**: Provide the count of students with one dose of two-dose vaccine. This field is optional.
* **Number of Students Not Vaccinated**: Provide the count of students with no vaccination doses. This field is required.
* **Number of Staff Members Fully Vaccinated**: Provide the count of fully vaccinated faculty and staff. This field is required.
* **Number of Staff Members Not Vaccinated**: Provide the count of staff with no vaccination doses. This field is required.
* **Overall School Vaccination Rate - Percentage**: Estimate the overall school vaccination rate as a percentage. This field is optional.
* **Confirmed Cases Among Students**: Report on confirmed COVID-19 cases among students this month. This field is required.
* **Confirmed Cases Among Staff**: Report on confirmed COVID-19 cases among staff this month. This field is required.
* **School Closure Status**: Select the current status regarding building access and operations. This field is required.
* **Percentage of Classes Offered Remotely**: Indicate the percentage of classes being conducted remotely. This field is optional.
* **School Testing Program Status**: Select the status of your on-site testing program. This field is required.
* **Current Safety Measures in Place**: Select all safety measures currently implemented. This field is optional.
* **Additional Notes or Concerns**: Provide any other relevant information about school COVID-19 status. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Ensure accurate reporting of vaccination rates to reflect the true status within your school.
* Verify the correctness of case counts and other reported metrics.
* Ensure the school closure status is up to date.
* Clearly indicate the safety measures implemented within your school.
* Provide thorough and accurate reporting to support informed decision-making.
