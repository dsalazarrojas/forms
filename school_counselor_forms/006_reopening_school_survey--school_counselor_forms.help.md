# Reopening School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from school counselors on the reopening of schools. Your input will help us understand their readiness and concerns, ensuring a successful and smooth reopening process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that apply to you.
2. Answer questions with a numerical value where applicable (e.g., 1-5 days).
3. Select your preferred reopening date from the options provided.
4. Ensure you complete all required fields to provide complete and accurate feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. School Counselor Forms** (`school_counselor_forms`, select_multiple, required): This question is to check if the school counselor is ready to reopen school.
  * Please select "Yes" if you are ready to reopen school.
  * Please select "No" if you are not ready to reopen school.
- **2. School Counselor Forms 2** (`school_counselor_forms_2`, text, required): This question is to collect any additional information or comments from the school counselor.
  * Please provide any additional comments or information that you may have regarding the reopening.
- **3. School Counselor Forms 3** (`school_counselor_forms_3`, number, required): This question is to ask for the number of days the school counselor needs to prepare for reopening.
  * Please enter the number of days you need to prepare for the reopening.
- **4. School Counselor Forms 4** (`school_counselor_forms_4`, number, required): This question is to ask for the total number of students the school counselor is expecting for the reopening.
  * Please enter the total number of students you are expecting.
- **5. School Counselor Forms 5** (`school_counselor_forms_5`, select_one, required): This question is to ask for the availability of the school counselor.
