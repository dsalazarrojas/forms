# Student Catering Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to order catering services for events at their school. It helps event planners and catering services process and fulfill student requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the catering options for your event (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
2. Enter the name of your school.
3. Choose the date and time of your event.
4. Provide your student name.
5. Enter the name of the event.
6. Select the catering options for the menu (Option 3, Option 4, etc.).
7. Add any notes or special instructions for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Catering Options**
(Catering Order, select_multiple, required)
Choose the catering options that suit your event. Select all relevant options that fit your needs.

### **School Name**
(School Name, text, required)
Enter the name of your school.

### **Date**
(Date, date, required)
Enter the date of your event. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Time**
(Time, time, required)
Enter the time of your event. Use the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### **Student Name**
(Student Name, text, required)
Enter your name as a student.

### **Event Name**
(Event Name, text, required)
Enter the name of the event you are ordering catering for.

### **Menu**
(Menu, select_multiple, required)
Choose the catering options for the menu (Option 3, Option 4, etc.). Select all relevant options that fit your needs.

### **Notes**
(Notes, note, optional)
Add any special instructions or notes for your order, if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all the necessary catering options for your event.
- Double-check the information you enter for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with this form, contact your school's event planner or catering services.
