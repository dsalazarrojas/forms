# Student Co Op Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from students participating in co-op programs. It is used by students, educators, or administrators in an educational institution to manage co-op programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide co-op information, including job hours, job type, and agreement with your employer.
3. Confirm your co-op employment and agreement with your employer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: This section collects general information about you, the student. 
* **Co Op Information**: This section is divided into several sub-sections that collect specific details about your co-op experience. 
  * **Co Op Details**: Provide details about your co-op job hours and type.
  * **Agreement**: This sub-section is required for co-op employment and confirms your agreement with your employer.
  * **Co Op Employment**: This sub-section provides details about your employment status and agreement with your employer.
  * **Job Description**: Provide a description of your co-op job.
  * **Student Co Op Details**: Provide details about your co-op experience.
  * **Employer Information**: Provide information about your employer.
  * **Co Op Terms 2**: This sub-section is not required for co-op employment.
  * **Co Op Employment 2**: This sub-section provides details about your employment status and agreement with your employer.
  * **Note 2**: This is an optional note section.
* **Agreement Employer 2**: This sub-section is not required for co-op employment.
* **Confirmation 2**: This sub-section is required for co-op employment and confirms your agreement with your employer.
* **Co Op Details 2**: This sub-section is not required for co-op employment.
* **Co Op Status**: Provide your co-op status.
* **Co Op Hours**: Provide details about your co-op hours.
* **Job Type**: Provide details about your co-op job type.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your co-op employment is properly registered.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your educator or administrator for assistance.
* This form is for use in educational institutions, so only provide information related to your co-op experience in these settings.
