# Student Co Op Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from students participating in co-op programs. It is used by students, educators, or administrators in an educational institution to manage co-op programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide co-op information, including job hours, job type, and agreement with your employer.
3. Confirm your co-op employment and agreement with your employer.

* **Student Information**: This section collects general information about you, the student. 
* **Co Op Information**: This section is divided into several sub-sections that collect specific details about your co-op experience.
