# ferpa_release_form_for_parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from parents or guardians about their child's release from school for activities, events, or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consents from your child's parents or guardians before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Ferpa Release** (Text): This field is used to capture any additional comments or remarks from the parent.
  - **parents_name** (Text): Please enter your name or the name of the person completing this form.
  - **email** (Email): Enter your email address or the email address of the person completing this form.
  - **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person completing this form.
  - **Student ID** (Text): Enter your child's student ID number.
  - **Student Name** (Text): Enter your child's name.
  - **Grade Level** (Select One): Select your child's current grade level from the options provided.
  - **Permission Given** (Select Multiple): Check the box if you give permission for your child to participate in the activity or event.
  - **Date** (Date): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
  - **School Year** (Text): Enter the current school year your child is in.
  - **Permission Given** (Select Multiple): Check the box if you give permission for your child to participate in the activity or event.
  - **Comments** (Text): Please provide any additional comments or questions about your child's participation in the activity or event.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consents from your child's parents or guardians before submitting this form.
- If you're unsure about any of the information or requirements, please reach out to the relevant school authorities for clarification.
