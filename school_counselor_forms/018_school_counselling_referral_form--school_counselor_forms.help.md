# School Counselling Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The School Counselling Referral Form is used to collect information and referrals from students, parents, or school staff members regarding a student's behavior or concerns. This form helps the school counsellor to identify the need for support and guidance and to take the necessary steps to address the situation. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's information, including name, and contact details.
2. Describe the student's behavior or concerns.
3. Choose the category of the referrer (Student/Parent/Guardian, School Staff/Teacher, or Other).
4. Provide the email and phone number of the person referring the student.
5. Enter the date of the last contact made with the student.
6. Enter the date the report was made.
7. Select the option for Signature, specifying who referred the student (Student/Parent/Guardian, School Staff/Teacher, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`Student/Parent/Guardian`): Enter the student's name and contact details.
* **Student Behaviour/Concerns** (`Student Behaviour`): Describe the student's behavior or concerns that led to the referral.
* **Privacy And Confidentiality** (`Privacy And Confidentiality`): If there are any privacy or confidentiality concerns, describe them here.
* **Referrer Name** (`Referrer Name`): Enter the name of the person making the referral (Student/Parent/Guardian, School Staff/Teacher, or Other).
* **Referrer Email** (`Referrer Email`): Enter the email of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Phone** (`Referrer Phone`): Enter the phone number of the person making the referral.
* **Signature** (`Student/Parent/Guardian`, `School Staff/Teacher`, or `Other`): Select the category of the referrer. 
* **Date Last Contacted** (`Date Last Contacted`): Enter the date the student was last contacted regarding their behavior or concerns.
* **Date Reported** (`Date Reported`): Enter the date the report was made.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the student's behavior or concerns.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
* Ensure that the referrer's contact details are accurate to enable easy follow-up communication.
