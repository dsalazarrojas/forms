# Parent Acknowledgement Form For Covid 19 School Procedures - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general-purpose parent acknowledgement form for COVID-19 school procedures. It is meant to be completed by parents or guardians to acknowledge and agree to the school's COVID-19 procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure you are completing the form for the correct student.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the school administration for clarification.
- Take your time to carefully read and understand the terms and conditions.
- Sign the electronic signature with your name and date, as this is a requirement for the form to be valid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): This field is for you to read and agree to the terms and conditions of the school's COVID-19 procedures. Please take some time to carefully read and understand the terms and conditions before signing.
* **E Signature** (`e_signature`, text, optional): This field is for you to sign the electronic acknowledgement. Please enter your name and date below.

## Tips
- Ensure you understand the school's COVID-19 procedures before signing this form.
- If you have concerns or questions, please contact the school administration.
- Complete this form with the intention of acknowledging and agreeing to the school's COVID-19 procedures.
