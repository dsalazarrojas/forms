# High School Student Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Student Check In Form is used to record students' attendance on campus or off campus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the date of your check-in from the "Date" field.
3. Choose the time of your check-in from the "Time" field.
4. Enter the location where you checked in on campus or off campus in the "Location" field.
5. Select your current status from the available options in the "Status" field.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes about your attendance in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name to identify yourself as a student.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of your check-in to record your attendance.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time you checked in to be more specific about your attendance.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required/optional): Describe where you are on campus or off campus when checking in.
* **Status** (`Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your status as On Campus, Off Campus, or Not Present to indicate your location.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or information, enter them in this field.
