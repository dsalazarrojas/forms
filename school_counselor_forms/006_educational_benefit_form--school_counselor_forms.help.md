# Educational Benefit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a student's educational journey, including details about their involvement with schools and institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`Student Information`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your full name, student ID, or any other unique identifier used by your educational institution.
* **School Information** (`School Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and address of the school or institution you're currently attending or have attended.
* **Benefit Information** (`Benefit Information`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any educational benefits you're currently receiving or have received, such as scholarships, grants, or other forms of support.
* **Other Benefit Information** (`Other Benefit Information`, `text`, required/optional): If there's any other relevant information about your educational benefits that don't fit into the previous category, please provide it here.
