# School Transport Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Transport Survey is designed to collect information about the school's transport services, including the category, description, admin's name, email, phone number, transport availability, transport frequency, transport cost, comments, survey date, survey time, survey comments, vehicle count, vehicle details, and the status of the survey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the school category and description section.
2. Enter the admin's name, email, and phone number in the admin's contact information section.
3. Provide information about the school's transport services in the transport services section.
4. Add survey details, including the survey date, survey time, survey comments, vehicle count, vehicle details, and survey status.
5. Finally, confirm whether the survey will be submitted or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Category**: Select the category that best describes the school's transport services.
* **School Description**: Provide a brief description of the school's transport services.
* **Admin Name**: Enter the name of the admin who is responsible for the school's transport services.
* **Admin Email**: Enter the email address of the admin who is responsible for the school's transport services.
* **Admin Phone**: Enter the phone number of the admin who is responsible for the school's transport services.
* **School Transport**: Select whether the school offers transport services.
* **Transport Frequency**: Select the frequency of the school's transport services.
* **Transport Cost**: Enter the cost of the school's transport services (optional).
* **School Transport Comment**: Provide any additional comments about the school's transport services.
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date of the survey.
* **Survey Time**: Enter the time of the survey (optional).
* **Survey Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the survey.
* **Vehicle Count**: Enter the number of vehicles used for the school's transport services (optional).
* **Vehicle Details**: Provide any additional details about the vehicles used for the school's transport services (optional).
* **Survey Type**: Select the type of survey (optional).
* **Survey Status**: Select the status of the survey (optional).
* **Survey Status Details**: Provide any additional details about the survey status (optional).
* **Survey Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the survey (optional).
* **Vehicle Count**: Enter the number of vehicles used for the school's transport services (optional).
* **Vehicle Details**: Provide any additional details about the vehicles used for the school's transport services (optional).
* **Survey Submit**: Confirm whether the survey will be submitted or not.
