# Student Grievance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Grievance Form is used to report incidents or concerns that require attention from school administrators or faculty.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or records.
2.  **Grievance Type**: Select the type of grievance or incident you are reporting. Choose from the options provided.
3.  **Description of Grievance**: Write a clear and detailed description of what happened.
4.  **Witness Name**: Enter the name of any witness(es) who may have seen or heard something about the incident.
5.  **Witness Email**: Enter the email address of the witness(es).
6.  **Witness Phone**: Enter the phone number of the witness(es), if applicable.
7.  **Date of Event**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
8.  **Time of Event**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
9.  **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, if applicable.
10.  **Additional Information**: Provide any additional details or evidence related to the incident.
11.  **Submitted By**: Enter your email address, as you are the one submitting the form.
12.  **Submitted To**: Enter the email address of the person or department you would like to receive the grievance form.
13.  **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time you are submitting the form.
14.  **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
15.  **School Email**: Enter the email address of your school.
16.  **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of your school.
17.  **School Address**: Enter the address of your school.
18.  **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
19.  **Faculty Member Involved**: Check Yes or No if a faculty member was involved in the incident.
20.  **Student Response**: Enter any comments or responses you would like to provide regarding the incident.
21.  **School Response**: Enter any responses or comments from the school or school administration.
22.  **Follow Up**: Check Yes or No if you would like to receive follow-up communication from the school administration.
23.  **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive follow-up communication.
24.  **Follow Up By**: Enter the name and email of who will follow up with you.
25.  **Status**: Enter the current status of the grievance (e.g., pending, resolved, etc.).
