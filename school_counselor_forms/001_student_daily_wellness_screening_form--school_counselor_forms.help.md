# Student Daily Wellness Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Wellness Screening Form is designed to help school administrators identify students who may need attention from a school nurse or other medical professional. This form helps you quickly assess a student's health and well-being and determine if they require any assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's contact information in the "Parent Info" field.
2. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
3. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
4. Check any symptoms the student is experiencing in the "Symptoms" field. Select all that apply.
5. Answer yes or no to questions about fever, cough, sore throat, sore eyes, and runny nose.
6. Enter the student's temperature, if applicable.
7. Enter the time of the student's last contact with someone outside the school.
8. Enter the date the student last attended school.
9. Enter the location of the student's last contact with someone outside the school.
10. Answer yes or no to questions about travel status, travel reason, travel duration, travel distance, travel mode, and travel group size.
11. Answer yes or no to questions about exposure and last questionnaire.
12. Enter any additional notes about the student's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Info**: Enter the parent's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Symptoms**: Select all symptoms the student is experiencing. Examples include:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Sore Throat
	+ Runny Nose
	+ Sore Eyes
* **Fever?**: Answer yes or no to whether the student has a fever.
* **Cough?**: Answer yes or no to whether the student has a cough.
* **Sore Throat?**: Answer yes or no to whether the student has a sore throat.
* **Sore Eyes?**: Answer yes or no to whether the student has sore eyes.
* **Runny Nose?**: Answer yes or no to whether the student has a runny nose.
* **Temperature**: Enter the student's current temperature, if applicable.
* **Time of Last Contact**: Enter the time of the student's last contact with someone outside the school.
* **Last Attended School**: Enter the date the student last attended school.
* **Last Contact Location**: Enter the location of the student's last contact with someone outside the school.
* **Travel Status**: Answer yes or no to whether the student has traveled recently.
* **Last Travel Location**: Enter the location of the student's last travel.
* **Travel Reason**: Enter the reason for the student's travel.
* **Travel Duration**: Enter the duration of the student's travel.
* **Travel Distance**: Enter the distance of the student's travel.
* **Travel Mode**: Select the mode of transportation used for travel (e.g. airplane, bus, car).
* **Travel Group Size**: Enter the size of the travel group.
* **Exposure**: Answer yes or no to whether the student has been exposed to someone with a contagious illness.
* **Exposure Description**: Enter a description of the student's exposure.
* **Last Questionnaire**: Answer yes or no to whether the student has completed a questionnaire within the past 24 hours.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the student's condition.
