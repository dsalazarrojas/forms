# student daily wellness assessment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Wellness Form is a tool used by school staff to gather information about a student's well-being on a daily basis. This form helps staff to quickly assess a student's situation and provide targeted support when needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions to fill out the form.
2. Enter the student's name in the **student name** field.
3. Enter the student's email address in the **student email** field.
4. Enter the school's email address in the **school email** field.
5. Enter the school's phone number in the **school phone** field.
6. If applicable, enter any additional parent contact information in the **parent contact info** field.
7. Provide any relevant comments or observations about the student in the **student comments** field.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the student from teachers or other school staff in the **teacher comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as they would like to be known.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
- **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required): Enter the school's email address where messages from this student can be sent.
- **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required): Enter the school's phone number where the student can be reached.
- **Parent Contact Info** (`parent_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information for the student's parent(s) or guardian(s).
- **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Provide any observations or comments about the student's well-being or behavior.
- **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the student from teachers or other school staff.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your input to ensure that the student receives the support they need.
* Only fill out this form for genuine student wellness assessments, do not use it for other purposes.
* Use this form only for students who have given their permission for this information to be collected.
