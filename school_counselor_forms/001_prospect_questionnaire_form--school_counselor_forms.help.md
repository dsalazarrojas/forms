# Prospect Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospect Questionnaire Form is a crucial document that helps the school administration to assess the suitability of a prospect for the school. It collects relevant information about the prospect's personal details, educational background, and preferences. Accurately filling out this form will help the school in making an informed decision about the prospect's enrollment in the school.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your birth date in the "Birth date" field (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Enter your school name (if applicable) in the "School name" field.
7. If applicable, enter your graduation date in the "Graduation date" field.
8. Enter your physical address in the "Address" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
10. For "Select one", choose one option from the dropdown list.
11. For "Select multiple", choose one or more options from the dropdown list.
12. (Optional) Enter a time of day in the "Time" field.
13. If you have any additional information or comments, use the "Note" field for that.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth date** (`birthdate`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **School name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school name (if applicable).
* **Graduation date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter your graduation date (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Select one** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options from the dropdown list.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): (Optional) Enter a time of day.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
