# transcript_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the authenticity of a student's transcript.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Name field with the student's name as per their student ID.
2. Enter the student's student ID number.
3. Provide your signature as a parent or authorized representative.
4. Enter the current date in the Date field.
5. Enter your school's name.
6. Select the grade level that the transcript is being authorized for (e.g. Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, etc.).
7. Choose the type of authorization that is being requested (e.g. Standard, Special Circumstances, etc.).
8. If you selected "Other" for the authorization type, please provide a brief explanation in the "Other Reason" field.
9. Enter your name and title as the authorized representative (e.g. School Principal).
10. Verify that the transcript is accurate and sign the form as the school counselor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as per their student ID.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Parent Signature**: This is the signature of the parent or authorized representative.
* **Date**: Enter the current date.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level**: Select the grade level that the transcript is being authorized for.
* **Transcripts Requested For**: Select the type of authorization that is being requested.
* **Authorization Type**: Select the type of authorization that is being requested.
* **Other Reason**: If you selected "Other" for the authorization type, provide a brief explanation.
* **Authorized By**: This is the name and title of the authorized representative.
* **School Email**: Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone**: Enter your school's phone number.
* **Student Permission**: This is the student's permission to release their transcript.
* **School Principal Signature**: This is the signature of the school principal.
* **School Principal Name**: Enter the name of the school principal.
* **School Principal Date**: Enter the date of the school principal.
* **School Principal Title**: Enter the title of the school principal.
* **School**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Counselor Signature**: This is the signature of the school counselor.
* **Authorized For**: This field is optional and can be used to specify who the transcript is authorized for.
* **School District**: Enter your school district.
* **School Address**: Enter your school's address.
* **School City**: Enter your school's city.
* **School State**: Enter your school's state.
* **School Counselor Signature**: This is the signature of the school counselor.
