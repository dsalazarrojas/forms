# Educational Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to apply for an educational grant to support a student's education. The form collects information from the school counselor and is used to evaluate the student's eligibility for the grant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as the school counselor.
2.  Enter the name of the school where you are working.
3.  Enter the amount of grant you are applying for.
4.  Enter the name of the student who is applying for the grant.
5.  Enter the date of birth of the student.
6.  Enter the name of the educational institution where the student is enrolled.
7.  Enter your email address as the contact person for this application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Grant Application** (`educational_grant_application_form_school_counselor_forms`, text): This field is where you enter your application for the educational grant. Please type a clear and concise description of the grant you are applying for.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text): Please enter the name of your school.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, number): Enter the amount of grant you are applying for.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text): Please enter the name of the student who is applying for the grant.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date): Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Educational Institution** (`educational_institution`, text): Enter the name of the educational institution where the student is enrolled.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): This field is for your contact information as the school counselor. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about this application.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise application description in the Educational Grant Application field.
* Double-check the student's date of birth to ensure it is accurate.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, don't hesitate to contact us.
