# Generic Transcript Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to generate unofficial transcripts for students in your school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID.
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Select the grade level of the student.
4. Enter the school ID.
5. Enter the school address.
6. Enter the school email.
7. Enter the school phone number.
8. Enter the student's name (same as step 2).
9. Enter the student's address.
10. Enter the student's phone number.
11. Enter the student's email.
12. Enter the student ID (same as step 1).
13. Select the transcript type (e.g., Standard Transcripts, Unofficial Transcripts, etc.).
14. Enter the transcript date.
15. Enter the date and time of the transcript request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the grade level of the student (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th).
* **School ID** (`school_id`, `number`, required): Enter the school ID.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required): Enter the school address.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `email`, required): Enter the school email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the school phone number.
* **School Counselor ID** (`school_counselor_id`, `number`, required): Enter the school counselor ID.
* **School Counselor Name** (`school_counselor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the school counselor's name.
* **Transcript Type** (`transcript_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of transcript (e.g., Standard Transcripts, Unofficial Transcripts, etc.).
* **Transcript Date** (`transcript_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the transcript.
* **Date** (`date`, `time`, required): Enter the date and time of the transcript request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the transcript request.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name (same as step 2).
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required): Enter the student's address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student ID** (`student_id_2`, `number`, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required): This is the same as step 1, enter the student's ID number.
* **2nd Grade Level** (`grade_level_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the 2nd grade level of the student (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th).
* **2nd Grade Level ID** (`grade_level_2_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the 2nd grade level ID.
* **2nd School ID** (`school_id_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the 2nd school ID.
* **Transcript Type ID** (`transcript_type_id`, `number`, required): Enter the transcript type ID.
* **Standard Transcripts** (`transcript_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the standard transcript type. 

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for the date and time fields.
