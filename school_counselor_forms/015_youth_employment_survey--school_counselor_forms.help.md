# Youth Employment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from youth who have participated in a certain program to understand their current employment status, work type, and other related details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions. The fields are not required, so you can skip any section that does not apply to you.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_info (Text)**: This field is a free text area where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
* **program_type (Select One)**: This field asks you to choose how you participated in the program. Choose the option that best describes your participation.
* **program_name (Text)**: If you wish to provide the name of the program you participated in, you can do so here.
* **program_start and program_end (Date)**: If you participated in the program for a specific duration, you can provide the start and end dates of your participation.
* **Are you working? (Select One)**: This field asks about your current employment status. Choose 'True' if you are working and 'False' if you are not.
* **What is your work type? (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more of the options that best describe your work type. You can select Part-time, Full-time, or Other.
* **How many hours do you work? (Number)**: If you are working, you can provide the number of hours you work.
* **Where do you work? (Text)**: Provide the location of your workplace.
* **What do you do in your job? (Text)**: Describe your job responsibilities.
* **What kind of job do you do? (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more of the options that best describe your job type. You can select Part-time, Full-time, or Internship.
* **Has your employment status changed recently? (Select One)**: If your employment status has changed recently, choose 'True'. Otherwise, choose 'False'.
