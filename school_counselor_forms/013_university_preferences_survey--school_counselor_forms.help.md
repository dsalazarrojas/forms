# University Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the University Preferences Survey, a brief questionnaire designed to gather your opinions and preferences regarding universities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select one or multiple options for "Choose University" (Page 1) from the dropdown menu, indicating your level of interest or affiliation with specific universities.
2. In the text field "Reasons choose university" (Page 2), provide a brief explanation for why you chose the universities you selected on Page 1. This is an opportunity to describe your personal reasons for choosing these institutions.
3. For "Future plans" (Page 3), select the option that best represents your future goals and aspirations regarding university involvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose University** (`Page 1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple universities that interest you or with which you have an affiliation. This field is designed to gauge your overall interest in various universities.
* **Reasons choose university** (`Page 2`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for your reasons behind choosing the universities selected on Page 1. This helps us understand your personal preferences and motivations.
* **Future plans** (`Page 3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose an option that reflects your future goals and aspirations related to universities. This field helps us understand your potential involvement or interests in these institutions over time.

## Tips
- Please complete the survey with honesty and accuracy to provide valuable insights for our team.
- If unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
