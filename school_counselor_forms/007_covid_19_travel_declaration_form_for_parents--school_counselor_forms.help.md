# COVID 19 Travel Declaration Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to declare their child's travel plans, health status, and other essential information related to COVID-19. The form ensures that the school is informed about the student's travel and takes necessary precautions to protect the student and the school community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the parent's name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide the student's full name, student ID number, and grade level.
3. Indicate if the family has planned or completed travel within the past 14 days or upcoming.
4. Enter the travel start and end dates if applicable.
5. Specify the travel destination (country and city) if applicable.
6. Choose the type of travel (domestic air, international air, road trip, cruise, train, or other).
7. Indicate if the destination is a high-risk COVID-19 area or if you are unsure.
8. Select the student's vaccination status (fully vaccinated, partially vaccinated, not vaccinated, or unknown).
9. Confirm if all traveling family members are fully vaccinated.
10. Declare if the family will get tested before or after travel.
11. Acknowledge understanding of potential quarantine requirements.
12. Inform the school about the travel and agree to remote learning upon return.
13. Enter any contact information while traveling.
14. Finally, confirm that you have received the travel guidelines and quarantine requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Email Address** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where the school can contact you.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where the school can contact you.
* **-- Student Information --** (`student_section`, note, optional): This section is for additional information about the student. You can add any relevant details about the student here.
* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **-- Travel Information --** (`travel_section`, note, optional): This section is for additional information about the travel. You can add any relevant details about the travel here.
* **Has your family planned or completed travel** (`travel_planned`, select_one, required): Indicate if your family has planned or completed travel within the past 14 days or upcoming.
* **Travel Start Date** (`travel_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the travel start date if applicable.
* **Travel End Date** (`travel_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the travel end date if applicable.
* **Travel Destination - Country and City** (`travel_destination`, text, optional): Enter the travel destination (country and city) if applicable.
* **Type of Travel** (`travel_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of travel (domestic air, international air, road trip, cruise, train, or other).
* **Is destination a high-risk COVID-19 area** (`high_risk_area`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the destination is a high-risk COVID-19 area or if you are unsure.
* **-- Health and Safety --** (`health_section`, note, optional): This section is for additional information about health and safety concerns. You can add any relevant details here.
* **Student Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Select the student's vaccination status (fully vaccinated, partially vaccinated, not vaccinated, or unknown).
* **All traveling family members fully vaccinated** (`family_vaccination`, select_one, optional): Confirm if all traveling family members are fully vaccinated.
* **Will family get tested before travel** (`test_before_travel`, select_one, optional): Declare if the family will get tested before travel.
* **Will family get tested after travel** (`test_after_travel`, select_one, optional): Declare if the family will get tested after travel.
* **I understand potential quarantine requirements** (`quarantine_understanding`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand the potential quarantine requirements.
* **School will be notified of travel** (`school_notification`, select_one, required): Inform the school about the travel and agree to remote learning upon return.
* **Student may need to participate in remote learning upon return** (`remote_learning`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand that the student may need to participate in remote learning upon return.
* **Contact Information While Traveling** (`contact_info_abroad`, text, optional): Enter any contact information while traveling.
* **I acknowledge receipt of travel guidelines and quarantine requirements** (`acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have received the travel guidelines and quarantine requirements.
