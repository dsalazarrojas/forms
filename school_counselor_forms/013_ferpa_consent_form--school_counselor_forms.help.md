# Ferpa Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from students for various aspects of their educational records and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and select the appropriate options for each field.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and completely.
3. Ensure you have read and understand the implications of your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **school_counselor_forms** (`text`, optional): Enter your name or identifier as a school counselor or administrator.
- **school_counselor_forms_school** (`select_multiple`, optional): 
  * Select 'Yes' if you are providing consent for school-related activities or records.
  * Select 'No' if you are not providing consent for school-related activities or records.
- **school_counselor_forms_1** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description or reason for providing or not providing consent.
- **school_counselor_forms_2** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description or reason for providing or not providing consent.
- **school_counselor_forms_3** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description or reason for providing or not providing consent.
- ... (Continue this pattern for all fields)
