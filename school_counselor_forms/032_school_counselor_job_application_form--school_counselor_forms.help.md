# School Counselor Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Counselor Job Application Form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for a school counselor position. This form is typically used by school administrators to gather necessary details about potential candidates, such as contact information, work experience, and education history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information fields on Page 1, including your name, email address, and other contact details.
2. Provide application details, which should include a brief description of your qualifications for the school counselor position.
3. Upload your resume or CV, if applicable.
4. Indicate if you are interested in a specific job position.
5. Describe your work experience, including previous positions held and job responsibilities.
6. List your education history, including any relevant degrees earned.
7. Provide references, including names and contact information.
8. List your skills and any relevant certifications.
9. Upload any supporting certificates, if applicable.
10. Repeat the above steps on Page 2, as some fields are duplicated for consistency and ease of use.
