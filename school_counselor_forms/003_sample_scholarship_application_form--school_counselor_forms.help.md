# Sample Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect application information from students to determine eligibility for a scholarship award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal contact information, including your name, email, and student ID.
2. Select the scholarship you are applying for and provide any additional scholarship details.
3. Write a brief essay explaining your background and goals (optional).
4. Enter your GPA, age, and other relevant student information.
5. Provide your school and parent information if applicable.
6. Review and sign the certification and acknowledgement sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Name
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required: false): This is your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Student ID
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required: false): Your student ID number, if applicable.

### Scholarship Name
* **Scholarship Name** (`scholarship_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of the scholarship you are applying for.

### Applicant Email
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required: false): Your email address where you can be contacted.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): A phone number where you can be reached for any follow-up questions or interviews.

### Scholarship Details
* **Scholarship Details** (`scholarship_details`, `text`, required: false): Any additional information about the scholarship you are applying for.

### Applicant Essay
* **Applicant Essay** (`applicant_essay`, `note`, required: false): A brief essay about yourself, your background, and your goals.

### GPA
* **GPA** (`applicant_gpa`, `number`, required: false): Your current GPA.

### School Name
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of your school or institution.

### School Address
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required: false): The address of your school or institution.

### Application Deadline
* **Application Deadline** (`application_deadline`, `date`, required: false): The deadline to submit your application.

### Application Start Date
* **Application Start Date** (`application_start_date`, `date`, required: false): The date you began your application process.

### Application End Date
* **Application End Date** (`application_end_date`, `date`, required: false): The date you completed your application.

### Time of Day
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required: false): The time of day you completed your application.

### Scholarship Amount
* **Scholarship Amount** (`scholarship_amount`, `number`, required: false): The amount of the scholarship award you are applying for.

### Scholarship Essay
* **Scholarship Essay** (`scholarship_essay`, `note`, required: false): A brief essay explaining why you are applying for this scholarship.

### Applicant Age
* **Applicant Age** (`applicant_age`, `number`, required: false): Your age as of the application date.

### Student Status
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select Single, Married, Divorced, or Widowed.

### Student Employment
* **Student Employment** (`student_employment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select Full-time, Part-time, Student, or Retired.

### Student English
* **Student English** (`student_english`, `select_one`, required: false): Select Native or Non-Native.

### Student GPA
* **Student GPA** (`student_gpa`, `number`, required: false): Your current GPA.

### Student Age
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, `number`, required: false): Your age as of the application date.

### Parent Name
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of a parent or guardian.

### Parent Address
* **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, `text`, required: false): The address of a parent or guardian.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Certify that all information is accurate and acknowledge that this form is for scholarship application purposes only.
