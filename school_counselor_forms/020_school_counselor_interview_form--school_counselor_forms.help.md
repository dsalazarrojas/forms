# School Counselor Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Counselor Interview Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from school counselors about student interviews. It captures details about the student, their parent or guardian, and the outcome of the interview.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and understand the purpose of each field.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_counselor_interview_form_student**: This section captures the student's name and demographic information.
  - **student_name**: Enter the student's name.
  - **student_grade**: Enter the student's grade level.
* **parent_name/guardian_name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **contact_number/contact_email**: Enter the contact information for the parent or guardian.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the student.
* **counselor_name**: Enter your name as the school counselor.
* **student_counseling_goals/student_counseling_outcomes/student_counseling_plan/student_counseling_progress/next_steps**: These sections capture details about the student's counseling goals, outcomes, plan, and progress.
* **contact_method**: Select the method of contact with the student or parent/guardian (e.g., Home, School, Email, Phone, Other).
* **contact_method_other**: If "Other" is selected in the "contact_method" field, enter the specific method of contact.
* **student_counselor_interview_form_completed**: Select whether the form is completed or not completed.
* **school_counselor_interview_form_completed**: Select whether the form is completed or not completed.
* **student_counselor_interview_form_signature/student_signature**: Select whether you, as the school counselor, have signed the form and whether the student has signed it.
* **student_counselor_interview_form_signature_date/student_signature_date**: Enter the date of your signature and the student's signature.
* **student_counselor_interview_form_completed_notes/school_counselor_interview_form_completed_notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the form completion.
* **student_signature**: Enter your signature as the school counselor.
* **school_counselor_interview_form_completed_date**: Enter the date the form was completed.
