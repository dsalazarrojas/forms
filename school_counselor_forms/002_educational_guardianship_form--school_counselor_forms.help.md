# Educational Guardianship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Guardianship Form is used to collect information from students' educational guardians, ensuring that their consent is captured and recorded in a secure and efficient manner.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure the student is under the age of 18 and needs a guardian's consent or approval.
* Gather all necessary information about the student's guardian, including their name, phone number, email, address, and relationship to the student.
* Enter the student's name, school name, and date of birth.
* Choose the type of educational guardian (e.g., high school diploma, GED, parent, or other).
* Provide any additional comments or notes for the guardian or school.
* Finally, sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Counselor Forms** (`school_counselor_forms`): A brief description of the form's purpose and instructions. This field is not required but helps clarify the form's context.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`): Enter the name of the student's guardian.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`): Enter the guardian's email address. If not required, you can leave this field blank.
* **Guardian Address** (`guardian_address`): Enter the guardian's home or work address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **School Email** (`school_email`): Enter the school's email address. If not required, you can leave this field blank.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`): Enter the school's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the student's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`): If the student's grade level is relevant, enter it.
* **Educational Guardian** (`educational_guardian`): Choose the type of educational guardian (e.g., high school diploma, GED, parent, or other).
* **Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`): Describe the relationship between the guardian and the student.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`): Sign the form to confirm the guardian's identity.
* **School Signature** (`school_signature`): Sign the form to confirm the school's acknowledgement.
* **Date** (`date`): Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of completion in 24-hour format.
* **Educational Guardian Type** (`educational_guardian_type`): Select the type of educational guardian from the provided options.
* **Guardian Note** (`guardian_note`): Provide any additional comments or notes for the guardian.
* **School Note** (`school_note`): Provide any additional comments or notes for the school.

This guide will help ensure accurate and efficient completion of the Educational Guardianship Form, providing clarity on each field's purpose and requirements.
