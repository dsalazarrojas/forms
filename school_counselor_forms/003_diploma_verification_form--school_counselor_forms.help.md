# Diploma Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diploma Verification Form is used to confirm the authenticity of a diploma. This form is intended for authorized personnel to verify the details of a student's educational background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the applicant's information, including their name, school name, and date of graduation.
2. Verify the degree received by the applicant, and indicate if they have a field of study and major.
3. Enter the applicant's GPA, if applicable.
4. If the diploma is verified, enter the verification date, the person who verified it, and the verification status.
5. Add any comments if necessary.
6. Enter the date and person who verified the diploma.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, required): Enter the name and other relevant details of the applicant.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school where the applicant earned their degree.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the school where the applicant earned their degree.
* **Date of Graduation** (`date_of_graduation`, date, required): Enter the date the applicant graduated.
* **Degree Received** (`degree_received`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether the applicant received a degree from the school.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): Enter the field of study the applicant completed.
* **Major** (`major`, text, optional): Enter the major of the applicant's field of study.
* **GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional): Enter the applicant's GPA, if applicable.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of graduation.
* **Verification** (`verification`, note, required): Enter a brief description of why this diploma is being verified.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the verification was performed.
* **Verification by** (`verification_by`, select_one, required): Indicate who verified the diploma.
* **Verification Status** (`verification_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the status of the verification (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the verification process.
* **Verified By** (`verified_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who verified the diploma.
* **Verified Date** (`verified_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the diploma was verified.
