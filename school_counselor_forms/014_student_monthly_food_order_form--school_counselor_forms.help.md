# Student Monthly Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Monthly Food Order Form is a form used to gather information from students about their meal preferences and special requests for the upcoming month. This information will be used to provide catering services to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your meal choices for the upcoming month.
2. Indicate if you have any special requests or dietary restrictions.
3. Enter your student ID number.
4. Choose the date for your food delivery.
5. Select the time for your food delivery.
6. Add any additional notes about your food preferences or requests.
7. Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
8. Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meal Choices** (`select_one_month`, optional): Choose one of the meal options provided.
    - Select one of the meal options, such as 'Yes' or 'No', to indicate if you would like to receive food for that month.
- **Special Requests** (`select_two_month`, optional): Indicate any special requests or dietary restrictions.
    - If you have any special requests, please select 'Yes' to indicate your needs.
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, optional): Enter your student ID number.
    - Please enter your student ID number as a way to identify your account.
- **Date** (`date`, optional): Choose the date for your food delivery.
    - Select the date you would like to receive your food.
- **Time** (`time`, optional): Choose the time for your food delivery.
    - Select the time you would like to receive your food.
- **Notes** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or requests.
    - Please add any additional notes about your food preferences or requests.
- **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address.
    - Please enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
- **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number.
    - Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information for your meal choices and special requests.
- Double-check the date and time for your food delivery to ensure it matches your schedule.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your convenience.
