# School Based Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Based Therapy Referral Form is designed to gather information for school-based therapy referrals. This form helps educators and school staff identify students who require additional support or therapy services, ensuring that necessary support is provided in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the instructions carefully, ensuring you understand the purpose and the type of information required.
2. Fill out each section of the form, providing accurate and detailed information about the student.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed, even if the information is not available.
4. Verify the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Page 1)**: This page collects personal details about the student, including their student ID, name, and any other relevant information required by your school's policies.
* **Support Staff Information (Page 2)**: Provide information about the support staff members involved in the student's care, including their names, roles, and contact details.
* **Referral Information (Page 3)**: Describe the reasons for referring the student for therapy, including any relevant medical or emotional issues.
* **Services (Page 4)**: List the specific services required or recommended for the student.
* **Parent/Guardian Information (Page 5)**: Gather contact details for the parent or guardian, ensuring they are aware of the referral and any subsequent actions.
* **Additional Information (Page 6)**: Provide any additional comments or context about the student's situation.
* **Review and Submission (Page 7)**: Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it for further action.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out each section of the form.
* Ensure that all necessary fields are completed, even if the information is not available.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to verify accuracy and completeness.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accurate, revising it as necessary.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact the relevant authorities or support staff for guidance.
