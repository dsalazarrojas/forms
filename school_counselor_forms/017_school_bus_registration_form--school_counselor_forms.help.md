# school_bus_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The school bus registration form is used to register a student for school bus services, ensuring accurate and complete information is collected for student identification and transportation coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Select the relevant trip dates for the student in the "Trip Dates" field.
4. Enter the bus number in the "Bus Number" field.
5. Enter the route number in the "Route Number" field.
6. Enter the stop number in the "Stop Number" field.
7. Choose the student type in the "Student Type" field.
8. Enter any relevant student notes in the "Student Notes" field.
9. Enter the school contact information in the "School Contact" field.
10. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
11. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
12. Enter the school bus phone number in the "School Bus Phone" field.
13. Enter the student's parents' information in the "Student Parents" field.
14. Enter the student's address in the "Student Address" field.
15. Enter any additional student notes in the "Student Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (1, number, required): Enter the student's unique identifier number.
* **Student Name** (2, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Trip Dates** (4, select_multiple, required): Select the dates for the trip.
* **Bus Number** (5, number, required): Enter the bus number.
* **Route Number** (6, number, required): Enter the route number.
* **Stop Number** (7, number, required): Enter the stop number.
* **Student Type** (8, select_one, required): Choose the type of student.
* **Student Notes** (9, note, required): Enter any relevant student notes.
* **School Contact** (10, text, required): Enter the school contact information.
* **Student Email** (11, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (12, text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **School Bus Phone** (13, text, required): Enter the school bus phone number.
* **Student Parents** (14, text, required): Enter the student's parents' information.
* **Student Address** (15, text, required): Enter the student's address.
* **Student Notes** (16, note, required): Enter any additional student notes.
