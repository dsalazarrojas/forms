# Work Study Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students to apply for work study programs. The form is used to evaluate student eligibility and schedule work study placements accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name.
2. Enter your school name.
3. Provide your contact phone number.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your student email address.
6. Enter your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
7. Enter the grade level of your school (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
8. Enter the number of hours you are available for work study per week.
9. Select the start date for your work study placement.
10. Select the end date for your work study placement.
11. Enter the location where you would like to be placed for work study (e.g., school site, department office, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your personal email address.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your school email address.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): Enter your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
* **School Grade** (`school_grade`, number, optional): Enter the grade level of your school (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
* **Work Study Hours** (`work_study_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are available for work study per week.
* **Work Study Start Date** (`work_study_start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date for your work study placement.
* **Work Study End Date** (`work_study_end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date for your work study placement.
* **Work Study Location** (`work_study_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you would like to be placed for work study (e.g., school site, department office, etc.).
