# Student Food Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on students' eating habits and food preferences. It's designed to help school counselors and administrators understand students' typical eating behaviors and identify any potential concerns or needs related to food.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your usual breakfast, lunch, and dinner choices.
2. Select your favorite food from the options provided.
3. Indicate how often you eat at home and at school.
4. Check if you have any food allergies or restrictions.
5. Provide your date of birth (if required).
6. Choose a time for meals at home.
7. Describe how you usually choose your food.
8. Provide any additional comments or remarks in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you usually eat for breakfast** (`usual_breakfast`): Please select all the foods you usually eat for breakfast from the options provided.
* **What do you usually eat for lunch** (`usual_lunch`): Select all the foods you typically eat for lunch from the options provided.
* **What do you usually eat for dinner** (`usual_dinner`): Choose all the foods you usually have for dinner from the options provided.
* **What is your favorite food** (`favorite_food`): Select one food that you enjoy the most from the options listed.
* **Which food do you like most** (`food_preference`): Choose all the foods you like the most from the options provided.
* **How often do you eat at home** (`eating_habits_home`): Describe in words how often you eat at home, e.g., "daily," "occasionally," or "rarely."
* **How often do you eat at school** (`eating_habits_school`): Describe in words how often you eat at school, e.g., "daily," "occasionally," or "rarely."
* **Do you have food allergies?** (`food_allergies`): Check "True" if you have any food allergies, and "False" otherwise.
* **Do you have food restrictions?** (`food_restrictions`): Check "True" if you have any food restrictions, and "False" otherwise.
* **What is your date of birth?** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY."
* **What time do you usually have meals at home?** (`time_of_meals`): Choose a time in the format "HH:MM".
* **How do you usually choose food?** (`food_choice`): Describe in words how you usually choose your food, e.g., "I like to try new things," "I follow a specific diet," etc.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_13`): Provide any additional comments or remarks you'd like to share with the surveyors.
