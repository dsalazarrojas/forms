# Student Representative Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for nominating a student representative for a school event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
*   Ensure that you are eligible to nominate someone.
*   Make sure the nominee is not already nominated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Nominator** (`student_nominator`, select_one, required: `false`): Choose `Yes` if you are nominating yourself, `No` otherwise.
*   **Student Nominatee Name** (`student_nominator_name`, text, required: `false`): Enter the name of the student you are nominating.
*   **Nomination Class** (`nomination_class`, select_multiple, required: `false`): Choose the class of the student you are nominating (e.g., high, low).
*   **Student School Year** (`student_school_year`, select_one, required: `false`): Choose the school year of the student you are nominating (e.g., yes, no).
*   **Student School Level** (`student_school_level`, select_multiple, required: `false`): Choose the school level of the student you are nominating (e.g., high, low).
*   **Student School Type** (`student_school_type`, select_multiple, required: `false`): Choose the type of school the student you are nominating attends (e.g., yes, no).
*   **Representative School** (`representative_school`, select_multiple, required: `false`): Choose the school that the representative will represent (e.g., yes, no).
*   **Nominee School** (`nominee_school`, select_multiple, required: `false`): Choose the school that the nominee attends (e.g., yes, no).
*   **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required: `false`): Enter the name of the student who is nominating.
*   **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required: `false`): Enter the date of the nomination (not required if not provided by the system).
*   **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required: `false`): Enter the reason for nominating the student.
*   **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required: `false`): Enter the email of the student who is nominating.
*   **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the student who is nominating.
*   **Student Nominator Education** (`student_nominator_education`, select_multiple, required: `false`): Choose the level of education of the student who is nominating (e.g., yes, no).

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and truthfully.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
