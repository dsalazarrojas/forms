# Academic Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about students' academic goals, strengths, challenges, and action plans, as well as any supports they may need from the school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Complete all required fields: "Student name", "Academic goals", "Current challenges", "Action plan and milestones", and "Counselor name and confirmation".
3. Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
4. Be specific when answering the "Student strengths" and "Current challenges" fields.
5. Create a realistic action plan with concrete steps and timelines for the "Action plan and milestones" field.
6. Specify the supports needed from the school in the "Supports needed from school" field.
7. Date the review in the "Planned review date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (Student name, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Student id** (Student id, text, optional): Enter the student's institutional identifier (e.g. student ID number).
* **Academic goals** (Academic goals, text, required): Describe the student's short-term and long-term academic goals.
* **Student strengths** (Student strengths, text, optional): List the student's academic strengths and achievements.
* **Current challenges** (Current challenges, text, required): Identify the areas where the student is struggling academically.
* **Action plan and milestones** (Action plan and milestones, text, required): Create a specific and achievable plan with concrete steps and timelines for the student's goals.
* **Supports needed from school** (Supports needed from school, select multiple, optional): Select all the relevant options that apply to the student's needs, such as tutoring, counseling, accessibility accommodations, and career advising.
* **Planned review date** (Planned review date, date, optional): Choose a specific date and time for the student's progress review.
* **Counselor name and confirmation** (Counselor name and confirmation, text, optional): Enter the counselor's initials or typed name for confirmation.
