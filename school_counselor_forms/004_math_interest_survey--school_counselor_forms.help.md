# Math Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Math Interest Survey is a tool to help school counselors and teachers gather information about students' interests and engagement in math classes. This data can be used to inform teaching strategies and create more inclusive learning environments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please select one or more of the categories that best describe your interests.
2. Provide a brief description of why you are interested in each selected category.
3. You may select one or all of the categories depending on your interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_counselor_forms** (`school_counselor_forms`, text, optional): Select this category if you are interested in topics related to school counselors. This could include activities like academic advising, mental health, or student support.
* **teacher_forms** (`teacher_forms`, text, optional): Choose this category if you are interested in topics related to teachers, such as instructional strategies or classroom management.
* **description** (`description`, note, optional): Use this field to provide a brief description of your interests or goals. For example, you might mention how you want to improve your math skills or what specific topics you are looking forward to learning about.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when selecting categories, as this will help school counselors and teachers better understand your interests.
* If you are unsure about a category, you can always ask for clarification or choose "Other" to provide more information.
* Remember to be honest about your interests and preferences, as this will help create a more accurate picture of your needs.
