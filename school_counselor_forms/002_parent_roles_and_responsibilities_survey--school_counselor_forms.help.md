# Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey" is designed to gather information about the roles and responsibilities parents take on in their children's education and well-being. This form is intended to collect detailed descriptions or open-ended answers from parents about their involvement, their children's skills, knowledge, and abilities, as well as their communication skills and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Carefully read through each field carefully.
- Type your responses to each question in the text box provided.
- Make sure to answer each question as thoroughly as possible to provide the best information.
- Once you've filled out all the required fields, submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey** (`parent_roles_and_responsibilities_survey`, text, not required): This field is a text box where you can describe your roles and responsibilities as a parent in your child's education and well-being.
- **Student Parent** (`student_parent`, text, not required): Describe your relationship with your child, such as your child's name, age, and a brief statement about your parental involvement.
- **Student Skills** (`student_skills`, text, not required): Describe your child's skills, abilities, or areas of improvement.
- **Parent Knowledge** (`parent_knowledge`, text, not required): Share your level of knowledge about your child's education, such as their academic performance, strengths, or areas of concern.
- **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, text, not required): Describe your communication methods or styles with your child, such as verbal or non-verbal cues.
- **Parent Abilities** (`parent_abilities`, text, not required): Share your parental abilities or skills that support your child's development, like patience, empathy, or discipline.
- **Parent Abilities 2** (`parent_abilities_2`, text, not required): This field seems to be a duplicate of "Parent Abilities" and might be removed for redundancy; however, it's included here to maintain consistency with the original form design. Describe additional parental abilities or skills that support your child's development.
- **Parent Responsibilities** (`parent_responsibilities`, text, not required): Describe the responsibilities you take on as a parent for your child's education and well-being, such as helping with homework or attending parent-teacher conferences.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
- Keep in mind that this form is for collecting detailed descriptions, so feel free to elaborate on your answers.
- If you need help or clarification on any of the questions, contact your school or the relevant authority for support.
