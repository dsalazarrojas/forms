# School Age Personal Analysis Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Age Personal Analysis Questionnaire is a tool used by school counselors or administrators to gather information about a student's personal and educational background, interests, and strengths. This information will be used to develop an educational plan that meets the student's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the student's personal and educational background information.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their report card or identification.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required: false): Select the student's grade level (e.g., Kindergarten, 1st Grade, 2nd Grade, etc.).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the parent's full name as it appears on their report card or identification.
* **Parent Contact Info** (`parent_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the parent's contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Student Birthday** (`student_birthday`, `date`, required: false): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Strengths and Weaknesses** (`strengths_and_weaknesses`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's strengths and weaknesses in the following format:
	+ Strengths: List 1-3 strengths, 1 sentence each.
	+ Weaknesses: List 1-2 weaknesses, 1 sentence each.
* **Educational History** (`educational_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's educational background, including previous schools or educational programs attended.
* **Social Skills** (`social_skills`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's social skills, including their ability to work with peers, communicate effectively, and interact with others.
* **Family Background** (`family_background`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about the student's family, including their home environment, family dynamics, and any relevant family history.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `text`, required: false): If the student has any special needs, such as a disability or health condition, describe them here.
* **Behavioral Concerns** (`behavioral_concerns`, `text`, required: false): Describe any behavioral concerns or issues the student is currently facing.
* **Emotional Intelligence** (`emotional_intelligence`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's emotional intelligence and ability to regulate their emotions.
* **Learning Difficulties** (`learning_difficulties`, `text`, required: false): Describe any learning difficulties the student is currently facing, such as reading or math challenges.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, `text`, required: false): List the student's interests, including hobbies, extracurricular activities, or other activities they enjoy.
* **Student Goal** (`student_goal`, `text`, required: false): Describe the student's short-term and long-term goals.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `text`, required: false): Comments from the student's teacher(s) about their performance and progress.
* **School Counselor Comments** (`school_counselor_comments`, `text`, required: false): Comments from the school counselor about the student's performance and progress.
* **School Principal Comments** (`school_principal_comments`, `text`, required: false): Comments from the school principal about the student's performance and progress.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, `text`, required: false): Additional comments or information about the student.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `text`, required: false): The student's signature, indicating they have reviewed and completed the form.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required: false): The parent's signature, indicating they have reviewed and completed the form.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, `text`, required: false): The teacher's signature, indicating they have reviewed and completed the form.
* **School Principal Signature** (`school_principal_signature`, `text`, required: false): The school principal's signature, indicating they have reviewed and completed the form.
* **School Counselor Signature** (`school_counselor_signature`, `text`, required: false): The school counselor's signature, indicating they have reviewed and completed the form.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required: false): The date the form was completed.
 
## Tips

* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Provide as much detail as possible in each field.
* Ask for help if you are unsure about any section or question.
