# release of academic records form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Release of Academic Records form is used to transfer or verify student information to authorized individuals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field. This is the student's official full name.
2. Enter the student's email address in the **Student Email** field. This is the student's official email address.
3. Select the authorized recipient from the list. This person may be a teacher, counselor, or other staff member at the student's current or future school.
4. Enter the authorized recipient's name in the **Authorized Recipient Name** field.
5. Enter the authorized recipient's email address in the **Authorized Recipient Email** field.
6. Enter the authorized recipient's phone number in the **Authorized Recipient Phone** field.
7. Enter the authorized recipient's fax number in the **Authorized Recipient Fax** field.
8. Enter the date the authorization was given in the **Authorization Date** field.
9. Enter the school's name in the **School Name** field.
10. Enter the school's address in the **School Address 1** field.
11. Enter the school's address in the **School Address 2** field.
12. Enter the school's city in the **School City** field.
13. Enter the school's state in the **School State** field.
14. Enter the school's zip code in the **School Zip** field.
15. Enter the school's phone number in the **School Phone** field.
16. Enter the school's fax number in the **School Fax** field.
17. Enter the authorized person's name in the **Authorized Person Name** field.
18. Enter the authorized person's title in the **Authorized Person Title** field.
19. Enter the authorized person's department in the **Authorized Person Department** field.
20. Enter the authorized person's phone number in the **Authorized Person Phone** field.
21. Enter the authorized person's email address in the **Authorized Person Email** field.
22. Enter the authorized person's fax number in the **Authorized Person Fax** field.
23. Enter the authorized person's signature in the **Authorized Person Signature** field.
24. Enter the authorized date in the **Authorized Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's official email address.
* **Authorized Recipient**: Select from the list who the records will be released to.
* **Authorized Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the person receiving the records.
* **Authorized Recipient Email**: Enter the email address of the person receiving the records.
* **Authorized Recipient Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person receiving the records.
* **Authorized Recipient Fax**: Enter the fax number of the person receiving the records.
* **Authorization Date**: Enter the date the authorization was given.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school.
* **School Address 1**: Enter the school's street address.
* **School Address 2**: Enter any additional address information.
* **School City**: Enter the city of the school.
* **School State**: Enter the state of the school.
* **School Zip**: Enter the zip code of the school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school.
* **School Fax**: Enter the fax number of the school.
* **Authorized Person Name**: Enter the name of the person authorizing the records release.
* **Authorized Person Title**: Enter the title of the person authorizing the records release.
* **Authorized Person Department**: Enter the department of the person authorizing the records release.
* **Authorized Person Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person authorizing the records release.
* **Authorized Person Email**: Enter the email address of the person authorizing the records release.
* **Authorized Person Fax**: Enter the fax number of the person authorizing the records release.
* **Authorized Person Signature**: Enter the signature of the person authorizing the records release.
* **Authorized Date**: Enter the date the authorization was given.
* **School ID**: Enter the school's identifier or student ID number.

## Tips

* Verify the authenticity of the request by checking internal records or systems.
* Ensure the student's consent is given before releasing records.
* Use the correct authorized recipient and school information to prevent errors.
