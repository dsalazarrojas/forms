# Student Data Base Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store student data for internal administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with the student's first name.
2. Fill in the Last Name field with the student's last name.
3. Enter the Date Of Birth field with the student's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter the Student Id field with the student's unique ID number.
5. Enter the Email field with the student's email address.
6. Fill in the Phone field with the student's phone number.
7. Select the Grade Level field with the student's current grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
8. Fill in the Teacher field with the name of the student's teacher.
9. Fill in the Class Name field with the name of the student's class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's unique ID number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the student's current grade level from the options 9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's teacher.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's class.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Grade Level) to ensure proper completion of the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your school counselor for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only and will not be shared publicly.
