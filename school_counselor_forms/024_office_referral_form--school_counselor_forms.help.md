# Office Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Referral Form is designed to provide a structured way for school administrators or counselors to document and track referrals for students who require support or interventions. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured, enabling the school to provide the most suitable assistance to the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the student's name**: In the "Student Name" field, please enter the name of the student who is being referred.
2. **Provide the parent's name**: In the "Parent Name" field, enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
3. **Select the referral type**: Choose the type of referral that best describes the student's situation from the "Referral Type" dropdown menu. This will help the school understand the nature of the referral.
4. **Enter the student's ID**: If applicable, please enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
5. **Date and Time**: Provide the date and time of the referral in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
6. **Reason for Referral**: Describe the reason for the referral in the "Reason for Referral" text field. Be as detailed as possible to ensure that the school understands the student's needs.
7. **Select requested services**: Check the boxes for any services the student requires or has expressed interest in. These services can include but are not limited to Counseling, Academic Support, or Social Support.
8. **Assign staff**: If applicable, select the assigned staff member from the "Assigned Staff" dropdown menu.
9. **Assigned tool**: If applicable, enter the name of the tool assigned to the student in the "Assigned Tool" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student who is being referred.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of referral that best describes the student's situation.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the student's ID number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the referral.
* **Reason for Referral** (`reason`, text, required/optional): Describe the reason for the referral in detail.
* **Requested Services** (`requested_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes for any services the student requires or has expressed interest in.
* **Assigned Staff** (`assigned_staff`, select_one, required/optional): If applicable, select the assigned staff member.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the tool assigned to the student.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the school provides the most suitable assistance to the student.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant school staff for clarification.
* Please do not use this form for non-educational purposes or for students not associated with the school.
* This form is for educational use only.
