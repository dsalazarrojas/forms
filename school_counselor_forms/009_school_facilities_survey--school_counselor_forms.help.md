# School Facilities Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Facilities Survey is designed to gather information about the condition, safety, and accessibility of school facilities. This information will be used to assess and improve the facilities to ensure a safe and conducive learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use the options provided for select fields, such as facilities condition or safety rating.
3. You can submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
4. If you need to make changes or corrections, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Environment (id: 2)**: Describe the overall condition and atmosphere of the school facilities, including cleanliness, maintenance, and overall feel.
* **Facilities Condition (id: 3)**: Select the condition of the facilities from the options provided, which are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Safety Rating (id: 4)**: Rate the safety of the facilities from 1-2 years, 2-3 years, 3-5 years, or more than 5 years.
* **Facility Equipment (id: 5)**: Describe the condition, availability, and functionality of the school's equipment and tools.
* **Facilities Accessibility (id: 6)**: Describe the condition and accessibility of the facilities for students with disabilities or special needs.
* **Facilities Security (id: 7)**: Describe the security features and measures in place to ensure student and staff safety.
* **Facilities Health (id: 8)**: Describe the health and hygiene conditions of the facilities, including cleanliness and maintenance.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please refer to the school administration or facilities management.
* If you need to make changes or corrections to the form, please contact the relevant authorities.
