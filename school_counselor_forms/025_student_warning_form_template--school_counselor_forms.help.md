# Student Warning Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Warning Form Template is a tool used by school counselors to document and address behavioral issues or concerns related to students. This form helps school counselors communicate their expectations, document incidents, and provide clear guidance to students in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the relevant form**: Choose the correct form from the provided options, such as "School Counselor Forms" or "Student Warning Form Template".
2. **Enter student information**: Fill in the student's name, grade level, and ID number.
3. **Document the issue**: Describe the behavioral issue or concern that requires attention. Be specific and provide details about the incident or pattern of behavior.
4. **Communicate expectations**: Clearly state the expectations or consequences for the student's behavior.
5. **Provide guidance and support**: Offer suggestions for the student to follow or improve their behavior.
6. **Review and sign**: Review the form for accuracy and completeness, and sign it to confirm that you have addressed the student's behavior.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Counselor Forms** (`school_counselor_forms`, text, required/optional): Select the correct form from the options provided. This field helps you to access the relevant form for documentation and communication.
- **Student Warning Form Template** (`student_warning_form_template`, text, required/optional): This field is a general title to remind you that you are creating a student warning form. While not required, it helps to keep you organized.
- **Document Behavioral Issues and Communicate Expectations to Students** (`document_behavioral_issues_and_communicate_expectations_to_students`, text, required/optional): Use this field to clearly and specifically describe the behavioral issue or concern. Be sure to include details about the incident or pattern of behavior, and state the expectations or consequences for the student's behavior.
- **Provide Guidance and Support** (text, required/optional): Offer suggestions or guidance for the student to improve their behavior or follow the expectations. This is an opportunity to provide support and help the student learn from their mistakes.
 
Remember to review and sign the form to confirm that you have addressed the student's behavior.
