# Student Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from students to register them for sports activities at their school. The form will be used to gather essential details such as the student's name, grade level, and contact information. This information will be used to communicate with parents, guardians, and school staff about sports-related activities and events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, please make sure you are on the correct page.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" on each field.
3. Double-check that all fields have valid and accurate information.
4. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below for more details.
