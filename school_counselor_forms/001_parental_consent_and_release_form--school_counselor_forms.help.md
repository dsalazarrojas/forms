# Parental Consent And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to obtain parental consent and release information from parents or guardians for their child's participation in school activities, programs, or events. It typically includes fields to collect basic contact information, emergency contact details, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all the necessary details.
3. Submit the completed form to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Parental Consent And Release Form

This is the main title of the form, indicating its purpose and scope.

### 2. Parent/Guardian Information

* **Parent/Guardian Information (parent_guardian_info)** (`text`, Optional): This field is for collecting basic contact information of the parent or guardian.

### 3. Consent

* **Consent (consent)** (`select_one`, Optional): Please indicate whether you give consent for your child's participation. Options: "True" or "False". Please note that this field is optional, but it's essential to answer it to provide consent.

### 4. Parent Contact Information

* **Parent Contact Information (parent_contact_info)** (`text`, Optional): This field is for you to provide any additional contact information you'd like to share with the school.

### 5. Emergency Contact

* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)** (`text`, Optional): In case of an emergency, this is the contact information you'd like us to use.

### 6. Student Information

* **Student Information (student_info)** (`text`, Optional): Provide any relevant details about your child that the school should know.

### 7. Program Information

* **Program Information (program_info)** (`text`, Optional): Describe the specific program or activity your child is participating in.

### 8. Other Information

* **Other Information (other_info)** (`text`, Optional): You can use this field for any other information you think is relevant or necessary. 

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all the required fields correctly.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the school for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
