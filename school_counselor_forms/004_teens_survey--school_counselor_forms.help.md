# Teens Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teens Survey form is designed to collect information from teenagers about their feelings, expectations, and future plans. This form is meant to gather qualitative feedback and insights to support school counselors and other stakeholders in understanding the needs and goals of the teenagers they work with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
2. You can type your answers in the text fields provided.
3. Make sure to fill out each page of the form completely before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feelings Question** (feelings_question, text, required: false): This question is an open-ended space for you to express your feelings and thoughts. Please describe your current emotional state or any concerns you may be experiencing.
- **Expectations Question** (expectations_question, text, required: false): This question is an open-ended space for you to express your expectations or goals. Describe what you hope to achieve or accomplish in the near future.
- **Future Plans Question** (future_plans_question, text, required: false): This question is an open-ended space for you to describe your future plans and goals. What are your aspirations, goals, or objectives for the next chapter of your life?

## Tips
- Please answer these questions honestly and thoughtfully.
- You can type as much or as little as you need to fully express your thoughts and feelings.
- If you need help or have questions, contact <form@gic.mx>.
