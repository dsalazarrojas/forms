# Academic Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Records Request Form is used for requesting official academic records, such as transcripts and proof of enrollment, for students or alumni.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as the requestor.
2. Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
3. Provide your contact email address and phone number.
4. Choose the type of records you are requesting (e.g., official transcript, unofficial transcript, proof of enrollment, etc.).
5. Select the number of copies of the records you require.
6. Specify your graduation status (current student, alumni, or former student).
7. Enter the year of graduation or last attendance.
8. Choose your preferred delivery method (email, standard mail, express mail, or pickup in person).
9. If applicable, provide a mailing address for physical delivery.
9. If you need rush processing, select the option to expedite the processing time for an additional fee.
10. Provide any special instructions or notes for the processing staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Student or Alumni** (`full_name`, required): This field is for the full name of the student or alumni being requested.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter the date of birth of the student or alumni for verification purposes.
* **Contact Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address for the school to contact you with updates and confirmations.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your phone number for the school to contact you with updates and confirmations.
* **Select the records you are requesting** (`records_requested`, required): Choose the type of records you are requesting (e.g., official transcript, unofficial transcript, proof of enrollment, etc.).
* **Number of copies required** (`number_of_copies`, required): Enter the total number of copies of the records you require.
* **Are you a current student or alumni?** (`graduation_status`, required): Choose whether you are a current student, alumni, or former student.
* **Year of Graduation or Last Attendance** (`graduation_year`, required): Enter the year of graduation or last attendance.
* **How should the records be delivered?** (`delivery_preference`, required): Choose your preferred delivery method (email, standard mail, express mail, or pickup in person).
* **Mailing Address - if applicable** (`delivery_address`, optional): Enter a full address for physical delivery if you selected standard mail or express mail.
* **Do you require rush processing for an additional fee?** (`rush_processing`, required): Choose whether you need rush processing for an additional fee.
* **Any special instructions for this request** (`special_instructions`, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes for the processing staff.
