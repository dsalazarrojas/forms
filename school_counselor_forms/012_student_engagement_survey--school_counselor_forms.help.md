# Student Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student engagement survey is designed to gather feedback from students and teachers on the effectiveness of the school's educational programs. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance student engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **School Name**: Provide the name of your school.
2. **Student Id**: Enter your student ID number.
3. **Student Grade**: Select your current grade level.
4. **School Grade**: Select your current school grade level.
5. **Student Subjects**: Choose the subjects you are currently studying or interested in.
6. **Teacher Subjects**: Choose the subjects your teacher covers in class (optional).
7. **Student Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the school's programs.
8. **Teacher Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for your teacher (optional).
9. **Student Email**: Enter your school email address.
10. **Teacher Email**: Enter your teacher's email address (optional).
11. **Teacher Phone**: Enter your teacher's phone number (optional).
12. **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number.
13. **Classroom Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your classroom or teacher (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Student Id**: Enter your student ID number to help us identify your responses accurately.
* **Student Grade**: Select your current grade level to help us understand your perspective.
* **School Grade**: Select your current school grade level to help us assess your progress.
* **Student Subjects**: Choose the subjects you are currently studying or interested in to help us understand your interests.
* **Teacher Subjects**: Choose the subjects your teacher covers in class (if applicable).
* **Student Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the school's programs.
* **Teacher Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for your teacher (if applicable).
* **Student Email**: Enter your school email address for communication purposes.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter your teacher's email address (if applicable).
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter your teacher's phone number (if applicable).
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Classroom Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your classroom or teacher (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your teacher or school administrator.
* Your responses will be treated with confidentiality and handled by authorized personnel only.
