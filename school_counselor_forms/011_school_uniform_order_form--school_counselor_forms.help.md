# School Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Uniform Order Form is designed to collect information from students to process uniform orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your School ID in the "School ID" field.
* Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
* Select your Date of Birth from the calendar.
* Choose your ordering preference for "Uniform Type" (if applicable).
* Select the items you would like to order from the multiple-select fields.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Add any additional notes or comments about your order in the "Note" field.
* Choose the time slot you would like to pick up your order from the time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (order_form_1)**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **School ID (order_form_2)**: Enter your student ID number.
* **School ID (school_id_label)**: Enter your school ID number (repeated to be sure the system captures it properly). 
* **Last Name (order_form_3)**: Enter your last name in this field. 
* **Date of Birth (order_form_4)**: Select your date of birth from the calendar. 
* **Uniform Type (order_form_5)**: Choose the type of uniform you want to order. 
* **Select Multiple 1 (select_multiple_1)**: Select one or more items from the list. 
* **Select Multiple 2 (select_multiple_2)**: Select one or more items from the list. 
* **Select Multiple 3 (select_multiple_3)**: Select one or more items from the list. 
* **Phone (phone_label)**: Enter your phone number. 
* **Email (email_label)**: Enter your email address. 
* **Note (note_label)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order. 
* **Time (time_label)**: Choose the time you would like to pick up your order. 
* **Submit (submit_button)**: Click to submit your order for processing.
