# Child Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect critical contact and medical information for a child in case of an emergency. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the necessary authorities and medical professionals are able to respond promptly and effectively in critical situations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the form submission date and time.
2. Enter the child's legal first name.
3. Enter the child's legal last name.
4. Provide the child's date of birth.
5. Select the child's current grade level.
6. Enter the child's school name.
7. Enter the primary contact person's legal first name.
8. Enter the primary contact person's legal last name.
9. Select the primary contact person's relationship to the child.
10. Enter the primary contact person's home phone number.
11. Enter the primary contact person's work phone number (if applicable).
12. Enter the primary contact person's cell phone number.
13. Enter the primary contact person's email address.
14. Enter the secondary contact person's legal first name.
15. Enter the secondary contact person's legal last name.
16. Select the secondary contact person's relationship to the child.
17. Enter the secondary contact person's phone number.
18. Enter the secondary contact person's email address (if applicable).
19. Enter the name and phone number of the child's primary care physician or medical provider.
20. Enter any additional instructions or medical conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission Date**: Select the date and time when the form was submitted. This is a required field to track when the information was collected.
* **Child First Name**: Enter the child's legal first name. This is the name by which the child is known in official documents and records.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the child's legal last name. This is the surname of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth. This is required to verify the child's age and any relevant medical conditions.
* **Child Grade Level**: Select the child's current grade level in school. This will help in identifying the child's school and educational requirements.
* **Child School Name**: Enter the child's school name. This is where the child currently attends school.
* **Primary Contact First Name**: Enter the primary contact person's legal first name.
* **Primary Contact Last Name**: Enter the primary contact person's legal last name.
* **Primary Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of the primary contact person to the child (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, etc.).
* **Primary Contact Home Phone**: Enter the primary contact person's home phone number.
* **Primary Contact Work Phone**: Enter the primary contact person's work phone number (if applicable).
* **Primary Contact Cell Phone**: Enter the primary contact person's cell phone number.
* **Primary Contact Email**: Enter the primary contact person's email address.
* **Secondary Contact First Name**: Enter the secondary contact person's legal first name.
* **Secondary Contact Last Name**: Enter the secondary contact person's legal last name.
* **Secondary Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of the secondary contact person to the child.
* **Secondary Contact Phone**: Enter the secondary contact person's phone number.
* **Secondary Contact Email**: Enter the secondary contact person's email address (if applicable).
* **Emergency Medical Provider**: Enter the name of the child's primary care physician or medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the child's primary care physician or medical provider.
* **Medical Insurance Company**: Enter the name of the child's medical insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Medical Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the child's medical insurance policy or member number (if applicable).
* **Known Medical Conditions**: List any known medical conditions or allergies of the child.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications the child is taking.
* **Additional Emergency Instructions**: Enter any additional instructions for emergency situations, such as medical conditions that require special care or procedures.

Note: The required fields are marked as such in the form. It's essential to complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure the form's purpose is fulfilled.
