# school_counseling_progress_note - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by school counselors to record the progress of students in the school counseling process. The form captures essential details about each student, including their name, progress date, location, and other relevant information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Select the date of the student's progress note in the `Date` field.
3. Enter the time of the student's progress note in the `Time` field.
4. Enter the location of the student's progress note in the `Location` field.
5. Provide a detailed note about the student's progress in the `Note` field.
6. Select the student's school year from the dropdown menu in the `School Year` field.
7. Select the semester in which the student is currently enrolled in the `Semester` field.
8. Select the student's grade level (9-12) in the `Grade Level` field.
9. Enter the school district in which the student attends in the `School District` field (if applicable).
10. Enter the name of the school the student attends in the `School Name` field (if applicable).
11. Enter the name of the teacher who is working with the student in the `Teacher Name` field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the full name of the student.
* **Date**: Select the date of the student's progress note using the calendar picker.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the day when the progress note was taken.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the progress note was taken.
* **Note**: Provide a detailed description of the student's progress and any relevant comments.
* **School Year**: Select the school year in which the student is currently enrolled.
* **Semester**: Select the semester in which the student is currently enrolled in the school.
* **Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level (9-12) to indicate their current academic level.
* **School District**: Enter the school district in which the student attends, if applicable.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the student attends, if applicable.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher who is working with the student, if applicable.
* **Student Level**: Provide additional information about the student's level of progress.
* **Subject Area**: Enter the subject area in which the student is making progress, if applicable.
* **Student Goals**: Enter the student's goals and objectives, if applicable.
