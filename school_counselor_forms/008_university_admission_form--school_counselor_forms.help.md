# University Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Admission Form is a tool for students to provide their personal and academic information to the admissions committee. This form helps the committee assess eligibility and evaluate student applications for admission to the university.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name, email address, contact number, and student ID (if applicable).
2. Choose your nationality (International or Domestic) and level of study (Bachelors, Masters, PhD, or Others).
3. Select your program of study (if you have already chosen a program, provide the name).
4. Enter your date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter a working contact number where you can be reached.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): If applicable, provide your student ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your nationality (International or Domestic).
* **Level of Study** (`level_of_study`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of study you are applying for (Bachelors, Masters, PhD, or Others).
* **Program of Study** (`program_of_study`, text, optional): If you have already chosen a program, provide the name.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid form submission errors.
- If you are unsure about your nationality or level of study, you can ask the admissions committee for guidance.
- If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the admissions team for support.
