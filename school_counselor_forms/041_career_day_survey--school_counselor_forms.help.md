# Career Day Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Day Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from students who participate in a Career Day event. The form is used to understand their experience, opinions, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you have attended the Career Day event.
2. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
3. Answer all required fields (Student name, Grade level, Usefulness rating, Would attend again, and Interest in follow-up).
4. You can leave optional fields (Student name, Additional comments) blank if not applicable.
5. Choose the options for each question based on your experience and opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (Student name, text, required): This field is for your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your school records.
* **Grade level** (Grade level, text, required): Please enter your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, etc.).
* **Favorite presentation or booth** (Favorite presentation or booth, text, optional): This field is for you to mention which presentation or booth you found most interesting or helpful.
* **Usefulness rating** (Usefulness rating, select_one, required): Choose one of the options (Very useful, Somewhat useful, Neutral, Not useful) that best describes your experience with the Career Day event.
* **Topics of interest** (Topics of interest, select_multiple, optional): Select all the career areas that interested you the most after the event. You can choose more than one option.
* **Suggest improvements** (Suggest improvements, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving future Career Day events, please mention them here.
* **Would attend again** (Would attend again, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options (True, False, Maybe) that indicates your intent to participate in similar events in the future.
* **Interest in follow up** (Interest in follow up, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options (True, False) that indicates your interest in receiving guidance from the school counselors.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments, text, optional): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please mention them here.
