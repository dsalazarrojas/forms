# Student Technology Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights from students on their technology needs, teacher support, technology access, and their own needs. Your responses will help us better understand how to support you with technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read through each question carefully to understand what we're asking.
2.  Choose your answers from the provided options.
3.  Fill out every field that requires an answer. You can leave the optional fields empty if you don't have an opinion or preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What technology do you need
* **What technology do you need** (Text, Optional): This is a free text field where you can type out any technology you feel you need for your education. You can mention any hardware, software, or other technology that you think would be helpful for you.

### Teacher support
* **Teacher support** (Select multiple, Optional): How would you rate your current teacher support? Select all options that apply to you:
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Technology Access
* **Technology Access** (Select one, Optional): How would you rate your access to technology? Select the option that best describes your situation:
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### What technology do students need
* **What technology do students need** (Select multiple, Optional): What technology do you think your peers or fellow students need? Select all options that apply to you:
	+ Hardware
	+ Software
	+ Other (please specify)
