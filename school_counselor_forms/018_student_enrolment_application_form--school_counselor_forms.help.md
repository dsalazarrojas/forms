# Student Enrolment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students and their parents/guardians for the student enrollment process. Please fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name, parent's name, and contact information (email and phone numbers) in the respective fields.
2. Provide the student's date of birth and class they are enrolling in.
3. Select the student's class from the options provided.
4. Sign and date the form with the parent's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required: false): Enter the parent's or guardian's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student's guardian (if different from the parent).
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the guardian's phone number (if applicable).
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, required: false): Enter the guardian's email address (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, select_one, required: false): Select the student's class from the options provided:
	+ First
	+ Second
	+ Third
	+ Fourth
	+ Fifth
	+ Sixth
	+ Seventh
	+ Eighth
	+ Ninth
	+ Tenth
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required: false): A parent or guardian must sign and date this form, indicating their agreement for the student's enrollment.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely before submission to avoid delays in the enrollment process. If any required fields are left blank, please revisit and complete them as soon as possible.
