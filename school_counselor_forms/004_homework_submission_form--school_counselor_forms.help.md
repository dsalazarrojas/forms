# homework submission form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The homework submission form is used to record and track student submissions for homework assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's name in the field labeled "Student Name".
2. Select the date of submission in the field labeled "Submission Date".
3. If applicable, enter the grade in the field labeled "Grade (if applicable)".
4. Enter the assignment title in the field labeled "Assignment Name".
5. Select the due date of the assignment in the field labeled "Assignment Due Date".
6. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes in the field labeled "Submission Comments" or "Assignment Notes".
7. Enter your name in the field labeled "Submitter Name".
8. Optionally, add your phone number in the field labeled "Submitter Phone".
9. Optionally, add your email in the field labeled "Submitter Email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date of submission.
* **Grade (if applicable)**: Enter the grade if applicable.
* **Assignment Name**: Enter the assignment title.
* **Assignment Due Date**: Select the due date of the assignment.
* **Submission Comments**: Enter any comments about the submission.
* **Assignment Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the assignment.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email.
* **Assignment Type**: Select the type of assignment, whether it is true or false.
* **Notes (if applicable)**: Enter any additional notes.
