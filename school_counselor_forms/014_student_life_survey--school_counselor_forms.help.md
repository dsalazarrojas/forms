# Student Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Life Survey" is a tool for gathering student feedback and experiences on various aspects of student life, aiming to improve the learning environment and student support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
4. Check your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Experience
* **Student Experience** (`student_experience`, text, required: false): Share your experiences about your student life, such as academic, social, or personal events.

### Academic Support
* **Academic Support** (`academic_support`, text, required: false): Tell us about the support services you received or needed to achieve academic success.

### Campus Life
* **Campus Life** (`campus_life`, text, required: false): Describe your experiences and involvement in campus activities, clubs, or events.
