# Course Transfer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Transfer Application Form is a document that helps school counselors and administrators manage the process of transferring students to another course. It collects information from students or parents about the transfer request, allowing the school to process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter student information, such as name, contact number, and email.
2. Provide transfer information, including the reason for transfer and the course details.
3. Sign the form as a counselor, indicating that you are aware of and agree to the transfer process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter the student's contact number for future reference.
* **Email** (`Email`, text, required): Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.

### Transfer Information
* **Reason for Transfer** (`Reason for Transfer`, text, required): Describe the reason why the student is requesting a transfer.
* **From Course/Campus** (`From Course/Campus`, text, required): Enter the course or campus where the student is currently enrolled.
* **To Course/Campus** (`To Course/Campus`, text, required): Enter the course or campus where the student is requesting to be transferred to.

### Counselor Signature
* **Counselor Signature** (`Counselor Signature`, note, required): As a school counselor, sign below to confirm that you are aware of and agree to the transfer process.
