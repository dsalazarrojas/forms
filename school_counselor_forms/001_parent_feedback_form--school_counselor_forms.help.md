# Parent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Feedback Form is a tool to collect feedback from parents on how the school or educational institution can improve. It's used to gather suggestions and comments on how to enhance the overall experience for parents and their children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and title in the **Parent Name** field. This is the name of the parent who is submitting the feedback.
2. Share your comments and suggestions with us in the **Feedback** field. This field is a text area where you can type in any comments, suggestions, or ideas you have for improvement.
3. If you have any personal feedback for the school or educational institution, you can type it in the **Parent Feedback** field. This field is optional and is where you can share any personal experiences or concerns you may have.
4. Enter the name of the school or educational institution in the **School Name** field. This is the name of the school or educational institution you are providing feedback for.
5. Enter the name of the student in the **Student Name** field. This is the name of the student who is associated with the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name (Parent Name)** (`text`, required): Enter your name and title in this field.
* **Feedback (Feedback)** (`text`, required): Share your comments and suggestions with us in this field.
* **Personal Feedback (Parent Feedback)** (`text`, optional): If you have any personal feedback for the school or educational institution, type it in this field.
* **School Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the school or educational institution you are providing feedback for.
* **Student Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the student who is associated with the feedback.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please let us know in the **Personal Feedback** field.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask our support team for help.
