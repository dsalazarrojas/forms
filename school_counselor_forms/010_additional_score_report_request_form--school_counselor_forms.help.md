# Additional Score Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Additional Score Report Request Form is used to request additional score reports for students from school counselors and administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form**: Provide all required information in the designated fields.
2. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed the form, submit it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Additional Score Report --**: A header note for the form, indicating that this is an additional score report request.
* **Student Full Name**: Enter the student's full name as listed on their official documents.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter the student's official identification number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's birth date.
* **Requester Name**: Enter your name as the person requesting the score report.
* **Relationship to Student**: Select how you are related to the student (e.g., Student, Parent/Guardian, Educator, etc.).
* **Requester Email**: Enter a valid email address for contacting you about the score report.
* **Requester Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of your institution (school, college, etc.) (optional).
* **Type of Test**: Select the type of test or assessment for which you are requesting scores (e.g., SAT, ACT, etc.).
* **Date of Test Administration**: Enter the date the test was taken.
* **Subject Area**: Select the subject area of the test (e.g., Mathematics, Reading, etc.) (optional).
* **Number of Reports Needed**: Enter the number of score reports you require.
* **Delivery Method**: Select how you would like to receive the score report (e.g., Email, Mail, etc.).
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address (if you selected Mail as the delivery method).
* **Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level for delivering the score report (e.g., Standard, Rush, etc.).
* **Purpose of Request**: Enter the reason you are requesting the score report.
* **Previous Requests**: Indicate if you have previously requested scores for this test.
* **Fee Payment Method**: Select how you will pay for the fees (if applicable).
* **Authorization Signature**: Enter your signature authorizing the release of scores.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the authorization signature.
* **Counselor Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the school counselor (optional).
