# Return To School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Return To School Survey form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's attendance to school. The form helps school administrators track attendance patterns and communicate effectively with families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Use the provided options or text fields to provide the required information.
4. Make sure to save your changes before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Has child attended school today** (has_child_attended_school): This field asks whether your child attended school today. Select "Yes" if they did attend, and "No" if they did not.
* **Has child attended school yesterday** (has_child_attended_school_yesterday): This field asks if your child attended school yesterday. Choose "Yes" if they did attend, and "No" if they did not.

* **Will child attend** (will_child_attend): This field is similar to "Will child attend tomorrow". It's also for planning purposes and has similar options.
* **Reason for absence** (reason_for_absence): Provide a clear and concise reason for any absence in this text field. This helps the school understand the reason for the absence.
* **Reason for virtual absence** (reason_for_virtual_absence): If your child is attending virtually, explain the reason for the virtual absence here.
* **Comments** (comments): If you have any comments or concerns, use this text field to share them with the school.
* **Parent signature** (parent_signature): Sign and date this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **School name** (school_name): Provide the name of your child's school.
* **Contact number** (contact_number): Enter your contact number so the school can reach out to you.
