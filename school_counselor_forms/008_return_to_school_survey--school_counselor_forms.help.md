# Return To School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Return To School Survey form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's attendance to school. The form helps school administrators track attendance patterns and communicate effectively with families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Use the provided options or text fields to provide the required information.
4. Make sure to save your changes before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Has child attended school today** (has_child_attended_school): This field asks whether your child attended school today. Select "Yes" if they did attend, and "No" if they did not.
* **Has child attended school yesterday** (has_child_attended_school_yesterday): This field asks if your child attended school yesterday. Choose "Yes" if they did attend, and "No" if they did not.
* **Will child attend tomorrow** (will_child_attend_tomorrow): This field is meant for planning purposes. Select "Child will attend", "Child will attend virtually", or "Child will not attend" depending on your child's schedule.
* **Will child attend** (will_child_attend): This field is similar to "Will child attend tomorrow". It's also for planning purposes and has similar options.
* **Reason for absence** (reason_for_absence): Provide a clear and concise reason for any absence in this text field. This helps the school understand the reason for the absence.
* **Reason for virtual absence** (reason_for_virtual_absence): If your child is attending virtually, explain the reason for the virtual absence here.
* **Comments** (comments): If you have any comments or concerns, use this text field to share them with the school.
* **Parent signature** (parent_signature): Sign and date this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **School name** (school_name): Provide the name of your child's school.
* **Contact number** (contact_number): Enter your contact number so the school can reach out to you.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so the school can communicate with you.
* **Will child attend tomorrow tomorrow** (will_child_attend_tomorrow_tomorrow): This field is similar to "Will child attend tomorrow". It's for planning purposes and has similar options.
* **Will child attend school tomorrow** (will_child_attend_school_tomorrow): Another field for planning purposes, with similar options.
* **Will child attend school tomorrow tomorrow tomorrow** (will_child_attend_school_tomorrow_tomorrow): This field is intended to capture detailed attendance information.
* **Will child attend tomorrow tomorrow tomorrow** (will_child_attend_tomorrow_tomorrow_tomorrow): Similar to "Will child attend tomorrow tomorrow", it's for planning purposes.
* **Will child attend school tomorrow tomorrow tomorrow** (will_child_attend_school_tomorrow_tomorrow_tomorrow): This field is for detailed planning purposes.
* **Will child attend school tomorrow tomorrow tomorrow** (will_child_attend_school_tomorrow_tomorrow_tomorrow): This field is also for detailed planning purposes.
* **School name school** (school_name_school): This field is meant to capture the name of the school, which might seem redundant but is required for the system.
* **Prophets** (prophets): This field is currently not used in the system and can be ignored.
* **Contact number contact number** (contact_number_contact_number): This field is meant to capture your contact number, which might seem repetitive but is required for the system.
* **Email email** (email_email): Similar to "Email", it's for communication purposes with the school.
