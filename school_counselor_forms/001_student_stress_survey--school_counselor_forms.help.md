# Student Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Stress Survey" form is designed to collect information on students' current stress levels, stressors, and support needs. This data will help identify areas of concern and provide a foundation for support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please go through each page and fill out the relevant fields. The form is divided into several pages, each with fields for specific information. Please select or type in the required fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **What's your current stress level** (`form_page_1`, select multiple options, required): Select the multiple options that best describe your current stress level. These options include High, Low, and other intermediate levels.
*   **Stress score** (`stress_score`, number, required): Enter a numerical value indicating your current stress level.
*   **School workload** (`school_workload`, text, required): Describe your current school workload, including coursework, homework, and other academic responsibilities.
*   **Academic pressure** (`academic_pressure`, text, required): Describe your current academic pressure or expectations.
*   **Relationships** (`relationships`, text, required): Describe your relationships with family, friends, or peers that may be affecting your stress level.
*   **Family** (`family`, text, required): Describe your current family life or any family-related stressors.
*   **Self-care** (`self_care`, select one option, required): Select one option that best describes your self-care habits.
*   **School support** (`school_support`, select multiple options, required): Select multiple options that describe your current school support needs.
*   **Other stressors** (`other_stressors`, text, required): Describe any additional stressors not covered by other fields.
*   **Submit form** (`submit_form`, text, required): Confirm your form submission.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact information.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact information.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or descriptions.
*   **Form page 2-13** (`form_page_2`, `form_page_3`, ..., `form_page_13`, text, optional): These fields are for additional comments or descriptions.
