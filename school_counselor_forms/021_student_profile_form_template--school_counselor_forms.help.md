# Student Profile Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a student's profile. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's First Name in the text field provided.
2. Enter the student's Last Name in the text field provided.
3. Enter the student's Date of Birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter the student's Student ID, which is a unique identifier for the student.
5. Enter the student's Email address.
6. Enter the student's Contact Number.
7. Enter the emergency contact's name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the student's first name, as per their official documentation.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the student's last name, as per their official documentation.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's unique ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the student's contact number, e.g., phone or mobile.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name, address, and contact information.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the data entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need assistance with the form, please contact the support team.

Note that the form is designed to be flexible, allowing users to fill it out in various settings.
