## School Fee Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Fee Payment Form is used to record payments made by students to the school. This includes any supplies or swags purchased during the payment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information and fill in the required fields.
2. Make sure to choose the correct payment method and date of payment.
3. Indicate the items purchased, such as supplies or swags.
4. If ordering books, please fill in the required information.
5. Add any comments about the payment or swags.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student** (`Student`, text, false): Enter the student's name and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, true): Choose the method used for the payment, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Online Transfer.
* **Fee Amount Paid** (`fee_amount`, number, true): Enter the exact amount paid.
* **Fee Paid Date** (`payment_date`, date, true): Select the date of payment.
* **Supplies** (`supplies_purchased`, select_multiple, true): Choose the supplies purchased, such as Pencils, Pens, or Paper.
* **Swags** (`swags_purchased`, select_multiple, true): Select the swags purchased, such as Notebooks or Folders.
* **Book Order Form** (`book_order`, text, false): If ordering books, fill in this section with the required information.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, false): Add any comments about the payment or swags.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Choose the correct payment method and date of payment.
* Be accurate when selecting the supplies or swags purchased.
* If ordering books, make sure to fill in all required information.
* Add any comments that may be useful for the school.
