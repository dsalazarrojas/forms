# Enrollment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by school counselors to verify student enrollment information for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant school year from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or more of the available options.
2. Enter the student's ID number.
3. Enter the student's first name.
4. Enter the student's last name.
5. Enter the student's date of birth.
6. Select the student's grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
7. Enter the school name where the student is enrolled.
8. Enter the school address.
9. Enter the student's phone number.
10. Enter the student's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_counselor_form_1** (Select Multiple, required: false): Select the school year for which you want to verify enrollment information. This field is a dropdown menu with options 1, 2, 3, and 4.
* **student_id** (Text, required: false): Enter the student's unique ID number.
* **student_first_name** (Text, required: false): Enter the student's first name.
* **student_last_name** (Text, required: false): Enter the student's last name.
* **student_dob** (Date, required: false): Enter the student's date of birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **student_grade_level** (Select One, required: false): Select the student's grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* **school_name** (Text, required: false): Enter the school's name where the student is enrolled.
* **school_address** (Text, required: false): Enter the school's address.
* **student_phone_number** (Text, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **student_email** (Email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and access rights to complete this form.
* Double-check the form fields for accurate information before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, contact your supervisor or IT department for assistance.
