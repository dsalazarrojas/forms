# Math Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math Student Registration Form is designed to collect information from math students to help the school administration and math department provide necessary support and resources for their math courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Student Information section with your details.
2. Provide your Parent Information, if applicable.
3. Enter your Contact Information.
4. Select the Math Course you want to register for.
5. Choose the start and end dates of your course.
6. Enter the time of day when you plan to attend your course.
7. Write any additional Note, if needed.
8. If you have a preferred Email address, please enter it.
9. If you have a Phone number, please enter it.
10. Finally, select your Math Course Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student name, student ID, or any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, `text`, required: false): If you are not an adult student, you can enter your parent's name, contact information, or other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address, phone number, or any other contact information that you would like to share with us.
* **Select Course** (`course_selection`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the math course you want to register for from the list of options provided.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date when you plan to start your math course.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date when you plan to end your math course.
* **Time Of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when you plan to attend your math course.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional information or notes about your course, you can write them here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your preferred email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Math Course** (`math_course`, `number`, required: false): Select your math course number, as provided by the math department.
