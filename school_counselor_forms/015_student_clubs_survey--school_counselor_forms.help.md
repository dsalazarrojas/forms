# Student Clubs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about student clubs at the school, including details about their name, meeting schedule, advisor information, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Review each page of the form carefully.
3. Use the options provided in each field to ensure you select the most relevant answer.
4. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **club name** (`school_club_name`, text, required): This is the name of the student club.
* **meeting days** (`meeting_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the days on which the club meets.
* **meeting time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the club's meetings.
* **meeting location** (`meeting_location`, select_one, optional): Select the location where the club meets.
* **club purpose** (`club_purpose`, text, optional): Describe the purpose of the club.
* **student count** (`student_count`, number, optional): Enter the estimated number of students in the club.
* **advisor name** (`advisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the club advisor.
* **advisor email** (`advisor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the club advisor.
* **advisor phone** (`advisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the club advisor.
* **club status** (`club_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the club (Active or Inactive).
* **reason inactive** (`reason_inactive`, text, optional): If the club is inactive, provide a reason for its inactivity.
* **club sponsor** (`club_sponsor`, select_one, optional): Select the sponsor of the club (School or Student).
* **club type** (`club_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of club (School Club or Student Organization).
* **club formation** (`club_formation`, date, optional): Enter the date the club was formed.
* **club founded** (`club_founded`, date, optional): Enter the date the club was founded.
* **club formation date** (`club_formation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the club was formed.
* **club founding member** (`club_founding_member`, text, optional): Enter the name of the founding member of the club.
* **club sponsorship** (`club_sponsorship`, select_one, optional): Select the type of sponsorship the club has (Self-funded or School-funded).
* **club advisor** (`club_advisor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the club advisor.
* **club sponsor justification** (`club_sponsorship_justification`, text, optional): Enter the justification for the club sponsor.
* **club termination** (`club_termination`, select_one, optional): Select the termination status of the club (Active or Inactive).
* **reason termination** (`reason_termination`, text, optional): If the club is terminated, provide a reason for its termination.
* **club formation year** (`club_formation_year`, date, optional): Enter the year the club was formed.
* **club status date** (`club_status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the club's status was last updated.
* **club notes** (`club_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the club.

## Tips
* Please double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, refer to this help guide.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the relevant authority at your school.
