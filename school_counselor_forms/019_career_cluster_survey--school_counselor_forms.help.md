# Career Cluster Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Career Cluster Survey form helps you identify your career interests and preferences by asking you to rate your level of interest in various career clusters. It's an essential step in exploring your potential career paths and understanding your strengths, weaknesses, and areas of interest.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Choose your level of interest for each cluster:
	* Very interested
	* Somewhat interested
	* Neutral
	* Not very interested
	* Not interested at all
3. Select all relevant career options that sound interesting to you for each cluster.
4. List your top three career clusters at the end of the survey.
