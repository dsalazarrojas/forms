# Preschool Student Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Preschool Student Skills Assessment Form is designed to evaluate a preschool student's skills in areas such as pre-reading abilities, social interaction, and showing attention and interest in activities. This form helps teachers and caregivers to assess a student's progress and development in these critical areas.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided text fields.
2. Be as detailed and specific as possible when providing information.
3. If a student has a particular skill or difficulty, describe it in detail.
4. If a student does not possess a particular skill or has difficulty with it, please mark it as "None" or "Not yet".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is the student's ability to engage in pre-reading activities alone?**
	+ A text field where you can describe the student's ability to read and engage in activities before actually beginning a lesson or activity.
* **2. How well can the student interact socially with their peers?**
	+ A text field where you can describe the student's ability to interact and connect with their peers.
* **3. How does the student show attention and interest in activities?**
	+ A text field where you can describe the student's behavior and engagement during activities, such as staying focused, showing curiosity, or participating enthusiastically.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough when assessing the student's skills.
* Provide examples or anecdotes to support your observations.
* If you're unsure about a particular skill or behavior, try to provide context or describe the situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or school staff.
