# Emergency Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Permission Form is a critical document that helps the school to contact the student's emergency contact in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and the information required.
2. Fill in the student's name and your name as their emergency contact.
3. Provide your contact information, including your phone number.
4. Check the box indicating you grant permission for the school to contact you in case of an emergency.
5. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **emergency_permission_form_1** (`emergency_permission_form_1`, text, required: false): This field is the main section of the form where you can enter any emergency information or instructions that the school needs to know.
* **guardian_name_1** (`guardian_name_1`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as the student's emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_1** (`emergency_contact_1`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person you are contacting in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_phone_1** (`emergency_contact_phone_1`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so the school can contact you quickly and efficiently.
* **emergency_permission_1** (`emergency_permission_1`, text, required: false): This field is intended for you to indicate any special instructions or permissions you want to grant to the school in case of an emergency.
* **signature_1** (`signature_1`, note, required: false): Sign the form to confirm your agreement and consent.

## Tips
- Fill in all the required fields carefully and accurately.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all the information is correct.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact the school administration.
- Make sure to keep a copy of the form for your records.
