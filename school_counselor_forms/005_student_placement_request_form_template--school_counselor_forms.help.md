# Student Placement Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Placement Request Form Template is a tool used to collect information related to student placement requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the **student_name** field.
2. Select the student's grade level from the **grade_level** drop-down menu.
3. Choose the subject area(s) the student is interested in being placed in from the **subject_area** field.
4. Enter the date of the placement request in the **placement_request_date** field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
5. If applicable, enter the school name in the **school_name** field.
6. Enter the school address in the **school_address** field.
7. Enter the student's email address in the **email** field.
8. Enter the student's phone number in the **phone** field.
9. Enter the name of the placement officer in the **placement_officer** field.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes about the student's placement request in the **additional_comments** field.
11. If necessary, enter the student's ID number in the **student_id** field.
12. Enter the name of the teacher responsible for the student's placement request in the **teacher_name** field.
13. Enter the name of the department the student's placement request is for in the **department_name** field.
14. Enter the name of the course the student is being placed for in the **course_name** field.
15. Enter notes about the student's placement request in the **placement_notes** field.
16. Enter the teacher's phone number in the **teacher_phone** field.
17. Enter the student's phone number in the **student_phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name.
*   **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level.
*   **Subject Area**: Select the subject area(s) the student is interested in being placed in.
*   **Placement Request Date**: Enter the date of the placement request in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the student is requesting placement for.
*   **School Address**: Enter the address of the school the student is requesting placement for.
*   **Email**: Enter the student's email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
*   **Placement Officer**: Enter the name of the placement officer responsible for the student's placement.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the student's placement request.
*   **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number if necessary.
*   **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher responsible for the student's placement request.
*   **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department the student's placement request is for.
*   **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course the student is being placed for.
*   **Placement Notes**: Enter notes about the student's placement request.
*   **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
*   **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
