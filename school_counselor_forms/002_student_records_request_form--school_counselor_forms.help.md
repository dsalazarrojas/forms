# Student Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Records Request Form is used by school counselors to collect and maintain records about students. This form helps to track student performance, progress, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to fill out this form.
2. Enter the student's ID (if applicable).
3. Provide the student's name.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about the student's performance.
5. Enter the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): This field is for entering the student's identification number.
* If the student has a unique identifier, please enter it here.
* If you do not know the student's ID, you can proceed without it, but please provide the student's name and other details instead.

### **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): This field is for entering the student's name.
* Enter the student's full name, including any relevant initials or nicknames.
* This field is optional, but please provide accurate and consistent information for future reference.

### **Exam Results** (`exam_results`, note, required: false): This field is for adding any exam-related notes or comments.
* Enter any relevant information about the student's exam performance.
* This field is optional and will help provide context for the student's records.

### **Homework Results** (`homework_results`, note, required: false): This field is for adding any homework-related notes or comments.
* Enter any relevant information about the student's homework performance.
* This field is optional and will help provide context for the student's records.

### **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes about the student.
* Enter any relevant information about the student's performance, progress, or behavior.
* This field is optional and will help provide context for the student's records.

### **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, number, required: false): This field is for recording the ID of the person submitting this form.
* This field is typically used by the school's system to track who submitted the form.
* If you're unsure, you can usually find this information in the system's records.

### **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required: false): This field is for recording the date and time of submission.
* Enter the exact date and time you submit this form.
* This field is used by the system to track when the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and consistently.
* If you're unsure about any information, please consult with the relevant authorities.
* This form is for authorized and legitimate purposes only.
