# University Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The University Application Form is designed to collect essential information from students applying to a university or college. This form helps the admissions team gather details about a student's background, education, and activities, which can be used to evaluate their eligibility and suitability for admission.

## How to Complete This Form

1.  **Personal Information (Page 1)**: This section is used to collect your basic details.
    * **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill out this section with your name, address, and any other relevant personal details.
2.  **Education Background (Page 2)**: This section gathers information about your previous educational experiences.
    * **Education Background** (`education_background`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your previous educational history, including any degrees, diplomas, or certifications you have earned.
3.  **SAT Information (Page 3)**: This section is used to collect information about your SAT score.
    * **SAT Information** (`sat_information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your SAT score, or explain why you don't have one (e.g., because you took the ACT instead).
4.  **Activity Information (Page 4)**: This section is used to gather information about your extracurricular activities.
    * **Activity Information** (`activity_information`, `text`, required/optional): List any notable activities, hobbies, or interests that demonstrate your skills and achievements.
5.  **Tips**: Please ensure that you complete this form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Information**: Please fill out this section with your name, address, and any other relevant personal details.
*   **Education Background**: Describe your previous educational history, including any degrees, diplomas, or certifications you have earned.
*   **SAT Information**: Enter your SAT score, or explain why you don’t have one (e.g., because you took the ACT instead).
*   **Activity Information**: List any notable activities, hobbies, or interests that demonstrate your skills and achievements.
