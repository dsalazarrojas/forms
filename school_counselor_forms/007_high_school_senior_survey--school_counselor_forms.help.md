# High School Senior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help high school seniors complete the High School Senior Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form online or print it for completion in paper format.
*   Answer each question according to your experiences and plans.
*   Select the relevant options for each multiple-choice question.
*   Submit the form for review and analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Graduation Plans
*   **Graduation plans** (`graduation_plans`, text, optional): Describe your plans after graduation, such as college, vocational training, or the workforce.

### School Performance
*   **School performance** (`school_performance`, text, optional): Rate your performance in school, using numbers or words to explain your strengths and weaknesses.

### Extracurricular Activities
*   **Extracurricular activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, text, optional): List any extracurricular activities you have participated in, such as sports, music, or clubs.

### School Counselor Support
*   **School counselor support** (`school_counselor_support`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the support options provided by your school counselor, such as college applications, career guidance, or personal issues.

### Teacher Support
*   **Teacher support** (`teacher_support`, select_one, optional): Choose the support option that best fits the help you've received from your teachers, such as advice, resources, or encouragement.

### Student Support Services
*   **Student support services** (`student_support_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the support services you've used, such as tutoring, mentoring, or counseling.

### Student Organizations and Clubs
*   **Student organizations and clubs** (`student_organization_clubs`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the student organizations and clubs you've participated in, such as sports teams, debate team, or school choir.

### College and Career Plans
*   **College and career plans** (`college_career_plans`, text, optional): Describe your short-term and long-term career goals, such as major, industry, or career path.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
*   If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your school counselor for guidance.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, contact your school counselor or the form administrators.
