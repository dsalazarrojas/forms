# College Counselor Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for college counselors to provide their professional recommendation about a student's academic and personal qualifications. Please provide accurate and honest information to help the student's application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and title as the college counselor.
2.  Enter the name of the school or institution where you work.
3.  Provide your email address and phone number.
4.  Enter the student's first name and last name.
5.  Specify how long you have known the student (less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-3 years, or 3+ years).
6.  Describe the context of your relationship with the student (e.g., academic advisor, dorm counselor, etc.).
7.  Assess the student's academic performance (Exceptional, Strong, Satisfactory, Developing).
8.  Evaluate the student's intellectual curiosity and engagement (Outstanding, Very Good, Good, Adequate).
9.  List the student's key personal strengths.
10. Assess the student's leadership potential (Exceptional, Strong, Developing, Limited).
11. Provide specific examples of the student's leadership skills.
12. Describe the student's extracurricular involvement (clubs, sports, volunteer work, etc.).
13. Evaluate the student's interpersonal and social skills (Exceptional, Strong, Good, Needs Development).
14. Assess the student's resilience and adaptability (Excellent, Good, Adequate, Needs Support).
15. Identify areas where the student could improve.
16. Provide an overall recommendation (Enthusiastically Recommend, Recommend, Recommend with Reservations, Cannot Recommend).
17. Summarize your recommendation and key points in the text box.
18. Enter the date of your recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Counselor Name**: Please enter your name as the college counselor.
* **Counselor Title**: Enter your title as the college counselor (e.g., school counselor, college advisor, etc.).
* **School or Institution Name**: Enter the name of the school or institution where you work.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Student First Name**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Student Last Name**: Enter the student's last name.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
* **How long have you known this student?**: Choose the time frame for how long you have known the student (less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-3 years, or 3+ years).
* **Context of Your Relationship with Student**: Describe the context of your relationship with the student (e.g., academic advisor, dorm counselor, etc.).
* **Student Academic Performance**: Assess the student's academic performance (Exceptional, Strong, Satisfactory, Developing).
* **Intellectual Curiosity and Engagement**: Evaluate the student's intellectual curiosity and engagement (Outstanding, Very Good, Good, Adequate).
* **Key Personal Strengths**: Describe the student's key personal strengths.
* **Leadership Potential**: Assess the student's leadership potential (Exceptional, Strong, Developing, Limited).
* **Leadership Examples**: Provide specific examples of the student's leadership skills.
* **Extracurricular Involvement**: Describe the student's extracurricular involvement (clubs, sports, volunteer work, etc.).
* **Interpersonal and Social Skills**: Evaluate the student's interpersonal and social skills (Exceptional, Strong, Good, Needs Development).
* **Resilience and Adaptability**: Assess the student's resilience and adaptability (Excellent, Good, Adequate, Needs Support).
* **Areas for Growth or Development**: Identify areas where the student could improve.
* **Overall Recommendation**: Provide an overall recommendation (Enthusiastically Recommend, Recommend, Recommend with Reservations, Cannot Recommend).
* **Additional Recommendation Statement**: Summarize your recommendation and key points.
* **Date of Recommendation**: Enter the date of your recommendation.
