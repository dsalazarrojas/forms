## Purpose
This help guide is intended to provide an overview of the Request For Student And School Resources Funding Form, its purpose, and the steps to complete it. Before proceeding, please ensure that you are familiar with the form's purpose and that you are not duplicating existing efforts.

The form is designed to capture information from students and schools about their financial situations, school funding needs, and resource allocation requirements. It serves as a means to gather data that can inform decision-making processes related to school resources and funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's sections and fields carefully.
2. For each section, provide the relevant information based on your specific situation.
3. Be accurate and honest in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Enrollment** (Page 1, Field 1): Enter your school enrollment number or ID.
* **Financial Situation** (Page 1, Field 2): Describe your current financial situation, including any relevant details about your income, expenses, or debt.
* **Student Resources** (Page 1, Field 3): Select all the resources you require from the list, such as computers, library materials, or extracurricular activities.
* **School Funding** (Page 1, Field 4): Indicate whether your school has sufficient funding for its needs.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 1, Field 5): Add any additional information or context that may be helpful for the administrators to understand your situation.
* **Category** (Page 1, Field 6): Select the category that best describes your request, such as "Yes" for resources and "No" for funding.
* **School Financial Resources** (Page 1, Field 7): Enter the amount of financial resources available at your school.
* **Student Resources Allocation** (Page 1, Field 8): Select which resources you would like to allocate for your students.
* **School Funding Status** (Page 1, Field 9): Indicate the status of your school's funding, such as "Active" for funded and "Inactive" for unfunded.
* **Date** (Page 1, Field 10): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time** (Page 1, Field 11): Enter the time you are submitting this form.

## Tips
* Be thorough and detailed in your responses to ensure that your request is understood correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field or section, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
* This form is intended to gather information only, and responses will not affect your school's or student's status directly.
