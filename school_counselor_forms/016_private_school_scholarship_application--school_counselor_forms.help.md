# private_school_scholarship_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The private_school_scholarship_application form is a tool for students to gather and submit their information for private school scholarships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the required information in the fields provided.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Review your application before submission to ensure it is complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the school you currently attend or the school you plan to attend. This will help the scholarship provider understand your academic background.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the student applying, such as their current grade level or academic achievements.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the student's parents or guardians, such as their contact information or occupation.
* **Student Contact** (`student_contact`, text, optional): Enter the student's contact information, such as their phone number or email address.
* **Student Identification** (`student_identification`, text, optional): Provide any relevant identification information about the student, such as their student ID number or social security number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's address, including street number and postal code.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your application before submission to ensure it is complete and accurate.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to your application or provide a link to where they can be accessed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the scholarship provider directly.
