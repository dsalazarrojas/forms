# Parent Portal Account Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or access a parent portal account. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your account is properly configured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the type of account you need to create or access (School Counselor Forms or Other).
2. Fill in your parent portal password, account type, name, email, school email, contact number, emergency contact number, emergency contact name, and emergency contact relationship fields accurately.
3. Provide a brief description of your account purpose (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the category that best describes the type of account you need to create or access. Selecting "School Counselor Forms" is recommended for parents and guardians who are creating or accessing accounts for their child.
* **Parent Portal Password** (`parent_portal_password`, `text`, required: false): Enter your parent portal password. This will be used to access your account. Please ensure that you enter the correct password to avoid any issues with account access.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you are a Parent or Guardian.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it should be displayed on your account.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address associated with your school (if applicable).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number (mobile or landline).
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact number for your child (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact person and your child (if applicable).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your account purpose (if required).
