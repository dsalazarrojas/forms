# Sex Education Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This sex education survey is designed to collect feedback from students, parents, and teachers on various aspects of sex education, including topics covered, quality of teaching, and overall satisfaction. Your input will help us assess our sex education program and make it more effective.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each question, choose the option that best answers your response.
3. If you have additional comments or feedback, please use the "Other" or "Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sex_education_survey** (text): This is the title of the sex education survey.
* **school_name** (text): Please enter the name of your school.
* **teacher_name** (text): Please enter the name of your teacher.
* **parent_name** (text): Please enter your parent's name (if applicable).
* **student_name** (text): Please enter your name (if you are the student filling this out).
* **student_grade** (number): Please enter your grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
* **sex_education_topics** (select multiple):
	+ Basic human anatomy
	+ Reproductive health
	+ Safety and consent
	+ Sex and relationships
* **other_topics** (text): If you have other topics that you would like to include, please type them here.
* **other_topics_other** (text): This is an optional field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **teacher_feedback** (select one):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **student_feedback** (select multiple):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **sex_education_survey_comments** (note): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **parent_feedback** (select one):
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **school_comments** (note): This is an optional field for any additional comments or feedback about the school.
* **teacher_comments** (note): This is an optional field for any additional comments or feedback about the teacher.
* **parent_comments** (note): This is an optional field for any additional comments or feedback about the parent.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve our sex education program.
