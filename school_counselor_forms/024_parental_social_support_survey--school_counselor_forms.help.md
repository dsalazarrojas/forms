# Parental Social Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Social Support Survey is designed to collect information about the parental social support available to a student. This form is intended to be completed by school administrators or counselors to assess the level of support provided by parents or guardians to their children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the student's current level of parental support.
2. Answer each question based on the student's current support level.
3. Indicate the student's response for each field.
4. Submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental Attentiveness** (`parent_attentiveness`, `number`, required: `false`):
This field assesses the level of attentiveness provided by the parent or guardian, such as paying attention to the student's needs, being available to provide care and support.
* **Parental Involvement** (`parent_involvement`, `number`, required: `false`):
This field measures the level of involvement of the parent or guardian in the student's life, such as attending school events, participating in activities, and engaging in conversations.
* **Parental Support** (`parent_support`, `number`, required: `false`):
This field evaluates the level of emotional and practical support provided by the parent or guardian, such as providing emotional support, helping with schoolwork, and offering guidance.

## Tips
Please provide honest and accurate responses based on the student's current level of parental support. If you are unsure about a particular field, please use your best judgment and consult with your supervisor or administrator if necessary.
