# Spirit Wear Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spirit Wear Order Form is designed to collect information about orders for spirit wear, a type of merchandise that shows school spirit or school pride. School counselors use this form to place orders for spirit wear for events, games, or other school activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and which fields apply to your specific situation.
2. Select the spirit wear you want to order for each category, such as for events, games, or other activities.
3. Enter your desired quantity and date for each selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Do you want to order spirit wear for events? **Yes/No**
  * This question determines whether or not you want to order spirit wear for events. Select "Yes" if you would like to place an order for spirit wear for events.
  * This field is optional.

### 2. Do you want to order spirit wear for games? **Yes/No**
  * This question determines whether or not you want to order spirit wear for games. Select "Yes" if you would like to place an order for spirit wear for games.
  * This field is optional.

### 3. Enter a note about your order
  * This field is for any additional information or notes about your order.
  * This field is optional.

### 4. Enter a date for your order
  * This field is for the date you would like to place your order.
  * This field is optional.

### 5. Enter a time for your order
  * This field is for the time you would like to place your order.
  * This field is optional.

### 6. Select a number for your order
  * This field is for any numerical information related to your order.
  * This field is optional.

### 7. Enter an email address for your order
  * This field is for any email address related to your order.
  * This field is optional.

### 8. Enter a text for your order
  * This field is for any additional text information related to your order.
  * This field is optional.

### 9-25. These fields are for any additional text information related to your order.
  * These fields are optional.

## Tips

* Please make sure to review your order carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate.
* If you have any questions or need help with your order, contact the school counselor's office.
* This form is for spirit wear orders only. If you have any other questions or requests, please use a different form.
