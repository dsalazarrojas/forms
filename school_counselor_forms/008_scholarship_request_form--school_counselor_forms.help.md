# Scholarship Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Request Form is a document used to collect information from students who are applying for a scholarship to support their educational goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your student name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for additional information if needed.
3. Provide your address, which will be used as a point of contact for correspondence.
4. In the "Career Goals" field, describe your educational or career aspirations, such as your desired degree, major, or professional path.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you about your application.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Address** (4, text, required): Enter your home address or any other address where we can contact you.
* **Career Goals** (5, text, required): Explain your educational or career goals, such as pursuing a degree in a specific field or pursuing a specific job opportunity. 

## Tips

* Please make sure to submit the form only once to avoid duplication of effort and potential delays in processing your request.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely for a smooth and efficient scholarship process.
