# Student Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about students' interests, preferences, and expectations from their school year. This data will help school administrators and counselors understand students' needs and provide support, resources, or accommodations accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions that allow multiple selections, choose all the options that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much information as you need to clearly express your thoughts and feelings.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional comments or questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What activities you enjoy (`student_interest_survey`)**: This question is asking about your preferred activities or hobbies outside of school. Please provide one or more answers that you enjoy doing in your free time.
* **What expectations you have from your school year (`student_expectation`)**: This question is asking about your hopes or expectations from your school year. Be specific about what you would like to achieve or get out of your school year.
* **Favorite subjects (`favorite_subjects`)**: Select all the subjects that you are interested in or enjoy learning about. You can choose from the following options: Math, Science, English, History, Art, Music, Physical education, Foreign languages, Other (please specify).
* **What activities you enjoy (`favorite_activities`)**: This question is asking about your favorite activities or hobbies. Select all the options that apply from: Sports, Clubs, Parties, Games, Travel, Hobbies, Reading, Music, Other (please specify).
* **What classes are you interested in (`favorite_classes`)**: Select the class that you are most interested in or enjoy attending. Choose from the following options: Math class, Science class, English class, History class, Art class, Physical education class, Foreign languages class, Other class (please specify).
* **What time of day do you like to study (`favorite_time`)**: This question is asking about your study habits. Select a time of day when you prefer to study.
* **What time of day do you like to be at school (`favorite_day`)**: This question is asking about your preference for school hours. Select a time of day when you prefer to be at school.
* **Additional comments or questions (`student_interest_survey_note`)**: If you have any additional comments or questions that you would like to share, please use this field.
* **Student email (`student_email`)**: Please enter your student email address for contact purposes.
* **Student phone (`student_phone`)**: This is for your parent/guardian contact information, please enter your phone number.

Note: This form is not intended to be a comprehensive assessment, but rather a tool to gather information and provide a starting point for further conversation with school administrators and counselors.
