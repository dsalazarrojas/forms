# Anonymous Bullying Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report incidents of bullying in a safe and anonymous way. This form will gather information about the incident, the person being bullied, and the desired outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of bullying incident that occurred.
2. Choose the date and time of the incident.
3. Select the location where the incident happened.
4. Provide details about the person being bullied, including their grade level.
5. Choose your relationship to the person being bullied.
6. Describe the individuals involved in the incident.
7. Indicate if there were any witnesses present.
8. Provide witness names if known.
9. Describe what happened during the incident.
10. Indicate how often the bullying occurs.
11. Choose whether you have reported this incident before.
12. If you have previously reported an incident, provide details.
13. Indicate if you have any evidence related to the incident.
14. If you have evidence, specify its type.
15. Describe how the bullying has affected you or the person being bullied.
16. Indicate if anyone is in immediate danger.
17. Choose whether any weapons were used during the incident.
18. Describe the desired outcome for the incident.
19. Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
20. Provide an optional contact email or phone number.
21. Choose whether you would like to remain anonymous.
22. Indicate if you need support, and if so, what type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Type**: Select the type of bullying incident that occurred (Bullying, Cyberbullying, Harassment, etc.).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date when the bullying incident happened.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time when the bullying incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Select the location where the incident took place (Classroom, Hallway, etc.).
* **Location Details**: Provide additional details about the location (optional).
* **Person Being Bullied**: Enter the name and grade level of the person being bullied.
* **Your Relationship**: Choose how you know the person being bullied (I am the Victim, Friend, Classmate, etc.).
* **Persons Involved**: Describe the individuals involved in the incident.
* **Witnesses Present**: Indicate if there were any witnesses present during the incident.
* **Witness Names**: If known, enter the names of the witnesses.
* **Incident Description**: Describe what happened during the incident.
* **Frequency**: Choose how often the bullying occurs (First Time, Occasionally, Frequently, etc.).
* **Previous Reports**: Indicate if you have reported this incident before.
* **Previous Report Details**: If you have previously reported, provide details about the incident.
* **Evidence Available**: Indicate if you have any evidence related to the incident.
* **Evidence Type**: If you have evidence, specify its type (photos, screenshots, etc.).
* **Impact Description**: Describe how the bullying has affected you or the person being bullied.
* **Safety Concern**: Indicate if anyone is in immediate danger.
* **Weapons Involved**: Indicate if any weapons were used during the incident.
* **Desired Outcome**: Describe the desired outcome for the incident.
* **Contact Preference**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Contact Email**: Enter an optional contact email.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter an optional contact phone number.
* **Anonymity Request**: Choose if you want to remain anonymous.
* **Support Needed**: Indicate if you need support and if so, what type (Counseling, Safety Plan, etc.).
