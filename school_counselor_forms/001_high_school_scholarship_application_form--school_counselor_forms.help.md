# high_school_scholarship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for high school students to apply for a scholarship to support their education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Applicant Details" page with your information.
2. Check your "Academic Achievements" and indicate whether you have achieved any of the listed goals.
3. List your "Extracurricular Activities" on the next page.
4. Check your "Community Service" activities.
5. Select whether you have received any "Awards" that are relevant to your application.
6. Describe your "Financial Need" and how it relates to your education.
7. Provide details about your "References" (if applicable).
8. Indicate your "Academic History" (if applicable).
9. Write a "Personal Statement" about yourself and your goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details**: This section is for you to provide your name, email, and other contact information.
* **Academic Achievements**: Check all the academic achievements you have accomplished, such as earning good grades or achieving academic recognition.
* **Extracurricular Activities**: List any extracurricular activities you have participated in, such as sports, clubs, or volunteer work.
* **Community Service**: Describe any community service activities you have participated in that demonstrate your character and commitment to helping others.
* **Awards**: Check if you have received any awards or recognition for your academic or extracurricular achievements.
* **Financial Need**: Describe any financial needs you are experiencing that would affect your education, such as tuition costs or living expenses.
* **References**: If you have any references from teachers, counselors, or mentors, provide their name and contact information.
* **Academic History**: If you have any learning difficulties or special needs, describe them.
* **Personal Statement**: Write a 1-2 paragraph statement about yourself and why you are a good fit for the scholarship.
