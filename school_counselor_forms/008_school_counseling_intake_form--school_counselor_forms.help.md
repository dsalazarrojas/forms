# School Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Counseling Intake Form is a collection of information that helps school counselors gather essential details about each student. This form is designed to facilitate communication between students and counselors and ensure that students receive the support they need. To use this form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter the required information accurately**: Please fill in all the required fields with precise and complete data.
2. **Use the provided fields**: Use the available fields to provide additional information if needed.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send the information to the designated authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: Enter the student's identification number or any unique identifier.
* **Student Name**: Type the student's full name as it appears on their records.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address if available.
* **Student Phone**: Provide the student's contact phone number.
* **Parent Contacted On**: Record the date when the parent/guardian was last contacted.
* **Student Birthdate**: Enter the student's birthdate (month, day, year).
* **Student Parent Name**: Type the student's parent/guardian name.
* **Student Parent Contacted**: Select the status of the last parent contact: "True" if the parent was contacted, "False" otherwise.
* **Student Concerns**: Select all the concerns that apply to the student, such as **Academic**, **Personal**, or **Social**.
* **Counselor Name**: Type the name of the student's assigned counselor.
* **Counselor Title**: Enter the title of the counselor's position.
* **Counselor Department**: Enter the department where the counselor works.
* **Student Grade**: Enter the student's current grade level.
* **Student Year**: Enter the student's year level.
* **Student Course**: Type the course the student is currently enrolled in, if applicable.
* **Student Section**: Type the student's section or class, if applicable.
* **Counselor Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes from the counselor.
* **Student Parent Phone**: Enter the parent's phone number if available.
* **Student Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about the student.

Please ensure to review the form carefully and fill it out accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions, contact the designated authority.
