# Career Choice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to help students explore and identify their career interests, goals, and aspirations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Please select all relevant options for each field that apply to you.
3. Be honest and take your time to think carefully about each question.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts not addressed in this form, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)**: This is your full name.
* **Student ID Number (2)**: This is your school-issued student ID number. If you don't have one, leave this field blank.
* **School Email Address (3)**: This is the email address associated with your school account. Please make sure it's a valid email address.
* **Current Grade Level (4)**: Select your current grade level from the options provided.
* **Age (5)**: Enter your age.
* **Gender (6)**: Select your gender identity from the options provided.
* **Survey Date (7)**: This will automatically be filled in based on the current date.
* **Where Are You in Career Exploration (8)**: Select where you are in the career exploration process.
* **Career Areas of Interest (9)**: Select all career areas that interest you.
* **Preferred Work Environment (10)**: Select all preferred work environments.
* **Preferred Work Schedule (11)**: Select your preferred work schedule from the options provided.
* **Expected Starting Salary Range (12)**: Select the range of your expected starting salary.
* **Highest Level of Education Planned (13)**: Select the highest level of education you plan to achieve.
* **Current Major or Field of Study (14)**: If you have declared a major, enter it here.
* **Majors You Are Considering (15)**: Select all majors you are considering.
* **Who Has Most Influenced Your Career Thinking (16)**: Select who has influenced your career thinking.
* **Do You Have Any Work Experience (17)**: Select your level of work experience.
* **Your Top Skills and Strengths (18)**: Select all relevant skills and strengths.
* **Most Important Career Values (19)**: Select top 3 most important career values.
* **Perceived Barriers to Your Career Goals (20)**: Select all perceived barriers to your career goals.
* **Are You Interested in Career Counseling (21)**: Select your level of interest in career counseling.
* **Have You Taken Any Career Assessments (22)**: Select if you have taken any career assessments.
* **Are You Interested in Internship Opportunities (23)**: Select your level of interest in internship opportunities.
* **How Comfortable Are You With Professional Networking (24)**: Select your level of comfort with professional networking.
* **What Career Questions Do You Have (25)**: If you have any career questions, type them here.
* **Additional Comments (26)**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts, type them here.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Select all relevant options for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, select the option that best represents your current thoughts.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your school counselor or career advisor.
* This form is meant to be a starting point for career exploration and is not a comprehensive assessment.
* Take your time and carefully consider each question to ensure accurate and thoughtful responses.
