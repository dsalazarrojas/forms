# Student Adaptation To College Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about a student's adaptation to college, including their background, parental involvement, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
* For questions with options, select the relevant answer(s) that best describe the student's situation.
* For date fields, select the start date of the student's adaptation to college.
* For time fields, select the start time of the student's adaptation to college.
* For fields like "School Phone?" and "School Email?", provide the contact information of the school or counselor.
* For fields like "Student Ethnicity?" and "Student Disability?", select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the student's status.
* For "Student Gender?" field, select either "Male" or "Female".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How does the student adapt to college?** (`adaptation_to_college`)
	+ A text field where you can provide a detailed description of the student's adaptation to college.
* **What is the student's background?** (`student_background`)
	+ A text field for the student's background information.
* **How involved are the parents?** (`parental_involvement`)
	+ A text field for the parental involvement details.
* **How involved is the student?** (`student_involvement`)
	+ A text field for the student's level of involvement.
* **School Counselor Form** (`school_counselor_form`)
	+ A text field for any additional comments or information from the school counselor.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share about the student?** (`other`)
	+ A text field for any additional comments or information about the student.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`)
	+ A text field for any additional notes or comments.
* **Does the student adapt to college?** (`student_adaptation`)
	+ A single selection to indicate if the student adapts to college.
* **Student Adaptation 2** (`student_adaptation_2`)
	+ A multiple selection to indicate other aspects of the student's adaptation to college.
* **What is the student's start date?** (`date`)
	+ A date field for the start date of the student's adaptation to college.
* **What is the student's start time?** (`time`)
	+ A time field for the start time of the student's adaptation to college.
* **School Name** (`school_name`)
	+ A text field for the name of the school.
* **School Year?** (`school_year`)
	+ A number field for the school year of the student's adaptation to college.
* **Student Grade Level?** (`grade_level`)
	+ A number field for the student's grade level.
* **Student Gender?** (`student_gender`)
	+ A single selection to indicate the student's gender.
* **Student Ethnicity?** (`student_ethnicity`)
	+ A single selection to indicate if the student has a specific ethnicity.
* **Student Disability?** (`student_disability`)
	+ A single selection to indicate if the student has a disability.
* **School Phone?** (`school_phone`)
	+ A text field for the school's phone number.
* **School Email?** (`school_email`)
	+ An email field for the school's email address.
* **Student Adaptation 3** (`student_adaptation_3`)
	+ A multiple selection to indicate other aspects of the student's adaptation to college.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`)
	+ A text field for any additional comments or information.
* **School Email? (2)** (`school_email`)
	+ A duplicate field of "School Email?".
* **School Phone? (2)** (`school_phone`)
	+ A duplicate field of "School Phone?".

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and accurate information for each field.
* If you're unsure about a field, you can always contact the school counselor for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
