# Registration Adjustment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Adjustment Form is a tool used by school counselors to collect and store important information about each student. It helps to keep student records up-to-date and ensures that necessary adjustments are made to their registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to submit this form.
2. Select the student whose registration you want to adjust.
3. Fill in the required information, such as last name, phone number, and parent permission.
4. Review and fill in any other relevant information, such as email, teacher, and student notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether parent permission is needed for adjustments.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
* **Teacher** (`teacher`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if the student has a teacher assigned.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the teacher's email address (if applicable).
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the student.
* **School Counselor Comments** (`school_counselor_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments or remarks from the school counselor.
* **School Counselor Notes** (`school_counselor_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes from the school counselor.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Be accurate and clear in your entries.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to contact the school administration or relevant parties for clarification.
