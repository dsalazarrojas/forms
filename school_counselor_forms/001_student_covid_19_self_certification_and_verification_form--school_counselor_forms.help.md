# Student Covid 19 Self Certification And Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your health status in relation to COVID-19 to support public health measures.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, review it carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Daily Symptom Screening**: Describe your current health status.
*   **Name**: Enter your full name.
*   **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
*   **Date Screened**: Enter the date you completed this form.
*   **Time Screened**: Enter the time you completed this form.
*   **Have You Been Exposed To COVID 19**: Select 'Yes' if you have been exposed to COVID-19 or have been in close contact with someone who has COVID-19; otherwise, select 'No'.
*   **Do You Currently Feel Fever**: Select 'Yes' if you are experiencing fever; otherwise, select 'No'.
*   **Do You Currently Have A Cough**: Select 'Yes' if you are experiencing a cough; otherwise, select 'No'.
*   **Have You Been In Contact With Anyone Who Has COVID 19**: Select 'Yes' if you have been in contact with someone who has COVID-19; otherwise, select 'No'.
*   **Note**: Provide any additional information or comments you think are relevant.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address if you prefer to be contacted regarding this form.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when providing information.
*   If you are unsure about any question, consult with a healthcare professional.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
