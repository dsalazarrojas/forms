# Intention of Returning to Campus Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your intention of returning to campus for school. It will help us understand your preferences and expectations regarding remote and face-to-face learning, campus safety, and your commitment to returning to campus if possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and select the answer that best represents your thoughts and feelings.
2. For questions with multiple options, you can select one or more answers, depending on your preferences.
3. If you have any other thoughts or concerns that are not listed, please use the "Other" field to share them with us.
4. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How much interest do you have in returning to campus? (return_to_campus_interests)**: This question helps us understand the level of enthusiasm you have for returning to campus. Please select a number that best reflects your interest, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **What type of remote learning are you interested in? (remote_learning_preference)**: This question asks about your preferences for remote learning, which can include online courses, virtual discussions, or other online resources. Please select all that apply.
* **What type of face-to-face learning are you interested in? (face_to_face_learning_preference)**: This question asks about your preferences for face-to-face learning, which can include in-person discussions, workshops, or other activities that require physical presence. Please select all that apply.
* **How safe do you feel on campus? (campus_safety)**: This question helps us understand your perception of campus safety. Please select the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **How important is campus culture to your experience? (campus_culture)**: This question asks about your preferences for campus culture, which can include aspects such as social events, campus traditions, or community engagement. Please select the option that best reflects your priorities.
* **What benefits do you see from remote learning? (remote_learning_benefits)**: This question helps us understand the advantages you see from remote learning. Please select all that apply.
* **What benefits do you see from face-to-face learning? (face_to_face_benefits)**: This question asks about the advantages you see from face-to-face learning. Please select all that apply.
* **Are there any other benefits you see? (other_benefits)**: If you have other benefits that are not listed, please use this field to share them with us.
* **Are you committed to returning to campus if possible? (return_to_campus_commitment)**: This question helps us understand your commitment to returning to campus. Please select the option that best reflects your commitment.
* **How important is returning to campus to you? (return_to_campus_important)**: This question asks about the importance you place on returning to campus. Please select a number that best reflects your priorities, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
