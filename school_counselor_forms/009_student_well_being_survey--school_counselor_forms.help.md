# Student Well Being Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your well-being and experiences at school. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to help us understand your needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each question and make your selection or enter your answer in the text fields. Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting your response.
