# General Electives Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The General Electives Survey is a form used by students to inform school administrators and counselors about their elective course preferences. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the student's interests and skills, which will then be used to determine which electives to offer to the students in the upcoming academic year.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select your elective preferences**: For each section, carefully read through the available options and select the electives that best match your interests and skills.
2. **Be honest and thorough**: Provide thoughtful and honest responses to each question, as your selections will help the school administrators make informed decisions about elective courses.
3. **Collaborate with your counselor**: Review your selections with your school counselor to ensure that you have made the best choices for your interests and abilities.
