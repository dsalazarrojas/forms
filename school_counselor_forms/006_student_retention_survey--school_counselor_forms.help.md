# Student Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Retention Survey is a form used to gather information from students about their reasons for considering leaving school or their educational background. The survey helps the school administration understand the needs and concerns of students, which can inform decisions to improve student retention and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Student Retention Survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most relevant option that best describes your situation.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you need to fully explain your answer.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think is most important?**: This question asks you to identify the main reason why you are considering leaving school. Choose the option that best describes your situation.
* **What is the main reason for leaving?** (Select one of the following):
	+ Financial aid
	+ Family circumstances
	+ Academic standards
	+ Bullying
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What do you plan to enroll in next semester?** (Select all that apply):
	+ Career
	+ Technical
* **Who did you talk to before making this decision?** (Select one of the following):
	+ Teacher
	+ Parent
	+ Counselor
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How long have you been thinking about leaving?**: Answer this question as honestly and accurately as possible.
* **Do you have any previous education?** (Select one of the following):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **If yes, what previous school attended?**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, please provide the name of the previous school you attended.
* **What is your education level?** (Select one of the following):
	+ High school
	+ Vocational
	+ College
	+ Post-bachelor
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What program are you interested in?** (Select all that apply):
	+ Career
	+ Technical
* **What grade are you currently in?** (Select one of the following):
	+ 9th
	+ 10th
	+ 11th
	+ 12th
* **What subjects are you interested in?** (Select all that apply):
	+ Math
	+ Science
	+ English
* **Do you have any other schools you are considering?** (Select all that apply):
	+ Yes
	+ No

Note: This form is designed to be completed by students who are currently enrolled or have been enrolled in the past. If you are not a student or an authorized representative of the student, please do not submit this form.
