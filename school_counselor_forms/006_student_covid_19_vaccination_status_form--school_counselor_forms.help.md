# Student COVID 19 Vaccination Status Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student COVID-19 Vaccination Status Form is used to collect and update a student's COVID-19 vaccination status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are the authorized person to submit this form and the student's vaccination status has changed.
2. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student COVID-19 Vaccine Information** (`student_vaccination_form_1`, text, optional):
	+ A field to provide information about the student's COVID-19 vaccine, such as the type and manufacturer.
* **Student Vaccine Dosage Information** (`student_vaccination_form_2`, text, optional):
	+ A field to provide the student's vaccine dosage information.
* **Student Vaccine Manufacturer and Type** (`student_vaccination_form_3`, text, optional):
	+ A field to provide details about the student's vaccine type and manufacturer.
* **Student Vaccination Date 1** (`student_vaccination_form_4`, date, optional):
	+ A field to provide the date the student received their first dose of the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Student Vaccination Date 2** (`student_vaccination_form_6`, date, optional):
	+ A field to provide the date the student received their second dose of the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Student Vaccination Status** (`student_vaccination_form_5`, number, optional):
	+ A field to select the student's vaccination status. Please refer to the options for further guidance.

Note: The options for the "Student Vaccination Status" field can be provided separately or may be referenced in other supporting documentation.
