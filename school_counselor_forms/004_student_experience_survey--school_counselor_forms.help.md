## Student Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Experience Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and opinions regarding your classes. Your feedback will help us understand what you like and dislike about the class, what you would like to see changed or improved, and how we can better support you in the classroom. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in this survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. Select "Option 1," "Option 2," or "Option 3" for multiple-choice questions, and type your responses in the text fields. You can submit your responses by clicking the "Submit" button at the end of the form. If you have any concerns or suggestions, feel free to share them in the last question.
