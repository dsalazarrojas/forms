# School Meal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Meal Survey is designed to collect feedback on the quality and variety of school meals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:
1. Select your answer for each question from the provided options.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by \*).
3. If you have additional comments, use the "Other Comments -" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate the quality of school meals?** (`survey_question_1`, select_one, required): This question asks for your honest opinion about the quality of school meals. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What do you think about the variety of foods offered?** (`survey_question_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the options that apply to you: Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Meat, Fruit/Veggie.
* **How would you rate the taste of school meals?** (`survey_question_3`, number, required): Rate the taste of school meals on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How about the quantity of food provided?** (`survey_question_4`, time, required): Select one of the time ranges: 1-2 minutes, 2-3 minutes, 3-4 minutes, 4-5 minutes, More than 5 minutes.
* **Would you like to leave a comment?** (`survey_question_5`, note, optional): Share any thoughts or suggestions you have about school meals.
* **What is your school email?** (`survey_question_6`, email, required): Enter your school email address.
* **What is your school phone number?** (`survey_question_7`, text, required): Enter your school phone number.
* **How often would you like school meals to be served?** (`survey_question_8`, date, optional): Select a date for your preferred meal service frequency.
* **Would you like more salad options?** (`survey_question_9`, select_one, optional): Choose Yes, No, or Maybe.
* **Other Comments -** (`survey_question_10`, note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions.
