# Cyberbullying And Online Aggression Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather information from participants about their experiences and perceptions regarding cyberbullying and online aggression. This form will help us better understand the prevalence, effects, and prevention strategies related to these issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read each question carefully and fill out your response in the corresponding text box.
2. Be as detailed and accurate as possible in your answers.
3. If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
4. You can complete this form at your own pace, and save your progress if needed.
