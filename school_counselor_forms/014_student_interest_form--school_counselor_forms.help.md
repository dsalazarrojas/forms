# Student Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Interest Form is a tool used to gather relevant student information for academic support and guidance. It helps counselors and administrators understand students' interests, strengths, and areas of concern. By filling out this form, students can help their school better understand their needs and provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your student name and grade level.
3. Choose your subjects of interest from the list.
4. Select your career goals.
5. Indicate your hobbies and extracurricular preferences.
6. If applicable, enter other hobbies not listed above.
7. Write a brief statement about your interests and goals.
8. Provide your parent contact information (if applicable).
9. If you have any concerns or issues, please write them down.
10. Leave any additional notes or comments about your interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your school records.
* **Student Grade**: Enter your current grade level (e.g., 9, 10, 11, or 12).
* **Student Subjects of Interest**: Select all subjects you are interested in or enjoy learning about (e.g., English, Math, Science, Social Studies).
* **Career Goals**: Choose one or more career goals that align with your aspirations (e.g., Business, Engineering, Healthcare, Arts).
* **Hobbies**: Select all activities you enjoy doing in your free time (e.g., Reading, Writing, Music, Sports).
* **Extracurricular Preferences**: Choose activities you would be interested in joining or participating in at school (e.g., Debate Team, School Newspaper, School Play, School Choir).
* **Other Hobbies**: If you have other interests or hobbies not listed above, enter them here.
* **Student Interest Statement**: Write a brief statement about your goals, aspirations, and interests.
* **Parent Contact Info**: If you have any parent contact information (e.g., email, phone number), please enter it here.
* **Student Concerns**: If you have any concerns or issues, please write them down.
* **Student Notes**: Leave any additional notes or comments about your interests and goals.
