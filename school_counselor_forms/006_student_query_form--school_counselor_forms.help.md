# Student Query Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for students to submit queries or concerns to the school's administration or counselors. It is used to provide a clear and organized way for students to communicate their needs and questions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your name, student ID, and email address to identify yourself as a student.
2. If you have a phone number, you can optionally provide it to allow for further contact.
3. In the message field, describe your query or concern in detail.
4. Select the department that you would like to contact (Yes/No).
5. Choose all the concerns that apply to your query (e.g., Yes/No).
6. Select if you are submitting on behalf of someone else (Yes/No).
7. Provide a date for your concern (if applicable).
8. Choose whether your query is for a specific concern (Yes/No).
9. Finally, describe your concern in detail and select a department that you would like to contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **Student ID** (number, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.

* **Message** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of your query or concern.
* **Department** (select_one, required): Select the department you would like to contact (e.g., 'Yes' for Yes or 'No' for No).
* **Concerns** (select_multiple, required): Select all concerns that apply to your query (e.g., 'Yes' for each concern).
* **Submitted By** (select_one, required): Select if you are submitting on behalf of someone else (Yes/No).
* **Concern Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your concern (if applicable).
* **Concern Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your concern (if applicable).

* **Query Details** (text, optional): Describe your query in more detail (if applicable).
* **Concern Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of your concern (if applicable).
