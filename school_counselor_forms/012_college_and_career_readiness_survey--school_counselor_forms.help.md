# College And Career Readiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to assess the level of college and career readiness among students. It helps school counselors, administrators, and advisors understand students' preparedness, interests, and challenges to provide guidance and support throughout their college and career journey.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly to provide accurate information.
2. Select the most relevant option for each question, even if it's a brief description or a specific date.
3. If you're unsure or have no answer, choose "Not Informed" or "Not Prepared" where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official student records.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter your student ID number assigned by your school or district.
* **Current Grade Level** (`grade_level`): Select the current grade level you're in (9th Grade, 10th Grade, 11th Grade, 12th Grade).
* **Academic Readiness** (`academic_readiness`): Indicate how prepared you feel academically for college (Very Prepared, Prepared, Somewhat Prepared, Not Prepared).
* **Have you taken SAT or ACT prep courses** (`test_prep`): Answer whether you've taken SAT or ACT prep courses (Yes, No, Plan to).
* **Do you plan to attend college** (`college_goals`): Indicate your intention to attend college (Yes, No, Unsure).
* **When do you plan to attend college** (`college_timeline`): Select when you plan to attend college (Immediately after high school, 1-2 years after high school, 3+ years after high school, Not planning).
* **Type of college or university preferred** (`college_preferences`): Select all types of colleges or universities you prefer (Public University, Private University, Community College, Liberal Arts College, Technical School, Online Program).
* **Potential areas of study or majors** (`major_interest`): Enter your potential areas of study or majors.
* **College knowledge** (`college_knowledge`): Indicate how informed you are about college requirements and application process (Very Informed, Informed, Somewhat Informed, Not Informed).
* **Understanding of financial aid and scholarships** (`financial_aid_understanding`): Answer whether you have a good understanding of financial aid and scholarships (Very Knowledgeable, Knowledgeable, Somewhat Knowledgeable, Not Knowledgeable).
* **Career field interests** (`career_interests`): Select all career fields that interest you (Science and Technology, Healthcare, Business, Creative Arts, Education, Trades and Skilled Crafts, Other).
* **How prepared are you for career decisions** (`career_readiness`): Indicate how prepared you are for career decisions (Very Prepared, Prepared, Somewhat Prepared, Not Prepared).
* **Do you have work or internship experience** (`work_experience`): Answer whether you've had work or internship experience (Yes, No).
* **Describe your work or internship experience** (`work_description`): Describe your work or internship experience (Optional).
* **Confidence in soft skills** (`soft_skills_confidence`): Indicate your confidence in soft skills (communication, teamwork, problem-solving) (Very Confident, Confident, Somewhat Confident, Not Confident).
* **Social-emotional readiness** (`social_emotional`): Rate your social-emotional readiness (Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Improvement).
* **Areas where you need support or guidance** (`support_needs`): Select areas where you need support or guidance (College preparation, Career planning, Academics, Financial planning, Social skills, Mental health).
* **Have you met with a school counselor about college-career plans** (`counselor_meetings`): Answer whether you've met with a school counselor about college-career plans (Yes, No).
* **Top 3 priorities for the next 2 years** (`future_goals`): List your top 3 priorities for the next 2 years.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure or have no answer, choose "Not Informed" or "Not Prepared" where applicable.
* Use specific dates or descriptions to provide clear answers.
* Select all relevant career fields that interest you.
* List your top 3 priorities for the next 2 years in clear and specific terms.
