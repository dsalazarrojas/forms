# safety-incident-report-access-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about incidents that have occurred within the organization. Its main goal is to facilitate incident reporting and management for improved decision-making and response.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1. **Incident Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
2. **Incident Time**: Enter the time when the incident occurred.
3. **Incident Location**: Enter a brief description of where the incident occurred.
4. **Incident Summary**: Enter a brief summary of what happened.
5. **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred from the dropdown menu.
6. **Incident Cause**: Enter a brief description of the cause of the incident.
7. **Injuries**: Select the type of injuries sustained from the dropdown menu.
8. **Incident Reporter**: Select the department and role of the person reporting the incident from the dropdown menus.
9. **Incident Manager**: Select the incident manager from the dropdown menu.
10. **Incident Investigator**: Select the incident investigator from the dropdown menu.
11. **Incident Report Form**: Indicate if this is a reportable incident by selecting "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
12. **Injuries Description**: Enter a brief description of any injuries sustained.
13. **Reporting Date**: Enter the date when the incident was reported.
14. **Reporting Time**: Enter the time when the incident was reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: The date when the incident occurred. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time**: The time when the incident occurred. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Incident Location**: A brief description of where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Summary**: A brief summary of what happened.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred from the dropdown menu:
	+ First Aid: Minor injuries or incidents that do not require medical treatment.
	+ Medical: Incidents that require medical treatment.
	+ Other: Other types of incidents not mentioned above.
* **Incident Cause**: Enter a brief description of the cause of the incident.
* **Injuries**: Select the type of injuries sustained from the dropdown menu:
	+ Minor: Minor injuries that do not require medical treatment.
	+ Serious: Serious injuries that require medical attention.
	+ No injuries: No injuries were sustained.
* **Incident Reporter**: Select the department and role of the person reporting the incident from the dropdown menus.
* **Incident Manager**: Select the incident manager from the dropdown menu.
* **Incident Investigator**: Select the incident investigator from the dropdown menu.
* **Incident Report Form**: Indicate if this is a reportable incident by selecting "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
* **Injuries Description**: Enter a brief description of any injuries sustained.
* **Reporting Date**: Enter the date when the incident was reported.
* **Reporting Time**: Enter the time when the incident was reported.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can address the incident effectively.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or incident team for guidance.
* This form is used to improve incident reporting and management within the organization. Your input is valuable in helping us identify and address potential risks and issues.
