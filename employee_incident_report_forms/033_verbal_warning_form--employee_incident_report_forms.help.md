# Verbal Warning Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Complete the form title and employee details

* **Verbal Warning Form** (`Verbal Warning Form`, text, required): Enter the title of the incident report.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who is involved in the incident.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, text, required): Enter the position of the employee who is involved in the incident.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager who is addressing the incident.
* **Manager Position** (`manager_position`, text, required): Enter the position of the manager addressing the incident.

### Step 2: Report the incident

* **Date Occurred** (`date_occurred`, date, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Summary** (`incident_summary`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of the incident.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Describe the incident in more detail.
* **Reason for Warning** (`reason_for_warning`, text, optional): Explain the reason for the warning.
* **Action Planned** (`action_planned`, text, optional): Describe the steps being taken to address the incident.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline any follow-up actions or steps that will be taken.

### Step 3: Signatures and Confirmation

* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): The employee involved in the incident should sign the form to confirm they have received and understand the warning.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, optional): The manager who addressed the incident should sign the form to confirm they have given the warning and documented the incident.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is not applicable, please mark it as "Not Applicable" or "Not Applicable (N/A)" to avoid confusion.
* Please keep the tone professional and objective when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department or supervisor for assistance.
