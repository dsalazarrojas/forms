# Fitness Center Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Center Incident Report Form is used to document incidents that occur at the fitness center, such as injuries, accidents, or other events that require reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the employee's name who was involved in the incident.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
4. Describe the incident in detail, including what happened and any witness statements (if available).
5. Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Broken Equipment, Other, Slip/Trip and Fall, or Injury).
6. Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
7. Enter the date the incident report was made.
8. (Optional) Attach an image or video of the incident (e.g., a photo or a recording of the incident).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (`employee_name`):** Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident. This is a required field.
* **Incident Date (`incident_date`):** Enter the date of the incident in MM/DD/YYYY format. This is a required field.
* **Incident Time (`incident_time`):** Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour format (e.g., 09:00 for 9:00 AM). This is a required field.
* **Incident Location (`incident_location`):** Enter the location where the incident occurred. This can be a specific room or area within the fitness center. This is a required field.
* **Incident Description (`incident_description`):** Describe what happened during the incident, including any witness statements (if available). This is a required field.
* **Incident Type (`incident_type`):** Choose the type of incident that occurred from the options provided (e.g., Broken Equipment, Other, Slip/Trip and Fall, or Injury). This is a required field.
* **Incident Reporter (`incident_reporter`):** Enter the name of the person reporting the incident. This is a required field.
* **Reporting Date (`reporting_date`):** Enter the date the incident report was made. This is a required field.
* **Incident Image (`incident_image`):** (Optional) Attach an image or video of the incident, if available.
* **Witness Name (`witness_name`):** Enter the name of the witness, if applicable. This is an optional field.
* **Witness Statement (`witness_statement`):** Enter the statement from the witness, if applicable. This is an optional field.
* **Incident Type (`incident_type`):** The options for this field are:
	+ Broken Equipment
	+ Other
	+ Slip/Trip and Fall
	+ Injury

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* Attach images or videos of the incident if available, as they can provide helpful evidence.
* Fill out the form as soon as possible after the incident occurs.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
