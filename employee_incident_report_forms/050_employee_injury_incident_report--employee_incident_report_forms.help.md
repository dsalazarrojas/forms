# Employee Injury Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report incidents related to workplace injuries. It helps us understand the details of each incident and take necessary actions to ensure employee safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields (marked with *) with accurate and clear information.
2. For fields with select options, choose the most relevant option from the provided list.
3. Enter detailed information for fields with text or date/time type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, Text): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (2, Text): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Job Title** (3, Text): Enter the employee's job title (optional).
* **Date of Injury** (4, Date): Enter the date of the incident in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Injury** (5, Time): Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour format (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Incident Type** (6, Select One): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the incident was related to an injury.
* **Description of Injury** (7, Text): Enter a brief description of the injury or incident.
* **Supervisor Name** (8, Text): Enter the supervisor's name of the injured employee.
* **Supervisor Phone** (9, Text): Enter the supervisor's phone number (optional).
* **Supervisor Email** (10, Email): Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Employee Name** (11, Text): Enter the full name of the injured employee.
* **Employee Job Title** (12, Text): Enter the employee's job title (optional).
* **Witness Name** (13, Text): Enter the name of any witness (optional).
* **Witness Relationship** (14, Text): Describe the witness's relationship to the injured employee (optional).
* **Witness Contact Method** (15, Text): Choose a method to contact the witness (e.g., phone, email).
* **Witness Phone** (16, Text): Enter the witness's phone number (optional).
* **Witness Email** (17, Email): Enter the witness's email address (optional).
* **Location Name** (18, Text): Enter the location where the incident occurred (optional).
* **Location City** (19, Text): Enter the city where the incident occurred (optional).
* **Location Street** (20, Text): Enter the street where the incident occurred (optional).
* **Location State** (21, Text): Enter the state where the incident occurred (optional).
* **Location Zip** (22, Text): Enter the zip code where the incident occurred (optional).
* **Supervisor Department** (23, Text): Enter the supervisor's department (optional).
* **Witness Description** (24, Text): Enter a brief description of the witness (optional).
* **Incident Description** (25, Text): Enter a detailed description of the incident (optional).
