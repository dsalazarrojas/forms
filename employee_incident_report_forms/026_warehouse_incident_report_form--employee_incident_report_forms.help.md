# Warehouse Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Incident Report Form is a standardized report used to document and investigate incidents occurring in the warehouse. Fill out this form to report any incidents that may have occurred, and ensure that they are properly investigated and resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the date of the incident.
- Provide a detailed description of the incident.
- Enter the time of the incident.
- Specify the location where the incident occurred.
- Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Fire, Theft, Accident, Vandalism, Equipment malfunction).
- If necessary, provide additional information on the incident's investigation.
- Select the person who reported the incident (e.g., Warehouse Manager, Warehouse Foreman, Warehouse Supervisor, Warehouse Worker).
- Choose the current status of the incident (e.g., Open, Closed, Solved, In Progress).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Warehouse Incident Date** (1): This field is used to specify the date of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (2): Provide a clear and concise description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Time** (3): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (4): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (5): Choose the type of incident that occurred from the provided options (e.g., Fire, Theft, Accident, Vandalism, Equipment malfunction).
* **Incident Investigation** (6): If necessary, provide additional information on the incident's investigation.
* **Incident Reported by** (7): Select the person who reported the incident from the provided options (e.g., Warehouse Manager, Warehouse Foreman, Warehouse Supervisor, Warehouse Worker).
* **Incident Status** (8): Choose the current status of the incident from the provided options (e.g., Open, Closed, Solved, In Progress).

## Tips
- If unsure, please ask a supervisor or manager for clarification.
- Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible, as it will help ensure that incidents are properly investigated and resolved.
- Review and submit the form once you have gathered all the necessary information.
