# Workplace Accident Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that occurred in the workplace, including accidents or absences due to injuries or illnesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling this form for a valid reason related to a workplace accident or absence.
2. Review each field carefully, and provide detailed information for each field as required.
3. Make sure to fill out every field that is marked as "required."
4. If a field is marked as "optional," feel free to provide additional information if you think it's relevant to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Accident Absence Form** (Page 1): This is the title of the form, which is already filled out and doesn't require any input from you.
* **Incident Date** (Page 2): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Page 3): Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (Page 4): Enter the location where the incident took place.
* **Incident Description** (Page 5): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Reporting Employee** (Page 6): Enter your name and ID number, as you are reporting this incident.

Note: All fields are optional except for "Reporting Employee." Please ensure you fill out this field accurately.
