# Injury Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Injury Declaration Form is used by employees to report incidents that occurred to them, whether they are injuries or other workplace hazards. The form is used to gather relevant information about the incident, including the employee's details, the nature of the incident, and any medical attention that may be needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant answers to each field, and type your responses into the text fields. Make sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Info** (`Employee Name`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the name of the employee who experienced the incident from the dropdown menu. This will help identify the person affected and ensure proper communication with the relevant authorities.
* **Incident Details** (`Incident Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident, including any relevant circumstances or conditions that led to the injury or hazard.
* **Injury Description** (`Injury Description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the injury or hazard experienced by the employee, including the severity and any effects it may have had on their well-being.
* **Injury Type** (`Injury Type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any of the following types of injuries that apply to the incident:
	+ Sprains and Strains
	+ Muscle Strains
	+ Bone Fractures
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate if the employee has any pre-existing medical conditions that may be relevant to the incident:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Medical Attention Needed** (`Medical Attention Needed`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if the employee will need medical attention as a result of the incident:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Reported By** (`Reported By`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name and title of the person reporting the incident, if different from the employee who experienced the incident.
* **Reporting Manager** (`Reporting Manager`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name and title of the manager who should be informed about the incident, if different from the person reporting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete reporting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or your manager for assistance.
