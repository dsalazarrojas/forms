# Workplace Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Harassment Complaint Form is a tool to gather information about workplace harassment incidents for reporting and handling. This form helps ensure that incidents are documented accurately and thoroughly, which aids in addressing and resolving them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Determine if the workplace harassment complaint has occurred and if you are the complainant.
2. Select the correct answer to "Workplace Harassment Complaint Form" on Page 1.
3. Fill out the "complainant_name" field with your name and title/position.
4. Provide the name of the harasser in the "harasser_name" field.
5. Describe the incident in the "description_of_harassment" field, including dates and details.
6. Specify the time and date of the incident in the "date_harassment_occurred" and "time_harassment_occurred" fields, respectively.
7. Indicate where the incident occurred in the "location_harassment_occurred" field.
8. Check if there were any witnesses and indicate if they are available to provide further information.
9. Select the method used to resolve the incident.
10. Indicate if the resolution was successful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Harassment Complaint Form** (Page 1, select_multiple): Select "Yes" if the incident was related to a workplace harassment complaint.
* **Complainant Name** (complainant_name, text, required): Enter your name as the person filing the complaint.
* **Harasser Name** (harasser_name, text, required): Provide the name of the person who harassed you.
* **Description of Harassment** (description_of_harassment, text, required): Briefly describe the incident, including dates and details. This is a crucial field for documenting the incident accurately.
* **Date Harassment Occurred** (date_harassment_occurred, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Harassment Occurred** (time_harassment_occurred, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Location Harassment Occurred** (location_harassment_occurred, text, required): Specify where the incident took place, including the department, floor, or specific room number.
* **Witnesses** (witnesses, select_one, required): Indicate if there were any witnesses and if they are available to provide further information.
* **Resolution Method** (resolution_method, select_one, required): Select the method used to resolve the incident (e.g., mediation, counseling, disciplinary action).
* **Resolution Outcome** (resolution_outcome, select_one, required): Indicate if the resolution was successful.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, required): Include any other relevant comments or details about the incident.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact your HR representative or supervisor for guidance.
