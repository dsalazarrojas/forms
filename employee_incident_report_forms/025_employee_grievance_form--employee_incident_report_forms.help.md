# Employee Grievance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Grievance Form is designed to provide a platform for employees to report any grievances or concerns they may be experiencing in the workplace. This form is intended to be a safe and confidential space for employees to voice their concerns without fear of repercussions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, review the form and make sure you're comfortable with the questions and options provided.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much detail as possible about the issue or concern.
3. Be sure to upload any relevant supporting evidence, such as screenshots or documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Grievance Form Page 1**: This page is a note field where you can provide a brief description of your issue or concern. Please keep it concise and relevant.
* **Employee Grievance Form Page 2**: This page is another note field where you can provide additional context or details about your issue or concern.
* **Reporting Officer**: Select the individual who is best suited to address your concern. For example, this could be a supervisor, HR representative, or member of the management team.
* **Grievance Description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue or concern you are experiencing. This could include what happened, how it affected you, and any impact it had on your work or well-being.
* **Grievance Details**: Add any additional context or supporting information about your issue or concern.
* **Supporting Evidence**: If you have any supporting evidence, such as screenshots or documents, please upload them here.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name, so we can address this issue or concern directly with you.
* **Employee Title**: Provide your job title, so we can understand your role and position within the company.
* **Employee Department**: Enter the department you belong to, so we can better understand the context of your issue or concern.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your email address, so we can contact you if needed.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to provide any supporting evidence that may help us understand the issue or concern better.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* Remember to keep the form confidential and only share it with authorized personnel.
