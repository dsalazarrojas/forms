# Transportation Worker Injury Recovery Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to be used by employees to report incidents involving injury while transporting goods, equipment, or personnel. It serves to document the employee's injury, including the date it occurred, a brief description of the incident, and any actions taken or planned to mitigate the injury.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

- Start by filling out the employee information section, including their ID, name, department, and employee status.
- Report the details of the incident, including the injury date, a description of what happened, and whether you were actively working or not.
- Provide your supervisor's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
- Indicate whether you have the manager's status, including their name, email, and phone number.
- Enter the dates you plan to take off for leave, as well as whether you will be taking leave.
- Choose the type of leave and the number of leave days you will be taking.
- Provide the reason for your leave.
- Indicate whether you have your manager's approval for the leave.
- Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.
- Finally, indicate whether any actions have been taken or planned to mitigate the injury.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Injury Date** (`injury_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the injury.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Briefly describe what happened.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are active or inactive.
* **Manager Status** (`manager_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether your manager is active or inactive.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are taking leave.
* **Leave Days** (`leave_days`, number, optional): Enter the number of leave days you will be taking.
* **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your leave.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have your manager's approval.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether any actions have been taken or planned to mitigate the injury.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, select_multiple, optional): Select any actions planned to mitigate the injury.
