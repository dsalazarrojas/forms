# Armidale YJCO Vehicle Use Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record information about a trip taken by an employee in a company vehicle. It helps with administrative and maintenance tasks by ensuring that accurate and detailed information is available about the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the User Information section with your basic contact details, including your name, employee ID, and email address.
2. In the Trip Details section, enter the date of the trip, the purpose of the trip, the destination, and the start and end times.
3. In the Vehicle Information section, provide the vehicle's registration number, type, odometer readings before and after the trip, and its current fuel level.
4. In the Vehicle Inspection section, assess the vehicle's exterior and interior condition before and after the trip, and record its current fuel level.
5. If any damage or incidents occurred during the trip, report them in the Damage or Incidents section.
6. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate in the Submission section, and date and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Employee ID** (`user_employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Email Address** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears in the company's system.
* **Date of Trip** (`trip_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the trip in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Purpose of Trip** (`trip_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for the trip from the options provided.
* **Trip Destination** (`trip_destination`, text, required): Enter the destination of the trip, including the name of the place or location.
* **Trip Start Time** (`trip_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the trip in the format HH:MM.
* **Trip End Time** (`trip_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the trip in the format HH:MM.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's registration number, also known as its plate or VIN.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of vehicle from the options provided.
* **Odometer Reading - Start** (`vehicle_odometer_start`, number, required): Enter the odometer reading before the trip.
* **Odometer Reading - End** (`vehicle_odometer_end`, number, required): Enter the odometer reading after the trip.
* **Exterior Condition** (`exterior_condition`, select_one, required): Assess the exterior condition of the vehicle before and after the trip from the options provided.
* **Interior Condition** (`interior_condition`, select_one, required): Assess the interior condition of the vehicle before and after the trip from the options provided.
* **Fuel Level at Start** (`fuel_level_start`, select_one, required): Choose the fuel level of the vehicle before the trip from the options provided.
* **Fuel Level at End** (`fuel_level_end`, select_one, required): Choose the fuel level of the vehicle after the trip from the options provided.
* **Any Damage Noted During Trip** (`damage_noted`, select_one, required): Report any damage or incidents that occurred during the trip from the options provided.
* **Description of Damage** (`damage_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of any damage or incidents that occurred during the trip.
* **Any Incidents Occurred During Trip** (`incident_occurred`, select_one, required): Report any incidents that occurred during the trip from the options provided.
* **Description of Incident** (`incident_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of any incidents that occurred during the trip.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission of the form.
* **I Certify the Above Information is Accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or the person who assigned you the vehicle.
* Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy.
* Sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
