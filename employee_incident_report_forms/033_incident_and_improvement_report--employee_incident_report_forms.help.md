# Incident And Improvement Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Incident and Improvement Report form is used to report incidents that occurred during work hours. This form is a means to document and track incidents, allowing for improvement and incident review processes to take place. Please use this form only to report incidents that happened on the job site, during work hours. Do not use this form for reporting personal or non-work-related incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Report Form section (page 1) with a brief description of what happened.
2. Select the relevant Category from the drop-down menu in the Category section (page 2).
3. If applicable, describe the actions taken in the Actions section.
4. If applicable, provide further details of the injury, in the Injury Details section.
5. Summarize any further actions planned or recommended in the Further Actions section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Form** (Report Form, Text, Optional):
	+ This is the section to briefly describe what happened. Try to be as clear and concise as possible. Include any relevant details about the incident.
* **Category** (Category, Select One, Required):
	+ Choose the Category that best fits the incident. This will help with tracking and improvement processes.
* **Actions** (Actions, Text, Optional):
	+ If you've taken any actions as a result of the incident, describe what they were.
* **Injury Details** (Injury Details, Text, Optional):
	+ If anyone was injured, provide further details about what happened and any treatment received.
* **Further Actions** (Further Actions, Text, Optional):
	+ If any further actions are planned or recommended, describe them here.

## Tips
* Be thorough but concise in your description.
* If you are unsure about the Category, ask your supervisor for clarification.
* If you have taken any photos or videos of the incident, attach them as supporting documentation.
