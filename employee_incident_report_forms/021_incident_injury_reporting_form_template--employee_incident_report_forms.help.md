# incident_injury_reporting_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents or injuries that have occurred during work hours. It helps to document the details of what happened, which is used to ensure that proper steps are taken to prevent similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your employee information, including your name and department.
2. Describe the incident or injury that occurred.
3. List any witnesses who saw or heard about the incident.
4. Check which injuries you sustained during the incident.
5. Note any medical care you received or will receive.
6. Provide the name and signature of your supervisor or safety officer.
7. Enter the date and time of the incident.
8. Enter any additional notes about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_info** (text, required): Enter your name and employee department.
* **incident_info** (text, optional): Briefly describe the incident or injury that occurred.
* **injury_info** (text, optional): Describe the nature of your injuries.
* **witness_info** (text, optional): List the names of any witnesses who saw or heard about the incident.
* **employee_injuries** (select_multiple, optional): Check all the injuries you sustained during the incident.
* **medical_care** (text, optional): Note any medical care you received or will receive.
* **medical_provider** (text, optional): Provide the name of the medical provider who treated you.
* **supervisor_signature** (text, optional): Sign and date this form with your supervisor's name.
* **safety_officer_signature** (text, optional): Sign and date this form with your safety officer's name.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **witness_signature** (text, optional): Sign and date this form with any witnesses' names.
* **incident_location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the incident.
* **incident_date** (text, optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **witness_count** (text, optional): Count the number of witnesses.
* **witness_names** (text, optional): List the names of any witnesses.
* **witness_relationships** (text, optional): Describe the relationship of any witnesses.
* **witness_notes** (text, optional): Enter any notes from witnesses.
* **employee_name** (text, optional): Enter your name.
* **employee_position** (text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **employee_department** (text, optional): Enter your department.

Note: All fields marked as required or optional are, as such, in the source form yaml. The optional fields allow for additional detail or clarification but are not mandatory.
