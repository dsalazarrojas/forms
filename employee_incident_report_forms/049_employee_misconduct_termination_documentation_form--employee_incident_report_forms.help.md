# Employee Misconduct Termination Documentation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's misconduct termination. It helps gather and record all relevant information about the incident, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for future reference and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start with the **Introduction** section, which provides a brief overview of the purpose and procedure for completing the form.
2. Move on to the **Employee Information** section, which asks for basic employee details, such as name, date of birth, and employment status.
3. The **Incident Details** section requires you to select one or more of the given options to describe the misconduct incident.
4. Enter the **Termination Date** of the employee's termination.
5. In the **Supervisor Signature** section, select the appropriate option confirming your role and authority to terminate the employee.
6. The **Employee Signature** section requires the terminated employee to acknowledge and agree to their termination.
7. Enter the **Date Signed** and **Date Reviewed** (if applicable) to record the dates of these events.
8. The **Review Date** (if required) is the date the form is reviewed and finalized.
9. In the **Termination Justification** section, provide a detailed explanation for the termination, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and procedure.
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, `text`, required): This section requires your basic employee details, such as name, date of birth, and employment status.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select one or more of the options to describe the misconduct incident. For example, choose **Option 1**, **Option 2**, or **Option 3**.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the employee's termination in the standard `YYYY-MM-DD` format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, `select_one`, required): Select **Option 1** or **Option 2** to confirm your role and authority to terminate the employee.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `select_one`, required): The terminated employee must acknowledge and agree to their termination by selecting **Option 1** or **Option 2**.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date the employee signed the form in the standard `YYYY-MM-DD` format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, `date`, optional): If the form requires review and approval, enter the date it was reviewed.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, optional): This is the date the form is reviewed and finalized.
* **Termination Justification** (`termination_justification`, `text`, optional): If necessary, provide a detailed explanation for the termination.
