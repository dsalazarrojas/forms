# Glen Innes YJCO Vehicle Use Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents involving company vehicles, including any necessary inspection reports and the submitter's confirmation of vehicle usage.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the vehicle_use_details field with a description of the incident, including the date, time, and location.
- Provide any necessary information for the vehicle_inspection_report field, including any issues or concerns with the vehicle.
- If the vehicle was being used for work-related purposes, select 'Yes' in the submitters_signature field.
- If you are the employee who submitted the form, sign in the submitters_signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Use Details** (`vehicle_use_details`, text, required/optional): A detailed description of the incident, including the date, time, and location.
* **Vehicle Inspection Report** (`vehicle_inspection_report`, text, required/optional): A report of any issues or concerns with the vehicle, including any necessary maintenance or repairs.
* **Submitter's Signature** (`submitters_signature`, note, required/optional): A signature from the employee who submitted the form, confirming that the vehicle was being used for work-related purposes.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, required/optional): A confirmation of who is submitting the form, with options 'Yes' and 'No'.
