# Employee Accident/Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about work-related accidents or incidents that affect employees, allowing for a prompt and thorough investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with page 1, titled "Report Details".
2. Provide a brief description of the accident or incident in the "Report Details" text field.
3. Proceed to page 2, "Employee Details", and fill out the form with the relevant information regarding the employee involved, such as their name, ID number, and contact information.
4. Move on to page 3, "Incident Details", and describe the incident, including the date, time, location, and any witnesses.
5. Lastly, page 4, "Witness Information", is optional, but if applicable, provide details about any witnesses, including their names, contact information, and statements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Report Details (Page 1)**: A text field to provide a brief description of the accident or incident.
  - Enter a concise account of what happened.
  - Include as much detail as possible.
  - Use plain language to explain the incident.
- **Employee Details (Page 2)**: This field is for employee information related to the incident.
  - Enter the employee's name as it appears on their employee ID card or badge.
  - Provide the employee's ID number if applicable.
  - Include the employee's email address or other relevant contact information.
- **Incident Details (Page 3)**: This field is for detailed information about the incident.
  - Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
  - Describe the location where the incident happened, including any relevant details such as rooms or areas within the building.
  - Mention if there were any witnesses and their roles or positions within the company.
- **Witness Information (Page 4)**: This field is optional and only if applicable.
  - If there were any witnesses, provide their names, contact information, and statements or observations regarding the incident.
