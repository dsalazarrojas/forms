# Disciplinary Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Communication Form is used to document employee incidents or disciplinary actions taken against an employee. It helps to maintain a record of employee conduct and any actions taken by the management or supervisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are accessing the correct form for your needs. The form is designed to document incidents or disciplinary actions.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee involved.
	* **Incident Details**: Choose how the incident was reported (e.g., Employee Incident Report Forms, Other).
	* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
	* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
	* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
	* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
	* **Employee Department**: Enter the department the employee is associated with.
	* **Incident Reported By**: Enter who reported the incident (e.g., supervisor, colleague).
	* **Incident Resolved**: Indicate if the incident has been resolved (optional).
	* **Disciplinary Action Taken**: Describe any disciplinary action taken against the employee (optional).
3. Sign and date the form:
	* **Employee Signature**: Sign the form to confirm you are the employee involved.
	* **Supervisor Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm you, as the supervisor, have reviewed and approved the incident report.
	* **Date Signed**: Enter the date the supervisor signed the form.
	* **Time Signed**: Enter the time the supervisor signed the form.
