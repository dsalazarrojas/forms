# Employee Safety Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Safety Incident Report form is used to document and report workplace safety incidents, such as injuries, illnesses, and property damage, that occur within the organization. This form helps gather essential information about the incident, including its date, type, location, description, and involved employees, as well as the status of the follow-up activities. This information is crucial for maintaining a safe and healthy work environment and for investigating and preventing future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Incident Date field with the date the incident occurred.
2. Select the type of incident that best describes what happened (Injury, Illness, Property Damage, or Other).
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the Incident Description field.
4. If applicable, add the location where the incident occurred in the Incident Location field.
5. If applicable, provide more information about the location in the Incident Location Details field.
6. List the names of any employees involved in the incident in the Involved Employees field.
7. Choose the name of the employee who reported the incident in the Incident Reported By field.
8. Enter the phone number of the employee who reported the incident in the Incident Reporter Phone field.
9. Enter the email address of the employee who reported the incident in the Incident Reporter Email field.
10. If applicable, attach any supporting files that can help with the investigation in the Supporting Files field.
11. Fill in the Incident Follow-up field with the date of the follow-up activity.
12. Select the current status of the incident in the Incident Status field (Open or Closed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that best describes what happened. This helps us understand the severity and urgency of the incident.
* **Incident Location**: If you are reporting an incident that occurred on company property, enter the address or the location where the incident happened.
* **Incident Location Details**: Provide more information about the location where the incident occurred, if necessary.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including any injuries or damage sustained.
* **Involved Employees**: List the names of any employees who were involved in the incident.
* **Incident Reported By**: Choose the name of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Email**: Enter the email address of the employee who reported the incident.
* **Supporting Files**: If you have any files that can help with the investigation, attach them here.
* **Incident Follow-up**: Enter the date of any follow-up activities related to the incident.
* **Incident Status**: Select the current status of the incident (Open or Closed).
