# Incident Investigation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide employees in reporting incidents or accidents within the workplace, providing a structured approach to collecting and documenting necessary information for investigation and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and answer all fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Fill out the form on your own or with the assistance of a supervisor or HR representative if needed.
3.  Make sure to provide detailed information in the fields marked as "required" as these are essential for a thorough investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <label>Employee Incident Report Form</label>
**Employee Incident Report Form** (`employee_incident_report_form`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main form where you can describe the incident in detail, including what happened, when, and where.

### 2. <label>Investigation Actions</label>
**Investigation Actions** (`investigation_actions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the actions that have been or will be taken during the investigation, such as reviewing the incident report form, conducting a thorough investigation, interviewing witnesses, or reviewing security footage.

### 3. <label>Root Cause</label>
**Root Cause** (`root_cause`, `select_one`, optional): Select the root cause of the incident from the options provided, such as human error, equipment malfunction, environmental factor, or unforeseen circumstance.

### 4. <label>Employee Injury Details</label>
**Employee Injury Details** (`employee_injury_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the details of the employee's injury, including any medical treatment received or required.

### 5. <label>Investigation Status</label>
**Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of the investigation from the options provided, such as not started, in progress, completed, or on hold.

# Tips
*   Make sure to answer all fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
*   Keep a copy of the form for your records, in case you need to refer back to it later.
