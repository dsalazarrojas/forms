# Disciplinary action form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary action form is used to document and track disciplinary actions taken against employees. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records of incidents and actions taken, ensuring that disciplinary actions are properly addressed and resolved in a timely and fair manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information and materials before starting the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields, such as Employee Name, Date of Incident, Description of Incident, Action Taken, Reviewer Name, Reviewer Comment, Form Submitted By, Date Submitted, and Time Submitted.
3.  Provide a brief description of the incident in the Description of Incident field.
4.  Select the appropriate action taken from the Action Taken dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the Reviewer's name and comment in the corresponding fields.
6.  Enter the form submitter's name in the Form Submitted By field.
7.  Fill in the Date and Time Submitted fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Incident** (`employee_incident`, note, required/optional): This field is for describing the nature of the incident.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required): Please enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident** (`description_of_incident`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident. This is a crucial field for maintaining a clear record of what happened.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name involved in the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the disciplinary action was taken, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the manager who handled the incident.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Reviewer Comment** (`reviewer_comment`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name and role as the form submitter.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form.

Please submit this form accurately and thoroughly filled out.
