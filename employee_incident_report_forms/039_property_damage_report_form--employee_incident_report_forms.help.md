# Property Damage Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document property damage incidents, providing a standardized way to collect relevant information and ensure consistency in reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the incident that caused the property damage.
2. Specify the location where the damage occurred.
3. Describe the nature of the damage in detail.
4. Enter the date when the damage occurred.
5. If you are not the owner of the damaged property, provide the name and contact information of the person or party responsible for the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of the incident that caused the property damage.
* **Damage Location** (`damage_location`, text, required: false): Specify the location where the damage occurred, including the room number or building name.
* **Description of Damage** (`damage_description`, text, required: false): Describe the nature of the damage in detail, including any visible signs or symptoms.
* **Date of Damage** (`damage_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the damage occurred.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): If you are not the owner of the damaged property, provide the name of the person or party responsible for the property.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): If you are not the owner of the damaged property, provide the contact phone number of the person or party responsible for the property.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident and damage.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or manager for assistance.
* Do not submit a report if you have already reported the incident.
