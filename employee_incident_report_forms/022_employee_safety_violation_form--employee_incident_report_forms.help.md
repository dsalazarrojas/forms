# Employee Safety Violation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Safety Violation Form is designed to gather information about any safety-related incidents or hazards that occurred in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the report date of the incident.
2. Enter the time of the incident (or closest approximation if you're not sure).
3. Describe the safety violation or hazard that occurred in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Report Date**
(`report_date`, date, Required: no)
Enter the date of the incident.

### 2. **Time of Violation**
(`time_of_violation`, time, Required: no)
Enter the time of the incident (if possible). If you're not sure, your best approximation is fine.

### 3. **Employee Safety Violation Form**
(`employee_safety_violation_form`, text, Required: no)
Please provide a detailed description of what happened. What did you see, hear, or experience? What safety protocols were not followed or were compromised? Try to be as detailed as possible to help us get a clear picture of the incident.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible.
* Do not duplicate reports if you've already submitted one.

Note: This form is for reporting safety concerns or hazards in the workplace. If you have questions or concerns not related to safety, please contact your HR or facilities department directly.
