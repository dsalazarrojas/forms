# Defective Product Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Defective Product Log Form is used to document and report incidents involving defective products. This form helps to gather essential information about the defective product, including its details, defect date and time, and the employee's contact information. The collected data will help in maintaining accurate records and facilitating swift resolution of product defects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are reporting a genuine incident involving a defective product.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any field, use your best judgment and provide as much detail as you can.
4. Submit the form to ensure the relevant teams are notified.

## Tips
- Ensure you report the incident in a timely manner to facilitate swift resolution.
- Use clear and concise language in filling in the form to ensure easy understanding.
- If any field is required, do not leave it blank; instead, enter "N/A" or "No Data Available" if applicable.
