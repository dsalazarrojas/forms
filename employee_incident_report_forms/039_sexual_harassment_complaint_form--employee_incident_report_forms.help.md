It is intended for use by employees to report incidents of sexual harassment that they have experienced or witnessed. The form should only be completed by employees who have experienced or witnessed such incidents. It is not intended for use by managers or other staff members to report incidents on behalf of employees. If you are a manager or staff member, please direct the employee to complete this form on their own. This form should only be completed once an incident has occurred. If you have already reported this incident, please do not complete this form again. If you are unsure whether the incident you have experienced or witnessed falls under this category, please consult with HR before completing this form.

# Sexual Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a sexual harassment complaint from an employee. It is intended for use by employees who have experienced or witnessed such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, date of incident, location of incident, and description of incident.
2. If you witnessed the incident, enter the name of the person who was harassed. If you were the victim, enter your own name.
3. Select the type of harassment that occurred (e.g., verbal, physical, etc.).
4. Describe the actions you took or would have taken in response to the harassment.
5. Check the support you need to feel safe and supported in the workplace.
6. Choose the reason you are reporting the incident (e.g., to report a crime, for HR review, etc.).
7. Select the status of the incident (e.g., new, under investigation, etc.).
8. Provide any follow-up actions you would like to have taken.
9. Describe any next steps you think should be taken.
10. Finally, sign the form with your signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge or in our HR system.
* **Date Of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required): Please enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Location Of Incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, required): Please enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description Of Incident** (`description_of_incident`, note, required): Please describe what happened, including any details you feel are relevant to the incident.
* **Witness** (`witness`, text, required): If someone witnessed the incident, please enter their name.
* **Harasser Name** (`harasser_name`, text, required): If you were the victim, please enter your own name. If someone witnessed the incident, please enter the name of the harasser.
* **Type Of Harassment** (`type_of_harassment`, select_multiple, required): Please select the type of harassment that occurred (e.g., verbal, physical, etc.).
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, text, required): Please describe the actions you took or would have taken in response to the harassment.
* **Support Needed** (`support_needed`, select_one, required): Please choose how you would like to be supported (e.g., counseling, HR meeting, etc.).
* **Reporting Employee** (`reporting_employee`, select_one, required): Please select the reason you are reporting the incident (e.g., to report a crime, for HR review, etc.).
* **Reporting Reason** (`reporting_reason`, text, required): Please describe the reason you are reporting the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): Please select the status of the incident (e.g., new, under investigation, etc.).
* **Follow Up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text, required): Please describe any follow-up actions you would like to have taken.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Please describe any next steps you think should be taken.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, required): Please sign your name with a dated signature.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please enter the current date.

**Tips**

* Please be as thorough as possible when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask HR for guidance.
* Completing this form is a confidential process, and the information you provide will be treated as such.
