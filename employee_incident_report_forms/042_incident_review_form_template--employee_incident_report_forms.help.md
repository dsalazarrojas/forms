# Incident Review Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Incident Review Form Template is used for documenting and reviewing incidents that have occurred within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out the form accurately and completely, using your best judgment and expertise.
*   Review each section carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, please seek clarification from your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): The date on which the incident occurred.
    *   Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): The time at which the incident occurred.
    *   Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): The location where the incident occurred.
    *   Please enter the location in plain text.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): A brief description of what happened during the incident.
    *   Please be as detailed and accurate as possible.
*   **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`): The underlying reason for the incident.
    *   Please investigate and identify the root cause of the incident.
*   **Incident Effect** (`incident_effect`): The impact of the incident on the organization, employees, or property.
    *   Please quantify the effect, if possible (e.g., `loss of X dollars` or `Y number of injuries`).
*   **Incident Effect Details** (`incident_effect_details`): Additional information about the incident's effect.
    *   Please provide as much detail as possible.
*   **Incident Effect Summary** (`incident_effect_summary`): A brief summary of the incident's effect.
    *   Please keep the summary concise and to the point.
*   **Incident Recommendations** (`incident_recommendations`): Suggestions for next steps or changes to prevent similar incidents in the future.
    *   Please be as specific and actionable as possible.
*   **Incident Next Steps** (`incident_next_steps`): Steps taken or planned to mitigate or rectify the incident's effects.
    *   Please list all next steps and their corresponding deadlines.
*   **Incident Review Status** (`incident_review_status`): The current status of the incident review process.
    *   Select from the options: `Review In Progress`, `Completed`, `Deferred`, or `Approved`.
*   **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`): The name of the person who submitted the incident report.
    *   Please enter the name as it appears on the report.
*   **Incident Reviewer** (`incident_reviewer`): The name of the person reviewing the incident.
    *   Please enter the name as it appears on the report.
*   **Incident Reviewer Department** (`incident_reviewer_department`): The department of the incident reviewer.
    *   Please enter the department as it appears on the report.
*   **Incident Reviewer Role** (`incident_reviewer_role`): The role of the incident reviewer.
    *   Please enter the role as it appears on the report.
*   **Incident Reviewer Contact** (`incident_reviewer_contact`): The contact information of the incident reviewer.
    *   Please enter the contact information as it appears on the report.
*   **Incident Reviewer Email** (`incident_reviewer_email`): The email of the incident reviewer.
    *   Please enter the email as it appears on the report.
*   **Incident Review Date** (`incident_review_date`): The date the incident was reviewed.
    *   Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Incident Review Time** (`incident_review_time`): The time the incident was reviewed.
    *   Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
*   **Incident Review Status** (`incident_review_status`): The status of the incident review process.
    *   Select from the options: `Approved`, `In Progress`, `Completed`, or `Deferred`.
*   **Incident Review Comment** (`incident_review_comment`): Comments or feedback from the incident reviewer.
    *   Please provide constructive comments or suggestions.
*   **Incident Recommendation** (`incident_recommendation`): Additional recommendations or changes to prevent similar incidents.
    *   Please provide specific, actionable suggestions.
*   **Incident Next Review Date** (`incident_next_review_date`): The date for the next review of the incident.
    *   Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Incident Next Review Time** (`incident_next_review_time`): The time for the next review of the incident.
    *   Please enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
*   **Incident Next Reviewer** (`incident_next_reviewer`): The name of the person responsible for the next review.
    *   Please enter the name as it appears on the report.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields, and explanations are provided with the best-effort intent of the field labels.
