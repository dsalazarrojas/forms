# Equipment Damage Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report equipment damage incidents, including the date and description of the incident, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date the equipment was damaged in the "Equipment Damage Date" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the damage in the "Equipment Damage Description" field.
3. Check if the equipment was reported by answering "Yes" or "No" in the "Reported By" field.
4. Enter the date the report was made in the "Reported On" field.
5. Select the type of equipment damage using the "Equipment Damage Type" field.
6. Enter the location where the equipment was damaged in the "Location" field.
7. Enter the name of the damaged equipment in the "Equipment Name" field.
8. Enter the equipment's model in the "Equipment Model" field (optional).
9. Enter the equipment's serial number in the "Equipment Serial Number" field.
10. Set the equipment's status to "Active" or "Inactive" using the "Equipment Damage Status" field.
11. Enter the department of the person reporting the incident in the "Reported By Department" field.
12. Check if the incident was reported by a supervisor in the "Reported By Supervisor" field.
13. Enter any witness information in the "Witness" field.
14. Enter the witness's name in the "Witness Name" field.
15. Enter any witness's contact information in the "Witness Contact Info" field (optional).
16. Enter the estimated cost of the equipment's repair or replacement in the "Equipment Cost" field.
17. Enter any additional damage location information in the "Damage Location" field.
18. Enter any other equipment status details in the "Equipment Status" field.
19. Select the type of damage using the "Damage Type" field.
20. Enter any additional notes about the incident in the "Additional Notes" field.
21. Enter any details about the causes of the equipment damage in the "Equipment Damage Causes" field.
22. Enter the last used by person's name in the "Last Used By" field.
23. Enter the date the equipment was last used in the "Last Used Date" field (optional).
24. Enter the last maintenance date for the equipment in the "Last Maintained" field (optional).
25. Enter the next scheduled maintenance date for the equipment in the "Next Maintenance" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Damage Date**: Enter the date the equipment was damaged.
* **Equipment Damage Description**: Provide a detailed description of the damage.
* **Reported By**: Check if the equipment was reported by a supervisor.
* **Reported On**: Enter the date the report was made.
* **Equipment Damage Type**: Select the type of equipment damage.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment was damaged.
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the damaged equipment.
* **Equipment Model**: Enter the model of the equipment (if applicable).
* **Equipment Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Equipment Damage Status**: Set the equipment's status to "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Reported By Department**: Enter the department of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported By Supervisor**: Check if the incident was reported by a supervisor.
* **Witness**: Enter any witness information.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the witness's name.
* **Witness Contact Info**: Enter any contact information for the witness (optional).
* **Equipment Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of repairing or replacing the equipment.
* **Damage Location**: Enter any additional location information.
* **Equipment Status**: Enter any other equipment status details.
* **Damage Type**: Select the type of damage.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Equipment Damage Causes**: Enter any details about the causes of the equipment damage.
* **Last Used By**: Enter the name of the last used by person.
* **Last Used Date**: Enter the date the equipment was last used (optional).
* **Last Maintained**: Enter the last maintenance date for the equipment (optional).
* **Next Maintenance**: Enter the next scheduled maintenance date for the equipment (optional).

# Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to avoid delays in processing.
* If you are unsure whether you need to report an incident, contact the maintenance team for guidance.
* Please note that this form is not used for reporting minor scratches or small issues.
