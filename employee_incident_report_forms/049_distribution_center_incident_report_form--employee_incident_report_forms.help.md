# Distribution Center Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that occurred at our distribution centers. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about the incident, including the date, time, description, location, cause, and any injuries involved. This form helps our team to better understand and address incidents in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the `incident_date` field.
2. Enter the time of the incident in the `incident_time` field.
3. Describe the incident in detail in the `incident_description` field.
4. If the incident occurred at a specific location, enter the location in the `incident_location` field. This field is optional.
5. Specify the cause of the incident in the `incident_cause` field.
6. Provide additional details about the incident in the `incident_details` field.
7. If anyone was injured during the incident, indicate "Yes" in the `incident_injuries` field.
8. If you have a photograph or video of the incident, you can attach it in the `incident_incident_image` and `incident_incident_video` fields, respectively. These fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **incident_date** (Date): Enter the date of the incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **incident_time** (Time): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (`HH:MM`).
* **incident_description** (Description): Describe the incident in detail, including what happened and any relevant details.
* **incident_location** (Location): Enter the location where the incident occurred. This field is optional.
* **incident_cause** (Cause): Specify the reason why the incident occurred.
* **incident_details** (Details): Provide additional information about the incident.
* **incident_injuries** (Injuries): Indicate if anyone was injured during the incident.
* **incident_incident_image** (Incident Image): Attach a photograph of the incident.
* **incident_incident_video** (Incident Video): Attach a video of the incident.
