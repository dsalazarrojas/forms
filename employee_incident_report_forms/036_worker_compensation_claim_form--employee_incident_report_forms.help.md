# Worker Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that may lead to a worker's compensation claim. It gathers information from employees about the incident, including their details and relevant contact information for their employer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the necessary information about yourself and the incident.
2. Enter your name and ID as required by your employer's worker's compensation policy.
3. Provide the date and time of the incident.
4. Describe the location where the incident occurred.
5. Give a detailed account of what happened during the incident.
6. Enter your employer's name and address.
7. Provide the name and contact information of your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number as required by your employer's worker's compensation policy.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Accident Description** (`accident_description`, text, required): Give a detailed account of what happened during the incident, including any injuries or damages sustained.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter your employer's name as it appears on your ID or payroll records.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required): Enter your employer's address as it appears on your ID or payroll records.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor or manager, who can be contacted for further information.
