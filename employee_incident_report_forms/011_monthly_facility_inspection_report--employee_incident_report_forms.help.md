# monthly_facility_inspection_report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The monthly_facility_inspection_report form is used to document and report on the inspection of facilities within the organization on a monthly basis. The information collected helps ensure that facilities are regularly inspected and any issues are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the month of the inspection from the Month dropdown menu. This will help the facilities management team track the inspection schedule.
2. Enter the Year of the inspection in the Year field. This is a required field as it helps the team track the historical data.
3. Provide the name of the facility being inspected in the Facility Name field. This is also a required field as it helps the team identify the inspected facility.
4. Select any issues found during the inspection from the Issues Found dropdown menu. Choose all applicable options to ensure accurate reporting.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Month** (`month`, `select_one`, required): Select the month of the inspection from the dropdown menu. This helps the facilities management team track the inspection schedule.
* **Year** (`year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of the inspection. This helps the team track the historical data.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the facility being inspected. This helps the team identify the inspected facility.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any issues found during the inspection from the dropdown menu. Choose all applicable options to ensure accurate reporting.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection. This helps provide context and information for the facilities management team.

## Tips
* Please ensure you only complete this form if it's a monthly facility inspection report for a specific facility.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to your supervisor or facilities management team for assistance.
