# Employee Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents that have occurred during work hours or work-related activities. This form will help the organization to track and manage incidents, ensuring a safe and incident-free work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name.
2. Provide the Date Of Incident when the incident occurred.
3. Describe the Description Of Incident in a few sentences.
4. Enter the Supervisor Name who is aware of this incident.
5. Provide the Supervisor Email so that they can be contacted for further information.
6. If applicable, enter the Incident Location where the incident occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your official employee name as it appears on your personnel records.
  - **Date Of Incident** (date, required): Select the date when the incident occurred.
  - **Description Of Incident** (text, optional): Briefly describe what happened during the incident. Be as detailed as possible, including any circumstances leading up to and following the incident.
  - **Supervisor Name** (text, optional): The name of your supervisor or manager who is aware of this incident.
  - **Supervisor Email** (email, optional): The email address of the supervisor or manager who can be contacted for further information.
  - **Incident Location** (text, optional): The location where the incident occurred. If not applicable, please leave blank.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in the form.
- If unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
- This form will help us keep track of incidents and improve our work environment, so please report all incidents that you feel comfortable sharing.
