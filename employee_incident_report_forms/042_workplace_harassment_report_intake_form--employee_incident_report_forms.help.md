# Workplace Harassment Report Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Harassment Report Intake Form is a tool used to report incidents of harassment in the workplace. It is designed to collect and document information about reported incidents in a systematic and consistent manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the incident date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Enter the incident time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
3. Describe the incident in detail, including the location where it occurred.
4. Enter your name and contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Select one of the options for Select 1, Select 2, and Select 3 (if applicable).
6. Review the terms and conditions and select your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Incident Location**: Describe the location where the incident occurred.
- **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
- **Reporter**: Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
- **Email**: Enter your work email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **Select 1**: Choose one of the options provided.
- **Select 2**: Choose one of the options provided.
- **Select 3**: Choose one of the options provided.
- **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions**: Select your agreement to the terms and conditions.
- **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to the designated contact person for assistance.
- If you have any additional information or supporting documents, please attach them to the report.
- The form should take no more than 10-15 minutes to complete.
