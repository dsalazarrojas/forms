# Risk Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Risk Incident Form is used to report any incidents that pose a risk to the well-being or safety of employees. This includes, but is not limited to, accidents, injuries, near-misses, and any other events that may potentially harm employees or affect their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure that you are providing all relevant information about the incident.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Info** (General Info, text, required: false):
    *   This field is used to enter your general information and any relevant details about the incident.
*   **Risk Incident** (Risk Incident, text, required: false):
    *   Describe the risk incident that occurred, including any relevant circumstances or factors that contributed to it.
*   **Incident Information** (Incident Information, text, required: false):
    *   Provide additional information about the incident, such as the date and time it occurred, the location, and any witnesses.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed as accurately and completely as possible.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have questions about the form, do not hesitate to reach out for assistance.
*   Reporting incidents is crucial for maintaining a safe work environment, so please take the time to fill out this form carefully.
*   You can contact form@gic.mx if you need further assistance or clarification.
